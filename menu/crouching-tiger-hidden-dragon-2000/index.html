Snacks

Teahouse dumplings

Steamed pork and cabbage dumplings with black vinegar and chilli oil.

Prep 32 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead on a floured tray in the fridge, or freeze for up to 1 month; steam fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 2 cups shredded wombok
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the pork with wombok, ginger and spring onions.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat.
  3. Steam in bamboo baskets.
  4. Serve with black vinegar and chilli oil.

Desert lamb skewers

Cumin and chilli lamb skewers, Lo and Jen's desert cave.

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Toss the lamb with the spices and thread onto skewers up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds, crushed, plus extra to serve
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Toss the lamb with cumin, chilli flakes and salt.
  2. Thread onto skewers.
  3. Grill hot until charred at the edges.
  4. Sprinkle with more cumin to serve.

Bamboo forest garlic greens

Bok choy, snow peas and shoots flashed in a smoking wok, oyster sauce and sesame oil.

How it is made
  • 4 baby bok choy
  • 150g snow peas
  • 225g tinned bamboo shoots
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bottle oyster sauce
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Baby bok choy, snow peas and bamboo shoots stir-fried fast in a smoking wok with garlic, oyster sauce and sesame oil, served in a bamboo steamer basket. Halve 4 baby bok choy and trim 150g snow peas. Drain a 225g tin of bamboo shoots. Heat a wok until smoking, add 2 tbsp oil and 4 sliced garlic cloves and stir 10 seconds. Add the bok choy stems, snow peas and bamboo shoots and toss 1 minute, then the leaves with a splash of water. Add 2 tbsp oyster sauce and a few drops of sesame oil, toss and serve in a bamboo basket.

Mains

Yu Shu Lien's steamed whole fish

Whole snapper steamed with ginger, topped with soy, spring onion and sizzling oil.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Slice the ginger and spring onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; steam the fish and pour over the hot oil on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg whole snapper
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Score the snapper and slice the ginger and spring onions.
  2. Lay the fish on sliced ginger and spring onion.
  3. Steam until the flesh flakes.
  4. Pile shredded spring onion and coriander on top.
  5. Pour over soy, then smoking hot oil so it sizzles.

Peking-style crisp duck with pancakes

Crackling roast legs shredded into thin wraps with cucumber and hoisin, served at Governor Yu's.

How it is made
  • 4 duck legs
  • 1 jar five spice
  • 20 Chinese pancakes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 jar hoisin sauce
  1. Duck legs salted overnight and roasted until the skin crackles, shredded into steamed pancakes with cucumber, spring onion and sweet hoisin. Rub 4 duck legs with 2 tsp salt and a little five spice and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Roast skin up on a rack at 180°C for 90 minutes, then at 220°C for 10 minutes until crisp. Shred with two forks. Steam 20 Chinese pancakes for 3 minutes. Cut a cucumber and 4 spring onions into thin batons. Serve everything with a bowl of hoisin to build your own.

Sweets

Green Destiny sesame ballsVegetarian

Fried red bean rice balls in sesame. The sword breaks every blade.

Prep 27 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Wrap the dough around the bean paste and roll in sesame up to 1 day ahead on a tray in the fridge; fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g glutinous rice flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 200ml hot water
  • 200g red bean paste
  • 3 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the rice flour and sugar with hot water into a dough.
  2. Wrap the dough around small balls of red bean paste.
  3. Roll in sesame seeds.
  4. Heat the oil to 160°C and fry in batches until puffed and golden.

Drinks

Teahouse jasmine pearl green teaNo alcohol

Jasmine pearl green tea in a pot, small cups.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Nothing worth doing ahead; brew fresh at the table so the tea stays sweet
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L water
  • 4 tsp jasmine pearl green tea
  • 1 packet dried longan
  1. Heat the water to about 80°C, or boil it and leave it to stand for 5 minutes.
  2. Warm a teapot with hot water and tip it out.
  3. Add the tea, pour over the water and steep.
  4. Pour into small cups and refill the pot for a second and third steep.
  5. Serve a bowl of dried longan on the side.

When to serve

Serve the tea when the fight breaks out over the tables.
