Sonny's sports bar barbecue wings

Crisp baked wings glazed in sticky barbecue sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings in the baking powder mix and keep uncovered in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; mix the ranch up to 2 days ahead; bake and sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 100g sour cream
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Toss the wings with the baking powder, paprika and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once.
  3. Simmer the barbecue sauce with the honey and hot sauce, toss the wings through and bake again.
  4. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, chives and garlic powder for the ranch.
  5. Scatter the wings with spring onion and serve with the ranch.

Central Park everything bagel bites

Mini bagels with cream cheese and everything seasoning.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the seasoning up to 1 week ahead; beat the cream cheese and crisp the bacon up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; toast the bagels and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp poppy seeds
  • 1 tsp dried onion flakes
  • 1/2 tsp garlic flakes
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 4 rashers of bacon
  • 12 mini bagels
  1. Mix the seeds, onion flakes, garlic flakes and salt for the seasoning.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the spring onions until fluffy.
  3. Grill the bacon until crisp and crumble.
  4. Split and toast the bagels.
  5. Spread with the cream cheese, top with bacon and sprinkle with the seasoning.

Tollbooth shift cheeseburgers

Beef cheeseburgers with bacon, crinkle fries and onion rings.

Prep 4 min, cook 44 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls up to 1 day ahead and prep the toppings up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; bake the fries and cook the burgers on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g frozen crinkle-cut fries
  • 300g frozen onion rings
  • 6 rashers of bacon
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 8 slices of cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 small iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Bake the fries and onion rings at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Grill the bacon.
  3. Roll the mince into balls, smash flat in a very hot pan with the vegetable oil, season and cook, then flip and top with the cheddar.
  4. Toast the buns.
  5. Set out the lettuce, tomatoes, pickles, tomato sauce, mustard and mayonnaise and let everyone build their own.

Sweets

Blue jelly ice cream sundaes

Blue jelly, vanilla ice cream, cream, biscuit sand and gummy fish.

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and set the jelly up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cube it, whip the cream and build the sundaes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet blue jelly crystals
  • 6 plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 1 bag gummy fish
  • 1 bottle chocolate sauce
  1. Make up the jelly with boiling water and pour into a shallow dish.
  2. Set the jelly in the fridge, then cut into cubes.
  3. Crush the biscuits into sand.
  4. In tall glasses layer jelly cubes, ice cream and more jelly.
  5. Top with whipped cream, biscuit sand, gummy fish and a drizzle of chocolate sauce.

Frankenstein's chocolate malt milkshakeNo alcohol

Ice cream, milk, chocolate and malt blended thick, a monster-sized shake.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer and whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead; blend just before serving as the shake melts fast
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold full cream milk
  • 6 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 5 tbsp chocolate malt powder, plus extra to sprinkle
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 bottle chocolate sauce
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Chill tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Drizzle chocolate sauce down the inside of each glass.
  3. Blend the milk, ice cream, malt powder and cocoa until thick, then pour into the glasses.
  4. Top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of malt powder.
