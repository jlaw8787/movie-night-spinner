Snacks

Tennessee snowfall popcornVegetarian

Popcorn drizzled with white chocolate and sea salt, broken into clusters.

Prep 0 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 200g white chocolate
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Pop the corn and toss with the melted butter. Spread on a lined tray.
  2. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle all over, then sprinkle with sea salt flakes.
  3. Leave to set for 30 minutes and break into clusters like snow.

Malibu mansion prawns

Butterflied prawns with garlic, chilli and lemon, grilled hot, with lemon mayo.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 2 hours, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 2 lemons, one zested and one juiced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g mayonnaise
  1. Butterfly the prawns and toss with the garlic, red chillies, lemon zest and oil.
  2. Grill over high heat for 2 minutes a side until charred.
  3. Pile onto a platter and serve with mayonnaise mixed with lemon juice.

Harley's garage ham and cheese toastiesOn the printed menu

Thick ham, sharp cheddar and mustard in buttered bread, toasted oozing, for fixing the suit.

How it is made
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 1 jar mustard
  • 200g sliced ham
  • 160g cheddar
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Thick ham, sharp cheddar and a scrape of mustard toasted in buttered bread until crisp and oozing, cut into fingers for fixing the suit. Butter one side of 8 slices of white bread. Lay 4 slices butter side down and spread lightly with 2 tbsp mustard. Top with 200g sliced ham and 160g grated cheddar, then the remaining bread, butter side up. Cook in a frying pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side, pressing down, until golden and melted. Cut each into 3 fingers and serve with pickles.

Mains

Tennessee hot chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken brushed with cayenne hot oil, served on white bread.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate overnight, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour, seasoned
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp cayenne pepper
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  1. Marinate the chicken in the buttermilk in the fridge, at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. Dredge in seasoned flour and fry at 165°C until crisp and cooked through.
  3. Whisk a few spoonfuls of the hot oil with the cayenne, brown sugar and paprika and brush over the chicken.
  4. Serve on white bread with pickles.

Christmas turkey roll with sage stuffingOn the printed menu

Rolled turkey breast with bacon stuffing.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg turkey breast
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 bacon rashers
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 egg
  • 50g butter
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Turkey breast rolled around sage, onion and bacon stuffing, roasted golden, with crisp roast potatoes, gravy and cranberry sauce. Mix 100g breadcrumbs, 1 softened onion, 2 chopped bacon rashers, 1 tbsp chopped sage and 1 egg. Butterfly a 1.2kg turkey breast, spread with the stuffing, roll and tie with string. Rub with butter and salt and roast at 180°C for 90 minutes, resting 15 minutes. Roast 1kg parboiled potato chunks in oil for 50 minutes. Make gravy from the pan juices, 1 tbsp flour and 300ml stock. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Pepper's giant Christmas bunny cake

Vanilla bunny cake with coconut. It's a big bunny. Relax about it.

Prep 50 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead, decorate on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 250g butter, soft
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 cup desiccated coconut
  • 1 cup marshmallows
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two round tins at 175°C. Cool.
  3. Cut one round into ears and a bow tie and assemble into a bunny.
  4. Mix the icing sugar with a little water, cover the bunny and press on coconut. Add marshmallows.

Rose Hill malted milkshakesVegetarianNo alcohol

Chocolate malted milkshakes topped with whipped cream and crushed chocolate.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L chocolate ice cream
  • 500ml cold milk
  • 6 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 cup chocolate malt balls
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and malt powder until thick and smooth, in batches.
  2. Pour into four chilled tall glasses. Whip the cream to soft peaks and spoon on top.
  3. Crush the malt balls and scatter over. Serve straight away with wide straws and long spoons.
