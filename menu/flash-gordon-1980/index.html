Snacks

Panko chicken bites with honey mustard

Football-shaped panko chicken pieces with a honey Dijon dipping sauce.

Prep 17 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 4 hours ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs, diced
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Press the chicken into small oval football shapes.
  2. Coat in flour, egg and panko.
  3. Shallow fry in the oil until golden and cooked through.
  4. Mix the honey with the Dijon for dipping and pile the footballs on a platter.

Hawkmen sky city wings

Baked wings in a sticky honey, soy and chilli glaze with sesame.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
  1. Toss the wings with salt and bake at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Warm the honey, soy and chilli until sticky.
  3. Toss the wings through the glaze and scatter with sesame seeds.

Arboria forest stuffed mushrooms

Flat caps packed with garlic butter, bacon, crumbs and parmesan, before the tree stump test.

How it is made
  • 8 flat mushrooms
  • 100g bacon
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon
  1. Flat mushrooms packed with garlic butter, crisp bacon, breadcrumbs, parmesan and parsley, baked until golden and bubbling, finished with lemon. Remove the stems from 8 flat mushrooms and chop them. Fry 100g diced bacon until crisp, add the stems and 2 garlic cloves and cook 2 minutes. Mix with 60g breadcrumbs, 40g grated parmesan, chopped parsley and 40g melted butter. Pile into the mushroom caps and bake at 200°C for 12 minutes until golden. Squeeze over lemon to serve.

Mains

Zarkov's rocket fuel curry

Red curry prawns with coconut milk, red capsicum and Thai basil. Fuel for the rocket ship.

Prep 0 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the curry sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; add the prawns on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 600g green prawns, peeled
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Fry the curry paste in the oil until fragrant. Add the prawns and brown.
  2. Pour in the coconut milk and simmer, adding the capsicum for the last 5 minutes.
  3. Cook the rice. Stir the Thai basil through the curry and serve with the rice.

Red banquet beef fillet

Roasted medium rare, roast onions, beetroot and glossy wine sauce, for the forced wedding to Dale.

How it is made
  • 4 beetroot
  • 4 red onions
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 bottle balsamic vinegar
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 200ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 20g butter
  1. Beef eye fillet roasted to medium rare and sliced on a long platter with roasted red onions, beetroot and a rich, glossy red wine and thyme sauce. Roast 4 beetroot wrapped in foil at 200°C for 1 hour, then peel and cut into wedges. Halve 4 red onions and roast with oil and balsamic for 40 minutes. Sear a 1kg eye fillet on all sides and roast at 220°C for 20 minutes to medium rare, then rest. Reduce 200ml red wine with 300ml beef stock and thyme until glossy and whisk in 20g butter. Slice and arrange on a long platter.

Sweets

Mongo red and gold trifle

Sponge, raspberry jelly, mango, custard and cream with gold sprinkles.

Prep 25 min, then 11 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the night before, finish on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sponge cake, cubed
  • 1 packet raspberry jelly crystals
  • 2 mangoes, sliced
  • 500ml custard
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 1 tbsp gold sprinkles
  1. Press the sponge cubes into a glass bowl. Make up the jelly following the packet and pour over.
  2. Chill until set.
  3. Layer with sliced mango, custard and whipped cream. Chill overnight.
  4. Finish with gold sprinkles just before serving.

Drinks

Princess Aura's red punchNo alcohol

Pomegranate and cranberry juice with soda and lime over ice, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pomegranate juice
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 1 L cold soda water
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice. Pour in equal parts pomegranate and cranberry juice to about halfway.
  2. Top with soda water, squeeze in a wedge of lime and stir once. Serve straight away.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Flash starts the football brawl in Ming's palace.
