Miss Torso cucumber soup shotsVegetarian

Chilled cucumber, yoghurt, dill and mint blitzed smooth

Prep 10 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cucumbers
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 handful dill
  • 1 handful mint leaves
  • 1 small clove garlic
  • 1 lemon
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Blend the cucumbers with the yoghurt, dill, mint, garlic and lemon juice until smooth. Season well.
  2. Chill.
  3. Pour into 8 small glasses.

Dijon, sliced ham and Swiss cheese on rye, cut in quarters

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 250g ham, sliced
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  1. Spread the rye slices with Dijon.
  2. Fill 4 with ham and Swiss cheese and close.
  3. Cut each into quarters and stack on a plate.

Mains

Lisa's lobster dinner

Baked lobster tails in Dijon cream sauce with parmesan

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 800g potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 300g green beans
  1. Halve the lobster tails and bake at 200C.
  2. Boil the potatoes. Steam the beans.
  3. Simmer the cream with Dijon until thick and stir in the parmesan.
  4. Spoon over the lobster and grill until bubbling.
  5. Toss the potatoes in butter and serve with the beans.

Sweets

Plaster cast lemon icebox pieVegetarian

Tart lemon condensed milk filling in a biscuit crust, chilled

Prep 20 min, cook 230 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 4 lemons
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Crush the biscuits with the melted butter and press into a pie dish.
  2. Whisk the condensed milk with the lemon juice and zest until it thickens.
  3. Pour into the base and chill, which is the setting time.
  4. Top with whipped cream.

Drinks

Heatwave iced coffee with ice creamVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled sweet coffee and milk over vanilla ice cream

Prep 5 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml strong coffee
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 480ml cold milk
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream (about 250 g)
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Brew the coffee, stir in the sugar and chill.
  2. Put 4 tall glasses in the freezer. Fill each a third with ice and add coffee and milk.
  3. Add a scoop of ice cream, spoon on whipped cream and dust with cocoa. Serve with a long spoon and a straw.

Serve the mains when Lisa's dinner arrives with a waiter in tow.
