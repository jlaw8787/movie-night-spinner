Snacks

Toothpick cheese cubes

Cheddar, salami and cherry tomato on toothpicks, laid out in neat rows of ten.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheddar up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; thread the toothpicks on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar
  • 100g salami
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 20 toothpicks
  1. Cut the cheddar into 20 even cubes.
  2. Thread each onto a toothpick with a fold of salami and a cherry tomato.
  3. Lay out in two neat rows of exactly ten.

Vegas buffet shrimp

Poached shrimp served on ice with horseradish cocktail sauce and lemon.

Prep 6 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Poach and peel the prawns up to 1 day ahead and mix the sauce up to 2 days ahead, both chilled; pile on ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  1. Poach the prawns in salted water for 2 minutes, plunge into iced water and peel.
  2. Mix the tomato sauce with horseradish and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Pile the prawns on crushed ice with the sauce and lemon wedges.

Pecan cheddar cheese balls on toothpicks

Cheddar and cream cheese balls rolled in toasted pecans, each on a toothpick.

How it is made
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • cayenne pepper
  • 100g pecans
  • 1 box toothpicks
  1. Bite-size balls of sharp cheddar, cream cheese, spring onion and Worcestershire rolled in toasted pecans, each speared on a toothpick and lined up in rows. Beat 250g softened cream cheese with 200g finely grated vintage cheddar, 2 finely chopped spring onions, 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce and a pinch of cayenne. Chill 30 minutes until firm. Toast 100g pecans in a dry pan for 4 minutes and chop finely. Roll the mix into 24 small balls and coat in the pecans. Chill 1 hour, push a toothpick into each and line them up in neat rows.

Mains

Pancakes, syrup firstVegetarian

Buttermilk pancakes cooked in butter, stacked high, with maple syrup.

Prep 3 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Whisk the batter up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g butter
  1. Put the maple syrup on the table before anything else.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, buttermilk and eggs and rest, 15 min.
  3. Cook in butter until bubbles set, then flip, 17 min.
  4. Stack high.

$86,000 Caesars Palace sirloin with loaded baked potato

Thick seared sirloin with garlic butter, and a baked potato with bacon and cheddar.

How it is made
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 150g bacon
  • 250g baby spinach
  • 150ml cream
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • ground nutmeg
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 40g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Thick sirloin seared hard and finished with garlic butter, a jacket potato loaded with sour cream, bacon, cheddar and chives, and creamed spinach. Rub 4 potatoes with oil and salt and bake at 200°C for 60 minutes. Fry 150g diced bacon until crisp. Wilt 250g spinach, squeeze dry and stir into 150ml cream simmered with garlic and nutmeg. Season 4 sirloin steaks and sear in a smoking pan 3 minutes a side, add 40g butter and 2 crushed garlic cloves and baste for 1 minute. Rest 5 minutes. Split the potatoes and load with sour cream, bacon, cheddar and chives.

Sweets

Fresh strawberry gelatin with vanilla cream

Set fresh strawberry gelatin topped with vanilla cream and crumbled shortbread.

Prep 20 min, 4 hr 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Set up to 1 day ahead, whip cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 600g strawberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 4 gelatine leaves
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 2 shortbread fingers
  1. Hull and blend most of the strawberries with the sugar and lemon juice, then strain.
  2. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water, squeeze and dissolve in a portion of the warmed puree, 10 min.
  3. Stir into the rest of the puree and pour into four glasses over sliced strawberries, 5 min.
  4. Chill until set, 4 hr.
  5. Whip the cream with vanilla paste and icing sugar, spoon on and crumble over the shortbread.

Drinks

Maple syrup lemon smashNo alcohol

Cold black tea shaken with maple syrup, lemon and fresh mint.

Prep 14 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead, chilled; slice the apple and press and shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml cold black tea
  • 60ml maple syrup
  • 3 lemons, juiced
  • 32 mint leaves
  • 1 green apple
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  1. Juice the lemons and slice the apple thinly.
  2. Press the mint gently with the syrup and lemon juice in a shaker, one drink at a time.
  3. Add the cold tea and ice, shake hard, and strain into rocks glasses packed with crushed ice.
  4. Garnish with a mint sprig and an apple slice.
