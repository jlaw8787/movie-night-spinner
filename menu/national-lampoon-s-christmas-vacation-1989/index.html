Snacks

Eggnog in moose mugsVegetarian

Cooked custard eggnog with fresh nutmeg, chilled overnight.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 whole nutmeg, to grate
  1. Separate the eggs and whisk the yolks with the sugar.
  2. Heat the milk and cream, pour over the yolks and stir over low heat to 75°C, 10 min.
  3. Chill overnight.
  4. Stir in the vanilla and grate fresh nutmeg on top.

Cousin Eddie's RV sausages

Charred kielbasa with slow cooked onions and wholegrain mustard on toothpicks.

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Slice and griddle the kielbasa and cook the onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; reheat in a pan and toss with the mustard to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g kielbasa
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Slice the kielbasa thick on the diagonal and slice the onions.
  2. Griddle the sausage until charred, 8 min.
  3. Cook the onions slowly in the butter until soft, 15 min.
  4. Toss together with the mustard and spear on toothpicks.

Jelly of the Month Club lime mould

Green Jell-O set with crushed pineapple and a creamy layer, no cat food inside.

How it is made
  • 2 x 85g packets lime jelly
  • 1 lime
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 440g tin crushed pineapple
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. A retro lime jelly mould set with crushed pineapple and a creamy whipped layer, turned out wobbling onto a plate with fresh mint. Dissolve two 85g packets lime jelly in 500ml boiling water and stir in 300ml cold water and 1 tbsp lime juice. Pour half into a 1.2L mould and chill 1 hour until set. Chill the other half until thick but not set, then fold through 200ml whipped cream and a drained 440g tin crushed pineapple. Pour over and chill 4 hours. Dip the mould in hot water and turn out.

Mains

Juicy roast turkey

Salted turkey breast with thyme butter, stuffing and gravy, nothing like Clark's.

Prep 8 min, cook 1 hr 23 min, 10 hr waiting · Make ahead: Salt a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast roast
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 50g soft butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 200g stuffing mix
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 375ml chicken stock
  1. Rub the turkey with the salt and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Push the butter and thyme under the skin.
  3. Roast at 170°C until it hits 65°C inside, 1 hr 15 min.
  4. Rest under foil. Make the stuffing as the packet says, 20 min.
  5. Make gravy from the pan juices with the flour and stock.

Griswold glazed ham

Leg ham studded with cloves and glazed with brown sugar and pineapple.

How it is made
  • 3kg half leg ham
  • 1 jar whole cloves
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 200ml pineapple juice
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml cream
  • 1 onion
  • 100g cheddar
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  1. Leg ham scored in diamonds, studded with cloves and glazed with brown sugar, pineapple and mustard until sticky, with potato bake and peas. Remove the skin from a 3kg half leg ham, score and stud with cloves. Simmer 150g brown sugar, 200ml pineapple juice and 2 tbsp Dijon for 5 minutes. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes, glazing every 15 minutes. Layer 1kg sliced potatoes with 300ml cream, 1 sliced onion and 100g cheddar and bake at 180°C for 60 minutes. Serve with 400g buttered peas.

Pecan pieVegetarian

Crisp shortcrust with a gooey corn syrup and pecan filling.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 20 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250ml golden syrup
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g pecans
  1. Line a tin with the pastry and blind bake, 20 min.
  2. Toast the pecans. Whisk the eggs, golden syrup, sugar, melted butter and vanilla, 10 min.
  3. Stir in the pecans, pour into the case and bake at 170°C until just set, 50 min.
  4. Cool before slicing, 30 min.

Drinks

Sledding hot chocolateNo alcohol

Hot milk whisked with dark chocolate, topped with marshmallows.

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh; chop the chocolate up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100g marshmallows
  1. Chop the chocolate.
  2. Heat the milk until steaming, then whisk in the chocolate until frothy, 7 min.
  3. Pour into four mugs and pile marshmallows on top.
