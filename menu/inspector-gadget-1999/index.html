Snacks

Gadget's toolkit skewers

Cherry tomato, mozzarella, salami and basil with olive oil and balsamic.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 250g bocconcini
  • 150g salami, sliced
  • 1 cup basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Thread tomatoes, bocconcini, folded salami and basil onto skewers.
  2. Lay on a platter and drizzle with oil and balsamic. Season. Go go gadget skewers.

Helicopter hat pizza pinwheels

Puff pastry rolls with tomato paste, ham and mozzarella, sliced and baked.

Prep 15 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Rolls up to 1 day chilled, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 4 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 150g leg ham, chopped
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  1. Spread the pastry with tomato paste mixed with oregano. Scatter with ham and mozzarella.
  2. Roll each sheet up tightly and chill for 15 minutes so it slices cleanly.
  3. Cut into 2cm rounds, lay on a lined tray and bake at 200°C until golden.
  4. Rest for a couple of minutes before serving.

Riverton soft pretzels with cheese sauce

Bicarb-dipped knots, rock salted and baked deep brown, with a hot cheddar dunk.

How it is made
  • 450g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 20g butter
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 150g cheddar
  1. Soft pretzel knots dipped in bicarb water, sprinkled with rock salt and baked deep brown, with a hot cheddar sauce for dunking. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Shape 8 knots and dip each in 2L boiling water with 60g bicarb soda for 30 seconds. Sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Melt 20g butter, stir in 1 tbsp flour, whisk in 250ml milk until thick, then stir in 150g grated cheddar.

Mains

Detective's spaghetti with beef ragu

Beef ragu on spaghetti with parmesan.

Prep 15 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 600g beef mince
  • 2 carrots, finely diced
  • 2 celery sticks, finely diced
  • 800g crushed tomatoes
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the mince in the oil, then add the carrot and celery and soften.
  2. Add the tomatoes and simmer, stirring now and then.
  3. Cook the spaghetti and toss through the sauce. Cover with plenty of parmesan.

Dr Claw's metal hand chipotle chilli

Slow-cooked chipotle beef and bean chilli. Kramer built Scolex the claw.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 can chipotles in adobo
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 can kidney beans
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  • 200g sour cream
  • 150g cheddar
  1. Beef chuck simmered slowly with kidney beans, mild chipotle, cumin and tomato until thick and smoky, with sour cream, cheddar and buttered cornbread. Brown 1kg diced beef chuck in batches. Soften an onion and 3 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp cumin and 1 chopped chipotle in adobo, then a can of crushed tomatoes and 500ml beef stock. Return the beef, cover and simmer 3 hours. Add a drained can of kidney beans for the last 30 minutes. Bake a packet of cornbread mix following the directions. Serve with sour cream and grated cheddar.

Sweets

Gadgetmobile cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with grey icing and chocolate button wheels.

Prep 29 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, iced
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 200g icing sugar
  • black food colouring, a drop
  • 12 chocolate buttons
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then the eggs. Fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Spoon into a lined muffin tin and bake at 175°C. Cool.
  3. Mix the icing sugar with a little water and a tiny drop of black colouring to make grey icing. Ice the cakes.
  4. Press on chocolate buttons for wheels and let the icing set.

Drinks

Riverton lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade topped with soda water and sliced lemon over ice.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, mix fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 6 lemons, juiced, plus 1 sliced
  • 1 L cold soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Mix the lemon juice and syrup in a jug and top with soda water.
  3. Pour over ice with sliced lemon.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Gadget finally gets the helicopter hat working.
