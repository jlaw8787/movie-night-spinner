Lobby boy's invisible consommé

A lobby boy is completely invisible

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make and clarify the consommé up to 2 days ahead; reheat and poach the dumplings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l good beef stock, cold
  • 250g lean beef mince
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1 celery stick, finely chopped
  • 1 leek, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 60g semolina
  • 1 egg
  • 20g butter, soft
  • 2 tbsp chives, finely chopped
  1. Whisk the mince, egg whites, carrot, celery, leek and tomato into the cold stock in a deep pot.
  2. Bring slowly to a bare simmer without stirring; a raft forms on top. Simmer gently, untouched.
  3. Ladle the clear stock out through a muslin-lined sieve and season.
  4. Beat the semolina, egg, butter and chives, shape small dumplings with two teaspoons and poach them in the consommé until they swell.

Crossed Keys smoked trout on rye

The Society of the Crossed Keys helps out

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the horseradish cream and pickle the cucumber up to a day ahead; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g hot-smoked trout, flaked
  • 8 slices dark rye bread
  • 150g creme fraiche
  • 2 tbsp grated horseradish
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp dill sprigs
  1. Toss the cucumber with the vinegar, sugar and a pinch of salt and leave 10 minutes.
  2. Stir the horseradish into the creme fraiche.
  3. Spread the rye with the cream, top with trout and drained cucumber.
  4. Finish with dill and cut into neat fingers.

Cheap cuts beef cheek goulash

Gustave moves on to the cheap cuts

Prep 25 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Braise the cheeks up to 2 days ahead and chill in the sauce; reheat and make the spätzle to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef cheeks, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp plain flour, plus 250g for the spätzle
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 3 eggs
  • 125ml milk
  • 40g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Dust the cheeks in flour, season and brown well in the oil, then lift out.
  2. Soften the onions and garlic, stir in the paprika, caraway and tomato paste, then add the stock, capsicum and cheeks.
  3. Cover and braise at 160C until the cheeks give to a spoon.
  4. Beat 250g flour, the eggs, milk and salt to a thick batter, push it through a colander into boiling water, then drain and toss in the butter.
  5. Serve the cheeks on the spätzle with a spoon of sour cream.

Schloss Lutz roast duck with red cabbage

The will is read at Schloss Lutz

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Braise the cabbage up to 2 days ahead; it improves; roast the duck to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 duck breasts, skin on
  • 1/2 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 2 green apples, grated
  • 60ml red wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 30g butter
  • 4 juniper berries, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tbsp redcurrant jelly
  1. Soften the onion in the butter, add the cabbage, apple, vinegar, sugar, juniper and cinnamon with a splash of water, cover and braise until soft and glossy.
  2. Score the duck skin, season and start skin-side down in a cold pan over medium heat until the fat renders and the skin is crisp.
  3. Flip, roast at 200C for 6 minutes, then rest.
  4. Warm the redcurrant jelly with the pan juices, slice the duck and serve on the cabbage.

Mendl's Courtesan au chocolatVegetarian

Agatha bakes the tools into the pastries

Prep 45 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake the choux and make the custard a day ahead; fill and glaze on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 500ml vanilla custard, thick
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp instant coffee, dissolved in 2 tbsp hot water
  • 1 tbsp pink food colouring and a little icing sugar for the trim
  • 4 cocoa beans or chocolate buttons for the top
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour until it leaves the sides, cool a little and beat in the eggs one by one.
  2. Pipe 4 large, 4 medium and 4 small rounds and bake at 200C until puffed and dry.
  3. Poke a hole in each and fill with the custard.
  4. Mix the icing sugar with the cocoa and coffee for a mocha glaze and dip the tops.
  5. Stack large, medium and small, pipe a little pink icing trim and top with a cocoa bean.

Checkpoint Nineteen bison grass and appleVegetarian

Gustave breaks out of Checkpoint Nineteen

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the vodka and apple juice well; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml Zubrowka bison grass vodka
  • 600ml cloudy apple juice, chilled
  • 4 thin apple slices
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in the vodka and top with apple juice.
  3. Add an apple slice and the faintest dust of cinnamon.

When to serve

Serve the courtesans when Agatha and Zero fall into the van full of pastries.
