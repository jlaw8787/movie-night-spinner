Snacks

Etta's ham and cheddar picnic baguettes

Ham, sharp cheddar, green apple and cornichons with mustard butter, pressed

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 baguettes
  • 60g salted butter
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 250g leg ham, sliced
  • 150g sharp cheddar, sliced
  • 1 green apple, thinly sliced
  • 8 cornichons, halved
  1. Split the baguettes lengthways and cut each in half.
  2. Spread with butter and mustard.
  3. Layer with the ham, cheddar, apple and cornichons.
  4. Press closed, wrap tightly in baking paper and tie with string.

Mains

Hole-in-the-Wall chilli

Beef chilli with cornbread. Rules? In a knife fight?

Prep 25 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 dried chillies, soaked and blitzed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tin of chopped tomatoes
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches.
  2. Add the chillies, cumin, garlic and tomatoes and braise until tender.
  3. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, buttermilk and egg into cornbread batter.
  4. Bake the cornbread at 200C alongside.
  5. Serve the chilli with cornbread.

Candy-dipped pretzel dynamite sticksVegetarian

Pretzel rods dipped in red candy coating with a licorice fuse

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g red candy melts
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 4 liquorice straps
  1. Melt the candy melts.
  2. Dip each pretzel rod two thirds of the way.
  3. Push a short piece of liquorice into the top of each as a fuse.
  4. Set on baking paper.

Bicycle ride lemon cakeVegetarian

Lemon loaf soaked in hot lemon syrup under a lemon glaze

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, softened
  • 175g caster sugar, for the cake
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 lemons, zested
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 60g caster sugar, for the syrup
  • 60ml lemon juice, for the syrup
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, for the icing
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a loaf tin.
  2. Beat the butter and the cake sugar, add the eggs and zest, and fold in the flour.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Heat the syrup sugar and syrup lemon juice until the sugar dissolves and soak the hot cake.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the icing lemon juice and spread on top.

Drinks

Hole-in-the-Wall bourbon sourVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and sugar shaken with egg white, bitters dotted on top

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the bourbon, lemon juice and syrup up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; add the egg whites and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 100ml lemon juice
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 2 egg whites
  • 4 cup ice
  • 8 drops aromatic bitters
  • 4 cherries
  1. Dry shake the bourbon, lemon juice, syrup and egg whites.
  2. Add ice and shake hard.
  3. Strain into glasses over ice.
  4. Shake bitters onto the foam and add a cherry.
