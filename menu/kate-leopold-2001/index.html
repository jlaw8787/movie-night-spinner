Snacks

Rooftop crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette, whipped goat's cheese, honey poached pear and thyme

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Toast the slices up to 1 day ahead, airtight, and poach the pears up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 pears
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 150g goat's cheese, whipped
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  1. Slice the baguette thinly on the bias, brush with oil and toast at 200C.
  2. Poach the sliced pears in simmering water with a little honey.
  3. Spread the toasts with goat's cheese, top with pear and drizzle with honey and thyme.

Butter tastingVegetarian

Cultured, salted Irish and honey-whipped brown butter on a board with warm sourdough and radishes

Prep 16 min, cook 6 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Butters up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g cultured butter
  • 100g salted Irish butter
  • 100g butter, for browning
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Brown 100g of the butter in a pan until nutty.
  2. Cool the browned butter, then whip with the honey.
  3. Set out the cultured butter, salted Irish butter and whipped brown butter in small dishes.
  4. Warm the sourdough loaf at 180C for 8 minutes and slice.
  5. Halve the radishes and serve with the flaky salt for dipping, alongside the three butters and bread.

Mains

Leopold's rooftop dinner

Whole roast snapper with beurre blanc, candlelight on the roof

Prep 17 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Slice the lemons and fennel and chop the shallot up to 1 day ahead; roast the fish and whisk the sauce fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg whole snapper
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 1 fennel bulb, sliced
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 100ml white wine
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 150g cold butter, cubed
  • salt, to taste
  1. Score the snapper on both sides, season with salt and stuff with the lemon slices, sliced fennel and dill.
  2. Roast the fish at 220C for 25 minutes.
  3. While the fish roasts, boil the new potatoes in salted water until tender, about 20 minutes, then drain.
  4. Reduce the shallot with the white wine and white wine vinegar until about 2 tbsp is left.
  5. Take off the heat and whisk in the cold butter cube by cube until thick and glossy.
  6. Serve the fish with the potatoes and the sauce.

Sweets

Chocolate mousseVegetarian

Dark chocolate mousse lightened with whipped egg whites and cream

Prep 22 min, cook 8 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 30g butter
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter and cool slightly.
  2. Separate the eggs and whisk the yolks into the chocolate.
  3. Whip the egg whites to soft peaks with the sugar.
  4. Softly whip the cream.
  5. Fold the cream, then the whites, through the chocolate.
  6. Spoon into glasses and chill until set. This chilling counts toward the cooking time.

Leopold's claret cupVegetarian

Red wine punch with brandy, orange liqueur, sliced orange and cucumber

Prep 12 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled before adding ice
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 60ml brandy
  • 30ml orange liqueur
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 orange
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 1 tray ice
  • 400ml soda water
  1. Mix the wine, brandy, liqueur and sugar in a jug with the sliced orange and cucumber.
  2. Chill.
  3. Add ice and top with soda water.
