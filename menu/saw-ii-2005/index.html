Billy's red spiral pinwheelsVegetarian

Puff pastry rolled with sundried tomato pesto and parmesan, sliced into red spirals and baked crisp.

Prep 15 min, cook 19 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Roll and chill up to 2 days ahead, or freeze for a month; slice and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 120g sundried tomato pesto
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line two trays, 3 min.
  2. Spread the pesto over the pastry sheets and scatter with the parmesan and oregano.
  3. Roll each sheet up tightly and chill until firm, 20 min.
  4. Slice into 1.5cm rounds, lay flat on the trays and brush with egg.
  5. Bake until puffed and golden, 16 min.
  6. Cool for a few minutes before serving, 5 min.

Matthews' stakeout jalapeño poppersVegetarian

Jalapeño halves filled with cream cheese, cheddar and scallion under a crunchy crumb.

Prep 16 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Fill up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; add the crumb and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 large jalapeños, halved and seeded
  • 200g cream cheese, soft
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 50g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a tray, 3 min.
  2. Halve and seed the jalapeños, wearing gloves.
  3. Mix the cream cheese, cheddar, spring onion and paprika and fill the jalapeño halves.
  4. Toss the panko with the oil and press on top.
  5. Bake until the chillies blister and the crumb is golden, 18 min.
  6. Serve hot with the sour cream.

Jigsaw's long game beef ragu

Beef chuck braised for hours in tomato with garlic and rosemary.

Prep 24 min, cook 3 hr 27 min · Make ahead: Make the ragu up to 3 days ahead or freeze for 3 months; cook the pasta on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, cut into 4 pieces
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 375ml beef stock
  • 2 rosemary sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 500g dried pappardelle
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  1. Season the beef with the salt and pepper and brown well in the oil in a heavy pot, then lift out, 12 min.
  2. Soften the onion, carrot, celery and garlic in the same pot.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, add the tomatoes, 375ml beef stock, rosemary and bay, return the beef, cover and simmer on low, 3 hr.
  4. Shred the beef with two forks in the pot and simmer uncovered until thick, 15 min.
  5. Cook the pappardelle in salted boiling water and toss through the ragu with a splash of the pasta water, 12 min.
  6. Serve with the parmesan.

Jigsaw puzzle-piece shortbreadVegetarian

Buttery vanilla shortbread cut into puzzle pieces, half dipped in dark chocolate.

Prep 23 min, cook 40 min, 1 hr 5 min waiting · Make ahead: Chill the dough up to 3 days ahead; baked and dipped shortbread keeps 1 week airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, soft
  • 110g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g plain flour
  • 60g cornflour
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the butter, icing sugar and vanilla until pale.
  2. Mix in the flour and cornflour to a soft dough.
  3. Wrap and chill, 30 min.
  4. Roll out 7mm thick and cut into puzzle pieces with a cutter or a sharp knife and card template.
  5. Lay on lined trays and chill again so they hold their shape, 15 min.
  6. Bake at 160°C until pale gold, 20 min.
  7. Cool on the trays, 20 min.
  8. Melt the chocolate, dip half of each piece and leave to set, 20 min.

Interrogation room Irish coffee

Hot strong coffee with Irish whiskey and brown sugar, under a layer of lightly whipped cream.

Prep 8 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled; brew the coffee and build the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream, cold
  • 600ml hot strong black coffee
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  1. Whip the cream lightly until just thick but still pourable.
  2. Warm 4 heatproof glasses with hot water and tip it out.
  3. Stir 1 tsp brown sugar into 150ml coffee in each glass and add 40ml whiskey.
  4. Pour the cream over the back of a spoon so it floats on top.
