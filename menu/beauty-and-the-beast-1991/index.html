Snacks

Gaston's eggsVegetarian

Twelve eggs halved and piped with Dijon mayo yolk filling, paprika and chives.

Prep 16 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 20g soft butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool, peel and halve, 15 min.
  2. Whip the yolks with the mayonnaise, Dijon and butter until smooth.
  3. Pipe back into the whites and finish with paprika and chives.

Brie, pear and honey baguette toastsVegetarian

Toasted baguette slices with brie, sliced pear and a drizzle of honey, served warm.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Slice and toast the baguette up to 1 day ahead and keep it airtight; top, warm and drizzle on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 200g brie
  • 2 pears
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the baguette and brush with olive oil.
  2. Toast at 200°C, 8 min.
  3. Top with sliced brie and a thin slice of pear, return briefly to warm, and drizzle with honey, 7 min.

Mains

Gaston's tavern roast chicken

Whole roast chicken with potatoes and thyme gravy, torn apart at the table.

Prep 15 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with butter and salt up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • salt, to taste
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Rub the chicken with butter and salt and chop the potatoes into the tray.
  2. Roast at 220°C to start, then lower to 180°C until cooked through.
  3. Rest the chicken, 15 min.
  4. Make gravy with the pan juices, thyme and stock. Tear the chicken apart at the table.

Castle kitchen beef bourguignon

Beef stew for the girl he told to go ahead and starve.

How it is made
  • 150g bacon
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml red grape juice
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 12 baby onions
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  1. Beef chuck braised slowly with bacon, baby onions, mushrooms, thyme and a rich grape and beef stock gravy, over buttery mash with crusty bread. Brown 150g diced bacon, then 1.2kg diced beef chuck in batches. Cook 1 onion and 3 garlic cloves, add 2 tbsp tomato paste and 2 tbsp flour, then 250ml red grape juice, 500ml beef stock, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, thyme and bay. Return the meat, cover and braise at 160°C for 3 hours. Brown 12 peeled baby onions and 250g mushrooms in butter and stir in. Serve with mashed potato.

Sweets

Enchanted rose panna cotta

Rosewater panna cotta topped with crushed raspberries and a sugared rose petal.

Prep 18 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 gelatine leaves
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp rosewater
  • 150g raspberries
  • 4 sugared rose petals
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water, 5 min.
  2. Warm the cream with most of the sugar, stir in the squeezed gelatine and the rosewater, and pour into four glasses, 8 min.
  3. Set in the fridge, 4 hr.
  4. Crush the raspberries with the remaining sugar and spoon on top with a rose petal.

Lumière's cookies and cream mousse

Whipped chocolate mousse folded with crushed biscuits and cream.

How it is made
  • 1 packet chocolate cream sandwich biscuits
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g mascarpone
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  • 50g white chocolate
  1. Silky cookies and cream mousse of crushed chocolate biscuits, whipped cream and mascarpone, piped into little cups and topped with a crunchy biscuit crumb. Crush 12 chocolate cream sandwich biscuits, keeping 2 tbsp crumbs. Whip 300ml thickened cream with 250g mascarpone, 2 tbsp icing sugar and 1 tsp vanilla to soft peaks. Fold through the crushed biscuits so it turns grey. Pipe into 8 small cups or glasses and chill 1 hour. Top with the saved crumbs and a white chocolate curl just before serving.

LeFou's apple slushNo alcohol

Frozen apple juice and lemon scraped to slush, topped with marshmallow cream.

Prep 15 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cloudy apple juice
  • 1 lemon
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 3 tbsp marshmallow creme
  • 1 box English breakfast tea
  1. Mix the apple juice with the lemon juice and pour into a tray to freeze, 5 min.
  2. Freeze, scraping with a fork every 30 minutes.
  3. Whip the cream and fold in the marshmallow creme.
  4. Spoon the slush into glasses and top with the cream. Brew a pot of English breakfast tea for the grown-ups.
