Snacks

Huntsman in the pants harbour prawns

Chilled cooked prawns on ice with lemon and spicy cocktail sauce

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep on ice
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked prawns
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • hot sauce, a dash
  • 8 cups crushed ice
  1. Pile crushed ice onto a platter and arrange the prawns on top with lemon wedges.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with tomato sauce and hot sauce.
  3. Serve in a bowl beside the prawns.

Sydney meat pies

Mini beef mince pies with puff lids, served with tomato sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  1. Dice the onion and brown with the mince.
  2. Stir in the flour and stock and simmer until thick, then cool.
  3. Cut the shortcrust into rounds and line a muffin tin. Fill with the mince and top with puff lids.
  4. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C until golden.
  5. Serve with tomato sauce.

Opera House oysters with eschalot dressingOn the printed menu

Freshly shucked rock ones on crushed ice, sharp red wine vinegar, cracked pepper and lemon

How it is made
  • 24 freshly shucked Sydney rock oysters
  • 2 eschalots
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 2 lemons
  • 2kg ice
  1. Freshly shucked Sydney rock oysters on crushed ice with a sharp red wine vinegar and eschalot dressing, cracked pepper and lemon wedges. Finely dice 2 eschalots and mix with 80ml red wine vinegar, a pinch of sugar and plenty of cracked black pepper. Leave 15 minutes. Pile crushed ice onto a large platter. Check 24 freshly shucked oysters for shell grit and loosen each from its shell. Sit them on the ice with the dressing in a small bowl and a teaspoon, plus 2 lemons cut into wedges. Serve straight away.

Mains

Harbour barramundi

Crisp-skinned barramundi, crushed baby potatoes, lemon caper butter

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 4 barramundi fillets, skin on
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 lemon, juiced, for the pan sauce
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges, for serving
  • 2 tbsp capers
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and crush lightly.
  2. Pan fry the barramundi in the olive oil, skin side down, until crisp, then flip briefly.
  3. Add butter, lemon juice and capers to the pan and spoon over the fish.
  4. Serve with the potatoes and lemon wedges.

Rosemary lamb cutlets with potato saladOn the printed menu

Frenched chops in garlic and lemon, grilled pink, warm potatoes with mustard and capers

How it is made
  • 12 frenched lamb cutlets
  • olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 lemons
  • 1kg chat potatoes
  • red wine vinegar
  • 2 tsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 3 spring onions
  1. Frenched lamb cutlets marinated in garlic, rosemary and lemon, grilled pink over high heat, with a warm potato salad of mustard, capers and herbs. Marinate 12 lamb cutlets in 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tbsp chopped rosemary and lemon zest for 30 minutes. Boil 1kg halved chat potatoes 15 minutes until tender. Whisk 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 2 tsp wholegrain mustard and 1 tbsp capers. Toss through the warm potatoes with parsley and sliced spring onion. Grill the cutlets on a very hot barbecue 2 to 3 minutes a side, rest 3 minutes and serve with lemon.

Sweets

Halle and Claudia's pavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue shell, whipped cream and fresh passionfruit

Prep 30 min, cook 5 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day without toppings, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 2 kiwifruit
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar.
  2. Shape into a round on baking paper.
  3. Bake at 120°C, then leave to cool in the oven with the door closed.
  4. Cool completely in the oven.
  5. Whip the cream and top the pavlova with cream, passionfruit, sliced kiwifruit and strawberries.

Drinks

Harbour passionfruit fizzVegetarian

Sparkling wine, passionfruit pulp and elderflower

Prep 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the flutes and the sparkling wine up to 1 day ahead; build the glasses just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bottle Australian sparkling wine (750 ml), chilled
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 60ml elderflower cordial
  • 4 sprigs mint
  1. Chill the flutes and the sparkling wine.
  2. Spoon passionfruit pulp into each glass with a measure of elderflower cordial.
  3. Top slowly with sparkling wine, stirring once to lift the pulp. Finish with mint. For non-drinkers, use sparkling apple juice or soda water.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the wedding party heads out on the harbour.
