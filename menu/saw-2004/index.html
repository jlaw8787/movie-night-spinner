Snacks

Choose your fate chip and dipVegetarian

Chive sour cream, smoked red pepper and habanero tomato dips, mild to hot.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Dips up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 roasted red capsicum
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 habanero chilli
  • 300g corn chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix some of the sour cream with the chives for the mild dip.
  2. Blitz the capsicum with more sour cream and the paprika for the medium dip.
  3. Blitz the tomatoes with the habanero and salt for the hot dip.
  4. Line them up mild to hot with the corn chips.

Tapp's stakeout coffee-glazed doughnutsVegetarian

Fluffy yeast doughnuts dipped in a strong coffee glaze, a box for the stakeout.

Prep 22 min, cook 13 min, then 95 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g soft butter
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp hot espresso
  • 2 tbsp cinnamon sugar
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt, add the milk, eggs and butter and knead for 8 minutes.
  2. Prove for 1 hour.
  3. Roll 1.5cm thick, cut into rings and prove for 30 minutes.
  4. Fry in the oil at 175°C for 1 minute a side and drain.
  5. Whisk the icing sugar with the espresso for the glaze. Dip half the doughnuts and toss the rest in cinnamon sugar while warm, 10 min.

Puzzle-piece cheddar and jalapeño crackers

Cheese and chilli dough jigsaw pieces, smoky bell pepper walnut dip.

How it is made
  • 150g plain flour
  • 150g sharp cheddar
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 200g roasted capsicum
  • 60g walnuts
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bottle pomegranate molasses
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Crisp homemade cheddar and jalapeño crackers cut into jigsaw pieces, baked golden and served with a smoky red capsicum and walnut dip. Pulse 150g plain flour, 150g grated sharp cheddar, 60g cold butter, 1 chopped pickled jalapeño and a pinch of salt until crumbly, then add 2 tbsp cold water to bring it together. Roll 3mm thick between baking paper, cut into jigsaw shapes and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes until golden. Blend 200g roasted capsicum, 60g toasted walnuts, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tbsp pomegranate molasses and 2 tbsp olive oil for the dip.

Dr Gordon's roast chicken dinner

Roast chicken with roast potatoes and gravy, steamed vegetables.

Prep 10 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the butter, lemon and thyme up to 1 day ahead and keep it uncovered in the fridge; roast, make the gravy and steam the beans on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 30g butter, softened
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 300g green beans
  1. Rub the chicken with butter, lemon and thyme.
  2. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then at 180°C for 50 minutes with the potatoes.
  3. Rest the chicken and make gravy from the pan juices and stock, 10 min.
  4. Steam the beans and serve, 15 min.

Detective Tapp's beef chilli con carne

Chuck and kidney beans with chipotle, sour cream, for a detective who won't let go.

How it is made
  • 1kg chuck steak
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 tin chipotles in adobo
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 tin kidney beans
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 1 bag corn chips
  1. Chuck steak and kidney beans slow-cooked with chipotle, cumin and tomato until thick and smoky, topped with sour cream, cheddar and pickled jalapeños, with corn chips. Brown 1kg diced chuck steak in batches. Add 2 diced onions and 3 garlic cloves and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika and 2 chopped chipotles in adobo, then 2 tins crushed tomatoes and 250ml beef stock. Cover and simmer for 2.5 hours until the beef falls apart. Add 1 drained tin kidney beans for the last 20 minutes. Serve with sour cream, grated cheddar, pickled jalapeños and corn chips.

Billy's red velvet cupcakesVegetarian

Deep red cupcakes with cream cheese frosting, delivered by tricycle.

Prep 27 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes up to 1 day ahead, airtight; beat the cream cheese icing and pipe on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 250g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk and colouring.
  2. Spoon into a muffin tin and bake at 175°C for 18 minutes. Cool.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar and pipe in a spiral.

Jigsaw's lemonadeNo alcohol

One jug of lemon mint lemonade, one of sugar free lime and soda, take your pick.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons and limes and make the sugar syrup up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; add the water, soda water, ice and mint to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 lemons
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 4 limes
  • 1 L soda water
  • 2 L cold water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Make one jug with lemon juice, sugar syrup and mint, topped with cold water.
  2. Make the other with lime juice and soda water, no sugar.
  3. Fill glasses with ice, pour from either jug and let everyone choose.
