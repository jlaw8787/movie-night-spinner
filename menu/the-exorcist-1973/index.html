Snacks

Georgetown crab cakes

Pan fried crab cakes with Dijon mayo, served with lemon

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min, then 32 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Shape up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g crab meat
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  1. Fold the crab with the mayonnaise, egg, mustard and breadcrumbs.
  2. Shape into cakes and chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Pan-fry in butter until golden and serve with lemon.

Baba ganoush and hummus with flatbreadVegetarian

Smoky eggplant dip and chickpea hummus with pickled turnips and flatbread

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Dips up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 eggplant
  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained
  • 1 jar pickled turnips
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 1 tsp sumac
  1. Char the eggplant whole over a flame until collapsed, then scoop out the flesh.
  2. Mash the eggplant with half the tahini, garlic and lemon juice.
  3. Blitz the chickpeas with the remaining tahini for hummus.
  4. Warm the flatbreads and serve with pickled turnips, dusted with sumac.

Mains

Pea and ham soup

Thick split pea soup simmered with smoked ham hock, with crusty bread

Prep 20 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g split peas
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2l water
  • 1 crusty Italian loaf
  1. Put the peas, hock, onion, carrots, celery and water in a large pot.
  2. Simmer for 2 hours, stirring now and then.
  3. Pick the ham off the bone and stir it back in. Season.
  4. Serve with crusty bread. Eat it, don't throw it.

Sweets

Devil's food cakeVegetarian

Rich chocolate coffee layer cake with frosting

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cocoa
  • 250ml hot strong coffee
  • 150g butter, softened
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the cocoa into the hot coffee and cool a little.
  2. Beat the butter, sugar and eggs, then add the cocoa mix, buttermilk, flour and bicarb.
  3. Divide between two tins and bake at 175C for 30 minutes. Cool.
  4. Melt the chocolate with the cream and spread over the cake.

Holy water gin and elderflower fizzVegetarian

Gin, elderflower cordial and lemon shaken hard and strained over ice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the gin, cordial and lemon juice in a jar up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; shake with ice and top with soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 80ml elderflower cordial
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 400ml chilled soda water
  • 1 sprig mint, per glass
  • 4 thin lemon wheels
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Shake the gin, cordial and lemon juice hard with ice for 15 seconds.
  2. Strain into tall glasses over fresh ice and top with soda water.
  3. Add a mint sprig and a lemon wheel. For non-drinkers, swap the gin for chilled cucumber juice.

When to serve

Serve the soup when the priests climb the stairs to Regan's room.
