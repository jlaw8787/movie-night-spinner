Snacks

Spectre egg salad picnic sandwichesVegetarian

Egg salad with mayo and chives on soft white bread, crusts off

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, wrap well
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 2 apples
  • 1 lemon
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Mash with the mayonnaise, chives, salt and white pepper.
  3. Spread over four slices of bread, top with the rest and cut off the crusts, then into triangles.
  4. Slice the apples and toss in lemon juice. Lay it all out on a picnic blanket.

Alabama boiled peanutsVegetarian

Raw peanuts simmered in salty Cajun water, served warm in paper cones

Prep 5 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 500g raw peanuts in the shell
  • 80g fine salt
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 2l water
  1. Put the peanuts in a large pot with the water, salt and Cajun seasoning.
  2. Simmer until soft.
  3. Soak off the heat.
  4. Drain and serve warm in paper cones.

Mains

The Beast, the big fish

Whole snapper rubbed with Cajun spice, hush puppies, cabbage slaw

Prep 30 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the hush puppy batter up to 4 hours ahead and the slaw up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; roast the fish and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg whole snapper
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 50g butter
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 lemons
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Score the snapper and rub with Cajun seasoning and butter.
  2. Roast at 200C.
  3. Mix the cornmeal, flour, buttermilk and egg into a hush puppy batter.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil to 180C and fry spoonfuls until golden.
  5. Shred the cabbage and mix with mayonnaise for slaw.
  6. Serve the fish with lemon, hush puppies and slaw.

Sweets

Daffodil lemon cakeVegetarian

Lemon syrup sponge and lemon icing. Daffodils, Sandra's favourite

Prep 30 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter
  • 275g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 3 lemons
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 drop yellow food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and part of the sugar, add the eggs and lemon zest and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake at 170C.
  3. Heat the remaining sugar with some lemon juice into a syrup and soak the warm cake.
  4. Cool.
  5. Ice with the icing sugar mixed with lemon juice and a touch of yellow.

Drinks

Calloway Circus cherry cream sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Cherry vanilla syrup and lime over ice with soda. Always thirsty

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen pitted cherries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 1 lime
  • 800ml soda water
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the cherries with the sugar, water and split vanilla bean for 10 minutes.
  2. Mash, strain and cool.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice, add syrup and lime juice to each and top with soda water.
  4. Pour a little cream gently over the top of each and add a cherry.
