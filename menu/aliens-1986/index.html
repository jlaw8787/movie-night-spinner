Snacks

Sulaco mess hall cornbreadVegetarian

Buttermilk, cornmeal and egg batter poured into a hot buttered pan, baked and cut into squares.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best warm, up to 1 day airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 80g plain flour
  • 20g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • pinch of salt
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 40g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C with a 20cm ovenproof frying pan inside. Mix the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the buttermilk, egg and melted butter, then stir into the dry mix.
  3. Carefully butter the hot pan, pour in the batter and bake until golden and a skewer comes out clean, 20 min.
  4. Cool briefly and cut into squares, 5 min.

Bishop's knife trick breadsticksVegetarian

Long sesame and salt breadsticks, spread across the table for the knife trick, fingers optional.

Prep 5 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pizza dough
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 15g sesame seeds
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C and line a tray. Roll the dough into long thin sticks, 15 min.
  2. Brush with oil and roll in the sesame seeds and salt.
  3. Bake until crisp and golden, then cool slightly, 12 min.

Mains

Sulaco mess hall meatloaf

Beef meatloaf with mashed potatoes, peas and gravy. Breakfast for the marines, served at dinner.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 15 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Loaf up to 1 day, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 260ml milk
  • 350g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp anchovy-free Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 60g tomato sauce
  • 800g potatoes
  • 40g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Soak the breadcrumbs in some of the milk, 5 min.
  2. Mix with the beef and pork mince, egg, onion, Worcestershire, salt and pepper, and shape into a loaf on a lined tray.
  3. Glaze with the tomato sauce and bake until at least 75°C in the centre, 1 hr.
  4. Meanwhile peel and chop the potatoes, boil until tender, then mash with the butter and warm milk, 10 min.
  5. Rest the loaf, slice and serve with the mash, 10 min.

Colonial Marines breakfast burritos

Tortillas rolled round chorizo, scrambled eggs, crisp potato, cheddar and salsa, toasted.

How it is made
  • 400g potatoes
  • 2 chorizo sausages
  • 8 eggs
  • 20g butter
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 120g cheddar
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  1. Flour tortillas stuffed with chorizo, soft scrambled eggs, crispy potato, cheddar and salsa, rolled tight and toasted in a hot pan until golden. Dice 400g potatoes and fry in oil for 12 minutes until crisp. Slice 2 chorizo sausages and fry until the fat runs. Whisk 8 eggs with salt and scramble softly in 20g butter. Lay out 4 large flour tortillas and fill each with potato, chorizo, egg, 30g grated cheddar and 2 tbsp salsa. Fold in the sides, roll tight and toast seam side down in a dry pan for 2 minutes a side.

Sweets

Facehugger panna cotta

Vanilla panna cotta turned out with candied peel legs, pale, wobbly and unsettling.

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min, 4 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4g gelatine leaves
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 1/2 vanilla bean
  • 40g candied orange peel strips
  • 1 tsp neutral oil, for the moulds
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water and lightly oil four small round moulds, 5 min.
  2. Warm the cream, milk, sugar and vanilla seeds until steaming, without boiling, 8 min.
  3. Squeeze the gelatine dry and stir in until dissolved, then divide between the moulds.
  4. Refrigerate until set, 4 hr.
  5. Dip each mould briefly in warm water, turn out and fan the orange peel around as legs, 5 min.

Drinks

Condensed milk iced coffeeNo alcohol

Strong iced coffee over sweet condensed milk, enough to get you out of cryosleep.

Prep 15 min, 16 hr waiting · Make ahead: Cold brew keeps 3 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 100g coarsely ground dark roast coffee
  • 800ml cold water
  • 1 tin sweetened condensed milk
  • ground cinnamon, a pinch
  • 2 cups ice
  1. The night before, stir the coffee into the cold water, cover and refrigerate for 12 to 16 hours.
  2. Strain through a paper filter.
  3. Spoon condensed milk into four tall glasses, fill with ice, pour in the cold brew and stir until creamy.
  4. Dust with cinnamon.
