Snacks

Pete's Diner friesVegetarian

Twice-fried chips tossed with oregano, lemon zest and crumbled feta

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cut the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead and keep them in cold water; drain, dry and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g feta
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips.
  2. Fry in 140C oil until soft but pale, then drain.
  3. Fry again in 185C oil until golden and crisp.
  4. Toss while hot with oregano, lemon zest, salt and crumbled feta. Squeeze the lemon over just before serving.

Rizzo's kitchen sandwichesVegetarian

Buttered white bread with Dijon and sliced cheddar, griddled until golden

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; butter the outsides and griddle just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 200g cheddar, sliced
  • 40g butter
  1. Spread the bread with Dijon and fill with cheddar.
  2. Butter the outsides and griddle over medium heat until golden and melted, turning once.
  3. Cut into soldiers.

Mains

Pete's Diner club sandwich

Triple-decker toasted club with turkey, bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayo

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Grill the bacon up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; toast the bread and build the sandwiches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices bread
  • 300g sliced turkey
  • 1 small lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bag potato chips
  1. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  2. Toast the bread.
  3. Layer turkey, bacon, lettuce, sliced tomato and mayo across three slices for each sandwich.
  4. Pin with toothpicks, cut into triangles and serve with potato chips.

Kermit and Piggy's wedding cakeVegetarian

Two-layer butter cake with pink and green buttercream flowers

Prep 40 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Bake the cakes up to 1 day ahead and wrap them once cool; ice and pipe on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 250g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 400g icing sugar
  • pink food colouring, a few drops
  • green food colouring, a few drops
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line two tins. Beat the butter and sugar until pale.
  2. Add the eggs, fold in the flour and divide between the tins.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Cool the cakes completely.
  5. Beat the icing butter with the icing sugar, split in half and tint one half pink and one half green.
  6. Ice the cakes and pipe flowers.

Pete's honey yoghurt frappéVegetarianNo alcohol

Banana, Greek yoghurt, milk and honey blended thick with ice

Prep 6 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Peel, slice and freeze the bananas up to 1 week ahead for a thicker drink; blend just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 cup ice
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Peel and slice the bananas.
  2. Blend with the yoghurt, milk, some of the honey and the ice until thick and frothy. Taste and add more honey if needed.
  3. Pour into tall glasses, drizzle with honey and dust with cinnamon.

When to serve

Serve dessert at Kermit and Piggy's wedding.
