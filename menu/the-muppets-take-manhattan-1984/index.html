Luncheonette friesVegetarian

Twice-cooked fries with oregano, lemon zest and crumbled feta, the Greek way.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cut the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead and keep them in cold water; drain, dry and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g feta
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips.
  2. Fry in 140°C oil until soft but pale, then drain, 12 min.
  3. Fry again in 185°C oil until golden and crisp, 8 min.
  4. Toss while hot with oregano, lemon zest, salt and crumbled feta. Squeeze the lemon over just before serving.

Rizzo's griddled cheese sandwichesVegetarian

Cheddar and Dijon on buttered white bread, griddled golden by the rat crew in the kitchen.

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Fill the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; butter the outsides and griddle just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 200g cheddar, sliced
  • 40g butter
  1. Spread the bread with Dijon and fill with cheddar.
  2. Butter the outsides and griddle over medium heat until golden and melted, turning once, 7 min.
  3. Cut into soldiers.

Fozzie's hibernation honey bunsVegetarian

Sticky cinnamon buns glazed in warm honey butter, enough to sleep through winter on.

Prep 13 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut the buns up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake and glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Brush the pastry with half the butter and scatter with the sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Roll up from the long side, cut into eight and sit the buns in a buttered tin.
  3. Bake at 200°C until golden, 18 min.
  4. Warm the honey with the rest of the butter and spoon over the hot buns, 3 min.

Pete's Diner club sandwich

Triple-decker toasted club with turkey, bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayo.

Prep 15 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Grill the bacon up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; toast the bread and build the sandwiches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 12 slices of bread
  • 300g sliced turkey
  • 1 small lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bag potato chips
  1. Grill the bacon until crisp, 8 min.
  2. Toast the bread, 5 min.
  3. Layer turkey, bacon, lettuce, sliced tomato and mayo across three slices for each sandwich.
  4. Pin with toothpicks, cut into triangles and serve with potato chips.

Big Greek salad with souvlaki

Tomato, cucumber, olives and feta with chicken souvlaki skewers.

How it is made
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 1 red onion
  • 100g kalamata olives
  • 200g feta
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • dried oregano
  • red wine vinegar
  • olive oil
  • 8 skewers
  1. Chunky tomato, cucumber, capsicum, kalamatas and a slab of feta with oregano, served with grilled lemon chicken skewers. Marinate 600g diced chicken thigh in 3 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, 2 garlic cloves and 1 tsp dried oregano for 30 minutes, thread onto 8 skewers and grill 10 minutes, turning. Cut 4 tomatoes, 1 cucumber, 1 green capsicum and half a red onion into chunks. Add 100g kalamatas, top with a 200g slab of feta, sprinkle oregano and dress with oil and red wine vinegar.

Kermit and Piggy's wedding cakeVegetarian

Two-layer butter cake with pink and green buttercream flowers.

Prep 33 min, cook 35 min, 40 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake the cakes up to 1 day ahead and wrap them once cool; ice and pipe on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 250g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 400g icing sugar
  • pink food colouring, a few drops
  • green food colouring, a few drops
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line two tins. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, 10 min.
  2. Add the eggs, fold in the flour and divide between the tins.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean, 25 min.
  4. Cool the cakes completely, 40 min.
  5. Beat the icing butter with the icing sugar, split in half and tint one half pink and one half green.
  6. Ice the cakes and pipe flowers.

Honey yogurt frappéNo alcohol

Banana, Greek yogurt, milk and honey blended thick with ice.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Peel, slice and freeze the bananas up to 1 week ahead for a thicker drink; blend just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 cup ice
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Peel and slice the bananas.
  2. Blend with the yoghurt, milk, some of the honey and the ice until thick and frothy. Taste and add more honey if needed.
  3. Pour into tall glasses, drizzle with honey and dust with cinnamon.
