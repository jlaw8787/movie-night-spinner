Snacks

Friendship, Maine lobster rolls

Lobster salad with mayo, celery and chives in a buttered toasted bun

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g cooked lobster meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 30g butter
  1. Chop the lobster and fold with the mayonnaise, lemon juice, finely diced celery and chives.
  2. Butter the split buns and toast in a pan.
  3. Pile the filling in.

Mains

Fatso's clam chowder in bread bowls

Creamy bacon and potato clam chowder in hollowed toasted cob loaves

Prep 5 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Make the chowder up to the potato stage 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; add the cream and clams and toast the loaves on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg clams
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 500g potatoes
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 cob loaves
  1. Soak and scrub the clams. Dice the bacon, onion, celery and potatoes.
  2. Steam the clams open in a covered pot and strain the liquid.
  3. Render the bacon, add the onion and celery and cook until soft.
  4. Add the potatoes, clam liquid and milk and simmer.
  5. Stir in the cream and clams. Hollow out and toast the cob loaves and ladle the chowder in.

Sweets

Ghost meringuesVegetarian

Crisp piped meringue ghosts with chocolate chip eyes

Prep 25 min, cook 90 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 pinch cream of tartar
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 8 mini choc chips
  1. Whisk the egg whites with the cream of tartar, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until stiff and glossy.
  2. Pipe tall teardrop ghosts and press in choc chips for eyes.
  3. Bake at 100C.
  4. Cool in the oven with the door closed.

Maine blueberry pieVegetarian

Lattice topped blueberry pie, served with vanilla ice cream

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 750g blueberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the blueberries with the sugar, lemon zest and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets, fill, and weave a lattice from the other two.
  3. Bake at 190C.
  4. Cool before slicing.
  5. Serve with vanilla ice cream.

Whipstaff cream soda floatsVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream with cream soda poured over, served with long spoons

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Chill the cream soda and glasses up to 1 day ahead; scoop and pour just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 1/4l cream soda
  1. Put two scoops of ice cream in each of four tall glasses.
  2. Pour cold cream soda over slowly so it foams up the sides. Serve with long spoons and straws.
