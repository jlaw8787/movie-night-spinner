Frosty Palace chilli cheese fries

Double-fried shoestrings under beef chilli and melted cheddar, onion and jalapeños, in paper boats

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days ahead; cut and soak the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 200g tomato passata
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 150g cheddar
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeños
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries and soak in cold water.
  2. Brown the beef with 1 chopped onion, add the cumin, chilli and passata and simmer.
  3. Dry the fries well. Fry at 140°C for 5 minutes and drain, then at 190°C for 3 minutes until crisp, and salt them.
  4. Warm the evaporated milk and melt in the grated cheddar.
  5. Pile the fries into paper boats with the chilli, cheese sauce, the other onion diced and the jalapeños.

Drive-in butter toffee popcornVegetarian

Popcorn baked in butter and brown sugar toffee with roasted peanuts

Prep 16 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 100g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100g roasted peanuts
  1. Pop the kernels in the coconut oil in a covered pot, shaking, and tip into a large bowl.
  2. Melt the butter with the sugar and golden syrup and boil for 4 minutes, then stir in the bicarb and salt.
  3. Pour over the popcorn with the peanuts and toss well.
  4. Spread on lined trays and bake at 120°C, stirring twice.
  5. Cool and break up.

Frosty Palace burger

Two smashed beef patties with American cheese, onion, pickles and mustard

Prep 25 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Dice the onion and portion the mince into balls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; smash and stack on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns, toasted
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Dice the onion and portion the mince into eight balls.
  2. Smash into thin patties on a smoking griddle, salt and flip at 90 seconds, adding cheese.
  3. Toast the buns.
  4. Stack two patties per bun with onion, pickles, mustard and tomato sauce.

Greased Lightnin' fried chicken basket

Buttermilk-soaked pieces in paprika flour, cooked golden, crinkle-cut chips, coleslaw, soft roll

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in buttermilk up to 1 day ahead; make the coleslaw up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks and thighs
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 600g frozen crinkle-cut chips
  • 300g shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 4 soft dinner rolls
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with salt.
  2. Toss in the flour mixed with paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  3. Fry in hot oil in batches until golden and cooked through, then hold in a 160°C oven. Bake the chips at the same time.
  4. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayonnaise and serve with the chicken, chips and rolls.

Frosty Palace sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with strawberry sauce and chocolate fudge, cream and a cherry

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make both sauces up to 2 days ahead and keep them in the fridge, warming the chocolate gently; whip the cream and scoop on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream, for the chocolate sauce
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped, for the topping
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Blitz the strawberries with the sugar.
  2. Melt the chocolate with the thickened cream for the sauce.
  3. Whip the thickened cream for the topping.
  4. Scoop ice cream into four tall glasses and spoon over both sauces.
  5. Top with whipped cream and a cherry.

Pink Ladies coconut iceVegetarian

Pink and white coconut and condensed milk candy squares

Prep 20 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g desiccated coconut
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 2 drops pink food colouring
  1. Mix the coconut, condensed milk and icing sugar into a stiff mix.
  2. Press half into a lined tin.
  3. Tint the rest pink and press it on top.
  4. Chill until firm, about 3 hours, then cut into squares.

Cherry cola floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Cola over vanilla ice cream and cherry syrup

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best built to order or the ice cream melts and the cola goes flat; chill the cola and glasses the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml cherry syrup
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 1l cola, cold
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Spoon cherry syrup into each tall glass and add a big scoop of ice cream.
  2. Pour cold cola slowly down the side so it foams up.
  3. Top with a cherry and serve with a long spoon and a straw.
