Snacks

Nui Dat concert night satay skewers

Chicken satay with peanut sauce, for the Little Pattie concert at Nui Dat.

Prep 21 min, cook 11 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 lemongrass stalk
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 150g crunchy peanut butter
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 limes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  1. Dice the chicken and toss with chopped lemongrass, turmeric, garlic, fish sauce and brown sugar.
  2. Marinate in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill, turning.
  5. Simmer the peanut butter, coconut milk, soy, brown sugar, chilli and lime juice into a satay sauce.
  6. Serve with cucumber and lime wedges.

Corned beef and pickle sandwiches

Sliced corned beef with mustard pickles and lettuce on buttered white bread, cut in quarters.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 hours ahead, wrapped in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 340g tin corned beef
  • 4 tbsp mustard pickles
  • 1 small iceberg lettuce
  1. Butter the bread.
  2. Lay the sliced corned beef over half the slices, spread with pickles and add shredded lettuce.
  3. Top, press down and cut into quarters.

Mains

D Company field kitchen beef and beans

Beef and beans on rice. Smith won't leave 11 Platoon behind.

Prep 32 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chuck steak
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g crushed tomatoes (tin)
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 400g cannellini beans (tin)
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar of pickled chillies
  1. Dice the chuck, onion, capsicum and garlic.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  3. Add the onion, garlic and capsicum and cook.
  4. Stir in the paprika, cumin, crushed tomatoes, stock and drained cannellini beans.
  5. Cover and simmer until tender.
  6. Cook the rice and stir through the butter.
  7. Serve with sliced pickled chilli.

Delta Company curried sausages

Beef sausages in a mild curry gravy with peas and sultanas, over rice, before the long patrol.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the curry up to 2 days ahead and reheat; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 tbsp mild curry powder
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp sultanas
  • 300g white rice
  1. Brown the sausages, then cut into thick slices.
  2. Soften the onion and carrot, then stir in the curry powder, flour and stock.
  3. Add the sausages, peas and sultanas and simmer until thick, cooking the rice alongside.
  4. Serve the curry over the rice.

Sweets

Ration pack chocolate orange potsVegetarian

Dark chocolate cream pots scented with orange.

Prep 10 min, cook 11 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 2 oranges
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 shortbread biscuits
  1. Heat the cream with the orange zest until steaming.
  2. Pour over the chopped chocolate, leave to melt and stir until smooth.
  3. Stir in the orange juice.
  4. Pour into small glasses.
  5. Chill for 2 hours.
  6. Simmer thin strips of orange zest in sugar and water.
  7. Top the pots with whipped cream, the candied zest and crumbled shortbread.

Drinks

Nui Dat Vietnamese iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong dark-roast coffee with condensed milk over ice.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g dark-roast ground coffee
  • 8 tbsp condensed milk
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Brew very strong dark-roast coffee, using a Vietnamese phin filter if you have one or a plunger at double strength.
  2. Spoon condensed milk into each glass.
  3. Pour the hot coffee over and stir until creamy.
  4. Fill with ice and stir again until cold.

When to serve

Serve the main when 11 Platoon calls the artillery in close.
