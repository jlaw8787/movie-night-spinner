Snacks

Willy's salmon bites

Soy, honey and ginger glazed salmon cubes, baked and served on toothpicks.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g salmon fillet, skin off
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  1. Cut the salmon into bite-sized cubes. Mix the soy sauce, honey and ginger and toss the salmon through.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and bake at 200°C until just cooked and sticky.
  3. Spear on toothpicks and serve with lime wedges.

Glen and Annie's grilled cheese soldiersVegetarian

Golden grilled cheddar sandwiches cut in fingers, tomato sauce to dip.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 8 slices of cheddar
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce, for dipping
  1. Butter the bread on the outside and sandwich with the cheddar.
  2. Cook in a pan over medium heat until golden and melted on both sides.
  3. Cut each sandwich into 4 fingers and serve warm with tomato sauce.

Oregon coast clam chowder cups

Creamy bacon, potato and thyme soup, chives and oyster crackers, from Willy's home waters.

How it is made
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 cans baby clams
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 packet oyster crackers
  1. Creamy clam chowder with bacon, potato, onion and thyme, ladled into small cups and topped with crumbled bacon, chives and oyster crackers. Fry 150g diced bacon until crisp and set half aside. Soften an onion and 2 celery sticks in the fat, stir in 2 tbsp flour, then add 2 cans baby clams with their juice, 400ml milk, 2 diced potatoes and thyme. Simmer 15 minutes until the potato is tender. Stir in 150ml cream and heat through without boiling. Ladle into cups and top with bacon, chives and oyster crackers.

Randolph's chilli crab linguine

Linguine with crab, garlic, chilli and lemon, topped with toasted breadcrumbs.

Prep 9 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g linguine
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 80ml extra virgin olive oil, plus a little extra
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1/2 red chilli, seeded and sliced
  • 300g crab meat
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Cook the linguine in well salted water.
  2. Toast the breadcrumbs in a little oil until golden. Set aside.
  3. Gently fry the garlic and chilli in the oil for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the crab, lemon zest and juice and a ladle of pasta water and warm through.
  5. Toss with the drained pasta and parsley, then scatter with the crumbs.

Nobody steals a whale chilli cheese dogs

Franks in toasted rolls under beef and bean chilli, melted cheddar and spring onion.

Prep 8 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; reheat it and heat the franks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog rolls
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tinned kidney beans, drained
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil until soft.
  2. Add the mince, chilli powder and cumin and brown well.
  3. Add the tomatoes and kidney beans and simmer until thick.
  4. Simmer the franks in water for 5 minutes and toast the split rolls.
  5. Put a frank in each roll, spoon over the chilli, top with cheddar and grill a minute to melt. Scatter with sliced spring onion.

Breakwater cookies and creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with crushed cookies and a white chocolate fin.

Prep 20 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 150g chocolate cream biscuits, crushed
  • 100g white chocolate
  1. Soften the ice cream slightly and fold through the crushed biscuits. Freeze for 2 hours.
  2. Melt the white chocolate, pipe little fin shapes onto baking paper and set in the fridge.
  3. Scoop the ice cream into bowls and stand a fin on top.

Drinks

Blue lemonade ice cream floatNo alcohol

Lemonade tinted blue with a scoop of vanilla ice cream floating on top.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cold lemonade
  • 1 tsp blue food colouring
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  1. Pour the lemonade into tall glasses and add a drop or two of blue colouring to each, stirring until ocean blue.
  2. Gently add a scoop of ice cream so it floats and foams. Serve straight away with a long spoon and a straw.
