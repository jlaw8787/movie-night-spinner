Snacks

Hotel bar edamame and nutsVegetarian

Salted sesame edamame with rice crackers and wasabi peas.

Prep 4 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Best steamed just before serving; the crackers and wasabi peas can be set out in bowls up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g frozen edamame
  • flaky salt, to taste
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 packet rice crackers
  • 100g wasabi peas
  1. Steam the edamame, 4 min.
  2. Toss with flaky salt and a little sesame oil.
  3. Serve in small bowls with rice crackers and wasabi peas.

Karaoke night karaage

Soy and ginger marinated chicken, double fried until crisp, with mayo and lemon.

Prep 14 min, cook 17 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate a few hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sake
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100g potato starch
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  1. Cut the chicken into pieces and mix with the soy, sake, ginger and crushed garlic.
  2. Marinate in the fridge, 1 hr.
  3. Coat in potato starch and fry in the vegetable oil at 160°C, then rest, 12 min.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until crisp, 5 min.
  5. Serve with mayonnaise and lemon.

3am tuna mayo and salmon onigiri

Seasoned rice round tuna mayo and salmon, nori wrapped just before eating, for a sleepless night.

How it is made
  • 400g short grain rice
  • 1 x 185g tin tuna
  • Japanese mayonnaise
  • soy sauce
  • 200g salmon fillet
  • nori
  1. Warm seasoned short grain rice pressed into triangles around tuna mayo or flaked salted salmon, wrapped in crisp nori just before eating. Wash 400g short grain rice until clear and cook with 480ml water. Season with salt and cool until just warm. Mix a 185g tin of tuna with 3 tbsp Japanese mayo and a little soy. Salt a 200g salmon fillet, grill 8 minutes and flake. With wet, salted hands shape 8 triangles, pushing a spoon of filling into the middle of each. Wrap with nori strips just before eating.

Shabu-shabu

Thin sliced beef, tofu and vegetables swished in kombu broth, dipped in ponzu.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Soak the kombu in the water up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; slice the vegetables and tofu up to 1 day ahead and keep covered and chilled. Cook the rice and simmer the broth at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 1 piece kombu
  • 2 L water
  • 600g thinly sliced beef
  • 300g firm tofu
  • 1/4 wombok
  • 100g enoki mushrooms
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bottle ponzu
  • 300g short grain rice
  1. Soak the kombu in the water, 30 min.
  2. Slice the wombok, enoki and spring onions, slice the tofu and arrange on platters with the beef.
  3. Cook the rice, 20 min.
  4. Bring the broth to a simmer at the table. Swish the beef, tofu and vegetables through the broth and dip in ponzu, 20 min.

Kyoto kitsune udon with soft egg

Thick noodles in kombu and bonito dashi, sweet tofu and tempura flakes, slurped slowly.

How it is made
  • kombu
  • 20g bonito flakes
  • soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp mirin
  • 4 fried tofu pouches
  • caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 800g fresh udon noodles
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • tempura flakes
  1. Thick udon in a dashi of kombu and bonito with soy and mirin, topped with sweet simmered tofu, a soft egg, spring onion and tempura flakes. Soak a 10cm piece of kombu in 1.6L water for 30 minutes, bring to a simmer, remove and add 20g bonito flakes. Steep 5 minutes and strain, then season with 4 tbsp soy and 3 tbsp mirin. Simmer 4 fried tofu pouches in 200ml of the broth with 1 tbsp sugar for 10 minutes. Boil 4 eggs for 6.5 minutes. Cook 800g fresh udon for 2 minutes, divide into bowls and top with broth, tofu, halved eggs, spring onion and tempura flakes.

Sweets

Matcha soft serveVegetarian

Creamy matcha ice cream scooped into waffle cones.

Prep 13 min, 6 hr waiting · Make ahead: Freeze on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp matcha powder
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 4 waffle cones
  1. Whisk the matcha into the condensed milk.
  2. Whip the cream to soft peaks and fold through.
  3. Freeze, 6 hr.
  4. Scoop soft into waffle cones.

Drinks

Japanese whisky highball

Japanese whisky and cold soda water over ice with a lemon twist.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the whisky and soda water up to 1 day ahead; nothing else to do. Build each glass over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml Japanese whisky
  • 1 L soda water
  • 1 lemon
  • ice, to serve
  1. Pack four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Add the whisky to each and top with very cold soda water.
  3. Stir once and add a twist of lemon. Sip it slowly, jet-lagged, at the hotel bar.
