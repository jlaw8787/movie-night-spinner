Snacks

Overworld loaded jacket potatoes

Baked potatoes with butter, cheddar, crisp bacon, sour cream and chives

Prep 15 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Bake the potatoes and fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; split, top and return to the oven on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large potatoes
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 150g diced bacon
  • 60g butter
  • 120g cheddar
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Heat the oven to 200C.
  2. Prick the large potatoes, rub with oil and salt and bake directly on the rack for 60 to 70 minutes until crisp outside and soft inside.
  3. Fry the diced bacon until crisp.
  4. Split the potatoes, fluff the insides with a fork and add the butter.
  5. Top with the grated cheddar and return to the oven for 5 minutes.
  6. Finish with bacon, the sour cream and chives.

Mains

Steve's honey drumsticks

Sticky roast drumsticks in honey, chilli flakes and smoked paprika

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 chicken drumsticks
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Toss the chicken drumsticks with the honey, the chilli flakes, the smoked paprika, the oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 200C for 45 minutes, turning and basting twice, until sticky and charred at the edges.

Sweets

Pixel chocolate cookiesVegetarian

Chewy chocolate chip cookie bars baked in a tin and cut in squares

Prep 15 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 150g chocolate chips
  1. Beat the butter with the brown sugar and the egg, then mix in the flour, the bicarbonate of soda and the chocolate chips.
  2. Press into a lined 20cm square tin and bake at 175C for 22 minutes.
  3. Cut into neat squares while warm.

Pumpkin pieVegetarian

Roasted pumpkin and condensed milk filling with cinnamon in a crisp crust

Prep 25 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g butternut pumpkin
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Roast the pumpkin until soft and purée.
  2. Whisk with the condensed milk, the eggs, the cinnamon and a pinch of nutmeg.
  3. Pour into a blind-baked shortcrust case and bake at 180C for 45 minutes until just set.
  4. Cool and serve with the whipped cream.

Drinks

Milk bucket chocolate malt shakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream, milk and malted milk powder blended thick

Prep 8 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; crush the malt balls and drizzle the glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chocolate ice cream
  • 400ml milk
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • chocolate syrup, to taste
  • 80g chocolate malt balls
  1. Drizzle chocolate syrup inside the tall glasses.
  2. Blend the chocolate ice cream, the cold milk, the malted milk powder and the chocolate syrup until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into the glasses.
  4. Roughly crush the chocolate malt balls in a bag with a rolling pin and scatter over the top.
  5. Serve with straws.
