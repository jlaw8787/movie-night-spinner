Overworld loaded jacket potatoes

Baked potatoes with butter, cheddar, crisp bacon, sour cream and chives.

Prep 6 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Bake the potatoes and fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; split, top and return to the oven on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large potatoes
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 150g diced bacon
  • 60g butter
  • 120g cheddar
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Prick the large potatoes, rub with oil and salt and bake directly on the rack for 60 to 70 minutes until crisp outside and soft inside.
  3. Fry the diced bacon until crisp.
  4. Split the potatoes, fluff the insides with a fork and add the butter.
  5. Top with the grated cheddar and return to the oven for 5 minutes.
  6. Finish with bacon, the sour cream and chives.

Melon block skewers with mint sugar

Watermelon, rockmelon and honeydew in neat cubes on sticks, lime herb sugar, chicken jockey approved.

How it is made
  • quarter watermelon
  • half rockmelon
  • half honeydew melon
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lime
  • 12 skewers
  1. Watermelon, rockmelon and honeydew cut into perfect cubes, stacked onto skewers like blocks and sprinkled with lime and mint sugar. Cut a quarter of a watermelon, half a rockmelon and half a honeydew into neat 2.5cm cubes, trimming them square. Thread onto 12 skewers in alternating colours, or stack into little towers on a board. Pound 2 tbsp caster sugar with 6 mint leaves and the zest of 1 lime in a mortar until green. Squeeze lime juice over the melon and sprinkle with the mint sugar just before serving.

Honey drumsticks

Sticky roast drumsticks in honey, chilli flakes and smoked paprika.

Prep 0 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 chicken drumsticks
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Toss the chicken drumsticks with the honey, the chilli flakes, the smoked paprika, the oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 200°C for 45 minutes, turning and basting twice, until sticky and charred at the edges.

Crafted mushroom stew with cheesy toast

The stew crafted from red and brown mushrooms.

How it is made
  • 800g mixed mushrooms
  • 60g butter
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 baguette
  • 80g cheddar
  1. Mixed mushrooms browned in butter and simmered with onion, garlic, thyme, stock and cream until rich and thick, with cheesy garlic toast for dunking. Brown 800g mixed sliced mushrooms in batches in 40g butter over high heat. Remove. Soften 1 diced onion and 2 garlic cloves for 5 minutes, then stir in 1 tbsp plain flour and thyme. Add 500ml vegetable stock and the mushrooms and simmer 15 minutes. Stir in 150ml cream and season. Top 8 slices of baguette with garlic butter and 80g grated cheddar and grill until bubbling. Serve the stew in bowls with the toast.

Pixel chocolate cookiesVegetarian

Chewy chocolate chip cookie bars baked in a tin and cut in squares.

Prep 10 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 150g chocolate chips
  1. Beat the butter with the brown sugar and the egg, then mix in the flour, the bicarbonate of soda and the chocolate chips.
  2. Press into a lined 20cm square tin and bake at 175°C for 22 minutes.
  3. Cut into neat squares while warm.

Pumpkin pieVegetarian

Roasted pumpkin and condensed milk filling with cinnamon in a crisp crust.

Prep 11 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g butternut pumpkin
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Roast the pumpkin until soft and purée.
  2. Whisk with the condensed milk, the eggs, the cinnamon and a pinch of nutmeg.
  3. Pour into a blind-baked shortcrust case and bake at 180°C for 45 minutes until just set.
  4. Cool and serve with the whipped cream.

Drinks

Milk bucket chocolate malt shakeNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream, milk and malted milk powder blended thick.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; crush the malt balls and drizzle the glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chocolate ice cream
  • 400ml milk
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • chocolate syrup, to taste
  • 80g chocolate malt balls
  1. Drizzle chocolate syrup inside the tall glasses.
  2. Blend the chocolate ice cream, the cold milk, the malted milk powder and the chocolate syrup until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into the glasses.
  4. Roughly crush the chocolate malt balls in a bag with a rolling pin and scatter over the top.
  5. Serve with straws.
