Marigold bridge cornVegetarian

Charred corn with lime mayo, feta, chilli powder and coriander

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime mayonnaise up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; grill the corn and finish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 1 tbsp orange nasturtium petals
  1. Grill the corn until charred.
  2. Brush with mayonnaise mixed with lime juice.
  3. Roll in the feta, dust with chilli powder and coriander and scatter a few petals on top.

Abuelita's tamales

Pork and red chile tamales in masa dough

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Filled tamales up to 1 day ahead, steam to reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork shoulder
  • 4 tbsp red chilli sauce
  • 300g masa harina
  • 100g lard
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 400ml warm chicken stock
  • 16 corn husks, soaked
  • 200g salsa
  1. Simmer the pork until tender, then shred and mix with the red chilli sauce.
  2. Soak the corn husks in warm water.
  3. Beat the masa with the lard, baking powder and stock to a soft dough.
  4. Spread on the husks, add the pork and fold.
  5. Steam for 60 minutes.
  6. Serve with salsa.

Santa Cecilia plaza guacamole and totoposOn the printed menu

Chunky avocado with lime and chilli, pomegranate, pepitas, corn chips, no music at this table

How it is made
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 litre vegetable oil
  • 3 avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 pomegranate
  • 50g pepitas
  1. Chunky guacamole with lime, chilli, onion and coriander, topped with pomegranate and toasted pepitas, with crisp fried corn tortilla chips. Cut 8 corn tortillas into wedges and fry at 180°C for 2 minutes until crisp, then salt. Mash 3 ripe avocados roughly with the juice of 2 limes and salt. Fold in half a finely diced white onion, 1 seeded chopped jalapeño, 1 diced tomato and a handful of coriander. Top with 2 tbsp pomegranate seeds and 2 tbsp toasted pepitas and serve with the warm chips.

Rivera family enchiladas

Chicken enchiladas in a dried chile sauce, topped with cheese

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 200g Monterey Jack, grated
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 white onion, diced
  1. Soak the chillies, blitz with the garlic and stock and simmer into a sauce.
  2. Poach and shred the chicken.
  3. Dip the tortillas in sauce, roll with chicken and some of the Monterey Jack, then cover with sauce and the rest of the cheese.
  4. Bake at 190°C for 20 minutes. Serve with sour cream and onion.

Ofrenda red pork pozoleOn the printed menu

Shoulder and hominy in deep red chilli broth, cabbage, radish and lime, for Día de Muertos

How it is made
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 4 dried guajillo chillies
  • 2 dried ancho chillies
  • 2 tins hominy
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 3 limes
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 packet tostadas
  1. Pork shoulder and hominy simmered in a deep red chilli broth, topped at the table with shredded cabbage, radish, lime, oregano and crisp tostadas. Simmer 1.2kg diced pork shoulder in 2.5 litres water with 1 halved onion, 4 garlic cloves and 2 tsp salt for 1.5 hours. Soak 4 dried guajillo and 2 ancho chillies in hot water for 20 minutes, then blend with 2 garlic cloves and a little soaking water and strain into the pot. Add 2 drained tins hominy and simmer 30 minutes more. Serve with shredded cabbage, sliced radish, lime wedges, dried oregano and tostadas.

Pan de muertoVegetarian

Sweet orange yeast bread topped with dough bones

Prep 35 min, cook 35 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 450g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 2 oranges, zested
  • 3 eggs
  • 120g butter, 80g soft and 40g melted
  • 100ml warm water
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, part of the sugar, orange zest, eggs, soft butter and warm water into a soft dough and knead.
  2. Prove for 2 hours.
  3. Shape into rounds with dough bones on top.
  4. Prove again for 30 minutes.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30 minutes.
  6. Brush with melted butter and roll in the remaining sugar.

ChampurradoVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick Mexican hot chocolate made with masa, cinnamon and brown sugar

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; it thickens, so reheat with a splash of milk
Recipe for 4
  • 50g masa harina
  • 250ml water
  • 1l milk
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 60g brown sugar
  1. Whisk the masa into the water until smooth.
  2. Heat the milk with the chocolate, cinnamon and sugar, then whisk in the masa.
  3. Simmer, stirring, until thick and silky. Pour into mugs.
