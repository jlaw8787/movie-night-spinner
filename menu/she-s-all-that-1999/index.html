Laney's falafel shift wrapsVegetarian

Laney in the falafel hat

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Blend and shape the falafel up to a day ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 400g tins chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 1 small brown onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup flat-leaf parsley
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  1. Pulse the chickpeas, onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, flour and salt to a coarse paste and roll into 16 balls.
  2. Fry in hot oil in batches until deep brown and crisp.
  3. Whisk the tahini with lemon juice, salt and water until pourable.
  4. Warm the pita, fill with falafel and tomato and drizzle with tahini.

Brock Hudson's Real World nachosVegetarian

Taylor dumps Zack

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Season the beans and make the guacamole up to 4 hours ahead; layer and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 230g plain corn chips
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 cup tomato salsa
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1 lime
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp sliced pickled jalapenos
  1. Warm the beans with the paprika, cumin, a splash of water and salt, crushing a few.
  2. Mash the avocados with the lime juice and salt.
  3. Layer the chips, beans and cheddar on a tray and bake at 200C until the cheese melts.
  4. Top with salsa, guacamole, sour cream and jalapenos and serve straight away.

Hacky sack meatball subs

The hacky sack act

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead; reheat and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700g jar tomato passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 8 slices provolone
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  1. Mix the mince, crumbs, egg, parmesan, half the garlic and salt, and roll into 20 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in a hot pan.
  3. Add the passata, oregano and the rest of the garlic and simmer until thick.
  4. Split the rolls, fill with meatballs and sauce, top with provolone and grill until melted.
  5. Tuck in the basil and serve.

Beach day fish tacos

Laney says yes to the beach

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw and chipotle cream up to 4 hours ahead; grill the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g firm white fish fillets
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 3 limes
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Rub the fish with the spices, oil and salt.
  2. Toss the cabbage with the juice of 2 limes and salt, and stir the chipotle into the sour cream.
  3. Grill the fish until just flaking, then break into chunks.
  4. Warm the tortillas and fill with slaw, fish, chipotle cream, coriander and lime.

Red dress on the stairs strawberry shortcakeVegetarian

Laney on the stairs

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake the shortcakes up to a day ahead; macerate the berries and whip the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 60g caster sugar, plus 2 tbsp for the berries
  • 80g cold butter, cubed
  • 180ml buttermilk
  • 500g strawberries, sliced
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Toss the strawberries with 2 tbsp sugar and leave to go juicy.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and 60g sugar, stir in the buttermilk and pat out 3cm thick.
  3. Cut 4 rounds and bake at 210C until golden.
  4. Whip the cream with the vanilla, split the shortcakes and fill with berries and cream.

Foghorn for Dean

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices up to a day ahead and chill; add lemonade and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 750ml lemonade
  • 2 limes, sliced
  • 1 cup frozen raspberries
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Stir the pineapple and cranberry juices together in a punch bowl.
  2. Add the lime slices, frozen raspberries and ice.
  3. Top with lemonade just before serving.
