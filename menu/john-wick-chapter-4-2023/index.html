Snacks

Berlin currywurst

Sliced grilled bratwurst in curry tomato sauce, dusted with curry powder.

Prep 3 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat; fry the fries and grill the bratwurst fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bratwurst
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 200ml water
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Cut the potatoes into fries and fry in the vegetable oil until golden.
  2. Grill the bratwurst slowly until browned, then slice.
  3. Soften the onion, add the tomato paste, curry powder, sugar, vinegar and water and simmer.
  4. Pour over the sausage and dust with curry powder. Serve with the fries.

Cheese puffs (gougères)Vegetarian

Light choux pastry puffs baked with gruyère and nutmeg.

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best fresh from the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g gruyere
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour and stir over heat for a minute.
  2. Cool slightly, beat in the eggs one at a time, then the grated gruyere and nutmeg.
  3. Pipe small rounds.
  4. Bake at 200°C.

Shimazu's Osaka Continental takoyaki

Crisp octopus balls with spring onion and ginger, tangy sauce, Japanese mayo and bonito flakes.

How it is made
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 jar dashi powder
  • 200g cooked octopus
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 bottle okonomiyaki sauce
  • 1 bottle Japanese mayonnaise
  • 1 packet bonito flakes
  • 1 packet nori flakes
  1. Crisp golden octopus balls with spring onion and pickled ginger, drizzled with tangy okonomiyaki sauce and Japanese mayo, topped with dancing bonito flakes. Whisk 200g plain flour, 2 eggs, 600ml water and 1 tsp dashi powder into a thin batter. Heat an oiled takoyaki pan until smoking, fill the holes, then drop in 200g diced cooked octopus, sliced spring onion and pickled ginger. Cook 2 minutes, then turn each ball with a skewer every minute until round and crisp, about 6 minutes. Drizzle with okonomiyaki sauce and Japanese mayo and top with bonito flakes and nori.

Mains

Paris steak frites

Pan seared sirloin in peppercorn brandy cream, with thin cut fries.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the steaks the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp black peppercorns
  • 60ml brandy
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 30g butter
  1. Dry-brine the steaks overnight.
  2. Cut the potatoes thin and fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C, drain, then fry at 185°C until crisp.
  3. Sear the steaks hard in cast iron, pull at 54°C and rest.
  4. Crush the peppercorns in the pan, flame the brandy, add the cream and reduce until it coats a spoon.
  5. Finish with the butter and serve.

Pork belly okonomiyaki

Cabbage pancakes crisped under thin belly slices, sweet glaze, mayo, bonito flakes and ginger.

How it is made
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1 jar dashi powder
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 small wombok cabbage
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 300g thin-sliced pork belly
  • 1 bottle okonomiyaki sauce
  • 1 bottle Japanese mayonnaise
  • 1 packet bonito flakes
  • 1 packet nori flakes
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  1. Thick savoury cabbage pancakes crisped under thin pork belly slices, flipped and lacquered with okonomiyaki sauce, mayo, bonito flakes and pickled ginger. Whisk 200g plain flour, 250ml water, 1 tsp dashi powder and 4 eggs. Fold in half a wombok, finely shredded, and 4 sliced spring onions. Heat an oiled pan, add a quarter of the batter, press into a thick round and lay 3 thin slices of pork belly on top. Cook 5 minutes, flip and cook 5 more until the pork crisps. Repeat. Brush with okonomiyaki sauce, zigzag with mayo and top with bonito, nori and pickled ginger.

Sweets

Sacré-Coeur croissant puddingVegetarian

Croissant and dark chocolate baked in vanilla custard.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best fresh from the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 6 stale croissants
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 60g caster sugar
  1. Tear the croissants into a buttered dish and scatter with chopped chocolate.
  2. Whisk the eggs, cream, milk and sugar and pour over.
  3. Rest so the croissants soak it up.
  4. Bake at 170°C until puffed and just set.

Drinks

Sacré-Cœur steps cognac sidecar

Cognac, orange liqueur and lemon shaken and strained into a sugar rimmed coupe.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; chill the coupes and shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml cognac
  • 80ml orange liqueur
  • 3 lemons
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill four coupes. Rub the rims with lemon and dip in sugar.
  2. Shake the cognac, orange liqueur and fresh lemon juice hard with ice for 15 seconds, one drink at a time.
  3. Strain into the coupes and twist orange peel over the top.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the fight reaches the steps at sunrise.
