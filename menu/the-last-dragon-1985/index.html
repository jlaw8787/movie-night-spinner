Chopstick popcornVegetarian

Stovetop popcorn tossed in melted butter and salt

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Pop the corn up to 4 hours ahead and keep it airtight; toss with the butter, furikake and sugar just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 120g popping corn kernels
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 3 tbsp furikake
  • 1 pinch caster sugar
  1. Heat the oil in a large pot with three kernels and cover.
  2. When they pop, add the rest of the kernels, cover and shake until the popping slows.
  3. Toss with the melted butter, furikake and sugar. Hand out chopsticks.

Harlem spring rollsVegetarian

Crisp rolls filled with cabbage, carrot, shiitake and glass noodles

Prep 23 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 1 day ahead, chilled; roll up to 4 hours ahead under a damp cloth and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 100g shiitake mushrooms
  • 50g glass noodles
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • white pepper, to taste
  • 16 spring roll wrappers
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, for stir-frying
  1. Soak the noodles in hot water, then drain.
  2. Stir-fry the cabbage, grated carrot and sliced shiitake with the noodles, soy and white pepper. Cool.
  3. Roll tightly in the wrappers, sealing with water.
  4. Fry in 180°C oil until golden, in batches. Serve with sweet chilli.

General Tso's chicken

Crispy fried chicken thigh in a sweet, spicy soy and dried chilli sauce

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Dice the chicken up to 1 day ahead, chilled, and mix the sauce up to 2 days ahead; coat, fry and toss on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 100g potato starch
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 8 dried chillies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 20g ginger
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 125ml chicken stock
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Dice the chicken and toss in the potato starch.
  2. Start the rice and cook until fluffy.
  3. Fry the chicken in 180°C oil in batches, drain, then fry a second time until crisp.
  4. Fry the chillies, crushed garlic and grated ginger in a hot wok, then add soy, vinegar, sugar and stock.
  5. Toss the chicken through the sauce and serve over rice.

Glowing mango mochiVegetarian

Soft coconut rice dough wrapped around mango and whipped cream

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g glutinous rice flour
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 40g cornflour
  • 1 mango
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Mix the rice flour, sugar and coconut milk until smooth and pour into a heatproof bowl.
  2. Steam, stirring halfway, until thick and translucent.
  3. Dust a board with cornflour, tip out the dough and divide it into pieces.
  4. Wrap each piece around a cube of mango and a spoonful of whipped cream.
  5. Chill until set.

Chrysanthemum iced teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled chrysanthemum flower tea, lightly sweet with rock sugar

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 15g dried chrysanthemum flowers
  • 1l boiling water
  • 50g rock sugar
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Steep the flowers and rock sugar in just-boiled water.
  2. Strain, cool and chill well in the fridge.
  3. Pour over ice in tall glasses and float a flower or two on top.
