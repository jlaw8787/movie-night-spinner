Snacks

Moulin Rouge oysters

Fresh shucked oysters on ice with shallot vinegar and lemon.

Prep 45 min · Make ahead: Mignonette up to 1 day, shuck oysters just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 24 oysters, freshly shucked
  • 6 cups crushed ice
  • 2 lemons, cut in wedges
  1. Mix the shallots, vinegar and pepper for the mignonette and rest, 20 min.
  2. Shuck the oysters.
  3. Lay the oysters on crushed ice with lemon wedges and the mignonette alongside.

Montmartre gougèresVegetarian

Warm gruyère cheese puffs with a hint of nutmeg.

Prep 14 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g gruyère, grated
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a tray. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour and stir over heat for a minute, 6 min.
  2. Cool slightly, then beat in the eggs, gruyère and nutmeg, 8 min.
  3. Pipe small rounds onto the tray.
  4. Bake until puffed and golden, 20 min.

Mains

Elephant supper duck with cherry sauce

Crisp-skinned duck breast, red wine cherry sauce and buttery mashed potatoes.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 5 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 4 duck breasts
  • 200g cherries, pitted
  • 200ml red wine
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled
  • 100g butter
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Score the duck skin, season with salt and pepper and render skin side down over medium heat, 8 min.
  2. Roast at 200°C, then rest, 15 min.
  3. Simmer the cherries with the wine and thyme until syrupy, 20 min.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter until silky, 22 min.
  5. Slice the duck and serve with the cherry sauce.

Toulouse's bohemian steak frites

Salted sirloin seared in cast iron, skinny chips, café de Paris butter, for the penniless poet.

How it is made
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 100g butter
  • Dijon mustard
  • anchovies
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • capers
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch watercress
  1. Salted sirloin seared hard in cast iron, sliced over skinny double-fried frites, with café de Paris butter melting on top and a sharp watercress salad. Salt 4 sirloin steaks and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Mash 100g soft butter with 1 tsp Dijon, 1 chopped anchovy, 1 small garlic clove, chopped parsley, tarragon and capers, roll into a log and chill. Fry 1kg skinny cut potatoes at 140°C for 5 minutes, rest, then at 185°C for 3 minutes. Sear the steaks in a smoking pan for 3 minutes a side to 54°C, rest 5 minutes and slice. Top with butter coins and serve with frites and dressed watercress.

Sweets

Red windmill raspberry tartVegetarian

Sweet pastry shell with vanilla custard and fresh raspberries.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min, 25 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 400ml milk
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 300g raspberries
  • 3 tbsp raspberry jam
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Line a tart tin with the pastry and blind bake, 25 min.
  2. Cook the milk, yolks, sugar and cornflour until thick, 10 min.
  3. Chill the custard and the case, 25 min.
  4. Fill the case with custard.
  5. Cover with raspberries edge to edge and brush with warm jam, 5 min.

Drinks

Green fairy absinthe or green apple mint

Absinthe dripped over a sugar cube, or green apple, mint and soda for everyone else.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Blitz and strain the apple mint juice up to 3 hours ahead and keep it chilled; make the absinthe drips and top with soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 40ml absinthe, for the grown ups
  • 2 sugar cubes, for the grown ups
  • 3 green apples
  • 1 handful of mint
  • 400ml soda water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. For the grown ups, drip cold water over a sugar cube into the absinthe until cloudy.
  2. For everyone else, blitz the apples with mint and strain.
  3. Pour the absinthe mix or the apple mint juice into small glasses over ice and top with soda water.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when the absinthe kicks in for the Bohemians.
