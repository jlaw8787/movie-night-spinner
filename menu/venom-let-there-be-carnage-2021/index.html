Snacks

Sonny and Cher's chicken bites

Chicken thigh chunks in panko, baked until golden.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled on the tray; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 can cooking oil spray
  1. Cut the chicken into chunks. Beat the eggs.
  2. Dip in egg and press into panko.
  3. Spread on a lined tray, spray with oil and bake at 220°C.
  4. Mix the honey and mustard for dipping.

Mrs Chen's sriracha mayo potato gems

Baked crunchy, tossed in Japanese mayo with chilli sauce, garlic and lime, for the rave.

How it is made
  • 1kg frozen potato gems
  • 150g Japanese mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle sriracha
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lime
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 jar sesame seeds
  1. Frozen potato gems baked at 220°C for 20 minutes until crunchy, served with mayo mixed with sriracha and garlic. Spread 1kg frozen potato gems on 2 lined trays and bake at 220°C for 20 minutes, shaking halfway, until crunchy. Mix 150g Japanese mayo with 2 tbsp sriracha, 1 small grated garlic clove and a squeeze of lime. Toss the hot gems with a pinch of salt, pile into bowls, drizzle with the sriracha mayo and scatter with sliced spring onion and toasted sesame seeds.

Mains

Bacon and scrambled eggs on toast

Crisp bacon and soft scrambled eggs on buttered white toast.

Prep 10 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead and reheat in a hot pan
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 50g butter
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • salt, to taste
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  2. Whisk the eggs with salt and scramble slowly in some of the butter until soft.
  3. Toast and butter the bread.
  4. Pile everything onto plates with a square of dark chocolate on the side.

Mission-style chipotle chicken burritos

Chipotle chicken burritos with rice and beans, Mission style, from San Francisco.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 2 limes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 cup long grain rice
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 400g tin black beans
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Chicken thighs grilled with chipotle and lime, rolled in big flour tortillas with rice, black beans, cheese and salsa, toasted in a pan. Marinate 700g chicken thigh with 2 tbsp chipotle in adobo, the juice of 1 lime, 2 crushed garlic cloves and 1 tsp cumin for 30 minutes. Grill 5 minutes a side and slice. Cook 1 cup rice and stir in coriander and lime. Warm 1 drained tin black beans. Lay out 4 large flour tortillas and fill with rice, beans, chicken, 150g grated cheese, salsa and sour cream. Roll up tightly and toast seam side down in a dry pan for 2 minutes a side.

Sweets

Venom's rocky road chocolate stash

Dark and milk chocolate with marshmallows, set in a tin.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Spread and chill until firm, then keep up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 150g milk chocolate
  • 150g marshmallows
  • 100g honeycomb
  • 100g salted peanuts
  • 80g raspberry lollies
  1. Line a 20cm square tin with baking paper.
  2. Melt both chocolates together in short bursts in the microwave.
  3. Stir in the marshmallows, broken honeycomb, peanuts and lollies.
  4. Spread into the tin and chill until firm.
  5. Smash or cut into chunks and pile in a bowl.

Carnage red velvet cakeVegetarian

Buttermilk red velvet layer cake with cream cheese frosting, red as Carnage.

Prep 22 min, cook 35 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 2 tsp red food colouring
  • 500g cream cheese
  • 300g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and prepare two tins.
  2. Whisk the plain flour, cocoa, bicarb soda and caster sugar, then the eggs, oil, buttermilk and colouring until smooth.
  3. Bake in two tins.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with icing sugar, then stack the cakes and cover the whole cake.

Drinks

Venom's triple chocolate thickshakeNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream shake with dark chocolate fudge down the glass.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; warm gently and blend the shake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 8 scoops chocolate ice cream
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 4 small brownies
  1. Warm the chocolate and cream, stirring until smooth, and cool slightly.
  2. Drizzle a spoon of the sauce inside four tall glasses.
  3. Blend the ice cream, milk and cocoa until thick.
  4. Pour into the glasses, drizzle with the rest of the sauce and crumble one of the small brownies over each.
