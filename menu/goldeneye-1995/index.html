Snacks

Zukovsky's caviar blini

Buckwheat pancakes with crème fraîche, salmon roe and chives, frozen vodka alongside.

Prep 73 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Batter rests up to 2 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 150g buckwheat flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm full cream milk
  • 30g butter
  • 150ml crème fraîche
  • 100g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and warm milk and rest until bubbly.
  2. Cook small spoonfuls in butter until golden.
  3. Top each with crème fraîche, salmon roe and snipped chives.
  4. Serve beside frozen vodka.

Tank chase beef pirozhki

Baked yeast buns filled with beef, onion and dill.

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm full cream milk
  • 60g butter
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, warm milk and some butter into a soft dough.
  2. Prove.
  3. Fry the mince with the onion and dill and cool.
  4. Fill rounds of dough and seal.
  5. Bake at 200°C.
  6. Brush with melted butter.

Mains

Beef stroganoff

Beef fillet strips with mushrooms, paprika, brandy and sour cream sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Slice the beef, mushrooms and onion up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; cook on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef fillet strips
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 1 brown onion
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 30ml brandy
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  1. Slice the beef, mushrooms and onion.
  2. Sear the beef fast and set aside.
  3. Cook the mushrooms and onion in the butter, add the paprika and flame the brandy.
  4. Add the stock and simmer.
  5. Cook the noodles and butter them.
  6. Stir in the sour cream off the heat and return the beef.

Cuban mojo pork, black beans and plantain

Sour orange and garlic shoulder pulled, rice and fried plantain, near the hidden satellite dish.

How it is made
  • 2 oranges
  • 3 limes
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tin black beans
  • 2 ripe plantains
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Pork shoulder marinated in sour orange mojo, slow roasted and pulled, with black beans and rice, fried sweet plantain and quick pickled red onion. Blitz the juice of 2 oranges and 2 limes with 6 garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin and oregano and marinate 1.5kg pork shoulder overnight. Roast covered at 160°C for 4 hours and pull. Cook 300g rice, then fold through a drained, warmed tin of black beans. Slice 2 ripe plantains and fry until caramelised. Toss a sliced red onion in lime juice and salt for 20 minutes.

Syrniki cottage cheese pancakes, berry jamVegetarian

Pan fried cottage cheese patties, golden outside, with warm jam and sour cream.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead, covered on a tray in the fridge; fry on the night and warm the jam
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g butter
  • 4 tbsp berry jam
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Mix the cottage cheese, eggs, sugar, vanilla and some of the flour.
  2. Shape small patties and dust in the remaining flour.
  3. Fry in butter until golden.
  4. Warm the jam.
  5. Serve with sour cream and the jam.

Drinks

Vodka martini

Vodka and dry vermouth shaken hard and strained, with a lemon twist.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best shaken to order; chill the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml vodka
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Shake the vodka and vermouth hard with ice, one drink at a time.
  2. Strain into chilled martini glasses and twist lemon peel over the top.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Bond orders his vodka martini at the casino in Monte Carlo.
