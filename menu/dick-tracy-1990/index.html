Snacks

Club Ritz canapés

Egg yolk mayo topped with salmon roe, plus prosciutto wrapped asparagus.

Prep 18 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch asparagus
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool, peel and halve, 18 min.
  2. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise and Dijon, pipe back into the whites and top with salmon roe.
  3. Blanch the asparagus, 3 min.
  4. Wrap each spear in half a slice of prosciutto.

Yellow coat cheese and pineapple sticksVegetarian

Cubes of sharp cheddar and pineapple on toothpicks, a 1930s club tray.

Prep 16 min · Make ahead: Thread up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sharp cheddar
  • 1 pineapple piece (440g tin)
  • 1 grapefruit, halved
  • 24 toothpicks
  1. Cut the cheddar into 2cm cubes and drain the pineapple well.
  2. Thread a cube of cheese and a piece of pineapple onto each toothpick.
  3. Stand them in the grapefruit half and chill until serving.

Mains

Big Boy's spaghetti

Meatballs in San Marzano tomato, basil.

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Sauce and meatballs up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork and beef mince
  • 2 slices of white bread
  • 60ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 800g San Marzano tomatoes (2 tins)
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Soak the bread in the milk, squeeze and mix with the mince, egg and half the parmesan, 10 min.
  2. Roll into balls and brown in the oil, 12 min.
  3. Simmer in the tomatoes with basil, 1 hr.
  4. Cook the spaghetti, 10 min.
  5. Toss with the sauce and serve with the rest of the parmesan, 3 min.

Tracy's diner pot roast

Chuck braised slow with carrots, onions and gravy.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 onions
  • 4 carrots
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 250ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • bay leaves
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  1. Beef chuck braised low and slow with onion, carrot, red wine and stock until it falls apart, with buttery potatoes and a glossy gravy. Season a 1.5kg chuck roast and brown it all over in a heavy pot. Add 2 quartered onions, 4 carrots in chunks and 3 garlic cloves. Pour in 250ml red wine and 500ml beef stock, add thyme and bay, cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours. Add 800g halved potatoes and cook 1 hour more. Lift out the meat and veg and boil the liquid with 1 tbsp cornflour slurry until glossy.

Lemon meringue pieVegetarian

Tart lemon filling under torched meringue, set firm as a gangster's grudge.

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 lemons
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 250ml water
  • 3 eggs
  • 50g butter
  1. Line a tin with the pastry and blind-bake, 25 min.
  2. Cook the lemon juice and zest, half the sugar, cornflour and water until thick, 10 min.
  3. Beat in the egg yolks and butter, then fill the pastry case.
  4. Whip the egg whites with the remaining sugar to stiff peaks and pile on top.
  5. Torch the meringue until golden.
  6. Chill to set, 5 min.

Breathless Mahoney's gin gimlet

Gin and Rose's lime, shaken frosty.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the coupes in the freezer and juice the limes up to 1 day ahead; shake each round to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml gin
  • 3 limes
  • 40ml lime cordial
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill four coupes in the freezer, 15 min.
  2. Shake the gin, fresh lime juice and lime cordial with plenty of ice until the shaker frosts over.
  3. Strain into the coupes and float a thin lime wheel on each.
