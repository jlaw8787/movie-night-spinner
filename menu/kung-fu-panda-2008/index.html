Snacks

Training bowl dumplings

Shifu makes Po fight for the last one.

Prep 47 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 2 cups finely shredded wombok
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the pork with the wombok, ginger and spring onions and season.
  2. Fold spoonfuls into the wrappers, sealing the edges with water.
  3. Steam in batches over simmering water until cooked through, 8 min.
  4. Mix the soy, black vinegar and chilli oil for dipping.

Oogway's peachesVegetarian

From the tree where Oogway sits.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 ripe peaches
  • 200g ricotta
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 40g almonds, crushed
  1. Halve and stone the peaches.
  2. Grill the peaches cut side down until caramelised, 8 min.
  3. Whip the ricotta with a spoon of honey and the cinnamon, then serve with the peaches, more honey and the almonds.

Mr Ping's red bean steamed bunsVegetarian

Stacked high, worth the climb.

Prep 28 min, cook 37 min, 1 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • salt, a pinch
  • 160ml milk, warm
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 250g red bean paste
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, caster sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl, then add the warm milk and oil and bring together into a dough, 10 min.
  2. Knead for 8 minutes until smooth.
  3. Cover and prove in a warm spot for 1 hour.
  4. Divide into 12, flatten each piece, fill with a tablespoon of red bean paste and pinch closed. Sit each bun on a square of baking paper in a bamboo steamer.
  5. Cover and prove the buns for another 20 minutes.
  6. Steam over boiling water for 12 minutes and serve hot, stacked in the steamer.

Secret ingredient soup

Noodle soup, no secret ingredient.

Prep 20 min, cook 3 hr 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken frames
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 3 L water
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 1 bunch bok choy
  • 300g chicken thigh fillets
  1. Put the frames in a pot with the ginger, garlic and spring onions and cover with the water.
  2. Simmer gently for 3 hours, skimming now and then, then strain. Poach the chicken thighs in the stock for the last 15 minutes.
  3. Season the stock with soy and white pepper and shred the chicken.
  4. Cook the noodles and bok choy briefly, then pour the hot stock over them with the chicken. The secret is there isn't one, 10 min.

Valley of Peace kung pao chicken

The busy village below the Jade Palace.

Prep 17 min, cook 24 min
Recipe for 4
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornflour, for the chicken
  • 1 tsp cornflour, for the sauce
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 8 dried chillies
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 20g ginger
  • 4 spring onions
  • 80g roasted peanuts
  1. Rinse the jasmine rice, then simmer covered in 450ml water on low for 15 minutes and rest off the heat.
  2. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp of the soy sauce and the 1 tbsp cornflour for the chicken. Mix the remaining 3 tbsp soy, the black vinegar, sugar, the cornflour for the sauce and 60ml water for the sauce.
  3. Slice the garlic, grate the ginger and cut the spring onions into 3cm lengths.
  4. Heat the oil in a wok until very hot, stir-fry the dried chillies and Sichuan peppercorns for 20 seconds, then add the chicken and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the garlic, ginger and spring onion, then the sauce, and toss until glossy. Stir in the peanuts and serve with the rice.

Almond cookiesVegetarian

The Furious Five's training snack.

Prep 20 min, cook 14 min
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 120g almond meal
  • 120g plain flour
  • 16 whole almonds
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the almond meal and flour.
  2. Roll into 16 balls, press an almond on each and brush with egg.
  3. Bake at 170°C until golden, 14 min.

Oogway's iced peach jasmine teaNo alcohol

A quiet moment, then Skadoosh.

Prep 13 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 jasmine green tea bags
  • 1 L water at 80°C
  • 3 ripe peaches, 2 for the puree and 1 for slices
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, half juiced
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the water for 3 minutes, remove and chill.
  2. Blend two peeled peaches with the honey and lemon juice until smooth.
  3. Stir the puree into the cold tea and pour over ice with thin peach slices.
