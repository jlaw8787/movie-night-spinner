Snacks

Mail run sandwich

Ham, cheddar and pickle on buttered white, crusts off, cut in fingers, eaten between deliveries.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 30g butter, softened
  • 120g leg ham
  • 80g cheddar, sliced
  • 50g pickles, thinly sliced
  1. Butter the bread.
  2. Layer the ham, cheddar and pickles across four slices and top with the other slices.
  3. Trim off the crusts and cut each sandwich into three fingers.
  4. Wrap in baking paper and refrigerate until serving.

Kansas corn frittersVegetarian

Spoonfuls of kernel batter with spring onion, shallow-fried golden, sour cream for dipping.

Prep 9 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Cut the kernels off the cobs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; mix the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 corn cobs, about 200g of kernels
  • 80g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 60ml milk
  • 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • 100g sour cream
  1. Cut the kernels from the cobs.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, egg and milk into a thick batter. Stir in the corn, spring onions and salt.
  3. Shallow-fry spoonfuls of batter in the neutral oil in batches over medium heat, turning once, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Drain and serve with sour cream for dipping.

Mains

Executive rib eye

Dry-brined, seared hard and basted in butter, thyme and garlic, for Brantley's double life upstairs.

Prep 0 min, cook 21 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the steaks and refrigerate uncovered to dry-brine up to 1 day ahead; sear and baste on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g rib eye steak, in four pieces
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 50g butter
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  1. Pat the steaks dry, salt them evenly and refrigerate uncovered to dry-brine, at least 4 hours.
  2. Heat a heavy pan over high heat with the oil.
  3. Sear the steaks in batches, turning once.
  4. Lower the heat, add the butter, thyme and garlic and baste repeatedly.
  5. Rest the steaks, then slice and spoon over the pan butter.

Sweets

Apple pie à la modeVegetarian

Double crust with cinnamon apples, egg wash and sugar, baked golden, vanilla ice cream.

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 600g Granny Smith apples
  • 85g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 200ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Line a 20cm pie dish with half the pastry.
  2. Peel, core and slice the apples and toss with sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Fill the crust, cover with the remaining pastry, crimp, cut steam vents, brush with egg and sprinkle with a little sugar.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden and tender.
  5. Rest, then divide into four portions and serve with ice cream.

Drinks

Gin martini

Gin and a little dry vermouth, frozen glass, lemon twist, for the weekend party at Vera's.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Freeze the glasses up to 1 day ahead; stir the drinks over ice in two batches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 400g ice
  • 1 lemon, for four thin twists
  1. Freeze four martini glasses.
  2. Working in two batches, stir the gin and vermouth with ice.
  3. Strain equally into the frozen glasses.
  4. Express the lemon twist oils over each drink and garnish.

When to serve
