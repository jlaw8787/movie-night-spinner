Snacks

Húsavík hot dogs

Franks with crispy fried onions, sweet mustard and remoulade, the Húsavík way.

Prep 3 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Dice the onion up to 1 day ahead; grill the frankfurts and fill the buns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 onion
  • 60g crispy fried onions
  • 1 jar sweet mustard
  • 100g tartare sauce
  1. Grill the frankfurts.
  2. Put in the hot dog buns with diced raw onion, the crispy fried onions, sweet mustard and the remoulade or tartare.
  3. Serve straight away on a big platter in the middle of the table.

Smoked trout crispbreads

Rye crispbread with crème fraîche, smoked trout, pickled cucumber, dill.

Prep 6 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Flake the trout and slice the pickles up to 1 day ahead; top the crispbreads just before serving or they go soft
Recipe for 4
  • 16 rye crispbreads
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 250g smoked trout
  • 1 jar pickled cucumbers
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Spread the rye crispbreads with the crème fraîche.
  2. Top with the flaked smoked trout, thin pickled cucumber and dill.
  3. Serve straight away on a big platter in the middle of the table.

Mains

Erick's lamb with caramelised potatoes

Slow-roast lamb shoulder, sugar-glazed potatoes, gravy and buttered peas.

Prep 3 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Roast the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep it in its juices in the fridge; reheat covered at 160°C, then glaze the potatoes, make the gravy and cook the peas fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 80g sugar
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 400g frozen peas
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C.
  2. Rub the lamb shoulder with salt, pepper, thyme and the crushed garlic cloves and roast covered for 3 hours, uncovering for the last 30 minutes.
  3. Boil the baby potatoes until tender and peel.
  4. Melt the sugar in a pan until golden, add the butter and the potatoes and toss 5 minutes until glazed.
  5. Make gravy from the pan juices, the flour, the red wine and the stock.
  6. Serve with buttered peas.

Sweets

Elf house skyr cheesecake potsVegetarian

Tangy skyr cheesecake pots with honey and blueberries, from the elf house in Húsavík.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min, then 66 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 60g plain flour
  • 50g brown sugar
  • 60g butter
  • 400g skyr or Greek yoghurt
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 250g blueberries
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 lemon
  1. Mix the rolled oats, the plain flour, the brown sugar and the melted butter, spread on a tray and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes until golden.
  2. Cool and crumble.
  3. Beat the skyr or thick Greek yoghurt with the cream cheese and the honey until smooth.
  4. Simmer the blueberries with the sugar and lemon juice for 5 minutes.
  5. Layer crumble, skyr and compote in the glasses and chill 1 hour.

Recipe for 4

  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 500ml soda water
  1. Pour the elderflower cordial into each of the flutes and top with chilled sparkling wine.
  2. Elderflower and soda water for the kids.
  3. Make it just before the film starts and keep the jug cold.
