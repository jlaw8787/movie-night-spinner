LaPadite farm fromage blanc with honey and warm baguetteVegetarian

Whipped farm cheese, thyme honey, baguette. A glass of milk at the LaPadite farm.

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the cheese up to a day ahead and chill; warm the bread to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fromage blanc or quark
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Whip the fromage blanc with the cream, lemon zest and a pinch of salt until smooth and light.
  2. Warm the honey with the thyme leaves for a minute and let it sit, 5 min.
  3. Slice the baguette, brush with oil and warm in a 200°C oven until crisp at the edges.
  4. Spread the cheese in a shallow bowl, pour over the thyme honey and finish with salt flakes. Serve with the bread.

Wiener schnitzel fingers with lemon and capers

Werner's wiener schnitzel lickin' finger. Crumbed veal fingers, lemon, brown butter capers.

Prep 17 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Crumb the veal up to 6 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g veal schnitzel, cut in fingers
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g fine dry breadcrumbs
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp baby capers, rinsed
  • 2 lemons, in wedges
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  1. Season the flour, beat the eggs and set out the breadcrumbs, 5 min.
  2. Dip each veal finger in flour, egg, then crumbs, pressing them on.
  3. Shallow-fry in hot oil in batches until golden, about 2 minutes a side, then drain.
  4. Brown the butter in a small pan, add the capers until they pop, then spoon over with parsley and lemon.

Gorlomi veal saltimbocca with sage and Marsala

Veal, prosciutto and sage, Marsala butter, soft polenta.

Prep 8 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Make the polenta up to an hour ahead and keep warm; build and fry the veal to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thin veal escalopes
  • 8 slices of prosciutto
  • 16 sage leaves
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 60g butter
  • 125ml dry Marsala
  • 125ml chicken stock
  • 200g instant polenta
  • 1 L milk and water, half and half
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  1. Bring the milk and water to the boil, whisk in the polenta and stir until thick, then beat in half the butter and the parmesan.
  2. Lay a slice of prosciutto and two sage leaves on each escalope and pin with a toothpick.
  3. Dust the veal with flour and fry, prosciutto side down first, in the oil and a little butter, 2 minutes a side.
  4. Lift the veal out, add the Marsala and stock to the pan and boil until syrupy, then swirl in the remaining butter.
  5. Spoon the polenta onto plates, top with the veal and pour over the pan sauce.

Basement tavern choucroute garnie

Smoked pork, sausages and sauerkraut, mustard and spuds.

Prep 8 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Braise the whole pot up to 2 days ahead; it is better reheated
Recipe for 4
  • 800g smoked pork hock or kassler
  • 4 thick smoked pork sausages
  • 150g speck, cut in batons
  • 1kg sauerkraut, rinsed and squeezed
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 2 Granny Smith apples, sliced
  • 8 juniper berries
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 250ml cloudy apple juice
  • 800g waxy potatoes
  • 4 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Fry the speck in a heavy pot until the fat runs, then soften the onion in it.
  2. Stir in the sauerkraut, apple, juniper and bay, then pour in the stock and apple juice.
  3. Bury the pork in the cabbage, cover and braise at 160°C for an hour.
  4. Add the sausages and whole potatoes and braise until the potatoes are tender.
  5. Pile onto a board, slice the pork and serve with plenty of mustard.

Landa's apple strudel with whipped creamVegetarian

Warm apple and raisin strudel, cold cream.

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the apple filling up to a day ahead; roll and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Granny Smith apples, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 60g raisins
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 8 sheets of filo pastry
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Toss the apples with the raisins, sugar, cinnamon, lemon zest and juice.
  2. Fry the breadcrumbs in 2 tbsp of the butter until golden.
  3. Layer the filo, brushing each sheet with butter, then scatter the crumbs along one long edge and pile the apples on top.
  4. Roll up tightly, tuck in the ends, brush with butter and bake at 190°C until deep golden.
  5. Whip the cream to soft peaks, dust the strudel with icing sugar and serve it warm with the cold cream, 5 min.

Hellstrom's Scotch and soda

Single malt, cold soda, lemon peel. No more German than that Scotch.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the soda and glasses well; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml single malt Scotch whisky
  • 500ml soda water, well chilled
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Fill 4 tumblers with ice and pour 60ml of Scotch into each.
  2. Top with cold soda, twist a strip of lemon peel over each and drop it in.
