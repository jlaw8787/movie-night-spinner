Snacks

Ash's runaway hand piesVegetarian

Potato, leek, cheddar and thyme in crimped half moons, baked golden, sealed so they can't crawl.

Prep 8 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Fill and crimp up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled, or freeze for a month; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 sheets frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 400g potatoes, cut into 1cm dice
  • 1 leek, thinly sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 120g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Boil the diced potato until just tender and drain well.
  2. Soften the leek in the butter.
  3. Mix the potato, leek, cheddar, thyme, salt and pepper and leave to cool.
  4. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 8 rounds of 12cm from the pastry, spoon in the filling, fold into half moons, crimp the edges and brush with egg.
  5. Bake on a lined tray until deep golden.

Jake and Bobby Joe's hush puppiesVegetarian

Cornmeal and buttermilk batter with spring onion, deep fried golden, honey butter, backwoods style.

Prep 15 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry ingredients and the honey butter up to 2 days ahead; stir the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 egg
  • 180ml buttermilk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
  2. Whisk the egg and buttermilk, stir into the dry mix with the spring onion and rest.
  3. Beat the butter with the honey.
  4. Heat the oil in a deep pot to 180°C.
  5. Drop in heaped tablespoons of batter in batches and fry for 3 to 4 minutes, turning, until golden. Drain on paper towel.
  6. Serve hot with the honey butter.

Mains

Henrietta's fruit cellar pork chops

Seared, with golden apples, onion and sage in a mustard pan sauce, crushed potatoes.

Prep 4 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Best cooked on the night; boil the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead and slice the onion in the morning
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 40g butter
  • 4 thick pork loin chops
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 red apples, cored and cut into wedges
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 8 sage leaves
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and crush lightly with a fork and half the butter.
  2. Season the chops with the salt and pepper and sear in the oil for 4 minutes a side until just cooked. Rest on a plate.
  3. Fry the apples, onion and sage in the rest of the butter until golden.
  4. Add 250ml chicken stock and the mustard, bubble to reduce by half, then stir in the cream.
  5. Return the chops and their juices to warm through and serve with the potatoes.

Annie Knowby's apple-braised chicken with root vegetables

Chicken braised with apples, carrots and parsnips. Annie turns up with the missing pages.

Prep 6 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Braise up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 2 onions, in wedges
  • 3 carrots, in chunks
  • 2 parsnips, in chunks
  • 2 apples, in wedges
  • 300ml apple juice
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Brown the chicken skin side down in the oil in a heavy pot, then set aside.
  2. Add the onion, carrot and parsnip and colour lightly.
  3. Add the apple, apple juice, stock and thyme, put the chicken back on top and simmer covered.
  4. Uncover and simmer until the sauce reduces. Serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

Knowby cabin pecan pieVegetarian

Buttery pecan pie, baked late and finished before sunrise in the Knowby cabin.

Prep 13 min, cook 80 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep covered at room temperature; whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 200g pecans
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 125ml maple syrup
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Line a 23cm pie tin with the pastry and chill.
  2. Line with baking paper and rice and blind bake, then lift out the paper and rice.
  3. Whisk the eggs, brown sugar, maple syrup, butter, vanilla and salt.
  4. Scatter the pecans in the pastry case and pour over the filling.
  5. Bake until set with a slight wobble in the centre.
  6. Cool completely.
  7. Whip the cream to soft peaks and serve with slices of pie.

Drinks

Laughing room peach iced teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled black tea with poached fruit syrup and lemon, over ice with mint.

Prep 4 min, cook 16 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brew and chill the tea and peach syrup up to 2 days ahead; add ice, peach slices and mint to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 3 ripe peaches, sliced
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 600ml cold water
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 5 minutes and remove.
  2. Simmer half the peach slices with the sugar and 100ml of the cold water until soft, then strain to a syrup.
  3. Stir the syrup, lemon juice and the rest of the cold water into the tea and chill.
  4. Pour over ice with the remaining peach slices and a sprig of mint.
