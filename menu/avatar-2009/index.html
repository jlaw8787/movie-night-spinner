Snacks

Glowing forest fruit skewersVegetarian

Dragon fruit, kiwi, pineapple and blueberries with lime and honey.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Cut the fruit and thread the skewers up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; drizzle with lime and honey to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 dragon fruit
  • 2 kiwifruits
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Cut the dragon fruit, kiwifruit and pineapple into chunks.
  2. Thread onto skewers with the blueberries.
  3. Drizzle with lime juice and honey.
  4. Serve on a dark board under low light.

Hallelujah Mountains bao

Soy ginger braised pork belly in steamed buns with pickled cucumber and peanuts.

Prep 22 min, cook 2 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Pork up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly
  • 125ml soy sauce
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 2 star anise
  • 12 bao buns
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 50g peanuts, crushed
  1. Put the pork belly in a pot with soy, ginger, star anise and water.
  2. Simmer until tender, 2 hr.
  3. Slice the pork and pickle the cucumber in a little vinegar and salt.
  4. Steam the bao buns, 10 min.
  5. Fill with pork, pickled cucumber, coriander and crushed peanuts.

Lemongrass fish in banana leaf

White fish in chilli, garlic and lime paste, grilled in banana leaves, opened for the clan, nam jim.

How it is made
  • 600g ling fillets
  • 2 lemongrass stalks
  • 3 red chillies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bottle fish sauce
  • 1 packet banana leaves
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  1. Firm white fish rubbed with a paste of lemongrass, chilli, garlic and lime, wrapped in banana leaves and grilled, unwrapped at the table with nam jim. Blitz 2 lemongrass stalks, 2 red chillies, 3 garlic cloves, a knob of ginger, a handful of coriander, the zest of a lime and 1 tbsp fish sauce to a paste. Cut 600g ling fillets into 8 pieces and coat in the paste. Wrap each in a square of softened banana leaf and pin with a toothpick. Grill over medium heat for 10 minutes. Mix lime juice, fish sauce, sugar and chilli for nam jim.

Mains

Hometree hunter's spit-roast

Lamb shoulder roasted 5 hours for the clan.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 hr, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; roast on the day, or roast up to 2 days ahead and reheat the shredded lamb in its juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg lamb shoulder
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 2 sprigs of rosemary
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 6 flatbreads
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Rub the lamb with crushed garlic, rosemary and paprika.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C until it pulls apart, 5 hr.
  3. Rest and shred the lamb, 10 min.
  4. Toss mint and parsley into a salad.
  5. Serve with warm flatbreads and the salad, 4 min.

Ikran flight prawn and pumpkin curry

King prawns simmered in coconut milk with green paste, lime leaves and Thai basil, jasmine rice.

How it is made
  • 1 jar green curry paste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1 packet kaffir lime leaves
  • 500g pumpkin
  • 600g green king prawns
  • 1 bottle fish sauce
  • 1 packet palm sugar
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. King prawns and pumpkin simmered in coconut milk with green curry paste, lime leaves and fish sauce, Thai basil folded through, with jasmine rice. Fry 3 tbsp green curry paste in 2 tbsp coconut cream for 2 minutes until fragrant. Add 400ml coconut milk, 200ml water, 4 torn lime leaves and 500g pumpkin in 2cm chunks and simmer 12 minutes until tender. Add 600g peeled green king prawns and simmer 3 minutes. Season with 2 tbsp fish sauce and 1 tsp palm sugar, then fold through Thai basil. Serve with 300g steamed jasmine rice.

Sweets

Tree of Souls puddingVegetarian

Tapioca pearls like the seeds of the sacred tree, with coconut, mango and passionfruit.

Prep 6 min, cook 18 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g tapioca pearls
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 400ml water
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 mangoes, diced
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 30g shredded coconut, toasted
  1. Simmer the tapioca in coconut milk, water and sugar until clear, 15 min.
  2. Chill, 2 hr.
  3. Layer in glasses with mango and passionfruit pulp.
  4. Top with toasted coconut.

Drinks

Woodsprite blue lagoon

Coconut water and pineapple juice over ice, blue curaçao added for adults.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the coconut water and pineapple juice up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice and curacao to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml coconut water
  • 600ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml blue curaçao
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in coconut water and pineapple juice.
  3. Add a splash of blue curacao for the adults.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge.
