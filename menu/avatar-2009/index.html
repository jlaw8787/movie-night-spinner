Snacks

Glowing forest fruit skewersVegetarian

Dragon fruit, kiwi, pineapple and blueberries with lime and honey

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cut the fruit and thread the skewers up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; drizzle with lime and honey to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 dragon fruit
  • 2 kiwifruit
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Cut the dragon fruit, kiwifruit and pineapple into chunks.
  2. Thread onto skewers with the blueberries.
  3. Drizzle with lime juice and honey.
  4. Serve on a dark board under low light.

Hallelujah Mountains bao

Soy ginger braised pork belly in steamed buns with pickled cucumber and peanuts

Prep 30 min, cook 122 min · Make ahead: Pork up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly
  • 125ml soy sauce
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 2 star anise
  • 12 bao buns
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 50g peanuts, crushed
  1. Put the pork belly in a pot with soy, ginger, star anise and water.
  2. Simmer until tender.
  3. Slice the pork and pickle the cucumber in a little vinegar and salt.
  4. Steam the bao buns.
  5. Fill with pork, pickled cucumber, coriander and crushed peanuts.

Mains

Hometree hunter's spit-roast

Lamb shoulder roasted 5 hours for the clan, before Hometree falls

Prep 20 min, cook 310 min · Make ahead: Rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; roast on the day, or roast up to 2 days ahead and reheat the shredded lamb in its juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg lamb shoulder
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 6 flatbreads
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Rub the lamb with crushed garlic, rosemary and paprika.
  2. Roast covered at 150C until it pulls apart.
  3. Rest and shred the lamb.
  4. Toss mint and parsley into a salad.
  5. Serve with warm flatbreads and the salad.

Sweets

Tree of Souls puddingVegetarian

Tapioca pearls like the seeds of the sacred tree, with coconut, mango and passionfruit

Prep 20 min, cook 124 min · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g tapioca pearls
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 400ml water
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 mangoes, diced
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 30g shredded coconut, toasted
  1. Simmer the tapioca in coconut milk, water and sugar until clear.
  2. Chill.
  3. Layer in glasses with mango and passionfruit pulp.
  4. Top with toasted coconut.

Drinks

Woodsprite blue lagoonVegetarian

Coconut water and pineapple juice over ice, blue curaçao added for adults

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the coconut water and pineapple juice up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice and curacao to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml coconut water
  • 600ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml blue curaçao
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in coconut water and pineapple juice.
  3. Add a splash of blue curacao for the adults.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge.
