Mains

Three Broomsticks beef stew

Beef chuck braised 3 hours in dark stock with carrots, crusty bread.

Prep 28 min, cook 3 hr · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 775ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 loaf of crusty bread
  • 50g butter
  1. Brown the beef in the vegetable oil in batches, 15 min.
  2. Soften the onions and carrots with the thyme.
  3. Return the beef, add the stock and Worcestershire, cover and braise at 160°C, 3 hr.
  4. Serve with crusty bread and butter.

Fireside ham and cheese toasties

Ham and gruyère griddled in butter, tomato soup to dunk, by the fire.

How it is made
  • 1kg tomatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 100ml cream
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 200g sliced ham
  • 200g gruyère
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Thick-cut ham and gruyère toasties with mustard, griddled in butter until crisp, with a mug of creamy roast tomato soup for dunking. Roast 1kg halved tomatoes, 1 onion and 4 garlic cloves with oil at 200°C for 30 minutes, then blend with 500ml vegetable stock and 100ml cream and season. Butter one side of 8 slices of thick white bread. Spread 4 unbuttered sides with Dijon and layer 200g sliced ham and 200g grated gruyère, then close. Cook in a pan over medium heat for 4 minutes a side until golden and melted. Serve with the soup in mugs.

Sweets

Mischief managed fudgeVegetarian

Soft condensed milk fudge, cut into squares and wrapped in paper.

Prep 8 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 150ml milk
  • 450g brown sugar
  • 115g butter
  1. Line a 20cm tin.
  2. Melt the condensed milk, milk, sugar and butter together, 7 min.
  3. Boil to 115°C, stirring all the time, 15 min.
  4. Off the heat, beat until thick, 5 min.
  5. Pour into the tin, cool and cut, 5 min.

Drinks

Three Broomsticks butterbeerNo alcohol

Butterscotch cream soda under a thick cream foam, served in tankards.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.3 L cream soda
  • 4 tbsp butterscotch sauce
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whip the cream with the icing sugar to soft, floppy peaks.
  2. Stir a tablespoon of butterscotch sauce into each tankard and pour in the cold cream soda.
  3. Spoon the cream foam on top and serve straight away.
