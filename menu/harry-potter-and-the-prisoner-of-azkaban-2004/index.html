Snacks

Lupin's chocolateVegetarian

Crunchy honeycomb broken into slabs and half dipped in dark chocolate.

Prep 3 min, cook 17 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  • 2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 100g dark chocolate, melted
  • 100g milk chocolate, broken
  1. Line a tin with baking paper.
  2. Boil the sugar and golden syrup to amber, then whisk in the bicarbonate of soda and pour into the tin.
  3. Leave to set, then break into slabs.
  4. Dip half the slabs in melted dark chocolate.
  5. Serve with the milk chocolate in rough pieces.

One-Eyed Witch coconut ice

Condensed milk and coconut in white and pink layers, cut chunky, from the Honeydukes jar.

How it is made
  • 1 tin sweetened condensed milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 250g desiccated coconut
  • 1 bottle pink food colouring
  • 1 bottle raspberry essence
  1. Old-fashioned coconut ice in two layers, snowy white and raspberry pink, set firm, cut into chunky squares and piled into a glass sweet jar. Line a 20cm square tin. Mix 395g sweetened condensed milk with 250g icing sugar, then work in 250g desiccated coconut until stiff. Press half into the tin. Tint the rest pink with a few drops of colouring and 1 tsp raspberry essence, then press on top. Chill for 4 hours until firm, then cut into 3cm squares and pile into a sweet jar.

Mains

Leaky Cauldron stew

Beef chuck braised 3 hours in dark stock with carrots, crusty bread.

Prep 28 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 775ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 loaf of crusty bread
  • 50g butter
  1. Brown the beef in the vegetable oil in batches.
  2. Soften the onions and carrots with the thyme.
  3. Return the beef, add the stock and Worcestershire, cover and braise at 160°C.
  4. Serve with crusty bread and butter.

Knight Bus ham and cheese toasties

Ham and gruyère griddled in butter, tomato soup to dunk, a ride with Stan and Ernie.

How it is made
  • 1kg tomatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 100ml cream
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 200g sliced ham
  • 200g gruyère
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Thick-cut ham and gruyère toasties with mustard, griddled in butter until crisp, with a mug of creamy roast tomato soup for dunking. Roast 1kg halved tomatoes, 1 onion and 4 garlic cloves with oil at 200°C for 30 minutes, then blend with 500ml vegetable stock and 100ml cream and season. Butter one side of 8 slices of thick white bread. Spread 4 unbuttered sides with Dijon and layer 200g sliced ham and 200g grated gruyère, then close. Cook in a pan over medium heat for 4 minutes a side until golden and melted. Serve with the soup in mugs.

Tea leaf reading biscuitsVegetarian

Slice and bake Earl Grey and lemon cookies.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Roll and chill the log up to 3 days ahead, or freeze up to 1 month; slice and bake on the day. Baked biscuits keep up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tsp Earl Grey tea leaves
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 280g plain flour
  1. Grind the tea leaves finely.
  2. Beat the butter, icing sugar, lemon zest and tea until creamy, then mix in the flour.
  3. Roll into a log and chill.
  4. Heat the oven to 160°C. Slice and bake until pale golden.
  5. Cool on the tray before eating.

Mischief managed fudgeVegetarian

Soft condensed milk fudge, cut into squares and wrapped in paper.

Prep 8 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 150ml milk
  • 450g brown sugar
  • 115g butter
  1. Line a 20cm tin.
  2. Melt the condensed milk, milk, sugar and butter together.
  3. Boil to 115°C, stirring all the time.
  4. Off the heat, beat until thick.
  5. Pour into the tin, cool and cut.

Drinks

Pumpkin juiceNo alcohol

Butternut and apple juice with honey and ginger, served chilled.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butternut pumpkin, cubed
  • 750ml apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Roast the pumpkin at 200°C until soft and cool.
  2. Blend with the juice, honey, cinnamon and ginger until smooth.
  3. Strain, chill and serve in glasses over ice.

When to serve

Serve the chocolate when Professor Lupin hands Harry a piece after the Dementors.
