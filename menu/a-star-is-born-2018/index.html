Car park frozen pea smash on toastVegetarian

The bag of frozen peas for a sore hand in the supermarket car park

Prep 8 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Smash the peas up to 4 hours ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; toast and build just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 bunch mint, leaves picked
  • 1 tsp salt and pepper, to taste
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 1 clove of garlic, halved
  • 200g fresh ricotta
  • 1/2 tsp dried chilli flakes
  • 30g parmesan, shaved
  1. Cook the peas in boiling salted water for 3 minutes and drain.
  2. Smash roughly with 2 tbsp of the oil, the lemon zest and juice, most of the mint and salt and pepper.
  3. Toast the sourdough, rub with the cut garlic and drizzle with the rest of the oil.
  4. Spread with ricotta, pile on the peas and finish with chilli flakes, parmesan and the rest of the mint.

Lorenzo's kitchen table aranciniVegetarian

Crisp fried rice balls with melting mozzarella, warm tomato sauce to dunk, Sinatra on the stereo

Prep 50 min, cook 15 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and chill the risotto up to 2 days ahead, or roll and crumb the balls up to 1 day ahead; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 small brown onion, finely chopped
  • 30g butter
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1l hot vegetable stock
  • 50g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas
  • 125g mozzarella, in 16 cubes
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 400g passata
  • 4 basil leaves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Soften the onion in the butter, stir in the rice, then add the stock a ladle at a time until thick and creamy.
  2. Stir in the parmesan and peas, spread on a tray and chill until firm.
  3. Roll into 16 balls around a cube of mozzarella, roll in flour, dip in egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  4. Deep-fry at 180C in batches until deep golden, and drain.
  5. Warm the passata with the salt and basil and serve with the arancini.

Lorenzo's limo crew meatballs and spaghetti

Dinner for the drivers who crowd into Ally's dad's house

Prep 5 min, cook 72 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep them together in the fridge; cook the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef and pork mince
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves of garlic, 1 grated for the meatballs, 3 sliced for the sauce
  • 2 700g bottles passata
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 tsp dried chilli flakes
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 60g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 egg
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 60ml milk
  • 1/2 bunch flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix with the mince, egg, grated garlic, half the parmesan, parsley and 1 tsp of the salt. Roll into 16 balls.
  2. Bake on an oiled tray at 200C for 15 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile soften the onion and sliced garlic in the oil, add the passata, chilli, basil stalks and the rest of the salt and simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Slip the meatballs into the sauce and simmer gently.
  5. Cook the spaghetti, drain and toss with some of the sauce, then serve with the meatballs, torn basil and the rest of the parmesan.

Catering shift cannoliVegetarian

Something sweet for Ally's long waitressing shifts

Prep 13 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Drain the ricotta and make the filling up to 1 day ahead; fill the shells just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 500g firm ricotta
  • 100g icing sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 50g dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 12 cannoli shells
  • 40g pistachios, chopped
  1. Drain the ricotta in a sieve in the fridge.
  2. Beat with 80g of the icing sugar, the vanilla and orange zest until smooth, then fold in the chocolate.
  3. Spoon into a piping bag and chill.
  4. Pipe into the shells from both ends, dip the ends in pistachios and dust with the rest of the icing sugar.

Drag night cosmopolitanVegetarian

Ordered at the bar while the queens hold the stage

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lime up to 1 day ahead and chill everything; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 240ml cranberry juice
  • 3 limes, juiced
  • 1 orange, peel only, for twists
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Chill 4 coupe or martini glasses.
  2. Shake the vodka, orange liqueur, cranberry juice and lime juice hard with the ice.
  3. Strain into the glasses and twist an orange peel over the top.
