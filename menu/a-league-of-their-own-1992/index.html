Snacks

Oregon dairy grilled cheese bitesVegetarian

Sharp cheddar grilled sandwiches with tomato relish.

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Build the sandwiches up to 4 hours ahead and chill; toast to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 125ml tomato relish
  1. Spread the bread with mustard and pile the cheddar on half the slices, then close.
  2. Butter the outsides and toast in a pan over medium heat until golden and melting.
  3. Cut into fingers and serve with the relish.

Betty Spaghetti meatball subs

Pork and beef meatballs in red sauce on soft rolls, for Betty Spaghetti.

Prep 23 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead; reheat and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 40g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700ml tomato passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 soft slider rolls
  • 4 slices of provolone
  1. Mix both minces with the breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, half the garlic and salt, and roll into 16 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil, then add the passata, oregano and remaining garlic, 8 min.
  3. Simmer gently until the meatballs are cooked through and the sauce thickens.
  4. Tuck two meatballs and sauce into each roll with half a slice of provolone.

Mains

Splits catch ballpark hot dog plate

Beef franks, slow onions, mustard and relish. Dottie catches a pop fly in the splits.

Prep 3 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  1. Cook the onions slowly in the butter with a pinch of salt until soft and golden.
  2. Grill the franks, turning, until blistered and hot through.
  3. Toast the rolls in the pan, 4 min.
  4. Fill and top with onions, mustard and relish.

Roadhouse night pulled pork rolls

Smoky pulled pork, apple slaw and pickles.

Prep 20 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Cook the pork up to 2 days ahead and chill in its juices; reheat and pull to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder, boneless
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml apple juice
  • 125ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 8 soft white rolls
  • 1/4 green cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 green apple, cut in matchsticks
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 16 dill pickle slices
  1. Rub the pork with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and salt and sit it in a lidded pot with the apple juice.
  2. Cover and cook at 160°C until it pulls apart easily.
  3. Shred the pork with two forks and stir through the barbecue sauce and some pan juices.
  4. Toss the cabbage and apple with the mayo, then fill the rolls with pork, slaw and pickles.

No crying in baseball candy bar sliceVegetarian

Chocolate, salted peanut and caramel slice. Rockford Peaches fuel.

Prep 8 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Make up to 4 days ahead and keep in the fridge; slice cold
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 395g tin sweetened condensed milk
  • 60g golden syrup
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 120g salted roasted peanuts
  • 200g milk chocolate
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  1. Mix the biscuit crumbs with half the butter and press into a lined slice tin.
  2. Stir the condensed milk, golden syrup, sugar and remaining butter over low heat until thick and golden.
  3. Stir in the peanuts, pour over the base and bake at 170°C for 10 minutes, then cool.
  4. Melt the chocolate with the oil, pour over and chill until set, then slice.
