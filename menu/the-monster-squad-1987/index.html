Garlic pizza scrolls with three cheesesVegetarian

Garlic pizza straight to a vampire's face. Roast garlic butter and three melting cheeses.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roast the garlic and roll the scrolls up to a day ahead; chill and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 whole garlic bulb
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1/2 cup flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  1. Cut the top off the garlic bulb, drizzle with oil, wrap in foil and roast at 200°C until soft.
  2. Squeeze the garlic out and mash it with the butter and parsley.
  3. Spread each pastry sheet with tomato paste, then the garlic butter, oregano and cheeses.
  4. Roll up tightly, cut into 2cm rounds and lay them flat on a lined tray.
  5. Bake at 200°C until puffed, golden and bubbling.

Black Lagoon fish bites with tartare

Crisp cod with tartare and lemon.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare up to 2 days ahead; crumb and fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cod fillets, cut in bite-size pieces
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 tbsp gherkins, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, capers, gherkins and a squeeze of lemon into a tartare.
  2. Dip the fish in seasoned flour, beaten egg, then panko.
  3. Shallow-fry in batches in hot oil until golden and just cooked.
  4. Drain and serve with the tartare and lemon wedges.

Wolf Man meatball subs with provolone

Beef meatballs in red sauce under provolone.

Prep 21 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs and make the sauce up to 2 days ahead; simmer together and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g dried breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 700g bottle tomato passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long soft sub rolls
  • 8 slices of provolone
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  1. Mix the mince, egg, breadcrumbs, parmesan and half the garlic, then roll into 16 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil and lift them out.
  3. Soften the onion and the rest of the garlic, add the passata and oregano, return the meatballs and simmer until cooked through.
  4. Split the rolls, fill with meatballs and sauce, lay over the provolone and grill until melted.
  5. Tear over the basil and serve.

Scary German Guy's bratwurst and kraut

Grilled bratwurst with kraut and hot mustard.

Prep 5 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Warm the sauerkraut with the onion up to a day ahead and reheat; grill the bratwurst to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 pork bratwurst
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 500g jar sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 8 crusty bread rolls
  • 4 tbsp hot German mustard
  1. Cook the onion slowly in the butter until soft, then add the sauerkraut and caraway and warm through.
  2. Grill the bratwurst over medium heat, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  3. Split the rolls, add the kraut and bratwurst and finish with mustard.

Last-chance-for-more apple pie with creamVegetarian

Cinnamon apple pie with thick cream. Scary German Guy serves the boys pie.

Prep 8 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to a day ahead; warm through and serve with the cream
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 6 granny smith apples, peeled and sliced
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 300ml thick cream
  1. Cook the apples with the sugar, cinnamon, lemon juice and butter until just tender, then stir in the cornflour and cool.
  2. Line a 23cm pie dish with one pastry sheet and fill with the apples.
  3. Lay over the second sheet, crimp the edges, cut steam holes and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until deep golden and bubbling, then rest 15 minutes.
  5. Cut big wedges and serve with thick cream.
