Snacks

Jersey gravy fries with melted cheese

Fries under mozzarella and hot beef gravy, Jersey diner style.

Prep 4 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 3 days ahead in the fridge and reheat; bake the fries and melt the cheese on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen crinkle-cut fries
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • Worcestershire sauce, to taste
  • 150g mozzarella
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Bake the frozen crinkle-cut fries at 220°C for 25 minutes until crisp.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then whisk in the beef stock and the Worcestershire and simmer 5 minutes until thick.
  3. Pile the fries onto a tray, scatter with the grated mozzarella and grill 2 minutes until melted.
  4. Pour over the hot gravy and top with pepper and sliced spring onion.

Freakshow fried pickle spearsVegetarian

Dill pickle spears in a polenta crumb, fried, with herb sour cream.

Prep 20 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; make the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 16 dill pickle spears
  • 1 egg
  • 185ml buttermilk
  • 75g plain flour
  • 75g fine polenta
  • smoked paprika, to taste
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 100g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 garlic clove
  1. Pat the dill pickle spears very dry.
  2. Whisk the egg and the buttermilk.
  3. Mix the plain flour, the fine polenta, the smoked paprika and salt.
  4. Dip the spears in buttermilk, then the flour mix.
  5. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and drain.
  6. Mix the sour cream, the buttermilk, the mayonnaise, chopped dill, chives and a crushed garlic clove for dipping.

Mains

Cheetah ride steamed onion sliders

Thin beef patties steamed over itty-bitty onions, cheddar, pickles.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Dice the onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and press and cut the patties up to 4 hours ahead; steam and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 8 slices of cheddar
  • 16 slider buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Press the beef mince thin across a lined tray and cut into squares.
  2. Spread the finely diced onions in a hot pan with a splash of water, lay the patties on top, cover and steam 3 minutes.
  3. Add the cheddar and serve in the small soft buns with pickles.

Crispy fish sliders with tartare

Breaded fish in soft little buns with tartare, on the side of Harold's thirty sliders.

Prep 9 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g white fish fillets, cut into squares
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100ml vegetable oil
  • 12 slider buns
  • 6 slices of cheddar, halved
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp capers and gherkins, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, capers, gherkins and a squeeze of lemon for the tartare.
  2. Dust the fish in flour, dip in beaten egg and coat in panko.
  3. Shallow fry until golden and cooked through.
  4. Fill the buns with lettuce, fish, cheese and tartare.

Doogie's caramel malt shakesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, malt powder and caramel with whipped cream.

Prep 2 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make the caramel up to 1 week ahead and keep it in the fridge; blend the shakes to order
Recipe for 4
  • 100g sugar
  • 180ml thickened cream
  • 20g butter
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml milk
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Make caramel: melt the sugar until amber, whisk in the cream and the butter, then a little salt, and cool.
  2. Drizzle some inside the tall glasses.
  3. Blend the vanilla ice cream with the cold milk, the malted milk powder and the caramel until thick.
  4. Pour into the glasses, top with whipped cream and a drizzle more caramel.

Drinks

Harold's large cherry vanilla colaNo alcohol

Cherry vanilla syrup and cola over ice, one of his four.

Prep 7 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Simmer and strain the cherry syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep it chilled; build the glasses with ice and cola on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g frozen pitted cherries
  • 80g sugar
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 2 limes
  • 1 L cola
  • 3 cups crushed ice
  • 80ml water
  1. Simmer the frozen pitted cherries with the sugar and the water for 10 minutes, mashing them, then strain and stir in the vanilla.
  2. Cool.
  3. Fill the tall glasses with crushed ice.
  4. Add the cherry syrup and a squeeze of lime to each, then top with the cold cola.
  5. Stir gently and add a cherry and a lime wedge.
