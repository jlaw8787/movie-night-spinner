Snacks

Café olives and almondsVegetarian

Warm olives with preserved lemon and cumin, plus roasted almonds.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 300g green olives
  • 60ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 preserved lemon
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 150g almonds
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • salt, to taste
  1. Chop the preserved lemon rind.
  2. Warm the olives in the oil with the lemon rind, cumin and chilli, 5 min.
  3. Toss the almonds with oil and salt and roast at 180°C, 8 min.
  4. Serve both warm in small bowls, 4 min.

Letters of transit beef briouats

Crisp filo parcels of cinnamon and cumin spiced beef.

Prep 12 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 handful coriander, chopped
  • 8 sheets of filo pastry
  • 80g butter, melted
  • salt, to taste
  1. Fry the mince with the onion, cinnamon, cumin and salt until browned, then stir in the coriander. Cool, 20 min.
  2. Cut the filo into long strips and brush with melted butter.
  3. Add a spoonful of filling to each strip and fold into triangles.
  4. Bake at 190°C until crisp, 15 min.

Mains

Captain Renault's chicken tagine

Chicken with saffron, preserved lemon and green olives, couscous.

Prep 18 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 onions
  • 1 knob ginger, grated
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 375ml water
  • 1/2 preserved lemon
  • 150g green olives
  • 300g couscous
  • 375ml chicken stock
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil, 10 min.
  2. Soften the sliced onions with the ginger and saffron.
  3. Return the chicken with the water, chopped preserved lemon rind and olives and simmer, 40 min.
  4. Pour the couscous into the boiling stock, cover for a few minutes and fluff.

Lamb and apricot tagine with couscous

Lamb and apricot slow cooked with cinnamon, served over couscous.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg diced lamb shoulder
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 2 tbsp ras el hanout
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 800ml chicken stock
  • 150g dried apricots
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 300g couscous
  • 30g butter
  • 50g flaked almonds
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Lamb shoulder braised slowly with ras el hanout, cinnamon, dried apricots and honey, scattered with toasted almonds and coriander, over buttered couscous. Brown 1.2kg diced lamb shoulder in oil in batches. Fry 2 sliced onions for 5 minutes, add 3 garlic cloves, 2 tsp grated ginger, 2 tbsp ras el hanout and 1 cinnamon stick. Pour in 400g tinned tomatoes and 500ml stock, return the lamb, cover and simmer 1.5 hours. Add 150g dried apricots and 1 tbsp honey and cook 30 minutes more. Pour 300ml boiling stock over 300g couscous with 30g butter, cover 5 minutes, fluff. Top the tagine with 50g toasted flaked almonds and coriander.

Orange blossom almond cakeVegetarian

Moist almond cake soaked in orange syrup, a hint of orange blossom.

Prep 2 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar, for the cake
  • 250g almond meal
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 80g caster sugar, for the syrup
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  1. Beat the eggs and the cake sugar until pale, then fold in the almond meal, orange zest and baking powder.
  2. Bake in a 20cm tin at 170°C, 40 min.
  3. Simmer the orange juice with the syrup sugar and orange blossom water, 6 min.
  4. Pour over the warm cake and serve with Greek yoghurt.
