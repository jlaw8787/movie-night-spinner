Catalina Wine Mixer canapés

Prawn on cucumber, honey ricotta toasts, figs wrapped in prosciutto

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 200g cooked prawns
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 baguette
  • 150g ricotta
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 6 figs
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Slice the cucumber into rounds and top with prawns bound in lemon mayonnaise.
  2. Toast baguette slices and top with ricotta whipped with honey.
  3. Halve the figs and wrap each half in prosciutto.
  4. Serve on silver trays.

Bunk bed parmesan garlic butter popcornVegetarian

Did we just become best friends? Garlic butter and parmesan.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best popped just before serving; grate the parmesan and chop the parsley up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g popping corn
  • 60g butter
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Heat the oil in a large pot with a lid, add the corn, cover and shake over medium heat until the popping slows.
  2. Melt the butter with the crushed garlic.
  3. Toss the popcorn with the garlic butter, finely grated parmesan, pepper, salt and chopped parsley. Split between two big bowls.

Prestige Worldwide smash slidersOn the printed menu

We put Liquid Paper on a bee. And it died.

How it is made
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 600g chuck mince
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 12 slider rolls
  1. Little chuck patties smashed thin and crisp on a hot griddle, cheddar melted on, with buttery onions, pickles and burger sauce in soft slider rolls. Cook 2 sliced onions in 30g butter over low heat for 20 minutes. Roll 600g chuck mince into 12 balls. Mix 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tsp mustard and 1 tbsp chopped pickles. Heat a griddle until smoking, smash the balls thin, season and cook 90 seconds, flip and add a piece of cheddar. Fill 12 toasted slider rolls with patty, onions, pickles and sauce.

Robert's sailboat lemon caper snapper

Snapper roasted with lemon, garlic and capers, herb buttered new potatoes

Prep 13 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and whisk the dressing up to 2 days ahead; roast the fish and toss the salad on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 6 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and toss with the butter, parsley and dill.
  2. Lay the snapper in a dish with lemon slices, sliced garlic, capers and some of the oil.
  3. Roast at 200C until the fish flakes.
  4. Whisk the Dijon, vinegar and remaining oil and toss through the leaves.
  5. Serve the fish with the potatoes and salad.

Fancy sauce glazed meatloafOn the printed menu

First dinner, fancy sauce guarded by Brennan

How it is made
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 200ml milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 300g green beans
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with grated onion and milk-soaked crumbs, glazed sticky with tomato and brown sugar, with buttery mash and green beans. Soak 80g breadcrumbs in 100ml milk. Mix lightly with 500g beef mince, 250g pork mince, a grated onion, 1 egg, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and salt. Press into a loaf tin. Mix 80ml tomato sauce with 2 tbsp brown sugar and spread on top. Bake at 180C for 1 hour and rest 10 minutes. Mash 1kg potatoes with 60g butter and milk. Steam 300g green beans and toss with butter.

Vanilla and chocolate ice cream with hot fudge, whipped cream, sprinkles, cherries

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fudge sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 1l chocolate ice cream
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 200ml thickened cream for whipping
  • 1 jar sprinkles
  • 1 jar maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate into the cream gently for hot fudge.
  2. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  3. Put the ice cream tubs on ice and set out the fudge, whipped cream, sprinkles and cherries so everyone builds their own.

Drinks

Catalina Wine Mixer strawberry rosé spritzVegetarian

Dry rosé over pressed strawberries and elderflower, topped up and served over ice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the strawberries and squeeze the lemon up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; add ice, rose and soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 8 strawberries
  • 4 tbsp elderflower cordial
  • 1 lemon
  • 480ml dry rose
  • 240ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Slice the strawberries and divide between four large wine glasses.
  2. Add elderflower cordial and a squeeze of lemon to each and press lightly.
  3. Fill with ice, pour in the cold rose and top with soda water.
  4. Stir gently and add a lemon wheel.

When to serve

Serve the wine when Brennan runs the Catalina Wine Mixer and the two of them take the stage.
