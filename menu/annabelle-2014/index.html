Stovetop maple bacon brown butter popcorn

Bacon, maple and brown butter. Don't leave the pan on the stove, Mia.

Prep 3 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Best popped and tossed just before serving; crumble the bacon up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rashers streaky bacon
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 pinch flaky sea salt
  1. Fry the bacon until very crisp, then crumble.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat, add the kernels, cover and shake constantly until the popping slows. Pull it off straight away.
  3. Cook the butter in a small pan until golden brown and nutty, then stir in the maple syrup.
  4. Pour over the popcorn and toss with the bacon and a good pinch of salt.

1967 cocktail party bites

Devilled eggs, grape jelly glazed cocktail franks, celery filled with cream cheese.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Fill the eggs up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; simmer the franks and fill the celery on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 100ml barbecue sauce
  • 1 tbsp grape jelly
  • 3 celery sticks, cut into boats
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 8 olives, sliced
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool, peel and halve. Whip the yolks with the mayonnaise and paprika and pipe back in.
  2. Simmer the franks in the barbecue sauce and grape jelly until sticky.
  3. Fill the celery boats with cream cheese and sliced olives.

Sewing basket parmesan cheese straws

Cheddar puff twists, baked crisp. Like knitting needles.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 60g parmesan
  • 60g cheddar
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  1. Puff pastry twisted with parmesan, sharp cheddar, paprika and a little mustard, baked crisp and golden and stood up in a jar like knitting needles. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brush 2 thawed sheets of puff pastry with beaten egg and spread one lightly with Dijon mustard. Scatter 60g grated parmesan, 60g grated cheddar and 1 tsp paprika over the mustard sheet, lay the second sheet on top and press firmly. Cut into 1.5cm strips, twist each one and lay on lined trays. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until puffed and golden, then cool on a rack.

Evelyn's chicken and rice mushroom casserole

Chicken thighs baked over creamy mushroom rice.

Prep 0 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night, adding 10 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 250g long grain rice
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil and set aside.
  2. Soften the mushrooms in the same pan, then stir in the rice, stock, cream and thyme.
  3. Pour into a baking dish, lay the chicken on top and cover.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 50 minutes.

Pasadena apartment beef stroganoff

Fillet strips seared fast, sour cream and mushroom sauce, buttered noodles, for a fresh start.

How it is made
  • 700g scotch fillet
  • 1 brown onion
  • 50g butter
  • 300g button mushrooms
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 50ml brandy
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Strips of scotch fillet seared fast, finished in a sour cream, mushroom and paprika sauce with a dash of brandy, over buttered egg noodles with parsley. Slice 700g scotch fillet into thin strips and sear in batches in a very hot pan for 1 minute, then set aside. Soften 1 sliced onion in 30g butter, add 300g sliced mushrooms and cook until golden. Add 2 tsp sweet paprika and 2 tbsp brandy, then 250ml beef stock and 1 tbsp Dijon and simmer 5 minutes. Stir in 200ml sour cream and the beef and warm through without boiling. Toss 400g egg noodles with butter and parsley and serve.

John's gift-box doll cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes piped with toasted meringue and a ribbon.

Prep 32 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes up to 2 days ahead, airtight; make the buttercream up to 2 days ahead in the fridge, beat again and pipe on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 325g butter, softened
  • 270g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean, seeds scraped
  • 4 eggs, 2 whole and 2 whites
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 4 tiny pink sugar roses
  1. Beat the first share of butter with the first share of sugar and the vanilla seeds, add the whole eggs and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a muffin tin at 170°C for 18 minutes. Cool.
  3. Whisk the egg whites with the rest of the sugar over hot water, then beat in the remaining butter to make buttercream.
  4. Pipe white buttercream onto each cupcake and add a tiny pink sugar rose.

1967 grasshopper shakes

Mint chip milkshakes, spiked with crème de menthe and crème de cacao for adults.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Grown-up version adds liqueur
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L mint choc chip ice cream
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 120ml crème de menthe, grown-ups only
  • 120ml crème de cacao, grown-ups only
  1. Blend the ice cream with the milk until thick.
  2. Pour into glasses. For the grown-ups, blend in the crème de menthe and crème de cacao.
