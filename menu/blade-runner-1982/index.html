Snacks

Noodle bar gyoza

Pan fried pork and cabbage gyoza with a soy and chilli oil dip

Prep 40 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 2 cup finely shredded cabbage
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 knob ginger, grated
  • 30 gyoza wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100ml water
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Salt the cabbage, let it sit, then squeeze dry.
  2. Mix with the pork, soy and ginger.
  3. Fill the wrappers and pleat the edges.
  4. Fry in oil until crisp underneath, then add water and steam with a lid.
  5. Mix soy, rice vinegar and chilli oil for dipping.

Origami crackersVegetarian

Rice paper triangles fried until puffed, dusted with shichimi togarashi

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 10 rice paper sheets
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 tsp shichimi togarashi
  1. Cut the rice paper into small triangles with scissors.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil to 180C and fry a few at a time for a few seconds until they puff and turn white.
  3. Drain and toss with flaky salt and shichimi togarashi.

Mains

Rain-soaked ramen

Braised pork belly ramen with soft-boiled egg, nori and spring onion

Prep 25 min, cook 140 min · Make ahead: Pork and eggs up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly
  • 125ml soy sauce
  • 125ml mirin
  • 125ml sake
  • 500ml water
  • 4 eggs
  • 400g ramen noodles
  • 1 1/2l chicken stock
  • 1 packet nori
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  1. Braise the pork belly in the soy, mirin, sake and water, covered, on a low simmer.
  2. Soft-boil the eggs, peel and marinate in some of the braising liquid.
  3. Slice the pork. Heat the stock and season with a little soy.
  4. Cook the noodles and divide between bowls.
  5. Top with pork, halved egg, nori and spring onion.

Sweets

Origami cookiesVegetarian

Thin crisp sesame cookies folded into shapes while still warm

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 egg whites
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 50g plain flour
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar, then stir in the flour, melted butter and sesame seeds.
  2. Spread thin circles on a lined tray.
  3. Bake until golden at the edges.
  4. Fold or twist into shapes while still hot and soft.

Drinks

Deckard's Black Label highballVegetarian

Scotch whisky and soda water over ice with a lemon peel twist

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the soda water up to 1 day ahead and cut the lemon peel up to 4 hours ahead; build the glasses just before serving to keep the fizz
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml Scotch whisky
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 bags ice
  1. Pack four tall glasses with ice, stir to chill, then pour off any water.
  2. Add the whisky to each and stir.
  3. Top slowly with very cold soda water down the spoon. Squeeze a strip of lemon peel over each.

When to serve

Serve the noodles when Deckard sits down at the street noodle bar in the rain.
