Noodle bar gyoza

Pan fried pork and cabbage gyoza with a soy and chilli oil dip.

Prep 43 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 2 cups finely shredded cabbage
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 knob ginger, grated
  • 30 gyoza wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100ml water
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Salt the cabbage, let it sit, then squeeze dry.
  2. Mix with the pork, soy and ginger.
  3. Fill the wrappers and pleat the edges.
  4. Fry in oil until crisp underneath, then add water and steam with a lid, 8 min.
  5. Mix soy, rice vinegar and chilli oil for dipping.

Origami crackersVegetarian

Rice paper triangles fried until puffed, dusted with shichimi togarashi.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 10 rice paper sheets
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 tsp shichimi togarashi
  1. Cut the rice paper into small triangles with scissors.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil to 180°C and fry a few at a time for a few seconds until they puff and turn white, 10 min.
  3. Drain and toss with flaky salt and shichimi togarashi.

Neon salmon and avocado inside-out rolls

Salmon and cucumber in tobiko and sesame-coated rice, soy and wasabi, more human than human.

How it is made
  • 400g sushi rice
  • 80ml rice vinegar
  • 2 nori sheets
  • 250g sashimi salmon
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 50g tobiko
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • soy sauce
  • 1 tube wasabi
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  1. Inside-out rolls of salmon, avocado and cucumber, coated in bright tobiko and toasted sesame, sliced and served with soy, wasabi and pickled ginger. Rinse 400g sushi rice, cook with 480ml water, then fold in 80ml rice vinegar mixed with 1 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp salt and cool. Lay half a nori sheet on a mat covered in cling film, spread with rice and flip so the rice is underneath. Lay strips of 250g sashimi salmon, avocado and cucumber along the middle and roll tight. Roll in 50g tobiko and sesame. Slice each roll into 8 with a wet knife.

Rain-soaked ramen

Braised pork belly ramen with soft-boiled egg, nori and scallion.

Prep 3 min, cook 2 hr 30 min · Make ahead: Pork and eggs up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly
  • 125ml soy sauce
  • 125ml mirin
  • 125ml sake
  • 500ml water
  • 4 eggs
  • 400g ramen noodles
  • 1.5 L chicken stock
  • 1 packet nori
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  1. Braise the pork belly in the soy, mirin, sake and water, covered, on a low simmer, 2 hr.
  2. Soft-boil the eggs, peel and marinate in some of the braising liquid, 15 min.
  3. Slice the pork. Heat the stock and season with a little soy, 10 min.
  4. Cook the noodles and divide between bowls, 5 min.
  5. Top with pork, halved egg, nori and spring onion.

Deckard's noodle bar shrimp tempura udon

Thick wheat noodles in hot dashi, soy and mirin, crisp battered shrimp, scallion, shichimi.

How it is made
  • 2 tsp dashi powder
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp mirin
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 180ml soda water
  • 12 green king prawns
  • 4 packets fresh udon
  • 2 spring onions
  • shichimi pepper
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Thick udon in a hot dashi broth seasoned with soy and mirin, topped with crisp prawn tempura, spring onion and a pinch of shichimi. Heat 1.6 litres water with 2 tsp dashi powder, 4 tbsp soy sauce and 3 tbsp mirin to a simmer. Whisk 100g plain flour, 1 tbsp cornflour and 180ml ice-cold soda water until just mixed. Dip 12 peeled green prawns in flour, then the batter, and fry at 180°C for 2 minutes until crisp. Cook 4 packets fresh udon for 2 minutes and divide between bowls. Ladle over the broth and top with prawns, spring onion and shichimi.

Sweets

Origami cookiesVegetarian

Thin crisp sesame cookies folded into shapes while still warm.

Prep 9 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 egg whites
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 50g plain flour
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar, then stir in the flour, melted butter and sesame seeds, 8 min.
  2. Spread thin circles on a lined tray.
  3. Bake until golden at the edges, 6 min.
  4. Fold or twist into shapes while still hot and soft.

Deckard's Black Label highball

Scotch whisky and soda water over ice with a lemon peel twist.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the soda water up to 1 day ahead and cut the lemon peel up to 4 hours ahead; build the glasses just before serving to keep the fizz
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml Scotch whisky
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 bags of ice
  1. Pack four tall glasses with ice, stir to chill, then pour off any water.
  2. Add the whisky to each and stir.
  3. Top slowly with very cold soda water down the spoon. Squeeze a strip of lemon peel over each.
