Snacks

Quicksand queso with corn chipsVegetarian

Hot cheese queso with corn chips. Falco says his fear is quicksand.

Prep 10 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Make the queso up to a day ahead; reheat gently with a splash of milk
Recipe for 4
  • 250g tasty cheese, grated
  • 125g Monterey Jack or mozzarella, grated
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 250ml evaporated milk
  • 1 small brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 jalapenos, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 300g salted corn chips
  1. Toss the cheeses with the cornflour.
  2. Soften the onion and jalapenos in the butter with the cumin.
  3. Add the evaporated milk and bring to a simmer, then stir in the cheese a handful at a time until smooth.
  4. Stir in the tomato and serve hot with the corn chips.

Nigel Gruff's 65-yard Welsh rarebitVegetarian

Welsh rarebit on toast. Nigel kicks a 65-yard field goal.

Prep 5 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese mix up to 2 days ahead and chill; spread and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 2 tsp English mustard
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 4 thick slices sourdough
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until thick.
  2. Take off the heat and beat in the cheddar, mustard and egg yolk.
  3. Toast the bread, spread thickly with the cheese and grill until bubbling and spotted brown.
  4. Dust with paprika and cut into fingers.

Mains

Mini-mart chilli dogs

Chilli dogs with onions. Franklin fears going back to the mini-mart.

Prep 5 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead; reheat and grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced, plus 1 for topping
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 200g tomato passata
  • 100g tasty cheese, grated
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Brown the mince with the diced onion, add the spices and passata and simmer until thick.
  2. Grill the franks, turning, until blistered.
  3. Toast the rolls, add a frank and smother with chilli.
  4. Top with cheese, raw onion and mustard.

Houseboat grilled swordfish rolls

Grilled swordfish rolls with lemon caper mayo. McGinty finds Falco on his houseboat.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the lemon mayo up to 2 days ahead; grill the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g swordfish steaks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Old Bay seasoning
  • 4 long soft rolls
  • 5 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 cup shredded cos lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  1. Mix the mayo with the lemon zest, a squeeze of juice and the capers.
  2. Rub the swordfish with oil and Old Bay and grill on a very hot pan 2 to 3 minutes a side.
  3. Toast the rolls and spread with the lemon mayo.
  4. Fill with lettuce, tomato and thick slices of swordfish.

Sweets

Glory lasts forever brownie sundaeVegetarian

Warm brownie sundae. Murphy catches the winner as time expires: glory lasts forever.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake the brownies up to 3 days ahead; warm and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 80g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 50g roasted peanuts, chopped
  1. Melt the dark chocolate and butter, then beat in the sugar and eggs and fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Bake in a lined 20cm tin at 170C until just set with a fudgy middle.
  3. Heat the cream and pour over the milk chocolate, stirring until smooth.
  4. Serve warm brownie squares with ice cream, hot fudge and peanuts.

Bar brawl boilermakerVegetarian

Cold lager with a whisky shot. The scabs brawl with Martel at the bar.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the beer and the shot glasses; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 375ml bottles lager
  • 120ml bourbon
  1. Pour the lager into 4 chilled glasses.
  2. Pour a 30ml shot of bourbon for each and serve alongside.

When to serve

Serve the sundae when Falco hits Murphy for the winning touchdown.
