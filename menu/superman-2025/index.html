Snacks

Daily Planet bagel bites

Mini bagels with cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and dill

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Slice the onion and pick the dill up to 1 day ahead; toast and top the bagels just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 12 mini bagels
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 12 dill sprigs
  1. Split and toast the bagels.
  2. Spread with cream cheese and top with salmon, capers, red onion and dill.

Pocket universe prawn rice paper rolls

Prawns and vermicelli wrapped in rice paper

Prep 35 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, covered with damp paper towel
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rice vermicelli
  • 300g cooked peeled prawns
  • 12 rice paper sheets
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into sticks
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  • 4 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp fish sauce
  1. Soak the vermicelli in boiling water, drain and cool.
  2. Halve the prawns lengthways.
  3. Dip each rice paper in warm water until pliable, lay flat, fill with prawns, noodles, cucumber, mint and coriander, fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  4. Mix the sweet chilli sauce with lime juice and fish sauce for dipping.

Mains

Ma Kent's beef pot roast

Beef chuck braised with carrots, onion and thyme, potatoes added

Prep 15 min, cook 195 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef chuck
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 3 carrots
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  1. Brown the beef all over.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, stock and thyme.
  3. Cover and braise at 160C.
  4. Add the potatoes for the last hour.
  5. Whisk the plain flour into the juices over the heat and simmer into a gravy, then serve family style.

Sweets

Red cape strawberry shortcakeVegetarian

Cream biscuits layered with sugared strawberries

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Shortcakes up to 1 day, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 80g cold butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml cream, for the shortcake dough
  • 300ml cream, whipped, for filling
  1. Toss the strawberries with sugar.
  2. Leave to macerate.
  3. Rub the butter into the flour, add the cream and mix to a soft dough.
  4. Cut rounds and bake at 220C.
  5. Split and fill with whipped cream and strawberries.

Drinks

Smallville honey malt milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream, honey and malted milk powder, blended thick

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the glasses up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml cold milk
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream, honey, malted milk powder and vanilla until thick and frothy.
  2. Pour into chilled tall glasses and top with whipped cream and a drizzle of honey.
