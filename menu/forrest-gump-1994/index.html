Snacks

Bubba's barbecue prawns

Skewered prawns brushed with smoked paprika garlic butter, lemon to squeeze

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Skewer up to 3 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns
  • 60g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 1 lemon
  1. Peel the prawns and thread onto skewers.
  2. Melt the butter with crushed garlic, paprika and cayenne and brush over.
  3. Grill, turning once, and squeeze lemon over.

Bus stop ham and honey mustard biscuitsOn the printed menu

Flaky buttermilk scones split warm, filled with shaved leg ham, sweet tangy butter and cheddar

How it is made
  • 140g butter
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 200g shaved leg ham
  • 100g sharp cheddar
  1. Flaky buttermilk biscuits split warm and filled with shaved leg ham, a sweet honey mustard butter and a little sharp cheddar. Rub 100g cold butter into 300g plain flour with 3 tsp baking powder and 1 tsp salt. Stir in 250ml buttermilk, fold the dough over itself three times and pat to 2cm. Cut 8 rounds and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Beat 40g soft butter with 1 tbsp honey and 1 tbsp Dijon. Split the warm biscuits, spread with the butter and fill with 200g shaved ham and sliced cheddar.

Bubba's prawn gumbo

Dark roux gumbo with smoked sausage, vegetables and prawns

Prep 23 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Make the roux and gumbo base up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then add the prawns and cook the rice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 80g plain flour
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1l chicken stock
  • 250g smoked sausage
  • 500g green prawns
  • 300g long grain rice
  1. Stir the oil and flour over medium heat into a dark roux.
  2. Dice the onion, celery and capsicum and slice the sausage. Peel the prawns.
  3. Add the vegetables and garlic to the roux, then the stock and sausage. Simmer.
  4. Cook the rice.
  5. Add the prawns to the gumbo for the last few minutes and serve over the rice.

Greenbow buttermilk fried chickenOn the printed menu

Brined with hot sauce, dredged in spiced flour and cooked crunchy, with garlicky greens and mash

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 2 bunches silverbeet
  • 1 garlic bulb
  1. Chicken pieces brined overnight in buttermilk and hot sauce, dredged in spiced flour, fried crunchy, with garlicky greens and buttery mash. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces overnight in 500ml buttermilk with 2 tbsp hot sauce and 2 tsp salt. Mix 300g plain flour with 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp pepper and salt. Dredge the chicken and fry at 165°C for 12 to 15 minutes until 74°C inside. Mash 1kg potatoes with 80g butter. Saute 2 bunches silverbeet or kale with garlic and a splash of vinegar.

Forrest's chocolatesVegetarian

Handmade dark chocolates filled with caramel, raspberry ganache or peanut butter

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 100g caramels
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 50g raspberries
  • 80g peanut butter
  1. Melt the chocolate and paint it into chocolate moulds. Set.
  2. Fill some moulds with caramel melted with a little cream, some with raspberry ganache and some with peanut butter.
  3. Seal with more chocolate and set.
  4. Pack them in a box and mix them up.

Peach cobblerVegetarian

Cinnamon peaches under a soft biscuit topping, with vanilla ice cream

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 6 peaches
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 80g butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Slice the peaches and toss with the sugar and cinnamon in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, add the milk and drop spoonfuls over the fruit.
  3. Bake at 190°C.
  4. Serve with vanilla ice cream.

Drinks

Fifteen Dr PeppersVegetarianNo alcohol

Dr Pepper poured over vanilla ice cream, one for each of the fifteen he drank at the White House

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1500ml Dr Pepper (4 x 375ml cans), chilled
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Put two scoops of vanilla ice cream in each of four tall glasses.
  2. Pour a can of cold Dr Pepper slowly down the side of each glass so it foams up without spilling.
  3. Top with a maraschino cherry and serve with a straw and a long spoon.

When to serve

Serve the prawns when Bubba starts in on shrimp creole, shrimp kabobs and the rest.
