Snacks

Pickled herring on rye

Herring on rye. Ridzik slips Danko his spare gun

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pickled herring
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 6 slices dark rye bread
  • 20g butter, softened
  1. Drain the herring and cut into bite pieces.
  2. Mix with the sour cream, chopped dill and thinly sliced red onion.
  3. Butter the rye, cut into squares and spoon the herring on top.

Rostavili's Georgian cheese breadVegetarian

Melted cheese bread. Rostavili wants his key back

Prep 25 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Mix the mozzarella and feta up to 1 day ahead, chilled; shape, bake and add the egg on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 200g feta
  • 4 eggs
  • 40g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 230C. Roll the dough into ovals.
  2. Fill with mozzarella mixed with crumbled feta and fold the edges up into boat shapes.
  3. Bake until golden.
  4. Crack an egg into each and bake a little longer.
  5. Add a knob of butter and stir at the table.

Ridzik's Chicago beef stroganoff

Seared beef strips in a mushroom, paprika and sour cream sauce over egg noodles

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Slice the onion and mushrooms up to 1 day ahead, chilled; sear, simmer and stir in the sour cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g eye fillet, cut into strips
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  1. Slice the onion and mushrooms.
  2. Sear the eye fillet strips fast in batches in the neutral oil and set aside.
  3. Brown the mushrooms and onion, add the paprika, Dijon and stock and reduce.
  4. Boil the noodles and drain.
  5. Stir in the sour cream off the heat and return the beef. Serve over the noodles.

Watch swap medovikVegetarian

Honey cake. Swap watches at the airport, cops not politicians

Prep 73 min, cook 510 min · Make ahead: Rests overnight, up to 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g honey
  • 100g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 450g plain flour
  • 600g sour cream
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk (1 tin)
  1. Melt the honey, butter and sugar, then whisk in the eggs and bicarbonate of soda, then the flour to a dough.
  2. Roll the dough into thin discs.
  3. Bake each disc, keeping the offcuts for crumbs, in a 180C oven.
  4. Beat the sour cream with the condensed milk.
  5. Layer the discs with the cream.
  6. Crush the offcuts and coat the cake in the crumbs.
  7. Rest the cake overnight in the fridge.

Danko's cranberry mors and vodka coolerVegetarian

Cranberry and vodka over ice. Pour before the bus hits the freight train

Prep 10 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen cranberries, for the mors
  • 600ml water
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 tray ice
  • 160ml vodka
  • 240ml soda water
  • 20g frozen cranberries, for garnish
  1. Simmer the cranberries with the water and honey, crushing the berries.
  2. Strain, add the juice of one lemon and chill the mors.
  3. Fill tall glasses with ice. Add vodka and mors to each, top with soda water and stir.
  4. Garnish with a few frozen cranberries and a lemon slice.

When to serve

Pour the vodka when Danko answers how Soviet cops handle stress.
