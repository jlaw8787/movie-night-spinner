Snacks

Pickled herring on rye

Herring in sour cream, dill and red onion on buttered dark rye.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pickled herring
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 6 slices of dark rye bread
  • 20g butter, softened
  1. Drain the herring and cut into bite pieces.
  2. Mix with the sour cream, chopped dill and thinly sliced red onion.
  3. Butter the rye, cut into squares and spoon the herring on top.

Rostavili's Georgian cheese breadVegetarian

Boat-shaped Georgian bread filled with melted cheese and butter.

Prep 12 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Mix the mozzarella and feta up to 1 day ahead, chilled; shape, bake and add the egg on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 200g feta
  • 4 eggs
  • 40g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 230°C. Roll the dough into ovals, 15 min.
  2. Fill with mozzarella mixed with crumbled feta and fold the edges up into boat shapes.
  3. Bake until golden, 12 min.
  4. Crack an egg into each and bake a little longer, 4 min.
  5. Add a knob of butter and stir at the table.

Mains

Ridzik's Chicago beef stroganoff

Seared beef strips in a mushroom, paprika and sour cream sauce over egg noodles.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Slice the onion and mushrooms up to 1 day ahead, chilled; sear, simmer and stir in the sour cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g eye fillet, cut into strips
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  1. Slice the onion and mushrooms.
  2. Sear the eye fillet strips fast in batches in the neutral oil and set aside, 8 min.
  3. Brown the mushrooms and onion, add the paprika, Dijon and stock and reduce, 10 min.
  4. Boil the noodles and drain, 8 min.
  5. Stir in the sour cream off the heat and return the beef. Serve over the noodles, 4 min.

Ridzik's Chicago deep dish sausage pizza

Buttery cornmeal crust up a tall pan, mozzarella, Italian sausage and chunky tomato, parmesan on top.

How it is made
  • 350g plain flour
  • 50g fine cornmeal
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 60g butter
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 400g Italian sausages
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • dried oregano
  • 40g parmesan
  1. Buttery cornmeal crust pressed up a deep tin, layered with mozzarella, Italian sausage and chunky tomato sauce, topped with parmesan and baked golden. Mix 350g plain flour, 50g cornmeal, 7g yeast, 1 tsp salt, 60g melted butter and 230ml warm water, knead and prove 1 hour. Press into a greased 25cm deep tin, up the sides. Layer 250g sliced mozzarella, then 400g Italian sausage meat pressed into a flat layer. Simmer 400g crushed tomatoes with garlic and oregano for 10 minutes and spread on top. Scatter 40g grated parmesan and bake at 220°C for 35 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.

Watch swap medovikVegetarian

Layered honey cake with sour cream frosting, cut in tall slices.

Prep 58 min, cook 45 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Rests overnight, up to 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g honey
  • 100g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 450g plain flour
  • 600g sour cream
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk (1 tin)
  1. Melt the honey, butter and sugar, then whisk in the eggs and bicarbonate of soda, then the flour to a dough, 15 min.
  2. Roll the dough into thin discs.
  3. Bake each disc, keeping the offcuts for crumbs, in a 180°C oven, 30 min.
  4. Beat the sour cream with the condensed milk.
  5. Layer the discs with the cream.
  6. Crush the offcuts and coat the cake in the crumbs.
  7. Rest the cake overnight in the fridge.

Danko's cranberry mors and vodka cooler

Cranberry and vodka over ice. Pour before the bus hits the freight train.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min, then 93 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen cranberries, for the mors
  • 600ml water
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 tray ice
  • 160ml vodka
  • 240ml soda water
  • 20g frozen cranberries, for garnish
  1. Simmer the cranberries with the water and honey, crushing the berries, 12 min.
  2. Strain, add the juice of one lemon and chill the mors, 1 hr 33 min.
  3. Fill tall glasses with ice. Add vodka and mors to each, top with soda water and stir.
  4. Garnish with a few frozen cranberries and a lemon slice.

When to serve

Pour the vodka when Danko answers how Soviet cops handle stress.
