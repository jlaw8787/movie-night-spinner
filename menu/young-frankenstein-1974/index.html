Snacks

Castle cheese and crackersVegetarian

Cheddar, gouda and Swiss with rye crispbread, pickles and grainy mustard

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheeses up to 4 hours ahead and cover; add the crispbread just before serving so it stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 120g vintage cheddar
  • 120g gouda
  • 120g Swiss cheese
  • 1 pack rye crispbread
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 1 pot grainy mustard
  1. Cut the vintage cheddar, gouda and Swiss cheese into rough chunks on a dark wooden board.
  2. Add the crispbread, pickled onions and mustard.
  3. Leave out so the cheese softens before serving.

Abby Normal's brain dipVegetarian

Roasted beetroot, chickpea and tahini dip with crackers

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Dip keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 beetroots
  • 400g chickpeas, drained
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 150g crackers
  1. Wrap the beetroots in foil and roast at 200C.
  2. Peel the beetroots.
  3. Blitz with the chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic and water until smooth.
  4. Swirl into a bowl and serve with crackers.

Mains

Transylvanian goulash

Beef and paprika goulash over buttered noodles with sour cream

Prep 20 min, cook 205 min · Make ahead: Braise the goulash up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; boil the noodles fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 3 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 20g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  2. Cook the onions slowly.
  3. Stir in the paprika and caraway, then add the tomatoes, stock and beef.
  4. Cover and braise at 160C.
  5. Boil the noodles and toss with butter.
  6. Serve over the noodles with sour cream.

Sweets

Puttin' on the Ritz cupcakesVegetarian

Chocolate cupcakes with chocolate swirls and white chocolate brim hats

Prep 50 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Frost on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 170g self-raising flour
  • 60g cocoa
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 100g white chocolate
  1. Beat about half the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, half the cocoa and the milk.
  2. Bake in 12 cases at 175C.
  3. Cool.
  4. Melt the white chocolate into 12 thin discs and set.
  5. Beat the remaining butter, icing sugar and cocoa.
  6. Pipe a tall swirl on each and sit a disc under each as a hat brim.

Drinks

Frau Blücher's brandy milk punchVegetarian

Warm honey vanilla milk with cinnamon and brandy, nutmeg on top

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Warm the milk with the honey, cinnamon and vanilla up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; reheat, whisk and add the brandy to the mugs
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 120ml brandy
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg, freshly grated
  1. Warm the milk with the honey, cinnamon and vanilla until steaming without boiling.
  2. Rest and remove the cinnamon.
  3. Whisk hard until frothy.
  4. Pour into mugs, add brandy to each and grate over a little nutmeg. Leave out the brandy for the warm milk crowd.
