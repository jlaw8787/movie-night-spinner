Snacks

Lake-town smoked fish

Rye crispbread with crème fraîche, smoked trout, dill and pickled onion.

Prep 11 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g smoked trout
  • 1 packet rye crispbread
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Slice a few pickled onions into thin rings and pick the dill into small sprigs.
  2. Spread each rye crispbread with crème fraîche.
  3. Flake the smoked trout over the top and finish with a dill sprig and a few onion rings.
  4. Arrange on a wooden board and serve cold.

Dáin's Iron Hills meat pies

Muffin tin pies of beef and onion gravy under puff pastry.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled (bake fresh)
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Dice the onion and cut the pastry into rounds to fit a 12-hole muffin tin.
  2. Brown the mince and onion in oil, add the stock and simmer until thick, 15 min.
  3. Cool the filling for 15 minutes while you line the tin with shortcrust rounds.
  4. Fill each case, top with a puff pastry lid and cut a small steam hole, 10 min.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden, then serve with tomato sauce, 20 min.

Dale campfire bread with golden syrup

Flatbread baked in the coals and torn warm, drizzled with golden syrup.

How it is made
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 100g butter
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  1. Damper of self-raising flour, butter and milk, baked at 200°C for 30 minutes, torn and served with butter and golden syrup. Rub 60g cold butter into 450g self-raising flour and a pinch of salt. Stir in 300ml milk to make a soft dough and knead lightly. Shape into a round, sit on a lined tray, cut a deep cross on top and dust with flour. Bake at 200°C for 30 minutes until it sounds hollow when tapped. Tear into chunks while hot and serve with soft butter and a jug of golden syrup.

Mains

Erebor roast beef and Yorkshire puddings

Roast beef sirloin with Yorkshire puddings baked in a muffin tin.

Prep 0 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Season the beef and make the batter up to 1 day ahead and keep both in the fridge; roast and bake the puddings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef sirloin
  • 140g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Take the beef out of the fridge, season well and make the batter by whisking flour, eggs and milk. Rest the batter while the beef cooks, 15 min.
  2. Roast the beef at 220°C, 20 min.
  3. Drop to 180°C and keep roasting.
  4. Rest the beef under foil, then heat oil in a muffin tin at 230°C, 15 min.
  5. Pour the batter into the hot tin and bake until puffed and golden, 20 min.
  6. Make gravy from the pan juices and stock, then carve the beef.

Elvish lemon and herb roast chicken

Bird under zest and garlic butter, roast roots, salad, as the elves march on the mountain.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 50g butter
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1kg mixed root vegetables
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 120g rocket
  1. Chicken roasted with lemon, garlic and herbs, with roasted root vegetables and a herb salad. Mix 50g soft butter with lemon zest, 2 crushed garlic cloves and chopped parsley, thyme and tarragon. Push it under the skin of a 1.8kg chicken and put the zested lemon inside. Toss 1kg parsnips, carrots and beetroot in chunks with oil and salt. Roast the chicken on top of the veg at 200°C for 75 minutes. Rest 10 minutes. Dress rocket and herbs with lemon juice and oil and serve with the carved chicken and veg.

Sweets

Bag End auction day crumbleVegetarian

Green apple and blackberries under an oat crumble.

Prep 23 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled (bake fresh)
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green apples
  • 250g blackberries
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 100g plain flour
  • 500ml custard
  1. Peel and slice the apples.
  2. Cook the apples with the blackberries and some of the sugar until just soft, then spoon into a dish, 5 min.
  3. Rub the cold butter into the oats, flour and remaining sugar and scatter over the fruit.
  4. Bake at 180°C until golden and bubbling, 35 min.
  5. Warm the custard and serve over the crumble, 5 min.

Drinks

Bag End hot spiced apple juiceNo alcohol

Warm apple juice with cinnamon, cloves, star anise and orange peel.

Prep 6 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Simmer the juice with the spices, zest and sugar up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently and strain into mugs to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cloudy apple juice
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 5 whole cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Peel three strips of zest from the orange and slice the rest of it thinly, 3 min.
  2. Simmer the apple juice with the cinnamon, cloves, star anise, zest and sugar over low heat, without boiling hard, 15 min.
  3. Strain into mugs and add a fresh cinnamon stick and an orange slice to each.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the people of Lake-town shelter in the ruins of Dale.
