Snacks

George's eight-and-eight hot dogs

Franks in toasted buns with fried onions, mustard, relish and cheddar.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Onions up to 1 day ahead, reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 2 onions
  • 20g butter
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 bottle of American mustard
  • 1 jar of sweet relish
  • 80g cheddar
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Slowly fry the onions in butter until golden.
  3. Simmer the frankfurts.
  4. Grill the frankfurts for colour.
  5. Toast the hot dog buns, no more and no less.
  6. Fill each with a frankfurt, the onions, a squeeze of mustard, a spoon of relish and grated cheddar.

MacKenzie in-laws smoked salmon blinis

Warm blinis with lemon crème fraîche and smoked salmon.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Topping up to 4 hours ahead, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail blinis
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch of dill
  1. Warm the blinis at 180°C.
  2. Mix the crème fraîche with lemon zest and a pinch of salt.
  3. Top each blini with the crème fraîche mix, a ribbon of smoked salmon, capers and a sliver of red onion.
  4. Finish with a dill sprig and cracked pepper and serve with lemon wedges.

Mains

Franck's reception chicken with tarragon

Seared chicken breast in tarragon cream sauce, potatoes and beans.

Prep 20 min, cook 64 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day ahead, sear the chicken fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 skin-on chicken breasts
  • 1 shallot
  • 100ml white wine
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch of tarragon
  • 300g green beans
  • 20g butter
  1. Halve the potatoes and dice the shallot.
  2. Roast the potatoes in oil at 220°C.
  3. Season the chicken breasts.
  4. Sear skin-side down in a hot pan.
  5. Flip and roast at 200°C.
  6. Rest the chicken.
  7. Fry the shallot in the pan, add the wine and reduce.
  8. Add the cream, Dijon and tarragon and simmer.
  9. Cook the beans and toss in butter.
  10. Serve with the potatoes.

Franck's reception beef fillet

Roast beef fillet carved thick, horseradish cream and roast shallots.

Prep 4 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Mix the horseradish cream up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; sear and roast the beef on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef eye fillet, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 eschalots, peeled and halved
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Mix the horseradish cream into the sour cream with chopped chives and salt.
  2. Rub the beef with oil, salt and pepper and sear on all sides in a hot pan.
  3. Roast the beef at 200°C on a bed of the eschalots and thyme until medium rare.
  4. Rest the beef under foil, then slice thick and serve with the eschalots and horseradish cream.

Annie's lemon wedding cakeVegetarian

Lemon cake layers, lemon curd and buttercream, edible flowers.

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cake up to 2 days ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 lemons
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 140ml full cream milk
  • 150g lemon curd
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 punnet of edible flowers
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs and lemon zest, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide between two lined 20cm tins.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Sandwich with lemon curd.
  6. Beat the butter with icing sugar, milk and vanilla until white and fluffy and spread over.
  7. Decorate with edible flowers.

Drinks

Late-night basketball malted milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream, milk and malted milk powder blended thick.

Prep 8 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 5 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 tbsp caramel sauce
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Chill the glasses in the freezer for 15 minutes.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream, malted milk powder and vanilla until thick.
  3. Drizzle caramel sauce down the insides of the glasses.
  4. Pour in the shake and top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of malt.
