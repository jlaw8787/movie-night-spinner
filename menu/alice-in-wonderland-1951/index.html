Eat me cakesVegetarian

Little butter cakes with white icing

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep iced cakes in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 125g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 125g self-raising flour
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • pink food colouring, a few drops
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale and creamy.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour.
  3. Spoon into a greased mini muffin tin and bake at 175°C until risen and golden.
  4. Cool the cakes on a wire rack.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the water until thick, then ice the cakes white. Tint a little icing pink and pipe EAT ME on top.

Walrus and Carpenter oysters mignonetteOn the printed menu

Shucked on crushed ice, eschalot, red wine vinegar and pepper, from the Tweedles' tale

How it is made
  • 1 eschalot
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 24 fresh oysters
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Freshly shucked oysters on crushed ice with a sharp mignonette of eschalot, red wine vinegar and cracked pepper, and lemon wedges. Finely dice 1 eschalot and mix with 80ml red wine vinegar, 1 tsp caster sugar and plenty of cracked black pepper, then chill for 30 minutes. Fill a large platter with crushed ice. Shuck 24 oysters, or buy them freshly shucked, and sit them on the ice. Spoon a little mignonette onto each just before eating and serve with lemon wedges.

Croquet lawn chicken and leek pie

Chicken thigh and leek pie in a creamy thyme sauce

Prep 7 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with the pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry (or 2 for a deeper lid)
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in batches, seasoning with salt and pepper, then set aside. Slice the leeks.
  2. Soften the leeks in the same pan.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream and thyme. Return the chicken and simmer until thick.
  4. Pour into a pie dish and top with the pastry, pressing the edges down.
  5. Bake at 200°C until puffed and deep golden.

March Hare's tea party finger sandwichesOn the printed menu

Crustless cucumber and cream cheese, egg and cress, ham and mustard, tiered, everyone's mad here

How it is made
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 loaf soft white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 punnet cress
  • 150g sliced ham
  • 1 jar mustard
  1. Three kinds of crustless finger sandwiches, cucumber and cream cheese, egg and cress, and ham and mustard, stacked high on tiered plates. Boil 4 eggs for 10 minutes, cool, peel and mash with 3 tbsp mayo and salt. Mix 150g cream cheese with chopped dill. Spread 12 slices of soft white bread with butter. Make 2 rounds of thin cucumber and cream cheese, 2 of egg and cress, and 2 of ham and mustard, using 150g ham. Trim the crusts and cut each round into 3 fingers. Stack on tiered plates.

Queen of Hearts tartsVegetarian

Puff pastry hearts with strawberry jam and cream. Off with her head!

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 120g strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Cut hearts from the pastry sheets and lay them on lined trays.
  2. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.
  3. Cool completely on a rack.
  4. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  5. Split each heart in half, fill with jam and cream, then dust the tops with icing sugar.

Tea party cakeVegetarian

Vanilla sponge sandwiched with strawberry jam, iced in pastel colours

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep iced cake covered
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 120g strawberry jam
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • pastel food colouring, a few drops
  • 8 birthday candles
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour.
  2. Divide between 2 lined tins and bake at 175°C until a skewer comes out clean.
  3. Cool the cakes completely in the tins, then turn out.
  4. Mix the icing sugar with the milk and tint in pastel colours.
  5. Sandwich the cakes with jam, ice in pastel colours and add the candles.

Drink me iced earl grey lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Earl Grey tea with honey and lemonade, over ice

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep the tea chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 earl grey tea bags
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water, stir in the honey and remove the bags.
  2. Cool the tea, then chill if you have time.
  3. Mix the tea with the lemonade and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Pour over ice in mismatched teacups.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the unbirthday party gets going.
