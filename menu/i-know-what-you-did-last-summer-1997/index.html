Car boot crab cakes with remoulade

Golden blue swimmer crab cakes and lemon. Julie finds crabs all over Max.

Prep 5 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Shape the crab cakes up to a day ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g blue swimmer crab meat, picked
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs, plus 60g for coating
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise, plus 125g for the remoulade
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Old Bay or seafood seasoning
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 small gherkins, chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 lemon
  1. Mix the crab, 60g panko, egg, 3 tbsp mayo, mustard, seasoning and spring onions without breaking up the crab too much.
  2. Shape into 8 small cakes, press into the rest of the panko and chill for 10 minutes.
  3. Fry in the oil until golden, about 3 minutes a side.
  4. Stir the capers and gherkins through the rest of the mayo with a squeeze of lemon and serve alongside.

Southport hush puppies with honey butterVegetarian

Crisp cornmeal bites and whipped honey butter.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Whip the honey butter up to 3 days ahead; mix the batter and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 75g self-raising flour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 185ml buttermilk
  • 1/2 small brown onion, grated
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar and a pinch of salt, then stir in the egg, buttermilk and onion to a thick batter.
  2. Drop teaspoonfuls into oil at 180°C and fry until deep golden, in batches.
  3. Beat the butter with the honey and a pinch of salt and serve with the hot hush puppies.

Croaker Queen fried whiting and chips

Cornmeal-crusted whiting with thick-cut chips and tartare.

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cut and soak the chips up to a day ahead in the fridge; crumb and fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g whiting fillets
  • 1kg floury potatoes, cut in thick chips
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp Old Bay or seafood seasoning
  • 125g tartare sauce
  • 2 lemons
  1. Fry the chips at 150°C until soft but pale, then drain.
  2. Dip the whiting in buttermilk, then in the cornmeal, flour and seasoning.
  3. Raise the oil to 185°C and fry the fish until crisp and golden, then drain.
  4. Fry the chips again until crisp and salt them.
  5. Serve with tartare sauce and lemon wedges.

Billy Blue prawns and grits

Garlic butter prawns over cheesy grits. Ray's boat is called the Billy Blue.

Prep 2 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns up to a day ahead and keep cold; cook the grits and prawns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g green king prawns, peeled
  • 180g polenta or quick grits
  • 750ml milk
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 60g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  1. Bring the milk and 250ml water to a simmer, whisk in the grits and cook, stirring, until thick and soft.
  2. Stir in the cheddar and half the butter and season.
  3. Fry the garlic in the rest of the butter, add the prawns and paprika and cook until pink.
  4. Spoon the grits into bowls, top with the prawns and pan butter, spring onions and a squeeze of lemon.

Fourth of July banana pudding cupsVegetarian

Vanilla custard, banana and wafer layers.

Prep 13 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Layer the cups up to a day ahead so the biscuits soften; top with cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml milk
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 ripe bananas, sliced
  • 150g vanilla wafer biscuits
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour, then whisk in the hot milk and stir over the heat until thick.
  2. Stir in the vanilla and cool with baking paper on the surface.
  3. Layer wafers, banana and custard in 4 cups, twice over.
  4. Top with whipped cream and a crumbled wafer.

Yellow slicker rum punch

Golden rum, pineapple, orange and lime.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices and rum up to 4 hours ahead and chill; pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml golden rum
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 200ml orange juice
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp grenadine
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Stir the rum, pineapple and orange juice and the juice of 1 lime in a jug.
  2. Pour over ice, drip a little grenadine down each glass and add a lime wheel.
