Snacks

Ferdinand's farmyard egg sandwichesVegetarian

Egg mayo with chives on soft white bread, cut in triangles

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch chives
  • white pepper, to taste
  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  1. Boil the eggs hard, then cool in cold water and peel.
  2. Mash the eggs with the mayonnaise, chopped chives and white pepper.
  3. Spread on the bread with shredded lettuce, close up and cut into triangles.

Sheepdog trial cheeseVegetarian

Pecorino, sheep's feta and ricotta with fresh figs and honey

Prep 8 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pecorino
  • 150g sheep's feta
  • 200g fresh ricotta
  • 4 figs
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Take the cheeses out of the fridge an hour ahead so they lose the chill.
  2. Halve the figs, then arrange the pecorino, sheep's feta and fresh ricotta on a board with the figs, drizzle over the honey and put the crackers alongside.

Ferdinand's crudités and green goddessOn the printed menu

Carrots, radishes and celery with green goddess dip. Ferdinand sneaking round the vegie patch.

How it is made
  • 150g sour cream
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch baby carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 150g snow peas
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  1. Crisp baby carrots, radish, cucumber, snow peas and cherry tomatoes with a bright green goddess dip of herbs, sour cream and lemon. Blitz 150g sour cream, 80g mayonnaise, a handful each of parsley, basil and chives, 1 spring onion, the juice of half a lemon and salt until smooth and green. Chill 30 minutes. Trim 1 bunch baby carrots and 1 bunch radishes, cut 1 Lebanese cucumber into sticks and top 150g snow peas. Arrange around the dip with 250g cherry tomatoes.

Mains

Shepherd's pie

Beef mince in tomato gravy under baked mashed potato

Prep 15 min, cook 97 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble then bake
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Dice the onion and carrots. Peel the potatoes and cut into chunks.
  2. Brown the mince hard in a large pan, then add the onion and carrot and cook a few minutes.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and stock and simmer until thick, then season with salt and pepper.
  4. Boil the potatoes until soft, drain and mash with the butter.
  5. Spoon the mince into a baking dish, top with mash and fork the surface. Bake at 200°C until golden.

Farmhouse leek, mushroom and cheddar pieOn the printed menu

Potato and leek in cheesy thyme sauce under golden puff, that'll do

How it is made
  • 500g potatoes
  • 2 leeks
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml milk
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 400g frozen peas
  1. Leek, mushroom and potato in a creamy cheddar and thyme sauce under a golden puff pastry lid, with buttered peas on the side. Boil 500g diced potato for 8 minutes and drain. Cook 2 sliced leeks and 300g sliced mushrooms in 40g butter for 10 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp flour, then 400ml milk and simmer 3 minutes. Add 150g grated cheddar, thyme, the potato, salt and pepper. Pour into a pie dish, cover with 2 sheets puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 30 minutes. Serve with 400g buttered peas.

Sweets

Hoggett farm apple crumbleVegetarian

Stewed cinnamon apples under a butter crumble, with custard

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh to serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 6 apples
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 100g butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 600ml custard
  1. Peel, core and slice the apples.
  2. Stew the apples with the caster sugar and cinnamon until just soft, then tip into a baking dish.
  3. Rub the cold butter into the flour, then stir in the oats and brown sugar. Scatter over the apples.
  4. Bake at 180°C until golden and bubbling. Heat the custard and serve alongside.

Drinks

Hoggett farm vanilla malted shakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream and malt blended thick, topped with whipped cream

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled with the glasses; blend just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 6 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder, for the shake
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tsp malted milk powder, for dusting
  1. Chill four tall glasses. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream, the malted milk powder for the shake and the honey until thick and frothy.
  3. Pour into the glasses, spoon the cream on top and dust with the extra malted milk powder.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Babe steps out to herd at the sheepdog trials.
