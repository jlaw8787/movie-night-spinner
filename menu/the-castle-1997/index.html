Snacks

Bonnie Doon cheese and kabana

Sliced smoked sausage and cubed cheese with pickled onions on picks

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cut and thread onto toothpicks up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; add the crackers to the platter on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g kabana
  • 250g tasty cheese
  • 1 cup pickled onions
  • 1 packet round butter crackers
  1. Slice the kabana on the diagonal.
  2. Cut the cheese into cubes.
  3. Thread onto toothpicks with pickled onions.
  4. Lay out on a plastic platter with crackers. It is the vibe.

Pool room prawns

Peeled king prawns on crushed ice with creamy tomato sauce and lemon

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead, kept chilled; pile on crushed ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g cooked king prawns
  • 4 cup crushed ice
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 lemons
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Pile on crushed ice.
  3. Mix the mayo, tomato sauce, Worcestershire and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges, and put the platter in the pool room after.

Mains

Sal's rissoles

Beef rissoles simmered in onion gravy, with mash and peas

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Cook the rissoles in the gravy up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and reheat gently; boil and mash the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 60g butter
  1. Mix the mince with one grated onion, the carrot, egg, breadcrumbs and Worcestershire.
  2. Shape into eight rissoles.
  3. Fry until browned.
  4. Make gravy with the other sliced onion, flour and stock.
  5. Simmer the rissoles in the gravy.
  6. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter, and cook the peas.

Sal's sponge cakeVegetarian

Light sponge filled with jam and whipped cream, dusted with icing sugar

Prep 40 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 60g cornflour
  • 60g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar for 10 minutes until pale and thick.
  2. Sift over the cornflour, flour and baking powder and fold gently.
  3. Bake in two tins at 180C.
  4. Cool.
  5. Fill with jam and whipped cream.
  6. Dust with icing sugar.

Bonnie Doon lager and lime shandyVegetarian

Lager with lime and lemonade in a chilled glass

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; pour the lager and lemonade to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 1 1/2l Australian lager (4 stubbies of 375 ml)
  • 600ml lemonade, cold
  1. Chill four pot glasses in the freezer.
  2. Squeeze lime juice into each.
  3. Pour in cold lager, tilting the glass, then top with cold lemonade.
  4. Stir once gently and add a lime wedge. Make lemon, lime and lemonade ones for the kids and the driver.
