Snacks

Bonnie Doon cheese and kabana

Sliced smoked sausage and cubed cheese with pickled onions on picks.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Cut and thread onto toothpicks up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; add the crackers to the platter on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g kabana
  • 250g tasty cheese
  • 1 cup pickled onions
  • 1 packet round butter crackers
  1. Slice the kabana on the diagonal.
  2. Cut the cheese into cubes.
  3. Thread onto toothpicks with pickled onions.
  4. Lay out on a plastic platter with the crackers.

Pool room prawns

Peeled king prawns on crushed ice with creamy tomato sauce and lemon.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead, kept chilled; pile on crushed ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g cooked king prawns
  • 4 cups crushed ice
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 lemons
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Pile on crushed ice.
  3. Mix the mayo, tomato sauce, Worcestershire and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges, and put the platter in the pool room after.

Sal's party pies and sausage rolls

Carrot and thyme mince in flaky puff, little beef pastries and tomato sauce.

How it is made
  • 500g sausage mince
  • 2 onions
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 3 sheets puff pastry
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Homemade sausage rolls with carrot and thyme and little beef party pies in flaky pastry, piping hot with a big bowl of tomato sauce. Mix 500g sausage mince with 1 grated onion, 1 grated carrot, thyme and 40g breadcrumbs. Roll in 2 halved puff pastry sheets, egg wash and cut into 16. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 onion, add 1 tbsp flour, 200ml stock and Worcestershire and simmer 15 minutes, then cool. Line 12 patty tins with shortcrust rounds, fill, top with puff and egg wash. Bake everything at 200°C for 25 minutes.

Mains

Sal's rissoles

Beef rissoles simmered in onion gravy, with mash and peas.

Prep 28 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Cook the rissoles in the gravy up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and reheat gently; boil and mash the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 60g butter
  1. Mix the mince with one grated onion, the carrot, egg, breadcrumbs and Worcestershire.
  2. Shape into eight rissoles.
  3. Fry until browned, 10 min.
  4. Make gravy with the other sliced onion, flour and stock.
  5. Simmer the rissoles in the gravy, 20 min.
  6. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter, and cook the peas, 20 min.

Kerrigan backyard barbie

Thick snags, garlic lamb chops, fried onions and potato salad, with planes roaring overhead.

How it is made
  • 8 lamb loin chops
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 2 onions
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Thick beef snags and rosemary garlic lamb chops off the hotplate, fried onions, buttered white bread and a creamy potato salad, Darryl's way. Marinate 8 lamb loin chops in olive oil, rosemary and 2 crushed garlic cloves for 30 minutes. Boil 1kg potatoes, cool and toss with 150g mayonnaise, chopped spring onion and parsley. Cook 8 thick beef sausages on a medium barbecue for 15 minutes, turning. Fry 2 sliced onions on the hotplate with oil until soft. Grill the chops 3 minutes a side. Serve with buttered white bread and tomato sauce.

Sweets

Sponge cakeVegetarian

Light sponge filled with jam and whipped cream, dusted with icing sugar.

Prep 21 min, cook 26 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 60g cornflour
  • 60g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar for 10 minutes until pale and thick.
  2. Sift over the cornflour, flour and baking powder and fold gently.
  3. Bake in two tins at 180°C, 20 min.
  4. Cool, 20 min.
  5. Fill with jam and whipped cream.
  6. Dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Bonnie Doon lager and lime shandy

Lager with lime and lemonade in a chilled glass.

Prep 5 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; pour the lager and lemonade to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 1.5 L Australian lager (4 stubbies of 375 ml)
  • 600ml lemonade, cold
  1. Chill four pot glasses in the freezer, 15 min.
  2. Squeeze lime juice into each.
  3. Pour in cold lager, tilting the glass, then top with cold lemonade.
  4. Stir once gently and add a lime wedge. Make lemon, lime and lemonade ones for the kids and the driver.
