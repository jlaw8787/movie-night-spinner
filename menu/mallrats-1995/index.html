Cinnamon pretzel knotsVegetarian

Soft pretzel knots rolled in cinnamon sugar, with a sweet cream cheese dip.

Prep 18 min, cook 22 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 250g cream cheese, sweetened to taste
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and warm water to a dough and knead briefly, 10 min.
  2. Rest the dough for an hour until puffy.
  3. Cut into bites and boil briefly in water with the bicarb soda, 10 min.
  4. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes until deep golden.
  5. Brush with butter and roll in cinnamon sugar. Serve with the sweetened cream cheese.

Comic store popcornVegetarian

Buttered popcorn tossed with parmesan and garlic powder.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 50g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  1. Pop the corn and toss straight away with the melted butter.
  2. Sprinkle with parmesan and garlic powder, tossing until every piece is coated. Pour into paper bags.

Pork egg rolls

Pork, cabbage, carrot and scallion rolled tight and fried crisp, with sweet and sour sauce.

How it is made
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 0.25 cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 12 large spring roll wrappers
  • 1 bottle sweet and sour sauce
  • 2L vegetable oil
  1. Pork, cabbage, carrot and spring onion rolled tight in egg roll wrappers, fried crisp and blistered, sliced on the angle with sweet and sour sauce. Stir-fry 300g pork mince with 2 garlic cloves and grated ginger until browned. Add 3 cups shredded cabbage, a grated carrot and 3 spring onions and cook 3 minutes. Season with 2 tbsp soy and 1 tsp sesame oil, then cool. Roll tightly in 12 large spring roll wrappers, sealing with water. Fry at 180°C for 4 minutes until golden. Slice on the angle and serve with sweet and sour sauce.

Teriyaki chicken bowl

Glazed grilled chicken thighs over jasmine rice with stir-fried broccoli.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Glaze up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 120ml soy sauce
  • 120ml mirin
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 2 heads of broccoli, cut into florets
  1. Simmer the soy sauce, mirin, sugar and ginger until syrupy, 10 min.
  2. Steam the rice. Grill the chicken, brushing with the glaze, until sticky and charred, 20 min.
  3. Stir-fry the broccoli quickly. Slice the chicken over the rice and spoon over the extra sauce, 15 min.

Pepperoni and mushroom pizza

Thin bases, garlic tomato, mozzarella, pepperoni and mushrooms, baked hot and cut in squares.

How it is made
  • 400g can diced tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 120g pepperoni
  • 150g mushrooms
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  1. Thin pizza bases spread with garlic tomato sauce, loaded with mozzarella, pepperoni, mushrooms and oregano, baked blistering hot and cut into squares. Blitz a 400g can of tomatoes with a garlic clove, 1 tsp oregano, salt and olive oil. Stretch 500g pizza dough into 2 thin bases on oiled trays. Spread with the sauce, then top with 250g grated mozzarella, 120g pepperoni and 150g sliced mushrooms. Bake at 250°C, or the hottest your oven goes, for 10 minutes until blistered. Scatter with chilli flakes and cut into squares.

Sailboat poster cookiesVegetarian

Big warm chocolate chip cookies, from the cookie stand upstairs.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days chilled, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 225g plain flour
  • 200g dark chocolate chips
  1. Beat the butter, sugar and egg until creamy. Mix in the flour and chocolate chips.
  2. Roll into big balls and bake at 180°C so the centres stay gooey, 10 min.
  3. Cool for 5 minutes and serve warm.

Truth or Date lemonadeNo alcohol

Muddled strawberries, lemon juice and soda water over ice.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 6 lemons, juiced
  • 100g caster sugar, to taste
  • 1 L cold soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Muddle the strawberries in the bottom of a big jug.
  2. Add the lemon juice and sugar to taste, then top with soda water.
  3. Pour into big cups packed with ice and add a straw to each.
