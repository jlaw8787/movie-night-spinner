Snacks

Cinnamon pretzel bitesVegetarian

Soft pretzel bites rolled in cinnamon sugar, with a sweet cream cheese dip

Prep 30 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 250g cream cheese, sweetened to taste
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and warm water to a dough and knead briefly.
  2. Rest the dough for an hour until puffy.
  3. Cut into bites and boil briefly in water with the bicarb soda.
  4. Bake at 220C for 12 minutes until deep golden.
  5. Brush with butter and roll in cinnamon sugar. Serve with the sweetened cream cheese.

Comic store popcornVegetarian

Buttered popcorn tossed with parmesan and garlic powder

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 50g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  1. Pop the corn and toss straight away with the melted butter.
  2. Sprinkle with parmesan and garlic powder, tossing until every piece is coated. Pour into paper bags.

Mains

Teriyaki chicken bowl

Glazed grilled chicken thighs over jasmine rice with stir-fried broccoli

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Glaze up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 120ml soy sauce
  • 120ml mirin
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 2 heads broccoli, cut into florets
  1. Simmer the soy sauce, mirin, sugar and ginger until syrupy.
  2. Steam the rice. Grill the chicken, brushing with the glaze, until sticky and charred.
  3. Stir-fry the broccoli quickly. Slice the chicken over the rice and spoon over the extra sauce.

Sailboat poster cookiesVegetarian

Big warm chocolate chip cookies, from the cookie stand upstairs

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days chilled, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 225g plain flour
  • 200g dark chocolate chips
  1. Beat the butter, sugar and egg until creamy. Mix in the flour and chocolate chips.
  2. Roll into big balls and bake at 180C so the centres stay gooey.
  3. Cool for 5 minutes and serve warm.

Truth or Date lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Muddled strawberries, lemon juice and soda water over ice

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 6 lemons, juiced
  • 100g caster sugar, to taste
  • 1l cold soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Muddle the strawberries in the bottom of a big jug.
  2. Add the lemon juice and sugar to taste, then top with soda water.
  3. Pour into big cups packed with ice and add a straw to each.
