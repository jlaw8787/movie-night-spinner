Snacks

Diner leftovers steak bites

Seared rump steak pieces in garlic butter, on toothpicks with horseradish.

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Season the steak up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; sear, cut and toss in the garlic butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g rump steak
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  1. Season the steak.
  2. Sear hard in a very hot pan.
  3. Rest, then cut into bite-sized pieces.
  4. Toss in melted butter with crushed garlic and sea salt.
  5. Serve on toothpicks with a small bowl of horseradish.

Ozarks cornbreadVegetarian

Pan buttermilk cornbread with honey butter.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep covered, rewarm in a 160°C oven for 10 minutes; beat the honey butter up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 100g butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, egg, buttermilk and melted butter until just combined.
  2. Pour into a hot greased cast iron pan and bake at 220°C.
  3. Beat softened butter with honey.
  4. Serve with warm wedges.

Mains

Diner steak and eggs

Pepper-crusted rump steak with two fried eggs and crisp hash browns.

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; fry the hash browns up to 1 hour ahead and keep warm in a 120°C oven
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 8 eggs
  • 600g potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 4 slices white bread
  1. Grate the potatoes and squeeze dry.
  2. Fry as hash brown cakes in butter until crisp.
  3. Season the steaks with cracked pepper and sear to medium-rare.
  4. Rest the steaks.
  5. Fry the eggs and toast the bread.
  6. Serve each steak with two eggs, hash browns and buttered toast.

Lemon oregano chicken with riceOn the printed menu

Thighs in zest and garlic, grilled charred, rice, broccolini and tzatziki, for a fighter in training.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 200g tzatziki
  1. Chicken thighs marinated in lemon, garlic and oregano, grilled until charred, over steamed rice with garlicky broccolini and a spoon of tzatziki. Marinate 800g chicken thighs for 1 hour in the zest and juice of 2 lemons, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp dried oregano, olive oil and salt. Rinse 300g long grain rice and simmer covered in 450ml water for 12 minutes, then rest 5 minutes. Grill the chicken over high heat for 5 minutes a side until charred and cooked through. Blanch 2 bunches broccolini and toss with garlic oil. Serve with tzatziki and lemon wedges.

Lemon meringue pieVegetarian

Tart homemade curd under tall meringue. No canned filling.

Prep 10 min, cook 29 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day it is made
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 4 lemons
  • 350g caster sugar, divided
  • 4 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 eggs
  • 60g butter
  1. Blind bake the pastry case until golden.
  2. Separate the eggs. Whisk the lemon juice and zest, most of the sugar, cornflour, egg yolks and butter over heat until thick.
  3. Pour into the case.
  4. Whisk the egg whites with the remaining sugar to stiff glossy peaks and pile on top.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  6. Cool before slicing.

Diner coffee float with vanilla ice creamVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold brown sugar coffee poured over scoops of vanilla ice cream.

Prep 17 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Brew, sweeten and chill the coffee up to 2 days ahead; scoop, pour and top with cream to order
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml strong drip coffee (from 60 g ground coffee)
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  1. Brew the coffee strong and stir in the brown sugar while hot.
  2. Cool, then chill for an hour.
  3. Put two scoops of ice cream in each of four tall glasses.
  4. Pour the cold coffee slowly over the ice cream so it foams.
  5. Top with whipped cream and a dusting of cocoa.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Frankie and Maggie stop at the roadside diner for lemon pie.
