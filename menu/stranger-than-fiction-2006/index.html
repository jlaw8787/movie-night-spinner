Stranger than Fiction (2006) movie night dinner menu

A tax auditor starts hearing a voice narrating his life and learns he is about to die. Somebody's narrating dinner. Make it sound good.

The menu is set at Ana Pascal's Bakery, from the world of the film.

Snacks

Ham and cheddar grilled sandwiches

Pan-toasted leg ham and cheddar sandwiches, cut into triangles.

Prep 6 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, wrapped in the fridge; butter the outsides and cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 30g butter
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g cheddar
  1. Butter the outsides of the slices of bread and fill the sandwiches with the ham and the grated cheddar.
  2. Cook in a pan over medium heat for exactly 3 minutes a side and cut into neat triangles.

Ana's bakery cheddar and chive sconesVegetarian

Cheddar and chive, warm from the oven with butter.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best baked to serve; cut rounds can be frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 160g butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 300ml full cream milk
  1. Rub 60g cold butter into the flour, salt and cayenne.
  2. Stir in 120g grated cheddar and half the chives, snipped, then the milk to a soft dough.
  3. Pat 3cm thick, cut 8 rounds, brush with milk and top with the rest of the cheddar, 6 min.
  4. Bake at 220°C until risen and golden, 15 min.
  5. Whip the rest of the butter soft and serve with the warm scones, 4 min.

Mains

Harold's tax audit pot pie

Chicken thigh and leek in creamy sauce under a puff pastry lid.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 11 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 leeks
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Cook the diced chicken thigh with the sliced leeks in the butter, 13 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, the chicken stock and the cream and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Spoon into a dish, top with puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes.

Professor Hilbert's lemon pepper fish

Crisp-skin sea bass, citrus butter, crushed potatoes and salad, comedy not tragedy.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make the lemon pepper butter up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 barramundi fillets, skin on
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g mixed salad leaves
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  1. Boil the potatoes for 18 minutes, drain and crush lightly with a fork and 1 tbsp of the oil.
  2. Mash the butter with the lemon zest, pepper and a pinch of salt.
  3. Score the fish skin, salt it and cook skin side down in the rest of the oil for 5 minutes until crisp, then flip for 2 minutes.
  4. Toss the leaves and sliced cucumber with the vinegar and a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Top the fish with the lemon pepper butter and serve with the potatoes and salad.

Sweets

Ana's three-flour brown butter cookiesVegetarian

Brown butter cookies made with three flours, her answer to plain sugar cookies.

Prep 8 min, cook 24 min, 44 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 100g plain flour
  • 60g spelt flour
  • 40g rye flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 40g demerara sugar
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Brown the butter until nutty and cool 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk with the brown sugar, the caster sugar, the egg and the vanilla, 4 min.
  3. Stir in the plain flour, the spelt flour, the rye flour, the baking powder and a pinch of salt.
  4. Chill 30 minutes.
  5. Heat the oven to 180°C, 4 min.
  6. Roll into balls, roll in demerara sugar and bake 11 to 12 minutes.
  7. Sprinkle with flaky salt while warm, 4 min.

Drinks

Cold vanilla malted milkNo alcohol

Cold milk with vanilla bean, malt powder and honey in frosted glasses.

Prep 7 min, 18 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the vanilla malt paste up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; add the cold milk and whisk just before pouring
Recipe for 4
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 5 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 L full cream milk
  1. Put the tall glasses in the freezer for 20 minutes.
  2. Scrape the seeds from half a vanilla bean into a jug.
  3. Add the malted milk powder, the honey and the milk and whisk into a smooth paste.
  4. Add the very cold full cream milk and whisk hard, or blend for 20 seconds, until frothy.
  5. Pour into the frosted glasses and dust with a little more malt.

When to serve
