Snacks

Eden-bound border tacos

Chilli-braised beef chuck in corn tortillas with onion, coriander and lime

Prep 20 min, cook 200 min · Make ahead: Meat up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck
  • 3 dried chillies, soaked
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 16 corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion, diced
  • 1 handful of coriander
  • 2 limes, cut in wedges
  1. Soak the chillies and blend with the garlic and cumin.
  2. Braise the beef in the chilli paste until it shreds.
  3. Shred the beef and warm the tortillas.
  4. Serve in the tortillas with onion, coriander and lime.

Farm corn frittersVegetarian

Fresh corn, spring onion and chilli fritters fried golden, with sour cream

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 3 corn cobs
  • 120g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml milk
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • sour cream, to serve
  1. Cut the kernels off the corn cobs.
  2. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk, then fold in the corn, spring onions and chilli.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in oil until golden.
  4. Serve with sour cream.

Mains

Munson farm pork chops

Pan-fried pork chops in apple mustard sauce, mash and beans, at the Munson table

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork chops
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 apple, sliced
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 800g potatoes, peeled
  • 50g butter
  • 300g green beans
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the chops and pan fry in the olive oil on each side.
  2. Fry the onion and apple in the pan, add the stock and mustard and reduce.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter.
  4. Steam the green beans.
  5. Serve the chops with the sauce, mash and beans.

Sweets

Farmhouse peach pieVegetarian

Lattice-top peach pie with brown sugar and cinnamon, served with ice cream

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 6 peaches, sliced
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • ice cream, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 190C. Toss the peaches with the sugar, cinnamon and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two sheets of pastry.
  3. Fill, then weave a lattice from the other two sheets.
  4. Bake until golden and bubbling.
  5. Serve with ice cream.

Drinks

Logan's limo bourbon and maple sourVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and maple shaken with egg white, over one big ice cube

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Shake the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 100ml lemon juice
  • 60ml maple syrup
  • 2 egg whites
  • 3 cup ice
  • 1 pinch of smoked salt
  • 1 orange, peel only
  1. Dry shake the bourbon, lemon juice, maple syrup and egg whites for a few seconds.
  2. Add ice and shake hard.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in a heavy glass.
  4. Add a tiny pinch of smoked salt on the foam and twist orange peel over the top.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the Munson family invite them in for dinner.
