Snacks

Hab-grown potato skins

Grown in Martian soil and crew waste. Cheddar, bacon

Prep 8 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Bake, halve and scoop the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; fill and bake again on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g large potatoes
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g cooked bacon, chopped
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200C.
  2. Halve, scoop and brush with melted butter.
  3. Fill with cheddar and bacon.
  4. Bake again until crisp.
  5. Top with sour cream and chives.

Red planet hummusVegetarian

Red pepper hummus. Pathfinder, dug up to call home

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight in the fridge; swirl with the oil and warm the flatbread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 400g can chickpeas, drained
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Roast the capsicum until black, then peel and seed.
  2. Blitz with chickpeas, tahini, garlic, lemon juice and paprika until smooth.
  3. Swirl with olive oil.
  4. Serve with warm flatbread.

Mains

Sol 100 potato and leek soup

Watney makes water from rocket fuel

Prep 19 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 leeks
  • 1 onion
  • 40g butter
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1.2l chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Soften the leeks and onion in butter.
  2. Add the potatoes and stock.
  3. Simmer until tender.
  4. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  5. Blend with the cream until silky.
  6. Top with bacon and chives and serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

Rich Purnell manoeuvre sundaeVegetarian

Fudge sundae. Hermes slingshots back to Mars

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate fudge up to 3 days ahead, chilled, and rewarm; whip the cream and build the sundaes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream, divided
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 100g honeycomb, crushed
  1. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream.
  2. Whip the remaining cream.
  3. Scoop ice cream into tall glasses and pour over the fudge.
  4. Pipe whipped cream into a tall rocket shape and scatter crushed honeycomb at the base for flames.

Drinks

Lewis's disco condensed milk iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong drip brew stirred into sweet tinned milk, chilled, over ice, for a space pirate

Prep 7 min, cook 2 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the coffee and condensed milk up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add the ice and cold milk to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml strong coffee (from 60 g ground coffee)
  • 8 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the strong coffee.
  2. Put condensed milk into each of four heatproof glasses and pour in hot coffee, stirring until it dissolves.
  3. Cool, then chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Add plenty of ice, top with a splash of cold milk and stir once.
