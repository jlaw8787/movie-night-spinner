Venezuelan black bean arepasVegetarian

Cornmeal arepas filled with black beans and cheese. It came from a tepui in Venezuela.

Prep 15 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Cook the beans up to 2 days ahead; shape and griddle the arepas to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pre-cooked white cornmeal (masarepa)
  • 375ml warm water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 1 small brown onion, diced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 120g queso fresco or feta, crumbled
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 lime
  1. Mix the masarepa with the warm water and a pinch of salt into a soft dough and rest for 5 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion, garlic and cumin in the oil, add the beans and a splash of water and mash roughly.
  3. Shape the dough into 8 small patties and cook on an oiled griddle until crusted on both sides.
  4. Split the arepas and stuff with beans, cheese, avocado and a squeeze of lime.

Dr. Death's devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs with paprika. Ross gets nicknamed Doctor Death.

Prep 14 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill the eggs up to a day ahead and keep covered; add the shallots to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp fried shallots
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes, then cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Halve them, mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and half the paprika and season well.
  3. Pipe the yolk back into the whites.
  4. Dust with the rest of the paprika and top with fried shallots and chives.

Mains

Country doctor's chicken pot pie

Chicken and vegetable pot pie. Dr Metcalf won't retire yet.

Prep 8 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 125ml pouring cream
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in half the butter and set aside.
  2. Soften the leeks, carrots and celery in the rest of the butter, then stir in the flour for a minute.
  3. Whisk in the stock and cream, add the chicken and thyme and simmer until thick, then stir in the peas.
  4. Spoon into a pie dish, lay the pastry over, crimp the edges, brush with egg and cut a steam hole.
  5. Bake at 200°C until the pastry is puffed and deep golden.

San Francisco cioppino

Fish and shellfish stewed in tomato with fennel. They left the city for this.

Prep 16 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the tomato broth up to 2 days ahead; add the seafood when it is back at a simmer
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 fennel bulb, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 400g firm white fish fillets, in chunks
  • 300g green prawns, peeled
  • 500g mussels, cleaned
  • 1 handful parsley, chopped
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  1. Soften the onion, fennel and garlic in the oil with the chilli.
  2. Add the tomatoes and stock and simmer until rich.
  3. Add the fish and mussels, cover for 3 minutes, then add the prawns until pink and the mussels open.
  4. Scatter with parsley and serve with thick slices of grilled sourdough.

Spun sugar web chocolate tartVegetarian

Dark chocolate tart under a spun sugar web. Ross takes on the general in the cellar.

Prep 5 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Bake and fill the tart up to 2 days ahead; spin the sugar web on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 300ml pouring cream
  • 250g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 30g butter
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 150g caster sugar
  1. Line a 22cm tart tin with the pastry, fill with baking paper and rice and blind bake at 190°C, then bake uncovered until golden.
  2. Bring the cream to a simmer, pour it over the chocolate and butter, stir until glossy and add the salt.
  3. Pour into the cooled case and chill until set.
  4. Melt the sugar in a dry pan to a pale caramel, then flick thin threads from a fork over a rolling pin to make a web.
  5. Lay the sugar web over the tart just before serving.

The Chateau red sangriaVegetarian

Cabernet, orange, brandy and soda over ice, in big glasses

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the wine, fruit and brandy up to 4 hours ahead and chill; add the soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 x 750ml bottle cabernet sauvignon
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 oranges, sliced
  • 1 red apple, diced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml soda water
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Stir the wine, brandy, sugar and fruit together in a jug and chill.
  2. Add ice and soda water and pour.

When to serve

Pour the red when Ross falls through the floor into his wine cellar.
