Mr Potato Head loaded potato bites

Smashed baby potatoes with cheddar, bacon, sour cream and chives.

Prep 11 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; smash, roast and top on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 100g cheddar
  • 3 rashers of bacon
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and drain, 15 min.
  2. Smash flat on an oiled tray and roast at 220°C until crisp, 25 min.
  3. Grate the cheddar, crisp and chop the bacon, 8 min.
  4. Top with cheese and bacon and return to the oven.
  5. Add sour cream and chopped chives.

Army men pea and broad bean saladVegetarian

Peas and broad beans tossed with mint, lemon and olive oil, served in little buckets.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 200g broad beans
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Blanch the peas and broad beans and drain, 4 min.
  2. Toss with chopped mint, lemon zest and juice, olive oil and flaky salt.
  3. Spoon into little toy buckets or cups.

Pizza Planet garlic knots

Dough tied and baked golden, parsley butter and parmesan, marinara for dipping.

How it is made
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 60g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 30g parmesan
  • 300g tomato passata
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  1. Pizza dough tied into knots, baked golden and tossed in garlic parsley butter and parmesan, with warm marinara for dunking. Cut 500g pizza dough into 12 pieces, roll each into a rope and tie into a knot. Place on a lined tray and bake at 220°C for 12 to 14 minutes until golden. Melt 60g butter with 3 crushed garlic cloves and chopped parsley and toss the hot knots in it with 30g grated parmesan. Simmer 300g passata with garlic, oregano and salt for 10 minutes for dipping.

Moon crater pepperoni pizza

Thin crust pepperoni and mozzarella pizza with black olive craters.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Stretch the dough up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; sauce, top and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 60g black olives
  1. Heat the oven and a tray to 250°C, 20 min.
  2. Stretch the dough into two rounds and spread with crushed tomatoes.
  3. Cover with mozzarella, then arrange pepperoni like planets and olive slices like craters.
  4. Bake on the hot tray until blistered, 10 min.

Moving day grilled sandwich and tomato soup

Cheesy sourdough grilled sandwiches, roast tomato soup for dunking.

How it is made
  • 1kg tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 60ml cream
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 50g butter
  • 200g cheddar
  • 100g mozzarella
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Golden sourdough toasties oozing cheddar and mozzarella, dunked into roast tomato soup topped with basil, cream and crunchy croutons. Roast 1kg tomatoes, 1 onion and 3 garlic cloves with oil at 200°C for 35 minutes. Blend with 500ml chicken stock and 1 tsp sugar, then heat with 60ml cream. Butter the outsides of 8 slices sourdough and fill with 200g grated cheddar and 100g grated mozzarella. Cook in a pan over medium-low heat for 4 minutes a side. Cut into soldiers and serve with the soup and basil.

Andy's birthday cakeVegetarian

Chocolate layer cake with chocolate buttercream and a fondant cowboy hat.

Prep 30 min, cook 30 min, 40 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 90g cocoa
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 350g icing sugar
  • 100g fondant
  1. Beat 200g of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, 50g cocoa and milk.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool the cakes completely, 40 min.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and remaining cocoa and sandwich and cover the cake.
  5. Top with a fondant cowboy hat and candles.

Pizza Planet alien punchNo alcohol

Pineapple, lemonade and lime punch with green grape aliens.

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Mix the juice, lime and colouring up to 1 day ahead and chill; top with lemonade and ice just before serving so it stays fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L pineapple juice
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 2 limes
  • 3 drops of green food colouring
  • 250g green grapes
  • ice, to serve
  1. Mix the pineapple juice, lemonade, lime juice and colouring in a jug.
  2. Thread the grapes on sticks as aliens.
  3. Add ice and pour into cups.
