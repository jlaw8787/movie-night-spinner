31 to 0 taro chipsVegetarian

Paper thin fried taro chips with lime and chilli.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g taro
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 lime, zested
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Peel the taro and slice paper thin on a mandoline.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil in batches at 175°C until crisp and pale golden, 15 min.
  3. Drain and toss with salt mixed with lime zest and chilli flakes.

Samoan oka raw fish in coconut

Fish cured in lime juice, tossed with coconut cream and cucumber.

Prep 20 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Cure and chill the fish in the lime juice up to 1 hour ahead; stir in the coconut cream and vegetables just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g very fresh white fish fillets
  • 4 limes
  • 200ml coconut cream
  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1/2 red onion
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the fish into 1cm cubes and cover with lime juice.
  2. Chill until the fish turns white, 30 min.
  3. Dice the cucumber, tomatoes and onion.
  4. Drain most of the juice, then stir in the coconut cream and vegetables. Season and serve cold.

Palusami coconut spinach parcels

Taro leaves baked in coconut cream, a feast staple.

How it is made
  • 1 bunch silverbeet
  • 400ml coconut cream
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 800g taro
  • vegetable oil
  • foil
  1. Silverbeet leaves wrapped around onion, garlic and seasoned coconut cream, baked in foil parcels until rich and soft, served with roast taro. Heat the oven to 180°C. Peel 800g taro, cut into chunks, toss in oil and salt and roast 45 minutes. Strip the stalks from 1 bunch silverbeet. Mix 400ml coconut cream with 1 finely diced onion, 1 crushed garlic clove and 1 tsp salt. Stack 3 leaves into a cup in your hand, add a few spoons of coconut mix, fold closed and wrap tightly in foil. Make 8 parcels and bake 40 minutes. Open and serve with the taro.

Tavita's team sapasui

Samoan chop suey: glass noodles, chicken, garlic, ginger and soy.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g glass noodles
  • 600g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 knob ginger (about a thumb)
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 250ml water
  1. Soak the glass noodles in hot water and drain, 10 min.
  2. Slice the chicken, carrots and onion. Crush the garlic and chop the ginger.
  3. Fry the chicken in the vegetable oil with the garlic and ginger until browned, then add the carrots and onion, 10 min.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce and water and toss until the noodles soak it all up, 7 min.

Pisupo corned beef with fried rice

Tinned meat crisped with onion, tomato and cabbage, garlic rice and lime, on every island table.

How it is made
  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 340g tinned corned beef
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • soy sauce
  • vegetable oil
  • 2 limes
  1. Tinned corned beef fried with onion, tomato and cabbage until crispy at the edges, over garlic fried rice with a squeeze of lime and soy. Cook 2 cups rice the day before and chill. Fry 1 sliced onion in oil for 3 minutes, add 2 diced tomatoes and a quarter of a cabbage, shredded, and cook 5 minutes. Break in 340g tinned corned beef and fry over high heat, stirring now and then, until browned in spots. In another pan, fry 3 crushed garlic cloves in oil, add the rice and 2 tbsp soy and toss until hot. Serve the beef over the rice with lime.

Panipopo coconut bunsVegetarian

Soft yeast buns baked in sweet coconut cream.

Prep 15 min, cook 38 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Best the day it's baked
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar, for the dough
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 300ml warm water
  • 400ml coconut cream
  • 100g caster sugar, for the sauce
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, the 50 g of sugar, butter and warm water into a dough, 12 min.
  2. Prove the dough, which is the resting time, 1 hr.
  3. Shape 12 buns into a baking dish.
  4. Mix the coconut cream with the 100 g of sugar for the sauce and pour over.
  5. Bake at 180°C until golden and sticky, 30 min.

Jaiyah's Samoan hot chocolateNo alcohol

Dark chocolate melted into hot water and milk, served in mugs.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh, serve hot or over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 500ml hot water
  • 500ml milk
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 cup ice, if serving cold
  1. Melt the chocolate into the hot water, 4 min.
  2. Whisk in the milk and sugar and heat until steaming, 6 min.
  3. Serve hot in mugs, or cool and pour over ice.
