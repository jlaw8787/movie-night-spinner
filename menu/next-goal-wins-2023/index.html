Snacks

31 to 0 taro chipsVegetarian

Paper thin fried taro chips with lime and chilli

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g taro
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 lime, zested
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Peel the taro and slice paper thin on a mandoline.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil in batches at 175C until crisp and pale golden.
  3. Drain and toss with salt mixed with lime zest and chilli flakes.

Samoan oka raw fish in coconut

Fish cured in lime juice, tossed with coconut cream and cucumber

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cure and chill the fish in the lime juice up to 1 hour ahead; stir in the coconut cream and vegetables just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g very fresh white fish fillets
  • 4 limes
  • 200ml coconut cream
  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1/2 red onion
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the fish into 1cm cubes and cover with lime juice.
  2. Chill until the fish turns white.
  3. Dice the cucumber, tomatoes and onion.
  4. Drain most of the juice, then stir in the coconut cream and vegetables. Season and serve cold.

Mains

Tavita's team sapasui

Samoan chop suey: glass noodles, chicken, garlic, ginger and soy

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g glass noodles
  • 600g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 knob ginger (about a thumb)
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 250ml water
  1. Soak the glass noodles in hot water and drain.
  2. Slice the chicken, carrots and onion. Crush the garlic and chop the ginger.
  3. Fry the chicken in the vegetable oil with the garlic and ginger until browned, then add the carrots and onion.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce and water and toss until the noodles soak it all up.

Sweets

Panipopo coconut bunsVegetarian

Soft yeast buns baked in sweet coconut cream

Prep 20 min, cook 93 min · Make ahead: Best the day it's baked
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar, for the dough
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 300ml warm water
  • 400ml coconut cream
  • 100g caster sugar, for the sauce
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, the 50 g of sugar, butter and warm water into a dough.
  2. Prove the dough, which is the resting time.
  3. Shape 12 buns into a baking dish.
  4. Mix the coconut cream with the 100 g of sugar for the sauce and pour over.
  5. Bake at 180C until golden and sticky.

Jaiyah's Samoan hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Dark chocolate melted into hot water and milk, served in mugs

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh, serve hot or over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 500ml hot water
  • 500ml milk
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 cup ice, if serving cold
  1. Melt the chocolate into the hot water.
  2. Whisk in the milk and sugar and heat until steaming.
  3. Serve hot in mugs, or cool and pour over ice.
