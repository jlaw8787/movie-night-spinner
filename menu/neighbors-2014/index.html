Snacks

Frat party pork and fennel sausage rolls

Pork, apple and fennel sausage rolls in puff pastry.

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Roll the sausage rolls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; glaze, cut and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 apple, grated
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 2 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 30g breadcrumbs
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 200g tomato relish
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Mix the sausage mince with the apple, onion, fennel, breadcrumbs and pepper.
  2. Cut the pastry in half. Lay a log of filling along each strip, roll up and seal underneath.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and cut each roll into pieces.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  5. Stir the paprika into the relish and serve alongside.

Baby's first rave baked brieVegetarian

Baked brie with honey thyme walnuts, baguette toasts and grapes.

Prep 0 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Slice the baguette and score the brie up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake and serve hot
Recipe for 4
  • 200g brie wheel
  • 50g walnuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 1 baguette, sliced thinly
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g grapes
  1. Score the top of the brie in a criss-cross and sit it in a small ovenproof dish.
  2. Brush the baguette slices with oil and bake at 180°C alongside the brie until crisp. Remove the toasts first.
  3. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan, then warm with the honey and thyme leaves.
  4. Spoon the nuts over the hot brie and serve with the toasts and grapes.

Mains

Second strike barbecue steak with chimichurri

Grilled rump with parsley chimichurri, corn, sausages and apple slaw.

Prep 12 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chimichurri up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; grill the meat and corn and toss the slaw on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 800g rump steak
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 1 apple, julienned
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  1. Blitz the parsley, garlic, oregano, chilli, vinegar and oil into chimichurri.
  2. Grill the corn, turning.
  3. Grill the sausages.
  4. Season the steak and grill over high heat, then rest and slice.
  5. Toss the cabbage and apple with the mayonnaise and lemon juice.
  6. Spoon the chimichurri over the steak and serve with corn, sausages and slaw.

Robert De Niro party chicken parm heroes

Crumbed breast fried crisp, baked under napoli and mozzarella in toasted rolls, eaten in character.

Prep 2 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large chicken breasts, halved and flattened
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 150ml vegetable oil
  • 300ml napoli sauce
  • 200g mozzarella, sliced
  • 4 hero rolls
  • 100g rocket
  • 20g parmesan, grated
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Dust the chicken in flour, dip in beaten egg and coat in panko.
  2. Fry until golden and cooked through.
  3. Lay in the split rolls, spoon over the napoli, top with mozzarella and bake until melted.
  4. Serve with rocket and parmesan.

Delta Psi Hall of Fame sheet cakeVegetarian

Vanilla sprinkle cake with cream cheese frosting.

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 280g butter, soft
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 100g rainbow sprinkles
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 300g icing sugar
  1. Beat most of the butter with the sugar until pale. Beat in the eggs and vanilla, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Fold through some of the sprinkles and spread into a lined 23 x 33cm tin.
  3. Bake at 180°C and cool completely.
  4. Beat the cream cheese with the remaining butter and icing sugar until fluffy.
  5. Spread over the cake and finish with more sprinkles.

Drinks

Delta Psi tropical rum punchVegetarian

White rum, pineapple juice, passionfruit and lime in a big bowl.

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix and chill the pineapple juice, passionfruit, lime juice and rum up to 1 day ahead; add the lemonade, orange, ice and mint just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L pineapple juice
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 200ml white rum
  • 1 L cold lemonade
  • 2 oranges, sliced
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the pineapple juice, passionfruit pulp, lime juice and rum in a big jug or bowl.
  2. Chill.
  3. Just before serving, add the lemonade, orange slices and plenty of ice. Stir gently and scatter with mint.
