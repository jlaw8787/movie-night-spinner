Snacks

Curly's brisket sliders with slaw

Foil-baked brisket shredded in barbecue sauce on slider buns, with slaw

Prep 30 min, cook 241 min · Make ahead: Brisket up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef brisket
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 12 slider buns
  • salt, to taste
  1. Rub the brisket with paprika, brown sugar, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Wrap tightly in foil.
  3. Bake at 150C until it pulls apart.
  4. Shred with two forks and stir through the barbecue sauce and juices.
  5. Toss the cabbage with mayonnaise and vinegar.
  6. Fill the buns with brisket and slaw.

New York bagel bites

Mini bagels with cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and dill

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best built just before serving; slice the red onion and chop the dill up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 mini bagels
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Split and toast the bagels.
  2. Spread with cream cheese.
  3. Top with smoked salmon, capers, thin red onion and dill.

Mains

Cookie's chuckwagon chilli

Beef chilli with dried chilies, tomatoes and kidney beans, pan cornbread

Prep 20 min, cook 215 min · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 400g can crushed tomatoes
  • 400g can kidney beans, drained
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water and blitz.
  2. Brown the beef in batches.
  3. Add the chillies, cumin, garlic, tomatoes and kidney beans.
  4. Braise until the beef is tender.
  5. Bake the cornbread in a pan and serve with the chilli.

Sweets

Norman's campfire s'mores

Toasted marshmallow and milk chocolate between digestive cookies

Prep 10 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Toast fresh at the fire; snap the chocolate into squares and pack everything the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 16 large marshmallows
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 32 digestive biscuits
  1. Toast the marshmallows on sticks over the fire until blistered.
  2. Sandwich each with a square of milk chocolate between 2 biscuits and press so it melts.

Mitch's fresh coffee with cinnamon creamVegetarianNo alcohol

Boiled coffee with cinnamon, topped with brown sugar cream

Prep 10 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Best brewed fresh; whip the cinnamon cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 80g coarse ground coffee
  • 100ml cold water
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Bring the water to the boil with the cinnamon stick and stir in the coffee.
  2. Boil, then take off the heat and splash in cold water to settle the grounds.
  3. Whip the cream with brown sugar and ground cinnamon to soft peaks.
  4. Pour the coffee carefully into tin mugs, leaving the grounds behind.
  5. Top each with a spoon of the cream.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Cookie sends the chuck wagon over the cliff.
