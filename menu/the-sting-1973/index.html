Snacks

Big con Waldorf saladVegetarian

Apple, celery and walnut Waldorf salad. Hooker wants to learn the big con.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Toast the walnuts and mix the dressing up to a day ahead; cut the apples and toss to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 red apples, diced
  • 3 celery sticks, sliced
  • 1 cup red grapes, halved
  • 80g walnuts
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 butter lettuce, leaves separated
  1. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan until fragrant and roughly chop.
  2. Whisk the mayo, yoghurt and a squeeze of lemon with salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the apple, celery and grapes through the dressing.
  4. Pile onto the lettuce leaves and scatter with walnuts.

Four-threes crab cakes

Crab cakes with mustard mayo. Floyd swears he dealt Shaw four threes.

Prep 5 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Shape the cakes up to a day ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked crab meat
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs, plus 60g for coating
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise, plus 4 tbsp for the remoulade
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1 tbsp chopped gherkins
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Mix the crab, 60g panko, egg, 3 tbsp mayo, mustard and spring onions and shape 12 small cakes.
  2. Coat in the rest of the panko and chill for 10 minutes.
  3. Fry in the oil until golden on both sides.
  4. Stir the rest of the mayo with the capers, gherkins and lemon and serve alongside.

Mains

Lucky Dan roast prime rib

Garlic and thyme prime rib, roasted pink. Place it on Lucky Dan.

Prep 30 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Season the beef the night before and keep it uncovered in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast, 2 bones
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp grated horseradish
  • 800g small potatoes
  1. Rub the beef with the oil, garlic, thyme and plenty of salt and pepper.
  2. Roast on the potatoes at 230C for 20 minutes, then at 160C until rare in the middle.
  3. Rest the beef under foil and keep the potatoes warm.
  4. Deglaze the tin with the stock for a jus and stir the horseradish into the sour cream.
  5. Carve thick slices and serve with the potatoes, jus and horseradish cream.

Lonnegan's packing house pork chops

Seared pork chops with apples. Lonnegan owns a packing house.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Brine the chops up to a day ahead; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 thick bone-in pork chops
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g butter
  • 2 green apples, cut in wedges
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 200ml cloudy apple juice
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 2 sage sprigs
  1. Season the chops and sear in the oil until deep golden on both sides and just cooked, then rest.
  2. Melt the butter and fry the apples, onion and sage until caramelised.
  3. Add the apple juice and mustard and bubble until glossy.
  4. Return the chops with their juices and spoon the apples over.

Loretta's diner apple pieVegetarian

Loretta is only filling in for June

Prep 20 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to a day ahead and warm through to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 6 Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 20g butter, diced
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Line a pie dish with one sheet of pastry.
  2. Toss the apples with the sugar, cornflour and cinnamon, pile into the case and dot with butter.
  3. Lay over the second sheet, crimp, cut steam holes and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 190C until golden and bubbling, then rest.
  5. Serve warm with ice cream.

Gin with the markVegetarian

Always drink gin with the mark

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the tonic well; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml London dry gin
  • 800ml tonic water, chilled
  • 2 limes, cut in wedges
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Fill 4 tumblers with ice and pour 60ml gin into each.
  2. Top with tonic and squeeze in a lime wedge.
