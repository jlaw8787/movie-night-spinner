Snacks

Miami croquetas

Crisp fried ham croquettes with a creamy centre, lime to squeeze over

Prep 21 min, cook 22 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Roll up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 80g plain flour
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200g leg ham
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 2 limes
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until very thick.
  2. Stir in the chopped ham and chill overnight.
  3. Shape into logs, roll in beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches.
  5. Serve with lime.

Tropical fruit skewersVegetarian

Grilled pineapple, mango and papaya with lime honey and chilli salt

Prep 18 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the pineapple, mangoes and papaya into chunks and thread onto skewers.
  2. Grill hot, turning once.
  3. Brush with lime juice mixed with honey and sprinkle with salt and chilli flakes.

Ace's white choc animal crackersOn the printed menu

Honey cinnamon shortbread shapes half dipped in white chocolate with sprinkles, alrighty then

How it is made
  • 150g butter
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 jar honey
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 1 jar rainbow sprinkles
  1. Honey and cinnamon shortbread cut into animal shapes and baked golden, half dipped in white chocolate and showered with rainbow sprinkles. Beat 150g soft butter with 80g caster sugar and 2 tbsp honey. Mix in 250g plain flour and 1 tsp cinnamon to a dough and chill 30 minutes. Roll to 4mm and cut with animal cutters. Bake at 170°C for 10 minutes and cool. Melt 150g white chocolate, dip half of each biscuit and scatter with sprinkles. Set on baking paper.

Mains

Cuban sandwich

Slow roasted citrus pork, ham, Swiss and pickles pressed until crisp

Prep 10 min, cook 4 hours, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate and roast the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pork shoulder
  • 2 oranges
  • 2 limes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long rolls
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g Swiss cheese
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 40g butter
  1. Mix the juice of the oranges and limes, crushed garlic and oregano and marinate the pork overnight.
  2. Marinate in the fridge.
  3. Roast at 160°C, then slice.
  4. Layer the rolls with pork, ham, Swiss cheese, pickles and mustard.
  5. Press in a pan with the butter until crisp.

Game day buffalo wings with blue cheeseOn the printed menu

Dried overnight and baked crisp, hot sauce and butter, blue dip and celery, Super Bowl week

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken wings
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch celery
  1. Wings dried overnight and baked crisp, tossed in hot sauce and melted butter, with a chunky blue cheese dip and cold celery sticks. Pat 1.5kg chicken wings dry, toss with 1 tbsp baking powder and salt and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Bake on a rack at 220°C for 45 minutes, turning once, until crisp. Melt 60g butter with 125ml hot sauce and toss the wings through. Mix 100g crumbled blue cheese with 100g sour cream, 2 tbsp mayo and lemon juice. Serve with celery sticks.

Snowflake's key lime pieVegetarian

Tart lime custard in a cookie crust, topped with whipped cream

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 200g digestive biscuits
  • 80g butter
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 6 limes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a pie tin.
  2. Whisk the condensed milk with the egg yolks, lime zest and lime juice.
  3. Pour in and bake at 160°C.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Top with whipped cream.

Ace's Miami mojitoVegetarian

White rum muddled with mint, lime and sugar over crushed ice, topped with soda

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Cut the limes and pick the mint up to 4 hours ahead; muddle and build in each glass just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 mint sprigs
  • 2 limes
  • 8 tsp caster sugar
  • 180ml white rum
  • 500ml soda water
  • crushed ice, to serve
  1. Cut the limes into wedges.
  2. Gently muddle the mint, lime and sugar in each glass.
  3. Add the rum and fill with crushed ice.
  4. Top with soda water and stir from the bottom.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Ace finds Snowflake in the yacht storage yard.
