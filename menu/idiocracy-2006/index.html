Snacks

Ranch pretzel and peanut snack mix

Pretzels, corn chips, crackers and peanuts baked in ranch butter.

Prep 0 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp dried ranch seasoning
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 150g pretzels
  • 150g corn chips
  • 150g cheese crackers
  • 150g roasted peanuts
  1. Melt the butter with the Worcestershire, ranch seasoning, paprika and garlic powder, 4 min.
  2. Toss with the pretzels, corn chips, crackers and peanuts in a large roasting tin.
  3. Bake at 150°C, stirring every ten minutes, 30 min.
  4. Cool on the tray so it crisps, then tip into a very big bowl, 10 min.

Future cheese fries with seasoned salt

Chicken salted fries with bacon and melted cheese sauce.

Prep 4 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Cook and chop the bacon up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; bake the chips and make the cheese sauce just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen fries
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 4 rashers of bacon
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 tsp American mustard
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp, then toss with chicken salt, 25 min.
  2. Grill the bacon until crisp and chop, 8 min.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and simmer until thick, 5 min.
  4. Stir in the cheddar and mustard until smooth.
  5. Pile the chips on a platter, pour over the cheese sauce and scatter with bacon and spring onion.

Joe's crop field chicken salad bowl

Lemon chicken, grilled corn, romaine, tomato and avocado, buttermilk dressing.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and whisk the dressing up to 2 days ahead; grill and toss the salad on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 100ml buttermilk
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  • 1 small bunch dill, chopped
  • 2 cos lettuces, chopped
  • 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1 avocado
  1. Marinate the chicken in the oil, lemon zest and juice, garlic and oregano, 20 min.
  2. Grill the chicken, turning once, then rest and slice, 14 min.
  3. Grill the corn and cut off the kernels, 8 min.
  4. Whisk the buttermilk, mayonnaise, chives and dill into a dressing.
  5. Toss the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber and avocado, then top with chicken and corn and drizzle with dressing.

Camacho's monster meatball subs

Beef and pork meatballs, provolone, toasted rolls.

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs in their sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, fill and grill the rolls on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 700g tomato passata
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 long sub rolls
  • 8 slices of provolone
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 60g rocket
  1. Mix the minces, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, garlic, oregano and a good pinch of salt, and roll into 16 big meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil all over, 8 min.
  3. Add the passata and simmer 20 minutes, turning now and then.
  4. Split and toast the rolls, fill with meatballs and sauce, top with provolone and grill until melted, 5 min.
  5. Finish with torn basil and rocket.

Sweets

Year 2505 blue raspberry cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes filled with raspberry jam, blue raspberry buttercream.

Prep 22 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 120ml full cream milk
  • 4 tbsp raspberry jam
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g raspberries, mashed
  • 1 bottle blue food colouring
  • 1 jar silver sprinkles
  1. Beat some of the butter with the caster sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide between cupcake cases and bake at 180°C, 18 min.
  3. Cool the cupcakes completely, 15 min.
  4. Cut a small hole in each and fill with jam.
  5. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar, mashed raspberries and blue colouring until fluffy.
  6. Pipe on top and finish with silver sprinkles.

Brawndo lime coolerNo alcohol

Cucumber, mint, lime and orange with soda.

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Blend, strain and chill the green mix up to 1 day ahead; add ice and soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 4 limes, plus extra for wheels
  • 2 oranges
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 600ml cold soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend the cucumber, mint, lime juice, orange juice, honey and a pinch of salt until smooth.
  2. Strain into a jug and chill, 30 min.
  3. Fill tall glasses with ice, pour in the green mix to halfway and top up with soda water.
  4. Stir and garnish with lime wheels.
