Sunrise Studios craft services quesadillasVegetarian

Crisp tortillas filled with cheddar, mozzarella, black beans, corn and jalapeños, with sour cream.

Prep 14 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Mix the filling and mash the beans up to 1 day ahead; fill and cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 cup frozen corn kernels, thawed
  • 3 tbsp pickled jalapeños, chopped
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 8 flour tortillas, 20cm
  • 1/2 cup coriander, chopped
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Roughly mash the beans with the cumin.
  2. Mix the cheddar, mozzarella, corn, jalapeños and spring onions.
  3. Spread the beans over four tortillas, top with the cheese mix and coriander and lid with the other four.
  4. Cook one at a time in a little butter over medium heat, pressing down, until golden on both sides and melted.
  5. Cut into wedges and serve with the sour cream.

Milton's party mushroom vol-au-ventsVegetarian

Garlic mushroom puffs. Roman's birthday party at Milton's mansion.

Prep 8 min, cook 41 min · Make ahead: Bake the cases up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; make the filling up to 1 day ahead, then reheat both and fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 30g butter
  • 300g button mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 16 rounds from the pastry, score a smaller circle in the middle of each without cutting through and brush with the egg.
  2. Bake until risen and golden.
  3. Press the centres down to make cases.
  4. Cook the shallots and garlic in the butter until soft.
  5. Add the mushrooms and thyme and cook until dry.
  6. Stir in the flour, then the cream, and simmer until thick. Finish with the parmesan, salt and pepper.
  7. Fill the cases, scatter with chives and serve hot.

Hollywood studio Cobb salad

Chicken, bacon, egg, avocado. Part three: anyone can die.

Prep 17 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Poach the chicken, boil the eggs, fry the bacon and make the dressing up to 1 day ahead; cut the avocado and build the platter on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts, about 500g
  • 4 eggs
  • 6 bacon rashers
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 90ml olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 baby cos lettuces, chopped
  • 2 avocados, diced
  • 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 100g blue cheese, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Put the chicken in a pan of cold salted water, bring to a gentle simmer, then turn off the heat, cover and leave to finish cooking.
  2. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes and cool in cold water.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp and chop it.
  4. Whisk the vinegar, mustard, honey, garlic, olive oil, salt and pepper.
  5. Dice the chicken and the peeled eggs.
  6. Spread the lettuce on a platter, lay rows of chicken, bacon, egg, avocado, tomato and blue cheese across it, scatter with chives and dress at the table.

Sunrise Studios French dip

Thin-sliced roast beef in soft rolls with hot jus for dipping.

Prep 2 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the jus up to 2 days ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rare roast beef, thinly sliced
  • 4 crusty long rolls
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 20g butter
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Cook the onion in the butter until soft and golden.
  2. Add the stock and Worcestershire and simmer to make the jus.
  3. Warm the beef in a little jus, then pile into the rolls with the cheese and onion and grill until melted.
  4. Spread with mustard and serve with cups of jus for dipping.

Stab 3 fake blood red velvet cupcakesVegetarian

Red velvet cupcakes with a raspberry drip, Stab 3 fake blood.

Prep 20 min, cook 33 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes and make the coulis up to 1 day ahead; frost and drizzle on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 190g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 150g caster sugar, for the cupcakes
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 125g frozen raspberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the coulis
  • 125g cream cheese, softened
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a 12-hole muffin tin.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa, bicarb and salt.
  3. Whisk the sugar, oil, egg, buttermilk, colouring, vanilla and vinegar, then fold in the dry mix.
  4. Fill the cases two-thirds full and bake until springy.
  5. Cool the cupcakes completely.
  6. Simmer the raspberries with the sugar for the coulis, push through a sieve and cool.
  7. Beat the cream cheese, butter and icing sugar until smooth.
  8. Pipe the frosting on the cupcakes and drizzle the coulis down the sides.

100% Cotton lemon drop

Vodka, orange liqueur and fresh lemon shaken ice cold in a sugar-rimmed glass with a lemon twist.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead and chill the glasses; rim and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 50g caster sugar, for the syrup
  • 50ml hot water
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges and twists
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the rims
  • 180ml vodka
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 3 lemons, juiced
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Stir the sugar for the syrup into the hot water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Run a lemon wedge round the rims of four chilled glasses and dip them in the sugar for the rims.
  3. Shake the vodka, orange liqueur, lemon juice and syrup hard with ice, in two batches.
  4. Strain into the glasses and finish with a lemon twist.
