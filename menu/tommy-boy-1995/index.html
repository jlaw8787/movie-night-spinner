Snacks

Helen's fryer-on wings

Hot sauce butter wings, blue cheese dip, celery. Helen turns the fryers back on.

Prep 7 min, cook 50 min, then 11 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry the wings overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 40g butter
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 125g sour cream
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Pat the wings dry and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream for a dip.
  5. Serve with celery sticks.

Tommy's tight coat sausage rolls

Herby pork sausage rolls in pastry a size too small. Fat guy in a little coat.

How it is made
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 3 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 200g tomato relish
  1. Herby pork sausage rolls wrapped in pastry that's a size too small, so the filling bursts out the seams, with smoky tomato relish. Mix 500g pork sausage mince with 1 grated onion, 1 grated carrot, 1 tbsp chopped sage and 40g breadcrumbs. Cut 3 sheets puff pastry in half. Lay a sausage of filling along each, wrap so the edges only just meet and press loosely so they split in the oven. Brush with beaten egg and cut into 4 pieces each. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes. Serve with 200g tomato relish.

Mains

Cincinnati chilli spaghetti

Cinnamon beef chilli on spaghetti with cheddar. Ohio road trip food for Tommy and Richard.

Prep 8 min, cook 112 min · Make ahead: Chilli keeps up to 3 days chilled or 3 months frozen; reheat and cook the spaghetti fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 750ml water
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 15g dark chocolate
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g cheddar, finely grated
  1. Simmer the mince in the water, breaking it up fine.
  2. Add the onion, tomatoes, chilli powder, cinnamon, cumin, allspice, Worcestershire and chocolate.
  3. Simmer until thick.
  4. Cook the spaghetti.
  5. Ladle the chilli over the spaghetti and pile the cheddar on top.

Callahan Auto factory barbecue plate

Sausages and cheeseburgers off the grill, chilli butter cobs, potato salad.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 500g beef mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 40g butter
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 4 burger buns
  1. Beef sausages and cheeseburgers off the grill, with charred corn in chilli butter and a creamy potato salad, the way you feed a factory floor. Boil 1kg washed potatoes until tender, cool, cut up and toss with 150g mayonnaise, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, 2 chopped spring onions and dill. Shape 500g beef mince into 4 patties. Grill 8 thick beef sausages over medium heat for 15 minutes, turning. Grill the patties 4 minutes a side with a cheddar slice on top. Grill 4 corn cobs 10 minutes and brush with 40g butter mixed with chilli flakes. Serve with buns and sauces.

Chocolate peanut butter buckeyesVegetarian

Peanut butter fudge in dark chocolate, for the road trip with Richard.

Prep 18 min, cook 5 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g smooth peanut butter
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 200g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the peanut butter with the butter and icing sugar.
  2. Roll into 24 small balls and chill.
  3. Melt the chocolate.
  4. Dip each ball on a toothpick, leaving a round of peanut butter showing at the top.
  5. Set on baking paper in the fridge.

Diner cherry lattice pieVegetarian

Sweet-sour fruit under a woven top in crisp shortcrust, ice cream, holy schnikes.

Prep 28 min, cook 50 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g frozen cherries, pitted
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 4 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the cherries with most of the sugar, cornflour and lemon juice.
  2. Line a 23cm pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill with the cherries.
  3. Cut the remaining sheets into strips and weave a lattice on top.
  4. Brush with egg and sprinkle with the remaining sugar.
  5. Bake at 190°C until bubbling.
  6. Cool and serve with ice cream.

Drinks

Lemon shandy

Fresh lemon and soda water topped with cold lager.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons and mix with the syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add soda water and lager to pour
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemons
  • 100ml sugar syrup
  • 750ml soda water
  • 1.32 L lager, cold
  1. Juice the lemons and mix the juice with the syrup, then chill.
  2. Chill four pint glasses in the freezer.
  3. Pour some lemon mix into each glass, add soda water, then top slowly with cold lager.
  4. Stir once and add a lemon wheel. Use extra soda water and lemon for anyone driving.

When to serve

Serve the wings when Tommy talks the diner waitress into reopening the kitchen.
