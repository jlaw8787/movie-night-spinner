Austin's first-night hot honey haloumiVegetarian

Seared haloumi with chilli honey, lemon, mint and sesame

Prep 6 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Slice the haloumi up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; warm the honey and sear the haloumi to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g haloumi
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • chilli flakes, to taste
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • sesame seeds, to taste
  1. Slice the blocks of haloumi 1cm thick and pat dry.
  2. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes and the cider vinegar for 2 minutes.
  3. Heat a heavy pan over medium-high heat with the oil and sear the haloumi 1 to 2 minutes a side until deep golden.
  4. Arrange on a platter, drizzle with the hot honey and a squeeze of lemon, and scatter with torn mint and toasted sesame seeds.

Just ask French onion dip and chips

Slow-cooked onion and sour cream dip with potato chips

Prep 13 min, cook 28 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 300g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • garlic powder, to taste
  • Worcestershire sauce, to taste
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 200g thick-cut potato chips
  1. Slowly cook the thinly sliced onions in the butter for 25 minutes until deep golden.
  2. Cool and chop.
  3. Mix with the sour cream, the mayonnaise, a little garlic powder, the Worcestershire, the chopped chives, salt and pepper.
  4. Chill 1 hour so the flavours settle.
  5. Serve with the thick-cut salted potato chips.

Cheap shot rump with chimichurri

Rump steak with parsley chimichurri and smashed roast potatoes

Prep 20 min, cook 61 min · Make ahead: Make the chimichurri up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; roast the potatoes and grill the steak on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg rump steak
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • olive oil, to taste
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • red wine vinegar, to taste
  • dried oregano, to taste
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  1. Boil the baby potatoes 15 minutes, smash on an oiled tray and roast at 220C for 30 minutes.
  2. Chop a bunch of parsley, the garlic cloves and the red chilli and mix with the olive oil, the red wine vinegar and dried oregano.
  3. Season the rump steak and grill on a very hot barbecue 4 minutes a side for medium rare.
  4. Rest 8 minutes, slice and spoon the chimichurri over.
  5. Serve with the potatoes and a dressed green salad.

Play hooky broccolini with whipped fetaVegetarian

Charred broccolini, whipped feta, chilli and lemon

Prep 6 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the feta up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; char the broccolini just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Blend the feta with the yoghurt until smooth and spread over a platter.
  2. Toss the broccolini in oil and salt and char in a very hot pan, turning, until tender.
  3. Pile onto the feta and finish with lemon zest, a squeeze of juice, chilli flakes and a drizzle of oil.

Welcome-back party pavlovasVegetarian

Tall meringue clouds with cream, berries, passionfruit and lemon curd

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min, then 85 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and cool the meringues up to 2 days ahead, airtight; top with cream and fruit just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 220g caster sugar
  • cornflour, to taste
  • white vinegar, to taste
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 150g mixed berries
  • 2 passionfruit
  • 3 tbsp lemon curd
  1. Heat the oven to 120C.
  2. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the caster sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Fold in the cornflour and the white vinegar.
  4. Spoon the tall cloud shapes onto lined trays and bake 50 minutes, then cool in the turned-off oven.
  5. Top with the whipped cream, the berries, the pulp of the passionfruit and a drizzle of lemon curd.

Yellow sports car grapefruit radlerVegetarian

Pale ale with pink grapefruit juice and soda in frosted glasses

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer and the ale in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; mix the grapefruit soda and pour to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 375ml cans pale ale
  • 1 pink grapefruit
  • 1 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 400ml soda water
  • 1 lime
  1. Chill the pint glasses in the freezer for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the juice of the pink grapefruit with the sugar syrup and the cold soda water.
  3. Pour the cold pale ale into each glass, then top with the grapefruit soda.
  4. Add a squeeze of lime and a grapefruit wedge.
  5. For non-drinkers, use ginger beer in place of the ale.
