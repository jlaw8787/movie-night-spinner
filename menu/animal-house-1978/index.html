Snacks

Toga! Toga! party grapesVegetarian

Frozen grapes, olives, feta in oil and warm pita wedges

Prep 11 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the grapes up to 2 days ahead; warm the pita and plate up just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g grapes
  • 200g olives
  • 200g feta
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Freeze the grapes.
  2. Warm the pita and cut into wedges.
  3. Pile the grapes on a big platter with the olives and feta drizzled with oil, and the pita. Eat lying down like a Roman.

Dexter Lake Club wings

Crisp honey barbecue wings, with Otis Day on stage

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • salt, to taste
  • 100ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Pat the wings dry and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Toss with garlic powder and salt.
  3. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  4. Warm the barbecue sauce with the honey and toss the wings through.

Mains

Delta house sloppy joes

Beef, onion and green pepper in tangy tomato sauce on toasted buns

Prep 20 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Make the mince up to 3 days ahead and reheat gently; toast the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 4 soft burger buns
  1. Dice the onion and capsicum.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion and capsicum.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, Worcestershire sauce, sugar and mustard and simmer until thick.
  4. Toast the buns and pile the mince on.

Sweets

Homecoming cake float cupcakesVegetarian

Thank you, sir, may I have another? Iced for the Deathmobile

Prep 24 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp food colouring
  1. Beat 125g of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Spoon into 12 cupcake cases and bake at 175C.
  3. Cool the cupcakes.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar, tint bright colours with the food colouring and swirl on.

Toga party punchVegetarian

Pineapple, orange and lemonade punch, with vodka in the adults' bowl only

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices and slice the orange up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice, lemonade and the vodka to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 750ml lemonade
  • 150ml vodka
  • 1 orange
  • ice, to serve
  1. Slice the orange.
  2. Pour the juices into a big bowl with ice and sliced orange. Top with lemonade.
  3. Add the vodka to the adults' bowl only.
