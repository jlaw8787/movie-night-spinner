Snacks

Tarasco Bar chicharrones

Oven-puffed pork crackling dusted with chilli salt and lime zest.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry overnight, then up to 1 day airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork rind
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 lime
  1. Score the pork rind and rub with salt.
  2. Dry the rind uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  3. Roast at 240°C until puffed and crackling.
  4. Break up and dust with flaky salt mixed with chilli powder and lime zest.

Mariachi guacamoleVegetarian

Smashed avocado with lime, onion, green chilli and coriander, warm corn chips.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Chop the onion, chilli and coriander up to 1 day ahead, chilled; smash the avocado with the lime just before serving so it stays green
Recipe for 4
  • 3 avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 300g corn chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Finely chop the onion, seed and chop the chilli and chop the coriander.
  2. Smash the avocados with lime juice, then stir in the onion, chilli, coriander and salt.
  3. Serve with warm corn chips.

Bookshop jalapeño cheese quesadillas

Monterey Jack, cheddar and pickled chillies in flour tortillas, pan crisped, salsa verde.

How it is made
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 200g Monterey Jack
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  • 1 jar salsa verde
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Flour tortillas filled with Monterey Jack, cheddar and pickled jalapeños, cooked in a dry pan until golden and melted, cut into wedges with salsa verde. Mix 200g grated Monterey Jack, 100g grated cheddar and 3 tbsp chopped pickled jalapenos. Spread over half of each of 4 large flour tortillas and fold over. Cook in a dry frying pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side, pressing down, until golden and the cheese has melted. Rest 1 minute, cut each into 4 wedges and serve with salsa verde and sour cream.

Mains

Bucho's tacos al pastor

Chilli and achiote marinated pork with pineapple in small tortillas.

Prep 13 min, cook 50 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 dried chillies
  • 50g achiote paste
  • 200g fresh pineapple, plus extra to serve
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder, sliced
  • 16 small corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water, then blitz with achiote, pineapple and vinegar.
  2. Coat the sliced pork in the marinade.
  3. Marinate overnight in the fridge.
  4. Roast at 200°C until cooked through and caramelised.
  5. Slice the pork and crisp it in a hot pan.
  6. Serve in the tortillas with pineapple, white onion and coriander.

Chipotle chicken tinga tostadas

Smoky shredded chicken in tomato and onion on crisp shells, crema, from the flat upstairs.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken thighs
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 400g can diced tomatoes
  • 1 can chipotles in adobo
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 can refried beans
  • 2 avocados
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Shredded chicken simmered in a smoky tomato, onion and chipotle sauce, piled on crisp tostadas with refried beans, avocado, crema and pickled onion. Poach 700g chicken thighs in salted water for 20 minutes and shred. Fry 2 sliced onions in oil until soft, add 2 garlic cloves, a 400g can of tomatoes and 2 chopped chipotles in adobo. Simmer 10 minutes, then stir in the chicken. Fry 8 corn tortillas at 180°C until crisp. Spread with warm refried beans, top with the tinga, sliced avocado, sour cream and red onion soaked in lime juice.

Carolina's bookshop tres lechesVegetarian

Sponge cake soaked in three milks and topped with whipped cream.

Prep 35 min, cook 25 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick and pale.
  2. Fold in the flour and pour into a lined tin.
  3. Bake at 175°C until golden and springy.
  4. Prick the cake all over and pour over the condensed, evaporated and full cream milk.
  5. Soak overnight in the fridge.
  6. Whip the cream, spread over the top and dust with cinnamon.

Drinks

Blood orange margarita

Tequila, blood orange, lime and agave shaken hard, chilli salt rim.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Juice the oranges and limes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; salt the rims and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 4 blood oranges
  • 4 limes
  • 60ml agave syrup
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the salt and chilli powder on a saucer.
  2. Wet the rims of four glasses with lime and dip them in the salt.
  3. Juice the blood oranges and limes.
  4. Shake the tequila, juices and agave hard over ice for 15 seconds, in two batches if needed.
  5. Strain over fresh ice and garnish each glass with an orange wheel.
