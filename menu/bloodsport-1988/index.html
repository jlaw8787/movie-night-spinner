Hong Kong egg wafflesVegetarian

Puffy bubble waffles made from custard batter, crisp outside.

Prep 4 min, cook 23 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Whisk the batter up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; cook the waffles just before serving as they go soft quickly
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp custard powder
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 100ml water
  • 1 tbsp oil, for greasing
  1. Whisk the flour, custard powder, baking powder and sugar.
  2. Add the eggs, evaporated milk and water and whisk smooth.
  3. Rest the batter for 30 minutes.
  4. Cook in a hot egg waffle pan brushed with the oil, 3 minutes a side, in batches until crisp and golden.

Kowloon Walled City curry fish balls

Fish balls simmered in coconut curry sauce and served on skewers.

Prep 5 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Make the curry sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; heat it with the fish balls and skewer on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fish balls
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  1. Crush the garlic.
  2. Fry the curry powder and garlic in oil.
  3. Add the coconut milk, sugar and water and simmer, 5 min.
  4. Add the fish balls and simmer until hot through, 10 min.
  5. Thread onto skewers and spoon over the sauce.

Arcade night char siu bao

Fluffy steamed buns with diced roast pork in sticky oyster and hoisin sauce, split on top.

How it is made
  • 300g char siu pork
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 bottle oyster sauce
  • 1 jar hoisin sauce
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 box cornflour
  • 300g bao flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  1. Fluffy steamed buns filled with diced char siu pork in a sticky oyster, hoisin and sugar sauce, steamed until puffed and split open on top. Dice 300g char siu pork. Fry a diced shallot, add 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp hoisin, 1 tbsp sugar and 100ml water, thicken with 1 tsp cornflour and stir in the pork. Cool. Mix 300g bao flour, 5g dried yeast, 30g sugar and 160ml warm water into a soft dough, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Divide into 12, fill, pinch closed and prove 20 minutes. Steam 12 minutes.

Kumite black pepper beef

Wok-fried sirloin steak with onion and peppers in black pepper oyster sauce.

Prep 14 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Slice the steak, onion, capsicum and garlic and marinate the steak in the soy and cornflour up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the rice and stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rump steak
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Slice the steak thinly and slice the onion, capsicum and garlic.
  2. Marinate the steak in soy and cornflour, 20 min.
  3. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  4. Heat the oil in a very hot wok and stir-fry the beef hard, 4 min.
  5. Add the onion, capsicum and garlic.
  6. Finish with oyster sauce and cracked black pepper and serve over the rice.

Shrimp wonton noodle soup

Pork and shellfish dumplings in clear ginger broth, egg noodles, choy sum, a bowl between bouts.

How it is made
  • 1.5L chicken stock
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 packet dried shrimp
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 jar white pepper
  • 200g pork mince
  • 200g green prawns
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 300g thin egg noodles
  • 2 bunches choy sum
  1. Pork and prawn wontons in a clear chicken, ginger and dried shrimp broth with thin egg noodles, blanched choy sum and a drizzle of sesame oil. Simmer 1.5L chicken stock with 4 slices ginger and 1 tbsp dried shrimp for 20 minutes, then season with soy and white pepper. Mix 200g pork mince, 200g chopped green prawns, sesame oil and soy, and fold into 24 wonton wrappers. Boil the wontons for 3 minutes. Cook 300g thin egg noodles for 1 minute and blanch 2 bunches choy sum. Divide into bowls, top with wontons and pour over the hot broth.

Chong Li's egg tartsVegetarian

Puff pastry tarts filled with silky baked egg custard.

Prep 18 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Line the tins and make the custard up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; strain, fill and bake on the night, best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 100ml evaporated milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  1. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a patty tin.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, evaporated milk and sugar until dissolved.
  3. Strain until silky and fill the cases.
  4. Bake at 200°C until just set with a wobble in the centre, 15 min.
  5. Cool a little before serving, 15 min.

Milk teaNo alcohol

Very strong black tea with evaporated milk and sugar, hot or over ice.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Brew the strong tea up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; stir in the evaporated milk and sugar to serve hot or over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 750ml water
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, or to taste
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the tea bags in water until very strong, 5 min.
  2. Strain through a fine cloth or sieve into a jug.
  3. Stir in the evaporated milk and sugar to taste.
  4. Serve hot in cups, or chill and pour over ice.
