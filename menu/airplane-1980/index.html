Snacks

Flight deck snack trayVegetarian

Cheese, crackers, grapes and onions

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Thread the picks up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; lay them out with the crackers when ready
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar
  • 300g green grapes
  • 1 small jar cocktail onions
  • 1 packet crackers
  • 24 toothpicks
  1. Cut the cheddar into cubes.
  2. Thread each cube onto a toothpick with a green grape and a cocktail onion.
  3. Lay the picks out in neat rows on a tray beside a row of crackers.

Airline peanutsVegetarian

Prep 7 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Warm and pack into the paper packets up to 3 days ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dry-roasted peanuts
  • 100g almonds
  • 1 pinch caster sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 4 small paper packets
  1. Mix the peanuts and almonds with the sugar and salt.
  2. Warm on a tray at 160C.
  3. Cool slightly and divide into the paper packets, one for each passenger.

Mains

The steak, not the fish

Seared sirloin in peppercorn cream, mashed potato and steamed green beans

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Boil and mash the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and reheat with a splash of milk; sear the steaks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp black peppercorns
  • 250ml cream
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 80ml milk
  • 300g green beans
  1. Peel and boil the potatoes.
  2. Sear the steaks hard in oil and rest.
  3. Crush the peppercorns into the pan, add the cream and reduce until thick.
  4. Mash the potatoes with butter and milk and steam the beans.
  5. Serve on trays, airline style.

Sweets

Airline cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked cheesecake with sour cream, cut into little squares

Prep 30 min, cook 530 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 250g biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 750g cream cheese, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g sour cream
  • 12 cherries
  1. Mix the biscuits with the butter and press into a lined slab tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs and sour cream and pour over the base.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150C.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Cut into squares and top each with a cherry.

Drinks

Wrong week to quit espresso martiniVegetarian

Vodka, coffee liqueur and fresh espresso shaken over ice

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Brew and cool the espresso up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 shots espresso (about 120 ml)
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 20ml sugar syrup
  • 1 tray ice
  • 12 coffee beans
  1. Brew the espresso and cool.
  2. Shake the espresso, vodka, liqueur and syrup with ice very hard to build the foam.
  3. Strain into chilled coupes and float coffee beans on top.

When to serve

Serve the mains right after the fish course takes out the crew.
