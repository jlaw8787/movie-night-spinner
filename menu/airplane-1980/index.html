Snacks

First class shrimp cocktail

Shrimp on shredded iceberg with tomato and horseradish sauce, row one before the fish course.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to a day ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g cooked prawns
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 1 lemon
  • Worcestershire sauce
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving a few tails on for the rims.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, horseradish, a squeeze of lemon and a dash of Worcestershire.
  3. Shred the iceberg into 4 glasses and pile in the prawns.
  4. Spoon over the sauce and hang a prawn and a lemon wedge on each rim.

Flight deck cheddar and onion puffsVegetarian

Puff pastry squares with slow cooked onion and sharp cheddar, green grapes on the side.

Prep 7 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 20g butter
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 150g sharp cheddar
  • 300g green grapes
  1. Slice the onions thinly and cook slowly in the butter until soft and golden.
  2. Cut each pastry sheet into 6 squares and lay them on a lined tray.
  3. Spoon onion into the middle of each square and top with the grated cheddar.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden, then serve warm with the grapes.

Mains

I had the lasagne

Beef and pork lasagne, the safe choice when dinner is steak or fish.

Prep 25 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the ragu up to 2 days ahead, or build the whole dish the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stick
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 125ml red wine
  • 700ml passata
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 60g butter
  • 60g plain flour
  • 700ml milk
  • 50g parmesan
  • 375g fresh lasagne sheets
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  1. Brown the beef and pork mince, then add the onion, carrot, celery and garlic, all finely diced, 15 min.
  2. Add the red wine, passata and tomato paste and simmer gently, 2 hr.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and cook until thick, then add nutmeg and the parmesan.
  4. Layer ragu, lasagne sheets and bechamel three times in a dish and top with the mozzarella.
  5. Bake at 180°C until bubbling and golden, then rest before cutting.

Sweets

Airline cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked cheesecake with sour cream, cut into little squares.

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 250g biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 750g cream cheese, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g sour cream
  • 12 cherries
  1. Mix the biscuits with the butter and press into a lined slab tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs and sour cream and pour over the base.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150°C, 50 min.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Cut into squares and top each with a cherry.

Ted's drinking problem biscotti

Twice-baked almond and orange with small espressos and cream, crumbs everywhere but his mouth.

How it is made
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 orange
  • 100g whole almonds
  • 4 shots espresso coffee
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Twice-baked almond and orange biscotti, crisp enough to dunk, served with small cups of espresso topped with a spoon of whipped cream. Whisk 2 eggs with 150g caster sugar, then mix in 250g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder, the zest of 1 orange and 100g whole almonds. Shape into 2 logs on a lined tray and bake at 175°C for 25 minutes. Cool 10 minutes, slice 1cm thick on an angle and bake again at 150°C for 10 minutes a side. Brew 4 espressos and top each with a spoon of whipped cream.

Wrong week to quit Black Russian

Vodka and coffee liqueur stirred over ice in short glasses.

Prep 4 min · Make ahead: Chill the vodka and coffee liqueur up to 1 day ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 1 tray ice
  • 12 coffee beans
  1. Fill short glasses with ice.
  2. Pour over the vodka and coffee liqueur and stir.
  3. Finish each glass with coffee beans.
