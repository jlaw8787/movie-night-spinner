Omi's pretzels with obatzda cheese dipVegetarian

Soft baked pretzels with a creamy camembert and paprika dip.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mash the dip with the alcohol-free beer up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; shape and bake the pretzels fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 L water
  • 60g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 200g ripe camembert
  • 50g butter, soft
  • 50g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp alcohol-free beer
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 jar cornichons
  1. Roll the dough into ropes and twist into pretzels.
  2. Dip each in simmering water with the bicarb briefly.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with sea salt and bake at 220°C.
  4. Mash the camembert with the butter, cream cheese, alcohol-free beer, paprika, caraway and onion.
  5. Serve the pretzels warm with the dip and cornichons.

Aunt Dorothy's French onion dip

Slow-cooked onion dip with sour cream.

Prep 7 min, cook 45 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  • 3 tbsp crispy fried shallots
  • 200g kettle chips
  1. Slowly cook the onions in the butter and oil over low heat until jammy and deep brown.
  2. Add the Worcestershire and a splash of water, scrape the pan, then cool.
  3. Mix with the sour cream, cream cheese, most of the chives and salt.
  4. Chill the dip.
  5. Top with crispy shallots and chives and serve with kettle chips.

Table fight lasagne

Beef mince and tomato lasagne layered with white sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night and add 10 minutes to the time
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 700g passata
  • 1 box lasagne sheets
  • 40g butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  1. Brown the mince, add the passata and simmer.
  2. Make a white sauce from the butter, flour and milk.
  3. Layer the meat sauce, lasagne sheets and white sauce in a baking dish and top with mozzarella.
  4. Bake at 180°C until golden and bubbling.

Der Klown fennel and apple saladVegetarian

Shaved fennel, apple, rocket and parmesan. Fresh before the jack in the box opens.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the dressing up to 2 days ahead; shave the fennel and apple and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 fennel bulbs
  • 2 green apples
  • 100g rocket
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  1. Whisk the juice of the lemon with the mustard, olive oil and salt.
  2. Shave the fennel and apples thin and toss straight into the dressing so the apple stays white.
  3. Toss through the rocket and shave the parmesan over the top.

Gingerbread men with icing grinsVegetarian

Ginger cookies with icing teeth, like the one that took Howie Jr.

Prep 27 min, cook 28 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 80g dark brown sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 egg
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 bottle red food colouring
  1. Melt the butter with the golden syrup and sugar. Mix in the flour, bicarb, spices and egg to a soft dough.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll out and cut gingerbread men.
  4. Bake at 180°C, then cool.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with water and pipe jagged teeth. Add red eyes with a little tinted icing.

Omi's apple glühwein (alcohol-free)No alcohol

Hot red wine and apple juice with cinnamon, clove and star anise.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Warm the juices with the tea and spices, then cool and keep chilled up to 2 days ahead; reheat gently to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red grape juice
  • 750ml cloudy apple juice
  • 1 black tea bags
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 2 oranges, sliced
  1. Pour the grape juice and apple juice into a saucepan with the tea bag, sugar, spices and half the orange slices.
  2. Heat gently without letting it simmer, lifting out the tea bag after 5 minutes.
  3. Ladle into mugs with an orange slice each.

Omi's apple glühwein

Hot red wine and apple juice with cinnamon, clove and star anise.

How it is made
  • 750ml red wine
  • 1.5L cloudy apple juice
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 12 cloves
  • 4 star anise
  • 2 oranges
  1. Red wine and cloudy apple juice warmed with cinnamon, cloves, star anise and orange, served steaming in mugs. Plain spiced apple for non-drinkers. Pour 750ml red wine and 500ml cloudy apple juice into a saucepan. Add 3 tbsp brown sugar, 2 cinnamon sticks, 6 cloves, 2 star anise and 1 sliced orange. Heat gently for 15 minutes without letting it simmer. Ladle into mugs with an orange slice each. For non-drinkers, warm 1L apple juice with the same spices in a second pan.

When to serve

Serve the gingerbread when the gingerbread men come to life.
