Snacks

Silo corn frittersVegetarian

Pan fried corn fritters with scallion and chives.

Prep 12 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Stir the chives through the sour cream up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; mix the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 can corn kernels, drained
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  1. Mix the corn with the flour, eggs, spring onion and salt.
  2. Stir the chives through the sour cream for the dip.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in a little oil until golden on both sides, in batches, 12 min.
  4. Serve the fritters warm with the dip.

Spot's foraged berriesVegetarian

Blueberries, raspberries and blackberries with honey and lime, over cream.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; toss the berries with the honey and lime just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g blueberries
  • 125g raspberries
  • 125g blackberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Toss the blueberries, raspberries and blackberries with the honey and a squeeze of lime.
  2. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  3. Spoon the cream into small bowls and top with the berries.

Firefly night sweet potato wedges

Smoky maple sweet potato wedges, for Arlo and Spot watching the fireflies light up the field.

How it is made
  • 1kg sweet potatoes
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Sweet potato wedges roasted with smoked paprika and maple until caramelised at the edges, with a cool lime and chive sour cream dip. Cut 1kg sweet potatoes into wedges and toss with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Spread on 2 lined trays and roast at 220°C for 25 minutes, turning once. Drizzle with 1 tbsp maple syrup and roast 5 minutes more until the edges caramelise. Mix 200g sour cream with the zest and juice of 1 lime and a handful of chopped chives. Serve the wedges hot with the dip.

Rancher's beef ribs

Short ribs rubbed with paprika and pepper, cooked low for five hours.

Prep 12 min, cook 5 hr 22 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Rub the ribs up to 1 day ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; mix the coleslaw up to 4 hours ahead and char the corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1/2 small cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  1. Rub the ribs with smoked paprika, pepper and salt.
  2. Cook at 140°C until tender with a dark crust, 5 hr.
  3. Rest the ribs, 15 min.
  4. Char the corn cobs on a hot grill, turning, 12 min.
  5. Shred the cabbage and grate the carrot, then mix with the mayonnaise and vinegar for a crunchy coleslaw.

Farmhouse bacon and corn chowder

Bacon, onion and potato, kernels and stock, finished with cream and chives, a bowl for Spot.

How it is made
  • 200g bacon
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 500g potatoes
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Bacon, onion and potato softened, corn and stock added and simmered 20 minutes, finished with cream and chives. Fry 200g diced bacon until crisp, then add 1 diced onion and 2 celery sticks and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 500g diced potato, the kernels from 4 corn cobs and 1L chicken stock and simmer 20 minutes until the potato is soft. Blitz a third of the soup and stir it back in to thicken. Add 150ml cream, season and heat through. Serve in bowls with chopped chives and buttered crusty bread.

Harvest cornbreadVegetarian

Buttermilk cornbread baked in a hot cast iron pan.

Prep 4 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Beat the honey butter up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; mix and bake the cornbread on the night so it stays tender
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 50g butter, melted, for the batter
  • 100g butter, soft, for serving
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Heat a greased cast iron pan in the oven, 10 min.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, egg, buttermilk and melted butter until just combined.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake at 220°C, 20 min.
  4. Beat the soft butter with the honey.
  5. Serve wedges with the honey butter.

Farm stand watermelon and mint coolerNo alcohol

Blended watermelon with lime and mint, strained.

Prep 22 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Blitz, strain and chill the watermelon juice up to 1 day ahead; add ice and top with sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg seedless watermelon
  • 2 limes
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 400ml sparkling water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Cut the watermelon flesh into chunks, keeping four wedges for serving.
  2. Blitz the watermelon with the lime juice and mint, then strain into a jug and stir in the honey if the melon is not sweet.
  3. Chill, 30 min.
  4. Fill the jug with ice and top with sparkling water. Pour into glasses with a watermelon wedge and mint sprig each.
