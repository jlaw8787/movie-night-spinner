Psyduck's sweet corn fritters with avocado smashVegetarian

Golden sweet corn fritters, lime avocado smash spooned on top.

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 corn cobs, kernels cut off
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80ml milk
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 50g tasty cheese, grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1 lime
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk into a thick batter, then stir in the corn, spring onion, cheese and a pinch of salt.
  2. Mash the avocados with the lime juice and a little salt.
  3. Fry heaped spoonfuls in the oil until golden, about 2 minutes a side.
  4. Drain and serve with the avocado smash.

Mr Mime's grilled cheese with tomato soup shotsVegetarian

Toasted cheese fingers, little cups of tomato soup.

Prep 6 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 2 days ahead and reheat; toast the sandwiches to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 x 400g tins of crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 60ml pouring cream
  • 8 slices of white sandwich bread
  • 150g tasty cheese, grated
  • 40g soft butter
  1. Soften the onion in the oil, add the tomatoes, stock and sugar and simmer for 15 minutes.
  2. Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and season.
  3. Fill the bread with cheese, butter the outsides and toast in a pan until golden and melted, 8 min.
  4. Cut into fingers and serve with small cups of soup for dipping.

Hi-Hat Cafe fried chicken and waffles with maple butter

Buttermilk fried chicken on crisp waffles, maple butter melting over.

Prep 13 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in buttermilk overnight; make the waffle batter up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken tenderloins
  • 375ml buttermilk, plus 250ml for the waffles
  • 150g plain flour, plus 200g for the waffles
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g butter, melted, plus 60g soft
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup, plus extra to serve
  1. Soak the chicken in 375ml buttermilk with a pinch of salt for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Whisk the waffle flour, baking powder, eggs, 250ml buttermilk and melted butter and cook in a hot waffle iron until crisp, 15 min.
  3. Toss the chicken in the flour mixed with paprika, garlic powder and salt, and fry at 175°C until golden and cooked through, 15 min.
  4. Beat the soft butter with the maple syrup.
  5. Stack the chicken on the waffles, top with maple butter and pour over extra syrup.

Charizard's flame-grilled beef skewers with rice

Sticky soy beef skewers, rice, crunchy slaw.

Prep 18 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Marinate the beef up to a day ahead; thread and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rump steak, in cubes
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red capsicum, in chunks
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 lime
  • 8 bamboo skewers, soaked
  1. Mix the soy, honey, sesame oil and garlic, toss with the beef and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  3. Thread the beef and capsicum onto the skewers.
  4. Grill on a hot barbecue or grill pan, turning, until charred and sticky, brushing with the leftover marinade, 8 min.
  5. Toss the cabbage and carrot with lime juice and serve with the skewers and rice.

Sweet Mother of Arceus lemon sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, lemon curd, honeycomb bolts.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Build to order; everything keeps in the pantry and freezer
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 250g lemon curd
  • 100g honeycomb, broken into shards
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 8 plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  1. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  2. Layer crushed biscuits, ice cream and lemon curd in 4 tall glasses.
  3. Top with cream and stick in shards of honeycomb like lightning bolts.

Pikachu's piping hot mochaNo alcohol

Espresso, hot chocolate and steamed milk, kids skip the coffee.

Prep 3 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 shots of espresso, or 4 tsp instant coffee
  • 4 tbsp drinking chocolate
  • 750ml milk
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  • 1 tsp cocoa powder
  1. Heat the milk until steaming and whisk in the drinking chocolate, 5 min.
  2. Pour a shot of espresso into each grown-up mug, top up with the chocolate milk and finish with cream and a dusting of cocoa.
