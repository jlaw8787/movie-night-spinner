Snacks

Diamond mine cheese gemsVegetarian

Cheddar, gouda and brie cubes on toothpicks with red and green grapes.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cheddar, cubed
  • 150g gouda, cubed
  • 150g brie, cubed
  • 300g green grapes
  • 300g red grapes
  1. Cut the cheddar, gouda and brie into small cubes.
  2. Thread onto toothpicks with red and green grapes, alternating colours.
  3. Pile them high on a dark wooden board and serve at room temperature.

Honey rosemary roasted nutsVegetarian

Walnuts, hazelnuts and almonds roasted with honey and rosemary.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g walnuts
  • 100g hazelnuts
  • 100g almonds
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  1. Toss the walnuts, hazelnuts and almonds with honey, rosemary and salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170°C, stirring once.
  3. Cool completely, then break into clusters.

Cottage garden veg with dill yoghurt dip

Baby carrots, radishes, cucumber and snow peas in little jars around a cool lemon garlic dip.

How it is made
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 bunch baby carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 150g snow peas
  1. Baby carrots, radishes, cucumber and snow peas standing in little jars around a cool dip of Greek yoghurt, dill, lemon and garlic. Mix 300g Greek yoghurt with 2 tbsp chopped dill, the zest and juice of half a lemon, 1 small crushed garlic clove, 1 tbsp olive oil and salt, then chill. Trim 1 bunch baby carrots, leaving a little green on top. Halve 1 bunch radishes, cut 1 cucumber into sticks and string 150g snow peas. Stand the vegetables upright in small jars around the bowl of dip.

Mains

Dwarfs' supper soup

Chicken thighs, leeks and potatoes simmered with thyme.

Prep 29 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 500g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 600g potatoes, diced
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 1.5 L water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 loaf of crusty bread
  1. Soften the leeks in the butter.
  2. Add the chicken, potatoes, thyme, water and salt.
  3. Simmer until the potatoes break down.
  4. Stir in the cream and serve in big bowls with torn bread.

Cottage herb roast chicken and veg

Whole bird with lemon over potatoes and carrots that soak up the juices, carved at table.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 lemon
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Whole chicken roasted with lemon and herbs on a bed of potatoes and carrots that soak up the juices, carved at a long wooden table. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with 40g butter, salt, thyme and rosemary and put half a lemon inside. Toss 1kg potato chunks and 500g carrots with oil and salt in a roasting tin and sit the chicken on top. Roast at 200°C for 75 minutes until the juices run clear and the vegetables are golden. Rest 10 minutes, carve and spoon the pan juices over everything.

Sweets

Snow White's gooseberry pieVegetarian

Gooseberries cooked with sugar under a pastry lid.

Prep 30 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g gooseberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Cook the gooseberries with sugar and cornflour until thick, then cool.
  2. Line a pie dish with pastry, fill and cover with a pastry lid.
  3. Cut slits, brush with milk and bake at 190°C.
  4. Rest and serve warm with cream.

Drinks

Shiny red apple and cinnamon punchNo alcohol

Apple juice simmered with cinnamon, cloves and orange peel, served chilled.

Prep 0 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add ice and sparkling juice at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks, plus 4 to serve
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 750ml sparkling apple juice
  • 1 red apple, thinly sliced
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the apple juice with the cinnamon, cloves and orange peel.
  2. Cool and chill, then strain into a jug.
  3. Just before serving, add plenty of ice and top with sparkling apple juice.
  4. Float apple slices on top and serve with a cinnamon stick each.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Snow White bakes her pie for Grumpy.
