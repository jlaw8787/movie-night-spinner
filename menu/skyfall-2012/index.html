Snacks

Macau casino dumplings

Steamed pork and prawn dumplings with ginger and shiitake

Prep 40 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Uncooked dumplings up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns, peeled and chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 shiitake mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 50g salmon roe
  1. Mix the pork, prawns, ginger, mushrooms and soy.
  2. Spoon into the wonton wrappers, wet the edges and pinch up the sides.
  3. Steam in batches for 8 minutes. Top each with a dot of roe.

Highland oatcakes and smoked salmon

Crisp oatcakes topped with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and dill

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Oatcakes up to 2 days ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g rolled oats
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the oats with the melted butter, salt and a splash of hot water into a stiff dough.
  2. Press thin and cut into rounds.
  3. Bake at 180C for 20 minutes and cool.
  4. Top with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, dill and a squeeze of lemon.

Mains

Skyfall lodge venison

Juniper rubbed venison backstrap in whisky redcurrant sauce, with mash

Prep 35 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Boil and mash the swede up to 1 day ahead and reheat; roast the potatoes and cook the venison and sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g venison backstrap
  • 1 tbsp juniper berries, crushed
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 60ml Scotch whisky
  • 2 tbsp redcurrant jelly
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 600g swede, peeled and chopped
  • 50g butter
  • 800g potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Roast the potatoes with the oil at 200C until golden and crisp.
  2. Boil the swede until soft, drain and mash with the butter.
  3. Rub the venison with juniper, thyme and pepper. Sear in a hot pan until browned all over, then rest.
  4. Add the whisky, redcurrant jelly and stock to the pan and reduce to a glossy sauce.
  5. Slice the venison and serve with the mash, potatoes and sauce.

Sweets

CranachanVegetarian

Whipped honey whisky cream layered with raspberries and toasted oats

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 30ml Scotch whisky
  • 300g raspberries
  1. Toast the oats in a dry pan until nutty, then cool.
  2. Whip the cream with the honey and whisky to soft peaks.
  3. Lightly crush the raspberries.
  4. Layer cream, raspberries and oats in four glasses, finishing with oats on top.

Drinks

Silva's island Scotch penicillinVegetarian

Scotch, lemon, honey and ginger syrup shaken and served over ice

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g honey
  • 60ml water
  • 1 piece ginger, 4cm, sliced
  • 200ml blended Scotch whisky
  • 80ml lemon juice
  • 40ml peated single malt whisky
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 4 pieces candied ginger
  1. Simmer the honey, water and sliced ginger for 5 minutes, then cool and strain.
  2. Shake the blended Scotch, lemon juice and honey ginger syrup with ice, in batches, and strain over a large ice cube in each tumbler.
  3. Float the peated whisky on top and garnish with candied ginger.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Bond drives M to Skyfall in the Aston Martin DB5.
