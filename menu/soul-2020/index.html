Snacks

Barber shop bagel bites

Mini bagels with cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and red onion.

Prep 8 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Slice the red onion up to 1 day ahead; assemble just before serving so the bagels stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 12 mini bagels
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  1. Split and toast the bagels.
  2. Spread with cream cheese.
  3. Top each with a fold of smoked salmon, a few capers and thin slices of red onion.
  4. Lay them out on a tray.

Half Note smoky nuts and warm olives

Almonds and cashews roasted with smoked paprika and honey, olives warmed with orange and rosemary.

How it is made
  • 150g almonds
  • 150g cashews
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  • 250g mixed olives
  • 1 orange
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  1. Almonds and cashews roasted with smoked paprika, honey and salt, served warm beside olives heated through with orange peel, garlic and rosemary. Toss 150g almonds and 150g cashews with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp smoked paprika and 1 tsp sea salt. Roast on a lined tray at 170°C for 12 minutes, stirring once, and cool for 5 minutes so they crisp. Warm 250g mixed olives in a small pan with 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 strips orange peel, 2 smashed garlic cloves and a rosemary sprig for 5 minutes. Serve both in small bowls.

Mains

New York pizza sliceVegetarian

Thin crust cheese pizza with tomato sauce and mozzarella.

Prep 23 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Best baked just before serving; grate the mozzarella and parmesan up to 1 day ahead, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200ml pizza sauce
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  1. Heat the oven and a tray to 250°C.
  2. Stretch the dough as thin as you can into 2 big rounds.
  3. Spread with sauce and mozzarella.
  4. Bake on the very hot tray until blistered, one pizza at a time.
  5. Dust with oregano and parmesan, cut into big slices and fold to eat.

Libba's lemon roast chicken and mac cheese

Whole bird roasted with zest and thyme, baked macaroni and garlicky sautéed silverbeet.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 80g butter
  • 300g macaroni
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 250g cheddar
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 bunch silverbeet
  • 3 garlic cloves
  1. Chicken roasted with lemon and thyme at 200°C for 75 minutes, served with baked macaroni cheese and garlicky sautéed silverbeet. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with butter, salt and thyme, put a halved lemon inside and roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Cook 300g macaroni until just al dente. Melt 40g butter, stir in 3 tbsp plain flour, whisk in 600ml milk until thick and stir in 200g grated cheddar. Mix with the pasta, tip into a dish, top with 50g more cheddar and breadcrumbs and bake 20 minutes alongside the chicken. Sauté a bunch of silverbeet with garlic and oil to serve.

Homemade boiled lollipopsVegetarian

Hard boiled sugar lollipops poured in little rounds.

Prep 0 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight and away from humidity; nothing is left to do on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 80ml glucose syrup
  • 80ml water
  • food colouring, a drop
  • 12 lollipop sticks
  1. Boil the sugar, glucose syrup and water to 150°C without stirring.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in a drop of colouring.
  3. Pour small rounds onto baking paper and press a stick into each.
  4. Leave to harden.

Maple seed cookiesVegetarian

Brown sugar cookies with maple syrup and chopped pecans.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 egg
  • 200g plain flour
  • 80g pecans, chopped
  1. Cream the butter, brown sugar and maple syrup, then beat in the egg.
  2. Fold in the flour and pecans.
  3. Roll into balls, flatten slightly and lay on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 180°C.
  5. Cool on the tray.

New York chocolate egg creamNo alcohol

Chocolate syrup, cold milk and soda water, stirred hard to a foam.

Prep 8 min, cook 3 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and chill the chocolate syrup up to 5 days ahead, covered in the fridge; add the milk and soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cocoa
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 125ml water
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 500ml very cold full cream milk
  • 600ml cold soda water
  1. Whisk the cocoa, sugar and water over low heat until smooth, then stir in the vanilla.
  2. Chill the syrup.
  3. Spoon syrup into tall glasses and add the milk.
  4. Pour in the soda water quickly while stirring hard with a long spoon until a thick foam rises. Serve straight away.
