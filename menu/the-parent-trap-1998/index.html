Napa vineyard cheese boardVegetarian

Cheddar, brie and goat's cheese with grapes, fig jam and walnuts.

Prep 40 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Toast the walnuts up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; take the cheeses out of the fridge 30 minutes before and arrange on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g aged cheddar
  • 150g brie
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 500g red grapes
  • 4 tbsp fig jam
  • 100g walnuts
  • 1 box crackers
  1. Bring the cheddar, brie and goat's cheese to room temperature.
  2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan, 6 min.
  3. Arrange the cheeses on a wooden board with grapes, fig jam, walnuts and crackers.

Martin's cucumber sandwichesVegetarian

Crustless white bread with butter, cucumber and dill, in honour of Martin the family butler.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 hours ahead under a damp tea towel in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1 telegraph cucumber
  • 12 slices of soft white bread
  • 80g butter, soft
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Slice the cucumber very thinly, salt lightly and leave in a sieve for 10 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Beat the butter and cream cheese with chopped dill, a squeeze of lemon and pepper.
  3. Spread over all the bread, layer cucumber on half and close.
  4. Cut off the crusts and cut each sandwich into three fingers.

Beef fillet with potato gratin

Roast beef fillet, potato bake and gravy for the yacht dinner.

Prep 15 min, cook 1 hr 21 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Assemble the gratin up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, and add 10 minutes to the bake; sear and roast the beef on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 300g green beans
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the potatoes thinly and layer with cream and garlic in a dish.
  2. Bake the potatoes at 180°C, 1 hr.
  3. Season the beef with salt and pepper and sear all over in the oil, 6 min.
  4. Roast the beef at 200°C alongside the potatoes until medium-rare.
  5. Rest the beef, 10 min.
  6. Deglaze the pan with beef stock and reduce until glossy, 10 min.
  7. Cook the green beans, 5 min.
  8. Serve by candlelight on a boat.

Camping trip sausages with fried onions

Thick beef sausages in soft rolls with sticky onion, mustard and beans, campout supper.

How it is made
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 3 onions
  • 20g butter
  • brown sugar
  • 8 soft rolls
  • American mustard
  • baked beans
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Thick beef sausages grilled on the barbecue, tucked into soft rolls with sticky fried onions and mustard, smoky baked beans on the side. Slice 3 onions and cook slowly in 2 tbsp oil and 20g butter for 20 minutes until golden and sticky, adding a pinch of brown sugar at the end. Grill 8 thick beef sausages on a medium barbecue for 12 minutes, turning often. Warm 2 tins baked beans with 1 tsp smoked paprika. Split 8 soft rolls, add a sausage, pile on onions and finish with mustard. Serve the beans on the side.

London afternoon tea sconesVegetarian

Light scones made with cream and lemonade, baked close together.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, keep airtight and rewarm at 160°C for 5 minutes before serving with jam and clotted cream
Recipe for 4
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 300ml lemonade
  • 120g strawberry jam
  • 150g clotted cream
  1. Mix the flour, cream and lemonade until just combined.
  2. Pat out and cut into rounds.
  3. Bake close together at 220°C until risen and golden, 15 min.
  4. Cool slightly, 5 min.
  5. Serve warm with jam and clotted cream, 3 min.

Eton messVegetarian

Strawberries, whipped cream and broken meringue folded together.

Prep 19 min · Make ahead: Hull the strawberries and make the sauce up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; whip the cream and assemble just before serving so the meringue stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 6 meringue nests
  1. Hull the strawberries.
  2. Blitz some of the strawberries with the sugar into a sauce and quarter the rest, 3 min.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Fold in the broken meringue nests and the quartered strawberries.
  5. Spoon into glasses and drizzle with the sauce.

Camp Walden chocolate cookies and PB dip

Chocolate cookies with peanut butter dip.

How it is made
  • 200g smooth peanut butter
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 60ml full cream milk
  • chocolate cream biscuits
  • 150g salted pretzels
  1. Chocolate cream sandwich biscuits with a whipped peanut butter and honey dip, salted pretzels on the side for the sweet and salty dunk. Beat 200g smooth peanut butter with 100g softened cream cheese, 2 tbsp honey, a pinch of salt and 60ml milk until light and fluffy. Spoon into a bowl and swirl a little extra honey through the top. Pile 2 packets of chocolate cream sandwich biscuits and 150g salted pretzels on a platter around the dip and let everyone dunk.

Napa Valley grape and rosemary sodaNo alcohol

Red grape juice with rosemary syrup and frozen grapes.

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min, 2 hr 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 6 sprigs of rosemary
  • 300g red grapes
  • 600ml chilled red grape juice
  • 1 lime
  • 320ml sparkling water
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and rosemary sprigs, 3 min.
  2. Cool and strain the syrup, 20 min.
  3. Freeze the grapes, 2 hr.
  4. Mix the grape juice with the rosemary syrup and lime juice.
  5. Drop frozen grapes into stemmed glasses, pour in the juice and top with sparkling water. Finish with a small rosemary sprig.
