Snacks

Pan cornbreadVegetarian

Buttered cornbread. "Wouldst thou like the taste of butter?"

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best straight from the oven; mix the dry ingredients up to 1 day ahead and whisk in the wet just before baking
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 60g butter, melted, for the batter
  • 80g butter, soft, for serving and pan
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Heat a buttered cast iron pan in a 220C oven.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, buttermilk, egg and melted butter briefly.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake.
  4. Beat soft butter with the honey and serve alongside.

Woodland apples and cheeseVegetarian

Sliced red apples with sharp aged cheddar and toasted walnuts

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Toast the walnuts up to 2 days ahead, airtight; slice the apples and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 red apples
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 80g walnuts
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the apples thin and toss in lemon juice.
  2. Toast the walnuts at 170C.
  3. Fan the apples on a board with sliced cheddar.
  4. Scatter over the walnuts and drizzle with honey.

Farmhouse baked eggs with soldiersOn the printed menu

Set in ramekins over cream, spinach and bacon, sourdough fingers to dip, from the farm hens

How it is made
  • 100g bacon
  • 150g baby spinach
  • 20g butter
  • 60ml cream
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 4 slices sourdough
  1. Eggs baked in buttered ramekins over cream, wilted spinach and smoky bacon, with thyme and cracked pepper, and buttered sourdough soldiers to dip. Fry 100g diced bacon until crisp. Wilt 150g spinach in the pan and squeeze dry. Butter 4 ramekins and divide the bacon and spinach between them. Add 1 tbsp cream to each, crack in an egg, season and add a pinch of thyme. Stand in a roasting tin with hot water halfway up and bake at 180C for 12 to 14 minutes until the whites set. Serve with 4 slices buttered sourdough toast cut into soldiers.

Mains

Black Phillip's goat stew

Goat or lamb braised with onion and thyme. Phillip would not approve.

Prep 28 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg goat or lamb shoulder, in big pieces
  • 2 brown onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Brown the goat or lamb in oil in batches.
  2. Soften the onions, carrots and garlic.
  3. Add the stock, thyme and bay leaves.
  4. Cover and braise at 160C until it falls apart.
  5. Serve with crusty bread.

Colonial ham hock and barley pottageOn the printed menu

Barley, split peas and leek in smoky stock with pulled meat, for a New England winter

How it is made
  • 150g pearl barley
  • 150g green split peas
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 2 leeks
  • 1 turnip
  • 2 parsnips
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 50g butter
  1. A thick farmhouse pottage of pearl barley, split peas, leek and root vegetables simmered in smoky ham hock stock, with the meat pulled back through. Rinse 150g pearl barley and 150g green split peas. Simmer 1 smoked ham hock in 2.5L water with bay leaves for 90 minutes. Lift out the hock, add the barley and peas and simmer 40 minutes. Add 2 sliced leeks, 1 diced turnip, 2 diced parsnips and 2 carrots and simmer 25 minutes more. Shred the ham back in, season with pepper and parsley, and serve with crusty bread and butter.

Sweets

Baked applesVegetarian

Apples baked in butter, sugar and dates. Mother wished for one.

Prep 16 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Core and fill the apples up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; add the water and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 apples
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 60g dates, chopped
  • 300ml pouring cream
  1. Core the apples.
  2. Mix the butter, sugar, cinnamon and dates and fill each apple.
  3. Add a splash of water to the dish.
  4. Bake at 180C, basting twice.
  5. Serve with pouring cream.

Drinks

Mulled ciderVegetarianNo alcohol

Apple juice simmered with cinnamon, cloves, star anise and orange, no alcohol

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Simmer up to 2 days ahead, strain and chill; reheat gently to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 3 strips orange peel
  1. Put the juice, cinnamon sticks, cloves, star anise and orange peel in a saucepan.
  2. Simmer gently.
  3. Strain and ladle into four mugs. Drink it by the fire with the door shut.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Black Phillip speaks to Thomasin.
