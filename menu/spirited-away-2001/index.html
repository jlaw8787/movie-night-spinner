Haku's onigiri with pickled plumVegetarian

Salted rice balls, umeboshi, nori. Haku gives Chihiro rice balls.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered; shape and wrap to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g Japanese short-grain rice
  • 500ml water
  • 4 umeboshi pickled plums, stoned
  • 2 sheets of nori, cut in strips
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook it with the water, covered, on low heat.
  2. Rest the rice for 10 minutes off the heat, then spread it out to cool a little.
  3. Wet and salt your hands, take a handful of rice, push a piece of plum into the middle and press into a triangle.
  4. Wrap a strip of nori around the base of each and sprinkle with sesame.

No-Face's feast sushi rolls

Salmon and cucumber rolls, egg rolls, soy and pickled ginger. No-Face pays the staff in gold.

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cook and season the rice up to 4 hours ahead; roll and slice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sushi rice
  • 60ml sushi seasoning
  • 4 sheets of nori
  • 150g sashimi-grade salmon, in strips
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, in strips
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 50g pickled ginger
  1. Cook the sushi rice, then fold through the seasoning and cool to room temperature, 25 min.
  2. Beat the eggs with the sugar, cook into a thin omelette and cut into strips, 5 min.
  3. Lay a nori sheet on a bamboo mat, spread with rice, line up salmon and cucumber or egg, and roll up tightly.
  4. Slice each roll into 8 with a wet knife and serve with soy and ginger.

Spirit town char siu pork buns

Sticky char siu pork, soft steamed buns, cucumber.

Prep 10 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Roast the pork up to 2 days ahead; slice and warm with the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork neck fillet
  • 80ml char siu sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 12 frozen steamed bao buns
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 3 spring onions, shredded
  • 60ml hoisin sauce
  1. Coat the pork in the char siu sauce and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Roast on a rack at 200°C for 35 minutes, then brush with honey and soy and roast 10 minutes more until sticky.
  3. Rest, then slice thinly, 5 min.
  4. Steam the buns until soft and puffy.
  5. Fill each bun with hoisin, pork, cucumber and spring onion.

Kamaji's boiler room chicken yakitori

Chicken and spring onion skewers, sweet soy glaze, rice. Kamaji bribes Lin with a roasted newt.

Prep 12 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Thread the skewers and make the glaze up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, in cubes
  • 6 spring onions, in 3cm lengths
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 60ml apple juice
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 300g Japanese short-grain rice
  • 12 bamboo skewers, soaked
  1. Simmer the soy, apple juice, sugar and ginger until syrupy, then cool.
  2. Cook the rice, 20 min.
  3. Thread the chicken and spring onion onto the skewers.
  4. Grill over high heat, turning and brushing with the glaze, until charred and cooked through.
  5. Serve on the rice with the rest of the glaze.

Lin's late-night red bean bunsVegetarian

Soft steamed buns, sweet red bean paste.

Prep 45 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Shape the buns up to a day ahead and chill on paper squares; steam to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp instant dried yeast
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 170ml warm milk
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g sweetened red bean paste
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, baking powder and sugar, then add the milk and oil and knead to a smooth dough.
  2. Cover and leave somewhere warm until puffy.
  3. Divide into 10, flatten each, wrap around a spoonful of bean paste and pinch closed.
  4. Sit each on a square of baking paper and steam over boiling water until puffed and springy.

Zeniba's iced barley tea with honey and yuzuNo alcohol

Roasted barley tea, honey, a squeeze of yuzu. Zeniba makes everyone some tea.

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 roasted barley tea bags (mugicha)
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp yuzu juice or lemon juice
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 5 minutes, then remove and stir in the honey.
  2. Cool, add the yuzu and chill, 3 min.
  3. Pour over ice to serve.
