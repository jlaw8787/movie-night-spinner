Snacks

Montana camp jerky

Chewy beef jerky marinated in soy, Worcestershire and garlic, oven dried. A desire for more cows.

Prep 30 min, then 17 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry within a day of marinating, keeps 1 week airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef topside
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 3 cloves garlic
  1. Freeze the beef for an hour.
  2. Slice very thin across the grain.
  3. Mix the soy, Worcestershire, pepper and crushed garlic and coat the beef.
  4. Marinate overnight.
  5. Lay on racks over trays.
  6. Dry in the oven at 75C until chewy.

Canary seed crackersVegetarian

Crunchy sunflower, pepita, linseed and chia crackers with soft goat cheese

Prep 13 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 60g sunflower seeds
  • 60g pepitas
  • 60g linseeds
  • 60g sesame seeds
  • 40g chia seeds
  • 250ml water
  • 150g soft goat's cheese
  1. Mix the sunflower seeds, pepitas, linseeds, sesame seeds and chia seeds with the water.
  2. Rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Spread thin on a lined tray.
  4. Bake at 160C until crunchy.
  5. Break into shards and serve with goat's cheese.

Logogram parmesan and black sesame crispsOn the printed menu

Grated hard cheese and seeds baked into lacy rings, each splashed with squid ink mayo

How it is made
  • 150g parmesan
  • 1 packet black sesame seeds
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 sachet squid ink
  1. Parmesan and black sesame spooned into rings and baked into lacy crisps, each splattered with a little squid ink mayo like a heptapod logogram. Mix 150g finely grated parmesan with 2 tbsp black sesame seeds. Spoon into 16 thin rings about 8cm wide on baking paper, leaving the middles open. Bake at 190C for 6 minutes until golden and lacy, then cool on the tray to crisp. Mix 4 tbsp mayonnaise with 1 tsp squid ink. Flick or pipe the black mayo around each crisp in a rough circle just before serving.

Mains

Abbott and Costello camp chilli

Beef chuck simmered 3 hours with chipotles, tomatoes and kidney beans

Prep 47 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 400g can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 2 chipotles in adobo, chopped
  • 800g cans kidney beans, drained
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the beef in batches.
  2. Add the tomatoes, cumin, chipotles and water to cover.
  3. Simmer until it falls apart.
  4. Add the kidney beans for the last 30 minutes.
  5. Ladle into bowls with cheddar.

Lake house dill salmon, new potatoesOn the printed menu

Fillets roasted on lemon slices with oil, crushed spuds and cucumber crème fraîche, for Hannah

How it is made
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 150g crème fraîche
  1. Salmon fillets roasted on lemon slices with dill and olive oil, with crushed new potatoes and a cucumber, crème fraîche and dill salad. Boil 800g new potatoes until tender, then crush lightly with butter and salt. Lay 2 sliced lemons in a tray, top with 4 salmon fillets, drizzle with olive oil and scatter with dill and salt. Roast at 200C for 12 minutes until just cooked. Slice 2 Lebanese cucumbers thinly and toss with 150g crème fraîche, chopped dill, a squeeze of lemon and salt. Serve together.

Sweets

Circle in time pavlovaVegetarian

Ring-shaped meringue filled with whipped cream and blackberries

Prep 20 min, cook 100 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Meringue up to 2 days airtight, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g blackberries
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Pipe into a large ring on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120C.
  4. Cool in the oven.
  5. Whip the cream and melt the chocolate.
  6. Fill with cream and blackberries and drizzle chocolate around the edge.

Long night teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong Earl Grey with honey and lemon, served hot or over ice

Prep 5 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Brew up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge to serve iced, or reheat for mugs
Recipe for 4
  • 4 earl grey tea bags
  • 1l freshly boiled water
  • 4 tbsp honey, to taste
  • 2 lemons
  1. Brew the tea bags in freshly boiled water until strong.
  2. Stir in honey to taste.
  3. Add a slice of lemon to each cup.
  4. Serve hot in big mugs, or chill and pour over ice.
