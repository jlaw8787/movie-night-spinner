Snacks

Miniature sandwiches

Tiny egg and cress, cucumber cream cheese and ham Dijon sandwiches.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the egg filling up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; assemble up to 3 hours ahead, covered with a damp tea towel
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp cress
  • 12 slices of white bread
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 100g leg ham
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  1. Boil the eggs, cool, peel and mash with the mayonnaise and cress, 15 min.
  2. Use the slices of white bread to make egg sandwiches, cucumber and cream cheese sandwiches, and ham and Dijon sandwiches.
  3. Cut off the crusts and cut into exact little squares.
  4. Stand them up in rows.

Charlie's nut-free seed crackersVegetarian

Sunflower, pumpkin, sesame and chia crackers.

Prep 27 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Crackers up to 1 week, airtight; blitz the beetroot dip up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g sunflower seeds
  • 80g pumpkin seeds
  • 80g sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp chia seeds
  • 250ml water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250g cooked beetroot
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  1. Mix the seeds, chia, water and salt and stand, 15 min.
  2. Spread thin on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 160°C, 50 min.
  4. Break up.
  5. Blitz the beetroot with the yoghurt for the dip.

Dollhouse miniature cheese board

Tiny squares of cheddar, brie and blue set out to scale, quince paste and honeyed walnuts.

How it is made
  • 80g walnuts
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 150g aged cheddar
  • 150g brie
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 100g quince paste
  • 1 bunch grapes
  • lavash crackers
  1. Tiny squares of aged cheddar, brie and blue arranged like rooms on a board, with mini lavash crackers, quince paste, grapes and honeyed walnuts. Toss 80g walnuts with 1 tbsp honey and a pinch of salt and bake at 180°C for 8 minutes, then cool. Cut 150g aged cheddar, 150g brie and 100g blue cheese into small neat squares. Arrange them in a grid of little rooms on a large board, with 100g quince paste cut into cubes, 1 small bunch grapes, the walnuts and a packet of mini lavash crackers in the hallways.

Mains

Creamy chicken and mushroom casserole

Chicken thighs, mushrooms and leeks in a white wine cream sauce.

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the chicken filling up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; cover with pastry, brush with egg and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, diced
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 150ml white wine
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Brown the chicken in oil, 10 min.
  2. Soften the mushrooms and leeks.
  3. Add the wine and cream and simmer until thick, 15 min.
  4. Tip into a dish, cover with puff pastry and brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden, 30 min.

The family dinner roast chicken

Dry-brined bird, crisp skin, roast potatoes, greens and lemon thyme gravy, served in silence.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 300g green beans
  • 20g butter
  1. Dry-brined roast chicken with crisp skin, roast potatoes, buttered greens and a sharp lemon and thyme pan gravy, served at a very quiet table. Salt a 1.8kg chicken all over and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then 180°C for 45 to 50 minutes until the thigh hits 75°C, and rest 15 minutes. Parboil 1kg potatoes for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in 60ml oil at 220°C for 45 minutes. Make gravy with the juices, 1 tbsp flour, 300ml stock and lemon. Steam 300g green beans with butter.

Nut-free chocolate cakeVegetarian

Dense dark chocolate cake, glazed, no nuts in this one.

Prep 13 min, cook 47 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days. Check every label for nuts first
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Melt most of the chocolate with the butter, 7 min.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick.
  3. Fold in the chocolate mix and the flour.
  4. Bake at 170°C, 35 min.
  5. Cool the cake, 30 min.
  6. Melt the remaining chocolate with the cream and pour over, 5 min.

Drinks

Séance night cherry red wine sangria

Red wine sangria with cherries, orange, cinnamon and brandy.

Prep 10 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Mix everything except the soda water and chill for at least 2 hours, up to 1 day ahead; add the soda water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen cherries, pitted
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 750ml Shiraz
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml soda water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Put the cherries, orange and cinnamon in a jug.
  2. Add the Shiraz, brandy and sugar and stir to dissolve.
  3. Chill, 2 hr.
  4. Add the soda water just before serving and stir.
  5. Pour into heavy glasses over ice with some fruit in each.
