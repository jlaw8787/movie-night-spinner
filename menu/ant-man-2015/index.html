Snacks

Ant trail sesame crackersVegetarian

Crisp sesame lavash shards with whipped feta and yoghurt dip, an ant trail Antony would follow

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Crackers up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of lavash bread
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp black sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp white sesame seeds
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Brush the lavash with oil.
  2. Sprinkle the black and white sesame seeds in winding trails.
  3. Bake until crisp, then snap into shards.
  4. Whip the feta with the yoghurt and a little oil for dipping.

Luis's baked chicken taquitos

Rolled corn tortillas filled with shredded chicken, cheddar and salsa, baked crisp

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Rolled taquitos up to 1 day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g shredded cooked chicken
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp salsa
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • sour cream, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 220C. Mix the chicken, cheddar and salsa.
  2. Warm the tortillas so they stay pliable.
  3. Roll a spoonful tightly in each tortilla.
  4. Brush with oil and bake seam side down until crisp.
  5. Serve with sour cream.

Mains

San Francisco burritos

Grilled chicken, rice, black beans, cheddar and salsa in a toasted flour tortilla

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 200g long grain rice
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 tin of black beans, drained
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 4 tbsp salsa
  • 4 tbsp sour cream
  1. Grill the chicken thighs and slice.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Fill the tortillas with chicken, rice, beans, cheddar, salsa and sour cream.
  4. Roll tight and toast in a dry pan.

Sweets

Ice cream shop hot fudge brownie sundaesVegetarian

Warm chocolate brownie with hot fudge sauce and ice cream

Prep 35 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Brownies up to 1 day, assemble to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate, for the brownies
  • 120g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 120g dark chocolate, chopped, for the fudge
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • whipped cream, to serve
  • 4 tbsp roasted peanuts
  • 4 cherries
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line a 20cm tin.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate for the brownies with the butter, whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  3. Bake and cool slightly.
  4. Heat the cream until steaming and stir in the chopped dark chocolate until smooth for the hot fudge.
  5. Put a brownie square in each bowl and top with ice cream, fudge, whipped cream, peanuts and a cherry.

Shrunk-down raspberry lemonade shootersVegetarianNo alcohol

Raspberry and mint lemonade with soda in shot glasses, sized for the Thomas the Tank Engine fight

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries, for the lemonade
  • 5 lemons, juiced
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 handful of mint leaves
  • 500ml chilled soda water
  • 12 raspberries, for the rims
  1. Blend the raspberries, lemon juice, sugar and mint, then strain into a jug.
  2. Add the soda water and stir.
  3. Line up shot glasses on a tray and fill each.
  4. Hang a raspberry on each rim. Keep the jug in the fridge for refills.
