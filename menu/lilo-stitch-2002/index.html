Snacks

Pudge's peanut butter and banana toastiesVegetarian

Peanut butter and sliced banana toasted in buttered white bread.

Prep 14 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving so they stay crisp; spread the peanut butter up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 40g soft butter
  • 4 tbsp crunchy peanut butter
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Butter one side of every slice of bread.
  2. Turn over and spread half with peanut butter, top with banana and honey, then close with the other slices, butter side out.
  3. Cook in a pan over medium-low heat for 3 minutes a side until golden.
  4. Cut into fish shapes with a cutter, or into triangles.

Kauai poke

Tuna, soy and sesame on rice crackers. Hold the tuna for the fish.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sashimi tuna, diced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 packet rice crackers
  1. Toss the tuna with soy, sesame oil, spring onion and chilli.
  2. Spoon onto rice crackers and serve straight away.

North Shore garlic butter prawns

Shell-on, fried hard with whole garlic and lemon, over rice, after the morning surf.

How it is made
  • 800g green prawns
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 80g butter
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 garlic bulbs
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Shell-on prawns fried hard in butter with a whole bulb of garlic, lemon and parsley, served over sticky rice with lemon wedges, island prawns truck style. Cut down the backs of 800g shell-on green prawns and devein, leaving the shells on. Toss with 2 tbsp plain flour, 1 tsp paprika and salt. Cook 300g sushi rice. Melt 80g butter with 2 tbsp oil and fry 12 chopped garlic cloves gently for 3 minutes until golden. Add the prawns and cook over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes, tossing, until pink. Finish with lemon juice and parsley and spoon over the rice.

Mains

Loco moco, patties over rice with gravy

Seared beef patties on jasmine rice under onion gravy.

Prep 0 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; sear, make the gravy, cook the rice and fry the eggs on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 4 eggs
  1. Shape the mince into patties and sear in a hot pan. Cook through.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. In the same pan, soften the onion in butter, stir in the flour and then the stock until thick.
  4. Fry the eggs. Serve patties on rice with gravy and a fried egg.

Nani's luau slow-roast kalua pork

Smoky shoulder cooked in banana leaves, coconut rice and pineapple salsa, a seat saved for Stitch.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 box sea salt
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 packet banana leaves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 270ml coconut milk
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  1. Smoky pork shoulder slow-roasted in banana leaves until it falls apart, with coconut rice, pineapple salsa and a crunchy cabbage slaw. Rub a 2kg pork shoulder with 1 tbsp sea salt and 1 tsp smoked paprika, wrap in banana leaves or foil and roast in a covered dish at 150°C for 5 hours until it pulls apart. Shred in the juices. Cook 300g jasmine rice with 270ml coconut milk and 250ml water. Dice half a pineapple with red onion, chilli, coriander and lime for salsa. Toss shredded cabbage with lime and sesame oil. Serve all together.

Sweets

Experiment 626 pineapple soft serveVegetarian

Pineapple and coconut cream soft serve, dairy free.

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Needs frozen pineapple, serve straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen pineapple
  • 100ml coconut cream
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Blitz the pineapple with the coconut cream, lime juice and sugar until thick and smooth.
  2. Pipe into cups and serve straight away before it melts.

Drinks

Blue Hawaiian mocktailNo alcohol

Pineapple, coconut cream and blue raspberry cordial blended slushy.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the pineapple juice, coconut cream and cordial up to 1 day ahead and chill; shake it up and blend with ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 150ml coconut cream
  • 60ml blue raspberry cordial
  • 3 cups ice
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  1. Blend the pineapple juice, coconut cream, cordial and ice until slushy.
  2. Pour into four tall glasses with a pineapple wedge. Ohana means family.
