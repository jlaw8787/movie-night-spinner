Snacks

Charles's running late canapés

Smoked salmon blinis, and asparagus spears wrapped in crisp prosciutto

Prep 17 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Blanch the asparagus up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; wrap, grill and top the blinis on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch asparagus
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Trim the asparagus and blanch for a minute.
  2. Wrap each spear in prosciutto and grill until crisp.
  3. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, salmon and dill.
  4. Arrange both on trays.

Goat's cheese oatcakesVegetarian

Oatcakes with goat's cheese, roast beetroot, thyme and honey

Prep 12 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Roast the beetroot up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet oatcakes
  • 150g soft goat's cheese
  • 2 beetroot
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Wrap the beetroot in foil and roast at 200°C until tender.
  2. Cool, peel and slice the beetroot thin.
  3. Spread the oatcakes with goat's cheese.
  4. Top with beetroot and thyme leaves, drizzle with honey and lay on a slate.

Lucky Boatman ploughman's boardOn the printed menu

Aged cheddar, thick ham, pickled onions, mustard-salted eggs and chutney with crusty bread and apple

How it is made
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g aged cheddar
  • 200g thick-cut ham
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  • 1 green apple
  • 1 tin mustard powder
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch watercress
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 100g butter
  1. Aged cheddar, thick-cut ham, sharp pickled onions, a jammy egg with mustard salt and tomato chutney, with crusty bread, cold butter and apple. Boil 4 eggs for 7 minutes, cool in iced water, peel, halve and sprinkle with salt mixed with a pinch of mustard powder. Cut 250g aged cheddar into thick wedges and fold 200g thick-cut ham onto a big board. Add 8 pickled onions, a bowl of tomato chutney, 1 sliced green apple tossed in lemon, a handful of watercress and the eggs. Slice a crusty loaf thickly and serve with a block of cold butter.

English wedding roast beef

Beef topside, Yorkshire puddings baked in muffin tins, roast potatoes

Prep 25 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Make the batter up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; roast the beef and bake the puddings on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef topside
  • 150g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 500ml beef stock
  1. Season the beef. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk into a batter and rest it.
  2. Roast the beef at 200°C to medium.
  3. Peel and chop the potatoes, toss them in some of the vegetable oil and put them in alongside.
  4. Rest the beef. Heat the muffin tins with the rest of the vegetable oil.
  5. Pour in the batter and bake.
  6. Make gravy from the pan juices and stock.

Highland braised lamb, neeps and tattiesOn the printed menu

Shoulder braised with barley, carrots and rosemary, mashed swede and spuds

How it is made
  • 1.5kg lamb shoulder
  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 75g pearl barley
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 800g potatoes
  • 600g swede
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  1. Lamb shoulder braised 3 hours with onion, carrots and barley, with mashed neeps and tatties. Season 1.5kg lamb shoulder and brown all over in a heavy pot. Add 2 chopped onions, 3 carrots in chunks, 75g pearl barley, rosemary and 750ml chicken stock. Cover and braise at 160°C for 3 hours until it pulls apart. Boil 800g peeled potatoes and 600g swede separately until soft. Mash the potatoes with 50g butter and a splash of milk and the swede with 30g butter and pepper. Serve the lamb and barley over both with the juices.

Four weddings lemon curd cakeVegetarian

Lemon sponge layers filled with lemon curd and whipped cream

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lemon
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 jar lemon curd
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 handful of fresh edible flowers
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and prepare two tins. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs and lemon zest, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Whip the cream and sandwich with lemon curd and cream.
  5. Ice white with icing sugar and water and decorate with flowers.

Gareth's waistcoat elderflower fizzVegetarian

Elderflower cordial and lemon in a flute with a cucumber ribbon, topped with bubbles

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Chill the wine and sparkling water up to 1 day ahead and curl the cucumber ribbons up to 3 hours ahead; top up to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 80ml elderflower cordial
  • 1 lemon
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 750ml sparkling water
  1. Peel the cucumber into long ribbons and curl one into each of four glasses.
  2. Add cordial and a squeeze of lemon to each.
  3. Top slowly with cold sparkling wine.
  4. For drivers, use sparkling water and a little extra cordial.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the guests pour into the first wedding reception.
