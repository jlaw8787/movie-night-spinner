Snacks

Mining camp curried egg sandwichesVegetarian

Curried egg and lettuce on buttered white bread.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the egg filling up to a day ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 12 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  1. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
  2. Mash with the mayonnaise, curry powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Butter the bread, add lettuce and the egg, sandwich and cut into triangles.

Barbecued squid

Garlic and chilli squid grilled hot and fast, finished with lime and coriander.

Prep 13 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 600g cleaned squid tubes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 handful of coriander
  1. Score the squid and cut into pieces.
  2. Toss with the oil, garlic, chilli and salt.
  3. Grill on a very hot barbecue for a minute a side.
  4. Finish with lime juice and coriander.

Loaded pub chips with gravy and cheddar

Thick double-fried chips under gravy, melted cheddar and spring onion, a pub bowl for yarns.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg potatoes
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  • 1 jar chicken salt
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Thick double-fried chips hit with chicken salt, smothered in gravy and melted cheddar, with spring onion on top. Cut 1.2kg potatoes into thick chips, fry at 140°C for 6 minutes, rest, then at 180°C for 4 minutes until crisp. Toss with 2 tsp chicken salt. Make gravy: melt 30g butter, stir in 2 tbsp flour, whisk in 400ml beef stock and a dash of Worcestershire and simmer until thick. Pile the chips on a tray, scatter with 150g grated cheddar, pour over the hot gravy and top with sliced pickled jalapeño and spring onion.

Mains

Pub sausages, steak and potato salad

Charcoal beef sausages and rump steak, fried onions, mayo potato salad, white bread.

Prep 3 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Potato salad up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 loaf of white bread
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, cool and dress with mayonnaise and chives.
  2. Grill the sausages and steaks over hot coals.
  3. Fry the onions on the hotplate until golden.
  4. Serve with a stack of white bread and the potato salad.

Pilbara grilled barra with lime slaw

Spice-dusted barramundi seared on a hot plate, lime and chilli slaw, pickled onion and tartare.

How it is made
  • 800g barramundi fillets
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 limes
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar tartare sauce
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Spice-dusted barramundi seared on a hot plate, served with lime and chilli slaw, pickled red onion and tartare sauce. Mix 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cumin and salt and rub over 800g barramundi fillets. Pickle 1 sliced red onion in lime juice and salt. Toss a quarter cabbage, shredded, with lime juice, chilli and coriander. Mix 120g mayonnaise with 1 chopped chipotle. Sear the fish in a hot oiled pan for 3 minutes a side and flake. Char 12 small flour tortillas and fill with fish, slaw, onion and mayo.

Sweets

Carnarvon mango cheeks with coconut ice creamVegetarian

Fresh mango cheeks with coconut ice cream and toasted coconut.

Prep 8 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Cut the mango up to 2 hours ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 4 mangoes
  • 1 L coconut ice cream
  • 1 lime
  • 30g shredded coconut
  1. Toast the coconut in a dry pan until golden.
  2. Cut the cheeks from the mangoes and score the flesh.
  3. Serve with a scoop of ice cream, lime zest and the toasted coconut.

Drinks

Red Dog ruby grapefruit radlerNo alcohol

Ginger beer topped with fresh ruby grapefruit soda over ice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Grapefruit soda up to 1 day, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ruby grapefruits, juiced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 400ml cold soda water
  • 1 L cold ginger beer
  • 1 grapefruit, cut into wedges
  1. Juice the grapefruit and stir with the sugar until dissolved.
  2. Mix with the soda water and chill well.
  3. Half fill 4 tall glasses with the ginger beer.
  4. Top with the grapefruit soda and add a wedge.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the miners at the pub start telling Red Dog stories.
