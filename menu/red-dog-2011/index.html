Snacks

Mining camp sausage rolls

Pork sausage rolls with grated onion and carrot, served hot with tomato sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry, halved
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a tray.
  2. Mix the sausage mince, onion and carrot.
  3. Lay the filling along the edge of the pastry, roll up and seal.
  4. Brush with egg, cut into pieces and bake.
  5. Serve hot with tomato sauce.

Dampier barbecued squid

Garlic and chilli squid grilled hot and fast, finished with lime and coriander

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 600g cleaned squid tubes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 handful of coriander
  1. Score the squid and cut into pieces.
  2. Toss with the oil, garlic, chilli and salt.
  3. Grill on a very hot barbecue for a minute a side.
  4. Finish with lime juice and coriander.

Mains

Dampier sausages, steak and potato salad

Charcoal beef sausages and rump steak, fried onions, mayo potato salad, white bread

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Potato salad up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 loaf of white bread
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, cool and dress with mayonnaise and chives.
  2. Grill the sausages and steaks over hot coals.
  3. Fry the onions on the hotplate until golden.
  4. Serve with a stack of white bread and the potato salad.

Sweets

Red Dog lamingtons

Sponge cubes dipped in raspberry jelly and rolled in coconut

Prep 50 min, cook 160 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 packet of raspberry jelly crystals
  • 200g desiccated coconut
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line a square tin. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake the sponge and cool overnight.
  3. Make up the jelly and chill until just starting to set.
  4. Cut the sponge into cubes, dip in the jelly and roll in coconut.
  5. Chill until firm.

Drinks

Red Dog ruby grapefruit radlerVegetarian

Lager topped with fresh ruby grapefruit soda over ice, soda only for drivers

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Grapefruit soda up to 1 day, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ruby grapefruits, juiced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 400ml cold soda water
  • 1l cold lager
  • 1 grapefruit, cut into wedges
  1. Juice the grapefruit and stir with the sugar until dissolved.
  2. Mix with the soda water and chill well.
  3. Half fill 4 tall glasses with lager.
  4. Top with the grapefruit soda and add a wedge. Serve the soda on its own for the drivers.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the miners at the pub start telling Red Dog stories.
