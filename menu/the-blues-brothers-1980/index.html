Snacks

Soul Food Cafe hush puppiesVegetarian

Fried onion cornmeal fritters with hot sauce butter for dipping

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry ingredients up to 1 day ahead; stir in the onion, egg and buttermilk and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, sugar, onion, egg and buttermilk.
  2. Heat the oil to 175C.
  3. Drop spoonfuls into the oil and fry until golden.
  4. Melt the butter with the hot sauce for dipping.

Bob's Country Bunker peanutsVegetarian

Peanuts roasted in the shell with salt and cayenne

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roast up to 2 days ahead, airtight, and warm in the oven for 5 minutes before tossing with salt and cayenne
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peanuts in the shell
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Spread the peanuts on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180C.
  3. Toss straight away with salt and cayenne, then tip into a tin bucket.

Mains

Jake's fried chickens

Buttermilk brined chicken pieces, double dredged and fried crisp

Prep 30 min, cook 740 min · Make ahead: Brine overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Brine the chicken overnight in the buttermilk and hot sauce.
  2. Season the flour with salt and paprika and dredge the chicken twice.
  3. Heat the oil to 165C.
  4. Fry in batches until 74C inside.
  5. Pile high on a platter.

Sweets

Ruler-straight shortbread fingersVegetarian

Buttery rice flour shortbread baked pale and cut into fingers

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Bake and cut up to 1 week ahead, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, soft
  • 130g caster sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 60g rice flour
  1. Beat the butter with most of the sugar until pale, then work in the flours.
  2. Press into a lined tin, 20 by 30cm, and prick in neat rows.
  3. Chill.
  4. Bake at 150C until pale gold.
  5. Sprinkle with the remaining sugar and cut into fingers while warm.

Soul Food Cafe peach sweet teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Sweet black tea blended with peach, served over ice with lemon and mint

Prep 15 min, cook 130 min · Make ahead: Chill up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1l boiling water
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 2 ripe peaches
  • 750ml cold water
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in boiling water, then remove and stir in the sugar.
  2. Blend the peaches with some of the cold water and strain into the tea.
  3. Add the rest of the cold water and chill.
  4. Pour over ice with lemon slices and mint.
