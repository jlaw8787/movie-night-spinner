Soul Food Cafe hush puppiesVegetarian

Fried onion cornmeal fritters with hot sauce butter for dipping

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry ingredients up to 1 day ahead; stir in the onion, egg and buttermilk and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, sugar, onion, egg and buttermilk.
  2. Heat the oil to 175°C.
  3. Drop spoonfuls into the oil and fry until golden.
  4. Melt the butter with the hot sauce for dipping.

Bob's Country Bunker peanutsVegetarian

Peanuts roasted in the shell with salt and cayenne

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roast up to 2 days ahead, airtight, and warm in the oven for 5 minutes before tossing with salt and cayenne
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peanuts in the shell
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Spread the peanuts on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180°C.
  3. Toss straight away with salt and cayenne, then tip into a tin bucket.

Jake's fried chickens

Buttermilk brined chicken pieces, double dredged and fried crisp

Prep 17 min, cook 24 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brine overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Brine the chicken overnight in the buttermilk and hot sauce.
  2. Season the flour with salt and paprika and dredge the chicken twice.
  3. Heat the oil to 165°C.
  4. Fry in batches until 74°C inside.
  5. Pile high on a platter.

Soul Food Cafe smothered chops and greensOn the printed menu

Pork chops smothered in onion gravy with smoky collard greens, from Aretha's Soul Food Cafe

How it is made
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 3 onions
  • 2 bunches kale
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 4 thick pork chops
  • 60g plain flour
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 50g butter
  1. Thick pork chops browned and simmered in onion gravy, with smoky ham hock collard greens and buttered white toast to mop the plate. Simmer 1 smoked ham hock in 1.5L water with 1 onion for 60 minutes. Add 2 bunches shredded silverbeet or kale, 1 tbsp cider vinegar and chilli flakes and cook 20 minutes. Season 4 thick pork chops, dust in flour and brown in oil 3 minutes a side, then remove. Cook 2 sliced onions in the pan 8 minutes, stir in 2 tbsp flour, then 500ml chicken stock. Return the chops, cover and simmer 30 minutes. Serve with the greens and buttered toast.

Ruler-straight shortbread fingersVegetarian

Buttery rice flour shortbread baked pale and cut into fingers

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake and cut up to 1 week ahead, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, soft
  • 130g caster sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 60g rice flour
  1. Beat the butter with most of the sugar until pale, then work in the flours.
  2. Press into a lined tin, 20 by 30cm, and prick in neat rows.
  3. Chill.
  4. Bake at 150°C until pale gold.
  5. Sprinkle with the remaining sugar and cut into fingers while warm.

Soul Food Cafe peach sweet teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Sweet black tea blended with peach, served over ice with lemon and mint

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1l boiling water
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 2 ripe peaches
  • 750ml cold water
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in boiling water, then remove and stir in the sugar.
  2. Blend the peaches with some of the cold water and strain into the tea.
  3. Add the rest of the cold water and chill.
  4. Pour over ice with lemon slices and mint.
