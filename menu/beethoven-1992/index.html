Da-da-da-dum cheese and herb scrollsVegetarian

Soft bread scrolls rolled with tasty cheese and chives.

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut the scrolls up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 375g self-raising flour
  • 60g cold butter, cubed
  • 250ml milk
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 200g tasty cheese, grated
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Rub the butter into the flour with a pinch of salt, then stir in the milk to a soft dough.
  2. Roll out to a 30 x 40cm rectangle, spread with tomato paste and scatter with cheese, chives and oregano.
  3. Roll up from the long side, cut into 12 rounds and pack into a lined tin.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.

Sparky's mini corn dogs with tomato sauce

Little franks in sweet corn batter. Sparky bites Dr Varnick.

Prep 8 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry batter up to a day ahead; dip and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 16 mini frankfurts
  • 100g fine cornmeal (polenta)
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 16 short wooden skewers
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt, then whisk in the egg and milk to a thick batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push a skewer into each.
  3. Pour the batter into a tall glass, dip each frank and fry at 180°C until deep golden.
  4. Drain and serve with tomato sauce.

George's backyard barbecue cheeseburgers

Beef patties, cheese, pickles, burger sauce. Grilled before the dog gets to it.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties and mix the sauce up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  1. Season the mince well and shape into 4 patties a little wider than the buns.
  2. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and mustard into burger sauce.
  3. Grill or barbecue the patties 3 to 4 minutes a side, laying the cheese on for the last minute.
  4. Toast the buns and build with sauce, lettuce, tomato, patty and pickles, 10 min.

Brad and Brie's three-cheese mac with crunchy crumbsVegetarian

The ride Brad and Brie deserved. Cheddar, mozzarella and brie, golden crumbs.

Prep 0 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese sauce up to a day ahead; mix with the pasta and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g macaroni
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml milk
  • 150g tasty cheese, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 125g brie, rind trimmed and chopped
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Cook the macaroni 2 minutes short of the packet time and drain.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and simmer until thick.
  3. Stir in the cheeses until melted, then fold through the pasta and tip into a baking dish.
  4. Scatter with the panko and paprika and bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling.

Late-night giant choc chip cookieVegetarian

A giant choc chip cookie baked in a pan, warm and gooey.

Prep 5 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Make the dough up to 2 days ahead and keep in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, melted
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g milk chocolate chips
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Whisk the melted butter with both sugars, then beat in the egg and vanilla.
  2. Stir in the flour, bicarb and a pinch of salt, then the chocolate chips.
  3. Press into a buttered 25cm ovenproof frying pan and bake at 180°C until golden at the edges and soft in the middle.
  4. Cool for 5 minutes and serve straight from the pan with scoops of ice cream.
