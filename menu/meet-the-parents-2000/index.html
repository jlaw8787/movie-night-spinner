Snacks

Mr Jinx's tuna bites

Tuna, celery, lemon mayo, cucumber. Mr Jinx only, not the spray-painted stray.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 185g tinned tuna
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 spring onion
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Drain the tuna and mix with the mayonnaise, finely diced celery, spring onion and lemon juice.
  2. Cut the cucumbers into thick rounds.
  3. Spoon on the tuna and finish with dill and black pepper. Keep it away from the cat.

Garden party devilled eggsVegetarian

Eggs, Dijon yolk, paprika and chives, kept well clear of the urn.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 splash pickle juice
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool and peel.
  2. Halve and whip the yolks with the mayonnaise, Dijon and pickle juice.
  3. Pipe back and finish with paprika and chives.

Pool volleyball pulled chicken nachos

Chipotle chicken nachos, post pool volleyball.

How it is made
  • 400g chicken thighs
  • 1 tin chipotles in adobo
  • 300g corn chips
  • 1 tin black beans
  • 200g cheddar
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Corn chips piled with smoky chipotle chicken, black beans and melted cheddar, topped with pico de gallo, pickled jalapeno, guacamole and sour cream. Poach 400g chicken thighs for 20 minutes, shred and toss with 1 chopped chipotle in adobo and a splash of the sauce. Layer 300g corn chips with the chicken, a drained tin of black beans and 200g grated cheddar, and bake at 200°C for 8 minutes. Dice 3 tomatoes, half a red onion and coriander with lime. Mash 2 avocados with lime and salt. Top with pico, jalapeno, guacamole and sour cream.

Mains

Wedding rehearsal salmon

Salmon with a dill, parsley and lemon crust, herbed new potatoes, asparagus.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix the herb crumb up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Press onto the salmon and roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 2 bunches of asparagus
  1. Mix the breadcrumbs with chopped dill, parsley and lemon zest.
  2. Press onto the salmon and roast at 200°C, 10 min.
  3. Boil the potatoes and toss with herbs, 15 min.
  4. Steam the asparagus, 5 min.
  5. Plate with lemon wedges.

Byrnes family roast sirloin and gravy

Beef cooked pink and carved thick, crisp potatoes, red wine sauce.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg sirloin roast
  • 1kg roasting potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 500g carrots
  • 30g butter
  • 1 jar honey
  • 150ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 packet plain flour
  1. Sirloin roasted pink and carved thick, with crisp roast potatoes, honey buttered carrots and a rich red wine gravy. Season a 1.5kg sirloin roast the day before. Parboil 1kg potatoes for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in oil at 220°C for 50 minutes. Sear the beef, then roast at 200°C to 52°C inside, about 40 minutes, and rest 20 minutes. Boil 500g carrots and toss with butter and honey. Add 150ml red wine to the roasting tray and reduce, then add 300ml beef stock and 1 tbsp flour paste and simmer for gravy.

Wedding altar lemon tartVegetarian

Silky lemon tart, dusted with sugar.

Prep 10 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Bake the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 4 lemons
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Line a tart tin with the pastry and blind-bake, 25 min.
  2. Whisk the lemon juice and zest, eggs, sugar and cream.
  3. Pour in and bake at 140°C until just set.
  4. Chill, 20 min.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Jack's malted vanilla milkshakeNo alcohol

Thick vanilla ice cream milkshake with malt powder, served in a frozen glass.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Put the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead and whip the cream up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled. Blend each batch to order
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml ice-cold full cream milk
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Put four tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream, malted milk powder and vanilla until thick and frothy.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Pour into the frosted glasses, top with whipped cream and dust with a little extra malt powder.
