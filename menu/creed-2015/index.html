Adrian's restaurant antipastoVegetarian

Chargrilled eggplant, provolone, warm olives, artichokes, roasted peppers and Italian bread.

Prep 6 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Lay the eggplant, provolone and artichokes on the board up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; warm the olives and tear the bread just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g chargrilled eggplant slices
  • 150g provolone, sliced
  • 200g olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 jar marinated artichoke hearts
  • 2 red capsicums, roasted
  • 1 loaf Italian bread
  1. Fold the chargrilled eggplant and provolone onto a big board.
  2. Warm the olives gently in olive oil and spoon over, 3 min.
  3. Add the artichoke hearts and strips of roasted capsicum.
  4. Tear the bread for mopping up the oil.

Date, oat and peanut butter bitesVegetarian

No-bake date, oat, peanut butter and cocoa balls rolled in coconut.

Prep 13 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 5 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pitted medjool dates
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 3 tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 40g desiccated coconut
  1. Blitz the dates, oats, peanut butter, cocoa and salt until it clumps.
  2. Roll into small balls and coat in coconut.
  3. Chill for an hour.

Italian hoagie bites with hot peppers

Ham, salami, capocollo and provolone in long rolls with cherry chillies, Philly style.

How it is made
  • 2 long Italian rolls
  • olive oil
  • red wine vinegar
  • dried oregano
  • 100g leg ham
  • 100g salami
  • 100g capocollo
  • 100g provolone
  • 0.25 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • hot cherry peppers
  1. Long rolls layered with ham, salami, capocollo, provolone, lettuce, tomato and onion, dressed with oil, vinegar and oregano, cut into bites with hot cherry peppers. Split 2 long Italian rolls. Drizzle the insides with olive oil and red wine vinegar and sprinkle with dried oregano. Layer with 100g leg ham, 100g salami, 100g capocollo and 100g provolone. Top with shredded iceberg, sliced tomato, thin red onion and chopped hot cherry peppers. Press closed, wrap tightly for 15 minutes, then cut each into 6 bites with a toothpick through each.

Mains

Date night cheesesteaks

Shaved rib eye with onion, peppers and melted provolone on long rolls.

Prep 8 min, cook 14 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Freeze and shave the steak up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; slice the onions and capsicum ahead and cook everything fresh on a hot plate
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rib eye steak
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 150g provolone
  • 4 long bread rolls
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Freeze the steak for 30 minutes.
  2. Shave the steak paper thin and slice the onions and capsicum.
  3. Fry the onion and capsicum in the oil on a hot plate until soft, 8 min.
  4. Add the beef and sear fast, 3 min.
  5. Lay provolone over the top to melt, 3 min.
  6. Scoop into split bread rolls.

Liverpool scouse with pickled cabbage

Beef chuck and lamb neck stewed thick with carrot and potato, a Liverpool matchday bowl.

How it is made
  • 600g beef chuck
  • 400g lamb neck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1L beef stock
  • bay leaves
  • pickled red cabbage
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 50g butter
  1. Beef chuck and lamb neck browned and simmered with onion, carrot and potato until the potato breaks down, with pickled red cabbage and crusty bread. Brown 600g diced beef chuck and 400g lamb neck in batches. Add 2 sliced onions and 3 carrots in chunks and cook 5 minutes. Add 1kg potatoes in chunks, 1L beef stock and a bay leaf. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 2 hours, stirring now and then, until the potatoes break down and thicken the stew. Season well. Serve with a jar of pickled red cabbage and buttered crusty bread.

Italian rainbow cookiesVegetarian

Layers of almond cake with apricot jam, topped with chocolate.

Prep 15 min, cook 51 min, 12 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g almond meal
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp green food colouring
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 200g apricot jam
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the almond meal, eggs, sugar and melted butter.
  2. Split in three and tint one green and one red.
  3. Bake each in a thin layer at 175°C, 36 min.
  4. Stack the layers with apricot jam between.
  5. Press overnight.
  6. Spread with melted chocolate and slice.

Training camp honey lemon ginger fizzNo alcohol

Honey ginger syrup and lemon with a pinch of salt, topped with sparkling water.

Prep 6 min, cook 4 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Simmer and cool the ginger syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; mix with the lemon juice and top with sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cm knobs of ginger
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 100ml water
  • 3 lemons
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  • 800ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Grate the ginger into a small pan with honey and water and simmer, then strain, 4 min.
  2. Cool the syrup, 15 min.
  3. Squeeze the lemons into a jug and add the ginger syrup and salt.
  4. Fill four tall glasses with ice and divide the lemon mix between them.
  5. Top with sparkling water and finish with a lemon wheel.
