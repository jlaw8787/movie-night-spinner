Snacks

Swedish meatballs

Little beef and pork meatballs in a cream sauce with lingonberries, Swedish style

Prep 22 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brown and simmer in the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 tbsp lingonberry jam
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix with both minces, the onion, allspice and egg.
  2. Roll small.
  3. Brown in butter.
  4. Simmer in the stock and cream.
  5. Serve with lingonberry jam.

Rye crispbreadVegetarian

Rye crispbread with cold butter, shaved hard cheese and thin cucumber

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Shave the cheese and slice the cucumber up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; build just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 12 rye crispbreads
  • 50g butter, cold
  • 150g aged hard cheese, shaved
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 2 tbsp dill
  1. Shave the cheese and slice the cucumber paper thin.
  2. Spread the crispbreads with cold butter.
  3. Top with cheese and cucumber, finish with white pepper and dill.
  4. Build just before serving so they stay crisp.

Aged cheese tart with roeOn the printed menu

Aged cheese custard in butter pastry, cut in squares, crème fraîche and roe, solved by morning

How it is made
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250ml cream
  • 200g aged hard cheese
  • 1 jar white pepper
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 50g salmon roe
  1. A Swedish-style aged cheese tart with a crisp butter pastry, cut into neat cubes and topped with crème fraîche, dill and a little salmon roe. Line a 20cm square tin with 1 sheet shortcrust pastry and blind bake at 190°C for 15 minutes. Whisk 3 eggs with 250ml cream, 200g grated aged hard cheese (or vintage cheddar), salt and white pepper. Pour into the case and bake at 170°C for 30 minutes until just set. Cool completely, then cut into 16 cubes. Top each with a dab of crème fraîche, a dill frond and a few pearls of salmon roe.

Toast Skagen

Prawns, dill and mayo on buttered toast

Prep 14 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the prawn topping up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; fry the bread and pile on the topping just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked prawns
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 4 slices white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Chop the prawns.
  2. Fold with the mayo, crème fraîche, dill, lemon juice and horseradish.
  3. Fry the bread in butter until golden.
  4. Pile the prawns on top and add a little roe if you have it.

Blackeberg pyttipanna with fried eggsOn the printed menu

Diced potato, onion and sausage fried in butter, fried eggs on top. A Blackeberg winter supper.

How it is made
  • 1kg waxy potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions
  • 300g smoked ham
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 jar pickled beetroot
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Swedish hash of crisp diced potato, smoked ham and onion fried in butter, topped with runny fried eggs, pickled beetroot and parsley. Dice 1kg waxy potatoes into 1cm cubes and parboil 4 minutes, then drain and steam dry. Fry in 40g butter and 2 tbsp oil in a big frying pan for 15 minutes until crisp. Add 2 diced onions and 300g diced smoked ham and cook 8 minutes more. Season well. Fry 4 eggs in butter until the whites set. Serve the hash topped with the eggs, 200g sliced pickled beetroot and chopped parsley.

Jungle gym cinnamon bunsVegetarian

Soft cardamom dough rolled with cinnamon sugar butter

Prep 40 min, cook 10 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough can prove overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 155g caster sugar
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 175g butter
  • 2 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp pearl sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, cardamom, some of the sugar, the warm milk and a little of the butter into a dough and knead.
  2. Prove until doubled.
  3. Roll out and spread with the remaining butter, sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Cut into strips and knot.
  5. Prove again.
  6. Brush with egg, add pearl sugar and bake at 220°C.

Hot spiced blackcurrant glöggVegetarianNo alcohol

Blackcurrant and apple juice simmered with cardamom, cinnamon and orange, no alcohol

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the spiced juice and strain it up to 3 days ahead, then chill; reheat gently and pour over the raisins and almonds
Recipe for 4
  • 1l blackcurrant juice
  • 250ml cloudy apple juice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 6 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 50g raisins
  • 50g blanched almonds
  1. Put the juices, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, peel and sugar in a saucepan.
  2. Heat gently to just below a simmer and hold.
  3. Strain.
  4. Put raisins and almonds in four heatproof glasses and pour over the hot glogg.
