Snacks

Swedish meatballs

Lingonberry and cream sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brown and simmer in the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 tbsp lingonberry jam
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix with both minces, the onion, allspice and egg.
  2. Roll small.
  3. Brown in butter.
  4. Simmer in the stock and cream.
  5. Serve with lingonberry jam.

Swedish crispbreadVegetarian

Rye crispbread with cold butter, shaved hard cheese and thin cucumber

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Shave the cheese and slice the cucumber up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; build just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 12 rye crispbreads
  • 50g butter, cold
  • 150g aged hard cheese, shaved
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 2 tbsp dill
  1. Shave the cheese and slice the cucumber paper thin.
  2. Spread the crispbreads with cold butter.
  3. Top with cheese and cucumber, finish with white pepper and dill.
  4. Build just before serving so they stay crisp.

Mains

Toast Skagen

Prawns, dill and mayo on buttered toast

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the prawn topping up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; fry the bread and pile on the topping just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked prawns
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 4 slices white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Chop the prawns.
  2. Fold with the mayo, crème fraîche, dill, lemon juice and horseradish.
  3. Fry the bread in butter until golden.
  4. Pile the prawns on top and add a little roe if you have it.

Sweets

Jungle gym cinnamon bunsVegetarian

Soft cardamom dough rolled with cinnamon sugar butter

Prep 45 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Dough can prove overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 155g caster sugar
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 175g butter
  • 2 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp pearl sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, cardamom, some of the sugar, the warm milk and a little of the butter into a dough and knead.
  2. Prove until doubled.
  3. Roll out and spread with the remaining butter, sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Cut into strips and knot.
  5. Prove again.
  6. Brush with egg, add pearl sugar and bake at 220C.

Drinks

Hot spiced blackcurrant glöggVegetarianNo alcohol

Blackcurrant and apple juice simmered with cardamom, cinnamon and orange, no alcohol

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the spiced juice and strain it up to 3 days ahead, then chill; reheat gently and pour over the raisins and almonds
Recipe for 4
  • 1l blackcurrant juice
  • 250ml cloudy apple juice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 6 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 50g raisins
  • 50g blanched almonds
  1. Put the juices, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, peel and sugar in a saucepan.
  2. Heat gently to just below a simmer and hold.
  3. Strain.
  4. Put raisins and almonds in four heatproof glasses and pour over the hot glogg.
