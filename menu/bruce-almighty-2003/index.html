Pulled-closer half-moon pierogiVegetarian

Potato and cheddar pierogi, browned onion butter.

Prep 25 min, cook 73 min · Make ahead: Fill and shape the pierogi up to a day ahead and chill on a floured tray, or freeze; boil and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour, plus extra for rolling
  • 1 egg
  • 150g sour cream, plus extra to serve
  • 100g butter
  • 500g floury potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 120g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 2 brown onions, 1 finely diced, 1 thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Mix the flour, egg, 3 tbsp sour cream, 30g melted butter and half a teaspoon of salt into a soft dough, knead 3 minutes and rest under a bowl.
  2. Boil the potatoes until soft, mash with the cheddar, fry the diced onion in a little butter and stir it through. Season hard and cool.
  3. Roll the dough 2mm thick, cut 8cm rounds, fill each with a teaspoon of potato, fold into half moons and pinch the edges shut.
  4. Boil the pierogi in salted water until they float, then 1 minute more, and lift out.
  5. Fry the sliced onion in the rest of the butter until golden, add the pierogi and fry until crisp at the edges. Serve with sour cream and chives.

Stanley Cup Buffalo wings

Hot sauce wings with blue cheese dip and celery, Buffalo's own.

Prep 5 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Make the blue cheese dip up to 2 days ahead; bake the wings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings, tips off, split
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 125ml Frank's RedHot sauce
  • 60g butter
  • 100g blue cheese, crumbled
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 celery sticks, cut in batons
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder and a teaspoon of salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp and golden.
  3. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream, mayo and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Melt the butter into the hot sauce and toss the wings through it.

Red Sea tomato soup and Grace's grilled cheeseVegetarian

Roast tomato soup, cheddar toastie on sourdough. Bruce parts his tomato soup.

Prep 0 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead and reheat; toast the sandwiches to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.6kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 brown onion, quartered
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 700ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 8 thick slices of sourdough
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 50g soft butter
  1. Toss the tomatoes, onion and garlic with the oil and salt and roast at 200°C until soft and blistered.
  2. Blend with the stock, sugar and half the basil, then simmer, stir in the cream and season.
  3. Fill the bread with both cheeses, butter the outsides and toast in a pan over medium heat until golden and melting.
  4. Ladle the soup into wide bowls, tear over the rest of the basil and serve each with a whole grilled cheese, cut corner to corner, for parting or dunking.

Sweets

Every prayer answered Yes sundaeVegetarian

Every topping at once, under hot fudge.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to a week ahead and rewarm; build the sundaes to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 250g strawberries, halved
  • 60g salted peanuts, chopped
  • 300ml whipped cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 2 tbsp rainbow sprinkles
  1. Heat the cream and golden syrup, pour over the chocolate and stir into a glossy fudge.
  2. Layer banana, strawberries and scoops of ice cream in 4 tall glasses.
  3. Pour over the hot fudge, then add the cream, peanuts, sprinkles and a cherry each.

Juan Valdez iced coffeeNo alcohol

Colombian iced coffee, condensed milk, ice. Bruce manifests himself some coffee.

Prep 6 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep the iced coffee overnight in the fridge; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 80g Colombian coffee, coarsely ground
  • 800ml cold water
  • 120ml sweetened condensed milk
  • 200ml milk
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Stir the coffee into the cold water and leave in the fridge for 12 hours, then strain.
  2. Spoon condensed milk into 4 glasses and fill with ice.
  3. Pour over the iced coffee and top with milk, then stir.
