Snacks

Camp Chippewa sloppy joe sliders

Saucy beef mince on slider buns with cheddar and dill pickles

Prep 27 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the mince mixture up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; build the sliders just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp American mustard
  • 100ml water
  • 12 slider buns
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  1. Dice the onion and capsicum.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion and capsicum.
  3. Add the sauce, Worcestershire, sugar, mustard and water and simmer until thick.
  4. Split the buns and lay half a slice of cheddar on each base.
  5. Spoon on the mince and top with pickles.

Mains

Debbie's black widow lobster

Grilled lobster tails with garlic lemon butter, for the black widow's honeymoon

Prep 10 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Split the tails and make the garlic lemon butter up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 120g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 lemons
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 150g salad leaves
  1. Split the lobster tails.
  2. Melt some of the butter with the garlic and lemon zest and brush over.
  3. Grill flesh side up until cooked through.
  4. Boil the potatoes until tender and toss with butter.
  5. Serve with dressed salad leaves and lemon wedges.

Sweets

Wednesday's smile cookiesVegetarian

Butter cookies with a thin chocolate smile, as Joel screams

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, soft
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 50g dark chocolate, melted
  1. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut rounds.
  3. Bake at 160C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Pipe a thin line of chocolate across each as a small smile.

Prettiest pilgrim pumpkin pieVegetarian

Roasted pumpkin pie with mixed spice, cooled before slicing

Prep 8 min, cook 105 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pumpkin
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  1. Roast the pumpkin until soft and puree.
  2. Line a pie dish with the pastry and blind-bake.
  3. Whisk the puree with eggs, sugar, cream and spice.
  4. Pour into the case and bake at 170C.
  5. Cool before slicing.

Drinks

Camp canteen raspberry lemonade slushVegetarianNo alcohol

Raspberry and lemon blended into an icy slush

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the strained raspberry base up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; blend with the water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g frozen raspberries
  • 5 lemons, juiced
  • 150ml sugar syrup
  • 500ml cold water
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Blend the raspberries with lemon juice and syrup until smooth, then strain out the seeds.
  2. Return to the blender with the cold water and ice and blend until thick and slushy.
  3. Pour into tall cups with a lemon wheel and a spoon straw.
