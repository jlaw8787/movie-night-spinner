Snacks

Circus caramel butter popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in brown sugar butter caramel and sea salt.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, airtight; crisp it in a low oven for 5 minutes if it softens
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 50g butter
  • 50g brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the corn in the oil in a large lidded pot.
  2. Melt the butter with the sugar until bubbling and toss through the popcorn with the salt.
  3. Spread out to cool, then pour into striped paper cones.

Riddler question mark soft pretzelsVegetarian

Pizza dough pretzels shaped as question marks, dipped in bicarb water.

Prep 17 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 L water
  • 4 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 4 tbsp English mustard
  1. Roll the dough into eight ropes and shape each into a question mark with a small ball for the dot.
  2. Boil the water with bicarb soda and dip each piece briefly.
  3. Lay on lined trays, brush with egg and sprinkle with salt.
  4. Bake at 220°C until deep brown.
  5. Serve warm with mustard.

Two minds, beef and chicken mini burgers

One cheddar patty with pickles, one crumbed chicken with slaw, picked by coin toss.

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100g panko crumbs
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 mini burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Two mini burgers per plate: a beef and cheddar mini burger with pickles, and a crumbed chicken mini burger with slaw, decided by a coin toss. Shape 400g beef mince into 4 small patties and grill 2 minutes a side, adding cheddar for the last minute. Cut 2 chicken breasts into 4 flat pieces, coat in flour, egg and panko, and shallow fry 3 minutes a side. Toss 2 cups shredded cabbage with 2 tbsp mayo and lemon. Toast 8 mini burger buns. Fill 4 with beef, pickles and tomato sauce, and 4 with chicken and slaw.

Mains

Riddler Box noodles

Chicken and bell pepper egg noodles in sweet chilli and soy, served in a takeout box.

Prep 3 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Slice the chicken and capsicum up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g egg noodles, cooked
  • 500g chicken thigh fillets, sliced
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 4 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Cook the noodles and drain.
  2. Stir-fry the chicken in the vegetable oil in a hot wok until browned.
  3. Add the capsicum and cook briefly.
  4. Toss through the noodles with sweet chilli and soy sauce until sticky and pack into takeaway boxes.

Wayne Manor tenderloin with red wine jus

Seared tenderloin with red wine jus, Alfred's sandwich upgraded.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml cream
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bottle red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 50g butter
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  1. Beef eye fillet seared and roasted pink, with a glossy red wine jus, creamy potato gratin and grilled asparagus. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes in a buttered dish with 300ml cream, 2 garlic cloves and salt, and bake at 180°C for 1 hour. Season a 1kg eye fillet, sear all over and roast at 200°C for 20 minutes for medium-rare, then rest 10 minutes. Boil 250ml red wine and 250ml beef stock in the pan until reduced by half and whisk in 30g butter. Grill 2 bunches asparagus.

Sweets

Trapeze funnel cakesVegetarian

Looping fried batter swirls, golden and crisp.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Whisk the batter up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; fry and dust just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 4 tbsp icing sugar
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk and sugar into a smooth batter.
  2. Pipe looping swirls into 180°C oil and fry until golden on both sides.
  3. Drain, dust thickly with icing sugar and serve warm with strawberries.

Drinks

Question mark lime spider floatNo alcohol

Lime cordial, lemonade and vanilla ice cream.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best made just before serving so it stays fizzy; chill the lemonade and glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp lime cordial
  • 1 L lemonade
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  1. Pour a splash of lime cordial into each tall glass and top with cold lemonade.
  2. Add a scoop of ice cream so it floats and fizzes up green and serve straight away.
