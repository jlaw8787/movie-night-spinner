Snacks

Minted pea and feta toastsVegetarian

Toasted baguette with smashed peas, mint, feta and lemon

Prep 8 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Smash the peas up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; toast the baguette and spread just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves, chopped
  • 100g feta
  • 1 lemon
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the baguette and brush with oil.
  2. Toast the slices under the grill or in a hot oven until golden.
  3. Cook the peas in boiling water until tender, then drain.
  4. Smash the peas with the mint, crumbled feta, a squeeze of lemon and salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Spread thickly on the toasts and run a fork along the top for crocodile ridges.

Mermaid lagoon prawns

Green prawns poached in lemon water, chilled in iced water, tails on

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1kg ice
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  1. Bring a pot of water to the boil with the salt and the lemon slices.
  2. Poach the prawns until pink.
  3. Plunge into iced water to stop the cooking.
  4. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, a squeeze of lemon and the hot sauce.
  6. Serve the prawns on ice with the sauce.

Mains

Captain Hook's beef and allspice stew

Beef chuck stewed with allspice and molasses, no surprise package inside

Prep 45 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tbsp molasses or brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Toss the beef in the flour. Slice the onions and chop the carrots.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in the olive oil in a heavy pot.
  3. Soften the onions and carrots.
  4. Return the beef and add the allspice, molasses, tomato paste and stock.
  5. Cover and simmer gently until thick and tender.
  6. Season well with salt and pepper and serve in big bowls with crusty bread.

Sweets

Tinker Bell fairy breadVegetarian

Buttered white bread covered in sprinkles, cut into triangles. Think a wonderful thought.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make up to 3 hours ahead and cover with a damp tea towel; dust with the glitter at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 12 slices soft white bread
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 100g hundreds and thousands
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  1. Butter the bread right to the edges.
  2. Press each buttered side into a plate of hundreds and thousands until well covered.
  3. Cut into triangles.
  4. Dust with the edible glitter and pile on a plate.

Nana's bedtime malted vanilla milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Warm milk whisked with malt powder, honey and vanilla

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; heat the milk just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1l milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 8 plain biscuits
  1. Heat the milk in a saucepan until steaming but not boiling.
  2. Off the heat, whisk in the malted milk powder, honey and vanilla until frothy.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Pour into mugs, spoon a little cream on each and grate over nutmeg. Serve with a plain biscuit.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the children fly over London.
