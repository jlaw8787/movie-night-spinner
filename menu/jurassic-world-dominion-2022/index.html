Snacks

Ricotta and pea pastizziVegetarian

Puff pastry parcels filled with ricotta or curried peas

Prep 23 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the parcels up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 pinch curry powder
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Dice the onion and cut the pastry into squares.
  2. Mix the ricotta with the egg.
  3. Cook the peas with the onion and curry powder in oil.
  4. Fill the squares with one or the other, fold into parcels.
  5. Bake until flaky.

Locust swarm cornVegetarian

Cobs boiled, brushed with chilli lime butter and charred, for Ellie's crop samples

Prep 9 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime and chilli butter up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; boil and char the corn on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 limes
  1. Boil the corn.
  2. Zest and juice the limes and mix with the soft butter and chilli.
  3. Brush the corn with the butter.
  4. Char on a hot grill, turning, until blistered all over.
  5. Cut into thick rounds and pile high.

Mains

Sausage and mushroom ragu polenta

Sausage ragu over soft polenta

Prep 12 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Make the ragu up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; cook the polenta fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Italian sausages
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 200g polenta
  • 1l water
  • 60g butter
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Squeeze the sausages from their skins and slice the mushrooms.
  2. Brown the sausage meat in oil, add the mushrooms and tomatoes and simmer.
  3. Cook the polenta in the boiling water, stirring, until thick.
  4. Beat in the butter and parmesan.
  5. Spoon the ragu over the polenta.

Sweets

Golden honeycomb toffeeVegetarian

Crunchy golden syrup honeycomb

Prep 7 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tsp bicarb soda
  1. Line a tin with baking paper.
  2. Boil the sugar, golden syrup and honey to 150C.
  3. Off the heat, whisk in the bicarb soda so it foams, and pour into the tin.
  4. Leave to set.
  5. Break into amber shards.

Sweet almond milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Blanched almonds blended with cold water and strained, sweetened with vanilla and clove, over ice

Prep 15 min, cook 540 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g blanched almonds
  • 1l cold water
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch ground cloves
  • 30g flaked almonds
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Soak the almonds in water overnight and drain.
  2. Soak.
  3. Blend with the cold water and strain through a clean cloth, squeezing well.
  4. Stir in the sugar, vanilla and cloves, then chill.
  5. Toast the flaked almonds in a dry pan.
  6. Pour over ice in four glasses and scatter with the almonds.
