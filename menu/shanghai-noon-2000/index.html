Snacks

Imperial court dumplings

Steamed pork and shrimp dumplings with soy and black vinegar.

Prep 34 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 month ahead and freeze on a tray; steam straight from frozen, a few minutes longer
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar
  1. Mix the pork, prawns, ginger and spring onion.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat.
  3. Steam until cooked through, 8 min.
  4. Serve with soy and black vinegar.

Chon Wang cornbreadVegetarian

Skillet cornbread, served warm with honey butter.

Prep 3 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, wrapped, and rewarm for 10 minutes; the honey butter keeps 3 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 120g butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Heat a buttered cast iron pan in a 220°C oven, 5 min.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, buttermilk, egg and melted butter briefly.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake, 20 min.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with honey and serve alongside.

Mains

Roy O'Bannon's five-spice ribs

Five spice pork ribs baked slow and glazed with hoisin and honey.

Prep 20 min, cook 2 hr 38 min · Make ahead: Rub and wrap the ribs up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake low and slow, then glaze and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork ribs
  • 2 tsp five spice
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Rub the ribs with five spice, brown sugar, crushed garlic and salt, 10 min.
  2. Wrap in foil.
  3. Bake at 150°C until tender, 2 hr 30 min.
  4. Mix the hoisin with honey and unwrap the ribs.
  5. Brush with the glaze and glaze under the grill, 8 min.

Campfire steak with molasses beans

Sirloin over hot coals, sticky mustard beans.

How it is made
  • 200g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • cannellini beans
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1kg rump steak
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Rump steak seared over hot coals and sliced, with beans simmered slowly with bacon, onion, molasses and mustard until thick and sticky, and charred bread. Fry 200g diced bacon and an onion until golden. Add 2 drained 400g cans of cannellini beans, 3 tbsp molasses, 1 tbsp Dijon, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 300ml water and simmer 1 hour until thick. Season 2 large rump steaks, about 1kg, and sear over very hot coals or a grill pan for 4 minutes a side. Rest 5 minutes and slice. Serve with the beans and grilled crusty bread.

Sweets

Imperial almond jelly with lychees

Chilled almond milk jelly cut in diamonds over lychees.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min, 3 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp powdered gelatine
  • 60ml water
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 565g tin lychees
  1. Soak the gelatine in the water, 3 min.
  2. Heat the milk with the sugar, stir in the gelatine until dissolved and add the almond extract, 6 min.
  3. Set in a shallow dish for 3 hours.
  4. Cut into diamonds.
  5. Serve over the lychees with their syrup.

Drinks

Rye and jasmine tea highball

Rye whisky, honey, lemon and chilled jasmine tea topped with soda.

Prep 6 min, cook 4 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Steep and chill the jasmine tea up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; build over ice with the rye, honey syrup and lemon, then top with soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tsp jasmine tea
  • 400ml water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 180ml rye whisky
  • 1 lemon
  • 500ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the jasmine tea in water just off the boil, then strain, 4 min.
  2. Chill the tea, 30 min.
  3. Stir the honey into a little hot water to loosen it.
  4. Fill four highball glasses with ice and add rye, cold jasmine tea, honey syrup and lemon juice to each.
  5. Stir, top with soda water and add a strip of lemon peel.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Chon and Roy brawl in the saloon on the way to Carson City.
