Snacks

Imperial court dumplings

Steamed pork and prawn dumplings with soy and black vinegar

Prep 40 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 month ahead and freeze on a tray; steam straight from frozen, a few minutes longer
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar
  1. Mix the pork, prawns, ginger and spring onion.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat.
  3. Steam until cooked through.
  4. Serve with soy and black vinegar.

Chon Wang cornbreadVegetarian

John Wayne? Chon Wang. Pan cornbread, honey butter.

Prep 15 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, wrapped, and rewarm for 10 minutes; the honey butter keeps 3 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 120g butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Heat a buttered cast iron pan in a 220C oven.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, buttermilk, egg and melted butter briefly.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with honey and serve alongside.

Mains

Roy O'Bannon's five-spice ribs

Five spice pork ribs baked slow and glazed with hoisin and honey

Prep 20 min, cook 158 min · Make ahead: Rub and wrap the ribs up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake low and slow, then glaze and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork ribs
  • 2 tsp five spice
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Rub the ribs with five spice, brown sugar, crushed garlic and salt.
  2. Wrap in foil.
  3. Bake at 150C until tender.
  4. Mix the hoisin with honey and unwrap the ribs.
  5. Brush with the glaze and glaze under the grill.

Sweets

Imperial almond jelly with lychees

Chilled almond milk jelly cut in diamonds over lychees

Prep 15 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp powdered gelatine
  • 60ml water
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 565g tin lychees
  1. Soak the gelatine in the water.
  2. Heat the milk with the sugar, stir in the gelatine until dissolved and add the almond extract.
  3. Set in a shallow dish for 3 hours.
  4. Cut into diamonds.
  5. Serve over the lychees with their syrup.

Drinks

Rye and jasmine tea highballVegetarian

Rye whisky, honey, lemon and chilled jasmine tea topped with soda

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Steep and chill the jasmine tea up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; build over ice with the rye, honey syrup and lemon, then top with soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tsp jasmine tea
  • 400ml water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 180ml rye whisky
  • 1 lemon
  • 500ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the jasmine tea in water just off the boil, then strain.
  2. Chill the tea.
  3. Stir the honey into a little hot water to loosen it.
  4. Fill four highball glasses with ice and add rye, cold jasmine tea, honey syrup and lemon juice to each.
  5. Stir, top with soda water and add a strip of lemon peel.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Chon and Roy brawl in the saloon on the way to Carson City.
