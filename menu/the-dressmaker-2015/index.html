Footy final party pies with tomato sauce

Beef and black pepper pies, proper tomato sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; fill and bake the pies on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  1. Brown the onion and mince in the oil, breaking it up as it goes, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, tomato paste, Worcestershire and pepper, and simmer until thick, 20 min.
  3. Cool the filling, then line a greased 12-hole muffin tin with rounds of shortcrust, 15 min.
  4. Fill, top with rounds of puff, press the edges and brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C until puffed and deep golden, then serve hot with tomato sauce, 25 min.

Footballers dance asparagus rollsVegetarian

Asparagus, lemon butter, soft white bread.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Roll up to 4 hours ahead, cover with a damp tea towel and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches of asparagus, trimmed
  • 12 slices of soft white sandwich bread
  • 80g soft butter
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 tbsp chives, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Blanch the asparagus in salted boiling water for 2 minutes, then plunge into iced water and pat dry.
  2. Beat the butter with the lemon zest, chives and pepper.
  3. Cut the crusts off the bread and roll each slice thin with a rolling pin.
  4. Spread with the butter, lay a spear across each slice, roll up tightly and cut in half.

Pratt's counter sausage rolls with tomato chutney

Pork and fennel, flaky pastry, tomato chutney.

Prep 23 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Roll and freeze the sausage rolls up to a month ahead; bake from frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 40g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 3 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 125ml bush tomato or tomato chutney
  1. Mix the pork, onion, carrot, breadcrumbs, fennel and a good pinch of salt.
  2. Halve the pastry sheets, lay a log of filling along each and roll up, seam underneath.
  3. Brush with egg, scatter with sesame and cut into short lengths.
  4. Bake at 210°C until puffed and golden, then serve with the chutney, 25 min.

Sergeant Farrat's chicken à la king vol-au-vents

Creamy chicken, mushrooms and tarragon in puff cases.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to a day ahead and reheat gently; warm the cases to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 40g butter
  • 200g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp tarragon, chopped
  • 8 large vol-au-vent cases
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, then lift it out, 8 min.
  2. Cook the mushrooms and capsicum in the same pan until soft, 7 min.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock and cream, and simmer until it coats a spoon, 8 min.
  4. Return the chicken with the peas and tarragon and simmer until cooked through, 10 min.
  5. Warm the cases at 180°C for 5 minutes, fill generously and serve.

Wedding breakfast roast lamb with mint sauce

Garlic and rosemary leg, crisp spuds, fresh mint sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 1 hr 25 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the mint sauce up to 2 days ahead; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg, bone in
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 3 rosemary sprigs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes, in chunks
  • 500g pumpkin, in wedges
  • 1 cup mint leaves, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 80ml malt vinegar
  • 80ml boiling water
  1. Stir the sugar into the boiling water, add the vinegar and mint and set the mint sauce aside, 5 min.
  2. Cut small slits in the lamb, push in the garlic and rosemary, rub with oil and season well.
  3. Roast at 180°C for 1 hour 20 minutes for pink, adding the potatoes and pumpkin for the last hour.
  4. Rest the lamb for 20 minutes while the potatoes crisp up at 220°C.
  5. Carve and serve with the vegetables and mint sauce.

Molly's chocolate slice with vanilla creamVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate slice with whipped vanilla cream, for Irma's arthritis.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight; whip the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 180g butter
  • 250g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together over low heat, then cool a little, 5 min.
  2. Whisk in the sugar, eggs and vanilla, then fold in the flour, cocoa and a pinch of salt.
  3. Pour into a lined 20cm tin and bake at 170°C until just set, with a fudgy middle, 30 min.
  4. Whip the cream with the vanilla and icing sugar and serve on the cooled brownies.

Paris via Melbourne raspberry champagne cocktail

Raspberry syrup, a sugar cube, cold sparkling.

Prep 6 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the raspberry syrup up to 3 days ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 1 bottle Australian sparkling wine, chilled
  1. Simmer the raspberries, sugar and water for 5 minutes, then push through a sieve and chill.
  2. Drop a sugar cube and a spoonful of syrup into each flute.
  3. Top slowly with the cold sparkling and serve.
