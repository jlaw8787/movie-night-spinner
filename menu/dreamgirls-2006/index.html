Curtis's car lot Coney dogs

Beef franks in soft rolls under a fine spiced beef chilli sauce, yellow mustard and onion.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; warm the dogs and rolls to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 long soft bread rolls
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml beef stock
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Brown the beef mince with half the onion, the garlic and the oil, breaking it up very fine with a wooden spoon, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the chilli powder, cumin, paprika and tomato paste, then the stock. Simmer, stirring, until thick and saucy, and season, 20 min.
  3. Heat the dogs in a pan of barely simmering water, 5 min.
  4. Warm the rolls, put a dog in each, spoon over the chilli and finish with a stripe of mustard and the rest of the onion, 5 min.

Effie's fried green tomatoes with hot sauce mayoVegetarian

Thick slices of green tomato in a crunchy spiced polenta crust, fried golden.

Prep 22 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the hot sauce mayo up to 3 days ahead; crumb the tomatoes up to 1 hour ahead and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green tomatoes, in 1cm slices
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 125g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 120g fine polenta
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Salt the tomato slices and leave on a rack for 10 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, hot sauce and lemon juice.
  3. Set out the flour, then the eggs whisked with the buttermilk, then the polenta mixed with the paprika and cayenne. Coat each slice in all three, 8 min.
  4. Shallow fry in the oil over medium high heat, in batches, for about 3 minutes a side until golden.
  5. Drain on paper towel, sprinkle with salt and serve hot with the hot sauce mayo.

Rainbow Records Sunday fried chicken with baked mac and cheese

Buttermilk-soaked chicken in a crisp spiced coating, fried deep golden.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 17 min, 4 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead; make the mac and cheese up to 1 day ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg chicken thighs and drumsticks, bone in
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 250g plain flour, for the coating
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 300g macaroni
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour, for the sauce
  • 600ml milk
  • 250g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • 50g dry breadcrumbs
  1. Whisk the buttermilk, hot sauce and half the salt and turn the chicken through it.
  2. Marinate in the fridge, 4 hr.
  3. Boil the macaroni in salted water until just tender and drain, 10 min.
  4. Melt the butter, stir in the sauce flour, then whisk in the milk until thick. Stir in most of the cheddar and the mustard powder and fold through the macaroni, 12 min.
  5. Heat the oven to 180°C. Tip into a baking dish, top with the rest of the cheddar and the breadcrumbs and bake until golden and bubbling, 25 min.
  6. Meanwhile heat the oil in a deep heavy pot to 165°C. Mix the coating flour with the paprika, garlic powder, cayenne, pepper and the rest of the salt.
  7. Lift the chicken from the buttermilk, press firmly into the flour and fry in batches, turning, for 12 to 14 minutes until deep golden and cooked through.
  8. Drain on a rack for 5 minutes and serve with the mac and cheese.

Curtis Taylor's payola pork chops

Pan-fried and simmered in onion gravy, garlicky collard greens on the side, One Night Only.

Prep 12 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Wash and shred the greens up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork loin chops
  • 60g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bunch silverbeet or collard greens
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  1. Season the chops, dust in half the flour mixed with the paprika and brown in the oil for 3 minutes a side. Lift out.
  2. Fry the sliced onions in the same pan for 8 minutes until soft, stir in the rest of the flour, then whisk in the stock.
  3. Put the chops back with the thyme, cover and simmer for 20 minutes until tender in a thick gravy.
  4. Fry the sliced garlic in a little oil, add the shredded greens and a splash of water and cook for 6 minutes, then add the vinegar.
  5. Serve the chops smothered in gravy with the greens.

Lorrell and Jimmy's sweet potato pieVegetarian

Silky spiced sweet potato custard in a crisp shortcrust case, with soft whipped cream.

Prep 15 min, cook 1 hr 25 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; whip the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g orange sweet potato, peeled and chunked
  • 1 sheet frozen shortcrust pastry
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 160ml evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • pinch of salt
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Boil the sweet potato until very soft, drain well, 20 min.
  2. Mash until completely smooth and cool slightly, 5 min.
  3. Heat the oven to 180°C. Line a 23cm pie dish with the pastry, prick, line with baking paper and rice and blind bake, then remove the paper and rice, 20 min.
  4. Whisk the sweet potato with the butter, sugar, eggs, evaporated milk, vanilla, spices and salt.
  5. Pour into the pastry case and bake until set at the edges with a slight wobble in the middle, 45 min.
  6. Cool to room temperature, 1 hr.
  7. Whip the cream to soft peaks and serve with slices of the pie.

Drinks

New Year's Eve 1966 champagne cocktail

Sugar cube soaked in bitters under chilled sparkling wine, orange twist.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the sparkling wine and the flutes up to 1 day ahead; build each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 8 dashes of aromatic bitters
  • 600ml chilled sparkling wine
  • 1 orange, peel cut into 4 strips
  1. Drop a sugar cube into each flute and soak it with 2 dashes of bitters.
  2. Top slowly with the sparkling wine.
  3. Twist a strip of orange peel over each glass and drop it in.
