Snacks

Fievel's potato latkesVegetarian

Crisp grated potato and onion pancakes

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fry up to 2 hours ahead, then re-crisp on a tray in a 200C oven for 8 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g sour cream
  • 200g apple sauce
  • salt, to taste
  1. Grate the potatoes and onion and squeeze out the liquid well.
  2. Mix with the eggs, flour and salt.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in hot oil until crisp, turning once. Work in batches.
  4. Serve with sour cream and apple sauce.

Beef and dill pirozhki

Soft yeast dough buns filled with seasoned beef, onion and dill

Prep 40 min, cook 97 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and reheat at 180C for 10 minutes; the filling keeps 1 day in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 30g butter
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, warm milk, sugar and butter into a soft dough and knead briefly.
  2. Cover and let the dough prove.
  3. Heat the oil and fry the mince with the chopped onion and dill.
  4. Cool the filling.
  5. Cut the dough into rounds, fill, and seal.
  6. Bake at 200C until golden.

Mains

Mama's beetroot and cabbage borscht

Beef stock soup with beetroot, carrot, cabbage and potato, sharpened with vinegar

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 beetroots
  • 2 carrots
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 1/2l beef stock
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 120g sour cream
  • 8 slices rye bread
  1. Grate the beetroot and carrots, shred the cabbage and dice the potatoes.
  2. Simmer all the vegetables in the stock until tender.
  3. Add the vinegar and plenty of dill.
  4. Serve with a big spoon of sour cream and rye bread.

Sweets

Shostka honey cakeVegetarian

Spiced honey cake with brown sugar and coffee, baked in a tin

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g honey
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 125ml strong coffee
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a tin.
  2. Whisk the honey, oil, eggs, sugar and coffee.
  3. Fold in the flour, baking powder and mixed spice and pour into the tin.
  4. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool in the tin.

Drinks

Papa's fiddle-night Russian teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot black tea in glasses with lemon, raspberry jam stirred in for sweetness

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best brewed just before serving; slice the lemon up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp black tea leaves
  • 1l boiling water
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g raspberry jam
  1. Brew the tea in just-boiled water and strain into heatproof glasses.
  2. Add a lemon slice to each and serve with jam for stirring in.
