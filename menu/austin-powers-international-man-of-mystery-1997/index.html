Snacks

Carnaby Street devilled eggs with bacon

Boiled eggs piled with yolk mayo and crisp bacon, bald as Mr Bigglesworth.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 3 rashers bacon
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • salt, to taste
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook, then cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs and scoop out the yolks. Mash with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and salt until smooth.
  3. Grill the bacon until crisp and crumble finely.
  4. Spoon the yolk mix back into the whites, dust with paprika and top with bacon and chives.

Sixties cheese and pineapple hedgehog

Cheddar, pineapple and cabana on picks, frickin' laser beams on a rockmelon.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Cube the cheese and pineapple and thread the picks up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; spike the rockmelon to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1/2 rockmelon
  • 2 cloves
  • 250g vintage cheddar
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 150g cabana
  • 1 jar cocktail onions
  • 1 box toothpicks
  1. Cut the rockmelon into pieces and place one piece cut-side down on a platter as the body, with cloves for eyes.
  2. Cut the cheddar and pineapple into cubes and slice the cabana into rounds.
  3. Thread toothpicks with cheese, pineapple and either a cocktail onion or cabana.
  4. Push them all over the rockmelon until covered in spikes.

Mains

Dr Evil's one million dollar steak Diane

Sirloin in mushroom, brandy and cream sauce, fries and watercress.

Prep 2 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms and dice the shallot up to 1 day ahead; sear the steaks and make the sauce just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen shoestring fries
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 30g butter
  • 200g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 shallot, diced
  • 40ml brandy
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch watercress
  1. Bake the fries at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Bat out the steaks to about 1cm thick, season and sear in the vegetable oil in a very hot pan, then rest on a plate.
  3. In the same pan melt the butter and fry the mushrooms and shallot.
  4. Add the brandy and let it bubble off, then add the Worcestershire, mustard and cream and simmer.
  5. Pour the sauce over the steaks and serve with the fries and watercress.

Sweets

Union Jack berry trifleVegetarian

Jam sponge, sherry, raspberries, custard, cream and strawberry flag top.

Prep 25 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g jam rollette
  • 60ml sweet sherry
  • 200g raspberries
  • 600ml thick vanilla custard
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 250g strawberries, halved
  • 125g blueberries
  1. Slice the jam rollette and line a large glass bowl. Drizzle with the sherry.
  2. Scatter over the raspberries and pour over the custard.
  3. Chill until the custard firms up.
  4. Whip the cream with the icing sugar to soft peaks and spread over the top.
  5. Lay strawberries in a cross and diagonals, then fill the gaps with blueberries to make a Union Jack.

Drinks

Austin's gin martiniVegetarian

Gin and dry vermouth stirred over ice, with an olive. “Oh, behave!”

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses and the gin in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; stir with ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar green olives
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill the martini glasses in the freezer.
  2. Fill a jug with ice, add the gin and vermouth and stir steadily until very cold.
  3. Strain into the glasses. Twist a strip of lemon zest over each, or add an olive on a pick.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Austin is unfrozen in the nineties.
