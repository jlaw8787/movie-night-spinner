Snacks

Pocket totsVegetarian

Homemade grated potato tots fried until crisp.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Shape and freeze up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Parboil the whole potatoes, then cool, 15 min.
  2. Grate, squeeze dry and season with salt and onion powder.
  3. Shape into little cylinders and freeze, 30 min.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until golden and crisp all over, 10 min.

Idaho potato skins

Crisp potato skins with cheddar and bacon, sour cream and chives.

Prep 13 min, cook 62 min · Make ahead: Bake and scoop the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; crisp, fill and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 small potatoes
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 4 rashers of bacon
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C until tender, 45 min.
  2. Halve and scoop out most of the flesh. Cook the bacon until crisp and crumble, 12 min.
  3. Brush with butter and crisp at 220°C, 10 min.
  4. Fill with cheddar and bacon and bake until melted, 5 min.
  5. Top with sour cream and chives.

Mains

Napoleon's quesadillaVegetarian

Flour tortillas with cheddar and Monterey Jack, pan crisped, salsa and sour cream.

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheeses and dice the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the quesadillas just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g Monterey Jack, grated
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Fill the tortillas with the grated cheddar and Monterey Jack and fold in half.
  2. Cook in a dry pan over medium heat until golden and melted, turning once, 14 min.
  3. Serve with diced tomato salsa and sour cream.

Uncle Rico's sirloin steak and corn

Seared sirloin, cobs in paprika butter, smashed potatoes, if only Rico had thrown it in '82.

How it is made
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 50g butter
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Rump steak seared hard in a smoking pan, rested and sliced, with corn on the cob in paprika butter and a pile of crispy smashed potatoes. Season 4 rump steaks (about 220g each) with salt and pepper 30 minutes ahead. Boil 800g baby potatoes until tender, smash flat on an oiled tray and roast at 220°C for 25 minutes. Boil 4 corn cobs for 6 minutes and roll in 50g butter mixed with 1 tsp paprika. Sear the steaks in a smoking hot pan with 1 tbsp oil for 3 minutes a side for medium rare, rest 5 minutes, slice and serve with the corn and potatoes.

Pedro's campaign piñata cakeVegetarian

Hollowed butter cake filled with candy, covered in bright buttercream stripes.

Prep 18 min, cook 62 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 450g butter, soft
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 200g lollies
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp food colouring (assorted)
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and grease an ovenproof bowl. Beat half the butter with the sugar, then the eggs, 12 min.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk, pour into the bowl and bake, 50 min.
  3. Cool completely, 40 min.
  4. Hollow out the centre and fill with the lollies.
  5. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar, split it into portions and tint each with a different food colouring, then cover the cake in bright stripes.

Drinks

FFA blue ribbon chocolate malt shakeNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream and malt blended with milk, topped with cream and dark chocolate.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses and grate the chocolate up to 1 day ahead; blend just before serving so the shake stays thick
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk, cold
  • 6 scoops of chocolate ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • pinch of salt
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 20g dark chocolate
  1. Chill four tall glasses, 3 min.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream, malt powder and salt until thick and smooth.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Pour into the glasses, spoon on cream and grate chocolate over each.
