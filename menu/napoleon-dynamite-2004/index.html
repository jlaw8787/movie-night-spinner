Snacks

Pocket totsVegetarian

Homemade grated potato tots fried until crisp

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Shape and freeze up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Parboil the whole potatoes, then cool.
  2. Grate, squeeze dry and season with salt and onion powder.
  3. Shape into little cylinders and freeze.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180C in batches until golden and crisp all over.

Idaho potato skins

Crisp potato skins with cheddar and bacon, sour cream and chives

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Bake and scoop the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; crisp, fill and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 small potatoes
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 4 rashers bacon
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200C until tender.
  2. Halve and scoop out most of the flesh. Cook the bacon until crisp and crumble.
  3. Brush with butter and crisp at 220C.
  4. Fill with cheddar and bacon and bake until melted.
  5. Top with sour cream and chives.

Mains

Napoleon's quesadillaVegetarian

Flour tortillas with cheddar and Monterey Jack, pan crisped, salsa and sour cream

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheeses and dice the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the quesadillas just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g Monterey Jack, grated
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Fill the tortillas with the grated cheddar and Monterey Jack and fold in half.
  2. Cook in a dry pan over medium heat until golden and melted, turning once.
  3. Serve with diced tomato salsa and sour cream.

Pedro's campaign piñata cakeVegetarian

Hollowed butter cake filled with candy, covered in bright buttercream stripes

Prep 50 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 450g butter, soft
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 200g lollies
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp food colouring (assorted)
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and grease an ovenproof bowl. Beat half the butter with the sugar, then the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk, pour into the bowl and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Hollow out the centre and fill with the lollies.
  5. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar, split it into portions and tint each with a different food colouring, then cover the cake in bright stripes.

Drinks

FFA blue ribbon chocolate malt shakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream and malt blended with milk, topped with cream and dark chocolate

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses and grate the chocolate up to 1 day ahead; blend just before serving so the shake stays thick
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk, cold
  • 6 scoops chocolate ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • pinch of salt
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 20g dark chocolate
  1. Chill four tall glasses.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream, malt powder and salt until thick and smooth.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Pour into the glasses, spoon on cream and grate chocolate over each.
