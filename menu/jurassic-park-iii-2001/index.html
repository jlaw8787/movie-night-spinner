Snacks

Raptor egg devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs with Dijon and chives, dusted with smoked paprika.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp snipped chives
  • smoked paprika, to dust
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool and peel.
  2. Halve and mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, Dijon and chives until smooth.
  3. Pipe back into the whites and dust with paprika so they look speckled like eggs from a nest.

Isla Sorna tostones with garlic lime mojoVegetarian

Twice-fried green plantain, smashed and salted, with garlic lime mojo.

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  1. Peel the plantains and cut into thick pieces. Mix the garlic, lime juice, olive oil, coriander and a pinch of salt for the mojo.
  2. Fry the plantain at 160°C for 4 minutes until soft but pale. Drain and smash each flat with a heavy glass.
  3. Fry again at 190°C for 2 minutes until crisp and golden, then salt well.
  4. Serve hot with the mojo for dipping.

Parasail chilli lime prawn skewers

Green prawns with garlic grilled hot, mango and red onion salsa, for Eric's boat ride.

How it is made
  • 800g green prawns
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 red chilli
  • 3 limes
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 8 skewers
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Garlic, chilli and lime prawns threaded on skewers and grilled over high heat, with a sweet mango and red onion salsa. Toss 800g peeled green prawns with 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 chopped red chilli, the zest and juice of 1 lime and 2 tbsp olive oil, and leave for 20 minutes. Thread onto 8 skewers. Dice 2 mangoes with half a red onion, a handful of coriander and the juice of another lime. Grill the skewers over high heat for 2 minutes a side until pink and charred. Serve with the salsa and lime wedges.

Costa Rican rice and beansVegetarian

Rice and black beans with peppers and coriander, topped with fried eggs.

Prep 7 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Rice up to 1 day, fry eggs fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400g black beans, with their liquid
  • 500ml water
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tomatoes, diced, for the salsa
  1. Soften the onion and capsicum in the oil.
  2. Add the rice, beans with their liquid and water. Simmer covered until the rice is tender, then stir through the coriander.
  3. Fry the eggs and mix the tomatoes with a little salt for a fresh salsa.
  4. Serve the rice topped with fried eggs and salsa.

Ellie's lemon and thyme roast chicken

Whole bird with garlic, roasted till the skin crackles, crisp potatoes and gravy, for Grant's visit.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 50g butter
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 packet plain flour
  1. Whole chicken roasted with lemon, garlic and thyme until the skin crackles, with crisp roast potatoes, honey carrots and pan gravy. Stuff a 1.8kg chicken with 1 halved lemon, 4 garlic cloves and thyme, rub with butter and salt, and roast at 200°C for 1 hour 20 minutes. Parboil 1kg potato chunks for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in oil alongside for 50 minutes. Roast 500g carrots with 1 tbsp honey for 35 minutes. Rest the chicken, then stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the pan juices with 300ml chicken stock and simmer until thick.

Pteranodon nest pavlovasVegetarian

Small meringue nests filled with whipped cream and passionfruit.

Prep 25 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 12 blueberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Pipe into small nests and bake at 120°C. Cool in the oven.
  3. Fill with whipped cream and passionfruit and add three blueberries to each like eggs.

Pineapple, orange and passionfruit juice topped with soda water, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L pineapple juice
  • 1 L orange juice
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 1 L cold soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Pour the pineapple and orange juice into a big jug. Scoop in the passionfruit pulp and stir well.
  2. Top with soda water and plenty of ice. Pour into glasses.
