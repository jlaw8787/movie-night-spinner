Snacks

Amity Island fried calamari

Buttermilk soaked calamari in a cornmeal crust, with tartare and lemon

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 500g calamari
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp tartare sauce
  • 1 lemon
  1. Cut the calamari into strips and soak in the buttermilk.
  2. Soak.
  3. Dredge in cornmeal mixed with flour.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil to 185C and fry in batches.
  5. Serve with tartare and lemon wedges.

Chum bucket dip

Hot smoked trout, cream cheese, dill and horseradish dip with crackers

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g hot-smoked trout
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Flake the trout.
  2. Mix into the cream cheese and sour cream with lemon juice, chopped dill and horseradish.
  3. Spoon into a small bucket and serve with crackers.

Mains

Lobster rolls

Lobster salad with celery and chives in a toasted buttered bun, homemade chips

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix the lobster filling up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; fry the chips and toast the buns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked lobster meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 30g butter
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips.
  2. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil once at a lower heat, then again hot until crisp.
  3. Chop the lobster and fold with the mayonnaise, lemon juice, diced celery and chives.
  4. Butter and toast the split buns and fill.

Sweets

Amity Island sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla and chocolate ice cream, hot fudge, peanuts and a chocolate fin

Prep 10 min, then 32 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the chocolate fins and the fudge sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; whip the cream and scoop on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml chocolate ice cream
  • 50g peanuts
  1. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream. Spread a little into fin shapes on baking paper and chill.
  2. Chill the fins.
  3. Whip the remaining cream and chop the peanuts.
  4. Scoop both ice creams into bowls, pour over the fudge and top with cream, peanuts and a fin.

Drinks

Quint's chilli lime lager michelada

Lager with lime, hot sauce and tomato juice, in a chilli salt rimmed glass

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Mix the chilli salt up to 1 week ahead and juice the limes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; build each glass with cold lager to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 375ml cans lager
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 12 dashes hot sauce
  • 4 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • 240ml tomato juice
  • ice, to serve
  1. Mix the chilli flakes with the salt on a plate.
  2. Rub the rims of four pint glasses with lime and dip in the salt. Fill with ice.
  3. Add lime juice, hot sauce, Worcestershire sauce and tomato juice to each glass.
  4. Top each with cold lager, stir once and crush the can.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Quint and Hooper compare scars.
