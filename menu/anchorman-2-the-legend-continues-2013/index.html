Anchorman 2: The Legend Continues (2013) movie night dinner menu

Ron Burgundy and his old news team land jobs at the first 24-hour news channel in New York. Yes, the news is now open 24 hours. So is the kitchen.

The menu is set at GNN Newsroom Canteen, from the world of the film.

Snacks

Ron's lighthouse wings

Floured fried wings tossed in a honey, sriracha and soy glaze

Prep 6 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the glaze up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; fry the wings just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 100ml honey
  • 3 tbsp sriracha
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Toss the chicken wings in the flour seasoned with paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C for 10 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  3. Simmer the honey, the sriracha and the soy until glossy and toss the wings through.

Doby's lighthouse fish bites

Panko crumbed white fish bites with lemon and tartare sauce

Prep 10 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 3 hours ahead and keep chilled; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g white fish fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g tartare sauce
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Cut the white fish into bite-size pieces.
  2. Roll in the flour, the beaten eggs, then the panko and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C for 3 minutes until golden.
  3. Serve with lemon wedges and the tartare sauce.

Mains

GNN late shift steak

Garlic butter sirloin with oven fries and creamed spinach

Prep 5 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; crush the garlic up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 50g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 400g spinach
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Bake the frozen chips following the packet.
  2. Season the sirloin steaks and sear in a very hot pan for 3 minutes a side, then baste with the butter and the crushed garlic cloves.
  3. Rest 5 minutes.
  4. Serve with the oven fries and the spinach wilted with the cream.

Champ Kind's Chicken of the Cave

Buttermilk fried pieces with hot honey, pickles and crunchy slaw, definitely not bat

Prep 12 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 300g white cabbage
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 100g dill pickles
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with half the paprika and salt for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Mix the flour with the rest of the paprika, the garlic powder and salt. Toss the chicken in it, pressing to coat.
  3. Heat the oil to 170°C and fry in batches for 6 to 7 minutes until deep golden and cooked through. Drain on a rack.
  4. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes.
  5. Toss the shredded cabbage with the mayonnaise and salt and serve with the chicken, hot honey and pickles.

Sweets

Dark chocolate fondue

Warm dark chocolate and cream fondue for dipping strawberries

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate sauce up to 2 days ahead, keep it in the fridge and reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries
  • 150g marshmallows
  • 1 sponge cake
  1. Melt the dark chocolate with the cream and a pinch of salt over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  2. Pour into a warm bowl and serve with the strawberries, the marshmallows and a cut-up sponge cake for dipping.

Drinks

Ron's Scotch Rob RoyVegetarian

Blended Scotch, sweet vermouth and bitters, stirred and chilled

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml blended Scotch whisky
  • 100ml sweet vermouth
  • 1 tsp aromatic bitters
  • 4 brandied cherries
  • 1 orange
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Chill the coupes in the freezer.
  2. For each drink, fill a mixing glass with ice and add the blended Scotch, the sweet vermouth and a few dashes of aromatic bitters.
  3. Stir for 30 seconds until very cold.
  4. Strain into the chilled coupe and drop in a brandied or maraschino cherry.
  5. Twist a strip of orange peel over the top and discard it.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the news team gets back together.
