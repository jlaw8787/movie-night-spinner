Alpine gruyère fondueVegetarian

Gruyère and emmental melted with white wine, bread and boiled potatoes for dunking.

Prep 9 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheeses and boil the baby potatoes up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; make the fondue on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 300ml dry white wine
  • 200g gruyère
  • 200g emmental
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp kirsch
  • nutmeg, to taste
  • 1 baguette
  • 500g baby potatoes
  • 1 jar cornichons
  1. Rub a heavy pot with a halved garlic clove.
  2. Heat the dry white wine until just simmering, 3 min.
  3. Toss the grated gruyère and the grated emmental with the cornflour and add a handful at a time, stirring in a figure eight until smooth.
  4. Stir in the kirsch or lemon juice and nutmeg.
  5. Keep warm over a burner and serve with the cubed baguette, the boiled baby potatoes and cornichons, 18 min.

Eighties pepperoni pizza rolls

Spiral pizza dough rolls with pepperoni and mozzarella, marinara to dip.

Prep 23 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Roll and slice the pizza rolls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake and brush with garlic butter on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 100ml pizza sauce
  • 100g sliced pepperoni
  • 150g mozzarella
  • 30g butter
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 200ml marinara sauce
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C, 6 min.
  2. Roll the pizza dough into a 30 x 40cm rectangle.
  3. Spread with the pizza sauce and scatter the sliced pepperoni and the grated mozzarella.
  4. Roll up from the long side and cut into slices.
  5. Place in a lined tin, bake 20 to 25 minutes and brush with the butter melted with the garlic clove.
  6. Warm the marinara for dipping, 5 min.

Great White Buffalo chilli

Slow-simmered beef chilli with cumin and chilli powder.

Prep 38 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Cook the chilli up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled or freeze up to 3 months; reheat and add the toppings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ground cumin, to taste
  • chilli powder, to taste
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 800g tinned tomatoes
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 20g dark chocolate
  • 2 x 400g tins of kidney beans
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Brown the beef mince in a heavy pot, 5 min.
  2. Add the diced onion and the garlic cloves for 5 minutes, then the cumin, the chilli powder and the chipotle in adobo.
  3. Stir in the tinned tomatoes, the beef stock and the dark chocolate and simmer 45 minutes.
  4. Add the drained tins kidney beans for the last 15 minutes.
  5. Serve with sour cream, grated cheddar, pickled jalapeños and crusty bread.

Lodge chicken schnitzel

Breaded chicken breast, warm potato salad, dill cucumbers and lemon.

Prep 4 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large chicken breasts, halved and flattened
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 150ml vegetable oil
  • 800g chat potatoes
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers, sliced
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then toss warm with the mayonnaise and mustard, 20 min.
  2. Dust the chicken in flour, dip in beaten egg and coat in panko.
  3. Fry until golden and cooked through, 10 min.
  4. Toss the cucumber with chopped dill and lemon juice and serve with the schnitzel and lemon wedges.

Snowcapped fudge browniesVegetarian

Dark chocolate fudge brownies under thick powdered sugar, vanilla ice cream.

Prep 7 min, cook 62 min · Make ahead: Bake and cut up to 2 days ahead, airtight; dust with icing sugar on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 180g butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • icing sugar, to taste
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate with the butter.
  3. Whisk in the caster sugar, then the eggs one at a time until glossy.
  4. Fold in the plain flour, the cocoa and a pinch of salt.
  5. Pour into a lined 20 x 30cm tin and bake 25 minutes so the middle is still soft.
  6. Cool completely, cut into squares and dust heavily with icing sugar.
  7. Serve with vanilla ice cream.

Chernobly citrus ginger fizz

Fresh ginger syrup, lemon and lime topped with soda, vodka optional.

Prep 6 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Simmer, cool and strain the ginger syrup up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled; build over ice with the citrus juice and soda water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 80g ginger
  • 100g sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 limes
  • 120ml vodka
  • 750ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 cups ice, as needed
  1. Simmer the sliced ginger with the sugar and the water for 10 minutes, cool and strain.
  2. Fill the tall glasses with ice.
  3. Add the ginger syrup, the lemon juice and the lime juice to each, plus the vodka if you like.
  4. Top with the soda water, stir and add a lime wheel and mint sprig.
