Snacks

Honey chilli roasted vegetablesVegetarian

Broccolini, purple carrots and radishes roasted with honey and chilli, herb sauce

Prep 16 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Halve the vegetables up to 1 day ahead and make the dip up to 2 days ahead; roast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 1 bunch purple carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 handful mixed soft herbs
  1. Halve the broccolini, carrots and radishes.
  2. Toss with oil, honey and chilli flakes and roast at 220C until charred at the edges.
  3. Blend the herbs into the yoghurt for a green dip.
  4. Serve the vegetables on a dark plate with the dip.

Mains

Chicken, coconut black rice and pickles

Sliced grilled chicken thighs, coconut black rice, quick pickled carrot and cabbage

Prep 13 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Pickle the carrot and cabbage up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; cook the rice and chicken on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g black rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 carrots
  • 1/4 red cabbage
  • 100ml rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • salt, to taste
  1. Shred the carrot and cabbage and pickle them in the vinegar and sugar for 30 minutes.
  2. Simmer the black rice in the coconut milk plus a splash of water until tender.
  3. Brush the chicken thighs with the vegetable oil, season with salt and grill until cooked through, then slice.
  4. Serve the chicken on the purple rice with the pickles.

Sweets

Scott's book signing coffee and cookiesVegetarian

Brown sugar cookies with dark chocolate chips, baked soft and served warm

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 225g plain flour
  • 150g dark chocolate chips
  • 1 bag ground coffee
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Beat the butter and sugar, then the egg.
  2. Mix in the flour and chocolate chips and roll into balls.
  3. Bake on lined trays until golden at the edges.
  4. Brew small cups of coffee and serve with the warm cookies.

Probability storm cupcakesVegetarian

Butter sponge minis and one giant, like Scott's many copies

Prep 10 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, for the cupcakes
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 100g butter, softened, for buttercream
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and prepare mini cupcake cases and one large cake tin.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, beat in the eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Fill the mini cases with half the batter and bake.
  4. Bake the rest as one giant cupcake.
  5. Cool, beat the softened butter with the icing sugar and the red food colouring, and ice them all.

Drinks

Colour-changing quantum lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Blue butterfly pea tea poured over lemonade and ice

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep and chill the butterfly pea tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; build over ice and add the lemon to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 600ml lemonade
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the flowers in boiling water for 5 minutes.
  2. Strain and chill until deep blue.
  3. Fill four glasses with ice and lemonade, then pour the blue tea over.
  4. Add a squeeze of lemon and watch it turn purple.
