Snacks

Cheddar, pork pie and pickle board

Aged cheddar, pork pie, pickled onions, apple and crusty bread

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheddar and slice the pork pies up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; cut the apples and tear the loaf just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g aged cheddar
  • 2 pork pies
  • 200g pickled onions
  • 3 green apples
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Cut the cheddar into wedges and slice the pork pies.
  2. Cut the apples into wedges and tear the crusty loaf.
  3. Pile everything onto a big board with the pickled onions so guests can raid it at once.

Green salad with mint and pepitasVegetarian

Salad leaves, pea shoots, cucumber and mint with a lemon dressing

Prep 13 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Toast the pepitas up to 3 days ahead, airtight, and wash the leaves up to 1 day ahead; dress just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g mixed salad leaves
  • 1 punnet pea shoots
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves, torn
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 50g pepitas
  1. Toast the pepitas in a dry pan.
  2. Slice the cucumber and tear the mint.
  3. Toss the leaves, pea shoots, cucumber and mint, then dress with lemon juice and olive oil.
  4. Scatter with pepitas and pile lightly onto a pale platter.

Gollum's crispy flathead with tartareOn the printed menu

Crisp fried flathead with tartare, for riddles in the dark and a precious ring

How it is made
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko crumbs
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 150g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 jar gherkins
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 lemons
  1. Flathead pieces dusted in seasoned flour, fried in hot oil until crisp, served with tartare sauce and lemon wedges. Cut 600g flathead fillets into fingers and pat dry. Season 100g plain flour with salt, pepper and a pinch of paprika. Dust the fish in flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs, then coat in 120g panko crumbs. Shallow fry in 1cm of oil at 180C for 2 minutes a side until golden. Mix 150g mayo with 1 tbsp chopped capers, 1 tbsp chopped gherkin, chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon. Serve the fish with the tartare and lemon wedges.

Mains

Sausage, bacon and eggs breakfast

Pork sausages, bacon, mushrooms, tomatoes and fried eggs

Prep 5 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; slice the mushrooms and halve the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick pork sausages
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 tomatoes, halved
  • 4 eggs
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Fry the sausages until browned.
  2. Add the bacon, mushrooms and tomatoes to the pan.
  3. Fry the eggs in the fat at the end.
  4. Toast the bread and butter it.
  5. Plate it all up.

Trolls' garlic and rosemary roast lambOn the printed menu

Leg studded and salted, roasted with potatoes and carrots, no dwarves on the spit

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 jar mint sauce
  1. Lamb leg rubbed with garlic, rosemary and salt, roasted at 180C for 90 minutes, with roast potatoes and carrots. Cut small slits all over a 2kg lamb leg and push in slivers from 4 garlic cloves and small rosemary sprigs. Rub with olive oil and plenty of salt and pepper. Roast at 180C for 90 minutes for medium, adding 1kg potatoes in chunks and 4 carrots tossed in oil to the tray for the last hour. Rest the lamb 15 minutes under foil. Carve thick and serve with the roast veg, pan juices and mint sauce.

Bilbo's seed cakeVegetarian

Butter loaf cake with caraway seeds

Prep 17 min, cook 50 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, softened
  • 175g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp caraway seeds
  • 2 tbsp milk
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, caraway seeds and milk.
  3. Pour into a lined loaf tin.
  4. Bake at 170C.
  5. Cool the cake.
  6. Slice to serve with a cup of tea.

Drinks

Dwarvish spiced ale punchVegetarian

Warm apple juice and brown ale with honey, cinnamon, cloves and orange peel

Prep 7 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Simmer the juice with the honey, spices and zest up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; reheat gently and add the ale just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml cloudy apple juice
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 orange
  • 750ml brown ale
  1. Peel strips of zest from the orange.
  2. Simmer the apple juice with the honey, 2 cinnamon sticks, cloves and zest.
  3. Turn the heat to low and add the ale, warming through without boiling.
  4. Ladle into mugs and add a cinnamon stick to each. For non-drinkers, swap the ale for ginger beer and serve over ice.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the dwarves raid Bilbo's pantry.
