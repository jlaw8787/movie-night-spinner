Snacks

Thing's finger crackersVegetarian

Buttery crackers shaped like fingers, with almond nails.

Prep 28 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, cold
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 20g flaked almonds
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then add the egg to form a dough.
  2. Roll into 16 finger shapes.
  3. Press a flaked almond into the tip of each and score knuckles with a knife.
  4. Bake at 180°C, 12 min.

Baked beets rose petal chipsVegetarian

Paper thin beets slices baked crisp with olive oil and flaky salt.

Prep 19 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best eaten within a day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 beetroots
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Slice the beetroots paper thin on a mandoline.
  2. Toss with oil and spread on lined trays.
  3. Bake at 150°C until curled and crisp.
  4. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Mamushka herring and beets rye

Dark toasts with pickled herring, sour cream and dill, roasted roots and horseradish cream.

How it is made
  • 3 beetroot
  • 8 slices dark rye bread
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 1 jar horseradish
  • 200g pickled herring
  • 1 red onion
  1. Dark rye toasts topped with pickled herring, sour cream and dill, and roast beetroot with horseradish cream, a Russian-style plate for the family dance. Wrap 3 beetroot in foil and roast at 200°C for 50 minutes, then peel and dice. Toast 8 slices dark rye and cut in half. Mix 100g sour cream with chopped dill. Mix 100g crème fraîche with 1 tbsp horseradish. Top half the toasts with dill cream and 200g pickled herring pieces and red onion. Top the rest with horseradish cream and beetroot. Finish with dill and pepper.

Mains

Gomez's candlelit roast

Rib roast salted overnight with garlic and rosemary, roast potatoes, horseradish.

Prep 10 min, cook 95 min, then 12.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Season the beef overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp rosemary, chopped
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  1. Season the beef with salt, garlic and rosemary overnight.
  2. Roast at 220°C, 20 min.
  3. Lower to 160°C and roast until 52°C inside, adding the potatoes.
  4. Rest, 20 min.
  5. Carve at the candlelit table with horseradish.

Grandmama's cauldron beef stew

Shin braised dark in red wine with carrot and mushrooms, over mash, for Gomez and Morticia.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef shin
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 375ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 150ml milk
  1. Beef shin braised black-dark in red wine with onion, carrot, garlic and mushrooms until it falls apart, ladled over buttery mash from a big black pot. Toss 1.2kg diced beef shin with 2 tbsp flour and brown in batches. Cook 2 onions, 2 carrots and 3 garlic cloves for 8 minutes. Add 2 tbsp tomato paste, 375ml red wine, 500ml beef stock, thyme and bay. Return the beef, cover and cook at 160°C for 3 hours, adding 250g mushrooms for the last 30 minutes. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 80g butter and 150ml milk. Serve from the pot.

Black forest cakeVegetarian

Chocolate sponge layered with kirsch, cream and morello cherries.

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 120g self-raising flour
  • 30g cocoa
  • 45ml kirsch
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 1 jar morello cherries
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Bake at 175°C, 25 min.
  3. Cool and split the cake, 25 min.
  4. Brush with cherry syrup and kirsch.
  5. Whip the cream and layer with the cherries.
  6. Shave dark chocolate on top.

Wednesday's blackberry lemonadeNo alcohol

Blackberry and fresh lemon lemonade over ice with mint.

Prep 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Blend, strain and chill the lemonade up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; add ice, blackberries and mint to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen blackberries
  • 150ml sugar syrup
  • 5 lemons, juiced
  • 1 L cold water
  • 2 cups ice
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Blend the blackberries with the syrup and lemon juice.
  2. Strain through a sieve, pressing hard.
  3. Pour into a jug with the cold water and stir.
  4. Chill, 30 min.
  5. Serve over ice with a few whole blackberries, a lemon slice and a mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the lemonade when Wednesday sets up her lemonade stand.
