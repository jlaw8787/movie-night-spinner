Snacks

Five-note crackers

Seeded crackers topped five ways: cream cheese, pâté, cheddar, salmon, fig

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving so the crackers stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 20 seeded crackers
  • 80g cream cheese
  • 80g chicken liver pate
  • 80g cheddar, sliced
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 1 sprig dill
  • 4 figs, halved
  1. Lay the crackers out in rows of five.
  2. Top each row with a different note: cream cheese, pate, cheddar, smoked salmon with dill, and fig.
  3. Serve straight away.

Muncie sweetcorn frittersVegetarian

Indiana sweetcorn fritters with sour cream and chives, from Roy's home town

Prep 15 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Make the batter up to 2 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 corn cobs
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml milk
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Cut the kernels off the corn cobs.
  2. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk, paprika and a good pinch of salt into a thick batter, then stir in the corn and spring onions.
  3. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat and drop in heaped tablespoons of batter, pressing them flat.
  4. Fry 2 to 3 minutes a side until golden and cooked through, in batches, and drain on paper towel.
  5. Serve hot with sour cream and snipped chives.

Mains

Mashed potato mountain

Buttery mash sculpted tall, with slow cooked onion gravy poured over

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the onion gravy up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and reheat; boil and beat the potatoes on the night so they stay light
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg floury potatoes
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 120g butter
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Peel and boil the potatoes until soft.
  2. Cook the onions slowly in some of the butter until golden.
  3. Stir in the flour, add the stock and simmer for gravy.
  4. Rice the potatoes and beat with the hot milk and the rest of the butter.
  5. Pile into a tall mountain and pour the gravy over.

Sweets

UFO meringuesVegetarian

Saucer shaped meringues with a dot of lemon curd underneath

Prep 25 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp lemon curd
  1. Whisk the egg whites and sugar over simmering water until hot, then beat to stiff peaks.
  2. Pipe into flat saucer shapes with a small dome in the middle.
  3. Bake at 100C, then cool in the oven.
  4. Spread a dot of lemon curd underneath.

Devils Tower cakeVegetarian

Tall chocolate cake with ridged chocolate buttercream

Prep 45 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Cake up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butter, soft
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 90g cocoa
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 350g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and prepare three small tins. Beat half the butter with the sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, some of the cocoa and the milk, divide between the tins and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and remaining cocoa for buttercream.
  5. Stack the layers tall, cover in buttercream and drag ridges with a fork.

Drinks

Neary kitchen strawberry lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh strawberry and lemon juice lemonade over ice, no alcohol

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g strawberries
  • 120ml sugar syrup
  • 5 lemons
  • 700ml cold water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Hull the strawberries, keeping a few to slice. Blitz most of them with the sugar syrup and strain into a jug.
  2. Stir in the lemon juice and the water.
  3. Add plenty of ice, sliced strawberries and lemon wheels.
  4. Pour into glasses and top with mint.
