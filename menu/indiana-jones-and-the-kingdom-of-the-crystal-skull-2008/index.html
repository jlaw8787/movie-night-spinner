Snacks

Canteen fries in paper conesVegetarian

Thick twice-fried fries with sea salt and ketchup, eaten between crates.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into thick chips
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp sea salt
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips, rinse and dry well.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C for 6 minutes, then rest.
  3. Fry again at 190°C until crisp, 10 min.
  4. Salt with flaky salt and serve in paper cones with tomato sauce.

Paprika devilled eggsVegetarian

Hard-boiled eggs with mustard and mayo yolks, paprika on top, laid out on a canteen tray.

Prep 9 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill the eggs up to a day ahead and keep chilled; dust with paprika to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 bunch chives, snipped
  1. Boil the eggs, cool in iced water and peel, 12 min.
  2. Halve them and mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  3. Spoon back into the whites and dust with paprika and chives.

Mains

Garlic drumsticks with green sauce

Charred garlic cumin drumsticks with cilantro jalapeño sauce and fries.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 20 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, roast fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken drumsticks
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 cup coriander leaves
  • 1 jalapeno
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Marinate the drumsticks in the garlic, cumin, paprika and lime juice, 15 min.
  2. Roast at 220°C until charred and cooked through, 40 min.
  3. Blitz the coriander, jalapeno, mayonnaise and a squeeze of lime for the sauce.
  4. Cut the potatoes into chips and fry them twice in the vegetable oil, first at a low heat then until crisp. Serve with the chicken, 40 min.

Meatloaf with mashed potatoes

Glazed beef meatloaf in thick slices over buttery mashed potatoes and gravy, government issue.

How it is made
  • 750g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  1. Mix the mince with the onion, breadcrumbs, egg, milk, Worcestershire and salt, press into a loaf tin and spread with the ketchup and brown sugar glaze. Bake at 180°C until cooked through, then rest. Boil the potatoes until soft, mash with the butter and milk and season. Slice the meatloaf and serve over the mash with gravy.

Sweets

Crystal skull Jell-O

Clear lemon Jell-O set with green grapes and blueberries, kept well away from the fridge.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, 4 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 gelatine sheets
  • 500ml water
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 lemons, juiced
  • 200g green grapes, halved
  • 125g blueberries
  1. Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water, 5 min.
  2. Heat the water, sugar and lemon juice, then stir in the squeezed gelatine, 10 min.
  3. Layer the grapes and blueberries in glasses and pour over the jelly.
  4. Set for 4 hours in the fridge.

Mutt's greaser malt shakeNo alcohol

Thick vanilla malted milkshakes in tall glasses.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 12 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and malt powder in batches until thick.
  2. Pour into tall glasses and serve at once.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Mutt and Indy meet at the diner.
