Snacks

Marshall College diner friesVegetarian

Thick twice-fried fries with flaky salt in paper cones, tomato sauce.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into thick fries
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp flaky salt
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick fries, rinse and dry well.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C for 6 minutes, then rest.
  3. Fry again at 190°C until crisp.
  4. Salt with flaky salt and serve in paper cones with tomato sauce.

Peruvian ceviche cups

Snapper cured in lime juice with red onion, chilli and coriander, sweet potato and corn.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g snapper fillets, very fresh, diced
  • 6 limes, juiced
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 1 sweet potato, boiled and cubed
  • 100g corn kernels
  1. Boil the sweet potato until tender, then cool and cube.
  2. Dice the snapper and cover with lime juice for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the onion, chilli and coriander. Serve in small cups with the sweet potato and corn.

Mains

Nazca chicken with green sauce

Charred garlic cumin drumsticks with green coriander jalapeño sauce, and fries.

Prep 5 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, roast fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken drumsticks
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 cup coriander leaves
  • 1 jalapeno
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into fries
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Marinate the drumsticks in the garlic, cumin, paprika and lime juice.
  2. Roast at 220°C until charred and cooked through.
  3. Blitz the coriander, jalapeno, mayonnaise and a squeeze of lime for the sauce.
  4. Cut the potatoes into fries and fry them twice in the vegetable oil, first at a low heat then until crisp. Serve with the chicken.

Peruvian lomo saltado

Wok-seared rump, onion, tomato and soy tossed with crisp chips, Peruvian style.

How it is made
  • 1kg frozen fries
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 700g rump steak
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Rump strips seared in a smoking wok with red onion, tomato, soy and vinegar, tossed with crisp chips and served with rice the Peruvian way. Cook 1kg frozen fries at 220°C until crisp. Cook 300g rice. Slice 700g rump steak into strips and toss with soy, garlic and cumin. Sear in a smoking wok in batches for 1 minute. Add a red onion in wedges and 2 tomatoes in wedges and toss 1 minute. Add 3 tbsp soy and 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, return the beef, toss with the fries and coriander. Serve with rice.

Sweets

Akator crystal skull jelly

Clear lemon jelly set with green grapes and blueberries.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 gelatine sheets
  • 500ml water
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 lemons, juiced
  • 200g green grapes, halved
  • 125g blueberries
  1. Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water.
  2. Heat the water, sugar and lemon juice, then stir in the squeezed gelatine.
  3. Layer the grapes and blueberries in glasses and pour over the jelly.
  4. Set for 4 hours in the fridge.

Mutt's greaser malt shakeNo alcohol

Thick vanilla malted milkshakes in tall glasses.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 12 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and malt powder in batches until thick.
  2. Pour into tall glasses and serve at once.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Mutt and Indy meet at the diner.
