Seoul street chicken

Double-fried crispy wings in a sticky gochujang, honey and garlic glaze.

Prep 17 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Pat the wings dry and leave them uncovered in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; fry and glaze just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Pat the wings very dry.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 160°C in batches until cooked, then rest on a rack.
  3. Fry again at 190°C until crackly and golden.
  4. Warm the gochujang with the honey, garlic and soy until glossy.
  5. Toss the wings through the glaze and scatter with sesame seeds.

Sokovia pierogiVegetarian

Cheddar potato dumplings boiled then fried with onions, served with sour cream.

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 60g butter
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with the cheddar and cool.
  2. Fill the wrappers with the potato mixture and pinch closed.
  3. Boil in batches until they float, then drain.
  4. Fry in butter with the onion until golden on both sides.
  5. Serve with sour cream.

Excelsior beef carpaccio crostiniOn the printed menu

Paper-thin seared fillet on garlic toasts, rocket and parmesan, while nobody lifts the hammer.

How it is made
  • 400g beef eye fillet
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 head garlic
  • 60g rocket
  • 60g parmesan
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 lemon
  1. Crisp garlic crostini topped with paper-thin seared beef, rocket, shaved parmesan, capers and a drizzle of lemon olive oil. Season a 400g eye fillet, sear hard on all sides for 3 minutes, then wrap tightly in cling film and freeze for 1 hour. Slice a baguette into 20 thin rounds, brush with olive oil and bake at 180°C for 10 minutes, then rub with garlic. Slice the beef wafer thin. Top each crostini with rocket and beef, then shaved parmesan from 60g, a few capers, lemon juice and olive oil.

Farmhouse roast chicken and vegetables

Roast chicken, potatoes and carrots at the Barton farm, where the team hides.

Prep 10 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Rub the chickens with lemon, thyme, oil and salt up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 whole chickens
  • 2 lemons
  • 6 thyme sprigs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1.5kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 6 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  1. Rub the chickens with lemon, thyme, oil and salt and set in a large roasting tray with the potatoes and carrots.
  2. Roast at 200°C until the chicken is golden and cooked through and the vegetables are tender.
  3. Rest the chickens on a board.
  4. Make gravy in the tray with the flour, juices and stock.
  5. Carve and serve at a big farmhouse table.

Hulkbuster boerewors with pap and relishOn the printed menu

Coriander-spiced beef coils over coals, buttery maize porridge, after Wanda gets in Hulk's head.

How it is made
  • 1kg thick beef sausages
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 jar ground coriander
  • 1 jar curry powder
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 250g fine polenta
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Coriander-spiced beef sausage coils grilled over coals, with buttery maize meal pap and a warm tomato and onion relish. Grill 1kg thick beef sausages over medium coals for 15 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through. For the relish, soften 2 sliced onions in oil, add 1 tsp ground coriander, 1 tsp curry powder, 4 chopped tomatoes and 1 tbsp sugar, and simmer 15 minutes. Bring 1L salted water to the boil, whisk in 250g fine polenta and cook, stirring, for 10 minutes, then beat in 40g butter.

Floating city pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue, cream and berries, a floating Sokovia you can eat.

Prep 30 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Meringue up to 1 day, keep airtight; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries
  • 125g blueberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  3. Shape into a round on a lined tray.
  4. Bake at 120°C, then switch the oven off and cool the meringue inside.
  5. Whip the cream, spread over the meringue and top with the strawberries and blueberries.

Asgardian mead punch, not for mortal menVegetarian

Mead with honey, cinnamon and clove syrup, lemon and ginger beer over ice.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 cloves
  • 500ml mead
  • 1 lemon
  • 500ml ginger beer
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Warm the honey with the water, cinnamon and cloves until the honey melts.
  2. Cool and strain the syrup.
  3. In a jug, mix the mead, honey syrup and lemon juice.
  4. Add plenty of ice, top with ginger beer and pour into glasses with orange slices.
