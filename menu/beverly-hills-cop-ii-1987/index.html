Snacks

Beverly Hills crudités

Baby vegetables, crisp and cold, with a green herb dip.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 4 tarragon sprigs
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 2 anchovies
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 200g baby carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 150g sugar snap peas
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Blitz the mayonnaise, sour cream, parsley, tarragon, chives, anchovies and lemon juice until green and smooth.
  2. Cut the baby carrots, radishes and cucumber into sticks or halves and trim the sugar snap peas.
  3. Arrange the vegetables on ice around the bowl of dip.

Axel's Detroit pierogiVegetarian

Potato and cheese pierogi fried in butter with onions, a taste of Detroit.

Prep 5 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Fry the onions up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat in the pan; boil and fry the pierogi just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen potato and cheese pierogi
  • 2 onions
  • 50g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Fry the onions in butter until golden.
  3. Boil the pierogi, then drain.
  4. Add the pierogi to the onions and fry until crisp on each side.
  5. Serve with sour cream and chopped dill.

Mansion party bacon and gruyère quiches

Shortcrust bites of bacon, onion, cheese and set custard.

How it is made
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 3 bacon rashers
  • 1 onion
  • 80g gruyère
  • 3 eggs
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Bite-size shortcrust cases filled with smoky bacon, caramelised onion, gruyère and a just-set egg custard, finished with chives. Heat the oven to 180°C. Cut 24 rounds from 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry and press into mini muffin trays. Fry 3 diced bacon rashers with 1 thinly sliced onion for 10 minutes until golden and divide between the cases with 80g grated gruyère. Whisk 3 eggs with 200ml cream, salt and pepper, pour into the cases and bake 15 minutes until just set. Top with snipped chives.

Mains

Empyrean Fields rib eye with peppercorn sauce and corn

Rib eyes and corn cobs off the coals, peppercorn sauce, potato salad.

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Chimichurri up to 2 days ahead and potato salad up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; grill the steaks and corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 chilli
  • 60ml red wine vinegar
  • 125ml olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 4 spring onions
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 4 corn cobs
  1. Blitz the parsley, oregano, garlic, chilli, vinegar and oil for the peppercorn sauce.
  2. Boil the potatoes until tender and drain.
  3. Dress the potatoes with mayonnaise and sliced spring onion.
  4. Grill the rib eyes and corn over hot coals.
  5. Rest the steaks briefly and serve with peppercorn sauce.

Johnny Wishbone's club sandwich and fries

Triple-decker of roast chicken, crisp bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayo, hot salted shoestrings.

How it is made
  • 800g frozen shoestring fries
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 400g roast chicken
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 jar whole egg mayo
  1. Triple-decker toasted club of roast chicken, crisp bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayo, pinned and cut into triangles with hot salted fries. Bake 800g frozen shoestring fries at 220°C for 20 minutes, turning once, and salt them. Grill 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Toast 12 slices white bread and spread with mayo. Layer each sandwich toast, 100g sliced roast chicken and lettuce, toast, 2 bacon rashers and sliced tomato, toast. Pin each with 4 skewers, cut into 4 triangles and serve with the fries.

Sweets

Cement truck banana splitVegetarian

Banana, three ice creams, fudge, nuts and a cherry.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate sauce up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm gently; split the bananas, scoop and whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bananas
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 4 scoops of chocolate ice cream
  • 4 scoops of strawberry ice cream
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 250ml cream
  • 30g chopped nuts
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Split the bananas lengthways into long dishes and add a scoop of each ice cream.
  2. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream and pour over.
  3. Whip the rest of the cream.
  4. Top with whipped cream, nuts and a cherry.

Drinks

Dirty Rosewood's tequila sunrise

Tequila, orange juice and grenadine, poured in layers like a Rosewood sunset.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Squeeze and chill the orange juice up to 1 day ahead; build the glasses just before serving so the grenadine sinks
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tray ice
  • 180ml tequila
  • 600ml fresh orange juice
  • 60ml grenadine
  • 1 orange
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice. Add tequila and orange juice to each and stir.
  2. Slowly pour grenadine down the inside of each glass so it sinks. Do not stir again.
  3. Garnish with an orange wheel and a cherry.
