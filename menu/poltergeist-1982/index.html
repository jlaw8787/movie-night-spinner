Snacks

TV static popcornVegetarian

Buttered popcorn, pepper and sesame static. They're here.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Pop the corn up to 1 day ahead in an airtight tin; toss with the butter, salt, pepper and sesame seeds on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp black sesame seeds
  1. Pop the kernels in a lidded pot with a little oil.
  2. Toss with the butter, salt, pepper and sesame seeds until it looks like TV static.
  3. Serve in a bowl in front of the screen.

Stacked chair cheese towerVegetarian

Thick cheese slices stacked with crackers between each layer

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Slice the cheeses up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; stack just before serving so the crackers stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cheddar
  • 150g gouda
  • 150g Swiss cheese
  • 16 crackers
  1. Slice the cheddar, gouda and Swiss cheese thickly.
  2. Stack in crooked towers with a cracker between each layer.
  3. Push a long skewer through the middle to hold each tower. Make four.

Mains

Steak that stays put

Seared rump steaks basted in garlic butter, with baked potatoes

Prep 20 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Bake the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and reheat in the oven; cook the steaks and spinach on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 potatoes
  • 1kg rump steak
  • 40g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 300g spinach
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200C.
  2. Season the steaks.
  3. Sear in a smoking pan, then add the butter, garlic and thyme to baste and pull at 54C inside.
  4. Rest.
  5. Wilt the spinach with the cream and serve alongside.

Sweets

Ghost-white meringuesVegetarian

Crisp white meringue. This house is clean.

Prep 20 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1 pinch cream of tartar
  • 180g caster sugar
  1. Whisk the egg whites with the cream of tartar to soft peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until stiff and glossy.
  3. Pipe tall drops onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 100C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven.

Drinks

Cuesta Verde orange spiderVegetarianNo alcohol

Orange soda poured over vanilla ice cream

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best made on the spot; scoop the ice cream into the glasses and freeze up to 2 hours ahead, pour the soda at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 1 1/4l orange soda, cold
  1. Put scoops of ice cream in each of four glasses.
  2. Pour over the cold orange soda slowly so it foams.
  3. Serve straight away while it is still ice cold.

When to serve

Serve the steak when one crawls across the kitchen bench.
