Snacks

Uncle Albert's tea party sandwiches

Cucumber and cream cheese, egg and cress, and ham and mustard

Prep 32 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep covered and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 punnet mustard cress
  • 150g leg ham
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the eggs until hard, then cool.
  2. Mash the eggs with salt and mayonnaise.
  3. Spread bread with cream cheese and layer with cucumber.
  4. Fill more with the egg and mustard cress, and the rest with ham and a little mustard.
  5. Cut off the crusts, slice into fingers and stack on a tiered plate.

Mrs Brill's Cornish pasties

Pastry rounds filled with beef, potato, swede and onion

Prep 43 min, cook 59 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chuck steak, finely diced
  • 250g potatoes, diced
  • 150g swede, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • pepper, to taste
  • 4 sheets shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 20g butter
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 4 tbsp brown sauce
  1. Dice the steak, potato, swede and onion.
  2. Mix with the salt and plenty of pepper.
  3. Cut rounds about 20cm wide from the pastry.
  4. Pile the filling onto one half of each, dot with butter, fold over and crimp the edge.
  5. Brush with beaten egg and cut a small slit.
  6. Bake at 200C.
  7. Reduce to 170C and bake until golden and the filling is cooked.
  8. Serve with brown sauce.

Mains

Cherry Tree Lane roast

Roast beef topside, Yorkshire puddings baked in muffin tins, roast potatoes

Prep 35 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Make the batter up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; peel the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep in cold water. Roast the beef, potatoes and puddings on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef topside
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g plain flour, for the batter
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 100ml vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp plain flour, for gravy
  1. Season the beef and rub with oil.
  2. Whisk the batter flour, eggs and milk into a batter and rest.
  3. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  4. Roast the beef at 200C to medium.
  5. Rest the beef under foil.
  6. Meanwhile roast the potatoes in oil alongside the beef.
  7. Pour the batter into hot oiled muffin tins and bake until puffed.
  8. Cook the peas.
  9. Make gravy from the pan juices, flour and stock.

Mr Banks's chicken and leek pie

Chicken and leeks in a creamy thyme sauce under puff pastry, with buttered peas

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, in chunks
  • 2 leeks, washed and sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter in a wide pan.
  2. Add the leeks and cook until soft.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream, mustard and thyme leaves. Simmer 10 minutes until thick, then cool a little.
  4. Tip into a pie dish, top with the pastry, trim, cut a steam hole and brush with beaten egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C for 25 to 30 minutes until puffed and golden.
  6. Cook the peas and toss with a knob of butter to serve.

Sweets

Jolly Holiday raspberry granitaVegetarian

Raspberry and lemon ice scraped with a fork into flakes

Prep 15 min, cook 255 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 500g frozen raspberries, thawed
  • 1 lemon
  • 12 ice cream wafers
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water.
  2. Cool the syrup.
  3. Blend the raspberries with the syrup and lemon juice, then strain out the seeds.
  4. Freeze in a tray, scraping with a fork every hour.
  5. Serve in glass dishes with wafers.

Drinks

Admiral Boom's ginger cannon fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh ginger syrup in a sugar rimmed glass, topped up with bubbles

Prep 15 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 piece ginger (6cm), sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1l soda water
  • 30g crystallised ginger, cut in strips
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Simmer most of the sugar with the water and ginger.
  2. Cool and strain the syrup.
  3. Rub the rims of the glasses with lemon and dip in the remaining sugar.
  4. Fill with ice and add syrup and a little lemon juice to each.
  5. Top with soda water, stir and garnish with crystallised ginger.
