Snacks

Hotel minibar mixVegetarian

Nuts and chocolate, the overpriced stuff.

Prep 0 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 300g mixed nuts
  • 150g chocolate almonds
  • 100g pretzels
  1. Melt the butter with the brown sugar and rosemary, then toss through the nuts.
  2. Spread on a tray and roast at 170°C, stirring once.
  3. Cool completely, then mix with the chocolate almonds and pretzels.

Kevin's room service club sandwiches

Triple-decker of chicken, bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayo, part of a $967 hotel bill.

Prep 6 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken breast
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  1. Poach the chicken breasts in simmering water until cooked through, then slice.
  2. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  3. Toast the bread and spread with mayonnaise. Slice the tomatoes and shred the lettuce.
  4. Build 4 triple-deckers with chicken, bacon, lettuce and tomato. Cut into quarters and pin each with a toothpick.

Mains

Lobster mac and cheese

Macaroni in a Gruyère and cheddar sauce with butter-poached lobster, buttery crumbs, baked.

Prep 0 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lobster tails
  • 100g butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 600ml full cream milk, warm
  • 120g grated Gruyere
  • 100g grated cheddar
  • 400g cooked macaroni
  • 40g panko breadcrumbs
  1. Remove the lobster meat from the shells. Boil the macaroni and drain well.
  2. Gently poach the lobster meat in some of the butter, then chop.
  3. Melt more butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the warm milk and simmer. Stir in the Gruyere and cheddar.
  4. Fold through the macaroni and lobster and tip into a baking dish.
  5. Top with the panko mixed with melted butter and bake at 200°C until golden.

Sweets

Room service sundaeVegetarian

Hot fudge, caramel, nuts and a cherry, merry Christmas, ya filthy animal.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fudge up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 40g chopped mixed nuts
  • 1 jar sprinkles
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream for the hot fudge.
  2. Whip the remaining cream.
  3. Pile the ice cream into four tall glasses, pour over the fudge, then add cream, nuts, sprinkles and a cherry on each.

Hot apple ciderVegetarianNo alcohol

Winter in Central Park with the pigeon lady.

Prep 3 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cloudy apple juice
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Peel strips of orange peel.
  2. Warm the apple juice with some of the cinnamon sticks, the cloves, peel and sugar over low heat without boiling.
  3. Strain into 4 mugs and serve steaming with a fresh cinnamon stick in each.

When to serve

Order room service when Kevin gets his own suite.
