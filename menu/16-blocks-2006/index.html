Snacks

Salt and vinegar pork crackling

Jack's stop at the liquor store before the walk starts

Prep 20 min, cook 745 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, re-crisp in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg boneless pork belly, skin on
  • 4 tsp malt vinegar
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Score the skin of the pork belly, pat dry and refrigerate uncovered overnight to dry it out.
  2. Rub the skin with 1 tbsp of the malt vinegar, then the salt.
  3. Roast skin up at 160C.
  4. Raise to 240C and roast until puffed and crackling.
  5. Rest, then cut into bite-sized pieces.
  6. Sprinkle with the flaky salt mixed with the last 1 tsp of malt vinegar. Serve with pickled onions and hot mustard.

Chinatown dumplings

Pork parcels crisp underneath then steamed, soy, black vinegar and ginger, for the hideout

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; fry the dumplings straight from frozen just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 frozen pork dumplings
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 120ml water
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp ginger, cut into fine matchsticks
  1. Mix the soy, black vinegar and ginger for dipping.
  2. Fry the dumplings flat side down in oil.
  3. Add the water, cover and steam.
  4. Uncover and fry again until crisp underneath.
  5. Serve straight from the pan with the dip.

Mains

Chopped cheese

Griddled beef mince and onion, American slices melted through, in a long roll with salad

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Dice the onion and slice the tomato and lettuce up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; cook the mince just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 600g beef mince
  • 6 slices American cheese
  • 4 hero rolls
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Fry the onion in the oil on a hot griddle until soft.
  2. Add the mince and chop it up with a spatula as it browns, seasoning well with salt and pepper.
  3. Lay the cheese on top and chop through until melted.
  4. Toast the rolls. Pile in the meat with lettuce, tomato and mayonnaise.

Sweets

Eddie's birthday cakeVegetarian

He wants to open a bakery and make birthday cakes

Prep 40 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 350g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 220g self-raising flour
  • 80g cocoa
  • 120ml milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 pack birthday candles
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line two 20cm tins.
  2. Beat the first batch of butter with the caster sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, half the cocoa and the milk.
  3. Bake in the two tins.
  4. Cool the cakes completely.
  5. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and remaining cocoa.
  6. Sandwich and cover the cakes with the buttercream and add candles.

Drinks

Bodega iced coffee with condensed milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Jack needs it, badly, for a long walk to the courthouse

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g ground coffee
  • 600ml water, boiling
  • 8 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 bag ice
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Brew the coffee strong with the boiling water and let it cool.
  2. Spoon condensed milk into each of 4 glasses.
  3. Fill with ice and pour the coffee over.
  4. Stir well until creamy and dust with cinnamon.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Eddie talks about opening a bakery.
