Chicharrones

Pork belly roasted till the skin shatters, salt and malt vinegar, pickled onions, hot mustard.

Prep 20 min, cook 1 hr 55 min, 10 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg boneless pork belly, skin on
  • 4 tsp malt vinegar
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Score the skin of the pork belly, pat dry and refrigerate uncovered overnight to dry it out.
  2. Rub the skin with 1 tbsp of the malt vinegar, then the salt.
  3. Roast skin up at 160°C, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Raise to 240°C and roast until puffed and crackling, 25 min.
  5. Rest, then cut into bite-sized pieces, 10 min.
  6. Sprinkle with the flaky salt mixed with the last 1 tsp of malt vinegar. Serve with pickled onions and hot mustard.

Pan-Fried Pork Dumplings

Six, crisp underneath, with soy, black vinegar and ginger to dip.

Prep 5 min, cook 13 min
Recipe for 4
  • 24 frozen pork dumplings
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 120ml water
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp ginger, cut into fine matchsticks
  1. Mix the soy, black vinegar and ginger for dipping.
  2. Fry the dumplings flat side down in oil.
  3. Add the water, cover and steam, 4 min.
  4. Uncover and fry again until crisp underneath, 4 min.
  5. Serve straight from the pan with the dip.

Chopped Beef & Cheese

Ground chuck and onions worked fine on the griddle, melted American, lettuce, tomato, mayo, toasted hero.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 600g beef mince
  • 6 slices of American cheese
  • 4 hero rolls
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Fry the onion in the oil on a hot griddle until soft, 5 min.
  2. Add the mince and chop it up with a spatula as it browns, seasoning well with salt and pepper.
  3. Lay the cheese on top and chop through until melted.
  4. Toast the rolls. Pile in the meat with lettuce, tomato and mayonnaise, 5 min.

Chocolate Birthday CakeVegetarian

By the slice, two cocoa layers, dark buttercream. Whole cake 16.00, any name on top.

Prep 40 min, cook 30 min, 20 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 350g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 220g self-raising flour
  • 80g cocoa
  • 120ml milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 pack birthday candles
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line two 20cm tins, 5 min.
  2. Beat the first batch of butter with the caster sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, half the cocoa and the milk.
  3. Bake in the two tins, 25 min.
  4. Cool the cakes completely, 20 min.
  5. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and remaining cocoa.
  6. Sandwich and cover the cakes with the buttercream and add candles.

Sweet Iced CoffeeNo alcohol

Seattle-style dark roast over ice, condensed milk from the can, pinch of cinnamon.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 60g ground coffee
  • 600ml water, boiling
  • 8 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 bag ice
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Brew the coffee strong with the boiling water and let it cool, 10 min.
  2. Spoon condensed milk into each of 4 glasses.
  3. Fill with ice and pour the coffee over.
  4. Stir well until creamy and dust with cinnamon.
