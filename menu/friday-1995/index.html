Snacks

Craig's peanut butter jelly toastiesVegetarian

White bread, peanut spread and strawberry jam fried golden, icing sugar, before Deebo rolls up.

Prep 11 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 160g smooth peanut butter
  • 160g strawberry jam
  • 40g soft butter
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Spread peanut butter on four slices of the bread and jam on the other four, then press together.
  2. Butter the outsides of the sandwiches.
  3. Cook in a frying pan over medium heat, pressing down, until golden and the filling is warm, turning once.
  4. Dust with icing sugar, cut into triangles and serve warm.

Porch ham and beef sliders

Small beef patties under grilled ham and melted cheese, soft buns, for a sacked man's day off.

Prep 13 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 200g leg ham
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 12 dill pickle slices
  1. Shape the mince into 12 small patties and season well.
  2. Cook on a hot pan for about 2 minutes a side.
  3. Brown folded ham slices in the pan, top each patty with ham and half a slice of cheddar, and cover for a minute to melt.
  4. Put in the slider buns with pickles.

Pineapple spearsVegetarian

Something cold and sharp for a hot day on the porch.

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 large pineapple
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 12 skewers
  1. Peel and core the pineapple and cut into 12 long spears.
  2. Heat a grill pan until smoking and char the spears for 2 minutes a side until marked.
  3. Mix the chilli powder, salt, caster sugar and the zest of the lime.
  4. Squeeze the lime over the warm pineapple, sprinkle with the chilli mix and push a skewer into each spear.
  5. Chill for 20 minutes before serving.

Mains

Porch day BBQ plate

A long hot day on the block.

Prep 4 min, cook 56 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 12 chicken drumsticks
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250ml barbecue sauce
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 40g butter
  • 500g potato salad
  1. Toss the drumsticks with salt, pepper and smoked paprika.
  2. Roast at 200°C until starting to colour.
  3. Brush with barbecue sauce and grill or roast, basting twice, until sticky.
  4. Boil the corn for 6 minutes and roll in the butter.
  5. Serve the chicken with the corn and potato salad.

Fried catfish sandwich

Friday on the block, hot from the market counter.

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw up to 4 hours ahead; fry the fish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g basa or catfish fillets
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp cajun seasoning
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 300g shredded cabbage
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 1 jar dill pickle slices
  1. Mix the cabbage with the mayonnaise, hot sauce and a pinch of the salt for the slaw.
  2. Cut the fish into 8 pieces and dip in the buttermilk.
  3. Mix the cornmeal, flour, cajun seasoning and the rest of the salt, and press the fish into it on all sides.
  4. Fry at 180°C in batches until crisp and golden, about 4 minutes a batch. Drain.
  5. Build each sandwich on white bread with two pieces of fish, slaw and pickles, and cut in half.

Sweets

Big Worm's conesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream in a hard chocolate shell, rolled in cornflakes and sprinkles, off the truck.

Prep 17 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make up to 1 week ahead, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 8 ice cream cones
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 3 tbsp coconut oil
  • 40g cornflakes, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil and let it cool to room temperature.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into the cones.
  3. Freeze the filled cones for 20 minutes (included in cook time).
  4. Dip each top in the chocolate and roll straight away in the crushed cornflakes mixed with sprinkles.
  5. Freeze until serving.

Drinks

Red drinkNo alcohol

A jug of red drink for a hot day on the block.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 500ml cherry juice
  • 1 L cold water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend the strawberries with the sugar and lemon juice, then strain into a big jug.
  2. Add the cherry juice and cold water and stir until the sugar is gone.
  3. Pour over plenty of ice in big plastic cups.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Craig and Smokey settle in on the porch.
