Poison Apple tavern applesVegetarian

Apple wedges dipped in a hard red candy coating.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Same day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 apples
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  1. Cut the apples into thick wedges and pat very dry.
  2. Oil a tray of baking paper.
  3. Boil the sugar, water, golden syrup and colouring without stirring until it reaches 150°C, 8 min.
  4. Dip each wedge quickly, letting the extra drip off, and set on the baking paper, 5 min.
  5. Leave to harden, 10 min.

Puss's chorizo and patatas bravas

Crisp potato cubes, smoky tomato sauce and aioli, sausage with honey, served with big sad eyes.

How it is made
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 chorizo sausages
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Crisp fried potato cubes with smoky tomato bravas sauce and garlic aioli, alongside sizzling slices of chorizo finished with honey and parsley. Boil 800g potato cubes for 6 minutes, drain, then roast with oil and salt at 220°C for 30 minutes until crisp. Simmer 1 tin crushed tomatoes with 1 crushed garlic clove, 2 tsp smoked paprika and a pinch of chilli for 15 minutes. Mix 120g mayonnaise with 1 grated garlic clove and lemon. Fry 2 sliced chorizo in a dry pan until crisp, then add 1 tbsp honey. Serve the potatoes with both sauces and the chorizo.

Far Far Away royal roast pork

Crackling pork shoulder with roast potatoes and apple sauce.

Prep 0 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Roast can be cooked 2 hours ahead and rested
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork shoulder, skin on
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g apple sauce
  1. Heat the oven to 230°C. Score the pork skin and rub well with the salt, 10 min.
  2. Roast the pork at 230°C to start the crackling, 30 min.
  3. Drop the oven to 170°C and keep roasting.
  4. Toss the potatoes in oil, add to the tray and roast with the pork, 1 hr.
  5. Rest the pork under foil, then carve and serve with the potatoes and apple sauce, 15 min.

Friar's Fat Boy drive-thru burgers

Double beef patties, cheese, bacon, onion rings and smoky sauce in soft buns, shoestring fries.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 cheese slices
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle barbecue sauce
  • 800g frozen shoestring fries
  • 300g frozen onion rings
  1. Double beef burgers with cheese, bacon, onion rings and a smoky sauce in soft buns, with shoestring fries, the way a fairytale drive-thru would do it. Roll 800g beef mince into 8 balls. Mix 4 tbsp mayonnaise with 2 tbsp barbecue sauce. Fry 8 rashers of bacon until crisp. Bake 800g frozen shoestring fries and 300g frozen onion rings at 220°C for 20 minutes. Smash the balls thin on a smoking hot pan, salt and cook 2 minutes, flip, add cheese and cook 1 minute. Toast 4 buns and stack 2 patties, bacon, onion rings and sauce in each.

Muffin Man's muffinsVegetarian

Buttermilk muffins full of blueberries, from the Muffin Man on Drury Lane.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour, for the muffins
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 200g blueberries
  • 50g plain flour, for the crumble
  • 40g cold butter, for the crumble
  • 30g brown sugar, for the crumble
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a 12-hole muffin tin, 3 min.
  2. Rub the crumble flour, cold butter and brown sugar together into rough crumbs, 4 min.
  3. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder in a large bowl.
  4. Whisk the buttermilk, melted butter and egg, tip into the dry mix and stir only until just combined.
  5. Fold in the blueberries, spoon into the tin and scatter with crumble.
  6. Bake until risen and golden, 20 min.

Giant gingerbread manVegetarian

Spiced gingerbread cookie cut in one giant man shape.

Prep 12 min, cook 32 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 120ml golden syrup
  • 375g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 bag of gumdrops
  1. Melt the butter, brown sugar and golden syrup together and let cool a little, 8 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, ginger, cinnamon and bicarbonate of soda to make a dough.
  3. Wrap and chill the dough for an hour.
  4. Heat the oven to 180°C. Roll out and cut one huge gingerbread man and lots of small ones, 12 min.
  5. Bake the small ones first, then the big one, until firm at the edges, 12 min.
  6. Cool, mix the icing sugar with a little water, pipe on and add gumdrop buttons, 7 min.

Happily Ever After blue lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade turned blue with raspberry cordial and a pinch of edible glitter.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Nothing to do ahead; pour the lemonade and cordial just before serving so it stays fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cold lemonade
  • 100ml blue raspberry cordial
  • 1 pinch of edible glitter
  1. Pour the lemonade into small bottles or tall glasses.
  2. Add a splash of cordial to each and stir gently.
  3. Drop in a pinch of glitter, give one more swirl and serve straight away.
