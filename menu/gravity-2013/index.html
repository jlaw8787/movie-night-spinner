Snacks

Tiangong dumplings

Pan-fried pork and cabbage dumplings with black vinegar and chilli oil.

Prep 32 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead, covered on a floured tray in the fridge, or freeze for 1 month; fry and steam on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 2 cups of shredded wombok
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the pork with wombok, ginger and spring onions.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat.
  3. Fry until crisp underneath, 4 min.
  4. Add water, cover and steam, 4 min.
  5. Serve with black vinegar and chilli oil.

Soyuz pelmeni

Boiled beef and pork dumplings tossed in butter, with sour cream.

Prep 33 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Fold the pelmeni up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, or freeze for 1 month and boil from frozen; toss in butter on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g beef mince
  • 200g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 32 dumpling wrappers
  • 30g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Mix the beef, pork and onion with salt and pepper.
  2. Fill the wrappers, fold into little hats and pinch closed.
  3. Boil until they float, plus 3 minutes.
  4. Toss in butter.
  5. Serve with sour cream and chopped dill.

Mains

Kowalski's spacewalk curry

Chicken thighs in garam masala tomato cream sauce, with basmati rice and naan.

Prep 16 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Simmer the curry up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; stir in the cream and kasoori methi, cook the rice and warm the naan on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 400g can crushed tomatoes
  • 1kg chicken thighs, diced
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 pinch kasoori methi
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 naan breads
  1. Soften the onion with ginger and garlic.
  2. Add the garam masala and tomatoes.
  3. Add the chicken and simmer, 25 min.
  4. Cook the basmati, 15 min.
  5. Stir in the cream and kasoori methi.
  6. Warm the naan, 3 min.

Crisp-skin sea bass, lemon caper butter

Sea bass seared till the skin crackles, brown butter, crushed potatoes and greens, back on land.

How it is made
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 4 barramundi fillets
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  1. Barramundi fillets pan-fried skin side down until crisp, with a nutty lemon and caper brown butter, crushed potatoes and a green salad. Boil 800g baby potatoes until tender, then crush with olive oil and salt. Score the skin of 4 barramundi fillets, pat very dry and season. Fry skin side down in hot oil for 5 minutes, pressing flat, then flip for 1 minute. Remove, add 60g butter and cook until nutty brown, then add 2 tbsp capers and the juice of a lemon. Spoon over the fish and serve with potatoes and salad leaves.

Sweets

Floating meringue cloudsVegetarian

Crisp meringue clouds with whipped cream and passionfruit.

Prep 20 min, cook 1 hr 30 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Meringues up to 3 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 4 passionfruit
  1. Whisk the egg whites with sugar to stiff glossy peaks.
  2. Pipe into clouds on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 100°C, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Cool in the oven, 1 hr.
  5. Whip the cream and serve the meringues with cream and passionfruit pulp.

Drinks

Mission control orange ginger fizzNo alcohol

Fresh orange juice with ginger syrup over ice, topped with sparkling water.

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min, 45 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the syrup up to 1 week ahead, then chill the mixed juice jug for 30 minutes or up to 1 day; top with sparkling water and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cm knobs of ginger, sliced
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 80ml water
  • 8 oranges
  • 1 lime
  • 800ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the ginger with sugar and water, then strain, 7 min.
  2. Cool the syrup and squeeze the oranges and lime, 15 min.
  3. Stir the juice and syrup together in a jug.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes.
  5. Fill four tall glasses with ice, half fill with juice and top with sparkling water, then add an orange wheel.
