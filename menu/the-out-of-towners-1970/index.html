Snacks

Raspberry Jam FingersVegetarian

Butter shortbread sandwiched with raspberry jam, dusted with sugar. Boxed for the handbag.

Prep 22 min, cook 28 min
Recipe for 4
  • 200g soft butter
  • 120g icing sugar, plus extra to dust
  • 250g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 120g raspberry jam
  1. Beat the butter and icing sugar until pale. Mix in the flour and cornflour to a soft dough.
  2. Press into a lined 20 x 30cm tin and prick all over.
  3. Bake at 160°C until pale golden, 28 min.
  4. Cut into 24 fingers while warm and cool in the tin, 20 min.
  5. Sandwich pairs with jam and dust with icing sugar.

Cocktail Franks

Glazed sticky with honey, mustard, brown sugar and Worcestershire, on picks with pearl onions.

Prep 1 min, cook 11 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cocktail franks
  • 20g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 jar cocktail onions
  1. Simmer the franks in water, then drain, 4 min.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, Dijon, sugar and Worcestershire in a frying pan.
  3. Add the franks and toss over medium heat until sticky and glazed, 5 min.
  4. Serve on toothpicks with cocktail onions.

Mains

Rib Eye Steak and Eggs

Seared to order and rested, two eggs fried, hash browns and buttered toast.

Prep 10 min, cook 32 min
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 80g butter
  • 800g rib eye steak, in four pieces
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 slices of sourdough
  1. Grate the potatoes and squeeze dry in a tea towel. Season and shape into 4 hash browns.
  2. Fry the hash browns in some of the butter over medium heat, turning once, 12 min.
  3. Sear the steaks in a very hot pan with the oil to about 54°C, then rest, 12 min.
  4. Fry the eggs in butter, 3 min.
  5. Toast the sourdough and butter it. Serve a steak, two eggs, a hash brown and a toast each, 5 min.

Sweets

New York CheesecakeVegetarian

Baked slowly in a water bath on a crumb crust, chilled overnight, crowned with sliced strawberries.

Prep 24 min, cook 75 min, then 23 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 120g digestive biscuits
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 400g cream cheese
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100g sour cream
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g strawberries, sliced
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a lined 18cm springform tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar, then mix in the sour cream, eggs and vanilla. Pour over the base.
  3. Wrap the tin in two layers of foil and stand in a roasting dish with hot water halfway up its sides, 5 min.
  4. Bake at 150°C until the centre gently wobbles, 1 hr 10 min.
  5. Cool, then refrigerate overnight.
  6. Top with strawberries and cut into portions.

Drinks

Dry Martini

Gin and dry vermouth stirred very cold, strained, one green olive.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 500g ice
  • 4 green olives
  1. Freeze the martini glasses, 5 min.
  2. Stir the gin and vermouth over ice in batches, then strain into the glasses.
  3. Garnish each with an olive.

When to serve

Serve dessert when George cracks a tooth on Cracker Jacks.
