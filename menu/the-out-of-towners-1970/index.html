Snacks

Handbag shortbread fingers with jamVegetarian

Pale butter biscuits sandwiched with raspberry jam, icing sugar, kinder than Cracker Jacks.

Prep 22 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g soft butter
  • 120g icing sugar, plus extra to dust
  • 250g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 120g raspberry jam
  1. Beat the butter and icing sugar until pale. Mix in the flour and cornflour to a soft dough.
  2. Press into a lined 20 x 30cm tin and prick all over.
  3. Bake at 160°C until pale golden.
  4. Cut into 24 fingers while warm and cool in the tin.
  5. Sandwich pairs with jam and dust with icing sugar.

Diverted flight cocktail franks

Little sausages sticky with honey, mustard and brown sugar, for the flight that lands in Boston.

Prep 1 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cocktail franks
  • 20g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 jar cocktail onions
  1. Simmer the franks in water, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, Dijon, sugar and Worcestershire in a frying pan.
  3. Add the franks and toss over medium heat until sticky and glazed.
  4. Serve on toothpicks with cocktail onions.

Mains

Hotel rib eye breakfast

Seared steak, two fried eggs, crisp hash browns and buttered toast, the breakfast George never gets.

Prep 10 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Squeeze and shape the hash browns up to 2 hours ahead, chilled; cook the steaks and eggs on the morning
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 80g butter
  • 800g rib eye steak, in four pieces
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 slices sourdough
  1. Grate the potatoes and squeeze dry in a tea towel. Season and shape into 4 hash browns.
  2. Fry the hash browns in some of the butter over medium heat, turning once.
  3. Sear the steaks in a very hot pan with the oil to about 54°C, then rest.
  4. Fry the eggs in butter.
  5. Toast the sourdough and butter it. Serve a steak, two eggs, a hash brown and a toast each.

Sweets

Checked-in cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked New York style in a water bath, chilled overnight and topped with strawberries.

Prep 45 min, cook 75 min, then 23 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 120g digestive biscuits
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 400g cream cheese
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100g sour cream
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g strawberries, sliced
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a lined 18cm springform tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar, then mix in the sour cream, eggs and vanilla. Pour over the base.
  3. Wrap the tin in two layers of foil and stand in a roasting dish with hot water halfway up its sides.
  4. Bake at 150°C until the centre gently wobbles.
  5. Cool, then refrigerate overnight.
  6. Top with strawberries and cut into portions.

Drinks

Hotel bar martiniVegetarian

Gin and a little dry vermouth stirred ice cold, frozen glass, olive, George has earned it.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Freeze the glasses up to 1 day ahead and mix the gin and vermouth in a jug in the fridge; stir over ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 500g ice
  • 4 green olives
  1. Freeze the martini glasses.
  2. Stir the gin and vermouth over ice in batches, then strain into the glasses.
  3. Garnish each with an olive.

When to serve

Serve dessert when George cracks a tooth on Cracker Jacks.
