Maple cheddar apple grilled sandwichesVegetarian

Aged cheddar, thin apple and maple butter on toasted sourdough.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the maple butter up to 3 days ahead; slice the apples and cook the toasties just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 crisp apples
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 250g aged cheddar
  • wholegrain mustard, to taste
  1. Mix the soft butter with the maple syrup.
  2. Core the crisp apples and slice very thin.
  3. Fill the slices of sourdough with the sliced aged cheddar and the apple, spreading a little wholegrain mustard inside.
  4. Brush the outsides with the maple butter.
  5. Cook in a pan over medium-low heat 3 to 4 minutes a side, pressing down, until golden and melting.
  6. Cut into triangles.

Shenanigans loaded cheese fries

Crisp fries under cheddar sauce, bacon, pickled jalapeño, sour cream.

Prep 6 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat with a splash of milk; bake the chips and crisp the bacon on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut chips
  • 150g diced bacon
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 2 spring onions
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Bake the frozen thick-cut chips at 220°C for 25 minutes until crisp.
  2. Fry the diced bacon until crisp, 7 min.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and simmer 2 minutes, then stir in the grated cheddar until smooth.
  4. Pile the chips on a platter, pour over the cheese sauce and top with bacon, sliced pickled jalapeños and spring onion.
  5. Serve with the sour cream.

Mains

Double bacon cheeseburgers

Two smashed beef patties, bacon and cheddar in a toasted bun.

Prep 5 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Mix the burger sauce up to 3 days ahead and roll the beef into balls up to 1 day ahead; grill the bacon and smash the patties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices of cheddar
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • tomato sauce, to taste
  • mustard, to taste
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 white onion
  • 4 burger buns
  1. Grill the bacon rashers until crisp, 10 min.
  2. Roll the beef mince into balls and smash flat on a very hot plate, season and cook 2 minutes.
  3. Flip, top each with cheddar and cook 1 minute.
  4. Mix the mayo, the tomato sauce, the mustard and the chopped pickles.
  5. Toast the burger buns, spread with sauce and stack the patties, bacon, pickles and diced onion in, 6 min.

Rabbit's first week chicken pot pie

Chicken and vegetables in creamy gravy under pastry. The rookie brings dinner.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300g green beans
  1. Brown the chicken in half the butter, add the onion and carrot and cook until soft, 12 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream and thyme, simmer until thick, then add the peas, 8 min.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spoon into a pie dish, cover with pastry and brush with egg, 5 min.
  4. Bake until golden, 30 min.
  5. Boil the beans and toss with the rest of the butter, 5 min.

Sweets

Syrup-chug maple bacon pancakes

Buttermilk pancakes with crisp bacon, whipped butter and maple syrup.

Prep 4 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Beat the butter up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; whisk the batter, grill the bacon and cook the pancakes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 150g butter
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 200ml maple syrup
  1. Whisk the self-raising flour, the sugar, the eggs, the buttermilk and the melted butter into a thick batter.
  2. Grill the bacon rashers until crisp, 12 min.
  3. Cook ladles of batter in a buttered pan over medium heat for 2 minutes a side until golden and risen.
  4. Beat the soft butter until fluffy.
  5. Stack the pancakes per plate with bacon and a spoon of butter, and pour over warm maple syrup, 4 min.

Homemade spiced colaNo alcohol

Citrus and spice cola syrup topped with soda, served by the liter o' cola.

Prep 8 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make, strain and cool the spiced syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep chilled; mix with soda water and ice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 orange
  • 2 limes
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • coriander seeds, to taste
  • 1 star anise
  • 200g sugar
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 1 L soda water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Put the zest of the orange, the lime and the lemon in a saucepan with the cinnamon stick, a little coriander seeds, the star anise and the water.
  2. Simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Melt the sugar until dark amber, carefully pour in the spiced water and stir until smooth.
  4. Add the vanilla and the juice of the lime.
  5. Strain and cool.
  6. Mix the syrup with the soda water in a 1 litre jug full of ice.
