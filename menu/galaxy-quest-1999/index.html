Snacks

Fan expo smoky garlic nut mix

Mixed nuts and pretzels baked in garlic, smoked paprika and Worcestershire butter

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • pinch of salt
  • 300g mixed nuts
  • 150g pretzels
  1. Heat the oven to 140°C. Melt the butter with the Worcestershire, garlic powder, paprika and salt.
  2. Toss with the nuts and pretzels until coated and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Bake, stirring twice, until toasted and dry.
  4. Cool and serve in paper cups.

Rock monster crackers

Black olive, caper and anchovy tapenade with jagged seeded cracker shards

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Tapenade up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g pitted black olives
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 3 anchovies
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 packet seeded crackers
  1. Pulse the olives, capers, anchovies, garlic, lemon juice and oil until coarse.
  2. Break the crackers into jagged shards.
  3. Serve the tapenade with the cracker shards.

Mains

Protector mess hall chilli

Beef and kidney bean chilli simmered 90 minutes, sour cream and cheddar

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 800g crushed tomatoes (2 tins)
  • 800g kidney beans (2 tins), drained
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and chilli powder.
  2. Add the tomatoes and kidney beans.
  3. Simmer until thick.
  4. Serve in bowls topped with sour cream and cheddar.

Jason's morning after turkey clubOn the printed menu

Toasted triple-decker of turkey, bacon, lettuce and tomato, chips, never give up, never surrender

How it is made
  • 800g frozen chips
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 400g sliced roast turkey
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 jar whole egg mayo
  1. Toasted triple-decker of roast turkey, crisp bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayo, cut into quarters and pinned with toothpicks, salted chips on the side. Bake 800g frozen chips at 220°C for 20 minutes and salt them. Grill 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Toast 12 slices white bread and spread with mayo. Build each sandwich as toast, 100g sliced turkey and lettuce, toast, 2 rashers bacon and sliced tomato, toast. Pin each with 4 toothpicks and cut into quarters. Serve with the chips.

Sweets

Commander Taggart browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies baked with a slightly wobbly centre

Prep 7 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 80g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a tin. Melt the chocolate and butter together.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  3. Bake, pulling it out while the centre still wobbles.
  4. Cool completely before cutting.

Thermian pink raspberry marshmallowsOn the printed menu

Soft cubes set with fresh raspberry purée, rolled in icing sugar, sweet, wobbly and very polite

How it is made
  • 150g raspberries
  • 20g powdered gelatine
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp glucose syrup
  • 50g icing sugar
  • 50g cornflour
  1. Soft pink marshmallows made with fresh raspberry purée, cut into cubes and rolled in icing sugar and cornflour, sweet, tangy and wobbly. Blitz and sieve 150g raspberries. Sprinkle 20g powdered gelatine over 100ml of the purée to bloom. Boil 300g caster sugar, 2 tbsp glucose syrup and 100ml water to 120°C. Melt the gelatine mix and pour it into a mixer bowl, then pour in the hot syrup with the beaters running and whisk 10 minutes until thick and fluffy. Spread in a lined 20cm tin dusted with icing sugar and cornflour. Set 4 hours, then cut and roll in more dusting.

Drinks

NSEA Protector blue lagoon mocktailVegetarianNo alcohol

Blue syrup, pineapple, lime and lemonade over ice, no alcohol

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Cut the pineapple wedges up to 3 hours ahead and keep chilled; build over ice and top with lemonade to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml non-alcoholic blue curacao syrup
  • 240ml pineapple juice
  • 1 lime
  • 600ml lemonade
  • 1 small pineapple
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Cut pineapple wedges and fill four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Add the blue syrup, pineapple juice and a squeeze of lime to each.
  3. Top with cold lemonade, stir gently and garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry on a pick.
