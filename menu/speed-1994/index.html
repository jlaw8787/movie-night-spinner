Snacks

Jack's runway nachosVegetarian

Corn chips baked with black beans, melted cheddar and sliced jalapeños.

Prep 4 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best baked and eaten straight away; grate the cheddar and drain the beans up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g corn chips
  • 1 tin black beans, drained
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 4 tbsp sliced jalapenos
  • 120g sour cream
  • 1 jar salsa (300g)
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spread the corn chips on a tray, 4 min.
  2. Scatter over the black beans, cheddar and jalapenos.
  3. Bake until melted.
  4. Top with sour cream and salsa.

Subway train snacksVegetarian

Smoky buttered pretzels and peanuts, baked and bagged for the ride.

Prep 6 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days ahead in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g hard pretzels
  • 200g roasted peanuts
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Melt the butter and stir in the smoked paprika, 5 min.
  2. Toss the pretzels and peanuts in the paprika butter and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Bake for 15 minutes, stirring once halfway.
  4. Cool on the tray and tip into paper bags, 3 min.

Mains

LA street tacos

Grilled lime and orange marinated skirt steak in double corn tortillas, salsa verde.

Prep 22 min, cook 6 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the steak up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 16 small corn tortillas
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 150g salsa verde
  1. Juice the limes and orange, mix with the crushed garlic and cumin and marinate the steak, 8 min.
  2. Leave to marinate, 2 hr.
  3. Grill very hot on both sides, 6 min.
  4. Rest the steak, then chop, 5 min.
  5. Dice the onion and warm the tortillas, 6 min.
  6. Serve in doubled tortillas with onion, coriander and salsa verde.

Harry's pop quiz chicken burgers

Breaded chicken burgers with spicy slaw. Pop quiz: Harry asks, Jack says shoot the hostage.

Prep 4 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken and make the slaw dressing up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken thigh fillets
  • 60g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 150ml vegetable oil
  • 4 brioche buns
  • 200g white cabbage
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo
  • 100g dill pickles
  • 1 lime
  1. Flatten the chicken, then dip in flour, beaten egg and panko.
  2. Shallow fry in the oil for 4 minutes a side until golden and cooked through.
  3. Mix half the mayonnaise with the chopped chipotle. Toss the shredded cabbage with the rest and the lime juice, 4 min.
  4. Toast the buns, spread with chipotle mayo and fill with chicken, slaw and pickles, 4 min.

Fifty mile an hour ice cream sandwichesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream between soft chocolate chip cookies.

Prep 5 min, cook 17 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and freeze up to 1 week ahead, wrapped; take out 5 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 8 soft choc chip cookies
  • 50g sprinkles
  1. Soften the ice cream.
  2. Sandwich a thick scoop between two cookies.
  3. Roll the edges in sprinkles and wrap in baking paper.
  4. Freeze until firm.

Annie's coffeeNo alcohol

Strong iced coffee over ice with milk and a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Prep 15 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep the coffee in the fridge and strain up to 3 days ahead; pour over ice with the milk and ice cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g coarsely ground coffee
  • 1 L cold water
  • 1 tray ice
  • 4 tbsp milk
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  1. Stir the coffee into the cold water.
  2. Steep in the fridge overnight.
  3. Strain.
  4. Pour over ice in tall glasses, add a splash of milk and float a scoop of ice cream on top.
