Portuguese kale soup

Smooth potato soup with kale and smoked sausage, olive oil on top

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1.2l chicken stock
  • 1 bunch kale
  • 200g smoked sausage
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Simmer the potatoes and onion in the stock until soft, then mash smooth.
  2. Shred the kale finely and slice the sausage.
  3. Add both and simmer.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil.

Pizza parlour garlic knotsVegetarian

Baked pizza dough knots tossed in garlic parsley butter and parmesan

Prep 20 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Tie the knots up to 3 hours ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; bake and toss in the garlic butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 60g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Roll the dough into ropes and tie each in a knot.
  2. Bake at 220°C until golden.
  3. Toss straight away in melted garlic butter with parsley and parmesan.

Lobster boat garlic chilli prawnsOn the printed menu

Sizzled in garlic butter, white wine and lemon, crusty bread, for Bill off the trawler

How it is made
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 red chilli
  • 600g green prawns, peeled
  • 80ml white wine
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  1. Prawns sizzled in butter, garlic and chilli, splashed with white wine and lemon, finished with parsley and served with crusty bread to mop up. Heat 60g butter and 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pan. Add 5 sliced garlic cloves and a chopped red chilli and cook 1 minute. Add 600g peeled green prawns and cook 2 minutes, turning. Pour in 80ml white wine and the juice of a lemon and bubble 1 minute. Toss with chopped parsley and serve in the pan with a sliced baguette.

Leona's secret sauce pizzaVegetarian

Thin crust with honey oregano tomato sauce, mozzarella, linguiça and peppers

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 150g linguica
  • 1 green capsicum
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with crushed garlic, oregano, honey and chilli.
  2. Stretch the dough thin and spread with sauce.
  3. Top with mozzarella, sliced linguica and capsicum.
  4. Bake on a hot steel at maximum heat.

Jojo's wedding clam and chorizo bakeOn the printed menu

Clams, prawns, potatoes and corn steamed in wine and butter, once she finally says I do

How it is made
  • 600g new potatoes
  • 200g chorizo
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 250ml white wine
  • 60g butter
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1kg clams
  • 500g green prawns
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Clams, prawns, new potatoes and corn steamed in a big pot with white wine, garlic, butter and chorizo, tipped onto the table with lemon and bread. Boil 600g new potatoes for 10 minutes in a big pot and drain. Fry 200g sliced chorizo in the pot, add 4 sliced garlic cloves, then 250ml white wine and 60g butter. Add the potatoes, 4 halved corn cobs, 1kg clams and 500g green prawns in the shell. Cover and steam 6 minutes until the clams open. Tip onto a table lined with paper and serve with lemon wedges and bread.

Portuguese custard tartsVegetarian

Flaky pastry shells filled with blistered cream custard, dusted with cinnamon

Prep 28 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Roll the pastry into a tight log, slice into 12 and press into a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk the yolks, cream, sugar and lemon zest.
  3. Fill the cases and bake at 250°C until blistered.
  4. Cool slightly and dust with cinnamon.

Jojo's wedding day sangriaVegetarian

Portuguese red wine, brandy, orange, lemon and apple, chilled with cinnamon

Prep 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 orange
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 red apple
  • 750ml Portuguese red wine
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 250ml lemonade
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Slice the orange, lemon and apple into a big jug.
  2. Add the wine, brandy, sugar and cinnamon stick and stir until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Chill for an hour.
  4. Fill four glasses with ice, pour in the sangria with some fruit and top with a splash of lemonade.
