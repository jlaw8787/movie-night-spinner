Angels game roasted peanutsVegetarian

Peanuts roasted in the shell with salt.

Prep 3 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roast the peanuts up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; warm in a 180°C oven for 5 minutes, then toss with salt
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peanuts in the shell
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  1. Spread the peanuts on a tray and roast at 180°C.
  2. Toss with salt while still hot and divide between paper bags. Leave a bowl for the shells.

Umpire's hot dogs

Griddled franks in steamed buns with yellow mustard and sweet relish.

Prep 4 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; set out the buns, mustard and relish up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp sweet relish
  1. Simmer the franks, then griddle until blistered.
  2. Steam the buns over the pot.
  3. Load each with a frank, mustard and relish and wrap in paper.

Ballpark chilli dogs

Steamed buns and griddled franks under slow simmered beef chilli.

Prep 21 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; griddle the franks and steam the buns on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Dice the onions.
  2. Brown the mince with one onion and the chilli powder. Add the tomatoes.
  3. Simmer.
  4. Griddle the franks and steam the buns.
  5. Fill the buns, spoon over chilli and top with cheddar and the diced raw onion.

Queen's reception roast beef, Yorkshires

Topside roasted pink and carved thin, puffed batter puddings, crisp roast potatoes and rich gravy.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef topside
  • 1 jar English mustard
  • 3 eggs
  • 170g plain flour
  • 200ml milk
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g beef dripping
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Topside roasted pink and carved thin, with puffed Yorkshire puddings cooked in smoking dripping, crisp roast potatoes and a rich gravy. Rub a 1.5kg beef topside with mustard, salt and pepper and roast at 200°C until 52°C inside, about 60 minutes, then rest 20 minutes. Whisk 3 eggs, 150g plain flour and 200ml milk and rest. Roast 1kg parboiled potatoes in oil at 200°C for 45 minutes. Heat 1 tsp dripping in each hole of a 12-hole muffin tin at 220°C until smoking, pour in the batter and bake 20 minutes. Make gravy from the pan juices with flour and 400ml beef stock.

Ninth inning malt and fudge ice cream cupsVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with warm chocolate fudge and malt.

Prep 83 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Ripple and refreeze the ice cream up to 1 week ahead; keep the cooled fudge in the fridge up to 3 days and warm it to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 100ml cream
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup
  • 80g chocolate honeycomb, crushed
  • 40g salted peanuts
  • 4 paper cups
  1. Soften the ice cream.
  2. Heat the cream until steaming and stir in the chocolate and golden syrup until smooth.
  3. Cool the fudge.
  4. Ripple half the fudge through the ice cream with the honeycomb.
  5. Refreeze.
  6. Scoop into the paper cups, warm the rest of the fudge, spoon over and scatter with peanuts.

Seventh inning spiked frozen lemonade

Fresh lemon, sugar and vodka blended with ice into a frozen lemonade, for the seventh inning stretch.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Dissolve and cool the lemon syrup up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; blend with the vodka and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml hot water
  • 150ml lemon juice
  • 160ml vodka
  • 200ml cold water
  • 4 cups ice
  • 1 lemon
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Blend the syrup with the lemon juice, vodka, cold water and ice until slushy.
  3. Pour into cups, garnish with lemon wheels and serve with straws.
