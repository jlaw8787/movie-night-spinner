Snacks

Neverland rainbow fruitVegetarian

Melon, kiwi, pineapple and blueberries in rainbow rows, lime and honey drizzle. Real fruit, for a grown-up who can't imagine it yet.

Prep 20 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1/4 watermelon
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 2 kiwifruits
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Cut the watermelon, rockmelon, kiwifruit and pineapple into chunks.
  2. Arrange in rainbow rows with the blueberries.
  3. Drizzle with lime juice mixed with honey.

Rufio's rainbow dips

Beet hummus, pea and mint, and cumin carrot, with warm pita chips. Made for flicking across the table. Bangarang, Rufio!

How it is made
  1. Blitz 1 drained 400g tin chickpeas with 2 tbsp tahini, lemon, garlic and 3 tbsp oil, then split in two.
  2. Blend one half with 1 cooked beetroot.
  3. Blend 200g frozen peas, cooked, with mint, 50g feta and lemon.
  4. Boil 3 carrots until soft and blend with 1 tsp cumin, honey and oil.
  5. Cut 3 pitas into wedges, brush with oil and bake at 180°C for 8 minutes.

Tinkerbell sprinkle toastVegetarian

Buttered white bread covered in rainbow sprinkles, cut into triangles. For Tink. She once drank poison for Peter.

Prep 12 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 60g soft butter
  • 1/2 cup rainbow sprinkles
  • sparkling sugar, to dust
  1. Spread the bread with butter right to the edges.
  2. Press face down into the rainbow sprinkles and dust with sparkling sugar.
  3. Trim the crusts and cut into triangles.

Mains

Lost Boys roast chicken, potatoes and corn

Spatchcock chicken with garlic, lemon and smoked paprika, roast potatoes, grilled corn. “I want steak, I want eggs, I want a cup of coffee.” Peter, before he imagines it.

Prep 23 min, cook 57 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 corn cobs
  1. Spatchcock the chicken and rub with crushed garlic, lemon zest and paprika. Chop the potatoes.
  2. Roast at 220°C with the potatoes in the tray, 45 min.
  3. Grill the corn cobs, turning until charred, 12 min.
  4. Pile it all in the middle and eat with your hands.

Smee's Jolly Roger beef stew

Chuck braised with smoked paprika, tomato and molasses, with cheesy damper. From the galley of the Jolly Roger, for Hook's crew.

How it is made
  1. Brown 1.2kg diced beef chuck in batches.
  2. Cook 2 onions and 2 carrots 5 minutes, add 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp molasses, 400g tinned tomatoes and 600ml beef stock.
  3. Cover and braise at 160°C for 3 hours.
  4. Rub 50g butter into 300g self-raising flour, add 80g cheddar and 200ml milk, shape into 8 rounds and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.

Sweets

Tick-tock crocodile cakeVegetarian

Pandan sponge with pandan buttercream and a chocolate clock face piped on top. Hook turned the crocodile into a quiet clock.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 5 min, 30 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 210g butter
  • 1 tsp pandan extract
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick, then fold in the flour, 60g melted butter and half the pandan.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 175°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool the cake, 30 min.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and rest of the pandan, then cover the cake, 12 min.
  5. Pipe a clock face with melted dark chocolate.

Drinks

Pixie dust lemonadeNo alcohol

Cold lemonade, fresh lime juice and a pinch of sparkling sugar. Peter ran out of fairy dust. Tink has plenty.

Prep 4 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1.3 L lemonade
  • 1 lime
  • 1 pinch sparkling sugar
  1. Pour the cold lemonade into a jug with the lime juice.
  2. Stir in the sparkling sugar and pour into four glasses straight away.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Peter finally imagines the Lost Boys' feast.
