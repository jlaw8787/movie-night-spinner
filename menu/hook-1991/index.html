Snacks

Neverland rainbow fruitVegetarian

Melon, kiwi, pineapple and blueberries in rainbow rows, lime and honey drizzle

Prep 22 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1/4 watermelon
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 2 kiwifruit
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Cut the watermelon, rockmelon, kiwifruit and pineapple into chunks.
  2. Arrange in rainbow rows with the blueberries.
  3. Drizzle with lime juice mixed with honey.

Tinkerbell fairy breadVegetarian

Buttered white bread covered in hundreds and thousands, cut into triangles

Prep 12 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 60g soft butter
  • 1/2 cup hundreds and thousands
  • edible glitter, to dust
  1. Spread the bread with butter right to the edges.
  2. Press face down into the hundreds and thousands and dust with edible glitter.
  3. Trim the crusts and cut into triangles.

Mains

Lost Boys roast chicken, potatoes and corn

Spatchcock chicken with garlic, lemon and smoked paprika, potatoes, grilled corn

Prep 23 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Spatchcock and rub the chicken up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; roast and grill the corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 corn cobs
  1. Spatchcock the chicken and rub with crushed garlic, lemon zest and paprika. Chop the potatoes.
  2. Roast at 220C with the potatoes in the tray.
  3. Grill the corn cobs, turning until charred.
  4. Pile it all in the middle and eat with your hands. Bangarang.

Sweets

Tick-tock crocodile cakeVegetarian

Pandan sponge with pandan buttercream and a chocolate clock face piped on top

Prep 35 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 210g butter
  • 1 tsp pandan extract
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick, then fold in the flour, 60g melted butter and half the pandan.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 175C.
  3. Cool the cake.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and rest of the pandan, then cover the cake.
  5. Pipe a clock face with melted dark chocolate.

Pixie dust lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemonade with lime juice and a pinch of edible glitter

Prep 4 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the lime and chill the lemonade up to 1 day ahead; stir in the glitter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/4l lemonade
  • 1 lime
  • 1 pinch edible glitter
  1. Pour the cold lemonade into a jug with the lime juice.
  2. Stir in the glitter and pour into four glasses straight away.
