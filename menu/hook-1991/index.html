Snacks

Neverland rainbow fruitVegetarian

Melon, kiwi, pineapple and blueberries in rainbow rows, lime and honey drizzle

Prep 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1/4 watermelon
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 2 kiwifruit
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Cut the watermelon, rockmelon, kiwifruit and pineapple into chunks.
  2. Arrange in rainbow rows with the blueberries.
  3. Drizzle with lime juice mixed with honey.

Tinkerbell fairy breadVegetarian

Buttered white bread covered in hundreds and thousands, cut into triangles

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Make just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 60g soft butter
  • 1/2 cup hundreds and thousands
  • edible glitter, to dust
  1. Spread the bread with butter right to the edges.
  2. Press face down into the hundreds and thousands and dust with edible glitter.
  3. Trim the crusts and cut into triangles.

Rufio's rainbow dipsOn the printed menu

Beetroot hummus, pea and mint, and cumin carrot with warm pita chips, bangarang

How it is made
  • 400g tinned chickpeas
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 1 cooked beetroot
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 50g feta
  • 3 carrots
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 3 pita breads
  1. Three bright dips, beetroot hummus, pea and mint, and carrot with cumin, swirled in bowls with warm pita chips for flicking at the table. Blitz 1 drained 400g tin chickpeas with 2 tbsp tahini, lemon, garlic and 3 tbsp oil, then split in two. Blend one half with 1 cooked beetroot. Blend 200g frozen peas, cooked, with mint, 50g feta and lemon. Boil 3 carrots until soft and blend with 1 tsp cumin, honey and oil. Cut 3 pitas into wedges, brush with oil and bake at 180°C for 8 minutes.

Mains

Lost Boys roast chicken, potatoes and corn

Spatchcock chicken with garlic, lemon and smoked paprika, potatoes, grilled corn

Prep 23 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Spatchcock and rub the chicken up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; roast and grill the corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 corn cobs
  1. Spatchcock the chicken and rub with crushed garlic, lemon zest and paprika. Chop the potatoes.
  2. Roast at 220°C with the potatoes in the tray.
  3. Grill the corn cobs, turning until charred.
  4. Pile it all in the middle and eat with your hands. Bangarang.

Smee's Jolly Roger beef stewOn the printed menu

Chuck braised with smoked paprika, tomato and molasses, cheesy damper, for Hook's crew below deck

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp molasses
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 600ml beef stock
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 50g butter
  • 80g cheddar
  • 200ml milk
  1. Beef chuck braised three hours with smoky paprika, tomato, carrot and molasses until sticky and dark, served with cheesy ship's biscuit damper. Brown 1.2kg diced beef chuck in batches. Cook 2 onions and 2 carrots 5 minutes, add 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp molasses, 400g tinned tomatoes and 600ml beef stock. Cover and braise at 160°C for 3 hours. Rub 50g butter into 300g self-raising flour, add 80g cheddar and 200ml milk, shape into 8 rounds and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.

Sweets

Tick-tock crocodile cakeVegetarian

Pandan sponge with pandan buttercream and a chocolate clock face piped on top

Prep 5 min, cook 65 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 210g butter
  • 1 tsp pandan extract
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick, then fold in the flour, 60g melted butter and half the pandan.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 175°C.
  3. Cool the cake.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and rest of the pandan, then cover the cake.
  5. Pipe a clock face with melted dark chocolate.

Lemonade with lime juice and a pinch of edible glitter

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Juice the lime and chill the lemonade up to 1 day ahead; stir in the glitter just before serving
