Snacks

Clams casino

Steamed clams baked with bacon, capsicum and garlic butter under crumbs, for the count room

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Stuff up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg clams
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 red capsicum, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 4 rashers bacon, cut into small pieces
  1. Steam the clams in a covered pot until they open, discarding any that stay shut. Remove the top shells.
  2. Mix the butter with the garlic, capsicum, parsley and breadcrumbs.
  3. Top each clam with a spoon of the mix and a piece of bacon.
  4. Grill for 4 minutes until crisp.

Oysters Rockefeller

Grilled under spinach, herb butter and crumbs with aniseed, for the high rollers Ace keeps happy

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the butter up to 1 day ahead; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g spinach
  • 1 handful parsley
  • 2 spring onions
  • 80g butter, soft
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp aniseed liqueur
  • 16 oysters, freshly shucked
  • 1kg rock salt
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  1. Blitz the spinach, parsley, spring onions, butter, breadcrumbs and liqueur.
  2. Settle the oysters on a tray of rock salt and spoon over the butter.
  3. Grill on high for 3 minutes until bubbling and serve hot with lemon.

Mains

Tangiers rib eye

Cooked exactly right, no excuses

Prep 20 min, cook 730 min · Make ahead: Salt the steaks the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 2 rib eye steaks, about 400g each
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 500g spinach
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  1. Salt the steaks and leave uncovered on a rack in the fridge overnight.
  2. Sear in a smoking pan for 3 minutes a side, then add the butter, thyme and garlic and baste until 54C inside.
  3. Rest for 8 minutes while you wilt the spinach, squeeze it dry and simmer with the cream and nutmeg.
  4. Slice the steaks and serve with the creamed spinach.

Ham and eggs

Two eggs any style with thick griddled steaks and crisp hash browns, for the graveyard shift

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Par-boil and grate the potatoes up to 1 day ahead; keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g waxy potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 thick ham steaks, about 150g each
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 pinch black pepper
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Boil the whole potatoes for 10 minutes, cool, peel and grate coarsely. Season with the salt.
  2. Press the potato flat into a hot pan with half the butter and the oil and fry until crisp and gold on both sides.
  3. Fry the ham steaks in a second pan until browned at the edges, 2 minutes a side.
  4. Fry the eggs in the rest of the butter, any style.
  5. Plate the ham with two eggs and a wedge of hash browns, pepper and chives over.

Sweets

Equal-blueberry muffinsVegetarian

Buttermilk and butter batter baked golden, exactly ten blueberries pressed into every one

Prep 15 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 90g butter, melted
  • 120 blueberries (10 per muffin)
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Mix the eggs, buttermilk and butter and fold into the dry mix until just combined.
  3. Spoon into 12 lined muffin cases and press exactly 10 blueberries into the top of each.
  4. Bake at 190C for 20 minutes.

Drinks

Dry martiniVegetarian

Gin and dry vermouth stirred cold, frozen glass, olive, under the eye in the sky

Prep 8 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Freeze the martini glasses up to 1 day ahead; stir and strain each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 4 green olives on picks
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Freeze four martini glasses for 30 minutes.
  2. For each drink stir gin and vermouth over plenty of ice for 30 seconds and strain into a frozen glass.
  3. Garnish with an olive on a pick.

When to serve

Bring out the muffins and count the blueberries before anyone takes one.
