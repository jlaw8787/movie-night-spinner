Snacks

Doughboy's welcome-home wings

Crispy baked wings tossed in honey barbecue sauce with a splash of hot sauce

Prep 8 min, cook 50 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry the wings the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 120ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • salt, to taste
  1. Leave the wings uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Toss with garlic powder and salt.
  3. Bake at 220°C until crisp.
  4. Warm the barbecue sauce with honey and hot sauce.
  5. Toss the wings through the sauce and serve with plenty of napkins.

Doughboy's porch devilled eggsVegetarian

Eggs with mustard, sweet relish and hot sauce, dusted with smoked paprika

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp sweet relish
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • smoked paprika, to dust
  1. Boil the eggs, then cool and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs and mash the yolks with mayonnaise, mustard, relish and hot sauce until smooth.
  3. Pipe back into the whites.
  4. Chill, then dust with smoked paprika.

Corner store onion dip with ridged chipsOn the printed menu

Slow-browned onions in sour cream and cream cheese, salted crinkle-cuts, from the corner store

How it is made
  • 3 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 250g sour cream
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 2 bags ridged potato chips
  1. Onions cooked slow until deep brown, folded through sour cream and cream cheese with chives and a dash of Worcestershire, served with ridged salted chips. Slice 3 brown onions thinly and cook in 30g butter and a splash of oil over low heat for 35 minutes, stirring, until deep brown. Cool completely. Beat 250g sour cream with 125g soft cream cheese, a dash of Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper. Fold in the onions and a handful of chopped chives. Chill for an hour and serve with ridged potato chips.

Furious Styles' backyard ribs

Foil-baked paprika pork ribs finished over coals, with crunchy cabbage slaw

Prep 25 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Rub and wrap the ribs up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; make the coleslaw up to 4 hours ahead; bake, then finish on the coals on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork ribs
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 120ml barbecue sauce
  • 1/2 small cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Rub the ribs with paprika, brown sugar, garlic powder, cayenne and salt.
  2. Wrap the ribs in foil.
  3. Bake at 150°C until tender.
  4. Shred the cabbage and carrot and toss with mayonnaise for a crunchy coleslaw.
  5. Unwrap the ribs, brush with barbecue sauce and finish over hot coals until sticky.

Furious's table fried chicken and greensOn the printed menu

Brined, floured and fried crisp, mac and cheese, ham hock collards, after the fishing trip

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 2 bunches silverbeet
  • 300g macaroni
  • 250g cheddar
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  1. Chicken pieces brined in buttermilk, fried crisp in seasoned flour, served with baked mac and cheese and collard-style greens braised with smoked ham hock. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces in 500ml buttermilk with salt and hot sauce overnight. Simmer a ham hock in 1L water for 1 hour, add 2 bunches shredded silverbeet and cook 20 minutes. Boil 300g macaroni, stir through 250g grated cheddar and 300ml milk and bake at 190°C for 20 minutes. Dredge the chicken in 300g flour with paprika and garlic powder and fry at 165°C for 12 to 14 minutes until 74°C inside.

Ricky's 710 sweet potato pieVegetarian

Spiced mashed sweet potato custard in a sweet pastry crust

Prep 8 min, cook 105 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  1. Roast the sweet potatoes until soft.
  2. Line a tin with the pastry and blind bake until pale golden.
  3. Mash the sweet potato with butter, sugar, eggs, evaporated milk, cinnamon and nutmeg.
  4. Pour into the case and bake at 170°C until just set.
  5. Cool before slicing.

Red cordial punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry, pineapple and lemonade punch served from a cooler, no alcohol

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the cranberry and pineapple juice up to 1 day ahead and chill; add the ice and lemonade just before serving so it stays fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cranberry juice
  • 1l pineapple juice
  • 2l cold lemonade
  • 2 bags ice
  1. Fill a big drink cooler with ice.
  2. Pour in the cranberry and pineapple juice and stir.
  3. Top up with cold lemonade just before everyone arrives so it stays fizzy.
  4. Set out cups and let people help themselves.
