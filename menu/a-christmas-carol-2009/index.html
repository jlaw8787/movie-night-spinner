Fezziwig's ball pork and sage rolls

Pork, apple and sage sausage rolls in puff pastry.

Prep 20 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Roll the logs up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; brush with egg, cut and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 apple, grated
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped sage
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 150g apple chutney
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Mix the pork with the apple, onion, sage, breadcrumbs, salt and pepper.
  2. Cut the pastry in half, lay a log of filling along each strip, roll and seal underneath.
  3. Brush with egg and cut each into small rolls.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  5. Stir the mustard into the chutney and serve alongside.

Brown butter roast chestnutsVegetarian

Roasted chestnuts tossed in brown butter, rosemary and sea salt.

Prep 8 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Roast and peel the chestnuts up to 1 day ahead, covered; brown the butter and toss them through just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g fresh chestnuts
  • 40g butter
  • 1 sprig rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Cut a cross through the shell on the flat side of each chestnut.
  2. Soak in water, then drain.
  3. Roast on a tray at 200°C until the shells peel back.
  4. Wrap in a tea towel to steam, then peel while warm.
  5. Melt the butter until golden brown, add the rosemary and toss the chestnuts through with sea salt. Serve in paper cones.

Scrooge's prize turkey with sage stuffing

Butter roast turkey breast with sage and onion stuffing.

Prep 5 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Mix the stuffing and stew the apples up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; roast the turkey and potatoes on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 70g butter
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 small bunch sage, chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 apples, peeled and diced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  1. Rub the turkey with some of the butter, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast at 180°C.
  3. Fry the onion in butter, mix with the breadcrumbs, sage and egg, press into a dish and bake alongside for the last part of the roasting. Roast the potatoes in oil.
  4. Simmer the apples with the sugar and water until soft.
  5. Rest the turkey. Make gravy from the pan juices with the flour and stock.

Fred's Christmas party roast beef and Yorkshire puddings

Roast beef with puffed Yorkshire puddings and gravy, carved for the Christmas party.

Prep 0 min, cook 76 min · Make ahead: Make the pudding batter up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef sirloin roast
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 4 carrots, halved
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 200ml milk
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp plain flour, extra
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk into a batter and rest.
  2. Season the beef, sear it, then roast with the potatoes and carrots tossed in half the oil until pink inside.
  3. Rest the beef. Turn the oven to 230°C, heat the rest of the oil in a muffin tin, pour in the batter and bake until puffed.
  4. Stir the extra flour and stock into the roasting tin juices and simmer for gravy. Glaze the carrots with honey.

Cratchit Christmas pudding with custardVegetarian

Steamed Christmas pudding with warm vanilla custard.

Prep 12 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Steam the pudding up to 2 days ahead and reheat to serve; make the custard up to 1 day ahead and whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g Christmas pudding
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 4 eggs (yolks only)
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 orange, zested
  1. Steam the pudding over simmering water, or follow the packet.
  2. Warm the milk with some of the cream. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour, pour in the milk while whisking and return to the pan.
  3. Stir until thick, then add the vanilla.
  4. Whip the remaining cream with the icing sugar and orange zest.
  5. Turn out the pudding, top with a holly sprig and serve with both.

Smoking bishop spiced orange punchNo alcohol

Warm red grape juice with roasted clove oranges, apple and ginger.

Prep 3 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Stud and roast the oranges up to 1 day ahead; heat the punch gently on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 oranges
  • 12 cloves
  • 1 L red grape juice
  • 250ml apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  1. Stud the oranges with the cloves and roast at 200°C until browned.
  2. Slice them into a saucepan with the grape juice, apple juice, cinnamon, honey and ginger.
  3. Heat gently without boiling.
  4. Ladle into heatproof glasses with an orange slice in each.
