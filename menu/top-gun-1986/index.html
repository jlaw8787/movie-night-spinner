Snacks

Danger Zone hot wings

Crisp baked wings tossed in hot sauce butter and honey, blue cheese dip

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Wings can dry overnight uncovered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 celery sticks, cut into sticks
  1. Pat the wings dry and season. Bake at 220C for 45 minutes, turning once.
  2. Melt the hot sauce with the butter and honey and toss the wings through it.
  3. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream. Serve the wings with the dip and celery.

Beach volleyball fish tacos

Lager battered fish strips in tortillas with cabbage, tomato and lime sour cream

Prep 23 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Slaw and salsa up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 500g flathead fillets, cut into strips
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 limes
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Whisk the flour with the lager into a batter. Mix the sour cream with lime juice.
  2. Dice the tomato and onion and shred the cabbage.
  3. Dip the fish in batter and fry in the oil at 180C for 3 minutes, in batches, until golden.
  4. Warm the tortillas and fill with fish, cabbage, tomato, onion and the lime cream.

Mains

Charlie's lemon chicken and Greek salad

Lemon oregano chicken, Greek salad. I could tell you, but then I'd have to kill you.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chicken can marinate up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 100g olives
  • 150g feta
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Mix the chicken with the juice of one lemon, the garlic and oregano.
  2. Marinate in the fridge for an hour.
  3. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through.
  4. Toss the tomato, cucumber, red onion, olives and feta for a Greek salad, with lemon juice.
  5. Warm the pita and serve everything together.

Sweets

Aviator sunglasses browniesVegetarian

Fudgy chocolate rectangles, served above the hard deck.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 280g dark chocolate
  • 170g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 80g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Melt most of the chocolate with most of the butter. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Pour into a lined tin and bake at 170C for 25 minutes. Cool.
  3. Melt the remaining chocolate with the last of the butter and drizzle over. Cut into rectangles.

Drinks

Iceman's frozen lemon lager slushieVegetarian

Frozen lemon slush and lager. "You can be my wingman anytime."

Prep 10 min, then 3.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Slush base freezes up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 200ml water
  • 4 bottles lager, 250ml each
  1. Stir the lemon juice, sugar syrup and water together and pour into a shallow tray.
  2. Freeze for 3 hours, scraping with a fork every hour into a slush.
  3. Put four glasses in the freezer for 30 minutes to frost.
  4. Spoon the slush into the frosted glasses and top each with ice-cold lager.
