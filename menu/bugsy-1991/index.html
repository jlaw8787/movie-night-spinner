Snacks

Hollywood party blini and caviar

Warm buckwheat pancakes with crème fraîche, roe or caviar and chives, for Ben's screen test

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 buckwheat blini
  • 4 tsp crème fraîche
  • 8 tsp salmon roe
  • 1 tbsp snipped chives
  1. Warm the blini at 180C for 5 minutes.
  2. Top each with a teaspoon of crème fraîche, salmon roe and chives.
  3. Serve straight away on a tray.

Hollywood Cobb salad cups

Gem lettuce cups of chicken, bacon, avocado, egg, tomato and blue cheese with red wine dressing

Prep 13 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Components up to 1 day ahead; fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken breast (2)
  • 4 rashers bacon
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 avocado
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 12 little gem lettuce leaves
  • 60g blue cheese, crumbled
  • 4 tbsp red wine vinaigrette
  1. Poach the chicken for 12 minutes, cool and dice.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp and crumble. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes and chop.
  3. Dice the avocado and tomatoes.
  4. Fill the lettuce leaves with everything, scatter over the blue cheese and spoon over the vinaigrette.

Mains

Flamingo steak Diane

Scotch fillet in flamed brandy, Dijon and cream sauce, for the desert casino nobody believed in

Prep 5 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Flatten the steaks and chop the shallots up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; cook it all just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g scotch fillet steaks (4)
  • 30g butter
  • 2 shallots, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 60ml brandy
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 150ml cream
  1. Flatten the steaks and sear hot for 2 minutes a side, then rest.
  2. In the same pan soften the shallots and garlic in the butter, add the brandy and flame.
  3. Stir in the mustard, Worcestershire, stock and cream and reduce.
  4. Return the steaks to coat in the sauce.

Prime rib with popovers

Carved to order on opening night, black tie

Prep 5 min, cook 2.5 hours, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the beef the day before; make the popover batter 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg beef rib roast, on the bone
  • 3 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 sprigs rosemary
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  1. Rub the beef with 2 tsp of the salt and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Rub with the pepper, garlic and rosemary and roast at 220C for 20 minutes, then 160C until 52C in the middle, about 1 hour 20 minutes.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, flour, the last 1 tsp salt and half the butter into a smooth batter and rest it.
  4. Rest the beef under foil. Turn the oven to 220C, brush a muffin tin with the rest of the butter and heat it for 5 minutes.
  5. Fill the hot tin halfway with batter and bake until tall and crisp, about 25 minutes. Do not open the door.
  6. Simmer the pan juices with the stock for the jus. Carve the beef thick and serve with the popovers, jus and horseradish.

Sweets

Pink flamingo pavlovaVegetarian

Raspberry cream for the hotel's namesake

Prep 28 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Meringue up to 2 days ahead airtight; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 125g raspberries, crushed
  1. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar and spread into a round.
  3. Bake at 120C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven.
  4. Whip the cream and fold through the raspberries. Pile on top just before serving.

Drinks

Gin rickey for VirginiaVegetarian

Gin, fresh lime juice and soda water over ice, crisp and sharp, a 1940s favourite

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best made to order; chill the gin and soda water up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml gin
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four highball glasses with ice.
  2. Add gin and the juice of half a lime to each and drop in the squeezed shell.
  3. Top with soda water.
