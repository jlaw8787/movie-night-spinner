Snacks

Oasis of Ahm Shere fruitVegetarian

Watermelon wedges, figs, dates, pomegranate, mint and feta with lime.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour ahead, add lime last
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg watermelon
  • 6 fresh figs, halved
  • 200g medjool dates, torn
  • 1 pomegranate, seeds only
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 lime
  1. Cut the watermelon into wedges and lay on a big platter.
  2. Add the figs and dates, then scatter with pomegranate seeds, mint and feta.
  3. Squeeze over a little lime just before serving.

Airship falafel wrapsVegetarian

Mini pita wraps with falafel, tahini, pickled turnip and tomato.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the chickpeas a day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dried chickpeas
  • 1/2 cup parsley leaves
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 8 mini pita breads
  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 100g pickled turnips
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  1. Soak the chickpeas in plenty of water overnight, drain, then blitz with parsley, onion, garlic and cumin.
  2. Shape into small balls and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until dark golden.
  3. Tuck into the pita with tahini, pickled turnip and tomato, then wrap in paper for the flight.

O'Connell pork and sage sausage rollsOn the printed menu

Mince, onion, herbs and crumbs in buttery puff pastry, egg washed and baked golden, brown sauce.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 packet breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry
  • 1 bottle brown sauce
  1. Pork mince, onion, sage and breadcrumbs rolled in buttery puff pastry, egg washed and baked golden, served hot with brown sauce. Mix 500g pork mince with 1 grated onion, 1 tbsp chopped sage, 40g breadcrumbs, 1 egg, salt and pepper. Halve 2 thawed puff pastry sheets, lay a sausage of filling along each strip and roll up, seam down. Brush with beaten egg, cut into 5cm pieces and score the tops. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve hot with brown sauce.

Mains

Scorpion King spiced lamb

Butterflied lamb leg grilled with harissa, sliced over couscous with garlic yoghurt.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg lamb leg, butterflied
  • 3 tbsp harissa
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 250g couscous
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed, for the sauce
  1. Rub the lamb with the harissa, garlic and cumin.
  2. Marinate for 2 hours.
  3. Grill over high heat until charred outside and pink inside.
  4. Rest for 15 minutes. Steep the couscous and mix the garlic into the yoghurt. Slice the lamb over couscous with the sauce.

London beef and stout pie with mashOn the printed menu

Chuck braised in dark ale with onion and thyme, under flaky puff, buttery potato, minted peas.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 can stout
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Beef chuck braised in stout with onions and thyme for three hours, under a flaky puff lid, with buttery mash and minted peas. Toss 1kg diced beef chuck in 2 tbsp plain flour and brown in batches. Soften 2 sliced onions, add the beef, 440ml stout, 250ml beef stock and 4 thyme sprigs. Cover and simmer gently for 3 hours, then cool slightly. Spoon into a pie dish, top with a puff pastry sheet, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes. Serve with 1kg potatoes mashed with butter and milk, and peas tossed with mint.

Sweets

Golden pyramid baklavaVegetarian

Layered filo with pistachio and cardamom, soaked in honey lemon syrup.

Prep 5 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, rests for several hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet filo pastry
  • 200g butter, melted
  • 250g pistachios, chopped
  • 2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 200g honey
  • 100ml water
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Layer filo in a tin, brushing every sheet with the melted butter and scattering pistachios mixed with cardamom between every few layers.
  2. Cut into triangles and bake at 180°C for 40 minutes until golden.
  3. Warm the honey with the water and lemon juice and pour over the hot pastry.
  4. Leave for several hours to soak.

Drinks

Iced karkadé with orange and mintVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled hibiscus tea, cinnamon, orange, mint, with ice for Alex.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5 L water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 40g dried hibiscus flowers
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 2 oranges, one sliced and one for squeezing
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Bring the water to the boil with the cinnamon, then take off the heat and add the hibiscus. Steep for 15 minutes.
  2. Strain and stir in the sugar until dissolved. Chill for at least 2 hours.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice, orange slices and torn mint, pour over the karkade and finish with a squeeze of orange.
