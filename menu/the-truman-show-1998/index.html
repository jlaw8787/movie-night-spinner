Snacks

Another-slice pepperoni pizza squares

A guard offers another slice at the end

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the dough the night before and keep it in the fridge; top and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pizza dough
  • 200g passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 100g sliced pepperoni salami
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Oil a large baking tray and press the dough out to the edges.
  2. Spread with passata seasoned with the oregano and a pinch of salt.
  3. Scatter over the mozzarella and pepperoni, then the parmesan.
  4. Bake at 240C until the base is crisp and the cheese is blistered.
  5. Cut into squares and serve hot.

Fiji kokoda cups with coconut and lime

Truman still dreams of getting to Fiji

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Cure the fish up to 4 hours ahead; stir in the coconut cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g very fresh snapper fillets, diced
  • 4 limes
  • 200ml coconut cream
  • 1 small red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 baby cos lettuce, leaves separated
  1. Toss the fish with the juice of 3 limes and a pinch of salt and chill until it turns white.
  2. Drain off most of the lime juice.
  3. Stir in the coconut cream, chilli, cucumber, tomato and spring onion.
  4. Spoon into the lettuce cups and finish with the last lime.

Mains

Meryl's I-made-macaroni bake

Baked macaroni and cheese, the one Meryl made

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce and boil the pasta up to a day ahead; combine and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g macaroni
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml milk
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 150g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g gruyere, grated
  • 200g smoked ham, diced
  • 40g panko breadcrumbs
  1. Boil the macaroni until just short of tender and drain.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for a minute, then whisk in the milk until thick.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the mustard, cheddar and half the gruyere.
  4. Fold through the pasta and ham and tip into a baking dish.
  5. Top with the rest of the gruyere and the panko and bake at 200C until golden and bubbling.

Painted-sky sailor's fish pie

Truman sails into the painted sky

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the filling and the mash up to a day ahead; assemble and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g skinless snapper fillets, in chunks
  • 250g raw prawns, peeled
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 60g butter, plus 50g for the mash
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml milk
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 100ml warm milk, for the mash
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with 50g butter and the warm milk.
  2. Soften the leek in 60g butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until thick.
  3. Stir in the fish, prawns, dill and lemon zest and tip into a baking dish.
  4. Spread over the mash, rough up the top with a fork and bake at 200C until golden.

Mount Nicaragua Mococoa potsVegetarian

Meryl pitches Mococoa in the middle of a fight

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Bake the pots up to 2 days ahead and keep them in the fridge; whip the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml thickened cream, plus 150ml for whipping
  • 200ml milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Dutch cocoa
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Heat 300ml cream and the milk until steaming, then stir in the chocolate and cocoa until smooth.
  2. Whisk the yolks, sugar and vanilla, then whisk in the hot chocolate cream.
  3. Pour into 4 small cups in a deep tray, add hot water halfway up, and bake at 150C until just set with a wobble.
  4. Chill, then top with the rest of the cream, softly whipped.

Marlon holds the can right up to the camera

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the cans overnight; crack to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 355ml cans American lager
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Chill the cans on ice until very cold.
  2. Hand them out with a straight face and the label facing the room.
