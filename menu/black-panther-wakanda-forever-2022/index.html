Snacks

Lime shrimp tostadas

Lime-cured shrimp with red onion and cilantro on crisp tostadas with avocado.

Prep 10 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, but add the prawns to the lime juice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g green prawns, peeled and diced
  • 4 limes
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  • 2 avocados
  • 12 corn tostadas
  1. Cover the prawns with lime juice.
  2. Leave until opaque, then drain and toss with the onion and coriander, 20 min.
  3. Spread smashed avocado on the tostadas and pile the prawns on top just before serving.

Heart-shaped herb plantain chipsVegetarian

Plantain chips with a peanut, chilli and lime dip.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best fried and eaten the same day; whisk the peanut butter dip up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled, and loosen with warm water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 tsp salt flakes
  • 4 tbsp peanut butter
  • 3 tbsp warm water
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  1. Peel the plantains and slice very thin.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil to 180°C and fry in batches until crisp and golden, then salt well, 12 min.
  3. Whisk the peanut butter with warm water, chilli and lime to a smooth dip and serve alongside, 3 min.

Haiti accra taro fritters with pikliz

Grated taro with garlic, chilli and thyme fried in spoonfuls, with fiery pickled cabbage.

How it is made
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 red chillies
  • 300ml white vinegar
  • 1 lime
  • 600g taro
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1L vegetable oil
  1. Grated taro mixed with garlic, chilli, spring onion and thyme, fried in spoonfuls until crisp, with a fiery pickled cabbage pikliz. A day ahead, pack a quarter of a shredded cabbage, 1 grated carrot, half a sliced onion and 2 sliced chillies into a jar, cover with 300ml white vinegar and the juice of 1 lime. Grate 600g peeled taro and mix with 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 chopped chilli, 3 spring onions, thyme, 1 egg, 2 tbsp plain flour and salt. Fry spoonfuls at 175°C for 4 minutes until crisp. Serve with the pikliz.

Mayan pibil pork

Achiote pork in banana leaf, roasted 4 hours, in tortillas.

Prep 20 min, cook 4 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, pibil reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder
  • 3 tbsp achiote paste
  • 2 oranges
  • 3 limes
  • 3 banana leaves
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  1. Blend the achiote paste with the orange and two limes' juice and rub over the pork.
  2. Wrap the pork in banana leaves.
  3. Roast covered at 150°C until it falls apart, 4 hr.
  4. Pickle the onion in lime juice while the pork cooks.
  5. Shred the pork and serve in warm tortillas with the pickled onion, 5 min.

Queen Ramonda's jollof with grilled chicken

Smoky jollof rice with grilled chicken, an olive branch.

How it is made
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 400g canned tomatoes
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g long grain rice
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • paprika
  • 1 head garlic
  • vegetable oil
  1. Smoky jollof rice cooked in a rich tomato, capsicum and chilli base with thyme, topped with charred spiced chicken thighs and fried onions. Blend 2 red capsicums, 400g canned tomatoes, 1 onion and 1 chilli. Fry 1 sliced onion in oil, add 2 tbsp tomato paste and the blend and cook 15 minutes until thick. Add 400g rinsed long grain rice, 500ml chicken stock, thyme, 1 tsp curry powder and salt, cover tightly and cook gently 30 minutes. Rub 1kg chicken thighs with paprika, garlic and oil and grill 6 minutes a side.

K'uk'ulkan coconut flanVegetarian

Coconut flan baked in a caramel-lined pan, turned out cold.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 15 min, 11 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 tbsp toasted coconut flakes
  • 1 lime, zested
  1. Melt the sugar in a pan until deep amber and pour into a 20cm round dish, 13 min.
  2. Whisk the eggs, coconut milk, condensed milk and vanilla and pour over the caramel.
  3. Sit the dish in a roasting tin of hot water and bake at 160°C until just set, 50 min.
  4. Cool, then chill overnight.
  5. Run a knife round the edge, turn out and top with toasted coconut and lime zest.

Drinks

Coconut and lime agua frescaNo alcohol

Coconut and lime blended over ice, from a city hidden for centuries.

Prep 10 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L coconut water
  • 100g desiccated coconut
  • 3 limes
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend the coconut water, coconut, lime juice and sugar until smooth.
  2. Strain through a fine sieve, pressing hard.
  3. Chill for an hour.
  4. Pour into glasses of ice and garnish with lime wheels and mint.
