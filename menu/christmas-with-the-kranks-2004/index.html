Rooftop snowman cheese ballVegetarian

Cheddar and cream cheese ball rolled in mozzarella, with crackers

Prep 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 375g cream cheese, soft
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 100g mozzarella, finely grated
  • 1 jar pitted black olives
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 bag pretzel sticks
  • 250g crackers
  1. Beat the cream cheese with the cheddar, chives and garlic powder.
  2. Chill the mixture.
  3. Shape into one large and one small ball, roll both in the mozzarella and stack on a platter.
  4. Press in sliced olives for eyes and buttons, a small carrot stick for the nose and pretzel sticks for arms.
  5. Chill until serving and surround with crackers.

Caribbean cruise prawn cocktail

Cooked king prawns with lime and a hot sauce Marie Rose

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; build the glasses just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g cooked king prawns
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 2 limes
  • hot sauce, a few drops
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 baby cos lettuce
  • 1 avocado, cubed
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the tomato sauce, juice of one lime, hot sauce and Worcestershire.
  3. Shred the lettuce into glasses and add cubes of avocado.
  4. Spoon over the sauce and hang the prawns around the rims. Dust with paprika and serve with lime wedges.

Nora's last honey glazed ham

Clove studded leg ham glazed with honey, brown sugar and Dijon

Prep 24 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Simmer the glaze up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; bake the ham up to 1 day ahead and serve at room temperature, with the salsa made on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 1/2kg half leg ham
  • 1 tbsp cloves
  • 120ml honey
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 100ml pineapple juice
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/2 pineapple, diced
  • 1 red chilli, diced
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Remove the rind from the ham, score the fat and stud with cloves.
  2. Simmer the honey, sugar, mustard and pineapple juice until syrupy.
  3. Bake the ham at 180C, brushing with glaze every so often. Roast the potatoes in oil alongside.
  4. Toss the pineapple, chilli, mint and lime juice for the salsa.
  5. Slice the ham and serve with the salsa.

Christmas Eve party green beansVegetarian

Brown butter, toasted almonds and lemon. Vic Frohmeyer will check

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Trim the beans and toast the almonds up to 1 day ahead; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green beans, trimmed
  • 50g butter
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • 1 lemon
  1. Trim the beans and zest the lemon.
  2. Boil the beans in salted water until just tender, then drain.
  3. Melt the butter in a frying pan and cook until it smells nutty and turns golden. Add the almonds and toast.
  4. Toss the beans through the brown butter with the lemon zest, a squeeze of juice and salt.

Last-minute Christmas Eve trifle

Sponge, orange juice, berries, jelly, custard, cream and almonds

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet raspberry jelly crystals
  • 300g sponge cake
  • 100ml orange juice
  • 300g mixed berries, plus a few extra to finish
  • 600ml thick vanilla custard
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 30g flaked almonds, toasted
  1. Make up the jelly with boiling water.
  2. Break the sponge into a large glass bowl and drizzle with orange juice. Scatter over the berries.
  3. Pour the cooled jelly over and chill until set.
  4. Spoon the custard on top.
  5. Whip the cream with the icing sugar and spread over. Finish with almonds and a few berries.

Hemlock Street cranberry fizz punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry and orange juice punch with ginger ale, rosemary and berries

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix the juices, lime and rosemary and chill up to 1 day ahead; add the ginger ale, orange slices, berries and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cranberry juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1l cold ginger ale
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 100g frozen cranberries or raspberries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the cranberry juice, orange juice and lime juice in a punch bowl. Add the rosemary.
  2. Chill.
  3. Just before serving, add the ginger ale, orange slices, frozen berries and plenty of ice. Stir gently.
  4. Ladle into glasses with a little rosemary in each.

When to serve

Serve the main when the neighbours rally round for the Christmas Eve party.
