Snacks

Beer-battered chips

Thick-cut chips in beer batter, chicken salt, garlic mayo, bubbles included.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Parboil and steam dry the chips up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, and mix the dipping mayonnaise up to 2 days ahead; batter and fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml lager
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips and parboil, then drain and steam dry.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager and salt.
  3. Dip the chips in the batter.
  4. Fry in batches in the oil at 180°C until golden.
  5. Drain and toss with chicken salt.
  6. Mix the mayonnaise with garlic and lemon juice for dipping.

Rock and roll scallop pies

Curried scallop pies, fuel for inventing rock and roll.

Prep 23 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the curry cream sauce up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stir in the scallops, fill the pies and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 300g scallops
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  1. Soften the onion in butter.
  2. Add the curry powder and cream and simmer until thick.
  3. Stir in the scallops off the heat and cool.
  4. Line patty tins with shortcrust and fill.
  5. Top with puff.
  6. Bake at 200°C.

Orchard cinnamon apple crisps

Apple slices sugared and dried until crunchy, honey yoghurt dip, Tasmanian orchard style.

How it is made
  • 4 crisp apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  1. Paper-thin apple slices dusted with cinnamon sugar and dried slowly until crisp, with a whipped honey and vanilla yoghurt dip. Core 4 crisp apples and slice 2mm thin on a mandoline. Toss with the juice of 1 lemon, then 2 tbsp caster sugar mixed with 1 tsp cinnamon. Lay in a single layer on lined trays and bake at 110°C for about 2 hours, turning once, until dry. Cool on the trays so they crisp. Stir 200g Greek yoghurt with 2 tbsp honey and half a tsp vanilla to dip.

Einstein farm cider pork chops

Pork chops simmered in Tassie cider with apple, onion and sage, over mash with buttered beans.

Prep 8 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Cook the pork in the cider up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently; boil and mash the potatoes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork chops
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 4 sage leaves
  • 375ml apple cider
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g green beans
  1. Brown the chops in oil and set aside.
  2. Soften the onion and apples with sage.
  3. Pour in the cider, return the chops and simmer until the sauce reduces.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter.
  5. Boil the beans 4 minutes, drain and toss with a knob of the butter.

Atom-split beer-battered flathead

Fish in a batter made with the beer he put bubbles in.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 150g plain flour
  • 60g rice flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 375ml beer
  • 4 flathead fillets
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 jar gherkins
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 lemons
  1. Flathead in an extra-crisp beer and rice flour batter with chunky chips, lemon and a tartare sauce full of capers and gherkins. Cut 1kg potatoes into chips, fry at 140°C for 6 minutes, then 190°C for 3 minutes. Whisk 120g plain flour, 60g rice flour, 1 tsp baking powder and salt with 300ml ice-cold beer until just combined. Dust 4 flathead fillets in flour, dip and fry at 180°C for 4 minutes until crisp. Mix 150g mayonnaise with chopped gherkins, capers, parsley and lemon juice. Serve with lemon wedges.

Einstein family apple pieVegetarian

Granny Smith apple pie with cinnamon, served warm with cream.

Prep 31 min, cook 50 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg Granny Smith apples, sliced
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Toss the apples with the sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill.
  3. Cover with two more sheets and crimp.
  4. Bake at 190°C.
  5. Cool slightly, then serve warm with cream.

Drinks

E=mc2 lager and cider snakebite

Lager and cider over blackcurrant, not Preston's keg.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook; chill the cider, lager and pint glasses up to 1 day ahead and build each glass just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cloudy apple cider, cold
  • 1 L lager, cold
  • 40ml blackcurrant cordial
  1. Chill four pint glasses in the freezer.
  2. Add cordial to each glass.
  3. Pour in the cider down the side, then top slowly with lager so it layers and foams.
  4. Serve straight away.
