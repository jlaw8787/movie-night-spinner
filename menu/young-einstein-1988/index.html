Snacks

Beer-battered Tassie chips

Beer battered chips, chicken salt, garlic mayo, bubbles included

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Parboil and steam dry the chips up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, and mix the dipping mayonnaise up to 2 days ahead; batter and fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml lager
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips and parboil, then drain and steam dry.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager and salt.
  3. Dip the chips in the batter.
  4. Fry in batches in the oil at 180C until golden.
  5. Drain and toss with chicken salt.
  6. Mix the mayonnaise with garlic and lemon juice for dipping.

Rock and roll scallop pies

Scallops in curry cream in shortcrust with a puff lid, brain food for relativity

Prep 30 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Make the curry cream sauce up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stir in the scallops, fill the pies and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 300g scallops
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  1. Soften the onion in butter.
  2. Add the curry powder and cream and simmer until thick.
  3. Stir in the scallops off the heat and cool.
  4. Line patty tins with shortcrust and fill.
  5. Top with puff.
  6. Bake at 200C.

Mains

Einstein farm cider pork chops

Pork chops simmered in Tassie cider with apple, onion and sage, over mash with buttered beans

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook the pork in the cider up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently; boil and mash the potatoes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork chops
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 4 sage leaves
  • 375ml apple cider
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g green beans
  1. Brown the chops in oil and set aside.
  2. Soften the onion and apples with sage.
  3. Pour in the cider, return the chops and simmer until the sauce reduces.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter.
  5. Boil the beans 4 minutes, drain and toss with a knob of the butter.

Sweets

Einstein family apple pieVegetarian

Granny Smith apple pie with cinnamon, served warm with cream

Prep 35 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg Granny Smith apples, sliced
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Toss the apples with the sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill.
  3. Cover with two more sheets and crimp.
  4. Bake at 190C.
  5. Cool slightly, then serve warm with cream.

Drinks

E=mc2 lager and cider snakebiteVegetarian

Lager and cider over blackcurrant, not Preston's keg

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook; chill the cider, lager and pint glasses up to 1 day ahead and build each glass just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cloudy apple cider, cold
  • 1l lager, cold
  • 40ml blackcurrant cordial
  1. Chill four pint glasses in the freezer.
  2. Add cordial to each glass.
  3. Pour in the cider down the side, then top slowly with lager so it layers and foams.
  4. Serve straight away.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Einstein puts the bubbles into beer.
