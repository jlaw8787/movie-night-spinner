Snacks

Beach whale prawns

Garlic chilli prawns. Walter the whale took out a sailboat.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Toss the prawns with the garlic, chillies, zest and butter up to 3 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 3 limes
  • 60g butter, melted
  1. Toss the prawns with garlic, chopped red chillies, lime zest and melted butter.
  2. Grill for a couple of minutes a side until charred.
  3. Pile high on a platter with lime wedges and pour over any butter left in the pan.

All Heart bruschettaVegetarian

Garlic baguette, tomato and basil. Mary doesn't trust him yet

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Toast the slices up to 1 day ahead and keep them airtight; mix the tomato topping up to 2 hours ahead and pile on just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup basil leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Slice the baguette and toast the slices.
  2. Mix the tomatoes with one crushed garlic clove, basil, olive oil and salt.
  3. Rub the toasts with the cut garlic clove and pile the tomato on just before serving.

Prison yard toasted cheese and pickleOn the printed menu

Sharp cheddar and pickle toasted until oozing. Jail watch, day five: good job.

How it is made
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 1 jar sweet pickle relish
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  1. Sharp cheddar and tangy pickle toasted in buttered bread until golden and oozing, cut into squares and lined up on a canteen tray. Butter one side of 8 slices of bread. Lay 4 slices butter side down and spread each with 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish. Top with 200g grated vintage cheddar, then the remaining bread, butter side up. Cook in a frying pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side, pressing down, until golden and melted. Cut each into 4 squares and line them up on a tray.

Mains

PR makeover steak

Sirloin, pepper cream sauce, thick chips. Ray's plan: say sorry, then go to prison

Prep 15 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Cut the chips up to 1 day ahead and keep them in cold water in the fridge, dried well before frying; cook the steaks and sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil for frying
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 tbsp cracked peppercorns
  • 200ml cream
  • 100g mixed salad leaves
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips.
  2. Fry twice in the oil until crisp.
  3. Grill the steaks to medium-rare and rest.
  4. In the pan, toast the peppercorns, add the cream and simmer until thick.
  5. Serve with the chips and a green salad.

Ray's family spaghetti and meatballsOn the printed menu

Parmesan beef balls in slow basil and tomato sauce over pasta, crisp garlic bread

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 packet breadcrumbs
  • 60g parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 baguette
  • 60g butter
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 100g salad leaves
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Parmesan beef meatballs simmered in a slow tomato and basil sauce over spaghetti, with crisp garlic bread and a green salad. Mix 600g beef mince, 50g breadcrumbs, 40g parmesan, 1 egg, parsley and salt and roll 20 meatballs. Brown in olive oil. Soften 3 garlic cloves and 1 onion, add 2 cans crushed tomatoes and basil, simmer 20 minutes, then add the meatballs for 20 minutes more. Split a baguette, spread with 60g butter mashed with garlic and parsley and bake at 200C for 10 minutes. Cook 500g spaghetti and serve with salad leaves.

Ray's All-Heart browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies. Mary throws Hancock through a wall

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Bake in a lined tin at 170C.
  4. Cool slightly, cut into squares and serve warm with ice cream. Good job.

Hancock's whisky sourVegetarian

Whisky, lemon juice, sugar syrup and egg white shaken

Prep 7 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemon juice and make the sugar syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml whisky
  • 4 lemons
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 2 egg whites
  • 3 dashes bitters
  • 1 bag ice
  1. For each drink, measure 50ml whisky, the juice of 1 lemon, 15ml sugar syrup, 1 egg white and 1 dash bitters into a shaker.
  2. Shake each drink without ice for 10 seconds.
  3. Add ice and shake again, then strain into a short glass over fresh ice.
  4. Shake drops of bitters onto the foam. For the sober version, make iced tea with lemon and honey.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Hancock comes over for dinner at Ray's house.
