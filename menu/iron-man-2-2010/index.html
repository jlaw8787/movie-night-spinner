Snacks

Tony's strawberries and whipped creamVegetarian

Sugared strawberries with lemon and a bowl of softly whipped cream for dipping.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Berries up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 punnets of strawberries, hulled and halved
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Toss the strawberries with the sugar and a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Whip the cream with the icing sugar to soft peaks.
  3. Serve the cream in a bowl beside the berries for dipping.

Vanko's Russian pelmeni

Beef and pork dumplings boiled and tossed in butter and dill, with sour cream.

Prep 48 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill up to 1 day ahead, boil fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Mix the beef and pork minces with salt and pepper.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pinch into little round parcels.
  3. Boil until they float, then a minute more.
  4. Toss in melted butter and dill and serve with plenty of sour cream.

Monaco smoked salmon blinis

Warm buckwheat pancakes, crème fraîche, cured fish, roe and chives, passed round trackside.

How it is made
  • 100g buckwheat flour
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 30g butter
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Warm little buckwheat blinis topped with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, salmon roe and chives, passed round like trackside in Monaco. Whisk 100g buckwheat flour, 50g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 egg and 200ml milk into a thick batter. Drop teaspoons into a buttered frying pan over medium heat and cook 1 minute a side until golden, making about 24. Cool slightly. Top each with a little crème fraîche from 150g, a fold of smoked salmon from 200g, a few salmon roe and snipped chives.

Mains

Stark miso salmon rice bowl

Miso honey glazed salmon over sushi rice with edamame, avocado and pickled ginger.

Prep 9 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 200g frozen edamame, thawed
  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • 2 tbsp pickled ginger
  1. Mix the miso and honey and brush over the salmon.
  2. Roast at 200°C until glazed. Cook the sushi rice at the same time.
  3. Serve the salmon over rice with edamame, avocado and pickled ginger.

Giant sprinkle doughnutsVegetarian

Fried yeast doughnut rings with a simple icing glaze and sprinkles.

Prep 55 min, cook 15 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 4 tbsp milk, for the glaze
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, butter, eggs and warm milk into a soft dough and knead briefly. Prove for an hour.
  2. Roll out, cut into rings and prove again.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C until golden, in batches.
  4. Dip in a glaze of icing sugar and milk and add sprinkles to some.

Drinks

Arc reactor blue lagoon

Vodka, blue curaçao and lime topped with lemonade, with a booze-free swap.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml vodka
  • 80ml blue curacao
  • 40ml fresh lime juice
  • 600ml chilled lemonade
  • 4 lime wheels
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with crushed ice.
  2. Into each pour the vodka, blue curacao and lime juice, then top with chilled lemonade and stir gently.
  3. Garnish with a lime wheel. For a booze-free version, swap the vodka and curacao for blue cordial and extra lime.
