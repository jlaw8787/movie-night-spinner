Snacks

Bagel bites

Toasted minis spread thick with chive and black pepper cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers, sesame, poppy seed and garlic. Softer than a paperweight.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 mini bagels
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 3 tbsp snipped chives
  • black pepper, to taste
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 1 tsp garlic flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Halve the bagels and toast until golden, 5 min.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the milk, chives and pepper.
  3. Mix the sesame, poppy seeds, garlic flakes and salt for the bagel seasoning.
  4. Pipe or spread the schmear thickly onto the bagels. Top with salmon, capers and seasoning.

Dipped coneVegetarian

Vanilla soft serve dipped in dark chocolate that sets to a shell, then crushed peanuts or sprinkles. Better than the truck.

Prep 20 min, cook 8 min, 20 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 8 ice cream cones
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 40ml coconut oil
  • 50g roasted peanuts, crushed, or sprinkles
  1. Soften the ice cream on the bench.
  2. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil, then cool to room temperature, 8 min.
  3. Pipe the ice cream into the cones with a large star nozzle and freeze, 20 min.
  4. Dip the tops into the chocolate, then straight into the crushed peanuts or sprinkles.
  5. Freeze until the shell sets, 5 min.

Last Stop burger

Hand-formed chuck, salted right before the flat top, cooked medium under melted cheddar. Raw onion, dill pickles, toasted bun.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef chuck mince
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 80g cheddar, sliced
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 80g dill pickles, sliced
  1. Hand-form the mince into four thick patties without overworking it.
  2. Heat a griddle over medium-high heat. Season the patties just before cooking, 5 min.
  3. Griddle until cooked through and the centre reaches 75°C, topping with cheddar in the last minute, 12 min.
  4. Toast the buns cut-side down. Assemble with the patties, raw onion and pickles, 5 min.

Plaster of Paris cakeVegetarian

Vanilla butter layers under seven-minute frosting, roughed up with a palette knife to set white and matte. For the sculptors on the block.

Prep 21 min, cook 49 min
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs, 2 whole and 2 whites
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 120g self-raising flour
  • 30ml milk
  • 1 tbsp water
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C conventional and line a 16cm cake tin, 5 min.
  2. Beat the butter with half the sugar until pale. Beat in the 2 whole eggs and vanilla, then fold in the flour and milk.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean, then cool completely, 35 min.
  4. Whisk the egg whites, remaining sugar and water in a heatproof bowl over gently simmering water until thick, glossy and hot throughout.
  5. Remove from the heat and whisk until stiff.
  6. Cover the cake in a thick matte layer, roughly smoothed like plaster. Cut into four.

Whiskey sour

Bourbon, lemon, a little sugar and egg white shaken silky, bitters on the foam, a cherry. Tom shakes them hard.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 50ml hot water
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 4 lemons, juiced for 90ml
  • 1 egg white
  • 8 drops of aromatic bitters
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool, 4 min.
  2. Shake the bourbon, lemon juice, the cooled syrup and egg white hard without ice, then again with ice.
  3. Strain into 4 ice-filled rocks glasses. Add bitters to each foam and a cherry.
