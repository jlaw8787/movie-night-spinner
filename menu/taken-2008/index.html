Snacks

96 hour croque monsieur

Ninety-six hours to find her. Ham, gruyère and Dijon under béchamel.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Assemble ahead, bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g gruyere
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and cook until thick.
  2. Spread the bread with Dijon and bechamel.
  3. Fill with ham and half the gruyere, then close.
  4. Top with the rest of the sauce and cheese.
  5. Bake at 200C until bubbling.

Marko's good luck spinach byrekVegetarian

Marko's whole reply: Good luck. Spinach, feta and ricotta in filo.

Prep 30 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g spinach
  • 200g feta
  • 200g ricotta
  • 2 eggs
  • 8 sheets filo pastry
  • 80g butter
  1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Mix with the feta, ricotta and eggs.
  3. Brush the filo sheets with melted butter, spread filling along each and roll into ropes.
  4. Coil in a round tin and bake at 190C.
  5. Cut into wedges.

Mains

Jean-Claude's tarragon roast chicken

Roast chicken with tarragon butter under the skin, creamy potato gratin

Prep 25 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Gratin can be made 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 150ml white wine
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 300g green beans
  1. Slice the potatoes thin and layer in a dish with the cream, garlic and salt.
  2. Mash the butter with chopped tarragon and push under the chicken skin.
  3. Roast the chicken at 220C, then at 180C, with the gratin on the lower shelf until tender and golden.
  4. Rest the chicken.
  5. Add the wine and Dijon to the tray, scrape and reduce by half.
  6. Steam the beans and serve.

Sweets

Crème caramelVegetarian

Silky baked vanilla custard with a dark caramel sauce

Prep 30 min, cook 755 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 vanilla bean
  1. Melt part of the sugar to amber caramel and pour into four ramekins.
  2. Whisk the eggs, yolks, remaining sugar, milk and vanilla seeds.
  3. Pour over and bake in a water bath at 150C.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Turn out to serve.

Drinks

Kim's U2 tour lemon sodaVegetarian

Fresh squeezed lemon, sugar syrup and soda water over ice, cognac optional

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemons and mix with the sugar syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice, cognac and soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 7 lemons
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 120ml cognac
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Squeeze the lemons.
  2. Fill four tall glasses with ice and add lemon juice and sugar syrup to each.
  3. Add cognac to the glasses that want it.
  4. Top with cold soda water, stir and add a lemon wheel.
