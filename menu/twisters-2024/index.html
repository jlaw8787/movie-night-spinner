Fried okra with comeback sauceVegetarian

Cornmeal-crusted okra and a smoky dip. The Wranglers roll in from Arkansas.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the comeback sauce up to 3 days ahead; crumb and fry the okra to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g fresh okra, cut in 2cm pieces
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/2 lemon
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, chilli sauce, tomato sauce, hot sauce and a squeeze of lemon into the comeback sauce.
  2. Soak the okra in the buttermilk, then toss it in the cornmeal, flour, paprika and a big pinch of salt.
  3. Fry in batches at 180°C until crisp and golden, then drain.
  4. Salt straight away and serve hot with the sauce.

El Reno fried onion burgers

Smashed beef with crisp onions and American cheese, an El Reno classic.

Prep 4 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Slice the onions and ball the mince up to a day ahead; smash and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince, 20 percent fat
  • 2 brown onions, sliced paper thin
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 8 slider buns
  • 20g butter
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Roll the beef into 8 balls and season well.
  2. Heat a heavy pan very hot, add a ball, pile on a handful of onions and smash flat through the onions with a spatula.
  3. Cook until the edges crust, flip so the onions fry underneath, and lay on a slice of cheese.
  4. Toast the buttered buns, then build with pickle and mustard and serve hot.

Sapulpa chicken-fried steak

Crumbed steak, pepper gravy, mash and greens.

Prep 12 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Crumb the steaks up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fry and make the gravy to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 150g beef minute steaks
  • 150g plain flour, plus 2 tbsp for the gravy
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 250ml vegetable oil
  • 500ml milk
  • 800g floury potatoes
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch silverbeet, shredded
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with the butter and a splash of the milk.
  2. Season the flour with garlic powder, pepper and salt; whisk the eggs with the buttermilk.
  3. Dip the steaks in flour, egg, then flour again, pressing it on well.
  4. Shallow-fry in hot oil until deep golden, about 3 minutes a side, and drain.
  5. Pour off all but 2 tbsp of oil, whisk in 2 tbsp flour, then the rest of the milk, and simmer into a peppery gravy.
  6. Wilt the silverbeet and serve with the steak, mash and gravy.

Smoked brisket rolls

Slow-smoked beef brisket in soft rolls with jalapeño slaw.

Prep 18 min, cook 4.5 hours · Make ahead: Cook the brisket up to 2 days ahead and chill in its juices; reheat, slice and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef brisket
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 6 soft long rolls
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapenos, chopped
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  1. Rub the brisket all over with the paprika, sugar, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Sit it in a roasting dish with the stock, cover tightly and cook at 150°C until it pulls apart.
  3. Rest, then slice or shred and toss with the barbecue sauce and some of the pan juices.
  4. Mix the cabbage, carrot, jalapenos, mayo and vinegar into a slaw.
  5. Pile the brisket and slaw into the rolls.

Rodeo funnel cakes with strawberriesVegetarian

Fried batter swirls, icing sugar, berries.

Prep 6 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 330ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  • 4 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, eggs, milk and vanilla into a smooth pouring batter.
  2. Heat the oil to 180°C in a wide pan.
  3. Pour a cup of batter through a funnel or a squeeze bottle in a loose swirl and fry until golden, turning once.
  4. Drain, dust heavily with icing sugar and top with strawberries.

Storm chaser bourbon lemonade

Bourbon, fresh lemonade and a mint sprig.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the lemonade up to 2 days ahead and keep cold; add the bourbon to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml fresh lemon juice
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 600ml cold water
  • 160ml bourbon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Stir the lemon juice and sugar until dissolved, then add the water.
  2. Fill 4 jars with ice, add 40ml bourbon each, top with lemonade and a mint sprig.
