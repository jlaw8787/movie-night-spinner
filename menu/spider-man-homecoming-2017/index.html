Decathlon bus salted caramel popcornVegetarian

Caramel popcorn. Peter saves the team in the Washington Monument lift

Prep 8 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 60ml golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100g pretzels
  • 100g roasted peanuts
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot.
  2. Melt the butter with the sugar and golden syrup and boil, then take off the heat and stir in the bicarb soda and salt.
  3. Pour over the popcorn, pretzels and peanuts and stir to coat.
  4. Spread on lined trays and bake at 120°C, stirring twice.
  5. Cool and break up.

Liz's party buffalo cauliflowerVegetarian

Hot sauce cauliflower. Karen finds his Thor impression very funny

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Cut the florets up to 1 day ahead and mix the hot sauce and butter up to 2 days ahead; batter and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 3/4 cup plain flour
  • 180ml water
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 200ml blue cheese dressing
  1. Whisk a batter of flour, water and garlic powder and toss the cauliflower through.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and bake at 220°C.
  3. Toss in the hot sauce mixed with the melted butter.
  4. Bake again until sticky and serve with blue cheese dressing.

Mains

Delmar's number five, smushed flat

Salami, ham, provolone, pickles and mustard on a long roll, pressed flat and crisp

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped and chilled; press just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long bread rolls
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 150g salami, sliced
  • 150g leg ham, sliced
  • 150g provolone, sliced
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  1. Split the rolls and spread with mustard.
  2. Layer with salami, ham, provolone and pickles.
  3. Press hard in a sandwich press or heavy pan with a weight on top until flat, crisp and melted.
  4. Cut in half to serve.

Staten Island Ferry chicken parm heroesOn the printed menu

Chicken parm in a hero roll, split like the ferry

How it is made
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko crumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 250ml passata
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 4 long rolls
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Crumbed chicken schnitzels fried crisp, baked under tomato sauce and melted mozzarella, stuffed into long toasted rolls with basil. Halve 2 large chicken breasts horizontally and pound thin. Coat in flour, beaten egg and 150g panko mixed with 30g parmesan. Shallow fry in oil for 3 minutes a side until golden. Lay on a tray, spoon over 250ml passata cooked with garlic and oregano, top with 200g grated mozzarella and grill until bubbling. Tuck into 4 toasted long rolls with basil leaves.

Homecoming dance cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes in red and blue swirls. "Just not too good," says Toomes.

Prep 30 min · Make ahead: Cakes up to 1 day, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 200g butter for buttercream, softened
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 1 tsp blue food colouring
  • 50g white icing
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then the eggs, and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Fill a lined cupcake tin and bake at 175°C, then cool completely.
  3. Beat the buttercream and split it in two, colouring one half red and the other blue.
  4. Pipe swirls on top and draw a little white icing web over each.

Death Star bubble teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Brown sugar pearls. Ned drops the Lego Death Star

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Pearls and tea up to 1 day; assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g tapioca pearls
  • 4 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp hot water
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 600ml boiling water
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Cook the pearls as the pack says, drain and soak in the brown sugar and hot water.
  2. Brew the tea in the boiling water.
  3. Chill the tea.
  4. Spoon pearls and syrup into tall glasses and add ice.
  5. Shake the tea with the milk and sugar until frothy and pour over.
