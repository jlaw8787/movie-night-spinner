Snacks

Invisible ink lemon chicken bites

Grilled lemon oregano chicken pieces on toothpicks, lemon mayo to dip

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 2 lemons, one for the marinade and one for the sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g mayonnaise
  1. Marinate the chicken in lemon juice, garlic, oregano and oil for an hour.
  2. Grill until charred and cooked through.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with lemon zest and juice. Spear the chicken on toothpicks and serve with the sauce.

Silence Dogood soft pretzelsVegetarian

Soft figure-eight pretzels with rock salt, butter and yellow mustard

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp rock salt
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and warm water to a dough and knead briefly. Rest for an hour.
  2. Shape into figure eights.
  3. Dip in boiling water with the bicarb soda and sprinkle with rock salt.
  4. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Brush with butter and serve with mustard.

Arctic ship clam chowder cupsOn the printed menu

Creamy bacon, potato and shellfish soup with thyme, oyster crackers, before Ian turns on them

How it is made
  • 1kg clams
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch celery
  • 500g potatoes
  • 500ml milk
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 packet oyster crackers
  1. Creamy chowder of bacon, potato, onion and clams finished with cream and thyme, served in small cups with oyster crackers on top. Steam 1kg clams in 250ml water in a covered pot for 5 minutes until open, then pick out the meat and strain the liquid. Fry 150g diced bacon, add 1 diced onion and 2 celery sticks and soften. Add 500g diced potatoes, the clam liquid and 500ml milk, and simmer 15 minutes. Stir in 150ml cream, the clams and thyme, and season. Serve in cups with oyster crackers.

Mains

2:22 Independence Hall cheesesteaks

Shaved rib eye with onion and melted provolone on long rolls

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the steak, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rib eye steak
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 150g provolone, sliced
  • 4 long bread rolls
  1. Freeze the steak for 30 minutes, then shave it paper thin.
  2. Fry the onions in the oil until soft.
  3. Add the beef and sear fast on high heat.
  4. Lay the provolone over the top to melt and pile into split rolls.

Founding Fathers chicken and leek pot pieOn the printed menu

Thighs and mushroom in creamy thyme sauce under a golden puff lid, buttered peas

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 leeks
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 400g frozen peas
  1. Chicken, leek and mushroom in a creamy thyme sauce, baked under a golden puff pastry lid, with buttered peas on the side. Brown 800g diced chicken thighs, then remove. Soften 2 sliced leeks and 200g mushrooms in 40g butter, stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 300ml chicken stock and 150ml cream. Simmer until thick, add thyme and the chicken, and season. Pour into a pie dish, top with a puff pastry sheet, brush with egg and cut a slit. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes. Serve with 400g buttered peas.

Philly lemon water iceVegetarian

Frozen lemon ice scraped fluffy with a fork as it freezes

Prep 15 min, then 4.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, scrape and serve fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 750ml water
  • 250ml lemon juice
  • 2 lemons, zested, from the 8 lemons in total
  • 125g raspberries
  1. Boil the water and sugar until dissolved, then cool. Stir in the lemon juice and zest.
  2. Pour into a shallow metal tray and freeze for 4 hours, scraping and stirring with a fork every 45 minutes so it stays smooth and fine.
  3. Blitz briefly in a food processor just before serving, scoop into cups and top with raspberries.

Drinks

Invisible ink lemon, ginger and mint fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemon, ginger syrup and muddled mint over crushed ice, topped with soda water

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, mix fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 1 knob ginger (about 5cm), sliced
  • 1 cup mint leaves, plus sprigs
  • 200ml lemon juice
  • 800ml chilled soda water
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and ginger for 5 minutes, then cool and strain.
  2. Muddle the mint with the lemon juice and ginger syrup in a jug.
  3. Fill four glasses with crushed ice, pour in the lemon mix and top with soda water.
  4. Garnish with lemon wheels and mint sprigs.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Ben brushes lemon juice on the back of the Declaration.
