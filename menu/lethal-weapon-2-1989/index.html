Leo Getz's drive-thru friesVegetarian

Thin russet fries fried twice until crisp.

Prep 12 min, cook 18 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cut and soak the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead in cold water; dry well and do both fries on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg russet potatoes
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 4 paper bags
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries.
  2. Soak in water, then dry well.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C until cooked but pale, then drain and cool.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until crisp.
  5. Toss with salt and serve in paper bags.

Consulate biltong

Thin biltong with dried peaches and salted macadamias.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Roast and salt the macadamias up to 3 days ahead in an airtight jar; slice the biltong up to 1 day ahead, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 150g macadamias
  • salt, to taste
  • 250g beef biltong
  • 150g dried peaches
  1. Roast the macadamias at 170°C and toss with salt while warm.
  2. Slice the beef biltong thinly across the grain.
  3. Pile on a board with the dried peaches and macadamias in little bowls.

Mains

Boerewors rolls

Grilled boerewors sausage in rolls with tomato chilli relish.

Prep 13 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the relish up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; grill the boerewors just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 onions
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 chilli
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1kg boerewors, in a coil
  • 4 long bread rolls
  1. Slice the onions, chop the tomatoes and chilli.
  2. Simmer with the sugar into a relish.
  3. Grill the boerewors over coals or a hot grill, turning once.
  4. Cut the boerewors and serve in rolls with relish.

Leo's double smash cheeseburger

Two smashed patties, melted American, onion, pickles and mustard, okay, okay, okay.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • yellow mustard
  • tomato sauce
  1. Two smashed beef patties with melted American cheese, onion, pickles, mustard and tomato sauce on a toasted soft bun, wrapped in paper. Roll 800g beef mince into 8 balls of 100g. Heat a heavy pan or flat grill until smoking. Add a ball, smash it flat with a spatula and season. Cook 2 minutes until crusty, flip, top with a slice of cheese and cook 1 minute more. Toast 4 buns cut side down. Stack two patties on each bun with finely diced onion, sliced pickles, mustard and tomato sauce, then wrap tightly in baking paper for 2 minutes to steam.

TV ad cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with swirled pastel buttercream and sprinkles.

Prep 18 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g butter, softened, for icing
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp food colouring, in pastel shades
  • 1 jar sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 12-hole tin. Beat the butter for the cakes with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake the cupcakes.
  3. Cool on a rack.
  4. Beat the butter for the icing with the icing sugar and tint in pastel colours with the food colouring.
  5. Swirl on the icing and add sprinkles.

Drinks

Roger's ginger and lime lager shandy

Cold lager topped with ginger beer and fresh lime over ice.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the limes and chill the lager and ginger beer up to 1 day ahead; build over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tray ice
  • 3 limes
  • 800ml cold lager
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and squeeze lime into each.
  2. Pour in the lager, top with ginger beer and stir gently once.
  3. Garnish with a lime wheel and mint and serve straight away.
