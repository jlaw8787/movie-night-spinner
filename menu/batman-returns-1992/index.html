Snacks

Smoked salmon on rye

Dark rye fingers with smoked salmon, crème fraîche, capers and dill.

Prep 8 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 slices of dark rye bread
  • 30g butter
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Butter the ginger.
  2. Top with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, a few capers and dill sprigs.
  3. Cut into fingers with a sharp knife and serve cold on a dark plate.

Gotham Plaza roasted chestnutsVegetarian

Chestnuts roasted until the shells curl, peeled warm and salted.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Cut a cross in each chestnut up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; soak and roast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chestnuts
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Cut a cross into each chestnut and soak in water, 20 min.
  2. Roast at 220°C until the shells curl back, 20 min.
  3. Wrap in a tea towel to steam, 5 min.
  4. Peel while warm and sprinkle with flaky salt, 5 min.

Catwoman's nine lives crispy whitebait

Whitebait dusted in flour and fried crisp, a little fish for the cat in the house.

How it is made
  • 500g whitebait
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • chilli flakes
  • 1 bulb black garlic
  • 120g mayo
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Whitebait tossed in seasoned flour and fried shatteringly crisp, with lemon, chilli salt and a black garlic aioli, piled high in paper cones. Rinse and pat dry 500g whitebait. Toss in 100g plain flour mixed with 1 tsp paprika, salt and pepper and shake off the excess. Fry in batches at 190°C for 2 minutes until crisp. Mash 2 cloves black garlic into 120g mayo with a squeeze of lemon. Toss the whitebait with chilli salt and serve in paper cones with lemon wedges and the aioli.

Mains

Max Shreck's masquerade roast

Crackling pork loin with roast potatoes and chunky sage apple sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 50 min, 1 day waiting · Make ahead: Dry the pork the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork loin, skin on
  • 2 tbsp sea salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 apples
  • 1 small bunch sage
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Score the pork and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Salt well and roast at 240°C to start the crackling, 30 min.
  3. Drop to 180°C and roast until 63°C inside, adding the potatoes tossed in oil alongside, 1 hr.
  4. Meanwhile cook the apples with sage and a little sugar to a chunky sauce, 20 min.
  5. Rest the pork before carving, 15 min.

Sweets

Snow globe cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with buttercream, a sugar tree and coconut snow.

Prep 13 min, cook 28 min, 12 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g shredded coconut
  • 12 sugar Christmas trees
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 12-hole tin. Beat half the butter with the sugar, then the eggs, 10 min.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk, divide between the cases and bake, 18 min.
  3. Cool completely, 12 min.
  4. Beat the rest of the butter with the icing sugar and ice the cupcakes, 8 min.
  5. Add a sugar tree and shredded coconut snow to each.

Drinks

Milk punchNo alcohol

Cold milk, vanilla and black tea shaken cold, with grated nutmeg on top.

Prep 6 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Steep and cool the tea, then mix with the milk, sugar syrup and vanilla up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; shake with ice and strain to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 black tea bags
  • 150ml boiling water
  • 360ml full cream milk
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 4 minutes, remove and cool.
  2. Shake the cold tea, milk, sugar syrup and vanilla hard with ice, a drink at a time.
  3. Strain into chilled coupes and grate nutmeg over the top.
