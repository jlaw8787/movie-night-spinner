Cafe 80's friesVegetarian

Spiralized potatoes fried crisp, tossed with smoked paprika salt

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Fries can be soaked up to 4 hours ahead, fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Spiralise the potatoes and soak in cold water so the starch rinses out.
  2. Drain and dry very well on clean tea towels, any water will spit in the oil.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry in batches until crisp and golden.
  4. Mix the salt with the smoked paprika, toss through the hot fries and serve straight away.

Hoverboard tortilla chipsVegetarian

Pink beetroot dusted tortilla chips with fresh guacamole

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Chips up to 1 day, airtight. Guacamole best made just before
Recipe for 4
  • 6 flour tortillas
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp beetroot powder
  • 3 avocados
  • 1 lime
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the tortillas into hoverboard shapes with a knife or template.
  2. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry the shapes in batches until crisp, then drain on paper towel.
  3. Toss the hot chips with salt and the beetroot powder until pink.
  4. Mash the avocados with the lime juice and salt to make the guacamole. Serve with the chips.

Sports almanac parmesan crackersOn the printed menu

Thin rosemary and cheese crackers cut like little books, baked golden, with fig and onion jam

How it is made
  • 120g plain flour
  • 60g butter
  • 80g parmesan
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 jar fig and onion jam
  1. Crisp parmesan and rosemary crackers rolled thin and cut into little book shapes, baked golden and served with a fig and onion jam. Rub 60g cold butter into 120g plain flour, then mix in 80g finely grated parmesan, 1 tsp chopped rosemary and a pinch of salt. Add 2 to 3 tbsp cold water to bring it together and chill 20 minutes. Roll 3mm thick and cut into 4 by 6cm rectangles. Score a line down the middle of each like a book spine. Bake at 180°C for 12 minutes until golden. Serve with fig jam.

Mains

Hydrated pizza

Thin crust pizza with crushed tomato, mozzarella and pepperoni

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Slice the pepperoni and cheese up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; stretch the dough and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 mini pizza bases
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 300g low-moisture mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  1. Heat the oven to its highest setting with a pizza steel or heavy tray inside.
  2. Top the mini bases with a little sauce and cheese and bake for the reveal.
  3. Stretch the dough as thin as you can and spread with the crushed tomatoes.
  4. Scatter over the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  5. Bake on the hot steel at maximum heat until the crust blisters and the cheese bubbles.

Biff's Paradise steak and lobster macOn the printed menu

Butter-basted rib eye, peppercorn sauce, cheddar macaroni and lobster, in alternate 1985

How it is made
  • 4 rib eye steaks (300g each)
  • 300g macaroni
  • 120g butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 600ml milk
  • 200g cheddar
  • 2 raw lobster tails
  • 2 tbsp brandy
  • black peppercorns
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Rib eye seared and butter-basted with peppercorn sauce, beside a gooey cheddar macaroni with butter-poached lobster folded through. Salt 4 rib eye steaks the night before. Cook 300g macaroni. Melt 40g butter, stir in 40g flour, whisk in 600ml milk and cook until thick, then add 200g cheddar and the pasta. Poach 2 raw lobster tails, cut into chunks, in 60g butter for 4 minutes and fold through. Sear the steaks 3 minutes a side, basting with butter, and rest 8 minutes. Add 2 tbsp brandy, 1 tbsp cracked pepper and 150ml cream to the pan and reduce.

Sweets

Jaws 19 shark jelly

Blue jelly with gummy sharks, topped with whipped cream waves

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets blue jelly crystals
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 500ml cold water
  • 1 bag gummy sharks
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Dissolve the jelly crystals in the boiling water, then stir in the cold water.
  2. Pour halfway into four glasses and chill until just starting to set.
  3. Push the gummy sharks in, top up with the rest of the jelly and chill until fully set.
  4. Whip the cream to soft peaks and spoon on top as waves.

Cafe 80's milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Strawberry ice cream, fresh strawberries and milk blended thick

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Make one after the other so they stay thick
Recipe for 4
  • 12 scoops strawberry ice cream
  • 16 strawberries
  • 600ml full cream milk
  1. Chill four tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Blend each shake in turn with its ice cream, strawberries and milk until thick.
  3. Pour into the chilled glasses and add a straw.
