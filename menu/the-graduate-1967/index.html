Snacks

Graduation party prawn cocktail

Prawn cocktail with a pink sauce. Mr. McGuire has one word for Ben: plastics.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 2 days ahead; build the glasses to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked king prawns, peeled
  • 5 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 drops Tabasco
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/4 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Stir the mayo, tomato sauce, Worcestershire, Tabasco and a squeeze of lemon into a pink sauce.
  2. Divide the lettuce and avocado between 4 glasses.
  3. Pile on the prawns and spoon over the sauce.
  4. Dust with paprika and hang a lemon wedge on each glass.

Drifting-in-the-pool crab and cucumber cups

Crab and dill in cucumber cups. Ben is just drifting here in the pool.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the crab up to 4 hours ahead and chill; spoon onto the cucumber to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cooked crab meat
  • 3 tbsp creme fraiche
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 2 telegraph cucumbers
  • 1 tbsp salmon roe
  1. Mix the crab with the creme fraiche, lemon zest, a squeeze of juice, dill and pepper.
  2. Cut the cucumbers into thick rounds and scoop a small hollow in each.
  3. Spoon in the crab.
  4. Top each with a few beads of roe.

Mains

Mr. Gladstone's room service steak Diane

Steak Diane with crisp potatoes. Ben signs in at the Taft as Mr. Gladstone.

Prep 7 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms and bring the steaks to room temperature an hour ahead; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 beef fillet steaks, about 180g each
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 40g butter
  • 2 French shallots, finely chopped
  • 150g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 600g potatoes, cut into thin chips
  1. Bake the potato chips with a little oil at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Sear the seasoned steaks in the oil until done to your liking, then rest.
  3. Melt the butter and cook the shallots and mushrooms until golden.
  4. Add the brandy and let it bubble off, then stir in the mustard, Worcestershire and cream.
  5. Return the steaks with their juices, scatter with parsley and serve with the chips.

Elaine's drive-in cheeseburger

Cheeseburger with fries. Ben and Elaine finally talk at a drive-in.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to a day ahead and chill; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 sesame burger buns
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1/2 white onion, sliced into rings
  • 4 tbsp burger sauce
  • 750g frozen shoestring fries
  1. Bake the fries at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Shape the mince into 4 patties and season well.
  3. Grill over high heat, adding the cheese for the last minute.
  4. Build the toasted buns with sauce, lettuce, tomato, onion and patty, and serve with the fries.

Santa Barbara runaway wedding cakeVegetarian

Vanilla wedding cake with buttercream. Elaine runs out on her own wedding.

Prep 35 min · Make ahead: Bake the sponges up to a day ahead and wrap well; fill and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 250g lemon curd
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  2. Fold in the flour, vanilla and milk and split between 2 lined 20cm tins.
  3. Bake at 180°C until springy, then cool.
  4. Whip the cream with the icing sugar, spread the curd and half the cream between the layers, and cover the top with the rest.

Mrs. Robinson's late-night bourbonVegetarian

Bourbon over a big ice cube. Mrs. Robinson pours Ben a drink.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Freeze large ice cubes the day before; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml Kentucky bourbon
  • 1 orange
  • 4 large ice cubes
  1. Put a large ice cube in each of 4 short glasses and pour over 60ml bourbon.
  2. Twist a strip of orange peel over each glass and drop it in.
