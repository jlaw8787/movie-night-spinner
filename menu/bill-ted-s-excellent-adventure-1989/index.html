Snacks

Olives and fetaVegetarian

Marinated feta and olives with lemon, oregano and crusty bread

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g feta, cubed
  • 200g mixed olives
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • pinch of chilli flakes
  • 1 crusty loaf, sliced
  1. Mix the feta with the olives, oil, lemon zest, oregano and chilli.
  2. Leave at room temperature to marinate.
  3. Serve with the sliced crusty loaf.

Medieval turkey legs

Brined roast turkey drumsticks glazed with honey and wholegrain mustard

Prep 5 min, cook 90 min, then 11 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brine the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 80g sea salt
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 2l water
  • 4 turkey drumsticks
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Dissolve the salt and sugar in the water and brine the drumsticks overnight in the fridge.
  2. Pat the drumsticks dry.
  3. Roast at 160°C for most of the cooking time.
  4. Mix the honey and mustard, brush over and roast until glazed.

Genghis Khan's cinnamon pretzel bitesOn the printed menu

Cinnamon pretzel bites. Genghis Khan likes a sugar rush

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 60g bicarb soda
  • 50g butter
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  1. Soft pretzel bites baked deep brown, rolled in butter and cinnamon sugar, with a sweet cream cheese icing dip, straight from a food court stall. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead and prove 1 hour. Roll into ropes and cut into 3cm bites. Dip in 2 litres boiling water with 60g bicarb soda for 30 seconds and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Toss in 50g melted butter, then 80g caster sugar mixed with 2 tsp cinnamon. Beat 100g cream cheese with 80g icing sugar and 2 tbsp milk for the dip.

Napoleon's chicken Marengo

Chicken thighs braised with mushrooms, tomato and white wine, crusty bread

Prep 9 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 125ml white wine
  • 400g diced tomatoes (1 tin)
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in batches and set aside.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in the same pan.
  3. Add the mushrooms, wine and tomatoes.
  4. Return the chicken, cover and simmer.
  5. Scatter with parsley and serve with the baguette.

Socrates' chicken souvlaki, Greek saladOn the printed menu

Lemon, garlic and oregano skewers, pita and tzatziki, tomato salad with feta, for So-crates

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • dried oregano
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 100g kalamata olives
  • 150g feta
  • 4 pita breads
  • olive oil
  1. Lemon, garlic and oregano chicken skewers grilled until charred, in warm pita with tzatziki, beside a chunky Greek salad with a slab of feta. Cube 800g chicken thighs and marinate 1 hour in 3 tbsp olive oil, the juice of 1 lemon, 2 crushed garlic cloves and 2 tsp dried oregano. Thread onto 8 skewers and grill 10 to 12 minutes, turning. Grate 1 cucumber, squeeze dry and mix with 200g Greek yoghurt, garlic and dill. Chop 3 tomatoes, the rest of a cucumber, half a red onion and 1 green capsicum with olives, top with 150g feta, oregano and oil. Warm 4 pita.

Ziggy Piggy sundaeVegetarian

Bananas, three ice creams, cream and sauce. Eat the pig!

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream and chop the nuts up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; split the bananas and scoop the ice cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bananas
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml chocolate ice cream
  • 500ml strawberry ice cream
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100ml chocolate sauce
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  • 50g mixed nuts, chopped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Split the bananas and line a long dish.
  2. Add scoops of the three ice creams down the middle.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Pile on cream, chocolate sauce, sprinkles, nuts and cherries. Give everyone a long spoon.

Drinks

Wyld Stallyns grape soda spiderVegetarianNo alcohol

Grape juice and soda water poured over vanilla ice cream, no alcohol

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the grape juice and lime juice up to 1 day ahead and chill; add the ice cream and soda water just before serving so it foams
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml grape juice
  • 1 lime
  • 4 big scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml soda water
  1. Mix the grape juice with the lime juice.
  2. Add a scoop of ice cream to each of four tall glasses and pour the grape mix over.
  3. Slowly top with cold soda water so it foams up. Serve straight away with long spoons and straws.
