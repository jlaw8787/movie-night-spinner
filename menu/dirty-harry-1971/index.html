Snacks

Six shots or five stuffed jalapeñosVegetarian

Cheese-stuffed jalapeños, crumbed and fried. Did he fire six shots, or only five?

Prep 15 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Stuff and crumb the poppers up to a day ahead and chill on a tray; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 12 large jalapeños, halved and seeded
  • 200g cream cheese, softened
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chives and dill, chopped
  1. Mix the cream cheese, cheddar and spring onion and fill each jalapeño half.
  2. Dust in flour, dip in egg and press into the panko. Chill for 10 minutes.
  3. Fry at 180°C in batches until golden, about 3 minutes, and drain.
  4. Stir the sour cream, mayo and herbs for a quick ranch and serve alongside.

Kezar Stadium soft pretzelsVegetarian

Salted soft pretzels with mustard. Harry finds Scorpio at Kezar Stadium.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Shape the pretzels up to a day ahead and chill; boil and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ball pizza dough
  • 2 L water
  • 60g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp pretzel or sea salt
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml milk
  • 120g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 3 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Cut the dough into 8, roll into long ropes and twist into pretzel shapes.
  2. Bring the water and bicarb to the boil and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds, then lift onto a lined tray.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with salt and bake at 220°C until deep brown.
  4. Cook the butter and flour, whisk in the milk until thick, then melt in the cheddar.
  5. Serve the pretzels warm with the cheese sauce and mustard.

Mains

Burger Den jumbo hot dogs

Jumbo hot dogs with mustard. Harry only gets one bite of lunch.

Prep 15 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 jumbo beef frankfurts
  • 8 long split-top bread rolls
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml water
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince with one of the onions and the garlic, breaking it up very fine.
  2. Stir in the chilli powder, cumin and tomato paste, add the water and simmer until thick.
  3. Grill the franks until blistered and warm the rolls.
  4. Load each roll with a frank, a ladle of chilli, mustard, the rest of the raw onion and cheddar.

Sweets

Ghirardelli hot fudge sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with Ghirardelli hot fudge. Harry throws his badge in the water.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to a week ahead and warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 150g Ghirardelli dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 20g butter
  • 50g flaked almonds, toasted
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Heat the cream, golden syrup and butter until steaming, then stir in the chocolate until glossy.
  2. Toast the almonds in a dry pan.
  3. Scoop the ice cream into glasses, pour over the hot fudge and top with whipped cream, almonds and a cherry.

Drinks

Feeling lucky Irish coffee

Hot coffee, Irish whiskey and cream. Harry asks the punk if he feels lucky.

Prep 8 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Lightly whip the cream up to an hour ahead and keep it cold; brew the coffee to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  • 600ml hot strong coffee
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Warm 4 heatproof glasses with hot water, then tip it out.
  2. Stir a teaspoon of sugar and 40ml whiskey into each, then fill with hot coffee.
  3. Whip the cream until just thick and float it over the back of a spoon.
