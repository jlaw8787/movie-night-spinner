Snacks

Munchkinland cheese tartsVegetarian

Mini gruyère and chive custard tarts baked until puffed

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Cut and line the pastry cases up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; fill and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 80g gruyère
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Line a 12-hole tray with rounds cut from the shortcrust pastry.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the cream, the grated gruyère and snipped chives, pour into the cases and bake at 180C for 18 minutes until puffed and golden.

Mains

Yellow Brick Road roast chicken

Tarragon lemon butter roast chicken with potatoes and green beans

Prep 20 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Mix the tarragon butter and push it under the skin up to 1 day ahead, chilled; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1 lemon
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 800g potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  1. Push the butter mixed with chopped tarragon and lemon zest under the skin of the chicken.
  2. Roast at 200C for 75 minutes with the potatoes in chunks around it.
  3. Steam the green beans and toss in the pan juices.

Sweets

Pistachio shortbread cookiesVegetarian

Buttery pistachio shortbread, green as the elixir bottle

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g pistachios
  • 150g butter
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 180g plain flour
  1. Blitz the pistachios fine.
  2. Beat the butter with the icing sugar, then mix in the flour and the pistachios.
  3. Roll into a log, chill 30 minutes, slice into rounds and bake at 160C for 15 minutes.
  4. Dust with icing sugar.

Nessarose's enchanted shoe raspberry tartsVegetarian

Sweet pastry cases with vanilla custard and glazed raspberries

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Bake the tart cases up to 2 days ahead in an airtight tin; fill with cooled custard and top with raspberries on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 tbsp custard powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml milk
  • 250g raspberries
  • 2 tbsp raspberry jam
  1. Bake the small tart cases cut from the sweet shortcrust at 180C for 12 minutes.
  2. Make custard from the custard powder, the sugar and the milk, cool and fill the cases.
  3. Top with the raspberries brushed with warm raspberry jam.

Glinda's bubble emerald mint fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Muddled mint, lime and sugar topped with soda water

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Muddle the mint, lime juice and sugar in the jug up to 4 hours ahead and chill; add ice and soda water just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 3 limes
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1l soda water
  1. Muddle a handful of mint leaves with the juice of the limes and the sugar in a jug.
  2. Add ice and top with the soda water, stir and pour into glasses with a mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the banquet when Oz celebrates the opening of the Yellow Brick Road.
