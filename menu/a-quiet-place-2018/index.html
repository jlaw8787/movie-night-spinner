Snacks

Lettuce leaf chicken and rice cups

Shredded poached chicken and jasmine rice in lettuce leaves with lime and fish sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Poach and shred the chicken and mix the dressing up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; cook the rice and fill the cups on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chicken breast
  • 200g jasmine rice
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 2 butter lettuces
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  • 50g roasted peanuts, crushed
  1. Poach the chicken in simmering salted water, rest, then shred.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Mix the lime juice, fish sauce, sugar and chilli for the dressing.
  4. Separate the lettuce into cups.
  5. Fill each with rice, chicken, cucumber, mint and coriander, spoon over the dressing and finish with peanuts.

Silent soft pretzelsVegetarian

Soft pretzels with warm cheddar sauce

Prep 10 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese dip up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; warm the pretzels just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 soft pretzels
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp rock salt
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  1. Brush the pretzels with butter and sprinkle with salt.
  2. Warm at 200°C.
  3. Heat the evaporated milk and stir in the cheddar until smooth.
  4. Serve warm, and dip quietly.

Corn silo cornbread bitesOn the printed menu

Mini muffins with sweet kernels and cheddar, split warm with whipped honey butter

How it is made
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 140g butter
  • 150g corn kernels
  • 60g cheddar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Mini cornbread muffins with sweet corn kernels and cheddar, baked golden and split warm with whipped honey butter. Mix 150g fine cornmeal, 100g plain flour, 2 tsp baking powder, half a tsp salt and 1 tbsp sugar. Whisk in 300ml buttermilk, 1 egg, 60g melted butter, 150g corn kernels and 60g grated cheddar until just combined. Spoon into a buttered 24-hole mini muffin tin and bake at 200°C for 12 to 14 minutes. Beat 80g soft butter with 2 tbsp honey and serve with the warm bites.

Mains

Foil baked trout with lemon and dill

Whole trout stuffed with lemon, dill and garlic, baked in foil

Prep 16 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Stuff and wrap the trout in foil up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bake and boil the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 whole trout
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 2 tbsp dill
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 70g butter
  • 800g new potatoes
  1. Stuff the trout with lemon slices, dill and garlic.
  2. Dot with some of the butter and wrap each in foil.
  3. Bake at 200°C and open at the table.
  4. Boil the potatoes until tender.
  5. Toss with the remaining butter.

Slow-cooked farmhouse beefOn the printed menu

Beef chuck braised for hours in red wine with buttery mash, a pot that cooks without a sound

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 150ml milk
  1. Beef chuck braised for hours in red wine and stock until it falls apart, with soft buttery mash so nothing at the table makes a crunch. Toss 1.2kg diced beef chuck with 2 tbsp flour and brown in batches. Soften 1 onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery sticks for 8 minutes, add 2 tbsp tomato paste, 250ml red wine, 500ml beef stock, thyme and bay. Return the beef, cover and cook at 160°C for 3 hours. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash very smooth with 80g butter and 150ml warm milk. Spoon the beef over the mash.

Soft vanilla custardVegetarian

Pears poached in sugar syrup under soft vanilla custard

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 pears, peeled and halved
  • 100g caster sugar, for syrup
  • 500ml water, for syrup
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 50g caster sugar, for the custard
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Dissolve the sugar for the syrup in the water, add the pears and poach until tender.
  2. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour.
  3. Heat the milk and cream and pour over the yolk mix, then stir over low heat until thick.
  4. Add the vanilla.
  5. Pour into cups and chill.
  6. Top with the sliced pears.

Drinks

Waterfall cucumber and mint spritzVegetarianNo alcohol

Cucumber ribbons, mint, lime and elderflower with sparkling water, no alcohol

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Mix the mint, lime juice and cordial up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add the cucumber, sparkling water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 60ml elderflower cordial
  • 1l sparkling water, chilled
  • 3 cups crushed ice
  1. Shave the cucumber into ribbons with a peeler.
  2. Lightly crush the mint with the lime juice and cordial in a jug.
  3. Add the cucumber, then pour in the sparkling water slowly.
  4. Fill glasses with crushed ice rather than cubes so they stay quiet, pour over and top with a mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the family eats dinner off leaves instead of plates.
