Snacks

Willow's magic acorn bitesVegetarian

Bocconcini rolled in hazelnuts and chives to look like acorns, Willow's magic acorn threat.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g hazelnuts
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 250g bocconcini
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Toast the hazelnuts at 180°C, rub off the skins and chop finely. Mix with the chives, 12 min.
  2. Pat the bocconcini dry and roll each ball in the hazelnut mix so it looks like a little acorn.
  3. Arrange on a plate and drizzle with a little honey.

Nelwyn harvest breadVegetarian

Warm homemade rolls served with parsley and chive butter.

Prep 27 min, cook 18 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 20g butter, soft, for the dough
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100g butter, soft, for serving
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 200g brie
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, the butter for the dough and warm water to a dough and knead until smooth.
  2. Prove the dough for an hour until doubled.
  3. Shape into rolls and bake at 200°C until golden.
  4. Mash the butter for serving with the parsley and chives. Serve the warm rolls with it and a wedge of brie, 12 min.

Tavern brawl beef and root veg stew

Beef chuck, carrots and parsnips simmered 3 hours in tomato beef stock.

Prep 23 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 3 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 2 parsnips, cut into chunks
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 L beef stock
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 50g butter
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches, 15 min.
  2. Add the onion, carrots and parsnips and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the flour and tomato paste, then the stock, thyme and bay leaf.
  4. Cover and simmer for 3 hours until the beef is tender and the sauce is rich.
  5. Serve in bowls with crusty bread and butter.

Bavmorda's pig spell crackling pork

Fennel-rubbed pork shoulder with shattering crackling.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 jar fennel seeds
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 apples
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Pork shoulder scored and rubbed with salt and fennel seed, roasted slow until tender, the crackling blasted until it shatters, with roast apples and potatoes. Score the skin of a 2kg pork shoulder, rub with salt and 2 tsp crushed fennel seeds and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Roast at 160°C for 4 hours. Add 1kg potatoes and 4 halved apples tossed in oil for the last hour. Turn up to 240°C for 25 minutes until the crackling blisters. Rest 15 minutes and carve.

Nockmaar castle blackberry tartVegetarian

Blackberries pressed into almond cream in a sweet pastry shell.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep cool
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g almond meal
  • 300g frozen blackberries
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 300ml thick cream
  1. Line a 23cm tart tin with the pastry and chill for 20 minutes.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, then the eggs and almond meal. Spread in the case and press in the blackberries.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 40 minutes until golden and set.
  4. Cool, dust with icing sugar and serve with thick cream, 15 min.

Franjean and Rool's berry tartlets

Vanilla cream and berry tartlets, brownie sized.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 300g mixed berries
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 bag icing sugar
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  1. Tiny crisp pastry cases filled with vanilla whipped cream and piled with mixed berries tossed in sugar and lemon zest, finished with icing sugar. Cut 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust into 24 rounds and press into a mini muffin tray. Prick and bake at 190°C for 12 minutes until golden, then cool. Toss 300g mixed berries with 1 tbsp caster sugar and the zest of a lemon. Whip 200ml cream with 1 tbsp icing sugar and vanilla. Spoon cream into the cases, pile on the berries and dust with icing sugar.

Nelwyn warm spiced apple punchNo alcohol

Warm apple juice with cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and honey.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make fresh, keep warm on low heat
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5 L cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 orange, peeled into strips
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 apple, thinly sliced
  1. Pour the apple juice into a pot with the cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and honey.
  2. Warm gently for 15 minutes without boiling so the spices come through. Strain out the cloves.
  3. Ladle into mugs and float a thin apple slice in each.
