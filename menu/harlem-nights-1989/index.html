Snacks

Vera's supper club devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs, Dijon, hot sauce, paprika. Quick swears he'll shoot Vera's pinky toe.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • smoked paprika, to dust
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Boil the eggs, cool, peel and halve.
  2. Whip the yolks with mayonnaise, Dijon and hot sauce.
  3. Pipe back into the whites.
  4. Finish with smoked paprika and chives and lay them on silver trays.

Vault hush puppiesVegetarian

Cornmeal balls fried golden with honey butter, enough to last a few days in the vault.

Prep 2 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Beat the honey butter up to 3 days ahead; mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead in the fridge and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 60g soft butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, buttermilk, egg and grated onion.
  2. Spoon into 175°C oil and fry in batches until golden.
  3. Beat the soft butter with honey to serve.

Mains

Quick's late-night chicken and waffles

Buttermilk fried chicken thighs on waffles with maple syrup and hot butter.

Prep 2.5 hours, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Brine the chicken a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 700ml buttermilk, divided
  • 500g plain flour, divided
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g butter, divided
  • 100ml maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • salt, to taste
  1. Brine the chicken in some of the buttermilk.
  2. Brine for at least 2 hours.
  3. Whisk flour, baking powder, eggs, buttermilk and melted butter into a waffle batter.
  4. Dredge the chicken in seasoned flour.
  5. Fry in the vegetable oil at 165°C until the centre reaches 74°C.
  6. Cook the waffles in a waffle iron.
  7. Serve with maple syrup and butter melted with hot sauce.

Smothered pork chops in onion gravy

Floured, browned and simmered tender in dark roux, rice and greens, from the club kitchen.

How it is made
  • 4 thick pork chops
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 40g butter
  • 2 brown onions
  • 600ml chicken stock
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 bunch kale
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Thick pork chops floured and browned, then simmered in a dark roux onion gravy until tender, served over white rice with garlicky braised greens. Season 4 thick pork chops, dust in flour and brown in oil for 3 minutes a side, then set aside. Melt 40g butter in the pan, stir in 3 tbsp flour and cook to a dark brown roux. Add 2 sliced onions and cook 5 minutes, then whisk in 600ml chicken stock. Return the chops, cover and simmer 40 minutes. Serve over 300g rice with a bunch of kale wilted with garlic.

Bag of sugar lemon chess pieVegetarian

Sweet lemon custard pie with a little cornmeal in a shortcrust shell.

Prep 0 min, cook 60 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 110g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp cornmeal
  • 3 lemons
  • 60ml milk
  1. Line a 23cm tin with pastry and blind bake until pale golden.
  2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, melted butter, cornmeal, lemon juice, zest and milk.
  3. Pour into the case and bake at 170°C until just set.
  4. Cool completely.

Drinks

Fight night champagne cocktail

Bitters sugar cube, sparkling wine, lemon.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best built to order; soak the sugar cubes in the bitters up to 1 day ahead, covered
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 1 tsp aromatic bitters
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 1 lemon
  1. Drop a sugar cube soaked in a few dashes of bitters into each of four flutes.
  2. Top slowly with chilled sparkling wine.
  3. Twist a strip of lemon peel over each.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Jack Jenkins steps into the ring against Kirkpatrick.
