Snacks

PanelleVegetarian

Chickpea fritters, salt and lemon, for the trip back to Sicily.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Set up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g chickpea flour
  • 750ml cold water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 2 lemons, cut in wedges
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Whisk the chickpea flour into the cold water with the salt until smooth.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until very thick.
  3. Stir in the parsley and spread 1cm thick on an oiled tray. Leave to set for 1 hour.
  4. Cut into squares and fry in 180°C oil in batches until puffed and golden.
  5. Drain, season with the flaky salt and serve with lemon wedges.

Prosciutto e melone

A starter before Don Altobello's birthday cannoli.

Prep 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 rockmelon
  • 150g prosciutto
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Leave the rockmelon at room temperature for an hour.
  2. Halve, deseed, cut into 12 long wedges and cut off the skin.
  3. Drape each wedge with prosciutto, add pepper and a drizzle of olive oil just before serving.

Caponata crostini

Sweet and sour Sicilian eggplant on toast.

How it is made
  • eggplants
  • celery
  • capers
  • green olives
  • red wine vinegar
  • baguette
  1. Fry cubed eggplant until golden, then soften celery and onion. Add tomato, capers, green olives, a spoon of sugar and red wine vinegar, simmer 10 minutes, fold in the eggplant. Cool, spoon onto toasted baguette with basil.

Mains

Pasta alla NormaVegetarian

Eggplant, tomato and ricotta salata, straight from Sicily.

Prep 8 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days ahead; fry eggplant on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggplants
  • 120ml olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 800g San Marzano tomatoes, tinned
  • 1 handful basil
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 100g ricotta salata
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the eggplants into thick half moons and fry in batches in most of the olive oil until deep gold. Drain.
  2. Fry the garlic in 2 tbsp of oil, add the tomatoes and basil, simmer and season.
  3. Cook the rigatoni until al dente.
  4. Toss the pasta with the sauce, eggplant and a splash of pasta water. Grate ricotta salata heavily over the top.

Mary and Vincent's gnocchi

Hand-rolled in the kitchen, far too close for cousins.

How it is made
  • floury potatoes
  • 00 flour
  • eggs
  • butter
  • sage
  • parmesan
  1. Bake 1kg floury potatoes, rice while hot, cool slightly, work in 1 yolk, salt and about 250g 00 flour until just smooth. Roll ropes, cut, cook until they float. Burnt butter with sage until nutty, parmesan.

Sweets

CannoliVegetarian

Ricotta and candied orange, eaten carefully in the opera box.

Prep 20 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead; fill shells just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fresh ricotta
  • 80g icing sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 40g candied orange peel, finely chopped
  • 8 cannoli shells
  • 30g pistachios, chopped
  1. Drain the ricotta in a sieve for 2 hours.
  2. Beat with icing sugar, cinnamon and candied peel.
  3. Pipe into the shells just before serving.
  4. Dip each end in pistachios and dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Limoncello e spumante

Limoncello, prosecco and soda over ice, lemon peel, just when he thought he was out.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Chill the limoncello, prosecco and soda up to 1 day ahead; build over ice just before serving so it stays fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml limoncello
  • 360ml prosecco, chilled
  • 120ml soda water
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four large wine glasses with ice.
  2. Pour limoncello into each, top with prosecco and soda and stir once gently.
  3. Peel a wide strip of lemon zest per glass, twist over the drink and drop in. Finish with mint.

When to serve

Serve the cannoli when Michael is made a papal knight at the cathedral.
