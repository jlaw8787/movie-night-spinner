Snacks

Light cycle pretzel rodsVegetarian

Pretzel rods dipped in white chocolate and piped with thin blue frosting lines.

Prep 16 min, cook 6 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 100g icing sugar
  • blue food colouring, a few drops
  1. Line a tray with baking paper. Melt the chocolate, 6 min.
  2. Dip the pretzel rods two-thirds of the way and set on the paper, 8 min.
  3. Mix the icing sugar with a little water and blue colouring and pipe thin straight lines along each rod.
  4. Let set, 30 min.

End of Line Club canapés

Dark rye squares with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, dill and lemon.

Prep 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 loaf dark rye bread
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Cut the rye into neat squares and pick the dill into sprigs.
  2. Spread with crème fraîche.
  3. Top with a fold of salmon, a sprig of dill and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Lay out in sharp rows on black slate.

Mains

Grid neon noodles

Stir fried chicken thigh, red bell pepper, broccoli and egg noodles.

Prep 11 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Slice the chicken, capsicum and broccoli up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; cook the noodles and stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 500g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slice the capsicum and cut the broccoli into florets.
  2. Cook the noodles and drain, 6 min.
  3. Stir-fry the chicken in the oil in a hot wok until browned, 8 min.
  4. Add the capsicum and broccoli and cook, 3 min.
  5. Toss in the noodles with soy and honey until sticky and glossy.

Flynn's hideout lemon roast chicken

Whole chicken with garlic thyme butter under the skin, roasted, with potatoes and greens.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 bunches green beans
  1. Chicken roasted with lemon, garlic and thyme at 200°C for 75 minutes, carved with roast potatoes and greens, served on white plates. Mix 40g soft butter with 2 crushed garlic cloves, lemon zest and thyme and rub over and under the skin of a 1.8kg chicken. Stuff with the halved lemon. Roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potatoes, rough them up and roast in oil for 50 minutes alongside. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Steam 2 bunches green beans and toss in butter. Carve and serve on white plates with the pan juices.

Sweets

Grid neon cookiesVegetarian

Butter cookies cut into squares and iced.

Prep 28 min, cook 12 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 100g icing sugar, for the dough
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 300g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar, for the icing
  • neon food colouring, a few drops
  1. Beat the butter and the icing sugar for the dough, add the yolk, then the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into squares.
  3. Bake at 170°C, 12 min.
  4. Cool, 20 min.
  5. Mix the icing sugar for the icing with water and colouring and ice in straight lines.

Flynn's zen iced matcha with honeyNo alcohol

Matcha whisked frothy with honey, poured over ice and milk.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Whisk the matcha with the hot water and honey up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; fill glasses with ice and milk and pour the matcha over to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp matcha powder
  • 200ml hot water, about 80°C
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Sift the matcha into a bowl.
  2. Whisk with the water and honey until smooth and frothy.
  3. Fill four glasses with ice and add milk to each.
  4. Slowly pour the matcha over the top to form a green layer. Stir before drinking.
