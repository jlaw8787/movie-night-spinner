Snacks

Light cycle pretzel rodsVegetarian

Pretzel rods dipped in white chocolate and piped with thin blue icing lines

Prep 22 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 100g icing sugar
  • blue food colouring, a few drops
  1. Line a tray with baking paper. Melt the chocolate.
  2. Dip the pretzel rods two-thirds of the way and set on the paper.
  3. Mix the icing sugar with a little water and blue colouring and pipe thin straight lines along each rod.
  4. Let set.

End of Line Club canapés

Dark rye squares with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, dill and lemon

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 loaf dark rye bread
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Cut the rye into neat squares and pick the dill into sprigs.
  2. Spread with crème fraîche.
  3. Top with a fold of salmon, a sprig of dill and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Lay out in sharp rows on black slate.

Mains

Grid neon noodles

Stir fried chicken thigh, red capsicum, broccoli and egg noodles

Prep 8 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Slice the chicken, capsicum and broccoli up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; cook the noodles and stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 500g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slice the capsicum and cut the broccoli into florets.
  2. Cook the noodles and drain.
  3. Stir-fry the chicken in the oil in a hot wok until browned.
  4. Add the capsicum and broccoli and cook.
  5. Toss in the noodles with soy and honey until sticky and glossy.

Sweets

End of Line Club neon cookiesVegetarian

Butter cookies cut into squares and iced

Prep 28 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 100g icing sugar, for the dough
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 300g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar, for the icing
  • neon food colouring, a few drops
  1. Beat the butter and the icing sugar for the dough, add the yolk, then the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into squares.
  3. Bake at 170C.
  4. Cool.
  5. Mix the icing sugar for the icing with water and colouring and ice in straight lines.

Drinks

Flynn's zen iced matcha with honeyVegetarianNo alcohol

Whisked matcha with honey, over ice

Prep 8 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Whisk the matcha with the hot water and honey up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; fill glasses with ice and milk and pour the matcha over to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp matcha powder
  • 200ml hot water, about 80C
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Sift the matcha into a bowl.
  2. Whisk with the water and honey until smooth and frothy.
  3. Fill four glasses with ice and add milk to each.
  4. Slowly pour the matcha over the top to form a green layer. Stir before drinking.
