Scarlet A pita crispsVegetarian

Sumac pita chips cut as As, with a beet and yoghurt dip.

Prep 7 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 2 days, crisps up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp sumac
  • 2 beetroots
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 200g Greek yoghurt, plus extra to swirl
  1. Roast the whole beetroot at 200°C until tender, then cool and peel, 30 min.
  2. Cut the pita into A shapes, brush with oil and dust with sumac. Bake at 200°C until crisp, 8 min.
  3. Blend the beetroot with the garlic and yoghurt. Swirl a little extra yoghurt through the top and serve with the crisps.

Ojai citrus olivesVegetarian

Warm green and kalamata olives with citrus peel, garlic and fennel seed.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled and warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g green olives
  • 150g kalamata olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 orange, peeled into strips
  • 1 lemon, peeled into strips
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  1. Slice the garlic and peel the orange and lemon into strips.
  2. Warm the olives gently in the oil with the peel, garlic, chilli and fennel seeds. Tip into a bowl and serve warm with toothpicks, 5 min.

Woodchuck soft pretzel bites

Dough rounds dipped in baking soda water, rock salted and baked dark, warm with honey mustard.

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  1. Soft pretzel bites dipped in bicarb water, sprinkled with rock salt and baked deep brown, served warm with a honey mustard dip. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Roll into ropes and cut into 3cm bites. Boil 2L water with 60g bicarb soda and dip the bites for 30 seconds. Sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Mix 3 tbsp Dijon, 2 tbsp honey and 2 tbsp mayonnaise for the dip.

Penderghast takeout pad thai

Rice noodles with tofu, egg, tamarind and fish sauce, topped with peanuts and sprouts.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g flat rice noodles
  • 200g firm tofu, cubed
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 150g bean sprouts
  • 60g roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  1. Soak the noodles in warm water until pliable, then drain, 10 min.
  2. Fry the tofu and garlic in the oil, push aside and scramble the eggs, 6 min.
  3. Add the noodles, tamarind, fish sauce and brown sugar and toss until the noodles are tender, 6 min.
  4. Finish with bean sprouts, peanuts and a squeeze of lime.

California chilli-lime fish tacos

Cumin cod fried crisp in corn tortillas, slaw, crema, Pocketful of Sunshine on repeat.

How it is made
  • 600g cod or flathead fillets
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 limes
  • 0.25 red cabbage
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Cod strips dusted in chilli and cumin, pan-fried crisp, in corn tortillas with cabbage slaw, lime crema, sliced radish and coriander. Cut 600g cod fillets into strips and toss in 2 tbsp flour, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin and salt. Mix 150g sour cream with the zest and juice of a lime. Toss a quarter red cabbage, shredded, with lime juice and salt. Pan-fry the fish in hot oil for 2 minutes a side. Warm 12 corn tortillas and fill with slaw, fish, crema, sliced radish and coriander.

Red velvet whoopie piesVegetarian

Red velvet cake rounds sandwiched with cream cheese filling.

Prep 28 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, filled the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 250g cream cheese, softened
  • 150g icing sugar
  1. Whisk the flour, cocoa, bicarb soda and caster sugar, then the egg, oil, buttermilk and colouring into a thick batter.
  2. Pipe rounds onto lined trays and bake at 180°C. Cool completely, 12 min.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar until smooth.
  4. Sandwich the cooled rounds with the filling.

Ojai strawberry lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh strawberry lemonade with sparkling water over ice.

Prep 7 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, mix fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 120ml hot water
  • 300g strawberries, hulled
  • 6 lemons, juiced
  • 1 L cold sparkling water
  • 1 handful mint leaves
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool the syrup, 15 min.
  2. Blitz most of the strawberries with the syrup and strain into a jug.
  3. Add the lemon juice and sparkling water and stir.
  4. Fill tall glasses with ice, sliced strawberries and mint, then pour in the lemonade.
