Snacks

Garlic prawn skewers

King prawns in garlic, chilli and lemon, grilled hot on skewers over the coals

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Marinate and skewer the prawns up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g king prawns, peeled
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 chilli
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp lemon myrtle
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  1. Soak the skewers. Toss the prawns with crushed garlic, chopped chilli, zest of one lemon and the oil.
  2. Thread onto skewers and marinate in the fridge.
  3. Grill hot until just cooked, turning once.
  4. Melt the butter with the lemon myrtle and brush over. Serve with lemon wedges.

Street cart hot dogs with sauerkraut

Frankfurts in soft rolls with mustard, sauerkraut and fried onion, off the cart.

Prep 5 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Fry the onions up to 1 day ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 soft hot dog rolls
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 200g sauerkraut
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Fry the sliced onions slowly until soft and golden.
  2. Simmer or grill the frankfurts.
  3. Warm the rolls and fill with frankfurt, onion, sauerkraut and mustard.

Walkabout Creek barbecued barra

Crisp-skin barra, grilled. Stay clear of the billabong.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; score the skin and pat dry up to 4 hours ahead, uncovered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 barramundi fillets, skin on
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 80g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp lemon myrtle
  • 2 lemons
  1. Score the skin of the barramundi fillets, pat very dry and brush with the oil.
  2. Grill skin down over high heat for about 4 minutes until crisp, then flip for 1 minute.
  3. Melt the butter with the garlic and lemon myrtle, spoon over the fish and serve with the lemons cut into wedges.

New York baked cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked cheesecake on a biscuit base, with a warm berry sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 71 min, then 3.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before; it needs at least 3 hours in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits
  • 100g butter
  • 750g cream cheese
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 lemon
  • 300g frozen mixed berries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for sauce
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the melted butter and press into the tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar, then the eggs, sour cream, vanilla and lemon juice.
  3. Pour onto the base and bake at 160°C until set with a slight wobble.
  4. Turn the oven off and cool the cake inside.
  5. Chill until firm.
  6. Simmer the berries with the sugar and spoon over each slice.

Drinks

Walkabout Creek rum and ginger beerVegetarian

Dark rum and ginger beer over ice

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Best built to order; squeeze the lime juice up to 1 day ahead and keep the ginger beer chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tray ice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 600ml ginger beer, chilled
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 4 lime wedges
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and add lime juice to each.
  2. Add the ginger beer to each glass.
  3. Slowly pour the rum over the back of a spoon so it floats on top.
  4. Add a lime wedge and stir at the table.
