Snacks

Garlic prawn skewers

King prawns in garlic, chilli and lemon, grilled hot on skewers over the coals.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min, 25 min waiting · Make ahead: Marinate and skewer the prawns up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g king prawns, peeled
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 chilli
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 50g butter
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  1. Soak the skewers. Toss the prawns with crushed garlic, chopped chilli, zest of one lemon and the oil, 10 min.
  2. Thread onto skewers and marinate in the fridge, 15 min.
  3. Grill hot until just cooked, turning once, 4 min.
  4. Melt the butter with grated lemon zest and brush over. Serve with lemon wedges, 4 min.

Crumbed calamari rings

Squid rings in golden breadcrumbs, fried crisp, with lemon wedges and tartare.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Crumb the rings up to 4 hours ahead and keep them in the fridge on a tray
Recipe for 4
  • 500g squid tubes
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  • 150g tartare sauce
  1. Cut the squid tubes into 1cm rings and pat dry.
  2. Dust in flour, dip in egg and press into the breadcrumbs.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry the rings in batches until golden, 10 min.
  4. Drain, salt and serve with lemon wedges and tartare.

Pork crackling

Scored belly skin dried overnight, salted hard and roasted until it puffs, cut into shards.

How it is made
  • pork belly
  • sea salt
  • fennel seeds
  1. Score pork belly skin, dry uncovered in the fridge overnight, salt heavily, roast at 240°C for 30 minutes then 180°C for 40 until it puffs and crackles. Cut into shards.

Mains

Walkabout Creek barbecued barra

Barramundi fillets grilled skin side down until crisp, with lemon.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; score the skin and pat dry up to 4 hours ahead, uncovered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 barramundi fillets, skin on
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 80g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 lemons
  1. Score the skin of the barramundi fillets, pat very dry and brush with the oil.
  2. Grill skin down over high heat for about 4 minutes until crisp, then flip for 1 minute.
  3. Melt the butter with the garlic and a squeeze of lemon, spoon over the fish and serve with the lemons cut into wedges, 5 min.

Sweets

Apple crumble with custardVegetarian

Stewed Granny Smiths under a buttery oat crumble, hot custard poured over.

Prep 30 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Stew the apples and rub the crumble up to 1 day ahead; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 Granny Smith apples
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g cold butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 600ml custard
  1. Peel, core and slice the apples.
  2. Stew the apples with the sugar, cinnamon and a splash of water, 10 min.
  3. Rub the butter into the flour, stir in the oats and brown sugar and scatter over the apples, 8 min.
  4. Bake at 180°C until golden, then serve hot with warm custard, 30 min.

Drinks

Walkabout Creek cold pot

Ice-cold lager pulled into a frosted pot glass on a beer coaster, outback pub style.

Prep 3 min · Make ahead: Freeze the glasses 30 minutes ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles of lager (375 ml each), cold
  1. Put 4 pot glasses in the freezer for 30 minutes.
  2. Pour the lager slowly down the side of each glass, straightening up for a two finger head.

When to serve

Pour a cold pot when Mick first sees a bidet in the hotel.
