Snacks

Garlic prawn skewers

Grilled garlic chilli prawns

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Marinate and skewer the prawns up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g king prawns, peeled
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 chilli
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp lemon myrtle
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  1. Soak the skewers. Toss the prawns with crushed garlic, chopped chilli, zest of one lemon and the oil.
  2. Thread onto skewers and marinate in the fridge.
  3. Grill hot until just cooked, turning once.
  4. Melt the butter with the lemon myrtle and brush over. Serve with lemon wedges.

Wally's Walkabout Creek party pies

Seasoned minced beef in gravy, shortcrust base and puff pastry lid

Prep 35 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; assemble and bake on the day, or bake up to 1 day ahead and reheat at 180C
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  1. Brown the mince with the diced onion, then add Worcestershire, tomato sauce and stock. Thicken with cornflour mixed with a little water.
  2. Cool the filling completely.
  3. Line patty tins with shortcrust, fill, and top with puff lids.
  4. Bake at 200C until golden.

Mains

Walkabout Creek barbecued barra

Crisp-skin barra. Bring a big knife.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; score the skin and pat dry up to 4 hours ahead, uncovered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 barramundi fillets, skin on
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 80g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp lemon myrtle
  • 2 lemons
  1. Score the skin of the barramundi fillets, pat very dry and brush with the oil.
  2. Grill skin down over high heat for about 4 minutes until crisp, then flip for 1 minute.
  3. Melt the butter with the garlic and lemon myrtle, spoon over the fish and serve with the lemons cut into wedges.

Sweets

LamingtonsVegetarian

Chocolate and coconut sponge squares

Prep 38 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g butter sponge
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 40g cocoa
  • 20g butter, melted
  • 150ml milk
  • 200g desiccated coconut
  1. Cut the sponge into squares.
  2. Whisk the icing sugar, cocoa, butter and milk into a thin icing.
  3. Dip each square, drain and roll in coconut.
  4. Set on a rack.

Drinks

Walkabout Creek rum and ginger beerVegetarian

Dark rum and ginger beer over ice

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Best built to order; squeeze the lime juice up to 1 day ahead and keep the ginger beer chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tray ice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 600ml ginger beer, chilled
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 4 lime wedges
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and add lime juice to each.
  2. Add the ginger beer to each glass.
  3. Slowly pour the rum over the back of a spoon so it floats on top.
  4. Add a lime wedge and stir at the table.
