Snacks

Buttermilk sconesVegetarian

Buttermilk scones with rubbed in butter, baked hot.

Prep 11 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Same day, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 100g cold butter
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 50g butter, to serve
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub the cold butter into the flour and salt, 8 min.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk until just combined.
  3. Pat out, cut into rounds and place on a tray.
  4. Bake, 14 min.
  5. Serve warm with butter and honey.

Berries and whipped cream cupsVegetarian

Blackberries and blueberries with honey and mint over whipped cream.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; toss the berries and spoon the cups together just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g blackberries
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 sprig mint
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  2. Toss the blackberries and blueberries with honey and torn mint.
  3. Spoon cream into small cups and top with the berries.

Amos Slade's peppered beef jerky

Thin topside in soy, Worcestershire, garlic and pepper, dried until chewy, for the game reserve.

How it is made
  • 800g beef topside
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar cracked black pepper
  1. Beef topside sliced thin, marinated overnight in soy, Worcestershire, pepper and garlic, dried at 75°C for 4 hours until chewy. Freeze 800g beef topside for 1 hour so it slices easily, then cut across the grain into 3mm strips, trimming off fat. Mix 80ml soy, 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 2 crushed garlic cloves and 1 tbsp cracked black pepper. Marinate the beef overnight in the fridge. Pat dry, lay on wire racks over trays and dry at 75°C for 4 hours with the oven door slightly open until chewy. Cool before serving.

Farmhouse chicken and dumplings

Chicken thighs simmered with carrot and celery, parsley dumplings.

Prep 18 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Simmer the chicken and vegetables up to 2 days ahead and keep in the fridge; reheat, then mix and cook the dumplings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery stalks
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1.5 L water
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Slice the carrots, celery and onion.
  2. Simmer the chicken with the vegetables in the water, 40 min.
  3. Rub the butter into the flour, add parsley and enough water for a soft dough.
  4. Drop spoonfuls onto the stew, cover and cook, 20 min.

Country beef pot roast and gravy

Chuck braised three hours with onion, carrots and potatoes, juices spooned over, Big Mama approved.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 onions
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 carrots
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 box cornflour
  1. Beef chuck braised at 160°C for 3 hours with onion, carrots and potatoes in stock, gravy from the pot. Season a 1.5kg beef chuck roast and brown all over in a heavy pot. Add 2 quartered onions, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp tomato paste, a few thyme sprigs and 500ml beef stock. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours. Add 4 carrots and 800g potatoes in big chunks and braise 1 hour more. Lift out the meat and veg, simmer the liquid with 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with water until it thickens to gravy, and pour over the sliced beef.

Blackberry cobblerVegetarian

Blackberries under a drop scone topping.

Prep 13 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep covered; reheat at 160°C for 15 minutes and serve with cold ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 500g blackberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 80g butter
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Toss the blackberries with sugar and spread in a dish, 5 min.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, then add the milk to make a soft batter.
  3. Drop rough spoonfuls over the fruit.
  4. Bake, 40 min.
  5. Serve warm with ice cream.

Strawberry farm milkshakeNo alcohol

Strawberries, vanilla ice cream, milk and honey blended frothy.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Hull the strawberries up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; blend to order so it stays frothy
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries, hulled, for the shake
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 strawberries, for garnish
  1. Hull the strawberries.
  2. Blend with the milk, ice cream, honey and vanilla until frothy.
  3. Pour into four glass jars and top each with a halved strawberry.
