Rey's instant breadVegetarian

Quick soda bread with a deep cross on top, torn apart warm

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 450g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 50g butter, to serve
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Mix the flour, bicarb and salt. Stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough.
  2. Shape into a round, cut a deep cross on top and bake at 200°C until risen and hollow sounding.
  3. Tear warm and serve with butter and honey.

Niima Outpost datesVegetarian

Medjool dates filled with goat cheese and walnut, with honey, apricots and figs

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep cool
Recipe for 4
  • 16 medjool dates
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 16 walnut halves
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 100g dried apricots
  • 100g dried figs
  1. Slit the dates and remove the stones.
  2. Fill each with a little goat's cheese and press a walnut half on top.
  3. Drizzle with honey and arrange on a board with the dried apricots and figs.

BB-8 saffron aranciniOn the printed menu

Saffron risotto balls with molten mozzarella, fried deep orange like BB-8 rolling over the sand

How it is made
  • 1 packet saffron
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 1 brown onion
  • 30g butter
  • 250g arborio rice
  • 40g parmesan
  • 125g mozzarella block
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 2L vegetable oil
  1. Saffron risotto balls with a melting mozzarella centre, crumbed and fried deep orange, with a smoky paprika aioli for the white stripe. Soak a pinch of saffron in 750ml hot stock. Soften an onion in butter, add 250g arborio and stir in the stock a ladle at a time for 18 minutes. Stir in 40g parmesan and spread out to cool. Shape 16 balls around a cube of mozzarella. Roll in flour, beaten egg and panko and fry at 180°C for 4 minutes. Mix 4 tbsp mayonnaise, a crushed garlic clove and smoked paprika to dip.

Maz Kanata's slow-roast lamb feast

Garlic rosemary lamb shoulder roasted 5 hours, flatbreads, garlic yoghurt

Prep 10 min, cook 5 hours · Make ahead: Roast a day ahead, shred and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed, for the yoghurt
  • 600g mixed vegetables for roasting (pumpkin, zucchini, red onion)
  1. Rub the lamb with the garlic, rosemary, paprika, oil and salt.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C for 5 hours until it pulls apart easily.
  3. Roast the vegetables alongside for the last 40 minutes. Stir the garlic into the yoghurt and warm the flatbreads to serve with the lamb.

Starkiller Base barramundi parcelsOn the printed menu

Fish baked in paper with lemon, dill, capers and butter, opened at the table, fennel slaw

How it is made
  • 4 barramundi fillets
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 jar capers
  • 60g butter
  • 1 roll baking paper
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 fennel bulb
  1. Barramundi baked in paper parcels with lemon, dill, capers and butter, torn open at the table, with crushed potatoes and a sharp fennel slaw. Lay 4 barramundi fillets on squares of baking paper. Top each with lemon slices, dill, 1 tsp capers and 15g butter, season and fold into sealed parcels. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes. Boil 800g baby potatoes, crush with olive oil and salt. Shave a fennel bulb thin and toss with lemon juice, olive oil and dill. Open the parcels at the table.

Ahch-To island sconesVegetarian

Light cream and lemonade scones served warm with strawberry jam

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best warm, same day
Recipe for 4
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml thickened cream, for the dough
  • 300ml lemonade
  • 4 tbsp strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped, to serve
  1. Mix the flour, cream and lemonade until just combined.
  2. Pat out on a floured bench, cut into rounds and bake close together at 220°C until risen and golden.
  3. Serve warm with strawberry jam and whipped cream.

Millennium Falcon spiced mochaVegetarianNo alcohol

Coffee with hot cocoa milk, brown sugar and cinnamon

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml very strong coffee, or 4 espresso shots
  • 700ml milk
  • 3 tbsp cocoa, plus extra to dust
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Brew the coffee.
  2. Warm the milk with the cocoa, sugar and cinnamon, whisking until frothy, without boiling.
  3. Divide the coffee between four metal mugs and pour in the hot chocolate milk. Top with whipped cream and dust with cocoa.
