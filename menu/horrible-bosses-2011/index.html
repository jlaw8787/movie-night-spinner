Snacks

Murder consultant loaded beef nachos

Corn chips with spiced beef, black beans, cheddar, guacamole and salsa

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the beef and bean mix up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat, layer, bake and top on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 300g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 lime
  • 150g sour cream
  • 200g tomato salsa
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  1. Brown the mince with the onion. Add the ground cumin, smoked paprika, chilli powder, tomato paste and a splash of water and simmer. Stir in the black beans.
  2. Layer the corn chips in a baking dish with the beef and cheddar.
  3. Bake at 200C until the cheese melts.
  4. Mash the avocados with lime and salt.
  5. Top with guacamole, sour cream, salsa and jalapenos.

Dive bar buffalo chicken wings

Baked chicken wings tossed in hot sauce butter, with blue cheese dip and celery

Prep 14 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings, jointed
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 50g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Heat the oven to 230C. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack over a tray for 45 minutes, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream with a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Serve with the dip and celery sticks.

Mains

Swap your boss double bacon cheeseburgers

Double beef patties, bacon and special sauce, with oven fries

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake the fries and cook the bacon and patties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen fries
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill pickles, plus slices to serve
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 milk buns
  • 1 onion, sliced
  1. Bake the fries at 220C until crisp.
  2. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, mustard and chopped pickles into a sauce.
  4. Roll the mince into balls and smash each flat onto a very hot greased pan. Season and cook, flip, top with the sliced cheddar and cook briefly.
  5. Stack two patties in each toasted bun with bacon, pickles, onion and sauce.

Sweets

Break room cinnamon jam doughnutsVegetarian

Yeast doughnuts in cinnamon sugar, filled with raspberry jam

Prep 40 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Best eaten the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 140g caster sugar
  • 150ml warm milk
  • 1 egg
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 250g raspberry jam, warmed
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, some of the sugar and a pinch of salt. Add the milk, egg and butter and knead.
  2. Prove until doubled.
  3. Roll into balls and prove again.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170C, turning once, then drain.
  5. Toss in the remaining sugar mixed with cinnamon.
  6. Poke a hole in each and pipe in the jam.

Bad plan salted caramel browniesVegetarian

Dark chocolate salted caramel brownies. Keep an EpiPen handy.

Prep 20 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead, airtight; warm briefly in the oven before serving with ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 6 tbsp thick caramel sauce
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1l vanilla ice cream, to serve
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter. Whisk in the sugar, then the eggs one at a time. Fold in the flour, cocoa and a pinch of salt.
  2. Spread into a lined 20cm square tin. Drop the caramel over the top and swirl with a knife. Sprinkle with sea salt.
  3. Bake at 170C until just set.
  4. Cool, then serve warm with ice cream.

Drinks

Glengoolie Blue whisky sourVegetarian

Scotch, lemon, honey syrup and egg white shaken foamy

Prep 10 min, cook 1 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep chilled; juice the lemons the day before and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 240ml scotch whisky
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 4 egg whites (from 4 eggs)
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the honey into hot water and cool to make a syrup.
  2. Shake the whisky, lemon juice, honey syrup and egg white in a jar without ice. Repeat for each drink.
  3. Add ice and shake hard again, then strain into short glasses over a large ice cube.
  4. Twist a strip of orange peel over the top and drop it in.
