Snacks

Thick-cut friesVegetarian

Cooked twice in hot oil until crisp, salted hard. White vinegar on every table.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg russet potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips, rinse and dry well.
  2. Fry in oil at 140°C until pale and soft, in batches, 10 min.
  3. Rest the chips on a rack, 20 min.
  4. Fry again at 185°C until crisp and golden. Salt hard and put the vinegar on the table, 10 min.

Cheddar, saltines and picklesVegetarian

Thick slabs of the sharp stuff, dill spears, a sleeve of crackers and hot English mustard.

Prep 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sharp cheddar
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 1 packet saltine crackers
  • 1 small jar hot English mustard
  1. Take the cheddar out of the fridge an hour ahead.
  2. Cut the cheddar into thick slabs and the pickles into spears. Lay out with the crackers and a small dish of mustard for smearing.

Mains

Hot open roast beef sandwich

Rare slices warmed in brown gravy on thick white toast, more gravy poured over. Buttered mashed potatoes and peas.

Prep 2 min, cook 30 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sliced rare roast beef
  • 400ml hot beef gravy
  • 4 thick slices of white bread
  • 800g potatoes
  • 40g butter, for the mash
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 20g butter, for the peas
  1. Peel and boil the potatoes until soft, then mash with the butter, 20 min.
  2. Cook the peas and toss with butter, 5 min.
  3. Warm the beef in the hot gravy.
  4. Toast the bread, lay on plates, pile on the beef and pour over more gravy. Serve with the mash and peas, 5 min.

Sweets

Peach brandy bread puddingVegetarian

Buttered white loaf layered with brandied peaches, baked in vanilla custard. Served warm.

Prep 10 min, cook 48 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of thick white bread
  • 2 tins of sliced peaches, drained
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Soak the peaches in the brandy and brown sugar, 5 min.
  2. Butter the bread and layer with the peaches in a baking dish.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the milk and vanilla and pour over.
  4. Rest so the bread soaks up the custard, 20 min.
  5. Bake at 170°C until set and golden. Serve warm, 40 min.

Drinks

Night man's coffee

Strong drip with a nip of peach brandy and brown sugar, a spoonful of soft whipped cream on top.

Prep 13 min
Recipe for 4
  • 50g ground coffee
  • 800ml water
  • 120ml peach brandy
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the coffee strong using the water.
  2. Lightly whip the cream until just thick.
  3. Put brown sugar and peach brandy into 4 diner mugs, pour in hot coffee and stir. Spoon a little cream on top and serve straight away, 5 min.

When to serve

Pour the coffee when Travis sits with the other cabbies at the all-night diner.
