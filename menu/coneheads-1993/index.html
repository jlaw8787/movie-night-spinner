Snacks

Bowl of chipsVegetarian

Thin sliced potatoes fried crisp and salted, piled by the bowlful.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 6 potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp fine salt
  1. Slice the potatoes thin on a mandoline, rinse and dry well.
  2. Heat the oil to 170°C and fry in batches until golden and crisp, 20 min.
  3. Salt well while hot and pile into a big bowl.

Lager battered onion ringsVegetarian

Buttermilk and lager batter, crisp enough to knarftle a Garthok.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the onion rings in the buttermilk up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; make the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 brown onions
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the onions into thick rings and soak in the buttermilk, 30 min.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager into a batter.
  3. Heat the oil to 185°C. Dip the rings in the batter and fry in batches until puffed and golden, 10 min.
  4. Salt and serve hot.

Remulak chicken salad waffle cones

Mini waffle cones of creamy chicken salad with celery and chives, Remulak style, very pointy.

How it is made
  • 500g chicken breast
  • 120g mayo
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 30g flaked almonds
  • 1 lemon
  • 12 mini waffle cones
  • 12 cherry tomatoes
  1. Mini waffle cones filled with creamy poached chicken salad with celery, chives and toasted almonds, topped with a cherry tomato point. Poach 500g chicken breast in simmering salted water for 15 minutes, cool and shred finely. Mix with 120g mayo, 2 tbsp sour cream, 2 finely diced celery sticks, 2 tbsp chives, 30g chopped toasted almonds, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Chill 20 minutes. Just before serving, spoon into 12 mini waffle cones and top each with a cherry tomato.

Mains

Beldar's driving school burgers

Griddled beef patties with American cheese, pickles and onion, two per bun.

Prep 32 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls and slice the onion up to 1 day ahead, chilled; smash and cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef mince
  • 12 slices of American cheese
  • 6 burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • salt, to taste
  1. Roll the mince into 12 balls and slice the onion.
  2. Smash thin on a hot griddle in batches, salt, and flip after 90 seconds, adding cheese.
  3. Toast the buns, 3 min.
  4. Stack two patties per bun with pickles and onion and pile onto a platter.

Suburban cookout sausages and chicken

Chicken and sausages, charred mammal flesh.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 100g mayo
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  1. Thick beef sausages and lemon garlic chicken thighs grilled over coals, with buttered corn on the cob and a creamy mustard potato salad. Marinate 800g chicken thighs in 2 tbsp oil, 2 crushed garlic cloves, lemon zest and salt for 1 hour. Boil 1kg baby potatoes until tender, halve and dress warm with 100g mayo, 1 tbsp Dijon, chopped spring onion and dill. Grill 8 thick beef sausages and the chicken over medium coals for 15 to 20 minutes, turning. Grill 4 corn cobs for 10 minutes and brush with butter.

Cone-shaped ice creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream piled high in waffle cones with a chocolate shell.

Prep 19 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream, softened
  • 4 waffle cones
  1. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil and cool slightly, 6 min.
  2. Pipe or scoop the softened ice cream high into the cones.
  3. Freeze until firm, 20 min.
  4. Dip the tops in the chocolate so it sets into a shell.

Drinks

Paramus lager shandy jug

A jug of lager and lemonade with lemon and lime wedges.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill the lager and lemonade up to 1 day ahead; add the ice and citrus and pour together just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5 L lager (4 bottles of 375ml)
  • 1.25 L lemonade
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill the lager and lemonade until very cold, 5 min.
  2. Fill a large jug with ice and add the lemon and lime wedges, squeezing as they go in.
  3. Pour in the lager and lemonade together, slowly. Stir once and pour into big glasses.
