Boomstick mini sausage rolls

Mini pork sausage rolls in puff pastry, small, loud and impossible to stop at one.

Prep 27 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled, or freeze for a month; bake from cold on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 6 sage leaves, chopped
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 125ml tomato relish
  1. Heat the oven to 210°C and line two trays.
  2. Mix 500g pork sausage mince with the grated onion, crushed fennel, sage, breadcrumbs and pepper.
  3. Halve each pastry sheet, lay a line of filling along one long edge, roll up and seal with beaten egg.
  4. Cut into 4cm lengths, brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and snip a small slash in each top.
  5. Bake until puffed, golden and cooked through.
  6. Serve warm with the tomato relish.

Skeleton army garlic bread bonesVegetarian

Garlic bread twisted into bones, a whole army rattling out of the oven.

Prep 20 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Shape up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge; mix the garlic butter up to 2 days ahead; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C and line two trays.
  2. On a lightly floured bench, divide the dough into 12, roll each into a 20cm rope and tie a loose knot at each end so it looks like a bone.
  3. Lay on the trays and leave to rise a little.
  4. Mix the butter, garlic and parsley and brush half over the bones.
  5. Bake until golden.
  6. Brush with the rest of the garlic butter and sprinkle with the salt and parmesan.

Lord Arthur's castle roast chicken

Golden roast chicken carried into the great hall on a board for the whole court.

Prep 5 min, cook 101 min · Make ahead: Season the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep uncovered in the fridge for crisper skin; cut the vegetables in the morning
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 garlic bulb, halved crossways
  • 4 carrots, halved lengthways
  • 3 parsnips, quartered
  • 2 red onions, quartered
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Pat the chicken dry, rub with the butter, thyme, salt and pepper and push the lemon halves inside.
  2. Toss the carrots, parsnips, onions and garlic with the oil in a roasting tin and sit the chicken on top.
  3. Roast for 1 hour.
  4. Brush the chicken with the honey and roast until the juices run clear.
  5. Rest the chicken on a board and keep the vegetables warm.
  6. Stir the flour into the pan juices, whisk in 300ml chicken stock and simmer to a gravy.
  7. Carve and serve with the vegetables and gravy.

Great hall roast roots with honey and thymeVegetarian

Carrots, parsnips and turnips. Shop smart, shop S-Mart.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cut the vegetables up to 1 day ahead; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 carrots, halved lengthways
  • 3 parsnips, halved lengthways
  • 2 turnips, cut in wedges
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  1. Cut the carrots, parsnips and turnips.
  2. Toss with the oil and salt and roast at 210°C until browned.
  3. Drizzle with honey, add the thyme and roast until sticky.

Ash's forgotten words honey cakeVegetarian

Honey and brown sugar loaf with ginger and cloves, almonds on top, Klaatu barada nikto.

Prep 10 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep wrapped, it gets stickier; drizzle and slice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g honey, for the cake
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml milk
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • 2 tbsp honey, for drizzling
  • 200ml thick cream
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and grease and line a loaf tin.
  2. Melt 250g honey with the butter and brown sugar and cool a little.
  3. Whisk in the eggs and milk, then fold in the flour, ginger, cinnamon and cloves.
  4. Pour into the tin and scatter with the almonds.
  5. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Cool in the tin.
  7. Drizzle with the extra honey and serve in thick slices with the cream.

Henry the Red's spiced mead punch

Mead warmed gently and steeped, served in heavy mugs with orange, for the truce with Arthur.

Prep 3 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Steep the spiced punch up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; warm gently to serve without boiling
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml mead
  • 500ml cloudy apple juice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 4 slices fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Put everything in a pot and warm gently without letting it boil.
  2. Take off the heat and leave to steep.
  3. Ladle into 4 heavy mugs with a slice of orange in each.
