Snacks

Instant Polaroid grilled cheese with tomato soupVegetarian

Grilled cheese with tomato soup to dip. Polaroid of everyone.

Prep 7 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 2 days ahead and reheat; press the sandwiches to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 800g tinned whole tomatoes
  • 250ml vegetable stock
  • 60ml pouring cream
  • 8 slices white sandwich bread
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 100g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil, add the tomatoes and stock and simmer for 15 minutes.
  2. Blend smooth, stir in the cream and season.
  3. Butter the outsides of the bread, fill with the cheddar and mozzarella, and press the parmesan onto the buttered sides.
  4. Cook in a pan under a weight until crisp and melting, then trim into neat squares like little Polaroids and serve with shot glasses of soup.

Room 304 loaded nachosVegetarian

Corn chips loaded with beans and melted cheese. Burt rents out a second room.

Prep 17 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the beans and the salsa up to a day ahead; build and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 230g bag plain corn chips
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapenos
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 avocado
  1. Warm the beans with the cumin and a splash of water, mashing a few.
  2. Mix the tomato and red onion with half the lime juice and a pinch of salt.
  3. Layer the chips, beans and cheese twice in a baking dish and bake at 200°C until the cheese bubbles.
  4. Mash the avocado with the rest of the lime, then top the nachos with salsa, jalapenos, avocado and sour cream.

Mains

Two hundred grand steak frites with peppercorn sauce

Steak frites with peppercorn sauce. Jimmy's cash in the Jaguar's trunk.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Cut the chips and soak them up to a day ahead in the fridge; cook the steak to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 250g rib-eye steaks
  • 1kg floury potatoes, cut in thin chips
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 30g butter
  • 1 French shallot, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp green peppercorns in brine, drained
  • 60ml brandy
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  1. Fry the chips at 150°C until soft but pale, then lift out and drain.
  2. Season the steaks and sear in a very hot pan for 3 to 4 minutes a side for medium rare, then rest.
  3. Melt the butter in the steak pan, soften the shallot and peppercorns, add the brandy and let it bubble off.
  4. Add the cream and mustard and simmer until it coats a spoon.
  5. Fry the chips again at 190°C until crisp, salt them and serve with the steak and sauce.

Backwards pineapple upside-down cakeVegetarian

Pineapple upside-down cake, baked bottom first. The colour scenes run in reverse.

Prep 17 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Bake up to a day ahead and keep covered; warm through to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, melted, plus 125g softened
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 440g tin pineapple rings, drained
  • 8 glace cherries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 185g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Pour the melted butter into a 22cm cake tin, scatter over the brown sugar and lay the pineapple rings on top with a cherry in each hole.
  2. Beat the softened butter and caster sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  3. Fold in the flour and milk and spread the batter over the fruit.
  4. Bake at 180°C until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, turn out upside down and serve warm with the cream.

Cold lager with a bourbon chaser. He drinks it even after Natalie spits in it.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the beer and glasses; pour to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 355ml bottles American lager
  • 120ml bourbon
  1. Pour the lager into 4 chilled glasses.
  2. Pour 30ml bourbon into each shot glass and serve on the side.
