Link's ice block cheddar cheese ball

Cheddar and cream cheese ball with jalapeño, rolled in bacon and pretzel crumbs

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese, softened
  • 200g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapenos, chopped
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 rashers bacon
  • 60g pretzels, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  • crushed ice, to serve
  • 1 packet of crackers
  1. Beat the cream cheese, cheddar, jalapeno, paprika and Worcestershire sauce.
  2. Shape into a ball, wrap and chill.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp and crumble with the pretzels and chives.
  4. Roll the ball in the crumb.
  5. Serve on a plate of crushed ice with crackers.

Estonian exchange student drumsticks

Smoky paprika drumsticks roasted until sticky with barbecue sauce

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken drumsticks
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 tbsp barbecue sauce
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub the drumsticks with the paprika, garlic powder and sugar.
  2. Roast the drumsticks.
  3. Brush with barbecue sauce and roast until sticky.

Dave's backyard loaded nachosOn the printed menu

Corn chips baked under beans, chorizo and two cheeses, guacamole and sour cream, wheezin' the juice

How it is made
  • 1 chorizo sausage
  • 300g corn chips
  • 1 tin black beans
  • 150g cheddar
  • 100g mozzarella
  • 2 avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Corn chips baked under black beans, chorizo and two cheeses, piled with guacamole, sour cream, pickled jalapeño and a squeeze of lime. Fry 1 diced chorizo until crisp. Layer 300g corn chips on a tray with the chorizo, 1 drained tin black beans, 150g grated cheddar and 100g grated mozzarella, and bake at 200°C for 8 minutes. Mash 2 avocados with lime and salt. Top the nachos with guacamole, sour cream, pickled jalapeños, coriander and lime wedges.

Pool party teriyaki chicken

Teriyaki chicken thigh skewers, grilled and basted, with steamed rice

Prep 17 min, cook 30 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chicken can marinate up to 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh, diced
  • 125ml teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 12 skewers
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the chicken in the teriyaki sauce, ginger and garlic.
  2. Marinate.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill, turning and basting.
  5. Steam the rice.
  6. Serve with sesame seeds.

Link's caveman beef short ribs

Meat group! A big hunk of beef on the bone, eaten with both hands

Prep 5 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Roast the ribs up to 2 days ahead; glaze and finish under the grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.6kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 300g shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Rub the ribs with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Put the ribs in a deep tray with the stock, cover tightly with foil and roast until tender.
  3. Brush with barbecue sauce and roast uncovered until sticky.
  4. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayonnaise and vinegar and serve with the ribs.

Block of ice yoghurt barkVegetarian

Honey vanilla yoghurt frozen with berries and pistachios, broken into shards

Prep 10 min, cook 2 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the bark until firm (about 4 hours) and keep it frozen up to 1 week; break into shards just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g mixed berries
  • 40g pistachios, crushed
  1. Line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Mix the yoghurt, honey and vanilla and spread thin.
  3. Scatter with berries and pistachios.
  4. Freeze until firm.
  5. Break into shards.

Link's tropical mango smoothieVegetarianNo alcohol

Strawberry layered under a thick mango, banana, pineapple and orange smoothie

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Blend the strawberry and honey layer up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; blend the mango mix and pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200g frozen strawberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 mangoes, chopped
  • 2 bananas, frozen
  • 200g pineapple
  • 400ml orange juice
  • 1 handful of ice
  1. Blend the strawberries with the honey and spoon into the bottom of 4 tall glasses.
  2. Blend the mango, bananas, pineapple, orange juice and ice until thick.
  3. Pour slowly over the strawberry so it swirls up the sides.
  4. Serve with a straw.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Link is thawed out in the garage.
