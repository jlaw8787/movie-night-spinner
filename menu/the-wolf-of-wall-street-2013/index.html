Twisted Robin Hood king prawn cocktail

Forbes calls Jordan a twisted Robin Hood

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Poach the prawns and mix the sauce up to a day ahead; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 16 raw king prawns, peeled with tails on
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/4 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Drop the prawns into simmering salted water for 2 minutes until just pink, then into iced water.
  2. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, Worcestershire, hot sauce and a squeeze of lemon into Marie Rose sauce.
  3. Fill 4 chilled glasses with lettuce and avocado.
  4. Hang the prawns over the rim, spoon over the sauce and dust with paprika.

Aunt Emma's smoked salmon blini with caviar

Aunt Emma holds the Swiss money

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Cook the blini up to a day ahead and warm in the oven to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g buckwheat flour
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 150g creme fraiche
  • 50g salmon roe or caviar
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Whisk the flours, baking powder and a pinch of salt with the milk, egg and half the butter.
  2. Brush a frying pan with butter and cook tablespoons of batter until bubbles show, then flip.
  3. Top each blini with creme fraiche and a curl of salmon.
  4. Finish with a little caviar, dill and a squeeze of lemon.

Naomi's lobster thermidor with shoestring fries

Jordan throws lobsters at the FBI

Prep 30 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to a day ahead; fill and grill the lobsters to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cooked lobsters, halved lengthways
  • 50g butter
  • 1 French shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml milk
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp tarragon, chopped
  • 80g gruyere, grated
  • 750g frozen shoestring fries
  • 1 lemon, in wedges
  1. Bake the fries at 220C until crisp while you make the sauce.
  2. Pull the lobster meat from the shells and tails, cut into chunks and keep the shells.
  3. Soften the shallot in the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and cream and simmer until thick.
  4. Stir in the mustard, tarragon, half the cheese and the lobster, then pile back into the shells.
  5. Scatter with the rest of the cheese and grill until bubbling and golden, then serve with the fries and lemon.

Steve Madden IPO steak au poivre with frites

Steve Madden goes public

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Crack the pepper and trim the steaks up to a day ahead; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 200g eye fillet steaks
  • 2 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely cracked
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 40g butter
  • 60ml cognac
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 750g frozen thick-cut chips
  • 1 bunch watercress
  1. Bake the chips at 220C until golden.
  2. Press the pepper into both sides of the steaks and season with salt.
  3. Sear in the oil and butter over high heat, 3 to 4 minutes a side for medium rare, then rest.
  4. Pour the cognac into the pan and let it bubble off, then add the stock and cream and boil until it coats a spoon.
  5. Serve the steaks with the sauce, frites and watercress.

Lemmon 714 lemon meringue tartVegetarian

Jordan calls the Lemmons the holy grail

Prep 30 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake the tart case and make the curd up to a day ahead; top with meringue to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 23cm sweet shortcrust tart case, blind baked
  • 4 lemons, zested and juiced
  • 200g caster sugar, plus 150g for the meringue
  • 4 eggs, separated, plus 2 yolks
  • 150g butter, cubed
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 tsp cream of tartar
  1. Whisk the lemon zest and juice, 200g sugar, 6 yolks and cornflour in a pan over low heat until thick.
  2. Take off the heat, whisk in the butter, pour into the tart case and chill until set.
  3. Whisk the whites with the cream of tartar to soft peaks, then add the rest of the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  4. Pile the meringue over the curd and bake at 200C until golden at the tips.
  5. Cool for 10 minutes before slicing.

Mark Hanna's Absolut martini, straight upVegetarian

Two more every five minutes

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Keep the vodka in the freezer and chill the glasses; stir to order
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml Absolut vodka
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Stir the vodka and vermouth with ice in a jug for 30 seconds until very cold.
  2. Strain into 4 chilled martini glasses and twist a strip of lemon peel over each.
