Snacks

Gourmet Club antipasto, all legal

Salami, mortadella, provolone, roast capsicum and seeded bread, a Komodo dragon nowhere in sight

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, serve at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 100g salami
  • 100g mortadella
  • 150g provolone
  • 2 roasted red capsicums, cut in strips
  • 200g marinated artichokes
  • 1 seeded Italian loaf
  1. Arrange the salami, mortadella and provolone on a board.
  2. Add the capsicum strips and artichokes.
  3. Tear the loaf at the table.

Little Italy fried calamari

Tender squid in light semolina, lemon and marinara, for an NYU film student

Prep 15 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Soak up to 1 hour ahead; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g squid tubes
  • 200ml milk
  • 120g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp semolina
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 2 lemons, cut in wedges
  • 250g marinara sauce
  1. Slice the squid into rings and soak in the milk for 20 minutes.
  2. Toss in the flour mixed with semolina and salt.
  3. Fry in the oil at 190C for 1 minute a batch.
  4. Warm the marinara and serve with lemon.

Mains

Carmine Sabatini's veal marsala

Thin veal seared in butter, mushroom and fortified wine pan sauce, an offer Clark can't refuse

Prep 15 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; dust the veal in the seasoned flour and slice the mushrooms up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g veal escalopes
  • 40g seasoned plain flour
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 150ml marsala
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 40g cold butter
  1. Dust the veal in seasoned flour and sear in butter and oil for 1 minute a side, then set aside.
  2. Brown the mushrooms, add the marsala and stock and reduce by half.
  3. Whisk in the cold butter, return the veal and warm through.

Sweets

CannoliVegetarian

A box from Little Italy for the drive back to campus

Prep 20 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead; fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fresh ricotta
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 50g mini chocolate chips
  • 8 cannoli shells
  • 30g pistachios, chopped
  1. Drain the ricotta for 2 hours.
  2. Beat with icing sugar and vanilla, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  3. Pipe into the shells just before serving and dip the ends in pistachios.

Drinks

Carmine's amaro spritzVegetarian

Amaro and prosecco over ice with soda and orange, bittersweet like Clark's first day in town

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the amaro, prosecco and soda up to 1 day ahead; build over ice just before serving so it stays fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml Italian amaro
  • 360ml prosecco, chilled
  • 120ml soda water
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four large wine glasses with ice.
  2. Add amaro to each, then prosecco and soda, and stir once gently.
  3. Slide a thin orange wheel into each glass and serve straight away.
