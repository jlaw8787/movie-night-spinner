Snacks

Khan family samosasVegetarian

Fried pastry triangles of spiced potato, peas and onion.

Prep 18 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 1 day ahead, chilled, and fold the samosas up to 4 hours ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 packet spring roll pastry
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 handful mint
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Boil the potatoes, then crush them with the peas.
  2. Fry the onion and garam masala and mix into the potato.
  3. Cut the pastry into strips, fill and fold into triangles.
  4. Fry in the oil at 180°C in batches until crisp.
  5. Blend the mint into the yoghurt for dipping.

Space station popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in lime zest, chilli flakes and melted butter.

Prep 2 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Zest the limes and melt the butter with the chilli up to 1 day ahead; pop the corn just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 2 limes
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  1. Zest the limes and melt the butter with the zest and chilli.
  2. Heat the oil in a large lidded pot and pop the corn, shaking now and then.
  3. Toss the butter through, add a squeeze of lime juice and serve in silver bowls.

Mains

Jersey City chicken biryani

Yoghurt marinated chicken layered with basmati and crisp fried onions.

Prep 0 min, cook 73 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the yoghurt and garam masala up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the rice and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp garam masala
  • 400g basmati rice
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • salt, to taste
  1. Marinate the chicken in the yoghurt, garam masala and a good pinch of salt for 2 hours.
  2. Rest the chicken in the fridge.
  3. Slice the onions and fry in the oil until crisp, soak the saffron in warm milk.
  4. Par-cook the rice in boiling water.
  5. Layer chicken, rice and onions in an ovenproof pot, drizzle with saffron milk and seal tightly.
  6. Bake at 170°C.

Aladna lemon and oregano lamb skewers

Lamb leg marinated in garlic, chargrilled, with flatbread and herb salad, fit for Prince Yan.

How it is made
  • 800g lamb leg
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 red onion
  • 200g tzatziki
  1. Lamb skewers marinated in lemon, garlic and oregano, grilled and served with flatbread and herb salad. Cut 800g lamb leg into 3cm cubes and marinate 1 hour with the juice and zest of 1 lemon, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp dried oregano, 3 tbsp olive oil and salt. Thread onto 8 skewers and grill over high heat for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until charred outside and pink inside. Warm 4 flatbreads. Toss parsley, mint, tomato, cucumber and red onion with lemon and oil. Serve with the skewers and 200g tzatziki.

Sweets

Kamala's fan art cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes piped with red, blue and gold swirls.

Prep 25 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 125g butter, softened, for the buttercream
  • 3 tubes gel food colouring (red, blue and gold)
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a muffin tin.
  2. Beat the butter for the cakes with the caster sugar, beat in the eggs, then fold in flour and milk.
  3. Bake until golden.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Beat the icing sugar with the butter for the buttercream, split into three colours with the food colouring and pipe swirls on top with a little star on each.

Drinks

Mango lassiNo alcohol

Mango, yoghurt and cardamom blended over ice, a cold lassi on the S.A.B.E.R. station.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Peel and slice the mangoes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; blend and pour over ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 pinch ground cardamom
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Peel and slice the mangoes.
  2. Blend with the yoghurt, milk, sugar and cardamom until smooth.
  3. Pour over ice in tall glasses.
