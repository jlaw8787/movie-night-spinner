Snacks

Harbour pork and prawn dumplings

Steamed pork, prawn and shiitake dumplings with chilli oil

Prep 40 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat the dumplings up to 1 day ahead on a floured tray in the fridge; steam just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 50g shiitake mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the pork, prawns, ginger, shiitake and soy.
  2. Spoon into the wonton wrappers and pinch up the sides.
  3. Steam until cooked through.
  4. Serve with chilli oil.

Han's island tournament spring rollsVegetarian

Crisp rolls of cabbage, carrot, shiitake and glass noodles with sweet chilli

Prep 40 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Roll the spring rolls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cups shredded wombok
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 100g shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 50g glass noodles
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 16 spring roll wrappers
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Soak the glass noodles and prepare the vegetables.
  2. Stir-fry the wombok, carrot and shiitake.
  3. Add the noodles and soy.
  4. Cool the filling.
  5. Roll tight in the wrappers.
  6. Fry at 180C until golden.
  7. Serve hot with the sweet chilli sauce for dipping.

Mains

Mirror room char siu with fried rice

Honey hoisin glazed roast pork neck with egg fried rice

Prep 30 min, cook 756 min · Make ahead: Marinate a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork neck
  • 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp five spice
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 300g cooked jasmine rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 spring onions
  1. Mix the hoisin, soy, honey, five spice and Shaoxing and coat the pork.
  2. Marinate overnight.
  3. Roast at 200C, glazing twice.
  4. Rest the pork.
  5. Fry the rice with the eggs and spring onion.
  6. Slice the pork over the rice.

Sweets

Mango pancakesVegetarian

Thin crepes wrapped around whipped cream and fresh mango

Prep 40 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 120g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 mangoes
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk and sugar and rest.
  2. Cook 8 thin crepes in a non-stick pan and cool.
  3. Whip the cream and slice the mango.
  4. Wrap each crepe around cream and a thick slice of mango.
  5. Fold into parcels and chill for an hour.

Drinks

Iced chrysanthemum and goji honey teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled chrysanthemum tea with goji berries, rock sugar and honey

Prep 10 min, cook 69 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l water
  • 15g dried chrysanthemum flowers
  • 2 tbsp goji berries
  • 50g rock sugar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Bring the water to the boil and take off the heat.
  2. Add the chrysanthemum, goji and rock sugar and steep, stirring until the sugar melts.
  3. Strain, keeping the goji berries, and stir in the honey.
  4. Cool, then chill for an hour.
  5. Pour over ice in four glasses, drop in a few goji berries and add a squeeze of lemon.
