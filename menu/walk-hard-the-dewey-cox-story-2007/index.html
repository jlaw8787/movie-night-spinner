Springberry Cajun boiled peanutsVegetarian

Cajun spiced peanuts boiled until soft, served warm in cones.

Prep 9 min, cook 3 hr 1 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Boil up to 2 days ahead and keep in the brine in the fridge; reheat gently to serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 500g raw peanuts in the shell
  • 80g salt
  • 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tbsp chilli flakes
  • 1 head garlic
  • paper cones, to taste
  • 3 L water
  1. Rinse the raw peanuts in the shell.
  2. Put in a large pot with the water, the salt, the Cajun seasoning, the chilli flakes and the halved head of garlic.
  3. Weigh down with a plate so they stay under.
  4. Simmer 3 hours, topping up with water, until the nuts are soft like cooked beans.
  5. Leave in the brine 30 minutes more, then drain and serve warm in paper cones.

Dewey's shoestring fries and gravyVegetarian

Shoestring fries under hot brown onion gravy, scallions on top.

Prep 3 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 2 days ahead and reheat; bake the chips on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen shoestring chips
  • 1 onion, finely sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Bake the frozen shoestring chips at 220°C until crisp, 20 min.
  2. Soften the onion in the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the stock and soy sauce and simmer until thick, 12 min.
  3. Pour the gravy over the chips and scatter with sliced spring onion.

Mains

Mama Cox's buttermilk fried chicken

Crisp buttermilk chicken with mashed potatoes and pepper gravy.

Prep 18 min, cook 39 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk overnight, up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; dredge and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken pieces
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • hot sauce, to taste
  • 270g plain flour
  • paprika, to taste
  • garlic powder, to taste
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 500ml milk
  • 2 tsp salt
  1. Soak the chicken pieces overnight in the buttermilk with the hot sauce and the salt.
  2. Dredge in the plain flour mixed with the paprika, the garlic powder and the pepper.
  3. Fry at 160°C for 12 to 15 minutes until 75°C inside.
  4. Mash the potatoes with the butter and the milk.
  5. For gravy, whisk the frying oil with the flour, then the milk, simmer until thick and season with lots of pepper, 21 min.

Maharishi ashram curryVegetarian

Pumpkin, cauliflower and chickpeas in coconut with spinach and basmati, eaten with the Beatles.

Prep 4 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the curry up to 2 days ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 20g ginger
  • 3 tbsp mild curry paste
  • 500g butternut pumpkin
  • 1/2 cauliflower
  • 400g tinned chickpeas, drained
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 tbsp mango chutney
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Fry the chopped onion, garlic and ginger in the oil for 6 minutes, then the curry paste for 1 minute.
  2. Add the diced pumpkin, cauliflower florets, chickpeas, coconut milk and 200ml water and simmer for 25 minutes until tender.
  3. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, then rest for 5.
  4. Stir the spinach through the curry until wilted.
  5. Serve with the rice, mango chutney and coriander.

Sweet potato meringue pieVegetarian

Spiced sweet potato custard pie under a toasted swirl of meringue.

Prep 1 min, cook 2 hr 25 min · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to 1 day ahead without the meringue and chill; top and torch on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 500g sweet potato
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • ground cinnamon, to taste
  • nutmeg, to taste
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C, 6 min.
  2. Line a 23cm tart tin with the shortcrust sheet and blind bake 15 minutes.
  3. Mash the cooked sweet potato with the brown sugar, the eggs plus the yolks, the evaporated milk, the cinnamon and a little nutmeg until smooth, 6 min.
  4. Pour into the case and bake 40 minutes until just set.
  5. Cool.
  6. Whisk the egg whites with the caster sugar until glossy, pile on top and torch or grill until golden, then serve with the thickened cream, lightly whipped, 6 min.

Drinks

Peach sweet teaNo alcohol

Peach syrup black tea with lemon and mint over ice.

Prep 4 min, cook 16 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe peaches
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 5 black tea bags
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 4 cups ice
  • 100ml water
  1. Simmer the sliced ripe peaches with the brown sugar and the water for 10 minutes, then strain.
  2. Steep the black tea bags in the just-boiled water for 5 minutes, remove and stir in the peach syrup.
  3. Chill 2 hours.
  4. Fill the tall glasses with ice, add fresh peach slices and a lemon wedge, pour in the tea and top with mint.
