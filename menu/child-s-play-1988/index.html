Snacks

Burnt toast soldiers with soft eggsVegetarian

Brown butter toast fingers and runny yolks.

Prep 0 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Brown the butter up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; boil the eggs and toast to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 8 eggs
  • 8 thick slices of white sourdough
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  1. Melt the butter and cook until it smells nutty and turns golden brown, then pour into a bowl, 4 min.
  2. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 6 minutes for runny yolks.
  3. Toast the bread dark at the edges, brush heavily with the brown butter and cut into soldiers, 4 min.
  4. Sit the eggs in cups, take the tops off and scatter salt and chives over the toast.

Chicago-style hot dogs

Franks with seven toppings dragged through the garden. Norris chases Ray through Chicago.

Prep 12 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Chop the toppings up to a day ahead and keep cold; cook the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 all-beef frankfurts
  • 8 poppy seed split-top bread rolls
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp bright green sweet relish
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  • 2 tomatoes, cut in wedges
  • 8 dill pickle spears
  • 16 pickled sport peppers or pepperoncinis
  • 1 tsp celery salt
  1. Simmer the franks in water until hot through, about 6 minutes.
  2. Steam or warm the rolls so they stay soft, 3 min.
  3. Tuck in the franks and add mustard, relish and onion.
  4. Add tomato wedges, a pickle spear and two peppers to each, and finish with celery salt.

Damballa Haitian griot with pikliz

Citrus-braised crisp pork and spicy slaw.

Prep 5 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Braise the pork and make the pikliz up to 2 days ahead; fry the pork to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder, cut in 4cm pieces
  • 2 oranges, juiced
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 1 habanero chilli, pierced
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 250ml white vinegar
  • 300g white rice
  • 400g tin red kidney beans, drained
  1. Toss the pork with the orange and lime juice, garlic, onion, thyme, chilli and salt and leave for 20 minutes.
  2. Tip into a pot with water to just cover and simmer until tender and most of the liquid has gone, 1 hr 15 min.
  3. Mix the cabbage and carrot with the vinegar, a pinch of chilli and salt for the pikliz.
  4. Cook the rice with the beans and a pinch of salt, 20 min.
  5. Fry the drained pork in hot oil until crisp at the edges and serve with the rice and pikliz, 10 min.

Good Guy macaroni cheeseVegetarian

Three-cheese bake with a crunchy top.

Prep 0 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce and cook the pasta up to a day ahead; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g macaroni
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml milk
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g Gruyère, grated
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  1. Boil the macaroni until just short of tender and drain, 10 min.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and simmer until thick, 8 min.
  3. Stir in the mustard, cheddar and Gruyère, fold through the pasta and tip into a baking dish.
  4. Scatter the panko and parmesan over and bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling, 20 min.

Birthday sprinkle cakeVegetarian

Vanilla sponge with rainbow sprinkles. Maggie digs into the birthday cake.

Prep 29 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Bake the sponges up to 2 days ahead and keep wrapped; ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened, plus 200g for the icing
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 185ml milk
  • 80g rainbow sprinkles, plus more to finish
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 6 birthday candles
  1. Beat 250g butter with the sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  2. Fold in the flour, milk and sprinkles and divide between two lined 20cm tins.
  3. Bake at 175°C until a skewer comes out clean, then cool, 32 min.
  4. Beat 200g butter with the icing sugar and a splash of milk until fluffy.
  5. Sandwich and cover the cakes, shower with sprinkles and add 6 candles.
