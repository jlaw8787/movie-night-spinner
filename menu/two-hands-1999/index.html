Snacks

Bondi fish and chips

Lager-battered flathead and chips. Mind the envelope.

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in thick chips
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g plain flour, for the batter
  • 250ml ice cold lager
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 50g plain flour, for dusting
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  • 4 tbsp tartare sauce
  1. Fry the potato chips in the oil at 140°C until soft and drain.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager to a thin batter.
  3. Dip the flathead fillets in the dusting flour, then batter, and fry in the oil at 180°C.
  4. Fry the chips again in the oil until crisp.
  5. Wrap it all in paper with lemon and tartare.

Kings Cross pie floaters

Beef pie sitting on a bowl of buttery crushed peas

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck, diced
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 500g peas
  • 30g butter
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Braise the beef in the stock until tender.
  2. Thicken with the flour and cool.
  3. Line pie tins with shortcrust, fill and top with puff lids.
  4. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C.
  5. Cook the peas and mash roughly with the butter.
  6. Spoon peas into bowls, sit a pie on top and finish with tomato sauce.

Pando's crew potato scallopsOn the printed menu

Thick slices battered and fried golden, hit with chicken salt, tomato sauce and lemon

How it is made
  • 3 large potatoes
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 250ml soda water
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  • 1 jar chicken salt
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 lemon
  1. Thick potato slices dipped in a crisp batter and fried golden, the fish-shop classic, hit with chicken salt and served with tomato sauce and lemon. Peel 3 large potatoes and slice 1cm thick, then simmer for 4 minutes until just tender and pat dry. Whisk 150g self-raising flour, a pinch of salt and 230ml ice-cold soda water into a batter. Dust the slices in flour, dip in the batter and fry at 180°C for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain, sprinkle with chicken salt and serve with tomato sauce and lemon.

Chinatown squid and egg fried rice

Crisp rice-flour squid with chilli and spring onion, jasmine rice fried with egg

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 500g squid tubes
  • 100g rice flour
  • 1 tsp ground Sichuan pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1l vegetable oil, for deep frying
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • 600g cold cooked jasmine rice
  • 3 eggs, scrambled
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, for frying the rice
  1. Score the squid and cut into pieces.
  2. Toss in rice flour, Sichuan pepper and salt.
  3. Deep fry in the oil at 190°C for a minute, then toss with chilli and spring onion.
  4. Heat the 2 tbsp of oil, scramble the eggs, then fry the rice with the eggs and spring onion.
  5. Serve together.

Suburban Sunday roast pork and cracklingOn the printed menu

Belly roasted until the skin shatters, crisp potatoes, honey pumpkin, apple sauce and gravy

How it is made
  • 1.5kg pork belly with skin
  • 1 box sea salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 600g pumpkin
  • 1 jar honey
  • 3 green apples
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Pork belly roasted until the crackling shatters, with crisp potatoes, honey pumpkin, apple sauce and gravy, the full Sydney Sunday spread. Score the skin of a 1.5kg pork belly, pat dry, rub with 1 tbsp salt and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Roast at 240°C for 30 minutes, then 170°C for 90 minutes. Roast 1kg potatoes and 600g pumpkin drizzled with honey alongside for the last hour. Simmer 3 chopped apples with 2 tbsp sugar and water until soft for sauce. Make gravy with the pan juices, 1 tbsp flour and 300ml stock.

Loukoumades with honey and walnutsVegetarian

Hot puffs of fried yeast dough in honey syrup with cinnamon and walnuts.

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; syrup keeps for 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 400ml warm water
  • 150g honey
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 60g walnuts
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm water to a thick batter.
  2. Rest in a warm spot until bubbly.
  3. Simmer the honey, water and cinnamon into a syrup.
  4. Fry spoonfuls of batter in 180°C oil, turning, in 3 batches.
  5. Toss in the syrup and top with chopped walnuts.

Ten-grand passionfruit shandyVegetarian

Cold lager topped with passionfruit, lime and soda, half a passionfruit on top

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Stir the passionfruit, lime and sugar base up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add the soda and lager on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 passionfruit, pulp, for the soda mix
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 400ml cold soda water
  • 800ml cold lager
  • 1 passionfruit, halved, for floating
  1. Stir the passionfruit pulp, lime juice and sugar until dissolved.
  2. Add the soda water.
  3. Pour lager into 4 chilled schooner glasses.
  4. Top with the passionfruit soda and float half a passionfruit on top.
