Mary's grilled cheese and tomato soup

Creamy tomato soup with sourdough cheddar and mozzarella grilled sandwiches.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead and reheat; fill and fry the sandwiches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tins of crushed tomatoes (400g each)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 50ml thickened cream
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 150g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  1. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil until soft, 5 min.
  2. Add the tomatoes, paprika, sugar and stock and simmer, 15 min.
  3. Blitz the soup smooth and stir in the cream.
  4. Butter the outsides of the sourdough and fill with the cheddar and mozzarella.
  5. Fry over medium heat until golden on both sides. Cut into fingers and serve with the soup, 8 min.

Boston trip clam chowder

Creamy clam chowder with bacon, potato and celery.

Prep 0 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Simmer the base up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; add the clams and cream when reheating so the clams stay tender
Recipe for 4
  • 3 rashers of bacon, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml fish stock
  • 300g potatoes, diced
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 1kg clams, cleaned
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 box oyster crackers
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp, add the onion and celery and cook until soft, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, potato and thyme and simmer, 12 min.
  3. Add the clams, cover and cook until open, then remove the empty shells, 5 min.
  4. Stir in the cream and heat through. Season with pepper and serve with oyster crackers, 3 min.

Mains

Barton Academy Christmas roast chicken

Roast chicken with sage and lemon breadcrumb stuffing.

Prep 10 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Make the stuffing up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; stuff and roast the chicken on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 80g butter
  • 150g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 small bunch sage, chopped
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 egg
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 350ml chicken stock
  1. Fry the onion in some of the butter, then mix with the breadcrumbs, sage, lemon zest and egg. Stuff the chicken, 10 min.
  2. Rub the chicken with butter and salt and roast at 200°C, 1 hr 20 min.
  3. Roast the potatoes in oil on another tray, 5 min.
  4. Glaze the carrots in butter and honey.
  5. Rest the chicken, then make gravy from the juices with the flour and stock, 10 min.

Mary's family-style Christmas roast

Chuck braised for hours with carrots, onions and potatoes in its own gravy, for the holdovers.

Prep 4 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Braise up to 2 days ahead and reheat in the gravy; it slices better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 4 carrots
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Season the beef well and brown all over in the oil in a heavy pot. Lift out, 12 min.
  2. Fry the onion wedges and garlic for 5 minutes, stir in the tomato paste, then add the stock, Worcestershire and thyme.
  3. Put the beef back, cover and braise in the oven for 2 hours.
  4. Add the carrots and halved potatoes and braise for 1 more hour until everything is tender, 1 hr.
  5. Lift out the beef and vegetables, thicken the juices with the cornflour mixed with a little water and simmer for 2 minutes.
  6. Slice the beef and serve with the vegetables, gravy and chopped parsley.

Mr Hunham's flaming cherries jubileeVegetarian

Cherries warmed with orange and brown sugar, over vanilla ice cream.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the cherry sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; reheat, stir in the vanilla and serve over ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 680g jar pitted cherries
  • 30g butter
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Drain the cherries, keeping some of the syrup.
  2. Melt the butter with the sugar in a wide pan, add the syrup and the orange zest and juice and simmer, 5 min.
  3. Stir in the cornflour mixed with water, then add the cherries and warm through, 3 min.
  4. Off the heat, stir in the vanilla and let the sauce settle for a minute, 3 min.
  5. Spoon over scoops of ice cream.

Drinks

Barton Academy honey lemon teaNo alcohol

Hot black tea, honey, lemon and cinnamon with a clove lemon slice.

Prep 4 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh in the mug; stud the lemon slices with cloves up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 800ml boiling water
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 12 cloves
  1. Boil water in a kettle, 5 min.
  2. Steep a tea bag in each heatproof mug of boiling water for 3 minutes, then remove.
  3. Stir in honey, lemon juice, a drop of vanilla and a cinnamon stick until the honey dissolves.
  4. Stud a lemon slice with cloves and float it on top.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Mr Hunham orders the cherries jubilee.
