Office party beef and onion sausage rolls

Flaky pastry rolled round beef and caramelised onion, baked golden for the office.

Prep 17 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook and cool the onions and mix the filling up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roll, glaze and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 300g beef mince
  • 200g pork mince
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs (1 for the filling, 1 beaten for glazing)
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 200g tomato relish
  1. Fry the onions in the butter until golden, then cool.
  2. Mix the minces with the onions, thyme, breadcrumbs, one egg and salt.
  3. Cut the pastry in half, lay a log of filling along each strip, roll and seal.
  4. Brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame and cut into pieces.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden. Serve with relish.

Baked camembert with honey and rosemaryVegetarian

Hot camembert with rosemary and honey, toast, grapes and fig jam.

Prep 4 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Slice the baguette and slit the cheese up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; add the garlic, rosemary and honey, then bake and serve hot
Recipe for 4
  • 250g camembert
  • 1 garlic clove, in slivers
  • 1 sprig rosemary, in small sprigs
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g grapes
  • 4 tbsp fig jam
  1. Sit the camembert in a small dish and cut slits in the top. Push in garlic and rosemary and drizzle with honey.
  2. Bake at 180°C until soft in the middle.
  3. Brush the baguette slices with oil and toast, 8 min.
  4. Serve the cheese hot on a board with toasts, grapes and fig jam.

Zenotek smash cheeseburger sliders

Smashed beef, melted cheese and pickles on soft buns, for Clay's pitch to Walter Davis.

Prep 3 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Fry the onions and mix the sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; smash and cook the patties just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp American mustard
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill pickles, plus slices to serve
  • 600g beef mince
  • 6 slices of cheddar, halved
  • 12 brioche slider buns
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slowly fry the onions in the butter until jammy.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, mustard and chopped pickles into a sauce.
  3. Roll the mince into balls, smash flat in a very hot pan with the vegetable oil, season and cook, then flip and top each with half a slice of cheddar, 8 min.
  4. Toast the buns and fill with sauce, patty, onions and pickles, 6 min.

Mary from HR's approved roast salmon

Honey mustard roast salmon with crushed potatoes, signed off by Mary from HR, non-denominational.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the honey mustard glaze up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g side of salmon, skin on
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g green beans
  • 20g butter
  1. Boil the potatoes for 18 minutes, drain and crush with the oil and salt.
  2. Mix the mustard, honey, lemon zest and half the chopped dill.
  3. Lay the salmon on a lined tray, spread with the glaze and roast at 200°C for 15 minutes.
  4. Boil the beans for 4 minutes and toss with the butter.
  5. Scatter the salmon with the rest of the dill and serve with lemon wedges, potatoes and beans.

Red and green cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with red and green buttercream.

Prep 22 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 120ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 pack food colouring (red and green)
  1. Beat some of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide into cupcake cases and bake at 180°C, 18 min.
  3. Cool completely, 15 min.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar, tint half red and half green.
  5. Pipe on top of the cupcakes.

Cinnamon vanilla eggnogNo alcohol

Homemade egg custard nog with cinnamon and nutmeg.

Prep 6 min, cook 11 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs (yolks only)
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar.
  2. Heat the milk, cream, cinnamon and vanilla until steaming, then slowly whisk into the yolks.
  3. Return to the pan and stir over low heat until it coats a spoon.
  4. Strain and chill, 2 hr.
  5. Pour into glasses and grate fresh nutmeg over the top.
