Snacks

Harbour prawns with lemon

Chilled king prawns, horseradish cocktail sauce, buttered white bread.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the cocktail sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; pile the prawns on ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg cooked king prawns
  • 2 lemons
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Pile the cooked king prawns on crushed ice with lemon wedges.
  2. Mix the tomato sauce with the horseradish and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Serve with white bread spread with the butter, the way you would on the harbour.

Beef sausages with beer onions

Grilled sausages in buttered bread with beer onion relish.

Prep 4 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Cook the beer onions up to 3 days ahead and keep them in the fridge; reheat while the sausages grill
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 3 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 can lager
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • mustard, to taste
  • tomato sauce, to taste
  1. Slowly cook the sliced onions in the butter for 20 minutes until soft and golden.
  2. Add the brown sugar, the balsamic and the lager and simmer 5 minutes until jammy.
  3. Grill the thick beef sausages on a medium barbecue for 12 to 15 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Butter the slices of white bread, wrap each round a sausage and top with the onion relish, mustard and tomato sauce.

Stunt crew party pies

Mini meat pies with tomato sauce, a pie between takes on set.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • tomato sauce
  1. Little beef and gravy pies in shortcrust with flaky puff lids, baked golden and served hot with plenty of tomato sauce for the crew. Brown 500g beef mince with 1 diced onion. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, 300ml beef stock, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and 1 tbsp tomato paste and simmer 15 minutes until thick. Cool. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 12 rounds from 2 shortcrust sheets and press into a greased muffin tin. Fill with the mince, top with rounds cut from 1 puff pastry sheet, press the edges, brush with egg and bake 20 to 25 minutes.

Mains

Jean-Claude's Bondi fish and chips

Beer-battered flathead fillets with thick double-fried chips.

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the tartare up to 2 days ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml lager
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 125g mayonnaise
  • 2 dill pickles
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips and fry in the oil at 150°C for 6 minutes, then 190°C for 3 minutes.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager into a batter, dip the flathead fillets and fry at 180°C for 4 minutes.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped pickles for tartare and serve with lemon.

Film set beef lasagne

Mince ragù layered with fresh pasta and nutmeg béchamel, baked bubbling, for the Sydney shoot.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 700g passata
  • 125ml red wine
  • 60g butter
  • 60g plain flour
  • 750ml milk
  • nutmeg
  • 50g parmesan
  • 375g fresh lasagne sheets
  • 200g mozzarella
  1. Slow-cooked beef ragù layered with fresh pasta and nutmeg béchamel, topped with mozzarella and parmesan and baked until bubbling and crisp at the edges. Brown 800g beef mince, add 1 diced onion, 1 carrot and 2 garlic cloves, then 700g passata and 125ml red wine. Simmer 45 minutes. Melt 60g butter, stir in 60g plain flour, whisk in 750ml milk and cook until thick, adding nutmeg and 50g parmesan. Heat the oven to 180°C. Layer ragù, 375g fresh lasagne sheets and béchamel three times in a deep dish. Top with 200g mozzarella and bake 45 minutes. Rest 15 minutes.

Sweets

PavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue shell with soft marshmallow centre and cream.

Prep 10 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Bake and cool the meringue in the oven up to 1 day ahead, kept airtight; whip the cream and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the caster sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Fold in the cornflour and the vinegar, shape into a round and bake at 120°C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven.
  3. Top with the whipped cream, the passionfruit and the strawberries.

Drinks

Colt Seavers stunt iced coffeeNo alcohol

Iced milk coffee topped with vanilla ice cream and cream.

Prep 2 min, then 32 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Brew and chill the coffee up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; build with ice, milk, ice cream and cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 50g ground coffee
  • 400ml milk
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the strong coffee from the ground coffee and chill.
  2. Pour into tall glasses with ice and the cold milk, then top each with a scoop of vanilla ice cream and a spoon of whipped cream.

When to serve
