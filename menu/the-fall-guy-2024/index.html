Snacks

Harbour prawns with lemon

Chilled king prawns, horseradish cocktail sauce, buttered white bread

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the cocktail sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; pile the prawns on ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg cooked king prawns
  • 2 lemons
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Pile the cooked king prawns on crushed ice with lemon wedges.
  2. Mix the tomato sauce with the horseradish and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Serve with white bread spread with the butter, the way you would on the harbour.

Beef sausages with beer onions

Grilled sausages in buttered bread with beer onion relish

Prep 12 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook the beer onions up to 3 days ahead and keep them in the fridge; reheat while the sausages grill
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 3 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 can lager
  • 8 slices white bread
  • mustard, to taste
  • tomato sauce, to taste
  1. Slowly cook the sliced onions in the butter for 20 minutes until soft and golden.
  2. Add the brown sugar, the balsamic and the lager and simmer 5 minutes until jammy.
  3. Grill the thick beef sausages on a medium barbecue for 12 to 15 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Butter the slices of white bread, wrap each round a sausage and top with the onion relish, mustard and tomato sauce.

Mains

Jean-Claude's Bondi fish and chips

Beer-battered flathead fillets with thick double-fried chips

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the tartare up to 2 days ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml lager
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 125g mayonnaise
  • 2 dill pickles
  • 1 lemon
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips and fry in the oil at 150C for 6 minutes, then 190C for 3 minutes.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager into a batter, dip the flathead fillets and fry at 180C for 4 minutes.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped pickles for tartare and serve with lemon.

Sweets

PavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue shell with soft marshmallow centre and cream

Prep 20 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Bake and cool the meringue in the oven up to 1 day ahead, kept airtight; whip the cream and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the caster sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Fold in the cornflour and the vinegar, shape into a round and bake at 120C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven.
  3. Top with the whipped cream, the passionfruit and the strawberries.

Drinks

Colt Seavers stunt iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Iced milk coffee topped with vanilla ice cream and cream

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the coffee up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; build with ice, milk, ice cream and cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 50g ground coffee
  • 400ml milk
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the strong coffee from the ground coffee and chill.
  2. Pour into tall glasses with ice and the cold milk, then top each with a scoop of vanilla ice cream and a spoon of whipped cream.
