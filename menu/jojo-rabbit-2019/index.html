Snacks

Rosie's potato pancakesVegetarian

Crisp grated potato and onion pancakes with apple sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g apple sauce
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Grate the potatoes and onion and squeeze out as much liquid as you can.
  2. Mix with the eggs, flour and salt.
  3. Fry heaped spoonfuls in oil until crisp on both sides, in batches.
  4. Serve hot with apple sauce and sour cream.

Yorki's brown bread and radishesVegetarian

Dark rye with salted butter, sliced radishes and chives

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 1 dark rye loaf
  • 100g salted butter, softened
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 pinch flaky salt
  1. Slice the rye loaf thin.
  2. Spread each slice with butter right to the crust.
  3. Top with thinly sliced radishes, snipped chives and flaky salt, then cut into fingers.

Mains

Sausage and cabbage stew

Smoked sausage, potato and cabbage simmered with caraway

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 500g smoked sausages
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1/2 cabbage
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Slice the onion and sausages and dice the potatoes. Shred the cabbage.
  2. Fry the onion in the vegetable oil in a big pot, add the sausages and brown.
  3. Add the potatoes, cabbage, caraway and stock. Simmer until the potatoes are soft and the stew is thick.
  4. Serve with crusty bread.

Captain K's plum cakeVegetarian

Yeast tray cake with baked plums. Feathers for air dynamics.

Prep 25 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Best on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar, for the dough
  • 150ml warm milk
  • 1 egg
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 1kg plums, halved
  • 50g caster sugar, for the topping
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, caster sugar for the dough, milk, egg and butter into a dough.
  2. Prove the dough, which is the resting time.
  3. Press into a lined tray and cover with halved plums, cut side up.
  4. Sprinkle with the caster sugar for the topping and the cinnamon and bake at 190C.
  5. Cool a little before cutting.

Elsa in the walls spiced apple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Warm apple juice with cinnamon, cloves, star anise and orange

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l cloudy apple juice
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 3 strips orange peel
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 red apple
  1. Pour the apple juice into a saucepan with two of the cinnamon sticks, cloves, star anise, orange peel and honey.
  2. Bring just to a simmer, then turn to low and steep without boiling.
  3. Strain into 4 mugs and float a thin apple slice and a cinnamon stick in each. In summer, chill it and top with sparkling water over ice.
