Snacks

Melbourne Cup marquee canapés

Smoked salmon blinis, prawn cocktail shots and prosciutto-wrapped asparagus

Prep 27 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 blinis
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 300g cooked prawns
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 12 asparagus spears
  • 6 slices of prosciutto
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche and smoked salmon.
  2. Toss the prawns in mayonnaise mixed with tomato sauce and spoon into shot glasses.
  3. Blanch the asparagus for a minute.
  4. Wrap in prosciutto.
  5. Arrange everything on platters.

Splashdown festival hot chips

Twice-fried fries tossed in chicken salt, hot gravy for dipping

Prep 12 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in thick chips
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tbsp chicken salt
  • 1 cup hot gravy
  1. Rinse and dry the potato chips well.
  2. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil at 140C until soft, then drain and rest.
  3. Fry again at 190C until golden and crisp.
  4. Toss in chicken salt and serve with gravy for dipping.

Mains

Dad's chops and veg

Grilled rosemary lamb chops, mashed potatoes, peas and pan gravy

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 lamb loin chops
  • 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 800g potatoes, peeled
  • 50g butter
  • 300g peas
  • 250ml beef stock
  1. Rub the chops with rosemary, garlic and salt.
  2. Grill hot on each side and rest.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter.
  4. Cook the peas.
  5. Make gravy in the pan with the juices and stock.

Sweets

Vanilla sliceVegetarian

Crisp puff pastry layers with set vanilla custard and passionfruit icing

Prep 13 min, cook 32 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 750ml milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 80g cornflour
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 passionfruit, pulp
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Bake the pastry flat between two trays until crisp.
  2. Whisk the milk, eggs, sugar and cornflour over heat until very thick.
  3. Pour between the pastry sheets in a lined tin.
  4. Top with passionfruit icing.
  5. Set overnight, then cut with a hot knife.

Drinks

Knock-off lager michelada

Lager over lime, tomato juice, Worcestershire and hot sauce, chilli salt rim

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime juice, tomato juice, Worcestershire and hot sauce up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; rim the glasses, add ice and top with lager to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 limes
  • 120ml lime juice
  • 240ml tomato juice
  • 4 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • 12 drops hot sauce
  • 2 cup ice
  • 4 bottles cold lager
  1. Mix the salt and chilli powder on a plate.
  2. Rub the rims of 4 pot glasses with lime and dip in the salt.
  3. Add ice, lime juice, tomato juice, Worcestershire and hot sauce to each glass.
  4. Top slowly with lager, stir once and add a lime wedge.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Kenny works the Melbourne Cup.
