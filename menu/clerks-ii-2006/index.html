Snacks

Onion ringsVegetarian

Buttermilk-soaked onion rings in a smoky paprika crumb, fried crisp.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 large onions
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  1. Slice the onions into thick rings and soak in the buttermilk.
  2. Dredge in flour mixed with paprika and salt.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry in batches until crunchy.
  4. Serve with tomato sauce stirred with a pinch more smoked paprika.

Donkey show jalapeño poppers

Jalapeños stuffed with cream cheese and cheddar, wrapped in bacon.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fill up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 12 jalapenos
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 6 rashers bacon
  1. Halve the jalapenos lengthways and scrape out the seeds.
  2. Mix the cream cheese with the cheddar and fill each half.
  3. Wrap each in half a rasher of bacon.
  4. Bake at 200°C until the bacon is crisp.

Rooftop chicken salad croissants

Flaky pastries filled with poached breast, grapes and almonds in tarragon mayo, for the ABC number.

How it is made
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 100g red grapes
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 4 large croissants
  • 1 cos lettuce
  1. Buttery croissants filled with poached chicken, celery, grapes and toasted almonds in a lemon and tarragon mayo, with crisp cos leaves. Poach 2 chicken breasts in simmering salted water for 15 minutes, cool and shred. Mix with 2 finely diced celery sticks, 100g halved red grapes, 40g toasted flaked almonds, 120g mayo, the juice of half a lemon and 1 tbsp chopped tarragon. Season well. Split 4 large croissants, line with cos leaves and pile in the chicken salad. Wrap in baking paper and pack in a basket.

Mains

Double cheeseburger

Two smashed beef patties with American cheese and special sauce.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 sesame burger buns
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls. Mix the mayonnaise with tomato sauce.
  2. Smash each flat on a smoking griddle, season and cook, then flip and add cheese.
  3. Stack 2 per toasted bun with pickles, diced onion and sauce.

Mooby's honey chilli crispy chicken burger

Buttermilk soaked thighs fried crackling, brushed with hot sauce, in soft buns with pickles.

How it is made
  • 4 chicken thigh fillets
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • paprika
  • cayenne pepper
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • chilli flakes
  • white vinegar
  • 4 burger buns
  • garlic mayonnaise
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Buttermilk-soaked chicken thighs fried until crackling, brushed with honey chilli, in soft buns with shredded lettuce, pickles and a garlic mayo. Soak 4 chicken thigh fillets in 250ml buttermilk with 1 tsp salt for at least 2 hours. Mix 150g plain flour, 1 tsp paprika, half a tsp cayenne and salt. Dredge and fry in oil at 170°C for 7 to 8 minutes until golden and cooked through. Warm 4 tbsp honey with 1 tsp chilli flakes and a dash of vinegar and brush over the chicken. Toast 4 burger buns, spread with garlic mayo and fill with lettuce, chicken and pickles.

Sweets

Emma's farewell sheet cakeVegetarian

Vanilla sheet cake with buttercream and sprinkles, cut in squares.

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g butter, softened, for the buttercream
  • 300g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter for the cake with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 175°C.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the butter for the buttercream with the icing sugar, spread over and pipe a goodbye message across the top.

Udderly Delicious chocolate shakeNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream, milk and chocolate syrup blended thick.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Blend fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L chocolate ice cream
  • 500ml milk
  • 4 tbsp chocolate syrup
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and chocolate syrup until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into 4 tall paper cups and serve with straws.
