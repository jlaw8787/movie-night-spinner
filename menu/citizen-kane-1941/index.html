Declaration of Principles oysters Rockefeller

Oysters baked under green herb butter. Kane's Declaration of Principles.

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the green butter up to 2 days ahead and chill; top the oysters and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 24 oysters in the half shell
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup flat-leaf parsley
  • 1/2 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 40g fine breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 lemon, in wedges
  • 2 cups rock salt
  1. Blitz the butter, spring onions, spinach, parsley and fennel to a green paste and season.
  2. Spread the rock salt on a tray and nestle the oysters in it.
  3. Top each oyster with a spoon of green butter, then the crumbs mixed with parmesan.
  4. Bake at 230°C until bubbling and golden, and serve with lemon.

Everglades picnic fried chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken, for the fight in the tent at the Everglades picnic

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in buttermilk up to a day ahead; dredge and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, halved
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp Tabasco
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with a big pinch of salt.
  2. Mix the flour with the paprika, garlic powder and pepper, and dredge the chicken well.
  3. Fry in oil at 170°C in batches until deep golden and cooked through.
  4. Warm the honey with the Tabasco and drizzle over the hot chicken.

Mains

Welcome-to-the-Inquirer roast sirloin

Roast sirloin, rare and carved thin. Kane hires the Chronicle's best men.

Prep 25 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Make the pudding batter up to a day ahead; roast the beef on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef sirloin roast
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 140g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 200ml milk
  • 4 tbsp beef dripping or vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  1. Rub the beef with oil, salt and pepper and roast at 220°C, then turn down to 180°C until rare in the middle.
  2. Brush with the mustard and rest under foil.
  3. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk to a smooth batter.
  4. Heat the dripping in a muffin tray at 230°C, pour in the batter and bake until tall and crisp.
  5. Carve the beef thinly and serve with the puddings and horseradish.

Still-hungry lobster Newburg

Lobster in a creamy sherry sauce. Leland asks if Kane is still eating.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Cook and slice the lobster up to a day ahead; make the sauce to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked lobster meat, in chunks
  • 50g butter
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 4 thick slices white bread
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Warm the lobster gently in the butter, then add the lemon juice.
  2. Whisk the cream with the yolks, pour in and stir over low heat until it coats a spoon.
  3. Season with the cayenne, paprika and salt.
  4. Toast and butter the bread, spoon over the lobster and finish with chives.

Rosebud snowfall pavlovaVegetarian

Snow-white pavlova with berries. Kane's last word was Rosebud.

Prep 35 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Bake the meringue a day ahead and leave it in the oven to cool; top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g raspberries
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  2. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar and pile into a round on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120°C, then turn off the oven and let it cool inside.
  4. Whip the cream, spread over the top, pile on the raspberries and dust with icing sugar like snow.

Susan's nightclub highballVegetarian

Rye and ginger ale over ice. Susan won't talk to the reporter.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the ginger beer; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml rye whiskey
  • 800ml ginger beer, chilled
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Fill 4 highball glasses with ice and add 60ml rye to each.
  2. Top with ginger beer and twist a strip of lemon peel over each.
