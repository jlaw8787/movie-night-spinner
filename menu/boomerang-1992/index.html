Strangé's cocktail meatballs

Pork and beef meatballs in chilli grape jelly, for the merger party

Prep 23 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled, or bake and freeze up to 1 month; glaze and serve hot
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork and beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 250ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 150g grape jelly
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, garlic and salt.
  2. Roll into 30 small balls.
  3. Bake on a lined tray at 200°C until browned.
  4. Simmer the sweet chilli sauce with the grape jelly in a wide pan until glossy.
  5. Add the meatballs and turn until coated, then serve hot on toothpicks.

Marcus's smoked salmon pinwheels

Smoked salmon and dill cream cheese pinwheels, rolled tight for the New Orleans business trip

Prep 13 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 200g smoked salmon
  1. Mix the cream cheese with chopped dill and lemon zest and spread over the tortillas.
  2. Lay the smoked salmon over and roll up tightly.
  3. Wrap and chill for 1 hour.
  4. Slice into 2cm pinwheels.

Tyler and Gerard's black bean nachosVegetarian

Smoky beans and cheddar. Tyler and Gerard say he's too picky

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Cook the beans and make the salsa up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g can black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 230g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeños
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 1 lime
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Warm the beans in the oil with the cumin and paprika, mashing a few.
  2. Mix the tomato, onion, lime juice and salt for the salsa.
  3. Layer the corn chips, beans and cheddar in a baking dish and bake at 200°C until melted.
  4. Top with jalapeños, salsa and sour cream.

Date night steak

Eye fillet with creamy potato gratin. Jacqueline watches the basketball instead.

Prep 15 min, cook 89 min · Make ahead: Bake the potato layers up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and reheat; sear and roast the steaks and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eye fillet steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 40g butter
  • 150ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the potatoes thinly.
  2. Layer with cream in a dish, season with salt and pepper and bake at 180°C until tender.
  3. Season the steaks and sear hard in butter.
  4. Roast at 200°C until the centre reaches 54°C, then rest.
  5. Deglaze the pan with red wine and stock and reduce until glossy.
  6. Char the asparagus and serve with the steak and sauce.

Roast turkey with cornbread stuffingOn the printed menu

Brined breast roasted juicy, sage dressing, maple sweet potato, cranberry, Thanksgiving at Marcus's

How it is made
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 1 bag salt
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 300g cornbread
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  • 1 bag plain flour
  1. Brined turkey breast roasted juicy, sliced with cornbread and sage stuffing, maple candied sweet potato, cranberry sauce and a rich pan gravy. Brine a 1.5kg turkey breast overnight in 2L water with 100g salt and 50g sugar. Pat dry, rub with butter and roast at 170°C to 70°C inside, about 75 minutes, then rest. Toss 300g cubed cornbread with a fried onion, celery, sage and 250ml stock and bake 30 minutes. Roast 800g sweet potato chunks with butter and 3 tbsp maple syrup. Make gravy from the pan juices with flour and stock. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Sweet potato pieVegetarian

Sweet potato custard pie, with Star Trek reruns after dinner

Prep 13 min, cook 105 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Roast the sweet potatoes until soft.
  2. Line a tin with the pastry and blind bake until pale golden.
  3. Mash the sweet potato with butter, sugar, eggs, evaporated milk, nutmeg and vanilla.
  4. Pour into the case and bake at 170°C.
  5. Cool.
  6. Whip the cream and spoon on top.

Strangé's French 75Vegetarian

Gin, lemon and sugar syrup topped with sparkling wine in a flute

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead and squeeze the lemons up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; shake and top with sparkling wine to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 360ml cold sparkling wine
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in a little warm water for a quick sugar syrup and cool, and juice the lemons.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and sugar syrup with ice and strain into four flutes.
  3. Top each with cold sparkling wine and add a lemon twist.
