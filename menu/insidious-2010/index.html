Snacks

Specs and Tucker's spiced snack mixVegetarian

Corn chips, pretzels and cheese crackers baked in smoked paprika butter.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g corn chips
  • 100g pretzels
  • 100g cheese crackers
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  1. Toss the corn chips, pretzels and crackers with the butter, paprika and garlic powder.
  2. Bake at 150°C for 20 minutes.
  3. Tip into paper bags labelled like evidence.

The Further fog dipVegetarian

Charred eggplant blended with tahini, garlic and lemon, with warm flatbread.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggplants
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Char the eggplants whole over a gas flame until collapsed, 15 min.
  2. Scoop out the flesh and blend with tahini, garlic, lemon juice and paprika.
  3. Warm the flatbreads and serve with the dip, 7 min.

Séance night smoky devilled eggs

Yolks whipped with mayo, Dijon and pickle juice, piped high, smoked paprika, bacon crumbs and chives.

How it is made
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 3 rashers bacon
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Creamy devilled eggs whipped with mayo, Dijon and a little pickle juice, piped high and topped with smoked paprika, crisp bacon crumbs and chives. Boil 8 eggs for 10 minutes, cool in iced water, peel and halve. Mash the yolks with 4 tbsp mayonnaise, 2 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp pickle juice, salt and pepper until smooth. Fry 3 rashers of bacon until very crisp and crumble. Pipe the yolk mix into the whites, dust with smoked paprika and top with the bacon and snipped chives.

Mains

Lambert family lasagne

Beef ragù and béchamel lasagne in layers. Baked while the metronome ticks.

Prep 40 min, cook 2 hr 45 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 tins of crushed tomatoes
  • 150ml red wine
  • 50g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 600ml milk
  • 300g lasagne sheets
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  1. Brown the mince, add the tomatoes and wine and simmer for 2 hours.
  2. Make a bechamel with the butter, flour and milk.
  3. Layer the meat sauce, lasagne sheets and bechamel in a dish, finishing with mozzarella.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes.
  5. Rest for 20 minutes before cutting.

Elise's chicken noodle soup with dill

Golden thigh broth, egg pasta, lemon and crusty bread, for the psychic who comes to help.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs on the bone
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 carrots
  • 3 celery sticks
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 200g egg noodles
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Chicken thighs simmered with carrot, celery and onion into a golden broth, with egg noodles, fresh dill, lemon and cracked pepper, crusty bread alongside. Put 800g chicken thighs on the bone in a pot with 2 litres water, 1 halved onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks, 2 bay leaves and 2 tsp salt. Simmer gently for 1 hour, skimming. Lift out the chicken, shred the meat and strain the broth. Dice another carrot and celery stick, simmer in the broth for 8 minutes, then add 200g egg noodles for 5 minutes. Return the chicken, add a handful of chopped dill and a squeeze of lemon, and serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

Red-faced cherry pieVegetarian

Sour cherry lattice pie, red face and all.

Prep 33 min, cook 50 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Cool well before cutting
Recipe for 4
  • 800g frozen sour cherries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1 tub vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the cherries with the sugar, lemon juice and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill with the cherries.
  3. Cut the other sheets into strips and weave a lattice on top.
  4. Bake at 190°C for 50 minutes.
  5. Cool, then serve with ice cream, 30 min.

Further blackberry fizzNo alcohol

Muddled blackberries and lemon topped with soda water over ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Muddle and strain the blackberry syrup up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; add ice and soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 250g blackberries
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 800ml soda water
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Muddle the blackberries with the sugar and lemon juice, then strain into a jug.
  2. Fill glasses with ice, pour in the blackberry syrup and top with soda water.
