Snacks

Big Jeff's friesVegetarian

Thin fries fried twice, served with ketchup.

Prep 2 min, cook 23 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak and dry up to 2 hours ahead, fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg russet potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • fine salt, to taste
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin chips and soak in cold water, 30 min.
  2. Drain and dry very well.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C for the first cook until pale and soft, then lift out and rest, 15 min.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until golden and crisp, 8 min.
  5. Salt straight away and serve with tomato sauce in a squeeze bottle.

Tech Noir bar nachosVegetarian

Corn chips with refried beans, cheddar and jalapeños, sour cream and salsa.

Prep 7 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best baked just before serving; have the beans, cheddar and toppings ready in the fridge up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g corn chips
  • 400g refried beans (1 tin)
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1 jar sliced jalapenos
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 jar salsa
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spread the chips over a tray, 3 min.
  2. Dot with the refried beans and scatter over the cheddar and jalapenos.
  3. Bake until the cheese is melted and bubbling, 8 min.
  4. Top with sour cream and salsa and serve at once.

Mains

Big Jeff's burger

Thick chuck cheeseburger with iceberg, tomato, onion and pickles.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; season, griddle and build just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck mince
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Shape the mince into four thick patties. Prep the lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles.
  2. Season just before cooking and griddle on a hot surface to medium, 10 min.
  3. Lay cheddar on top and cover with a lid until melted.
  4. Toast the buns and build the burgers with all the toppings, 3 min.

Kyle's buttermilk fried chicken bucket

Chicken pieces soaked in buttermilk and hot sauce, floured and fried crisp, for a soldier from 2029.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • hot sauce
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  1. Chicken pieces soaked in buttermilk, dredged in peppery spiced flour and fried crisp, served in a paper-lined bucket with hot sauce and pickles. Soak 1.2kg chicken pieces overnight in 500ml buttermilk with 1 tbsp hot sauce and 2 tsp salt. Mix 250g plain flour with 2 tsp paprika, 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp cayenne and 2 tsp pepper. Lift the chicken from the buttermilk, dredge well and rest 10 minutes. Fry in batches at 165°C for 12 to 15 minutes until deep golden and cooked through. Drain on a rack and serve with hot sauce and pickles.

Big Jeff's cherry pieVegetarian

Lattice topped cherry pie with a sweet shortcrust base.

Prep 22 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pitted cherries, fresh or frozen
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Toss the cherries with the sugar, lemon juice and cornflour, 8 min.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill with the cherries.
  3. Cut the other two sheets into strips and weave a lattice over the top.
  4. Bake until deep golden and the filling bubbles. Cool a little before slicing, 50 min.

Drinks

Dark rum Cuba libre

Dark rum, lime and cola over ice with a dash of bitters.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Cut the limes up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; build the drinks over ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 600ml cola
  • 4 dashes of aromatic bitters
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Cut the limes into quarters.
  2. Fill four highball glasses with ice and squeeze two lime quarters into each, dropping the shells in.
  3. Add the rum and a dash of bitters to each glass, top with cold cola and stir once.
