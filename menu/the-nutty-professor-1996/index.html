Hartley's ten million dollar crab cakes

Golden crab cakes with lemon mayo, for Hartley's ten million dollar cheque

Prep 17 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Shape the crab cakes up to a day ahead and chill them on a tray; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked crab meat, picked
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs, plus 60g panko for coating
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise, plus 125g for the remoulade
  • 1 egg
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tsp Old Bay or Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Creole or Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 lemon
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  1. Mix the crab, breadcrumbs, 3 tbsp mayo, egg, spring onion and seasoning gently, and shape into 8 cakes.
  2. Coat in the panko and chill for 15 minutes so they hold.
  3. Stir the 125g mayo with the capers, mustard, hot sauce and a squeeze of lemon for the remoulade.
  4. Shallow fry the cakes until deep golden on both sides, about 3 minutes a side.
  5. Drain and serve with the remoulade and lemon wedges.

Ernie Junior's honey butter cornbreadVegetarian

Ernie Junior laughs his dinner out

Prep 5 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Whip the honey butter up to a week ahead; bake the cornbread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal (polenta)
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 80g butter, melted, plus 80g softened
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Heat a 25cm cast-iron pan in the oven at 200°C.
  2. Whisk the dry ingredients, then stir in the eggs, buttermilk and melted butter until just combined.
  3. Pour into the hot greased pan and bake until golden and springy.
  4. Whip the softened butter with the honey and a pinch of salt and serve with the cornbread in wedges.

Mama Klump's fried chicken with mash

Buttermilk fried chicken with mash. The skin holds all the calories.

Prep 15 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the buttermilk overnight; make the mash up to a day ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken thighs and drumsticks, skin on
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tsp hot sauce
  • 250g plain flour, plus 2 tbsp for the gravy
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled
  • 80g butter
  • 125ml milk
  • 375ml chicken stock
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk and hot sauce for at least 2 hours, or overnight.
  2. Mix the flour with the paprika, garlic powder, cayenne and 2 tsp salt, and dredge the chicken twice, pressing it on.
  3. Fry in 170°C oil in batches, turning, until deep golden and cooked through, about 14 minutes. Drain on a rack.
  4. Boil the potatoes until soft, then mash with 60g butter and the hot milk.
  5. Cook the remaining butter and 2 tbsp flour, whisk in the stock and simmer to a peppery gravy.

Buddy Love's T-bone with baked potato

T-bone with a crisp baked potato. Buddy orders six T-bones at The Scream.

Prep 5 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Bake the potatoes up to a day ahead and reheat; make the creamed spinach on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 T-bone steaks, about 400g each
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 120g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 40g butter
  • 500g baby spinach
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  1. Rub the potatoes with oil and salt and bake at 200°C until crisp outside and soft inside.
  2. Bring the steaks to room temperature, season well and grill on a very hot barbecue or pan to medium-rare, about 5 minutes a side.
  3. Rest the steaks while you wilt the spinach with the butter and garlic, then simmer with the cream and parmesan until thick.
  4. Split the potatoes and fill with sour cream and chives, and serve with the steak and spinach.

Rocky Road hot fudge sundaesVegetarian

Rocky road ice cream sundaes with hot fudge. Buddy's Rocky Road punchline gets Reggie.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to a week ahead and warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l chocolate ice cream
  • 100g mini marshmallows
  • 80g roasted peanuts, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Soften the ice cream for 10 minutes, fold in half the marshmallows and half the peanuts and refreeze.
  2. Heat the cream with the golden syrup until steaming and stir in the chocolate until glossy.
  3. Scoop the ice cream into glasses, pour over the hot fudge and finish with the rest of the marshmallows, peanuts and a cherry.

Sherman's formula lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Butterfly pea lemonade that turns from blue to pink. Sherman tests the formula on himself.

Prep 7 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Brew the blue tea and make the syrup up to 3 days ahead and chill; add the lemon at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 750ml boiling water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 250ml soda water
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Steep the butterfly pea flowers in the boiling water until deep blue, then strain and chill.
  2. Simmer the sugar and 100ml water until dissolved and cool.
  3. Fill 4 glasses with ice, the blue tea and syrup, then top with soda.
  4. Pour in the lemon juice at the table and watch it turn purple.
