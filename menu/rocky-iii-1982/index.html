Italian Stallion tomato bruschettaVegetarian

Grilled Italian bread rubbed with garlic, topped with tomato and basil.

Prep 11 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make the tomato mix up to 3 hours ahead, covered at room temperature; grill the bread and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Italian loaf
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 500g ripe tomatoes
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, plus extra for brushing
  • 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • 50g parmesan, shaved
  • sea salt, to taste
  1. Slice the loaf into 12 pieces and brush with olive oil.
  2. Grill until charred and rub each piece with the cut garlic.
  3. Dice the tomatoes and mix with torn basil, olive oil, balsamic and salt.
  4. Rest the tomato mix.
  5. Pile on the bread just before serving and top with shaved parmesan.

Venice beach fried clams

Beer-battered fried cod with cabbage and lime sour cream in corn tortillas.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime sour cream up to 1 day ahead, chilled; whisk the batter and fry the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cod fillets, cut in strips
  • 300g plain flour
  • 330ml cold lager
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 2 limes
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1/4 small cabbage, shredded
  • 4 tbsp fresh salsa
  1. Whisk the flour with cold lager to a light batter.
  2. Mix the sour cream with lime juice.
  3. Dip the cod strips in the batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until crisp.
  4. Warm the tortillas.
  5. Fill with cabbage, fish, sour cream and salsa.

Chilli lime chicken street tacos

Thighs marinated with garlic, charred and chopped, small corn tortillas, coriander and salsa roja.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken thighs
  • 3 limes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 packet dried chillies
  • 16 small corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Chicken thighs marinated in chilli, lime and garlic, grilled until charred and chopped, on small corn tortillas with onion, coriander and a smoky salsa roja. Marinate 700g chicken thighs for 1 hour in the juice of 2 limes, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin and oil. Grill over high heat for 5 minutes a side and chop. Char 3 tomatoes and a soaked dried chilli, then blitz with a garlic clove and salt for salsa. Warm 16 small corn tortillas and fill with chicken, diced white onion, coriander and salsa.

I pity the fool rematch chicken

Grilled lemon oregano chicken thighs with brown rice and broccoli.

Prep 10 min, cook 51 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g brown rice
  • 2 heads broccoli
  1. Mix lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano and olive oil and coat the chicken.
  2. Marinate for an hour.
  3. Cook the brown rice.
  4. Grill the chicken over high heat until charred and cooked through.
  5. Steam the broccoli florets.
  6. Serve with an extra squeeze of lemon.

Philly Sunday gravy with rigatoni

Pork shoulder, sausages and meatballs simmered hours in garlic basil tomato, save some for Paulie.

How it is made
  • 600g pork shoulder
  • 4 Italian sausages
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 100g parmesan
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Pork shoulder, Italian sausages and meatballs browned and simmered for hours in a garlic and basil tomato sauce, served over rigatoni with parmesan. Brown 600g pork shoulder in chunks and 4 Italian sausages in olive oil. Mix 400g beef mince with an egg, 50g breadcrumbs and parmesan, roll into balls and brown. Soften 4 garlic cloves, add 2 cans crushed tomatoes, 250ml water and basil. Return all the meat and simmer gently for 3 hours, covered. Serve over 500g rigatoni with grated parmesan.

Thunderlips charity cannoliVegetarian

Coffee-dipped ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream.

Prep 27 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 250g mascarpone
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 24 savoiardi biscuits
  • 300ml strong coffee, cooled
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale.
  2. Fold in the mascarpone and whipped cream.
  3. Dip the savoiardi briefly in coffee and layer with the cream in a dish.
  4. Repeat and finish with cream.
  5. Chill overnight.
  6. Dust with cocoa.

Eye of the Tiger lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade with mint over lots of ice.

Prep 14 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the sugar syrup and squeeze the lemons up to 2 days ahead, chilled; add the cold water, mint and ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 6 lemons
  • 1.5 L cold water
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in hot water and cool.
  2. Squeeze the lemons into a big jug.
  3. Add the syrup and cold water.
  4. Bruise the mint and drop it in with plenty of ice.
  5. Stir well and pour into tall glasses.
