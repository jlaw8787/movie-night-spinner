Kitchen raid banana frittersVegetarian

Thick banana slices fried in light batter and rolled in cinnamon sugar.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the cinnamon sugar up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 200ml sparkling water
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 4 bananas
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Whisk the flour, sparkling water and a pinch of sugar into a batter.
  2. Slice the bananas thickly and dip in the batter.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until puffed and golden, 10 min.
  4. Roll in the remaining sugar mixed with cinnamon while hot.

Stampede refried bean nachosVegetarian

Corn chips with refried beans, melted cheese, salsa, sour cream and jalapeños.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheese up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; assemble and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g corn chips
  • 400g refried beans
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 200g tasty cheese
  • 200g tomato salsa
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapenos
  1. Warm the refried beans with the cumin and grate the cheese, 5 min.
  2. Spread the chips on a tray, spoon over the beans and cover with cheese.
  3. Bake at 200°C until melted, 10 min.
  4. Top with salsa, sour cream and jalapenos and serve straight from the tray.

Giant pod pork and chive dumplings

Ginger and soy pork steamed in bamboo over cabbage, chilli oil dip, your turn to roll.

How it is made
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 bunch garlic chives
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 packet round dumpling wrappers
  • 1 small wombok cabbage
  • 1 bottle Chinese black vinegar
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Pork and garlic chive dumplings steamed in a bamboo basket lined with cabbage leaves, with a soy, black vinegar and chilli oil dipping sauce. Mix 400g pork mince with a bunch of finely chopped garlic chives, grated ginger, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp sesame oil and white pepper. Fill 30 round dumpling wrappers and pleat closed. Line a bamboo steamer with cabbage leaves and steam the dumplings over simmering water for 8 minutes. Mix 3 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp black vinegar and 1 tsp chilli oil for dipping.

Van Pelt's juniper venison and redcurrant

Juniper rubbed venison backstrap, seared rare, with redcurrant sauce.

Prep 18 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Rub the venison with the juniper mix up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; sear it and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g venison backstrap
  • 1 tsp juniper berries
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp redcurrant jelly
  • 80g butter
  • 600g parsnips
  • 400g potatoes
  1. Peel and boil the parsnips and potatoes until soft, 20 min.
  2. Crush the juniper with the pepper and thyme and rub over the venison.
  3. Sear in a hot pan, a few minutes a side for rare, 6 min.
  4. Rest the venison, 8 min.
  5. Add the stock and redcurrant jelly to the pan and reduce until glossy, then whisk in a little of the butter, 8 min.
  6. Mash the vegetables with the remaining butter and serve with the sliced venison.

Parrish house New England pot roast

Chuck braised with onion, carrot and potatoes in the pot, worth a 26 year wait.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 800g potatoes
  1. Beef chuck seared and braised slowly with onion, carrot, celery, garlic and thyme in a rich tomato beef gravy, with potatoes cooked in the pot. Season a 1.5kg beef chuck roast and sear all over in a heavy pot. Remove, then soften an onion, 3 carrots, 2 celery sticks and 4 garlic cloves. Stir in 2 tbsp tomato paste and 1 tbsp Worcestershire, add 750ml beef stock and thyme and return the beef. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours. Add 800g halved potatoes and cook 1 hour more. Slice and spoon over the gravy.

New Hampshire maple walnut fudgeVegetarian

Creamy maple fudge studded with toasted walnuts.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 1 hr 5 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml maple syrup
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 40g butter
  • 60g walnuts
  1. Toast and chop the walnuts, 8 min.
  2. Boil the maple syrup, sugar, cream and butter to 115°C, stirring gently, 12 min.
  3. Cool, then beat until thick and losing its gloss, 15 min.
  4. Stir in the walnuts and spread in a lined tin.
  5. Cool and set, then cut into squares, 50 min.

Brantford mulled apple and maple punchNo alcohol

Apple juice warmed with cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and maple syrup, a mug for a long game.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the punch up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat gently without boiling
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 orange
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Warm the apple juice with the cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and maple syrup over low heat without boiling, 15 min.
  2. Strain into four mugs and add a cinnamon stick and an orange slice to each.
