Tijuana prom night tacos

Chopped grilled skirt steak in corn tortillas with tomatillo salsa verde

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Blend the salsa verde up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; grill the steak and warm the tortillas just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g skirt steak
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 400g can tomatillos
  • salt, to taste
  1. Season the steak with salt and grill over high heat.
  2. Rest, then chop finely.
  3. Blend charred tomatillos with coriander, lime and a little onion for a sharp salsa verde.
  4. Warm the tortillas and pile in the steak with diced onion, coriander and salsa.

1950s soda fountain friesVegetarian

Thin double-fried fries in paper boats with tomato sauce

Prep 12 min, cook 16 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cut and soak the potatoes in cold water up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; dry well and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • fine salt, to taste
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries.
  2. Soak in cold water for an hour, then dry well.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C until soft.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until golden and crisp.
  5. Toss with fine salt and serve in paper boats with tomato sauce.

Red chilli pork tamalesOn the printed menu

Light masa round spicy braised pork, steamed firm in corn husks, for the backyard party

How it is made
  • 600g pork shoulder
  • 3 dried chillies
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 16 dried corn husks
  • 150g lard
  • 450g masa harina
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 500ml chicken stock
  1. Masa beaten light with lard and stock, spread on soaked corn husks, filled with pork braised in red chilli, folded and steamed until firm. Simmer 600g pork shoulder in salted water for 2 hours, then shred. Soak 3 dried chillies, blitz with 2 garlic cloves and 1 tsp cumin, and stir through the pork. Soak 16 corn husks for 30 minutes. Beat 150g lard until fluffy, then beat in 450g masa harina, 1 tsp baking powder, salt and 450ml warm stock. Spread masa on each husk, add pork, fold and steam upright for 75 minutes.

Mum's red chilli pork pozole

Pork and hominy stew in a dried chilli broth, topped with cabbage

Prep 20 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 dried chillies
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1kg pork shoulder
  • 2l water
  • 800g can hominy, drained
  • 1/4 small cabbage, shredded
  • 6 radishes
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 limes
  1. Toast the dried chillies and soak in hot water.
  2. Blend the chillies with garlic and a little water.
  3. Simmer the pork in water.
  4. Add the chilli sauce and hominy and simmer.
  5. Shred the pork.
  6. Serve with cabbage, radish, oregano and lime.

Fifties diner double cheeseburgersOn the printed menu

Two smashed patties with American cheese and pickles, from a 1950s diner in the San Fernando Valley

How it is made
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Two thin chuck patties smashed on a smoking griddle, American cheese melted between, with pickles, diced onion, mustard and tomato sauce on toasted soft buns. Roll 800g chuck mince into 8 balls of 100g. Heat a heavy pan or flat plate until smoking. Add the balls, smash flat with a spatula, season with salt and cook 2 minutes until deeply browned. Flip, add a cheese slice and cook 1 minute. Stack two patties on each toasted bun with dill pickles, finely diced white onion, mustard and tomato sauce.

Connie's arroz con lecheVegetarian

Creamy cinnamon rice pudding with lemon peel

Prep 34 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g short grain rice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 lemon
  • 500ml water
  • 750ml full cream milk
  • 190g sweetened condensed milk
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Simmer the rice with the cinnamon stick, a strip of lemon peel and water until absorbed.
  2. Add the milk and stir slowly until creamy.
  3. Stir in the condensed milk and remove the cinnamon stick.
  4. Dust with ground cinnamon.

Del-Fi Records malted shakesVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick vanilla malted milkshakes in tall chilled glasses

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the glasses in the fridge up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  1. Chill four tall glasses.
  2. Blend the ice cream with the milk and malted milk powder until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into the glasses, add a straw to each and serve straight away.
