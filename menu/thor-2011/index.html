Snacks

New Mexico diner hash brownsVegetarian

Crisp pan-fried potato and onion cakes with sour cream.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 80g butter
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp snipped chives
  1. Grate the potatoes and onion, then squeeze out as much liquid as you can. Season well and shape into small flat cakes.
  2. Fry in butter over medium heat until deep golden and crisp, in batches, and serve with sour cream and snipped chives.

Asgard golden honey wings

Crisp roast wings glazed with honey, garlic and thyme butter.

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  1. Toss the wings with salt and roast at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, garlic and thyme.
  3. Toss the hot wings through the glaze until shiny and golden and serve on a big platter.

Green chilli queso

Warm green chilli cheese dip with tortilla chips.

How it is made
  • 1 brown onion
  • 20g butter
  • 2 long green chillies
  • 1 tomato
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 100ml milk
  • 250g cheddar
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 300g corn chips
  1. Melted cheddar and cream cheese stirred with roasted green chillies, tomato and onion, served bubbling hot with warm corn chips. Soften 1 diced onion in 20g butter, then add 2 chopped long green chillies and 1 diced tomato and cook 3 minutes. Lower the heat and stir in 250g cream cheese and 100ml milk until smooth, then add 250g grated cheddar a handful at a time until melted. Season with 1 tsp cumin and salt. Pour into a hot dish, top with coriander and serve with 300g corn chips.

Mains

Asgardian feast beef ribs with roast roots

Beef short ribs roasted 5 hours with carrots, parsnips and potatoes in the juices.

Prep 13 min, cook 5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 4 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 3 parsnips, cut into chunks
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Rub the ribs with salt and cracked pepper.
  2. Roast covered at 140°C for 4 hours.
  3. Add the carrots, parsnips and potatoes to the tray to roast in the juices for the last hour.
  4. Serve on a big board with crusty bread.

Diner pancake, bacon and egg stack

Buttermilk rounds layered with rashers and fried eggs, maple syrup, a god's first diner breakfast.

How it is made
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 40g butter
  • 12 bacon rashers
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  1. Fluffy buttermilk pancakes stacked high with crisp bacon and fried eggs between the layers, with maple syrup poured over the lot. Whisk 300g self-raising flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 eggs and 400ml buttermilk into a thick batter. Cook 12 pancakes in a buttered pan over medium heat, about 2 minutes a side. Fry 12 bacon rashers until crisp and fry 4 eggs sunny side up. Stack 3 pancakes per plate with bacon between the layers and an egg on top, then pour over 125ml maple syrup.

Puente Antiguo churros with hot chocolateVegetarian

Fried churros with a cup of thick hot chocolate for dipping.

Prep 0 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar, for the dough
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 100g caster sugar, for rolling
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  1. Boil the water with the butter, sugar and a pinch of salt, then beat in the flour until it forms a ball.
  2. Cool for 5 minutes and beat in the egg. Pipe long lengths through a star nozzle into oil at 180°C.
  3. Fry until golden, then roll in the cinnamon sugar.
  4. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate, stirring smooth, for dipping.

Drinks

Smashed mug iced coffee floatNo alcohol

Brown sugar iced coffee over vanilla ice cream, cold milk and whipped cream.

Prep 10 min, then 65 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day ahead, build fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml strong coffee
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml cold milk
  • 150ml cream
  • 50g dark chocolate, grated
  1. Brew the coffee, stir in the brown sugar and chill for at least 1 hour.
  2. Put the ice cream into four tall glasses, then pour over the coffee and cold milk.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks, spoon on top and grate over dark chocolate. Serve with a straw and a long spoon.
