Crisp pan-fried potato and onion cakes with sour cream

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 80g butter
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp snipped chives
  1. Grate the potatoes and onion, then squeeze out as much liquid as you can. Season well and shape into small flat cakes.
  2. Fry in butter over medium heat until deep golden and crisp, in batches, and serve with sour cream and snipped chives.

Asgard golden honey wings

Crisp roast wings glazed with honey, garlic and thyme butter

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  1. Toss the wings with salt and roast at 220C until crisp.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, garlic and thyme.
  3. Toss the hot wings through the glaze until shiny and golden and serve on a big platter.

Mains

Asgardian feast beef ribs with roast roots

Beef short ribs roasted 5 hours with carrots, parsnips and potatoes in the juices

Prep 20 min, cook 300 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 4 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 3 parsnips, cut into chunks
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Rub the ribs with salt and cracked pepper.
  2. Roast covered at 140C for 4 hours.
  3. Add the carrots, parsnips and potatoes to the tray to roast in the juices for the last hour.
  4. Serve on a big board with crusty bread.

Puente Antiguo churros with hot chocolateVegetarian

Fried churros with a cup of thick hot chocolate for dipping

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar, for the dough
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 100g caster sugar, for rolling
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  1. Boil the water with the butter, sugar and a pinch of salt, then beat in the flour until it forms a ball.
  2. Cool for 5 minutes and beat in the egg. Pipe long lengths through a star nozzle into oil at 180C.
  3. Fry until golden, then roll in the cinnamon sugar.
  4. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate, stirring smooth, for dipping.

Drinks

Smashed mug iced coffee floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Brown sugar iced coffee over vanilla ice cream, cold milk and whipped cream

Prep 15 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day ahead, build fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml strong coffee
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml cold milk
  • 150ml cream
  • 50g dark chocolate, grated
  1. Brew the coffee, stir in the brown sugar and chill for at least 1 hour.
  2. Put the ice cream into four tall glasses, then pour over the coffee and cold milk.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks, spoon on top and grate over dark chocolate. Serve with a straw and a long spoon.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Thor smashes his mug in the diner and asks for another.
