Snacks

Devils on horseback, bacon-wrapped prunes

Prunes stuffed with blue cheese, wrapped in bacon and baked crisp

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 24 pitted prunes
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 12 rashers streaky bacon, halved
  • 24 toothpicks
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Stuff each prune with a small piece of blue cheese.
  2. Wrap each in half a rasher of bacon and secure with a toothpick.
  3. Bake until the bacon is crisp.

Murderess' Row cheese strawsVegetarian

Twisted puff pastry strips with parmesan and paprika

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 1 pinch of paprika
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a tray. Brush the pastry with egg and cover with parmesan and paprika.
  2. Cut into thin strips like cell bars.
  3. Twist each a few times and lay on the tray.
  4. Bake until puffed and golden.

Onyx Club oysters Rockefeller

Velma's All That Jazz at the Onyx Club, the night it all starts

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the spinach butter up to 1 day ahead; top and bake the oysters just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 oysters, freshly shucked
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 3 spring onions
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 lemon
  • hot sauce, to taste
  • 500g rock salt
  1. Heat the oven grill to high.
  2. Wilt the spinach in a dry pan, squeeze it out and chop finely with the parsley and spring onions.
  3. Mash into the butter with the breadcrumbs, parmesan, a squeeze of lemon and a few drops of hot sauce.
  4. Sit the oysters on a tray of rock salt, top each with the spinach butter and grill for 4 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.

Mains

Razzle dazzle sausage pie

Billy Flynn's courtroom razzle dazzle: mozzarella, sausage and garlicky tomato

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 300g mozzarella, sliced
  • 300g Italian sausage, crumbled
  • 1 tin of crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 60g parmesan, grated
  1. Press the dough mixed with butter into a deep greased tin.
  2. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic.
  3. Layer the mozzarella, then sausage, then tomato.
  4. Finish with parmesan and bake at 220C.
  5. Rest before slicing.

Sweets

Champagne jelly

Mister Cellophane's clear sparkling jelly, raspberries

Prep 20 min, cook 250 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 5 gelatine leaves
  • 150ml water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 500ml cold sparkling wine
  • 1 punnet of raspberries
  1. Soak the gelatine in cold water.
  2. Warm the water with the sugar, squeeze the gelatine and stir in to dissolve.
  3. Cool slightly, then gently stir in the sparkling wine.
  4. Pour into coupes over raspberries.
  5. Chill.

Drinks

Hotel Cicero gin fizzVegetarian

Plenty of ice, unlike Cicero. Gin, lemon and syrup shaken, topped with soda

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml gin
  • 100ml lemon juice
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 4 cup ice
  • 400ml soda water
  1. Make the syrup ahead by dissolving equal parts sugar and hot water, then cool.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and syrup hard with ice.
  3. Strain into tall glasses over fresh ice and top with soda water.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when the murderesses start the Cell Block Tango.
