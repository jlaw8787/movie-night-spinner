Snacks

Devils on horseback, bacon-wrapped prunes

Prunes stuffed with blue cheese, wrapped in bacon and baked crisp.

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 24 pitted prunes
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 12 rashers of streaky bacon, halved
  • 24 toothpicks
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Stuff each prune with a small piece of blue cheese, 8 min.
  2. Wrap each in half a rasher of bacon and secure with a toothpick.
  3. Bake until the bacon is crisp, 15 min.

Mains

Razzle dazzle sausage pie

Sausage, mozzarella and garlicky tomato in a deep crust.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 300g mozzarella, sliced
  • 300g Italian sausage, crumbled
  • 1 tin of crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 60g parmesan, grated
  1. Press the dough mixed with butter into a deep greased tin.
  2. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic, 15 min.
  3. Layer the mozzarella, then sausage, then tomato.
  4. Finish with parmesan and bake at 220°C, 35 min.
  5. Rest before slicing, 10 min.

Billy Flynn's Italian beef rolls

Beef, jus, giardiniera. Five thousand dollars first, then Billy Flynn talks.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef topside
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 4 crusty rolls
  • 1 jar hot giardiniera
  1. Rare roast beef shaved thin and dunked in garlicky oregano jus, piled into crusty rolls with hot giardiniera and roasted sweet capsicum. Rub a 1.2kg beef topside with oil, salt, pepper and oregano and roast at 220°C for 15 minutes, then 180°C to 52°C inside. Rest, chill and slice paper thin. Simmer 750ml beef stock with 4 garlic cloves, 1 tsp oregano, chilli flakes and the roasting juices for 10 minutes. Slice and roast 2 red capsicums. Dunk the beef in the jus and pile into 4 crusty rolls with capsicum and hot giardiniera, spooning over extra jus.

Sweets

Champagne gelatin

Clear sparkling grape gelatin set with raspberries, see-through like Mister Cellophane.

Prep 20 min, 4 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 5 gelatine leaves
  • 150ml water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 500ml cold sparkling wine
  • 1 punnet of raspberries
  1. Soak the gelatine in cold water, 5 min.
  2. Warm the water with the sugar, squeeze the gelatine and stir in to dissolve, 8 min.
  3. Cool slightly, then gently stir in the sparkling wine, 5 min.
  4. Pour into coupes over raspberries.
  5. Chill, 4 hr.
