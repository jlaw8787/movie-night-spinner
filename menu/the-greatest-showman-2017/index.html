Snacks

General Tom Thumb's roasted peanutsVegetarian

Peanuts roasted in the shell with flaky salt and smoked paprika, in paper cones

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peanuts in the shell
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Spread the peanuts on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180C.
  3. Toss hot with flaky salt and smoked paprika.
  4. Roll paper into cones and fill them up.

The Greatest Show caramel popcornVegetarian

Popcorn coated in brown sugar caramel and baked until crisp

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 pinch of salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil and spread on two lined trays.
  2. Boil the sugar, butter and golden syrup, then stir in the bicarbonate of soda and salt.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss.
  4. Bake at 120C, stirring every 15 minutes.

Mains

Jenny Lind opening night roast

Sirloin roast, potatoes, Yorkshire puddings and red wine gravy

Prep 20 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Rest 20 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef sirloin
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 200ml red wine
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Season the beef and roast until 52C inside.
  2. Rest the beef under foil.
  3. Roast the potatoes in oil.
  4. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk and bake in hot oiled tins at 220C.
  5. Reduce the red wine with the pan juices for gravy.

Sweets

Ringmaster's red velvet cakeVegetarian

Phillip takes over as ringmaster. Red velvet, cream cheese frosting.

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp gold sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line two tins.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk and colouring.
  3. Bake in two tins.
  4. Cool, then beat the cream cheese and icing sugar.
  5. Layer and cover the cake and add gold sprinkles.

Drinks

Barnum's champagne cocktailsVegetarian

Sugar cube, bitters, cognac and champagne, sparkling apple juice for non-drinkers

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the champagne up to 1 day ahead; build the cocktails in the coupes just before serving so they stay fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 60ml cognac
  • 1 bottle of cold champagne
  • 1 lemon, peel only
  1. Put a sugar cube in each of 4 coupes and soak with bitters.
  2. Add cognac to each.
  3. Slowly top with cold champagne.
  4. Twist lemon peel over and drop it in. Use sparkling apple juice and a dash of bitters for the younger crowd.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the troupe sings This Is Me.
