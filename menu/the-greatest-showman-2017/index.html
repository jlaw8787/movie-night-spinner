Snacks

General Tom Thumb's roasted peanutsVegetarian

Peanuts roasted in the shell with flaky salt and smoked paprika, in paper cones.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peanuts in the shell
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Spread the peanuts on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180°C, 20 min.
  3. Toss hot with flaky salt and smoked paprika.
  4. Roll paper into cones and fill them up.

The Greatest Show caramel popcornVegetarian

Popcorn coated in brown sugar caramel and baked until crisp.

Prep 13 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 pinch of salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil and spread on two lined trays.
  2. Boil the sugar, butter and golden syrup, then stir in the bicarbonate of soda and salt, 6 min.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss.
  4. Bake at 120°C, stirring every 15 minutes.

Mains

Jenny Lind opening night roast

Sirloin roast, potatoes, Yorkshire puddings and red wine gravy.

Prep 0 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Rest 20 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef sirloin
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 200ml red wine
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Season the beef and roast until 52°C inside, 1 hr.
  2. Rest the beef under foil, 20 min.
  3. Roast the potatoes in oil, 45 min.
  4. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk and bake in hot oiled tins at 220°C, 20 min.
  5. Reduce the red wine with the pan juices for gravy, 10 min.

Swedish Nightingale meatballs

Pork and beef meatballs in creamy gravy, for Jenny Lind, the Swedish singer on her American tour.

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bag breadcrumbs
  • 80ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar ground allspice
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 jar lingonberry jam
  1. Pork and beef meatballs with allspice, browned in butter and simmered in a creamy brown gravy, with buttery mash, lingonberry jam and quick pickled cucumber. Mix 400g beef mince, 300g pork mince, 1 grated onion, 50g breadcrumbs soaked in 80ml milk, 1 egg, half a teaspoon of allspice and salt. Roll 28 balls and brown in butter. Stir 2 tbsp flour into the pan, whisk in 400ml beef stock, 100ml cream and 1 tsp soy, add the meatballs and simmer 10 minutes. Toss sliced cucumber in vinegar, sugar and dill. Serve with mash and lingonberry jam.

Ringmaster's red velvet cakeVegetarian

Red velvet sponge layered with cream cheese frosting.

Prep 23 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp gold sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line two tins, 5 min.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk and colouring.
  3. Bake in two tins, 30 min.
  4. Cool, then beat the cream cheese and icing sugar, 12 min.
  5. Layer and cover the cake and add gold sprinkles.

Sideshow red candy apples

Glossy red candy apples on sticks, with salted peanuts.

How it is made
  • 8 small green apples
  • 8 wooden skewers
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  • 1 bottle red food colouring
  • 60g salted peanuts
  1. Crisp green apples on sticks dipped in a glassy red toffee, half rolled in crushed salted peanuts before it sets. Wash and dry 8 small green apples and push a stick into each. Boil 400g caster sugar, 150ml water, 2 tbsp golden syrup and 1 tsp vinegar to 150°C without stirring, then add red food colouring. Tip the pan and dip each apple, twisting, then roll the bottom in 60g crushed salted peanuts and set on baking paper for 15 minutes.

Drinks

Champagne cocktails

Sugar cube, bitters, cognac and champagne, sparkling apple juice for non-drinkers.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill the champagne up to 1 day ahead; build the cocktails in the coupes just before serving so they stay fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 8 dashes of aromatic bitters
  • 60ml cognac
  • 1 bottle of cold champagne
  • 1 lemon, peel only
  1. Put a sugar cube in each of 4 coupes and soak with bitters, 3 min.
  2. Add cognac to each.
  3. Slowly top with cold champagne.
  4. Twist lemon peel over and drop it in. Use sparkling apple juice and a dash of bitters for the younger crowd.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the troupe sings This Is Me.
