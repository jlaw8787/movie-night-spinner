Boston clam chowder cups

Bacon, potato and clam chowder with milk and cream

Prep 13 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the chowder up to 2 days ahead without the cream and keep it chilled; reheat and stir in the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 brown onion
  • 400g potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 2 cans clams
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 packet oyster crackers
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Dice the bacon, onion and potatoes.
  2. Fry the bacon, then soften the onion and potato in butter.
  3. Add the clams with their juice and the milk and simmer.
  4. Stir in the cream, season well and ladle into small cups with oyster crackers.

Iceman frozen grapesVegetarian

Red and green grapes frozen solid, served straight from the freezer

Prep 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Wash, dry and freeze the grapes on a tray up to 1 week ahead, then bag them; serve straight from the freezer
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green grapes
  • 500g red grapes
  • 1 handful mint
  1. Pull the grapes from the stems, wash and dry well.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and freeze until solid.
  3. Serve from the freezer in a cold bowl with mint leaves.

Boston baked beans on buttered toastOn the printed menu

Navy beans slow cooked with molasses, mustard and bacon, piled high, for Storm and Jean's search

How it is made
  • 400g dried navy beans
  • 200g bacon
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bottle molasses
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 thick-sliced loaf
  • 60g butter
  1. Navy beans slow cooked with molasses, mustard, onion and bacon at 150°C for three hours, piled onto hot buttered toast. Soak 400g dried navy beans overnight, then simmer 45 minutes until just tender and drain, keeping 500ml of the water. Fry 200g diced bacon and 1 diced onion in a heavy pot. Stir in 80ml molasses, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, 2 tbsp tomato paste, the beans and the reserved water. Cover and bake at 150°C for 3 hours, stirring now and then and adding water if dry. Pile onto thick buttered toast.

Alkali Lake beef stew

Beef chuck stewed with red wine, onions and carrots

Prep 27 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Braise the stew up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; boil and mash the potatoes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 375ml red wine
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Dice the onions and carrots.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  3. Soften the vegetables, return the beef with red wine and water to cover and simmer.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with plenty of butter.
  5. Cook the potatoes.
  6. Spoon the stew over the mash.

Xavier school roast chicken and vegOn the printed menu

Roast chicken with potatoes, carrots and gravy. Dinner at the school, before Stryker invades.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Chicken rubbed with butter and thyme, roasted at 200°C for 75 minutes, with roast potatoes, carrots and a gravy made from the pan. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with 40g soft butter, thyme and salt. Toss 800g potatoes and 4 carrots in chunks with oil and salt and spread around the bird. Roast at 200°C for 75 minutes, turning the veg once. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the pan juices, whisk in 300ml chicken stock and simmer until thick. Carve and serve with the roast veg and gravy.

Iceman ice cream sandwichesVegetarian

Chocolate cookies sandwiched around ice cream

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and cool the cookies up to 3 days ahead in an airtight tin; sandwich with ice cream and freeze for up to 1 week
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g plain flour
  • 40g cocoa
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Beat the butter and sugar with the egg, then mix in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Roll into balls and flatten on lined trays.
  3. Bake.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Sandwich thick slabs of ice cream between pairs of cookies.
  6. Freeze until firm.

Iceman frozen apple and mint spritzVegetarianNo alcohol

Apple juice ice cubes in a glass with muddled mint and lime

Prep 12 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the apple juice cubes overnight, up to 1 week ahead; muddle and build the drinks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml cloudy apple juice
  • 1 handful mint
  • 1 lime
  • 1 green apple
  • 400ml soda water
  1. Freeze half the apple juice in ice cube trays overnight.
  2. Freeze.
  3. Muddle mint and a squeeze of lime in the bottom of four glasses. Slice the apple thinly.
  4. Fill with apple ice cubes and apple slices.
  5. Pour in the remaining apple juice, top with soda water and stir gently.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Bobby brings the team home to his parents' place in Boston.
