Cool Runnings (1993) movie night dinner menu

A team of Jamaican sprinters take up bobsledding for the 1988 Winter Olympics. Bobsleds in the snow. Jerk chicken on the table.

The menu is set at Jamaican Bobsled Camp, from the world of the film.

Snacks

Bobsled team beef patties

Flaky pastry filled with spiced beef, thyme and scotch bonnet.

Prep 23 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, or freeze
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1/2 scotch bonnet chilli
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  1. Chop the onion and chilli finely.
  2. Fry the mince with the onion, thyme, chilli, allspice and curry powder, then cool, 12 min.
  3. Cut the pastry into rounds and brush with turmeric water. Fill, fold and crimp.
  4. Bake at 200°C, 25 min.

Plantain chips with lime chilli mayoVegetarian

Thin fried green plantain chips salted with lime zest, chilli mayo to dip.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Chips up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 2 limes
  • 1/4 scotch bonnet chilli
  • 150g whole egg mayonnaise
  • salt, to taste
  1. Peel the plantains by scoring the skin lengthways and prising it off. Slice very thin with a mandoline.
  2. Heat the oil to 175°C and fry in batches until crisp, then drain on paper towel, 12 min.
  3. Toss with salt mixed with lime zest.
  4. Stir the mayonnaise with lime juice and finely chopped chilli for dipping.

Sanka's lucky jerk devilled eggs

Devilled eggs with jerk spice and Scotch bonnet. Sanka kisses his lucky egg before every run.

How it is made
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar jerk seasoning
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  1. Halved boiled eggs with yolks whipped with mayo, jerk seasoning and lime, piped back in and topped with spring onion and a pinch of smoked paprika. Lower 8 eggs into simmering water and cook 10 minutes, then cool in iced water and peel. Halve and tip the yolks into a bowl. Mash with 4 tbsp mayo, 2 tsp jerk seasoning, the juice of half a lime and a pinch of salt until smooth. Pipe or spoon back into the whites. Finish with sliced spring onion and a dusting of smoked paprika. Give one a kiss before eating.

Calgary '88 jerk chicken

Jerk marinated chicken grilled until charred, rice and kidney beans.

Prep 12 min, cook 1 hr 5 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 scotch bonnet chillies
  • 2 tsp allspice
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 4 spring onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 limes
  • 4 chicken marylands
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tin kidney beans
  • 200ml coconut milk
  1. Blitz the chillies, allspice, thyme, spring onions, garlic and lime juice into a paste and coat the chicken.
  2. Marinate in the fridge overnight.
  3. Grill slowly over coals until charred and cooked through, 45 min.
  4. Cook the rice with the drained kidney beans and coconut milk, 20 min.

Curry goat with roti

Goat curry slow cooked with allspice, warm roti.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg diced goat or lamb shoulder
  • 1 jar curry powder
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 scotch bonnet chilli
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 500g potatoes
  • 8 roti
  • 1 jar lime pickle
  1. Goat shoulder marinated in curry powder, garlic and thyme, browned then braised slowly with potato and scotch bonnet, served with warm roti and lime pickle. Toss 1.2kg diced goat or lamb shoulder with 3 tbsp curry powder, 4 crushed garlic cloves, thyme and salt and marinate overnight. Brown in batches in a heavy pot with 2 tbsp oil. Add a diced onion, a whole scotch bonnet and 750ml chicken stock, cover and simmer gently for 2.5 hours. Add 500g diced potato and cook 30 minutes more until the sauce thickens. Warm 8 roti in a dry pan and serve with lime pickle.

Jamaican coconut toto cakeVegetarian

Spiced coconut tray cake with nutmeg and ginger.

Prep 4 min, cook 56 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g soft butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 100g desiccated coconut
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 2 limes
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 200ml coconut cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 20 by 30cm tray, 5 min.
  2. Beat the butter with the brown sugar, add the eggs, then stir in the flour, baking powder, ground nutmeg, ground ginger, coconut and coconut milk, 12 min.
  3. Bake, 35 min.
  4. Simmer the lime juice with the caster sugar and pour over the warm cake, 4 min.
  5. Serve with whipped coconut cream.

Cool Runnings sorrel ginger punchNo alcohol

Hibiscus steeped with ginger, cloves and orange peel, sweetened and chilled.

Prep 4 min, cook 1 hr 8 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 30g dried hibiscus flowers
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 2 oranges
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 2 limes
  • 1.2 L water
  1. Bring the water to the boil with the hibiscus, sliced ginger, cloves and the peel of one orange, 8 min.
  2. Turn off the heat and steep, then strain, 1 hr.
  3. Stir in the sugar until dissolved and chill, 1 hr.
  4. Mix with the pineapple juice and lime juice. Pour over ice and garnish with orange slices.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the team lands in Calgary.
