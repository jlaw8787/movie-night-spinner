Bodega beef patties

Turmeric pastry turnovers of curried beef mince, grabbed between Simon's riddles

Prep 20 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400g beef mince
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 125ml beef stock
  • 30g dry breadcrumbs
  • 4 sheets frozen shortcrust pastry
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  1. Soften the onion in the oil, then brown the mince with the curry powder, thyme and salt.
  2. Add the stock and simmer until almost dry, then stir in the breadcrumbs and cool.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brush the pastry sheets with turmeric mixed into the beaten egg and cut two 12cm rounds from each.
  4. Spoon the filling onto half of each round, fold over and crimp with a fork. Brush the tops with the rest of the egg.
  5. Bake on lined trays until golden and flaky, about 25 minutes. Serve hot with hot sauce.

Bacon egg and cheese on a roll

Bacon, fried egg and American cheese in a toasted roll, for the 3 train to Wall Street.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 kaiser rolls
  • 20g butter
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  2. Fry the eggs in the same pan, breaking the yolks, and lay a slice of cheese on each to melt.
  3. Split, butter and toast the rolls. Fill with bacon and egg, season and add tomato sauce.

Harlem chicken and waffles

Fried chicken on waffles with maple syrup, in Zeus's part of Harlem.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chicken soaks up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g boneless chicken thighs
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 300g waffle mix
  • 100ml honey
  • 2 tsp hot sauce
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk and salt.
  2. Soak in the fridge.
  3. Dredge in the flour mixed with paprika and make the waffle batter.
  4. Fry the chicken in oil at 175°C in batches until at least 74°C inside.
  5. Cook the waffles in a waffle iron. Warm the honey with the hot sauce and drizzle over the chicken on the waffles.

Zeus's salted peanut caramel slabVegetarian

Chewy brown sugar caramel set with roasted peanuts, topped with milk chocolate and flaky salt

Prep 5 min, cook 13 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 200g roasted salted peanuts
  • 150g milk chocolate
  • flaky sea salt, to taste
  1. Line a 20cm square tin. Heat the sugar, butter, cream and golden syrup, stirring, then boil to 118°C.
  2. Stir in the peanuts and pour into the tin.
  3. Cool.
  4. Spread with melted chocolate and sprinkle with flaky salt.
  5. Chill, then cut into squares.

Crumb cakeVegetarian

Butter cake under a thick cinnamon crumble, icing sugar, for the counter at the electrical shop

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 205g butter
  • 125g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Rub the plain flour, brown sugar, cinnamon and cold butter into big crumbs.
  2. Beat more butter with the caster sugar, add the eggs and fold in the self-raising flour and milk.
  3. Spread in a lined 20cm tin and cover with the crumble.
  4. Bake at 180°C. Dust with icing sugar.

Water jug lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemon juice and sugar syrup topped up cold, solve the fountain jugs first

Prep 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 300ml hot water
  • 400ml lemon juice
  • 3.4l cold water
  • 1kg ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Juice about 10 lemons.
  3. Chill the syrup, add the lemon juice and top up with cold water to exactly 4 litres. Serve over ice.

When to serve

Pour drinks when Simon sends McClane and Zeus to the park fountain.
