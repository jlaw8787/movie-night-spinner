Snacks

Fireplace shop hot nutsVegetarian

Cashews and peanuts roasted with honey, chilli and smoked paprika.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cashews
  • 200g peanuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Toss the cashews and peanuts with the honey, chilli flakes and paprika.
  2. Spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170°C until glossy, 12 min.
  4. Cool until crunchy, 20 min.

Feta cucumber roundsVegetarian

Cucumber rounds topped with whipped feta and yoghurt.

Prep 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 150g feta
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • cracked pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the cucumbers into thick rounds.
  2. Whip the feta, yoghurt and a squeeze of lemon until smooth.
  3. Pipe or spoon onto each round.
  4. Finish with dill and cracked pepper.

Firetown harissa flatbreads with labneh

Yoghurt dough brushed with chilli oil, grilled charred at the edges, with thick strained yoghurt.

How it is made
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 jar harissa
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 250g labneh
  • 1 jar za'atar
  1. Flatbreads brushed with harissa stirred into olive oil, grilled until charred at the edges, torn and served with thick labneh. Mix 300g self-raising flour with 300g Greek yoghurt and a pinch of salt into a soft dough and knead 1 minute. Divide into 4 and roll each into a thin oval. Stir 2 tbsp harissa into 3 tbsp olive oil. Cook the flatbreads on a very hot dry pan or grill for 2 minutes a side until puffed and charred at the edges, brushing with the harissa oil as they come off. Spread 250g labneh on a plate, drizzle with oil and za'atar and serve with the torn flatbreads.

Mains

Bernie's fiery lamb

Lamb shoulder braised with harissa, cumin, tomato and chickpeas.

Prep 23 min, cook 3 hr · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp harissa
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 250ml water
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Dice the lamb and brown it in the oil in batches, 15 min.
  2. Stir in the harissa and cumin.
  3. Add the tomatoes, chickpeas and water.
  4. Cover and braise at 160°C until it pulls apart, 3 hr.
  5. Warm the flatbread and serve alongside, 3 min.

Vivisteria pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue topped with whipped cream, blueberries and edible purple flowers.

Prep 24 min, cook 1 hr 30 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Meringue up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs, whites only
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g blueberries
  • 1 punnet edible purple flowers
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Shape into a round and bake at 120°C, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Cool in the oven with the door closed, 1 hr.
  5. Whip the cream, top the pavlova with cream, blueberries and purple flowers.

Firetown hot spiced appleNo alcohol

Apple juice warmed with cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and ginger.

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Infuse the juice with the spices up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat, strain into mugs and add a cinnamon stick to each
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L apple juice
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 orange
  • 1 piece ginger (3cm), sliced
  1. Peel strips of zest from the orange and slice the ginger.
  2. Heat the apple juice with 2 cinnamon sticks, the cloves, zest and ginger until steaming, 8 min.
  3. Keep warm so the flavours infuse, 10 min.
  4. Strain into mugs and add a cinnamon stick to each.
