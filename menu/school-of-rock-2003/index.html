Snacks

Mr. S's ham and egg sandwiches

Small ham, cheese and pickle and egg and lettuce sandwiches, each labeled with a name

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Boil and mash the eggs and make all the sandwiches up to 4 hours ahead; wrap and keep chilled until needed
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 100g leg ham
  • 100g cheddar
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool, peel and mash with the mayonnaise.
  2. Make ham, cheddar and pickle sandwiches, and egg and lettuce sandwiches, on the white bread.
  3. Cut small, wrap each in baking paper and write a name on it in marker.
  4. Chill until needed.

Gold star cheese starsVegetarian

Crisp cheddar and paprika shortbread stars

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 120g vintage cheddar
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 egg
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then add the grated cheddar and paprika.
  2. Bring together with a splash of water, then chill briefly.
  3. Roll thin, cut into stars and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 180C until golden.

Mains

Beat The Man pizza

Thin crust pepperoni and mozzarella pizza drizzled with chilli honey

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Dough can be stretched 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Stretch the dough into two thin bases.
  2. Spread with crushed tomatoes and top with mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Bake on a hot steel at maximum heat.
  4. Warm the honey with chilli flakes and drizzle over.

Sweets

Guitar-shaped cookiesVegetarian

Cutout sugar cookies shaped like guitars, iced in bright colours

Prep 40 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Decorate the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 bottle red food colouring
  • 1 bottle black food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and sugar with one egg, then mix in the flour to a dough.
  2. Roll out, cut into guitar shapes and bake at 180C until firm.
  3. Cool on a rack.
  4. Mix the icing sugar with the second egg white and a little water, colour some red and black and decorate.

Drinks

Horace Green raspberry fruit punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Raspberry, pineapple and orange juice punch topped with lemonade

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Crush the raspberries and mix in the lime and fruit juices up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice, lemonade, orange and mint just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g raspberries
  • 2 limes
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 750ml cold lemonade
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Lightly crush the raspberries in a big jug with the lime juice.
  2. Add the pineapple and orange juices and stir.
  3. Just before serving, fill with ice, top with lemonade and add sliced orange and mint.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the class gets their band parts.
