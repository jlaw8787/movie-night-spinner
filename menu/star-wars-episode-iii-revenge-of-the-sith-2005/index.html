Snacks

Kashyyyk garlic mushrooms on sourdoughVegetarian

Butter-fried mushrooms with garlic and thyme on toast, shaved parmesan

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 50g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 4 thick slices sourdough
  • 50g parmesan, shaved
  1. Fry the mushrooms in the butter over high heat until golden. Add the garlic and thyme and cook another minute.
  2. Toast the sourdough, pile the mushrooms on top and finish with parmesan and a crack of pepper.

Utapau creamy chicken vol-au-vents

Chicken, leek and tarragon in cream, baked in puff pastry cases

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 16 vol-au-vent cases
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 300g chicken breast, finely diced
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp chopped tarragon
  1. Soften the leek in the butter, add the chicken and cook through.
  2. Stir in the cream and tarragon and simmer until thick.
  3. Spoon into the cases and bake at 200°C for 10 minutes until golden and hot.

Galaxies Opera House smoked salmon blinisOn the printed menu

Warm little pancakes, lemony crème fraîche, cured fish, roe and chives on a dark tray

How it is made
  • 16 blinis
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 jar salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Warm blinis topped with smoked salmon, lemony crème fraîche, salmon roe and chives, served on a dark tray like the box at the opera. Warm 16 blinis at 160°C for 5 minutes. Mix 150g crème fraîche with the zest of a lemon, salt and pepper. Top each blini with a spoon of the crème fraîche and a fold of smoked salmon from 200g. Finish with a small spoon of salmon roe and snipped chives. Serve straight away on a dark tray.

Mains

Coruscant opera house roast duck

Five spice roast duck brushed with an orange, honey and soy glaze

Prep 10 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg whole duck
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp five spice
  • 2 oranges, juiced
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 star anise
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Prick the duck all over and rub with salt and five spice. Set on a rack in a tray.
  2. Roast at 180°C for 1 hour 30 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile simmer the orange juice, honey, soy and star anise until syrupy.
  4. Brush the glaze over the duck and roast another 15 minutes. Steam the rice.
  5. Rest the duck for 10 minutes, carve and serve over rice with the rest of the glaze.

Senate chamber beef fillet, red wine jusOn the printed menu

Roasted medium rare, crunchy potato gratin and honey carrots, served as the Empire is declared

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 40g parmesan
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 200ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 20g butter
  1. Eye fillet roasted to medium rare and sliced, with a glossy red wine jus, a crunchy-topped potato gratin and honey roasted carrots. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes with 300ml cream, garlic and salt and bake at 180°C for 1 hour, adding 40g grated parmesan for the last 15 minutes. Roast 500g carrots with oil and honey. Sear a 1kg eye fillet on all sides, then roast at 220°C to 54°C inside, about 20 minutes, and rest. Reduce 200ml red wine with 300ml beef stock and thyme to a glossy jus and whisk in 20g butter.

Sweets

Mustafar lava cakesVegetarian

High ground: molten dark chocolate cakes, berry coulis

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day ahead in moulds, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 3 eggs, plus 3 yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 50g plain flour
  • 250g raspberries
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter. Whisk the eggs, yolks and sugar until thick, then fold in the chocolate and flour.
  2. Pour into greased moulds and bake at 200°C for 10 minutes.
  3. Blend the raspberries and strain into a coulis. Turn out the cakes and pour the coulis over.

Twin sunset cocktailVegetarianNo alcohol

Orange and pineapple juice over ice with grenadine sunk to the bottom, no alcohol

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 4 tbsp grenadine
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice. Pour in equal parts of the orange and pineapple juice, leaving room at the top.
  2. Slowly pour a splash of grenadine down the side so it sinks and glows red and orange like a double sunset.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Anakin and Obi-Wan rescue the Chancellor.
