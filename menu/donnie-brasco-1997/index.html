Snacks

Miami Garlic Prawns

Seared with garlic, chilli flakes, white wine and butter. Bread for the juices.

Prep 12 min, cook 3 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 80ml white wine
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Butterfly the prawns.
  2. Heat the olive oil until very hot, add the prawns with garlic and chilli and cook.
  3. Add the wine, bubble for a minute, then swirl in the butter and parsley.
  4. Serve in the pan with crusty bread for the juices.

Mozzarella KatsuVegetarian

Cheese fingers crumbed twice and fried golden, warm tomato sauce to dip.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g low-moisture mozzarella
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 250ml passata
  • 1 pinch dried oregano
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Cut the mozzarella into 16 fingers and freeze for 20 minutes.
  2. Dip in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, then egg and crumbs again.
  3. Fry in the neutral oil at 180°C for 90 seconds in batches until golden.
  4. Warm the passata with salt and oregano for dipping.

Mains

Teriyaki Sirloin

Seared hot, glazed with soy, mirin and sake. Steamed rice, bok choy.

Prep 8 min, cook 30 min
Recipe for 4
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 80ml mirin
  • 60ml sake
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 800g sirloin steaks, 4 pieces
  • 400g short grain rice
  • 4 baby bok choys
  1. Simmer the soy, mirin, sake and sugar for 5 minutes until syrupy.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Sear the steaks in a smoking pan for 3 minutes a side, brushing with glaze in the last minute.
  4. Rest for 5 minutes, slice, and blanch the bok choy to serve alongside.

Sweets

Ricotta CheesecakeVegetarian

Baked slow with lemon zest and vanilla, cooled in the oven.

Prep 8 min, cook 90 min, then 2 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 750g fresh ricotta
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • icing sugar, to dust
  1. Drain the ricotta in a sieve for 2 hours.
  2. Beat with sugar, eggs, lemon zest and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Pour into a lined 22cm springform tin and bake at 160°C for 60 minutes.
  4. Turn off the oven and cool inside with the door ajar. Dust with icing sugar to serve.

Drinks

Social Club Rob Roy

Scotch, sweet vermouth and bitters over one big cube, orange peel, a cherry.

Prep 4 min
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml blended Scotch
  • 80ml sweet vermouth
  • 8 dashes of aromatic bitters
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 orange
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Put four heavy tumblers in the freezer for 15 minutes.
  2. Stir the Scotch, vermouth and bitters with ice in a jug for 30 seconds.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in each tumbler, twist orange peel over and drop it in, and add a cherry.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Lefty first sizes Donnie up over the fake diamond.
