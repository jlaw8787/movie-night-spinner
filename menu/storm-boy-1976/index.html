Snacks

Coorong smoked mullet pâté on toast

Smoked mullet whipped with cream cheese and lemon, piled on hot toast.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the pâté up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; toast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g smoked mullet fillets
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 cucumber
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Flake the smoked mullet into a bowl, taking out every bone and the skin.
  2. Beat in the cream cheese, sour cream, lemon zest and juice, chives and pepper until creamy but rough.
  3. Toast the sourdough, slice the cucumber and cut the lemon into wedges.
  4. Pile the pâté on the toast and serve with cucumber and lemon.

Coorong garlic chilli pippis

Pipis steamed open in garlic, chilli, lemon butter, with crusty bread

Prep 9 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the pipis in cold salted water up to 2 hours ahead, then rinse; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pipis
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 100ml fish stock
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Soak the pipis in cold salted water to purge the sand, then rinse.
  2. Slice the garlic and chilli.
  3. Fry the garlic and chilli in olive oil.
  4. Add the pipis and fish stock, cover and steam until they open, discarding any that stay shut.
  5. Stir in butter, lemon juice and parsley.
  6. Serve with crusty bread.

Hideaway Tom's crisp fish and chips

Battered white fish with chicken salt chips and homemade tartare

Prep 14 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Eat straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut chips
  • 180g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml soda water
  • 4 firm white fish fillets
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 jar of dill pickles
  • 1 bunch of dill
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 2 lemons
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder and cold soda water into a batter.
  3. Dust the fish fillets in flour and dip in the batter.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until golden.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped capers, pickles and dill for tartare.
  6. Toss the chips in chicken salt and serve wrapped in paper with lemon.

Sweets

Shack baked honey applesVegetarian

Apples baked soft with honey, sultanas and cinnamon, with cream.

Prep 15 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Stuff the apples up to 4 hours ahead; bake just before dinner
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Granny Smith apples
  • 60g sultanas
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100ml hot water
  • 300ml pouring cream
  1. Core the apples and score a line around the middle of each.
  2. Mix the sultanas, butter, honey and cinnamon and pack into the apples.
  3. Stand the apples in a dish with the hot water and bake at 180°C, spooning the juices over twice.
  4. Rest 5 minutes and serve warm with cream.

Drinks

Coorong shack black tea with honeyVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong black tea with honey and milk, poured hot from the pot.

Prep 7 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 1.2l water
  1. Boil the water and pour it over the tea bags in a large pot.
  2. Steep for 4 minutes, stir in the honey and warm the milk.
  3. Pour into mugs and serve with the milk on the side.
