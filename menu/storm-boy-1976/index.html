Snacks

Fingerbone Bill's golden syrup damperVegetarian

Bill names him Storm Boy. Damper, butter, golden syrup, lemon myrtle salt

Prep 22 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/2 tsp ground lemon myrtle
  • 100g butter for serving
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour and salt until it looks like breadcrumbs.
  2. Stir in the milk to make a soft dough and knead briefly.
  3. Shape into a round, cut a cross in the top and dust with flour.
  4. Bake at 200C until it sounds hollow when tapped.
  5. Mix the sea salt with ground lemon myrtle.
  6. Serve warm with butter, golden syrup and the salt.

Coorong garlic chilli pippis

Pipis steamed open in garlic, chilli, lemon butter, with crusty bread

Prep 9 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the pipis in cold salted water up to 2 hours ahead, then rinse; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pipis
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 100ml fish stock
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Soak the pipis in cold salted water to purge the sand, then rinse.
  2. Slice the garlic and chilli.
  3. Fry the garlic and chilli in olive oil.
  4. Add the pipis and fish stock, cover and steam until they open, discarding any that stay shut.
  5. Stir in butter, lemon juice and parsley.
  6. Serve with crusty bread.

Hideaway Tom's crisp fish and chips

Battered white fish with chicken salt chips and homemade tartare

Prep 14 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Eat straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut chips
  • 180g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml soda water
  • 4 firm white fish fillets
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 jar of dill pickles
  • 1 bunch of dill
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 2 lemons
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Bake the chips at 220C.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder and cold soda water into a batter.
  3. Dust the fish fillets in flour and dip in the batter.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180C until golden.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped capers, pickles and dill for tartare.
  6. Toss the chips in chicken salt and serve wrapped in paper with lemon.

Beach shack golden syrup dumplingsVegetarian

Soft dumplings poached in hot golden syrup sauce

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 80ml full cream milk
  • 125ml golden syrup
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the egg and milk to a soft dough.
  2. In a wide pan, bring the water, golden syrup, brown sugar and butter to the boil.
  3. Drop in spoonfuls of dough.
  4. Cover and simmer gently without lifting the lid.
  5. Spoon into bowls with the sauce and serve with vanilla ice cream.

Shack lemon myrtle billy teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong black tea with lemon myrtle and honey, brewed in a billy

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Brew fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1 tsp dried lemon myrtle
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 300ml full cream milk
  1. Bring the water to the boil in a billy or saucepan.
  2. Add the tea bags, lemon myrtle and a clean gum leaf if you have one, and take off the heat.
  3. Steep, then remove the bags and leaf.
  4. Stir in the honey.
  5. Pour into enamel mugs and serve with milk on the side.
