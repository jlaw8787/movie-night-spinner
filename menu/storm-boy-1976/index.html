Snacks

Fingerbone Bill's smoked Coorong mullet

Smoked mullet on buttered toast with lemon and pepper. Bill knows where the fish run.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Flake the fish up to a day ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 400g hot-smoked mullet or other smoked fish
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Flake the fish, discarding the skin and bones.
  2. Toast and butter the sourdough.
  3. Pile on the fish and finish with lemon juice, pepper and chopped parsley.

Coorong garlic chilli pipis

Pipis steamed open in garlic, chilli, lemon butter, with crusty bread.

Prep 9 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the pipis in cold salted water up to 2 hours ahead, then rinse; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pipis
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 100ml fish stock
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Soak the pipis in cold salted water to purge the sand, then rinse.
  2. Slice the garlic and chilli.
  3. Fry the garlic and chilli in olive oil.
  4. Add the pipis and fish stock, cover and steam until they open, discarding any that stay shut.
  5. Stir in butter, lemon juice and parsley.
  6. Serve with crusty bread.

Mains

Hide-Away Tom's crisp fish and chips

Battered white fish with chicken salt chips and homemade tartare.

Prep 12 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Eat straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut chips
  • 180g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml soda water
  • 4 firm white fish fillets
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 jar of dill pickles
  • 1 bunch of dill
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder and cold soda water into a batter.
  3. Dust the fish fillets in flour and dip in the batter.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until golden.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped capers, pickles and dill for tartare.
  6. Toss the chips in chicken salt and serve wrapped in paper with lemon.

Sweets

Hide-Away Tom's apple turnoversVegetarian

Puff pastry turnovers of apple and raisin with a sugar crust. Baked in the shack's old wood stove.

Prep 0 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold up to 4 hours ahead and chill; bake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green apples, peeled and diced
  • 40g raisins
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Cook the apple, raisins, 2 tbsp of the sugar and the cinnamon with a splash of water until soft, then cool.
  2. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the pastry into quarters, spoon on the apple, fold into triangles and press the edges.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with the rest of the sugar and bake until puffed and golden.

Drinks

Shack gum leaf billy teaNo alcohol

Strong black tea brewed in a billy with a gum leaf, honey and milk on the side.

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Brew fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 300ml full cream milk
  1. Bring the water to the boil in a billy or saucepan.
  2. Add the tea bags and a clean gum leaf, and take off the heat.
  3. Steep, then remove the bags and leaf.
  4. Stir in the honey.
  5. Pour into enamel mugs and serve with milk on the side.
