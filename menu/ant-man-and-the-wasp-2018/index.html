Snacks

Shrinking lab mini quiches

Puff pastry cups filled with ham, cheddar and egg custard.

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 4 eggs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 100g leg ham, diced
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  1. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a greased mini muffin tray.
  2. Whisk the eggs and cream, then stir through the ham and cheddar.
  3. Fill the cases.
  4. Bake at 190°C until puffed and set, 18 min.

Luis's chips and guacamoleVegetarian

Warm corn chips with chunky guacamole and pico de gallo at the X-Con office.

Prep 12 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the pico de gallo up to 4 hours ahead; mash the avocado just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 red onion, finely diced
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 300g corn chips
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C and warm the corn chips on a tray for 5 minutes.
  2. Mash the avocados roughly with half the lime juice, a third of the onion and salt.
  3. Mix the tomatoes, the rest of the onion, the jalapeño, chopped coriander and the rest of the lime juice for the pico de gallo, 6 min.
  4. Spoon the pico de gallo over the guacamole and serve with the warm chips.

Mains

Luis's truth serum tacos

Grilled chipotle lime chicken in corn tortillas with pineapple salsa.

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min, 1 hr 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 chipotle chillies in adobo, chopped
  • 3 limes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 pineapple, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Marinate the chicken in the chipotle, lime juice and salt, 10 min.
  2. Leave in the fridge to marinate, 1 hr.
  3. Grill the chicken until charred and slice, 10 min.
  4. Mix the pineapple with onion and lime juice for salsa.
  5. Fill warm tortillas with chicken, salsa and sour cream, 3 min.

House arrest salami and mushroom pizza

Thin crisp bases, garlicky tomato, mozzarella, hot cured sausage, mushrooms and olives.

How it is made
  • 1kg pizza dough
  • 250ml passata
  • 1 head garlic
  • dried oregano
  • 400g mozzarella
  • 150g hot salami
  • 150g mushrooms
  • black olives
  • chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Thin crisp bases with garlicky tomato sauce, mozzarella, hot salami, sliced mushrooms and olives, blistered in a hot oven, with chilli flakes. Divide 1kg pizza dough into 4 and stretch into thin rounds. Mix 250ml passata with 1 crushed garlic clove, oregano and salt and spread over the bases. Top with 400g grated mozzarella, 150g sliced hot salami, 150g sliced mushrooms and a handful of olives. Bake on hot trays at 250°C for 10 minutes until blistered. Finish with chilli flakes and torn basil.

Giant-size chocolate chip pan cookieVegetarian

One big chocolate chip cookie baked in a pan, soft in the center.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, warm before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g plain flour
  • 150g dark chocolate chips
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then beat in the egg.
  2. Mix in the flour and chocolate chips.
  3. Press into a greased ovenproof pan.
  4. Bake at 180°C until golden at the edges and soft in the centre, 20 min.
  5. Cut into wedges.

Quantum realm layered Jell-O cups

Raspberry, lime, blue and orange Jell-O layers topped with whipped cream.

Prep 38 min, 4 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 packets of jelly crystals (raspberry, lime, blue, orange)
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Make up the raspberry jelly with half the usual water and pour a thin layer into clear glasses.
  2. Chill until set, 1 hr.
  3. Repeat with the lime, then blue, then orange jelly, letting each set before the next goes on, 20 min.
  4. Chill the finished glasses until firm, 3 hr.
  5. Top with whipped cream just before serving.

San Francisco custard egg tarts

Flaky custard egg tarts, for racing round the steep streets of San Francisco.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 5 eggs
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 150ml milk
  • 100ml cream
  • vanilla extract
  1. Flaky pastry cases filled with silky sweet egg custard and baked until just set and lightly caramelised on top, served warm. Cut 12 rounds from 2 sheets of puff pastry and press into a greased muffin tin. Whisk 3 eggs, 2 egg yolks, 80g caster sugar, 150ml milk, 100ml cream and 1 tsp vanilla, then strain. Pour into the cases until three quarters full. Bake at 220°C for 15 to 18 minutes until the custard is just set with a slight wobble and spotted brown on top. Cool 10 minutes before serving.

Drinks

House arrest root beer floatsNo alcohol

Root beer poured over vanilla ice cream in frosted mugs.

Prep 5 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Freeze the mugs up to 1 day ahead; add ice cream and root beer just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 1 L root beer
  • 4 spoons whipped cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Freeze the glass mugs for an hour.
  2. Put ice cream into each frozen mug.
  3. Tilt the mug and slowly pour in cold root beer down the side, letting it foam, then top up as it settles.
  4. Add whipped cream and a cherry and serve with a straw and long spoon.
