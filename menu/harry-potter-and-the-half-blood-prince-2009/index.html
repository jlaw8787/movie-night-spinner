Snacks

Slug Club party canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche and potted prawns, small shortcrust fruit mince tarts.

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake and cool the tarts up to 2 days ahead, airtight; top the blinis on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200g potted prawns
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 8 sprigs of dill
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 200g fruit mince
  1. Cut the shortcrust into small rounds and press into a patty tin.
  2. Fill with fruit mince.
  3. Bake at 190°C until golden, 15 min.
  4. Cool the tarts, 15 min.
  5. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, a fold of potted prawns and a sprig of dill.
  6. Arrange both on trays.

Slughorn's mushroom vol-au-ventsVegetarian

Pastry cases of creamy mushroom, passed round the Slug Club party.

Prep 4 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Bake the cases a day ahead and keep in a tin; reheat the filling and fill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 30g butter
  • 400g button mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 16 rounds of pastry with a 6cm cutter, then cut a smaller circle half way through each, 8 min.
  2. Brush with egg and bake 15 minutes until puffed. Push the centres down while warm.
  3. Cook the mushrooms, garlic and thyme leaves in the butter for 8 minutes until the liquid is gone.
  4. Stir in the flour, then the cream, and simmer 2 minutes until thick. Season.
  5. Spoon into the cases and finish with chopped parsley.

Mains

Stuffed turkey breast roast

Turkey breast rolled around sage and onion sourdough stuffing, roasted.

Prep 31 min, cook 1 hr 46 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the stuffing up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; stuff, roll and tie the turkey the day before and roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp sage, chopped
  • 30g butter, for the stuffing
  • 200g sourdough bread, torn
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 500g brussels sprouts
  • 40g butter, for the sprouts
  1. Fry the onion with the sage in the stuffing butter, 8 min.
  2. Mix with the torn sourdough and season for a stuffing.
  3. Butterfly the turkey breast.
  4. Spread with the stuffing, roll and tie.
  5. Chop the potatoes.
  6. Roast the turkey at 180°C with the potatoes around it, rubbed with oil, 1 hr 30 min.
  7. Rest the turkey, 15 min.
  8. Cook the sprouts and toss with the sprout butter, 8 min.

Sweets

Crystallised pineappleVegetarian

Pineapple chunks simmered in syrup, then dried.

Prep 15 min, cook 3 hr 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pineapple, peeled
  • 400g caster sugar, for the syrup
  • 400ml water
  • 100g caster sugar, for rolling
  1. Cut the pineapple into chunks.
  2. Simmer the sugar for the syrup with the water to a syrup, 5 min.
  3. Add the pineapple and cook very gently until translucent, 1 hr.
  4. Drain and dry on a rack in a 90°C oven until tacky.
  5. Roll in the caster sugar set aside for rolling, 5 min.

Drinks

Honey lemon ginger beer coolerNo alcohol

Honey, lemon and cinnamon water over ice, topped with cold ginger beer.

Prep 7 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Infuse and cool the honey lemon syrup up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; pour over ice and top up with ginger beer to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 250ml warm water
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 L cold ginger beer
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Stir the honey into the warm water with the juice of a lemon and the cinnamon stick, 3 min.
  2. Leave to cool and infuse, 30 min.
  3. Pour over ice in glasses and top up with ginger beer.
