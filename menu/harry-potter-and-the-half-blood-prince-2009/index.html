Slug Club party canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche and smoked salmon, small shortcrust fruit mince tarts

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Bake and cool the tarts up to 2 days ahead, airtight; top the blinis on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 8 sprigs dill
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 200g fruit mince
  1. Cut the shortcrust into small rounds and press into a patty tin.
  2. Fill with fruit mince.
  3. Bake at 190C until golden.
  4. Cool the tarts.
  5. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, a fold of smoked salmon and a sprig of dill.
  6. Arrange both on trays.

Slughorn's mushroom vol-au-ventsVegetarian

Puff pastry cases of creamy mushrooms with thyme, an old fashioned party nibble

Prep 5 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Bake the cases a day ahead and keep in a tin; reheat the filling and fill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 30g butter
  • 400g button mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut 16 rounds of pastry with a 6cm cutter, then cut a smaller circle half way through each.
  2. Brush with egg and bake 15 minutes until puffed. Push the centres down while warm.
  3. Cook the mushrooms, garlic and thyme leaves in the butter for 8 minutes until the liquid is gone.
  4. Stir in the flour, then the cream, and simmer 2 minutes until thick. Season.
  5. Spoon into the cases and finish with chopped parsley.

Mains

Stuffed turkey breast roast

Turkey breast rolled around sage and onion sourdough stuffing, roasted

Prep 31 min, cook 106 min · Make ahead: Make the stuffing up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; stuff, roll and tie the turkey the day before and roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg turkey breast
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp sage, chopped
  • 30g butter, for the stuffing
  • 200g sourdough bread, torn
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 500g brussels sprouts
  • 40g butter, for the sprouts
  1. Fry the onion with the sage in the stuffing butter.
  2. Mix with the torn sourdough and season for a stuffing.
  3. Butterfly the turkey breast.
  4. Spread with the stuffing, roll and tie.
  5. Chop the potatoes.
  6. Roast the turkey at 180C with the potatoes around it, rubbed with oil.
  7. Rest the turkey.
  8. Cook the sprouts and toss with the sprout butter.

Peppery skirt, potato, swede and onion in crimped shortcrust, for the train ride with Luna

How it is made
  • 400g beef skirt steak
  • 1 potato
  • 150g swede
  • 1 onion
  • 4 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 20g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  1. Shortcrust filled with diced beef skirt, potato, swede and onion with lots of pepper, crimped and baked at 180C for 50 minutes. Mix 400g diced beef skirt or chuck with 1 diced potato, 150g diced swede, 1 diced onion, 1 tsp salt and 1 tsp cracked pepper. Cut 4 sheets shortcrust pastry into 4 large rounds about 20cm across. Pile the filling on one half of each, dot with butter, fold over and crimp the edge tightly. Brush with beaten egg and cut a steam hole. Bake at 200C for 15 minutes, then 170C for 35 minutes until golden. Serve with tomato chutney.

Ron's love potion trufflesVegetarian

Dark chocolate and cream truffles rolled in cocoa

Prep 20 min, cook 4 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 250g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 12 pink sugar hearts
  1. Heat the cream until steaming.
  2. Pour over the chocolate and stir until smooth.
  3. Chill until firm.
  4. Roll into small balls, coat in cocoa and press a sugar heart on top.
  5. Keep chilled.

Slughorn's crystallised pineappleVegetarian

Pineapple chunks simmered in syrup, then dried

Prep 15 min, cook 65 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pineapple, peeled
  • 400g caster sugar, for the syrup
  • 400ml water
  • 100g caster sugar, for rolling
  1. Cut the pineapple into chunks.
  2. Simmer the sugar for the syrup with the water to a syrup.
  3. Add the pineapple and cook very gently until translucent.
  4. Drain and dry on a rack in a 90C oven until tacky.
  5. Roll in the caster sugar set aside for rolling.

Wizard Wheezes chocolate honeycombOn the printed menu

Syrup toffee foamed with bicarb, set crunchy, half dipped in dark chocolate

How it is made
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Golden honeycomb that foams up in the pot like a joke shop trick, set crunchy, broken into shards and half dipped in dark chocolate. Line a 20cm square tin with baking paper. Heat 200g caster sugar and 80g golden syrup in a deep saucepan, stirring until dissolved, then boil without stirring until it turns deep amber, about 6 minutes. Take off the heat, whisk in 1.5 tsp bicarb quickly and pour straight into the tin as it foams. Leave 1 hour to set, then smash into shards. Dip half of each into 150g melted dark chocolate and set on baking paper.

Honey, lemon and cinnamon water over ice, topped with cold ginger beer

Prep 7 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Infuse and cool the honey lemon syrup up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; pour over ice and top up with ginger beer to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 250ml warm water
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1l cold ginger beer
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Stir the honey into the warm water with the juice of a lemon and the cinnamon stick.
  2. Leave to cool and infuse.
  3. Pour over ice in glasses and top up with ginger beer.
