Snacks

Pink slime dipVegetarian

Roast beets, feta, yoghurt, garlic, lemon and dill, with warm flatbread.

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 beetroots
  • 100g feta
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Wrap the beetroots in foil.
  2. Roast at 200°C until tender, then cool a little and peel, 1 hr.
  3. Blitz the beetroot with the feta, yoghurt, garlic, lemon juice and a little dill until bright pink and smooth. Warm the flatbreads, cut into wedges and serve for scooping, 10 min.

Dancing toaster crumpetsVegetarian

Honey butter. Play them something upbeat.

Prep 7 min, cook 6 min
Recipe for 4
  • 60g soft butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 8 crumpets
  1. Beat the butter with the honey and salt.
  2. Toast the crumpets until the holes are crisp, 6 min.
  3. Spread with the honey butter while hot so it runs into the holes, then cut into quarters.

Mains

Vigo's goulash

Slow paprika beef, onions, caraway and tomato. Egg noodles, sour cream.

Prep 5 min, cook 3.5 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 3 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 pinch hot paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp oil, for browning
  1. Brown the beef in batches in a hot pan with oil, 15 min.
  2. Cook the onions slowly in the same pot until soft, 20 min.
  3. Stir in the sweet paprika, hot paprika and caraway, then add the tomatoes, stock and beef.
  4. Cover and braise at 160°C until the beef is very tender, 2 hr 30 min.
  5. Boil the noodles and serve the goulash over them with the sour cream, 10 min.

Sweets

Pink lemonade jelly

Raspberries inside. It wobbles to music. Keep cheering, kids.

Prep 13 min, cook 5 min, then 6 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 8 gelatine leaves
  • 1 L pink lemonade
  • 125g raspberries
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water, 5 min.
  2. Warm part of the lemonade, squeeze the gelatine and stir in until melted, then add the rest of the lemonade, 5 min.
  3. Pour into a jelly mould with the raspberries.
  4. Chill in the fridge until set, 6 hr.
  5. Dip the mould in hot water, turn out and put on something upbeat.

Drinks

Pink slime floatNo alcohol

Pink lemonade over raspberry sorbet.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml raspberry sorbet
  • 1 L pink lemonade
  1. Put a scoop of raspberry sorbet in each of four tall glasses.
  2. Pour chilled pink lemonade slowly over the top so it fizzes up pink. Serve with a spoon and a straw.
