Snacks

Clan DunBroch Scotch eggs

Soft boiled eggs wrapped in sage sausage meat, crumbed and fried

Prep 33 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 7 eggs
  • 500g sausage meat
  • 2 tbsp sage, chopped
  • 50g plain flour
  • 120g breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • pepper, to taste
  1. Boil 6 of the eggs until just set.
  2. Cool and peel the eggs.
  3. Mix the sausage meat with the sage and pepper.
  4. Wrap the sausage meat around each egg.
  5. Roll in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  6. Heat the vegetable oil to 170C and fry in two batches.
  7. Halve and serve with mustard.

Bear mum's salmon bites

Oatcakes, cream cheese and smoked salmon, Elinor's river feast

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 oatcakes
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 8 sprigs dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Spread the oatcakes with cream cheese.
  2. Top with folds of smoked salmon and a sprig of dill.
  3. Squeeze over a little lemon and lay out on a slate.

Mains

Scotch broth

Lamb neck, pearl barley, leeks, carrots and turnips

Prep 26 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb neck
  • 150g pearl barley
  • 2l water
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 3 carrots, diced
  • 2 turnips, diced
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Simmer the lamb and barley in the water.
  2. Slice and dice the leeks, carrots and turnips.
  3. Add the vegetables and simmer again.
  4. Shred the lamb back into the pot.
  5. Season well and stir through the parsley.

Sweets

The witch's spell cakeVegetarian

Honey cake with oats and raspberries. Fate be changed

Prep 16 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and store airtight; glaze with the warm honey and top with a raspberry on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g honey, for the cake
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g rolled oats
  • 125g raspberries
  • 2 tbsp honey, for glazing
  1. Beat the butter and honey until pale.
  2. Beat in the eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour, oats and most of the raspberries.
  4. Bake at 175C.
  5. Cool the cake.
  6. Glaze with warm honey and set a single raspberry on top.

Highland cranachan shakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Raspberries, milk, honey and toasted oats blended thick

Prep 11 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Toast the oats up to 3 days ahead, airtight; whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead; blend just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp rolled oats
  • 200g raspberries
  • 750ml cold milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Toast the oats in a dry pan until golden.
  2. Blend the raspberries, milk, honey and most of the oats until smooth.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Pour into tall glasses and top with whipped cream and the rest of the oats.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Merida feeds her mum the witch's cake.
