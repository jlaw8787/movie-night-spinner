Snacks

Prawn Cocktail

Poached iced prawns on shredded iceberg with Marie Rose and a dusting of paprika.

Prep 6 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Poach the prawns in salted water with the lemon and bay leaf, then plunge into iced water and peel.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Serve on shredded iceberg in glasses, with sauce and a dusting of paprika.

Red Caviar Blini

Warm buckwheat pancakes, crème fraîche, a big spoon of roe and chives, spend, spend, spend.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 buckwheat blini
  • 150ml crème fraîche
  • 100g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives, snipped
  1. Warm the blini on a tray at 180°C.
  2. Top each with crème fraîche, then salmon roe and chives.
  3. Serve straight away while warm.

Hackensack Bulls mini chilli dogs

Little frankfurts in soft buns under beef chilli, cheddar and onion, for the minor league dugout.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 12 cocktail franks
  • 12 mini hot dog buns
  • 100g cheddar
  1. Little franks in soft buns under beef chilli, melted cheddar and diced onion, ballpark style. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 diced onion, add 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 200ml water, and simmer 20 minutes until thick. Simmer 12 cocktail franks 3 minutes. Split 12 mini hot dog buns, add a frank, spoon over the chilli and top with 100g grated cheddar and diced onion. Grill 1 minute to melt.

Mains

Surf and Turf

Eye fillet and half a lobster tail with garlic butter and béarnaise.

Prep 8 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Sauce best made just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 250g butter, clarified and kept warm
  • 4 eye fillet steaks
  • 2 lobster tails
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  1. Reduce the vinegar with the tarragon to a few spoonfuls.
  2. Whisk with the yolks over low heat and drizzle in 200g of the warm clarified butter for the béarnaise.
  3. Sear the steaks to medium rare, then rest.
  4. Split the lobster tails and brush with garlic butter made from the remaining butter.
  5. Grill the lobster until cooked through.
  6. Plate the steak with lobster and sauce.

Gold leaf fried chicken

Buttermilk thighs brushed with honey chilli butter and edible gold, on buttery mash.

Prep 15 min, cook 58 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in buttermilk the night before; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg boneless chicken thighs
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 80g butter
  • 80ml honey
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 pack edible gold leaf
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with half the salt, covered in the fridge, overnight.
  2. Peel and boil the potatoes until soft, then mash with half the butter, the milk and a pinch of salt. Keep warm.
  3. Mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder and the rest of the salt. Lift the chicken from the buttermilk and toss it in the flour until thickly coated.
  4. Fry in batches at 170°C until deep gold and cooked through, about 7 minutes a batch. Drain on a rack.
  5. Melt the rest of the butter with the honey and hot sauce and brush over the hot chicken.
  6. Lay a little gold leaf on each piece with dry tweezers and serve on the mash.

Postage StampsVegetarian

Butter, icing sugar and rice flour biscuits with pinked edges, for the world's priciest postage.

Prep 32 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 250g plain flour
  • 60g rice flour
  1. Beat the butter with half the icing sugar, then mix in the flours.
  2. Roll to 6mm and cut into rectangles with a pinking cutter.
  3. Bake at 160°C until pale.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Mix the rest of the icing sugar with a drop of water and pipe a little stamp design on each.

Champagne Cocktail

Brut poured over a bitters-soaked sugar cube with orange liqueur and an orange twist.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the champagne and flutes up to 1 day ahead; build each glass just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 480ml chilled brut champagne
  • 1 orange, for peel
  1. Drop a sugar cube into each of 4 flutes and shake bitters onto each.
  2. Add orange liqueur to each glass.
  3. Top slowly with champagne.
  4. Twist a strip of orange peel over each glass and drop it in.
