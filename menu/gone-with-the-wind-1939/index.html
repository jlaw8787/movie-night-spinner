Snacks

Never-hungry-again radishes on buttermilk biscuitsVegetarian

Crisp radishes on buttermilk biscuits with salted butter. Scarlett swears she'll never go hungry.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Rub the butter into the flour up to a day ahead and chill; bake the biscuits to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 80g cold butter, cubed, plus 100g softened for spreading
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 2 bunches radishes, trimmed
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour with a pinch of salt, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough.
  2. Pat out 3cm thick, cut rounds and bake at 220°C until risen and golden.
  3. Slice the radishes thinly.
  4. Split the warm biscuits, spread thickly with soft butter and pile on radishes, salt flakes and chives.

Gold-bid devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs with mustard and paprika. $150 in gold for one dance.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill the eggs up to a day ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes, then cool in iced water.
  2. Peel, halve and mash the yolks with the mayo, mustard, vinegar and salt.
  3. Pipe or spoon back into the whites.
  4. Dust with paprika and scatter with chives.

Mains

Twelve Oaks barbecue pork shoulder

Slow-roast barbecue pork shoulder with vinegar slaw, the Wilkes barbecue at Twelve Oaks.

Prep 50 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Rub the pork the night before; roast on the day, and make the slaw up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg boneless pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 150ml cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1/2 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 8 soft white rolls
  1. Rub the pork with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and plenty of salt.
  2. Roast covered at 160°C until it pulls apart easily.
  3. Warm 100ml of the vinegar with the chilli flakes and a pinch of sugar for a sauce.
  4. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the rest of the vinegar and salt.
  5. Pull the pork, splash with the chilli vinegar and serve with the slaw and rolls.

Green velvet drapes herb-crusted lamb

Lamb racks under a bright green herb crust, like Scarlett's dress from the green drapes.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the herb crust up to a day ahead and chill; crust and roast the lamb to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lamb racks, 8 cutlets each
  • 1 cup flat-leaf parsley
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 500g baby potatoes
  • 30g butter
  1. Blitz the parsley, mint, garlic, breadcrumbs and 2 tbsp oil to a bright green crumb.
  2. Sear the lamb racks all over in the rest of the oil, then brush with mustard and press on the crumb.
  3. Roast at 200°C until pink in the middle, then rest.
  4. Boil the potatoes, toss with butter and serve with the carved cutlets.

Fiddle-dee-dee Georgia peach cobblerVegetarian

Baked Georgia peaches under a buttermilk crust. Fiddle-dee-dee to all the war talk.

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Slice and sugar the peaches up to 6 hours ahead; top and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 ripe yellow peaches, sliced
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 80g cold butter, cubed
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 300ml pouring cream
  1. Toss the peaches with the brown sugar, cornflour and cinnamon in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and caster sugar, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over the peaches and bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling.
  4. Serve warm with the cream.

Drinks

Frankly-my-dear mint julep

Bourbon over crushed ice with fresh mint.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the mint syrup up to 3 days ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml Kentucky bourbon
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml water
  • 4 cups crushed ice
  1. Heat the sugar and water until dissolved, add half the mint and cool.
  2. Pack 4 cups with crushed ice, add 60ml bourbon and a splash of strained syrup to each.
  3. Stir until frosted and crown with mint.
