Snacks

Brie, ham and chutney sandwich bites

Crusty bread filled with brie, ham and tomato chutney, cut into small pieces

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Fill and cut the bites up to 3 hours ahead, wrapped and chilled; pin with toothpicks and add the olives to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 150g brie
  • 150g leg ham
  • 3 tbsp tomato chutney
  • 200g green olives
  1. Split the bread and fill with sliced brie, ham and chutney.
  2. Cut into small pieces and pin with toothpicks.
  3. Serve on a board with a bowl of green olives.

Spike's sandwichVegetarian

Toasted cheese sandwich with sliced cheddar, tomato and pickle relish

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best toasted just before serving; butter the bread and slice the cheddar and tomatoes up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 200g cheddar
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp pickle relish
  1. Butter the bread on the outside. Slice the cheddar and tomatoes.
  2. Fill with cheddar, tomato and relish.
  3. Toast in a pan over medium heat, pressing down, until golden and melting.
  4. Cut into quarters.

Mains

Creamy tarragon chicken

Chicken thighs and mushrooms in a white wine, cream and tarragon sauce

Prep 14 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Cook the chicken in its wine and cream sauce up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and reheat gently; mash the potatoes and steam the beans fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 250ml white wine
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 200g green beans
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Peel the potatoes, slice the mushrooms and chop the tarragon.
  2. Brown the chicken in oil.
  3. Add the mushrooms and cook until golden.
  4. Pour in the wine and simmer.
  5. Stir in the cream and tarragon.
  6. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter and steam the beans.

Sweets

The last brownieVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies cut into squares

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a tin. Melt the chocolate and butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  3. Bake.
  4. Cool and cut into squares.

Drinks

Spilled orange juice mimosasVegetarian

Fresh orange juice topped with sparkling wine

Prep 13 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Squeeze and strain the orange juice up to 1 day ahead and chill it with the sparkling wine; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 6 oranges
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 750ml sparkling water
  1. Squeeze the oranges and strain the juice.
  2. Chill the juice and the sparkling wine.
  3. Pour orange juice into each of four flutes, then slowly top with sparkling wine.
  4. Twist a strip of orange peel over each and hang on the rim. For non-drinkers, top with sparkling water.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the dinner party guests compete for the last brownie.
