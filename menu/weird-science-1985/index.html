Snacks

Cheesy bacon dip cob loaf

Bread bowl baked with cream cheese, sour cream, bacon and cheddar dip.

Prep 17 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Fry the bacon and mix the dip up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; fill the loaf and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 packet French onion soup mix
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  2. Hollow out the cob loaf, keeping the lid.
  3. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, bacon, cheddar and soup mix.
  4. Fill the loaf and wrap in foil.
  5. Bake at 180°C.
  6. Tear the hollowed bread into chunks for dipping.

Pink Cadillac blues bar wings

Crispy baked wings in hot sauce butter with blue cheese dip.

Prep 6 min, cook 49 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry the wings the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Leave the wings uncovered in the fridge overnight to dry.
  2. Bake at 220°C until crisp.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream for the dip.
  5. Serve with celery sticks.

Salt and vinegar chips with onion dipOn the printed menu

Paper-thin potatoes fried crisp with malt, French onion and chive cream, for Lisa's party.

How it is made
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  • 1 bottle malt vinegar
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 250g sour cream
  • 1 packet French onion soup mix
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Potatoes sliced paper thin, soaked in vinegar, fried until crisp and tossed with salt and malt vinegar, with a creamy French onion and chive dip. Slice 4 large potatoes paper thin on a mandoline and soak in 500ml water with 200ml white vinegar for 30 minutes. Dry very well on tea towels. Fry in small batches at 170°C for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and crisp. Toss with flaky salt and a sprinkle of malt vinegar. Mix 250g sour cream with half a packet of French onion soup mix and chopped chives for the dip.

Mains

Missile-through-the-roof party pizza

Thin pepperoni and mushroom pizza in party squares.

Prep 16 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms up to 1 day ahead; stretch the dough, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • 150g pepperoni
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  1. Stretch the dough thin on a floured tray.
  2. Spread with sauce and scatter with mozzarella, pepperoni and mushrooms.
  3. Bake on a hot steel or tray at the highest oven heat.
  4. Slice and leave in a box on the bench.

Chicago Italian beef with giardinieraOn the printed menu

Rare topside shaved thin, dipped in garlic oregano jus, hot pickled veg, with the parents away.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef topside
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1L beef stock
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 4 long soft rolls
  • 1 jar giardiniera
  1. Beef topside roasted rare, sliced paper thin and dunked in garlic and oregano beef jus, piled into soft rolls with hot pickled giardiniera. Rub 1kg beef topside with oil, salt, pepper and 1 tsp oregano and roast at 200°C for 35 minutes to rare. Rest, chill for an hour and slice paper thin. Simmer 1L beef stock with 4 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp oregano, 1 tsp chilli flakes and the roasting juices for 15 minutes. Dip the beef in the jus for 1 minute, pile into 4 split long rolls, spoon over more jus and top with giardiniera.

Chet the toad's sheet cakeVegetarian

Chocolate sheet cake covered in fudge frosting.

Prep 27 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, divided
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 100g cocoa, divided
  • 250ml milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Beat part of the butter with the sugar, then add the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, some cocoa and the milk.
  3. Bake in a lined tray at 175°C and cool.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with icing sugar and remaining cocoa for the fudge frosting.
  5. Spread on the cake and cover with sprinkles.

Blind Dog bourbon lemon smashVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and mint, shaken hard over ice.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; muddle and shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 2 lemons
  • 16 mint leaves, plus sprigs to garnish
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in hot water and cool.
  2. For each drink, muddle lemon wedges with mint leaves and syrup in a shaker.
  3. Add bourbon and ice and shake hard for 10 seconds.
  4. Pour into a short glass and top with crushed ice.
  5. Garnish with a mint sprig.
