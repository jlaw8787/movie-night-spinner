Snacks

Mittens' tuna melt bites

Tuna, mayo, celery and pickle on toasted English muffins

Prep 20 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Mix the tuna filling up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; toast the muffins, top and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins tuna (185g each), drained
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped pickles
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 4 English muffins
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • pepper, to taste
  1. Split and toast the muffins.
  2. Mix the tuna with the mayonnaise, celery, pickles, a squeeze of lemon and pepper.
  3. Spread over the muffin halves and top with cheddar.
  4. Grill until the cheese is bubbling and golden.
  5. Cut each half into quarters and serve warm.

Rhino's cheese ballsVegetarian

Cheddar cream cheese balls in walnuts. Let it begin!

Prep 26 min, cook 62 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 100g walnuts, finely chopped
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Mix the cheddar, cream cheese and chives until well combined.
  2. Roll into small balls.
  3. Coat in the chopped walnuts.
  4. Chill until firm.
  5. Serve piled in a bowl with the crackers.

Mains

Road trip diner meatloaf

Ohio in the back of a U-Haul. Meatloaf and potatoes

Prep 25 min, cook 79 min · Make ahead: Mix and press the meatloaf into the tin up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bake it and mash the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Mix the mince, onion, breadcrumbs, egg and salt.
  2. Press into a loaf tin and spread with tomato sauce.
  3. Bake at 180C until cooked through.
  4. Peel and boil the potatoes, then mash with butter and milk.
  5. Slice the meatloaf thickly and serve on the mash.

Sweets

Lightning bolt cookiesVegetarian

Shortbread bolts. The power of Styrofoam

Prep 28 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until creamy.
  2. Mix in the flour to a soft dough.
  3. Roll out and cut into lightning bolt shapes.
  4. Bake at 160C until pale golden.
  5. Cool on the tray.
  6. Mix the icing sugar and water and ice the cookies white.

Drinks

Chocolate malt thickshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick enough for a super bark

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Crush the malt balls and chill the glasses up to 4 hours ahead; blend just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml chocolate ice cream
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 100ml whipped cream
  • 50g chocolate malt balls
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk, malted milk powder and cocoa until thick.
  2. Pour into chilled tall glasses and top with whipped cream.
  3. Crush the malt balls and scatter over in a zigzag like a lightning bolt.
