Mittens' tuna melt bites

Tuna, mayo, celery and pickle on toasted English muffins.

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the tuna filling up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; toast the muffins, top and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins of tuna (185g each), drained
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped pickles
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 4 English muffins
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • pepper, to taste
  1. Split and toast the muffins, 5 min.
  2. Mix the tuna with the mayonnaise, celery, pickles, a squeeze of lemon and pepper.
  3. Spread over the muffin halves and top with cheddar.
  4. Grill until the cheese is bubbling and golden.
  5. Cut each half into quarters and serve warm, 3 min.

Rhino's cheese ballsVegetarian

Cheddar cream cheese balls in walnuts.

Prep 28 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 100g walnuts, finely chopped
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Mix the cheddar, cream cheese and chives until well combined.
  2. Roll into small balls.
  3. Coat in the chopped walnuts.
  4. Chill until firm.
  5. Serve piled in a bowl with the crackers.

Packing peanut cheese popcorn

Popcorn tossed in butter, cheddar and parmesan, packed like peanuts in a shipping box.

How it is made
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 50g cheddar
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Popcorn tossed in melted butter with finely grated cheddar, parmesan, smoked paprika and a little chilli, hitting the jackpot in every handful. Heat 3 tbsp oil and 120g popcorn kernels in a big lidded pot over medium-high heat, shaking until the popping slows. Tip into a large bowl. Melt 60g butter with 1 tsp smoked paprika and 1/2 tsp chilli powder and pour over. Blitz 50g grated cheddar and 30g parmesan to a fine powder and toss through with 1 tsp salt. Serve in paper cones.

Road trip diner meatloaf

Beef meatloaf with roast potatoes and a tomato glaze.

Prep 19 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Mix and press the meatloaf into the tin up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bake it and mash the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Mix the mince, onion, breadcrumbs, egg and salt.
  2. Press into a loaf tin and spread with tomato sauce.
  3. Bake at 180°C until cooked through.
  4. Peel and boil the potatoes, then mash with butter and milk, 25 min.
  5. Slice the meatloaf thickly and serve on the mash.

Hollywood studio chicken burrito bowl

Spiced chicken over rice with black beans, corn and salsa.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 3 limes
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 can black beans
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 can chipotles in adobo
  1. Lime and garlic chargrilled chicken over coriander rice with black beans, charred corn, avocado, pickled onion and chipotle mayo. Marinate 800g chicken thighs in the juice of 2 limes, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin and oil for 30 minutes. Cook 300g rice and stir through chopped coriander. Grill the chicken 5 minutes a side and slice. Char 2 corn cobs and cut off the kernels. Slice 1 red onion and soak in lime juice for 10 minutes. Build bowls with rice, warmed black beans, chicken, corn, avocado, onion and mayo mixed with chipotle.

Lightning bolt cookiesVegetarian

Buttery shortbread cut into lightning bolts and iced yellow.

Prep 20 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until creamy.
  2. Mix in the flour to a soft dough.
  3. Roll out and cut into lightning bolt shapes.
  4. Bake at 160°C until pale golden.
  5. Cool on the tray, 20 min.
  6. Mix the icing sugar and water and ice the cookies white.

Chocolate malt thickshakeNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream, milk and malt blended thick enough.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Crush the malt balls and chill the glasses up to 4 hours ahead; blend just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml chocolate ice cream
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 100ml whipped cream
  • 50g chocolate malt balls
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk, malted milk powder and cocoa until thick.
  2. Pour into chilled tall glasses and top with whipped cream.
  3. Crush the malt balls and scatter over in a zigzag like a lightning bolt.
