Vince's double cheese mini burgers with fries

Double beef, American cheese, onion, skinny fries.

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Cut and soak the fries up to a day ahead; shape the patties the morning of and griddle to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 8 mini burger buns
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 600g potatoes, cut in skinny fries
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Fry the potatoes at 160°C until soft, drain, then fry again at 190°C until crisp and salt them.
  2. Roll the mince into 16 small balls, smash thin on a hot griddle and season.
  3. Flip, top half the patties with cheese and stack a plain one on each.
  4. Build in the toasted buns with onion, pickle, sauce and mustard and serve with the fries.

Cha Cha Cha jerk chicken skewers with mango

Allspice and chilli chicken, grilled mango, lime. Mia's pick: the place with little red candles.

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken overnight; thread and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, cubed
  • 3 spring onions, chopped
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 limes
  • 1 mango, cut in chunks
  • 8 bamboo skewers, soaked
  1. Blend the spring onions, chilli, garlic, allspice, thyme, soy, sugar and the juice of a lime to a paste.
  2. Toss the chicken in the paste and leave for at least 10 minutes, or overnight.
  3. Thread the chicken and mango onto the skewers.
  4. Grill over high heat, turning, until charred and cooked through, and serve with lime wedges.

Toretto's tuna on white, no crust

Tuna, celery, dill pickle, lemon mayo, iceberg. Brian orders it every single day.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the tuna salad up to a day ahead and keep it cold; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 2 x 185g tins tuna in olive oil, drained
  • 2 celery sticks, finely diced
  • 2 dill pickles, finely diced
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  1. Flake the tuna into a bowl and mix with the celery, pickle, mayonnaise, dill, a little lemon zest and juice, salt and lots of pepper.
  2. Spread the tuna thickly over 4 slices of bread, add a lettuce leaf and close with the other slices.
  3. Press down gently, cut the crusts off with a sharp knife, then cut each sandwich in half.

Quarter-mile Cuban pressed sandwich

Mojo pork, ham, Swiss, pickles, mustard, pressed. Dom lives life a quarter mile at a time.

Prep 17 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Roast the mojo pork up to 2 days ahead and slice it cold; press the sandwiches to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long crusty bread rolls
  • 800g boneless pork shoulder
  • 2 oranges
  • 2 limes
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 150g sliced leg ham
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 3 tbsp American mustard
  • 30g butter, softened
  1. Mix the orange and lime juice with the garlic, cumin, oregano and 2 teaspoons of salt and rub it over the pork.
  2. Roast covered at 160°C until tender, then uncover for the last 20 minutes to brown, and rest.
  3. Slice the pork thinly, split the rolls and spread with mustard.
  4. Layer the ham, pork, cheese and pickles, butter the outsides and press in a hot pan under a heavy pan, turning once, until crisp and melting.
  5. Cut on the diagonal and serve.

Ten-second churros with Mexican chocolateVegetarian

Cinnamon sugar churros, thick spiced chocolate.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; fry the churros to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar, plus 80g for coating
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 250ml milk
  • 1/4 tsp chilli powder
  1. Bring the water, butter and a tablespoon of sugar to the boil, tip in the flour and beat until it forms a ball, then cool for 5 minutes.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time and spoon the dough into a piping bag with a star nozzle.
  3. Warm the milk, stir in the chocolate, half the cinnamon and the chilli until thick and smooth.
  4. Pipe 12cm lengths into oil at 180°C and fry until golden, a few at a time.
  5. Roll the hot churros in the 80g sugar mixed with the rest of the cinnamon and serve with the chocolate.
