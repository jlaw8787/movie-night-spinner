Boston clam chowder with oyster crackers

Creamy clam chowder with bacon and potato, oyster crackers on the side

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chowder base through to the tender potato up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; add the clams and cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 rashers bacon, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml fish stock
  • 300g potatoes, diced
  • 1kg clams or vongole, cleaned
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 box oyster crackers
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp, then add the onion and celery and cook until soft.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the fish stock and potato. Simmer until the potato is tender.
  3. Add the clams, cover and cook until open, then pick out the empty shells.
  4. Stir in the cream and heat through. Season with pepper and serve with oyster crackers and chives.

Watermelon toss buffalo wings

Crisp baked wings in hot sauce butter, blue cheese dip and celery

Prep 5 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings in the baking powder and salt and leave them uncovered in the fridge up to 1 day ahead, and mash the dip up to 2 days ahead; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch celery, cut into sticks
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Mash the blue cheese with the sour cream, mayonnaise and a squeeze of lemon for the dip.
  4. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through it. Serve with the dip and celery.

Mullins family roast beef and gravy

Roast beef, crisp potatoes, honey carrots and pan gravy

Prep 17 min, cook 71 min · Make ahead: Rub the beef up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge, and parboil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead; roast and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef topside
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 5 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1.2kg potatoes, quartered
  • 4 carrots, halved
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Rub the beef with the mustard, garlic, a little oil and salt.
  2. Parboil the potatoes, drain, rough up and toss in oil.
  3. Roast the beef at 200°C for medium rare, with the potatoes on a separate tray.
  4. Toss the carrots in the honey and add for the last part of the roasting.
  5. Rest the beef.
  6. Whisk the flour into the pan juices, add the stock and simmer to a gravy.

Boston cream pieVegetarian

Sponge cake layered with vanilla custard and chocolate glaze

Prep 11 min, cook 44 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs (3 yolks for the custard, 2 whole for the cake)
  • 210g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 420ml full cream milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the yolks, some of the sugar and the cornflour, then whisk in hot milk. Return to the pan and stir until thick. Add the vanilla.
  2. Chill the custard.
  3. Beat the butter and remaining sugar, add the whole eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  4. Bake in a 20cm tin at 180°C, then cool.
  5. Split the cake and fill with custard.
  6. Melt the chocolate with the cream and pour over the top.

Ashburn and Mullins whiskey sourVegetarian

Irish whiskey, lemon and egg white shaken foamy, bitters and cherry

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; shake with the egg white and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml Irish whiskey
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 4 egg whites (from 4 eggs)
  • 1 bottle aromatic bitters
  • 1 jar cocktail cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Shake the whiskey, lemon juice, sugar syrup and egg white hard without ice in a jar or shaker. Repeat for each drink.
  2. Add ice and shake again, then strain into short glasses over fresh ice.
  3. Top the foam with a couple of dashes of bitters and drop in a cherry.
