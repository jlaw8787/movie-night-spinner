Carnival corn dogs

Frankfurts on sticks in a sweet polenta and buttermilk batter, fried golden.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry batter ingredients up to 1 week ahead; add the egg and buttermilk and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 150g fine polenta
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  • 100g American mustard
  • 100g tomato sauce
  1. Whisk the polenta, flour, baking powder, sugar and salt, then whisk in the egg and buttermilk to a thick batter. Pour into a tall glass.
  2. Heat the oil to 175°C in a deep pot.
  3. Pat the frankfurts dry and push a skewer into each.
  4. Dip each frankfurt into the batter, twirl to coat and fry 2 at a time, turning, until deep golden. Drain.
  5. Serve hot with mustard and tomato sauce.

Tupelo fried green tomatoesVegetarian

Thick slices of green tomato in a crisp polenta and flour crust, fried golden.

Prep 28 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the buttermilk dressing up to 2 days ahead; crumb and fry the tomatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large green tomatoes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 185ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine polenta
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch chives, chopped
  • 1 tsp hot chilli sauce
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  1. Slice the tomatoes 1cm thick, salt lightly and sit on paper towel.
  2. Set out the flour, the eggs beaten with 60ml of the buttermilk, and the polenta mixed with the cayenne.
  3. Dip each slice in flour, egg and polenta.
  4. Shallow-fry in the oil for 2 to 3 minutes a side until golden, and drain.
  5. Stir the rest of the buttermilk with the mayonnaise, chives, hot sauce and pepper and serve with the tomatoes.

Beale Street dry rub pork ribs with slaw

Pork ribs in a smoky paprika, brown sugar and garlic dry rub, slow-baked and mopped with vinegar.

Prep 18 min, cook 3 hours, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Rub the ribs up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; bake on the day and toss the slaw while they finish
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg American-style pork ribs
  • 60ml apple cider vinegar, for the mop
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/4 green cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 100g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar, for the slaw
  • 1 loaf soft white bread
  1. Mix the paprikas, brown sugar, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, thyme, salt and pepper and rub all over the ribs, keeping 1 tbsp back.
  2. Leave the ribs in the fridge to take in the rub.
  3. Wrap the ribs tightly in foil on a tray and bake at 150°C.
  4. Unwrap, turn the oven to 220°C, splash with the mop vinegar, dust with the last of the rub and roast until crusted.
  5. Toss the cabbages and carrot with the mayonnaise, the rest of the vinegar and the sugar.
  6. Cut the ribs and serve with the slaw and slices of white bread.

Memphis meatloaf with mash and gravy

Beef and pork meatloaf with mash and onion gravy, Elvis's favourite from his mama Gladys's kitchen.

Prep 0 min, cook 101 min · Make ahead: Shape the meatloaf up to 1 day ahead; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Mix the mince, onion, egg and breadcrumbs, press into a loaf tin and spread with the tomato sauce mixed with sugar.
  2. Bake until cooked through, then rest.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with half the butter and the milk.
  4. Stir the flour into the loaf tin juices with the stock and simmer for gravy. Cook the peas and serve.

Fried peanut butter and banana sandwichesVegetarian

Peanut butter and mashed ripe banana in white bread, fried golden in butter.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mash the bananas no more than 1 hour ahead with a squeeze of lemon
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 160g smooth peanut butter
  • 80g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Mash the bananas.
  2. Spread the bread with peanut butter, spread banana over 4 slices and sandwich together.
  3. Fry 2 at a time in half the butter over medium heat, pressing, until deep golden on both sides. Repeat with the rest of the butter.
  4. Cut into fingers, drizzle with honey and dust with icing sugar.

Memphis peach sweet teaNo alcohol

Strong black tea sweetened while hot, chilled with fresh peach syrup and lemon.

Prep 3 min, cook 16 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brew and chill the sweet tea up to 2 days ahead and keep it in a jug in the fridge; pour over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe peaches, 2 sliced for syrup, 1 for serving
  • 120g sugar
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 3 cups ice
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Simmer 2 of the peaches with the sugar and 250ml water, then mash and strain.
  2. Pour 1 litre boiling water over the tea bags, steep 5 minutes and remove.
  3. Stir in the peach syrup, lemon juice and 500ml cold water and chill.
  4. Pour over ice with slices of the last peach and the mint.
