Speakeasy devilled eggs with crispy bacon

Mustard mayo yolks piped back in, crisp bacon and smoked paprika on top.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled, top with pancetta just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 8 slices of pancetta
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool in iced water, peel and halve.
  2. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and a pinch of paprika until smooth. Pipe back into the whites.
  3. Bake the pancetta on a tray at 200°C until crisp, cool and break into shards.
  4. Top each egg with pancetta, a dusting of paprika and snipped chives.

Florida beach shrimp

Shrimp poached in lemon water, chilled in iced water, served on ice.

Prep 11 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, raw and shell on
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • hot sauce, to taste
  • 2 tbsp salt, for the water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Bring a pot of water to the boil with the salt and lemon slices.
  2. Poach the prawns until pink, then plunge into iced water.
  3. Peel the prawns.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with tomato sauce, a squeeze of lemon and hot sauce. Serve the prawns on ice with the sauce.

Upper berth pimento cheese crackers

Sharp cheddar, roast bell pepper, mayo and cayenne on crackers with salami.

How it is made
  • 250g vintage cheddar
  • 100g roasted red capsicum
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 50g cream cheese
  • cayenne pepper
  • hot sauce
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 100g sliced salami
  • 1 jar cornichons
  1. Sharp cheddar beaten with roasted capsicum, mayo and cayenne into a spread, piled on crackers with thin salami and a slice of cornichon. Grate 250g vintage cheddar. Finely chop 100g drained roasted red capsicum. Mix the cheese and capsicum with 100g mayonnaise, 50g soft cream cheese, a pinch of cayenne and a few drops of hot sauce until spreadable. Chill 30 minutes. Spread generously on 32 water crackers, top each with a fold of salami (100g total) and a slice of cornichon, and stack them on a tray.

Millionaire's lobster

Grilled lobster tails with garlic butter, baby potatoes and green beans.

Prep 9 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 150g butter, melted
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  • 2 lemons
  1. Split the lobster tails, brush with butter mixed with crushed garlic.
  2. Boil the potatoes. Steam the green beans.
  3. Grill the lobster until just cooked.
  4. Toss the potatoes and beans with butter and serve with lemon wedges.

Friends of Italian Opera meatball spaghetti

Beef and pork balls with parmesan in tomato basil sugo, piled high, for Spats Colombo.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 60ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 80g parmesan
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1.4kg passata
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 500g spaghetti
  1. Beef and pork meatballs with parmesan and parsley, browned and simmered in a rich tomato and basil sugo, piled over spaghetti on one big platter. Mix 300g beef mince, 300g pork mince, 50g breadcrumbs soaked in 60ml milk, 1 egg, 40g grated parmesan, 2 tbsp chopped parsley, 1 garlic clove and salt. Roll into 20 balls and brown in olive oil. Remove. Soften 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, add 1.4kg passata and simmer 10 minutes. Return the meatballs, cover and simmer 30 minutes, adding basil at the end. Cook 500g spaghetti, toss with some sauce and pile onto a platter with the meatballs and more parmesan.

Big birthday cakeVegetarian

Three butter sponge layers with strawberry jam and whipped cream.

Prep 50 min, cook 25 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g butter, softened
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 200g strawberry jam
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 12 candles
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour. Divide between 3 tins.
  2. Bake at 175°C until golden and springy.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Whip the cream and stack the cakes with jam and cream.
  5. Ice white with icing sugar mixed with milk and top with candles.

Bourbon Manhattans

Bourbon, sweet vermouth and bitters stirred cold, with a cherry.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix a jug ahead, strain over ice per glass
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 120ml sweet vermouth
  • aromatic bitters, 8 dashes
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Stir the bourbon, vermouth and bitters with ice in a jug.
  2. Strain into chilled glasses.
  3. Drop a cherry into each glass.
