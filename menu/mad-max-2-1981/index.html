Wasteland tin dinner

Corned beef hash and a fried egg, in a tin.

Prep 2 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes and dice the onion up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Fry the hash and eggs on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 340g corned beef
  • 4 eggs
  1. Boil the diced potatoes for 6 minutes, then drain.
  2. Fry the onion in the butter until soft.
  3. Add the potato and break in the corned beef, press flat and fry without stirring until crusty, then turn and repeat.
  4. Fry the eggs.
  5. Serve the hash in clean empty tins with an egg on top.

Gyro Captain's frankfurt pastry snake

Frankfurts coiled in sesame puff with peppercorn eyes and two dips, this one doesn't bite.

Prep 15 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Roll and coil the snake up to 4 hours ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; brush with egg and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 3 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 black peppercorns
  • 125ml barbecue sauce
  • 125ml American mustard
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a large tray.
  2. Cut each pastry sheet into 3 strips. Lay the frankfurts end to end along the strips, roll the pastry around them and pinch the joins so they make one long roll.
  3. Coil the roll on the tray like a snake. Shape a head at one end and press in the peppercorns for eyes.
  4. Brush with egg, scatter with sesame seeds and bake for 25 minutes until puffed and golden.
  5. Serve on a board with the barbecue sauce and mustard to dip.

Compound barbecue

Lamb shoulder roasted low with rosemary and paprika, pulled into warm flatbread at the compound.

Prep 14 min, cook 5 hours · Make ahead: Rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; roast on the day. Chop the salad up to 3 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp rosemary, chopped
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 8 flatbreads
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  1. Rub the lamb with the garlic, rosemary, smoked paprika and salt.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C for 5 hours until it pulls apart.
  3. Chop the tomatoes and slice the red onion for the salad.
  4. Warm the flatbreads.
  5. Pull the lamb apart and serve with the flatbreads, Greek yoghurt and tomato salad.

Tanker convoy beef stew and damper

Beef and vegetable stew with hot buttered damper, a big pot for the tanker run.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 600g potatoes
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 60g butter
  • 100g cheddar
  • 300ml milk
  1. Beef chuck braised for three hours in a dark tomato and stock gravy with carrot and potato, with warm cheesy damper torn at the table. Brown 1.2kg diced beef chuck in batches. Cook 2 onions and 2 carrots 5 minutes, add 2 tbsp tomato paste, 400g tinned tomatoes and 750ml beef stock and return the beef. Cover and cook at 160°C for 2 hours, add 600g diced potatoes and cook 1 hour more. Mix 450g self-raising flour, 60g rubbed-in butter, 100g grated cheddar and 300ml milk, shape a round and bake at 200°C for 30 minutes.

Feral Kid's boomerang biscuitsVegetarian

Boomerang biscuits dipped in dark chocolate.

Prep 22 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and mix in the flour to a dough.
  2. Roll 5mm thick and cut into boomerang shapes.
  3. Bake at 160°C.
  4. Cool.
  5. Melt the chocolate and dip the tips.

Wasteland tinned peach crumbleVegetarian

Tinned peaches under a buttery oat crumble, with custard. Found in a tin, shared at the compound.

Prep 9 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to a day ahead and chill; bake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 825g tins sliced peaches
  • 100g butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 600ml custard
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and drain the peaches into a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the oats and sugar.
  3. Scatter over the peaches and bake until golden.
  4. Serve warm with custard.

Drinks

Guzzoline dark and stormy

Dark rum floated over ginger beer and lime.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Build each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 600ml ginger beer, cold
  • 2 limes
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice.
  2. Squeeze lime juice into each and add the ginger beer.
  3. Pour the rum slowly over the back of a spoon so it floats dark on top.
  4. Add a lime wedge and serve unstirred. Skip the rum for a plain ginger beer and lime.
