Snacks

Cypher's steak bites

Seared rump cubes tossed in garlic butter, with a peppercorn sour cream dip.

Prep 14 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Cube and season the steak up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and mix the dip up to 2 days ahead; sear and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rump steak
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 tsp cracked black peppercorns
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the rump into cubes and season well.
  2. Mix the sour cream with the cracked pepper for the dip.
  3. Sear the steak hard in a smoking pan in batches.
  4. Toss with the butter and garlic. Serve on toothpicks with the dip.

Green code crackersVegetarian

Lavash strips baked crisp with green parsley and dill salt.

Prep 10 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 2 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 3 lavash breads
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Blitz the parsley and dill with the salt until green.
  2. Brush the lavash with oil, sprinkle with the herb salt and cut into long thin strips.
  3. Bake until crisp.

Tasty Wheat honey oat clusters

Crunchy oats, puffed grains and seeds in brown butter and sugar, how Mouse imagines it tasted.

How it is made
  • 60g butter
  • 80g honey
  • 40g brown sugar
  • 200g rolled oats
  • 50g puffed wheat
  • 60g mixed seeds
  1. Crunchy clusters of oats, puffed wheat, honey, brown butter and toasted seeds, baked golden and broken into chunks, much better than the ship's gruel. Heat the oven to 160°C. Brown 60g butter in a pan until nutty, then stir in 80g honey and 40g brown sugar. Toss with 200g rolled oats, 50g puffed wheat, 60g mixed seeds and a pinch of salt. Press firmly onto a lined tray and bake 25 minutes until golden. Cool completely on the tray, then break into clusters.

Mains

Cypher's steak

Dry-brined sirloin basted in butter and thyme, red wine pan sauce.

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min, then 24 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry-brine the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 70g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 125ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  1. Salt the steaks and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Sear hard in a hot pan.
  3. Baste with part of the butter and thyme for a minute, then rest the steaks.
  4. Add the wine and stock to the pan and reduce by half, then whisk in the rest of the butter.
  5. Slice the steak and pour over the sauce.

Neo's street noodles

Wheat strands with spicy pork mince, chilli oil, bok choy and peanuts.

How it is made
  • 400g pork mince
  • 5 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 3 tbsp chilli oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Sichuan peppercorns
  • 500g fresh wheat noodles
  • 4 baby bok choy
  • 40g roasted peanuts
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Wheat noodles tossed with spicy pork mince, sesame and chilli oil sauce, Sichuan pepper and blanched bok choy, topped with crushed peanuts and spring onion. Fry 400g pork mince in 1 tbsp oil until crisp, then add 2 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine. Mix 3 tbsp tahini, 3 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp black vinegar, 3 tbsp chilli oil, 1 tsp sugar, 1 crushed garlic clove and half a tsp ground Sichuan pepper. Cook 500g fresh wheat noodles and 4 halved baby bok choy for 2 minutes. Divide the sauce between 4 bowls, add the noodles and bok choy, top with pork, peanuts and spring onion and toss.

The Oracle's cookiesVegetarian

Chocolate chip cookies finished with flaky salt.

Prep 9 min, cook 12 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days chilled, baked cookies up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g choc chips
  • pinch of sea salt flakes
  1. Brown the butter and cool.
  2. Beat with the sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips.
  3. Rest the dough overnight in the fridge.
  4. Roll into 16 balls and bake at 180°C until golden.
  5. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Drinks

Red pill or blue pill gin fizz

Two gin fizzes: red with raspberry and cranberry, blue with curaçao.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Two red and two blue, choose carefully
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml gin
  • 2 lemons
  • 30ml raspberry syrup
  • 60ml cranberry juice
  • 30ml blue curacao
  • 15ml sugar syrup
  • 350ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. For the red drinks, shake half the gin with half the lemon juice, the raspberry syrup and cranberry juice with ice and strain into two ice-filled glasses.
  2. For the blue drinks, shake the rest of the gin and lemon juice with the blue curacao and sugar syrup and strain into two ice-filled glasses.
  3. Top all four with soda water and add a lemon wheel.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Neo first eats the gruel on the Nebuchadnezzar.
