Snacks

Radishes with butter and sea saltVegetarian

Radishes and baby carrots with soft butter and flaky salt

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep cool
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch radishes, leaves on
  • 200g baby carrots
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Wash the radishes and pile them on a board with the baby carrots.
  2. Set out the soft butter and a pile of sea salt flakes.

Brie, fig jam and ham baguette toasts

Toasted baguette: brie with fig jam, and Dijon with folded ham

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 150g brie
  • 2 tbsp fig jam
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 150g leg ham
  1. Slice the baguette and toast until golden.
  2. Top half with brie and fig jam.
  3. Spread the rest with Dijon and top with folded ham.
  4. Lay out on a wooden board.

Canal Saint-Martin thyme salt crispsOn the printed menu

Paper thin potatoes fried crisp, tossed with herby salt and lemon zest, garlic mayo to dip

How it is made
  • 3 large washed potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • sea salt flakes
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 150g mayonnaise
  1. Potatoes sliced paper thin on a mandoline, fried until crisp and tossed with thyme salt and lemon zest, with a garlicky aioli for dipping. Slice 3 large washed potatoes 1 to 2mm thick on a mandoline. Soak in cold water for 30 minutes, drain and dry very well on tea towels. Heat oil in a deep saucepan to 170C and fry in small batches for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until golden. Drain on paper towel. Crush 1 tsp sea salt with 1 tsp thyme leaves and the zest of half a lemon and toss through. Stir a crushed garlic clove into 150g mayonnaise for the aioli.

Mains

Paris bistro chicken

Chicken thighs and mushrooms in a white wine cream sauce, over mash

Prep 18 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 250ml white wine
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk, for the mash
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and brown it in the oil.
  3. Add the mushrooms and cook until golden.
  4. Pour in the wine and simmer.
  5. Boil the potatoes, mash with butter and milk.
  6. Stir the cream into the chicken and serve over the mash.

Montmartre café croque madameOn the printed menu

Ham and gruyère under béchamel, grilled bubbling, topped with a runny fried egg and chives

How it is made
  • 1 loaf white sandwich bread
  • 60g butter
  • 30g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 200g gruyère
  • 200g sliced ham
  • Dijon mustard
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 bunch chives
  • nutmeg
  1. Ham and gruyère between slices of white bread, spread with béchamel, grilled until bubbling and blistered, topped with a runny fried egg and chives. Make béchamel: melt 30g butter, stir in 30g plain flour for 1 minute, whisk in 300ml milk and simmer 3 minutes until thick, then add nutmeg, salt and 40g grated gruyère. Spread 8 slices of white bread with Dijon, fill 4 sandwiches with 200g ham and 120g gruyère. Toast in a buttered pan 3 minutes a side. Spread the tops thickly with béchamel, add more gruyère and grill at 220C for 4 minutes until blistered. Top each with a fried egg and chives.

Amélie's crème brûléeVegetarian

Vanilla bean custard in ramekins with a burnt sugar crust

Prep 15 min, cook 43 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, torch just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar, for the custard
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the top
  1. Heat the cream with the split vanilla bean.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the custard sugar, pour in the cream and strain into ramekins.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150C until just set.
  4. Chill the custards until firm.
  5. Sprinkle with sugar and torch. Crack the top with a spoon.

Drinks

Kir royale with cassisVegetarian

Dry sparkling wine over a splash of blackcurrant liqueur

Prep 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the flutes and the sparkling wine up to 1 day ahead; pour just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bottle dry sparkling wine (750 ml), chilled
  • 60ml creme de cassis
  • 8 raspberries
  1. Chill the flutes and the sparkling wine for at least 2 hours.
  2. Pour the cassis into each flute.
  3. Tilt the glass and top slowly with the sparkling wine. Drop a raspberry into each and serve.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Amélie cracks the top of her crème brûlée with a spoon.
