Maple syrup popcornVegetarian

Buddy puts syrup on everything

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 60g popping corn
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 200ml maple syrup
  • 40g butter
  1. Pop the corn in the oil in a covered pot.
  2. Boil the maple syrup with the butter to 120°C.
  3. Pour over the popcorn, toss quickly and spread on a lined tray to set. Break into clusters.

Candy cane pretzel rodsVegetarian

Buddy's long walk south from the North Pole

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep cool
Recipe for 4
  • 12 pretzel rods
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 60g crushed candy canes
  1. Melt the white chocolate in a bowl over barely simmering water.
  2. Dip each pretzel rod two-thirds into the chocolate and roll immediately in the crushed candy canes.
  3. Set on baking paper until firm.

New York street hot dogs

Griddled frankfurts, yellow mustard, tomato-stewed onions and sauerkraut, son of a nutcracker

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Tomato onions up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 4 large hot dog franks
  • 200g sauerkraut
  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Cook the onions in the butter over medium-low heat until soft.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer.
  4. Simmer the franks in water, drain and griddle until browned. Warm the sauerkraut and steam the buns.
  5. Assemble with mustard and the tomato onions.

Snowball fight cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes under fluffy white coconut frosting

Prep 5 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 40ml full cream milk
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 40g shredded coconut
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Beat some of the butter with the sugar, add the egg and vanilla, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide between 4 large cupcake cases and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the cream cheese with the remaining butter and icing sugar. Dome over the cakes and roll in the coconut.

Buddy's maple mochaNo alcohol

Buddy's coffee, sweetened the elf way

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml milk
  • 100g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 4 tbsp maple syrup
  • 200ml strong brewed coffee
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bag mini marshmallows
  • 2 candy canes, crushed
  1. Whip the cream.
  2. Heat the milk with the chocolate and most of the maple syrup, whisking until smooth and steaming.
  3. Stir in the coffee, or leave it out for the kids.
  4. Pour into 4 mugs and top with cream, marshmallows, a drizzle of maple syrup and crushed candy cane.

When to serve

Serve the coffee when Buddy bursts into the diner to congratulate the staff.
