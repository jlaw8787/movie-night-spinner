Snacks

Admiral Benbow honey mustard sausages

Roasted mini sausages glazed in honey and wholegrain mustard.

Prep 16 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 20 chipolatas
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 bunch of thyme
  1. Twist each chipolata in half and snip apart into little sausages.
  2. Roast on a lined tray at 200°C, 15 min.
  3. Mix the honey, wholegrain mustard and soy sauce.
  4. Pour over the sausages, toss and roast until sticky and glazed.
  5. Scatter with thyme leaves and serve on toothpicks.

Hispaniola cheddar ship's biscuitsVegetarian

Crisp cheddar and black pepper crackers with apple and pickled onion.

Prep 26 min, cook 14 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Biscuits up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 120g vintage cheddar
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 apples
  • 1 jar of pickled onions
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour, then stir in the grated cheddar, pepper and salt.
  2. Add cold water and press into a dough.
  3. Chill the dough for 20 minutes.
  4. Roll to 3mm, cut into 5cm squares and prick with a fork.
  5. Bake at 180°C until golden.
  6. Cool on a rack, 10 min.
  7. Serve with sliced apples and pickled onions.

Long John's galley fish stew

White fish, prawns and potato in a smoky tomato broth, with garlic toast from the galley.

Prep 13 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Stew up to 1 day, add the seafood when reheating
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 400g crushed tomatoes (tin)
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 400g potatoes
  • 500g firm white fish fillets
  • 200g green prawns
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  1. Dice the onion, capsicum and potatoes and crush the garlic.
  2. Fry the onion, capsicum and garlic in oil, 5 min.
  3. Add smoked paprika, crushed tomatoes, fish stock and potatoes.
  4. Simmer until the potato is nearly tender.
  5. Add the fish chunks and prawns and simmer until just cooked.
  6. Stir in parsley.
  7. Toast the sourdough, rub with garlic and serve alongside, 5 min.

Billy Bones' Benbow Inn steak and mushroom pie

Steak and mushroom under puff pastry, Billy Bones' usual by the fire.

Prep 12 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chuck steak, diced
  • 250g mushrooms, quartered
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  1. Toss the beef in the flour and brown in the oil, then add the onion and mushrooms, 12 min.
  2. Add the stock and Worcestershire and simmer covered until tender. Cool a little.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spoon into a pie dish, cover with pastry and brush with egg. Bake until golden.
  4. Boil the peas and roughly mash with the butter, 6 min.

Treasure Island chocolate coin browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies piled with gold chocolate coins, buried treasure.

Prep 21 min, cook 35 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 4 plain sweet biscuits
  • 16 gold chocolate coins
  • 1 jar of gold sprinkles
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter and let it cool slightly, 10 min.
  2. Whisk in the sugar, then the eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour and cocoa.
  4. Spread into a lined 20cm square tin.
  5. Bake at 170°C until just set.
  6. Cool completely in the tin, 30 min.
  7. Crush the biscuits and sprinkle over like sand.
  8. Cut into squares and top each with a gold chocolate coin and gold sprinkles.

Captain Smollett's ginger beer fruit punchNo alcohol

Ginger beer, pineapple, lime and orange over ice, a cabin boy's punch.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L ginger beer
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 2 limes
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. In a jug, mix the ginger beer, pineapple juice and lime and orange juice.
  2. Fill tall glasses or tin mugs with ice and pour over.
  3. Garnish with lime wedges and orange slices.
