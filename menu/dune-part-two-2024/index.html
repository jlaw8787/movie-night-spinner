Snacks

Spice-roasted chickpeasVegetarian

Crunchy chickpeas roasted with cumin, smoked paprika and cinnamon.

Prep 8 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Roast up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; re-crisp in a 180°C oven for 5 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chickpeas
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Drain and dry the chickpeas well.
  2. Toss with the oil, the cumin, the smoked paprika, the cinnamon and salt.
  3. Roast at 200°C for 35 minutes, shaking twice, until crunchy.
  4. Cool 5 minutes before serving.

Stuffed desert datesVegetarian

Medjool dates filled with goat's cheese and almond, honey drizzle.

Prep 14 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Split and fill the dates up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; warm the honey and drizzle just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 medjool dates
  • 100g goat's cheese
  • 16 almonds
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Split the medjool dates and remove the stones.
  2. Fill each with a little goat's cheese and a toasted almond.
  3. Lay on a plate and drizzle with the warm honey just before serving.

Sietch flatbread with date butter

Bread in the deep desert sietch.

How it is made
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 100g butter
  • 4 Medjool dates
  • ground cardamom
  • sea salt flakes
  1. Soft yoghurt flatbreads blistered in a dry pan, brushed with butter whipped with Medjool dates, cardamom and flaky salt. Mix 300g self-raising flour, 250g Greek yoghurt and a pinch of salt into a soft dough and rest 15 minutes. Divide into 8 and roll thin. Cook in a very hot dry pan 1 to 2 minutes a side until blistered. Blitz 100g soft butter with 4 pitted Medjool dates, a pinch of ground cardamom and a pinch of salt until smooth. Brush over the hot breads, sprinkle with flaky salt and tear to share.

Stilgar's spice-rubbed lamb

Cumin, coriander and cinnamon lamb roasted until it pulls apart.

Prep 14 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; roast on the day and cook the rice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg lamb shoulder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  1. Rub the lamb shoulder with the cumin, the coriander, the cinnamon, chilli and salt.
  2. Roast covered at 160°C for 3.5 hours until it pulls apart.
  3. Steep a pinch of saffron in hot water and cook the basmati rice with it until golden and fluffy.

Fremen spiced red lentil stew

Red lentils stewed with cumin and turmeric, every drop of water counted.

How it is made
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • ground cumin
  • ground turmeric
  • ground coriander
  • 300g red lentils
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 1L vegetable stock
  • 1 lemon
  • 40g butter
  • chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Red lentils simmered thick with onion, garlic, tomato, cumin and turmeric, finished with lemon, a sizzle of chilli butter and fresh coriander. Fry 1 diced onion in 2 tbsp oil for 5 minutes. Add 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp grated ginger, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp coriander for 1 minute. Stir in 300g rinsed red lentils, 400g tinned tomatoes and 1L vegetable stock and simmer 25 minutes, stirring, until thick. Add the juice of 1 lemon and salt. Melt 40g butter with 1 tsp chilli flakes and cumin seeds until sizzling, pour over and top with coriander. Serve with flatbread.

Shai-Hulud melange spice cakeVegetarian

Moist honey cake with cardamom, cinnamon and ginger.

Prep 8 min, cook 81 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; brush with warm honey on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 180g honey
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml milk
  1. Melt the butter with the honey and the brown sugar.
  2. Mix with the self-raising flour, the cardamom, the cinnamon, the ginger, the eggs and the milk.
  3. Bake in a loaf tin at 170°C for 50 minutes and brush with warm honey.

Drinks

Cardamom spice coffee with date syrupNo alcohol

Coffee simmered with cardamom, cinnamon and clove, sweetened with dates.

Prep 3 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Simmer the cardamom, cinnamon and cloves in the water up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat, steep the coffee and froth the milk when serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • whole cloves, to taste
  • ground coffee, to taste
  • 3 tbsp date syrup
  • 200ml milk
  • 600ml water
  1. Lightly crush the cardamom pods.
  2. Put them in a saucepan with the water, the cinnamon stick and the cloves and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the finely ground coffee, take off the heat and steep 4 minutes, then strain through a fine sieve or plunger.
  4. Stir in the date syrup.
  5. Froth the hot milk and spoon over the small cups of the coffee, with a pinch of cinnamon on top.
