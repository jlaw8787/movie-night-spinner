Snacks

Sabacc rosemary nuts and olivesVegetarian

Warm roasted almonds and cashews with rosemary and smoked salt, mixed olives.

Prep 0 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g almonds
  • 150g cashews
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tsp smoked salt
  • 150g green olives
  • 150g kalamata olives
  1. Toss the almonds and cashews with the oil, rosemary and smoked salt on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180°C until fragrant. Serve warm in one bowl beside a second bowl of mixed olives.

Corellia street dumplings

Pan-fried pork and cabbage dumplings with chilli oil and black vinegar.

Prep 55 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1/4 small wombok, finely shredded
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp chilli oil
  • 3 tbsp black vinegar
  1. Mix the pork with the wombok, ginger and salt.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Fry in oil until crisp underneath, add a splash of water, cover and steam for 5 minutes.
  4. Serve with chilli oil and black vinegar.

Han's golden dice fried cheeseOn the printed menu

Haloumi cubes fried golden with smoked paprika and honey, like Han's lucky gold dice.

How it is made
  • 500g haloumi
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  • 1 packet black sesame seeds
  1. Cubes of haloumi dusted in smoked paprika and fried golden on every side, drizzled with hot honey and lemon, with pips of toasted sesame like dice spots. Cut 2 blocks of haloumi, about 500g, into 2cm cubes and pat dry. Toss in 2 tbsp flour mixed with 1 tsp smoked paprika. Fry in a little oil over medium high heat for 1 minute a side until golden all over. Warm 3 tbsp honey with a pinch of chilli flakes and the juice of half a lemon. Drizzle over the cubes and dot with toasted black and white sesame seeds.

Mains

Vandor lodge roast pork knuckle

Pork knuckle roasted until the crackling blisters, with buttered potatoes.

Prep 15 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Simmer a day ahead, roast fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 pork knuckles
  • 1 brown onion, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 800g potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 500g sauerkraut
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Simmer the knuckles with the onion, bay leaves and caraway seeds for an hour and a half.
  2. Lift out, pat very dry, score and rub with salt.
  3. Roast at 220°C until the crackling blisters.
  4. Boil the potatoes and toss with butter and parsley. Warm the sauerkraut with a little cooking liquid.
  5. Serve with wholegrain mustard.

First Light duck with cherry and portOn the printed menu

Rendered till the skin crackles, sliced pink on celeriac purée, crisp kale, for the yacht party.

How it is made
  • 1 celeriac
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 40g butter
  • 4 duck breasts
  • 100ml port
  • 200g frozen pitted cherries
  • 150ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch kale
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Duck breasts scored and rendered until the skin crackles, sliced pink over silky celeriac purée with a cherry and port sauce and crisp kale. Simmer a peeled, chopped celeriac in milk and water for 20 minutes, drain and blitz with 40g butter. Score 4 duck breasts, start skin side down in a cold pan and cook over medium heat for 8 minutes, then flip and roast at 200°C for 6 minutes. Rest. Pour off most fat, add 100ml port, 200g pitted cherries and 150ml stock and reduce until glossy. Bake kale leaves with oil until crisp.

Coaxium popping candy chocolate barkVegetarian

Dark chocolate bark with popping candy and freeze-dried raspberries.

Prep 3 min, cook 10 min, then 37 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 2 sachets popping candy
  • 15g freeze-dried raspberries
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Line a tray with baking paper. Melt the chocolate over barely simmering water, stirring until smooth.
  2. Spread thin on the tray. While still soft, scatter with popping candy, raspberries and salt.
  3. Set in the fridge for 30 minutes, then snap into jagged shards.

Lando's cape cocktailVegetarian

Bourbon, honey syrup and orange bitters stirred over a big ice cube.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, build fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp warm water
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 8 dashes orange bitters
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the honey into the warm water to make a syrup.
  2. For each drink, stir the bourbon, syrup and bitters with ice, then strain over one big ice cube.
  3. Twist a strip of orange peel over the top.
