Snacks

Sixties devilled eggs and cheese hedgehogVegetarian

Paprika devilled eggs and cheese picks on a foil hedgehog, sixties party style.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 200g cheddar, cubed
  • 1/2 pineapple, cubed
  • 1 grapefruit, halved
  1. Boil the eggs, cool, peel and halve.
  2. Mash the yolks with mayonnaise, pipe back into the whites and dust with paprika.
  3. Thread cheddar and pineapple onto toothpicks and stick them into the grapefruit, cut side down.

Silver Surfer foil potatoesVegetarian

Foil-roasted baby potatoes, butter, rosemary and a little more garlic.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Wrap the potatoes in their foil parcels up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; add 5 minutes to the roast
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes, halved
  • 100g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Divide the potatoes among squares of foil and add butter, garlic, rosemary and salt to each.
  2. Wrap tightly into parcels and roast at 200°C.
  3. Open at the table and add a spoon of sour cream.

H.E.R.B.I.E.'s retro prawn cocktail cups

Cooked prawns on shredded iceberg in little glasses, pink sauce, paprika and a lemon wedge.

How it is made
  • 600g cooked prawns
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  1. Cooked prawns on shredded iceberg in little glasses, under a pink cocktail sauce with a dusting of paprika and a lemon wedge. Peel 600g cooked prawns, keeping 8 whole for the tops, and chop the rest roughly. Mix 120g mayo, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, a squeeze of lemon and a dash of Tabasco. Shred half an iceberg lettuce finely and divide between 8 small glasses. Spoon in the chopped prawns and the sauce, top each with a whole prawn, a dusting of paprika and a lemon wedge.

Mains

Retro roast chicken with glazed carrots

Butter, lemon and thyme roast chicken, glazed carrots, mashed potatoes.

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the butter, lemon and thyme up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; roast and cook the sides on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 whole chicken
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 lemons
  • 6 thyme sprigs
  • 4 carrots, sliced
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 40g butter for mash
  • 500g frozen peas
  1. Rub the chicken with butter, lemon and thyme.
  2. Roast at 200°C until golden and cooked through.
  3. Glaze the carrots in butter and a little sugar.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter and cook the peas.
  5. Serve everything on one big platter like a sixties advertisement.

Space race glazed meatloaf

Beef mince under sticky tomato and brown sugar, sliced thick, buttered beans and mash.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 packet breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 jar mustard
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g green beans
  1. Beef meatloaf with onion and breadcrumbs under a sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, sliced thick with buttered green beans and mash. Mix 800g beef mince, 1 grated onion, 60g breadcrumbs, 2 eggs, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce and salt, then press into a loaf tin. Mix 100ml tomato sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tsp mustard and spread on top. Bake at 180°C for 1 hour and rest 10 minutes. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with butter and milk. Blanch 300g green beans for 3 minutes and toss with butter.

Sweets

Galactus-sized jelly mould

Strawberry jelly set in a big ring mould with grapes.

Prep 23 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 packets strawberry jelly crystals
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  • 300g green grapes
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Make up the jelly with a little less water than the packet says.
  2. Pour a layer into a ring mould with sliced strawberries and grapes.
  3. Chill until set, then fill with the rest.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Turn out and serve with whipped cream.

Drinks

Space race orange cream shakeNo alcohol

Orange juice blended with vanilla ice cream and milk, whipped cream on top.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; measure the juice and milk into a jug up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml orange juice
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  • 4 orange slices
  1. Blend the orange juice, ice cream, milk and vanilla until thick and creamy.
  2. Pour into tall glasses and top with whipped cream and an orange slice.
