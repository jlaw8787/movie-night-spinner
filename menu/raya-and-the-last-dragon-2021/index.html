Snacks

Boun's shrimp boat bites

Prawns grilled with garlic, chilli and lime, with a fish sauce dip

Prep 23 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead; peel and skewer the prawns up to 4 hours ahead, chilled; grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, peeled
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 8 skewers
  1. Peel the prawns. Toss with the garlic, red chillies and lime zest.
  2. Mix the fish sauce, lime juice and sugar for the dipping sauce.
  3. Thread the prawns onto skewers.
  4. Grill in batches, turning once, until pink.
  5. Serve from the boat with the dipping sauce.

Talon night market spring rolls

Prawns, vermicelli and herbs in rice paper, with a peanut hoisin dip

Prep 33 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the peanut dipping sauce up to 3 days ahead and prep the fillings up to 1 day ahead, chilled; roll on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 rice paper sheets
  • 300g cooked prawns
  • 100g rice vermicelli
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  • 6 lettuce leaves
  • 3 tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
  1. Soak the vermicelli in boiling water until soft, then drain.
  2. Prepare the prawns, mint leaves, coriander leaves and lettuce.
  3. Warm the peanut butter with the hoisin and a splash of water for a thick dipping sauce.
  4. Soak the rice paper sheets one at a time, fill with prawns, vermicelli, herbs and lettuce and roll tightly.

Mains

Boun's prawn congee

Creamy jasmine rice porridge with prawns, lemongrass, ginger and bamboo

Prep 20 min, cook 64 min · Make ahead: Congee up to 2 days, add prawns when reheating
Recipe for 4
  • 200g jasmine rice
  • 2l chicken stock
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, bruised
  • 1 piece ginger (4cm), sliced
  • 400g green prawns, peeled
  • 1 can sliced bamboo shoots, drained
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp caster sugar
  1. Bruise the lemongrass and slice the ginger.
  2. Simmer the rice in the stock with the lemongrass and ginger, stirring often, until creamy.
  3. Peel the prawns.
  4. Add the prawns and bamboo shoots for the last few minutes.
  5. Top with chilli and a pinch of sugar.

Sisu's mango sticky riceVegetarian

Steamed glutinous rice soaked in sweet coconut milk, with mango

Prep 15 min, cook 524 min · Make ahead: Soak the rice the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 300g glutinous rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • pinch of salt
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Soak the rice in cold water overnight, then drain.
  2. Steam the rice until tender.
  3. Warm most of the coconut milk with the sugar and salt.
  4. Pour over the hot rice and rest.
  5. Slice the mango.
  6. Serve with the remaining coconut milk and sesame seeds.

Tuk Tuk's lemongrass lime iced teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Black tea steeped with lemongrass and palm sugar, with lime, over ice

Prep 10 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 lemongrass stalks
  • 1l boiling water
  • 3 black tea bags
  • 60g palm sugar or brown sugar
  • 2 limes
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Bruise the lemongrass with the back of a knife and cut into pieces.
  2. Pour the boiling water over the lemongrass with the tea bags and sugar, stir and steep.
  3. Remove the tea bags, leave the lemongrass in as it cools, then strain and chill.
  4. Pour over ice with lime juice and a mint sprig.
