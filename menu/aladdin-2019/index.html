Snacks

Sumac and sesame flatbreads with hummus and labnehVegetarian

Flatbreads charred and dusted with sumac and sesame, hummus and labneh to scoop.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; brush with oil and sprinkle with sumac and sesame up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp sumac and sesame
  • 200g hummus
  • 200g labneh
  1. Brush the flatbreads with oil and sprinkle generously with sumac and sesame.
  2. Grill until blistered, 6 min.
  3. Tear into pieces and serve with hummus and labneh drizzled with more oil.

Almond butter stuffed datesVegetarian

Medjool dates filled with almond butter and dipped in dark chocolate.

Prep 24 min, cook 4 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 medjool dates
  • 5 tbsp almond butter
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 60g pistachios, crushed
  1. Slit the dates and remove the stones.
  2. Fill each with almond butter and press closed.
  3. Melt the chocolate gently, 4 min.
  4. Dip the dates halfway in chocolate and roll the end in crushed pistachios.
  5. Set in the fridge, 30 min.

Cave of Wonders golden falafel

Crunchy herb and cumin chickpea balls, green inside, lemony tahini, pickled turnip, warm pita.

How it is made
  • 250g dried chickpeas
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • ground cumin
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 100g tahini
  • 1 lemon
  • pickled turnips
  • pita bread
  1. Crunchy golden falafel bright green inside with herbs and cumin, with lemony tahini, pickled turnip and warm pita, treasure from the Cave of Wonders. Soak 250g dried chickpeas overnight and drain well. Blitz with 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, a bunch of parsley, a bunch of coriander, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp bicarb soda until fine. Shape into 20 balls and fry at 180°C for 4 minutes until deep golden. Whisk 100g tahini with the juice of 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove and about 80ml water. Serve with pickled turnip and warm pita.

Mains

Prince Ali's parade lamb

Lamb shoulder rubbed with cumin and cinnamon, saffron basmati rice.

Prep 13 min, cook 5 hr 20 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Roast the lamb up to 1 day ahead, keep covered in the fridge and reheat; cook the rice and toast the almonds on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g basmati rice
  • pinch of saffron
  • 100g almonds
  1. Rub the lamb with garlic, cumin, cinnamon, salt and oil.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C until it pulls apart, 5 hr.
  3. Rest the lamb, 15 min.
  4. Toast the almonds in a dry pan, 5 min.
  5. Cook the basmati with the saffron, 15 min.
  6. Pile the lamb over the rice and scatter with almonds.

Sultan's palace apricot chicken tagine

Thighs slow cooked with apricot, ginger, saffron and honey, on couscous, for Jasmine, born to rule.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • olive oil
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 knob ginger
  • ground cinnamon
  • ground cumin
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 150g dried apricots
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 300g couscous
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 40g flaked almonds
  1. Chicken thighs slow cooked with onion, dried apricots, ginger, saffron and honey until sticky, over herby couscous with toasted almonds. Brown 1kg chicken thighs in oil and remove. Soften 2 sliced onions, add 2 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp cumin and a pinch of saffron. Return the chicken with 500ml chicken stock, 150g dried apricots and 2 tbsp honey. Cover and simmer 45 minutes, then uncover and cook 15 minutes until sticky. Pour 300g couscous into 375ml boiling stock, cover 5 minutes, fluff and stir through herbs and 40g toasted almonds.

Sweets

Magic carpet baklavaVegetarian

Crisp filo with pistachio and walnut filling, cut in diamonds.

Prep 21 min, cook 1 hr 5 min, 3 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 375g filo pastry, thawed
  • 250g butter, melted
  • 150g pistachios, chopped
  • 150g walnuts, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 300g honey
  • 100ml water
  1. Chop the pistachios and walnuts and mix with the cardamom.
  2. Layer filo sheets brushed with melted butter in a tin with the nut mix between every few layers.
  3. Cut into diamonds.
  4. Bake at 180°C until golden, 40 min.
  5. Warm the honey with the water for the syrup and pour it over the hot baklava, 8 min.
  6. Leave to soak, 3 hr.

Drinks

Genie's blue mint iced teaNo alcohol

Butterfly pea flower and mint tea sweetened with honey, chilled.

Prep 9 min, cook 10 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Steep, sweeten and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; add the ice and fresh mint to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 2 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 3/4 cup mint leaves
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Pour the boiling water over the butterfly pea flowers and some mint, steep, then strain, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the honey.
  3. Chill the tea, 2 hr.
  4. Fill glasses with ice and fresh mint.
  5. Pour in the blue tea and serve with lemon wedges to squeeze in and change the colour.
