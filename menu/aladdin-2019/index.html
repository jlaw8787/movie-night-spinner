Snacks

Za'atar flatbreads with hummus and labnehVegetarian

Charred za'atar flatbread with hummus and labneh

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; brush with oil and sprinkle with za'atar up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp za'atar
  • 200g hummus
  • 200g labneh
  1. Brush the flatbreads with oil and sprinkle generously with za'atar.
  2. Grill until blistered.
  3. Tear into pieces and serve with hummus and labneh drizzled with more oil.

Almond butter stuffed datesVegetarian

Medjool dates filled with almond butter and dipped in dark chocolate

Prep 24 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 medjool dates
  • 5 tbsp almond butter
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 60g pistachios, crushed
  1. Slit the dates and remove the stones.
  2. Fill each with almond butter and press closed.
  3. Melt the chocolate gently.
  4. Dip the dates halfway in chocolate and roll the end in crushed pistachios.
  5. Set in the fridge.

Mains

Prince Ali's parade lamb

Lamb shoulder rubbed with cumin and cinnamon, saffron basmati rice

Prep 25 min, cook 325 min · Make ahead: Roast the lamb up to 1 day ahead, keep covered in the fridge and reheat; cook the rice and toast the almonds on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g basmati rice
  • pinch of saffron
  • 100g almonds
  1. Rub the lamb with garlic, cumin, cinnamon, salt and oil.
  2. Roast covered at 150C until it pulls apart.
  3. Rest the lamb.
  4. Toast the almonds in a dry pan.
  5. Cook the basmati with the saffron.
  6. Pile the lamb over the rice and scatter with almonds.

Sweets

Magic carpet baklavaVegetarian

Crisp filo with pistachio and walnut filling, cut in diamonds

Prep 38 min, cook 228 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 375g filo pastry, thawed
  • 250g butter, melted
  • 150g pistachios, chopped
  • 150g walnuts, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 300g honey
  • 100ml water
  1. Chop the pistachios and walnuts and mix with the cardamom.
  2. Layer filo sheets brushed with melted butter in a tin with the nut mix between every few layers.
  3. Cut into diamonds.
  4. Bake at 180C until golden.
  5. Warm the honey with the water for the syrup and pour it over the hot baklava.
  6. Leave to soak.

Genie's blue mint iced teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Butterfly pea flower and mint tea sweetened with honey, chilled

Prep 9 min, cook 130 min · Make ahead: Steep, sweeten and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; add the ice and fresh mint to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l boiling water
  • 2 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 3/4 cup mint leaves
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Pour the boiling water over the butterfly pea flowers and some mint, steep, then strain.
  2. Stir in the honey.
  3. Chill the tea.
  4. Fill glasses with ice and fresh mint.
  5. Pour in the blue tea and serve with lemon wedges to squeeze in and change the colour.
