Snacks

Bogey's party spinach dipVegetarian

Hot baked spinach and artichoke dip with cream cheese and mozzarella.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble and bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese, softened
  • 100g sour cream
  • 250g frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 1 jar artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
  1. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, spinach, artichokes, garlic and mozzarella and spoon into a baking dish.
  2. Bake at 190°C until bubbling. Meanwhile slice and toast the baguette, 20 min.
  3. Serve the dip hot with the toasted baguette.

Cameron's croque monsieur squares

Ham and gruyère sandwiches under creamy béchamel, grilled and cut small.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble and grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 200g leg ham
  • 200g gruyère, grated
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for a minute. Whisk in the milk and simmer until thick to make the bechamel, 8 min.
  2. Spread the bread with Dijon and fill with ham and some of the gruyère.
  3. Top with bechamel and the rest of the cheese. Grill until bubbling and golden, then cut into small squares, 15 min.

Mains

Prom night vodka penneVegetarian

Penne in a creamy tomato vodka sauce, the prom where Bianca punched Joey.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g penne
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 60ml vodka
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  • 1 cup basil leaves, torn
  1. Soften the garlic and chilli in the oil. Add the tomatoes and vodka and simmer, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the cream until the sauce turns rosy. Meanwhile cook the penne in salted water until al dente, 10 min.
  3. Toss the pasta through the sauce and finish with parmesan and basil.

Seattle salmon burgers with dill mayo

Chopped salmon with scallion, lemon zest and crumbs, seared, on a toasted bun with lettuce.

How it is made
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bottle rice vinegar
  • 600g skinless salmon fillet
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 lemon
  • 50g dry breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 butter lettuce
  1. Salmon chopped with spring onion, lemon zest and breadcrumbs, seared into patties and served in toasted buns with dill mayo, lettuce and pickled cucumber. Slice a Lebanese cucumber thinly and soak in 3 tbsp rice vinegar with a pinch of sugar and salt. Chop 600g skinless salmon finely and mix with 2 sliced spring onions, lemon zest, 50g panko, an egg and salt. Shape into 4 patties and chill 20 minutes. Sear in oil for 3 minutes a side. Mix 4 tbsp mayonnaise with chopped dill and lemon juice. Build in toasted brioche buns with rocket and pickles.

Sweets

Ten things browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies cut into ten squares, one for each thing Kat hates.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 125g raspberries
  • 50g mixed nuts, chopped
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together and let it cool slightly. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour, 15 min.
  2. Pour into a lined tin and bake at 170°C. Cool completely in the tin, 25 min.
  3. Cut into ten squares and top each differently with raspberries, nuts, salt flakes or anything you like.

Drinks

Club Skunk iced vanilla latteNo alcohol

Espresso, vanilla and cold milk over ice, sipped to the band at Club Skunk.

Prep 9 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml water
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 250ml very strong coffee, or 4 espresso shots
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and vanilla bean for 3 minutes. Cool and remove the bean.
  2. Brew the strong coffee and let it cool, 5 min.
  3. Shake the coffee with 2 tablespoons of the syrup and a handful of ice until frothy.
  4. Fill four tall glasses with ice and the cold milk, then pour the frothy coffee slowly over the top so it sits in layers.
