Snacks

Delta Nu popcornVegetarian

Popcorn drizzled with white chocolate and sea salt.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 200g white chocolate
  • pink food colouring, a drop
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the corn and spread on a lined tray.
  2. Melt the white chocolate with a drop of pink colouring and drizzle all over.
  3. Sprinkle with sea salt flakes and leave to set, then break into clusters.

Bruiser's pork and sage sausage rolls

Pork mince with onion, apple and herbs in flaky puff, sesame topped, baked golden, tomato relish.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green apple
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 jar tomato relish
  1. Pork mince with onion, sage and a little apple rolled in flaky puff pastry, sesame on top, baked golden and served with tomato relish. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix 500g pork mince with 1 finely chopped onion, 1 grated apple, 1 tbsp chopped sage, 40g breadcrumbs, 1 egg, salt and pepper. Cut 2 thawed puff pastry sheets in half, lay a sausage of filling along each, roll up and seal with beaten egg. Cut into 4cm pieces, brush with egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Bake on a lined tray for 20 to 25 minutes until golden. Serve warm with tomato relish.

Mains

Warner's break up penne

Penne with chicken, garlic, sun-dried tomato, spinach and cream sauce.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 50g sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 500g penne, cooked weight
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  1. Cook the penne and drain.
  2. Sear the chicken in the oil until golden.
  3. Add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes, then the cream, and simmer.
  4. Wilt in the spinach and toss through the penne with parmesan.

Harvard clam chowder in bread bowls

Round loaf chowder for Elle's Boston arrival.

How it is made
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml fish stock
  • 400ml milk
  • 500g potatoes
  • 1kg vongole clams
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 small cob loaves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  1. Bacon, onion and potato simmered with clams, milk and cream into a thick New England chowder, ladled into hollowed cob loaves with crackers. Fry 150g diced bacon in a pot until crisp. Add 1 diced onion and 2 celery sticks and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 300ml fish stock, 400ml milk and 500g diced potato. Simmer 15 minutes until the potato is tender. Add 1kg clams (vongole) and cook covered for 5 minutes until open, then pick out the shells. Stir in 150ml cream, thyme and pepper. Hollow out 4 small cob loaves and ladle the chowder in.

White chocolate strawberriesVegetarian

Strawberries dipped in white chocolate with sprinkles.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 3 tbsp pink sprinkles
  1. Melt the white chocolate.
  2. Dip the strawberries halfway and roll in pink sprinkles straight away.
  3. Set on baking paper.

Pink lemonade cupcakesVegetarian

Lemon cupcakes topped with raspberry buttercream.

Prep 22 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 125g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 60g raspberries
  1. Beat the butter for the cakes with the sugar, add the eggs and lemon zest, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Spoon into a lined muffin tin and bake at 175°C.
  3. Cool the cupcakes.
  4. Beat the butter for the icing with the icing sugar and crushed raspberries until pink and pipe on top.

Drinks

Pink lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice and raspberries with syrup, over ice.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 125g raspberries
  • 6 lemons
  • 1.5 L cold water
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Crush the raspberries in a jug with the lemon juice, the syrup and cold water, then strain.
  3. Pour over ice in glasses.
