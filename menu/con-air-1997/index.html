Snacks

Jailbird peanutsVegetarian

Honey roasted peanuts with chilli flakes and smoked salt

Prep 10 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight container; warm on a tray just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g honey-roasted peanuts
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 pinch smoked salt
  1. Toss the peanuts with chilli flakes and smoked salt.
  2. Warm on a tray at 160°C.
  3. Cool for a few minutes and divide into small paper bags.

Vegas strip prawns

Poached prawns served cold with horseradish cocktail sauce

Prep 23 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Poach, chill and peel the prawns up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead and serve on the lettuce just before
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 1 tray ice
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 dash hot sauce
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  1. Poach the prawns until just pink.
  2. Plunge into a bowl of cold water with the ice, then peel.
  3. Mix the tomato sauce with horseradish, lemon juice and hot sauce.
  4. Serve on shredded iceberg in martini glasses with lemon wedges.

Lerner Airfield smoky beef jerkyOn the printed menu

Thin beef in soy, brown sugar and smoked paprika, dried slow until chewy

How it is made
  • 800g beef topside
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • black peppercorns
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  1. Thin-sliced beef marinated in soy, brown sugar, smoked paprika and black pepper, slow-dried in the oven until chewy with a sticky, smoky bite. Freeze 800g topside for 1 hour, then slice 3mm thick across the grain. Mix 100ml soy sauce, 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 tsp cracked black pepper and 1 tsp garlic powder. Marinate the beef overnight. Pat dry and lay on wire racks over trays. Dry at 70°C with the door slightly open for 4 hours until leathery but still bendy. Cool and store airtight.

Mains

Vegas buffet steak

Seared sirloin with garlic butter, plus a baked potato

Prep 15 min, cook 68 min · Make ahead: Mash the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; bake the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and reheat at 200°C for 15 minutes, then sear the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 120g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C.
  2. Mash the butter with crushed garlic.
  3. Sear the steaks hard in a hot pan with the oil.
  4. Rest the steaks and put a slice of garlic butter on each.
  5. Split the potatoes and load with sour cream and chives.

Desert airfield bacon cheeseburgerOn the printed menu

Thick chuck patty, melted cheddar, sticky onion and smoky barbecue mayo on brioche

How it is made
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 2 onions
  • 20g butter
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 brioche buns
  • 3 tbsp mayo
  • 2 tbsp barbecue sauce
  1. Thick chuck patty griddled to medium with melted cheddar, crisp bacon, sticky grilled onion and smoky barbecue mayo on a toasted brioche bun. Shape 800g chuck mince into 4 patties 2cm thick. Cook 2 sliced onions slowly in butter for 15 minutes. Grill 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Season the patties and griddle 4 minutes a side on high heat, then top with 2 slices cheddar each, add a splash of water and cover 1 minute. Mix 3 tbsp mayo with 2 tbsp barbecue sauce. Toast 4 brioche buns and build with sauce, patty, bacon and onions.

Casey's birthday cakeVegetarian

Sponge cake with buttercream and coconut, cut and shaped like a rabbit

Prep 45 min · Make ahead: Cut and ice the cake up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; add the coconut and the bow on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 round sponge cake
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 350g icing sugar
  • 100g shredded coconut
  • pink food colouring, a few drops
  • 1 ribbon bow
  1. Beat the butter and icing sugar into buttercream.
  2. Cut the sponge into a body and two ears.
  3. Cover the cake with buttercream.
  4. Press coconut all over.
  5. Tint a little coconut pink for the ears.
  6. Put it in a box with a bow.

Cyrus the Virus bourbon smashVegetarian

Bourbon shaken with muddled lemon, mint and sugar syrup over ice

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; muddle and shake each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 large bunch mint
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 1 tray ice
  • 1 tray crushed ice
  1. Muddle the lemon wedges with the mint leaves and syrup in a shaker.
  2. Add the bourbon and ice and shake.
  3. Strain into tumblers packed with crushed ice.
  4. Slap a big sprig of mint between your hands and set on top.
