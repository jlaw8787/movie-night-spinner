Snacks

Beach bar jerk chicken skewers

Jerk chicken skewers with lime, passed along the Jamaican beach bar.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken overnight; thread and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, cut in chunks
  • 3 tbsp jerk seasoning paste
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 habanero chilli, finely chopped
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 8 bamboo skewers, soaked
  1. Mix the jerk paste, half the lime juice, sugar and oil and coat the chicken.
  2. Thread onto the skewers.
  3. Grill over high heat, turning, until charred and cooked through.
  4. Squeeze over the rest of the lime and scatter with chilli and spring onion.

Coconut prawns with mango chilli dip

Coconut prawns with a mango chilli dip. Brian meets Jordan the artist in Jamaica.

Prep 6 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 4 hours ahead and chill; make the dip a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 16 large green prawns, peeled with tails on
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g desiccated coconut
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • 3 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Blitz the mango with the sweet chilli and lime for the dip.
  2. Coat the prawns in flour, egg, then the coconut and panko mixed together.
  3. Shallow-fry in batches at 180°C until golden.
  4. Drain and serve hot with the dip.

Mains

Escovitch snapper for the Bonnie bet

Fried snapper under pickled vegetables, the day's catch.

Prep 8 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the pickle up to 2 days ahead; fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 snapper fillets, skin on
  • 50g plain flour
  • 125ml neutral oil
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, cut in matchsticks
  • 1 red capsicum, thinly sliced
  • 1 habanero chilli, sliced
  • 200ml white vinegar
  • 2 tsp caster sugar
  • 8 allspice berries
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  1. Simmer the vinegar, sugar, allspice and peppercorns, pour over the onion, carrot, capsicum and chilli and leave to pickle.
  2. Season the fish, dust with flour and fry skin-side down in the oil until crisp, then flip.
  3. Lay the fish on a platter and spoon the hot pickle over the top.
  4. Leave for a few minutes before serving so the fish soaks it up.

Married to Kerry curry goat

Slow-cooked curry goat with rice. Kerry is the surprise.

Prep 25 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Cook the curry up to 2 days ahead; it is better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg goat shoulder on the bone, cut in chunks
  • 3 tbsp Jamaican curry powder
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 1 habanero chilli, whole
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 300g long-grain rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 400g tin red kidney beans, drained
  1. Rub the goat with the curry powder, onion, garlic, ginger and thyme and leave for 20 minutes.
  2. Brown the goat in the oil, then cover with water, add the whole chilli and simmer gently, covered, until tender.
  3. Add the potatoes for the last 25 minutes so they thicken the sauce.
  4. Cook the rice with the coconut milk, beans, 300ml water and salt until fluffy, and serve with the curry.

Cocktails and Dreams grilled pineappleVegetarian

Grilled pineapple in cinnamon sugar. Flanagan's finally opens its doors.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Peel and slice the pineapple up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 ripe pineapple, peeled and cut in thick rings
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 500ml coconut ice cream
  • 1 lime, zested
  • 30g toasted coconut flakes
  1. Brush the pineapple with the butter and press into the sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Grill on a hot pan until caramelised on both sides.
  3. Serve warm with coconut ice cream, lime zest and toasted coconut.

Drinks

The barman poet's Sex on the BeachVegetarian

Vodka, peach schnapps and orange shaken hard, poured with a flourish.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the juices well; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml peach schnapps
  • 320ml orange juice
  • 320ml cranberry juice
  • 4 orange wheels
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Shake the vodka, schnapps and orange juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into 4 tall glasses of ice.
  3. Pour the cranberry juice slowly over the top so it sits in a layer, and add an orange wheel.

When to serve

Pour the Sex on the Beach when Brian recites his bar poem.
