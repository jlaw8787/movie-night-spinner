Morning after hash brownsVegetarian

Crisp grated potato cakes topped with a fried egg.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best made just before serving so the cakes stay crisp; fry them up to 1 hour ahead and keep warm in a 120°C oven
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Grate the potatoes, squeeze out as much liquid as you can and season well.
  2. Press into 8 thin cakes.
  3. Fry in butter and oil until deep golden each side.
  4. Fry the eggs and top each cake with one and chives.

Suite cornflake chicken nuggets

Buttermilk chunks in crushed cereal, fried golden, honey mustard dip, packed in Alan's satchel.

How it is made
  • 600g chicken breast
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g cornflakes
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  1. Buttermilk-brined chicken breast chunks rolled in crushed cornflakes and seasoned flour, fried crunchy and golden, with a honey mustard dip. Cut 600g chicken breast into chunks and soak in 250ml buttermilk with salt for 1 hour. Crush 100g cornflakes and mix with 50g plain flour, 1 tsp paprika and 1 tsp garlic powder. Coat the chicken and fry at 175°C for 4 minutes until golden and cooked through. Mix 3 tbsp honey, 3 tbsp Dijon and 2 tbsp mayo for dipping.

Mains

Baby Carlos breakfast burrito

Scrambled eggs, crisp potato, chorizo and cheddar in flour tortillas, fresh salsa.

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa and cook the potato and chorizo up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; reheat, scramble the eggs and roll on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g potatoes
  • 200g chorizo
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 100g cheddar
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  1. Dice and fry the potatoes until crisp, then add the diced chorizo.
  2. Make a fresh salsa of diced tomatoes and onion.
  3. Softly scramble the eggs.
  4. Fill the tortillas with the eggs, potato, chorizo, cheddar and salsa.
  5. Roll and toast in a dry pan.

Vegas rib eye with béarnaise

Dry-brined and seared hard, buttery tarragon sauce, creamed spinach, with a Phil Collins drum fill.

How it is made
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 4 baking potatoes
  • 1 bottle white wine vinegar
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 3 eggs
  • 180g butter
  • 400g spinach
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g bacon
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Dry-brined rib eye seared hard and rested, with a buttery tarragon béarnaise, creamed spinach and a loaded baked potato. Salt 4 rib eyes and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Bake 4 potatoes at 200°C for 1 hour. Reduce 3 tbsp white wine vinegar with a chopped shallot and tarragon to 1 tbsp, whisk with 3 yolks over simmering water, then slowly whisk in 180g melted butter. Sear the steaks to 54°C inside and rest. Wilt 400g spinach with garlic and 100ml cream. Load the potatoes with sour cream, bacon and chives.

Wedding cake for DougVegetarian

Lemon sponge layers filled with lemon curd and covered in buttercream.

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min, then 80 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 450g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp lemon curd
  • 400g icing sugar
  1. Beat part of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and lemon zest.
  2. Bake in two tins at 170°C.
  3. Cool.
  4. Fill with lemon curd and cover with the remaining butter beaten with the icing sugar.
  5. Chill before slicing.

Drinks

Rooftop herbal liqueur ginger fizz

Herbal liqueur and lime topped with ginger beer, served over ice with orange.

Prep 8 min, cook 2 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Put the herbal liqueur in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; pour over ice with the lime and ginger beer to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml herbal liqueur
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Put the herbal liqueur in the freezer.
  2. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  3. Add liqueur and lime juice to each, then top with cold ginger beer.
  4. Stir once, add an orange slice and toast on the roof.
