Snacks

Pete's fantasy draft nachos

Corn chips with chorizo, beans, cheddar, guacamole and sour cream.

Prep 19 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Fry the chorizo and warm the beans up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; assemble, bake and mash the guacamole on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chorizo sausages
  • 400g tin refried beans
  • ground cumin, to taste
  • 300g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Fry the diced chorizo until crisp, 7 min.
  2. Warm the refried beans with the cumin, 4 min.
  3. Spread the corn chips on a big tray, spoon over the beans and chorizo and cover with the grated cheddar.
  4. Bake at 200°C for 10 minutes.
  5. Mash the avocados with lime, salt and coriander.
  6. Top the nachos with guacamole, the sour cream, pickled jalapeños and spring onion.

Sadie's chicken pox popcorn chicken

Crispy chicken bites soaked in dill pickle brine, pickle ranch dip.

Prep 14 min, cook 7 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the pickle brine up to 8 hours ahead in the fridge and mix the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 egg
  • 100ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • paprika, to taste
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • dried dill, to taste
  • 100g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Cut the chicken thigh into bite-sized pieces and soak in the dill pickle brine for 2 hours.
  2. Drain, dip in the beaten egg with the buttermilk, then the plain flour mixed with the paprika and salt.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C for 4 to 5 minutes until crisp and toss with dried dill and salt.
  4. Mix the sour cream, the mayo, the chopped pickles and dill for dipping.

LA Thai green chicken curry

Chicken simmered in coconut green curry with Thai basil, over rice.

Prep 21 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the curry up to 2 days ahead without the beans and basil and keep chilled; reheat, add the beans and basil and cook the rice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2 cups jasmine rice
  • 3 tbsp green curry paste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 100ml chicken stock
  • 4 makrut lime leaves
  • fish sauce, to taste
  • palm sugar, to taste
  • 150g snake beans
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 1 red chilli
  1. Cook the jasmine rice, 18 min.
  2. Fry the green curry paste in the thick top of the coconut milk until fragrant, 9 min.
  3. Add the sliced chicken thigh and stir 3 minutes.
  4. Pour in the rest of the coconut milk, the stock, the torn makrut lime leaves, the fish sauce and the palm sugar, 6 min.
  5. Simmer 12 minutes, adding the snake beans for the last 5.
  6. Stir in Thai basil and serve with rice and sliced chilli.

Roommates' pepperoni and chilli pizza

Pepperoni, jalapeños and mozzarella on a crisp base, chilli flakes and arugula on top.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; stretch, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pizza dough
  • 250g tomato passata
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 150g sliced pepperoni
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 60g rocket
  • 40g parmesan
  1. Heat the oven as high as it goes with two trays inside, 15 min.
  2. Mix the passata with the garlic, oregano and a pinch of salt.
  3. Stretch the dough into 4 bases on baking paper, spread with the sauce and top with the mozzarella, pepperoni and jalapeños.
  4. Slide onto the hot trays and bake 8 to 10 minutes until blistered, two at a time.
  5. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes, drizzle over and finish with rocket and shaved parmesan, 3 min.

Sweets

Birthday party lemon cupcakesVegetarian

Lemon cupcakes with raspberry buttercream swirled on top.

Prep 2.5 hours, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 325g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 120g raspberry jam
  • 400g icing sugar
  • pink and blue food colouring, to taste
  • freeze-dried raspberries, to taste
  • 12 cupcake cases
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C, 6 min.
  2. Beat the butter and the caster sugar with lemon zest, add the eggs, then the self-raising flour and the milk.
  3. Bake in the cases for 18 minutes and cool.
  4. Fill each centre with raspberry jam.
  5. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and the milk, split in two and tint pale pink and pale blue.
  6. Pipe on and crumble over freeze-dried raspberries, 2 hr 6 min.

Grape juice, lime, soda water and mint over ice.

Prep 4 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the limes and chill the grape juice up to 1 day ahead; add the ice, soda water and mint to order
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml grape juice
  • 2 limes
  • 500ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Fill the glasses with ice, add the grape juice and a squeeze of lime to each and top with soda water and mint.
  2. Make it just before the film starts and keep the jug cold.
