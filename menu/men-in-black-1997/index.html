Edgar's farmhouse cornbreadVegetarian

Buttermilk cornbread baked in a hot cast-iron pan.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep covered, rewarm in a 160°C oven for 10 minutes; beat the honey butter up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cornmeal
  • 125g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 350ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g butter, melted, for the batter
  • 1 tbsp butter, for greasing the pan
  • 50g butter, softened, for the honey butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Put a 25cm cast-iron pan in the oven and heat to 210°C, 5 min.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, salt and sugar, then stir in the buttermilk, eggs and the melted butter.
  3. Swirl the butter for greasing around the hot pan, pour in the batter and bake until golden.
  4. Beat the softened butter with the honey and serve with the cornbread cut in wedges.

Black bean dipVegetarian

Smoky beans mashed with feta and coriander, corn chips for scooping.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins of black beans, drained (400g each)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 chipotle in adobo
  • 80g feta
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 1 bag corn chips
  1. Blitz the beans with the garlic, lime juice, cumin and chipotle until smooth.
  2. Spoon into a bowl, top with crumbled feta and coriander and serve with corn chips.

Deli pastrami sandwiches

Toasted rye with pastrami, Swiss cheese and deli mustard.

Prep 16 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Fill and close the sandwiches up to 4 hours ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; toast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of rye bread
  • 2 tbsp deli mustard
  • 500g sliced pastrami
  • 8 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar pickles
  1. Spread the rye slices with mustard and fill four of them with pastrami and Swiss cheese. Close the sandwiches.
  2. Toast in a pan with the butter over medium heat until the cheese melts, turning once.
  3. Cut on the diagonal and serve with pickles.

Neuralyser cake trufflesVegetarian

Butter cake and cream cheese icing balls on sticks, dipped in white chocolate.

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min, then 75 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g butter cake
  • 100g cream cheese icing
  • 16 lolly sticks
  • 300g white chocolate
  • 1 tsp silver lustre dust
  1. Crumble the cake and mix with the icing until it holds together.
  2. Roll into balls, push in the sticks and chill, 15 min.
  3. Leave to chill so the balls firm up, 1 hr.
  4. Melt the white chocolate, dip each ball and stand upright to set, 15 min.
  5. Brush with silver lustre dust once set, 20 min.

Edgar's sugar water lemonade slushNo alcohol

Icy blended lemonade slush with a lemon wheel.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep it chilled; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 150ml lemon juice
  • 300ml cold water
  • 4 cups of ice
  • 4 lemon wheels
  • 4 mint sprigs
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool, 12 min.
  2. Blend the syrup with the lemon juice, cold water and ice until slushy.
  3. Pour into glasses, add a lemon wheel and mint sprig, and serve with spoons and straws.
