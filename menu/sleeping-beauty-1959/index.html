Pink or blue dip duoVegetarian

Pink beetroot yoghurt dip and blue cheese sour cream dip, pita crisps.

Prep 15 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep dips chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 medium beetroot
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 4 pita breads
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Wrap the beetroot in foil and roast at 200°C until tender, then peel.
  2. Blend the beetroot with the yoghurt and garlic until bright pink.
  3. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream.
  4. Cut the pita into wedges and brush with oil.
  5. Bake the wedges at 180°C until crisp.
  6. Serve the dips side by side with the pita crisps.

King Hubert's wedding toast cheese board

Aged cheddar, baked brie and blue with honey walnuts, quince paste, grapes and crackers.

How it is made
  • 100g walnuts
  • 1 jar honey
  • 200g brie wheel
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 200g aged cheddar
  • 150g blue cheese
  • 150g quince paste
  • 300g grapes
  • 1 box crackers
  1. Aged cheddar, gooey baked brie and blue cheese with honey-roasted walnuts, quince paste, grapes and crackers, built for two kings toasting a wedding. Toss 100g walnuts with 1 tbsp honey and a pinch of salt and roast at 180°C for 8 minutes, then cool. Score the top of a 200g brie wheel, drizzle with honey and thyme and bake at 180°C for 12 minutes until wobbly. Set it on a big board with 200g aged cheddar in shards, 150g blue cheese, 150g quince paste, the walnuts, 300g grapes and crackers.

Woodcutter's cottage stew

Beef, carrots, parsnips, thyme. Sixteen years hiding out in the woodcutter's cottage.

Prep 35 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 2 parsnips, chopped
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 L water
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 40g butter
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Chop the beef and vegetables.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in the oil in a heavy pot.
  3. Add the onion, carrots, parsnips, thyme and enough water to cover.
  4. Cover and simmer gently until tender.
  5. Season and serve in bowls with crusty bread and butter.

King Stefan's crackling pork with apples

Fennel salt pork with roast apples and mustard gravy.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 jar fennel seeds
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 apples
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml apple juice
  • 200ml chicken stock
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  1. Fennel and salt rubbed pork shoulder roasted low then blasted for shattering crackling, with roast apples, crispy potatoes and a mustard apple gravy. Score the skin of a 2kg pork shoulder, dry well and rub with 1 tbsp crushed fennel seeds and plenty of salt. Roast at 160°C for 3.5 hours, adding 1kg potato chunks and 4 quartered apples for the last hour. Turn the oven to 240°C for 20 minutes until the crackling blisters. Rest 15 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan juices, add 300ml apple juice, 200ml stock and 1 tsp mustard and simmer until thick.

Lots of berries, white chocolate dippedVegetarian

Berries dipped in white chocolate with raspberry dust, picked fresh from the forest.

Prep 2 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 250g strawberries
  • 125g blackberries
  • 15g freeze-dried raspberries, crushed
  1. Melt the white chocolate gently in a heatproof bowl over simmering water.
  2. Dip the strawberries and blackberries halfway and set on baking paper.
  3. While still wet, dust with the crushed freeze-dried raspberries.
  4. Chill until set.

The fairies' no magic birthday cakeVegetarian

Three vanilla layers stacked with raspberry jam and buttercream.

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep iced cake covered
Recipe for 4
  • 300g butter, softened
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 200g raspberry jam
  • 200g butter for buttercream, softened
  • 400g icing sugar
  • pink and blue food colouring, a drop of each
  1. Beat the cake butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour.
  2. Divide between 3 lined tins and bake at 175°C until a skewer comes out clean.
  3. Cool completely in the tins.
  4. Beat the buttercream butter with the icing sugar until fluffy.
  5. Stack the layers with jam and buttercream. Ice half pink and half blue and leave it proudly a little crooked.

Skumps! Two kings' toast punchNo alcohol

Pomegranate and orange juice with cinnamon syrup, raised high.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 300ml pomegranate juice
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 pomegranate
  • 750ml sparkling apple juice, chilled
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and cinnamon stick for a few minutes to make a syrup.
  2. Cool the syrup.
  3. Juice one orange and slice the other. Mix the pomegranate juice, orange juice and syrup in a jug.
  4. Add the orange slices and half the pomegranate seeds.
  5. Just before serving, add the ice and top with the sparkling apple juice. Pour into goblets.
