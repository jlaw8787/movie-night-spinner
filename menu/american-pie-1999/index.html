Snacks

Stifler's party wings

Crispy baked wings in hot butter sauce, blue cheese dip

Prep 7 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Wings can be dried a day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 60g butter
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 celery sticks
  1. Pat the wings dry and leave them uncovered in the fridge, ideally overnight. Season with salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220C until crisp and golden.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through it.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream for the dip. Serve with celery sticks.

Michelle's grilled cornVegetarian

Charred corn brushed with smoked paprika butter and lime

Prep 5 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Boil ahead, char fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • salt, to taste
  • 1 lime
  1. Cut each cob into thirds and boil for 4 minutes. Drain well.
  2. Char on a hot grill pan, turning, until blistered.
  3. Melt the butter with the paprika and salt, brush over the corn and squeeze over the lime.

Prom night chicken parmesan and fries

Panko chicken under ham, tomato sauce and mozzarella, with fries and salad

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts, halved through the middle
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 250ml napoli sauce
  • 4 slices leg ham
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 150g salad leaves
  1. Crumb the chicken in flour, egg then panko. Cut the potatoes into chips, soak in cold water, then dry well.
  2. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil at a low heat until soft, drain, then fry again at a high heat until golden.
  3. Shallow fry the chicken in some of the oil until golden and cooked through.
  4. Top each piece with napoli sauce, ham and mozzarella and grill until bubbling. Serve with the chips and salad.

Mum's apple pieVegetarian

Lattice apple pie with cinnamon, served warm with ice cream

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg green apples, peeled and sliced
  • 50g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Cook the apples with the butter, sugar and cinnamon until just soft. Cool.
  2. Line a pie dish with 2 sheets of pastry and fill. Cut the other 2 sheets into strips and weave a lattice on top.
  3. Bake at 190C until golden. Serve warm with ice cream.

Finch's mochaccinoVegetarianNo alcohol

Espresso with hot chocolate milk, whipped cream and grated dark chocolate

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 72g ground coffee
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 600ml milk
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 30g dark chocolate, grated
  1. Brew the coffee into four mugs, about one espresso each.
  2. Heat the milk and whisk in the cocoa until frothy.
  3. Pour the chocolate milk over the coffee and top with whipped cream and grated chocolate.
