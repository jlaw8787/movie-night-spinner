Stifler's party wings

Crispy baked wings in hot butter sauce, blue cheese dip.

Prep 7 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Wings can be dried a day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 60g butter
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 celery sticks
  1. Pat the wings dry and leave them uncovered in the fridge, ideally overnight. Season with salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C until crisp and golden, 45 min.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through it, 3 min.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream for the dip. Serve with celery sticks.

Michelle's grilled cornVegetarian

Charred corn brushed with smoked paprika butter and lime.

Prep 5 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Boil ahead, char fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • salt, to taste
  • 1 lime
  1. Cut each cob into thirds and boil for 4 minutes. Drain well.
  2. Char on a hot grill pan, turning, until blistered, 8 min.
  3. Melt the butter with the paprika and salt, brush over the corn and squeeze over the lime, 3 min.

Dog Years cheese and gravy fries

Thick fries fried twice, melting curds and hot beef sauce, at the gang's booth.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 200g fresh mozzarella
  1. Thick-cut chips fried twice until crisp, piled with melting cheese curds or torn mozzarella and smothered in hot rich beef gravy. Cut 1kg potatoes into thick chips, soak 30 minutes and dry well. Fry at 140°C for 6 minutes, then at 190°C until golden and crisp, and salt. Melt 30g butter, stir in 2 tbsp flour until brown, whisk in 400ml beef stock and a dash of Worcestershire and simmer until thick. Pile the chips onto a platter, scatter with 200g torn fresh mozzarella and pour over the hot gravy.

Prom night chicken parmesan and fries

Breaded chicken under ham, marinara and mozzarella, with fries and salad.

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts, halved through the middle
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 250ml napoli sauce
  • 4 slices of leg ham
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 150g salad leaves
  1. Crumb the chicken in flour, egg then panko. Cut the potatoes into chips, soak in cold water, then dry well, 15 min.
  2. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil at a low heat until soft, drain, then fry again at a high heat until golden, 15 min.
  3. Shallow fry the chicken in some of the oil until golden and cooked through, 8 min.
  4. Top each piece with napoli sauce, ham and mozzarella and grill until bubbling. Serve with the chips and salad, 10 min.

Graduation barbecue pork ribs

Rubbed with paprika, sugar and garlic, foil-cooked tender, glazed over coals, to end senior year.

How it is made
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 2kg pork ribs
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 0.25 cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle apple cider vinegar
  1. Pork ribs rubbed with paprika, brown sugar and garlic, slow cooked in foil until tender, then glazed with barbecue sauce over hot coals, with creamy coleslaw. Mix 2 tbsp smoked paprika, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp salt and half a tsp cayenne. Rub over 2kg pork ribs, wrap tightly in foil and bake at 150°C for 3 hours. Unwrap, brush with 200ml barbecue sauce and cook over hot coals or under the grill for 10 minutes, basting, until sticky. Toss a quarter cabbage and a carrot, shredded, with mayonnaise and cider vinegar.

Mom's apple pieVegetarian

Lattice apple pie with cinnamon, served warm with ice cream.

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg green apples, peeled and sliced
  • 50g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Cook the apples with the butter, sugar and cinnamon until just soft. Cool, 10 min.
  2. Line a pie dish with 2 sheets of pastry and fill. Cut the other 2 sheets into strips and weave a lattice on top.
  3. Bake at 190°C until golden. Serve warm with ice cream, 45 min.

Finch's mochaccinoNo alcohol

Espresso with hot chocolate milk, whipped cream and grated dark chocolate.

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 70g ground coffee
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 600ml milk
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 30g dark chocolate, grated
  1. Brew the coffee into four mugs, about one espresso each.
  2. Heat the milk and whisk in the cocoa until frothy, 3 min.
  3. Pour the chocolate milk over the coffee and top with whipped cream and grated chocolate.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Jim's dad walks into the kitchen.
