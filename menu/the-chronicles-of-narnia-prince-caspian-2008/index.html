Snacks

Cair Paravel applesVegetarian

Red and green apple slices with warm honey peanut butter.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Slice the apples within the hour so they do not brown; the peanut butter and honey can be mixed up to 2 days ahead and warmed to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 red apples
  • 2 green apples
  • 4 tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  1. Slice the apples and fan them on a board.
  2. Warm the peanut butter with honey until loose.
  3. Spoon into a bowl in the middle and dust the apples with cinnamon.

Trumpkin's dwarf breadVegetarian

Self-raising bread with rubbed in butter, scored and baked.

Prep 7 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Same day, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 60g butter, for the dough
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 100g butter, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub the butter for the dough into the flour and salt.
  2. Mix in the milk with a knife until it just comes together.
  3. Shape into a round and score the top.
  4. Bake.
  5. Tear warm and serve with butter.

Reepicheep's cheddar and chive sconesOn the printed menu

Mouse sized, packed with vintage cheddar and herbs, baked tall, split and spread with cold butter.

How it is made
  • 350g self-raising flour
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tin mustard powder
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 250ml full cream milk
  1. Little scones packed with vintage cheddar and chives, baked tall and golden with a cheesy crust, split and spread with cold butter. Rub 60g cold butter into 350g self-raising flour with a pinch of salt and half a teaspoon mustard powder. Stir in 120g grated vintage cheddar and a handful of chopped chives, then 250ml milk to make a soft dough. Pat out 3cm thick and cut 12 small rounds. Brush with milk, top with 30g more cheese and bake at 220°C for 12 to 14 minutes until risen and golden. Serve warm with butter.

Mains

Aslan's How camp stew

Lamb shoulder simmered with pearl barley, carrots, leeks and rosemary.

Prep 24 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 150g pearl barley
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 leeks
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1.5 L water
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slice the carrots and leeks.
  2. Brown the lamb in oil.
  3. Add the barley, vegetables, rosemary and water and simmer until the lamb is tender.
  4. Serve in deep bowls with crusty bread.

Telmarine fennel roast pork loinOn the printed menu

Scored joint rubbed with fennel seed and garlic, roasted with apples, for Miraz's coronation.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg pork loin
  • 1 jar fennel seeds
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 apples
  1. Pork loin rubbed with fennel seed and garlic, roasted at 200°C for an hour with apples and potatoes, sliced with pan juices. Score the skin of a 1.5kg boneless pork loin and pat dry. Crush 2 tsp fennel seeds with 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp salt and pepper and rub into the meat, then rub the skin with oil and salt. Roast at 220°C for 25 minutes, then 180°C for 40 minutes. Add 800g potatoes in chunks for the last 60 minutes and 4 halved apples for the last 30. Rest 10 minutes, slice and spoon over the pan juices.

Sweets

Narnian apple crumbleVegetarian

Cinnamon green apples under an oat crumble.

Prep 19 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook the apples and rub the topping up to 1 day ahead, stored apart in the fridge; assemble and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 5 green apples
  • 30g butter, for the apples
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 70g butter, for the topping
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 100g plain flour
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 500ml custard
  1. Peel and slice the apples.
  2. Melt the butter for the apples in a pan, add the apples and cinnamon and cook, then spoon into a dish.
  3. Rub the topping butter into the oats, flour and sugar and scatter over.
  4. Bake at 180°C.
  5. Warm the custard and serve over the crumble.

Drinks

Station platform malted hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Milk whisked with dark chocolate, malt powder and brown sugar.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best heated and whisked just before serving; chop the chocolate up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 120g dark chocolate
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 can whipped cream
  • 1 pinch malted milk powder, to finish
  1. Chop the chocolate.
  2. Heat the milk until steaming.
  3. Whisk in the chocolate, malted milk powder and sugar until smooth and frothy.
  4. Pour into mugs and top with cream and a pinch of malt.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Lucy finds an apple among the ruins of Cair Paravel.
