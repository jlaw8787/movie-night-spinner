Dark chocolate gummy lolly bark

Dark and white chocolate swirled with gummy lollies, honeycomb and marshmallows

Prep 13 min, cook 8 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the bark until set up to 1 week ahead, then smash into shards and keep in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 100g gummy lollies, chopped
  • 50g honeycomb, crushed
  • 50g mini marshmallows
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Melt the dark and white chocolate separately.
  2. Spread the dark chocolate over a lined tray and drizzle with the white, then swirl with a skewer.
  3. Scatter over the lollies, honeycomb, marshmallows and sprinkles, pressing them in lightly.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Smash into shards.

Candy kart pretzel rodsVegetarian

Pretzel rods dipped in white chocolate and rolled in sprinkles

Prep 12 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Dip and set the rods up to 2 days ahead, airtight at room temperature; stand them in a glass to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 3 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Melt the white chocolate.
  2. Dip each pretzel rod two-thirds of the way in, letting the extra drip off, and roll in sprinkles straight away.
  3. Lay on baking paper to set.
  4. Stand them in a glass like a bunch of candy karts.

Hero's Duty loaded nachosOn the printed menu

Corn chips with spiced beef, black beans, cheddar, jalapeño, salsa and guacamole

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 packet taco spice
  • 1 can black beans
  • 300g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 lime
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Corn chips loaded with spiced beef and beans, melted cheddar and pickled jalapeño, blitzed in the oven, with salsa, guacamole and sour cream. Brown 400g beef mince with 1 diced onion and 2 tsp taco spice, add a drained can of black beans and 100ml water and simmer 5 minutes. Spread 300g corn chips over a large tray, spoon over the beef and scatter with 200g grated cheddar and sliced jalapeños. Bake at 200°C for 8 minutes until melted. Top with tomato salsa, mashed avocado with lime and sour cream.

Ralph's wrecking ball meatballs

Eight big parmesan beef meatballs browned and simmered in sauce

Prep 28 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Mix and roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brown, simmer and boil the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 700g tomato passata
  • 200g spaghetti
  1. Mix the mince, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan and garlic. Roll into big meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs all over in the oil.
  3. Add the passata and simmer.
  4. Boil the spaghetti until al dente and drain.
  5. Serve the meatballs and sauce over the spaghetti.

Niceland chicken and leek pot pieOn the printed menu

Chicken thighs, leek and mushroom in creamy thyme sauce under golden puff pastry

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 leeks
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Chicken, leek and mushroom in a creamy thyme sauce under a golden puff pastry lid, the kind of pie the Nicelanders bake for Felix. Brown 800g diced chicken thighs, then remove. Soften 2 sliced leeks and 200g mushrooms in 40g butter, stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 300ml chicken stock and 150ml cream. Simmer until thick, add thyme and the chicken and season. Pour into a pie dish, top with a puff pastry sheet, brush with egg and cut a slit. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes until golden.

Nicelander window pieVegetarian

Cinnamon green apples under a lattice crust

Prep 32 min, cook 55 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 green apples, sliced
  • 50g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 1 tube gold icing
  1. Peel and slice the apples.
  2. Cook the apples with the butter, brown sugar and cinnamon until just soft.
  3. Cool the filling.
  4. Line a pie dish with shortcrust.
  5. Fill and weave a lattice top.
  6. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  7. Pipe a gold star on top and serve with ice cream.

Cola Mountain floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold cola poured over vanilla ice cream until it froths

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Best poured just before serving so it froths; chill the cola and glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml vanilla ice cream
  • 1l cola, cold
  1. Put a scoop of ice cream into each tall glass.
  2. Slowly pour the cola over the top so it fizzes up and froths over the rim like a volcano.
  3. Stand the glasses on plates and serve with long spoons.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the Nicelanders throw Felix's 30th anniversary party.
