Snacks

Prizzi wedding antipasto

Olives, salami and marinated mushrooms for the reception

Prep 15 min, cook 1 min · Make ahead: Mushrooms need to marinate overnight, serve at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 200g button mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 100g salami, sliced
  • 120g mixed olives
  • 150g marinated artichokes, drained
  1. Blanch the mushrooms in boiling water for 1 minute and drain well.
  2. Toss with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic and parsley. Refrigerate covered overnight.
  3. Serve on a platter with the salami, olives and artichokes.

Fried zucchiniVegetarian

Church hall wedding style

Prep 15 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 zucchini, about 400g
  • 130g plain flour, divided
  • 150ml cold sparkling water
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 lemon, cut in wedges
  1. Cut the zucchini into batons, pat dry and toss with 30g of the flour.
  2. Whisk the remaining flour with the sparkling water into a light batter.
  3. Heat the oil to 185C and fry the battered batons in small batches for 3 minutes until crisp and golden.
  4. Drain, season with salt and serve with lemon.

Mains

Don Corrado's baked ziti

Pasta tubes in sausage sugo with ricotta and mozzarella, baked blistered, the old man's way

Prep 30 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; add 10 minutes to the bake
Recipe for 4
  • 400g Italian sausages
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700g passata
  • 150ml water
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 350g ziti or penne
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1/2 tsp salt and pepper, mixed
  • 200g mozzarella
  1. Remove the sausages from their casings and brown in the oil, breaking up the meat.
  2. Add the onion for 5 minutes, then the garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Add the passata, water and oregano and simmer gently, stirring.
  4. Boil the ziti for 2 minutes less than the packet time and drain. Mix the ricotta with the seasoning.
  5. Layer pasta, sugo, ricotta and mozzarella in an ovenproof dish.
  6. Bake at 200C for 25 minutes until blistered and rest for 10 minutes.

Wedding almond cookiesVegetarian

Chewy almond amaretti rolled in icing sugar and crackled, wrapped in tulle as bomboniere

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 egg whites
  • 250g almond meal
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp almond extract
  • 40g icing sugar
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks in a clean bowl.
  2. Gently fold in the almond meal, caster sugar and almond extract.
  3. Shape into 20 small balls, roll in icing sugar and space on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 170C for 15 minutes until crackled and lightly golden.
  5. Cool on the trays for 10 minutes, then move to a rack.

Drinks

Prizzi wedding white peach BelliniVegetarian

Ripe peach purée with lemon topped with prosecco, for the moment Charley spots Irene

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Purée up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe white peaches
  • 2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 480ml prosecco, chilled
  1. Peel and stone the peaches, blend with the lemon juice and sugar until smooth.
  2. Chill the purée for 30 minutes.
  3. Spoon some purée into each chilled flute and top slowly with prosecco poured down the side. Stir once and serve straight away.

When to serve

Serve dessert at the wedding, when Charley first spots Irene.
