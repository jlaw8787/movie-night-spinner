Seafood risotto

Shrimp, squid and mussels. No cheese, no spaghetti.

Prep 31 min, cook 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L fish stock
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 280g carnaroli rice
  • 125ml white wine
  • 300g green prawns, peeled
  • 200g squid rings
  • 500g mussels, cleaned
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  1. Peel the prawns, scrub and debeard the mussels, dice the onion, chop the garlic and chop the parsley.
  2. Warm the fish stock in a saucepan. Soften the onion and garlic in the olive oil over medium heat for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the carnaroli rice and toast for 2 minutes, then pour in the white wine and stir until it is absorbed.
  4. Add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 16 minutes until the rice is nearly tender.
  5. Stir in the prawns, squid rings and mussels, cover and cook for 4 minutes until the mussels open. Throw out any that stay shut.
  6. Take off the heat and stir through the butter, the parsley and a squeeze of lemon. Serve straight away, no cheese.

Risotto in three colours

Spinach, parmesan and tomato risottos, side by side.

Prep 20 min, cook 24 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 70g butter
  • 240g arborio rice
  • 125ml white wine
  • 850ml chicken stock
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 60g parmesan, grated
  • 4 tbsp tomato passata
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  1. Heat the chicken stock in a saucepan. Soak the saffron in 2 tbsp of hot water. Blitz the baby spinach and basil leaves with a splash of the stock to a smooth paste, 10 min.
  2. Soften the onion in 40g of the butter for 5 minutes, add the arborio rice and toast for 2 minutes, then add the white wine and stir until absorbed.
  3. Add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 17 minutes until the rice is just tender.
  4. Split the risotto into three pans over low heat. Stir the spinach and basil paste into the first, the parmesan and the last 30g butter into the second, and the passata with the saffron and its water into the third, 5 min.
  5. Spoon the green, white and red risotto side by side on each plate.

Mains

Timpano

Ziti, ragù, egg and provolone in a pasta drum. The big night's star.

Prep 20 min, cook 2 hours, then 35 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 300g 00 flour
  • 3 eggs, for dough
  • 40g butter, for the bowl
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 700ml passata
  • 180g dried ziti
  • 100g salami, diced
  • 3 eggs, boiled and sliced, for the filling
  • 150g provolone, diced
  1. Cook 180g dried ziti in well salted water until just tender, then drain.
  2. Make a dough with the flour and eggs, knead and rest, 20 min.
  3. Brown the mince with the onion, add the passata and simmer to a ragù, 40 min.
  4. Roll the dough thin and line an ovenproof bowl greased with the butter.
  5. Toss the cooked ziti with ragù and layer in the bowl with salami, boiled egg and provolone.
  6. Fold the dough over and bake at 180°C for 1 hour.
  7. Rest for 30 minutes, turn out and slice.

Roast pork, whole-pig style

Pork with fennel, garlic and rosemary, for the feast.

Prep 25 min, cook 2.5 hours, then 12.5 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg boned pork belly
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp fennel seeds
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  1. Score the skin of the pork belly with a sharp knife. Turn it over and rub the flesh side with the fennel seeds, crushed garlic and chopped rosemary.
  2. Rub the skin with the salt, roll the pork belly up and tie with string every 3cm.
  3. Sit it on a rack and dry uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  4. Roast at 240°C for 30 minutes until the skin starts to blister.
  5. Turn the oven down to 160°C and roast for 2 hours until tender with crisp crackling.
  6. Rest for 15 minutes, cut off the string and carve thick.

Secondo's omeletteVegetarian

Eggs, olive oil, crusty bread. The silent morning after.

Prep 6 min, cook 4 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat, pour in the eggs and stir gently for about 3 minutes until just set underneath and soft on top.
  3. Fold the omelette, slide it onto a plate and cut into four. Serve with the crusty bread torn into pieces.
