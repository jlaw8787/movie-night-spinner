Snacks

Pool float pizza bites

Mini pepperoni and mozzarella pizzas baked crisp, oregano on top.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • 150g pepperoni, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Roll the dough thin and cut into small rounds.
  2. Top each with sauce, mozzarella, a slice of pepperoni and a pinch of oregano.
  3. Bake at 220°C until crisp and bubbling, 10 min.

Desert bar jalapeño poppers

Jalapeño halves stuffed with cream cheese and cheddar, wrapped in bacon.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fill up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 12 large jalapeno chillies
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 12 rashers of streaky bacon, halved
  1. Halve the jalapenos lengthways and scrape out the seeds.
  2. Mix the cream cheese with the cheddar and fill each half.
  3. Wrap each half with half a rasher of bacon.
  4. Bake at 200°C until the bacon is crisp, 20 min.

Desert wedding tuna tostadas

Crisp corn tostadas, raw tuna in lime, soy and sesame, smashed avocado, pickled jalapeño, chipotle.

How it is made
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 250g sashimi-grade tuna
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 avocado
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  1. Mini tostadas topped with raw tuna dressed in lime, soy and sesame, smashed avocado, pickled jalapeño and a squeeze of chipotle mayo. Cut 8 small corn tortillas into rounds with a 7cm cutter, brush with oil and bake at 200°C for 8 minutes until crisp. Dice 250g sashimi-grade tuna and toss with 1 tbsp soy, the juice of 1 lime, 1 tsp sesame oil and sliced spring onion. Mash 1 avocado with salt and lime. Mix 3 tbsp mayo with 1 tsp chipotle in adobo. Top each tostada with avocado, tuna, a slice of pickled jalapeño and a dot of mayo.

Tala and Abe's wedding fish tacos

Spiced pan-fried white fish, lime crema, pickled red onion, cabbage.

Prep 22 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g white fish fillets
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 limes
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 1/4 small cabbage
  • 12 corn tortillas
  1. Quickly pickle the sliced red onion in some of the lime juice.
  2. Mix the sour cream with lime juice for a crema and shred the cabbage.
  3. Dust the fish with chilli, cumin and salt and pan fry in the vegetable oil until just cooked, 8 min.
  4. Warm the tortillas and fill with cabbage, fish, crema and pickled onion.

Reception chicken with spinach and ricotta

Breasts stuffed with spinach, ricotta and lemon, lemon caper butter, crushed potatoes.

How it is made
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 150g ricotta
  • 120g baby spinach
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 80g butter
  • 800g baby potatoes
  1. Chicken breasts stuffed with spinach, ricotta and lemon, roasted golden, with a lemon caper butter sauce and crushed baby potatoes. Heat the oven to 200°C. Boil 800g baby potatoes 15 minutes. Mix 150g ricotta, 60g wilted chopped spinach, 30g parmesan and lemon zest. Cut a deep pocket in 4 chicken breasts and fill. Sear in oil 3 minutes a side, then roast 18 minutes. Crush the potatoes with butter and salt. In the pan, melt 60g butter with the juice of 1 lemon and 1 tbsp capers and spoon over the chicken.

Sweets

Wedding cake againVegetarian

Butter cake layers, lemon curd, whipped cream, white frosting.

Prep 40 min, cook 30 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 200g lemon curd
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp milk
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour. Divide between 2 tins.
  2. Bake at 175°C until golden and springy, 30 min.
  3. Cool completely, 40 min.
  4. Whip the cream and fill the cakes with lemon curd and cream.
  5. Ice white with icing sugar mixed with milk.

Drinks

Roy's crossbow michelada

Mexican lager, lime and tomato juice, hot sauce, chilli salt rim.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 4 limes
  • 240ml tomato juice
  • hot sauce, 16 dashes
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 bottles of Mexican style lager (330 ml each)
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Mix the salt with the chilli powder on a plate.
  2. Rub the rims of 4 tall glasses with lime and dip into the salt. Fill with ice.
  3. Into each glass add lime juice, tomato juice, hot sauce and Worcestershire.
  4. Top slowly with cold lager, stir gently and add a lime wedge. Use soda water instead of beer for anyone not drinking.
