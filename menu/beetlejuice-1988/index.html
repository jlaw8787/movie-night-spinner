Snacks

Shrimp cocktail

Chilled shrimp, iceberg, lemony horseradish sauce. Served lively. Keep your face clear of the bowl.

Prep 17 min, cook 3 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g peeled green prawns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 1 lemon, squeezed
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  1. Poach the prawns in salted simmering water for 2 minutes until just cooked.
  2. Chill in iced water, then drain well, 5 min.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, horseradish, lemon and hot sauce.
  4. Shred half the lettuce into four glasses, pile in the prawns and spoon the sauce over.

Model town cheesesVegetarian

Cheese houses, tomato and cornichon trees.

Prep 25 min
Recipe for 4
  • 120g cheddar
  • 120g gouda
  • 120g pepper jack
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 100g cornichons
  1. Cut the cheddar, gouda and pepper jack into cubes.
  2. Stack the cubes into tiny houses on a long board.
  3. Thread the tomatoes and cornichons onto toothpicks as trees and stand them between the houses, like Adam's model town, 5 min.

Mains

Grilled lobster tails

Garlic and parsley butter, shoestring fries, salad.

Prep 24 min, cook 16 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 80g soft butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil
  • 100g salad leaves
  1. Split the lobster tails lengthways. Mash the butter with the garlic, lemon zest and parsley and brush over the flesh.
  2. Cut the potatoes into shoestrings, rinse and dry well.
  3. Fry the potatoes in the vegetable oil in batches at 180°C until crisp, drain and salt, 10 min.
  4. Grill the lobster flesh side up until just opaque, 6 min.
  5. Dress the salad leaves in lemon juice and serve with the lobster and chips.

Black and white cakeVegetarian

Vanilla and chocolate sponge, white ganache.

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min, 20 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 80ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 60ml thickened cream
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Stir the cocoa into half the batter.
  3. Pipe or spoon alternating stripes into a lined loaf tin.
  4. Bake at 170°C until a skewer comes out clean, 50 min.
  5. Cool the cake in the tin, 20 min.
  6. Melt the white chocolate with the cream and pour over the cake. Pipe stripes with the melted dark chocolate, 5 min.

Drinks

Banana daiquiri

Banana, lime, sugar syrup and ice. No rum. May cause sudden dancing. Resist.

Prep 10 min, 10 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 180ml white rum
  • 3 limes, juiced to give 80ml
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1kg ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water.
  2. Cool the syrup for 10 minutes.
  3. Blend the rum, lime juice, syrup, bananas and ice until smooth and pour into chilled glasses.

When to serve

Serve the shrimp when the dinner party breaks into the Banana Boat song.
