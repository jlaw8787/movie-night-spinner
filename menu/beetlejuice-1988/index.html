Snacks

Deetz dinner prawn cocktail

Poached iced prawns on iceberg with horseradish Marie Rose, keep them away from faces

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, prawns poached up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g peeled green prawns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 1 lemon, squeezed
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  1. Poach the prawns in salted simmering water for 2 minutes until just cooked.
  2. Chill in iced water, then drain well.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, horseradish, lemon and hot sauce.
  4. Shred half the lettuce into four glasses, pile in the prawns and spoon the sauce over.

Model town cheese bitesVegetarian

Cheddar, gouda and pepper jack cubes stacked like houses, gherkin trees, for Adam's attic model

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 120g cheddar
  • 120g gouda
  • 120g pepper jack
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 100g cornichons
  1. Cut the cheddar, gouda and pepper jack into cubes.
  2. Stack the cubes into tiny houses on a long board.
  3. Thread the tomatoes and cornichons onto toothpicks as trees and stand them between the houses, like Adam's model town.

Mains

Dinner party lobster

Split tails in garlic lemon butter, grilled, chips and salad, for the Maitlands' old house

Prep 30 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Butter up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 80g soft butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil
  • 100g salad leaves
  1. Split the lobster tails lengthways. Mash the butter with the garlic, lemon zest and parsley and brush over the flesh.
  2. Cut the potatoes into shoestrings, rinse and dry well.
  3. Fry the potatoes in the vegetable oil in batches at 180C until crisp, drain and salt.
  4. Grill the lobster flesh side up until just opaque.
  5. Dress the salad leaves in lemon juice and serve with the lobster and fries.

Striped suit cakeVegetarian

Black and white stripes, like the suit

Prep 35 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 80ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 60ml thickened cream
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Stir the cocoa into half the batter.
  3. Pipe or spoon alternating stripes into a lined loaf tin.
  4. Bake at 170C until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool the cake in the tin (included in cook time).
  6. Melt the white chocolate with the cream and pour over the cake. Pipe stripes with the melted dark chocolate.

Drinks

Banana daiquiriVegetarian

White rum, lime, sugar syrup and half a banana blended with ice, Day-O

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 180ml white rum
  • 3 limes, juiced to give 80ml
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1kg ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water.
  2. Cool the syrup for 10 minutes.
  3. Blend the rum, lime juice, syrup, bananas and ice until smooth and pour into chilled glasses.

When to serve

Serve the prawns when the dinner party breaks into the Banana Boat song.
