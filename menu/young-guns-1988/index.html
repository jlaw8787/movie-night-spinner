Snacks

English sandwiches

Cucumber and ham with English mustard on white bread, crusts off.

Prep 19 min · Make ahead: Butter and fill the sandwiches up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped in damp paper towel in the fridge; trim the crusts and cut just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 150g leg ham
  • 2 tsp English mustard
  1. Butter the bread.
  2. Fill half with thin cucumber.
  3. Fill the rest with ham and English mustard, 5 min.
  4. Cut off the crusts and cut into triangles.

New Mexico green chilli quesoVegetarian

Hot cheddar dip with roasted green chilies, served with warm tortillas.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Roast, peel and chop the chillies up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; melt the cheese sauce just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green chillies
  • 375ml can evaporated milk
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 250g cheddar, grated
  • 300g corn chips
  1. Roast the chillies until blackened, then peel and chop, 8 min.
  2. Warm the evaporated milk with crushed garlic, 4 min.
  3. Stir in the cheddar until smooth.
  4. Add the chillies and serve hot with the corn chips.

Regulators' peppered beef jerky

Thin top round in Worcestershire, soy, cracked pepper and chilli, dried dark, for the saddlebag.

How it is made
  • 800g beef topside
  • 80ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 40ml soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • black peppercorns
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Thin-sliced beef topside marinated in Worcestershire, soy, cracked pepper and chilli, dried low in the oven until chewy and dark. Freeze 800g beef topside for 1 hour, then slice 3mm thick against the grain. Mix 80ml Worcestershire sauce, 40ml soy sauce, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp cracked black pepper and 1 tsp chilli flakes. Marinate the beef overnight in the fridge. Pat dry, press on more pepper and lay on wire racks over trays. Dry at 70°C with the door ajar for 4 hours until leathery. Cool and store airtight.

Mains

Green chilli pork

Pork stewed with roasted green chillies, served over rice.

Prep 15 min, cook 2 hr 55 min · Make ahead: Braise the pork up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; cook the rice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder, diced
  • 4 green chillies
  • 400g tomatillos
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the pork in batches in the oil, 10 min.
  2. Char the chillies and tomatillos, 10 min.
  3. Blitz with onion, garlic and cumin.
  4. Braise the pork in the sauce until it falls apart, 2 hr 30 min.
  5. Cook the rice and serve the pork over it, 15 min.

Red chilli ranch beef stew

Chuck braised slow with onion, carrot, potato and toasted dried chillies, warm flour tortillas.

How it is made
  • 4 dried red chillies
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • ground cumin
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 carrots
  • 500g potatoes
  • 8 flour tortillas
  1. Beef chuck browned and braised slowly with onion, carrot, potato and toasted dried red chillies until thick and rich, with warm flour tortillas. Toast 4 dried red chillies in a dry pan, soak in 300ml hot water for 20 minutes and blitz smooth. Brown 1.2kg diced beef chuck in batches. Soften 2 onions and 3 garlic cloves, add 1 tsp cumin, the chilli puree and 500ml beef stock, and return the beef. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours. Add 2 carrots and 500g potatoes in chunks and cook 1 hour more. Serve with warm flour tortillas.

Sweets

Biscochitos, anise cinnamon cookiesVegetarian

Crisp star-shaped anise cookies dipped in cinnamon sugar.

Prep 24 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 180g caster sugar, divided
  • 1 tsp aniseed, crushed
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Beat the butter with some of the sugar and aniseed.
  2. Work in the flour.
  3. Roll out and cut into stars.
  4. Dip the tops in sugar mixed with cinnamon.
  5. Bake at 175°C until pale gold, 10 min.

Drinks

Lincoln saloon rye old fashioned

Rye whisky, demerara syrup and bitters stirred cold over ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the demerara syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; stir each drink with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 50g demerara sugar
  • 50ml hot water
  • 240ml rye whisky
  • 1 tsp aromatic bitters
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the sugar into hot water to make a syrup and cool, 3 min.
  2. For each drink, stir rye, syrup and bitters with ice until very cold.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in a short glass.
  4. Squeeze a wide strip of orange peel over the top and drop it in.
