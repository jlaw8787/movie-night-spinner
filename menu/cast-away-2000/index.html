Snacks

Chargrilled crab claws with garlic butter

Crab claws charred over the fire with garlic butter

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead; grill the crab to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg cooked crab claws, cracked
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup coriander, chopped
  • 2 limes
  1. Mash the butter with the garlic, chilli, coriander and the zest of one lime.
  2. Grill the claws over hot coals or a barbecue, turning, until smoky and hot through.
  3. Toss with the garlic butter until it melts, then serve with lime wedges.

Cracked coconut and lime ceviche

Fresh fish cured in lime and coconut. Chuck works out how to open coconuts.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Cut the fish and vegetables up to 4 hours ahead and keep cold; dress just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sashimi-grade kingfish, diced
  • 4 limes, juiced
  • 125ml coconut milk
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  • 1 bag plain corn chips
  1. Toss the fish with the lime juice and a good pinch of salt and chill for 10 minutes.
  2. Drain off half the lime juice and stir in the coconut milk, chilli, onion and cucumber.
  3. Scatter with coriander and serve in coconut shells or bowls with corn chips.

Spearfished whole snapper over the coals

Whole snapper stuffed with lemon and herbs, grilled over the coals. Chuck learns to spear fish.

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Score and stuff the fish up to 4 hours ahead and keep it cold; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 whole snapper, about 800g each, cleaned and scaled
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 banana leaves or sheets of foil
  • 1 lime
  1. Score each fish three times on both sides, rub with oil and salt, and stuff with lemon, dill and garlic.
  2. Wrap loosely in banana leaf or foil.
  3. Cook over medium coals or a hot barbecue, turning once, until the flesh flakes at the bone.
  4. Unwrap, squeeze over lime and serve straight from the leaf.

Memphis dry-rub pork ribs

Paprika dry-rub pork ribs, slow cooked. Chuck keeps the watch on Memphis time.

Prep 15 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Rub the ribs up to a day ahead; bake slowly and finish over the coals to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg American-style pork ribs
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 125ml apple cider vinegar
  1. Mix the paprika, sugar, garlic and onion powders, cayenne, thyme and 2 tsp salt, and rub all over the ribs.
  2. Wrap tightly in foil and bake at 150°C until tender.
  3. Unwrap, mop with the vinegar, dust with a little more rub and finish over hot coals or under the grill until crusty.
  4. Cut between the bones and serve.

Angel wings pavlova with mango and passionfruitVegetarian

Pavlova with mango and passionfruit, for the one box Chuck never opens

Prep 25 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Bake the pavlova up to 2 days ahead and keep it airtight; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 mangoes, sliced
  • 4 passionfruit
  1. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, then add the sugar a spoonful at a time until thick and glossy.
  2. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar and shape into a round on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120°C for 75 minutes, then cool in the oven with the door ajar.
  4. Whip the cream, pile it on and top with mango and passionfruit pulp.
