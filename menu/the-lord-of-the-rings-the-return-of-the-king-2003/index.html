Snacks

Steward's roast drumsticks and grapes

Thyme butter drumsticks with cherry tomatoes, red grapes and crusty bread.

Prep 7 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 250g red grapes
  • 1 loaf of crusty bread
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub the drumsticks with butter, thyme and salt, 8 min.
  2. Roast until cooked through, 40 min.
  3. Lay on a long board with the tomatoes, grapes and torn bread.

Shire strawberries with honey creamVegetarian

Sugared strawberries layered with honey whipped cream and shortbread crumbs.

Prep 15 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Berries and cream up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 8 butter shortbread biscuits
  1. Halve the strawberries and toss with the sugar and lemon juice.
  2. Leave to go juicy, 20 min.
  3. Whip the cream with the honey and vanilla to soft peaks.
  4. Crumble the shortbread roughly.
  5. Layer the strawberries, cream and shortbread in glasses and finish with a few whole berries.

Minas Tirith garlic and herb flatbreads

Soft yeasted rounds blistered in a smoking pan, parsley and thyme butter, feta dip and honey.

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 200g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Soft yeasted flatbreads blistered in a smoking pan, brushed with garlic, parsley and thyme butter, with a bowl of feta dip and honey. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt, 250ml warm water and 2 tbsp olive oil and knead 8 minutes, then prove 1 hour. Divide into 8, roll thin and cook in a smoking dry pan for 1 to 2 minutes a side until blistered. Melt 60g butter with 2 crushed garlic cloves, parsley and thyme and brush over. Blend 200g feta with 100g Greek yoghurt, then drizzle with honey.

Mains

Coronation roast sirloin and potatoes

Balsamic gravy, roast potatoes and honey-roasted carrots and parsnips.

Prep 5 min, cook 2 hr 30 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef sirloin
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 carrots, cut in batons
  • 3 parsnips, cut in batons
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Season the beef with salt and pepper and roast until 52°C in the middle, 1 hr.
  2. Rest the beef under foil, 20 min.
  3. Roast the potatoes in oil, 45 min.
  4. Toss the carrots and parsnips with honey and roast, 35 min.
  5. Reduce the stock and balsamic with the pan juices for gravy, 10 min.

Green Dragon chicken and leek pie

Thigh, leek and mushroom in thyme cream under puff, minted peas, for four hobbits home again.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 80g butter
  • 2 leeks
  • 250g button mushrooms
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Chicken thigh, leek and mushroom in a creamy thyme sauce under a golden puff pastry lid, with buttery mash and minted peas. Brown 1kg diced chicken thighs in butter, then add 2 sliced leeks and 250g sliced mushrooms and cook 6 minutes. Stir in 3 tbsp plain flour, then 400ml chicken stock and 150ml cream, add thyme and simmer 15 minutes until thick. Cool slightly, tip into a pie dish, top with puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 30 minutes. Serve with mash and peas tossed with butter and mint.

Sam's wedding cakeVegetarian

Fruit and almond cake filled with apricot jam under white icing.

Prep 25 min, cook 1 hr 35 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, softened
  • 175g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 300g mixed dried fruit
  • 50g almonds, chopped
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  • 300g white icing
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C and line a cake tin, 5 min.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, fruit and almonds.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Cool, split and fill with apricot jam, 10 min.
  5. Cover with white icing and add small flowers.

Drinks

Green Dragon honey and ginger cupNo alcohol

Ginger beer over honey ginger syrup and lemon, served over ice.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g honey
  • 100ml water
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 x 375ml bottles of ginger beer
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Simmer the honey, water and ginger, then cool and strain, 10 min.
  2. Put some syrup and a squeeze of lemon in each of 4 pint glasses with ice.
  3. Pour in the ginger beer, tilting the glass.
  4. Stir once and add a lemon wheel.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Merry and Pippin are feasting in the ruins of Isengard.
