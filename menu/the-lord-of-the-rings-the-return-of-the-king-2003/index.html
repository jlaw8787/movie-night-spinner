Snacks

Steward's roast drumsticks and grapes

Thyme butter drumsticks with cherry tomatoes, red grapes and crusty bread

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 250g red grapes
  • 1 loaf of crusty bread
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Rub the drumsticks with butter, thyme and salt.
  2. Roast until cooked through.
  3. Lay on a long board with the tomatoes, grapes and torn bread.

Shire strawberries with honey creamVegetarian

Sugared strawberries layered with honey whipped cream and shortbread crumbs

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Berries and cream up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 8 butter shortbread biscuits
  1. Halve the strawberries and toss with the sugar and lemon juice.
  2. Leave to go juicy.
  3. Whip the cream with the honey and vanilla to soft peaks.
  4. Crumble the shortbread roughly.
  5. Layer the strawberries, cream and shortbread in glasses and finish with a few whole berries.

Mains

Coronation roast sirloin and potatoes

Red wine gravy, roast potatoes and honey-roasted carrots and parsnips

Prep 30 min, cook 140 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef sirloin
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 carrots, cut in batons
  • 3 parsnips, cut in batons
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200ml red wine
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Season the beef and roast until 52C in the middle.
  2. Rest the beef under foil.
  3. Roast the potatoes in oil.
  4. Toss the carrots and parsnips with honey and roast.
  5. Reduce the red wine with the pan juices for gravy.

Sweets

Sam's wedding cakeVegetarian

Fruit and almond cake filled with apricot jam under white icing

Prep 40 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, softened
  • 175g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 300g mixed dried fruit
  • 50g almonds, chopped
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  • 300g white icing
  1. Heat the oven to 160C and line a cake tin.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, fruit and almonds.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Cool, split and fill with apricot jam.
  5. Cover with white icing and add small flowers.

Drinks

Green Dragon honey and ginger ale cupVegetarian

English bitter or pale ale over honey ginger syrup and lemon, ginger beer if you prefer

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g honey
  • 100ml water
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 375ml English bitter or pale ale
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Simmer the honey, water and ginger, then cool and strain.
  2. Put some syrup and a squeeze of lemon in each of 4 pint glasses with ice.
  3. Pour in the ale, tilting the glass.
  4. Stir once and add a lemon wheel. Use ginger beer for the non drinkers.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Merry and Pippin are feasting in the ruins of Isengard.
