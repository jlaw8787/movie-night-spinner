Snacks

Dolores's equatorial fruit platterVegetarian

Pineapple, mango, papaya and passionfruit with lime juice.

Prep 22 min · Make ahead: Cut the fruit up to 4 hours ahead, keep covered in the fridge; add the lime, salt and chilli to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pineapple
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 2 limes
  • 1 packet banana leaves
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  1. Lay banana leaves on a big platter.
  2. Cut the pineapple, mangoes and papaya into wedges and halve the passionfruit.
  3. Pile the fruit on the leaves and squeeze over lime juice.
  4. Sprinkle with salt mixed with chilli.

Mosasaur ocean prawns

Green prawns grilled with garlic, chilli, lime and butter.

Prep 3 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Crush the garlic and chop the chilli and lime up to 1 day ahead; toss and grill the prawns fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 3 limes
  • 60g butter
  1. Crush the garlic, chop the chilli, zest and juice a lime and cut the rest into wedges. Melt the butter.
  2. Toss the prawns with the garlic, red chillies, zest and butter.
  3. Grill a couple of minutes a side until charred.
  4. Pile onto a platter with lime wedges and pour over the pan butter.

Capsized sailboat fish bites and lime mayoOn the printed menu

Flathead chunks in panko, shallow fried golden, with a zesty chilli dip, rescued at sea.

How it is made
  • 500g flathead fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko crumbs
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 150g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bottle sriracha
  1. Chunks of flathead in crunchy panko, shallow fried golden and served in paper with a zesty lime and chilli mayo for dipping. Cut 500g flathead fillets into bite-size chunks and season. Dust in 50g plain flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs and coat in 120g panko. Shallow fry in 1cm oil at 180°C for 2 minutes a side until golden. Drain on paper towel. Mix 150g mayo with the zest and juice of 1 lime and 1 tsp sriracha. Serve the fish in paper cones with the mayo and lime wedges.

Mains

ParkerGenix expedition grilled fish

Snapper rubbed with lime and chilli, grilled in banana leaf, coconut rice.

Prep 17 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Soften the banana leaves up to 1 day ahead; rub, wrap and grill the fish fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 3 limes
  • 2 red chillies
  • 4 banana leaves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • salt, to taste
  1. Soften the banana leaves in hot water. Rub the fish with lime juice, chopped red chillies and salt.
  2. Wrap each fillet in a leaf.
  3. Cook the rice in the coconut milk with a pinch of salt and a splash of water.
  4. Grill the parcels.
  5. Serve the fish in its leaf on the rice.

Distortus rex beef chilliOn the printed menu

Chuck simmered three hours with kidney beans, cumin and tomato, cornbread, for the stranded crew.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tins kidney beans
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 60g butter
  1. Beef chuck browned and simmered 3 hours with kidney beans, chilli, cumin and tomato until thick, served with cornbread. Brown 1kg diced beef chuck in batches. Fry 2 onions and 4 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika and 2 tsp chilli powder. Return the beef with 2 tins crushed tomatoes and 250ml beef stock and simmer covered for 2.5 hours. Add 2 drained tins kidney beans for the last 30 minutes. Mix 150g cornmeal, 150g flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 egg, 250ml buttermilk and 60g melted butter and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes for cornbread.

Quetzalcoatlus nest egg mousseVegetarian

Dark chocolate, cream and egg white mousse spooned into cups.

Prep 16 min, cook 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Spoon into glasses and chill up to 1 day ahead; top with the biscuits and a speckled egg just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 bag speckled chocolate eggs
  • 1 packet chocolate biscuits
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly. Separate the eggs.
  2. Whip the cream and whisk the whites to soft peaks.
  3. Stir the yolks into the chocolate, then fold in the cream and whites.
  4. Spoon into glasses and chill.
  5. Top with crushed biscuits and a speckled egg.

Drinks

Raft run coconut coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Coconut water and pineapple juice over ice with mint and lime.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Mix the coconut water, pineapple juice and lime up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; add ice and mint to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L coconut water
  • 1 L pineapple juice
  • 2 limes
  • 1 handful mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and a few mint leaves.
  2. Pour in equal parts coconut water and pineapple juice.
  3. Squeeze in a wedge of lime, stir and serve cold.
