Snacks

Magic ticket popcornVegetarian

Popcorn popped in coconut oil and tossed with browned butter

Prep 4 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Brown the butter up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; melt it again and pop the corn just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 120g popping corn kernels
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Cook the butter until golden and nutty.
  2. Heat the coconut oil with three kernels, add the rest when they pop, cover and shake until the popping slows.
  3. Toss with the brown butter and salt and fill striped paper bags.

Times Square knishesVegetarian

Puff pastry squares filled with mashed potato and fried onion

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Fill and shape the knishes up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brush with egg and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 30g butter
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender.
  2. Fry the chopped onion in butter until soft, then mash with the potatoes, salt and pepper.
  3. Cut the pastry into pieces, put a mound of potato on each, fold up and pinch closed.
  4. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200C.
  5. Serve hot with mustard.

Mains

Slater's LA rib eye

Dry-brined seared rib eye with garlic butter and baked potatoes

Prep 20 min, cook 570 min · Make ahead: Dry-brine overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 thick rib eye steaks (about 600g each)
  • 1 tbsp flaky salt
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 60g garlic butter
  • 150g bacon
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Salt the rib eyes and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Dry-brine overnight.
  3. Bake the potatoes at 200C.
  4. Crisp the bacon.
  5. Sear the steaks in the oil, finish at 200C to 54C inside and rest.
  6. Top with garlic butter, slice to share and load the potatoes with sour cream and bacon.

Sweets

To cone a phrase sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with hot fudge sauce and whipped cream

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to 1 week ahead and keep chilled; reheat gently, then whip the cream and scoop on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 150ml cream
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 40g salted peanuts
  • 4 cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate with the cream and golden syrup into a hot fudge.
  2. Whip the cream.
  3. Scoop ice cream into bowls and pour over the fudge.
  4. Top with whipped cream, peanuts and a cherry.

Danny's Pandora choc malt thickshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick chocolate malt shake blended with vanilla ice cream

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best blended to order; crush the malt balls up to 1 day ahead in an airtight container and whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml cold milk
  • 6 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 100ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 40g chocolate malt balls
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream, cocoa and malted milk powder until thick.
  2. Whip the cream and crush the malt balls.
  3. Pour into tall glasses and top with cream and malt balls. Serve with wide straws and long spoons.
