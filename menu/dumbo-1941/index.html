Snacks

Big top popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in melted butter, brown sugar and salt

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 50g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  1. Pop the corn in the oil in a large lidded pot.
  2. Melt the butter with the sugar and salt until bubbling and toss through.
  3. Spread out to cool, then pour into striped paper cones.

Casey Jr. mini corn dogsOn the printed menu

Cocktail frankfurts in sweet cornmeal and buttermilk batter, fried crunchy on sticks, mustard

How it is made
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 20 cocktail frankfurts
  • 20 short skewers
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 jar mustard
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Cocktail franks dipped in a sweet cornmeal and buttermilk batter, fried golden and crunchy on sticks, with mustard and tomato sauce. Whisk 150g cornmeal, 150g plain flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt, 1 egg and 250ml buttermilk into a thick batter, then pour into a tall glass. Pat 20 cocktail frankfurts dry, push a skewer into each and dust in flour. Dip into the batter, letting the excess drip off, and fry at 180°C for 3 minutes until golden. Serve with mustard and tomato sauce.

Mains

Ringmaster's roast

Pork loin rubbed with fennel and garlic, roasted with apple, onion and potatoes

Prep 35 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Rub the pork with the fennel, garlic and salt up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork loin
  • 1 tbsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 green apples, in wedges
  • 2 brown onions, in wedges
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml water
  1. Rub the pork with fennel, garlic and salt.
  2. Roast at 200°C with apple and onion around it and the potatoes alongside.
  3. Rest the pork and make pan gravy with the juices, flour and water.
  4. Slice the pork and spoon over the gravy.

Clown act spaghetti and meatballsOn the printed menu

Parmesan beef balls in slow tomato sauce, tossed through pasta with basil, after the fire act

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 packet breadcrumbs
  • 80g parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Parmesan beef meatballs browned and simmered in a slow tomato sauce, tossed through spaghetti and finished with basil and plenty of parmesan. Mix 600g beef mince, 50g breadcrumbs, 40g parmesan, 1 egg, chopped parsley and salt, and roll into 20 meatballs. Brown in olive oil and set aside. Soften 1 diced onion and 3 garlic cloves, add 2 cans crushed tomatoes and simmer 20 minutes. Add the meatballs and simmer 20 minutes more. Cook 500g spaghetti, toss with some of the sauce and top with meatballs, torn basil and grated parmesan.

Sweets

Dark chocolate peanut clustersVegetarian

Roasted peanuts in dark chocolate with sea salt

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 250g roasted peanuts
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Melt the chocolate and stir in the peanuts and salt.
  2. Spoon small clusters onto baking paper.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Pile into paper bags like peanuts sold under the big top.

Pink elephant marshmallow slice

Biscuit base topped with set marshmallow

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 6 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 4 gelatine sheets
  • 150ml hot water
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp pink food colouring
  • 4 tbsp desiccated coconut
  1. Crush the biscuits with the butter and press into a lined tin.
  2. Soak the gelatine, then dissolve in the hot water with the sugar and simmer.
  3. Cool slightly, whip until thick and white and add pink colouring.
  4. Spread over the base, dust with coconut and set overnight.

Mrs Jumbo's pink lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice and crushed raspberries over ice

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 125g raspberries
  • 6 lemons
  • 1 1/2l cold water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Crush the raspberries in a jug, add the lemon juice, syrup and cold water, then strain.
  3. Pour over ice in tall glasses.
