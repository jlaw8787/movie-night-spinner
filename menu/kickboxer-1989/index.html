Tong Po's crying tiger beef with jaew

Charred rump, smoky chilli and lime dip.

Prep 24 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Marinate the beef and make the jaew up to a day ahead; grill and slice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef rump steak
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp jasmine rice
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 3 limes
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 red shallots, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup coriander and mint leaves
  • 1 baby cos lettuce, leaves separated
  1. Rub the steak with the soy, oyster sauce and half the sugar and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Toast the rice in a dry pan until deep golden, then grind it to a coarse powder.
  3. For the jaew, mix the fish sauce, juice of 2 limes, the rest of the sugar, chilli flakes, shallots and a spoon of the ground rice, 4 min.
  4. Grill the steak over high heat until charred outside and pink inside, then rest it for 5 minutes.
  5. Slice thinly, scatter with herbs and the rest of the rice powder, and serve with lettuce cups, lime wedges and the jaew, 5 min.

Banana blossom and prawn salad

Banana blossom, prawns, chilli jam, coconut. Kurt kicks the banana tree down.

Prep 19 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Make the dressing and toast the coconut up to 2 days ahead; slice the blossom and dress to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 banana blossom (or 2 cups shredded wombok)
  • 2 limes
  • 300g cooked prawns, peeled
  • 2 tbsp Thai chilli jam
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar, grated
  • 3 tbsp coconut cream
  • 3 tbsp shredded coconut
  • 3 tbsp roasted peanuts, chopped
  • 1/2 cup mint and coriander leaves
  1. Peel off the tough outer layers of the blossom, slice the pale heart very thinly and drop it into water with the juice of a lime.
  2. Toast the coconut in a dry pan until golden.
  3. Whisk the chilli jam, fish sauce, sugar, coconut cream and the juice of the other lime.
  4. Drain the blossom well and toss with the prawns, dressing and herbs.
  5. Pile onto a plate and finish with the peanuts and toasted coconut.

Pedicab pad kra pao with a crisp fried egg

Chilli pork, holy basil, jasmine rice.

Prep 6 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice up to a day ahead and chill; stir-fry the pork and fry the eggs to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork mince
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 4 bird's eye chillies
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, plus extra for the eggs
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp white sugar
  • 2 cups holy basil or Thai basil leaves
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  1. Rinse the rice and cook it with 3 cups of water, covered, then rest it off the heat, 20 min.
  2. Pound the garlic and chillies to a rough paste.
  3. Fry the paste in the oil in a smoking wok for 30 seconds, add the pork and stir-fry until browned and catching.
  4. Add the oyster, fish and soy sauces and the sugar, toss, then wilt in the basil.
  5. Fry the eggs in hot oil, spooning it over until the edges are crisp and the yolks still soft, and serve on the rice and pork.

White warrior green curry of chicken

Chicken thigh in green curry with Thai eggplant, basil and coconut.

Prep 5 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the curry up to a day ahead without the basil; reheat and add the basil to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, sliced
  • 4 tbsp green curry paste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 250ml coconut cream
  • 6 Thai eggplants, quartered (or 1 small eggplant, diced)
  • 4 kaffir lime leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar, grated
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • 1 long red chilli, sliced
  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  1. Put the rice on to cook, 5 min.
  2. Boil the coconut cream in a wok until it splits and the oil shows, then fry the curry paste in it until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and stir to coat, then add the coconut milk, eggplant and lime leaves and simmer until the chicken is cooked.
  4. Season with the fish sauce and sugar, stir in the basil and top with the chilli.
  5. Serve with the rice.

Mango sticky rice with smashed palm sugar glassVegetarian

Coconut sticky rice, mango, shards of toffee. Hemp rope, resin and broken glass.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Soak the rice overnight; make the toffee glass up to 3 days ahead and keep it airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g glutinous rice, soaked overnight
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar, plus 150g for the glass
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 2 ripe mangoes
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  1. Drain the rice and steam it in a lined steamer until tender and sticky.
  2. Meanwhile warm 300ml coconut milk with the 100g sugar and the salt, pour most of it over the hot rice, cover and leave to soak in, 10 min.
  3. Melt the 150g sugar in a dry pan until amber, pour thinly onto baking paper, scatter with sesame and leave to set hard, then smash into shards.
  4. Simmer the rest of the coconut milk with the cornflour until thick for the topping.
  5. Serve the rice with sliced mango, the thick coconut and shards of glass stuck in the top.

Mekhong bucket with lime and soda

Thai spirit, lime, energy drink, soda and ice, served by the bucket.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill everything well; build in the bucket to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml Mekhong Thai spirit
  • 2 limes, cut in wedges
  • 250ml Red Bull
  • 500ml soda water
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Squeeze the lime wedges into a small ice bucket or large jug and drop them in.
  2. Fill with ice, pour in the Mekhong and Red Bull and top with soda.
  3. Stir and serve with a straw each.
