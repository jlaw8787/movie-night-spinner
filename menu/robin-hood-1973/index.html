Snacks

Sherwood ploughman's picnic board

Pork pies, aged cheddar, pickled onions, green apple and crusty bread

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheddar up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; slice the apples, tear the loaf and lay out on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 small pork pies
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 200g aged cheddar
  • 200g pickled onions
  • 2 green apples
  1. Spread a cloth on the floor or table.
  2. Cut the cheddar into wedges and slice the apples.
  3. Tear the crusty loaf.
  4. Lay everything out with the pork pies and pickled onions for everyone to help themselves.

Golden Arrow skewers

Chicken thigh and capsicum skewers marinated in honey, mustard and garlic

Prep 23 min, cook 12 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  1. Cut the chicken and capsicums into chunks.
  2. Mix the honey, mustard and garlic and toss through the chicken and capsicum.
  3. Marinate in the fridge.
  4. Thread onto long skewers.
  5. Grill until charred and cooked through.

Mains

Outlaws' camp stew

Lamb neck simmered with pearl barley, carrots, leeks and rosemary

Prep 28 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb neck pieces
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g pearl barley
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1 1/2l water
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the carrots and leeks.
  2. Brown the lamb in batches in the olive oil in a heavy pot.
  3. Add the barley, carrots, leeks, rosemary and water.
  4. Simmer until the lamb falls from the bone.
  5. Season and serve in deep bowls with crusty bread.

Sweets

Skippy's birthday cakesVegetarian

Small honey cakes baked in patty pans, brushed with warm honey

Prep 13 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 125g honey, for the batter
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 3 tbsp honey, for brushing
  • 12 gold chocolate coins
  1. Beat the butter and honey until pale, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour.
  3. Spoon into patty pans.
  4. Bake at 175C until risen and golden.
  5. Cool slightly.
  6. Brush with warm honey and press a chocolate coin on top of each.

Drinks

Merry Men hot spiced apple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Warm apple juice with cinnamon, cloves, orange and honey

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Simmer and strain the punch up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat gently or serve cold
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 2 oranges
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Peel strips of zest from one orange, juice it and slice the other.
  2. Warm the apple juice, 2 cinnamon sticks, cloves, zest, orange juice and honey over low heat without boiling.
  3. Strain and ladle into mugs, each with an orange slice and a cinnamon stick.
  4. For a chilled version, cool the punch and top with sparkling apple juice.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Robin robs Prince John as a fortune teller.
