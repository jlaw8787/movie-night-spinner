Snacks

Desk pop smoked salmon bagel bites

Mini bagels with caper dill cream cheese, smoked salmon, pickled onion

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Pickle the red onion up to 3 days ahead in the fridge and beat the cream cheese up to 1 day ahead; toast the bagels and build on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red onion
  • 80ml white vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 mini bagels
  • 200g smoked salmon
  1. Slice the red onion thin and cover with the white vinegar, the sugar and a pinch of salt for 20 minutes.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the chopped capers, the chopped dill and lemon zest.
  3. Split and toast 8 mini bagels.
  4. Spread each half with the cream cheese, fold on smoked salmon, top with drained pickled onion and dill and grind over pepper.

Hoitz's pakorasVegetarian

Crisp chickpea fritters with onion and spinach, mint yoghurt

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the mint yoghurt up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; make the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g chickpea flour
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 2 onions
  • 100g spinach
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 180ml water
  1. Mix the chickpea flour, the cumin, the chilli, salt and the water into a batter.
  2. Stir in the sliced onions and the chopped spinach.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in the vegetable oil at 180C for 4 minutes and serve with the yoghurt mixed with mint.

Gamble's sesame tuna bowls

Seared tuna, avocado and edamame over rice. The lion loses.

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Whisk the dressing up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and cook the rice up to 3 hours ahead; sear the tuna and slice the avocado just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cup sushi rice
  • rice vinegar, to taste
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 600g sashimi-grade tuna steaks
  • 60g sesame seeds
  • soy sauce, to taste
  • sesame oil, to taste
  • wasabi paste, to taste
  • 200g frozen edamame
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • pickled ginger, to taste
  1. Cook the sushi rice and season with the rice vinegar and the sugar.
  2. Coat the tuna steaks in the mixed sesame seeds.
  3. Sear in a very hot oiled pan 45 seconds a side and slice.
  4. Whisk the soy, the rice vinegar, the sesame oil and a little wasabi.
  5. Boil the frozen edamame 3 minutes.
  6. Build bowls with rice, tuna, sliced avocado, cucumber, edamame and pickled ginger and spoon over the dressing.

Bike messenger browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies baked in a tin and cut in squares

Prep 15 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead, cool completely and keep airtight; cut on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 180g butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Melt the dark chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and the eggs, then fold in the flour and the cocoa.
  3. Bake in a lined 20cm tin at 170C for 28 minutes and cool before cutting.

Gator's whiskey sourVegetarian

Foamy bourbon sour with lemon, bitters and a cherry

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemons and make the sugar syrup up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 4 lemons
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp aromatic bitters
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 4 cup ice
  1. For each drink, put the bourbon, the lemon juice, the sugar syrup and a little egg white in a shaker.
  2. Shake without ice for 10 seconds to build the foam, then add ice and shake hard for 10 seconds more.
  3. Strain over fresh ice in a rocks glass.
  4. Add a few drops of aromatic bitters on the foam and a cherry.
  5. For non-drinkers, swap the bourbon for cold black tea.
