Snacks

Jersey disco fries

Thick chips under beef gravy and melted mozzarella

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Cut and soak the chips in cold water up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, then dry well; make the gravy up to 2 days ahead and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips, soak and dry well.
  2. Fry in the oil at 150C, then again at 190C until crisp.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the stock and simmer until thick.
  4. Pile the chips on a tray, pour over the gravy, cover with mozzarella and grill until melted.

Bluntman and Chronic pizza bagels

After the Meow Mix: pizza sauce and mozzarella.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 plain bagels
  • 8 tbsp pizza sauce
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 60g pepperoni, sliced
  1. Split the bagels and toast lightly.
  2. Spread each half with pizza sauce and top with mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Grill until bubbling. Cut into halves to share.

Mains

Jersey Italian hot dogs

Fried franks in pizza bread with fried potatoes, peppers and mustard

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes, diced
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 green capsicums
  • 2 onions
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 4 pizza bread rounds
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Dice the potatoes and slice the capsicums and onions.
  2. Fry the potatoes in oil until golden, then add the capsicums and onions and cook until soft.
  3. Fry the franks until blistered.
  4. Split the pizza bread rounds, fill with franks and the potato and pepper mix and finish with mustard.

Sweets

Diner rice puddingVegetarian

Creamy vanilla rice pudding, warm or cold. Painting of birds not included.

Prep 5 min, cook 155 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g arborio rice
  • 1l milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Simmer the rice in the milk with the sugar over low heat, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  2. Stir in the vanilla, pour into 4 glasses.
  3. Chill for the setting time.
  4. Dust with cinnamon before serving.

Jersey diner root beer floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Root beer over vanilla ice cream and whipped cream, for the hockey game

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Put the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; scoop, pour and whip the cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g vanilla ice cream (8 scoops)
  • 1l root beer, cold
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Put 4 tall glasses in the freezer to chill.
  2. Drop 2 scoops of ice cream into each glass and pour the root beer slowly down the side.
  3. Whip the cream and finish each with a swirl of cream and a cherry. Serve with a long spoon and a straw.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Holden sits down with Jay and Silent Bob at the diner.
