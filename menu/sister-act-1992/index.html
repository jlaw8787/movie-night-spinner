Snacks

Reno lounge stuffed mushrooms

Button mushrooms stuffed with bacon, garlic, breadcrumbs and parmesan.

Prep 13 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Fill the caps up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 20 button mushrooms
  • 2 rashers of bacon
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  1. Remove the stems from the mushrooms and chop the stems.
  2. Fry the stems with diced bacon and crushed garlic, 6 min.
  3. Mix with breadcrumbs and parmesan.
  4. Fill the caps.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden, 15 min.

Choir practice cheese sconesVegetarian

Cheddar and chive scones with a pinch of cayenne, served warm.

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Rub the butter into the flour and grate the cheddar up to 1 day ahead; add the milk and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 60g butter, plus extra to serve
  • 150g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 300ml full cream milk
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in cheddar, chives and cayenne.
  2. Mix in the milk to a soft dough.
  3. Pat out and cut into rounds.
  4. Bake at 220°C until risen and golden, 12 min.
  5. Serve warm with butter.

Mains

Mary Clarence's convent stew, spiced up

Beef stew with smoked paprika, tomato and a splash of hot sauce.

Prep 23 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 400g can tomatoes
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 800g potatoes
  • 3 carrots
  1. Brown the beef in batches, 10 min.
  2. Add the onions, garlic, paprika and thyme and cook until soft, 10 min.
  3. Pour in the stock, tomatoes and hot sauce.
  4. Braise covered at 150°C, 2 hr.
  5. Chop the potatoes and carrots.
  6. Add the potatoes and carrots and braise until tender, 1 hr.

Late night bar cheeseburgers and fries

Chuck patties, melted cheddar, pickles and sauce on toasted buns, crisp fries, for nuns out late.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 brown onion
  1. Thick chuck patties grilled to medium with melted cheddar, onion, lettuce, pickles and burger sauce on a toasted bun, with crisp double-cooked fries. Cut 1kg potatoes into fries, soak 30 minutes, dry and fry at 140°C for 6 minutes, then at 190°C until crisp. Shape 800g chuck mince into 4 thick patties and season. Grill over high heat for 4 minutes a side, adding a slice of cheddar for the last minute. Mix 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp tomato sauce and 1 tbsp chopped pickles. Build on toasted buns with lettuce, onion and pickles.

St Katherine's bread and butter puddingVegetarian

White bread layered with golden raisins and baked in vanilla custard.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Assemble and soak up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; sprinkle with nutmeg and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 80g sultanas
  • 3 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 70g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  1. Butter the bread, cut into triangles and layer in a dish with sultanas.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream, sugar and vanilla and pour over.
  3. Soak for 20 minutes.
  4. Sprinkle with nutmeg.
  5. Bake at 170°C until puffed and golden, 40 min.

Drinks

Hail Holy Queen honey ginger lemon teaNo alcohol

Hot black tea with fresh ginger, cinnamon, lemon and honey.

Prep 6 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Simmer the ginger and cinnamon up to 2 days ahead and chill; reheat, steep the tea bags and add the lemon and honey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 5 cm knobs of ginger
  • 1.2 L water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the ginger.
  2. Simmer the ginger in water with the cinnamon stick, 10 min.
  3. Turn off the heat, add the tea bags and steep, then remove the bags, 4 min.
  4. Stir in the lemon juice and honey.
  5. Pour into white cups and float a thin lemon slice in each.
