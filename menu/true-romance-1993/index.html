Snacks

Triple feature popcornVegetarian

Half salted, half sweet. Alabama dumps hers on Clarence.

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best popped just before serving; mix the soy and sugar into the butter up to 2 days ahead and rewarm to toss
Recipe for 4
  • 150g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  1. Pop the kernels in the coconut oil.
  2. Toss half with melted butter and fine salt.
  3. Toss the rest with butter melted with soy and sugar.
  4. Serve in big paper buckets for the triple feature.

Comic shop cheese puffsVegetarian

Cheddar choux. Clarence's purple Cadillac is parked out front.

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best fresh from the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g vintage cheddar
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute.
  2. Cool slightly, beat in the eggs, cheddar and cayenne.
  3. Pipe small rounds.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and hollow.

Detroit Coney dog sliders

Griddled cocktail frankfurts in steamed rolls, spicy beef chilli sauce, yellow mustard, raw onion.

How it is made
  • 250g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 16 mini rolls
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  1. Griddled cocktail franks in steamed mini rolls under a fine, spicy beef chilli sauce, yellow mustard and plenty of diced raw onion. Brown 250g fine beef mince with half a finely diced onion, breaking it up very small. Add 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 200ml water and simmer 20 minutes into a loose sauce. Griddle 16 cocktail franks until blistered. Steam 16 mini rolls for 1 minute. Fill with franks, spoon over the sauce and top with mustard and diced raw onion.

Mains

Ambassador Hotel club sandwich and fries

Triple decker and fries. Dick throws the suitcase in the air.

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Cut and fry the shoestrings the first time up to 2 hours ahead; grill the bacon, build the sandwiches and finish the fries just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 300g roast chicken, shredded
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Cut the potatoes into shoestrings and fry once in the oil.
  2. Grill the bacon and toast the bread.
  3. Layer chicken, bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayonnaise across three slices each and cut into triangles.
  4. Fry the fries a second time until crisp.

Steve's double cheeseburgers

Two crispy chuck patties, cheese and pickles, crinkle-cut fries, the biggest, fattest on the menu.

How it is made
  • 800g frozen crinkle-cut fries
  • 1 small red cabbage
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 brown onion
  • 4 burger buns
  1. Two crispy smashed chuck patties with American cheese, onion, pickles and burger sauce in soft buns, wrapped in paper, with crinkle-cut fries and purple slaw. Bake 800g crinkle-cut fries at 220°C for 25 minutes. Toss a quarter of a red cabbage, shredded, with 2 tbsp mayo and lemon. Roll 800g chuck mince into 8 balls. Smash thin on a smoking griddle, season, cook 2 minutes, flip and add American cheese. Mix 4 tbsp mayo, 2 tbsp tomato sauce and chopped pickles for sauce. Stack two patties per toasted bun with onion, pickles and sauce, and wrap in paper.

Diner cherry pieVegetarian

Lattice cherry pie with almond. “You want a bite of my pie?”

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 800g frozen sour cherries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 drop almond extract
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the cherries with the sugar, lemon juice, almond extract and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two sheets of pastry and fill.
  3. Weave a lattice from the other two sheets.
  4. Bake at 190°C.
  5. Cool.
  6. Serve with ice cream.

Drinks

Cancún beach margarita

Tequila and fresh lime shaken with ice, in a salt-rimmed glass.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; shake each drink with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 80ml orange liqueur
  • 4 limes
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the limes and rim four glasses with salt.
  2. Shake the tequila, orange liqueur and lime juice hard with ice, one drink at a time.
  3. Strain into the glasses.

When to serve

Serve the pie when Clarence and Alabama head to the diner after the kung fu triple feature.
