Snacks

Triple feature popcornVegetarian

Half salted, half sweet. Alabama dumps hers on Clarence.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best popped just before serving; mix the soy and sugar into the butter up to 2 days ahead and rewarm to toss
Recipe for 4
  • 150g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  1. Pop the kernels in the coconut oil.
  2. Toss half with melted butter and fine salt.
  3. Toss the rest with butter melted with soy and sugar.
  4. Serve in big paper buckets for the triple feature.

Comic shop cheese puffsVegetarian

Cheddar choux. Clarence's purple Cadillac is parked out front

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh from the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g vintage cheddar
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute.
  2. Cool slightly, beat in the eggs, cheddar and cayenne.
  3. Pipe small rounds.
  4. Bake at 200C until puffed and hollow.

Mains

Ambassador Hotel club sandwich and fries

Triple decker and fries. Dick throws the suitcase in the air

Prep 30 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cut and fry the shoestrings the first time up to 2 hours ahead; grill the bacon, build the sandwiches and finish the fries just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 300g roast chicken, shredded
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Cut the potatoes into shoestrings and fry once in the oil.
  2. Grill the bacon and toast the bread.
  3. Layer chicken, bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayonnaise across three slices each and cut into triangles.
  4. Fry the fries a second time until crisp.

Sweets

Diner cherry pieVegetarian

Lattice cherry pie with almond. "You want a bite of my pie?"

Prep 35 min, cook 290 min · Make ahead: Bake the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 800g frozen sour cherries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 drop almond extract
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the cherries with the sugar, lemon juice, almond extract and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two sheets of pastry and fill.
  3. Weave a lattice from the other two sheets.
  4. Bake at 190C.
  5. Cool.
  6. Serve with ice cream.

Drinks

Cancún beach margaritaVegetarian

Salt rimmed tequila and lime. Mexico, on Donowitz's money.

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; shake each drink with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 80ml orange liqueur
  • 4 limes
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the limes and rim four glasses with salt.
  2. Shake the tequila, orange liqueur and lime juice hard with ice, one drink at a time.
  3. Strain into the glasses.

When to serve

Serve the pie when Clarence and Alabama head to the diner after the kung fu triple feature.
