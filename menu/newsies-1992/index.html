Snacks

Hot pretzelsVegetarian

Dough knots dipped in a baking soda bath, coarse salt, baked golden, a penny on the corner.

Prep 23 min, cook 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g bread flour
  • 4g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 160ml warm water
  • 1 L water, for the bath
  • 30g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp coarse salt
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, fine salt and warm water into a dough, 5 min.
  2. Knead until smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover and rise until doubled, 1 hr.
  4. Shape into small pretzels.
  5. Simmer the water with the bicarbonate of soda and bathe each pretzel briefly, then drain onto a lined tray and sprinkle with coarse salt, 8 min.
  6. Bake at 220°C conventional until deep golden, 12 min.

Hot corn on the cobVegetarian

Boiled cobs charred on the grill and rolled in butter and salt.

Prep 2 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Boil the corn up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; char on a hot grill and butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 small corn cobs
  • 30g butter, softened
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Boil the corn in water, 5 min.
  2. Drain, then char on a hot grill, turning often, 7 min.
  3. Roll in the softened butter and sprinkle with sea salt.

Lodging house cinnamon breakfast buns

Soft milk buns rolled with butter, brown sugar and cinnamon, iced, a penny a piece.

How it is made
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g sugar
  • 300ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 110g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 120g icing sugar
  • vanilla extract
  1. Soft milk buns rolled with butter, brown sugar and cinnamon, baked golden and glazed with vanilla icing. Mix 500g bread flour, 7g yeast, 50g sugar, 1 tsp salt, 250ml warm milk, 1 egg and 50g soft butter, knead 8 minutes and rise 1 hour. Roll to 40 x 30cm, spread with 60g butter and 100g brown sugar mixed with 2 tsp cinnamon. Roll up, cut 12 buns and rise 30 minutes. Bake at 190°C for 22 minutes. Glaze with 120g icing sugar mixed with milk and vanilla.

Brooklyn hot dogs

Griddled franks in steamed buns, mustard and sauerkraut, wrapped in yesterday's papers.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best griddled and wrapped just before serving; drain the sauerkraut up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 soft hot dog buns
  • 8 tsp yellow mustard
  • 200g sauerkraut, drained
  1. Griddle the franks over medium-high heat until blistered and piping hot, 8 min.
  2. Steam the buns until warm.
  3. Fill each bun with a frank, mustard and sauerkraut, then wrap in food-safe paper.

Apple turnoversVegetarian

Puff pastry squares folded over buttery spiced apple, egg-washed and baked golden.

Prep 22 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Cook and cool the apple filling up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; fill, glaze and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g apples
  • 20g butter
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Peel, core and dice the apples.
  2. Cook with the butter, sugar and cinnamon until tender and fairly dry, 10 min.
  3. Cool the filling, 15 min.
  4. Cut each pastry sheet into four squares, fill, fold diagonally and seal the edges.
  5. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C until golden, 22 min.

Sarsaparilla ice cream spidersNo alcohol

Sarsaparilla poured over vanilla until it fizzes into a creamy foam, seize the day.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Chill the sarsaparilla and the glasses up to 1 day ahead; scoop and pour just before serving so the foam holds
Recipe for 4
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 1 L sarsaparilla, chilled
  1. Put a scoop of ice cream in each tall glass.
  2. Slowly pour the sarsaparilla over, letting the foam rise and settle before topping up.
  3. Add a second small scoop and serve with long spoons and straws.
