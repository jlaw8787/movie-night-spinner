Stadium security pretzelsVegetarian

Homemade soft pretzels dipped in bicarb water, topped with rock salt.

Prep 10 min, cook 22 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fresh, up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp rock salt
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and warm water into a dough.
  2. Rest until doubled.
  3. Shape into pretzels, dip in boiling water with bicarb soda and sprinkle with rock salt.
  4. Bake at 220°C, brush with butter and serve with mustard.

Limited Edition purple potato chipsVegetarian

Paper thin purple sweet potato fried crisp.

Prep 15 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 purple sweet potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Slice the sweet potatoes paper thin on a mandoline.
  2. Soak in cold water, then drain and dry very well on tea towels.
  3. Fry in the oil in small batches at 160°C, stirring, until the bubbling slows and they crisp up.
  4. Drain on paper towel and season straight away with the sea salt and smoked paprika.
  5. Cool so they crisp fully.

Philly scrapple with apple butter

Fried pork and cornmeal scrapple with apple butter. Philly breakfast before the stadium shift.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 1 jar dried sage
  • 180g polenta
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 40g butter
  • 1 jar apple butter
  • 4 eggs
  1. Pork, sage and polenta set into a loaf, sliced and fried until crunchy outside and soft inside, with apple butter and a fried egg. Brown 500g pork mince with 1 diced onion, then add 1L chicken stock, 1 tsp dried sage, 1/2 tsp pepper and salt and bring to the boil. Whisk in 180g polenta and stir over low heat for 15 minutes until very thick. Press into a lined loaf tin and chill overnight. Slice 1.5cm thick, dust with flour and fry in butter for 4 minutes a side until crisp. Serve with apple butter and fried eggs.

Weight room rump with garlic butter

Rump steaks with parsley garlic butter and roast potatoes.

Prep 15 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Mash the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; roast, grill and blanch on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 4 rump steaks, about 250g each
  • 300g green beans
  • 20g butter for beans
  1. Toss the potatoes in oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 220°C until crisp.
  3. Mash the soft butter with garlic and parsley.
  4. Season the steaks and grill over high heat for medium.
  5. Rest the steaks and blanch the beans, tossing them with butter.
  6. Top each steak with a slice of garlic butter.

Dunn family bacon and scrambled eggs

Soft buttery curds with chives, crisp rashers, grilled tomato and toast.

How it is made
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 8 eggs
  • 60ml milk
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 loaf thick white bread
  • 1L orange juice
  1. Soft buttery scrambled eggs with chives, crisp bacon, grilled tomatoes and thick buttered toast, served family style at the kitchen table. Fry 8 bacon rashers until crisp and keep warm. Halve 4 tomatoes, season and grill cut side up for 5 minutes. Whisk 8 eggs with 60ml milk and salt. Melt 30g butter in a non-stick pan over low heat, add the eggs and stir slowly for 4 minutes until just set and creamy, then stir in chopped chives. Toast 4 thick slices of bread and butter well. Serve with orange juice.

Sweets

Mr Glass lemon and berry jelly

Lemon jelly set with blueberries and raspberries.

Prep 20 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 3 lemons
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 5 gelatine sheets
  • 125g blueberries
  • 125g raspberries
  1. Warm the water with the lemon juice and sugar.
  2. Soak the gelatine, squeeze and stir in until dissolved.
  3. Divide the blueberries and raspberries among round glasses and pour over the jelly.
  4. Chill until clear and set.

Drinks

Green poncho honey ginger tea

Fresh ginger simmered, then steeped with green tea and honey.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Simmer the ginger water up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat, steep the tea and finish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 5cm piece ginger, sliced
  • 1 L water
  • 4 green tea bags
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 120ml whisky, optional
  1. Simmer the ginger in the water, then take off the heat and let it cool for a couple of minutes.
  2. Add the tea bags and steep, then remove.
  3. Stir in the honey and juice of one lemon.
  4. Pour into mugs with a lemon slice each and add whisky to any mug where it is wanted.
