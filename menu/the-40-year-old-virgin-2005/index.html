Andy's egg salad, bread includedVegetarian

Egg salad with Japanese mayo, Dijon and celery on bread

Prep 20 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Make the egg salad up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; butter the bread and build the sandwiches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 100g Japanese mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 loaf soft milk bread
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  1. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Chop roughly and fold with the Japanese mayonnaise, the Dijon, the finely diced celery stick, the chives, salt and plenty of pepper.
  3. Butter the slices of soft milk bread.
  4. Lay iceberg lettuce on the slices, pile on the egg salad and close.
  5. Cut off the crusts, slice in half and press lightly.

Smart Tech poker night queso dip

Chorizo queso and corn chips. Poker night, when the guys find out

Prep 10 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Make the queso up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently with a splash of milk; serve hot with the corn chips and peanuts
Recipe for 4
  • 1 chorizo sausage
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 250g cheddar
  • 80g roasted red capsicum
  • ground cumin, to taste
  • 250g corn chips
  • 150g salted peanuts
  1. Fry the diced chorizo until crisp.
  2. Add half a diced onion and the chopped jalapeño for 3 minutes.
  3. Stir in the cornflour, then the evaporated milk, and simmer until it thickens.
  4. Over low heat, stir in the grated cheddar a handful at a time until smooth, then the chopped roasted capsicum and a pinch of cumin.
  5. Serve hot in a pan with the corn chips and the salted peanuts.

Trish's lemon garlic roast chicken

Lemon roast chicken. Trish sells his collectibles to fund the store

Prep 6 min, cook 87 min · Make ahead: Rub and stuff the chicken up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, and cut the vegetables the same day; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Rub the chicken with the soft butter, salt and pepper and stuff with the halved lemon, the garlic cloves and thyme.
  3. Toss the potatoes and the carrots in chunks with oil and salt around it.
  4. Roast 1 hour 15 minutes, turning the vegetables once.
  5. Rest the chicken 10 minutes.
  6. Whisk the flour into the pan juices, add the stock and simmer 3 minutes for gravy.

Mooj's lamb rogan josh

Slow-cooked lamb. Mooj says life is about passion and spirit

Prep 16 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Make the curry up to 2 days ahead and reheat; it gets better
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 2 brown onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 20g ginger
  • 3 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 6 cardamom pods
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Fry the sliced onions in the ghee for 12 minutes until deep golden, then add the grated garlic and ginger, chilli, garam masala and cardamom for 1 minute.
  2. Add the lamb and brown on all sides, then stir in the tomatoes, 100g of the yoghurt and 250ml water.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 1 and a half hours until the lamb is tender.
  4. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, then rest for 5.
  5. Grate the cucumber, squeeze it out and mix with the rest of the yoghurt and a pinch of salt.
  6. Scatter the curry with coriander and serve with the rice and raita.

Speed dating salted chocolate trufflesVegetarian

Salted truffles. saved for the 20th date

Prep 10 min, cook 2.5 hours, then 3.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the ganache until firm, then roll and coat the truffles up to 1 week ahead and keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml thickened cream
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 20g butter
  • cocoa powder, to taste
  • 40g hazelnuts
  • freeze-dried raspberries, to taste
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Heat the cream until just simmering and pour over the chopped dark chocolate.
  2. Stand 2 minutes, then stir until smooth with the butter and a pinch of salt.
  3. Chill 2 hours until firm.
  4. Scoop into balls and roll quickly in your hands.
  5. Roll a third in cocoa, a third in the crushed toasted hazelnuts and a third in crushed freeze-dried raspberries.
  6. Chill until serving.

Andy's chocolate malt milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick chocolate malt. before the bike crash

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the glasses up to 2 hours ahead and crush the malt balls up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chocolate ice cream
  • 400ml milk
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 60g chocolate malt balls
  1. Blend the chocolate ice cream, the cold milk and the malted milk powder until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into tall chilled glasses.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks and spoon on top.
  4. Crush the chocolate malt balls and scatter over.
  5. Serve with straws.
