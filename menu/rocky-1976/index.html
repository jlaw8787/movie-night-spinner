Snacks

Philly cheesesteak bites

Shaved rib eye and melted provolone in mini rolls.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Freeze and shave the steak up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; sear and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g rib eye steak
  • 1 brown onion
  • 6 slices of provolone
  • 12 mini bread rolls
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Freeze the steak for 30 minutes so it firms up, then shave as thin as you can.
  2. Sear the onion on a hot plate in oil until soft, then push aside and sear the beef quickly, 8 min.
  3. Lay the provolone over the meat to melt.
  4. Split the rolls and pile the filling in.

Italian Market antipasto

Salami, prosciutto, provolone, artichokes, olives and roasted peppers, crusty bread.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, cover and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g salami
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 150g provolone
  • 1 jar artichoke hearts
  • 1 jar olives
  • 1 jar roasted capsicum
  • 1 Italian loaf
  1. Drain the artichokes, olives and capsicum.
  2. Arrange the salami, prosciutto and sliced provolone on a board with the jar items.
  3. Serve with the loaf torn into pieces.

Uncle Sam soft pretzels with beer cheese

Chewy figure-eights dipped in baking soda water, baked with rock salt, cheddar dip, for Apollo.

How it is made
  • 520g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 1 packet bicarb soda
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 20g butter
  • 150ml lager
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  1. Chewy figure-eight pretzels dipped in hot bicarb water, baked deep brown with rock salt, served with yellow mustard and a sharp cheddar beer cheese dip. Mix 500g plain flour, 7g yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 320ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Roll 8 ropes and twist into figure eights. Dip each for 30 seconds in 2L simmering water with 80g bicarb soda, sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Melt 20g butter, stir in 1 tbsp flour, 150ml lager and 200g grated cheddar until smooth.

Mains

Meat locker steak

Cast iron seared sirloin basted in garlic thyme butter, with roast potatoes.

Prep 8 min, cook 48 min, 12 hr waiting · Make ahead: Salt the steaks the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  1. Dry-brine the steaks with salt, uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Cut the potatoes into chunks, toss in oil and roast at 220°C until crisp, 40 min.
  3. Season the steaks with cracked pepper. Sear in a smoking cast iron pan, flipping every minute, 8 min.
  4. Add the butter, garlic and thyme and baste until the steaks reach 54°C inside.
  5. Rest the steaks before serving with the potatoes, 5 min.

Paulie's Thanksgiving turkey and stuffing

Brined breast, sage and onion stuffing, cranberry and gravy, keep it away from the back door.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 100g salt
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 200g white bread
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 egg
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Brined turkey breast roasted golden and sliced thick, with sage and onion stuffing, cranberry sauce and a rich pan gravy. Keep it on the table. Brine a 1.5kg turkey breast overnight in 2L water with 100g salt and 50g sugar. Pat dry, rub with 40g butter and roast at 170°C to 70°C inside, about 80 minutes, then rest 15 minutes. Mix 200g torn bread with a sauteed onion, 40g butter, sage and an egg and bake 30 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the tray juices, add 400ml chicken stock and simmer for gravy. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Sweets

Cuff and Link turtle clustersVegetarian

Toasted pecans with caramel and dark chocolate, shaped like little turtles.

Prep 5 min, cook 23 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pecans
  • 200g caramels
  • 30ml thickened cream
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Toast the pecans in a dry pan and arrange in clusters of four on baking paper, 10 min.
  2. Melt the caramels with the cream and spoon over each cluster, 8 min.
  3. Melt the chocolate and spoon over the top, 5 min.
  4. Set in the fridge until firm, 30 min.

Banana, milk, peanut butter and oats blended thick, no raw eggs.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before drinking, as it thins out on standing
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 40g rolled oats
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Blend the ripe bananas, full cream milk, peanut butter, rolled oats and honey until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into four glasses. Skip the raw eggs, Rocky.
