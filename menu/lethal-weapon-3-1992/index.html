Snacks

Hockey game hot dogs

Griddled franks in steamed buns with yellow mustard

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Simmer the franks up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; griddle, fill and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp sweet relish
  • 1 onion
  1. Simmer the franks in water, then griddle until blistered all over.
  2. Steam the buns and finely chop the onion.
  3. Fill with franks and top with mustard, relish and onion.

Building implosion popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in melted butter, garlic powder and grated parmesan

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best popped just before serving; melt the butter and grate the parmesan up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 120g popping corn kernels
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  1. Heat the oil in a big pot with three kernels.
  2. When they pop, add the rest of the kernels, cover and shake until popping slows.
  3. Toss with the butter, garlic powder and parmesan while hot.

Mains

Trish's chicken and dumplings

Chicken thighs simmered in stock with vegetables and self-raising dumplings

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Simmer and shred the chicken in the stock up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; make and steam the dumplings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 carrots
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 1/2l chicken stock
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g butter, cold
  • 150ml milk
  1. Chop the onion, celery sticks and carrots and put them in a pot with the chicken thighs, thyme sprigs and chicken stock.
  2. Simmer until the chicken is tender.
  3. Shred the chicken back into the pot.
  4. Rub the butter into the flour and mix with the milk.
  5. Drop spoonfuls on top and steam covered.

Scar comparison sticky beef short ribs

Riggs and Lorna compare scars, you compare sauce

Prep 5 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Roast the ribs up to 2 days ahead; glaze and finish under the grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.6kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 250ml cola
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Rub the ribs with the sugar, paprika and salt and put in a deep tray with the stock.
  2. Cover tightly with foil and roast until tender.
  3. Simmer the cola and barbecue sauce until syrupy, brush over the ribs and roast uncovered until sticky.
  4. Boil the corn and serve with butter alongside the ribs.

Sweets

Retirement cakeVegetarian

Lemon zest butter cake soaked in lemon syrup

Prep 5 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, softened
  • 175g caster sugar, for the cake
  • 3 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 4 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar, for the syrup
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a loaf tin. Beat the butter with the caster sugar for the cake, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and the zest of two lemons.
  2. Bake the loaf.
  3. Mix the juice of two lemons with the caster sugar for the syrup and pour over the hot loaf.
  4. Cool in the tin.

Drinks

Eight days to go whiskey sourVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and sugar syrup shaken with egg white until foamy

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemon juice and mix it with the syrup up to 1 day ahead and chill; shake with the bourbon and egg whites to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 120ml lemon juice
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 tray ice
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Shake the bourbon, lemon juice, syrup and egg whites hard without ice to foam.
  2. Add a big handful of ice and shake again.
  3. Strain into tumblers over fresh ice.
  4. Shake bitters onto the foam and add a cherry.

When to serve

Pour drinks during the retirement countdown.
