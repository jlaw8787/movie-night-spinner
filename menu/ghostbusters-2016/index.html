Snacks

Plenty-of-wontons soup

Pork and prawn wontons in ginger chicken broth with bok choy.

Prep 31 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the wontons up to 1 day ahead, covered on a tray in the fridge, or freeze for 1 month; make the broth and cook the wontons on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger, plus slices for the broth
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, divided
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 1.5 L chicken stock
  • 2 bunches bok choy, halved
  1. Mix the pork and prawns with grated ginger and some soy.
  2. Fill the wrappers and fold.
  3. Simmer the stock with ginger slices and soy.
  4. Add the wontons and cook.
  5. Add the bok choy for the last minute and load up the bowls.

Ectoplasm lime dip

Avocado, edamame and lime dip with prawn crackers and cucumber sticks.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead with cling wrap pressed onto the surface so it stays green; cut the cucumber on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 avocados
  • 200g edamame, thawed
  • 2 limes
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 1 bag prawn crackers
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • salt, to taste
  1. Blitz the avocado, edamame, lime juice, garlic, coriander and salt until smooth and very green.
  2. Spoon into a bowl.
  3. Cut the cucumber into sticks.
  4. Serve with prawn crackers and cucumber for dipping.

Proton pack vegetable spring rollsVegetarian

Long crisp rolls of cabbage, shiitake and glass noodles, sweet chilli to dip.

Prep 26 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Roll up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled in one layer, or freeze up to 1 month; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rice vermicelli
  • 1/4 wombok, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 150g shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 spring onions
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 20 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Soak the vermicelli in hot water 5 minutes, drain and snip into short lengths.
  2. Stir-fry the wombok, carrot, mushrooms and sliced spring onion in a splash of the oil until wilted, then add the soy and sesame oil.
  3. Toss with the noodles and cool completely.
  4. Mix the cornflour with a little water. Roll a spoonful of filling tightly in each wrapper, folding in the sides, and seal with the paste.
  5. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry the rolls in batches until golden. Drain and serve with sweet chilli sauce.

Zhu's sweet and sour chicken with egg fried rice

Crispy chicken tossed with pineapple and peppers, egg fried rice on the side.

Prep 10 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice a day ahead and keep it cold in the fridge; cut the chicken up to 1 day ahead; fry and sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 100g cornflour
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 80ml rice vinegar
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 1/2 pineapple, in pieces
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 4 eggs
  • 600g cooked cold long grain rice
  1. Cut the chicken into chunks and coat in cornflour.
  2. Fry in the oil in batches until crisp.
  3. Simmer rice vinegar, sugar and tomato sauce, add pineapple and sliced capsicum.
  4. Make egg fried rice from the cold rice.
  5. Toss the chicken through the sauce and serve with the rice.

Times Square onion sauce hot dogs

Frankfurts in soft rolls with onion sauce and mustard. Kevin's floating hot dog, Times Square style.

How it is made
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  • 8 all-beef franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 jar sweet pickle relish
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. All-beef franks griddled until split, in soft buns with spicy brown mustard, sweet onions stewed in tomato and a little chilli, and pickle relish. Slice 2 onions thinly and cook in oil over medium heat for 10 minutes until soft. Add 3 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tsp sugar, half a tsp chilli flakes, 1 tsp vinegar and 150ml water and simmer 15 minutes until thick and sticky. Griddle 8 all-beef franks until blistered and split. Warm 8 hot dog buns. Fill with franks, mustard, the onion sauce and a spoon of pickle relish.

Zhu's fortune cookiesVegetarian

Crisp vanilla cookies folded around paper fortunes.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake and fold on the day, cookies keep 3 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 egg whites
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 60g plain flour
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1 drop vanilla extract
  1. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar, then mix in the flour, melted butter and vanilla.
  2. Spread thin rounds on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 180°C in batches of a few at a time.
  4. Working fast while hot, lay a paper fortune on each, fold in half and bend over a cup edge.

Drinks

Ectoplasm kiwi lime slime sodaNo alcohol

Kiwi, lime and mint puree over crushed ice, topped with soda water.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep it chilled; blitz the kiwi mix on the night and add soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 4 kiwifruit
  • 3 limes
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 600ml cold soda water
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in hot water and cool.
  2. Peel the kiwifruit and blitz with lime juice, mint leaves and the syrup until smooth.
  3. Spoon into four tall glasses over crushed ice.
  4. Top each slowly with cold soda water so it foams up.
  5. Stir once and add a lime wheel.
