Prom night canapés

Devilled eggs, ham and pineapple picks, chive cream cheese on cucumber.

Prep 28 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 150g leg ham, diced
  • 1/4 pineapple, diced
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into rounds
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool, peel and halve.
  2. Whip the yolks with the mayonnaise and paprika and pipe back into the whites.
  3. Thread the ham and pineapple onto toothpicks.
  4. Pipe the cream cheese mixed with chives onto the cucumber rounds.
  5. Serve on silver trays.

Tommy Ross corsage cruditésVegetarian

Radish roses, cucumber and carrot sticks bunched like a corsage, ranch dip.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into batons
  • 2 carrots, cut into batons
  • 100ml buttermilk
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Cut the radishes into roses.
  2. Bunch the cucumber and carrot batons like a corsage.
  3. Mix the buttermilk, sour cream, garlic, dill and chives for ranch.

Gym class soft pretzels with cheese dip

Chewy knots dipped in baking soda water, salted and baked brown, warm cheddar mustard dip.

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 box rock salt
  • 20g butter
  • 250ml milk
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  1. Chewy soft pretzels dipped in hot bicarb water, salted and baked deep brown, with a warm cheddar and mustard cheese dip. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water and knead 8 minutes, then prove 1 hour. Roll into ropes and cut into 3cm bites. Simmer 2 litres water with 60g bicarb, drop the bites in for 20 seconds, drain, sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Melt 20g butter, stir in 1 tbsp flour, whisk in 250ml milk until thick, then melt in 150g grated cheddar and 1 tsp mustard.

Prom dinner chicken

Pan seared chicken breast in white wine mushroom cream, mashed potatoes and green beans.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr · Make ahead: Make the mash up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled to reheat; brown the chicken and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 60g butter
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 150ml white wine
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled
  • 300g green beans
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and mash with some of the butter, 25 min.
  2. Brown the chicken in butter, then finish in a 200°C oven, 20 min.
  3. Fry the mushrooms in the pan, add the wine and cream and reduce to a sauce, 10 min.
  4. Steam the beans and toss with butter, 5 min.

Margaret's chicken and dumplings

Thighs simmered with carrot, celery and thyme in creamy broth, fluffy parsley dough steamed on top.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 litre chicken stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 100ml cream
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g butter
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 120ml milk
  1. Chicken thighs simmered with carrot, celery and thyme in a creamy broth, topped with fluffy parsley dumplings steamed right on top in the pot. Brown 1kg chicken thighs in a large pot, then add 1 diced onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery sticks and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 1 litre chicken stock and thyme. Simmer covered for 30 minutes and stir in 100ml cream. Mix 200g self-raising flour, 50g grated cold butter, chopped parsley and a pinch of salt with about 120ml milk to a soft dough. Drop 8 spoonfuls on top, cover and simmer for 15 minutes until puffed.

Bates High prom queen strawberry cakeVegetarian

Vanilla sponge layers filled with strawberries and whipped cream.

Prep 35 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Cake layers up to 1 day ahead, assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 350g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300g strawberries, most sliced and some mashed
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 300g icing sugar
  1. Beat the larger share of butter with the caster sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175°C for 25 minutes, then cool completely.
  3. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and the mashed strawberry into a pink buttercream.
  4. Fill the cake with sliced strawberries and whipped cream, then cover in the buttercream.

Bucket-free prom punchNo alcohol

A pineapple juice ice ring in lemon-lime soda and pineapple juice, with strawberries.

Prep 5 min, 16 hr waiting · Make ahead: Ring needs to freeze overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml pineapple juice, 500ml to freeze
  • 1.3 L lemonade
  • 150g strawberries, sliced
  • 1 orange, sliced
  1. Freeze part of the pineapple juice in a ring tin overnight.
  2. Wait for it to freeze solid, 8 hr.
  3. Put the ring in a big glass bowl, pour over the lemonade and the rest of the pineapple juice and add the sliced strawberries and orange, 5 min.
