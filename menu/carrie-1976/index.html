Snacks

Prom night canapés

Devilled eggs, ham and pineapple picks, chive cream cheese on cucumber

Prep 35 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 150g leg ham, diced
  • 1/4 pineapple, diced
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into rounds
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool, peel and halve.
  2. Whip the yolks with the mayonnaise and paprika and pipe back into the whites.
  3. Thread the ham and pineapple onto toothpicks.
  4. Pipe the cream cheese mixed with chives onto the cucumber rounds.
  5. Serve on silver trays.

Tommy Ross corsage cruditésVegetarian

Radish roses, cucumber and carrot sticks bunched like a corsage, ranch dip

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into batons
  • 2 carrots, cut into batons
  • 100ml buttermilk
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Cut the radishes into roses.
  2. Bunch the cucumber and carrot batons like a corsage.
  3. Mix the buttermilk, sour cream, garlic, dill and chives for ranch.

Mains

Prom dinner chicken

Pan seared chicken breast in white wine mushroom cream, mash and green beans

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the mash up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled to reheat; brown the chicken and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 60g butter
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 150ml white wine
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled
  • 300g green beans
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and mash with some of the butter.
  2. Brown the chicken in butter, then finish in a 200C oven.
  3. Fry the mushrooms in the pan, add the wine and cream and reduce to a sauce.
  4. Steam the beans and toss with butter.

Sweets

Bates High prom queen strawberry cakeVegetarian

Vanilla sponge layers filled with strawberries and whipped cream

Prep 45 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Cake layers up to 1 day ahead, assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 350g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300g strawberries, most sliced and some mashed
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 300g icing sugar
  1. Beat the larger share of butter with the caster sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175C for 25 minutes, then cool completely.
  3. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and the mashed strawberry into a pink buttercream.
  4. Fill the cake with sliced strawberries and whipped cream, then cover in the buttercream.

Drinks

Bucket-free prom punchVegetarianNo alcohol

A pineapple juice ice ring in lemonade and pineapple juice, with strawberries

Prep 10 min, cook 480 min · Make ahead: Ring needs to freeze overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml pineapple juice, 500ml to freeze
  • 1 1/4l lemonade
  • 150g strawberries, sliced
  • 1 orange, sliced
  1. Freeze part of the pineapple juice in a ring tin overnight.
  2. Wait for it to freeze solid.
  3. Put the ring in a big glass bowl, pour over the lemonade and the rest of the pineapple juice and add the sliced strawberries and orange.
