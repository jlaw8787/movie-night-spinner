Snacks

Sam's crispy smashed potatoesVegetarian

Baby potatoes boiled, smashed flat and roasted until crisp

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  • 40g butter
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • 1 tsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and steam dry.
  2. Heat the oven to 230C and press each potato flat on an oiled tray with a mug.
  3. Drizzle with oil and salt and roast until deep golden.
  4. Melt the butter with the garlic, thyme and rosemary and pour over.
  5. Mix the sour cream with chives and salt for the dip.

Rohan honeyed oatcakesVegetarian

Honey oat bars baked crisp, served with sharp cheddar

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200g rolled oats
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  • 2 apples, sliced
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line a tray.
  2. Melt the butter and honey and stir into the oats and salt.
  3. Press thin into the tray and bake.
  4. Cut while warm.
  5. Serve with cheddar and apple.

Mains

Sam's rabbit stew

Rabbit or chicken stewed with ale, carrots and potatoes

Prep 25 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg rabbit pieces or chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 200ml ale
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 600g potatoes, chopped
  1. Brown the meat in the oil in batches.
  2. Add the onions, carrots and thyme and soften.
  3. Add the ale, stock and potatoes.
  4. Simmer covered until tender.

Sweets

Elvish lembas shortbreadVegetarian

Buttery honey shortbread cut into squares

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 350g plain flour
  • 1 pinch of salt
  1. Heat the oven to 160C and line a tray.
  2. Beat the butter, sugar and honey, then mix in the flour and salt.
  3. Press 1cm thick into the tray and score into squares.
  4. Bake.
  5. Cut while warm and wrap each piece in a clean leaf or baking paper.

Meduseld spiced honey mead punchVegetarian

Mead and apple juice warmed with orange, cinnamon, cloves and honey

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml mead
  • 500ml cloudy apple juice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Pour the mead and apple juice into a pot with the orange, cinnamon, cloves and honey.
  2. Warm gently without boiling.
  3. Ladle into mugs with an orange slice. For a booze free pot use more apple juice instead of mead.
