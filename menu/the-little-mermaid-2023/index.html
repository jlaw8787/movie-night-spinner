Snacks

Dinglehopper island fruit platterVegetarian

Mango, pineapple and papaya wedges with passionfruit and lime

Prep 19 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cut the fruit up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; add the lime juice and chilli salt just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 2 limes
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  1. Cut the mangoes, pineapple and papaya into wedges and halve the passionfruit.
  2. Mix the salt and chilli flakes.
  3. Pile the fruit on a platter, squeeze over lime juice and sprinkle with the chilli salt.

Plantain chipsVegetarian

Thin fried green plantain chips with a coriander and lime sour cream dip

Prep 18 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the coriander dip up to 1 day ahead, chilled; slice and fry the plantains just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 limes
  • 1 handful coriander
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Peel the plantains and slice them thin.
  2. Stir chopped coriander and lime juice through the sour cream for a dip.
  3. Fry at 180C in the oil in batches until crisp.
  4. Salt well and serve with the dip.

Mains

Castle dinner jerk chicken

Grilled chicken thighs in an allspice, thyme, chilli and lime paste, with rice

Prep 8 min, cook 160 min · Make ahead: Blitz the paste and marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the rice and grill the chicken on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 tbsp ground allspice
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 2 red chillies
  • 2 limes
  • 3 spring onions
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 can kidney beans
  1. Blitz the allspice, thyme, chilli, lime juice and spring onion into a paste.
  2. Marinate the chicken in the paste.
  3. Cook the rice with the drained kidney beans.
  4. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through.

Sweets

Ariel's grotto jelly

Blue jelly with mango and kiwi fish inside. Ursula keeps voices in shells.

Prep 24 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Chill the jelly until set up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; whip the cream and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets blue jelly crystals
  • 1 mango
  • 2 kiwifruit
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Cut the mango and kiwifruit into little fish shapes.
  2. Make up the jelly and pour into clear glasses with the fruit suspended inside.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Whip the cream and spoon on top like sea foam.

Sebastian's seashell coconut coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Coconut water and pineapple juice over ice with mint and lime

Prep 6 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the coconut water and pineapple juice up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice, mint and lime on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1l coconut water
  • 1l pineapple juice
  • 2 limes
  • 1 handful mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and mint leaves.
  2. Pour in equal parts coconut water and pineapple juice.
  3. Squeeze in a lime wedge, stir and serve cold.
