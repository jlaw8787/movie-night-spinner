Snacks

Watto's no Republic credits fruit platterVegetarian

Sliced pears and nectarines with honey, feta and toasted pistachios

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 pears
  • 3 nectarines
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 100g pistachios, toasted and roughly chopped
  1. Slice the pears and nectarines thinly and fan out on a big board.
  2. Drizzle with honey, then scatter with feta and pistachios. Serve straight away.

Podrace grandstand skewers

Yoghurt and cumin lamb skewers grilled hot, drizzled with lemon tahini

Prep 5 min, cook 6 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g lamb leg, cubed
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 3 tbsp water
  1. Marinate the lamb in the yoghurt, cumin, garlic and salt for an hour.
  2. Thread onto skewers and grill hot and fast, turning.
  3. Whisk the tahini with lemon juice and water until smooth and drizzle over to serve.

Mains

Shmi's harissa chicken and chickpeas

Harissa chicken thighs roasted over chickpeas and onions, flatbread, cucumber yoghurt

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp harissa
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 800g chickpeas, drained
  • 2 red onions, sliced
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, grated
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  1. Rub the chicken with harissa, lemon juice and salt.
  2. Spread the chickpeas and onions in a tray and sit the chicken on top.
  3. Roast at 200C for 40 minutes.
  4. Mix the cucumber into the yoghurt. Warm the flatbreads and serve everything together.

Sweets

Duel of the Fates red and blue tartVegetarian

Raspberry and blueberry custard tart. Red half, blue half

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 500ml thick vanilla custard
  • 125g raspberries
  • 125g blueberries
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  1. Line a tart tin with the pastry and blind bake at 180C until golden. Cool.
  2. Fill with the custard. Cover one half with raspberries and the other half with blueberries, meeting in a clean line.
  3. Brush with warm apricot jam to glaze and chill.

Podracer blue coconut milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold milk and coconut milk blended with honey and tinted blue, no alcohol

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml cold milk
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp blue food colouring
  • 1 handful ice
  1. Blend the milk, coconut milk, honey and ice until frothy.
  2. Add a few drops of blue colouring and blend until evenly pale blue. Serve icy cold.
