Flying V fried cheese bitesVegetarian

Battered mozzarella cubes fried golden with ranch for dipping.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Cubes can be coated and frozen, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g firm mozzarella
  • 1 cup self-raising flour
  • 250ml cold milk
  • salt, to taste
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 200ml ranch dressing
  1. Cut the mozzarella into 2cm cubes. Whisk the flour, milk and salt into a batter.
  2. Dip the cubes in the batter and chill briefly so they hold their shape.
  3. Fry in 5cm of hot oil for 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
  4. Drain and serve with ranch dressing.

Juicy Lucy mini burgers

Cheese-stuffed beef mini burgers with American cheese melted in the middle.

Prep 21 min · Make ahead: Shape up to 4 hours ahead, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 700g beef mince
  • 4 slices American cheese, halved
  • 8 mini burger buns
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  1. Divide the mince into 16 thin patties. Sandwich half a slice of cheese between 2 patties and pinch the edges sealed to make 8.
  2. Griddle 4 minutes a side, then rest 2 minutes so the cheese doesn't burst.
  3. Serve in the mini burger buns with pickles.

Creamy chicken wild rice soup cups

Chicken and wild rice soup, creamy with thyme, for a cold Minneapolis winter.

How it is made
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1.2L chicken stock
  • 120g wild rice blend
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 400g chicken thighs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 loaf white bread
  1. Wild rice simmered with chicken, carrot, celery and onion in a creamy thyme stock, ladled into small cups with buttered bread fingers. Soften a diced onion, a carrot and 2 celery sticks in 30g butter. Stir in 2 tbsp flour, then 1.2L chicken stock, 120g wild rice blend and thyme. Add 400g chicken thighs and simmer 40 minutes until the rice is tender. Shred the chicken back in and stir through 150ml cream. Ladle into cups and serve with fingers of buttered toast.

Minnesota hotdish

Beef, mushroom, corn and green bean casserole under crisp potato gems.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 200g green beans, chopped
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 500g frozen potato gems
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and mushrooms.
  2. Stir in the corn, beans and cream. Tip into a baking dish and cover with potato gems.
  3. Bake at 200°C for 35 minutes until golden.

Team dinner fish fry

Crisp battered fish, chips and tartare. Ducks stick together.

Prep 13 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; batter and fry the fish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml cold soda water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in thick chips
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 gherkins, chopped
  • 2 lemons
  1. Mix the mayonnaise with the capers, gherkins and a squeeze of lemon for the tartare.
  2. Toss the chips in the olive oil and salt and bake at 220°C, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Whisk 120g of the flour with the soda water to a thin batter. Dust the fish in the rest of the flour.
  4. Dip the fish in the batter and fry in hot oil at 180°C, a few at a time, until golden.
  5. Drain, salt and serve with the chips, tartare and lemon wedges.

Hans' frozen pond browniesVegetarian

Fudgy brownies covered with a smooth layer of white chocolate.

Prep 0 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 150g white chocolate
  1. Melt the dark chocolate and butter. Whisk in the sugar and eggs and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 170°C for 25 minutes. Cool.
  3. Melt the white chocolate, spread it smooth over the top like ice, set, then cut into squares.

District 5 rinkside hot chocolateNo alcohol

Thick dark hot chocolate topped with marshmallows.

Prep 7 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 100g marshmallows
  1. Heat the milk until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate until frothy.
  3. Pour into big mugs and pile marshmallows on top. Ducks fly together.
