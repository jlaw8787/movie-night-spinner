Venice cicchetti, three toppings on toast

Baguette toasts with smoked cod cream, prosciutto and fig, or artichoke and parsley.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cod spread up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g smoked cod
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 4 fresh figs, sliced
  • 1 jar artichoke hearts, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Toast the baguette slices with a little olive oil, 5 min.
  2. Poach the cod until flaking, then whip with olive oil and garlic until creamy, 10 min.
  3. Spread a third of the toasts with the cod.
  4. Top a third with prosciutto and fig, and the rest with artichoke and parsley, 5 min.

Paper cone friesVegetarian

Thick twice-fried fries in paper cones with a big spoon of mayonnaise, for the walk back.

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Cut and soak the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; do the first fry up to 2 hours ahead and crisp just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 150g mayonnaise
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips, rinse and dry well.
  2. Fry in the oil at 140°C until soft, then rest, 15 min.
  3. Fry again at 190°C until crisp, 8 min.
  4. Toss with sea salt and serve in paper cones with mayonnaise on top.

Cinnamon walnut sugar pastry twists

Yeast dough twisted, baked golden and rolled in spiced sugar and walnuts, still warm.

How it is made
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 140g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 80g butter
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 50g walnuts
  • 1 roll foil
  1. Sweet yeast dough spiralled round foil-wrapped rolling pins, baked golden and rolled in cinnamon sugar and crushed walnuts, warm and chewy. Mix 300g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 40g sugar, 1 egg, 40g soft butter and 150ml warm milk into a soft dough, knead 5 minutes and prove 1 hour. Wrap 2 rolling pins in buttered foil. Roll the dough into long 1cm ropes and coil tightly around the pins. Brush with melted butter, roll in 100g sugar mixed with 2 tsp cinnamon and 50g crushed walnuts, and bake at 200°C for 15 minutes. Slide off and cut into rings.

Water elemental seafood spaghetti

Spaghetti with prawns, mussels and clams in garlic, white wine and chilli, before the water rises.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh; scrub the mussels and purge the clams up to 4 hours ahead, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250ml white wine
  • 300g green prawns
  • 500g mussels, scrubbed
  • 500g clams, purged
  • 225g dried spaghetti
  • 3 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Soften the garlic and chilli in the olive oil.
  2. Add the wine, prawns, mussels and clams, cover and cook until the shells open, discarding any that stay shut, 10 min.
  3. Toss through the spaghetti and parsley.

Venetian fried cod and chips

Salt cod fried in a light batter, thick chips and lemon, eaten on the terrace.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 can lager
  • 600g cod or flathead fillets
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 jar tartare sauce
  • 1 lemon
  1. Cod fillets in a crisp lager batter fried golden, with thick double-cooked chips, minted mushy peas and tartare sauce. Cut 1kg potatoes into thick chips and fry at 140°C for 6 minutes, then drain. Whisk 200g self-raising flour with 300ml cold lager and salt. Dust 600g cod fillets in flour, dip in the batter and fry at 180°C for 4 minutes until crisp. Fry the chips again at 190°C for 3 minutes. Simmer 400g frozen peas for 4 minutes, mash with butter and mint. Serve with tartare and lemon.

E.D.I.T.H. tiramisuVegetarian

Coffee-dipped ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream, dusted with cocoa.

Prep 22 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 250g mascarpone
  • 200ml cream, whipped
  • 200g savoiardi biscuits
  • 300ml strong coffee, cooled
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale, then fold in the mascarpone and whipped cream.
  2. Dip the savoiardi briefly in the coffee and layer with the cream in a dish. Repeat.
  3. Chill overnight.
  4. Dust with cocoa to serve.

Ned and Betty's peach bellini mocktailNo alcohol

Peach puree topped with sparkling apple juice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Blend the peach puree up to 1 day ahead, chilled; top with chilled sparkling apple juice at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 ripe peaches, peeled and stoned
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 600ml sparkling apple juice
  1. Blend the peaches with the sugar and lemon until smooth.
  2. Spoon a few spoons of puree into each glass.
  3. Slowly top with chilled sparkling apple juice and stir gently.
