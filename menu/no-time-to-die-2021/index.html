Snacks

Paloma's Cuban party croquetas

Creamy ham béchamel croquettes, crumbed and fried crisp.

Prep 30 min, cook 4.5 hours · Make ahead: Make and chill the béchamel at least 4 hours, up to 2 days ahead; crumb up to 1 day ahead or freeze, and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 70g plain flour, for the béchamel
  • 500ml milk, hot
  • 150g ham, finely chopped
  • nutmeg, a pinch
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 50g plain flour, for dusting
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g fine dried breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Melt the butter, add the flour for the béchamel and cook for 3 minutes. Whisk in the hot milk a little at a time and cook, stirring, for 8 minutes until very thick.
  2. Stir in the ham, nutmeg, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread in a lined tray, press baking paper onto the surface and chill for at least 4 hours.
  4. With wet hands shape into 20 small logs.
  5. Roll in the flour for dusting, then the egg, then the breadcrumbs.
  6. Heat the oil to 180°C in a deep heavy pan, 8 min.
  7. Fry in batches for 2 to 3 minutes until deep golden, then drain.
  8. Serve hot.

Port Antonio festival dumplingsVegetarian

Lightly sweet Jamaican fried cornmeal dumplings, crisp outside and soft in the middle.

Prep 9 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry ingredients up to 1 day ahead; make the dough and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 40g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 200ml milk
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  2. Stir in the milk, knead for 2 minutes to a firm smooth dough and rest for 10 minutes.
  3. Shape into 12 logs the length of a finger.
  4. Heat the oil to 170°C in a deep heavy pan, 8 min.
  5. Fry in batches for about 6 minutes, turning, until deep golden and cooked through.
  6. Drain and serve warm.

Mains

Matera orecchiette with broccolini and anchovy

Ear-shaped pasta tossed with broccolini, garlic, anchovy and chilli.

Prep 0 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Toast the breadcrumbs up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; cook the pasta just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 80ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 400g dried orecchiette
  • 500g broccolini, cut into short lengths
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 6 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 40g pecorino, grated
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • salt, to taste
  1. Toast the breadcrumbs in 1 tbsp of the oil until golden and crisp. Set aside.
  2. Boil the orecchiette in well salted water for 8 minutes, adding the broccolini for the last 4.
  3. Meanwhile warm the rest of the oil with the garlic, anchovies and chilli on low heat, stirring, until the anchovies melt.
  4. Drain, keeping a cup of the pasta water. Toss the pasta and broccolini in the pan with a splash of the water, the pecorino and lemon zest until glossy.
  5. Serve topped with the toasted crumbs.

Jerk chicken with rice and peas

Jerk chicken with scotch bonnet, allspice and thyme, grilled charred, coconut rice and beans.

Prep 11 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 4 spring onions
  • 1 Scotch bonnet or red chilli
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 300g shredded cabbage
  1. Blitz the spring onion, chilli, allspice, thyme leaves, soy and lime juice and rub over the chicken.
  2. Grill or roast at 200°C until charred and cooked through.
  3. Simmer the rice with the coconut milk, beans, 250ml water and salt, covered, until tender.
  4. Serve with the shredded cabbage.

Madeleine's Norway cardamom bunsVegetarian

Cardamom buns twisted with cinnamon butter, from Madeleine's Norway house.

Prep 40 min, cook 15 min, then 100 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and warm briefly, or shape the buns and let them rise slowly in the fridge overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250ml milk, warmed
  • 2 eggs
  • 75g butter, softened, for the dough
  • 90g butter, softened, for the filling
  • 70g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp pearl sugar or demerara
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, caster sugar, 1 tsp of the cardamom, salt, warm milk, 1 egg and the butter for the dough. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  2. Cover and rise for an hour until doubled.
  3. Beat the butter for the filling with the brown sugar, cinnamon and the rest of the cardamom, 3 min.
  4. Roll the dough to 40 x 30cm, spread with the filling, fold in thirds, cut into 12 strips and twist each into a knot. Lay on lined trays.
  5. Prove for 40 minutes until puffy, heating the oven to 200°C.
  6. Brush with the second egg, beaten, scatter with the pearl sugar and bake for 15 minutes until golden.
  7. Cool for 5 minutes before serving.

Drinks

Paloma's Santiago mojito

White rum, fresh lime, mint and sugar syrup over crushed ice, topped with soda.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lime juice and chill the soda a few hours ahead; muddle and build each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 3 limes
  • 3 cups crushed ice
  • 180ml white rum
  • 400ml soda water, chilled
  1. Juice 2 of the limes and cut the third into wedges. Gently muddle 8 mint leaves with 15ml of the sugar syrup and a quarter of the lime juice in each glass.
  2. Fill with crushed ice, add 45ml of the rum to each and stir.
  3. Top with the soda, stir once and finish with a mint sprig and a lime wedge.
