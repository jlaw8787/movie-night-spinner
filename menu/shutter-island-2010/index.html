Lighthouse cheese strawsVegetarian

Puff pastry twists with parmesan, paprika and cayenne.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Cut and twist the straws up to 1 day ahead and keep them uncooked on a tray in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Brush the pastry with egg.
  2. Cover one sheet with parmesan, paprika and cayenne, lay the other on top and press.
  3. Cut into strips and twist.
  4. Bake at 200°C, 12 min.

Barefoot Rachel baked beans on toast

Navy beans baked slowly with speck and molasses, served on toast.

Prep 5 min, cook 4 hr 40 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Soak the beans the night before. Cooked beans keep 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dried navy beans
  • 150g speck, diced
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • 4 slices of sourdough
  • 30g butter
  1. Soak the beans overnight
  2. Simmer the beans, 30 min.
  3. Bake at 150°C with the speck, onion, molasses, mustard powder and enough water to cover, 4 hr.
  4. Toast the sourdough and butter it, 8 min.
  5. Spoon the beans over the toast.

Ferry crossing lobster rolls

Lobster in lemony mayo with celery and chives in a toasted split-top bun.

How it is made
  • 400g cooked lobster meat
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lemon
  • 12 small soft rolls
  • 40g butter
  • paprika
  1. Sweet lobster meat in lemony mayonnaise with celery and chives, piled into buttered, toasted small soft rolls with a pinch of paprika. Chop 400g cooked lobster meat (or cooked prawns) into chunks. Mix with 80g mayonnaise, 1 finely diced celery stick, chopped chives, the zest and juice of half a lemon, salt and pepper. Split 12 small soft rolls, brush the cut sides with 40g melted butter and toast in a hot pan until golden. Fill with the lobster, dust with paprika and serve cold with lemon wedges.

Chuck and Teddy's storm chowder

Steamed clams in fish stock with bacon, potato and celery.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the chowder with the fish stock up to the milk step, up to 1 day ahead, and chill it with the clams; reheat, then stir in the cream, clams and thyme to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg clams
  • 125ml fish stock
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  1. Steam the clams open in the fish stock, then strain the liquid, 10 min.
  2. Render the bacon, then add the onion, celery and potato.
  3. Add the clam liquid and milk and simmer, 15 min.
  4. Stir in the cream, clams and thyme.

Ashecliffe Boston cream pieVegetarian

Sponge layers filled with vanilla custard and covered in chocolate glaze.

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 7 eggs
  • 230g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk four eggs with the sugar until thick and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175°C, 20 min.
  3. Cook the milk, yolks, a little sugar and cornflour until thick, then cool, 25 min.
  4. Cool the sponges, 30 min.
  5. Fill the sponges with the custard.
  6. Melt the chocolate with the cream and pour over, 5 min.

Dr Cawley's cocoa AlexanderNo alcohol

Cocoa, cream and a grating of nutmeg, shaken cold.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Steep and cool the tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; shake with the syrup, cream and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 black tea bags
  • 150ml boiling water
  • 60ml chocolate syrup
  • 160ml thickened cream
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg, freshly grated
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 4 minutes, remove and cool.
  2. Chill four coupe glasses.
  3. For each drink, measure 30ml cold tea, 15ml chocolate syrup, 40ml thickened cream and a drop of vanilla extract into a shaker.
  4. Shake each drink with ice for 15 seconds.
  5. Strain into the coupes and grate nutmeg over the top.
