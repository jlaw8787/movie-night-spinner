Snacks

Lighthouse cheese strawsVegetarian

Puff pastry twists with parmesan, paprika and cayenne

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Cut and twist the straws up to 1 day ahead and keep them uncooked on a tray in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Brush the pastry with egg.
  2. Cover one sheet with parmesan, paprika and cayenne, lay the other on top and press.
  3. Cut into strips and twist.
  4. Bake at 200C.

Barefoot Rachel baked beans on toast

Navy beans baked slowly with speck and molasses, served on toast

Prep 20 min, cook 290 min · Make ahead: Soak the beans the night before. Cooked beans keep 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dried navy beans
  • 150g speck, diced
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • 4 slices sourdough
  • 30g butter
  1. Soak the beans overnight (not counted in the time)
  2. Simmer the beans.
  3. Bake at 150C with the speck, onion, molasses, mustard powder and enough water to cover.
  4. Toast the sourdough and butter it.
  5. Spoon the beans over the toast.

Mains

Chuck and Teddy's storm chowder

Steamed clams in white wine with bacon, potato and celery

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chowder up to the milk step up to 1 day ahead and chill it with the clams; reheat, then stir in the cream, clams and thyme to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg clams
  • 125ml white wine
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  1. Steam the clams open in the wine, then strain the liquid.
  2. Render the bacon, then add the onion, celery and potato.
  3. Add the clam liquid and milk and simmer.
  4. Stir in the cream, clams and thyme.

Sweets

Ashecliffe Boston cream pieVegetarian

Sponge layers filled with vanilla custard and covered in chocolate glaze

Prep 45 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 7 eggs
  • 230g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk four eggs with the sugar until thick and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175C.
  3. Cook the milk, yolks, a little sugar and cornflour until thick, then cool.
  4. Cool the sponges.
  5. Fill the sponges with the custard.
  6. Melt the chocolate with the cream and pour over.

Drinks

Dr Cawley's brandy AlexanderVegetarian

Brandy, crème de cacao and cream shaken cold

Prep 7 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best shaken to order; chill the coupes and measure the brandy and creme de cacao into a jug up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml brandy
  • 120ml creme de cacao
  • 120ml thickened cream
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg, freshly grated
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Chill four coupe glasses.
  2. For each drink, measure 40ml brandy, 30ml creme de cacao and 30ml thickened cream into a shaker.
  3. Shake each drink with ice for 15 seconds.
  4. Strain into the coupes and grate nutmeg over the top. Serve a strong coffee alongside for anyone who would rather not.
