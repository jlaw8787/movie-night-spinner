Snacks

Carnival corn dogs

Cocktail franks in a cornmeal buttermilk batter, fried

Prep 16 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Skewer the franks up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; make the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cocktail franks
  • 24 skewers
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, baking powder and buttermilk to a thick batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push a skewer into each.
  3. Dip in the batter and fry in the oil at 180C in batches until golden.
  4. Serve with mustard and tomato sauce.

Forky's spork salad cupsVegetarian

Corn, cherry tomato, cucumber and avocado with lime, in little cups

Prep 19 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Drain the corn, halve the tomatoes and dice the cucumber up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; dice the avocado and mix with lime and salt just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 can corn kernels, drained
  • 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 2 avocados, diced
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 sporks
  1. Drain the corn, halve the tomatoes and dice the cucumber and avocado.
  2. Mix with lime juice and salt.
  3. Spoon into little cups and stick a spork in each one.

Mains

Duke Caboom's poutine

Crisp chips under hot gravy and shredded barbecue chicken

Prep 11 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Shred the chicken and tear the mozzarella up to 1 day ahead; bake the chips, heat the gravy and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut chips
  • 500ml beef gravy
  • 400g barbecue chicken, shredded
  • 200g fresh mozzarella, torn
  1. Bake the chips at 220C until crisp.
  2. Shred the chicken and tear the mozzarella.
  3. Heat the gravy until bubbling.
  4. Pile the chips into bowls with the chicken, scatter over the mozzarella and pour the hot gravy over so the cheese goes melty.

Sweets

Fairground funnel cakeVegetarian

Looping swirls of fried batter, golden and crisp

Prep 14 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Whisk the batter up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; fry to order and dust with icing sugar just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp icing sugar
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk and sugar into a smooth batter.
  2. Heat the oil to 180C.
  3. Pipe the batter in looping swirls into the oil and fry until golden on both sides.
  4. Drain and dust thickly with icing sugar.
  5. Slice the strawberries and serve with the warm cake.

Carnival frozen strawberry lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Frozen strawberries, lemon juice and syrup blended with ice

Prep 16 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make and chill the syrup up to 1 week ahead; juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead and blend with the ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 400g frozen strawberries
  • 4 lemons
  • 250ml cold water
  • 3 cup ice
  • 4 fresh strawberries
  1. Simmer the sugar and water until dissolved.
  2. Cool the syrup.
  3. Juice the lemons.
  4. Blend the frozen strawberries, lemon juice, syrup, cold water and ice until thick and slushy. Add more syrup if it is too sharp.
  5. Pour into big cups with a strawberry on each rim and serve with straws.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the toys drive the RV back to the carnival.
