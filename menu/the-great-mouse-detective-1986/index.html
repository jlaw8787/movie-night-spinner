Snacks

Mrs Judson's cheese crumpetsVegetarian

Toasted crumpets under melted cheddar and English mustard

Prep 6 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheddar and mix with the mustard up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; toast and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 crumpets
  • 50g butter
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 tbsp English mustard
  1. Grate the cheddar and mix with the mustard.
  2. Toast the crumpets until golden and butter them.
  3. Pile the cheese onto each crumpet.
  4. Grill until bubbling and golden.

Rat Trap Scotch eggs

Soft boiled eggs wrapped in pork sausage, crumbed and fried

Prep 12 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Boil, peel and wrap the eggs up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; crumb and fry on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs, for boiling
  • 400g pork sausage mince
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten, for coating
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp English mustard
  1. Boil the four eggs, cool and peel.
  2. Boil.
  3. Cool.
  4. Wrap each egg in sausage mince.
  5. Roll in flour, beaten egg and panko.
  6. Fry in the oil at 170C until golden.
  7. Halve and serve with mustard.

Mains

Baker Street shepherd's pie

Lamb mince with carrots and peas under mash

Prep 17 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; bake from cold and add 15 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  1. Dice the onion, grate the carrots and peel the potatoes.
  2. Brown the mince with onion and carrot, add peas and a little water and simmer until thick.
  3. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter.
  4. Spread the mince in a dish and top with mash.
  5. Bake at 200C until golden.

Sweets

Victoria spongeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers with strawberry jam and whipped cream

Prep 9 min, cook 37 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and prepare two tins. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Whip the cream. Sandwich the cakes with jam and cream.
  5. Dust the top with icing sugar.

Drinks

Baker Street Earl Grey lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Earl Grey tea with honey and lemon juice, over ice

Prep 14 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; add the lemon, ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Earl Grey tea bags
  • 600ml boiling water
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 lemons
  • 300ml sparkling water
  • 1 packet shortbread fingers
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags for 5 minutes, remove and stir in the honey.
  2. Chill.
  3. Juice three lemons and slice the fourth.
  4. Add the juice to the tea. Fill four glasses with ice and lemon slices, pour in the tea two thirds full and top with sparkling water.
  5. Serve with shortbread fingers.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Mrs Judson brings in the cheese crumpets.
