Snacks

KFBR392 chilli nuts

Honey chilli rosemary nuts and jerky. Say KFBR392 till they're gone.

Prep 10 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 400g mixed raw nuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 sprig rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 150g beef jerky, sliced
  1. Toss the nuts with the honey, oil, chilli, paprika and rosemary.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170C, stirring twice, until golden.
  3. Sprinkle with sea salt while hot and cool so the coating crisps.
  4. Serve in a bowl next to the sliced jerky.

Old celery trick with blue cheese dipVegetarian

Celery, thick end first, with blue cheese dip

Prep 15 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp buttermilk
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 bunch celery
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Mash the blue cheese with a fork, then mix in the sour cream, mayonnaise, buttermilk, lemon juice and pepper, leaving some chunks.
  2. Chill the dip.
  3. Trim the celery and carrots into long sticks and keep in iced water so they stay crisp.
  4. Spoon the dip into a bowl, drizzle with hot sauce and serve with the drained sticks.

Mains

Action hero rib eye with onion rings

Grilled rib eye with garlic parsley butter, battered onion rings

Prep 25 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Make the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled. The batter, onion rings and steaks are best cooked just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml cold soda water
  • 2 large onions
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 rib eye steaks (300g each)
  • 100g rocket
  1. Mash the butter with the garlic and parsley.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder and soda water into a batter.
  3. Slice the onions into thick rings, dip in batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 180C until golden. Drain.
  4. Season the steaks and grill over high heat for medium rare.
  5. Rest the steaks, top with the garlic butter and serve with the onion rings and rocket.

Piper's human shield chicken souvlaki with tzatziki

Chicken in pita with tzatziki, vest not included

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken and make the tzatziki up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, cubed
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, grated
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 600g frozen chips
  1. Marinate the chicken in the juice of 1 lemon, the oregano, 2 garlic cloves and the oil, then thread onto skewers.
  2. Bake the chips to the packet directions.
  3. Squeeze the cucumber dry and mix with the yoghurt, the last garlic clove and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Grill the skewers until charred and cooked through.
  5. Warm the pita and fill with chicken, tzatziki, tomato, onion and a few chips.

X-5 warhead countdown chocolate potsVegetarian

Dark chocolate cream pots topped with whipped cream, sea salt, honeycomb

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 30g honeycomb, crushed
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Heat some of the cream with the sugar until just steaming. Pour over the chocolate, leave a minute, then stir until smooth and glossy.
  2. Whisk in the egg yolk and a pinch of salt. Pour into small glasses.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Whip the remaining cream and top each pot with cream, sea salt and crushed honeycomb.

Drinks

Vicki St. Elmo's debrief michelada

Beer with lime, tomato juice and hot sauce over a chilli salt rim

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the salt and chilli powder for the rim up to 1 week ahead and juice the limes up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled. Build each glass over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 3 limes
  • 240ml tomato juice
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 bottles cold lager (330ml each)
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the salt and chilli powder on a saucer. Rub a lime wedge around each glass rim and dip into the salt.
  2. Fill the glasses with ice. Add lime juice, tomato juice, a couple of dashes of hot sauce and a dash of Worcestershire to each.
  3. Top each with a bottle of cold lager, stir gently and serve with a lime wedge.
