Snacks

Rooftop mixed nutsVegetarian

A handful of nuts and pumpkin seeds roasted with soy, a little honey and sesame

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 250g mixed nuts
  • 50g pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the nuts and pumpkin seeds with the soy, honey and sesame seeds. Spread on a lined tray.
  2. Roast at 170C, stirring once.
  3. Cool on the tray so they crisp.

EdamameVegetarian

Young soybeans boiled with flaky salt and sesame oil, a quiet snack between chapters of Hagakure

Prep 5 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen edamame in pods
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  1. Boil the edamame in salted water and drain well.
  2. Toss with the sea salt and sesame oil and serve warm, with another bowl for the empty pods.

Mains

Teriyaki rice bowl

Glazed chicken on rice, eaten up on Ghost Dog's rooftop

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 80ml mirin
  • 60ml sake
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 800g chicken thighs, skin on
  • 400g short grain rice
  • 40g pickled ginger
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Cook the rice and keep warm.
  2. Simmer the soy, mirin, sake and sugar until syrupy.
  3. Sear the chicken skin side down, flip and cook through, then pour in the sauce and turn until glazed.
  4. Slice the chicken over the rice and top with pickled ginger and spring onion.

Sweets

Coconut ice creamVegetarian

Tinned coconut, sugar and thickened dairy churned smooth, toasted flakes through

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze up to 1 week ahead; scoop and finish with the rest of the coconut to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 50g shredded coconut
  • 400ml coconut cream
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 120g caster sugar
  1. Toast the coconut in a dry pan until golden.
  2. Blend the coconut cream, cream and sugar until the sugar dissolves, then stir in most of the coconut.
  3. Churn, or freeze in a tub, stirring every hour.
  4. Scoop and finish with the rest of the coconut.

Drinks

Rooftop iced matcha honey lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Whisked matcha over sweetened lemon juice and ice, lemon wheel and mint, bittersweet and green

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Lemonade up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tsp matcha powder
  • 60ml honey
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 800ml water, warm and cold
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Juice the lemons. Stir the honey into some warm water, then add the lemon juice and cold water.
  2. Whisk the matcha with hot water (about 80C) until frothy.
  3. Fill 4 glasses with ice, pour in the lemonade, float the matcha on top and add a lemon wheel and mint. Stir at the table.
