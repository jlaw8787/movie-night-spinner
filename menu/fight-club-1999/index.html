Snacks

Lou's Tavern spiced nutsVegetarian

Mixed nuts roasted with butter, brown sugar, cayenne and rosemary

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight; roast and cool, then store
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 350g mixed nuts
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Melt the butter with the brown sugar, the cayenne, the chopped rosemary and the salt.
  2. Toss through the mixed nuts, spread on a lined tray and roast at 170C for 12 minutes, stirring halfway.
  3. Cool 5 minutes so they crisp.

Chicken mushroom vol-au-vents

Puff pastry cases filled with creamy chicken and mushroom

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the chicken filling up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat gently and fill the warm cases on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g chicken breast
  • 20g butter
  • 150g button mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 16 vol-au-vent cases
  1. Fry the diced chicken breast in butter, add the sliced mushrooms, then stir in the flour and the cream and simmer until thick.
  2. Warm the vol-au-vent cases at 180C for 5 minutes, fill them and pass around on a tray.

Mains

Airline tray chicken and rice

Braised chicken thighs with onion and garlic on long grain rice

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Brown and simmer the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then cook the rice and peas fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 20g butter
  • 200g frozen peas
  1. Brown the chicken thighs, then add the diced onion and the garlic cloves.
  2. Pour in the chicken stock, cover and simmer 25 minutes.
  3. Cook the long grain rice, stir through the butter and the peas.
  4. Plate in tidy single portions, like the airline trays.

Sweets

Support group chocolate potsVegetarian

Rich dark chocolate cream set in small cups

Prep 10 min, cook 185 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 200g dark chocolate
  1. Heat the cream with the sugar until steaming.
  2. Pour over the chopped dark chocolate and stir until smooth.
  3. Divide between the small cups and chill for 3 hours until set.

Drinks

Lou's Tavern boilermakerVegetarian

Cold lager with a bourbon shot on the side, salted peanuts

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Chill the cans and freeze the pint glasses up to 1 day ahead; pour and serve on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 375ml cans lager
  • 120ml bourbon
  • 200g salted peanuts
  1. Chill the cans of lager until icy and the pint glasses in the freezer for 30 minutes.
  2. Pour the lager into the frosted glasses.
  3. Pour the bourbon into shot glasses.
  4. Drink the shot and chase it with the beer, or drop the shot glass carefully into the beer.
  5. Serve with a bowl of salted peanuts.
  6. Ginger beer and a squeeze of lime for anyone not drinking.
