Lou's Tavern spiced nutsVegetarian

Mixed nuts roasted with butter, brown sugar, cayenne and rosemary.

Prep 4 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight; roast and cool, then store
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 350g mixed nuts
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Melt the butter with the brown sugar, the cayenne, the chopped rosemary and the salt, 4 min.
  2. Toss through the mixed nuts, spread on a lined tray and roast at 170°C for 12 minutes, stirring halfway.
  3. Cool 5 minutes so they crisp.

Chicken mushroom vol-au-vents

Puff pastry cases filled with creamy chicken and mushroom.

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the chicken filling up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat gently and fill the warm cases on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g chicken breast
  • 20g butter
  • 150g button mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 16 vol-au-vent cases
  1. Fry the diced chicken breast in butter, add the sliced mushrooms, then stir in the flour and the cream and simmer until thick, 18 min.
  2. Warm the vol-au-vent cases at 180°C for 5 minutes, fill them and pass around on a tray.

Airline tray chicken and rice

Braised chicken thighs with onion and garlic on long grain rice.

Prep 10 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Brown and simmer the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then cook the rice and peas fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 20g butter
  • 200g frozen peas
  1. Brown the chicken thighs, then add the diced onion and the garlic cloves, 7 min.
  2. Pour in the chicken stock, cover and simmer 25 minutes.
  3. Cook the long grain rice, stir through the butter and the peas, 12 min.
  4. Plate in tidy single portions, like the airline trays.

Paper Street fried egg and bacon rolls

Lacy eggs and thick rashers in soft rolls, cheddar and chilli mayo, for the space monkeys.

How it is made
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • hot sauce
  1. Crispy-edged fried eggs and thick bacon in toasted soft rolls with melted cheddar, hot sauce and a smear of chilli mayo. Fry 8 bacon rashers in a large pan until crisp. Remove and fry 4 eggs in the fat over high heat until the edges are lacy and brown but the yolks are runny. Lay a slice of cheddar on each egg for the last 30 seconds. Split and toast 4 soft rolls. Mix 3 tbsp mayo with 1 tsp hot sauce and spread on the rolls. Fill with bacon and the egg, and add more hot sauce to taste.

Support group chocolate potsVegetarian

Rich dark chocolate cream set in small cups.

Prep 3 min, cook 3 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 200g dark chocolate
  1. Heat the cream with the sugar until steaming, 3 min.
  2. Pour over the chopped dark chocolate and stir until smooth.
  3. Divide between the small cups and chill for 3 hours until set.

Support group shortbread with coffee glaze

Nutty browned butter fingers baked pale and crumbly, strong coffee icing, next to the urn.

How it is made
  • 200g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 250g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 120g icing sugar
  • espresso coffee
  1. Brown butter shortbread fingers baked pale and crumbly, drizzled with a strong coffee icing, set out next to the urn on a folding table. Melt 200g butter and cook until nutty brown, then chill until firm again. Heat the oven to 160°C. Beat the butter with 100g caster sugar, then mix in 250g plain flour, 50g cornflour and a pinch of salt. Press into a lined 20 x 30cm tin, prick and bake 35 minutes until pale gold. Cut into fingers while warm. Mix 120g icing sugar with 2 tbsp strong cold espresso and drizzle over once cool.

Lou's Tavern boilermaker

Cold lager with a bourbon shot on the side, salted peanuts.

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the cans and freeze the pint glasses up to 1 day ahead; pour and serve on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 375ml cans of lager
  • 120ml bourbon
  • 200g salted peanuts
  1. Chill the cans of lager until icy and the pint glasses in the freezer for 30 minutes.
  2. Pour the lager into the frosted glasses.
  3. Pour the bourbon into shot glasses.
  4. Drink the shot and chase it with the beer, or drop the shot glass carefully into the beer.
  5. Serve with a bowl of salted peanuts.
  6. Ginger beer and a squeeze of lime for anyone not drinking.
