Snacks

Alphabet crackersVegetarian

Cheddar crackers cut into letters, so dinner can spell out phone home.

Prep 18 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 60g cold butter, cubed
  • 100g plain flour
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp cold water
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Pulse the cheddar, butter, flour and salt until it looks like crumbs.
  2. Add a splash of water and pulse until it forms a dough.
  3. Roll thin and cut into letters with small cutters.
  4. Bake on lined trays until crisp and golden.
  5. Cool on the trays so they crisp up.

Halloween caramel popcorn and candy mixVegetarian

Popped corn baked in a brown sugar and golden syrup glaze, chocolate buttons, jellies and peanuts.

Prep 7 min, cook 61 min · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 100g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 150g chocolate buttons
  • 150g jelly lollies
  • 80g roasted peanuts
  1. Heat the oven to 120°C. Pop the corn and tip it into a large roasting tray.
  2. Melt the butter with the brown sugar and golden syrup, boil for 4 minutes, then stir in the bicarb and a pinch of salt.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss well.
  4. Bake for 45 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes.
  5. Cool, break up and toss with the chocolate buttons, jelly lollies and peanuts.

Mains

Game night pepperoni pizza

Pepperoni pizza in squares, as Michael's friends wait on the pizza man.

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Stretch the bases and keep them covered in the fridge up to 4 hours; top and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 jar pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  1. Heat the oven to its highest setting with a steel or tray inside.
  2. Stretch the dough into two thin bases.
  3. Spread with pizza sauce and top with the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  4. Bake until the crust is blistered and the cheese is bubbling.
  5. Cut into squares for the whole family.

Toasted ham and cheese triangles

Leg ham, Swiss and mustard butter pan-fried, apple slaw, for when the frogs go free.

How it is made
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp mild mustard
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 200g leg ham
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 2 apples
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • 1 lemon
  1. Leg ham, Swiss cheese and a smear of mustard butter toasted in a pan until golden and melting, cut into triangles with crunchy apple slaw. Mash 60g soft butter with 1 tsp mild mustard. Butter the outsides of 8 slices of white bread. Fill each pair with 2 slices leg ham and 2 slices Swiss cheese. Toast in a pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side until golden and melted, pressing down with a spatula. Toss 2 grated apples and 1 grated carrot with 2 tbsp mayo and lemon juice. Cut the toasties into triangles and serve with the slaw.

Peanut butter candy trailVegetarian

Peanut butter discs in candy melts, like Reese's Pieces.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 200g peanut butter
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 30g butter, soft
  • 250g coloured candy melts
  1. Beat the peanut butter, icing sugar and soft butter until smooth.
  2. Roll into small discs and chill until firm.
  3. Melt the candy melts gently, dip each disc and set on baking paper.
  4. Lay them in a trail across the table.

Homemade peanut butter cupsVegetarian

Milk chocolate and peanut butter cups.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g milk chocolate
  • 150g peanut butter
  • 50g icing sugar
  1. Melt the chocolate gently.
  2. Spoon a little into 12 cupcake liners, spreading up the sides, and chill until set.
  3. Mix the peanut butter with the icing sugar and put a spoon in each cup.
  4. Cover with the rest of the chocolate and chill until set.

Drinks

Fridge raid root beer floatsNo alcohol

Root beer, vanilla ice cream, chocolate.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses first, assemble at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L root beer
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp chocolate syrup
  1. Chill four tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Add two scoops of ice cream to each glass.
  3. Pour the cold root beer slowly down the side so the foam rises without spilling over.
  4. Drizzle with chocolate syrup and serve with long spoons and straws.
