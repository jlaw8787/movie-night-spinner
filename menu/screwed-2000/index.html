Limo back seat prosciutto grissini

Prosciutto wrapped breadsticks with warm herbed olives and cheddar

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Warm the olives with the orange zest, garlic and rosemary up to 3 days ahead and keep in the fridge; wrap the grissini on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 12 grissini breadsticks
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 200g mixed olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 orange
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 150g vintage cheddar, cubed
  1. Wrap each breadstick with prosciutto, spiralling it around the top half.
  2. Warm the olives in a small pan with the oil, orange zest, garlic and rosemary, then cool slightly.
  3. Cube the cheddar.
  4. Stand the grissini in a tall glass and serve with the olives and cheese.

Rusty's diner gravy fries

Chicken salted chips with onion gravy

Prep 5 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 3 days ahead in the fridge and reheat; bake the chips just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 30g butter
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C, turning once, and toss with chicken salt while hot.
  2. Melt the butter in a small pan and cook the onion.
  3. Stir in the flour, then whisk in the stock and Worcestershire and simmer.
  4. Strain into a jug. Serve the chips in a big basket with the gravy for pouring.

Ransom night meat lovers pizza

Pepperoni, ham, bacon and Italian sausage pizza on barbecue sauce

Prep 15 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Mix the pizza and barbecue sauces up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; stretch, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750g pizza dough
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 100ml pizza sauce
  • 3 tbsp barbecue sauce
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 100g sliced pepperoni
  • 100g leg ham, chopped
  • 3 rashers bacon, chopped
  • 2 Italian sausages
  1. Stretch the dough into two rounds on trays brushed with the olive oil.
  2. Mix the pizza sauce with the barbecue sauce and spread over the bases. Scatter with mozzarella.
  3. Top with pepperoni, ham, bacon and sausage squeezed from the skins in small pieces.
  4. Bake at 240°C until the crust is golden and the meat is crisp.

Rusty's Chicken Hole fried chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken with a peppery crust, creamy slaw and hot sauce

Prep 23 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk overnight and shred the slaw veg up to 1 day ahead; dress the slaw and fry the chicken on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken drumsticks and thighs
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tsp hot sauce
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/4 red cabbage
  • 1/4 green cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 100g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with the hot sauce and a good pinch of salt for at least 4 hours.
  2. Mix the flour with the paprika, garlic powder, cayenne, salt and pepper.
  3. Lift the chicken from the buttermilk and dredge in the flour, pressing to build a shaggy crust.
  4. Heat the oil to 160°C and fry the chicken in batches for 12 to 14 minutes, turning, until cooked through. Drain on a rack.
  5. Shred the cabbages and carrot and toss with the mayonnaise, vinegar and a pinch of salt. Serve with the chicken and extra hot sauce.

Cuff link cookiesVegetarian

Chunky chocolate chip cookies baked soft

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Roll the dough into balls up to 2 days ahead in the fridge, or freeze them; flatten and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 220g self-raising flour
  • 150g chocolate chips
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the egg, then mix in the flour and chocolate chips.
  2. Roll into balls and flatten slightly on lined trays.
  3. Bake at 180°C until golden at the edges.

Willard's cookie milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream, milk and crushed chocolate chip cookies blended thick

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead and crush the cookies; blend with the milk and ice cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold full cream milk
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 12 chocolate chip cookies
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Chill tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Crush most of the cookies and blend with the milk and ice cream until thick but still a little chunky.
  3. Pour into the glasses, top with whipped cream and crushed cookie.
  4. Sit a whole cookie on the rim of each glass.

When to serve
