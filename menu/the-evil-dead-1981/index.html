Cheryl's card trick cheese strawsVegetarian

Puff pastry cheese straws twisted with sharp cheddar.

Prep 15 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; crisp in a hot oven for 3 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 100g vintage cheddar, finely grated
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line two trays.
  2. Brush the pastry with egg, scatter the cheddar, parmesan and paprika over half of each sheet, fold over and press down.
  3. Cut into 1.5cm strips, twist each one, lay on the trays and sprinkle with the salt.
  4. Bake until puffed and deep golden.
  5. Cool on the trays so they crisp.

Tennessee backwoods pimento cheeseVegetarian

Cheddar and capsicum spread on crackers, packed before the bridge goes out.

Prep 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 4 days ahead in the fridge, it gets better; cut the celery on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g cream cheese, soft
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 120g roasted red capsicum, drained and chopped
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 200g crackers
  • 4 celery sticks, cut into batons
  1. Beat the cream cheese and mayonnaise until smooth.
  2. Fold in the cheddar, capsicum, cayenne and lemon juice.
  3. Cover and chill to firm up.
  4. Serve in a bowl with the crackers and celery batons.

Cellar trapdoor beef and bean stew

Beef chuck slow-braised with smoky bacon, white beans, carrot and thyme, with crusty bread.

Prep 25 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead, the flavour deepens; reheat gently with a splash of water if it's thick
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 900g beef chuck, cut into 3cm pieces
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery sticks, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 400g can cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Fry the bacon in the oil in a heavy pot until crisp, then lift out.
  2. Toss 900g beef in the flour and brown in batches in the bacon fat.
  3. Soften the onion, carrots, celery and garlic in the same pot.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, then add 750ml beef stock, the thyme, bay, salt, pepper, bacon and beef. Cover and simmer on low.
  5. Add the beans and simmer uncovered until thick.
  6. Serve in bowls with torn crusty bread.

Woodstove pan cornbreadVegetarian

Cast iron cornbread with honey butter.

Prep 3 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and warm through wrapped in foil; whip the honey butter up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 110g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Put 30g of the butter in a cast iron pan and heat it in the oven at 220°C.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, sugar and a pinch of salt, then stir in the eggs and buttermilk.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake until risen and golden.
  4. Beat the rest of the butter with the honey and serve with the cornbread warm.

Naturom Demonto devil's food cakeVegetarian

Dark cocoa cake with fudge icing, too good to throw on the fire.

Prep 18 min, cook 55 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the layers up to 2 days ahead and keep wrapped; ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 50g cocoa powder
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250ml hot strong black coffee
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 30g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and grease and line two 20cm cake tins.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa, sugar, bicarb and salt in a big bowl.
  3. Whisk the eggs, buttermilk, oil and vanilla, stir into the dry mix, then whisk in the hot coffee to a thin batter.
  4. Divide between the tins and bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool completely.
  6. Heat the cream until steaming, pour over the chocolate and butter and stir until glossy, then cool until thick enough to spread.
  7. Sandwich the layers and cover the cake with the icing.

Drinks

Knowby cabin hot toddy

Whisky, honey and lemon, hot in a mug by the cabin fire.

Prep 4 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Stud the lemon slices with cloves up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; pour the toddies to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml whisky
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 lemon, sliced into 4 rounds
  • 12 whole cloves
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 600ml boiling water
  1. Push 3 cloves into each lemon round.
  2. Divide the honey, lemon juice and whisky between 4 heatproof mugs.
  3. Top with the boiling water, stir, add a studded lemon round and a cinnamon stick and leave to steep for a few minutes.
