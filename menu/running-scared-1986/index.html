Snacks

Chicago hot dogs

All-beef franks dragged through the garden on poppy seed buns, for the stakeout at the cargo ship.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Dice the onion and slice the tomato up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; heat the franks and fill the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 small all-beef franks
  • 4 small poppy seed hot dog buns
  • 20g yellow mustard
  • 30g sweet relish
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 4 pickle spears
  • 8 sport peppers
  • 1/4 tsp celery salt
  1. Dice the onion and slice the tomato.
  2. Heat the franks in simmering water until piping hot, 5 min.
  3. Warm the buns in a steamer.
  4. Fill the buns with a frank, mustard, relish, onion, tomato, a pickle spear and sport peppers, then finish with celery salt.

Key West shrimp fritters

Chopped shrimp in a bell pepper, onion and celery batter, fried in spoonfuls, lime mayo.

Prep 14 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Chop the prawns and vegetables and mix the lime mayonnaise up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; make the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g peeled prawns
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml milk
  • 1/2 red capsicum
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 lime
  1. Finely chop the prawns, capsicum, onion and celery.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder, egg and milk to a thick batter, then fold in the prawns and vegetables.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C, 5 min.
  4. Fry spoonfuls until golden, in batches, 12 min.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise with lime juice and serve.

Best of the worst Italian beef

Rare top round shaved thin, warmed in garlic jus, piled in dipped rolls with hot giardiniera.

Prep 16 min, cook 50 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Roast, rest and shave the beef up to 2 days ahead; keep it and the jus apart in the fridge, then warm together and fill the rolls to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef topside
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long rolls
  • 100g hot giardiniera
  1. Season the beef.
  2. Roast at 200°C until 52°C at the centre, 40 min.
  3. Rest the beef, 20 min.
  4. Simmer the stock with crushed garlic and oregano into a jus, 10 min.
  5. Shave the beef very thin.
  6. Warm the beef gently in the jus.
  7. Pile into the rolls, dip briefly in the jus and top with giardiniera.

Grilled snapper tacos

Fish grilled with lime and chilli in charred corn tortillas, cabbage, mango salsa and sour cream.

How it is made
  • 700g snapper fillets
  • 3 limes
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 mango
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 150g sour cream
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • quarter red cabbage
  1. Snapper strips grilled with lime and chilli in charred corn tortillas, with crunchy cabbage, sweet mango salsa and a cool lime sour cream. Toss 700g snapper fillets with the zest and juice of 1 lime, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, salt and 2 tbsp oil. Grill on a hot plate for 2 to 3 minutes a side, then flake into chunks. Dice 1 mango, half a red onion, 1 red chilli and coriander and dress with lime. Mix 150g sour cream with lime zest and juice. Char 12 corn tortillas and fill with shredded cabbage, fish, salsa and sour cream.

Lime pieVegetarian

Key lime custard in a crumb crust, chilled hard and sliced thick.

Prep 21 min, cook 15 min, 4 hr 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake, cool and chill the pie up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; whip the cream and spoon over just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g digestive biscuits
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 400g sweetened condensed milk (1 tin)
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 120ml lime juice
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 20cm pie dish.
  2. Whisk the condensed milk, yolks and lime juice, then pour into the crust.
  3. Bake at 160°C until just set, 15 min.
  4. Cool, 30 min.
  5. Chill, 4 hr.
  6. Softly whip the cream and spoon over the pie.

Drinks

Dark rum punch

Rum with pineapple, orange and lime juice, a dash of grenadine over ice, grated nutmeg.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the rum, juices and grenadine in a jug up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; pour over ice and grate the nutmeg to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g ice
  • 180ml golden rum
  • 120ml pineapple juice
  • 120ml orange juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 20ml grenadine
  • nutmeg, grated, to taste
  1. Fill four glasses with ice.
  2. Add rum, pineapple juice, orange juice, lime juice and grenadine to each.
  3. Stir and finish with a light grating of nutmeg.
