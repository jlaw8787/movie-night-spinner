Covey kitchen kimchi pancakesVegetarian

Crisp kimchi and spring onion pancakes with a soy and sesame dipping sauce.

Prep 7 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g vegetarian kimchi, chopped
  • 3 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp rice flour
  • 200ml cold water
  • 4 spring onions, cut in lengths
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  1. Mix the flours, cold water, kimchi and kimchi juice into a thick batter, then stir in the spring onions.
  2. Heat half the oil in a large frying pan and spread in half the batter thinly.
  3. Fry 4 minutes until crisp underneath, flip and cook 3 minutes more. Repeat.
  4. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar and sesame seeds for dipping.
  5. Cut the pancakes into wedges and serve hot.

Kitty's honey butter popcorn

Popcorn in a sweet and salty honey butter glaze, for the sister who started it all.

How it is made
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • sea salt flakes
  1. Freshly popped corn tossed in a sweet and salty Korean-style honey butter glaze, baked until crunchy and finished with flaky salt. Heat the oven to 150°C. Pop 100g popcorn kernels in 2 tbsp oil in a large covered pot over medium heat, shaking often, and tip into a big bowl. Melt 60g butter with 3 tbsp honey and 2 tbsp brown sugar and bubble for 1 minute. Pour over the popcorn, add half a tsp salt and toss well. Spread on lined trays and bake 10 minutes, stirring once. Cool, break up and sprinkle with flaky salt.

Dr Covey's Korean marinated beef

Soy, pear and garlic beef seared hot, jasmine rice, quick cucumber kimchi.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, sear fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef rump
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 pear, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 300g jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  1. Slice the beef thin and marinate in soy, grated pear, crushed garlic and sesame oil for an hour.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Toss sliced cucumbers with salt, chilli and vinegar as a quick kimchi.
  4. Sear the beef hot in batches.
  5. Serve with the rice and cucumbers.

Fake date diner cheeseburgers

Smashed double cheeseburgers with pickles and sauce.

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 brioche buns
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • tomato sauce
  • mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 750g frozen shoestring fries
  1. Smashed beef patties with double cheddar, pickles, lettuce and a tangy burger sauce in toasted brioche buns, with crunchy shoestring fries. Roll 600g beef mince into 8 balls. Mix 4 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tbsp mustard and 1 tbsp chopped pickles for the sauce. Bake 750g frozen shoestring fries at 220°C for 20 minutes. Heat a heavy pan very hot, smash the balls flat and cook 2 minutes, flip, add a slice of cheddar to each and cook 1 minute. Toast 4 brioche buns, spread with sauce and stack 2 patties each with lettuce and pickles.

Korean yoghurt drink popsiclesVegetarian

Frozen yoghurt and honey pops swirled with strawberry purée.

Prep 20 min, then 6 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze for at least 6 hours; keeps up to 2 weeks frozen, run under warm water to release
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain yoghurt
  • 200ml milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 150g strawberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 8 ice block moulds and sticks
  1. Blend the yoghurt with the milk, honey and lemon juice until smooth and pourable.
  2. Blitz the strawberries with the sugar.
  3. Half fill the moulds with the yoghurt, add a spoonful of strawberry puree to each, then top up and swirl with a skewer.
  4. Insert sticks and freeze for at least 6 hours.
  5. Run the moulds under warm water to release.

Peppermint choc dipped marshmallows

Marshmallows dipped in dark chocolate and rolled in crushed peppermint.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 20 marshmallows
  • 50g peppermint lollies
  1. Melt the chocolate.
  2. Dip the marshmallows halfway and roll the chocolate end in crushed peppermint lollies.
  3. Set on baking paper.

Hatbox love letter choc chip cookiesVegetarian

Nutty brown butter cookies with dark and milk chocolate, flaky salt.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days chilled, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 170g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 100g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan and cook until nutty and golden brown.
  2. Cool.
  3. Whisk with the sugars, egg, yolk and vanilla. Stir in the flour, bicarb and salt, then the chocolate.
  4. Chill the dough, which is the resting time.
  5. Heat the oven to 180°C, roll into 16 balls and bake.
  6. Sprinkle with flaky salt and cool on the tray.

Covey house strawberry milkNo alcohol

Mashed sugared strawberries stirred into cold milk over ice.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g strawberries
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Mash the strawberries with the sugar.
  2. Stand until juicy.
  3. Spoon into 4 glasses, add ice and top with cold milk. Stir before drinking.
