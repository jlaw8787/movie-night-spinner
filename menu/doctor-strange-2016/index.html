Snacks

Kathmandu momos

Kamar-Taj is hidden somewhere in Kathmandu. Chicken and ginger dumplings, tomato achar

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken mince
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  1. Mix the chicken with the spring onions, ginger and cumin.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam in batches until cooked through.
  4. Roast the tomatoes, then blitz with the chilli and sesame seeds into an achar to serve alongside.

Mirror Dimension pesto parmesan pinwheelsVegetarian

Folded like New York in the Mirror Dimension. Pesto and parmesan puff pastry spirals

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 3 tbsp basil pesto
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Spread the pastry with pesto and parmesan.
  2. Roll up, slice into spirals and brush with egg.
  3. Bake at 200C until golden.

Mains

Kamar-Taj dal bhatVegetarian

The Ancient One shows Strange the astral plane first. Red lentil dal, rice, spinach

Prep 13 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 300g red lentils
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1l water
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 200g spinach
  1. Rinse the lentils and put in a pot with the turmeric, garlic, ginger and water.
  2. Simmer until soft and thick.
  3. Cook the rice.
  4. Fry the cumin seeds in the ghee and pour over the dal.
  5. Wilt the spinach with a little garlic and serve with the dal and rice.

Sweets

Time loop cinnamon scrollsVegetarian

Brown sugar cinnamon scrolls. Dormammu, I've come to bargain

Prep 25 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh for best results
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 50g butter, melted, for the dough
  • 60g butter, softened, for the filling
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, warm milk and melted butter into a dough and knead briefly.
  2. Cover and prove until doubled.
  3. Roll out and spread with softened butter, brown sugar and cinnamon, then roll up and cut into twelve.
  4. Bake at 190C until golden.
  5. Glaze with icing sugar mixed with milk. Dormammu, we've come to bargain.

Drinks

Masala chaiVegetarianNo alcohol

Ginger, cardamom and cinnamon milk tea. Just tea.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh and served hot; slice the ginger and crush the cardamom up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml water
  • 1 piece ginger, sliced
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp raw sugar
  1. Simmer the tea bags in the water with the ginger, cardamom and cinnamon.
  2. Add the milk and sugar and bring back to a simmer.
  3. Strain into cups.
