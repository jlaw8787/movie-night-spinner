Snacks

Kathmandu momos

Chicken and ginger dumplings, tomato achar. Kamar-Taj is hidden somewhere in Kathmandu.

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken mince
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  1. Mix the chicken with the spring onions, ginger and cumin.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam in batches until cooked through.
  4. Roast the tomatoes, then blitz with the chilli and sesame seeds into an achar to serve alongside.

Mirror Dimension pesto parmesan pinwheelsVegetarian

Pesto and parmesan puff pastry spirals. Folded like New York in the Mirror Dimension.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 3 tbsp basil pesto
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Spread the pastry with pesto and parmesan.
  2. Roll up, slice into spirals and brush with egg.
  3. Bake at 200°C until golden.

Wong's chicken and avocado sandwiches

White bread, shredded roast chicken, herbed mayo, avocado and cucumber, crusts off, for library duty.

How it is made
  • 1 barbecue chicken
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  1. Soft white bread layered with shredded roast chicken, herbed mayo, smashed avocado and cucumber, crusts off, cut into neat fingers. Shred 400g roast chicken meat. Mix 4 tbsp mayo with chopped chives, parsley, a squeeze of lemon and salt, then fold through the chicken. Smash 2 avocados with lemon juice and salt. Spread 6 slices of white bread with avocado, top with the chicken and thin cucumber slices, and close with 6 more slices. Trim the crusts and cut each into 3 fingers.

Mains

Kamar-Taj dal bhatVegetarian

Red lentil dal, rice, spinach. The Ancient One shows Strange the astral plane first.

Prep 8 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 300g red lentils
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 L water
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 200g spinach
  1. Rinse the lentils and put in a pot with the turmeric, garlic, ginger and water.
  2. Simmer until soft and thick.
  3. Cook the rice.
  4. Fry the cumin seeds in the ghee and pour over the dal.
  5. Wilt the spinach with a little garlic and serve with the dal and rice.

Strange's butter-basted rib eye

Seared hard with garlic and thyme, wine sauce and gratin, from his life before the crash.

How it is made
  • 2 x 500g rib eye steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml cream
  • 1 head garlic
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bottle red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  1. Thick rib eye seared hard and basted with garlic thyme butter, sliced with a red wine sauce, potato gratin and charred asparagus. Bake 1kg thinly sliced potatoes layered with 300ml cream, garlic and salt at 180°C for 1 hour. Season 2 x 500g rib eyes and sear in a very hot pan for 4 minutes a side. Add 60g butter, 2 crushed garlic cloves and thyme, and spoon the foaming butter over for 2 minutes. Rest 10 minutes. Reduce 250ml red wine and 250ml stock in the pan by half. Grill 2 bunches asparagus.

Sweets

Time loop cinnamon scrollsVegetarian

Soft brown sugar cinnamon scrolls with a vanilla glaze.

Prep 30 min, cook 37 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh for best results
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 50g butter, melted, for the dough
  • 60g butter, softened, for the filling
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, warm milk and melted butter into a dough and knead briefly.
  2. Cover and prove until doubled.
  3. Roll out and spread with softened butter, brown sugar and cinnamon, then roll up and cut into twelve.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  5. Glaze with icing sugar mixed with milk. Dormammu, we've come to bargain.

Drinks

Masala chaiNo alcohol

Ginger, cardamom and cinnamon milk tea. Just tea.

Prep 0 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh and served hot; slice the ginger and crush the cardamom up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml water
  • 1 piece ginger, sliced
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp raw sugar
  1. Simmer the tea bags in the water with the ginger, cardamom and cinnamon.
  2. Add the milk and sugar and bring back to a simmer.
  3. Strain into cups.
