Snacks

Village bagels and lox

Toasted with cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and red onion, for the beard and the beads

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bagels
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Split and toast the bagels.
  2. Spread each half thickly with cream cheese, then top with smoked salmon, capers, onion and small sprigs of dill. Cut each half in two to share.

Deli pastrami on rye

Hot cured beef sliced thin and piled high with brown mustard, a pickle on the side

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sliced pastrami
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 4 tbsp deli mustard
  • 4 dill pickles
  1. Steam the pastrami in a covered colander over simmering water until hot and soft.
  2. Spread the rye with mustard, pile the pastrami high on half the slices and close. Cut in half and serve with a pickle each.

Squad room maple-glazed doughnutsOn the printed menu

Yeast rings dipped in brown butter and maple glaze, finished with flaky salt

How it is made
  • 8 plain yeast doughnuts
  • 40g butter
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 60ml maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • flaky sea salt
  1. Bakery yeast doughnuts warmed and dipped in a maple and brown butter glaze, finished with a pinch of flaky salt. Brown 40g butter in a small pan until nutty, about 3 minutes. Whisk in 150g icing sugar, 60ml maple syrup and 1 tbsp milk until smooth and pourable. Warm 8 plain yeast doughnuts at 160°C for 2 minutes. Dip the tops into the glaze, set on a rack for 10 minutes and sprinkle with a few flakes of salt.

Mains

Deli hero, paid for

Steamed pastrami in a long roll with Swiss, coleslaw and Russian dressing

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Dressing and slaw up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sliced pastrami
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 1 dill pickle, chopped
  • 250g coleslaw mix
  • 4 long rolls
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  1. Steam the pastrami until glossy.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, horseradish and pickle for Russian dressing.
  3. Toss the coleslaw mix with half the dressing.
  4. Fill the rolls with pastrami, Swiss cheese, slaw and the rest of the dressing.

Sweets

Black and white cookiesVegetarian

Soft vanilla rounds iced half white fondant, half chocolate, like a precinct split down the middle

Prep 15 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 115g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 80g sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the egg, sour cream and vanilla, then mix in the flour and baking powder.
  2. Scoop 12 mounds onto lined trays.
  3. Bake at 180°C until set, then cool completely.
  4. Make a thick icing with the icing sugar and a little hot water. Ice half of each flat side, then stir the cocoa into the rest and ice the other half.

Drinks

Village vanilla iced coffee, paid forVegetarianNo alcohol

Iced coffee with vanilla syrup and milk over ice, a scoop of ice cream on top

Prep 12 min, cook 3 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Iced coffee keeps 3 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 80g coarse ground coffee
  • 800ml cold water
  • 50g sugar
  • 50ml water, for syrup
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 350ml milk
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Stir the coffee into the cold water the night before and refrigerate.
  2. Steep for 12 hours, then strain through a paper filter.
  3. Simmer the sugar with the syrup water and vanilla and cool.
  4. Fill 4 glasses with ice and add coffee, syrup and milk to each. Top with a small scoop of ice cream.
