Snacks

Iceberg Lounge salmon rye and spiced olives

Smoked salmon on rye with crème fraîche, olives warmed with orange peel.

Prep 2 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Warm the olives with the peel and chilli up to 3 days ahead, kept chilled; top the rye just before serving so it stays firm
Recipe for 4
  • 4 slices rye bread
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 300g green olives
  • 1 orange, peel only
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Cut the rye into small squares, spread with crème fraîche and top each with a fold of smoked salmon.
  2. Warm the olives in a pan with strips of orange peel and chilli flakes.
  3. Serve both on a dark board.

Riddler's question mark pretzelsVegetarian

Soft pretzels shaped as question marks, dipped in bicarb water.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fresh, up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1/4 tsp sugar
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp rock salt
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and warm water into a dough.
  2. Rest until doubled.
  3. Roll into ropes and shape into question marks.
  4. Dip in boiling water with bicarb soda, sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C. Serve warm with the yellow mustard for dipping.

Mains

Rainy Gotham beef stew

Beef chuck stewed with onions, stout and thyme.

Prep 20 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 375ml stout
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  1. Brown the beef in batches.
  2. Add the onions and soften, then pour in the stout and enough water to cover.
  3. Add the thyme, cover and simmer gently until tender.
  4. Boil the potatoes, mash with plenty of butter and serve the stew on top.

Alfred's lemon herb roast chicken

Whole bird with garlic, roasted golden, crisp potatoes and pan gravy, put in front of Bruce.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Whole chicken roasted with lemon, garlic and herbs until golden, with crisp roast potatoes and a pan gravy, the meal Alfred puts in front of Bruce. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with 40g butter, salt, thyme and rosemary, put a halved lemon and 4 garlic cloves inside and roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potato chunks for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in oil for 50 minutes. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the pan juices, add 300ml chicken stock and simmer until thick.

Sweets

Flood night chocolate potsVegetarian

Dark chocolate cream pots as Gotham floods.

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml cream, for the pots
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 300ml cream, whipped, to serve
  • 100g honeycomb, crushed
  1. Heat the cream for the pots until steaming and pour over the chocolate.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar and stir through until glossy.
  3. Pour into small glasses.
  4. Chill for 4 hours.
  5. Top with whipped cream and crushed honeycomb just before serving.

Drinks

Batmobile chase espresso martini

Vodka, coffee liqueur and fresh espresso shaken cold.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the coffee up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake each drink to order with ice
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 160ml strong coffee (4 espresso shots)
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the coffee and cool, and chill the martini glasses.
  2. For each drink, measure 40ml vodka, 20ml coffee liqueur, 40ml strong coffee and 10ml sugar syrup into a shaker.
  3. Shake each drink very hard with ice for 15 seconds.
  4. Strain into a glass and float three coffee beans on top.
