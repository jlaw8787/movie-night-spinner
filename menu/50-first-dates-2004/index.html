Snacks

Hukilau Café waffle housesVegetarian

Crisp buttermilk waffles cut into squares and stacked.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day, cook waffles fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 60g butter, melted, for the batter
  • 40g butter, for the iron and serving
  • 100ml maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, eggs, buttermilk and melted butter into a batter.
  2. Cook in a hot waffle iron until golden, in batches, 20 min.
  3. Cut into squares and stack into little houses, then drizzle with maple syrup.

Hawaiian poke cups

Cubed tuna in soy and sesame over sushi rice in small cups.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh, keep chilled up to 2 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sashimi tuna
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions
  • 250g sushi rice, uncooked
  1. Rinse and cook the sushi rice, then cool, 20 min.
  2. Cube the tuna and toss with soy sauce, sesame oil and sliced spring onion.
  3. Spoon the rice into small cups and top with the tuna. Serve chilled.

Marine park chilli lime grilled pineapple

Fresh spears brushed with butter and zesty sugar, charred until caramelised, with chilli salt.

How it is made
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 2 limes
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  1. Pineapple spears grilled until caramelised and charred, finished with lime juice and a little chilli salt. Peel and core 1 ripe pineapple and cut into 12 long spears. Mix 2 tbsp brown sugar with the zest of 1 lime. Brush the spears with a little melted butter and sprinkle with the lime sugar. Grill on a hot barbecue or grill pan for 2 to 3 minutes a side until caramelised and charred. Squeeze over lime juice and sprinkle with a mix of 1 tsp salt and half a teaspoon chilli powder. Serve warm.

Mains

Loco moco

Seared beef patties with onion gravy.

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Gravy up to 2 days, cook patties fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 300g jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Shape the mince into 4 patties and season.
  2. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  3. Sear the patties in the oil until cooked through and set aside, 10 min.
  4. Soften the sliced onion in the pan, stir in the flour and stock and simmer, 17 min.
  5. Fry the eggs. Serve the patties on rice, topped with a fried egg and the gravy, 5 min.

Huli huli pineapple soy chicken

Thighs in ginger, garlic and brown sugar, grilled sticky, rice and slaw, for Dad's birthday again.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 125ml pineapple juice
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1.5 cups jasmine rice
  • 1/4 green cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 lime
  1. Chicken thighs marinated in pineapple juice, soy, ginger and brown sugar, grilled until sticky, with rice and slaw. Mix 125ml pineapple juice, 80ml soy, 60g brown sugar, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tbsp grated ginger and 2 crushed garlic cloves. Marinate 1kg chicken thigh in half of it for 4 hours. Simmer the rest for 5 minutes until syrupy. Grill the chicken over medium-high heat for 5 to 6 minutes a side, basting with the glaze, until sticky and charred. Serve with 1.5 cups steamed rice and a slaw of cabbage, carrot and lime.

Sweets

Coconut haupiaVegetarian

Firm set coconut milk pudding cut into squares.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 3 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 6 tbsp cornflour
  • 50g shredded coconut
  1. Toast the shredded coconut in a dry pan, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the coconut milk, sugar and cornflour over low heat until very thick, 10 min.
  3. Pour into a lined tray and chill for the setting time until firm, 3 hr.
  4. Cut into squares and scatter with the toasted coconut.

Sue's Hukilau pineapple ginger coolerNo alcohol

Fresh pineapple blended with ginger, lime and mint, strained.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled without the soda
Recipe for 4
  • 600g fresh pineapple chunks
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 1 lime
  • 6 mint leaves
  • 250ml cold water
  • 330ml soda water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Blend the pineapple, ginger, lime juice, mint and water until smooth.
  2. Strain through a sieve into a jug, pressing on the pulp.
  3. Fill 4 glasses with ice, divide the juice between them, and top each with a splash of soda water and a pineapple wedge.
