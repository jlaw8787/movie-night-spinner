Snacks

Midnight fried chicken

Buttermilk brined fried drumsticks, best served before midnight

Prep 16 min, cook 510 min · Make ahead: Brine the day before, fry on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken drumsticks
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • white pepper, to taste
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Brine the drumsticks overnight in the buttermilk and hot sauce.
  2. Mix the flour with the paprika, garlic powder and white pepper. Dredge the chicken twice.
  3. Fry in the oil at 165C in batches until 75C at the bone.
  4. Serve well before midnight.

Kingston Falls gingerbreadVegetarian

Ginger and cinnamon gingerbread cut into little shapes

Prep 40 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Melt the butter, sugar and golden syrup, then stir in the flour, ground ginger, cinnamon and bicarb.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll out and cut into little houses.
  4. Bake at 180C until firm.
  5. Cool, then pipe with icing made from the icing sugar and a little water.

Mains

Dorry's Tavern shepherd's pie

Lamb and carrot filling under baked buttery mashed potato

Prep 15 min, cook 77 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble then bake
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Dice the onion and carrots. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion and carrot.
  3. Add the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and stock and simmer.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and milk.
  5. Top the mince with mash and bake at 200C until golden.

Sweets

Snow White cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with a fluffy meringue frosting rolled in coconut

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50ml water
  • 100g desiccated coconut
  1. Beat the butter with half the sugar, add 2 eggs and the flour and spoon into 12 cupcake cases.
  2. Bake at 175C until risen and golden, then cool.
  3. Whisk the 2 remaining egg whites, boil the rest of the sugar with the water to 118C and pour in while beating until thick.
  4. Dome the meringue on each cupcake and roll in coconut.

Drinks

Rule two sparkling citrus coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemon, lime and orange with mint and elderflower in sparkling water

Prep 12 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Slice the citrus and crush the mint with the cordial and lime juice up to 4 hours ahead, chilled; add sparkling water and ice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 60ml elderflower cordial
  • 1l sparkling water
  • crushed ice, to serve
  1. Slice the lemon, one lime and the orange into thin wheels.
  2. Lightly crush a handful of mint with the cordial and the juice of the other lime in a jug.
  3. Add the citrus wheels and sparkling water.
  4. Fill four glasses with crushed ice and pour over. Keep it well away from anything furry.

When to serve
