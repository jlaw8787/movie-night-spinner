Snacks

Midnight fried chicken

Buttermilk brined fried drumsticks, best served before midnight.

Prep 16 min, cook 30 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brine the day before, fry on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken drumsticks
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • white pepper, to taste
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Brine the drumsticks overnight in the buttermilk and hot sauce.
  2. Mix the flour with the paprika, garlic powder and white pepper. Dredge the chicken twice.
  3. Fry in the oil at 165°C in batches until 75°C at the bone, 30 min.
  4. Serve well before midnight.

Kingston Falls gingerbreadVegetarian

Ginger and cinnamon gingerbread cut into little shapes.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min, then 75 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Melt the butter, sugar and golden syrup, then stir in the flour, ground ginger, cinnamon and bicarb, 10 min.
  2. Chill the dough, 1 hr.
  3. Roll out and cut into little houses.
  4. Bake at 180°C until firm, 10 min.
  5. Cool, then pipe with icing made from the icing sugar and a little water, 15 min.

Mrs Peltzer's blender smoothie shots

Berry, banana and yoghurt smoothie shots.

How it is made
  • 400g frozen mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 banana
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200ml milk
  1. Frozen berries, banana and Greek yoghurt blitzed thick with honey, poured into little shot glasses with a swirl of berry sauce down the side. Simmer 100g frozen berries with 1 tbsp sugar for 5 minutes and cool for the sauce. Blend 300g frozen mixed berries, 1 banana, 250g Greek yoghurt, 2 tbsp honey and 200ml milk until thick and smooth. Drizzle a little sauce down the inside of 12 shot glasses, pour in the smoothie and serve straight away with tiny spoons.

Dorry's Tavern shepherd's pie

Lamb and carrot filling under baked buttery mashed potato.

Prep 27 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble then bake
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Dice the onion and carrots. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion and carrot, 12 min.
  3. Add the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and stock and simmer, 20 min.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and milk, 20 min.
  5. Top the mince with mash and bake at 200°C until golden, 25 min.

Peltzer family roast pork

Pork loin with crackling, roast apples and apple gravy, Christmas at the Peltzer house.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg rolled pork loin
  • 1 box salt
  • 4 apples
  • 2 onions
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml apple juice
  • 200ml chicken stock
  1. Pork loin with shattering crackling, roasted apples and onions, buttery mash and a apple gravy, a snowy small-town Christmas dinner. Score the rind of a 1.5kg rolled pork loin, salt well and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Roast at 240°C for 30 minutes, then 180°C for 60 minutes with 4 halved apples and 2 quartered onions around it until 65°C inside. Rest 15 minutes. Mash 1kg potatoes with 60g butter and milk. Make gravy from the pan with 1 tbsp flour, 200ml apple juice and 200ml stock.

Snow White cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with a fluffy meringue frosting rolled in coconut.

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50ml water
  • 100g desiccated coconut
  1. Beat the butter with half the sugar, add 2 eggs and the flour and spoon into 12 cupcake cases.
  2. Bake at 175°C until risen and golden, then cool, 18 min.
  3. Whisk the 2 remaining egg whites, boil the rest of the sugar with the water to 118°C and pour in while beating until thick, 12 min.
  4. Dome the meringue on each cupcake and roll in coconut.

Drinks

Rule two sparkling citrus coolerNo alcohol

Lemon, lime and orange with mint and honey in sparkling water.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Slice the citrus and crush the mint with the cordial and lime juice up to 4 hours ahead, chilled; add sparkling water and ice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 60ml honey syrup
  • 1 L sparkling water
  • crushed ice, to serve
  1. Slice the lemon, one lime and the orange into thin wheels.
  2. Lightly crush a handful of mint with the cordial and the juice of the other lime in a jug.
  3. Add the citrus wheels and sparkling water.
  4. Fill four glasses with crushed ice and pour over. Keep it well away from anything furry.

When to serve

Serve the snacks well before midnight.
