Charlene's Venice Beach charred cornVegetarian

Chargrilled cobs with butter, lime mayo, cotija and chilli, eaten on the safe house balcony

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime mayo up to 1 day ahead; grill the corn just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 20g butter
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 80g cotija or feta
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Brush the corn with melted butter and cook in a very hot grill pan, turning, for 10 minutes until charred all over.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the zest and juice of 1 lime.
  3. Brush the hot corn with the lime mayo, roll in crumbled cotija, dust with chilli and scatter with coriander. Serve with lime wedges.

Ocean view oysters

Fresh shucked oysters on ice with lemon and shallot mignonette

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mignonette up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 shallots
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 24 freshly shucked oysters
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Finely dice the shallots and mix with the vinegar and pepper.
  2. Rest.
  3. Lay the oysters on crushed ice with lemon wedges and the mignonette alongside.

Hanna's three-hour beef and pork ragù

Beef and pork ragù simmered with red wine and a parmesan rind

Prep 20 min, cook 210 min · Make ahead: Better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stick
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 400g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 200ml red wine
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 1 parmesan rind
  • 250ml water
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 60g parmesan
  1. Soften the diced onion, carrot and celery in the oil.
  2. Add both minces and brown hard.
  3. Pour in the wine and reduce, then add the tomatoes, parmesan rind and water.
  4. Simmer gently, topping up with water.
  5. Cook the rigatoni and toss through the ragu with a splash of pasta water.
  6. Grate the parmesan over.

Diner coffee and pieVegetarian

Granny Smith apples and cinnamon baked in a sweet shortcrust

Prep 30 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Pie up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg Granny Smith apples
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 60g ground coffee
  1. Peel and slice the apples and toss with the sugar, cinnamon and flour.
  2. Line a 23cm dish with one sheet of pastry, fill, cover with the second sheet and cut vents.
  3. Bake at 190C.
  4. Cool slightly.
  5. Brew strong drip coffee and serve the pie in wedges with cream.

Eady's baked cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked cheesecake on a cookie crust, chilled until set

Prep 25 min, cook 780 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 250g digestive biscuits
  • 100g butter
  • 750g cream cheese
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, eggs and sour cream.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150C for an hour.
  4. Cool in the oven.
  5. Chill overnight.

Face-to-face cold brew with cinnamon creamVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold brew steeped 12 hours, topped with brown sugar cinnamon cream

Prep 10 min, cook 720 min · Make ahead: Brew the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 100g coarse ground coffee
  • 1l cold water
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the coffee into the cold water and leave in the fridge for 12 hours.
  2. Strain through a fine sieve lined with paper towel.
  3. Whisk the cream with the sugar and cinnamon until just thickened.
  4. Fill four glasses with ice, pour in the cold brew and spoon the cream over.
