Santa Cruz boardwalk corn dogs

Franks dipped in buttermilk cornmeal batter and fried golden

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 3 hours ahead in the fridge; dip and fry just before serving, best hot
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, egg and buttermilk into a thick batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push onto sticks.
  3. Dip in batter and fry in the oil at 180C for 4 minutes until golden, in batches.
  4. Serve with mustard.

Mains

Beach house barbecue chicken and corn

Grilled chicken thighs in barbecue sauce, charred corn, creamy slaw, soft rolls

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; grill the chicken and corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 4 soft rolls
  1. Grill the chicken and corn over coals.
  2. Brush the chicken with barbecue sauce in the last minutes.
  3. Mix the cabbage with the mayonnaise for slaw.
  4. Serve with soft rolls.

Sweets

Rabbit-free carrot cakeVegetarian

Carrot and walnut cake with cinnamon and nutmeg, frosted

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days
Recipe for 4
  • 300g carrots, grated
  • 100g walnuts, chopped
  • 200ml vegetable oil
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Mix the carrot, walnuts, oil, sugar, eggs, cinnamon and nutmeg. Fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 170C for 50 minutes. Cool.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar and lemon zest and frost the cake.

Hands Across America punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Pineapple and orange juice with grenadine, topped with lemonade

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add the ice, grenadine and lemonade just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 60ml grenadine
  • 750ml lemonade
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Pour the juices into a big bowl with ice.
  2. Add the grenadine and top with lemonade.
  3. Float orange slices on top and hold hands while you drink it.
