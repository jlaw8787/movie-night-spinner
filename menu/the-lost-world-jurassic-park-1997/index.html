Beach picnic roast beef finger sandwiches

Rare roast beef and arugula on white bread with horseradish butter, cut into fingers.

Prep 16 min · Make ahead: Make the horseradish butter up to 3 days ahead; build the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, covered with a damp tea towel in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 8 slices of white sandwich bread
  • 300g rare roast beef, thinly sliced
  • 50g rocket
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Beat the butter with the horseradish until smooth.
  2. Spread over all 8 slices of bread. Layer 4 slices with the beef, rocket and a grind of pepper and close with the rest, 8 min.
  3. Trim off the crusts and cut each sandwich into 3 fingers.

Ajay's spiced potato samosasVegetarian

Crisp pastry triangles of potato and peas with cumin, garam masala and green chilli.

Prep 58 min, cook 48 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; shape the samosas up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled, or freeze raw up to 1 month; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 5 tbsp vegetable oil, 3 for the dough, 2 for the filling
  • 80ml water
  • 500g potatoes, diced small
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch coriander, chopped
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch mint
  • salt, to taste
  1. Rub the 3 tbsp dough oil into the flour and a pinch of salt, add the water and knead to a firm smooth dough. Cover and rest, 35 min.
  2. Boil the potatoes in salted water until just tender and drain, 15 min.
  3. Fry the cumin seeds in the 2 tbsp filling oil until they pop, add the onion and chilli and soften. Stir in the garam masala, turmeric, potato and peas, then a squeeze of lemon, the coriander and salt, 10 min.
  4. Spread out and cool the filling, 15 min.
  5. Roll the dough into 6 thin rounds and cut each in half. Fold each half into a cone, fill, wet the edge and press to seal.
  6. Heat the frying oil in a deep pot to 170°C, 8 min.
  7. Fry the samosas in batches for 5 minutes, turning, until golden and blistered. Drain.
  8. Chop the mint, stir it through the yoghurt with salt and serve with the samosas.

San Diego waterfront carne asada

Citrus and garlic marinated skirt steak, charred and sliced, with charred tomato salsa.

Prep 18 min, cook 25 min, 2 hr 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Marinate the steak up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, 2 hours at least; make the salsa up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef skirt steak
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 3 limes, 2 for the marinade, 1 for the salsa
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 4 ripe tomatoes
  • 1/2 white onion
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 avocado
  • salt, to taste
  1. Blend the orange juice, juice of 2 limes, garlic, cumin, chilli powder, oil, half the coriander and a good pinch of salt.
  2. Pour over the steak, turn to coat and marinate in the fridge, 2 hr.
  3. Char the tomatoes, onion and jalapeño in a dry heavy pan until blackened in patches, 10 min.
  4. Blitz with the rest of the coriander, the juice of the last lime and salt to a rough salsa, 5 min.
  5. Heat a grill or heavy pan until very hot. Grill the steak for 4 minutes a side.
  6. Rest the steak, loosely covered, 10 min.
  7. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan and slice the avocado, 5 min.
  8. Slice the steak thinly across the grain and serve with the tortillas, avocado and salsa.

Roland Tembo's hunters' camp chicken curry

Chicken, potato and tomato simmered with ginger and spice, rice and chapati, for the buck rex.

Prep 2 min, cook 1 hr 1 min · Make ahead: Make the curry up to 2 days ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 20g ginger
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 400g potatoes
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 chapatis
  • 4 tbsp lime pickle
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Fry the sliced onions in the oil for 8 minutes, then the grated garlic, ginger and curry powder for 1 minute.
  2. Add the diced chicken, diced potato, tomatoes and 250ml water and simmer for 30 minutes until the potato is tender.
  3. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, then rest for 5.
  4. Warm the chapati in a dry pan, 4 min.
  5. Scatter the curry with coriander and serve with rice, chapati and lime pickle.

Rex parents' mud pieVegetarian

Chocolate graham cracker base under dense baked dark chocolate, softly whipped cream.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 8 min, 2 hr 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake and chill up to 2 days ahead, the chill takes 2 hours; whip the cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g chocolate biscuits
  • 250g butter, 100g melted for the base, 150g for the filling
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 350ml thickened cream, 150ml for the filling, 200ml to whip
  1. Crush the biscuits to fine crumbs, mix with the melted base butter and press firmly into a 23cm springform tin.
  2. Chill the base. Heat the oven to 160°C, 20 min.
  3. Melt the chocolate with the filling butter over low heat, 5 min.
  4. Whisk the eggs and brown sugar until thick, then whisk in the chocolate, cocoa and 150ml of the cream, 5 min.
  5. Pour onto the base and bake until set at the edges with a slight wobble, 35 min.
  6. Cool in the tin, 30 min.
  7. Chill until firm, 2 hr.
  8. Whip the rest of the cream to soft peaks and serve with wedges of pie, 5 min.

Backyard pool blue margarita

Tequila and blue orange liqueur shaken with fresh lime and sugar syrup, in a salt-rimmed glass.

Prep 10 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; rim the glasses up to 1 hour ahead and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 40ml hot water
  • 6 limes, juiced to make 120ml
  • 2 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 180ml tequila
  • 60ml blue curaçao liqueur
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Stir the sugar into the hot water until dissolved.
  2. Cool the syrup, 10 min.
  3. Juice the limes. Rub the rims of 4 glasses with a squeezed lime half and dip in the salt.
  4. Shake the tequila, blue curaçao, lime juice and syrup hard with half the ice.
  5. Strain into the glasses over the rest of the ice.
