Snacks

Cleveland pierogi with fried onionsVegetarian

Potato and cheese pierogi boiled then served with butter-fried onions

Prep 5 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Fry the onions up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat. Boil and fry the pierogi on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 70g butter
  • 24 frozen potato and cheese pierogi
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  1. Slowly fry the onions in some of the butter over low heat until deep golden.
  2. Boil the pierogi until they float, then drain well.
  3. Fry the pierogi in the remaining butter in a large pan until crisp and golden on both sides.
  4. Pile onto a platter, top with the onions and serve with sour cream and chives.

Bleacher honey chilli roasted peanutsVegetarian

Roasted peanuts coated in honey, butter and chilli flakes

Prep 5 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 400g raw unsalted peanuts
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Melt the butter with the honey and brown sugar, then toss the peanuts and chilli flakes through until coated.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and bake at 170°C, stirring every few minutes, until deep golden.
  3. Sprinkle with sea salt while hot, then cool completely and break into clusters.

Mains

Cleveland Polish boy with fries and slaw

Charred kielbasa in a roll with chips, barbecue sauce and slaw

Prep 5 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Bake the chips and grill the kielbasa on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g frozen chips
  • 1/2 green cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 4 kielbasa sausages
  • 4 long soft rolls
  • 150ml smoky barbecue sauce
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayonnaise, vinegar and sugar for the slaw.
  3. Score the kielbasa and grill, turning, until charred.
  4. Split the rolls, lay a kielbasa in each, pile on hot chips, drizzle with barbecue sauce and top with slaw.

Wild Thing lattice cherry pieVegetarian

Cherry pie with a woven top and ice cream, for Vaughn's entrance

Prep 23 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 2 jars pitted cherries (680g each)
  • 100g caster sugar, plus extra to sprinkle
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1l vanilla ice cream, to serve
  1. Drain the cherries, keeping some of the juice. Simmer the juice with the sugar and cornflour until thick, then stir in the cherries and a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Line a 23cm pie dish with one pastry sheet and fill with the cherries.
  3. Cut the other sheets into strips and weave a lattice over the top. Brush with egg and sprinkle with sugar.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  5. Rest, then serve with ice cream.

Jobu's dark rum and ginger punchVegetarian

Dark rum, lime, ginger beer. Very bad to steal Jobu's rum.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Build each glass over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml dark rum, plus a small extra shot for Jobu
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml ginger beer, cold
  • 1 bottle aromatic bitters
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice. Add the rum and lime juice to each glass.
  2. Top with ginger beer and a couple of dashes of bitters. Stir once and garnish with a lime wedge.
  3. Pour one extra small shot of rum into a glass and set it aside for Jobu before anyone drinks.

When to serve

Serve the drink for Jobu when Cerrano steps up to bat.
