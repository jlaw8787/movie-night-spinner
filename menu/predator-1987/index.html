Snacks

Mud-camo bean dipVegetarian

Black beans, garlic, cumin and lime blended, topped with feta, corn chips.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g black beans (2 tins), drained
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 limes
  • 80g feta, crumbled
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 green chilli, sliced
  • 1 bag corn chips
  1. Blitz the beans with the garlic, cumin and lime juice until smooth.
  2. Spread in a bowl and top with the feta, coriander and green chilli.
  3. Serve with the corn chips.

Billy's plantain chipsVegetarian

Thin fried green plantain chips with lime zest salt.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 1 lime, zested
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Peel the plantains and slice very thin on a mandoline.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C in batches until crisp, about 2 minutes each.
  3. Mix the sea salt with the lime zest and toss through the hot chips.

Blain's chilli beef jerky

Top round in soy and chilli, dried thin in the oven.

How it is made
  • 800g beef topside
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • chilli flakes
  • cayenne pepper
  • black peppercorns
  1. Thin-sliced topside marinated in soy, Worcestershire, brown sugar and dried chilli, slow-dried until chewy with a slow-building heat. Freeze 800g topside for 1 hour, then slice 3mm thick across the grain. Mix 100ml soy sauce, 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp chilli flakes, 1 tsp cayenne and 1 tsp cracked pepper. Marinate the beef overnight. Pat dry and lay on wire racks over trays. Dry at 70°C with the oven door slightly open for 4 hours until leathery but bendy. Cool and store airtight.

Mains

Chopper pad steak

Smoked paprika sirloin steaks grilled hard, with garlic parsley chilli sauce.

Prep 15 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Chimichurri up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 100ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Rub the steaks with salt, pepper and smoked paprika.
  2. Chop the parsley and garlic and mix with the vinegar, oil and chilli flakes.
  3. Grill over high heat, turning once, 8 min.
  4. Rest the steaks, then spoon over the chimichurri, 5 min.

Achiote chicken, rice and beans

Thighs braised tender in annatto, orange and sofrito, with sweet fried plantain on the side.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar achiote paste
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 oranges
  • 200ml chicken stock
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400g tin black beans
  • 2 ripe plantains
  1. Chicken thighs braised in achiote, orange and sofrito until tender, with black beans, white rice and sweet fried plantain. Fry 1 diced onion, 1 diced red capsicum and 3 garlic cloves in oil for 8 minutes. Add 1 tbsp achiote paste, 1 tsp cumin, the juice of 2 oranges and 200ml chicken stock. Add 1kg chicken thighs, cover and simmer 35 minutes until tender. Cook 300g rice. Warm a drained 400g tin black beans with a pinch of cumin. Slice 2 ripe plantains on the angle and fry in oil until caramelised.

Dutch's mud camouflage chocolate cakeVegetarian

Dense chocolate mud cake with a chocolate cream glaze.

Prep 8 min, cook 1 hr 27 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 400g dark chocolate
  • 250g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 150g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C and line a cake tin. Melt part of the chocolate with the butter and let it cool slightly, 12 min.
  2. Whisk in the eggs and sugar, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out with just a few crumbs, then cool in the tin, 1 hr 10 min.
  4. Heat the cream, melt in the remaining chocolate, pour over the cake and drag with a fork, 5 min.

Val Verde jungle pineapple punchNo alcohol

Dark coconut, pineapple, passionfruit and lime over crushed ice.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Steep and chill the vanilla tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; juice the limes and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml dark coconut
  • 120ml pineapple juice
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 3 limes (60ml juice)
  • 20ml grenadine
  • 1 small pineapple
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 2 cups ice, for shaking
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 5 minutes, lift out, add the vanilla and chill.
  2. Juice the limes and scoop out the passionfruit pulp. Cut pineapple wedges for garnish.
  3. Shake the cold tea, pineapple juice, passionfruit, lime juice and grenadine with ice for 15 seconds.
  4. Strain into tumblers packed with crushed ice, grate nutmeg on top and garnish with a pineapple wedge.
