Snacks

Lamplighter crumpets with honey butterVegetarian

Spongy crumpets toasted brown, whipped honey butter melting into the holes.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Beat the honey butter up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; let it soften before spreading. Toast the crumpets just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp flaky salt
  • 8 crumpets
  • 100ml golden syrup
  1. Beat the butter, honey and salt until pale and fluffy.
  2. Toast the crumpets until crisp on the edges and golden on top, 3 min.
  3. Spread the honey butter on straight away so it sinks into the holes.
  4. Stack on a warm plate and serve with golden syrup.

Crispy cheese crust toastVegetarian

White bread pressed onto fried cheese until golden and lacy.

Prep 8 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheddar and snip the chives up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Fry just before serving, as it is best eaten hot
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 50g butter
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Grate the cheddar.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat, scatter the cheese into the hot pan, press a slice of bread on top and fry until the cheese forms a golden lacy crust. Flip and toast the other side, in batches, 16 min.
  3. Cut into fingers and sprinkle with chives.

Bathtub ocean prawn cocktail glasses

King prawns on shredded lettuce, mayo and tomato dressing, for the bath that becomes a sea.

How it is made
  • 600g cooked king prawns
  • 120g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bottle Tabasco
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  1. Poached prawns on shredded lettuce in small glasses with a cocktail sauce of mayo, tomato sauce and lemon. Peel and devein 600g cooked king prawns, leaving a few tails on for the rims. Mix 120g whole egg mayonnaise with 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tsp Worcestershire, a few drops of Tabasco and the juice of half a lemon. Shred half an iceberg lettuce and divide between 4 glasses. Pile the prawns on top, spoon over the sauce, dust with a pinch of paprika and serve with lemon wedges.

Mains

Music hall bangers and mash

Browned pork sausages, mash and onion gravy.

Prep 9 min, cook 1 hr 19 min · Make ahead: Slice the onions and peel the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, onions chilled and potatoes in cold water. Cook everything on the night; the onion gravy reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick pork sausages
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 500g frozen peas
  1. Slice the onions and peel the potatoes.
  2. Brown the sausages slowly in the oil in a pan, 20 min.
  3. Cook the onions in the same pan until soft, 10 min.
  4. Add the stock and simmer until thick, 20 min.
  5. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with butter and milk, 25 min.
  6. Cook the peas, 4 min.
  7. Serve the sausages on the mash with peas and plenty of gravy.

Cherry Tree Lane chicken, leek and ham pie

Thighs and smoked pork in a creamy sauce under puff, buttered peas, for the Banks family.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 150g ham
  • 2 leeks
  • 50g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  1. Chicken, leek and ham in a creamy sauce under a puff pastry lid, baked at 200°C for 25 minutes, with buttered peas. Brown 800g diced chicken thigh in 30g butter and set aside. Soften 2 sliced leeks in the same pan, stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 300ml chicken stock and 150ml cream and simmer until thick. Add the chicken, 150g diced ham and chopped thyme and season. Spoon into a pie dish, cover with 1 sheet puff pastry, cut a slit and brush with beaten egg. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes until golden. Serve with 2 cups peas tossed in butter.

Sweets

Balloon lady jam tartsVegetarian

Shortcrust tarts filled with strawberry, apricot and blackcurrant jam.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 80g strawberry jam
  • 80g apricot jam
  • 80g blackcurrant jam
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a patty tin.
  2. Fill a third with strawberry jam, a third with apricot and a third with blackcurrant.
  3. Bake at 190°C until the pastry is golden, 15 min.
  4. Cool, 15 min.
  5. Dust the edges with icing sugar.

Drinks

Kite day strawberry mint lemonadeNo alcohol

Muddled strawberries and mint with lemon juice and syrup.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Make and cool the syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep in the fridge; juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead. Muddle the strawberries and top up with soda water on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 300g strawberries, hulled
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  • 5 lemons
  • 600ml chilled soda water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Simmer the sugar and water until dissolved, 5 min.
  2. Cool the syrup, 15 min.
  3. Muddle the strawberries with the mint leaves in a jug.
  4. Add the syrup and lemon juice and stir well.
  5. Fill tall glasses with ice, divide the mix between them and top with soda water. Garnish with a strawberry half and a mint sprig.
