Snacks

Cream cheese and apricot pastriesVegetarian

Puff pastry squares with sweet cream cheese and apricot jam.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar, for sweetening the filling
  • 4 tbsp apricot jam
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar, for dusting
  1. Cut the pastry into squares.
  2. Sweeten the cream cheese with icing sugar and spoon with the jam into the centre of each square. Fold the corners in.
  3. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C until golden.
  4. Dust with icing sugar.

Party canapés for Holly's crowdVegetarian

Paprika devilled eggs, and celery filled with chive cream cheese.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 8 celery stalks
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs, mash the yolks with mayonnaise and pipe back, then dust with paprika.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with chopped chives and fill short celery sticks.

Crumbed blue cheese stuffed olives

Green ones filled with sharp blue, crumbed and fried, for the crowded party.

How it is made
  • 24 large pitted green olives
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 40g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 100g mayonnaise
  1. Big green olives stuffed with blue cheese, crumbed and fried until crunchy, served hot on picks with a lemon and chive mayo. Drain 24 large pitted green olives and pat dry. Push a little blue cheese (80g total) into each. Roll in 40g plain flour, then 1 beaten egg, then 60g dried breadcrumbs. Heat 5cm oil in a saucepan to 180°C and fry the olives in two batches for 1 to 2 minutes until golden. Drain on paper towel. Stir the zest of 1 lemon and 1 tbsp chopped chives into 100g mayonnaise. Serve the olives hot on toothpicks with the mayo.

Mains

Sally Tomato's Thursday steak

Seared sirloin, cracked pepper cream sauce, thin fries.

Prep 13 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day, fry chips fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns, cracked
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries.
  2. Fry the fries twice in the oil until crisp.
  3. Season the steaks with salt, sear to your liking and rest.
  4. Toast the cracked peppercorns in the pan, add the cream and simmer until thick.
  5. Serve with the fries and a green salad.

Day out chicken, bacon and avocado club

Toasted triple decker of poached breast, crisp rashers, cos and lemon mayo, after the library visit.

How it is made
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 1 loaf white sandwich bread
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  1. Toasted triple-decker with poached chicken, crisp bacon, avocado, cos and tomato, a lemon mayo through every layer, cut into triangles and pinned. Poach 2 chicken breasts in simmering salted water for 15 minutes, rest 10 minutes and slice. Grill 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Toast 12 slices of white sandwich bread. Mix 100g mayo with the juice of half a lemon. For each sandwich, spread 3 slices with mayo and layer chicken, sliced avocado (2 total) and cos on the first, bacon and sliced tomato on the second, and top with the third. Pin with 4 toothpicks and cut into triangles.

Sweets

Custard blueberry Danish pastriesVegetarian

Puff pastry rounds filled with custard and blueberries.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 250ml thick custard
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  1. Cut the pastry into rounds.
  2. Spoon custard into the centre of each and top with blueberries.
  3. Brush the edges with beaten egg and bake at 200°C until golden.
  4. Brush with warm apricot jam.

Drinks

Champagne cocktails

Sparkling wine with a sugar cube, bitters and brandy.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bottle champagne or dry sparkling wine (750 ml)
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • aromatic bitters, a few dashes
  • 60ml brandy
  • 1 lemon
  1. Chill the coupes and the bottle.
  2. Drop a sugar cube into each glass and shake 3 dashes of bitters over it. Add the brandy.
  3. Tilt the glass and pour the sparkling slowly down the side so it does not foam over.
  4. Peel long strips of lemon zest, twist over the drinks to spray the oil and drop them in.
