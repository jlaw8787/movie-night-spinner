PAGAN parmesan stuffed mushroomsVegetarian

Button mushrooms stuffed with garlic cream cheese and parmesan, crumb topping

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Fill the caps and add the crumb up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 20 button mushrooms
  • 150g cream cheese, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 30g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Pull the stalks from the mushrooms and chop finely.
  2. Mix the stalks with the cream cheese, garlic, parmesan and parsley.
  3. Fill each mushroom cap.
  4. Mix the breadcrumbs with the butter and press on top.
  5. Bake until bubbling and golden.

Friday's by-the-book glazed doughnut holesVegetarian

Yeast doughnut holes dipped in vanilla icing

Prep 40 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Make and shape the dough up to 1 day ahead and prove it covered in the fridge overnight; fry and ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml milk, for the dough
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 egg
  • 350g plain flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp milk, for icing
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Warm the milk with the butter and sugar, add the yeast and egg, then mix into the flour and salt.
  2. Knead.
  3. Prove until doubled.
  4. Roll into small balls.
  5. Prove again.
  6. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175C in batches, turning, until golden.
  7. Whisk the icing sugar, milk and vanilla, dip the warm doughnuts and set on a rack.

Mains

Streebek's LA carnitas tacos

Orange and cumin pork shoulder braised, then crisped, in corn tortillas

Prep 25 min, cook 165 min · Make ahead: Braise and shred the pork up to 2 days ahead, chilled in its juices; crisp in a hot pan and serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 orange
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 250ml water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 16 corn tortillas
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  1. Cut the pork into chunks and rub with cumin, oregano and salt.
  2. Braise at 160C with the orange, garlic and water.
  3. Shred the pork.
  4. Crisp in a hot pan with oil.
  5. Serve in tortillas with diced onion, coriander and lime.

Sweets

By-the-book vanilla sliceVegetarian

Puff pastry layers with thick vanilla custard and white icing

Prep 35 min, cook 255 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1l milk
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 90g cornflour
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Bake the pastry pricked and weighted at 200C.
  2. Whisk the milk, yolks, sugar, cornflour and vanilla over medium heat until very thick.
  3. Spread the custard between the pastry sheets in a lined tin.
  4. Chill.
  5. Ice with icing sugar mixed with a little water.
  6. Cut into squares.

Friday's cold brew tonicVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold brew coffee poured over tonic water with an orange peel twist

Prep 15 min, cook 720 min · Make ahead: Steep the coffee overnight, up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g coarsely ground coffee
  • 800ml cold water
  • 1 tray ice
  • 600ml tonic water
  • 1 orange
  1. Stir the coffee into the cold water in a jug, cover and refrigerate overnight.
  2. Leave to steep.
  3. Strain through a paper filter.
  4. Fill glasses with ice, pour in tonic, then slowly pour cold brew over the top so it layers.
  5. Squeeze orange peel over each glass and drop it in.
