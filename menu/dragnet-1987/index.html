PAGAN parmesan stuffed mushroomsVegetarian

Button mushrooms stuffed with garlic cream cheese and parmesan, crumb topping.

Prep 17 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Fill the caps and add the crumb up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 20 button mushrooms
  • 150g cream cheese, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 30g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Pull the stalks from the mushrooms and chop finely, 12 min.
  2. Mix the stalks with the cream cheese, garlic, parmesan and parsley.
  3. Fill each mushroom cap.
  4. Mix the breadcrumbs with the butter and press on top.
  5. Bake until bubbling and golden, 15 min.

Friday's by-the-book glazed doughnut holesVegetarian

Yeast doughnut holes dipped in vanilla glaze.

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min, 1 hr 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Make and shape the dough up to 1 day ahead and prove it covered in the fridge overnight; fry and ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml milk, for the dough
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 egg
  • 350g plain flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp milk, for icing
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Warm the milk with the butter and sugar, add the yeast and egg, then mix into the flour and salt, 10 min.
  2. Knead.
  3. Prove until doubled, 1 hr.
  4. Roll into small balls.
  5. Prove again, 20 min.
  6. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C in batches, turning, until golden, 15 min.
  7. Whisk the icing sugar, milk and vanilla, dip the warm doughnuts and set on a rack, 10 min.

Streebek's griddled mini cheeseburgers

Griddled beef patties, cheese and pickles in soft buns, no sausage press needed.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 3 slices cheddar
  • 12 mini burger buns
  • 3 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • yellow mustard
  1. Beef smashed thin on a smoking griddle, cheddar melted under a lid, with pickles, mustard and a slick of burger sauce on mini burger buns. Divide 500g beef mince into 12 balls. Heat a griddle or heavy pan until smoking. Smash each ball flat with a spatula, season and cook 2 minutes until crusty. Flip, add a quarter slice of cheddar, splash in a little water and cover for 30 seconds. Mix 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp tomato sauce and 1 tbsp chopped pickles for sauce. Toast 12 mini burger buns and fill with sauce, patty, pickles and mustard.

Streebek's LA carnitas tacos

Orange and cumin pork shoulder braised, then crisped, in corn tortillas.

Prep 25 min, cook 2 hr 40 min · Make ahead: Braise and shred the pork up to 2 days ahead, chilled in its juices; crisp in a hot pan and serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 orange
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 250ml water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 16 corn tortillas
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  1. Cut the pork into chunks and rub with cumin, oregano and salt.
  2. Braise at 160°C with the orange, garlic and water, 2 hr 30 min.
  3. Shred the pork.
  4. Crisp in a hot pan with oil, 10 min.
  5. Serve in tortillas with diced onion, coriander and lime.

Brown Derby birthday meatloaf and gravy

Beef and pork meatloaf with tomato glaze and gravy, for Granny Mundy's birthday and Pep's toast.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 onions
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 350ml beef stock
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with a tangy tomato glaze, cut into thick slices over creamy mash and smothered in rich brown onion gravy. Mix 500g beef mince, 300g pork mince, 60g breadcrumbs, 1 egg, 1 grated onion, 2 tbsp Worcestershire and salt. Shape in a lined loaf tin, spread with 80ml tomato sauce mixed with 1 tbsp brown sugar and bake at 180°C for 1 hour. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml hot milk. Fry 1 sliced onion in butter until brown, stir in 1 tbsp flour, then 350ml beef stock and simmer until thick.

By-the-book vanilla sliceVegetarian

Puff pastry layers with thick vanilla custard and white frosting.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min, 4 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 L milk
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 90g cornflour
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Bake the pastry pricked and weighted at 200°C, 15 min.
  2. Whisk the milk, yolks, sugar, cornflour and vanilla over medium heat until very thick, 15 min.
  3. Spread the custard between the pastry sheets in a lined tin.
  4. Chill, 4 hr.
  5. Ice with icing sugar mixed with a little water.
  6. Cut into squares.

Badge 714 lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade over ice with a mint sprig, made by the book.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the syrup up to a week ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemons
  • 100g sugar
  • 800ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in 100ml hot water and cool, 3 min.
  2. Squeeze the lemons and stir the juice into the syrup in a jug.
  3. Add the soda water and ice and pour into tall glasses with a mint sprig in each.
