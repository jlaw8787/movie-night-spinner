Snacks

Hot dog cart sausages

Mini hot dogs glazed sticky with Dijon and honey, from Louie's cart

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer and drain the franks up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; glaze in a hot pan just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cocktail franks
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 24 toothpicks
  1. Simmer the franks and drain.
  2. Toss in a hot pan with the mustard and honey until sticky and glazed.
  3. Spear each on a toothpick and serve hot.

Fish crackersVegetarian

Crisp cheddar and butter crackers cut into little fish shapes

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 60g cold butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 splash cold water
  1. Pulse the cheddar, butter, flour and salt until it forms a dough, adding a splash of water.
  2. Roll thin and cut into little fish shapes.
  3. Bake at 180C until crisp.

Mains

Louie's hot dogs with everything

Franks liberated from Louie, in steamed buns with sauerkraut, mustard, onion and relish

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best griddled just before serving; chop the onion and set out the sauerkraut and relish up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 250g sauerkraut
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 onion
  • 4 tbsp sweet relish
  1. Simmer the franks, then griddle until blistered.
  2. Warm the sauerkraut in a small pan.
  3. Steam the buns and chop the onion.
  4. Fill the buns with franks, mustard, onion, relish and sauerkraut.

Sweets

Jenny's eighth birthday kitten cookiesVegetarian

Iced cat-shaped butter cookies for Jenny's birthday party

Prep 35 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 100g icing sugar, for the dough
  • 350g plain flour
  • 200g icing sugar, for icing
  • 3 drops orange food colouring
  • 1 tube white writing icing
  1. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into cat shapes.
  3. Bake at 160C until just coloured.
  4. Cool on a rack.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with a little water, tint orange, ice the cookies and pipe on whiskers in white.

Drinks

Fagin's barge honey warm milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Whole milk warmed with honey, cinnamon and vanilla, no alcohol

Prep 3 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Warm the milk with the honey, cinnamon and vanilla up to 1 day ahead and chill it; reheat gently and whisk until foamy to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 5 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  1. Warm the milk with the honey, one cinnamon stick and vanilla until steaming but not boiling.
  2. Remove the cinnamon, whisk hard or use a frother until foamy.
  3. Pour into mugs, dust with nutmeg and add a fresh cinnamon stick to each.
