Snacks

Hot dog cart sausages

Mini hot dogs glazed sticky with Dijon and honey, from Louie's cart.

Prep 2 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer and drain the franks up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; glaze in a hot pan just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cocktail franks
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 24 toothpicks
  1. Simmer the franks and drain, 5 min.
  2. Toss in a hot pan with the mustard and honey until sticky and glazed.
  3. Spear each on a toothpick and serve hot.

Fish crackersVegetarian

Crisp cheddar and butter crackers cut into little fish shapes.

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 60g cold butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 splash cold water
  1. Pulse the cheddar, butter, flour and salt until it forms a dough, adding a splash of water.
  2. Roll thin and cut into little fish shapes.
  3. Bake at 180°C until crisp, 12 min.

Fifth Avenue brown butter chestnuts

Roasted until the shells curl, peeled warm, tossed in nutty sage butter and sea salt.

How it is made
  • 500g fresh chestnuts
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 box flaky sea salt
  1. Chestnuts scored and roasted until the shells curl, peeled warm and tossed in sage brown butter with sea salt, served in paper cones. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut a cross into the flat side of 500g chestnuts and soak them in water for 15 minutes. Roast on a tray for 20 to 25 minutes until the shells curl back. Wrap in a tea towel for 5 minutes, then peel while warm. Melt 40g butter in a pan with 6 sage leaves until it smells nutty, toss the chestnuts through, season with sea salt and serve in paper cones.

Mains

Louie's hot dogs with everything

Franks liberated from Louie, in steamed buns with sauerkraut, mustard, onion and relish.

Prep 4 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best griddled just before serving; chop the onion and set out the sauerkraut and relish up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 250g sauerkraut
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 onion
  • 4 tbsp sweet relish
  1. Simmer the franks, then griddle until blistered, 10 min.
  2. Warm the sauerkraut in a small pan, 4 min.
  3. Steam the buns and chop the onion, 4 min.
  4. Fill the buns with franks, mustard, onion, relish and sauerkraut.

Jenny's lemon and thyme roast chicken

A whole chicken roasted with garlic, dripping potatoes, green beans and gravy, for Oliver's new home.

How it is made
  • 1 whole chicken (1.8kg)
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  • 60g butter
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  1. Whole chicken roasted with lemon, garlic and thyme, crunchy potatoes cooked in the drippings, buttered green beans and a quick pan gravy. Heat the oven to 220°C. Stuff a 1.8kg chicken with 1 halved lemon, 4 garlic cloves and 6 thyme sprigs, rub with 30g soft butter and salt. Roast 20 minutes, then drop to 180°C. Add 1kg peeled, quartered potatoes tossed in oil around the bird and roast another 50 minutes until the thigh juices run clear. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp plain flour and 300ml chicken stock into the pan juices for gravy. Boil 300g green beans 3 minutes and toss in butter.

Jenny's eighth birthday kitten cookiesVegetarian

Iced cat-shaped butter cookies, one for every guest at the party.

Prep 35 min, cook 18 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 100g icing sugar, for the dough
  • 350g plain flour
  • 200g icing sugar, for icing
  • 3 drops of orange food colouring
  • 1 tube white writing icing
  1. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into cat shapes.
  3. Bake at 160°C until just coloured, 18 min.
  4. Cool on a rack, 20 min.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with a little water, tint orange, ice the biscuits and pipe on whiskers in white.

Fagin's barge honey warm milkNo alcohol

Whole milk warmed with honey, cinnamon and vanilla.

Prep 3 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Warm the milk with the honey, cinnamon and vanilla up to 1 day ahead and chill it; reheat gently and whisk until foamy to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 5 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  1. Warm the milk with the honey, one cinnamon stick and vanilla until steaming but not boiling, 5 min.
  2. Remove the cinnamon, whisk hard or use a frother until foamy.
  3. Pour into mugs, dust with nutmeg and add a fresh cinnamon stick to each.
