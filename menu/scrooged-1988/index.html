Snacks

TV studio canapés

Salmon blinis. The props man can't glue antlers on the mouse

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 blinis
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche and smoked salmon.
  2. Slice and toast the baguette, then rub with garlic.
  3. Dice the tomatoes, mix with torn basil and pile on the toasts.

Toaster fairy toast soldiersVegetarian

Eggs and buttered toast strips, with a toaster-swinging fairy

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Nothing keeps ahead; boil the eggs and toast the bread to order so the yolks stay soft and the soldiers crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices thick white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Boil the eggs for a soft yolk.
  2. Toast the bread golden, butter it and cut into soldiers.
  3. Put each egg in a cup, take the top off and dip away.

Mains

Claire's soup kitchen stew

Braised beef, crusty bread. Frank drops in at Claire's shelter

Prep 42 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 600g potatoes
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 Italian loaf
  1. Dice the beef, onions, carrots and potatoes.
  2. Brown the beef in batches, then soften the onion and carrot.
  3. Add the potatoes, stock and thyme, cover and braise at 160C until the meat falls apart.
  4. Serve with slices of the Italian loaf.

Christmas puddingVegetarian

Steamed fruit pudding soaked in brandy, flamed at the table, with custard

Prep 25 min, cook 4 hours, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the dried fruit in the brandy the night before; the steamed pudding keeps up to 2 weeks wrapped, steam to reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed dried fruit
  • 100ml brandy, to soak the fruit
  • 125g butter
  • 125g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 2 tbsp brandy, to flame
  • 600ml custard
  1. Soak the dried fruit in the brandy overnight.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, beat in the eggs, then mix in the breadcrumbs, flour, spice and the soaked fruit.
  3. Pack into a greased pudding basin, cover and steam.
  4. Warm the extra brandy, light it and pour over at the table. Serve with warm custard.

Drinks

EggnogVegetarian

Cooked custard eggnog with brandy and fresh nutmeg, served chilled

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 100ml brandy
  • fresh nutmeg, grated, to taste
  1. Whisk the egg yolks with the sugar.
  2. Heat the milk and cream, pour over the yolks and stir over low heat to 75C.
  3. Chill well.
  4. Stir in the brandy and grate fresh nutmeg on top.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the Ghost shows Frank his brother's Christmas dinner.
