Snacks

Villeneuve market breadVegetarian

Toasted baguette with brie and apple, or goat's cheese and honey.

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Toast the baguette slices up to 1 day ahead and keep them airtight; slice the apples and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g brie
  • 2 red apples
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the baguette and brush with oil.
  2. Toast until golden.
  3. Slice the apples thinly.
  4. Top half with brie and apple, the rest with goat's cheese and a drizzle of honey.

Gaston's tavern devilled eggsVegetarian

Boiled eggs with a Dijon, mayo and chive yolk filling, dusted with paprika.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 eggs
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Boil the eggs until hard.
  2. Cool and peel.
  3. Halve the eggs and mash the yolks with mayonnaise, Dijon and chives until smooth.
  4. Pipe back into the whites.
  5. Dust with smoked paprika and pile onto one big platter.

Mrs Potts's coronation chicken sandwiches

Curried chicken mayonnaise with sultanas in soft white bread. Mrs Potts and Chip serving tea.

How it is made
  • 1 barbecue chicken
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 jar mild curry powder
  • 1 jar apricot jam
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 loaf soft white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 100g cream cheese
  1. Crustless finger sandwiches of curried coronation chicken with apricot and almonds, and cucumber with cream cheese, on a tiered stand. Shred 400g roast chicken. Mix 4 tbsp mayo, 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt, 2 tsp mild curry powder, 1 tbsp apricot jam, 40g toasted flaked almonds and chopped coriander, then fold through the chicken. Spread 12 slices of soft bread with butter. Make 4 rounds of coronation chicken and 2 of thin cucumber with 100g cream cheese. Trim the crusts and cut each round into 3 fingers.

Mains

Lumière's beef and mushroom braise

Beef chuck braised with bacon, baby onions and mushrooms.

Prep 51 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 12 baby onions, peeled
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Dice the bacon and beef and peel the baby onions.
  2. Brown the bacon in the olive oil, then brown the beef in batches.
  3. Add the onions and mushrooms and colour lightly.
  4. Stir in the flour and tomato paste, then add the stock, balsamic vinegar, thyme and bay leaves.
  5. Cover and simmer until the beef is tender.
  6. Peel and boil the potatoes, then mash with butter and warm milk.
  7. Serve the braise over the mash with crusty bread.

Maurice's leek and thyme chicken

Chicken braised with leeks, thyme and cream, for Maurice heading off to market.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 leeks
  • 20g butter
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Chicken thighs braised with leeks, thyme, mustard and a splash of cream until tender, with crusty bread to mop up every bit of sauce. Season 1kg chicken thighs and brown skin side down in a little oil for 6 minutes, then turn and remove. Soften 2 sliced leeks in 20g butter, add 2 crushed garlic cloves and thyme, then pour in 300ml chicken stock and 1 tbsp Dijon mustard. Return the chicken, cover and simmer for 30 minutes. Stir in 100ml cream and bubble for 5 minutes. Serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

Enchanted rose tartVegetarian

Sweet pastry case with custard and thin red apple slices in circles.

Prep 35 min, cook 40 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 500ml thick custard
  • 3 red apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 500ml water, for softening the apples
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  • 1 tbsp water, for the glaze
  1. Line a tart tin with the pastry.
  2. Blind bake at 180°C until pale gold.
  3. Cool the case.
  4. Slice the apples paper thin. Squeeze the lemon into the 500 ml of water, soften the slices in it briefly, then drain.
  5. Fill the case with custard.
  6. Arrange the apple slices in overlapping circles into a rose.
  7. Bake at 180°C.
  8. Warm the jam with 1 tbsp of water and brush over to glaze.
  9. Cool before slicing.

Drinks

Ballroom golden peach punchNo alcohol

Peach nectar and orange juice with sliced orange and fresh peach.

Prep 13 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix the nectar, orange juice and vanilla and chill the orange and peach slices in it up to 1 day ahead; add ice and sparkling apple juice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml chilled peach nectar
  • 3 oranges
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 fresh peach
  • 750ml chilled sparkling apple juice
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Juice two oranges and slice the third. Slice the peach.
  2. Mix the peach nectar, orange juice and vanilla in a big jug.
  3. Add the orange and peach slices and chill.
  4. Just before serving, add ice and top with sparkling apple juice. Stir gently and pour into wide glasses.
