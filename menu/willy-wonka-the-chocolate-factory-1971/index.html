Snacks

White chocolate teacups with creamVegetarian

White chocolate cups filled with vanilla whipped cream, with a chocolate handle.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the cups a day ahead, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 30g dark chocolate
  1. Melt the white chocolate and brush two coats inside small paper cups, setting between coats, 20 min.
  2. Chill until firm, then peel off the paper, 30 min.
  3. Whip the cream with the icing sugar and vanilla seeds and fill the cups.
  4. Pipe dark chocolate handles and stick them on. Eat the whole thing.

Snozzberry crackersVegetarian

Seeded crackers with cream cheese and crushed raspberries, blackberries, blueberries.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Berries up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g raspberries
  • 100g blackberries
  • 100g blueberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 16 seeded crackers
  • 200g ricotta
  1. Crush the raspberries, blackberries and blueberries with the sugar using a spoon.
  2. Whip the ricotta until smooth.
  3. Spread on the crackers and top with the berries. Taste them and decide what a snozzberry is.

Golden goose caramel eggs

Dark chocolate shells filled with caramel ganache and brushed with gold lustre, Veruca wants one now.

How it is made
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 1 tin caramel
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 jar edible gold lustre
  1. Dark chocolate egg shells filled with caramel ganache, joined and brushed with edible gold lustre until they shine like golden eggs. Melt 300g dark chocolate gently and brush into 16 half-egg moulds in two coats, chilling between, then set. Heat 80ml cream and pour over 100g chopped milk chocolate, then stir in 3 tbsp thick caramel and a pinch of sea salt and cool until thick. Fill the shells, warm the rims on a hot tray and press halves together. Chill, unmould and brush with edible gold lustre.

Mains

Bucket house stew

Beef shin, carrots, potatoes and pearl barley braised 3 hours, with bread.

Prep 33 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef shin, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 400g potatoes
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 1.2 L beef stock
  • 1 Italian loaf
  1. Brown the beef in batches, 15 min.
  2. Soften the onions, then add the carrots, potatoes, pearl barley and stock.
  3. Cover and braise at 160°C until thick and tender, 3 hr.
  4. Serve with slices of the Italian loaf to stretch it further.

Chocolate room edible garden board

Marshmallow mushrooms, choc strawberries, toffee apple and honeycomb rocks, pure imagination.

How it is made
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 250g strawberries
  • 1 packet pretzel sticks
  • 12 large marshmallows
  • 2 red apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tin caramel
  • 150g honeycomb
  • 150g chocolate biscuits
  • 1 tub fairy floss
  1. A big board planted like a garden: toasted marshmallow mushrooms on pretzel stalks, chocolate strawberries, toffee apple wedges, honeycomb rocks and a fairy floss cloud. Melt 200g dark chocolate. Dip 250g strawberries halfway and set on baking paper. Push pretzel sticks into 12 large marshmallows, toast the tops with a blowtorch or under the grill and dip the bases in chocolate. Cut 2 apples into wedges, toss in lemon juice and drizzle with 3 tbsp warm caramel. Break 150g honeycomb into rocks. Arrange on a board with crushed chocolate biscuit soil and fairy floss in the middle.

Sweets

Gobstopper trufflesVegetarian

Cherries wrapped in white then dark ganache, rolled in cocoa and sprinkles.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 12 maraschino cherries
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 4 tbsp coloured sugar
  1. Heat part of the cream and pour over the white chocolate to make ganache, 10 min.
  2. Chill until firm enough to handle, 1 hr.
  3. Wrap each drained cherry in white ganache and chill again, 20 min.
  4. Heat the rest of the cream, pour over the dark chocolate and cool to a dark ganache, 15 min.
  5. Wrap the white balls in dark ganache and chill, 1 hr.
  6. Roll in cocoa and coloured sugar. Bite to see the layers.

Drinks

Fizzy lifting raspberry lemon sodaNo alcohol

Raspberry and lemon with soda water and a scoop of lemon sorbet.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Blitz and strain the raspberries and mix with the lemon juice and sugar syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; top with soda water and sorbet at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 1 L soda water
  • 4 scoops of lemon sorbet
  1. Blitz the raspberries and strain.
  2. Mix with the lemon juice and sugar syrup and divide between four tall glasses.
  3. Top with very cold soda water and drop in a scoop of lemon sorbet so it fizzes up. Don't burp.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Charlie and Grandpa Joe sneak a taste in the bubble room.
