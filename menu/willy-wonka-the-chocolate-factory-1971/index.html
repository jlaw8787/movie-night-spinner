Snacks

White chocolate teacups with creamVegetarian

White chocolate cups filled with vanilla whipped cream, with a chocolate handle

Prep 40 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the cups a day ahead, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 30g dark chocolate
  1. Melt the white chocolate and brush two coats inside small paper cups, setting between coats.
  2. Chill until firm, then peel off the paper.
  3. Whip the cream with the icing sugar and vanilla seeds and fill the cups.
  4. Pipe dark chocolate handles and stick them on. Eat the whole thing.

Snozzberry crackersVegetarian

Seeded crackers with whipped ricotta and crushed raspberries, blackberries, blueberries

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Berries up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g raspberries
  • 100g blackberries
  • 100g blueberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 16 seeded crackers
  • 200g ricotta
  1. Crush the raspberries, blackberries and blueberries with the sugar using a spoon.
  2. Whip the ricotta until smooth.
  3. Spread on the crackers and top with the berries. Taste them and decide what a snozzberry is.

Mains

Bucket house stew

Beef shin, carrots, potatoes and pearl barley braised 3 hours, with bread

Prep 30 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef shin, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 400g potatoes
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 1.2l beef stock
  • 1 Italian loaf
  1. Brown the beef in batches.
  2. Soften the onions, then add the carrots, potatoes, pearl barley and stock.
  3. Cover and braise at 160C until thick and tender.
  4. Serve with slices of the Italian loaf to stretch it further.

Sweets

Gobstopper trufflesVegetarian

Cherries wrapped in white then dark ganache, rolled in cocoa and sprinkles

Prep 60 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 12 maraschino cherries
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 4 tbsp coloured sugar
  1. Heat part of the cream and pour over the white chocolate to make ganache.
  2. Chill until firm enough to handle.
  3. Wrap each drained cherry in white ganache and chill again.
  4. Heat the rest of the cream, pour over the dark chocolate and cool to a dark ganache.
  5. Wrap the white balls in dark ganache and chill.
  6. Roll in cocoa and coloured sugar. Bite to see the layers.

Drinks

Fizzy lifting raspberry lemon sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Raspberry and lemon with soda water and a scoop of lemon sorbet

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Blitz and strain the raspberries and mix with the lemon juice and sugar syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; top with soda water and sorbet at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 1l soda water
  • 4 scoops lemon sorbet
  1. Blitz the raspberries and strain.
  2. Mix with the lemon juice and sugar syrup and divide between four tall glasses.
  3. Top with very cold soda water and drop in a scoop of lemon sorbet so it fizzes up. Don't burp.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Charlie and Grandpa Joe sneak a taste of the fizzy lifting drinks.
