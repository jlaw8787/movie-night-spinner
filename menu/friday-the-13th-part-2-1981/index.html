Snacks

Last night in town spiced bar nutsVegetarian

Mixed nuts roasted with brown sugar, smoked paprika, cayenne and rosemary.

Prep 0 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week ahead in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 400g mixed raw nuts
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbsp rosemary leaves, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a tray with baking paper, 3 min.
  2. Toss 400g mixed nuts with 30g melted butter, 2 tbsp brown sugar, the paprika, cayenne and rosemary, 4 min.
  3. Spread on the tray and roast, stirring halfway, until golden and fragrant.
  4. Sprinkle with the salt and leave to cool on the tray so the coating sets crisp, 20 min.

Crystal Lake beer-battered onion ringsVegetarian

Thick onion rings in a crisp beer batter with ranch dip.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; slice the onions and mix the ranch dip up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large brown onions, sliced into thick rings
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp chipotle chilli powder
  1. Separate the onion rings and toss in 2 tbsp of the flour.
  2. Whisk the rest of the flour with the cornflour, baking powder and salt, then whisk in 250ml cold lager to a thick batter, 3 min.
  3. Heat the oil in a deep pot to 180°C, 10 min.
  4. Dip the rings in the batter and fry in batches for about 3 minutes until golden, then drain on paper towel.
  5. Stir the chipotle powder into the mayonnaise and serve with the hot rings.

Mains

Camp Blood chilli con carne

Beef chilli with beans over rice, topped with grated cheddar.

Prep 3 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead, it is better on day two; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 800g beef chuck, cut into 2cm pieces
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp chipotle chilli powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 375ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 400g can kidney beans, drained
  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain rice
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a heavy pot and brown 800g diced beef chuck in batches over high heat, then lift out, 12 min.
  2. Turn the heat down and fry the onion, garlic and red capsicum in the same pot until soft.
  3. Stir in 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 tsp chipotle powder, 1 tsp oregano and 2 tbsp tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Return the beef with 800g crushed tomatoes, 375ml beef stock and 1 tsp salt, cover and simmer on low until the beef falls apart.
  5. Add the drained kidney beans and simmer uncovered until thick. Meanwhile rinse 1.5 cups rice and simmer with 2.25 cups water, covered, until the water is absorbed.
  6. Serve the chilli over the rice with a spoon of sour cream and a handful of grated cheddar.

Sweets

Sack-head s'mores barsVegetarian

Biscuit base, chocolate ganache, toasted marshmallow.

Prep 5 min, cook 17 min, then 80 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the base and chocolate layer up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; toast the marshmallows just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 200g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 250g white marshmallows
  1. Mix the crushed biscuits with the melted butter and press firmly into a lined 20cm square tin.
  2. Chill until firm.
  3. Heat the cream until steaming, pour over the chocolate, stir until smooth and spread over the base.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Cover with the marshmallows and grill on high until toasted, watching closely.
  6. Cool for a few minutes and cut into 12 bars with a hot knife, 5 min.

Drinks

Crystal Lake bourbon lemonade

Bourbon, lemon, honey and soda, poured for the walk into the woods.

Prep 6 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup and squeeze the lemons up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 80ml honey
  • 80ml boiling water
  • 400ml soda water, chilled
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  1. Stir the honey into the boiling water until dissolved and leave to cool.
  2. Shake the bourbon, lemon juice and honey syrup hard with a handful of ice.
  3. Strain into 4 ice-filled glasses, top with soda water and add a lemon slice.
