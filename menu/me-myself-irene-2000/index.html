Rhode Island clear clam chowder

Clear broth, clams, potato and bacon.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the broth with the potatoes up to a day ahead; add the clams when you reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 3 rashers of bacon, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 500g waxy potatoes, diced
  • 2 x 290g tins of baby clams, juice kept
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 2 thyme sprigs
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 cup small salted crackers
  1. Dice the vegetables, then fry the bacon until crisp, add the onion and celery and soften, 20 min.
  2. Add the potatoes, clam juice, stock and thyme and simmer until the potatoes are tender, 20 min.
  3. Stir in the clams and warm through, then season with lots of pepper, 3 min.
  4. Finish with parsley and serve with crackers.

Hank's black bean chilli nachosVegetarian

Smoky black beans, cheese, jalapenos, sour cream.

Prep 4 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the bean chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; bake the nachos to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 400g tins of black beans, drained
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 230g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1/4 cup pickled jalapenos
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 avocado
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in a little oil, add the spices, beans and tomatoes and simmer until thick, 15 min.
  2. Layer the corn chips, chilli and cheese on a tray and bake at 200°C until the cheese melts, 10 min.
  3. Top with jalapenos, sour cream and smashed avocado.

Lucky prize cow sirloin

Grilled sirloin with a creamy pepper sauce and fries.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the pepper sauce up to a day ahead and reheat; grill the steaks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 250g sirloin steaks
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg frozen shoestring fries
  • 20g butter
  • 1 French shallot, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 125ml beef stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  1. Bake the fries at 220°C until crisp, 25 min.
  2. Rub the steaks with oil and salt and grill over high heat, 3 to 4 minutes a side, then rest.
  3. Soften the shallot in the butter, add the pepper, stock, cream and mustard and bubble until thick, 8 min.
  4. Serve the steaks with pepper sauce and fries.

Hank's free foot-long hot franks

Rhode Island wieners, meat sauce, onion, celery salt. Hank yells: free hot dogs, all you can eat.

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the meat sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; cook the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 long frankfurts
  • 8 long split-top bread rolls
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced, plus 1 more for topping
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 250ml water
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp celery salt
  1. Brown the mince with the diced onion, breaking it up very finely, 10 min.
  2. Add the paprika, cumin, allspice, water and salt and simmer until saucy, 20 min.
  3. Simmer the franks in hot water and warm the rolls, 5 min.
  4. Fill the rolls with franks, mustard, meat sauce, chopped raw onion and a shake of celery salt.

Lieutenant Baileygates' apple pieVegetarian

Apple pie, cheddar crust, vanilla ice cream.

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make and chill the pastry up to 2 days ahead; bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 180g cold butter, cubed
  • 60g sharp cheddar, finely grated
  • 4 tbsp iced water
  • 6 Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, stir in the cheddar and enough iced water to bring it together, then chill, 15 min.
  2. Toss the apples with the sugar, cornflour and cinnamon.
  3. Roll out two thirds of the pastry to line a 23cm pie dish, fill with apples and top with the rest, 10 min.
  4. Brush with egg, cut steam holes and bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling, 45 min.
  5. Cool a little and serve with ice cream, 5 min.

The triplets' study-night coffee milkNo alcohol

Coffee milk over ice, study fuel for the genius boys.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the coffee syrup up to 2 weeks ahead and keep it in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml strong brewed coffee
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 1 L cold full-cream milk
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Simmer the coffee and sugar until slightly thickened, then cool, 5 min.
  2. Stir 3 tablespoons of syrup into each glass of cold milk.
  3. Add ice and serve.
