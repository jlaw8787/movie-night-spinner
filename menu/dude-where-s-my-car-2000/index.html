Tattoo parlour pork spring rolls

Crisp rolls of ground pork, cabbage and carrot in oyster sauce.

Prep 29 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Cook the filling and roll up to 1 day ahead, covered and chilled; deep fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1/4 savoy cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 16 spring roll wrappers
  • 150ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Fry the pork mince until browned, add the finely shredded cabbage, the grated carrot, the crushed garlic cloves, the oyster sauce and the soy, and cook 3 minutes.
  2. Cool completely.
  3. Roll the filling in each of the spring roll wrappers, sealing the edge with a little water.
  4. Deep fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C for 3 to 4 minutes until golden, then drain on paper towel.
  5. Stir the juice of the lime into the sweet chilli sauce and serve with coriander.

Zoltan's popping candy caramel cornVegetarian

Baked caramel popcorn with white chocolate and popping candy.

Prep 8 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and store airtight; add the popping candy just before serving so it keeps its pop
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 100g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 60ml golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 100g white chocolate
  • 1 bottle blue food colouring
  • 2 packets of popping candy
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Pop the popcorn kernels in the oil and tip into a big bowl, picking out any unpopped kernels.
  2. Boil the butter, the brown sugar and the golden syrup for 4 minutes, take off the heat and stir in the bicarbonate of soda and a pinch of salt.
  3. Pour over the popcorn, toss and spread on a lined tray.
  4. Bake at 120°C for 20 minutes, stirring once.
  5. Melt the white chocolate with the blue colouring, drizzle over and scatter with the popping candy, 4 min.

Drive-through lemon chicken and fried rice

Crispy chicken in lemon sauce, egg fried rice and garlic broccoli.

Prep 8 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Cook and chill the rice the day before and make the lemon sauce up to 2 days ahead; fry the chicken just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 eggs
  • 120g cornflour
  • 3 lemons
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 500ml vegetable oil
  • 150ml water
  1. Cook the jasmine rice the day before and chill.
  2. Cut the chicken thigh into strips, dip in the beaten egg, then toss in the cornflour.
  3. Shallow fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C for 5 minutes until crisp.
  4. Simmer the juice of the lemons, the caster sugar, the water and the cornflour until glossy, and pour over, 18 min.
  5. Fry the cold rice in a hot wok with the scrambled eggs, the peas, the soy and the spring onions, 11 min.
  6. Stir-fry the head broccoli with the garlic cloves for 3 minutes.

'And then?' beef and black bean noodles

Wok-fried strips with bell pepper and onion in a glossy fermented sauce, over thick egg noodles.

Prep 7 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Slice and marinate the beef up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef rump, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp black bean sauce
  • 1 green capsicum, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 500g fresh hokkien noodles
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Toss the beef with the cornflour and 1 tbsp soy.
  2. Stir-fry the beef in half the oil in a very hot wok until browned, then lift out, 4 min.
  3. Stir-fry the onion, capsicum and garlic in the rest of the oil, add the noodles, black bean sauce and the rest of the soy, 5 min.
  4. Toss the beef back through and top with spring onion.

Fridge full of chocolate pudding potsVegetarian

Silky stovetop chocolate custard set cold in little pots, a spoon in each.

Prep 3 min, cook 15 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 30g cornflour
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 tbsp salted caramel sauce
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 4 chocolate biscuits
  1. Whisk the caster sugar, the cocoa, the cornflour and a pinch of salt in a saucepan, then slowly whisk in the milk.
  2. Stir over medium heat until it boils and thickens, about 6 minutes.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the chopped dark chocolate and the vanilla, 3 min.
  4. Pour into jars, press cling film on top and chill 2 hours.
  5. Top each with a spoon of caramel sauce, whipped cream from the thickened cream and the crushed chocolate biscuits.

Drive-through lychee lime slushieNo alcohol

Frozen lychee, lime and lemon-lime soda blended into a slush.

Prep 6 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the lychees flat on a tray up to 1 week ahead and keep frozen; blend with the lime, ice and lemonade to serve
Recipe for 4
  • a 565g tin lychees
  • 4 limes
  • 1 bag ice
  • 600ml lemonade
  1. Drain the tin of lychees, keeping the syrup, and freeze the fruit flat on a tray for at least 3 hours.
  2. Blend the frozen lychees with the syrup, the juice of the limes and the ice until smooth and slushy.
  3. Add the cold lemonade and pulse twice to mix without losing the fizz.
  4. Pour into big cups and finish each with a lime wheel and a straw.
