Snacks

Soup kitchen cheddar and onion rollsVegetarian

The Reverend's shelter, ready for dunking into the stew

Prep 30 min, cook 140 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 70g butter
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 150g sharp cheddar, grated
  1. Fry the onion in some of the butter until golden.
  2. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, sugar, warm milk and the rest of the melted butter. Knead.
  3. Rise in a covered bowl until doubled.
  4. Knead in the onion and most of the cheddar. Shape 12 rolls and pack into a tin.
  5. Rise again.
  6. Top with the remaining cheddar and bake at 200C.

Manhole cover mini pizzas

Round, cheesy and heavy

Prep 25 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut the dough rounds up to 1 day ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; top and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 80g pepperoni
  1. Roll the dough thin and cut 12 rounds.
  2. Spread with sauce and top with mozzarella.
  3. Slice the pepperoni into thin strips and lay them in a crosshatch like a manhole grate.
  4. Bake at 230C until bubbling.

The Reverend's soup kitchen stew

Hearty, nothing from below ground

Prep 30 min, cook 155 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery sticks, chopped
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1l beef stock
  • 600g potatoes, in chunks
  • 1 loaf Italian bread
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  2. Add the onions, carrots, celery and tomato paste and cook.
  3. Add the stock, cover and braise at 160C.
  4. Add the potatoes and braise for the last hour.
  5. Serve with Italian bread.

Western omelette

Three eggs round ham, capsicum and onion, crisp potatoes and rye toast, for Bosch's night shift

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Dice the ham, capsicum and onion and par-boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g waxy potatoes
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 150g leg ham, diced
  • 12 eggs
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 slices rye bread, toasted
  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins for 12 minutes, cool a little and cut into chunks.
  2. Fry the potatoes in the oil with the paprika and half the salt until crisp, about 12 minutes, for the home fries.
  3. Soften the capsicum and onion in a little of the butter, then add the ham and warm through.
  4. For each omelette, beat 3 eggs with a pinch of salt, cook in butter until just set, fill with a quarter of the ham mix and fold.
  5. Repeat for the other three and serve with the home fries and rye toast.

Manhattan cider doughnutsVegetarian

Soft cake rings made with reduced apple juice, tossed in cinnamon sugar

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml apple juice
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 100g sugar
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1l vegetable oil for frying
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Simmer the apple juice until reduced to a quarter and cool.
  2. Mix with the butter, egg, sugar and flour into a soft dough.
  3. Pat out and cut 12 rings.
  4. Fry in oil at 175C, turning once.
  5. Toss in caster sugar and cinnamon.

Lime cordial topped with tonic, which glows under a black light, like what's under the manhole

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best made to order; chill the tonic water up to 1 day ahead so it holds its fizz over the ice
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml lime cordial
  • 1l tonic water
  • 1kg ice
  1. Pour cordial into four tall glasses over ice.
  2. Top with tonic water and switch off the lights so it glows under a black light.
