Snacks

Cheddar & Onion RollsVegetarian

A pull-apart pan, baked this morning with sharp cheese on top.

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min, then 90 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 70g butter
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 150g sharp cheddar, grated
  1. Fry the onion in some of the butter until golden.
  2. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, sugar, warm milk and the rest of the melted butter. Knead, 12 min.
  3. Rise in a covered bowl until doubled.
  4. Knead in the onion and most of the cheddar. Shape 12 rolls and pack into a tin.
  5. Rise again, 30 min.
  6. Top with the remaining cheddar and bake at 200°C, 20 min.

Manhole Cover Pizzas

Little rounds of tomato sauce and mozzarella, pepperoni in a crosshatch.

Prep 25 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 80g pepperoni
  1. Roll the dough thin and cut 12 rounds.
  2. Spread with sauce and top with mozzarella.
  3. Slice the pepperoni into thin strips and lay them in a crosshatch like a manhole grate.
  4. Bake at 230°C until bubbling.

Mains

Beef Stew

Chuck braised for hours with carrot, celery, onion and potato. Italian bread.

Prep 19 min, cook 2.5 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery sticks, chopped
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 L beef stock
  • 600g potatoes, in chunks
  • 1 loaf Italian bread
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the beef in batches in oil, 15 min.
  2. Add the onions, carrots, celery and tomato paste and cook, 5 min.
  3. Add the stock, cover and braise at 160°C, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Add the potatoes and braise for the last hour, 1 hr.
  5. Serve with Italian bread.

Sweets

Cider DoughnutsVegetarian

Boiled-down apple juice in the batter, rolled in cinnamon sugar.

Prep 18 min, cook 37 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml apple juice
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 100g sugar
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Simmer the apple juice until reduced to a quarter and cool.
  2. Mix with the butter, egg, sugar and flour into a soft dough.
  3. Pat out and cut 12 rings.
  4. Fry in oil at 175°C, turning once, 12 min.
  5. Toss in caster sugar and cinnamon.

Lime CoolerNo alcohol

Lime cordial and tonic water over a glass full of ice. Glows green under the sign.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml lime cordial
  • 1 L tonic water
  • 1kg ice
  1. Pour cordial into four tall glasses over ice.
  2. Top with tonic water and switch off the lights so it glows under a black light.
