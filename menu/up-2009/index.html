Wilderness Explorer honey trail mix barsVegetarian

Toasted oat bars bound with honey, peanut butter and butter

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 5 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g rolled oats
  • 150g honey
  • 120g peanut butter
  • 60g butter
  • 80g roasted peanuts
  • 80g sultanas
  • 50g banana chips, crushed
  • 80g chocolate chips
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a 20 x 30cm tin. Toast the oats for 10 minutes.
  2. Warm the honey, peanut butter and butter until smooth. Stir in the oats, peanuts, sultanas and banana chips.
  3. Cool the mixture for 10 minutes, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Press firmly into the tin and bake for 15 minutes.
  5. Cool completely before cutting into squares.

Choux puffs with sharp cheese and mustard, crisp outside, hollow inside, served by the bunch

How it is made
  • 125ml milk
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g sharp cheddar
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  1. Light, puffy choux balls with sharp cheddar and a little mustard baked in, crisp outside and hollow inside, served warm by the bunch. Heat the oven to 200°C. Bring 125ml water, 125ml milk, 100g butter and a pinch of salt to the boil. Tip in 150g plain flour and beat over the heat for 2 minutes until it leaves the pan. Cool 5 minutes, then beat in 4 eggs one at a time until glossy. Stir in 120g grated cheddar and 1 tsp Dijon. Pipe 30 small balls on lined trays, top with a little more cheese and bake for 22 minutes until puffed and golden.

Muntz's roast sirloin dinner

Roast sirloin with roast potatoes, rich gravy and green beans

Prep 5 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Reduce the beef stock up to 2 days ahead and reheat, whisking in the butter at the end; roast the beef and potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef sirloin
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 50g butter
  • 300g green beans
  1. Roast the potatoes with the oil at 200°C until golden.
  2. Roast the sirloin at 200°C to 52°C inside, then rest.
  3. Reduce the beef stock to a sauce and whisk in some butter.
  4. Steam the beans and toss in butter.

Paradise Falls shredded chicken arepasOn the printed menu

Corn cakes stuffed with chicken and avocado, coriander sauce, for the trip Ellie dreamed of

How it is made
  • 600g chicken breast
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 300g pre-cooked white corn flour
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Crisp golden corn arepas split open and stuffed with shredded chicken and avocado salad, with grated cheese and a fresh green coriander sauce. Poach 600g chicken breast for 15 minutes and shred. Mix with 1 mashed avocado, 3 tbsp mayonnaise, lime juice, coriander and salt. Mix 300g pre-cooked white corn flour with 450ml warm water and 1 tsp salt, rest 5 minutes and shape 8 discs 1.5cm thick. Cook in an oiled pan over medium heat for 6 minutes a side until crisp. Blend coriander, 1 green chilli, garlic, lime and 80ml oil for the sauce. Split the arepas and fill.

Grape soda badge jellies

Grape juice jellies set in small round moulds

Prep 15 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 gelatine leaves
  • 500ml grape juice
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 100ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water.
  2. Warm the grape juice with the sugar and a squeeze of lemon and stir in the squeezed gelatine.
  3. Pour into small round moulds and set in the fridge for 3 hours.
  4. Turn out and top each with a dot of whipped cream.

Kevin's chocolateVegetarian

Dark chocolate bark with hazelnuts, dried cherries and flaky salt

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Set in the fridge and break into shards up to 1 week ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 60g hazelnuts, toasted
  • 60g dried cherries
  • 1 pinch flaky sea salt
  1. Melt the chocolate and spread thin on a lined tray.
  2. Scatter with hazelnuts, cherries and flaky salt.
  3. Set in the fridge, then break into shards. Save some for the bird.

Ellie's grape soda floatsVegetarianNo alcohol

Grape juice and soda with lime, vanilla ice cream and frozen grapes

Prep 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the grapes up to 1 week ahead in a bag; mix the soda and build the floats on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g red grapes
  • 600ml purple grape juice
  • 400ml chilled soda water
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  1. Freeze the grapes for 2 hours.
  2. Mix the grape juice with the soda water and a squeeze of lime.
  3. Put ice cream in each tall glass and pour the grape soda over slowly so it foams up.
  4. Drop in a few frozen grapes and serve with a straw and a long spoon.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Carl and Russell sit on the kerb with their ice creams.
