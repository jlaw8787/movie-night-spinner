Sugar puck mini jam doughnutsVegetarian

Yeast doughnuts rolled in sugar and piped with raspberry jam.

Prep 40 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 350g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 180ml milk
  • 30g butter
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Warm the milk with the butter and a little of the sugar, then mix with the yeast, egg, flour and salt.
  2. Knead until smooth and elastic.
  3. Prove until doubled.
  4. Roll into 20 balls and prove again.
  5. Fry in the oil at 175°C for a couple of minutes, turning, until golden.
  6. Roll in the remaining sugar while warm, then poke a hole in each and pipe in raspberry jam.

Product placement pizza bites

Pizza rounds, box to the camera.

Prep 9 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cut the rounds up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; top and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 jar pizza sauce
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 16 slices of pepperoni (100g)
  1. Heat the oven to 230°C. Roll out the dough and cut into 16 rounds.
  2. Spread each with pizza sauce, add mozzarella and a slice of pepperoni.
  3. Bake until bubbling.
  4. Present one to the camera with a cheesy grin before eating.

Mirthmobile cheesy chilli popcorn

Popcorn tossed in butter, cheddar and chilli.

How it is made
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 60g butter
  • 4 tbsp cheddar cheese powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • chilli powder
  • 100g small pretzels
  1. Big batch of popcorn tossed in butter, cheddar powder, chilli and smoked paprika, mixed with salty pretzels and passed round the back seat. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large pot, add 120g popcorn kernels, cover and shake over medium heat until the popping slows. Melt 60g butter and pour over the popcorn. Mix 4 tbsp cheddar cheese powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika and half a tsp chilli powder and toss through until coated. Mix in 100g small pretzels and serve in a big bowl.

Mirthmobile pepperoni pizza squares

Thin crust squares of tomato, mozzarella and pepperoni.

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stretch the dough, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 jar pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 150g mushrooms, sliced
  1. Heat the oven to its highest setting with a steel or tray inside.
  2. Stretch the dough into two thin bases.
  3. Top with sauce, mozzarella, pepperoni and mushrooms.
  4. Bake until the crust blisters. Cut into squares.

Noah's Arcade loaded chilli dogs

Blistered frankfurts in steamed buns, beef chilli, cheddar, jalapeños and onion, party on.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 8 franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • yellow mustard
  1. Franks griddled until blistered in steamed buns, loaded with beef chilli, melted cheddar, pickled jalapeños, diced onion and a stripe of mustard. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 diced onion, add 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 200ml water and simmer 25 minutes. Griddle 8 franks for 6 minutes until blistered. Steam 8 hot dog buns. Fill each with a frank, a spoon of chilli and grated cheddar and grill 1 minute to melt. Top with jalapeños, diced onion and mustard.

Stan Mikita's crullersVegetarian

Fried choux pastry dipped in honey icing.

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Best eaten the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water, for the dough
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp water, for the glaze
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute. Cool.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Pipe rings onto squares of baking paper.
  4. Fry in the oil at 180°C, paper and all, until puffed, then lift the paper out.
  5. Whisk the icing sugar with the honey and water and dip the crullers.

Aurora cherry cola floatNo alcohol

Cherry syrup, ice cream and cola, for a Crucial Taunt night at the Gasworks.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses first
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml cherry syrup
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 1 L cold cola
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Chill four big glasses in the freezer.
  2. Add cherry syrup and two scoops of ice cream to each.
  3. Pour the cola slowly down the side so the foam rises without spilling.
  4. Top with a cherry and serve with straws.
