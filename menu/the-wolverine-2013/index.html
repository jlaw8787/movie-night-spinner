Snacks

Bullet train rice balls

Sushi rice triangles around flaked grilled salmon, wrapped in nori

Prep 23 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Grill and flake the salmon up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the rice and shape the rice balls on the day, best eaten the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cup sushi rice
  • 200g salmon fillet
  • 4 sheets nori
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  • salt, to taste
  1. Rinse and cook the rice, then let it cool until warm.
  2. Salt and grill the salmon, then flake it.
  3. Shape the rice into triangles around a little salmon.
  4. Wrap the base in nori and sprinkle with sesame.
  5. Pack into a bento box with pickled ginger.

Tokyo yakitori

Chicken thigh skewers grilled with a soy and mirin glaze

Prep 17 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Thread the skewers and simmer the glaze up to 1 day ahead, chilled; grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Soak 12 bamboo skewers in water. Cut the chicken into pieces and thread onto skewers.
  2. Simmer the soy, mirin and sugar until syrupy.
  3. Grill the skewers, basting with the glaze, until sticky and charred.
  4. Scatter with sliced spring onion.

Mains

Snowy village ramen

Braised pork belly, miso broth, ramen noodles and soft boiled eggs

Prep 25 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Braise the pork belly up to 2 days ahead in its liquid in the fridge; reheat, slice, and cook the eggs and noodles on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • 4 portions ramen noodles
  • 3 tbsp white miso
  • 1l chicken stock
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 spring onions
  • 4 sheets nori
  1. Braise the pork belly in soy and water, then slice.
  2. Simmer the pork.
  3. Soft-boil the eggs, cool and halve.
  4. Whisk the miso into hot stock.
  5. Cook the noodles and build the bowls with pork, eggs, nori and spring onion.

Sweets

Matcha ice cream with sesame brittleVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream folded with matcha, topped with sesame brittle

Prep 18 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp matcha powder
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp black sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Soften the ice cream.
  2. Whisk the matcha with the hot water into a paste and fold through the ice cream.
  3. Refreeze.
  4. Melt the sugar in a pan without stirring until golden, stir in the sesame seeds and pour onto baking paper.
  5. Cool the brittle, then break into shards.
  6. Scoop the ice cream and top with brittle and honey.

Drinks

Warm sake with yuzu and gingerVegetarian

Sake warmed with sliced ginger and yuzu

Prep 4 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best warmed just before serving; slice the ginger up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml sake
  • 4 slices fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp yuzu juice
  • 1 tsp honey
  1. Pour the sake into a heatproof jug with the ginger.
  2. Stand the jug in a pan of hot water over low heat until warm to the touch, about 50C, never boiling.
  3. Stir in the yuzu and honey and pour into small cups.

When to serve
