Snacks

Cheese DanishVegetarian

Breakfast in the transit dispatch room.

Prep 17 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 60g icing sugar
  1. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, egg yolk and lemon zest. Cut the pastry into 8 squares.
  2. Spoon the filling into the middle of each square and fold in the corners.
  3. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  4. Cool, then drizzle with the icing sugar mixed with lemon juice.

Jelly DoughnutVegetarian

Jam doughnuts for the night shift at Garber's command centre.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 8 jam doughnuts
  • 80g caster sugar
  1. Warm the doughnuts on a tray at 160°C.
  2. Roll each straight away in the sugar so it sticks and serve on a plate in the middle of the table.

Mr Green's sesame lavash with feta dip

Paper-thin, for Mr Green, the hijacker with the cold.

How it is made
  • 4 sheets lavash
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 200g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • olive oil
  • flaky sea salt
  • chilli flakes
  1. Paper-thin lavash brushed with oil, salt and sesame, baked crisp and snapped into shards, with lemony feta dip for dipping. Brush 4 sheets lavash with 3 tbsp olive oil, sprinkle with 2 tbsp sesame seeds and flaky salt. Bake at 180°C for 8 minutes until crisp, cool and snap into shards. Blitz 200g feta with 100g Greek yoghurt, the zest of 1 lemon, 1 tbsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp olive oil until smooth. Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with oil and scatter with chilli flakes.

Mains

Turkey & Swiss Hero

Long roll of turkey, Swiss, iceberg and tomato wrapped tight, for the transit cop on radio.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long rolls
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 400g sliced turkey
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Split the rolls and spread with mayonnaise.
  2. Layer with turkey, Swiss cheese, lettuce and tomato. Season.
  3. Close, wrap tightly in paper and cut in half.

Sweets

RugelachVegetarian

Cream cheese pastry crescents rolled with apricot jam, walnuts and cinnamon.

Prep 20 min, cook 22 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 225g butter
  • 225g cream cheese
  • 280g plain flour
  • 1 jar apricot jam
  • 100g walnuts, chopped
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Rub the butter and cream cheese into the flour to form a dough.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll into circles, spread with jam, walnuts and cinnamon sugar, cut into wedges and roll up from the wide end.
  4. Bake at 180°C until golden.

Drinks

Hot Toddy

Scotch, honey, ginger and citrus topped with boiling water, cinnamon stick.

Prep 5 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml whisky
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 piece ginger, about 3cm
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 600ml just-boiled water
  1. Thinly slice the ginger and juice the lemons, keeping a few wheels.
  2. Into each of 4 heatproof glasses put whisky, honey, lemon juice and ginger slices, top with boiling water and stir until the honey dissolves. Add a lemon wheel and a cinnamon stick.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the four men in moustaches board the train.
