Snacks

Island platter of haloumi, feta and figsVegetarian

Griddled haloumi, honey thyme feta, olives, fresh figs and warm pita.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 250g haloumi, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g feta
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • 1 cup olives
  • 4 figs, halved
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Griddle the haloumi until golden and squeeze lemon over.
  2. Drizzle the feta with honey and thyme.
  3. Warm the pitas.
  4. Arrange everything on a big platter with the olives and figs.

London 1918 pasties

Beef, potato and onion in crimped shortcrust, English mustard on the side.

Prep 18 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 potato, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • English mustard, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the mince, potato, onion and pepper.
  2. Cut the pastry into rounds, fill and fold into half moons.
  3. Crimp, egg wash and bake.
  4. Serve with English mustard.

Veld frites with garlic mayonnaiseOn the printed menu

Thick-cut potatoes double fried until crisp, salted, in paper cones Belgian style with thick aioli.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  1. Thick-cut potatoes double fried until crisp and golden, salted and served Belgian style in paper cones with a thick garlic mayonnaise. Cut 1.2kg floury potatoes into 1cm batons, rinse and dry well. Fry at 140°C for 6 minutes until soft but pale, drain and rest 20 minutes. Fry again at 180°C for 3 minutes until deep golden and crisp. Salt well. Mix 150g mayonnaise with 1 grated garlic clove, a squeeze of lemon and 1 tsp Dijon. Serve the frites in paper cones with the mayo on top.

Mains

Liberation night stew

Beef chuck braised 3 hours in dark beer and Dijon, crisp double-fried fries.

Prep 20 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Stew up to 2 days ahead, fry the fries fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, for browning
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 375ml dark beer
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in chips
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Brown the beef in batches in the browning oil.
  2. Cook the onions slowly.
  3. Add the beer, thyme, bay leaves and mustard and braise at 160°C.
  4. Fry the potatoes in the frying oil at 140°C, then again at 180°C until crisp.
  5. Serve the stew with the frites.

Charlie's Cullen skinkOn the printed menu

Smoked cod, leek and potato, cream and chives, oatcakes, for the sniper's song at the piano.

How it is made
  • 500g smoked cod
  • 600ml milk
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 2 leeks
  • 1 brown onion
  • 60g butter
  • 600g potatoes
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 packet oatcakes
  1. Scotland's smoked fish soup: smoked cod poached in milk with leek and potato, finished with cream and chives, with buttered oatcakes. Poach 500g smoked cod in 600ml milk with a bay leaf for 5 minutes, lift out and flake, keeping the milk. Soften 2 sliced leeks and 1 onion in 40g butter for 8 minutes. Add 600g diced potatoes and 400ml water and simmer 15 minutes. Mash about a third of the potato into the soup, then add the poaching milk, 100ml cream and the fish. Warm through, season with pepper and scatter chives. Serve with oatcakes and butter.

Diana's raspberry ripple ice cream conesVegetarian

Homemade vanilla custard ice cream rippled with raspberry sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min, then 4.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the ice cream a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 120g caster sugar, for the ice cream
  • 200g frozen raspberries
  • 50g caster sugar, for the sauce
  • 8 ice cream cones
  1. Heat the milk and cream with the split vanilla bean.
  2. Whisk the yolks and the ice cream sugar, whisk in the hot milk and cook to 82°C, stirring.
  3. Strain and chill overnight, then churn.
  4. Simmer the raspberries with the sugar for the sauce, sieve and cool.
  5. Layer the ice cream and sauce, swirl once with a knife and freeze.
  6. Scoop into cones.

Drinks

Veld Belgian ale with orange and honeyVegetarian

Belgian white ale over orange honey syrup and lemon, with an orange peel twist.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 orange, peeled zest and 1 strip for garnish
  • 60g honey
  • 60ml water
  • 1 pinch of crushed coriander seed
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 bottles Belgian white ale, 330ml each
  1. Simmer the orange zest with the honey, water and coriander seed, then cool and strain.
  2. Put a spoon of syrup and a squeeze of lemon in each of 4 chilled glasses.
  3. Pour the ale slowly so it builds a head.
  4. Twist orange peel over the top.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Diana tastes ice cream for the first time in London.
