Snacks

Island platter of halloumi, feta and figsVegetarian

Griddled halloumi, honey thyme feta, olives, fresh figs and warm pita.

Prep 16 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 250g haloumi, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g feta
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • 1 cup olives
  • 4 figs, halved
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Griddle the haloumi until golden and squeeze lemon over, 8 min.
  2. Drizzle the feta with honey and thyme.
  3. Warm the pitas, 3 min.
  4. Arrange everything on a big platter with the olives and figs.

Amazon spinach and leek filo pieVegetarian

Spinach, leek and dill in crackling filo, cut in squares, golden as the Lasso of Truth.

Prep 28 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and warm through to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spinach
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch dill, chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 12 sheets of filo pastry
  • 60g butter, melted
  1. Wilt the spinach, cool and squeeze dry, 6 min.
  2. Soften the leeks in the oil.
  3. Mix the spinach, leeks, dill, eggs and parmesan.
  4. Layer 6 buttered filo sheets in a tin, spread the filling and top with 6 more buttered sheets, then score into squares.
  5. Bake at 190°C until golden and crisp, 40 min.

Lemon and oregano island potatoes

Potato wedges roasted crisp in olive oil, lemon and oregano, feta crumbled over at the Great Hall.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 80g feta
  1. Potato wedges roasted crisp in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic and oregano, with feta crumbled over. Cut 1kg potatoes into wedges and toss with 60ml olive oil, the juice of 2 lemons, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp dried oregano and salt. Roast at 210°C for 45 minutes, turning once, until crisp and golden. Crumble 80g feta over to serve.

Mains

Hippolyta's lamb kleftiko

Lamb shoulder baked slow in paper with garlic, lemon, oregano and potatoes, a feast for a queen.

Prep 4 min, cook 4 hr 4 min · Make ahead: Rub the lamb the night before and keep it covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg lamb shoulder, bone in
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 800g potatoes, halved
  • 2 onions, sliced
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Rub the lamb with the garlic, lemon juice, oregano, oil and salt, 8 min.
  2. Sit it on the potatoes and onions, wrap tight in baking paper and foil.
  3. Bake low until the lamb falls apart, 3 hr 30 min.
  4. Open the parcel, raise the oven to 200°C and brown, 20 min.
  5. Pull the lamb into pieces and serve with the potatoes and juices.

Antiope's kakavia fish soup

White fish, potato and tomato simmered in olive oil and lemon, crusty bread, a general's lunch.

How it is made
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 600g potatoes
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1.2 L fish stock
  • 700g snapper fillets
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Greek fisherman's soup: white fish, potato, onion, celery and tomato simmered in olive oil and lemon, with crusty bread. Soften 1 sliced onion and 2 celery sticks in 80ml olive oil for 6 minutes. Add 600g diced potatoes, 2 chopped tomatoes and 1.2 L fish stock and simmer 15 minutes. Add 700g snapper fillets in chunks and simmer 6 minutes. Finish with the juice of 1 lemon and chopped parsley, and serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

Diana's raspberry ripple ice cream conesVegetarian

Homemade vanilla custard ice cream rippled with raspberry sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min, 12 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the ice cream a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 120g caster sugar, for the ice cream
  • 200g frozen raspberries
  • 50g caster sugar, for the sauce
  • 8 ice cream cones
  1. Heat the milk and cream with the split vanilla bean, 8 min.
  2. Whisk the yolks and the ice cream sugar, whisk in the hot milk and cook to 82°C, stirring, 10 min.
  3. Strain and chill overnight, then churn.
  4. Simmer the raspberries with the sugar for the sauce, sieve and cool, 12 min.
  5. Layer the ice cream and sauce, swirl once with a knife and freeze, 4 hr.
  6. Scoop into cones.

Drinks

Honeyed white wine with orange

Chilled white wine stirred with warm honey and orange peel, the old Greek oenomeli.

Prep 6 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup a day ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml dry white wine, chilled
  • 80g honey
  • 2 oranges
  • 60ml water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Warm the honey, the peel of one orange and the water until runny, 3 min.
  2. Cool the syrup, then stir it into the chilled wine, 10 min.
  3. Pour over ice with a twist of the other orange.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Diana tastes ice cream for the first time in London.
