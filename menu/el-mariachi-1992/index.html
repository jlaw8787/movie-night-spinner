Snacks

Border town street tacos

Slow-roasted chilli pork crisped in a pan, with pineapple, onion and coriander.

Prep 15 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep the pork chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork shoulder
  • 3 dried chillies
  • 2 oranges
  • 200g fresh pineapple, plus extra to serve
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 small white onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water, then blitz with orange juice, some pineapple and garlic to a smooth marinade.
  2. Coat the pork in the marinade, season and rub in well.
  3. Roast the pork covered at 160°C until it pulls apart.
  4. Shred or slice the pork and crisp it in a very hot pan.
  5. Warm the tortillas and serve with diced pineapple, onion and coriander.

Charred jalapeño guacamole and chipsVegetarian

Smashed avocado with charred jalapeño, lime, onion and coriander.

Prep 13 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Char and chop the jalapenos, onion and coriander up to 1 day ahead; smash the avocados and mix just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 jalapenos
  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 white onion
  • 1 handful coriander
  • 50g feta
  • 1 bag corn chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Char the jalapenos in a dry pan until blackened, then seed and chop fine.
  2. Smash the avocados roughly with a fork.
  3. Stir through the jalapeno, lime juice, onion and coriander, then season well.
  4. Top with crumbled feta.
  5. Warm the corn chips at 180°C and serve straight away.

Cantina garlic and chilli fried peanutsOn the printed menu

Toasted with garlic, lime and salt at Dominó's bar, where she keeps a letter-opener handy.

How it is made
  • 400g raw peanuts
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 packet dried red chillies
  • 1 lime
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Raw peanuts fried with whole garlic cloves and dried chillies until golden, tossed hot with lime and salt, served by the bowl on the bar. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add 400g raw peanuts and 8 peeled garlic cloves and stir for 6 minutes until golden. Add 6 dried red chillies broken in half and cook 1 more minute. Tip into a bowl, squeeze over a lime and toss with 1 tsp salt and a pinch of chilli powder. Cool for 10 minutes so they crisp up before serving.

Mains

Moco's carne asada

Lime and cumin skirt steak, rice and beans. Save a scrap for Dominó's pit bull.

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400g can black beans
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix lime juice, a little orange juice, crushed garlic, cumin and a good pinch of salt, then add the steak.
  2. Marinate the steak at room temperature for 2 hours.
  3. Cook the rice until tender and fluffy, and warm the beans in a small pot.
  4. Sear the steak over very high heat for 3 minutes a side, char the spring onions in the same pan.
  5. Rest the steak, then slice thin against the grain.
  6. Serve with the rice, beans and spring onions.

Red chilli chicken enchiladasOn the printed menu

Chicken and cheese in corn tortillas, baked under crema, for the shootout.

How it is made
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 3 dried chillies
  • 400g can diced tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 250g cheddar
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Corn tortillas rolled around shredded chicken and cheese, covered in a red chilli and tomato sauce and more cheese, baked bubbling and finished with crema and coriander. Poach 600g chicken thighs in salted water for 20 minutes, then shred. Soak 3 dried chillies in hot water, then blitz with a 400g can of tomatoes, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin and salt, and simmer 10 minutes. Dip 8 corn tortillas in the sauce, fill with chicken and 150g grated cheddar, roll and pack into a dish. Pour over the rest of the sauce, top with 100g more cheese and bake at 190°C for 20 minutes. Finish with sour cream and coriander.

Sweets

Cocadas, coconut condensed milk bitesVegetarian

Baked coconut mounds, for the free coconut at the edge of town.

Prep 10 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 250g desiccated coconut
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Mix the coconut, condensed milk, egg yolks and vanilla.
  2. Spoon 20 small mounds onto a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 170°C until golden on the edges.
  4. Cool on the tray until firm.

Drinks

Mixed-up guitar case michelada beer

Lager, lime, tomato juice, hot sauce. Azul wants his in a bottle.

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Mix the chilli salt up to 1 week ahead in a jar; rim the glasses and pour just before serving so the lager stays cold
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles Mexican lager
  • 4 limes
  • 250ml tomato juice
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the salt and chilli powder on a plate.
  2. Run a lime wedge around four tall glasses and dip the rims in the chilli salt.
  3. Fill the glasses with ice.
  4. Add lime juice, tomato juice, hot sauce and Worcestershire sauce to each glass.
  5. Top with cold lager, stir once and garnish with a lime wedge.
