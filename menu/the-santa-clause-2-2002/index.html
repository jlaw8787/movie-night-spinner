Faculty party cranberry cheese ballVegetarian

Cheddar and cream cheese ball with cranberries, rolled in walnuts

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep wrapped and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese, soft
  • 150g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 60g dried cranberries, chopped
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 70g walnuts
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • 200g crackers
  1. Beat the cream cheese with the cheddar, cranberries, chives and garlic powder.
  2. Shape into a ball, wrap in cling film and chill until firm.
  3. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan, chop finely and mix with the parsley.
  4. Roll the ball in the walnut mix until coated and serve with crackers.

North Pole pumpkin and sage soupVegetarian

Roast pumpkin soup with crisp sage and toasted pepitas

Prep 10 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 2 days ahead; fry the sage to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pumpkin, peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1l vegetable stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 40g pepitas
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Roast the pumpkin and onion with the oil and salt for 35 minutes.
  2. Blend with the stock until smooth, then heat through with the cream. Season.
  3. Fry the sage leaves in the butter until crisp and toast the pepitas in the same pan.
  4. Serve topped with the sage butter and pepitas.

Mrs. Claus roast chicken

Thyme butter roast chicken with potatoes and carrots

Prep 15 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the butter, thyme and salt up to 1 day ahead and leave it uncovered in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 whole chicken
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 800g potatoes, halved
  • 3 carrots, cut into chunks
  1. Rub the chicken with the butter, thyme and salt.
  2. Roast at 200C.
  3. Add the potatoes and carrots to the tray and keep roasting.
  4. Rest the chicken before carving.

Mother Nature's maple roast vegetablesVegetarian

Maple pumpkin, carrot and parsnip for 33,000 Easter Bunny offspring.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cut the vegetables up to 1 day ahead; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pumpkin, cut in wedges
  • 3 carrots, halved lengthways
  • 3 parsnips, halved lengthways
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 40g pecans, chopped
  1. Cut the pumpkin, carrots and parsnips.
  2. Toss with the oil and salt and roast at 210C until browned and tender.
  3. Drizzle with the maple, add the thyme and pecans and roast until sticky.

North Pole elf cookiesVegetarian

Buttery cut-out cookies iced red and green

Prep 40 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 350g icing sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 pack food colouring (red and green)
  1. Beat the butter with some of the icing sugar, add the egg yolk and flour and mix to a dough.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll out and cut stars and trees.
  4. Bake at 170C and cool completely.
  5. Mix the remaining icing sugar with water, tint red and green and ice the cookies.

Toy Santa candy cane browniesVegetarian

Chocolate brownies with white chocolate and crushed candy cane

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 tsp peppermint extract
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 120g white chocolate
  • 4 candy canes, crushed
  1. Melt the dark chocolate with the butter. Whisk in the sugar, eggs, peppermint extract and a pinch of salt, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Spread into a lined 20cm square tin and bake at 170C until just set.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Melt the white chocolate, spread over the top and scatter with crushed candy canes.
  5. Chill, then cut into squares.

Abby's designer bean peppermint cocoaVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot chocolate with peppermint, whipped cream and a candy cane

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Chop the chocolates up to 2 days ahead and whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead; heat the milk and whisk on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 100g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 50g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1/4 tsp peppermint extract
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 candy canes
  1. Heat the milk with the sugar until steaming but not boiling.
  2. Whisk in the chocolates and cocoa until smooth and frothy, then stir in the peppermint extract.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and hang a candy cane on each mug to stir.

When to serve

Serve the cocoa when Toy Santa drinks his and feels a little buzz.
