Soup kitchen beef and barley soup with crusty bread

Beef chuck slow-cooked with pearl barley, ladled hot.

Prep 30 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead; it thickens, so loosen with stock when you reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef chuck, cut in 2cm pieces
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150g pearl barley, rinsed
  • 1.5 L beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches and lift it out.
  2. Soften the onion, carrot, celery and garlic, then stir in the tomato paste.
  3. Return the beef, add the barley, stock and bay leaves and simmer gently, half covered, until the beef is tender.
  4. Season well and serve with thick slices of the loaf.

Consume meatloaf with mash and onion gravy

Glazed beef meatloaf on buttery mash.

Prep 15 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Shape the meatloaf up to a day ahead and chill; bake and make the mash and gravy to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled
  • 80g butter
  • 125ml milk
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  1. Mix the mince, grated onion, egg, breadcrumbs, Worcestershire and half the tomato sauce, and press into a loaf tin.
  2. Stir the rest of the tomato sauce with the sugar, spread it on top and bake at 180°C until cooked through.
  3. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with half the butter and the milk.
  4. Cook the sliced onions in the rest of the butter until brown, stir in the flour, then whisk in the stock and simmer to a gravy.
  5. Rest the meatloaf, slice thickly and serve on the mash with the gravy.

Back alley chilli con carne with cornbread

Smoky beef chilli with hot cornbread, glasses on to see it properly.

Prep 5 min, cook 97 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead, it gets better; bake the cornbread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef mince
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 150g fine polenta
  • 125g self-raising flour
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince in the oil, then add the onion and garlic and cook until soft.
  2. Stir in the spices, then the tomatoes and beans, and simmer gently until thick and dark.
  3. Mix the polenta, flour, buttermilk, egg and melted butter, pour into a hot greased pan and bake at 200°C until risen and golden.
  4. Serve the chilli with cheddar on top and wedges of cornbread.

All out of bubble gum ice cream sundaesVegetarian

Pink bubble gum ice cream and hot fudge. Nada is all out of bubble gum.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the fudge sauce up to a week ahead and rewarm; scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L bubble gum ice cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 50g rainbow sprinkles
  1. Heat the cream and golden syrup, pour over the chocolate and stir until smooth.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into tall glasses.
  3. Pour over the hot fudge, add whipped cream, sprinkles and a cherry.
