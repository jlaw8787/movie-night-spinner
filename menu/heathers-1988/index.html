Snacks

Corn nuts, chilli lime toastedVegetarian

Crunchy fried corn kernels tossed in butter, chilli and lime.

Prep 3 min, cook 26 min, 12 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dried corn kernels
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 20g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Soak the corn in water overnight.
  2. Drain and dry very well on tea towels.
  3. Fry in batches in 180°C oil, using a lid as they pop, until puffed and crunchy, 24 min.
  4. Drain on paper.
  5. Toss hot with melted butter, chilli powder, lime zest and salt.
  6. Cool so they stay crisp, 15 min.

Red scrunchie raspberry crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette with ricotta, crushed raspberries and honey.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Toast the baguette up to 1 day ahead, airtight; whip the ricotta up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; crush the raspberries and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g ricotta, whipped
  • 200g raspberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the baguette, brush with oil and toast, 4 min.
  2. Whip the ricotta until smooth and spread on the toasts.
  3. Crush the raspberries with a little sugar.
  4. Top the ricotta with raspberries and drizzle with honey.

Mains

Funeral reception lasagne

Beef and pork ragu layered with béchamel and mozzarella, baked 45 minutes.

Prep 35 min, cook 2 hr 45 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Assemble a day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 800g tins crushed tomatoes
  • 150ml red wine
  • 50g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 300g lasagne sheets
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  1. Brown the mince, 10 min.
  2. Simmer with tomatoes and red wine, 2 hr.
  3. Make a bechamel with butter, flour and milk.
  4. Layer the meat sauce, bechamel, lasagne sheets and mozzarella.
  5. Bake at 180°C, 45 min.
  6. Rest before cutting, 20 min.

Cafeteria schnitzel with mashed potatoes and gravy

Breast pounded thin, breaded and fried golden in butter, minted peas, a cafeteria tray to rule with.

How it is made
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 90g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Chicken breasts pounded thin, crumbed and fried golden in butter and oil, with buttery mashed potato, minted peas and a pan gravy. Halve 2 large chicken breasts horizontally and pound to 1cm. Dust in flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs and press into 120g panko. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml milk. Fry the schnitzels in 30g butter and oil for 3 minutes a side until golden. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan, whisk in 300ml chicken stock and simmer until thick. Cook 300g peas with mint.

Pep rally red velvet cupcakesVegetarian

Deep red cupcakes under cream cheese frosting, for the pep rally.

Prep 25 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes up to 1 day ahead, airtight; make the cream cheese icing and pipe on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 250g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar and add the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk and red colouring.
  3. Bake 12 cupcakes at 175°C and cool, 18 min.
  4. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar.
  5. Pipe on the cupcakes and add a red sugar dot.

Drinks

Snappy Snack Shack cherry slushieNo alcohol

Cherry juice and lemon-lime soda blended with ice into a slushie.

Prep 4 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the cherry juice and lemonade up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml cherry juice
  • 300ml lemonade
  • 4 cups of ice
  1. Blend the cherry juice, lemonade and ice until slushy.
  2. Pour into tall cups with a straw and a spoon.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when J.D. and Veronica stop at the Snappy Snack Shack for slushies.
