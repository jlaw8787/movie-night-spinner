Elm Street chips and dipVegetarian

Sour cream and cream cheese dip blended with parsley, chives and garlic

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dip keeps 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 cup parsley
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 300g kettle chips
  1. Blitz the sour cream, cream cheese, garlic, lemon juice, parsley and chives until green and smooth.
  2. Season with salt.
  3. Chill.
  4. Serve with kettle chips.

Five finger pretzelsVegetarian

Rods with tips dipped in dark chocolate, set in fives on baking paper with flaky salt

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Dip and set up to 1 day ahead, kept cool in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Melt the chocolate in short bursts.
  2. Dip the tips of the pretzel rods.
  3. Lay them on baking paper in groups of five like fingers.
  4. Sprinkle each tip with sea salt.
  5. Leave to set.

Mains

Stay-awake chilli

Slow cooked beef chuck chilli with chipotle, cumin and cocoa

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp chipotle in adobo
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 800g crushed tomatoes
  • 400g kidney beans, drained
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  2. Soften the onions and garlic.
  3. Add the chipotle, cumin, cocoa, tomatoes, beans and beef.
  4. Cover and braise at 160C until thick.
  5. Serve topped with sour cream and cheddar.

Boiler room sloppy joesOn the printed menu

Saucy beef mince with onion, capsicum and tomato in toasted brioche, melted cheddar and pickles

How it is made
  • 700g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 250ml tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 4 brioche buns
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Saucy beef mince cooked down with onion, capsicum, tomato and brown sugar, piled into toasted brioche buns with melted cheddar and crunchy pickles. Brown 700g beef mince with 1 diced onion and 1 diced green capsicum for 10 minutes. Stir in 250ml tomato sauce, 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp American mustard and 125ml water. Simmer 15 to 20 minutes until thick. Toast 4 brioche buns, lay a slice of cheddar on each base, pile on the mince and top with sliced dill pickles. Serve with plenty of napkins.

Glen's convertible cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes piped in red and green stripes, like the jumper that locks them in

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Unfrosted cakes keep 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 3 drops red food colouring
  • 3 drops green food colouring
  1. Beat about half the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Spoon into 12 cases.
  3. Bake at 175C.
  4. Cool the cupcakes.
  5. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar, split in two and colour one red and one green.
  6. Spoon both side by side into one piping bag and pipe swirls.

Strong black coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Plunger coffee, strong and black in big mugs. Nancy lives on caffeine now.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Best plunged fresh and hot; nothing worth doing ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 60g ground coffee
  • 1l water, just off the boil
  • 100ml full cream milk
  1. Put the coffee in a large plunger and pour over the water.
  2. Steep.
  3. Plunge and pour into four big mugs. Serve black, with milk on the side for anyone who is not trying to stay awake.

When to serve

Serve the coffee when Nancy tries to stay awake.
