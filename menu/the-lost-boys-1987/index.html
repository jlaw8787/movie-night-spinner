Snacks

Santa Carla boardwalk friesVegetarian

Thin fries tossed with garlic powder, parsley and salt, served in paper cones.

Prep 32 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fries can be soaked up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg russet potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries. Soak in cold water, then drain and dry very well.
  2. Fry in batches in oil at 140°C for about 5 minutes until pale and soft, then rest on a rack.
  3. Reheat the oil to 190°C and fry again until golden and crisp.
  4. Toss with salt, garlic powder and parsley and serve in paper cones.

Garlic breadVegetarian

Garlic baguette baked in foil. Get yourself a garlic T-shirt, buddy.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mash the garlic butter and spread and wrap the baguette up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 baguette
  1. Mash the butter with the garlic, parsley and salt.
  2. Split the baguette and spread generously, then wrap in foil.
  3. Bake at 200°C, then open the foil and bake again to crisp the top.

Frog brothers' roast garlic bean dip

Silky cannellini with a whole head of roasted garlic, lemon and chilli oil, toasted pita chips.

How it is made
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 2 x 400g tins cannellini beans
  • 1 lemon
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 3 pita breads
  1. Silky cannellini dip loaded with a whole head of roasted garlic, lemon and olive oil, topped with chilli oil and parsley, with toasted pita chips. Wrap 1 whole garlic bulb in foil and roast at 200°C for 40 minutes. Cut 3 pita breads into wedges, brush with olive oil and bake for 8 minutes until crisp. Squeeze the soft garlic into a food processor with 2 drained 400g tins cannellini beans, the juice of 1 lemon, 60ml olive oil and salt, then blitz until smooth with a splash of water. Spread in a bowl and top with 1 tbsp chilli oil and chopped parsley.

Mains

Chinese takeaway noodles

Wok chicken and egg noodles in oyster sauce, with egg fried rice on the side.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Slice the chicken and shred the cabbage up to 1 day ahead, chilled; stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 200g bean sprouts
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 300g cooked jasmine rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slice the chicken, shred the cabbage and slice the spring onions.
  2. Stir-fry the chicken in a smoking wok until browned.
  3. Add the cabbage, bean sprouts and noodles and toss with the oyster sauce, soy and sesame oil.
  4. Scramble the eggs in a second pan, add the rice and spring onion and fry until hot, then serve alongside.

Grandpa's glazed meatloaf and mash

Beef and pork loaf, sticky tomato glaze, buttery mash and peas, stay off the second shelf.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 packet gravy mix
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with a sticky brown sugar tomato glaze, sliced thick over buttery mashed potato with minted peas and gravy. Mix 500g beef mince, 250g pork mince, 1 grated onion, 1 egg, 60g breadcrumbs soaked in 80ml milk, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce and salt. Press into a loaf tin. Mix 100ml tomato sauce with 2 tbsp brown sugar and spread over the top. Bake at 180°C for 60 minutes and rest 10 minutes. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml hot milk. Cook 400g frozen peas with mint and butter. Slice the meatloaf over the mash with peas and gravy.

Sweets

Boardwalk caramel popcornVegetarian

Popcorn coated in brown sugar and golden syrup caramel, baked crisp.

Prep 8 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  1. Pop the kernels in a covered pot and tip into a large bowl.
  2. Boil the sugar, butter and golden syrup for 4 minutes, then stir in the bicarb.
  3. Pour over the popcorn, toss and bake at 120°C, stirring every 15 minutes.
  4. Cool on the tray and break up.

Drinks

Holy water cucumber and lime fizzNo alcohol

Cucumber, lime and mint topped with soda water in small bottles, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Muddle the cucumber, lime and mint with the syrup and strain up to 4 hours ahead, chilled; add soda water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 3 limes, juiced
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 cup ice
  1. Chop half the cucumber and muddle with the lime juice, mint and syrup in a jug.
  2. Strain into small glass bottles or tall glasses filled with ice.
  3. Top with chilled soda water and a thin cucumber ribbon. Tie paper cross tags round the necks.

When to serve
