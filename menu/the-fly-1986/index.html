Snacks

Teleported steak bites

Seared garlic butter sirloin cubes with a horseradish sour cream dip

Prep 17 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Cube the steak up to 4 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge, and mix the horseradish cream up to 2 days ahead; season and sear to order
Recipe for 4
  • 600g sirloin steak
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Cut the steak into cubes and season well.
  2. Sear in a smoking pan in batches.
  3. Baste with the butter, garlic and thyme.
  4. Rest.
  5. Mix the sour cream with the horseradish and serve with the steak on toothpicks.

Mains

Sticky glazed pork belly

Roast pork belly glazed with soy, honey and ginger, rice and greens

Prep 7 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Roast the pork belly and make the glaze up to 2 days ahead and keep both in the fridge; slice, brush and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pork belly
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 bunch bok choy
  1. Roast the pork belly at 150C.
  2. Simmer the soy, honey, sugar and ginger until syrupy.
  3. Slice the pork and brush with glaze.
  4. Grill until sticky.
  5. Steam the rice and cook the bok choy.

Vomit drop doughnutsVegetarian

Seth dissolves his before eating. Caster sugar doughnut rounds.

Prep 75 min, cook 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the dough and rest it in the fridge up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 350g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 40g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 130g caster sugar
  • 180ml warm water
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, butter, egg, a little of the sugar and the warm water into a dough and rest.
  2. Roll out and cut rounds.
  3. Prove.
  4. Heat the oil to 175C.
  5. Fry a few at a time, 90 seconds a side.
  6. Toss in caster sugar while warm.

Brundle's caramel baked pearsVegetarian

Pears baked in brown sugar, butter and vanilla, served with cream

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; rewarm in a low oven and serve with cold cream
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pears
  • 60g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 100ml water
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Halve and core the pears.
  2. Lay cut side up in a dish with the butter, sugar, vanilla and water.
  3. Bake at 180C, basting.
  4. Serve with cream.

Drinks

Brundle's brown sugar iced latteVegetarianNo alcohol

Double espresso, brown sugar syrup and cold milk, topped with cream

Prep 14 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the syrup up to 1 week ahead, and brew and chill the coffee up to 2 days ahead, both in the fridge; shake and pour to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark brown sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 250ml strong coffee
  • 600ml full cream milk, cold
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Simmer the sugar with the water for 2 minutes to make a syrup and cool.
  2. Brew very strong coffee.
  3. Shake each coffee with syrup and ice.
  4. Fill tall glasses with ice and milk and pour over the coffee.
  5. Top with lightly whipped cream.
