Snacks

Ascot race day cucumber sandwichesVegetarian

Paper-thin slices on soft white bread with butter, cream cheese and dill, Eliza's first outing

Prep 32 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 hours ahead, covered with a damp tea towel in the fridge so the bread stays soft
Recipe for 4
  • 2 telegraph cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 125g cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 bunch dill, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
  • 16 slices soft white sandwich bread
  1. Toss the cucumber with the salt and drain on paper towel.
  2. Beat the butter with the cream cheese, dill and white pepper.
  3. Spread the bread to the edges, lay cucumber over 8 slices and close with the rest.
  4. Press down gently, cut off the crusts and cut each into 3 fingers.

Doolittle's pub Scotch eggs

Soft-boiled, wrapped in sage sausage meat, crumbed, fried, piccalilli, with a little bit of luck

Prep 50 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make and crumb the Scotch eggs up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; fry on the night or serve cold
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork sausage meat
  • 6 eggs, 4 for boiling, 2 beaten
  • 1 tbsp chopped sage
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp salt and pepper, to taste
  • 50g plain flour
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120g piccalilli
  1. Lower 4 eggs into boiling water for 6 1/2 minutes, then into iced water. Peel.
  2. Mix the sausage meat with the sage, mustard, nutmeg, salt and pepper.
  3. Flatten a quarter of the mixture on wet hands, wrap it round an egg and seal. Chill 20 minutes.
  4. Roll each in flour, then the beaten eggs, then the panko.
  5. Deep-fry at 170C for 7 minutes until deep golden and cooked through. Drain, halve and serve with piccalilli.

Mains

Embassy Ball beef Wellington

Seared fillet in mustard, wrapped in mushroom duxelles and puff pastry, sliced for Eliza's big night

Prep 55 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Sear the beef and make the duxelles up to 1 day ahead, or wrap it up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg centre-cut beef fillet
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, for searing
  • 500g swiss brown mushrooms
  • 2 shallots, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 40g butter
  • 4 sprigs of thyme, leaves picked
  • 2 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 750g baby potatoes
  • 300g green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Season the beef with half the salt and pepper, sear all over in the vegetable oil in a very hot pan, brush with the mustard and cool.
  2. Blitz the mushrooms finely and cook with the shallots, garlic, thyme and butter until dry and dark. Cool.
  3. Lay the pastry sheets slightly overlapping, press to join, spread with the mushrooms, sit the beef on top, roll up and seal. Brush with egg and chill 20 minutes.
  4. Heat the oven to 200C. Toss the potatoes with the olive oil and the rest of the salt and pepper and roast.
  5. Score the pastry and bake the Wellington for medium rare.
  6. Steam the beans for 4 minutes and rest the Wellington 10 minutes before slicing thickly.

Rain in Spain saffron potatoesVegetarian

Roasted golden with saffron and garlic. By George, she's got it

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Par-boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 50g butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  1. Soak the saffron in 2 tbsp hot water.
  2. Boil the potatoes for 8 minutes and drain well.
  3. Melt the butter with the oil, garlic and saffron water, toss through the potatoes and roast at 220C until golden.
  4. Scatter with chopped parsley and salt.

Sweets

Flower girl's violet Victoria spongeVegetarian

For the bunches of violets Eliza sells under the portico at Covent Garden

Prep 68 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Bake the sponges up to 1 day ahead and keep them airtight; fill and ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 160g icing sugar
  • 150g blackberry jam
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 drop purple food colouring
  • 1 punnet edible violas or violets
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line two 20cm tins. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  2. Fold in the flour, vanilla and milk, split between the tins and bake.
  3. Cool the sponges completely.
  4. Whip the cream with 1 tbsp of the icing sugar. Spread one sponge with jam, then cream, and top with the second.
  5. Mix the rest of the icing sugar with lemon juice and the food colouring, spread over the top and finish with the flowers.

Drinks

Wimpole Street elderflower gin fizzVegetarian

A long cold drink for Pickering in the Wimpole Street study

Prep 6 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Ribbon the cucumber and squeeze the lemons up to 4 hours ahead and keep cold; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml dry gin
  • 80ml elderflower cordial
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, in long ribbons
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice and add a couple of cucumber ribbons to each.
  2. Shake the gin, elderflower cordial and lemon juice with ice and strain into the glasses.
  3. Top with soda water and add a mint sprig.

When to serve
