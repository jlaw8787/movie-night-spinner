Snacks

Cleary wedding Maryland crab cakes

Pan fried crab cakes with celery salt and paprika, served with lemon.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Shape and chill the cakes up to 1 day ahead on a tray, covered in the fridge; pan-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g crab meat
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp celery salt
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon
  1. Fold the crab with the mayonnaise, egg, Dijon, celery salt, paprika and breadcrumbs.
  2. Shape into 8 cakes.
  3. Chill, 30 min.
  4. Pan-fry in the butter until golden, 10 min.
  5. Serve with lemon.

Sailing trip shrimp rolls

Shrimp salad with celery and chives in a buttered toasted hot dog bun.

Prep 13 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the prawn filling up to 4 hours ahead and chill; butter and toast the buns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked prawns
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 30g butter
  1. Chop the prawns and fold with the mayonnaise, lemon juice, diced celery and chives.
  2. Butter the cut sides of the buns and toast, 5 min.
  3. Pile the prawns in.

Reception fig and goat's cheese crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette with whipped chèvre, fresh fig, honey, thyme and cracked pepper.

Prep 3 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Toast the crostini up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; whip the goat's cheese up to 1 day ahead; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g soft goat's cheese
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese
  • 4 figs
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  1. Slice the baguette thin on an angle, brush with oil and bake at 200°C until crisp, 8 min.
  2. Beat the goat's cheese with the cream cheese until smooth.
  3. Spread the toasts with the cheese, top with fig wedges, a drizzle of honey, thyme leaves and cracked pepper.

Mains

Tarragon cream chicken with potato gratin

Pan roasted chicken in white wine and Dijon tarragon cream, creamy potato gratin.

Prep 3 min, cook 1 hr 52 min · Make ahead: Gratin can be made 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 450ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 150ml white wine
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 300g green beans
  1. Slice the potatoes thin, layer with part of the cream and the garlic and bake at 160°C, 1 hr 15 min.
  2. Pan-roast the chicken breasts until cooked through, 20 min.
  3. Add the wine, Dijon and tarragon to the pan, then the remaining cream, and simmer, 10 min.
  4. Steam the beans, 7 min.
  5. Serve together.

Chazz's sticky glazed meatloaf

Beef and pork with a sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, buttery mashed potatoes and gravy.

How it is made
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 200ml milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 packet plain flour
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with grated onion, egg and Worcestershire, glazed sticky with tomato and brown sugar, with buttery mash and gravy. Soak 80g breadcrumbs in 100ml milk. Mix lightly with 500g beef mince, 250g pork mince, a grated onion, 1 egg, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and salt and press into a loaf tin. Spread with 80ml tomato sauce mixed with 2 tbsp brown sugar. Bake at 180°C for 1 hour and rest 10 minutes. Mash 1kg potatoes with 60g butter and milk. Simmer the pan juices with 250ml beef stock and 1 tbsp flour for gravy.

Rule 76 wedding cakeVegetarian

Vanilla layer cake with meringue buttercream and raspberry.

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 450g butter
  • 350g caster sugar
  • 7 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 jar raspberry jam
  1. Beat part of the butter with part of the sugar, add 4 eggs and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 165°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool, 20 min.
  4. Heat 3 egg whites with the remaining sugar to 70°C, whip, then beat in the remaining butter, 25 min.
  5. Layer with raspberry jam and buttercream and cut into fingers.

Drinks

Reception French 75

Gin, lemon and sugar syrup shaken and topped with sparkling wine.

Prep 10 min, 3 hr waiting · Make ahead: Chill the sparkling wine up to 1 day ahead; shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 120ml gin
  • 2 lemons
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  1. Chill the sparkling wine for three hours, 3 hr.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and sugar syrup with ice, one drink at a time.
  3. Strain into flutes and top slowly with sparkling wine.
  4. Add a long twist of lemon peel and raise them high while someone gives a speech.
