North's workshop pelmeni with sour cream

Beef and pork pelmeni in butter with sour cream. The yetis make the toys; the elves think they do.

Prep 35 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Fold up to 1 day ahead, or freeze; boil to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 40 round dumpling wrappers
  • 40g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Mix the beef, pork, grated onion, salt and pepper.
  2. Put a teaspoon on each wrapper, fold into a half moon, seal and pinch the ends together.
  3. Boil in salted water in batches for 5 minutes after they float.
  4. Toss with the melted butter and serve with sour cream and chopped dill.

Jamie's snow day mac and cheese bitesVegetarian

Elbow pasta in cheddar sauce baked in mini muffin tins, crisp tops, after Jack's sled ride

Prep 5 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; reheat at 180°C for 8 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 200g macaroni
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 egg
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Cook the macaroni until just tender and drain.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until thick.
  3. Off the heat stir in the grated cheddar, mustard, egg and macaroni.
  4. Spoon into a greased 24-hole mini muffin tin, top with grated parmesan and bake at 200°C.
  5. Cool 10 minutes before lifting out.

Mains

North's beef stroganoff and noodles

Rump strips and mushrooms in creamy sauce over egg noodles

Prep 12 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rump steak
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 1 bunch of dill
  1. Slice the rump into thin strips, the onion and the mushrooms.
  2. Sear the beef in batches in a hot pan with oil, then remove.
  3. Fry the onion and mushrooms in butter.
  4. Add the paprika, Dijon and stock and simmer.
  5. Stir in the sour cream and the beef and warm through without boiling.
  6. Cook the egg noodles, toss in butter and dill and serve under the beef.

Bunnymund's Aussie lamb chops with minted peas

Barbecued lamb chops with minted peas. "You don't wanna race a rabbit, mate."

Prep 5 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chops up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 lamb loin chops
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g chat potatoes, halved
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lemon
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the potatoes with 2 tbsp oil and salt and roast until crisp.
  2. Rub the chops with garlic, chopped rosemary and the rest of the oil.
  3. Grill the chops on a hot barbecue or pan until charred and pink inside, then rest.
  4. Boil the peas, then crush with the butter, chopped mint and a squeeze of lemon.

Bunnymund's hot cross bun egg puddingVegetarian

Baked hot cross bun pudding with mini chocolate eggs

Prep 10 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 hot cross buns
  • 30g butter
  • 150g mini chocolate eggs
  • 3 eggs
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Butter and halve the hot cross buns and lay them in a baking dish, cut-side up and overlapping.
  2. Tuck the mini chocolate eggs between them.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream, sugar and vanilla and pour over.
  4. Stand for 15 minutes.
  5. Bake at 170°C until set and golden.
  6. Dust with icing sugar and serve warm with cream and a few more mini eggs.

Iced gingerbread yetis and elvesVegetarian

Spiced gingerbread men iced as white-furred yetis and red-hatted elves

Prep 38 min, cook 15 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 bottle of red food colouring
  1. Melt the butter with golden syrup and brown sugar.
  2. Mix in the flour, ginger, cinnamon and egg.
  3. Chill the dough for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll to 5mm and cut big gingerbread men for yetis and small ones for elves.
  5. Bake at 180°C.
  6. Cool completely.
  7. Mix the icing sugar with water, pipe shaggy white fur on the yetis and tint a little red for the elves' hats.

Sandy's golden dream honeycombVegetarian

Crunchy golden honeycomb dipped in milk chocolate

Prep 5 min, cook 24 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 5 tbsp golden syrup
  • 2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 1 jar of edible gold glitter
  1. Line a 20cm square tin with baking paper.
  2. Heat the sugar and golden syrup in a deep pan, stirring until dissolved.
  3. Boil without stirring until deep golden.
  4. Take off the heat, quickly whisk in the bicarbonate of soda and pour into the tin without spreading.
  5. Set for 1 hour, then break into chunks.
  6. Dip half of each chunk in melted milk chocolate and dust with edible gold glitter.

Jack's Snow Day frosty blue slushieVegetarianNo alcohol

Icy blue lime slushie with a sugar rim

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Rims up to 1 hour ahead, blend fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 limes
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 bag of ice
  • 120ml blue cordial
  • 1 bunch of mint
  • 600ml lemonade
  1. Dip the rims of the glasses in lime juice, then in caster sugar.
  2. Freeze the glasses for 10 minutes.
  3. Blend the ice with blue cordial, lime juice and mint leaves until slushy.
  4. Add the lemonade and pulse twice.
  5. Pour into the frosted glasses and garnish with a mint sprig.
