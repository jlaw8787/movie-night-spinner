Snacks

Desert crossing fried tortillasVegetarian

Fried corn tortillas with chilli salt and a smoky charred tomato and onion salsa.

Prep 12 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Salsa up to 1 day, chips fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Cut the tortillas into wedges.
  2. Fry in the oil at 180°C until crisp, then toss in salt and chilli powder.
  3. Char the tomatoes and onion in a dry pan until blackened.
  4. Chop together with lime juice and salt for a smoky salsa.
  5. Serve with the chips.

Gold coin potato roundsVegetarian

Crispy roast potato coins with garlic mayonnaise.

Prep 3 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 800g small potatoes, sliced in thick coins
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 150g mayonnaise
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Slice the potatoes and toss in oil and salt.
  2. Spread on a tray and roast, turning once.
  3. Stir the garlic into mayonnaise for aioli.
  4. Pile the coins beside the aioli.

Father Ramirez's focaccia and warm olivesOn the printed menu

Dimpled rosemary bread baked golden in oil, olives with orange peel, fennel seed and chilli.

How it is made
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 box flaky sea salt
  • 300g mixed olives
  • 1 orange
  • 1 jar fennel seeds
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  1. Dimpled rosemary focaccia baked golden in good oil, with olives warmed through with orange peel, fennel seed and chilli. Mix 500g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 2 tsp salt and 400ml warm water and prove for 2 hours. Tip into an oiled 30 x 20cm tin with 4 tbsp olive oil, stretch to the edges, dimple with your fingers and prove 30 minutes. Scatter rosemary and flaky salt and bake at 220°C for 22 minutes. Warm 300g mixed olives in olive oil with orange peel, 1 tsp fennel seeds and chilli flakes for 5 minutes.

Mains

Farmhouse bean stew

Borlotti beans simmered with smoked ham hock, tomatoes and rosemary.

Prep 25 min, cook 2 hours, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak beans overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 400g dried borlotti beans
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 1 tin of crushed tomatoes
  • 2 sprigs of rosemary
  • 1 loaf of Italian bread
  1. Soak the beans overnight and drain.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil.
  3. Add the beans, ham hock, tomatoes, rosemary and water to cover.
  4. Simmer until thick.
  5. Shred the ham back in and serve with bread.

Tuco's spaghetti with beef and pork ragùOn the printed menu

Pasta tossed through slow-cooked mince with red wine and tomato, pecorino, chilli oil, basil.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 150ml red wine
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tin tomato paste
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 60g pecorino
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Spaghetti tossed through a slow-cooked beef and pork ragù with red wine and tomato, finished with pecorino, chilli oil and torn basil. Brown 500g beef mince and 300g pork mince. Add 1 diced onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stick and 3 garlic cloves and cook 8 minutes. Pour in 150ml red wine, then 2 tins crushed tomatoes and 2 tbsp tomato paste, and simmer gently for 2 hours, adding water if needed. Cook 500g spaghetti, toss through the ragù with a splash of pasta water, then top with grated pecorino, chilli oil and basil.

Sad Hill chocolate mousse cupsVegetarian

Dark chocolate mousse in cups with a chocolate coin buried in each.

Prep 22 min, cook 8 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keeps 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 4 chocolate coins
  • 8 chocolate biscuits, crushed
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly.
  2. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks and whip the cream.
  3. Fold the yolks into the chocolate, then fold in the cream and whites.
  4. Spoon into cups with a chocolate coin buried in each.
  5. Chill.
  6. Top with crushed biscuits.

Blondie's rye old fashionedVegetarian

Rye whisky, demerara syrup and orange bitters stirred over a big ice cube.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the demerara syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; stir with ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g demerara sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 240ml rye whisky
  • 8 dashes orange bitters
  • 3 cups ice
  • 1 orange, peel only
  1. Stir the sugar with the hot water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Stir the whisky, syrup and bitters with ice.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in a heavy glass.
  4. Twist orange peel over the top and drop it in.
