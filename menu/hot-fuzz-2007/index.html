Snacks

The Crown pub ploughman's

Mature cheddar, pork pie, pickled onions, chutney, apple and crusty bread

Prep 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Assemble up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g vintage cheddar
  • 1 pork pie
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  • 1 apple
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 100g salted butter
  1. Take the cheddar out of the fridge an hour ahead so it comes up to room temperature.
  2. Cut the cheddar into thick slices and the pork pie into wedges.
  3. Arrange on a board with pickled onions, chutney, sliced apple and torn pieces of the crusty loaf, with a dish of butter.

Village of the Year fete scotch eggs

Soft-boiled eggs wrapped in sausage meat, crumbed and fried golden, for the Village of the Year fete

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, serve cold or reheat at 180°C
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs
  • 400g pork sausage mince
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 jar English mustard
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Boil four of the eggs for 6 minutes, cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Wrap each egg in seasoned sausage mince, pressing it smooth and even.
  3. Roll in flour, the remaining beaten egg, then panko.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170°C until deep golden and cooked through, then halve.
  5. Serve with English mustard.

Greater good cheddar and leek rarebitVegetarian

Cheddar, mustard and leek on toast. For the greater good

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese mix up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; spread and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g mature cheddar, grated
  • 1 leek, finely sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 2 tsp English mustard
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 4 thick slices sourdough
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Soften the leek in the butter.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the milk, and cook until thick.
  3. Off the heat, beat in the cheddar, mustard and egg yolk.
  4. Toast the sourdough, spread the cheese thickly to the edges and grill until bubbling and browned in spots.
  5. Cut into fingers and scatter with snipped chives.

Mains

Sandford steak and ale pie

Beef braised in ale and baked under puff pastry, served with chips

Prep 20 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 375ml English ale
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 2l vegetable oil
  1. Brown the beef in batches and soften the sliced onions.
  2. Add thyme, ale and Worcestershire sauce. Cover and braise gently until the beef is tender.
  3. Thicken the sauce with the flour mixed to a paste, simmer, then tip into a pie dish and cool slightly.
  4. Top with puff pastry, brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.
  5. Cut the potatoes into chips, fry in the vegetable oil until golden, and boil the peas. Serve alongside.

Supermarket roast chicken and bread sauceOn the printed menu

Whole bird roasted golden, potatoes in its fat, clove-studded sauce, Skinner grinning at the till

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1kg roasting potatoes
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 400ml milk
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar whole cloves
  • 80g white bread
  1. Whole chicken roasted golden, with roast potatoes cooked in the chicken fat, honey roast carrots and a creamy clove-studded bread sauce. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with butter and salt and roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then 180°C for 50 minutes, and rest. Parboil 1kg potatoes, roast in the chicken fat for 50 minutes. Toss 500g carrots in honey and oil and roast 30 minutes. Simmer 400ml milk with an onion studded with 4 cloves for 10 minutes, remove the onion and stir in 80g fresh breadcrumbs and 20g butter.

Sweets

Strawberry Cornetto conesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream rippled with strawberry in chocolate and peanut dipped cones

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 4 waffle cones
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 50g chopped peanuts
  1. Blitz the strawberries with the sugar to a sauce.
  2. Soften the ice cream, ripple the sauce through and refreeze until firm.
  3. Melt the chocolate and dip the rims of the cones, then dip in chopped peanuts.
  4. Fill with scoops and freeze until serving.

Drinks

Angel's cranberry, ginger and lime coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Shaken with ginger syrup and lime, no ice. Angel orders cranberry juice

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml cranberry juice
  • 2 limes
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 1 piece fresh ginger, 3cm
  • 500ml soda water
  1. Chill the cranberry juice and four pint glasses in the fridge.
  2. Simmer the sugar, water and sliced ginger for 5 minutes and cool.
  3. Shake the cranberry juice with lime juice and ginger syrup, pour into cold glasses and top with soda water. No ice.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Danny puts the action DVDs on at his house.
