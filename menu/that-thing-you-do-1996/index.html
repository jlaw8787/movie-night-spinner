Snacks

Oh-Needers garlic breadVegetarian

Everyone reads it as the Oh-Needers

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Butter the bread up to 6 hours ahead and wrap; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Italian loaf, sliced not quite through
  • 100g soft butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  1. Mash the butter with the garlic and parsley.
  2. Spread it between the slices and over the top, then scatter with mozzarella.
  3. Bake at 200C until golden and bubbling.

Villapiano's bonus antipasto

Villapiano lays a bonus on the bar

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Slice everything up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge; plate to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g sliced salami
  • 150g provolone, cut in sticks
  • 1 cup green and black olives
  • 200g marinated roasted peppers
  • 150g marinated artichoke hearts
  • 8 grissini
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Fold the salami and lay it on a large platter.
  2. Add the provolone, olives, peppers and artichokes in piles.
  3. Tuck in the grissini and drizzle with oil.

Mains

Spaghetti and meatballs out by the airport

The spaghetti place out by the airport

Prep 10 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead; reheat and cook the pasta to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 40g parmesan, grated, plus extra to serve
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 x 700ml bottles passata
  • 1 cup basil leaves
  • 500g spaghetti
  1. Mix the minces, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan and a third of the garlic and roll into 20 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in batches, then soften the onion and the rest of the garlic in the same pan.
  3. Add the passata, return the meatballs and simmer gently until cooked through.
  4. Cook the spaghetti, toss with some of the sauce and top with meatballs, basil and parmesan.

Talent show pepperoni pizza

The paying gig after the talent show

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheese up to a day ahead; stretch and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 250g balls pizza dough
  • 250ml pizza sauce
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 150g pepperoni, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven as hot as it goes with a tray inside.
  2. Stretch the dough thin and spread with sauce.
  3. Top with mozzarella, pepperoni and oregano.
  4. Bake on the hot tray until blistered and crisp.
  5. Drizzle with oil and cut into squares.

Number 7 on the charts cannoliVegetarian

The song climbs to number 7

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; fill the shells just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fresh ricotta, drained
  • 80g icing sugar, plus extra to dust
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 60g dark chocolate chips
  • 8 cannoli shells
  • 30g pistachios, chopped
  1. Beat the ricotta with the icing sugar, vanilla and orange zest until smooth.
  2. Fold in the chocolate chips and spoon into a piping bag.
  3. Pipe into the shells, dip the ends in pistachios and dust with icing sugar.
