Snacks

Pit stop corn dogs

Franks dipped in buttermilk cornmeal batter and fried deep golden, with mustard.

Prep 8 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry ingredients up to 1 week ahead; whisk in the egg and buttermilk and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, egg and buttermilk into a thick batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push onto sticks.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C. Dip each frank in batter and fry until deep golden.
  4. Serve with mustard.

Jean Girard's ham and cheese crêpes

Thin crêpes, some folded around ham and gruyère, some with sugar and lemon.

Prep 65 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml full cream milk
  • 30g butter
  • 100g leg ham
  • 100g gruyère
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk to a smooth batter and rest.
  2. Cook thin crepes in a pan brushed with the butter.
  3. Fold half around the ham and gruyère and warm until melted.
  4. Sprinkle the rest with sugar and lemon juice.

Talladega Cajun boiled peanuts

Simmered soft in the shell in spiced garlic chilli brine.

How it is made
  • 500g raw peanuts in shell
  • 80g salt
  • 1 jar Cajun seasoning
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 packet dried chillies
  1. Raw peanuts in the shell boiled soft in a salty Cajun brine with garlic and chilli, left to soak up the spice, served warm in paper cups. Rinse 500g raw peanuts in the shell. Put in a large pot with 3L water, 80g salt, 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning, a halved garlic bulb and 2 dried chillies. Weigh down with a plate so they stay under and simmer for 2 hours until soft. Turn off the heat and leave to soak for 1 hour. Drain and serve warm in paper cups with a bowl for shells.

Mains

Fried chicken, pizza and tacos dinner

Buttermilk fried chicken, homemade pizza and hard shell tacos, Bobby family style.

Prep 40 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Chicken can be brined the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken pieces
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 300g plain flour
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 jar pizza sauce
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 400g beef mince
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 8 taco shells
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Brine the chicken in the buttermilk.
  2. Dredge in seasoned flour and fry in the vegetable oil at 165°C until cooked through and crisp.
  3. Stretch the dough, top with sauce and mozzarella and bake at 230°C until bubbling.
  4. Brown the beef mince with the cumin and taco spices.
  5. Fill the taco shells and put everything out together.

Race day pulled pork rolls

Paprika and brown sugar pulled pork in soft rolls with slaw, a Sunday race day spread.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 8 soft rolls
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Pork shoulder rubbed with paprika and brown sugar, slow roasted until it falls apart, tossed in tangy vinegar sauce, on soft rolls with coleslaw and pickles. Mix 2 tbsp paprika, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp cayenne and 2 tsp salt and rub over a 1.5kg pork shoulder. Roast covered at 150°C for 5 hours until it pulls apart. Simmer 125ml cider vinegar, 80ml tomato sauce and 1 tbsp brown sugar for 3 minutes and toss through the shredded pork. Toss half a shredded cabbage with mayo. Pile into 8 soft rolls with slaw and pickles.

Sweets

Chequered flag browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies, every second square dusted white like a flag.

Prep 3 min, cook 40 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 170°C.
  3. Cool completely, then cut into squares.
  4. Dust every second square with icing sugar like a chequered flag.

Drinks

Iced caramel macchiato, extra FrenchNo alcohol

Double espresso over ice and milk with homemade salted caramel.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Caramel up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 20g butter
  • 4 double espressos
  • 800ml milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Heat the sugar in a pan until amber, then carefully whisk in the cream, butter and a pinch of salt. Cool.
  2. Spoon caramel into four glasses, fill with ice and add cold milk.
  3. Brew the espressos and pour slowly over the back of a spoon so it layers.
  4. Drizzle with more caramel.
