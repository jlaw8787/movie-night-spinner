Snacks

Firehouse pizza night

Thin crust pepperoni pizza with mozzarella.

Prep 8 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best baked just before serving; grate the mozzarella and slice the pepperoni up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1/2 jar pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 150g pepperoni
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Heat the oven to 250°C. Stretch the dough thin on a big tray.
  2. Spread with sauce, grated mozzarella, pepperoni and oregano.
  3. Bake until bubbling.
  4. Cut into squares.

Cocktail franks in puff pastry

Cocktail sausages in puff pastry. Melody the chess ghost can't eat them.

Prep 17 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Wrap the sausages up to 1 day ahead, covered on the tray in the fridge; brush with egg and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 24 cocktail sausages
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar American mustard
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the pastry into 24 strips.
  2. Wrap a strip around each sausage, seam underneath, and lay on a lined tray.
  3. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  4. Bake until puffed and golden.
  5. Line up on a board with tomato sauce and mustard.

Mains

Winston's research lab Reuben sandwiches

Corned beef, sauerkraut and Swiss cheese on buttered rye, with dressing.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the dressing up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; layer and grill the sandwiches on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 400g corned beef
  • 150g sauerkraut
  • 200g Swiss cheese
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 2 pickles
  • 50g butter
  1. Mix the mayonnaise with tomato sauce and chopped pickle for the dressing.
  2. Layer the rye with corned beef, sauerkraut, Swiss cheese and dressing.
  3. Butter the outsides.
  4. Grill in a pan until golden and melting.
  5. Cut in half to serve.

Firemasters' hot chicken noodle soup

Hot soup for Garraka's deep freeze.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2L chicken stock
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 pack bay leaves
  • 150g dried egg noodles
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Chicken noodle soup simmered with carrot, celery and dill, served piping hot with crusty bread. Put 800g chicken thighs in a pot with 2L chicken stock, 1 onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery sticks, all diced, plus 2 bay leaves. Simmer 30 minutes, then lift out the chicken and shred it. Add 150g egg noodles and cook 6 minutes. Return the chicken, season well and stir in a handful of chopped dill and the juice of half a lemon. Serve piping hot with buttered crusty bread.

Sweets

Mini-Puft marshmallow chocolate s'mores

Toasted marshmallow and dark chocolate sandwiched between sweet biscuits.

Prep 3 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best toasted just before serving; break the chocolate into squares up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 12 marshmallows
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 24 plain sweet biscuits
  1. Break the chocolate into squares.
  2. Toast the marshmallows over a flame or under a hot grill until golden.
  3. Sandwich each between two biscuits with chocolate, squashing so it melts.

Ray's occult shop brown butter shortbread

Butter cooked till nutty, creamed with sugar and flour, pressed into a scored round, baked pale.

How it is made
  • 250g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 330g plain flour
  • 30g cornflour
  1. Butter, sugar and flour shortbread pressed into a round, scored and baked at 160°C until pale golden. Melt 250g butter in a pan and cook until it smells nutty and turns golden brown, then chill until firm but soft. Beat with 110g caster sugar until pale. Mix in 330g plain flour, 30g cornflour and a pinch of salt. Press into a lined 23cm round tin, mark into 12 wedges and prick with a fork. Bake at 160°C for 35 to 40 minutes until pale golden. Sprinkle with caster sugar and cut along the lines while warm.

Drinks

Slimer's ectoplasm lime fizzNo alcohol

Lime cordial and soda over crushed ice with a scoop of lime sorbet.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Best built just before serving so the sorbet holds; chill the glasses and soda water the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml lime cordial
  • 800ml soda water
  • 4 scoops lime sorbet
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with crushed ice.
  2. Add lime cordial to each and top with soda water.
  3. Float a scoop of sorbet on top.
