Snacks

Stan Mikita's sugar pucksVegetarian

Fried doughnut balls tossed in cinnamon sugar.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix and roll the dough up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the flour, a little of the sugar, egg, milk and butter into a soft dough.
  2. Roll into small balls.
  3. Fry in the oil at 175°C, turning, until golden, working in batches.
  4. Toss in the remaining sugar mixed with cinnamon while still warm.

Waynestock chilli lime corn cobsVegetarian

Grilled corn with chilli lime butter.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli lime butter up to 3 days ahead, covered in the fridge; grill the corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 corn cobs, husked
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  1. Mash the butter with the lime zest, chilli, paprika and a pinch of salt.
  2. Grill the corn over high heat, turning, until charred in spots.
  3. Brush the hot cobs with the chilli lime butter and roll in the parmesan.
  4. Scatter with coriander and serve with lime wedges.

Mains

Aurora basement deep-dish pizza

Deep-dish pizza with mozzarella, Italian sausage and tomato sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Simmer the tomato sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; assemble and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g mozzarella, sliced
  • 300g Italian sausages, meat squeezed out
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 pinch sugar
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  1. Press the dough into a 26cm cast iron pan or cake tin brushed with the olive oil, pushing it well up the sides.
  2. Layer the mozzarella over the base, then the sausage meat torn into small pieces.
  3. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic, oregano and sugar, then spread over the top.
  4. Scatter with parmesan and bake at 220°C.
  5. Rest before cutting.

Sweets

Backstage choc cookie ice cream sandwichesVegetarian

Chocolate cookies sandwiching vanilla ice cream, edges in sprinkles.

Prep 18 min, cook 27 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the cookies up to 2 days ahead; sandwich and freeze to firm up, then keep frozen up to 1 week
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 180g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 100g chocolate chips
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 3 tbsp rainbow sprinkles, for the edges
  1. Beat the butter and sugar with the egg. Mix in the flour, cocoa, bicarbonate of soda and chocolate chips.
  2. Roll into balls, flatten slightly and bake at 180°C. Cool completely.
  3. Soften the ice cream.
  4. Sandwich a thick scoop between two cookies and press together. Roll the edges in sprinkles.
  5. Freeze to firm up.

Drinks

Waynestock pink lemonade slushieVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemon and raspberry slush, tart and icy.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the syrup and lemon juice mix up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; blend with the raspberries, water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 6 lemons, 5 juiced and 1 sliced
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 400ml cold water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Heat the sugar with water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Blend the syrup with the lemon juice, raspberries, cold water and ice until slushy. Taste and adjust.
  3. Pour into big cups and garnish with lemon wheels and a few raspberries.
