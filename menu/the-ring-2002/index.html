Smoked trout and cheddar snack plate

Hot smoked trout, sharp cheddar, apple, pickled onions and crackers

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Set out the crackers, cheddar, apple and pickled onions up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; flake the trout just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g hot-smoked trout
  • 1 packet crackers
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  • 1 apple, sliced
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Flake the trout onto a wooden board.
  2. Add the crackers, cheddar, apple and pickled onions the way a mountain lodge would set it out.

Cabin 12 dill picklesVegetarian

Thin sliced cucumbers pickled in vinegar with dill and garlic, chilled overnight

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 7 days to rest or set · Make ahead: Needs 7 days in the fridge, keeps 1 month
Recipe for 4
  • 500g small cucumbers, sliced thin
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 250ml white vinegar
  • 250ml water
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp salt
  1. Pack the cucumbers into a jar with the dill, garlic and peppercorns.
  2. Boil the vinegar, water, sugar and salt, then pour over the cucumbers.
  3. Refrigerate for seven days before eating.

Seattle clam chowder cupsOn the printed menu

Fresh vongole, smoky bacon, potato and cream, thick and served small with crackers and chives

How it is made
  • 1kg vongole clams
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 400g potatoes
  • 200ml cream
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Thick New England clam chowder of fresh clams, smoky bacon, potato and cream, served in small cups with crackers and snipped chives. Steam 1kg vongole in 200ml water in a covered pot for 4 minutes until open, then pick out the meat and strain the liquid. Fry 150g diced bacon, add 1 diced onion and 2 diced celery sticks and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then the clam liquid, 300ml milk and 400g diced potato. Simmer for 15 minutes until the potato is tender, add 200ml cream and the clams and warm through. Season with pepper and serve with crackers and chives.

Ferry crossing salmon

Salmon brushed with miso, honey and soy and roasted, wild rice, charred broccolini

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Mix the miso glaze up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; cook the rice and roast the salmon on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 250g wild rice blend
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  1. Mix the miso, honey and soy and brush over the salmon.
  2. Cook the rice until tender.
  3. Roast the salmon at 220°C for 8 minutes.
  4. Char the broccolini in a hot pan and serve.

Moesko Island beef and dark ale stewOn the printed menu

Chuck braised with pearl barley and onion, horseradish cream, for the Morgans' horse farm

How it is made
  • 1.2kg chuck steak
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 375ml dark ale
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 80g pearl barley
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 jar horseradish
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Chuck steak braised low with dark ale, pearl barley, carrot and onion until spoon-soft, with crusty bread and a sharp horseradish cream. Toss 1.2kg diced chuck steak in 2 tbsp seasoned flour and brown in batches in oil. Add 2 sliced onions and 3 chunky carrots and cook for 5 minutes. Pour in 375ml dark ale and 500ml beef stock, add 80g pearl barley, 2 bay leaves and thyme, cover and cook at 160°C for 2.5 hours until tender. Stir 2 tbsp grated horseradish into 150ml sour cream with a pinch of salt. Serve the stew with crusty bread and the horseradish cream.

Well-deep chocolate potsVegetarian

Baked dark chocolate custard in deep cups

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 450ml thickened cream, 300ml for the pots and the rest for piping
  • 200ml milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  1. Warm the milk and most of the cream, pour over the chocolate and stir smooth.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar and combine with the chocolate cream.
  3. Pour into deep cups and bake in a water bath at 150°C for 30 minutes.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Pipe a ring of whipped cream on top.

Shelter Mountain hot toddyVegetarian

Whisky, honey and lemon topped with boiling water and a cinnamon stick

Prep 5 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemon up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; build each mug with boiling water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 160ml whisky
  • 600ml boiling water
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  1. Put honey, a splash of lemon juice and whisky in each of four mugs.
  2. Top with boiling water, stir and add a cinnamon stick.
