Snacks

Wisconsin cheese curdsVegetarian

Cheese curds in a crisp lager batter, fried and served with dipping sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml lager
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 400g cheese curds
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150ml ranch dressing
  1. Whisk the flour, egg, lager and salt into a smooth batter.
  2. Heat the oil to 185°C.
  3. Dip the curds in batches and fry until puffed and golden.
  4. Drain on paper and serve straight away with ranch dressing.

Golden friesVegetarian

Double-fried russet fries with salt and paprika in paper cones.

Prep 45 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Cut and soak the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead in cold water, then dry well; best fried fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg russet potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries, soak and dry well.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C and cool.
  3. Fry again at 190°C until golden.
  4. Toss with salt and paprika and serve in paper cones.

Dining car turkey club bites

Triple-stack turkey club bites with bacon.

How it is made
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 1 loaf white sandwich bread
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch chives
  • Dijon mustard
  • 300g sliced roast turkey
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  1. Toasted triple-stack club sandwiches of roast turkey, crispy bacon, tomato, cos and herb mayo, cut into small triangles and pinned with toothpicks. Grill 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Toast 12 slices of white bread. Mix 100g mayo with 1 tbsp chopped chives and a little Dijon. For each club, spread 3 slices of toast with mayo, then layer 300g sliced roast turkey and cos on the first, bacon and sliced tomato on the second, and close with the third. Press down, pin with 4 toothpicks and cut into 4 triangles. Serve with crisps.

Mains

Mooby's cow burgers

Smashed beef patties, American cheese, pickles, onion, special sauce.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 burger buns
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and a chopped pickle for the sauce.
  2. Smash each ball flat on a smoking griddle, season and cook, then flip and top with cheese.
  3. Toast the buns. Stack 2 patties per bun with sauce, sliced pickles and diced onion.

Bethany's chicken and leek pot pie

Thigh, leek, carrot and peas in creamy thyme sauce under puff.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 20g butter
  • 2 leeks
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Chicken, leek, carrot and peas in a creamy thyme sauce under a golden puff pastry lid, baked until it bubbles up through the edges. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brown 800g diced chicken thigh in 20g butter, remove. Cook 2 sliced leeks, 2 diced carrots and 2 celery sticks for 6 minutes. Stir in 3 tbsp plain flour, then 400ml chicken stock and 150ml cream and simmer until thick. Add the chicken, 150g frozen peas, thyme, salt and pepper. Spoon into a pie dish, top with 1 puff pastry sheet, cut a vent, brush with egg and bake 25 to 30 minutes.

Sweets

Golden calf caramel sliceVegetarian

Shortbread base, golden caramel and milk chocolate top.

Prep 12 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, cold, for the base
  • 200g plain flour
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 60g butter, for the caramel
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 200g milk chocolate
  1. Rub the butter into the flour and sugar, press into a lined tin.
  2. Bake the base at 180°C.
  3. Simmer the condensed milk, butter and golden syrup until thick and golden, then pour over the base.
  4. Bake again.
  5. Cool, top with melted chocolate and cut into squares.

Vanilla shakeNo alcohol

Thick vanilla ice cream milkshake served in tall cups.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Blend fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and vanilla until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into 4 tall cups and serve with straws straight away.
