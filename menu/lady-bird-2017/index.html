Snacks

Danny's Thanksgiving crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette with goat cheese, roast pumpkin, crisp sage and honey

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Pumpkin up to 1 day, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 400g butternut pumpkin, diced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 12 sage leaves
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Toss the pumpkin in oil and roast at 200C until soft and golden.
  2. Brush the baguette slices with oil and toast. Fry the sage leaves in a little oil until crisp.
  3. Spread the toasts with goat's cheese, top with pumpkin and sage, and drizzle with honey.

Rosemary smoked salt almondsVegetarian

Almonds roasted with olive oil, rosemary and smoked salt, with green olives

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g almonds
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tsp smoked salt
  • 300g green olives
  1. Toss the almonds with the oil, rosemary and smoked salt on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180C until fragrant. Serve warm in a bowl beside the olives.

Mains

Mum's chicken, broccoli and rice bake

Chicken and broccoli baked over rice in cream stock, cheddar on top

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, diced
  • 2 heads broccoli, cut into florets
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 750ml hot chicken stock
  • 150ml cream
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in batches.
  2. Spread the rice in a baking dish, add the chicken and broccoli, then pour over the hot stock mixed with the cream.
  3. Cover and bake at 180C.
  4. Top with cheddar and bake uncovered until golden.

Sweets

Immaculate Heart bake sale browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies cut in squares

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together and cool slightly. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Pour into a lined tin and bake at 170C.
  3. Cool completely, cut into squares and wrap each in paper for the stall.

Drinks

Café shift brown sugar cinnamon latteVegetarianNo alcohol

Espresso with brown sugar cinnamon syrup and steamed milk

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, make lattes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 250ml very strong coffee, or 4 espresso shots
  • 600ml milk
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and cinnamon stick for 3 minutes, then cool.
  2. Brew the coffee. Put 1 tablespoon of syrup in each of four glasses and add the coffee.
  3. Heat the milk until steaming and froth it with a whisk or frother.
  4. Pour the milk slowly over the coffee, holding back the foam, then spoon the foam on top and dust with cinnamon.
