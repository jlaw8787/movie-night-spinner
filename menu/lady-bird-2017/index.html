Snacks

Danny's Thanksgiving crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette with goat cheese, roast pumpkin, crisp sage and honey

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Pumpkin up to 1 day, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 400g butternut pumpkin, diced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 12 sage leaves
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Toss the pumpkin in oil and roast at 200°C until soft and golden.
  2. Brush the baguette slices with oil and toast. Fry the sage leaves in a little oil until crisp.
  3. Spread the toasts with goat's cheese, top with pumpkin and sage, and drizzle with honey.

Rosemary smoked salt almondsVegetarian

Almonds roasted with olive oil, rosemary and smoked salt, with green olives

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g almonds
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tsp smoked salt
  • 300g green olives
  1. Toss the almonds with the oil, rosemary and smoked salt on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180°C until fragrant. Serve warm in a bowl beside the olives.

Communion wafer rice paper crispsOn the printed menu

Lady Bird and Julie eating the unblessed wafers like chips

How it is made
  • 1 packet rice paper sheets
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 jar fine salt
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime
  1. Rice paper rounds fried for seconds until they puff into crisp white wafers, dusted with salt and chilli and served with a sweet chilli lime dip. Cut 10 rice paper sheets into quarters or rounds with scissors. Heat 3cm oil in a wok to 180°C. Drop pieces in one at a time; they puff in 2 seconds, so lift out straight away and drain on paper. Dust with fine salt and a pinch of chilli powder. Mix 100ml sweet chilli sauce with the juice of a lime for the dip.

Mains

Mum's chicken, broccoli and rice bake

Chicken and broccoli baked over rice in cream stock, cheddar on top

Prep 10 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, diced
  • 2 heads broccoli, cut into florets
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 750ml hot chicken stock
  • 150ml cream
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in batches.
  2. Spread the rice in a baking dish, add the chicken and broccoli, then pour over the hot stock mixed with the cream.
  3. Cover and bake at 180°C.
  4. Top with cheddar and bake uncovered until golden.

Thanksgiving turkey with sage stuffingOn the printed menu

Brined breast roasted in thyme butter, onion dressing, mash and gravy, in the house she wanted

How it is made
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 1 bag salt
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 200g day-old bread
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 egg
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 bag plain flour
  1. Brined turkey breast roasted with thyme butter until juicy, with sage and onion stuffing, buttery mash, green beans and pan gravy. Brine a 1.5kg turkey breast overnight in 2L water with 100g salt and 50g sugar. Pat dry, rub with 40g butter and thyme and roast at 180°C to 70°C inside, about 75 minutes, then rest. Mix 200g torn bread with a fried onion, chopped sage, an egg and 150ml stock and bake 30 minutes. Mash 1kg boiled potatoes with butter and milk. Steam 300g beans. Make gravy from the pan juices.

Immaculate Heart bake sale browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies cut in squares

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together and cool slightly. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Pour into a lined tin and bake at 170°C.
  3. Cool completely, cut into squares and wrap each in paper for the stall.

Café shift brown sugar cinnamon latteVegetarianNo alcohol

Espresso with brown sugar cinnamon syrup and steamed milk

Prep 12 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, make lattes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 250ml very strong coffee, or 4 espresso shots
  • 600ml milk
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and cinnamon stick for 3 minutes, then cool.
  2. Brew the coffee. Put 1 tablespoon of syrup in each of four glasses and add the coffee.
  3. Heat the milk until steaming and froth it with a whisk or frother.
  4. Pour the milk slowly over the coffee, holding back the foam, then spoon the foam on top and dust with cinnamon.
