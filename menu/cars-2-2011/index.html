Snacks

Sesame chilli edamameVegetarian

Tokyo party edamame, tossed warm in sesame oil, chilli and sea salt

Prep 5 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Best boiled and tossed just before serving; mix the sesame oil and chilli flakes up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen edamame
  • 1 tbsp salt, for the water
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil.
  2. Boil the edamame and drain.
  3. Toss while hot with sesame oil, chilli flakes and sea salt.
  4. Pile into small bowls.

Ham and gruyere crêpes

Thin crêpes folded around ham and gruyere, crisped in the pan

Prep 13 min, cook 23 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml milk
  • 50g butter
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g gruyere, grated
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk into a smooth batter.
  2. Rest the batter.
  3. Melt the butter and cook thin crepes in a pan, brushing it with a little butter each time.
  4. Fill each with ham and gruyere and fold into quarters.
  5. Crisp again in the pan.
  6. Cut into triangles and serve hot.

London chip shop chips with curry sauceOn the printed menu

Thick-cut spuds fried twice and salted in paper cones, mild spiced gravy and malt vinegar

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 1 onion
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar mild curry powder
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 1 bag sultanas
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle malt vinegar
  1. Thick-cut chips fried twice until crunchy, salted and served in paper cones with a mild chip shop curry sauce and malt vinegar. Cut 1kg washed potatoes into thick chips and dry well. Fry at 140C for 6 minutes, drain and cool, then fry at 190C for 3 minutes until crisp and golden. For the sauce, soften 1 diced onion in 20g butter, stir in 1 tbsp mild curry powder and 1 tbsp plain flour, then whisk in 400ml chicken stock, 1 tbsp sultanas and 1 tsp honey. Simmer 10 minutes and blitz smooth. Salt the chips and serve with sauce and vinegar.

Mains

Porto Corsa prawn pasta

Prawns and cherry tomatoes with garlic, chilli and chicken stock

Prep 18 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and chop the parsley up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; cook the sauce and pasta on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 400g green prawns
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • pinch of chilli flakes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 100ml chicken stock
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  1. Peel the prawns, halve the tomatoes and chop the parsley.
  2. Boil the spaghetti until al dente.
  3. Soften the garlic and chilli in the olive oil.
  4. Add the prawns, tomatoes and stock and cook until the prawns turn pink.
  5. Toss through the spaghetti with the parsley.

Uncle Topolino's chicken cacciatoreOn the printed menu

Chicken simmered with tomato, olives and rosemary, from Uncle Topolino's little Italian village

How it is made
  • 8 chicken thigh cutlets
  • 1 onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 125ml chicken stock
  • 80g kalamata olives
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Chicken thighs browned and simmered 45 minutes with tomato, olives, capsicum and rosemary until the sauce is thick, served with crusty bread. Season 8 chicken thigh pieces on the bone and brown in olive oil, skin side first, then set aside. Soften 1 sliced onion, 1 sliced red capsicum and 3 garlic cloves in the pan. Add 2 rosemary sprigs, a 400g tin crushed tomatoes and 125ml chicken stock. Return the chicken, cover and simmer 45 minutes. Stir in 80g pitted kalamata olives for the last 10 minutes. Serve with a torn crusty loaf for the sauce.

Sweets

Pistachio ice creamVegetarian

Green, nutty ice cream with crushed pistachios and honey. No wasabi this time.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Toast and crush the pistachios up to 3 days ahead and store airtight; scoop and serve on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1l pistachio ice cream
  • 100g pistachios
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Toast the pistachios in a dry pan, then crush.
  2. Scoop ice cream into chilled bowls.
  3. Scatter with pistachios and drizzle with honey.

Porto Corsa blood orange sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Blood orange and lemon juice with honey syrup. A lemon that works.

Prep 14 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to 3 days ahead and the juice mix up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; add ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp warm water
  • 400ml blood orange juice
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 orange
  • 400ml cold sparkling water
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Stir the honey into the warm water to make a syrup.
  2. Mix the blood orange juice, lemon juice and syrup.
  3. Slice the orange thinly.
  4. Fill wine glasses with ice and an orange slice each, pour in the juice mix to half full and top with sparkling water.
  5. Finish with a mint sprig and serve straight away.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Mater eats the wasabi in Tokyo thinking it's ice cream.
