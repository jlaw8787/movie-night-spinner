Snacks

Dino's rare steak sliders

Dino gets to Barney's steak first

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; sear the steak to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g rump steak
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 soft slider rolls
  • 4 tbsp tomato relish
  • 1 cup baby rocket
  1. Cook the onions slowly in the butter with a pinch of salt until soft and golden.
  2. Rub the steak with oil and salt and sear in a very hot pan for 2 to 3 minutes a side, then rest it.
  3. Slice the steak thinly across the grain.
  4. Split the rolls and fill with rocket, steak, onions and a spoon of relish.

Number 2 chisel cheese strawsVegetarian

Barney swaps his test for Fred's

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Twist the straws up to a day ahead and chill on the tray; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 60g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250ml passata
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brush the pastry with egg and scatter with the parmesan and paprika.
  2. Cut into 2cm strips and twist each one like a chisel handle.
  3. Bake on a lined tray at 200C until puffed and golden.
  4. Warm the passata with the garlic and oil for a dip.

Bronto ribs at the Bedrock Drive-In

Family night at the Bedrock Drive-In

Prep 20 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Braise the ribs up to 2 days ahead and chill in their liquid; glaze and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.6kg beef short ribs
  • 1 brown onion, quartered
  • 1l beef stock
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Season the ribs and brown them all over in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the onion and stock, cover and bake at 160C until the meat almost falls off the bone.
  3. Simmer the barbecue sauce with the sugar and half the vinegar until sticky.
  4. Brush the ribs with the glaze and grill until charred at the edges.
  5. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayo and the rest of the vinegar and serve with the ribs.

Ripe old age of 38 T-bones

Fred says red meat never hurt his dad

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Mix the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead and chill; bake the potatoes and grill the steaks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 T-bone steaks, about 350g each
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 80g soft butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Prick the potatoes, rub with oil and salt and bake at 200C until the skins are crisp.
  2. Mash the butter with the garlic and parsley.
  3. Grill the T-bones over high heat, 4 to 5 minutes a side, then rest them.
  4. Split the potatoes and top with sour cream and chives.
  5. Serve each steak with a slice of garlic butter melting on top.

Concretia's chocolate concrete slabVegetarian

Mr Slate names it after his daughter

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep in a tin; add the rubble to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 200g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 125ml milk
  • 6 chocolate sandwich cookies, crushed
  • 250ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together, then stir in the sugar.
  2. Beat in the eggs, then fold in the flour, cocoa and milk.
  3. Pour into a lined 20cm square tin and bake at 160C until just set.
  4. Cool, cut into slabs and top with cream and crushed cookie rubble.

Barney raises his lava juice to Fred

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices up to a day ahead and chill; top with ginger beer to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml blood orange juice
  • 250ml mango nectar
  • 1 lime
  • 750ml ginger beer
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Stir the blood orange juice, mango nectar and lime juice together in a jug.
  2. Fill 4 glasses with ice, pour in the juice and top with ginger beer.
