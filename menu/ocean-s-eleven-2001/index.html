Rusty's prawn cocktail

Poached prawns on shredded iceberg with tomato horseradish sauce in martini glasses

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce
  1. Poach the prawns in salted water for 2 minutes, plunge into iced water and peel.
  2. Mix the tomato sauce, horseradish, lemon juice and Worcestershire sauce.
  3. Chill the prawns.
  4. Serve over shredded iceberg in martini glasses with lemon wedges.

High roller chipsVegetarian

Take a pinch. Basher's blacks out all of Las Vegas.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Slice and rinse the potatoes up to 2 hours ahead in cold water, then dry very well; fry and season just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp truffle salt
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Slice the potatoes paper thin on a mandoline, rinse and dry very well.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170C in batches until golden.
  3. Toss straight away with truffle salt and finely grated parmesan so it sticks.
  4. Serve in a silver bowl.

Mains

Benedict's casino rib eye with béarnaise

Benedict would give up Tess to get the money back.

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the steaks the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1 shallot
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 200g butter
  • 500g spinach
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 600g frozen shoestring fries
  1. Dry-brine the steaks overnight.
  2. Bake the fries at 220C.
  3. Reduce the vinegar with tarragon and the chopped shallot to a spoonful.
  4. Whisk with the yolks over simmering water and drizzle in the warm melted butter.
  5. Wilt the spinach, squeeze and simmer in the cream.
  6. Sear the steaks, pull at 54C and rest.

Saul's matzo ball chicken soupOn the printed menu

Chicken soup and matzo balls. Saul makes ten.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs on the bone
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 120g matzo meal
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Golden chicken broth simmered with onion, carrot and celery, with fluffy matzo balls, shredded chicken, fresh dill and cracked pepper. Simmer 1kg chicken thighs on the bone in 2L water with an onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery sticks for 1 hour. Strain, shred the chicken and slice the carrots back in. Mix 120g matzo meal, 3 eggs, 3 tbsp melted chicken fat or oil, 3 tbsp broth and salt and chill 30 minutes. Roll into 12 balls and simmer in the broth, covered, for 30 minutes. Finish with dill.

Fountain show sundaeVegetarian

Eat it by the Bellagio fountains at dawn, then leave one by one.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fudge up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 1 jar gold sprinkles
  1. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream for hot fudge.
  2. Whip the remaining cream.
  3. Scoop ice cream into four tall glasses and pour over the fudge.
  4. Pipe whipped cream high and scatter gold sprinkles so they catch the light.

Casino martiniVegetarian

Gin and a touch of dry vermouth stirred ice cold, with a green olive

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses and the gin in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; stir each drink fresh over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 1 jar green olives
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the gin and vermouth over plenty of ice until very cold, one drink at a time.
  2. Strain into chilled martini glasses and add a green olive on a pick.

When to serve

Serve the prawn cocktail whenever Rusty is eating, which is most scenes.
