Amber's party potato skinsVegetarian

Crisp baked potato skins loaded with melted cheddar, sour cream, scallion and jalapeño.

Prep 11 min, cook 1 hr 20 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake and scoop the potatoes up to 2 days ahead and keep them in the fridge; crisp, fill and top on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 medium potatoes, about 1.2kg, scrubbed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeños, chopped
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C, prick the potatoes and bake until tender, 1 hr.
  2. Cool until you can handle them, 10 min.
  3. Halve and scoop out most of the flesh, leaving a thin wall.
  4. Brush the skins with the oil, season with salt, pepper and paprika and roast, turning once, until crisp, 15 min.
  5. Fill with the cheddar and bake until melted, 5 min.
  6. Top with sour cream, spring onions and jalapeños.

Dewey's trailer bar mixVegetarian

Peanuts, cashews and pretzels roasted with butter, brown sugar, smoked paprika and cayenne.

Prep 3 min, cook 28 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200g raw peanuts
  • 150g raw cashews
  • 100g pretzels
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Mix the butter with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder, cayenne and salt, 3 min.
  2. Toss the nuts and pretzels in the butter mix and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast, stirring every 10 minutes, until golden.
  4. Cool on the tray until crisp, 20 min.

Woodsboro motel smash burgers

Double smashed beef patties with melted cheese, onion, pickles and burger sauce on toasted buns.

Prep 13 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce and portion the mince into balls up to 1 day ahead; smash and cook to order
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince, 20 percent fat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp pickle brine
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 20g butter
  • 1 onion, very finely sliced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 8 slices of burger cheese
  • 1 cup iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1/2 cup dill pickles, sliced
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls and keep cold.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, mustard and pickle brine.
  3. Toast the cut sides of the buns in the butter, 4 min.
  4. Heat a heavy pan until very hot. In batches, put in the balls, top with a little onion and smash flat. Season with salt and pepper, cook until deeply browned, flip, add cheese and cook a minute more, 12 min.
  5. Build the buns with sauce, lettuce, two patties each and pickles.

Mindy's movie rules pepperoni pizza

Crisp base with passata, mozzarella, pepperoni and chilli flakes, arugula on the side.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large pizza bases
  • 200ml tomato passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • 120g pepperoni, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 100g rocket
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven to 240°C with the trays inside, 10 min.
  2. Spread the bases with passata and oregano, then top with mozzarella, pepperoni and onion.
  3. Bake until crisp and bubbling, 12 min.
  4. Scatter with chilli flakes and serve with rocket dressed with oil.

Tara's Babadook browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies with a raspberry swirl and a crackly top.

Prep 12 min, cook 36 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight; they cut cleaner the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 80g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp raspberry jam
  • 125g raspberries, fresh or frozen
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a 20cm square tin, 3 min.
  2. Melt the chocolate with the butter, 5 min.
  3. Whisk the sugar, eggs and vanilla until pale and thick.
  4. Fold in the chocolate, then the flour, cocoa and salt.
  5. Pour into the tin, dot with jam and raspberries and swirl with a skewer.
  6. Bake until just set in the middle, 28 min.
  7. Cool completely in the tin before cutting, 1 hr.

Drinks

Macher house cherry rum and cola

Dark rum over ice with muddled cherries and lime, topped with cola.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Pit the cherries and cut the limes up to 1 day ahead; build each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cherries, pitted, fresh or frozen
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 3 cups ice
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 800ml cola, chilled
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  1. Muddle the cherries with the lime juice in four tall glasses.
  2. Fill with ice and add the rum.
  3. Top with cola, stir and finish with a lime wedge.
