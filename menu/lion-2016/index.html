Mum's potato and pea samosasVegetarian

Fried pastry cones filled with spiced potato and peas.

Prep 43 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes, peeled
  • 150g peas
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 250g plain flour
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil, for the dough
  • 120ml water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • tamarind chutney, to serve
  1. Boil the potatoes, drain and crush with the peas and spices, 20 min.
  2. Make a firm dough from the flour, the vegetable oil for the dough and the water, then rest, 15 min.
  3. Roll into rounds, cut in half and fold into cones.
  4. Fill and seal the samosas.
  5. Heat the vegetable oil for frying to 175°C and fry until blistered and golden, 15 min.
  6. Serve with tamarind chutney.

Tasmanian smoked salmon

Rye bites with lemon crème fraîche, smoked salmon, capers and dill.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 slices of rye bread
  • 30g butter
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 4 sprigs of dill
  • 1 pinch of cracked black pepper
  1. Butter the rye and cut into small pieces.
  2. Whip the crème fraîche with the lemon zest.
  3. Top each piece with smoked salmon, a spoon of cream, capers and dill.
  4. Finish with black pepper.

Masala tea with cardamom nankhatai

Ghee and pistachio shortbread with hot sweet spiced tea, a taste he never lost.

How it is made
  • 100g ghee
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 150g plain flour
  • 30g fine semolina
  • 1 jar ground cardamom
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 30g pistachios
  • 1 packet loose black tea
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 jar cardamom pods
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  1. Crumbly Indian shortbread biscuits with cardamom, ghee and pistachio, baked pale and cracked on top, served with small cups of hot sweet chai. Beat 100g ghee with 100g icing sugar until fluffy. Mix in 150g plain flour, 30g fine semolina, half a teaspoon of ground cardamom and a pinch of baking powder. Roll into 16 balls, press a pistachio on each and bake at 170°C for 15 minutes until pale and cracked. Simmer 500ml water with 2 tbsp loose black tea, ginger and 4 crushed cardamom pods for 3 minutes, add 500ml milk and sugar, simmer 2 minutes and strain.

Sue's yellow dal and riceVegetarian

Yellow split pea dal topped with cumin, garlic and chilli ghee, served with rice.

Prep 5 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Dal up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g yellow split peas
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 3 cloves of garlic, sliced
  • 1 dried chilli
  • 300g basmati rice
  • salt, to taste
  1. Simmer the split peas with the turmeric in plenty of water until soft, 45 min.
  2. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  3. Heat the ghee and fry the cumin, garlic and chilli until fragrant, 5 min.
  4. Pour over the dal and season.
  5. Serve with the rice.

Hobart roast lamb with honey roast veg

Garlic and rosemary leg, sticky carrots and parsnips, peas and gravy, for the Brierleys' table.

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 parsnips
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Tasmanian lamb leg roasted with garlic and rosemary, with honey-roasted carrots, parsnips and potatoes, peas and a rich pan gravy. Heat the oven to 220°C. Stud a 2kg lamb leg with garlic and rosemary, rub with oil and salt and roast 20 minutes, then at 180°C for 60 minutes. Toss 1kg potatoes, 3 carrots and 2 parsnips in oil and 1 tbsp honey and roast alongside for the last hour. Rest the lamb 20 minutes. Make gravy with the tray juices, 1 tbsp flour and 400ml stock. Cook 300g peas and carve.

Jalebi, crisp saffron syrup spiralsVegetarian

Fried yoghurt batter spirals soaked in cardamom and saffron sugar syrup.

Prep 3 min, cook 28 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Batter rests overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 100g plain yoghurt
  • 1 pinch of saffron, for the batter
  • 150ml water, for the batter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 200ml water, for the syrup
  • 3 cardamom pods
  • 1 pinch of saffron, for the syrup
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the flour, yoghurt, the saffron for the batter and the water for the batter into a thick batter and rest overnight.
  2. Boil the sugar with the water for the syrup, the cardamom and the saffron for the syrup, 8 min.
  3. Heat the oil to 170°C, 8 min.
  4. Pipe spirals into the oil and fry until crisp, 12 min.
  5. Soak in the warm syrup for a minute, 3 min.

Masala chaiNo alcohol

Spiced milk tea with cardamom, ginger, cinnamon and clove.

Prep 1 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 whole clove
  • 4 tea bags
  • 500ml milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  1. Bring the water to the boil with the cardamom, ginger, cinnamon and clove, 4 min.
  2. Add the tea bags and simmer.
  3. Pour in the milk and sugar and simmer, 5 min.
  4. Strain into small cups.
