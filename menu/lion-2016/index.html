Snacks

Kolkata street samosasVegetarian

Fried pastry cones filled with spiced potato and peas

Prep 60 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes, peeled
  • 150g peas
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 250g plain flour
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil, for the dough
  • 120ml water
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • tamarind chutney, to serve
  1. Boil the potatoes, drain and crush with the peas and spices.
  2. Make a firm dough from the flour, the vegetable oil for the dough and the water, then rest.
  3. Roll into rounds, cut in half and fold into cones.
  4. Fill and seal the samosas.
  5. Heat the vegetable oil for frying to 175C and fry until blistered and golden.
  6. Serve with tamarind chutney.

Tasmanian smoked salmon

Rye bites with lemon crème fraîche, smoked salmon, capers and dill

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 slices of rye bread
  • 30g butter
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 4 sprigs of dill
  • 1 pinch of cracked black pepper
  1. Butter the rye and cut into small pieces.
  2. Whip the crème fraîche with the lemon zest.
  3. Top each piece with smoked salmon, a spoon of cream, capers and dill.
  4. Finish with black pepper.

Mains

Village dal and riceVegetarian

Yellow split pea dal topped with cumin, garlic and chilli ghee, served with rice

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Dal up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g yellow split peas
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 3 cloves of garlic, sliced
  • 1 dried chilli
  • 300g basmati rice
  • salt, to taste
  1. Simmer the split peas with the turmeric in plenty of water until soft.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Heat the ghee and fry the cumin, garlic and chilli until fragrant.
  4. Pour over the dal and season.
  5. Serve with the rice.

Sweets

Jalebi, crisp saffron syrup spiralsVegetarian

Fried yoghurt batter spirals soaked in cardamom and saffron sugar syrup

Prep 30 min, cook 720 min · Make ahead: Batter rests overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 100g plain yoghurt
  • 1 pinch of saffron, for the batter
  • 150ml water, for the batter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 200ml water, for the syrup
  • 3 cardamom pods
  • 1 pinch of saffron, for the syrup
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the flour, yoghurt, the saffron for the batter and the water for the batter into a thick batter and rest overnight.
  2. Boil the sugar with the water for the syrup, the cardamom and the saffron for the syrup.
  3. Heat the oil to 170C.
  4. Pipe spirals into the oil and fry until crisp.
  5. Soak in the warm syrup for a minute.

Masala chaiVegetarianNo alcohol

Spiced milk tea with cardamom, ginger, cinnamon and clove

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 whole clove
  • 4 tea bags
  • 500ml milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  1. Bring the water to the boil with the cardamom, ginger, cinnamon and clove.
  2. Add the tea bags and simmer.
  3. Pour in the milk and sugar and simmer.
  4. Strain into small cups.
