Snacks

Factory stakeout cheese crispsVegetarian

Lacy baked Monterey Jack crisps with a pinch of chilli flakes.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheese up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; bake just before serving so the crisps stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 250g Monterey Jack cheese, grated
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 200g salsa
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line two trays.
  2. Make small piles of cheese on the trays and sprinkle with chilli flakes.
  3. Bake until lacy and golden.
  4. Cool on the tray until crisp and serve with salsa.

Dog bone crackersVegetarian

Crisp parmesan and butter crackers cut into little bone shapes.

Prep 23 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 80g cold butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 60g parmesan, grated
  • 1 egg
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then mix in the parmesan and egg into a dough.
  2. Roll thin and cut into bone shapes.
  3. Bake at 180°C until golden.
  4. Cool on a rack.

Seafood plant garlic chilli prawns

Prawns sizzled with butter, parsley and lemon, garlic strong enough for a dog's nose.

How it is made
  • 800g green king prawns
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 red chilli
  • 80ml fish stock
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 baguette
  • olive oil
  1. Prawns sizzled in olive oil with sliced garlic, chilli, fish stock and butter, finished with parsley and lemon, crusty bread for the pan juices. Peel and devein 800g green king prawns, leaving the tails on. Heat 80ml olive oil in a large pan with 6 sliced garlic cloves and 1 sliced red chilli until the garlic just colours. Add the prawns and cook 2 minutes, turning. Add 80ml fish stock and 30g butter and bubble 1 minute until the prawns are pink. Toss with chopped parsley and lemon juice. Serve with a warm sliced baguette.

Mains

Steak, and the bone for Hooch

Seared T-bone topped with garlic butter, roast potatoes on the side.

Prep 3 min, cook 72 min · Make ahead: Mash the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; roast the potatoes and cook the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 T-bone steaks
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 300g green beans
  1. Halve the potatoes, toss in oil and roast at 220°C.
  2. Mash the butter with crushed garlic.
  3. Sear the T-bones hard in a hot pan.
  4. Finish in a 200°C oven, then rest.
  5. Steam the beans, top the steaks with garlic butter and save a bone for the dog.

Sweets

Caramel shortbread sundaesVegetarian

Shortbread crumbled over vanilla ice cream with caramel sauce.

Prep 4 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Caramel up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 pieces shortbread
  1. Cook the sugar to amber.
  2. Whisk in the cream and salt, then cool.
  3. Whip the thickened cream.
  4. Scoop ice cream into cups and top with whipped cream, crushed shortbread and a drizzle of caramel.

Drinks

Turner's tidy blood orange fizzNo alcohol

Fresh blood orange juice topped with sparkling apple juice and an orange twist

Prep 13 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Squeeze and chill the juice up to 1 day ahead; pour and top with sparkling apple juice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 blood oranges
  • 2 oranges
  • 360ml sparkling apple juice, cold
  1. Squeeze the oranges and strain the juice into a jug.
  2. Chill the juice.
  3. Pour juice into chilled flutes, tilting the glass, then top slowly with sparkling apple juice.
  4. Garnish with a thin twist of orange peel.
