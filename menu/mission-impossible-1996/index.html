Snacks

Prague café chlebíčky open sandwiches

Baguette slices topped with potato salad, ham and sliced egg

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, cover with a damp cloth
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 30g butter, softened
  • 300g potato salad
  • 150g leg ham
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 jar gherkins
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool and slice.
  2. Slice the baguette into rounds and spread with butter.
  3. Top each with potato salad, a fold of ham, a slice of egg, a gherkin slice and parsley.
  4. Line them up neatly on a long board.

Exploding gum bubble crackers

Fresh fried prawn crackers served with sweet chilli and lime sauce

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the sweet chilli and lime dip up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; fry the crackers just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet prawn crackers
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Heat the oil to 190°C.
  2. Fry a few crackers at a time for a few seconds until they puff up dramatically. Drain on paper towel.
  3. Mix the sweet chilli sauce with the lime juice and serve for dipping.

Langley white room cheese stacksOn the printed menu

White cheeses stacked on water crackers, like the white vault at Langley he drops into

How it is made
  • 150g ricotta
  • 150g brie
  • 120g goat's cheese
  • 2 pears
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 packet water crackers
  • 1 jar white pepper
  • 1 jar honey
  1. An all-white board of brie, goat's cheese and whipped ricotta stacked on white water crackers with crisp pear, white pepper and a little honey. Beat 150g ricotta with a pinch of salt until smooth. Slice 150g brie and 120g goat's cheese into neat pieces. Core and thinly slice 2 pears and toss in lemon juice. Stack crackers with a layer of cheese, a slice of pear and a crack of white pepper, and drizzle a little honey over. Lay out on a white plate and serve in silence.

Mains

Prague goulash

Beef braised 3 hours with onions, sweet paprika and caraway

Prep 20 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 3 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml water
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 crusty Italian loaf
  • salt, to taste
  1. Brown the beef in batches in the oil in a hot heavy pot.
  2. Cook the onions slowly for 20 minutes, then stir in the paprika, caraway and tomato paste.
  3. Add the water, return the beef and cover. Braise at 160°C for 3 hours.
  4. Season and serve with crusty bread.

Aquarium restaurant snapper, caper butterOn the printed menu

Fillets fried skin down until crackling, nutty capers and lemon, crushed dill potatoes

How it is made
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 90g butter
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 lemon
  1. Snapper fillets pan-fried skin down until crackling, finished with nutty brown butter, capers and lemon, with crushed dill potatoes. Boil 800g small potatoes for 15 minutes, drain and crush with 30g butter, chopped dill and salt. Score the skin of 4 snapper fillets, season and fry skin side down in 2 tbsp oil for 4 minutes until crisp, then flip for 1 minute. Remove the fish, add 60g butter to the pan and cook until nut brown, then add 1 tbsp capers and the juice of a lemon. Spoon over the fish.

Trdelník cinnamon walnut pastryVegetarian

Sweet yeast dough baked around rolling pins, rolled in cinnamon sugar and walnuts

Prep 35 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best eaten the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 200ml warm milk
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 80g walnuts, chopped
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, a little of the sugar, milk, butter and egg into a soft dough and knead.
  2. Prove for 1 hour.
  3. Roll out, cut into strips and wrap around foil-covered rolling pins. Roll in cinnamon, the rest of the sugar and the walnuts.
  4. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes.
  5. Slide off the pins and fill with whipped cream.

Self-destruct ginger pilsner shandyVegetarianNo alcohol

Pilsner and ginger syrup in a smoked salt rimmed glass

Prep 6 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 50ml water
  • 1 piece ginger, 3cm, sliced
  • 3 lemons
  • 1 tbsp smoked salt
  • 4 bottles Czech pilsner, 330ml each
  • 250ml soda water
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and ginger for 5 minutes, then cool and strain.
  2. Rub the rims of four glasses with lemon and dip in smoked salt.
  3. Add some ginger syrup and the juice of half a lemon to each glass. Pour in the pilsner, top with soda water and finish with grated lemon zest.
