Snacks

Chinatown pork and chive dumplings

Crisp underneath, then steamed. Soy and black vinegar. From the supply run. Splinter only lets them up top for that.

Prep 35 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 bunch garlic chives
  • 1 ginger
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 30 round dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp Chinese black vinegar
  1. Mix the pork, chives, ginger, half the soy and the sesame oil.
  2. Fill, pleat and seal the wrappers.
  3. Fry until the bases are golden, add water, cover and steam.
  4. Mix the rest of the soy with the black vinegar for dipping.

Superfly's smash sliders

Beef smashed thin, American cheese, pickles, plenty of tomato sauce. Molly-whopped. Ketchup everywhere.

Prep 4 min, cook 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 8 slider buns
  • 1 dill pickles
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tomato ketchup
  • 1 American mustard
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls.
  2. Smash flat in a smoking hot pan, season and cook until the edges crust.
  3. Flip, add cheese and cook 1 minute more.
  4. Serve on toasted buns with pickles, onion, mustard and tomato sauce.

Staten Island chilli cheese fries

Thick fries, beef chilli, melted cheddar, jalapeños. Leatherhead says g'day, then takes the big bowl.

How it is made
  1. Bake 1kg frozen thick fries at 220°C for 25 minutes.
  2. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 diced onion and 2 tsp chilli powder, add a 400g tin kidney beans and 200ml passata and simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Spoon over the fries, top with 150g grated cheddar, melt under the grill and finish with sliced jalapeños and spring onion.

Mains

Pizza-box pepperoni pie

Thin New York pie, pepperoni, mozzarella, pickled jalapeños, ranch drizzle. “Can we explain this over some pizza?” Michelangelo

Prep 14 min, cook 12 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 250g grated mozzarella
  • 150g sliced pepperoni
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 50g pickled jalapeños
  • 80ml ranch dressing
  1. Stretch the dough thin on an oiled tray and spread with the sauce.
  2. Scatter the mozzarella, pepperoni and oregano.
  3. Bake at the oven's highest setting, 12 min.
  4. Scatter the jalapeños over the hot pie, drizzle with ranch and cut into big slices.

Superfly's kaiju supreme

Italian sausage, mushroom, bell pepper, red onion, olives. Everything on it. Whale-sized, like Superfly after the zoo.

How it is made
  1. Divide 1kg pizza dough in two and stretch thin.
  2. Spread each with 125ml passata and a pinch of oregano, scatter 400g grated mozzarella between them and top with 300g crumbled Italian sausage, 150g sliced mushrooms, 1 sliced green capsicum, 1 sliced red onion and a handful of black olives.
  3. Bake at the oven's highest setting for 12 minutes.

Sweets

Baxter Stockman's ooze cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes, bright green buttercream piped in drips. Stockman just wanted a family. Here's to Dad.

Prep 18 min, cook 18 min
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 green food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide between 12 cases and bake at 180°C, 18 min.
  3. Beat the butter, icing sugar and colouring until fluffy and pipe on in drips once cool.

Cherry cola over vanilla ice cream, a maraschino cherry on top. The canister that turned Superfly back into a pile of animals.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 1.3 L cherry cola
  • 1 maraschino cherries
  1. Scoop the ice cream into 4 tall glasses.
  2. Slowly pour the cola over and top with a cherry.
