Snacks

Chinatown pork and chive dumplings

Pork and garlic chive dumplings, crisp underneath then steamed, soy and black vinegar. Up top for supplies only

Prep 35 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fold up to a day ahead, or freeze on a tray
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 bunch garlic chives
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 30 round dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp Chinese black vinegar
  1. Mix the pork, chives, ginger, half the soy and the sesame oil.
  2. Fill, pleat and seal the wrappers.
  3. Fry until the bases are golden, add water, cover and steam.
  4. Mix the rest of the soy with the black vinegar for dipping.

Superfly's smash sliders

Beef smashed thin on a screaming hot plate, American cheese, pickles and plenty of ketchup on soft buns. Molly-whopped

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Roll the balls up to a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 8 slider buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bottle tomato ketchup
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls.
  2. Smash flat in a smoking hot pan, season and cook until the edges crust.
  3. Flip, add cheese and cook 1 minute more.
  4. Serve on toasted buns with pickles, onion, mustard and ketchup.

Mains

Pizza-box pepperoni pie with jalapeño ranch

Thin New York pie, red sauce, mozzarella and pepperoni, pickled jalapeños and a ranch drizzle. Can we explain this over some pizza?

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Stretch the base up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 250g grated mozzarella
  • 150g sliced pepperoni
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 50g pickled jalapeños
  • 80ml ranch dressing
  1. Stretch the dough thin on an oiled tray and spread with the sauce.
  2. Scatter the mozzarella, pepperoni and oregano.
  3. Bake at the oven's highest setting.
  4. Scatter the jalapeños over the hot pie, drizzle with ranch and cut into big slices.

Sweets

Baxter Stockman's ooze cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes piped with bright green buttercream ooze. Where the whole mutant family started

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 bottle green food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide between 12 cases and bake at 180C.
  3. Beat the butter, icing sugar and colouring until fluffy and pipe on in drips once cool.

Retro-mutagen cherry cola floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Cherry cola over vanilla ice cream with a maraschino cherry. The canister that turned Superfly back

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 1 1/4 L cherry cola
  • 1 jar maraschino cherries
  1. Scoop the ice cream into 4 tall glasses.
  2. Slowly pour the cola over and top with a cherry.
