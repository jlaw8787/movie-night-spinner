Snacks

Carb-loading kimbapVegetarian

Sushi rice and nori rolls with omelette, carrot and vegetables.

Prep 0 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice and prepare the fillings up to 4 hours ahead; roll and slice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 4 nori sheets
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 carrots
  • 200g spinach
  • 100g pickled radish
  1. Season the cooked sushi rice with sesame oil and salt.
  2. Lay rice on the nori sheets, add strips of omelette from the eggs, sautéed carrot, blanched spinach and pickled radish, then roll tightly and slice into rounds.

Saja Boys' sugar-dusted hot dogs

Frankfurter and mozzarella skewers, battered, fried, rolled in sugar, then ketchup and mustard.

Prep 10 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Thread the skewers up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; batter, crumb and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  1. Thread the frankfurts onto skewers with a stick of mozzarella above.
  2. Whisk the self-raising flour, the egg, the sugar and the milk into a thick batter, pour into a tall glass, dip each skewer and roll in the panko.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170°C for 4 minutes, roll in sugar and drizzle with tomato sauce and mustard.

Honmoon cheesy tteokbokki

Chewy rice cakes in sticky gochujang under melted cheese, refuelling to keep the Honmoon strong.

How it is made
  • 500g Korean rice cakes
  • 1 tsp dashi powder
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 Korean fish cake sheets
  • 3 spring onions
  • 150g mozzarella
  • sesame seeds
  1. Chewy rice cakes and fish cake simmered in a sticky gochujang and anchovy broth, blanketed in melted mozzarella with spring onion and sesame. Soak 500g rice cakes in warm water for 10 minutes. Bring 500ml water with 1 tsp dashi powder to the boil. Stir in 3 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp gochugaru, 1 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp sugar and 2 crushed garlic cloves. Add the rice cakes and 2 sliced fish cakes and simmer 10 minutes, stirring, until thick and glossy. Add 3 chopped spring onions, scatter 150g grated mozzarella over, cover for 2 minutes to melt and finish with sesame seeds.

Pre-game ramyeon with eggVegetarian

Spicy noodles with kimchi, a soft egg and a slice of cheese, pre-show fuel.

Prep 0 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook ahead as the noodles go soft; slice the spring onions up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp gochugaru
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 packets ramen noodles
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g kimchi
  • 3 spring onions
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 2 L water
  1. Bring the water to the boil with the gochugaru, the soy and a stock cube.
  2. Cook the ramen noodles for 3 minutes, crack in the eggs and cover 1 minute.
  3. Divide into bowls with the kimchi, sliced spring onions and a slice of cheese melting on top.

Comfort night beef bone soup

Simmered for hours with noodles and spring onion, a comfort night bowl.

How it is made
  • 2kg beef marrow bones
  • 600g beef brisket
  • 100g somyeon noodles
  • 1.5 cups short-grain rice
  • 4 spring onions
  • 300g radish kimchi
  1. Milky beef bone broth simmered for hours, with sliced brisket, rice and noodles, seasoned at the table with salt and spring onion, radish kimchi alongside. Soak 2kg beef marrow bones and 600g brisket in cold water for 1 hour. Boil the bones 10 minutes, drain and rinse. Cover with 4L fresh water, boil hard with the lid off for 3 hours, topping up, until milky. Add the brisket for the last 90 minutes, then slice it thin. Cook 100g somyeon noodles. Serve bowls of broth with brisket, noodles, cooked rice, sliced spring onion, salt and pepper to season, and 300g radish kimchi.

Sweets

Cinnamon walnut hotteokVegetarian

Chewy fried yeast pancakes filled with brown sugar, cinnamon and walnut.

Prep 78 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Mix the dough and prove it in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; mix the filling up to 2 days ahead, then fill and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 220ml milk
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 40g walnuts
  1. Mix the flour, the dried yeast, the sugar and the warm milk into a sticky dough and prove 1 hour.
  2. Fill the balls with the brown sugar, the cinnamon and the chopped walnuts, flatten in an oiled pan and fry 2 minutes a side until crisp.

Drinks

Golden honey yuja teaNo alcohol

Ginger, yuja citron and honey tea with lemon, hot or iced.

Prep 1 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer the ginger water up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then stir in the citron tea and honey to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 jar yuja citron tea
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 L water
  1. Simmer the water with a 3cm knob of ginger, sliced thin, for 5 minutes.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in the yuja citron tea and the honey until dissolved.
  3. Pour into heatproof glasses, making sure each gets some citron peel, and add a thin lemon slice.
  4. For an iced version, cool it and pour over ice.

When to serve
