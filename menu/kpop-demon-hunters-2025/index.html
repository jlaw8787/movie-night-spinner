Sushi rice and nori rolls with omelette, carrot and vegetables

Prep 40 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice and prepare the fillings up to 4 hours ahead; roll and slice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 4 nori sheets
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 carrots
  • 200g spinach
  • 100g pickled radish
  1. Season the cooked sushi rice with sesame oil and salt.
  2. Lay rice on the nori sheets, add strips of omelette from the eggs, sautéed carrot, blanched spinach and pickled radish, then roll tightly and slice into rounds.

Frankfurter and mozzarella skewers, battered, fried, then ketchup and mustard

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Thread the skewers up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; batter, crumb and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  1. Thread the frankfurts onto skewers with a stick of mozzarella above.
  2. Whisk the self-raising flour, the egg, the sugar and the milk into a thick batter, pour into a tall glass, dip each skewer and roll in the panko.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170C for 4 minutes, roll in sugar and drizzle with tomato sauce and mustard.

Pre-game ramyeon with eggVegetarian

Pre-game ramyeon: spicy ramen, kimchi, egg and cheese

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook ahead as the noodles go soft; slice the spring onions up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp gochugaru
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 packets ramen noodles
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g kimchi
  • 3 spring onions
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 2l water
  1. Bring the water to the boil with the gochugaru, the soy and a stock cube.
  2. Cook the ramen noodles for 3 minutes, crack in the eggs and cover 1 minute.
  3. Divide into bowls with the kimchi, sliced spring onions and a slice of cheese melting on top.

Cinnamon walnut hotteokVegetarian

Chewy fried yeast pancakes filled with brown sugar, cinnamon and walnut

Prep 20 min, cook 72 min · Make ahead: Mix the dough and prove it in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; mix the filling up to 2 days ahead, then fill and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 220ml milk
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 40g walnuts
  1. Mix the flour, the dried yeast, the sugar and the warm milk into a sticky dough and prove 1 hour.
  2. Fill the balls with the brown sugar, the cinnamon and the chopped walnuts, flatten in an oiled pan and fry 2 minutes a side until crisp.

Golden honey yuja teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Ginger, yuja citron and honey tea with lemon, hot or iced

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Simmer the ginger water up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then stir in the citron tea and honey to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 jar yuja citron tea
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  • 1l water
  1. Simmer the water with a 3cm knob of ginger, sliced thin, for 5 minutes.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in the yuja citron tea and the honey until dissolved.
  3. Pour into heatproof glasses, making sure each gets some citron peel, and add a thin lemon slice.
  4. For an iced version, cool it and pour over ice.

When to serve

Serve the ramyeon when the girls clink cups for pre-game ramyeon.
