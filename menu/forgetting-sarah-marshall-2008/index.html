Resort poke on rice crackers

Cubed tuna in soy and sesame oil with spring onion, on rice crackers.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 hour ahead, top the crackers just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sashimi tuna
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 packet rice crackers
  1. Cut the tuna into small cubes.
  2. Toss with soy sauce, sesame oil and sliced spring onion.
  3. Spoon onto rice crackers just before serving so they stay crisp.

Kunu's grilled pineappleVegetarian

Pineapple spears brushed with honey and lime, grilled until charred.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 2 hours, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pineapple
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 limes
  1. Cut the pineapple into long spears.
  2. Brush with honey mixed with lime juice.
  3. Grill over high heat, turning, until caramelised and charred at the edges.
  4. Serve warm with extra lime.

Dracula's Taste for Love beetroot dip

Roast beets blitzed blood red with feta, garlic, walnuts and lemon, smoked paprika pita crisps.

How it is made
  • 3 beetroot
  • 130g feta
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 40g walnuts
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 4 pita breads
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  1. Roast beetroot blitzed blood red with feta, garlic, walnuts and lemon, served with oven-baked pita crisps dusted in smoked paprika. Wrap 3 medium beetroot in foil and roast at 200°C for 50 minutes until tender, then peel. Blitz with 100g feta, 1 garlic clove, 40g walnuts, the juice of 1 lemon and 3 tbsp olive oil until smooth and season. Cut 4 pita breads into wedges, brush with oil, sprinkle with smoked paprika and salt and bake at 180°C for 10 minutes until crisp. Serve the dip with crumbled feta on top.

Luau kalua pork

Pork shoulder slow cooked in banana leaves with smoked salt, over jasmine rice.

Prep 15 min, cook 5.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, shred and reheat covered
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp smoked salt
  • 4 banana leaves
  • 300g jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  1. Rub the pork with smoked salt.
  2. Wrap tightly in banana leaves, then foil.
  3. Cook at 150°C until it falls apart.
  4. While the pork rests, cook the jasmine rice and shred the cabbage with lime juice and sesame oil.
  5. Shred the pork and serve over rice with the cabbage salad.

Resort fish tacos with mango salsa

Chilli and cumin fish tacos with mango salsa, resort lunch by the pool.

How it is made
  • 600g firm white fish fillets
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 mango
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 120g sour cream
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1/4 green cabbage
  1. White fish dusted in chilli and cumin, pan-fried, tucked into warm corn tortillas with mango salsa, lime and coriander. Cut 600g firm white fish fillets into strips and toss with 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, salt and oil. Dice 1 mango, half a red onion and 1 red chilli and mix with coriander and the juice of 1 lime. Pan-fry the fish over high heat for 2 minutes a side until just cooked. Mix 120g sour cream with lime zest. Warm 12 small corn tortillas and fill with shredded cabbage, fish, salsa and lime crema.

Dracula puppet mango lime sorbetVegetarian

Frozen mango and lime ice, scraped with a fork.

Prep 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze up to 1 week ahead, scraping every hour at first; scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 3 ripe mangoes
  • 2 limes
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Blend the mango flesh with the syrup and lime juice until smooth.
  3. Freeze in a tray, scraping with a fork every hour.
  4. Scoop into glasses.

Drinks

Dark rum mai tai

Dark rum, orange juice, lime and sugar syrup over crushed ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix per glass, rum-free version on request
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 240ml orange juice
  • 80ml lime juice (about 3 limes)
  • 60ml sugar syrup (from caster sugar and water)
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  1. Make the sugar syrup by dissolving equal parts caster sugar and hot water, then cool.
  2. Shake the rum, juices and syrup over ice, one drink at a time.
  3. Strain over crushed ice and top with mint. Make a rum-free version for anyone not drinking.
