Otis's fries with Ed's sauceVegetarian

Crisp fries with a tangy pink sauce.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; cook the fries to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut fries
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  1. Bake the fries on two trays at 220°C, turning once, until deep gold and crisp.
  2. Start the sauce with a squeeze of lemon juice and the tomato sauce, then whisk in the mayo, relish, mustard, paprika and garlic powder.
  3. Salt the hot fries and serve with a big bowl of sauce.

Something-on-it cheeseburger mini burgers

Mini beef patties with cheese and pickles, something on it, says Ed.

Prep 19 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to a day ahead and keep them chilled; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 8 mini burger buns
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 16 dill pickle slices
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls and season well.
  2. Smash each ball flat on a very hot griddle and cook 2 minutes until crusty, then flip and top with cheese.
  3. Toast the buns on the griddle.
  4. Build with sauce, mustard, onion, pickles and the patties and serve hot.

The Good Burger with Ed's sauce

Double beef patties, cheese and the pink sauce. It saves Good Burger.

Prep 22 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce and shape the patties up to a day ahead; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g beef mince, 20 per cent fat
  • 4 sesame burger buns
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 100g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  1. Mix a squeeze of lemon with the tomato sauce, mayo and relish to make the sauce.
  2. Shape the mince into 8 thin patties and season well.
  3. Cook on a very hot griddle 2 minutes a side, laying cheese on each after the flip.
  4. Toast the buns cut side down.
  5. Stack two patties per bun with lettuce, tomato, onion and lots of sauce.

Kurt's Mondo grinder

Meatballs in red sauce with melted provolone on a long roll.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs in sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; fill the rolls to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork and veal mince
  • 50g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700ml jar tomato passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long sub rolls
  • 8 slices provolone
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  1. Mix the mince, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, half the garlic and salt and roll into 16 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in a hot oiled pan, then add the passata, oregano and remaining garlic and simmer.
  3. Split the rolls, fill with meatballs and sauce and lay the provolone on top.
  4. Grill until the cheese melts and bubbles, then finish with basil.

Ice cream truck getaway choc topsVegetarian

Ed pelts a truck with ice cream. Choc-dipped vanilla cones.

Prep 0 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Dip the cones and keep them in the freezer up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 8 waffle cones
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 60ml coconut oil
  • 2 tbsp rainbow sprinkles
  1. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil gently and cool to room temperature.
  2. Scoop the ice cream firmly into the cones, pressing a ball on top, and freeze for 10 minutes.
  3. Dip each top in the chocolate, scatter with sprinkles and stand upright in a glass until set.

Grape Nose Boy grape soda spiderNo alcohol

Grape soda over vanilla ice cream.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the soda and glasses well; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1.25 L grape soda
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 cup red grapes, halved
  1. Drop two scoops of ice cream into each of 4 tall chilled glasses.
  2. Pour the soda slowly down the side so it foams up.
  3. Top with a few grape halves and serve with a long spoon and straw.
