Snacks

Baby's watermelon wedgesVegetarian

Cold watermelon wedges with mint, lime and flaky salt, carried in with both arms.

Prep 3 min · Make ahead: Chill up to 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg watermelon
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 1 lime
  • 1/4 tsp flaky sea salt
  1. Cut the watermelon into thick wedges and chill well.
  2. Top with torn mint, a squeeze of lime and flaky salt.
  3. Keep ice cold until serving.

Staff cabin onion dip and pretzels

Slow caramelised onion sour cream dip with pretzels and potato chips.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dip keeps 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 250g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 200g pretzels
  • 170g potato chips
  1. Cook the onions in butter over low heat, stirring often, until deep brown and jammy.
  2. Cool and chop finely.
  3. Beat the sour cream with the cream cheese, Worcestershire and salt, then fold in the onions and chives.
  4. Chill the dip.
  5. Serve with pretzels and potato chips.

Devilled eggs and salmon rye

Paprika eggs with mustard yolks, plus dark toasts with dill cream cheese and smoked fish.

How it is made
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 6 slices rye bread
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g smoked salmon
  1. Creamy devilled eggs dusted with paprika next to rye toasts with dill cream cheese and smoked salmon, served on a silver tray like 1963. Boil 6 eggs for 10 minutes, cool in iced water and halve. Mash the yolks with 3 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, salt and a dash of hot sauce, pipe back in and dust with paprika. Toast 6 slices rye bread and cut into quarters. Mix 125g cream cheese with chopped dill and lemon zest, spread on the toasts and top with 150g smoked salmon and a sprig of dill.

Sunday buffet brisket

Beef brisket braised with onions, tomato paste and paprika, sliced across the grain.

Prep 15 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently in its juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef brisket
  • 3 brown onions, sliced
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Season the brisket with the salt and brown it in the oil on all sides.
  2. Lay the onions in a roasting dish and sit the brisket on top.
  3. Mix the stock with tomato paste and paprika, pour over, cover tightly and braise at 150°C.
  4. Rest, slice across the grain and spoon over the juices.

Double cheeseburgers

Two thin griddled patties, American cheese, pickles and mustard.

How it is made
  • 750g frozen crinkle-cut chips
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Two thin beef patties smashed on a hot griddle with American cheese, onion, pickles and mustard in a toasted soft bun, with a pile of crinkle-cut chips. Bake 750g frozen crinkle-cut chips at 220°C for 25 minutes. Roll 600g beef mince into 8 balls. Heat a griddle or heavy pan until smoking, smash each ball thin with a spatula, season and cook 2 minutes, flip and lay a slice of American cheese on top for 1 minute. Toast 4 burger buns. Stack two patties in each bun with finely diced onion, pickles, mustard and tomato sauce.

Sweets

Watermelon sorbetVegetarian

Watermelon and lime sorbet scraped with a fork as it freezes.

Prep 20 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg watermelon flesh
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml hot water
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Blitz the watermelon with lime juice and the syrup, then strain.
  3. Pour into a tray and freeze for 4 hours, scraping with a fork every 30 minutes.
  4. Scoop into cups.

Drinks

Pineapple and ginger punchNo alcohol

Pineapple, orange, ginger and lime topped with ginger ale.

Prep 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Stir the ginger, lime and sugar, add the juices and chill up to 1 day ahead; add the ginger ale, ice and fruit just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 cm pieces of ginger, grated
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 L pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 1 L ginger ale, cold
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Stir the ginger, lime juice and sugar in a punch bowl until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add the pineapple and orange juices and chill.
  3. Just before serving add the ginger ale, ice, orange slices, pineapple and torn mint.
