Snacks

Naboo floating fruitVegetarian

Pear, rockmelon and honeydew cubes on toothpicks with honey and lime

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 pears
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 1/4 honeydew melon
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Cut the pears, rockmelon and honeydew melon into cubes and spear on toothpicks.
  2. Drizzle with honey and lime and arrange on a long platter so they look like they're hovering.

Geonosis arena wings

Baked wings tossed in chilli honey butter with sesame and spring onion

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Wings fresh, sauce can be made ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 3 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Bake the wings at 220C until crisp.
  2. Melt the chilli sauce, honey and butter and toss the wings through.
  3. Scatter with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Mains

Dex's Diner patty melts and fries

Beef patty melts with onions and Swiss on rye, crinkle-cut fries

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Onions up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 50g butter
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into crinkle chips
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 4 dill pickles
  1. Cook the onions slowly until golden.
  2. Shape the mince into 4 thin patties and sear. Layer on rye with the onions and Swiss.
  3. Butter the outside of the bread and grill until melted.
  4. Fry the potato chips in the vegetable oil on a low heat until soft, then again on a high heat until golden. Serve with pickles.

Clone trooper white chocolate mousseVegetarian

White chocolate mousse topped with cream and a dark chocolate square

Prep 25 min, cook 245 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream, warmed, to melt the chocolate
  • 250ml thickened cream, plus extra to top, whipped
  • 1 egg white
  • 30g dark chocolate, shaved into thin squares
  1. Melt the white chocolate into the warmed cream and cool.
  2. Whip the cream. Whisk the egg white and fold both through the chocolate mixture.
  3. Spoon into 4 glasses and set for 4 hours.
  4. Top with whipped cream and a thin square of dark chocolate.

Dex's diner coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong drip coffee in thick mugs, hot milk and raw sugar on the side

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g ground coffee
  • 1l water
  • 200ml milk, hot
  • 4 tsp raw sugar
  1. Brew the coffee with the water in a drip machine so it comes out strong.
  2. Pour into thick diner mugs and serve with hot milk and raw sugar on the side.
