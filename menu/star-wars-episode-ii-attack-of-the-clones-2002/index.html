Snacks

Naboo floating fruitVegetarian

Pear, melon and honeydew cubes on toothpicks with honey and lime.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 pears
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 1/4 honeydew melon
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Cut the pears, rockmelon and honeydew melon into cubes and spear on toothpicks.
  2. Drizzle with honey and lime and arrange on a long platter so they look like they're hovering.

Geonosis arena wings

Baked wings tossed in chilli honey butter with sesame and spring onion.

Prep 3 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Wings fresh, sauce can be made ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 3 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Bake the wings at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Melt the chilli sauce, honey and butter and toss the wings through, 5 min.
  3. Scatter with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Tipoca City salt and vinegar fish bites

Cod in sharp malt crumbs, fried golden, caper tartare and lemon, for a rain-lashed landing.

How it is made
  • 600g cod or flathead fillets
  • 1 bottle malt vinegar
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 2L vegetable oil
  1. Cod pieces in a crisp salt and vinegar crumb, fried golden, with a sharp dill pickle and caper tartare and lemon wedges. Cut 600g cod fillets into bite pieces and soak in 3 tbsp malt vinegar for 10 minutes, then pat dry. Dust in flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs and coat in 100g panko mixed with 1 tsp salt. Fry at 180°C for 2 minutes until golden. Mix 150g mayonnaise with chopped dill pickle, capers, dill and lemon juice. Sprinkle the fish with more salt and vinegar.

Mains

Dex's Diner patty melts and fries

Beef patty melts with onions and Swiss on rye, crinkle-cut fries.

Prep 0 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Onions up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices of rye bread
  • 4 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 50g butter
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into crinkle fries
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 4 dill pickles
  1. Cook the onions slowly until golden.
  2. Shape the mince into 4 thin patties and sear. Layer on rye with the onions and Swiss, 10 min.
  3. Butter the outside of the bread and grill until melted.
  4. Fry the potato chips in the vegetable oil on a low heat until soft, then again on a high heat until golden. Serve with pickles.

Naboo meadow lamb cutlet picnic

Grilled pink with rosemary and lemon, warm new potatoes, beans, salsa verde and no sand.

How it is made
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 tin anchovies
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 12 lamb cutlets
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  1. Lamb cutlets grilled pink with rosemary and lemon, with a warm new potato salad, green beans and a bright salsa verde to spoon over. Boil 800g new potatoes until tender and halve. Blanch 300g green beans. Blitz parsley, mint, a garlic clove, 1 tbsp capers, 2 anchovies, lemon juice and 100ml olive oil into a salsa verde. Rub 12 lamb cutlets with oil, rosemary, lemon zest and salt and grill over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes a side. Toss the potatoes and beans with half the salsa verde and serve with the cutlets.

Clone trooper white chocolate mousseVegetarian

White chocolate mousse topped with cream and a dark chocolate square, armour plated.

Prep 18 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream, warmed, to melt the chocolate
  • 250ml thickened cream, plus extra to top, whipped
  • 1 egg white
  • 30g dark chocolate, shaved into thin squares
  1. Melt the white chocolate into the warmed cream and cool, 10 min.
  2. Whip the cream. Whisk the egg white and fold both through the chocolate mixture.
  3. Spoon into 4 glasses and set for 4 hours.
  4. Top with whipped cream and a thin square of dark chocolate.
