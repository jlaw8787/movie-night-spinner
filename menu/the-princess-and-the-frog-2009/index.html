Tiana's beignetsVegetarian

Fried yeast dough squares, soft and puffy.

Prep 28 min, cook 15 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the dough up to 1 day ahead and prove it slowly in the fridge overnight instead of the 2 hour prove; cut, fry and dust with icing sugar just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 250ml evaporated milk
  • 125ml warm water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g icing sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, eggs, butter and evaporated milk with enough warm water to a soft dough, 10 min.
  2. Knead briefly until smooth.
  3. Cover and prove until doubled, 2 hr.
  4. Roll out and cut into squares.
  5. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until puffed and golden, 15 min.
  6. Bury under icing sugar and serve hot.

Muffuletta round loaf sandwich

Round loaf layered with olive salad, salami, ham and provolone.

Prep 30 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the day before, press overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 150g salami
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g provolone
  • 200g green olives
  • 100g giardiniera
  1. Chop the olives and giardiniera together for an olive salad.
  2. Hollow the loaf slightly.
  3. Layer with olive salad, salami, ham and provolone, then more olive salad.
  4. Wrap tightly and press under a heavy weight overnight in the fridge.
  5. Cut into small wedges.

Bayou crab cakes with Creole remoulade

Crab bound with mayo and scallion, fried crisp, punchy pickle and hot sauce mayo.

How it is made
  • 400g crab meat
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 egg
  • 60g panko crumbs
  • 2 tsp Cajun spice
  • 20g butter
  • vegetable oil
  • dill pickles
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • paprika
  • 1 lemon
  1. Crab cakes bound with mayo, mustard and spring onion, fried crisp, with a punchy Creole remoulade of pickles, capers and hot sauce. Mix 400g drained crab meat with 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 2 sliced spring onions, 1 egg, 60g panko and 1/2 tsp Cajun spice. Shape into 8 cakes and chill 30 minutes. Fry in oil and butter for 3 minutes a side until golden. For the remoulade, mix 120g mayo with 1 tbsp chopped pickles, 1 tbsp capers, 1 tsp mustard, 1 tsp hot sauce and paprika. Serve with lemon.

Mains

Palace shrimp and grits

Garlic bacon shrimp over cheddar polenta.

Prep 16 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and slice the spring onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the polenta and finish the dish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g polenta
  • 1 L water
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 500g green prawns, peeled
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  1. Peel the prawns and prepare the spring onions.
  2. Simmer the polenta in the water, stirring, until thick, 20 min.
  3. Beat the cheddar into the polenta.
  4. Fry the bacon until crisp, 8 min.
  5. Add the garlic, prawns and Cajun seasoning and cook until the prawns are pink, 3 min.
  6. Spoon over the polenta and scatter with spring onion.

Daddy James's chicken and sausage gumbo

Sausage and chicken in a dark nutty roux, okra and bell pepper, over rice, shared with the street.

How it is made
  • 80g plain flour
  • vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 1 bunch celery
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 300g smoked sausage
  • 2 tsp Cajun spice
  • 1.2L chicken stock
  • 200g okra
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • hot sauce
  1. Chicken and smoky sausage in a dark, nutty roux with okra, capsicum, celery and onion, ladled over rice with spring onion and hot sauce. Stir 80ml oil and 80g plain flour in a heavy pot over medium heat for 20 minutes until dark brown. Add 1 diced onion, 1 green capsicum and 2 celery sticks and cook 5 minutes. Add 600g diced chicken thighs, 300g sliced smoked sausage, 2 tsp Cajun spice and 1.2L chicken stock. Simmer for 1 hour, adding 200g sliced okra for the last 15 minutes. Serve over 300g cooked rice with spring onion and hot sauce.

Caramel bananasVegetarian

Bananas cooked in butter, brown sugar and cinnamon.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Toast the pecans up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; the banana sauce is best cooked just before serving over the ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 50g pecans, chopped
  • 60g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 bananas
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Toast the pecans in a dry pan, 4 min.
  2. Melt the butter in a frying pan with the brown sugar and cinnamon and stir until bubbling, 4 min.
  3. Add the bananas, halved lengthways and across, and cook until caramelised on each side, 4 min.
  4. Pour in the orange juice and vanilla and let it bubble into a glossy sauce, 3 min.
  5. Spoon over scoops of ice cream and scatter with the pecans.

Iced chicory coffee with milkNo alcohol

Strong chicory coffee over ice with vanilla syrup and milk.

Prep 4 min, cook 18 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Coffee and syrup up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp ground coffee
  • 1 tbsp roasted chicory
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 600ml milk
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Brew the coffee with the chicory in the boiling water.
  2. Cool the coffee, 30 min.
  3. Simmer the sugar, water and vanilla to a syrup, 8 min.
  4. Fill glasses with ice, add syrup and cold milk, then pour over the coffee. For the kids, use decaf or just milk and syrup.
