Snacks

E.B.'s I Want Candy rocky road

Milk chocolate with mini marshmallows and jellybeans

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min, then 115 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g milk chocolate
  • 20g butter
  • 100g mini marshmallows
  • 150g jellybeans
  • 60g pretzels, broken
  • 30g shredded coconut
  1. Line a 20cm square tin with baking paper.
  2. Melt the chocolate with the butter in a bowl over simmering water, then cool briefly.
  3. Stir in the marshmallows, most of the jellybeans, pretzels and coconut.
  4. Spread into the tin and press more jellybeans on top.
  5. Chill until set, then cut into squares with a hot knife.

Rainbow carrots with green goddess dipVegetarian

Rainbow baby carrots and snow peas with avocado herb dip

Prep 17 min · Make ahead: Trim the carrots up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; blitz the dip on the day so the avocado stays green
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches rainbow baby carrots
  • 150g snow peas
  • 1 avocado
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp sunflower seeds, toasted
  1. Trim the carrots, leaving a little green top, and halve any big ones lengthways.
  2. Blanch the snow peas briefly and cool in iced water.
  3. Blitz the avocado, yoghurt, basil, parsley, chives, lemon juice and salt until smooth.
  4. Spoon into a bowl, scatter with sunflower seeds and serve with the vegetables.

Fred O'Hare's Hollywood loaded hot dogs

Hot dogs with cheddar sauce, corn relish and crispy onions

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese sauce and corn relish up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat the sauce, grill the frankfurts and fill the buns on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 200g tin corn kernels, drained
  • 2 tbsp tomato relish
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 4 tbsp crispy fried onions
  1. Simmer the frankfurts, then grill until marked.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and simmer until thick, then stir in the cheddar.
  3. Mix the corn with the relish.
  4. Toast the buns and fill with frankfurts, cheese sauce, corn relish and tomato sauce.
  5. Top with crispy fried onions.

O'Hare family Easter roast lamb

Garlic and rosemary roast lamb with mint sauce. Fred's parents want him out and into a job.

Prep 5 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Make the mint sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg, bone in
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg roasting potatoes
  • 2 bunches baby carrots
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut small slits in the lamb and push in garlic slivers and rosemary. Rub with 1 tbsp of the oil and salt.
  2. Roast the lamb for 1 and a half hours, with the parboiled potatoes in 1 tbsp of the oil on another tray.
  3. Toss the carrots with the rest of the oil and the honey and roast for the last 30 minutes.
  4. Chop the mint and mix with the vinegar, sugar and 2 tbsp boiling water.
  5. Rest the lamb for 15 minutes, carve and serve with the vegetables and mint sauce.

Easter Island chocolate crackle nestsVegetarian

Milk chocolate and cornflake nests baked in muffin cases

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the nests until set up to 3 days ahead, airtight in the fridge; fill with the mini chocolate eggs on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g milk chocolate
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup
  • 80g cornflakes
  • 150g mini chocolate eggs
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter and golden syrup.
  2. Stir in the cornflakes until coated.
  3. Spoon into paper cases in a muffin tin, pressing a dip in the centre of each with the back of a teaspoon.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Fill each nest with mini chocolate eggs.

Drinks

Carrot, orange and ginger sunriseVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh carrot, orange and ginger juice with raspberry cordial

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Juice and chill the carrot, orange and ginger juice up to 1 day ahead; pour over ice and add the raspberry cordial to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 carrots
  • 5 oranges
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 4 tbsp raspberry cordial
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the carrots, four oranges and the ginger, or blend with a little water and strain through a sieve.
  2. Chill the juice.
  3. Fill glasses with ice and pour in the juice.
  4. Slowly pour a spoon of raspberry cordial down the inside of each glass so it sinks. Garnish with an orange wheel.

When to serve

Serve the sweets when the Easter Island candy factory comes into view.
