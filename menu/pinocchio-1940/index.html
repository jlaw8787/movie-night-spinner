Snacks

Salami and provolone crostini

Toasted baguette slices topped with salami and provolone

Prep 13 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Toast the baguette up to 2 days ahead, airtight; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g salami, sliced
  • 150g provolone
  • 4 tbsp pickled capsicum strips
  1. Slice the baguette thinly and brush with olive oil.
  2. Toast at 200C until crisp.
  3. Top each with salami, a small slice of provolone and a strip of pickled capsicum, in rows on a board.

Cleo's fish-shaped crackersVegetarian

Crisp cheddar and smoked paprika crackers

Prep 21 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g cold butter, cubed
  • 150g plain flour
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then mix in the cheddar and paprika.
  2. Add cold water to bring it together.
  3. Chill the dough.
  4. Roll thin and cut into fish shapes.
  5. Bake at 180C until crisp.

Mains

Geppetto's spaghetti and meatballs

Beef and parmesan meatballs in tomato sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Meatballs and sauce up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 50g parmesan, grated, plus extra to serve
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/4 cup basil leaves, torn
  • 500g spaghetti
  1. Mix the mince with parmesan, parsley, salt and egg and roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs well.
  3. Add the tomatoes, garlic and basil and simmer.
  4. Cook the spaghetti.
  5. Serve with extra parmesan on top.

Sweets

Blue Fairy blueberry tartVegetarian

Sweet pastry case with vanilla custard and fresh blueberries

Prep 68 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 500ml thick vanilla custard
  • 250g blueberries
  • 2 tbsp apricot jam
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Blind bake a pastry case until golden and cool completely.
  2. Fill with custard and pile the blueberries on top.
  3. Warm the jam with water, brush over the berries and dust with icing sugar.
  4. Chill until set.

Drinks

Jiminy's sparkling mint lime crushVegetarianNo alcohol

Muddled mint, lime and honey with apple juice and bubbles

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Muddle the mint with the honey and lime juice and stir in the apple juice up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; add ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cup mint leaves
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 5 limes
  • 250ml cold apple juice
  • 1l sparkling water
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Muddle the mint with the honey and lime juice in a jug until bruised and fragrant.
  2. Add the apple juice and stir.
  3. Fill small glasses with crushed ice, pour in the mix to halfway and top with sparkling water.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel and a mint sprig.
