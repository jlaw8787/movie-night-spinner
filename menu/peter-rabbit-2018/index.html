Snacks

McGregor's radishes with whipped butterVegetarian

Crisp radishes with whipped salted butter and sea salt.

Prep 27 min · Make ahead: Whip the butter up to 2 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge, then bring to room temperature; crisp the radishes in iced water on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches of radishes
  • 120g salted butter, soft
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  • 1 baguette, warmed
  1. Wash and trim the radishes, leaving a little stem on, and halve the bigger ones.
  2. Keep in iced water so they crunch.
  3. Whip the butter with an electric beater until pale and fluffy.
  4. Spread the butter in a dish and sprinkle with sea salt and chives. Serve with the drained radishes and warm baguette, 4 min.

Cottontail's garden crudités with herb dipVegetarian

Baby carrots, mangetout, cucumber and cos with herb dip.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; trim the vegetables up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in damp paper towel
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch baby carrots
  • 150g snow peas
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into batons
  • 2 baby cos lettuces
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp chopped mint
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Trim the carrots and snow peas. Cut the cucumber into batons and separate the lettuces into small leaves.
  2. Mix the yoghurt with the garlic, dill, mint, lemon zest and a pinch of salt.
  3. Arrange the vegetables around the bowl of dip on a big board.

Mains

Lake District garden vegetable pieVegetarian

Potato, carrot and leek pie in creamy sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 1 day ahead, cool and keep chilled; cover with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 120g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Boil the potato and carrot briefly and drain, 8 min.
  2. Fry the leeks in the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and simmer until thick, 12 min.
  3. Stir in the cheddar, mustard, vegetables and peas.
  4. Tip into a pie dish, cover with the pastry and brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden, 25 min.

Sweets

Blackberry ambush crumbleVegetarian

Blackberries and apple under a buttery crumble.

Prep 7 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Assemble the crumble up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g blackberries (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 apples, peeled and diced
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 100g cold butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 600ml vanilla custard
  1. Toss the blackberries and apples with the sugar and cornflour and tip into a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the oats and brown sugar. Scatter over the fruit, 5 min.
  3. Bake at 180°C until golden and bubbling, 38 min.
  4. Serve warm with custard.

Drinks

Mrs Rabbit's chamomile apple iced teaNo alcohol

Chamomile tea with honey, apple juice and lemon over ice.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chamomile tea bags
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 400ml cold apple juice
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 apple, thinly sliced
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags with the honey in boiling water, 5 min.
  2. Remove the bags, cool, then chill, 1 hr.
  3. Stir in the apple juice and lemon juice.
  4. Fill glasses with ice and apple slices, pour over the tea and add a mint sprig.
