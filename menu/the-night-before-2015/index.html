Snacks

New York bodega bagel bites

Mini bagels with chive cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and dill.

Prep 7 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Beat the cream cheese with the chives and lemon up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; toast the bagels and build just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 12 mini bagels
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 red onion, in slivers
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Split the bagels and toast until golden.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the chives and a squeeze of lemon. Spread thickly on each bagel half.
  3. Top with smoked salmon, capers, red onion and dill.
  4. Finish with cracked pepper and serve on a board with lemon wedges.

Nutcracka hunt hot honey buffalo wings

Crisp baked wings tossed in hot sauce, butter and honey.

Prep 5 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings with the baking powder and salt up to 1 day ahead, uncovered in the fridge, and make the ranch up to 3 days ahead; bake and sauce the wings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 100g sour cream
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp buttermilk
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  • 1 small bunch dill, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 carrots, cut into sticks
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and honey and toss the wings through.
  4. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, buttermilk, chopped chives, chopped dill and garlic powder for the ranch.
  5. Serve with carrot and celery sticks.

Mains

Karaoke bar sliders and mac and cheese

Cheeseburger sliders with pickles and baked mac and cheese cups.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake the mac and cheese cups up to 1 day ahead and reheat at 180°C; shape and cook the patties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 6 slices cheddar, halved
  • 12 slider buns
  • 12 dill pickle slices
  • 250g macaroni
  • 200g tasty cheese, grated
  • 400ml full cream milk
  1. Cook the macaroni until just tender and drain.
  2. Mix the macaroni with the cheese and milk and spoon into greased muffin tins.
  3. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  4. Shape the mince into small patties and cook on both sides, topping with the cheddar slices to melt.
  5. Serve in slider buns with pickles alongside the mac and cheese cups.

Isaac's Christmas Eve beef and broccoli

Beef and broccoli stir-fried in oyster sauce over rice.

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Slice and marinate the beef up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rump steak, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 head broccoli, in small florets
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 15g ginger, cut in matchsticks
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Toss the beef with half the soy and the cornflour. Mix the oyster sauce, the rest of the soy, sesame oil, sugar and 4 tbsp water.
  2. Rinse the rice and simmer it, covered, until the water is gone. Rest it with the lid on.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in a very hot wok with half the oil in two batches. Lift out.
  4. Stir-fry the broccoli, garlic and ginger in the rest of the oil with a splash of water until bright, return the beef, add the sauce and toss until glossy.

Sweets

Midnight Mass gingerbread cakeVegetarian

Sticky ginger and golden syrup cake.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and wrap airtight once cool; ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g golden syrup
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 egg
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Melt the butter with the golden syrup and brown sugar.
  2. Mix in the flour, ginger, egg and milk.
  3. Pour into a lined tin and bake at 170°C.
  4. Cool the cake.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with lemon juice and spread over.

Drinks

Christmas Eve mulled wineVegetarian

Red wine simmered with orange, honey, cinnamon, star anise and clove.

Prep 6 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Infuse the wine with the spices up to 1 day ahead, cold in the fridge; warm gently on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 2 star anise
  • 6 cloves
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 750ml sparkling apple juice (for anyone not drinking)
  1. Pour the wine into a saucepan with the orange juice, honey, cinnamon sticks, star anise, cloves and orange slices.
  2. Heat gently, keeping it below a simmer so the alcohol stays.
  3. Ladle into mugs with an orange slice each. For anyone not drinking, warm sparkling apple juice with the same spices.
