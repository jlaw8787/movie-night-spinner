Snacks

Pizza Poppa's pizza balls

Mozzarella and pepperoni wrapped in soft dough, baked golden, tomato dip

Prep 20 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 150g pepperoni
  • 60g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar pizza sauce
  1. Cube the mozzarella and heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Cut the dough into small pieces and wrap each around a cube of cheese and a slice of pepperoni.
  3. Roll into balls and pack into a greased tin.
  4. Bake until golden.
  5. Melt the butter with crushed garlic and brush over the balls. Warm the sauce for dipping.

Chicken momo dumplings

Steamed chicken and ginger dumplings with a charred tomato chilli sauce

Prep 23 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat the momos up to 1 day ahead on a floured tray, covered in the fridge; make the achar up to 3 days ahead and steam to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken mince
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 packet dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the mince with sliced spring onion and ginger.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam the momos.
  4. Char the tomatoes in a dry pan, then blitz with chilli flakes, salt and garlic for the achar.

Christine's wedding salmon and fig canapésOn the printed menu

Salmon blinis and goat's cheese crostini. Christine's wedding, Stephen's a guest

How it is made
  • 16 cocktail blinis
  • 120g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 figs
  • 16 crostini
  • 120g goat's cheese
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Smoked salmon on blinis with crème fraîche and dill, and fig and goat's cheese bites. Warm 16 cocktail blinis at 180°C for 4 minutes. Mix 120g crème fraîche with lemon zest and top each blini with a spoon of it, a fold of smoked salmon and a dill sprig. Quarter 4 fresh figs. Sit each quarter on a small crostini with a little soft goat's cheese from 120g, drizzle with honey and add cracked pepper. Arrange both on a platter.

Mains

Multiverse noodle bowls

Braised pork belly, miso broth, ramen and soft eggs. One bowl per universe

Prep 5 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Braise the pork and soft-boil the eggs up to 2 days ahead, chilled; heat the stock and cook the noodles on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • 4 portions ramen noodles
  • 3 tbsp white miso
  • 1l chicken stock
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 can corn kernels
  • 4 sheets nori
  1. Braise the pork belly in soy and water until tender, then slice.
  2. Simmer the pork.
  3. Soft-boil the eggs, cool and halve.
  4. Heat the stock and whisk in the miso.
  5. Cook the noodles and build each bowl differently with pork, eggs, corn and nori.

Westview spaghetti and meatballsOn the printed menu

Beef and parmesan meatballs over pasta, garlic bread. Wanda wants Billy and Tommy back

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 60g parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 1 garlic bread
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Beef meatballs browned and simmered 40 minutes in tomato sauce, served over spaghetti with garlic bread and parmesan, family style. Mix 600g beef mince with 1 egg, 40g breadcrumbs, 30g grated parmesan, 2 crushed garlic cloves, parsley and salt and roll into 20 meatballs. Brown in olive oil and set aside. Soften 1 diced onion in the same pan, add 2 tins crushed tomatoes, a pinch of sugar and basil and simmer 15 minutes. Add the meatballs and simmer 25 minutes. Toss through 400g cooked spaghetti and serve with parmesan and garlic bread.

Sweets

Kamar-Taj sel rotiVegetarian

Rice flour and cardamom, crisp outside, soft inside. Round as a sling ring portal

Prep 8 min, cook 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 1 day ahead and rest it covered in the fridge; fry on the day, best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 300g rice flour
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the rice flour, sugar, butter, cardamom and milk into a thick batter.
  2. Rest the batter.
  3. Pour rings of batter into oil at 175°C and fry until golden on both sides.

Drinks

Kamar-Taj masala chaiVegetarianNo alcohol

Black tea simmered with milk, cardamom, cinnamon, ginger and cloves

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh and served hot; crush the cardamom and slice the ginger up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 slices fresh ginger
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Crush the cardamom pods.
  2. Simmer the water with the cardamom, cinnamon stick, cloves and ginger.
  3. Add the tea bags and milk, bring just to the boil and sweeten with the caster sugar.
  4. Strain into cups.
