Snacks

Cairo market hummus and baba ganoushVegetarian

Chickpea hummus and smoky eggplant dip, flatbread, dukkah, olives, cucumber

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Dips up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin chickpeas
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp iced water
  • 1 eggplant
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 2 tbsp dukkah
  • 100g olives
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Char the eggplant over a flame until collapsed and blackened inside.
  2. Blitz the drained chickpeas with the tahini, lemon juice, garlic and iced water until silky.
  3. Scoop out the eggplant flesh and mash with a little more tahini and lemon.
  4. Warm the flatbreads and serve everything on a platter with dukkah, olives, sliced cucumber, paprika and olive oil.

Mains

Uncle Jack's lamb roast

Garlic and rosemary roast lamb leg, roast potatoes and pumpkin, peas, gravy

Prep 20 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Stud the lamb with garlic and rosemary up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 800g potatoes
  • 600g pumpkin
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 300ml beef stock
  1. Stud the lamb with sliced garlic and rosemary. Peel and cut the potatoes and pumpkin.
  2. Roast the lamb at 180C until 58C inside, adding the potatoes and pumpkin for the last hour.
  3. Rest the lamb under foil.
  4. Cook the peas, and make gravy by simmering the pan juices with the stock.

Sweets

Anzac biscuitsVegetarian

Crisp oat and coconut cookies sweetened with golden syrup

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 90g rolled oats
  • 150g plain flour
  • 85g desiccated coconut
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 2 tbsp boiling water
  1. Mix the oats, flour, coconut and sugar in a bowl.
  2. Melt the butter with the golden syrup, then stir in the bicarb dissolved in the boiling water.
  3. Combine, roll into balls and flatten on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 160C until golden.

Golden syrup dumplingsVegetarian

Soft dumplings simmered in golden syrup sauce, served with cream

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; rub in the butter and make the dough up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 375ml water
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Rub a portion of the butter into the flour and mix in the milk to a soft dough.
  2. Bring the water, brown sugar, golden syrup and remaining butter to a simmer.
  3. Drop in spoonfuls of dough, cover and cook gently.
  4. Serve with the sauce and cream.

Drinks

Archy and Frank's billy teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong black tea boiled in a pot, tin mugs, milk and plenty of sugar

Prep 2 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best brewed fresh; nothing to make ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l water
  • 3 tbsp loose leaf tea
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • sugar, to taste
  1. Boil the water in a billy or pot.
  2. Add the tea, boil for a minute and swing to settle.
  3. Pour into tin mugs with milk and plenty of sugar.

When to serve

Serve the biscuits when Archy and Frank meet at the athletics carnival.
