Cairo market hummus and baba ganoushVegetarian

Chickpea hummus and smoky eggplant dip, flatbread, dukkah, olives, cucumber.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Dips up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin chickpeas
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp iced water
  • 1 eggplant
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 2 tbsp dukkah
  • 100g olives
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Char the eggplant over a flame until collapsed and blackened inside.
  2. Blitz the drained chickpeas with the tahini, lemon juice, garlic and iced water until silky.
  3. Scoop out the eggplant flesh and mash with a little more tahini and lemon.
  4. Warm the flatbreads and serve everything on a platter with dukkah, olives, sliced cucumber, paprika and olive oil.

Bully beef and onion fritters

Corned beef and onion fritters with tomato relish. Bully beef, the Anzac ration.

How it is made
  • 340g tin corned beef
  • 1 onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • vegetable oil
  • 1 jar tomato relish
  1. Mash 340g tinned corned beef with 1 grated onion, 2 eggs, 100g self-raising flour, 125ml milk and pepper into a thick batter. Fry spoonfuls in 3 tbsp oil for 3 minutes a side until crisp and brown. Serve hot with tomato relish.

Mena Camp mutton and barley stew

Lamb shoulder, pearl barley and carrots from the dixie.

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead and chill; it is better reheated
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg diced lamb shoulder
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150g pearl barley
  • 1.25 L beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Brown the lamb in the oil in batches.
  2. Soften the onion, carrot and garlic in the same pot.
  3. Add the barley, stock, bay leaves and thyme, cover and simmer until the lamb is tender and the barley soft.
  4. Season well and serve with crusty bread.

Frank and Archy's Cairo kofta wraps

Spiced lamb in flatbread, garlic yoghurt and sumac onions, on a night's leave before shipping out.

How it is made
  • 700g lamb mince
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar ground coriander
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 jar sumac
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 jar pickled chillies
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Spiced lamb kofta grilled over high heat, wrapped in warm flatbread with garlic yoghurt, tomato and cucumber salad, pickled chilli and sumac onions. Mix 700g lamb mince with 1 grated onion, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp coriander, 1 tsp paprika, chopped parsley and salt. Shape onto 8 skewers and chill 30 minutes. Slice 1 red onion and toss with 1 tsp sumac. Mix 200g Greek yoghurt with 1 crushed garlic clove and lemon. Dice 2 tomatoes and 1 cucumber. Grill the kofta 8 to 10 minutes, turning. Wrap in 4 warm flatbreads with everything else.

Oat and golden syrup biscuitsVegetarian

Crisp oat and coconut biscuits sweetened with golden syrup.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 90g rolled oats
  • 150g plain flour
  • 85g desiccated coconut
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 2 tbsp boiling water
  1. Mix the oats, flour, coconut and sugar in a bowl.
  2. Melt the butter with the golden syrup, then stir in the bicarb dissolved in the boiling water.
  3. Combine, roll into balls and flatten on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 160°C until golden.

Plum and apple jam tartsVegetarian

Little shortcrust tarts filled with plum and apple jam, the tin every digger got sick of.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 250g plum and apple jam
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Cut the pastry into 12 rounds and press into a patty tin.
  2. Fill each with a heaped teaspoon of jam.
  3. Bake until the pastry is golden.
  4. Cool in the tin and dust with icing sugar.

Sweet mint teaNo alcohol

Hot black tea with fresh mint and sugar, poured high the hawkers' way.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  1. Boil a litre of water.
  2. Steep the tea bags and most of the mint for 4 minutes, then take out the tea bags.
  3. Stir in the sugar and pour into glasses with a mint sprig in each.
