Snacks

Blue cheese, pear and walnut crostiniVegetarian

The pen is blue, says Fletcher

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Toast the crostini and candy the walnuts up to 2 days ahead; top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette, sliced thinly on an angle
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g creamy blue cheese
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 2 ripe pears, thinly sliced
  • 60g walnuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 thyme sprigs
  1. Brush the bread with oil and bake at 190C until crisp.
  2. Toss the walnuts with half the honey in a hot pan until sticky, then cool.
  3. Beat the blue cheese with the cream cheese until spreadable.
  4. Spread on the toasts, top with pear and walnuts, drizzle with the remaining honey and scatter with thyme.

Max's birthday party pigs in blankets

Max turns five. Dad never shows

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Wrap the franks up to a day ahead and chill on the tray; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cocktail frankfurts
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tsp sesame seeds
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Cut each pastry sheet into 12 strips.
  2. Roll a strip around each frankfurt, seam side down on a lined tray.
  3. Brush with egg, scatter with sesame and bake at 200C until puffed and golden.

Mains

Mr. Allen's good roast lamb

Mr. Allen loves a good roast

Prep 20 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Stud and rub the lamb up to a day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg, bone in
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.2kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 1 cup parsley leaves
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon
  • 100ml extra virgin olive oil
  1. Cut small slits all over the lamb and push in garlic slices and rosemary sprigs, then rub with oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 180C for about 1 hour 20 minutes for pink, then rest under foil.
  3. Parboil the potatoes, rough them up and roast in oil around the lamb for the last hour.
  4. Chop the mint, parsley and capers and stir in the lemon juice and olive oil.
  5. Carve the lamb and serve with the potatoes and salsa verde.

Max's birthday wish chocolate cakeVegetarian

Max wishes Dad can't lie for a day

Prep 30 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake the cakes up to 2 days ahead and keep wrapped; ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 225g plain flour
  • 60g cocoa powder, plus 40g for the icing
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml hot coffee or water
  • 150g soft butter
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 5 birthday candles
  1. Whisk the flour, 60g cocoa, sugar and baking powder, then beat in the eggs, milk and oil, and finally the hot coffee.
  2. Divide between two lined 20cm tins and bake at 180C until a skewer comes out clean.
  3. Beat the butter, icing sugar and 40g cocoa with a splash of milk until fluffy.
  4. Sandwich and cover the cooled cakes with the icing.
  5. Push in five candles, light them and make a wish.

Fletcher's truth serumVegetarian

24 hours of nothing but the truth

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to a week ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 4 tbsp warm water
  • 600ml soda water
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Stir the honey into the warm water to make a syrup.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and syrup with ice and strain into 4 tall glasses of ice.
  3. Top with soda and a lemon slice.

When to serve

Cut the cake when Max blows out his candles and makes his wish.
