Razzles sour candy popcorn balls

Caramel popcorn balls rolled in crushed sour candy. Matty's Razzles move, in popcorn form.

Prep 30 min, cook 8 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 50g butter, plus extra for your hands
  • 250g marshmallows
  • 100g sour straps, chopped
  • 80g jelly beans
  • 12 cellophane bags
  1. Pop the kernels and tip into a big bowl, discarding any unpopped kernels.
  2. Melt the butter over low heat, add the marshmallows and stir until smooth.
  3. Pour over the popcorn with the sour straps and jelly beans and fold quickly.
  4. With buttered hands, press into 12 tennis ball sized rounds.
  5. Set on baking paper until firm, which is the resting time.
  6. Wrap each in cellophane.

Poise party salmon blinis and melon

Dill crème fraîche, smoked fish and roe on warm pancakes, prosciutto cantaloupe, Thriller playing.

How it is made
  • 16 blinis
  • 150ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 rockmelon
  • 6 slices prosciutto
  1. Warm blinis with dill crème fraîche, smoked salmon and salmon roe, beside rockmelon wedges wrapped in prosciutto and cracked pepper, served on silver trays. Warm 16 blinis at 180°C for 4 minutes. Mix 150ml crème fraîche with 1 tbsp chopped dill and a squeeze of lemon. Top each blini with a spoon of the cream, a fold of smoked salmon (200g total) and a few beads of salmon roe. Cut half a rockmelon into 12 thin wedges, peel and wrap each in half a slice of prosciutto (6 slices). Grind over pepper and arrange both on trays.

Poise magazine desk noodles

Stir fried chicken, broccoli, red bell pepper and egg noodles in soy sauce.

Prep 17 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, or reheat the noodles in a pan
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 head broccoli, cut into florets
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 400g egg noodles, cooked weight
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the chicken, broccoli and capsicum.
  2. Stir fry the chicken in the oil until browned.
  3. Add the broccoli and capsicum and cook until bright.
  4. Toss through the noodles with soy sauce and honey until sticky.
  5. Pack into takeaway boxes.

Wishing dust sugar cookiesVegetarian

Butter sugar cookie rounds iced pale pink.

Prep 24 min, cook 40 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 300g plain flour
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • pink food colouring, a drop
  • 1 tbsp edible glitter
  1. Beat the butter and sugar with the egg, then mix in the flour to a dough.
  2. Chill the dough until firm, which is the resting time.
  3. Roll out and cut into rounds.
  4. Bake at 180°C until set and pale golden.
  5. Cool the cookies on a rack, 20 min.
  6. Mix the icing sugar with water and a drop of colouring, ice the cookies pale pink and dust with edible glitter.

Pink sprinkle thirteenth birthday cakeVegetarian

Vanilla sponge, pink buttercream, thirteen candles.

Prep 35 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake the layers up to 1 day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk, for the cake
  • 250g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 450g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk, for the icing
  • pink food colouring, a drop
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 100g sprinkles
  • 13 candles
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line two 20cm tins, 5 min.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time with the vanilla, then fold in the flour and milk.
  3. Divide between the tins and bake, 30 min.
  4. Cool completely, 25 min.
  5. Beat the icing butter with the icing sugar, milk and a drop of pink colouring.
  6. Sandwich the layers with jam, ice all over and press sprinkles around the sides. Top with candles.

Piña colada, not virgin, for adults

Pineapple juice and coconut cream blended with ice, rum added for adults.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Blend fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 200ml coconut cream
  • 120ml white rum, for the grown-ups (leave out for kids)
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Blend the pineapple juice, coconut cream and a big handful of ice until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into glasses for the kids. Add rum to the glasses for the grown-ups.
