Snacks

Loaded potato skins

Baked potato skins filled with cheddar and bacon, sour cream on top.

Prep 13 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Bake the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; scoop, crisp and fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 8 small potatoes
  • 40g butter
  • 150g cheddar
  • 150g cooked bacon
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C, 45 min.
  2. Halve, scoop out and brush with melted butter.
  3. Crisp the skins at 220°C, 10 min.
  4. Fill with cheddar and bacon and bake again, 5 min.
  5. Top with sour cream and chives.

TPS report crackersVegetarian

Crisp lavash rectangles around a feta and yoghurt dip.

Prep 14 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Crackers up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 3 lavash breads
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  1. Cut the lavash into neat rectangles like sheets of paper.
  2. Brush with oil, salt and bake at 180°C until crisp, 12 min.
  3. Whip the feta with the yoghurt and stack the crackers around it. Did you get the memo?

Red stapler roast bell pepper bruschetta

Blistered bell pepper with garlic over ricotta on sourdough, for a case of the Mondays.

How it is made
  • 4 red capsicums
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 bottle sherry vinegar
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  1. Red capsicums blistered and peeled, dressed with garlic, olive oil, sherry vinegar and basil, piled onto toasted sourdough over whipped ricotta. Roast 4 red capsicums whole at 220°C for 30 minutes, turning once, until blackened. Cover in a bowl for 10 minutes, then peel, seed and cut into strips. Dress with 3 tbsp olive oil, a crushed garlic clove, 1 tbsp sherry vinegar, salt and torn basil. Beat 250g ricotta with lemon zest and salt. Toast 8 slices sourdough, spread with ricotta and pile on the capsicum.

Mains

Peter's fish

Crisp skinned snapper with lemon and capers, twice fried chips, green salad.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Do the first, low fry of the chips up to 2 hours ahead; finish the chips hot and cook the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 1/2 cup plain flour
  • 50g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 150g salad leaves
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips and season the flour.
  2. Fry the chips twice in the vegetable oil, once low and once hot, until crisp, 25 min.
  3. Dust the snapper fillets in the flour and fry skin down in the butter until crisp, then flip, 8 min.
  4. Add lemon juice, capers and parsley to the pan.
  5. Serve with a green salad.

Chotchkie's sticky barbecue ribs

Paprika and sugar rubbed pork, baked low and glazed sticky, coleslaw, served by Joanna.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork ribs
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 250ml barbecue sauce
  • 1 cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  1. Pork ribs rubbed with paprika, brown sugar and garlic, baked low and slow in foil, then glazed sticky under the grill, with creamy coleslaw. Mix 2 tbsp smoked paprika, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp garlic powder and 2 tsp salt and rub over 2kg pork ribs. Wrap tightly in foil and bake at 150°C for 2.5 hours. Unwrap, brush with 250ml barbecue sauce and grill on high for 5 minutes until sticky and charred. Toss half a shredded cabbage and 2 grated carrots with 125g mayo and 1 tbsp cider vinegar for coleslaw.

Office birthday cakeVegetarian

Vanilla slab cake with buttercream and sprinkles.

Prep 27 min, cook 30 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 350g icing sugar
  • 1/2 cup sprinkles
  1. Beat 200g of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a slab tin at 175°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool the cake, 40 min.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar, spread on and cover with sprinkles. Cut a square for Milton first.

Lumbergh's vanilla iced coffeeNo alcohol

Strong chilled coffee with homemade vanilla syrup over ice.

Prep 6 min, cook 8 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Brew and chill the coffee up to 2 days ahead; make the vanilla syrup up to 1 week ahead; add ice, milk and ice cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml strong coffee
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 4 small scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • cocoa powder, to dust
  • ice, to serve
  1. Brew the coffee and chill, 30 min.
  2. Simmer the sugar with the water and split vanilla bean, then cool, 8 min.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice and add vanilla syrup, coffee and cold milk.
  4. Top each with ice cream and dust with cocoa.

When to serve

Serve the cake when the office birthday cake goes round and skips Milton.
