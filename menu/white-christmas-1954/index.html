Snacks

Midnight liverwurst on rye and buttermilk

Emma's snack bar snack: liverwurst, mustard, pickles, red onion, with cold buttermilk

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, covered with a damp cloth in the fridge; pour the buttermilk to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 30g butter, soft
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 250g liverwurst or smooth pork pate
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 1 red onion, in rings
  • 400ml well-chilled buttermilk
  1. Spread the bread with butter and a thin layer of mustard.
  2. Slice the liverwurst and lay it over half the slices. Top with pickles and red onion, then the other slices.
  3. Press down, trim the crusts and cut into fingers.
  4. Pour the buttermilk into small glasses and serve together.

Columbia Inn cheese puffsVegetarian

Cheddar choux puffs, the club car snack while the sisters take the berth.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the choux paste up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; pipe and bake on the night, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 120g vintage cheddar, grated
  1. Bring water and the butter to the boil, beat in the flour until it forms a ball, then cool.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the cheddar.
  3. Pipe small balls onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden.

Mains

Vermont inn roast turkey

Roast turkey breast with stuffing, cranberry sauce and roast potatoes

Prep 5 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Mix the stuffing up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; rub the turkey with butter and herbs up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg turkey breast
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp mixed dried herbs
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 small bunch sage
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  1. Rub the turkey with some of the butter and the herbs.
  2. Roast at 180C.
  3. Mix the breadcrumbs with the onion, chopped sage and melted butter and bake the stuffing alongside the turkey.
  4. Toss the potatoes in oil and roast on another tray.
  5. Rest the turkey and serve with stuffing, cranberry sauce and potatoes.

General Waverly's maple glazed ham

Leg scored and glazed with syrup, mustard and cloves, baked sticky, carved for the 151st

Prep 15 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Make the glaze up to 3 days ahead; bake the ham up to 1 day ahead and serve warm or at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg half leg ham
  • 1 tbsp whole cloves
  • 125ml maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 orange
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Peel the rind off the ham, leaving the fat, score it in diamonds and stud with cloves.
  2. Simmer the maple syrup with the mustard, sugar and juice of the orange until syrupy.
  3. Bake the ham at 180C, brushing with glaze every 10 minutes, until sticky and dark.
  4. Rest, then carve thick.

Sweets

Snowy coconut cakeVegetarian

Coconut cake, the only snow at the inn until Christmas Eve.

Prep 22 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Bake and wrap the cakes up to 2 days ahead; ice and roll in coconut on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 160g desiccated coconut
  • 150ml coconut milk
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar until pale, then add the eggs, flour, some of the coconut and the coconut milk.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175C.
  3. Cool the cakes.
  4. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar, sandwich and cover the cakes.
  5. Roll the sides in the rest of the coconut.

Drinks

White Christmas eggnogVegetarian

Chilled egg custard drink with nutmeg, brandy on the side

Prep 6 min, cook 12 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs (yolks only)
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 120ml brandy
  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar.
  2. Heat the milk, cream and cinnamon stick until steaming, whisk into the yolks and return to the pan.
  3. Stir over low heat until it coats a spoon.
  4. Chill.
  5. Serve with grated nutmeg and brandy on the side.
