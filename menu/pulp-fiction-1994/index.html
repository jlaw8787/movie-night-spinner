Buddy Holly chicken

Your waiter brings it to the car.

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken tenderloins
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk and hot sauce for at least 30 minutes in the fridge.
  2. Mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt. Lift the chicken out, let it drip, then press it into the flour so it gets a shaggy coat.
  3. Fry in oil at 175°C in batches for 4 to 5 minutes until deep golden and cooked through. Drain on a rack and salt again.
  4. Stir the honey, mustard and mayonnaise together for dipping.
  5. Pile the chicken into paper-lined baskets with the dip and lemon wedges.

Mamie's devilled eggsVegetarian

Paprika, crisp shallots, chives.

Prep 11 min, cook 25 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 2 shallots
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water for 10 minutes, then straight into iced water.
  2. Slice the shallots thin and fry them slowly in the oil until golden and crisp. Drain on paper, 10 min.
  3. Peel and halve the eggs. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and hot sauce until smooth, and season.
  4. Pipe or spoon the yolk back into the whites.
  5. Dust with smoked paprika and top with crisp shallots and snipped chives, 3 min.

Slim's friesVegetarian

Mayonnaise on the side, as in Holland. Royale with cheese? That's Paris.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, then 75 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg russet potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for deep frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  • mayonnaise, to serve
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin chips.
  2. Soak in cold water for an hour, then dry well.
  3. Fry in oil at 140°C for 5 minutes in batches.
  4. Rest the chips on a rack until cool, 15 min.
  5. Fry again at 190°C until crisp, salt and serve in red-checked paper boats with tomato sauce and a pot of mayonnaise, the way they do it in Holland, 5 min.

Mains

Douglas Sirk steak

Vincent's order. Bloody as hell.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min, then 60 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • salt, to taste
  • 800g frozen skin-on chips
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp black peppercorns, crushed
  • 30ml brandy
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 120g blue cheese
  • 80g sour cream
  1. Season the sirloin steaks with salt an hour ahead and leave them out of the fridge.
  2. Bake the frozen skin-on chips at 220°C for 25 minutes until crisp.
  3. Heat the oil in a pan until smoking and sear the steaks for 2 minutes a side until 48°C inside, then rest them for 5 minutes.
  4. In the same pan cook the shallot and crushed peppercorns for 1 minute, add the brandy and cream and simmer for 2 minutes.
  5. Cut the iceberg lettuce into wedges and spoon over the blue cheese mashed with the sour cream. Serve with the steaks, chips and peppercorn sauce.

Big Kahuna burger

This is a tasty burger! Jules, at Brett's.

Prep 4 min, cook 36 min
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 60ml mirin
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 pineapple rings
  • 800g beef chuck mince
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  1. Simmer the soy, mirin and sugar until syrupy, 8 min.
  2. Grill the pineapple rings until charred, 6 min.
  3. Shape the mince into 4 thick patties.
  4. Grill the patties, brushing with the teriyaki, then melt the cheddar on top, 12 min.
  5. Toast the buns and build with lettuce, patty and pineapple, 10 min.

Coffee shop stack

Vincent's pancakes, then Jules and Ezekiel 25:17.

Prep 0 min, cook 30 min
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g butter
  • maple syrup, to serve
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, buttermilk and eggs until just combined.
  2. Rest the batter for 15 minutes.
  3. Cook ladlefuls in butter in a frying pan until bubbles set, flip and cook through, keeping the stack warm, 15 min.
  4. Grill the bacon until crisp. Stack the pancakes with butter and maple syrup, with bacon on the side, 10 min.

Martin & Lewis shakeNo alcohol

Vanilla. Mia's order.

Prep 12 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5 L vanilla bean ice cream
  • 480ml cold full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Blend the ice cream and milk in batches, until just thick enough for a straw to stand up, 8 min.

Vanilla CokeNo alcohol

Vincent's order. Cola, with vanilla.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bag ice
  • 60ml vanilla syrup
  • 1.3 L cola, chilled
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Add 15ml vanilla syrup to each glass, or half a teaspoon of vanilla extract with a teaspoon of sugar syrup.
  3. Top each with 300ml cold cola and stir once.
