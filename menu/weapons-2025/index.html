Snacks

Cafeteria pepperoni pizza squares

Sheet-pan pizza cut in squares. Seventeen empty desks in third grade.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the dough up to a day ahead and keep it in the fridge; top and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bought pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 250ml pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • 100g sliced pepperoni salami
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Oil a large baking tray and press the dough out to the edges, then rest it for 10 minutes.
  2. Spread with the sauce, scatter over the cheese, pepperoni and oregano.
  3. Bake at 230°C on the lowest shelf until the base is crisp and the cheese bubbles.
  4. Cut into neat squares and serve.

Wellness check potato gems with ranchVegetarian

Crisp potato gems and buttermilk ranch.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the ranch up to 3 days ahead; bake the gems to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen potato gems
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 lemon
  1. Whisk the buttermilk, mayonnaise, herbs, garlic and a squeeze of lemon into a ranch dressing.
  2. Spread the gems on lined trays and bake at 220°C, shaking once, until deep golden.
  3. Salt well and serve with the ranch.

Mains

Aunt Gladys's sloppy joes

Saucy beef mince on soft buns. Aunt Gladys drops in on the principal.

Prep 8 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and reheat; toast the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 250ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 6 soft burger buns
  • 6 slices American cheese
  1. Brown the mince in the oil, breaking it up.
  2. Add the onion, capsicum and garlic and cook until soft.
  3. Stir in the tomato sauce, sugar, mustard and Worcestershire and simmer until thick and glossy.
  4. Toast the buns, add a slice of cheese and pile on the filling.

Pennsylvania Dutch chicken pot pie

Chicken and vegetables under flaky pastry.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 40g butter
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 sheet frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in half the butter and lift it out.
  2. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the rest of the butter, stir in the flour, then the stock and cream.
  3. Return the chicken with the peas and thyme, simmer until thick, then tip into a pie dish.
  4. Lay over the pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C until puffed and deep golden.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes and serve.

Lights-out chocolate pudding cupsVegetarian

Dark chocolate custard and whipped cream.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the puddings up to 2 days ahead and chill; add the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 30g cornflour
  • 25g cocoa powder
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 chocolate wafer biscuits, crushed
  1. Whisk the sugar, cornflour, cocoa and yolks with a little of the milk until smooth.
  2. Heat the rest of the milk, whisk it in and stir over medium heat until thick and bubbling.
  3. Take off the heat, stir in the chocolate and vanilla and pour into 4 cups.
  4. Chill until set, then top with cream and crushed wafers.
