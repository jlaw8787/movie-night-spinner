Franco's housewarming bruschettaVegetarian

Charred sourdough with tomato, basil, buffalo mozzarella and balsamic.

Prep 11 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Toss the tomatoes with the oil, garlic and basil up to 2 hours ahead; grill the sourdough and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cherry tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • 2 buffalo mozzarella balls
  • 1 bottle balsamic glaze
  1. Quarter the tomatoes and toss with some oil, one crushed garlic clove, torn basil and salt.
  2. Leave the tomatoes to sit.
  3. Slice the sourdough, brush with oil and grill until charred.
  4. Rub each warm slice with a cut garlic clove.
  5. Top with the tomatoes and pieces of mozzarella, drizzle with balsamic glaze and add a basil leaf.

Rationed pretzels with beer cheese dipVegetarian

Pretzel bites with beer cheese dip, strictly rationed.

Prep 12 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cut the dough into pieces up to 2 hours ahead, covered with a tea towel; boil, bake and make the dip on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 L water
  • 60g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml lager
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp American mustard
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  1. Cut the dough into bite-size pieces.
  2. Simmer the water with the bicarbonate of soda and drop the pieces in briefly, in batches. Drain.
  3. Lay on a lined tray, brush with egg, sprinkle with sea salt and bake at 220°C.
  4. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the lager and milk.
  5. Add the cheddar, mustard and paprika and stir until smooth.

Mains

Danny's big breakfast fry-up

Eggs, bacon, sausages, hash browns, mushrooms and tomato with toast.

Prep 0 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms and halve the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook everything just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frozen hash browns
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 tomatoes, halved
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 60g butter
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 8 eggs
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Bake the hash browns at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Fry the sausages over medium heat.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp, keeping the fat in the pan.
  4. Fry the tomatoes cut-side down. Fry the mushrooms in some of the butter with the garlic.
  5. Fry the eggs in the bacon fat.
  6. Toast the bread and butter it well. Load up plates and serve with tomato sauce.

Franco's housewarming hot honey pizza

Pepperoni, mozzarella and hot honey, before the sinkhole opens.

Prep 5 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make the hot honey up to 1 week ahead; stretch and bake the pizzas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 balls pizza dough, about 250g each
  • 200g tomato passata
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 250g mozzarella, torn
  • 120g sliced pepperoni
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 small bunch basil
  1. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes and leave to infuse.
  2. Heat the oven as high as it goes with a tray or stone inside. Mix the passata with the garlic and salt.
  3. Stretch each dough thin on baking paper, spread with sauce and top with mozzarella and pepperoni.
  4. Slide onto the hot tray and bake until blistered.
  5. Drizzle with the hot honey and tear over basil.

Sweets

That's my Milky Way, homemade nougat bars

Nougat, caramel and milk chocolate. Don't touch it, Jonah.

Prep 0 min, cook 17 min, then 95 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g marshmallows
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g soft caramels
  • 2 tbsp thickened cream
  • 250g milk chocolate
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil
  1. Melt the marshmallows with the butter, stir in the malted milk powder and vanilla, and press into a lined 20cm square tin.
  2. Melt the caramels with the cream until smooth and spread over the nougat.
  3. Chill.
  4. Melt the chocolate with the oil and pour over the top.
  5. Chill until set, then cut into bars with a hot knife.

Rationed water watermelon agua frescaVegetarianNo alcohol

Watermelon, lime and mint, cold. Danny wasted the real water.

Prep 12 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Blend, strain and chill the agua fresca up to 1 day ahead in a jug in the fridge; pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg seedless watermelon, chunked
  • 4 limes
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml cold water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend the watermelon with the juice of three limes, mint and sugar until smooth.
  2. Strain through a sieve into a jug, pressing on the pulp. Stir in the cold water.
  3. Chill.
  4. Pour over ice and garnish with lime wheels and mint.

When to serve

Serve the sweets when James won't share his Milky Way.
