Snacks

Candlelit gruyere gougeresVegetarian

Gruyère cheese puffs, baked golden. Estelle shows the house by candlelight.

Prep 16 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Pipe the gougeres and freeze them on the tray up to a week ahead; bake from frozen, 5 minutes longer
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 125ml milk
  • 100g butter, diced
  • 140g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 120g gruyere, finely grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  1. Bring the water, milk, butter and a pinch of salt to the boil, tip in the flour and beat over the heat until it forms a smooth ball.
  2. Cool for 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy.
  3. Beat in 100g of the gruyere, the mustard, thyme and plenty of pepper.
  4. Pipe walnut-sized balls onto lined trays and top with the rest of the cheese.
  5. Bake at 200°C until puffed, deep golden and crisp, and serve warm.

Short-line farm vegetables with green goddessVegetarian

Crisp asparagus and snap peas with green goddess dip. Anna was on the short line once.

Prep 30 min · Make ahead: Make the dressing and whip the ricotta up to 2 days ahead; prep the vegetables the morning of
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch baby carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 150g sugar snap peas
  • 1 bunch asparagus
  • 1 baby cos lettuce
  • 250g fresh ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 cup mixed soft herbs (parsley, tarragon, chives)
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  1. Blanch the asparagus and snap peas for 1 minute, then plunge into iced water.
  2. Trim and halve the carrots and radishes, and split the cos into spears.
  3. Whip the ricotta with lemon zest, a pinch of salt and a splash of oil until smooth.
  4. Blitz the mayo, sour cream, herbs and vinegar until pale green.
  5. Spread the ricotta on a board, pile on the vegetables and serve the dressing alongside.

Mains

Flying herb-butter roast turkey

The turkey shoots out of the oven

Prep 17 min, cook 98 min · Make ahead: Make the stuffing up to a day ahead and chill; roast the turkey and make the gravy to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg turkey breast roast, skin on
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp sage and thyme, chopped
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 300g sourdough, torn and dried
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 60g dried cranberries
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  1. Mash half the butter with the herbs and lemon zest and push it under the turkey skin. Season well.
  2. Roast at 180°C, basting twice, until the thickest part reaches 70°C, then rest under foil.
  3. Meanwhile soften the onion and celery in the rest of the butter, toss with the bread, cranberries, egg and 200ml stock, and bake in a dish alongside the turkey for 40 minutes.
  4. Stir the flour into the pan juices over the heat, whisk in the rest of the stock and simmer to a gravy.
  5. Slice the turkey and serve with the stuffing and gravy.

Collapsing staircase chocolate cakeVegetarian

Rich chocolate cake. The staircase comes down under Walter.

Prep 16 min, cook 44 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep covered; it gets fudgier
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g butter
  • 5 eggs, separated
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 125g raspberries
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together and cool slightly.
  2. Beat the yolks with half the sugar until pale, then stir in the chocolate and the cocoa.
  3. Whisk the whites to soft peaks, whisk in the rest of the sugar, then fold into the chocolate.
  4. Bake in a lined 22cm springform at 170°C until risen and just set. It will crack and sink as it cools.
  5. Fill the crater with whipped cream, scatter raspberries and dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Bathtub gin fizzVegetarian

Gin, lemon and soda fizz. The bathtub goes through the floor.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 2 weeks ahead; squeeze the lemons on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml gin
  • 120ml lemon juice
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 500ml soda water
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and syrup hard with ice.
  3. Strain into 4 tall glasses of ice, top with soda and add a lemon slice.
