Snacks

Thumper's clover salad cupsVegetarian

Baby romaine cups with pea shoots, mint and shaved parmesan.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Separate the cos into cups up to 1 day ahead and keep wrapped in damp paper in the fridge; toss and fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 baby cos lettuce
  • 1 punnet pea shoots
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g parmesan, shaved
  • 3 tbsp pepitas, toasted
  1. Separate the cos into small cups.
  2. Toss the pea shoots and mint with lemon juice and olive oil.
  3. Spoon into each cup and top with parmesan and pepitas.

Flower's garden cruditésVegetarian

Feta and yoghurt dip with carrots, radishes and snow peas.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g feta
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 bag baby carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 150g snow peas
  1. Whip the feta with yoghurt, honey and dill until smooth.
  2. Spread in a shallow dish.
  3. Arrange carrots, radishes and snow peas around it like a garden bed and serve chilled.

Great Prince's woodland mushroom pieVegetarian

Creamy mushroom and leek pie under puff pastry.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 60g butter
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 200ml cream
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 tbsp milk
  1. Cook the mushrooms and leek in butter with thyme until soft and golden, 15 min.
  2. Add the cream and simmer until thick, 5 min.
  3. Spoon into a pie dish, top with puff pastry and brush with milk.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden, 25 min.

First snow roast pumpkin soup

Roasted squash and carrot blended silky with stock, cream and nutmeg, a white swirl, garlic toasts.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg pumpkin
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1L vegetable stock
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 head garlic
  1. Roasted pumpkin and carrot blended silky with stock, cream and nutmeg, served with a snowy swirl of cream and crunchy garlic toasts. Toss 1.2kg pumpkin chunks, 2 chopped carrots and 1 quartered onion with oil and salt and roast at 200°C for 35 minutes. Blend with 1L chicken or vegetable stock until smooth, then reheat with 100ml cream and a pinch of nutmeg and season. Slice a baguette, brush with oil, rub with garlic and toast at 200°C for 8 minutes. Serve the soup with a swirl of cream and the toasts.

Frozen pond pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue pond, cream and berries, for Bambi's slip on the ice.

Prep 28 min, cook 1 hr 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 125g raspberries
  • 125g blueberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy, then fold in the cornflour.
  3. Spread into a flat round like a frozen pond.
  4. Bake at 120°C, then cool in the oven, 1 hr 30 min.
  5. Top with whipped cream, raspberries and blueberries.

Forest berry custard tartlets

Berry custard, sweet as Flower the skunk.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 300ml milk
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  • 3 eggs
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 box cornflour
  • 125g strawberries
  • 125g blueberries
  • 125g raspberries
  • 1 packet icing sugar
  1. Crisp little shortcrust cases filled with vanilla custard and piled with strawberries, blueberries and raspberries, dusted with icing sugar. Cut 12 rounds from 2 shortcrust sheets and press into a muffin tin. Prick and bake at 180°C for 12 minutes until golden, then cool. Heat 300ml milk with 1 tsp vanilla. Whisk 3 egg yolks, 60g sugar and 2 tbsp cornflour, pour in the hot milk, return to the pan and stir until thick. Cool, then spoon into the cases and top with 250g mixed berries. Dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Spring meadow coolerNo alcohol

Apple juice and soda with elderflower syrup, cucumber and mint.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the apple juice and elderflower cordial in a jug up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; slice the cucumber up to 4 hours ahead, then add ice, mint and soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, in ribbons
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 1 L apple juice
  • 4 tbsp elderflower cordial
  • 1 L soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice, cucumber ribbons and mint.
  2. Pour in apple juice to halfway and add a splash of elderflower cordial.
  3. Top with cold soda water and stir gently.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Thumper recites his greens rhyme.
