Snacks

Magda's continental platter

Salami, kabana, dill pickles, cream cheese and sliced dark rye

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the salami and kabana and cut the pickles up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; slice the rye just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g salami
  • 150g kabana
  • 6 dill pickles
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 1 dark rye loaf
  1. Slice the salami and the kabana and lay them out on a big board.
  2. Add the dill pickles cut into spears, the cream cheese in a bowl and a sliced dark rye loaf, the way Magda would set out a New Year's Eve spread.

Goode's lunch room egg sandwichesVegetarian

Mashed egg mayo and cress on soft white bread

Prep 12 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mash the egg filling up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; spread, trim and cut the sandwiches within 2 hours of serving
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 punnet cress
  • 8 slices white bread
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool, peel and mash with the mayonnaise, salt and pepper.
  2. Spread over the white bread, scatter with cress and top with more slices.
  3. Trim the crusts and cut into fingers.

Mains

Rudi's chicken paprikash

Chicken thighs braised in sweet paprika with onion and capsicum

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the paprikash up to 2 days ahead without the sour cream and keep chilled; reheat, stir the sour cream through and cook the noodles fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 onions
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 375ml chicken stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 20g butter
  1. Brown the chicken thighs, then soften the onions and the red capsicum in the pan.
  2. Stir in the sweet paprika, add the chicken stock and the chicken, and simmer 30 minutes.
  3. Cook the egg noodles and toss with the butter. Stir the sour cream through the paprikash off the heat and serve over the buttered noodles.

Sweets

35 guinea poppy seed cakeVegetarian

Lemon loaf cake with poppy seeds and a lemon icing

Prep 16 min, cook 68 min · Make ahead: Bake the loaf up to 2 days ahead and wrap once cool; ice it the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 180g butter
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 lemons
  • 220g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 80ml milk
  • 150g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, add the eggs and the zest of the lemons, then fold in the self-raising flour, the poppy seeds and the milk.
  2. Bake in a loaf tin at 170C for 50 minutes.
  3. Ice with the icing sugar mixed with lemon juice.

Drinks

Magda's New Year's Eve champagne punchVegetarian

Brandy, peach liqueur, orange juice and sparkling wine over ice

Prep 8 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix the brandy, peach liqueur, orange juice and peaches and chill up to 1 day ahead; add the ice, sparkling wine and soda water just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml brandy
  • 60ml peach liqueur
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 2 ripe peaches
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 250ml soda water
  • 1 bag block ice
  1. In a large punch bowl, mix the brandy, the peach liqueur, the fresh orange juice and the sliced ripe peaches.
  2. Chill 1 hour.
  3. Just before serving add a big block of ice and pour in the chilled bottle of sparkling wine and the soda water.
  4. Stir once gently.
  5. Ladle into coupes with a slice of peach in.
  6. For non-drinkers, use peach nectar and sparkling apple juice.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Magda welcomes everyone to her New Year's Eve party.
