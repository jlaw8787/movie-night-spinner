Snacks

Rorschach's beans, upgradedVegetarian

Cannellini beans in olive oil with garlic, rosemary and chilli, baked beans on the side

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Warm the cannellini beans with the garlic and rosemary up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the sourdough on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g cannellini beans (2 tins), drained
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 4 slices sourdough
  • 1 tin baked beans, served cold
  1. Warm the cannellini beans in the oil with the garlic, rosemary and chilli, crushing a few.
  2. Grill the sourdough.
  3. Pile the beans onto the toast.
  4. Put one cold tin of baked beans and a spoon on the side for authenticity.

Midnight onion bhajisVegetarian

Onion fritters, mint yoghurt. Rorschach's journal goes in the crank file

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 green chilli, chopped
  • 1 small bunch coriander, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150g chickpea flour
  • 80ml water
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 4 tbsp tamarind chutney
  1. Mix the onions with the chilli, coriander, cumin seeds, ground turmeric, chilli powder and salt.
  2. Stir in the chickpea flour and water to a thick batter that coats the onions.
  3. Fry rough spoonfuls in the oil at 175C in batches until crisp.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with chopped mint and serve with the tamarind chutney.

Mains

Midnight curry

Creamy chicken curry, rice, naan. Dr Manhattan exiles himself to Mars

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 400g crushed tomatoes (1 tin)
  • 1kg chicken thighs, diced
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 naan breads
  1. Soften the onion, ginger and garlic in the oil.
  2. Add the garam masala, tomatoes and chicken.
  3. Simmer until the chicken is tender.
  4. Cook the rice.
  5. Stir the cream into the curry and serve with rice and warm naan.

Comedian's badge cookiesVegetarian

Butter cookies with yellow icing, a black smile and a drop of strawberry jam

Prep 26 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp yellow food colouring
  • 1 tsp black food colouring
  • 4 tbsp strawberry jam
  1. Beat the butter, sugar and egg, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll to 5mm and cut rounds.
  3. Bake at 180C and cool.
  4. Mix the icing sugar with water to a thick icing and tint most of it yellow, a little black.
  5. Ice the cookies, pipe two eyes and a smile in black and add a drop of jam.

Archie's midnight coffee old fashionedVegetarian

Archie flies to Antarctica. Coffee bourbon

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep the coffee in the bourbon and strain up to 3 days ahead, keeping it in the fridge; stir with ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 2 tbsp coarsely ground coffee
  • 4 tsp maple syrup
  • 8 dashes chocolate or aromatic bitters
  • 1 orange
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 2 cup ice, for stirring
  1. Steep the coffee in the bourbon, then strain through a paper filter.
  2. Stir the bourbon with the maple syrup and bitters over ice in a jug until very cold.
  3. Strain into four tumblers over one large cube each.
  4. Twist orange peel over each and float three coffee beans.

When to serve

Serve the beans when Rorschach tells Dan he helped himself to the beans.
