Blockbuster brown butter parmesan popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed with nutty brown butter and parmesan salt.

Prep 2 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Brown the butter up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled to rewarm; pop the corn just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 80g butter
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp rosemary leaves
  1. Heat the oil and kernels in a big lidded pot over medium-high heat, shaking until the popping slows.
  2. Cook the butter in a small pan until golden brown and nutty.
  3. Blitz the salt with the rosemary.
  4. Toss the popcorn with the brown butter, parmesan and rosemary salt and serve in big tubs.

Goose's fish bites

Panko-crumbed salmon bites with tartare sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day before baking, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g salmon fillet, skin off
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp oil spray or oil
  • 120g tartare sauce
  1. Cut the salmon into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Dust in flour, dip in egg and press into the panko.
  3. Spread on a lined tray, spray with a little oil and bake at 220°C until golden.
  4. Serve with tartare sauce. Keep them away from the cat.

Louisiana crab cakes with remoulade

Sweet crab bound with mayo, mustard and crumbs, pan-fried crisp, tangy caper mayo.

How it is made
  • 400g crab meat
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 egg
  • 60g panko crumbs
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 lemon
  1. Sweet crab meat bound with mayo, mustard, spring onion and breadcrumbs, pan-fried crisp and golden, with a tangy caper remoulade. Mix 400g drained crab meat with 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 2 sliced spring onions, 1 egg, 60g panko and a pinch of cayenne. Shape into 8 small cakes and chill 30 minutes. Fry in oil and butter over medium heat for 3 minutes a side until golden. For the remoulade, mix 100g mayo with 1 tbsp chopped capers, 1 tbsp chopped pickles, 1 tsp mustard, paprika and lemon juice.

Maria's Louisiana gumbo

Dark roux gumbo with chicken, smoked sausage and okra, served over rice.

Prep 5 min, cook 103 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 60g plain flour
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, chopped
  • 300g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 200g okra, sliced
  • 1.5 L water
  • 300g long grain rice
  1. Stir the flour into the oil over medium heat until it turns dark brown.
  2. Add the onion, capsicum and celery and soften.
  3. Add the chicken, sausage, okra and water and simmer until rich and thick.
  4. Cook the rice and serve the gumbo over it.

Rambeau family honey butter fried chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken with honey butter, at Maria's table.

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in buttermilk up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; dredge and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 60g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with a big pinch of salt for at least 1 hour.
  2. Mix the flour with the paprika, garlic powder, half the cayenne and salt. Dredge the chicken, dip back in buttermilk and dredge again.
  3. Fry in oil at 170°C, a few pieces at a time, until deep golden and cooked through.
  4. Melt the butter with the honey, hot sauce and the rest of the cayenne and brush over the hot chicken.

Photon blast pieVegetarian

Tart lemon meringue pie. Take the pie, leave the lunchbox.

Prep 10 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 4 lemons
  • 350g caster sugar
  • 4 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 60g butter
  1. Blind bake a pastry case at 180°C.
  2. Separate the eggs. Whisk the lemon juice and zest, a portion of the sugar, cornflour, egg yolks and butter over heat until thick, and pour into the case.
  3. Whisk the egg whites with the remaining sugar to glossy peaks and pile on top.
  4. Bake at 200°C until the meringue is golden.

Drinks

1995 fuzzy navel slushies

Frozen peach and orange slush with peach schnapps, or a no-alcohol swap.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best blended to order so it stays thick; chill the glasses and orange juice up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen peach slices
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 160ml peach schnapps
  • 1 lime
  • 2 cups ice
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 4 orange wheels
  1. Blend the peaches, orange juice, schnapps, lime juice and ice until thick and slushy.
  2. Pour into chilled glasses and top each with a cherry and an orange wheel.
