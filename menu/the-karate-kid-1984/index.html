Snacks

Bonsai green beans and sesame cucumbersVegetarian

Salted chilli green beans in the pod, rice vinegar pickled cucumber with sesame.

Prep 25 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 400g frozen green beans
  • flaky salt, to taste
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Cut the cucumber into batons and pickle in the vinegar, sugar and salt.
  2. Pickle.
  3. Steam the green beans in their pods, then toss with flaky salt, chilli and sesame oil.
  4. Drain the cucumber and scatter with toasted sesame seeds. Serve both in small bowls with chopsticks only.

Bonsai broccoli

Charred garlic and oyster broccoli, stood up like little bonsai.

Prep 7 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Blanch the broccoli up to 1 day ahead and chill; char and toss with the sauce just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches broccoli
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  1. Blanch the broccoli and drain. Slice the garlic.
  2. Char in a smoking pan with the garlic.
  3. Toss with oyster sauce and sesame oil. Stand them up in a dish like trees.

Okinawan purple sweet potato chips

Sliced paper thin and fried crisp, sea salt and chilli salt, creamy mustard mayo for dipping.

How it is made
  • 2 purple sweet potatoes
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 jar chilli salt
  • 1 jar Japanese mayonnaise
  • 1 tube wasabi paste
  • 1 lime
  1. Purple sweet potato sliced paper thin and fried crisp, dusted with sea salt and chilli salt, with a creamy mustard mayo for dipping. Peel 2 purple sweet potatoes and slice paper thin on a mandoline. Soak in cold water for 20 minutes, then dry very well on tea towels. Heat 1L vegetable oil to 170°C and fry in small batches for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp. Drain and toss with sea salt and a pinch of chilli salt. Mix 150g mayo with 2 tsp wasabi paste and a squeeze of lime for dipping.

Mains

Johnny's country club spaghetti

Beef meat sauce spaghetti, Cobra Kai style. Strike first, strike hard.

Prep 18 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the meat sauce up to 3 days ahead and reheat; cook the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 700g passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion and garlic.
  3. Add the passata and oregano and simmer.
  4. Cook the spaghetti, drain and toss with the sauce.
  5. Serve with grated parmesan and basil.

Okinawan soki soba pork noodles

Braised belly in soy and ginger, thick wheat noodles in bonito broth, after sanding the floor.

How it is made
  • 800g pork belly
  • 125ml soy sauce
  • 125ml mirin
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1.5L chicken stock
  • 1 jar dashi powder
  • 800g fresh udon noodles
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Pork belly braised soft in soy, mirin and ginger, on thick wheat noodles in a bonito and chicken broth, topped with pickled ginger and spring onion. Cut 800g pork belly into 4cm pieces, blanch 5 minutes and drain. Simmer with 125ml soy, 125ml mirin, 3 tbsp sugar, sliced ginger and 750ml water for 2 hours until tender. Heat 1.5L chicken stock with 2 tsp dashi powder and 2 tbsp of the braising liquid. Cook 800g fresh udon for 2 minutes and divide into bowls. Pour over broth, add the pork and top with pickled ginger and spring onion.

Miyagi's red bean mochiVegetarian

Sweet red bean paste in soft rice dough.

Prep 23 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 200g glutinous rice flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 220ml water
  • 100g red bean paste
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  1. Stir the rice flour, sugar and water into a dough.
  2. Steam.
  3. Knead smooth on a cornflour-dusted bench.
  4. Divide into 8, wrap each around a ball of red bean paste and dust with cornflour.

Drinks

Miyagi's honey ginger green teaNo alcohol

Sencha with ginger, honey and lemon. First learn balance.

Prep 13 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Simmer the ginger water up to 2 days ahead and chill it; reheat to about 80°C and steep the sencha fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L water
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 4 tsp sencha green tea
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  1. Simmer the water with sliced ginger.
  2. Cool to about 80°C.
  3. Add the sencha and steep for 1 minute only, then strain.
  4. Stir in honey and a squeeze of lemon. Pour into small cups, or cool completely and serve over ice with a slice of lemon.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Daniel learns wax on, wax off.
