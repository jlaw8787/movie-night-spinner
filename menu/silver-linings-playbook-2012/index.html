Snacks

Dolores's crabby snacks

Baguette slices with crab, cream cheese and spring onion, grilled

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 baguette
  1. Mix the crab with the cream cheese, mayonnaise, lemon zest and sliced spring onion.
  2. Slice the baguette and spread the mix on each slice.
  3. Grill until golden at the edges. Serve hot.

Baked meatballs with sweet tomato sauce

Beef and pork meatballs baked with parmesan, warm brown sugar tomato sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • toothpicks, to serve
  1. Mix the mince with the parmesan, breadcrumbs and egg. Roll into small balls.
  2. Bake at 200C until cooked through.
  3. Warm the tomato sauce with brown sugar until sticky and toss the meatballs through.
  4. Serve on toothpicks.

Mains

Philly cheesesteaks

Shaved rib eye with onion, capsicum and provolone

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Slice the onions and capsicum up to 1 day ahead; freeze the steak until firm (30 minutes, or up to 1 day in advance) and shave and cook on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rib eye steak
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g provolone, sliced
  • 4 long bread rolls
  1. Freeze the steak until firm, which is the resting time, then shave thin.
  2. Slice the onions and capsicum.
  3. Fry the onion and capsicum in the oil on a hot plate until soft.
  4. Add the beef and sear fast.
  5. Lay the provolone on top to melt and pile into the rolls.

Sweets

Diner date bran and raisin muffinsVegetarian

Raisin Bran, so nobody mistakes it for a date. Bran muffins, buttermilk.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g wheat bran
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  • 100g raisins
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g butter, to serve
  1. Soak the bran in the buttermilk.
  2. Stir in the sugar, egg, oil and raisins, then fold in the flour.
  3. Spoon into a lined muffin tin and bake at 190C.
  4. Cool slightly and serve warm with butter.

Drinks

Eagles green apple shandyVegetarian

The parlay: Eagles win and the dance scores five. Lager, green apple, lime.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer up to 1 hour ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cans lager (375 ml each), cold
  • 600ml cloudy apple juice, chilled
  • 2 limes
  • 1 green apple
  1. Put 4 pint glasses in the freezer for the chilling time.
  2. Juice the limes and slice the apple very thin.
  3. Pour lager into each glass, top with apple juice and a squeeze of lime, then float apple slices. For non-drinkers, use ginger beer in place of the lager.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Dolores puts out her crabby snacks for the Eagles game.
