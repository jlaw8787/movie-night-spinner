Snacks

Dolores's crabby snacks

Baguette slices with crab, cream cheese and spring onion, grilled.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 baguette
  1. Mix the crab with the cream cheese, mayonnaise, lemon zest and sliced spring onion.
  2. Slice the baguette and spread the mix on each slice.
  3. Grill until golden at the edges. Serve hot.

Baked meatballs with sweet tomato sauce

Beef and pork meatballs baked with parmesan, warm brown sugar tomato sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • toothpicks, to serve
  1. Mix the mince with the parmesan, breadcrumbs and egg. Roll into small balls.
  2. Bake at 200°C until cooked through.
  3. Warm the tomato sauce with brown sugar until sticky and toss the meatballs through.
  4. Serve on toothpicks.

Philly soft pretzels with beer mustardOn the printed menu

Chewy knots dipped in bicarb, rock salted and baked deep brown, hot honey mustard to dunk.

How it is made
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 70g bicarb soda
  • 1 egg
  • rock salt
  • 80g wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 can lager
  1. Chewy yeasted pretzels dipped in a bicarb bath, scattered with rock salt and baked deep brown, with a hot honey and beer mustard for dunking. Mix 500g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp salt, 1 tbsp sugar and 320ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and rest 1 hour. Heat the oven to 220°C. Roll into 8 long ropes and twist into knots. Simmer 2L water with 70g bicarb soda and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds. Place on lined trays, brush with egg, sprinkle rock salt and bake 12 to 14 minutes. Mix 80g wholegrain mustard, 2 tbsp honey and 2 tbsp lager for dipping.

Mains

Philly cheesesteaks

Shaved rib eye with onion, capsicum and provolone.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Slice the onions and capsicum up to 1 day ahead; freeze the steak until firm (30 minutes, or up to 1 day in advance) and shave and cook on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rib eye steak
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g provolone, sliced
  • 4 long bread rolls
  1. Freeze the steak until firm, which is the resting time, then shave thin.
  2. Slice the onions and capsicum.
  3. Fry the onion and capsicum in the oil on a hot plate until soft.
  4. Add the beef and sear fast.
  5. Lay the provolone on top to melt and pile into the rolls.

Game day Sunday gravy with rigatoniOn the printed menu

Pork ribs, Italian sausage and meatballs simmered for hours in garlicky tomato, with parmesan.

How it is made
  • 600g pork spare ribs
  • 4 Italian pork sausages
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 80g parmesan
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1.4kg passata
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Pork ribs, Italian sausages and meatballs browned and simmered for hours in a garlicky tomato sauce, tossed through rigatoni with parmesan and basil. Brown 600g pork ribs and 4 Italian sausages in olive oil in a large pot. Roll 400g beef mince with 1 egg, 40g breadcrumbs and 30g parmesan into 16 meatballs and brown. Remove all the meat. Fry 4 sliced garlic cloves for 1 minute, add 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1.4kg passata and 250ml water. Return the meat, cover partly and simmer gently for 2 to 3 hours, stirring often. Cook 500g rigatoni, toss with sauce and serve with the meat, basil and parmesan.

Sweets

Diner date bran and raisin muffinsVegetarian

Bran muffins, buttermilk. Raisin Bran, so nobody mistakes it for a date.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g wheat bran
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  • 100g raisins
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g butter, to serve
  1. Soak the bran in the buttermilk.
  2. Stir in the sugar, egg, oil and raisins, then fold in the flour.
  3. Spoon into a lined muffin tin and bake at 190°C.
  4. Cool slightly and serve warm with butter.

Drinks

Eagles green apple shandyVegetarian

Lager, green apple, lime. The parlay: Eagles win and the dance scores five.

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer up to 1 hour ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cans lager (375 ml each), cold
  • 600ml cloudy apple juice, chilled
  • 2 limes
  • 1 green apple
  1. Put 4 pint glasses in the freezer for the chilling time.
  2. Juice the limes and slice the apple very thin.
  3. Pour lager into each glass, top with apple juice and a squeeze of lime, then float apple slices. For non-drinkers, use ginger beer in place of the lager.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Dolores puts out her crabby snacks for the Eagles game.
