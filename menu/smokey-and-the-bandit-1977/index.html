Snacks

Georgia boiled peanutsVegetarian

Raw peanuts simmered in salty Cajun water, served warm in paper cups

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 500g raw peanuts in the shell
  • 80g fine salt
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 2l water
  1. Rinse the peanuts well and put them in a large pot with the water, salt and Cajun seasoning.
  2. Simmer with the lid on, topping up with water as needed, until the peanuts are soft.
  3. Turn off the heat and let the peanuts soak in the brine.
  4. Drain and serve warm in paper cups.

Sheriff Justice's pickled okraVegetarian

Garlic and dill pickled okra with chilli, served cold with sharp cheddar

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min, then 7 days to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 2 weeks in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 400g okra
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 red chilli
  • 300ml white vinegar
  • 200ml water
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  1. Pack the okra into a clean jar with the garlic, dill and chilli.
  2. Boil the vinegar, water and salt together and pour over while hot.
  3. Cool, lid and pickle in the fridge for a week.
  4. Serve cold with the cheddar cut into chunks.

Fred's cayenne pork cracklingOn the printed menu

Pork crackling dusted with cayenne, roasted until it puffs, split with Fred

How it is made
  • 600g pork rind
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Pork rind dried overnight then roasted until puffed and crackling, broken into shards and dusted with smoked paprika, cayenne and salt, with a ranch dip. Score 600g pork rind in strips, rub well with salt and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Pat dry, lay skin up on a rack and roast at 240°C for 30 to 40 minutes until fully puffed and crackling. Break into shards and dust with 1 tsp smoked paprika, half a tsp cayenne and salt. Mix 150g sour cream, 2 tbsp mayo, garlic powder, dill and chives for dip.

Mains

Truck stop chicken fried steak

Crisp breaded steak under creamy black pepper gravy

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Mash the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and reheat with a splash of milk; pound the steaks, fry them and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 minute steaks
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp plain flour for the gravy
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 250ml vegetable oil
  1. Peel and boil the potatoes until soft, then mash with the butter and some of the milk. Keep warm.
  2. Pound the steaks thin and season the flour with the salt and plenty of cracked black peppercorns.
  3. Dredge each steak in flour, then buttermilk, then flour again.
  4. Shallow fry in hot oil until deep golden and crisp, then keep warm on a rack.
  5. Whisk the gravy flour into the pan fat, add the remaining milk and lots of cracked pepper and simmer until thick.
  6. Serve the steaks over the mash with the gravy.

28 hour Texarkana barbecue ribsOn the printed menu

Pork rubbed with paprika and cayenne, tangy vinegar glaze, coleslaw, 400 cases of beer to haul

How it is made
  • 2kg pork ribs
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  1. Pork ribs rubbed with paprika, brown sugar and cayenne, baked low and slow, glazed with a tangy vinegar barbecue sauce and charred, with crunchy coleslaw. Mix 2 tbsp paprika, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp cayenne and 2 tsp salt and rub over 2kg pork ribs. Wrap in foil and bake at 150°C for 2.5 hours. Simmer 125ml cider vinegar, 125ml tomato sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tsp chilli flakes for 5 minutes. Brush on the ribs and grill hot for 5 minutes. Toss half a shredded cabbage and a grated carrot with 125g mayo and vinegar.

Double or nothing peach cobblerVegetarian

Cinnamon peaches under a soft biscuit topping, with vanilla ice cream

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Best warm, bake the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 peaches
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 lemon
  • 80g cold butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Slice the peaches and toss with the sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the milk to a thick batter.
  3. Drop spoonfuls of batter over the fruit and bake until golden and bubbling.
  4. Serve warm with scoops of ice cream.

Drinks

Bandit's bourbon peach sweet teaVegetarian

Sweet black tea with peaches and a shot of bourbon over ice

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Tea base up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 black tea bags
  • 1l boiling water
  • 2 peaches
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 120ml bourbon
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water with the sliced peaches and sugar for 6 minutes, then remove the bags.
  2. Chill the tea with the peaches in it until very cold.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice and add bourbon to each.
  4. Top with tea and finish with a squeeze of lemon, a peach slice and a sprig of mint.
