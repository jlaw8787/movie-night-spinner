Snacks

Mikaela's loaded potato wedges

Paprika roast wedges topped with cheddar and crisp bacon.

Prep 12 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Fry the bacon and grate the cheddar up to 1 day ahead; roast the wedges and top on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 120g cheddar
  • 100g bacon
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Cut the potatoes into wedges and toss with oil and paprika.
  2. Roast at 220°C.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp, grate the cheddar and slice the spring onions.
  4. Top the wedges with cheddar and bacon and bake.
  5. Add sour cream and spring onion.

Mission City loaded chicken nachos

Chicken, black beans and cheese on corn chips, loaded and ready to roll out.

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Shred the chicken, drain the beans and grate the cheese up to 1 day ahead, chilled; assemble and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g corn chips
  • 250g cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 can black beans
  • 200g tasty cheese
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Drain the beans and grate the cheese.
  2. Spread the chips on a tray and top with chicken, beans and cheese.
  3. Bake at 200°C.
  4. Finish with salsa and sour cream.

Desert base lamb kofta with garlic yoghurt

Spiced lamb mince with cumin and cinnamon, shaped on skewers and grilled, garlic yoghurt to dip.

How it is made
  • 600g lamb mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar ground coriander
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 2 tomatoes
  1. Lamb mince with onion, cumin and parsley shaped onto skewers, grilled and served with garlic yoghurt. Mix 600g lamb mince with 1 grated onion squeezed dry, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, half a teaspoon cinnamon, chopped parsley and salt. Shape around 8 skewers into long sausages and chill 20 minutes. Grill over high heat for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until charred. Mix 200g Greek yoghurt with 1 crushed garlic clove, lemon juice and mint. Serve with warm flatbread and sliced tomato.

Ron's backyard ribs

Pork ribs slow-cooked and glazed with sticky barbecue sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Rub and wrap the ribs up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake them, make the coleslaw and char to finish on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork ribs
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 100ml barbecue sauce
  • 1/2 small cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Rub the ribs with paprika, sugar and salt and wrap in foil.
  2. Bake at 150°C.
  3. Brush with barbecue sauce and char over hot coals.
  4. Shred the cabbage, grate the carrot and toss with mayonnaise for coleslaw.

Hoover Dam beef chilli and cornbread

Beef chilli with beans, served with a wedge of cornbread.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tins kidney beans
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 60g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Beef chuck browned and simmered 3 hours with kidney beans, chilli, cumin and tomato until thick, with sour cream and cornbread. Brown 1kg diced beef chuck in batches. Fry 2 onions and 4 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika and 2 tsp chilli powder. Return the beef with 2 tins crushed tomatoes and 250ml beef stock and simmer covered for 2.5 hours. Add 2 drained tins kidney beans for the last 30 minutes. Mix 150g cornmeal, 150g flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 egg, 250ml buttermilk and 60g melted butter and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes. Serve with sour cream.

Sweets

AllSpark cube browniesVegetarian

Fudgy brownies cut into cubes and dusted gold.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • edible glitter, to dust
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a tin. Melt the chocolate and butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Bake.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Cut into neat cubes and dust with glitter.
