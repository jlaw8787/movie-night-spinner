Snacks

Frozone's frozen grapesVegetarian

Grapes rolled in lime juice and sugar, frozen solid

Prep 10 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 500g grapes
  • 1 lime
  • 50g caster sugar
  1. Pick the grapes off the stem, wash and pat dry.
  2. Roll in the lime juice, then in the sugar.
  3. Spread on a lined tray and freeze until solid. Serve from the freezer in a chilled bowl.

Nomanisan Island coconut prawns

Coconut panko prawns with a mango, chilli and lime dip

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 2 hours ahead, kept chilled, and blitz the mango dip up to 1 day ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, peeled
  • 80g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 60g shredded coconut
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 1 mango
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 lime
  1. Butterfly the prawns. Mix the panko and coconut.
  2. Dip in flour, egg, then the coconut crumb.
  3. Blitz the mango with a little chilli and lime for the dip.
  4. Fry in the oil at 180C in batches until golden.

Mains

Family dinner meatloaf

Beef and pork meatloaf with tomato glaze, mashed potato and peas

Prep 30 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Loaf up to 1 day, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix lightly with both minces, onion, egg and Worcestershire.
  2. Shape in a loaf tin, glaze with tomato sauce and bake at 180C.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter. Cook the peas.
  4. Rest the loaf, slice and serve with mash and peas.

Sweets

Edna Mode's choc cakeVegetarian

Rich almond meal chocolate cake with a plain chocolate glaze

Prep 25 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 100g almond meal
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 160C and line a tin. Melt most of the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until thick, fold in the chocolate and almond meal.
  3. Bake, then cool completely in the tin.
  4. Melt the remaining chocolate with the cream and pour over. No decoration, no capes.

Drinks

Frozone's frozen mint lemonade slushVegetarianNo alcohol

Blended lemon and mint slush with a splash of lemonade, no alcohol

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons and mix the juice, syrup, mint and water up to 1 day ahead, chilled; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 5 lemons
  • 120ml sugar syrup
  • 10 mint leaves
  • 4 mint sprigs, to garnish
  • 200ml cold water
  • 4 cup ice
  • 300ml lemonade
  1. Juice four lemons. Slice the last into wheels.
  2. Blend the juice, sugar syrup, mint leaves, water and ice until slushy.
  3. Pour into glasses, top with a splash of lemonade and garnish with a lemon wheel and mint.
