Snacks

Frozone's frozen grapesVegetarian

Grapes rolled in lime juice and sugar, frozen solid.

Prep 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 500g grapes
  • 1 lime
  • 50g caster sugar
  1. Pick the grapes off the stem, wash and pat dry.
  2. Roll in the lime juice, then in the sugar.
  3. Spread on a lined tray and freeze until solid. Serve from the freezer in a chilled bowl.

Nomanisan Island coconut shrimp

Coconut panko shrimp with a mango, chilli and lime dip.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 2 hours ahead, kept chilled, and blitz the mango dip up to 1 day ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, peeled
  • 80g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 60g shredded coconut
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 1 mango
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 lime
  1. Butterfly the prawns. Mix the panko and coconut.
  2. Dip in flour, egg, then the coconut crumb.
  3. Blitz the mango with a little chilli and lime for the dip.
  4. Fry in the oil at 180°C in batches until golden.

Jack-Jack's flaming cheese corn puffs

Puffed corn tossed in butter, cheddar powder, paprika and chilli, hot enough to flame.

How it is made
  • 150g puffed corn snacks
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp cheddar cheese powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • garlic powder
  • mild chilli powder
  1. Puffed corn tossed in butter, cheddar powder, smoked paprika and a pinch of mild chilli, baked until toasty and orange like a baby on fire. Heat the oven to 160°C. Melt 50g butter and toss with 150g puffed corn snacks. Mix 3 tbsp cheddar cheese powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, half a tsp garlic powder and a small pinch of mild chilli powder, and shake it over the corn until coated. Spread on a tray and bake 5 minutes until toasty. Cool 5 minutes before serving.

Mains

Family dinner meatloaf

Beef and pork meatloaf with tomato glaze, mashed potato and peas.

Prep 8 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Loaf up to 1 day, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix lightly with both minces, onion, egg and Worcestershire.
  2. Shape in a loaf tin, glaze with tomato sauce and bake at 180°C.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter. Cook the peas.
  4. Rest the loaf, slice and serve with mash and peas.

Mirage's Nomanisan Island steak dinner

Seared tenderloin with balsamic jus, garlic potato gratin and green beans, a supervillain's spread.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 4 eye fillet steaks (200g each)
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 50g butter
  • 300g green beans
  • 30g flaked almonds
  1. Eye fillet seared and rested with a glossy balsamic beef jus, creamy garlic potato gratin and buttered green beans with toasted almonds. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes in a buttered dish with 300ml cream warmed with 2 crushed garlic cloves, salt and thyme, and bake at 160°C for 75 minutes. Season 4 eye fillet steaks (200g each), sear 3 minutes a side and rest 5 minutes. Add 400ml beef stock and 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar to the pan and reduce to a glaze, then whisk in 20g butter. Blanch 300g green beans and toss with butter and 30g toasted flaked almonds.

Edna Mode's choc cakeVegetarian

Rich almond meal chocolate cake with a plain chocolate glaze.

Prep 10 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 100g almond meal
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C and line a tin. Melt most of the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until thick, fold in the chocolate and almond meal.
  3. Bake, then cool completely in the tin.
  4. Melt the remaining chocolate with the cream and pour over. No decoration, no capes.

Drinks

Frozone's frozen mint lemonade slushNo alcohol

Blended lemon and mint slush with a splash of lemonade, icy enough for a summer suburb.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons and mix the juice, syrup, mint and water up to 1 day ahead, chilled; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 5 lemons
  • 120ml sugar syrup
  • 10 mint leaves
  • 4 mint sprigs, to garnish
  • 200ml cold water
  • 4 cups ice
  • 300ml lemonade
  1. Juice four lemons. Slice the last into wheels.
  2. Blend the juice, sugar syrup, mint leaves, water and ice until slushy.
  3. Pour into glasses, top with a splash of lemonade and garnish with a lemon wheel and mint.
