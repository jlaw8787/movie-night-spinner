Snacks

Garden party canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche and smoked salmon, cucumber rounds with whipped goat's cheese

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Whip the goat's cheese up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; top the blinis and pipe the rounds just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp dill
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 120g goat's cheese
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, dill and lemon zest.
  2. Cut the cucumber into thick rounds.
  3. Whip the goat's cheese with chives and pipe onto the rounds.
  4. Lay both neatly on silver trays.

Georgina's garden tomato toastsVegetarian

Grilled sourdough with whipped ricotta, garden tomatoes and basil, served on the terrace

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the ricotta up to a day ahead; toast and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 500g mixed heirloom tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Whip the ricotta with the lemon zest, a pinch of salt and 1 tbsp of the oil.
  2. Cut the tomatoes into wedges and season with salt and the rest of the oil.
  3. Cut 8 slices of sourdough and grill or toast until charred at the edges.
  4. Rub each toast with the cut garlic clove.
  5. Spread with ricotta, pile on the tomatoes and finish with torn basil.

Mains

Lawn party lamb

Lamb marinated overnight with garlic, rosemary and lemon, grilled and rested

Prep 20 min, cook 765 min · Make ahead: Marinate overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg butterflied lamb leg
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 rosemary sprigs
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 cup parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp capers
  1. Marinate the lamb overnight with garlic, rosemary, lemon and oil.
  2. Grill over high heat until 55C inside.
  3. Rest.
  4. Chop the parsley, capers, garlic and lemon with oil for salsa verde.
  5. Slice the lamb and spoon over the salsa.

Sweets

Brown butter marshmallow cereal bars

Marshmallow bars made with coloured fruit cereal, served with cold milk

Prep 15 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead, airtight; pour the cold milk to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 300g marshmallows
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 180g coloured fruit cereal
  • 1l full cream milk, cold
  1. Melt the butter in a large pan and cook until it smells nutty.
  2. Add the marshmallows and salt and stir until melted.
  3. Off the heat, fold in the cereal quickly.
  4. Press into a lined 20cm square tin with a buttered spatula.
  5. Cool, then cut into 16 bars.
  6. Serve with tall glasses of cold milk, kept separate.

Teacup panna cotta

Vanilla bean panna cotta set in teacups

Prep 20 min, cook 250 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 gelatine leaves
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 200g mixed berries
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves.
  2. Warm the cream with most of the sugar and the split vanilla bean, then stir in the squeezed gelatine.
  3. Pour into four teacups and set.
  4. Simmer the berries with a spoon of sugar and spoon on top.
  5. Serve with silver spoons.

Missy's spiced Earl Grey milk teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Earl Grey simmered with cinnamon and cardamom, honey, topped with frothy milk

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Simmer the spices and steep the tea up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat, add the honey and top with frothy milk
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 4 Earl Grey tea bags
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 400ml full cream milk
  1. Boil the water with the cinnamon and cardamom and simmer.
  2. Take off the heat, add the tea bags and steep, then remove.
  3. Stir in the honey.
  4. Heat the milk until steaming and whisk until frothy.
  5. Pour the tea into teacups, top with milk and serve with a silver teaspoon.

When to serve

Serve the cereal when Rose eats her coloured cereal with the milk in a separate glass.
