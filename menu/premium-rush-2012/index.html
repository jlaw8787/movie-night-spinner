Snacks

Fuel barVegetarian

Oats and honey eaten mid-ride.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 80g honey
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 250g rolled oats
  • 100g dates, chopped
  • 80g peanuts
  • 40g mixed seeds
  1. Melt the butter, honey and brown sugar together.
  2. Stir through the oats, dates, peanuts and seeds.
  3. Press into a lined tin and bake at 170°C.
  4. Cool completely before cutting into bars.

Char siu bao

Steamed pork buns from Chinatown, where Nima's package passes through.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Pork up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork neck
  • 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp five spice
  • 12 frozen bao buns
  1. Mix the hoisin, honey, soy and five spice and coat the pork.
  2. Marinate in the fridge overnight, at least 4 hours.
  3. Roast at 200°C, basting with the marinade.
  4. Rest the pork, then chop.
  5. Steam the bao buns until soft and fill with the pork.

Mains

Chicken platter

One hand on the bars, eaten between drops.

Prep 8 min, cook 35 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the lemon juice and spices up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; mix the white sauce up to 2 days ahead and cook the rice and chicken on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Marinate the chicken in the lemon juice, oregano, cumin and coriander.
  2. Cook the rice with the turmeric.
  3. Mix the yoghurt, mayo, vinegar, sugar and pepper into a white sauce.
  4. Griddle the chicken until cooked through, then chop.
  5. Serve on shredded lettuce with rice, white sauce and hot sauce.

Chinatown pan-fried pork dumplings

Wilee's drop-off in Chinatown.

How it is made
  • 400g pork mince
  • 200g wombok
  • 2 spring onions
  • 5cm piece ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • sesame oil
  • 32 gow gee wrappers
  • black vinegar
  • chilli oil
  • vegetable oil
  1. Crisp-bottomed pork and cabbage dumplings steamed under a lid, with black vinegar, ginger and chilli oil for dipping. Salt 200g finely chopped wombok for 10 minutes and squeeze dry. Mix with 400g pork mince, 2 sliced spring onions, 1 tbsp grated ginger, 2 tbsp soy and 1 tsp sesame oil. Fill 32 gow gee wrappers and pleat shut. Fry in 2 tbsp oil for 2 minutes until golden underneath, add 80ml water, cover and steam 6 minutes. Serve with black vinegar mixed with shredded ginger and chilli oil.

Sweets

CookieVegetarian

The dispatch office jar.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days, baked cookies up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g choc chips
  • 1/2 tsp flaky salt
  1. Brown the butter and cool.
  2. Beat with the sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips.
  3. Rest the dough overnight in the fridge.
  4. Roll into 16 balls on lined trays.
  5. Bake at 180°C and sprinkle with flaky salt.

No brakesNo alcohol

Banana, oat and berry smoothie. Wilee's bike has no brakes, so drink fast.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before drinking; portion the bananas and berries into a bag in the freezer up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bananas
  • 250g frozen berries
  • 40g rolled oats
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 800ml milk
  • 4 shots espresso, optional
  1. Blend the bananas, berries, oats, peanut butter and milk until completely smooth.
  2. Add a shot of espresso for anyone riding through traffic.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses and drink straight away.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Wilee hits the first red light and keeps going.
