Snacks

Fuel barVegetarian

Oats, dates, peanuts and seeds baked with honey, butter and brown sugar.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min, 10 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 80g honey
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 250g rolled oats
  • 100g dates, chopped
  • 80g peanuts
  • 40g mixed seeds
  1. Melt the butter, honey and brown sugar together, 5 min.
  2. Stir through the oats, dates, peanuts and seeds.
  3. Press into a lined tin and bake at 170°C, 25 min.
  4. Cool completely before cutting into bars, 10 min.

Char siu bao

Pork neck marinated overnight in hoisin, honey, soy and five spice, roasted, in steamed buns.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min, 4 hr 10 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork neck
  • 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp five spice
  • 12 frozen bao buns
  1. Mix the hoisin, honey, soy and five spice and coat the pork.
  2. Marinate in the fridge overnight, at least 4 hours.
  3. Roast at 200°C, basting with the marinade, 35 min.
  4. Rest the pork, then chop, 10 min.
  5. Steam the bao buns until soft and fill with the pork, 10 min.

Mains

Chicken platter

Thighs in lemon, oregano, cumin and cilantro, griddled and chopped. Turmeric rice, lettuce, white and hot sauce.

Prep 8 min, cook 35 min, 1 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Marinate the chicken in the lemon juice, oregano, cumin and coriander, 1 hr.
  2. Cook the rice with the turmeric, 20 min.
  3. Mix the yoghurt, mayo, vinegar, sugar and pepper into a white sauce.
  4. Griddle the chicken until cooked through, then chop, 15 min.
  5. Serve on shredded lettuce with rice, white sauce and hot sauce.

Sweets

CookieVegetarian

Chocolate chip, browned butter dough rested overnight, flaky salt.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, 12 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g choc chips
  • 1/2 tsp flaky salt
  1. Brown the butter and cool, 10 min.
  2. Beat with the sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips.
  3. Rest the dough overnight in the fridge.
  4. Roll into 16 balls on lined trays.
  5. Bake at 180°C and sprinkle with flaky salt, 10 min.

No brakesNo alcohol

Smoothie of banana, berries, oats, peanut butter and milk, blended thick. Espresso shot 75 cents.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bananas
  • 250g frozen berries
  • 40g rolled oats
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 800ml milk
  • 4 shots of espresso, optional
  1. Blend the bananas, berries, oats, peanut butter and milk until completely smooth.
  2. Add a shot of espresso for anyone riding through traffic.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses and drink straight away.
