Snacks

Boston clam chowder cups

With oyster crackers, the Boston way

Prep 11 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled; reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 500ml clam juice or fish stock
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  • 250g chopped clam meat
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 100g oyster crackers
  1. Dice the bacon, onion, celery and potatoes.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp, then sweat the onion and celery in the fat until soft.
  3. Add the potatoes, clam juice and thyme and simmer until the potato is tender.
  4. Stir in the clam meat and cream and warm through. Serve in cups with oyster crackers.

Irish soda bread and butterVegetarian

Wholemeal buttermilk loaf baked crusty with a deep cross, salted butter, for Frank Costello's crowd

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 250g wholemeal flour
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 450ml buttermilk
  • 100g salted butter
  1. Mix the flours, bicarb and salt, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough. Shape into a round and cut a deep cross on top.
  2. Bake at 220C until golden and it sounds hollow when tapped underneath.
  3. Rest the loaf, then slice thick and serve with the salted butter.

Mains

Southie fish and chips

Flathead in crisp lager batter, triple-cooked potatoes, minty mushy peas and malt vinegar

Prep 10 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour, for batter
  • 50g cornflour
  • 330ml ice-cold lager
  • 800g flathead fillets
  • 50g plain flour, for dusting
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp malt vinegar
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips, rinse and dry well. Whisk the batter flour, cornflour and cold lager together.
  2. Fry the chips in oil at 140C until pale and soft, then drain.
  3. Cook the peas, then crush with the butter and chopped mint and keep warm.
  4. Dust the fish in flour, dip in batter and fry at 180C in batches until golden and crisp.
  5. Fry the chips again at 185C until crisp. Salt well and serve with the fish, peas and malt vinegar.

Staties' steak tips

The cops' side of the bar at Fitzy's

Prep 5 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Marinate the beef up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef sirloin
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 2 onions
  • 60g butter
  • 120g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Cut the sirloin into big 5cm chunks. Mix the soy, Worcestershire, sugar, crushed garlic and half the oil, and marinate the beef in it for at least 2 hours.
  2. Prick the potatoes, rub with salt and bake at 200C for an hour until the skins are crisp.
  3. Slice the onions into thick rings and cook slowly in the butter until soft and brown.
  4. Thread the beef onto skewers or leave loose and grill over high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, turning, until charred outside and pink in the middle. Rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Split the potatoes, fill with sour cream and chives, and serve with the steak tips under the onions.

Sweets

Boston cream pieVegetarian

Two genoise sponges filled with vanilla crème pâtissière under glossy dark chocolate ganache

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min, then 48 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 7 eggs
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 120g plain flour
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 25g cornflour
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk 4 of the eggs with half the sugar until thick and pale. Fold in the flour and divide between two lined 20cm tins.
  2. Bake at 180C until springy, then turn out and cool completely.
  3. Heat the milk. Whisk 3 yolks with the remaining sugar and cornflour, whisk in the hot milk, return to the pan and stir until thick.
  4. Chill the custard until cold and set.
  5. Sandwich the sponges with custard. Melt the chocolate with the cream and pour over the top.

Drinks

Costigan's cranberry lime crushVegetarian

Costigan's bar order, and Mr. French who won't let it go

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the base up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml cranberry juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 60ml orange juice
  • 20ml sugar syrup
  • 200ml soda water
  • 4 cup crushed ice
  • 4 lime wedges
  • 120ml vodka, optional (30ml per glass)
  1. Juice the limes and orange.
  2. Shake the cranberry juice, lime juice, orange juice and syrup with ice, then strain into 4 short glasses of crushed ice. Top with soda and a lime wedge. Add vodka first for the grown-up version.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Costigan and Sullivan both graduate from the academy.
