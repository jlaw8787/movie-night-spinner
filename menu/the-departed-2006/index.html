Snacks

Clam chowder, cup

Clams, potato and bacon in cream. Oyster crackers.

Prep 11 min, cook 22 min
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 500ml clam juice or fish stock
  • 2 sprigs of thyme
  • 250g chopped clam meat
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 100g oyster crackers
  1. Dice the bacon, onion, celery and potatoes.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp, then sweat the onion and celery in the fat until soft, 7 min.
  3. Add the potatoes, clam juice and thyme and simmer until the potato is tender, 15 min.
  4. Stir in the clam meat and cream and warm through. Serve in cups with oyster crackers, 3 min.

Soda bread and butterVegetarian

Baked here each morning, cut thick, salted butter.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min, 10 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 250g wholemeal flour
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 450ml buttermilk
  • 100g salted butter
  1. Mix the flours, bicarb and salt, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough. Shape into a round and cut a deep cross on top.
  2. Bake at 220°C until golden and it sounds hollow when tapped underneath, 35 min.
  3. Rest the loaf, then slice thick and serve with the salted butter, 10 min.

Mains

Academy fish and chips

White fish in lager batter, chips, minted mushy peas.

Prep 10 min, cook 33 min
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour, for batter
  • 50g cornflour
  • 330ml ice-cold lager
  • 800g flathead fillets
  • 50g plain flour, for dusting
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp malt vinegar
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips, rinse and dry well. Whisk the batter flour, cornflour and cold lager together.
  2. Fry the chips in oil at 140°C until pale and soft, then drain, 15 min.
  3. Cook the peas, then crush with the butter and chopped mint and keep warm, 8 min.
  4. Dust the fish in flour, dip in batter and fry at 180°C in batches until golden and crisp, 6 min.
  5. Fry the chips again at 185°C until crisp. Salt well and serve with the fish, peas and malt vinegar, 4 min.

Sweets

Boston cream pieVegetarian

Sponge, vanilla custard, ganache. Nobody talks.

Prep 10 min, cook 33 min, 30 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 7 eggs
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 120g plain flour
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 25g cornflour
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk 4 of the eggs with half the sugar until thick and pale. Fold in the flour and divide between two lined 20cm tins.
  2. Bake at 180°C until springy, then turn out and cool completely, 18 min.
  3. Heat the milk. Whisk 3 yolks with the remaining sugar and cornflour, whisk in the hot milk, return to the pan and stir until thick, 10 min.
  4. Chill the custard until cold and set, 30 min.
  5. Sandwich the sponges with custard. Melt the chocolate with the cream and pour over the top, 5 min.

Costigan's cranberry

Lime, orange, soda, crushed ice. Cleans you out.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml cranberry juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 60ml orange juice
  • 20ml sugar syrup
  • 200ml soda water
  • 4 cups crushed ice
  • 4 lime wedges
  • 120ml vodka, optional (30ml per glass)
  1. Juice the limes and orange.
  2. Shake the cranberry juice, lime juice, orange juice and syrup with ice, then strain into 4 short glasses of crushed ice. Top with soda and a lime wedge. Add vodka first for the grown-up version.
