Snacks

Rafiki's fruitVegetarian

Mango, papaya, pineapple and passionfruit with lime, chilli salt and mint

Prep 17 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 2 passionfruit
  • 1 lime
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Cut the mangoes, papaya and pineapple into wedges.
  2. Lay on ice with the passionfruit pulp.
  3. Squeeze over lime and sprinkle with chilli salt and torn mint.

Hakuna Matata chakalakaVegetarian

Smoky South African relish of peppers, carrot, beans and chilli, with charred bread

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the chakalaka up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; it tastes better the next day. Char the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 long red chilli, chopped
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 400g tinned diced tomatoes
  • 400g tinned baked beans
  • 1 Turkish loaf
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Soften the onion in the oil with the garlic, ginger and chilli.
  2. Add the capsicums and carrot and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the curry powder and paprika, then the tomatoes and baked beans. Simmer 15 minutes until thick.
  4. Slice and char the bread on a hot grill pan. Serve with the chakalaka, warm or at room temperature, and scatter with coriander.

Mains

Pride Lands bobotie

South African spiced beef bake with curry, chutney and an egg custard topping

Prep 13 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 slices white bread
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 tbsp mango chutney
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 bay leaves
  1. Soak the bread in some of the milk and chop the onion.
  2. Fry the mince with the onion, curry powder and turmeric.
  3. Stir in the chutney and the squeezed bread, then press into a dish.
  4. Beat the eggs with the remaining milk and pour over. Add bay leaves and bake at 180C.

Timon's grub chocolate mousseVegetarian

Chocolate mousse, cookie dirt, gummy worms and mint. Slimy yet satisfying.

Prep 23 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 eggs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 100g chocolate biscuits
  • 1 bag gummy worms
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly, then whisk in the egg yolks.
  2. Whip the cream and the egg whites separately, then fold into the chocolate.
  3. Spoon into four cups and chill.
  4. Top with crushed chocolate biscuits, gummy worms and a mint leaf.

Zazu's morning report mandaziVegetarian

Cardamom coconut doughnut triangles, fried and dusted with icing sugar

Prep 19 min, cook 72 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cardamom
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 50g icing sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, cardamom and warm coconut milk into a soft dough.
  2. Rest the dough.
  3. Roll out and cut into triangles.
  4. Fry at 170C in the oil in batches until puffed and golden.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Pride Rock punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Mango, pineapple and passionfruit punch topped with lemonade and lime

Prep 7 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the mango nectar, pineapple juice and passionfruit up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice, lemonade and lime on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml mango nectar
  • 400ml pineapple juice
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 1 lime
  • crushed ice, to serve
  1. Mix the mango nectar, pineapple juice and passionfruit pulp.
  2. Pour over crushed ice in four glasses, top with lemonade and squeeze in some lime.
