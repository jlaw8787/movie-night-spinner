Rafiki's fruitVegetarian

Mango, papaya, pineapple and passionfruit with lime, chilli salt and mint.

Prep 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 2 passionfruit
  • 1 lime
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Cut the mangoes, papaya and pineapple into wedges.
  2. Lay on ice with the passionfruit pulp.
  3. Squeeze over lime and sprinkle with chilli salt and torn mint.

Hakuna Matata maharagweVegetarian

Kidney beans simmered in coconut milk with tomato and turmeric, with warm chapati.

Prep 6 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the beans up to 2 days ahead and keep them chilled. Warm the chapati on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 long red chilli, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 800g tinned red kidney beans, drained
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 4 chapatis
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Soften the onion in the oil with the garlic, ginger and chilli.
  2. Stir in the turmeric and cumin, add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add the beans and coconut milk and simmer until thick.
  4. Warm the chapati in a dry pan, scatter the beans with coriander and serve together.

Pride Rock mishkaki

Lime and chilli beef skewers, charred on the grill.

How it is made
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 800g rump steak
  • 8 skewers
  1. Swahili street skewers, and no lunch lost to Zazu. Mix the juice of 2 limes, 1 tbsp grated ginger, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp chilli flakes, 1 tsp cumin and 2 tbsp oil. Toss 800g diced rump through it and marinate 2 hours. Thread onto 8 skewers and grill over high heat 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until charred. Serve with lime wedges.

Pride Lands beef pilau

Swahili spiced rice cooked with beef, cumin, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves.

Prep 10 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat with a splash of water
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 600g rump steak, diced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 400g basmati rice
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 lime
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the onions and cook until golden.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger and spices for a minute.
  4. Add the rice and stir to coat, then the stock. Cover and simmer on low for 18 minutes.
  5. Rest off the heat for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork and serve with lime wedges.

Lioness hunt kuku choma

Grilled chilli chicken with kachumbari salad.

How it is made
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar ground turmeric
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 lime
  1. Kenyan grilled chicken, the way the lionesses would want it. Blitz 4 garlic cloves, 2 red chillies, the juice of a lemon, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp paprika and 60ml oil. Rub over a butterflied 1.8kg chicken and marinate 2 hours. Roast at 220°C for 45 minutes until the skin crackles. For the kachumbari, toss 3 diced tomatoes, a finely sliced red onion, coriander, lime juice and salt. Serve together.

Timon's grub chocolate mousseVegetarian

Chocolate mousse, cookie dirt, gummy worms and mint.

Prep 13 min, cook 8 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 eggs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 100g chocolate biscuits
  • 1 bag gummy worms
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly, then whisk in the egg yolks.
  2. Whip the cream and the egg whites separately, then fold into the chocolate.
  3. Spoon into four cups and chill.
  4. Top with crushed chocolate biscuits, gummy worms and a mint leaf.

Zazu's morning report mandaziVegetarian

Cardamom coconut doughnut triangles, fried and dusted with icing sugar.

Prep 19 min, cook 12 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cardamom
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 50g icing sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, cardamom and warm coconut milk into a soft dough.
  2. Rest the dough.
  3. Roll out and cut into triangles.
  4. Fry at 170°C in the oil in batches until puffed and golden.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Mango, pineapple and passionfruit punch topped with lemonade and lime.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Mix the mango nectar, pineapple juice and passionfruit up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice, lemonade and lime on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml mango nectar
  • 400ml pineapple juice
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 1 lime
  • crushed ice, to serve
  1. Mix the mango nectar, pineapple juice and passionfruit pulp.
  2. Pour over crushed ice in four glasses, top with lemonade and squeeze in some lime.
