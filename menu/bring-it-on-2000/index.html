Sparky's spirit fingers

Sparky Polastri's famous spirit fingers

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Crumb the tenders up to a day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken tenderloins
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Season the flour, beat the eggs, and mix the panko with the parmesan and paprika.
  2. Dip each tender in flour, egg, then crumbs, and lay on a lined tray.
  3. Drizzle with oil and bake at 220C, turning once, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Stir the mustard, honey and mayo together and serve with the tenders.

Car wash fish tacos

The Toros' car wash fundraiser

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the crema and shred the cabbage up to a day ahead; fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g firm white fish fillets, cut in strips
  • 100g plain flour
  • 150ml chilled soda water
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Stir the sour cream with the chipotle and a squeeze of lime.
  2. Whisk the flour, cumin, a pinch of salt and the soda water into a light batter.
  3. Dip the fish and fry in hot oil in batches until crisp and golden.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  5. Fill with cabbage and fish, spoon over the crema and finish with coriander and lime.

Rancho Carne carne asada plates

Home of the Rancho Carne Toros

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Marinate the steak up to a day ahead; make the pico on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 3 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g long-grain rice
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • 1 cup coriander, chopped
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  1. Marinate the steak in the juice of 2 limes and the orange, the garlic, cumin, oil and salt for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the rice, then stir through the warmed beans and a squeeze of lime.
  3. Mix the tomato, onion, coriander and remaining lime juice into a pico de gallo.
  4. Grill the steak over high heat, 3 minutes a side, rest it, then slice thinly across the grain.
  5. Serve with the rice and beans, pico and warm tortillas.

Daytona Cuban sandwiches

Daytona, where the Clovers win

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Slice the pork and build the rolls up to 4 hours ahead; press to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 300g roast pork, thinly sliced
  • 200g shaved leg ham
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1 cup dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 40g soft butter
  1. Split the rolls and spread both sides with mustard.
  2. Layer the pork, ham, cheese and pickles, then close.
  3. Butter the outsides and press in a hot pan under a heavy pan, turning once, until crisp and melted.
  4. Cut on the diagonal and serve hot.

Spirit Stick churrosVegetarian

The Spirit Stick never touches the ground

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate dip up to 2 days ahead and rewarm; pipe and fry the churros to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar, plus 100g for rolling
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Bring the water, butter, 1 tbsp sugar and a pinch of salt to the boil, tip in the flour and beat until it leaves the pan.
  2. Cool for 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy.
  3. Heat the cream, pour over the chocolate and stir until smooth.
  4. Pipe 12cm lengths into oil at 180C and fry in batches until golden.
  5. Roll in the remaining sugar mixed with the cinnamon and serve with the dip.

Cliff's minty lime coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Brushing teeth with Cliff, not a word said

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make the mint syrup up to 3 days ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 limes
  • 750ml soda water
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Heat the sugar and water until dissolved, add half the mint and cool.
  2. Muddle the remaining mint with lime juice in 4 glasses.
  3. Add the strained syrup and ice and top with soda.

When to serve

Bring out the churros when Torrance drops the Spirit Stick at cheer camp.
