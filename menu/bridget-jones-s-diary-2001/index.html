Snacks

Vicar's caramelised onion quichesVegetarian

Slow cooked onions with brown sugar and thyme in small shortcrust tarts.

Prep 10 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep the onions chilled and assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 3 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 150g goat's cheese
  1. Slice the onions and cook slowly in the oil with the sugar and thyme until soft and jammy.
  2. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a patty tin.
  3. Bake the cases at 190°C until pale golden.
  4. Fill with the onions and a piece of goat's cheese and bake until the cheese softens.

Capers on toast

Toasted baguette with cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and lemon zest.

Prep 4 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon
  1. Slice the baguette and toast until golden.
  2. Spread with cream cheese and top with folds of smoked salmon.
  3. Scatter with capers and a little lemon zest, then serve.

Una's turkey curry buffet crackers

Turkey in mild spiced mayo with mango chutney and sultanas, watch out for Uncle Geoffrey.

How it is made
  • 300g cooked turkey breast
  • 100g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  • 1 jar mild curry powder
  • 1 jar mango chutney
  • 1 lemon
  • 40g sultanas
  • 2 spring onions
  • 24 crackers
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 30g flaked almonds
  1. Leftover turkey or chicken tossed in a mild curry mayo with sultanas and spring onion, spooned onto crackers. Shred 300g cooked turkey breast or chicken. Mix 100g whole egg mayo with 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt, 2 tsp mild curry powder, 1 tbsp mango chutney and a squeeze of lemon. Fold in the turkey, 40g sultanas and 2 sliced spring onions and season. Spoon onto 24 crackers or crisp poppadom pieces just before serving and scatter with chopped coriander and toasted flaked almonds.

Bridget's not-blue leek soup

Leeks softened in butter, simmered with potato and chicken stock.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep the soup chilled and the toppings fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 leeks
  • 500g potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream, plus a little extra to swirl
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 60ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • salt and white pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the leeks, keeping a few green tops aside, and dice the potatoes.
  2. Soften the leeks and potatoes in the butter without browning.
  3. Add the stock and simmer until the potato is very soft.
  4. Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and season with salt and white pepper.
  5. Fry thin strips of the green leek tops in hot oil until crisp, and blitz the chives with the olive oil.
  6. Serve the soup with a swirl of cream, the chive oil, crispy leeks and buttered crusty bread.

Mark Darcy's gruyère and chive omelette

Fluffy eggs folded round gruyère and chives. Mark Darcy rescuing the birthday dinner.

How it is made
  • 12 eggs
  • 60g butter
  • 120g gruyère
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 120g rocket
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Three-egg omelettes cooked in butter, filled with gruyère and chives, folded onto warm plates with a green salad. Make one at a time. Beat 3 eggs with a pinch of salt. Melt 15g butter in a 20cm non-stick pan over medium-high heat until foaming. Pour in the eggs and stir quickly with a spatula for 20 seconds, then let it set for 30 seconds until just soft on top. Scatter 30g grated gruyère and chives over one half, fold and slide onto a warm plate. Repeat for 4. Serve with rocket dressed in lemon and oil.

Sweets

Orange marmalade puddingVegetarian

Orange marmalade under a soft butter sponge, cooked in a basin.

Prep 15 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat by steaming
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp orange marmalade
  • 125g butter, softened, plus extra to grease
  • 125g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 500ml custard
  1. Grease a pudding basin and spoon the marmalade into the base.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs, then fold in the flour. Spoon on top of the marmalade.
  3. Cover tightly with pleated baking paper and foil, then steam.
  4. Turn out and serve with hot custard.

Drinks

Bridget's chardonnay and peach sangria

Chardonnay, peach nectar and honey with sliced peaches and orange.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled without the soda
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe peaches
  • 1 orange
  • 750ml chardonnay, chilled
  • 200ml peach nectar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 330ml soda water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Slice the peaches and orange into a big jug.
  2. Add the chardonnay, peach nectar and honey and stir.
  3. Chill for at least 1 hour, which is the resting time.
  4. Fill 4 wine glasses with ice and fruit, pour in the sangria and top with a splash of soda water.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Bridget cooks her birthday dinner and the soup turns blue.
