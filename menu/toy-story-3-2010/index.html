Snacks

Tortilla head quesadillasVegetarian

Flour tortillas filled with cheddar and Monterey Jack, cut in wedges, fresh salsa

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa up to 1 day ahead; fill the tortillas up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge, and cook in batches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g Monterey Jack, grated
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  1. Dice the tomatoes with the onion and coriander for salsa.
  2. Fill half the tortillas with the cheddar and Monterey Jack, top with the rest and press.
  3. Cook in a dry pan until golden and melted, in batches.
  4. Cut into wedges.

Sunnyside ham and cheese pinwheels

Puff pastry spirals with tomato relish, ham and cheddar

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Rolls can be chilled up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 2 tbsp tomato relish
  • 8 slices leg ham
  • 120g cheddar, grated
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Lay the pastry flat and spread with tomato relish.
  2. Cover with ham and cheddar and roll up tightly from one edge.
  3. Chill the logs for 15 minutes.
  4. Cut each log into slices, lay flat on lined trays and brush with beaten egg.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes until puffed and golden.

Andy's spaghetti and meatballs

Beef and pork meatballs in tomato sauce over spaghetti, extra parmesan

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Cook the meatballs in the sauce up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and reheat gently; cook the spaghetti fresh on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 2 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Mix the mince with some of the parmesan and the parsley. Roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in oil.
  3. Simmer the meatballs in the tomatoes for 40 minutes.
  4. Cook the spaghetti, toss with the sauce and more parmesan, and save a plate for the toys.

College road trip chicken Caesar wrapsOn the printed menu

Chicken Caesar in soft wraps for Andy's drive to college

How it is made
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 4 rashers bacon
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  1. Grilled garlic chicken, crisp cos, bacon bits and parmesan tossed in a creamy Caesar dressing, rolled in soft tortillas and wrapped tight for the drive. Toss 600g chicken thigh fillets with oil, 1 crushed garlic clove and salt, and grill for 5 minutes a side, then rest and slice. Fry 4 rashers of bacon until crisp and chop. Whisk 4 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp Dijon, 1 small grated garlic clove and 30g grated parmesan. Toss 1 shredded cos lettuce with the dressing, bacon and chicken. Fill 4 large tortillas, roll up tight and wrap in baking paper, then halve.

Sweets

Strawberry bear shortcakesVegetarian

Warm biscuits split and filled with sugared strawberries and cream

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake the shortcakes up to 1 day ahead, airtight, and warm briefly; toss the berries and whip the cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 80g cold butter
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 400g strawberries, sliced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, stir in the milk and pat out.
  2. Cut into rounds and bake at 200°C for 15 minutes.
  3. Toss the strawberries with the sugar and stand for 15 minutes.
  4. Split the shortcakes and fill with whipped cream and the berries.

Bonnie's tea party jam and cream sconesOn the printed menu

Little buttermilk rounds split warm, strawberry conserve and cream, served to Mr Pricklepants

How it is made
  • 60g butter
  • 350g self-raising flour
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 50ml milk
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Little buttermilk scones baked tall and golden, split warm and piled with strawberry jam and softly whipped cream for a pretend tea party. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub 60g cold butter into 350g self-raising flour, 2 tbsp caster sugar and a pinch of salt. Stir in 250ml buttermilk with a knife until it just comes together. Pat to 3cm thick, cut 12 small rounds with a floured cutter and pack close together on a lined tray. Brush with milk and bake for 12 minutes until risen and golden. Serve warm with strawberry jam and 200ml cream whipped soft.

Sunnyside strawberry malt shakesVegetarianNo alcohol

Strawberries, vanilla ice cream, milk and malt powder blended thick and pink

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; hull the strawberries and chill the glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g ripe strawberries, 2 kept for garnish
  • 6 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  1. Hull the strawberries and keep two for garnish.
  2. Blend the rest with the ice cream, milk, malt and honey until thick and pink.
  3. Pour into glasses and top with whipped cream and half a strawberry each.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Andy packs up for college and the toys go in the attic bag.
