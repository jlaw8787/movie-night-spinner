Venice cod, prosciutto and sardine toasts

Baguette toasts with smoked cod, prosciutto and fig, or sardines and raisins.

Prep 28 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Toppings up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g smoked cod
  • 250ml milk, for poaching
  • 1 potato, boiled
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 60g prosciutto
  • 2 figs, sliced
  • 1 tin sardines
  • 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
  • 30g raisins
  1. Boil the potato until soft. Poach the cod in milk for 8 minutes, then flake.
  2. Beat the flaked cod with the potato and olive oil into a rough spread.
  3. Toast the baguette slices, 7 min.
  4. Top a third with the cod spread and parsley, a third with prosciutto and fig, and the rest with sardines, onion and raisins, 23 min.

Austrian castle pretzelsVegetarian

Homemade soft pretzels with a camembert paprika butter dip.

Prep 38 min, cook 12 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp rock salt
  • 200g ripe camembert
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped onion
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and water into a dough and knead until smooth.
  2. Prove until doubled in size, 30 min.
  3. Shape into pretzels and dip briefly in bicarb water. Sprinkle with salt.
  4. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes.
  5. Mash the camembert with the butter, paprika and onion for obatzda.

Circus train peanut caramel popcorn balls

Nuts and kernels in golden syrup toffee, fist-sized, for the chase on the train roof.

How it is made
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 150g roasted salted peanuts
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  • 60g butter
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Popcorn and salted roasted peanuts bound in a golden syrup caramel, pressed into fist-sized balls and wrapped in paper like a circus stall treat. Pop 100g popcorn kernels in 2 tbsp oil in a covered pot and tip into a large bowl with 150g roasted salted peanuts. Boil 200g caster sugar, 80g golden syrup, 60g butter and 3 tbsp water to 125°C, then stir in half a teaspoon of bicarb and 1 tsp salt. Pour over the popcorn and toss with an oiled spoon. Shape into 12 balls with buttered hands, cool for 20 minutes and wrap each in baking paper.

Zeppelin dining room schnitzel

Crisp pork schnitzel with warm potato salad.

Prep 4 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Boil and dress the potato salad up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; crumb the schnitzels up to 4 hours ahead and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork schnitzels
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 800g potatoes, boiled
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, slice and toss while warm with vinegar, oil and onion, 20 min.
  2. Pound the schnitzels thin. Crumb in flour, egg and panko.
  3. Fry in butter and oil until golden and puffy, 10 min.
  4. Serve with the potato salad, sliced cucumber and lemon.

Hatay lamb kebabs with garlic yoghurt

Spiced ground lamb with sumac and parsley pressed onto skewers, grilled hot, with flatbread.

How it is made
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar sumac
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 8 flatbreads
  1. Lamb mince with onion, garlic, sumac, chilli and parsley pressed onto skewers and grilled hot, with warm flatbread, charred tomatoes and garlic yoghurt. Grate 1 onion and squeeze out the juice. Knead 800g lamb mince with the onion, 2 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp sumac, 1 tsp chilli flakes, a handful of chopped parsley and 1.5 tsp salt for 2 minutes. Press around 8 flat skewers and chill for 30 minutes. Grill on a very hot barbecue for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, with 4 halved tomatoes. Stir 1 grated garlic clove and a pinch of salt into 250g Greek yoghurt. Serve on warm flatbread with the tomatoes and yoghurt.

Petra rose and pistachio cakeVegetarian

Almond and pistachio cake soaked in lemon rosewater syrup.

Prep 13 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 200g almond meal
  • 130g pistachios, 100g ground and the rest crushed
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tsp rosewater
  • 1 tbsp edible rose petals
  1. Whisk the eggs with most of the sugar, then fold in the almond meal and ground pistachios.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 170°C for 40 minutes.
  3. Simmer the remaining sugar with the lemon juice and a splash of water, add the rosewater and pour over the cake, 10 min.
  4. Top with crushed pistachios and rose petals.

Chosen wisely pomegranate red wine cup

Red wine, pomegranate juice, orange and brandy over ice, juice version for drivers.

Prep 8 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml pomegranate juice
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 whole cloves
  • 750ml tempranillo or shiraz
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 60ml brandy
  • 1 pomegranate, seeds only
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Warm half the pomegranate juice with the sugar, cinnamon and cloves until the sugar dissolves, then cool, 10 min.
  2. Pour into a jug with the tempranillo or shiraz, the rest of the juice, orange slices and brandy, 5 min.
  3. Chill for at least 2 hours.
  4. Pour over ice into cups and scatter pomegranate seeds on top.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Indy has to choose the right cup.
