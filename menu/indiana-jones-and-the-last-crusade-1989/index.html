Venice cod, prosciutto and sardine toasts

Baguette toasts with smoked cod, prosciutto and fig, or sardines and raisins

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Toppings up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g smoked cod
  • 250ml milk, for poaching
  • 1 potato, boiled
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 60g prosciutto
  • 2 figs, sliced
  • 1 tin sardines
  • 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
  • 30g raisins
  1. Boil the potato until soft. Poach the cod in milk for 8 minutes, then flake.
  2. Beat the flaked cod with the potato and olive oil into a rough spread.
  3. Toast the baguette slices.
  4. Top a third with the cod spread and parsley, a third with prosciutto and fig, and the rest with sardines, onion and raisins.

Austrian castle pretzelsVegetarian

Homemade soft pretzels with a camembert paprika butter dip

Prep 40 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp rock salt
  • 200g ripe camembert
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped onion
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and water into a dough and knead until smooth.
  2. Prove until doubled in size.
  3. Shape into pretzels and dip briefly in bicarb water. Sprinkle with salt.
  4. Bake at 220C for 12 minutes.
  5. Mash the camembert with the butter, paprika and onion for obatzda.

Zeppelin dining room schnitzel

Crisp pork schnitzel with warm potato salad

Prep 35 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Boil and dress the potato salad up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; crumb the schnitzels up to 4 hours ahead and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork schnitzels
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 800g potatoes, boiled
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, slice and toss while warm with vinegar, oil and onion.
  2. Pound the schnitzels thin. Crumb in flour, egg and panko.
  3. Fry in butter and oil until golden and puffy.
  4. Serve with the potato salad, sliced cucumber and lemon.

Petra rose and pistachio cakeVegetarian

Almond and pistachio cake soaked in lemon rosewater syrup

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 200g almond meal
  • 130g pistachios, 100g ground and the rest crushed
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tsp rosewater
  • 1 tbsp edible rose petals
  1. Whisk the eggs with most of the sugar, then fold in the almond meal and ground pistachios.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 170C for 40 minutes.
  3. Simmer the remaining sugar with the lemon juice and a splash of water, add the rosewater and pour over the cake.
  4. Top with crushed pistachios and rose petals.

Drinks

Chosen wisely pomegranate red wine cupVegetarian

Red wine, pomegranate juice, orange and brandy over ice, juice version for drivers

Prep 15 min, cook 135 min · Make ahead: Chill up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml pomegranate juice
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 whole cloves
  • 750ml tempranillo or shiraz
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 60ml brandy
  • 1 pomegranate, seeds only
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Warm half the pomegranate juice with the sugar, cinnamon and cloves until the sugar dissolves, then cool.
  2. Pour into a jug with the tempranillo or shiraz, the rest of the juice, orange slices and brandy.
  3. Chill for at least 2 hours.
  4. Pour over ice into cups and scatter pomegranate seeds on top.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Indy has to choose the right cup.
