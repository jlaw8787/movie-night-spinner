Chicago Italian beef sliders

Garlic oregano beef on rolls, Chicago crash style.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Simmer the jus up to 2 days ahead, chilled; reheat, warm the beef and build the sliders to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 400g thinly sliced roast beef
  • 8 slider buns
  • 150g mild pickled vegetables, chopped
  1. Simmer the stock with the garlic, oregano, chilli and Worcestershire, 10 min.
  2. Drop in the roast beef briefly to warm through.
  3. Split the buns and dip the cut sides quickly in the jus.
  4. Pile in the beef and top with pickled vegetables. Serve with little cups of jus for dunking.

Christmas Eve spinach and artichoke dipVegetarian

Hot spinach and artichoke dip, baked bubbling.

Prep 4 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Mix up to a day ahead in the dish; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen spinach, thawed
  • 1 jar marinated artichoke hearts (280g)
  • 250g cream cheese, soft
  • 150g sour cream
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 baguette
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Squeeze the spinach dry and chop the drained artichokes, 6 min.
  2. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, garlic, parmesan, spinach and artichokes. Season.
  3. Spread in a small baking dish and top with mozzarella.
  4. Bake 20 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  5. Slice and toast the baguette and serve with the hot dip, 6 min.

Pierce family bacon-wrapped meatloaf

Beef meatloaf wrapped in bacon and baked until glazed.

Prep 20 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Shape and wrap the meatloaf up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake, mash and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 180ml full cream milk
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 8 rashers of streaky bacon
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in some of the milk, then mix with the mince, onion, egg, Worcestershire and salt, 8 min.
  2. Shape into a loaf on a lined tray and wrap with the bacon.
  3. Bake at 180°C, then brush with the tomato sauce mixed with the sugar and bake again, 1 hr.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and the rest of the milk, 20 min.
  5. Make gravy from the tray juices with the flour and stock.

Christmas Eve garlic mash and green beansVegetarian

Butter mash and beans with crisp shallots, for under the meatloaf.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the mash up to 1 day ahead and reheat with a splash of milk; cook the beans on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 80g butter
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 400g green beans, trimmed
  • 3 tbsp fried shallots
  1. Peel and chop the potatoes and trim the beans.
  2. Boil the potatoes with the garlic cloves in salted water until soft.
  3. Drain and mash with the butter and hot milk, garlic and all, until smooth. Season well.
  4. Boil the beans until just tender, drain and pile beside the mash with the fried shallots on top.

Jailhouse salted caramel choc chip cookiesVegetarian

Chewy chocolate chip cookies hiding a soft salted caramel centre.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Shape and fill the dough balls up to 2 days ahead and keep them chilled; bake from the fridge, adding 2 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 220g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 120g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 100g soft caramels
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Beat the butter, both sugars, egg and vanilla. Mix in the flour, bicarb and chocolate.
  2. Roll into balls, press a caramel into the centre of each and close the dough around it.
  3. Chill, 20 min.
  4. Bake at 180°C, sprinkle with sea salt and cool on the tray, 15 min.

Iced reindeer cookiesVegetarian

Vanilla sugar cookies cut into reindeer and iced white.

Prep 16 min, cook 39 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 350g icing sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 bottle red food colouring
  • 1 jar gold sprinkles
  1. Beat the butter with some of the icing sugar, add the egg yolk and vanilla, then mix in the flour.
  2. Chill the dough, 30 min.
  3. Roll out and cut reindeer and stars.
  4. Bake at 170°C, then cool, 20 min.
  5. Mix the remaining icing sugar with water. Ice the cookies white, tint a little red for noses and finish with gold sprinkles.

Sleigh ride salted caramel hot chocolateNo alcohol

Hot chocolate stirred with caramel and a pinch of salt.

Prep 0 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh; chop the chocolate up to 2 days ahead and whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 120g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 6 tbsp salted caramel sauce
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 8 marshmallows
  1. Heat the milk until steaming, then whisk in the chocolate and most of the caramel sauce until smooth.
  2. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and a drizzle of caramel.
  3. Add marshmallows and brown quickly with a kitchen torch or under the grill.
