Berlin handover currywurst

Sliced bratwurst in a warm curried tomato and paprika sauce with soft rolls.

Prep 3 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the curry sauce up to 3 days ahead and reheat; fry the sausages on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp curry powder, plus extra to dust
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 400ml tomato passata
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 6 bratwurst sausages
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 soft white rolls
  1. Cook the onion in the butter until soft.
  2. Stir in the curry powder and paprika, then add the passata, tomato sauce, sugar, vinegar and salt and simmer until thick.
  3. Meanwhile fry the sausages in the oil, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Slice thick, pile in a dish, spoon over the sauce, dust with curry powder and serve with the rolls.

Grand Palais gala gougèresVegetarian

Warm gruyère choux puffs with mustard and nutmeg.

Prep 25 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Pipe the unbaked puffs onto trays and freeze them up to 1 month, then bake from frozen with 5 extra minutes; baked puffs re-crisp in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 125ml milk
  • 100g butter, cubed
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 120g gruyère, grated, for the dough
  • 30g gruyère, grated, for the tops
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line two trays.
  2. Bring the water, milk, butter and salt to the boil.
  3. Tip in the flour all at once and beat over the heat until the dough pulls away from the pan.
  4. Cool the dough in a bowl.
  5. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the mustard, nutmeg, pepper and gruyère for the dough.
  6. Pipe or spoon walnut-sized mounds onto the trays and top with the rest of the gruyère.
  7. Bake until puffed, deep golden and crisp.
  8. Serve warm.

Three cores rogan josh

Slow-cooked lamb in a deep red Kashmiri chilli.

Prep 10 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Make the curry up to 2 days ahead; it is better reheated. Cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg boneless lamb shoulder, in 4cm pieces
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp ghee
  • 6 cardamom pods
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 onion, finely sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 2 tsp ground fennel
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 200g Greek yoghurt, whisked
  • 500ml water
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Season the lamb with the salt. Heat the ghee, fry the cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and bay leaves, then brown the lamb in batches.
  2. Add the onion and fry until golden.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, fennel, chilli powder and coriander with a splash of water.
  4. Stir in the yoghurt a spoonful at a time so it doesn't split.
  5. Add the water, cover and simmer gently until the lamb is very tender.
  6. Cook the rice.
  7. Stir the garam masala into the curry, scatter with coriander and serve with the rice.

Kashmir camp saffron phirniVegetarian

Saffron rice pudding with pistachio, cooling at the Kashmir camp.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge; add the pistachios and petals just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g basmati rice
  • 1 L full-cream milk
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp rosewater
  • 30g pistachios, chopped
  • 1 tbsp dried rose petals
  1. Soak the rice in cold water.
  2. Drain and blitz to a coarse grit.
  3. Warm the saffron in 2 tbsp of the milk.
  4. Bring the rest of the milk to a simmer, whisk in the rice and stir on low until thick like custard.
  5. Stir in the sugar, cardamom, saffron milk and rosewater.
  6. Pour into four small bowls and chill until set.
  7. Top with pistachios and rose petals.

White Widow's French 75

Gin, lemon and sugar syrup shaken cold and topped with sparkling wine, with a lemon twist.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead and chill the flutes and wine; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 40ml hot water
  • 120ml gin
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 2 cups ice
  • 500ml sparkling wine, chilled
  • 1 lemon, peeled into long twists
  1. Stir the sugar into the hot water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and syrup hard with ice.
  3. Strain into four chilled flutes and top with sparkling wine.
  4. Twist a strip of lemon peel over each glass and drop it in.
