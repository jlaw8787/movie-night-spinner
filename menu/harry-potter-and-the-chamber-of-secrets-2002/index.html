Snacks

Weasleys' full English breakfast

Sausages, bacon, tomatoes and fried eggs with buttered toast

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 tomatoes, halved
  • 8 eggs
  • 8 thick slices white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Fry the sausages until browned.
  2. Push the sausages aside, add the bacon and tomatoes and cook.
  3. Crack in the eggs and fry until the whites set.
  4. Toast and butter the bread.
  5. Load up the plates and pass them down the table.

Mains

Burrow shepherd's pie

Lamb and carrot pie in tomato Worcestershire gravy under buttery mash

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Brown the lamb mince in a large pan.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, tomato paste and Worcestershire sauce and simmer.
  3. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with the butter and milk.
  4. Spread the mince in a dish, top with mash and bake at 200C until golden.

Sweets

Lockhart's treacle toffeeVegetarian

Hard buttery toffee made with brown sugar, treacle and golden syrup

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 weeks, airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 2 tbsp black treacle
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  1. Line a small tray with baking paper.
  2. Boil the sugar, butter, treacle and golden syrup to 140C, stirring gently.
  3. Pour into the tray and score into squares as it cools.
  4. Leave to set fully, then break apart.

Bubbling Polyjuice jelly

Fresh green apple juice jelly set in small glasses

Prep 20 min, cook 240 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green apples, juiced
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 4 gelatine leaves
  • 2 tbsp popping candy
  1. Juice the apples with a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Warm the juice with the sugar.
  3. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water, squeeze, and stir into the warm juice until dissolved.
  4. Pour into small glasses and chill until set.
  5. Sprinkle with popping candy just before serving.

Drinks

Butterscotch cream sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Homemade butterscotch sauce topped with cold cream soda

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Butterscotch up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 50g butter
  • 50ml thickened cream, for the butterscotch
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1l cold cream soda
  • 100ml thickened cream, lightly whipped, for the topping
  1. Cook the sugar and butter until bubbling.
  2. Stir in the cream for the butterscotch and the salt.
  3. Spoon a little into each glass, pour in the cream soda and stir.
  4. Top with the lightly whipped cream and serve in tall mugs.
