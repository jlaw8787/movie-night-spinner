Osborn mansion egg bitesVegetarian

Baked egg bites with cheddar and chives, made in a muffin tin.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and chill; reheat at 160°C for 8 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 100ml milk
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 20g butter, for greasing
  1. Whisk the eggs with the milk, cheddar, chives and salt.
  2. Butter a 12-hole muffin tin and pour the mix in evenly.
  3. Bake at 180°C until puffed and just set.
  4. Ease out with a knife and serve warm.

Jazz club fig and goat's cheese bites

Figs with goat's cheese, honey and walnuts, for Peter's black suit dance at the jazz club.

How it is made
  • 8 fresh figs
  • 150g soft goat's cheese
  • 16 walnut halves
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Fresh figs halved and topped with soft goat's cheese, honey, walnuts and thyme, flashed under the grill and laid out on black trays. Halve 8 ripe figs and lay cut side up on a tray. Top each with a teaspoon of soft goat's cheese from 150g and a walnut half. Drizzle with 2 tbsp honey and grill on high for 3 minutes until the cheese is warm and just colouring. Scatter with thyme leaves and cracked pepper and serve on a black tray with a few prosciutto ribbons alongside.

French restaurant coq au vin

Chicken thighs braised in red wine with bacon and mushrooms.

Prep 18 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 500ml red wine
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  1. Brown the chicken and bacon.
  2. Add the mushrooms and cook until golden.
  3. Pour in the wine and enough water to cover.
  4. Simmer until rich and glossy.
  5. Boil and mash the potatoes with plenty of butter and serve under the chicken.

Aunt May's lemon thyme roast chicken

Whole bird under butter and zest, roasted golden, crisp potatoes, gravy, and advice on the side.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Whole chicken rubbed with butter, lemon and thyme and roasted golden, with crispy roast potatoes and a rich gravy from the pan juices. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with 40g butter, the zest of 1 lemon, thyme and salt, put the lemon inside and roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potato chunks for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in oil for 50 minutes. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the pan juices, add 300ml chicken stock and simmer, scraping the pan, until thick.

Sandman sand cookiesVegetarian

Nutty brown butter slice and bake cookies rolled in raw sugar.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 3 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 280g plain flour
  • 4 tbsp raw sugar
  1. Brown the butter until nutty and cool until firm.
  2. Beat with the brown sugar, then mix in the flour.
  3. Roll into logs, coat in raw sugar and chill.
  4. Slice into rounds and bake at 170°C until golden and sandy.

Proposal crème brûléeVegetarian

Vanilla bean custard baked in ramekins with a crisp sugar top.

Prep 13 min, cook 45 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, torch just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar for the tops
  1. Heat the cream with the vanilla bean until steaming.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar, pour in the cream and strain into ramekins.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150°C until just set.
  4. Chill until cold.
  5. Sprinkle with sugar and torch until amber and crackly.

Broadway opening night raspberry fizz

Fresh raspberry puree in a flute, topped up slowly with chilled bubbles.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Puree up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries, for the puree
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 750ml prosecco
  • 8 whole raspberries, to finish
  1. Blend the puree raspberries with the sugar and lemon juice, then push through a sieve.
  2. Spoon a little puree into each chilled flute.
  3. Slowly top with chilled prosecco and drop in two raspberries.
