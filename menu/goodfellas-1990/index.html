Snacks

Razor-thin garlic bruschettaVegetarian

Paulie's razor blade garlic, sliced so fine it melts in the oil

Prep 7 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Slice the garlic and cook it in the oil up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; grill the ciabatta and top on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 80ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 8 slices ciabatta
  • 2 tomatoes, halved
  • 1 handful basil
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the garlic paper thin on a mandoline.
  2. Cook the garlic in the olive oil over low heat until soft and barely golden.
  3. Grill the ciabatta, rub with the cut side of the tomatoes and season with salt.
  4. Spoon over the garlic and its oil and finish with torn basil.

Crew's back booth salami and provolone

Thick-cut Genoa and sharp cheese, pepperoncini and torn bread, as served at the Bamboo Lounge

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Cut the provolone and arrange the board up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; tear the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150g sliced Genoa salami
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 200g sharp provolone
  • 150g pepperoncini
  • 150g marinated mushrooms
  • 1 loaf seeded Italian bread
  1. Cut the provolone into thick batons.
  2. Arrange the salami, prosciutto and provolone on a board.
  3. Put the pepperoncini and marinated mushrooms in small bowls beside the board.
  4. Tear the Italian bread by hand and set it next to the board.

Mains

Prison gravy with three-meat meatballs

Veal, beef and pork, and fewer onions than Vinnie uses

Prep 12 min, cook 103 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 slices bread
  • 80ml milk
  • 200g veal mince
  • 200g beef mince
  • 200g pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g pecorino, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 800g San Marzano tomatoes, tinned
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Soak the bread in the milk, then mix with the minces, egg, pecorino, parsley and 1 crushed garlic clove.
  2. Roll into 16 balls and brown in olive oil.
  3. In the same pot gently cook the remaining garlic sliced razor thin and the onion, add the tomatoes and the meatballs, and simmer.
  4. Cook the spaghetti and serve with the sauce and meatballs.

Johnny Dio's pan steaks

Prep 37 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Season the steaks and leave them out of the fridge for 30 minutes before cooking; best cooked and rested just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rump steaks, about 250g each
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed
  1. Take the rump steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes early and season well with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a heavy pan until smoking.
  3. Cook the steaks two at a time, 2 to 3 minutes each side for rare or 4 minutes for medium.
  4. In the last minute add the butter and smashed garlic and spoon them over the steaks.
  5. Rest the steaks for 5 minutes before serving.

Prep 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Open the bottles an hour before dinner
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bottle Chianti red wine
  • 1 bottle Montepulciano red wine
  1. Open the bottles of Chianti and Montepulciano red wine an hour before dinner and leave them to breathe.
  2. Pour the wine into short tumblers at the table.
