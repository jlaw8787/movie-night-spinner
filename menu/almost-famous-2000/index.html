Elaine's soy cutletsVegetarian

Breaded tofu cutlets with lemon and dill mayo.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Press and crumb the tofu up to a day ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 450g firm tofu, pressed and sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 125ml olive oil
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  1. Brush the tofu slices with the soy sauce.
  2. Coat in flour, egg, then panko.
  3. Shallow-fry in the oil, turning, until crisp and golden, 12 min.
  4. Stir the dill and a squeeze of lemon into the mayo and serve with lemon wedges.

Everything Elaine banned club sandwich

Bacon, egg and buttered white toast, stacked high.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the bacon up to 4 hours ahead and keep at room temperature; fry the eggs and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 4 eggs
  • 12 slices of white bread
  • 40g soft butter
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 8 slices of tasty cheese
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp, then fry the eggs in the fat until just set, 12 min.
  2. Toast and butter the bread, 5 min.
  3. Layer toast, mayo, lettuce, tomato and bacon, then toast, cheese and egg, then a final slice, 8 min.
  4. Pin with toothpicks and cut into triangles.

Golden god cheeseburgers

Double beef, American cheese and pickles, with fries.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to a day ahead and chill; cook the chips and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 white onion, finely sliced
  • 1/2 cup dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 750g frozen shoestring chips
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp, 30 min.
  2. Shape the mince into 8 thin patties and season well.
  3. Sear the patties on a very hot grill, 2 minutes a side, laying cheese on each after the flip.
  4. Toast the buns and stack 2 patties in each with onion, pickles, sauce and mustard, 4 min.
  5. Serve with the chips.

Riot House room service steak Diane

Seared sirloin, mushroom and brandy sauce, fries.

Prep 2 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms up to a day ahead; cook the chips and steaks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks, about 220g each
  • 30g butter
  • 200g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 60ml brandy
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 750g frozen thick-cut chips
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until golden, 30 min.
  2. Sear the seasoned steaks in half the butter, 3 minutes a side, then rest.
  3. Fry the mushrooms and garlic in the rest of the butter, then add the brandy and let it bubble off, 6 min.
  4. Stir in the Worcestershire, mustard and cream and simmer until glossy, 4 min.
  5. Spoon the sauce over the steaks, scatter with parsley and serve with chips.

Penny Lane's Morocco orange cakeVegetarian

Orange and almond cake, honey yoghurt, pistachios.

Prep 7 min, cook 2 hr 13 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead; it gets better in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 2 oranges
  • 6 eggs
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 250g almond meal
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 40g pistachios, chopped
  1. Simmer the whole oranges in water until very soft, then cool and blitz to a puree, skins and all, 1 hr 15 min.
  2. Beat the eggs and sugar until thick, then fold in the almond meal, baking powder and orange puree.
  3. Pour into a lined 22cm tin and bake at 170°C until set, 50 min.
  4. Stir the honey through the yoghurt and serve with the cake and pistachios.

Ice-cold lager in a bucket with lime wedges.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the beer at least 4 hours ahead; ice the bucket to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 x 330ml bottles of lager
  • 2 limes, cut in wedges
  • 2kg bag of ice
  1. Fill a bucket with ice and bury the bottles in it.
  2. Serve with lime wedges on the side.
