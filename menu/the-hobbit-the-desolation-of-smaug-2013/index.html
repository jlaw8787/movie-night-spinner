Snacks

Ricotta honey toast fingersVegetarian

Toasted sourdough fingers with whipped honey ricotta

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Beat the ricotta with the honey up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; toast and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g ricotta
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 6 thick slices sourdough bread
  • 100g walnuts, chopped
  1. Beat the ricotta with some of the honey until light and smooth.
  2. Toast the sourdough until golden and cut into fingers.
  3. Spread with the ricotta, drizzle with more honey and scatter with walnuts.

Mirkwood mushroom toastsVegetarian

Garlic and thyme mushrooms fried in butter, on toasted crusty bread

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Fry the mushrooms up to 1 day ahead and reheat in a hot pan; toast the bread and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 8 slices crusty bread
  • 50g parmesan, shaved
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the mushrooms.
  2. Fry in the butter over high heat until golden.
  3. Add the garlic and thyme and cook another minute.
  4. Toast the bread.
  5. Spoon the mushrooms on top and finish with parmesan and pepper.

Elvenking's baked brie with red wine figsOn the printed menu

Whole wheel baked oozing under fruit stewed in claret and honey, walnuts, from the elves' cellar

How it is made
  • 200g brie
  • 6 dried figs
  • 125ml red wine
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 50g walnuts
  • 1 bunch red grapes
  • 200g crackers
  1. A whole brie baked until oozing, topped with figs stewed in red wine and honey, with walnuts, grapes and crackers for scooping. Simmer 6 halved dried figs in 125ml red wine with 1 tbsp honey and a thyme sprig for 10 minutes until sticky. Score the top of a 200g brie, sit it in a small dish and bake at 180C for 15 minutes until soft in the middle. Spoon the figs and syrup over the top and scatter with toasted walnuts. Serve straight away with a bunch of red grapes and 200g crackers.

Mains

Erebor gold roast

Pork belly with a fennel salt rub, brushed with honey, slow roasted

Prep 25 min, cook 3 hours, then 10 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the pork the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork belly
  • 2 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tbsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 green apples, halved
  1. Score the pork skin and rub with salt and fennel seeds.
  2. Leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  3. Roast at 160C, brushing the meat side with honey.
  4. Add the apple halves and roast for the last part of the cooking.
  5. Blast at 240C until the crackling is golden.
  6. Rest before carving.

Lake-town whole roast snapperOn the printed menu

Bard smuggling the dwarves in barrels of fish

How it is made
  • 2 whole snapper
  • 3 lemons
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 bunches parsley
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 bunches green beans
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar capers
  1. Whole snapper roasted with lemon, garlic and parsley at 200C for 25 minutes, served with roast potatoes and greens. Score 2 whole snapper of about 800g each on both sides. Stuff with lemon slices, garlic and parsley stalks and rub with olive oil and salt. Parboil 800g halved potatoes for 8 minutes and roast at 200C in oil for 20 minutes first. Lay the fish on top of the potatoes and roast 25 minutes until the flesh flakes at the bone. Toss 2 bunches green beans in butter and lemon. Serve with salsa verde of parsley, capers, lemon and oil.

Smaug's golden honey cakeVegetarian

Butter honey cake soaked in hot syrup

Prep 26 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter, softened
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 150g honey, for the cake
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100g honey, for the syrup
  • 50ml water
  • 2 tbsp gold sprinkles
  1. Beat the butter, brown sugar and the honey for the cake until pale.
  2. Beat in the eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour.
  4. Pour into a lined tin.
  5. Bake at 175C.
  6. Warm the honey for the syrup with the water.
  7. Pour the syrup over the warm cake.
  8. Cool the cake.
  9. Finish with gold sprinkles.

Beorn's honey bourbon milk punchVegetarian

Cold milk shaken with bourbon and honey syrup

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; shake with the milk and bourbon to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 600ml cold full cream milk
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg, freshly grated
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Warm the honey with the water until runny.
  2. Cool the honey syrup.
  3. Shake the first batch with ice: half the milk, bourbon, honey and vanilla, then strain into two cups.
  4. Repeat for the second batch.
  5. Grate fresh nutmeg over the top. For a non-drinking version, leave out the bourbon and add a pinch of cinnamon.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the dwarves are smuggled into Lake-town in Bard's fish barrels.
