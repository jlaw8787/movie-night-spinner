Snacks

Julieta's cheese stuffed arepasVegetarian

Corn arepas filled with melted mozzarella, griddled golden

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Shape and fill the arepas up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; cook on the griddle just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g arepa flour
  • 350ml warm water
  • 30g butter
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  1. Mix the arepa flour with the warm water, salt and butter into a soft dough.
  2. Shape into discs, fill with mozzarella and seal.
  3. Cook on a griddle for 5 minutes a side until golden and crisp.

Pandebono cheese breadVegetarian

Colombian cheese bread rolls with tapioca and feta, baked puffed and golden

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 150g tapioca starch
  • 50g arepa flour
  • 250g feta, crumbled
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Mix the starch, arepa flour, feta, egg and sugar into a soft dough.
  2. Roll into balls.
  3. Bake at 200C for 15 minutes until puffed and golden. Eat warm.

Mains

Shredded beef, rice, beans and plantains

Cumin braised shredded beef with rice, black beans and fried green plantain

Prep 10 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Beef braise up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 tin black beans
  • 2 green plantains, sliced
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 tbsp oil, for frying
  1. Braise the beef with the tomatoes, onions and cumin for 3 hours, then shred.
  2. Cook the rice and warm the black beans.
  3. Fry the plantain slices in the oil until golden.
  4. Fry the eggs.
  5. Serve it all on one plate.

Sweets

Colombian cheese buñuelosVegetarian

Fried cheese dough balls, crisp outside and soft inside

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 250g feta, crumbled
  • 60g cornflour
  • 60g tapioca starch
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the feta, cornflour, starch, egg and sugar into a soft dough.
  2. Roll into balls.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 160C for 6 minutes until golden and round, in batches.
  4. Drain and serve warm.

Colombian hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot dark chocolate with a cube of mozzarella melting in each mug

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate milk up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat, pour and add the mozzarella cubes when serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 4 cubes mozzarella
  1. Heat the milk and whisk in the chocolate and cinnamon until frothy.
  2. Pour into four mugs and drop a cube of mozzarella into each so it goes soft and stringy.
  3. Fish it out with a spoon.
