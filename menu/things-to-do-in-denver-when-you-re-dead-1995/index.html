Snacks

Devilled EggsVegetarian

Boiled whites piped with yolk whipped with mayo, Dijon and pickle juice, paprika and chives.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp pickle juice
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the eggs hard in a pot of simmering water.
  2. Cool the eggs in iced water, then peel and halve them.
  3. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, Dijon, pickle juice, salt and pepper until smooth.
  4. Pipe or spoon the yolk mix back into the whites. Finish with smoked paprika and chopped chives.

Green Chile QuesoVegetarian

Colorado green chile, with chips for dunking.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best served straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 250g white American cheese
  • 125ml milk
  • 120g roasted green chiles, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 200g corn chips
  1. Crush the garlic and drain the green chiles.
  2. Melt the cheese with the milk over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  3. Stir in the chiles, garlic and cumin and simmer, then pour into a warm bowl. Serve straight away with the corn chips.

Mains

Patty Melt

Thin beef patty on rye, Swiss and slow onions, griddled crisp, for planning the job.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Onions can be cooked 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 60g butter
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cook the onions in a little of the butter over low heat until soft and golden.
  2. Shape the mince into 4 thin oval patties, season, and sear in a hot pan until browned on both sides.
  3. Butter the bread on the outside. Build each sandwich with a patty, onions and two slices of Swiss cheese.
  4. Griddle the sandwiches in a pan over medium heat, turning once, until crisp and the cheese has melted.

SopapillasVegetarian

Puffed pastry pillows split and drizzled with honey and cinnamon, for Jimmy's date with Dagney.

Prep 12 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm, same day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30g butter
  • 180ml warm water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120g honey
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt. Rub in the butter, then add the warm water and mix to a soft dough.
  2. Rest the dough, covered, so it relaxes.
  3. Roll the dough out 3mm thick and cut into 16 squares.
  4. Fry in batches in oil heated to 180°C for about a minute a side, until puffed and golden. Drain on paper towel.
  5. Drizzle with honey and dust with cinnamon.

Drinks

Crew Colada

White rum blended with pineapple and coconut cream, toasted coconut rim, umbrella, boat drinks.

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Toast the coconut up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 30g desiccated coconut
  • 200ml white rum
  • 240ml pineapple juice
  • 160ml coconut cream
  • 2 limes
  • 1 small pineapple
  • 4 cocktail umbrellas
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Toast the coconut in a dry pan until golden, then cool on a plate.
  2. Wet the rims of 4 glasses with a lime wedge and dip in the toasted coconut. Juice the limes and cut the pineapple into wedges.
  3. Blend the rum, pineapple juice, coconut cream, lime juice and ice until thick and smooth.
  4. Pour into the glasses and finish with a pineapple wedge and a cocktail umbrella.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when the Man With The Plan calls in Jimmy's debt.
