Snacks

Persian rug flatbreadVegetarian

Charred yoghurt flatbread striped with za'atar, sumac and nigella, whipped feta, pomegranate, mint and dill. Laid out like the rug that really tied the room together

Prep 28 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Whipped feta up to 2 days ahead, chilled. Cook the flatbreads just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 350g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200g feta
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp za'atar
  • 2 tsp sumac
  • 2 tsp nigella seeds
  • 80g pomegranate seeds
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Mix the flour, 250g of the yoghurt and the salt into a soft dough, knead for 1 minute, then rest 10 minutes.
  2. Whip the feta with the remaining 100g yoghurt, the lemon zest and juice and 2 tbsp of the olive oil until smooth.
  3. Divide the dough into 4 and roll each piece into a thin rectangle about 25cm long.
  4. Cook each flatbread in a hot dry frying pan for 2 minutes a side, until puffed and charred in spots.
  5. Brush with the rest of the olive oil and sprinkle the za'atar, sumac and nigella seeds in stripes across each one, like a rug.
  6. Spread the whipped feta on a platter, lay the flatbreads alongside and scatter with the pomegranate, mint and dill.

Jesus Quintana's chorizo taquitos

Corn tortillas rolled round chorizo, potato and cheddar, fried crisp, with purple pickled onion, lime crema and salsa verde. Rolled tight and dressed in purple. Nobody messes with the Jesus

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Filling and pickled onion up to 1 day ahead, chilled. Roll and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 400g potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 250g fresh chorizo sausages, skins removed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 16 small corn tortillas
  • neutral oil, for shallow frying
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 lime
  • 150g salsa verde
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  1. Toss the onion with the vinegar, sugar and half the salt and leave it to turn purple while you cook.
  2. Boil the potatoes in water with the rest of the salt for 10 minutes until tender, then drain.
  3. Fry the chorizo, broken up, in a dry pan for 6 minutes, add the cumin and potatoes and crush together, then stir in the cheddar.
  4. Warm the tortillas in the microwave under a damp tea towel for 1 minute so they roll without cracking.
  5. Spoon the filling along each tortilla, roll tightly and hold with a toothpick.
  6. Shallow fry the taquitos in 1cm of hot oil, seam side down first, for 3 to 4 minutes, turning, until crisp. Drain on paper towel.
  7. Mix the sour cream with the lime zest and juice. Serve the taquitos with the lime crema, salsa verde, pickled onion and coriander.

Over the line gochujang wings

Crisp oven-roasted chicken wings in a gochujang, honey and sesame glaze, spring onion, quick-pickled cucumber. This is not 'Nam. This is bowling. There are rules. Walter

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Glaze up to 3 days ahead, chilled. Roast the wings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings, jointed
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  1. Heat the oven to 220C (200C fan). Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Roast the wings on a rack over a lined tray for 40 to 45 minutes, turning once, until crisp and golden.
  3. While they roast, simmer the gochujang, honey, soy sauce, 1 tbsp of the rice vinegar, the sesame oil and garlic in a small pan for 2 minutes until glossy.
  4. Slice the cucumber thinly and toss with the rest of the rice vinegar.
  5. Toss the hot wings in the glaze and scatter with the sesame seeds and spring onion. Serve with the cucumber.

Mains

In-N-Out double-double, animal style

Two smashed beef patties seared in mustard, American cheese, slow-cooked onion, pickles and spread on a toasted bun. Fries on the side. The drive-thru order, three in the car. Those are good burgers, Walter. Donny

Prep 30 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Spread up to 3 days ahead, chilled. Onions up to 1 day ahead, warm through to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g potatoes
  • neutral oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 640g beef mince, 20% fat
  • 2 onions, finely diced
  • 20g butter
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 80g dill pickles, sliced
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries, rinse well and pat completely dry.
  2. Fry the fries in the oil at 150C for 5 minutes, then drain and cool for 15 minutes.
  3. Cook the onions in the butter over low heat for 15 minutes, stirring, until soft and golden.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and relish for the spread.
  5. Roll the mince into 8 balls. Smash each one thin on a very hot griddle, season and brush the top with mustard. Cook 2 minutes, flip mustard side down, add a slice of cheese and cook 1 minute more.
  6. Fry the fries again at 190C for 3 minutes until crisp and toss with the salt.
  7. Toast the buns cut side down. Build each with spread, lettuce, tomato, a patty, onions, pickles, a second patty and more spread. Serve with the fries.

Shomer shabbos coffee brisket on challah

Brisket braised low with onions, paprika and black Folgers coffee, piled on challah with horseradish cream and pickles. Cooked on Friday so Walter is done by sundown. He does not roll on Shabbos

Prep 34 min, cook 225 min · Make ahead: Braise up to 2 days ahead and chill in its juices; it slices more neatly cold. Warm through in the juices to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef brisket
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 250ml strong black coffee
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 challah loaf
  • 4 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 150g dill pickles, sliced
  1. Heat the oven to 160C (140C fan). Rub the brisket with the paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Brown the brisket well in the oil in a heavy casserole, about 4 minutes a side, then lift it out.
  3. Cook the onions in the same pot for 10 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and tomato paste and cook 1 minute more.
  4. Stir in the brown sugar, coffee and stock, return the brisket, cover and braise in the oven for 3 and a half hours until it pulls apart.
  5. Rest the brisket in its juices for 15 minutes, then slice it across the grain.
  6. Slice the challah thickly. Pile on the brisket with a spoon of the onions and juices, the horseradish cream and pickles.

Nihilist lingonberry Dutch babyVegetarian

A puffed oven pancake with lingonberry jam, whipped crème fraîche, lemon and icing sugar. What the nihilists order at the coffee shop. They believe in nothing, except pancakes

Prep 14 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day ahead, chilled; bake just before serving, it falls as it cools
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 160ml milk
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 40g butter
  • 200g lingonberry jam
  • 200g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 220C (200C fan) with a 26cm cast-iron or ovenproof frying pan inside.
  2. Blend the eggs, milk, flour, caster sugar, salt and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Add the butter to the hot pan, swirl until it foams, pour in the batter and bake for 18 to 20 minutes until puffed high and golden. Keep the oven door shut.
  4. Whip the crème fraîche with the lemon zest until it just holds its shape.
  5. Take it to the table straight away. Spoon over the lingonberry jam and crème fraîche, squeeze over a little lemon juice and dust with the icing sugar.

Larry's homework cookiesVegetarian

Brown butter choc chip cookies with dark chocolate and sea salt flakes, packed warm in a zip-lock bag. Found under the car seat. Is this your homework, Larry?

Prep 30 min, cook 76 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 3 days ahead, chilled. Baked cookies keep 3 days in a sealed bag
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Melt the butter in a small pan and cook for 4 minutes until it smells nutty and turns golden brown, then cool for 10 minutes.
  2. Beat the brown butter with both sugars, the egg and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Mix in the flour, bicarb soda, salt and chocolate.
  4. Chill the dough for 1 hour.
  5. Heat the oven to 180C (160C fan). Roll 16 balls, space them on 2 lined trays and bake for 11 to 12 minutes until golden at the edges and soft in the middle.
  6. Sprinkle with the sea salt flakes, cool on the trays for 10 minutes and pack in a zip-lock bag for the couch.

Drinks

White RussianVegetarian

Vodka and coffee liqueur over ice, topped with half-and-half and stirred lazily. The Dude calls it a Caucasian. Careful, man, there's a beverage here!

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the cream and milk ahead and keep chilled; build each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 400g ice
  • 180ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 60ml thickened cream
  • 60ml full-cream milk
  1. Fill four rocks glasses with the ice and pour 45ml vodka and 20ml coffee liqueur into each.
  2. Stir the cream and milk together for a half-and-half, float 30ml on each drink and stir lazily.

The Stranger's sarsaparilla floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Ice-cold sarsaparilla poured over vanilla ice cream, with a curl of lemon peel. The Stranger asks the barman for a good one

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the sarsaparilla well ahead; build each float to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 375ml bottles sarsaparilla, chilled
  • 400g vanilla ice cream
  • 1 lemon
  1. Chill four tall glasses in the freezer for 10 minutes.
  2. Drop 2 scoops of ice cream into each glass.
  3. Pour the sarsaparilla slowly down the side so it foams up without spilling over, and finish each with a curl of lemon peel.
