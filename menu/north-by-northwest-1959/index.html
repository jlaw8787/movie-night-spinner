Snacks

Hotel bar cheese gougèresVegetarian

Light choux puffs with gruyère, baked golden.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, or freeze and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g gruyère, grated
  1. Boil the water with the butter, then beat in the flour until it leaves the pan, 8 min.
  2. Cool, then beat in the eggs and the gruyère, 10 min.
  3. Pipe 20 small puffs onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 200°C until hollow and golden, 22 min.

Dining car relish trayVegetarian

Celery, carrot, radishes, olives and dill pickles on ice.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep on ice
Recipe for 4
  • 4 celery sticks
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 150g mixed olives
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  1. Cut the celery and carrots into sticks and trim the radishes.
  2. Arrange on crushed ice with the olives and pickle spears.
  3. Add a small dish of cream cheese for spreading.

Mains

Dining car brook trout

Whole trout pan-fried crisp, brown butter and flaked almonds.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 whole trout, cleaned
  • 50g plain flour
  • 40g butter, for frying
  • 40g butter, for the sauce
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Dust the trout in flour and season.
  2. Fry two at a time in butter until crisp on both sides, 8 min.
  3. Brown the sauce butter with the almonds, add lemon juice and chopped parsley, 5 min.
  4. Spoon over the fish.

Mount Rushmore hot roast beef sandwich

Top round sliced thin on white bread under red wine gravy with mash, a South Dakota diner plate.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef topside roast
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 100ml red wine
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 800g potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 loaf thick white bread
  1. Slow-roasted beef sliced thin on thick white bread, smothered in hot red wine gravy, with creamy mashed potato and buttered green beans. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub a 1kg beef blade or topside roast with oil, salt and pepper and roast 50 minutes for medium. Rest 15 minutes, then slice thin. Whisk 2 tbsp pan fat with 2 tbsp flour, then 100ml red wine and 400ml beef stock and simmer 5 minutes. Mash 800g potatoes with butter and milk. Boil 300g green beans. Lay the beef on 8 slices of thick white bread, pour over the gravy and serve with mash and beans.

Baked AlaskaVegetarian

Sponge cake, vanilla ice cream and toasted meringue.

Prep 25 min, cook 4 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the ice cream dome a day ahead, bake at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sponge cake round (20cm)
  • 1 L firm vanilla ice cream
  • 4 egg whites
  • 200g caster sugar
  1. Lay the sponge round on a tray and dome the ice cream on top.
  2. Freeze until very firm, 2 hr.
  3. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar to stiff, glossy peaks.
  4. Cover the ice cream completely in meringue.
  5. Bake at 230°C until browned. Serve straight away, 4 min.

Drinks

Gin Gibson with cocktail onion

Gin and dry vermouth stirred over ice, sparkling water if you prefer.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 4 cocktail onions
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Stir the gin and vermouth over ice, one drink at a time.
  2. Strain into a chilled glass with a cocktail onion.
  3. For anyone off the gin, pour sparkling water over ice with onions.

When to serve

Serve the trout when Roger meets Eve in the dining car.
