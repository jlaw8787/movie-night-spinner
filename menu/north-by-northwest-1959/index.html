Snacks

Hotel bar cheese gougèresVegetarian

Light choux puffs with gruyère, baked golden

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, or freeze and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g gruyere, grated
  1. Boil the water with the butter, then beat in the flour until it leaves the pan.
  2. Cool, then beat in the eggs and the gruyere.
  3. Pipe 20 small puffs onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 200C until hollow and golden.

Dining car relish trayVegetarian

Celery, carrot, radishes, olives and dill pickles on ice

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep on ice
Recipe for 4
  • 4 celery sticks
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 150g mixed olives
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 2 cup crushed ice
  1. Cut the celery and carrots into sticks and trim the radishes.
  2. Arrange on crushed ice with the olives and pickle spears.
  3. Add a small dish of cream cheese for spreading.

Mains

Dining car brook trout

Whole trout pan-fried crisp, brown butter and flaked almonds

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 whole trout, cleaned
  • 50g plain flour
  • 40g butter, for frying
  • 40g butter, for the sauce
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Dust the trout in flour and season.
  2. Fry two at a time in butter until crisp on both sides.
  3. Brown the sauce butter with the almonds, add lemon juice and chopped parsley.
  4. Spoon over the fish.

Sweets

Baked AlaskaVegetarian

Sponge cake, vanilla ice cream and toasted meringue

Prep 25 min, cook 124 min · Make ahead: Make the ice cream dome a day ahead, bake at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sponge cake round (20cm)
  • 1l firm vanilla ice cream
  • 4 egg whites
  • 200g caster sugar
  1. Lay the sponge round on a tray and dome the ice cream on top.
  2. Freeze until very firm.
  3. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar to stiff, glossy peaks.
  4. Cover the ice cream completely in meringue.
  5. Bake at 230C until browned. Serve straight away.

Drinks

Gin Gibson with cocktail onionVegetarian

Gin and dry vermouth stirred over ice, sparkling water if you prefer

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 4 cocktail onions
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Stir the gin and vermouth over ice, one drink at a time.
  2. Strain into a chilled glass with a cocktail onion.
  3. For anyone off the gin, pour sparkling water over ice with onions.

When to serve

Serve the trout when Roger meets Eve in the dining car.
