Mobil Avenue platform pigs in a blanket

Pork, fennel and thyme sausage meat rolled in puff pastry with sesame, with ketchup.

Prep 22 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled, or freeze raw and bake from frozen for 30 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 30g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C, line a tray and thaw the pastry, 10 min.
  2. Mix the sausage mince with the onion, fennel, thyme, breadcrumbs and pepper.
  3. Cut each pastry sheet in half, lay a log of filling along each strip, roll up and seal the seam.
  4. Brush with the egg, scatter with sesame, cut each roll into 6 and slash the tops.
  5. Chill on the tray for 15 minutes.
  6. Bake for 25 minutes until puffed, golden and cooked through.
  7. Serve hot with the tomato sauce.

Club Hel devilled eggsVegetarian

Halved eggs piped with mustard yolk cream, dusted with smoked paprika, black sesame and chives.

Prep 15 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Boil and peel the eggs and mix the filling up to 1 day ahead; pipe and dust within an hour of serving
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • cayenne pepper, a pinch
  • salt, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp black sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp chives, finely chopped
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes.
  2. Cool in iced water for 10 minutes.
  3. Peel, halve lengthways and scoop the yolks into a bowl.
  4. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, lemon juice, cayenne and salt until smooth.
  5. Pipe or spoon the filling back into the whites.
  6. Dust with smoked paprika, black sesame and chives.

Trainman's dining car chicken and leek pie

Chicken thigh, leek and thyme in a creamy mustard sauce under golden puff pastry.

Prep 5 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Make and chill the filling up to 2 days ahead; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, cut into chunks
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g butter
  • 2 leeks, washed and sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Season the chicken and brown it in the oil in a wide pan in batches. Set aside, 12 min.
  2. Melt the butter in the pan and soften the leeks and garlic for 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in the flour for a minute, then the stock, cream, mustard and thyme. Return the chicken and simmer for 20 minutes until thick.
  4. Cool the filling for 20 minutes.
  5. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spoon the filling into a pie dish, top with the pastry, crimp the edges, brush with egg and cut 2 vents, 8 min.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes until puffed and deep golden.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Rama-Kandra's family dalVegetarian

Golden red lentils with spinach under a cumin chilli tarka, basmati and naan, karma included.

Prep 6 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Make the dal up to 2 days ahead and reheat with a splash of water; tarka on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g red lentils
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 20g ginger
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 150g baby spinach
  • 40g ghee
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 naans
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Simmer the rinsed lentils with the turmeric and 1 litre of water for 20 minutes until soft, stirring now and then.
  2. Fry the chopped onion, garlic and ginger in half the ghee for 8 minutes, add the tomatoes and stir into the lentils with the spinach.
  3. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, then rest for 5.
  4. Sizzle the cumin seeds and chillies in the rest of the ghee for 1 minute and pour over the dal.
  5. Warm the naan and serve with the dal, rice, lemon and coriander, 4 min.

Trainman's steamed maple syrup puddingVegetarian

Light steamed lemon sponge with a corn syrup top, turned out and served with vanilla custard.

Prep 18 min, cook 16 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Steam up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled in the basin and resteam for 30 minutes; make the custard on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  • 125g butter, softened, plus extra for the basin
  • 125g caster sugar, for the pudding
  • 2 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 3 tbsp milk, for the pudding
  • 400ml full cream milk, for the custard
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 50g caster sugar, for the custard
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  1. Butter a 1.2 litre pudding basin and spoon the golden syrup into the base.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar until pale.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour, lemon zest and milk.
  4. Spoon into the basin, cover with pleated buttered baking paper and foil and tie with string.
  5. Stand on a trivet in a large pot with boiling water halfway up the basin, cover and steam for 90 minutes, topping up the water.
  6. For the custard, heat the milk and vanilla. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour, pour on the hot milk, return to the pan and stir over low heat until thick, 10 min.
  7. Rest the pudding for 5 minutes, turn out onto a plate and serve with the custard.

Drinks

Sati's sunrise sodaNo alcohol

Orange juice and soda over ice with grenadine sunk to the bottom like a sunrise.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Chill the juice and soda for at least 2 hours ahead; build each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cups ice
  • 500ml orange juice, chilled
  • 400ml soda water, chilled
  • 80ml grenadine syrup
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 4 orange slices
  1. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in the orange juice and top with the soda water.
  3. Slowly pour the grenadine down the inside of each glass so it sinks to the bottom.
  4. Squeeze in a lime wedge and finish with an orange slice.
