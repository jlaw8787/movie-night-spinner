Snacks

Sobel wedding fried calamari

Buttermilk-soaked squid in semolina, cooked crisp, for the Miami nuptials Vitti keeps interrupting

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Soak up to 2 hours ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g squid tubes
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine semolina
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 2 lemons, cut in wedges
  1. Slice the squid into rings and soak in the buttermilk for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the semolina, flour and plenty of salt.
  3. Drain the squid and toss through the flour mix.
  4. Fry in the neutral oil at 190C in batches for 90 seconds each.
  5. Salt and serve with lemon wedges.

Mozzarella in carrozzaVegetarian

Comfort food for a man in crisis

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 250g mozzarella, sliced
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 250ml neutral oil, for frying
  1. Cut the crusts off the bread and make 4 sandwiches with the mozzarella. Cut each into triangles.
  2. Dip in the eggs beaten with milk, then press into breadcrumbs.
  3. Shallow-fry in 1cm of oil for 2 minutes a side until golden and the cheese melts.

Mains

Paul Vitti's veal parm

Crumbed veal under marinara and mozzarella with spaghetti, you're good, you

Prep 25 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g veal escalopes
  • 40g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g breadcrumbs
  • 250ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 700ml passata
  • 200g mozzarella, sliced
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 400g spaghetti
  1. Dust the veal in flour, dip in egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  2. Shallow-fry in the neutral oil, 2 minutes a side, until golden.
  3. Warm about half the passata, top each cutlet with sauce, mozzarella and parmesan and grill until bubbling.
  4. Cook the spaghetti and toss in the rest of the passata, heated.

Vitti's ricotta cannoliVegetarian

Vitti's thank you to his shrink, in pastry form

Prep 20 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead; pipe just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fresh ricotta
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 50g dark chocolate
  • 8 cannoli shells
  1. Drain the ricotta for 2 hours, then beat with icing sugar and orange zest.
  2. Melt the chocolate and dip the ends of the shells, then let set.
  3. Pipe the filling in just before serving.

Drinks

Dr Sobel's calming NegroniVegetarian

Gin, bitter red aperitif and sweet vermouth over a big cube, for a 3am house call

Prep 3 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Batch the gin, aperitif and vermouth in a bottle up to 1 week ahead; stir over ice and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 120ml bitter red aperitif
  • 120ml sweet vermouth
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 orange
  1. For each drink, stir equal parts gin, aperitif and vermouth over ice for 20 seconds.
  2. Strain over a big ice cube in a tumbler and add a slice of orange.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Paul first turns up at Ben's office.
