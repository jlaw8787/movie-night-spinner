Snacks

Five-minute gritsVegetarian

The grits cross-examination, nobody can cook them that fast

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; hold up to 1 hour in a covered pot and loosen with a splash of milk
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml milk
  • 500ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150g stone-ground grits or polenta
  • 40g butter
  • 80g sharp cheddar, grated
  1. Bring the milk, water and salt to the boil.
  2. Whisk in the grits, then stir over low heat until creamy. It takes much longer than five minutes.
  3. Finish with the butter and cheddar.

Fried green tomatoesVegetarian

Sliced unripe tomatoes dipped in buttermilk and cornmeal, crisp with a caper and hot sauce remoulade

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fry to serve; remoulade up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green tomatoes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 120g fine cornmeal
  • salt and pepper, to taste (for the cornmeal)
  • 250ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • hot sauce, a dash
  1. Slice the tomatoes 1cm thick, salt and leave for 10 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Dip in buttermilk, then in the cornmeal seasoned with salt and pepper.
  3. Shallow-fry in the oil at 180C for 2 minutes a side in batches.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise, mustard, capers and hot sauce for the remoulade.

Biscuits and sausage gravy

Flaky buttermilk scones under peppery white sausage sauce, the breakfast Vinny can't get used to

Prep 20 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Bake the biscuits up to 1 day ahead, keep covered and rewarm in a 160C oven for 8 minutes; make the gravy fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour, for the biscuits
  • 2 tbsp self-raising flour, extra for the gravy
  • 80g butter, frozen
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 400g pork sausages
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • salt, to taste
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 220C. Grate the frozen butter into the 300g self-raising flour with a pinch of salt and toss to coat.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk to make a soft dough, fold it over on itself three times, pat out 2cm thick and cut 8 biscuits.
  3. Bake the biscuits on a lined tray until risen and golden.
  4. Squeeze the sausage meat out of the skins and brown it in a frying pan, breaking it up as it cooks.
  5. Stir in the extra flour for a minute, then add the milk a little at a time and simmer until thick, with plenty of black pepper. Split the biscuits and spoon the gravy over.

Mains

Sac-O-Suds chicken fried steak

Pounded beef dipped in flour and buttermilk, fried crisp, peppery cream gravy, for the two yutes

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Pound the steaks up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled between sheets of baking paper; crumb, fry and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g minute steaks (4)
  • 150g plain flour, seasoned, for the coating
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 250ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp plain flour, for the gravy
  • 400ml milk
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 800g mashed potato
  1. Pound the steaks very thin.
  2. Dredge in seasoned flour, dip in buttermilk, then flour again.
  3. Shallow-fry 3 minutes a side until crisp.
  4. Pour off most of the oil, stir the gravy flour into the pan, whisk in the milk and simmer thick with lots of black pepper.
  5. Serve over the mashed potato.

Sweets

Mona Lisa's blackberry cobblerVegetarian

Wazoo, Alabama, ticking clock and all

Prep 20 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 4 hours ahead; serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 750g blackberries
  • 100g sugar, for the filling
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 80g butter, cold
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g sugar, for the topping
  • 150ml milk
  • 300ml cream
  1. Toss the blackberries with the sugar and cornflour in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and the topping sugar, add the milk and drop spoonfuls on top.
  3. Bake at 190C for 35 minutes.
  4. Serve with cream.

Drinks

Wazoo peach sweet teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Southern style in Wazoo, Alabama, so sweet your teeth hurt

Prep 15 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe peaches, sliced, plus 1 for serving
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1l boiling water
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 sprig mint per glass
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the peaches with the sugar and water for 10 minutes, mash lightly and strain, keeping the syrup.
  2. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 5 minutes and remove.
  3. Stir in the peach syrup and lemon juice and chill for 2 hours.
  4. Serve over ice with fresh peach slices and mint.
