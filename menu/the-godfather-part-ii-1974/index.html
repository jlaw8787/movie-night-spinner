Snacks

Arancini Genco

Saffron rice, beef ragù, peas and a mozzarella heart.

Prep 33 min, cook 12 min, 20 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 900ml chicken stock
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 150g beef mince
  • 50g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp passata
  • 60g mozzarella, cubed
  • 40g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  1. Cook the rice in the stock with the saffron, stirring, until thick. Stir in the parmesan, spread on a tray and chill, ideally overnight.
  2. Brown the mince with the peas and passata until thick and dry. Cool, 10 min.
  3. Wrap golf-ball portions of cold rice around a spoon of filling and a cube of mozzarella.
  4. Roll in flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
  5. Fry in the oil at 175°C in batches until deep gold and hot through, then drain, 12 min.

Medianoches

Ham, roast pork, Swiss, pickle, mustard, pressed hot.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slider rolls
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 150g leg ham, sliced
  • 150g roast pork, sliced
  • 8 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  • 30g butter, melted
  1. Split the rolls and smear the cut sides with mustard.
  2. Layer with ham, pork, Swiss cheese and pickle.
  3. Brush the tops with melted butter.
  4. Press in a heavy pan over medium heat with a second pan on top, about 3 minutes a side, until the cheese melts.

Mains

Ziti al forno

Eggplant and slow tomato, ricotta, blistered mozzarella.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 large eggplant, diced
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 700ml passata
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 500g ziti or penne
  • 400g ricotta
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 200g mozzarella, torn
  1. Fry the eggplant in the oil until soft and browned, add the garlic for a minute, then the passata and half the basil, torn. Simmer slowly until thick, 30 min.
  2. Cook the ziti or penne 3 minutes short of done and drain.
  3. Mix the ricotta with the parmesan, nutmeg and the rest of the basil, chopped, 5 min.
  4. Layer pasta, sauce and ricotta in a baking dish and top with mozzarella.
  5. Bake at 200°C until blistered, 25 min.

Sweets

Torta de la IslaVegetarian

Vanilla slab cake iced pale green, shaped like the island.

Prep 32 min, cook 30 min, 12 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 150g butter, soft, for icing
  • 300g icing sugar
  • green food colouring, a few drops
  1. Beat the butter and caster sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a lined slab tin at 180°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool completely, then trim into a rough island shape, 12 min.
  4. Beat the icing butter with icing sugar and colouring. Spread thinly, then again, and pipe happy birthday across.

Drinks

Daiquiri

Rum, lime and sugar, ice cold. Banana on request.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 240ml white rum
  • 4 limes, juiced (about 100 ml)
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool to make syrup, 5 min.
  2. Chill four coupes.
  3. For each drink, shake rum, lime juice and syrup hard with plenty of ice, then double strain into a coupe.
