Biker bar pickled eggsVegetarian

Pickled eggs with beetroot. Cherry and Hickok are parked outside

Prep 25 min, cook 490 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 weeks, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 375ml white vinegar
  • 125ml water
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 beetroot
  1. Boil the eggs, then cool and peel.
  2. Heat the vinegar with the water, sugar, salt, mustard seeds and sliced beetroot.
  3. Pour over the eggs in a jar.
  4. Chill at least overnight before serving.

Same old Cadillac glovebox jerky

Soy and pepper beef jerky. Jack spent 25 grand of Reggie's money on this car

Prep 35 min, cook 780 min · Make ahead: Partly freeze, slice and marinate the beef the day before; finished jerky keeps up to 2 weeks in a bag
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef topside
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  1. Partly freeze the beef until firm.
  2. Slice very thin.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar and cracked black pepper, add the beef and marinate overnight in the fridge.
  4. Pat dry and lay on racks.
  5. Dry at 70C.
  6. Cool and store in a bag.

Mains

Sourdough bread bowl chowder

Clam chowder in a sourdough bowl. Sit away from the window, Jack

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Simmer the bacon, onion, celery and potato base up to 2 days ahead and reheat; add the clams and cream and toast the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 500ml water
  • 1kg clams, scrubbed
  • 250ml cream
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 4 sourdough rounds
  1. Dice the bacon, onion, celery and potatoes.
  2. Fry the bacon, then add the onion and celery.
  3. Add the potatoes and water and simmer.
  4. Add the clams, cover until they open, then stir in the cream and thyme.
  5. Hollow and toast the sourdough rounds, then ladle in the chowder.

San Francisco Chinatown egg tartsVegetarian

Egg custard tarts. Reggie's got Jack's lighter again

Prep 20 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked; the custard mix can be strained and chilled up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 3 eggs
  • 80ml evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a greased muffin tin.
  2. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  3. Whisk in the eggs, evaporated milk and vanilla, then strain into a jug.
  4. Fill the pastry cases three quarters full.
  5. Bake, then drop to 180C and bake until the custard is just set with a slight wobble.
  6. Cool before lifting out.

Jack Cates' rye and ginger highballVegetarian

Rye, lemon and bitters over ginger ale and ice. You're having a bad day.

Prep 6 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Build to order; cut the lemon peel up to 4 hours ahead and chill the ginger ale
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tray ice
  • 180ml rye whiskey
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 600ml ginger ale, cold
  1. Fill highball glasses with ice and pour in the rye.
  2. Add a squeeze of lemon and a dash of bitters to each.
  3. Top with ginger ale, stir once and add a strip of lemon peel.
