Snacks

Parade chestnuts in sage brown butterVegetarian

Kris plays Santa in the parade. Chestnuts in sage brown butter, peeled warm

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chestnuts
  • 60g butter
  • 12 sage leaves
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Cut a cross in the flat side of each chestnut and soak.
  2. Roast at 220C until the shells open.
  3. Wrap in a tea towel and peel while warm.
  4. Melt the butter until nutty brown, add the sage and fry until crisp.
  5. Toss the chestnuts in the butter with maple syrup and salt.

Dead letter office cheese and celeryVegetarian

Santa's letters, emptied on the judge's desk. Celery with cream and blue cheese

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 50g blue cheese
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 6 celery sticks
  • 1 packet of crackers
  • 100g vintage cheddar, sliced
  1. Beat the cream cheese with the blue cheese and chives until smooth.
  2. Cut the celery into short sticks.
  3. Pipe or spoon the filling along each.
  4. Serve on a platter beside crackers topped with cheddar.

Mains

Fred Gailey's New York pot roast

Macy's own Santa sends you to Gimbels. Beef chuck in red wine, carrots

Prep 25 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef chuck, in one piece
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery sticks, chopped
  • 250ml red wine
  • 800g potatoes, halved
  1. Sear the beef all over in the oil.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, celery, wine and enough water to come halfway.
  3. Cover and braise at 160C.
  4. Add the potatoes for the last hour.
  5. Slice and serve with the juices.

Christmas puddingVegetarian

For the house with the cane inside. Brandy-soaked pudding, flamed with brandy

Prep 30 min, cook 270 min · Make ahead: Pudding can be steamed up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed dried fruit
  • 100ml brandy, for soaking the fruit
  • 125g butter, melted
  • 125g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 30ml brandy, for flaming
  • 500ml custard
  1. Soak the fruit in the brandy overnight.
  2. Mix with the butter, sugar, eggs, breadcrumbs, flour and mixed spice.
  3. Pack into a greased basin and cover.
  4. Steam.
  5. Warm the brandy for flaming, pour over and light at the table.
  6. Serve with custard.

Kris Kringle's maple mulled ciderVegetarianNo alcohol

Susan believes now. Apple juice simmered with maple, cinnamon, cloves and orange

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 cinnamon sticks, for simmering
  • 3 star anise
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 4 cinnamon sticks, for serving
  1. Pour the apple juice into a pot with the maple syrup, the cinnamon sticks for simmering, star anise, cloves and orange.
  2. Bring to a simmer, then keep just below simmering.
  3. Strain into mugs.
  4. Add a cinnamon stick and an orange slice studded with cloves to each.
