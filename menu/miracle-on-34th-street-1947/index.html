Parade chestnuts in sage brown butterVegetarian

Kris plays Santa in the parade. Chestnuts in sage brown butter, peeled warm

Prep 23 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chestnuts
  • 60g butter
  • 12 sage leaves
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Cut a cross in the flat side of each chestnut and soak.
  2. Roast at 220C until the shells open.
  3. Wrap in a tea towel and peel while warm.
  4. Melt the butter until nutty brown, add the sage and fry until crisp.
  5. Toss the chestnuts in the butter with maple syrup and salt.

Dead letter office cheese and celeryVegetarian

Santa's letters, emptied on the judge's desk. Celery with cream and blue cheese

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 50g blue cheese
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 6 celery sticks
  • 1 packet of crackers
  • 100g vintage cheddar, sliced
  1. Beat the cream cheese with the blue cheese and chives until smooth.
  2. Cut the celery into short sticks.
  3. Pipe or spoon the filling along each.
  4. Serve on a platter beside crackers topped with cheddar.

Fred Gailey's New York pot roast

Macy's own Santa sends you to Gimbels. Beef chuck in red wine, carrots

Prep 78 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef chuck, in one piece
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery sticks, chopped
  • 250ml red wine
  • 800g potatoes, halved
  1. Sear the beef all over in the oil.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, celery, wine and enough water to come halfway.
  3. Cover and braise at 160C.
  4. Add the potatoes for the last hour.
  5. Slice and serve with the juices.

Doris's Thanksgiving roast turkeyOn the printed menu

The Thanksgiving dinner Fred talks his way into

How it is made
  • 1.5kg turkey breast roll
  • 1 box sea salt
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 120g butter
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 300g cornbread
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 egg
  • 450ml chicken stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Brined turkey breast roasted juicy with herb butter, cornbread and sage stuffing, buttery mash, cranberry sauce and a deep pan gravy. Brine a 1.5kg turkey breast roll in 2 litres water with 100g salt and 50g sugar overnight. Pat dry, rub with 50g herb butter and roast at 170C to 70C inside, about 80 minutes. Rest 15 minutes. Bake 300g crumbled cornbread with 1 diced onion softened in butter, sage, 1 egg and 150ml stock for 30 minutes. Mash 1kg potatoes with butter and milk. Make gravy from the tray juices, flour and 300ml stock.

Christmas puddingVegetarian

For the house with the cane inside. Brandy-soaked pudding, flamed with brandy

Prep 30 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Pudding can be steamed up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed dried fruit
  • 100ml brandy, for soaking the fruit
  • 125g butter, melted
  • 125g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 30ml brandy, for flaming
  • 500ml custard
  1. Soak the fruit in the brandy overnight.
  2. Mix with the butter, sugar, eggs, breadcrumbs, flour and mixed spice.
  3. Pack into a greased basin and cover.
  4. Steam.
  5. Warm the brandy for flaming, pour over and light at the table.
  6. Serve with custard.

Kris Kringle's brown butter shortbreadOn the printed menu

Nutty rounds dipped in white chocolate and crushed peppermint, for the kids in the queue

How it is made
  • 200g butter
  • 90g caster sugar
  • 250g plain flour
  • 30g cornflour
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 1 packet candy canes
  1. Brown butter shortbread rounds dipped in white chocolate and crushed candy cane, the kind Kris might hand out to the children in line. Brown 200g butter until nutty, chill until soft-set, then beat with 90g caster sugar. Mix in 250g plain flour, 30g cornflour and a pinch of salt. Roll into a log, chill 30 minutes, slice into 20 rounds and bake at 160C for 18 minutes. Cool, dip half of each in 150g melted white chocolate and sprinkle with crushed candy cane.

Kris Kringle's maple mulled ciderVegetarianNo alcohol

Susan believes now. Apple juice simmered with maple, cinnamon, cloves and orange

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 cinnamon sticks, for simmering
  • 3 star anise
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 4 cinnamon sticks, for serving
  1. Pour the apple juice into a pot with the maple syrup, the cinnamon sticks for simmering, star anise, cloves and orange.
  2. Bring to a simmer, then keep just below simmering.
  3. Strain into mugs.
  4. Add a cinnamon stick and an orange slice studded with cloves to each.

When to serve
