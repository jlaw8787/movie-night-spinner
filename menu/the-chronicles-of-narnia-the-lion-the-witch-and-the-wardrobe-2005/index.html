Snacks

Mr Tumnus's tea and toast

Buttered toast fingers with mashed sardine and lemon, or honey

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mash the sardines with the lemon up to 1 day ahead, chilled; toast and spread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 100g butter
  • 1 can sardines
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Toast the bread and butter it while hot.
  2. Mash the sardines with a squeeze of lemon and spread on half the toast.
  3. Drizzle honey over the rest.
  4. Cut into fingers and serve with a pot of tea.

Mr Tumnus's sardines on toast

Sardines mashed with lemon and parsley on hot buttered toast, as Tumnus promised Lucy

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mash the sardines up to 4 hours ahead; toast and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins sardines in olive oil (110g each)
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 8 slices wholemeal bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Drain the sardines and mash roughly with the lemon zest, a squeeze of juice, the shallot, chopped parsley and pepper.
  2. Toast the bread and butter it while hot.
  3. Pile the sardines on the toast and grill 2 minutes until hot.
  4. Cut into fingers and serve with lemon wedges.

Mrs Beaver's fish dinner

Flathead fried in butter, parsley butter potatoes, buttered peas

Prep 12 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Peel and cut the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, covered in water in the fridge; cook the fish and peas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 flathead fillets
  • 100g butter
  • 800g potatoes
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Peel and cut the potatoes, chop the parsley and pat the fish dry.
  2. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and toss with some of the butter and the parsley.
  3. Season the fish with salt and pepper, then pan-fry in butter until golden on both sides.
  4. Cook the peas, then toss with butter and a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Serve everything together.

Aslan's camp rosemary roast lambOn the printed menu

Leg studded with garlic, roast potatoes and carrots, served for the whole army

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 jar mint sauce
  1. Roast lamb with rosemary and garlic, roast potatoes and carrots, served for the whole army. Make small slits in a 2kg lamb leg and push in garlic slivers and rosemary sprigs. Rub with olive oil, salt and pepper. Roast at 180C for 90 minutes, adding 1kg potatoes and 4 carrots in chunks tossed in oil for the last hour. Rest the lamb 15 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan juices, whisk in 300ml stock and bubble into gravy. Carve and serve on a big platter with the veg and mint sauce.

Professor's house sconesVegetarian

Light scones made with cream and lemonade, served warm

Prep 15 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Same day, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml thickened cream, for the dough
  • 300ml lemonade
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 220C and flour a tray.
  2. Mix the flour, the cream for the dough and the lemonade with a knife until just combined.
  3. Pat out the dough, cut into rounds and place them close together on the tray.
  4. Bake until risen and golden.
  5. Serve warm with jam and whipped cream.

Turkish delightVegetarian

Chewy rosewater candy, pink and set firm

Prep 13 min, cook 65 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Set overnight, then cut and roll in icing sugar; keeps up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g caster sugar
  • 120g cornflour
  • 750ml water
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp rosewater
  • pink food colouring, a few drops
  • 100g icing sugar
  1. Line a tin with baking paper. Juice the lemon.
  2. Stir the sugar, cornflour, water and lemon juice in a pot over low heat for an hour until very thick.
  3. Stir in the rosewater and a few drops of pink colouring, then pour into the tin.
  4. Leave to set overnight.
  5. Cut into cubes and roll in icing sugar.

Father Christmas cocoaVegetarianNo alcohol

Milk whisked with dark chocolate and cinnamon, topped with whipped cream

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Heat and whisk on the night; chop the chocolate up to 1 day ahead and keep the whipped cream chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Chop the chocolate and whip the cream to soft peaks.
  2. Heat the milk until steaming.
  3. Whisk in the chocolate and cinnamon until frothy.
  4. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the White Witch offers Edmund the Turkish delight.
