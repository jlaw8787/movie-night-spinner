Warbucks mansion canapés

Creamy chicken and mushroom pastry cases, plus asparagus wrapped in prosciutto

Prep 12 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; warm it, fill the cases and wrap the asparagus on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 vol-au-vent cases
  • 250g chicken thigh fillets
  • 150g mushrooms
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 12 slices prosciutto
  • 1 tbsp butter
  1. Bake the vol-au-vent cases until crisp.
  2. Dice the chicken and mushrooms and fry in butter until browned.
  3. Add the cream and thyme and reduce until thick, then fill the cases.
  4. Blanch the asparagus and wrap each spear in half a slice of prosciutto.

Radio City popcornVegetarian

Stovetop popcorn popped in coconut oil, tossed in melted butter and salt

Prep 8 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best popped just before serving; keeps crisp in an airtight container up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 120g popping corn kernels
  • 50g butter, melted
  • fine salt, to taste
  1. Heat the oil with three kernels in a covered pot.
  2. When they pop, add the rest of the kernels, cover and shake until the popping slows.
  3. Toss with the butter and salt, then fill paper cones.

Warbucks roast chicken

Whole chicken with tarragon butter under the skin, roasted golden

Prep 15 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Dry-brine the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 tbsp flaky salt, for dry-brining
  • 60g butter, softened, for under the skin
  • 1 small bunch tarragon
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 20g butter, for the peas
  1. The night before, salt the chicken and leave uncovered in the fridge.
  2. Mix the softened butter with chopped tarragon and push it under the skin.
  3. Roast at 220°C with the halved potatoes.
  4. Lower to 180°C and roast until the thigh reads 72°C.
  5. Rest the chicken and make gravy from the pan juices and stock.
  6. Cook the peas and toss with butter.

Punjab's lamb curry

Lamb shoulder simmered with ginger, garlic, garam masala and tomato, yoghurt, for the bodyguard

Prep 12 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Cook the curry up to 2 days ahead, it gets better; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg boneless lamb shoulder, in chunks
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, finely sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 20g ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 400g can crushed tomatoes
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 300g basmati rice
  1. Brown the lamb in the oil in batches. Lift out.
  2. Cook the onions slowly in the same pot until deep golden, then add the garlic, ginger and spices and stir for a minute.
  3. Return the lamb with the tomatoes and a cup of water, cover and simmer gently until tender.
  4. Rinse the rice and simmer it, covered, until the water is gone. Rest it with the lid on.
  5. Take the curry off the heat, stir through the yoghurt and finish with coriander.

Mansion sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream under hot fudge and cream, easier than Mrs. Pugh's baked Alaska

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the fudge up to 3 days ahead and reheat gently; toast the almonds up to 2 days ahead, then whip the cream and assemble to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 150ml cream
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 40g flaked almonds, toasted
  • 4 cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate with the cream and golden syrup into a hot fudge.
  2. Toast the almonds in a dry pan and whip the cream.
  3. Scoop the ice cream into tall glasses, pour over the fudge and top with whipped cream, almonds and a cherry.

Orange juice, lime, mint and ginger beer over ice, no alcohol

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices and lime up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add the ginger beer and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml orange juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 1 handful mint
  • 1 tray ice
  • 800ml ginger beer, chilled
  • 1 orange
  1. Combine the juices and torn mint in a large jug.
  2. Add ice, pour in the ginger beer and stir gently.
  3. Add orange slices and serve with a mint sprig.
