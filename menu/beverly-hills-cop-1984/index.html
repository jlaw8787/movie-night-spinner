Banana in the tailpipe breadVegetarian

Overripe banana loaf with brown sugar and vanilla, sliced thick and toasted in butter.

Prep 4 min, cook 73 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: The loaf keeps 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 3 overripe bananas
  • 120g butter, melted, for the loaf
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 20g butter, for toasting
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a small loaf tin. Mash the bananas and mix with the butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
  2. Fold in the flour and salt and spoon into the tin.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Cool the loaf completely.
  5. Cut thick slices, halve into bites and toast in a pan with the extra butter on both sides.

Detroit coney dogs

Cocktail franks in soft buns under a loose beef chilli, straight from Detroit.

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat it and heat the franks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 150ml water
  • 8 cocktail franks
  • 8 mini hot dog buns
  • 20g yellow mustard
  1. Finely dice half the onion and the other half for topping.
  2. Brown the mince with the diced onion in oil.
  3. Stir in the chilli powder, cumin and water and simmer gently, adding water if needed to keep it loose.
  4. Heat the franks in a griddle pan until piping hot.
  5. Fill the buns with franks, chilli, mustard and the raw onion.

Harrow Club smoked trout brioche toasts

Crème fraîche, flaked fish and chives on toasted rounds, plus grapes rolled in goat's cheese.

How it is made
  • 1 brioche loaf
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 150g hot-smoked trout
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 16 seedless grapes
  • 120g soft goat's cheese
  • 50g shelled pistachios
  1. Toasted brioche rounds with crème fraîche, smoked trout and chives, plus grapes rolled in goat's cheese and crushed pistachios. Cut 16 rounds from 4 thick slices of brioche and toast at 180°C for 6 minutes. Top each with crème fraîche, a flake of smoked trout and snipped chives. Wrap 16 seedless grapes in a thin layer of soft goat's cheese, roll in 50g finely crushed pistachios and chill 15 minutes. Serve both on a tray.

Beverly Palms room service

Sirloin steak sandwich with fried onions, put on someone else's tab.

Prep 12 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Caramelise the onions and mix the horseradish mayo up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; bake the fries and cook the steak on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 tbsp oil, for the fries
  • 2 brown onions
  • 20g butter
  • 60g mayonnaise
  • 20g horseradish
  • 600g sirloin steak
  • 1 tsp oil, for the steak
  • 4 brioche rolls
  • 100g Swiss cheese
  1. Cut the potatoes into fries, toss with oil and bake at 220°C, turning halfway.
  2. While the fries bake, slowly cook the sliced onions in butter until caramelised.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with the horseradish.
  4. Season the steak and sear hard in a hot pan.
  5. Rest the steak, then slice thin.
  6. Toast the rolls and fill with steak, onions, Swiss cheese and horseradish mayo.
  7. Serve with the fries alongside.

Serge's gallery lunch chicken Caesar

Grilled chicken, cos, parmesan and sharp anchovy dressing, from Serge at the gallery desk.

Prep 9 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Make the dressing up to 2 days ahead; grill the chicken and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken breast
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 baby cos lettuces
  • 4 thick slices sourdough
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 4 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 lemon
  • 100ml light olive oil
  • 60g parmesan
  1. Tear the bread into chunks, toss with 2 tbsp of the olive oil and 1 crushed garlic clove and bake at 200°C for 10 minutes until golden.
  2. Blend the egg yolk, anchovies, the other garlic clove, mustard and lemon juice, then drizzle in the light olive oil until thick. Grate in half the parmesan.
  3. Slice the chicken in half through the middle, rub with the rest of the oil and salt and cook in a hot grill pan for 4 minutes a side. Rest and slice.
  4. Toss the cos leaves with the dressing and croutons, top with the chicken and shave over the rest of the parmesan.

Banana splitVegetarian

Vanilla, chocolate and strawberry scoops, hot fudge, nuts and a cherry.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; reheat gently and build the splits just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bananas
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4 scoops chocolate ice cream
  • 4 scoops strawberry ice cream
  • 80g dark chocolate
  • 160ml thickened cream
  • 100g crushed pineapple, drained
  • 80ml strawberry sauce
  • 30g chopped nuts
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Split the bananas lengthways between dishes and add a scoop of each ice cream.
  2. Gently melt the chocolate with some of the cream for hot fudge.
  3. Softly whip the rest of the cream.
  4. Add the pineapple, strawberry sauce, hot fudge and whipped cream, then finish with nuts and a cherry. Serve immediately.

Axel's Beverly Palms piña colada

White rum, pineapple and coconut cream blended frosty.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the pineapple juice, coconut cream and lime juice in a jug up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; blend with the rum and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml white rum
  • 360ml pineapple juice
  • 200ml coconut cream
  • 1 lime
  • 3 cups ice
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Blend the rum, pineapple juice, coconut cream, lime juice and ice until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into tall glasses.
  3. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.

When to serve

Serve the bananas when Axel stuffs them up the cop car's exhaust.
