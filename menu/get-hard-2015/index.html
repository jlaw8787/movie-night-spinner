Snacks

Flamethrower rescue hot wings

Crisp baked wings in hot sauce butter, ranch for dipping

Prep 5 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings in the baking powder and salt up to 1 day ahead, uncovered in the fridge; bake and sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 50g butter
  • 150ml ranch dressing
  1. Bake the chicken wings tossed with the baking powder and salt at 220°C for 45 minutes.
  2. Toss in the hot sauce melted with the butter and serve with the ranch.

Engagement party salmon roe blinis

Blinis with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and roe. Eat fast; the FBI turns up mid-party.

Prep 14 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Mix the chive cream up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; warm the blinis and top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 16 small blinis
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Warm the small blinis at 180°C for 4 minutes.
  2. Mix the crème fraîche with the chopped chives and lemon zest.
  3. Top each blini with a spoon of the cream, a ribbon of smoked salmon and half a teaspoon of salmon roe.
  4. Finish with a tiny dill sprig and a squeeze of lemon and arrange on a platter.

Mains

Darnell's family barbecue chicken

Chicken grilled in sticky barbecue sauce, slaw and corn. Dinner at Darnell's, prison story included.

Prep 5 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Make the mac and cheese up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and reheat; bake the chicken on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 300g macaroni
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 bunch silverbeet
  1. Bake the chicken thighs at 200°C for 25 minutes, brush with the barbecue sauce and bake some more.
  2. Make mac and cheese from the macaroni, the butter, the flour, the milk and the cheddar.
  3. Serve with the sautéed silverbeet.

James King's Bel Air steak frites

Seared sirloin with thin chips and garlic butter. King's last fancy dinner before San Quentin.

Prep 7 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the herb butter up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 100g rocket
  • 1 lemon
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the chips in 2 tbsp oil and salt and bake, turning once, until crisp.
  2. Mash the butter with the garlic and chopped parsley.
  3. Sear the steaks in a very hot pan to your liking, then rest with a slice of herb butter on each.
  4. Dress the rocket with lemon and the last of the oil and serve with the steak and chips.

San Quentin chocolate pudding cupsVegetarian

Silky dark cocoa custard under salted biscuit crumb, whipped cream and shaved curls, canteen style

Prep 5 min, cook 11 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 60g chocolate biscuits
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Whisk the milk, the caster sugar, the cocoa, the cornflour and a pinch of salt in a saucepan over medium heat until it thickens, about 6 minutes.
  2. Off the heat, stir in the chopped dark chocolate and the vanilla until smooth.
  3. Pour into the serving dishes, cover the surface and chill 2 hours.
  4. Top with whipped cream, crushed chocolate biscuits with a pinch of flaky salt and chocolate curls.

Drinks

Capoeira cucumber jalapeño margaritaVegetarian

Tequila, cucumber, lime and jalapeño with a salted rim

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Blitz and strain the cucumber juice up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 180ml tequila
  • 5 limes
  • 60ml agave syrup
  • salt flakes, to taste
  • 2 cups ice
  • 50ml water
  1. Blitz the continental cucumber with the water and strain.
  2. Rim the glasses with lime and salt.
  3. For each drink, muddle the slices of jalapeño in a shaker, add the tequila, the cucumber juice, the lime juice and the agave, shake with ice and strain over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with a cucumber ribbon.
  5. For non-drinkers, top the cucumber, lime and agave with soda instead.
