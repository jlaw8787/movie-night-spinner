Big Kahuna pineapple sliders

Seth's burger run back to the motel

Prep 5 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Mix the patties and grill the pineapple up to a day ahead; cook the patties to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 4 pineapple rings, fresh or tinned
  • 60ml teriyaki sauce
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 8 slider buns
  • 8 cos lettuce leaves
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Shape the beef into 8 thin patties and season.
  2. Grill the pineapple rings until charred and cut each in half.
  3. Cook the patties in a hot pan, brushing with teriyaki, and melt a slice of cheese on each.
  4. Build in the toasted buns with mayo, lettuce and pineapple.

Earl McGraw's no-microwave burritos

Chipotle beef and bean burritos. Earl says microwave food will kill you.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cook the filling up to 2 days ahead; roll and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp chipotle chilli powder
  • 400g tin refried beans
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 125g sour cream
  • 200g tomato salsa
  1. Brown the beef with the onion, cumin and chipotle until dark and dry.
  2. Spread each tortilla with beans, add the beef and cheese, and roll up tight.
  3. Brush with oil and bake seam down at 200°C until crisp.
  4. Halve and serve with sour cream and salsa.

Mains

El Rey pork carnitas tacos

Slow-cooked orange pork carnitas tacos. Paid up for El Rey.

Prep 20 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Braise the pork up to 2 days ahead; crisp it in a hot pan to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder, in large chunks
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  • 1 bunch coriander, chopped
  • 3 limes
  • 200g salsa verde
  1. Put the pork in a heavy pot with the orange juice, garlic, oregano, cumin, bay leaves, salt and water to just cover.
  2. Simmer uncovered until the water is gone and the pork is tender and frying in its own fat.
  3. Shred and fry in batches until crisp at the edges.
  4. Warm the tortillas and fill with pork, onion, coriander, salsa verde and lime.

Aztec temple chicken mole

Chicken in a dark chilli and chocolate mole. The bar sits on an Aztec temple.

Prep 5 min, cook 83 min · Make ahead: Make the mole up to 3 days ahead; it gets better. Poach the chicken in it to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 dried ancho chillies, seeded
  • 2 dried chipotle chillies, seeded
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 40g almonds
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 40g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 300g white rice
  1. Toast the chillies in a dry pan, then soak them in hot water for 15 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion, garlic, almonds and half the sesame, then blend with the drained chillies, tomatoes, cinnamon and stock until smooth.
  3. Simmer the sauce until thick and dark, then melt in the chocolate.
  4. Add the chicken and simmer until cooked through, while the rice cooks.
  5. Serve over the rice with the rest of the sesame on top.

Wooden stake churros with dark chocolateVegetarian

Cinnamon churros with dark chocolate. Before the chandelier comes down.

Prep 10 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Make the dough up to 2 hours ahead; pipe and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar, plus 100g for rolling
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 200ml pouring cream
  1. Boil the water, butter, 1 tbsp sugar and a pinch of salt, tip in the flour and beat until it leaves the pan.
  2. Cool for 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs until smooth and glossy.
  3. Pipe 12cm lengths through a star nozzle into oil at 180°C and fry until golden.
  4. Roll in the cinnamon sugar while hot.
  5. Melt the chocolate into the hot cream and serve as a dip.

Chango micheladaVegetarian

Chango beer with lime, chilli salt and spice. The Titty Twister pours Chango.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime and sauce base up to a day ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 355ml bottles Mexican lager
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tsp hot sauce
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp chilli salt
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Rim 4 glasses with lime and dip them in chilli salt.
  2. Add ice, the juice of half a lime, hot sauce and soy to each and top with the lager.
