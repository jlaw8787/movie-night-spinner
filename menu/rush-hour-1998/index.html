Consulate spring rollsVegetarian

Crisp vegetable spring rolls with cabbage, carrot and glass noodles.

Prep 27 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the filling and roll the spring rolls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 30g glass noodles
  • 120g cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot
  • 60g shiitake mushrooms
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp light soy sauce
  • 8 spring roll pastry sheets
  • 1 tsp plain flour
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 40ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Soak the glass noodles in hot water, drain and cut short.
  2. Shred the cabbage, grate the carrot and slice the shiitake.
  3. Stir-fry the vegetables in oil, add the noodles and soy and stir briefly. Cool completely.
  4. Divide among the pastry sheets, roll tightly and seal with a paste of flour and water.
  5. Fry in 180°C oil until golden.
  6. Serve with sweet chilli sauce.

Five spice chicken wings

Wings in a soy and five spice crust, baked crisp, tossed in lemon butter and spring onion.

Prep 10 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Coat the wings and keep them covered in the fridge up to 4 hours ahead; bake and toss in the lemon butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken wings
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 25g butter
  • 35ml light soy sauce
  • 80g egg, beaten
  • 20g cornflour
  • 1 tbsp sliced spring onion
  • 1/4 tsp Chinese five spice
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the salt, soy, five spice, beaten egg and cornflour until coated.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning halfway, until crisp and at least 74°C inside.
  3. Slice the spring onion and squeeze the lemon juice.
  4. Melt the butter with the lemon juice, toss through the wings and scatter with the spring onion.

Prawn and bamboo shoot dumplings

Plump parcels of prawn, bamboo shoot and ginger, steamed, chilli oil and black vinegar soy.

How it is made
  • 400g green prawns
  • 1 tin bamboo shoots
  • 1 small piece ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • sesame oil
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • soy sauce
  • black vinegar
  • chilli oil
  1. Plump prawn dumplings with bamboo shoot, ginger and sesame, folded into wonton wrappers, steamed and served with chilli oil and black vinegar soy. Finely chop 400g peeled green prawns with 60g bamboo shoots, 1 tsp grated ginger, 2 sliced spring onions, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp cornflour and a pinch of salt. Place a teaspoon in the centre of each of 24 wonton wrappers, wet the edges and pleat closed. Steam on baking paper in a bamboo steamer for 7 minutes. Mix 3 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp black vinegar and 1 tbsp chilli oil for dipping.

Beef chow fun

Rice noodles wok-fried with beef and sprouts, smoky and fast enough to beat a van.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Slice and marinate the beef up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stir-fry everything to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef rump
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 600g fresh wide rice noodles
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 150g bean sprouts
  • 3 spring onions
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  1. Thinly slice the beef and marinate with the light soy, cornflour and sesame oil.
  2. Gently separate the noodles.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in two batches in a hot wok and set aside.
  4. Fry the noodles in more oil.
  5. Add the bean sprouts, sliced spring onions and soy sauces. Return the beef and toss over high heat until piping hot.

Lemon chicken

Chicken strips in a light egg and cornflour batter, fried crisp, glossy lemon sauce, jasmine rice.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 eggs
  • light soy sauce
  • 100g cornflour
  • 3 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 small piece ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • sesame seeds
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • honey
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Chicken strips in a light egg and cornflour batter, fried crisp and served with a glossy lemon sauce over jasmine rice. Cut 800g chicken thigh fillets into strips and marinate with 1 tbsp light soy and 1 tsp grated ginger for 20 minutes. Dip in 2 beaten eggs, then toss in 100g cornflour. Fry in batches at 175°C for 5 to 6 minutes until golden. Simmer the juice and zest of 3 lemons with 250ml chicken stock, 100g caster sugar and 1 tbsp honey, then thicken with 2 tsp cornflour mixed with a little water. Cook 300g jasmine rice. Slice the chicken, pour over the sauce and scatter with spring onion and sesame seeds.

Ransom fortune cookiesVegetarian

Crisp vanilla cookies folded round a fortune.

Prep 50 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 8 paper fortunes (food-safe strips)
  • 2 egg whites
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 25g butter, melted
  • 40g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp water
  1. Write the fortunes on small clean paper strips.
  2. Whisk the egg whites and sugar until foamy and stir in the butter, flour, vanilla and water.
  3. Spread two thin circles at a time on a lined tray and bake at 180°C until the edges turn golden.
  4. Working quickly while hot, place a fortune in each, fold in half and bend over a mug rim. Cool in muffin-tin cups. Repeat for the rest.
  5. Remind everyone to remove the paper before eating.

Consulate lychee and jasmine martini

Vodka shaken with jasmine tea, lychee syrup and lime, a whole lychee in the coupe.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Brew and cool the jasmine tea and drain the lychees up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; shake with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 jasmine tea bag
  • 120ml hot water
  • 1 tin lychees (565g)
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml lychee syrup (from the tin)
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Brew the tea bag in the hot water and cool.
  2. Drain the lychees, keeping the syrup.
  3. Shake the vodka, tea, lychee syrup and lime juice with ice.
  4. Strain into chilled coupes and drop a lychee into each.
