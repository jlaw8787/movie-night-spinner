Snacks

Pan-Pacific canapés

Blinis with smoked salmon and crème fraîche, cucumber cups with whipped goat's cheese

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 blinis
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 100g goat's cheese
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and dill.
  2. Cut the cucumber into thick rounds, scoop a small hollow in each and whip the goat's cheese smooth.
  3. Fill the cucumber cups with the cheese and lay everything on trays.

Federation approved potato tortillaVegetarian

Slow cooked potato and onion folded into eggs, cut into squares

Prep 15 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, serve warm or at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 250ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 6 eggs
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the potatoes and onion thin.
  2. Cook slowly in the olive oil until soft but not coloured.
  3. Drain, then fold into the beaten eggs and season.
  4. Cook in a pan over low heat until nearly set, flip with a plate and cook the other side. Cut into squares.

Rico's backyard paella

Chicken and chorizo paella with saffron rice and prawns

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Slice the chorizo and chop the onion and capsicum up to 1 day ahead; cook the paella on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh pieces
  • 150g chorizo
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 300g paella rice
  • 1l chicken stock
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 300g green prawns
  1. Slice the chorizo, dice the onion and capsicum, and crush the garlic. Heat the stock with the saffron.
  2. Brown the chicken and chorizo in a wide pan.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, capsicum and paprika and cook until soft.
  4. Stir in the rice and hot stock, then cook without stirring.
  5. Push the prawns into the rice for the last 5 minutes, then rest briefly before serving.

Sweets

Churros and chocolateVegetarian

Fried cinnamon sugar churros with a hot chocolate dip

Prep 13 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 hours ahead, fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 300ml full cream milk
  1. Boil the water with the butter, beat in the flour until a smooth dough forms, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Pipe strips into oil heated to 180C and fry in batches until golden.
  3. Toss the churros in the sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Melt the chocolate into the hot milk for dipping.

Paso doble sangriaVegetarian

Red wine sangria with brandy, orange juice and fruit, topped with soda

Prep 10 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add the soda just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 60ml brandy
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 1 orange
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 apple
  • 300ml soda water
  • ice, to serve
  1. Slice the orange, lemon and apple and mix in a jug with the wine, brandy and juice.
  2. Chill for 2 hours so the flavours come together.
  3. Top with the soda water and pour over ice.
