Snacks

Julie's bruschettaVegetarian

Toasted ciabatta rubbed with garlic, salted tomato and basil, made after a bad day at work

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Tomatoes up to 2 hours ahead, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 ciabatta, sliced
  • 1 garlic clove, halved
  • 4 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 small bunch basil, torn
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Dice the tomatoes, salt them and leave 10 minutes (included in cook time).
  2. Toast the ciabatta slices and rub with the cut garlic.
  3. Mix the tomatoes with basil and oil and pile on just before serving.

GougèresVegetarian

A French classic to warm up the kitchen

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and reheat, or freeze piped
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g gruyere, grated
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  1. Boil the water with the butter, beat in the flour and cook for a minute.
  2. Cool slightly, beat in the eggs one at a time, then the gruyere and nutmeg.
  3. Pipe small rounds onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden. Bon appetit.

Mains

365-day project boeuf bourguignon

Chuck and lardons braised in red wine, pearl onions, mushrooms, the one Julie sleeps through

Prep 40 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better made 1 day ahead, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 150g bacon lardons
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 750ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 thyme sprigs and 2 bay leaves
  • 12 pearl onions, peeled
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  1. Dry the beef and brown it in batches with the lardons.
  2. Soften the carrots, onion and garlic in the same pot.
  3. Return the meat, add the wine, stock, thyme and bay leaves and bring to a simmer.
  4. Braise covered at 160C for 3 hours until the beef is tender.
  5. Glaze the pearl onions and mushrooms in the butter and stir through at the end.

Sweets

Reine de SabaVegetarian

A classic French chocolate almond cake from the book

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep cool
Recipe for 4
  • 185g dark chocolate
  • 120g butter
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 60g almond meal
  • 30g plain flour
  1. Melt most of the chocolate with most of the butter and cool a little.
  2. Separate the eggs and beat the yolks with the sugar, add the chocolate, then fold in the almond meal and flour.
  3. Whip the whites and fold in.
  4. Bake in a lined tin at 180C, then cool.
  5. Melt the remaining chocolate with the remaining butter and glaze the cake.

Drinks

Burgundy kir with crème de cassisVegetarian

Chilled dry white wine over blackcurrant liqueur, a blackberry on top, for Julia's years in Paris

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the wine up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml dry white Burgundy or chardonnay
  • 60ml creme de cassis
  • 4 blackberries
  1. Chill the bottle for 2 hours (included in cook time).
  2. Pour the cassis into wine glasses, top slowly with the cold Burgundy or chardonnay so it blends from the bottom, and drop a blackberry in each. Serve before the bourguignon.
