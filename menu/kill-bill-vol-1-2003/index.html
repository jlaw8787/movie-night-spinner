Hattori Hanzo's sushi

Salmon and tuna nigiri with wasabi, served with pickled ginger

Prep 29 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150g sashimi salmon
  • 150g sashimi tuna
  • wasabi, to taste
  • pickled ginger, to serve
  1. Cook the sushi rice, season with the vinegar, sugar and salt and cool.
  2. Shape the rice into small fingers.
  3. Slice the salmon and tuna, top the rice with a dab of wasabi and a slice of fish.
  4. Serve on a board with pickled ginger.

Yellow tracksuit cornVegetarian

Charred corn brushed with miso butter, shichimi togarashi and spring onion

Prep 6 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the miso butter up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled; grill the corn fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 50g soft butter
  • 1 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tsp shichimi togarashi
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Grill the corn until charred all over.
  2. Mix the butter with the miso and brush over.
  3. Sprinkle with shichimi togarashi and sliced spring onion.

House of Blue Leaves crispy pork gyozaOn the printed menu

Pork and cabbage dumplings crisp underneath, chilli oil dip, before the showdown in the snow garden

How it is made
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 small wombok cabbage
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 packet gyoza wrappers
  • 1 bottle Chinese black vinegar
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Pork, cabbage, garlic and ginger dumplings pan-fried until crisp underneath and steamed tender, with a soy, black vinegar and chilli oil dip. Mix 400g pork mince with 150g finely shredded cabbage squeezed dry, 2 crushed garlic cloves, grated ginger, 2 chopped spring onions, 1 tbsp soy and 1 tsp sesame oil. Fill 32 gyoza wrappers and pleat. Fry in batches in an oiled pan for 2 minutes until brown underneath, add 80ml water, cover and steam 3 minutes until dry. Mix 3 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp black vinegar and 1 tsp chilli oil to dip.

Mains

House of Blue Leaves yakitori

Chicken thigh skewers grilled and brushed with soy and mirin glaze

Prep 18 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the tare up to 1 week ahead in the fridge and thread the skewers up to 1 day ahead; cook the rice and grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 80ml mirin
  • 60ml sake
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Simmer the soy, mirin, sake and sugar until syrupy for the tare.
  2. Dice the chicken and thread onto skewers, some with spring onion pieces.
  3. Cook the rice.
  4. Grill over high heat, brushing with tare.

O-Ren's spicy miso pork ramenOn the printed menu

Rich sesame broth, crisp chilli mince, springy noodles, jammy soy egg, corn and nori

How it is made
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle mirin
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1 jar chilli bean paste
  • 1.6L chicken stock
  • 1 tub white miso
  • 1 jar tahini
  • 4 portions fresh ramen noodles
  • 1 tin corn kernels
  • 200g bean sprouts
  • 1 packet nori
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Rich miso and sesame broth with spicy pork mince, springy ramen noodles, a jammy soy egg, corn, bean sprouts, nori and chilli oil. Boil 4 eggs for 6.5 minutes, chill and peel, then soak in 3 tbsp soy and 3 tbsp mirin for 2 hours. Fry 300g pork mince with garlic, ginger and 1 tbsp chilli bean paste until crisp. Heat 1.6L chicken stock and whisk in 4 tbsp white miso and 2 tbsp tahini. Cook 4 portions fresh ramen noodles for 2 minutes and divide into bowls. Ladle over broth and top with pork, halved eggs, corn, sprouts, nori and chilli oil.

Matcha ice creamVegetarian

Custard based ice cream flavoured with matcha, served cold

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min, then 10 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in the freezer
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp matcha powder
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 120g caster sugar
  1. Whisk the matcha into the warm milk and cream.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar, pour over and cook to 82°C.
  3. Strain and chill overnight.
  4. Churn, or freeze and stir every 30 minutes.

Hanzo's yuzu sake sourVegetarian

Sake, yuzu juice and honey shaken with egg white until frothy

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml junmai sake
  • 80ml yuzu juice
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Mix the honey with warm water for honey syrup.
  2. Shake the sake, yuzu juice, syrup and egg white hard without ice, one drink at a time.
  3. Add ice and shake again.
  4. Strain into chilled coupes and finish with a twist of lemon peel.
