Snacks

Lex's gala canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche and smoked salmon, figs with goat's cheese and honey

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 6 fresh figs
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Halve the figs and tear the salmon into small folds.
  2. Top each blini with crème fraîche and smoked salmon.
  3. Top each fig half with goat's cheese and a drizzle of honey.
  4. Arrange both on silver trays.

Smoked trout crispbreads

Rye crispbread with crème fraîche, smoked trout, pickled onion and dill

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet rye crispbread
  • 200g smoked trout
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Slice a few pickled onions into rings and pick the dill into sprigs.
  2. Spread the crispbreads with crème fraîche.
  3. Flake the trout over the top and finish with onion rings and dill.
  4. Serve cold on a slate.

Martha Kent's corn frittersOn the printed menu

Kernels and spring onion in paprika batter, shallow fried golden, sour cream, Smallville farm style

How it is made
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 3 corn cobs
  • 3 spring onions
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lime
  1. Corn kernels mixed with flour, egg and spring onion, fried in spoonfuls until golden, with sour cream. Mix 100g self-raising flour with 1 egg, 100ml milk, half a teaspoon smoked paprika and salt into a thick batter. Stir in the kernels from 3 corn cobs and 3 sliced spring onions. Heat 5mm of oil in a frying pan and drop in tablespoons of batter, cooking 2 minutes a side until golden. Drain on paper towel. Mix 200g sour cream with chopped chives and lime zest and serve with the hot fritters.

Metropolis gala beef Wellington

Beef fillet wrapped in mushrooms, prosciutto and puff pastry

Prep 25 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Wrap the Wellington up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled in the fridge; brush with egg and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 400g mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Chop the mushrooms very fine and crush the garlic.
  2. Season the beef with salt and pepper, sear it all over in hot oil, then brush with mustard and cool.
  3. Cook the mushrooms with garlic until dry.
  4. Lay prosciutto on a pastry sheet, spread with mushrooms and wrap around the beef, sealing with the second sheet. Brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 210°C.
  6. Rest before slicing.

Daily Planet chicken club sandwichesOn the printed menu

Grilled breast, crisp bacon, avocado and tomato stacked three high on toast, for Perry's newsroom

How it is made
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 bag potato crisps
  1. Club sandwiches of grilled chicken, crisp bacon, avocado and tomato on toasted bread with mayo, stacked three high and cut into triangles. Season 2 chicken breasts, grill 6 minutes a side and slice. Cook 8 rashers bacon until crisp. Toast 12 slices white bread. Spread 4 slices with mayo and layer with chicken, sliced tomato and lettuce. Add a second slice of toast, spread with mayo, and layer with bacon and sliced avocado. Top with a third slice, push 4 toothpicks through and cut each into 4 triangles. Serve with potato crisps.

LexCorp gala chocolate souffléVegetarian

Dark chocolate soufflés baked in ramekins

Prep 10 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Butter and sugar the ramekins and melt the chocolate with the yolks up to 4 hours ahead; whisk the whites, fold and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 4 eggs
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 20g butter
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Butter and sugar four ramekins and heat the oven to 190°C.
  2. Melt the chocolate and stir in three egg yolks.
  3. Whisk the four egg whites with sugar to soft peaks and fold into the chocolate.
  4. Fill the ramekins and bake.
  5. Serve straight away with cream.

Drinks

LexCorp gala pear and ginger fizzVegetarian

Pear nectar and ginger syrup with bubbles, served ice cold

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the ginger syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep it chilled; chill the flutes and top with sparkling wine to order
Recipe for 4
  • 100g fresh ginger
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 lemon
  • 250ml pear nectar
  • 400ml sparkling wine
  • 40g candied ginger
  1. Slice the ginger and simmer with the sugar and water, then strain and cool.
  2. Chill four flutes. Juice the lemon.
  3. Pour pear nectar, ginger syrup and lemon juice into each flute.
  4. Top with sparkling wine and stir once gently.
  5. Thread candied ginger onto picks and rest across each glass.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Finch finds the jar of peach tea on her desk at the hearing.
