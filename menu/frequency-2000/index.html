Snacks

Firehouse chilli bites

Beef chilli in baked wonton cups under melted cheddar, for Frank between call-outs

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 12 wonton wrappers
  • 1 tsp oil, plus extra to grease
  • 200g beef mince
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 200g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 120g tinned kidney beans, drained
  • 60g cheddar, grated
  1. Press the wonton wrappers into a lightly oiled mini muffin tin. Bake at 180°C until crisp.
  2. Brown the mince in oil, add the onion and cook until soft.
  3. Stir in the chilli powder, tomatoes and beans. Simmer until thick.
  4. Spoon into the baked cups, top with cheddar and return to the oven to melt.

Miracle Mets pretzels with beer mustardVegetarian

Glossy bicarb-bathed soft knots with coarse salt and a sharp beer and wholegrain dip

Prep 22 min, cook 18 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2l water for boiling
  • 60g bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp coarse salt
  • 100g wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tbsp lager
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm water and knead.
  2. Rise in a covered bowl until doubled.
  3. Shape into 8 pretzels.
  4. Boil each briefly in the water with the bicarb, then drain and sprinkle with coarse salt.
  5. Bake at 220°C until deep brown.
  6. Mix the mustard, lager and honey for the dip.

Queens pizza

Thin base, crushed tomato, mozzarella and crumbled sausage, baked fast, eaten by the ham radio

Prep 2.5 hours, cook 64 min, then 24 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough needs 1 to 2 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 380g bread flour
  • 2g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 245ml cool water
  • 200g Italian sausages
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 200g low-moisture mozzarella, grated
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and water and knead. Divide into two balls.
  2. Refrigerate covered to cold-ferment for a day or two.
  3. Bring to room temperature.
  4. Heat a pizza steel at the oven's maximum, about 250 to 275°C.
  5. Remove the sausage from its casings, crumble and pan-fry until cooked.
  6. Stretch the dough thin into two pizzas. Top with tomatoes, mozzarella and sausage.
  7. Bake each on the steel until crisp and cooked, then cut into wedges.

Julia's apple pieVegetarian

Granny Smiths and cinnamon in a double crust, egg washed, for a 1969 kitchen

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 600g Granny Smith apples
  • 85g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 15g plain flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Line a 20cm pie dish with half of the pastry.
  2. Peel, core and thinly slice the apples. Toss with sugar, cinnamon and flour.
  3. Fill the pastry, cover with the rest and crimp. Cut two steam vents, brush with egg and sprinkle with a little sugar.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden and the apples are tender.
  5. Rest before cutting into four portions.

Firehouse lemon shandyVegetarian

Cold lager topped with homemade lemonade and mint, for nine innings of the '69 Series

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Lemon mix up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100ml lemon juice
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 300ml cold water
  • 800ml cold lager
  • 400ml lemonade
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 1 lemon, cut into wheels
  1. Stir the lemon juice, sugar and water until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Chill 4 glasses in the freezer.
  3. Pour lager into each glass and top with lemonade. Add a splash of the lemon mix.
  4. Add a sprig of mint and a lemon wheel.

When to serve

Serve dinner during the 1969 World Series broadcast.
