Snacks

Secret Service smoked brisket sliders

Two years with Carter and four with Reagan

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the brisket up to 2 days ahead and keep it in its juices; warm and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g smoked beef brisket, thinly sliced
  • 8 soft milk buns
  • 125ml beef stock
  • 3 tbsp smoky barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 16 dill pickle slices
  1. Warm the stock and barbecue sauce in a wide pan, add the brisket and heat through gently.
  2. Split and toast the buns.
  3. Mix the mustard and mayonnaise and spread it on the bases.
  4. Pile on the brisket and pickles, put the lids on and serve hot.

Katana-cut seared tuna with ponzu

Frank's sword slices Rachel's scarf in two

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the ponzu up to 2 days ahead; sear and slice the tuna to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sashimi-grade tuna loin
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut in thin ribbons
  1. Stir the soy, lemon juice, rice vinegar and sugar together for the ponzu.
  2. Roll the tuna in the sesame seeds.
  3. Sear in the hot oil for 30 seconds a side so the middle stays raw, then rest a minute.
  4. Slice thinly with your sharpest knife and lay over the cucumber ribbons.
  5. Spoon over the ponzu and scatter with spring onion.

Mains

Miami benefit snapper with mango salsa

Rachel meets Portman at the Miami party

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the coconut rice up to a day ahead and reheat with a splash of water; cook the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 snapper fillets, skin on
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • 1 small red onion, finely diced
  • 1 long red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 cup coriander leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Rinse the rice and simmer it with the coconut milk, 200ml water and a pinch of salt, covered, until tender.
  2. Mix the mango, onion, chilli, lime juice and coriander for the salsa.
  3. Score the fish skin, season and fry skin-side down in the hot oil until crisp, then flip for a minute.
  4. Serve the snapper on the rice with the salsa on top.

Herb Farmer's lake cabin beef pot pie

Hiding out at Herb's cabin on the lake

Prep 25 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, cut in chunks
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 250g button mushrooms, halved
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Toss the beef in seasoned flour and brown it in batches in the oil.
  2. Soften the onion and carrots, stir in the tomato paste, then return the beef with the stock and thyme.
  3. Cover and simmer gently until the beef falls apart, adding the mushrooms for the last 30 minutes.
  4. Spoon into a pie dish, lay the pastry over, brush with egg and bake at 200C until puffed and golden.

Best Actress gold chocolate fondantVegetarian

And the Best Actress is Rachel Marron

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill the moulds up to a day ahead and chill; bake 2 minutes longer from cold
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g butter, plus extra for the moulds
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp edible gold dust
  • 200ml thickened cream, softly whipped
  1. Butter 4 moulds and dust them with cocoa.
  2. Melt the chocolate and butter together gently.
  3. Whisk the eggs, yolks and sugar until pale and thick, then fold in the chocolate and flour.
  4. Fill the moulds and bake at 200C until set on top but still soft in the middle.
  5. Turn out, dust with gold and serve with the cream.

The bodyguard's orange juice, straight or notVegetarian

Frank asks for orange juice on the job

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the oranges up to 4 hours ahead and chill; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 6 blood oranges, juiced
  • 180ml vodka
  • 300ml soda water, chilled
  • 4 orange slices
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in the vodka and blood orange juice.
  3. Top with soda, stir once and add an orange slice.
