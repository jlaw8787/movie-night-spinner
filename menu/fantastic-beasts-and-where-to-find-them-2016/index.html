Snacks

Cinnamon sugar puff pastry shapesVegetarian

Flaky layers cut into little animals, egg washed, spiced sugar on top, baked golden

Prep 17 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Cut the pastry shapes up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brush with egg, sugar and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Cut the pastry into little animal shapes with cutters or a knife.
  2. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle generously with cinnamon sugar.
  3. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden.
  4. Cool on a rack and arrange like a bakery window.

Smoked paprika devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs, no silver Occamy shells

Prep 22 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 300g green olives
  1. Boil the eggs until hard.
  2. Cool and peel.
  3. Halve the eggs and mash the yolks with mayonnaise and Dijon until smooth.
  4. Pipe back into the whites and dust with smoked paprika.
  5. Set out on the bar beside a bowl of green olives.

Mains

Polish pierogiVegetarian

Jacob grabs the wrong suitcase. Potato and cheddar dumplings

Prep 40 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Fill and pinch the pierogi up to 1 day ahead on a floured tray in the fridge; boil and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 100g butter
  • 300ml sour cream
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash and mix with the cheddar, seasoning with salt to taste.
  2. Slice the onions.
  3. Fill the wrappers and pinch closed.
  4. Boil the pierogi in batches until they float.
  5. Fry in butter with the onion until golden on both sides.
  6. Serve with sour cream.

Sweets

Queenie's apple strudelVegetarian

Strudel it is: filo, cinnamon apple and sultanas

Prep 34 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green apples, sliced
  • 80g sultanas
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
  • 8 sheets filo pastry
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Peel and slice the apples and toss with sultanas, cinnamon, sugar and breadcrumbs.
  2. Layer buttered filo sheets on a tray.
  3. Spread the filling along one edge and roll up.
  4. Brush with butter.
  5. Bake at 190C until golden.
  6. Cool slightly.
  7. Dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Speakeasy lemonadeVegetarian

Queenie reads your order first. Fresh lemon, syrup and soda

Prep 15 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml hot water
  • 6 lemons
  • 1l cold soda water
  • 4 cup ice
  • 100ml gin, for the grown-ups
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water.
  2. Cool the syrup.
  3. Squeeze the lemons into a jug and add the syrup.
  4. Add plenty of ice and top with soda water.
  5. Pour into teacups and add a splash of gin to the grown-ups' cups.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Queenie magics up the apple strudel.
