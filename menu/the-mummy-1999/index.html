Cairo bazaar falafelVegetarian

Crisp fried chickpea falafel with a lemon tahini drizzle

Prep 30 min, cook 750 min · Make ahead: Soak the chickpeas a day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dried chickpeas
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup parsley leaves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp water
  1. Soak the chickpeas in plenty of water overnight, then drain.
  2. Blitz the chickpeas with the onion, garlic, parsley and cumin until coarse. Shape into balls.
  3. Fry in oil at 180C in batches until deep golden.
  4. Whisk the tahini with lemon juice and water until smooth and drizzle over to serve.

Scarab date bitesVegetarian

Dates filled with almond butter, dipped in dark chocolate and pistachios

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 medjool dates
  • 5 tbsp almond butter
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 1/2 cup pistachios, crushed
  1. Slit the dates and remove the stones. Fill each with a teaspoon of almond butter and press closed.
  2. Dip halfway into melted dark chocolate and roll the chocolate end in crushed pistachios.
  3. Set on baking paper in the fridge.

Egyptian koshariVegetarian

Lentils, rice and macaroni with spicy tomato sauce, chickpeas and crispy onions

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 200g brown lentils
  • 200g long grain rice
  • 200g macaroni
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 400g chickpeas, drained
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying the onions
  1. Cook the lentils, rice and macaroni separately and drain.
  2. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic, chilli and vinegar into a sauce.
  3. Fry the onions in oil until dark and crisp. Drain on paper.
  4. Layer lentils, rice, pasta and chickpeas in bowls, then top with sauce and crisp onions.

Book of the Dead walnut honey pastriesVegetarian

Layered filo with cinnamon walnuts, soaked in lemon honey syrup

Prep 35 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, rests for several hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet filo pastry
  • 200g butter, melted
  • 250g walnuts, chopped
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 200g honey
  • 100ml water
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Layer filo in a tin, brushing every sheet with the melted butter and scattering walnuts mixed with cinnamon between every few layers.
  2. Cut into diamonds and bake at 180C for 40 minutes until golden.
  3. Warm the honey with the water and lemon juice and pour over the hot pastry.
  4. Leave for several hours to soak.

Desert ruby hibiscus and lime coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled hibiscus tea with ginger and cinnamon over ice, with lime

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 30g dried hibiscus flowers
  • 1l just-boiled water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 4 ginger slices
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 limes
  • 4 mint sprigs
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the hibiscus in the hot water with the sugar, ginger and cinnamon.
  2. Strain into a jug and chill well.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice, pour in the hibiscus and add a good squeeze of lime to each. Finish with mint sprigs.
