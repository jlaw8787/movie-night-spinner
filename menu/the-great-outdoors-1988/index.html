Snacks

Lakeside crumbed fish bites with tartare

Panko crumbed flathead bites with homemade tartare sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Crumb the fish up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled, and mix the tartare up to 2 days ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g flathead fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill pickles
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 2 lemons
  1. Cut the flathead into bite-size pieces.
  2. Toss in flour, dip in egg, then coat in panko.
  3. Shallow fry in the oil at 180C, turning once, until golden. Drain.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with the capers, pickles, parsley and a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Serve hot with the tartare and lemon wedges.

Chet's Wisconsin fried cheese curdsVegetarian

Squeaky nuggets in soda water batter, fried golden, hot with ranch, safe from the bin raccoons

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cheese curds or mild cheddar, cubed
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml cold soda water
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150ml ranch dressing
  1. Whisk the flour, paprika and salt with the egg and soda water into a smooth batter.
  2. Heat the oil to 180C.
  3. Dip the cheese in the batter and fry in batches for 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
  4. Drain and serve hot with the ranch.

The Old 96er rump steak

Big rump steak with garlic butter, onion rings, roast potatoes, ranch wedge

Prep 20 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Rub the steak with oil and salt up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; bring it to room temperature, then roast the potatoes and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg rump steak
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  • 60g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 400g frozen onion rings
  • 1 iceberg lettuce, cut into wedges
  • 150ml ranch dressing
  1. Rub the steak with oil and salt. Toss the potatoes in oil.
  2. Roast the potatoes at 220C.
  3. Grill the steak over high heat for medium rare.
  4. Rest the steak. Bake the onion rings.
  5. Melt the butter with the garlic. Drizzle the lettuce wedges with ranch.
  6. Slice the steak thickly, pour over the garlic butter and serve.

Roman's big shot barbecue chicken with potato salad

Marylands brushed with smoky sauce and grilled sticky over the coals, creamy dill spuds alongside

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the potato salad up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken marylands
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 800g chat potatoes
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp sour cream
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 bunch dill
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and cool a little.
  2. Toss the potatoes with the mayonnaise, sour cream, spring onion, chopped dill and salt.
  3. Rub the chicken with paprika and salt and grill over medium coals, turning, until nearly cooked.
  4. Brush with barbecue sauce and grill until sticky. Serve with the potato salad.

Bald-headed bear blueberry pieVegetarian

Blueberry pie with a lemon scented filling and pastry crust

Prep 20 min, cook 77 min · Make ahead: Bake and cool the pie up to 1 day ahead, covered at room temperature; serve with cold ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen blueberries
  • 135g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1l vanilla ice cream, to serve
  1. Toss the blueberries with most of the sugar, cornflour and the lemon zest and juice.
  2. Line a 23cm pie dish with one pastry sheet and fill with the berries. Cover with the second sheet, crimp the edges and cut slits on top.
  3. Brush with egg and sprinkle with the remaining sugar.
  4. Bake at 200C, then at 180C until bubbling.
  5. Cool, then serve with ice cream.

Lake cabin campfire s'mores

Toasted marshmallow, chocolate and caramel sandwiched between biscuits

Prep 10 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best built while the marshmallows are warm; lay out the biscuits and chocolate on the tray up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 24 plain sweet wheatmeal biscuits
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 4 tbsp caramel sauce
  • 16 large marshmallows
  1. Lay half the biscuits on a lined tray. Top some with milk chocolate and the rest with dark chocolate and a little caramel.
  2. Thread the marshmallows onto skewers and toast over a fire or gas flame until golden, or grill on a tray.
  3. Put marshmallows on each chocolate biscuit and press another biscuit on top. Eat while warm.

Chet's waterski root beer floatsVegetarianNo alcohol

Root beer over vanilla ice cream with a cocktail cherry

Prep 5 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Chill the mugs in the freezer and the root beer in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; scoop and pour just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 1/4l cold root beer
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Chill large mugs in the freezer.
  2. Add ice cream to each mug.
  3. Slowly pour root beer down the side until it foams to the top, letting it settle and topping up.
  4. Add a cherry and serve with straws and long spoons.
