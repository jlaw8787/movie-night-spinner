Fried bread with golden syrupVegetarian

Puffy fried bread squares served warm with butter and golden syrup

Prep 25 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 325ml warm water
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 60g butter, to serve
  • 125ml golden syrup
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm water into a soft dough and knead briefly.
  2. Prove the dough until doubled, which is the resting time.
  3. Roll out 2cm thick and cut into 12 squares.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil to 175C and fry a few at a time until puffed and golden on both sides.
  5. Serve warm with butter and golden syrup.

Rocky's special powers corn frittersVegetarian

Creamed corn and spring onion fritters fried crisp

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, reheat in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 420g creamed corn
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 spring onions
  • 100ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Mix the creamed corn, kernels, flour, eggs and sliced spring onion.
  2. Fry heaped tablespoons in oil until golden on both sides, in batches.
  3. Drain and serve hot with sweet chilli sauce.

Mains

Shogun's homecoming hāngī-style pork

Pork shoulder, kūmara, pumpkin and potato roasted under cabbage in foil

Prep 30 min, cook 245 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat covered
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 600g kumara
  • 600g butternut pumpkin
  • 400g potatoes
  • 1 cabbage
  • 1 roll foil, to wrap
  1. Season the pork well. Cut the kumara, pumpkin and potatoes into chunks.
  2. Line a deep roasting tin with cabbage leaves and add the pork and vegetables. Cover with more cabbage and wrap tightly in foil.
  3. Roast at 160C until the pork falls apart.
  4. Rest and shred the pork, then serve with the vegetables.

Hokey pokey ice creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream folded through crunchy honeycomb toffee

Prep 10 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Fold through and refreeze up to 1 week ahead; the honeycomb keeps 3 days airtight if made separately
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1l vanilla ice cream, slightly softened
  1. Heat the sugar and golden syrup until bubbling and golden.
  2. Take off the heat, whisk in the bicarbonate of soda and pour onto baking paper to set.
  3. Smash into small pieces and fold through the softened ice cream.
  4. Refreeze until firm.

Crazy Horses dairy lime milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Milk, vanilla ice cream and lime cordial blended frothy

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Blend fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk, cold
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp lime cordial
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream and lime cordial until frothy.
  2. Pour into 4 tall glasses and serve straight away with straws.
