Fried bread with golden syrupVegetarian

Puffy fried bread squares served warm with butter and golden syrup

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 325ml warm water
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 60g butter, to serve
  • 125ml golden syrup
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm water into a soft dough and knead briefly.
  2. Prove the dough until doubled, which is the resting time.
  3. Roll out 2cm thick and cut into 12 squares.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil to 175C and fry a few at a time until puffed and golden on both sides.
  5. Serve warm with butter and golden syrup.

Rocky's special powers corn frittersVegetarian

Creamed corn and spring onion fritters fried crisp

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, reheat in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 420g creamed corn
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 spring onions
  • 100ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Mix the creamed corn, kernels, flour, eggs and sliced spring onion.
  2. Fry heaped tablespoons in oil until golden on both sides, in batches.
  3. Drain and serve hot with sweet chilli sauce.

Fish shop hot chips with garlic aioliOn the printed menu

The fish and chip shop down the road from Waihau Bay

How it is made
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes
  • 3L vegetable oil
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • tomato sauce
  1. Thick-cut chips fried twice until crunchy outside and fluffy inside, salted hot, wrapped in paper with a garlicky aioli and tomato sauce. Cut 1.2kg floury potatoes into 1.5cm chips, soak in cold water for 30 minutes and pat dry. Heat oil to 150C and fry in batches for 6 minutes until soft but pale. Drain and rest 15 minutes. Heat the oil to 190C and fry again for 3 minutes until golden and crunchy. Salt straight away. Mix 150g mayo with 2 crushed garlic cloves and a squeeze of lemon. Serve in paper with the aioli and tomato sauce.

Shogun's homecoming hāngī-style pork

Pork shoulder, kūmara, pumpkin and potato roasted under cabbage in foil

Prep 20 min, cook 4.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat covered
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 600g kumara
  • 600g butternut pumpkin
  • 400g potatoes
  • 1 cabbage
  • 1 roll foil, to wrap
  1. Season the pork well. Cut the kumara, pumpkin and potatoes into chunks.
  2. Line a deep roasting tin with cabbage leaves and add the pork and vegetables. Cover with more cabbage and wrap tightly in foil.
  3. Roast at 160C until the pork falls apart.
  4. Rest and shred the pork, then serve with the vegetables.

Nan's savoury mince on buttered toastOn the printed menu

What's in the pot at Nan's house

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • tomato sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 loaf thick white bread
  • butter
  • 80g tasty cheese
  1. Beef mince simmered with onion, carrot, peas and a rich gravy until thick, spooned over thick buttered toast with grated tasty cheese on top. Brown 600g beef mince in a large pan, breaking it up. Add 1 diced onion and 1 grated carrot and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and 400ml beef stock. Simmer 15 minutes until thick, adding 150g frozen peas for the last 5. Toast 8 thick slices of white bread and butter well. Spoon the mince over and top with 80g grated tasty cheese.

Hokey pokey ice creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream folded through crunchy honeycomb toffee

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Fold through and refreeze up to 1 week ahead; the honeycomb keeps 3 days airtight if made separately
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1l vanilla ice cream, slightly softened
  1. Heat the sugar and golden syrup until bubbling and golden.
  2. Take off the heat, whisk in the bicarbonate of soda and pour onto baking paper to set.
  3. Smash into small pieces and fold through the softened ice cream.
  4. Refreeze until firm.

Milk, vanilla ice cream and lime cordial blended frothy

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Blend fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk, cold
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp lime cordial
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream and lime cordial until frothy.
  2. Pour into 4 tall glasses and serve straight away with straws.
