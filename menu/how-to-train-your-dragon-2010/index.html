Snacks

Toothless's fish bites

Panko fish strips and lemon dill dip, if Toothless leaves any.

Prep 14 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Crumb the fish strips up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; fry just before serving. Mix the dill yoghurt up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g white fish fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Cut the white fish fillets into strips.
  2. Dip in the flour, the beaten eggs and the panko, and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C for 3 minutes until golden.
  3. Mix the Greek yoghurt with lemon and chopped dill and serve with lemon wedges.

Gobber's Lesson Two smoked sausages

Charred smoked sausage with mustard. Douse the correct end.

Prep 4 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; nothing worth doing ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g smoked sausages
  • 4 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Cook the thick smoked sausages over high heat for 8 minutes, turning, until charred and split.
  2. Cut into chunks and serve on a board with the wholegrain mustard for dipping.

Stoick's pan bread with honey butter

Crusty seeded loaf baked in blazing cast iron, torn at the table, whipped sea salt butter.

How it is made
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 4 tbsp mixed seeds
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • sea salt flakes
  1. Crusty seeded bread baked in a blazing hot pan, torn at the table and slathered with whipped honey and sea salt butter. Mix 500g bread flour, 7g yeast, 10g salt, 2 tbsp mixed seeds and 350ml warm water and knead 8 minutes. Rest 1 hour. Shape into a round and put in an oiled 26cm ovenproof frying pan, slash the top, brush with water and scatter more seeds. Rest 30 minutes. Bake at 230°C for 30 minutes until it sounds hollow. Beat 125g soft butter with 2 tbsp honey and flaky salt and serve with the warm bread.

Mains

Viking lamb stew

Lamb shoulder, carrot, parsnip and pearl barley in beef stock.

Prep 13 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Cook up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; the barley thickens, so loosen with a splash of water when reheating
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb shoulder
  • 1 onion
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 parsnips
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1.25 L beef stock
  1. Brown the diced lamb shoulder, then add the onion, the carrots and the parsnips in chunks, the pearl barley, thyme and the beef stock.
  2. Cover and simmer 2 hours until the lamb is tender and the barley is plump.
  3. Serve in big bowls with bread.

Night Fury fire-roasted chicken legs

Marylands with garlic and rosemary, roasted charred with smoky honey.

How it is made
  • 4 chicken marylands
  • vegetable oil
  • smoked paprika
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 6 carrots
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • cider vinegar
  1. Chicken marylands rubbed with smoked paprika, garlic and rosemary, roasted until charred, then lacquered with smoky honey and served with roast carrots. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub 4 chicken marylands with 2 tbsp oil, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 crushed garlic cloves, chopped rosemary and salt. Roast on a tray with 6 halved carrots for 40 minutes. Warm 3 tbsp honey with half a tsp smoked paprika and 1 tbsp cider vinegar. Brush over the chicken and roast 10 minutes more until sticky and charred at the edges. Rest 5 minutes and spoon the pan juices over.

Sweets

Honey cakesVegetarian

Soft cinnamon cakes baked in muffin cases and brushed with warm honey.

Prep 10 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead, airtight; brush with warm honey on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 170g honey
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 80ml milk
  1. Beat the butter with the honey, add the eggs, then fold in the self-raising flour, the cinnamon and the milk.
  2. Spoon into muffin cases and bake at 175°C for 18 minutes.
  3. Brush with the warm honey while hot.

Drinks

Meridian of Misery hot spiced berry punchNo alcohol

Apple juice simmered with berries, cinnamon, star anise and orange.

Prep 10 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Simmer and strain the punch up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat and add the blueberries to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen mixed berries
  • 1 L cloudy apple juice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 star anise
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 punnet blueberries
  1. Put the frozen mixed berries, the cloudy apple juice, the cinnamon stick, the star anise, the strips of orange peel and the honey in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook 10 minutes, pressing the berries.
  3. Strain through a sieve into a jug.
  4. Pour into mugs and drop in a few fresh blueberries.
  5. Serve hot, or chill and pour over ice in summer.
