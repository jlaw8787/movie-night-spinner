Snacks

Spicoli's pizza bites

Mini pizza rounds with sauce, mozzarella and pepperoni.

Prep 20 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Cut and top the rounds up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 16 slices of pepperoni
  1. Roll out the dough and cut into 16 rounds.
  2. Spread with sauce and top with mozzarella and a slice of pepperoni.
  3. Bake at 230°C until bubbling and golden. Serve in a pizza box, 8 min.

Food court pretzels with cheddar sauceVegetarian

Soft pretzels with warm cheddar sauce and yellow mustard.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the cheddar sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and reheat gently with a splash of milk; warm the pretzels just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 soft pretzels
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp rock salt
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 tsp mustard powder
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  1. Brush the pretzels with butter and sprinkle with salt.
  2. Warm in the oven at 200°C, 5 min.
  3. Heat the evaporated milk until steaming, take off the heat and stir in the cheddar and mustard powder until smooth, 7 min.
  4. Pour into paper cups and serve with the pretzels and a squeeze of mustard.

Captain Hook cod bites and fries

Cod strips in crisp batter with skin-on fries, tartare and lemon.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 375ml beer
  • 500g cod fillets
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 jar gherkins
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 lemons
  1. Cod strips in crisp beer batter with skin-on chips, chunky tartare sauce and lemon, served in paper cones like the shop on the strip. Cut 1kg potatoes into chips, fry at 140°C for 6 minutes, drain, then fry again at 190°C for 3 minutes. Mix 200g plain flour with 1 tsp baking powder, salt and 300ml cold beer. Cut 500g cod fillets into strips, dust in flour, dip in batter and fry at 180°C for 3 minutes. Mix 150g mayonnaise with chopped gherkins, capers, dill and lemon juice. Serve in paper cones with lemon wedges.

Mains

All-American Burger

Cheddar cheeseburger on a sesame bun with lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles and fries.

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and cut the chips up to 4 hours ahead in cold water; cook everything just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck mince
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 4 sesame burger buns
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips and dry well.
  2. Shape the mince into patties and salt them.
  3. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil once at a lower heat, then again at a higher heat until golden. Salt well, 20 min.
  4. Grill the patties to medium and melt the cheddar on top, 8 min.
  5. Toast the buns and build with lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles, 7 min.

Spicoli's classroom pepperoni pizza

Big New York base, tangy tomato, mozzarella, cupped pepperoni, mushrooms, chilli flakes, oregano.

How it is made
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 120g pepperoni
  • 160g button mushrooms
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  1. A big New York style pie with tangy tomato sauce, stretchy mozzarella, cupped pepperoni and mushrooms, finished with chilli flakes, oregano and parmesan. Put a pizza steel or stone in the oven at the highest setting for 45 minutes. Stretch 2 balls of pizza dough (250g each) into thin 30cm rounds. Spread each with 100g crushed tomato seasoned with salt, oregano and garlic. Top with 125g grated mozzarella, 60g sliced pepperoni and 80g sliced mushrooms. Bake each for 7 to 8 minutes until blistered. Finish with grated parmesan, oregano and chilli flakes and cut into big slices.

Food court chocolate chip cookiesVegetarian

Big cookies with dark chocolate chips, chilled overnight.

Prep 15 min, cook 26 min, 11 hr waiting · Make ahead: Dough rests overnight. Baked cookies keep 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 200g dark choc chips
  1. Cook the butter until nutty and golden, then cool, 15 min.
  2. Beat with the sugar, egg and vanilla.
  3. Mix in the flour, bicarb, salt and choc chips.
  4. Cover and chill overnight.
  5. Scoop big balls onto lined trays.
  6. Bake at 180°C, 11 min.
  7. Cool on the tray, 10 min.

Mall strawberry shakeNo alcohol

Strawberry, milk and vanilla blended thick with ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Hull the strawberries up to 1 day ahead; blend with the ice just before serving or it melts thin
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries, hulled
  • 500ml milk
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Hull the strawberries.
  2. Blend with the milk, sugar, vanilla and ice until frothy and thick.
  3. Pour into tall glasses straight away.
