Snacks

Atomic Comics popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in butter, grated parmesan and cracked pepper.

Prep 1 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Heat the oil with 3 kernels, covered, until they pop, then add the rest and shake until the popping slows.
  2. Toss with the melted butter, parmesan and cracked pepper.
  3. Fill four paper bags.

D'Amico's prosciutto melon bites

Cantaloupe cubes wrapped in prosciutto with basil, balsamic and olive oil.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1/2 rockmelon
  • 10 slices of prosciutto
  • 20 small basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp aged balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Peel and seed the rockmelon and cut into 20 cubes.
  2. Tear the prosciutto in half lengthways.
  3. Wrap each cube in prosciutto with a basil leaf tucked inside and spear with a pick.
  4. Arrange on a platter, drizzle with balsamic and olive oil and finish with pepper.

Bodega bacon, egg and cheese rolls

Bacon, fried egg and melted cheese in a soft roll. Dave's breakfast roll, Mister Bitey gets none.

How it is made
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 6 eggs
  • 20g butter
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft rolls
  • hot sauce
  1. Soft rolls stuffed with folded fried egg, crisp bacon and melted American cheese, salt, pepper and hot sauce, wrapped in foil like the corner store does it. Fry 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Whisk 6 eggs with salt and pepper and cook as a thin omelette in 20g butter, folding it into 4 squares. Lay 2 slices American cheese on each square to melt. Split and toast 4 soft rolls, add the egg and cheese, then the bacon and a good shake of hot sauce. Wrap each in foil for 2 minutes so everything melts together.

Mains

Bowling alley burger

Two smashed beef patties with pickles and special sauce, twice-fried chips.

Prep 17 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead and roll the mince into balls up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; fry the chips and smash the patties fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sweet relish
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, relish and mustard for the sauce. Cut the potatoes into chips.
  2. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil at 140°C, then again at 185°C until crisp, and salt.
  3. Roll the mince into eight balls and smash thin on a smoking griddle, 5 min.
  4. Top with cheese and stack two per toasted bun with pickles and sauce.

Atomic Comics New York pepperoni slice

Big thin-crust slices, garlicky tomato, mozzarella and crisp pepperoni, chilli flakes and parmesan.

How it is made
  • 500g bread flour
  • 2g dried yeast
  • 300ml passata
  • 1 garlic clove
  • dried oregano
  • 400g low-moisture mozzarella
  • 150g sliced pepperoni
  • chilli flakes
  • 40g parmesan
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Big thin-crust New York slices with garlicky tomato sauce, mozzarella and crisp pepperoni, folded to eat, with chilli flakes and parmesan. Mix 500g bread flour, 325ml water, 2g yeast, 10g salt and 1 tbsp oil and refrigerate 24 hours. Divide into 2 balls and rest at room temperature for 2 hours. Heat the oven with a pizza steel at its highest setting for 45 minutes. Stretch each into a 35cm base, spread with 150ml passata mixed with garlic and oregano, top with 200g grated mozzarella and pepperoni. Bake 7 minutes. Cut into big slices and serve with chilli flakes and parmesan.

Sweets

Mindy's hot fudge sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla and chocolate ice cream with hot fudge, whipped cream, sprinkles and a cherry.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm; whip the cream and scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml chocolate ice cream
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream into a hot fudge, 6 min.
  2. Whip the remaining cream.
  3. Layer scoops of both ice creams in four tall glasses with the fudge.
  4. Finish with whipped cream, sprinkles and a cherry.

Drinks

Hit-Girl's purple grape ginger fizzNo alcohol

Grape and fresh ginger juice with lime and soda over frozen grapes.

Prep 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Grapes freeze ahead, mix up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g red grapes
  • 1 piece ginger (2 cm)
  • 500ml grape juice
  • 1 lime
  • 250ml soda water
  1. Freeze the grapes, 2 hr.
  2. Grate the ginger and squeeze the juice through a sieve into a jug.
  3. Add the grape juice and lime juice and stir.
  4. Fill small tumblers with the frozen grapes, pour in the mix and top with soda water.

When to serve
