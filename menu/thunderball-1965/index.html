Nassau prawn fritters

Bahamian fritters from the dock market, made with prawns

Prep 14 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Dice the vegetables and mix the dipping sauce up to 1 day ahead; mix the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g raw prawns, peeled and deveined
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml milk
  • 1/2 green capsicum, finely diced
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 small red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot chilli sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Chop the prawns into small pieces.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt, egg and milk into a thick batter.
  3. Stir in the prawns, capsicum, onion, celery, chilli and thyme.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, chilli sauce and a squeeze of the lime for the dipping sauce.
  5. Heat the oil to 180°C in a deep heavy pan.
  6. Fry heaped spoonfuls in batches for 3 to 4 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked through. Drain on paper towel.
  7. Serve hot with the dipping sauce and the rest of the lime in wedges.

Shrublands health farm cruditésVegetarian

Carrot, cucumber, celery, capsicum and radishes on ice, green herb yoghurt dip, Bond's clinic diet

Prep 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Blitz and chill the dip for at least 30 minutes, up to 2 days ahead; cut the vegetables up to 4 hours ahead and keep them in iced water
Recipe for 4
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1/2 bunch basil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 garlic clove
  • salt, to taste
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 4 celery sticks
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 bunch radishes
  1. Blitz the yoghurt, mayonnaise, parsley, chives, basil leaves, lemon juice, garlic and salt until green and smooth.
  2. Chill the dip for 30 minutes.
  3. Cut the carrots, cucumbers, celery and capsicum into sticks, trim the radishes and crisp in iced water for 10 minutes.
  4. Drain well and arrange around the dip.

Palmyra grilled lobster tails with Bahamian baked macaroni

Grilled lobster tails with Bahamian baked macaroni, on Largo's terrace above the shark pool

Prep 11 min, cook 79 min · Make ahead: Bake the macaroni up to 1 day ahead and reheat covered at 170°C; make the garlic butter ahead and grill the lobster just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g macaroni
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml tin evaporated milk
  • 250g tasty cheese, grated
  • 1/2 green capsicum, finely diced
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • cayenne pepper, a pinch
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 20g butter, for the macaroni
  • 80g butter, for the garlic butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 lime
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 4 raw lobster tails, or 8 Moreton Bay bugs
  • 1 tbsp parsley, chopped
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Cook the macaroni in salted water for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain.
  2. Whisk the eggs, evaporated milk, 200g of the cheese, the capsicum, onion, cayenne, salt, pepper and melted butter for the macaroni. Stir in the macaroni, tip into a baking dish and top with the rest of the cheese.
  3. Bake for 40 minutes until set and golden, then rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Melt the butter for the garlic butter with the garlic, the zest and juice of the lime and the paprika.
  5. Split the lobster tails lengthways through the shell with a heavy knife and brush the flesh with the garlic butter.
  6. Grill flesh side up under a hot grill or on a barbecue for 6 to 8 minutes, basting, until just opaque.
  7. Scatter with parsley and serve with the macaroni.

Junkanoo stew chicken with peas and rice

Browned in dark caramel with thyme, allspice and tomato, pigeon pea pilaf, for the parade chase

Prep 5 min, cook 73 min · Make ahead: Stew up to 2 days ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 green capsicum, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400g tin pigeon peas or kidney beans, drained
  • 2 ripe plantains or firm bananas
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Melt the sugar in 1 tbsp oil until dark, then brown the chicken in it.
  2. Add the onion, capsicum, garlic, allspice, thyme, tomato paste and 300ml water and simmer covered until tender.
  3. Cook the rice with the peas, 600ml water and salt, covered, until tender.
  4. Fry the sliced plantain in the rest of the oil and serve with the chicken and rice.

Nassau guava duff with rum butter sauceVegetarian

Soft dough rolled round spiced pink fruit paste, steamed, under warm brown sugar and cream

Prep 11 min, cook 99 min · Make ahead: Steam up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled in its foil and resteam for 20 minutes; make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour, plus extra for rolling
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • salt, a pinch
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 60g cold butter, diced, plus extra for the paper
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml milk
  • 250g guava paste
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 80g butter, for the sauce
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp dark rum
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar, then stir in the egg and milk to a soft dough.
  2. Roll out on floured baking paper to a 25 x 30cm rectangle.
  3. Warm the guava paste with the water, cinnamon and nutmeg until spreadable, then spread over the dough leaving a 2cm border.
  4. Roll up from a long side, wrap loosely in buttered baking paper, then foil, and twist the ends.
  5. Steam on a rack in a covered pot over simmering water for 75 minutes, topping up the water.
  6. For the sauce, melt the butter and brown sugar, stir in the cream and bubble for 2 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the rum.
  7. Rest the duff for 5 minutes, unwrap, slice thickly and pour over the sauce.

Disco Volante goombay smashVegetarian

Rum and pineapple on the aft deck of Largo's yacht

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Mix the coconut rum and juices up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; float the dark rum to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml coconut rum
  • 360ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml orange juice
  • 30ml lime juice
  • 3 cups ice
  • 120ml dark rum
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Stir the coconut rum, pineapple, orange and lime juices together in a jug.
  2. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice and pour in the mix.
  3. Float 30ml of the dark rum on top of each over the back of a spoon.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
