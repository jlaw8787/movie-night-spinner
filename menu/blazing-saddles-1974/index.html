Waco Kid's whiskey nutsVegetarian

Peanuts and pecans roasted with bourbon, brown sugar and smoked paprika

Prep 5 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 30ml bourbon
  • 200g peanuts
  • 150g pecans
  1. Melt the butter with the sugar, paprika and bourbon.
  2. Toss with the peanuts and pecans on a tray.
  3. Roast at 160°C, stirring once.
  4. Cool on the tray.

Rock Ridge pan cornbreadVegetarian

Cornbread baked in a hot iron pan, with chilli butter

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Mix the chilli butter up to 3 days ahead; the cornbread is best baked on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g fine polenta
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 100g butter
  • 1 long red chilli, finely chopped
  1. Heat the oven to 210°C with a 25cm cast iron pan inside.
  2. Mix the polenta, flour, baking powder and salt. Whisk the eggs into the buttermilk with 60g of the butter, melted, then stir into the dry mix.
  3. Take out the hot pan, swirl in a knob of butter and pour in the batter.
  4. Bake 20 to 22 minutes until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Mash the rest of the butter with the chilli and a pinch of salt, and serve the cornbread in wedges with it.

Campfire smoky bacon baked beans

Pinto beans simmered with bacon, molasses, mustard and smoked paprika

Prep 8 min, cook 78 min · Make ahead: Make the beans up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat; bake the cornbread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 tins pinto beans
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250ml water
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 80g butter
  1. Dice the bacon and onion. Drain the beans.
  2. Fry the bacon and onion in a heavy pot until golden.
  3. Add the beans, tomatoes, molasses, mustard, paprika and water and simmer until thick.
  4. For the cornbread, mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, buttermilk, egg and melted butter.
  5. Pour into a hot buttered pan and bake at 220°C.

Chuck wagon pepper rib eyesOn the printed menu

Salt crusted and seared over coals, butter-fried potatoes and onions, for the railroad crew

How it is made
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 1 jar cracked black pepper
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 2 brown onions
  1. Rib eyes crusted with salt and cracked pepper, seared in cast iron over hot coals, with potatoes and onions fried in the butter and steak juices. Season 4 rib eyes of 300g heavily with salt and cracked pepper and leave 30 minutes. Boil 800g halved small potatoes for 10 minutes and drain. Sear the steaks in a smoking cast iron pan 3 to 4 minutes a side for medium-rare and rest 8 minutes. Add 50g butter and 2 sliced onions to the pan, then the potatoes, and fry 12 minutes until golden. Pour the resting juices over and serve.

Rock Ridge welcome pieVegetarian

Cinnamon apple filling in small shortcrust pies with pastry lids

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 Granny Smith apples
  • 30g butter
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Dice the apples and cook with the butter, sugar and cinnamon until soft. Cool.
  2. Cut rounds from the pastry, line a 12-hole tin, fill and lid with rounds from the third sheet.
  3. Egg wash and bake at 200°C.

Frontier cobblerVegetarian

Peaches and blackberries under a soft biscuit topping, sprinkled with raw sugar

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 4 peaches
  • 250g blackberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 80g butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp raw sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Slice the peaches and toss with the blackberries, sugar and cornflour in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, add the milk and drop over the fruit.
  3. Sprinkle with raw sugar and bake at 190°C.
  4. Serve with cream.

Candygram salted caramel trufflesOn the printed menu

Dark chocolate ganache rolled in cocoa and crushed honeycomb, boxed with a bow, no fuse

How it is made
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 1 tin caramel
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 tin cocoa powder
  • 60g honeycomb
  1. Dark chocolate and salted caramel ganache rolled into truffles, coated in cocoa and crushed honeycomb, packed in a little gift box with a bow. Heat 150ml cream until steaming and pour over 250g chopped dark chocolate. Stir until smooth, then mix in 3 tbsp thick caramel and a good pinch of sea salt. Chill 3 hours until firm. Roll into 24 balls with cool hands, then coat half in cocoa and half in 60g crushed honeycomb. Pack into a small box lined with baking paper and tie with a bow.

Drinks

Saloon sarsaparilla spider floatVegetarian

Sarsaparilla poured over vanilla ice cream, with a dash of bitters

Prep 8 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the sarsaparilla up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; scoop, add the bitters and pour just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 375ml cans sarsaparilla
  • 4 big scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 120ml bourbon
  1. Chill the sarsaparilla.
  2. Put a big scoop of ice cream into each of four tall glasses.
  3. Pour the sarsaparilla slowly down the side so it foams up over the ice cream.
  4. Add a dash of bitters to each. For the cowboys, add bourbon before pouring. Serve with long spoons and straws.

When to serve

Serve the beans when the cowboys sit around the campfire.
