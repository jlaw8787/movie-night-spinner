Snacks

Nigel's large bread salami and provolone rolls

Big soft rolls so nothing needs folding. The miniature bread backstage won't do.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the olive mayo up to 2 days ahead; build the rolls to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large soft white rolls
  • 200g sliced hot salami
  • 150g sliced provolone
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp green olives, finely chopped
  • 1 cup rocket leaves
  • 1 roasted red capsicum from a jar, sliced
  1. Stir the chopped olives through the mayo.
  2. Split the rolls and spread both sides with the olive mayo.
  3. Lay the salami flat, no folding, then the provolone, capsicum and rocket.
  4. Close, press down and cut in half.

Crumbed olives with the pimentos left inVegetarian

Pimento-stuffed green olives, crumbed and fried crisp.

Prep 4 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Crumb the olives up to a day ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pimento-stuffed green olives, drained and patted dry
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 80g panko breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  1. Roll the olives in flour, then egg, then the panko mixed with parmesan.
  2. Fry in hot oil in batches until golden, about 2 minutes.
  3. Stir lemon zest and juice through the yoghurt and serve with the hot olives.

Mains

None more black pastrami on dark rye

Peppered pastrami, Swiss and pickles on rye. Smell the Glove comes out none more black.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the Russian dressing up to 3 days ahead; steam the pastrami and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sliced beef pastrami
  • 8 slices of dark rye bread
  • 8 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 1 cup dill pickles, sliced
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish cream
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Mix the mayo, tomato sauce and horseradish into a Russian dressing.
  2. Steam the pastrami in a covered pan with a splash of water until hot and soft.
  3. Spread the rye with mustard and dressing, pile on the pastrami and crack over pepper.
  4. Add the Swiss, toast the sandwiches in a pan until the cheese melts, and serve with pickles.

Shark Sandwich battered flake rolls

Crisp battered flake in a soft roll with tartare.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare up to 2 days ahead; batter and fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g flake (gummy shark) fillets, cut in 4
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 250ml chilled soda water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 soft long rolls
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 tbsp gherkins, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  1. Stir the capers, gherkins and a squeeze of lemon through the mayo.
  2. Whisk the flour, soda water and a pinch of salt into a smooth batter.
  3. Dip the flake and fry at 180°C until puffed and golden.
  4. Split the rolls, add lettuce and fish, and spoon over the tartare.

Stonehenge brownie trilithonsVegetarian

Fudgy brownie slabs stood up in a circle.

Prep 12 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Bake the brownie up to 3 days ahead and keep it airtight; cut and stand to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter, then whisk in the sugar and eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa and a pinch of salt and spread in a lined 20cm tin.
  3. Bake at 170°C until just set, then cool completely.
  4. Cut into tall slabs and stand them in a circle with one laid across the top, then dust with icing sugar.
  5. Whip the cream to soft peaks and serve on the side.

One louder rum and Coke

Dark rum and Coca-Cola over 11 ice cubes.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the Coke and fill the ice trays the day before; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml dark rum
  • 1 L Coca-Cola
  • 44 ice cubes
  • 1 lime, cut in wedges
  1. Drop 11 ice cubes into each of 4 tall glasses.
  2. Pour 60ml rum into each, top with Coke and squeeze in a lime wedge.
