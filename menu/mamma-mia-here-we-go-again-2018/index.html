Snacks

Harry's Paris croque bites

Ham, gruyère and mustard in buttered bread, fried, under bubbling cheese sauce

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese sauce up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; build, fry and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150g sliced leg ham
  • 8 slices white sandwich bread
  • 30g butter, plus extra for the bread
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 1 pinch of ground nutmeg
  • 2 tsp dijon mustard
  • 200g gruyere, grated
  • 1/2 tsp salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Melt the 30g butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick, then add the nutmeg, salt, pepper and half the gruyere.
  2. Spread 4 slices of bread with mustard, top with the ham and a little gruyere and close with the other 4 slices.
  3. Butter the outside, fry in a pan until golden on both sides, then spread the tops with the cheese sauce and the rest of the gruyere.
  4. Grill until bubbling and browned, cut each into 4 squares and serve hot.

Kalokairi tomato keftedesVegetarian

Ripe tomato, onion, mint and feta in light batter, fried, with tzatziki, for Bill's sailboat

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the tzatziki up to 2 days ahead; mix the batter and fry just before serving or it goes watery
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch mint, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 120g self-raising flour
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 200ml olive oil, for frying
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, grated
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 clove of garlic, grated
  • 1 lemon, half juiced, half in wedges
  1. Squeeze the cucumber dry and mix with the yoghurt, garlic and lemon juice for the tzatziki.
  2. Salt the tomatoes and drain in a sieve.
  3. Mix the tomatoes with the onion, mint, oregano, feta, flour and pepper to a thick batter.
  4. Fry tablespoons in the oil for 2 minutes a side until golden, and drain.
  5. Serve hot with the tzatziki and lemon wedges.

Bella Donna reopening moussaka

Roasted eggplant, cinnamon lamb and tomato under thick golden white sauce, baked to cut clean

Prep 40 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge, or bake it a day ahead and reheat; it cuts better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 750g lamb mince
  • 2 large eggplants, sliced 1cm thick
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 60g plain flour
  • 700ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g kefalograviera or parmesan, grated
  • 1 pinch of ground nutmeg
  • 2 tsp salt
  1. Heat the oven to 220C. Brush the eggplant with 60ml of the oil, season with half the salt and roast on trays until golden.
  2. Brown the lamb in the rest of the oil, add the onion and garlic, then the cinnamon, oregano, tomato paste and tomatoes. Simmer until thick.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick, then off the heat beat in the eggs, half the cheese, the nutmeg and the rest of the salt.
  4. Layer the eggplant and lamb in a baking dish, finish with the white sauce and the rest of the cheese.
  5. Bake at 180C until deep golden, then rest 15 minutes before cutting.

Donna's lemon chicken souvlaki

Thigh marinated in citrus, garlic and oregano, grilled on skewers, in warm pita with tzatziki

Prep 25 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken and make the tzatziki up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, cubed
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, grated
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 8 skewers
  1. Toss the chicken with the juice of a lemon, two of the garlic cloves, the oregano, oil and salt.
  2. Squeeze the cucumber dry and mix into the yoghurt with the last garlic clove and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Thread the chicken onto skewers and grill, turning, until charred and cooked through.
  4. Warm the pitas on the grill and wrap with the chicken, tzatziki, tomato and onion.

Sweets

Hotel Bella Donna portokalopitaVegetarian

Baked for the reopening, ferries or no ferries

Prep 78 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep covered at room temperature; it gets stickier as it sits
Recipe for 4
  • 375g filo pastry
  • 450g sugar
  • 3 oranges
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 eggs
  • 500g Greek yoghurt, half for serving
  • 180ml olive oil, plus extra for the tin
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  1. Tear the filo into small pieces, spread on trays and dry in a 150C oven.
  2. Simmer 300g of the sugar with 250ml water, the juice and peel of 1 orange and the cinnamon for the syrup, then cool.
  3. Turn the oven to 180C. Whisk the eggs with the rest of the sugar, then whisk in half the yoghurt, the oil, the zest and juice of the other 2 oranges and the baking powder.
  4. Fold in the filo, pour into an oiled 23 x 33cm tin and bake until golden.
  5. Pour the cool syrup over the hot cake and leave to soak.
  6. Serve with the rest of the yoghurt.

Drinks

Grand reopening peach fizzVegetarian

Raised to the uninvited guest, Ruby, at the party

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the wine, nectar and glasses up to 1 day ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml dry sparkling wine
  • 200ml peach nectar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 ripe peach, thinly sliced
  1. Chill the sparkling wine, peach nectar and 4 flutes.
  2. Pour 50ml peach nectar into each flute with a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Top slowly with sparkling wine and drop in a thin peach slice.
