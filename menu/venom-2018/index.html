Symbiote black sesame chicken

Crispy chicken in a soy and honey glaze, rolled in black sesame

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Cut the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; fry and toss through the sauce just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, cut into pieces
  • 100g cornflour
  • 500ml vegetable oil for frying
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 tbsp black sesame seeds
  1. Toss the chicken in cornflour.
  2. Fry in the oil in batches until crisp.
  3. Warm the soy with the honey until bubbling and toss the chicken through.
  4. Roll in black sesame seeds until coated and dark, and serve on toothpicks.

Eddie's frozen potato gems with sriracha mayoVegetarian

Oven baked potato gems with a garlic and sriracha mayo

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best baked just before serving; mix the sriracha mayo up to 3 days ahead, covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen potato gems
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  1. Bake the gems at 220°C, turning once, until crunchy.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the sriracha and garlic.
  3. Serve in a big bowl with the mayo for dunking.

Mains

Lobster tank garlic butter lobster tails

Grilled tails with garlic parsley butter and fries. Fresh from the restaurant

Prep 29 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the garlic parsley butter up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; fry the fries and grill the tails on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil for frying
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 2 lemons
  1. Cut the potatoes into fries.
  2. Fry in the oil twice until crisp.
  3. Split the lobster tails and brush with garlic parsley butter.
  4. Grill until just cooked.
  5. Serve with the fries and lemon wedges.

Eddie's late-night bacon cheeseburgerOn the printed menu

Double bacon cheeseburger with pickles, for two very hungry flatmates at all hours

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar mustard
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Double beef patties with melted cheddar, crisp bacon and pickles in toasted buns, with a pile of crunchy double-cooked fries. Cut 1kg potatoes into fries and cook at 140°C for 6 minutes, then drain. Fry 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Shape 800g beef mince into 8 thin patties, season and grill 2 minutes a side, adding cheddar slices for the last minute. Fry the chips again at 190°C for 3 minutes and salt. Toast 4 buns and stack 2 patties each with bacon, pickles, onion, mustard and tomato sauce.

Mrs Chen's mango pudding

Chilled mango pudding set with gelatine, fresh mango cubes on top

Prep 25 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tsp gelatine powder
  • 100ml hot water
  • 3 ripe mangoes
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 100ml cream
  1. Dissolve the gelatine in the hot water.
  2. Blend the mango flesh with the sugar until smooth, keeping a few cubes aside.
  3. Stir in the gelatine, then evaporated milk and cream, and pour into glasses.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Pour a little evaporated milk over each and top with mango cubes.

Mrs Chen's iced milk teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong black tea with condensed milk, poured over ice

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Steep and chill the tea with the condensed milk up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; pour over ice and add the cold milk to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 4 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea in the boiling water.
  2. Remove the bags, stir in the condensed milk and chill.
  3. Chill until cold.
  4. Pour over ice in glasses and top each with a splash of cold milk.
