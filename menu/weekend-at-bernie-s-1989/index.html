Snacks

Hamptons clam dip

Clams in cream cheese and sour cream, with chips.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin chopped clams
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 bag potato chips
  1. Drain the clams, keeping a little juice.
  2. Fold the clams into the cream cheese and sour cream with lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce and chopped chives.
  3. Serve with potato chips.

Rumaki bacon wrapped water chestnuts

Water chestnuts wrapped in bacon, glazed with soy, brown sugar and ginger.

Prep 4 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Wrap up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin water chestnuts
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  1. Drain the water chestnuts and wrap each in half a rasher of bacon.
  2. Brush with the soy, sugar and ginger.
  3. Bake at 200°C until sticky.

Bernie's deck party devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs with mustard yolk and chives.

Prep 14 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Make up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 2 tbsp crispy fried shallots
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool in cold water and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs and mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard and salt.
  3. Pipe the filling back into the whites.
  4. Dust with paprika and top with chives and crispy shallots.

Mains

Bernie's beach house clambake

Clams, lobster tails, corn and potatoes steamed in white wine and garlic butter.

Prep 12 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Scrub the clams and prep the corn and garlic up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; cook it all on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g new potatoes
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 250ml white wine
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 80g butter
  • 1kg clams
  • 4 lobster tails
  1. Halve the corn, crush the garlic and scrub the clams.
  2. Boil the potatoes in a big pot, 10 min.
  3. Add the corn, wine, garlic and butter, then the clams and lobster tails. Cover and steam, 8 min.
  4. Tip it all onto the table.

Teriyaki sirloin with charred pineapple

Beef in soy, garlic, ginger and pineapple juice, grilled medium-rare, coconut rice and sticky glaze.

How it is made
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 1 tin pineapple rings in juice
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  1. Sirloin marinated in soy, garlic, ginger and pineapple juice, grilled medium-rare, with charred pineapple, coconut rice and a sticky teriyaki glaze. Mix 80ml soy, 80ml pineapple juice, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 crushed garlic cloves and grated ginger. Marinate 4 sirloin steaks for 2 hours. Cook 300g jasmine rice with 400ml coconut milk and 200ml water. Grill the steaks on very high heat 3 minutes a side and rest 5 minutes. Grill 4 pineapple rings until charred. Boil the leftover marinade for 3 minutes until sticky and spoon over the sliced steak.

Pineapple upside-down cakeVegetarian

Caramel pineapple sponge flipped out warm, so good you keep coming back for more.

Prep 18 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 185g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tin pineapple rings
  • 6 maraschino cherries
  • 125g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  1. Melt some of the butter with the brown sugar in a tin and lay pineapple rings with a cherry in each, 8 min.
  2. Beat the remaining butter and caster sugar, add the eggs and fold in the flour.
  3. Spread over the fruit and bake at 175°C, 45 min.
  4. Rest briefly, then flip out, 8 min.

Drinks

Larry and Richard's mai tai

Two rums, orange liqueur, orgeat and lime over crushed ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; shake each batch with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml white rum
  • 120ml dark rum
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 60ml orgeat syrup
  • 100ml lime juice
  • 1 bunch mint
  • crushed ice, to serve
  1. Juice the limes.
  2. Shake the rums, liqueur, orgeat and lime juice with ice, in batches.
  3. Strain over crushed ice and top with a mint sprig.
