Snacks

Tick Tock's spiced racetrack peanutsVegetarian

Roasted peanuts with smoked paprika, cayenne and brown sugar.

Prep 4 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 400g raw peanuts
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Toss the peanuts with melted butter, brown sugar, paprika, cayenne and salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170°C, stirring every 5 minutes, until deep golden.
  4. Cool so the coating crisps.
  5. Serve in paper bags.

Seabiscuit's apple and cheddar hand piesVegetarian

Hand pies filled with cinnamon apple and cheddar.

Prep 31 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 100g vintage cheddar
  • 1 egg
  1. Peel and dice the apples.
  2. Cook the apples with butter, brown sugar and cinnamon until soft, then cool.
  3. Cut rounds from the pastry.
  4. Put a spoon of apple and a few cubes of cheddar on each, fold over and crimp with a fork.
  5. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with sugar.
  6. Bake at 200°C until golden.

Mains

Pimlico match race crab cakes

Pan-fried crab cakes with paprika chips and slaw.

Prep 14 min, cook 41 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cakes up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g crab meat
  • 6 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp celery salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 800g frozen chips
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 2 lemons
  1. Mix the crab with mayonnaise, egg, Dijon, Worcestershire, celery salt, paprika, parsley and panko.
  2. Shape into cakes.
  3. Chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Fry in oil over medium heat until golden each side.
  5. Bake the chips and toss with paprika salt.
  6. Toss shredded cabbage with mayonnaise and vinegar.
  7. Serve with lemon wedges.

Ridgewood Ranch tri-tip with beans and cornbread

Beef rubbed with garlic and paprika, grilled thin, smoky pintos and cornbread, for Charles Howard.

Prep 10 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Rub the beef up to 1 day ahead; beans up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef rump cap or tri-tip
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 800g tinned pinto beans, drained
  • 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Rub the beef with the garlic powder, paprika, pepper and salt.
  2. Soften the onion in the oil, add the beans, tomatoes and sugar and simmer. Meanwhile bake the cornbread to the packet directions.
  3. Grill the beef over medium heat, turning, until medium rare, then rest.
  4. Slice thin across the grain and serve with the beans and buttered cornbread.

Sweets

Santa Anita cinnamon apple pieVegetarian

Cinnamon apples under a buttery crumble top, with ice cream.

Prep 29 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 Granny Smith apples
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 60g butter
  • 80g plain flour
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Peel and slice the apples and toss with sugar, cinnamon and cornflour.
  2. Line a 23cm pie dish with pastry and fill.
  3. Rub the butter into the flour and brown sugar and scatter over the top.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling.
  5. Cool, then serve with ice cream.

Drinks

The Iceman's orange iced teaNo alcohol

Honey orange black tea with mint over ice.

Prep 9 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 black tea bags
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 oranges
  • 1 bunch of mint
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in boiling water with the honey and strips of orange peel.
  2. Remove the bags and peel, then chill for 1 hour.
  3. Juice the oranges and stir the juice in.
  4. Fill tall glasses with ice, pour over the tea and garnish with orange slices and mint.
