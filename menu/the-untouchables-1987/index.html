Snacks

Capone's arancini

Fried in the hotel kitchen, marinara on the side.

Prep 23 min, cook 37 min
Recipe for 4
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 150g mozzarella, cubed
  • 40g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 400g passata
  1. Cook the rice in the stock to a firm risotto, stir in the parmesan and cool completely, 25 min.
  2. Roll into 16 balls around cubes of mozzarella.
  3. Roll in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  4. Heat the neutral oil to 180°C and fry for 4 minutes in batches.
  5. Warm the passata and serve for dipping, 3 min.

Banquet antipasto

Laid down the middle of the round table, before the bat comes out.

Prep 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 100g salami
  • 100g mortadella
  • 80g prosciutto
  • 200g marinated artichokes
  • 1 jar roast peppers, torn into strips
  • 200g bocconcini
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Italian loaf
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Fold the salami, mortadella and prosciutto onto a long board.
  2. Add the artichokes and peppers, and dress the bocconcini with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Slice the loaf and serve alongside so everyone builds their own.

Mains

Italian beef

Shaved thin, dipped in the jus, hot peppers on top.

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min, 30 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef topside
  • 2 tsp dried oregano, plus 1 tsp for the jus
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper, plus more for the jus
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 long rolls
  • 200g hot giardiniera
  1. Rub the beef with salt, pepper and oregano and roast at 200°C to 52°C inside, 35 min.
  2. Chill the beef well, then shave it thin, 30 min.
  3. Simmer the stock with the garlic, oregano and plenty of pepper, 20 min.
  4. Warm the beef in the jus, pile into the rolls, dip the whole sandwich and top with giardiniera, 15 min.

Sweets

Ricotta zeppoleVegetarian

From the Italian bakers on Taylor Street.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 250g ricotta
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 60g icing sugar
  1. Mix the ricotta, eggs, sugar, vanilla and lemon zest, then fold in the flour.
  2. Drop teaspoons into 175°C oil and fry in batches for 3 minutes until puffed and golden.
  3. Drain and toss in icing sugar.

Drinks

Bootleg lager

Capone's own beer. Every bar in town has to stock it.

Prep 5 min, 3 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 8 bottles of crisp lager
  • 2kg ice
  1. Chill the bottles of lager in the fridge for at least 3 hours, then keep them cold in a tub of ice.
  2. Pour the lager into heavy glass steins straight down the middle so each gets a proper head.

Canadian rye

Down from the border by the truckload.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml Canadian rye whisky
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 40ml cold water
  1. Put one large ice cube in each of 4 short glasses.
  2. Pour 30ml Canadian rye whisky over the ice in each glass.
  3. Add a small splash of cold water to each to open up the whisky and serve.

Rye whiskey old fashioned

Rye and bitters stirred over one big cube, for when Ness finally drinks. Here endeth the lesson.

Prep 7 min
Recipe for 4
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 40ml hot water
  • 240ml rye whiskey
  • 8 dashes of aromatic bitters
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  • 4 large ice cubes
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water to make syrup.
  2. For each drink, stir rye, syrup and bitters over ice for 30 seconds and strain over a large cube.
  3. Twist a strip of orange peel over the top.

When to serve

Serve Ness his drink when he meets Malone after the failed raid.
