Snacks

Splice O' Life dumplings

Pan fried pork and cabbage dumplings with ginger, steamed until tender.

Prep 40 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 2 cups shredded wombok, salted and squeezed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 30 gyoza wrappers
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar
  • 50ml water
  1. Mix the pork, wombok and ginger.
  2. Fill the gyoza wrappers and pleat.
  3. Fry until browned, add water and steam under a lid for 4 minutes.
  4. Mix the soy and Chinkiang vinegar for dipping.

Microwave Marge nachosVegetarian

Corn chips with refried beans, cheddar and jalapeños, microwaved hot.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best baked just before serving; dice the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g corn chips
  • 1 tin black beans, drained
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp sliced jalapeños
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Layer the corn chips with the beans, cheddar and jalapeños.
  2. Bake in the oven at 200°C for 8 minutes, not the microwave.
  3. Top with diced tomato and sour cream.

Mains

Manhattan deli pastrami

Pastrami on rye, spicy mustard, potato salad.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Boil, cool and dress the potato salad up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; steam the pastrami and build the sandwiches on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pastrami
  • 8 slices of rye bread
  • 3 tbsp American mustard
  • 600g potatoes
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick, diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 4 whole dill pickles
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, cool and toss with the mayonnaise, celery and dill.
  2. Steam the pastrami for 10 minutes until soft.
  3. Pile high on the rye spread with spicy mustard.
  4. Serve each sandwich with a whole dill pickle.

Brain Gremlin's steak Diane

Pounded fillet, mushroom, brandy and mustard cream, crisp rösti, for Marla's dinner with Billy.

How it is made
  • 4 x 180g beef fillet steaks
  • 800g potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 shallot
  • 150g button mushrooms
  • 50ml brandy
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 150ml cream
  • 300g green beans
  1. Pounded beef fillet seared and finished in a pan sauce of mushrooms, brandy, mustard, Worcestershire and cream, with crisp potato rösti and green beans. Grate 800g potatoes, squeeze dry, mix with salt and fry as 4 rösti in butter for 6 minutes a side. Pound 4 x 180g beef fillet steaks to 1.5cm and sear in a hot pan for 2 minutes a side, then rest. Add 20g butter, 1 diced shallot and 150g sliced mushrooms and cook 3 minutes. Add 50ml brandy, then 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and 150ml cream and bubble 2 minutes. Serve the steaks with sauce, rösti and buttered beans.

Clamp tower rainbow cookiesVegetarian

Red, white and green almond sponge layers stacked with apricot jam.

Prep 30 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g almond meal
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 1 tsp green food colouring
  • 4 tbsp apricot jam
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the almond meal, butter, sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  2. Divide in three, leaving one plain and colouring the others red and green.
  3. Spread each thin in a lined tin and bake at 180°C for 12 minutes each, in batches.
  4. Stack the layers with apricot jam.
  5. Top with melted chocolate and chill to set, 20 min.
  6. Cut into small bars.

Drinks

Mogwai-safe lychee and lime fizzNo alcohol

Lychee, lime and mint on crushed ice.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Muddle the lychees with the lime juice and mint up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; add the syrup, ginger beer, soda and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin lychees, 565g, drained with 100ml syrup kept
  • 3 limes, juiced
  • 1 handful mint, plus sprigs
  • 500ml ginger beer
  • 300ml soda water
  • 3 cups crushed ice
  1. Muddle some of the lychees with the lime juice and mint in a jug.
  2. Add the reserved syrup, fill with crushed ice and top with ginger beer and soda water. Stir gently.
  3. Pour into glasses and garnish with a lychee on a skewer and a mint sprig.
