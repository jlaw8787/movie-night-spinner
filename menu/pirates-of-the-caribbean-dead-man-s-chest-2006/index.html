Liar's dice fried cheese with chilli honeyVegetarian

Pan-fried cheese cubes with warm chilli honey, lemon and thyme.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 360g halloumi, cut into 2cm cubes
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  1. Pat the halloumi cubes dry and toss in the flour.
  2. Fry in the oil over medium-high heat for 1 minute a side until golden all over. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes.
  3. Pile into a cup, tip onto a board like a throw of dice, and drizzle with chilli honey, lemon and thyme.

Pelegosto island skewers

Pork and pineapple skewers glazed with chilli lime honey, grilled over coals.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Skewer up to 4 hours ahead, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork scotch fillet, cut into chunks
  • 1/2 pineapple, cut into chunks
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 2 limes, juiced
  1. Thread the pork and pineapple onto skewers.
  2. Warm the honey with the chilli and lime juice, 5 min.
  3. Grill over hot coals, brushing with the glaze, until sticky and charred on all sides, 15 min.

Chicken and sausage gumbo

Chicken and smoked sausage in a dark roux with okra and sweet pepper, on rice, up the swamp river.

How it is made
  • 80g plain flour
  • vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 1 bunch celery
  • 500g chicken thighs
  • 300g smoked sausage
  • 2 tsp Cajun spice
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 200g okra
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Chicken and smoky sausage simmered in a dark roux with okra, capsicum, celery and onion, ladled over rice with spring onion. Stir 80ml oil and 80g plain flour in a heavy pot over medium heat for 20 minutes until the roux is dark brown. Add 1 diced onion, 1 green capsicum and 2 celery sticks and cook 5 minutes. Add 500g diced chicken thighs, 300g sliced smoked sausage, 2 tsp Cajun spice and 1L chicken stock. Simmer for 45 minutes, adding 200g sliced okra for the last 15. Serve in cups over 300g cooked rice with sliced spring onion.

Davy Jones's seafood boil

Potatoes, corn, prawns and mussels boiled with paprika, garlic and lemon, garlic butter.

Prep 4 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 4 garlic cloves, 2 in the water and 2 crushed for the butter
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 4 corn cobs, halved
  • 500g green prawns
  • 1kg mussels, cleaned
  • 150g butter, melted
  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water with the paprika, garlic and lemon, 15 min.
  2. Add the corn and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the prawns and mussels for 4 minutes until the mussels open.
  4. Drain, tip onto paper and pour over melted butter mixed with the crushed garlic.

Tortuga jerk chicken with rice and peas

Allspice and scotch bonnet chicken charred with rice and peas, recruiting a crew in Tortuga.

Prep 8 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 4 spring onions
  • 1 Scotch bonnet or red chilli
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  1. Blitz the spring onion, chilli, allspice, thyme leaves, soy and lime juice and rub over the chicken.
  2. Grill or roast at 200°C until charred and cooked through, 35 min.
  3. Simmer the rice with the coconut milk, beans, 250ml water and salt, covered, until tender, 20 min.

Kraken ink chocolate tartVegetarian

Dark chocolate ganache tart with a pinch of sea salt on a cocoa pastry.

Prep 2 min, cook 45 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the night before, slice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 300g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • a pinch of sea salt, to taste
  1. Rub the cocoa into the pastry, line a tart tin and blind bake at 180°C. Cool, 35 min.
  2. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate, stirring until glossy, 10 min.
  3. Pour into the case and chill overnight.
  4. Sprinkle with a pinch of sea salt before slicing.

Drinks

Hibiscus ginger sorrel

Chilled hibiscus, ginger and clove drink over ice, dark rum for adults.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, rum added at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 30g dried hibiscus flowers
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 orange, peeled into strips
  • 1.2 L boiling water
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 60ml dark rum, optional for the grown-ups
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the hibiscus, ginger, cloves and orange peel in the boiling water for 30 minutes.
  2. Strain, stir in the sugar and chill, 8 min.
  3. Serve over ice, with a splash of dark rum for the grown-ups.
