Snacks

Baltimore crab cakes

Pan-fried crab cakes with Dijon and breadcrumbs, golden on both sides.

Prep 17 min, cook 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cakes can be chilled up to 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 400g crab meat
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 pinch of paprika
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  • tartare sauce, to serve
  1. Mix the crab with the mayonnaise, egg, mustard, paprika and breadcrumbs.
  2. Shape into 8 small cakes.
  3. Chill.
  4. Pan fry in butter until golden on both sides.
  5. Serve with lemon and tartare.

Fallout shelter devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs with mustard and hot sauce, dusted with smoked paprika and chives.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 dash of hot sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Boil the eggs, cool in cold water and peel.
  2. Halve and whip the yolks with mayonnaise, mustard and hot sauce until smooth.
  3. Pipe back into the whites.
  4. Dust with paprika and finish with chives.

Corny Collins sloppy joe slidersOn the printed menu

Saucy mince with tomato and brown sugar, melted cheese, pickles, for running home after school.

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 tin tomato paste
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 12 slider buns
  • 12 cheese slices
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Saucy beef mince simmered with tomato, mustard and brown sugar, piled on soft slider buns with melted cheese and crunchy dill pickles. Brown 600g beef mince with 1 diced onion and 1 diced green capsicum. Stir in 150ml tomato sauce, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp mustard, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and 100ml water and simmer 15 minutes until thick. Toast 12 slider buns, add a slice of cheese, spoon on the beef and top with pickles.

Mains

Baltimore pit beef sandwiches

Charred rare beef sliced paper thin on rolls with raw onion and horseradish sauce.

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Beef can be sliced up to 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef topside, in one piece
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 4 bread rolls
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp horseradish sauce
  1. Rub the beef with the salt, pepper, garlic powder and paprika.
  2. Grill over high heat, turning, until charred outside and rare inside.
  3. Rest, then slice paper thin.
  4. Pile onto rolls with raw onion and horseradish sauce.

Edna's buttermilk fried chicken dinnerOn the printed menu

Buttermilk-soaked with a craggy crust, honey hot sauce, mash and gravy, for Wilbur and Tracy.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 60g butter
  • 4 dinner rolls
  1. Buttermilk fried chicken with a crunchy craggy crust, honey hot sauce, creamy mash and gravy, buttered corn and a soft dinner roll. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces in 400ml buttermilk with 2 tsp salt overnight. Dredge in 250g flour with paprika, garlic powder and pepper, drizzling a little buttermilk into the flour for crags. Fry at 165°C for 12 to 15 minutes to 75°C inside. Mix 3 tbsp honey with 2 tbsp hot sauce. Serve with mash, gravy from 300ml stock, buttered corn cobs and dinner rolls.

Pageant beehive cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with tall piped buttercream beehive swirls.

Prep 37 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 drop pink food colouring
  • 2 tbsp pastel sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a 12-hole tin.
  2. Beat the cake butter and sugar, then the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  3. Bake in cases and cool.
  4. Beat the icing butter and icing sugar with a drop of colouring.
  5. Pipe tall beehive swirls on top and finish with sprinkles.

Drinks

Har-De-Har Hut black and white maltsVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla malt with chocolate swirled through.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best blended to order; chill the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 100ml chocolate sauce
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  • 4 cherries
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and malted milk powder until thick.
  2. Drizzle chocolate sauce down the inside of 4 tall chilled glasses.
  3. Pour in the shake and swirl a little more sauce through the top.
  4. Finish with whipped cream and a cherry.
