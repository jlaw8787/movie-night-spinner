Snacks

Mars red rock dipVegetarian

Roasted red peppers blended with walnuts and pomegranate molasses, flatbread

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 red capsicums
  • 100g walnuts, toasted
  • 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • pinch of chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Roast the capsicums over a flame or under a hot grill until black.
  2. Rest in a covered bowl, then peel and seed.
  3. Blitz with the walnuts, pomegranate molasses, garlic, cumin and chilli.
  4. Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with oil and serve with warm flatbread.

Rekall memory chipsVegetarian

Thin fried potato chips dusted with vinegar powder and sweet paprika

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 4 potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp vinegar powder
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  1. Slice the potatoes thin on a mandoline, rinse and dry well.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170C in batches until crisp.
  3. Toss straight away with the vinegar powder, paprika and salt so they turn red like Mars dust.

Mains

Mars colony stewVegetarian

Red lentils, red peppers and smoked paprika, with Greek yoghurt on top

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 red capsicums, diced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250g red lentils
  • 400g crushed tomatoes (1 tin)
  • 750ml vegetable stock
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Soften the onion, garlic and capsicums in the oil.
  2. Add the paprika, lentils, tomatoes and stock.
  3. Simmer until thick.
  4. Serve in bowls with yoghurt on top.

Red planet raspberry pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue with whipped cream, fresh raspberries and raspberry sauce

Prep 30 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Meringue up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 250g fresh raspberries
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Heat the oven to 150C. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add most of the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar.
  2. Spread into a 20cm round on baking paper.
  3. Drop the oven to 120C and bake, then cool in the oven.
  4. Simmer the frozen raspberries with the rest of the sugar and cool for a sauce.
  5. Top the pavlova with whipped cream, fresh raspberries, the sauce and mint.

Mars red blood orange martiniVegetarian

Vodka, blood orange juice and orange liqueur shaken and strained

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: If blood oranges are out of season, use orange juice and a splash of grenadine
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 40ml orange liqueur
  • 4 blood oranges
  • 1 lime
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Chill four coupes. Squeeze three blood oranges for juice and juice the lime.
  2. Shake the vodka, blood orange juice, orange liqueur and a little lime juice with ice.
  3. Strain into the coupes and garnish with a thin blood orange wheel.
