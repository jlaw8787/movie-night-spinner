Venusville red rock dipVegetarian

Roasted red peppers blended with walnuts and pomegranate molasses, flatbread.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 red capsicums
  • 100g walnuts, toasted
  • 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • pinch of chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Roast the capsicums over a flame or under a hot grill until black, 20 min.
  2. Rest in a covered bowl, then peel and seed, 10 min.
  3. Blitz with the walnuts, pomegranate molasses, garlic, cumin and chilli.
  4. Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with oil and serve with warm flatbread.

Rekall memory chipsVegetarian

Thin fried potato chips dusted with vinegar powder and sweet paprika.

Prep 16 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 4 potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp vinegar powder
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  1. Slice the potatoes thin on a mandoline, rinse and dry well.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170°C in batches until crisp, 14 min.
  3. Toss straight away with the vinegar powder, paprika and salt so they turn red like Mars dust.

Last Resort red salsa loaded nachos

Corn chips, spiced black beans, cheddar, jalapeños and smoky salsa, open your mind.

How it is made
  • 400g tin black beans
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 1 onion
  • 1 lime
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Corn chips layered with spiced black beans, cheddar and jalapeños, baked until melted, then piled with smoky red salsa, sour cream and coriander. Heat the oven to 200°C. Warm a drained 400g tin black beans with 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp smoked paprika, lightly crushing them. Layer 250g corn chips on a tray with the beans, 200g grated cheddar and sliced jalapeños in two layers. Bake 8 minutes until melted. Blitz 3 roasted tomatoes, 1 chipotle in adobo, half an onion, lime and salt for salsa. Top the nachos with salsa, sour cream and coriander.

Mars colony stewVegetarian

Red lentils, red peppers and smoked paprika, with Greek yogurt on top.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 red capsicums, diced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250g red lentils
  • 400g crushed tomatoes (1 tin)
  • 750ml vegetable stock
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Soften the onion, garlic and capsicums in the oil.
  2. Add the paprika, lentils, tomatoes and stock.
  3. Simmer until thick, 25 min.
  4. Serve in bowls with yoghurt on top.

Venusville smoky red beans and rice

Kidney beans cooked creamy with smoked sausage, celery, bell pepper and Cajun spice, hot sauce.

How it is made
  • 300g smoked sausage
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • bay leaves
  • 2 tins kidney beans (400g)
  • 600ml chicken stock
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 spring onions
  • hot sauce
  1. Red kidney beans simmered with smoked sausage, onion, celery, capsicum and Cajun spice until creamy, over fluffy white rice with spring onion and hot sauce. Brown 300g sliced smoked sausage in a heavy pot. Add 1 diced onion, 2 celery sticks and 1 green capsicum and cook 8 minutes. Stir in 3 garlic cloves, 2 tsp Cajun seasoning and 2 bay leaves. Add 2 drained 400g tins kidney beans and 600ml chicken stock and simmer 40 minutes, mashing some beans to thicken. Cook 300g long grain rice. Serve the beans over rice with sliced spring onion and hot sauce.

Red planet raspberry meringueVegetarian

Meringue with whipped cream, fresh raspberries and raspberry sauce.

Prep 7 min, cook 2 hr 28 min · Make ahead: Meringue up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 250g fresh raspberries
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add most of the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar, 15 min.
  2. Spread into a 20cm round on baking paper.
  3. Drop the oven to 120°C and bake, then cool in the oven, 2 hr.
  4. Simmer the frozen raspberries with the rest of the sugar and cool for a sauce, 8 min.
  5. Top the meringue with whipped cream, fresh raspberries, the sauce and mint.

Rekall blood orange martini

Vodka, blood orange juice and orange liqueur shaken and strained.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: If blood oranges are out of season, use orange juice and a splash of grenadine
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 40ml orange liqueur
  • 4 blood oranges
  • 1 lime
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Chill four coupes. Squeeze three blood oranges for juice and juice the lime, 6 min.
  2. Shake the vodka, blood orange juice, orange liqueur and a little lime juice with ice.
  3. Strain into the coupes and garnish with a thin blood orange wheel.
