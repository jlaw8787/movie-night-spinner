Snacks

Sprinkler-soaked dumplings

Pork, shrimp and shiitake dumplings with ginger and soy.

Prep 32 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill the wrappers up to 1 day ahead, covered on a lined tray in the fridge; steam just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 150g raw prawns, peeled and chopped
  • 3 shiitake mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 1 small carrot
  1. Chop the prawns and mushrooms. Mix with the pork, ginger and soy.
  2. Spoon into the wonton wrappers, pinch up the sides leaving the top open and add a dot of grated carrot.
  3. Steam in batches until cooked through, 10 min.

Leo's wonton crispsVegetarian

Fried wonton triangles tossed in salt and five spice, sweet chilli dip.

Prep 6 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Fry and cool up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight; re-crisp in a 150°C oven for 3 minutes, then toss with the salt and five spice
Recipe for 4
  • 20 wonton wrappers
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 1 pinch Chinese five spice
  • 120ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Cut the wrappers into triangles.
  2. Fry in 180°C oil in batches until puffed and golden, 6 min.
  3. Drain and toss with salt and five spice. Serve with sweet chilli sauce.

Mains

Uncle Benny's roast duck

Duck dried overnight, rubbed with five spice and salt, then roasted and glazed.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 40 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Dry the duck in the fridge overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg whole duck
  • 2 tsp Chinese five spice
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 20 mandarin pancakes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 4 spring onions
  • 100ml hoisin sauce
  1. The night before, prick the duck all over and leave uncovered in the fridge to dry.
  2. Rub inside the cavity with five spice and salt.
  3. Roast at 180°C, 1 hr 15 min.
  4. Glaze with honey and soy and roast for the last stretch, 15 min.
  5. Rest the duck, then carve, 15 min.
  6. Slice the cucumber and spring onions. Serve with pancakes and hoisin.

Murtaugh's whole barbecued snapper

Whole snapper stuffed with lemon, garlic and herbs, cooked on the barbecue.

How it is made
  • 2 whole snapper (800g each)
  • 3 lemons
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 60g butter
  • olive oil
  1. Whole snapper scored and stuffed with lemon, garlic and herbs, grilled over hot coals until the skin blisters, with charred lemon and garlic butter. Score 2 whole snapper (about 800g each) three times on each side, rub with oil and salt, and stuff with lemon slices, 2 sliced garlic cloves, parsley and dill. Grill over medium-hot coals or a barbecue for 8 to 10 minutes a side until the flesh flakes at the bone. Grill 2 halved lemons cut side down. Melt 60g butter with 1 crushed garlic clove and chopped parsley and spoon over the fish.

Sweets

Fortune cookiesVegetarian

Crisp vanilla wafers folded round a paper fortune while still warm.

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 60g plain flour
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 12 paper fortunes
  1. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar, then stir in the flour, butter and vanilla.
  2. Write the fortunes on strips of paper.
  3. Spread the batter into thin circles on a lined tray.
  4. Bake at 180°C in batches, 18 min.
  5. Working fast, fold each warm cookie around a fortune, 7 min.

Drinks

Uncle Benny's lychee ginger highball

Gin, lychee syrup and lime topped with ginger ale, lychee garnish.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Drain the lychees and keep the syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; build over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin lychees (565g)
  • 180ml gin
  • 1 tray ice
  • 1 lime
  • 600ml ginger ale, cold
  • 1 knob fresh ginger
  1. Drain the lychees, keeping the syrup.
  2. Fill glasses with ice, add the gin, some lychee syrup and a squeeze of lime to each.
  3. Top with ginger ale and stir once.
  4. Thread a lychee onto a cocktail pick with a thin slice of ginger and lay it across each glass.

When to serve

Serve the Chinese food when Riggs sets off the sprinklers.
