Snacks

Meatloaf bites

Small baked beef and onion meatballs, meatloaf in bite form

Prep 17 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 60ml full cream milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1/2 brown onion
  • 1 egg
  • salt, to taste
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk. Grate the onion.
  2. Mix lightly with the mince, egg and salt, then roll into 20 balls.
  3. Bake at 200C until cooked through.
  4. Mix the tomato sauce with the brown sugar, brush over and bake a few minutes more.

Prosciutto wrapped breadsticks and ricotta

Breadsticks wound in fishnet style prosciutto, whipped ricotta to dip

Prep 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 16 breadsticks
  • 200g ricotta
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the prosciutto into long thin strips and wind criss-cross around the breadsticks like fishnet.
  2. Stand them up in a tall glass.
  3. Whip the ricotta with salt and pepper and serve in a small bowl alongside.

Randy's loaded mashed potato bitesOn the printed menu

Piped mash rounds baked crisp with sour cream, bacon and cheddar, eat like a little piggy

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 80ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 80g cheddar
  • 150g bacon
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Fluffy mash piped into rounds, baked crisp on top, loaded with sour cream, bacon, cheddar and chives, perfect finger food without the face-down approach. Boil 1kg potatoes, pass through a ricer and beat with 60g butter, 80ml hot milk, 1 egg yolk and salt. Pipe 20 rounds onto a lined tray and sprinkle with 80g grated cheddar. Bake at 220C for 15 minutes until golden. Fry 150g diced bacon until crisp. Top each with a little sour cream from 150g, bacon and chopped chives.

Mains

Christmas duck

Shredded roast duck legs, steamed pancakes, cucumber, spring onion, hoisin

Prep 8 min, cook 97 min, then 10 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 duck legs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 20 Chinese pancakes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 4 spring onions
  • 120ml hoisin sauce
  1. Salt the duck legs and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Roast at 180C until the skin is crisp, then shred the meat.
  3. Cut the cucumber and spring onions into matchsticks.
  4. Steam the pancakes.
  5. Serve the duck with the pancakes, vegetables and hoisin for rolling at the table.

Bumpus-proof roast turkey dinnerOn the printed menu

Christmas dinner before the Bumpus hounds get it

How it is made
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 60g salt
  • 30g sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 egg
  • 450ml chicken stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Brined turkey breast roasted juicy and sliced, with stuffing, cranberry sauce, mash, buttered greens and gravy, guarded closely from the neighbours' dogs. Brine a 1.5kg turkey breast overnight in 2L water with 60g salt and 30g sugar. Rub with 40g butter and roast at 170C for 75 minutes to 70C, then rest 20 minutes. Bake 150g breadcrumbs with 1 sautéed onion, sage, 1 egg and 150ml stock for 30 minutes. Mash 1kg potatoes with 60g butter and milk. Steam 300g green beans. Make gravy with 1 tbsp flour and 300ml stock.

Sweets

Christmas sugar cookiesVegetarian

Buttery vanilla cutout cookies in stars and trees

Prep 24 min, cook 23 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days, iced cookies up to 4 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 280g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the egg and vanilla, then mix in the flour.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll 5mm thick and cut stars and trees.
  4. Bake at 180C until just golden.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with a little water and colouring and ice the cooled cookies.

Drinks

Chocolate malted milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Malt powder, cocoa and vanilla ice cream blended with milk, in tall glasses

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Whisk the malt and cocoa into the milk up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; blend with the ice cream to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  1. Whisk the malt and cocoa into the cold milk.
  2. Blend with the ice cream until frothy.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses.
