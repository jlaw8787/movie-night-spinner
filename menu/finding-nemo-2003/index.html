Snacks

Bruce's shark bite veggie platterVegetarian

Cucumber, carrot and capsicum cut like teeth, sugar snap peas, sour cream dip.

Prep 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 200g sugar snap peas
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove
  1. Cut the cucumber, carrots and capsicum into sharp triangles like teeth and arrange with the sugar snap peas.
  2. Blitz the sour cream with parsley, lemon juice and garlic.
  3. Serve the dip in the middle. Fish are friends, not food.

Coral reef fairy breadVegetarian

Buttered white bread with hundreds and thousands, cut into little fish.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Make just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of fresh white bread
  • 60g soft butter
  • 1/2 cup hundreds and thousands
  1. Spread the bread with butter right to the edges.
  2. Press face down into the hundreds and thousands.
  3. Cut into little fish with a cutter and serve straight away.

Sydney dentist's sausage rolls

Pork and zucchini in flaky sesame puff, smoky tomato relish, hide them from Darla.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 3 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  • 200g tomato relish
  1. Pork and zucchini sausage rolls with herbs in flaky puff pastry, sesame crusted and baked golden, with a smoky tomato relish. Mix 500g pork mince with 1 grated zucchini squeezed dry, 1 grated onion, chopped parsley, 40g breadcrumbs and salt. Cut 3 sheets puff pastry in half, lay a log of filling along each and roll up. Brush with beaten egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and cut into 42 bite-size pieces, or as many as you can count. Bake at 200°C for 22 minutes. Serve with 200g tomato relish.

Mains

42 Wallaby Way chicken schnitzel

Panko crumbed chicken schnitzel with lemon, oven chips and green salad.

Prep 12 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 3 hours ahead and keep chilled; fry and bake the chips on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups panko breadcrumbs
  • 250ml vegetable oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  1. Halve the chicken breasts into thin fillets.
  2. Dip in flour, beaten egg and panko.
  3. Bake the chips as the packet says, 30 min.
  4. Shallow fry the schnitzels in the vegetable oil until golden on each side, 10 min.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges, chips and a green salad.

Crush's EAC pulled pork tacos

Orange and chipotle pulled pork tacos, riding the East Australian Current, dude.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 jar chipotle powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 250g pineapple
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 150g sour cream
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  1. Pork shoulder braised with orange and mild chipotle until it shreds, crisped up and stuffed into soft tacos with pineapple salsa and lime crema. Brown 1.2kg pork shoulder in chunks. Add the juice of 2 oranges, 1 tsp mild chipotle powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves and 250ml stock. Cover and cook at 160°C for 3 hours, then shred and fry until crisp at the edges. Dice 250g pineapple with coriander and lime. Mix 150g sour cream with lime juice. Fill 12 soft tortillas.

Sweets

Clownfish orange yoghurt popsVegetarian

Frozen layers of orange juice and vanilla yoghurt.

Prep 10 min, then 7.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in the freezer
Recipe for 4
  • 6 oranges
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200g vanilla yoghurt
  • 1 packet icy pole sticks
  1. Juice the oranges and mix 400ml of the juice with the honey.
  2. Freeze a layer in moulds, 40 min.
  3. Add a layer of yoghurt, add sticks and freeze, 40 min.
  4. Top with the orange layer and freeze solid, 6 hr.

Drinks

Sea breeze mocktailNo alcohol

Pineapple juice and coconut water over crushed ice with blue raspberry cordial.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Mix the pineapple juice and coconut water up to 1 day ahead and chill; cut the garnish and pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml pineapple juice
  • 400ml coconut water
  • 2 tbsp blue raspberry cordial
  • 1 small pineapple
  • crushed ice, to serve
  1. Cut a few pineapple wedges for garnish.
  2. Fill four glasses with crushed ice and pour in the pineapple juice and coconut water.
  3. Add a splash of cordial and garnish with a pineapple wedge.
