Snacks

Patches's dip platterVegetarian

Vegetable sticks and crackers with hummus, tzatziki and sweet chilli sour cream.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 200g hummus
  • 200g tzatziki
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Cut the carrots, celery, continental cucumber and red capsicum into sticks.
  2. Stir the sweet chilli sauce into the sour cream.
  3. Serve with the hummus, tzatziki and crackers for anyone who skips the vegetables.

Dodgeball meatballs

Beef and pork meatballs simmered in tomato sauce, served on toothpicks.

Prep 18 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make ahead up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  1. Mix the minces with parmesan, chopped parsley, garlic and some salt. Roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in batches in a pan, 10 min.
  3. Simmer in the crushed tomatoes until cooked through, 30 min.
  4. Serve on toothpicks.

Mains

Vegas steak and eggs

Seared scotch fillet with hash browns, grilled tomato and a fried egg.

Prep 5 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Season the steaks up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; bake the hash browns, cook the steaks and fry the eggs on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 scotch fillet steaks
  • 8 frozen hash browns
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • 20g butter
  1. Bake the hash browns at 220°C until crisp, 25 min.
  2. Halve the tomatoes and grill until soft, 10 min.
  3. Season the steaks and sear in a hot pan for 3 to 4 minutes a side, then rest.
  4. Fry the eggs sunny side up in the butter and place one on each steak, 5 min.

Patches O'Houlihan's chicken pot pie

Chicken and vegetables in creamy gravy under a pastry lid.

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, then add the leek and carrot and soften, 12 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream and thyme leaves and simmer until thick. Stir in the peas and cool a little, 10 min.
  3. Spoon into a pie dish, lay the pastry over, trim, crimp and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and deep golden, 30 min.

Sweets

Underdog cakeVegetarian

Dense chocolate cake covered in fudge frosting.

Prep 13 min, cook 55 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 280g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 40g cocoa
  • 100g red fondant
  1. Melt the chocolate with part of the butter. Whisk in the eggs and sugar, then fold in the flour, 15 min.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 170°C, 40 min.
  3. Cool completely, 10 min.
  4. Beat the icing sugar, cocoa and remaining butter into a fudge icing and spread over the top.
  5. Top with a red fondant ball.

Drinks

Average Joe's orange honey coolerNo alcohol

Fresh orange and lemon juice sweetened with honey, over ice and orange wheels.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Stir the juice, lemon juice and honey up to 1 day ahead and chill; add ice, orange wheels and soda water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml fresh orange juice
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 oranges
  • 400ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the orange juice, lemon juice, honey and a pinch of salt until the honey dissolves.
  2. Fill four tall glasses with ice and orange wheels.
  3. Pour in the juice and top with soda water. Finish with mint.
