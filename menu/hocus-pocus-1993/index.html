Snacks

Witch finger pretzelsVegetarian

Pretzel rods dipped in green white chocolate with almond nails.

Prep 13 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days once set, airtight and in a cool place
Recipe for 4
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 2 drops of green food colouring
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 20g flaked almonds
  1. Melt the white chocolate and tint it green, 7 min.
  2. Dip the pretzel rods most of the way.
  3. Press a flaked almond on the tip of each as a nail.
  4. Lay on baking paper to set, 20 min.

Binx black sesame cat crackers

Crisp parmesan and sesame biscuits cut into cats, feta dip and honey, cursed for 300 years.

How it is made
  • 80g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g parmesan
  • 40g black sesame seeds
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Crisp black sesame and parmesan crackers cut into cat shapes, with a feta dip and honey dip for little paws. Rub 80g cold butter into 150g plain flour, 50g grated parmesan, 40g black sesame seeds and half a tsp salt. Add 2 to 3 tbsp cold water to form a dough. Chill 20 minutes, roll 3mm thick and cut out cat shapes. Bake at 180°C for 10 to 12 minutes until crisp. Blitz 150g feta with 100g Greek yoghurt and 1 tbsp honey until smooth.

Mains

Cauldron pumpkin stew

Lamb shoulder braised with cumin, cinnamon and pumpkin, over couscous.

Prep 95 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Braise the lamb, onions and stock up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat, add the pumpkin for the last hour and cook the couscous on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 brown onions, chopped
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 600g pumpkin, cubed
  • 300g couscous
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Brown the lamb in oil, 15 min.
  2. Soften the onions with the cumin and cinnamon.
  3. Add the stock and tomatoes and braise at 160°C, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Add the pumpkin for the last hour.
  5. Prepare the couscous as the packet says.
  6. Serve the stew over the couscous.

Sweets

Spiced apple doughnutsVegetarian

Apple doughnut rings rolled in cinnamon sugar, a Salem autumn treat.

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Reduce the apple juice up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; mix, cut and fry on the day, they are best eaten the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml apple juice
  • 350g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 3 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 80ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Simmer the apple juice down until reduced and cool.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and half the sugar, then the reduced apple juice, buttermilk, egg and butter.
  3. Roll 1.5cm thick and cut 12 rings.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C, 1 minute a side.
  5. Toss in the remaining sugar mixed with cinnamon.

Candy applesVegetarian

Apples dipped in a shiny red toffee coating.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best made on the day; push the sticks into the apples up to 1 day ahead and keep them in a cool spot
Recipe for 4
  • 4 apples
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 125ml water
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 3 drops of red food colouring
  1. Push sticks into the apples.
  2. Boil the sugar, water and golden syrup to 150°C, 15 min.
  3. Add the red colouring.
  4. Dip and twirl each apple.
  5. Set on oiled baking paper until hard.

Drinks

Bubbling lime sorbet punchNo alcohol

Lime sorbet, lemonade and pineapple juice punch with gummy worms, bubbling like a cauldron.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best scooped and poured just before serving; chill the lemonade and pineapple juice up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml lime sorbet
  • 1 L lemonade, cold
  • 250ml pineapple juice
  • 12 gummy worms
  1. Scoop the sorbet into a punch bowl.
  2. Pour over the lemonade and pineapple juice so it froths up.
  3. Hang gummy worms over the side of the bowl.
