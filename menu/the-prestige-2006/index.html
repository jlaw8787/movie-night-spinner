Snacks

Something ordinary: cucumber sandwichesVegetarian

White bread, mint butter, salted cucumber.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the mint butter up to 3 days ahead; salt the cucumber and build up to 2 hours ahead under a damp cloth
Recipe for 4
  • 1 telegraph cucumber
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped mint
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 slices of thin white bread
  • 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
  1. Peel the cucumber, slice it paper thin, toss with the salt and leave in a sieve for 10 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Beat the butter with the mint and a little lemon zest.
  3. Butter every slice, lay the cucumber over half of them, add white pepper and close.
  4. Cut off the crusts and cut each sandwich into three neat fingers.

Birdcage devilled quail eggs with cressVegetarian

Quail eggs, mustard yolks, cayenne, cress.

Prep 25 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Boil and peel the eggs up to a day ahead and keep them covered; fill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 16 quail eggs
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 punnet mustard cress
  1. Lower the quail eggs into boiling water for 2 and a half minutes, then plunge into iced water.
  2. Peel carefully, halve lengthways and tip the yolks into a bowl.
  3. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard and a pinch of salt.
  4. Pipe back into the whites, dust with cayenne and top with cress.

Mains

Fallon's twin chicken and leek pies

Matching pies, chicken, leek, tarragon cream.

Prep 8 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp chopped tarragon
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, then add the leeks and cook until soft, 12 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream and mustard, and simmer until thick; add the tarragon and cool a little, 13 min.
  3. Divide between 2 identical small pie dishes, top each with pastry, crimp and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and deep gold, then serve the two side by side, 30 min.

Don't-forget-your-hat roast beef with Yorkshire puddings

Rare roast sirloin, top hat puddings, gravy. Tesla tells Angier not to forget his hat.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 30 min · Make ahead: Make the pudding batter up to a day ahead and keep it in the fridge; roast the beef on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef sirloin roast
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, plus extra for the tins
  • 1 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 140g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 tbsp plain flour, extra
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp horseradish cream
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk and a pinch of salt into a smooth batter and rest it, 5 min.
  2. Rub the beef with the oil, mustard, salt and pepper and roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then at 180°C until rare in the middle.
  3. Rest the beef and turn the oven up to 230°C; heat a little oil in each hole of a deep muffin tin until smoking, 10 min.
  4. Pour in the batter and bake, without opening the door, until tall like top hats, 20 min.
  5. Stir the extra flour into the roasting tin juices, whisk in the stock and boil into gravy; serve the beef sliced with puddings, gravy and horseradish, 15 min.

The prestige: Victoria sponge, sliced and restoredVegetarian

Butter sponge, raspberry jam, vanilla cream.

Prep 28 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Bake the sponges up to a day ahead and keep them airtight; fill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  2. Fold in the flour, milk and half the vanilla and divide between 2 lined 20cm tins.
  3. Bake at 180°C until springy and golden, then cool on a rack, 22 min.
  4. Whip the cream with the rest of the vanilla, 3 min.
  5. Spread one sponge with jam and cream, put the other on top, dust with icing sugar, then cut it in front of everyone and put it back together.

Tesla's glowing gin and tonic

London dry gin, tonic, lime. Glows under black light.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the gin, tonic and glasses well
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml London dry gin
  • 800ml tonic water, chilled
  • 1 lime, cut in wedges
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice and pour 40ml gin into each.
  2. Top with tonic, add a squeeze of lime and stir once.
  3. Turn off the lights and shine a black light torch on the glasses: the tonic glows blue.
