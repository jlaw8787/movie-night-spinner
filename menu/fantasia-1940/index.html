Snacks

Garlic parmesan stuffed mushroomsVegetarian

Button mushrooms filled with garlic butter, breadcrumbs and parmesan

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill the mushroom caps up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g button mushrooms, stalks removed
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Mix the butter with garlic, breadcrumbs, parmesan and parsley.
  2. Press a spoonful into each mushroom cap.
  3. Bake at 200C until golden and bubbling.

Pastoral grape platterVegetarian

Red and green grapes, brie, fresh figs and honey

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Wash the grapes up to 1 day ahead; take the brie out 1 hour before serving and assemble the platter on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g red grapes
  • 500g green grapes
  • 200g brie
  • 6 fresh figs, halved
  • 4 tbsp honey
  1. Wash the grapes and pile them on a big platter.
  2. Add the brie at room temperature and halve the figs around it.
  3. Set a little pot of honey in the middle for drizzling.

Mains

Lamb moussaka

Eggplant layered with cinnamon spiced lamb and tomato

Prep 35 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggplants
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 600g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 400g diced tomatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 60g plain flour
  • 600ml milk
  • 1 egg
  1. Slice the eggplants 1cm thick, brush with oil and grill or fry until golden.
  2. Brown the lamb with the onion and cinnamon, add the tomatoes and simmer.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick and beat in the egg off the heat.
  4. Layer eggplant and lamb in a dish and pour over the bechamel.
  5. Bake at 180C until golden.

Sweets

Meringue kissesVegetarian

Small baked meringues, crisp outside and light inside

Prep 10 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  • 250g raspberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  3. Pipe small kisses onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 100C, then cool in the oven.
  5. Dust with edible glitter and serve with raspberries.

Drinks

Bacchus grape and rosemary spritzVegetarianNo alcohol

Purple grape juice with rosemary syrup and frozen grapes

Prep 5 min, cook 123 min · Make ahead: Frozen grapes up to 1 week; syrup up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g red grapes
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 2 rosemary sprigs, plus extra to garnish
  • 600ml purple grape juice
  • 1 lemon
  • 800ml sparkling water
  1. Freeze the grapes on a tray.
  2. Simmer the sugar, water and rosemary, then cool.
  3. Mix the grape juice, rosemary syrup and lemon juice in a jug.
  4. Pour into goblets over the frozen grapes, top with sparkling water and garnish with rosemary.
