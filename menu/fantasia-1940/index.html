Snacks

Garlic parmesan stuffed mushroomsVegetarian

Button mushrooms filled with garlic butter, breadcrumbs and parmesan.

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill the mushroom caps up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g button mushrooms, stalks removed
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Mix the butter with garlic, breadcrumbs, parmesan and parsley.
  2. Press a spoonful into each mushroom cap.
  3. Bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling, 12 min.

Pastoral grape platterVegetarian

Red and green grapes, brie, fresh figs and honey.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Wash the grapes up to 1 day ahead; take the brie out 1 hour before serving and assemble the platter on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g red grapes
  • 500g green grapes
  • 200g brie
  • 6 fresh figs, halved
  • 4 tbsp honey
  1. Wash the grapes and pile them on a big platter.
  2. Add the brie at room temperature and halve the figs around it.
  3. Set a little pot of honey in the middle for drizzling.

Hippo ballet gruyère puffs

Golden choux beaten with Swiss cheese and mustard, crisp outside, hollow inside, served warm.

How it is made
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g gruyère
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  1. Light, golden choux puffs beaten with gruyère and a little mustard, crisp outside and hollow inside, served warm. Boil 250ml water with 100g butter and a pinch of salt, tip in 150g plain flour and beat over the heat for 1 minute until it leaves the sides. Cool 5 minutes, then beat in 4 eggs one at a time until glossy. Stir in 120g grated gruyère and 1 tsp Dijon mustard. Pipe 30 small balls onto lined trays, top with a little extra cheese and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until puffed and golden.

Mains

Lamb moussaka

Aubergine layered with cinnamon spiced lamb and tomato.

Prep 20 min, cook 1 hr 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggplants
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 600g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 400g diced tomatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 60g plain flour
  • 600ml milk
  • 1 egg
  1. Slice the eggplants 1cm thick, brush with oil and grill or fry until golden, 25 min.
  2. Brown the lamb with the onion and cinnamon, add the tomatoes and simmer, 25 min.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick and beat in the egg off the heat, 20 min.
  4. Layer eggplant and lamb in a dish and pour over the bechamel.
  5. Bake at 180°C until golden, 40 min.

Rite of Spring garlic rosemary lamb

Leg studded with slivers and roasted pink, crisp potatoes, pumpkin and gravy, for the dinosaur march.

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 600g pumpkin
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  1. Lamb leg studded with garlic and rosemary, roasted pink, sliced with crispy roast potatoes, pumpkin and a rich pan gravy. Make small cuts all over a 2kg lamb leg and push in slivers of 4 garlic cloves and rosemary sprigs. Rub with olive oil and salt. Roast at 180°C for 90 minutes for medium, with 1kg potato chunks and 600g pumpkin pieces tossed in oil around it. Rest the lamb 15 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the pan juices, add 300ml beef stock and simmer until thick.

Sweets

Meringue kissesVegetarian

Small baked meringues, crisp outside and light inside.

Prep 23 min, cook 1 hr · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  • 250g raspberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  3. Pipe small kisses onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 100°C, then cool in the oven, 1 hr.
  5. Dust with edible glitter and serve with raspberries.

Drinks

Bacchus grape and rosemary sodaNo alcohol

Purple grape juice with rosemary syrup and frozen grapes.

Prep 5 min, cook 3 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Frozen grapes up to 1 week; syrup up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g red grapes
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 2 rosemary sprigs, plus extra to garnish
  • 600ml purple grape juice
  • 1 lemon
  • 800ml sparkling water
  1. Freeze the grapes on a tray, 2 hr.
  2. Simmer the sugar, water and rosemary, then cool, 3 min.
  3. Mix the grape juice, rosemary syrup and lemon juice in a jug.
  4. Pour into goblets over the frozen grapes, top with sparkling water and garnish with rosemary.

Serve the snacks when the little mushrooms start dancing.
