Porpoise Spit prawn cocktail

Prawns on shredded iceberg with creamy tomato cocktail sauce.

Prep 22 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked king prawns
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 lemons
  1. Peel the prawns, keeping them chilled.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Shred the lettuce into four glasses, pile on the prawns and spoon over the sauce. Hang a lemon wedge on each rim.

Chicken tarragon vol-au-vents

Puff pastry cases filled with creamy chicken, mushroom and tarragon.

Prep 15 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Cases up to 1 day, fill and warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 250g chicken thigh fillets
  • 150g mushrooms
  • 20g butter
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp chopped tarragon
  • 1 lemon
  1. Thaw the pastry. Cut 16 rounds, press a smaller cutter into each, and place on trays.
  2. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden, then push the centres down.
  3. Dice the chicken and chop the mushrooms. Fry both in the butter until cooked.
  4. Add the cream, tarragon and a squeeze of lemon and simmer until thick.
  5. Fill the cases and warm through in the oven.

Hibiscus Island tropical fruit platter

Pineapple, mango and lychees on crushed ice with lime cheeks, for dancing to Waterloo.

How it is made
  • 1 pineapple
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 papaya
  • 250g lychees
  • 3 limes
  • 1 box sea salt
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 200g vanilla yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Pineapple, mango, papaya and lychees on crushed ice with lime cheeks and a passionfruit yoghurt dip. Peel and cut 1 pineapple and 2 mangoes into wedges, slice half a papaya and peel 250g lychees. Arrange over a tray of crushed ice with 2 limes cut into cheeks. Mix 1 tbsp sea salt with half a tsp chilli powder and the zest of 1 lime. Stir the pulp of 3 passionfruit into 200g coconut yoghurt with 1 tbsp honey. Serve the salt and dip on the side.

Sweet and sour pork with rice

Crispy pork in a sweet and sour sauce with capsicum and pineapple.

Prep 17 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Cube the pork and toss with the soy up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; coat, fry and sauce just before serving so the coating stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork shoulder
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 90g cornflour
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 200g pineapple pieces
  • 3 tbsp white vinegar
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 100ml water
  • 400g jasmine rice
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Cube the pork, toss with the soy sauce, then coat in most of the cornflour. Slice the capsicum.
  2. Cook the rice by the absorption method and keep warm.
  3. Fry the pork in batches in oil at 180°C until crisp, then drain.
  4. Stir fry the capsicum and pineapple in a wok, add the vinegar, sugar, tomato sauce and water, and thicken with the last of the cornflour mixed with a little water.
  5. Toss the pork through the sauce and serve with the rice.

Van Arckle wedding alternate drop

Chicken in mushroom cream or eye fillet with red wine jus, alternating.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 450ml cream
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 eye fillet steaks
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 125ml red wine
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 300g green beans
  1. Every second guest gets chicken breast in creamy mushroom sauce, the rest get eye fillet with red wine jus, both with dauphinoise and green beans. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes with 300ml cream, garlic and salt and bake at 180°C for 60 minutes. Sear 2 chicken breasts and 2 eye fillet steaks. Roast the chicken at 200°C for 15 minutes and the beef 8 minutes, then rest. In one pan cook 200g mushrooms, add 150ml cream. In another reduce 125ml red wine with 200ml beef stock. Steam 300g green beans. Plate alternately.

Muriel's wedding cakeVegetarian

Vanilla cake with raspberry jam and buttercream under white fondant.

Prep 50 min, cook 35 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 500g white fondant
  • 1 punnet of fresh edible flowers, to decorate
  1. Beat 250g of the butter with the caster sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs. Fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide between two lined 20cm tins and bake at 165°C.
  3. Cool the cakes completely in the tins and on a rack.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar for the buttercream.
  5. Sandwich the cakes with jam and buttercream, coat thinly with buttercream, then cover with rolled fondant.
  6. Decorate with fresh flowers and serve in fingers.

Hibiscus Island piña colada

Frozen blended rum, pineapple and coconut cream with a paper umbrella.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Cut the pineapple wedges up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml white rum
  • 250ml pineapple juice
  • 150ml coconut cream
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 1 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 4 cups ice
  • 1 small pineapple
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 4 paper umbrellas
  1. Cut the pineapple into wedges for garnish.
  2. Blend the rum, pineapple juice, coconut cream, lime juice, syrup and ice until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses and garnish with a pineapple wedge, a cherry and a paper umbrella. Swap the rum for extra pineapple juice to make it alcohol-free.

When to serve

Serve the cocktails when Muriel and Rhonda take the stage with Waterloo.
