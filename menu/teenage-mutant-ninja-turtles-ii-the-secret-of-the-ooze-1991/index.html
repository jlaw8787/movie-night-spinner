TGRI ooze dipVegetarian

Charred green chilli and avocado mashed with lime and coriander, warm corn chips. Glows a little

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 hours ahead, cling wrap on the surface
Recipe for 4
  • 2 long green chillies
  • 3 avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 300g corn chips
  1. Char the chillies until blackened, then peel, seed and chop.
  2. Mash the avocados with the lime juice and salt, then fold in the chilli, onion and coriander.
  3. Press cling wrap on the surface until serving. Warm the corn chips.

Sewer lair pizza bagels

Split mini bagels, red sauce, bubbling mozzarella and pepperoni. Nobody stops at one

Prep 7 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 mini bagels
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 150g grated mozzarella
  • 80g sliced pepperoni
  1. Split the bagels and spread with the sauce.
  2. Top with the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Grill until bubbling.

Mutant dandelion saladOn the printed menu

Bitter greens, shaved parmesan, lemon and crisp garlic crumbs. TGRI found them growing by the leak

How it is made
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g dandelion greens or rocket
  • 40g parmesan
  1. Fry 60g fresh breadcrumbs in 2 tbsp olive oil with 1 crushed garlic clove until crisp. Whisk the juice of 1 lemon with 3 tbsp olive oil and salt. Toss 200g dandelion greens or rocket with the dressing, scatter 40g shaved parmesan and the crumbs.

Mains

Keno's delivery pie

Pizza with tomato, mozzarella, pepperoni, pineapple and jalapeño. How Keno got in the door at April's

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Stretch the base up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200ml pizza sauce
  • 250g grated mozzarella
  • 100g sliced pepperoni
  • 200g fresh pineapple
  • 1 jalapeño
  1. Stretch the dough thin on an oiled tray and spread with the sauce.
  2. Scatter the mozzarella, pepperoni, pineapple and sliced jalapeño.
  3. Bake at the oven's highest setting until the edges char.

City Hall station meatball subsOn the printed menu

Beef and parmesan meatballs in red sauce, melted mozzarella in a toasted roll. Mikey found the old station

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  • 400ml pizza sauce
  • 4 sub rolls
  • 200g sliced mozzarella
  1. Mix 500g beef mince with 1 egg, 40g breadcrumbs and 30g grated parmesan, roll into 16 balls and brown in olive oil. Add 400ml pizza sauce and simmer 15 minutes. Split 4 sub rolls, fill with the meatballs and sauce, lay on 200g sliced mozzarella and grill until melted.

Perry's antidote doughnutsVegetarian

Buttermilk doughnuts fried golden, dipped in a sharp lime glaze. The bait for Tokka and Rahzar

Prep 14 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they're made
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 40g butter
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 limes
  1. Whisk the dry ingredients, then beat in the buttermilk, egg and melted butter to a soft dough.
  2. Pat out 1.5cm thick and cut 12 rings.
  3. Fry at 175°C, 2 minutes a side, and drain.
  4. Mix the icing sugar with lime zest and juice, dip the warm doughnuts and let the glaze set.

Ooze canister lime and kiwi slushieVegetarianNo alcohol

Kiwifruit, lime and honey blitzed with ice and lemonade. A safe batch

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 kiwifruit
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 cups ice
  • 400ml cold lemonade
  • 4 lime wheels
  1. Peel and chop the kiwifruit.
  2. Blend the kiwi with the lime juice, honey and ice until slushy.
  3. Add the lemonade and pulse twice. Pour into 4 glasses, add a lime wheel and serve with thick straws.

When to serve

Bring out the doughnuts when the turtles try the antidote on Tokka and Rahzar.
