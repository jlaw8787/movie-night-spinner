The breadless bread plateVegetarian

Cultured butter, whipped ricotta and olive oil with salt flakes.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Whip the ricotta up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; warm the sourdough just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g cultured butter
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 150g ricotta
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  1. Whip the ricotta until light.
  2. Set out the butter, oil and ricotta in separate dishes, each with a few salt flakes.
  3. Because you're not on that island, warm the sourdough and serve it with them.

Raw scallop with finger lime

Thinly sliced raw scallops with lime, finger lime pearls, olive oil and sea salt.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Serve straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 300g scallops
  • 1 lime
  • 2 finger limes
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  1. Chill the plates.
  2. Slice the scallops very thin and lay on the plates.
  3. Dress with lime juice, finger lime pearls, a few drops of oil and sea salt.

Etched tortillas with chipotle salsa

Corn rounds charred dry, smoky tomato dip, smashed avocado and feta, no secrets printed on them.

How it is made
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tin chipotles in adobo
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 2 avocados
  • 16 small corn tortillas
  • 80g feta
  1. Warm corn tortillas charred on a dry pan, with a smoky charred tomato and chipotle salsa, smashed avocado with lime and crumbled feta. Char 4 tomatoes, half a white onion and 2 garlic cloves in a dry pan until blackened in spots. Blitz with 1 chipotle in adobo, a handful of coriander, lime juice and salt. Mash 2 avocados with lime and salt. Warm 16 small corn tortillas in a hot dry pan for 30 seconds a side until lightly charred and keep wrapped in a tea towel. Serve with the salsa, avocado and 80g crumbled feta.

The cheeseburger

Smashed beef patties with American cheese, onion and pickles, crinkle cut fries.

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and do the first fry of the fries up to 4 hours ahead; smash the patties and finish the fries on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Cut the potatoes crinkle style and fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C until soft, then drain.
  2. Shape the beef mince into eight thin patties and slice the onion and pickles.
  3. Smash the patties on a smoking griddle and add the cheese.
  4. Toast the buns and stack two patties per bun with onion and pickles.
  5. Fry the fries again at 185°C until crisp. Wrap one to take home.

Hawthorn lamb rack with celeriac purée

Roasted pink and cut into cutlets over silky mash, charred baby leeks, glossy red wine jus.

How it is made
  • 1 celeriac
  • 400ml milk
  • 60g butter
  • 2 lamb racks
  • 8 baby leeks
  • 200ml red wine
  • 1 shallot
  • 300ml beef stock
  1. Lamb rack roasted pink and carved into cutlets, on silky celeriac purée with charred baby leeks and a glossy red wine jus. Simmer a peeled, diced celeriac in 400ml milk for 20 minutes, then blitz with 40g butter until silky. Season 2 lamb racks, sear fat side down, then roast at 200°C to 56°C inside, about 15 minutes, and rest 10 minutes. Blanch 8 baby leeks for 2 minutes and char in a hot pan. Reduce 200ml red wine with a shallot by half, add 300ml beef stock and reduce until glossy, then whisk in 20g butter.

Campfire s'mores tartVegetarian

Chocolate ganache in a cookie crust, topped with torched meringue.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, torch to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g digestive biscuits
  • 100g butter
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a tart tin.
  2. Heat the cream, pour over the chocolate and stir to a ganache. Pour into the case.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar over hot water, then beat stiff.
  5. Spread on top and torch until blistered.

Hawthorn smoked rosemary negroni

Gin, bitter aperitivo and sweet vermouth with a smoking rosemary sprig.

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Measure the gin, aperitivo and vermouth into a jug up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stir over ice and smoke the rosemary on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 120ml bitter red aperitivo
  • 120ml sweet vermouth
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 orange
  • 4 large ice cubes
  1. Stir the gin, aperitivo and vermouth with ice for 20 seconds, in batches.
  2. Strain over a large ice cube in each tumbler.
  3. Light the tip of a rosemary sprig until it smokes, blow it out and lay it across the glass with a twist of orange peel.
