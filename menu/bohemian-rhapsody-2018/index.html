Rockfield farm Welsh rarebitVegetarian

Thick toast under bubbling sharp cheddar, mustard and milk, grilled blistered, for the Galileo takes

Prep 9 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese mixture up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; spread and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 250g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 2 tsp English mustard
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 pinch of cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 8 thick slices sourdough
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for a minute, then whisk in the milk until thick.
  2. Off the heat stir in the cheddar, mustard, egg yolk, cayenne and pepper.
  3. Toast the sourdough on one side under the grill, turn and spread thickly with the cheese mixture.
  4. Grill until bubbling and blistered, scatter with spring onion and serve hot.

Bulsara kitchen chicken farcha

Pieces marinated in ginger, garlic and spice, semolina crusted, fried crisp, Sunday lunch in Feltham

Prep 142 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; crumb and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken drumettes and wings
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 lemon, half juiced, half in wedges
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 60g fine semolina
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120g mango chutney
  1. Mix the ginger garlic paste, lemon juice, cumin, turmeric, chilli powder, garam masala and salt and rub into the chicken.
  2. Cover and marinate in the fridge.
  3. Roll each piece in the semolina and breadcrumbs, dip in the beaten eggs and roll again.
  4. Shallow-fry in 2cm of oil at 170C in batches, turning, until deep golden and cooked through. Drain on paper towel.
  5. Serve hot with lemon wedges and the chutney.

Mains

Parsi family lamb dhansak with brown rice

Braised into tangy lentils with pumpkin and eggplant, caramelised rice, good thoughts, words, deeds

Prep 42 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Make the dhansak up to 2 days ahead, it gets better; cook the brown rice and kachumber on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 1 cup toor dal, rinsed
  • 1/2 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 300g butternut pumpkin, peeled and diced
  • 1 small eggplant, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 brown onions, 1 chopped, 1 thinly sliced for the rice
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • 2 tbsp dhansak masala or garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 bunch coriander, chopped
  • 4 tbsp ghee
  • 2 cup basmati rice, rinsed
  • 1 tbsp sugar, for the brown rice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced, for the kachumber
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Brown the lamb in 2 tbsp of the ghee in batches and lift out.
  2. Fry the chopped onion until golden, add the ginger garlic paste, chillies, masala and cumin and cook 2 minutes.
  3. Return the lamb with the toor dal, red lentils, pumpkin, eggplant, tomatoes, turmeric and 1.5 litres water. Cover and simmer until the lamb is soft.
  4. Lift out the lamb, blend the dal roughly, return the lamb with the tamarind, brown sugar and half the salt and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. For the rice, fry the sliced onion in the rest of the ghee until deep brown, add the sugar and let it caramelise, then add the cinnamon, rice, the rest of the salt and 3 cups water. Cover and cook on low for 15 minutes, then rest 5.
  6. Toss the red onion, cucumber and tomato with the lime juice and a pinch of salt for the kachumber.
  7. Scatter the dhansak with coriander and serve with the brown rice and the kachumber.

Jer Bulsara's patra ni machhi

Fish in banana leaf with green coconut chutney, from his mother's table

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Blend the chutney up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; wrap the fish up to 4 hours ahead and steam on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 firm white fish fillets, about 150g each
  • 60g desiccated coconut
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 2 green chillies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 limes
  • 4 pieces banana leaf or baking paper
  1. Soak the coconut in 3 tbsp hot water for 5 minutes, then blend with the herbs, chillies, garlic, cumin, sugar, juice of a lime and salt to a thick paste.
  2. Soften the banana leaf over a flame or in hot water.
  3. Coat each fillet thickly in chutney and wrap in a piece of leaf, tied with string.
  4. Steam over simmering water until the fish flakes.
  5. Serve in the leaf with lime wedges to open at the table.

Lagan nu custardVegetarian

Milk slowly reduced with condensed milk, cardamom and nutmeg, almonds on top, for a family wedding

Prep 28 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge; serve cold or warm it gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 395g tin condensed milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 whole nutmeg, for grating
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 10g butter, for the dish
  • 40g flaked almonds
  1. Simmer the milk in a wide pan, stirring often, until reduced by about a third.
  2. Stir in the condensed milk and cool a little.
  3. Heat the oven to 160C and butter a 20cm baking dish. Whisk in the eggs, cardamom, a grating of nutmeg and vanilla, then strain into the dish.
  4. Scatter with the almonds and bake in a water bath until just set and golden on top.
  5. Cool a little before serving, or chill.

Drinks

Wembley summer shandyVegetarian

A hot July day in front of the biggest crowd on earth

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the lager, lemonade and glasses up to 1 day ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 375ml bottles lager
  • 1 1/2l cold lemonade
  • 1 lemon, in wedges
  1. Chill the lager, lemonade and glasses well.
  2. Half fill each glass with lager, tilting the glass.
  3. Top with lemonade and drop in a lemon wedge.
