Colombian arepas con quesoVegetarian

Griddled white corn cakes with cheese and a fried tomato and onion hogao.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the hogao up to 3 days ahead, chilled; cook the arepas fresh and stuff them while warm
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pre-cooked white cornmeal
  • 500ml warm water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 40g butter
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 4 spring onions
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Mix the cornmeal, warm water and salt into a soft dough and rest, 5 min.
  2. Shape 8 patties about 1.5cm thick.
  3. Cook in a buttered pan over medium heat until golden on both sides, 10 min.
  4. Fry diced tomatoes and sliced spring onions in oil to make the hogao, 10 min.
  5. Split the arepas and stuff with sliced mozzarella and a knob of butter.
  6. Serve with the hogao.

Cartagena empanadas

Fried beef and potato empanadas with a green coriander sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fill a day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 potato, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 250g arepa flour
  • 350ml warm water
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 lime
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • salt, to taste
  1. Fry the mince with the potato and cumin until cooked through, 10 min.
  2. Cool the filling completely, 20 min.
  3. Mix the arepa flour with warm water and salt to a dough, 5 min.
  4. Flatten balls of dough, fill, fold and crimp the edges.
  5. Fry at 180°C until crisp and golden, 10 min.
  6. Blitz coriander, chilli, lime and a little oil for the sauce.

Colombian sancocho

Chicken soup with corn, potatoes, cassava and green plantain.

Prep 20 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2.5 L water
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 3 corn cobs
  • 500g potatoes
  • 400g cassava
  • 1 green plantain
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 avocados
  1. Peel and chop the onion, garlic, potatoes, cassava and plantain, and cut the corn into chunks.
  2. Simmer the chicken in water with the onion and garlic, 30 min.
  3. Add the corn, potatoes, cassava and plantain and simmer until thick, 1 hr.
  4. Cook the rice while the soup simmers, 15 min.
  5. Finish with coriander and serve with rice and sliced avocado.

Cartagena snapper with coconut rice

Snapper rubbed with lime, garlic and chilli, grilled crisp with coconut rice and fried plantain.

How it is made
  • 2 whole snapper
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 3 limes
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1 green plantain
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Whole snapper scored and rubbed with lime, garlic and chilli, grilled crisp, served with Caribbean coconut rice, fried green plantain and a tomato and onion salsa. Score 2 whole 600g snapper and rub with 3 crushed garlic cloves, a chopped chilli, lime juice, oil and salt. Cook 300g rice in 400ml coconut milk and 200ml water, covered, for 15 minutes. Slice a green plantain, fry at 180°C until golden, smash flat and fry again. Grill the fish over high heat for 8 minutes a side. Serve with the rice, plantain and a salsa of diced tomato, red onion and lime.

El Corazón emerald jelly

Fresh lime jelly set in a mould, with chilled coconut cream and mint.

Prep 16 min, cook 5 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 gelatine leaves
  • 300ml water
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 250ml fresh lime juice (about 10 limes)
  • 200ml coconut cream
  • 1 small bunch mint
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water, 5 min.
  2. Heat the water with the sugar until dissolved, 5 min.
  3. Squeeze the gelatine and stir it in, then add the lime juice.
  4. Pour into a mould.
  5. Set in the fridge, 4 hr.
  6. Turn out and serve with chilled coconut cream and mint.

Joan Wilder's iced coffee with panela creamNo alcohol

Strong cinnamon panela coffee over ice under lightly whipped cream.

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g ground Colombian coffee
  • 1 L hot water
  • 100g panela or dark brown sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the coffee strong with the hot water.
  2. While hot, stir in most of the panela and the cinnamon stick.
  3. Cool for 30 minutes and remove the cinnamon stick.
  4. Lightly whip the cream with a spoonful of the panela until just thick.
  5. Fill four glasses with ice, pour in the coffee, float the cream on top and dust with cinnamon.
