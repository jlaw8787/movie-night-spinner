Snacks

Hamburger phone sliders

Beef sliders with cheddar, pickles and tomato sauce on small buns

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Shape up to 4 hours ahead, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 8 slider buns
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  1. Shape the mince into 8 small patties and season well.
  2. Fry for 2 minutes a side, adding a slice of cheddar to each at the end.
  3. Split the buns and add tomato sauce and pickles. Stack the patties in, scatter sesame seeds and push a toothpick through each.

Mains

Minnesota potato gem hotdish

Beef, mushroom, corn and green bean casserole under crisp potato gems

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh with the tots on top
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 cup green beans, chopped
  • 300ml cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 500g frozen potato gems
  1. Brown the mince with the onion in the oil. Add the mushrooms and cook until their liquid has gone.
  2. Stir in the corn, beans and cream and simmer briefly. Spread in a baking dish.
  3. Cover with neat rows of potato gems and bake at 200C until golden.

Bleeker's orange candied almondsVegetarian

Almonds and cashews candied with orange zest and cinnamon

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 orange, finely zested
  • 200g almonds
  • 200g cashews
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Heat the sugar, water and orange zest until the sugar dissolves and bubbles. Take off the heat and stir in the almonds, cashews and cinnamon until coated.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170C, stirring halfway, until glassy.
  3. Sprinkle with sea salt, cool completely and break apart.

Blue raspberry jelly trifle cups

Blue jelly with raspberries, vanilla custard and whipped cream

Prep 20 min, cook 200 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets blue jelly crystals
  • 125g raspberries
  • 300ml thick vanilla custard
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 4 shortbread biscuits, crushed
  1. Make up the jelly following the packet. Drop a few raspberries into 4 clear cups and pour in the jelly.
  2. Chill for 3 hours until set.
  3. Spoon the custard over the jelly. Whip the cream to soft peaks and spoon on top.
  4. Finish with the crushed shortbread and the rest of the raspberries just before serving.

Drinks

Big jug of orangeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh orange juice and mango nectar with soda water over ice

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Squeeze up to 1 day ahead, add soda fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 oranges
  • 250ml mango nectar
  • 500ml cold soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the oranges into a big jug over plenty of ice.
  2. Stir in the mango nectar, then top up with soda water just before serving. Add orange slices and pour into tall glasses.

When to serve

Serve the drink when Juno is sitting on the front lawn with her big jug of orange juice.
