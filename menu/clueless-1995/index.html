Cher's Valley party corn chipsVegetarian

Lime guacamole and fresh tomato salsa with a bag of corn chips

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Guacamole and salsa up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 avocados
  • 3 limes
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small bunch coriander, chopped
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 300g corn chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mash the avocados with the juice of two limes, half the onion, coriander and a little chilli.
  2. Mix the diced tomatoes with the remaining onion, lime juice and salt for salsa.
  3. Serve both with the corn chips.

Mall pretzelsVegetarian

Warm soft pretzels rolled in cinnamon sugar, with a sweet cream cheese dip

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Best warm from the oven; mix the cream cheese dip up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and the cinnamon sugar up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 soft pretzels
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 60g icing sugar
  1. Brush the pretzels with butter and roll in the sugar mixed with cinnamon.
  2. Warm at 200°C.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar for dipping.

Full-on Monet tomato and fresh mozzarellaVegetarian

Heirloom tomato, torn creamy cheese, basil oil and balsamic on sourdough, from far away it's OK

Prep 17 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Make the basil oil up to 1 day ahead; slice the tomatoes and tear the fresh mozzarella just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g heirloom tomatoes
  • 2 fresh mozzarella
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 80ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • 1 garlic clove
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Blend half the basil with 60ml of the olive oil and a pinch of salt for the basil oil.
  2. Cut the tomatoes into wedges and rounds, salt them and leave for 10 minutes.
  3. Slice the sourdough, brush with the rest of the oil and toast in a hot grill pan until charred, then rub with the cut garlic.
  4. Lay the tomatoes on a platter, tear the fresh mozzarella over, spoon on the basil oil and balsamic and scatter the rest of the basil. Serve with the toast.

Tai's makeover sushi bowls

Sushi rice topped with sashimi salmon, avocado, cucumber, edamame and pickled ginger

Prep 6 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Cook and season the rice up to 3 hours ahead, covered at room temperature; mix the dressing up to 2 days ahead and slice the salmon and avocado just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 400g sashimi salmon
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 200g frozen edamame
  • 4 tbsp pickled ginger
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  1. Rinse and cook the rice, then season with the rice vinegar and sugar and cool slightly.
  2. Slice the salmon, avocados and cucumber. Cook the edamame.
  3. Mix the soy with the sesame oil.
  4. Top bowls of rice with salmon, avocado, cucumber, edamame and pickled ginger and dress.

Mel's grilled snapper with salsa verdeOn the printed menu

Grilled snapper with a sharp parsley and caper salsa verde, Cher's no-fat rules for Daddy

How it is made
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 120ml olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g mixed salad leaves
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 avocado
  1. Snapper fillets grilled skin side down until the skin crackles, a sharp parsley, caper and lemon salsa verde, and a crunchy green salad with avocado. Pat 4 snapper fillets (about 160g each) dry and score the skin. Chop 1 bunch parsley, half a bunch mint, 1 tbsp capers and 1 garlic clove, then mix with 100ml olive oil, the juice of 1 lemon and salt. Heat a heavy pan over high heat with 1 tbsp oil, lay the fish in skin side down, press for 10 seconds and cook 4 minutes until crisp, then flip for 1 minute. Toss 150g mixed leaves, 1 sliced Lebanese cucumber and 1 sliced avocado with a little of the salsa. Serve the fish with salsa verde spooned over.

Amber's pastel cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with pink and white buttercream and sprinkles

Prep 13 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 100g strawberries, mashed
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 12-hole tin. Beat half the butter with the sugar, then the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk, divide between the cases and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and mix some with the mashed strawberry for pink.
  5. Pipe the icing and add sprinkles.

Rodeo Drive mango and strawberry smoothieVegetarianNo alcohol

Mango, strawberry and banana blended thick with orange juice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chop and freeze the fruit up to 1 week ahead, keeping four strawberries fresh for the rims; blend with the juice, yoghurt and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 large mango
  • 300g strawberries
  • 1 banana
  • 500ml orange juice, cold
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Peel and chop the mango and banana and hull the strawberries, keeping four for the rims.
  2. Blend the fruit with the orange juice, Greek yoghurt and ice on high until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into tall glasses, perch a strawberry on each rim and serve with straws.
