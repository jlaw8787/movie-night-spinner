Snacks

Dugout lamb and rosemary pies

Mini lamb and rosemary pies under puff lids, eaten out of the heat.

Prep 28 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; assemble and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tsp chopped rosemary
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil.
  2. Brown the lamb mince.
  3. Stir in the flour, paste, stock and rosemary and simmer until thick, then cool.
  4. Cut the pastry into rounds, line the muffin tin, fill and add the puff lids. Brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C until deep golden.

Palace Hotel chicken parmigiana sliders

Crumbed chicken, ham, napoli sauce and melted cheese in soft rolls.

Prep 19 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts (about 600 g)
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g breadcrumbs
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g napoli sauce
  • 4 slices ham
  • 150g grated tasty cheese
  • 8 slider rolls
  1. Slice and flatten the chicken into 8 thin steaks.
  2. Crumb through the flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
  3. Shallow fry, 3 minutes a side, in two batches.
  4. Top with the napoli sauce, ham and cheese and grill until bubbling.
  5. Fill the rolls.

Coober Pedy opal jelly gemsOn the printed menu

Blue, green and purple cubes set in lemon jelly with edible glitter, sparkly enough for Felicia

How it is made
  • 3 x 85g packets jelly crystals
  • 6 gelatine leaves
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar edible glitter
  1. Shimmering cubes of blue, green and purple jelly set inside clear lemon jelly and cut into chunks, with a little edible glitter like raw opal. Make three 85g packets of blue, green and purple jelly with 250ml boiling water each, set in shallow trays for 2 hours and cut into small cubes. Soak 6 gelatine leaves, dissolve in 500ml hot lemonade with 2 tbsp lemon juice and cool. Scatter the cubes into a lined 20cm tin, pour over the clear jelly and set 3 hours. Cut into chunks and dust with edible glitter.

Pepperberry kangaroo with sweet potato

Seared kangaroo fillet with roast sweet potato wedges and warm plum sauce

Prep 13 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Rub the kangaroo with the pepperberry, garlic and oil up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast the wedges and sear on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g kangaroo fillet
  • 1 tsp ground pepperberry
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 800g sweet potato
  • 1/2 cup plum sauce
  1. Cut the sweet potato into wedges, toss in oil and roast at 200°C.
  2. Rub the kangaroo with the pepperberry, crushed garlic and oil.
  3. Roast the wedges until golden.
  4. Sear the fillet hard in a hot pan to keep it rare.
  5. Rest the fillet, then slice. Warm the plum sauce and serve alongside.

Bob's roadside barbecueOn the printed menu

Thick snags, garlic lamb chops and fried onions off the tailgate while the bus gets fixed

How it is made
  • 8 lamb chops
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 3 onions
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Thick beef snags grilled slowly, rosemary and garlic lamb chops, fried onions and a tomato, cucumber and red onion salad, served on the tailgate. Marinate 8 lamb chops in olive oil, rosemary, 2 crushed garlic cloves and lemon zest for 30 minutes. Cook 8 thick beef sausages over medium coals for 15 minutes, turning. Fry 2 sliced onions on the hotplate with oil until soft and golden. Grill the chops 3 minutes a side. Toss 3 chopped tomatoes, 1 cucumber and half a red onion with oil, vinegar and salt. Serve with bread and sauce.

Kings Canyon wattleseed and macadamia sliceVegetarian

Shortbread base, wattleseed caramel and macadamias, dark chocolate top.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead; keeps in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 210g butter
  • 225g plain flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tin sweetened condensed milk (395 g)
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp ground wattleseed
  • 100g macadamias, chopped
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour and sugar and press into a lined tin.
  2. Bake at 170°C until pale gold.
  3. Cook the condensed milk, butter, sugar and wattleseed until thick, then fold in the macadamias.
  4. Spread the caramel over the base and cool.
  5. Pour over the melted chocolate and chill, then cut into squares.

Bernadette's pink champagne belliniVegetarian

Raspberry and peach purée topped with sparkling rosé

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Purée up to 1 day, top with sparkling just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 1 ripe peach
  • 1 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 150ml sparkling rose
  1. Blend most of the raspberries with the peach and sugar syrup until smooth, then strain.
  2. Spoon the purée into four chilled flutes.
  3. Top slowly with cold sparkling rosé, stir once gently and drop in a raspberry. Use sparkling grape juice for an alcohol-free version.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the bus pulls into Broken Hill.
