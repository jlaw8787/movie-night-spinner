Snacks

Lamb and rosemary pub pies

Mini lamb and rosemary pies under puff lids, eaten out of the heat.

Prep 28 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; assemble and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tsp chopped rosemary
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil.
  2. Brown the lamb mince.
  3. Stir in the flour, paste, stock and rosemary and simmer until thick, then cool.
  4. Cut the pastry into rounds, line the muffin tin, fill and add the puff lids. Brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C until deep golden.

Silver City pikelets with jam and creamVegetarian

Warm pikelets with strawberry jam and whipped cream. Afternoon tea in Broken Hill.

Prep 9 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Cook up to 2 hours ahead and keep covered; warm to order
Recipe for 4
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 180ml milk
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, egg and milk into a thick batter.
  2. Cook tablespoons in a buttered pan until bubbles show, then flip for a minute, in batches.
  3. Whip the cream and serve the pikelets warm with jam and cream.

Opal jelly gems

Blue, green and purple cubes set in lemon jelly with edible glitter, sparkly enough for Felicia.

How it is made
  • 3 x 85g packets jelly crystals
  • 6 gelatine leaves
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar silver cachous
  1. Shimmering cubes of blue, green and purple jelly set inside clear lemon jelly and cut into chunks, with a few silver cachous like raw opal. Make three 85g packets of blue, green and purple jelly with 250ml boiling water each, set in shallow trays for 2 hours and cut into small cubes. Soak 6 gelatine leaves, dissolve in 500ml hot lemonade with 2 tbsp lemon juice and cool. Scatter the cubes into a lined 20cm tin, pour over the clear jelly and set 3 hours. Cut into chunks and dust with silver cachous.

Mains

Pepperberry kangaroo with sweet potato

Seared kangaroo fillet with roast sweet potato wedges and warm plum sauce.

Prep 13 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Rub the kangaroo with the pepperberry, garlic and oil up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast the wedges and sear on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g kangaroo fillet
  • 1 tsp ground pepperberry
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 800g sweet potato
  • 1/2 cup plum sauce
  1. Cut the sweet potato into wedges, toss in oil and roast at 200°C.
  2. Rub the kangaroo with the pepperberry, crushed garlic and oil.
  3. Roast the wedges until golden.
  4. Sear the fillet hard in a hot pan to keep it rare.
  5. Rest the fillet, then slice. Warm the plum sauce and serve alongside.

Bob's roadside barbecue

Thick snags, garlic lamb chops and fried onions off the tailgate while the breakdown gets fixed.

How it is made
  • 8 lamb chops
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 3 onions
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Thick beef snags grilled slowly, rosemary and garlic lamb chops, fried onions and a tomato, cucumber and red onion salad, served on the tailgate. Marinate 8 lamb chops in olive oil, rosemary, 2 crushed garlic cloves and lemon zest for 30 minutes. Cook 8 thick beef sausages over medium coals for 15 minutes, turning. Fry 2 sliced onions on the hotplate with oil until soft and golden. Grill the chops 3 minutes a side. Toss 3 chopped tomatoes, 1 cucumber and half a red onion with oil, vinegar and salt. Serve with bread and sauce.

Frock on a rock pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue with whipped cream, passionfruit and strawberries.

Prep 25 min, cook 90 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Meringue up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Whip the egg whites to soft peaks, add the sugar gradually until glossy, then fold in the cornflour and vinegar.
  2. Shape on a lined tray and bake at 120°C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven with the door closed.
  3. Cool completely in the oven.
  4. Whip the cream and top the pavlova with cream, passionfruit and sliced strawberries.

Drinks

Bernadette's pink champagne bellini

Raspberry and peach purée topped with sparkling rosé.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Purée up to 1 day, top with sparkling just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 1 ripe peach
  • 1 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 150ml sparkling rose
  1. Blend most of the raspberries with the peach and sugar syrup until smooth, then strain.
  2. Spoon the purée into four chilled flutes.
  3. Top slowly with cold sparkling rosé, stir once gently and drop in a raspberry. Use sparkling grape juice for an alcohol-free version.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the bus pulls into Broken Hill.
