Snacks

Aunt May's egg bites

Baked egg muffins with cheddar, scallion and ham.

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat in a 160°C oven for 8 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 100ml milk
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 100g leg ham, diced
  1. Whisk the eggs with the milk, cheddar, spring onion and ham.
  2. Pour into a greased 12-hole muffin tin.
  3. Bake at 180°C until puffed and set, 15 min.
  4. Serve warm.

Midtown Science pepperoni pizza rolls

Puff pastry rolls with pizza sauce, pepperoni and mozzarella.

Prep 10 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry, thawed
  • 4 tbsp pizza sauce
  • 100g pepperoni, sliced
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 250ml passata
  1. Spread the pastry with pizza sauce, leaving a 1cm border, and scatter with pepperoni, mozzarella and oregano.
  2. Roll each sheet up tightly.
  3. Chill, then cut into 2cm slices, 15 min.
  4. Lay cut side up on lined trays, brush with egg and bake at 200°C until golden, 18 min.
  5. Warm the passata and serve for dipping.

Captain Stacy's red wine brisket

Brisket braised in red wine, stock and tomato paste, roast carrots.

Prep 35 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef brisket
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 250ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 6 carrots
  1. Brown the brisket and soften the onions, 15 min.
  2. Add the wine, stock and tomato paste.
  3. Braise covered at 160°C, 2 hr 50 min.
  4. Add the carrots and roast for the last 40 minutes.
  5. Slice the meat across the grain.

Queens glazed meatloaf with mashed potatoes

Beef and pork meatloaf glazed with tomato, buttery mashed potatoes, a Queens kitchen classic.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 packet breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 jar mustard
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with onion and herbs, brushed with a sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, baked and sliced thick over buttery mash. Mix 500g beef mince, 300g pork mince, 1 grated onion, 60g breadcrumbs, 2 eggs, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, parsley and salt. Press into a loaf tin. Mix 100ml tomato sauce with 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tsp mustard and spread on top. Bake at 180°C for 1 hour, then rest 10 minutes. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml warm milk. Slice the meatloaf thick over the mash.

Gwen's black and white cookiesVegetarian

Soft vanilla cookies iced half and half.

Prep 15 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 120g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 4 tbsp water
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, milk and vanilla, then the flour.
  2. Spoon 12 rounds onto trays and bake at 180°C, 12 min.
  3. Cool the cookies, 10 min.
  4. Mix the icing sugar with the water to a thick icing and stir the cocoa through half of it. Ice half of each cookie with the white icing and half with the chocolate icing.

Drinks

Gwen's dark hot chocolate with marshmallowNo alcohol

Hot milk whisked with dark chocolate, cocoa and vanilla, marshmallows on top.

Prep 0 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh and served hot; chop the chocolate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 12 large marshmallows
  1. Heat the milk until steaming but not boiling, 5 min.
  2. Off the heat, whisk in the chocolate, cocoa, sugar, vanilla and salt until smooth and frothy, 3 min.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with marshmallows and toast with a blowtorch or under a hot grill.
