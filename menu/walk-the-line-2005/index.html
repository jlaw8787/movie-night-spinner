Snacks

Mean man spiced peanutsVegetarian

Warm roasted peanuts with smoky chilli salt. John keeps snatching June's peanut.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Roast up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 400g raw shelled peanuts
  • 1 tbsp peanut oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Toss the peanuts with the oil and spread on a tray.
  2. Roast at 180°C, shaking once, until golden, 12 min.
  3. Toss hot with the paprika, cayenne, sugar and salt, then cool a little, 3 min.

Dyess farm biscuits and sausage gravy

Buttermilk biscuits under peppery gravy, from the Dyess cotton farm.

Prep 16 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Cut the biscuits up to a day ahead and chill on the tray; bake and make the gravy to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 90g cold butter, cubed
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 300g pork sausage mince
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Rub the butter into the flour with a pinch of salt, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough.
  2. Pat out 3cm thick, cut 8 rounds and bake at 220°C until risen and golden, 15 min.
  3. Brown the sausage mince, breaking it up, then stir in the plain flour, 6 min.
  4. Whisk in the milk and simmer until thick, then season with plenty of pepper, 6 min.
  5. Split the biscuits and ladle over the gravy.

Mains

Hendersonville Thanksgiving turkey

Roast turkey with cornbread stuffing, gravy and cranberry sauce.

Prep 15 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Make the cornbread stuffing up to a day ahead and chill; roast the turkey on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast roast
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 4 sage leaves, chopped
  • 300g day-old cornbread, crumbled
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 250ml chicken stock, plus 250ml for the gravy
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 tbsp cranberry sauce
  1. Rub the turkey with half the butter, the sage and salt, and roast at 180°C until the juices run clear, 1 hr 15 min.
  2. Soften the onion and celery in the rest of the butter, then mix with the cornbread, egg and 250ml stock, 10 min.
  3. Bake the stuffing in a dish alongside the turkey for the last 35 minutes.
  4. Rest the turkey, stir the flour into the pan juices, whisk in the rest of the stock and simmer to a gravy, 10 min.
  5. Slice and serve with the stuffing, gravy and cranberry sauce.

Sun Records Memphis dry-rub ribs

Slow pork ribs in a Memphis spice rub. The audition for Sam Phillips at Sun.

Prep 20 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Rub the ribs up to a day ahead and chill; slow cook on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg American-style pork ribs
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 125ml cider vinegar
  • 1/4 white cabbage, shredded
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Mix the sugar, spices and 2 tsp salt and rub all over the ribs.
  2. Wrap in foil with a splash of the vinegar and bake at 150°C until tender, 2 hr.
  3. Unwrap, sprinkle with a little more rub and roast at 220°C until crusted, 15 min.
  4. Toss the cabbage with the mayo and the rest of the vinegar, 5 min.
  5. Cut the ribs and serve with the slaw.

Sweets

Ring of Fire cinnamon bundt cakeVegetarian

Warm slices of brown sugar bundt cake in cinnamon glaze.

Prep 18 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g brown sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Beat the butter and brown sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time, 8 min.
  2. Fold in the flour and half the cinnamon, alternating with the buttermilk.
  3. Spoon into a greased bundt tin and bake at 170°C until a skewer comes out clean, 45 min.
  4. Whisk the icing sugar, milk, the rest of the cinnamon and the cayenne, and pour over the cooled ring, 5 min.

Drinks

Folsom yellow water lemonadeNo alcohol

Lemon barley water on ice. Pour it before Folsom.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 1.5 L water
  • 4 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Simmer the barley in the water with the lemon peel, 15 min.
  2. Strain, stir in the sugar to dissolve and cool, 5 min.
  3. Add the lemon juice and pour over ice.
