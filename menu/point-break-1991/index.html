Snacks

Beach bonfire prawns

King prawns with garlic, chilli and lime, grilled on skewers

Prep 9 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Soak the skewers and crush the garlic, chop the chilli and zest the limes up to 1 day ahead; toss, thread and grill the prawns at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 600g king prawns, peeled
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 chilli
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  1. Soak the skewers. Toss the prawns with crushed garlic, chopped chilli, lime zest and juice, and oil.
  2. Thread onto skewers.
  3. Grill over hot coals on both sides.
  4. Serve with lime wedges.

Bodhi's surf shack fish tacos

Beer battered white fish in flour tortillas with slaw and chipotle mayo

Prep 23 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the chipotle mayo up to 2 days ahead and shred the cabbage up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; batter and fry the fish at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 500g firm white fish fillets
  • 120g plain flour
  • 180ml cold beer
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 2 limes
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  1. Cut the fish into strips. Whisk the flour, beer and salt into a batter.
  2. Toss the cabbage with lime juice and salt.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with the chipotle.
  4. Dip the fish in batter and fry in 180°C oil until golden, in batches.
  5. Warm the tortillas and fill with slaw, fish, chipotle mayo and coriander.

Utah's meatball subs

Beef and pork meatballs in tomato sauce on long rolls with provolone. Get me two!

Prep 32 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Brown the meatballs and simmer them in the passata up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat and grill in the rolls on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 700ml passata
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 8 long rolls
  • 8 slices provolone
  1. Mix the mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, parmesan and salt and roll into meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs in oil.
  3. Simmer in the passata with crushed garlic.
  4. Pack into the rolls, top with provolone and grill until melted.

Bonfire whole snapper with chilli limeOn the printed menu

Whole snapper with chilli and lime, for Bodhi's crew on the beach.

How it is made
  • 2 whole snapper (800g each)
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 3 limes
  • fish sauce
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1 green mango
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 50g roasted peanuts
  1. Whole snapper slashed and rubbed with garlic, chilli, lime and coriander, grilled over hot coals until charred, with coconut rice and a green mango salad. Blend 3 garlic cloves, 2 red chillies, a bunch of coriander stems, the zest and juice of 2 limes, 2 tbsp fish sauce and 2 tbsp oil. Slash 2 whole snapper (800g each) and rub the paste inside and out. Grill over medium-hot coals 8 to 10 minutes a side. Simmer 300g jasmine rice with 400ml coconut milk and 200ml water, covered, for 15 minutes. Toss shredded green mango, mint, peanuts and lime juice for salad.

Reagan banana splitVegetarian

Banana with vanilla, chocolate and strawberry ice cream, fudge, nuts and a cherry

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate fudge up to 3 days ahead in the fridge and reheat gently; slice the bananas and whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bananas
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4 scoops chocolate ice cream
  • 4 scoops strawberry ice cream
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 250ml cream
  • 4 strawberries
  • 30g chopped nuts
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Split the bananas lengthways into dishes and add a scoop of each ice cream.
  2. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream for the fudge.
  3. Whip the rest of the cream and slice the strawberries.
  4. Top with fudge, strawberries, whipped cream, nuts and a cherry.

Ex-Presidents tequila and lime margaritasVegetarian

Tequila, lime and orange liqueur on the rocks with a salt rim. Vaya con Dios.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Jug can be mixed ahead for the esky
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp flaky salt
  • 1 tray ice
  • 180ml tequila
  • 90ml fresh lime juice
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 30ml agave syrup
  1. Rub the tumbler rims with lime and dip in flaky salt. Fill with ice.
  2. Shake the tequila, lime juice, orange liqueur and agave with ice.
  3. Strain over the ice and garnish with a lime wheel.
