Peggy Hodgson's sausage rolls with brown sauce

Pork, onion and sage sausage rolls with brown sauce, Peggy feeding four kids in Enfield

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roll and chill the uncooked rolls up to 1 day ahead, or freeze for 1 month; brush with egg and bake from cold
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp dried sage
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 125ml brown sauce
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a tray. Mix the sausage mince with the onion, sage and white pepper.
  2. Cut each pastry sheet in half, lay a log of filling along each strip, roll up and press the seam closed.
  3. Cut each roll into 4, put seam side down on the tray and brush with the egg.
  4. Bake until puffed and deep golden.
  5. Cool for 5 minutes and serve warm with the brown sauce.

Bill Wilkins's armchair cheese and pickle toastiesVegetarian

White bread fried golden around vintage cheddar, brown relish and mustard, in the haunted front room

Prep 10 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Assemble the toasties up to 4 hours ahead, wrap and chill; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 2 tsp hot English mustard
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 4 tbsp brown pickle
  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Turn 4 slices butter side down, spread thinly with mustard, pile on the cheddar and pickle and close with the other slices, butter side up.
  3. Fry in a frying pan over medium low heat, pressing with a spatula, for 4 minutes a side until golden and melted. Do it in two batches.
  4. Cut into triangles and serve hot.

Green Street shepherd's pie

A hot dinner on the table at 284 Green Street before the lights go out

Prep 10 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Make the pie to the cheddar stage up to 2 days ahead and chill, or freeze for 1 month; bake from cold for 40 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 150g frozen peas
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes, peeled and chunked
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 60g cheddar, grated
  1. Cook the onion, carrots and celery in the oil in a large pan over medium heat until soft.
  2. Turn up the heat, add the lamb mince and brown it well, breaking it up.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste and flour, then the stock, worcestershire and thyme. Simmer gently, stirring now and then, until thick.
  4. Meanwhile boil the potatoes until tender, drain and mash with the butter and milk. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Stir the peas through the mince, season and tip into a 2 litre baking dish. Heat the oven to 200C.
  6. Spread the mash over the top, rough it up with a fork and scatter with the cheddar.
  7. Bake until the top is golden and the edges bubble.
  8. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Maurice Grosse's Friday fish and chips

Maurice, the investigator with the tape recorder, brings tea home wrapped in newspaper

Prep 5 min, cook 54 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare and mushy peas up to 1 day ahead; batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 1kg frozen thick chips
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon
  • malt vinegar, to serve
  1. Bake the chips at 220C for 25 minutes.
  2. Cook the peas for 4 minutes, drain and crush with the butter, chopped mint and salt.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with the chopped capers and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Whisk 120g of the flour with the baking powder and lager until smooth.
  5. Heat the oil to 180C. Dust the fish in the rest of the flour, dip in the batter and fry in batches for 4 minutes until golden.
  6. Serve with the chips, mushy peas, tartare, lemon and malt vinegar.

Janet's school jam roly-poly with custardVegetarian

Butter dough rolled round raspberry preserve, steam-baked, hot vanilla pouring sauce, for the press

Prep 13 min, cook 67 min · Make ahead: Make the custard up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; roll and wrap the pudding up to 4 hours ahead and chill, then bake
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 125g cold butter, grated
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the dough
  • pinch of salt
  • 150ml milk, for the dough
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 500ml milk, for the custard
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar, for the custard
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Rub the butter into the flour, sugar and salt, then stir in the milk for the dough to make a soft dough.
  2. Roll out on a floured bench to 30cm by 20cm, spread with the jam leaving a 2cm border, roll up from a short side and pinch the ends closed.
  3. Wrap loosely in buttered baking paper, then foil, and sit it on a rack over a roasting tin half full of boiling water.
  4. Bake until risen and firm.
  5. Meanwhile heat the milk for the custard. Whisk the yolks, custard sugar and cornflour, pour on the hot milk, return to the pan and stir over low heat until thick, then add the vanilla.
  6. Rest the pudding for 5 minutes, slice thickly and serve with the hot custard.

Enfield night watch hot toddyVegetarian

Whisky, honey and lemon, boiling water, a clove-studded slice and cinnamon

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Stud the lemon slices with cloves up to 4 hours ahead; boil the kettle and build each glass to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml whisky
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, half juiced and half cut into 4 slices
  • 8 cloves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 600ml boiling water
  1. Press 2 cloves into each lemon slice.
  2. Divide the honey, lemon juice and whisky between 4 heatproof glasses.
  3. Top with the boiling water, stir, add a lemon slice and a piece of cinnamon stick to each and steep for a few minutes.
  4. Serve hot.
