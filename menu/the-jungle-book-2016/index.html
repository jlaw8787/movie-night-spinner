Ricotta honey toast fingersVegetarian

Toasted sourdough fingers with honey ricotta, extra honey drizzled over.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Beat the ricotta with the honey and crush the walnuts up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; toast the sourdough and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf sourdough bread
  • 250g ricotta
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 100g walnuts
  1. Beat the ricotta with some of the honey until light. Crush the walnuts.
  2. Toast thick slices of sourdough and cut into fingers, 8 min.
  3. Spread with ricotta, drizzle with more honey and scatter with walnuts.

King Louie's fruitVegetarian

Mango, pineapple, banana and lychees with lime juice and chilli salt.

Prep 16 min · Make ahead: Cut the mango and pineapple up to 4 hours ahead, chilled; add the bananas, lime and chilli salt just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 mango
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 2 bananas
  • 1 can lychees
  • 2 limes
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  1. Cut the mango, pineapple and bananas into chunks and drain the lychees.
  2. Toss together in a big bowl and squeeze over lime juice.
  3. Sprinkle with salt mixed with chilli flakes.

Peace Rock potato and pea samosas

Fried pastry triangles of crushed potato, cumin seed and ginger, mint yoghurt, for the dry season.

How it is made
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 jar cumin seeds
  • 1 jar garam masala
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 pack spring roll pastry
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lemon
  1. Spiced potato and pea filling with garam masala folded into pastry triangles, fried at 180°C until crisp, with mint yoghurt. Boil 600g diced potatoes until just tender. Fry 1 diced onion in oil, add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp grated ginger and a green chilli. Add the potatoes and 1 cup frozen peas and crush lightly, then season and cool. Cut 6 sheets spring roll pastry into 3 strips each, fold spoonfuls into triangles and seal with flour paste. Fry at 180°C for 3 minutes until golden. Serve with yoghurt mixed with mint and lemon.

Wolf pack lamb

Lamb shoulder rubbed with cumin and garlic, roasted five hours, with flatbread.

Prep 18 min, cook 5 hr · Make ahead: Rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; roast on the day, or roast up to 2 days ahead and reheat in its juices
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 500g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 packet flatbread
  • salt, to taste
  1. Rub the lamb with cumin, crushed garlic, oil and salt.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C until it pulls apart, 5 hr.
  3. Stir crushed garlic through the yoghurt.
  4. Pull the lamb apart and serve on a board with warm flatbread and garlic yoghurt, 5 min.

Jungle coconut chicken curry

Thighs simmered in tomato, ginger and garam masala with coconut milk, served with basmati and naan.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 jar garam masala
  • 1 jar ground turmeric
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 lime
  • 1.5 cups basmati rice
  • 4 naan
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Chicken thighs simmered in a tomato and coconut curry with ginger and garam masala, with basmati rice and naan. Fry 1 diced onion in oil for 8 minutes, add 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp grated ginger, 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chilli powder for 1 minute. Add 800g diced chicken thigh and stir to coat. Pour in a 400g tin crushed tomatoes and a 400ml tin coconut milk and simmer 30 minutes until thick. Stir in coriander and lime. Serve with 1.5 cups basmati rice and 4 warm naan.

Honeycomb crunchVegetarian

Crunchy honeycomb made with golden syrup, honey and bicarb soda.

Prep 5 min, cook 29 min, 45 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g dark chocolate
  1. Line a tin with baking paper.
  2. Boil the sugar, golden syrup and honey to 150°C, 12 min.
  3. Off the heat, whisk in the bicarb soda so it foams, and pour into the tin.
  4. Leave to set, 45 min.
  5. Break into chunks and dip half of each in melted dark chocolate.

Mango lassiNo alcohol

Mango, Greek yoghurt, milk and cardamom blended thick, over ice.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; peel and slice the mangoes up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 pinch ground cardamom
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Peel and slice the mangoes.
  2. Blend with the yoghurt, milk and cardamom until smooth.
  3. Pour over ice in tall glasses.
