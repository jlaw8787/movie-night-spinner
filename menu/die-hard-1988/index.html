Nakatomi party canapés

Smoked salmon blini on silver trays

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail blini
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Warm the blini on a tray at 180C.
  2. Top each with crème fraîche and a curl of smoked salmon.
  3. Finish with salmon roe and a dill sprig.
  4. Pass around on a tray straight away.

Argyle's limo snacksVegetarian

Cashews and almonds roasted with butter, rosemary, brown sugar and cayenne, from the back of Argyle's limo

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp chopped rosemary
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150g cashews
  • 150g almonds
  1. Melt the butter with the sugar, rosemary, cayenne and salt.
  2. Toss through the cashews and almonds and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170C, stirring halfway.
  4. Cool so they crisp, then serve warm in a glass bowl.

Mains

Christmas party roast beef rolls

Rare topside shaved thin on soft rolls with horseradish cream, rocket and pickled onions, Christmas Eve supper on 30

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Beef up to 1 day ahead, slice before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef topside
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 8 soft rolls
  • 60g rocket
  • 100g pickled onions, sliced
  1. Rub the beef with Dijon, pepper and salt.
  2. Roast at 200C until 52C inside.
  3. Rest, then shave thin.
  4. Stir the horseradish into the sour cream.
  5. Split the rolls, spread with horseradish cream and fill with beef, rocket and pickled onions.

Snack cakes for AlVegetarian

Vanilla sponge fingers split and piped with marshmallow cream, wrapped in baking paper

Prep 40 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 125g soft butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs, 3 whole and 2 whites
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 80ml milk
  • 50ml water
  1. Beat the butter with half of the sugar, beat in the whole eggs one at a time and the vanilla, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a lined 20 x 30cm tin at 180C.
  3. Cool and cut into 12 fingers.
  4. Boil the rest of the sugar with the water to 118C and pour into the egg whites, whisking hard until thick and glossy.
  5. Split the fingers and pipe the cream inside.

Drinks

Argyle's limo bar old fashionedVegetarian

Bourbon, brown sugar syrup and orange bitters over one big cube, the drink Argyle offers on the way in

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead, chilled; stir with ice and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 8 dashes orange bitters
  • 1 orange, for peel strips
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 1 bag ice for stirring
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water for a syrup.
  2. Stir the bourbon, syrup and bitters with ice in a jug until very cold.
  3. Strain into 4 heavy tumblers over one large ice cube each.
  4. Squeeze orange peel over each, rub the rim and drop it in.
