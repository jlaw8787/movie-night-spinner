Nakatomi party canapés

Smoked salmon blini on silver trays

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail blini
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Warm the blini on a tray at 180°C.
  2. Top each with crème fraîche and a curl of smoked salmon.
  3. Finish with salmon roe and a dill sprig.
  4. Pass around on a tray straight away.

Argyle's limo snacksVegetarian

Cashews and almonds roasted with butter, rosemary, brown sugar and cayenne, from the back of Argyle's limo

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp chopped rosemary
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150g cashews
  • 150g almonds
  1. Melt the butter with the sugar, rosemary, cayenne and salt.
  2. Toss through the cashews and almonds and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170°C, stirring halfway.
  4. Cool so they crisp, then serve warm in a glass bowl.

Nakatomi salmon and tuna sushi platterOn the printed menu

Salmon and tuna nigiri with cucumber and avocado maki, wasabi and pickled ginger, for a Japanese company Christmas party

How it is made
  • 400g sushi rice
  • 80ml rice vinegar
  • 200g sashimi salmon
  • 200g sashimi tuna
  • 3 nori sheets
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 tube wasabi
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  • soy sauce
  1. Salmon and tuna nigiri with cucumber and avocado maki on seasoned sushi rice, served with wasabi, pickled ginger and soy. Rinse 400g sushi rice until clear, cook with 480ml water, then fold through 80ml rice vinegar mixed with 1 tbsp caster sugar and 1 tsp salt and cool. Slice 200g sashimi salmon and 200g sashimi tuna. Shape 16 small oblongs of rice, dab with wasabi and top with fish. Lay rice on 3 nori sheets, add cucumber and avocado strips, roll tight with a mat and cut each into 8. Serve with soy and pickled ginger.

Mains

Christmas party roast beef rolls

Rare topside shaved thin on soft rolls with horseradish cream, rocket and pickled onions, Christmas Eve supper on 30

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Beef up to 1 day ahead, slice before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef topside
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 8 soft rolls
  • 60g rocket
  • 100g pickled onions, sliced
  1. Rub the beef with Dijon, pepper and salt.
  2. Roast at 200°C until 52°C inside.
  3. Rest, then shave thin.
  4. Stir the horseradish into the sour cream.
  5. Split the rolls, spread with horseradish cream and fill with beef, rocket and pickled onions.

Nakatomi teriyaki chicken skewersOn the printed menu

Chicken thigh glazed in soy, mirin and ginger, grilled and passed round on trays at the Christmas party

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • 100ml mirin
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 12 skewers
  1. Chicken thigh skewers glazed in a soy, mirin and ginger teriyaki, grilled sticky and served on a tray, the way the Nakatomi caterers did it. Simmer 100ml soy sauce, 100ml mirin, 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tbsp grated ginger for 5 minutes until syrupy. Cut 800g chicken thigh fillets into strips and thread onto 12 skewers with spring onion pieces. Grill or pan-fry 8 to 10 minutes, brushing with the glaze as they cook. Scatter with sesame seeds.

Sweets

Snack cakes for AlVegetarian

Vanilla sponge fingers split and piped with marshmallow cream, wrapped in baking paper

Prep 23 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 125g soft butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs, 3 whole and 2 whites
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 80ml milk
  • 50ml water
  1. Beat the butter with half of the sugar, beat in the whole eggs one at a time and the vanilla, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a lined 20 x 30cm tin at 180°C.
  3. Cool and cut into 12 fingers.
  4. Boil the rest of the sugar with the water to 118°C and pour into the egg whites, whisking hard until thick and glossy.
  5. Split the fingers and pipe the cream inside.

Argyle's limo bar old fashionedVegetarian

Bourbon, brown sugar syrup and orange bitters over one big cube, the drink Argyle offers on the way in

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead, chilled; stir with ice and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 8 dashes orange bitters
  • 1 orange, for peel strips
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 1 bag ice for stirring
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water for a syrup.
  2. Stir the bourbon, syrup and bitters with ice in a jug until very cold.
  3. Strain into 4 heavy tumblers over one large ice cube each.
  4. Squeeze orange peel over each, rub the rim and drop it in.
