Snacks

Smoked Salmon Blini

Warm blini, crème fraîche, salmon roe and dill.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail blinis
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Warm the blini on a tray at 180°C, 4 min.
  2. Top each with crème fraîche and a curl of smoked salmon.
  3. Finish with salmon roe and a dill sprig.
  4. Pass around on a tray straight away.

Rosemary Roasted NutsVegetarian

Cashews and almonds, brown sugar butter, cayenne.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp chopped rosemary
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150g cashews
  • 150g almonds
  1. Melt the butter with the sugar, rosemary, cayenne and salt, 3 min.
  2. Toss through the cashews and almonds and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170°C, stirring halfway, 12 min.
  4. Cool so they crisp, then serve warm in a glass bowl, 8 min.

Mains

Roast Beef on Soft Rolls

Rare top round, horseradish cream, arugula, pickled onions.

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min, 15 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef topside
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 8 soft rolls
  • 60g rocket
  • 100g pickled onions, sliced
  1. Rub the beef with Dijon, pepper and salt.
  2. Roast at 200°C until 52°C inside, 35 min.
  3. Rest, then shave thin, 15 min.
  4. Stir the horseradish into the sour cream.
  5. Split the rolls, spread with horseradish cream and fill with beef, rocket and pickled onions.

Cream-Filled Snack CakesVegetarian

Vanilla sponge fingers piped with marshmallow cream. A night patrol favourite. Take two for the road.

Prep 23 min, cook 32 min, 15 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 125g soft butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs, 3 whole and 2 whites
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 80ml milk
  • 50ml water
  1. Beat the butter with half of the sugar, beat in the whole eggs one at a time and the vanilla, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a lined 20 x 30cm tin at 180°C, 22 min.
  3. Cool and cut into 12 fingers, 15 min.
  4. Boil the rest of the sugar with the water to 118°C and pour into the egg whites, whisking hard until thick and glossy, 10 min.
  5. Split the fingers and pipe the cream inside.

Drinks

Old Fashioned

Bourbon, brown sugar, orange bitters, one big cube.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 8 dashes of orange bitters
  • 1 orange, for peel strips
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 1 bag ice for stirring
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water for a syrup.
  2. Stir the bourbon, syrup and bitters with ice in a jug until very cold.
  3. Strain into 4 heavy tumblers over one large ice cube each.
  4. Squeeze orange peel over each, rub the rim and drop it in.
