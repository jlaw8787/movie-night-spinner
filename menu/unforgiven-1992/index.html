Snacks

Trail cornbreadVegetarian

Buttermilk cornbread with sweet corn and smoked paprika, baked in a hot pan

Prep 15 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Measure the dry ingredients up to 1 day ahead; mix, add the corn and bake just before serving, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter, melted in the hot pan
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 egg
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 150g corn kernels
  • 60g butter, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 220C with a cast iron pan inside, then melt the butter in the hot pan.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, smoked paprika, egg and buttermilk.
  3. Stir in the corn.
  4. Pour into the hot buttered pan and bake.
  5. Serve with butter.

Saloon pickled eggsVegetarian

Beetroot eggs, for Bill's lecture on gunfighters.

Prep 20 min, cook 4320 min · Make ahead: Pickle 3 days ahead, keep chilled up to 2 weeks
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 250ml white vinegar
  • 425g can beetroot with its juice
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  1. Boil the eggs, cool and peel.
  2. Heat the vinegar with the beetroot juice, sugar, mustard seeds and peppercorns.
  3. Pour over the eggs in a jar with the beetroot.
  4. Pickle in the fridge for 3 days.

Mains

Rainy night beef and bean stew

Beef and bean chilli

Prep 20 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 1 brown onion
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 dried chillies
  • 400g can crushed tomatoes
  • 800g cans kidney beans, drained
  1. Brown the beef in batches.
  2. Add the sliced onion, crushed garlic and crumbled chillies.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes and water to cover.
  4. Braise at 160C.
  5. Add the kidney beans for the last 30 minutes.

Sweets

Big Whiskey bread pudding, whiskey sauceVegetarian

Raisin bread pudding baked in custard with a buttery whiskey sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Bake the pudding until set up to 1 day ahead and chill; reheat at 160C for 15 minutes and make the whiskey sauce fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g day-old white bread
  • 80g raisins
  • 4 eggs
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 220g brown sugar, divided
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g butter, divided
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 40ml whiskey
  1. Tear the bread into a buttered dish with the raisins.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, some of the sugar and cinnamon and pour over.
  3. Soak for 20 minutes.
  4. Bake at 170C until set and golden.
  5. Melt the remaining butter with the remaining sugar and cream and simmer.
  6. Stir in the whiskey and pour over the warm pudding.

Drinks

Whiskey trail coffee with creamVegetarian

Boiled coffee with whiskey and brown sugar, lightly whipped cream floated on top

Prep 10 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh; whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1l water
  • 60g coarse ground coffee
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 8 tsp brown sugar
  • 120ml whiskey
  1. Bring the water to the boil, stir in the coffee and simmer.
  2. Take off the heat, splash in a little cold water to settle the grounds and rest.
  3. Lightly whip the cream until just thick.
  4. Put the sugar and whiskey in each mug and pour in the coffee carefully.
  5. Spoon the cream over the back of a spoon to float it on top.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the riders finally get in out of the rain.
