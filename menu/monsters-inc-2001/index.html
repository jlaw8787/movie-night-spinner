Snacks

Sushi rolls

Salmon, avocado and cucumber rolled in nori, with soy and pickled ginger.

Prep 35 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 nori sheets
  • 200g sashimi salmon
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • soy sauce, to serve
  • pickled ginger, to serve
  1. Rinse and cook the rice, then season with the vinegar and sugar and cool.
  2. Slice the salmon, avocado and cucumber into strips.
  3. Spread rice over the nori, lay the fillings along each and roll tight.
  4. Slice with a wet knife and serve with soy and pickled ginger.

Harryhausen's vegetable gyozaVegetarian

Pan-fried cabbage and mushroom gyoza with a soy and rice vinegar dipping sauce.

Prep 38 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fold up to 4 hours ahead and keep on a floured tray in the fridge, or freeze; cook from cold
Recipe for 4
  • 250g Chinese cabbage, finely shredded
  • 150g shiitake mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • 24 round gyoza wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  1. Salt the cabbage, leave 10 minutes, then squeeze out the water.
  2. Mix the cabbage, mushrooms, spring onions, ginger, garlic, 1 tbsp soy sauce and the sesame oil.
  3. Put a teaspoon of filling on each wrapper, wet the edge and pleat it closed.
  4. Fry the gyoza flat side down in the oil for 2 minutes, add 80ml water, cover and steam 4 minutes, then uncover until crisp again.
  5. Mix the rest of the soy sauce with the rice vinegar for dipping.

Mains

Teriyaki chicken

Grilled chicken thighs in a sticky teriyaki glaze, jasmine rice, bok choy.

Prep 0 min, cook 33 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; grill and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 60ml honey
  • 60ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 bunch bok choy
  1. Mix the soy, honey, chicken stock and sugar and marinate the chicken.
  2. Marinate in the fridge.
  3. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through.
  4. Reduce the marinade to a glaze and brush over. Cook the rice and steam the bok choy.

Monstropolis miso ramen

Miso broth with ramen noodles, pork belly and a soft egg. Dinner after a shift on the scare floor.

How it is made
  • 600g pork belly
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 4 eggs
  • 1.5L chicken stock
  • 1 tub white miso
  • 400g ramen noodles
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 2 bok choy
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Ramen noodles in a rich miso and chicken broth with sticky soy pork belly, a jammy soy egg, corn, greens and chilli oil. Roast 600g pork belly at 160°C for 2 hours, then slice and glaze with 2 tbsp soy and 1 tbsp honey in a hot pan. Boil 4 eggs 6.5 minutes, peel and soak in 60ml soy with 60ml water. Heat 1.5L chicken stock with 3 tbsp white miso and 1 tbsp soy. Cook 400g ramen noodles. Build bowls with noodles, broth, pork, halved eggs, 200g corn, blanched bok choy, spring onion and chilli oil.

Yeti's lemon snow conesVegetarian

Shaved ice with homemade lemon syrup, eat it fast.

Prep 0 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg ice
  • 3 lemons
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  1. Simmer the sugar, water, lemon juice and zest.
  2. Chill the syrup.
  3. Crush or shave the ice, pack into four cups and pour the syrup over. Eat fast before it melts.

Boo's raspberry jam thumbprint cookiesVegetarian

Buttery cookies with a thumbprint of raspberry jam.

Prep 22 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g soft butter
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 180g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp raspberry jam
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the yolk and vanilla, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll into 20 balls, place on a lined tray and press a deep hole in each. Fill with jam.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Cool on the tray and dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Scream canister pineapple slushieNo alcohol

Pineapple, mango and lemon blended with ice, topped with fizzy lemon sherbet.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chop the mango and juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; blend with the ice and add the sherbet just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml pineapple juice
  • 1 mango
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 cups ice
  • 1 tsp lemon sherbet
  1. Chop the mango and juice the lemons.
  2. Blend the pineapple juice, mango, lemon juice and ice until thick and slushy.
  3. Pour into four clear glasses and sprinkle sherbet over each so it fizzes. Serve straight away with spoon straws.
