Snacks

Human Torch flaming wings

Baked chicken wings tossed in hot sauce and honey butter.

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Mix the herbed sour cream up to 1 day ahead; bake and toss the wings just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped herbs
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Toss the wings with salt.
  2. Bake at 220°C until crisp, 45 min.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and honey and toss the wings through, 5 min.
  4. Serve with sour cream mixed with the chopped herbs and celery sticks to put the fire out.

Stretchy cheese pull breadVegetarian

Round loaf stuffed with garlic herb butter and melted cheese.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Stuff the loaf and wrap it in foil up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake just before serving, adding 5 minutes if it comes straight from the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  1. Cut the loaf in a deep crosshatch without slicing through the base.
  2. Mix the butter with garlic and parsley and push into the cuts with the mozzarella.
  3. Wrap in foil and bake at 180°C, 20 min.
  4. Unwrap and bake until golden, 5 min.

Mains

Thing's rock-hard ribs

Short ribs rubbed with paprika, garlic and brown sugar, cooked five hours.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Rub the ribs up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; slow cook on the day and make the slaw just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 20g butter
  • 1/2 small cabbage, shredded
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Rub the ribs with paprika, garlic, sugar and salt.
  2. Cook uncovered at 140°C until a dark crust forms and the meat is tender, 5 hr.
  3. Grill the corn, brush with butter, and toss the cabbage with mayonnaise for a slaw, 10 min.

Lemon dill salmon

Salmon roasted on citrus slices with herb butter, crushed garlic baby potatoes, charred asparagus.

How it is made
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 70g butter
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Salmon fillets roasted on lemon slices with dill butter, with crushed garlic baby potatoes and charred asparagus. Boil 800g baby potatoes for 15 minutes, drain and lightly crush with 30g butter, 1 crushed garlic clove and salt. Lay 2 sliced lemons in a roasting dish and set 4 salmon fillets on top. Mix 40g soft butter with 2 tbsp chopped dill and dot over the fish. Roast at 200°C for 12 minutes. Grill 2 bunches asparagus with olive oil for 4 minutes. Serve together with the pan juices.

Sweets

Cosmic storm sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream under jammy blueberry sauce.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g blueberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 2 packets of popping candy
  1. Simmer the blueberries with the sugar and a splash of water until jammy, 8 min.
  2. Cool the sauce, 10 min.
  3. Scoop ice cream into glasses, spoon over the sauce and top with whipped cream.
  4. Scatter popping candy on top just before serving.

Drinks

Blueberry smoothieNo alcohol

Blueberries, banana, Greek yoghurt, milk and honey, blended thick.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Peel and freeze the bananas up to 1 week ahead for a thicker smoothie; blend and top with granola just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen blueberries
  • 2 bananas
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 tbsp granola
  • 125g fresh blueberries
  1. Blend the frozen blueberries, bananas, yoghurt, milk and honey until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into glasses and top with granola and fresh blueberries.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the four save the day on the Brooklyn Bridge.
