Snacks

Central Park corn chipsVegetarian

Noon broadcast, South Street Seaport. Corn chips, charred corn salsa

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Salsa up to 1 day, chips up to 1 day airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 bunch coriander, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the tortillas into wedges. Heat the oil to 180C and fry in batches until crisp, then salt.
  2. Char the corn cobs on a hot grill or pan, then cut off the kernels.
  3. Mix the corn with the tomatoes, coriander, lime juice and salt.
  4. Serve the salsa in a bowl with the chips.

Pantry stash grilled peaches and honeyVegetarian

Say hello to Fred. Grilled canned peaches, honey yoghurt, almonds

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Toast the almonds and stir the honey into the yoghurt up to 1 day ahead, keeping the yoghurt chilled; grill the peaches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g tinned peach halves (2 tins)
  • 15g butter
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Drain the peaches and pat dry. Mix the yoghurt with the honey.
  2. Toast the almonds in a dry pan.
  3. Heat a griddle pan over high heat, brush with butter and grill the peaches cut side down until charred and caramelised.
  4. Serve warm with the yoghurt, almonds and a dusting of cinnamon.

Mains

Pan-fried steak

Garlic butter sirloin. Keep a plain piece for Sam.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; season the steaks up to 2 hours ahead and leave on the bench to lose the chill
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 50g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the steaks well. Sear in a hot pan, turning once.
  2. Add the butter, garlic and thyme and baste.
  3. Rest the steaks, then slice. Keep a plain unseasoned piece aside for the dog.

Sweets

Neville's Shrek night rice puddingVegetarian

Slow baked creamy rice pudding with nutmeg and raspberry jam

Prep 10 min, cook 160 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 100g short grain rice
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 30g butter
  • 4 tbsp raspberry jam
  1. Heat the oven to 150C and butter a baking dish.
  2. Stir the rice, sugar and milk together in the dish and add a long strip of lemon peel.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top and dot with butter.
  4. Bake, stirring once after 30 minutes, until thick and creamy with a golden skin.
  5. Rest, then serve warm with a spoon of raspberry jam.

Drinks

Light up the darkness bourbon peach smashVegetarian

Bourbon shaken with canned peach, lemon and mint, over crushed ice

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Drain the peaches and squeeze the lemon up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; muddle and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 410g tinned sliced peaches, drained (keep 60ml syrup)
  • 1 lemon
  • 10 mint leaves
  • 4 mint sprigs, to garnish
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 2 cup ice, for shaking
  1. Muddle half the peach slices with the syrup, lemon juice and mint leaves in a shaker.
  2. Add the bourbon and ice and shake hard.
  3. Strain into tumblers packed with crushed ice and garnish with a peach slice and a mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Robert and Sam go hunting deer in Times Square.
