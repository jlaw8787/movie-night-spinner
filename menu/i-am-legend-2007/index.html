Snacks

Central Park corn chipsVegetarian

Corn chips with a charred corn and lime salsa.

Prep 7 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Salsa up to 1 day, chips up to 1 day airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 bunch coriander, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the tortillas into wedges. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry in batches until crisp, then salt, 12 min.
  2. Char the corn cobs on a hot grill or pan, then cut off the kernels, 8 min.
  3. Mix the corn with the tomatoes, coriander, lime juice and salt.
  4. Serve the salsa in a bowl with the chips.

Pantry stash grilled peaches and honeyVegetarian

Grilled canned peaches, honey yoghurt, almonds.

Prep 6 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Toast the almonds and stir the honey into the yoghurt up to 1 day ahead, keeping the yoghurt chilled; grill the peaches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g tinned peach halves (2 tins)
  • 15g butter
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • ground cinnamon, to dust
  1. Drain the peaches and pat dry. Mix the yoghurt with the honey.
  2. Toast the almonds in a dry pan.
  3. Heat a griddle pan over high heat, brush with butter and grill the peaches cut side down until charred and caramelised, 4 min.
  4. Serve warm with the yoghurt, almonds and a dusting of cinnamon, 3 min.

Mains

Pan-fried steak

Garlic butter sirloin. Keep a plain piece for Sam.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; season the steaks up to 2 hours ahead and leave on the bench to lose the chill
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 50g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the steaks well. Sear in a hot pan, turning once, 6 min.
  2. Add the butter, garlic and thyme and baste.
  3. Rest the steaks, then slice. Keep a plain unseasoned piece aside for the dog, 8 min.

Central Park charred corn chicken bowls

Smoky grilled thighs over rice with black beans, chilli lime butter, avocado and cilantro.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 40g butter
  • chilli flakes
  • 2 limes
  • 400g tin black beans
  • 1 avocado
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Smoky grilled chicken thighs over rice with charred corn, black beans, chilli lime butter, parmesan, avocado and coriander. Toss 800g chicken thighs with 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cumin, salt and oil. Grill 4 cobs of corn over hot coals for 10 minutes until charred, then grill the chicken 5 minutes a side. Cook 300g rice. Mix 40g soft butter with chilli flakes and lime zest. Cut the corn from the cobs and toss with the butter. Build bowls with rice, sliced chicken, corn, a drained 400g tin black beans, avocado, grated parmesan and coriander.

Neville's Shrek night rice puddingVegetarian

Slow baked creamy rice pudding with nutmeg and raspberry jam.

Prep 5 min, cook 2 hr 23 min, 12 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 100g short grain rice
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 30g butter
  • 4 tbsp raspberry jam
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C and butter a baking dish, 3 min.
  2. Stir the rice, sugar and milk together in the dish and add a long strip of lemon peel.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top and dot with butter.
  4. Bake, stirring once after 30 minutes, until thick and creamy with a golden skin.
  5. Rest, then serve warm with a spoon of raspberry jam, 12 min.

Drinks

Light up the darkness bourbon peach smash

Bourbon shaken with canned peach, lemon and mint, over crushed ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Drain the peaches and squeeze the lemon up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; muddle and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 410g tinned sliced peaches, drained (keep 60ml syrup)
  • 1 lemon
  • 10 mint leaves
  • 4 mint sprigs, to garnish
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 2 cups ice, for shaking
  1. Muddle half the peach slices with the syrup, lemon juice and mint leaves in a shaker.
  2. Add the bourbon and ice and shake hard.
  3. Strain into tumblers packed with crushed ice and garnish with a peach slice and a mint sprig.
