Viktor's Ukrainian derunyVegetarian

Grated potato and onion fritters fried deep golden and crisp, with sour cream and dill

Prep 17 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the dill sour cream up to 2 days ahead. Keep fried deruny crisp in a 120C oven for up to 30 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 800g floury potatoes, peeled
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Grate the potatoes and onion on the coarse side of a grater.
  2. Tip into a clean tea towel and squeeze out as much liquid as you can.
  3. Mix with the egg, flour and plenty of salt and pepper.
  4. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and fry heaped spoonfuls, flattened, for 3 to 4 minutes a side, in batches, until deep golden and crisp. Drain.
  5. Stir half the dill through the sour cream and serve with the hot deruny and the rest of the dill on top.

Proposal night tuna tostadas

Lime and soy tuna on crisp tostadas, while Adonis rehearses a proposal he can't get out

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake the tostadas up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight; mix the chipotle mayonnaise up to 3 days ahead; dress the tuna just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g sashimi-grade tuna
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp chipotle chillies in adobo, chopped
  • 1 avocado
  • 2 tbsp fried shallots
  • salt, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Brush the tortillas with the oil and bake on a tray until crisp, turning once.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the chipotle.
  3. Mash the avocado with the juice of half a lime and salt.
  4. Dice the tuna and toss with the juice of 1 lime, the soy sauce, sesame oil and spring onions.
  5. Spread the tostadas with avocado, top with the tuna, a little chipotle mayonnaise and the fried shallots. Serve with the last lime half in wedges.

The desert camp chilli

Slow beef chilli for the desert comeback

Prep 21 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled, or freeze up to 3 months; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp chipotle chillies in adobo, chopped
  • 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 400g tinned kidney beans, drained
  • 20g dark chocolate
  • 300g long-grain rice
  • 150g sour cream
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • salt, to taste
  1. Season the beef with salt and brown well in the oil in batches in a heavy pot. Lift out.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in the same pot.
  3. Stir in the cumin, paprika, oregano and chipotle for 1 minute, then return the beef with the tomatoes and stock. Simmer, covered, on low until the beef is very tender.
  4. Stir in the beans and chocolate and simmer, uncovered, until thick. Meanwhile cook the rice.
  5. Season and serve over the rice with sour cream, cheddar and spring onion.

Adrian's chicken cacciatore with soft polenta

Thighs braised with tomato, capsicum, olives and rosemary over soft parmesan cornmeal

Prep 10 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Braise up to 2 days ahead and reheat; make the polenta on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 red capsicums, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 800g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 80g black olives
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 200g instant polenta
  • 1l chicken stock
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil, then lift out.
  2. Soften the onion, capsicum and garlic in the pan.
  3. Add the tomatoes, olives and rosemary, put the chicken back and simmer covered until tender.
  4. Bring the stock to the boil, whisk in the polenta and stir until thick, then beat in the parmesan. Serve with the chicken.

Moscow fight night honey cakeVegetarian

Honey and sour cream layers. Ivan throws in the towel.

Prep 20 min, cook 71 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Assemble 1 day ahead, the overnight chill is what softens the layers; keeps 3 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 100g honey
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 350g plain flour
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 500g sour cream
  • 100g icing sugar
  1. Melt the honey, caster sugar and butter in a bowl over simmering water.
  2. Whisk in the eggs, then the bicarb, until it foams and turns golden. Off the heat, work in the flour to a soft dough.
  3. Cover and rest until cool enough to handle. Heat the oven to 180C.
  4. Divide into 6, roll each thinly on baking paper, cut to a 20cm round using a plate and bake each for about 6 minutes until golden. Keep the offcuts and bake them too.
  5. Blitz the offcuts to fine crumbs.
  6. Whip the cream to soft peaks, then whisk in the sour cream and icing sugar.
  7. Stack the layers with the cream between each, cover the top and sides and press on the crumbs.
  8. Chill overnight.

Desert camp watermelon agua frescaVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh fruit blended with lime, sugar and water, over ice with mint, coldest thing for miles

Prep 12 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Blend and strain up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; stir before pouring over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg seedless watermelon flesh, chopped
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 250ml cold water
  • 2 cup ice
  • 4 sprigs mint
  1. Blend the watermelon, lime juice, sugar and water until smooth.
  2. Strain through a sieve into a jug.
  3. Chill until very cold.
  4. Pour over ice in 4 glasses and add a mint sprig.
