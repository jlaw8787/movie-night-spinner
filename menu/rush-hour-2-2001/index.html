Snacks

Red Dragon casino prawns

Crisp fried prawns tossed in a wok with garlic, red chilli and spring onion

Prep 10 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Best fried and tossed just before serving; slice the garlic, chilli and spring onions up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g shell-on green prawns
  • 1 pinch white pepper
  • 60g potato starch
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 spring onions
  • salt, to taste
  1. Dry the prawns well, season with salt and white pepper and toss in the potato starch.
  2. Fry in 190C oil in batches until crisp.
  3. In a wok, fry the sliced garlic, chilli and spring onions in a little oil, then add the prawns and toss well.

Hong Kong pork and prawn siu maiOn the printed menu

Pork and prawn dumplings steamed open-topped, the way they come at dim sum in Hong Kong

How it is made
  • 4 dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 300g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns
  • 1 small piece ginger
  • soy sauce
  • sesame oil
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 20 round wonton wrappers
  • 1 carrot
  • chilli oil
  1. Open-topped dumplings of pork mince, chopped prawn, shiitake and ginger, steamed until juicy, topped with a dot of carrot and served with chilli soy. Soak 4 dried shiitake in hot water for 20 minutes, then chop finely. Mix with 300g pork mince, 150g chopped green prawns, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp cornflour. Cup 20 round wonton wrappers in your hand, fill and squeeze into open baskets. Top with finely diced carrot. Steam 8 minutes over boiling water. Serve with soy and chilli oil.

Ricky Tan's Hong Kong roast goose

Duck for goose: five spice, dried overnight and roasted, plum sauce and jasmine rice

Prep 10 min, cook 90 min, then 8.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg whole duck
  • 2 tsp Chinese five spice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp hot water
  • 150ml plum sauce
  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  1. Rub the cavity with five spice and salt, then leave uncovered in the fridge overnight to dry the skin.
  2. Brush with honey loosened with hot water.
  3. Roast at 180C, brushing again twice.
  4. Rest the duck, cook the rice and chop through the bone.
  5. Serve with plum sauce and rice.

Caesars Palace winnings prime rib plateOn the printed menu

Garlic and herb crusted beef roasted pink, red wine gravy, horseradish, roast potatoes and corn

How it is made
  • 1.5kg boneless beef rib roast
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 40g butter
  • 1 jar horseradish cream
  1. Garlic and herb-crusted beef rib roasted pink and carved thick, with red wine gravy, creamy horseradish, crispy roast potatoes and buttery corn. Rub a 1.5kg boneless beef rib roast with 3 crushed garlic cloves, chopped rosemary, olive oil, salt and pepper. Roast at 220C for 20 minutes, then 160C for about 50 minutes until 54C inside. Rest 20 minutes. Roast 1kg halved potatoes in oil at 220C for 45 minutes. Make gravy from the pan juices with 1 tbsp flour, 150ml red wine and 300ml beef stock. Boil 4 corn cobs 6 minutes and butter. Serve with horseradish cream.

Hong Kong egg tartsVegetarian

Small bites. Hu Li tapes a grenade in Lee's mouth.

Prep 18 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make and strain the custard and press the pastry into the tin up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; fill and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 70g caster sugar
  • 120ml hot water
  • 3 eggs
  • 80ml evaporated milk
  • 40ml full cream milk
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Whisk in the eggs and both milks, then strain.
  3. Cut rounds from the pastry and press into a greased patty tin.
  4. Fill the cases three quarters full.
  5. Bake at 200C until the custard is just set with a slight wobble.

Mango sagoVegetarian

Chilled sago and fresh mango in coconut milk, paid for in Reign's superdollars that burn red

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g sago pearls
  • 3 mangoes
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 100ml evaporated milk
  • 1/2 pomelo
  1. Boil the sago in plenty of water until clear.
  2. Rinse under cold water and chill.
  3. Blend the flesh of two mangoes with the coconut milk and evaporated milk.
  4. Fold in the sago and divide into bowls.
  5. Top with diced mango from the third mango and pomelo segments.

Hong Kong milk teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong black tea pulled silky with evaporated milk, for the party on Ricky Tan's yacht

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Simmer and strain the tea up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; add the evaporated milk and sugar and serve hot or over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp loose black tea
  • 800ml water
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 4 tsp caster sugar
  1. Simmer the tea in the water.
  2. Strain twice through a fine cloth or sieve.
  3. Add the evaporated milk and sugar and stir. Serve hot or over ice.
