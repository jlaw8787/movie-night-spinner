Freedom Town croquetas de jamón

Camp food from under the freeway, before the green card

Prep 15 min, cook 22 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the bechamel overnight; crumb up to 4 hours ahead and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 70g plain flour
  • 500ml warm milk
  • 100g serrano ham, finely chopped
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g fine breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 1 lime, cut in wedges
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for 2 minutes, then whisk in the warm milk and cook until very thick.
  2. Stir in the ham and nutmeg, spread on a tray, cover and chill overnight.
  3. Roll into 16 logs, dip in egg and then breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in the oil at 180°C in batches until golden and serve with lime.

TostonesVegetarian

Garlic mojo, Little Havana's side order

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Fry once up to 2 hours ahead; second fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 60ml olive oil
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Peel the plantains and cut into 3cm chunks.
  2. Fry at 160°C for 4 minutes until soft.
  3. Smash flat with a heavy glass and fry again at 190°C until crisp.
  4. Mix the garlic, lime juice and olive oil, spoon over and finish with flaky salt.

Mains

Ropa vieja

Shredded beef with capsicum, rice and black beans

Prep 5 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Beef up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled in its broth
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg flank steak
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 1/2 onions
  • 2 capsicums, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 80g green olives
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 800g tinned black beans (2 tins)
  1. Simmer the steak with the bay leaves and half an onion in water until tender. Shred it and keep 250ml of the broth.
  2. Fry the sliced onion, capsicums and garlic in olive oil until soft.
  3. Add the cumin, oregano, tomatoes, olives, beef and broth and simmer.
  4. Cook the rice and warm the black beans, and serve alongside.

Miami blimp guava pastelitosVegetarian

Puff pastry squares of guava paste and cream cheese, sugared on top, for Mama Montana

Prep 18 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 120g guava paste
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  1. Cut the pastry sheets into 8 squares in all.
  2. Put a slice of guava paste and a teaspoon of cream cheese in the middle of 4 squares.
  3. Cover with the other squares, press the edges with a fork, brush with egg and sprinkle with sugar.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed.

Babylon Club mojitoVegetarian

Mint, lime and white rum over crushed ice. The eyes, chico.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 32 mint leaves
  • 100ml lime juice (about 4 limes)
  • 200ml white rum
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  • 200ml soda water
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool to make syrup.
  2. In each tall glass, gently muddle mint with some syrup and lime juice.
  3. Add rum, fill with crushed ice, top with soda and stir.
