Hermosa Beach fish tacos

Lager battered fish strips in corn tortillas with cabbage and lime crema.

Prep 23 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Shred the cabbage and make the lime crema up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; whisk the batter and fry the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml lager
  • 500g flathead fillets
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 chipotle in adobo
  • 2 limes
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Whisk the flour and cold lager into a batter.
  2. Mix the sour cream with chipotle and lime juice to make crema.
  3. Shred the cabbage and cut the flathead into strips.
  4. Dip the fish in batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with cabbage, crema and lime wedges, 8 min.

Cart lime chilli peanutsVegetarian

Roasted peanuts with lime zest, chilli and sea salt, in little foil packets.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week
Recipe for 4
  • 300g peanuts
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  1. Toss the peanuts with lime zest, chilli powder and sea salt.
  2. Roast at 160°C, stirring once, 15 min.
  3. Cool and fold into four little foil packets, 15 min.

Del Amo food court teriyaki skewers

Teriyaki chicken skewers charred with sesame, from a Del Amo bag of cash and beach towels.

How it is made
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 80ml mirin
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 12 skewers
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Chicken thigh glazed in sticky soy, ginger and mirin teriyaki, charred on skewers, with sesame, spring onion and pickled cucumber in paper food court boats. Simmer 80ml soy, 80ml mirin, 2 tbsp brown sugar and grated ginger for 4 minutes until syrupy. Thread 800g diced chicken thighs onto 12 skewers. Grill on high for 10 minutes, turning and brushing with the glaze in the last 4 minutes until sticky and charred. Slice a cucumber thin and toss with 2 tbsp rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar. Serve in paper boats with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Max Cherry's steak dinner

Seared sirloin, loaded baked potato and iceberg wedge with blue cheese dressing.

Prep 20 min, cook 1 hr 15 min · Make ahead: Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; bake the potatoes and sear the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 potatoes
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 300g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C for an hour.
  2. Cut the iceberg into four wedges and mash the blue cheese with some of the sour cream.
  3. Sear the steaks, pull at 54°C and rest, 15 min.
  4. Spoon the dressing over the wedges and load the potatoes with sour cream and chives.

Ordell's South Bay spicy fried chicken

Buttermilk-brined and shatter-crisp, a hot sauce drizzle, smoky greens and baked mac and cheese.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 bunches kale
  • 100g bacon
  • cider vinegar
  • 300g macaroni
  • 200g cheddar
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 egg
  1. Buttermilk-brined chicken fried shatter-crisp, drizzled with hot sauce chilli, with smoky braised greens and a creamy baked mac and cheese. Brine 1.5kg chicken pieces overnight in 500ml buttermilk with 2 tbsp hot sauce. Dredge in 300g flour with 2 tsp paprika and fry at 165°C until 74°C inside. Warm 4 tbsp honey with 1 tsp chilli flakes and drizzle over. Wilt 2 bunches kale with bacon and cider vinegar. Toss 300g cooked macaroni with 200g grated cheddar, 250ml milk and 1 egg and bake at 180°C for 30 minutes.

Max Cherry's walnut banana breadVegetarian

Moist banana bread studded with walnuts, sliced and buttered.

Prep 12 min, cook 1 hr 13 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 160g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 80g walnuts
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Mash the bananas with part of the melted butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla.
  2. Fold in the flour and walnuts and pour into a loaf tin.
  3. Bake at 170°C, 55 min.
  4. Beat the soft butter with the maple syrup and a pinch of salt.
  5. Toast thick slices and spread with the maple butter, 10 min.

Ordell's blood orange screwdriver

Vodka over ice with fresh blood orange juice, a deep red screwdriver.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 50ml water
  • 1 piece fresh ginger, 3cm
  • 5 blood oranges
  • 2 oranges
  • 180ml vodka
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and sliced ginger for 5 minutes.
  2. Cool, 15 min.
  3. Squeeze the oranges.
  4. Fill tall glasses with ice and add vodka and ginger syrup to each.
  5. Top with the mixed juice, stir and add a blood orange wheel.
