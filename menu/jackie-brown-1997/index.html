Hermosa Beach fish tacos

Lager battered fish strips in corn tortillas with cabbage and chipotle crema

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Shred the cabbage and make the chipotle crema up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; whisk the batter and fry the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml lager
  • 500g flathead fillets
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 chipotle in adobo
  • 2 limes
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Whisk the flour and cold lager into a batter.
  2. Mix the sour cream with chipotle and lime juice to make crema.
  3. Shred the cabbage and cut the flathead into strips.
  4. Dip the fish in batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 180C for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with cabbage, crema and lime wedges.

Cabo flight lime chilli peanutsVegetarian

Roasted peanuts with lime zest, chilli and flaky salt, in little foil packets

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week
Recipe for 4
  • 300g peanuts
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  1. Toss the peanuts with lime zest, chilli powder and flaky salt.
  2. Roast at 160C, stirring once.
  3. Cool and fold into four little foil packets.

Mains

Max Cherry's steak dinner

Seared sirloin, loaded baked potato and iceberg wedge with blue cheese dressing

Prep 20 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; bake the potatoes and sear the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 potatoes
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 300g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200C for an hour.
  2. Cut the iceberg into four wedges and mash the blue cheese with some of the sour cream.
  3. Sear the steaks, pull at 54C and rest.
  4. Spoon the dressing over the wedges and load the potatoes with sour cream and chives.

Max Cherry's walnut banana breadVegetarian

Banana loaf with walnuts. Put on the Delfonics.

Prep 20 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 160g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 80g walnuts
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Mash the bananas with part of the melted butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla.
  2. Fold in the flour and walnuts and pour into a loaf tin.
  3. Bake at 170C.
  4. Beat the soft butter with the maple syrup and a pinch of salt.
  5. Toast thick slices and spread with the maple butter.

Ordell's blood orange screwdriverVegetarian

Vodka and blood orange over ice. Let me have a screwdriver, homes.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 50ml water
  • 1 piece fresh ginger, 3cm
  • 5 blood oranges
  • 2 oranges
  • 180ml vodka
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and sliced ginger for 5 minutes.
  2. Cool.
  3. Squeeze the oranges.
  4. Fill tall glasses with ice and add vodka and ginger syrup to each.
  5. Top with the mixed juice, stir and add a blood orange wheel.
