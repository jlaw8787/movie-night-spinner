Kimberly's road trip pretzel bitesVegetarian

Soft pretzel bites with flaky salt and a sweet honey mustard dip.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best baked the same day; mix the honey mustard up to 5 days ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1.5 L water
  • 60g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 3 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Roll the dough into 4 long ropes and cut into 3cm bites.
  3. Bring the water and bicarb to the boil and boil the bites for 30 seconds in batches, then drain.
  4. Lay on the tray, brush with egg and sprinkle with the salt.
  5. Bake until deep brown.
  6. Mix the mustard, honey and mayonnaise and serve with the warm bites.

Death's design devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs, piped and dusted with paprika. Start at the far end of the plate.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; dust with paprika just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes.
  2. Cool in iced water.
  3. Peel, halve lengthways and scoop the yolks into a bowl.
  4. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and salt until smooth and pipe back into the whites.
  5. Dust with the paprika and scatter with chives.

Evan's jackpot spaghetti and meatballs

Meatballs in red sauce over spaghetti. Evan, watch the stove.

Prep 3 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead; the meatballs in sauce keep 3 days or freeze for a month; cook the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 60ml milk
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g parmesan, grated, for the meatballs
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed, for the meatballs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced, for the sauce
  • 700g tomato passata
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 40g parmesan, grated, for serving
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix with the beef and pork mince, egg, parmesan for the meatballs, the crushed garlic and half the salt. Roll into 20 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil in a wide pan and lift out.
  3. Soften the onion and sauce garlic in the pan, add the passata, the basil stalks and the rest of the salt, return the meatballs and simmer.
  4. Meanwhile cook 400g spaghetti in well salted boiling water until al dente and drain.
  5. Toss the spaghetti with a little sauce, top with the meatballs, torn basil leaves and the parmesan for serving.

Kat Jennings' Route 23 chicken caesar wraps

Chicken, bacon and parmesan rolled in wraps, packed in Kat's SUV for Route 23.

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Grill the chicken and make the dressing up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken breast
  • 4 rashers of bacon
  • 1 cos lettuce, shredded
  • 40g parmesan, shaved
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 large soft wraps
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, a squeeze of lemon, garlic, mustard and half the parmesan for the dressing.
  2. Rub the chicken with oil and salt and grill until cooked, then rest and slice. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  3. Toss the cos with the dressing and the rest of the parmesan.
  4. Fill the wraps with the lettuce, chicken and crumbled bacon, roll tight and halve. Serve with tomatoes and cucumber.

Spring break key lime pieVegetarian

Tangy key lime pie. Only new life can defeat Death; Isabella's baby gets the first slice.

Prep 10 min, cook 33 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and chill up to 2 days ahead; whip and spread the cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 395g can sweetened condensed milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 6 limes, zested and juiced
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Mix the biscuit crumbs with the butter and press into a 23cm pie tin.
  2. Bake the base.
  3. Whisk the condensed milk, egg yolks, lime zest and 150ml lime juice until smooth.
  4. Pour into the base and bake until just set.
  5. Cool, then chill.
  6. Whip the cream with the icing sugar and spread over the pie.

Daytona Beach rum punch

Golden rum with pineapple, orange and lime over ice, with a grenadine sunset.

Prep 6 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix the rum and juices up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; pour over ice and add the grenadine to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml golden rum
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp grenadine
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  1. Stir the rum, pineapple juice, orange juice and lime juice in a jug.
  2. Chill.
  3. Pour over ice in 4 tall glasses, drizzle in the grenadine so it sinks and add an orange slice.
