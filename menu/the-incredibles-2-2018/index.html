Pesto parmesan pinwheelsVegetarian

Puff pastry rolled with basil pesto and parmesan, sliced and baked

Prep 17 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 4 tbsp basil pesto
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Spread the pastry with pesto and sprinkle with parmesan.
  2. Roll up tightly and slice into rounds.
  3. Lay on a tray and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.

Happy Platter cheeseburger slidersOn the printed menu

Cheddar and pickle beef sliders with fries, for Violet's movie date with Tony

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 3 slices cheddar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 500g frozen fries
  1. Beef sliders with cheddar and pickles on soft buns, with a little bowl of fries. Shape 500g beef mince into 12 small balls and season. Smash them thin onto a very hot greased pan or flat plate and cook 2 minutes until crusty, flip and top each with a quarter slice of cheddar for 1 minute more. Toast 12 slider buns. Mix 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp tomato sauce and 1 tbsp chopped pickles for burger sauce. Build each bun with sauce, a patty and pickle slices. Serve with a bowl of 500g oven fries baked at 220°C for 20 minutes.

Spaghetti with beef ragu

Beef ragu with spaghetti and parmesan, on Winston's dime in the new mansion.

Prep 25 min, cook 72 min · Make ahead: Make the ragu up to 3 days ahead and reheat; boil the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  1. Dice the carrots and celery.
  2. Brown the mince in the oil.
  3. Add the carrot and celery and soften.
  4. Add the tomatoes and simmer.
  5. Boil the spaghetti until al dente.
  6. Toss with the sauce and plenty of parmesan.

Edna's lemon dill salmon and asparagusOn the printed menu

Salmon and asparagus with lemon dill butter. Edna babysits

How it is made
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 600g baby potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Salmon fillets roasted with lemon and dill, with crushed potatoes and asparagus, plated very neatly. Halve 600g baby potatoes and boil 15 minutes until tender, then drain and lightly crush with 30g butter, salt and chopped dill. Lay 4 salmon fillets on a lined tray, season, top with lemon slices and dill and drizzle with oil. Roast at 200°C for 12 minutes. Add 2 bunches trimmed asparagus to the tray for the last 6 minutes. Plate neatly with the crushed potatoes and a spoon of crème fraîche mixed with lemon zest.

Jack-Jack's choc chip cookiesVegetarian

Soft choc chip cookies. Say no to the baby and he turns monster.

Prep 22 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 225g plain flour
  • 150g dark chocolate chips
  1. Beat the butter and brown sugar until creamy, then beat in the egg and vanilla.
  2. Mix in the flour and chocolate chips.
  3. Roll into balls onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 180°C until soft in the middle.
  5. Cool on the tray.

Backyard fight browniesVegetarian

Fudgy chocolate brownies. Bob's first week as a stay-at-home parent.

Prep 14 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1l cold milk
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour.
  4. Pour into a lined tin.
  5. Bake at 170°C.
  6. Cool in the tin.
  7. Cut into squares and serve each with a glass of cold milk.

Elastigirl's raspberry lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice and raspberry syrup over ice

Prep 11 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen raspberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 lemons
  • 1l cold water
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Simmer some of the raspberries with the sugar and water.
  2. Strain to make a syrup.
  3. Cool the syrup.
  4. Juice the lemons.
  5. Mix the syrup, lemon juice and cold water in a jug, add ice and the rest of the raspberries, and stir well.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Jack-Jack fights the raccoon in the backyard.
