Snacks

Elf mission control popcornVegetarian

Popcorn drizzled with white chocolate and red and green sprinkles

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight once set
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 2 tbsp red and green sprinkles
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the popping corn and spread on a lined tray.
  2. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle all over.
  3. Scatter with sprinkles and a pinch of sea salt.
  4. Leave to set.
  5. Break into clusters.

Mains

North Pole Christmas roast

Rolled turkey breast with bacon sage stuffing, roast potatoes and brussels sprouts

Prep 30 min, cook 94 min · Make ahead: Make the stuffing up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; stuff, roll and roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg turkey breast
  • 200g bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 6 sage leaves
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g brussels sprouts
  • 100g cranberry sauce
  1. Fry the bacon and onion with sage and breadcrumbs for a stuffing.
  2. Butterfly the turkey breast, spread with stuffing, roll and tie.
  3. Roast at 180C with the potatoes alongside.
  4. Rest the turkey and roast the brussels sprouts.
  5. Serve sliced with sprouts and cranberry sauce.

Sweets

Grandsanta's old-school mince pies

Shortcrust mince pies with orange zest and a pastry star on top

Prep 30 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight; warm at 160C and dust with icing sugar to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 400g fruit mince
  • 1 orange
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a patty tin, saving offcuts for stars.
  2. Fill with fruit mince mixed with orange zest.
  3. Top each with a small pastry star and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 190C.
  5. Cool a little and dust with icing sugar.

Christmas pudding with orange custardVegetarian

Hot Christmas pudding with orange vanilla custard and whipped cream

Prep 20 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make the orange custard up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently; whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and heat the pudding on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Christmas pudding
  • 500ml custard
  • 1 orange
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the pudding following the packet directions until hot right through.
  2. Warm the custard gently with the orange zest and vanilla, stirring, without boiling.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Cut the pudding into thick slices, pour over the custard and top with cream and a little more zest.

Drinks

Reindeer gingerbread eggnogVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled custard eggnog with ginger, cinnamon and nutmeg, gingernut cookie on top

Prep 20 min, cook 117 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream, for the eggnog
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 100ml thickened cream, for whipping
  • 2 gingernut biscuits
  1. Whisk the egg yolks with the brown sugar in a pot.
  2. Stir in the milk, cream, ginger, cinnamon and nutmeg.
  3. Heat gently, stirring, until it coats the back of a spoon, without boiling.
  4. Strain and chill for 2 hours.
  5. Pour into glasses, top with whipped cream and crumble a gingernut over each.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Arthur and Grandsanta take off in the old sleigh.
