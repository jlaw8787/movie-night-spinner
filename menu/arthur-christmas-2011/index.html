Snacks

Elf mission control popcornVegetarian

Popcorn drizzled with white chocolate and red and green sprinkles.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight once set
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 2 tbsp red and green sprinkles
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the popping corn and spread on a lined tray.
  2. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle all over, 5 min.
  3. Scatter with sprinkles and a pinch of sea salt.
  4. Leave to set, 30 min.
  5. Break into clusters.

Mains

North Pole Christmas roast

Rolled turkey breast with bacon sage stuffing, roast potatoes and brussels sprouts.

Prep 11 min, cook 113 min · Make ahead: Make the stuffing up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; stuff, roll and roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 200g bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 6 sage leaves
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g brussels sprouts
  • 100g cranberry sauce
  1. Fry the bacon and onion with sage and breadcrumbs for a stuffing, 8 min.
  2. Butterfly the turkey breast, spread with stuffing, roll and tie.
  3. Roast at 180°C with the potatoes alongside, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Rest the turkey and roast the brussels sprouts, 15 min.
  5. Serve sliced with sprouts and cranberry sauce.

Cornish pasties for Gwen

Short pastry round peppery skirt, potato, swede and onion, crimped, for the letters department.

How it is made
  • 150g butter
  • 400g plain flour
  • 400g beef skirt
  • 1 potato
  • 1 swede
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar tomato relish
  1. Short pastry filled with diced beef skirt, potato, swede and onion with plenty of pepper, crimped along the side, baked golden with tomato relish. Rub 150g cold butter into 400g plain flour with a pinch of salt and bind with about 120ml cold water, then chill 30 minutes. Dice 400g beef skirt, 1 potato, a quarter swede and an onion small and toss with salt and lots of pepper. Roll the pastry into 4 rounds, pile the filling on one half, fold and crimp. Brush with egg and bake at 180°C for 50 minutes.

Sweets

Grandsanta's old-school mince pies

Shortcrust mince pies with orange zest and a pastry star on top.

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight; warm at 160°C and dust with icing sugar to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 400g fruit mince
  • 1 orange
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a patty tin, saving offcuts for stars.
  2. Fill with fruit mince mixed with orange zest.
  3. Top each with a small pastry star and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 190°C, 18 min.
  5. Cool a little and dust with icing sugar, 10 min.

Christmas pudding with orange custardVegetarian

Hot Christmas pudding with orange vanilla custard and whipped cream.

Prep 6 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the orange custard up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently; whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and heat the pudding on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Christmas pudding
  • 500ml custard
  • 1 orange
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the pudding following the packet directions until hot right through, 15 min.
  2. Warm the custard gently with the orange zest and vanilla, stirring, without boiling, 5 min.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Cut the pudding into thick slices, pour over the custard and top with cream and a little more zest.

Drinks

Reindeer gingerbread eggnogNo alcohol

Chilled custard eggnog with ginger, cinnamon and nutmeg, gingernut cookie on top.

Prep 9 min, cook 8 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream, for the eggnog
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 100ml thickened cream, for whipping
  • 2 gingernut biscuits
  1. Whisk the egg yolks with the brown sugar in a pot, 3 min.
  2. Stir in the milk, cream, ginger, cinnamon and nutmeg.
  3. Heat gently, stirring, until it coats the back of a spoon, without boiling, 8 min.
  4. Strain and chill for 2 hours.
  5. Pour into glasses, top with whipped cream and crumble a gingernut over each.

When to serve
