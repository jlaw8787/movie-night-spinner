Snacks

Flint coney dogs

Grilled hot dogs with spiced beef chilli sauce, mustard and onion.

Prep 0 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Cook the coney sauce up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; reheat it, grill the frankfurts and fill the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 1 onion
  • 60ml water
  1. Brown the beef mince until very fine and dry, then add the chilli powder, the cumin, the paprika and the water and cook 10 minutes.
  2. Grill the frankfurts and put in the hot dog buns with mustard, the coney sauce and diced onion, 13 min.

Coconut shrimp

Coconut breaded shrimp, fried golden, with mango chilli sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the mango sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and crumb the prawns up to 4 hours ahead; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green king prawns
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g desiccated coconut
  • 60g panko crumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 1 mango
  • 3 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 1 red chilli
  1. Peel the green king prawns, leaving tails on.
  2. Dust in the plain flour, dip in the beaten eggs, then press into the desiccated coconut mixed with the panko.
  3. Fry in oil at 180°C for 2 minutes until golden and drain.
  4. Simmer the flesh of the mango with the sweet chilli sauce, the juice of the lime and the chopped red chilli for 3 minutes, then blitz smooth.
  5. Serve the prawns hot with the sauce.

Jackie Moon's barbecue pork ribs

Slow-baked ribs glazed with barbecue sauce, buttered corn and slaw.

Prep 0 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Rub and wrap the ribs up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake low and slow, then glaze and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork ribs
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 250ml barbecue sauce
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bag coleslaw mix
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Rub the pork ribs with salt, pepper and smoked paprika, wrap in foil and bake at 160°C for 2 hours.
  2. Unwrap, brush with the barbecue sauce and grill until sticky, 5 min.
  3. Boil the corn cobs and toss in the butter. Serve with the ribs and the coleslaw mixed with mayonnaise, 10 min.

Let's get Tropical teriyaki chicken skewers

Grilled thigh and pineapple, sticky teriyaki.

Prep 17 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Thread and marinate the skewers up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, cubed
  • 1/2 pineapple, cut in chunks
  • 100ml teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 8 skewers
  1. Thread the chicken and pineapple onto skewers and coat in half the teriyaki.
  2. Rinse the rice and simmer it, covered, until the water is gone. Rest it with the lid on, 15 min.
  3. Grill the skewers, turning and brushing with the rest of the teriyaki, until charred and cooked through, 12 min.
  4. Serve on the rice with sesame and spring onion.

Seventies orange and cream cakeVegetarian

Orange butter cake with mascarpone cream and orange glaze.

Prep 25 min, cook 71 min · Make ahead: Bake and cool the cakes up to 1 day ahead, airtight; whip the mascarpone cream and fill and top on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 oranges
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 250g mascarpone
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 120g icing sugar
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C, 8 min.
  2. Beat the butter and the caster sugar, add the eggs one at a time and the zest of the oranges, then fold in the self-raising flour and the orange juice.
  3. Divide between two lined 20cm tins and bake 25 minutes.
  4. Cool.
  5. Whip the mascarpone, the cream, the icing sugar and the vanilla.
  6. Sandwich and top the cakes with the cream and drizzle over the icing sugar mixed with the orange juice.

Coffee Black's White Russian

Vodka, coffee liqueur and cream stirred over ice.

Prep 4 min · Make ahead: Chill the vodka and coffee liqueur up to 1 day ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 120ml thickened cream
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Fill short glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in the vodka and coffee liqueur.
  3. Float the cream on top and stir gently.
