Snacks

Himalayan beef momo with tomato achar

Steamed beef and ginger dumplings with a roasted tomato chilli dip

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Cook straight after filling
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 1 handful of coriander, chopped
  • 30 round dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tomatoes, roasted
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 clove of garlic, for the achar
  1. Mix the mince, onion, garlic, ginger and coriander.
  2. Fill the dumpling wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam in batches.
  4. Blitz the roasted tomatoes with the chilli and garlic for achar.

Cream cheese fingers with cucumber or salmon and dill, crusts off

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, cover with damp paper
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  1. Spread the bread with cream cheese.
  2. Fill half with cucumber and half with smoked salmon and dill.
  3. Cut off the crusts and cut into neat fingers.
  4. Lay on a silver tray.

Narrows pan-fried pork potstickersOn the printed menu

Pork, wombok and ginger parcels pleated by hand, crunchy underneath, black vinegar and chilli oil

How it is made
  • 1 wombok
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 30 gow gee wrappers
  • 1 bottle black vinegar
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Pork, cabbage and ginger dumplings pleated by hand, fried until the bottoms crunch then steamed, with soy, black vinegar and chilli oil. Salt 2 cups finely shredded wombok for 10 minutes, then squeeze dry. Mix with 400g pork mince, 1 tbsp grated ginger, 2 sliced spring onions, 2 tbsp soy and 1 tsp sesame oil. Put a teaspoon in each of 30 round gow gee wrappers, wet the edges and pleat closed. Fry flat side down in oil for 2 minutes, add 100ml water, cover and steam 5 minutes, then uncover until crisp. Mix 3 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp black vinegar and chilli oil to dip.

Wayne Manor Sunday roast

Rib roast, Yorkshires and gravy, Alfred's spread for Bruce's return

Prep 5 min, cook 2.5 hours, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Season the beef and leave it uncovered in the fridge overnight, up to 1 day ahead; rest 20 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400ml beef stock
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the beef well and leave overnight if possible.
  2. Heat the oven to 220C and roast the beef, then drop to 160C until 52C inside.
  3. Rest the beef under foil.
  4. Roast the potatoes in oil.
  5. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk and bake in hot oiled tins at 220C.
  6. Make gravy with the stock and pan juices.

Himalayan spiced beef shin stewOn the printed menu

Bruce training in the snow with the League

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef shin
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar ground turmeric
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar garam masala
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 600ml beef stock
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Beef shin braised with ginger, garlic, cumin, chilli and tomato until it falls apart, finished with spinach, with steamed rice and warm flatbread. Brown 1.2kg diced beef shin in batches. Soften 2 onions, 4 garlic cloves and 2 tbsp grated ginger. Add 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli and 1 tsp garam masala and stir 1 minute. Return the beef with 1 tin crushed tomatoes and 600ml beef stock, cover and simmer 2.5 hours until tender. Stir in 100g spinach and coriander. Serve with steamed basmati and warm flatbread.

Bruce's birthday cakeVegetarian

Two-layer chocolate cake, one candle. Eat before the manor burns

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, for the cake
  • 200g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 150g dark chocolate, for the ganache
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 birthday candle
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line two tins.
  2. Melt the chocolate for the cake and the butter, whisk in the eggs and sugar and fold in the flour.
  3. Bake in two tins.
  4. Cool, melt the ganache chocolate with the cream and let it thicken.
  5. Layer and cover the cake and add a single candle.

Applied Sciences elderflower gin collinsVegetarian

Gin, elderflower, lemon and cucumber, with mint. Does it come in black?

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the gin, cordial and lemon juice in a jug up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; slice the cucumber ribbons up to 4 hours ahead, then shake over ice and top with soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml gin
  • 80ml elderflower cordial
  • 80ml lemon juice
  • 1 cucumber
  • 400ml soda water
  • 6 cup ice
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice and 2 thin cucumber ribbons each.
  2. Shake the gin, cordial and lemon juice with ice and strain evenly into the glasses.
  3. Top with soda water, stir once and add a mint sprig. For non drinkers use chilled cucumber water instead of gin.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Bruce comes home to Wayne Manor and Alfred.
