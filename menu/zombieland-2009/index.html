Snacks

Enjoy the little things mini corn dogs

Cocktail franks in sweet corn batter, fried on sticks.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry batter up to a day ahead; whisk in the wet and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail frankfurts
  • 16 short bamboo skewers
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 180ml milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Pat the franks dry and push a skewer into each.
  2. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, salt, egg and milk into a thick batter and pour it into a tall glass.
  3. Dip each frank in the batter and fry in oil at 180°C, turning, until deep golden, 12 min.
  4. Drain and serve with mustard and tomato sauce.

Double tap loaded potato skins

Twice-baked potato skins with bacon and cheddar.

Prep 8 min, cook 1 hr 11 min · Make ahead: Bake and scoop the potatoes up to 2 days ahead; fill and bake again to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 medium potatoes, scrubbed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 rashers of bacon, chopped
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 125g sour cream
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  1. Rub the potatoes with oil and salt and bake at 200°C until tender, 50 min.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp, 6 min.
  3. Halve the potatoes, scoop out most of the flesh and save it for mash.
  4. Fill the skins with bacon and cheddar and bake again until crisp and melted, 15 min.
  5. Top with sour cream and spring onion.

Mad cow double cheeseburgers

Two smashed beef patties and American cheese.

Prep 5 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls and mix the sauce up to a day ahead; smash and cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince, 20 percent fat
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 soft potato buns
  • 1 brown onion, very finely sliced
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls and stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and mustard together.
  2. Heat a heavy pan until smoking, add a ball with some onion on top and smash it flat with a spatula, 5 min.
  3. Cook until the edges crisp, flip, add cheese and stack two patties per burger, 8 min.
  4. Toast the buns, spread with sauce and build with the patties and pickles, 5 min.

Riviera club sandwiches with fries

Turkey, bacon and tomato in toasted triple decks with fries, Hollywood mansion lunch.

Prep 12 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Cook the bacon and slice the fillings up to 4 hours ahead; toast and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 12 slices of white sandwich bread
  • 300g shaved roast turkey
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 baby cos lettuce
  • 6 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1kg frozen shoestring chips
  • 16 toothpicks
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp, 25 min.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp, 8 min.
  3. Toast the bread and spread each slice with mayonnaise, 5 min.
  4. Layer turkey, lettuce, bacon and tomato over three slices per sandwich.
  5. Pin with toothpicks, cut into triangles and serve with the chips.

Deep-fried Twinkies with vanilla ice creamVegetarian

Battered hot and dusted with powdered sugar, with vanilla ice cream.

Prep 9 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Freeze the Twinkies for at least 2 hours ahead; batter and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 Hostess Twinkies, frozen
  • 8 short bamboo skewers
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Whisk the flour, egg and milk into a smooth batter.
  2. Skewer the frozen Twinkies and dip them in the batter.
  3. Fry at 180°C for 2 to 3 minutes until golden, then drain.
  4. Dust with icing sugar and serve with ice cream.

Nut up or shut up peanut butter shakesNo alcohol

Peanut butter and banana blended thick with vanilla ice cream.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Freeze the bananas the night before; blend to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe bananas, sliced and frozen
  • 4 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml milk
  • 2 tbsp chocolate topping
  1. Blend the banana, peanut butter, ice cream and milk until thick.
  2. Swirl chocolate topping round 4 glasses and pour in the shakes.
