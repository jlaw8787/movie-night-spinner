Snacks

Clock tower corn on the cobVegetarian

Boiled then charred corn rolled in butter, flaky salt and smoked paprika.

Prep 0 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Boil the corn up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; char and butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Boil the corn in salted water.
  2. Char on a hot grill, turning, until blackened in spots.
  3. Melt the butter, roll each cob in it and sprinkle with flaky salt and smoked paprika.

Clara's apple slicesVegetarian

Fanned apple slices with honey and cinnamon whipped cream for dipping.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 crisp apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 250g thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Slice the apples and toss in the lemon juice so they stay white.
  2. Whip the cream with the honey and cinnamon.
  3. Fan the apples around a bowl of the cream for dipping.

Mains

Doc's blacksmith shop stew

Beef chuck braised with onion, carrot and dark ale, crusty bread.

Prep 13 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled, flavour improves
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 375ml dark ale
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C. Season the beef and brown in the oil in batches in a heavy ovenproof pot.
  2. Soften the sliced onions and chopped carrots in the same pot.
  3. Return the beef, add the ale and stock and bring to a simmer.
  4. Cover and braise in the oven until the beef falls apart.
  5. Serve with the crusty bread for mopping up.

Hill Valley festival smoked brisket

Smoked brisket and beans, carved off the Hill Valley pit.

How it is made
  • 2.5kg beef brisket
  • coarse salt
  • black peppercorns
  • 2 tins cannellini beans
  • 1 onion
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  • 30g butter
  1. Salt and pepper brisket smoked low and slow until it wobbles, sliced thick with molasses baked beans and buttery pan cornbread. Rub a 2.5kg brisket with 2 tbsp coarse salt and 2 tbsp cracked pepper. Smoke or roast at 120°C for about 10 hours until it reaches 95°C inside, wrapping in baking paper at 70°C. Rest 1 hour, then slice. Simmer 2 drained tins cannellini beans with 1 diced onion, 3 tbsp molasses, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp mustard and 250ml stock for 45 minutes. Bake cornbread mix in a buttered hot pan at 200°C for 20 minutes.

Clara's festival apple pieVegetarian

Double crust Granny Smith apple pie with cinnamon and nutmeg.

Prep 10 min, cook 72 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg Granny Smith apples
  • 120g caster sugar, for the filling
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar, for sprinkling
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Peel, core and slice the apples and toss with the filling sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and flour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill with the apples.
  3. Cover with the other two sheets, seal and trim the edges, cut a few steam vents.
  4. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with the remaining caster sugar.
  5. Bake until deep golden and the juices bubble. Rest before slicing.

Drinks

Wake-up juiceNo alcohol

Fresh lemon, ginger, honey and chilli topped with ginger beer, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice mix up to 1 day, top with ginger beer at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 3 lemons
  • 1 knob ginger (5 cm), grated
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 small red chilli, chopped
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Juice the lemons and blitz with the ginger, honey and chilli, then strain.
  2. Fill four glasses with ice and divide the juice between them.
  3. Top each with very cold ginger beer.

When to serve

Pour the drinks when Marty walks into the Palace Saloon and asks for water.
