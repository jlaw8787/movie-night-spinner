Snacks

Babysitter's popcornVegetarian

Buttered salted popcorn, half coated in brown sugar caramel

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 80g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Pop the kernels in a covered pot and tip into a bowl.
  2. Toss half with the melted butter portion and salt.
  3. Boil the sugar, remaining butter and golden syrup for 4 minutes and pour over the other half.
  4. Bake that half at 120C, stirring every 15 minutes.
  5. Mix both halves together.

Jack-o'-lantern cheese ball

Cheddar and cream cheese ball with smoked paprika, scored like a pumpkin

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Beat the cheddar with the cream cheese, half the paprika and the Worcestershire.
  2. Shape into a ball and chill.
  3. Score lines like a pumpkin and roll in the remaining paprika.
  4. Push a capsicum stem on top and serve with crackers.

Mains

Haddonfield chilli in a pumpkin

Beef and kidney bean chilli served in hollowed small pumpkins

Prep 30 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; hollow and roast the pumpkins on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 800g crushed tomatoes (2 tins)
  • 800g kidney beans (2 tins), drained
  • 4 small pumpkins
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and chilli powder.
  2. Add the tomatoes and beans and simmer.
  3. Hollow the pumpkins.
  4. Roast the pumpkins at 200C.
  5. Fill with chilli, sour cream and cheddar.

Sweets

Pumpkin pieVegetarian

Roasted pumpkin and mixed spice pie topped with a piped whipped cream mask

Prep 40 min, cook 140 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pumpkin
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  1. Roast the pumpkin until soft and puree.
  2. Line a pie dish with the pastry and blind-bake.
  3. Whisk the puree with eggs, sugar, half the cream and the spice.
  4. Pour into the case and bake at 170C.
  5. Cool completely.
  6. Whip the remaining cream and pipe a pale mask on top.

Hot apple ciderVegetarianNo alcohol

Apple juice simmered with cinnamon, cloves and orange peel, no alcohol

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Simmer and strain up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently and ladle into mugs
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 3 strips orange peel
  1. Combine the juice, cinnamon, cloves and orange peel in a saucepan.
  2. Simmer gently.
  3. Strain and ladle into four mugs while hot. Keep the porch light on.
