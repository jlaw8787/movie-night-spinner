Reimers' half-hour-late doughnutsVegetarian

Where are my donuts? Warm cake ones, cinnamon sugar

Prep 5 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Mix the cinnamon sugar up to 1 week ahead, airtight; warm the doughnuts and coat them on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 cake doughnuts
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Warm the doughnuts on a tray at 160C.
  2. Drop into a paper bag with the cinnamon sugar, fold the top and shake until coated.

Movie trivia snack mix

Pretzels, peanuts, cereal squares and seeds baked with Worcestershire butter

Prep 10 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pretzels
  • 150g peanuts
  • 150g cereal squares
  • 50g sunflower seeds
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  1. Toss the pretzels, peanuts, cereal squares and sunflower seeds with the melted butter, Worcestershire and garlic powder.
  2. Bake at 130C, stirring every 15 minutes.
  3. Cool before bagging up.

Maria's home cooking

Baked chicken enchiladas in red chilli sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce and poach and shred the chicken up to 2 days ahead, chilled; roll and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes (400g)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water for 15 minutes.
  2. Blitz the chillies with the crushed tomatoes and garlic and simmer 10 minutes into a sauce.
  3. Poach the chicken thighs in simmering water for 20 minutes, then shred.
  4. Roll the chicken in the corn tortillas and pack into a baking dish.
  5. Cover with the sauce and cheddar and bake at 190C for 20 minutes.
  6. Serve with the sour cream.

Couch stash doughnut holesVegetarian

Yeast dough balls fried golden and served warm

Prep 30 min, cook 74 min · Make ahead: Mix the dough and prove it up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, covered; shape, fry and roll in sugar on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the dough
  • 1 egg
  • 160ml warm milk
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar, for rolling
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, caster sugar, egg and warm milk into a soft dough and knead briefly.
  2. Cover and prove.
  3. Roll into small balls.
  4. Fry in the oil at 175C in batches until golden.
  5. Mix the rolling sugar with the cinnamon and roll the doughnuts in it while warm.

Reimers' night shift Irish coffeeVegetarian

Hot coffee with brown sugar and Irish whiskey, floated with lightly whipped cream

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; brew the coffee and stir in the sugar and whiskey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml strong coffee
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the coffee.
  2. Stir the sugar and whiskey into the hot coffee.
  3. Lightly whip the cream until thick but still pourable.
  4. Pour the coffee into mugs and float the cream on top over the back of a spoon.
