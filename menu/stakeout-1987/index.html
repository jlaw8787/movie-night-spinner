Reimers' half-hour-late doughnutsVegetarian

Warm cake doughnuts rolled in cinnamon sugar.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the cinnamon sugar up to 1 week ahead, airtight; warm the doughnuts and coat them on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 cake doughnuts
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Warm the doughnuts on a tray at 160°C.
  2. Drop into a paper bag with the cinnamon sugar, fold the top and shake until coated.

Movie trivia snack mix

Pretzels, peanuts, cereal squares and seeds baked with Worcestershire butter.

Prep 5 min, cook 53 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pretzels
  • 150g peanuts
  • 150g cereal squares
  • 50g sunflower seeds
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  1. Toss the pretzels, peanuts, cereal squares and sunflower seeds with the melted butter, Worcestershire and garlic powder.
  2. Bake at 130°C, stirring every 15 minutes.
  3. Cool before bagging up, 20 min.

Seattle hot-smoked salmon rye crisps

Flaked fish on crispbread with crème fraîche, capers, dill and lemon.

How it is made
  • 250g hot-smoked salmon
  • 1 box rye crispbread
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 capers
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Hot-smoked salmon flaked onto rye crispbread with crème fraîche, capers, dill and a squeeze of lemon. Slice half a capers very thin and cover with 3 tbsp white wine vinegar, 1 tsp sugar and a pinch of salt for 20 minutes. Break 16 rye crispbreads in half. Spread each with crème fraîche, top with a flake of hot-smoked salmon from 250g, a few pickled onion rings and a sprig of dill. Squeeze lemon over and crack black pepper on top.

Maria's home cooking

Baked chicken enchiladas in red chilli sauce.

Prep 1 min, cook 1 hr 4 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the sauce and poach and shred the chicken up to 2 days ahead, chilled; roll and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes (400g)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water for 15 minutes.
  2. Blitz the chillies with the crushed tomatoes and garlic and simmer 10 minutes into a sauce.
  3. Poach the chicken thighs in simmering water for 20 minutes, then shred.
  4. Roll the chicken in the corn tortillas and pack into a baking dish.
  5. Cover with the sauce and cheddar and bake at 190°C for 20 minutes.
  6. Serve with the sour cream.

Night shift beer-battered cod and fries

Cod in a lager batter with twice-fried fries, lemon and chunky caper tartar sauce.

How it is made
  • 700g cod fillets
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 300ml lager
  • 150g mayo
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • gherkins
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 lemons
  • malt vinegar
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  1. Cod fillets in a crisp lager batter, fried golden, with double-fried chips, lemon, malt vinegar and a chunky caper tartare sauce. Cut 1kg potatoes into chips, soak 30 minutes, dry and fry at 140°C for 6 minutes. Whisk 150g plain flour and 50g cornflour with 300ml ice-cold lager and salt. Dust 700g cod fillets in flour, dip in batter and fry at 180°C for 4 to 5 minutes. Fry the chips again at 190°C for 3 minutes. Mix 150g mayo with chopped capers, gherkins and dill. Serve with lemon and malt vinegar.

Couch stash doughnut holesVegetarian

Yeast dough balls fried golden and served warm.

Prep 32 min, cook 12 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Mix the dough and prove it up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, covered; shape, fry and roll in sugar on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the dough
  • 1 egg
  • 160ml warm milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar, for rolling
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, caster sugar, egg and warm milk into a soft dough and knead briefly, 12 min.
  2. Cover and prove, 1 hr.
  3. Roll into small balls.
  4. Fry in the oil at 175°C in batches until golden, 12 min.
  5. Mix the rolling sugar with the cinnamon and roll the doughnuts in it while warm, 5 min.

Reimers' night shift Irish coffee

Hot coffee with brown sugar and Irish whiskey, floated with lightly whipped cream.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; brew the coffee and stir in the sugar and whiskey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml strong coffee
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the coffee.
  2. Stir the sugar and whiskey into the hot coffee.
  3. Lightly whip the cream until thick but still pourable.
  4. Pour the coffee into mugs and float the cream on top over the back of a spoon.
