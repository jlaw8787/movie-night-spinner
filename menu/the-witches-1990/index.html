Snacks

Hotel tea sandwichesVegetarian

Egg and cress, cucumber cream cheese, and beetroot and dill finger sandwiches.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the egg filling and slice the beetroot up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; assemble and cut up to 3 hours ahead, covered with a damp tea towel
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp cress
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 100g cooked beetroot
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 2 tbsp dill
  • 12 slices white bread
  1. Boil the eggs, cool, peel and mash with the mayonnaise and cress.
  2. Slice the beetroot paper thin and dress with a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Use the white bread to make cucumber and cream cheese, egg, and beetroot, cream cheese and dill sandwiches.
  4. Cut off the crusts and cut into fingers.
  5. Lay on a tiered stand.

Hotel Excelsior crab sandwiches

Lemon chive crab mayo and cucumber on soft white bread, crusts off.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the crab filling up to 1 day ahead, chilled; assemble, trim and cut up to 2 hours ahead, covered with a damp tea towel
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 30g butter
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  1. Mix the crab with the mayonnaise, lemon zest, a squeeze of juice, chives and pepper.
  2. Butter the bread.
  3. Spread the crab over half the slices and lay thin cucumber on top.
  4. Close with the remaining bread, press gently, trim off the crusts and cut each into three fingers.

Helga's Norwegian gravlax

Salmon cured two days in salt, sugar and dill, sliced thin on rye, sweet mustard sauce.

How it is made
  • 600g skin-on salmon fillet
  • 60g salt
  • 60g sugar
  • 1 jar white pepper
  • 2 bunches dill
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  • 8 slices rye bread
  1. Salmon cured for two days in salt, sugar, dill and white pepper, sliced paper thin onto rye with a sweet mustard and dill sauce. Mix 60g salt, 60g sugar and 1 tsp crushed white pepper. Lay a 600g skin-on salmon fillet in a dish, cover with the cure and a big bunch of chopped dill, wrap tightly and weigh down. Refrigerate 48 hours, turning every 12 hours. Scrape off the cure and slice very thin. Mix 3 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp vinegar, 60ml oil and chopped dill. Serve on 8 slices rye bread.

Mains

Formula 86 watercress soup

Bright green watercress and leek soup. Luke's secret ingredient.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, chilled; reheat gently and stir in the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 40g butter
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 2 bunches watercress
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Sweat the leeks and potatoes in the butter.
  2. Add the stock and simmer.
  3. Stir in the watercress for a minute.
  4. Blitz until bright green.
  5. Finish with the cream.

Hotel Excelsior roast beef and Yorkshires

Sirloin roasted pink, roast potatoes, puffed batter puddings and gravy from the pan.

Prep 11 min, cook 81 min · Make ahead: Make the Yorkshire batter up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef sirloin roast
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 100ml vegetable oil
  • 140g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  1. Whisk 120g of the flour with the eggs, milk and a pinch of salt to a smooth batter and rest it.
  2. Rub the beef with mustard, salt and pepper and roast at 220°C with the potatoes in a little oil until medium rare.
  3. Rest the beef under foil. Turn the oven to 230°C, heat a little oil in each hole of a muffin tin, pour in the batter and bake until puffed.
  4. Stir the rest of the flour into the beef pan juices, whisk in the stock and simmer into gravy.
  5. Carve the beef and serve with the potatoes, Yorkshires and gravy.

Bruno's chocolate cakeVegetarian

Dark chocolate cake, cream glaze. Bruno's bar had Formula 86.

Prep 12 min, cook 55 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 350g dark chocolate
  • 200g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 30g cocoa
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Melt most of the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the sugar and fold in the chocolate, flour and cocoa.
  3. Bake at 170°C.
  4. Cool the cake.
  5. Melt the remaining chocolate with the cream and pour over. Serve thin slices.

Drinks

Seaside hotel peach iced teaNo alcohol

English breakfast tea sweetened with honey and blended peach, over ice.

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep, blend and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead in a jug in the fridge; pour over ice with lemon and mint to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 English breakfast tea bags
  • 800ml boiling water
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 ripe peaches
  • 200ml cold water
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water, then remove and stir in the honey.
  2. Cool.
  3. Blend the peaches with the cold water and strain into the tea.
  4. Chill.
  5. Pour over ice with lemon slices and mint.
