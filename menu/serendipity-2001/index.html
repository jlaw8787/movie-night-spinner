Gingerbread squaresVegetarian

Christmas shopping in Manhattan

Prep 23 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 150g golden syrup
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml milk
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a 20cm square tin. Melt the butter with the brown sugar and golden syrup.
  2. Stir into the flour, bicarbonate of soda, ginger and cinnamon with the egg and milk. Pour into the tin.
  3. Bake until firm.
  4. Cool in the tin.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the lemon juice, glaze the top and cut into squares.

Wollman Rink roasted chestnutsVegetarian

Ice skating in Central Park

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Score the chestnuts up to 1 day ahead; soak, roast and peel on the night while they are still warm
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chestnuts
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Score a cross through the shell of each chestnut.
  2. Soak in water and drain.
  3. Roast on a tray at 220C until the shells curl back.
  4. Wrap in a clean towel to steam.
  5. Peel while still warm, sprinkle with salt and serve.

Mains

Manhattan bistro steak

Sirloin seared and basted with butter and thyme, brandy peppercorn cream sauce, double-fried chips

Prep 20 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Cut and fry the frites the first time up to 3 hours ahead and leave on a tray; fry again, cook the steaks and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1l neutral oil, for frying
  • 4 sirloin steaks (about 200g each)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, for searing the steaks
  • 40g butter
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 tsp cracked black peppercorns
  • 40ml brandy
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin frites, rinse and dry thoroughly.
  2. Fry in 140C oil until cooked but pale, drain and cool.
  3. While the frites cool, season the steaks.
  4. Fry the frites again at 185C until crisp. Season with salt.
  5. Sear the steaks in a heavy hot pan with oil on both sides.
  6. Lower the heat, add the butter and thyme and baste briefly, then rest the steaks.
  7. Add the peppercorns to the pan, then the brandy, carefully and away from the flame, and simmer briefly.
  8. Add the stock and reduce, then stir in the cream and simmer. Serve with the steaks and frites.

Frozen hot chocolateVegetarian

Dark chocolate and cocoa blended with milk and ice until slushy, whipped cream and shavings

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Melt the chocolate mix and cool it completely up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; blend with the milk and ice and top with the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate, for melting
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 350ml full cream milk
  • 450g ice (about 3 cups)
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 20g dark chocolate, shaved, to finish
  1. Gently melt the chocolate with the cocoa, sugar and part of the milk, stirring until smooth.
  2. Cool completely.
  3. Blend with the rest of the milk and the ice until slushy.
  4. Divide into small glasses, spoon over softly whipped cream and finish with shaved chocolate.

Mulled cranberry punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry juice warmed with orange slices, cinnamon and cloves, served hot in mugs

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Warm the juice with the orange and spices up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently without boiling and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cranberry juice
  • 2 oranges
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  1. Warm the juice with one sliced orange, the cinnamon and cloves over low heat without boiling.
  2. Strain into mugs and float a fresh orange slice on top of each.
