Snacks

Pâté maison

Chicken livers seared pink and flamed with cognac, blended with butter, shallot and thyme. Cornichons, warm baguette.

Prep 11 min, cook 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken livers, trimmed
  • 200g butter
  • 30ml cognac
  • 1 shallot, diced
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 1 jar cornichons
  • 1 baguette
  1. Soften the shallot in a little butter, then sear the livers until just pink.
  2. Add the cognac and flame it off carefully.
  3. Blitz the livers, shallot and thyme with soft butter until smooth, then pack into a dish.
  4. Pour melted butter over the top and chill until set.
  5. Serve with cornichons and warm baguette, 5 min.

Mains

Steak frites béarnaise

Sirloin seared hard and rested, warm tarragon and shallot butter sauce, hand-cut potatoes fried twice.

Prep 0 min, cook 45 min
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 1 small bunch tarragon
  • 1 shallot
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 200g butter, clarified and warm
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Reduce the vinegar with tarragon and shallot until almost dry. Cut the potatoes into chips and dry them.
  2. Fry the chips in the oil at 140°C, then drain, 10 min.
  3. Whisk the yolks with the reduction over low heat until thick, then drizzle in the warm butter to make the sauce.
  4. Season the steaks with salt and pepper, sear hard, then rest, 12 min.
  5. Fry the chips again until crisp and serve with the steaks and sauce.

Sweets

Crème caramelVegetarian

Vanilla custard baked in a water bath, chilled, turned out under amber syrup.

Prep 0 min, cook 60 min, then 8.5 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 vanilla bean
  1. Melt part of the sugar to an amber caramel and pour into 4 ramekins, 10 min.
  2. Whisk the eggs and yolks with the remaining sugar, milk and the seeds of the vanilla bean, then pour over the caramel.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150°C until just set.
  4. Chill overnight, then run a knife around the edge and turn out.

Drinks

Mauresque

Pastis and orgeat over ice, clouded with water.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml pastis
  • 60ml orgeat syrup
  • 12 ice cubes
  • 600ml chilled water
  1. Put pastis and orgeat in each of 4 tall glasses, add ice, then pour in chilled water slowly and watch it cloud. Stir once and serve a jug of cold water on the side.

When to serve

Serve the steak frites when Charnier sits down to dinner while Popeye freezes outside with his pizza.
