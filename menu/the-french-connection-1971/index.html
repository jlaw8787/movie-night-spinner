Frog One's bistro pâté

Chicken livers flamed with cognac, blitzed with butter and thyme, cornichons and warm baguette

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken livers, trimmed
  • 200g butter
  • 30ml cognac
  • 1 shallot, diced
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 1 jar cornichons
  • 1 baguette
  1. Soften the shallot in a little butter, then sear the livers until just pink.
  2. Add the cognac and flame it off carefully.
  3. Blitz the livers, shallot and thyme with soft butter until smooth, then pack into a dish.
  4. Pour melted butter over the top and chill until set.
  5. Serve with cornichons and warm baguette.

Santa suit coffee and doughnutsVegetarian

Cinnamon sugar cake rings and drip brew in paper cups, then a question about Poughkeepsie

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 8 cake doughnuts
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 60g ground coffee
  • 1l water
  1. Warm the doughnuts in a 160°C oven.
  2. Toss in the sugar mixed with the cinnamon.
  3. Brew the coffee with the water and pour into paper cups.

Popeye's stakeout pizza slicesOn the printed menu

Popeye eating a slice in the cold while Charnier dines in style across the road

How it is made
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g passata
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 20g parmesan
  • chilli flakes
  • olive oil
  1. Crisp-bottomed tray pizza with tomato, mozzarella, oregano and chilli, cut into slices you can eat standing up in the street. Press 500g pizza dough into an oiled 30 x 40cm tray and rest 20 minutes. Mix 200g passata with 1 crushed garlic clove, 1 tsp dried oregano and salt and spread over the dough. Top with 250g grated mozzarella and 20g grated parmesan. Bake at 240°C for 15 minutes until the base is crisp and the cheese is browned. Finish with chilli flakes and cut into 12 slices.

Charnier's steak frites

Seared rested sirloin with tarragon béarnaise and hand-cut chips fried twice

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Sauce best made just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 1 small bunch tarragon
  • 1 shallot
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 200g butter, clarified and warm
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Reduce the vinegar with tarragon and shallot until almost dry. Cut the potatoes into chips and dry them.
  2. Fry the chips in the oil at 140°C, then drain.
  3. Whisk the yolks with the reduction over low heat until thick, then drizzle in the warm butter to make the sauce.
  4. Season the steaks with salt and pepper, sear hard, then rest.
  5. Fry the chips again until crisp and serve with the steaks and sauce.

Marseille fish stew with rouilleOn the printed menu

Charnier's home port of Marseille, where the film opens

How it is made
  • 1 onion
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • saffron threads
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 1 litre fish stock
  • 1 orange
  • 500g firm white fish fillets
  • 12 green prawns
  • 500g mussels
  • 100g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • cayenne pepper
  • 1 baguette
  • olive oil
  1. Firm white fish, prawns and mussels in a saffron, fennel and tomato broth, with toasted baguette and garlicky rouille. Soften 1 sliced onion, 1 sliced fennel bulb and 3 garlic cloves in 3 tbsp olive oil for 10 minutes. Add a pinch of saffron, 400g tinned tomatoes, 1 litre fish stock and a strip of orange peel, and simmer 20 minutes. Add 500g firm white fish chunks for 4 minutes, then 12 green prawns and 500g mussels, covered, for 3 minutes until open. Mix 100g mayonnaise with 1 crushed garlic clove and a pinch each of saffron and cayenne. Serve with toasted baguette.

Crème caramelVegetarian

Silky vanilla custard baked over dark caramel in a water bath, chilled and turned out

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min, then 8.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 vanilla bean
  1. Melt part of the sugar to an amber caramel and pour into 4 ramekins.
  2. Whisk the eggs and yolks with the remaining sugar, milk and the seeds of the vanilla bean, then pour over the caramel.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150°C until just set.
  4. Chill overnight, then run a knife around the edge and turn out.

Marseille mauresqueVegetarian

A taste of Charnier's Marseille, where the heroin deal begins

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml pastis
  • 60ml orgeat syrup
  • 12 ice cubes
  • 600ml chilled water
  1. Put pastis and orgeat in each of 4 tall glasses, add ice, then pour in chilled water slowly and watch it cloud. Stir once and serve a jug of cold water on the side.
