Adrian's aranciniVegetarian

Passed round while Rocky works the room

Prep 30 min, cook 93 min · Make ahead: Make and chill the risotto up to 2 days ahead; roll and crumb up to 1 day ahead, or freeze crumbed up to 1 month; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 20g butter
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 250g arborio rice
  • 750ml vegetable stock, hot
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 80g frozen peas
  • 100g mozzarella, in 12 cubes
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 250ml tomato passata
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 4 basil leaves
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Melt the butter and soften the onion. Stir in the rice, then add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring, until the rice is tender.
  2. Stir in the parmesan, peas and salt and pepper. Spread on a tray and chill until firm.
  3. Roll into 12 balls, pushing a cube of mozzarella into the middle of each and sealing it in.
  4. Roll each in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
  5. Heat the oil to 175C. Meanwhile simmer the passata with the garlic and basil.
  6. Fry the arancini in batches for 4 minutes until deep golden. Drain.
  7. Serve hot with the warm tomato sauce.

Little Marie's hot honey wings

Bar food at the Lucky Seven, where Rocky finds Little Marie again

Prep 10 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Mix the hot honey butter up to 1 week ahead; season the wings up to 1 day ahead, uncovered in the fridge so the skin dries; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wing pieces
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp hot chilli sauce
  • 1 lemon
  1. Heat the oven to 220C. Pat the wings very dry and toss with the baking powder, salt and garlic powder.
  2. Spread on a rack over a tray and bake, turning once, until crisp and golden.
  3. Melt the butter with the honey, chilli flakes and hot sauce and add a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Toss the hot wings in the honey butter and serve.

Adrian's braciole with soft polenta

Thin topside rolled with prosciutto, pecorino and pine nuts, simmered in passata, soft cornmeal

Prep 25 min, cook 112 min · Make ahead: Braise the braciole up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled in the gravy, it slices better cold; make the polenta on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef topside, in 4 thin slices
  • 60g pecorino, grated
  • 40g dried breadcrumbs
  • 4 garlic cloves, 2 crushed for the filling, 2 sliced for the gravy
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 30g pine nuts, toasted
  • 4 slices prosciutto
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1.4l tomato passata
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 200g instant polenta
  • 1l water
  • 40g butter
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Mix the pecorino, breadcrumbs, crushed garlic, parsley, pine nuts and pepper.
  2. Pound the beef slices thin between baking paper. Lay a slice of prosciutto on each, spread with the filling, roll up tightly and tie with kitchen string.
  3. Brown the rolls all over in the oil in a heavy pot. Lift out.
  4. Soften the onion and sliced garlic in the same pot, add the passata and half the basil, return the rolls and simmer, covered, on low, turning now and then.
  5. Bring the water to the boil with salt, whisk in the polenta and cook, stirring, until thick, then beat in the butter and parmesan.
  6. Cut the string off the braciole, slice thickly and serve over the polenta with plenty of gravy and the rest of the basil.

Adrian's chicken cacciatore

Adrian's own recipe, the way she cooked it on Tusculum Street

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead and reheat; it is better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 200g button mushrooms
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 80g black olives
  • 1 ciabatta loaf
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Season the chicken and brown skin side down in the oil. Lift out.
  2. Fry the sliced onion, capsicum, mushrooms and garlic for 6 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes and rosemary, put the chicken back skin side up and simmer, half covered, for 35 minutes.
  4. Stir in the olives, scatter with parsley and serve with torn bread.

Marie's ricotta cheesecakeVegetarian

Ricotta baked with lemon and orange zest and vanilla, cooled slowly

Prep 15 min, cook 362 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled, it needs at least 4 hours in the fridge; dust with icing sugar just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 10g butter, softened, for the tin
  • 900g fresh ricotta
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 160C. Butter a 23cm springform tin and dust it with a little of the flour.
  2. Beat the ricotta, caster sugar, both zests and the vanilla until smooth.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the rest of the flour.
  4. Pour into the tin and bake until set at the edges with a slight wobble in the middle.
  5. Turn the oven off and leave the cake inside with the door ajar to cool slowly.
  6. Chill until cold and set.
  7. Dust with the icing sugar and cut into wedges.

Lucky Seven Tiger Bar whisky sourVegetarian

Rye shaken with lemon, sugar syrup and egg white, over ice with bitters

Prep 6 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 180ml rye whiskey
  • 3 lemons, juiced to make 90ml
  • 1 egg white
  • 4 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Stir the sugar into the hot water until dissolved.
  2. Cool the syrup.
  3. Shake the whiskey, lemon juice, syrup and egg white hard without ice.
  4. Add half the ice and shake hard again.
  5. Strain into 4 glasses over the rest of the ice, dot the foam with the bitters and add a cherry.
