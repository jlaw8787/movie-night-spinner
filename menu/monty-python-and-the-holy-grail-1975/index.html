Snacks

Patsy's coconut prawns

Patsy bangs two coconut halves together.

Prep 6 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g raw king prawns, peeled with tails on
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g desiccated coconut
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lime
  1. Dust the prawns in seasoned flour, dip in beaten egg, then press into the coconut mixed with panko.
  2. Fry in hot oil in batches until golden, about 2 minutes.
  3. Stir lime zest and juice into the mayo.
  4. Serve the prawns hot with the lime mayo.

Unladen swallow sticky wings

Sticky honey soy wings. The bridgekeeper asks about the unladen swallow.

Prep 8 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Marinate the wings up to a day ahead; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings, tips removed
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Toss the wings with the honey, soy, garlic and ginger.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and bake at 200°C, turning once, until sticky and dark.
  3. Scatter with sesame seeds and spring onions to serve.

Mains

Rabbit of Caerbannog pie

Rabbit pie under golden pastry. The rabbit that sees off three knights.

Prep 15 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Braise the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg rabbit pieces
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g smoky bacon, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 250ml cloudy apple juice
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 3 thyme sprigs
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Toss the rabbit in seasoned flour and brown in the oil, then set aside.
  2. Fry the bacon, carrot and onion until soft.
  3. Return the rabbit with the apple juice, stock, mustard and thyme and simmer covered until tender.
  4. Pull the meat from the bones and tip the filling into a pie dish.
  5. Top with pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.

Feast-upon-the-lambs roast shoulder

Garlic and rosemary roast lamb shoulder. And the people did feast upon the lambs.

Prep 10 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Stud and rub the lamb the night before; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg bone-in lamb shoulder
  • 6 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 2 brown onions, in wedges
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp mint jelly
  1. Stab the lamb all over and push in the garlic and rosemary, then rub with oil and salt.
  2. Sit the lamb on the potatoes and onions, pour in the stock, cover and roast at 160°C.
  3. Uncover and roast at 200°C until the skin is crisp and the potatoes are golden.
  4. Rest, then pull the meat from the bone and serve with mint jelly.

Smelt-of-elderberries apple crumbleVegetarian

Apple and elderberry crumble. Your father smelt of elderberries.

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the crumble topping up to 3 days ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 5 Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced
  • 150g frozen blackberries
  • 3 tbsp elderflower cordial
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 150g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 100g cold butter, cubed
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml vanilla custard
  1. Toss the apples and berries with the cordial and brown sugar in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, oats and caster sugar until it clumps.
  3. Scatter over the fruit and bake at 180°C until golden and bubbling.
  4. Serve warm with the custard.

Drinks

A shrubbery coolerNo alcohol

Mint and lime cooler, green as a shrubbery.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the mint syrup up to 3 days ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 2 limes
  • 750ml soda water
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Heat the sugar and water until dissolved, add half the mint and cool.
  2. Fill 4 glasses with ice, cucumber, the rest of the mint and a squeeze of lime.
  3. Add the strained syrup and top with soda.
