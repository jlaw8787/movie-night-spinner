Broccoli pizza squaresVegetarian

Tray pizza with garlic oil, mozzarella and broccolini.

Prep 16 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Chop the broccolini and make the garlic oil up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, for the tray
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 bunch broccolini, cut small
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Press the dough into an oiled tray and brush with the garlic oil.
  2. Top with mozzarella and broccolini.
  3. Bake at 240°C for 15 minutes until crisp at the edges.
  4. Finish with parmesan and chilli flakes.

Core memory cheese ballsVegetarian

Cheddar cream cheese balls rolled in paprika, parsley, sesame and pistachio.

Prep 30 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 40g pistachios, crushed
  • 2 tbsp black sesame seeds
  1. Beat the cream cheese with the cheddar.
  2. Roll into small balls.
  3. Coat in five colours: paprika, parsley, sesame, pistachio and black sesame.
  4. Chill until firm.

Family dinner lemon and rosemary chicken

Lemon and garlic rubbed chicken thighs roasted over small potatoes.

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the lemon, garlic and rosemary up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1kg small potatoes, halved
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g green beans
  • 30g butter
  • salt, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the potatoes in oil and salt on a large tray.
  2. Rub the chicken with lemon zest, garlic, rosemary, oil and salt and sit it skin side up on the potatoes.
  3. Roast for 35 to 40 minutes until the skin is crisp and the juices run clear. Squeeze over the lemon juice.
  4. Boil the beans for 3 minutes and toss with butter.

Minnesota potato gem hotdish

Mince, beans and corn in mushroom sauce under potato rounds, before the move to San Francisco.

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 200g button mushrooms
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 200g green beans
  • 1 tin corn kernels
  • 700g frozen potato gems
  • 100g cheddar
  1. Beef mince, green beans and corn baked in a creamy mushroom sauce under rows of crisp golden potato gems, with a little cheddar melted over. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brown 600g beef mince with 1 diced onion. Make a sauce: soften 200g sliced mushrooms in 30g butter, stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 300ml milk and 200ml beef stock and simmer until thick. Stir the beef, 200g chopped green beans and 1 cup corn kernels into the sauce and pour into a baking dish. Cover with 700g frozen potato gems in rows and bake for 35 minutes, adding 100g grated cheddar for the last 5.

Five feelings cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with buttercream in five colours.

Prep 35 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight; make the icing and pipe on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 box food colouring in yellow, blue, red, purple and green
  1. Beat part of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a muffin tin at 175°C for 18 minutes. Cool.
  3. Beat the rest of the butter with the icing sugar, split into five bowls and tint each a feeling colour with the food colouring.
  4. Pipe onto the cupcakes.

Pink fairy floss meringues

Crisp strawberry kisses with chewy middles, spun sugar on top, take her to the moon.

How it is made
  • 3 eggs
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 1 packet freeze-dried strawberries
  • 1 bottle pink food colouring
  • 1 tub pink fairy floss
  1. Crisp pink strawberry meringue kisses with soft chewy middles, topped with a puff of fairy floss just before serving. Heat the oven to 110°C. Whisk 3 egg whites to soft peaks, then add 170g caster sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy. Fold in 1 tbsp freeze-dried strawberry powder and a drop of pink food colouring. Pipe 24 kisses onto lined trays and bake for 1 hour, then turn the oven off and leave them inside to cool for 1 hour. Top each with a little pink fairy floss right before serving.

Bing Bong's fairy floss milkshakeNo alcohol

Strawberry vanilla ice cream shake topped with pink fairy floss.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; hull the strawberries and chill the milk the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml cold milk
  • 250g strawberries
  • 1 tub fairy floss
  1. Blend the ice cream with the milk and strawberries until thick.
  2. Pour into glasses and top each with a tuft of pink fairy floss just before serving so it starts to melt into the shake.
