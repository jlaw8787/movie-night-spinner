Snacks

Clone 8 devilled eggsVegetarian

Eight halves, one for every number on the tanks

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Boil and peel the eggs and mix the filling up to 1 day ahead, kept separate in the fridge; fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp chives, finely chopped
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Lower the eggs into a pot of boiling water and cook for 10 minutes.
  2. Lift into iced water and cool completely.
  3. Peel the eggs and cut in half lengthways. Tip the yolks into a bowl.
  4. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, curry powder and salt and pepper until smooth.
  5. Pipe or spoon the filling into the 8 whites, dust with the smoked paprika and scatter with the chives.

No-look basketball gougèresVegetarian

Warm cheese puffs, after Ripley's no-look shot

Prep 20 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Pipe the raw puffs onto a tray and freeze up to 1 month ahead; bake from frozen, adding 5 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g gruyère, finely grated
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • salt, a pinch
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a tray with baking paper. Bring the water, butter and salt to the boil in a saucepan.
  2. Tip in the flour all at once and beat with a wooden spoon over the heat until it forms a smooth ball that leaves the sides, about 2 minutes.
  3. Take off the heat and cool.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy, then stir in most of the gruyère and the nutmeg.
  5. Pipe or spoon 16 small balls onto the tray and top with the rest of the gruyère.
  6. Bake until puffed, deep golden and crisp. Do not open the oven for the first 15 minutes.
  7. Cool for a few minutes and serve warm.

Mains

Flooded galley bouillabaisse

Fish stew, from the kitchen the crew swims through

Prep 25 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Make the broth with the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat and poach the seafood on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 fennel bulb, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced, for the broth
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • saffron threads, a good pinch
  • 1 orange, zest peeled in a strip
  • 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1l fish stock
  • 400g waxy potatoes, sliced 1cm thick
  • 500g firm white fish fillets, in chunks
  • 300g green prawns, peeled
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed, for the rouille
  • cayenne pepper, a pinch
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the onion, fennel, broth garlic and potatoes. Cut the fish into chunks and peel the prawns.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide heavy pot and soften the onion, fennel, garlic and fennel seeds without browning.
  3. Add the saffron, orange zest, tomatoes, stock and potatoes. Simmer until the potatoes are just tender.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with the rouille garlic and cayenne.
  5. Slice the baguette and toast until golden.
  6. Add the fish and simmer gently for 4 minutes, then add the prawns for 3 minutes more. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Ladle into wide bowls, scatter with parsley and serve with the toasts and rouille.

Acid blood lime tartVegetarian

Sweet shortcrust with a zesty citrus custard, baked to a wobble, sharp enough to burn through

Prep 15 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Bake and chill the tart up to 1 day ahead, the chill takes 2 hours; zest the top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet frozen sweet shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 7 limes, 3 zested, all juiced to make 150ml
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Line a 23cm loose-based tart tin with the pastry, prick the base and chill.
  2. Heat the oven to 180C. Line the pastry with baking paper and rice, bake for 15 minutes, lift out the paper and bake 5 minutes more. Turn the oven down to 150C.
  3. Whisk the eggs and sugar, then whisk in the zest of 3 limes, the lime juice and cream. Skim off any froth.
  4. Pour into the warm shell and bake until just set with a slight wobble in the middle.
  5. Cool, then chill until firm.
  6. Grate over a little more lime zest and cut into wedges.

Drinks

Betty smugglers' spiced rum and gingerVegetarian

Dark rum over ice with cinnamon clove syrup and lime, topped with spicy fizz

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the spiced syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep it in the fridge; build the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 whole cloves
  • 240ml dark rum
  • 3 limes, 2 juiced, 1 in wedges
  • 600ml ginger beer, chilled
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Simmer the brown sugar, water, cinnamon and cloves for 3 minutes.
  2. Cool the syrup and strain.
  3. Fill 4 glasses with ice. Add the rum, the spiced syrup and the lime juice, sharing it between the glasses.
  4. Top with the ginger beer, stir once and add a lime wedge.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the Betty docks with the Auriga and the smugglers come aboard with their cargo.
