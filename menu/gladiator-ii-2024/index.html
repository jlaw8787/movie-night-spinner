Snacks

Numidian honey-fried stuffed datesVegetarian

Medjool dates with almond and pepper, fried in butter and honey

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Slit and stuff the dates up to 2 days ahead, airtight in the fridge; fry in the butter and honey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 24 Medjool dates
  • 24 whole almonds
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Toast the whole almonds in a dry pan for 4 minutes.
  2. Slit and pit the Medjool dates, push an almond and a grind of black pepper into each and press closed.
  3. Melt the butter in a frying pan over medium heat and fry the dates 2 minutes, turning, until warm and glossy.
  4. Add the honey and let it bubble for 1 minute, turning the dates to coat.
  5. Tip onto a plate and sprinkle with flaky salt.

Macrinus's baked feta with figs and honeyVegetarian

Baked feta with olives, figs, oregano and chilli, crusty bread

Prep 12 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Assemble the dish up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, without the honey; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g feta block
  • 150g mixed olives
  • 4 fresh figs
  • olive oil, to taste
  • dried oregano, to taste
  • chilli flakes, to taste
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Heat the oven to 200C.
  2. Put the block of feta in a small baking dish with the mixed olives around it.
  3. Halve the fresh figs and nestle them in.
  4. Drizzle with the olive oil and sprinkle with dried oregano, chilli flakes and thyme sprigs.
  5. Bake 15 minutes until the feta is soft.
  6. Drizzle with the honey and serve with torn crusty bread.

Mains

Emperors' honey roast chicken

One bird for two emperors, Geta and Caracalla. Honey roast chicken on carrots

Prep 15 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the oil, salt, garlic and thyme up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 lemon
  • 6 carrots
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Rub the whole chicken with oil, salt, crushed garlic and thyme and stuff with a halved lemon.
  2. Roast at 200C on a bed of the carrots for 60 minutes, then brush with the honey and roast 15 minutes more until sticky.
  3. Rest 10 minutes and carve.

Sweets

Roman honey cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked ricotta and honey cheesecake with lemon zest

Prep 15 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead, then cool and keep covered in the fridge; drizzle with honey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ricotta
  • 3 eggs
  • 6 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  1. Beat the ricotta with the eggs, the honey and the zest of the lemon until smooth.
  2. Pour into a lined 20cm tin and bake at 170C for 40 minutes until just set.
  3. Cool and drizzle with more honey.

Drinks

Emperors' spiced honey wineVegetarian

Poured for the sea battle in the flooded Colosseum. Warm red, honey, cinnamon, bay

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Warm and strain up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently and do not boil
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • black peppercorns, to taste
  • bay leaves, to taste
  • 1 orange
  1. Pour the bottle of red wine into a saucepan with the honey, the cinnamon stick, the black peppercorns, the bay leaves and the strips of orange peel.
  2. Warm gently over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring until the honey dissolves.
  3. Do not let it boil.
  4. Strain into heavy cups.
  5. For non-drinkers, make it the same way with red grape juice and less honey.
