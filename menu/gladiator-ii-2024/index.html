Snacks

Numidian honey-fried stuffed datesVegetarian

Medjool dates with almond and pepper, fried in butter and honey.

Prep 11 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Slit and stuff the dates up to 2 days ahead, airtight in the fridge; fry in the butter and honey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 24 Medjool dates
  • 24 whole almonds
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Toast the whole almonds in a dry pan for 4 minutes.
  2. Slit and pit the Medjool dates, push an almond and a grind of black pepper into each and press closed.
  3. Melt the butter in a frying pan over medium heat and fry the dates 2 minutes, turning, until warm and glossy.
  4. Add the honey and let it bubble for 1 minute, turning the dates to coat.
  5. Tip onto a plate and sprinkle with flaky salt.

Macrinus's baked feta with figs and honeyVegetarian

Baked feta with olives, figs, oregano and black pepper, crusty bread.

Prep 6 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Assemble the dish up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, without the honey; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g feta block
  • 150g mixed olives
  • 4 fresh figs
  • olive oil, to taste
  • dried oregano, to taste
  • chilli flakes, to taste
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Put the block of feta in a small baking dish with the mixed olives around it.
  3. Halve the fresh figs and nestle them in.
  4. Drizzle with the olive oil and sprinkle with dried oregano, chilli flakes and thyme sprigs.
  5. Bake 15 minutes until the feta is soft.
  6. Drizzle with the honey and serve with torn crusty bread.

Bread and circuses rosemary flatbreadOn the printed menu

Thin yoghurt dough blistered in a pan, garlic herb oil and labneh, first slice to Dondus.

How it is made
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • sea salt flakes
  • 250g labneh
  1. Thin flatbreads blistered in a hot pan, brushed with garlic and rosemary oil, sprinkled with sea salt and served with labneh for dipping. Mix 300g self-raising flour, 250g Greek yoghurt and half a tsp salt into a dough and rest 10 minutes. Warm 4 tbsp olive oil with 2 sliced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp chopped rosemary for 3 minutes. Divide the dough into 8, roll thin and cook in a hot dry pan 1 to 2 minutes a side until charred in spots. Brush with the rosemary oil and sprinkle with flaky salt. Serve with 250g labneh drizzled with oil.

Emperors' honey roast chicken

Honey roast chicken on carrots. One bird for two emperors, Geta and Caracalla.

Prep 11 min, cook 79 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the oil, salt, garlic and thyme up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 lemon
  • 6 carrots
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Rub the whole chicken with oil, salt, crushed garlic and thyme and stuff with a halved lemon.
  2. Roast at 200°C on a bed of the carrots for 60 minutes, then brush with the honey and roast 15 minutes more until sticky.
  3. Rest 10 minutes and carve.

Numidian lamb, apricot and chickpea stewOn the printed menu

Shoulder braised with cumin, cinnamon, dates and dried fruit, couscous, minted yoghurt, for Hanno.

How it is made
  • 1kg diced lamb shoulder
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • ground cumin
  • ground cinnamon
  • paprika
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 800ml chicken stock
  • 400g tin chickpeas
  • 120g dried apricots
  • 300g couscous
  • 1 tbsp harissa
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Lamb shoulder braised with chickpeas, tomato, cumin, cinnamon and dried apricots, spooned over fluffy couscous with harissa yoghurt and mint. Brown 1kg diced lamb shoulder in batches in oil. Fry 2 onions for 5 minutes, add 3 garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp cinnamon and 1 tsp paprika. Add 400g tinned tomatoes, 500ml stock and the lamb, cover and simmer 1.5 hours. Add 400g drained chickpeas and 120g dried apricots and cook 30 minutes. Pour 300ml boiling stock over 300g couscous, cover 5 minutes. Swirl 1 tbsp harissa through 200g yoghurt. Serve with mint.

Roman honey cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked ricotta and honey cheesecake with lemon zest.

Prep 9 min, cook 108 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead, then cool and keep covered in the fridge; drizzle with honey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ricotta
  • 3 eggs
  • 6 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  1. Beat the ricotta with the eggs, the honey and the zest of the lemon until smooth.
  2. Pour into a lined 20cm tin and bake at 170°C for 40 minutes until just set.
  3. Cool and drizzle with more honey.

Emperors' spiced honey wineVegetarian

Warm red, honey, cinnamon, bay. Poured for the sea battle in the flooded Colosseum.

Prep 2 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Warm and strain up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently and do not boil
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • black peppercorns, to taste
  • bay leaves, to taste
  • 1 orange
  1. Pour the bottle of red wine into a saucepan with the honey, the cinnamon stick, the black peppercorns, the bay leaves and the strips of orange peel.
  2. Warm gently over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring until the honey dissolves.
  3. Do not let it boil.
  4. Strain into heavy cups.
  5. For non-drinkers, make it the same way with red grape juice and less honey.
