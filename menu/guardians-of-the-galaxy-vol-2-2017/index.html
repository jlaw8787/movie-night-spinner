Baby Groot's twig friesVegetarian

Thin sweet potato sticks baked crisp with smoked paprika and salt

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cut the sweet potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep them in cold water in the fridge, dried well before baking; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt flakes
  1. Cut the sweet potatoes into thin sticks and toss with oil, paprika and salt.
  2. Spread in a single layer on lined trays and bake at 220°C, turning once, until crisp at the edges.
  3. Pile into little pots to serve.

Sovereign golden saffron arancini

Crumbed saffron risotto balls with mozzarella centres, fried golden

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the risotto up to 1 day ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1l chicken stock, hot
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 150g mozzarella, cubed
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil for frying
  1. Cook the rice with the hot stock and saffron into a risotto, stir in the parmesan, then spread out to cool.
  2. Chill overnight or at least a few hours until firm.
  3. Roll into balls around cubes of mozzarella, coat in egg and then panko.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until golden all over.

Contraxia mini beef and gravy piesOn the printed menu

Shortcrust cases of peppery mince and onion sauce under puff, for the Iron Lotus

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Little shortcrust pies packed with peppery minced beef in thick onion gravy, under golden puff lids, with tomato sauce for dunking. Brown 500g beef mince with 1 diced onion, then stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, 250ml beef stock, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce and plenty of pepper. Simmer 20 minutes until thick and cool. Cut 12 rounds from 2 shortcrust sheets to line a greased muffin tin, fill with the beef and top with rounds cut from 2 puff sheets. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes. Serve with tomato sauce.

Yondu's Ravager stew

Beef chuck, carrots and onions simmered 3 hours in stout, with crusty bread

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 375ml stout
  • 1 loaf of crusty bread
  1. Brown the beef in batches in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the onions, carrots and paprika and stir for a few minutes.
  3. Pour in the stout and enough water to cover.
  4. Simmer gently until thick and tender.
  5. Serve in big bowls with crusty bread.

Ego's garden lemon chicken with farroOn the printed menu

Thighs roasted with garlic and herbs over grains, pumpkin and feta, before catch with Dad

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 500g pumpkin
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 250g farro
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 100g feta
  • 1 pomegranate
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Chicken thighs roasted with lemon, garlic and herbs over a big herby farro salad with roast pumpkin, capsicum, feta and pomegranate. Toss 1kg chicken thighs with the zest and juice of 1 lemon, 3 crushed garlic cloves, oregano, olive oil and salt. Roast at 200°C for 35 minutes alongside 500g pumpkin chunks and 1 sliced red capsicum. Simmer 250g farro in salted water for 25 minutes and drain. Toss the farro with the roast veg, parsley, mint, 100g feta, pomegranate seeds and lemon juice and olive oil. Serve the chicken on top.

Ravager fireworks popping candy sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, warm chocolate sauce and popping candy that crackles

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 2 packets popping candy
  1. Heat the cream until steaming and pour over the chocolate, stirring until smooth.
  2. Scoop ice cream into bowls and pour over the warm sauce.
  3. Scatter popping candy on top at the table so it crackles.

Mantis's pear and passionfruit coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Pear nectar, passionfruit, lime and mint over crushed ice

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add the sparkling water at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml pear nectar
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1l sparkling water
  • 1 pear, thinly sliced
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Mix the pear nectar with the passionfruit pulp and lime juice, and bruise the mint through it.
  2. Chill for half an hour.
  3. Fill glasses with crushed ice, pour in the nectar until three quarters full and top with sparkling water.
  4. Garnish with mint sprigs and a pear slice.
