Snacks

Rope bridge banana bitesVegetarian

Frozen banana slices dipped in dark chocolate and crushed peanuts

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 3 bananas
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100g peanuts, crushed
  1. Slice the bananas into thick rounds. Melt the chocolate.
  2. Dip each slice halfway in the chocolate and roll the chocolate side in crushed peanuts. Lay on a lined tray.
  3. Freeze for 30 minutes until set. Serve straight from the freezer.

Jungle fruit cupsVegetarian

Pineapple, mango, papaya and lychee with lime, mint and chilli salt

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1 mango
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 400g lychees, drained
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 tbsp torn mint
  • chilli salt, to taste
  1. Cube the pineapple, mango and papaya and mix with the lychees.
  2. Spoon into cups, squeeze over the lime juice and scatter with mint.
  3. Finish each cup with a pinch of salt mixed with chilli for a little kick.

Mains

Mountain fortress goulash

Beef goulash with sweet paprika and red pepper, over buttered egg noodles

Prep 20 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches. Add the onions and paprika and stir for a minute.
  2. Add the tomatoes, capsicum and enough water to cover.
  3. Simmer for 3 hours until thick and tender.
  4. Cook the noodles, toss with butter and serve the goulash over the top.

Sweets

Falcon jewel jelly

Lime jelly, kiwi and grapes. Jelly, never cake, for Mouse's sake.

Prep 20 min, cook 185 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, top with cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets lime jelly crystals
  • 3 kiwifruit, diced
  • 300g green grapes, plus 4 extra whole
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Make up the jelly following the packet. Halve most of the grapes.
  2. Pour the jelly into clear glasses with the kiwifruit and halved grapes.
  3. Chill for 3 hours until set.
  4. Top with whipped cream and finish each glass with one perfect grape like a jewel.

Drinks

Oberon's sand and sun coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Blended watermelon, lime and mint. Oberon's weakness is heat.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg watermelon flesh
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 handful mint, plus sprigs to serve
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend the watermelon, lime juice and mint until smooth.
  2. Strain into a jug and chill well.
  3. Pour over ice in tall glasses with a fresh mint sprig on top.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the friends get sucked back into the game.
