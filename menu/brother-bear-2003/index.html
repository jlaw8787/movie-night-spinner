Snacks

Salmon run bites

Rye crispbread with cream cheese, smoked salmon, dill and lemon

Prep 13 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet rye crispbread
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Pick the dill into sprigs.
  2. Spread the crispbreads with cream cheese.
  3. Top with folds of salmon and a sprig of dill.
  4. Squeeze over a little lemon and lay on a wooden board.

Rutt and Tuke's maple nutsVegetarian

Maple roasted pecans and walnuts, bigger than Bucky's pine nut

Prep 6 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pecans
  • 150g walnuts
  • 4 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Toss the pecans and walnuts with the maple syrup and salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170C.
  3. Cool until set.
  4. Break into clusters.

Tanana's wild greens and roast beetroot saladVegetarian

Tanana sends Kenai off to the mountain of lights

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Roast the beetroot up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beetroot, scrubbed
  • 120g rocket and baby spinach
  • 100g goat's cheese
  • 40g pepitas
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Wrap the beetroot in foil and roast until tender.
  2. Toast the pepitas in a dry pan.
  3. Peel and cut the beetroot into wedges. Whisk the maple syrup, vinegar and oil.
  4. Toss the leaves and beetroot with the dressing and top with goat's cheese and pepitas.

Mains

Kenai's venison with blueberry sauce

Venison steaks with a blueberry sauce and rosemary roast potatoes

Prep 18 min, cook 68 min · Make ahead: Cut and parboil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; roast them, sear the venison and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 venison steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 150g blueberries
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 sprig thyme
  • 20g butter
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chunks. Bring the steaks to room temperature, then oil and season.
  2. Parboil the potatoes.
  3. Toss with oil, rosemary and salt and roast at 220C.
  4. Sear the venison in a very hot pan for rare, then rest.
  5. Add the stock, balsamic, blueberries, sugar and thyme to the pan and simmer until syrupy, then whisk in the butter.
  6. Slice the venison and spoon over the sauce.

Sweets

Honey berry crumbleVegetarian

Mixed berries with honey under an oat crumble

Prep 13 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled (bake fresh)
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed berries
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 100g butter
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 100g plain flour
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Toss the berries with honey and spread in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the oats, flour and sugar.
  3. Scatter over the fruit.
  4. Bake at 180C until bubbling.
  5. Serve with cream.

Spirit lights blueberry lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Blueberry syrup mixed with lemon juice and water, over ice

Prep 10 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the blueberry syrup up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; juice the lemons, mix and add the soda water just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g blueberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml water
  • 4 lemons
  • 400ml cold water
  • 1 handful mint
  • 330ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer most of the blueberries with the sugar and the water, then strain and cool.
  2. Cool.
  3. Juice the lemons and mix with two spoons of syrup and the cold water.
  4. Fill four glasses with ice, mint and the remaining berries.
  5. Pour in the lemonade, spoon the rest of the syrup over and finish with soda water.
