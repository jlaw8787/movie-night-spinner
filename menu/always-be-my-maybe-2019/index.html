Beef and veg bibimbap cups

Rice cups with soy beef, spinach, carrot, fried egg and gochujang.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Components up to 1 day, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 2 carrots
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  1. Cook the rice. Julienne the carrots, 15 min.
  2. Cook the beef with soy and garlic, 8 min.
  3. Saute the spinach and carrot separately.
  4. Fry the eggs, 5 min.
  5. Spoon rice into small bowls, arrange the beef and vegetables on top with an egg and a dot of gochujang. Mix it all up before eating.

Band night wings

Twice-fried wings tossed in a sticky gochujang and honey glaze.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, reheat in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Fry the wings in the vegetable oil at 160°C, then rest, 20 min.
  2. Fry again at 190°C until crackly, 10 min.
  3. Warm the gochujang with honey and crushed garlic until glossy, 5 min.
  4. Toss the wings through and scatter with sesame seeds.

Tiny tasting menu salmon cucumber cups

Hollowed cucumber rounds, yuzu crème fraîche, cured salmon, roe and a chive, for the Keanu dinner.

How it is made
  • 2 continental cucumbers
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • yuzu or lemon juice
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 40g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Thick cucumber rounds hollowed into cups, filled with yuzu crème fraîche and cured salmon, finished with salmon roe and a single chive. Cut 2 continental cucumbers into 2cm thick rounds and scoop a hollow from each with a melon baller, leaving a base. Mix 100g crème fraîche with 1 tsp yuzu or lemon juice and a pinch of salt. Dice 150g smoked salmon finely. Spoon or pipe the cream into each cup, top with salmon, a few beads of salmon roe (40g total) and a short piece of chive. Serve on a long plate, deliberately small.

Kimchi jjigae

Spicy stew of pork belly and kimchi with gochugaru, simmered until rich.

Prep 8 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork belly, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g kimchi, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • 750ml water
  • 300g firm tofu
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Slice the pork and chop the kimchi.
  2. Fry the pork in the vegetable oil until browned, then add the kimchi, garlic and gochugaru, 7 min.
  3. Pour in the water and simmer, 15 min.
  4. Add sliced tofu and spring onion and simmer for the last few minutes. Serve with rice, 10 min.

Sasha's miso-glazed salmon rice bowls

Salmon roasted under a sticky miso honey glaze, pickled cucumber, edamame and chilli mayo.

How it is made
  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • soy sauce
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 200g frozen shelled edamame
  • 2 nori sheets
  • Japanese mayonnaise
  • chilli sauce
  1. Salmon roasted under a sticky white miso and honey glaze, over sushi rice with quick pickled cucumber, edamame, nori strips and chilli mayo. Rinse and cook 2 cups sushi rice, then season with 3 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar and salt. Mix 3 tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp mirin and 1 tsp soy. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brush over 4 salmon fillets and roast 12 minutes. Slice 1 cucumber thin and toss with 2 tbsp rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar. Boil 200g frozen edamame 3 minutes. Build bowls with rice, salmon, cucumber, edamame, sliced nori and mayo mixed with chilli sauce.

Sweet red bean pancakesVegetarian

Thin pancakes filled with sweet red bean paste and folded.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 100g butter
  • 200g red bean paste
  1. Whisk the flour, egg and milk into a smooth batter.
  2. Cook small thin pancakes in butter, 15 min.
  3. Spoon a little red bean paste into each, fold over and press.
  4. Fry again in butter until golden on both sides and serve warm, 10 min.

Roasted barley teaNo alcohol

Roasted barley steeped in hot water, served hot or over ice.

Prep 2 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Hot or iced, keep up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp roasted barley tea
  • 1 L water
  • 2 cups ice, if serving cold
  1. Steep the barley tea in just boiled water, 10 min.
  2. Strain and serve hot in small cups, or cool and pour over ice in tall glasses.
