Snacks

Chips and dip, party bowlVegetarian

Caramelised onion in sour cream and cream cheese, ridged crisps, while Sonny yells Attica.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  • 300g ridged potato chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Cook the onions in the oil over low heat until deep brown, then cool.
  3. Stir into the sour cream and cream cheese with the chives and salt. Serve with the chips.

Party hero, three foot

Long rolls of ham, salami, provolone, lettuce and tomato, cut for passing round to the hostages.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 long hero rolls
  • 150g sliced ham
  • 100g sliced salami
  • 100g sliced provolone
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Split the rolls lengthwise.
  2. Layer the ham, salami, provolone, lettuce and tomato.
  3. Dress with the oil, vinegar, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Press down firmly, then cut each hero into 6 wedges.

Mains

Pepperoni pie

Delivered to the door, the delivery kid becomes a star.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough can be made a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g grated low-moisture mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Rest the dough at room temperature so it relaxes. Season the tomatoes with salt and oregano.
  2. Stretch the bases thin, spread with sauce and top with the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Bake on a preheated tray at the oven's highest setting until bubbling and golden.

Sweets

Lemon iceVegetarian

Lemon juice, zest and sugar frozen and scraped to icy flakes, for a sweltering bank job.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 500ml water
  • 6 lemons
  1. Zest 2 lemons and simmer the zest with the sugar and water, then cool.
  2. Juice all the lemons and stir the juice into the syrup.
  3. Freeze in a shallow tray, scraping with a fork every 30 minutes until fluffy.
  4. Serve in paper cups.

Drinks

Cold lager with lime

Ice-cold beer over fresh citrus, spicy salted rim, for Sal, who has never flown.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Rim the glasses up to 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles lager
  • 3 limes
  • 1 tbsp flaky sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 8 ice cubes
  1. Mix the salt, chilli powder and lime zest on a plate. Run a lime wedge around the rims of 4 chilled glasses and dip in the chilli salt.
  2. Squeeze lime juice into each glass, add ice, then pour in the cold lager slowly.

When to serve
