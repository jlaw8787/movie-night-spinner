Snacks

Chips and dip, party bowlVegetarian

Ridged chips, slow-browned onions in sour cream, cream cheese and chives.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  • 300g ridged potato chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Cook the onions in the oil over low heat until deep brown, then cool, 40 min.
  3. Stir into the sour cream and cream cheese with the chives and salt. Serve with the chips.

Party hero, three foot

Ham, salami, provolone, iceberg, tomato, oil, vinegar and oregano. Pressed, cut in wedges.

Prep 20 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 long hero rolls
  • 150g sliced ham
  • 100g sliced salami
  • 100g sliced provolone
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Split the rolls lengthwise.
  2. Layer the ham, salami, provolone, lettuce and tomato.
  3. Dress with the oil, vinegar, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Press down firmly, then cut each hero into 6 wedges.

Mains

Pepperoni pie

Thin crust, crushed tomato, oregano, mozzarella and spicy cured sausage.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min, 1 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g grated low-moisture mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Rest the dough at room temperature so it relaxes. Season the tomatoes with salt and oregano, 1 hr.
  2. Stretch the bases thin, spread with sauce and top with the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Bake on a preheated tray at the oven's highest setting until bubbling and golden, 8 min.

Sweets

Lemon iceVegetarian

Fresh juice and zest, frozen and scraped fluffy. Paper cups.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min, 3 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 500ml water
  • 6 lemons
  1. Zest 2 lemons and simmer the zest with the sugar and water, then cool, 5 min.
  2. Juice all the lemons and stir the juice into the syrup.
  3. Freeze in a shallow tray, scraping with a fork every 30 minutes until fluffy.
  4. Serve in paper cups.

Drinks

Cold lager with lime

Over two cubes, a fresh squeeze, glass rimmed with chilli salt and zest.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles of lager
  • 3 limes
  • 1 tbsp flaky sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 8 ice cubes
  1. Mix the salt, chilli powder and lime zest on a plate. Run a lime wedge around the rims of 4 chilled glasses and dip in the chilli salt.
  2. Squeeze lime juice into each glass, add ice, then pour in the cold lager slowly.
