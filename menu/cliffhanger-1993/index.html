Snacks

Rocky Mountain smoked trout pâté

Smoked trout and cream cheese spread on rye crackers with pickled red onion

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g hot-smoked trout
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp horseradish
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 pinch caster sugar
  • 1 packet rye crackers
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the red onion thin and soak in the vinegar with the sugar to pickle.
  2. Flake the trout, removing any skin and bones.
  3. Blitz half the trout with the cream cheese, lemon juice, horseradish and pepper.
  4. Fold in the rest of the trout with chopped dill so it stays chunky.
  5. Spread thick on rye crackers and top with the pickled onion.

Ranger hut roast pumpkin soup

Roasted pumpkin soup finished with cream and toasted pepitas

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead in the fridge, or freeze for up to 1 month; reheat and toast the pepitas on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pumpkin, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 30g butter
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 100ml cream
  • 40g pepitas
  • salt, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Toss the pumpkin with oil and salt.
  2. Roast until caramelised.
  3. Soften the diced onion and garlic in butter.
  4. Add the pumpkin and stock and simmer.
  5. Blitz until smooth and stir in the cream.
  6. Toast the pepitas in a dry pan.
  7. Pour into a warmed thermos and scatter pepitas on each mug.

Mains

Gabe's ranger hut rump steak with foil-baked potatoes

Rosemary rump steaks grilled over coals, potatoes baked in foil

Prep 13 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Wrap the potatoes with the butter in foil up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; rub the steaks up to 4 hours ahead and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 potatoes
  • 40g butter
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Wrap each potato with a knob of butter in foil.
  2. Bake in the coals.
  3. Rub the steaks with salt, pepper and chopped rosemary.
  4. Grill over hot coals on both sides.
  5. Rest on a board.

Hal Tucker's maple mustard chicken with charred corn

Thighs glazed with maple and wholegrain in a cast iron pan, cobs blackened over coals, lime

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix the glaze and marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lime
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Mix the maple syrup, mustard, garlic and oil and coat the chicken.
  2. Grill or pan-fry the chicken over medium heat, turning and brushing with the glaze, until sticky and cooked through.
  3. Char the corn over the coals or in a hot pan, turning, until blackened in spots.
  4. Rub the corn with butter and lime and serve with the chicken.

Treasury cash apple strudelVegetarian

Cinnamon apple and raisin filo strudel, served with cream

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Fry the breadcrumbs up to 2 days ahead in an airtight tub; roll and bake on the day, or bake up to 1 day ahead and warm through
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green apples
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 50g raisins
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 20g butter, for frying the breadcrumbs
  • 8 sheets filo pastry
  • 60g butter, melted, for brushing the filo
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 200ml cream
  1. Heat the oven to 190C. Fry the breadcrumbs in the 20g of butter. Toss the sliced apples with the sugar, cinnamon, raisins and crumbs.
  2. Layer the filo, brushing each sheet with the melted butter.
  3. Pile the apple along one edge and roll up.
  4. Bake.
  5. Cool a little, dust with icing sugar and serve with cream.

Gabe Walker's hot buttered rumVegetarian

Dark rum with spiced brown sugar butter and boiling water

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Beat the spiced butter up to 1 week ahead and keep it chilled; boil the water and build the mugs to order
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 1 pinch ground cloves
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 600ml boiling water
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and cloves.
  2. Put a heaped tablespoon in each mug with rum.
  3. Top with boiling water and stir until melted.
