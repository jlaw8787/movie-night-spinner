Snacks

Esqueleto's corn chipsVegetarian

Fried corn tortilla wedges with lime zest and chilli, fresh tomato salsa

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 lime, zested and juiced
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 4 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  1. Cut the tortillas into wedges and fry in the vegetable oil at 180C until crisp.
  2. Toss in salt, lime zest and chilli powder.
  3. Mix the tomatoes and onion with lime juice and salt.
  4. Serve the chips warm with the salsa.

Elote, get that corn outta my faceVegetarian

Charred corn with lime mayo, feta, chilli powder and coriander

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 80g feta, crumbled
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp coriander, chopped
  • 2 limes, cut in wedges
  1. Grill the corn over high heat until charred all over.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with lime juice and brush on.
  3. Roll in feta.
  4. Sprinkle with chilli powder and coriander.
  5. Serve hot with lime wedges.

Mains

Nacho's chorizo and potato tacos

Crispy chorizo and potato in corn tortillas with avocado and pickled onion

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chorizo, diced
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 1 white onion, sliced, for the filling
  • 1 pinch of chilli flakes
  • 1 white onion, sliced, for pickling
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • 1 handful of coriander
  1. Fry the chorizo, potatoes, onion and chilli until crisp and golden.
  2. Quickly pickle the extra onion in lime juice.
  3. Warm the tortillas.
  4. Spoon the filling into the tortillas with avocado, pickled onion and coriander.

Sweets

Cinnamon churros with chocolate dipVegetarian

Fried dough sticks rolled in cinnamon sugar with a chocolate dipping sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Boil the water, butter and salt, then beat in the flour until it leaves the sides of the pan.
  2. Cool, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Pipe 12cm lengths through a star nozzle into 180C oil and fry until golden.
  4. Roll in the sugar and cinnamon.
  5. Melt the chocolate with the cream and serve warm for dipping.

Drinks

Horchata, cinnamon rice drinkVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled sweet rice and cinnamon milk poured over ice

Prep 10 min, cook 720 min · Make ahead: Soak overnight, keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g long grain rice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1l water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 250ml milk
  • 2 cup ice
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  1. Soak the rice and cinnamon sticks in the water overnight.
  2. Blend until smooth and strain through a fine cloth.
  3. Stir in the sugar and milk until dissolved.
  4. Chill well, pour over ice and dust with cinnamon.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Nacho spends his wrestling pay on the orphans.
