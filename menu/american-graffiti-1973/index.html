Snacks

Knuckle sandwich ham hock sliders

Vic offers Toad a knuckle sandwich

Prep 30 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Simmer and pull the hocks up to 2 days ahead; warm through and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 smoked ham hocks
  • 1 brown onion, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 tbsp smoky barbecue sauce
  • 8 soft slider rolls
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  1. Cover the hocks, onion and bay leaves with water and simmer until the meat falls from the bone.
  2. Pull the meat into shreds, discarding skin and bone, and toss with the barbecue sauce and a splash of the liquid.
  3. Mix the cabbage with the mayo, mustard and vinegar.
  4. Pile the ham into the rolls, top with slaw and serve warm.

Paradise Road chilli cheese fries

Fries under chilli and melted cheese, for the drag race out on Paradise Road

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead; bake the fries and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut fries
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Brown the mince with the onion, then add the spices and tomatoes and simmer until thick.
  2. Bake the fries at 220°C until crisp.
  3. Pile the fries on a tray, spoon over the chilli and scatter with cheese.
  4. Flash under a hot grill until melted and top with spring onion.

Mains

The double Chubby Chuck

Double cheeseburger. Toad's waiting on a double Chubby Chuck.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls up to a day ahead and chill; smash and cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince, 20 percent fat
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 brown onion, very finely sliced
  • 1/2 cup dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls and season well.
  2. Smash each ball flat onto a very hot greased pan with a sturdy spatula and cook until crusty.
  3. Flip, top with cheese and onion and cook another minute.
  4. Mix the mayo, tomato sauce and mustard and spread on the toasted buns.
  5. Stack two patties per bun with lettuce and pickles.

Wolfman's midnight chilli dogs

Chilli dogs with onions. The Wolfman is everywhere tonight.

Prep 5 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced, plus 1 for topping
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 3 tbsp American mustard
  1. Brown the mince with 1 onion, add the chilli powder and tomatoes and simmer until thick.
  2. Grill the franks, turning, until blistered.
  3. Toast the rolls, tuck in the franks and spoon over the chilli.
  4. Finish with cheese, mustard and the raw diced onion.

Sweets

White '56 T-bird vanilla malt sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla malt sundae with hot fudge, for a white '56 T-bird cruising Third Street

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to a week ahead and rewarm; scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream, plus 200ml for whipping
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 100g Maltesers, crushed
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Heat 150ml cream with the golden syrup, pour over the chocolate and stir to a glossy fudge.
  2. Whip the rest of the cream with the malted milk powder to soft peaks.
  3. Scoop the ice cream into tall glasses and pour over the warm fudge.
  4. Top with malted cream, crushed Maltesers and a cherry.

Toad's Old Harper and colaVegetarian

Bourbon and cola over ice. Toad lost his ID in a flood.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the cola well; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml Kentucky bourbon
  • 1l Coca-Cola, chilled
  • 2 limes, cut in wedges
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice and pour 60ml bourbon into each.
  2. Top with cola and squeeze in a lime wedge.
