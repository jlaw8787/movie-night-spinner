Snacks

The President's wrong hors d'oeuvres

Cheese puffs and devils on horseback, the wrong hors d'oeuvres for the reception.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Wrap the dates and pipe the puffs up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 12 medjool dates, pitted
  • 60g blue cheese
  • 6 rashers streaky bacon, halved
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g gruyère, grated
  • 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard
  1. Stuff each date with a little blue cheese and wrap tightly in half a rasher.
  2. Boil the water and butter, tip in the flour and beat until it comes away from the pan.
  3. Cool slightly, beat in the eggs one at a time, then the gruyère and mustard.
  4. Pipe or spoon the dough into small balls on a lined tray.
  5. Bake the puffs and the dates at 200°C until the puffs are risen and golden and the bacon is crisp.

Prime Minister Soto's prawn tempura

Light tempura prawns, tentsuyu dipping sauce. Tug begs Japan not to make such good cars.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the dipping sauce up to 3 days ahead; batter and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 16 green king prawns, peeled, tails on
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 200ml ice-cold soda water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml dashi stock
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1 5cm piece daikon, finely grated
  1. Warm the dashi, soy and mirin together for the dipping sauce, then cool.
  2. Score the underside of each prawn so it stays straight.
  3. Stir the yolk and soda water, then flick in the flours and mix with chopsticks, leaving it lumpy.
  4. Dip the prawns and fry at 180°C in small batches until pale gold and crisp.
  5. Serve at once with the sauce and grated daikon.

Topper's bow-and-arrow roast chicken

Lemon and garlic roast chicken, pan gravy.

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Salt the chicken up to a day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 garlic bulb, halved
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 40g soft butter
  • 1kg small potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 375ml chicken stock
  1. Rub the chicken with butter and salt and stuff it with the lemon, garlic and thyme.
  2. Toss the potatoes in oil and salt around the bird in a roasting tin.
  3. Roast at 200°C until the juices run clear and the potatoes are golden.
  4. Rest the chicken, then stir the flour into the pan juices and whisk in the stock to make gravy.
  5. Carve and serve with the potatoes and gravy.

Topper and Ramada's spaghetti and meatballs

Beef and pork meatballs, slow tomato sauce.

Prep 3 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs in sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; boil the pasta to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef and pork mince
  • 50g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 60ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 50g parmesan, grated, plus extra
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 x 400g tins crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  • 400g spaghetti
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix with the mince, egg, parmesan, a third of the garlic and salt and roll into 20 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil and set aside.
  3. Soften the remaining garlic in the pan, add the tomatoes and simmer, then return the meatballs and cook gently.
  4. Boil the spaghetti until al dente and toss with some sauce.
  5. Serve topped with meatballs, basil and parmesan.

Sweets

Fire extinguisher pistachio semifreddoVegetarian

Frozen honey and pistachio cream, rose syrup.

Prep 30 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to a week ahead and keep frozen; slice straight from the freezer
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100g shelled pistachios, chopped
  • 2 tbsp rosewater
  • 50g sugar, for the syrup
  • 60ml water
  1. Whisk the yolks with half the sugar and the honey until pale and thick.
  2. Whip the cream to soft peaks, and in a clean bowl whisk the whites with the remaining sugar to firm peaks.
  3. Fold the cream, then the whites and most of the pistachios, into the yolks and spoon into a lined loaf tin.
  4. Freeze overnight. Boil the sugar and water for the syrup, cool and stir in the rosewater.
  5. Slice, drizzle with rose syrup and scatter with the remaining pistachios.

Drinks

Thai village iced teaNo alcohol

Strong spiced black tea, condensed milk, ice.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead; pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp loose black tea
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 125ml sweetened condensed milk
  • 125ml evaporated milk
  • 3 cups ice cubes
  1. Steep the tea, star anise and cinnamon in the boiling water for 5 minutes, then strain.
  2. Stir in the condensed milk and chill.
  3. Pour over ice in 4 tall glasses and float the evaporated milk on top.
