Snacks

Dockside lamb skewers

Spiced lamb kofta grilled on skewers, harissa yoghurt to dip.

Prep 22 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day ahead, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp harissa
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  1. Mix the lamb with the onion, cumin, parsley and salt.
  2. Shape around skewers.
  3. Grill over high heat until charred and cooked through, 10 min.
  4. Stir the harissa through the yoghurt and serve as a dip.

Aegean dive grilled octopus

Baby octopus marinated in lemon and oregano, grilled hot, with capers.

Prep 4 min, cook 5 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate up to 3 hours, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g baby octopus, cleaned
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons, one juiced and one in wedges
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp capers
  1. Marinate the octopus in the oil, lemon juice and oregano for an hour.
  2. Grill on a very hot plate for 2 to 3 minutes until charred and just tender.
  3. Pile onto a plate, scatter with capers and serve with lemon wedges.

Helena's panelle in sesame rolls

Chickpea fritters fried crisp in soft sesame rolls with lemon and salt, eaten standing at the dock.

How it is made
  • 250g chickpea flour
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 500ml vegetable oil
  • 4 sesame rolls
  • 1 lemon
  1. Sicilian street food: chickpea flour cooked to a thick paste with parsley, set, sliced and fried crisp, tucked into soft sesame rolls with lemon and salt. Whisk 250g chickpea flour into 750ml cold water with 1 tsp salt, then stir over medium heat for 10 minutes until very thick. Stir in 2 tbsp chopped parsley, spread 1cm thick on an oiled tray and cool 1 hour. Cut into squares and shallow fry in hot oil for 3 minutes a side. Pile into 4 split sesame rolls with lemon juice and salt.

Sicilian chicken with lemon and olives

Chicken thighs simmered with saffron, preserved lemon and green olives, over couscous.

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • saffron threads, a pinch
  • 500ml water
  • 1 preserved lemon, chopped
  • 100g green olives
  • 250g couscous
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil, 10 min.
  2. Soften the onion with the ginger and saffron. Add the water, preserved lemon and olives.
  3. Cover and simmer until tender, 45 min.
  4. Steep the couscous in hot water, fluff with a fork and serve under the tagine, 5 min.

Syracuse swordfish with caponata

Steaks seared golden under sweet and sour eggplant, tomato, olives, capers and pine nuts.

How it is made
  • 1 eggplant
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 400g can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 50g green olives
  • 1 jar capers
  • 30g pine nuts
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 4 swordfish steaks
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Swordfish steaks seared golden, finished with a sweet and sour caponata of eggplant, tomato, olives, capers and pine nuts, with crusty bread. Cube an eggplant and fry in olive oil until golden. Add a sliced onion and 2 celery sticks and soften. Stir in 400g crushed tomatoes, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 50g green olives and 1 tbsp capers and simmer 15 minutes. Toast 30g pine nuts and stir through with basil. Season 4 swordfish steaks and sear 2 minutes a side. Serve on the caponata with crusty bread.

Syracuse almond granita with briocheVegetarian

Icy almond granita scraped into glasses, a soft brioche bun to dip.

Prep 10 min, 3 hr 40 min waiting · Make ahead: Granita up to 2 days frozen, warm brioche to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g ground almonds
  • 800ml water
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 4 brioche buns
  1. Blend the almonds, water and sugar, stand for 30 minutes and strain well through a cloth, squeezing hard.
  2. Stir in the almond extract, pour into a shallow tray and freeze, scraping with a fork every 40 minutes until fluffy ice crystals form.
  3. Warm the brioche at 160°C for 5 minutes. Spoon the granita into glasses and serve with the brioche.

Syracuse blood orange sodaNo alcohol

Fresh blood orange juice topped with soda water over ice, a wheel of orange on top.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Juice up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 blood oranges
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 750ml soda water
  1. Juice 5 oranges and stir with the sugar until it dissolves.
  2. Pour over ice, top with soda water and add a wheel of the last orange.
