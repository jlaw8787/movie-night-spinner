Snacks

Tangier lamb kofta skewers

Spiced lamb kofta grilled on skewers, harissa yoghurt to dip.

Prep 22 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day ahead, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp harissa
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  1. Mix the lamb with the onion, cumin, parsley and salt.
  2. Shape around skewers.
  3. Grill over high heat until charred and cooked through.
  4. Stir the harissa through the yoghurt and serve as a dip.

Aegean dive grilled octopus

Baby octopus marinated in lemon and oregano, grilled hot, with capers.

Prep 4 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 3 hours, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g baby octopus, cleaned
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons, one juiced and one in wedges
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp capers
  1. Marinate the octopus in the oil, lemon juice and oregano for an hour.
  2. Grill on a very hot plate for 2 to 3 minutes until charred and just tender.
  3. Pile onto a plate, scatter with capers and serve with lemon wedges.

Apollo 11 parade soft pretzels

Street-cart twists dipped in bicarb water, rock salted and baked deep brown, spicy brown mustard.

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 jar hot English mustard
  • 1 jar honey
  1. New York street-cart soft pretzels dipped in bicarb water, sprinkled with rock salt and baked deep brown, served warm with spicy brown mustard. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Shape 8 big pretzels and dip each in 2L boiling water with 60g bicarb soda for 30 seconds. Sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Mix 3 tbsp wholegrain mustard with 1 tsp hot English mustard and 1 tsp honey to serve.

Mains

Moroccan chicken tagine

Chicken thighs simmered with saffron, preserved lemon and green olives, over couscous.

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • saffron threads, a pinch
  • 500ml water
  • 1 preserved lemon, chopped
  • 100g green olives
  • 250g couscous
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil.
  2. Soften the onion with the ginger and saffron. Add the water, preserved lemon and olives.
  3. Cover and simmer until tender.
  4. Steep the couscous in hot water, fluff with a fork and serve under the tagine.

Syracuse swordfish with caponata

Steaks seared golden under sweet and sour eggplant, tomato, olives, capers and pine nuts.

How it is made
  • 1 eggplant
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 400g can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 50g green olives
  • 1 jar capers
  • 30g pine nuts
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 4 swordfish steaks
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Swordfish steaks seared golden, finished with a sweet and sour caponata of eggplant, tomato, olives, capers and pine nuts, with crusty bread. Cube an eggplant and fry in olive oil until golden. Add a sliced onion and 2 celery sticks and soften. Stir in 400g crushed tomatoes, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 50g green olives and 1 tbsp capers and simmer 15 minutes. Toast 30g pine nuts and stir through with basil. Season 4 swordfish steaks and sear 2 minutes a side. Serve on the caponata with crusty bread.

Sweets

Syracuse almond granita with briocheVegetarian

Icy almond granita served with soft brioche.

Prep 10 min, then 3.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Granita up to 2 days frozen, warm brioche to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g ground almonds
  • 800ml water
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 4 brioche buns
  1. Blend the almonds, water and sugar, stand for 30 minutes and strain well through a cloth, squeezing hard.
  2. Stir in the almond extract, pour into a shallow tray and freeze, scraping with a fork every 40 minutes until fluffy ice crystals form.
  3. Warm the brioche at 160°C for 5 minutes. Spoon the granita into glasses and serve with the brioche.

Drinks

Mint tea in TangierNo alcohol

Green tea and fresh mint with sugar, poured from a height to froth.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp green tea leaves
  • 1 big bunch mint
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar, to taste
  • 1 L just-boiled water
  1. Put the green tea, mint and sugar in a teapot and pour over the hot water.
  2. Steep for 4 minutes.
  3. Pour from a height into small glasses so it froths, then pour back and pour again.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the tuk-tuk chase tears through Tangier.
