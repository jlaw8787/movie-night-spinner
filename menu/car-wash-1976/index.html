Lunch truck tacos

Spiced ground beef in corn tortillas with lettuce, cheddar and salsa.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the mince filling up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled to reheat; shred the lettuce and warm the tortillas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 4 tbsp salsa
  1. Dice the onion and garlic.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion, garlic, cumin and chilli, 12 min.
  3. Shred the lettuce and warm the tortillas, 3 min.
  4. Fill with the beef, lettuce, cheddar and a spoon of salsa.

Mona's diner chilli cheese fries

Thick fries under beef and bean chilli and melted cheddar.

Prep 18 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; fry the chips fresh and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 tbsp chilli powder
  • 400g can kidney beans, drained
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips.
  2. Brown the mince with chopped onion and chilli powder, 8 min.
  3. Add the kidney beans and a splash of water and simmer, 20 min.
  4. Fry the chips in the oil until soft, then fry again until crisp, 10 min.
  5. Spoon the chilli over, top with cheddar and grill to melt, 5 min.

Shoeshine stand boiled peanuts

Peanuts boiled soft in a Cajun brine, a paper bag of them at the shoeshine stand.

How it is made
  • 750g raw peanuts in shell
  • 80g salt
  • 3 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • dried chillies
  1. Raw peanuts in the shell boiled soft in a salty Cajun spice brine with garlic and chilli, soaked to take on the flavour, served warm in paper cups. Rinse 750g raw peanuts in the shell and put in a big pot with 3L water, 80g salt, 3 tbsp Cajun seasoning, 4 smashed garlic cloves and 2 dried chillies. Weigh them down with a plate, bring to the boil and simmer for 2 hours until the nuts are soft. Turn off the heat and leave them soaking in the brine for an hour. Drain and serve warm in paper cups.

Dee-Luxe hot dogs with the works

Grilled franks in steamed buns with beef chilli, mustard, onion and relish.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Simmer the chilli up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled to reheat; griddle the franks and dice the onion on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 150ml water
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 tbsp sweet relish
  1. Simmer the mince with cumin, chilli powder and water until loose, 20 min.
  2. Griddle the franks and steam the buns, 8 min.
  3. Dice the onion.
  4. Fill the buns with franks and top with chilli, mustard, raw onion and relish.

Buttermilk fried chicken with slaw

Buttermilk fried chicken with creamy slaw, for the crew's lunch hour.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • hot sauce
  • 800g potatoes
  • mayonnaise
  • yellow mustard
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 0.25 cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • white vinegar
  • 300g plain flour
  • smoked paprika
  • garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  1. Chicken pieces brined in buttermilk, dredged in seasoned flour, fried crisp, with a creamy mustard potato salad and crunchy cabbage coleslaw. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces overnight in 500ml buttermilk with salt and hot sauce. Boil 800g potatoes until tender, cool and mix with mayonnaise, mustard, chopped celery and spring onion. Shred a quarter cabbage and a carrot and toss with mayo and vinegar. Dredge the chicken in 300g flour with paprika, garlic powder and pepper and fry at 165°C for 12 to 14 minutes until 74°C inside.

Wilson Sisters fruit popsiclesVegetarian

Fresh orange juice frozen around berries on sticks.

Prep 6 min, 7 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150g mixed frozen berries
  • 8 icy pole sticks
  1. Stir the sugar into the orange juice until dissolved.
  2. Drop a few berries into each mould and pour the juice over.
  3. Freeze for an hour, then push the sticks in.
  4. Freeze solid, 6 hr.

Lunch truck watermelon agua frescaNo alcohol

Fresh watermelon, lime and sugar blended and strained, over ice.

Prep 10 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg seedless watermelon flesh
  • 500ml cold water
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend the watermelon with water, lime juice and sugar until smooth.
  2. Strain into a jug.
  3. Chill, 1 hr.
  4. Pour over ice with lime wedges.
