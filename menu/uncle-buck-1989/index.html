Miles's birthday caramel popcorn balls

Popcorn bound with sticky caramel and marshmallow, rolled in rainbow sprinkles.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the balls up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 200g marshmallows
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 50g rainbow sprinkles
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot and tip into a big bowl, discarding unpopped kernels.
  2. Melt the butter, marshmallows and brown sugar over low heat, stirring until smooth, with a pinch of salt, 5 min.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and stir quickly to coat. Cool briefly.
  4. With buttered hands squeeze into balls and roll in the sprinkles.
  5. Set on baking paper, 20 min.

Bowling night cheesy gravy fries

Hot fries under melted cheddar and rich beef gravy, a bowling alley classic.

Prep 2 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 3 days ahead and reheat with a splash of water; bake the chips and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 1 tsp chicken salt
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C, turning once, then toss with chicken salt, 25 min.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the stock and Worcestershire. Simmer until thick and glossy, 8 min.
  3. Pile the chips on a platter, scatter with cheddar and pour over the hot gravy.
  4. Finish with chives.

Big breakfast for dinner

Eggs, sausages, bacon, beans and toast, for a six-year-old who's a dreamer.

Prep 5 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead and reheat in a hot pan
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frozen hash browns
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 420g tin baked beans
  • 8 eggs
  • 60ml full cream milk
  • 60g butter
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Bake the hash browns at 220°C until crisp, 20 min.
  2. Fry the sausages over medium heat, turning, 12 min.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp, 6 min.
  4. Warm the baked beans, 4 min.
  5. Whisk the eggs with the milk and salt and cook in some of the butter over low heat, stirring gently, until just set, 6 min.
  6. Toast and butter the bread. Pile everything onto big plates with tomato sauce, 4 min.

Family-size beef lasagna

Layers of beef ragù, pasta and bubbling cheese, sized for a family, not the microwave.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 21 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700g tomato passata
  • 250g dried lasagne sheets
  • 40g butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 500ml milk
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the mince in the oil, add the onion and garlic, then the passata, and simmer, 20 min.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until thick, 8 min.
  3. Heat the oven to 180°C. Layer the ragù, pasta and white sauce in a dish, finishing with sauce and mozzarella, 8 min.
  4. Bake until golden and bubbling, then rest. Serve with the lettuce and tomato, 45 min.

Uncle Buck's giant buttermilk pancake

One giant pancake with maple syrup, banana and crumbled bacon.

Prep 3 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Cook and crumble the bacon up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; make the batter and cook the pancake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml maple syrup
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 3 rashers of bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar and a pinch of salt. Whisk in the eggs, buttermilk and some melted butter until just combined.
  2. Heat a large 28cm non-stick pan over low heat and grease with butter. Pour in all the batter and cover with a lid.
  3. Cook until bubbles set on top, 10 min.
  4. Slide onto a plate, flip back in and cook the other side, 5 min.
  5. Top with butter, maple syrup, banana and crumbled bacon.

Chocolate malted milkNo alcohol

Milk, chocolate malt, cocoa and vanilla ice cream blended thick.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the milk and the glasses the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cold full cream milk
  • 6 tbsp chocolate malt powder, plus extra to sprinkle
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  1. Put the milk, malt powder, cocoa, honey and ice cream in a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and very frothy.
  3. Pour into big glasses, sprinkle malt powder over the froth and serve with straws.
