Snacks

Pork & chive dumplings

Everybody relax, I'm here.

Prep 46 min, cook 9 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 bunch garlic chives, chopped
  • 10g ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100ml water
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the pork with the chives, ginger, soy, Shaoxing and sesame oil.
  2. Pleat spoonfuls into the dumpling wrappers.
  3. Fry until the bottoms crisp, 5 min.
  4. Add the water and steam with the lid on, 4 min.
  5. Serve with vinegar and chilli oil.

Salt & pepper squid

Thunder, Rain and Lightning.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g squid tubes
  • 100g potato starch
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 1 pinch five spice
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 red chillies, sliced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Score the squid and cut into strips.
  2. Toss in the starch with salt, white pepper and five spice.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil to 190°C and fry in batches until crisp, 4 min.
  4. Wok-toss with fried garlic, red chillies and spring onion.

Mains

Wang Chi's beef ho fun

Fast hands. It's all in the reflexes.

Prep 26 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef rump, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 800g fresh flat rice noodles
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 4 spring onions, cut into lengths
  • 200g bean sprouts
  • 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  1. Toss the beef with 1 tbsp of the soy sauce, the cornflour and 1 tbsp of the oil, and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Loosen the noodles by hand under warm water and drain well, 5 min.
  3. Sear the beef in a smoking hot wok with 1 tbsp of the oil for 1 minute, then tip it out.
  4. Fry the onion in the last of the oil, add the noodles and toss over high heat until they catch at the edges, 5 min.
  5. Add the rest of the soy sauce, the dark soy and sugar, then the beef, bean sprouts and spring onion, and toss for 1 minute. Serve straight away.

Pork-Chop Express ribs

Straight off Jack's truck.

Prep 40 min, cook 1 hr 30 min, 8 hr 10 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork ribs
  • 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp five spice
  • 1 cube red fermented bean curd
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  1. Mix the hoisin, honey, soy, five spice, bean curd and garlic and rub over the ribs.
  2. Marinate in the fridge overnight, at least 8 hours.
  3. Roast covered at 160°C, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Uncover, glaze and blast at 220°C until sticky.
  5. Rest briefly before serving, 10 min.

House fried riceVegetarian

Chang Sing or Wing Kong, all order it.

Prep 7 min, cook 6 min
Recipe for 4
  • 240g jasmine rice
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  1. Cook 240g jasmine rice ahead (the day before is best), spread it out and chill. Cold rice fries and holds better.
  2. Beat the eggs and scramble them softly in a hot wok with half the oil, then tip them out.
  3. Fry the rice in the rest of the oil over high heat, breaking up the clumps, until it starts to toast, 6 min.
  4. Add the peas, soy sauce and white pepper, then the egg and spring onion, and finish with the sesame oil.

Garlic gai lanVegetarian

Green as Miao Yin's eyes.

Prep 4 min, cook 7 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches of gai lan
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  1. Trim the gai lan and halve any thick stems.
  2. Blanch in boiling salted water for 1 minute and drain.
  3. Fry the garlic in the oil until golden, then toss the gai lan through with the soy, sugar and water for 1 minute.

Sweets

Fortune cookiesVegetarian

Jack would ignore his anyway.

Prep 36 min, cook 6 min
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 60g plain flour
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 12 paper fortunes
  1. Write 12 fortunes on thin paper strips, two of each: It's all in the reflexes. Green eyes mean big trouble. Never lend out your truck. The bag holds six things, don't ask. Pay what you owe before you go underground. Somebody at this table owes you twenty bucks.
  2. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar, then stir in the flour, butter and vanilla.
  3. Spread 8cm circles on a lined tray, a few at a time.
  4. Bake at 180°C, 6 min.
  5. Working fast, fold each around a paper fortune while hot.

Drinks

Egg Shen's magic potion

The flask for the trip below.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 50g sugar
  • 50ml water
  • 20g ginger, sliced
  • 240ml plum wine
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 1 egg white
  • 4 pickled plums or cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and ginger, then cool and strain, 8 min.
  2. Shake the plum wine, lemon juice, ginger syrup and egg white hard without ice, then with ice.
  3. Strain into 4 ice-filled glasses and garnish with a pickled plum.
