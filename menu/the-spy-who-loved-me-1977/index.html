Sheikh Hosein's tent ta'ameyaVegetarian

Green Egyptian broad bean falafel with herbs and sesame, with tahini lemon sauce and warm pita.

Prep 19 min, cook 23 min, then 12.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the broad beans overnight, at least 12 hours; blitz the paste up to 1 day ahead and chill; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dried split broad beans (fava)
  • 1 small onion, quartered
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 3 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 4 tbsp water
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Soak the broad beans in plenty of cold water overnight.
  2. Drain well and blitz with the onion, garlic, coriander, parsley, spring onions, cumin, ground coriander, chilli and most of the salt to a coarse green paste. Chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Whisk the tahini with the lemon juice, water and the last of the salt until smooth.
  4. Stir the bicarb into the paste, shape into 20 small flat patties and press the tops into the sesame seeds.
  5. Heat the oil to 175°C in a deep heavy pan, 8 min.
  6. Fry in batches for about 4 minutes, turning, until deep green inside and crisp outside.
  7. Warm the pita and serve with the ta'ameya and tahini sauce, 4 min.

Mujaba Club lamb kofta

Spiced lamb kofta on skewers, chargrilled, with cucumber and mint yoghurt.

Prep 24 min, cook 10 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Shape the kofta up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; soak the skewers for 30 minutes; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 12 bamboo skewers
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1/2 Lebanese cucumber, grated
  • 1 tbsp mint, chopped
  1. Soak the skewers in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Knead the lamb with the onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, paprika, cinnamon, allspice, salt and pepper for 2 minutes.
  3. Divide into 12 and mould each into a long sausage around a skewer.
  4. Chill for 20 minutes.
  5. Squeeze the cucumber dry and mix with the yoghurt and mint.
  6. Brush the kofta with the oil and cook on a hot chargrill pan or barbecue for 8 minutes, turning, until browned and just cooked.
  7. Serve with the yoghurt.

Stromberg's Sardinian fregola with clams

Toasted pasta pearls cooked in saffron broth with clams, cherry tomatoes, chilli and parsley.

Prep 4 min, cook 31 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; purge the clams 30 minutes ahead and keep them cold, damp and covered
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg clams (vongole)
  • 800ml fish stock
  • saffron threads, a pinch
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 small red chilli, chopped
  • 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 300g fregola or pearl couscous
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Soak the clams in cold salted water for 30 minutes to purge any sand, then drain.
  2. Heat the stock with the saffron and keep it hot, 5 min.
  3. Fry the garlic and chilli in 3 tbsp of the oil for a minute, then add the tomatoes and cook for 4 minutes until collapsing.
  4. Add the fregola and toast for 2 minutes, then add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 12 minutes until just tender.
  5. Add the clams, cover and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until opened. Throw out any that stay shut.
  6. Stir in the parsley, lemon zest and the rest of the oil and season.
  7. Rest for 2 minutes and serve in warm bowls.

Sardinian roast pork with rosemary

The island's feast, crackling and potatoes.

Prep 10 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Score and salt the pork up to 1 day ahead, uncovered in the fridge so the skin dries; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg boneless pork shoulder, skin scored
  • 1 tbsp fennel seeds
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 small bunch rosemary
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 1 lemon
  1. Pound the fennel seeds, garlic, rosemary leaves and salt with a little oil and rub into the meat, not the skin. Salt the skin well.
  2. Roast at 240°C until the skin starts to blister.
  3. Turn the oven to 170°C, add the potatoes tossed in oil around the pork and roast until tender.
  4. Rest the pork, then carve with the crackling and serve with the potatoes and lemon, 15 min.

Costa Smeralda seadas with honeyVegetarian

Fried pastry pockets filled with molten lemon-scented cheese, drenched in warm honey.

Prep 23 min, cook 20 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Fill and seal the pastries up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled on floured baking paper; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour, plus extra for rolling
  • 40g butter, softened
  • salt, a pinch
  • 110ml warm water
  • 200g fior di latte, torn and squeezed dry
  • 50g pecorino, grated
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp honey
  1. Knead the flour, butter, salt and warm water to a smooth dough, wrap and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the fior di latte, pecorino and lemon zest and press into 4 flat discs.
  3. Roll the dough thin on a floured bench and cut 8 rounds of 12cm. Sandwich each cheese disc between two rounds, press out the air and seal the edges well with a fork.
  4. Heat the oil to 175°C in a wide deep pan, 8 min.
  5. Fry 2 at a time for about 3 minutes a side until blistered and golden, then drain.
  6. Warm the honey, pour over the hot seadas and serve straight away, 3 min.

Drinks

Atlantis myrtle spritz

Myrtle berry liqueur topped with sparkling wine and soda over ice, with lemon peel and blueberries.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Chill the sparkling wine and soda for at least 2 hours ahead; build in the glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cups ice
  • 120ml myrtle liqueur (mirto)
  • 480ml dry sparkling wine, chilled
  • 200ml soda water, chilled
  • 4 strips of lemon peel
  • 12 blueberries
  1. Fill 4 wine glasses with ice.
  2. Add 30ml of the myrtle liqueur to each.
  3. Top with the sparkling wine, then a splash of soda.
  4. Twist a strip of lemon peel over each glass, drop it in and add 3 blueberries.
