Snacks

Nostromo crew noodlesVegetarian

Wheat noodles in sesame, soy, black vinegar and chilli oil, spring onion

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g fresh wheat noodles
  • 3 tbsp sesame paste
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Cook the noodles, then rinse cold and drain.
  2. Whisk the sesame paste, soy, vinegar, chilli oil and garlic.
  3. Cut the cucumber into matchsticks and slice the spring onion.
  4. Toss the noodles through the dressing and top with cucumber and spring onion.

Freighter stores crackers

Seeded crackers with lemon cream cheese and a smoked oyster on each

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 16 seeded crackers
  • 170g smoked oysters (2 small tins), drained
  • 1 bunch chives
  • cracked pepper, to taste
  1. Whip the cream cheese with the lemon zest.
  2. Spread onto the crackers.
  3. Top each with a smoked oyster, cracked pepper and snipped chives. Serve straight away.

Mains

Nostromo crew dinner

Pork and vegetable noodle stir-fry in bowls

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the pork and vegetables up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; stir-fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork shoulder
  • 5 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 knob ginger, grated
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slice the pork thin and toss with a little of the soy.
  2. Slice the cabbage, carrots and capsicum.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok until smoking and stir-fry the pork until browned.
  4. Add the vegetables and ginger and stir-fry briefly.
  5. Add the noodles, oyster sauce and remaining soy, toss and serve in bowls.

Red velvet chestburster cakeVegetarian

Red velvet cake filled with raspberry jam, cream cheese frosting

Prep 40 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Cake up to 1 day, assemble to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and grease a heatproof bowl. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk, vinegar and red colouring.
  3. Bake in the bowl until a skewer comes out clean, then cool completely.
  4. Turn out and hollow out the centre. Fill with the raspberry jam.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar and cover the dome in frosting.

Hypersleep wake-up iced mochaVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled coffee blended with dark chocolate and milk, whipped cream on top

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Coffee mix up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml strong brewed coffee
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 2 cup ice
  • 1 can whipped cream
  1. Brew the coffee. Stir in most of the chocolate, chopped, and the sugar until melted, then cool.
  2. Blend with the milk and ice until frothy.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses, top with whipped cream and shave the remaining chocolate over.
