Snacks

Nostromo crew noodlesVegetarian

Wheat noodles in sesame, soy, black vinegar and chilli oil, scallion.

Prep 12 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g fresh wheat noodles
  • 3 tbsp sesame paste
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Cook the noodles, then rinse cold and drain, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the sesame paste, soy, vinegar, chilli oil and garlic.
  3. Cut the cucumber into matchsticks and slice the spring onion.
  4. Toss the noodles through the dressing and top with cucumber and spring onion.

Freighter stores crackers

Seeded crackers with lemon cream cheese and a smoked oyster on each.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 16 seeded crackers
  • 170g smoked oysters (2 small tins), drained
  • 1 bunch chives
  • cracked pepper, to taste
  1. Whip the cream cheese with the lemon zest.
  2. Spread onto the crackers.
  3. Top each with a smoked oyster, cracked pepper and snipped chives. Serve straight away.

Mains

Nostromo crew dinner

Pork and vegetable noodle stir-fry in bowls.

Prep 23 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Slice the pork and vegetables up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; stir-fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork shoulder
  • 5 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 knob ginger, grated
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slice the pork thin and toss with a little of the soy.
  2. Slice the cabbage, carrots and capsicum.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok until smoking and stir-fry the pork until browned, 4 min.
  4. Add the vegetables and ginger and stir-fry briefly, 3 min.
  5. Add the noodles, oyster sauce and remaining soy, toss and serve in bowls.

Ripley's escape shuttle beef chilli

Ground beef browned hard with onion, garlic and cumin, simmered with tomato and beans, sour cream.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tin kidney beans
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Beef mince browned hard with onion, garlic, chilli and cumin, simmered with tomato and kidney beans until thick, with sour cream, cheddar and crusty bread. Brown 800g beef mince hard in a heavy pot in two batches. Add 2 diced onions and 3 garlic cloves and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp chilli powder, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika and 2 tbsp tomato paste. Add 800g crushed tomatoes, a drained 400g tin kidney beans and 250ml beef stock. Simmer 90 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. Serve with sour cream, grated cheddar and crusty bread.

Red velvet chestburster cakeVegetarian

Red velvet cake filled with raspberry jam, cream cheese frosting.

Prep 25 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Cake up to 1 day, assemble to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and grease a heatproof bowl. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs, 10 min.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk, vinegar and red colouring.
  3. Bake in the bowl until a skewer comes out clean, then cool completely, 1 hr.
  4. Turn out and hollow out the centre. Fill with the raspberry jam.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar and cover the dome in icing.

Hypersleep wake-up iced mochaNo alcohol

Chilled coffee blended with dark chocolate and milk, whipped cream on top.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Coffee mix up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml strong brewed coffee
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 2 cups ice
  • 1 can whipped cream
  1. Brew the coffee. Stir in most of the chocolate, chopped, and the sugar until melted, then cool, 12 min.
  2. Blend with the milk and ice until frothy.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses, top with whipped cream and shave the remaining chocolate over.
