Corn syrup drought popcornVegetarian

Honey butter popcorn and flaky salt. Wade was born after the corn syrup droughts.

Prep 2 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Pop and coat up to 4 hours ahead and keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pot, add the corn, cover and shake until the popping stops, 5 min.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey until bubbling, 3 min.
  3. Toss through the popcorn with the salt.

Copper Key buffalo wings

Hot sauce wings with blue cheese dip.

Prep 3 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Mix the dip up to 2 days ahead; bake the wings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings, tips off and halved
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 125ml Louisiana-style hot sauce
  • 60g butter
  • 100g blue cheese, crumbled
  • 125g sour cream
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 4 celery sticks, cut in batons
  1. Pat the wings dry, toss with the baking powder and salt and lay on a rack over a tray.
  2. Bake at 220°C, turning once, until crisp and golden, 40 min.
  3. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream and mayo.
  4. Melt the butter into the hot sauce and toss the wings through, then serve with celery and dip, 4 min.

Jade Key green chicken curry

Thai green curry, beans and jasmine rice.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the curry up to a day ahead and reheat gently; cook the rice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, sliced
  • 3 tbsp green curry paste
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 200g green beans, trimmed
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • 1 lime
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Fry the curry paste in a splash of the coconut milk until fragrant, 3 min.
  2. Add the chicken and stir until coated, then pour in the rest of the coconut milk, 5 min.
  3. Simmer with the beans and zucchini until the chicken is cooked, 12 min.
  4. Season with fish sauce, sugar and lime and stir through the basil.
  5. Serve with the jasmine rice.

Sixer number six cheeseburger

Two smashed patties, cheese, pickles and fries.

Prep 2 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Mix the burger sauce up to 2 days ahead; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 4 brioche buns
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 750g frozen shoestring chips
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp, 25 min.
  2. Mix the mayo, tomato sauce and mustard into a burger sauce.
  3. Roll the mince into 8 balls, smash thin on a hot griddle with onion and cook 2 minutes a side, cheese on top.
  4. Toast the buns and stack two patties each with pickles and sauce, 5 min.

Halliday's golden Easter eggVegetarian

Chocolate egg with salted caramel and gold dust.

Prep 0 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make the shells and caramel up to 3 days ahead; fill and dust to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 40g butter
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp edible gold dust
  • 250g vanilla ice cream
  1. Melt two thirds of the chocolate, take off the heat and stir in the rest until glossy, 8 min.
  2. Brush into 8 half-egg moulds in two coats and chill until set, 12 min.
  3. Cook the sugar to deep amber, whisk in the cream, butter and salt and cool, 10 min.
  4. Unmould, fill the halves with ice cream and caramel, join and brush with gold dust.
