Quick Stop nacho cheese dipVegetarian

Smooth melted cheddar dip with a pinch of chilli, corn chips to dunk.

Prep 2 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, rewarm gently
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 1 pinch chilli powder
  • 230g corn chips
  1. Toss the cheddar with the cornflour.
  2. Heat the evaporated milk until steaming, then stir in the cheese a handful at a time over low heat until smooth.
  3. Season with a pinch of chilli and keep warm. Serve with corn chips for dunking.

Doctor's orders crudités with green goddess

Carrot, celery, cucumber and snow peas around a herby avocado yoghurt dip, chilled crisp.

How it is made
  • 1 avocado
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 carrots
  • 3 celery sticks
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 150g snow peas
  1. Crisp carrot, celery, cucumber, capsicum and snow peas around a bright green goddess dip of herbs, yoghurt, avocado and lemon. Blitz 1 avocado, 150g Greek yoghurt, 2 tbsp mayo, a big handful each of parsley, basil and chives, 1 garlic clove, the juice of 1 lemon and salt until smooth and bright green. Chill. Cut 2 carrots, 3 celery sticks, 1 cucumber and 1 red capsicum into batons and trim 150g snow peas. Stand the vegetables in iced water for 10 minutes, drain well and arrange around the dip.

RST Video pork roll, egg and cheese

Fried pork roll, fried egg and American cheese on a soft roll.

Prep 3 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork roll, in 8 slices
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Nick the edges of the pork roll slices and fry until curled and browned.
  2. Fry the eggs.
  3. Stack pork roll, egg and cheese into the rolls, add tomato sauce, salt and pepper.
  4. Wrap in foil for a couple of minutes to melt.

Chicken parm heroes

Chicken parm in a roll, lunch on the Inconvenience set.

How it is made
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  • vegetable oil
  • 400g passata
  • 1 garlic clove
  • dried oregano
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 4 long rolls
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Crumbed chicken fried golden, topped with marinara and mozzarella, grilled until bubbling and stuffed into toasted long rolls with basil. Halve 2 large chicken breasts horizontally and pound to 1cm. Coat in flour, beaten egg and 120g breadcrumbs mixed with 30g grated parmesan. Shallow fry in oil 3 minutes a side until golden. Simmer 400g passata with 1 garlic clove and oregano for 10 minutes. Put the chicken on a tray, spoon over sauce, top with 200g grated mozzarella and grill 3 minutes until bubbling. Tuck into 4 toasted long rolls with basil leaves.

Buddy Christ crumb cake squaresVegetarian

Cinnamon crumb cake, cut into squares and dusted with icing sugar.

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, cold, for the crumb
  • 150g self-raising flour, for the crumb
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour, for the cake
  • 100ml milk
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Rub the cold butter into the crumb flour, brown sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Beat the soft butter with the sugar, eggs, flour and milk and spread in a lined tin.
  3. Cover with the crumb and bake at 175°C.
  4. Cool and dust with icing sugar.

Coffee cakeVegetarian

Buttery coffee cake with a walnut and cinnamon swirl.

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g sour cream
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 80g walnuts, chopped
  • 50g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs and sour cream, then fold in the flour.
  2. Mix the walnuts with the brown sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Layer half the batter, the walnut mix, then the rest in a greased ring tin.
  4. Bake at 175°C.
  5. Cool before turning out.

Black and white cold brewNo alcohol

Cream on top. Silent Bob shoots it in black and white.

Prep 15 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Start the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 100g coarsely ground coffee
  • 800ml cold water
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Stir the coffee into the water and leave in the fridge overnight, which is the resting time.
  2. Strain through a sieve lined with a paper towel.
  3. Whisk the cream with a little of the milk, the brown sugar and cinnamon until thick but pourable.
  4. Fill 4 glasses with ice, pour in cold brew and a splash of milk, then spoon the cinnamon foam on top.
