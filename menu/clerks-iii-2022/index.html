Snacks

Quick Stop nacho cheese dipVegetarian

Smooth melted cheddar dip with a pinch of chilli, corn chips to dunk

Prep 10 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Best fresh, rewarm gently
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 1 pinch chilli powder
  • 230g corn chips
  1. Toss the cheddar with the cornflour.
  2. Heat the evaporated milk until steaming, then stir in the cheese a handful at a time over low heat until smooth.
  3. Season with a pinch of chilli and keep warm. Serve with corn chips for dunking.

Mains

RST Video pork roll, egg and cheese

Fried pork roll, fried egg and American cheese on a soft roll

Prep 10 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork roll, in 8 slices
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Nick the edges of the pork roll slices and fry until curled and browned.
  2. Fry the eggs.
  3. Stack pork roll, egg and cheese into the rolls, add tomato sauce, salt and pepper.
  4. Wrap in foil for a couple of minutes to melt.

Sweets

Buddy Christ crumb cake squaresVegetarian

Elias and Blockchain sell Buddy Christ kites. Cinnamon crumb

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, cold, for the crumb
  • 150g self-raising flour, for the crumb
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour, for the cake
  • 100ml milk
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Rub the cold butter into the crumb flour, brown sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Beat the soft butter with the sugar, eggs, flour and milk and spread in a lined tin.
  3. Cover with the crumb and bake at 175C.
  4. Cool and dust with icing sugar.

Quick Stop coffee cakeVegetarian

Emma lends Dante thirty grand. Walnut swirl

Prep 20 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g sour cream
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 80g walnuts, chopped
  • 50g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs and sour cream, then fold in the flour.
  2. Mix the walnuts with the brown sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Layer half the batter, the walnut mix, then the rest in a greased ring tin.
  4. Bake at 175C.
  5. Cool before turning out.

Drinks

Black and white cold brewVegetarianNo alcohol

Silent Bob shoots it in black and white. Cream on top

Prep 10 min, cook 725 min · Make ahead: Start the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 100g coarsely ground coffee
  • 800ml cold water
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Stir the coffee into the water and leave in the fridge overnight, which is the resting time.
  2. Strain through a sieve lined with a paper towel.
  3. Whisk the cream with a little of the milk, the brown sugar and cinnamon until thick but pourable.
  4. Fill 4 glasses with ice, pour in cold brew and a splash of milk, then spoon the cinnamon foam on top.
