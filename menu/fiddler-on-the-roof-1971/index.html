Golde's Friday night challahVegetarian

The candles lit on the Friday night table as the sun goes down

Prep 30 min, cook 177 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep wrapped; the dough can do its first prove overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 200ml warm water
  • 500g bread flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 egg yolk, for glazing
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Stir the yeast and honey into the warm water and leave until foamy.
  2. Add the flour, salt, eggs and oil and knead for 10 minutes until smooth and springy.
  3. Cover and prove until doubled.
  4. Divide into 3, roll each into a 40cm rope and plait them, tucking the ends under.
  5. Set on a lined tray, cover loosely and prove until puffy. Heat the oven to 190C.
  6. Brush with the egg yolk, scatter with sesame seeds and bake until deep golden and hollow when tapped underneath.
  7. Cool on a rack before tearing.

Yente's chopped herring on rye

Pickled fish chopped with egg, apple and red onion, on dark bread with dill, matchmaker's choice

Prep 20 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggs
  • 300g pickled herring fillets, drained
  • 1 green apple, peeled and cored
  • 1/2 small red onion
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • black pepper, to taste
  • 8 slices dark rye bread
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
  2. Chop the herring, eggs, apple and onion finely by hand on a board.
  3. Mix with the vinegar, sugar and plenty of black pepper.
  4. Cover and chill so the flavours settle.
  5. Spread thickly on the rye bread and scatter with dill.

Tevye's dairyman cheese blintzesVegetarian

Tevye's milk cart doing the rounds while the horse limps along

Prep 25 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Cook the pancakes and mix the filling up to 1 day ahead, keep separately in the fridge; make the cherries up to 3 days ahead; fill and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 375ml milk
  • pinch of salt
  • 30g butter, melted, for the batter
  • 400g ricotta
  • 150g cream cheese, softened
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the filling
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 400g frozen pitted cherries
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar, for the cherries
  • 2 tsp cornflour
  • 40g butter, for frying
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk, salt and melted butter to a smooth thin batter and rest it.
  2. Beat the ricotta, cream cheese, egg yolk, filling sugar, vanilla and lemon zest until smooth.
  3. Simmer the cherries with their sugar and the lemon juice until juicy, then stir in the cornflour mixed with a splash of water and bubble until glossy.
  4. Cook 12 thin pancakes in a lightly buttered 20cm frying pan, on one side only, and stack them cooked side up.
  5. Put a heaped spoonful of filling on each, fold in the sides and roll into a parcel.
  6. Fry the parcels in the frying butter, seam side down first, until golden on both sides.
  7. Serve 3 each with sour cream and the warm cherries.

Golde's potato kugelVegetarian

Crisp-edged potato and onion bake. Tradition!

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and reheat in a hot oven to crisp the edges again
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 eggs
  • 40g matzo meal
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  1. Pour half the oil into a baking tin and heat it in the oven at 200C.
  2. Grate the potatoes and onion, then squeeze out as much liquid as you can in a clean tea towel.
  3. Mix with the eggs, matzo meal, the rest of the oil, plenty of salt and pepper.
  4. Spread into the hot tin and bake until deep golden with crisp edges.

Grandma Tzeitel's honey cakeVegetarian

Dark and moist with cinnamon, clove, ginger, orange and tea, almonds on top, for Tevye's dream

Prep 20 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 4 days ahead; it gets stickier and better wrapped at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 250g honey
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 180ml strong black tea, cooled
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 50g flaked almonds
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a 23cm square cake tin.
  2. Whisk the eggs and brown sugar until thick, then whisk in the honey, oil and tea.
  3. Whisk in the flour, baking powder, bicarbonate of soda, spices and orange zest to a smooth, runny batter.
  4. Pour into the tin, scatter with the almonds and bake until dark and springy and a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool in the tin before cutting into squares.

Samovar glass tea with lemon and cherry jamVegetarianNo alcohol

The whole family round Tevye's table with the samovar always on

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Slice the lemon up to 4 hours ahead; brew the tea fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp strong loose leaf black tea
  • 1l boiling water
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp cherry jam
  • 8 sugar cubes
  1. Brew the tea in a small pot with 250ml of the boiling water for 5 minutes to make a strong concentrate.
  2. Pour a little concentrate into 4 heatproof glasses and top up with the rest of the boiling water to taste.
  3. Serve each glass with a lemon slice, a spoonful of cherry jam and sugar cubes on the side.
