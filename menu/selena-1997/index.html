Snacks

Tour bus breakfast tacos

Potato, chorizo and soft scrambled egg in flour tortillas with red salsa

Prep 20 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Make the red salsa up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; fry the potatoes and chorizo, scramble the eggs and warm the tortillas to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 6 eggs
  • 300g potatoes
  • 1 chorizo
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 1 small red chilli
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Dice the potatoes and fry in oil until crisp.
  2. Add the sliced chorizo and cook through.
  3. Scramble the eggs softly and fold through.
  4. Chop tomato, onion and chilli for a quick red salsa.
  5. Warm the tortillas, fill with the mix and cheddar and top with the salsa.

Corpus Christi prawn cocktail

Chopped prawns in chilled tomato lime broth with avocado, crackers on the side

Prep 15 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Chill up to 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked prawns
  • 500ml cold tomato juice
  • 2 limes
  • 2 avocados
  • 1/2 white onion
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 box crackers
  1. Chop the prawns and dice the avocado and onion.
  2. Mix with tomato juice, lime juice, coriander and hot sauce.
  3. Chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Spoon into glasses with crackers on the side.

Los Dinos Tex-Mex cheese enchiladas

Corn tortillas rolled around cheddar and onion under a chilli gravy

Prep 25 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; roll, sauce and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 300g cheddar, grated
  • 1 brown onion
  1. Cook the flour in oil, then stir in the chilli powder and cumin.
  2. Add the stock gradually and simmer until thick.
  3. Roll the tortillas around grated cheddar and diced onion and lay in a dish.
  4. Pour over the gravy and scatter with more cheddar.
  5. Bake at 190C until bubbling.

Sweets

Tres leches cakeVegetarian

Sponge soaked in three milks and topped with whipped cream

Prep 30 min, cook 740 min · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 395g can sweetened condensed milk
  • 375ml can evaporated milk
  • 125ml milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in a lined tin at 175C.
  3. Prick all over and pour over the condensed milk, evaporated milk and milk mixed together.
  4. Soak overnight in the fridge.
  5. Whip the cream and top the cake with cream and strawberries.

Drinks

Astrodome watermelon agua frescaVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh watermelon and lime drink over crushed ice with a chilli salt rim

Prep 15 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg watermelon flesh
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml cold water
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Blend the watermelon with lime juice, sugar and water until smooth.
  2. Strain into a big jug.
  3. Chill for an hour.
  4. Mix the salt with chilli powder and rim four tall glasses with lime and the chilli salt.
  5. Fill with crushed ice, pour in the agua fresca and top with torn mint.
