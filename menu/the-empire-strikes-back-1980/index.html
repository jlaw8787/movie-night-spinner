Snacks

Dagobah swamp dipVegetarian

Baked spinach, artichoke and cream cheese dip with corn chips

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g spinach
  • 280g marinated artichokes, chopped (1 jar)
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 bag corn chips
  1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Mix with the artichokes, cream cheese, sour cream and parmesan.
  3. Spread in a dish and top with mozzarella.
  4. Bake at 190C until bubbling and golden. Serve with corn chips.

Mains

Yoda's rootleaf stewVegetarian

Parsnip, carrot, swede, celeriac and potato stew with miso, crusty bread

Prep 25 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 parsnips
  • 2 carrots
  • 1/2 swede
  • 1/2 celeriac
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 1/4l vegetable stock
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp miso paste
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Cut the parsnips, carrots, swede, celeriac and potatoes into chunks.
  2. Soften the onion in the oil.
  3. Add the vegetables, stock, thyme and bay leaf and simmer until tender.
  4. Stir in the miso, scatter with parsley and serve with the crusty loaf.

Hoth snow conesVegetarian

Shaved ice with homemade raspberry syrup tinted blue

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen raspberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 drop blue food colouring
  • 1 bag ice, shaved or crushed
  1. Simmer the raspberries with the sugar and water, then strain.
  2. Cool the syrup and add a drop of blue colouring.
  3. Pack ice into four cups and pour the syrup over.

Carbonite ice creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream frozen in a block, coated in dark chocolate and sliced

Prep 15 min, cook 780 min · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream, softened
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 3 tbsp coconut oil
  1. Line a loaf tin with baking paper and press in the softened ice cream.
  2. Freeze hard overnight.
  3. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil and cool slightly.
  4. Turn out the slab, pour the chocolate over and let it set.
  5. Slice thick.

Drinks

Cloud City peach fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Peach nectar and orange juice topped with sparkling water, no alcohol

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the peach nectar and orange juice up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml peach nectar
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 400ml sparkling water
  • 1 peach
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Fill four glasses with ice.
  2. Add peach nectar and orange juice to each.
  3. Top with sparkling water and garnish with a peach slice on the rim.
