Dagobah swamp dipVegetarian

Baked spinach, artichoke and cream cheese dip with corn chips

Prep 18 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g spinach
  • 280g marinated artichokes, chopped (1 jar)
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 bag corn chips
  1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Mix with the artichokes, cream cheese, sour cream and parmesan.
  3. Spread in a dish and top with mozzarella.
  4. Bake at 190°C until bubbling and golden. Serve with corn chips.

Tauntaun beef and stout piesOn the printed menu

Chuck braised in dark ale with mushrooms in flaky pastry, for a freezing night on patrol

How it is made
  • 600g beef chuck
  • 1 onion
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 330ml stout
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 4 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 jar horseradish
  • Dijon mustard
  1. Flaky hand pies packed with chuck braised in stout with mushrooms and thyme, baked golden and served with a sharp horseradish and mustard dip. Brown 600g diced chuck in batches, add 1 diced onion and 200g sliced mushrooms, then 1 tbsp flour, 330ml stout, 200ml beef stock and thyme. Cover and simmer 2 hours until thick and tender, then cool. Cut 8 rounds from 4 sheets shortcrust for bases and 8 lids from 2 sheets puff. Fill, seal, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes. Mix 100g sour cream with 2 tsp horseradish and 1 tsp Dijon.

Yoda's rootleaf stewVegetarian

Parsnip, carrot, swede, celeriac and potato stew with miso, crusty bread

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 parsnips
  • 2 carrots
  • 1/2 swede
  • 1/2 celeriac
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 1/4l vegetable stock
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp miso paste
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Cut the parsnips, carrots, swede, celeriac and potatoes into chunks.
  2. Soften the onion in the oil.
  3. Add the vegetables, stock, thyme and bay leaf and simmer until tender.
  4. Stir in the miso, scatter with parsley and serve with the crusty loaf.

Cloud City beef WellingtonOn the printed menu

Beef fillet in mushroom and puff pastry, baked golden. Vader waits at the end of the table.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • Dijon mustard
  • 400g mushrooms
  • 2 shallots
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 8 slices prosciutto
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  1. Seared eye fillet wrapped in mushroom duxelles, prosciutto and golden puff pastry, carved pink with red wine jus and buttery mash. Season a 1kg eye fillet, sear all over and brush with Dijon. Cook 400g finely chopped mushrooms with 2 shallots and thyme until dry. Lay 8 slices prosciutto on cling film, spread with the mushrooms, roll the beef up tight and chill 20 minutes. Wrap in 2 sheets puff pastry, brush with egg and chill 20 minutes. Bake at 220°C for 25 minutes until 52°C inside and rest 10 minutes. Reduce 200ml red wine with 300ml beef stock for jus. Serve with mash.

Hoth snow conesVegetarian

Shaved ice with homemade raspberry syrup tinted blue

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen raspberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 drop blue food colouring
  • 1 bag ice, shaved or crushed
  1. Simmer the raspberries with the sugar and water, then strain.
  2. Cool the syrup and add a drop of blue colouring.
  3. Pack ice into four cups and pour the syrup over.

Carbonite ice creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream frozen in a block, coated in dark chocolate and sliced

Prep 12 min, cook 14 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream, softened
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 3 tbsp coconut oil
  1. Line a loaf tin with baking paper and press in the softened ice cream.
  2. Freeze hard overnight.
  3. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil and cool slightly.
  4. Turn out the slab, pour the chocolate over and let it set.
  5. Slice thick.

Peach nectar and orange juice topped with sparkling water, no alcohol

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the peach nectar and orange juice up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml peach nectar
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 400ml sparkling water
  • 1 peach
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Fill four glasses with ice.
  2. Add peach nectar and orange juice to each.
  3. Top with sparkling water and garnish with a peach slice on the rim.
