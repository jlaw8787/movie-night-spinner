Duckie's dance-off popcornVegetarian

Popcorn in pink white chocolate and raspberry, for Duckie's Try a Little Tenderness lip-sync

Prep 9 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 50g popcorn kernels
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 80g white chocolate
  • 10g freeze-dried raspberries
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot, shaking regularly.
  2. Melt the white chocolate gently.
  3. Crush the raspberries to a powder and stir into the chocolate to tint it pink.
  4. Toss through the popcorn and spread on a lined tray.
  5. Leave to set.

Steff's party salmon maki

Salmon and cucumber sushi rolls with wasabi, from Steff's house party where the richies needle Andie

Prep 25 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cook and season the rice up to 3 hours ahead and keep it covered at room temperature; roll and slice close to serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sushi rice
  • 80ml rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 nori sheets
  • 300g sashimi-grade salmon
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 3 tbsp Japanese mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp sriracha
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 1 tube wasabi
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook with 480ml water, covered, on low for 12 minutes. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. Stir the vinegar with the sugar and 1 tsp salt until dissolved, fold through the rice and cool.
  3. Cut the salmon and cucumber into long strips. Mix the mayonnaise with the sriracha.
  4. Spread a quarter of the rice over each nori sheet, leaving a strip bare at the far edge. Lay salmon, cucumber and a line of spicy mayo along the middle and roll up tight with a mat.
  5. Slice each roll into 8 with a wet knife. Serve with soy, wasabi and ginger.

Andie's dad's pot roast

Beef chuck braised three hours with carrots and potatoes. Jack buys her a dress; Andie cuts it up.

Prep 5 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 900g beef chuck roast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 brown onions, quartered
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 600g potatoes, peeled and chunked
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Season the beef and brown all sides in the neutral oil in a heavy casserole.
  2. Add the onions, carrots, garlic and stock, then cover and braise.
  3. Add the potatoes, cover and cook until the beef and potatoes are tender.
  4. Rest the beef, slice or pull apart and serve with the vegetables and stock.

Toasted triple-decker with roast turkey, crisp bacon, lettuce, tomato and lemon mayo, thin chips

How it is made
  • 800g frozen shoestring fries
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 4 tbsp mayo
  • 1 lemon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 400g sliced roast turkey
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 avocado
  • 2 tomatoes
  1. Toasted triple-decker of roast turkey, crisp bacon, avocado, lettuce, tomato and lemon mayo, pinned and cut into triangles with shoestring fries. Iona's Trax counter pink candied almondsVegetarian

Almonds in a crackly raspberry sugar coat with sea salt, tumbled with salty pretzels

Prep 5 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 50ml water
  • 250g whole almonds
  • 2 tbsp freeze-dried raspberry powder
  • pinch of salt
  • 100g salted pretzels
  1. Heat the sugar and water until dissolved, then add the almonds.
  2. Stir over medium heat until the sugar crystallises and coats them, then keep stirring until some melts glossy again.
  3. Off the heat, toss with the raspberry powder and salt.
  4. Spread on baking paper to cool.
  5. Mix with the pretzels.

Two-dress prom cakeVegetarian

Half vanilla, half strawberry sponge stitched with buttercream, pink and white like her prom dress

Prep 28 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g strawberries, chopped
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 140g self-raising flour
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 160g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp milk
  • 1 drop pink food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line two 12cm round tins. Cook the strawberries over low heat until reduced to a thick puree and cool.
  2. Beat part of the butter with the caster sugar until pale, beat in the eggs and fold in the flour.
  3. Divide the batter. Stir vanilla into one half and the strawberry puree into the other.
  4. Bake side by side until a skewer comes out clean, then cool.
  5. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and milk, and tint half pink.
  6. Cut both cakes in half and join one vanilla and one strawberry half with buttercream. Cover half pink and half white and pipe a seam. Cut into four portions.

Records and Tapes pink lemonade punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry, pineapple and lemonade with orange slices, for the walk out of the nightclub

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the oranges and mix the cranberry and pineapple juices up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; add the lemonade, raspberries and ice at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 750ml lemonade
  • 2 oranges, sliced
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Mix the cranberry juice, pineapple juice and lemonade in a big glass bowl.
  2. Add the orange slices and raspberries so they float on top.
  3. Ladle into glasses over ice.
