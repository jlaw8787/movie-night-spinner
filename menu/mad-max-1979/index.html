Snacks

MFP roadhouse chips

Thick chips tossed in chicken salt, with gravy for dipping

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Cut the chips up to 1 day ahead and keep in cold water in the fridge. Dry well and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp chicken salt
  • 3 tbsp gravy powder
  • 375ml boiling water
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips and dry.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140C until cooked through.
  3. Fry again at 190C until golden.
  4. Toss in chicken salt.
  5. Whisk the gravy powder into the boiling water.
  6. Serve in butcher's paper with gravy for dipping.

Pub pickled onionsVegetarian

Pickling onions in sweet malt vinegar with chilli, served with cheese and crackers

Prep 25 min, cook 10080 min · Make ahead: Soak the onions in salted water overnight, then pickle in the jar for a week before eating; keeps up to 1 month in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pickling onions
  • 2 tbsp salt, for soaking
  • 500ml malt vinegar
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 200g tasty cheese
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Peel the onions and soak overnight in water with the salt dissolved in it.
  2. Pack into a jar.
  3. Boil the vinegar with the sugar, chilli and peppercorns.
  4. Pour over, seal and leave a week.
  5. Serve with sliced cheese and crackers.

Mains

Last of the V8s meat pie

Slow braised beef pie with a puff pastry lid, tomato sauce on top

Prep 45 min, cook 215 min · Make ahead: Braise, thicken and cool the filling up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled. Line the tins, fill, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  1. Brown the beef in batches.
  2. Add the stock and Worcestershire and braise.
  3. Thicken with flour and cool.
  4. Line four pie tins with shortcrust and fill.
  5. Top with puff and bake at 200C.
  6. Add sauce on top.

Sweets

Sproggo's ice creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream in waffle cones with a hard chocolate shell

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil up to 1 day ahead and keep covered; warm gently to drizzle. Scoop and serve straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 4 waffle cones
  1. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil and cool slightly.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into the waffle cones.
  3. Drizzle over the chocolate so it sets hard in seconds. Eat it before it drips.

Iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled coffee and milk with vanilla ice cream and whipped cream

Prep 15 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the coffee up to 3 days ahead in a jar in the fridge. Add the milk, ice cream and whipped cream to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g ground coffee
  • 500ml water
  • 600ml full cream milk, cold
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Brew the coffee with the water.
  2. Chill the coffee.
  3. Pour over ice in tall glasses with the cold milk.
  4. Add a scoop of ice cream to each.
  5. Whip the cream and add a swirl to each.
