Snacks

Lobster canapés

Sweet meat folded with mayonnaise, lemon, celery and chives on toasted, buttered brioche. By the dozen. Shelled. None behind the refrigerator.

Prep 18 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 300g cooked lobster meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 6 slices of brioche
  • 20g butter, softened
  1. Chop the lobster and fold with mayonnaise, lemon juice, celery and chives.
  2. Spread the brioche slices with the softened butter and cut 16 rounds from them.
  3. Toast the rounds, 5 min.
  4. Pile the lobster on top.

Potato knishVegetarian

Flaky pastry around Idahos mashed with slow fried onion, egg washed and baked golden.

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash, 20 min.
  2. Fry the onions slowly in the butter, 15 min.
  3. Mix the onions into the mash with salt and pepper.
  4. Cut each pastry sheet into 4 squares, fill, and pinch closed into rounds.
  5. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C, 25 min.

Mains

Pastrami on white

Steamed until glossy, piled high on soft white bread with mayonnaise, iceberg and tomato. Yes, we make it. Max winces.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sliced pastrami
  • 8 slices of soft white bread
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 jar yellow mustard, on the side
  • 4 slices of rye bread, on the side
  1. Steam the pastrami until glossy, 10 min.
  2. Spread the white bread with mayonnaise.
  3. Add lettuce and tomato and pile the pastrami high.
  4. Put rye and mustard on the side for anyone who wants it.

Sweets

CheesecakeVegetarian

Cream cheese, sour cream and lemon zest on a buttery crumb crust, baked slow in a water bath.

Prep 20 min, cook 70 min, then 24 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 200g digestive biscuits
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 900g cream cheese
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 200g sour cream
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 22cm tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, sour cream, eggs and lemon zest.
  3. Pour over the base and bake in a water bath at 150°C, 1 hr 10 min.
  4. Cool in the oven, 1 hr.
  5. Chill overnight.

Drinks

Garden rickeyNo alcohol

Cucumber, lime, mint and honey blended and strained, poured over ice and topped with seltzer.

Prep 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 handful mint, plus 4 sprigs
  • 600ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend one chopped cucumber with the lime juice, honey and mint until smooth, then strain.
  2. Cut the other cucumber into ribbons and fill glasses with ice and ribbons.
  3. Pour some of the mix into each glass and top with sparkling water.
  4. Add a mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the lobsters escape behind the fridge.
