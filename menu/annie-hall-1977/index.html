Snacks

Lobster bites

Chopped lobster in lemony mayo with celery and chives on toasted buttered brioche rounds

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g cooked lobster meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 6 slices brioche
  • 20g butter, softened
  1. Chop the lobster and fold with mayonnaise, lemon juice, celery and chives.
  2. Spread the brioche slices with the softened butter and cut 16 rounds from them.
  3. Toast the rounds.
  4. Pile the lobster on top.

Potato knishesVegetarian

Flaky rounds of mashed potato and slow fried onion, baked golden, for Alvy's Brooklyn childhood

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat at 180C
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash.
  2. Fry the onions slowly in the butter.
  3. Mix the onions into the mash with salt and pepper.
  4. Cut each pastry sheet into 4 squares, fill, and pinch closed into rounds.
  5. Brush with egg and bake at 200C.

Mains

Pastrami on white with mayo

Steamed glossy slices piled on soft bread with mayo, lettuce and tomato, no rye or mustard

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Steam the pastrami up to 2 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge; rewarm it and build the sandwiches to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sliced pastrami
  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 jar yellow mustard, on the side
  • 4 slices rye bread, on the side
  1. Steam the pastrami until glossy.
  2. Spread the white bread with mayonnaise.
  3. Add lettuce and tomato and pile the pastrami high.
  4. Put rye and mustard on the side for anyone who wants it.

Sweets

New York cheesecakeVegetarian

Dense baked cream cheese, sour cream and lemon on biscuit, Alvy's city in a slice

Prep 20 min, cook 70 min, then 24 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 200g digestive biscuits
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 900g cream cheese
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 200g sour cream
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 22cm tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, sour cream, eggs and lemon zest.
  3. Pour over the base and bake in a water bath at 150C.
  4. Cool in the oven.
  5. Chill overnight.

Drinks

Alvy's LA cucumber mint coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Alvy's health food lunch in LA, alfalfa sprouts and all

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 handful mint, plus 4 sprigs
  • 600ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blend one chopped cucumber with the lime juice, honey and mint until smooth, then strain.
  2. Cut the other cucumber into ribbons and fill glasses with ice and ribbons.
  3. Pour some of the mix into each glass and top with sparkling water.
  4. Add a mint sprig.

When to serve
