Snacks

Turin bagna cauda

Garlic and anchovy dip, raw veg, bread. Great Aunt Nellie taught us loyalty.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the anchovy sauce up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and rewarm over low heat; cut the vegetables and bread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 150ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 8 garlic cloves
  • 1 tin anchovies
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Slice the fennel, capsicum and radishes and the bread.
  2. Melt the butter in the oil over very low heat with crushed garlic and the anchovies until the anchovies dissolve.
  3. Keep warm over a candle and dip the vegetables and bread.

Charlie Croker's cheese toastsVegetarian

Toast bars topped with cheddar, parmesan and English mustard, grilled until bubbling.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the cheese topping up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; spread and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1 egg
  • 6 slices white bread
  1. Mix the grated cheddar and parmesan with the mustard and egg.
  2. Cut the bread into neat bars and toast one side.
  3. Spread the cheese mix on the other side.
  4. Grill until golden and bubbling.

Mr Bridger's cucumber and egg tea fingers

Minted cream cheese with cucumber, curry mayo with cress, for the funeral briefing.

How it is made
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar curry powder
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 1 telegraph cucumber
  • 1 punnet cress
  1. Crustless fingers of buttered white bread with cucumber and mint cream cheese, and curried egg and cress, stacked on a plate like a proper English tea. Boil 4 eggs for 10 minutes, peel and mash with 3 tbsp mayo, a pinch of curry powder and salt. Beat 150g cream cheese with chopped mint. Butter 12 slices white bread. Spread 3 with cream cheese and top with thin cucumber slices, spread 3 with egg and cress, then close all six. Cut off the crusts and cut each into 3 fingers.

Mains

Pork with creamy tuna sauce

Cold pork under tuna and caper mayo. Everybody in the world is bent, says Bridger.

Prep 23 min, cook 45 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork loin
  • 185g tinned tuna
  • 3 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  1. Poach the pork gently in salted water until 63°C inside.
  2. Chill the pork.
  3. Blitz the tuna, anchovies, capers, mayonnaise and lemon juice until smooth.
  4. Slice the pork thin and cover with the sauce and more capers.

Turin beef agnolotti with brown butter

Beef-filled agnolotti tossed in brown butter and sage. Mr Bridger hopes you like spaghetti.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 80g parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 400g fresh lasagne sheets
  • 100g butter
  • 1 bunch sage
  1. Little pinched pasta pockets of braised beef and parmesan, tossed in nutty brown butter with crispy sage and more parmesan. Mix 300g shredded braised beef or cooked beef mince with 40g parmesan, 1 egg and nutmeg. Roll 400g fresh lasagne sheets thinner and cut into 6cm wide strips. Dot teaspoons of filling along each strip, fold over and pinch between each mound, then cut into pockets. Boil for 3 minutes. Melt 100g butter until nut brown with 12 sage leaves, toss the pasta through and finish with parmesan.

Sweets

Bonet chocolate amaretti puddingVegetarian

Cold baked custard with cocoa, amaretti and dark rum under a caramel top.

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 80g amaretti biscuits
  • 30ml dark rum
  1. Cook part of the sugar to a dark caramel and pour into a loaf tin.
  2. Whisk the eggs, remaining sugar, milk, cocoa, crushed amaretti and rum.
  3. Pour over and bake in a water bath at 160°C.
  4. Chill and turn out.

Drinks

Cliffhanger hot chocolate and espressoNo alcohol

Rich hot chocolate with a shot of espresso stirred in.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the hot chocolate up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and reheat; brew the espressos and whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml milk
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 espressos
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the milk and whisk in the chopped chocolate and sugar until thick and smooth.
  2. Brew the espressos. Lightly whip the cream.
  3. Pour the hot chocolate into four small heatproof glasses.
  4. Gently pour an espresso over the back of a spoon on top, then float the cream. Serve without stirring.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the three little cars start the getaway through Turin.
