Snacks

Turin bagna cauda

Great Aunt Nellie taught us loyalty. Garlic and anchovy dip, raw veg, bread.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the anchovy sauce up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and rewarm over low heat; cut the vegetables and bread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 150ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 8 garlic cloves
  • 1 tin anchovies
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Slice the fennel, capsicum and radishes and the bread.
  2. Melt the butter in the oil over very low heat with crushed garlic and the anchovies until the anchovies dissolve.
  3. Keep warm over a candle and dip the vegetables and bread.

Charlie Croker's cheese toastsVegetarian

Toast bars topped with cheddar, parmesan and English mustard, grilled until bubbling

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the cheese topping up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; spread and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1 egg
  • 6 slices white bread
  1. Mix the grated cheddar and parmesan with the mustard and egg.
  2. Cut the bread into neat bars and toast one side.
  3. Spread the cheese mix on the other side.
  4. Grill until golden and bubbling.

Mains

Pork with creamy tuna sauce

Everybody in the world is bent, says Bridger. Cold pork under tuna and caper mayo.

Prep 25 min, cook 255 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork loin
  • 185g tinned tuna
  • 3 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  1. Poach the pork gently in salted water until 63C inside.
  2. Chill the pork.
  3. Blitz the tuna, anchovies, capers, mayonnaise and lemon juice until smooth.
  4. Slice the pork thin and cover with the sauce and more capers.

Sweets

Bonet chocolate amaretti puddingVegetarian

Cold baked custard with cocoa, amaretti and dark rum under a caramel top

Prep 25 min, cook 285 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 80g amaretti biscuits
  • 30ml dark rum
  1. Cook part of the sugar to a dark caramel and pour into a loaf tin.
  2. Whisk the eggs, remaining sugar, milk, cocoa, crushed amaretti and rum.
  3. Pour over and bake in a water bath at 160C.
  4. Chill and turn out.

Drinks

Cliffhanger hot chocolate and espressoVegetarianNo alcohol

Hang on a minute, lads, I've got a great idea. Hot chocolate and espresso.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the hot chocolate up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and reheat; brew the espressos and whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml milk
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 espressos
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the milk and whisk in the chopped chocolate and sugar until thick and smooth.
  2. Brew the espressos. Lightly whip the cream.
  3. Pour the hot chocolate into four small heatproof glasses.
  4. Gently pour an espresso over the back of a spoon on top, then float the cream. Serve without stirring.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the three little cars start the getaway through Turin.
