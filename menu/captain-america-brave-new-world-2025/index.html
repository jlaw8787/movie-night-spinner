Cherry blossom crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette with whipped ricotta and honey thyme cherries.

Prep 3 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Cook the cherries up to 3 days ahead and keep them chilled; toast the bread and whip the ricotta on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 250g ricotta
  • 300g frozen cherries
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  1. Slice the baguette and toast until golden.
  2. Simmer the cherries with honey and thyme until jammy, then cool.
  3. Whip the ricotta until smooth.
  4. Spread the toasts with ricotta and spoon the cherries on top.

Falcon wings

Chicken wings baked crisp with paprika and honey barbecue glaze.

Prep 7 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings in the paprika and salt up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; bake and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 125ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 30g butter
  • salt, to taste
  1. Toss the wings with paprika and salt.
  2. Bake at 220°C until crisp.
  3. Warm the barbecue sauce, honey and butter until glossy.
  4. Toss the wings through the glaze and serve with napkins.

Louisiana crab cakes with remoulade

Crab, mayo, mustard and panko patties, pan-fried golden, lemony dip, for the new Captain America.

How it is made
  • 400g crab meat
  • 250g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 egg
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 120g panko crumbs
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 jar gherkins
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Crab meat bound with mayo, mustard, spring onion and breadcrumbs, shaped and pan-fried until golden, with a lemon remoulade. Mix 400g crab meat with 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 beaten egg, 2 sliced spring onions, a pinch of cayenne and 50g panko crumbs. Shape into 8 cakes and chill 30 minutes. Coat in more panko and pan-fry in oil and butter for 3 minutes a side until golden. For remoulade, mix 120g mayo with 1 tbsp chopped capers, 1 tbsp chopped gherkin, 1 tsp paprika, lemon juice and parsley. Serve with lemon wedges.

Sam's Louisiana gumbo

Dark roux gumbo with chicken, smoked sausage, capsicum and celery.

Prep 23 min, cook 98 min · Make ahead: Make the gumbo up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; reheat gently and cook the rice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g plain flour
  • 60ml vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 2 celery stalks
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 300g smoked sausage
  • 200g okra
  • 1.5 L water
  • 300g long grain rice
  1. Dice the onion, capsicum and celery and slice the sausage and chicken.
  2. Cook the flour in the oil, stirring, until dark brown.
  3. Add the onion, capsicum and celery and soften them.
  4. Add the chicken, sausage, okra and water and simmer.
  5. Cook the rice and serve the gumbo over it.

Red Hulk eye fillet with red wine jus

Eye fillet roasted medium-rare with red wine jus. Since when were they red?

How it is made
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 200ml red wine
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 40g butter
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  1. Beef eye fillet roasted to medium-rare with a red wine sauce, potato gratin and asparagus. Season a 1kg beef eye fillet, sear all over in a hot pan and roast at 200°C for 20 to 25 minutes for medium-rare. Rest 10 minutes. Slice 1kg potatoes thinly, layer in a buttered dish with 300ml cream, garlic and salt, and bake at 180°C for 60 minutes before the beef goes in. Deglaze the beef pan with 200ml red wine, add 200ml beef stock and reduce by half, then whisk in 20g butter. Serve with steamed asparagus.

Cherry blossom pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue with cream, cherries and rose petals.

Prep 19 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Bake the meringue and cool it in the oven up to 1 day ahead, stored airtight; cook the cherries, whip the cream and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar, for the meringue
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 500g frozen cherries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the cherries
  • 1 packet edible rose petals
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the meringue sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Shape into a round on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120°C, then cool in the oven.
  4. Cook the cherries briefly with the cherry sugar and whip the cream.
  5. Top the pavlova with cream, cherries and rose petals.

Ponchatoula strawberry lemon fizzNo alcohol

Strawberry puree and lemon juice over ice, topped with bubbles.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Blitz and sieve the strawberry puree up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; add the ice and soda water just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g strawberries
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 lemons
  • 800ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Hull the strawberries, keeping four for garnish. Juice one lemon and peel strips from the other.
  2. Blitz the rest of the strawberries with sugar and lemon juice, then sieve.
  3. Fill four glasses with ice and share the puree between them.
  4. Top with soda water, stir gently, and finish with a halved strawberry and lemon peel.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Sam reminds Red Hulk Ross of the cherry blossoms.
