Snacks

Hummell's gingerbread menVegetarian

Spiced golden syrup gingerbread cookies, decorated with icing

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 120g golden syrup
  • 375g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 3 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 egg
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line trays. Melt the butter, sugar and golden syrup.
  2. Stir into the flour, bicarbonate of soda and ginger with the egg.
  3. Chill, roll 5mm thick and cut 20 men.
  4. Bake until just firm.
  5. Cool and pipe buttons with icing sugar mixed with lemon juice.

Parade float popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in white chocolate with red and green sprinkles and sea salt

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g white chocolate, melted
  • 2 tbsp red and green sprinkles
  • 1 pinch of flaky salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the vegetable oil in a large lidded pot and spread on a lined tray.
  2. Melt the white chocolate.
  3. Drizzle over the popcorn and toss.
  4. Scatter with sprinkles and salt before it sets.
  5. Break into clusters.

Mains

Mickey's Diner beef gem hotdish

Creamy beef and mushroom casserole under crisp potato gems and cheddar

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 500g frozen tater tots
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and mushrooms.
  2. Stir in the cream and simmer until thick.
  3. Tip into a dish and cover with tater tots and cheddar.
  4. Bake at 200C until crisp.

Sweets

Peppermint barkVegetarian

Dark and white chocolate layers with crushed candy canes

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, melted
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 1 drop peppermint extract
  • 6 candy canes, crushed
  1. Line a tray with baking paper and spread the dark chocolate thin.
  2. Set.
  3. Melt the white chocolate with the peppermint extract and spread over.
  4. Scatter with crushed candy canes and set again.
  5. Break into shards.

Turbo Time spiced eggnogVegetarianNo alcohol

Homemade custard eggnog with cinnamon, nutmeg and maple, topped with whipped cream

Prep 20 min, cook 250 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml milk
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100ml whipped cream, to serve
  1. Whisk the yolks and sugar until pale.
  2. Heat the milk and cream with the cinnamon and nutmeg until steaming.
  3. Whisk into the yolks, return to the pan and stir over low heat to 75C until it coats the spoon.
  4. Strain and stir in the maple syrup and vanilla.
  5. Chill.
  6. Serve topped with whipped cream and more nutmeg.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Howard races to name Santa's reindeer on air.
