Hummell's gingerbread menVegetarian

Spiced golden syrup gingerbread cookies, decorated with icing.

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 120g golden syrup
  • 375g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 3 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 egg
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line trays. Melt the butter, sugar and golden syrup, 5 min.
  2. Stir into the flour, bicarbonate of soda and ginger with the egg.
  3. Chill, roll 5mm thick and cut 20 men, 35 min.
  4. Bake until just firm.
  5. Cool and pipe buttons with icing sugar mixed with lemon juice, 6 min.

Parade float popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in white chocolate with red and green sprinkles and sea salt.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g white chocolate, melted
  • 2 tbsp red and green sprinkles
  • 1 pinch of flaky salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the vegetable oil in a large lidded pot and spread on a lined tray.
  2. Melt the white chocolate, 5 min.
  3. Drizzle over the popcorn and toss.
  4. Scatter with sprinkles and salt before it sets.
  5. Break into clusters.

Mickey's Diner beef and potato bite hotdish

Creamy beef and mushroom casserole under crisp crispy potato bites and cheddar.

Prep 17 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 500g frozen potato gems
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and mushrooms, 12 min.
  2. Stir in the cream and simmer until thick.
  3. Tip into a dish and cover with potato gems and cheddar.
  4. Bake at 200°C until crisp.

Langstons' maple-glazed Christmas Eve ham

Scored leg brushed with maple, mustard and orange, roasted sticky, mash, honey carrots, cranberry.

How it is made
  • 3kg cooked half leg ham
  • 1 jar whole cloves
  • 125ml maple syrup
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Leg ham scored and glazed with maple, mustard and orange, roasted sticky and dark, with buttery mash, honey carrots and a cranberry relish. Heat the oven to 180°C. Peel the rind off a 3kg cooked half leg ham, score the fat in diamonds and stud with cloves. Simmer 125ml maple syrup, 2 tbsp Dijon, the juice of an orange and 2 tbsp brown sugar for 3 minutes. Roast the ham for 50 minutes, brushing with glaze every 15 minutes. Serve with mash, carrots roasted in honey and cranberry sauce stirred with orange zest.

Peppermint barkVegetarian

Dark and white chocolate layers with crushed candy canes.

Prep 2 min, cook 14 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, melted
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 1 drop peppermint extract
  • 6 candy canes, crushed
  1. Line a tray with baking paper and spread the dark chocolate thin.
  2. Set, 20 min.
  3. Melt the white chocolate with the peppermint extract and spread over, 8 min.
  4. Scatter with crushed candy canes and set again, 30 min.
  5. Break into shards.

Ted's too-perfect iced Christmas cookies

Vanilla butter cookie stars and trees in smooth royal icing and cachous.

How it is made
  • 200g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  • 350g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 packet silver cachous
  • 1 jar Christmas sprinkles
  1. Crisp vanilla butter cookies cut into stars and trees, iced with flawless white royal icing and finished with silver cachous and sprinkles. Beat 200g soft butter with 150g caster sugar, then 1 egg and 1 tsp vanilla. Mix in 350g plain flour and a pinch of salt, chill 30 minutes, roll 5mm thick and cut stars and trees. Bake at 180°C for 9 minutes and cool. Beat 1 egg white with 250g icing sugar and lemon juice to thick icing, pipe outlines, flood the centres and decorate with cachous and sprinkles.

Turbo Time spiced eggnogNo alcohol

Homemade custard eggnog with cinnamon, nutmeg and maple, topped with whipped cream.

Prep 11 min, cook 15 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml milk
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100ml whipped cream, to serve
  1. Whisk the yolks and sugar until pale.
  2. Heat the milk and cream with the cinnamon and nutmeg until steaming.
  3. Whisk into the yolks, return to the pan and stir over low heat to 75°C until it coats the spoon.
  4. Strain and stir in the maple syrup and vanilla.
  5. Chill, 4 hr.
  6. Serve topped with whipped cream and more nutmeg.
