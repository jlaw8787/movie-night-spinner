Snacks

Outpost 31 rec room nachosVegetarian

Corn chips baked with black beans, cheddar and jalapeños

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best baked just before serving; drain the beans and grate the cheddar up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 300g corn chips
  • 400g black beans, drained
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 jalapenos, sliced
  • 150g sour cream
  • 150g salsa
  1. Spread the corn chips over a large oven tray.
  2. Scatter over the beans, cheddar and jalapenos.
  3. Bake at 200C until the cheese melts.
  4. Top with sour cream and salsa and serve straight from the tray.

Hot buttered toastVegetarian

Thick white toast spread with salted butter right to the crusts

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best toasted and buttered just before serving; leave the butter out to soften an hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 80g salted butter
  1. Toast the bread until deep golden.
  2. Spread each slice right away with butter to the crusts so it melts in.
  3. Cut into soldiers and pile on a warm plate.

Mains

Antarctic base chilli

Slow cooked beef chuck chilli with chipotle and cumin

Prep 28 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, in chunks
  • 2 brown onions, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chipotle in adobo
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 800g crushed tomatoes
  • 400g kidney beans, drained
  • 350g cornbread mix
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Cut the beef and brown in batches in oil.
  2. Soften the onions and garlic.
  3. Add the chipotle, cumin, tomatoes, beans and beef.
  4. Cover and braise at 160C.
  5. Bake the cornbread as the packet says and serve alongside.

Sweets

Petri dish jelly

Wobbly set raspberry jelly

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 5 gelatine leaves
  • 425g raspberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 300ml water
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water.
  2. Blitz most of the raspberries with the sugar and water, warm until steaming and strain.
  3. Squeeze the gelatine dry and stir into the hot puree with the lemon juice.
  4. Pour into four shallow glass dishes and set in the fridge.
  5. Top with the remaining fresh raspberries.

Drinks

MacReady's Rob RoyVegetarian

Blended Scotch, sweet vermouth and bitters stirred cold

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Mix the Scotch, vermouth and bitters in a bottle up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; stir with ice and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml blended Scotch
  • 100ml sweet red vermouth
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Chill four coupe or rocks glasses.
  2. Stir the Scotch, vermouth and bitters with plenty of ice in a jug (do it in two rounds)
  3. Strain into the glasses and drop in a cherry. Serve over one big ice block for anyone who likes it on the rocks.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when MacReady runs the blood test.
