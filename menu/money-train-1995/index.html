Token booth honey roasted nutsVegetarian

A mixed handful glazed in honey, sugar and cinnamon, oven-toasted, before the Torch turns up

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g mixed nuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch of salt
  1. Toss the nuts with the honey, sugar, cinnamon and salt until coated.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170°C, stirring halfway.
  3. Keep stirring as they cool so they do not clump.

Gold token cookiesVegetarian

Round, gold cookies stamped like a subway token

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g soft butter
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 small jar edible gold dust
  1. Beat the butter and icing sugar until pale, then mix in the flour to a soft dough.
  2. Chill the dough for 15 minutes so it rolls cleanly.
  3. Roll 5mm thick, cut rounds the size of a subway token and cut a small hole from the middle of each.
  4. Bake at 160°C until just set and pale gold.
  5. Cool on the tray, then brush with gold dust mixed with a drop of water.

Mains

Arroz con pollo

Browned thighs, sofrito, annatto, olives and capers, cooked with rice, for Grace the decoy cop

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat with a splash of stock
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 capsicum, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 handful coriander, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground annatto
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 60g green olives
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 650ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp oil for browning
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in a wide lidded pan until golden, then set aside.
  2. Fry the onion, capsicum, garlic and coriander in the same pan until soft.
  3. Stir in the annatto, rice, olives, capers and stock and bring to a simmer.
  4. Sit the chicken on top, cover and cook on low until the rice is tender.
  5. Rest, covered, before serving.

Guava pastelitosVegetarian

Flaky puff pastry squares filled with guava paste and cream cheese, glazed with syrup

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, crisp in the oven before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 250g guava paste
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  1. Thaw the pastry, then cut each sheet into 8 squares.
  2. Put a slice of guava paste and a little cream cheese on half the squares, cover with the rest and seal the edges with a fork.
  3. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.
  4. Simmer the sugar with the water for a few minutes to make a syrup, then brush it over the warm pastries.

Drinks

Money Train payday michelada

Lager, lime, tomato juice and hot sauce. A train full of fare money.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the chilli salt up to 1 week ahead and keep it airtight; build the drinks and add the lager just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp flaky salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 5 limes, juiced, with some cut into wedges
  • 240ml tomato juice
  • 16 dashes hot sauce
  • 8 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 bottles cold lager, 375ml each
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the salt and chilli powder on a plate.
  2. Rub a lime wedge around each of 4 glass rims and dip in the chilli salt.
  3. Add ice a good squeeze of lime juice, the tomato juice, hot sauce and Worcestershire, dividing evenly.
  4. Top each with a cold lager, stir gently and slip the glasses into small paper bags.

When to serve

Pour drinks when Charlie sneaks aboard the money train on New Year's Eve.
