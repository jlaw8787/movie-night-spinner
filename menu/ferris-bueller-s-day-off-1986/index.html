Snacks

Gruyère puffs with chive cream cheeseVegetarian

Small choux puffs filled with chive cream cheese.

Prep 17 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Puffs up to 1 day airtight, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g Gruyère, grated
  • 120g cream cheese
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute. Cool.
  2. Beat in the eggs and Gruyère.
  3. Pipe small rounds and bake at 200°C.
  4. Whip the cream cheese with chopped chives.
  5. Split the puffs and fill.

Deep dish bites

Mini deep dish pizzas baked in a muffin tin with sausage, mozzarella and tomato.

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Press the dough into the tin and fill the cases up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp fine cornmeal
  • 150g mozzarella, diced
  • 2 Italian sausages
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  1. Knead the cornmeal into the dough and press into a 12-hole mini muffin tin.
  2. Squeeze the sausage meat out of its casings and crumble.
  3. Fill the cases with mozzarella, sausage and crushed tomato.
  4. Bake at 210°C until bubbling.

Wrigley Field soft pretzels

Knots from a bicarb bath, coarse salt and yellow mustard, for the foul ball Ferris catches.

How it is made
  • 500g plain flour
  • 1 sachet dried yeast
  • 30g butter
  • 1 packet bicarb soda
  • 1 egg
  • coarse salt
  • yellow mustard
  1. Big soft pretzels boiled in a bicarb soda bath, baked dark and glossy, scattered with coarse salt and served warm with yellow mustard. Mix 500g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt, 30g soft butter and 320ml warm water into a smooth dough and prove 1 hour. Cut into 8, roll into long ropes and twist into pretzels. Dip each in 2L simmering water with 60g bicarb soda for 30 seconds, lift onto a lined tray, brush with beaten egg and scatter with coarse salt. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes until deep brown. Serve warm with mustard.

Mains

Abe Froman's hot sausages

Grilled Italian sausages with soft peppers and onions in crusty rolls, hot mustard.

Prep 10 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Slice the capsicums and onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill the sausages and soften the vegetables just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 Italian sausages
  • 2 capsicums, sliced
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 4 tbsp hot mustard
  1. Grill the sausages slowly until browned all over.
  2. Slice the capsicums and onions.
  3. In the same pan, soften the capsicums and onions in the fat with the oregano.
  4. Pile the sausages and peppers into the rolls and add hot mustard.

Steak frites

Sirloin seared in butter and rested, thin crisp chips, shallot and parsley butter melting on top.

How it is made
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 80g butter
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 800g frozen thin fries
  1. Sirloin seared hard in butter and rested, with thin crisp fries and a shallot and parsley butter melting on top. Mix 80g soft butter with a finely chopped shallot and 2 tbsp chopped parsley, roll into a log and chill. Bake 800g frozen thin fries at 220°C for 20 minutes. Season 4 sirloin steaks of 250g and sear in a hot pan with a little oil 3 minutes a side for medium-rare, then rest 5 minutes. Serve each steak with a slice of the butter on top and a pile of fries.

Sweets

Profiteroles with chocolate sauceVegetarian

Cream filled choux puffs with warm dark chocolate sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Puffs up to 1 day airtight, fill and sauce to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 380ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour until it leaves the pan, then cool.
  2. Beat in the eggs and pipe 16 small puffs.
  3. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden, then cool.
  4. Whip most of the cream with the vanilla, split the puffs and fill.
  5. Melt the chocolate with the remaining cream and pour over.

Drinks

Abe Froman's sparkling lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice and sparkling mineral water served in wine glasses.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the lemon base up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 L sparkling mineral water, chilled
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Squeeze the lemons, keeping a few thin slices, and stir with the sugar until dissolved.
  2. Pour into a jug with the sparkling water and plenty of ice.
  3. Serve in wine glasses with a lemon slice.
