Snacks

Gruyère puffs with goat's cheeseVegetarian

Small choux puffs filled with chive whipped goat's cheese

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Puffs up to 1 day airtight, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g Gruyere, grated
  • 120g goat's cheese
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute. Cool.
  2. Beat in the eggs and Gruyere.
  3. Pipe small rounds and bake at 200C.
  4. Whip the goat's cheese with chopped chives.
  5. Split the puffs and fill.

Deep dish bites

Mini deep dish pizzas baked in a muffin tin with sausage, mozzarella and tomato

Prep 25 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Press the dough into the tin and fill the cases up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp fine cornmeal
  • 150g mozzarella, diced
  • 2 Italian sausages
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  1. Knead the cornmeal into the dough and press into a 12-hole mini muffin tin.
  2. Squeeze the sausage meat out of its casings and crumble.
  3. Fill the cases with mozzarella, sausage and crushed tomato.
  4. Bake at 210C until bubbling.

Mains

Abe Froman's hot sausages

Grilled Italian sausages with soft peppers and onions in crusty rolls, hot mustard

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Slice the capsicums and onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill the sausages and soften the vegetables just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 Italian sausages
  • 2 capsicums, sliced
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 4 tbsp hot mustard
  1. Grill the sausages slowly until browned all over.
  2. Slice the capsicums and onions.
  3. In the same pan, soften the capsicums and onions in the fat with the oregano.
  4. Pile the sausages and peppers into the rolls and add hot mustard.

Sweets

Chez Quis profiterolesVegetarian

Cream filled choux puffs with warm dark chocolate sauce

Prep 40 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Puffs up to 1 day airtight, fill and sauce to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 380ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour until it leaves the pan, then cool.
  2. Beat in the eggs and pipe 16 small puffs.
  3. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden, then cool.
  4. Whip most of the cream with the vanilla, split the puffs and fill.
  5. Melt the chocolate with the remaining cream and pour over.

Abe Froman's sparkling lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice and sparkling mineral water served in wine glasses, no alcohol

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the lemon base up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1l sparkling mineral water, chilled
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Squeeze the lemons, keeping a few thin slices, and stir with the sugar until dissolved.
  2. Pour into a jug with the sparkling water and plenty of ice.
  3. Serve in wine glasses with a lemon slice.
