Susan Johnson engagement canapés

Rye squares with smoked salmon, lemon crème fraîche and dill

Prep 12 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 slices rye bread
  • 30g butter, softened
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Butter the rye slices and cut each into small squares.
  2. Whip the crème fraîche with the lemon zest until it holds a soft peak.
  3. Top each square with smoked salmon, a small dot of crème fraîche, a dill sprig and cracked pepper.

Caviar blini

Warm buckwheat blini, crème fraîche, salmon roe and snipped chives

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Snip the chives up to 4 hours ahead; warm the blini and top them just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 buckwheat blini
  • 150ml crème fraîche
  • 1 small jar salmon roe
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Warm the blini on a tray in a 180°C oven.
  2. Top each with a teaspoon of crème fraîche, a little roe and snipped chives.
  3. Serve straight away on a silver tray if you have one.

Hobson's cucumber and cress sandwichesOn the printed menu

Crustless white with dill cream cheese and paper-thin cucumber, peppery leaves, cut in neat fingers

How it is made
  • 1 telegraph cucumber
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 1 punnet cress
  1. Crustless white bread with herbed cream cheese, paper-thin cucumber and peppery cress, cut into neat fingers. Peel and very thinly slice 1 telegraph cucumber, salt lightly for 10 minutes and pat dry. Beat 150g cream cheese with 2 tbsp chopped dill, lemon zest and pepper. Spread over 12 slices white bread. Top 6 with cucumber and a punnet of cress, close with the rest and press gently. Cut off the crusts and slice into 4 fingers each.

Hobson's Sunday roast beef

Topside roasted rare to medium rare, with gravy from the pan juices

Prep 5 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Make the pudding batter up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast the beef and bake the puddings on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef topside
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, for the tins
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub the beef with oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk into a smooth batter and leave to rest.
  3. Roast the beef until the centre reads 52°C on a probe.
  4. Rest the beef under foil. Raise the oven to 220°C, heat the oiled tins until smoking, pour in the batter and bake the puddings until risen and golden.
  5. Make gravy by simmering the stock with the pan juices until slightly thickened. Carve and serve.

Linda's diner ReubenOn the printed menu

Corned beef, Swiss and sauerkraut on rye with Russian dressing, fried crisp in butter

How it is made
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 400g sliced corned beef
  • 200g sauerkraut
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 1 gherkin
  • 40g butter
  1. Corned beef, Swiss and sauerkraut on rye with Russian dressing, fried in butter until crisp and melting. Mix 80g mayonnaise, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tsp horseradish and 1 tbsp chopped gherkin for the dressing. Butter 8 slices rye on one side. On the unbuttered side spread dressing, then layer 400g sliced corned beef, 200g drained sauerkraut and 8 slices Swiss. Close and fry over medium heat for 4 minutes a side, pressing down, until crisp and melted.

Crème brûléeVegetarian

Vanilla bean custard baked in ramekins under a crackly sugar crust

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min, then 6 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled before torching
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar, for the custard
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar, for the top
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C. Warm the cream with the split vanilla bean until steaming.
  2. Whisk the yolks and sugar, then pour the cream over, whisking gently. Strain into ramekins.
  3. Stand the ramekins in a tray of hot water and bake until just set with a wobble.
  4. Cool, then chill overnight. Chilling counts toward the cooking time.
  5. Sprinkle with sugar and torch to amber.

Kir royaleVegetarian

Champagne poured over a splash of crème de cassis

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the champagne in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; freeze the flutes 10 minutes before pouring
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml creme de cassis
  • 480ml brut champagne, chilled
  • 4 raspberries
  1. Chill the flutes in the freezer.
  2. Pour the cassis into each glass, tilt and top slowly with champagne so it does not foam over.
  3. Drop a raspberry into each glass and serve straight away.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Hobson says something devastatingly dry.
