Budapest langosVegetarian

Fried puffed dough with garlic butter, sour cream and grated cheddar.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough can prove up to 1 day in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and warm water to a soft dough.
  2. Prove until doubled.
  3. Stretch into rounds and fry in hot oil until puffed and golden.
  4. Brush with garlic butter, spread with sour cream and top with cheddar.

Norway hideout fish

Rye crispbreads with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, cucumber and dill.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best assembled just before serving so the crispbreads stay crisp; slice the cucumber up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 12 rye crispbreads
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 12 dill sprigs
  • 1 lemon
  1. Spread the crispbreads with crème fraîche.
  2. Top with folds of smoked salmon, cucumber and a sprig of dill.
  3. Squeeze over a little lemon just before serving.

Yelena's honey chilli nut mixOn the printed menu

Almonds and cashews roasted glassy with sea salt, apricot and dark choc chunks, in paper bags.

How it is made
  • 200g almonds
  • 200g cashews
  • 1 jar honey
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 packet sea salt flakes
  • 100g dried apricots
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Almonds and cashews roasted in honey, chilli and sea salt until glassy, tossed with dried apricots and dark chocolate chunks, in little paper bags. Toss 200g almonds and 200g cashews with 3 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp melted butter, 1/2 tsp chilli flakes and 1 tsp sea salt. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170°C for 15 minutes, stirring halfway, until glossy and golden. Cool completely so they crisp up, then break apart. Mix with 100g chopped dried apricots and 100g dark chocolate chunks and portion into little paper bags.

Melina's pork chops (not Alexei)

Seared pork chops roasted with apples and onions, mashed potatoes, braised cabbage.

Prep 0 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Mash the potatoes and braise the cabbage up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and reheat; sear the chops and roast with the apples on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork chops
  • 2 green apples, sliced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 1/2 small cabbage, shredded
  • 100ml water
  1. Sear the pork chops until golden.
  2. Add the apples and onions to the pan and roast at 200°C.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter.
  4. Braise the cabbage in a little butter and water until soft.
  5. Serve it all on big plates.

Red Guardian's beef borschtOn the printed menu

Chuck, beetroot, cabbage, carrot and potato in a crimson broth with dill, sour cream, dark rye.

How it is made
  • 600g beef chuck
  • 3 beetroots
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 loaf rye bread
  1. Beef, beetroot, cabbage, carrot and potato simmered in a deep red broth with dill, finished with sour cream and dark rye bread. Brown 600g diced beef chuck in oil, cover with 2L water and simmer 1 hour. Add 3 grated beetroots, 2 grated carrots, 1 diced onion and 2 diced potatoes and cook 20 minutes. Add a quarter of a shredded cabbage, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 2 tbsp red wine vinegar and simmer 15 minutes more. Season well, stir through chopped dill and serve with sour cream and rye bread.

Ohio childhood cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes piped with pastel buttercream and sprinkles.

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Cakes up to 1 day, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g butter for buttercream, softened
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp pastel food colouring
  • 3 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Spoon into patty pans and bake at 175°C, then cool completely.
  3. Beat the buttercream with icing sugar and colouring and pipe on top.
  4. Cover with sprinkles.

Red Guardian's black tea with cherry jamVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong black tea stirred with strawberry jam, served hot in glasses with lemon.

Prep 0 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best brewed fresh; nothing to do ahead except slice the lemon up to 1 day in advance
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 4 tbsp cherry jam
  • 4 lemon slices
  1. Brew the tea in the boiling water.
  2. Pour into heatproof glasses, stir a spoon of jam into each and add a slice of lemon.
