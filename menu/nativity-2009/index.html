Snacks

Christmas fair pork and apple rolls

Pork, apple and sage sausage rolls to share with Mr Poppy.

Prep 18 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Mix the filling and roll it up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; glaze, cut and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 apple, grated
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped sage
  • 30g breadcrumbs
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 200g tomato relish
  1. Mix the sausage mince with the apple, onion, sage and breadcrumbs.
  2. Cut the pastry in half, lay a log of filling along each strip, roll up and seal underneath.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and cut into pieces.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden. Serve with relish.

Coventry cheese and onion pastiesVegetarian

Cheese and onion, packed for the flight to America.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake up to a day ahead and reheat in a warm oven
Recipe for 4
  • 3 sheets frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 300g potatoes, finely diced
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Boil the potatoes 6 minutes until just tender and drain.
  2. Mix the potatoes, onion, cheddar, mustard, salt and pepper.
  3. Cut 12cm rounds from the pastry, fill one side, fold over and crimp the edges.
  4. Brush with egg and bake 25 minutes until golden.

Coventry Christmas roast chicken dinner

Roast chicken, pigs in blankets, roast potatoes, maple sprouts, gravy.

Prep 3 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with butter and salt and parboil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast on the day and make the gravy from the pan juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 30g butter
  • 12 chipolatas
  • 12 rashers streaky bacon (8 to wrap, 4 to fry)
  • 1kg potatoes, parboiled
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400g Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 350ml chicken stock
  1. Rub the chicken with butter and salt.
  2. Roast at 200°C.
  3. Wrap the chipolatas in half rashers of bacon and add for the last part of the roasting.
  4. Roast the parboiled potatoes in oil.
  5. Fry the sprouts with diced bacon, then toss with maple syrup.
  6. Make gravy from the pan juices with the flour and stock.

Oakmoor School's posh salmon en croûte

Salmon with spinach and herbed cheese in golden puff pastry, posh Oakmoor's five-star pick.

Prep 10 min, cook 44 min · Make ahead: Wrap the salmon up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled, bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g skinless salmon fillet
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 120g baby spinach
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  • 30g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Wilt the spinach and squeeze dry. Mix the cream cheese with chopped dill and lemon zest.
  2. Lay the salmon on the pastry, spread with the cream cheese and spinach, wrap and brush with egg.
  3. Bake until golden. Boil the potatoes at the same time.
  4. Boil the beans and toss the beans and potatoes with butter. Slice the salmon to serve.

St Bernadette's staffroom mince pies

Buttery mince pies topped with pastry stars and icing sugar.

Prep 17 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead, airtight, and rewarm in a low oven; dust with icing sugar and whip the cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 jar fruit mince (400g)
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 orange, zested
  1. Cut rounds from two pastry sheets and press into a greased patty pan tin.
  2. Fill each with fruit mince. Cut stars from the third sheet and lay on top.
  3. Brush with egg and bake at 190°C until golden.
  4. Whip the cream with some of the icing sugar and the orange zest.
  5. Dust the warm pies with icing sugar and serve with the cream.

Mr Poppy's Christmas pudding sundaesVegetarian

Christmas pudding, vanilla ice cream, custard, toffee sauce and honeycomb.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead in the fridge; warm the pudding and custard and build the sundaes just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g Christmas pudding
  • 300ml thick vanilla custard
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 1 bottle toffee sauce
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 40g crushed honeycomb
  1. Warm the pudding in the microwave or steamer as the packet says, then break half of it into chunks.
  2. Warm the custard.
  3. In sundae glasses layer pudding chunks, a scoop of ice cream, custard and a drizzle of toffee sauce, then repeat.
  4. Finish with whipped cream and crushed honeycomb.

Nativity Hollywood hot blackcurrant punchNo alcohol

Blackcurrant and hot apple juice, with cinnamon, clove and ginger.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Combine everything in the saucepan up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; heat gently just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cloudy apple juice
  • 400ml water
  • 150ml blackcurrant cordial
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 1 orange, sliced
  1. Pour the apple juice and water into a saucepan with the cordial, cinnamon, cloves, ginger and orange.
  2. Heat gently without boiling.
  3. Strain into mugs and add an orange slice to each.

When to serve

Serve the sweets when Mr Poppy calls the helicopter Hollywood.
