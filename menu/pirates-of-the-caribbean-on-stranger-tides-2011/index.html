Snacks

Whitecap Bay prawns

Green prawns poached in lemon water, chilled, served on crushed ice.

Prep 11 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Poach, chill and peel the prawns up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; mix the sauce up to 2 days ahead and serve on ice
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 2 lemons
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Slice the lemons. Bring salted water with lemon slices to the boil and fill a bowl with iced water, 8 min.
  2. Poach the prawns, then plunge into the iced water, 4 min.
  3. Peel the prawns.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and hot sauce and serve the prawns on crushed ice.

Spanish expedition olivesVegetarian

Green and kalamata olives marinated in orange peel, garlic and chilli.

Prep 11 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g green olives
  • 150g kalamata olives
  • 1 orange
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g manchego
  1. Peel strips of orange zest and slice the garlic.
  2. Toss the olives with zest, garlic, chilli and oil.
  3. Leave to marinate, 1 hr.
  4. Serve with thin wedges of manchego.

Mains

Queen Anne's Revenge paella

Chicken thighs, chorizo, sweet pepper and saffron rice, simmered without stirring.

Prep 18 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Slice the chorizo, onion and capsicum up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the paella just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken thighs
  • 1 chorizo
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 300g paella rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 900ml chicken stock
  • 300g green prawns
  • 500g mussels
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Slice the chorizo, onion and capsicum and scrub the mussels.
  2. Brown the chicken and chorizo in the olive oil in a wide pan, then add the onion and capsicum, 12 min.
  3. Add the rice, saffron, paprika and stock and simmer without stirring, 15 min.
  4. Add the prawns and mussels and cover until the mussels open.

Jungle island pulled jerk pork

Shoulder rubbed with allspice, chilli and lime, slow cooked four hours, shredded with rice and beans.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder
  • 4 spring onions
  • 2 red chillies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground allspice
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 2 limes
  • 1.5 cups long grain rice
  • 400g tin kidney beans
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  1. Pork shoulder rubbed with jerk spices, slow cooked at 150°C for 4 hours, pulled with rice and beans. Blitz 4 spring onions, 2 scotch bonnet or red chillies, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp allspice, 1 tbsp thyme leaves, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 3 tbsp soy and the juice of 2 limes into a paste. Rub over a 1.5kg pork shoulder and marinate overnight. Roast covered at 150°C for 4 hours until it pulls apart, then shred with the juices. Simmer 1.5 cups rice with a tin of drained kidney beans, 400ml coconut milk and water for 18 minutes and serve together.

Sweets

Custard cream puffsVegetarian

Choux puffs filled with custard folded with whipped cream.

Prep 11 min, cook 40 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake and cool the puffs up to 1 day ahead, airtight; whip the cream, fill and dust on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 250ml water
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 250ml custard
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Boil the water and butter, beat in the flour, cool slightly, then beat in the eggs, 15 min.
  2. Pipe small mounds onto lined trays and bake, 25 min.
  3. Cool completely, 20 min.
  4. Whip the cream and fold into the custard.
  5. Fill each puff and dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Fountain of Youth rum punch

White rum, coconut water, pineapple and lime over crushed ice.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes and cut the pineapple up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; mix the punch in a jug up to 4 hours ahead and add ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml white rum
  • 550ml coconut water
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 pineapple
  1. Juice the limes and cut the pineapple into wedges.
  2. Mix the rum, coconut water, pineapple juice, lime juice and syrup in a jug.
  3. Fill four glasses with crushed ice and pour in the punch.
  4. Grate nutmeg over the top and add a pineapple wedge.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Jack helps himself to the cream puffs at King George's palace.
