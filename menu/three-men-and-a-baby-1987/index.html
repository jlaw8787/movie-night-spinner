Snacks

Shrimp Toast

Ginger and scallion on white bread, sesame, fried. Chilli dip.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 300g peeled green prawns
  • 1 egg white
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 spring onion, chopped
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 6 slices of white bread
  • 4 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • plum sauce, to serve
  1. Blitz the prawns with the egg white, ginger, spring onion and salt.
  2. Spread on the bread and press in the sesame seeds.
  3. Cut into triangles.
  4. Fry in oil at 180°C until golden, then drain, 10 min.
  5. Serve with plum sauce.

Chips & SchmearVegetarian

Day-old bagels, baked crisp. Chive and lemon cream cheese.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min
Recipe for 4
  • 3 day-old bagels
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Slice the bagels into thin rounds and brush with oil.
  2. Bake at 180°C until crisp, 12 min.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with chives and lemon zest.
  4. Serve the chips around the schmear.

Steak Sandwich

Sirloin, onions, Swiss, horseradish mayo on sourdough.

Prep 2 min, cook 50 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 600g sirloin steak
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 4 slices of Swiss cheese
  1. Cook the onions in butter over low heat until soft and golden, 30 min.
  2. Sear the steak hard, turning once, then rest and slice thin, 12 min.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with the horseradish.
  4. Toast the sourdough, spread with mayo, pile on steak and onions, top with Swiss and grill until melted, 8 min.

Vanilla Bean CustardVegetarian

Yolks, sugar and hot milk. The phone goes in the shower.

Prep 4 min, cook 16 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  1. Heat the milk with the vanilla bean until steaming, 6 min.
  2. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour in a bowl.
  3. Pour on the hot milk, then return to the pan and stir over low heat until thick, 10 min.
  4. Serve warm, or chill with plastic pressed on the surface.

Drinks

Shaken Vanilla Milk

Milk, vanilla, honey. Jack leaves disguised as a pregnant woman.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml bourbon
  • 400ml cold full cream milk
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 whole nutmeg, to grate
  1. Shake the bourbon, milk, syrup and vanilla with ice until frothy.
  2. Strain into 4 ice-filled glasses.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the guys sing the baby to sleep.
