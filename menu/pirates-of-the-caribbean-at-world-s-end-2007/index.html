Snacks

Singapore bathhouse dumplings

Steamed pork and prawn dumplings with soy, black vinegar and chilli oil.

Prep 42 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead, steam fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, plus extra for dipping
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tbsp black vinegar
  • 3 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the pork, prawns, soy and salt.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam in a lined bamboo basket, 8 min.
  4. Mix the black vinegar, chilli oil and a little extra soy sauce for dipping.

Jack's honey and smoked salt peanutsVegetarian

Roasted peanuts glazed with butter, honey and smoked salt, broken into clusters.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g peanuts
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp smoked salt
  1. Melt the butter with the honey and toss through the peanuts with the smoked salt, 5 min.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 180°C for 10 minutes, stirring once.
  3. Cool until crunchy, then break into clusters, 10 min.

Locker crab and chive brioche toasts

Sweet crab with lemon mayo on toasted brioche, like the crabs carrying the Pearl across the sand.

How it is made
  • 1 brioche loaf
  • 30g butter
  • 250g cooked crab meat
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  1. Sweet crab meat folded with lemon mayo and chives, piled on golden toasted brioche rounds with a dusting of paprika. Cut 16 rounds from 4 thick slices of brioche with a small cutter, brush with 30g melted butter and toast at 180°C for 8 minutes. Drain 250g cooked crab meat well and mix with 3 tbsp mayo, the zest and a squeeze of 1 lemon, 2 tbsp chopped chives, salt and pepper. Pile onto the cooled toasts, dust with sweet paprika and add a few chive tips.

Mains

Empress tamarind fish curry

Snapper simmered in sour tamarind, chilli and lemongrass with okra and tomato, rice to soak it up.

Prep 5 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Make the broth up to 1 day ahead; add the okra and fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 red chillies
  • 4 shallots
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 stalks of lemongrass
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp tamarind puree
  • 1 tbsp white sugar
  • 150g okra
  • 2 tomatoes, in wedges
  • 700g snapper fillets
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  2. Pound the chillies, shallots, garlic and lemongrass to a paste and fry in the oil until fragrant, 8 min.
  3. Add the tamarind, 600ml water, the sugar and 1 tsp salt and simmer, 5 min.
  4. Add the okra and tomatoes and simmer, 5 min.
  5. Add the snapper in large pieces and simmer until just cooked, then serve with the rice, 6 min.

Brethren Court Hainanese chicken rice

Poached with ginger and spring onion, grains cooked in the stock, cucumber and chilli sauce.

Prep 13 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Make the sauces up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 40g ginger
  • 5 spring onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Poach the chicken in water with half the ginger, 2 spring onions and salt at a gentle simmer, then cool in ice water, 35 min.
  2. Fry 2 crushed garlic cloves in the oil, add the rice and 600ml of the poaching stock and cook covered, 20 min.
  3. Blitz the chillies with the rest of the garlic, a little ginger and lime juice. Pour hot oil over the rest of the ginger and spring onion, finely chopped, 8 min.
  4. Slice the chicken and serve with the rice, cucumber, coriander, soy and both sauces.

Sweets

Green flash pandan puddingVegetarian

Pandan coconut pudding set green with fresh mango, for the green flash at sunset.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, 3 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp pandan paste
  • 5 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 cup shredded coconut, toasted
  • 1 mango, diced
  1. Whisk the coconut milk, sugar, pandan paste and cornflour over low heat until thick and glossy, 10 min.
  2. Pour into glasses and chill for 3 hours.
  3. Top with toasted coconut and diced mango.

Drinks

Sao Feng's Singapore sling

Gin, cherry liqueur, pineapple, lime and grenadine, shaken over ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 60ml cherry liqueur
  • 360ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 40ml grenadine
  • 4 dashes of bitters
  • 100ml soda water
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Shake the gin, cherry liqueur, pineapple juice, lime juice, grenadine and bitters hard with ice, in batches, until frothy.
  2. Strain into tall glasses filled with ice and top with a splash of soda water.
  3. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
