Snacks

Singapore bathhouse dumplings

Steamed pork and prawn dumplings with soy, black vinegar and chilli oil

Prep 40 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead, steam fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, plus extra for dipping
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tbsp black vinegar
  • 3 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the pork, prawns, soy and salt.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam in a lined bamboo basket.
  4. Mix the black vinegar, chilli oil and a little extra soy sauce for dipping.

Jack's honey and smoked salt peanutsVegetarian

Roasted peanuts glazed with butter, honey and smoked salt, broken into clusters

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g peanuts
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp smoked salt
  1. Melt the butter with the honey and toss through the peanuts with the smoked salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 180C for 10 minutes, stirring once.
  3. Cool until crunchy, then break into clusters.

Mains

Singapore chilli crab

Crab pieces in a sweet chilli tomato sauce with egg ribbons, crusty bread to mop up

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg blue swimmer crabs, cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 150ml tomato sauce
  • 3 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 250ml water
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 baguette
  1. Fry the garlic, ginger and chilli in the oil. Add the tomato sauce, sweet chilli, soy and water.
  2. Add the crab, cover and cook until the shells turn red.
  3. Stir in the beaten eggs until just set. Serve with the baguette to mop up the sauce.

Brethren Court Hainanese chicken rice

Poached with ginger and spring onion, grains cooked in the stock, cucumber and chilli sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the sauces up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 40g ginger
  • 5 spring onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Poach the chicken in water with half the ginger, 2 spring onions and salt at a gentle simmer, then cool in ice water.
  2. Fry 2 crushed garlic cloves in the oil, add the rice and 600ml of the poaching stock and cook covered.
  3. Blitz the chillies with the rest of the garlic, a little ginger and lime juice. Pour hot oil over the rest of the ginger and spring onion, finely chopped.
  4. Slice the chicken and serve with the rice, cucumber, coriander, soy and both sauces.

Sweets

Green flash pandan puddingVegetarian

Pandan coconut pudding, mango

Prep 10 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp pandan paste
  • 5 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 cup shredded coconut, toasted
  • 1 mango, diced
  1. Whisk the coconut milk, sugar, pandan paste and cornflour over low heat until thick and glossy.
  2. Pour into glasses and chill for 3 hours.
  3. Top with toasted coconut and diced mango.

Sao Feng's Singapore slingVegetarian

Gin, cherry liqueur, pineapple, lime and grenadine shaken, over ice with a splash of soda

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 60ml cherry liqueur
  • 360ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 40ml grenadine
  • 4 dashes bitters
  • 100ml soda water
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Shake the gin, cherry liqueur, pineapple juice, lime juice, grenadine and bitters hard with ice, in batches, until frothy.
  2. Strain into tall glasses filled with ice and top with a splash of soda water.
  3. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
