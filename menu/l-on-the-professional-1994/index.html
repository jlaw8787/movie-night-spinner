Snacks

Jambon-beurre baguette bites

A sandwich as plain as Léon's habits

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 80g soft cultured butter
  • 200g thinly sliced leg ham
  • 8 cornichons, sliced
  • 1 pinch cracked black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Warm the baguette at 200°C and cool slightly.
  2. Split lengthways and spread both sides generously with butter. Lay the ham inside, add cornichons and pepper.
  3. Close, press firmly and cut into 12 pieces. Serve with Dijon on the side.

Olives and cornichonsVegetarian

Olives warmed in oil with orange peel, thyme and garlic, tiny gherkins and pickled onions alongside

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 300g mixed olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 orange
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 jar cornichons
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Peel a few strips of orange peel.
  2. Warm the olives gently in the oil with the peel, thyme and garlic.
  3. Tip into a bowl and serve with cornichons and pickled onions in little dishes.

Tony's bocconcini with basil oilOn the printed menu

Torn fresh mozzarella and cherry tomatoes in bright herb oil, flaky salt, pepper and grilled bread

How it is made
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 300g bocconcini
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 ciabatta loaf
  • flaky sea salt
  • black pepper
  1. Torn bocconcini and sweet cherry tomatoes dressed in bright basil oil, flaky salt and black pepper, with grilled bread. Blitz 1 bunch basil leaves with 80ml olive oil and a pinch of salt until bright green. Tear 300g bocconcini and halve 250g cherry tomatoes. Arrange on a plate, spoon over the basil oil and finish with flaky salt and cracked pepper. Brush 8 slices ciabatta with oil, grill 2 minutes a side and serve alongside.

Mains

Tony's spaghettiVegetarian

Hand-crushed San Marzano, garlic, basil, butter and parmesan, where the jobs get handed out

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tins San Marzano tomatoes
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 30g butter
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the garlic and cook gently in the oil.
  2. Crush the tomatoes by hand, add with a good pinch of salt and simmer.
  3. Cook the spaghetti, toss through the sauce with basil and butter, and finish with parmesan.

Léon's croque monsieurOn the printed menu

A plain sandwich for a man of very simple habits

How it is made
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 200g sliced leg ham
  • 200g Gruyère
  • 30g butter
  • 30g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • Dijon mustard
  • whole nutmeg
  1. Ham and Gruyère toasted sandwich spread with Dijon, topped with béchamel and more Gruyère, baked until golden and blistered. Melt 30g butter, stir in 30g plain flour for 1 minute, then whisk in 300ml milk and simmer 3 minutes. Add nutmeg, salt and 50g grated Gruyère. Spread 8 slices thick white bread with Dijon, fill 4 with 200g ham and 100g Gruyère and close. Top with the béchamel and 50g more Gruyère. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes, then grill for 2 minutes until blistered.

Sweets

Potted plant dirt cupsVegetarian

Dark chocolate mousse layered with crushed chocolate biscuit soil, a mint sprig planted on top

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 150g chocolate biscuits, crushed
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly, then whisk in the yolks.
  2. Whip the cream and whites separately, then fold both through the chocolate.
  3. Layer in four small cups with the crushed biscuits as soil.
  4. Chill until set, then plant a mint sprig in each.

Léon's malted vanilla milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Léon drinks milk, two quarts a day

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold full cream milk
  • 750ml vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder, for the shake
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp malted milk powder, for dusting
  1. Chill 4 tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream, the 4 tbsp malted milk powder and honey until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into the frosted glasses and dust with the 2 tbsp malted milk powder for dusting.

When to serve

Pour a glass of milk every time Léon does.
