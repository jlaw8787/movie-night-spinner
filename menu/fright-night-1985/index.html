Snacks

Charley's garlic breadVegetarian

Baguette with raw garlic parsley butter, baked in foil.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the garlic butter and wrap the filled baguette in foil up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 5 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 baguette
  1. Mash the butter with the raw garlic, parsley and salt.
  2. Split the baguette, spread thickly and wrap in foil.
  3. Bake at 200°C, opening the foil for the last 3 minutes to crisp.

Jerry's applesVegetarian

Sliced apples with a peanut butter honey dip.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Whip the peanut butter dip up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; slice the apples just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 3 red apples
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 150g peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Slice the apples and toss in lemon juice.
  2. Whip the peanut butter with honey and salt until light.
  3. Fan the apples around the dip. Do not invite anyone in without checking first.

Peter Vincent's garlic parmesan popcorn

Fresh kernels in roasted garlic butter with grated hard cheese, parsley and smoked paprika.

How it is made
  • 60g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  1. Fresh popcorn tossed in roasted garlic butter, finely grated parmesan, parsley and a pinch of smoked paprika, garlic strong enough to keep the neighbour out. Melt 60g butter with 3 crushed garlic cloves over low heat for 3 minutes until fragrant, then strain. Pop 120g kernels in 2 tbsp oil in a covered pot, shaking until the popping slows. Tip into a big bowl and toss with the garlic butter, 40g finely grated parmesan, 1 tbsp chopped parsley, half a tsp smoked paprika and fine salt while still hot.

Mains

Forty-clove garlic chicken

Chicken thighs baked with 40 whole garlic cloves, thyme and white wine.

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Brown the chicken and assemble the pan up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 40 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 6 thyme sprigs
  • 200ml white wine
  • 1 Italian loaf
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the chicken skin side down in oil.
  2. Add the garlic, thyme and wine.
  3. Cover and bake at 180°C.
  4. Squeeze the soft garlic onto slices of the Italian loaf and eat with the chicken.

Jerry's blood-rare steak with rocket

Seared sirloin sliced pink, peppery leaves, parmesan and garlic potatoes.

Prep 11 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bring the steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking
Recipe for 4
  • 2 thick sirloin steaks
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg small potatoes
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 120g rocket
  • 40g parmesan
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  1. Halve the potatoes, toss with 2 tbsp of the oil, the bashed garlic cloves and rosemary and roast at 220°C for 35 minutes.
  2. Rub the steaks with the rest of the oil and salt and sear in a very hot pan for 3 minutes a side for rare.
  3. Rest the steaks for 8 minutes, then slice thinly.
  4. Lay the steak over the rocket, shave over the parmesan, drizzle with balsamic and serve with the potatoes.

Sweets

Blood orange possetVegetarian

Silky blood orange and cream posset.

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 blood oranges
  1. Bring the cream and sugar to the boil and simmer.
  2. Juice and zest the blood oranges.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the juice and zest.
  4. Pour into glasses and chill until set.
  5. Top with thin slices of blood orange to serve.

Drinks

Holy water lemon, lime and bitters

Lime, lemon and bitters topped with lemonade over crushed ice, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the limes and lemon up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; add the bitters and top with lemonade to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 800ml lemonade, cold
  • 3 cups crushed ice
  1. Squeeze the limes and lemon.
  2. Fill glasses with crushed ice.
  3. Add lime juice, lemon juice and bitters to each.
  4. Top with cold lemonade, stir once and drop in a lime wedge. For the bottle look, mix it all in a jug and pour into small glass bottles before serving over ice.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Peter Vincent's late night TV show starts.
