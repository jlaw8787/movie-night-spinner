Snacks

Mess hall cornbreadVegetarian

Buttermilk cornbread baked in a hot pan.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best the day it's baked
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cornmeal
  • 125g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 350ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g butter, melted, for the batter
  • 50g butter, to serve
  1. Heat a greased 25cm pan in the oven at 210°C, 10 min.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder and salt with the buttermilk, eggs and melted butter.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake, 20 min.
  4. Cut into squares and serve with butter.

Zihuatanejo guacamoleVegetarian

Chunky avocado with lime, red onion, jalapeño and coriander.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 1 jalapeno
  • 1 handful coriander
  • 230g corn chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mash the avocados roughly with lime juice and salt.
  2. Stir in the finely diced red onion, jalapeno and chopped coriander.
  3. Serve straight away with warm corn chips.

Mains

Brooks's library beef stew

Beef chuck, potatoes and carrots slow-cooked in rich stock.

Prep 35 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 600g potatoes
  • 3 carrots
  • 8 slices of white bread
  1. Chop the onions, potatoes and carrots.
  2. Brown the beef in the vegetable oil in batches, 15 min.
  3. Soften the onions, return the beef with the flour, stock, potatoes and carrots.
  4. Cover and simmer until tender.
  5. Ladle into bowls with thick white bread.

Zihuatanejo grilled fish tacos

Chilli cumin white fillets chargrilled in corn tortillas, pineapple salsa, by Andy's boat.

How it is made
  • 600g firm white fish fillets
  • chilli powder
  • ground cumin
  • vegetable oil
  • 3 limes
  • 200g pineapple
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 150g sour cream
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  1. White fish rubbed with chilli, cumin and lime, chargrilled and flaked into warm corn tortillas with cabbage, pineapple salsa and lime crema. Rub 600g firm white fish fillets with 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, oil, salt and lime zest. Dice 200g pineapple with half a red onion, 1 chopped chilli, coriander and lime juice. Shred a quarter of a cabbage. Mix 150g sour cream with the juice of 1 lime. Grill the fish on a hot oiled plate for 3 minutes a side and flake. Warm 12 small corn tortillas and fill with cabbage, fish, salsa and crema.

Sweets

Rock hammer rocky road

Milk chocolate with marshmallows, peanuts and broken biscuits.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g milk chocolate
  • 150g marshmallows
  • 100g roasted peanuts
  • 100g plain biscuits
  1. Melt the chocolate gently, 8 min.
  2. Stir in the marshmallows, peanuts and broken biscuits.
  3. Spread into a lined 20cm tin.
  4. Chill until set, which is the setting time.
  5. Cut into rough rocks.

Drinks

Rooftop ginger beer shandy

Cold lager topped with ginger beer, fresh ginger and lime.

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Put the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; slice the ginger and build the drinks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles of lager (375 ml each), cold
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 1 knob ginger (3cm)
  • 2 limes
  1. Put 4 glasses in the freezer to chill, 30 min.
  2. Slice the ginger into thin coins and cut the limes into wedges.
  3. Pour lager into each glass, top with ginger beer, squeeze in a lime wedge and add 2 ginger coins. For anyone not drinking, use all ginger beer with extra lime.

When to serve

Pour the beers when the tarring crew gets their cold ones on the roof.
