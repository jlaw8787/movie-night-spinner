Late for work breakfast sandwich

Bacon, fried egg and cheddar in toasted English muffins.

Prep 0 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat it, fry the eggs and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 English muffins
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 rashers of bacon
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 50g butter
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  2. Fry the eggs until just set.
  3. Split and toast the muffins and butter them, 4 min.
  4. Fill each with bacon, egg and cheddar and wrap tightly in paper so the cheese melts.

Chronobowl popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in lime zest, chilli flakes and melted butter.

Prep 8 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Make the lime and chilli butter up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and melt it to serve; pop the corn just before serving so it stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 lime
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  1. Zest and juice the lime. Melt the butter with the zest and chilli, 4 min.
  2. Pop the corn in a big lidded pot with the oil.
  3. Pour over the butter while tossing hard, then add a little lime juice.

Wayne Manor baked brie with quince

Wheel warmed runny under quince paste, thyme and walnuts, for Barry's knock at Bruce's door.

How it is made
  • 200g brie
  • 80g quince paste
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 40g walnuts
  • 1 jar honey
  • 200g crackers
  • 1 bunch red grapes
  1. A wheel of brie baked until runny, topped with warm quince paste, thyme and toasted walnuts, served with crackers and red grapes. Score the top of a 200g brie and sit it in a small baking dish. Warm 80g quince paste with 1 tbsp water in a small pan until spoonable. Bake the brie at 180°C for 12 minutes until soft in the middle. Spoon the quince over, scatter with thyme leaves and 40g toasted walnuts and drizzle with a little honey. Serve straight away with 200g crackers and a bunch of red grapes.

Nora's spaghetti with tomato sauceVegetarian

Slow simmered tomato sauce with basil, tossed through spaghetti.

Prep 14 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat, stir in the basil and cook the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cans of crushed tomatoes
  • 1 handful basil
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 50g parmesan
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Dice the onion and crush the garlic.
  2. Soften them in olive oil.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer gently, 45 min.
  4. Stir in torn basil and season.
  5. Cook the spaghetti, toss through the sauce and finish with parmesan, 12 min.

Allen family lemon thyme roast chicken

Whole chicken roasted with lemon, thyme and garlic.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  1. Chicken rubbed with butter, lemon and thyme, roasted at 200°C for 75 minutes, with roast potatoes, peas and a pan gravy. Mix 40g soft butter with lemon zest and thyme and rub over a 1.8kg chicken. Put the zested lemon inside and roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potatoes in chunks for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in hot oil alongside for 50 minutes. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the pan juices, whisk in 300ml chicken stock and simmer into gravy. Serve with buttered peas.

Sweets

Lightning bolt browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies piped with a yellow bolt.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g icing sugar
  • yellow food colouring, a drop
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a tin. Melt the chocolate and butter, 10 min.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Bake, 25 min.
  4. Cool completely, 30 min.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with colouring and a little water, cut the brownies and pipe a bolt on each.

Central City blood orange lightning fizzNo alcohol

Blood orange juice and honey ginger syrup in a salt rimmed glass.

Prep 9 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Simmer and cool the ginger syrup up to 5 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; rim the glasses, mix and top with sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 3 limes
  • 400ml blood orange juice
  • 320ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Simmer the honey, water and ginger for 2 minutes, then cool and strain.
  2. Mix the sugar and salt on a saucer, wet the glass rims with lime and dip them in.
  3. Fill the glasses with crushed ice.
  4. Mix the blood orange juice, lime juice and ginger syrup and pour in.
  5. Top each glass with sparkling water.
