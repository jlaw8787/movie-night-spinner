Snacks

Cirque Arcanus mushroom and gruyère galettesVegetarian

Buckwheat crêpes folded round mushrooms, gruyère and a soft egg, Paris street style

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the batter up to a day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 150g buckwheat flour
  • 1 egg
  • 400ml water
  • 40g butter
  • 300g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 150g gruyère, grated
  • 4 eggs, extra
  1. Whisk the buckwheat flour, egg, water and a pinch of salt into a thin batter and rest 20 minutes.
  2. Fry the mushrooms in half the butter with thyme until golden. Season.
  3. Butter a hot frying pan and swirl in a ladle of batter. Cook 2 minutes until set.
  4. Scatter with gruyère and mushrooms, crack an egg in the middle and fold the edges in to make a square. Cover and cook 3 minutes until the white sets.
  5. Repeat with the rest and serve hot.

Mains

Coq au vin

Chicken pieces braised in red wine with bacon, small onions and mushrooms

Prep 18 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Cook through the covered simmer up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; boil and mash the potatoes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken pieces
  • 150g bacon lardons
  • 12 pickling onions
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 500ml red wine
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 800g potatoes
  • 40g butter
  1. Peel the onions and potatoes and slice the mushrooms.
  2. Brown the chicken and lardons.
  3. Add the onions and mushrooms, then the wine, stock and thyme.
  4. Simmer covered.
  5. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter.

Sweets

Circus Arcanus chocolate croissantsVegetarian

Puff pastry rolled around dark chocolate and baked into crescents

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Roll the croissants up to 1 day ahead, covered on the tray in the fridge; brush with egg and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 1 egg
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut the pastry into long triangles.
  2. Lay a stick of chocolate at the wide end and roll up towards the point.
  3. Curve into crescents on a lined tray and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake until puffed and golden.

Plum jam doughnuts (pączki)Vegetarian

Fried yeast doughnuts filled with plum jam

Prep 33 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Prove the dough slowly in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; shape, prove again, fry and fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g butter
  • 200ml warm milk
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 jar plum jam
  • 50g icing sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, eggs, butter and milk and knead into a soft dough.
  2. Prove.
  3. Roll into balls.
  4. Prove again.
  5. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175C until golden.
  6. Pipe jam into each and dust with icing sugar.

Apple strudelVegetarian

Filo rolled around cinnamon green apple and sultanas

Prep 26 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best baked on the day; slice and toss the apple filling up to 4 hours ahead, covered, and roll the strudel just before baking
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green apples
  • 80g sultanas
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 6 sheets filo pastry
  • 100g butter
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Peel, core and slice the apples and toss with sultanas, cinnamon and breadcrumbs.
  2. Melt the butter. Layer buttered filo sheets.
  3. Spread the filling along one edge and roll up.
  4. Brush with butter and bake at 190C.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Paris café au lait with vanilla ChantillyVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong coffee with hot milk, topped with vanilla whipped cream

Prep 13 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Whip the vanilla cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge; brew the coffee and heat the milk just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 500ml strong coffee
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 pinch cocoa
  1. Whip the cream with icing sugar and vanilla to soft peaks.
  2. Brew strong coffee in a plunger or stovetop pot.
  3. Heat the milk until steaming and froth with a whisk.
  4. Pour coffee and milk into four cups at the same time, half and half.
  5. Top with vanilla cream and a pinch of cocoa.
