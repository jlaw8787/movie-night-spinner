Snacks

Owen's cabin campfire sausages

Beef sausages with jammy onions and mustard.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Onions up to 2 days, grill sausages fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Cook the onions slowly in the oil with the sugar until jammy, 30 min.
  2. Grill the sausages over coals until blistered and cooked through, 15 min.
  3. Slice thickly, spear on toothpicks and serve with the onions and mustard.

Dino egg scotch eggs

Soft eggs in sausage meat and breadcrumbs, fried golden.

Prep 12 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Fry or bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 7 eggs
  • 500g sausage meat
  • 1/2 cup plain flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for deep frying
  1. Boil six of the eggs for 6 minutes, cool and peel.
  2. Wrap each egg in sausage meat, then roll in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  3. Deep fry in the vegetable oil at 170°C for 6 minutes, or bake at 200°C for 25 minutes, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Halve to serve.

Lockwood auction smoked salmon on rye

Cured fish on rye, lemon crème fraîche.

How it is made
  • 6 slices rye bread
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • capers
  • 1 bunch dill
  • olive oil
  1. Toasted rye squares topped with lemon crème fraîche, folds of smoked salmon, capers and dill, passed round on trays like at the auction. Cut 6 slices dark rye into quarters, brush with olive oil and toast at 180°C for 8 minutes until crisp, then cool. Mix 150g crème fraîche with the zest of 1 lemon and a pinch of salt. Spread a little on each square, top with a fold of smoked salmon from 200g, 2 capers and a dill sprig. Finish with cracked pepper and serve within the hour.

Mains

Isla Nublar stampede chilli lime chicken and beans

Chilli lime chicken thighs grilled smoky, rice and black beans.

Prep 15 min, cook 18 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 limes, 2 for the marinade and 1 for the salsa
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 800g black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  1. Marinate the chicken in lime juice, garlic, chilli, oil and salt for an hour.
  2. Grill over high heat until charred and cooked through. Cook the rice at the same time, 18 min.
  3. Warm the beans with the cumin and paprika, 5 min.
  4. Mix the tomatoes, onion, coriander and a squeeze of lime for the salsa. Serve the chicken over rice and beans with the salsa.

Lockwood Manor tenderloin with red wine jus

Seared hard and roasted pink, glossy reduction, potato gratin and beans, for Maisie upstairs.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml cream
  • 1 head garlic
  • 250ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 30g butter
  • 300g green beans
  1. Beef eye fillet seared hard and roasted pink, sliced with a glossy red wine jus, creamy potato gratin and buttered green beans. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes in a buttered dish with 300ml cream, 2 garlic cloves and salt, and bake at 180°C for 1 hour. Season a 1kg eye fillet, sear all over in a hot pan and roast at 200°C for 20 minutes for medium-rare, then rest 10 minutes. In the same pan, boil 250ml red wine and 250ml beef stock until reduced by half, then whisk in 30g butter. Blanch 300g green beans for 3 minutes.

Owen's campfire banana boats

Bananas stuffed with chocolate and marshmallows, foil-wrapped and cooked on the barbecue.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bananas
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 50g mini marshmallows
  • 4 digestive biscuits, crushed
  1. Slit the bananas lengthways through the top skin without cutting through the bottom and open slightly.
  2. Stuff with chocolate and marshmallows. Wrap each in foil.
  3. Cook on a barbecue or at 200°C in the oven until melted, 10 min.
  4. Open the foil, scatter with crushed biscuits and eat with spoons.

Blue's butterfly pea lime fizzNo alcohol

Butterfly pea tea, honey, lemon-lime soda and lime.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Tea up to 1 day ahead, top with lemonade fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1.3 L lemonade
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Steep the butterfly pea flowers in the boiling water for 10 minutes. Strain, stir in the honey and chill.
  2. Fill four glasses with crushed ice and pour in the blue tea until half full.
  3. Top each with lemonade. Serve with lime wedges and let everyone squeeze in the lime to watch the colour change.
