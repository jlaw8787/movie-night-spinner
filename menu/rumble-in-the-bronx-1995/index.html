Bronx corner-store Reuben bites

Toasted rye topped with pastrami, sauerkraut and melted Swiss cheese.

Prep 12 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Mix the dressing up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; toast, top and bake the bites just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 6 slices of rye bread
  • 250g sliced pastrami
  • 150g sauerkraut, drained
  • 150g Swiss cheese
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 1 jar dill pickles, some chopped, some sliced
  1. Cut the rye into quarters and toast lightly at 200°C, 8 min.
  2. Top each with folded pastrami, sauerkraut and a square of Swiss cheese.
  3. Bake until melted, 5 min.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with tomato sauce, horseradish and chopped pickle.
  5. Dab on each bite and spear with a slice of dill pickle.

Pork and chive dumplings

Pan-fried pork and garlic chive dumplings with black vinegar.

Prep 31 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead on a floured tray, covered in the fridge; fry and steam to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 bunch garlic chives, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Mix the pork with chives, ginger and sesame oil.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat.
  3. Fry until crisp underneath, 4 min.
  4. Add water and steam under a lid, 4 min.
  5. Serve with black vinegar.

Danny's diamond cushion bao

Soft steamed buns stuffed with chicken, mushroom, scallion and ginger in sticky oyster sauce.

Prep 18 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fill and freeze up to 1 week ahead; steam from frozen with 5 extra minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken mince
  • 6 dried shiitake mushrooms, soaked and chopped
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 10g ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 12 frozen plain bao buns
  1. Mix the chicken, shiitake, spring onion, ginger, oyster sauce, soy and sesame oil.
  2. Stir-fry the filling until cooked and sticky, 8 min.
  3. Open the bao and spoon in the filling.
  4. Steam until hot through, 12 min.

Hovercraft sweet and sour pork

Crispy battered pork with pineapple and red bell pepper in sweet and sour sauce, over jasmine rice.

Prep 12 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Cut the pineapple and capsicum up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; fry the pork just before serving so it stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 700g pork shoulder, diced
  • 1 egg
  • 100g cornflour
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 80ml white vinegar
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Cut the pineapple and capsicum into chunks and toss the pork in egg and cornflour.
  2. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  3. Fry the pork in batches in the oil at 180°C until crisp, 12 min.
  4. Simmer the pineapple and capsicum with vinegar, sugar and tomato sauce, then thicken, 8 min.
  5. Toss the pork through and serve with the rice.

Ginger and scallion lobster noodles

Shell-on tails wok-fried in light oyster sauce, over crisp-edged soft egg noodles.

Prep 11 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Slice the ginger and spring onion up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the lobster and noodles just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lobster tails (or 800g king prawns)
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 50g ginger, thinly sliced
  • 6 spring onions, cut into lengths
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 150ml chicken stock
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Cut the lobster tails through the shell into chunks and dust in cornflour.
  2. Boil the egg noodles, drain well, then pan-fry in oil until crisp underneath, 10 min.
  3. Fry the lobster in a hot wok for 2 minutes, then set aside.
  4. Stir-fry the ginger and spring onion, then add the lobster, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine and stock and toss for 2 minutes.
  5. Pour over the noodles and serve.

Sweets

Golf course almond jelly

Chilled almond milk jelly cubes with lychees and mandarin segments.

Prep 18 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 gelatine leaves
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 425g tin lychees
  • 2 mandarins
  1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water, 5 min.
  2. Warm the milk with the sugar, then stir in the gelatine and almond extract, 5 min.
  3. Set in a tray for 3 hours.
  4. Cut into cubes.
  5. Serve in bowls with lychees in their syrup and fresh mandarin segments.

Uncle Bill's wedding iced jasmine teaNo alcohol

Jasmine tea iced with lychee syrup, lime and whole lychees in the glass.

Prep 9 min, cook 4 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L water
  • 5 tsp jasmine tea
  • 565g tin lychees
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Heat the water to just off the boil and steep the jasmine tea, 4 min.
  2. Strain and cool, and drain the lychees keeping the syrup, 4 min.
  3. Stir some of the lychee syrup and lime juice into the tea.
  4. Chill for an hour.
  5. Fill four tall glasses with ice and a few lychees, pour in the tea and finish with a twist of lime peel.
