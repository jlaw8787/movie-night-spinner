Snacks

Crop circle cornVegetarian

Charred corn cut into wheels, brushed with lime zest and chilli butter.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime and chilli butter up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; grill the corn, cut and brush to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 lime, zested
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 pinch flaky salt
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  1. Grill the corn over high heat, turning, until charred in rings.
  2. Cut into thick wheels.
  3. Brush with the butter mixed with lime zest and chilli flakes. Finish with flaky salt and coriander.

Foil hat popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed with nutty brown butter, parmesan and garlic powder.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Brown the butter and grate the parmesan up to 2 days ahead; pop and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 50g butter
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp oil, for popping
  1. Pop the kernels in a covered pot with the oil.
  2. Brown the butter until nutty and toss through the popcorn with the parmesan and garlic powder.
  3. Serve in bowls lined with crumpled foil.

Merrill's ballpark chilli dogs

Grilled frankfurts, spiced beef and cheddar in toasted rolls, watched with the Brazil footage.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 tin tomato paste
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog rolls
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  1. Grilled frankfurts in toasted soft rolls loaded with beef chilli, melted cheddar, diced white onion and a line of yellow mustard, ballpark style. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 diced onion, then add 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tbsp tomato paste and 200ml beef stock and simmer for 20 minutes until thick. Grill 8 frankfurts for 6 minutes until blistered. Toast 8 hot dog rolls. Fill each with a frankfurt, spoon over the chilli, then top with grated cheddar, finely diced white onion and a line of American mustard.

Mains

Pennsylvania farmhouse pot roast

Beef chuck braised 3 hours with carrots, celery and potatoes.

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions, quartered
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery sticks, chopped
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 800g potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour, for gravy
  1. Brown the beef hard on all sides in the oil in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the onions, carrots, celery, stock and thyme. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours.
  3. Add the potatoes and braise for the last hour.
  4. Lift out the meat and veg. Thicken the juices with the flour into gravy.

Boarded-up night chicken parmigiana

Crumbed chicken under napoli, ham and mozzarella, everyone's favourite for the last meal.

How it is made
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko crumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 jar napoli sauce
  • 4 slices leg ham
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 800g frozen chips
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Crumbed chicken breast fried golden, topped with napoli, leg ham and bubbling mozzarella, finished under the grill, with chips and a sharp green salad. Halve 2 large chicken breasts horizontally and pound to 1cm. Dust in flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs and press into 150g panko mixed with 30g grated parmesan. Shallow fry in 1cm of oil for 3 minutes a side until golden. Heat the oven grill. Top each with 3 tbsp napoli sauce, a slice of leg ham and 50g grated mozzarella, then grill for 4 minutes until bubbling. Bake 800g frozen chips at 220°C for 25 minutes and toss a green salad in lemon and oil.

Sweets

Hess farm strawberry shortcakeVegetarian

Buttermilk biscuits split and filled with strawberries and cream.

Prep 17 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Bake the biscuits up to 1 day ahead, airtight, and rewarm; slice and sugar the strawberries up to 2 hours ahead, then whip the cream and fill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g cold butter, cubed
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 5 tbsp caster sugar
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 500g strawberries, sliced
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Rub the butter into the flour and a little of the sugar, add the buttermilk and pat out 2cm thick.
  2. Cut into rounds and bake at 220°C for 13 minutes.
  3. Toss the strawberries with the rest of the sugar and stand for 20 minutes.
  4. Split the biscuits and fill with strawberries and whipped cream.

Drinks

Bo's half-full cucumber mint spritzersNo alcohol

Cucumber, mint, lime and elderflower blended and strained, served cold.

Prep 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Base up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 continental cucumbers
  • 1 cup mint leaves, plus sprigs to serve
  • 4 limes
  • 80ml elderflower cordial
  • 1 L chilled sparkling water
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Blend one cucumber with the mint, the juice of three limes and the cordial, then strain through a sieve into a jug.
  2. Chill for an hour.
  3. Slice the second cucumber into thin ribbons. Fill glasses with ice and cucumber ribbons.
  4. Pour the base in to a third full and top with sparkling water. Finish with a mint sprig and a lime wheel.

Serve the mains when the family sits down to their last supper of favourites.
