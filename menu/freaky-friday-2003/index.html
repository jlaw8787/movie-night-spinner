Chinese restaurant spring rolls

Fried rolls of pork mince, wombok, carrot and rice vermicelli

Prep 38 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roll the spring rolls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 50g rice vermicelli
  • 300g pork mince
  • 300g wombok, shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 12 spring roll pastry sheets
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Soak the vermicelli until soft, drain and chop.
  2. Shred the wombok and grate the carrots.
  3. Cook the pork mince until browned.
  4. Mix the pork with the vermicelli and vegetables and cool.
  5. Roll tightly in the spring roll pastry.
  6. Fry at 180C in batches until crisp and blistered.
  7. Serve hot with sweet chilli sauce.

Garage rehearsal popcorn chicken

Bite size chicken thigh coated in panko and paprika, shallow fried

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 3 hours ahead and keep chilled; fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup plain flour
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 tbsp honey mustard
  1. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces.
  2. Mix the flour, panko, paprika and salt. Dip the chicken in egg, then in the crumb mix.
  3. Shallow fry in the vegetable oil in batches until golden.
  4. Drain and serve with honey mustard.

Rehearsal dinner salmon blinis and melonOn the printed menu

Little pancakes, crème fraîche, cured fish and chives, rockmelon in prosciutto, before the big toast

How it is made
  • 24 cocktail blinis
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1/2 rockmelon
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Blinis topped with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and chives, beside wedges of rockmelon wrapped in thin prosciutto, on silver trays. Warm 24 cocktail blinis at 180C for 4 minutes. Mix 150g crème fraîche with the zest of half a lemon and a pinch of salt. Top each blini with a spoon of crème fraîche, a fold of smoked salmon from 150g and a few snipped chives. Cut half a rockmelon into 12 thin wedges, remove the skin and wrap each in a slice of prosciutto from 100g. Arrange both on a silver tray with lemon wedges.

Mains

Honey chicken

Crispy chicken thigh tossed in a honey and soy glaze, with sesame

Prep 18 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Cut the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; coat, fry and sauce it on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, in pieces
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Cut the chicken and toss in cornflour.
  2. Cook the jasmine rice.
  3. Fry the chicken in the vegetable oil in batches until crisp.
  4. Warm the honey with the soy sauce until bubbling and toss the chicken through.
  5. Scatter with sesame seeds and serve over the rice.

Pei-Pei's family black pepper beef

Dinner at Pei-Pei's family restaurant, fortunes to follow

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Slice and marinate the beef up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the rice and stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rump steak, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tsp black pepper, coarsely cracked
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion, cut in wedges
  • 1 green capsicum, cut in squares
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 bunch gai lan, cut in lengths
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Toss the beef with half the soy sauce and the cornflour and leave it while you prep.
  2. Mix the oyster sauce, the rest of the soy, the pepper, sugar and 3 tbsp water.
  3. Rinse the rice and simmer it, covered, until the water is gone. Rest it with the lid on.
  4. Stir-fry the beef in a very hot wok with half the oil in two batches until browned. Lift out.
  5. Stir-fry the onion, capsicum, garlic and gai lan in the rest of the oil, return the beef, add the sauce and toss until glossy. Serve over the rice.

Sweets

Homemade fortune cookiesVegetarian

Thin vanilla cookies folded into the classic fortune shape

Prep 26 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Bake and fold up to 3 days ahead, airtight; the batter can be made 1 day ahead and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 egg whites
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 60g plain flour
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 12 paper fortunes
  1. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar, then mix in the flour, butter and vanilla.
  2. Spread thin rounds on a lined tray.
  3. Bake until the edges are golden, in batches.
  4. Working quickly while hot, lay a fortune on each, fold and bend over a cup.
  5. Cool until crisp.

Lychee jasmine iced tea with limeVegetarianNo alcohol

Jasmine tea chilled with lychees, lychee syrup and lime

Prep 14 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Tea up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 jasmine tea bags
  • 800ml hot water (about 85C)
  • 565g tin lychees
  • 2 limes
  • 200ml soda water
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the hot water, then remove.
  2. Chill the tea.
  3. Drain the lychees, keeping the syrup.
  4. Mix the cold tea with some of the lychee syrup and the juice of a lime.
  5. Fill glasses with ice and lychees, pour over the tea and top with soda water and a lime wheel.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the fortune cookies arrive at the Chinese restaurant.
