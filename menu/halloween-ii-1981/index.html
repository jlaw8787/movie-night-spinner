Snacks

Jack-o'-lantern pumpkin seedsVegetarian

Pumpkin seeds roasted with brown sugar and smoked paprika, pumpkins on every porch

Prep 5 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 200g pepitas
  • 20g butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Toss the pepitas with the butter, sugar, paprika, cinnamon, cayenne and salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast, stirring halfway, until puffed and crunchy.
  3. Cool on the tray.

Night shift egg sandwichesVegetarian

Soft white bread with creamy egg, mayo, mustard and chives, crusts off, cut in fingers

Prep 14 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the egg filling up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; fill the bread no more than 2 hours before serving and keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 30g butter, softened
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes.
  2. Cool the eggs in iced water.
  3. Peel and mash with the mayonnaise, mustard, chives, salt and pepper.
  4. Butter the bread, fill, cut off the crusts and cut into fingers.

Mains

Night shift chicken pot pie

The hot meal on the cafeteria line at Haddonfield Memorial

Prep 21 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, in 3cm pieces
  • 40g butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 40g plain flour
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in half the butter and set aside.
  2. Soften the onion, carrots, celery and garlic in the rest of the butter.
  3. Stir in the flour, then gradually add the stock and cream with the thyme and simmer until thick.
  4. Return the chicken with the peas, season with salt and pepper and cool a little while the oven heats to 200°C.
  5. Pour into a 2 litre pie dish, top with the pastry, crimp, cut a slit and brush with egg.
  6. Bake until puffed and deep golden.
  7. Rest before serving.

Mr Garrett's meatball subs

Pork and beef balls in tomato sauce, toasted rolls under melted provolone

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; toast and fill the rolls on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 700ml passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 8 slices provolone
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 100g rocket
  1. Mix the mince, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, 1 crushed garlic clove and salt, and roll into 20 meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil and lift out.
  3. Fry the rest of the garlic for 1 minute, add the passata and oregano, put the meatballs back and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Split and toast the rolls, fill with meatballs and sauce, lay over the provolone and grill for 2 minutes until melted.
  5. Tuck in the basil and serve with the rocket on the side.

Sweets

Haddonfield candy applesVegetarian

Crisp red fruit dipped in glassy cinnamon toffee, set hard on baking paper

Prep 5 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Best dipped on the day; they keep 1 day uncovered somewhere cool and dry, never in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 6 small crisp red apples
  • 6 wooden sticks
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil, for the paper
  • 400g white sugar
  • 125ml water
  • 3 tbsp glucose syrup
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/2 tsp red food colouring
  1. Wash the apples in hot water to strip the wax, dry well and push a stick into each. Oil a sheet of baking paper.
  2. Heat the sugar, water, glucose and cinnamon stick, stirring until dissolved, then boil without stirring to 150°C.
  3. Take off the heat, lift out the cinnamon and stir in the colouring.
  4. Tilt the pan and dip each apple, twirling to coat, then stand on the paper.
  5. Leave to set hard.

Night shift mulled apple juiceVegetarianNo alcohol

Kept hot in an urn by the nurses' station

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mull up to 2 days ahead, strain and keep in the fridge; reheat gently without boiling
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 3cm piece ginger, sliced
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Put everything in a pot and bring to just below a simmer.
  2. Keep on the lowest heat to infuse, without boiling.
  3. Strain into mugs and garnish with orange and cinnamon.
