Ricky's camp oven damperVegetarian

Hollow-crusted bush bread served warm with butter and golden syrup.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, cold, for the dough
  • 40g butter, to serve
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300ml milk
  • 125ml golden syrup
  1. Rub the cold butter for the dough into the flour and salt.
  2. Stir in the milk to a soft dough and shape into a round. Cut a cross on top.
  3. Bake at 200°C until it sounds hollow, 30 min.
  4. Serve warm, torn, with the butter to serve and golden syrup.

Wild pork sausage rolls

Pork, green apple and sage rolled in flaky puff pastry.

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, or freeze
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 green apple, grated
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp sage, chopped
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Mix the sausage mince with the apple, onion and sage.
  2. Roll into logs along the pastry sheets and roll up, then cut each into 6.
  3. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C until golden, 25 min.
  4. Rest briefly before serving, 5 min.

Curried egg and watercress sandwiches

Soft white bread, egg in mild spiced mayo, chives and peppery greens, wrapped for the tramp.

How it is made
  • 8 eggs
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 loaf soft white bread
  • butter
  • 1 bunch watercress
  1. Soft white bread filled with chopped egg in a mild curry mayo with chives, packed with peppery watercress, cut into triangles and wrapped for the tramp. Boil 8 eggs for 10 minutes, cool in cold water and peel. Chop roughly and mash with 100g mayo, 1 tsp mild curry powder, 2 tbsp chopped chives, salt and pepper. Butter 12 slices of soft white bread. Spread the egg over 6 slices, top with a good handful of watercress and close the sandwiches. Cut into triangles and wrap in baking paper.

Bella's roast pork

Slow-roasted pork shoulder with crackling, green apples and kūmara.

Prep 5 min, cook 2 hr 40 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Score and salt the pork up to 1 day ahead and leave it uncovered in the fridge; make the gravy fresh from the pan juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 green apples, quartered
  • 2 onions, quartered
  • 800g kumara, in chunks
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml water or stock
  1. Score the pork and rub with salt, then leave uncovered in the fridge, which is the resting time, 1 hr.
  2. Roast at 230°C, 30 min.
  3. Reduce to 170°C and roast on a bed of apple and onion, adding the kumara for the last hour, 2 hr.
  4. Make gravy with the flour, water and pan juices.

Hāngī-style lamb and kūmara

Lamb, kūmara and pumpkin wrapped and slow roasted hāngī style, a feed for the whole search party.

How it is made
  • 2kg bone-in lamb shoulder
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 cabbage
  • 800g kūmara
  • 600g pumpkin
  1. Lamb shoulder, kūmara, pumpkin and cabbage wrapped tight and slow roasted with garlic and rosemary until the meat falls apart, with the rich pan juices spooned over. Heat the oven to 160°C. Rub a 2kg bone-in lamb shoulder with salt, pepper, 4 crushed garlic cloves and rosemary. Line a deep roasting tin with foil and cabbage leaves. Add the lamb, 800g kūmara and 600g pumpkin in big chunks and 250ml water. Cover with more cabbage leaves and seal tightly with foil. Roast 4 hours. Pull the lamb into chunks and spoon the juices over everything.

Ricky's birthday cakeVegetarian

Chocolate cake with cocoa buttercream icing and candles.

Prep 30 min, cook 40 min, 25 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 50g cocoa powder, for the cake
  • 200ml milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 25g cocoa powder, for the icing
  • 50g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 1 pack birthday candles
  1. Beat the cake butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, the cake cocoa and milk.
  2. Bake in a 22cm tin at 175°C, 40 min.
  3. Cool completely, 25 min.
  4. Beat the icing sugar, icing cocoa and icing butter with a splash of milk until smooth and ice the cake.
  5. Add candles and sing badly.

Haiku hot cocoaNo alcohol

Cocoa whisked into hot milk, pretty majestical for a night in the bush.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 L milk
  1. Whisk the cocoa and sugar with a splash of milk into a paste.
  2. Whisk in the rest of the milk and heat until steaming without boiling, 8 min.
  3. Pour into 4 tin mugs and drink around the fire.
