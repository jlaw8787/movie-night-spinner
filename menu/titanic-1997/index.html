Snacks

First class oysters

Fresh shucked oysters on ice with lemon and shallot mignonette

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mignonette up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp cracked pepper
  • 24 freshly shucked oysters
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  • 4 cup crushed ice
  1. Mix the shallots with the vinegar and cracked pepper.
  2. Rest the mignonette for 20 minutes.
  3. Lay the oysters on crushed ice with lemon wedges and serve with the mignonette.

Steerage ham sandwiches

Crusty ham and English mustard sandwiches with pickled onions

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Fill the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, wrap and chill; open the pickled onions to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 crusty Italian loaf
  • 50g butter
  • 300g leg ham
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Butter thick slices of loaf and fill with ham and mustard.
  2. Cut in halves and serve with the pickled onions below deck.

Mains

Molly Brown's first class filet mignon

Seared tied eye fillet with pommes Anna, the layered potato cake

Prep 45 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Bake the pommes Anna up to 1 day ahead and reheat at 180C; cook the steaks and sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 80g butter
  • 4 eye fillet steaks
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 200ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 300g green beans
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Layer the potatoes with melted butter in a pan, season and bake at 200C for 50 minutes for pommes Anna.
  2. Tie the steaks, sear in butter and roast to 54C.
  3. Reduce the shallots, wine and stock to a sauce in the steak pan.
  4. Steam the beans and serve everything together.

Sweets

1912 apple puddingVegetarian

Baked custard with apples and sultanas, nutmeg on top

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, serve warm or cold
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 60g sultanas
  • 3 eggs
  • 500ml milk
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  1. Cook the apples in the butter with the sultanas until soft. Tip into a dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk and sugar and pour over.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top and set in a water bath.
  4. Bake at 160C for 40 minutes.

Drinks

Black velvet, stout and sparkling wineVegetarian

Irish stout layered under chilled sparkling wine in tall flutes

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill drinks 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml sparkling wine, chilled
  • 4 cans Irish stout, 440ml, chilled
  1. Half fill four tall glasses with stout, poured slowly so it settles.
  2. Top each slowly with sparkling wine poured over the back of a spoon so it sits on top. Serve straight away.
