Snacks

Oysters on ice

Fresh shucked oysters on ice with lemon and shallot mignonette.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Mignonette up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp cracked pepper
  • 24 freshly shucked oysters
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  • 4 cups crushed ice
  1. Mix the shallots with the vinegar and cracked pepper.
  2. Rest the mignonette for 20 minutes.
  3. Lay the oysters on crushed ice with lemon wedges and serve with the mignonette.

Steerage ham sandwiches

Crusty ham and English mustard sandwiches with pickled onions.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Fill the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, wrap and chill; open the pickled onions to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 crusty Italian loaf
  • 50g butter
  • 300g leg ham
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Butter thick slices of loaf and fill with ham and mustard.
  2. Cut in halves and serve with the pickled onions below deck.

Poached salmon with mousselineOn the printed menu

Gently poached in lemon and herb stock, chilled and flaked onto toasts with whipped hollandaise.

How it is made
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 jar bay leaves
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bottle white wine vinegar
  • 400g salmon fillet
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g butter
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 1 baguette
  1. Salmon gently poached in a lemon and herb court bouillon, chilled and flaked onto toasts with a light mousseline of hollandaise folded with whipped cream. Simmer 1L water with a sliced lemon, a bay leaf, peppercorns, dill stalks and a splash of vinegar for 10 minutes. Add a 400g salmon fillet, turn off the heat and leave 12 minutes, then chill and flake. Whisk 2 yolks with 1 tbsp lemon juice over simmering water until thick, then drizzle in 100g warm melted butter. Fold in 80ml softly whipped cream. Toast 16 baguette slices and top with salmon, mousseline and dill.

Mains

Molly Brown's filet mignon

Seared tied eye fillet with pommes Anna, the layered potato cake.

Prep 0 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Bake the pommes Anna up to 1 day ahead and reheat at 180°C; cook the steaks and sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 80g butter
  • 4 eye fillet steaks
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 200ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 300g green beans
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Layer the potatoes with melted butter in a pan, season and bake at 200°C for 50 minutes for pommes Anna.
  2. Tie the steaks, sear in butter and roast to 54°C.
  3. Reduce the shallots, wine and stock to a sauce in the steak pan.
  4. Steam the beans and serve everything together.

Lamb rack with mint sauceOn the printed menu

Lamb racks roasted pink with fresh mint sauce, straight off the real menu that night.

How it is made
  • 1kg roasting potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 lamb racks
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 1 bottle malt vinegar
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 30g butter
  1. Lamb racks seared and roasted pink, carved into cutlets, with fresh sharp mint sauce, buttery minted peas and crisp roast potatoes. Parboil 1kg potatoes and roast in oil at 220°C for 50 minutes. Season 2 lamb racks, sear fat side down, roast at 200°C to 56°C inside, about 15 minutes, and rest 10 minutes. Chop a bunch of mint with 2 tbsp sugar, 3 tbsp boiling water and 4 tbsp malt vinegar. Cook 400g peas with 30g butter and chopped mint. Carve the racks into cutlets.

1912 apple puddingVegetarian

Baked custard with apples and sultanas, nutmeg on top.

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, serve warm or cold
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 60g sultanas
  • 3 eggs
  • 500ml milk
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  1. Cook the apples in the butter with the sultanas until soft. Tip into a dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk and sugar and pour over.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top and set in a water bath.
  4. Bake at 160°C for 40 minutes.

Drinks

Black velvet, stout and sparkling wineVegetarian

Irish stout layered under chilled sparkling wine in tall flutes.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill drinks 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml sparkling wine, chilled
  • 4 cans Irish stout, 440ml, chilled
  1. Half fill four tall glasses with stout, poured slowly so it settles.
  2. Top each slowly with sparkling wine poured over the back of a spoon so it sits on top. Serve straight away.
