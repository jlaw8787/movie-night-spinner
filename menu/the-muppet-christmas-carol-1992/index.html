Snacks

Fozziwig's cheese and pickle sandwichesVegetarian

Aged cheddar and brown pickle on buttered white bread, crusts off, cut in quarters.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 12 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 4 tbsp brown pickle
  • 250g aged cheddar
  1. Butter the bread.
  2. Spread half the slices with pickle, cover with thinly sliced cheddar and top with the other slices.
  3. Trim the crusts and cut each into 4 triangles.

Rizzo's cheese plateVegetarian

Cheddar and Red Leicester wedges with pickled onions and apple chutney.

Prep 8 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 200g Red Leicester
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 1 jar apple chutney
  • 1 packet oatcakes
  1. Take the cheeses out of the fridge an hour ahead.
  2. Cut the vintage cheddar and Red Leicester into wedges and arrange on a wooden board with pickled onions, a bowl of chutney and a stack of oatcakes.

Christmas Present's feast board

Roast chestnuts, Stilton, walnuts, honey ham and gingerbread piled high, a mountain for the spirit.

How it is made
  • 300g chestnuts
  • 100g walnuts
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 6 clementines
  • 1 bunch grapes
  • 200g Stilton
  • 200g honey ham
  • 8 gingerbread biscuits
  • 1 packet oat crackers
  1. A towering board of roast chestnuts, clementines, grapes, Stilton, walnuts, honey-glazed ham and gingerbread, piled high like the spirit's feast. Cut a cross in 300g chestnuts and roast at 200°C for 25 minutes, then peel while warm. Toss 100g walnuts with 1 tbsp honey and bake 8 minutes. Pile everything onto a big board: the chestnuts and walnuts, 6 clementines, 1 bunch grapes, a 200g wedge of Stilton, 200g sliced honey ham, 8 gingerbread biscuits and a packet of oat crackers.

Mains

The Cratchits' Christmas goose

Crisp roast goose legs, goose fat potatoes, braised red cabbage with apple.

Prep 5 min, cook 4 hr, 10 hr waiting · Make ahead: Salt a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 goose legs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1/2 red cabbage
  • 3 apples
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  1. Salt the goose legs and leave overnight in the fridge.
  2. Roast at 170°C until crisp, pouring off the fat as it renders, 2 hr.
  3. Halve the potatoes and roast in the goose fat at 200°C, 45 min.
  4. Shred the cabbage and slice the apples, then braise together with the vinegar, 15 min.
  5. Braise gently until soft, 1 hr.

The prize turkey from the shop window

Roast turkey in thyme and lemon butter, chestnut stuffing and bacon sprouts. The prize one.

How it is made
  • 4kg turkey
  • 80g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 100g cooked chestnuts
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 450ml chicken stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 400g Brussels sprouts
  • 100g bacon
  1. Butter-basted roast turkey with thyme and lemon, chestnut stuffing, roast potatoes, Brussels sprouts with bacon and plenty of gravy for the whole table. Rub a 4kg turkey with 80g butter mashed with thyme and lemon zest under the skin. Roast at 170°C for 2 hours 15 minutes until the breast reaches 65°C, then rest under foil 45 minutes. Bake 150g breadcrumbs with 100g chopped cooked chestnuts, 1 onion, 1 egg and 150ml stock for 30 minutes. Roast 1kg potatoes. Fry 400g halved sprouts with 100g bacon. Make gravy with 300ml stock.

Sweets

Christmas puddingVegetarian

Steamed fruit pudding soaked in orange juice and tea, carried in steaming.

Prep 25 min, cook 4 hr, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Soak the dried fruit in the orange juice and tea up to 1 day ahead; steam the pudding up to 2 weeks ahead, keep wrapped and reheat by steaming
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed dried fruit
  • 150ml orange juice
  • 50ml cold black tea
  • 125g soft butter
  • 125g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 1 orange
  • 600ml custard
  1. Soak the dried fruit in the orange juice and tea overnight.
  2. Mix with the butter, sugar, eggs, breadcrumbs, flour, spice and orange zest.
  3. Spoon into a greased basin, cover tightly with baking paper and foil, and steam, topping up the water, 4 hr.
  4. Turn out and serve hot with warm custard, 5 min.

Drinks

God bless us mulled grape punchNo alcohol

Warm grape and cranberry juice with roasted orange, cinnamon and star anise.

Prep 3 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Stud and roast the oranges up to 1 day ahead; warm the punch gently on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 oranges
  • 12 whole cloves
  • 750ml red grape juice
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Stud the oranges with cloves and roast at 180°C, 30 min.
  2. Put the juices in a saucepan with the oranges, cinnamon, star anise and sugar.
  3. Warm gently without boiling, 20 min.
  4. Ladle into cups with a slice of roasted orange in each.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Scrooge sends for the prize turkey.
