House party onion dipVegetarian

Slow caramelised onion dip with sour cream and chives, ridged potato chips.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 bag potato chips
  1. Cook the sliced onions in the butter for 40 minutes until deep and jammy.
  2. Cool, then chop finely, 10 min.
  3. Fold into the sour cream and cream cheese with salt, pepper and chives.
  4. Serve with a big bag of ridged potato chips.

Squad car doughnutsVegetarian

Warm cake doughnuts, glazed or cinnamon sugar, spun in a parking lot.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the cinnamon sugar up to 1 week ahead; warm the doughnuts and glaze them just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 cake doughnuts
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp full cream milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Warm the doughnuts in a 160°C oven, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the icing sugar, milk and vanilla into a glaze.
  3. Dip four doughnuts in the glaze and toss the rest in cinnamon sugar, 10 min.

Corner store Worcestershire snack mix

Pretzels, cheese crackers and peanuts baked in garlic butter, chocolate buttons tossed through.

How it is made
  • 80g butter
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 150g pretzels
  • 150g cheese crackers
  • 150g roasted peanuts
  • 100g chocolate buttons
  1. Pretzels, cheese crackers and peanuts baked in Worcestershire garlic butter until toasty and crunchy, cooled with chocolate buttons tossed through. Melt 80g butter with 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 1 tsp garlic powder and half a tsp smoked paprika. Toss with 150g pretzels, 150g cheese crackers and 150g roasted peanuts. Spread on a tray and bake at 150°C for 30 minutes, stirring twice. Cool completely, then mix through 100g chocolate buttons.

Liquor store hold-up burritos

Eggs, bacon, potato and cheddar rolled in flour tortillas with salsa.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the bacon and potato up to 1 day ahead and reheat; scramble the eggs and fill the tortillas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 20g butter
  • 200g bacon, diced
  • 2 potatoes
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 100g cheddar
  • 4 tbsp tomato salsa
  1. Dice the potatoes and fry with the bacon until crisp, 15 min.
  2. Scramble the eggs softly in the butter.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill with eggs, bacon, potato, grated cheddar and salsa, 10 min.
  4. Roll tightly and toast seam-side down in a dry pan, 10 min.

Jules's party pepperoni and jalapeño pizza

Pepperoni pizza drizzled with honey and chilli, a slice for everyone at the party.

How it is made
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 120g pepperoni
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Thin crisp base with garlicky crushed tomato, mozzarella and cupped pepperoni, baked very hot and finished with chilli honey and fresh basil. Heat the oven to its highest setting with a tray or steel inside for 30 minutes. Mix 400g crushed tomatoes with a crushed garlic clove, oregano and salt. Stretch 500g pizza dough into 2 thin bases, spread with sauce and top with 250g mozzarella and 120g pepperoni. Bake each for 7 minutes. Warm 3 tbsp honey with half a tsp chilli flakes and drizzle over with torn basil.

I love you, man browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies with a soft middle.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 80g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until glossy, fold in the chocolate, flour and cocoa.
  3. Bake at 170°C so the middle stays fudgy, 25 min.
  4. Cool before cutting, 30 min.

McLovin's cherry cola floatNo alcohol

Cherry syrup and vanilla ice cream with cold cola poured over.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g frozen pitted cherries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 1 L cola
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Simmer the cherries with the sugar and water for 8 minutes, then mash and strain.
  2. Cool the syrup, 5 min.
  3. Put syrup and ice cream in each of four tall glasses.
  4. Pour cola slowly down the side so it foams up. Top with a maraschino cherry and serve with a spoon and straw.
