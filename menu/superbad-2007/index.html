Snacks

House party onion dipVegetarian

Slow caramelised onion dip with sour cream and chives, ridged potato chips

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 bag potato chips
  1. Cook the sliced onions in the butter for 40 minutes until deep and jammy.
  2. Cool, then chop finely.
  3. Fold into the sour cream and cream cheese with salt, pepper and chives.
  4. Serve with a big bag of ridged potato chips.

Squad car doughnutsVegetarian

Warm cake doughnuts, glazed or cinnamon sugar, spun in a car park

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the cinnamon sugar up to 1 week ahead; warm the doughnuts and glaze them just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 cake doughnuts
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp full cream milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Warm the doughnuts in a 160C oven.
  2. Whisk the icing sugar, milk and vanilla into a glaze.
  3. Dip four doughnuts in the glaze and toss the rest in cinnamon sugar.

Mains

Liquor store hold-up burritos

Eggs, bacon, potato and cheddar rolled in flour tortillas with salsa

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the bacon and potato up to 1 day ahead and reheat; scramble the eggs and fill the tortillas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 20g butter
  • 200g bacon, diced
  • 2 potatoes
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 100g cheddar
  • 4 tbsp tomato salsa
  1. Dice the potatoes and fry with the bacon until crisp.
  2. Scramble the eggs softly in the butter.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill with eggs, bacon, potato, grated cheddar and salsa.
  4. Roll tightly and toast seam-side down in a dry pan.

Sweets

I love you, man browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies with a soft middle

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 80g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until glossy, fold in the chocolate, flour and cocoa.
  3. Bake at 170C so the middle stays fudgy.
  4. Cool before cutting.

Drinks

McLovin's cherry cola floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Cherry syrup and vanilla ice cream with cold cola poured over

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g frozen pitted cherries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1l cola
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Simmer the cherries with the sugar and water for 8 minutes, then mash and strain.
  2. Cool the syrup.
  3. Put syrup and ice cream in each of four tall glasses.
  4. Pour cola slowly down the side so it foams up. Top with a maraschino cherry and serve with a spoon and straw.

When to serve
