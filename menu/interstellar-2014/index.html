Snacks

Last crop corn on the cobVegetarian

Charred corn with smoked paprika lime butter and grated parmesan

Prep 19 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the paprika and lime butter up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; boil and char the corn just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 lime
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the corn cobs.
  2. Mix the butter with smoked paprika and lime zest.
  3. Brush the corn with the butter, then char on a hot grill.
  4. Sprinkle with salt and parmesan.

Dust storm smoky caramel popcornVegetarian

Caramel popcorn with smoked paprika and sea salt

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 60ml golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the popping corn in oil and tip into a large bowl.
  2. Boil the butter, brown sugar and golden syrup.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the bicarb soda, paprika and salt.
  4. Pour over the popcorn and toss to coat.
  5. Spread on lined trays and bake at 120C, stirring twice.
  6. Cool and break into clusters.

Mains

Cooper Station chicken and corn pie

Chicken, leek and corn in a cream sauce under a shortcrust lid

Prep 30 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with the pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml chicken stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  1. Poach the chicken and shred.
  2. Soften the leek in butter.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock and cream.
  4. Add the chicken, corn and thyme.
  5. Tip into a dish and top with the pastry.
  6. Bake at 200C.

Murph's bookshelf buttermilk pieVegetarian

Buttermilk custard pie with nutmeg. The books said Stay.

Prep 30 min, cook 138 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 whole nutmeg, to grate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Line a 23cm tin with the sweet shortcrust pastry and blind bake at 180C.
  2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, melted butter, flour, buttermilk, vanilla and nutmeg until smooth.
  3. Pour into the case and bake at 170C until just set with a slight wobble.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Whip the cream and serve with the strawberries.

Donald's porch lager shandyVegetarian

Cold lager topped with fresh lemon soda in frosted glasses

Prep 30 min, cook 1 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup and squeeze the lemons up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; frost the glasses and pour with cold lager on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles lager
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml hot water
  • 5 lemons
  • 300ml soda water
  • 200ml lemonade
  1. Frost four glasses in the freezer.
  2. Dissolve the sugar in hot water and cool.
  3. Squeeze 4 lemons and mix the juice with the syrup and soda water.
  4. Half fill each glass with cold lager and top with the lemon mix and lemonade.
  5. Add a lemon wheel and serve on the porch.

When to serve
