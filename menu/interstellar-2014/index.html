Last crop corn on the cobVegetarian

Charred corn with smoked paprika lime butter and grated parmesan.

Prep 2 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Mix the paprika and lime butter up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; boil and char the corn just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 lime
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the corn cobs.
  2. Mix the butter with smoked paprika and lime zest.
  3. Brush the corn with the butter, then char on a hot grill.
  4. Sprinkle with salt and parmesan.

Dust storm smoky caramel popcornVegetarian

Caramel popcorn with smoked paprika and sea salt.

Prep 9 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 60ml golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the popping corn in oil and tip into a large bowl.
  2. Boil the butter, brown sugar and golden syrup.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the bicarb soda, paprika and salt.
  4. Pour over the popcorn and toss to coat.
  5. Spread on lined trays and bake at 120°C, stirring twice.
  6. Cool and break into clusters.

Wormhole pretzel bites with beer cheese

Bicarb-dipped dough, rock salted and baked brown, hot lager cheddar sauce, after the drone chase.

How it is made
  • 450g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 20g butter
  • 150ml lager
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 jar American mustard
  1. Soft pretzel bites dipped in bicarb water, sprinkled with rock salt and baked deep brown, with a hot beer and cheddar dipping sauce. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Roll into ropes and cut into 3cm bites. Boil 2L water with 60g bicarb soda and dip the bites for 30 seconds. Sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Melt 20g butter, stir in 1 tbsp flour, whisk in 150ml lager and 100ml milk, then 200g grated cheddar and 1 tsp mustard.

Chicken and corn pie

Chicken, leek and corn in a cream sauce under a shortcrust lid.

Prep 18 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with the pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml chicken stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  1. Poach the chicken and shred.
  2. Soften the leek in butter.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock and cream.
  4. Add the chicken, corn and thyme.
  5. Tip into a dish and top with the pastry.
  6. Bake at 200°C.

Ice planet crackling pork roast

Slow-roast pork shoulder with crackling as brittle as ice, for Mann's frozen planet and faked data.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 3 green apples
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  1. Pork shoulder rubbed with garlic, rosemary and salt, roasted slow until tender, the crackling blasted until it shatters, with roast potatoes and apple sauce. Score the skin of a 2kg pork shoulder, rub with salt and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Make cuts in the flesh and push in 4 garlic slivers and rosemary. Roast at 160°C for 4 hours. Turn up to 240°C for 25 minutes until the crackling blisters. Roast 1kg potatoes in oil alongside for the last hour. Simmer 3 chopped apples with 2 tbsp sugar and water and mash for apple sauce.

Murph's bookshelf buttermilk pieVegetarian

Buttermilk custard pie with a crisp crust and fresh nutmeg.

Prep 5 min, cook 73 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 whole nutmeg, to grate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Line a 23cm tin with the sweet shortcrust pastry and blind bake at 180°C.
  2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, melted butter, flour, buttermilk, vanilla and nutmeg until smooth.
  3. Pour into the case and bake at 170°C until just set with a slight wobble.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Whip the cream and serve with the strawberries.

Donald's porch lager shandy

Cold lager topped with fresh lemon soda in frosted glasses.

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup and squeeze the lemons up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; frost the glasses and pour with cold lager on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles lager
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml hot water
  • 5 lemons
  • 300ml soda water
  • 200ml lemonade
  1. Frost four glasses in the freezer.
  2. Dissolve the sugar in hot water and cool.
  3. Squeeze 4 lemons and mix the juice with the syrup and soda water.
  4. Half fill each glass with cold lager and top with the lemon mix and lemonade.
  5. Add a lemon wheel and serve on the porch.
