Mutt Cutts road snack mixVegetarian

Pretzels, cheese puffs and chips tossed in butter and ranch, baked crisp for the van.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day in a sealed container
Recipe for 4
  • 60g potato chips
  • 60g cheese puffs
  • 60g dog-bone-shaped pretzels
  1. Tip the potato chips, cheese puffs and dog-bone-shaped pretzels into a big shared bowl.
  2. Toss gently.
  3. Share between four people for the road trip.

Truck stop onion ringsVegetarian

Thick beer-battered onion rings fried crisp, from Sea Bass's truck stop diner.

Prep 7 min, cook 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Slice and soak the onions in the buttermilk up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; make the batter and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 120g plain flour
  • 150ml lager
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 L neutral oil, for frying
  1. Slice the onions into thick rings and soak in the buttermilk.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager and salt.
  3. Heat the oil to 185°C.
  4. Drain the onions, dip in the batter and fry in small batches until puffed and golden.
  5. Drain and salt lightly.

Petey's seeded crackers and herb cream

Homemade seeded crackers with herbed cream cheese, for Petey, whose head has fallen off.

How it is made
  • 60g sunflower seeds
  • 60g pumpkin seeds
  • 60g sesame seeds
  • 30g chia seeds
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Homemade seeded crackers loaded with sunflower, pumpkin and sesame seeds, with a cream cheese whipped with chives, dill and lemon for dipping. Mix 60g each of sunflower, pumpkin and sesame seeds with 30g chia seeds, 60g plain flour, 1 tsp salt, 2 tbsp olive oil and 200ml water. Stand 15 minutes, then spread very thin between sheets of baking paper. Bake at 160°C for 45 minutes, peel off the top sheet, and bake 15 minutes more until crisp. Snap into shards. Beat 250g cream cheese with chopped chives, dill, lemon zest and pepper.

Extra hot chilli burger

Cheddar beef burger under beef chilli and jalapeños, spiked for Mental, mild for you.

Prep 14 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead, chilled; shape the patties up to 1 day ahead and cook on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 200g tinned tomatoes, chopped
  • 100ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 80g cheddar, sliced
  • 4 burger buns
  • 40g pickled jalapenos
  • 40ml hot sauce
  1. Brown a portion of the mince with the onion in the oil.
  2. Add the chilli powder, cumin, tomatoes and water, simmer until thick and season.
  3. Shape the remaining mince into four patties.
  4. Griddle over medium-high heat until cooked through (75°C at the centre) and top with cheddar to melt.
  5. Toast the buns and fill with patties, chilli and jalapenos.
  6. Put the hot sauce on the table for anyone wanting the extra-hot version.

Aspen room service surf and turf

Lobster tail and steak with the works, on someone else's money.

How it is made
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 210g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 4 small sirloin steaks
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 bottle tarragon vinegar
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  1. Grilled lobster tails in garlic parsley butter beside seared sirloin, with crunchy double-cooked chips and a bowl of béarnaise to share. Cut 800g potatoes into chips, fry at 140°C for 6 minutes, then at 190°C for 3 minutes. Split 4 lobster tails and brush with 60g butter melted with 2 garlic cloves and parsley, then grill flesh side up 6 to 8 minutes. Season 4 small sirloin steaks and sear 3 minutes a side, then rest. Whisk 3 egg yolks with 1 tbsp tarragon vinegar over simmering water, then slowly whisk in 150g melted butter and chopped tarragon. Serve together.

Sweets

Aspen snowflake cookiesVegetarian

Vanilla sugar cookies in wintry shapes, iced white, silver cachous, for the trip to the Rockies.

Prep 18 min, cook 18 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Biscuits keep 3 days in a tin. Ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 130g plain flour
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 2 tsp water
  • 10g edible silver cachous
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the yolk and vanilla.
  2. Mix in the flour and chill.
  3. Roll 5mm thick and cut into eight snowflakes.
  4. Bake at 180°C until pale gold at the edges.
  5. Cool completely.
  6. Mix the icing sugar with the water to a spreadable icing, coat the cookies and decorate with cachous.

Ski lodge hot chocolateNo alcohol

Dark couverture melted into hot milk, whisked frothy, marshmallows, after the chairlift stops.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the hot chocolate up to 2 days ahead, chilled, and reheat gently; whisk until frothy and top with cream and marshmallows
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 40g marshmallows
  1. Heat the milk until steaming without boiling.
  2. Add the chocolate and whisk until melted and frothy.
  3. Divide among four mugs.
  4. Softly whip the cream, spoon on top and finish with marshmallows.
