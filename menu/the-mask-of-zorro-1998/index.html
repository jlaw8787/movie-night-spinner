Snacks

Chorizo, Manchego and quince tapas

Red wine chorizo, Manchego with quince paste, warm olives with orange peel

Prep 20 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Slice the chorizo and cut the Manchego up to 1 day ahead, chilled; fry the chorizo and warm the olives on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chorizo
  • 100ml red wine
  • 200g Manchego
  • 1 small tub quince paste
  • 300g olives
  • 1 orange
  1. Slice the chorizo and fry until crisp.
  2. Add a splash of red wine and cook until sticky.
  3. Cut the Manchego into triangles and top each with a sliver of quince paste.
  4. Warm the olives with strips of orange peel.
  5. Serve everything on small plates.

Z-slash quesadillasVegetarian

Monterey Jack and roasted green chilli quesadillas with a Z scored on top

Prep 20 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Roast the chillies and grate the cheese up to 1 day ahead, chilled; fill and cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 250g Monterey Jack, grated
  • 2 green chillies, roasted
  • 40g butter
  1. Roast and chop the green chillies and grate the cheese.
  2. Fill four tortillas with the Monterey Jack and chilli and top with the remaining tortillas, or fold in half.
  3. Cook in butter over medium heat until golden on both sides and melted inside.
  4. Score a sharp Z into the top with a knife and cut into wedges.

Mains

Don Diego's rancho carne asada

Lime and orange marinated skirt steak, sliced thin, with rice and black beans

Prep 20 min, cook 145 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400g can black beans
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1/2 red onion
  1. Mix lime and orange juice with crushed garlic and cumin and coat the steak.
  2. Marinate for 2 hours.
  3. Cook the rice and warm the beans.
  4. Chop the tomatoes and onion for a fresh salsa.
  5. Grill the steak over very high heat for 3 minutes a side.
  6. Rest, then slice thinly against the grain.

Sweets

Churros with chocolateVegetarian

Cinnamon sugar churros with thick hot chocolate for dipping

Prep 30 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; the churros are best mixed and fried just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 250ml milk
  1. Bring the water and butter to the boil and beat in the flour until a smooth ball forms.
  2. Beat in the eggs.
  3. Pipe lengths into 180C oil and fry in batches until golden.
  4. Roll the churros in cinnamon sugar.
  5. Melt the chocolate into hot milk until thick and serve in small cups for dipping.

Drinks

Elena's flamenco sangriaVegetarian

Red wine, brandy, orange and apple chilled in a jug and topped with soda

Prep 15 min, cook 114 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled, add soda just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bottle red wine
  • 60ml brandy
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 apple
  • 1l soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Slice the oranges and apple into a large jug.
  2. Pour in the red wine, brandy and orange juice.
  3. Chill for 2 hours so the fruit soaks it up.
  4. Top with cold soda water just before pouring into glasses over ice.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Alejandro and Elena dance at Don Rafael's party.
