Snacks

Chorizo, Manchego and quince tapas

Red wine chorizo, Manchego with quince paste, warm olives with orange peel.

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the chorizo and cut the Manchego up to 1 day ahead, chilled; fry the chorizo and warm the olives on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chorizo
  • 100ml red wine
  • 200g Manchego
  • 1 small tub quince paste
  • 300g olives
  • 1 orange
  1. Slice the chorizo and fry until crisp.
  2. Add a splash of red wine and cook until sticky.
  3. Cut the Manchego into triangles and top each with a sliver of quince paste.
  4. Warm the olives with strips of orange peel, 5 min.
  5. Serve everything on small plates.

Z-slash quesadillasVegetarian

Monterey Jack and roasted green chilli quesadillas with a Z scored on top.

Prep 6 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Roast the chillies and grate the cheese up to 1 day ahead, chilled; fill and cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 250g Monterey Jack, grated
  • 2 green chillies, roasted
  • 40g butter
  1. Roast and chop the green chillies and grate the cheese, 8 min.
  2. Fill four tortillas with the Monterey Jack and chilli and top with the remaining tortillas, or fold in half.
  3. Cook in butter over medium heat until golden on both sides and melted inside.
  4. Score a sharp Z into the top with a knife and cut into wedges.

Red chilli chicken tamales

Light masa and spicy shredded chicken steamed firm in corn husks, a hacienda feast.

How it is made
  • 16 dried corn husks
  • 500g chicken thighs
  • 250ml red enchilada sauce
  • 150g lard
  • 450g masa harina
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 jar salsa
  1. Masa beaten light with lard and stock, spread on soaked corn husks, filled with chicken in red chilli sauce, folded and steamed until firm. Soak 16 dried corn husks in hot water for 30 minutes. Poach 500g chicken thighs, shred and mix with 250ml red enchilada sauce. Beat 150g lard until fluffy, then beat in 450g masa harina, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp salt and 450ml warm chicken stock. Spread 3 tbsp masa on each husk, add a spoon of chicken, fold and stand upright in a steamer. Steam covered for 75 minutes and serve with salsa.

Mains

Don Diego's rancho carne asada

Lime and orange marinated skirt steak, sliced thin, with rice and black beans.

Prep 23 min, cook 22 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400g can black beans
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1/2 red onion
  1. Mix lime and orange juice with crushed garlic and cumin and coat the steak.
  2. Marinate for 2 hours.
  3. Cook the rice and warm the beans, 15 min.
  4. Chop the tomatoes and onion for a fresh salsa.
  5. Grill the steak over very high heat for 3 minutes a side.
  6. Rest, then slice thinly against the grain, 8 min.

Governor's chicken with olives and capers

Chicken braised with white wine, green olives and capers, the dinner offered to the governor.

How it is made
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bottle white wine
  • 400g can diced tomatoes
  • 100g green olives
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Chicken thighs browned crisp, then braised with garlic, white wine, tomato, green olives and capers until tender, served with crusty bread to mop the sauce. Season 8 bone-in chicken thighs and brown skin side down in olive oil for 8 minutes. Remove, soften 4 sliced garlic cloves and a sliced onion, then add 200ml white wine and a 400g can of tomatoes. Stir in 100g green olives and 2 tbsp capers, return the chicken skin side up and braise uncovered at 170°C for 40 minutes. Scatter with parsley and serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

Churros with chocolateVegetarian

Cinnamon sugar churros with thick hot chocolate for dipping.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; the churros are best mixed and fried just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 250ml milk
  1. Bring the water and butter to the boil and beat in the flour until a smooth ball forms.
  2. Beat in the eggs.
  3. Pipe lengths into 180°C oil and fry in batches until golden.
  4. Roll the churros in cinnamon sugar.
  5. Melt the chocolate into hot milk until thick and serve in small cups for dipping.

Drinks

Elena's flamenco sangria

Red wine, brandy, orange and apple chilled in a jug and topped with soda.

Prep 9 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled, add soda just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bottle red wine
  • 60ml brandy
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 apple
  • 1 L soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Slice the oranges and apple into a large jug.
  2. Pour in the red wine, brandy and orange juice.
  3. Chill for 2 hours so the fruit soaks it up.
  4. Top with cold soda water just before pouring into glasses over ice.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Alejandro and Elena dance at Don Rafael's party.
