Snacks

Homemade Scooby SnacksVegetarian

Cheese and oat biscuits baked crisp. Mary Jane says they're 100% vegetarian.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 5 days ahead and keep in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g rolled oats
  • 100g cold butter, cubed
  • 120g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Pulse the flour, oats, butter, cheese and paprika in a food processor until it looks like breadcrumbs.
  2. Add the yolk and water and pulse until it comes together, then wrap and chill for 10 minutes.
  3. Roll out 5mm thick and cut into bone shapes.
  4. Bake on a lined tray at 180C until golden, then cool on the tray.

Hot pepper challenge pizza bitesVegetarian

Pizza bites with a hot pepper topping. Scooby eats the whole jar of peppers.

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead; top and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 small pita breads or pizza bases
  • 125ml passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 12 cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 100g mild pickled banana peppers
  • 2 tbsp basil leaves
  1. Stir the passata with the oregano and a pinch of salt.
  2. Spread over the bases and top with mozzarella and tomatoes.
  3. Bake at 220C until bubbling and crisp, then cut into wedges.
  4. Scatter with basil and serve the peppers in a jar on the side for the brave.

Mains

All-you-can-eat huli huli chicken with pineapple rice

Pineapple soy chicken grilled huli huli with pineapple rice. Spooky Island's all-you-can-eat deal.

Prep 10 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 125ml pineapple juice
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 270ml tin coconut milk
  • 225g tin pineapple pieces, drained
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Whisk the pineapple juice, soy, tomato sauce, sugar, ginger and garlic, keep a third aside and marinate the chicken in the rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Cook the rice in the coconut milk topped up with water until fluffy, then fold through the pineapple.
  3. Grill the chicken over medium heat, turning and basting with the kept sauce, until sticky and cooked through.
  4. Slice and serve over the rice with spring onions.

Unmasked Scrappy-Doo meatball subs

Meatball subs in tomato sauce with cheese. Mondavarious is really Scrappy-Doo.

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Roll and simmer the meatballs up to 2 days ahead; reheat and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 40g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 700g jar tomato pasta sauce
  • 4 long soft rolls
  • 150g mozzarella, sliced
  1. Mix the mince, crumbs, egg, parmesan, oregano and salt and roll into 16 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil, then pour in the sauce and simmer until cooked through.
  3. Split the rolls, fill with meatballs and sauce and lay the mozzarella on top.
  4. Grill until the cheese melts and serve hot.

Sweets

Disco ball coconut snowballsVegetarian

Toasted coconut snowballs. Daphne bounces the sun off a disco ball.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Roll the snowballs up to 3 days ahead and keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 150g shredded coconut
  • 1 ripe mango, chopped
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime
  1. Toast the coconut in a dry pan until golden, then cool.
  2. Scoop 8 big balls of ice cream, roll in the coconut and freeze on a lined tray.
  3. Blend the mango with the honey and a squeeze of lime.
  4. Serve two snowballs each in a puddle of mango sauce.

Jinkies Mystery Machine punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Orange, pineapple and passionfruit punch. Scrappy gets kicked out of the van.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices up to a day ahead and chill; add soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 2 passionfruit
  • 500ml lemonade, chilled
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Mix the orange and pineapple juice with the passionfruit pulp in a big jug.
  2. Add the ice and orange slices and top with lemonade just before serving.
