Coleslaw king pulled chicken sliders

Smoky pulled chicken under creamy coleslaw. Rob crowns himself coleslaw king.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cook and pull the chicken up to 2 days ahead; make the slaw up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 tbsp smoky barbecue sauce
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 8 soft slider rolls
  1. Rub the chicken with paprika and salt and pan-fry until cooked through.
  2. Shred with two forks and toss with the barbecue sauce.
  3. Toss both cabbages and the carrot with the mayo and vinegar.
  4. Fill the rolls with chicken and a big pile of slaw.

Not-Josie-Grossie mini corn dogs

Cornmeal-battered mini franks with mustard.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry batter up to a day ahead; add the wet and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 12 cocktail frankfurts
  • 120g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp American mustard
  • 12 wooden skewers
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt, then the egg and milk, to a thick batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push a skewer into each.
  3. Dip in the batter and fry at 180°C until deep golden.
  4. Drain and serve hot with mustard.

Sun-Times deep dish pizza

Chicago deep dish with sausage and mozzarella. Undercover for the Sun-Times.

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the dough and sauce the day before and chill; build and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g Italian pork sausages, skins removed
  • 350g mozzarella, sliced
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic, oregano and salt until thick.
  2. Oil a deep 25cm cake tin and press the dough up the sides.
  3. Layer the mozzarella on the base, then pinches of sausage, then the sauce, and finish with parmesan.
  4. Bake at 220°C on the lowest shelf until the crust is golden and the filling bubbles.

The Court bonfire barbecue ribs

Sticky pork ribs with charred corn. Aldys sizes up the fire in a trash can.

Prep 10 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Bake the ribs in foil up to 2 days ahead; glaze and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork spare ribs
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 200ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  1. Rub the ribs with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Wrap in foil and bake at 150°C until tender.
  3. Mix the barbecue sauce with the honey, brush over the ribs and grill hot until sticky and charred.
  4. Char the corn on the grill, brush with butter and serve with the ribs.

Who-ate-my-entire-pie cherry pieVegetarian

Lattice cherry pie with vanilla ice cream.

Prep 30 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to a day ahead and warm through to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 x 680g jars pitted morello cherries, drained
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Line a pie dish with one sheet of pastry.
  2. Toss the cherries with the sugar, cornflour and lemon zest and pile in.
  3. Cut the second sheet into strips, weave a lattice over the top and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling, then rest.
  5. Serve warm with ice cream.

Prom queen pink lemonadeNo alcohol

Raspberry pink lemonade with mint over ice.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the raspberry syrup up to 3 days ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 lemons
  • 750ml soda water
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Simmer the raspberries, sugar and water until syrupy, then strain and cool.
  2. Squeeze the lemons into a jug with the syrup.
  3. Fill glasses with ice and mint, pour in the pink base and top with soda.
