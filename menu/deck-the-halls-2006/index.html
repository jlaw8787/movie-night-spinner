Christmas lights caprese skewersVegetarian

Tomato and bocconcini skewers, strung like Buddy's lights.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Make the basil oil up to 2 days ahead, chilled; thread the skewers up to 4 hours ahead, covered, and drizzle on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 12 baby bocconcini, halved
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bottle balsamic glaze
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Blitz some basil leaves with the oil and a pinch of salt, then strain.
  2. Halve the bocconcini.
  3. Thread skewers with a cherry tomato, a basil leaf and a bocconcini half, then another tomato.
  4. Lay them in rows on a long platter like strings of lights. Drizzle with basil oil and balsamic glaze and finish with cracked pepper and sea salt.

Atomic Warlord cranberry chilli meatballs

Baked beef and pork meatballs glazed in cranberry sweet chilli sauce.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bake and toss in the glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 200g pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 200g cranberry sauce
  • 3 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Mix the minces with the egg, breadcrumbs, garlic and salt.
  2. Roll into small balls and bake on a lined tray at 200°C.
  3. Simmer the cranberry sauce, sweet chilli, soy and lemon juice until glossy.
  4. Toss the meatballs through, scatter with spring onion and serve on toothpicks.

Mains

Feud's-over turkey with cranberry glaze

Roast turkey with orange cranberry glaze, cooked by both neighbours together.

Prep 15 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Rub the turkey with butter and salt and mix the stuffing up to 1 day ahead, chilled; roast, glaze and cook the potatoes on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 90g butter
  • 150g cranberry sauce
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 small bunch sage, chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g green beans
  1. Rub the turkey with some of the butter and salt.
  2. Roast at 180°C.
  3. Simmer the cranberry sauce with the orange juice and honey and brush over the turkey for the last part of the roasting.
  4. Fry the onion, mix with the breadcrumbs, sage, egg and melted butter and bake in a dish. Roast the potatoes in oil alongside.
  5. Toss the beans in butter and serve.

Steve Finch's perfect roast potatoesVegetarian

Par-boiled, roughed up, roasted crisp. Planned to the minute.

Prep 3 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Par-boil and rough up the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, keep uncovered in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 3 sprigs rosemary
  1. Heat the oil in a roasting tin at 220°C.
  2. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 8 minutes, drain and shake in the pot to rough up the edges.
  3. Tip into the hot oil, turn to coat and roast, turning once, until deep golden.
  4. Add the garlic and rosemary for the last 10 minutes, then salt well.

Cul-de-sac Christmas trifle

Sponge, red jelly, custard and whipped cream layered in a bowl.

Prep 18 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet strawberry jelly crystals
  • 300g sponge cake
  • 100ml orange juice
  • 250g mixed berries
  • 600ml thick vanilla custard
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 40g crushed honeycomb
  1. Make up the jelly with boiling water and cool.
  2. Break the sponge into a glass bowl, drizzle with orange juice and add the berries. Pour over the jelly.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Spoon over the custard.
  5. Whip the cream with the icing sugar and spread on top. Scatter with honeycomb just before serving.

Neighbourly vanilla eggnogNo alcohol

Chilled vanilla nog with nutmeg, for the WinterFest skating race.

Prep 4 min, cook 11 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs (yolks only)
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 700ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar.
  2. Heat the milk, cream, cinnamon and vanilla until steaming, then slowly whisk into the yolks.
  3. Return to the pan and stir over low heat until it coats a spoon.
  4. Strain and chill.
  5. Pour into glasses and grate fresh nutmeg over the top.
