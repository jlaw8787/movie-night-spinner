Snacks

Klendathu bug leg crab claws

The drop on Klendathu goes badly

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead; steam the claws to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg cooked crab claws
  • 100g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 baguette
  1. Crack the claws with the back of a heavy knife so the shell opens.
  2. Melt the butter with the garlic and chilli until fragrant.
  3. Steam or warm the claws in a covered pan with a splash of water.
  4. Pour over the garlic butter, scatter with parsley and serve with lemon and bread.

Buenos Aires beef empanadas

Rico is from Buenos Aires

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Fill and crimp up to a day ahead and chill on the tray; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 1/2 cup green olives, chopped
  • 3 sheets frozen shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup parsley leaves
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 80ml olive oil
  1. Brown the mince, add the onion, capsicum and spices and cook until soft, then cool and fold in the eggs and olives.
  2. Cut 12cm rounds from the pastry, fill, fold and crimp the edges.
  3. Brush with egg and bake at 200C until golden.
  4. Chop the parsley with a pinch of salt and stir in the vinegar and oil for chimichurri.

Captured Brain Bug roast cauliflowerVegetarian

Zim captures the Brain Bug

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the tahini sauce up to 3 days ahead; roast the cauliflower on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 large whole cauliflower, leaves trimmed
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 80g tahini
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1/2 pomegranate, seeds only
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 300g couscous
  1. Boil the whole cauliflower for 6 minutes, then drain and dry.
  2. Rub with the oil, paprika, cumin and salt and roast at 220C until deep golden.
  3. Whisk the tahini with lemon juice, garlic and enough water to pour.
  4. Pour boiling water over the couscous, cover for 5 minutes and fluff.
  5. Pour the tahini over the cauliflower, scatter with pomegranate and mint and carve at the table.

Roughnecks beer-braised short ribs

Come on you apes, live forever

Prep 15 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Braise the ribs up to 2 days ahead and chill in the liquid; reheat and glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.6kg beef short ribs
  • 2 brown onions, cut in wedges
  • 375ml dark lager
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 125ml milk
  1. Brown the ribs well in a heavy pot, then lift out and colour the onions.
  2. Add the beer, stock, tomato paste and sugar, return the ribs, cover and braise at 160C.
  3. Boil and mash the potatoes with the butter and milk.
  4. Reduce the braising liquid to a glaze and spoon it over the ribs to serve with the mash.

Service Guarantees Citizenship alfajoresVegetarian

Service guarantees citizenship

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 32 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the biscuits up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight; fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 150g cornflour
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 380g tin caramel (dulce de leche)
  • 40g desiccated coconut
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then the yolks and zest, and mix in the flours and baking powder to a soft dough.
  2. Roll 5mm thick, cut 5cm rounds and bake at 170C until pale and set.
  3. Cool, sandwich pairs with a thick layer of caramel and roll the edges in coconut.
