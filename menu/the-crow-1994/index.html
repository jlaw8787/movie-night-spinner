Snacks

Devil's Night popcornVegetarian

Buttered popcorn with black salt and a pinch of chilli flakes

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best popped and tossed just before serving; melt the butter ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1/2 tsp black salt
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pot and pop the corn, shaking often.
  2. Toss with the melted butter, black salt and chilli flakes.
  3. Serve in dark bowls by candlelight.

Ham and cheddar toasted sandwiches

Buttered white bread filled with ham and cheddar, pan toasted

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Fill the sandwiches up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 200g leg ham, sliced
  1. Butter the bread on the outside and fill four sandwiches with cheddar and ham.
  2. Cook in a pan over medium heat, pressing down, until golden and melting.
  3. Cut each into long soldiers and serve hot.

Detroit coney dog bitesOn the printed menu

Cocktail frankfurts in soft little buns under spiced beef chilli, yellow mustard and diced onion

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 16 cocktail frankfurts
  • 16 small soft rolls
  • 1 jar mustard
  1. Cocktail franks in soft little buns under a spiced beef chilli, with yellow mustard and finely diced white onion, Detroit style. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 finely diced onion, breaking it up well. Add 1 tbsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 200ml water, and simmer 20 minutes until thick and saucy. Simmer 16 cocktail frankfurts in water for 5 minutes. Tuck into 16 small soft rolls, spoon over the chilli and top with mustard and diced onion.

Mains

Albrecht's diner meatloaf

Beef meatloaf with a brown sugar tomato glaze

Prep 13 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  1. Mix the mince with the onion, breadcrumbs, egg and salt and press into a loaf tin.
  2. Spread with tomato sauce mixed with brown sugar.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter, and make a gravy from the stock and flour.
  5. Serve thick slices with mash and gravy.

Darla's bacon and egg breakfast plateOn the printed menu

Bacon, fried eggs and buttered toast on one plate. Darla finally cooking breakfast for her daughter.

How it is made
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 loaf thick white bread
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Crisp bacon, sunny side up eggs, golden potato hash browns and thick buttered toast, a proper diner-style plate cooked with care. Grate 800g potatoes, squeeze very dry in a tea towel and mix with salt and 1 grated onion. Fry 8 flat cakes in oil and butter over medium heat for 5 minutes a side until crisp. Fry 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Fry 4 eggs sunny side up in butter. Toast 4 thick slices of bread and butter well. Plate it all up with tomato sauce on the side.

Devil's Night blackberry crumbleVegetarian

Blackberries under an oat and brown sugar crumble

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen blackberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 100g cold butter, cubed
  • 100g plain flour
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the blackberries with the sugar and cornflour in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, stir in the oats and brown sugar and scatter over the fruit.
  3. Bake at 180°C until golden and bubbling at the edges.
  4. Rest, then serve hot with ice cream.

Drinks

Rainy night Irish coffeeVegetarian

Hot coffee, Irish whiskey and brown sugar

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh and hot; whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead, kept chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 tsp brown sugar
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  • 600ml hot strong coffee
  • 150ml cream
  1. Warm the heatproof glasses with hot water and tip it out.
  2. Add brown sugar and whiskey to each, pour in hot coffee and stir until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Lightly whip the cream until just thickened but still pourable.
  4. Pour slowly over the back of a spoon so it floats on top. For anyone not drinking, make hot chocolate instead.
