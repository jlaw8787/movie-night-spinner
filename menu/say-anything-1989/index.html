Snacks

Graduation party pigs in a blanket

Fennel pork sausages wrapped in puff pastry.

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Assemble a day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sausage mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 30g breadcrumbs
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 tbsp tomato relish
  • pepper, to taste
  1. Mix the sausage mince with onion, fennel, breadcrumbs and pepper.
  2. Halve the pastry sheets, lay a log of filling along each strip and roll up, sealing with beaten egg.
  3. Cut each into 4, brush with egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden and puffed, 25 min.
  5. Serve hot with tomato relish.

Seattle smoked salmon bites

Hot-smoked salmon cream cheese with dill and capers on rye crispbread.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Flake the salmon and mix the cream cheese spread up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; spread on the crispbread just before serving so it stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 200g hot-smoked salmon
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 packet rye crispbread
  1. Flake the salmon.
  2. Fold through the cream cheese with chopped dill, lemon zest and capers.
  3. Spread thickly on rye crispbread.
  4. Finish each with a squeeze of lemon.

Sweet and salty driving lesson snack mix

Pretzels, peanuts and cereal baked in a Worcestershire butter, for the stick shift driving lesson.

How it is made
  • 80g butter
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 150g mini pretzels
  • 150g roasted peanuts
  • 150g wheat cereal squares
  • 100g chocolate drops
  1. Pretzels, peanuts and cereal squares baked in a Worcestershire, garlic and brown sugar butter until crunchy, tossed with chocolate drops once cool. Melt 80g butter with 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp garlic powder and half a tsp smoked paprika. Toss with 150g mini pretzels, 150g roasted peanuts and 150g wheat cereal squares in a large tray. Bake at 140°C for 40 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes. Cool completely, then toss through 100g chocolate drops. Tip into paper bags for the car.

Jim Court's Dijon crusted salmon

Salmon baked under a Dijon and breadcrumb crust, a careful dinner with Diane's dad.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Crumb the salmon up to 4 hours ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; roast the fish and cook the potatoes and beans to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • 800g potatoes
  • 60g butter, divided
  • 300g green beans
  • salt, to taste
  1. Spread the salmon with Dijon and press on breadcrumbs mixed with parsley and lemon zest.
  2. Roast at 220°C, 10 min.
  3. Boil the potatoes, then crush with butter and salt, 20 min.
  4. Steam the green beans and toss with butter, 5 min.

Seattle teriyaki chicken rice bowls

Thighs in a sticky soy, mirin and ginger glaze with sesame slaw and pickled ginger, Seattle style.

How it is made
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • 1 bottle mirin
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 0.25 cabbage
  • 1 bottle rice vinegar
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  1. Chicken thighs grilled in a sticky soy, mirin, ginger and garlic glaze, sliced over steamed rice with a sesame cabbage salad and pickled ginger. Simmer 100ml soy sauce, 60ml mirin, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp grated ginger and 2 crushed garlic cloves for 5 minutes until syrupy. Cook 300g jasmine rice. Grill or pan-fry 800g chicken thighs for 5 minutes a side, brushing with the glaze for the last few minutes until sticky. Toss shredded cabbage with rice vinegar and sesame oil. Slice the chicken over the rice with the salad, sesame seeds and pickled ginger.

Sweets

Dare-to-be-great summer berry crumbleVegetarian

Mixed summer berries baked jammy under a buttery oat crumble, cream on the side.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Rub the crumble topping together up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; toss the berries and bake on the day, then serve warm with ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 500g mixed berries
  • 130g caster sugar, divided
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g butter
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the berries with some of the sugar and a squeeze of lemon and spread in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the oats, flour and remaining sugar and scatter over.
  3. Bake at 180°C until bubbling, 35 min.
  4. Serve with vanilla ice cream.

Drinks

Boombox Seattle mocha with whipped creamNo alcohol

Espresso and hot chocolate milk, cream on top, held high at dawn.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; make the mocha fresh so it stays hot
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml very strong coffee or 4 espresso shots
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 120g dark chocolate, divided
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the strong coffee.
  2. Warm the milk with chopped chocolate and sugar, whisking until melted and frothy, without boiling, 5 min.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Divide the coffee between four big mugs and pour over the chocolate milk.
  5. Top with whipped cream and shave a little chocolate over the top.
