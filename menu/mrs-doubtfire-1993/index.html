Snacks

Restaurant garlic shrimp

Shrimp tossed in garlic and chilli butter with stock and lemon.

Prep 14 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Serve straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 600g green prawns
  • 60ml chicken stock
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Peel the prawns and slice the garlic.
  2. Melt the butter until foaming, add the garlic and chilli and cook briefly.
  3. Add the prawns and toss over high heat until just pink.
  4. Add the stock and lemon juice and bubble briefly. Finish with chopped parsley and serve with crusty bread.

Euphegenia's tea sandwichesVegetarian

Egg and cress, and dill cream cheese with cucumber, crusts off, cut in fingers.

Prep 22 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch watercress
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 12 slices of white bread
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool and peel, 13 min.
  2. Mash the eggs with the mayonnaise and chopped watercress. Mix the cream cheese with dill.
  3. Spread the bread with the cream cheese, layer with egg and thin cucumber, and close up.
  4. Cut off the crusts and cut into fingers.

San Francisco sourdough fresh mozzarella bites

Garlic-rubbed toasts, torn fresh mozzarella, balsamic tomatoes and basil, help is on the way dear.

How it is made
  • 1 sourdough baguette
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bottle balsamic vinegar
  • 2 fresh mozzarella
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Garlic-rubbed sourdough toasts topped with torn fresh mozzarella, balsamic cherry tomatoes and basil, a crack of pepper and a drizzle of good olive oil. Slice 1 sourdough baguette into 16 pieces, brush with oil and grill or toast until golden, then rub with a cut garlic clove. Halve 250g cherry tomatoes and toss with 1 tbsp balsamic, olive oil and salt. Tear 2 fresh mozzarella over the toasts, spoon on the tomatoes and finish with basil, pepper and a drizzle of oil.

Mains

Shepherd's pie, no questions asked

Lamb and carrot pie in Worcestershire gravy under baked buttery mash.

Prep 15 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble then bake
Recipe for 4
  • 750g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250ml water
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  1. Dice the onion and carrots. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  2. Brown the mince, add the onion and carrot, then the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and water. Simmer, 40 min.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and milk, 20 min.
  4. Top the mince with the mash and bake at 200°C until golden.

Miranda's birthday steak, no cayenne

Grilled rump with chips and a mild pepper sauce, no cayenne.

How it is made
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 150ml cream
  1. Sirloin seared hard and basted with butter and thyme, sliced over crispy potatoes with a peppercorn sauce, spice kept well off the plate. Salt 4 sirloin steaks the night before. Roast 1kg diced potatoes in oil at 220°C for 40 minutes. Sear the steaks in a smoking pan for 3 minutes a side, add 40g butter and thyme and baste for 1 minute, then rest 5 minutes. In the pan cook 1 shallot with 1 tbsp crushed peppercorns, add 150ml beef stock and 150ml cream and simmer until thick.

Sweets

Frosting face cakeVegetarian

Sponge layers with mascarpone cream, piled high with fresh strawberries.

Prep 35 min, cook 25 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 230g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 250g mascarpone
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 500g strawberries
  1. Beat the butter with most of the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175°C, 25 min.
  3. Cool the cakes, 40 min.
  4. Whip the mascarpone with the cream and remaining sugar.
  5. Fill and cover the cake, then pile on halved strawberries.

A proper cuppaNo alcohol

Black tea brewed in a warmed pot, milk and sugar on the side.

Prep 4 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Best brewed just before drinking; set out the cups, milk and sugar up to 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tea bags of English breakfast tea
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • caster sugar, to serve
  1. Warm the teapot with hot water and tip it out.
  2. Add the tea bags and freshly boiled water and brew.
  3. Pour into four cups with milk, and sugar on the side.

When to serve
