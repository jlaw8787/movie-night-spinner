Valentine's night gruyère heart toastsVegetarian

Heart-shaped sourdough toasts with tomato chutney and melted gruyère

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cut the hearts up to 1 day ahead, airtight; butter, top and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 2 tsp tomato chutney
  • 150g gruyere, grated
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  1. Cut hearts from the sourdough with a heart cutter.
  2. Brush both sides with butter and toast under the grill.
  3. Spread with chutney, top with gruyere and thyme.
  4. Grill until the cheese is melted and bubbling with brown spots. Finish with cracked pepper and serve hot.

Firecracker smoked paprika cornVegetarian

Grilled corn with smoked paprika chilli butter

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mash the paprika butter up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled; grill the corn just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 4 corn cobs, husked
  • 80g feta, finely crumbled
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 1 lime
  1. Mash the butter with the paprika, chilli, garlic and a pinch of salt.
  2. Grill the corn over high heat, turning, until charred in places.
  3. Brush the hot cobs with the butter and roll in the feta.
  4. Scatter with coriander and serve with lime wedges.

Mains

Calendar-hopping turkey dinner

Sage thyme butter turkey, mash, green beans and cranberry sauce

Prep 35 min, cook 106 min · Make ahead: Push the herb butter under the skin up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; roast and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg turkey breast
  • 140g butter
  • 1 small bunch sage
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Mash some of the butter with chopped sage, thyme and one garlic clove and push it under the skin of the turkey.
  2. Roast at 180C.
  3. Rest the turkey.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with some of the butter and the milk.
  5. Whisk the flour into the pan juices, add the stock and simmer to a gravy.
  6. Blanch the beans and toss with garlic butter. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Easter Parade honey glazed ham with spring greens

Clove-studded leg brushed with mustard and sugar, new potatoes, asparagus, for Jim and Linda

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the glaze up to 3 days ahead; the ham is also good cold the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg half leg ham, on the bone
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp whole cloves
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 40g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Score the ham skin in diamonds, stud with cloves and brush with the honey, mustard and sugar.
  2. Bake, basting a few times, until glossy and hot through.
  3. Boil the potatoes until tender.
  4. Cook the asparagus and peas and toss everything with butter. Slice the ham to serve.

Christmas plum pudding with orange custardVegetarian

Steamed Christmas pudding with warm vanilla and orange custard

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Steam the pudding up to 2 days ahead and reheat to serve; make the orange custard up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and rewarm gently
Recipe for 4
  • 800g alcohol-free Christmas pudding
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Steam the pudding in its basin over simmering water, or follow the packet.
  2. Warm the milk and cream until steaming.
  3. Whisk the yolks with the sugar and cornflour, pour in the hot milk while whisking, then return to the pan.
  4. Stir over low heat until it thickens, then stir in the orange zest and vanilla.
  5. Turn out the pudding and pour over the custard.

White Christmas hot buttered apple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cloudy juice warmed with cinnamon, cloves and orange, topped with melting spiced sugar butter

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Simmer and strain the spiced apple juice up to 2 days ahead and mash the spiced butter up to 3 days ahead; reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 1 orange (peel in strips)
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp molasses
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  1. Simmer the apple juice with the cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and ginger without boiling.
  2. Mash the butter with the sugar, molasses, vanilla and nutmeg.
  3. Strain the punch into mugs and drop in a teaspoon of spiced butter to melt on top.

When to serve

Serve the main when the calendar turkey can't pick a Thursday for Thanksgiving.
