Galley bread rollsVegetarian

Soft milk rolls brushed with butter and flaky salt.

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight, warm through before serving; brush with butter and flaky salt on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 50g butter, softened, for the dough
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 20g butter, melted, for brushing
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, milk, butter, sugar and egg into a soft dough and knead.
  2. Prove until doubled.
  3. Shape into rolls.
  4. Prove again, 30 min.
  5. Bake at 190°C, 18 min.
  6. Brush with the melted butter and flaky salt.

Saffron rouille and shrimp crostini

Toasted bread with shrimp and saffron garlic rouille.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the rouille up to a day ahead and keep it in the fridge; poach the prawns and toast the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 garlic cloves, 1 crushed and 1 halved
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 150ml olive oil
  • 16 green prawns, peeled with tails on
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 fennel bulb with frond
  1. Soak the saffron in 1 tbsp hot water for 5 minutes.
  2. Whisk the egg yolk with the crushed garlic, saffron water, chilli and 1 tsp lemon juice, then whisk in the olive oil a few drops at a time until thick. Season with salt.
  3. Poach the prawns in simmering salted water for 2 minutes until just pink, then cool.
  4. Slice the baguette into 16 rounds and toast at 200°C for 6 minutes, then rub each with the cut garlic.
  5. Spread with rouille, top with a prawn, a few fennel fronds and lemon zest.

Ryback's spit-free bouillabaisse

Saffron fish and shrimp stew with rouille toast.

Prep 30 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the broth up to 2 days ahead and chill; reheat, then add the fish, mussels and prawns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 leek
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 2 strips of orange peel
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
  • 1 L fish stock
  • 500g firm white fish fillets
  • 500g mussels
  • 300g prawns
  • 2 garlic cloves, for the rouille
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 125ml olive oil, for the rouille
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 baguette
  1. Chop the fennel, leek, onion and garlic.
  2. Soften in oil with the saffron, orange peel and tomatoes.
  3. Add the fish stock and simmer, 20 min.
  4. Add the fish, then the mussels and prawns, and cook until the shells open.
  5. For the rouille, crush the garlic with a pinch of salt, whisk in the egg yolk and a few saffron threads soaked in a spoon of the hot broth, then whisk in the olive oil drop by drop until thick. Finish with a squeeze of lemon.
  6. Toast sliced baguette and serve with the rouille, 5 min.

Navy galley glazed meatloaf and mash

Glazed meatloaf with mash and gravy, pulled hot from the galley ovens.

How it is made
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 180ml milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 onions
  • 1 egg
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with a sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, sliced thick over buttery mash with peas and onion gravy. Soak 60g breadcrumbs in 80ml milk. Mix with 500g beef mince, 300g pork mince, 1 grated onion, 1 egg, 2 tbsp Worcestershire and salt. Shape in a lined loaf tin. Mix 80ml tomato sauce with 2 tbsp brown sugar, spread on top and bake at 180°C for 1 hour. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml hot milk. Fry 1 sliced onion, stir in 1 tbsp flour and 300ml beef stock for gravy. Cook 300g frozen peas.

Captain's birthday cakeVegetarian

Two-layer vanilla butter cake with whipped buttercream.

Prep 25 min, cook 40 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 250g butter, softened, for icing
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1/4 tsp blue food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line two tins. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, 15 min.
  2. Bake, 25 min.
  3. Cool completely, 40 min.
  4. Beat the butter and icing sugar for the buttercream.
  5. Fill and cover the cake.
  6. Tint a little buttercream blue with the food colouring and pipe the message.

USS Missouri navy grog

Dark and white rum with lime, grapefruit and honey over crushed ice.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Dissolve the honey syrup up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; shake each round to order over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 90ml dark rum
  • 90ml white rum
  • 3 limes
  • 60ml grapefruit juice
  • 1 tray ice
  • 1 tray crushed ice
  • 1 whole nutmeg, to grate
  1. Dissolve the honey in the hot water.
  2. Shake both rums, lime juice, grapefruit juice and the honey syrup with ice.
  3. Strain into tumblers packed with crushed ice.
  4. Add a lime wheel to each and grate a little nutmeg on top.

When to serve

Serve dinner when Krill shuts Ryback in the walk-in fridge on the captain's birthday.
