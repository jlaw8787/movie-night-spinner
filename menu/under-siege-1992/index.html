Galley bread rollsVegetarian

Soft milk rolls brushed with butter and flaky salt

Prep 30 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight, warm through before serving; brush with butter and flaky salt on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 50g butter, softened, for the dough
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 20g butter, melted, for brushing
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, milk, butter, sugar and egg into a soft dough and knead.
  2. Prove until doubled.
  3. Shape into rolls.
  4. Prove again.
  5. Bake at 190C.
  6. Brush with the melted butter and flaky salt.

Saffron rouille and prawn crostini

A taste of the captain's birthday dinner

Prep 25 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the rouille up to a day ahead and keep it in the fridge; poach the prawns and toast the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 garlic cloves, 1 crushed and 1 halved
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 150ml olive oil
  • 16 green prawns, peeled with tails on
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 fennel bulb with fronds
  1. Soak the saffron in 1 tbsp hot water for 5 minutes.
  2. Whisk the egg yolk with the crushed garlic, saffron water, chilli and 1 tsp lemon juice, then whisk in the olive oil a few drops at a time until thick. Season with salt.
  3. Poach the prawns in simmering salted water for 2 minutes until just pink, then cool.
  4. Slice the baguette into 16 rounds and toast at 200°C for 6 minutes, then rub each with the cut garlic.
  5. Spread with rouille, top with a prawn, a few fennel fronds and lemon zest.

Ryback's spit-free bouillabaisse

Saffron fish and prawn stew with rouille toast. Fifty gallons, due tomorrow.

Prep 15 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the broth up to 2 days ahead and chill; reheat, then add the fish, mussels and prawns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 leek
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
  • 1l fish stock
  • 500g firm white fish fillets
  • 500g mussels
  • 300g prawns
  • 2 garlic cloves, for the rouille
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 125ml olive oil, for the rouille
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 baguette
  1. Chop the fennel, leek, onion and garlic.
  2. Soften in oil with the saffron, orange peel and tomatoes.
  3. Add the fish stock and simmer.
  4. Add the fish, then the mussels and prawns, and cook until the shells open.
  5. For the rouille, crush the garlic with a pinch of salt, whisk in the egg yolk and a few saffron threads soaked in a spoon of the hot broth, then whisk in the olive oil drop by drop until thick. Finish with a squeeze of lemon.
  6. Toast sliced baguette and serve with the rouille.

Captain's birthday cakeVegetarian

Two-layer butter cake. Jordan Tate came in a bigger one.

Prep 40 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 250g butter, softened, for icing
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1/4 tsp blue food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line two tins. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the butter and icing sugar for the buttercream.
  5. Fill and cover the cake.
  6. Tint a little buttercream blue with the food colouring and pipe the message.

USS Missouri navy grogVegetarian

Dark and white rum with lime, grapefruit and honey over crushed ice

Prep 8 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Dissolve the honey syrup up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; shake each round to order over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 90ml dark rum
  • 90ml white rum
  • 3 limes
  • 60ml grapefruit juice
  • 1 tray ice
  • 1 tray crushed ice
  • 1 whole nutmeg, to grate
  1. Dissolve the honey in the hot water.
  2. Shake both rums, lime juice, grapefruit juice and the honey syrup with ice.
  3. Strain into tumblers packed with crushed ice.
  4. Add a lime wheel to each and grate a little nutmeg on top.

When to serve

Serve dinner when Krill shuts Ryback in the walk-in fridge on the captain's birthday.
