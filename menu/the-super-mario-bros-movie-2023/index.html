Jump Man arcade garlic knotsVegetarian

Garlic knots, brushed with butter, garlic and parsley, baked soft.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Tie the knots up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ready-made pizza dough
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 250ml marinara sauce
  1. Roll the dough into 12 ropes and tie each one in a loose knot.
  2. Bake on a lined tray at 220°C until golden, 12 min.
  3. Stir the garlic and parsley into the melted butter and brush it over the hot knots.
  4. Scatter with parmesan and serve with the warmed marinara.

Fire Flower aranciniVegetarian

Mozzarella risotto balls fried crisp and red-hot, Fire Flower and Fire Peach.

Prep 40 min, cook 20 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Roll and crumb the arancini up to a day ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1 L vegetable stock
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, cut in small cubes
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 250ml passata
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Soften the onion in the butter, stir in the rice, then add the hot stock a ladle at a time until creamy and tender.
  2. Stir in the parmesan, spread on a tray and cool completely, 10 min.
  3. Roll into balls around a cube of mozzarella, then coat in flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in oil at 180°C until deep golden, 10 min.
  5. Simmer the passata with the chilli and serve as a dip, 10 min.

Red and green pepper Brooklyn pizzaVegetarian

Thin Brooklyn pizza, half red pepper, half green, mozzarella bubbling.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Slice the peppers and grate the cheese up to a day ahead; stretch and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 250g balls of pizza dough
  • 250ml pizza sauce
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 1 green capsicum, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 cup basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven as hot as it goes with a tray inside, 10 min.
  2. Stretch the dough into 2 large rounds and spread with sauce.
  3. Scatter with mozzarella, then red capsicum on one half and green on the other, and the oregano.
  4. Bake on the hot tray until blistered and bubbling, 12 min.
  5. Finish with basil and a drizzle of oil.

Foreman Spike's meatball heroes

Meatballs, red sauce and provolone on a hero roll, Spike's favourite failure.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead; reheat and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700ml passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 8 slices of provolone
  • 1 cup basil leaves
  1. Mix the mince, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan and half the garlic and roll into 16 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in a hot pan, then add the passata, oregano and remaining garlic and simmer until cooked through, 25 min.
  3. Split the rolls, pile in the meatballs and sauce and lay over the provolone.
  4. Grill until the cheese melts, then add basil and serve, 5 min.

Blue Mini Mushroom cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with blue frosting and white chocolate spots.

Prep 21 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep in a tin; ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g soft butter, plus 125g for the icing
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 60ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp blue food colouring gel
  • 50g white chocolate melts
  1. Beat 150g butter and the sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  2. Fold in the flour, milk and vanilla and spoon into 12 lined cupcake cases.
  3. Bake at 180°C until springy, then cool, 20 min.
  4. Beat the rest of the butter with the icing sugar and blue colouring, then dome it on each cupcake, 8 min.
  5. Press on white chocolate melts for the mushroom spots.

Bowser's Peaches iced teaNo alcohol

Cold peach iced tea with lemon and fresh mint over ice.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 400ml peach nectar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water, then stir in the honey and cool, 5 min.
  2. Mix the cold tea with the peach nectar.
  3. Pour over ice with lemon slices and mint.
