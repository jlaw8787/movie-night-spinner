Snacks

Dr Farthing's gambling debt wings

Baked hot sauce wings with blue cheese dip and celery.

Prep 4 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings with the baking powder and salt up to 1 day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge; bake, sauce and make the dip on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 50g butter
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch celery, cut into sticks
  1. Toss the wings with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Bake at 220°C, turning once.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through it.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream with a squeeze of lemon. Serve the wings hot with the dip and celery.

Mitch's job-a-week pretzel bitesVegetarian

Soft pretzel bites, for fourteen jobs in three months.

Prep 14 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the mustard and honey dip up to 3 days ahead; boil, glaze and bake the bites on the day, best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 L water
  • 60g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 3 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Cut the dough into small pieces.
  2. Simmer the water with the bicarbonate of soda, drop in the pieces briefly in batches and drain.
  3. Lay on a lined tray, brush with egg and sprinkle with sea salt.
  4. Bake at 220°C until deep brown.
  5. Stir the mustards and honey together for dipping and serve the bites warm.

Mains

Pops's diner patty melt

Patty, Swiss, fried onions on rye.

Prep 4 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and shape the patties up to 1 day ahead, chilled; cook and fry the sandwiches on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 60g butter
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  1. Slowly fry the onions in some of the butter.
  2. Shape the mince into four thin oval patties and cook on both sides.
  3. Layer each patty on buttered rye with Swiss cheese and onions.
  4. Fry the sandwiches until golden and melted.

Sam's barbecue chicken

Sticky grilled chicken thighs, buttered corn and slaw.

Prep 12 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken and make the sauce up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; shred the slaw up to 4 hours ahead and dress it just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thigh fillets
  • 250ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 40g butter
  • 1/4 green cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 100g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Rub the chicken with the oil, paprika and salt.
  2. Simmer the barbecue sauce with the vinegar and sugar for 5 minutes.
  3. Grill the chicken over medium heat 6 to 7 minutes a side, brushing with the sauce for the last few minutes until sticky and charred.
  4. Grill the corn, turning, until charred, and roll in the butter.
  5. Shred the cabbage, carrot and red onion and toss with the mayonnaise, a splash of vinegar and salt.

Opera night hot fudge peanut sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, hot fudge and peanuts. For opera night; skip the brownies.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge sauce up to 5 days ahead, chilled, and reheat gently; whip the cream and scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 30g butter
  • 120g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 80g salted roasted peanuts
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Heat some of the cream with the sugar and butter until it bubbles. Off the heat, stir in the chocolate and a pinch of salt until glossy.
  2. Roughly chop the peanuts. Whip the remaining cream.
  3. Scoop ice cream into sundae glasses and pour over the warm fudge.
  4. Top with whipped cream and peanuts and finish each with a cherry.

Drinks

Pops's rye and ginger highball

Rye and ginger ale over ice, for opening night.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the limes up to 1 day ahead; build over ice and top with ginger ale just before serving to keep the fizz
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml rye whiskey
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bottle aromatic bitters
  • 600ml cold ginger ale
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and pour the rye into each.
  2. Add a squeeze of lime and a couple of dashes of bitters.
  3. Top with ginger ale, stir once gently and garnish with a lime wedge.
