Snacks

Cocktail franks in puff pastry

Cocktail franks wrapped in puff pastry strips and baked

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cocktail franks
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  1. Simmer the franks briefly and drain well.
  2. Cut the pastry into strips and roll one around each frank.
  3. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200C until golden.
  4. Serve with mustard and tomato sauce.

Eighties cheese ballVegetarian

Cheddar, cream cheese and spring onion rolled in walnuts, with crackers

Prep 10 min, cook 2 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100g walnuts, chopped
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Mix the cheddar, cream cheese and sliced spring onion.
  2. Shape into one big ball and roll in the walnuts.
  3. Chill until firm.
  4. Serve with crackers.

Vegas flight honey peanut brittleOn the printed menu

Honey nut brittle, bagged for the Vegas flight where Billy Idol lends a hand.

How it is made
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 1 jar honey
  • 200g salted roasted peanuts
  • 20g butter
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  1. Salted roasted peanuts set in a golden honey toffee with a little butter and bicarb so it shatters, broken into shards and bagged like airline snacks. Line a tray with baking paper. Heat 200g caster sugar, 60ml honey and 60ml water in a saucepan, stirring until dissolved, then boil without stirring until light golden, about 8 minutes. Stir in 200g salted roasted peanuts and 20g butter and cook 1 to 2 minutes more until amber. Take off the heat, stir in half a teaspoon bicarb and pour onto the tray. Cool 30 minutes, then smash into shards and bag up.

Rosie's meatballs

Rosie's fee for singing lessons. Beef and pork in tomato sauce, over spaghetti.

Prep 23 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes (400 g each)
  • 500g spaghetti
  1. Mix the mince with the parmesan, parsley, breadcrumbs and egg. Roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer.
  4. Cook the spaghetti and serve with the meatballs on top.

Reception stuffed chicken and lemon butterOn the printed menu

Chicken breast filled with spinach and ricotta, roasted, with potatoes and beans

How it is made
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 200g ricotta
  • 60g baby spinach
  • 30g parmesan
  • 2 lemons
  • 800g chat potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 bunches green beans
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Chicken breasts stuffed with spinach and ricotta, roasted at 200C for 20 minutes, with roast potatoes and beans. Mix 200g ricotta with 60g chopped baby spinach, 30g grated parmesan, lemon zest and pepper. Cut a pocket into the side of 4 chicken breasts and stuff. Sear in oil 3 minutes a side, then roast at 200C for 20 minutes. Roast 800g halved chat potatoes in oil at 200C for 40 minutes. Steam 2 bunches green beans. Melt 60g butter with the juice of 1 lemon and parsley and spoon over the sliced chicken.

Jam and buttercream layer cakeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers with strawberry jam, iced white with buttercream

Prep 35 min, cook 25 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 250g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g strawberry jam
  • 150g butter, softened, for the buttercream
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp silver cachous
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour. Divide between 2 tins.
  2. Bake at 175C until golden and springy.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the buttercream butter with the icing sugar until pale.
  5. Sandwich the cakes with jam, ice white with buttercream and scatter silver cachous over the top.

Grow Old With You pineapple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Pineapple, orange juice and lemonade punch with scoops of lime sherbet floating on top

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep cold
Recipe for 4
  • 1l pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 1 1/4l lemonade
  • 1 orange
  • 1l lime sherbet or orange sherbet
  1. Chill everything well.
  2. Pour the pineapple and orange juice into a big punch bowl and slice the orange.
  3. Just before serving, top with the cold lemonade and add the sliced orange. Float scoops of sherbet on top and ladle into cups.
