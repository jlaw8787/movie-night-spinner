Snacks

Cocktail franks in puff pastry

Cocktail franks wrapped in puff pastry strips and baked

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cocktail franks
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  1. Simmer the franks briefly and drain well.
  2. Cut the pastry into strips and roll one around each frank.
  3. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200C until golden.
  4. Serve with mustard and tomato sauce.

Eighties cheese ballVegetarian

Cheddar, cream cheese and spring onion rolled in walnuts, with crackers

Prep 10 min, cook 62 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100g walnuts, chopped
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Mix the cheddar, cream cheese and sliced spring onion.
  2. Shape into one big ball and roll in the walnuts.
  3. Chill until firm.
  4. Serve with crackers.

Mains

Rosie's meatballs

Rosie's fee for singing lessons. Beef and pork in tomato sauce, over spaghetti.

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes (400 g each)
  • 500g spaghetti
  1. Mix the mince with the parmesan, parsley, breadcrumbs and egg. Roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer.
  4. Cook the spaghetti and serve with the meatballs on top.

Sweets

Jam and buttercream layer cakeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers with strawberry jam, iced white with buttercream

Prep 30 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 250g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g strawberry jam
  • 150g butter, softened, for the buttercream
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp silver cachous
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour. Divide between 2 tins.
  2. Bake at 175C until golden and springy.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the buttercream butter with the icing sugar until pale.
  5. Sandwich the cakes with jam, ice white with buttercream and scatter silver cachous over the top.

Drinks

Grow Old With You pineapple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Pineapple, orange juice and lemonade punch with scoops of lime sherbet floating on top

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep cold
Recipe for 4
  • 1l pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 1 1/4l lemonade
  • 1 orange
  • 1l lime sherbet or orange sherbet
  1. Chill everything well.
  2. Pour the pineapple and orange juice into a big punch bowl and slice the orange.
  3. Just before serving, top with the cold lemonade and add the sliced orange. Float scoops of sherbet on top and ladle into cups.
