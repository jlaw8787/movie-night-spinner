Roquefort's cheese boardVegetarian

Blue cheese with sliced pears, walnuts, honey and water crackers

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Toast the walnuts up to 3 days ahead, airtight; slice the pears and set out the cheese on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g blue cheese
  • 2 pears
  • 100g walnuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Set the cheese out at room temperature.
  2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan.
  3. Slice the pears thinly and fan them beside the cheese.
  4. Add the walnuts, honey and crackers.

Ham and gruyere croque bites

Ham and gruyere sandwiches topped with bechamel and more cheese

Prep 29 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make the bechamel up to 2 days ahead with cling film on the surface, chilled; assemble and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g gruyere
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Make a bechamel from the butter, flour and milk.
  2. Spread the bread with Dijon, fill with ham and gruyere and arrange on a tray.
  3. Top with bechamel and more grated cheese.
  4. Grill until bubbling.
  5. Cut into small squares and serve on a silver tray.

Parisian gruyère gougèresOn the printed menu

Gruyère cheese puffs baked golden and hollow, for Madame's Paris mansion

How it is made
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g gruyère
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  1. Choux pastry with gruyère piped into small balls, baked at 200C for 20 minutes until puffed. Bring 125ml water, 125ml milk, 100g butter and a pinch of salt to the boil. Tip in 150g plain flour and beat over the heat for 2 minutes until the dough comes away from the sides. Cool 5 minutes, then beat in 4 eggs one at a time until glossy. Stir in 120g grated gruyère, a pinch of nutmeg and pepper. Pipe walnut-size balls onto lined trays, top with a little more cheese and bake at 200C for 20 minutes until puffed and golden.

Mains

Countryside coq au vin

Chicken thighs braised in red grape juice with bacon and mushrooms

Prep 25 min, cook 118 min · Make ahead: The braise keeps up to 2 days chilled, reheat gently; mash the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 500ml red grape juice
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  1. Peel the potatoes and slice the mushrooms.
  2. Brown the chicken and bacon.
  3. Add the mushrooms and cook until golden.
  4. Pour in the red grape juice and water to cover and simmer.
  5. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter.

Abigail and Amelia's chicken leek pieOn the printed menu

A very English chicken and leek pie

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 30g butter
  • 1 leek
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  1. Chicken, leek and mushroom pie in a creamy sauce under puff pastry, baked at 200C for 25 minutes. Brown 800g diced chicken thigh in 30g butter and set aside. Cook 1 sliced leek and 200g sliced mushrooms for 6 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 300ml chicken stock and 150ml cream and simmer until thick. Add the chicken and thyme and season. Spoon into a pie dish, cover with 1 sheet puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200C for 25 minutes until golden. Serve with buttered peas.

Vanilla bean baked custardVegetarian

Baked vanilla bean custard in ramekins

Prep 13 min, cook 44 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar, for the custard
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the top
  1. Split the vanilla bean and heat with the cream until steaming.
  2. Heat.
  3. Whisk the yolks with the sugar for the custard and pour in the cream. Strain into ramekins.
  4. Bake in a water bath at 150C.
  5. Chill.
  6. Sprinkle with the sugar for the top and torch until amber.

Vanilla bean, ice cream, milk and honey blended

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving so it stays thick and frothy; the vanilla bean can steep in the milk up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  1. Split the bean and scrape the seeds into a blender.
  2. Add the milk, ice cream and honey and blend until frothy.
  3. Pour into four glasses and dust with nutmeg.

When to serve
