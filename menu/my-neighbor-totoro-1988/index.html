Snacks

Granny's stream-cooled cucumbersVegetarian

Iced cucumber spears with a sweet white miso dip.

Prep 8 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the cucumbers in iced water up to 2 hours ahead, then cut to serve; mix the miso dip up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 6 small cucumbers
  • 3 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp water
  1. Soak the small cucumbers in a bowl of iced water for 30 minutes, the way Granny cools them in the stream.
  2. Cut into spears.
  3. Mix the white miso with the honey and the water and serve in a little dish for dipping.

Grilled corn with soy butterVegetarian

Charred corn cobs brushed with soy and butter.

Prep 0 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; melt the butter with the soy and sugar up to 1 day ahead and rewarm to brush
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  1. Grill the corn cobs over high heat for 10 minutes, turning, until charred in spots.
  2. Melt the butter with the soy sauce and the sugar and brush all over the corn while it's hot.

Salmon and pickled plum onigiriOn the printed menu

Seasoned rice triangles around grilled fish or sour umeboshi, nori wrapped, for lunch on the porch.

How it is made
  • 2 cups short-grain rice
  • 250g salmon fillet
  • 1 jar pickled plums (umeboshi)
  • 3 nori sheets
  • sesame seeds
  1. Warm seasoned rice shaped into triangles around flaked grilled salmon or pickled plum, wrapped in crisp nori with toasted sesame. Rinse 2 cups short-grain rice until clear and cook with 2.5 cups water. Grill a 250g salmon fillet sprinkled with salt for 8 minutes and flake. With wet, salted hands, take a handful of warm rice, press a hollow, add salmon or a little pickled plum, close and press into a triangle. Make 12. Wrap the base of each in a strip of nori and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Mains

Satsuki's chicken and egg bento

Sticky soy chicken, thin omelette, peas and pickled ginger on rice.

Prep 9 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice, chicken and omelette up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; pack the boxes on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sushi rice
  • 500g chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  1. Cook the sushi rice.
  2. Fry the chicken thighs, then glaze with the soy and the sugar until sticky.
  3. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of sugar and roll into a thin omelette in a pan.
  4. Pack each box with rice topped with peas, sliced chicken, omelette and pickled ginger.

Bus stop rainy night udonOn the printed menu

Thick noodles in hot soy dashi, fish cake, jammy egg and spinach, after the umbrella swap.

How it is made
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tsp dashi powder
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 60ml mirin
  • 800g fresh udon noodles
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 1 Japanese fish cake
  • 3 spring onions
  1. Thick udon in a hot soy and dashi broth with fish cake, a jammy soft-boiled egg, wilted spinach and plenty of spring onion. Boil 4 eggs for 6.5 minutes, cool and peel. Heat 1.2L water with 2 tsp dashi powder, 60ml soy, 60ml mirin and 1 tsp sugar until just simmering. Cook 4 packs (800g) fresh udon in boiling water for 2 minutes and drain. Divide between bowls with a handful of spinach, pour over the hot broth and top with sliced fish cake, a halved egg and sliced spring onion.

Sweets

Red bean dorayaki pancakesVegetarian

Small honey pancakes sandwiched with sweet red bean paste.

Prep 6 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Cook the pancakes up to 1 day ahead and keep them airtight; sandwich with the red bean paste on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 300g red bean paste
  • 60ml water
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar and the honey, then the flour, the baking powder and the water.
  2. Cook the small pancakes in a lightly oiled pan for 1 minute a side.
  3. Sandwich in pairs with the red bean paste.

Drinks

Catbus iced barley tea with honeyVegetarianNo alcohol

Roasted barley tea sweetened with honey and lemon, over ice.

Prep 1 min, cook 17 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp roasted barley tea
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cups ice, as needed
  • 1.2 L water
  1. Bring the water to the boil, add the roasted barley tea (or the tea bags) and simmer 5 minutes.
  2. Turn off the heat, stir in the honey and steep 10 minutes more.
  3. Strain and chill for at least 2 hours.
  4. Fill the tall glasses with ice, add the thin lemon slices to each and pour in the tea.

When to serve

Serve the cucumbers when Granny tells the girls to keep pumping till the water gets cold.
