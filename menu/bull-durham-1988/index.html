Hit Bull Win Steak franks

Grilled franks in soft buns with yellow mustard and pickle relish

Prep 5 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp pickle relish
  1. Grill the franks until they split and blister.
  2. Tuck each into a soft bun with a stripe of mustard and a spoon of relish.
  3. Wrap tightly in paper so the bun steams.

Carolina hushpuppiesVegetarian

Fried cornmeal and onion fritters, crisp outside, soft inside

Prep 8 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cup fine cornmeal
  • 1/2 cup self-raising flour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 180ml buttermilk
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • butter or hot sauce, to serve
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar and onion with the egg and buttermilk.
  2. Heat the oil to about 5cm deep.
  3. Drop teaspoons into the hot oil and fry until golden.
  4. Drain and serve with butter or hot sauce.

Dugout honey chilli roasted peanutsOn the printed menu

Raw nuts roasted with butter, brown sugar and cayenne until glossy, warm in paper bags

How it is made
  • 400g raw unsalted peanuts
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • cayenne pepper
  • sea salt flakes
  • paper bags
  1. Raw peanuts roasted with butter, honey, brown sugar and cayenne until glossy and crunchy, finished with flaky salt and served warm in paper bags. Heat the oven to 170°C. Melt 30g butter with 3 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp brown sugar, half a tsp cayenne and 1 tsp salt. Toss with 400g raw unsalted peanuts until coated. Spread on a lined tray and roast 20 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes, until deep golden. Sprinkle with flaky salt and leave to cool on the tray for 10 minutes so they crisp. Break up any clumps and scoop into paper bags.

Carolina pulled pork

Pork shoulder roasted until it pulls apart, tangy cider vinegar sauce

Prep 22 min, cook 5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, reheat covered
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 250ml apple cider vinegar
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 8 burger buns
  • 1/2 small cabbage
  1. Rub the pork with salt and roast covered at 150°C until it pulls apart.
  2. Simmer the vinegar with the brown sugar and chilli flakes.
  3. Shred the pork and toss through the sauce. Shred the cabbage for a slaw and dress it with a splash of the sauce.
  4. Pile the pork into buns with the slaw.

Annie's buttermilk fried chickenOn the printed menu

Buttermilk fried drumsticks, crisp and spiced, from Annie Savoy's house of season-long lessons

How it is made
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • hot sauce
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 400ml chicken stock
  1. Chicken drumsticks soaked overnight in spiced buttermilk, dredged in seasoned flour and fried until crackling, with creamy mash and peppery gravy. Soak 8 drumsticks overnight in 500ml buttermilk with 2 tsp salt and 1 tsp hot sauce. Mix 250g plain flour with 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp pepper and 1 tsp salt. Dredge the chicken, pressing to coat. Fry in oil at 160°C for 12 to 14 minutes until cooked through to 75°C inside. Mash 1kg potatoes with butter and milk. For gravy, whisk 2 tbsp of the frying flour into 2 tbsp butter, then 400ml chicken stock and plenty of pepper.

Carolina banana puddingVegetarian

Vanilla custard layered with bananas and sweet biscuits

Prep 22 min, cook 8 min, then 5.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, chill overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 50g cornflour
  • 750ml milk
  • 20g butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g vanilla wafer biscuits
  • 4 bananas
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour in a saucepan, then whisk in the milk.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until thick.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the butter and vanilla. Cover the surface and cool.
  4. Layer the wafers, sliced bananas and custard in a dish, repeating twice.
  5. Whip the cream with icing sugar and spread on top.
  6. Chill, ideally overnight.

Nuke LaLoosh bourbon sweet teaVegetarian

Sweet black tea with bourbon, lemon and mint over ice

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Tea up to 2 days, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 1l water, just boiled
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the just boiled water for 5 minutes, remove and stir in the sugar until dissolved.
  2. Cool, then chill.
  3. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice, add bourbon and tea to each and squeeze in a lemon wedge.
  4. Slap a mint sprig between your hands and drop it in. Leave out the bourbon for non-drinkers.
