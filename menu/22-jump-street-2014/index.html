MC State crab cakes

Crab cakes, then the golf cart ends up on the football field

Prep 5 min, cook 14 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix, shape and chill the cakes up to 1 day ahead, covered; crumb and fry on the night and mix the chipotle mayo up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g crab meat
  • 7 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 1 lime
  1. Mix the crab with some of the mayonnaise, egg, spring onions, lemon zest, mustard and half the panko.
  2. Shape into small cakes and chill.
  3. Roll the cakes in the remaining panko.
  4. Fry in oil over medium heat until golden on both sides.
  5. Mix the remaining mayonnaise with the chipotle and a squeeze of lime. Serve with lime wedges.

Frat house sticky barbecue wings

Sticky honey barbecue wings with sesame. Work hard? Yes. Play hard? Yes. WHY-PHY.

Prep 5 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings in the baking powder mix up to 1 day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge to dry; bake and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Toss the wings with the baking powder, paprika and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220C, turning once.
  3. Simmer the barbecue sauce with the honey, soy and vinegar until sticky.
  4. Toss the hot wings in the glaze and return to the oven.
  5. Scatter with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Captain Dickson's fish tacos

Lager-battered flathead, slaw, chipotle crema in warm tortillas

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Soak the onion in the lime juice up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shred the cabbage and mix the crema up to 1 day ahead, batter and fry the fish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 4 limes
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 600g flathead fillets, cut into strips
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/2 cabbage, shredded
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 tsp chipotle sauce
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  1. Soak the onion in the juice of two limes with a pinch of salt.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder and lager to a batter.
  3. Dip the flathead strips in the batter and fry in the oil at 180C until golden, working in batches.
  4. Toss the cabbage with lime juice and salt. Mix the sour cream with the chipotle sauce.
  5. Warm the tortillas and fill with slaw, fish, crema, onion and coriander.

Spring break carne asada burritos

Skirt steak chargrilled with lime and garlic, rolled with rice, beans and pico, still undercover

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Marinate the steak up to 1 day ahead; make the pico de gallo up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 3 limes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g long grain rice
  • 400g tinned black beans, drained
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Rub the steak with the juice of 2 limes, crushed garlic, cumin, oil and salt and leave for 30 minutes.
  2. Simmer the rice with 400ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, rest for 5, then stir through the beans.
  3. Dice the tomatoes, onion and jalapeño and mix with chopped coriander and the last lime for the pico de gallo.
  4. Grill the steak very hot for 3 minutes a side, rest for 5 minutes and slice thin across the grain.
  5. Warm the tortillas, fill with rice and beans, steak, cheddar, pico de gallo and sour cream, and roll up tight.

Dining hall pan choc chip cookieVegetarian

A chocolate chip cookie baked in one pan and cut in wedges. Schmidt's slam poetry date with Maya.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Mix the dough and press it into the pan up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake just before serving so it is warm and soft
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, melted
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 150g chocolate chips
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 bottle chocolate sauce
  1. Beat the butter with both sugars, the egg and vanilla. Stir in the flour, bicarbonate of soda, a pinch of salt and chocolate chips.
  2. Press into a greased 25cm ovenproof frying pan.
  3. Bake at 180C until golden at the edges but still soft in the centre.
  4. Rest, then top with ice cream and drizzle with chocolate sauce. Serve with spoons.

Drinks

Spring break frozen mango margaritaVegetarian

Tequila, mango and lime blended with ice. Mercedes's bar, and no one's getting shot.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; rim the glasses and blend with the ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 5 limes
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 400g frozen mango
  • 160ml tequila
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Rub a lime wedge around the rims of the glasses and dip in sea salt flakes.
  2. Blend the mango, tequila, orange liqueur, lime juice, sugar syrup and ice until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into the glasses and garnish with lime wheels.
