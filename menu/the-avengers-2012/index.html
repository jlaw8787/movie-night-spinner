Post-battle hummus with spiced lamb

Chickpea hummus topped with crisp spiced lamb mince, pine nuts and flatbread.

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Hummus up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chickpeas (2 tins), drained
  • 100g tahini
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 80ml ice water
  • 250g lamb mince
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 30g pine nuts
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 4 flatbreads
  • salt, to taste
  1. Blitz the chickpeas with the tahini, garlic, lemon juice, salt and ice water until very smooth.
  2. Fry the lamb in a little oil until crisp, adding the cumin, cinnamon and paprika.
  3. Toast the pine nuts and warm the flatbreads.
  4. Swirl the hummus onto a plate and spoon the hot lamb over.
  5. Add pine nuts, chilli oil and parsley.

Asgardian honey roasted nutsVegetarian

Mixed nuts roasted with honey, butter and thyme.

Prep 5 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 400g mixed nuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves, chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Toss the nuts with the honey, butter and thyme.
  2. Roast on a lined tray, stirring once.
  3. Sprinkle with flaky salt while warm and stir as they cool so they don't stick together.

Stark Tower seared tuna and wasabi bites

Sesame tuna on cucumber with pickled ginger, mini Yorkshires with beef, for Tony's penthouse bar.

How it is made
  • 300g sashimi tuna
  • 3 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 3 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tube wasabi
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g rare roast beef
  • 1 jar horseradish cream
  1. Sesame-crusted tuna seared rare on crisp cucumber with wasabi mayo and pickled ginger, beside mini Yorkshires stuffed with rare beef and horseradish. Roll 300g sashimi tuna in sesame seeds and sear 30 seconds a side, then slice. Mix 3 tbsp mayo with 1 tsp wasabi. Top 16 thick cucumber rounds with mayo, tuna and pickled ginger. Whisk 2 eggs, 100g flour and 125ml milk, pour into a hot oiled mini muffin tin and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Fill with sliced rare roast beef and horseradish cream.

Shawarma

Yoghurt and spice marinated chicken in flatbread with garlic sauce and pickled turnips.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 4 tbsp garlic sauce
  • 4 tbsp pickled turnips
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  1. Mix the chicken with the yoghurt, garlic, cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon and lemon juice. Marinate overnight in the fridge.
  2. Grill the chicken until cooked through and charred.
  3. Rest and slice.
  4. Warm the flatbreads and fill with chicken, garlic sauce, pickled turnips, tomato and parsley.

Captain America shield cookiesVegetarian

Iced butter cookies in red, white and blue shield rings with a white star.

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 1 tsp blue food colouring
  1. Beat the butter, sugar and egg, then mix in the flour.
  2. Cut 12 rounds and bake at 180°C. Cool completely.
  3. Mix the icing sugar with water to a thick icing and tint some red and some blue.
  4. Ice the rings, add a blue centre and a white star.

Asgardian honey ginger beer or mead

Honey and lemon topped with ginger beer, or chilled mead for the grown-ups.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Alcohol is optional, for the grown-ups
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 L ginger beer
  • 120ml chilled mead, optional for the grown-ups
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Stir a spoon of honey and a squeeze of lemon into each of four glasses until dissolved.
  2. Fill with ice and top with ginger beer.
  3. For the grown-ups, pour chilled mead instead. Raise the glasses high.
