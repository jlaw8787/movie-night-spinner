Snacks

Minnie's herbal tonic shotsVegetarian

Ginger, turmeric, lemon, honey and apple juice shots, no alcohol

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 cm knob ginger
  • 5 cm piece fresh turmeric
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 250ml apple juice
  1. Roughly chop the ginger and turmeric and juice the lemons.
  2. Blitz with the lemon juice, honey and apple juice.
  3. Strain and pour into shot glasses. No tannis root.

Bramford welcome canapés

Brandy flamed chicken liver pâté on toasted baguette with fig jam

Prep 25 min, cook 250 min · Make ahead: Pate sets 4 hours, keeps 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken livers, trimmed
  • 200g butter
  • 30ml brandy
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 1 baguette
  • 3 tbsp fig jam
  1. Sear the livers in some butter until pink inside.
  2. Flame with the brandy.
  3. Blitz with the soft butter and thyme.
  4. Set in a dish and chill.
  5. Slice and toast the baguette and spread with pate and a little fig jam.

Mains

Chicken club sandwich

Triple-decker toasted club with roast chicken, bacon, lettuce and tomato

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Grill the bacon up to 1 day ahead, chilled; toast the bread and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices sliced bread
  • 300g roast chicken, sliced
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bag potato chips
  1. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  2. Toast the bread.
  3. Layer chicken, bacon, lettuce and tomato with mayonnaise across three slices per sandwich.
  4. Pin with toothpicks, cut into triangles and serve with potato chips.

Sweets

Minnie's chocolate mousseVegetarian

Smooth dark chocolate mousse topped with whipped cream

Prep 25 min, cook 240 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 30g butter
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter and cool slightly.
  2. Separate the eggs and whisk the yolks into the chocolate.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Whip the whites with the sugar to soft peaks.
  5. Fold in the whites, then the cream.
  6. Spoon into small cups and set. Unlike Minnie's, nothing chalky in it.

Tannis root teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Ginger and lemongrass tea with honey and lemon, no alcohol

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the ginger and bruise the lemongrass up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; steep on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, bruised
  • 1l boiling water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Slice the ginger and bruise the lemongrass.
  2. Steep in boiling water.
  3. Strain, sweeten with honey and lemon juice and pour into four cups.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Minnie brings round her chocolate mousse.
