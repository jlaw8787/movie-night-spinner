Snacks

Herbal tonic shotsVegetarian

Ginger, mint, lemon, honey and apple juice shots, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 cm knob ginger
  • 5 cm piece fresh ginger
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 250ml apple juice
  1. Roughly chop the ginger, pick the mint and juice the lemons.
  2. Blitz with the lemon juice, honey and apple juice.
  3. Strain and pour into shot glasses. No tannis root.

Bramford welcome canapés

Brandy flamed chicken liver pâté on toasted baguette with fig jam.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Pate sets 4 hours, keeps 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken livers, trimmed
  • 200g butter
  • 30ml brandy
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 1 baguette
  • 3 tbsp fig jam
  1. Sear the livers in some butter until pink inside.
  2. Flame with the brandy.
  3. Blitz with the soft butter and thyme.
  4. Set in a dish and chill.
  5. Slice and toast the baguette and spread with pate and a little fig jam.

Rosemary's craving steak tartare

Hand-chopped eye fillet with capers, shallot and a raw yolk.

How it is made
  • 400g eye fillet
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 jar cornichons
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 bag potato crisps
  1. Hand-chopped eye fillet with shallot, capers, cornichons, Dijon and Worcestershire, topped with a raw egg yolk and served with crisp potato crisps and toast. Chill 400g very fresh eye fillet in the freezer for 20 minutes, then chop very finely by hand. Mix with 1 finely diced shallot, 1 tbsp chopped capers, 4 chopped cornichons, 2 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, a dash of hot sauce, chopped parsley, salt and pepper. Shape into 4 rounds on cold plates and set an egg yolk on each. Serve straight away with thin toast and potato crisps.

Mains

Chicken club sandwich

Triple-decker toasted club with roast chicken, bacon, lettuce and tomato.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Grill the bacon up to 1 day ahead, chilled; toast the bread and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices sliced bread
  • 300g roast chicken, sliced
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bag potato chips
  1. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  2. Toast the bread.
  3. Layer chicken, bacon, lettuce and tomato with mayonnaise across three slices per sandwich.
  4. Pin with toothpicks, cut into triangles and serve with potato chips.

Rare rib eye with frites

Rosemary can't stop eating meat barely cooked.

How it is made
  • 2 rib eye steaks
  • 800g frozen frites
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100g watercress
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  1. Dry-brined rib eye seared hard and pulled rare, sliced over golden frites, with garlic butter and a watercress salad sharp with Dijon dressing. Salt 2 rib eye steaks (about 450g each) and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Bake 800g frozen frites at 220°C for 25 minutes. Sear the steaks in a smoking cast iron pan, flipping every minute, until 48°C inside, about 6 minutes. Add 40g butter and 2 garlic cloves, baste for 1 minute, then rest 8 minutes. Toss 100g watercress with 1 tsp Dijon, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar and 3 tbsp olive oil. Slice the steak and serve.

Sweets

Minnie's chocolate mousseVegetarian

Smooth dark chocolate mousse topped with whipped cream.

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 30g butter
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter and cool slightly.
  2. Separate the eggs and whisk the yolks into the chocolate.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Whip the whites with the sugar to soft peaks.
  5. Fold in the whites, then the cream.
  6. Spoon into small cups and set. Unlike Minnie's, nothing chalky in it.

Drinks

Tannis root teaNo alcohol

Ginger and lemongrass tea with honey and lemon, no alcohol.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Slice the ginger and bruise the lemongrass up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; steep on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, bruised
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Slice the ginger and bruise the lemongrass.
  2. Steep in boiling water.
  3. Strain, sweeten with honey and lemon juice and pour into four cups.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Minnie brings round her chocolate mousse.
