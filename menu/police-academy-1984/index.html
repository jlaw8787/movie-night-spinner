Snacks

Mess hall cornbreadVegetarian

Golden skillet cornbread with butter, cut in wedges.

Prep 0 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and wrap once cool; warm in the oven for 10 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g butter, melted, for the batter
  • 20g butter, to grease the pan
  • 40g butter, to serve
  1. Grease a cast iron pan with the butter and heat it in a 200°C oven, 10 min.
  2. Whisk the cornmeal, plain flour, caster sugar and baking powder with the buttermilk, eggs and melted butter.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake, 20 min.
  4. Serve warm with butter.

Blue Oyster bar oysters

Fresh oysters on crushed ice with red wine vinegar and shallot.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the mignonette up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; shuck the oysters just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 shallot
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  • 16 oysters, freshly shucked
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 2 lemons
  1. Finely dice the shallot and mix with the vinegar and pepper for the mignonette.
  2. Shuck the oysters and lay on crushed ice.
  3. Spoon a little mignonette over each and serve with lemon wedges.

Mains

Mess hall meatloaf

Beef and pork meatloaf under a sticky tomato glaze, sliced thick.

Prep 17 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Mix and shape the meatloaf up to 1 day ahead in the tin, covered in the fridge; bake and make the mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Mix the mince, breadcrumbs, egg, grated onion and Worcestershire.
  2. Press into a loaf tin and glaze with tomato sauce mixed with brown sugar, 5 min.
  3. Bake at 180°C, 1 hr.
  4. Boil the potatoes while the meatloaf bakes.
  5. Rest the meatloaf, mash the potatoes with butter and milk and serve thick slices, 15 min.

Hightower's chicken parmigiana

Crumbed chicken with tomato and cheese.

Prep 2 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken and make the napoli up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 200ml vegetable oil
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 100g rocket
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with the crushed garlic, oregano and a pinch of salt for 10 minutes until thick.
  2. Slice each breast in half through the middle and flatten. Dip in flour, then beaten egg, then panko.
  3. Bake the chips at 220°C for 25 minutes.
  4. Shallow fry the chicken in the vegetable oil for 3 minutes a side until golden.
  5. Spoon the napoli over the chicken, cover with mozzarella and grill for 5 minutes until bubbling.
  6. Dress the rocket with the olive oil and lemon and serve with the parmigiana and chips.

Graduation cakeVegetarian

Vanilla slab cake with buttercream and a blue badge, for the bravery awards.

Prep 30 min, cook 45 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 200g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 350g icing sugar
  • 4 drops of blue food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a slab tin. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk, 15 min.
  2. Bake, 30 min.
  3. Cool the cake completely, 40 min.
  4. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and tint blue.
  5. Spread on the cake.
  6. Pipe a badge and the word Graduates in blue.

Drinks

Blue Oyster blue lagoon

Vodka, blue curaçao and lemon juice shaken and served over ice.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemon juice up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake the vodka and curacao to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tray ice
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml blue curacao
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 480ml cold lemonade
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice.
  2. Shake the vodka, curacao and lemon juice with ice and strain into the glasses.
  3. Top with lemonade, stir once and garnish with a lemon wheel and a cherry.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the cadets end up at the Blue Oyster bar.
