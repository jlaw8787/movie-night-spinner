Snacks

Cooler box pressed loaf

Hollowed loaf filled with ham, roast pepper, pesto, mozzarella and spinach, pressed and sliced. Packed tight, like the cooler he carries his finds home in.

Prep 30 min, then 12 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 1 round cob loaf, about 20cm
  • 4 tbsp basil pesto
  • 150g leg ham, sliced
  • 150g roasted capsicum
  • 150g mozzarella, sliced
  • 1 handful of baby spinach
  • 100g salami, sliced
  • 100g semi-dried tomatoes
  1. Cut the top off the loaf and pull out most of the soft inside.
  2. Spread the pesto inside the loaf.
  3. Layer in the ham, capsicum, mozzarella, spinach, salami and tomatoes, pressing each layer down.
  4. Put the lid back on, wrap tightly in cling wrap and press under a heavy pot in the fridge overnight.
  5. Cut into wedges to serve.

Plant-in-a-boot pea smash

Peas crushed with mint, feta, lemon and olive oil, green shoots on top, crunchy pita chips. He found the seedling in an old fridge and kept it in a boot.

How it is made
  1. Cook 400g frozen peas for 3 minutes, drain and cool under cold water.
  2. Pulse with a handful of mint, 100g feta, the zest and juice of a lemon, 3 tbsp olive oil and salt until roughly smashed.
  3. Cut 4 pita breads into triangles, brush with oil and bake at 190°C for 10 minutes until crisp.
  4. Pile the smash into a bowl, stand a few pea shoots in the middle and serve with the chips.

Trash cube rice bars

Marshmallow puffed rice, cut into cubes and stacked into towers. Compacted and stacked, like his towers over the city.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 250g marshmallows
  • 150g puffed rice
  1. Line a square tray with baking paper.
  2. Melt the butter and marshmallows together over low heat, 7 min.
  3. Stir through the puffed rice until well coated.
  4. Press firmly into the tray and leave to set, 1 hr.
  5. Cut into neat cubes and stack into towers.

Mains

Pumpkin lentil soup in a mugVegetarian

Pumpkin, red lentil and coconut soup with ginger, in big mugs with a straw. Sipped through a straw, the way everyone on the Axiom eats.

Prep 12 min, cook 38 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 800g pumpkin, diced
  • 150g red lentils
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 600ml water
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 150g cheddar, sliced
  • 40g butter
  1. Soften the onion and ginger in the oil.
  2. Add the pumpkin, lentils, coconut milk and water and simmer until everything is soft.
  3. Blitz the soup until smooth.
  4. Make grilled cheese from the sourdough, cheddar and butter.
  5. Serve the soup in big mugs with a straw and the toasties on the side.

Earth garden veg and haloumi tray

Pumpkin, bell pepper, zucchini and chickpeas roasted with honey and cumin, golden haloumi, mint and lemon yoghurt. The captain learns food comes out of the ground.

How it is made
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Toss 600g pumpkin chunks, 2 capsicums, 2 red onions in wedges, 2 zucchini and 1 drained tin chickpeas with 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tbsp honey and salt.
  3. Roast for 35 minutes, turning once.
  4. Slice 250g haloumi and fry until golden.
  5. Mix 200g Greek yoghurt with lemon juice and salt.
  6. Top the tray with haloumi, drizzle with yoghurt and scatter with mint.

Sweets

Golden sponge cakesVegetarian

Light sponge fingers split and filled with piped vanilla cream. Cream-filled snack cakes, the one thing tougher than his cockroach.

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100g plain flour
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a tray, 3 min.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and thick, then fold in the flour.
  3. Pipe 12 fingers onto the tray and bake, 12 min.
  4. Cool, then whip the cream with the vanilla, 8 min.
  5. Split the sponges and pipe in the cream.

Drinks

Axiom blue slushieNo alcohol

Frozen raspberries, lemon and blue cordial blended with ice. The chair screens switch the whole deck from red to blue.

Prep 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 80ml blue raspberry cordial
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml cold water
  • 4 cups of ice
  • 1 lemon, cut into wheels
  1. Blend the raspberries, lemon juice, cordial, sugar, water and ice until smooth and slushy.
  2. Pour into tall cups, add a lemon wheel and a straw and serve straight away.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Wall-E watches Hello, Dolly! at home after work.
