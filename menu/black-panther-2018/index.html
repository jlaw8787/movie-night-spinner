Snacks

Busan casino gochujang wings

Double-fried crispy wings in a sticky gochujang, honey and soy glaze.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 100g potato starch
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the wings in the potato starch.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil to 160°C and fry the wings, then rest, 15 min.
  3. Simmer the gochujang, honey, soy and garlic until glossy, 5 min.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until crisp, 10 min.
  5. Toss in the sauce and top with sesame.

Heart-shaped herb dipVegetarian

Roast beet and dill yogurt dip with flatbread, fit for a Black Panther.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 beetroots
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Roast the beetroot until tender, then peel and grate, 45 min.
  2. Mix with the yoghurt, garlic, lemon juice and dill.
  3. Warm the flatbreads and serve with the deep purple dip, 5 min.

Beef suya skewers with onion and tomato

Spiced peanut beef skewers grilled smoky with onion and tomato, street food from hidden Wakanda.

How it is made
  • 800g beef rump
  • 80g roasted peanuts
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 limes
  • bamboo skewers
  1. Thin beef rump strips coated in a peanut, ginger and chilli spice, grilled smoky on skewers and served with sliced tomato, red onion and lime. Blitz 80g roasted peanuts to a fine powder and mix with 2 tsp ground ginger, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cayenne, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder and 1 tsp salt. Slice 800g beef rump into thin strips, toss in oil and then in most of the spice. Thread onto soaked skewers and grill over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes a side. Scatter with the rest of the spice and serve with sliced tomato, red onion and lime.

Waterfall feast jollof rice

Spicy tomato rice and grilled chicken.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 15 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin of chopped tomatoes
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 onion
  • 1/2 scotch bonnet chilli
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400g long grain rice
  • 700ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 800g chicken thighs
  1. Blitz the tomatoes, capsicum, onion and chilli.
  2. Fry the blended sauce in oil until deep red, 15 min.
  3. Stir in the rice, stock and thyme, cover and bake at 170°C, 40 min.
  4. Grill the chicken thighs until charred and cooked through, 20 min.
  5. Lay the chicken on the rice and serve.

M'Baku's sweet potato and peanut stew

Sweet potato and peanut stew with chickpeas and spinach over rice, from the Jabari mountains.

How it is made
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 knob ginger
  • ground cumin
  • ground coriander
  • 1 red chilli
  • crushed tomatoes
  • 120g smooth peanut butter
  • 600ml vegetable stock
  • 800g sweet potato
  • chickpeas
  • 120g baby spinach
  • 1 lime
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 50g roasted peanuts
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Sweet potato, chickpeas and spinach simmered in a rich peanut, tomato and ginger sauce with chilli, over rice with roasted peanuts and coriander. Soften 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger in oil. Add 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander and 1 chopped red chilli. Stir in 1 tin crushed tomatoes, 120g smooth peanut butter and 600ml vegetable stock. Add 800g diced sweet potato and 1 drained tin chickpeas and simmer 25 minutes. Stir in 120g spinach and lime juice. Serve over rice with roasted peanuts and coriander.

Dora Milaje fried plantain with honeyVegetarian

Caramelised ripe plantain with honey, chilli flakes and coconut ice cream.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe plantains
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch of chilli flakes
  • 1 tub of coconut ice cream
  1. Slice the plantains on the diagonal.
  2. Fry in the butter until caramelised, 10 min.
  3. Drizzle with honey and chilli flakes and serve with scoops of ice cream.

Wakandan hibiscus ginger fizz

Hibiscus and ginger tea with pineapple and lime, over ice with sparkling water.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 40 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L water
  • 30g dried hibiscus flowers
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 200ml pineapple juice
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 500ml sparkling water, to top
  • 2 cups ice
  • 30ml white rum, optional per glass
  1. Boil the water with the hibiscus, ginger and sugar, then turn off and steep, 20 min.
  2. Strain and chill, 40 min.
  3. Stir in the pineapple juice and lime juice.
  4. Pour over ice, top with sparkling water and add a lime wheel. Add rum per glass for the adults if you like.
