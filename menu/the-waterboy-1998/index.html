Bayou hush puppies with Cajun remouladeVegetarian

Spicy Cajun mayo dip. Captain Insano shows no mercy.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the remoulade up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; make the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill pickles
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, salt and cayenne. Whisk in the egg and buttermilk, then fold in the spring onions.
  2. Rest the batter.
  3. Stir together the mayonnaise, mustard, pickles, Cajun seasoning and lemon juice for the remoulade.
  4. Heat the oil to 180°C and drop in heaped teaspoons of batter. Fry, turning, until deep golden, working in batches.
  5. Drain on paper towel and serve hot with the remoulade.

Bourbon Bowl Cajun butter prawns

Cajun spiced prawns swirled in garlic butter, with baguette

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Peel and devein the prawns up to 1 day ahead, chilled; toss with the seasoning, sear and serve straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 800g green prawns
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  1. Peel and devein the prawns, leaving the tails on, and toss with the Cajun seasoning.
  2. Sear the prawns in a very hot pan with oil in a single layer, turning once.
  3. Drop the heat, add the butter and garlic and swirl until foaming.
  4. Squeeze over the lemon, scatter with parsley and serve from the pan with the baguette.

Mama Boucher's chicken and sausage gumbo

Dark roux gumbo, chicken, smoked sausage, okra, rice. Mama's right!

Prep 5 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Whole gumbo up to 2 days ahead, reheat gently; steam the rice and slice the spring onions fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 80g plain flour
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 300g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1.2l chicken stock
  • 200g okra, sliced
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • hot sauce, to serve
  1. Stir the flour into the oil in a heavy pot over medium-low heat until the roux turns dark brown like chocolate.
  2. Add the onion, celery and capsicum and cook until soft.
  3. Add the chicken, sausage, Cajun seasoning and stock. Simmer.
  4. Add the okra and cook until tender. Meanwhile steam the rice.
  5. Serve over rice with spring onion and hot sauce.

Vicki Vallencourt's blackened catfish po' boys

Blackened catfish in soft rolls with lettuce, tomato and remoulade. Vicki knows a good sandwich.

Prep 11 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the remoulade up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 700g basa or catfish fillets
  • 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 40g butter
  • 4 long soft rolls
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 lemon
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, mustard, hot sauce and a squeeze of lemon for the remoulade.
  2. Coat the fish in the Cajun seasoning.
  3. Melt the butter in a very hot pan and cook the fish until blackened and just cooked.
  4. Split the rolls, spread with remoulade and fill with lettuce, tomato, pickles and fish.

Bourbon Bowl pecan pralinesVegetarian

Creamy brown sugar pecan pralines spooned out to set

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g pecans
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 120ml thickened cream
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Toast the pecans in a dry pan.
  2. Combine the sugars, cream and butter in a heavy saucepan and stir over medium heat until boiling.
  3. Cook without stirring to 115°C on a sugar thermometer.
  4. Off the heat, add the vanilla and pecans and beat with a wooden spoon until thick and cloudy.
  5. Quickly spoon rounds onto baking paper and leave to set.

Bobby Boucher's cucumber lime coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Cucumber, lime, mint and honey in sparkling water. H2O, not Gatorade.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the honey syrup up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; muddle and top with sparkling water just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 4 limes, juiced, plus extra for wheels
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1l sparkling water, chilled
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Stir the honey into hot water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Peel the cucumber into thin ribbons.
  3. Muddle the lime juice, mint and honey syrup in a jug.
  4. Fill glasses with ice and cucumber, add the sparkling water to the jug, stir gently and pour over. Garnish with lime wheels and mint.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Bobby hands out the water.
