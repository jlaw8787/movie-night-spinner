Snacks

Repo man Chicago-style hot dogs

Beef frank in a poppy seed bun with mustard, relish, tomato, pickle, peppers

Prep 15 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Best built just before serving; dice the onion and cut the tomatoes up to 3 hours ahead, covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 1 jar sweet green relish
  • 1 white onion, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 8 dill pickle spears
  • 16 pickled peppers
  • 1 tsp celery salt
  1. Simmer the frankfurts in water, then grill briefly for colour.
  2. Steam or warm the buns and sprinkle the tops with poppy seeds.
  3. Put a frankfurt in each bun, add mustard and relish, then onion, tomato, a pickle spear and pickled peppers.
  4. Finish with a pinch of celery salt.

Iced gingerbread menVegetarian

Spiced ginger cookies cut into men and piped with icing

Prep 35 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 150g icing sugar
  1. Melt the butter with the golden syrup and brown sugar, then mix in the flour and ginger to a dough.
  2. Roll out and cut gingerbread men.
  3. Bake at 180C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with a little water and pipe on faces and buttons.

Mains

Claus family honey mustard glazed ham

Honey Dijon clove ham, the dinner Nick talked him into

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the glaze up to 3 days ahead and score the ham up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake and roast the potatoes on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 1/2kg half leg ham
  • 1 tbsp cloves
  • 120ml honey
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g green beans
  • 1 bunch broccolini
  • 30g butter
  1. Remove the rind from the ham, score the fat in a diamond pattern and stud with cloves.
  2. Simmer the honey, mustard, sugar and orange juice for the glaze.
  3. Bake the ham at 180C, brushing with glaze every so often. Roast the potatoes in oil alongside.
  4. Toss the beans and broccolini in butter.
  5. Slice the ham thickly to serve.

Siblings Anonymous chicken pot pie

Chicken, leek and peas in creamy thyme sauce under puff, Stephen Baldwin's seat saved

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 1 day ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 leeks
  • 2 carrots
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Brown the diced chicken in half the butter, then add the sliced leeks, diced carrots and the rest of the butter and cook for 6 minutes.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream and thyme leaves and simmer for 10 minutes until thick. Stir in the peas.
  3. Heat the oven to 200C. Spoon the filling into a pie dish and cover with the pastry, pressing the edges.
  4. Brush with beaten egg, cut a steam hole and bake for 30 minutes until puffed and golden.

Mother Claus's choc chip cookiesVegetarian

Brown butter milk chocolate chip cookies with sea salt, served with milk

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roll the dough balls up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; bake on the night and serve with the cold milk
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 220g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 150g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1l cold full cream milk, to serve
  1. Melt the butter and cook until golden brown and nutty, then cool.
  2. Beat in both sugars, the egg and vanilla. Stir in the flour, bicarbonate of soda and chocolate.
  3. Roll into balls and chill.
  4. Bake at 180C until golden at the edges.
  5. Sprinkle with sea salt and serve warm with cold milk.

Drinks

Everybody's nice marshmallow cocoaNo alcohol

Toasted marshmallow hot chocolate, for kids Fred marked nice

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh; chop the chocolate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 150g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 16 large marshmallows
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Heat the milk until steaming, then whisk in the chocolate and cocoa until smooth and frothy.
  2. Pour into heatproof mugs, pile marshmallows on top and put the mugs on a tray under a hot grill, watching closely, until puffed and golden.
  3. Dust with cinnamon and serve.
