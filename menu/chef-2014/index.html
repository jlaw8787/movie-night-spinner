The grilled cheese for PercyVegetarian

White bread buttered outside, cheddar, gruyère and parmesan cooked low, Carl's toastie for his son

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best made to order
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 150g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 80g gruyere, grated
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  1. Butter the outside of each slice of bread.
  2. Fill four sandwiches with the cheddar, gruyere and a little parmesan.
  3. Cook slowly in a pan over low heat with a lid on, turning once, until the cheese pours. Cut on the diagonal.

New Orleans beignets with chicory caféVegetarian

Pillowy yeasted squares fried puffed under icing sugar, milky coffee, a road trip stop with Percy

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the dough up to 1 day ahead and rise it in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 180ml full cream milk, warmed, for the dough
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 350g plain flour
  • salt, a pinch
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 60g ground coffee with chicory
  • 500ml full cream milk, for the café
  1. Mix the yeast, caster sugar and warm milk and stand for 10 minutes until foamy.
  2. Beat in the egg, melted butter, plain flour and a pinch of salt to a soft dough and knead for 3 minutes.
  3. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  4. Roll the dough 5mm thick and cut into 6cm squares. Heat the oil to 175°C and fry the squares for 1 minute a side until puffed and golden.
  5. Brew the ground coffee with chicory with 500ml water, heat the milk and mix half and half into four cups.
  6. Drain the beignets and bury them in the icing sugar. Serve with the chicory café.

El Jefe Cubano

Mojo pork shoulder roasted slow, ham, Swiss and pickles, pressed in butter, tweeted by Percy

Prep 25 min, cook 4 hours, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate overnight, roast the pork up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pork shoulder
  • 2 oranges, juiced
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g Swiss cheese
  • 12 dill pickle slices
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 40g butter
  1. Rub the pork with the orange and lime juice, garlic, oregano and salt.
  2. Marinate overnight (included in cook time).
  3. Roast at 160°C for 4 hours until tender, then rest and slice.
  4. Layer the rolls with pork, ham, Swiss, pickles and mustard.
  5. Press and griddle in butter until crisp and the cheese melts.

Carl's late-night aglio e olioVegetarian

Spaghetti with golden sliced garlic, chilli and parsley, glossy with oil, cooked late for Molly

Prep 5 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight from the pan; slice the garlic and chop the parsley up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 100ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 8 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • salt, to taste
  1. Thinly slice the garlic and chop the parsley. Bring a big pot of water to the boil and salt it well.
  2. Cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente and keep 1 cup of the pasta water before draining.
  3. While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil with the garlic and chilli flakes over low heat until the garlic is pale gold.
  4. Toss the spaghetti through the garlic oil with a splash of pasta water until glossy.
  5. Finish with the parsley, a squeeze of lemon and the parmesan.

Chocolate lava cakeVegetarian

Dark chocolate and butter baked hot in ramekins, centre runs, the critic eats his words

Prep 15 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Fill the ramekins up to 4 hours ahead and chill, add a minute to the bake
Recipe for 4
  • 125g dark chocolate
  • 140g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 30g plain flour
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Butter 4 ramekins and melt the chocolate with most of the butter over gentle heat.
  2. Whisk the eggs and yolks with the sugar until thick, combine with the chocolate and fold in the flour.
  3. Bake at 220°C until the edges are set and the centre runs.
  4. Turn out and serve with vanilla ice cream.
