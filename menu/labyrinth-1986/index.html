Snacks

The worm's tea and cakeVegetarian

Buttered toasted fruit loaf fingers with a pot of tea and milk

Prep 4 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best toasted and brewed to order; slice the fruit loaf up to 1 day ahead, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 1 fruit loaf
  • 60g butter
  • 4 tea bags English breakfast tea
  • 1l boiling water
  • 200ml full cream milk
  1. Warm a teapot, add the tea bags and boiling water and brew.
  2. Toast thick slices of fruit loaf until the edges crisp.
  3. Butter generously and cut into fingers. Serve with milk on the side, like a guest in a burrow.

Bog of Eternal Stench garlic dipVegetarian

Roast garlic beaten with cream cheese and sour cream, torn baguette to dip

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 garlic heads
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 baguette
  • salt, to taste
  1. Wrap the garlic heads in foil and roast at 180C.
  2. Squeeze out the soft cloves and beat with the cream cheese, sour cream, lemon juice and salt until smooth.
  3. Serve with torn baguette for dipping.

Mains

Hoggle's beef and mushroom stew

Beef chuck, carrots and mushrooms braised 3 hours in Worcestershire gravy

Prep 38 min, cook 200 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Dice the beef, onion and carrots and quarter the mushrooms.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in the oil, then soften the vegetables in the same pot.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, thyme and stock. Cover and braise at 160C.
  4. Peel and boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and milk.
  5. Serve the stew over the mash with chopped parsley.

Sweets

Vanilla poached peaches with mascarponeVegetarian

Peaches poached in vanilla apple syrup, chilled and served with mascarpone

Prep 15 min, cook 135 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml apple juice
  • 200ml water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 4 peaches
  • 250g mascarpone
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 packet almond biscuits
  1. Simmer the apple juice, water, sugar, honey and split vanilla bean.
  2. Add the halved peaches and poach gently, then slip off the skins.
  3. Chill the peaches in the syrup.
  4. Beat the mascarpone with the icing sugar.
  5. Serve two halves each with mascarpone, a little syrup and crumbled almond biscuits.

Drinks

Goblin King's spiced apple and pear punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Apple and pear juice simmered with cinnamon, star anise and orange peel, served warm

Prep 6 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Simmer and strain the spiced juice up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, or pour over ice and top with sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cloudy apple juice
  • 500ml pear juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 3 star anise
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 250ml sparkling water
  1. Put the juices in a pot with the cinnamon, star anise and orange peel.
  2. Simmer gently so the spices come through, then strain.
  3. Serve warm in mugs with a cinnamon stick, or cool it and pour over ice, topping each glass with sparkling water.

When to serve
