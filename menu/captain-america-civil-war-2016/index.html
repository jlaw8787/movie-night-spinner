Snacks

Lagos market suya

Peanut and chilli beef skewers charred on the grill, from the Lagos market

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Spice mix up to 1 week, store airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef rump, cut into thin strips
  • 100g peanuts, finely ground
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  1. Mix the ground peanuts with the paprika, ginger, chilli flakes and salt.
  2. Thread the beef onto soaked skewers and coat all over in the spice.
  3. Grill on high heat until charred.
  4. Serve with sliced onion and tomato, sprinkled with more spice.

Leipzig airport currywurst

Bratwurst in curry sauce, fries, for the tarmac fight

Prep 15 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Warm the curry sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled to rewarm; bake the fries and grill the bratwurst fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 bratwurst
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tsp curry powder, plus extra for dusting
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Cut the potatoes into fries, toss in oil and spread on trays.
  2. Bake at 220°C, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Grill the bratwurst until browned, then slice into rounds.
  4. Warm the tomato sauce with the curry powder and paprika.
  5. Pour over the sausage, dust with curry powder and serve with the fries.

Vienna lemon cream cheese pastriesOn the printed menu

Flaky puff squares folded round sweet citrus filling, baked golden, apricot glaze and icing sugar

How it is made
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 jar apricot jam
  • 1 packet icing sugar
  1. Flaky puff pastry squares folded around sweet lemon cream cheese, baked golden, glazed with apricot jam and dusted with icing sugar. Beat 250g cream cheese with 60g caster sugar, 1 egg yolk, the zest of 1 lemon and 1 tsp vanilla. Cut 2 thawed puff pastry sheets into 9 squares each. Spoon filling into the middle and fold two opposite corners over to meet. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C for 15 minutes until puffed and golden. Brush with 3 tbsp warmed apricot jam, cool a little and dust with icing sugar.

Siberian winter goulash

Paprika goulash over egg noodles, to lift Wanda's spirits.

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 20g butter
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Brown the beef in batches in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the onions and paprika and stir until fragrant.
  3. Add the tomatoes and enough water to cover.
  4. Cover and simmer gently, stirring now and then, until the beef is very tender and the sauce is thick.
  5. Cook the noodles and toss with butter.
  6. Serve the goulash over the noodles with sour cream.

Bucky's Bucharest chicken with plums

Thighs roasted with stone fruit, paprika and onion over soft polenta, from his morning market run

Prep 5 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 6 plums
  • 2 red onions
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 200g instant polenta
  • 1l milk
  • 30g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub the chicken with the paprika, crushed garlic, oil and salt.
  2. Roast the chicken on the onion wedges for 20 minutes, then add the halved plums drizzled with honey and roast for 20 minutes more.
  3. Bring the milk to the boil, whisk in the polenta and stir for 5 minutes, then beat in the butter.
  4. Serve the chicken and plums over the polenta with sour cream and dill.

Aunt May's walnut date loafVegetarian

Moist walnut date loaf baked with brown sugar

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g pitted dates, chopped
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 60g butter, plus extra for serving
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 80g walnuts, chopped
  1. Pour the boiling water over the dates and bicarb soda and leave to soften.
  2. Beat in the butter, sugar and egg, then fold in the flour and walnuts.
  3. Pour into a lined loaf tin and bake at 175°C until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Slice and serve buttered.

Drinks

Team Cap and Team Iron Man fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Red soda and blue lemonade, no alcohol

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Cap's side up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 125g raspberries
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 400ml cranberry juice
  • 1 lime
  • 750ml soda water
  • 1 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Team Iron Man: muddle the raspberries with the sugar, add the cranberry juice and lime juice, then strain.
  2. Pour over two glasses of ice and top with soda water.
  3. Team Cap: steep the butterfly pea flowers in the boiling water, strain and chill.
  4. Pour into two glasses of ice and top with lemonade.
  5. Line them up and let everyone choose.
