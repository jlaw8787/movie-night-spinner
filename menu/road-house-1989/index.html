Snacks

Double Deuce bacon jalapeño poppers

Jalapeños stuffed with cream cheese and cheddar, wrapped in bacon and baked

Prep 24 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fill and wrap the jalapenos up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; mix the dip up to 2 days ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 jalapenos
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 60g cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 12 rashers streaky bacon, halved
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 lemon
  1. Halve the jalapenos lengthways and scrape out the seeds.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with cheddar and paprika and pack into the halves.
  3. Wrap each in half a rasher of bacon and pin with a toothpick.
  4. Bake on a rack at 200C until the bacon is crisp.
  5. Mix the sour cream with chives, garlic powder and lemon juice for a dip.

Farm loft picklesVegetarian

Dill and garlic pickled baby cucumbers served with sharp cheddar

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min, then 7 days to rest or set · Make ahead: Pickle a week ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g small cucumbers
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 250ml white vinegar
  • 250ml water
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 200g sharp vintage cheddar
  1. Pack the cucumbers into a jar with garlic, dill and peppercorns.
  2. Boil the vinegar with the water and salt and pour over.
  3. Pickle for a week in the fridge.
  4. Serve with sliced cheddar.

Mains

Missouri barbecue ribs

Paprika-rubbed pork ribs baked slow and glazed with molasses tomato sauce

Prep 13 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Rub the ribs up to 1 day ahead and keep wrapped in the fridge; bake and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork ribs
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 150ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp molasses
  1. Rub the ribs with paprika, brown sugar, crushed garlic and cayenne.
  2. Wrap in foil.
  3. Bake at 150C until tender.
  4. Simmer the tomato sauce with molasses.
  5. Unwrap, brush on the glaze and glaze under the grill.

Missouri gooey butter cakeVegetarian

Buttery yellow cake, cream cheese layer. Not the big white mint.

Prep 8 min, cook 48 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and cool the cake up to 1 day ahead, covered; dust with icing sugar just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter cake mix
  • 3 eggs, divided
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 320g icing sugar, divided
  1. Mix the cake mix with an egg and melted butter and press into a 20 by 30cm tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, remaining eggs, vanilla and most of the icing sugar.
  3. Pour over the base and bake at 175C.
  4. Cool.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Double Deuce bourbon and gingerVegetarian

Bourbon, lime and bitters topped with cold ginger beer

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Cut the lime wedges up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; build each drink over ice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 3 limes
  • 1 tsp aromatic bitters
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Pour bourbon into each and squeeze in a lime wedge.
  3. Add a dash of bitters to each glass.
  4. Top with ginger beer, stir gently once and garnish with a fresh lime wedge.
