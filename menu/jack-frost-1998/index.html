Snacks

Snow day toastie fingers with tomato soup

Crisp cheese toasties cut in fingers, with homemade tomato soup

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the tomato soup up to 3 days ahead and reheat; build and fry the toasties just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes (400g each)
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 50ml thickened cream
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 40g butter, soft
  1. Fry the onion in the oil until soft.
  2. Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar and simmer, then blitz smooth and stir in the cream.
  3. Spread the bread with mustard and make sandwiches with ham and cheddar. Butter the outsides.
  4. Fry until golden on both sides.
  5. Cut into fingers and serve with cups of soup.

Rinkside pretzel bun hot dogs

Grilled franks in pretzel buns with slow-fried onions and mustard

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fry the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; simmer and grill the franks and warm the buns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 pretzel buns or soft rolls
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  1. Slowly fry the onions in the butter until golden.
  2. Simmer the frankfurts in water, then grill briefly for colour.
  3. Warm the buns in the oven.
  4. Split the buns, add a frankfurt to each and top with onions, mustard, pickles and cheddar.

Mains

Frost family roast beef and Yorkshires

Mustard rosemary roast beef, roast potatoes, Yorkshire puddings and gravy

Prep 30 min, cook 88 min · Make ahead: Rub the beef and parboil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and whisk the batter up to 1 day ahead; roast and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef topside
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 sprig rosemary, chopped
  • 1.2kg potatoes, parboiled
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 110g plain flour
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Rub the beef with the mustard, rosemary and salt. Parboil the potatoes and toss in oil.
  2. Roast the beef at 200C for medium rare, with the potatoes and honey-tossed carrots on another tray.
  3. Rest the beef and turn the oven to 220C. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk into a batter.
  4. Heat a little oil in each hole of a muffin tin, pour in the batter and bake without opening the oven.
  5. Make gravy from the juices with a spoon of the flour and the stock.

Snow day maple roast carrots and sproutsVegetarian

Maple, butter and thyme. Jack's nose is cork, not a carrot.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Trim the sprouts and cut the carrots up to 1 day ahead; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g carrots, cut in batons
  • 400g brussels sprouts, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 30g butter
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  1. Cut the carrots and halve the sprouts.
  2. Toss with the oil and salt and roast at 220C until browned at the edges.
  3. Melt the butter with the maple syrup and thyme leaves, pour over the hot vegetables and roast until glazed.

Sweets

Snowman coconut cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes rolled in coconut. Snowman? No, much more.

Prep 40 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 120ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 80g desiccated coconut
  • 1 tbsp chocolate chips
  • 12 small orange lollies
  1. Beat some of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Divide into cupcake cases and bake at 180C.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and vanilla until fluffy and white.
  5. Dome onto each cupcake and roll in coconut. Press in chocolate chips for eyes and a lolly for a nose.

Harmonica night white hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot white chocolate with cinnamon, whipped cream and coconut

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh; whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 150g white chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 2 tbsp desiccated coconut
  1. Heat the milk with the cinnamon until steaming.
  2. Remove the cinnamon and whisk in the white chocolate and vanilla until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and sprinkle with coconut like fresh snow.
