Snacks

Parmesan thyme crispsVegetarian

Thin baked parmesan rounds with thyme, golden and lacy.

Prep 12 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 4 tbsp fig jam
  1. Mix the parmesan with the thyme.
  2. Spoon small rounds onto a lined tray and flatten into coins.
  3. Bake at 190°C until golden and lacy.
  4. Cool on the tray, 10 min.
  5. Pile up like a vault full of gold and serve with fig jam.

Dragon escape chilli wings

Crisp baked wings glazed with honey, soy and red chilli.

Prep 14 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Salt the wings up to 1 day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge; bake and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the wings with salt.
  2. Bake at 220°C until crisp.
  3. Warm the honey with the soy sauce and the chopped red chillies until sticky.
  4. Toss the wings through the sauce.
  5. Scatter with sesame seeds.

Hogsmeade sausage rolls with brown sauce

Pork and sage sausage rolls with brown sauce, for sneaking back into Hogsmeade at night.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 jar fennel seeds
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar sesame seeds
  • 1 bottle brown sauce
  1. Pork mince with onion and sage rolled in puff pastry, egg washed and baked at 200°C for 25 minutes, with brown sauce. Mix 500g pork mince with 1 grated onion, 1 tbsp chopped sage, 1 tsp fennel seeds, 40g breadcrumbs and plenty of salt and pepper. Halve 2 sheets puff pastry, lay a log of filling along each strip, roll up and seal with beaten egg. Cut each log into 4, brush with egg and scatter with sesame seeds. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve warm with brown sauce.

Mains

Lamb and coffee stew over mash

Lamb stewed with coffee, over mash.

Prep 33 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Stew up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 brown onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 375ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp instant coffee
  • 500ml water
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Chop the onions and carrots.
  2. Brown the lamb in batches in the oil, 12 min.
  3. Add the onions, carrots, stock, coffee and water.
  4. Simmer until tender.
  5. Peel and boil the potatoes, then mash with butter and milk, 25 min.
  6. Serve over the mash with crusty bread on the side.

Great Hall lemon roast chicken

Bird roasted with thyme, potatoes, peas and gravy, for everyone who stayed to fight.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  1. Roast chicken with lemon and thyme, roast potatoes, peas and gravy, laid out for everyone who stayed to fight. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with 40g butter, salt and thyme and stuff with a halved lemon and garlic. Roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potatoes in chunks for 8 minutes, shake to rough the edges and roast in hot oil for 50 minutes. Rest the chicken 10 minutes, then stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan juices and whisk in 300ml chicken stock to make gravy. Serve with buttered peas, laid out on big platters.

Platform nine and three-quarters treacle tartVegetarian

Golden syrup and lemon treacle tart, for the train from platform nine and three-quarters.

Prep 17 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep at room temperature; warm gently before serving with cream
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 400g golden syrup
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1 lemon
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Line a tart tin with the pastry.
  2. Blind bake the case, 20 min.
  3. Warm the golden syrup with the breadcrumbs and lemon zest and juice, 5 min.
  4. Pour into the case.
  5. Bake at 180°C until just set.
  6. Cool slightly, 15 min.
  7. Serve warm with a pour of cream.

Victory pumpkin juiceNo alcohol

Pumpkin and apple juice with cinnamon, served chilled.

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g butternut pumpkin, chopped
  • 1 L apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Chop the pumpkin.
  2. Roast at 200°C until soft.
  3. Cool, 30 min.
  4. Blend with the apple juice, honey and cinnamon until smooth.
  5. Strain into a jug and chill, 1 hr.
  6. Pour over ice.
