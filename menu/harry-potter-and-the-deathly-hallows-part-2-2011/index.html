Snacks

Parmesan thyme crispsVegetarian

Thin baked parmesan rounds with thyme, golden and lacy

Prep 12 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 4 tbsp fig jam
  1. Mix the parmesan with the thyme.
  2. Spoon small rounds onto a lined tray and flatten into coins.
  3. Bake at 190C until golden and lacy.
  4. Cool on the tray.
  5. Pile up like a vault full of gold and serve with fig jam.

Dragon escape chilli wings

Crisp baked wings glazed with honey, soy and red chilli

Prep 10 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Salt the wings up to 1 day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge; bake and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the wings with salt.
  2. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  3. Warm the honey with the soy sauce and the chopped red chillies until sticky.
  4. Toss the wings through the sauce.
  5. Scatter with sesame seeds.

Mains

Lamb and stout stew over mash

Lamb shoulder stewed with onions, carrots and stout, over buttery mash

Prep 25 min, cook 213 min · Make ahead: Stew up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 brown onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 375ml stout
  • 500ml water
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Chop the onions and carrots.
  2. Brown the lamb in the olive oil in batches.
  3. Add the onions, carrots, stout and water.
  4. Simmer until tender.
  5. Peel and boil the potatoes, then mash with butter and milk.
  6. Serve over the mash with crusty bread on the side.

Sweets

Platform nine and three-quarters treacle tartVegetarian

Golden syrup and lemon tart

Prep 17 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep at room temperature; warm gently before serving with cream
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 400g golden syrup
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1 lemon
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Line a tart tin with the pastry.
  2. Blind bake the case.
  3. Warm the golden syrup with the breadcrumbs and lemon zest and juice.
  4. Pour into the case.
  5. Bake at 180C until just set.
  6. Cool slightly.
  7. Serve warm with a pour of cream.

Drinks

Victory pumpkin juiceVegetarianNo alcohol

Pumpkin, apple and cinnamon, chilled. After all this time? Always.

Prep 12 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g butternut pumpkin, chopped
  • 1l apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Chop the pumpkin.
  2. Roast at 200C until soft.
  3. Cool.
  4. Blend with the apple juice, honey and cinnamon until smooth.
  5. Strain into a jug and chill.
  6. Pour over ice.
