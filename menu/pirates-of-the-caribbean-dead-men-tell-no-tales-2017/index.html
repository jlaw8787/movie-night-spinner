Snacks

Parmesan thyme crispsVegetarian

Parmesan and thyme crisps baked into coins, for Scrum, still asking where the dosh is

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g parmesan
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 1 jar fig jam
  1. Heat the oven to 190C. Mix the grated parmesan with chopped thyme.
  2. Spoon small rounds onto a lined tray and flatten into coins.
  3. Bake until golden.
  4. Cool and pile them high with fig jam.

Ghost shark tempura prawns

Prawns in an ice cold soda water batter, fried until crisp, with a lime dip

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; peel the prawns and mix the lime and sweet chilli dipping sauce up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 100g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 200ml soda water, ice-cold
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 limes
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Peel the prawns. Whisk the flour, cornflour and soda water to a thin, lumpy batter.
  2. Heat the oil to 180C.
  3. Dip the prawns in the batter and fry until puffed and crisp.
  4. Stir lime juice into the sweet chilli sauce for dipping.

Star map cheddar crackersOn the printed menu

Crisp cheddar crackers, for Carina reading the stars to find the Trident.

How it is made
  • 150g plain flour
  • 100g butter
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Cheddar cracker dough rolled thin, cut into stars, baked at 180C for 12 minutes until golden and crisp. Rub 100g cold diced butter into 150g plain flour with half a teaspoon salt and a pinch of cayenne. Mix in 150g finely grated vintage cheddar and 2 tbsp cold water and press into a dough. Chill 20 minutes, roll out 3mm thick and cut into stars of different sizes. Bake on lined trays at 180C for 12 minutes until golden and crisp. Cool and serve with a whipped feta and honey dip.

Mains

Caribbean coconut fish

Barbossa lets the Black Pearl out of its bottle.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat gently and cook the fish in it just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 limes
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Crush the garlic, chop the red chillies and dice the tomatoes.
  2. Soften the garlic and chilli in oil, add the tomatoes and coconut milk and simmer.
  3. Add the snapper fillets and simmer gently.
  4. Cook the jasmine rice.
  5. Squeeze in lime and serve over the rice.

Reunion crackling pork bellyOn the printed menu

Skin dried overnight, slow roasted then blasted crisp, apple sauce, once Will is off the Dutchman

How it is made
  • 1.5kg pork belly
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 box cooking salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 apples
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  1. Pork belly scored and roasted at 160C for 3 hours, crackling blasted crisp at 240C, with apple sauce and roast potatoes. Score the skin of a 1.5kg pork belly, pat very dry and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Rub with oil and plenty of salt. Roast on a rack at 160C for 2.5 hours, then turn up to 240C for 20 to 30 minutes until the crackling blisters. Roast 1kg potatoes in the fat for the last hour. Simmer 3 peeled chopped apples with 2 tbsp sugar and a splash of water for 10 minutes for apple sauce. Rest the pork 10 minutes and cut into thick slices.

Bank vault guava duff puddingVegetarian

Rolled sponge with sticky guava, steamed and sliced warm. Jack's crew steals the vault; it's empty.

Prep 24 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Steam the pudding up to 2 days ahead, cool and keep chilled; steam again for 15 minutes and make the sauce on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 60g butter, cold, for the dough
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 250g guava paste
  • 80g butter, softened, for the sauce
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 30ml dark rum
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, add the sugar and milk to make a soft dough.
  2. Roll out, spread with guava paste and roll up.
  3. Wrap in buttered foil and steam.
  4. Beat the softened butter, icing sugar and rum together for the sauce.
  5. Slice and serve with the sauce.

Drinks

Jack's rum, ginger and lime highballVegetarian

Dark rum floated over spicy ginger beer and lime

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Cut the limes and ginger up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; build the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 2 limes
  • 750ml spicy ginger beer
  • 4 slices fresh ginger
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Squeeze a quarter of a lime into each and drop in the wedge.
  3. Pour in the ginger beer, then float rum over the top.
  4. Add a slice of ginger and a stirrer.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Jack trades his compass for a bottle of rum.
