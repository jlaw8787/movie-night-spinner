Wolfhouse giant pretzels with mustard butterVegetarian

Giant soft pretzels, salty and warm, with whipped mustard butter.

Prep 18 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Beat the mustard butter up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; boil and bake the pretzels on the day, as they are best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pizza dough
  • 90g bicarb soda
  • 1 egg
  • rock salt, to taste
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • white vinegar, to taste
  • 3 L water
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Divide the pizza dough into equal pieces and roll into 80cm ropes.
  3. Twist into pretzel shapes.
  4. Simmer the water with the bicarb soda and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds.
  5. Lay on lined trays, brush with egg, scatter with rock salt and bake 15 to 18 minutes until deep brown.
  6. Beat the soft butter with the wholegrain mustard and the honey.
  7. Serve with sliced radishes tossed in vinegar and salt.

Landfill's beer garden ObatzdaVegetarian

Camembert and paprika on dark rye.

Prep 12 min, then 34 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g camembert
  • 60g butter
  • 100g cream cheese
  • sweet paprika, to taste
  • caraway seeds, to taste
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 can lager
  • 1 loaf dark rye bread
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Mash the ripe camembert with a fork until mostly smooth.
  2. Beat in the soft butter, the cream cheese, the sweet paprika, a little caraway seeds, half a very finely diced red onion and the lager.
  3. Season with salt and pepper and chill 30 minutes.
  4. Spread on the slices of dark rye and top with thin red onion rings, chives and a dusting of paprika.

Mains

Schnitzengiggle Tavern pork schnitzel

Crumbed pork schnitzel with warm potato salad and cranberry jam.

Prep 19 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Cook and dress the potato salad up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; pound and crumb the pork up to 4 hours ahead and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork loin steaks
  • 1kg waxy potatoes
  • 100g diced bacon
  • white wine vinegar, to taste
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 100ml chicken stock
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 1 bunch chives
  • plain flour, to taste
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 50g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar cranberry or lingonberry jam
  1. Boil the waxy potatoes 20 minutes and slice.
  2. Fry the diced bacon, add the white wine vinegar, the mustard, the stock and the oil and toss through the warm potatoes with chives.
  3. Pound the pork steaks to 5mm.
  4. Coat in flour, the beaten eggs and the breadcrumbs.
  5. Fry in the butter and the oil for 3 minutes a side, shaking the pan so the crumb puffs.
  6. Serve with lemon and cranberry jam.

Apple strudelVegetarian

Apples in puff pastry with cinnamon, Great Gam Gam's own recipe.

Prep 11 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Roll the strudel up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; brush with egg and bake just before serving so the pastry stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green apples
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 60g sultanas
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Toss the sliced apples with the sugar, the cinnamon and the sultanas.
  2. Roll in the puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 30 minutes.
  3. Dust with icing sugar and serve with cream.

Drinks

Das Boot lemon radler

Cold lager topped with lemon soda in a big stein.

Prep 3 min, cook 1 min · Make ahead: Make the lemon syrup up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; mix with soda water and pour over the lager just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 500ml cans of German lager
  • 100g sugar
  • 4 lemons
  • 600ml soda water
  1. Make lemon syrup by simmering the sugar, the water and the zest of the lemons for 3 minutes, then add the juice of the lemons and chill.
  2. Mix the syrup with the cold soda water.
  3. Pour the cold German lager into each of the steins and top with the lemon soda.
  4. Add a lemon wheel.
  5. For non-drinkers, serve the lemon soda on its own over ice.
