Snacks

Bushwood club sandwiches

Triple decker turkey and bacon sandwiches with lettuce, tomato and mayo

Prep 12 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Grill the bacon up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; toast the bread, build and cut the sandwiches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 300g roast turkey
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  2. Toast the bread.
  3. Layer turkey, bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayonnaise across three slices each.
  4. Pin with toothpicks and cut into quarters.

Gopher hole dipVegetarian

Hot spinach and cream cheese dip baked in a hollowed loaf, bread to dip

Prep 16 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day, bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 250g spinach
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 garlic clove
  1. Hollow the cob and tear the inside into pieces.
  2. Wilt the spinach, squeeze and chop, then mix with the cream cheese, sour cream, parmesan and crushed garlic.
  3. Fill the cob and bake at 180C with the bread pieces around it.

Mains

Judge Smails's prime rib

Roast beef rib with horseradish sour cream and baked potatoes

Prep 8 min, cook 620 min · Make ahead: Season a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 potatoes
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Season the beef and leave in the fridge overnight.
  2. Roast at 220C to start, then lower to 160C until 52C inside.
  3. Rest the beef. Bake the potatoes alongside.
  4. Mix the horseradish into the sour cream.
  5. Carve thick and serve with the potatoes, sour cream and chives.

Sweets

Country club sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with hot fudge, whipped cream, peanuts and a cherry

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Hot fudge up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm; whip the cream and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 50g peanuts
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate with a little of the cream for hot fudge.
  2. Whip the remaining cream and chop the peanuts.
  3. Scoop ice cream into four tall glasses, pour over the fudge and top with cream, peanuts and a cherry. Keep it well away from the pool.

Drinks

Nineteenth hole peach iced tea lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Peach black iced tea mixed with fresh lemonade, served over ice

Prep 9 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 2 peaches
  • 4 lemons
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 400ml cold water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • ice, to serve
  1. Slice the peaches. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water with the peaches.
  2. Remove the bags and cool with the peaches in.
  3. Stir the juice of three lemons with the sugar and cold water.
  4. Fill four tall glasses with ice, pour in half tea and half lemonade, and add a peach slice, mint sprig and lemon wheel.

When to serve
