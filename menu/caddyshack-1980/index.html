Snacks

Bushwood club sandwiches

Triple decker turkey and bacon sandwiches with lettuce, tomato and mayo.

Prep 12 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Grill the bacon up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; toast the bread, build and cut the sandwiches just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 12 slices of white bread
  • 300g roast turkey
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Grill the bacon until crisp, 10 min.
  2. Toast the bread, 5 min.
  3. Layer turkey, bacon, lettuce, tomato and mayonnaise across three slices each.
  4. Pin with toothpicks and cut into quarters.

Gopher hole dipVegetarian

Hot spinach and cream cheese dip baked in a hollowed loaf, bread to dip.

Prep 16 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day, bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 250g spinach
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 garlic clove
  1. Hollow the cob and tear the inside into pieces.
  2. Wilt the spinach, squeeze and chop, then mix with the cream cheese, sour cream, parmesan and crushed garlic.
  3. Fill the cob and bake at 180°C with the bread pieces around it, 25 min.

Caddy shack mini chilli cheese dogs

Cocktail franks in soft rolls with beef chilli, melted cheddar and diced onion, in paper boats.

How it is made
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 16 mini rolls
  • 120g cheddar
  1. Cocktail franks in soft mini rolls, smothered in a quick beef chilli, melted cheddar and diced onion, served in paper boats. Brown 250g beef mince with half a diced onion, then add 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 150ml water and simmer 15 minutes until thick. Simmer 16 cocktail franks for 5 minutes. Split 16 mini rolls, add a frank, spoon over chilli and top with 120g grated cheddar. Grill 2 minutes until the cheese melts and finish with diced onion.

Mains

Judge Smails's prime rib

Roast beef rib with horseradish sour cream and baked potatoes.

Prep 8 min, cook 2.5 hours, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Season a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 potatoes
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Season the beef and leave in the fridge overnight.
  2. Roast at 220°C to start, then lower to 160°C until 52°C inside, 2 hr.
  3. Rest the beef. Bake the potatoes alongside, 20 min.
  4. Mix the horseradish into the sour cream.
  5. Carve thick and serve with the potatoes, sour cream and chives.

Al Czervik's garlic shrimp surf and turf

Tenderloin seared pink, topped with garlic shrimp in chilli butter, with shoestring fries.

How it is made
  • 4 eye fillet steaks
  • 60g butter
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 400g green prawns, peeled
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 800g frozen shoestring chips
  • 1 bag rocket
  1. Eye fillet seared and roasted pink, topped with garlic prawns cooked in butter, chilli and lemon, with shoestring chips and a rocket salad. Season 4 eye fillet steaks of 180g. Sear in a smoking pan 2 minutes a side, then roast at 200°C for 6 minutes to 54°C inside and rest 5 minutes. In the same pan melt 60g butter with 4 sliced garlic cloves and a pinch of chilli, add 400g peeled green prawns and cook 2 minutes. Squeeze in lemon and add parsley. Spoon the prawns over the steaks. Serve with 800g oven-baked shoestring chips and rocket.

Country club sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with hot fudge, whipped cream, peanuts and a cherry.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Hot fudge up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm; whip the cream and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 50g peanuts
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate with a little of the cream for hot fudge, 6 min.
  2. Whip the remaining cream and chop the peanuts.
  3. Scoop ice cream into four tall glasses, pour over the fudge and top with cream, peanuts and a cherry. Keep it well away from the pool.

Drinks

Nineteenth hole peach iced tea lemonadeNo alcohol

Peach black iced tea mixed with fresh lemonade, served over ice.

Prep 9 min, cook 7 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 2 peaches
  • 4 lemons
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 400ml cold water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • ice, to serve
  1. Slice the peaches. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water with the peaches.
  2. Remove the bags and cool with the peaches in, 30 min.
  3. Stir the juice of three lemons with the sugar and cold water.
  4. Fill four tall glasses with ice, pour in half tea and half lemonade, and add a peach slice, mint sprig and lemon wheel.
