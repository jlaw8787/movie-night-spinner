Snacks

Slap shot cheeseburger mini burgers

Puck-shaped chuck patties seared hard, melted cheddar, pickles, onion and smoky sauce in soft buns.

Prep 5 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; sear and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chuck mince
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 8 mini burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 pinch smoked paprika
  1. Shape the mince into 8 thick rounds like pucks and season well.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and smoked paprika for the burger sauce. Slice the pickles and the onion thinly.
  3. Sear the patties in a very hot pan for 3 minutes a side.
  4. Lay a half slice of cheddar on each and cover for 1 minute to melt.
  5. Toast the buns and fill with the patties, sauce, pickles and onion, 3 min.

Chubbs's golf ball cheese balls

Cream cheese and cheddar rolled into balls and coated in chopped pecans.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100g pecans
  1. Beat the cream cheese with grated cheddar, Worcestershire sauce and chopped spring onions.
  2. Roll into golf ball sizes and chill, 30 min.
  3. Toast and crush the pecans, roll the balls in them and spear each with a toothpick, 12 min.

Grandma's buttermilk sconesVegetarian

Fluffy buttermilk scones served warm with butter and honey.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 140g butter
  • 300g plain flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • honey, to serve
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour, baking powder and salt.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk, fold the dough over itself a few times and cut into rounds.
  3. Bake at 220°C, 12 min.
  4. Serve warm with butter and honey.

Grandma's meatloaf

Beef and pork meatloaf with a tomato glaze, mash and peas.

Prep 13 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Mix the loaf and press it into the tin up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; glaze, bake and mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 egg
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk. Grate the onion, 5 min.
  2. Mix lightly with the beef and pork mince and egg, and press into a loaf tin.
  3. Glaze with the tomato sauce mixed with the sugar and bake at 180°C, 1 hr.
  4. Peel and boil the potatoes and mash. Cook the peas, 25 min.

Sponsor's sweet onion chicken footlong

Teriyaki chicken, lettuce, tomato and sweet onion sauce in a toasted footlong.

Prep 13 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead and mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead; grill and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 80ml teriyaki sauce
  • 2 long crusty rolls
  • 4 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 2 cos lettuce leaves, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely sliced
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  1. Toss the chicken in half the teriyaki and leave while you slice the salad, 10 min.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, sweet chilli, honey and vinegar for the sweet onion sauce.
  3. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through, brushing with the rest of the teriyaki. Slice.
  4. Split and toast the rolls with the cheese inside, fill with the chicken and salad, drizzle with sauce and cut each into two, 6 min.

Gold jacket lemon barsVegetarian

Shortbread base with tart lemon filling, dusted with icing sugar.

Prep 18 min, cook 42 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 240g plain flour
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 150g soft butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 4 lemons, juiced and zested
  1. Press most of the flour, 60g of the icing sugar and the butter into a lined 20cm tin.
  2. Bake the base at 170°C, 20 min.
  3. Whisk the eggs, caster sugar, remaining flour and the juice and zest of the 4 lemons, and pour over the hot base.
  4. Bake until just set.
  5. Cool, dust with the remaining icing sugar and cut into bars, 45 min.

Drinks

Happy place lemon shandy pitchers

Lager and lemon soda over lemon wheels, served by the pitcher.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill the jugs in the freezer up to 1 day ahead and stir the lemon juice and syrup together up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add soda water and lager to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 L lager
  • 4 lemons
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 500ml soda water
  1. Chill two big jugs in the freezer, 20 min.
  2. Stir the juice of three lemons with the sugar syrup and soda water.
  3. Pour half the lager into each jug down the side, then top each with half the lemon soda.
  4. Add lemon wheels and pour into chilled glasses.
