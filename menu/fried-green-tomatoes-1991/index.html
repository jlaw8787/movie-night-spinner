Snacks

Fried Green Tomatoes

Thick slices in buttermilk and cornmeal, cooked in bacon fat, remoulade, the café's famous plate.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Remoulade up to 2 days ahead, fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green tomatoes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 150g fine cornmeal, seasoned
  • 150g bacon
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  1. Slice the tomatoes thick, salt them and leave for 10 minutes (included in cook time).
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped capers and lemon juice for the remoulade.
  3. Cook the bacon in a large pan until crisp and the fat has run, and set the bacon aside.
  4. Dip the tomatoes in buttermilk then seasoned cornmeal and fry in the bacon fat until golden, turning once.

Biscuits and HoneyVegetarian

Honeycomb and buttermilk biscuits, honey straight from Idgie's bee tree for Ruth.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 140g cold butter
  • 300g plain flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 100g honeycomb
  1. Rub most of the butter into the flour and baking powder.
  2. Fold in the buttermilk, pat out and fold three times, then cut into rounds.
  3. Bake at 220°C until risen and golden.
  4. Serve warm with the remaining butter, honey and a chunk of honeycomb.

Whistle Stop pimento cheese crackers

Sharp cheddar, cream cheese, roast capsicum and hot sauce, pickled jalapeño on top, towanda.

How it is made
  • 250g sharp cheddar
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar roasted red capsicum
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 packet crackers
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  1. Sharp cheddar mixed with mayo, cream cheese, roasted red capsicum and hot sauce, spread thick on crackers and topped with pickled jalapeno. Grate 250g sharp cheddar. Beat 100g cream cheese with 80g mayo until smooth, then fold in the cheddar, 100g finely chopped roasted red capsicum, a dash of hot sauce, half a tsp smoked paprika and pepper. Chill 30 minutes. Spread thickly on 24 crackers and top each with a slice of pickled jalapeno.

Big George's Barbecue Plate

Paprika rubbed pork shoulder, slow roasted and pulled, coleslaw, and the secret's in the sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 hours · Make ahead: Cook the pork up to 2 days ahead, reheat in its juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150ml tomato sauce
  • 80ml cider vinegar
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 loaf white bread
  1. Rub the pork with the paprika, brown sugar, garlic and salt.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C for 5 hours until falling apart (included in cook time).
  3. Simmer the tomato sauce with the vinegar until slightly thick, and pull the pork.
  4. Dress the pork with the sauce. Toss the cabbage with mayonnaise for slaw and serve with white bread. The secret's in the sauce.

Sipsey's buttermilk fried chicken

Brined overnight, spiced flour crust, cooked crunchy, greens and cornbread, for Idgie and Ruth.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 750ml buttermilk
  • 400g plain flour
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 2 bunches kale
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 60g butter
  1. Chicken brined overnight in buttermilk, dredged in peppery seasoned flour and fried crunchy, with garlicky braised greens and warm cornbread. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces overnight in 500ml buttermilk with 2 tsp salt. Mix 300g flour with 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder and 2 tsp pepper. Dredge and fry at 165°C for 12 to 15 minutes until 74°C inside. Wilt 2 bunches kale with garlic and a splash of cider vinegar. Bake cornbread from 150g cornmeal, 100g flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 250ml buttermilk, 1 egg and 60g butter at 220°C for 20 minutes.

Sweets

Pecan PieVegetarian

Toasted nuts in golden syrup and butter in a crisp case, for Evelyn's visits with Ninny.

Prep 0 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep cool
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250ml golden syrup
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 200g pecans, toasted
  1. Line a pie tin with the pastry and chill.
  2. Blind-bake with baking weights until pale gold, then remove the weights.
  3. Whisk the eggs, syrup, sugar and melted butter and stir in the pecans.
  4. Pour into the case and bake at 170°C until just set.
  5. Cool before slicing.

Peach Sweet TeaNo alcohol

Black tea brewed strong with ripe peaches and sugar, iced, with lemon and mint.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1 L just-boiled water
  • 3 peaches, 2 sliced and 1 for serving
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water with two sliced peaches for 5 minutes.
  2. Remove the bags and stir in the sugar while hot.
  3. Cool completely with the peaches in, then chill (included in cook time) and strain.
  4. Pour over ice in tall glasses with lemon, fresh peach slices and mint.
