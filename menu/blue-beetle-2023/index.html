Palmera City street cornVegetarian

Charred corn with lime mayo, crumbled feta and chilli flakes.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime mayonnaise and crumble the feta up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; grill the corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 100g feta
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Mix the mayonnaise with some lime juice and crumble the feta.
  2. Grill the corn over high heat until charred all over.
  3. Brush with the lime mayonnaise, roll in feta and sprinkle with chilli flakes.
  4. Serve hot with lime wedges.

Milagro's chunky guacamole and totoposVegetarian

Chunky avocado, lime, jalapeño and coriander with hot chilli-salted tortilla chips.

Prep 10 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Bake the totopos up to 1 day ahead and keep them airtight; make the guacamole just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 ripe avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp Tajín or chilli salt
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the tortillas into wedges, toss in the oil and bake 10 to 12 minutes until crisp. Dust with the Tajín.
  2. Roughly mash the avocados with the lime juice and a pinch of salt, leaving big chunks.
  3. Fold through the onion, tomato, jalapeño and chopped coriander.
  4. Pile into a bowl and serve with the warm totopos.

Nana's red chilli pork tamales

Masa spread on corn husks around shredded pork in guajillo sauce, steamed 90 minutes.

How it is made
  • 800g pork shoulder
  • 1 onion
  • 1 head garlic
  • 4 dried guajillo chillies
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 pack corn husks
  • 150g lard
  • 400g masa harina
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 500ml chicken stock
  1. Masa spread on corn husks, filled with pork in red chilli sauce, steamed 90 minutes. Simmer 800g pork shoulder in water with onion and garlic for 2 hours, then shred. Blend 4 soaked guajillo chillies with 2 garlic cloves, cumin and 250ml of the cooking liquid and stir through the pork. Soak 16 corn husks in hot water for 30 minutes. Beat 150g lard or butter until fluffy, then beat in 400g masa harina, 2 tsp baking powder, salt and 500ml warm stock. Spread on the husks, add pork, fold up and steam standing for 90 minutes.

Reyes family pozole rojo

Pork and hominy soup with guajillo chilli.

Prep 18 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the pozole up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat; shred the cabbage and slice the radishes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pork shoulder, cut into pieces
  • 1 brown onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 L water
  • 4 dried guajillo chillies
  • 800g tinned hominy
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 4 radishes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 limes
  1. Soak the chillies in hot water. Halve the onion and peel the garlic, 8 min.
  2. Simmer the pork with onion and garlic in the water, 2 hr.
  3. Blend the chillies with garlic, stir in with the hominy and simmer, 30 min.
  4. Shred the cabbage and slice the radishes.
  5. Serve topped with cabbage, radish, oregano and lime.

Graduation carne asada tacos

Skirt steak in lime, orange and garlic, grilled and sliced into tortillas, for Jaime's homecoming.

How it is made
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 1 orange
  • 3 limes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Skirt steak marinated in lime, garlic and orange, grilled and sliced into tacos with salsa. Marinate 800g skirt steak for 2 hours with the juice of 1 orange and 2 limes, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin, a handful of chopped coriander and 2 tbsp oil. Grill over very high heat for 3 to 4 minutes a side, rest 5 minutes and slice thinly across the grain. Mix 3 chopped tomatoes, half a diced white onion, 1 chopped jalapeño, coriander and lime for salsa. Serve in 12 warm corn tortillas with salsa and lime wedges.

Pan dulce sweet bunsVegetarian

Soft yeast buns with a sweet cocoa crumb topping.

Prep 35 min, cook 20 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour, for the dough
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 80g caster sugar, for the dough
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g butter, for the dough
  • 200ml warm milk
  • 60g butter, for the topping
  • 60g plain flour, for the topping
  • 60g caster sugar, for the topping
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, eggs, butter and warm milk for the dough and knead until soft.
  2. Prove the dough, 1 hr.
  3. Shape into buns.
  4. Mix the butter, flour and sugar for the topping with the cocoa into a paste, press a round on each bun and score the top.
  5. Prove the buns again, 30 min.
  6. Bake at 180°C, 20 min.

Cinnamon rice horchataNo alcohol

Chilled rice milk drink with cinnamon, sugar and milk.

Prep 10 min, then 18 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g long grain rice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 L water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Soak the rice and cinnamon sticks in the water overnight.
  2. Soak, 8 hr.
  3. Blend until smooth and strain through a fine cloth.
  4. Stir in the sugar and milk and chill, 2 hr.
  5. Pour over ice and top with ground cinnamon.
