Snacks

Agrabah za'atar yoghurt flatbreadVegetarian

Yoghurt dough flatbreads cooked on a hot pan, brushed with za'atar butter

Prep 13 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 1 day in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 100ml warm water
  • 50g butter
  • 2 tbsp za'atar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, yoghurt and warm water into a dough.
  2. Rest the dough.
  3. Roll into 6 rounds and cook on a smoking hot pan until puffed and blistered.
  4. Brush with butter mixed with za'atar.

Abu's datesVegetarian

Medjool dates filled with orange cream cheese and pistachios, dusted with cinnamon

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 12 medjool dates
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 orange
  • 40g pistachios
  • 1 pinch cinnamon
  1. Split the dates and remove the stones.
  2. Mix the cream cheese with orange zest and chopped pistachios.
  3. Fill each date and dust with cinnamon.

Mains

Sultan's palace chicken

Yoghurt and spice marinated chicken skewers, saffron basmati, garlic sauce, pickles

Prep 13 min, cook 510 min · Make ahead: Marinate a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 lemon
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 4 tbsp garlic sauce
  • pickled turnips, to serve
  1. Mix the yoghurt, crushed garlic, ground cumin, ground coriander, ground turmeric and lemon juice and coat the chicken.
  2. Marinate in the fridge overnight.
  3. Skewer the chicken and grill hot until charred and cooked through.
  4. Cook the basmati with the saffron.
  5. Serve with garlic sauce and pickled turnips.

Sweets

Magic carpet baklavaVegetarian

Walnut and pistachio filo pastry soaked in orange blossom honey

Prep 30 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g walnuts
  • 100g pistachios
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 12 sheets filo pastry
  • 150g butter
  • 200g honey
  • 1 tbsp orange blossom water
  1. Chop the walnuts and pistachios and mix with the cinnamon and sugar.
  2. Layer the filo brushed with melted butter, with the nuts between.
  3. Cut into diamonds and bake at 170C until golden.
  4. Warm the honey with the orange blossom water and pour over the hot baklava. Cool before serving.

Drinks

Genie's mint lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemons, mint and sugar syrup blended with ice and strained, served ice cold

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup and juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead; blend with the mint and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 5 lemons
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 750ml cold water
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Make a sugar syrup by dissolving the sugar in a little hot water, then cool.
  2. Blend the lemon juice, mint, syrup, cold water and ice until frothy.
  3. Strain into four glasses.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Aladdin steals the bread in the market.
