Snacks

Za'atar yoghurt flatbreadVegetarian

Yoghurt dough flatbreads cooked on a hot pan, brushed with za'atar butter.

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 1 day in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 100ml warm water
  • 50g butter
  • 2 tbsp za'atar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, yoghurt and warm water into a dough.
  2. Rest the dough.
  3. Roll into 6 rounds and cook on a smoking hot pan until puffed and blistered.
  4. Brush with butter mixed with za'atar.

Abu's datesVegetarian

Medjool dates filled with orange cream cheese and pistachios, dusted with cinnamon.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 12 medjool dates
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 orange
  • 40g pistachios
  • 1 pinch cinnamon
  1. Split the dates and remove the stones.
  2. Mix the cream cheese with orange zest and chopped pistachios.
  3. Fill each date and dust with cinnamon.

Market hummus and smoky baba

Silky chickpea dip and charred eggplant with sumac and pomegranate, flatbread, one jump ahead.

How it is made
  • 2 eggplants
  • 1 jar tahini
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 x 400g tins chickpeas
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 jar sumac
  • 1 pomegranate
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Silky hummus and charred eggplant baba ganoush, swirled with olive oil, sumac and pomegranate, with warm puffy flatbread to tear. Char 2 eggplants over a gas flame or under the grill for 15 minutes until collapsed, scoop out the flesh and mash with 2 tbsp tahini, garlic, lemon and salt. Blitz 2 drained 400g tins chickpeas with 80g tahini, the juice of 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove and 100ml iced water until silky. Top both with olive oil, sumac and pomegranate seeds. Serve with 4 warm flatbreads.

Sultan's palace chicken

Yoghurt and spice marinated chicken skewers, saffron basmati, garlic sauce, pickles.

Prep 13 min, cook 30 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 lemon
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 4 tbsp garlic sauce
  • pickled turnips, to serve
  1. Mix the yoghurt, crushed garlic, ground cumin, ground coriander, ground turmeric and lemon juice and coat the chicken.
  2. Marinate in the fridge overnight.
  3. Skewer the chicken and grill hot until charred and cooked through.
  4. Cook the basmati with the saffron.
  5. Serve with garlic sauce and pickled turnips.

Agrabah street lamb kofta

Spiced lamb kofta with garlic yoghurt and tomato salad, street food from the stalls.

How it is made
  • 700g lamb mince
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 1 jar ground coriander
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 1 jar pickled turnip
  1. Spiced lamb kofta charred on skewers, with garlic yoghurt, chilli, herby tomato salad and pickled turnip, wrapped in warm flatbread. Mix 700g lamb mince with 1 grated onion, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp cinnamon, 2 tsp ground coriander, chopped parsley and salt. Shape onto 8 skewers and chill 30 minutes. Mix 200g Greek yoghurt with 1 crushed garlic clove and lemon. Dice 2 tomatoes, 1 cucumber and parsley. Grill the kofta on high for 8 to 10 minutes, turning. Wrap in 4 warm flatbreads with everything and pickled turnip.

Magic carpet baklavaVegetarian

Walnut and pistachio filo pastry soaked in orange blossom honey.

Prep 40 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g walnuts
  • 100g pistachios
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 12 sheets of filo pastry
  • 150g butter
  • 200g honey
  • 1 tbsp orange blossom water
  1. Chop the walnuts and pistachios and mix with the cinnamon and sugar.
  2. Layer the filo brushed with melted butter, with the nuts between.
  3. Cut into diamonds and bake at 170°C until golden.
  4. Warm the honey with the orange blossom water and pour over the hot baklava. Cool before serving.

Genie's mint lemonadeNo alcohol

Lemons, mint and sugar syrup blended with ice and strained, served ice cold.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup and juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead; blend with the mint and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 5 lemons
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 750ml cold water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Make a sugar syrup by dissolving the sugar in a little hot water, then cool.
  2. Blend the lemon juice, mint, syrup, cold water and ice until frothy.
  3. Strain into four glasses.
