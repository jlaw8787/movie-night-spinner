Snacks

Orange grove saladVegetarian

Sliced orange, shaved fennel, red onion, black olives and mint

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour ahead, dress at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 4 oranges
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 60g black olives
  • 1 handful mint leaves
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 pinch flaky salt
  1. Peel and slice the oranges into rounds.
  2. Shave the fennel and red onion paper thin.
  3. Arrange on a platter with the olives and torn mint, then dress with oil and flaky salt.

Jake Gittes's spring rolls

Crisp rolls of pork mince, cabbage and carrot seasoned with soy

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Fill a day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, for the filling
  • 16 spring roll wrappers
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, for dipping
  1. Fry the mince until browned, then add the cabbage and carrot and cook until soft, seasoning with soy. Cool.
  2. Roll tablespoons of filling into the wrappers and seal with water.
  3. Heat the oil to 180C and fry in batches until crisp.
  4. Serve with sweet chilli sauce and soy sauce for dipping.

Mains

Whole grilled fish, head on

Whole snapper stuffed with lemon, parsley and garlic

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 whole snapper (about 800 g each)
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Score the snapper three times on each side.
  2. Stuff with lemon slices, parsley and sliced garlic, then rub with oil and salt.
  3. Grill or bake at 220C until the flesh flakes at the thickest part.
  4. Serve whole, heads on.

Albacore Club orange chiffon cakeVegetarian

Tall, light orange sponge made with zest and juice

Prep 30 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs, separated
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 125ml orange juice, for the batter
  • 2 oranges, zested
  • 150g caster sugar, for the yolks
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g caster sugar, for the whites
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp orange juice, for the glaze
  1. Whisk the egg yolks with the oil, the orange juice for the batter, zest, the caster sugar for the yolks and the flour.
  2. Whisk the egg whites stiff with the caster sugar for the whites and fold in.
  3. Bake in an ungreased tube tin at 165C.
  4. Cool upside down in the tin.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the orange juice for the glaze and spoon it over the cake.

Drinks

Orange grove bourbon smashVegetarian

Bourbon muddled with orange, mint and brown sugar syrup

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make per glass
Recipe for 4
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 orange, for the wheels
  • 16 mint leaves
  • 60ml brown sugar syrup
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 60ml lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 2 cup ice
  • 4 cup crushed ice
  1. Cut the oranges into small wedges.
  2. For each drink, muddle 3 orange wedges, mint leaves and brown sugar syrup in a shaker.
  3. Add bourbon, lemon juice and ice and shake hard.
  4. Strain over crushed ice in a rocks glass and garnish with an orange wheel and a mint sprig. For non-drinkers, swap the bourbon for fresh orange juice topped with soda.
