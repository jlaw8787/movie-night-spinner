Grinch green spinach and herb dipVegetarian

Spinach, avocado, basil and yogurt dip with pita chips and cucumber.

Prep 7 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; bake the pita chips up to 3 days ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 1 avocado
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into sticks
  1. Blanch the spinach in boiling water briefly, drain and squeeze dry, 3 min.
  2. Blitz with the avocado, yoghurt, basil, parsley, garlic, lemon juice and salt until smooth and very green.
  3. Cut the pita into wedges, brush with oil and bake at 190°C until crisp, 8 min.
  4. Serve the dip with the pita chips and cucumber sticks.

Mount Crumpit cheesy garlic pull-apartVegetarian

Round loaf stuffed with garlic butter and melted cheese.

Prep 11 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Stuff and wrap in foil up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; bake on the night and add 10 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cob loaf
  • 80g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 small bunch chives, chopped
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  1. Cut the loaf in a criss-cross pattern without cutting through the base.
  2. Melt the butter with the garlic and chives and spoon it into the cuts, 4 min.
  3. Stuff the cuts with mozzarella and parmesan and wrap in foil.
  4. Bake at 180°C, then unwrap and bake until golden and melted, 25 min.
  5. Pull apart at the table.

Whoville roast beast with crispy potatoes

Garlic rosemary beef sirloin roast with roast potatoes.

Prep 17 min, cook 58 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Rub the beef up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge, and parboil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead; roast and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef sirloin roast
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 small bunch rosemary, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.2kg potatoes, quartered
  • 3 tbsp duck fat
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Rub the beef with the garlic, rosemary, oil and salt.
  2. Parboil the potatoes, drain, rough up and toss in duck fat.
  3. Roast the potatoes at 200°C and the beef for medium rare, adding the carrots tossed in honey for the last part, 50 min.
  4. Rest the beef, 15 min.
  5. Whisk the flour into the pan juices with the stock and simmer to make gravy, 8 min.

Donna Who's after-night-shift mac and cheeseVegetarian

Three-cheese mac baked under crunchy crumbs, for Donna Who after her night shift.

Prep 2 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Make the mac and cheese up to 1 day ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g macaroni
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml milk
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g gruyère, grated
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 heads of broccoli
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Cook the macaroni for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, whisk in the milk and simmer until thick. Stir in the cheddar, gruyère and mustard.
  3. Fold the macaroni through the sauce and tip into a baking dish. Top with the panko and parmesan.
  4. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes until golden, with the broccoli florets tossed in the oil and salt on a second tray.
  5. Serve the mac and cheese with the broccoli.

Whoville Christmas pudding with custardVegetarian

Steamed pudding and custard for the Whos' Welcome Christmas.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Make the custard up to 1 day ahead with cling film pressed on the surface and reheat gently; steam the pudding on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g Christmas pudding
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 4 eggs (yolks only)
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 1 jar Christmas sprinkles
  1. Steam the pudding in its basin over simmering water, or follow the packet, 1 hr.
  2. Warm the milk and cream, 5 min.
  3. Whisk the yolks with the sugar and cornflour, pour in the hot milk while whisking, then return to the pan.
  4. Stir over low heat until thick, then add the vanilla, 5 min.
  5. Turn out the pudding, cut into wedges and serve with custard, ice cream and sprinkles.

Max's frothy whipped hot chocolateNo alcohol

Frothy chocolate, whipped cream and green sprinkles for a small heart.

Prep 4 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled; heat the milk and whisk in the chocolate just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp icing sugar
  • 1 jar green sprinkles
  1. Heat the milk with the sugar until steaming, 6 min.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and half the cocoa until melted and frothy, or blitz with a stick blender.
  3. Pour into mugs.
  4. Whip the cream with the icing sugar, spoon on top and finish with green sprinkles and the remaining cocoa dusted over.
