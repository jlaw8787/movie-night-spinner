Snacks

Nate's grilled cheeseVegetarian

Sourdough toasted with gruyère and cheddar.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of sourdough bread
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 100g gruyère, grated
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Butter the outside of the sourdough slices.
  2. Fill with the gruyère and cheddar.
  3. Cook in a pan over medium heat, pressing down, until golden and melting, 6 min.
  4. Cut into fingers and serve.

Paris fashion week canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche and smoked salmon, figs with goat's cheese and honey.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep covered and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 6 fresh figs
  • 100g goat's cheese
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and snipped chives.
  2. Halve the figs and top each with goat's cheese and a drizzle of honey.
  3. Arrange both in neat rows on a platter.

Runway crudités with feta dip and dill

Baby carrots, snow peas, radishes and cucumber around a feta yoghurt dip, Emily's diet food.

How it is made
  • 200g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch baby carrots
  • 150g snow peas
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • olive oil
  1. Baby carrots, snow peas, radishes and cucumber spears around a feta dip, lemon and dill dip finished with olive oil and chilli flakes. Blitz 200g feta with 100g Greek yoghurt, the zest and juice of half a lemon and 2 tbsp olive oil until smooth. Stir through 2 tbsp chopped dill. Spread into a shallow bowl, drizzle with more oil and scatter with chilli flakes. Trim 1 bunch baby carrots, 150g snow peas and 1 bunch radishes, cut 1 continental cucumber into spears, and stand everything in ice water for 10 minutes to crisp up. Drain well and arrange neatly around the dip.

Mains

Miranda's steak

Seared sirloin basted in garlic butter, creamed spinach, roast potatoes.

Prep 2 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day, cook steak fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 50g butter
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 300g baby spinach
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Cut the potatoes into chunks and roast at 220°C until crisp, 45 min.
  2. Sear the steaks in the olive oil in a smoking pan, baste with butter and garlic, then rest, 10 min.
  3. Wilt the spinach and simmer with the cream until thick, 8 min.
  4. Slice the steak and serve with the potatoes and creamed spinach.

Nate's tarragon chicken with leeks

Chicken braised with leeks, white wine and tarragon cream, for Nate's birthday that Andy misses.

How it is made
  • 8 skin-on chicken thighs
  • 2 leeks
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 200ml dry white wine
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 20g butter
  • 1 baguette
  1. Chicken thighs browned in butter, braised with white wine and leeks, finished with cream and fresh tarragon, with crusty baguette to mop the sauce. Season 8 skin-on chicken thighs and brown skin side down in a large pan with 20g butter over medium heat for 8 minutes. Turn and remove. Soften 2 sliced leeks and 2 garlic cloves in the fat for 5 minutes. Add 200ml white wine and boil 2 minutes, then 250ml chicken stock. Return the chicken skin side up, cover and simmer 25 minutes. Stir 150ml cream and 2 tbsp chopped tarragon into the sauce and bubble 3 minutes. Serve with warm baguette.

Cerulean blueberry tartVegetarian

Sweet pastry case with vanilla custard and blueberries, apricot glaze.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 500ml thick custard
  • 250g blueberries
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Press the pastry into a tart tin and blind bake, 20 min.
  2. Cool the case fully, 20 min.
  3. Fill with custard and pile the blueberries on top.
  4. Warm the apricot jam with the water and brush over to glaze, 5 min.
  5. Dust the edge with icing sugar.

Drinks

Miranda's espresso martini

Vodka, coffee liqueur and espresso shaken over ice.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Mix and shake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml vodka
  • 120ml coffee liqueur
  • 120ml espresso, cooled (4 shots)
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Chill 4 coupe glasses in the freezer, 5 min.
  2. Pull the espresso and let it cool, 5 min.
  3. Shake the vodka, liqueur, espresso and syrup hard over ice so it foams, one drink at a time.
  4. Strain into a chilled coupe and float 3 coffee beans on the crema.
