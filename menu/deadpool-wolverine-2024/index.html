Snacks

Chimichanga bites

Fried tortilla rolls of beef, refried beans and cheese

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 sachet taco seasoning
  • 400g refried beans
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 120g tasty cheese, grated
  • 1l vegetable oil for frying
  • 200g sour cream
  • 300g tomato salsa
  1. Brown the mince with the taco seasoning, add the refried beans and cool.
  2. Fill the tortillas with the mix and cheese and roll tightly.
  3. Fry seam down in the vegetable oil at 180C until golden.
  4. Halve and serve with sour cream and salsa.

Void wasteland chipsVegetarian

Paper thin fried potato chips tossed in smoked paprika and garlic powder

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Slice and rinse the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead in cold water; best fried and tossed in the spices straight before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Slice the potatoes paper thin on a mandoline and rinse well.
  2. Dry thoroughly.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170C in batches until crisp.
  4. Toss straight away with paprika, garlic powder and salt.

Johnny Storm's charred chicken shawarma bites

Spiced thigh meat on warm flatbread with garlic sauce, pickles and parsley

Prep 23 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken and make the garlic sauce up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tsp shawarma spice
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 100g pickled turnips or gherkins
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Toss the chicken with the spice, lemon juice, oil and salt and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the yoghurt with the crushed garlic and a pinch of salt.
  3. Cook the chicken in a very hot grill pan for 4 minutes a side until charred, then slice.
  4. Warm the flatbreads, cut each into quarters and top with chicken, garlic sauce, pickles, diced tomato and parsley.

Mains

Void campfire chilli

Beef mince chilli with tomatoes and kidney beans

Prep 10 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 400g kidney beans, drained
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 bag corn chips
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and garlic.
  2. Add the chilli powder, cumin, tomatoes and beans.
  3. Simmer until thick.
  4. Serve with cheddar and corn chips.

Sweets

Red and yellow sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with raspberry sauce and mango puree

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Sauces up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g raspberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 mango
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Blend the raspberries with the sugar and strain.
  2. Blend the mango until smooth.
  3. Scoop ice cream into tall glasses, spoon raspberry sauce down one side and mango down the other.
  4. Pile whipped cream on top.

Logan's whisky and ginger highballVegetarian

Whisky, lime and bitters topped with ginger, over big ice

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best built just before serving to keep the fizz; slice the lime up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml whisky
  • 1 lime
  • 4 dashes bitters
  • 600ml ginger ale
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Put big ice cubes into tall glasses.
  2. Pour in whisky, a little lime juice and a dash of bitters.
  3. Top slowly with cold ginger ale, stir once and garnish with a lime wedge.
