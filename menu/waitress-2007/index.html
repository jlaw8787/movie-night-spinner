Snacks

Bad Baby quiche bites

Mini quiche tarts with brie, ham and chives.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 100g brie
  • 80g leg ham
  • 2 eggs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Cut rounds from the pastry, line a 24 hole mini muffin tin and blind-bake.
  2. Dice the brie and ham and chop the chives.
  3. Fill the cases. Whisk the eggs with the cream, pour in and bake at 170°C.
  4. Bake until just set.

Diner biscuits and gravy

Buttermilk biscuits under creamy sausage gravy.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Bake the biscuits up to 1 day ahead, airtight, and warm through; make the gravy fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 450g plain flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 300g pork sausage mince
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  1. Rub the butter into part of the flour with the baking powder and stir in the buttermilk.
  2. Cut rounds and bake at 220°C.
  3. Brown the sausage mince, stir in the remaining flour and then the milk.
  4. Simmer with lots of cracked black peppercorns until thick.
  5. Split the biscuits and pour over the gravy.

Chicken pot pie

Chicken thigh, carrot, celery and peas in a creamy sauce under puff pastry.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, diced
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Brown the chicken, then add the diced onion, carrots, celery and thyme.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock and cream, and simmer until thick.
  3. Add the peas and tip into a pie dish.
  4. Cover with puff pastry and brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C.

Pie diner meatloaf with brown gravy

Beef and pork, tomato sugar glaze, buttery mash and onion sauce, for the lunch rush.

How it is made
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 100ml milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 300g green beans
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf glazed with tomato and brown sugar, sliced thick over buttery mash with onion brown gravy and green beans. Soak 80g breadcrumbs in 100ml milk and mix lightly with 500g beef mince, 250g pork mince, a grated onion, 1 egg and salt. Press into a loaf tin, spread with 80ml tomato sauce mixed with 2 tbsp brown sugar and bake at 180°C for 1 hour. Mash 1kg potatoes with 60g butter. Soften a sliced onion, stir in 1 tbsp flour and 400ml beef stock and simmer for gravy. Steam 300g green beans.

I Hate My Husband pieVegetarian

Cookie crust, caramel and dark chocolate ganache, finished with flaky salt.

Prep 0 min, cook 30 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 200g digestive biscuits
  • 80g butter
  • 250g caramels
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a pie tin.
  2. Melt the caramels with some of the cream and pour in.
  3. Heat the rest of the cream, pour over the chocolate and stir to a ganache. Pour over the caramel.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Drizzle with more caramel and scatter flaky salt on top.

Old Joe's cinnamon pie crust twists

Leftover pie pastry twisted with cinnamon sugar and baked crisp.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 40g butter
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 1 tin caramel
  • 50ml thickened cream
  1. Buttery shortcrust brushed with melted butter, showered in cinnamon sugar, cut into strips and twisted, baked crisp and caramelised, with warm caramel for dipping. Brush 2 sheets shortcrust pastry with 40g melted butter. Mix 60g sugar with 2 tsp cinnamon and sprinkle over both. Cut into 2cm strips, twist each a few times and lay on lined trays. Bake at 190°C for 10 to 12 minutes until crisp and caramelised. Warm 4 tbsp thick caramel with 1 tbsp cream for dipping.

Lulu's caramel coffee shakeNo alcohol

Chilled coffee blended with vanilla ice cream and salted caramel, whipped cream.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Coffee chilled up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml strong brewed coffee
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 200ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp thick caramel
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 4 shortbread biscuits
  1. Brew the coffee and chill.
  2. Whip the cream.
  3. Blend the coffee, ice cream, milk and caramel with a pinch of salt until smooth.
  4. Pour into tall glasses, top with whipped cream, a drizzle of caramel and crumbled shortbread.
