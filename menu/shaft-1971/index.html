Snacks

Times Square knishesVegetarian

Puff pastry parcels filled with mashed potato and soft fried onion

Prep 30 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the knishes up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brush with egg and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 750g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 30g butter
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender.
  2. Fry the onion soft in butter.
  3. Mash the potatoes with the onion, salt and pepper.
  4. Cut the pastry into 12 squares, add a spoon of filling to each and fold into parcels.
  5. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200C until golden.

Rosemary nuts and warm orange olivesVegetarian

Roasted mixed nuts with rosemary and butter, olives warmed with orange peel

Prep 20 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Roast the nuts up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight; warm the olives with the orange peel to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g mixed nuts
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 250g olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 orange
  1. Toss the nuts with melted butter, chopped rosemary and salt.
  2. Roast at 170C.
  3. Warm the olives in olive oil with strips of orange peel.
  4. Serve both in bowls.

Mains

Harlem smothered chicken

Chicken thighs simmered in onion gravy, over rice with garlicky kale

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Cook the chicken in the gravy up to 2 days ahead and chill; reheat gently and cook the rice and kale on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 100g plain flour, divided
  • 40g butter
  • 2 brown onions
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 bunch kale
  • 2 cloves garlic
  1. Flour the chicken and brown in batches.
  2. Make a dark roux in the pan with butter and flour.
  3. Add the sliced onions and stock.
  4. Simmer the chicken in the gravy.
  5. Cook the rice and braise the kale with garlic.
  6. Serve the chicken and gravy over the rice with the kale.

Sweets

New York cheesecakeVegetarian

Dense baked cream cheese cheesecake on a crushed cookie crust

Prep 25 min, cook 838 min · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g digestive biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 750g cream cheese
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with sugar, eggs and sour cream.
  3. Pour over the base and set the tin in a water bath.
  4. Bake at 150C.
  5. Cool in the oven.
  6. Chill overnight.

Bumpy Jonas whisky sourVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and sugar syrup shaken and topped with a cherry

Prep 9 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; shake with the bourbon and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 3 lemons
  • 60ml sugar syrup (made from caster sugar)
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the lemons.
  2. Shake the bourbon, lemon juice and sugar syrup with ice, in batches.
  3. Strain over fresh ice and top each with a cherry.
