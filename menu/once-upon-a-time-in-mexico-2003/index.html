Cantina chips and salsa rojaVegetarian

Fried tortilla chips with a charred tomato and dried chilli salsa

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Salsa up to 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 dried chillies
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 white onion
  • salt, to taste
  1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water and cut the tortillas into wedges.
  2. Fry the wedges in oil at 180°C in batches until crisp, drain and salt.
  3. Char the tomatoes, chillies, garlic and onion in a dry pan.
  4. Blitz the charred mix rough with salt and serve with the chips.

Plaza elote with chipotle mayoVegetarian

Grilled corn brushed with chipotle lime mayo and rolled in feta

Prep 8 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Boil the corn and mix the chipotle mayo up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; char the corn and finish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • 100g feta
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 small handful coriander
  1. Boil the corn cobs.
  2. Grill over high heat, turning, until charred all over.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with the chipotle and the juice of one lime.
  4. Brush the hot corn with the mayo, roll in crumbled feta and dust with chilli powder and coriander.
  5. Serve with lime wedges.

Day of the Dead concha bunsOn the printed menu

Soft sweet yeasted rolls with a crackly cocoa and vanilla sugar crust, served warm with butter

How it is made
  • 600g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 250g butter
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 1 tub cocoa powder
  1. Soft sweet yeasted buns with a crackly vanilla and cocoa sugar shell scored in a shell pattern, baked golden and served warm with butter. Mix 500g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 80g caster sugar, 2 eggs, 200ml warm milk, 80g soft butter and a pinch of salt into a soft dough and knead 10 minutes. Prove 1 hour, then shape 12 balls. Rub 100g icing sugar, 100g plain flour and 80g butter into a paste, split it and mix cocoa into half. Press a disc onto each bun and score a shell pattern. Prove 40 minutes and bake at 180°C for 18 minutes.

Puerco pibil, banana leaf pork

Pork shoulder marinated in achiote and citrus, wrapped in banana leaves, roasted 4 hours

Prep 50 min, cook 4 hours, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Cooks well a day ahead, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 50g achiote paste
  • 2 oranges
  • 3 limes
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 packet banana leaves
  • 1 red onion
  • 16 corn tortillas
  1. Mix the achiote with orange and lime juice, garlic, cumin and oregano and coat the pork.
  2. Marinate overnight in the fridge.
  3. Wrap the pork in banana leaves and then foil.
  4. Roast at 160°C until it falls apart.
  5. Slice the red onion and quick pickle it in lime juice and salt while the pork rests.
  6. Shred the pork and serve with the pickled onion and warm tortillas.

Chicken in red mole with riceOn the printed menu

Thighs simmered in guajillo, almond, sesame, cinnamon and dark chocolate, over rice, before the coup

How it is made
  • 4 dried guajillo chillies
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 50g almonds
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 40g dark chocolate
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 300g long grain rice
  1. Chicken thighs simmered in a rich red mole of dried chillies, tomato, almonds, sesame, cinnamon and dark chocolate, served on rice with toasted sesame. Toast 4 dried guajillo chillies, then soak in 500ml hot chicken stock for 20 minutes. Fry an onion and 3 garlic cloves, add 2 tomatoes, 50g almonds, 2 tbsp sesame seeds and 1 tsp cinnamon. Blitz with the chillies, stock and 40g dark chocolate until smooth. Brown 1kg chicken thighs, pour over the sauce and simmer 40 minutes. Serve on rice with more toasted sesame.

Mexican chocolate flanVegetarian

Caramel custard flan flavoured with dark chocolate and cinnamon

Prep 12 min, cook 53 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 230g caster sugar, divided
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 80g dark chocolate
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 eggs
  1. Melt most of the sugar to a golden caramel and pour into four ramekins.
  2. Warm the milk and cream with the chocolate and cinnamon until smooth.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the remaining sugar and whisk in the warm milk.
  4. Strain over the caramel and set the ramekins in a deep tray with hot water.
  5. Bake in the water bath at 150°C until just set with a slight wobble.
  6. Chill, then run a knife around the edge and turn out.

Tequila with sangrita chaserVegetarian

Blanco tequila with a chaser of orange, tomato, lime and chilli juice

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Mix the sangrita up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; pour the tequila to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml blanco tequila
  • 200ml orange juice
  • 1 lime
  • 100ml tomato juice
  • 1 pinch chilli powder
  1. Mix the orange juice, lime juice, tomato juice and chilli powder for the sangrita.
  2. Pour the tequila into four glasses.
  3. Serve each with a sangrita chaser alongside.
