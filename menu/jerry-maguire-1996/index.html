Laurel's caramelised onion dip with chips

Slow-cooked onion, sour cream and cream cheese dip with thick-cut chips

Prep 12 min, cook 40 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • 250g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • Worcestershire sauce, to taste
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 200g kettle chips
  • 3 celery sticks
  1. Slowly cook the thinly sliced onions in the butter and the oil over low heat for 35 to 40 minutes, stirring now and then, until deep brown.
  2. Add the balsamic and cool.
  3. Chop roughly and mix with the sour cream, the soft cream cheese, the Worcestershire, the chives, salt and pepper.
  4. Chill 1 hour.
  5. Serve with the salted kettle chips and celery sticks.

Rod's Arizona street cornVegetarian

Grilled corn with mayo, crumbled feta and chilli

Prep 6 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; crumble the feta up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 80g feta
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 lime
  1. Grill the corn cobs for 10 minutes, turning, until charred in spots.
  2. Brush each with mayonnaise, roll in the crumbled feta and dust with chilli powder.
  3. Squeeze over lime to serve.

Monday night chorizo pigs in blanketsOn the printed menu

Chorizo rolled in flaky puff with chipotle mayo, honey mustard dip, show me the money

How it is made
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 4 chorizo sausages
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 1 egg
  • sesame seeds
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Mini chorizo wrapped in flaky puff pastry with a smear of chipotle mayo, baked golden and served with a honey mustard for dunking. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut 2 thawed puff pastry sheets into 3cm by 8cm strips. Mix 2 tbsp mayo with 1 tsp chipotle in adobo and spread a little on each strip. Cut 4 chorizo into 6 pieces each and roll each piece in a strip, seam side down. Brush with egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Bake 18 to 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Mix 3 tbsp Dijon with 2 tbsp honey for dipping.

Rod's carne asada

Lime and orange marinated skirt steak with warm tortillas and salsa

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 250g salsa
  1. Marinate the skirt steak for 2 hours in the juice of the limes and the orange, the crushed garlic cloves, oil and salt.
  2. Grill hot for 3 minutes a side, rest and slice thin across the grain.
  3. Serve with the warm flour tortillas and the salsa.

Tidwell family chicken mole enchiladasOn the printed menu

Chicken enchiladas baked in rich mole sauce with cheese. Dinner at home with the Tidwell family.

How it is made
  • 3 dried ancho chillies
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ground cumin
  • ground cinnamon
  • 30g dark chocolate
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 600g roast chicken
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 150g tasty cheese
  • 1 red onion
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Shredded chicken rolled in corn tortillas, smothered in a smoky chilli and dark chocolate mole sauce and cheese, baked bubbling, with pickled onion and crema. Soak 3 dried ancho chillies in boiling water 15 minutes. Blend with 400g tinned tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin, half a tsp cinnamon, 30g dark chocolate and 300ml stock, then simmer 15 minutes. Mix 600g shredded roast chicken with a third of the sauce. Fill and roll 8 corn tortillas, lay in a dish, cover with the rest of the sauce and 150g grated cheese. Bake at 190C for 20 minutes. Top with pickled red onion, sour cream and coriander.

Jerry and Dorothy's wedding cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with white buttercream and sugared flowers, for Jerry and Dorothy's wedding

Prep 24 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes up to 2 days ahead, airtight; beat the icing and pipe on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 125g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 60ml milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, add the eggs and the vanilla, then fold in the self-raising flour and the milk.
  2. Bake in the cases at 175C for 18 minutes.
  3. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and pipe on top.

Tidwell's Kwan prickly pear margaritaVegetarian

Tequila and prickly pear. The Kwan is love, respect, community.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the prickly pear purée up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 6 prickly pears
  • 180ml tequila
  • 5 limes
  • 60ml agave syrup
  • chilli powder, to taste
  • 2 cup ice
  • 1 tbsp salt
  1. Peel the prickly pears (or use the prickly pear purée), blend and strain out the seeds.
  2. Mix the salt with a little chilli powder, wet the rims of the glasses with lime and dip.
  3. For each drink, shake the tequila, the purée, the lime juice and the agave syrup hard with ice.
  4. Strain over fresh ice.
  5. For non-drinkers, skip the tequila and top with soda water.
