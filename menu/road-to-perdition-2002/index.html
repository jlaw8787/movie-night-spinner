Snacks

Toasted cheeseVegetarian

Sharp cheddar on white, fried crisp in butter, cut in fingers. Bread and butter pickles.

Prep 12 min, cook 9 min, then 30 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 small onion
  • 150ml white vinegar
  • 100ml water
  • 60g sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 200g sharp cheddar, sliced
  • 40g butter
  1. Thinly slice the cucumbers and onion.
  2. Heat the vinegar, water, sugar, salt and mustard seeds until dissolved, then pour over the vegetables.
  3. Let the pickles cool and sit while you make the sandwiches, 30 min.
  4. Butter the bread on the outside and fill with the cheddar.
  5. Fry over medium heat until golden on both sides. Cut into fingers and serve with the pickles.

Irish soda breadVegetarian

Warm buttermilk loaf, salted butter, cheddar.

Prep 8 min, cook 40 min
Recipe for 4
  • 450g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 100g salted butter
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  1. Mix the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt, add the buttermilk and bring together into a round. Cut a deep cross on top.
  2. Bake at 220°C, 15 min.
  3. Reduce the oven to 200°C and bake until golden and hollow when tapped. Serve warm with the butter and cheddar.

Mains

Corned beef and cabbage

Simmered all day and sliced thick. Buttered cabbage, carrots, boiled potatoes, hot mustard.

Prep 15 min, cook 3 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg corned silverside
  • 1 onion
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 6 peppercorns
  • 600g potatoes
  • 3 carrots
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 30g butter
  • 1 jar hot English mustard
  1. Peel the onion, potatoes and carrots and cut the potatoes and carrots into chunks. Quarter the cabbage.
  2. Cover the corned silverside with water in a large pot with the onion, bay leaves and peppercorns and bring to a gentle simmer, 5 min.
  3. Simmer the corned silverside gently, topping up the water as needed, 2 hr.
  4. Add the potatoes and carrots and keep simmering.
  5. Add the cabbage for the last of the cooking.
  6. Slice the corned silverside thick and serve with the vegetables, butter and mustard.

Sweets

Apple pieVegetarian

Deep dish, cinnamon apples, sharp cheddar melted on top. Michael, eat your pie.

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg Granny Smith apples
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 120g sharp cheddar, sliced
  1. Peel, core and slice the apples. Cook them gently with the sugar and cinnamon for 5 minutes, then cool.
  2. Line a deep pie dish with one pastry sheet, fill with the apples, cover with the second sheet and cut slits in the top.
  3. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  4. Lay the cheddar on top for the last stretch of baking so it melts.

Drinks

Wake coffee

Brown sugar, soft cream, Irish style. Strictly medicinal.

Prep 12 min, cook 3 min
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml strong brewed coffee
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Whip the cream to soft ribbons so it just holds its shape. Brew the coffee.
  2. Warm 4 heatproof glasses with hot water and tip it out, 3 min.
  3. Add brown sugar, whiskey and coffee to each glass and stir until dissolved.
  4. Float the cream on top over the back of a spoon. Do not stir.

When to serve

Serve dessert at the diner stop on the road to Perdition.
