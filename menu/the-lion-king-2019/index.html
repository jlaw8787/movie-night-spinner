Snacks

Extra crunchy puffed rice noodlesVegetarian

Puffed rice noodles with chilli salt. They're local, says Pumbaa.

Prep 14 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; once cool the noodles hold airtight for up to 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 200g rice vermicelli
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 limes
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  1. Heat the oil to 190C.
  2. Drop in small handfuls of dry vermicelli so they puff up instantly.
  3. Drain and toss with chilli flakes, sea salt and a squeeze of lime.
  4. Pile onto a lettuce leaf and serve.

Rafiki's fruit platterVegetarian

Rockmelon, papaya, mango and passionfruit with lime and chilli salt

Prep 23 min · Make ahead: Cut the fruit up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; add the passionfruit, lime juice and chilli salt just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 rockmelon
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 2 mangoes
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp chilli powder
  1. Cut the rockmelon, papaya and mangoes into wedges.
  2. Arrange on a platter with halved passionfruit.
  3. Squeeze over lime juice and sprinkle with chilli salt.

Pride Rock beef samosas with mint yoghurtOn the printed menu

Spiced mince, onion, peas and garam masala in pastry triangles, fried crisp, cool herb dip

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar garam masala
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 pack spring roll pastry
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  1. Spiced beef mince with onion, peas and garam masala folded into pastry triangles, fried until crisp, with mint yoghurt. Fry 1 diced onion in oil until soft, add 400g beef mince, 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin and 2 grated garlic cloves and cook 8 minutes. Stir in 1 cup frozen peas and season, then cool. Cut 6 sheets spring roll pastry into 3 long strips each, fold a spoonful of filling into triangles and seal with flour paste. Fry at 180C for 3 minutes until crisp. Stir chopped mint and a squeeze of lemon into 200g Greek yoghurt for dipping.

Mains

Jungle hideaway chicken

Peri peri chicken thighs, grilled at the oasis where Simba grows up.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the peri peri sauce up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill and cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 3 red chillies
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  1. Blitz the chillies, lemon juice, garlic, paprika, oil and salt into a peri peri sauce.
  2. Marinate the chicken in the sauce.
  3. Grill until charred and cooked through.
  4. Cook the rice with the turmeric.
  5. Serve together.

Pride Lands braai rump with pap and relishOn the printed menu

Spiced rump grilled over coals with pap and relish. Protect everything the light touches.

How it is made
  • 800g rump steak
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar ground coriander
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 onions
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 250g white maize meal
  • 30g butter
  1. Beef rump steaks rubbed with paprika, coriander and garlic, grilled over coals, sliced with pap and a tomato and onion relish. Rub 800g rump steak with 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground coriander, 2 crushed garlic cloves, oil and salt and stand 30 minutes. For the relish, fry 2 sliced onions until soft, add 4 chopped tomatoes and a pinch of chilli and simmer 15 minutes. Whisk 250g white maize meal into 1L salted boiling water and stir over low heat for 20 minutes until thick, then beat in 30g butter. Grill the steak over hot coals 4 minutes a side, rest 5 minutes and slice over the pap with relish.

Sweets

Mufasa's malva puddingVegetarian

Apricot jam sponge, soaked while hot. Remember who you are.

Prep 16 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar, for the pudding
  • 1 tbsp apricot jam
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 30g butter, melted, for the pudding
  • 250ml milk
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 100g butter, for the sauce
  • 100g caster sugar, for the sauce
  • 500ml custard
  1. Beat the eggs with the caster sugar for the pudding and the jam.
  2. Fold in the flour, melted butter and milk.
  3. Pour into a baking dish and bake at 180C.
  4. Heat the cream with the butter and sugar for the sauce until melted.
  5. Pour the hot sauce over the pudding and let it soak in.
  6. Warm the custard and serve alongside.

Drinks

Watering hole peach rooibos iced teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Rooibos tea steeped with orange peel and honey, with peach, chilled

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rooibos tea bags
  • 1 orange
  • 1l boiling water
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 ripe peaches
  • 4 sprigs mint
  • 250ml sparkling water
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Steep the tea bags and orange peel in the just-boiled water.
  2. Remove the bags and peel and stir in the honey.
  3. Chill the tea.
  4. Slice the peaches.
  5. Fill tall glasses with ice and peaches, pour in the tea and top with sparkling water and a mint sprig.
