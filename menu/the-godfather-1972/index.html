Snacks

Antipasto, wedding style

Salami, aged provolone, olives and roasted peppers, at room temperature.

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min
Recipe for 4
  • 100g sliced salami
  • 100g aged provolone, sliced
  • 100g olives
  • 150g drained roast capsicum
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Arrange the salami, provolone and olives on a platter.
  2. Pile the roast capsicum alongside, drizzle with olive oil and add cracked pepper, 3 min.
  3. Leave out for a few minutes so it comes to room temperature before serving, 3 min.

AranciniVegetarian

Crisp risotto balls, fried golden round a melting mozzarella heart.

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min
Recipe for 4
  • 160g arborio rice
  • 500ml chicken or vegetable stock
  • 30g parmesan
  • 20g butter
  • 80g mozzarella
  • 40g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • sea salt, to taste
  1. Make the risotto the day before: soften 160g arborio in the butter, add 500ml hot stock a ladle at a time over about 20 minutes, stir in the parmesan, then spread it on a tray and chill overnight.
  2. Cut the mozzarella into 8 cubes. Divide the cold risotto into 8 portions and wrap each around a cube, rolling into balls.
  3. Roll the balls in flour, dip in the beaten egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  4. Chill the crumbed balls for 20 minutes so they hold their shape.
  5. Heat the oil to 170°C and fry in batches until golden and hot through, about 4 to 5 minutes. Drain and finish with the sea salt.

Rigatoni, Sunday gravy

Pork and beef meatballs, fennel sausage, slow tomato, pecorino.

Prep 20 min, cook 88 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork and beef mince
  • 3 fennel sausages
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 60g pecorino, grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800g tinned tomatoes
  • 200ml water
  • 400g rigatoni
  1. Mix the mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, a share of the pecorino and a pinch of salt. Form 12 meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs in olive oil with the sausages cut into chunks, then set aside, 12 min.
  3. Soften the onion in the same pot, add the garlic and tomato paste, then stir in the tomatoes and water.
  4. Return the meat, cover partly and simmer gently, stirring and adding water if needed. Check the meatballs are cooked through, 1 hr 15 min.
  5. Boil the rigatoni, toss with the sauce and serve with the meat and the rest of the pecorino, 13 min.

CannoliVegetarian

Crisp shells, orange-scented ricotta, dark chocolate, crushed pistachio.

Prep 20 min, then 2 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 250g ricotta
  • 40g icing sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 30g dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 8 small cannoli shells
  • 30g pistachios, crushed
  1. Drain the ricotta in a lined sieve in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  2. Beat the ricotta with the icing sugar and orange zest, then fold in the chocolate.
  3. Pipe into the cannoli shells just before serving.
  4. Dip the ends in crushed pistachios and dust lightly with icing sugar.

Blood orange sodaNo alcohol

Ruby citrus syrup, cold seltzer.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 100ml blood orange syrup
  • 800ml sparkling water
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice.
  2. Add the syrup to each glass and top with sparkling water. Stir gently, adjusting syrup to taste.
  3. Finish each with a thin twist of orange.
