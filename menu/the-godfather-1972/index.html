Snacks

Connie's wedding antipasto

Salami, provolone, olives and roast peppers laid out like the garden party

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled, serve at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 100g sliced salami
  • 100g aged provolone, sliced
  • 100g olives
  • 150g drained roast capsicum
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Arrange the salami, provolone and olives on a platter.
  2. Pile the roast capsicum alongside, drizzle with olive oil and add cracked pepper.
  3. Leave out for a few minutes so it comes to room temperature before serving.

Golden aranciniVegetarian

Crumbed risotto balls with a molten mozzarella centre, fried golden and served hot with a pinch of sea salt.

Prep 30 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Make the risotto up to 1 day ahead; shape and crumb up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cold cooked risotto, thick
  • 80g mozzarella
  • 40g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • sea salt, to taste
  1. Cut the mozzarella into 8 cubes. Divide the cold risotto into 8 portions and wrap each around a cube, rolling into balls.
  2. Roll the balls in flour, dip in the beaten egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  3. Chill the crumbed balls for 20 minutes so they hold their shape.
  4. Heat the oil to 170C and fry in batches until golden and hot through, about 4 to 5 minutes. Drain and finish with the sea salt.

Clemenza's Sunday sauce

Garlic, tomato, sausage and meatballs, taught to Michael in the hideout kitchen

Prep 30 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Sauce is better made 1 day ahead, keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork and beef mince
  • 3 fennel sausages
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 60g pecorino, grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800g tinned tomatoes
  • 200ml water
  • 400g rigatoni
  1. Mix the mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, a share of the pecorino and a pinch of salt. Form 12 meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs in olive oil with the sausages cut into chunks, then set aside.
  3. Soften the onion in the same pot, add the garlic and tomato paste, then stir in the tomatoes and water.
  4. Return the meat, cover partly and simmer gently, stirring and adding water if needed. Check the meatballs are cooked through.
  5. Boil the rigatoni, toss with the sauce and serve with the meat and the rest of the pecorino.

Clemenza's cannoliVegetarian

The pastry box Clemenza makes sure gets home, whatever else happens

Prep 20 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead; fill shells just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g ricotta
  • 40g icing sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 30g dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 8 small cannoli shells
  • 30g pistachios, crushed
  1. Drain the ricotta in a lined sieve in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  2. Beat the ricotta with the icing sugar and orange zest, then fold in the chocolate.
  3. Pipe into the cannoli shells just before serving.
  4. Dip the ends in crushed pistachios and dust lightly with icing sugar.

Blood orange Italian sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Blood orange syrup, sparkling water and a fresh orange twist.

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Syrup mix ready any time; add soda just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100ml blood orange syrup
  • 800ml sparkling water
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice.
  2. Add the syrup to each glass and top with sparkling water. Stir gently, adjusting syrup to taste.
  3. Finish each with a thin twist of orange.
