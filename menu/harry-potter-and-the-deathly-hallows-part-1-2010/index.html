Burrow wedding canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and dill, plus gruyère choux puffs.

Prep 16 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Bake the gougeres up to 1 day ahead and re-crisp in a hot oven; top the blinis on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 8 sprigs of dill
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 75g gruyère, grated
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and dill.
  2. Boil the water with the butter for the gougeres, 4 min.
  3. Beat in the flour until the dough pulls away from the pan.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the gruyère.
  5. Pipe small balls onto lined trays.
  6. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.
  7. Arrange both on silver trays.

Shell Cottage seaside crab toasts

Mayo and lemon crab on toasted sourdough fingers.

Prep 9 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Mix the crab filling up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; toast the sourdough and pile on just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 8 slices of sourdough bread
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Fold the crab through the mayonnaise with lemon zest, a squeeze of juice and the chives.
  2. Toast the sourdough and cut into fingers, 8 min.
  3. Pile the crab on top.
  4. Finish with smoked paprika.

Tottenham Court Road café toasties

Ham, vintage cheddar and English mustard toasted in buttered bread, a quick café stop.

How it is made
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar English mustard
  • 200g leg ham
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Thick white bread toasted in butter around leg ham, melted vintage cheddar and a scrape of hot English mustard, cut into triangles. Butter the outsides of 8 slices thick white bread. Spread the insides of 4 slices with English mustard, then layer with 200g leg ham and 200g grated vintage cheddar and close with the other slices, butter side out. Cook in a frying pan over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes a side, pressing down with a spatula, until deep golden and the cheese runs. Cut into triangles and serve with a pickle on the side.

Frozen pond foil baked trout with potatoes

Whole trout stuffed with lemon, dill and butter, cooked in foil.

Prep 17 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Stuff and wrap the trout up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; cook the trout and boil the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 whole trout, cleaned
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 8 sprigs of dill
  • 60g butter, for the trout
  • 800g potatoes
  • 30g butter, for the potatoes
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Stuff the trout with lemon slices, dill and the butter for the trout, then season with salt and pepper.
  2. Wrap in oiled foil.
  3. Cook on a hot barbecue or at 200°C, 18 min.
  4. Boil the potatoes until tender.
  5. Toss the potatoes in the butter for the potatoes and the parsley.
  6. Serve the trout in its foil.

Harry's birthday lemon curd spongeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers with lemon zest, filled with lemon curd.

Prep 32 min, cook 30 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 300g lemon curd
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 punnet edible white flowers
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time with the lemon zest, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in 2 tins at 175°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool completely, 30 min.
  4. Whip the cream.
  5. Fill with lemon curd and whipped cream.
  6. Dust with icing sugar and top with white flowers.

Hot cocoa in the tentNo alcohol

Milk whisked with dark chocolate and cinnamon until frothy.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Chop the chocolate and mix it with the cinnamon up to 1 week ahead in a jar; heat the milk and whisk on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Heat the milk until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and cinnamon until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into mugs.
