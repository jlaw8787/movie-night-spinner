Snacks

Safari camp beef samosas with mint yoghurt

Curried beef samosas, mint yoghurt. Eat before the Wachootoo darts

Prep 35 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Filled samosas up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 small bunch coriander, chopped
  • 6 spring roll wrappers
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 small bunch mint, chopped
  1. Fry the mince, onion and garlic until browned. Add the curry powder, garam masala, carrot and peas and cook, then stir through the coriander and cool.
  2. Cut the wrappers into strips, spoon filling at one end and fold into triangles, sealing with water.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180C until golden, working in batches.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with the mint and a pinch of salt for dipping.
  5. Serve the samosas hot with the mint yoghurt.

Monastery momos with chilli tomato sauce

Steamed pork, ginger and spring onion dumplings, fiery dip, for Ace's spiritual retreat

Prep 40 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat the momos up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled on a lined tray under a damp cloth; make the sauce up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, 2 crushed for the filling and 1 for the sauce
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 24 round dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Mix the pork with the ginger, two of the garlic cloves, spring onions, soy and sesame oil.
  2. Place a teaspoon of filling in each wrapper, wet the edges and pleat closed.
  3. Steam in a lined steamer over boiling water.
  4. Blitz the tomatoes, chillies, remaining garlic, salt and vinegar, then simmer to make the sauce.

Mains

Safari grilled chicken with chapati

Lemon chilli chicken, chapati and kachumbari. Circle of Death after dinner

Prep 30 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; make the chapati dough and kachumbari on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Marinate the chicken with the lemon juice, garlic, chilli flakes, paprika, some of the oil and salt.
  2. Marinate in the fridge.
  3. Mix the flour, salt, remaining oil and warm water into a dough, knead, then rest.
  4. Roll the dough into rounds and dry-fry each side in a hot pan.
  5. Grill the chicken, turning once, until cooked through.
  6. Toss the tomatoes, onion, coriander and lime juice for the kachumbari and serve with the chicken and chapati.

Wachootoo sweet potato and peanut stewVegetarian

Kumara, tomato and spinach in spiced peanut sauce over rice with lime, for the peace wedding

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Stew up to 3 days ahead, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 800g sweet potato, in chunks
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 20g ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 120g crunchy peanut butter
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 lime
  • 40g roasted peanuts
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Soften the onion, garlic, ginger, chilli and cumin in the oil.
  2. Add the sweet potato, tomatoes, peanut butter and stock and simmer until tender. Meanwhile cook the rice.
  3. Stir through the spinach until wilted.
  4. Serve over rice with lime and chopped peanuts.

Sweets

Coconut and cardamom mandaziVegetarian

Ace's Slinky stops short. These don't.

Prep 20 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Mix and rest the dough up to 4 hours ahead, covered; roll and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp honey
  1. Mix the flour, half the sugar, cardamom and a pinch of salt. Stir in the coconut milk and egg to a soft dough, then knead briefly.
  2. Rest the dough.
  3. Roll out 1cm thick and cut into triangles.
  4. Fry in oil at 170C, turning, until puffed and golden. Drain and toss in the remaining sugar.
  5. Serve warm with honey.

Drinks

Shikaka passionfruit lime coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh pulp shaken with lime and ginger syrup, topped with soda over crushed ice and mint

Prep 10 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the ginger syrup up to 5 days ahead in the fridge; mix with the passionfruit, lime and soda to order
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 6 passionfruit
  • 3 limes, juiced
  • 800ml soda water
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Heat the sugar with water and ginger until dissolved, then cool and strain.
  2. Scoop the passionfruit pulp into a jug and add the lime juice and ginger syrup.
  3. Fill glasses with crushed ice, divide the passionfruit mix between them and top with soda water.
  4. Stir and finish with mint.
