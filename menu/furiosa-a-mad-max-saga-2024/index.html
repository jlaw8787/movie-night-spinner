Snacks

Green Place garden veg with whipped fetaVegetarian

Garden radishes, carrots and snow peas with whipped feta dip, from the Green Place

Prep 21 min · Make ahead: Whip the feta dip up to 2 days ahead and trim the vegetables up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; arrange to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • olive oil, to taste
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 bunch baby carrots
  • 150g snow peas
  • 1 continental cucumber
  1. Blitz the feta with the Greek yoghurt, the olive oil, the zest and juice of half a lemon and a handful each of mint and dill until smooth and green flecked.
  2. Spread into a bowl and drizzle with oil.
  3. Trim the radishes and the baby carrots, string the snow peas and cut the cucumber into spears.
  4. Soak the vegetables in iced water 10 minutes, drain and arrange around the dip.

Bullet Farm chorizo with smoky honey

Chorizo coins blistered in paprika honey, from the Bullet Farm

Prep 5 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Slice the chorizo up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; fry, glaze and grill the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chorizo sausages
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • smoked paprika, to taste
  • 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
  • 8 slices sourdough
  1. Slice the chorizo sausages into 1.5cm coins.
  2. Heat a heavy pan over high heat and fry without oil for 4 minutes, turning, until blistered and the fat runs.
  3. Add the honey, the smoked paprika and the sherry vinegar and toss for 1 minute until sticky.
  4. Grill the slices of sourdough until charred.
  5. Serve the chorizo on toothpicks with the bread to mop up the pan juices.

Wasteland garlic butter flatbreadsOn the printed menu

Yoghurt flatbreads charred on hot steel and brushed with garlic butter, Wasteland style

How it is made
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 60g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • sea salt flakes
  1. Yoghurt flatbreads slapped onto smoking hot steel until blistered and charred, brushed with garlic and parsley butter and sprinkled with salt. Mix 300g self-raising flour, 250g Greek yoghurt and half a tsp salt into a soft dough and knead 1 minute. Rest 10 minutes, divide into 8 and roll thin. Melt 60g butter with 2 crushed garlic cloves and 2 tbsp chopped parsley. Heat a heavy pan or barbecue plate until smoking and cook each bread 1 to 2 minutes a side until blistered. Brush with garlic butter straight away and sprinkle with flaky salt.

Mains

War Rig barbecue lamb

Rosemary and garlic lamb chops grilled hot with charred corn, off the War Rig barbecue

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min, then 55 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 lamb chops
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  1. Marinate the lamb chops for 1 hour with the chopped rosemary, the garlic cloves, the juice of the lemon and the oil.
  2. Grill over high heat 3 minutes a side.
  3. Grill the corn cobs for 10 minutes alongside and brush with butter.

Gastown blackened chicken with slawOn the printed menu

Blackened spice-rubbed chicken thighs with crunchy slaw, from Gastown, black with fuel

How it is made
  • 8 chicken thigh fillets
  • smoked paprika
  • garlic powder
  • ground cumin
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • cayenne pepper
  • vegetable oil
  • quarter cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 1 red chilli
  1. Chicken thighs coated in a dark spice rub of smoked paprika, garlic, cumin and pepper, seared until blackened, with a sharp lime and chilli slaw. Mix 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp brown sugar, half a tsp cayenne and 1 tsp salt. Rub over 8 chicken thigh fillets with a little oil. Sear in a very hot cast-iron pan or on the barbecue for 5 to 6 minutes a side until blackened and cooked through. Toss a quarter of a shredded cabbage, 1 grated carrot and coriander with the juice of 2 limes, 2 tbsp oil and a sliced chilli.

Sweets

Grilled Green Place peachesVegetarian

Charred peach halves, thyme honey and vanilla ice cream

Prep 6 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; warm the honey with the thyme up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 peaches
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Halve and stone the peaches.
  2. Brush the cut sides with a little oil and grill cut side down for 4 minutes until charred and soft.
  3. Drizzle with the honey warmed with thyme and serve with the vanilla ice cream.

Drinks

Citadel honey malt milkshakesVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick malted milkshakes sweetened with Citadel honey

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml milk
  • 5 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Chill the tall glasses.
  2. Blend the vanilla ice cream, the cold milk, the malted milk powder and the honey until thick and smooth.
  3. Drizzle a little honey down the inside of each glass, pour in the shake and sprinkle with more malt powder.
  4. Serve straight away with straws.

When to serve

Serve the peaches when Furiosa plants the seed.
