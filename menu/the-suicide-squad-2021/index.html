Snacks

Corto Maltese empanadas

Baked beef empanadas with cumin and green olives.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min, 12 min waiting · Make ahead: Fill and crimp the empanadas up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, and bake on the night; chimichurri up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 100g green olives
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 80ml olive oil
  1. Dice the onion and chop the olives.
  2. Brown the mince with onion and cumin, stir in the olives and cool, 12 min.
  3. Cut pastry into rounds, fill, fold and crimp.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden, 20 min.
  5. Blitz parsley, garlic, vinegar and oil for chimichurri.

Polka-Dot Man's franks and beans

Sliced franks in sweet baked beans, spooned out by the ladle on the tray.

Prep 5 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best made on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 6 frankfurts, sliced into coins
  • 2 tins of baked beans
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  1. Tip the beans into a pan with the sugar and mustard.
  2. Add the frankfurt coins and simmer until hot through, 8 min.

Mains

Jungle mission chicken

Grilled lime and chilli chicken thighs, long grain rice and black beans.

Prep 16 min, cook 35 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the lime, garlic and chilli up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill and make the salsa on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 3 limes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 can black beans
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • salt, to taste
  1. Marinate the chicken in lime juice, crushed garlic, chopped chilli and salt, 8 min.
  2. Leave to marinate, 1 hr.
  3. Cook the rice and warm the drained beans, 20 min.
  4. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through, 15 min.
  5. Make a salsa of diced tomato, red onion and lime.

Belle Reve jambalaya

Chicken, smoked sausage and rice cooked in one pot with peppers and Cajun spice.

How it is made
  • 500g chicken thighs
  • 250g smoked sausage
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • crushed tomatoes
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 750ml chicken stock
  1. Brown the chicken and sausage in a big pot and set aside. Soften the onion, capsicum and celery in the same pot, then stir in the Cajun seasoning. Add the rice and stir to coat. Add the tomatoes, stock, chicken and sausage, cover and simmer until the rice is tender. Season.

Starro starfish cookiesVegetarian

Buttery starfish cookies in pink and blue, for Starro the Conqueror.

Prep 32 min, cook 15 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp pink and blue food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into starfish shapes.
  3. Bake at 160°C, 15 min.
  4. Cool completely, 20 min.
  5. Ice pink and blue with icing sugar, colouring and a little water, and pipe white dots along each arm.

Corto Maltese mango batidaNo alcohol

Mango, coconut milk and lime blended with ice.

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Peel and chop the mangoes and juice the limes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; blend with ice and vanilla just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups of ice
  1. Peel the mangoes and juice the limes.
  2. Blend the mango, coconut milk, lime juice, sugar, vanilla and ice until smooth.
  3. Pour into four glasses.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the squad cuts loose in a Corto Maltese bar.
