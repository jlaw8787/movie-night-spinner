Snacks

Corto Maltese empanadas

Baked beef empanadas with cumin and green olives

Prep 25 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Fill and crimp the empanadas up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, and bake on the night; chimichurri up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 100g green olives
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 80ml olive oil
  1. Dice the onion and chop the olives.
  2. Brown the mince with onion and cumin, stir in the olives and cool.
  3. Cut pastry into rounds, fill, fold and crimp.
  4. Bake at 200C until golden.
  5. Blitz parsley, garlic, vinegar and oil for chimichurri.

Polka-Dot Man's spotty skewersVegetarian

Bocconcini, cherry tomato and basil on toothpicks, balsamic dots

Prep 16 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Reduce the balsamic up to 1 week ahead; thread the skewers and drizzle on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g bocconcini
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 100ml balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Reduce the balsamic in a small pan until syrupy and cool.
  2. Thread bocconcini, tomatoes and basil onto toothpicks.
  3. Lay on a white platter and drizzle with oil.
  4. Dot the balsamic all over the plate.

Mains

Jungle mission chicken

Grilled lime and chilli chicken thighs, long grain rice and black beans

Prep 16 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the lime, garlic and chilli up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill and make the salsa on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 3 limes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 can black beans
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • salt, to taste
  1. Marinate the chicken in lime juice, crushed garlic, chopped chilli and salt.
  2. Leave to marinate.
  3. Cook the rice and warm the drained beans.
  4. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through.
  5. Make a salsa of diced tomato, red onion and lime.

Sweets

Starro starfish cookiesVegetarian

Buttery starfish cookies in pink and blue, for Starro the Conqueror

Prep 32 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp pink and blue food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into starfish shapes.
  3. Bake at 160C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Ice pink and blue with icing sugar, colouring and a little water, and pipe white dots along each arm.

Drinks

Corto Maltese mango batidaVegetarian

Mango, coconut milk and lime blended with ice, rum for adult glasses

Prep 11 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Peel and chop the mangoes and juice the limes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; blend with ice and add the rum just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 cups ice
  • 120ml white rum
  1. Peel the mangoes and juice the limes.
  2. Blend the mango, coconut milk, lime juice, sugar and ice until smooth.
  3. Pour into four glasses and add a measure of rum to the adult glasses only.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the squad cuts loose in a Corto Maltese bar.
