Snacks

Gus's corn frittersVegetarian

Lucifer traps Gus under a bowl. Bruno sorts him out

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the batter up to 4 hours ahead and the chive sour cream up to 1 day ahead, both in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1 can corn kernels, drained
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Mix the corn with flour, eggs, spring onion and paprika.
  2. Fry spoonfuls in a little oil until golden on both sides.
  3. Stir chives through sour cream for the dip and serve the fritters warm.

Beads and sash canapés

The mice sew the dress from the stepsisters' beads and sash

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 16 dill sprigs
  • 1 rockmelon
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, a fold of salmon and a sprig of dill.
  2. Cut the rockmelon into wedges, remove the skin and wrap each in prosciutto.
  3. Arrange both on silver trays and serve chilled.

Mains

Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo pumpkin risotto

Creamy arborio risotto with roast pumpkin

Prep 19 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Roast the pumpkin up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; make the risotto and fry the sage on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg butternut pumpkin, diced
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 50g butter
  • 1 1/4l hot chicken stock
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  • 100g butter for sage
  • 12 sage leaves
  1. Roast the pumpkin at 200C until soft.
  2. Stir the rice in butter.
  3. Add the hot stock a ladle at a time.
  4. Mash half the pumpkin through, fold in the rest with parmesan.
  5. Fry the sage leaves in butter and spoon over.

Sweets

Glass slipper meringuesVegetarian

Crisp baked meringue piped into little slippers

Prep 10 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Pipe into little slipper shapes on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 100C, then cool in the oven.
  5. Dust with glitter and serve with cream and strawberries.

Drinks

Midnight blueberry lemonade punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Blueberry juice, lemonade and lime over ice with frozen blueberries

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the blueberry juice and lemonade up to 1 day ahead; add the ice, berries and lime and stir just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1l blueberry juice
  • 1l cold lemonade
  • 1 lime
  • 1 cup frozen blueberries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill a jug with ice and frozen blueberries.
  2. Pour in the blueberry juice and lemonade, squeeze in the lime juice and stir gently.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the pumpkin turns into a coach.
