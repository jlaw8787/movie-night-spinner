Snacks

Gus's corn frittersVegetarian

Sweetcorn fritters fried golden with chive sour cream. Lucifer traps Gus under a bowl.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the batter up to 4 hours ahead and the chive sour cream up to 1 day ahead, both in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1 can corn kernels, drained
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Mix the corn with flour, eggs, spring onion and paprika.
  2. Fry spoonfuls in a little oil until golden on both sides.
  3. Stir chives through sour cream for the dip and serve the fritters warm.

Beads and sash canapés

Smoked salmon blini and prosciutto melon, from the beads and sash the mice sew into a dress

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 16 dill sprigs
  • 1 rockmelon
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, a fold of salmon and a sprig of dill.
  2. Cut the rockmelon into wedges, remove the skin and wrap each in prosciutto.
  3. Arrange both on silver trays and serve chilled.

Breakfast tray scrambled egg toastsOn the printed menu

Little buttered rounds, soft creamy curds, chives and ham, a mouse under the teacup

How it is made
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 6 eggs
  • 50ml cream
  • 100g sliced ham
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Little buttered toasts topped with soft, creamy scrambled egg, chives and a sliver of ham, laid out on silver trays. Cut 12 rounds from 6 slices of bread with a cutter, brush with 20g melted butter and toast at 180C for 8 minutes. Whisk 6 eggs with 50ml cream and salt. Melt 20g butter in a non-stick pan over low heat and stir the eggs slowly for 4 minutes until just set and creamy. Top each toast with a small piece of ham from 100g, a spoon of egg and chopped chives.

Mains

Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo pumpkin risotto

Creamy arborio risotto with roast pumpkin

Prep 26 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Roast the pumpkin up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; make the risotto and fry the sage on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg butternut pumpkin, diced
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 50g butter
  • 1 1/4l hot chicken stock
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  • 100g butter for sage
  • 12 sage leaves
  1. Roast the pumpkin at 200C until soft.
  2. Stir the rice in butter.
  3. Add the hot stock a ladle at a time.
  4. Mash half the pumpkin through, fold in the rest with parmesan.
  5. Fry the sage leaves in butter and spoon over.

Palace lemon thyme roast chickenOn the printed menu

Golden bird with butter and zest, crisp potatoes, honey carrots, for a King after grandchildren

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Golden roast chicken rubbed with butter, lemon and thyme, carved with crisp roast potatoes, honey carrots and a silky pan gravy. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with 40g butter, the zest of 1 lemon, thyme and salt, put the lemon inside and roast at 200C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potato chunks for 8 minutes and roast in oil for 50 minutes. Roast 500g carrots with 1 tbsp honey for 35 minutes. Rest the chicken, then stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the pan juices with 300ml chicken stock and simmer until smooth.

Sweets

Glass slipper meringuesVegetarian

Crisp baked meringue piped into little slippers

Prep 25 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Pipe into little slipper shapes on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 100C, then cool in the oven.
  5. Dust with glitter and serve with cream and strawberries.

Midnight blueberry lemonade punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Blueberry juice, lemonade and lime over ice with frozen blueberries

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the blueberry juice and lemonade up to 1 day ahead; add the ice, berries and lime and stir just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1l blueberry juice
  • 1l cold lemonade
  • 1 lime
  • 1 cup frozen blueberries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill a jug with ice and frozen blueberries.
  2. Pour in the blueberry juice and lemonade, squeeze in the lime juice and stir gently.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the pumpkin turns into a coach.
