Snacks

Smoked salmon

Rye crispbread with cream cheese, smoked salmon, dill and capers.

Prep 4 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rye crispbreads
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 4 sprigs dill
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon
  1. Spread the crispbreads with cream cheese.
  2. Top with folds of smoked salmon, a sprig of dill and a few capers.
  3. Squeeze over a little lemon just before serving.

Welcome party crab dip

Crab, cream cheese and spring onion baked until golden and bubbling.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Slice the spring onions and mix with the crab, cream cheese and mayonnaise.
  2. Spread in a small dish and bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling.
  3. Serve hot with water crackers.

Ramone's general store reindeer sausage rolls

Smoky sausage rolls with sage and hot mustard.

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roll and freeze up to 1 week ahead, bake from frozen with 5 extra minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 500g smoked sausage meat
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 tsp dried sage
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 sheets frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the sausage meat with the onion, sage and paprika.
  2. Pipe or roll the mix along each pastry sheet, roll up and brush with beaten egg.
  3. Cut into pieces and bake until puffed and golden.
  4. Serve hot with mustard.

Mains

Alaskan salmon dinner

Maple mustard salmon, potatoes, beans. Margaret's Canadian, hence the maple.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, roast the salmon fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  • 50g butter
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the salmon with salt and pepper. Mix the maple syrup and mustard and brush over the salmon.
  2. Roast the salmon at 200°C until just cooked through. While it roasts, boil the potatoes.
  3. Steam the green beans, then toss with the potatoes and butter.
  4. Serve everything together.

Barn wedding pot roast and horseradish

Pot roast for the barn wedding Margaret calls off.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 box cornflour
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar grated horseradish
  • 1 lemon
  1. Beef chuck braised for 3 hours with onion, carrots and stock, potatoes added late, sliced with pot gravy and a sharp horseradish cream. Season a 1.5kg beef chuck roast and brown all over in a heavy pot. Add 2 quartered onions, 3 carrots in chunks, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp tomato paste, thyme and 500ml beef stock. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours. Add 800g potatoes in chunks and braise 1 hour more. Lift out the beef and veg, thicken the liquid with 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with water and pour the gravy over the sliced beef. Mix 150g sour cream with 2 tbsp grated horseradish and a squeeze of lemon to serve on the side.

Sweets

Barn wedding cakeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers with strawberry jam and whipped cream, iced.

Prep 33 min, cook 25 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 150g strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1 bunch edible flowers, to decorate
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour. Divide between 2 tins.
  2. Bake at 175°C until golden and springy.
  3. Cool the cakes completely.
  4. Whip the cream, sandwich the cakes with jam and cream, then ice white with icing sugar mixed with milk.
  5. Top with fresh flowers.

Margaret's iced vanilla latteNo alcohol

Espresso, cold milk and vanilla syrup over ice, Margaret's daily order.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Syrup keeps 1 week chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml water
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 300ml strong coffee or 4 double shots espresso
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and split vanilla bean for 3 minutes, then cool.
  2. Make the espresso and cool for 5 minutes.
  3. Fill 4 tall cups with ice, then add a spoonful of vanilla syrup and the cold milk to each.
  4. Pour the espresso over the top, stir and serve with lids and straws.
