Snacks

Baby cornVegetarian

Eat it off the cob with both hands, like Josh

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Blanch the baby corn up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; char with the butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g baby corn
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 limes, zested, then cut into wedges
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Blanch the baby corn in salted boiling water, then drain well.
  2. Char on a hot griddle with the butter.
  3. Sprinkle with chilli flakes, lime zest and salt.
  4. Serve with lime wedges and eat it like a cob.

Caviar on crackers

Water biscuits with crème fraîche, salmon roe and chives, for Josh to spit out politely

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 20 water crackers
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 100g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives, snipped
  1. Spread each cracker with crème fraîche.
  2. Top with salmon roe and a few chives.
  3. Assemble just before serving so the crackers stay crisp.

Mains

Street cart hot dog

Josh and Billy loose in the city

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Onions up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 3 brown onions, one halved and two sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 jar sauerkraut
  1. Simmer the franks in water with the halved onion, then griddle until blistered.
  2. Soften the sliced onions in oil, add the tomato paste and vinegar.
  3. Steam the buns.
  4. Fill with franks, mustard, sauerkraut and onions.

Sweets

Giant choc chip cookieVegetarian

Brown butter dough baked whole in a pizza tray, soft centre, piano keys piped in icing

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g choc chips
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  1. Brown the butter and cool.
  2. Beat with the brown sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips.
  3. Press into a pizza tray and bake at 180C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the water, then pipe piano keys across the cookie.

Drinks

Chocolate egg creamVegetarianNo alcohol

Chocolate syrup, milk and soda stirred to foam, for a boy of twelve in a suit

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Add the syrup and milk to the glasses up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled; pour in the soda water just before serving so the foam rises
Recipe for 4
  • 8 tbsp chocolate syrup
  • 320ml cold milk
  • 600ml chilled soda water
  1. Add syrup and milk to each of 4 tall glasses.
  2. Pour in soda water hard from a height, stirring with a long spoon so a white foam head rises.
  3. Serve with a straw straight away.
