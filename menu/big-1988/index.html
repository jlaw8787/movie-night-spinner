Snacks

Griddled baby cornVegetarian

Charred in butter with crushed red pepper, lime zest and salt.

Prep 3 min, cook 7 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g baby corn
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 limes, zested, then cut into wedges
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Blanch the baby corn in salted boiling water, then drain well, 4 min.
  2. Char on a hot griddle with the butter, 3 min.
  3. Sprinkle with chilli flakes, lime zest and salt.
  4. Serve with lime wedges and eat it like a cob.

Caviar canapés

Salmon roe, crème fraîche and chives on crisp water crackers.

Recipe for 4
  • 20 water crackers
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 100g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives, snipped
  1. Spread each cracker with crème fraîche.
  2. Top with salmon roe and a few chives.
  3. Assemble just before serving so the crackers stay crisp, 4 min.

Mains

Big city hot dog

Steamed bun, mustard, sauerkraut and onions stewed in tomato.

Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 3 brown onions, one halved and two sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 jar sauerkraut
  1. Simmer the franks in water with the halved onion, then griddle until blistered, 10 min.
  2. Soften the sliced onions in oil, add the tomato paste and vinegar.
  3. Steam the buns, 3 min.
  4. Fill with franks, mustard, sauerkraut and onions.

Sweets

The giant cookieVegetarian

Brown-butter chocolate chip, iced with piano keys, cut at the table.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min, 25 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g choc chips
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  1. Brown the butter and cool, 10 min.
  2. Beat with the brown sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips, 8 min.
  3. Press into a pizza tray and bake at 180°C, 18 min.
  4. Cool completely, 15 min.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the water, then pipe piano keys across the cookie.

Drinks

Chocolate egg creamNo alcohol

Syrup, cold milk and seltzer, stirred until the foam rises.

Prep 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 tbsp chocolate syrup
  • 320ml cold milk
  • 600ml chilled soda water
  1. Add syrup and milk to each of 4 tall glasses.
  2. Pour in soda water hard from a height, stirring with a long spoon so a white foam head rises.
  3. Serve with a straw straight away.
