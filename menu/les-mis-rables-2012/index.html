Valjean's loaf with salted butter and radishesVegetarian

Dark-crusted country bread torn at the table, soft butter and salt flakes, for prisoner 24601.

Prep 5 min, cook 75 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep in a paper bag; refresh at 200°C for 5 minutes. Soften the butter and trim the radishes up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 400ml warm water
  • 125g salted butter, softened
  • 1 bunch radishes, trimmed
  • 1 tsp salt flakes
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and warm water to a shaggy, sticky dough. No kneading.
  2. Cover and leave to rise until doubled and bubbly.
  3. Tip onto a well floured bench, fold the edges in to make a round and set it seam side down on floured baking paper.
  4. Heat the oven to 240°C with a lidded cast iron pot inside while the loaf rests.
  5. Lift the loaf on its paper into the hot pot, slash the top and bake with the lid on, then lid off until dark and crusty.
  6. Cool on a rack, then tear and serve with the butter, radishes and salt flakes.

Montfermeil inn pork rillettes

Belly slow cooked with garlic and thyme, shredded into its fat, Master of the House style.

Prep 10 min, cook 3.5 hours, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 1 week ahead; keep sealed under its fat in the fridge and bring to room temperature for 30 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork belly, skin removed, in 3cm cubes
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 200ml water
  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
  • 16 cornichons
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C. Toss the pork with the salt and pepper in a small lidded casserole with the garlic, bay, thyme and water.
  2. Cover and cook slowly until the meat falls apart and sits in its own fat.
  3. Pick out the herbs, then shred the pork and garlic with two forks, working in enough of the fat to make it soft and spreadable.
  4. Pack into jars or a bowl, pour the rest of the fat over the top and chill until set.
  5. Serve at room temperature with the toasted baguette, cornichons and mustard.

Mains

Thénardier's inn coq au vin

Chicken braised in red wine with bacon and mushrooms, carved by Thénardier as he picks pockets.

Prep 28 min, cook 83 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead; it is better reheated. Chill in the pot and warm gently, boiling the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg bone-in chicken thighs, skin on
  • 150g smoked bacon lardons
  • 12 eschalots, peeled
  • 250g button mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml red wine
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 800g small waxy potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  1. Fry the lardons in a large heavy casserole until crisp, then lift out.
  2. Season the chicken and brown it well in the bacon fat in batches, skin side first. Lift out.
  3. Brown the eschalots and mushrooms in the same pot.
  4. Stir in the garlic and flour, then the wine, stock, bay and thyme. Return the chicken and bacon, cover and simmer gently.
  5. Meanwhile boil the potatoes in salted water until tender.
  6. Take the lid off and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon.
  7. Stir in the butter, scatter with parsley and serve with the potatoes.

Little Cosette's winter pears with chocolate sauceVegetarian

Spiced poached pears with warm chocolate sauce, for little Cosette sent into the winter woods.

Prep 3 min, cook 35 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Poach the pears up to 3 days ahead and keep them in their syrup in the fridge; make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L water
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 strips orange peel
  • 4 firm pears, peeled, stalks on
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 125ml thickened cream
  1. Simmer the water, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and orange peel in a saucepan that fits the pears snugly until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add the pears, cover with a circle of baking paper and poach gently, turning now and then, until a knife slides in easily.
  3. Cool the pears in the syrup.
  4. Heat the cream until steaming, pour over the chocolate and stir until smooth.
  5. Stand each pear in a shallow bowl with a spoonful of syrup and pour over the warm chocolate sauce.

ABC Café hot spiced wineVegetarian

Red wine warmed with honey, orange, cinnamon, clove and star anise.

Prep 0 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Warm and steep the wine up to 2 hours ahead, then reheat gently without boiling
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 250ml water
  • 80g honey
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 2 star anise
  1. Put everything in a saucepan and warm over low heat, stirring until the honey melts. Do not let it boil.
  2. Take off the heat and leave to steep.
  3. Ladle into 4 heatproof glasses with a slice of orange in each.
