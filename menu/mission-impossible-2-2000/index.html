Seville flamenco party garlic prawns

Sizzled in olive oil with chilli and smoked paprika, parsley and lemon, where Ethan meets Nyah

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and slice the garlic up to 1 day ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns, peeled, tails on
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 125ml olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 cup flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 baguette
  1. Pat the prawns dry and season with the salt.
  2. Warm the olive oil with the garlic and chilli flakes in a wide pan over low heat until the garlic is pale gold.
  3. Turn the heat up, add the prawns and smoked paprika and cook, turning once, until just pink.
  4. Stir through the parsley, squeeze over the lemon and serve in the pan with torn baguette.

Randwick race day sausage rolls

Pork and fennel in flaky puff, sesame topped and baked golden, tomato sauce, members' stand style

Prep 30 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Roll and chill the unbaked logs up to 1 day ahead, or freeze them for a month; brush, cut and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 onion, finely grated
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, toasted and crushed
  • 1/2 cup fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg, for the filling
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 3 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Mix the mince with the onion, fennel, breadcrumbs, egg for the filling, salt and pepper.
  2. Cut each pastry sheet in half, lay a log of filling along each strip, roll up and seal underneath.
  3. Chill the logs so they cut cleanly.
  4. Brush with the beaten egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and cut each log into four.
  5. Bake until puffed and deep golden.
  6. Rest, then serve with the tomato sauce.

Sydney Harbour barramundi with salsa verde

Crisp-skinned fillets on crushed lemon potatoes, sharp herb caper sauce, before the Chimera chase

Prep 15 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa verde up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; cook the potatoes and fish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 1 cup flat-leaf parsley leaves
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1 lemon, juiced, for the salsa verde
  • 125ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, for the potatoes
  • 1 lemon, zested, for the potatoes
  • 4 barramundi fillets, skin on, about 180g each
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, for the fish
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender.
  2. Finely chop the parsley, mint, capers and garlic and stir with the mustard, lemon juice, extra virgin olive oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Drain the potatoes and crush lightly with the olive oil, lemon zest and salt. Keep warm.
  4. Score the fish skin, pat dry and season with salt.
  5. Cook the fish skin side down in the hot oil, pressing flat, until crisp, then flip to finish.
  6. Serve the fish on the potatoes with the salsa verde spooned over.

Ambrose's Bare Island lamb

Ambrose's hideout in the old fort on Bare Island, out in Botany Bay

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lamb racks, 8 cutlets each
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 20g butter
  • 150ml red wine
  • 200ml beef stock
  1. Boil the potatoes for 18 minutes, drain and crush with a little oil.
  2. Rub the lamb with chopped rosemary, garlic, oil and salt, sear fat side down, then roast at 200C for 20 minutes. Rest for 10 minutes.
  3. Pour the wine into the roasting pan, bubble on the stove, add the stock and reduce for 5 minutes.
  4. Cook the peas for 3 minutes and crush with the butter and chopped mint.
  5. Cut into cutlets and serve on the potatoes and peas with the jus.

Sydney summer pavlovaVegetarian

Crisp shell, marshmallow centre, whipped cream, mango, kiwifruit and passionfruit, an Aussie finish

Prep 30 min, cook 137 min · Make ahead: Bake the pavlova up to 1 day ahead and leave it in the turned-off oven or an airtight container; top with cream and fruit just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 mango, sliced
  • 2 kiwifruit, peeled and sliced
  • 4 passionfruit
  1. Heat the oven to 150C and draw a 20cm circle on baking paper on a tray.
  2. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, then add the caster sugar a spoonful at a time and whisk until thick and glossy.
  3. Fold in the cornflour, vinegar and vanilla and spread inside the circle with a slight hollow.
  4. Turn the oven to 120C and bake.
  5. Turn the oven off and cool the pavlova inside with the door ajar.
  6. Whip the cream with the icing sugar.
  7. Pile the cream on the pavlova and top with mango, kiwifruit and passionfruit pulp.

Seville sherry cobblerVegetarian

Dry fino shaken with orange, lemon and syrup, crushed ice and raspberries

Prep 9 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 240ml fino sherry
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 4 cup crushed ice
  • 1 orange, cut into half wheels
  • 125g raspberries
  • 4 mint sprigs
  1. Stir the sugar into the hot water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Shake the sherry, orange juice, lemon juice and syrup hard with ice, in two batches.
  3. Strain into four glasses packed with crushed ice.
  4. Garnish with orange half wheels, raspberries and mint.
