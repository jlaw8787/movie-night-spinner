Snacks

Garden herb cheeseVegetarian

Goat's cheese log rolled in herbs and lemon zest, with honey and crackers.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Roll the cheese in the herbs up to 2 days ahead, wrapped in the fridge; drizzle with honey on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g goat's cheese log
  • 1 tbsp rosemary, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp thyme, finely chopped
  • 1 pinch dried lavender
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Chop the rosemary and thyme finely.
  2. Roll the cheese log in the herbs, lavender and lemon zest.
  3. Drizzle with honey and serve with crackers.
  4. Keep a sprig of rosemary on the board for luck.

Owens kitchen loaded nachos

Corn chips baked under black beans, cheddar and jalapeño, for dancing round the kitchen.

How it is made
  • 300g corn chips
  • 400g tinned black beans
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 2 avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Corn chips layered with black beans, cheddar and jalapeño, baked bubbling and topped with lime guacamole, sour cream and fresh tomato salsa. Layer 300g corn chips in a baking dish with a drained 400g tin black beans, 200g grated cheddar and 40g pickled jalapeños. Bake at 200°C for 8 to 10 minutes until melted. Mash 2 avocados with lime juice and salt. Dice 2 tomatoes with half a red onion, coriander and lime. Top the nachos with the guacamole, salsa and 150g sour cream.

Mains

Aunts' roast chicken

Lemon, garlic and rosemary roast chicken with potatoes, carrots and gravy.

Prep 28 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Stuff the chicken and cut the vegetables up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 800g potatoes
  • 3 carrots
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  1. Stuff the chicken with half a lemon, garlic and rosemary.
  2. Cut the potatoes and carrots and put them in the tray.
  3. Roast at 200°C.
  4. Rest the chicken.
  5. Make a quick gravy from the juices with flour and lemon.

Owens house roast pumpkin and sage soup

Roast pumpkin and garlic blended smooth with crisp sage leaves on top.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg pumpkin
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 40g butter
  • 40g pepitas
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  1. Roast pumpkin and garlic blended silky with stock and cream, topped with crisp sage brown butter, toasted pepitas and warm sourdough. Toss 1.5kg peeled pumpkin chunks and 1 garlic bulb, halved, with oil and salt and roast at 200°C for 40 minutes. Squeeze out the garlic and blend with the pumpkin, 1 litre chicken stock and 100ml cream until smooth, then heat through. Fry 12 sage leaves in 40g butter until crisp and the butter browns. Toast 40g pepitas. Serve topped with sage butter and pepitas, with sourdough.

Lavender shortbreadVegetarian

Buttery shortbread flavoured with dried lavender.

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, soft
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 1 tsp dried lavender
  1. Beat the butter with most of the sugar.
  2. Mix in the flour and the lavender rubbed between your fingers.
  3. Press into a tray and prick.
  4. Bake at 160°C.
  5. Dust with sugar and cut while warm.
  6. Cool in the tray.

Chocolate cake for breakfastVegetarian

Chocolate buttermilk cake with chocolate frosting.

Prep 17 min, cook 43 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 230g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 40g cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate with most of the butter.
  2. Whisk in the eggs, sugar and buttermilk.
  3. Fold in the flour and bicarb.
  4. Bake at 170°C.
  5. Cool.
  6. Beat the icing sugar with the cocoa and remaining butter and frost.

Drinks

Midnight margaritas

Tequila, orange liqueur and lime shaken, with a flaky salt rim.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; rim the glasses and shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 80ml orange liqueur
  • 4 limes, juiced
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Juice the limes and rim four glasses with flaky salt.
  2. For each drink, measure 45ml tequila, 20ml orange liqueur and the juice of 1 lime into a shaker.
  3. Shake each drink hard with ice.
  4. Strain into the glasses. Best after midnight in the kitchen.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when the aunts start dancing in the kitchen.
