Agamemnon's feta and honey feast platterVegetarian

Feta with honey, olives, vegetables and warm bread, laid out for a king.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 4 pita breads
  • 150g kalamata olives
  1. Blitz the feta with yoghurt and olive oil until smooth.
  2. Spread in a shallow bowl, drizzle with honey and sprinkle with oregano.
  3. Cut the cucumber into batons and halve the tomatoes.
  4. Warm the pita, brush with oil and cut into wedges, 5 min.
  5. Arrange everything on a big platter with the olives.

Ploughman's board with apple chutney

Cheddar, ham, apple, pickled onions and homemade apple chutney.

Prep 23 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Chutney up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples
  • 1 onion
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp mixed spice
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 150g leg ham
  • 1 jar of pickled onions
  1. Dice the apple and onion.
  2. Simmer with brown sugar, cider vinegar and mixed spice until thick and sticky, then cool, 20 min.
  3. Slice the loaf and cut the cheddar into wedges.
  4. Slice the remaining apples.
  5. Arrange on a board with the sliced ham, pickled onions and chutney.

Winston the ogre's beef and barley stew

Chuck steak, carrot, mushroom and barley stew.

Prep 40 min, cook 2 hr 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chuck steak
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1.2 L beef stock
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Dice the beef, onion, carrots and mushrooms and toss the beef in flour.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in oil, 15 min.
  3. Add the onion, carrots, mushrooms and garlic and cook, 5 min.
  4. Add tomato paste, stock, barley and bay leaves.
  5. Simmer covered until the beef is tender, 2 hr.
  6. Mash the butter with horseradish cream.
  7. Serve with the horseradish butter and a crusty loaf.

King Agamemnon's slow-roast lamb with lemon potatoes

Shoulder roasted low with garlic, oregano and citrus until falling apart, potatoes, Greek salad.

Prep 5 min, cook 4 hr · Make ahead: Rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg lamb shoulder, bone in
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, in wedges
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 3 tomatoes, in chunks
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, in chunks
  • 100g feta
  • 60g kalamata olives
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Stud the lamb with garlic and rub with oregano, the juice of 1 lemon, 2 tbsp oil and salt, 10 min.
  2. Roast covered in a deep tray with the stock until falling apart, 3 hr.
  3. Add the potatoes tossed in lemon juice and oil, uncover and roast at 200°C until golden, 50 min.
  4. Toss the tomato, cucumber, feta and olives with the last of the oil and serve with the lamb.

Napoleon's little custard tartsVegetarian

Tiny flaky custard tarts with caramelised tops, small enough for Napoleon.

Prep 20 min, cook 24 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Best the day they are made
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Roll the pastry into a tight log and cut into rounds.
  2. Press each round into a greased mini muffin tin.
  3. Whisk the egg yolks, sugar, cornflour, milk and vanilla in a pan and stir over medium heat until thick, 10 min.
  4. Spoon the custard into the cases.
  5. Bake at 230°C until the tops blister, 14 min.
  6. Cool, then dust with cinnamon, 15 min.

Titanic iceberg ginger fizzNo alcohol

Ginger syrup, lemon and soda water over a large ice sweet.

Prep 12 min, cook 5 min, 3 hr 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 3 lemons
  • 800ml soda water
  • 1 bunch of mint
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Slice the ginger.
  2. Simmer the sugar, water and ginger for 5 minutes.
  3. Cool and strain the syrup, 20 min.
  4. Freeze water in a small container to make big ice blocks, or use large ice cubes, 3 hr.
  5. Put a block in each glass, add ginger syrup and lemon juice, then top with soda water.
  6. Garnish with mint and a lemon wheel.

When to serve

Serve the main when the boys reach Agamemnon's feast.
