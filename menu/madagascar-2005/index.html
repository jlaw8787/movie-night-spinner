Snacks

Penguins' ice-cold sushi rolls

Salmon and avocado rolls in nori, served with soy

Prep 45 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 nori sheets
  • 200g sashimi salmon
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp pickled ginger
  1. Cook the rice and season with vinegar and sugar. Cool.
  2. Spread over the nori, fill with salmon and avocado and roll tight with a mat.
  3. Slice with a wet knife.
  4. Serve with soy and pickled ginger. Smile and wave, boys.

Island sweet potato fritters with mango salsaVegetarian

Crisp spiced sweet potato fritters with a mango, chilli and lime salsa

Prep 16 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa up to 4 hours ahead; fry the fritters just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g sweet potato, peeled and grated
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 eggs
  • 75g plain flour
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 1 long red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Squeeze the grated sweet potato in a clean tea towel to dry it.
  2. Mix with the spring onions, eggs, flour, cumin and a good pinch of salt.
  3. Fry heaped spoonfuls in the oil over medium heat, flattened, 3 minutes a side until crisp and cooked through. Drain on paper towel.
  4. Mix the mango, chilli, lime juice and chopped coriander with a pinch of salt.
  5. Serve the fritters hot with the salsa on top.

Mains

Alex's steak

Pan seared rib eye, garlic butter, fries. Sweet, juicy steak.

Prep 15 min, cook 520 min · Make ahead: Steaks dry-brine overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into shoestrings
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Salt the steaks and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Mash the butter with the garlic and parsley.
  3. Fry the shoestring potatoes in the vegetable oil until golden and crisp.
  4. Sear the steaks in a smoking cast iron pan, flipping every minute, and pull at 54C.
  5. Rest for 8 minutes and top with the garlic butter.

Sweets

Marty's birthday zebra browniesVegetarian

Zebra-striped chocolate brownies. Don't abuse the wish.

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 120g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 80g plain flour
  • 200g cream cheese
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter, whisk in most of the sugar and two eggs and fold in the flour.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the rest of the sugar and the last egg.
  3. Spread the brownie in a tin and swirl the cheesecake through in stripes.
  4. Bake at 170C for 30 minutes.

King Julien's caramel grilled pineappleVegetarian

Grilled pineapple wedges in brown sugar caramel butter

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the caramel up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm gently; toast the coconut up to 3 days ahead in an airtight container. Grill the pineapple on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pineapple, cut into long wedges
  • 60g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 tub coconut ice cream
  • 50g coconut flakes, toasted
  1. Grill the pineapple on a hot pan for 3 minutes a side until charred.
  2. Melt the butter with the sugar, bubble for 2 minutes, then stir in the lime juice and salt.
  3. Toss the pineapple in the caramel.
  4. Serve warm with scoops of coconut ice cream and toasted coconut flakes.

Drinks

King Julien's mango passionfruit punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Mango, pineapple, passionfruit and lime, for the New York giants

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the nectar, pineapple juice, passionfruit and lime juice up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Add the mango, ice and lemonade just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml mango nectar
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 4 passionfruit, pulp
  • 2 limes, juiced, plus wedges
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 600ml chilled lemonade
  • 3 cup crushed ice
  1. In a large jug stir the nectar, pineapple juice, passionfruit pulp and lime juice.
  2. Add diced mango and plenty of crushed ice.
  3. Top with lemonade just before serving and pour into tall glasses with a lime wedge.
