Snacks

Niagara Falls hot dogs

Clark was getting hot dogs at Niagara

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Cook the onions slowly in the butter until soft and golden.
  2. Grill the franks, turning, until blistered.
  3. Toast the rolls, tuck in the franks and top with onions, mustard and sauce.

Three outlaws cheese friesVegetarian

Three outlaws from Krypton land

Prep 3 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheeses up to a day ahead; bake the fries to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen thick-cut fries
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • 50g parmesan, finely grated
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 125g sour cream
  1. Bake the fries at 220C until crisp.
  2. Pile into an ovenproof dish, scatter over the three cheeses and grill until bubbling.
  3. Top with spring onions and serve with sour cream.

Mains

Annie's number one special meatloaf

Rocky calls Annie's special garbage

Prep 20 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Shape the loaf up to a day ahead and chill; bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 cup fresh breadcrumbs
  • 125ml milk, plus 125ml for the mash
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 375ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix with the mince, onion, egg, Worcestershire and salt and shape into a loaf in a tin.
  2. Mix the tomato sauce and sugar, spread over the top and bake at 180C.
  3. Boil and mash the potatoes with the butter and the rest of the milk.
  4. Simmer the stock with the pan juices and thicken with the cornflour.
  5. Slice thick and serve with mash and gravy.

Keep it on the flame minute steak

Keep it on the flame, Rock

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Batter the onion rings up to 2 hours ahead and chill; fry and sear to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 150g minute steaks
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, plus 1l for frying
  • 30g butter
  • 2 large onions, cut in thick rings
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 250ml chilled soda water
  • 750g frozen shoestring fries
  1. Bake the fries at 220C until crisp.
  2. Whisk the flour and soda water into a batter, dip the onion rings and fry at 180C until golden.
  3. Season the steaks and sear in a very hot pan with the oil, 1 minute a side, adding the butter at the end.
  4. Serve with the rings and fries.

Kneel before Zod chocolate cream pieVegetarian

Zod makes the President kneel

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the pie up to a day ahead and chill; top with cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g chocolate ripple biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 500ml milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 22cm pie dish, then chill.
  2. Whisk the milk, sugar, cornflour and yolks in a pan over medium heat until thick.
  3. Stir in the chocolate until smooth, pour into the base and chill until set.
  4. Whip the cream and pile it on top.
