Babysitter's pizza scrollsVegetarian

Dough rolled with tomato, mozzarella and oregano, sliced and baked, garlic dip, for Vicky and Julian

Prep 12 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Roll and slice the scrolls up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge, then rest 30 minutes before baking; make the dip up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 100g tomato passata, for the filling
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g tomato passata, for the dip
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Heat the oven to 200C, line a tray and roll the dough into a 40 x 30cm rectangle.
  2. Spread with the passata for the filling and sprinkle with the oregano, mozzarella and half the parmesan.
  3. Roll up from the long side, cut into 12 scrolls and lay them cut side up. Brush with the oil and sprinkle with the rest of the parmesan.
  4. Rest the scrolls so they puff.
  5. Bake until golden.
  6. Simmer the passata for the dip with the garlic, chilli flakes and salt and serve warm with the scrolls.

Laurie's stockpile beef jerky

Thin strips in soy, brown sugar, garlic and smoked paprika, dried low until chewy, basement rations

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 9 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead; once dried the jerky keeps 1 week airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef topside, trimmed
  • 125ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper, cracked
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Freeze the beef to firm it up for slicing.
  2. Slice the beef 4mm thick against the grain.
  3. Whisk the soy, sugar, Worcestershire and spices, toss the beef through and marinate in the fridge.
  4. Heat the oven to 75C, pat the strips dry and lay them on wire racks over trays.
  5. Dry in the oven with the door slightly open, turning once, until leathery but still bendy.
  6. Cool before packing.

Laurie's safe room chilli

Stockpiled for forty years and cooked in the compound kitchen

Prep 30 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead, or freeze it for 3 months; it is better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, in 3cm cubes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 chipotle chillies in adobo, chopped
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 800g tinned black beans, drained
  • 20g dark chocolate
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 200g corn chips
  • 1 lime, in wedges
  1. Season the beef with the salt and brown in batches in the oil.
  2. Soften the onions and capsicum in the pot.
  3. Stir in the garlic, chipotle, cumin, paprika, oregano and tomato paste.
  4. Add the tomatoes and stock, return the beef, cover and simmer until tender.
  5. Stir in the beans and chocolate and simmer uncovered to thicken.
  6. Serve with sour cream, cheddar, corn chips and lime wedges.

Strode family dinner roast chicken and pumpkin

Whole bird with lemon and thyme, roasted with red onion and potatoes, gravy, Karen's normal night

Prep 5 min, cook 91 min · Make ahead: Cut the vegetables up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 600g pumpkin, in wedges
  • 600g potatoes, in chunks
  • 2 red onions, in wedges
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 100g mixed salad leaves
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Stuff the chicken with the halved lemon and thyme, rub with oil and salt.
  2. Roast the chicken with the vegetables tossed in oil until golden and cooked through.
  3. Rest the chicken. Stir the flour and stock into the tray juices and simmer for gravy.
  4. Carve and serve with the vegetables, gravy and salad.

Burning basement whoopie piesVegetarian

Baked for the Halloween dance at Haddonfield High

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the cakes up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 40g cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 115g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 125g butter, softened, for the filling
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1/4 tsp orange food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line two trays.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa, bicarb and salt.
  3. Beat the butter for the cakes with the sugar until pale, then beat in the egg and half the vanilla.
  4. Fold in the dry mix and buttermilk alternately.
  5. Spoon 24 rounds onto the trays and bake until springy.
  6. Cool the cakes.
  7. Beat the butter for the filling with the icing sugar, milk, the rest of the vanilla and the colouring until fluffy.
  8. Sandwich the cakes together in pairs.

Laurie's rye and gingerVegetarian

Whiskey over ice with muddled fresh ginger and lime, topped with spicy ginger beer

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Slice the ginger and cut the limes up to 1 day ahead; build each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 5cm piece ginger, thinly sliced
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 3 cup ice
  • 180ml rye whiskey
  • 800ml ginger beer, chilled
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  1. Muddle the ginger slices with the lime juice in four tumblers.
  2. Fill with ice and add the rye.
  3. Top with ginger beer, stir and finish with a lime wedge.
