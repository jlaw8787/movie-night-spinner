Al's cheese puffsVegetarian

Baked choux puffs with cheddar and gruyère, light and crisp

Prep 5 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g vintage cheddar
  • 40g gruyère
  1. Boil the water with the butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute.
  2. Cool, beat in the eggs, then the grated vintage cheddar and gruyère.
  3. Pipe small rounds and bake at 200°C until puffed.

Woody's Roundup smoky cowboy beansOn the printed menu

Bacon beans for Woody's Finest Hour.

How it is made
  • 200g bacon
  • 1 onion
  • 2 x 400g tins cannellini beans
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp molasses
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 2 cornbread muffins
  • 40g cheddar
  1. Beans slow-cooked with smoky bacon, onion, molasses, mustard and tomato until thick and sticky, served in tin mugs with cheesy cornbread crumbs. Fry 200g diced bacon until crisp, add 1 diced onion and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 2 x 400g tins drained cannellini beans, 400g tinned tomatoes, 2 tbsp molasses, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp smoked paprika and 250ml water. Cover and bake at 160°C for 90 minutes, stirring once. Crumble 2 cornbread muffins with 40g cheddar and toast under the grill to scatter on top.

Prospector's chilli

Slow braised beef chuck chilli with chipotle and kidney beans, sour cream, cornbread

Prep 32 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 chipotles in adobo
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tins kidney beans
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  1. Dice the beef, onion and garlic.
  2. Brown the beef in batches.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, chipotles and cumin, then the tomatoes and drained beans. Braise until tender.
  4. Bake the cornbread as the packet says.
  5. Serve with sour cream and squares of cornbread.

Al's Toy Barn honey butter fried chickenOn the printed menu

Buttermilk drumsticks dredged twice, cooked crunchy, sweet glaze, coleslaw, as seen on the TV ad

How it is made
  • 12 chicken drumsticks
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Buttermilk-brined drumsticks double-dredged and fried crunchy, drizzled with honey butter, with creamy coleslaw and pickles on the side. Soak 12 drumsticks overnight in 500ml buttermilk with 1 tsp salt. Mix 250g plain flour with 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder and salt. Dredge, dip back in buttermilk and dredge again. Fry at 165°C for 12 to 14 minutes until 75°C inside. Melt 50g butter with 3 tbsp honey and drizzle over. Toss half a shredded cabbage and 1 carrot with 120g mayonnaise and vinegar.

Bullseye's caramel apple wedgesVegetarian

Apple wedges dipped in caramel and rolled in crushed roasted peanuts

Prep 8 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caramels
  • 2 tbsp thickened cream
  • 3 apples
  • 60g roasted peanuts
  1. Melt the caramels with the cream until smooth.
  2. Cut the apples into wedges and pat dry. Crush the peanuts.
  3. Dip the wedges halfway and roll in peanuts, then set on baking paper in the fridge.

Jessie's yodelling cookiesVegetarian

Cutout sugar cookies shaped like hats and boots, iced in cowgirl colours

Prep 22 min, cook 23 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 egg white
  • red food colouring, a few drops
  1. Beat the butter and sugar with the egg, then mix in the flour.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll and cut into cowboy hats and boots.
  4. Bake at 180°C.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the egg white, tint some red and ice the cooled cookies in cowgirl colours.

Jessie's yee-haw sarsaparilla floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Sarsaparilla over vanilla ice cream and caramel, served with long spoons

Prep 5 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses up to 2 hours ahead in the fridge; build each float just before serving so the foam holds
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp caramel sauce
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 1/4l sarsaparilla
  • 4 hat-shaped biscuits
  1. Chill four tall glasses.
  2. Drizzle caramel sauce down the inside of each glass and add ice cream.
  3. Pour sarsaparilla slowly down the side, letting the foam settle before topping up.
  4. Perch a biscuit on each rim and serve with long spoons and straws.
