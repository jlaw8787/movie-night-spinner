Snacks

Al's cheese puffsVegetarian

Baked choux puffs with cheddar and gruyère, light and crisp

Prep 10 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g vintage cheddar
  • 40g gruyère
  1. Boil the water with the butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute.
  2. Cool, beat in the eggs, then the grated vintage cheddar and gruyère.
  3. Pipe small rounds and bake at 200C until puffed.

Mains

Prospector's chilli

Slow braised beef chuck chilli with chipotle and kidney beans, sour cream, cornbread

Prep 17 min, cook 220 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 chipotles in adobo
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tins kidney beans
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  1. Dice the beef, onion and garlic.
  2. Brown the beef in batches.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, chipotles and cumin, then the tomatoes and drained beans. Braise until tender.
  4. Bake the cornbread as the packet says.
  5. Serve with sour cream and squares of cornbread.

Sweets

Bullseye's caramel apple wedgesVegetarian

Apple wedges dipped in caramel and rolled in crushed roasted peanuts

Prep 8 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caramels
  • 2 tbsp thickened cream
  • 3 apples
  • 60g roasted peanuts
  1. Melt the caramels with the cream until smooth.
  2. Cut the apples into wedges and pat dry. Crush the peanuts.
  3. Dip the wedges halfway and roll in peanuts, then set on baking paper in the fridge.

Jessie's yodelling cookiesVegetarian

Cutout sugar cookies shaped like hats and boots, iced in cowgirl colours

Prep 37 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 egg white
  • red food colouring, a few drops
  1. Beat the butter and sugar with the egg, then mix in the flour.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll and cut into cowboy hats and boots.
  4. Bake at 180C.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the egg white, tint some red and ice the cooled cookies in cowgirl colours.

Drinks

Jessie's yee-haw sarsaparilla floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Sarsaparilla over vanilla ice cream and caramel, served with long spoons

Prep 9 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses up to 2 hours ahead in the fridge; build each float just before serving so the foam holds
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp caramel sauce
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 1/4l sarsaparilla
  • 4 hat-shaped biscuits
  1. Chill four tall glasses.
  2. Drizzle caramel sauce down the inside of each glass and add ice cream.
  3. Pour sarsaparilla slowly down the side, letting the foam settle before topping up.
  4. Perch a biscuit on each rim and serve with long spoons and straws.
