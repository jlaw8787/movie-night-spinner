Snacks

Chinatown dumplings

Pork and chive parcels, crisp-bottomed and steamed, black vinegar dip, before the triad war.

Prep 3 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 12 frozen pork and chive dumplings
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 80ml water
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the black vinegar, soy sauce and chilli oil for dipping.
  2. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat and pan-fry the dumplings until the bottoms crisp.
  3. Add the water, cover immediately and steam until cooked through (or the packet time), then remove the lid and let the water evaporate.
  4. Serve with the dipping sauce.

Hotel suite prawn cocktail

Poached prawns on iceberg, sharp horseradish cocktail sauce, for Frank's release from Sing Sing.

Prep 18 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Prawns and sauce up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tsp horseradish
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • Worcestershire sauce, a dash
  1. Poach the prawns in simmering salted water.
  2. Plunge into iced water, peel and chill.
  3. Mix the tomato sauce, horseradish, juice of half the lemon and a dash of Worcestershire.
  4. Fill 4 glasses with shredded iceberg, hang the prawns over the rims and spoon the sauce in the middle.

Mains

Frank's suite steak dinner

Seared rib eye, cracked pepper, brandy and cream sauce, chips, room service for the crew.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 800g rib eye steak, in four pieces
  • 1 L neutral oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp cracked black peppercorns
  • 40ml brandy
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin frites, rinse and dry thoroughly.
  2. Fry the frites in oil at 140°C, then drain and cool.
  3. Fry again at 185°C until crisp. Season with salt.
  4. Pat the steaks dry and season. Sear in a heavy pan on both sides, then rest.
  5. Add the cracked black peppercorns to the pan, then carefully add the brandy away from the flame. Add the stock and reduce.
  6. Add the cream and simmer. Serve the steaks with the sauce and frites.

New York cheesecakeVegetarian

Dense baked cream cheese, sour cream and lemon on a biscuit base.

Prep 10 min, cook 2.5 hours, then 10.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 120g digestive biscuits
  • 50g butter
  • 450g cream cheese
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 80g sour cream
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 lemon
  1. Line a 16cm springform tin and wrap the outside in two layers of foil. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into the base.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, then beat in the sour cream, eggs and lemon zest. Pour into the tin.
  3. Set the tin in a roasting pan with hot water halfway up its sides. Bake at 150°C until the edges are set and the centre gently wobbles.
  4. Turn off the oven, leave the door ajar and cool inside.
  5. Refrigerate overnight, then cut into generous slices.

Frank's suite champagne cocktailsVegetarian

Bitters-soaked sugar cube and a splash of cognac, topped with brut and a lemon twist.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 1 bottle aromatic bitters
  • 60ml cognac
  • 1 bottle brut champagne, chilled
  • 1 lemon
  1. Put a sugar cube in each flute and shake bitters onto each. Add cognac, top slowly with chilled champagne and twist a strip of lemon peel over each glass.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Frank walks back into his Plaza suite fresh out of prison.
