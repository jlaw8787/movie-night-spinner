Snacks

Book tour canapés

Salmon blinis and melon. The fans only want Dash

Prep 30 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp snipped chives
  • 1/2 rockmelon
  • 100g prosciutto
  1. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, a fold of smoked salmon and chives.
  2. Cut the rockmelon into neat wedges, remove the skin and wrap each with a thin slice of prosciutto.
  3. Lay both out on trays in rows.

Cover model coconut prawns

Coconut prawns. Alan chases Fairfax's men on a motorbike

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 2 hours ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, peeled, tails on
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup shredded coconut
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Dip each in beaten egg, then press into the mixed coconut and panko.
  3. Fry in the oil at 180C for 2 minutes until golden and crisp, in batches. Serve with the sweet chilli sauce.

Mains

Island grilled fish

Lime chilli snapper grilled in banana leaf, with coconut rice and black beans

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 2 hours, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 3 limes, juiced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 banana leaves, softened
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 400g black beans, drained
  1. Rub the snapper with the lime juice, garlic, chilli and salt. Wrap each in a softened banana leaf.
  2. Grill for 10 minutes.
  3. Cook the rice in the coconut milk with a pinch of salt. Warm the black beans.
  4. Serve the fish in its leaf.

Crown of fire mango sorbetVegetarian

Mango lime sorbet. The crown was red seashells, not fire.

Prep 20 min, cook 250 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, scrape and serve fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 3 ripe mangoes
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 3 passionfruit
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Blend the mango flesh with the syrup and lime juice until smooth.
  3. Freeze in a tray, scraping with a fork every hour for 4 hours.
  4. Scoop into glasses and top with passionfruit pulp.

Coconut water, pineapple juice, mint and lime over ice, no alcohol

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1l coconut water
  • 1l pineapple juice
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1 handful mint leaves
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and a few mint leaves.
  2. Pour in equal parts coconut water and pineapple juice, squeeze in a lime wedge and stir well.
