Snacks

Delivery pizzettes

Sixteen thin rounds, tomato, mozzarella, pepperoni.

Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 150g grated mozzarella
  • 16 slices of pepperoni
  1. Roll the dough thin and cut 16 rounds with a glass.
  2. Top each with a spoon of sauce, a pinch of mozzarella and a slice of pepperoni.
  3. Bake at 230°C until golden underneath, 8 min.

Potato knishesVegetarian

Potato, onion cooked slow in butter, white pepper, flaky dough, baked golden. Hot mustard.

Prep 10 min, cook 55 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g potatoes, peeled
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 40g hot English mustard
  • salt and white pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and mash, 15 min.
  2. Cook the onion in the butter over low heat until soft and mix into the mash with salt and white pepper, 15 min.
  3. Spread along the pastry, roll into logs, cut into 8 and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden. Serve with the mustard, 25 min.

Mains

Sirloin on ciabatta

Seared hard, sliced thin, jammy onions, arugula, horseradish cream, toasted bread.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 600g sirloin steak
  • 4 ciabatta rolls
  • 100g horseradish cream
  • 60g rocket
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Cook the onions in the butter over low heat until jammy, 30 min.
  3. Sear the steaks hard on both sides, rest, then slice thin, 8 min.
  4. Split and toast the ciabatta, spread with horseradish cream and pile with steak, onions and rocket.

Sweets

Rainbow cookiesVegetarian

Three almond layers, green, white and red, apricot jam between, dark chocolate on top.

Prep 35 min, cook 50 min, then 8 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g almond meal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 150g apricot jam
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 1 pack food colouring
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, then beat in the eggs, almond meal and flour. Divide into 3, tint one green and one red with the food colouring.
  2. Spread each into a lined 20x30cm tin.
  3. Bake each layer at 180°C until just set, in batches, then cool, 36 min.
  4. Stack the layers with jam between.
  5. Press under a tray in the fridge overnight.
  6. Spread with melted dark chocolate, set, and cut into small squares, 14 min.

Drinks

Iced espresso sodaNo alcohol

A double shot over ice and tonic water, an orange slice. Stir before you sip.

Prep 13 min
Recipe for 4
  • 70g espresso coffee beans
  • 600ml tonic water, chilled
  • 1 orange
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Brew 4 double espressos and let them cool for a few minutes, 8 min.
  2. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice and an orange slice each, add chilled tonic, then pour an espresso slowly over the back of a spoon so it floats on top. Stir at the table.
