Snacks

Corn dodgers

Fried cornmeal cakes made with bacon fat, eaten warm with butter.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 100g bacon, chopped
  • 200g cornmeal
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 40g butter, to serve
  1. Fry the bacon for its fat and keep the fat, 8 min.
  2. Mix the cornmeal and salt with some of the fat and the boiling water.
  3. Shape into small ovals.
  4. Fry in a cast iron pan until crisp and eat warm with butter.

Fort Smith boarding house biscuitsVegetarian

Flaky buttermilk biscuits with butter and golden syrup.

Prep 6 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 100g cold butter
  • 300g plain flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  • extra butter, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub the butter into the flour, baking powder and salt, 6 min.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk and fold a few times.
  3. Cut into rounds and bake, 12 min.
  4. Serve with butter and golden syrup.

Mains

Dugout supper stew

Beef chuck, onion, carrots and potatoes cooked slowly until thick.

Prep 25 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 600g potatoes, chopped
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in a heavy pot, 15 min.
  2. Add the onions, carrots, potatoes, stock and thyme.
  3. Put the lid on and cook over low coals or at 160°C until thick.

Fort Smith buttermilk fried chicken

Buttermilk-soaked chicken fried crisp and hot, for a long ride out of Fort Smith.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  • 1 jar honey
  • 8 buttermilk biscuits
  1. Chicken pieces brined overnight in buttermilk and hot sauce, fried crisp in cast iron, with honey drizzle, warm biscuits and a vinegar slaw. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces in 400ml buttermilk, 1 tbsp hot sauce and 2 tsp salt overnight. Dredge in 250g plain flour mixed with 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt and pepper. Shallow fry in 3cm oil at 165°C for 12 to 15 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked to 75°C. Toss shredded cabbage with cider vinegar, sugar and salt. Serve with honey drizzled over and warm biscuits.

Mattie's hard-bargain molasses cookiesVegetarian

Chewy molasses cookies, priced the way Mattie haggles with Stonehill.

Prep 22 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 170g butter, softened
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 80ml molasses
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 50g caster sugar, for rolling
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line trays, 3 min.
  2. Beat the butter and brown sugar, add the egg and molasses, 7 min.
  3. Mix in the flour, bicarb, ginger and cinnamon.
  4. Roll into 24 balls and coat in caster sugar.
  5. Bake until cracked on top.

Rooster's bourbon hot toddy

Bourbon, honey, lemon and hot water with cinnamon and cloves.

Prep 4 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook ahead; cut the lemon wheels and stud them with cloves up to 1 day ahead, then build each mug to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 600ml just boiled water
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 12 whole cloves
  1. Put honey and lemon juice in each of 4 mugs.
  2. Add bourbon to each and top with just boiled water.
  3. Drop in a cinnamon stick and a lemon wheel studded with cloves. For Mattie use hot apple juice in place of the bourbon and water.

When to serve

Serve the cornbread when Rooster and LaBoeuf start shooting corn dodgers out of the air.
