Snacks

Green goo dipVegetarian

Flying rubber as a dip: avocado, peas, lime, mint, feta, pita

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, crisps fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 avocados
  • 200g frozen peas, thawed
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 tbsp chopped mint
  • 100g feta
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g sugar snap peas
  1. Cut the pita into wedges, brush with oil and bake at 200C until crisp.
  2. Blitz the avocado, peas, lime juice, mint and feta until smooth and very green.
  3. Serve the goo with the crisps and sugar snap peas.

Medfield basketball cheese puffsVegetarian

Cheddar choux puffs, bouncing like Medfield on Flubber

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Bring the water and butter to the boil, beat in the flour until smooth, then cool slightly.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the cheddar and paprika.
  3. Pipe small balls onto trays and bake at 200C until puffed and hollow.
  4. Cool for a few minutes before serving.

Mains

Lemon thyme chicken with baby potatoes

Roast lemon thyme chicken breasts, baby potatoes and buttered green beans

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 800g baby potatoes, halved
  • 300g green beans, trimmed
  • 100g butter
  1. Rub the chicken with lemon juice, thyme, oil and salt.
  2. Roast the potatoes at 200C for 20 minutes, then add the chicken and roast for another 20 minutes.
  3. Steam the beans and toss everything with butter.

Sweets

Flubber jelly

Lime jelly in a ring mould, wobbling like dancing Flubber

Prep 20 min, cook 485 min · Make ahead: Make the night before, turn out on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 packets lime jelly crystals
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 3 kiwifruit, sliced
  1. Make up the jelly with a little less water than the packet says so it holds its shape.
  2. Pour into a ring mould and chill overnight.
  3. Dip the mould briefly in hot water and turn out wobbling onto a plate.
  4. Fill the centre with cream and sliced kiwifruit.

Weebo's lime ice cream floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Lime cordial and lemonade with a scoop of vanilla ice cream floating on top

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100ml lime cordial
  • 1l cold lemonade
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  1. Pour a splash of lime cordial into tall glasses and top with lemonade.
  2. Gently add a scoop of ice cream so it floats and foams up green. Serve straight away with a long spoon and a straw.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the flubber starts dancing around the house.
