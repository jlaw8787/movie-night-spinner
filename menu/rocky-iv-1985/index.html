Snacks

Christmas Day Moscow blini

Buckwheat pancakes with sour cream, smoked salmon and dill.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Batter can rest up to 2 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 100g buckwheat flour
  • 100g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm full cream milk
  • 40g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Mix both flours, yeast and warm milk into a batter and rest until bubbly, 1 hr.
  2. Cook small spoonfuls in butter in a pan until golden on both sides, 15 min.
  3. Top each with sour cream, a curl of smoked salmon and dill.

Log cabin picklesVegetarian

Whole cucumbers brined 4 days with garlic and dill, served with buttered rye.

Prep 15 min, 4 days waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 2 weeks in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg small cucumbers
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 L water
  • 50g sea salt
  • 1 loaf rye bread
  • 50g butter
  1. Pack the cucumbers into a jar with the garlic, dill and peppercorns.
  2. Stir the salt into the water until dissolved and pour over to cover.
  3. Leave on the bench for 4 days until sour, 4 days.
  4. Serve with the rye bread and the butter.

Mains

Drago's beef pelmeni

Beef and pork dumplings in butter.

Prep 1 hr 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Freeze uncooked dumplings for up to 1 month
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml water
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 50g butter
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Mix the flour, egg and water into a firm dough, knead and rest under a bowl, 25 min.
  2. Grate the onion and mix with both minces and some salt and pepper.
  3. Roll the dough thin, cut rounds, fill and seal each one.
  4. Boil in salted water until they float and are cooked through, 5 min.
  5. Toss in the butter and dill and serve with sour cream.

Siberian beef and beet borscht

Beef and beet soup with cabbage and sour cream, hot after chopping wood in the snow.

How it is made
  • 800g beef shin
  • bay leaves
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 3 beetroot
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 loaf rye bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Beef shin simmered with beetroot, cabbage, carrot and potato into a deep red soup, sharpened with vinegar, with sour cream, dill and buttered rye. Simmer 800g beef shin in 2L water with a bay leaf for 2 hours, then shred the meat back into the broth. Soften a diced onion and carrot, add 3 grated beetroot and 2 tbsp tomato paste and cook 10 minutes. Add to the pot with 2 diced potatoes and a quarter of a cabbage, shredded, and simmer 25 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp red wine vinegar and season. Serve with sour cream, dill and buttered rye bread.

Snowball walnut tea cakesVegetarian

Buttery walnut cookies rolled twice in powdered sugar.

Prep 26 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Keeps 5 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 225g butter
  • 160g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 280g plain flour
  • 100g walnuts
  1. Beat the butter with some of the icing sugar and the vanilla until pale.
  2. Mix in the flour and finely chopped walnuts, then roll into balls.
  3. Bake at 180°C until set and lightly golden, 14 min.
  4. Roll in icing sugar while warm, cool, then roll again, 6 min.

Paulie's robot chocolate coconut balls

Chocolate coconut balls rolled in silver dragées, for the birthday robot.

How it is made
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • cocoa powder
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml milk
  • 125g cream cheese
  • 60g icing sugar
  • 20 cake truffle sticks
  • 300g white chocolate
  • edible silver lustre
  • silver cachous
  1. Chocolate cake crumbled into cream cheese icing, rolled into balls and dipped in silver-tinted white chocolate on sticks, with silver cachous for eyes. Bake a chocolate cake from 200g self-raising flour, 40g cocoa, 200g sugar, 2 eggs, 125ml oil and 250ml milk at 175°C for 35 minutes, then cool. Crumble and mix with 125g cream cheese beaten with 60g icing sugar. Roll into 20 balls, push in sticks and chill 1 hour. Melt 300g white chocolate, dip each pop, dust with edible silver lustre and press on 2 silver cachous for eyes.

Drago's Siberian cranberry vodka mule

Vodka, cranberry juice, lime and ginger beer over crushed ice.

Prep 10 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Freeze the vodka overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml vodka
  • 250ml cranberry juice
  • 2 limes
  • 500ml ginger beer
  • 1 bag frozen cranberries
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Keep the vodka in the freezer overnight.
  2. Fill four mugs with crushed ice and add vodka, cranberry juice and lime juice to each.
  3. Top with cold ginger beer and stir once.
  4. Drop in frozen cranberries and a rosemary sprig.
