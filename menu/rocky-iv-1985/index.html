Snacks

Christmas Day Moscow blini

Buckwheat pancakes with sour cream, smoked salmon and dill

Prep 60 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Batter can rest up to 2 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 100g buckwheat flour
  • 100g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm full cream milk
  • 40g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Mix both flours, yeast and warm milk into a batter and rest until bubbly.
  2. Cook small spoonfuls in butter in a pan until golden on both sides.
  3. Top each with sour cream, a curl of smoked salmon and dill.

Log cabin picklesVegetarian

Whole cucumbers brined 4 days with garlic and dill, served with buttered rye

Prep 15 min, then 4 days to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 2 weeks in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg small cucumbers
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1l water
  • 50g sea salt
  • 1 loaf rye bread
  • 50g butter
  1. Pack the cucumbers into a jar with the garlic, dill and peppercorns.
  2. Stir the salt into the water until dissolved and pour over to cover.
  3. Leave on the bench for 4 days until sour.
  4. Serve with the rye bread and the butter.

Mains

Drago's beef pelmeni

If he dies, he dies. Beef and pork dumplings in butter

Prep 65 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Freeze uncooked dumplings for up to 1 month
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml water
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 50g butter
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Mix the flour, egg and water into a firm dough, knead and rest under a bowl.
  2. Grate the onion and mix with both minces and some salt and pepper.
  3. Roll the dough thin, cut rounds, fill and seal each one.
  4. Boil in salted water until they float and are cooked through.
  5. Toss in the butter and dill and serve with sour cream.

Sweets

Siberian snowball walnut tea cakesVegetarian

Buttery walnut cookies rolled twice in icing sugar

Prep 26 min · Make ahead: Keeps 5 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 225g butter
  • 160g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 280g plain flour
  • 100g walnuts
  1. Beat the butter with some of the icing sugar and the vanilla until pale.
  2. Mix in the flour and finely chopped walnuts, then roll into balls.
  3. Bake at 180C until set and lightly golden.
  4. Roll in icing sugar while warm, cool, then roll again.

Drinks

Drago's Siberian cranberry vodka muleVegetarian

Vodka, cranberry juice, lime and ginger beer over crushed ice

Prep 10 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the vodka overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml vodka
  • 250ml cranberry juice
  • 2 limes
  • 500ml ginger beer
  • 1 bag frozen cranberries
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Keep the vodka in the freezer overnight.
  2. Fill four mugs with crushed ice and add vodka, cranberry juice and lime juice to each.
  3. Top with cold ginger beer and stir once.
  4. Drop in frozen cranberries and a rosemary sprig.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Rocky starts training in the Siberian snow.
