Snacks

Canadian Rockies poutine

Twice fried chips and gravy, a logger's lunch in 1979

Prep 15 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and reheat; fry the chips fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 200g mozzarella
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips and dry well.
  2. Fry the chips once in the oil at a lower heat until soft.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the stock and simmer until thick.
  4. Fry the chips again in the oil at high heat until crisp.
  5. Pile into bowls, scatter with torn mozzarella and pour over the hot gravy.

Team X bacon dates

Bacon dates for Team X, before Logan walks out

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Wrap the dates in bacon up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 16 medjool dates
  1. Halve the bacon rashers and pit the dates.
  2. Wrap bacon around each date and secure with a toothpick.
  3. Bake at 200°C on a lined tray, turning once, until the bacon is crisp.

The Hudsons' farmhouse pancakes, bacon and eggs

Buttermilk pancakes with crisp bacon and fried eggs on one plate

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Whisk the pancake batter up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the pancakes, bacon and eggs fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 6 eggs
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 100g butter
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, two eggs and buttermilk into a batter.
  2. Cook the pancakes in butter and keep warm.
  3. Fry the bacon.
  4. Fry the four remaining eggs.
  5. Pile it all on a big plate and pour over maple syrup.

Fred Dukes garlic butter rib eyeOn the printed menu

Steak seared hard, basted and rested, roast potatoes and greens, after a bout on the bag

How it is made
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  1. Rib eye seared hard and butter basted with garlic, rested and served with crispy roast potatoes and greens. Take 4 rib eye steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes early and season well. Parboil 1kg potatoes in chunks for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in oil at 220°C for 45 minutes. Sear the steaks in a smoking pan for 3 minutes a side, then add 60g butter, 3 crushed garlic cloves and rosemary and baste for 1 minute. Rest 5 minutes. Sauté 2 bunches broccolini in the pan butter. Serve the steak sliced with potatoes, greens and the pan juices.

Sweets

Maple syrup pieVegetarian

Maple syrup, egg and cream custard in a shortcrust case

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 250ml maple syrup
  • 3 eggs
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Line a pie dish with pastry and chill briefly.
  2. Blind bake the case.
  3. Whisk the syrup, eggs and cream until smooth.
  4. Pour into the case and bake at 170°C until just set with a slight wobble.
  5. Cool completely before slicing.

Maple chilli candied pecansOn the printed menu

Pecans roasted glassy in maple, brown sugar and chilli, packed for Three Mile Island

How it is made
  • 300g pecans
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  1. Pecans tossed in maple syrup, brown sugar, chilli and sea salt, roasted until glassy and crunchy, broken into sweet and salty clusters. Toss 300g pecans with 60ml maple syrup, 1 tbsp brown sugar, half a teaspoon chilli flakes, a pinch of cinnamon and 1 tsp sea salt. Spread on a tray lined with baking paper and roast at 170°C for 12 minutes, stirring halfway, until the syrup bubbles and darkens. Sprinkle with a little more salt and leave to cool for 15 minutes so the coating sets crunchy, then break into clusters.

Drinking to remember whisky sourVegetarian

Whisky, lemon and maple, at a bar in Japan after the credits

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake each round of drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml Canadian whisky
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 40ml maple syrup
  • 1 egg white
  • 1 bottle Angostura bitters
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the lemons. Make two drinks at a time.
  2. Shake the whisky, lemon juice, maple syrup and egg white without ice for 15 seconds.
  3. Add ice and shake again until very cold.
  4. Strain over fresh ice into rocks glasses and add 3 drops of bitters to the foam. Repeat for the other two.
