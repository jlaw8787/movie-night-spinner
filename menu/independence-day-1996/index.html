Snacks

Area 51 green chilli potato chipsVegetarian

Potato chips in salt and green chilli, a bowl for the base.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tsp green chilli powder
  1. Slice the potatoes very thin on a mandoline, rinse and dry well.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil to 170°C and fry in batches until golden and crisp, 15 min.
  3. Toss with salt and green chilli powder while hot.

Fireworks popcornVegetarian

White chocolate popcorn with popping candy that crackles like fireworks.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, 25 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 2 tbsp red and blue sprinkles
  • 2 packets of popping candy
  1. Pop the kernels in a covered pot and spread on a lined tray.
  2. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle over, tossing gently, 5 min.
  3. Scatter with the sprinkles and popping candy before it sets.
  4. Let set, then break into clusters, 25 min.

Russell's crop duster chilli jerky

Top round jerky in soy, chilli and smoked paprika, packed for the crop duster.

How it is made
  • 800g beef topside
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • chilli flakes
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Topside sliced thin and marinated in soy, Worcestershire, brown sugar, chilli and smoked paprika, slow-dried until chewy and smoky. Freeze 800g topside for 1 hour, then slice 3mm thick across the grain. Mix 100ml soy sauce, 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp chilli flakes and 2 tsp smoked paprika. Marinate the beef overnight in the fridge. Pat dry and lay on wire racks over trays. Dry at 70°C with the oven door slightly open for 4 hours until leathery but still bendy. Cool and store airtight.

Mains

Smoky pork ribs, corn and potato salad

Paprika rubbed ribs baked then glazed, charred corn, Dijon potato salad.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hr 13 min · Make ahead: Ribs up to 1 day ahead, glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork ribs
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  1. Rub the ribs with the brown sugar, paprika and garlic, then wrap tightly in foil, 10 min.
  2. Bake at 150°C until the meat is very tender, 2 hr 30 min.
  3. Boil the potatoes until tender, cool slightly, and toss with Dijon, oil and vinegar, 25 min.
  4. Unwrap the ribs, brush with barbecue sauce and glaze under a hot grill, 8 min.
  5. Char the corn on a grill, 10 min.

Captain Hiller's cookout cheeseburgers

Griddled beef patties and cheese on toasted buns, the barbecue he could've been at.

Prep 14 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Roll the beef into balls and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; smash and cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince, 20% fat
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 brioche buns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 20g butter
  1. Divide the mince into 8 balls and keep them cold.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, mustard, tomato sauce and a chopped pickle for the burger sauce. Slice the tomato and onion and shred the lettuce.
  3. Butter and toast the buns cut side down, 3 min.
  4. Smash the balls flat on a very hot greased flat plate or pan, season and cook 2 minutes. Flip, top with cheese and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Stack two patties on each bun with the sauce, lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles.

Sweets

Flag cakeVegetarian

Sponge with cream, berry stars and stripes.

Prep 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 slab sponge cake
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 125g blueberries
  • 500g strawberries
  1. Put the sponge on a board. Whip the cream with the icing sugar to soft peaks and spread over the top.
  2. Fill the top left corner with blueberries for the stars.
  3. Halve the strawberries and lay rows across for the stripes.

Drinks

Fourth of July berry lemonadeNo alcohol

Smoky tea and fresh lemonade over crushed strawberries and blueberries.

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Steep and cool the tea and mix the lemonade up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; crush the berries and add ice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 5 lemons
  • 120ml sugar syrup
  • 500ml cold water
  • 100g strawberries
  • 50g blueberries
  • 180ml butterscotch
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 4 minutes, remove and cool.
  2. Squeeze four of the lemons, keeping one for wheels, and stir into the syrup and cold water.
  3. Lightly crush the strawberries and blueberries in the bottom of four jars.
  4. Fill with ice, add the cold tea and vanilla and top with the lemonade.
  5. Stir and garnish with a lemon wheel and mint.
