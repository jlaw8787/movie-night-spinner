Snacks

Wednesday pink wraps

Roast chicken, arugula, feta, beetroot spread.

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Roast the beetroot a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 beetroot
  • 1 tin chickpeas
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 300g roast chicken, shredded
  • 60g rocket
  • 100g feta
  1. Wrap the beetroot in foil and roast at 200°C until tender, then peel.
  2. Blitz with the drained chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice and garlic until pink and smooth.
  3. Spread on the tortillas and fill with chicken, rocket and crumbled feta.
  4. Roll tight and slice into spirals.

All-carb diet crispy potato bitesVegetarian

Crisp crispy potato bites under melted cheddar and sour cream.

Prep 3 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Best straight from the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen potato gems
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C and spread the potato gems on two lined trays.
  2. Bake 20 minutes, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Pile into one tray, scatter with cheddar and paprika and bake 5 minutes more until melted.
  4. Top with spring onions and serve with sour cream.

Mains

Halloween party tacos

Spiced beef mince in hard shells with cheddar, lettuce, tomato and sour cream.

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Cook the taco mince up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grate the cheddar, shred the lettuce and chop the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead. Warm the shells on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 12 taco shells
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Brown the mince hard with the diced onion and garlic.
  2. Stir in cumin, paprika and tomato paste, then loosen with stock and simmer.
  3. Warm the taco shells and prepare grated cheddar, lettuce and tomato.
  4. Spoon into shells with toppings and sour cream.

Cafeteria crumbed chicken tenders

Parmesan and panko crumbed chicken tenders, baked crisp.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken breast
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Parmesan and panko crumbed chicken tenders baked crisp, with buttery mash and a crunchy cos salad, ranch dip on the side, served on trays. Cut 700g chicken breast into strips. Dust in flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs and coat in 150g panko mixed with 40g parmesan and paprika. Bake on an oiled tray at 220°C for 18 minutes, turning once. Mash 1kg potatoes with 60g butter and milk. Toss a chopped cos lettuce with cucumber and lemon dressing. Mix 150g sour cream, 2 tbsp mayo, dill and chives for ranch.

Sweets

Candy cane gram peppermint barkVegetarian

Dark chocolate bark set with white chocolate and crushed candy canes.

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 2 weeks in a cool place
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 1/2 tsp peppermint essence
  • 12 candy canes
  • 1 roll ribbon
  1. Line a tray with baking paper. Melt the dark chocolate, spread thin and chill until set.
  2. Melt the white chocolate with peppermint essence and spread over the top.
  3. Scatter with 4 crushed candy canes and press in lightly.
  4. Chill, then snap into shards.
  5. Tie the remaining candy canes in bundles of four with ribbon.

Pink frosted cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with raspberry pink buttercream.

Prep 32 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead, frost on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g frozen raspberries
  • 1 jar pink sprinkles
  1. Beat part of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake 12 at 175°C until springy.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and strained raspberry puree until pale pink.
  5. Pipe on top and add sprinkles.

Drinks

Plastics raspberry pink lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice blended with raspberries, topped with soda water.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 5 lemons
  • 150g raspberries
  • 1 L soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Blend with the lemon juice and most of the raspberries, then strain.
  3. Pour into a jug with plenty of ice and top with soda water.
  4. Add lemon wheels and a few whole raspberries and pour into four glasses.
