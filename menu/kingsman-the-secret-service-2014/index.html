Eggsy's pub crackling and scotch eggs

Pork crackling and sausage wrapped soft eggs, crumbed and fried, with pickled onions

Prep 35 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Crackling and eggs up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork rind
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 400g pork sausage mince
  • 3 tbsp plain flour, for dusting
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 2 tbsp English mustard
  • salt, to taste
  1. Score the pork rind, salt it well and roast at 240C until blistered and crisp. Break into pieces.
  2. Boil the eggs for 6 minutes, cool in iced water and peel.
  3. Wrap each egg in sausage mince, dust in flour, dip in beaten egg and crumb in panko.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170C for 7 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve with the crackling, pickled onions and English mustard.

Savile Row tea sandwiches

Cucumber mint cream cheese and smoked salmon dill butter sandwiches, crusts off

Prep 30 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, cover with a damp paper towel
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped mint
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 30g soft butter
  1. Mix the cream cheese with the mint and spread on half the bread.
  2. Top with thin cucumber and a second slice of bread.
  3. Mash the dill into the butter, spread on the remaining bread and layer with salmon.
  4. Trim the crusts and cut into perfectly straight fingers.

Burgers on fine china

Smashed beef patties with American cheese, pickles and mustard ketchup sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Patties shaped up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Shape the mince into balls ready to smash. Mix the mustard and tomato sauce.
  2. Heat a griddle until smoking. Smash each ball into a thin patty, season and cook for 2 minutes a side, adding cheese at the end.
  3. Toast the buns. Stack two patties per bun with sauce, pickles and onion.
  4. Plate on fine china with silver cutlery and linen napkins.

Sweets

Eton messVegetarian

Crisp meringue, strawberries and whipped cream folded together

Prep 20 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Meringue up to 2 days ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 500g strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 400ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the egg whites with most of the sugar to stiff peaks and spoon into rough mounds on a lined tray.
  2. Bake at 100C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven.
  3. Toss the strawberries with the remaining sugar and the lemon juice.
  4. Whip the cream, then fold through the broken meringue and berries.

Gin martiniVegetarian

Gin stirred ice cold into a frozen glass with a lemon twist, no vermouth

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best stirred to order; freeze the glasses up to 1 day ahead and cut the lemon peel strips up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 280ml gin
  • 1 bottle dry vermouth, just to look at
  • 1 lemon, peel in strips
  • 1 cup ice
  1. Stir the gin over ice for 10 seconds per drink and strain into frozen glasses. Look at the vermouth bottle, but never open it.
  2. Twist a strip of lemon peel over each glass.
