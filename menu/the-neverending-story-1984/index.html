Snacks

Ham and cheddar sandwiches, apple, nuts

Ham, cheddar, apple, nuts. Bastian skips class for the attic.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 30g butter, soft
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g cheddar, sliced
  • 2 tbsp pickle relish
  • 2 red apples, sliced
  • 150g mixed nuts
  1. Butter the bread and fill with the ham, cheddar and relish. Cut into triangles.
  2. Slice the apples and twist the nuts into paper cones.
  3. Pack it all together like a lunch hidden away in a school attic.

Swamp of Sadness spinach cob loafOn the printed menu

Spinach, cream cheese, parmesan and garlic baked bubbling in hollowed bread, toasted crust to dip.

How it is made
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 250g frozen spinach
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g sour cream
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 garlic clove
  1. Spinach, cream cheese, sour cream, parmesan and garlic baked bubbling inside a hollowed cob loaf, with the torn bread toasted for dipping. Cut the top off a cob loaf and hollow it out, tearing the inside into chunks. Mix 250g thawed spinach squeezed dry, 250g cream cheese, 200g sour cream, 50g grated parmesan, a crushed garlic clove and pepper. Fill the loaf, replace the lid and wrap in foil. Bake at 190°C for 25 minutes, adding the bread chunks to the tray for the last 10 minutes. Serve warm.

Mains

Urgl's healing soup

Chicken, pearl barley and vegetable soup with parsley and lemon.

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 2 celery sticks, sliced
  • 1.5 L water
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • salt, to taste
  1. Chop the vegetables.
  2. Simmer the chicken with the barley, carrots, leek and celery in the water for an hour.
  3. Shred the chicken back into the pot, then stir through the parsley and lemon juice and season with salt to taste. Serve with brown bread.

Ivory Tower tarragon roast chickenOn the printed menu

Tarragon butter, white bean mash, leeks. Fit for the Empress.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 120g butter
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cans cannellini beans
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100ml chicken stock
  • 2 leeks
  1. Whole chicken rubbed with butter, lemon and tarragon and roasted golden, with silky white bean mash, buttered leeks and the pan juices. Mix 60g soft butter with chopped tarragon, lemon zest and salt and rub under the skin of a 1.8kg chicken. Roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Warm 2 drained cans of cannellini beans with a garlic clove and 100ml stock, then blitz with 30g butter until smooth. Slice 2 leeks and cook gently in 30g butter for 10 minutes. Rest the chicken and spoon over the pan juices.

Sweets

Rockbiter's chocolate almond rocks

Dark chocolate, marshmallow, almond and coconut chunks. Very tasty.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 150g marshmallows
  • 100g roasted almonds
  • 100g sweet biscuits, broken
  • 1/2 cup desiccated coconut
  1. Melt the chocolate. Stir in the marshmallows, almonds, biscuits and coconut.
  2. Press into a lined tray and chill for 2 hours.
  3. Smash into rough chunky rocks with the handle of a knife.

Falkor cloud meringuesVegetarian

Crisp meringues piped tall like clouds, with cream. Never give up, and good luck will find you.

Prep 30 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight, add cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 250g strawberries
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  2. Spoon big fluffy clouds onto lined trays and bake at 100°C for 90 minutes. Cool in the oven.
  3. Serve with whipped cream and strawberries.

Southern Oracle teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Chamomile, lemon and honey. Get past the Sphinx Gate first.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chamomile tea bags
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 1 strip lemon peel
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 500ml apple juice, optional for the cold version
  1. Brew the tea bags with the lemon peel in the boiling water for 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the honey. Serve warm in small cups, or cool it and mix half and half with apple juice over ice.
