Snacks

Mosasaur feeding prawns

Garlic chilli lime butter prawns grilled and piled on a platter

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 hour, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns, peeled
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 3 limes, one zested and the rest in wedges
  • 60g butter, melted
  1. Toss the prawns with the garlic, red chillies, lime zest and melted butter.
  2. Grill for 2 minutes a side until charred.
  3. Pile high on a platter with lime wedges.

Gyrosphere cheese ballsVegetarian

Cream cheese and cheddar balls rolled in chopped walnuts and smoked paprika

Prep 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp snipped chives
  • 100g walnuts, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Mix the cheddar, cream cheese and chives. Roll into small balls.
  2. Coat in the walnuts mixed with the paprika.
  3. Chill for an hour until firm. Serve with water crackers.

Main Street cinnamon sugar pretzelsVegetarian

Knots baked chewy, buttered and rolled in sweet spice, caramel dip

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the dough up to a day ahead and keep it in the fridge, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 320ml warm water
  • 4 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 115g caster sugar (1 tbsp for the dough)
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 jar caramel sauce, warmed
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, 1 tbsp sugar and warm water into a dough and knead for 8 minutes.
  2. Cover and rest for 1 hour until puffy.
  3. Heat the oven to 220°C. Roll the dough into 8 ropes and twist each into a pretzel knot.
  4. Boil 2L water with the bicarb soda and dip each knot for 30 seconds, then lift onto a lined tray.
  5. Bake for 12 minutes until deep brown.
  6. Brush with the melted butter, toss in the rest of the sugar mixed with the cinnamon and serve with warm caramel sauce.

Mains

Smokehouse beef short ribs

Paprika and pepper rubbed, slow cooked off the bone, sticky glaze, corn

Prep 5 min, cook 5.5 hours · Make ahead: Slow cook a day ahead, chill in the foil, glaze and roast to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 50g butter
  • 1/2 cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  1. Heat the oven to 140°C. Rub the short ribs all over with the smoked paprika, pepper, brown sugar and salt.
  2. Wrap tightly in foil and cook for 5 hours until the meat pulls off the bone.
  3. Turn the oven up to 220°C, unwrap, brush with the barbecue sauce and roast for 15 minutes until sticky.
  4. Boil the corn for 6 minutes and butter it.
  5. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayonnaise and vinegar for slaw and serve with the ribs and corn.

Isla Nublar fish tacos

Chilli cumin flathead tacos with pineapple salsa, lime crema and cabbage

Prep 35 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Salsa up to 2 hours ahead, fish fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 pineapple, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely diced
  • 3 limes
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1/4 small cabbage, shredded
  1. Dice the pineapple and onion and mix with lime juice for the salsa. Mix the sour cream with lime for the crema.
  2. Dust the flathead fillets in the chilli, cumin and salt and pan-fry in the oil until just cooked. Flake into pieces.
  3. Warm the tortillas and shred the cabbage.
  4. Fill the tortillas with cabbage, fish, salsa and crema.

Sweets

Dino egg chocolate mousseVegetarian

Dark chocolate mousse in glasses, biscuit soil, speckled choc egg on top

Prep 12 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, keep chilled. The eggs stay raw, so use very fresh ones and skip for pregnant guests
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 4 chocolate biscuits, crushed
  • 8 speckled chocolate eggs
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly.
  2. Whip the cream and whisk the egg whites to soft peaks. Stir the yolks into the chocolate, then fold in the cream and whites.
  3. Spoon into glasses and chill for 2 hours.
  4. Top with crushed biscuits and a speckled chocolate egg.

Drinks

Theme park frozen lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade blended with ice till slushy, in big souvenir cups

Prep 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze a day ahead, blitz fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 lemons, juiced
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 500ml water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the lemon juice and water.
  2. Freeze in a tray for 2 hours.
  3. Blitz with a few handfuls of ice until slushy and pour into big cups with straws.
