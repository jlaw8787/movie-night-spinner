Snacks

Washington half-smoke sausages with chilli

Grilled half-pork, half-beef smoked sausages in buns with beef chilli and mustard

Prep 9 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 125ml water
  • 4 smoked half-pork half-beef sausages
  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Finely chop the onions, keeping half aside raw for serving.
  2. Simmer the mince with the onion, chilli powder, tomatoes and water, stirring now and then, until thick.
  3. Split the sausages and grill until browned and hot.
  4. Serve in the buns with the chilli, mustard and the raw chopped onion.

Brooklyn smoked salmon bagel bites

Mini bagels with dill cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and red onion

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assembled and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 12 mini bagels
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Split the bagels and toast until golden.
  2. Mix the cream cheese with the lemon zest, dill and salt.
  3. Spread thickly on each bagel half, then top with folds of smoked salmon, thin rings of red onion and a few capers.
  4. Finish with pepper and a little more dill.

Sam's safe house pancakes and baconOn the printed menu

Buttermilk pancakes with maple syrup and crisp bacon, at Sam's while Steve and Natasha lie low

How it is made
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 80g butter
  • 12 bacon rashers
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  1. Small fluffy buttermilk pancakes stacked with melting butter and maple syrup, with crisp streaky bacon on the side. Whisk 300g self-raising flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 eggs, 400ml buttermilk and 30g melted butter into a thick batter. Fry 4 tbsp at a time in a buttered pan over medium heat for 2 minutes a side until golden, making about 16. Keep warm in a low oven. Fry 12 bacon rashers until crisp. Stack the pancakes with a knob of butter and pour over maple syrup.

Mains

Thai basil chicken over rice

Stir-fried chicken with garlic, chilli and Thai basil over jasmine rice, crisp fried egg

Prep 5 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice and chop the chillies and garlic up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; stir-fry and fry the eggs just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, minced or finely chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 red chillies, chopped
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 4 eggs
  1. Rinse the rice and cook it, then keep warm.
  2. Stir-fry the chicken with the garlic and red chillies in a hot wok until cooked through.
  3. Add the fish sauce and sugar, then toss through the Thai basil.
  4. Fry the eggs in hot oil until the edges are crisp.
  5. Serve the chicken over rice with a fried egg on top.

Helicarrier beef and red wine stewOn the printed menu

Chuck braised hours with carrots, onions and potatoes till spoon tender, for Steve's catch-up list

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bottle red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 3 carrots
  • 500g potatoes
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Beef chuck braised for hours in red wine and stock with carrots, onions and potatoes until spoon tender, served with crusty bread. Toss 1.2kg diced beef chuck in 2 tbsp plain flour and brown in batches in oil. Soften 2 diced onions and 2 garlic cloves, then add the beef, 250ml red wine, 500ml beef stock, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 2 bay leaves. Cover and simmer gently for 2 hours. Add 3 chopped carrots and 500g potato chunks and cook 1 hour more. Season and serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

Shield pieVegetarian

Cherry and blueberry pie with a woven lattice top, served warm with ice cream

Prep 35 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g frozen cherries
  • 200g blueberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten for glazing
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the cherries and blueberries with the sugar and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with pastry, add the fruit and weave a lattice top from strips of pastry. Brush with egg.
  3. Bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling.
  4. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

Smithsonian cherry lime phosphateVegetarian

Cherry syrup, lime juice and bitters over crushed ice, topped with soda

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen pitted cherries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 4 dashes bitters
  • 800ml soda water
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 4 lime wedges
  1. Simmer the cherries with the sugar and water, then mash well.
  2. Strain and cool the syrup completely.
  3. Fill tall glasses with crushed ice and add the cherry syrup, lime juice and bitters to each.
  4. Top with soda water, stir once and garnish with a cherry and a lime wedge.
