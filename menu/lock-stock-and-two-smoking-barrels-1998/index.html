Snacks

Smoked mackerel pâté

Smoked mackerel blended with cream cheese and horseradish, on toasted rye with cucumber.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g smoked mackerel
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  • 1 lemon
  • 6 slices rye bread
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  1. Flake the mackerel, removing the skin and bones.
  2. Blitz with the cream cheese, horseradish, lemon juice and black pepper until mostly smooth.
  3. Toast the rye.
  4. Spread on the toast and top with thin cucumber slices.

Vinegar pork scratchings

Crisp pork crackling splashed with malt vinegar, by the bag.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min, then 11.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days in paper bags
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork rind
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 2 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  1. Score the pork rind into strips and rub well with salt.
  2. Dry uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  3. Roast on a rack at 220°C until puffed and crackling.
  4. Brush lightly with malt vinegar while hot, then toss in sea salt flakes.
  5. Tip into paper bags.

Three-card brag smoked chilli peanuts

Coated in egg white, paprika, sugar and spice, roasted glassy and crunchy, for Eddy's crooked game.

How it is made
  • 1 egg
  • 400g raw peanuts
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  1. Peanuts coated in egg white, smoked paprika, brown sugar and chilli, roasted until glassy and crunchy, served in a bowl in the middle of the card table. Whisk 1 egg white until frothy. Toss with 400g raw peanuts, then with 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 tbsp brown sugar, half a tsp chilli powder and 1 tsp salt. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 160°C for 15 to 18 minutes, stirring twice, until dry and crunchy. Cool on the tray and break up.

Mains

Big Chris's pie and chips

Beef and ale pie, chips, peas.

Prep 20 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 375ml English ale
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  1. Brown the beef with the sliced onions and thyme.
  2. Add the ale and braise.
  3. Thicken with the flour, pour into a dish, cover with puff pastry and bake at 200°C.
  4. Cut the potatoes into thick chips and fry twice in the vegetable oil until golden.
  5. Cook the peas and crush with the butter and mint.

Bacon's full English

Bangers, bacon, fried eggs, beans and mushrooms with brown sauce, at a market price.

How it is made
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 slices white bread
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 20g butter
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 tin baked beans
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 bottle brown sauce
  1. Bacon, bangers, fried eggs, beans, mushrooms, blistered tomato and fried bread crisped in the bacon fat, brown sauce on the table. Grill 8 pork sausages for 15 minutes, turning. Fry 8 rashers bacon and keep warm, then fry 4 slices of bread in the bacon fat until crisp. Fry 250g mushrooms in butter and halve 4 tomatoes and grill until blistered. Warm a tin of baked beans. Fry 4 eggs. Load onto four warm plates and put brown sauce on the table.

Sweets

Spotted dick and custardVegetarian

Steamed sponge pudding studded with currants, served with custard.

Prep 25 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Best fresh from the steamer
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 150g currants
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 600ml custard
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the sugar and currants.
  2. Add the milk to a soft dough.
  3. Press into a greased basin and cover with paper and foil.
  4. Steam.
  5. Turn out and serve with hot custard.

Drinks

Two smoking barrels peated whisky sour

Peated Scotch, lemon, honey and egg white.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep in the fridge; juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead. Shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml peated Scotch whisky
  • 3 lemons
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp warm water
  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 bottle aromatic bitters
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the honey with warm water for honey syrup.
  2. Shake the whisky, lemon juice, syrup and egg white hard without ice, one drink at a time.
  3. Shake again with ice and strain into a tumbler over ice.
  4. Dot the foam with aromatic bitters.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Eddie overhears the neighbours planning their robbery.
