Snacks

Warm olives, almonds and parmesan crispsVegetarian

Olives with orange and fennel, rosemary almonds, baked parmesan rounds

Prep 14 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 250g olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 strip of orange peel
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 150g almonds
  • 1 sprig of rosemary
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  1. Warm the olives in the oil with orange peel and fennel seeds.
  2. Roast the almonds at 170C with rosemary and salt.
  3. Bake small rounds of parmesan at 190C.
  4. Serve in little bowls.

Mains

Paris bistro chicken

Tarragon butter roast chicken, silky mashed potatoes, Dijon-dressed leaves

Prep 15 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Rest 15 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, softened, for the chicken
  • 1 tbsp fresh tarragon, chopped
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled
  • 100g butter, for the mash
  • 1 bag of salad leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  1. Mix the softened butter with the tarragon and push under the chicken skin.
  2. Roast at 220C, then drop to 180C.
  3. Rest and carve.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter for the mash until silky.
  5. Whisk the oil, mustard and vinegar and toss through the leaves.

Sweets

Studio lot almond croissantsVegetarian

Day-old croissants filled with almond cream and baked with flaked almonds

Prep 10 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60g almond meal
  • 1 egg
  • 4 day old croissants
  • 2 tbsp flaked almonds
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Beat the butter, sugar, almond meal and egg into almond cream.
  2. Split the croissants and fill with the cream.
  3. Top with flaked almonds.
  4. Bake.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Griffith Observatory starry chocolate tartVegetarian

Dark chocolate ganache tart with flaky salt and edible gold stars

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 pinch of flaky salt
  • 1 tbsp edible gold stars
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Line a tart tin with the sweet shortcrust pastry and blind bake.
  2. Melt the chocolate into the cream until smooth.
  3. Pour into the case and set.
  4. Finish with flaky salt and gold stars.

Drinks

Jazz club old fashionedVegetarian

Bourbon, sugar cube and bitters stirred over a big cube with orange peel

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Cut the orange peel up to 1 day ahead, wrapped; stir and strain to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 2 cup ice
  • 1 orange, peel only
  1. Muddle the sugar cubes with the bitters in a mixing glass.
  2. Add the bourbon and ice and stir.
  3. Strain over a big cube in each glass.
  4. Twist orange peel over the top.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Sebastian takes Mia to the jazz club.
