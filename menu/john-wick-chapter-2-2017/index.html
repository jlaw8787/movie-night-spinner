Snacks

Pecorino and prosciutto melon antipasti

Pecorino shards with chilli honey, prosciutto wrapped cantaloupe and mint.

Prep 30 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pecorino
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 baguette
  • 8 anchovy fillets
  1. Cut the pecorino into shards. Warm the honey with chilli flakes and drizzle over, 8 min.
  2. Wrap prosciutto around wedges of rockmelon and add mint leaves.
  3. Chop the parsley with capers, lemon zest and oil for salsa verde.
  4. Toast baguette slices, spread with salsa verde and top each with an anchovy, 10 min.
  5. Lay it all out in neat rows on a long board.

Rome supplì rice balls

Fried tomato risotto balls with a melted mozzarella centre.

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Risotto cooled a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g arborio rice
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 600ml chicken stock
  • 40g parmesan
  • 150g mozzarella
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Cook the rice with the tomatoes and stock into a thick risotto, 25 min.
  2. Stir in the parmesan and cool completely, 30 min.
  3. Roll around cubes of mozzarella.
  4. Crumb in beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  5. Deep fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until golden, 10 min.

Bowery King's chicken and mushroom pies

Puff parcels of thigh and mushroom in peppery thyme cream, hot mustard, no pigeons harmed.

How it is made
  • 400g chicken thighs
  • 30g butter
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 jar cracked black pepper
  • 3 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar English mustard
  1. Little puff pastry pies of chicken thigh and mushroom in a peppery thyme cream, baked golden and served in a wooden crate with hot English mustard. Fry 400g diced chicken thighs in 30g butter until browned, add 200g chopped mushrooms and thyme and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 1 tbsp flour, then 150ml cream and plenty of cracked pepper and simmer until thick, then cool. Cut 12 rounds from 2 sheets puff pastry and press into a muffin tin, fill, top with smaller rounds cut from a third sheet and brush with egg. Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes. Serve with English mustard.

Cacio e pepeVegetarian

Spaghetti with pecorino and cracked black pepper in a creamy sauce.

Prep 0 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best made just before serving; toast the pepper and grate the pecorino up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 150g pecorino
  1. Toast the pepper in a dry pan.
  2. Cook the spaghetti in a small amount of salted water, 12 min.
  3. Off the heat, toss with finely grated pecorino, the pepper and ladles of starchy pasta water until creamy, never stringy, 11 min.

Santino's anchovy and rosemary lamb

Butterflied leg rubbed with anchovy and garlic, charred hot, salsa verde, for Gianna's coronation.

How it is made
  • 1 tin anchovies
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1.5kg butterflied lamb leg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 jar capers
  1. Butterflied lamb leg marinated in rosemary, garlic, anchovy and lemon, charred on a screaming hot grill, sliced pink over crisp potatoes with salsa verde. Mash 4 anchovies, 4 garlic cloves, chopped rosemary, lemon zest and 4 tbsp olive oil and rub over a 1.5kg butterflied lamb leg. Marinate 2 hours. Parboil 1kg chunked potatoes for 8 minutes and roast in oil at 220°C for 45 minutes. Grill the lamb on high heat, turning, to 56°C inside, about 20 minutes, and rest 10 minutes. Chop parsley, capers, lemon juice and olive oil for salsa verde and spoon over the sliced lamb.

TiramisuVegetarian

Coffee and coffee liqueur soaked ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream.

Prep 30 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml strong brewed coffee
  • 60ml coffee liqueur
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500g mascarpone
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 24 savoiardi biscuits
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  1. Brew the coffee, cool and add the liqueur, 8 min.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar, then fold in the mascarpone and whipped cream.
  3. Dip the savoiardi quickly and layer with the cream in a dish.
  4. Set overnight in the fridge.
  5. Sift cocoa on top.

Drinks

Negroni

Gin, bitter red aperitivo and sweet vermouth stirred over a big cube, orange peel.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the gin, aperitivo and vermouth in a jar up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; stir over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 120ml red bitter aperitivo
  • 120ml sweet vermouth
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the gin, aperitivo and vermouth over ice for 20 seconds, one drink at a time.
  2. Strain over a big cube and twist orange peel over the top.

When to serve

Pour the Negronis when John visits the sommelier at the Rome Continental.
