Milwaukee beer-battered cheese curdsVegetarian

Lager battered haloumi cubes with a dill and chive ranch dip

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the ranch up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; make the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g haloumi
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml cold lager
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 lemon
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Cut the haloumi into cubes and pat dry.
  2. Whisk the flour, egg and lager into a batter.
  3. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, chopped dill and chives, garlic powder and lemon juice for ranch.
  4. Heat the oil to 185C.
  5. Dip the cheese in batter and fry in batches until golden.
  6. Drain and salt lightly.

Paris shower leek and gruyère tartletsVegetarian

Little puff pastry tartlets of buttery leek and gruyère, for Helen's French table

Prep 17 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Cook the leeks up to a day ahead; fill and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 2 leeks, thinly sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 100g gruyère, grated
  • 1 bunch thyme
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut 12 rounds from the pastry and press into a greased muffin tin.
  2. Cook the leeks gently in the butter with the thyme leaves for 12 minutes until soft and sweet.
  3. Whisk the eggs, cream, salt and pepper.
  4. Divide the leeks and gruyère between the cases and pour over the egg mix.
  5. Bake 18 to 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve warm.

Plane trip pastaVegetarian

Rigatoni in garlic chilli tomato sauce, torn burrata and basil on top

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Simmer the tomato sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; cook the rigatoni and tear the burrata on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 2 burrata
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with sliced garlic and chilli flakes.
  2. Cook the rigatoni 2 minutes short and finish it in the sauce.
  3. Tear the burrata over the top with basil just before serving.

Cake Baby cinnamon scrollsVegetarian

Soft yeast rolls swirled with cinnamon brown sugar butter

Prep 30 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm full cream milk
  • 110g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 100g icing sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, warm milk and part of the butter into a dough.
  2. Prove.
  3. Roll out, spread with the remaining butter, brown sugar and cinnamon and roll up.
  4. Cut into 12 and prove.
  5. Bake at 190C.
  6. Ice with the cream cheese beaten with icing sugar.

Annie's perfect cupcakeVegetarian

Vanilla bean cupcake with silky meringue buttercream

Prep 40 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead, frost on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 325g butter
  • 270g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 1 bottle pink food colouring
  • 50g fondant
  1. Beat part of the butter with part of the sugar and the vanilla seeds, add 2 eggs and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake at 170C.
  3. Cool.
  4. Whisk 2 egg whites with the remaining sugar over hot water, then beat in the remaining butter and tint pale pink.
  5. Pipe tall and add a fondant flower.

Drinks

Bridal party grapefruit rosé spritzVegetarian

Rosé, pink grapefruit and soda over ice with strawberries and mint

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the rose, juice and soda up to 1 day ahead and slice the strawberries up to 4 hours ahead; build the glasses to order
Recipe for 4
  • 480ml chilled rose
  • 160ml pink grapefruit juice
  • 240ml soda water
  • 250g strawberries
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Slice the strawberries and fill four large wine glasses with ice and strawberries.
  2. Add rose and grapefruit juice to each and top with soda water.
  3. Stir gently and finish with a sprig of mint.
