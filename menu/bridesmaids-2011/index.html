Milwaukee beer-battered cheese curdsVegetarian

Squeaky cheese in a crisp lager batter with a dill and chive ranch dip.

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the ranch up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; make the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g haloumi
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml cold lager
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Cut the haloumi into cubes and pat dry.
  2. Whisk the flour, egg and lager into a batter.
  3. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, chopped dill and chives, garlic powder and lemon juice for ranch.
  4. Heat the oil to 185°C, 10 min.
  5. Dip the cheese in batter and fry in batches until golden.
  6. Drain and salt lightly.

Paris shower leek and gruyère tartletsVegetarian

Little puff pastry tartlets of buttery leek and gruyère.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook the leeks up to a day ahead; fill and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 2 leeks, thinly sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 100g gruyère, grated
  • 1 bunch thyme
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 12 rounds from the pastry and press into a greased muffin tin, 8 min.
  2. Cook the leeks gently in the butter with the thyme leaves for 12 minutes until soft and sweet.
  3. Whisk the eggs, cream, salt and pepper.
  4. Divide the leeks and gruyère between the cases and pour over the egg mix.
  5. Bake 18 to 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve warm.

Plane trip pastaVegetarian

Rigatoni in garlic chilli tomato sauce, torn burrata and basil on top.

Prep 8 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Simmer the tomato sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; cook the rigatoni and tear the burrata on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins of crushed tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 2 burratas
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with sliced garlic and chilli flakes, 20 min.
  2. Cook the rigatoni 2 minutes short and finish it in the sauce.
  3. Tear the burrata over the top with basil just before serving.

Wedding lemon thyme chicken and gratin

Breast in white wine thyme cream, slow-baked potato layers.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 450ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 skin-on chicken breasts
  • 50g butter
  • 125ml white wine
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 150g green beans
  • 1 bunch broccolini
  1. Golden pan-roasted chicken breast in a white wine, lemon and thyme cream sauce, with a slow-baked potato gratin and buttered greens. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes with 300ml cream, a crushed garlic clove and salt and bake at 160°C for 75 minutes. Brine 4 skin-on chicken breasts in salted water for 1 hour, pat dry and fry skin down in 30g butter for 5 minutes, then flip and roast at 200°C for 12 minutes. Add 125ml white wine to the pan and reduce, then 150ml cream, lemon juice and thyme. Steam 300g green beans and broccolini with butter.

Cake Baby cinnamon scrollsVegetarian

Soft yeast rolls swirled with cinnamon brown sugar butter.

Prep 65 min, cook 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm full cream milk
  • 110g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 100g icing sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, warm milk and part of the butter into a dough, 15 min.
  2. Prove, 1 hr.
  3. Roll out, spread with the remaining butter, brown sugar and cinnamon and roll up, 10 min.
  4. Cut into 12 and prove, 30 min.
  5. Bake at 190°C, 20 min.
  6. Ice with the cream cheese beaten with icing sugar.

Annie's perfect cupcakeVegetarian

Vanilla bean cupcake with silky meringue buttercream.

Prep 40 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead, frost on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 325g butter
  • 270g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 1 bottle pink food colouring
  • 50g fondant
  1. Beat part of the butter with part of the sugar and the vanilla seeds, add 2 eggs and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake at 170°C, 18 min.
  3. Cool, 20 min.
  4. Whisk 2 egg whites with the remaining sugar over hot water, then beat in the remaining butter and tint pale pink.
  5. Pipe tall and add a fondant flower.

Bridal party grapefruit rosé spritz

Rosé, pink grapefruit and soda over ice with strawberries and mint.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill the rose, juice and soda up to 1 day ahead and slice the strawberries up to 4 hours ahead; build the glasses to order
Recipe for 4
  • 480ml chilled rose
  • 160ml pink grapefruit juice
  • 240ml soda water
  • 250g strawberries
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Slice the strawberries and fill four large wine glasses with ice and strawberries.
  2. Add rose and grapefruit juice to each and top with soda water.
  3. Stir gently and finish with a sprig of mint.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Annie bakes her one perfect cupcake.
