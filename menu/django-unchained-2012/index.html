Schultz's saloon pretzelsVegetarian

Soft baked pretzels with rock salt, grainy mustard and butter

Prep 30 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm water
  • 3 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tbsp rock salt
  • grainy mustard, to serve
  • 60g butter, to serve
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and water and prove until doubled.
  2. Shape into 8 pretzels.
  3. Dip in simmering water with the bicarbonate of soda.
  4. Sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220C.
  5. Serve with mustard and butter.

Dead or alive bounty hunter's jerky

Beef jerky marinated in soy, Worcestershire and smoked paprika, dried overnight

Prep 25 min, cook 840 min · Make ahead: Marinate overnight, keeps 2 weeks
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef topside, partly frozen
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Slice the partly frozen beef very thin.
  2. Mix the soy, Worcestershire, pepper and paprika and add the beef.
  3. Marinate overnight in the fridge.
  4. Lay on racks and dry at 70C.

Schultz's winter camp pumpkin and lentil soupVegetarian

Red lentils and roast pumpkin with cumin, blitzed smooth, rye, for Django's months of training

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Soup keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pumpkin, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150g red lentils
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1.2l vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 loaf dark rye bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil with the cumin.
  2. Add the pumpkin, lentils and stock and simmer until soft.
  3. Blitz until smooth and season.
  4. Serve with buttered rye bread.

Mains

German sausages and kraut

Grilled bratwurst, sauerkraut with apple and caraway, buttered potatoes

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1 jar of sauerkraut
  • 125ml white wine
  • 1 apple, grated
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 8 bratwurst
  • 800g potatoes, chopped
  • 40g butter
  1. Simmer the sauerkraut with the wine, apple and caraway.
  2. Grill the bratwurst slowly until browned.
  3. Boil the potatoes until tender and toss in butter.
  4. Serve together.

Candyland white cakeVegetarian

We're havin' white cake. Two layers, buttercream

Prep 40 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 170g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g plain flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml milk
  • 200g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 400g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line two 20cm tins.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, add the egg whites and vanilla.
  3. Fold in the flour, baking powder and milk.
  4. Bake.
  5. Cool, then beat the icing butter and icing sugar.
  6. Fill and cover the cake.

Dr Schultz's lemon wheat beerVegetarian

Wheat beer over lemon honey syrup. Prost?

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g honey
  • 60ml water
  • 1 lemon, peeled zest, for the syrup
  • 1 lemon, juiced, for the glasses
  • 4 bottles German wheat beer, 500ml each
  • 4 lemon wheels
  1. Simmer the honey, water and lemon zest, then cool and strain.
  2. Add a spoon of syrup and a squeeze of lemon to each of 4 chilled tall mugs.
  3. Pour in the beer slowly, tilting the mug, then straighten to build a thick head.
  4. Hang a lemon wheel on each rim.

When to serve

Serve the beer when Dr Schultz pours two at the empty saloon.
