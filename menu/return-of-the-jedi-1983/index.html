Snacks

Jabba's palace crumbed prawn bites

Panko prawns, sweet chilli. Before Leia chokes Jabba with his own chain.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Butterfly and crumb the prawns up to 4 hours ahead, laid on a tray in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, peeled
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime
  1. Butterfly the prawns.
  2. Dip in flour, then egg, then panko.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until golden.
  4. Drain and serve hot with sweet chilli sauce and lime wedges.

Endor forest berriesVegetarian

Berries and mascarpone. Ewoks beat stormtroopers with rocks and logs.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries, halved
  • 125g blueberries
  • 200g pitted cherries
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 250g mascarpone
  1. Toss the strawberries, blueberries and cherries with some honey and torn mint.
  2. Whip the mascarpone with a spoon of honey until soft.
  3. Serve the fruit in a bowl with the mascarpone alongside.

Salacious Crumb's crispy chicken bites

Crispy fried chicken bites with a cool herb mayo.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken thighs
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • chilli flakes
  • 120g mayo
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Buttermilk-brined chicken thigh nuggets in a garlic paprika crust, fried crisp and tossed in chilli salt, with a herby green mayo for dipping. Cut 700g chicken thighs into bite-size pieces and soak in 300ml buttermilk with 1 tsp salt for 2 hours. Mix 200g plain flour with 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp paprika and pepper. Dredge the chicken and fry at 170°C for 5 minutes until crisp and cooked through. Toss in salt mixed with a pinch of chilli flakes. Blitz 120g mayo with a handful of parsley, chives and lemon juice for the dip.

Mains

Ewok honey roast chicken

Honey roast chicken. Han nearly ended up on the Ewoks' spit.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Spatchcock the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; cut the vegetables, roast and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Spatchcock the chicken and cut the potatoes and carrots into chunks.
  2. Lay the chicken over the vegetables, season and drizzle with oil.
  3. Roast at 220°C.
  4. Warm the honey with the garlic and thyme, brush over and roast until glossy.
  5. Rest and pull apart to share.

Jabba's Bantha fodder smoked brisket

Beef brisket smoked low and slow, sliced thick. A big slab, served before the Sarlacc pit.

How it is made
  • 2.5kg beef brisket
  • coarse salt
  • black peppercorns
  • quarter green cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 3 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • barbecue sauce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 loaf white bread
  1. Salt and pepper brisket smoked low until it wobbles, sliced thick with sticky barbecue sauce, tangy slaw, pickles and soft white bread. Rub a 2.5kg brisket with 2 tbsp coarse salt and 2 tbsp cracked pepper. Smoke or roast at 120°C for about 10 hours until 95°C inside, wrapping in baking paper once it reaches 70°C. Rest 1 hour. Shred quarter of a cabbage and 1 carrot and toss with 3 tbsp mayo and 1 tbsp cider vinegar. Slice the brisket thick and serve with barbecue sauce, the slaw, dill pickles and white bread.

Sweets

Ewok forest berry crumbleVegetarian

Mixed berry crumble with oats and hazelnuts, vanilla ice cream.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen mixed berries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 100g cold butter
  • 120g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 50g hazelnuts, chopped
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Toss the berries with the caster sugar and cornflour in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour until crumbly, then stir in the oats, hazelnuts and brown sugar.
  3. Scatter over the berries and bake until golden and bubbling at the edges.
  4. Rest, then serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

Drinks

Blue pineapple lime milkNo alcohol

Cold milk blended with pineapple, lime and blue spirulina, no alcohol.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Juice the lime up to 1 day ahead; blend just before drinking or the pineapple melts and the milk separates
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml full cream milk, cold
  • 200g frozen pineapple
  • 1 lime
  • 1/2 tsp blue spirulina
  1. Blend the cold full cream milk, frozen pineapple, juice of the lime and blue spirulina until smooth and pale blue.
  2. Pour into four glasses and drink straight away.
