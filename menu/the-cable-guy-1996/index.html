Roast tomato bisque, drunk from the bowlVegetarian

Roast tomato bisque drunk straight from the bowl, no utensils at Medieval Times.

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead and reheat; make the basil oil on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 brown onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 cup basil leaves
  • 60ml extra virgin olive oil
  1. Toss the tomatoes, onion and garlic with the olive oil and salt and roast at 220°C until soft and charred at the edges.
  2. Squeeze the garlic from its skins and blend everything with the stock and sugar until smooth.
  3. Warm through, stir in the cream and season.
  4. Blitz the basil with the extra virgin oil and a pinch of salt, and swirl over the soup in mugs.
  5. Hand it over in mugs or bowls you can drink from. No spoons.

Hello Clarice chicken skin crackling

Chip asks Steven for his chicken skin.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Bake the crackling up to a day ahead and keep airtight; warm and drizzle to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken skin, from the butcher
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Scrape any fat from the skin, lay it flat between 2 sheets of baking paper on a tray and weigh it down with a second tray.
  2. Bake at 200°C until golden and crisp right through, then drain on paper towel.
  3. Rub the salt with the lemon zest and paprika, and warm the honey with the chilli.
  4. Break into shards, drizzle with honey and hot sauce and sprinkle with the lemon salt.

Tournament roast chicken with buttered corn

Herb butter roast chicken with buttered corn.

Prep 12 min, cook 78 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the herb butter up to a day ahead and chill uncovered; roast to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 whole chicken, about 1.8kg
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp thyme and rosemary, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 800g small potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 corn cobs, halved
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Mash 50g butter with the garlic, herbs and lemon zest and push it under and over the chicken skin. Put the halved lemon inside.
  2. Toss the potatoes with the oil and salt around the chicken in a big roasting tin.
  3. Roast at 200°C, basting with the pan juices twice, until the juices run clear and the potatoes are golden.
  4. Boil the corn for 6 minutes, then roll in the rest of the butter with the paprika and salt.

Pastry of the castleVegetarian

Cinnamon apples in golden pastry. Chip gave all the knights free cable.

Prep 13 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Cook the apple filling up to 2 days ahead; fill and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 Granny Smith apples, peeled and diced
  • 40g butter
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp raw sugar
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Cook the apples in the butter with the brown sugar, half the cinnamon and the lemon juice until soft and sticky, then cool.
  2. Cut each pastry sheet into 4 squares, spoon apple onto each, fold into triangles and press the edges with a fork.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with raw sugar and the rest of the cinnamon and bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.
  4. Whip the cream softly and serve with the warm turnovers.

A mug of ale for me and me mate

Chip orders ale for him and his mate.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to a week ahead; chill the ale and juice well
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 375ml cans pale ale
  • 250ml cloudy apple juice
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  1. Stir the honey into the hot water and let it cool.
  2. Divide the apple juice and honey syrup between 4 big mugs or tankards.
  3. Top slowly with the cold ale and squeeze in a lemon wedge.

When to serve

Bring out the chicken when Steven asks the serving wench for a fork.
