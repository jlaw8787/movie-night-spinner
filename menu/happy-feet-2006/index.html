Snacks

Colony crumbed sardines with lemon mayo

Panko crumbed sardines fried golden, herby lemon mayo to dip.

Prep 13 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Make the lemon mayonnaise up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and crumb the sardines up to 3 hours ahead on a tray in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g butterflied sardine fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g panko crumbs
  • 2 lemons
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Pat the butterflied sardine fillets dry.
  2. Toss in the plain flour, dip in the beaten eggs, then press into the panko mixed with the zest of the lemon and salt.
  3. Shallow fry in 1cm oil over medium-high heat for 1 to 2 minutes a side until golden, then drain.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with lemon juice, chopped parsley and dill.
  5. Serve hot with the mayo and lemon wedges.

Iceberg wedges

Crisp lettuce wedges with blue cheese dressing, bacon and tomato.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the dressing up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead; cut the wedges and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 4 rashers of bacon
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tomato
  1. Cut the iceberg lettuce into wedges.
  2. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream with lemon and pepper.
  3. Fry the bacon rashers until crisp and crumble.
  4. Spoon the dressing over the wedges and scatter with bacon and the diced tomato.

Snowstorm parmesan cheese puffs

Parmesan puffs for the huddle. Warm thy egg.

How it is made
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 80g cheddar
  • 70g parmesan
  1. Light choux puffs beaten with cheddar and parmesan, piped into balls and baked until hollow, then dusted snowy white with finely grated parmesan. Heat the oven to 200°C. Bring 250ml water and 100g butter to the boil, beat in 150g plain flour until it pulls away from the pan, then cool 5 minutes. Beat in 4 eggs one at a time, then 80g grated cheddar and 40g grated parmesan. Pipe 24 small balls onto lined trays. Bake 22 to 25 minutes until puffed and golden. While warm, dust heavily with 30g very finely grated parmesan.

Mumble's fish pie

White fish and prawns in creamy sauce under a mashed potato top.

Prep 0 min, cook 66 min · Make ahead: Assemble the pie up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g white fish fillets
  • 500ml milk
  • 80g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 200g green prawns
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1kg potatoes
  1. Peel the potatoes and boil in salted water for 15 minutes until tender, then mash with half the butter.
  2. Poach the white fish in the milk for 5 minutes and flake.
  3. Make a sauce with the rest of the butter, the flour and the poaching milk, add the peeled prawns and chopped parsley, and pour into a dish with the fish, 10 min.
  4. Top with the mash and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes.

Trawler seafood chowder bread bowls

White fish, prawns, corn and bacon in dill cream served in cob loaves, trawler style.

How it is made
  • 100g diced bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml fish stock
  • 400ml milk
  • 400g potatoes
  • 400g firm white fish fillets
  • 200g raw peeled prawns
  • 200g frozen corn kernels
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 small cob loaves
  1. A thick creamy chowder of white fish, prawns, potato, corn and bacon with dill, ladled into hollowed crusty cob loaves. Fry 100g diced bacon and 1 diced onion for 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 400ml fish stock, 400ml milk and 400g diced potato. Simmer 15 minutes. Add 400g diced white fish, 200g peeled raw prawns and 200g corn kernels and simmer 4 minutes until just cooked. Stir in 100ml cream and chopped dill. Cut the tops off 4 small cob loaves, hollow them out and ladle in the chowder.

Coconut iceVegetarian

Sweet pink and white coconut and condensed milk squares.

Prep 16 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g desiccated coconut
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 pack red food colouring
  1. Mix the desiccated coconut, the condensed milk and the icing sugar into a stiff paste.
  2. Press half into a lined 20cm tin, tint the rest pink with a drop of colouring and press on top, 8 min.
  3. Chill 2 hours until firm and cut into squares.

Gloria's pink strawberry krill shakesNo alcohol

Strawberry and vanilla ice cream milkshake topped with cream.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Best blended to order; hull the strawberries and chill the glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g strawberries
  • 750g vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • pink sprinkles, to taste
  1. Hull the strawberries and blend with the vanilla ice cream and the cold milk until thick and pink.
  2. Pour into tall chilled glasses.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks, spoon on top and scatter with pink sprinkles and a halved strawberry.
  4. Serve straight away with wide straws.
