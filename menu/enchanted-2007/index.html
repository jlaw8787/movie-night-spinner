Snacks

Pip's acorn nutsVegetarian

Honey and cinnamon nuts, Pip's favourite, no words needed.

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 150g hazelnuts
  • 150g almonds
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Toss the hazelnuts and almonds with the honey and cinnamon.
  2. Spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170°C, stirring once, 12 min.
  4. Cool on baking paper, then break into clusters, 20 min.

Central Park pretzels with cheddar dip

Chewy knots in a bicarb bath, salted and baked brown, cheese dip, for the park picnic.

How it is made
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 egg
  • 1 packet sea salt flakes
  • 20g butter
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 jar mustard
  1. Chewy soft pretzels dipped in a bicarb bath, salted and baked deep brown, with a warm sharp cheddar and mustard dip. Divide 500g pizza dough into 8, roll into long ropes and twist into pretzels. Boil 2L water with 4 tbsp bicarb soda and dip each for 30 seconds. Brush with egg, sprinkle with sea salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. For the dip, melt 20g butter, stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, whisk in 250ml milk until thick, then stir in 150g grated cheddar and 1 tsp mustard until smooth.

Mains

Pancakes, bacon and scrambled eggs

Buttermilk pancakes with crisp bacon and soft scrambled eggs.

Prep 12 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Make the batter up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; cook the pancakes, bacon and eggs just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 6 eggs
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 40g butter, for cooking
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 250g strawberries
  • 100ml maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, 2 of the eggs and the buttermilk into a batter.
  2. Cook the pancakes in half the butter in the pan and keep warm, 15 min.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp, 8 min.
  4. Scramble the remaining eggs softly in the rest of the butter, 5 min.
  5. Slice the strawberries.
  6. Pile everything onto big plates with the maple syrup on the side.

Narissa's dragon-fired tenderloin

Beef fillet roasted pink with mushroom thyme cream, potato gratin and asparagus, ballroom ready.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 450ml cream
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  1. Beef eye fillet roasted pink with a creamy mushroom and thyme sauce, potato gratin and grilled asparagus, dinner fit for a fairytale ball. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes with 300ml cream, garlic and salt and bake at 180°C for 1 hour. Sear a seasoned 1kg eye fillet and roast at 200°C for 20 minutes, then rest 10 minutes. In the same pan, fry 250g sliced mushrooms in 30g butter, add thyme, 200ml beef stock and 150ml cream, and simmer until thick. Grill 2 bunches asparagus. Slice the beef and spoon over the sauce.

Sweets

Curtain dress pink meringuesVegetarian

Pink meringue kisses and strawberries dipped in white chocolate.

Prep 20 min, cook 72 min · Make ahead: Meringues up to 2 days airtight, dip berries just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 egg whites
  • 120g caster sugar
  • pink food colouring, a drop
  • 250g strawberries
  • 200g white chocolate
  • edible glitter, to dust
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, adding the sugar a spoon at a time and a drop of pink colouring.
  2. Pipe small kisses onto lined trays.
  3. Bake at 100°C until crisp, then cool on the tray, 1 hr.
  4. Cool the meringues completely, 15 min.
  5. Melt the white chocolate gently, 4 min.
  6. Dip the strawberries halfway in the chocolate and set on baking paper, 8 min.
  7. Dust with edible glitter and serve both together.

Giselle's pink lemonade floatNo alcohol

Raspberry lemonade with soda water and a scoop of lemon sorbet.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the raspberry and lemon mix up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; stir into the soda water, scoop the sorbet and pour just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g raspberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 4 lemons
  • 800ml chilled soda water
  • 500ml lemon sorbet
  • 4 mint leaves
  1. Blend half the raspberries with the sugar and lemon juice, then strain through a sieve.
  2. Stir the strained mix into the soda water in a jug.
  3. Put a scoop of sorbet into each tall glass.
  4. Slowly pour over the pink lemonade so it foams up.
  5. Drop in a few whole raspberries and a mint leaf, and serve with a spoon and a straw.
