Snacks

Pip's acorn nutsVegetarian

Honey and cinnamon nuts. Pip can't say a word in New York.

Prep 5 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 150g hazelnuts
  • 150g almonds
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Toss the hazelnuts and almonds with the honey and cinnamon.
  2. Spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170C, stirring once.
  4. Cool on baking paper, then break into clusters.

Mains

Pancakes, bacon and scrambled eggs

Buttermilk pancakes with crisp bacon and soft scrambled eggs

Prep 12 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Make the batter up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; cook the pancakes, bacon and eggs just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 6 eggs
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 40g butter, for cooking
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 250g strawberries
  • 100ml maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, 2 of the eggs and the buttermilk into a batter.
  2. Cook the pancakes in half the butter in the pan and keep warm.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  4. Scramble the remaining eggs softly in the rest of the butter.
  5. Slice the strawberries.
  6. Pile everything onto big plates with the maple syrup on the side.

Sweets

Curtain dress pink meringuesVegetarian

Pink meringue kisses and strawberries dipped in white chocolate

Prep 32 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Meringues up to 2 days airtight, dip berries just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 egg whites
  • 120g caster sugar
  • pink food colouring, a drop
  • 250g strawberries
  • 200g white chocolate
  • edible glitter, to dust
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, adding the sugar a spoon at a time and a drop of pink colouring.
  2. Pipe small kisses onto lined trays.
  3. Bake at 100C until crisp, then cool on the tray.
  4. Cool the meringues completely.
  5. Melt the white chocolate gently.
  6. Dip the strawberries halfway in the chocolate and set on baking paper.
  7. Dust with edible glitter and serve both together.

Poison apple toffee applesVegetarian

Crisp red fruit in hard, glassy red toffee, set shiny on baking paper

Prep 11 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best the same day; dip the apples up to 4 hours ahead and leave them uncovered on baking paper in a cool, dry spot
Recipe for 4
  • 6 red apples
  • 6 wooden sticks
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • red food colouring, a few drops
  1. Push a stick into each apple.
  2. Boil the sugar, water and red colouring to 150C without stirring.
  3. Dip each apple and twist to coat.
  4. Set on baking paper and leave to harden.

Drinks

Giselle's pink lemonade floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Raspberry lemonade with soda water and a scoop of lemon sorbet

Prep 18 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the raspberry and lemon mix up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; stir into the soda water, scoop the sorbet and pour just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g raspberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 4 lemons
  • 800ml chilled soda water
  • 500ml lemon sorbet
  • 4 mint leaves
  1. Blend half the raspberries with the sugar and lemon juice, then strain through a sieve.
  2. Stir the strained mix into the soda water in a jug.
  3. Put a scoop of sorbet into each tall glass.
  4. Slowly pour over the pink lemonade so it foams up.
  5. Drop in a few whole raspberries and a mint leaf, and serve with a spoon and a straw.
