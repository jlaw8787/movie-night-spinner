Snacks

Tucson Sonoran hot dog bites

Bacon-wrapped cocktail franks in mini rolls with pinto beans, tomato and jalapeño salsa.

Prep 28 min · Make ahead: Dice the tomato and onion up to 1 day ahead; wrap the franks in bacon up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled, and griddle to order
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 8 rashers bacon, halved
  • 16 mini bread rolls
  • 400g can pinto beans
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp jalapeno salsa
  1. Dice the tomato and onion and warm the pinto beans.
  2. Wrap the franks in bacon.
  3. Griddle until crisp.
  4. Tuck into the rolls with beans, tomato, onion, mustard and salsa.

Anteater Ritual potato skins

Baked potato skins filled with bacon and cheddar, topped with sour cream.

Prep 2 min, cook 76 min · Make ahead: Bake the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; fill and bake again on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 medium potatoes
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C.
  2. Halve, scoop out most of the flesh and brush the skins with melted butter.
  3. Fry the bacon and fill the skins with bacon and cheddar.
  4. Bake again until the cheese melts.
  5. Top with sour cream and chives.

Cool table chicken tenders, honey mustard

Parmesan-crumbed tenderloins baked crisp, crunchy crispy potato bites on the side of the tray.

How it is made
  • 600g chicken tenderloins
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g dried breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 500g potato gems
  • 1 can cooking oil spray
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  1. Chicken tenderloins crumbed in breadcrumbs and parmesan, baked crisp, served on a tray with honey mustard dipping sauce and crunchy potato gems. Toss 600g chicken tenderloins in 50g flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs, then press into 100g panko mixed with 30g grated parmesan and paprika. Lay on a lined tray with 500g frozen potato gems, spray with oil and bake at 220°C for 20 minutes, turning once, until golden. Mix 3 tbsp Dijon, 3 tbsp honey and 3 tbsp mayonnaise for the dip. Serve on a tray.

Tucson red chilli enchiladas

Shredded chicken and cheddar rolled in corn tortillas under red enchilada sauce.

Prep 4 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Fill and roll the tortillas up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; add the sauce and cheddar and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g shredded cooked chicken
  • 750ml red enchilada sauce, divided
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 250g cheddar, grated, divided
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  1. Simmer the chicken in some of the sauce.
  2. Fill the warmed tortillas with the chicken and cheddar, roll and lay in a baking dish.
  3. Pour over the remaining sauce and top with cheddar.
  4. Bake at 190°C.
  5. Scatter with coriander.

Shredded beef chimichangas

Chuck braised with chillies, garlic and cumin, rolled with cheddar in tortillas and fried crisp.

How it is made
  • 3 dried chillies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 150g cheddar
  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 200g guacamole
  1. Beef chuck braised with dried chillies, garlic and cumin until it shreds, rolled with cheddar in flour tortillas, fried crisp, with sour cream and guacamole. Soak 3 dried chillies, blitz with 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin and 400ml beef stock. Braise 1kg beef chuck in the sauce, covered, at 160°C for 3 hours, then shred and reduce until thick. Lay the beef and 150g grated cheddar on 8 large flour tortillas, fold in the sides and roll tightly. Fry seam side down at 180°C for 2 minutes a side until crisp. Serve with sour cream and guacamole.

Thousand-dollar date night sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, dark hot fudge, whipped cream, peanuts and a cherry.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; whip the cream and scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream, divided
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 50g peanuts, chopped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate into some of the cream for hot fudge.
  2. Whip the remaining cream.
  3. Scoop ice cream into four tall glasses and pour over the fudge.
  4. Pipe whipped cream high and top with chopped peanuts and a cherry.
