Game night charades popcornVegetarian

Buttered popcorn drizzled with white chocolate and sea salt

Prep 12 min, cook 6 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the corn with the oil in a large lidded pot.
  2. Toss with the melted butter and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle all over.
  4. Sprinkle with sea salt.
  5. Leave to set.
  6. Break into clusters.

Olaf's honey roast carrots with fetaVegetarian

Honey and thyme roast baby carrots with whipped feta and yoghurt

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Whip the feta and yoghurt dip up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; roast the carrots on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g baby carrots
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g feta
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  1. Toss the carrots with the honey, thyme and oil.
  2. Roast at 200C until sticky.
  3. Whip the feta with the yoghurt until smooth.
  4. Serve the carrots warm around the dip.

Enchanted forest mushroom toastsOn the printed menu

Mixed fungi seared hard in brown butter with garlic and thyme, charred sourdough, whipped ricotta

How it is made
  • 200g ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • 40g butter
  • 400g mixed mushrooms
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Mixed mushrooms seared hard in brown butter with garlic and thyme, piled onto charred sourdough with whipped ricotta and shaved parmesan. Mix 200g ricotta with the zest of 1 lemon and salt and whip until smooth. Melt 40g butter in a large pan until golden and nutty, add 400g torn mixed mushrooms and fry over high heat for 5 minutes until browned. Add 2 crushed garlic cloves and thyme and cook 1 minute. Grill 4 slices of sourdough until charred. Spread with ricotta, pile on the mushrooms and finish with shaved parmesan.

Mains

Arendelle fish stew

Salmon, white fish and prawns simmered with leeks and potatoes

Prep 27 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is made
Recipe for 4
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 1l water
  • 300g salmon fillet
  • 300g white fish fillets
  • 200g green prawns, peeled
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1/4 cup dill, chopped
  • 8 slices rye bread
  1. Slice the leeks, dice the potatoes and cut the fish into chunks. Peel the prawns.
  2. Soften the leeks in the butter.
  3. Add the potatoes and water and simmer until tender.
  4. Add the salmon, white fish and prawns and cook until just done.
  5. Stir in the cream and dill and serve with rye bread.

Northuldra juniper roast lambOn the printed menu

Shoulder rubbed in rosemary and garlic, slow roasted, charred roots, for Mattias's lost soldiers

How it is made
  • 1 jar juniper berries
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 400g carrots
  • 300g parsnips
  • 300g beetroot
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Lamb shoulder rubbed with juniper, garlic and rosemary, slow roasted until it falls apart, with charred root vegetables and lingonberry-style jam. Crush 1 tbsp juniper berries with 4 garlic cloves, rosemary leaves, olive oil and salt and rub over a 2kg lamb shoulder. Put in a roasting tin with 250ml water, cover with foil and roast at 150C for 5 hours. Add 1kg chopped carrots, parsnips and beetroot for the last 90 minutes, uncovering for the final 30 minutes. Pull the lamb apart and serve with cranberry sauce.

Ahtohallan ice blue pavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue with whipped cream, blueberries and a dusting of icing sugar

Prep 24 min, cook 90 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Meringue up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs, whites only
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g blueberries
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Shape into a round on lined paper and bake at 120C.
  4. Cool in the oven with the door closed.
  5. Whip the cream, top the pavlova with cream and blueberries and dust with icing sugar.

Kristoff's hot spiced apple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Warm apple juice with cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and maple syrup

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Infuse the juice with the spices up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; rewarm gently and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 1 orange
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Peel strips of zest from the orange and slice the flesh.
  2. Warm the apple juice, 2 cinnamon sticks, cloves, zest and maple syrup over low heat without boiling.
  3. Strain into mugs and add a fresh cinnamon stick and an orange slice to each.
  4. Serve with carrots on the side for anyone who brought a reindeer.

Serve the snacks when the family plays charades on game night.
