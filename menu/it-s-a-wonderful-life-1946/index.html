Snacks

Coconut macaroonsVegetarian

Chewy coconut moons, baked golden, for George to lasso

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g desiccated coconut
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 egg whites
  1. Mix the coconut with the condensed milk, vanilla and salt.
  2. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks and fold through.
  3. Scoop 20 mounds onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 160°C until golden on the edges.
  5. Cool on the tray.

Christmas Eve cheese strawsVegetarian

Puff pastry twists with sharp cheddar, parmesan and cayenne

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 80g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Lay one sheet of the puff pastry on a tray, sprinkle with the cheddar, parmesan and cayenne, top with the second sheet and press.
  2. Cut into 2cm strips and twist each one.
  3. Bake at 200°C until crisp.

House-blessing rosemary salt focacciaOn the printed menu

Focaccia baked with rosemary and sea salt. Bread, salt and wine for the Martinis' new house.

How it is made
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • sea salt flakes
  • balsamic vinegar
  1. Thick, airy focaccia dimpled with olive oil, rosemary and flaky salt, baked golden and served warm with good olive oil and balsamic for dipping. Mix 500g bread flour, 7g yeast, 10g salt and 400ml warm water into a wet dough. Cover and rest 2 hours, folding it over itself twice. Pour 4 tbsp olive oil into a 23 x 33cm tin, tip in the dough and stretch to the corners. Rest 45 minutes. Heat the oven to 220°C. Dimple deeply with oiled fingers, drizzle with more oil, scatter rosemary and flaky salt and bake 25 minutes. Serve warm with oil and balsamic.

Mains

Honeymoon roast chicken

Whole chicken with garlic thyme butter, roasted over potatoes

Prep 10 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  1. Mix the butter with crushed garlic, thyme and salt and rub over the chicken.
  2. Cut the potatoes into chunks and set a rack over them in a roasting tin.
  3. Roast at 200°C, turning the potatoes halfway, until the juices run clear.
  4. Rest, then carve.

Ma Bailey's pot roastOn the printed menu

Beef chuck braised with carrots, potatoes and onions in gravy. Dinner at the Bailey house.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml red wine
  • 600ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • bay leaves
  • 4 carrots
  • 800g potatoes
  1. Beef chuck browned and braised for hours with onions, carrots and potatoes in red wine and stock until it falls apart, with thick gravy. Heat the oven to 160°C. Season 1.5kg beef chuck and brown well in oil in a casserole. Remove. Fry 2 onions in wedges for 5 minutes, add 2 tbsp tomato paste and 2 tbsp flour, then 200ml red wine and 600ml beef stock, thyme and bay. Return the beef, cover and cook 2 hours. Add 4 carrots and 800g potatoes in chunks and cook 1 hour more. Simmer the liquid on the stove to thicken into gravy.

Gower's chocolate sundaeVegetarian

Chocolate ice cream, hot fudge, whipped cream and a cherry

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream, for the fudge sauce
  • 1l chocolate ice cream
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped, for the topping
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the chocolate with the cream into a hot fudge sauce.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into 4 tall glasses and pour over the fudge.
  3. Whip the cream, top each sundae and add a cherry. No coconut.

EggnogVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled egg yolk, milk and cream custard drink with nutmeg, no alcohol

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml milk
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar.
  2. Heat the milk and cream until steaming, whisk into the yolks and return to the pan.
  3. Stir over low heat until it coats a spoon.
  4. Add the vanilla and chill, which is the resting time.
  5. Serve with grated nutmeg.
