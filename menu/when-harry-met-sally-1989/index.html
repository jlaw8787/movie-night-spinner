Snacks

Fried picklesVegetarian

Half-sour spears in crisp rye batter, sharp mustard dip, for the drive to New York.

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the mustard mayo up to 2 days ahead; batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 half-sour or dill pickles
  • 80g plain flour
  • 40g rye flour
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 150ml cold soda water
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Cut the pickles into spears and pat them very dry. Keep a teaspoon of the brine.
  2. Whisk the plain flour, rye flour, paprika and cold soda water into a thin batter.
  3. Dip the spears and fry at 180°C for about 2 minutes until crisp, in batches. Drain on a rack and salt lightly.
  4. Stir the mayonnaise with the mustard and the pickle brine and serve alongside.

Mains

Chef salad

Dressing on the side, everything on the side.

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Boil the eggs up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cos lettuces
  • 200g sliced leg ham
  • 200g sliced turkey
  • 150g swiss cheese, cut into strips
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 30ml red wine vinegar
  1. Hard-boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool in cold water, peel and halve.
  2. Wash the cos lettuces, dry well and tear into a large platter.
  3. Arrange the leg ham, turkey, swiss cheese strips, halved eggs, tomatoes and cucumber in rows over the lettuce.
  4. Pour the olive oil and red wine vinegar into two little jugs and serve them on the side.

Pastrami on rye

Steamed glossy, piled high on seeded bread with yellow mustard, I'll have what she's having.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best steamed just before serving; set out the mustard and pickles ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g sliced pastrami
  • 8 slices seeded rye bread
  • 40g yellow mustard
  • 4 full sour dill pickles
  1. Steam the pastrami over gently boiling water until glossy, soft and piping hot.
  2. Spread the rye with mustard.
  3. Pile the pastrami high on four slices and close the sandwiches.
  4. Serve each with a pickle on the side.

Sweets

Apple pie à la modeVegetarian

Warm slice with strawberry ice cream alongside, real whipped cream if they don't have it.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Cut the pie into slices up to 1 day ahead and keep covered; warm in the oven just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g ready-baked apple pie
  • 300ml strawberry ice cream, or 150ml thickened cream if unavailable
  1. Cut four slices from the pie.
  2. Warm on a tray at 180°C.
  3. Serve with the strawberry ice cream in a separate small bowl beside each slice.

Drinks

Chocolate egg creamNo alcohol

Chocolate syrup and cold milk with soda water blasted in hard, stirred to a foamy head.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the milk, syrup and soda water well ahead; build each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml chocolate syrup
  • 400ml full cream milk, ice cold
  • 1 L soda water, ice cold
  1. Pour 30ml chocolate syrup into each of four tall glasses and add 100ml ice cold milk.
  2. Blast the soda water in hard from a height until the foam reaches the rim, stirring fast with a long spoon as it goes. Serve straight away. No egg, no cream.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Harry and Sally sit down at the deli.
