Snacks

Ned Ryerson's eggsVegetarian

Yolks, mayo, Dijon and hot sauce piped into whites, am I right or am I right

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 30g mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Boil the eggs, cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Halve and remove the yolks.
  3. Whip the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, hot sauce and paprika until smooth.
  4. Pipe back into the white halves and refrigerate until serving.

Tip Top Cafe silver dollar pancakes

Little buttermilk stacks with crisp bacon and whipped maple butter, same as yesterday

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g butter
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 150g bacon, diced
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar and a pinch of salt with the buttermilk, eggs and 40g melted butter. Rest 15 minutes.
  2. Beat 40g soft butter with the maple syrup.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp and drain on paper.
  4. Cook tablespoons of batter in a little of the last butter over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes a side.
  5. Stack the pancakes, top with a little maple butter and scatter with the bacon.

Mains

Phil's diner feast

Pancakes, eggs, bacon, toast and pie, everything at once

Prep 30 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Mix the pancake batter up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook everything else on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 15g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 200g bacon
  • 40g butter
  • 4 slices white bread
  • 200g ready-baked pie
  • 60ml maple syrup
  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, one egg and buttermilk into a batter and rest.
  2. Render the bacon slowly over medium heat until crisp and keep warm.
  3. Cook small pancakes in half the butter until bubbles set and both sides are golden.
  4. Fry four eggs in the bacon pan until the whites are set.
  5. Toast the bread and butter with the remaining butter.
  6. Warm small slices of pie at 180C.
  7. Bring everything to the table at once, with maple syrup for the pancakes.

Sweets

Groundhog festival doughnutsVegetarian

Cinnamon sugar cake rings shaken in a paper bag, for Gobbler's Knob

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best warmed and sugared just before serving; mix the cinnamon sugar up to 1 week ahead in a jar
Recipe for 4
  • 4 small cake doughnuts
  • 30g caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Warm the doughnuts at 160C.
  2. Put the sugar and cinnamon in a clean food-safe paper bag.
  3. Add the warm doughnuts and shake gently until coated.

Angel food cakeVegetarian

Airy sponge of whipped egg whites and sugar, cooled upside down, served with berries and cream

Prep 30 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 5 egg whites
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 60g cake flour
  • 75g icing sugar
  • 150g berries
  • 80ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the egg whites with the cream of tartar to soft peaks.
  2. Add the caster sugar gradually and whisk until glossy.
  3. Sift the flour with the icing sugar and fold gently into the whites in three additions.
  4. Spoon into an ungreased 16cm tube pan.
  5. Bake at 175C until golden and springy.
  6. Invert the tin and cool upside down completely.
  7. Slice and serve with berries and softly whipped cream.

Rita's sweet vermouth on the rocksVegetarian

Rita's order at the bar, the one Phil learns by heart

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Freeze the large ice blocks up to 1 week ahead; pour and garnish to order
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml sweet red vermouth
  • 4 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 orange
  • 4 large ice blocks
  1. Put a large ice block in each rocks glass.
  2. Pour over the vermouth and add a dash of bitters.
  3. Peel long strips of orange zest, twist over the glass, rub round the rim and drop in. Stir twice.
