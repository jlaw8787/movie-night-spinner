Snacks

Metro City chicken quesadilla wedges

Tortillas filled with chicken, cheddar, mozzarella and capsicum

Prep 17 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the tortillas up to 3 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge. Cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cooked chicken, shredded
  • 200g cheddar and mozzarella mix
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Dice the capsicum and slice the spring onions.
  2. Mix the chicken, cheese, capsicum, spring onions and paprika.
  3. Spread over half of each tortilla and fold over.
  4. Cook in a dry pan over medium heat, a few minutes a side, until golden and melted.
  5. Cut each into four wedges and serve with salsa and sour cream.

Cheddar fish crackersVegetarian

Crisp cheddar and smoked paprika crackers

Prep 21 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 pinch smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp cold water
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then mix in the grated cheddar and paprika.
  2. Add cold water to bring together and chill.
  3. Roll thin and cut into fish shapes.
  4. Bake at 180C.
  5. Serve in a round glass bowl.

Mains

Villain's chilli

Beef chuck slow simmered with chipotle, cumin and kidney beans

Prep 10 min, cook 195 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 2 chipotles in adobo
  • 2 cans kidney beans
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Chop the chipotles and drain the beans.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  3. Add the tomatoes, cumin, chipotles and water to cover and simmer.
  4. Add the beans for the last stretch of cooking.
  5. Serve with sour cream.

Sweets

Blue velvet cupcakesVegetarian

Cocoa buttermilk cupcakes tinted blue, piped with frosting

Prep 20 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • blue food colouring, a few drops
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa, sugar, egg, oil, buttermilk and colouring to a smooth batter.
  3. Bake in cases.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with icing sugar and pipe on top.

Drinks

Cola ice cream floatsVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold cola poured slowly over vanilla ice cream in chilled glasses

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer and shave the chocolate up to 1 day ahead. Scoop and pour each float to order
Recipe for 4
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1.2l cola
  • 30g dark chocolate
  1. Chill four tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Put two scoops of ice cream in each glass.
  3. Pour cold cola slowly down the side, letting the foam settle before topping up.
  4. Shave chocolate over the foam and serve with a long spoon and a straw.
