Cammareri Bros. rosemary focacciaVegetarian

Warm bread from Ronny's bakery, the Cammareri Bros. ovens

Prep 5 min, cook 24 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked; keeps 1 day airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 400ml warm water
  • 90ml olive oil, divided
  • 3 sprigs rosemary, leaves picked
  • 1 tsp flaky salt, to finish
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and warm water into a wet dough. Cover and rise for 2 hours.
  2. Oil a 30 x 20cm tin with most of the oil, tip in the dough and stretch to the corners. Rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Dimple deeply with oiled fingers, scatter with rosemary and flaky salt and drizzle with the remaining oil.
  4. Bake at 230C until deep golden.

Thin salami and provolone, fennel olives and garlicky capsicum, for the Castorini family table

How it is made
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 200g mixed olives
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 100g sliced salami
  • 100g provolone
  • olive oil
  1. Thin salami and provolone, olives warmed with orange peel and fennel seed, and roasted capsicum dressed in garlic oil. Char 2 red capsicums under a hot grill for 15 minutes, turning, then cover in a bowl for 10 minutes. Peel, slice and dress with 1 sliced garlic clove, 2 tbsp olive oil and salt. Warm 200g mixed olives in 1 tbsp oil with 2 strips orange peel and 1 tsp fennel seeds for 3 minutes. Arrange with 100g thinly sliced salami and 100g provolone on a platter.

Eggs in the holeVegetarian

Rose's kitchen breakfast: a red capsicum ring and an egg in toast

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Cut the capsicum rings up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; fry the eggs in the hole to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 thick slices white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 1 red capsicum, cut in 4 thick rings
  • 4 eggs
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut a hole in each slice of bread.
  2. Melt the butter in a big pan and lay in the bread. Put a capsicum ring in each hole and crack an egg into it.
  3. Fry until the white sets, flipping the bread once. Season.

Chicken scaloppine

Thin breast seared, lemon, white wine, butter and caper sauce, for Cosmo and Mona's night out

Prep 9 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Slice and pound the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled between sheets of baking paper; sear and make the pan sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken breast (2 large)
  • 40g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 20g butter
  • 100ml white wine
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 40g cold butter
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Slice the chicken in half horizontally and pound thin.
  2. Dust in flour and sear in the oil and butter for 2 minutes a side, then set aside.
  3. Add the wine, lemon juice and capers, reduce by half and swirl in the cold butter and parsley.
  4. Return the chicken to coat.

CannoliVegetarian

Crisp shells piped with ricotta and orange zest, chocolate-dipped ends, snap out of it

Prep 15 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead; fill shells just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fresh ricotta
  • 80g icing sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 50g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 8 cannoli shells
  1. Drain the ricotta in a sieve for 2 hours.
  2. Beat with icing sugar and orange zest, then fold in the chocolate.
  3. Pipe into the shells just before serving and dust with icing sugar.

Brooklyn amaretto soursVegetarian

Loretta's family, bottle of wine and a full moon

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemon juice up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; shake the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml amaretto
  • 100ml lemon juice
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 bag ice
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Shake the amaretto, lemon juice and egg white hard without ice, in rounds for two drinks at a time.
  2. Shake again with ice.
  3. Strain over fresh ice in four short glasses and add a cherry to each.

When to serve

Bring out the bread when Ronny is in the bakery basement.
