Rosemary focacciaVegetarian

From a Brooklyn baker, dimpled with olive oil and coarse salt. Cut by hand.

Prep 5 min, cook 24 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 400ml warm water
  • 90ml olive oil, divided
  • 3 sprigs of rosemary, leaves picked
  • 1 tsp flaky salt, to finish
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and warm water into a wet dough. Cover and rise for 2 hours.
  2. Oil a 30 x 20cm tin with most of the oil, tip in the dough and stretch to the corners. Rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Dimple deeply with oiled fingers, scatter with rosemary and flaky salt and drizzle with the remaining oil.
  4. Bake at 230°C until deep golden, 24 min.

Eggs in the holeVegetarian

White toast fried in butter, an egg set in a ring of red pepper, yolk soft.

Prep 2 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 thick slices of white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 1 red capsicum, cut in 4 thick rings
  • 4 eggs
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut a hole in each slice of bread.
  2. Melt the butter in a big pan and lay in the bread. Put a capsicum ring in each hole and crack an egg into it, 3 min.
  3. Fry until the white sets, flipping the bread once. Season, 7 min.

Chicken scaloppine piccata

Pounded thin, floured and sautéed, with lemon, white wine, capers and butter.

Prep 9 min, cook 11 min
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken breast (2 large)
  • 40g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 20g butter
  • 100ml white wine
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 40g cold butter
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Slice the chicken in half horizontally and pound thin.
  2. Dust in flour and sear in the oil and butter for 2 minutes a side, then set aside.
  3. Add the wine, lemon juice and capers, reduce by half and swirl in the cold butter and parsley, 5 min.
  4. Return the chicken to coat.

Cannoli, twoVegetarian

Ricotta, sugar and orange zest in chocolate dipped shells, filled to order.

Prep 15 min, then 2 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 500g fresh ricotta
  • 80g icing sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 50g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 8 cannoli shells
  1. Drain the ricotta in a sieve for 2 hours.
  2. Beat with icing sugar and orange zest, then fold in the chocolate.
  3. Pipe into the shells just before serving and dust with icing sugar.

Amaretto sour

Shaken frothy with lemon and egg white, over ice, a cherry on top.

Prep 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml amaretto
  • 100ml lemon juice
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 bag ice
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Shake the amaretto, lemon juice and egg white hard without ice, in rounds for two drinks at a time.
  2. Shake again with ice.
  3. Strain over fresh ice in four short glasses and add a cherry to each.
