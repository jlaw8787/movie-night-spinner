Snacks

Duke & Duke club canapés

Almond-stuffed prunes wrapped in streaky bacon and baked crisp, for the Dukes' one-dollar bet

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, bake before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 20 pitted prunes
  • 20 almonds
  • 10 rashers streaky bacon
  1. Push an almond into each prune.
  2. Wrap each in half a rasher of bacon and secure with a toothpick.
  3. Bake at 200C until the bacon is crisp.
  4. Serve warm.

Heritage Club Caesar

Cos in a coddled egg, anchovy and lemon dressing, croutons and parmesan, before Louis is framed

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fry the croutons up to 1 day ahead and keep them airtight; dress the leaves at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cos lettuces
  • 200g sourdough loaf
  • 40g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 6 anchovy fillets
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 60g parmesan
  1. Tear the sourdough into small pieces and fry in the butter, tossing, until golden and crisp. Drain.
  2. Lower the egg into simmering water for 1 minute, then cool it under the tap.
  3. Mash the garlic and anchovies to a paste in a big bowl. Crack in the coddled egg, add the lemon juice, Worcestershire and mustard, then whisk in the oil slowly until thick.
  4. Add the cos leaves, whole or torn, and toss at the table until every leaf is coated.
  5. Shave the parmesan over, scatter the croutons and serve straight away.

Mains

Santa Winthorpe's pocket roast beef

Standing rib roasted low, jus and horseradish, for New Year's Eve on the train

Prep 30 min, cook 920 min · Make ahead: Salt the roast the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg standing rib roast
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 150g horseradish cream
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 3 tbsp oil for the potatoes
  1. Salt the roast the day before and leave it uncovered in the fridge.
  2. Roast at 120C until 52C inside.
  3. Rest the beef.
  4. Roast the potatoes in oil at 200C while the beef rests.
  5. Blast the beef at 260C for a crust.
  6. Simmer the pan juices with the stock for jus.
  7. Carve and serve with horseradish cream, potatoes and jus.

Sweets

Orange cakeVegetarian

Frozen concentrate glaze

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 oranges, zested
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml orange juice
  • 100ml orange juice concentrate
  1. Beat the butter, most of the sugar and the orange zest until pale.
  2. Add the eggs, then fold in the flour with the orange juice.
  3. Bake in a lined tin at 170C.
  4. Boil the concentrate with the remaining sugar and brush over the warm cake.

MimosaVegetarian

Fresh orange juice, the real winner

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Squeeze and chill the juice up to 1 day ahead; pour and top with sparkling wine just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 oranges, about 300ml juice
  • 300ml chilled sparkling wine
  1. Squeeze the oranges and chill the juice well.
  2. Fill four flutes halfway with juice and gently top with sparkling wine, pouring slowly.

Frozen orange juice shotsVegetarian

Fresh juice blended with ice and vodka into slush, in shot glasses, for the crop report

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the juice and the shot glasses up to 1 day ahead; blend with the ice and vodka just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 2 cup ice
  • 80ml vodka, optional
  1. Blend the juice, ice and vodka until slushy.
  2. Pour into shot glasses and serve straight away. Leave out the vodka for anyone not drinking.
