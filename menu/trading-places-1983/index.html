Snacks

Devils on horseback

Prunes stuffed with a whole almond, rolled in Lancaster bacon and baked crisp.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 20 pitted prunes
  • 20 almonds
  • 10 rashers of streaky bacon
  1. Push an almond into each prune.
  2. Wrap each in half a rasher of bacon and secure with a toothpick.
  3. Bake at 200°C until the bacon is crisp, 15 min.
  4. Serve warm.

Caesar salad

Romaine, coddled egg, anchovy and Parmesan, dressed at your table.

Prep 12 min, cook 13 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cos lettuces
  • 200g sourdough loaf
  • 40g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 6 anchovy fillets
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 60g parmesan
  1. Tear the sourdough into small pieces and fry in the butter, tossing, until golden and crisp. Drain, 10 min.
  2. Lower the egg into simmering water for 1 minute, then cool it under the tap.
  3. Mash the garlic and anchovies to a paste in a big bowl. Crack in the coddled egg, add the lemon juice, Worcestershire and mustard, then whisk in the oil slowly until thick.
  4. Add the cos leaves, whole or torn, and toss at the table until every leaf is coated.
  5. Shave the parmesan over, scatter the croutons and serve straight away.

Mains

Roast prime rib of beef

Standing rib, salted a day ahead, roasted slow and carved to order.

Prep 18 min, cook 3 hr 20 min, 13 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg standing rib roast
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 150g horseradish cream
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 3 tbsp oil for the potatoes
  1. Salt the roast the day before and leave it uncovered in the fridge.
  2. Roast at 120°C until 52°C inside, 2 hr 30 min.
  3. Rest the beef, 30 min.
  4. Roast the potatoes in oil at 200°C while the beef rests, 40 min.
  5. Blast the beef at 260°C for a crust.
  6. Simmer the pan juices with the stock for jus, 10 min.
  7. Carve and serve with horseradish cream, potatoes and jus.

Orange butter cakeVegetarian

Grated rind and juice in the batter, brushed warm with orange syrup.

Prep 18 min, cook 50 min
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 oranges, zested
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml orange juice
  • 100ml orange juice concentrate
  1. Beat the butter, most of the sugar and the orange zest until pale.
  2. Add the eggs, then fold in the flour with the orange juice.
  3. Bake in a lined tin at 170°C, 45 min.
  4. Boil the concentrate with the remaining sugar and brush over the warm cake, 5 min.

Drinks

Mimosa

Fresh-squeezed juice and chilled champagne, half and half.

Prep 5 min, 15 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 4 oranges, about 300ml juice
  • 300ml chilled sparkling wine
  1. Squeeze the oranges and chill the juice well, 15 min.
  2. Fill four flutes halfway with juice and gently top with sparkling wine, pouring slowly.
