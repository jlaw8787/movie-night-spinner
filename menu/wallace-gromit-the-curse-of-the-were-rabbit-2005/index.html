Wallace's cheese boardVegetarian

Wensleydale, cheddar and blue with crackers and chutney.

Prep 15 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Assemble up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g Wensleydale cheese
  • 150g aged cheddar
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 1 packet of water crackers
  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 1 jar of apple chutney
  1. Take the Wensleydale, aged cheddar and blue cheese out of the fridge to come to room temperature.
  2. Slice the apples and arrange the cheeses, crackers and apple on a board.
  3. Add a pot of chutney and serve.

Anti-Pesto cheese and onion pastiesVegetarian

Shortcrust pasties filled with cheddar, potato and onion.

Prep 27 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1 potato, finely diced
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a tray, 3 min.
  2. Mix the cheddar, potato and onion with seasoning.
  3. Cut the pastry into rounds, fill, fold over and crimp the edges.
  4. Brush with egg and bake until golden, 25 min.

Prize veg bubble and squeak cakes

Mashed potato and cabbage cakes fried crisp in butter. Gromit's prize marrow, squashed at the show.

How it is made
  • 800g potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 1 savoy cabbage
  • 1 leek
  • 1 carrot
  • 80g cheddar
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 bottle brown sauce
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  1. Mashed potato, cabbage, carrot and leek pan-fried into crisp golden cakes with melted cheddar, served with brown sauce and a dollop of chutney. Boil 800g potatoes and mash with 30g butter. Shred a quarter savoy cabbage and 1 leek, grate 1 carrot and fry in butter for 6 minutes until soft. Mix into the mash with 80g grated cheddar, salt and pepper. Shape into 12 small cakes and chill 20 minutes. Fry in butter and oil over medium heat for 4 minutes a side until deep golden. Serve with brown sauce and chutney.

Prize marrow lamb stuffed courgette

Courgette filled with lamb and tomato, topped with cheddar and baked.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large zucchini
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tin of chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Halve the zucchini and scoop out the middles, chopping the flesh, 10 min.
  2. Fry the onion and lamb mince in the oil with the zucchini flesh, tomatoes and thyme until thick, 15 min.
  3. Fill the zucchini shells and cover with cheddar.
  4. Bake until tender and bubbling, 35 min.

Tottington Hall roast beef and Yorkshires

Rare sirloin, tall puddings, crisp potatoes, honey carrots, rich gravy and horseradish.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef sirloin
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 eggs
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 jar horseradish
  1. Rare roast sirloin with tall Yorkshire puddings, crisp roast potatoes, honey carrots and rich beef gravy, horseradish on the side. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub a 1.2kg beef sirloin with oil, salt and pepper and roast 15 minutes, then at 180°C for 25 to 30 minutes to 52°C inside. Rest 20 minutes. Roast 1kg parboiled potatoes in hot oil for 45 minutes. Whisk 3 eggs, 150ml milk and 120g plain flour, pour into a very hot oiled 12-hole tin and bake 20 minutes at 220°C. Roast 500g carrots with honey. Make gravy with the pan juices, flour and 400ml beef stock.

Golden Carrot cakeVegetarian

Carrot and walnut cake with cream cheese icing.

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g carrot, grated
  • 100g walnuts, chopped
  • 200ml vegetable oil
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a cake tin, 5 min.
  2. Mix the carrot, walnuts, oil, sugar, eggs and spice, then fold in the flour.
  3. Pour into the tin and bake until a skewer comes out clean, 50 min.
  4. Cool completely, 20 min.
  5. Beat the cream cheese and icing sugar, spread over the cake.

Wallace's iced Earl Grey lemonadeNo alcohol

Earl Grey brewed strong and iced with lemon zest syrup, a proper cuppa for a hot day.

Prep 6 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add the sparkling water at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Earl Grey tea bags
  • 800ml boiling water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 lemons
  • 400ml sparkling water
  • 4 cups ice
  • mint leaves, to serve
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water, then remove and cool, 8 min.
  2. Peel the zest from 2 of the lemons, simmer it with the sugar and water, then cool and strain, 6 min.
  3. Juice 3 of the lemons, including the 2 peeled ones, and stir the syrup and lemon juice into the tea.
  4. Pour over ice, top with sparkling water and add wheels from the last lemon and mint.
