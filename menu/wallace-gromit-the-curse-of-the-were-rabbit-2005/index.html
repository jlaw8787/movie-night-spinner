Snacks

Wallace's cheese boardVegetarian

Wensleydale, cheddar and blue with crackers and chutney. "Cheese, Gromit!"

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g Wensleydale cheese
  • 150g aged cheddar
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 1 packet of water crackers
  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 1 jar of apple chutney
  1. Take the Wensleydale, aged cheddar and blue cheese out of the fridge to come to room temperature.
  2. Slice the apples and arrange the cheeses, crackers and apple on a board.
  3. Add a pot of chutney and serve.

Anti-Pesto cheese and onion pastiesVegetarian

Shortcrust pasties filled with cheddar, potato and onion

Prep 35 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1 potato, finely diced
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a tray.
  2. Mix the cheddar, potato and onion with seasoning.
  3. Cut the pastry into rounds, fill, fold over and crimp the edges.
  4. Brush with egg and bake until golden.

Mains

Prize marrow lamb stuffed zucchini

Zucchini filled with lamb and tomato, topped with cheddar and baked

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large zucchini
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tin of chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven to 190C. Halve the zucchini and scoop out the middles, chopping the flesh.
  2. Fry the onion and lamb mince in the oil with the zucchini flesh, tomatoes and thyme until thick.
  3. Fill the zucchini shells and cover with cheddar.
  4. Bake until tender and bubbling.

Sweets

Golden Carrot cakeVegetarian

Carrot and walnut cake with cream cheese frosting

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g carrot, grated
  • 100g walnuts, chopped
  • 200ml vegetable oil
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a cake tin.
  2. Mix the carrot, walnuts, oil, sugar, eggs and spice, then fold in the flour.
  3. Pour into the tin and bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Beat the cream cheese and icing sugar, spread over the cake.

Wallace's iced Earl Grey lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Iced Earl Grey tea sweetened with lemon zest syrup

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add the sparkling water at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Earl Grey tea bags
  • 800ml boiling water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 lemons
  • 400ml sparkling water
  • 4 cup ice
  • mint leaves, to serve
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water, then remove and cool.
  2. Peel the zest from 2 of the lemons, simmer it with the sugar and water, then cool and strain.
  3. Juice 3 of the lemons, including the 2 peeled ones, and stir the syrup and lemon juice into the tea.
  4. Pour over ice, top with sparkling water and add wheels from the last lemon and mint.

When to serve
