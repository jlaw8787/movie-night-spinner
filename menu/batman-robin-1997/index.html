Snacks

Iced shrimp

Cooked shrimp on crushed ice with a mayo dip.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Mix the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; pile the prawns on the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked prawns
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 dash hot sauce
  1. Pile crushed ice onto a platter and arrange the prawns on top.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with tomato sauce, lemon and hot sauce.
  3. Serve in a small bowl in the middle. Everybody chill, 5 min.

Poison Ivy garden salad cupsVegetarian

Cos cups with pea shoots and feta.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Whisk the lemon juice, honey and olive oil dressing up to 2 days ahead and separate the cos cups up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; toss and fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 baby cos lettuce
  • 1 punnet pea shoots
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g feta
  1. Separate the cos into small cups.
  2. Toss the pea shoots, cucumber and mint with lemon juice, honey and olive oil.
  3. Spoon into the cups and crumble feta over the top.

Diamond auction crab tartlets

Crisp pastry cases of crab, lemon mayo and chives, avocado and roe, for the botanical gala.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 250g crab meat
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 avocado
  • 50g salmon roe
  1. Crisp little pastry cases filled with crab, lemon mayo and chives, topped with avocado and a sparkle of salmon roe. Cut 24 rounds from 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry, press into mini muffin tins, prick and bake at 190°C for 12 minutes until golden, then cool. Mix 250g drained crab meat with 3 tbsp mayo, the zest and a squeeze of 1 lemon, 2 tbsp chopped chives and salt. Dice 1 avocado with lemon juice. Fill each case with crab, a little avocado and a few salmon roe.

Wayne Manor chicken noodle soup

Chicken, carrot and noodle soup, Alfred's cure, no pizzas required.

Prep 17 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery sticks, sliced
  • 1 brown onion, halved
  • 2 L water
  • 150g egg noodles
  • 3 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 lemon
  1. Put the chicken, carrots, celery and onion in a pot with the water.
  2. Simmer until the chicken is cooked, 40 min.
  3. Shred the chicken back into the pot.
  4. Add the noodles and cook, 5 min.
  5. Finish with parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

Gotham thaw chipotle beef chilli

Chipotle beef and bean chilli, for thawing out after Freeze's Observatory gun.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 can chipotles in adobo
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 cans kidney beans
  • 200g cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 80g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar
  1. Beef chuck slow cooked with kidney beans, chipotle, cumin and tomato until thick and smoky, with sour cream, cheddar and warm cornbread. Brown 1kg diced beef chuck in batches. Soften 2 onions and 3 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 chopped chipotles in adobo, 2 cans crushed tomatoes, 500ml beef stock and the beef. Cover and simmer 2.5 hours, adding 2 drained cans kidney beans for the last 30 minutes. Mix 200g cornmeal, 150g plain flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 2 eggs, 300ml milk and 80g melted butter and bake at 200°C for 22 minutes.

Frozen Gotham ice cream cakeVegetarian

Vanilla and mint ice cream cake on a chocolate base. Allow me to break the ice.

Prep 28 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 250g chocolate biscuits
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 1 L mint ice cream
  • 4 meringue nests, crushed
  1. Crush the biscuits with the butter and press into a tin.
  2. Soften the ice creams and layer over the base.
  3. Freeze overnight.
  4. Top with crushed meringue like fresh snow just before slicing.

Drinks

Blue lemonade slushieNo alcohol

Lemonade ice cubes and blue pea tea in a slush.

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min, then 13 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the lemonade cubes overnight, up to 1 week ahead; steep and chill the blue tea up to 1 day ahead and blend to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 750ml lemonade
  • 2 cups ice
  • 4 lime wedges
  1. Steep the pea flowers in the boiling water, strain, stir in the sugar and chill, 10 min.
  2. Pour the lemonade into ice cube trays.
  3. Freeze the lemonade cubes overnight.
  4. Blend the lemonade cubes with the blue tea and ice until slushy and serve with lime wedges.
