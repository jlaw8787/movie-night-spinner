Cheeseburgers, lotto and television sliders

Thin smashed cheeseburger sliders. People wanna eat cheeseburgers, lotto and television.

Prep 6 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to a day ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 8 soft slider buns
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  1. Shape the mince into 8 thin patties a little wider than the buns and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear in a very hot pan for 2 minutes, flip, add the cheese and cook a minute more.
  3. Toast the buns, then build with mustard, tomato sauce, onion, pickle and a patty.
  4. Serve on a plate in front of the television.

Crunchy beef tacos with hot sauce

Crunchy beef tacos with hot sauce. Mills wasn't guarding the Taco Bell.

Prep 14 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Cook the spiced mince up to a day ahead and reheat; fill the shells to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 12 hard taco shells
  • 2 cups iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  1. Brown the mince with the onion, breaking it up.
  2. Stir in the spices, oregano, tomato paste and half a cup of water and simmer until thick.
  3. Warm the shells in a 180°C oven for 5 minutes.
  4. Fill with the mince, lettuce, cheese and tomato and shake on the hot sauce.

Monday's spaghetti and meatballs in red sauce

Beef and pork meatballs in red sauce over spaghetti. Monday: gluttony. Pace yourself.

Prep 22 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; boil the spaghetti to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 50g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 40g parmesan, grated, plus extra to serve
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 x 400g tins crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 cup basil leaves
  • 400g spaghetti
  1. Mix the beef, pork, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan and a clove of garlic, season and roll into 20 small balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil, then lift out.
  3. Soften the onion and the rest of the garlic in the same pan, add the tomatoes and oregano and bring to a simmer.
  4. Return the meatballs and simmer gently, half covered, until the sauce is thick.
  5. Boil the spaghetti, toss with some sauce and basil, and serve with the meatballs and extra parmesan.

Tracy's Wednesday night roast chicken

Roast chicken for Wednesday night, while the subway rattles the flat.

Prep 17 min, cook 83 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the butter up to a day ahead; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 375ml chicken stock
  1. Mix the butter, garlic, lemon zest and thyme leaves and push it under the chicken skin, then put the halved lemon inside.
  2. Roast the chicken at 200°C with the oiled potatoes around it, turning the potatoes once, until the juices run clear.
  3. Rest the chicken, then stir the flour into the pan juices on the stove and whisk in the stock until it bubbles into gravy.
  4. Carve and serve with the potatoes and gravy.

Handle-with-care dark chocolate boxVegetarian

Dark chocolate truffles in a plain box.

Prep 28 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the truffles up to 5 days ahead and keep them in the fridge in a lidded box
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 20g butter
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 small cardboard cake box
  1. Bring the cream to a simmer, pour it over the chocolate and butter, wait a minute, then stir until smooth.
  2. Chill until firm enough to scoop.
  3. Roll teaspoons of the mix into balls, toss half in cocoa and top the rest with a few salt flakes.
  4. Pack into the box, write FRAGILE on the lid and hand it over at 7pm.
