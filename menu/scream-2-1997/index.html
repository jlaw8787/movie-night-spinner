Stab premiere caramel cornVegetarian

Popped kernels and salted peanuts in butter, brown sugar and golden syrup, baked glassy.

Prep 0 min, cook 84 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days airtight once cooled; keep it out of the fridge so it stays crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 100g salted peanuts
  • 100g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 60ml golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  1. Heat the oil in a big covered pot, add the kernels and shake constantly until the popping slows. Tip into a large roasting tin with the peanuts.
  2. Heat the oven to 120°C. Melt the butter with the brown sugar, golden syrup and salt, then boil without stirring.
  3. Take off the heat, stir in the bicarb soda, pour over the popcorn and toss well.
  4. Bake, stirring every 15 minutes.
  5. Spread on baking paper to cool, then break into clusters.

Cici's sorority house dipVegetarian

Spinach, artichoke, cream cheese, parmesan and mozzarella baked bubbling, pita chips, quiet night in.

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Mix the dip up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; bake it and the pita chips on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt, a pinch
  • 250g frozen spinach, thawed
  • 280g jar marinated artichoke hearts, drained
  • 250g cream cheese, soft
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 100g mozzarella, grated
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the pita breads into wedges, toss with the olive oil and salt, and spread on a tray.
  2. Bake the pita wedges until crisp.
  3. Squeeze the spinach dry and chop the artichoke hearts.
  4. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, garlic, parmesan and half the mozzarella, then stir in the spinach, artichokes and black pepper.
  5. Spread in a small baking dish, top with the rest of the mozzarella and bake until bubbling and golden.
  6. Rest, then serve with the pita chips.

Windsor College Cincinnati chilli

Cinnamon and clove beef chilli over spaghetti with cheddar, Sidney's new college town in Ohio.

Prep 15 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead; chilling it overnight lets you lift the fat off the top. Cook the spaghetti and grate the cheese on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef mince
  • 1 L cold water
  • 3 onions, diced, one kept raw for the top
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 140g tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 200g cheddar, very finely grated
  • 100g small salted crackers
  1. Put the mince in a large pot with the cold water and break it up with a whisk until fine.
  2. Add two of the onions, the garlic, tomato paste, chilli powder, cinnamon, cumin, allspice, cloves, cocoa, Worcestershire, vinegar, bay leaves and salt, and bring to the boil.
  3. Simmer uncovered, stirring now and then, until thick but still saucy.
  4. Skim off the fat and take out the bay leaves.
  5. Meanwhile cook the spaghetti, warm the kidney beans and finely dice the last onion.
  6. Divide the spaghetti between four plates, ladle over the chilli and top with the beans, raw onion and a mound of cheddar. Serve with the crackers.

Opening night chicken souvlaki

Lemon and oregano chicken skewers with pita and tzatziki, for Sidney's Greek tragedy opening night.

Prep 32 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken and make the tzatziki up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 150g feta
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Cut the chicken into chunks and toss with the juice of 1 lemon, the oregano, 2 crushed garlic cloves, half the oil and salt. Thread onto skewers.
  2. Grate half the cucumber, squeeze it out and mix with the yoghurt, the last garlic clove and salt.
  3. Chop the tomatoes, the rest of the cucumber and the onion, top with feta and dress with the rest of the oil and lemon.
  4. Grill the skewers on a hot grill for 10 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through.
  5. Warm the pita and serve with the skewers, tzatziki and salad.

Windsor College buckeyesVegetarian

Peanut butter balls half dipped in chocolate, Ohio's favourite sweet.

Prep 38 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week airtight in the fridge; roll, freeze and dip them up to 2 days ahead and serve straight from the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250g smooth peanut butter
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • salt, a pinch
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 250g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp coconut oil
  1. Beat the peanut butter, butter, vanilla and salt, then work in the icing sugar.
  2. Roll into 2.5cm balls on a lined tray.
  3. Freeze until firm.
  4. Melt the dark chocolate with the coconut oil over simmering water.
  5. Spear each ball with a toothpick and dip in the chocolate, leaving a circle of peanut butter showing on top. Smooth over the hole with a finger.
  6. Chill until set.

Drinks

Sorority party cherry bourbon smash

Bourbon shaken with muddled fresh fruit, mint, syrup and lemon, crushed ice, for one normal night.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; muddle and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 200g pitted cherries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 80ml lemon juice, from 2 lemons
  • 4 cups crushed ice
  1. Stir the sugar into the hot water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Muddle the cherries with a handful of mint leaves and the syrup in a shaker.
  3. Add the bourbon, lemon juice and a scoop of ice and shake hard, in two batches.
  4. Strain over crushed ice in four tumblers and top each with a cherry and a mint sprig.
