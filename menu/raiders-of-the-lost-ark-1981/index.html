Not-bad dates

Medjool dates stuffed with almonds, wrapped in bacon and drizzled with honey. Good ones.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Stuff and wrap the dates up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake and drizzle with honey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 medjool dates
  • 100g goat's cheese
  • 12 almonds, toasted
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Split the dates and press an almond into each.
  2. Wrap each in half a rasher of streaky bacon.
  3. Bake at 200°C for 12 minutes until the bacon crisps, then drizzle with honey.

Chickpea fritters with lemon tahiniVegetarian

Crisp fritters of raw chickpeas and herbs with lemon tahini sauce, by the stove.

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day ahead, soak chickpeas overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dried chickpeas
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 bunch parsley
  • 1/2 bunch coriander
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • salt, to taste
  1. Soak the chickpeas in cold water overnight, then drain well.
  2. Blitz the raw chickpeas with the onion, garlic, parsley, coriander, cumin and salt.
  3. Shape into balls and flatten slightly.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C until deep brown, 10 min.
  5. Thin the tahini with lemon juice and water for the sauce.

Golden idol gruyère cheese puffs

Gruyère and cayenne choux baked hollow, tomato dip, gold as the idol.

How it is made
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 80g gruyère
  • cayenne pepper
  • tomato chutney
  • smoked paprika
  1. Golden choux puffs loaded with gruyère and cayenne, baked until hollow and crisp, served warm in a hessian sack with a smoky tomato dip. Boil 125ml water with 50g butter, beat in 75g plain flour and stir over the heat for 1 minute. Cool slightly, beat in 2 eggs one at a time, then 80g grated gruyère and a pinch of cayenne. Pipe 20 small rounds, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve warm with tomato chutney mixed with smoked paprika.

Dal bhat with tomato acharVegetarian

Yellow lentil dal over steamed rice with potato curry and a sharp tomato achar.

Prep 3 min, cook 1 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Make the dal and achar a day ahead and keep chilled; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g red lentils
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 piece ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 500g potatoes, cubed
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Rinse the lentils and simmer with the turmeric and 1 L water until soft and thick, then season, 25 min.
  2. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  3. Fry the cumin seeds, onion, garlic and ginger in the oil, add the potatoes, garam masala and a splash of water and cook until tender, 20 min.
  4. Char the tomatoes and chilli in a dry pan, then pound with salt, the lemon juice and coriander for the achar, 10 min.
  5. Spoon the dal over the rice with the potatoes and achar on the side.

The Raven bar pork momo

Pork, coriander and ginger dumplings with a fiery tomato chilli achar, Nepal bar food.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • soy sauce
  • round dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 red chillies
  • ground cumin
  • 1 lemon
  1. Juicy pork and coriander dumplings with ginger and spring onion, steamed until tender, with a fiery charred tomato and chilli achar. Mix 500g pork mince with 2 tsp grated ginger, 2 garlic cloves, 3 spring onions, a handful of chopped coriander, 1 tbsp soy and salt. Fill 30 round dumpling wrappers, gather and pleat closed. Steam on oiled baking paper for 10 minutes. Char 3 tomatoes and 2 red chillies in a dry pan, then blitz with a garlic clove, 1 tsp cumin, lemon juice and salt for the achar.

Semolina coconut cakeVegetarian

Semolina, coconut and yoghurt cake with an almond on each square, soaked in syrup.

Prep 7 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days, covered
Recipe for 4
  • 200g fine semolina
  • 80g desiccated coconut
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 16 almonds
  • 150ml water
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp rosewater
  1. Mix the semolina, coconut, yoghurt, half the sugar and baking powder.
  2. Spread in a tin, score into squares and press an almond into each.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 30 minutes.
  4. Simmer the remaining sugar with the water and lemon juice for 5 minutes, add the rosewater and pour the hot syrup over.

The Raven bar masala chaiNo alcohol

Black tea simmered with milk, ginger, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves.

Prep 2 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer the spiced water up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then add the tea bags and milk on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 1 piece ginger, 4cm, sliced
  • 6 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml milk
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Simmer the water with the ginger, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tea bags and milk and simmer 3 minutes more.
  3. Stir in the sugar and strain into four mugs.
