Snacks

Golden idol gruyère cheese puffs

Gruyère and cayenne choux baked hollow, tomato dip, for the run to the seaplane.

How it is made
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 80g gruyère
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  1. Golden choux puffs loaded with gruyère and cayenne, baked until hollow and crisp, served warm in a hessian sack with a smoky tomato dip. Boil 125ml water with 50g butter, beat in 75g plain flour and stir over the heat for 1 minute. Cool slightly, beat in 2 eggs one at a time, then 80g grated gruyère and a pinch of cayenne. Pipe 20 small rounds, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve warm with tomato chutney mixed with smoked paprika.

The Raven bar pork momo

Pork, coriander and ginger dumplings with a fiery tomato chilli achar, Nepal bar food.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 packet round dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 lemon
  1. Juicy pork and coriander dumplings with ginger and spring onion, steamed until tender, with a fiery charred tomato and chilli achar. Mix 500g pork mince with 2 tsp grated ginger, 2 garlic cloves, 3 spring onions, a handful of chopped coriander, 1 tbsp soy and salt. Fill 30 round dumpling wrappers, gather and pleat closed. Steam on oiled baking paper for 10 minutes. Char 3 tomatoes and 2 red chillies in a dry pan, then blitz with a garlic clove, 1 tsp cumin, lemon juice and salt for the achar.

The Raven bar masala chaiNo alcohol

Black tea simmered with milk, ginger, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves.

Prep 2 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer the spiced water up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then add the tea bags and milk on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 1 piece ginger, 4cm, sliced
  • 6 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml milk
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Simmer the water with the ginger, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tea bags and milk and simmer 3 minutes more.
  3. Stir in the sugar and strain into four mugs.
