Kelly's-style roast beef three-way rolls

Thin roast beef, barbecue sauce, mayo and cheese on soft rolls, the Kelly's three-way order.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Slice the beef and mix the sauce up to a day ahead; warm and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g rare roast beef, thinly sliced
  • 8 soft roll rolls
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 tbsp smoky barbecue sauce
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 125ml beef stock
  1. Warm the beef stock in a wide pan, add the sliced beef and heat through for 2 minutes without boiling.
  2. Split and lightly toast the rolls, then lay a slice of cheese on each base so it softens.
  3. Pile on the beef, then spoon over the barbecue sauce and a little mayo, Kelly's three-way style.
  4. Put the lids on and serve hot.

Southie clam chowder cups

Clams, potato, bacon and cream, with crackers.

Prep 13 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make up to a day ahead without the clams; reheat gently and stir the clams through to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 rashers smoky bacon, chopped
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 2 x 290g tins baby clams, juice kept
  • 250ml milk
  • 250ml pouring cream
  • 2 thyme sprigs
  • 1 cup small salted crackers
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp, then add the onion and celery and cook until soft.
  2. Stir in the flour for a minute, then whisk in the clam juice, milk and cream.
  3. Add the potatoes and thyme and simmer until the potatoes are tender.
  4. Stir in the clams, warm through and season with pepper.
  5. Ladle into cups and serve with the crackers.

Game Six Fenway franks

Grilled franks in soft buns with mustard, relish and slow-cooked onions.

Prep 3 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  1. Cook the onions slowly in the butter with a pinch of salt until soft and golden.
  2. Grill or pan-fry the franks, turning, until blistered and hot through.
  3. Butter the flat sides of the buns and toast them in the pan until golden.
  4. Tuck the franks in and top with onions, mustard and relish.

Southie corned beef and cabbage

Buttered cabbage, spuds and mustard cream. Sunday dinner in Southie.

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Simmer the beef up to 2 days ahead and chill it in its liquid; reheat in the liquid and cook the vegetables on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg corned silverside
  • 1 brown onion, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 600g small potatoes
  • 3 carrots, cut in chunks
  • 1/2 green cabbage, cut in wedges
  • 40g butter
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Rinse the silverside, cover with cold water and add the onion, bay leaves, sugar and vinegar.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook, barely bubbling, until a skewer slides in easily.
  3. Lift the beef out to rest and cook the potatoes and carrots in the liquid.
  4. Add the cabbage wedges for the last minutes, then drain and toss with the butter.
  5. Warm the cream with the mustard, slice the beef thickly and serve with the vegetables and mustard cream.

Caramel apple crumble with vanilla custardVegetarian

Caramel apple crumble with vanilla custard, made with a bushel of them apples.

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the crumble topping up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 5 Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 60g butter, plus 100g cold butter for the crumble
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 150g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 500ml vanilla custard
  1. Melt the brown sugar and 60g butter until bubbling, stir in the cream and salt, then toss the apples through.
  2. Rub the cold butter into the flour, oats, caster sugar and cinnamon until it clumps.
  3. Tip the apples into a baking dish, scatter over the crumble and bake at 180°C until golden and bubbling.
  4. Warm the custard and serve it over the crumble.

Southie black and tan

Pale ale with Irish stout on top.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the beer well; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 330ml bottles pale ale
  • 2 x 440ml cans Irish stout
  1. Half fill 4 chilled pint glasses with pale ale, tilting the glass as you pour.
  2. Pour the stout slowly over the back of a spoon so it sits on top of the ale.
