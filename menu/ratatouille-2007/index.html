Snacks

The soup

Leek, potato and cream. Remy fixed it.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  • white pepper, to taste
  1. Sweat the leeks in the butter over low heat until soft.
  2. Add the potatoes and stock and simmer until the potato is very tender.
  3. Blend silky smooth with the cream and season.
  4. Serve topped with chives and white pepper. Anyone can cook.

Mushroom and cheeseVegetarian

Mushroom and gruyère, by lightning.

Prep 17 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 Swiss brown mushrooms
  • 120g gruyère, grated
  • 1 small bunch thyme, leaves picked
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Remove the mushroom stems.
  2. Mix the gruyère with the thyme and garlic and fill the caps.
  3. Drizzle with oil and roast at 220°C until bubbling.
  4. Rest a few minutes before serving.

Bread, with a proper crust

Warm baguette. Hear it crackle.

Prep 10 min, cook 6 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 baguettes
  • 100g salted butter, at room temperature
  • 200g sliced leg ham
  • 200g ripe brie
  1. Take the salted butter out of the fridge so it is soft at room temperature.
  2. Warm the baguettes in a 200°C oven for 6 minutes until the crust crackles when you squeeze it.
  3. Split each baguette lengthways and spread thickly with the butter.
  4. Fill with folds of the ham and slices of brie, close and cut into short lengths to share.

Mains

Ratatouille, layered fineVegetarian

Zucchini, eggplant, squash, tomato. Takes Ego home.

Prep 20 min, cook 2.5 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 5 tomatoes
  • 2 zucchinis
  • 1 eggplant
  • 2 yellow squashes
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Cook the capsicum, onion, garlic and 2 chopped tomatoes into a soft sauce and spread it in a baking dish.
  2. Slice the zucchini, eggplant, squash and remaining tomatoes thin and layer them in a spiral over the sauce.
  3. Drizzle with thyme infused oil and cover with baking paper.
  4. Bake covered at 140°C until completely tender.
  5. Rest briefly before serving.

Sweetbreads à la Gusteau

Veal sweetbreads in butter and lemon. The recipe Remy rewrote.

Prep 0 min, cook 33 min, then 3 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 600g veal sweetbreads
  • salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 50g butter
  • 180ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  1. Soak the veal sweetbreads in cold water in the fridge for 2 hours.
  2. Simmer the sweetbreads in salted water for 4 minutes, then plunge into iced water and peel off the membrane.
  3. Press the sweetbreads under a weighted tray in the fridge for an hour.
  4. Slice into 2cm pieces, season with salt, dust with the flour and pan-fry in 30g of the butter for 3 minutes a side until golden.
  5. Deglaze the pan with the chicken stock and lemon juice, reduce by half, then swirl in the remaining 20g cold butter and spoon the sauce over the sweetbreads.

Sweets

Colette's crème brûléeVegetarian

Custard, burnt sugar. Colette's lesson.

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min, then 3 hours to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 100g caster sugar, plus extra for the tops
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C. Split the vanilla bean, scrape the seeds into the cream and warm it with the pod until it just steams.
  2. Whisk the yolks and 100g caster sugar until pale, then pour in the warm cream slowly, whisking, and strain into a jug.
  3. Pour into 4 ramekins in a baking dish, fill the dish with hot water halfway up the ramekins and bake until set with a slight wobble.
  4. Cool, then chill at least 3 hours.
  5. Sprinkle each top with a thin layer of caster sugar and torch, or grill close to the element, until it cracks like glass.

Drinks

A good grape fizzNo alcohol

Grape juice, berries and soda. Ego's order.

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 100g blackberries
  • 4 thyme sprigs, plus extra to serve
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 600ml red grape juice
  • 1 lemon, half juiced
  • 330ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Lightly crush the blackberries with the thyme and honey in a jug.
  2. Add the grape juice and lemon juice and stir.
  3. Chill for 30 minutes, then strain.
  4. Pour into stemmed glasses over ice, top with sparkling water and garnish with thyme and a blackberry.

When to serve
