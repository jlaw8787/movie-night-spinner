Ivana's chessboard blini with smoked salmon

Buckwheat blini with salmon, sour cream and dill, Ivana's way to keep warm in Russia.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the blini up to a day ahead and keep them wrapped; warm and top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g buckwheat flour
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 200g sliced smoked salmon
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/2 cup dill sprigs
  1. Whisk the flours, baking powder and a pinch of salt with the egg, milk and half the butter into a thick batter.
  2. Brush a frying pan with butter and drop in tablespoons of batter; cook until bubbles show, flip and cook another minute.
  3. Season the sour cream with lemon zest and a squeeze of juice.
  4. Top half the blini with salmon and half with just sour cream and dill, and set them out in a chequerboard.

Mini-Me shrimp cocktails

Prawns, iceberg and Marie Rose in tiny glasses. Mini-sized, one-eighth the size.

Prep 16 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce and shred the lettuce up to a day ahead; build the glasses to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked king prawns, peeled
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Tabasco
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 avocado, diced
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, Tabasco and a squeeze of lemon into a Marie Rose sauce.
  2. Spoon lettuce and a little avocado into 8 small glasses.
  3. Pile on the prawns, hang one over each rim and spoon over the sauce.
  4. Dust with paprika and serve with lemon wedges.

Fat Bastard's bedside fried chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken with pickles, enough to share with Fat Bastard.

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the buttermilk up to a day ahead; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 cup dill pickles
  1. Soak the drumsticks in the buttermilk with a big pinch of salt for at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
  2. Mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt, then roll the drumsticks in it, pressing to coat.
  3. Fry in oil at 160°C, in batches, turning, until deep gold and cooked through.
  4. Warm the honey with the chilli, drizzle over and serve with pickles.

Get in my belly sticky baby back ribs

Slow-baked pork ribs, smoky glaze, slaw.

Prep 15 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Bake the ribs in foil up to 2 days ahead and chill; glaze and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork baby back ribs, in racks
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Rub the racks all over with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and 2 tsp salt.
  2. Wrap each rack tightly in foil and bake at 150°C until the meat pulls back from the bones.
  3. Simmer the barbecue sauce, half the vinegar and the honey until glossy.
  4. Unwrap, brush with glaze and grill under high heat, brushing twice more, until sticky and charred.
  5. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayo and remaining vinegar, and serve with the ribs.

The Alan Parsons Project steak Diane

Sirloin flamed with brandy, creamy pan sauce.

Prep 7 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Make the pan sauce base up to a day ahead; sear the steaks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 220g sirloin steaks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 40g butter
  • 2 French shallots, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 60ml brandy
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 600g thin-cut chips
  1. Bake the chips in a hot oven until crisp.
  2. Season the steaks and sear in the oil over high heat, 3 minutes a side for medium rare, then rest.
  3. Melt the butter and soften the shallots and garlic.
  4. Pour in the brandy, carefully set it alight and let the flames die down.
  5. Stir in the mustard, Worcestershire, cream and resting juices and bubble until glossy.
  6. Spoon the sauce over the steaks, scatter with parsley and serve with the chips.

Belgian Dip waffles with coffee creamVegetarian

Liege waffles to dunk in sweet coffee cream. The Belgian Dip, milk mustache and all.

Prep 80 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the coffee cream up to 2 days ahead; make the waffles on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 7g sachet dried yeast
  • 120ml warm milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g soft butter
  • 100g pearl sugar or crushed sugar cubes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 2 tsp instant coffee
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, milk and eggs into a dough, then knead in the butter until smooth.
  2. Cover and leave in a warm spot until puffy.
  3. Knead in the pearl sugar and cut into 8 balls.
  4. Cook in a hot waffle iron until deep gold and caramelised.
  5. Whip the cream with the icing sugar and coffee dissolved in a little hot water, and serve for dunking.

Drinks

Austin's mojo

White rum, lime, mint and soda, in a tall glass.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lime and pick the mint up to 4 hours ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml white rum
  • 4 limes
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 8 tsp caster sugar
  • 600ml soda water
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  1. Muddle the mint, sugar and juice of a lime gently in each of 4 tall glasses.
  2. Add the rum and fill with crushed ice.
  3. Top with soda, stir up from the bottom and garnish with mint.
