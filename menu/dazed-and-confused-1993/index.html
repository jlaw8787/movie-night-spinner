Snacks

Keg party chipsVegetarian

Thick-cut potato chips with a cold onion and chive dip.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Onion dip up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 1 big bag thick-cut potato chips
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cook the onions slowly in the oil until deep brown, 40 min.
  2. Cool and chop, 3 min.
  3. Fold into the sour cream with salt and chopped chives.
  4. Serve with the chips.

Freshman paddle pretzelsVegetarian

Pretzel rods dipped in peanut butter, then milk chocolate.

Prep 3 min, cook 10 min, 17 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 150g milk chocolate
  1. Warm the peanut butter with the honey, 3 min.
  2. Dip the pretzels halfway and chill, 17 min.
  3. Melt the chocolate and dip the pretzels again, 6 min.
  4. Set on baking paper, 4 min.

Emporium chilli butter beer nuts

Salted peanuts and pretzels roasted with spiced butter, chilli and brown sugar.

How it is made
  • 50g butter
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 250g salted roasted peanuts
  • 150g pretzel twists
  1. Salted peanuts and pretzel twists roasted in chilli butter, smoked paprika and a little brown sugar until glossy and crunchy, served warm in paper cups. Melt 50g butter with 1 tsp chilli flakes, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tbsp brown sugar and 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce. Toss with 250g salted roasted peanuts and 150g pretzel twists on a lined tray. Bake at 160°C for 15 minutes, stirring once halfway, until the coating is dry and glossy. Cool for 10 minutes so they crisp up, then tip into paper cups.

Emporium chicken fried steak

Breaded minute steak shallow fried, with peppery cream gravy.

Prep 13 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Pound the steaks up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; flour, fry and make the gravy just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 thin beef minute steaks
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 500ml vegetable oil
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • salt, to taste
  1. Pound the steaks thin and season the flour.
  2. Dip in seasoned flour, then egg, then flour again.
  3. Shallow fry in about 1cm oil until golden each side, 8 min.
  4. For the gravy, cook some flour in a little of the oil, whisk in the milk and simmer until thick. Season with lots of pepper, 10 min.

Texas salt and pepper brisket with pickles

Beef roasted low ten hours until it wobbles, sliced thick on white bread with barbecue sauce.

How it is made
  • 2.5kg beef brisket
  • 1 box coarse salt
  • 1 jar cracked black pepper
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 white onion
  • 250ml barbecue sauce
  1. Brisket rubbed with coarse salt and cracked pepper, roasted low for ten hours until it wobbles, sliced thick on white bread with pickles, onion and barbecue sauce. Rub a 2.5kg beef brisket with 2 tbsp coarse salt and 2 tbsp cracked black pepper and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Roast fat side up on a rack at 120°C for 9 to 10 hours until the thickest part reaches 95°C. Wrap in foil and baking paper and rest 1 hour. Slice thick against the grain and serve on soft white bread with sliced dill pickles, thin raw onion and 250ml smoky barbecue sauce.

Last day of '76 sweet tea popsiclesVegetarian

Black tea sweetened with sugar and lemon, frozen on sticks.

Prep 18 min, cook 5 min, 6 hr waiting · Make ahead: Freeze the blocks up to 1 week ahead and keep them frozen in the moulds until serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 750ml boiling water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water, then remove, 5 min.
  2. Stir in the sugar while hot and add the lemon juice. Cool, 15 min.
  3. Pour into ice block moulds and add sticks.
  4. Freeze until solid, 6 hr.

Drinks

Wooderson's red beer

Cold lager with lime, tomato juice and hot sauce, chilli salt rim.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the chilli salt for the rims up to 1 week ahead; build over ice and top with cold lager just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 4 limes
  • 120ml tomato juice
  • 12 dashes of hot sauce
  • 8 dashes of Worcestershire sauce
  • 1.32 L cold lager (4 x 330ml)
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Mix the salt with the chilli powder on a saucer. Rub the glass rims with lime and dip in the salt.
  2. Fill the glasses with ice. Add lime juice, tomato juice, hot sauce and Worcestershire to each.
  3. Top slowly with cold lager, stir once and serve with a lime wedge.
