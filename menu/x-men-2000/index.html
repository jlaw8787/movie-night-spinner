Snacks

Xavier's School cereal barsVegetarian

Oat, honey and peanut butter bars.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 80g honey
  • 100g peanut butter
  • 150g rolled oats
  • 80g puffed rice cereal
  1. Melt the butter, honey and peanut butter together.
  2. Stir through the oats and puffed rice until coated.
  3. Press firmly into a lined tin.
  4. Chill, then cut into bars.

Iceman frozen grapesVegetarian

Red and green grapes rolled in lime juice and sugar, frozen solid.

Prep 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 250g red grapes
  • 250g green grapes
  • 1 lime
  • 50g caster sugar
  1. Pick the grapes off the stem, wash and dry.
  2. Roll in the lime juice, then in the sugar.
  3. Spread on a tray and freeze until solid. Serve straight from the freezer.

Cage fight maple chilli peanuts

Nuts roasted glassy with maple syrup, smoked paprika and sea salt, warm in a tin bucket.

How it is made
  • 400g raw peanuts
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • flaky sea salt
  1. Peanuts roasted with maple syrup, chilli, smoked paprika and sea salt until glassy and crunchy, served warm in a tin bucket on the bar. Heat the oven to 170°C. Toss 400g raw peanuts with 3 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp chilli flakes, 1 tsp smoked paprika and 1 tsp salt. Spread on a lined tray and roast 15 to 18 minutes, stirring twice, until deep golden. Sprinkle with flaky salt and cool 10 minutes so the glaze hardens. Break up and serve in a bucket.

Mains

Cage fight poutine

Chips, curds and hot beef gravy.

Prep 13 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 2 days ahead in the fridge and reheat; cut the chips up to 4 hours ahead in cold water and fry them fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 250g cheese curds
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips.
  2. Fry in the oil at 140°C until soft and pale.
  3. Cook the butter and flour for 2 minutes, whisk in the stock and simmer until thick.
  4. Fry the chips again in the oil at 185°C until crisp.
  5. Pile chips into bowls, scatter the cheese curds and pour the hot gravy over.

Liberty Island summit roast chicken

Dry-brined bird, zesty herb butter under the skin, crunchy potatoes, garlicky beans, pan gravy.

How it is made
  • 1 whole chicken (1.8kg)
  • 80g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 garlic clove
  1. Dry-brined chicken with lemon and thyme butter under the skin, roasted golden, with crunchy roast potatoes, garlicky green beans and pan gravy. Salt a 1.8kg chicken and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Push 50g butter mashed with lemon zest and thyme under the breast skin. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then 180°C for 50 minutes, with 1kg halved potatoes in oil around it. Rest 10 minutes. Make gravy from the juices with 1 tbsp flour and 300ml stock. Blanch 300g green beans and toss with butter and 1 sliced garlic clove.

Sweets

Frozen ice creamVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream under a hard shell of set chocolate. Iceman's idea of pudding.

Prep 3 min, cook 3 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Put the four bowls in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; scoop the ice cream and melt the chocolate shell at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L very hard vanilla ice cream
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  1. Put four bowls in the freezer.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into the bowls.
  3. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil and pour over at the table so it sets into a shell in seconds.

Drinks

Logan's rye and maple old fashioned

Rye over one big ice cube, for the road out of Alberta with Rogue.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Stir the rye, maple syrup and bitters together up to 2 days ahead in a jar in the fridge; stir over ice and strain to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml Canadian rye whisky
  • 4 tsp maple syrup
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 orange
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 2 cups ice, for stirring
  1. Stir the rye, maple syrup and bitters over ice in a jug until very cold.
  2. Strain into four heavy tumblers over one large ice cube each.
  3. Squeeze a strip of orange peel over each glass, rub the rim and drop it in.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Logan wins the cage fight and is crowned King of the Cage.
