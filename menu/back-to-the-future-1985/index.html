Snacks

French friesVegetarian

Crisp golden fries from Lou's Cafe. 1955 prices, 1985 portions.

Prep 10 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Cut up to 4 hours ahead, keep in cold water then dry well
Recipe for 4
  • 500g potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for deep frying
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  1. Cut the potatoes into fries, rinse in cold water and dry thoroughly with a clean tea towel.
  2. Heat the oil in a sturdy deep pan to 140°C and blanch the fries in batches for 5 minutes without browning.
  3. Drain and cool the fries for 15 minutes (this rest is part of the cook time).
  4. Raise the oil to 185°C and fry in batches until crisp, drain, season with salt and serve in paper boats.

Plutonium green herb pretzel bites

Dusted bright green, safely.

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 60g bicarb soda
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 40g butter
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar cheddar cheese dip
  1. Soft pretzel bites dipped in a bicarb bath and baked deep brown, tossed in garlic butter and bright green parsley salt, with a cheddar dip. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead and prove 1 hour. Roll into ropes and cut into 3cm bites. Boil 2 litres water with 60g bicarb soda and dip the bites for 30 seconds. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Blitz a big handful of parsley with 1 tsp salt until bright green. Toss the bites in 40g melted butter with 1 crushed garlic clove, then the green salt.

Mains

Cheeseburger

Griddled onions, pickles and a slice of American.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 80g American-style cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 60g dill pickles, sliced
  • 30g yellow mustard
  1. Divide the mince into four loose balls and slice the onion very thin.
  2. Heat a griddle until very hot. Put the beef balls on it, top with onion and press firmly into thin patties. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Cook 3 minutes, flip and cook a further 2 to 3 minutes until the centre reaches 75°C. Lay the cheese on top to melt.
  4. Toast the buns and fill with the patties, pickles and mustard.

Breakfast plate

Eggs, bacon and toast, ideally not cooked by a robot arm.

Prep 0 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; the bacon fat and fresh toast are the whole point, so nothing here holds well
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 eggs
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 40g butter
  1. Lay the bacon in a cold frying pan and cook over medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until crisp. Lift out onto a plate.
  2. Fry the eggs in the bacon fat, spooning the fat over the yolks until the whites are set.
  3. Meanwhile toast the white bread and spread with the butter.
  4. Plate the eggs with the bacon and buttered toast.

Sweets

Yellow cake, by the sliceVegetarian

Prep 14 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Bake the day before, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 350g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 20 x 30cm tin. Beat 200g of the butter with the caster sugar until pale.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time with the vanilla, then fold in the self-raising flour and 125ml of the milk.
  3. Spread into the tin and bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a skewer comes out clean. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining 150g butter with the icing sugar and the last 2 tbsp milk until fluffy and spread over the top.

Drinks

Milk, chocolateNo alcohol

Prep 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to a day ahead and keep in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp cocoa
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 900ml full cream milk
  • 4 straws
  1. Whisk the cocoa and caster sugar with 100ml hot water until smooth.
  2. Stir in the cold full cream milk and chill for an hour.
  3. Stir again and pour into four tall glasses with a straw each.

  2. Pour the coffee black into four mugs. No sugar.

Lou's Cafe chocolate malt

Vanilla ice cream blended thick with milk, chocolate syrup and malted milk, cream, two straws.

How it is made
  • 2 litres vanilla ice cream
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 1 bottle chocolate syrup
  • 1 jar malted milk powder
  • 1 can whipped cream
  1. Vanilla ice cream blended thick with cold milk, chocolate syrup and malted milk powder, poured into tall glasses with whipped cream and two straws. Add 12 scoops vanilla ice cream, 800ml cold milk, 8 tbsp chocolate syrup and 8 tbsp malted milk powder to a blender, working in two batches if needed. Blend 30 seconds until thick but pourable. Pour into 4 tall chilled glasses, top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of malted milk powder, and serve each with two straws.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Marty walks into Lou's Cafe in 1955 and asks for a sugar-free drink.
