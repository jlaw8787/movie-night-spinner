Snacks

Market stall fruit skewers with honey dipVegetarian

Strawberry, rockmelon, kiwi and grape skewers with honey yoghurt dip.

Prep 28 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Skewers up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 2 kiwi fruit
  • 250g grapes
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 30g shredded coconut
  1. Hull the strawberries, cube the rockmelon, peel and cube the kiwi fruit and pick the grapes.
  2. Thread onto skewers in rainbow order.
  3. Mix the yoghurt with honey and vanilla.
  4. Toast the coconut in a dry pan until golden.
  5. Serve the skewers with the yoghurt dip and the coconut for dipping and rolling.

Dried fruit heist crunch mixVegetarian

Baked honey oat clusters with almonds, seeds and dried fruit.

Prep 16 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 100g almonds
  • 50g pumpkin seeds
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 80g dried apricots
  • 60g dried apple
  • 60g dried cranberries
  1. Mix the oats, almonds and pumpkin seeds with honey, oil and cinnamon.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and press flat.
  3. Bake at 160°C, stirring once.
  4. Cool completely so it clusters.
  5. Break up and toss with chopped dried apricots, dried apple and dried cranberries.

Mains

Gloucester chicken, leek and bacon pie

Chicken, leek and bacon in a creamy sauce under flaky pastry.

Prep 24 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake the pastry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 rashers bacon
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 leeks
  • 2 carrots
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch of thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  1. Dice the bacon, chicken, leeks and carrots.
  2. Fry the bacon, then add the chicken and brown.
  3. Add the leeks and carrots and cook.
  4. Stir in the flour.
  5. Add the stock, cream and thyme and simmer.
  6. Pour into a pie dish, top with the pastry and brush with egg.
  7. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  8. Toss the peas in butter and serve alongside.

Thomas McGregor's Windermere lamb hotpot

Lamb and carrot hotpot under crisp sliced potatoes, back from the honeymoon in Windermere.

Prep 0 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 800g potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 400g frozen peas
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Toss the lamb in the flour and brown in a casserole dish.
  2. Add the onion, carrot, stock, Worcestershire and thyme and bring to a simmer.
  3. Lay the potato slices over the top, dot with half the butter, cover and bake.
  4. Uncover and bake until the potatoes are golden and crisp.
  5. Boil the peas and toss with the rest of the butter.

Peter's cream cheese carrot cakeVegetarian

Spiced carrot and walnut cake with thick cream cheese icing.

Prep 36 min, cook 45 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 180g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 180ml vegetable oil
  • 3 carrots
  • 80g walnuts
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 60g butter
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 bottle of orange food colouring
  1. Mix the flour, bicarb, cinnamon, brown sugar, eggs, oil, grated carrot and walnuts.
  2. Pour into a lined 20cm square tin.
  3. Bake at 170°C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with butter and icing sugar and spread on top.
  6. Tint a little icing orange to pipe small carrots and scatter with walnuts.

Drinks

Orchard apple and pear sparklerNo alcohol

Apple and pear juice with lemon and ginger, topped with soda.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Juice mix up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml cloudy apple juice
  • 300ml pear juice
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 apple
  • 400ml soda water
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. In a jug, mix the apple juice, pear juice, lemon juice and grated ginger.
  2. Chill for 20 minutes.
  3. Fill glasses with ice and thin apple slices, pour in the juice to two-thirds and top up with soda water.
  4. Stir and serve.
