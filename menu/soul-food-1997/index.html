Snacks

Ahmad's Sunday devilled eggsVegetarian

Mustard, relish, paprika. Ahmad promised the hidden money if everyone came.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp sweet relish
  • 1 dash hot sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool and peel.
  2. Halve and whip the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, relish and hot sauce.
  3. Pipe back and dust with paprika.

Big Mama's ham hock braised greens

Kale or collards braised for hours with smoked ham hock and garlic.

Prep 20 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 1.5 L water
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 2 bunches kale
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  1. Simmer the hock in water with the sliced onion, garlic and chilli flakes.
  2. Strip the stems from the kale and chop. Add and braise gently.
  3. Pick the meat off the hock and stir it back in.
  4. Finish with the vinegar and serve in bowls with plenty of pot liquor.

Big Mama's pan cornbread, honey butter

Golden buttermilk batter poured into smoking hot cast iron so the edges crisp, cut in wedges.

How it is made
  • 160g butter
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Golden buttermilk cornbread baked in a smoking hot cast iron pan so the edges crisp, cut in wedges with whipped honey butter. Put 20g butter in a 25cm cast iron pan and heat in a 220°C oven for 10 minutes. Mix 150g cornmeal, 100g plain flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tbsp sugar and half a tsp salt. Stir in 250ml buttermilk, 1 egg and 60g melted butter until just combined. Pour into the hot pan and bake 20 minutes. Beat 80g soft butter with 2 tbsp honey and a pinch of salt.

Mains

Sunday fried chicken

Buttermilk brined chicken, double dredged and fried in cast iron.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min, then 12.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brine overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Brine the chicken overnight in the buttermilk and hot sauce.
  2. Season the flour with paprika and salt and dredge the chicken twice.
  3. Fry in the oil in a cast iron pan at 165°C in batches until 74°C at the bone.
  4. Drain on a rack.

Big Mama's hidden stash mac and cheese

Macaroni set in egg and evaporated milk custard with three cheeses, sharp cheddar crust.

How it is made
  • 500g macaroni
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tin evaporated milk
  • 250ml milk
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar mustard powder
  • 400g sharp cheddar
  • 100g mozzarella
  1. Macaroni baked in a custard of eggs, evaporated milk and three cheeses until set, with a golden crust of sharp cheddar on top. Boil 500g macaroni for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain. Whisk 2 eggs with a 375ml tin of evaporated milk, 250ml milk, 60g melted butter, 1 tsp mustard powder, salt and pepper. Toss the pasta with 300g grated sharp cheddar and 100g grated mozzarella, then the custard. Tip into a buttered dish, top with 100g more cheddar and bake at 180°C for 40 minutes until set and golden.

Sweets

Peach cobblerVegetarian

Spiced peaches, biscuit top. For the Sunday Ahmad gets everyone back.

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Best warm, bake the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 peaches
  • 120g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Cook the sliced peaches with a third of the butter, the sugar, cinnamon and nutmeg.
  2. Tip into a dish.
  3. Rub the rest of the butter into the flour, add the milk and drop over.
  4. Bake at 190°C.
  5. Serve with ice cream.

Drinks

Sweet tea lemonadeNo alcohol

Sweet black tea mixed with fresh lemonade.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 1 pinch bicarb soda
  • 160g caster sugar
  • 4 lemons
  • 500ml cold water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water with the bicarb for 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in half the sugar while hot and cool.
  3. Stir the lemon juice with the rest of the sugar and cold water.
  4. Mix both in a big jug with ice, lemon wheels and mint, and pour.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Ahmad gets the family back to Big Mama's for one more Sunday dinner.
