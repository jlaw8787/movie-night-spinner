Snacks

Sausage and peppers

Grilled Italian sausage in bites, soft sweet peppers and onion, a splash of red wine vinegar.

Prep 3 min, cook 33 min
Recipe for 4
  • 6 Italian sausages
  • 2 capsicums, sliced
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 splash red wine vinegar
  1. Grill the sausages until cooked through and slice into chunks, 18 min.
  2. Fry the capsicums and onions in oil until soft and sweet, 15 min.
  3. Toss with the sausage and a splash of red wine vinegar.

Disco fries

Crisp-cut potatoes under hot beef gravy and melted mozzarella. For after the last dance.

Prep 0 min, cook 26 min, then 35 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 L neutral oil, for frying
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml hot beef stock
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips, soak for 30 minutes and dry well.
  2. Fry in the oil at 150°C for 6 minutes, then at 190°C until crisp.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for a minute, whisk in the stock and simmer for 5 minutes into gravy.
  4. Pile the chips on a tray, pour over the gravy, top with mozzarella and grill for a minute, 3 min.

Mains

Two slices, stackedVegetarian

Thin New York pie, tomato, oregano and mozzarella. Stack, fold, strut down 86th Street.

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough (2 balls)
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g low-moisture mozzarella, grated
  1. Rest the dough at room temperature for an hour.
  2. Blend the tomatoes with salt and oregano. Stretch each base thin, spread with sauce and scatter with mozzarella.
  3. Bake on a preheated tray at the oven's highest setting, 10 min.
  4. Cut into big slices, stack two and fold.

Sweets

Mirror ball bitesVegetarian

Butter cake rolled with cream cheese, dipped in white chocolate and silver dragées, on a stick.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min, then 40 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butter cake
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 300g white chocolate, melted
  • 50g silver cachous
  • 16 lollipop sticks
  1. Crumble the cake and mix with the cream cheese until it holds together. Roll into 16 balls.
  2. Chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Push a stick into each, dip in melted white chocolate and roll in cachous.
  4. Stand in a glass to set, 10 min.

Drinks

Disco fizz

Whisky, lemon and sugar, topped with lemon-lime soda.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 40ml hot water
  • 180ml blended whisky
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 240ml lemonade
  • 240ml soda water
  • 1 lemon, in wheels
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool, 5 min.
  2. Shake the whisky, lemon juice and 40ml of the syrup with ice for 10 seconds.
  3. Strain into four ice-filled highballs, top with lemonade and soda, stir once and add a lemon wheel.

When to serve
