Snacks

Pumpkin pastiesVegetarian

Shortcrust half moons filled with roast pumpkin, feta and sage

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pumpkin, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Toss the pumpkin in the oil and roast until soft.
  2. Mash with the feta, sage and nutmeg and cool.
  3. Cut the pastry into rounds, fill and fold into half moons, crimping the edges.
  4. Brush with egg and bake until golden.

Mains

Great Hall honey chicken and Yorkshires

Honey mustard glazed drumsticks with roast potatoes and Yorkshire puddings

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 30g butter
  • 1 cup gravy
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Mix the honey and mustard and coat the drumsticks.
  2. Toss the potatoes in the olive oil and roast with the chicken.
  3. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk into a batter and rest.
  4. Heat oil in muffin tins, pour in the batter and bake until puffed.
  5. Cook the peas with the butter and warm the gravy.
  6. Serve everything together.

Sweets

Hogwarts rock cakesVegetarian

Crumbly fruit cakes with mixed spice. “You're a wizard, Harry.”

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Same day, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 125g cold butter, cubed
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp mixed spice
  • 150g mixed dried fruit
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp milk
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a tray.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, sugar and spice.
  3. Stir in the dried fruit, then bind with the egg and milk.
  4. Drop in rough piles on the tray and bake until golden.

Treacle tartVegetarian

Golden syrup and lemon tart in sweet shortcrust

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 400g golden syrup
  • 100g fresh white breadcrumbs
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • clotted cream, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Line a tart tin with the pastry and chill briefly.
  2. Blind bake the case.
  3. Warm the golden syrup with the breadcrumbs, lemon zest and juice.
  4. Pour into the case and bake until just set.
  5. Cool slightly and serve each slice with clotted cream.

Pumpkin juiceVegetarianNo alcohol

Roast pumpkin blended with apple juice, honey and cinnamon, served chilled

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pumpkin, diced
  • 1l cloudy apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  1. Roast the pumpkin at 200C until soft and cool completely.
  2. Blend with the apple juice, honey and cinnamon until smooth.
  3. Strain through a sieve into a jug and chill well.

When to serve

Serve the feast when the plates fill up at the welcome feast in the Great Hall.
