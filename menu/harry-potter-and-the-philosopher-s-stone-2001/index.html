Snacks

Pumpkin pastiesVegetarian

Shortcrust half moons filled with roast pumpkin, feta and sage

Prep 12 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pumpkin, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Toss the pumpkin in the oil and roast until soft.
  2. Mash with the feta, sage and nutmeg and cool.
  3. Cut the pastry into rounds, fill and fold into half moons, crimping the edges.
  4. Brush with egg and bake until golden.

Trolley chocolate frogs with honeycombOn the printed menu

Milk chocolate moulded round crunchy golden syrup toffee, a Dumbledore card with each

How it is made
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 250g milk chocolate
  1. Milk chocolate frogs moulded around crunchy homemade honeycomb, each wrapped in a little paper card like the ones from the trolley. Line a tray with baking paper. Boil 150g caster sugar and 3 tbsp golden syrup to 150°C, then whisk in 2 tsp bicarb, pour onto the tray and leave to set for 30 minutes. Break into small crumbs. Melt 250g milk chocolate gently. Spoon a little into frog moulds, add a pinch of honeycomb, top with more chocolate and tap out the air. Chill 30 minutes, turn out and wrap each in paper.

Mains

Great Hall honey chicken and Yorkshires

Honey mustard glazed drumsticks with roast potatoes and Yorkshire puddings

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 30g butter
  • 1 cup gravy
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the honey and mustard and coat the drumsticks.
  2. Toss the potatoes in the olive oil and roast with the chicken.
  3. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk into a batter and rest.
  4. Heat oil in muffin tins, pour in the batter and bake until puffed.
  5. Cook the peas with the butter and warm the gravy.
  6. Serve everything together.

Hogwarts Christmas roast turkeyOn the printed menu

Herb-buttered breast, chipolatas in bacon and honey parsnips, the first one Harry ever enjoys

How it is made
  • 1.5kg turkey breast roll
  • 50g butter
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 12 chipolatas
  • 6 rashers streaky bacon
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g parsnips
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Herb-buttered turkey breast roasted golden with chipolatas in bacon, roast potatoes, honey parsnips, cranberry sauce and a rich gravy. Heat the oven to 180°C. Rub a 1.5kg turkey breast roll with 50g butter mashed with sage and thyme and roast 75 minutes to 70°C inside, then rest. Wrap 12 chipolatas in half rashers of streaky bacon and roast for the last 30 minutes. Roast 1kg parboiled potatoes and 500g parsnips tossed in honey and oil at 200°C for 45 minutes. Make gravy with the tray juices, 1 tbsp flour and 400ml chicken stock. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Sweets

Hogwarts rock cakesVegetarian

Crumbly fruit cakes with mixed spice. “You're a wizard, Harry.”

Prep 9 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Same day, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 125g cold butter, cubed
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp mixed spice
  • 150g mixed dried fruit
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp milk
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a tray.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, sugar and spice.
  3. Stir in the dried fruit, then bind with the egg and milk.
  4. Drop in rough piles on the tray and bake until golden.

Treacle tartVegetarian

Golden syrup and lemon tart in sweet shortcrust

Prep 10 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 400g golden syrup
  • 100g fresh white breadcrumbs
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • clotted cream, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Line a tart tin with the pastry and chill briefly.
  2. Blind bake the case.
  3. Warm the golden syrup with the breadcrumbs, lemon zest and juice.
  4. Pour into the case and bake until just set.
  5. Cool slightly and serve each slice with clotted cream.

Pumpkin juiceVegetarianNo alcohol

Roast pumpkin blended with apple juice, honey and cinnamon, served chilled

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pumpkin, diced
  • 1l cloudy apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  1. Roast the pumpkin at 200°C until soft and cool completely.
  2. Blend with the apple juice, honey and cinnamon until smooth.
  3. Strain through a sieve into a jug and chill well.

When to serve

Serve the feast when the plates fill up at the welcome feast in the Great Hall.
