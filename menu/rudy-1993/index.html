Joliet steel mill meatloaf sandwiches

Sliced meatloaf with cheddar, pickles and relish on toasted bread

Prep 20 min, cook 94 min · Make ahead: Meatloaf up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 1 jar of dill pickles
  • 1 jar of tomato relish
  1. Mix the mince, grated onion, egg, breadcrumbs, Worcestershire and salt.
  2. Press into a lined loaf tin and spread with tomato sauce mixed with brown sugar.
  3. Bake at 180C.
  4. Cool completely, then slice thickly.
  5. Toast the bread.
  6. Fill with the meatloaf, cheddar, pickles and tomato relish and cut in half.

Notre Dame tailgate beer brats

Bratwurst simmered in lager, grilled, with beer onions and mustard

Prep 8 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 8 bratwurst
  • 375ml lager
  • 2 onions
  • 20g butter
  • 8 bread rolls
  • 1 jar of wholegrain mustard
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Simmer the bratwurst in lager with the onions and butter.
  3. Lift out the brats and grill, turning, until charred.
  4. Keep boiling the onions until the liquid reduces and they turn sticky.
  5. Toast the rolls.
  6. Fill with a brat and a pile of beer onions and finish with wholegrain mustard.

Ruettiger family Sunday pot roast

Slow-cooked chuck roast with carrots, potatoes and horseradish sour cream

Prep 24 min, cook 200 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg chuck roast
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch of thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 carrots
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Season the chuck roast and quarter the onions, peel the carrots and halve the potatoes.
  2. Brown the roast all over in oil in a heavy pot.
  3. Add the onions, garlic, tomato paste, stock, Worcestershire, thyme and bay leaves.
  4. Cover and cook at 160C.
  5. Add the carrots and potatoes.
  6. Cook for another hour.
  7. Thicken the juices with cornflour.
  8. Mix the sour cream with horseradish cream.
  9. Serve the roast and vegetables with the sour cream mix and the crusty loaf, torn, to mop up the juices.

Holy Cross Friday fish fry

Beer-battered flathead, thick chips, tartare and slaw, while waiting on the acceptance letter

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare and slaw up to 4 hours ahead; batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 1kg frozen thick chips
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 2 gherkins
  • 1 lemon
  • 300g white cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  1. Bake the chips at 220C for 25 minutes.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped capers, gherkins and a squeeze of lemon for the tartare. Toss the shredded cabbage and grated carrot with 2 tbsp of it.
  3. Whisk 120g of the flour with the baking powder and lager until smooth.
  4. Heat the oil to 180C. Dust the fish in the rest of the flour, dip in the batter and fry in batches for 4 minutes until golden.
  5. Serve with the chips, tartare, slaw and lemon wedges.

Sweets

South Bend apple crumbleVegetarian

Cinnamon Granny Smith apples under a buttery crumble

Prep 27 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 Granny Smith apples
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 50g pecans
  • 600ml vanilla custard
  1. Peel and slice the apples and toss with sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice.
  2. Tip into a baking dish.
  3. Rub the cold butter into the flour, then stir in the oats, brown sugar and chopped pecans.
  4. Scatter over the apples.
  5. Bake at 180C until golden.
  6. Warm the custard and serve alongside.

Golden Dome hot spiced ciderVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot apple cider with maple, cinnamon, clove and orange

Prep 6 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l cloudy apple juice
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 1 star anise
  • 1 orange
  1. Pour the apple juice into a saucepan with maple syrup, cinnamon sticks, cloves, star anise and sliced orange.
  2. Heat gently without boiling.
  3. Strain into mugs and add a cinnamon stick and orange slice to each.
