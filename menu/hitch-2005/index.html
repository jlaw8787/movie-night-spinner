Ellis Island crostiniVegetarian

Grilled ciabatta rubbed with garlic, topped with diced tomato and basil.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Grill the ciabatta up to 1 day ahead, airtight; make the tomato topping up to 2 hours ahead and spoon on just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 ciabatta
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the ciabatta and grill until charred.
  2. Rub with the cut garlic clove.
  3. Dice the tomatoes and mix with torn basil, finely chopped red onion, oil and salt.
  4. Spoon on just before serving.

Albert's meatball sliders

Pork and veal meatballs on slider buns. Albert wins Allegra by being himself.

Prep 25 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Cook the meatballs in the passata up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat, then fill the buns and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork and veal mince
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 700ml passata
  • 12 slider buns
  • 12 slices provolone
  • 1 small bunch basil
  1. Mix the mince with the breadcrumbs, egg and parmesan and roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in oil.
  3. Simmer in the passata.
  4. Put each in a slider bun with provolone and basil and grill until the cheese melts.

Allergy-safe feta dip and baba ganoush

No scallops here. Death on a leaf!

How it is made
  • 2 eggplants
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  • 4 pita breads
  • olive oil
  1. Feta dip with lemon and chilli oil beside smoky baba ghanoush, scooped up with warm oven-baked pita chips. Not a prawn in sight. Heat the oven to 220°C. Prick 2 eggplants and roast 40 minutes until collapsed, then scoop the flesh and mash with 2 tbsp tahini, 1 crushed garlic clove, the juice of 1 lemon and salt. Blitz 200g feta with 100g Greek yoghurt and lemon zest until smooth, then drizzle with 1 tbsp chilli oil. Cut 4 pita breads into wedges, brush with olive oil and bake at 200°C for 8 minutes until crisp.

Mains

Chicken piccata

Pan seared chicken in lemon, caper and white wine sauce. Sara's exposé runs.

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Pound the chicken thin up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; flour, sear and make the pan sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 40g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 125ml white wine
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 60g butter
  • 200g angel hair pasta
  1. Pound the chicken thin, cut in half, then flour.
  2. Boil the pasta until tender and drain.
  3. Sear the chicken in oil until golden and cooked through.
  4. Add the wine, lemon juice and capers and reduce.
  5. Whisk in the butter. Toss the pasta through and serve the chicken on top.

Rib eye steak frites with béarnaise

Basted seared steak, tarragon shallot sauce of yolks and butter, double-fried chips and sea salt.

How it is made
  • 4 rib eye steaks (300g each)
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g butter
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Rib eye seared and basted with butter, rested, served with tarragon béarnaise and double-fried frites with sea salt. Cut 1kg potatoes into chips, soak 30 minutes, dry and fry at 140°C for 6 minutes. Reduce 60ml white wine vinegar with 1 chopped shallot and tarragon to 1 tbsp, then whisk with 3 egg yolks over simmering water and slowly beat in 180g melted butter for the béarnaise. Season 4 rib eye steaks and sear 3 minutes a side, basting with butter and thyme, then rest 8 minutes. Fry the chips at 190°C for 3 minutes and salt.

Sweets

Sara's chocolate-dipped strawberriesVegetarian

Large strawberries dipped in melted dark chocolate.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Dip and set up to 4 hours ahead, in a single layer in the fridge; drizzle the white chocolate and serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g large strawberries
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 30g pistachios, crushed
  • 50g white chocolate
  1. Wash and fully dry the strawberries, leaving the tops on.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate in a bowl over simmering water.
  3. Dip each strawberry two thirds of the way in, then lay on baking paper.
  4. Sprinkle with pistachios.
  5. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle in thin lines.
  6. Chill until set.

Drinks

Date doctor French 75

Gin, lemon and sugar syrup shaken, then topped with sparkling wine.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Chill the flutes and juice the lemon up to 1 day ahead; shake the gin mix and top with sparkling wine to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 1 tray ice
  • 360ml brut sparkling wine, cold
  • 1 lemon
  1. Chill the flutes.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and syrup with ice.
  3. Strain into the flutes and top with sparkling wine, pouring slowly.
  4. Twist a strip of lemon peel over each glass and drop it in.
