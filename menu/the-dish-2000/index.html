Snacks

Country sconesVegetarian

Plain buttery scones baked packed close so they rise tall.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cold butter
  • 350g self-raising flour
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 50ml thickened cream, for the dough
  • 250ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  1. Rub the butter into the flour.
  2. Mix in the milk and the cream for the dough lightly with a knife, pat out and cut rounds.
  3. Pack close on a tray and bake at 220°C until risen and golden.
  4. Whip the cream and serve the scones with jam and cream.

Cheese and yeast spread scrollsVegetarian

Scone dough rolled up with yeast spread and tasty cheese, sliced and baked.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp yeast spread
  • 150g tasty cheese
  1. Rub the butter into the flour and mix in the milk to a soft dough.
  2. Roll into a rectangle, spread thinly with yeast spread and cover with grated cheese.
  3. Roll up, slice into 12 and bake at 200°C until golden.

Mayor's roast lamb cutlets and mint sauce

Roast lamb racks carved into cutlets, roast potatoes and pumpkin, peas.

Prep 5 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Mix the mint sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; roast the lamb and vegetables on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 600g pumpkin
  • 2 lamb racks
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp hot water, for mint sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes and pumpkin and roast at 200°C.
  2. Roast the lamb racks alongside at 200°C until 56°C inside.
  3. Roast the vegetables until golden.
  4. Rest the lamb, then carve into cutlets. Cook the peas.
  5. Mix chopped mint with the vinegar, sugar and hot water for the mint sauce.

Country pub steak sandwich

Minute steak, beetroot, cheese and a fried egg on toast. Eat it before the power cut.

How it is made
  • 2 onions
  • 20g butter
  • 750g frozen chips
  • 4 minute steaks
  • 4 slices tasty cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 Turkish rolls
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tin sliced beetroot
  • 100g garlic aioli
  1. Minute steak, caramelised onions, beetroot, cheese, lettuce, tomato and a fried egg on toasted Turkish bread, with chips and aioli. Cook 2 sliced onions in butter for 20 minutes until jammy. Bake 750g frozen chips at 220°C for 25 minutes. Season 4 minute steaks and sear 1 minute a side in a smoking pan, adding a slice of tasty cheese to each. Fry 4 eggs. Toast 4 halved Turkish rolls. Build with mayonnaise, lettuce, tomato, 8 slices beetroot, steak, onions and egg. Serve with chips and garlic aioli.

Sweets

Show day hot jam doughnutsVegetarian

Hot yeast doughnuts rolled in cinnamon sugar and filled with jam.

Prep 20 min, cook 16 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the dough the night before and prove it in the fridge; fry to order
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g sachet dried yeast
  • 150g sugar
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, 50g of the sugar, warm milk, egg and butter into a soft dough and knead for 8 minutes.
  2. Cover and leave to prove until doubled.
  3. Shape 16 balls on a floured tray and leave to prove again.
  4. Fry in 170°C oil for 2 minutes a side, in batches, then drain.
  5. Roll in the rest of the sugar mixed with the cinnamon, poke a hole in each and pipe in the jam.

Moon landing lamingtons

Sponge in chocolate icing and coconut, some jam-filled.

How it is made
  • 125g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 40g cocoa
  • 200g desiccated coconut
  • 1 jar raspberry jam
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Light butter sponge cubes dipped in glossy chocolate icing and rolled in coconut, some split and filled with raspberry jam and whipped cream. Bake a 20 x 30cm butter sponge (125g butter, 150g caster sugar, 2 eggs, 225g self-raising flour, 125ml milk) at 180°C for 25 minutes, cool and chill. Cut into 16 squares. Mix 400g icing sugar, 40g cocoa, 20g butter and 125ml boiling water. Dip each square, roll in 200g desiccated coconut and set. Split half and fill with jam and whipped cream.

Pub lemon lime shandy

Lemon and lime syrup, soda and cold lager, with a lemon wedge.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 limes
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 500ml soda water
  • 4 x 375ml Australian lager
  1. Juice the lemons and limes and mix with the sugar syrup.
  2. Chill four pot glasses in the freezer.
  3. Pour a measure of the citrus syrup into each glass, add soda water, then top slowly with cold lager. Stir once and add a lemon wedge.
  4. Make extra with just soda for the drivers.
