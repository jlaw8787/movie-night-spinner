Snacks

Pork roll sandwiches

Fried pork roll, fried egg and American cheese on a soft roll

Prep 20 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Slice the pork roll up to 1 day ahead, chilled; fry and build the rolls on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork roll or thick ham
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft rolls
  1. Cut the pork roll into 8 slices and nick the edges.
  2. Fry until they curl and brown.
  3. Fry the eggs.
  4. Stack the pork, eggs and cheese into the rolls.
  5. Wrap in foil to melt the cheese.

Italian market bitesVegetarian

Provolone, chargrilled eggplant, olive and roasted pepper skewers with Italian bread and oil

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Thread the toothpicks with eggplant up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; pile onto the board with the bread and oil on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g provolone
  • 150g chargrilled eggplant strips
  • 300g olives
  • 2 red capsicums, roasted
  • 1 loaf Italian bread
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, for dipping
  1. Cut the provolone into cubes.
  2. Thread onto toothpicks with a folded strip of chargrilled eggplant, an olive and a strip of roasted capsicum.
  3. Pile onto a board with sliced Italian bread and a small bowl of olive oil for dipping.

Mains

Adrian's spaghetti and meatballs

Beef and pork meatballs simmered in crushed tomato sauce over spaghetti

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brown, simmer and cook the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 50g parmesan, grated, plus extra to serve
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 egg
  • 800g cans crushed tomatoes
  • 500g spaghetti
  1. Mix the mince with breadcrumbs, parmesan, parsley and egg.
  2. Roll into balls and brown.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer.
  4. Cook the spaghetti.
  5. Serve the meatballs over the spaghetti with extra parmesan.

Sweets

CannoliVegetarian

Crisp shells filled with sweet ricotta, chocolate and pistachio

Prep 25 min, cook 715 min · Make ahead: Drain the ricotta the day before, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ricotta
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 1 orange
  • 50g dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 12 cannoli shells
  • 100g pistachios, chopped
  1. Drain the ricotta overnight.
  2. Beat with the icing sugar, orange zest and chocolate.
  3. Spoon into a piping bag.
  4. Fill the shells just before serving and dip each end in pistachios.

Drinks

Training shakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Banana, milk, oats, peanut butter and honey blended thick, no raw eggs

Prep 4 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving, as the oats thicken it while it sits
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 750ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp rolled oats
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Blend the bananas, milk, oats, peanut butter and honey until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into tall glasses.
