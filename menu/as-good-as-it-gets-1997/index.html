Verdell's bacon

Crisp bacon with maple syrup, a morning regular's order.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 12 rashers thick cut bacon
  • 100ml maple syrup
  1. Lay the bacon on a rack over a tray.
  2. Bake at 200°C until crisp.
  3. Cut into pieces and serve warm with a small bowl of maple syrup for dipping.

Café breakfast bites

Toast squares topped with scrambled egg, crisp bacon and chives

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rashers bacon
  • 6 eggs
  • 40g butter
  • 6 slices white bread
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp and chop.
  2. Scramble the eggs softly in butter.
  3. Toast the bread, cut into small squares and top each with egg, bacon and snipped chives.

Road trip pretzel chicken tendersOn the printed menu

Tenderloins rolled in crushed pretzels, baked crunchy, honey mustard dip, boxed up for the car

How it is made
  • 600g chicken tenderloins
  • 150g salted pretzels
  • 40g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • spray oil
  • 80g Dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Chicken tenderloins crumbed in crushed pretzels and baked crunchy, with a honey mustard dip, packed in a box for the car. Heat the oven to 210°C. Crush 150g salted pretzels to coarse crumbs. Toss 600g chicken tenderloins in 40g plain flour, then 2 beaten eggs, then the pretzel crumbs, pressing to coat. Lay on a lined, oiled tray, spray with oil and bake 15 to 18 minutes, turning once, until golden and cooked through. Mix 80g Dijon mustard, 3 tbsp honey and 2 tbsp mayo for dipping.

Mains

Baltimore crab cakes, jacket required

Pan-fried crab cakes with Dijon, spring onion and crisp breadcrumbs

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g crab meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 spring onions
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  1. Mix the crab with the mayonnaise, Dijon, sliced spring onion and breadcrumbs. Shape into 8 cakes.
  2. Chill for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Pan fry in the vegetable oil until golden on both sides.
  4. Serve with lemon and a green salad.

Carol's meatloaf with brown sugar glazeOn the printed menu

Beef meatloaf under a brown sugar tomato glaze, sliced thick.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 80g breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • 180ml milk
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Beef meatloaf with onion, garlic and herbs, baked under a sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, sliced thick with buttery mash and minted peas. Heat the oven to 180°C. Mix 1kg beef mince, 1 grated onion, 2 crushed garlic cloves, 80g breadcrumbs, 2 eggs, 80ml milk, 1 tbsp Worcestershire, 2 tbsp chopped parsley and 1 tsp salt. Shape into a loaf in a lined tray. Mix 80ml tomato sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tbsp cider vinegar and spread over. Bake 1 hour, rest 10 minutes and slice. Serve with 1kg potatoes mashed with butter and milk, and 400g peas boiled with mint.

Pay me a compliment banana cream pieVegetarian

Biscuit crust, vanilla custard, sliced banana and whipped cream

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before or in the morning
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits
  • 110g butter, melted
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 50g cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 bananas
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Crush the biscuits with the melted butter and press into a pie dish.
  2. Cook the milk, yolks, sugar and cornflour until thick, add the vanilla and cool.
  3. Layer sliced bananas in the crust and spread over the custard.
  4. Top with whipped cream.
  5. Chill for the setting time.

As good as it gets coffee milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong chilled coffee blended with vanilla ice cream and milk

Prep 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml strong coffee
  • 500g vanilla ice cream
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  1. Brew the coffee and chill it for at least an hour.
  2. Blend the coffee with the ice cream, milk and sugar until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into 4 tall chilled glasses. Whip the cream to soft peaks, spoon on top and dust with cocoa.
