Snacks

Wonder World popcornVegetarian

Popcorn coated in a brown sugar and golden syrup caramel

Prep 9 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popping corn kernels
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot.
  2. Boil the sugar, butter and golden syrup, then stir in the bicarbonate of soda.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss.
  4. Bake at 120°C, stirring every 15 minutes.
  5. Cool and break up.

Spider Rider midway corn dogs

Franks on sticks dipped in cornmeal batter and fried

Prep 5 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Whisk the batter up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; stick the franks and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 wooden sticks
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, egg and buttermilk into a batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push onto sticks.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C.
  4. Dip the franks in batter and fry in batches until deep gold.
  5. Serve with mustard.

Uncle Dave's theme park turkey legs

Drumsticks brined overnight, rubbed with paprika, roasted until falling off the bone

Prep 10 min, cook 2 hours, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brine overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 80g salt
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 2l water
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 4 turkey drumsticks
  1. Dissolve the salt and sugar in the water with some of the paprika.
  2. Brine the drumsticks in the fridge overnight.
  3. Pat dry and rub with the rest of the paprika.
  4. Roast at 150°C until the meat pulls easily from the bone.

Detroit-style square pepperoni pizzaOn the printed menu

Axel's home town, crispy cheese edges and all

How it is made
  • 400g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 100g sliced pepperoni
  • 250g mozzarella block
  • 100g cheddar
  • 250ml passata
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • olive oil
  1. Thick airy dough baked in an oiled tin with cheese right to the edges for lacy crisp corners, pepperoni underneath and rich tomato sauce striped on top. Mix 400g bread flour, 300ml warm water, 7g yeast, 1 tsp salt and 1 tbsp oil into a wet dough and prove 2 hours. Press into a well-oiled 23 by 33cm tin and prove 30 minutes. Lay 100g pepperoni over, then 350g cubed mozzarella and cheddar mixed, pushing it hard against the tin edges. Bake at 250°C for 15 minutes until the edges are dark and crisp. Spoon 250ml warm passata cooked with garlic and oregano in two stripes on top.

Sweets

Fairy floss and toffee applesVegetarian

Apples dipped in red hard toffee, served with a bag of fairy floss

Prep 5 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Dip the apples up to 4 hours ahead and keep them at room temperature, not the fridge; add the fairy floss when serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 apples
  • 4 wooden sticks
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 125ml water
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • red food colouring, a few drops
  • 1 bag fairy floss
  1. Push a stick into each apple. Line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Boil the sugar, water and vinegar to 150°C.
  3. Add a few drops of red colouring.
  4. Dip and twirl each apple, then set on the baking paper.
  5. Leave to set. Serve with a bag of fairy floss.

Theme park churros with chocolate dipOn the printed menu

Axel, are you on a coffee break?

How it is made
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Choux dough piped in long ridged lengths, fried crisp and golden, rolled in cinnamon sugar and served with a thick hot chocolate dipping sauce. Bring 250ml water, 50g butter, 1 tbsp sugar and a pinch of salt to the boil, beat in 150g plain flour, cool 5 minutes, then beat in 2 eggs. Pipe 12cm lengths through a star nozzle into oil at 180°C and fry 3 minutes until golden. Roll in 80g caster sugar mixed with 2 tsp cinnamon. Heat 150ml cream until steaming and stir in 150g dark chocolate for the dip.

Wonder World blue raspberry slushieVegetarianNo alcohol

Blue raspberry cordial, lemonade and lime blended with ice, no alcohol

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Best blitzed just before serving as it melts fast; squeeze the lime up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml blue raspberry cordial
  • 400ml lemonade
  • 1 lime
  • 4 cups ice
  • 40g frozen raspberries
  1. Blitz the cordial, lemonade, lime juice and ice in short bursts until fine and slushy.
  2. Drop frozen raspberries into tall glasses.
  3. Pour the slushie over and serve with straws straight away.
