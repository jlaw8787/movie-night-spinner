Barry Allen prawn cocktail

Poached prawns, Marie Rose, iceberg, lemon.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Poach the prawns and make the sauce up to a day ahead and chill; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green king prawns, peeled, tails on
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Tabasco
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil with the bay leaves and half the lemon, add the prawns and poach until just pink, about 2 minutes.
  2. Plunge into iced water, then drain and chill.
  3. Mix the mayo, tomato sauce, Tabasco and a squeeze of lemon for the Marie Rose.
  4. Fill 4 coupes with lettuce and avocado, hang the prawns over the rim and spoon over the sauce.
  5. Dust with paprika and serve with lemon wedges.

Strong family crab beignets

Crab beignets, Creole remoulade, lemon. Frank says grace at the Strongs' table.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the remoulade and the crab batter up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cooked crab meat, picked
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 150ml milk
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Creole or Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, egg and milk to a thick batter, then fold in the crab, spring onion and Cajun seasoning.
  2. Mix the mayo, mustard, capers and a squeeze of lemon for the remoulade.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in 180°C oil, turning, until puffed and golden, about 3 minutes.
  4. Drain, salt and serve hot with the remoulade and lemon.

Filet mignon from the aisle cart

Filet, bearnaise, pommes dauphine, beans. Frank flies as Pan Am pilot Frank Taylor.

Prep 6 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Make the dauphine mix up to a day ahead and chill; make the bearnaise and cook the steaks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 beef eye fillet steaks, about 200g each
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g butter, plus 30g for the steaks
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp tarragon, chopped
  • 500g floury potatoes, peeled
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g green beans
  1. Boil and mash the potatoes dry. Heat 60ml water with 30g of the butter, beat in the flour, then the eggs, and mix into the mash.
  2. Fry spoonfuls of the potato in 180°C oil until puffed and golden for the pommes dauphine.
  3. Simmer the vinegar with the shallot and half the tarragon until almost gone, whisk in the yolks over low heat, then the 120g melted butter in a thin stream. Stir in the rest of the tarragon.
  4. Sear the seasoned steaks in the oil and remaining butter, about 4 minutes a side for medium-rare, and rest.
  5. Blanch the beans and plate everything the way the cart did it, sauce on the side.

Bucket of cream pots de cremeVegetarian

Vanilla custard pots with brown butter shortbread. Two little mice fell in a bucket of cream.

Prep 5 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Bake the pots up to 2 days ahead and chill; make the shortbread up to a week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pouring cream
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 125g butter, browned and cooled
  • 60g icing sugar
  • 175g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Heat the cream with the vanilla until steaming. Whisk the yolks and caster sugar, then whisk in the hot cream.
  2. Strain into 4 small pots in a roasting tin, pour hot water halfway up and bake at 150°C until set with a wobble.
  3. Beat the browned butter with the icing sugar, mix in the flour and salt, press into a tin and bake at 160°C until golden.
  4. Chill the pots and serve with a wedge of the shortbread.

Pinstripe Manhattan

Rye, sweet vermouth, bitters and a cherry, worn with pinstripes.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Batch the drink up to a day ahead and keep it in the fridge; stir over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml rye whiskey
  • 120ml sweet vermouth
  • 8 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Stir the rye, vermouth and bitters with plenty of ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into 4 chilled coupes.
  3. Add a cherry and twist a strip of orange peel over each.
