Snacks

Rosas market breadVegetarian

Crusty toast rubbed with garlic and ripe tomato, olive oil and salt

Prep 11 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best toasted just before serving so it stays crisp; slice the bread and halve the tomatoes up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Halve the tomatoes and cut the garlic cloves in half. Slice the bread thickly.
  2. Toast the bread until golden.
  3. Rub each slice with garlic, then with tomato.
  4. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with salt.

Valentino's goat's cheese bitesVegetarian

Soft goat's cheese rolled in parsley or pistachios, drizzled with honey

Prep 19 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Roll the cheese balls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; coat in parsley and pistachios and drizzle with honey just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g soft goat's cheese
  • 1 handful parsley
  • 100g pistachios
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Chop the parsley and crush the pistachios.
  2. Roll the cheese into small balls.
  3. Roll half in parsley and half in pistachios.
  4. Arrange on a plate and drizzle with honey.

Mains

Sabino's paella

Chicken, chorizo and saffron rice for Sabino's 100th

Prep 13 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Slice the chorizo and capsicum up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the paella fresh so the base crisps
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 1 chorizo
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 300g paella rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1l chicken stock
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Slice the chorizo and capsicum.
  2. Brown the chicken and chorizo in the oil in a wide pan.
  3. Add the capsicum, rice and saffron, then pour in the hot stock.
  4. Simmer without stirring until the bottom crisps.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.

Sweets

Dahlia's star cookiesVegetarian

Dahlia bakes for the King. These ones are iced stars

Prep 32 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp yellow food colouring
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into stars.
  3. Bake at 160C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Ice yellow with icing sugar, colouring and a little water, then dust with glitter.

Star wish lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice and syrup with orange blossom water, topped with sparkling water

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup and cool it up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; squeeze the lemons, mix and top with sparkling water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 5 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml hot water
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water
  • 1l sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Cool.
  3. Squeeze the lemons into a jug and add the syrup and orange blossom water.
  4. Top with sparkling water and ice.
