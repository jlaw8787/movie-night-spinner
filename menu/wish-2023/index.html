Rosas market breadVegetarian

Crusty toast rubbed with garlic and ripe tomato, olive oil and salt.

Prep 11 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best toasted just before serving so it stays crisp; slice the bread and halve the tomatoes up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Halve the tomatoes and cut the garlic cloves in half. Slice the bread thickly.
  2. Toast the bread until golden, 6 min.
  3. Rub each slice with garlic, then with tomato.
  4. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with salt.

Valentino's goat's cheese bitesVegetarian

Soft goat's cheese rolled in parsley or pistachios, drizzled with honey.

Prep 19 min · Make ahead: Roll the cheese balls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; coat in parsley and pistachios and drizzle with honey just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g soft goat's cheese
  • 1 handful parsley
  • 100g pistachios
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Chop the parsley and crush the pistachios.
  2. Roll the cheese into small balls.
  3. Roll half in parsley and half in pistachios.
  4. Arrange on a plate and drizzle with honey.

Star sparkle paprika potato crisps

Thin potato crisps with smoked paprika and a sprinkle of stardust.

How it is made
  • 600g potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Potatoes sliced thin on a mandoline, fried at 170°C until crisp, tossed with smoked paprika and salt. Slice 600g washed potatoes 1 to 2mm thin on a mandoline. Soak in cold water for 20 minutes to remove starch, then dry very well on tea towels. Heat oil to 170°C and fry in small batches for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until golden and the bubbling stops. Drain on paper towel. Mix 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp sea salt and a pinch of sugar and toss through while hot. Serve with a sour cream and chive dip.

Sabino's paella

Chicken, chorizo and saffron rice, cooked for a 100th birthday.

Prep 13 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Slice the chorizo and capsicum up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the paella fresh so the base crisps
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 1 chorizo
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 300g paella rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Slice the chorizo and capsicum.
  2. Brown the chicken and chorizo in the oil in a wide pan, 12 min.
  3. Add the capsicum, rice and saffron, then pour in the hot stock.
  4. Simmer without stirring until the bottom crisps, 20 min.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.

Ceremony lemon garlic roast lamb

Lamb leg with rosemary and citrus, roasted with potato wedges, for Asha's interview day.

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  1. Lamb leg rubbed with garlic, rosemary and lemon, roasted at 180°C for 90 minutes, with potatoes. Cut slits in a 2kg lamb leg and push in slivers from 4 garlic cloves and rosemary sprigs. Rub with the zest and juice of 1 lemon, olive oil and salt. Roast at 180°C for 90 minutes for medium, adding 1kg potatoes in wedges tossed with oil, oregano and lemon juice for the last hour. Rest 15 minutes, carve and serve with the potatoes and a dollop of garlicky Greek yoghurt.

Dahlia's star cookiesVegetarian

Butter biscuits cut into stars and iced. Dahlia bakes for the King.

Prep 32 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp yellow food colouring
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into stars.
  3. Bake at 160°C, 15 min.
  4. Cool completely, 20 min.
  5. Ice yellow with icing sugar, colouring and a little water, then dust with glitter.

Star wish lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice and syrup with orange blossom water, topped with sparkling water.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup and cool it up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; squeeze the lemons, mix and top with sparkling water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 5 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml hot water
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water
  • 1 L sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool, 3 min.
  2. Cool, 15 min.
  3. Squeeze the lemons into a jug and add the syrup and orange blossom water.
  4. Top with sparkling water and ice.
