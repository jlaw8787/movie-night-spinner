Snacks

English tea sandwiches

Cucumber, egg and cress, smoked salmon.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 punnet cress
  • 12 slices of white bread
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1/2 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 100g cream cheese
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool, then mash with mayonnaise and cress.
  2. Butter the bread. Make 2 sandwiches with cucumber, 2 with the egg and 2 with smoked salmon on cream cheese.
  3. Cut off the crusts and cut each into 3 fingers.

Kansas City fried ravioliVegetarian

Breaded and fried, marinara, parmesan.

Prep 13 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g fresh ricotta ravioli
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g Italian breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 300ml passata
  • 1 pinch dried oregano
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Dip the ravioli in egg, then breadcrumbs.
  2. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry the ravioli in batches for 2 minutes until crisp and golden, then drain.
  3. Warm the passata with salt and oregano for dipping, 4 min.
  4. Shower the ravioli with parmesan and serve.

Linguine, white clam sauce

Clams in white wine, garlic, parsley.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 32 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg clams
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 150ml white wine
  • 400g linguine
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Soak the clams in salted water for 30 minutes to purge, then rinse.
  2. Gently fry the garlic and chilli in some of the olive oil, add the clams and wine, cover and steam for 4 minutes until open.
  3. Cook the linguine 1 minute short, finish it in the pan juices and toss with parsley and more olive oil.

TiramisuVegetarian

Espresso and marsala soaked ladyfingers, mascarpone cream, cocoa.

Prep 30 min, 8 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 500g mascarpone
  • 4 egg whites
  • 300ml strong espresso, cooled
  • 3 tbsp marsala
  • 24 savoiardi biscuits
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  1. Whisk the yolks and sugar until pale, then fold in the mascarpone.
  2. Whip the egg whites stiff and fold into the cream.
  3. Mix the espresso with the marsala, dip the biscuits quickly and layer with the cream in a dish.
  4. Dust with cocoa and chill overnight.

Gin collins

Lemon, sugar, soda, a curl of cucumber, mint.

Prep 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 160ml gin
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 400ml soda water
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Peel 8 long ribbons from the cucumber and curl two into each of four tall glasses, then fill with ice.
  2. Shake the gin, cordial and lemon juice with ice for 10 seconds.
  3. Strain over the glasses, top with soda and add a sprig of mint.
