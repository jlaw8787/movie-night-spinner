Snacks

Stark mansion canapés

Crisp wonton cups with soy and sesame sashimi tuna, smashed avocado and sesame

Prep 22 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Tuna mix up to 1 hour ahead, assemble to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 16 wonton wrappers
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 250g sashimi tuna
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 lime
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
  1. Fry the wonton wrappers at 180°C until crisp. Drain.
  2. Dice the tuna and dress with soy, sesame oil, lime juice and spring onion.
  3. Smash the avocado.
  4. Spoon the tuna onto the wontons and top with a dot of avocado and the sesame.

Cave flatbreadVegetarian

Yoghurt flatbreads and hummus, from a box of scraps

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Mix the dough up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge; roll and cook the flatbreads fresh, garlic butter can be melted a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tub hummus
  1. Mix the flour, yoghurt, baking powder and salt to a soft dough.
  2. Roll into six thin rounds.
  3. Cook in a dry hot pan until blistered, turning once.
  4. Brush with butter melted with the garlic and serve with hummus.

Tony's American cheeseburger

Two smashed patties with onion, pickles, mustard and ketchup on a toasted bun

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls and dice the onion up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; smash and cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • salt, to taste
  1. Roll the mince into eight balls and dice the onion.
  2. Smash each flat on a smoking griddle, season and cook.
  3. Flip, top with cheese and cook a minute more. Toast the buns.
  4. Stack two per bun with onion, pickles, mustard and tomato sauce.

Pepper Potts' Malibu rib eyeOn the printed menu

Rib eye seared in a hot pan, then rested. Dinner after the dry cleaning

How it is made
  • 4 rib eye steaks (300g each)
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch oregano
  • 1 red chilli
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 40g butter
  1. Dry-brined rib eye seared and butter-basted, rested and sliced with punchy chimichurri and crisp smashed baby potatoes with garlic and rosemary. Salt 4 rib eye steaks and refrigerate uncovered overnight. Boil 1kg baby potatoes 15 minutes, smash flat on an oiled tray and roast at 220°C for 30 minutes with garlic and rosemary. Chop a bunch of parsley, 1 tbsp oregano, 2 garlic cloves and 1 red chilli and mix with 3 tbsp red wine vinegar and 100ml olive oil. Sear the steaks 3 minutes a side, basting with butter, and rest 8 minutes.

Arc reactor cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes, blue ring. I am Iron Man.

Prep 10 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp blue food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 12-hole tin. Beat half the butter with the sugar, then the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk, divide between the cases and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and tint half blue.
  5. Pipe a blue ring with a white centre.

Tony's workshop scotch penicillinVegetarian

Scotch, lemon, honey and ginger with a float of peaty single malt

Prep 6 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 piece ginger (2 cm), sliced
  • 200ml blended Scotch
  • 40ml peaty single malt
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 pieces candied ginger
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 2 cups ice, for shaking
  1. Simmer the honey, water and ginger, then strain and cool.
  2. Shake the Scotch, lemon juice and the syrup with ice.
  3. Strain into four tumblers over one large cube each.
  4. Float a little peaty single malt on each and garnish with candied ginger.
