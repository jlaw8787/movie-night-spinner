Sumac flatbreads with beet and herb dipsVegetarian

Sumac flatbreads with a beetroot dip and a herb dip. Two dips, two lightsabers.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Dips up to 2 days, grill breads fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp sumac
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 beetroot
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp chopped herbs (parsley and mint)
  • 200g hummus
  1. Roast the beetroot at 200°C until tender, then cool and peel.
  2. Blend the beetroot with the garlic and some of the yoghurt for a pink dip. Stir the herbs through more yoghurt for a green dip.
  3. Brush the flatbreads with oil, dust with sumac and turmeric and grill until blistered. Serve with the dips and hummus.

Kijimi snowy city dumplings

Steamed ginger chicken dumplings in hot chicken stock with spring onion

Prep 41 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead, steam fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken mince
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 spring onions, sliced, plus extra to serve
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 1l hot chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Mix the chicken with the ginger, spring onion and soy.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam for 10 minutes.
  4. Serve a few in each bowl with hot stock, more spring onion and a drizzle of chilli oil.

Babu Frik's tiny mushroom toastsOn the printed menu

Bite-sized rounds under buttery mushroom, thyme and garlic pâté, cornichon and crispy shallot

How it is made
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 80g cream cheese
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 shallots
  • 1 jar cornichons
  1. Bite-sized toasts piled with a buttery mushroom, thyme and garlic pâté, topped with a sliver of cornichon and crispy shallot. Small enough for Babu Frik. Fry 300g chopped mushrooms in 40g butter with 2 garlic cloves and thyme until dark and dry. Blitz with 80g cream cheese, salt and pepper until smooth and chill. Cut a baguette into 20 thin slices, brush with oil and toast at 200°C for 6 minutes. Fry 2 sliced shallots in oil until crisp. Spread the pâté on the toasts and top with sliced cornichons and crispy shallots.

Kef Bir ocean seafood stew

Fish, prawns and mussels in a saffron fennel tomato broth

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 fennel bulb, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • saffron threads, a good pinch
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup white wine
  • 400g white fish fillets, in chunks
  • 300g green prawns
  • 500g mussels, cleaned
  • 4 slices bread, for garlic toast
  1. Soften the fennel and garlic in the oil. Add the saffron, tomatoes and wine.
  2. Simmer to a rich broth.
  3. Add the fish, prawns and mussels, cover and cook until the mussels open.
  4. Toast the bread, rub with a cut garlic clove and serve alongside.

Exegol black pepper rib eyeOn the printed menu

Black pepper rib eye seared and basted in garlic butter, served rare as Palpatine's fleet rises

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Rib eye seared in a smoking pan and basted in garlic butter, sliced over buttery mash with a punchy cracked peppercorn cream sauce. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml milk. Season 4 rib eye steaks and sear in a smoking pan for 3 minutes, flip, add 40g butter and 2 garlic cloves and baste for 3 minutes. Rest. Add 2 tsp crushed black peppercorns to the pan, then 150ml beef stock, 1 tsp Dijon and 150ml cream and simmer until thick. Slice the steaks over the mash and pour over the sauce.

Rey Skywalker's twin suns pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue topped with whipped cream, sliced mango and two pools of passionfruit

Prep 30 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 2 mangoes, sliced
  • 4 passionfruit
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Shape into a round on a lined tray and bake at 120°C. Cool in the oven.
  3. Top with whipped cream, mango and two round pools of passionfruit pulp.

Lars homestead blue coconut milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold milk and coconut milk blended with honey and tinted pale blue

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml cold milk
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp blue food colouring
  • 1 handful ice
  1. Blend the milk, coconut milk, honey and ice until frothy.
  2. Add a few drops of blue colouring and blend until evenly pale blue. Serve cold.
