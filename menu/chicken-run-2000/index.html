Snacks

Egg quota curried egg and cress sandwichesVegetarian

Mild curried egg mayo with cress on soft white bread.

Prep 18 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1/2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 bunch chives
  • salt and white pepper, to taste
  • 8 slices of soft white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 1 punnet cress
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Mash with the mayonnaise, curry powder, chopped chives, salt and white pepper.
  3. Butter the bread and spread the egg over half of it. Scatter with cress and close up.
  4. Cut off the crusts and cut each into 4 triangles.

Babs's knitted cheese strawsVegetarian

Twisted puff pastry with parmesan and paprika, baked crisp.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 80g parmesan
  • 1 pinch sweet paprika
  1. Brush one pastry sheet with beaten egg and cover with the parmesan and paprika.
  2. Lay the other sheet on top, press, cut into strips and twist.
  3. Bake at 200°C until golden and crisp.

Mains

Pie machine beef and onion pies

Slow braised beef and onion in Worcestershire gravy, baked in shortcrust.

Prep 35 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 30g butter
  1. Dice the beef and slice the onions.
  2. Brown the beef in batches, add the onions, thyme, tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and stock, cover and braise.
  3. Thicken with cornflour mixed in water, then cool.
  4. Line four pie tins with shortcrust, fill, top with puff and egg wash.
  5. Bake at 200°C.
  6. Simmer the peas with the butter and mash roughly.

Leek, mushroom and cheddar farm pie

Leeks, mushrooms and sharp cheddar in a creamy sauce under a shortcrust lid.

How it is made
  • 500g potatoes
  • 2 leeks
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 70g butter
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 400ml milk
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Leek, mushroom and potato in a creamy sharp cheddar and mustard sauce, baked under golden puff pastry with buttered peas alongside. Boil 500g diced potato for 8 minutes and drain. Soften 2 sliced leeks and 300g sliced mushrooms in 40g butter. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 400ml milk and cook until thick. Add 150g grated cheddar, 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard and the potato. Tip into a pie dish, top with a sheet of puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 30 minutes. Serve with 300g buttered peas.

Escape plane jam roly-polyVegetarian

Baked roll of soft dough and raspberry jam, served with custard.

Prep 10 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 150ml cold water
  • 150g raspberry jam
  • 600ml custard
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then add the water to a soft dough.
  2. Roll to a rectangle, spread with jam and roll up. Wrap loosely in baking paper and foil.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Heat the custard and serve with slices.

Drinks

Coop meeting honey malt hot chocolateNo alcohol

Hot milk with cocoa, malt powder and honey, topped with cream and dark chocolate.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep it chilled; warm the milk and whisk to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Warm the milk until steaming but not boiling. Whip the cream.
  2. Whisk in the cocoa, malt and honey until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into four mugs, top with cream and grate dark chocolate over.

When to serve

Serve the pies when Mr Tweedy runs the pie machine test.
