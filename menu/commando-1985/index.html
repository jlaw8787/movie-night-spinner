Jenny's mystery club sandwiches

Jenny's sandwich: you don't wanna know. Turkey, bacon, avocado and secret sauce.

Prep 9 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Mix the secret sauce up to 3 days ahead; build the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 12 slices of white sandwich bread
  • 250g shaved roast turkey
  • 6 rashers of streaky bacon
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tbsp pickle relish
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp and toast the bread.
  2. Stir the mayo with the sriracha and relish for the secret sauce.
  3. Build triple-deckers: sauce, turkey and lettuce, then toast, then bacon, avocado and tomato.
  4. Pin with toothpicks and cut each into 4 triangles.

Let off some steam pork buns

Steamed pork buns, sticky pork belly, cucumber.

Prep 15 min, cook 88 min · Make ahead: Braise the pork up to 2 days ahead and chill in its liquid; slice, glaze and steam the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly, skin off
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 60ml hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 thumb-sized piece ginger, sliced
  • 500ml water
  • 12 frozen Chinese steamed buns
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced thin
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Brown the pork belly all over in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the soy, hoisin, sugar, garlic, ginger and water, cover and simmer gently until tender.
  3. Lift out the pork, reduce the liquid to a sticky glaze and slice the pork thickly into it.
  4. Toss the cucumber with the rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar, and steam the buns.
  5. Fill the buns with pork, cucumber, peanuts and coriander.

Val Verde mojo chicken

Citrus garlic chicken, black beans and rice.

Prep 0 min, cook 78 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken overnight; cook the beans up to 2 days ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken thighs, bone in, skin on
  • 2 oranges, juiced
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 x 400g tins of black beans
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Mix the citrus juice, garlic, cumin, oregano, oil and salt and marinate the chicken for at least 2 hours.
  2. Roast the chicken at 200°C, skin side up, until crisp and cooked through, basting with the marinade.
  3. Soften the onion and capsicum, add the beans with their liquid and simmer until thick.
  4. Cook the rice and serve with the chicken, beans and coriander.

Green Berets for breakfast huevos rancherosVegetarian

Fried eggs, salsa roja, beans, green chilli.

Prep 6 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa up to 3 days ahead and reheat; fry the eggs to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 400g tin refried beans
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 2 long green chillies, sliced
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in a little oil, add the tomatoes and chipotle and simmer to a thick salsa.
  2. Warm the beans and fry the tortillas in a little oil until just crisp at the edges.
  3. Fry the eggs sunny side up in the rest of the oil.
  4. Spread beans on the tortillas, add the eggs and spoon the salsa round, then top with feta, green chilli and avocado.

I let him go tres lechesVegetarian

Milk-soaked sponge with coconut cream and fresh lime zest.

Prep 18 min, cook 30 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and soak the cake the day before; it needs the night in the fridge. Top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs, separated
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 395g tin sweetened condensed milk
  • 375ml tin evaporated milk
  • 270ml tin coconut cream
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 lime, zested
  • 2 tbsp toasted coconut flakes
  1. Beat the yolks with 150g sugar until pale, then stir in the milk and fold in the flour.
  2. Whisk the whites with the rest of the sugar to soft peaks and fold through. Bake in a lined 20 by 30cm tin at 175°C until springy.
  3. Prick the warm cake all over and pour over the condensed milk, evaporated milk and coconut cream, mixed. Chill overnight.
  4. Whip the cream and spread over, then finish with lime zest and coconut.

Fly or die seaplane rum punch

Dark rum, pineapple juice and lime with grated nutmeg.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the punch without ice up to 4 hours ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 60ml grenadine
  • 250ml soda water
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Stir the rum, pineapple, lime and grenadine in a jug.
  2. Pour into 4 tall glasses of ice and top with soda.
  3. Grate a little nutmeg over each.
