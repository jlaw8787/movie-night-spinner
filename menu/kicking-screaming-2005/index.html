Snacks

Halftime crispy orange chicken bites

Crispy chicken thigh in a sticky orange ginger sauce, sesame on top

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the orange sauce up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat; coat and fry the chicken just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • soy sauce, to taste
  • 70g cornflour
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 3 oranges
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 garlic clove
  • sesame seeds, to taste
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Cut the chicken thigh into bite-sized pieces, toss with the soy, then in the cornflour.
  2. Fry in batches in oil at 180C for 4 to 5 minutes until crisp.
  3. Simmer the juice of the oranges with the zest of one of them, the soy, the honey, the rice vinegar, the grated ginger and the garlic clove for 5 minutes, then thicken with the cornflour in water.
  4. Toss the chicken through and top with sesame and spring onion.

Phil's vitamin shop granola barsVegetarian

Oat, almond and cranberry bars with honey, peanut butter and dark chocolate

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g rolled oats
  • 80g almonds
  • 50g pepitas
  • 120g honey
  • 100g peanut butter
  • 50g butter
  • 50g brown sugar
  • 80g dried cranberries
  • 80g dark chocolate
  1. Heat the oven to 170C.
  2. Toast the rolled oats, the chopped almonds and the pepitas on a tray for 10 minutes.
  3. Warm the honey, the peanut butter, the butter and the brown sugar until smooth.
  4. Stir into the oats with the dried cranberries and a pinch of salt.
  5. Press firmly into a lined 20 x 30cm tin and bake 20 minutes.
  6. Cool, scatter with the melted dark chocolate and cut into bars once set.

Mains

Apprentice butcher Italian sausage rolls

Grilled fennel sausage, charred capsicum, onion and provolone in a roll

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the capsicum and onion up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; grill the sausages and toast the rolls fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 Italian fennel sausages
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 2 brown onions
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 8 long rolls
  • 8 slices provolone
  • 1 jar hot giardiniera
  1. Slice the red capsicums and the onions and cook in the oil over medium-high heat for 12 minutes until soft and charred.
  2. Grill the Italian fennel sausages on a medium barbecue for 12 to 15 minutes, turning, until cooked through.
  3. Split the long rolls, toast, and lay a slice of provolone in.
  4. Add a sausage, the capsicum and onion and a spoon of chopped hot giardiniera or pickled chillies.

Coffee shop biscottiVegetarian

Twice-baked almond biscotti, crisp and made for dunking

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 weeks, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g almonds
  1. Mix the plain flour, the sugar, the baking powder, the eggs and the whole almonds into a dough.
  2. Shape the logs and bake at 180C for 25 minutes.
  3. Cool 10 minutes, slice thinly and bake 10 minutes more until crisp.

Phil's quadruple-shot mochaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong espresso and chocolate milk topped with whipped cream and shavings

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate milk up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm; pull the shots and whip the cream fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 800ml milk
  • espresso coffee beans, to taste
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • chocolate-coated coffee beans, to taste
  1. Melt the chopped dark chocolate with the sugar in a saucepan with the milk, whisking until smooth.
  2. Add more milk and heat until steaming, whisking until frothy.
  3. Pull the shots of espresso.
  4. Pour the shots into each of the big mugs and top with the chocolate milk.
  5. Whip the cream and spoon on, then add chocolate shavings and the chocolate-coated coffee beans.
  6. Decaf is allowed.
