Snacks

Diner silver dollar pancakesVegetarian

Small buttermilk pancakes stacked in threes with butter and maple syrup.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: The batter keeps up to 4 hours in the fridge; cook the rounds fresh so they stay light
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 40g butter
  • 100ml maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, buttermilk and egg.
  2. Rest, 15 min.
  3. Cook small rounds in butter until bubbles set, 15 min.
  4. Stack in threes with butter and maple syrup.

Warehouse hash brownsVegetarian

Crisp potato and onion cakes, the diner breakfast before the job.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best grated and fried just before serving, as the potato turns grey if it waits
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Grate the potatoes and onion and squeeze dry.
  2. Season with fine salt and press into flat cakes.
  3. Fry in butter and oil until deep golden on both sides, 15 min.

Bacon and egg rolls

Scrambled egg, bacon and cheese on kaiser rolls, Mr Pink's diner order, no tip.

How it is made
  • 8 rashers streaky bacon
  • 8 eggs
  • 30g butter
  • 8 small soft rolls
  • whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 4 slices American cheese
  1. Soft scrambled eggs, crisp streaky bacon and melted American cheese in toasted small soft rolls with hot sauce mayo. Grill 8 rashers streaky bacon until crisp and halve. Whisk 8 eggs with salt and scramble softly in 30g butter over low heat. Toast 8 small soft rolls. Mix 4 tbsp mayo with 1 tbsp hot sauce. Fill each bun with egg, bacon, a half slice of American cheese and a spoon of the hot sauce mayo.

Mains

Nice Guy Eddie's steak sandwich

Seared sirloin, slow-cooked onions and provolone in a toasted roll.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and mix the horseradish mayonnaise up to 3 days ahead, both chilled; sear the steaks and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 2 rump steaks
  • 4 bread rolls
  • 4 slices of provolone
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp horseradish
  1. Cook the sliced onions slowly in the butter until golden, 25 min.
  2. Sear the steaks and rest, 15 min.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise and horseradish and toast the rolls, 5 min.
  4. Slice the steak and pile into rolls with onions and provolone.

Joe Cabot's big breakfast plate

Two eggs your way, thick bacon, sausages, crispy hash browns, buttered toast and blistered tomato.

How it is made
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 rashers thick-cut bacon
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 8 frozen hash browns
  • 8 eggs
  • 60g butter
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • tomato sauce
  1. Two eggs your way, thick-cut bacon, griddled pork sausages, crispy hash browns, buttered toast and a blistered tomato on a big oval plate. Grill 8 pork sausages for 15 minutes, turning. Fry 8 rashers thick-cut bacon. Halve 4 tomatoes and grill cut side down until blistered. Bake 8 hash browns at 220°C for 20 minutes. Fry 8 eggs in butter to order. Toast 8 slices of bread and butter well. Load everything onto four big plates with tomato sauce on the table.

Like a Virgin lemon meringue pieVegetarian

Tart lemon curd in a sweet pastry case under toasted meringue.

Prep 13 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Make the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 40g cornflour
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 eggs
  • 40g butter
  • 300ml water
  1. Blind bake the pastry case at 180°C, 20 min.
  2. Cook the water, part of the sugar, cornflour, lemon juice and zest and yolks until thick, then add the butter, 10 min.
  3. Pour into the case.
  4. Whisk the egg whites with the remaining sugar to stiff peaks and pile on top.
  5. Bake until golden, 12 min.

Drinks

Tip jar maple cream iced coffeeNo alcohol

Iced coffee poured over ice and topped with maple cream.

Prep 10 min, 12 hr waiting · Make ahead: Brew the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 100g coarse ground coffee
  • 1 L cold water
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the coffee into the cold water and steep in the fridge for 12 hours.
  2. Strain.
  3. Whisk the cream with the maple syrup until just thick.
  4. Fill four mugs with ice, pour in the iced coffee and spoon the maple cream on top.
