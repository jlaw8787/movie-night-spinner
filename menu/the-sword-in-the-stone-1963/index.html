The Sword in the Stone (1963) movie night dinner menu

Snacks

Sir Ector's bread and cheeseVegetarian

Crusty bread, aged cheddar, pickled onions and apple, for Wart on kitchen duty.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheddar up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; cut the apples and tear the bread just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 300g aged cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 3 green apples
  1. Tear the bread into thick chunks and cut the aged cheddar into wedges.
  2. Cut the apples into wedges.
  3. Pile everything onto a wooden board with the pickled onions.

Honey cinnamon roasted nutsVegetarian

Walnuts, hazelnuts and almonds in honey, for Wart the squirrel.

Prep 7 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g walnuts
  • 100g hazelnuts
  • 100g almonds
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  1. Toss the walnuts, hazelnuts and almonds with honey and cinnamon.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170°C, stirring once, 15 min.
  3. Cool completely so they set, 20 min.
  4. Break into clusters.

Moat fish bites with tartare

River trout crumbed and shallow fried crisp, with lemon and creamy pickle sauce, safe from the pike.

How it is made
  • 600g trout fillets
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko crumbs
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 150g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 jar gherkins
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 lemons
  1. Trout pieces dusted in seasoned flour, fried in hot oil until crisp, served with tartare sauce and lemon wedges. Cut 600g trout fillets into bite-size pieces and pat dry. Season 100g plain flour with salt, pepper and a pinch of paprika. Dust the fish in flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs, then coat in 120g panko. Shallow fry in 1cm of oil at 180°C for 2 minutes a side until golden. Mix 150g mayo with 1 tbsp chopped capers, 1 tbsp chopped gherkin, chopped dill and lemon juice. Serve hot with the tartare and lemon wedges.

Mains

London tournament pie

Beef chuck in stock and Worcestershire under pastry.

Prep 30 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Simmer the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake on the day, mash the potatoes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 375ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 egg
  1. Slice the onions and brown the beef in oil, 5 min.
  2. Brown, 15 min.
  3. Add the onions, stock, Worcestershire and water to cover and simmer, 3 hr.
  4. Thicken with the flour, spoon into a pie dish and top with pastry brushed with egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C, 25 min.
  6. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter, and cook the peas, 5 min.
  7. Cook, 20 min.

Castle kitchen roast pork and crackling

Salted shoulder slow cooked till the skin blisters, baked apples and gravy, for Kay's knighting.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 box cooking salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 apples
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Pork shoulder roasted at 160°C for 4 hours with crackling, with roast potatoes, apples and gravy. Score the skin of a 2kg pork shoulder, pat very dry and rub with oil and plenty of salt. Roast at 160°C for 3.5 hours. Add 1kg potatoes in chunks tossed in oil for the last hour and 4 halved apples for the last 30 minutes. Turn the oven to 240°C for 20 minutes to blister the crackling. Rest 15 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan juices, whisk in 300ml chicken stock and simmer into gravy. Carve and serve with the apples.

Sweets

Raspberry jam roly-polyVegetarian

Soft suet dough rolled round raspberry jam and steamed, with custard.

Prep 18 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Roll up the roly-poly with the jam up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; bake on the day and warm the custard to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 100g butter
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar raspberry jam
  • 500ml custard
  1. Rub the butter into the flour and add milk to make a soft dough.
  2. Roll into a rectangle, spread with jam and roll up.
  3. Wrap loosely in baking paper and bake at 180°C, 40 min.
  4. Warm the custard.
  5. Slice the roly-poly and pour over hot custard.

Drinks

Merlin's spiced milk tea and sugar cubesNo alcohol

Black tea, cinnamon, cardamom, ginger and milk.

Prep 4 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer the water with the spices up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; reheat, add the tea bags and milk and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cardamom pods
  • 5 slices of fresh ginger
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 box sugar cubes
  1. Crush the cardamom pods.
  2. Simmer the water with cinnamon, cardamom and ginger, 5 min.
  3. Add the tea bags and milk and bring back to a gentle simmer, 3 min.
  4. Strain into four cups and serve with sugar cubes.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Merlin magics the castle dishes into washing themselves.
