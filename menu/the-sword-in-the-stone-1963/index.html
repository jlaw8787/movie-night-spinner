Snacks

Sir Ector's bread and cheeseVegetarian

Crusty bread, aged cheddar, pickled onions and apple, for Wart on kitchen duty.

Prep 13 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheddar up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; cut the apples and tear the bread just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 300g aged cheddar
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 3 green apples
  1. Tear the bread into thick chunks and cut the aged cheddar into wedges.
  2. Cut the apples into wedges.
  3. Pile everything onto a wooden board with the pickled onions.

Honey cinnamon roasted nutsVegetarian

Walnuts, hazelnuts and almonds in honey, for Wart the squirrel

Prep 7 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g walnuts
  • 100g hazelnuts
  • 100g almonds
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  1. Toss the walnuts, hazelnuts and almonds with honey and cinnamon.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170C, stirring once.
  3. Cool completely so they set.
  4. Break into clusters.

Mains

London tournament pie

Beef chuck in brown ale under puff pastry. Wart left Kay's sword at the inn.

Prep 20 min, cook 240 min · Make ahead: Simmer the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake on the day, mash the potatoes fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 375ml brown ale
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 egg
  1. Slice the onions and brown the beef in oil.
  2. Brown.
  3. Add the onions, ale and water to cover and simmer.
  4. Thicken with the flour, spoon into a pie dish and top with pastry brushed with egg.
  5. Bake at 200C.
  6. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter, and cook the peas.
  7. Cook.

Sweets

Raspberry jam roly-polyVegetarian

Soft dough rolled round jam. Mim breaks out in spots.

Prep 18 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Roll up the roly-poly with the jam up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; bake on the day and warm the custard to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 100g butter
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar raspberry jam
  • 500ml custard
  1. Rub the butter into the flour and add milk to make a soft dough.
  2. Roll into a rectangle, spread with jam and roll up.
  3. Wrap loosely in baking paper and bake at 180C.
  4. Warm the custard.
  5. Slice the roly-poly and pour over hot custard.

Drinks

Merlin's spiced milk tea and sugar cubesVegetarianNo alcohol

Black tea, cinnamon, cardamom, ginger and milk. Manners, guests first!

Prep 4 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer the water with the spices up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; reheat, add the tea bags and milk and strain to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cardamom pods
  • 5 slices fresh ginger
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 box sugar cubes
  1. Crush the cardamom pods.
  2. Simmer the water with cinnamon, cardamom and ginger.
  3. Add the tea bags and milk and bring back to a gentle simmer.
  4. Strain into four cups and serve with sugar cubes.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Merlin magics the castle dishes into washing themselves.
