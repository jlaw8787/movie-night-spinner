Jersey wonders fried dough twistsVegetarian

Sweet fried pastry twists dusted with sugar

Prep 30 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Dough rests 1 hour, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 50g butter, cold
  • 250g plain flour
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, add some of the sugar and the eggs and knead into a dough.
  2. Rest the dough for an hour.
  3. Roll out, cut into strips, slit and twist through.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175C until golden, in batches.
  5. Dust with sugar and nutmeg.

Jersey crab on toast

Crab with mayo, lemon, chives and cayenne on buttered toast fingers

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fold the crab mixture up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; toast the bread and pile on just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 4 slices white bread
  • 30g butter
  1. Fold the crab with the mayo, lemon juice, chives and cayenne.
  2. Toast the bread, butter it and cut into fingers.
  3. Pile the crab on top.

Mr Tuttle's garden potatoes and fish

Golden pan fried white fish with brown butter and capers, minty new potatoes

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 2 hours ahead and keep them in the pot; pan-fry the fish and brown the butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 1 sprig mint
  • 100g butter
  • 4 white fish fillets, such as flathead
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Boil the potatoes with the mint until tender and toss in salted butter.
  2. Pan-fry the fish until golden.
  3. Brown some butter, add the capers and lemon juice, and spoon over the fish.

Curtains-drawn Jersey cream rice puddingVegetarian

Slow baked creamy rice pudding with vanilla and nutmeg

Prep 10 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, cover in the fridge and reheat gently with a splash of milk; grate the nutmeg on top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g short grain rice
  • 800ml milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 70g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  1. Put the rice, milk, cream, sugar and vanilla bean in a baking dish.
  2. Bake at 150C for 2 hours, stirring twice.
  3. Grate nutmeg on top. Serve warm, curtains drawn.

Drinks

Island apple cider toddy from the teapotVegetarian

Dry apple cider warmed with honey, lemon zest, cinnamon and cloves

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Warm the cider with the honey, lemon zest, cinnamon and cloves up to 1 day ahead; reheat gently, then add the brandy and lemon juice just before the teapot
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l dry apple cider
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, zest peeled and juice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 120ml brandy
  1. Pour the cider into a saucepan with the honey, lemon zest, cinnamon and cloves.
  2. Warm gently for 10 minutes without boiling.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the brandy and half the lemon juice.
  4. Warm a teapot with hot water, tip it out, strain the cider into the pot and pour into teacups at the table.
