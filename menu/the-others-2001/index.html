Jersey wonders fried dough twistsVegetarian

Sweet fried pastry twists dusted with sugar.

Prep 28 min, cook 10 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Dough rests 1 hour, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 50g butter, cold
  • 250g plain flour
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, add some of the sugar and the eggs and knead into a dough.
  2. Rest the dough for an hour.
  3. Roll out, cut into strips, slit and twist through.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C until golden, in batches, 10 min.
  5. Dust with sugar and nutmeg.

Jersey crab on toast

Crab with mayo, lemon, chives and cayenne on buttered toast fingers.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fold the crab mixture up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; toast the bread and pile on just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 4 slices of white bread
  • 30g butter
  1. Fold the crab with the mayo, lemon juice, chives and cayenne.
  2. Toast the bread, butter it and cut into fingers, 5 min.
  3. Pile the crab on top.

Mrs Mills's egg and cress tea sandwiches

Curried egg or cucumber with white pepper on crustless bread, for children kept from daylight.

How it is made
  • 6 eggs
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar curry powder
  • 1 loaf soft white bread
  • 125g salted butter
  • 1 punnet cress
  • 1 continental cucumber
  1. Crustless white sandwiches of creamy curried egg and cress, plus cucumber with salted butter and white pepper, cut into fingers and stacked on the tray. Boil 6 eggs for 10 minutes, cool in iced water, peel and mash with 3 tbsp mayonnaise, half a teaspoon of curry powder, salt and pepper. Butter 12 slices of soft white bread. Spread the egg on 4 slices, scatter a punnet of cress and top with 4 more. Slice 1 continental cucumber paper thin, salt lightly and pat dry, then layer on the last 4 slices with white pepper and close. Cut off the crusts and slice into fingers.

Mr Tuttle's garden potatoes and fish

Golden pan fried white fish with brown butter and capers, minty new potatoes.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 2 hours ahead and keep them in the pot; pan-fry the fish and brown the butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 1 sprig mint
  • 100g butter
  • 4 white fish fillets, such as flathead
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Boil the potatoes with the mint until tender and toss in salted butter, 20 min.
  2. Pan-fry the fish until golden, 8 min.
  3. Brown some butter, add the capers and lemon juice, and spoon over the fish, 7 min.

Candlelit lamb shepherd's pie

Lamb mince with carrot and peas under a golden buttery mash.

How it is made
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 tin tomato paste
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1.2kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Lamb mince slow-cooked with onion, carrot, peas, rosemary and a good splash of Worcestershire, under a crisp buttery mash crust baked golden. Brown 800g lamb mince in a hot pan, then add 1 diced onion and 2 diced carrots and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp plain flour, 1 tbsp Worcestershire, chopped rosemary and 400ml beef stock and simmer for 30 minutes until thick, adding 150g frozen peas at the end. Boil 1.2kg peeled potatoes, mash with 80g butter and 100ml hot milk. Spread the lamb in a baking dish, top with mash, rough it up with a fork and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes.

Curtains-drawn Jersey cream rice puddingVegetarian

Slow baked creamy rice pudding with vanilla and nutmeg.

Prep 5 min, cook 2 hr 5 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, cover in the fridge and reheat gently with a splash of milk; grate the nutmeg on top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g short grain rice
  • 800ml milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 70g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  1. Put the rice, milk, cream, sugar and vanilla bean in a baking dish.
  2. Bake at 150°C for 2 hours, stirring twice.
  3. Grate nutmeg on top. Serve warm, curtains drawn, 5 min.

Island apple cider toddy from the teapot

Dry apple cider warmed with honey, lemon zest, cinnamon and cloves.

Prep 7 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Warm the cider with the honey, lemon zest, cinnamon and cloves up to 1 day ahead; reheat gently, then add the brandy and lemon juice just before the teapot
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2 L dry apple cider
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, zest peeled and juice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 120ml brandy
  1. Pour the cider into a saucepan with the honey, lemon zest, cinnamon and cloves.
  2. Warm gently for 10 minutes without boiling.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the brandy and half the lemon juice, 3 min.
  4. Warm a teapot with hot water, tip it out, strain the cider into the pot and pour into teacups at the table, 4 min.
