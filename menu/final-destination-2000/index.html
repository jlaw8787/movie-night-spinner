Paris class trip croque monsieur bites

Ham and cheese croque monsieur bites with béchamel, the class trip's first taste of France.

Prep 2 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the béchamel up to 2 days ahead; build the sandwiches up to 4 hours ahead, then toast and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 8 slices white sandwich bread
  • 2 tsp dijon mustard
  • 150g sliced ham
  • 150g gruyère, grated
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and simmer to a thick béchamel. Season with the nutmeg and salt.
  2. Heat the grill. Spread 4 slices of bread with mustard, top with the ham and half the gruyère and close the sandwiches.
  3. Toast in a dry frying pan until golden on both sides.
  4. Spread the béchamel over the tops, scatter with the rest of the gruyère and grill until bubbling.
  5. Cut each into 4 squares.

Window seat French onion dipVegetarian

Slow-caramelised onion dip with crisps, packed for the long flight across the Atlantic.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead in the fridge, it's better after a night; serve with the crisps straight from the bag
Recipe for 4
  • 3 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250g sour cream
  • 125g cream cheese, soft
  • 1 bunch chives, snipped
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 200g plain salted crisps
  1. Cook the onions in the butter and oil with the salt over low heat, stirring often, until deep golden and jammy.
  2. Cool.
  3. Beat the sour cream and cream cheese until smooth and fold in the onions, most of the chives and the pepper.
  4. Chill so the flavours settle.
  5. Scatter with the rest of the chives and serve with the crisps.

Flight 180 coq au vin

The hot dinner service on the overnight route to Paris.

Prep 8 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Make up to 2 days ahead, the sauce improves; reheat gently and scatter the parsley on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thigh cutlets, skin on
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 12 small pickling onions, peeled
  • 250g button mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500ml red wine
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1 baguette
  1. Season the chicken with the salt and pepper and brown skin side down in the oil in a wide heavy pan, then turn, brown and lift out.
  2. Fry the bacon, onions and mushrooms in the same pan until golden, then add the garlic.
  3. Stir in the flour and tomato paste, pour in 500ml red wine and 250ml chicken stock, and add the thyme and bay.
  4. Return the chicken skin side up, cover and simmer gently.
  5. Uncover and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon.
  6. Scatter with parsley and serve with torn baguette.

Volée Airlines salmon en papillote

Fillets baked in paper with fennel, zucchini, lemon and dill, herbed rice, look both ways.

Prep 2 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make up the parcels up to 4 hours ahead and keep in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 zucchini
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 40g butter
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Slice the fennel, zucchini and 1 lemon thinly.
  2. Lay each salmon fillet on a sheet of baking paper on a bed of fennel and zucchini, top with lemon, dill and butter, season and fold into a tight parcel.
  3. Bake on a tray for 15 minutes.
  4. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, rest for 5, then stir through chopped parsley and the juice of the other lemon.
  5. Open the parcels at the table and serve with the rice.

Bludworth's funeral parlour madeleinesVegetarian

Honey and lemon butter sponges baked domed in shell tins, sugar dusted, with a quiet warning.

Prep 9 min, cook 18 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the batter up to 2 days ahead; bake just before serving, they're best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 110g butter, melted and cooled
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the eggs, caster sugar, honey and lemon zest until pale and thick.
  2. Fold in the flour and baking powder, then 100g of the melted butter.
  3. Cover and chill the batter.
  4. Heat the oven to 200°C and brush a madeleine tray with the rest of the butter.
  5. Spoon in the batter and bake until domed and golden.
  6. Turn out, dust with icing sugar and serve warm.

Drinks

Premonition French 75

A toast to Paris, raised before the seatbelt sign goes off.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemons and cut the twists up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 500ml sparkling wine, chilled
  • 1 cup ice
  • 1 lemon, peeled into twists
  1. Shake the gin, lemon juice and sugar syrup hard with the ice.
  2. Strain into 4 chilled flutes.
  3. Top with the sparkling wine and add a lemon twist.
