Snacks

Cleopatra Club grilled sandwiches

Bologna and cheese on griddled white.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Assemble the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, wrapped in the fridge; butter the outsides and griddle just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 20g yellow mustard
  • 120g devon
  • 80g American-style cheese slices
  • 30g butter, softened
  1. Spread the bread with mustard and layer devon and cheese between the slices to make sandwiches.
  2. Butter the outsides.
  3. Griddle under a weighted pan until crisp and the cheese melts, turning once. Work in batches, 12 min.
  4. Cut into fingers to share.

LA street tacos

Marinated beef grilled and chopped, warm corn tortillas, onion, coriander, salsa verde and lime.

Prep 10 min, cook 6 min, then 75 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the steak in the lime juice, garlic and cumin up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; grill and fill the tortillas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g skirt steak
  • 4 limes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 150g salsa verde
  1. Marinate the steak in lime juice, crushed garlic and cumin, 1 hr 5 min.
  2. Grill hot on both sides, 6 min.
  3. Rest the steak, then chop small, 10 min.
  4. Warm the tortillas and dice the onion, 5 min.
  5. Fill the tortillas with steak, onion, coriander and salsa verde, with lime wedges on the side.

Mains

Tango's tailored rib eye

Dry-brined, seared hard and basted with thyme butter, pulled at medium rare.

Prep 0 min, cook 25 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dry-brine 4 to 12 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rib eye steaks (about 200g each)
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 50g butter
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 3 garlic cloves
  1. Pat the steaks dry, season with salt and refrigerate uncovered to dry-brine, 4 hr 5 min.
  2. Heat a heavy pan with oil until hot and sear the steaks hard on both sides, in batches, 12 min.
  3. Lower the heat, add the butter, thyme and crushed garlic and baste. Take out at 54°C in the centre for medium rare.
  4. Rest the steaks, slice and spoon over the pan butter, 11 min.

Breakout glazed meatloaf and mash

Beef and pork loaf, sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, buttery mash and minted peas.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 onion
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with a sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, thick slices on buttery mash with minted peas. Mix 500g beef mince, 300g pork mince, 1 grated onion, 60g breadcrumbs, 1 egg, 2 tbsp Worcestershire and salt. Shape into a loaf in a lined tin. Mix 80ml tomato sauce with 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tbsp vinegar, spread over and bake at 180°C for 60 minutes. Boil 1kg potatoes, mash with 60g butter and 100ml hot milk. Cook 300g frozen peas 3 minutes and toss with butter and mint. Rest the loaf 10 minutes before slicing.

Kiki's cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with pink buttercream and sprinkles, for Kiki.

Prep 15 min, cook 28 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 40ml milk, for the cakes
  • 60g butter, softened, for icing
  • 120g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp milk, for icing
  • 1/4 tsp pink food colouring
  • 20g sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C with 8 cupcake cases in a tin. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the egg and vanilla, 10 min.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk, divide between the cases and bake, 18 min.
  3. Cool the cupcakes completely, 30 min.
  4. Beat the icing butter with the icing sugar and milk until fluffy and tint pink.
  5. Pipe onto the cupcakes and cover with sprinkles.
