Snacks

Oriental Saloon pickled eggsVegetarian

Hard boiled eggs pickled for 3 days in a beet and vinegar brine.

Prep 3 min, cook 30 min, then 3 days to rest or set · Make ahead: Three days ahead, keeps 2 weeks chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 250ml white vinegar
  • 125ml beetroot juice from a tin
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool and peel.
  2. Heat the vinegar, beetroot juice, sugar, mustard seeds and peppercorns.
  3. Pour over the eggs in a jar and leave in the fridge to pickle until pink.
  4. Drain and halve to serve.

Faro night cornbreadVegetarian

Cast iron cornbread, paid for by the Earps' stake in the saloon.

Prep 4 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 140g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Heat a buttered cast iron pan in a 220°C oven.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, buttermilk, egg and melted butter briefly.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake.
  4. Beat softened butter with the honey and serve alongside.

Mains

Earp brothers' chilli

Beef braised with dried chillies, hot enough to skin a smokewagon.

Prep 23 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 1 brown onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water, then blitz. Dice the beef, onion and garlic.
  2. Brown the beef in batches.
  3. Soften the onion and garlic, add the chillies, cumin and tomatoes.
  4. Braise covered until tender. Serve with the cornbread.

Big Nose Kate's chicken and dumplings

Thighs in thick broth, herbed buttermilk dough steamed on top, for Doc on a bad night.

Prep 16 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the stew up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; bring back to a simmer and add the dumplings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, in chunks
  • 30g butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery sticks, sliced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 180ml buttermilk
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, add the onion, carrot and celery and soften.
  2. Stir in the plain flour, then the stock and thyme, and simmer until the chicken is tender.
  3. Stir the self-raising flour, buttermilk, chopped parsley and a pinch of salt into a soft dough.
  4. Drop spoonfuls onto the simmering stew, cover tightly and cook without lifting the lid until the dumplings are puffed and cooked through.

Sweets

Josephine's peach pieVegetarian

Lattice peach pie for Josephine and the travelling theatre troupe.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 6 peaches
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Slice the peaches and toss with the sugar, lemon juice and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets, fill, and weave a lattice from the other two.
  3. Bake at 190°C.
  4. Cool slightly and serve with cream.

Drinks

Doc Holliday's rye old fashioned

Rye, demerara and orange bitters over one big ice cube.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Dissolve and cool the demerara syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; stir each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml rye whiskey
  • 2 tbsp demerara sugar
  • 8 dashes of orange bitters
  • 1 orange
  • large ice cubes, to serve
  1. Dissolve the demerara sugar in hot water and cool.
  2. Stir the whiskey, syrup and bitters with ice, one drink at a time.
  3. Strain over one large ice cube in a tumbler.
  4. Squeeze a strip of orange peel over the glass near a lit match so the oil flares, then drop it in.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Doc Holliday says “I'm your huckleberry.”
