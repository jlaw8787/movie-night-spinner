Snacks

Kelp forest seaweed crackersVegetarian

Crisp sesame nori, for a kelp forest that all looks the same

Prep 10 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 6 nori sheets
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Brush the nori lightly with sesame oil and sprinkle with sesame seeds and salt.
  2. Toast one at a time in a dry pan until crisp.
  3. Cut into strips with scissors.

Hank's camouflage fruit platterVegetarian

Watermelon, kiwi, berries and grapes in colour blocks, so Hank blends in

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Cut and arrange the fruit up to 3 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; serve with the yoghurt
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cup watermelon cubes
  • 250g strawberries, hulled
  • 2 kiwi fruit, sliced
  • 125g blueberries
  • 500g green grapes
  • 200g vanilla yoghurt
  1. Cut the watermelon, hull the strawberries and slice the kiwi fruit.
  2. Arrange the blueberries, green grapes and the cut fruit in colour blocks on a big platter so the fruit hides in patches.
  3. Serve with the yoghurt in a bowl for dipping.

Mains

California chicken bowls

Becky the loon brings a bucket. Lime chilli chicken over rice, avocado, corn, beans

Prep 19 min, cook 30 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill it and cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 3 limes
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 can corn kernels
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  1. Mix the chicken with lime juice, chilli and salt.
  2. Marinate the chicken.
  3. Grill the chicken until cooked through.
  4. Slice the chicken.
  5. Cook the rice.
  6. Build bowls of rice with chicken, sliced avocado, corn, black beans and a spoon of salsa.

Sweets

Dory blue jelly

Blue jelly, whipped cream, orange slice. Just keep swimming.

Prep 12 min, cook 6 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets blue jelly crystals
  • 1l boiling and cold water, as the packet says
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 oranges
  1. Make up the jelly following the packet, using the boiling and cold water as it says.
  2. Pour into glasses.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Whip the cream.
  5. Top with cream and tuck a slice of orange on each for a little orange fish.

Drinks

Blue lemonade ice cream floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Blue lemonade with a scoop of vanilla ice cream floating on top

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Best built just before serving; chill the lemonade and glasses up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cold lemonade
  • blue food colouring, a few drops
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Pour the lemonade into tall glasses and add a drop of blue colouring, stirring until ocean blue.
  2. Gently add a scoop of ice cream so it floats and foams.
  3. Serve straight away with a long spoon.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Hank sneaks Dory through the Marine Life Institute.
