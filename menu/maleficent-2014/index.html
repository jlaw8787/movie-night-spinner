Snacks

Berry ricotta brioche toastsVegetarian

Ricotta, honey and berries, for Beastie

Prep 16 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Whip the ricotta up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Toast the brioche and toss the berries just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g blackberries
  • 125g raspberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves, torn
  • 250g ricotta
  • 8 slices brioche
  1. Toss the blackberries and raspberries with the honey and torn mint.
  2. Whip the ricotta until smooth.
  3. Toast the brioche slices and cut into squares.
  4. Cut the squares.
  5. Spread with ricotta and spoon the berries on top just before serving.

Diaval's black olive tapenadeVegetarian

Black olives, dark as Diaval. Raven, wolf, horse, dragon

Prep 9 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Tapenade up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pitted black olives
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 baguette
  1. Blitz the olives, capers, garlic, lemon juice and a splash of oil until rough.
  2. Slice the baguette and brush with oil.
  3. Toast until crisp.
  4. Spread the tapenade thickly on the toasts.

Thorn wall parmesan pepper twistsOn the printed menu

Puff pastry with grated cheese and rosemary twisted into spiky sticks, baked crisp, smoky tomato dip

How it is made
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 60g parmesan
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 250ml passata
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 head garlic
  1. Puff pastry twisted with parmesan, black pepper and rosemary into spiky thorny sticks, baked crisp and golden, with a smoky tomato dip. Brush 2 thawed puff pastry sheets with beaten egg and scatter with 60g finely grated parmesan, 1 tsp cracked pepper and 1 tbsp chopped rosemary. Press in, cut into 1.5cm strips and twist each tightly. Bake on lined trays at 200°C for 12 minutes until crisp. For the dip, simmer 250ml passata with 1 tsp smoked paprika and 1 crushed garlic clove for 10 minutes.

Mains

King Stefan's crackling pork roast

Pork, crackling and apples. Stefan took her wings for a crown.

Prep 30 min, cook 4.5 hours, then 10.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the pork the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 4 green apples, halved
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  1. Score the pork skin and rub with salt.
  2. Leave uncovered in the fridge overnight to dry out.
  3. Roast at 160°C for the first part of the cooking.
  4. Chop the potatoes and halve the apples, toss with oil, and add to the pan.
  5. Roast for the last hour.
  6. Blast at 240°C until the crackling bubbles.
  7. Rest the pork.
  8. Steam the broccolini and serve on a long platter.

Godmother's cottage shepherd's pieOn the printed menu

Lamb mince and thyme under fluffy mash. "You're my Fairy Godmother," says Aurora.

How it is made
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 60g cheddar
  1. Lamb mince simmered with onion, carrot, thyme and Worcestershire until rich, under fluffy cheesy mash baked golden and crunchy on top. Brown 800g lamb mince, then add 1 diced onion and 2 diced carrots and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, thyme and 400ml beef stock, and simmer 25 minutes until thick. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml milk. Spread the lamb in a dish, top with mash, scatter with 60g cheddar and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes.

Aurora's christening cakeVegetarian

Jam and cream sponge. Wakes only to true love's kiss

Prep 42 min, cook 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 120g strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • pastel food colouring, a few drops
  • 1 punnet edible flowers
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour.
  2. Divide between 2 tins and bake at 175°C.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  5. Stack the layers with jam and cream.
  6. Mix the icing sugar and milk, tint in pastel colours, ice the cake and decorate with edible flowers.

Maleficent's green fire matcha lime fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Matcha, honey and lime over ice with cucumber ribbons

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Whisk the matcha with the water, honey and lime juice up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled, then stir before using; peel the cucumber ribbons up to 4 hours ahead. Fill the glasses and top with sparkling water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tsp matcha powder
  • 100ml warm water
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 limes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 1l sparkling water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Whisk the matcha with the warm water until smooth and frothy.
  2. Stir in the honey and the juice of 2 limes.
  3. Peel the cucumber into ribbons.
  4. Fill glasses with ice, cucumber ribbons and mint leaves.
  5. Divide the matcha mix between the glasses and top slowly with sparkling water so it swirls green.
  6. Stir gently and serve with a lime wheel.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Maleficent crashes the christening and curses baby Aurora.
