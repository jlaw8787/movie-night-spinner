Snacks

Stolen ATM hot links

Slow-grilled hot sausage slices on toothpicks with mustard and pickled jalapeños

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving, as the skins soften when reheated; the mustard and jalapenos can be set out earlier
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot smoked sausages
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 small jar pickled jalapenos
  1. Grill the sausages slowly over medium coals, turning often, until the skins blister and they are hot right through.
  2. Slice thickly on an angle and spear each piece with a toothpick.
  3. Serve with mustard and pickled jalapenos.

Chicago tamales

Spiced beef tamales in corn husks, steamed soft, with hot sauce

Prep 27 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, steam to reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 300g masa harina
  • 100g lard
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 400ml warm beef stock
  • 16 corn husks
  • 1 tsp hot sauce, to serve
  1. Soak the corn husks in hot water.
  2. Brown the mince with the diced onion, chilli powder and cumin.
  3. Beat the masa harina with lard, baking powder and warm stock to a soft dough.
  4. Spread the dough on the husks, add the beef, and roll up.
  5. Steam the tamales until the dough pulls away from the husk.
  6. Serve with hot sauce.

Calvin's barbershop rib tips

Paprika pork rib tips glazed with tangy barbecue sauce, piled over white bread

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Rub the ribs up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge. Roast them until tender up to 1 day ahead too, then glaze and finish hot on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork ribs, cut into short pieces
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Rub the ribs with paprika, brown sugar, crushed garlic and salt.
  2. Cover and roast at 150C until tender.
  3. Cut the potatoes into fries, toss in oil and bake at 220C until crisp.
  4. Uncover the ribs, brush with barbecue sauce and roast hot until sticky.
  5. Pile the ribs over slices of white bread with the oven fries.

Sweets

Sweet potato pieVegetarian

Spiced mashed sweet potato custard in a sweet pastry crust

Prep 8 min, cook 105 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  1. Roast the sweet potatoes until soft.
  2. Line a tin with the pastry and blind bake until pale golden.
  3. Mash the sweet potato with butter, sugar, eggs, evaporated milk, cinnamon and nutmeg.
  4. Pour into the case and bake at 170C until just set.
  5. Cool completely before slicing.

Drinks

Terri's apple ginger coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Ginger, apple and lime topped with soda, bottled with a name on each. Red sign: do not drink me.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Strain the ginger juice, add the apple and lime juice and keep it chilled in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; bottle, add ice and top with soda water on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cm knob of ginger
  • 1l cloudy apple juice
  • 3 limes
  • 500ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Grate the ginger and squeeze the juice into a big jug through a sieve.
  2. Add the apple juice and lime juice and stir.
  3. Pour into four bottles and add ice.
  4. Top each with soda water, write a name on each bottle in marker and serve with lime wedges.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Terri finds out someone drank her apple juice.
