Snacks

Ray's cornfield eloteVegetarian

Charred corn with lime mayo, feta and chilli

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 80g feta
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 bunch of coriander
  1. Husk the corn.
  2. Grill over high heat, turning, until charred in spots.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with lime zest.
  4. Brush the hot cobs all over with the mayonnaise, then roll in finely crumbled feta.
  5. Sprinkle with chilli powder and chopped coriander and serve with lime wedges.

Fenway sausage and peppers rolls

Grilled Italian sausage with capsicum and onion in a toasted roll

Prep 11 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Peppers up to 1 day ahead, reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 Italian sausages
  • 4 long rolls
  • 1 jar of wholegrain mustard
  1. Slice the capsicums and onions.
  2. Fry in the olive oil over medium heat until soft and sweet, seasoning with salt and oregano.
  3. Grill the sausages, turning, then split lengthways.
  4. Toast the long rolls.
  5. Fill with sausage, pile on the peppers and onions and finish with mustard.

Mains

Iowa breaded pork tenderloin sandwich

Crumbed pork fillet in a soft bun with pickles and slaw

Prep 20 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 2 hours ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork fillet
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g crackers
  • 70g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 5 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 bottle of American mustard
  • 1 jar of dill pickles
  • 1 onion
  • 500ml vegetable oil
  1. Cut the pork into pieces and pound each thin and wider than the bun.
  2. Dust in flour, dip in beaten egg and press into crushed crackers mixed with panko.
  3. Shallow fry in the vegetable oil at 180C until golden each side.
  4. Toss shredded cabbage with mayonnaise and vinegar.
  5. Serve in soft buns with mustard, mayo, pickles and sliced onion and the slaw on the side.

Annie's Iowa potato saladVegetarian

Dill, mustard, egg and celery, for a ball game at dusk

Prep 9 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; add the dill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg waxy potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until just tender, with the eggs in for the first 10 minutes.
  2. Drain, toss the warm potatoes with the vinegar and half the mustard and leave to cool.
  3. Peel and chop the eggs and dice the celery.
  4. Fold everything through the mayonnaise and the rest of the mustard, season and finish with chopped dill.

Kinsella farm Iowa apple pieVegetarian

Cinnamon Granny Smith apple pie with a pastry top

Prep 32 min, cook 50 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 Granny Smith apples
  • 110g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp salted caramel sauce
  1. Peel and slice the apples and toss with sugar, cinnamon, cornflour and lemon juice.
  2. Line a 23cm pie dish with pastry and fill.
  3. Cover with a second sheet, crimp and cut slits.
  4. Brush with egg and sprinkle with sugar.
  5. Bake at 200C.
  6. Reduce to 180C and bake until golden.
  7. Rest, then serve warm with ice cream and salted caramel sauce.

Shoeless Joe's strawberry lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh strawberry lemonade with mint

Prep 17 min, cook 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 300g strawberries
  • 7 lemons
  • 1 bunch of mint
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Simmer the sugar with water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Hull the strawberries and blend with the syrup, then strain.
  3. Juice the lemons and stir in the juice and cold water.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes.
  5. Pour over ice into tall glasses and garnish with sliced strawberries, lemon wheels and mint.
