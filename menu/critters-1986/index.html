Snacks

Brad's firecracker chicken wings

Crisp wings with a fiery glaze. A Krite swallows Brad's firecracker.

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings in the spice mix up to a day ahead and chill; bake and glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings, tips off and jointed
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 60ml hot sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 30g butter
  • 100g blue cheese, crumbled
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 celery sticks, cut in batons
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp and cooked through.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and honey and toss the hot wings through it.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream and serve with the wings and celery.

Rolling Krite corn fritter ballsVegetarian

Krites roll up and fire quills

Prep 18 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Roll the balls up to 4 hours ahead and chill; crumb in the corn chips and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 corn cobs, kernels cut off
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 100g plain corn chips, crushed
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  1. Mix the corn, flour, egg, spring onions, cheddar and a pinch of salt into a stiff batter.
  2. Roll into walnut-size balls with wet hands, then roll them in the crushed corn chips so they come up spiky.
  3. Shallow-fry in batches in the hot oil, turning, until golden all over.
  4. Stir the chives through the sour cream and serve with the balls.

Mains

Half-eaten cow burnt ends

Smoky glazed beef burnt ends. Krites ate half the cow.

Prep 20 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Roast the beef up to 2 days ahead and chill; cube, sauce and finish in the oven to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef brisket point
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 green cabbage, shredded
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 4 soft white rolls
  1. Rub the beef with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder, pepper and salt.
  2. Roast covered with the stock at 150°C until tender.
  3. Cut into 3cm cubes, toss with the barbecue sauce and honey and roast uncovered at 200°C until sticky and charred.
  4. Mix the cabbage with the mayo and vinegar.
  5. Serve the burnt ends with the slaw and rolls.

Bowling alley chilli with cornbread

Beef chilli and cornbread, as served before the bowling alley panic

Prep 17 min, cook 93 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead; it is better reheated. Bake the cornbread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef mince
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 125g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the beef in the oil with the onion and garlic.
  2. Add the chilli powder and cumin, then the tomatoes and beans, and simmer gently until thick and dark.
  3. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, eggs, buttermilk, melted butter and a pinch of salt and pour into a lined 20cm tin.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden and springy.
  5. Serve the chilli topped with cheddar and squares of buttered cornbread.

Brown family buttermilk pancakesVegetarian

Buttermilk pancakes at the Brown farm, before Charlie's warning

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Whisk the dry mix up to a week ahead; add the wet and cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 225g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 60g butter, melted, plus 60g softened
  • 125ml maple syrup
  • 150g blueberries
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, bicarb, sugar and a pinch of salt.
  2. Whisk the eggs, buttermilk and melted butter, then fold into the dry mix until just combined.
  3. Cook ladlefuls in a buttered pan until bubbles show, flip and cook until golden.
  4. Beat the softened butter with 2 tablespoons of the maple until fluffy and serve on the stack with the rest of the maple and the blueberries.

Charlie's whiskey sourVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and egg white shaken frothy. Charlie's bottle goes Molotov.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to a day ahead; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 120ml fresh lemon juice
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 2 egg whites
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Shake the bourbon, lemon, syrup and egg white hard without ice, then again with ice.
  2. Strain into 4 glasses and drop a cherry in each.
