Snacks

6000 SUX popcornVegetarian

Popcorn with melted butter, cheddar powder and smoked paprika

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 4 tbsp cheddar cheese powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • pinch of salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot, shaking until the popping slows.
  2. Toss with the melted butter, then the cheese powder, paprika and salt until every piece is orange.
  3. Serve in a big bowl.

OCP boardroom canapés

Smoked salmon and dill on rye, plus cucumber cups filled with whipped feta

Prep 30 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 slices rye bread
  • 20g butter
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 150g feta
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Butter the rye, cut into squares and top with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and dill.
  2. Cut the cucumbers into thick rounds and scoop a small hollow in each.
  3. Whip the feta with the olive oil until smooth and fill the cucumber cups.
  4. Arrange on a platter.

Mains

Detroit coney dog

Frank in a steamed bun with beef chilli, mustard and diced white onion

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 200ml water
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  1. Dice the onions, keeping half for topping.
  2. Brown the mince with half the onion, chilli powder and cumin.
  3. Add the water and simmer until loose and saucy.
  4. Griddle the franks and steam the buns.
  5. Fill the buns with franks and top with chilli, mustard and diced raw onion.

Sweets

Nukem cupcakesVegetarian

Chocolate cupcakes topped with tall piped meringue, toasted with a torch

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Cupcakes up to 1 day, torch the meringue at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 30g cocoa
  • 125ml full cream milk
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line a 12-hole tin. Beat the butter and half the sugar until pale, then beat in two eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa and milk, divide between the cases and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Whisk three egg whites with the remaining sugar over hot water until warm and glossy, then beat to stiff peaks.
  5. Pipe tall on each cupcake and torch the tops.

Antonowsky's gas station boilermakerVegetarian

Rye whiskey shot with bitters and lemon peel, chased with cold lager

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Chill the lagers in the fridge and cut the lemon twists up to 1 day ahead; pour the shots to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lagers (375ml)
  • 120ml rye whiskey
  • 4 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 lemon
  1. Chill the lagers and four shot glasses in the freezer.
  2. Pour the whiskey into the shot glasses with a dash of bitters and a small lemon twist.
  3. Serve each shot alongside a cold lager poured into a chilled glass.
