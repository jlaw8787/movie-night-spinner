6000 SUX popcornVegetarian

Popcorn with melted butter, cheddar powder and smoked paprika.

Prep 9 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 4 tbsp cheddar cheese powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • pinch of salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot, shaking until the popping slows.
  2. Toss with the melted butter, then the cheese powder, paprika and salt until every piece is orange.
  3. Serve in a big bowl.

OCP boardroom canapés

Smoked salmon and dill on rye, plus cucumber cups filled with feta dip.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 slices of rye bread
  • 20g butter
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 150g feta
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Butter the rye, cut into squares and top with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and dill.
  2. Cut the cucumbers into thick rounds and scoop a small hollow in each.
  3. Whip the feta with the olive oil until smooth and fill the cucumber cups.
  4. Arrange on a platter.

Old Detroit pizza squares

Oiled-pan base, cheese to the edges, tomato striped over.

How it is made
  • 400g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • olive oil
  • 100g sliced pepperoni
  • 300g low-moisture mozzarella
  • 100g cheddar
  • 250ml pizza sauce
  1. Detroit-style square pizza baked in a hot oiled pan, with cheese right to the edges for a crisp, dark crust and tomato sauce striped on top. Mix 400g bread flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp salt, 300ml warm water and 2 tbsp olive oil, then prove for 1 hour. Press into an oiled 23 x 33cm tin and prove 30 minutes more. Scatter over 100g sliced pepperoni, then 300g cubed mozzarella and 100g cubed cheddar right to the edges. Bake at 250°C for 15 to 18 minutes until the edges are dark and crisp. Stripe warm pizza sauce over the top and cut into 12 squares.

Detroit coney dog

Frank in a steamed bun with beef chilli, mustard and diced white onion.

Prep 18 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 200ml water
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  1. Dice the onions, keeping half for topping.
  2. Brown the mince with half the onion, chilli powder and cumin, 8 min.
  3. Add the water and simmer until loose and saucy.
  4. Griddle the franks and steam the buns, 5 min.
  5. Fill the buns with franks and top with chilli, mustard and diced raw onion.

Murphy's mushroom soup and grilled cheese sandwiches

Silky mushroom soup with crisp grilled cheese sandwiches. Real food, not the precinct paste.

How it is made
  • 2 onions
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 70g butter
  • 600g mushrooms
  • 800ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 150g gruyère
  1. Silky mushroom soup blitzed with thyme and cream until smooth as paste, served with crisp gruyère and onion toasties for dunking. Soften 1 diced onion and 2 garlic cloves in 40g butter. Add 600g sliced mushrooms and cook 10 minutes until browned. Add 800ml chicken stock and thyme and simmer 20 minutes. Blitz until smooth and stir in 150ml cream. Butter 8 slices sourdough, fill with 150g grated gruyère and thin sliced onion, and toast in a pan for 3 minutes a side. Cut into fingers for dunking.

Nukem cupcakesVegetarian

Chocolate cupcakes topped with tall piped meringue, toasted with a torch.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cupcakes up to 1 day, torch the meringue at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 30g cocoa
  • 125ml full cream milk
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 12-hole tin. Beat the butter and half the sugar until pale, then beat in two eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa and milk, divide between the cases and bake, 20 min.
  3. Cool completely, 15 min.
  4. Whisk three egg whites with the remaining sugar over hot water until warm and glossy, then beat to stiff peaks.
  5. Pipe tall on each cupcake and torch the tops.

Antonowsky's gas station boilermaker

Rye whiskey shot with bitters and lemon peel, chased with cold lager.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the lagers in the fridge and cut the lemon twists up to 1 day ahead; pour the shots to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lagers (375ml)
  • 120ml rye whiskey
  • 4 dashes of aromatic bitters
  • 1 lemon
  1. Chill the lagers and four shot glasses in the freezer, 15 min.
  2. Pour the whiskey into the shot glasses with a dash of bitters and a small lemon twist.
  3. Serve each shot alongside a cold lager poured into a chilled glass.
