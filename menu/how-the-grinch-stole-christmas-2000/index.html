Snacks

Whoville Christmas wreaths

Green marshmallow corn flake wreaths with red candy berries.

Prep 8 min, cook 16 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 50g butter
  • 200g marshmallows
  • 3 drops green food colouring
  • 120g corn flakes
  • 24 small red lollies
  1. Melt the butter and marshmallows with the green colouring.
  2. Stir through the corn flakes until coated.
  3. Shape spoonfuls into little wreaths on baking paper.
  4. Press on red lollies as berries.
  5. Set.

Mount Crumpit cheesy pull-apart breadVegetarian

Cob loaf cut and stuffed with garlic butter, mozzarella and cheddar.

Prep 6 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1 round cob loaf
  • 80g butter
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Cut the cob in a criss cross, not quite through the base.
  2. Melt the butter with the garlic, parsley and chives.
  3. Push the grated mozzarella and cheddar into the cuts and drizzle over the butter.
  4. Wrap in foil and bake.
  5. Unwrap and bake until bubbling and golden.

Mains

Roast beast

Beef rib roast, roast potatoes, Yorkshire puddings and gravy.

Prep 0 min, cook 2.5 hours, then 8.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Season the beef the night before and leave it uncovered in the fridge; rest for 20 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400ml beef stock
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the beef well, overnight if possible.
  2. Roast at 220°C, then drop to 160°C until 52°C inside.
  3. Rest the beef under foil.
  4. Roast the potatoes in oil.
  5. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk and bake in hot oiled tins at 220°C.
  6. Make gravy with the stock and pan juices.

Cindy Lou's Who-hash with fried eggs

Corned beef and potato crisped in butter, runny eggs, before his heart grows three sizes.

How it is made
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 40g butter
  • 400g corned beef
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  1. Corned beef, potato and onion fried hard in butter until crusty, topped with runny fried eggs, pickled jalapeño and a tangy green herb sauce. Boil 800g diced potatoes for 6 minutes and drain. Fry 1 diced onion in 40g butter, add the potatoes and fry for 10 minutes, pressing down. Add 400g diced corned beef and fry until crusty, turning in sections. Blend parsley, chives, 1 garlic clove, lemon and 80ml oil for sauce. Fry 4 eggs. Serve the hash with eggs, sauce and pickled jalapeño.

Who puddingVegetarian

Fruit loaf baked in a vanilla egg custard until puffed and golden.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of fruit loaf, buttered
  • 4 eggs
  • 400ml milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Layer the buttered fruit loaf in a dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream, sugar and vanilla and pour over.
  3. Rest.
  4. Bake until puffed and golden.

Grinch green punchNo alcohol

Lime sorbet, lemonade and pineapple juice in a frothy green punch.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best scooped and poured just before serving; chill the lemonade and pineapple juice up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml lime sorbet
  • 1 L lemonade
  • 250ml pineapple juice
  • 2 limes, sliced
  1. Scoop the sorbet into a punch bowl.
  2. Pour over the lemonade and pineapple juice so it froths up green.
  3. Add lime slices and ladle into cups.

When to serve

Serve the roast beast when the Whos sit down to their Christmas feast.
