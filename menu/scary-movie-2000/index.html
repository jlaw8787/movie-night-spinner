Snacks

Wassup buffalo wings

Hot wings, blue cheese dip, celery.

Prep 7 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Bake the wings up to a day ahead and chill; reheat in a hot oven and toss in the sauce to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings, tips off, split
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 125ml Frank's RedHot sauce
  • 60g butter
  • 100g blue cheese, crumbled
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 celery sticks, cut in batons
  1. Pat the wings dry, toss with the baking powder and salt and spread on a rack over a tray.
  2. Bake at 220°C, turning once, until crisp and deep golden, 40 min.
  3. Melt the butter into the hot sauce in a big bowl, 3 min.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream, mayo and a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Toss the hot wings in the sauce and pile up with the dip and celery.

Shorty's basement nachosVegetarian

Loaded black bean nachos, cheese, jalapeños.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the beans and the salsa up to a day ahead; build and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 230g bag corn chips
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 200g grated tasty cheese
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 lime
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  • 60g pickled jalapeños
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Warm the beans with the cumin, paprika and a splash of water, mashing about half of them, 6 min.
  2. Mix the tomato, onion, coriander and lime juice with a pinch of salt for the salsa.
  3. Layer the chips, beans and cheese twice in a big ovenproof dish.
  4. Bake at 200°C until the cheese is bubbling, 10 min.
  5. Top with salsa, jalapeños and sour cream and get it to the table fast.

Shakespeare in Love parmesan popcornVegetarian

Parmesan butter popcorn, chilli salt. Brenda talked all the way through the film.

Prep 5 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Grate the parmesan and mix the chilli salt up to 3 days ahead; pop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g popping corn
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Grind the chilli flakes and salt together.
  2. Heat the oil in a big lidded pot, add the corn, cover and shake until the popping slows right down, 6 min.
  3. Melt the butter and pour it over the popcorn, tossing as you go, 3 min.
  4. Toss through the parmesan and chilli salt and tip into a big bowl.

Special Officer Doofy's sloppy joes

Beef sloppy joe rolls, dill pickles, slaw.

Prep 3 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and reheat; toast the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 1 green capsicum, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 250ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 125ml water
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 200g coleslaw mix
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  1. Brown the mince hard in a big pan, breaking it up, then add the onion, capsicum and garlic and cook until soft, 12 min.
  2. Stir in the tomato sauce, sugar, mustard, vinegar and water and simmer until thick and glossy, 15 min.
  3. Toss the coleslaw mix with the mayo and a pinch of salt.
  4. Toast the buns, pile on the filling and top with pickles and slaw, 5 min.

I KNOW pumpkin mac and cheeseVegetarian

Roast pumpkin, three cheeses, crunchy crumb.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 8 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to a day ahead and chill; add the crumb and bake to serve, 10 minutes longer from cold
Recipe for 4
  • 600g butternut pumpkin, cubed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g macaroni
  • 50g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 750ml milk
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g gruyère, grated
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 6 sage leaves
  1. Roast the pumpkin in the oil at 210°C until soft and caramel at the edges, then mash half of it, 25 min.
  2. Boil the macaroni 2 minutes short of the packet time and drain.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until thick. Off the heat stir in the cheddar, gruyère, mustard and mashed pumpkin, 10 min.
  4. Fold in the pasta and the pumpkin cubes and tip into a baking dish.
  5. Top with the panko, parmesan and sage and bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling, 18 min.

Hide-and-seek brownie sundaesVegetarian

Hot fudge brownies under vanilla ice cream, eaten on the couch, eyes on the floor.

Prep 8 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Bake the brownies up to 3 days ahead and keep them airtight; warm and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 100g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Melt the dark chocolate and butter together, then beat in the sugar and the eggs one at a time, 8 min.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa and a pinch of salt and spread in a lined 20cm tin.
  3. Bake at 170°C until just set with a fudgy middle, then cool a little, 25 min.
  4. Heat the cream until steaming and stir in the milk chocolate for the hot fudge, 4 min.
  5. Cut warm brownies, add a scoop of ice cream, pour over the fudge and top with a cherry, 4 min.

Drinks

Garage fridge micheladas

Cold lager, lime, hot sauce, chilli salt rim. Tina went to the garage for beer.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill the beer and the tomato juice well; mix the rim salt up to a week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 355ml bottles of Mexican lager
  • 250ml tomato juice
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tsp Tabasco
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Mix the salt and chilli powder on a saucer, run a lime wedge round 4 glass rims and dip them.
  2. Squeeze the limes and mix the juice with the tomato juice and Tabasco.
  3. Fill the glasses with ice, add the tomato mix and top slowly with the lager.
