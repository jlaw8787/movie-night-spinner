Snacks

Spanakopita trianglesVegetarian

Spinach and feta filo triangles with dill, baked crisp, as the Melbourne Greek cafe makes them

Prep 30 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spinach
  • 200g feta, crumbled
  • 200g ricotta
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 egg
  • 1 packet of filo pastry
  • 100g butter, melted
  1. Heat the oven to 190C. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Mix with the feta, ricotta, dill, spring onion and egg.
  3. Cut buttered filo into strips, add filling and fold into triangles.
  4. Brush with butter and bake.

Get tzatziki with it, taramasalata and warm pitaVegetarian

Grated cucumber, yoghurt, garlic and dill, creamy fish roe dip, olives and soft flatbread

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Dips up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 500g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tub of taramasalata
  • 6 pita breads
  • 1 bowl olives
  1. Grate the cucumber and squeeze out the water.
  2. Mix into the yoghurt with the garlic, dill and salt.
  3. Spoon into a bowl beside the taramasalata.
  4. Warm the pita in the oven and cut into wedges.
  5. Serve with olives.

Steve's flaming saganakiOn the printed menu

Kefalograviera floured and fried crusty, flamed with brandy at the table, lemon, honey, oregano

How it is made
  • 400g kefalograviera
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 30ml brandy
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Kefalograviera dusted in flour and fried in olive oil until crusty, flamed at the table with a splash of brandy, then hit with lemon, honey and oregano. Cut 400g kefalograviera or haloumi into 2cm thick slabs, wet with water and dust in plain flour. Fry in 3 tbsp hot olive oil for 2 minutes a side until a deep golden crust forms. Move the pan to the table on a board, pour over 30ml warmed brandy and carefully light it, standing back. When the flame dies, squeeze over a lemon, drizzle with 1 tbsp honey and sprinkle with dried oregano. Serve with warm pita.

Mains

Karamitsis family lamb on the spit

Lemon and oregano lamb. Not a plaster lion.

Prep 40 min, cook 5 hours · Make ahead: Rest 20 minutes before pulling
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 6 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 2 tbsp dried oregano
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in wedges
  1. Rub the lamb with the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, oil and salt.
  2. Roast covered at 150C.
  3. Add the potato wedges to the pan for the last 90 minutes.
  4. Uncover to brown.
  5. Rest and serve.

Pork souvlaki wraps with chips insideOn the printed menu

Lemon oregano skewers in warm pita with tzatziki, tomato, onion and hot salty chips, rolled tight

How it is made
  • 800g pork shoulder
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 400g frozen chips
  • 4 large pita breads
  • 250g tzatziki
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Lemon and oregano pork skewers charred hot, wrapped in warm pita with tzatziki, tomato, onion and a handful of salty chips inside, Sydney late-night style. Marinate 800g diced pork shoulder in 4 tbsp olive oil, the juice of 2 lemons, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp dried oregano and salt for 2 hours. Thread onto skewers and grill over high heat for 10 to 12 minutes, turning. Bake 400g frozen chips at 220C for 20 minutes and salt well. Warm 4 large pita breads, spread with tzatziki and fill with pork, sliced tomato, red onion, parsley and chips, then roll tight in paper.

Loukoumades, Greek honey doughnutsVegetarian

Honey puffs with walnuts, the bingo thank you

Prep 70 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g honey
  • 100ml water, for the syrup
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 60g walnuts, chopped
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and water into a sticky batter and rest until bubbly.
  2. Warm the honey with the water and cinnamon for the syrup.
  3. Heat the oil to 175C.
  4. Drop in spoonfuls of batter and fry until puffed and golden.
  5. Toss in warm syrup and scatter with walnuts.

Drinks

Greek coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Greek coffee, slow in a small pot

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best served fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp finely ground Greek coffee
  • 4 tsp sugar, to taste
  • 4 small cups cold water
  1. Put the coffee, sugar and water in a small pot.
  2. Heat slowly, stirring once, until it foams up to the rim.
  3. Pour into small cups and let the grounds settle before sipping.
