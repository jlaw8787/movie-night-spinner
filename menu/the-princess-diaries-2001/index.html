Snacks

Grandma's first corn dog

Cocktail franks in cornmeal batter, deep fried on skewers, mustard.

Prep 7 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 24 cocktail franks
  • 24 skewers or sticks
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, baking powder and buttermilk to a thick batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push a skewer into each.
  3. Dip in the batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches.
  4. Serve with mustard.

Genovian pear crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette, blue cheese, sliced pear, honey and walnuts.

Prep 12 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 150g blue cheese
  • 2 pears
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 50g walnuts, chopped
  1. Slice the baguette and toast until golden.
  2. Top each with a little blue cheese and a thin slice of pear.
  3. Drizzle with honey and scatter with chopped walnuts.

Mains

State dinner chicken

Chicken breast stuffed with spinach and ricotta, lemon butter, potato gratin.

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Stuff up to 1 day ahead, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 2 lemons
  • 100g butter
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and mix with the ricotta.
  2. Cut a pocket into each chicken breast and fill.
  3. Slice the potatoes, layer in a dish with cream, garlic and seasoning, and bake alongside the chicken at 200°C.
  4. Sear the chicken in the olive oil, then roast with the gratin.
  5. Melt the butter, whisk in lemon juice and spoon over the chicken.

Beach party crispy fish tacos

Soda battered white fillets fried crisp in tortillas, lime cabbage, pickled onion and chipotle crema.

How it is made
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 1 red onion
  • 4 limes
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml soda water
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Soda-battered white fish fried crisp in warm tortillas with lime slaw, pickled red onion and a chipotle crema, plenty of coriander on top. Slice 1 red onion thin and cover with 80ml lime juice and a pinch of salt for 20 minutes. Shred a quarter of a cabbage and toss with lime and salt. Mix 150g sour cream with 1 tsp chipotle in adobo. Whisk 150g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder and 250ml cold soda water. Cut 600g flathead fillets into strips, dip in the batter and fry at 180°C for 3 minutes. Warm 8 tortillas and fill with slaw, fish, onion, crema and coriander.

Palate cleanser sorbetVegetarian

Lemon sorbet over tender poached pear with fresh mint.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Pears up to 1 day, scoop fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 pears
  • 250ml water
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml lemon sorbet
  • 4 mint leaves
  1. Slice the pears and simmer in the water with the sugar until tender, then cool.
  2. Chill small glasses. Scoop sorbet into the glasses.
  3. Add a few pear slices and a mint leaf.

Genovian pear fizzNo alcohol

Pear juice and sparkling water over ice with a squeeze of lemon.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L pear juice
  • 1 L sparkling water
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Fill glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in pear juice to halfway and top with cold sparkling water.
  3. Squeeze in a little lemon and stir once.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the sorbet arrives at the state dinner.
