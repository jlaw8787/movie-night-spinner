Kingston stamp and go saltfish fritters

Crisp Jamaican salt cod fritters with spring onion, chilli and thyme, with lime.

Prep 5 min, cook 47 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the salt cod overnight, changing the water twice, and flake it up to 1 day ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g salt cod
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 180ml water
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 small red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tomato, finely diced
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  1. Soak the salt cod in cold water in the fridge overnight, changing the water twice.
  2. Simmer the cod in fresh water for 15 minutes, drain, then flake it, picking out any skin and bones.
  3. Whisk the flour, baking powder and water into a thick batter.
  4. Stir in the cod, spring onions, chilli, thyme, garlic, tomato and pepper.
  5. Heat the oil to 180°C in a deep heavy pan.
  6. Fry heaped spoonfuls in batches for 3 minutes, turning, until puffed and golden. Drain on paper towel.
  7. Serve hot with the lime wedges.

Crab Key plantain chipsVegetarian

Thin green plantain chips fried crisp, tossed with lime zest, chilli and sea salt.

Prep 18 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Fry up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight; season again just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains or green cooking bananas
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 lime
  1. Cut the ends off the plantains, score the skin along the ridges and prise it off.
  2. Slice the plantains 2mm thick on a mandoline.
  3. Heat the oil to 175°C in a deep heavy pan.
  4. Fry in batches for about 3 minutes until crisp and pale gold, then drain on paper towel.
  5. Toss with the salt, chilli powder and the zest and a squeeze of the lime.

Quarrel's jerk chicken with rice and peas

Chicken thighs marinated in scotch bonnet, allspice and thyme, roasted and charred.

Prep 13 min, cook 73 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken at least 4 hours or overnight in the fridge; cook the rice and peas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken thighs, bone in, skin on
  • 6 spring onions
  • 1 habanero chilli, seeded
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 4 tsp ground allspice
  • 3 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 300g long grain rice, rinsed
  • 400g tin red kidney beans, drained
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 200ml water
  1. Blitz 4 of the spring onions, the chilli, 4 garlic cloves, the ginger, 3 tsp of the allspice, 2 tsp of the thyme, the cinnamon, nutmeg, sugar, soy sauce, juice of 1 lime, oil, pepper and 1 tsp of the salt to a paste.
  2. Slash the chicken to the bone, rub all over with the marinade and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Roast the chicken on a rack for 40 minutes, then char on a hot barbecue or under the grill for 5 minutes.
  4. For the rice and peas, bring the coconut milk, water, the last 2 spring onions bashed whole, the last garlic clove crushed, the remaining allspice, thyme and salt to a simmer. Add the rice and beans, cover and cook on low for 18 minutes, then stand for 5.
  5. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes and serve with the rice and peas and the second lime in wedges.

Honey Ryder's coconut totoVegetarian

Spiced coconut and brown sugar cake with a crunchy coconut top and lime whipped cream.

Prep 8 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; whip the cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 100g desiccated coconut
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 80g butter, melted, plus extra for the tin
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp demerara sugar
  • 2 tbsp shredded coconut
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 lime, zested
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and butter a 20cm square tin.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, brown sugar and desiccated coconut.
  3. Whisk the egg, coconut milk, melted butter and vanilla and stir into the dry mix.
  4. Spread into the tin and scatter with the demerara and shredded coconut.
  5. Bake for 35 minutes until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Cool in the tin for 20 minutes.
  7. Whip the cream with the lime zest to soft peaks and serve with the toto cut into squares.

Puss-Feller's planter's punch

Dark rum, fresh lime, sugar syrup and pineapple over ice with bitters and grated nutmeg.

Prep 6 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix and chill the punch in a jug up to 4 hours ahead; pour over ice and grate the nutmeg to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml lime juice, freshly squeezed
  • 120ml sugar syrup
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 240ml pineapple juice
  • aromatic bitters, 4 dashes
  • 3 cups ice
  • whole nutmeg, for grating
  • 1 lime, sliced into wheels
  1. Stir the lime juice, sugar syrup, rum, pineapple juice and bitters together in a jug.
  2. Chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Pour over ice in 4 glasses, grate over a little nutmeg and add a lime wheel.
