Snacks

Honour guard yaki onigiriVegetarian

Rice balls brushed with miso and soy, grilled crisp over the coals. Four guards went to New York, four turtles came back in their armour

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Shape up to 4 hours ahead, grill before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cup short grain rice
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 2 nori sheets
  1. Cook the rice and cool until you can handle it.
  2. With wet salted hands, press into 8 firm triangles.
  3. Mix the miso, soy and sugar.
  4. Grill the onigiri 4 minutes a side until crisp, brushing with the glaze at the end, then wrap the base in nori.

Charcoal chicken skewers with tare

Chicken thigh and spring onion skewers grilled over coals, basted with soy and mirin tare. Uh, o-hi-o, wasabi

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Thread up to a day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 80ml mirin
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Simmer the soy, mirin and sugar until syrupy.
  2. Cut the chicken and spring onions and thread onto soaked skewers.
  3. Grill over high heat, turning and basting with the tare at the end.

Mains

Mitsu's village hotpot

Chicken, daikon, shiitake and cabbage simmered in kombu dashi with soy, a pot in the middle of the table. Sixty hours to get home, time for one hotpot

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the dashi a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 piece kombu
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1/2 daikon
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 150g fresh shiitake
  • 1/4 Chinese cabbage
  • 300g firm tofu
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 2 cup short grain rice
  1. Soak the kombu in 1.5L water, bring to a simmer and lift it out. Add the soy and mirin.
  2. Simmer the daikon until starting to soften.
  3. Add the chicken and simmer.
  4. Add the shiitake, cabbage and tofu, cook through and finish with spring onions. Serve at the table with rice.

Sweets

Kinako dango with brown sugar syrupVegetarian

Soft rice flour dumplings rolled in roasted soybean flour, dark brown sugar syrup poured over. For Splinter's Elvis at the end

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the syrup a week ahead. Cook the dango on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark brown sugar
  • 200g glutinous rice flour
  • 40g kinako (roasted soybean flour)
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Simmer the brown sugar with 80ml water until syrupy and cool.
  2. Mix the rice flour with water to a soft dough and roll into 24 dimpled balls.
  3. Boil until they float, 1 minute more, then into iced water.
  4. Roll in kinako and sugar, skewer and pour the syrup over.

Drinks

Cold roasted barley teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Roasted barley steeped and chilled, nutty and clean. I told you these disguises weren't gonna work

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 60g pearl barley
  1. Toast the barley in a dry pan until dark golden.
  2. Simmer in 1.5L water, cool, strain and chill.
  3. Serve over ice.

When to serve

Serve the hotpot when the turtles follow Mitsu to her village.
