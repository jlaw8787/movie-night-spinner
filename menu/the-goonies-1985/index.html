Doubloon cheese coinsVegetarian

Crisp cheddar shortbread coins with smoked paprika and a little cayenne.

Prep 0 min, cook 24 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days, baked coins up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  • 1 pinch smoked paprika
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Grate the cheddar. Rub the butter into the flour, add the cheese, smoked paprika and cayenne and bring together.
  2. Roll into a log and chill, 1 hr.
  3. Slice into coins and bake at 180°C until golden.

Astoria clam chowder cups

Creamy clam and potato chowder in mugs, straight from the Astoria docks.

Prep 6 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Make the base up to a day ahead without the clam meat; reheat gently and add the clams to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg fresh clams (vongole), purged
  • 4 bacon rashers, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml milk
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 packet oyster crackers
  1. Steam the clams in a covered pot with 250ml water for 4 to 5 minutes until open, then strain and keep the liquid. Pick out the meat and throw away any that stay shut.
  2. Fry the bacon in a large pot until crisp, then add the onion and celery and soften for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the clam liquid, milk, potatoes and bay leaves.
  4. Simmer gently for 15 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
  5. Stir in the cream and clam meat and heat through without boiling. Season with pepper, 4 min.
  6. Serve in mugs with oyster crackers.

Mains

Fratelli family pizza

Thin crust pizza with Italian sausage, mushrooms, mozzarella and garlic tomato sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Mix the tomato sauce up to 2 days ahead and prep the toppings up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stretch, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 Italian sausages
  • 150g mushrooms
  • 250g mozzarella
  1. Heat a pizza steel in the oven at its maximum, 30 min.
  2. Mix the tomatoes with crushed garlic and oregano. Squeeze the sausage from its skins, slice the mushrooms and grate the mozzarella.
  3. Stretch the dough into two thin bases and top with sauce, sausage, mushrooms and cheese.
  4. Bake on the hot steel at maximum heat, 7 min.

Goon Docks crispy fish and chips

Crunchy battered fish in warm tortillas with lime slaw, for Rosalita and the marble bag.

How it is made
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 limes
  • 1 small red cabbage
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml soda water
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 1.5L vegetable oil
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  • 1 avocado
  1. Flathead in a crunchy soda water batter, piled into warm tortillas with lime slaw, pickled onion, avocado and lime, a pirate map's worth of crunch. Pickle 1 sliced red onion in the juice of 2 limes with salt for 20 minutes. Toss a quarter red cabbage, shredded, with 100g mayonnaise and 1 tsp chipotle in adobo. Whisk 150g plain flour with 250ml cold soda water and salt. Cut 600g flathead into strips, dip and fry at 180°C for 3 minutes. Warm 12 flour tortillas and fill with fish, slaw, onion, sliced avocado and lime.

Chunk's chocolate trufflesVegetarian

Dark chocolate cream truffles rolled in cocoa, crushed peanuts or sprinkles.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 20g butter
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 60g peanuts
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles, to coat
  1. Chop the chocolate. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate and butter. Stir smooth, 10 min.
  2. Chill until firm.
  3. Crush the peanuts. Scoop and roll into balls, then coat in cocoa, peanuts or sprinkles.

Pirate ship treasure chest cakeVegetarian

Chocolate loaf cake hollowed out and filled with chocolate coins, under ganache.

Prep 10 min, cook 77 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 200g chocolate coins
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Melt part of the chocolate with the butter, whisk in the eggs and sugar and fold in the flour, 15 min.
  2. Bake in a loaf tin at 170°C, 50 min.
  3. Cool the cake completely, 40 min.
  4. Cut off the top and hollow out the middle, fill with chocolate coins and replace the lid.
  5. Heat the cream, pour over the remaining chocolate to make ganache, cool slightly and cover the cake, 12 min.

Sloth's peanut caramel nougat bars

Peanut nougat and caramel bars dipped in chocolate, Baby Ruth style, Sloth's favourite.

How it is made
  • 200g marshmallows
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 150g roasted peanuts
  • 200g soft caramels
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • 300g milk chocolate
  1. Chewy peanut nougat and salted caramel loaded with roasted peanuts, dipped in milk chocolate and cut into fat bars, the way Sloth would want them. Melt 200g marshmallows with 40g butter and 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter, then stir through 150g roasted peanuts and press into a lined 20cm tin. Melt 200g soft caramels with 2 tbsp cream and a pinch of salt and pour over. Chill 1 hour. Cut into 12 bars, dip in 300g melted milk chocolate and set on baking paper in the fridge for 30 minutes.

Chunk's root beer floatNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream topped with root beer until it foams, served with long spoons.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the root beer and the glasses up to 1 day ahead; scoop and pour just before serving so it foams
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 375ml cans of root beer
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  1. Put two scoops of ice cream in each of four tall glasses.
  2. Pour root beer slowly over each until it foams to the top. Serve with long spoons.
