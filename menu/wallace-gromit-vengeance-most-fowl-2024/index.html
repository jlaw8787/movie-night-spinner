Snacks

Wensleydale cheese and chutney toastsVegetarian

Wensleydale and cheddar on toast, apple chutney and green apple

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the grated cheeses up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; toast, top and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 20g butter
  • 200g Wensleydale cheese
  • 80g cheddar
  • 150g apple chutney
  • 1 green apple
  1. Toast the slices of thick white bread on one side under the grill.
  2. Turn and spread the untoasted sides lightly with butter.
  3. Crumble over the Wensleydale mixed with the grated cheddar, pressing it to the edges.
  4. Grill 3 to 4 minutes until bubbling and golden in spots.
  5. Top each with a spoon of apple chutney and a few thin slices of green apple.
  6. Cut into triangles.

Breakfast machine French toast fingersVegetarian

Egg-dipped bread fingers in cinnamon sugar, strawberry jam to dip

Prep 12 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Whisk the egg mixture up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; dip and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 150ml milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 6 slices thick white bread
  • 40g butter
  • ground cinnamon, to taste
  • 150g strawberry jam
  1. Whisk the eggs with the milk, the caster sugar and the vanilla.
  2. Cut the thick slices of white bread into fingers.
  3. Dip each finger in the egg for a few seconds a side.
  4. Fry in batches in the butter over medium heat for 2 minutes a side until golden.
  5. Toss straight away in the caster sugar mixed with the cinnamon.
  6. Serve warm with the strawberry jam.

Mains

Bangers and mash

Pork sausages on buttery mash with onion gravy

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Boil and mash the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and reheat with a splash of milk; brown the sausages and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 2 onions
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Brown the pork sausages slowly in a pan for 15 minutes.
  2. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and the milk.
  3. Fry the sliced onions in the sausage pan until soft, stir in the flour and the beef stock and simmer until thick.
  4. Serve the sausages on mash with gravy.

Sweets

Jam roly-poly with custardVegetarian

Baked rolled pudding with strawberry jam and warm custard

Prep 20 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Bake the roly-poly up to 1 day ahead, covered; reheat in slices and make the custard fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 650ml milk
  • 200g strawberry jam
  • 3 tbsp custard powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Rub the cold butter into the self-raising flour, add the milk to make a soft dough and roll into a rectangle.
  2. Spread with the strawberry jam, roll up and bake on a lined tray at 190C for 35 minutes.
  3. Serve in slices with the custard made from custard powder, the caster sugar and milk.

Gromit's malted hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot milk with chocolate, cocoa and malted milk powder

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh; whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1l milk
  • 120g milk chocolate
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the milk in a saucepan until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chopped milk chocolate, the cocoa, the malted milk powder and the sugar until smooth and frothy, about 3 minutes.
  3. Do not let it boil.
  4. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  5. Pour into big mugs, spoon the cream on top and grate over a little more chocolate.
