Snacks

Continental gold coin parmesan crispsVegetarian

Lacy baked parmesan crisps with thyme

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Keeps 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g parmesan
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 4 tbsp tomato chutney
  1. Finely grate the parmesan and mix with picked thyme leaves.
  2. Spoon into small rounds on a tray lined with baking paper, leaving space between.
  3. Bake at 190C until lacy and golden.
  4. Crack black pepper over each and cool on the tray to crisp. Serve with chutney for dabbing.

Continental hotel bar olivesVegetarian

Warm green olives with orange peel, rosemary and garlic, plus toasted almonds

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g green olives
  • 60ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 orange
  • 2 rosemary sprigs
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100g almonds
  1. Warm the olives in the oil with orange peel, rosemary and sliced garlic over low heat.
  2. Toast the almonds in a dry pan with a pinch of salt.
  3. Serve both in small bowls, the hotel bar way.

Mains

Cleaners' braised short ribs

Beef short ribs braised 4 hours in red wine, served with the reduced sauce

Prep 35 min, cook 270 min · Make ahead: Ribs up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 375ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 200g polenta
  • 1l water
  • 40g butter
  • 40g parmesan
  1. Season and brown the short ribs in batches.
  2. Add the wine, stock, garlic and thyme. Cover and braise at 150C until tender.
  3. Lift out the ribs, skim the fat and reduce the sauce until glossy, then return the ribs to coat.
  4. Cook the polenta in the water, stirring, then beat in the butter and parmesan.
  5. Spoon the ribs over the polenta.

Daisy's puppy biscuitsVegetarian

Peanut butter shortbread bones, half dipped in dark chocolate

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 100g peanut butter
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 200g plain flour
  • 100g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the butter, peanut butter and icing sugar, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into small bone shapes.
  3. Bake at 170C until firm.
  4. Cool, melt the chocolate and half dip each biscuit. Human food only.

Drinks

Continental bourbon sourVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and egg white shaken frothy, over a big ice cube

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 3 lemons
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 bottle aromatic bitters
  • 1 bag large ice cubes
  1. Juice the lemons. Shake the bourbon, lemon juice, syrup and egg white hard without ice for 10 seconds, one drink at a time.
  2. Add ice and shake again.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in a heavy glass and dot the foam with bitters.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when John sits at the bar in the Continental.
