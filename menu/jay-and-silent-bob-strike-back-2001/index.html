Hitchhiker's jalapeño cheese toastiesVegetarian

Sourdough toasties with cheddar, pickled jalapeño and tomato relish.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 100g pepper jack or tasty cheese, grated
  • 3 tbsp pickled jalapenos
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 150g tomato relish
  • 1 pinch smoked paprika
  1. Mix the butter with crushed garlic. Grate the cheddar and the pepper jack or tasty cheese and drain and chop the jalapenos.
  2. Fill the sourdough with the grated cheddar and pepper jack and the jalapenos and spread garlic butter on the outsides.
  3. Cook in a pan over medium heat, pressing down, until golden and melting.
  4. Cut into fingers and serve with tomato relish stirred with a pinch of smoked paprika.

Craft service grilled fruit with chilli limeVegetarian

Charred pineapple, rockmelon and watermelon with chilli and lime.

Prep 19 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1/4 rockmelon
  • 300g watermelon wedge
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 limes
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 handful mint leaves
  1. Cut the pineapple, rockmelon and watermelon into thick spears.
  2. Heat a grill pan or barbecue very hot and char the pineapple and rockmelon, leaving the watermelon raw.
  3. Mix the salt, chilli powder and zest of a lime. Stir the yoghurt with honey, lime juice and chopped mint.
  4. Arrange the fruit, sprinkle with chilli lime salt and serve with the dip.

Jersey smoked ham and cheese slidersOn the printed menu

Thick ham slices fried until curled, on toasted buns with melted American, mustard and pickles.

How it is made
  • 400g thick-cut smoked ham
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 8 slider buns
  • 20g butter
  • yellow mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Thick smoked ham slices fried until caramelised and curled, stacked on toasted slider buns with melted American cheese, yellow mustard and pickles. Cut 400g thick smoked ham into 8 slices about 5mm thick and nick the edges. Fry in a little butter over medium heat for 2 minutes a side until caramelised. Lay a slice of American cheese on each, add a splash of water and cover for 30 seconds to melt. Toast 8 slider buns cut side down in the pan. Spread with yellow mustard, add the ham and cheese and top with sliced pickles.

Mooby's double burgers

Two smashed beef patties, American cheese, pickles and special sauce.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 dill pickles
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls. Mix the mayonnaise with tomato sauce.
  2. Smash the balls flat on a smoking griddle, season and cook, then flip, add cheese and cook a minute more.
  3. Stack 2 patties per toasted bun with pickles and sauce, then wrap in paper.

Studio commissary chicken clubOn the printed menu

Triple deckers of roast chicken, crisp bacon, avocado and garlic mayo, crisps on the side.

How it is made
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 500g roast chicken
  • 1 loaf white sandwich bread
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 150g salted crisps
  1. Triple-decker toasted sandwiches of roast chicken, crisp bacon, avocado, tomato and cos with garlic mayo, cut into quarters and served with salted crisps. Grill 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Shred 500g roast chicken. Toast 12 slices of white bread. Mix 100g mayo with 1 crushed garlic clove. For each sandwich, spread 3 slices with mayo, layer chicken, avocado and cos on the first, bacon and tomato on the second, and top with the third. Pin with 4 toothpicks, cut into quarters and serve with 150g salted crisps.

Orangutan banana splitsVegetarian

Banana with vanilla, chocolate and strawberry ice cream, chocolate sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bananas
  • 400ml vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml chocolate ice cream
  • 400ml strawberry ice cream
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream, for the fudge
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped, for the topping
  • 50g peanuts, chopped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Split the bananas lengthways into 4 dishes.
  2. Add a scoop each of vanilla, chocolate and strawberry ice cream.
  3. Melt the chocolate with the cream for the fudge and pour over.
  4. Top with the whipped cream for the topping, chopped peanuts and a cherry.

Convenience store raspberry slushiesVegetarianNo alcohol

Icy raspberry and lemon slush, served in big cups.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Blend fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml raspberry cordial
  • 600g ice
  • 200ml cold water
  • 1 lemon
  1. Blend the cordial, ice, water and lemon juice until smooth and slushy.
  2. Pour into 4 big cups and serve straight away with straws.

When to serve

Serve the banana splits when Suzanne the orangutan makes her escape.
