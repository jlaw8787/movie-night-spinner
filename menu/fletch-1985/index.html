Country club Bloody Mary shrimp cocktail

Jumbo shrimp in a Bloody Mary cocktail sauce over shredded romaine.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; assemble in the glasses to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g cooked king prawns
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • hot sauce, a few drops
  • 1/2 tsp celery salt
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 baby cos lettuce
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the tomato sauce, horseradish, Worcestershire, hot sauce, celery salt and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Shred the lettuce into glasses and spoon over some sauce.
  4. Hang the prawns around the rim, dust with paprika and serve with lemon wedges and a celery stick.

Beach kiosk fish bites with lime mayo

Crisp battered snapper bites with lime mayo.

Prep 7 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime mayo up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; batter and fry the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g snapper fillets
  • 120g plain flour
  • 30g cornflour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 220ml cold soda water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 lime
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • sea salt, to taste
  1. Cut the snapper into bite-size pieces.
  2. Whisk the flour, cornflour, baking powder and soda water into a batter.
  3. Dip the fish and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until crisp and golden, working in batches. Drain, 12 min.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with the garlic and lime zest and juice.
  5. Sprinkle the fish with sea salt and chilli flakes and serve with the mayo.

Fletch's steak sandwich

Seared sirloin, Swiss cheese, jammy onions, lettuce and mustard mayo.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Caramelise the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; sear the steaks and build the rolls on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 4 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 4 Turkish rolls, halved
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp hot English mustard
  • 60g rocket
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  1. Slowly fry the onions in the butter and sugar until jammy, 20 min.
  2. Bat the steaks to about 1cm thick, season and sear in a very hot pan, 4 min.
  3. Lay the cheese on the steaks and cover briefly to melt.
  4. Toast the rolls. Mix the mayonnaise with the mustard, 3 min.
  5. Build each roll with mayo, rocket, tomato, steak and onions. Make two each.

Gail Stanwyk's Cobb salad

Chicken, bacon, egg, avocado and blue cheese on lettuce.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Boil the eggs and make the dressing up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 6 rashers of bacon
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 avocados
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 2 baby cos lettuces
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 80ml olive oil
  1. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, cool and peel.
  2. Cook the chicken in a hot grill pan for 5 minutes a side, rest and dice. Fry the bacon until crisp and break up.
  3. Shake the vinegar, mustard, oil and salt in a jar.
  4. Chop the cos onto a platter and lay rows of chicken, bacon, chopped egg, avocado, halved tomatoes and crumbled blue cheese across the top.
  5. Scatter with chives and pour over the dressing at the table.

Hot fudge sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, salted peanuts, cream and a cherry.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the fudge sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled, then rewarm; whip the cream and build the sundaes to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 30g butter
  • 120g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 60g salted peanuts
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Heat some of the cream with the sugar and butter until bubbling. Off the heat, stir in the chocolate and a pinch of salt until glossy, 5 min.
  2. Toast the peanuts in a dry pan and chop, 3 min.
  3. Whip the remaining cream.
  4. Scoop ice cream into tall glasses, pour over the warm fudge and top with whipped cream, peanuts and a cherry, 4 min.

Underhills' tab Bloody Mary

Vodka tomato cocktail with celery salt rim and lemon.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the tomato juice, vodka, lemon juice and seasonings in a jug up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; rim the glasses and pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp celery salt
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 2 lemons
  • 800ml tomato juice
  • 180ml vodka
  • 2 tsp horseradish cream
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • hot sauce, to taste
  • 4 celery sticks
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the celery salt and pepper on a saucer, wet the rims of tall glasses with lemon and dip.
  2. Fill the glasses with ice.
  3. Stir the tomato juice, vodka, juice of one lemon, horseradish, Worcestershire and hot sauce in a jug.
  4. Pour into the glasses and add a celery stick and a lemon wedge.
