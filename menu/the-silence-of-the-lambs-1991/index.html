Snacks

Chicken liver pâté toasts

Silky chicken liver pâté on toasted baguette with cornichons.

Prep 0 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Pate up to 2 days chilled, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 30g butter, for frying the shallot
  • 100g butter, softened, to blend
  • 400g chicken livers, trimmed
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 300g broad beans
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 baguette
  • salt, to taste
  1. Fry the shallot in butter, add the livers and thyme and cook until just pink inside.
  2. Blitz with the softened butter until smooth and chill.
  3. Boil the broad beans, peel and smash with oil and salt.
  4. Serve both on toasted baguette slices.

Moth-wing parmesan crispsVegetarian

Grated parmesan baked thin and crisp with black pepper.

Prep 15 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g parmesan
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  1. Grate the parmesan finely.
  2. Spoon into small wing shapes on 2 lined trays and grind over black pepper.
  3. Bake at 200°C until lacy and golden.
  4. Cool on the tray so they crisp.

Baltimore crab cakes with remouladeOn the printed menu

Sweet crab with mayo, mustard and panko, pan-fried golden, caper gherkin sauce and lemon.

How it is made
  • 400g crab meat
  • 200g mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • Dijon mustard
  • paprika
  • 2 spring onions
  • 60g panko crumbs
  • 2 lemons
  • 30g butter
  • vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 2 gherkins
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Sweet crab meat bound lightly with mayo, mustard and breadcrumbs, pan-fried into golden cakes, served with a caper and gherkin remoulade and lemon. Mix 400g crab meat gently with 3 tbsp mayo, 1 egg, 1 tsp Dijon, 1 tsp paprika, 2 sliced spring onions, 60g panko crumbs and lemon zest. Shape into 12 small cakes and chill 30 minutes. Fry in 30g butter and 2 tbsp oil for 3 minutes a side until golden. For the remoulade, mix 120g mayo with 1 tbsp chopped capers, 2 chopped gherkins, 1 tsp Dijon, parsley and lemon juice. Serve with lemon wedges.

Lamb shanks in red wine

Lamb shanks braised in red wine, tomato and rosemary until tender.

Prep 25 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lamb shanks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 375ml red wine
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 200g polenta
  • 1 L water
  • 30g butter
  • salt, to taste
  1. Season the shanks with salt, then brown them in the oil.
  2. Soften the sliced onion and garlic.
  3. Add the wine, tomatoes and rosemary, cover and braise at 160°C.
  4. Cook the polenta in salted water, stir in the butter.
  5. Serve the shanks on top of the polenta.

West Virginia buttermilk fried chickenOn the printed menu

Pieces soaked overnight, dredged in peppery flour, fried crackling, with coleslaw and warm scones.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 700ml buttermilk
  • hot sauce
  • 250g plain flour
  • paprika
  • garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 80g butter
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • cider vinegar
  • honey
  1. Chicken pieces soaked in buttermilk, dredged in peppery flour and fried crackling crisp, with creamy coleslaw and warm buttermilk biscuits with honey. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces overnight in 500ml buttermilk with salt and hot sauce. Dredge in 250g plain flour seasoned with paprika, garlic powder and pepper. Fry at 160°C for 12 to 15 minutes until 75°C inside. For biscuits, rub 80g butter into 300g self-raising flour, add 200ml buttermilk, pat out, cut 8 rounds and bake at 220°C for 14 minutes. Toss half a shredded cabbage and 1 grated carrot with 100g mayo and vinegar.

Red wine poached pearsVegetarian

Pears poached in red wine with cinnamon and orange peel.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pears
  • 375ml red wine
  • 125ml water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 150g mascarpone
  1. Peel the pears, keeping the stalks.
  2. Simmer the wine with the water, sugar, cinnamon and orange peel, add the pears and poach, turning.
  3. Reduce the syrup.
  4. Serve the pears with mascarpone and syrup.

Drinks

Red wine and blood orange sangriaVegetarian

Red wine, blood orange juice, brandy and honey over sliced fruit.

Prep 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a jug ahead, chill
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml Chianti
  • 200ml blood orange juice
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 orange
  • 1 apple
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Stir the Chianti with the juice, brandy and honey until the honey dissolves.
  2. Add the sliced orange, diced apple and cinnamon stick.
  3. Chill for at least 2 hours, which is the resting time.
  4. Pour over ice in wine glasses, spooning some fruit into each. For non-drinkers, make it with sour cherry juice and blood orange juice topped with soda.

When to serve

Pour the red when Lecter mentions a nice Chianti with the census taker.
