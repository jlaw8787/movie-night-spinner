Venice Beach crispy fish tacos

Beer-battered fish, slaw, pickled onion and chipotle mayo in tortillas

Prep 18 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Pickle the onion, shred the cabbage and mix the chipotle mayo up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; make the batter and fry the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g firm white fish fillets
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 limes
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 120g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 can lager
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Slice the red onion thin and soak in lime juice and salt for 20 minutes.
  2. Toss a quarter of a shredded cabbage with lime and salt.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with the chipotle in adobo.
  4. Whisk the flour, the baking powder and the cold lager.
  5. Heat the oil, cut the white fish into strips, dip in the batter and fry at 180C for 3 minutes.
  6. Warm the tortillas and fill with slaw, fish, onion, mayo and coriander.

Man cave pulled pork nachos

Corn chips with pulled pork, black beans, cheddar and lime sour cream

Prep 9 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Toss the pulled pork with the barbecue sauce up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; build the nachos and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pulled pork
  • 3 tbsp barbecue sauce
  • 300g corn chips
  • 400g tin black beans
  • 200g cheddar
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 lime
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Toss the pulled pork with the barbecue sauce.
  2. Spread the corn chips on a big tray and scatter with the pork, the drained black beans and the grated cheddar.
  3. Bake at 200C for 10 minutes until melted.
  4. Mix the sour cream with the juice of the lime and drizzle over.
  5. Top with sliced pickled jalapeños, diced tomato, coriander and spring onion.

Open house roast beef baguettes

Rare roast beef, rocket, pickled onion and horseradish cream in a baguette

Prep 8 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Pickle the onion and mix the horseradish cream up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; layer the baguettes within 2 hours of serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red onion
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 100g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  • 2 baguettes
  • 400g rare roast beef
  • 1 bag rocket
  • 2 tbsp fried shallots
  1. Slice the red onion thin and soak in the red wine vinegar with the sugar for 20 minutes.
  2. Mix the sour cream with the horseradish and salt.
  3. Split the baguettes lengthways, spread with the horseradish cream and layer with the sliced rare roast beef, a handful of rocket, the drained onion and the fried shallots.
  4. Press down and cut each into pieces.

Lemon cupcakes for Peter and ZooeyVegetarian

Lemon cupcakes filled with lemon curd, cream cheese frosting on top

Prep 24 min, cook 8 min, then 41 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight; fill with curry and ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 205g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 120ml milk
  • 150g lemon curd
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 12 cupcake cases
  1. Heat the oven to 175C.
  2. Beat the butter and the caster sugar with the zest of the lemons, add the eggs, then the self-raising flour and the milk.
  3. Bake in the cases for 18 minutes and cool.
  4. Cut a small hole in each and fill with lemon curd.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with the soft butter and the icing sugar, pipe on top and finish with lemon zest.

Sydney's grapefruit palomaVegetarian

Tequila, pink grapefruit and lime topped with soda, salted rim

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the grapefruit and limes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; build each glass with the tequila and soda water on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 2 pink grapefruit
  • 3 limes
  • 40ml agave syrup
  • 400ml soda water
  • salt flakes, to taste
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Rub the rims of the tall glasses with lime and dip in salt.
  2. Fill with ice.
  3. To each add the tequila, the fresh pink grapefruit juice, the lime juice and the agave syrup and stir.
  4. Top with the soda water and add a grapefruit wedge.
  5. For drivers, skip the tequila and add some more grapefruit and soda.
