Bunnyburrow carrot friesVegetarian

Roasted carrot fries with smoked paprika and a garlic yoghurt dip.

Prep 19 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cut the carrots up to 1 day ahead in a bag in the fridge, and stir the garlic yoghurt dip up to 2 days ahead; roast fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g carrots
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  1. Cut the carrots into fries.
  2. Toss with the oil, paprika and salt.
  3. Roast at 220°C until caramelised at the edges.
  4. Stir the garlic into the yoghurt for a dip.

Clawhauser's doughnut holesVegetarian

Fried yeast doughnut balls rolled in cinnamon sugar.

Prep 98 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Knead the dough and prove it overnight in the fridge, up to 1 day ahead; roll, fry and coat the doughnuts just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar, for the dough
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar, for rolling
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, the sugar for the dough, eggs, butter and warm milk into a soft dough.
  2. Knead until smooth.
  3. Cover and prove.
  4. Roll into small balls.
  5. Fry in the oil at 175°C in batches until golden.
  6. Roll in the cinnamon and the sugar for rolling while warm.

Bunnyburrow blueberry ricotta toasts

Toasted brioche, honey-whipped cheese, jammy berries and almonds, the swap that fools Bellwether.

How it is made
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  • 250g blueberries
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 brioche loaf
  • 30g flaked almonds
  1. Toasted brioche spread with honey-whipped ricotta, piled with jammy blistered blueberries and toasted almonds, Hopps family farm produce. Whip 250g ricotta with 2 tbsp honey and 1 tsp vanilla until smooth. Cook 250g blueberries with 1 tbsp sugar and a squeeze of lemon in a pan over high heat for 3 minutes until some burst. Toast 4 thick slices of brioche, halve them and spread with the ricotta. Spoon over the warm blueberries and scatter with 30g toasted flaked almonds.

Chicken olive and lemon tagine

Chicken thighs with saffron, ginger, green olives and preserved lemon.

Prep 27 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • pinch of saffron
  • 500ml water
  • 1 preserved lemon, chopped
  • 200g green olives
  • 250g couscous
  • 250ml boiling water
  1. Slice the onion, grate the ginger and chop the preserved lemon.
  2. Brown the chicken in the oil.
  3. Soften the onion with the ginger and saffron.
  4. Add the water, preserved lemon and olives, cover and simmer until tender.
  5. Cover the couscous with the boiling water until fluffy, then fork through.
  6. Serve the tagine over the couscous.

Tundratown smoked fish chowder

Smoky white fillets and salmon in creamy potato and leek, bacon crumbs, for Fru Fru's wedding.

How it is made
  • 100g bacon
  • 1 leek
  • 30g butter
  • 500g potatoes
  • 800ml fish stock
  • 300ml cream
  • 250g smoked cod
  • 250g salmon fillet
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Smoked fish and salmon poached in a creamy potato and leek chowder with dill and bacon crumbs, with crusty bread for the snowy district. Fry 100g diced bacon until crisp and set aside. Soften 1 sliced leek in 30g butter, add 500g diced potatoes and 800ml fish stock and simmer 12 minutes. Stir in 300ml cream, then add 250g smoked cod and 250g salmon in chunks and poach gently for 5 minutes. Season, stir in chopped dill and serve topped with the bacon, with crusty bread.

Strawberry watermelon ice popsVegetarian

Strawberry, watermelon and lime frozen on sticks.

Prep 14 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the pops overnight, up to 1 week ahead, and keep frozen until serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 500g watermelon
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Chop the strawberries and watermelon.
  2. Blend with the lime juice and sugar until smooth.
  3. Pour into ice block moulds and push in sticks.
  4. Freeze overnight.

Rainforest district coolerNo alcohol

Kiwifruit, cucumber, mint and lime over ice with soda water.

Prep 16 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the kiwifruit, cucumber, mint, lime and sugar up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; pour over ice and top with soda water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 kiwifruit, peeled
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml soda water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Peel the kiwifruit and chop the cucumber.
  2. Blend the kiwifruit, cucumber, mint, lime juice and sugar.
  3. Strain into a jug.
  4. Pour over ice and top with soda water.
