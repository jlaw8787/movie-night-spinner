Emerald City pea and mint soupVegetarian

Bright green pea soup. Pay no attention to that man behind the curtain.

Prep 14 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make up to a day ahead and cool quickly to keep the colour; reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 750g frozen peas
  • 1l vegetable stock
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the butter for 6 minutes.
  2. Add the stock and bring to the boil, then the peas, and simmer 4 minutes.
  3. Add most of the mint leaves and blend until smooth and very green. Season.
  4. Serve with a swirl of cream and the rest of the mint.

Munchkinland cheese strawsVegetarian

Twisted puff pastry straws with cheddar, parmesan and paprika

Prep 12 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 80g vintage cheddar, finely grated
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line two trays.
  2. Brush one pastry sheet with egg, scatter with the cheeses and paprika, and lay the second sheet on top. Press down.
  3. Cut into 1.5cm strips and twist each one.
  4. Bake 12 to 14 minutes until puffed and golden. Cool on the trays.

Kansas farm fried chicken

Buttermilk soaked drumsticks in paprika flour, fried crisp, with mash

Prep 15 min, cook 39 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the drumsticks in the buttermilk overnight in the fridge, up to 1 day ahead; dredge and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • salt, to taste
  1. Soak the drumsticks in buttermilk in the fridge overnight.
  2. Rest the chicken in the buttermilk.
  3. Peel and boil the potatoes, then mash with butter.
  4. Dredge the chicken in flour seasoned with paprika and salt.
  5. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170°C in batches until crisp and cooked through.
  6. Simmer the stock with a little flour to make a gravy and serve with the mash.

Emerald City pesto penne with broccoliOn the printed menu

Green pesto penne and broccoli. Ride the horse of a different colour

How it is made
  • 400g penne
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 190g basil pesto
  • 1 lemon
  • 80g parmesan
  • 40g pine nuts
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Penne cooked with broccoli and peas in the same pot, tossed with basil pesto, parmesan and lemon until bright green. Cook 400g penne in salted water, adding 1 head broccoli in small florets and 1 cup frozen peas for the last 3 minutes. Drain, keeping a cup of the water. Toss with 190g basil pesto, the zest and juice of 1 lemon and 50g grated parmesan, adding pasta water until glossy. Toast 40g pine nuts in a dry pan. Serve topped with the pine nuts, extra parmesan and a handful of basil leaves.

Fried choux crullersVegetarian

Twisted rings of choux, just fried, says Auntie Em

Prep 22 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Same day
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp full cream milk
  1. Cut squares of baking paper and bring the water and butter to the boil.
  2. Beat in the flour until a smooth ball forms, cool slightly, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Pipe twisted rings onto the paper squares.
  4. Fry in oil at 180°C, paper and all, until golden, peeling the paper off once they float.
  5. Mix the icing sugar, honey and milk into a glaze and dip the warm crullers.

Lollipop Guild lollipopsVegetarian

Hard boiled sugar lollipops tinted in two colours

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 80ml glucose syrup
  • 80ml water
  • 1 tsp food colouring, two colours
  • 1 packet lollipop sticks
  1. Line trays with baking paper.
  2. Boil the sugar, glucose and water to 150°C without stirring.
  3. Split between two small pans, tint each a different colour with the food colouring and pour small rounds onto the paper, swirling the colours.
  4. Press a stick into each and leave to harden.

Ruby slipper jelly

Raspberry jelly in glasses. There's no place like home.

Prep 13 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and chill the jellies up to 2 days ahead; whip the cream and add the glitter to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet raspberry jelly crystals
  • 125g raspberries
  • 1 tsp edible glitter
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Make up the jelly as the packet says.
  2. Divide the raspberries between four glasses and pour the jelly over.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Whip the cream, dust the jellies with glitter and top with cream.

Grumpy tree salted caramel applesOn the printed menu

Caramel apple wedges. Ask the angry tree first.

How it is made
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 60g butter
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 4 red apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 60g roasted peanuts
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  1. Crisp red apple wedges with a warm salted caramel dip, crushed roasted peanuts and a dusting of cinnamon for dunking. Melt 100g brown sugar with 60g butter, stirring for 2 minutes, then pour in 125ml cream and simmer 3 minutes until thick. Take off the heat and stir in half a teaspoon of salt. Cut 4 red apples into wedges and toss in lemon juice so they don't brown. Pour the warm caramel into a bowl, scatter with 60g crushed roasted peanuts and a pinch of cinnamon and serve with the apples.

Rainbow pineapple lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemon, pineapple and ginger. Don't throw it at the Witch.

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Simmer, cool and strain the ginger syrup up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; juice the lemons and mix with soda water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml water
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 4 lemons
  • 400ml pineapple juice
  • 400ml soda water
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and ginger for 2 minutes, then cool and strain.
  2. Juice the lemons and mix with the syrup and pineapple juice.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice, share the mix between them and top with soda water.
  4. Finish each with a pineapple wedge on the rim.

When to serve
