Blades of Glory (2007) movie night dinner menu

Two rival figure skaters banned from singles competition team up as the first all-male pairs team. Two men, one pair of skates, plenty of snacks.

Snacks

Rinkside soft pretzelsVegetarian

Soft salted pretzels baked golden, with mustard and cheese sauce

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Shape the pretzels and keep them chilled on a tray up to 2 hours ahead; simmer in the bicarb water and bake on the night, best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 3 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  • 30g butter
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 200g cheese sauce
  • 1l water
  1. Roll the pizza dough into ropes and twist into pretzels.
  2. Dip each in the water simmering with the bicarb soda for 20 seconds, sprinkle with salt flakes and bake at 220C for 12 minutes.
  3. Brush with the melted butter and serve with mustard and the cheese sauce.

Fire and Ice prawns

Garlic and chilli prawns grilled hot, piled on ice with lime

Prep 15 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Toss the prawns with the oil, garlic and chilli up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; grill on the night and serve over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 limes
  1. Toss the peeled green prawns with the olive oil, the garlic cloves, the chopped red chilli and salt.
  2. Grill on high for 2 minutes a side.
  3. Pile over a platter of crushed ice and serve with lime wedges.

Mains

Lone Wolf chicken parmigiana

Crumbed chicken with passata, ham and mozzarella, chips and salad

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled on a tray; top and bake on the night, with the chips going in at the same time
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 200g passata
  • 4 slices leg ham
  • 150g mozzarella
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 1 bag salad leaves
  1. Halve the large chicken breasts into thin fillets.
  2. Crumb in flour, the eggs and the breadcrumbs and pan-fry until golden.
  3. Top with the passata, the ham and the grated mozzarella and bake at 200C for 12 minutes.
  4. Serve with chips and salad.

Montreal rinkside poutine

Chips, squeaky curds and hot gravy, the Montreal final way

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 3 days ahead and reheat; bake the chips and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg potatoes, cut in thick chips
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 300g cheese curds or torn fresh mozzarella
  1. Toss the chips in oil and salt and bake at 220C, turning once, until crisp.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook until nutty brown.
  3. Whisk in the stock and Worcestershire and simmer until glossy and thick. Pepper it well.
  4. Pile the hot chips into bowls, scatter the curds and pour the hot gravy over.

Sweets

Fairchild's pearl ice cream ballsVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream dipped in white chocolate and rolled in coconut

Prep 25 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Scoop, freeze, dip and roll the balls up to 1 week ahead and keep them in the freezer; nothing left to do on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 100g desiccated coconut
  1. Scoop the vanilla ice cream into balls and freeze for 1 hour.
  2. Melt the white chocolate with the coconut oil, dip each ball and roll straight away in the desiccated coconut.
  3. Freeze until serving.

Drinks

Grublets peppermint hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick hot chocolate with peppermint, whipped cream and candy cane

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Whisk the chocolate base up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat gently; whip the cream and crush the candy canes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1l milk
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • peppermint essence, to taste
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 candy canes
  1. Heat the milk in a saucepan until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chopped dark chocolate, the cocoa and the sugar until smooth and thick, about 4 minutes without boiling.
  3. Stir in a little peppermint essence.
  4. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  5. Pour the chocolate into mugs, top with the cream and scatter with the crushed candy canes.
