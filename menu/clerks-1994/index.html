Perfect dozen devilled eggsVegetarian

Twelve devilled eggs with Dijon, paprika and chives

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • smoked paprika, to dust
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool in cold water, peel and halve.
  2. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, Dijon and vinegar.
  3. Pipe the filling back into the whites, dust with paprika and add chives. Exactly twelve.

Quick Stop chips with nacho cheeseVegetarian

Chips with hot cheddar cheese sauce and pickled jalapeños

Prep 7 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped pickled jalapenos
  • 1 tbsp jalapeno pickling liquid
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 300g corn chips
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the milk and simmer until thick.
  2. Off the heat, stir in the cheddar until smooth, then the jalapenos, pickling liquid and paprika.
  3. Warm the corn chips on a tray at 180°C for 4 minutes. Serve with the hot cheese for dunking.

Rooftop hockey soft pretzelsOn the printed menu

Knots dipped in bicarb water, rock salted and baked dark, with a sharp two-mustard dip

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 jar hot English mustard
  1. Soft pretzel knots dipped in bicarb water, sprinkled with rock salt and baked deep brown, served warm with a sharp two-mustard dip. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Divide into 8, roll into ropes and tie into knots. Dip each in 2L boiling water with 60g bicarb soda for 30 seconds. Sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Serve with wholegrain mustard and a little hot English mustard mixed together.

Jersey hoagie

Salami, ham and provolone with lettuce, tomato, onion, oil and red wine vinegar

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead, wrap tight
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long bread rolls
  • 150g salami, sliced
  • 150g leg ham, sliced
  • 100g provolone, sliced
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Split the rolls and layer with the salami, ham, provolone, lettuce, tomato and onion.
  2. Drizzle with oil and vinegar and sprinkle with oregano. Wrap tight and cut in half.

Veronica's ricotta beef lasagneOn the printed menu

Red wine ragù, pasta, lemony cheese and mozzarella, for a man not supposed to be here

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 150ml red wine
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 500g ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 300g lasagne sheets
  • 250g mozzarella
  1. Beef ragù simmered slowly with tomato and red wine, layered with lasagne sheets, lemony ricotta and mozzarella, baked bubbling and rested before cutting. Brown 800g beef mince with an onion and 3 garlic cloves. Add 150ml red wine, 2 cans crushed tomatoes and oregano and simmer 2 hours. Mix 500g ricotta with an egg, 50g parmesan, lemon zest and parsley. In a dish, layer ragù, 300g lasagne sheets and ricotta three times, finishing with ragù and 250g mozzarella. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes and rest 20 minutes before cutting.

Quick Stop cream-filled chocolate cakes

Chocolate sponge rectangles sandwiched with marshmallow buttercream

Prep 55 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 70g self-raising flour
  • 20g cocoa
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 100g marshmallow fluff
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 50ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the eggs and caster sugar until thick, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Spread in a lined slice tin and bake at 180°C for 12 minutes. Cool and cut into 12 rectangles.
  3. Beat the butter, most of the icing sugar, marshmallow fluff and vanilla until fluffy. Sandwich pairs of cake with it.
  4. Melt the chocolate with the cream and dip the cakes in it. Let them set.
  5. Mix the leftover icing sugar with a few drops of water and pipe a white squiggle on top.

Back fridge chocolate malt shakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Chocolate malted milkshake with vanilla ice cream and crushed malt balls

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 cup chocolate malt balls
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream, cocoa and malt powder until thick and frothy.
  2. Crush the malt balls in a bag with a rolling pin.
  3. Pour into four chilled tall glasses, scatter the crushed malt balls over and add a straw.
