Snacks

Philly cheesesteak bites

Shaved rib eye and onion with melted provolone on slider buns.

Prep 0 min, cook 13 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the steak for 30 minutes and shave it up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; sear, melt the cheese and fill the buns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g rib eye steak
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 6 slices provolone
  • 12 slider buns
  1. Freeze the steak, then shave thin.
  2. Sear the beef with the onion in oil.
  3. Lay provolone over to melt.
  4. Toast the buns and pile the filling on.

Garlic parmesan potato gemsVegetarian

Crisp baked potato gems tossed in garlic salt and parmesan.

Prep 2 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best baked just before serving; grate the parmesan up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen potato gems
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 150ml tomato sauce
  1. Bake the potato gems at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Toss straight away with garlic salt and parmesan.
  3. Serve with tomato sauce.

Mains

Rosa's arroz con pollo

Chicken and rice baked with saffron, paprika and capsicum.

Prep 23 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Keeps 2 days chilled; reheat with a splash of stock and stir the peas and olives through after
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 100g green olives
  1. Brown the chicken in an ovenproof pan.
  2. Soften the onion and capsicum.
  3. Add the rice, saffron, paprika and stock and nestle in the chicken.
  4. Cover and bake at 180°C.
  5. Stir through the peas and olives.

Freddy's pepperoni and cheese pizza

Thin bases, tomato, mozzarella and spicy salami, baked blistered, while the power tests get filmed.

How it is made
  • 1kg pizza dough
  • 250ml passata
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 400g mozzarella
  • 200g pepperoni
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  1. Thin pizza bases spread with tomato sauce, loaded with mozzarella and pepperoni, baked blistered and crisp, with chilli flakes and parmesan. Divide 1kg pizza dough into 4 and stretch into thin rounds. Mix 250ml passata with 1 tsp dried oregano, a pinch of sugar and salt and spread over the bases. Top with 400g grated mozzarella and 200g sliced pepperoni. Bake on hot trays at 250°C for 10 minutes until blistered and bubbling. Finish with grated parmesan and chilli flakes for whoever wants them.

Rock of Eternity rocky road

Dark chocolate with marshmallows, almonds, biscuit and coconut.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g dark chocolate
  • 150g marshmallows
  • 100g roasted almonds
  • 100g plain sweet biscuits, broken
  • 30g desiccated coconut
  1. Melt the chocolate.
  2. Stir through the marshmallows, almonds, biscuits and coconut.
  3. Press into a lined tin, chill until set and cut into chunks.

Carnival cinnamon sugar doughnuts

Little yeasted rings fried fluffy, spiced sugar, warm chocolate sauce, for the fight at the fair.

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 40g butter
  • 200ml milk
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 packet paper bags
  1. Little yeasted ring doughnuts fried golden and fluffy, rolled hot in cinnamon sugar and served in paper bags with warm chocolate sauce. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 50g sugar, 1 egg, 40g soft butter and 200ml warm milk into a soft dough, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Roll out 1cm thick and cut 20 small rings. Prove 20 minutes, then fry at 175°C for 1 minute a side until golden. Toss hot in 100g sugar mixed with 2 tsp cinnamon. Melt 100g chocolate with 100ml cream for dipping.

Drinks

Cinnamon vanilla eggnogNo alcohol

Egg yolk custard with milk, cream, cinnamon and vanilla.

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 600ml milk
  • 200ml cream
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100ml whipped cream
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg, grated
  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale.
  2. Heat the milk, cream, cinnamon and vanilla until steaming and whisk slowly into the yolks.
  3. Return to the pan and stir over low heat until it coats a spoon, without boiling.
  4. Strain and chill.
  5. Pour into glasses, top with whipped cream and grate over nutmeg.
