F.E.A.S.T. shelter soup cupsVegetarian

Red lentil and tomato soup in mugs, grilled cheese fingers to dunk.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tins of crushed tomatoes
  • 150g red lentils
  • 800ml water
  • 4 slices of white bread
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 30g butter
  1. Soften the onion and garlic with the cumin.
  2. Add the tomatoes, lentils and water and simmer, then blend smooth, 25 min.
  3. Make grilled cheese from the bread, cheddar and butter and cut into fingers.
  4. Serve the soup in mugs with the cheese fingers.

Ned's lola's pork lumpia

Crisp fried Filipino spring rolls filled with seasoned pork and carrot.

Prep 30 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 20 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 4 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 3 tbsp white vinegar
  1. Mix the pork with the carrot, onion, garlic, egg, soy and pepper.
  2. Roll a thin line of filling in each wrapper, folding in the sides and sealing with water.
  3. Fry in the oil at 175°C until deep golden, 5 min.
  4. Serve with sweet chilli sauce and vinegar mixed with a little garlic.

Delmar's number 5, pickles, smushed

Turkey, Swiss and pickles on a hero roll, pressed flat.

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Build the rolls up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled, press on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sliced roast turkey
  • 8 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 2 long hero rolls
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 2 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 20g butter
  1. Split the rolls and spread with mustard and mayonnaise.
  2. Fill with turkey, cheese and pickles.
  3. Butter the outside and press in a hot pan under a heavy pan, turning once, until crisp and melted, 8 min.
  4. Cut into pieces to share.

Happy's apartment spaghetti

Beef meatballs simmered in tomato sauce over spaghetti, garlic butter baguette.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 2 min · Make ahead: Meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 80g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tins of crushed tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 1 baguette
  • 80g butter, softened
  1. Mix the mince with the breadcrumbs, egg and half the parmesan and roll into balls, then brown, 10 min.
  2. Simmer in the tomatoes with half the garlic, 40 min.
  3. Cook the spaghetti and toss with the sauce, 10 min.
  4. Split the baguette, spread with the butter mixed with the remaining garlic, and bake at 200°C, 12 min.

Liberty Island chilli cheese dogs

Grilled franks under spiced ground beef and melted cheddar, diced onion and mustard.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 tbsp chilli powder
  • ground cumin
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • kidney beans
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 150g cheddar
  • mustard
  1. Grilled beef franks in toasted buns, smothered in beef chilli and melted cheddar, with diced onion, mustard and grilled corn on the side. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 diced onion, then add 1 tbsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 400g crushed tomatoes and a drained can of kidney beans, and simmer 25 minutes. Grill 8 beef frankfurts for 6 minutes. Grill 4 corn cobs for 10 minutes and butter them. Toast 8 hot dog buns, add the franks, spoon over chilli, top with 150g grated cheddar, diced onion and mustard.

Coffee shop custard doughnutsVegetarian

Fried yeast doughnuts filled with thick custard and rolled in sugar.

Prep 50 min, cook 10 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, fill and roll in sugar fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 350g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 40g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 130g caster sugar
  • 180ml warm water
  • 300ml thick custard
  • 2 L vegetable oil for frying
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, butter, egg, some of the sugar and warm water into a dough, 15 min.
  2. Rest until doubled, 1 hr.
  3. Cut rounds and prove until puffy, 20 min.
  4. Fry in the oil at 175°C until golden, 10 min.
  5. Pipe in the custard and roll in the remaining sugar.

MJ's coffee shop iced vanilla latteNo alcohol

Vanilla syrup, cold milk and espresso over ice, a coffee shop regular.

Prep 3 min, cook 5 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Syrup and coffee up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 vanilla pod, split
  • 320ml strong coffee
  • 800ml cold milk
  • 150ml milk for frothing
  • 50ml cream
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and vanilla pod, then cool, 5 min.
  2. Brew the coffee and cool, 10 min.
  3. Fill glasses with ice, add vanilla syrup and cold milk, then pour over the coffee.
  4. Froth the milk and cream until thick and spoon on top.
