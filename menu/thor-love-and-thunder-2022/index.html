Snacks

New Asgard fish platter

Rye crispbreads with crème fraîche, smoked trout or pickled herring, dill and red onion

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rye crispbreads
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 150g smoked trout, flaked
  • 100g pickled herring
  • 8 dill sprigs
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  1. Spread the crispbreads with crème fraîche.
  2. Top half with flaked smoked trout and half with pickled herring.
  3. Finish each with a sprig of dill and a few thin slices of red onion. Serve on a board.

Goat's cheese bitesVegetarian

Goat cheese balls rolled in pistachio and chives, on crackers with fig jam

Prep 10 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled, assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g soft goat's cheese
  • 100g pistachios, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp snipped chives
  • 16 water crackers
  • 4 tbsp fig jam
  1. Roll the goat's cheese into small balls and chill for 20 minutes.
  2. Roll each in the pistachios mixed with chives.
  3. Set each on a water cracker with a small spoon of fig jam underneath. Arrange in rows.

Feta and honey filo trianglesOn the printed menu

Feta, spinach and mint filo triangles with honey, from Zeus's golden city. Mind the thunderbolt.

How it is made
  • 250g spinach
  • 200g feta
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 packet filo pastry
  • 80g butter
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  1. Feta, spinach and mint wrapped in buttered filo triangles, baked golden and crackling, brushed with honey and scattered with sesame. Wilt 250g spinach, squeeze dry and chop. Mix with 200g crumbled feta, 1 egg, chopped mint and pepper. Cut 8 filo sheets into 3 long strips each, brushing with 80g melted butter. Put a spoonful of filling at the end of each strip and fold into triangles. Brush with butter and bake at 190°C for 20 minutes until golden. Brush with 2 tbsp warm honey and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Mains

Stout-braised short ribs with mash

Beef short ribs braised 4 hours in stout and thyme, over buttery mashed potatoes

Prep 25 min, cook 4.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 375ml stout
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the ribs all over in the oil.
  2. Add the onions, thyme and stout, then water to come halfway.
  3. Cover and braise at 160°C for 4 hours until falling apart.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes with plenty of butter.
  5. Serve the ribs on top of the mash.

New Asgard creamy fish soupOn the printed menu

Salmon, white fillets and prawns poached in a leek, carrot and dill cream broth, crusty bread

How it is made
  • 1 leek
  • 2 carrots
  • 30g butter
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1L fish stock
  • 300ml cream
  • 300g salmon fillet
  • 300g white fish fillet
  • 200g peeled prawns
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Salmon, white fish and prawns poached in a creamy broth with leek, carrot and dill, served with crusty bread and butter. Soften 1 sliced leek and 2 diced carrots in 30g butter for 5 minutes. Add 2 diced potatoes and 1L fish stock and simmer 12 minutes. Stir in 300ml cream, then add 300g salmon and 300g white fish in chunks and simmer gently for 4 minutes. Add 200g peeled prawns and cook 2 minutes more. Season, stir through chopped dill and a squeeze of lemon, and serve with crusty bread.

Honey baklava with walnuts and pistachiosVegetarian

Layered filo with walnuts and pistachios, soaked in honey syrup

Prep 40 min, cook 55 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead, cool completely
Recipe for 4
  • 300g walnuts and pistachios, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 375g filo pastry
  • 200g butter, melted
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 100g honey
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Mix the walnuts and pistachios with the cinnamon.
  2. Layer filo in a 20 by 30cm tin, brushing every sheet with butter and scattering nuts every few sheets, ending with filo. Cut into diamonds.
  3. Bake at 170°C for 45 minutes until golden.
  4. Boil the sugar, water, honey and a squeeze of lemon for 5 minutes and pour over the hot baklava.
  5. Cool completely before serving. Leave for a few hours.

Korg's spiced pineapple rum punchVegetarian

Spiced rum, pineapple, passionfruit and lime topped with ginger beer

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill ahead, add ginger beer fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1l pineapple juice
  • 4 passionfruit, pulp only
  • 200ml spiced rum
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 750ml ginger beer
  • 1 small pineapple, cut into wedges
  • 4 mint sprigs
  • 1 bag ice
  1. In a big jug, mix the pineapple juice, passionfruit pulp, rum and lime juice. Chill until ready.
  2. Just before serving, add plenty of ice and the ginger beer and stir gently.
  3. Drop in pineapple wedges and mint sprigs. For a kids' jug, leave out the rum and add more ginger beer.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the giant screaming goats pull the longship into the sky.
