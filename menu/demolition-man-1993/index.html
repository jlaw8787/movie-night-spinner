Snacks

Three seashell crackersVegetarian

Boiled conchiglie fried until crisp, tossed in flaky salt and chilli

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Fry and toss with the salt and chilli up to 2 days ahead, airtight; re-crisp in a 150C oven for 5 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g conchiglie (shell pasta)
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Boil the shells until tender and drain.
  2. Dry well on a tea towel.
  3. Fry in 180C oil in batches until crisp and golden.
  4. Toss with salt and chilli flakes while hot.

Future fast food chips and salsaVegetarian

Fried corn tortilla chips with charred tomato jalapeño salsa

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa up to 2 days ahead, chilled; fry the chips up to 1 day ahead, airtight, or fresh on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jalapeno
  • 1 lime
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the tortillas into wedges.
  2. Fry in 180C oil until crisp, drain and salt.
  3. Char the tomatoes, onion, garlic and jalapeno under the grill.
  4. Blitz with lime juice and salt and serve in a bowl.

Mains

Underground rat burger

Thick seared chuck patties with fried onions and melted cheddar

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties and cook the onions up to 1 day ahead, chilled; grill and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 2 onions
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 tbsp barbecue sauce
  1. Shape the mince into thick patties.
  2. Fry the sliced onions in oil until golden.
  3. Sear the patties hard on a hot grill on both sides.
  4. Melt a slice of cheddar on each patty.
  5. Toast the buns and stack with onions and barbecue sauce.

Fine dining fish tacos with pico de gallo

Taco Bell is fine dining now. White tablecloths for the taco course

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the pico and crema up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g snapper fillets
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 limes
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 150g shredded cabbage
  • 1 avocado
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Mix the tomato, onion, chopped coriander and the juice of 1 lime for the pico de gallo.
  2. Blitz the avocado with the sour cream and a squeeze of lime for the crema.
  3. Rub the fish with the chilli, cumin, oil and salt and grill until just cooked, then flake.
  4. Warm the tortillas and fill with cabbage, fish, pico and crema.

Sweets

Layered jelly cubes

Three colours of layered jelly cut into neat cubes, with a little whipped cream

Prep 30 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 3 packets jelly crystals, different colours
  • 6 gelatine leaves
  • 1.2l water
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Make each jelly one at a time, adding extra gelatine to the water.
  2. Set each layer in the fridge before adding the next.
  3. Turn out and cut into neat cubes.
  4. Serve on a white plate with a little whipped cream.

San Angeles cucumber elderflower spritzVegetarianNo alcohol

Cucumber, mint, lime and elderflower topped with sparkling water, no alcohol

Prep 8 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Muddle the cucumber, mint, lime and cordial up to 4 hours ahead in the jug, chilled; strain over ice and top with sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 8 mint leaves
  • 1 lime
  • 80ml elderflower cordial
  • 4 small ice cubes
  • 600ml sparkling water, cold
  1. Chop half the cucumber and muddle with the mint and lime juice in a jug. Add the cordial and stir.
  2. Strain into coupe glasses over an ice cube each.
  3. Top with sparkling water.
  4. Shave the rest of the cucumber into ribbons and fold one into each glass.
