Snacks

Drover's damperVegetarian

Baked damper bread with a crisp cross on top, served with butter and golden syrup.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cold butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  • extra butter, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub the butter into the flour until crumbly, 5 min.
  2. Mix in the milk with a knife until it just comes together.
  3. Shape into a round, score a cross on top and bake until hollow when tapped, 30 min.
  4. Serve with butter and golden syrup.

Faraway Downs corned beef sandwiches

Sliced corned beef, hot mustard and mustard pickles on buttered white bread.

Prep 25 min, cook 3 hr, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Beef cooked a day ahead, sandwiches made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg corned silverside
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp peppercorns
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp hot mustard
  • 4 tbsp mustard pickles
  1. Simmer the silverside with the onion and peppercorns, 3 hr.
  2. Cool the beef in its liquid, 30 min.
  3. Slice thinly.
  4. Butter the bread and pile on the beef with mustard and mustard pickles.
  5. Cut into triangles.

Mains

Campfire beef and vegetables

Beef chuck, carrots and potatoes slow-cooked in stock until it falls apart.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hr 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 600g potatoes, chopped
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  1. Brown the beef in the vegetable oil in a camp oven or heavy pot, 15 min.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, potatoes, stock, thyme and pepper.
  3. Cover and cook over coals or at 160°C until the beef falls apart, 3 hr.

Darwin crisp-skin barra with lemon butter

Barramundi pan-fried skin down until crackling, with lemon butter, chips and a wedge of lemon.

How it is made
  • 4 barramundi fillets
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Barramundi pan-fried skin side down until the skin crackles, with brown lemon butter, thick chips and lemon. Pat 4 barramundi fillets dry and score the skin. Fry skin side down in hot oil for 5 minutes, pressing flat, then turn for 1 minute. Melt 60g butter until nutty, add the juice of a lemon and spoon over. Serve with thick-cut chips fried twice and lemon wedges.

Passionfruit pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue with white vinegar, whipped cream and passionfruit.

Prep 3 min, cook 2 hr, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Base up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 3 passionfruit, pulp
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  1. Heat the oven to 120°C. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy, 20 min.
  2. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar and shape on baking paper.
  3. Bake for 90 minutes.
  4. Cool in the oven, 30 min.
  5. Top with cream and passionfruit.

Drinks

Drover's rum and ginger beer billy punch

Cold black tea, dark rum, lime and ginger beer in a big pot, ladled into mugs.

Prep 15 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Tea can be chilled a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml strong black tea
  • 240ml dark rum
  • 4 limes, juiced
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 6 cups ice
  • 750ml cold ginger beer
  • mint leaves, to serve
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  1. Brew the tea and chill, 1 hr.
  2. Mix the tea with the rum, lime juice and sugar, stirring to dissolve.
  3. Add plenty of ice and top with ginger beer.
  4. Ladle into mugs with a lime wedge and mint. Leave out the rum for a booze free pot, 7 min.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the drive makes camp for the night.
