Snacks

Costume party cocktail franks

Cocktail frankfurts heated gently and piled up with tomato sauce

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cocktail frankfurts
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • toothpicks, to serve
  1. Bring a pot of water to a simmer.
  2. Add the frankfurts and heat without boiling so the skins do not split.
  3. Drain, pile into a bowl with toothpicks and serve with tomato sauce for dipping.

Kiwi onion dip with chippiesVegetarian

Creamy French onion dip with lemon, salted potato chips to dunk

Prep 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet French onion soup mix
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 200g potato chips
  1. Stir the soup mix into the cream with the lemon juice.
  2. Chill until thick, stirring once.
  3. Serve in a bowl surrounded by chips for dipping.

Kiwi mince and cheese pie bitesOn the printed menu

Peppery beef in thick gravy, a slice of tasty cheese, puff lids, for Jarrod's hometown

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 6 slices tasty cheese
  • 1 egg
  • tomato sauce
  1. Peppery beef mince in rich gravy with a slice of tasty cheese, baked in little shortcrust cases under flaky puff lids, tomato sauce for dunking. Brown 500g beef mince with 1 diced onion. Stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, 300ml beef stock, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and pepper, and simmer 15 minutes until thick. Cool. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 12 rounds from 2 shortcrust sheets and press into a greased muffin tin. Fill with mince, top each with a square of tasty cheese (6 slices), then a puff pastry round from 1 sheet. Brush with egg and bake 20 to 25 minutes.

Meaty Boy cheeseburgers

Fried beef patties with tasty cheese and lettuce in toasted buns

Prep 6 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 4 slices tasty cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 4 slices tinned beetroot
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Shape the mince into 4 patties and season.
  2. Fry the patties, adding a slice of cheese at the end.
  3. Toast the buns and build with lettuce, tomato, beetroot, the patty and tomato sauce.

Kiwi battered fish and chipsOn the printed menu

Beer-battered fish and thick chips wrapped in paper, takeaways the Kiwi way

How it is made
  • 4 hake or gurnard fillets
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300ml cold lager
  • 3L vegetable oil
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 2 lemons
  • tomato sauce
  1. Fresh white fish in a crisp beer batter with thick-cut double-fried chips, wrapped in paper with lemon, tartare sauce and tomato sauce. Cut 1kg potatoes into thick chips, soak 20 minutes and dry. Fry at 150°C for 6 minutes, drain. Whisk 200g self-raising flour with 300ml cold beer and a pinch of salt. Heat the oil to 180°C, fry the chips again 3 minutes until golden and keep warm. Dip 4 hake or gurnard fillets in flour, then batter, and fry 5 to 6 minutes. Mix 150g mayo with chopped pickles and capers for tartare. Wrap in paper with lemon.

Afghan biscuitsVegetarian

Crunchy cocoa cornflake biscuits, chocolate icing, walnut on top

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 175g plain flour
  • 25g cocoa powder, for the biscuits
  • 50g cornflakes
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder, for the icing
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 16 walnut halves
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, then mix in the flour, cocoa and cornflakes.
  2. Roll into 16 balls and flatten slightly.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Cool on a rack.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with the cocoa for the icing and the water, ice each biscuit and top with a walnut half.

Fight Man final lemon, lime and bittersVegetarian

Lime cordial, bitters and lemonade over ice, pink and fizzy

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml lime cordial
  • aromatic bitters, 16 dashes
  • 800ml lemonade
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 lime
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice.
  2. Add lime cordial and bitters to each, top with lemonade and stir until it turns pink.
  3. Garnish with lemon and lime wedges.
