Snacks

Thurman's toasted peanut butter and jamVegetarian

Peanut butter and strawberry jam sandwiches dipped in cinnamon egg and fried in butter

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of thick white bread
  • 4 tbsp crunchy peanut butter
  • 4 tbsp strawberry jam
  • 2 eggs
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Make 4 sandwiches from the thick white bread, with peanut butter on one slice and jam on the other.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk and cinnamon.
  3. Dip each sandwich briefly on both sides.
  4. Fry in butter until golden on each side.
  5. Cut into triangles and dust with icing sugar.

Mains

Diner roast turkey with stuffing and gravy

Brined turkey breast, stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy and cranberry sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 310 min · Make ahead: Rest 20 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg turkey breast
  • 4 tbsp salt, for the brine
  • 2l water, for the brine
  • 1 packet of stuffing mix
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled
  • 60g butter
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 jar of cranberry sauce
  1. Stir the salt into the water until dissolved, then brine the turkey in it for 3 hours and pat dry.
  2. Roast at 170C until it reaches 70C inside.
  3. Bake the stuffing mix in a dish alongside.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter.
  5. Make gravy from the pan juices, flour and stock.
  6. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Advent calendar chocolatesVegetarian

Dark and milk chocolate squares topped with toasted hazelnut or sea salt

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 150g milk chocolate
  • 12 hazelnuts, toasted
  • 1 pinch of sea salt
  1. Melt the dark and milk chocolate separately.
  2. Spoon into small squares on baking paper or an ice cube tray.
  3. Top half with a toasted hazelnut and half with sea salt.
  4. Set in the fridge.
  5. Line them up in a numbered tray.

Christmas fruit cake slicesVegetarian

Dense brandy-soaked fruit cake with almonds, baked slow and cut in slices

Prep 30 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Soak the fruit in the brandy overnight, up to 1 day ahead; the baked cake keeps up to 2 weeks, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg mixed dried fruit
  • 125ml brandy
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g brown sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 1 handful of almonds, chopped
  1. Soak the fruit in the brandy overnight.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar and beat in the eggs.
  3. Fold in the fruit, flour, spice and almonds.
  4. Bake in a lined tin at 150C.
  5. Cool and slice thin.

Drinks

Willie's bourbon and ginger highballVegetarian

Bourbon, lime and spicy ginger beer over ice with a cinnamon stick

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the bourbon and lime juice in a jug up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; pour over ice and top with ginger beer to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 600ml cold spicy ginger beer
  • 4 cup ice
  • 4 lime wedges
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  1. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice.
  2. Add the bourbon and lime juice to each.
  3. Top with ginger beer and stir once.
  4. Add a lime wedge and a cinnamon stick. Skip the bourbon for the non drinkers.
