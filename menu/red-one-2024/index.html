Snacks

MORA command centre loaded potato skins

Crisp potato skins with cheddar, bacon, sour cream and chilli honey.

Prep 4 min, cook 86 min · Make ahead: Bake and scoop the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; crisp, fill and top the skins on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 medium potatoes
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 4 rashers bacon, cooked and chopped
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Prick the potatoes and bake at 200°C until tender.
  2. Halve, scoop out most of the flesh and brush the skins with melted butter and salt.
  3. Bake skin-side up, then flip.
  4. Fill with cheddar and bacon and bake until melted.
  5. Top with sour cream, spring onion and jalapenos, and drizzle with honey mixed with chilli flakes.

Naughty List maple pecan pretzel crunchVegetarian

Maple roasted pecans, almonds and pretzels with chocolate and cranberries.

Prep 6 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pecans
  • 100g almonds
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g mini pretzels
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 80g dried cranberries
  1. Toss the pecans and almonds with the maple syrup, cinnamon and a pinch of salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray with the pretzels and roast at 170°C, stirring once.
  3. Cool completely so the glaze crisps.
  4. Toss with the chocolate and cranberries and tip into a big bowl.

Mains

Red One's training day steak plate

Grilled rump, parsley chimichurri, sweet potato wedges, broccolini.

Prep 7 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Make the chimichurri up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; roast the wedges, grill the steak and broccolini on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 800g rump steak
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  1. Toss the sweet potato with the oil, paprika and salt and roast at 220°C.
  2. Blitz the parsley, garlic, chilli, vinegar and olive oil into chimichurri.
  3. Season the steak and grill over high heat, then rest and slice.
  4. Grill the broccolini.
  5. Spoon the chimichurri over the steak and serve with the wedges and broccolini.

Command canteen roast turkey with cranberry

Roast turkey with cranberry sauce and gravy. Christmas Eve on duty at North Pole Command.

Prep 13 min, cook 78 min · Make ahead: Butter the turkey up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast roast, skin on
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1 small bunch sage
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 250g cranberry sauce
  1. Mix the butter with chopped sage, garlic and salt and push it under the turkey skin.
  2. Roast the turkey at 190°C with the potatoes tossed in oil around it until the juices run clear.
  3. Rest the turkey under foil. Stir the flour into the pan juices, whisk in the stock and simmer into gravy.
  4. Slice the turkey and serve with the potatoes, gravy and cranberry sauce.

Snow globe red velvet crinkle cookiesVegetarian

Red cocoa cookies with white chocolate, rolled in icing sugar.

Prep 9 min, cook 16 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and chill the dough up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; roll in icing sugar and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 115g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp full cream milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tube red food colouring paste
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 100g white chocolate, chopped
  • 60g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then add the egg, milk, vanilla and red colouring.
  2. Mix in the flour, cocoa, baking powder and a pinch of salt, then fold in the white chocolate.
  3. Chill the dough.
  4. Roll into balls, roll each in icing sugar and bake at 180°C.
  5. Cool on the tray.

Callum Drift's spiced hot cocoaVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot chocolate with cinnamon, brown sugar and a pinch of chilli.

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Heat the milk with the sugar, cinnamon and chilli up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat, whisk in the chocolate and top with cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 pinch chilli powder
  • 120g dark chocolate, chopped (plus a little to grate)
  • 50g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Heat the milk with the sugar, cinnamon and chilli until steaming.
  2. Remove the cinnamon and whisk in the chocolates and vanilla until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and a little grated chocolate.
