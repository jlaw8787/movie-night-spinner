San Francisco sourdoughVegetarian

Warm sourdough with salted butter and oil with dukkah

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the olive oil and dukkah dip up to 1 day ahead; warm the loaf just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • 125g soft salted butter
  • 100ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp dukkah
  1. Warm the loaf at 200C.
  2. Tear into chunks.
  3. Serve with butter and a shallow dish of olive oil with dukkah scattered on top for dipping.

Fisherman's Wharf prawns

Prawns tossed in hot olive oil with garlic, chilli and butter

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Slice the garlic and chilli up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the prawns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 chilli
  • 600g green prawns, peeled
  • 60g butter
  • 60ml white wine
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  1. Slice the garlic and chilli.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the garlic and chilli briefly.
  3. Toss the prawns over high heat.
  4. Add the butter, wine and parsley. Serve with the baguette for the juices.

VX green pea aranciniVegetarian

Goodspeed's little green glass beads, the ones nobody can drop

Prep 50 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make and roll the arancini up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion
  • 30g butter
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1l vegetable stock
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 60g parmesan, grated
  • 100g mozzarella
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  1. Soften the chopped onion in the butter, stir in the rice, then add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 18 minutes until thick.
  2. Blitz the peas with half the mint and stir through the risotto with the parmesan. Spread on a tray.
  3. Chill for 1 hour until firm.
  4. Roll into 16 balls with a cube of mozzarella in each. Roll in flour, then beaten egg, then panko.
  5. Heat the oil to 180C and fry in batches for 4 minutes until golden. Drain on paper towel.
  6. Mix the mayonnaise with the lemon zest and juice and the rest of the mint, chopped, and serve with the arancini.

Mason's hotel room service

Butter-basted rib eye with béarnaise and frites

Prep 30 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Do the first fry of the chips up to 3 hours ahead; the bearnaise holds for 30 minutes in a bowl over warm water, and the steaks are seared on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 2 sprigs tarragon
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 200g clarified butter, warm
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 40g butter
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 2 garlic cloves
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips.
  2. Fry the chips once in the vegetable oil at a lower heat and drain.
  3. Reduce the vinegar with the tarragon until a couple of tablespoons remain.
  4. Whisk the reduction with the yolks over low heat and drizzle in the warm clarified butter.
  5. Sear the rib eyes, then baste with butter, thyme and garlic.
  6. Rest the steaks and fry the chips again until crisp.
  7. Serve the steaks with the sauce and chips.

San Francisco chocolate squaresVegetarian

Dense baked brownie squares with toasted walnuts

Prep 24 min, cook 61 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g walnuts
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line a tin. Melt the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Toast the walnuts and fold them in.
  4. Pour into the tin and bake.
  5. Sprinkle with flaky salt and cool.
  6. Cut into squares.

Mason's single malt Rob RoyVegetarian

Single malt Scotch stirred with sweet vermouth and bitters

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Chill the coupes in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; stir the Scotch, vermouth and bitters over ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 coupe glasses
  • 200ml single malt Scotch
  • 80ml sweet vermouth
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 tray ice
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 1 orange
  1. Chill the coupes.
  2. Stir the Scotch, vermouth and bitters over ice until icy.
  3. Strain into the coupes, drop in a cherry and twist orange peel over each.
