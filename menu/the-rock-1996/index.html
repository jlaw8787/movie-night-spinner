Snacks

Sourdough with salted butterVegetarian

Warm sourdough torn thick, soft salted butter, oil and sesame to dip.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Mix the olive oil and sesame dip up to 1 day ahead; warm the loaf just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • 125g soft salted butter
  • 100ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp toasted sesame
  1. Warm the loaf at 200°C, 8 min.
  2. Tear into chunks.
  3. Serve with butter and a shallow dish of olive oil with toasted sesame and pepper scattered on top for dipping.

Fisherman's Wharf shrimp

Shrimp tossed in hot olive oil with garlic, chilli and butter.

Prep 11 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Slice the garlic and chilli up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cook the prawns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 chilli
  • 600g green prawns, peeled
  • 60g butter
  • 60ml white wine
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  1. Slice the garlic and chilli.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the garlic and chilli briefly, 3 min.
  3. Toss the prawns over high heat.
  4. Add the butter, wine and parsley. Serve with the baguette for the juices.

Mains

Mason's hotel room service

Butter-basted rib eye with béarnaise and frites.

Prep 10 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Do the first fry of the chips up to 3 hours ahead; the bearnaise holds for 30 minutes in a bowl over warm water, and the steaks are seared on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 2 sprigs of tarragon
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 200g clarified butter, warm
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 40g butter
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 2 garlic cloves
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips.
  2. Fry the chips once in the vegetable oil at a lower heat and drain, 12 min.
  3. Reduce the vinegar with the tarragon until a couple of tablespoons remain.
  4. Whisk the reduction with the yolks over low heat and drizzle in the warm clarified butter, 8 min.
  5. Sear the rib eyes, then baste with butter, thyme and garlic, 10 min.
  6. Rest the steaks and fry the chips again until crisp.
  7. Serve the steaks with the sauce and chips.

San Francisco cioppino and garlic toast

Fish, clams and mussels stewed in tomato and white wine, garlic toast to mop it up.

How it is made
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • chilli flakes
  • 250ml white wine
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 500g clams
  • 500g mussels
  • 400g snapper fillets
  • 12 green king prawns
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  • olive oil
  1. Fennel, garlic and chilli simmered in tomato and white wine with prawns, mussels, clams and snapper, served with charred garlic sourdough. Soften 1 sliced fennel bulb, 1 onion and 4 garlic cloves in olive oil with a pinch of chilli flakes. Add 250ml white wine, 400g crushed tomatoes and 500ml fish stock and simmer 20 minutes. Add 500g clams and 500g mussels, cover 3 minutes, then 400g snapper chunks and 12 green prawns for 4 minutes more until everything is cooked. Finish with parsley. Grill thick sourdough slices rubbed with garlic and olive oil.

Dark chocolate fudge squaresVegetarian

Dense baked brownie squares with toasted walnuts.

Prep 11 min, cook 43 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g walnuts
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a tin. Melt the chocolate with the butter, 8 min.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Toast the walnuts and fold them in, 10 min.
  4. Pour into the tin and bake, 25 min.
  5. Sprinkle with flaky salt and cool, 30 min.
  6. Cut into squares.

Drinks

Single malt Rob Roy

Single malt Scotch stirred with sweet vermouth and bitters.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the coupes in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; stir the Scotch, vermouth and bitters over ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 coupe glasses
  • 200ml single malt Scotch
  • 80ml sweet vermouth
  • 8 dashes of aromatic bitters
  • 1 tray ice
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 1 orange
  1. Chill the coupes, 15 min.
  2. Stir the Scotch, vermouth and bitters over ice until icy.
  3. Strain into the coupes, drop in a cherry and twist orange peel over each.
