Moses' crawfish and corn boil

Spiced crawfish boiled with corn and potatoes, with lemon butter.

Prep 0 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Mix the spice and lemon butter up to 2 days ahead; boil to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg cooked yabbies or crawfish, in the shell
  • 500g baby potatoes
  • 2 corn cobs, cut in thirds
  • 3 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 80g butter
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Bring a big pot of water to the boil with the seasoning, half a lemon and the garlic, 5 min.
  2. Boil the potatoes until nearly tender, then add the corn for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the yabbies just to heat through, then drain, 3 min.
  4. Melt the butter with the remaining lemon juice and parsley, pour over and tip it all out on the table, 17 min.

Evelle's round jalapeno cheese ballsVegetarian

Breaded cheddar and jalapeño balls with ranch dip, round, which isn't funny.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roll and crumb the balls up to a day ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese, softened
  • 200g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 3 tbsp pickled jalapenos, chopped
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150ml ranch dressing
  1. Mix the cream cheese, cheddar and jalapenos and roll into 20 balls, then chill until firm, 15 min.
  2. Roll in flour, then egg, then panko.
  3. Fry at 180°C in batches until golden, 10 min.
  4. Drain and serve with ranch.

Unpainted Arizona beef chimichangas

Crisp fried beef burritos, salsa, sour cream.

Prep 28 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Braise and shred the beef up to 2 days ahead; roll and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, in big chunks
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 150g Monterey Jack or cheddar, grated
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 250g tomato salsa
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Brown the beef in a heavy pot, add the onion, garlic, spices and tomatoes and a cup of water, 15 min.
  2. Cover and simmer gently until the beef falls apart, then shred it in the sauce, 1 hr 30 min.
  3. Spoon beef and cheese onto each tortilla, fold in the sides and roll up tight.
  4. Shallow-fry seam side down first, turning, until crisp and golden, 20 min.
  5. Serve with salsa and sour cream.

Lone Biker's scorched-earth chicken

Chilli lime chicken charred on the grill, with corn on the cob.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder, chopped
  • 3 limes
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 60g feta, finely crumbled
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Mix the chilli, juice of a lime, garlic, oil and salt and coat the chicken.
  2. Grill the corn over high heat, turning, until charred all over, 15 min.
  3. Grill the chicken over high heat until blackened in spots and cooked through, then rest, 15 min.
  4. Brush the corn with mayo, roll in feta and dust with chilli.
  5. Slice the chicken, scatter with coriander and serve with the corn and lime wedges.

Call-a-cop cinnamon doughnutsVegetarian

Hot doughnuts rolled in cinnamon sugar, with a caramel dip.

Prep 40 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the dough the night before and prove it in the fridge; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 350g plain flour
  • 7g sachet dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar, plus 100g for rolling
  • 180ml warm milk
  • 1 egg
  • 40g soft butter
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g dulce de leche
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, 50g sugar, milk, egg and butter into a soft dough and knead until smooth.
  2. Cover and leave until doubled, 1 hr.
  3. Roll out 1.5cm thick, cut rings and leave to puff for 20 minutes.
  4. Fry at 175°C, a minute or so a side, until golden, 15 min.
  5. Toss in the cinnamon sugar and serve with warmed dulce de leche.

Desert trailer prickly pear margarita

Tequila, lime and prickly pear, salted rim.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the limes up to 4 hours ahead; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 90ml orange liqueur
  • 120ml prickly pear syrup
  • 4 limes
  • 2 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Rub the glass rims with lime and dip in salt.
  2. Shake the tequila, liqueur, syrup and lime juice hard with ice.
  3. Strain over fresh ice.
