Snacks

Automat sandwiches

Leg ham and Swiss on white, mustard butter, crusts off, wrapped in paper, I'm walkin' here

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 40g soft butter
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 200g leg ham
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  1. Mash the butter with the Dijon and spread over the bread.
  2. Fill with the ham and Swiss cheese.
  3. Cut off the crusts, cut into quarters and wrap in baking paper.

Cinnamon doughnut holes, coffee custardVegetarian

Hot dough balls in spiced sugar, custard for dunking, bought with Joe's last coins

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm, same day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 50g caster sugar, for dough
  • 1 egg
  • 150ml milk
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 80g caster sugar, for rolling
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 300ml thick custard
  • 2 tsp instant coffee
  1. Mix the flour, sugar, egg, milk and butter into a soft dough and roll into 24 small balls.
  2. Fry in oil at 170°C in batches until golden and cooked through. Drain.
  3. Roll the hot doughnuts in the sugar mixed with the cinnamon.
  4. Warm the custard with the coffee dissolved in a little hot water and serve for dipping.

Mains

Florida coconut prawns

Butterflied prawns in crisp panko, sweet chilli and lime, for the Greyhound south

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g panko breadcrumbs
  • 80g shredded coconut
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 limes
  1. Peel and butterfly the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Dust in flour, dip in beaten egg, then press into the panko mixed with coconut.
  3. Fry in oil at 180°C in batches until golden. Serve with sweet chilli sauce and lime wedges.

Sweets

Florida orange cakeVegetarian

Whole boiled citrus blitzed into an almond meal and egg batter, moist and flourless

Prep 5 min, cook 70 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 2 oranges
  • 6 eggs
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 250g almond meal
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  1. Boil the whole oranges in water until soft.
  2. Cool the oranges, then blitz smooth, skin and all.
  3. Beat the eggs with the sugar, then mix in the oranges, almond meal and baking powder. Pour into a lined 22cm tin.
  4. Bake at 180°C until set and golden. Cool in the tin.

Drinks

Ratso's Florida coconut pineapple coolerVegetarian

Coconut water, lime and fruit juice shaken with cream, over ice, white rum on the side

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix a jug without ice up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml coconut water
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 2 limes
  • 60ml coconut cream
  • 1 small pineapple
  • 120ml white rum, optional
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Juice the limes and cut a pineapple wedge for each glass.
  2. Shake the coconut water, pineapple juice, lime juice and coconut cream with ice until frothy. Strain into 4 ice-filled glasses, add rum on the side if you like, and garnish with pineapple and a lime wheel.

When to serve

Serve the mains when a cab nearly hits Ratso and he yells I'm walkin' here!
