Covent Garden cevapi rolls with ajvar

Grilled beef and lamb sausages in soft rolls with ajvar and sour cream.

Prep 9 min, cook 11 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix and shape the cevapi up to 1 day ahead and chill, covered, so they firm up; grill and build the rolls on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 200g lamb mince
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 1 jar ajvar
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  1. Mix the minces with the garlic, paprika, bicarb and salt and knead briefly.
  2. Roll into short fingers and chill, 30 min.
  3. Grill over high heat, turning, until charred, 8 min.
  4. Split and toast the rolls and spread with ajvar, 3 min.
  5. Add cevapi to each and top with sour cream, onion and parsley.

Petra's cheese burekVegetarian

Coiled filo pie of feta and cottage cheese.

Prep 19 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 375g filo pastry
  • 250g feta, crumbled
  • 250g cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C and butter a 25cm round tin, 3 min.
  2. Mix the feta, cottage cheese and eggs.
  3. Brush two filo sheets with butter, lay one on the other, spoon a line of filling along one long edge and roll into a log.
  4. Coil the logs into the tin from the middle out and brush the top with butter.
  5. Bake 35 to 40 minutes until deep golden. Rest 10 minutes and serve in wedges with yoghurt.

Petra's stuffed peppers with yoghurt

Peppers stuffed with beef and rice, baked in tomato, garlic yoghurt.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Fill the capsicums up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night and mix the garlic yoghurt fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves (2 for the filling, 1 for the yoghurt)
  • 500g beef mince
  • 100g long grain rice
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 large red capsicums
  • 700g passata
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Fry the onion and two of the garlic cloves, add the mince and brown, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the rice, both paprikas, salt and a little water and cook briefly, 5 min.
  3. Cut the tops off the capsicums, remove the seeds and fill.
  4. Stand in a dish, pour around the passata mixed with water and bake covered at 180°C, 50 min.
  5. Mix the yoghurt with the remaining crushed garlic. Serve with crusty bread.

Tom's shelter Christmas turkey

Breast with sage butter under the skin, roast potatoes, sprouts and gravy, George Michael on.

Prep 5 min, cook 116 min · Make ahead: Make the sage butter and peel the potatoes up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast roast
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 lemon
  • 1.2kg roasting potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g Brussels sprouts
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Mash the butter with chopped sage, lemon zest and salt and push it under the turkey skin, 10 min.
  2. Roast the turkey for 1 hour 20 minutes, with the parboiled potatoes tossed in 2 tbsp of the oil in a second tray.
  3. Rest the turkey for 20 minutes. Halve the sprouts and roast them in the rest of the oil for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir the flour into the roasting pan juices on the stove, whisk in the stock and simmer for 4 minutes for gravy.
  5. Carve and serve with the potatoes, sprouts and gravy.

Sweets

Santa's Danish admirer mince pies

Shortcrust mince pies served with orange cream.

Prep 17 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Bake the pies up to 2 days ahead, airtight, and warm in a 160°C oven for 5 minutes; whip the orange juice cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 jar fruit mince (400g)
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 orange, zested and juiced
  1. Cut rounds from two pastry sheets and press into a greased patty pan tin.
  2. Fill each with fruit mince. Cut stars from the third sheet and lay on top.
  3. Brush with egg and bake at 190°C until golden, 18 min.
  4. Whip the cream with some of the icing sugar, the orange zest and a tablespoon of the orange juice.
  5. Dust the warm pies with icing sugar and serve with the orange cream.

Year-round Christmas shop gingerbreadVegetarian

Spiced gingerbread with golden syrup, cinnamon and orange.

Prep 27 min, cook 28 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 1 egg
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Melt the butter with the golden syrup and sugar. Mix in the flour, spices, orange zest and egg, 8 min.
  2. Chill the dough, 30 min.
  3. Roll out and cut stars and snowflakes.
  4. Bake at 180°C, then cool, 20 min.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with lemon juice and pipe on snowflake patterns.

Covent Garden mulled grape juiceNo alcohol

Red grape and apple juice simmered with orange, cinnamon, star anise and clove.

Prep 3 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Steep the tea and warm everything with the spices up to 1 day ahead, then chill; reheat gently to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml red grape juice
  • 300ml cloudy apple juice
  • 2 black tea bags
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 200ml orange juice
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 12 cloves
  • 4 star anise
  • 2 oranges
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 4 minutes, then remove.
  2. Pour the grape juice, apple juice and tea into a saucepan with the orange juice, sugar, cinnamon, cloves, star anise and one sliced orange.
  3. Warm gently, keeping it under a simmer, 15 min.
  4. Ladle into heatproof glasses with a wheel cut from the second orange.

When to serve

Serve the sweets when Kate clocks on at the Christmas shop.
