Snacks

Treehouse s'mores

Toasted marshmallows and milk chocolate squashed between crisp cookies.

Prep 0 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best toasted fresh over the flame; set out the biscuits and chocolate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 16 marshmallows
  • 32 digestive biscuits
  • 100g milk chocolate
  1. Toast the marshmallows over a flame until blistered and molten.
  2. Squash each between two biscuits with a square of chocolate so it melts. Eat them by the handful.

Honey roasted ballpark peanutsVegetarian

Peanuts in the shell roasted with honey butter, sea salt and smoked paprika.

Prep 0 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 400g raw peanuts in shell
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 paper bags
  1. Spread the peanuts on a tray and roast at 180°C.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, salt and paprika.
  3. Toss the hot peanuts through the glaze, spread back on the tray and roast until sticky.
  4. Cool so the glaze sets, then divide into paper bags with a bowl for the shells.

Mains

Fourth of July hot dogs

Grilled franks with slow cooked onions, mustard and sweet relish in toasted buns.

Prep 0 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions in butter up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks and toast the buns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • yellow mustard, to serve
  • sweet relish, to serve
  1. Slice the onions and cook slowly in the butter.
  2. Score the franks and grill over coals until blistered.
  3. Toast the buns and fill with franks, onions, mustard and sweet relish.

Backyard double cheeseburgers and corn

Two smashed chuck patties, melted cheddar, pickles, buttered cobs.

How it is made
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 50g butter
  1. Two thin chuck patties smashed on a hot grill, cheddar melted over, stacked with pickles, onion and burger sauce in a soft bun, with buttered corn. Roll 800g chuck mince into 8 balls. Mix 4 tbsp mayo, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tbsp mustard and 2 tbsp chopped pickles for sauce. Heat a hotplate to very hot, smash the balls flat, season and cook 2 minutes, flip and lay a slice of cheddar on each. Stack two per toasted bun with lettuce, onion, pickles and sauce. Boil 4 corn cobs for 6 minutes and roll in butter and salt.

Carnival fairy floss and toffee applesVegetarian

Apples dipped in red toffee, served with a bag of fairy floss.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 apples
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 125ml water
  • red food colouring, a few drops
  • 1 bag fairy floss
  1. Wash and dry the apples and push sticks in. Oil a sheet of paper.
  2. Boil the sugar, water and golden syrup to 150°C, then add the colouring.
  3. Dip and twirl the apples and set on the oiled paper.
  4. Serve with a bag of fairy floss.

Wendy Peffercorn watermelon ice blocks

Blended with lime and a little sugar, frozen around whole raspberries, dipped in sour lime sugar.

How it is made
  • 800g seedless watermelon
  • 3 limes
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 125g raspberries
  • 8 ice block sticks
  1. Fresh watermelon blended with lime and a little sugar, frozen in moulds with whole raspberries, then dipped in a little sour lime sugar. Blend 800g seedless watermelon with the juice of 2 limes and 2 tbsp caster sugar, then strain. Drop 3 raspberries into each of 8 ice block moulds and pour in the juice. Freeze for 1 hour, push in sticks and freeze 5 hours more. Mix 2 tbsp caster sugar with the zest of 1 lime. Unmould and dip the tips in the lime sugar to serve.

Drinks

Sandlot strawberry lemonadeNo alcohol

Strawberry syrup, fresh lemon juice and soda water over lemon wheels.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Syrup up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 6 lemons
  • 1 L soda water
  • ice, to serve
  1. Hull the strawberries. Simmer with the sugar and water, then mash and strain.
  2. Cool the syrup.
  3. Squeeze five lemons. Mix the syrup, lemon juice and soda water in a big jug.
  4. Add lemon wheels from the last lemon, a few sliced strawberries and plenty of ice. Stir well and pour.
