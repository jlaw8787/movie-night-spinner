Snacks

Brad's rainbow rice paper rollsVegetarian

Griff moves in and stays for dinner. Vermicelli, carrot, capsicum and cucumber rolls

Prep 30 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Roll up to 4 hours ahead under a damp tea towel in the fridge; make the peanut dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rice vermicelli
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 12 rice paper sheets
  • 3 tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 3 tbsp water
  1. Soak the rice vermicelli in boiling water 5 minutes, drain and cool.
  2. Cut the carrot, the red capsicum and the cucumber into thin strips and slice the avocado.
  3. Dip a rice paper sheet in warm water for 10 seconds, lay flat and add the vegetables, noodles and mint, then roll up tightly.
  4. Repeat until all the rolls are made.
  5. Whisk the peanut butter, the hoisin, the lime juice and the warm water for dipping.

Dusty's cool dad nachos

Corn chips, spiced beef, cheese. Tumor grows on you.

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Brown the beef with the taco seasoning up to 2 days ahead and chill; assemble and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 sachet taco seasoning
  • 300g corn chips
  • 200g tasty cheese
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Brown the beef mince with taco seasoning.
  2. Spread the corn chips on a tray, top with the beef and the grated cheese and bake at 200C for 10 minutes.
  3. Finish with salsa and the sour cream.

Mains

Backyard dad-off smash burgers

Brad and Dusty, co-dads at one grill. Smashed beef patties, cheese, onion, pickles

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions and mix the burger sauce up to 2 days ahead, chilled; roll the beef balls up to 1 day ahead and smash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 20g butter
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • tomato sauce, to taste
  • mustard, to taste
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 8 cheese slices
  • 4 potato buns
  1. Slowly cook the sliced onions in butter for 15 minutes until golden.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, the tomato sauce, the mustard and the chopped pickles.
  3. Roll the beef mince into balls.
  4. On a very hot barbecue plate, smash each flat, season and cook 2 minutes.
  5. Flip, add a cheese slice and cook 1 minute.
  6. Toast the potato buns, spread with sauce and stack the patties each with onion and pickles.

Brad's grilled corn saladVegetarian

Charred corn, tomato, avocado, lime and coriander. Brad made it, of course

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Grill the corn up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; add the avocado just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 avocados, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely sliced
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  1. Rub the corn with oil and salt and grill, turning, until charred in spots.
  2. Cut the kernels off the cobs once cool enough to hold.
  3. Toss with the tomatoes, onion, avocado, chopped coriander, lime juice and salt.

Sweets

Daddy-daughter dance cupcakesVegetarian

Jam-filled vanilla cupcakes, pink buttercream. Megan picks Dusty.

Prep 40 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Bake and fill the cupcakes up to 1 day ahead, airtight; make and pipe the icing on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 325g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 120g raspberry jam
  • 400g icing sugar
  • pink food colouring, to taste
  • sprinkles, to taste
  • 12 cupcake cases
  1. Heat the oven to 175C.
  2. Beat the butter and the caster sugar, add the eggs and the vanilla, then the self-raising flour and the milk.
  3. Divide between the paper cases and bake 18 minutes.
  4. Cool.
  5. Cut a small cone from the top of each, fill with raspberry jam and replace.
  6. Beat the butter with the icing sugar, the milk and pink colouring and pipe on top.
  7. Finish with sprinkles.

Drinks

Smooth jazz strawberry basil lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

For Brad's $18,000 Lakers seats. Strawberry basil syrup, lemon and soda over ice

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make, strain and chill the strawberry basil syrup up to 3 days ahead; add the lemon juice, ice and soda water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 250g strawberries
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 5 lemons
  • 800ml soda water
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Simmer the caster sugar, the water and the sliced strawberries for 5 minutes.
  2. Take off the heat, add a handful of basil and steep 15 minutes, then strain and chill.
  3. Squeeze the lemons for the juice.
  4. Fill a jug with ice, add the syrup, lemon juice and the cold soda water and stir.
  5. Pour into glasses with sliced strawberries and a basil leaf.
