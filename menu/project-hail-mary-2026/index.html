Fist my bump aranciniVegetarian

Risotto balls fried crisp with a cheesy centre. Rocky learns the fist bump his way.

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the risotto up to 2 days ahead and chill; roll, crumb and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 250g arborio rice
  • 800ml hot vegetable stock
  • 60g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas
  • 100g mozzarella, cut in small cubes
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Soften the onion in the butter, stir in the rice, then add the stock a ladle at a time until creamy and tender.
  2. Stir in the parmesan and peas, spread on a tray and chill until firm.
  3. Roll into 12 balls around a cube of mozzarella, dip in egg and roll in panko.
  4. Fry at 180C until golden and hot in the middle.

Astrophage black bean dipVegetarian

Spiced black bean dip with chips. Astrophage toots to scoot.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 400g tins black beans, drained
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 limes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 60g feta, crumbled
  • 230g bag plain corn chips
  1. Warm the spices in the oil for a minute.
  2. Blend the beans, garlic, spiced oil and lime juice with a splash of water until smooth.
  3. Spread in a bowl, scatter with feta and serve with corn chips.

Mains

Time go fishing salt-baked snapper

Whole snapper baked in a salt crust. Rocky's fishing plan: time go fishing.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Stuff and crust the fish up to 2 hours ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg whole snapper, cleaned and scaled
  • 1 1/2kg coarse cooking salt
  • 3 egg whites
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 80g butter
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  1. Mix the salt with the egg whites until it feels like wet sand.
  2. Stuff the fish with lemon slices and thyme, lay it on a bed of salt and pack the rest over it.
  3. Bake at 200C, with the potatoes boiling alongside.
  4. Melt the butter with lemon juice, crack the crust at the table and serve with the potatoes.

Amaze amaze amaze five-spice pork belly

Five-spice pork belly with crackling. Amaze, amaze, amaze, says Rocky from Erid.

Prep 10 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Score and dry the pork overnight in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork belly, skin scored
  • 2 tsp Chinese five-spice
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 bunches bok choy, halved
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Rub the flesh side of the pork with the five-spice, soy and honey, keeping the skin dry, then rub salt into the skin.
  2. Roast at 160C, then turn up to 240C until the skin crackles.
  3. Cook the rice and steam the bok choy, then dress it with sesame oil.
  4. Rest the pork, chop into thick pieces and serve with the rice and greens.

Small-to-medium whoop pavlovaVegetarian

Pavlova with fruit. Grace calls a dimming Sun a small-to-medium whoop.

Prep 25 min, then 80 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the meringue up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 250g strawberries, halved
  1. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  2. Fold in the cornflour and vinegar and spread into a 20cm round on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120C, then turn off the oven and leave it inside to cool.
  4. Whip the cream, pile it on and top with strawberries and passionfruit.

Petrova line infrared spritzVegetarian

Aperol and prosecco spritz, glowing orange like the Petrova line

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill everything well; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml Aperol
  • 500ml prosecco
  • 250ml soda water
  • 1 blood orange, sliced
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Fill 4 wine glasses with ice and add 60ml Aperol to each.
  2. Top with prosecco and a splash of soda and tuck in a slice of blood orange.
