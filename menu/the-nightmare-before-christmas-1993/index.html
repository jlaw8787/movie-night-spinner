Snacks

Pumpkin King pastiesVegetarian

Roast pumpkin, feta and sage in crimped pastry with a jack-o'-lantern face.

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pumpkin, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Roast the pumpkin in oil until soft.
  2. Mash with the feta, sage and nutmeg and cool.
  3. Cut the pastry into rounds, fill and fold into half moons.
  4. Cut a little jack o'lantern face into each and brush with egg.
  5. Bake until golden.

Candy cane cheese twists

Pastry hooks for Sandy Claws, not the Easter Bunny.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 60g parmesan
  • 40g cheddar
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar tomato relish
  1. Puff pastry twisted into candy cane hooks, half coated in smoked paprika and parmesan for the red stripes, baked crisp with a tomato relish dip. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brush 2 sheets of puff pastry with beaten egg. Scatter one sheet with 60g grated parmesan and 2 tsp smoked paprika and leave the other plain with a little cheddar. Cut both into 1cm strips. Twist a red strip and a plain strip together, bend the top into a hook and lay on lined trays. Bake 14 minutes until golden. Serve with tomato relish.

Mains

Sally's soup, no nightshade

Pumpkin and carrot soup, sour cream spiderweb.

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Soup up to 3 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pumpkin, cut in chunks
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 L hot chicken stock
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Roast the pumpkin, carrots, garlic and thyme in oil until soft.
  2. Blend with the hot stock until smooth.
  3. Pour into bowls.
  4. Pipe circles of sour cream on top and drag a skewer from the centre outwards to make a spiderweb.

Halloween Town beef and pumpkin stew

Shin braised dark with pumpkin, carrot and smoked paprika until spoon-soft, from a black pot.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef shin
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 tin tomato paste
  • 1 litre beef stock
  • 600g pumpkin
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 200ml sour cream
  1. Beef shin braised dark with pumpkin, carrot, smoked paprika and stock until spoon-soft, served from a black pot with crusty bread. Brown 1.2kg diced beef shin in batches. Soften 1 onion, 2 carrots and 3 garlic cloves. Return the beef, add 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 1 litre beef stock, cover and simmer 2 hours. Add 600g pumpkin chunks and simmer 40 minutes more. Serve with crusty bread and a spoon of sour cream.

Sweets

Zero's ghost dog meringuesVegetarian

Crisp piped meringue ghosts with a dot of red icing for the nose.

Prep 25 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1 pinch of cream of tartar
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 1 tube red icing
  1. Heat the oven to 100°C and line trays. Whisk the egg whites with the cream of tartar to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Pipe ghostly teardrops onto the trays.
  4. Bake for 90 minutes.
  5. Cool and add a dot of red icing for the nose.

Oogie Boogie chocolate mousse dirt cupsVegetarian

Chocolate mousse under crushed cookie dirt with gummy worms.

Prep 22 min, cook 8 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 8 chocolate biscuits, crushed
  • 1 handful of gummy worms
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly.
  2. Whip the cream and whisk the egg whites to soft peaks.
  3. Stir the yolks into the chocolate, then fold in the cream and whites.
  4. Spoon into cups and chill.
  5. Cover with crushed biscuits and push in gummy worms.

Drinks

Witch's cauldron blackcurrant apple punchNo alcohol

Warm apple and blackcurrant juice simmered with cinnamon, star anise and cloves.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2 L cloudy apple juice
  • 300ml blackcurrant juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks, for simmering
  • 3 star anise
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 strip of orange peel
  • 4 cinnamon sticks, to serve
  1. Pour the apple and blackcurrant juice into a pot with the cinnamon sticks, star anise, cloves and orange peel.
  2. Bring to a simmer and keep just below simmering.
  3. Ladle into mugs with a cinnamon stick.

When to serve

Serve the soup when Sally brings Dr Finkelstein his lunch.
