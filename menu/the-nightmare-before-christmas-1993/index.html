Snacks

Pumpkin King pastiesVegetarian

Roast pumpkin, feta and sage in crimped pastry with a jack-o'-lantern face

Prep 35 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pumpkin, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Roast the pumpkin in oil until soft.
  2. Mash with the feta, sage and nutmeg and cool.
  3. Cut the pastry into rounds, fill and fold into half moons.
  4. Cut a little jack o'lantern face into each and brush with egg.
  5. Bake until golden.

Mains

Sally's soup, no nightshade

Pumpkin and carrot soup, sour cream spiderweb. No frog's breath.

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Soup up to 3 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pumpkin, cut in chunks
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1l hot chicken stock
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Roast the pumpkin, carrots, garlic and thyme in oil until soft.
  2. Blend with the hot stock until smooth.
  3. Pour into bowls.
  4. Pipe circles of sour cream on top and drag a skewer from the centre outwards to make a spiderweb.

Zero's ghost dog meringuesVegetarian

Crisp piped meringue ghosts with a dot of red icing for the nose

Prep 30 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1 pinch of cream of tartar
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 1 tube red icing
  1. Heat the oven to 100C and line trays. Whisk the egg whites with the cream of tartar to stiff peaks.
  2. Add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Pipe ghostly teardrops onto the trays.
  4. Bake for 90 minutes.
  5. Cool and add a dot of red icing for the nose.

Oogie Boogie chocolate mousse dirt cupsVegetarian

Chocolate mousse under crushed cookie dirt with gummy worms

Prep 30 min, cook 240 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 8 chocolate biscuits, crushed
  • 1 handful of gummy worms
  1. Melt the chocolate and cool slightly.
  2. Whip the cream and whisk the egg whites to soft peaks.
  3. Stir the yolks into the chocolate, then fold in the cream and whites.
  4. Spoon into cups and chill.
  5. Cover with crushed biscuits and push in gummy worms.

Drinks

Witch's cauldron blackcurrant apple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Warm apple and blackcurrant juice simmered with cinnamon, star anise and cloves

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l cloudy apple juice
  • 300ml blackcurrant juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks, for simmering
  • 3 star anise
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 strip of orange peel
  • 4 cinnamon sticks, to serve
  1. Pour the apple and blackcurrant juice into a pot with the cinnamon sticks, star anise, cloves and orange peel.
  2. Bring to a simmer and keep just below simmering.
  3. Ladle into mugs with a cinnamon stick.

When to serve

Serve the soup when Sally brings Dr Finkelstein his lunch.
