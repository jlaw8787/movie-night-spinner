A Night at the Roxbury (1998) movie night dinner menu

Two brothers who work in their dad's fake plant shop try every night to get into the hottest club in LA. Head bobbing optional. Snacks mandatory.

The menu is set at The Roxbury Velvet Rope, from the world of the film.

Snacks

Roxbury club sliders

Mini cheeseburgers, pickles, slider buns. Steak's at home.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Shape the beef mince into small patties and season.
  2. Cook on a hot pan for 2 minutes a side, adding half a cheddar slice to each at the end.
  3. Put in the slider buns with pickles and sauce.

Fake plant shop cruditésVegetarian

Carrot, celery, cucumber and capsicum sticks with a herb sour cream dip.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Cut the vegetables and make the dip up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; stand the sticks in the jars on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lemon
  1. Cut the carrots, the celery sticks, the cucumber and the red capsicum into sticks and stand them in jars.
  2. Blitz the sour cream with parsley, chives and a squeeze of lemon for the dip.

Mains

Sunset Strip chipotle chicken tacos

Smoky chipotle lime chicken thighs in warm tortillas.

Prep 17 min, cook 11 min, then 52 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 4 limes
  • ground cumin, to taste
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 avocados
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Mix the chipotle in adobo, the crushed garlic cloves, the juice of the lime, the cumin and the oil.
  2. Marinate the chicken thigh fillets for 30 minutes.
  3. Slice the red onion thin and soak in lime juice and salt for 20 minutes.
  4. Grill the chicken on a hot barbecue for 5 minutes a side and slice.
  5. Mash the avocados with lime and salt.
  6. Warm the small tortillas and fill with avocado, chicken, onion and coriander.

Mr Zadir's lamb koobideh

Grilled minced kebabs with grated onion and turmeric, saffron rice, charred tomatoes, herb yoghurt.

Prep 15 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Mix and shape the kebabs up to 1 day ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tsp ground sumac
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 30g butter
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Grate the onion, squeeze out the juice and mix with the lamb, turmeric and salt until sticky. Shape around 8 flat skewers.
  2. Soak the saffron in 2 tbsp hot water. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, rest for 5, then stir through the saffron and butter.
  3. Grill the kebabs and halved tomatoes on a hot grill for 5 minutes a side.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with chopped mint and salt.
  5. Dust the kebabs with sumac and serve on the rice with the tomatoes, yoghurt and parsley.

Sweets

Neon pink raspberry cheesecake cupsVegetarian

Biscuit base and raspberry cream cheese mousse in glasses.

Prep 20 min, cook 4 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain sweet biscuits
  • 60g butter
  • 250g raspberries
  • 110g icing sugar
  • 250g cream cheese
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 30g white chocolate
  1. Crush the plain sweet biscuits and mix with the melted butter.
  2. Press into glasses.
  3. Blitz the raspberries with the icing sugar and strain.
  4. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar and the vanilla, then fold in the whipped cream.
  5. Fold half the raspberry sauce through to turn it pink.
  6. Spoon into the glasses, swirl the rest of the sauce on top and chill 2 hours.
  7. Finish with raspberries and grated white chocolate.

Drinks

Club cosmopolitanVegetarian

Vodka, orange liqueur, cranberry and lime, shaken and served up.

Prep 3 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes and mix everything but the ice up to 1 day ahead in a jug in the fridge; shake with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 2 limes
  1. Shake the vodka, the orange liqueur, the cranberry juice and the lime juice with ice for each glass.
  2. Strain into chilled glasses.
  3. Make it just before the film starts and keep the jug cold.
