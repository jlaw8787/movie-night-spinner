Blue corn chips with red chile salsaVegetarian

Blue corn chips with a smoky dried red chilli salsa

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the red chile salsa up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; serve with the chips on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 dried red chillies
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 lime
  • 230g blue corn chips
  1. Toast the dried red chillies in a dry pan, soak in hot water 15 minutes and drain.
  2. Blitz with the tomatoes, the garlic cloves, the juice of the lime and salt until smooth.
  3. Serve with the blue corn chips.

Los Alamos green chile cheeseburger sliders

Mini beef burgers with cheddar and charred green chilli

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Char, peel and chop the chillies and mix the chipotle mayonnaise up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; smash and cook the patties just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long green chillies
  • 500g beef mince
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 8 slider buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Char the long green chillies over a flame or under a grill until blackened, sweat in a bowl, peel, seed and chop.
  2. Roll the beef mince into balls.
  3. Smash flat in a very hot pan, season and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Flip, top with the chillies and a slice of cheddar each, cover 1 minute.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise with the chipotle in adobo.
  6. Toast the slider buns and build with the chipotle mayonnaise, patties and pickles.

Trinity test green chile stew

Pork, potato and chilli stew. Now I am become Death.

Prep 25 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Cook completely, cool and keep up to 3 days in the fridge, better the next day; reheat gently and warm the tortillas to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pork shoulder
  • 1 onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 6 green chillies
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1l chicken stock
  • 8 flour tortillas
  1. Brown the diced pork shoulder, then soften the onion and the garlic cloves.
  2. Char the large green chillies, peel, chop and add with the diced potatoes and the chicken stock.
  3. Simmer 90 minutes until the pork is tender.
  4. Serve with the warm flour tortillas.

Sopaipillas with honeyVegetarian

Puffed fried pastry pillows drizzled with honey

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the dough up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; roll and fry just before serving as they puff best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 20g butter
  • 125ml honey
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 160ml water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Mix the flour, the baking powder, the salt and the butter, then add the warm water to a soft dough.
  2. Rest 20 minutes, roll 5mm thick and cut into squares.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 185C for 1 minute a side until puffed.
  4. Tear open and fill with honey.

Oppie's very dry martiniVegetarian

Gin with a splash of dry vermouth, honey and lime on the rim

Prep 3 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses and the gin up to 1 day ahead; rim the glasses and stir to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 20ml dry vermouth
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Touch the rim of the chilled glasses with a little honey and lime.
  2. For each drink, stir the gin with a splash of dry vermouth over ice and strain in.
  3. Serve iced lime soda the same way for anyone off the gin.
