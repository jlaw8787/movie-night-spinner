Snacks

Late-night TV caramel popcornVegetarian

Popcorn coated in brown sugar and golden syrup caramel.

Prep 8 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 60ml golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot.
  2. Melt the butter with the sugar and golden syrup and boil, then take off the heat and stir in the bicarb soda and salt.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and stir to coat.
  4. Spread on lined trays and bake at 120°C, stirring twice.
  5. Cool and break up.

Gotham deli picklesVegetarian

Cucumber spears pickled with dill and garlic.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Lebanese cucumbers, cut into spears
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 250ml white vinegar
  • 250ml water
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 packet rye crispbread
  1. Pack the cucumbers into a jar with dill and garlic.
  2. Heat the vinegar with the water, salt and sugar and pour over.
  3. Chill overnight.
  4. Serve in the jar with rye crispbread alongside.

Mains

Diner meatloaf

Beef meatloaf with a sticky tomato glaze and mashed potatoes.

Prep 13 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Mix the meatloaf and press into the tin up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake it and make the mash and peas on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 500g frozen peas
  1. Mix the mince with the onion, breadcrumbs, egg and salt and press into a loaf tin.
  2. Spread with tomato sauce mixed with brown sugar.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter and cook the peas.
  5. Serve thick slices with mash and peas.

Green room roast beef on rye

Rare topside piled on dark bread with horseradish cream, pickles and lettuce, stacked backstage.

How it is made
  • 800g beef topside
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar horseradish
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 loaf rye bread
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 cos lettuce
  1. Rare roast beef piled on rye with horseradish cream, pickles and crisp lettuce, cut in halves and stacked on a backstage platter. Season a 800g beef topside, sear all over and roast at 200°C for 30 minutes for rare. Rest, cool and slice thinly. Mix 150g sour cream with 2 tbsp horseradish, a squeeze of lemon and salt. Spread 8 slices of rye with the horseradish cream, then layer 4 with beef, sliced dill pickles and lettuce. Top with the other slices, cut in half and stack on a platter.

Happy's candy applesVegetarian

Green apples dipped in glassy blood-red toffee. Red suit, long stairs.

Prep 5 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Best made on the day as the toffee goes sticky overnight; wash, dry and stick the apples up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 6 green apples
  • 6 wooden sticks
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 125ml water
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp red food colouring
  1. Wash and dry the apples well, remove the stalks and push in sticks.
  2. Heat the sugar, water and vinegar until dissolved, then boil without stirring until 150°C on a thermometer.
  3. Add the colouring and swirl.
  4. Tilt the pan and dip each apple, twisting to coat.
  5. Stand on baking paper to set.

Drinks

Bourbon Manhattan

Bourbon, sweet vermouth and bitters, stirred ice cold for Gotham.

Prep 2 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the coupe glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; stir each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 80ml sweet vermouth
  • 4 dashes bitters
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill coupe glasses in the freezer.
  2. Fill a jug with ice and add bourbon, vermouth and bitters, then stir steadily until very cold.
  3. Strain into the glasses, drop in a cherry and twist a strip of orange peel over the top.
