Avenue B cart soft pretzels with mustardVegetarian

Chewy and dark from a bicarb bath, coarse salt, yellow and wholegrain mustard to dunk

Prep 30 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Best baked on the day; shape the pretzels and keep them covered in the fridge up to 4 hours, then boil and bake
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 325ml warm water
  • 2l water, for the bath
  • 80g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp coarse sea salt
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, melted butter and warm water and knead until smooth.
  2. Cover and leave somewhere warm until doubled.
  3. Heat the oven to 220C. Cut the dough into 8, roll each into a long rope and twist into a pretzel.
  4. Boil the water for the bath with the bicarbonate of soda and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds, then lift onto lined trays.
  5. Brush with the egg, scatter with the coarse salt and bake until deep brown.
  6. Serve warm with the yellow and wholegrain mustard in bowls.

Mark's film reel pinwheelsVegetarian

Rolled up tight like the 16mm reels Mark never stops shooting

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Roll and chill the logs up to 1 day ahead, wrapped; slice and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 3 tbsp sun-dried tomato pesto
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Spread the pastry sheets with the pesto and scatter over the parmesan and cheddar.
  2. Roll each sheet up tightly from the long side.
  3. Chill so the logs slice cleanly. Heat the oven to 200C.
  4. Cut into 1.5cm rounds, lay flat on lined trays and brush with the egg.
  5. Bake until puffed and golden, then cool a few minutes.

Christmas Eve arroz con pollo for the loft

Dinner for everyone once Angel's drum money comes through

Prep 50 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and blitz the sofrito up to 2 days ahead; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs, skin on
  • 7 garlic cloves, 3 crushed for the rub, 4 for the sofrito
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 limes, 1 juiced, 1 in wedges
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 brown onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 green capsicum, roughly chopped
  • 1 red capsicum, roughly chopped
  • 1 handful coriander
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 400g medium grain rice
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 100g green olives
  1. Rub the chicken with the crushed garlic, oregano, cumin, lime juice and salt.
  2. Leave to marinate.
  3. Blitz the onion, both capsicums, the rest of the garlic and the coriander to a rough sofrito.
  4. Brown the chicken skin side down in the olive oil in a wide heavy pot, turning once, then lift out.
  5. Fry the sofrito in the fat until soft.
  6. Stir in the tomato paste, paprika, turmeric and rice.
  7. Pour in the stock, add the bay leaves, sit the chicken on top, cover and simmer gently.
  8. Scatter over the peas and olives, cover off the heat to finish, and serve with the lime wedges.

Collins' Santa Fe green chilli pork stew

Shoulder braised with roasted peppers, potato and cumin, tortillas and lime, the restaurant dream

Prep 25 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Stew up to 2 days ahead, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pork shoulder, diced
  • 4 long green chillies
  • 2 green capsicums
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Char the chillies and capsicums under the grill, then peel, seed and chop.
  2. Brown the pork in the oil in a heavy pot, add the onion, garlic and cumin and cook until soft.
  3. Add the chillies, capsicum and stock and simmer covered until the pork is tender.
  4. Add the potatoes and simmer until soft.
  5. Serve with warm tortillas, lime and coriander.

Angel's Christmas temblequeVegetarian

The wobbly coconut pudding, a gift for the loft on Christmas Day

Prep 5 min, cook 255 min · Make ahead: Make and chill up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; turn out and dust just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 80g cornflour
  • 110g caster sugar
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil, for the mould
  1. Whisk the cornflour into the coconut milk in a saucepan until smooth, then add the sugar, salt and cinnamon stick.
  2. Stir over medium heat until it boils and turns thick and glossy.
  3. Take off the heat, lift out the cinnamon stick and stir in the vanilla. Pour into a lightly oiled mould.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Turn out onto a plate and dust with the ground cinnamon.

Collins' hot toddy for a loft with no heatVegetarian

Whisky, honey and lemon topped with boiling water, cloves and cinnamon

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer the spiced water up to 1 day ahead and reheat; build each mug to order
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml water
  • 2 cinnamon sticks, broken in half
  • 8 whole cloves
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 160ml whisky
  • 2 lemons, 1 juiced, 1 sliced
  1. Simmer the water with the cinnamon sticks and cloves.
  2. Put the honey and whisky into 4 heatproof mugs and squeeze in the lemon juice.
  3. Top with the hot spiced water and stir until the honey melts.
  4. Finish each mug with a lemon slice and a piece of the cinnamon stick.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the power gets cut in Mark and Roger's loft on Christmas Eve.
