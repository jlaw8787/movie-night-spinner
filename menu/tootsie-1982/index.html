Snacks

Finger sandwichesVegetarian

Egg salad with watercress, and cucumber with cream cheese, on soft white bread. Crusts off, cut in triangles.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 handful cress
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 12 slices of soft white bread
  1. Boil the eggs, then cool, 10 min.
  2. Mash the eggs with mayonnaise and cress.
  3. Spread cream cheese on some of the bread and layer with cucumber.
  4. Make up the sandwiches, cut off the crusts and cut into triangles.

Red pepper dipVegetarian

Charred under the grill, peeled, whipped smooth with feta, yogurt, garlic, hot chilli flakes and olive oil. Sesame crackers.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to drizzle
  • 200g seeded crackers
  1. Char the capsicums under a hot grill, 15 min.
  2. Cover, then peel and seed.
  3. Blitz with the feta, yoghurt, garlic, chilli and oil until smooth.
  4. Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with oil and serve with crackers.

Mains

Roast chicken

Butter, grated lemon peel and thyme under the skin. Carved, with potatoes browned in the drippings.

Prep 8 min, cook 1 hr 10 min, 16 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 2 lemons, 1 zested, 1 halved
  • 6 thyme sprigs
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  1. Salt the chicken and refrigerate uncovered overnight to dry-brine.
  2. Mix the butter with lemon zest and thyme and push under the skin.
  3. Roast at 220°C with the potatoes in the tray, 20 min.
  4. Lower to 180°C and roast until cooked through, 50 min.
  5. Rest before carving, 15 min.

Strawberry shortcakeVegetarian

Cream biscuits baked this morning, split and filled with sugared berries and soft whipped cream.

Prep 21 min, cook 14 min
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 500g strawberries, sliced
  1. Mix the flour, 200ml of the cream and some of the sugar to a soft dough.
  2. Pat out, cut 4 rounds and bake at 220°C, 14 min.
  3. Toss the strawberries with sugar.
  4. Whip the remaining cream softly.
  5. Split the scones and fill with berries and cream.

Russian teaNo alcohol

Strong and black, poured into glasses. Lemon and cherry preserves on the side, to stir in.

Prep 2 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp loose black tea
  • 1 L just-boiled water
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 100g cherry jam
  1. Brew the tea in the just-boiled water and strain into 4 glasses.
  2. Serve with lemon slices and a small dish of cherry jam on the side for stirring in.
