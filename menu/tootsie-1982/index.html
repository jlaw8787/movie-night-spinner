Snacks

Southwest General green room sandwichesVegetarian

Egg and cress, ham and mustard, cucumber and cream cheese, crusts off, between soap takes

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep covered and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 handful cress
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 12 slices soft white bread
  1. Boil the eggs, then cool.
  2. Mash the eggs with mayonnaise and cress.
  3. Spread cream cheese on some of the bread and layer with cucumber.
  4. Make up the sandwiches, cut off the crusts and cut into triangles.

Sandy's audition capsicum and feta dipVegetarian

Smoky capsicum blitzed with salty cheese and yoghurt, crackers, for the part that goes to Dorothy

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to drizzle
  • 200g seeded crackers
  1. Char the capsicums under a hot grill.
  2. Cover, then peel and seed.
  3. Blitz with the feta, yoghurt, garlic, chilli and oil until smooth.
  4. Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with oil and serve with crackers.

Mains

Les's farm roast chicken

Dry-brined bird with lemon butter under the skin, roasted golden, potatoes cooked in the fat

Prep 20 min, cook 1033 min · Make ahead: Salt the chicken the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 2 lemons, 1 zested, 1 halved
  • 6 thyme sprigs
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  1. Salt the chicken and refrigerate uncovered overnight to dry-brine.
  2. Mix the butter with lemon zest and thyme and push under the skin.
  3. Roast at 220C with the potatoes in the tray.
  4. Lower to 180C and roast until cooked through.
  5. Rest before carving.

Sweets

Strawberry shortcakeVegetarian

Les's farm on a weekend off

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Scones best the same day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 500g strawberries, sliced
  1. Mix the flour, 200ml of the cream and some of the sugar to a soft dough.
  2. Pat out, cut 4 rounds and bake at 220C.
  3. Toss the strawberries with sugar.
  4. Whip the remaining cream softly.
  5. Split the scones and fill with berries and cream.

Drinks

Dorothy Michaels' Russian caravan teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong smoky black brew served in glasses with lemon slices and a spoon of cherry jam

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Brew fresh, as tea turns bitter standing; slice the lemon up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp loose black tea
  • 1l just-boiled water
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 100g cherry jam
  1. Brew the tea in the just-boiled water and strain into 4 glasses.
  2. Serve with lemon slices and a small dish of cherry jam on the side for stirring in.
