Awesome Mix pepperoni pizza rolls

Puff pastry rolls filled with pizza sauce, pepperoni and mozzarella

Prep 30 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Rolls can be filled a day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 8 tsp pizza sauce
  • 16 slices of pepperoni
  • 60g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp parmesan, grated
  • 300ml napoli sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch of chilli flakes
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line trays. Cut each pastry sheet into 8 rectangles.
  2. Top each with pizza sauce, pepperoni, mozzarella and oregano.
  3. Roll up, pinch the ends closed and lay seam down on the trays.
  4. Brush with egg, scatter with parmesan and bake until puffed and golden.
  5. Warm the napoli sauce with the honey and chilli flakes for dipping.

Knowhere bar wings

Baked wings tossed in a sticky blackberry, soy, honey and chilli glaze

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Wings can dry in the fridge overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 150g blackberries
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1 pinch of chilli flakes
  1. Dry the wings uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  3. Simmer the blackberries with the soy, honey, garlic and chilli until sticky, then strain.
  4. Toss the wings through the glaze.

Ravager barbecue ribs with corn and slaw

Slow-baked paprika pork ribs glazed with barbecue sauce, grilled corn

Prep 15 min, cook 170 min · Make ahead: Ribs can be baked a day ahead, glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork ribs
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 300g coleslaw mix
  • 100ml mayonnaise
  1. Rub the ribs with paprika and sugar and wrap tightly in foil.
  2. Bake at 150C until tender.
  3. Unwrap, brush with barbecue sauce and glaze under the grill.
  4. Grill the corn until charred.
  5. Toss the slaw mix with mayonnaise and serve.

Dance-off sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, whipped cream and rainbow sprinkles

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fudge sauce up to 3 days, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 100ml whipped cream
  • 2 tbsp rainbow sprinkles
  • 4 sparklers
  1. Melt the chocolate into the cream for the hot fudge.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into tall glasses.
  3. Pour over the fudge and add whipped cream and sprinkles.
  4. Light a sparkler for the final move.

Galaxy punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Grenadine, lemonade and blue raspberry cordial layered over ice, no alcohol

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best built just before serving to keep the layers and fizz; chill the lemonade the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp grenadine
  • 4 cup ice
  • 1l lemonade
  • 60ml blue raspberry cordial
  1. Put a spoon of grenadine in the bottom of each of four glasses.
  2. Fill with ice and lemonade.
  3. Pour the cordial slowly down a spoon so it layers like a nebula.
