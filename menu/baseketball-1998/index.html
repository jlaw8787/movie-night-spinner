Snacks

Milwaukee beer-battered cheese curdsVegetarian

Cheese curds fried in a crisp golden beer batter

Prep 8 min, cook 18 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Pat dry and freeze the cheese up to 1 day ahead, and make the ranch up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cheese curds or cherry bocconcini
  • 170g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • garlic powder, to taste
  • 1 egg
  • 1 can lager
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 100g sour cream
  • 60ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Pat the cheese curds or halved cherry bocconcini dry and freeze 20 minutes.
  2. Whisk the plain flour, the baking powder, a little garlic powder, the egg and the cold lager into a smooth batter.
  3. Dust the cheese in flour, dip in batter and fry in the vegetable oil in batches at 185C for 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
  4. For ranch, mix the sour cream, the buttermilk, the mayonnaise, chives, dill and garlic.

Psych-out pretzel bites with beer cheeseVegetarian

Soft pretzel bites with a warm lager cheddar sauce

Prep 7 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut the bites up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge, and make the beer cheese up to 2 days ahead; reheat the cheese gently, then dip and bake the bites on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 60g bicarb soda
  • 1 egg
  • rock salt, to taste
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 can lager
  • 200g cheddar
  • mustard, to taste
  • 2l water
  1. Heat the oven to 220C.
  2. Roll the pizza dough into ropes and cut into 3cm bites.
  3. Simmer the water with the bicarb soda and dip the bites for 15 seconds.
  4. Drain, place on lined trays, brush with egg, sprinkle with rock salt and bake 10 to 12 minutes.
  5. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and the lager, then the grated cheddar and the mustard until smooth.

Mains

Beers beer-braised bratwurst

Grilled bratwurst in rolls with beer onions, sauerkraut and hot mustard

Prep 5 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Simmer the bratwurst in the lager up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the liquid in the fridge; grill and finish the onions on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 bratwurst
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 375ml cans lager
  • 20g butter
  • 8 long rolls
  • 300g sauerkraut
  • hot mustard, to taste
  1. Put the bratwurst in a wide pan with the sliced onions, the cans of lager and the butter.
  2. Simmer gently for 15 minutes.
  3. Lift out the sausages and grill on a hot barbecue for 6 to 8 minutes until charred.
  4. Boil the onions in the beer until most of it evaporates and they're sticky.
  5. Toast the long rolls and fill with a bratwurst, onions, warmed sauerkraut and hot mustard.

Milwaukee warm potato saladVegetarian

Mustard, dill, celery and spring onion, warm beside the brats

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the dressing up to 3 days ahead; boil the potatoes and dress them warm on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg waxy potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 2 celery sticks, finely sliced
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Whisk the mustard, vinegar, sugar, oil and salt.
  2. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, then drain.
  3. Toss the hot potatoes with the dressing, celery, spring onion and chopped dill and serve warm.

Driveway hot fudge peanut sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, salted peanuts and crushed pretzels

Prep 6 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge sauce up to 1 week ahead and keep it chilled; reheat gently, whip the cream and scoop the ice cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 20g butter
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 60g salted peanuts
  • 40g pretzels
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Heat the cream, the chopped dark chocolate, the golden syrup and the butter in a saucepan, stirring until smooth and glossy, about 3 minutes.
  2. Scoop the vanilla ice cream into bowls.
  3. Pour over the warm fudge sauce.
  4. Top each with salted peanuts, crushed pretzels, a swirl of whipped cream and a maraschino cherry.

Denslow Cup brandy old fashionedVegetarian

Brandy muddled with orange, cherry, sugar and bitters

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Slice the oranges and cut the garnish up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; muddle and build over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml brandy
  • 2 oranges
  • 8 maraschino cherries
  • 4 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp aromatic bitters, as needed
  • 250ml lemon-lime soda
  • 4 cup ice, as needed
  1. In each of the rocks glasses, muddle the orange slice, the maraschino cherry, the sugar and a few dashes of aromatic bitters until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Fill with ice, add the brandy and top with the lemon-lime soda.
  3. Stir gently and garnish with another cherry and an orange slice.
  4. For non-drinkers, swap the brandy for cold black tea.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the Milwaukee Beers take the court.
