Snacks

Chilli cheese fries

Fries under beef chilli, cheddar and jalapeños.

Prep 4 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat it, then bake the chips and grill with the cheese on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tin kidney beans
  • 150g cheddar
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Bake the frozen chips at 220°C for 25 minutes, turning once.
  2. Meanwhile brown the beef mince with the diced onion, add the smoked paprika, the ground cumin, the chilli powder, the crushed tomatoes and the drained kidney beans, and simmer 20 minutes until thick.
  3. Pile the chips into a baking dish, spoon over the chilli, scatter with the grated cheddar and grill 3 minutes until melted.
  4. Top with the sour cream, sliced jalapeños and spring onion.

Soft pretzels with apple butterVegetarian

Chewy salted twists with spiced apple butter and hot mustard.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 5 min · Make ahead: Make the apple butter up to 5 days ahead and keep chilled; shape, dip and bake the pretzels on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 60g bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 4 apples
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100ml apple juice
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 2 L water
  1. Divide the pizza dough into pieces, roll each into a 50cm rope and twist into pretzels.
  2. Bring the water and the bicarb to a simmer and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds.
  3. Lay on a lined tray, brush with the beaten egg, sprinkle with sea salt flakes and bake at 220°C for 12 minutes until deep brown.
  4. For the apple butter, simmer the peeled diced apples with the brown sugar, the cinnamon and the apple juice for 25 minutes and mash smooth.
  5. Serve with American mustard.

Mains

Amish chicken and noodles over mashed potatoes

Shredded chicken and thick egg noodles in peppery broth over buttery mashed potatoes.

Prep 12 min, cook 1 hr 19 min · Make ahead: Simmer and shred the chicken in the broth up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; add the noodles and make the mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1.5 L chicken stock
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 300g wide egg noodles
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Simmer the chicken thighs in the chicken stock with the diced onion, the diced carrots and the celery sticks for 35 minutes.
  2. Lift out the chicken, shred it and return it to the pot.
  3. Add the wide egg noodles and simmer 8 minutes until the broth thickens.
  4. Season well with salt and plenty of white pepper.
  5. Meanwhile boil the peeled potatoes for 20 minutes and mash with the butter and the milk.
  6. Ladle the chicken and noodles over the mash and scatter with chopped parsley.

Gravy train pot roast

Beef chuck braised slowly with onions, carrots and potatoes, gravy from the pot.

Prep 5 min, cook 3 hr 17 min · Make ahead: Cook up to 2 days ahead, it slices better cold; reheat in the gravy
Recipe for 4
  • 1.4kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, cut in wedges
  • 4 carrots, in chunks
  • 800g potatoes, in chunks
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 3 sprigs of thyme
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  1. Season the beef and sear on all sides in the oil in a heavy pot, 12 min.
  2. Add the onions and tomato paste, then the stock and thyme. Cover and cook at 160°C for 2 hours.
  3. Add the carrots and potatoes, cover again and cook for 1 hour more, until the beef falls apart.
  4. Lift out the beef and vegetables. Thicken the pot juices with the cornflour mixed with water and simmer into gravy, 5 min.
  5. Pull the beef into chunks and serve with the vegetables and gravy.

Shoofly molasses crumb pieVegetarian

Sticky molasses pie with a brown sugar crumb, Amish country baking.

Prep 24 min, cook 43 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep at room temperature; whip the cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 150g plain flour
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 60g butter
  • 150ml molasses
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 180ml water
  1. Line a 23cm pie dish with the shortcrust pastry sheet.
  2. Rub the plain flour, the brown sugar and the cold butter into crumbs, 7 min.
  3. Whisk the molasses, the egg and the hot water with the bicarb.
  4. Pour into the pastry case, then scatter the crumbs evenly over the top.
  5. Bake at 200°C for 10 minutes, then drop to 180°C for 30 minutes until just set with a slight wobble.
  6. Cool 30 minutes and serve with the whipped cream.

Drinks

Vanilla malt milkshakeNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream, milk and malt, blended thick.

Prep 4 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the tall glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 5 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Chill the tall glasses in the freezer for 15 minutes.
  2. Blend the cold full cream milk with the vanilla ice cream, the malted milk powder and the vanilla extract until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into the cold glasses.
  4. Whip the thickened cream to soft peaks, spoon on top and sprinkle each with a little more malt powder.
  5. Serve straight away with thick straws.
