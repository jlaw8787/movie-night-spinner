Snacks

Slimer's slime dipVegetarian

Green spinach and artichoke dip, corn chips for scooping.

Prep 7 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day ahead, bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g spinach
  • 150g marinated artichokes, drained
  • 150g cream cheese, softened
  • 100g sour cream
  • 100g grated mozzarella
  • 30g grated parmesan
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 200g corn chips
  1. Wilt the spinach in a dry pan, cool, then squeeze very dry and chop with the artichokes.
  2. Mix the spinach and artichokes with the cream cheese, sour cream, most of the mozzarella, the parmesan and the garlic.
  3. Spoon into a small baking dish, top with the rest of the mozzarella and bake at 190°C for 20 minutes until bubbling.
  4. Serve hot with the corn chips for scooping.

Fridge devilled eggsVegetarian

Yolks whipped with mayo and Dijon, tinted a little green, piped back in, guarded by Zuul.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • green food colouring, a drop
  • salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp snipped chives
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, then cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs and scoop the yolks into a bowl.
  3. Whip the yolks with the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, a pinch of salt and a drop of green food colouring until smooth.
  4. Pipe back into the whites and scatter the chives on top.

Marshmallow skewers

Big blistered marshmallows on sticks dipped in dark chocolate and crushed biscuits.

Prep 3 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Crush the biscuits up to 3 days ahead, airtight; melt the chocolate and toast the marshmallows on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 large marshmallows
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 6 digestive biscuits
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  1. Melt the dark chocolate in a bowl over simmering water and crush the digestive biscuits in a bag with a rolling pin.
  2. Push 2 marshmallows onto each bamboo skewer.
  3. Toast over a gas flame or under a hot grill, turning often, until the outside blisters.
  4. Dip in the melted chocolate, then in the crushed biscuits, and eat while warm.

Mains

Firehouse meatball sub

Long enough for the whole crew.

Prep 17 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pork mince
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g grated parmesan
  • 30g breadcrumbs
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 700ml passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 long rolls
  • 8 slices provolone
  1. Mix the pork and beef mince with the egg, parmesan, breadcrumbs, garlic, salt and pepper and roll into 16 meatballs.
  2. Bake the meatballs on a lined tray at 200°C for 15 minutes.
  3. Simmer the passata with the oregano, add the meatballs and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Split the long rolls, pack in the meatballs and sauce and lay the provolone on top.
  5. Grill for 2 to 3 minutes until the cheese has melted.

Firehouse fried rice and dumplings

Dumplings and fried rice eaten out of the boxes on the desk.

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Cook the rice a day ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 lap cheong sausages
  • 4 spring onions
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 24 frozen pork dumplings
  • 80ml water
  1. Cook the jasmine rice the day before, spread it on a tray and chill overnight.
  2. Slice the lap cheong and spring onions and beat the eggs.
  3. Heat a wok until smoking with half the oil, scramble the eggs, then add the lap cheong, rice and 2 tbsp soy sauce and toss for 4 minutes until hot. Stir through the spring onions.
  4. Fry the frozen pork dumplings in the rest of the oil in a frying pan for 2 minutes until the bases brown, add the water, cover and steam for 6 minutes.
  5. Serve out of takeaway boxes with the rest of the soy sauce on the side.

Sweets

Stay Puft s'mores

Giant marshmallows, toasted until they melt down.

Prep 5 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Best toasted and eaten straight away; nothing to make ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 large marshmallows
  • 16 digestive biscuits
  • 100g milk chocolate
  1. Lay 8 digestive biscuits on a tray and put a square of milk chocolate on each.
  2. Toast the marshmallows over a flame until golden and melting, or sit one on each chocolate square and put the tray under a hot grill for 30 seconds.
  3. Press the remaining biscuits on top and eat straight away.

Drinks

Ectoplasm slushieNo alcohol

Lime, ice and very green.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Best blitzed just before serving; chill the lemonade and lime cordial up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g ice
  • 150ml lime cordial
  • 600ml lemonade, chilled
  • green food colouring, a drop
  • 1 lime
  1. Blitz the ice with the lime cordial and lemonade until slushy.
  2. Add a drop of green food colouring and blitz again.
  3. Pour into 4 glasses and add a slice of lime to each.

When to serve

Crack the drinks when Ecto-1 rolls out of the firehouse for the first time.
