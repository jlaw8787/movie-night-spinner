Aunt Cass's hot wings

Crisp baked wings in a sweet and spicy gochujang glaze

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings with salt and keep uncovered in the fridge up to 1 day ahead for crisper skin; make the glaze up to 3 days ahead; bake and toss on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the wings with salt.
  2. Bake at 220°C until crisp.
  3. Warm the gochujang with the honey, garlic and soy until glossy.
  4. Toss the wings through and scatter with sesame seeds.

Lucky Cat Café pork belly baoOn the printed menu

Braised in soy and star anise till melting, steamed buns with hoisin, Aunt Cass's stress snack

How it is made
  • 800g pork belly
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 jar star anise
  • 12 frozen bao buns
  • 1 jar hoisin sauce
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 50g roasted peanuts
  1. Pork belly braised in soy, ginger and star anise until meltingly soft, sliced into steamed bao with hoisin, cucumber, spring onion and peanuts. Cut 800g pork belly into 3 thick strips and simmer in 100ml soy, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 4 slices ginger, 2 star anise and 750ml water, covered, for 2 hours until soft. Lift out, slice 1cm thick and sear in a hot pan until caramelised. Steam 12 frozen bao buns for 8 minutes. Fill each with hoisin, pork, cucumber sticks, sliced spring onion and crushed peanuts.

San Fransokyo ramen

Braised pork belly, miso broth, ramen noodles and soft boiled eggs

Prep 19 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Pork can braise a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork belly
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1l water
  • 4 portions ramen noodles
  • 4 tbsp white miso
  • 1 1/2l hot chicken stock
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 nori sheets
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  1. Put the pork in a pot with soy and water.
  2. Braise gently until tender, then slice.
  3. Soft-boil the eggs.
  4. Whisk the miso into the hot stock for the broth and cook the noodles.
  5. Build bowls with noodles, pork, halved eggs, nori and spring onion.

Nerd lab salmon and avocado sushi rollsOn the printed menu

Seasoned rice and nori round fresh fish, avocado and cucumber, satisfied with your care?

How it is made
  • 400g sushi rice
  • 1 bottle rice vinegar
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 1 packet nori sheets
  • 250g sashimi salmon
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 tube wasabi
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  1. Seasoned sushi rice rolled in nori around fresh salmon, avocado and cucumber, sliced into rounds with soy, wasabi and pickled ginger. Rinse 400g sushi rice until the water runs clear, then cook with 480ml water, covered, for 12 minutes and rest 10 minutes. Stir in 60ml rice vinegar mixed with 2 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp salt and cool. Spread rice over 4 nori sheets on a bamboo mat, leaving a 2cm strip at the top. Lay strips of 250g sashimi salmon, 1 avocado and 1 cucumber along the bottom and roll tightly. Slice each into 8.

Baymax marshmallow bites

Marshmallows dipped in white chocolate with piped dark chocolate dots

Prep 5 min, cook 18 min, then 42 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 20 marshmallows
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Melt the white chocolate.
  2. Dip each marshmallow until fully coated and set on baking paper.
  3. Melt a little dark chocolate and pipe two small dots joined by a line on each for the face.
  4. Leave to set.

Café cream puffsVegetarian

Choux puffs filled with whipped cream

Prep 35 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Puffs up to 1 day, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 500ml custard
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Bring the water and butter to the boil, beat in the flour, then the eggs.
  2. Pipe small mounds on lined trays.
  3. Bake at 200°C until puffed and dry, then cool.
  4. Whip the cream, fold into the custard and fill each puff.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Honey Lemon's honey citron teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Thin lemon slices preserved in honey and sugar, for a warm tea

Prep 15 min, then 24 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Lemon honey keeps 2 weeks in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 3 lemons
  • 250g honey
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 800ml hot water or soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. A day ahead, slice the lemons very thinly, remove seeds and layer in a jar with honey and sugar.
  2. Leave at room temperature for 24 hours, shaking now and then, until syrupy.
  3. Stir the lemon honey into a mug of hot water, or into a glass of ice topped with soda water for a cold version.
