Riggs' trailer snacks

Crackers and whatever's left in his fridge

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheddar and fold the salami up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; set out on the board 20 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sharp cheddar
  • 150g sliced salami
  • 1 sleeve saltine crackers
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  1. Cut the cheddar into slabs and fold the salami into quarters.
  2. Lay them out on a chopping board with the saltine crackers and a small bowl of pickled jalapeños. Eat straight off the board.

Christmas party crab dip

Baked cream cheese and sour cream dip with crab and cheddar, crackers

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the dip up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night and add 5 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese, softened
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g crab meat
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 200g crackers
  1. Heat the oven to 190C. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, Worcestershire and a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Fold in the crab and half the cheddar, spoon into a dish and top with the rest.
  3. Bake until bubbling.
  4. Serve with crackers.

Mains

Brown sticky stew, done right

Beef chuck braised with onion, carrot, celery, tomato paste and red wine

Prep 35 min, cook 200 min · Make ahead: Better made the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg diced beef chuck
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the beef with salt and pepper and brown it in the neutral oil in batches.
  2. Soften the chopped onion, carrots and celery, then stir in the tomato paste.
  3. Add the wine, stock and thyme and return the beef.
  4. Braise in a 150C oven.
  5. Boil the potatoes towards the end of braising and mash with butter. Serve the stew on top.

Roger's birthday cakeVegetarian

Two-layer chocolate cake filled and covered with chocolate buttercream

Prep 40 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 50g cocoa, for the cake
  • 200ml milk
  • 200g butter, softened, for buttercream
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 40g cocoa, for buttercream
  • 1 packet birthday candles
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line two tins. Beat the butter for the cake with the sugar, then add the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, the cocoa for the cake and the milk and divide between the tins.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Cool the cakes completely.
  5. Beat the butter, icing sugar and the cocoa for the buttercream.
  6. Fill, cover and add candles.

Riggs' beach trailer michelada

Cold lager with lime, tomato juice, hot sauce and a chilli salt rim

Prep 8 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime juice, tomato juice, hot sauce and Worcestershire in a jug up to 1 day ahead and chill; rim the glasses, add ice and top with lager to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tray ice
  • 120ml lime juice
  • 240ml tomato juice
  • 16 dashes hot sauce
  • 8 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 bottles cold lager (375 ml)
  1. Mix the salt and chilli powder on a plate. Rub the glass rims with lime and dip.
  2. Fill the glasses with ice and add lime juice, tomato juice, hot sauce and Worcestershire.
  3. Top with cold lager poured slowly, stir once and garnish with lime wedges.
