Snacks

Za'atar flatbreads with hummusVegetarian

Grilled flatbreads with hummus, pickled turnips and olives.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp za'atar
  • 200g hummus
  • 100g pickled turnips
  • 100g kalamata olives
  1. Brush the flatbreads with oil and sprinkle generously with za'atar.
  2. Grill until blistered at the edges. Cut into wedges.
  3. Serve with hummus, pickled turnips and olives in little bowls.

Scarif beach prawns

Garlic chilli lime prawns grilled on skewers, with mango coriander salsa.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 4 hours, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g peeled green prawns
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 2 limes, one juiced and one for salsa
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  1. Toss the prawns with the garlic, chilli and juice of 1 lime. Marinate for 20 minutes.
  2. Dice the mango and mix with coriander and the juice of the second lime for the salsa.
  3. Thread the prawns onto skewers and grill until pink and charred. Serve with the salsa.

Mains

Jedha spiced lamb pilaf

Lamb, carrots and basmati rice cooked with cumin and cinnamon.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Pilaf can be reheated, serve with fresh almonds
Recipe for 4
  • 600g lamb, diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 2 carrots, cut into strips
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 300g basmati rice, rinsed
  • 600ml chicken stock
  • 50g flaked almonds
  • 50g raisins
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  1. Brown the lamb in the oil. Add the onion, carrots, cumin and cinnamon and cook 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the rice and stock, cover and cook on low.
  3. Rest the pilaf, covered, for 10 minutes. Toast the almonds meanwhile.
  4. Pile onto a platter with the raisins and almonds. Serve with yoghurt mixed with the garlic.

Yavin 4 rebel base chicken rice bowls

Smoked paprika and garlic thighs charred on the grill, black beans, corn, avocado and lime crema.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 limes
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 1 can black beans
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 2 avocados
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Smoky paprika and garlic chicken charred on the grill, sliced over rice with black beans, charred corn, avocado, pickled onion and lime crema. Rub 800g chicken thighs with 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 crushed garlic cloves, oil and salt. Cook 300g rice. Slice a red onion thin and soak in lime juice and salt. Char 2 corn cobs and cut off the kernels. Warm a drained can of black beans with cumin. Grill the chicken 5 minutes a side and slice. Build bowls with avocado, pickled onion and sour cream mixed with lime.

Death Star plans chocolate sphereVegetarian

Chocolate mousse spheres under a dark mirror glaze.

Prep 0 min, cook 30 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, chill overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate, for the mousse
  • 100g dark chocolate, for the glaze
  • 2 eggs, separated
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 150ml cream, whipped, for the mousse
  • 100ml thickened cream, for the glaze
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  1. Separate the eggs. Melt the mousse chocolate and whisk in the yolks. Whip the whites with the sugar and fold in with the whipped cream.
  2. Spoon into 4 round moulds and set overnight.
  3. Melt the glaze chocolate with the thickened cream and cocoa. Turn out the spheres and glaze them. Press a dent in each.

Chirrut's teaNo alcohol

Green tea with ginger and honey, hot or iced, for a Guardian of the Whills.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp green tea leaves
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 1 L water, about 80°C
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Steep the green tea and ginger in the hot water for 3 minutes.
  2. Strain and stir in the honey.
  3. Serve hot in small cups, or chill and pour over ice. I am one with the Force.
