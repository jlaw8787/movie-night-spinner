Snacks

Outback roadhouse pies

Slow-braised beef pies with a puff pastry lid, tomato sauce on the side

Prep 60 min, cook 200 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck, diced
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Braise the beef in the stock and Worcestershire sauce until tender.
  2. Thicken with the flour and cool.
  3. Line pie tins with shortcrust and fill with the beef.
  4. Top with puff pastry lids and brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200C and serve with tomato sauce.

Campfire damperVegetarian

Damper bread baked or wrapped on sticks over coals, with butter and golden syrup

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cold butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  • extra butter, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Rub the butter into the flour until crumbly.
  2. Mix in the milk with a knife until it just comes together.
  3. Shape into a round, score the top and bake. Or wrap around green sticks over coals.
  4. Serve with butter and golden syrup.

Mains

Mick's camp sausage and onion sandwiches

Beef sausages and fried onions in white bread, while Mick makes a show of fixing the car

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Cook the sausages slowly over low coals or a barbecue, turning often.
  2. Fry the onions in the butter on the hotplate until soft and golden.
  3. Fold each sausage into bread with onions and sauce.

Sweets

Golden syrup dumplingsVegetarian

Soft dumplings simmered in a golden syrup and brown sugar sauce

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 30g cold butter
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 375ml water
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 30g butter, for the syrup
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 200ml cream, to serve
  1. Rub the butter into the flour and mix in the milk to a soft dough.
  2. Simmer the water, golden syrup, butter and sugar in a wide pan.
  3. Drop in spoonfuls of dough, cover and cook until puffed.
  4. Serve with cream.

Drinks

Stopped-watches billy tea with lemon myrtleVegetarianNo alcohol

Billy tea with lemon myrtle and a gum leaf. Skip Mick's water

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2l water
  • 3 tbsp loose black tea
  • 1 tsp dried lemon myrtle
  • 1 gum leaf, if you have one
  • sweetened condensed milk, to taste
  1. Bring the water to the boil.
  2. Add the tea, lemon myrtle and gum leaf and take off the heat.
  3. Swing the billy or tap the side to settle the leaves.
  4. Pour into mugs and stir in a spoon of condensed milk to taste.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the backpackers fill up at the petrol station.
