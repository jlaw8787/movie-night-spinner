Snacks

Phoenix street corn prawn tacos

Chipotle prawns, charred corn, feta and lime crema in corn tortillas

Prep 8 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Pickle the onion in lime juice up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and mix the crema; grill the prawns and corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 3 limes
  • 600g green prawns, peeled
  • 1 tbsp chipotle sauce
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 150g sour cream
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  1. Soak the onion in the juice of two limes with a pinch of salt and sugar.
  2. Toss the prawns with the chipotle sauce, cumin and oil and grill 2 minutes a side.
  3. Char the corn and cut off the kernels.
  4. Mix the sour cream with lime juice. Warm the tortillas.
  5. Fill with prawns, corn, feta, crema, onion and coriander.

Mains

Kringle family maple roast chicken

Chicken roasted with a maple butter glaze. Nick's at a yoga retreat, Noelle saves him a plate.

Prep 5 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with butter, salt and thyme up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast on the day and make the gravy from the pan juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 350ml chicken stock
  1. Rub the chicken with the butter, salt and thyme leaves.
  2. Roast at 200°C, brushing with the maple syrup mixed with Dijon for the last part.
  3. Toss the potatoes in oil and roast alongside, adding the carrots tossed in honey part way through.
  4. Rest the chicken.
  5. Whisk the flour into the pan juices with the stock for gravy.

Sweets

Noelle's double candy cane barkVegetarian

Dark and white chocolate peppermint bark. Thank garland for it.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 1 week ahead; chill until set, then snap into shards and keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 1/4 tsp peppermint extract
  • 5 candy canes, crushed
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Line a tray with baking paper. Melt the dark chocolate and spread into a rectangle.
  2. Chill the dark layer.
  3. Melt the white chocolate with the peppermint extract, spread over the dark layer and drag a skewer through to swirl.
  4. Scatter with crushed candy cane and sea salt.
  5. Chill until set, then snap into shards.

Elf Polly's iced sugar cookiesVegetarian

Buttery cut-out sugar cookies with icing

Prep 16 min, cook 39 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 350g icing sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 pack food colouring (red and green)
  • 1 jar edible glitter
  1. Beat the butter with some of the icing sugar, add the egg yolk and vanilla, then mix in the flour.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll out and cut stars, trees and reindeer.
  4. Bake at 170°C, then cool.
  5. Mix the remaining icing sugar with water, divide into three bowls and tint red and green, leaving one white. Ice the cookies and dust with edible glitter.

Drinks

Snowcone blue raspberry slushieVegetarianNo alcohol

Raspberry and lemon slushie, bright blue, for Snowcone, who flies in with Mrs. Claus's letter

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 80ml blue raspberry cordial
  • 1 bag ice
  • 600ml cold lemonade
  • 100ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 50g raspberries
  1. Blend the frozen raspberries, lemon juice, cordial and ice until slushy.
  2. Add the lemonade and pulse twice to mix.
  3. Pour into cups, top with whipped cream and a few raspberries.
  4. Serve with spoons and straws.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the sleigh lands at the mall.
