Snacks

Elphaba's green dipVegetarian

Avocado, peas, lime and mint blended, served with cucumber sticks

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 avocados
  • 200g frozen peas, thawed
  • 2 limes
  • 1 handful mint
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 box water crackers
  • salt, to taste
  1. Thaw the peas, juice the limes and cut the cucumbers into sticks.
  2. Blitz the avocado, peas, lime juice, mint and salt until smooth and very green.
  3. Spoon into a bowl and serve with the cucumber sticks and crackers.

Mains

Emerald City green risotto

Creamy arborio risotto stirred through with pea and spinach puree

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Blitz the peas and spinach puree up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; cook the risotto fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1 1/4l chicken stock
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 100g parmesan
  • 100g butter
  1. Heat the stock, grate the parmesan and blitz the peas and spinach with a splash of stock until smooth.
  2. Stir the rice in some of the butter, then add the hot stock a ladle at a time.
  3. Stir through the green puree, the rest of the butter and the parmesan until bright green.

Sweets

Pink meringue kissesVegetarian

Crisp pink meringues for Galinda's Popular lesson

Prep 22 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 170g caster sugar
  • pink food colouring, a drop
  • 250g raspberries
  1. Heat the oven to 100C and line two trays.
  2. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  3. Fold in a drop of pink colouring and pipe small kisses onto the trays.
  4. Bake for an hour, then turn off the oven and cool the meringues inside it.
  5. Serve with raspberries.

Pink and green cakeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers, one tinted pink and one green

Prep 28 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • pink and green food colouring, a few drops of each
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 150g butter, softened, for buttercream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and grease and line two tins.
  2. Beat the butter for the cake with the caster sugar until pale, beat in the eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Split the batter in two and tint one half pink and one half green.
  4. Bake in separate tins.
  5. Cool completely.
  6. Beat the butter for the buttercream with the icing sugar and vanilla, then stack the cakes with it.

Ozdust Ballroom pink lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry pink lemonade, for the black hat dance

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Stir the lemon juice, sugar, water and cranberry juice together up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; pour over ice and add raspberries just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 6 lemons
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1l cold water
  • 100ml cranberry juice
  • 125g raspberries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the lemons and stir with the sugar until dissolved.
  2. Add the water and cranberry juice.
  3. Pour over ice in four glasses and float a few raspberries on top.
