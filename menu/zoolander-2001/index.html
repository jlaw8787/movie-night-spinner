Snacks

Derek's runway crudités

Cucumber, celery, carrot and radish sticks with a blue cheese sour cream dip

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 3 celery sticks
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  1. Cut the cucumber, celery, carrots and radishes into long lean sticks.
  2. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream with lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce and black pepper.
  3. Stand the sticks in a glass on ice and serve with the dip.

Day spa cucumber bites

Cucumber rounds with whipped goat cheese, smoked salmon and dill

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep very cold
Recipe for 4
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Cut the cucumbers into thick rounds.
  2. Whip the goat's cheese with lemon zest and pipe on top.
  3. Add a curl of smoked salmon and a dill frond.
  4. Chill until serving.

Mains

Mugatu's vodka pastaVegetarian

Rigatoni in a creamy vodka tomato sauce with chilli and parmesan

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Fry the garlic, chilli and tomato paste up to 1 day ahead and chill; add the vodka and cream and toss with freshly cooked pasta on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 40g butter
  • 4 tbsp tomato paste
  • 60ml vodka
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Cook the rigatoni 2 minutes short of done.
  2. Saute the garlic and chilli in the butter.
  3. Fry the tomato paste until brick red.
  4. Add the vodka and cook it off, then add the cream.
  5. Toss the pasta through the sauce with the parmesan and basil.

Sweets

Derek's tiny model cakeVegetarian

Vanilla cake with a tiny teacup cake beside it, covered in buttercream

Prep 30 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Cake can be baked a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 350g icing sugar
  1. Beat part of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs and fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake most of the batter in a tin and a little in a teacup at 175C, about 30 and 15 minutes.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and cover both cakes.

Drinks

Orange mocha iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Espresso melted with dark chocolate and orange zest, shaken with milk over ice

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Brew the coffee and melt in the chocolate and orange zest up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake with the milk and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 72g ground coffee
  • 60g dark chocolate
  • 1 orange
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew four double espressos.
  2. Melt the chocolate into the hot coffee with orange zest.
  3. Shake with the cold milk and ice.
  4. Strain over more ice in four tall glasses.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when the boys shout for orange mocha frappuccinos.
