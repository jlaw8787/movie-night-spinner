Snacks

Gene's mixed vegetable frittersVegetarian

Spiced chickpea flour fritters with corn, peas and carrot, lime yoghurt

Prep 16 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; make the yoghurt dip up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g besan (chickpea flour)
  • ground cumin, to taste
  • chilli powder, to taste
  • 150g frozen corn kernels
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 carrot
  • 3 spring onions
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 180ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Mix the besan (chickpea flour), the cumin, a little chilli powder, the salt and some the water into a thick batter.
  2. Stir in the corn kernels, the peas, the grated carrot and the sliced spring onions.
  3. Heat 3cm oil to 180°C and fry heaped tablespoons for 3 minutes, turning, until crisp.
  4. Drain.
  5. Mix the Greek yoghurt with lime juice and chopped coriander for dipping.

Camp Firewood mess hall corn dogs

Cornmeal corn dogs with yellow mustard. Gene runs the mess hall and has a few things to say.

Prep 7 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 150g fine polenta
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Whisk the polenta, flour, sugar and salt with the egg and milk into a thick batter, then pour into a tall glass.
  2. Heat the oil to 180°C. Pat the frankfurts dry, push in the skewers and dust with a little flour.
  3. Dip each frankfurt in the batter and fry for 3 to 4 minutes, turning, until deep golden.
  4. Drain and serve with the mustard and tomato sauce.

Mains

Mess hall sloppy joe sliders

Saucy beef mince with cheddar and pickles on slider buns

Prep 11 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Cook the mince filling up to 2 days ahead and reheat; toast the buns and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce, to taste
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • mustard, to taste
  • 12 slider buns
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 100ml water
  1. Brown the beef mince with the diced onion and the diced green capsicum for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic cloves, the tomato sauce, the Worcestershire, the brown sugar, the mustard and the water and simmer 15 minutes until thick.
  3. Toast the slider buns, add a slice of cheddar to each base, pile on the mince and top with sliced pickles.

Andy's Camp Tigerclaw game day fried chicken

Buttermilk tenders in spiced flour, crisp and golden, with slaw and pickles, before softball

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in buttermilk up to 1 day ahead; make the slaw up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken tenderloins
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 300g shredded cabbage
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with a pinch of salt.
  2. Mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt and toss the chicken through it.
  3. Shallow fry in batches in hot oil until golden and cooked through.
  4. Toss the cabbage with the mayonnaise and vinegar and serve with the chicken and pickles.

Last day bug juice ice blocksVegetarian

Frozen watermelon, strawberry, orange and lime pops sweetened with honey

Prep 6 min, then 6 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Blend and freeze the ice blocks up to 1 week ahead and keep frozen; release under warm water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g watermelon
  • 200g strawberries
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 8 ice block moulds and sticks
  1. Blend the watermelon, the strawberries, the juice of the oranges, the juice of the lime and the honey until smooth.
  2. Dice a few extra strawberries and drop them into ice block moulds.
  3. Pour in the punch, add sticks and freeze for at least 6 hours.
  4. Run the moulds under warm water for a few seconds to release.

Camp Firewood salted caramel s'mores

Toasted marshmallow, milk chocolate and salted caramel between biscuits

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the salted caramel up to 5 days ahead in the fridge and warm gently; toast the marshmallows and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g sugar
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 20g butter
  • 16 wheatmeal biscuits
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 16 large marshmallows
  1. Make the caramel: melt the sugar until amber, whisk in the cream and the butter, then a little salt and cool.
  2. Lay the digestive or wheatmeal biscuits on a board and top them with a square of milk chocolate.
  3. Toast the large marshmallows on skewers over a fire or gas flame until golden.
  4. Push the onto each chocolate biscuit, spoon over caramel and squash with a second biscuit.

Drinks

Camp Firewood bug juice punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry, pineapple and orange juice with raspberry cordial, chilled

Prep 9 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Mix and chill the juices and cordial up to 1 day ahead; add the ice, lemonade and berries just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml cranberry juice
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 3 oranges
  • 60ml raspberry cordial
  • 600ml lemonade
  • 100g frozen mixed berries
  • 4 cups ice
  1. In a large jug, mix the cranberry juice, the pineapple juice, the juice of the oranges and the raspberry cordial.
  2. Chill 1 hour.
  3. Just before serving, add plenty of ice, the cold lemonade, a sliced orange and a handful of frozen berries.
  4. Stir once and ladle into plastic cups.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the last day at Camp Firewood begins.
