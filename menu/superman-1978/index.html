Snacks

Smallville corn on the cobVegetarian

Boiled then grilled corn rolled in butter, sea salt and smoked paprika.

Prep 5 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Boil the corn up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; char on the grill and butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Boil the corn, 5 min.
  2. Char on a hot grill, turning, 5 min.
  3. Cut each cob in half, roll in the butter and sprinkle with salt and smoked paprika.

Kryptonite Jell-O

Firm lime Jell-O cut into jagged shards, served with whipped cream.

Prep 11 min, cook 4 min, 4 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets of lime jelly crystals
  • 400ml boiling water
  • 2 gelatine leaves
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Soften the gelatine leaves in cold water.
  2. Dissolve the jelly crystals in the boiling water and stir in the squeezed gelatine.
  3. Pour into a lined slab tin and chill until firm, 4 hr.
  4. Cut into jagged shards, pile on a plate and serve with whipped cream.

Mains

Ma Kent's fried chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken with mashed potatoes and gravy.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 10 min, 23 hr waiting · Make ahead: Soak the chicken the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 800g potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 400ml chicken stock
  1. Soak the chicken overnight in the buttermilk and salt.
  2. Dredge in the flour seasoned with the paprika and black pepper.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 165°C in batches until 74°C inside, 35 min.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and milk, 20 min.
  5. Make gravy from a little of the frying fat, flour and the stock.

Lex Luthor's lobster thermidor

Lobster in white wine, cream, Dijon and gruyère, grilled in the shell, for Otisburg.

How it is made
  • 2 cooked lobsters
  • 2 shallots
  • 30g butter
  • 100ml white wine
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 90g gruyère
  • 1 lemon
  • mixed salad leaves
  1. Lobster meat folded through a rich sauce of shallot, white wine, cream, Dijon and gruyère, piled back into the shells and grilled bubbling and golden. Halve 2 cooked lobsters lengthways, remove the meat and cut into chunks, keeping the shells. Soften 2 finely chopped shallots in 30g butter, add 100ml white wine and reduce by half. Stir in 200ml cream and 1 tsp Dijon and simmer 3 minutes until thick, then add 60g grated gruyère and the lobster. Spoon back into the shells, top with 30g more gruyère and grill 4 minutes until golden. Serve with lemon and a green salad.

Fortress of Solitude meringuesVegetarian

Tall crisp meringue crystals dusted with powdered sugar.

Prep 12 min, cook 1 hr 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 1 tsp icing sugar
  1. Whisk the egg whites and sugar in a bowl over simmering water until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Whisk off the heat with the cream of tartar until stiff and glossy, 10 min.
  3. Pipe tall jagged crystal shapes on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 100°C and cool in the oven, 1 hr 30 min.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.

Glazed doughnuts

Yeast doughnuts dipped in a sweet vanilla glaze. Perry White's newsroom running on sugar.

How it is made
  • 350g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 180ml milk
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • red and blue sprinkles
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Fluffy yeasted doughnuts fried golden and dipped in a vanilla glaze, half finished with red and blue sprinkles for the man in the cape. Warm 180ml milk with 30g butter and 2 tbsp caster sugar, add 7g yeast and 1 egg and mix into 350g plain flour with a pinch of salt. Knead 5 minutes and prove 1 hour. Roll 1.5cm thick, cut 10 rings and prove 20 minutes. Fry at 175°C for 1 minute a side. Whisk 200g icing sugar with 3 tbsp milk and 1 tsp vanilla, dip the warm doughnuts and finish half with red and blue sprinkles.

Iced coffee maltedNo alcohol

Strong cold coffee shaken with malted milk, under whipped cream and cocoa.

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Brew, sweeten and cool the malted coffee up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; pour over ice and milk and top with the cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml strong brewed coffee
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 tsp cocoa
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Brew the coffee strong, stir in the malt and sugar until dissolved and cool, 6 min.
  2. Fill four glasses with ice and cold milk, then pour the coffee over.
  3. Whisk the cream with the icing sugar until just thick but pourable, spoon on top and dust with cocoa.
