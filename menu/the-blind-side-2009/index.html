Snacks

Memphis dry rub barbecue wings

Crisp baked wings in smoky paprika dry rub, barbecue mustard dip.

Prep 5 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Rub up to 1 day ahead, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  1. Mix the paprika, brown sugar, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne and salt.
  2. Toss the wings with baking powder and the rub.
  3. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once.
  4. Mix the barbecue sauce, mustard and vinegar for dipping.

98th percentile devilled eggs

Creamy pickle yolk filling topped with crisp bacon and chives.

Prep 21 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp American mustard
  • 1 jar of dill pickles
  • 3 rashers bacon
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch of chives
  1. Cook the eggs in boiling water.
  2. Cool in iced water, then peel.
  3. Halve the eggs and scoop the yolks into a bowl.
  4. Mash with mayonnaise, mustard, chopped pickles and salt.
  5. Pipe back into the whites.
  6. Grill the bacon until crisp and crumble.
  7. Top the eggs with bacon, a dusting of smoked paprika and chives.

Mains

Tuohy Thanksgiving turkey at the table

Herb butter roast turkey breast with cornbread stuffing.

Prep 27 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Gravy and sides up to 1 day ahead, roast the turkey fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 120g butter
  • 1 bunch of sage
  • 300g cornbread
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 egg
  • 450ml chicken stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 jar of cranberry sauce
  1. Push the herb butter under the skin of the turkey breast.
  2. Roast at 180°C until cooked through, then rest.
  3. Crumble the cornbread with the fried onion, celery, chopped sage, egg and stock.
  4. Bake the stuffing until golden.
  5. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  6. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with butter and milk.
  7. Blanch the green beans and toss in butter.
  8. Make gravy from the pan juices with flour and stock.
  9. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Thanksgiving baked mac and cheeseVegetarian

Three-cheese baked macaroni with a golden top.

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; add the crumbs and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g macaroni
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml full cream milk
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g gruyère, grated
  • 60g cream cheese
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • 50g panko breadcrumbs
  1. Grate the cheeses.
  2. Boil the macaroni for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and simmer until thick. Stir in the cheeses, saving a handful, the cream cheese and mustard.
  4. Fold through the macaroni, tip into a baking dish and scatter with the saved cheese and the panko.
  5. Bake at 200°C until bubbling and golden.

Ferdinand the Bull pecan pieVegetarian

Pecan pie with a sticky brown sugar and golden syrup filling.

Prep 17 min, cook 60 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 150ml golden syrup
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g pecans
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Line a 23cm pie dish with the pastry.
  2. Bake blind at 190°C.
  3. Whisk the eggs, brown sugar, golden syrup, melted butter, vanilla and salt, then stir in the pecans.
  4. Pour into the case.
  5. Bake at 170°C until just set with a slight wobble.
  6. Cool for 1 hour and serve with ice cream.

Memphis peach sweet teaNo alcohol

Sweet black tea with peach nectar, lemon and mint over ice.

Prep 12 min, cook 3 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 300ml peach nectar
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 peach
  • 1 bunch of mint
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in boiling water with the sugar.
  2. Leave to steep, then remove the bags.
  3. Cool, then chill until cold.
  4. Stir in the peach nectar and lemon juice.
  5. Fill glasses with ice and peach slices, pour over the tea and finish with mint and lemon wheels.
