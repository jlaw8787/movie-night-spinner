Snacks

Salmon roe blinis

Warm blinis with crème fraîche, hot-smoked salmon and salmon roe.

Prep 14 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Mix the crème fraîche up to 1 day ahead and flake the salmon; warm the blinis and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail blinis
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g hot-smoked salmon
  • 50g jar salmon roe
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  1. Warm the blinis on a tray at 180°C.
  2. Mix the crème fraîche with the lemon zest and a pinch of salt. Flake the smoked salmon.
  3. Top each blini with crème fraîche, a few flakes of salmon and a little roe.
  4. Finish with chives and cracked pepper and serve with lemon wedges.

Lightstone mansion prawn cocktail

Cooked king prawns on cos with Marie Rose sauce, avocado and lemon.

Prep 14 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 2 days ahead; build the glasses just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g cooked king prawns, peeled
  • 1 baby cos lettuce
  • 1 avocado
  • 125g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, Worcestershire, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of paprika.
  2. Shred the cos and dice the avocado.
  3. Pile the cos and avocado into four glasses and top with the prawns.
  4. Spoon over the sauce and dust with paprika. Serve with lemon wedges.

Mains

Lightstone prime rib with horseradish

Garlic pepper beef rib roast with horseradish sauce.

Prep 8 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Rub the beef up to 1 day ahead and keep it uncovered in the fridge; mix the horseradish cream up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.2kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp duck fat
  • 100ml red grape juice
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 1 lemon
  1. Rub the beef with the garlic, pepper, oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 220°C, then lower to 180°C for medium rare.
  3. Rest the beef.
  4. Parboil the potatoes, rough up, toss in duck fat and roast.
  5. Simmer the pan juices with the grape juice, balsamic and stock until reduced by half.
  6. Mix the sour cream with the horseradish and a squeeze of lemon.

Gertrude's truffle potato gratinVegetarian

Thin potatoes baked in garlic cream and gruyère, a little truffle oil.

Prep 25 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, cool and keep in the fridge; reheat covered, then uncover to crisp the top
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 120g gruyère, grated
  • 2 tsp truffle oil
  1. Slice the potatoes thin and grate the gruyère.
  2. Warm the cream and milk with the garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Layer the potatoes in a buttered dish with most of the cheese, pour over the cream and top with the rest of the cheese.
  4. Bake at 180°C until tender and golden.
  5. Rest 10 minutes and drizzle with truffle oil.

Gingerbread's brown butter shortbreadVegetarian

Brown butter shortbread fingers half dipped in chocolate, left out on the mantel.

Prep 10 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 110g caster sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 60g cornflour
  • 150g dark chocolate, melted
  • 2 candy canes, crushed
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Melt the butter and cook until golden brown and nutty, then chill until firm but soft.
  2. Beat with the sugar, then mix in the flour, cornflour and a pinch of salt.
  3. Press into a lined tin and prick with a fork.
  4. Bake at 160°C, cut into fingers while warm, then cool.
  5. Dip one end of each in melted chocolate and sprinkle with candy cane and sea salt.

Naughty list black forest trifleVegetarian

Chocolate cake, cherries, cream and cocoa in a glass bowl.

Prep 20 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 680g jar pitted cherries
  • 400g chocolate cake
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 600ml thick vanilla custard
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Drain the cherries, keeping the syrup. Break the cake into a glass bowl and drizzle with some syrup. Scatter over the cherries.
  2. Whisk the cocoa into the custard and pour over.
  3. Chill.
  4. Whip the cream with the icing sugar and vanilla and spread on top.
  5. Shave the dark chocolate over with a peeler.

Drinks

Santa's dark brew and smoky tea chaserNo alcohol

Cold stout with a whisky shot on the side, lime and bitters.

Prep 6 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Smoky tea up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cans cold alcohol-free stout (440ml each)
  • 2 tsp smoky black tea leaves, such as lapsang souchong
  • 150ml boiling water
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Steep the tea leaves in the boiling water for 4 minutes, strain, stir in the maple syrup and chill.
  2. Chill the pint glasses.
  3. Pour the alcohol-free stout into each glass at an angle, then straighten up to build a creamy head. Let it settle before topping up.
  4. Pour a measure of the cold smoky tea into shot glasses and serve alongside.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Santa stops at the pub.
