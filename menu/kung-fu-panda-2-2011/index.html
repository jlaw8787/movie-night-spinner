Radish crate crispy daikon cakes

Steamed daikon and Chinese sausage cakes, fried crisp, oyster sauce.

Prep 16 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Steam and cool the cakes up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; slice and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g daikon
  • 2 Chinese sausages (lap cheong)
  • 4 dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 2 spring onions
  • 200g rice flour
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • oyster sauce, to taste
  • chilli oil, to taste
  • 200ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Grate the daikon and simmer in its juice for 10 minutes.
  2. Fry the diced Chinese sausages, the soaked chopped shiitake and the spring onions for 3 minutes.
  3. Mix into the daikon with the rice flour, the water, the salt and the sugar.
  4. Press into an oiled 20cm square tin and steam 45 minutes.
  5. Cool completely, then slice into squares and fry in oil 3 minutes a side until crisp.
  6. Serve with oyster sauce and chilli oil.

Bean bun warrior bunsVegetarian

Soft steamed buns filled with sweet red bean paste.

Prep 96 min, then 32 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the dough and prove it in the fridge up to 1 day ahead; fill, prove and steam on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 220ml milk
  • 300g red bean paste
  1. Mix the flour, the dried yeast, the sugar and the warm milk into a smooth dough and prove 1 hour.
  2. Divide into equal portions, fill each with a spoon of red bean paste and pinch closed.
  3. Prove 20 minutes, then steam 12 minutes until puffed.

Gongmen salt and pepper prawns

Cornflour dusted, fried crisp, tossed with garlic, chilli, spring onion and crackling five spice.

How it is made
  • 600g green king prawns
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • five spice powder
  • 60g cornflour
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Prawns dusted in cornflour and fried crisp, tossed with garlic, chilli, spring onion and a five spice salt that crackles like fireworks. Peel 600g green prawns leaving the tails on and pat dry. Toast 1 tsp Sichuan pepper, crush with 2 tsp salt and half a tsp five spice. Toss the prawns in 60g cornflour. Fry in oil at 180°C for 1 to 2 minutes until crisp and drain. Pour off most of the oil and fry 3 sliced garlic cloves, 2 sliced red chillies and 2 spring onions for 30 seconds. Toss in the prawns and 2 tsp of the salt.

Mr Ping's noodles

Chicken broth with shiitake, soft boiled eggs and egg noodles.

Prep 0 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Make the broth and boil the eggs up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat the broth and cook the noodles and bok choy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5 L chicken stock
  • 150g shiitake mushrooms
  • 4 eggs
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 2 bok choys
  • 3 spring onions
  1. Simmer the chicken stock with the sliced shiitake for 10 minutes.
  2. Boil the eggs for 6 minutes, cool and peel.
  3. Cook the egg noodles and divide into bowls, add the halved bok choy blanched in the broth, ladle over the stock and top with halved eggs and sliced spring onion, 13 min.

Lord Shen's firecracker chicken

Crisp thigh tossed with dried chillies, Sichuan pepper and peanuts in a tingly black vinegar glaze.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets
  • soy sauce
  • cornflour
  • 1 tbsp Chinkiang black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 15 dried red chillies
  • Sichuan peppercorns
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 60g roasted peanuts
  • vegetable oil
  • 1.5 cups jasmine rice
  1. Crisp chicken stir-fried with dried chillies, Sichuan pepper, garlic and roasted peanuts in a sweet, tingly soy and black vinegar glaze, with steamed rice. Cut 700g chicken thigh into 2cm pieces and toss with 1 tbsp soy and 2 tbsp cornflour. Mix 2 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp Chinkiang black vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp cornflour and 3 tbsp water. Fry the chicken in a hot wok with oil for 5 minutes until crisp, remove. Fry 15 dried chillies and 1 tsp Sichuan pepper for 30 seconds, then 3 garlic cloves and ginger. Return the chicken with the sauce and 60g roasted peanuts and toss. Serve with rice.

Peach bunsVegetarian

Steamed buns shaped like peaches, with a sweet lotus seed filling.

Prep 90 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Shape the buns up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; steam and dab with the colouring on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 5g dried yeast
  • 30g caster sugar
  • 250g red bean paste
  • 1 pack red food colouring
  • 170ml water
  1. Make a dough of the flour, the yeast, the sugar and the warm water and prove 1 hour.
  2. Shape the balls around red bean paste, pinch a point and score a crease like a peach.
  3. Steam 12 minutes, then dab the tips with a little red colouring.

Gongmen City sour plum coolerNo alcohol

Smoked plum and hawthorn drink sweetened with rock sugar.

Prep 1 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Soak, simmer, steep and chill the cooler up to 3 days ahead, covered in the fridge; serve over ice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 dried smoked plums
  • 15g dried hawthorn slices
  • 80g rock sugar
  • 1 tsp dried osmanthus
  • 3 cups ice, as needed
  • 1.5 L water
  1. Rinse the dried smoked plums (wumei) and the dried hawthorn slices and soak in the water for 30 minutes.
  2. Bring to the boil and simmer 40 minutes.
  3. Add the rock sugar and stir until dissolved, then take off the heat and stir in the dried osmanthus.
  4. Steep 10 minutes, strain and chill well.
  5. Serve over ice with a little extra osmanthus on top.
