Snacks

Devils on horseback bacon prunes

Prunes stuffed with almonds, wrapped in bacon and baked crisp.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 24 pitted prunes
  • 24 almonds
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 24 toothpicks, to secure
  1. Push an almond into each prune.
  2. Wrap each in a third of a bacon rasher and secure with a toothpick.
  3. Bake at 200°C until the bacon is crisp, 15 min.

Wet walk home grilled cheese sandwichesVegetarian

Crisp cheddar grilled sandwiches with hot tomato soup to dunk.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 800ml tomato soup
  1. Butter the bread on the outside and fill 4 slices with the cheddar.
  2. Toast in a pan over medium heat until golden and melted, 12 min.
  3. Heat the tomato soup and pour into mugs, 8 min.
  4. Cut the toasties into soldiers and serve for dunking.

Mains

Chicken à la king

Chicken, mushrooms and bell pepper in cream sauce, in puff pastry cases.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken breast, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 200g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 125ml chicken stock
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  1. Cut the pastry into squares, shape into cases and bake at 200°C until puffed, 15 min.
  2. Fry the chicken in the butter, then add the mushrooms and capsicum, 12 min.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the cream and stock, and simmer until thick, 8 min.
  4. Spoon into the pastry cases.

Premiere night roast beef with popovers

Sirloin carved pink, puffed batter puddings, horseradish cream and wine gravy, for The Royal Rascal.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef sirloin
  • 1kg potatoes
  • vegetable oil
  • 140g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 200ml milk
  • 150ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp horseradish
  1. Beef sirloin roasted pink and carved thin, with puffed popovers, crunchy roast potatoes, horseradish cream and a rich red wine gravy. Heat the oven to 220°C. Parboil 1kg potatoes 8 minutes, rough up and roast in oil 50 minutes. Season a 1.2kg sirloin, sear and roast 30 to 35 minutes for medium rare, then rest 15 minutes. Whisk 140g plain flour, 3 eggs and 200ml milk. Heat 1 tsp oil in each hole of a 12-hole muffin tin until smoking, pour in the batter and bake 20 minutes. Make gravy from the pan juices, 150ml red wine and 300ml stock. Mix 150ml whipped cream with 2 tbsp horseradish.

Pop-out party cakeVegetarian

Layer cake with pink vanilla buttercream and sprinkles.

Prep 35 min, cook 30 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 200g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 400g icing sugar
  • pink food colouring, a drop
  • 50g sprinkles
  1. Beat the cake butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk. Divide between two 20cm tins.
  2. Bake at 175°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool completely, 30 min.
  4. Beat the icing butter with the icing sugar and a drop of pink colouring.
  5. Stack and cover the layers, then shower with sprinkles.

Drinks

Sparkling lemonade in coupesNo alcohol

Homemade lemon syrup topped with sparkling water.

Prep 14 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 lemons
  • 600ml sparkling water, chilled
  1. Simmer the sugar with the water and the zest of 2 lemons until dissolved, 6 min.
  2. Cool, then add the lemon juice, 9 min.
  3. Pour into 4 coupe glasses, top with chilled sparkling water and a lemon twist.

When to serve

Serve the cake when Kathy pops out of it at the party.
