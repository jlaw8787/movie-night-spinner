Cat lady cover croque monsieur bites

Ham and gruyère grilled sandwiches under a bubbling béchamel, cut small.

Prep 7 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Make the bechamel up to 2 days ahead with cling wrap on the surface; fry, top and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 160g gruyère, grated
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 150g sliced leg ham
  • 8 cornichons
  1. Melt some of the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and simmer until thick. Season with nutmeg and stir in a little gruyère, 8 min.
  2. Spread the bread thinly with Dijon and make sandwiches with ham and gruyère.
  3. Fry the sandwiches in butter on both sides, 5 min.
  4. Spread the bechamel on top, scatter with more gruyère and grill until bubbling, 3 min.
  5. Cut into fingers and serve with cornichons.

Budapest garlic lángos with sour creamVegetarian

Fried flatbread rubbed with garlic and topped with sour cream.

Prep 24 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Divide the dough and rest the balls up to 4 hours ahead; mix the garlic oil up to 2 days ahead and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 120g tasty cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 small bunch dill, chopped
  1. Divide the dough into four balls and rest, 15 min.
  2. Stretch each by hand into a round, thinner in the middle.
  3. Fry in about 3cm of oil at 180°C, turning once, until puffed and golden. Drain, 8 min.
  4. Mix the garlic with the olive oil and brush over the hot bread.
  5. Spread with sour cream, scatter with cheese, paprika and dill.

Rome cacio e pepe with toasted pepperVegetarian

Spaghetti with pecorino, butter and toasted black pepper.

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best cooked and served at once; crack the pepper and grate the pecorino up to 1 day ahead, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns, coarsely cracked
  • 30g butter
  • 180g pecorino, finely grated, plus extra to serve
  1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water for 2 minutes less than the packet says.
  2. Toast the cracked black peppercorns in a large dry frying pan, then add the butter and a ladle of pasta water, 3 min.
  3. Mix most of the pecorino to a paste with cool pasta water, 7 min.
  4. Move the pasta into the pepper pan with a splash more water and toss. Off the heat, toss in the cheese paste until glossy. Serve with extra pecorino, 3 min.

CIA basement fudge sheet cakeVegetarian

Chocolate coffee cake with fudge frosting and toasted hazelnuts.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 60g cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml hot brewed coffee
  • 100g butter
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 60g hazelnuts, toasted and chopped
  1. Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa and bicarbonate of soda. Whisk in the eggs, most of the milk, oil and hot coffee to a thin batter.
  2. Pour into a lined 23 x 33cm tin and bake at 180°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool the cake, 15 min.
  4. Melt the butter and chocolate, then beat in the icing sugar and remaining milk, 5 min.
  5. Spread the icing over the cake and scatter with hazelnuts.

Rayna Boyanov's high roller spritz

Bitter orange, prosecco and soda for the owl toast.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best built in the glass; slice the oranges up to 1 day ahead and keep the prosecco and soda water in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bitter orange aperitif
  • 375ml prosecco, chilled
  • 120ml soda water
  • 2 blood oranges (or regular oranges)
  • 1 jar green olives
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill large wine glasses with ice.
  2. Pour the aperitif into each, then add the prosecco and soda water. Stir gently once.
  3. Slide a thick wedge of blood orange into each glass and serve with a bowl of olives.
