Ray Bones' Miami croquetas

Crumbed ham logs in nutmeg béchamel, crisp outside, before Chili gets his leather jacket back

Prep 20 min, cook 22 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the bechamel up to 1 day ahead; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 80g plain flour
  • 500ml milk
  • 200g ham, finely chopped
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  1. Melt the butter, cook the flour for 1 minute and whisk in the milk until very thick.
  2. Stir in the ham and nutmeg, spread on a tray and chill for 3 hours.
  3. Shape into 20 logs and roll in egg and breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in the neutral oil at 180C in batches until golden.

Hollywood power lunch crudités

Baby carrots, radishes and cos with tarragon anchovy green goddess, for a studio pitch meeting

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead; cut the vegetables up to 4 hours ahead and keep in iced water
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches baby carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 telegraph cucumber
  • 2 baby cos lettuces
  • 125g mayonnaise
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 3 anchovy fillets
  • 1 lemon
  1. Blitz the mayonnaise, sour cream, a big handful each of parsley and tarragon, half the chives, the anchovies and the lemon juice until smooth and green.
  2. Trim the carrots and radishes, leaving a little green on top. Cut the cucumber into batons and pull the cos into leaves.
  3. Stand the vegetables up in glasses of ice and serve with the green goddess in a bowl.

Chili Palmer's steak frites

Seared sirloin, shallot and red wine butter, double-fried chips, look at me

Prep 5 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce while the steak rests; fry the chips twice
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l neutral oil, for frying
  • 800g sirloin steaks (4)
  • 2 shallots, chopped
  • 150ml red wine
  • 50g cold butter
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin frites and fry in the neutral oil at 140C for 5 minutes, then rest.
  2. Season the steaks and sear hard for 3 minutes a side, then rest.
  3. Sweat the shallots in the pan fat, add the wine and reduce by two thirds, then whisk in the butter.
  4. Fry the frites again in the oil at 185C until crisp.
  5. Slice the steak and spoon the sauce over.

Harry Zimm's B-movie popcorn sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, caramel popcorn and peanuts, for the cinema showing Touch of Evil

Prep 13 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Caramel popcorn up to 2 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 40g popcorn kernels
  • 50g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml cream
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 50g salted peanuts
  1. Pop the popcorn kernels.
  2. Melt the butter with the brown sugar, toss through the popcorn and bake at 150C for 10 minutes to set.
  3. Melt the chocolate with the cream for hot fudge.
  4. Scoop ice cream into four bowls, pour over the fudge and top with popcorn and peanuts.

Cuban pastelitosVegetarian

Chili's Miami, before he heads out to LA

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 135g guava paste
  • 9 tsp cream cheese
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Cut each pastry sheet into 9 squares.
  2. Put a slice of guava paste and a teaspoon of cream cheese on half the squares.
  3. Top with the other squares, press the edges with a fork and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200C for 18 minutes until puffed and golden, then dust with sugar while hot.

Acting lesson Bloody Mary

Chili showing a movie star how to stare a man down

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the tomato juice, lemon juice and seasonings up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; add the vodka and shake with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml vodka
  • 480ml tomato juice
  • 40ml lemon juice
  • Worcestershire sauce, to taste
  • hot sauce, to taste
  • celery salt and pepper, to taste
  • 4 celery sticks
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Put a share of vodka, tomato juice, lemon, Worcestershire, hot sauce, celery salt and pepper in a tin with ice.
  2. Pour back and forth between two tins 5 times.
  3. Strain over fresh ice and add a celery stick. Repeat for each drink.
