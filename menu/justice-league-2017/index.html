Speedster pancake stack

Buttermilk pancakes stacked with crisp bacon

Prep 5 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Cook the pancakes up to 1 day ahead, keep covered and reheat in a low oven; fry the bacon fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml buttermilk
  • 50g butter
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, eggs and buttermilk to a thick batter.
  2. Cook small pancakes in butter until golden.
  3. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  4. Stack the pancakes with bacon between and pour over maple syrup.

Icelandic village smoked salmon

Rye crispbread with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, dill and pickled onion

Prep 14 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 packet rye crispbread
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Slice a few pickled onions into rings.
  2. Spread the crispbreads with crème fraîche.
  3. Top with a fold of salmon, a sprig of dill and onion rings.
  4. Serve cold on a slate.

Cyborg circuit choc pretzel rodsOn the printed menu

Pretzel rods dipped in white chocolate with blue circuit lines, for Victor Stone, the Cyborg

How it is made
  • 300g white chocolate
  • 24 pretzel rods
  • 1 tube blue gel food colouring
  • 1 jar silver cachous
  1. Pretzel rods dipped in melted white chocolate, set on baking paper, then drizzled with blue icing lines like circuits. Melt 250g white chocolate in short bursts in the microwave, stirring each time. Dip 24 pretzel rods two thirds of the way in, let the excess drip off and lay on baking paper. Chill 15 minutes until set. Melt 50g more white chocolate and tint it bright blue with gel colouring. Spoon into a snap-lock bag, snip a tiny corner and pipe thin straight lines and dots like circuits. Sprinkle with a pinch of silver cachous and chill to set.

Themyscira lemon lamb

Leg of lamb with lemon, garlic and oregano, tomato and cucumber salad

Prep 25 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Rub the lamb with the lemon, garlic and oregano up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast and chop the salad on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 200g feta
  • salt, to taste
  1. Rub the lamb with lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano, oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 180°C.
  3. Rest the lamb.
  4. Chop the tomatoes and cucumber and toss with feta, oil and lemon.
  5. Slice the lamb and serve with the salad.

Lake house garlic butter rib eyeOn the printed menu

Seared, basted steak, rested and sliced, crunchy roast potatoes, charred broccolini, Alfred serving

How it is made
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 1 lemon
  1. Rib eye seared hard and butter basted with garlic, rested 5 minutes, sliced with crispy roast potatoes and charred broccolini. Take 4 rib eye steaks out 30 minutes early and season well. Parboil 1kg potatoes in chunks for 8 minutes, rough them up and roast in oil at 220°C for 45 minutes. Sear the steaks in a smoking pan 3 minutes a side, then add 60g butter, 3 crushed garlic cloves and thyme and baste for 1 minute. Rest 5 minutes. Char 2 bunches broccolini in the same pan with a squeeze of lemon. Slice the steak and pour over the pan butter.

Speed force sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream under warm salted caramel

Prep 6 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Make the caramel up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm gently; scoop and add the popping candy at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 30g butter
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 2 packets popping candy
  1. Melt the sugar in a pan without stirring to an amber caramel.
  2. Whisk in the cream, butter and salt and let it cool slightly.
  3. Scoop ice cream into bowls and pour over the warm caramel.
  4. Scatter popping candy on just before serving.

Arthur's whisky ginger highballVegetarian

Scotch whisky with honey ginger syrup, over ice

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Simmer, cool and strain the ginger syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; build the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 180ml Scotch whisky
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml ginger ale
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the honey, water and ginger for 2 minutes, then cool and strain.
  2. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  3. Add whisky, ginger syrup and lime juice to each.
  4. Top with ginger ale, stir once and garnish with a lime wedge.
