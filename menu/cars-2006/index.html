Sally's Cozy Cone curly friesVegetarian

Oven baked curly fries with smoked paprika salt.

Prep 9 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Mix the seasoning and the lemon aioli up to 2 days ahead, aioli chilled; bake the chips on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen curly chips
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 lemon, zested
  • 150g garlic aioli
  1. Spread the chips on 2 lined trays.
  2. Bake at 220°C, turning once, until crisp, 20 min.
  3. Mix the paprika, salt and garlic powder and toss through the hot chips.
  4. Stir the lemon zest into the aioli.
  5. Roll baking paper into cones, fill with chips and serve with the aioli for dipping.

Tractor tipping cornVegetarian

Corn cobs brushed with chilli lime butter and charred.

Prep 6 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Mix the chilli lime butter up to 3 days ahead and keep it chilled; boil and char the corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 lime
  1. Boil the corn cobs, then drain, 6 min.
  2. Mix the butter with the chilli flakes and the lime zest and juice.
  3. Brush over the corn and char on a hot grill, turning, until blistered all over, 8 min.
  4. Brush with more butter to serve.

Luigi's tire stack onion rings

Thick brown onion rounds in fizzy soda water batter, fried crunchy, smoky chipotle mayo.

How it is made
  • 3 large brown onions
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 300ml soda water
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 150g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo
  • 1 lime
  1. Thick onion rings dunked in a fizzy soda water batter, fried until crunchy and stacked like tyres, with a smoky chipotle mayo. Slice 3 large brown onions into 1.5cm rings and separate. Whisk 150g plain flour, 50g cornflour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp salt and 300ml ice-cold soda water into a smooth batter. Heat oil to 180°C. Dust the rings in flour, dip in batter and fry in batches for 3 minutes until golden. Drain and salt. Mix 150g mayo with 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo and a squeeze of lime. Stack the rings on skewers like tyres and serve with the dip.

Flo's café burgers

Beef patties with melted cheddar, bacon and fried onion, crinkle fries.

Prep 27 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; fry the chips and cook the burgers on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 150g bacon
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 4 burger buns
  1. Cut the potatoes into crinkle chips.
  2. Fry the chips twice in the oil until crisp, 18 min.
  3. Shape the mince into patties and season.
  4. Grill the patties, melting cheddar on top at the end, 10 min.
  5. Fry the bacon and onion until golden, 12 min.
  6. Toast the buns, 3 min.
  7. Stack it all in the buns and serve with the chips.

Route 66 beef chilli with cornbread

Chuck simmered slow with kidney beans, cumin and tomato till thick, fuel for a rookie racer.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml beef stock
  • kidney beans
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 60g butter
  1. Beef chuck browned and simmered 3 hours with kidney beans, chilli, cumin and tomato until thick, served with cornbread. Brown 1kg diced beef chuck in batches. Fry 2 onions and 4 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika and 2 tsp chilli powder. Return the beef with 2 tins crushed tomatoes and 250ml beef stock and simmer covered for 2.5 hours. Add 2 drained tins kidney beans for the last 30 minutes. For cornbread, mix 150g cornmeal, 150g flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 egg, 250ml buttermilk and 60g melted butter and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.

Piston Cup sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream under hot dark chocolate fudge sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to 3 days ahead, keep it chilled and warm gently; scoop and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml whipped cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Heat the cream until steaming, 4 min.
  2. Pour over the chocolate and stir until glossy hot fudge.
  3. Scoop ice cream into tall glasses.
  4. Pour over the fudge and top with whipped cream and a cherry.

Flo's oil can floatNo alcohol

Cold cola with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Chill the cola and glasses up to 1 day ahead; add the ice cream just before serving so it fizzes
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L cold cola
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Pour the cola into tall glasses, leaving room at the top.
  2. Gently add a scoop of ice cream to each so it fizzes and foams up dark like an oil can.
  3. Serve with a long spoon and a straw straight away.
