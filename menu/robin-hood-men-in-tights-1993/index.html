Snacks

Little John's toll bridge bread rollsVegetarian

No tolls, then we don't eat no rolls

Prep 30 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Make the dough the night before and prove it in the fridge; bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g sachet dried yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 320ml warm water
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 rosemary sprigs, leaves chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes, plus 1 tsp for the dough
  • 100g soft butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil into a dough and knead until smooth.
  2. Cover and leave until doubled.
  3. Shape into 12 balls and pack them into a lined tin, then leave to puff.
  4. Brush with oil, scatter with rosemary and salt flakes and bake at 210C until golden.
  5. Beat the butter with the honey and serve with the warm rolls.

Loxley and Bagelle bagels with lox

Loxley and Bagel? It can't miss, says Tuckman

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the herbed cream cheese up to 2 days ahead; toast and top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bagels, halved
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 250g sliced smoked salmon
  • 1/2 red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp baby capers
  1. Beat the cream cheese with the dill, lemon zest and pepper.
  2. Toast the bagel halves until golden.
  3. Spread thickly with the cream cheese and drape over the salmon.
  4. Top with onion and capers and a squeeze of lemon.

Mains

Not-a-wild-boar roast pork with apples

A wild boar? No, that's a wild pig

Prep 20 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Score and salt the pork up to a day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge; roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg boneless pork belly, skin scored
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 4 red apples, halved
  • 2 brown onions, halved
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 375ml chicken stock
  • 125ml cloudy apple juice
  • 800g potatoes, for mash
  • 60g butter
  1. Pat the skin very dry and rub with the salt and fennel seeds.
  2. Roast on the onions at 230C for 30 minutes, then at 160C until tender, adding the apples for the last 40 minutes.
  3. Boil and mash the potatoes with the butter.
  4. Rest the pork, then stir the flour into the pan juices and whisk in the stock and apple juice for gravy.
  5. Carve into thick slabs and serve with apples, mash and gravy.

King Richard's homecoming chicken and leek pie

It's good to be the king, says the Rabbi

Prep 30 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 375ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in half the butter and set aside, then soften the leeks in the rest.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream, mustard and thyme, return the chicken and simmer until thick.
  3. Cool the filling, then spoon it into a pie dish.
  4. Cover with pastry, crimp, cut a steam hole and brush with egg.
  5. Bake at 200C until puffed and deep golden.

Latrine's eyeball souffle omeletteVegetarian

Latrine's omelette, eyeballs and all

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Stuff the lychee eyeballs up to a day ahead and chill; whisk and cook the omelettes to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs, separated
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 30g butter
  • 565g tin lychees, drained
  • 125g blueberries
  • 4 tbsp raspberry jam
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Push a blueberry into the hole of each lychee to make eyeballs.
  2. Beat the yolks with half the sugar and the vanilla, and whisk the whites with the rest to stiff peaks, then fold together.
  3. Melt the butter in an ovenproof pan, pour in the mix and cook gently for 3 minutes, then finish under the grill until puffed.
  4. Warm the jam, spoon it over, slide the omelette out, dust with icing sugar and stare back with the lychee eyeballs.

Rabbi Tuckman's spiced sacramental grape juiceVegetarianNo alcohol

Sacramental, says Tuckman. For blessing only

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Warm the punch up to a day ahead and reheat gently; it keeps in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l red grape juice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 star anise
  1. Put the grape juice, orange and spices in a pot.
  2. Warm gently without boiling for 10 minutes.
  3. Ladle into cups.
