Snacks

Frau Schmidt's picnic LiptauerVegetarian

Whipped quark and butter with paprika, caraway, capers and chives, with dark rye.

Prep 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250g quark
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika, plus extra to dust
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds, crushed
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1 eschalot, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • salt, to taste
  • 8 slices of dark rye bread
  • 1 bunch radishes, halved
  • 8 gherkins
  1. Beat the quark and butter together until smooth.
  2. Stir in the paprika, caraway, mustard, capers, eschalot, chives and salt.
  3. Cover and chill until firm.
  4. Pile into a bowl, dust with paprika and serve with the rye bread, radishes and gherkins.

Nonnberg Abbey pretzels with sweet mustardVegetarian

Soft baked pretzels with a dark chewy crust and salt flakes, with sweet mustard for dipping.

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min, then 70 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and warm at 180°C for 5 minutes; the dough can prove overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 L water, for the bath
  • 4 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tsp salt flakes
  • 125ml sweet mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, butter and warm water, then knead until smooth and elastic.
  2. Cover and prove until doubled.
  3. Cut into 8 pieces, roll each into a 50cm rope, thin at the ends, and twist into pretzels.
  4. Rest on lined trays.
  5. Simmer the bath water with the bicarbonate of soda and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds, then put back on the trays.
  6. Sprinkle with salt flakes, slash the thick part and bake at 220°C until deep brown.
  7. Cool a few minutes and serve warm with sweet mustard.

Uncle Max's cheese kransky board

Browned cheese kranskys with caraway sauerkraut, horseradish cream, hot mustard and cornichons.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Warm the sauerkraut up to 1 day ahead; brown the kranskys to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 8 cheese kranskys
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 4 tbsp hot mustard
  • 12 cornichons
  1. Warm the sauerkraut with the caraway and a splash of water.
  2. Score the kranskys and brown in the oil over medium heat, turning, until the cheese bubbles out.
  3. Slice on an angle.
  4. Pile on a board with the sauerkraut, horseradish cream, mustard and cornichons.

Mains

Captain von Trapp's Tafelspitz

Beef rump cap simmered with root vegetables in its own broth, apple horseradish.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Simmer the beef up to 1 day ahead and chill it in the broth, then reheat gently; make both sauces up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, halved
  • 1.5kg beef rump cap
  • 3 L cold water
  • 2 carrots, in chunks
  • 1 leek, in chunks
  • 250g celeriac, in chunks
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 10 black peppercorns
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 800g waxy potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 2 green apples
  • 3 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 200g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch chives, chopped
  1. Char the onion cut side down in a dry pan until black.
  2. Put the beef in a large pot with the cold water, bring to a simmer and skim.
  3. Add the onion, carrots, leek, celeriac, bay leaves, peppercorns and salt and simmer very gently, lid ajar, until tender.
  4. Meanwhile boil the potatoes until just tender, drain and slice thickly.
  5. Grate the apples into the horseradish cream with the lemon juice.
  6. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, vinegar and chives with a pinch of salt.
  7. Fry the potatoes in the butter until golden and toss with the parsley.
  8. Rest the beef in the broth, slice across the grain and serve in shallow bowls with a ladle of broth, the potatoes and both sauces.

Schnitzel with noodles

Veal schnitzel crumbed thin and fried, with buttered noodles.

Prep 2 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Crumb the schnitzels up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 veal schnitzels, about 120g each
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g dried breadcrumbs
  • 120ml vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 300g wide egg noodles
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 2 lemons
  1. Beat the veal thin between baking paper, then dip in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  2. Boil the noodles, drain and toss with half the butter, chopped parsley and salt.
  3. Fry the schnitzels in the oil and the rest of the butter, shaking the pan so the crumb puffs, until golden on both sides.
  4. Drain on paper and serve with the noodles and lemon wedges.

Sweets

Salzburger NockerlVegetarian

Three golden peaks of baked vanilla and lemon meringue souffle over warm raspberries.

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake to order; the raspberry base can sit ready in the dish up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 200g frozen raspberries
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 6 eggs, separated
  • pinch of salt
  • 1/2 lemon, zested
  • 30g plain flour, sifted
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Butter the dish, add the raspberries, 2 tbsp of the sugar, the milk and half the vanilla and warm in the oven.
  2. Whisk the 6 egg whites with the salt to soft peaks, then whisk in the rest of the sugar a spoonful at a time until stiff and glossy.
  3. Mix 4 of the yolks with the lemon zest and the rest of the vanilla, then fold through the whites with the flour.
  4. Pile into three tall peaks in the dish with a spatula.
  5. Bake until golden outside and soft in the middle.
  6. Dust with icing sugar and serve at once.

Drinks

Liesl's gazebo elderflower fizzNo alcohol

Elderflower cordial, cloudy apple and lemon over ice with mint, topped with soda.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the cordial, juice and lemon up to 4 hours ahead and chill; add the soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml elderflower cordial
  • 400ml cloudy apple juice
  • 3 lemons, 2 juiced and 1 sliced into wheels
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 4 cups ice
  • 1 L soda water
  1. Mix the elderflower cordial, apple juice and lemon juice in a jug.
  2. Bruise most of the mint and add to the jug.
  3. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice and pour in the elderflower mix.
  4. Top with soda water and stir gently.
  5. Garnish with a lemon wheel and a mint sprig.
