Snacks

Maximus's bread and olivesVegetarian

Warm olives with orange peel, fennel seed and garlic, with torn rustic bread

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Warm the olives with the oil, peel and spices up to 2 days ahead and keep them in the fridge; reheat gently and tear the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g mixed olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for dipping
  • 1 orange, peel in strips
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 rustic loaf
  • salt, to taste
  1. Warm the olives gently in the oil with the orange peel, fennel seeds and garlic for 5 minutes.
  2. Tear the loaf into chunks.
  3. Serve with the olives and a bowl of oil and salt for dipping.

Figs with honey and walnutsVegetarian

Baked figs with goat's cheese, honey, toasted walnuts and thyme

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill the figs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake and drizzle on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 ripe figs, halved
  • 120g goat's cheese
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 60g walnuts, toasted
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  1. Put a small spoon of goat's cheese on each fig half.
  2. Bake at 200C for 8 minutes.
  3. Drizzle with honey and scatter with walnuts and thyme leaves. Serve warm.

Mains

Colosseum lamb

Lamb leg studded with garlic, rosemary and anchovy, with tomato chickpeas

Prep 20 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Stud the lamb up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, and simmer the chickpeas up to 1 day ahead; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves, slivered
  • 3 sprigs rosemary
  • 4 anchovies
  • 2 tins chickpeas, drained
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 100g baby spinach
  1. Stud the lamb with garlic, rosemary and anchovies.
  2. Roast at 180C until 58C inside, about 90 minutes.
  3. Rest the lamb for 20 minutes. Meanwhile simmer the chickpeas with the tomatoes, then stir through the spinach.
  4. Carve and serve with the chickpeas.

Sweets

Roman honey cakesVegetarian

Ricotta cakes baked on bay leaves, drizzled with warm honey and poppy seeds

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 250g ricotta
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 8 bay leaves
  • 4 tbsp honey, warmed
  • 1 tsp poppy seeds
  1. Mix the ricotta, flour and egg into a soft dough.
  2. Shape into small rounds and set each on a bay leaf.
  3. Bake at 200C for 30 minutes.
  4. Pour over the warm honey and sprinkle with poppy seeds.

Drinks

Commodus's spiced honey wineVegetarian

Warm red wine with honey, pepper, bay, saffron and orange peel

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Warm and strain up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently without boiling
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 orange, peel in strips
  1. Pour the wine into a pot with the honey, peppercorns, bay leaves, saffron and orange peel.
  2. Warm gently for 15 minutes without boiling.
  3. Strain into cups and serve warm.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Maximus walks into the Colosseum.
