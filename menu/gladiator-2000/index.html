Maximus's bread and olivesVegetarian

Warm olives with orange peel, fennel seed and garlic, with torn rustic bread.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Warm the olives with the oil, peel and spices up to 2 days ahead and keep them in the fridge; reheat gently and tear the bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g mixed olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for dipping
  • 1 orange, peel in strips
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 rustic loaf
  • salt, to taste
  1. Warm the olives gently in the oil with the orange peel, fennel seeds and garlic for 5 minutes.
  2. Tear the loaf into chunks.
  3. Serve with the olives and a bowl of oil and salt for dipping.

Figs with honey and walnutsVegetarian

Baked figs with goat's cheese, honey, toasted walnuts and thyme.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill the figs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake and drizzle on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 ripe figs, halved
  • 120g goat's cheese
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 60g walnuts, toasted
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  1. Put a small spoon of goat's cheese on each fig half.
  2. Bake at 200°C for 8 minutes.
  3. Drizzle with honey and scatter with walnuts and thyme leaves. Serve warm.

Elysium wheat field crackersOn the printed menu

Wholemeal strips with rosemary, lemon ricotta and honey.

How it is made
  • 200g wholemeal flour
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Thin wholemeal crackers baked crisp with olive oil, sea salt and rosemary, with whipped lemon ricotta and a pool of honey and chilli oil. Mix 200g wholemeal flour, 60ml olive oil, 1 tsp salt and 90ml water into a firm dough. Roll very thin, cut into long strips, brush with oil and scatter with rosemary and sea salt. Bake at 180°C for 12 to 15 minutes until crisp. Beat 250g ricotta with lemon zest and salt. Swirl 1 tbsp honey with 1 tbsp chilli oil over the ricotta.

Mains

Colosseum lamb

Lamb leg studded with garlic, rosemary and anchovy, with chickpeas braised in wine and greens.

Prep 15 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Stud the lamb up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, and simmer the chickpeas up to 1 day ahead; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves, slivered
  • 3 sprigs rosemary
  • 4 anchovies
  • 2 tins chickpeas, drained
  • 250ml dry white wine
  • 100g baby spinach
  1. Stud the lamb with garlic, rosemary and anchovies.
  2. Roast at 180°C until 58°C inside, about 90 minutes.
  3. Rest the lamb for 20 minutes. Meanwhile simmer the chickpeas with the white wine, then stir through the spinach.
  4. Carve and serve with the chickpeas.

Proximo's sausage, barley and lentil stewOn the printed menu

Pork sausages, lentils, leeks and rosemary, olive oil and parmesan, fuel for the arena.

How it is made
  • 500g thick pork sausages
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 150g brown lentils
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1.2L chicken stock
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Thick and smoky stew of pork sausage, pearl barley and lentils with onion, carrot, tomato and rosemary, finished with olive oil and parmesan. Brown 500g thick pork sausages cut into chunks. Soften an onion, 2 carrots and 2 garlic cloves in the same pot. Add 100g pearl barley, 150g brown lentils, 400g crushed tomatoes, 1.2L chicken stock and rosemary. Simmer 50 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil, grated parmesan and crusty bread.

Roman honey cakesVegetarian

Ricotta cakes baked on bay leaves, drizzled with warm honey and poppy seeds.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 250g ricotta
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 8 bay leaves
  • 4 tbsp honey, warmed
  • 1 tsp poppy seeds
  1. Mix the ricotta, flour and egg into a soft dough.
  2. Shape into small rounds and set each on a bay leaf.
  3. Bake at 200°C for 30 minutes.
  4. Pour over the warm honey and sprinkle with poppy seeds.

Commodus's spiced honey wineVegetarian

Warm red wine with honey, pepper, bay, saffron and orange peel.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Warm and strain up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently without boiling
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 orange, peel in strips
  1. Pour the wine into a pot with the honey, peppercorns, bay leaves, saffron and orange peel.
  2. Warm gently for 15 minutes without boiling.
  3. Strain into cups and serve warm.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Maximus walks into the Colosseum.
