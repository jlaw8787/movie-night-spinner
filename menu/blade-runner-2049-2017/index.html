Snacks

Protein farm grub-free skewers

Grilled white miso and garlic chicken thigh skewers with a soy sesame dip

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; mix the dip up to 2 days ahead; thread and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs, diced
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Mix the miso with crushed garlic and coat the chicken.
  2. Marinate for an hour.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill until charred.
  5. Mix soy, sesame oil and sliced spring onion for dipping.

Rainy LA street mushroom gyozaVegetarian

Pan-fried mushroom, tofu and cabbage gyoza with soy, black vinegar and chilli oil

Prep 37 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled in a single layer, or freeze up to 1 month; fry and steam on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 200g firm tofu
  • 2 cups shredded wombok
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Salt the wombok, then squeeze it dry and mix with the mushrooms, crumbled tofu and a splash of the soy.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat.
  3. Fry in the oil until crisp underneath.
  4. Add water and steam.
  5. Serve with the rest of the soy, black vinegar and chilli oil.

Wooden horse soft pretzels with mustardOn the printed menu

Bicarb-dipped twists, rock salted and baked deep brown, served warm with a honey wholegrain dip

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Soft pretzel twists dipped in bicarb water, sprinkled with rock salt and baked deep brown, served warm with a honey and wholegrain mustard dip. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Divide into 8, roll into long ropes and twist into pretzels. Boil 2L water with 60g bicarb soda and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds. Sprinkle with rock salt and bake at 220C for 12 minutes. Mix 3 tbsp wholegrain mustard with 2 tbsp honey to dip.

Mains

Joi's rib eye, mash and red wine jus

Seared rib eye, mash, beans and jus. Joi's version was a hologram

Prep 11 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Mash the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and reheat; make the jus up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; sear and rest the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 rib eye steaks
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 300g green beans
  • 150ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  1. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter.
  2. Reduce the red wine with stock into a jus.
  3. Sear the steaks hard.
  4. Rest and slice the steaks.
  5. Steam the green beans.
  6. Plate it like a proper dinner and pour the jus over.

Off-world miso ramen with pork bellyOn the printed menu

Noodles in rich pork broth, seared soy-braised slices, jammy eggs, corn, nori and spring onion

How it is made
  • 600g pork belly
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle mirin
  • 4 eggs
  • 1.5L chicken stock
  • 1 tub white miso
  • 4 portions ramen noodles
  • 1 can corn kernels
  • 1 packet nori
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Ramen noodles in a rich pork and miso broth with seared soy-braised pork belly, jammy soy eggs, corn, nori and spring onion. Simmer 600g pork belly in 100ml soy, 100ml mirin and 500ml water for 90 minutes, then slice and sear. Boil 4 eggs for 6.5 minutes, peel and soak in some of the braising liquid. Heat 1.5L chicken stock, whisk in 4 tbsp white miso and 2 tbsp of the braising liquid. Cook 4 portions of ramen noodles. Fill bowls with noodles and broth and top with pork, halved eggs, corn, nori and spring onion.

Sweets

Snowfall panna cotta

Vanilla panna cotta under crushed meringue snow, K's last scene

Prep 18 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 gelatine sheets
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 2 meringue nests
  1. Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water.
  2. Warm the cream, milk, sugar and split vanilla bean.
  3. Stir in the squeezed gelatine and pour into glasses.
  4. Set in the fridge overnight.
  5. Crush the meringue nests over the top like falling snow.

Deckard's Scotch and honey highballVegetarian

Scotch, honey syrup and lemon topped with cold ginger ale

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep chilled; build over ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 180ml Scotch whisky
  • 1 lemon
  • 480ml cold ginger ale
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the honey into hot water to loosen and cool.
  2. Fill four tall glasses with ice and add Scotch, honey syrup and lemon juice, then stir.
  3. Top each with ginger ale and stir once more.
  4. Twist a strip of lemon peel over the top and drop it in.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when K finds Deckard in the empty Las Vegas casino.
