Snacks

Peameal back bacon on a bun

Cornmeal back bacon, onions, maple mustard. Hold the toast.

Prep 5 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Mix the maple mustard up to 3 days ahead; crumb and fry the bacon just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g piece back bacon
  • 60g fine cornmeal
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 8 soft white buns
  1. Cut the bacon into thick slices and press both sides into the cornmeal.
  2. Fry in oil over medium heat, turning once, until golden and cooked through.
  3. Fry the onions in the same pan.
  4. Mix the mustard with the maple syrup. Split and toast the buns, spread with maple mustard and fill with bacon and onions.

Doughnut shop honey glazed crullersVegetarian

Honey-glazed choux crullers from the doughnut shop counter.

Prep 7 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Pipe the rings onto baking paper up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; fry and glaze just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Bring water, butter and a pinch of salt to the boil. Beat in the flour until it forms a ball, then cool.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Pipe rings with a star nozzle onto squares of baking paper.
  4. Lower into oil at 180°C paper side up, peel off the paper and fry, turning once, until golden. Drain.
  5. Whisk the icing sugar, honey and water and dip the warm crullers.

Mains

McKenzie poutine with beef gravy

Chips, curds and hot beef gravy.

Prep 3 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 2 days ahead and reheat until very hot; bake the chips and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg frozen thick-cut chips
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 600ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 300g fresh cheese curds or bocconcini
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook until nutty.
  3. Whisk in the stock, Worcestershire and pepper and simmer until glossy.
  4. Tear the cheese into pieces. Pile the hot chips into bowls, scatter the cheese over and pour the very hot gravy on top.

Canadian maple butter tartsVegetarian

Flaky little tarts of gooey maple butter with pecans, the Canadian way.

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead, airtight; warm for 5 minutes at 150°C if you like them soft
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 80ml maple syrup
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 40g raisins
  • 40g pecans, chopped
  1. Cut rounds from the pastry and press into a greased muffin tin.
  2. Whisk the sugar, maple syrup, butter, egg, vanilla and a pinch of salt.
  3. Put raisins and pecans into each case and pour the filling two-thirds of the way up.
  4. Bake at 190°C until the filling bubbles and is just set.
  5. Cool in the tin.

Drinks

Elsinore maple lager shandy

Lager, lemonade and maple. Shot in 3B, three beers.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Stir the maple lemon mix up to 2 days ahead and chill; chill the glasses and lager, then pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 3 lemons
  • 4 bottles cold lager (330ml each)
  • 500ml cold lemonade
  1. Stir the maple syrup with the juice of two lemons in a jug. Chill the beer glasses.
  2. Pour a spoonful of the maple lemon mix into each glass and half fill with cold lager.
  3. Top with lemonade, pouring slowly down the side to keep the head. Add a lemon wedge.
