Snacks

Greek island mezzeVegetarian

Hummus, tzatziki, olives, cherry tomatoes and warm pita.

Prep 24 min · Make ahead: Make the hummus up to 3 days ahead and the tzatziki up to 1 day ahead, both covered in the fridge; warm the pita on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chickpeas
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 lemon
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 cucumber
  • 150g kalamata olives
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Blitz the drained chickpeas with the tahini, the garlic clove, lemon and ice water until smooth.
  2. Mix the Greek yoghurt with half a grated cucumber, garlic and salt for tzatziki.
  3. Lay both out with the kalamata olives, cherry tomatoes and the warm pita breads, 8 min.

Disruptors' hot sauce wings

Crisp baked wings tossed in homemade red chilli and garlic sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the hot sauce up to 3 days ahead in the fridge and mix the blue cheese dip up to 1 day ahead; bake the wings and toss on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 6 red chillies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 80ml white vinegar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 bunch celery
  • 150g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Toss the chicken wings with the baking powder and salt and bake at 220°C for 45 minutes, turning once.
  2. Blitz the red chillies with the garlic cloves, the vinegar and the honey, simmer 5 minutes and toss through the wings.
  3. Serve with celery and the blue cheese stirred into the sour cream.

Glass onion petals with whipped feta

Roasted onion petals, the centre in plain sight.

How it is made
  • 4 red onions
  • olive oil
  • 200g feta
  • 80g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 30g pistachios
  • dried oregano
  1. Red onions roasted until sweet, separated into glossy petals and filled with whipped feta, topped with honey, crushed pistachio and oregano. Heat the oven to 200°C. Halve 4 red onions through the root, keep the skins on, brush the cut sides with oil and roast cut side down for 30 minutes until soft. Cool, peel and separate into 20 to 24 petals. Blitz 200g feta with 80g Greek yoghurt, lemon zest and 1 tbsp oil until smooth. Spoon or pipe into each petal, drizzle with 2 tbsp honey and scatter with 30g crushed pistachios and dried oregano.

Mains

Puzzle box lamb souvlaki

Lemon and oregano lamb skewers in pita, quicker to solve than any puzzle box.

Prep 8 min, cook 16 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb leg
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 pita breads
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  1. Cut the lamb leg into cubes and marinate 2 hours with the juice of the lemon, the garlic cloves, the dried oregano and the oil.
  2. Thread onto skewers and grill 8 minutes, turning.
  3. Serve in the warm pitas with the Greek yoghurt mixed with grated cucumber, plus tomato and onion, 8 min.

Chargrilled octopus with lemon potatoes

Octopus chargrilled with lemon, oregano and roast potatoes, island lunch by the glass dome.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg cleaned octopus
  • bay leaves
  • 800g chat potatoes
  • 2 lemons
  • dried oregano
  • olive oil
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Octopus simmered until tender, charred hard on the grill and dressed in lemon, oregano and olive oil, with crispy lemon potatoes, capers and red onion. Simmer 1.2kg cleaned octopus in salted water with a bay leaf for 50 minutes until tender, then cool in the liquid. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss 800g halved chat potatoes with oil, the juice of 1 lemon and oregano and roast 40 minutes. Cut the octopus into legs, oil and char on a very hot grill 2 minutes a side. Toss with the potatoes, 1 tbsp capers, sliced red onion, lemon juice, olive oil and parsley.

Sweets

Duke's pineapple upside-down cakeVegetarian

Buttery sponge baked upside down on pineapple and cherries.

Prep 13 min, cook 68 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep covered at room temperature; warm gently and whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 185g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 440g tin pineapple rings
  • 8 glacé cherries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C, 8 min.
  2. Melt the butter with the brown sugar in a 23cm cake tin and lay the pineapple rings on top with a glacé cherry in the middle of each, 8 min.
  3. Beat the soft butter with the caster sugar, add the eggs and the vanilla, then fold in the self-raising flour and the milk.
  4. Spread over the fruit and bake 45 minutes.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, turn out and serve warm with whipped cream.

Drinks

Alpha poolside spiked kombucha

Hard kombucha for the pool: ginger, vodka, mint and lime.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Best poured just before serving to keep the fizz; juice the limes and pick the mint up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L ginger kombucha
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 120ml vodka
  1. Fill the tall glasses with ice, mint leaves and a squeeze of lime.
  2. Pour the cold ginger kombucha into each and add the vodka to the glasses of anyone who wants it spiked.
  3. Stir once and serve straight away.
