Snacks

Tour rider tropical fruit with chilli saltVegetarian

Mango, pineapple, watermelon and papaya wedges with chilli salt.

Prep 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Slice the fruit and chill the platter, covered, up to 4 hours ahead; make the chilli salt up to 1 week ahead and add the lime juice at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1/4 watermelon
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 3 limes
  1. Peel and slice the mangoes, pineapple, watermelon and papaya into long wedges and arrange on a big platter.
  2. Chill the platter, 30 min.
  3. Grind the salt with the chilli, sugar and lime zest.
  4. Just before serving, squeeze over lime juice and sprinkle with the chilli salt. Serve with lime wedges.

Party Wolf crispy chicken sliders

Buttermilk fried chicken tenders on small buns, wolf free.

Prep 8 min, cook 17 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; mix the sriracha mayo up to 2 days ahead and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chicken tenderloins
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 8 brioche slider buns
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with salt, 30 min.
  2. Toss the chicken in the flour mixed with paprika and garlic powder.
  3. Shallow fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C, turning once, until golden and cooked through, 12 min.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with the sriracha. Toss the cabbage with the vinegar.
  5. Toast the buns and fill with chicken, slaw, pickles and sriracha mayo, 5 min.

Mains

Donkey Roll fish tacos with mango salsa

Chilli lime grilled fish in tortillas with fresh mango salsa.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the mango salsa up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; blend the crema and grill the fish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g firm white fish fillets
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 limes
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 1 red chilli, diced
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 1 avocado
  • 100g sour cream
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1/4 red cabbage, shredded
  1. Rub the fish with the chilli powder, cumin, lime zest and oil.
  2. Grill the fish, turning once, then flake, 6 min.
  3. Toss the mango, onion, chilli, lime juice and coriander for the salsa.
  4. Blend the avocado with the sour cream and lime juice into a crema.
  5. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan, 4 min.
  6. Fill with cabbage, fish, salsa and crema.

Lawrence's Colorado farm steak with chimichurri

Chargrilled sirloin with chimichurri and smoky potatoes, from the farm in Colorado.

Prep 11 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the chimichurri up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g rump steak
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 120ml olive oil
  • 1kg small potatoes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  1. Chop the parsley, oregano, garlic and chilli and mix with the vinegar, 80ml of the oil and salt.
  2. Halve the potatoes, toss with 2 tbsp of the oil, the paprika and salt and roast at 220°C for 35 minutes.
  3. Rub the steak with the rest of the oil and salt and chargrill for 4 minutes a side, then rest for 8 minutes.
  4. Slice the tomatoes and onion thinly and season.
  5. Slice the steak, spoon over the chimichurri and serve with the potatoes and salad.

Platinum record vanilla bean cookiesVegetarian

Buttery vanilla bean shortbread, tinted grey and cut like records.

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Baked cookies up to 3 days, iced cookies up to 2 days in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 pack food colouring (black and one bright colour)
  1. Beat the butter with some of the icing sugar, then add the egg yolk, vanilla and flour to make a dough.
  2. Chill the dough, 30 min.
  3. Roll out thin and cut into rounds with an 8cm cutter.
  4. Bake at 170°C and cool completely, 15 min.
  5. Mix the remaining icing sugar with water and a drop of black colouring to make grey icing. Flood each cookie.
  6. Let the icing set, then pipe thin circles for grooves and a small coloured label in the centre, 15 min.

Drinks

Conner4Real cucumber gin spritz

Gin muddled with cucumber, mint and lime, topped with soda.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Muddle the cucumber, mint, lime juice and syrup and stir in the gin up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; strain over ice and top with sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 160ml gin
  • 500ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Muddle some cucumber slices with the mint, juice of two limes and sugar syrup in a jug. Add the gin and stir.
  2. Fill wine glasses with ice and the remaining cucumber.
  3. Strain the gin mix into the glasses and top with sparkling water. Garnish with mint and a lime wheel.
