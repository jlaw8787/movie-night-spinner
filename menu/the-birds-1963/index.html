Bodega Bay crab toasts

Crab with mayo, lemon and chives piled on garlic rubbed sourdough toasts

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the crab filling up to 1 day ahead, kept chilled; toast the sourdough and pile on at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 8 small slices sourdough
  • 1 garlic clove, cut in half
  1. Fold the crab with mayo, lemon juice, chives and cayenne.
  2. Toast the sourdough and rub with the cut garlic.
  3. Pile the crab on top and serve straight away.

Lovebird goat cheese filo cupsVegetarian

Crisp filo cups filled with whipped goat's cheese

Prep 22 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Cases up to 1 day ahead, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sheets filo pastry
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 120g goat's cheese, whipped
  • 100g frozen peas, blanched
  • 8 mint leaves
  1. Cut the filo into squares and brush with melted butter.
  2. Press in layers into a mini muffin tin.
  3. Bake at 180C for 8 minutes until crisp.
  4. Fill with whipped goat's cheese, peas and a mint leaf. Serve two to a plate.

Cathy's birthday chicken salad sandwichesOn the printed menu

Poached with mayo, celery, walnuts and grapes in white triangles, eaten before the dive-bombing

How it is made
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 40g walnuts
  • 150g green grapes
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 loaf soft white bread
  • 100g butter
  1. Poached chicken folded with mayo, celery, toasted walnuts, grapes and tarragon, in soft white bread cut into party triangles. Poach 2 chicken breasts in simmering salted water for 15 minutes, cool and shred finely. Mix with 5 tbsp mayonnaise, 2 finely diced celery sticks, 40g chopped toasted walnuts, a handful of halved green grapes, 1 tbsp chopped tarragon, a squeeze of lemon, salt and pepper. Butter 12 slices of soft white bread and fill 6 sandwiches. Trim the crusts and cut each into 4 triangles.

San Francisco cioppino

Seafood stew of mussels, clams and fish in a tomato and white wine broth

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Simmer the tomato, wine and stock base up to 2 days ahead, chilled; reheat and cook the seafood on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 fennel bulb, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml white wine
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 500g mussels
  • 500g clams
  • 300g green prawns
  • 300g snapper fillets, cut into chunks
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  1. Soften the onion, fennel and garlic in the oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes, wine and stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Add the mussels and clams, cover and cook until they open.
  4. Add the prawns and snapper and cook just until done. Serve with sourdough.

Lydia's roast chicken with sage stuffingOn the printed menu

Roast chicken with sage and onion stuffing, dinner at the Brenner farmhouse

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 brown onion
  • 80g butter
  • 150g sourdough loaf
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 1 bag plain flour
  1. Golden roast chicken filled with a sage, onion and bread stuffing, with roast potatoes, glazed carrots and a pan gravy made from the drippings. Heat the oven to 200C. Soften 1 diced onion in 40g butter, then mix with 150g fresh breadcrumbs, chopped sage, 1 egg, salt and pepper. Stuff a 1.8kg chicken, rub with butter and salt and roast with 1kg halved potatoes tossed in oil for 1 hour 20 minutes. Glaze 500g sliced carrots in a pan with butter, 1 tbsp honey and a splash of water. Rest the chicken 15 minutes, stir 1 tbsp flour and 250ml stock into the tray for gravy.

Meringue nests with lemon curdVegetarian

Crisp meringue nests filled with lemon curd and topped with speckled candy eggs

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Nests up to 2 days ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 1 jar lemon curd
  • 24 speckled chocolate eggs
  1. Whisk the egg whites and sugar over simmering water until hot, then beat to stiff peaks.
  2. Pipe small nests onto lined trays.
  3. Bake at 100C for 90 minutes, then cool.
  4. Fill with lemon curd and top each with three speckled eggs.

Irish coffeeVegetarian

Hot coffee with Irish whiskey and brown sugar, topped with floating cream

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh and hot; whip the cream lightly up to 4 hours ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g ground coffee
  • 1l water
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 120ml Irish whiskey
  • 200ml thickened cream, lightly whipped
  1. Brew the coffee with the water.
  2. For each glass stir sugar into hot coffee with whiskey.
  3. Pour the cream over the back of a spoon so it floats.
