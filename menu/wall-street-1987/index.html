Snacks

Steak tartare

Hand-chopped raw beef with shallot, capers, cornichons and egg yolk

Prep 18 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep well chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g eye fillet, well chilled
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 4 cornichons
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 4 slices brioche
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Chop the chilled eye fillet very finely by hand with a sharp knife.
  2. Mix with the diced shallot, capers, chopped cornichons, Dijon, Worcestershire and egg yolk. Season well.
  3. Toast the brioche slices and serve alongside.

Gekko's oysters on ice

Fresh oysters on crushed ice, red wine vinegar and shallot dressing, lemon

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the shallot dressing up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shuck the oysters just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 oysters, freshly shucked
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 lemon
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Shuck the oysters (or ask the fishmonger to) and lay them on crushed ice.
  2. Mix the vinegar with the finely diced shallot and cracked pepper.
  3. Cut the lemon into wedges and serve with the dressing on the side.

Raider's rib eye

Butter-basted rib eye with a flamed brandy and cracked peppercorn cream sauce

Prep 11 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Dry-brine the steaks the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 1 tbsp flaky salt, for dry-brining
  • 60g butter
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1 tbsp black peppercorns
  • 60ml brandy
  • 125ml beef stock
  • 200ml cream
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  1. The night before, salt the rib eyes and leave uncovered in the fridge to dry-brine. Bring to room temperature 30 minutes before cooking.
  2. Sear the steaks hard in the neutral oil in a very hot pan on both sides.
  3. Add the butter and rosemary and baste the steaks. Rest on a plate for 8 minutes.
  4. In the same pan, crush in the peppercorns, add the brandy and flame it carefully.
  5. Add the stock and cream and reduce until the sauce coats a spoon.
  6. Pour the sauce over the rested steaks.

Gekko's birthday cheesecakeVegetarian

Lemon cheesecake. Bud brings a box of Cuban cigars for Gekko's birthday.

Prep 10 min, cook 80 min, then 6 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, chill overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 750g cream cheese, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 300g sour cream
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C. Mix the crushed biscuits with the melted butter and press into a lined springform tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, eggs and lemon zest until smooth, then pour over the base.
  3. Bake in a water bath until just set with a slight wobble.
  4. Spread the sour cream on top and bake a little longer.
  5. Cool, then chill overnight before slicing. Chilling counts as part of the cooking time.

Teldar speech old fashionedVegetarian

Bourbon and bitters over one big cube. If you need a friend, get a dog.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 2 weeks ahead in the fridge; stir each drink over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 4 large ice cubes
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tray ice, for stirring
  1. Stir the bourbon, sugar syrup and bitters over ice, in batches of one drink at a time, for about 30 seconds each.
  2. Strain over one big ice cube in a short glass and twist a strip of orange peel over the top.

Serve dinner when Bud finally gets his five minutes in Gekko's office.
