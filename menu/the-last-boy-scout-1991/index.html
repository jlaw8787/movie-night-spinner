Snacks

Halftime phone call chilli dogs

Beef frank, slow chilli, cheddar, onion.

Prep 10 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and garlic, 10 min.
  2. Add the chilli powder, cumin and tomatoes and simmer until thick, 30 min.
  3. Grill the franks until blistered, 8 min.
  4. Fill the toasted rolls and top with chilli, cheddar and raw onion.

Game-ball soft pretzels with beer cheeseVegetarian

Chewy salted pretzels, sharp cheddar dip.

Prep 1 hr, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the dough the night before and prove it in the fridge; boil, bake and make the dip to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g sachet dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 300ml warm water
  • 3 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour, extra
  • 185ml lager
  • 200g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp American mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, a teaspoon of salt and the water and knead until smooth, then leave to double, 50 min.
  2. Roll into 8 ropes and twist into pretzels.
  3. Boil each for 30 seconds in 2 litres of water with the bicarbonate of soda, then drain.
  4. Brush with egg, sprinkle with salt and bake at 220°C until deep brown, 14 min.
  5. Melt the butter, stir in the extra flour, whisk in the lager, then melt in the cheddar and mustard, 8 min.

Marcone's six-million-dollar brisket rolls

Smoky brisket, pickled onion, slaw, soft roll.

Prep 21 min, cook 4 hr 7 min · Make ahead: Cook the brisket up to 2 days ahead, chill it in its juices and slice cold; reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef brisket
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 125ml apple cider vinegar
  • 1/4 green cabbage, finely shredded
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 6 soft white rolls
  1. Rub the brisket with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder, pepper and 2 teaspoons of salt.
  2. Sit it in a roasting dish with the stock, cover tightly and cook at 150°C until it pulls apart, 4 hr.
  3. Meanwhile cover the onion with the vinegar and a pinch of sugar and salt to pickle, and toss the cabbage with the mayonnaise.
  4. Slice or pull the brisket, toss with the barbecue sauce and some pan juices.
  5. Pile into the rolls with slaw and pickled onion.

Sweets

Leave-the-ice-alone shaved iceVegetarian

Shaved ice with cherry and lime syrups and condensed milk.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the syrups up to a week ahead and keep them in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen pitted cherries
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 limes
  • 6 cups ice
  • 4 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  1. Simmer the cherries with half the sugar and 100ml water until syrupy, then strain and cool, 8 min.
  2. Simmer the rest of the sugar with the lime zest and juice and 100ml water for 2 minutes, then cool.
  3. Shave or blitz the ice to snow in a strong blender.
  4. Pile into cups, pour over both syrups and drizzle with condensed milk.

Joe's whiskey, with ice

Bourbon over ice with a twist of orange, held tight.

Prep 2 min · Make ahead: Freeze big ice cubes the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 8 large ice cubes
  • 1 orange
  1. Put two big cubes in each of 4 tumblers and pour 60ml bourbon over.
  2. Twist a strip of orange peel over each and drop it in.
