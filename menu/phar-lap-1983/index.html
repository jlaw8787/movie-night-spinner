Snacks

Flemington Cup day beef party pies

Mini beef pies with puff lids, served with tomato sauce.

Prep 24 min, cook 50 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle of tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Dice the onions.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock, tomato sauce, Worcestershire and plenty of pepper and simmer until thick.
  4. Cool the filling.
  5. Cut bases from the shortcrust pastry and press into a greased patty pan tin.
  6. Fill, top with rounds of puff pastry and brush with egg.
  7. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  8. Serve with tomato sauce.

Strapper's apple and cheddar on toastVegetarian

Toasted baguette with chutney, vintage cheddar and red apple.

Prep 16 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 red apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar of fruit chutney
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 1 bunch of thyme
  1. Slice the baguette into rounds and brush with oil.
  2. Bake at 190°C until crisp.
  3. Thinly slice the apples and toss in lemon juice.
  4. Spread each toast with a little fruit chutney, top with cheddar and a slice of apple.
  5. Finish with thyme leaves and cracked pepper.

Big Red's Cup day roast beef rolls

Cold roast beef, caramelised onion, rocket, beetroot and horseradish cream.

Prep 21 min, cook 48 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Beef up to 1 day ahead, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef rump
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 onions
  • 20g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 60g watercress
  • 1 jar of sliced beetroot
  1. Rub the beef with oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Sear all over.
  3. Roast at 200°C for rare.
  4. Rest, then chill before slicing thinly.
  5. Slice the onions.
  6. Slowly fry the onions in butter.
  7. Mix the sour cream with horseradish cream.
  8. Split the rolls, spread with horseradish cream and fill with beef, onions, rocket and sliced pickled beetroot.

Stud farm hideout chicken and potato salad

Cold roast chook and egg and chive potato salad, on the rug at Flemington.

Prep 14 min, cook 83 min · Make ahead: Roast the chicken and make the potato salad 1 day ahead; both are served cold
Recipe for 4
  • 1.6kg whole chicken
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg waxy potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Rub the chicken with oil and salt, put the halved lemon inside and roast at 200°C until the juices run clear.
  2. Boil the potatoes until tender with the eggs in for the first 10 minutes. Drain and cool.
  3. Peel and chop the eggs and fold through the potatoes with the mayonnaise, mustard and chopped chives.
  4. Chill the chicken, then cut into pieces and pack with the potato salad.

Cup day passionfruit spongeVegetarian

Light sponge with whipped cream and passionfruit icing. Cut at the Cup day picnic.

Prep 10 min, cook 32 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the sponges a day ahead and keep airtight; fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 passionfruit
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Beat the eggs and sugar until thick and pale.
  2. Sift in and fold the flour, then bake in two lined 20cm tins.
  3. Cool on a rack.
  4. Whip the cream and sandwich the sponges, then ice with the icing sugar mixed with passionfruit pulp.

Agua Caliente tequila and grapefruitVegetarian

Tequila, pink grapefruit and lime with soda, raised to the Agua Caliente win.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 360ml pink grapefruit juice
  • 3 limes
  • 240ml soda water
  • 1 tbsp sea salt
  • 1 grapefruit
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Rub a lime wedge around the rims of tall glasses and dip in sea salt.
  2. Fill the glasses with ice.
  3. Add tequila, grapefruit juice and lime juice to each glass.
  4. Top with soda water and stir gently.
  5. Garnish with a grapefruit wedge.
