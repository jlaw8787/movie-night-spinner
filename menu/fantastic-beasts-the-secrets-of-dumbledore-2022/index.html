Snacks

Minister Vogel's pretzelsVegetarian

Soft pretzels dipped in bicarb water and topped with rock salt, for the German delegation.

Prep 28 min, cook 20 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Rest the dough for its hour up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; shape, dip and bake on the day, best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp rock salt
  • 100g butter
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and warm water and knead into a dough, 12 min.
  2. Rest the dough, 1 hr.
  3. Shape into pretzels.
  4. Dip in boiling water with bicarb soda and sprinkle with rock salt.
  5. Bake at 220°C, 12 min.
  6. Serve with butter and mustard.

Chicken momo dumplings

Steamed chicken and ginger dumplings with a charred tomato chilli sauce.

Prep 23 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat the momo up to 1 day ahead on a floured tray in the fridge; steam just before serving and make the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken mince
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 packet dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the mince with sliced spring onion and ginger.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam, 10 min.
  4. Char the tomatoes in a dry pan and blitz with chilli flakes and salt for dipping, 8 min.

British delegation sausage rolls

Pork, onion and sage rolled in puff and baked golden, with tangy brown sauce.

How it is made
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle brown sauce
  1. Sausage rolls of pork, onion and sage in puff pastry, baked at 200°C for 25 minutes, with brown sauce. Mix 500g pork mince with 1 grated onion, 1 tbsp chopped sage, 40g breadcrumbs, 1 tsp salt and plenty of pepper. Halve 2 sheets puff pastry lengthways. Lay a log of the filling down each strip, roll up and seal the seam with beaten egg. Cut each log into 4, brush with egg and score the tops. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve hot with brown sauce.

Mains

Crumbed pork schnitzel

Crumbed pork loin fried in butter, warm potato salad, served once the votes are in.

Prep 1 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Crumb the pork up to 4 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge; fry the schnitzel just before serving and toss the potatoes while warm
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork loin steaks
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 100g butter
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  • salt, to taste
  1. Pound the pork thin and crumb in flour, beaten egg and panko.
  2. Boil the potatoes, then slice and toss warm with vinegar, oil and salt, 3 min.
  3. Boil, 20 min.
  4. Fry the schnitzel in butter until golden on both sides, 12 min.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.

Jacob's potato pierogi

Dough parcels of mashed potato and cheddar, boiled then pan-fried with onions.

How it is made
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 300g sour cream
  • 500g potatoes
  • 100g cheddar
  • 3 onions
  • 80g butter
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Polish-style pierogi of potato and cheese, boiled then fried in butter with onions, with sour cream. Mix 300g plain flour, 1 egg, 125ml warm water, 2 tbsp sour cream and a pinch of salt into a soft dough, knead 3 minutes and rest 30 minutes. Mash 500g boiled potatoes with 100g grated cheddar and 1 onion fried in butter. Roll the dough thin, cut 7cm rounds, fill and pinch closed. Boil in batches until they float, then 2 minutes more. Fry 2 sliced onions in 60g butter until golden, add the pierogi to crisp and serve with sour cream and dill.

Sweets

Three-layer victory spongeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers with buttercream and raspberry jam, iced and cut for the winner.

Prep 23 min, cook 40 min, 40 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 1 jar raspberry jam
  • 200g butter, softened, for buttercream
  • 400g icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and prepare three tins. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs and fold in the flour, 15 min.
  2. Bake in three tins, 25 min.
  3. Cool completely, 40 min.
  4. Beat the icing sugar into the softened butter for the buttercream.
  5. Stack the layers with buttercream and jam and ice the whole cake white.

Drinks

Raspberry sparklerNo alcohol

Fresh raspberry puree in a flute, topped up with sparkling apple juice.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Crush and strain the raspberries with the sugar and lemon juice up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; top with cold sparkling apple juice at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 750ml sparkling apple juice
  1. Crush most of the raspberries, keeping four whole, with sugar and lemon juice, then push through a sieve.
  2. Spoon puree into each of four flutes.
  3. Slowly top with cold sparkling apple juice, letting the foam settle.
  4. Drop a whole raspberry into each glass.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Jacob and Lally portkey in to join Newt.
