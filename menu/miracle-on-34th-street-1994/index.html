Dorey's New York Waldorf saladVegetarian

Apple, celery, grapes and toasted walnuts in lemon mayo on crisp cos leaves.

Prep 14 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Toast the walnuts and mix the dressing up to 2 days ahead; dice the apple and dress the salad up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 crisp red apples
  • 4 celery stalks
  • 150g red grapes
  • 80g walnuts
  • 100g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 baby cos lettuce
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan and chop roughly.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, yoghurt, juice of half the lemon, salt and pepper.
  3. Dice the apples and toss in the rest of the lemon juice. Slice the celery and halve the grapes.
  4. Fold everything through the dressing with most of the walnuts.
  5. Pile onto cos leaves and finish with the last walnuts and snipped chives.

Kris Kringle's Thanksgiving turkey

Herb butter roast turkey breast with cornbread stuffing.

Prep 8 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Make the herb butter and the cranberry sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep them chilled; roast the turkey and make the gravy on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 120g butter, soft (60g mixed with herbs)
  • 1 small bunch sage, chopped
  • 300g cornbread
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 1 egg
  • 450ml chicken stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 150g cranberry sauce
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  1. Mash some butter with sage and push it under the skin of the turkey.
  2. Roast at 180°C.
  3. Fry the onion and celery, crumble in the cornbread and mix with sage, egg and some stock. Bake in a dish for the last part of the roasting.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter and milk.
  5. Simmer the cranberry sauce with the orange zest.
  6. Rest the turkey and make gravy from the juices with the flour and remaining stock.

In God We Trust gingerbread cookiesVegetarian

Ginger and cinnamon gingerbread with golden syrup.

Prep 22 min, cook 28 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 bottle red food colouring
  1. Melt the butter with the golden syrup and sugar. Mix in the flour, ground ginger, ground cinnamon and egg to a dough.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll out thin and cut shapes.
  4. Bake at 180°C, then cool.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with water, tint a third red and pipe on outlines and details.

New York pumpkin pie with maple creamVegetarian

Pumpkin pie in a crisp crust, served with maple cream.

Prep 8 min, cook 92 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 600g butternut pumpkin, peeled
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 50g pecans, toasted
  1. Line a 23cm pie dish with the pastry and bake blind at 190°C.
  2. Steam the pumpkin until soft, drain well and mash smooth.
  3. Beat the pumpkin with the sugar, eggs, some of the cream, the cinnamon, ginger and nutmeg. Pour into the case.
  4. Bake at 170°C until just set.
  5. Cool the pie.
  6. Whip the remaining cream with the maple syrup and serve with toasted pecans.

Hot spiced ciderNo alcohol

Hot apple cider with maple, cinnamon, clove and orange.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Steep the spices in the juice up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled, then reheat gently; strain and serve hot
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2 L cloudy apple juice
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 8 cinnamon sticks (4 to simmer, 4 to serve)
  • 6 cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 orange, sliced
  1. Pour the apple juice into a saucepan with the maple syrup, half the cinnamon, cloves, star anise and orange.
  2. Heat gently without boiling so the spices infuse.
  3. Strain into mugs and add a cinnamon stick and an orange slice to each.
