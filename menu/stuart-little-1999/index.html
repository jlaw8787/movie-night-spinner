Snacks

That's-a-lot-of-Littles pigs in blankets

Cocktail sausages rolled in puff pastry and baked golden, for the whole Little family.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Wrap up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cocktail sausages
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar American mustard
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and cut the pastry into 24 strips.
  2. Wrap each sausage in a strip, seam down on a lined tray, and brush with egg.
  3. Bake until puffed and golden.

Cereal box cornflake chicken tenders

Cornflake-crumbed chicken tenders with paprika. Anton mocks George's boat.

Prep 18 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to a day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken tenderloins
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g cornflakes, lightly crushed
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Season the flour, beat the eggs and mix the cornflakes with the paprika.
  2. Dip each tender in flour, egg, then cornflakes, and lay on a lined tray.
  3. Drizzle with oil and bake at 220°C, turning once, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Stir the mayonnaise, honey and mustard together and serve as a dip.

Mains

Eleanor's Cajun meatloaf with mash and peas

Cajun meatloaf with mash and peas. Frederick says it's delicious.

Prep 15 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Shape the meatloaf up to a day ahead and chill; bake and mash to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 1 red capsicum, finely diced
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml milk, plus a splash for the mash
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  1. Mix the mince, onion, capsicum, Cajun seasoning, breadcrumbs, egg, milk and salt with your hands.
  2. Shape into a loaf on a lined tray and brush with the tomato sauce mixed with the sugar.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 1 hour, then rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile boil the potatoes and mash with the butter and a splash of milk.
  5. Cook the peas, slice the meatloaf and serve with the mash.

Snowbell's not-on-the-menu fish pie

Buttery mash over a creamy fish pie. Snowbell: we do not eat family members.

Prep 0 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Assemble the pie up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g firm white fish fillets, in chunks
  • 200g raw prawns, peeled
  • 600ml milk
  • 50g butter, plus 40g for the mash
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 60g cheddar, grated
  1. Boil the potatoes and mash with the 40g butter and a splash of the milk.
  2. Melt the 50g butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the rest of the milk and simmer until thick.
  3. Stir in the fish, prawns, peas and parsley, season and tip into a baking dish.
  4. Spread the mash over, scatter with cheese and bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling.

Ten inches tall chocolate layer cakeVegetarian

Tall chocolate layer cake with fudge icing, for Stuart feeling ten inches tall.

Prep 18 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Bake the layers up to 2 days ahead and wrap well; frost on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 225g plain flour
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 60g cocoa powder, plus 30g for the frosting
  • 1 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 125ml hot water
  • 150g soft butter
  • 250g icing sugar
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, 60g cocoa, bicarb and a pinch of salt, then whisk in the eggs, buttermilk, oil and hot water.
  2. Divide between two lined 20cm tins and bake at 175°C until a skewer comes out clean.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and 30g cocoa until fluffy, then sandwich and cover the cake.

Boat race raspberry lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh raspberry lemonade over ice, for Stuart winning George's boat race.

Prep 3 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the raspberry syrup up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g frozen raspberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 750ml soda water, chilled
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Simmer the raspberries, sugar and water for 5 minutes, then sieve and cool.
  2. Stir in the lemon juice, pour over ice and top with soda.
