Snacks

Za'atar flatbreads with hummusVegetarian

Charred za'atar flatbread, hummus with pomegranate. Day one on Earth, 5000 BC

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Seed the pomegranate up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; brush and grill the flatbreads just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp za'atar
  • 300g hummus
  • 1/2 pomegranate, seeds only
  1. Brush the flatbreads with olive oil and sprinkle generously with za'atar.
  2. Grill over high heat until blistered and charred in spots.
  3. Spoon hummus into a bowl, make a well, scatter with pomegranate seeds and drizzle with oil.
  4. Tear the bread and dip.

Sersi's museum tea sandwichesVegetarian

Cucumber, egg and cress, crusts off. Packed for Dane at the Natural History Museum

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, covered with damp paper towel
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf of white bread
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 punnet mustard cress
  1. Hard boil the eggs, cool, peel and mash with mayonnaise.
  2. Spread bread with cream cheese and layer with cucumber.
  3. Spread more bread with egg mayonnaise and cress.
  4. Close the sandwiches, cut off the crusts and slice into neat fingers.

Kingo's film set samosas with mint chutneyOn the printed menu

Potato and pea samosas with mint chutney, from Kingo's career as a Bollywood star

How it is made
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 jar cumin seeds
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar garam masala
  • 1 jar ground turmeric
  • 2 green chillies
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 packet spring roll wrappers
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 150g natural yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  1. Crisp pastry triangles stuffed with spiced potato, peas and garam masala, fried golden and served with a bright mint and coriander chutney. Boil 600g diced potatoes until tender. Fry 1 tsp cumin seeds and 1 diced onion, add 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 chopped green chilli, the potatoes and 100g peas, mash roughly and cool. Cut 6 spring roll wrappers into strips, fill and fold into triangles, sealing with flour paste. Fry at 180C for 4 minutes. Blend mint, coriander, 1 green chilli, 150g yoghurt and lemon juice for the chutney.

Mains

Chicken mole with rice and tortillas

Chilli chocolate chicken at Druig's, before the Deviants arrive

Prep 15 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 6 dried chillies
  • 100g almonds
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 50g dark chocolate
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 8 corn tortillas
  1. Toast the chillies and soak in hot water.
  2. Blend with the almonds, garlic, cinnamon and stock.
  3. Simmer the sauce and melt in the chocolate.
  4. Add the chicken and simmer until tender.
  5. Cook the rice, warm the tortillas and serve with the chicken.

Phastos's Chicago Italian beef rollsOn the printed menu

Rare roast sliced thin, dunked in oregano juices, spicy pickled veg, for a quiet family man

How it is made
  • 1kg beef topside
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 4 soft long rolls
  • 1 jar giardiniera pickles
  1. Rare roast beef sliced paper thin, dunked in garlic and oregano beef juices, piled into soft rolls with spicy giardiniera pickles. Rub a 1kg beef topside with garlic, oregano, pepper and salt and roast at 220C for 15 minutes, then at 180C for 25 minutes until rare. Rest, chill for 1 hour and slice paper thin. Simmer 750ml beef stock with 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp oregano and 1 tsp chilli flakes for 10 minutes. Drop the beef into the hot stock for 1 minute, then pile into 4 soft long rolls with giardiniera and a spoon of juice.

Babylon date and sesame halvaVegetarian

Tahini and honey halva, sliced. Enough to put Tiamut back to sleep

Prep 12 min, cook 18 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g tahini
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g honey
  • 100g medjool dates, chopped
  • 40g pistachios
  1. Line a small loaf tin with baking paper and put the tahini in a bowl with the salt and vanilla.
  2. Heat the honey to 120C on a thermometer.
  3. Quickly pour onto the tahini and stir until it thickens and pulls away.
  4. Fold in the dates and pistachios and press into the tin.
  5. Chill overnight before cutting.

Drinks

Babylon date and honey sharbatVegetarianNo alcohol

Chilled date, honey and lemon drink with orange blossom and cardamom

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pitted dates
  • 300ml hot water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1l soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Soak the dates in hot water.
  2. Blend with the honey, lemon juice, orange blossom water and cardamom until smooth.
  3. Strain through a fine sieve and chill.
  4. Divide between glasses of ice, top with soda water and stir, and garnish with mint and a lemon slice.
