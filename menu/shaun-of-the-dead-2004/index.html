Snacks

Winchester pub nutsVegetarian

Smoky paprika peanuts. Queen on the jukebox

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight; cool completely before storing
Recipe for 4
  • 1 egg white
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300g peanuts
  1. Whisk the egg white until frothy and stir in the paprika, salt and sugar.
  2. Toss through the peanuts and spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 160°C, stirring once.
  4. Cool and serve in small bowls.

Corner shop crispsVegetarian

Thin homemade crisps with salt and malt vinegar.

Prep 23 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Fry up to 4 hours ahead and keep uncovered
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 tbsp malt vinegar
  1. Slice the potatoes paper thin on a mandoline, rinse well in cold water and dry completely on tea towels.
  2. Heat the oil to 170°C and fry the potato slices in small batches for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and crisp.
  3. Drain on paper towel, sprinkle with the sea salt flakes and splash with the malt vinegar.

Mains

Pub pie and chips

Beef and ale pie with a puff pastry lid, chips and crushed peas

Prep 20 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Braise the beef filling up to 2 days ahead and chill it; cover with pastry and bake on the day, and fry the chips to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 330ml English ale
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil
  1. Brown the beef in batches, then soften the onions.
  2. Stir in the flour first, then add the ale and stock, cover and braise at 160°C.
  3. Cool slightly, tip into a pie dish and cover with puff pastry.
  4. Bake at 200°C.
  5. Cut the potatoes into chips and fry them twice in the vegetable oil.
  6. Cook and crush the peas.

Full English fry-upOn the printed menu

Bacon, sausages, eggs, beans and mushrooms, a lazy start before anyone says you've got red on you

How it is made
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 slices black pudding
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 50g butter
  • 2 x 420g tins baked beans
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices thick white bread
  1. Bacon, pork sausages, fried eggs, baked beans, grilled tomato, buttery mushrooms, black pudding and toast, all on one big plate. Bake 8 pork sausages at 200°C for 25 minutes. Fry 8 rashers bacon and 4 slices black pudding until crisp and keep warm. Halve 4 tomatoes and grill cut side up for 8 minutes. Fry 300g sliced mushrooms in 30g butter until golden. Warm 2 x 420g tins baked beans. Fry 4 eggs in the bacon fat until the whites set. Toast and butter 4 thick slices of bread and plate everything together.

Sticky toffee puddingVegetarian

Date sponge with warm toffee sauce and cream

Prep 15 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Bake the pudding up to 2 days ahead, covered, and reheat for 15 minutes at 160°C; make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dates, chopped
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 180g butter
  • 300g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 180g self-raising flour
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Soak the dates in the boiling water with the bicarb.
  2. Beat some of the butter with half the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and dates.
  3. Bake in a greased dish at 180°C.
  4. Simmer the remaining butter, sugar and most of the cream.
  5. Pour the sauce over and serve with the remaining cream.

Cornetto-style strawberry ripple conesOn the printed menu

Waffle cones dipped in chocolate and nuts, vanilla swirled with berry sauce, for the morning after

How it is made
  • 250g strawberries
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 4 waffle cones
  • 40g roasted hazelnuts
  • 1L vanilla ice cream
  1. Waffle cones dipped in chocolate and crushed nuts, filled with vanilla ice cream rippled with fresh strawberry sauce, a chocolate plug at the bottom. Simmer 250g strawberries with 60g caster sugar and a squeeze of lemon for 8 minutes, then cool. Melt 100g dark chocolate. Drop a spoon of chocolate into each of 4 waffle cones, then dip the rims in chocolate and in 40g crushed roasted hazelnuts. Set in the freezer 10 minutes. Soften 1L vanilla ice cream, ripple through the sauce and pack into the cones. Freeze 30 minutes before serving.

Drinks

Pint at the WinchesterVegetarian

A cold pint of lager. Have a nice cold pint, and wait for all of this to blow over.

Prep 5 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the cans or bottles for at least 3 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1l English ale
  • 1l lager
  1. Chill the English ale and the lager in the fridge for at least 3 hours.
  2. Pour the ale, or the lager for anyone who prefers it, into pint glasses at a tilt so the head settles, and raise them to the Winchester.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when they make it to the Winchester.
