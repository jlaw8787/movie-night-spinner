Snacks

Clarksdale '32 juke joint hush puppiesVegetarian

Onion cornmeal fritters fried golden with honey butter.

Prep 0 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the dry ingredients and grate the onion up to 1 day ahead, and the hot sauce butter keeps 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch caster sugar
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, sugar, onion, egg and buttermilk and rest.
  2. Heat the oil to 175°C.
  3. Spoon into the oil and fry until golden.
  4. Melt the butter with the hot sauce to dip.

Prove-you're-human garlic toastVegetarian

Thick white bread spread with roast garlic butter and toasted.

Prep 10 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Roast the garlic and mash the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead, chilled; spread and toast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 garlic head
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 8 slices white bread, thick
  1. Roast the garlic head in foil at 180°C.
  2. Squeeze the garlic into the butter with parsley and salt and mash.
  3. Spread on the bread.
  4. Toast at 200°C until crisp at the edges.

Mains

Annie's smothered pork chops

Browned pork chops simmered in an onion roux gravy.

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Simmer the chops in the gravy up to 2 days ahead and chill; reheat gently and cook the rice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork chops, thick
  • 1/2 cup plain flour, for dusting the chops
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour, for the roux
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Season, flour and brown the chops.
  2. Make a dark roux with the butter and flour.
  3. Add the onions, then the stock and thyme.
  4. Simmer the chops in the gravy.
  5. Cook the rice and serve the chops over it.

Delta red beans and rice with sausage

Kidney beans simmered slow with smoked links, ham hock, capsicum, celery and Cajun spice.

How it is made
  • 400g smoked sausage
  • 1 onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar Cajun seasoning
  • 2 x 400g tins kidney beans
  • 1 small smoked ham hock
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  1. Red kidney beans simmered slow with smoked sausage, ham hock, capsicum, celery and Cajun spice, ladled over fluffy white rice with spring onion and hot sauce. Brown 400g sliced smoked sausage in a large pot. Add 1 onion, 1 green capsicum and 2 celery sticks, diced, and 3 garlic cloves and cook 8 minutes. Add 2 tsp Cajun seasoning, 2 x 400g tins drained kidney beans, 1 small smoked ham hock and 1L chicken stock. Simmer 75 minutes, mashing some beans to thicken. Shred the ham back in. Serve over 300g cooked long grain rice with spring onion and hot sauce.

Sweet potato pieVegetarian

Mashed roast sweet potato with brown sugar, nutmeg and vanilla in a short crust.

Prep 10 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g sweet potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  1. Roast the sweet potatoes until soft.
  2. Peel and mash with butter, sugar, eggs, evaporated milk, nutmeg and vanilla.
  3. Line a pie dish and blind-bake the case.
  4. Pour in the filling and bake at 170°C.

Stack's Chicago Irish stout

Irish stout poured slowly in two stages for a tight head.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the cans in the fridge for at least 2 hours, or up to 2 days ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 8 440 ml cans stout
  1. Tilt each glass and pour the stout three-quarters full.
  2. Let it settle for a minute, then top up slowly for a tight head. Allow 2 cans per person.
