Snacks

Black and white ball canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche and black roe, white chocolate dipped strawberries.

Prep 12 min, cook 12 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Dip the strawberries up to 4 hours ahead and keep them chilled; top the blinis with crème fraîche and roe just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 50g black lumpfish roe
  • 250g strawberries
  • 150g white chocolate
  1. Melt the white chocolate gently, 4 min.
  2. Dip the strawberries halfway in the chocolate and set on baking paper, 8 min.
  3. Leave the chocolate to set, 15 min.
  4. Top the blinis with crème fraîche and a little black roe.
  5. Lay both out on black trays in strict rows, black and white only.

Punk London pub chipsVegetarian

Thick twice fried chips shaken with malt vinegar and salt.

Prep 17 min, cook 16 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Do the first fry up to 2 hours ahead and rest the chips; fry again at 190°C just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100ml malt vinegar
  • 2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips, rinse and dry.
  2. Heat the oil to 140°C and fry until soft, 10 min.
  3. Rest the chips, 10 min.
  4. Reheat the oil to 190°C and fry until crisp, 6 min.
  5. Tip into paper and shake over plenty of malt vinegar and salt.

Mains

Beef Wellington

Beef fillet wrapped in mushrooms, prosciutto and pastry.

Prep 24 min, cook 53 min, 50 min waiting · Make ahead: Wrap up to 1 day ahead, brush with egg and bake when needed
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 400g mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Sear the beef all over in a hot pan with the oil, 8 min.
  2. Brush with Dijon and cool.
  3. Chop the mushrooms and cook until completely dry, 15 min.
  4. Cool the mushrooms, 10 min.
  5. Lay prosciutto on cling wrap, spread with the mushrooms and roll around the beef.
  6. Chill the roll, 30 min.
  7. Wrap in puff pastry and brush with egg wash.
  8. Bake at 200°C, 30 min.
  9. Rest before slicing, 10 min.

Jasper and Horace's full English fry-up

Pork sausages, bacon, fried eggs, mushrooms, grilled tomato, beans and toast, for the hideout crew.

How it is made
  • 8 thick pork sausages
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  • 2 cans baked beans
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 loaf thick white bread
  • 1 bottle brown sauce
  1. Thick pork sausages, crisp bacon, fried eggs, mushrooms, grilled tomato and baked beans with buttered toast, a hideout fry-up on one plate. Fry 8 thick pork sausages over medium heat for 12 minutes, turning, until browned. Fry 8 bacon rashers alongside until crisp. Halve 4 tomatoes and fry cut side down with 200g sliced mushrooms in butter for 5 minutes. Warm 2 cans baked beans. Fry 4 eggs in the pan fat. Toast and butter 4 thick slices of bread and load everything onto 4 plates with brown sauce.

Black and white cakeVegetarian

Cocoa sponge layers filled with white chocolate whipped cream.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min, 1 hr 33 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 40g cocoa
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 600ml thickened cream
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Bake in 2 tins at 175°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool the cakes completely, 30 min.
  4. Melt the white chocolate and cool, 14 min.
  5. Whip the cream with the melted chocolate.
  6. Split each layer and fill with the cream.
  7. Chill, then slice to show the stripes, 1 hr 3 min.

Drinks

Red dress hibiscus raspberry fizzNo alcohol

Hibiscus tea and raspberry syrup topped with bubbles.

Prep 12 min, cook 15 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp dried hibiscus flowers
  • 300ml boiling water
  • 250g raspberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 lime
  • 800ml sparkling water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Steep the hibiscus in the boiling water, then strain, 10 min.
  2. Simmer with half the raspberries and the sugar, 5 min.
  3. Mash and strain again.
  4. Chill the syrup, 30 min.
  5. Fill glasses with ice, add syrup and a little lime juice to each.
  6. Top with sparkling water and garnish with raspberries.
