Snacks

Black and white ball canapés

Blinis with crème fraîche and black roe, white chocolate dipped strawberries

Prep 24 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Dip the strawberries up to 4 hours ahead and keep them chilled; top the blinis with crème fraîche and roe just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 50g black lumpfish roe
  • 250g strawberries
  • 150g white chocolate
  1. Melt the white chocolate gently.
  2. Dip the strawberries halfway in the chocolate and set on baking paper.
  3. Leave the chocolate to set.
  4. Top the blinis with crème fraîche and a little black roe.
  5. Lay both out on black trays in strict rows, black and white only.

Punk London pub chipsVegetarian

Thick twice fried chips shaken with malt vinegar and salt

Prep 17 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Do the first fry up to 2 hours ahead and rest the chips; fry again at 190C just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100ml malt vinegar
  • 2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips, rinse and dry.
  2. Heat the oil to 140C and fry until soft.
  3. Rest the chips.
  4. Reheat the oil to 190C and fry until crisp.
  5. Tip into paper and shake over plenty of malt vinegar and salt.

Mains

The Baroness's beef Wellington

Beef fillet wrapped in mushrooms, prosciutto and pastry

Prep 45 min, cook 82 min · Make ahead: Wrap up to 1 day ahead, brush with egg and bake when needed
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 400g mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Sear the beef all over in a hot pan with the oil.
  2. Brush with Dijon and cool.
  3. Chop the mushrooms and cook until completely dry.
  4. Cool the mushrooms.
  5. Lay prosciutto on cling wrap, spread with the mushrooms and roll around the beef.
  6. Chill the roll.
  7. Wrap in puff pastry and brush with egg wash.
  8. Bake at 200C.
  9. Rest before slicing.

Sweets

Black and white cakeVegetarian

Cocoa sponge layers filled with white chocolate whipped cream

Prep 38 min, cook 130 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 40g cocoa
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 600ml thickened cream
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Bake in 2 tins at 175C.
  3. Cool the cakes completely.
  4. Melt the white chocolate and cool.
  5. Whip the cream with the melted chocolate.
  6. Split each layer and fill with the cream.
  7. Chill, then slice to show the stripes.

Drinks

Red dress hibiscus raspberry fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Hibiscus tea and raspberry syrup topped with bubbles

Prep 12 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp dried hibiscus flowers
  • 300ml boiling water
  • 250g raspberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 lime
  • 800ml sparkling water
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Steep the hibiscus in the boiling water, then strain.
  2. Simmer with half the raspberries and the sugar.
  3. Mash and strain again.
  4. Chill the syrup.
  5. Fill glasses with ice, add syrup and a little lime juice to each.
  6. Top with sparkling water and garnish with raspberries.
