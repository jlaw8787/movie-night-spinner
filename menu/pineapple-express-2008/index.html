Charred pineapple salsa with lime chipsVegetarian

Pineapple and jalapeño salsa. Saul steals a police car for the chase

Prep 11 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Char and dice the pineapple and mix the salsa up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; toss the corn chips just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 3 limes
  • 250g corn chips
  1. Cut half a pineapple into thick slices and char on a very hot grill pan for 3 minutes a side.
  2. Cool and dice.
  3. Mix with half a finely diced red onion, the chopped jalapeño, a handful of chopped coriander, the juice of the limes and salt.
  4. Rest 10 minutes.
  5. Toss the corn chips with the zest of the lime and a little salt and serve with the salsa.

Saul's cheesy garlic pull-apart breadVegetarian

Garlic cheese cob. Saul's in the dumpster already

Prep 6 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Cut, stuff and wrap in foil up to 6 hours ahead and keep in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 100g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 40g parmesan
  • foil, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 190C.
  2. Cut a cob loaf in a crosshatch pattern, 2cm apart, without cutting through the base.
  3. Melt the butter with the crushed garlic cloves and the chopped parsley.
  4. Spoon into all the cuts, then stuff with the grated mozzarella and the parmesan.
  5. Wrap in foil and bake 15 minutes, then unwrap and bake 10 minutes until golden and melted.

Red's pineapple teriyaki chicken

Teriyaki thighs, pineapple, rice. Red's boiling some eggs over here

Prep 8 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 100ml soy sauce
  • 60ml mirin
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1 1/2 cup jasmine rice
  • sesame seeds, to taste
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 red chilli
  • rice vinegar, to taste
  1. Simmer the soy, the mirin, the brown sugar, the grated ginger and the garlic clove for 5 minutes until syrupy.
  2. Marinate the chicken thighs in half the sauce for 30 minutes.
  3. Grill on a hot barbecue for 5 to 6 minutes a side, brushing with more sauce.
  4. Grill the pineapple slices 2 minutes a side.
  5. Cook the rice.
  6. Slice the chicken and serve with pineapple, sesame, spring onion and sliced chilli in vinegar.

Red's cat's pineapple upside-down cakeVegetarian

Pineapple upside-down. It's Red's cat's birthday today

Prep 17 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep covered; warm slices briefly and serve with chilled coconut cream
Recipe for 4
  • 185g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp dark rum
  • 1 pineapple
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 200ml coconut cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180C.
  2. Melt the butter with the brown sugar and the dark rum in a 23cm tin and lay the fresh pineapple rings on top.
  3. Beat the butter with the caster sugar, add the eggs, then the self-raising flour and the milk.
  4. Spread over the fruit and bake 45 minutes.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and turn out.
  6. Serve warm with the chilled coconut cream.

Drinks

Pineapple mint rum crushVegetarian

Rum, pineapple and mint. Chill as a cucumber

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Cut the pineapple and juice the limes up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; muddle and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 60ml brown sugar syrup
  • 4 limes
  • 180ml white rum
  • 250ml pineapple juice
  • chilli powder, to taste
  • 3 cup crushed ice
  • 1 tbsp salt
  1. Mix the salt with a little chilli powder and rim the glasses.
  2. For each drink, muddle the pineapple chunks, a few mint leaves, the brown sugar syrup and the lime juice in a shaker.
  3. Add the white rum and the pineapple juice, shake with ice and pour over crushed ice.
  4. For non-drinkers, skip the rum and top with soda.
