Snacks

Diamond cut cheeseVegetarian

Cheddar, Red Leicester and Wensleydale diamonds with crackers and pickled onions.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  • 150g red Leicester
  • 150g Wensleydale
  • 1 packet crackers
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Cut the cheddar, red Leicester and Wensleydale into sharp little diamond shapes.
  2. Arrange on a board with crackers and pickled onions. Don't swallow any of the diamonds.

Sausage Charlie's chipolatas

Browned chipolatas glazed in honey and wholegrain mustard, on toothpicks.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Grill the chipolatas up to 1 day ahead and chill; toss them with the honey and mustard in a hot pan on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chipolatas
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Grill the chipolatas until browned, 15 min.
  2. Toss in a hot pan with honey and mustard until sticky.
  3. Spear on toothpicks and serve hot with extra mustard.

Mini pie, mash and liquor

Minced beef pastries, buttery potato, herb sauce, chilli vinegar.

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 600ml beef stock
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 800g potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bottle malt vinegar
  • 1 red chilli
  1. Little minced beef and onion pies with a flaky lid, a scoop of buttery mash and bright green parsley liquor, with chilli vinegar to splash on. Brown 400g beef mince with a diced onion, add 1 tbsp flour and 200ml beef stock and simmer 15 minutes, then cool. Line 8 muffin holes with shortcrust, fill and top with puff. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes. Mash 800g potatoes with 50g butter. Melt 30g butter, stir in 2 tbsp flour, whisk in 400ml stock and simmer, then add a bunch of finely chopped parsley. Serve with malt vinegar and chilli.

Mains

Avi's New York strip steak

Sirloin seared medium rare, creamed spinach, baked potato.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 19 min · Make ahead: Make the horseradish cream up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake the potatoes and sear the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 baking potatoes
  • 4 thick sirloin steaks
  • 400g spinach
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 40g butter
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C until soft in the middle, 1 hr.
  2. Mix the sour cream with the horseradish cream and chill.
  3. Season the sirloins well and sear in a smoking hot pan 3 to 4 minutes a side, to 54°C inside for medium rare. Rest 5 minutes.
  4. Wilt the spinach with the garlic, add the cream and parmesan and simmer until thick, 6 min.
  5. Split the potatoes, add the butter and serve with the steak, creamed spinach and horseradish cream.

Turkish's full English fry-up

Bacon, sausage, eggs, black pudding, beans, tomato and toast.

How it is made
  • bacon
  • pork sausages
  • eggs
  • baked beans
  • black pudding
  • mushrooms
  1. Grill 8 rashers bacon and 8 pork sausages, fry 4 eggs, 4 slices black pudding and 200g mushrooms, warm 1 tin baked beans, grill 2 halved tomatoes and serve with 8 slices buttered toast.

Bullet Tooth Tony's banoffee pieVegetarian

Caramel and banana on a biscuit crust under whipped cream.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 2 hr 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits
  • 120g butter
  • 380g tinned caramel
  • 3 bananas
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a pie dish.
  2. Chill the base, 1 hr.
  3. Spread with the caramel and layer sliced bananas on top.
  4. Whip the cream and spread over.
  5. Chill to set, then grate the chocolate over, 1 hr 20 min.

Drinks

Mickey's bare-knuckle whiskey ginger

Irish whiskey, lime and ginger beer over ice.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best built to order; squeeze the limes and slice the ginger up to 1 day ahead, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml Irish whiskey
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice and add whiskey and lime juice to each.
  2. Top with cold ginger beer and stir once.
  3. Garnish with a lime wedge and a thin slice of fresh ginger.
