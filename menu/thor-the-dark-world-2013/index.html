Snacks

London pub scotch eggs

Soft-boiled eggs wrapped in sage sausage meat and breadcrumbs, fried, with English mustard.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Wrap up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 400g pork sausage mince
  • 1 tbsp chopped sage
  • 1/2 cup plain flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp English mustard
  1. Boil the eggs for 6 minutes, cool and peel.
  2. Mix the sausage mince with the sage and wrap around each egg.
  3. Roll in flour, beaten egg and panko.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170°C for 6 minutes, then halve and serve with mustard.

Greenwich tube stop chipsVegetarian

Twice-fried chips with malt vinegar. Thor took the Tube here.

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into thick chips
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Rinse the cut potatoes and dry well.
  2. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil at 140°C for 6 minutes until soft, then drain and rest.
  3. Fry again at 190°C until golden and crisp, 8 min.
  4. Tip into paper, shake over malt vinegar and plenty of sea salt.

Jane's date night tomato bruschetta

Garlic-rubbed ciabatta, ripe tomato, basil and torn buffalo mozzarella, for a dinner cut short.

How it is made
  • 500g ripe tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 ciabatta loaf
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 balls buffalo mozzarella
  1. Grilled ciabatta rubbed with garlic, piled with ripe tomato and basil, torn buffalo mozzarella, sea salt and a drizzle of good olive oil. Dice 500g ripe tomatoes and mix with a handful of torn basil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 3 tbsp olive oil and salt, and leave 15 minutes. Slice 1 ciabatta into 12 pieces, brush with olive oil and grill or toast until charred at the edges. Rub each with a cut garlic clove. Pile on the tomato, tear over 2 balls of buffalo mozzarella and finish with sea salt and more oil.

Mains

London pie and mash

Beef in brown ale under pastry, with mash.

Prep 15 min, cook 3 hr 35 min · Make ahead: Filling a day ahead, top and bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 375ml brown ale
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Brown the beef in the oil with the onions, then stir in the flour, 15 min.
  2. Add the brown ale and water to cover and simmer until tender, 3 hr.
  3. Pour into a dish and top with puff pastry. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter and milk. Cook the peas and serve everything together, 10 min.

Asgard honey and rosemary lamb shoulder

Lamb shoulder roasted with honey and rosemary until tender.

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 400g carrots
  • 300g parsnips
  • 400g potatoes
  1. Lamb shoulder rubbed with garlic, rosemary and honey, slow roasted until it pulls apart, with roasted root vegetables and the pan juices. Stab a 2kg lamb shoulder all over and push in slivers from 4 garlic cloves and rosemary sprigs. Rub with 3 tbsp honey, olive oil and salt. Put in a roasting dish with 250ml water, cover with foil and roast at 150°C for 5 hours. Add 1kg chopped carrots, parsnips and potatoes for the last 90 minutes, uncovering for the final 30. Pull the lamb apart and spoon over the juices.

Dark Elves blackberry tartVegetarian

Vanilla custard tart heaped with fresh blackberries.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min, 35 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 500ml thick vanilla custard
  • 250g blackberries
  • 3 tbsp blackberry jam
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Blind bake the pastry case at 180°C until golden and cool completely, 40 min.
  2. Fill with the custard and pile the blackberries on top.
  3. Warm the jam with a splash of water and brush over the fruit to glaze, 5 min.
  4. Chill, then dust the edge with icing sugar, 35 min.

Drinks

Aether blood orange and cranberry spritz

Vodka, blood orange, cranberry and lime with soda. Red as the Aether.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml vodka
  • 240ml blood orange juice
  • 240ml cranberry juice
  • 40ml lime juice
  • 240ml soda water
  • 1 blood orange, sliced
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Shake the vodka, juices and lime juice hard with ice, in batches.
  2. Strain into wine glasses full of ice and top with soda water.
  3. Garnish with a blood orange wheel and a rosemary sprig. Leave out the vodka for a booze-free version.
