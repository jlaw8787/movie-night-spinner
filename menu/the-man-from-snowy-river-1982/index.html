Snacks

Stockman's damperVegetarian

Damper and golden syrup. Earn your right to the mountain first.

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best the day it is baked
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • golden syrup, to serve
  1. Rub a portion of the butter into the flour and salt.
  2. Mix in the milk with a knife, shape into a round and score the top.
  3. Bake at 200°C until it sounds hollow.
  4. Serve warm with the remaining butter and golden syrup.

High country corned beef sandwiches

Sliced corned silverside with English mustard and pickles on buttered bread.

Prep 15 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Beef up to 3 days, assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg corned silverside
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp English mustard
  • 4 tbsp mustard pickles
  1. Put the beef in a pot with the halved onion, carrot and peppercorns, cover with water and bring to a simmer.
  2. Simmer gently, then leave to cool in the liquid.
  3. Butter the bread, slice the beef thin and layer with mustard and pickles.

Clancy's mutton piesOn the printed menu

Braised lamb, onion and rosemary in shortcrust pastry, a pie for the ride.

How it is made
  • 800g lamb shoulder
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Slow-braised lamb shoulder with onion, rosemary and gravy in shortcrust pastry with flaky lids, hot pies with tomato sauce for the saddlebag. Brown 800g diced lamb shoulder, add 1 onion, rosemary, 1 tbsp flour, 400ml beef stock and Worcestershire. Cover and simmer gently 2 hours, then shred and cool. Line 6 pie tins with 2 sheets shortcrust pastry, fill, top with lids cut from 2 sheets puff pastry and crimp. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes. Serve with tomato sauce.

Mains

Spur's gold strike lamb stew

Lamb, carrot, potato and rosemary stew. No wallaby.

Prep 32 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg lamb shoulder chops
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 600g potatoes
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Chop the onions, carrots and potatoes into chunks.
  2. Brown the lamb in a heavy pot in batches.
  3. Add the vegetables, stock and rosemary, cover and cook over coals or at 160°C until the lamb is tender.
  4. Finish with chopped parsley.

Harrison's homestead roastOn the printed menu

Sirloin roasted with Yorkshires and gravy. At dinner, Harrison says he has no brother.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef sirloin
  • 800g potatoes
  • 600g pumpkin
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 140g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 125ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar horseradish
  1. Beef sirloin roasted pink and carved at the table, with Yorkshire puddings, roast potatoes and pumpkin, horseradish cream and red wine gravy. Season a 1.5kg beef sirloin and roast at 200°C for 40 minutes until 52°C inside, then rest 20 minutes. Roast 800g potatoes and 600g pumpkin in oil at 220°C for 45 minutes. Whisk 140g flour, 3 eggs and 250ml milk, pour into 12 hot oiled muffin holes and bake at 230°C for 20 minutes. Make gravy with the juices, 125ml red wine and 300ml stock. Mix 150g sour cream with horseradish.

Sweets

Steamed spotted dog currant puddingVegetarian

Currant pudding, custard. Curly lets the colt loose.

Prep 22 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Pudding up to 1 day, reheat by steaming
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 150g currants
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • golden syrup, to serve
  • 600ml custard
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the sugar and currants.
  2. Add the milk to make a soft dough and wrap in a floured cloth or put in a basin.
  3. Steam until firm and cooked through.
  4. Slice and serve with golden syrup and warm custard.

Drinks

Clancy's billy teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Billy tea, milk and sugar. Clancy leads the muster.

Prep 2 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh in the billy; nothing to do ahead except measure out the tea and sugar
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2 L water
  • 3 tbsp loose leaf tea
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • sugar, to taste
  1. Boil the water in a billy or pot.
  2. Throw in the tea and a gum leaf if you have one, take it off the heat and swing it round to settle the leaves.
  3. Pour into tin mugs with milk and sugar.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Jim rides down the mountain after the brumbies.
