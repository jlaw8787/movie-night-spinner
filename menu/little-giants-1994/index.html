Annexation of Puerto Rico wings

Crisp baked wings in honey barbecue sauce, celery and ranch on the side

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, wings fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 125ml barbecue sauce
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp chopped herbs (chives and parsley)
  • 3 celery sticks
  1. Toss the wings with salt and bake at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Warm the honey with the barbecue sauce and toss the wings through.
  3. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise and herbs into a ranch dip. Serve with celery sticks.

Gas station cheesy beef taquitosOn the printed menu

Spiced mince and cheddar rolled tight in corn tortillas, baked crisp like the counter roller kind

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 jar mild salsa
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Spiced beef and melted cheddar rolled tight in corn tortillas, baked until crisp like the ones on the counter roller, with salsa and sour cream. Brown 400g beef mince with a diced onion, 2 tsp taco seasoning and 3 tbsp water. Warm 12 corn tortillas so they bend. Spoon beef and 150g grated cheddar along each, roll tightly and lay seam side down on an oiled tray. Brush with oil and bake at 220°C for 15 minutes until crisp. Serve with mild salsa and sour cream.

Urbania Cincinnati chilli spaghetti

Cinnamon and cocoa spiced beef chilli over spaghetti with cheddar and onion

Prep 30 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 onion, finely diced, to serve
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp mild chilli powder
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 400g diced tomatoes
  • 250ml water
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince with the diced onion.
  2. Add the cumin, cinnamon, chilli powder, cocoa, tomatoes and water and simmer until thick.
  3. Cook the spaghetti.
  4. Pile into bowls, ladle over the chilli and top with cheddar and finely diced onion.

Icebox's baked mac and cheeseOn the printed menu

Baked macaroni in cheese sauce, golden on top. Fuel for a 99 yard run.

How it is made
  • 400g macaroni
  • 70g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 750ml full cream milk
  • 250g cheddar
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  1. Macaroni baked in a cheddar and parmesan sauce with a hint of mustard, under a crunchy buttered breadcrumb crust, with a crisp green salad. Boil 400g macaroni for 2 minutes less than the packet says. Melt 50g butter, stir in 50g flour, whisk in 750ml milk and simmer until thick. Stir in 250g grated cheddar, 50g parmesan and 1 tsp mustard. Mix with the pasta and tip into a dish. Toss 60g breadcrumbs with 20g melted butter, scatter over and bake at 190°C for 25 minutes. Serve with salad leaves.

Ohio buckeye peanut butter chocolatesVegetarian

Peanut butter fudge balls dipped in dark chocolate, a round of peanut butter showing

Prep 20 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g smooth peanut butter
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 2 cups icing sugar
  • 200g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the peanut butter, butter and icing sugar until it holds together. Roll into 24 balls.
  2. Chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Dip each ball in melted chocolate, leaving a round of peanut butter showing on top, then chill until set.

Pee Wee football cookiesVegetarian

Chocolate cookies shaped as footballs with white icing laces

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 40g cocoa
  • 100g icing sugar
  1. Cream the butter and sugar, beat in the egg, then mix in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Shape into small footballs and flatten slightly. Bake at 180°C and cool.
  3. Mix the icing sugar with a little water and pipe white laces across the top. Let set.

Sideline orange honey coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Orange and lemon juice with honey and salt, for the 21 point halftime

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 7 oranges, 6 juiced and 1 cut into wedges
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • sea salt, a small pinch
  • 1l cold water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Squeeze the oranges and lemons into a big jug.
  2. Stir in the honey dissolved in a little warm water and the salt. Top with cold water and ice and stir well.
  3. Pour into paper cups and serve with orange wedges.
