Snacks

Fried egg canapés

Brioche, quail egg and bacon, minus Ramada's belly.

Prep 3 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Toast the brioche rounds up to 1 day ahead, airtight; fry the bacon and quail eggs just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 small brioche loaf
  • 4 rashers of bacon
  • 12 quail eggs
  • 30g butter
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Cut 12 small rounds from the brioche and toast them, 8 min.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp and break into pieces.
  3. Fry the quail eggs in butter, 4 min.
  4. Put an egg and a bit of bacon on each round and sprinkle with chives.

Dead Meat's last chipsVegetarian

Twice-fried thick chips served with homemade garlic aioli.

Prep 18 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Fry the chips the first time up to 4 hours ahead and rest them; make the aioli up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; fry again just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 200ml neutral oil, for the aioli
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips.
  2. Fry in 140°C oil, then rest, 16 min.
  3. Whisk the yolk, crushed garlic and lemon juice, then drip in the oil until thick.
  4. Fry again at 185°C until crisp.
  5. Salt the chips and serve with the aioli.

Airbase soft pretzels with beer cheese

Warm salted soft pretzels with hot cheddar beer cheese. Enemy toast, not coast, sir.

How it is made
  • 4 large soft pretzels
  • 60g butter
  • coarse salt
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml lager
  • 100ml milk
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  • Dijon mustard
  • wholegrain mustard
  • cayenne pepper
  1. Warm soft pretzels brushed with butter and coarse salt, served with a hot, smooth cheddar and lager dip and a pot of grainy mustard. Brush 4 large soft pretzels with 30g melted butter, sprinkle with coarse salt and warm at 200°C for 5 minutes. Melt 30g butter, stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then whisk in 200ml lager and 100ml milk until thick. Off the heat, stir in 200g grated sharp cheddar, 1 tsp Dijon and a pinch of cayenne until smooth. Serve the pretzels with the cheese dip and wholegrain mustard.

Mains

Airbase rib eye, corn and slaw

Grilled steak with buttered corn and slaw, served hot off the airbase grill.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; grill the steaks and corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 pinch caster sugar
  • salt, to taste
  1. Shred the cabbage and carrots and toss with the mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar and salt.
  2. Grill the steaks and corn over high heat, 15 min.
  3. Rest the steaks and serve with corn and slaw, 5 min.

Ramada's sizzling chicken fajitas

Chicken, bell pepper and onion sizzled in a hot pan, warm tortillas.

Prep 19 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 limes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 capsicums
  • 1 red onion
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 2 avocados
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Slice the chicken into strips and toss with the cumin, paprika, juice of 2 limes, 1 tbsp of the oil and salt.
  2. Mash the avocados with the last lime and salt.
  3. Get a cast iron pan smoking hot. Cook the chicken in the rest of the oil for 6 minutes, add the sliced capsicums and onion and cook for 4 minutes more.
  4. Take the pan to the table still sizzling, with warm tortillas, guacamole, sour cream and coriander, 3 min.

Sweets

Custard tartsVegetarian

Egg custard tarts with nutmeg on top, wobbling like a salute in the credits.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bring to room temperature to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 125ml cream
  • 80g caster sugar
  • nutmeg, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Line a 12-hole muffin tray with rounds of pastry, 10 min.
  2. Blind-bake the cases, 10 min.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream and sugar.
  4. Fill the cases and grate nutmeg on top.
  5. Bake at 150°C until just set.
  6. Cool before serving, 10 min.

Drinks

Officers' club Moscow mule

Vodka, lime and spicy ginger beer.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the mugs and juice the lime up to 1 day ahead; build over ice with the vodka and ginger beer to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 copper or steel mugs
  • 1 tray ice
  • 180ml vodka
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 600ml spicy ginger beer, cold
  • 1 lime
  • 4 mint sprigs
  1. Chill the mugs in the freezer, then fill with ice, 15 min.
  2. Add vodka and lime juice to each mug.
  3. Top with ginger beer and stir once from the bottom.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel and a slapped mint sprig.
