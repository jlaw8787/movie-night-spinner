Truffled Gruyere gougeresVegetarian

Gruyère choux puffs with a little truffle. Scuba gear off and a tuxedo underneath.

Prep 19 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Pipe the gougeres and freeze them raw up to a week ahead; bake from frozen with 5 extra minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml milk
  • 125ml water
  • 100g butter, diced
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g Gruyere, finely grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp truffle oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Bring the milk, water, butter and a pinch of salt to the boil, tip in the flour and beat over the heat until the dough leaves the sides.
  2. Cool for 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy.
  3. Beat in most of the Gruyere with the mustard and nutmeg, pipe walnut-sized balls onto lined trays and top with the rest of the cheese.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and deep golden, without opening the oven.
  5. Brush the hot puffs with the truffle oil and scatter with sea salt.

Florida Keys prawn cocktail

Prawns in a pink lime and hot sauce dressing. Warheads hidden in antique statues.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 2 days ahead; build the glasses to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked king prawns, peeled
  • 2 baby cos lettuces, shredded
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 2 limes
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 small bunch chives, snipped
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, hot sauce and the juice of 1 lime into a pink sauce.
  2. Toss the avocado with a squeeze of lime so it stays green.
  3. Layer the lettuce and avocado in 4 glasses and pile the prawns on top.
  4. Spoon over the sauce, dust with paprika, scatter chives and hang a lime wedge on each glass.

Mains

Code name Doris chicken Kiev

Crumbed chicken Kiev with garlic and parsley butter. Helen hoped for Natasha and got Doris.

Prep 12 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Crumb the Kievs up to a day ahead and chill; fry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, finely chopped
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g green beans
  1. Mash the butter with the garlic and parsley, roll into a log and freeze until firm.
  2. Cut a deep pocket in each breast, push in a slice of the butter and press closed.
  3. Dip in flour, egg and panko, then egg and panko again, and chill for 10 minutes.
  4. Fry in hot oil until golden all over, then finish in the oven at 200°C until cooked through.
  5. Meanwhile boil the potatoes and mash with a knob of butter and the milk, and steam the beans.
  6. Rest the Kievs for 2 minutes and serve with the mash and beans.

Seven Mile Bridge snapper

Snapper over coconut rice with lime. Harriers blow up the Seven Mile Bridge.

Prep 16 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa up to 4 hours ahead and chill; cook the rice and fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 180g snapper fillets, skin on
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 long red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 small bunch coriander, chopped
  • 2 limes
  1. Simmer the rice with the coconut milk, 250ml water and a pinch of salt, covered, until tender.
  2. Mix the mango, onion, chilli, coriander and the juice of 1 lime for the salsa.
  3. Rub the snapper with oil, allspice and salt and cook skin down in a hot pan until crisp, then turn for a minute.
  4. Serve the fish on the rice with the salsa piled on top and lime wedges.

Missed birthday chocolate layer cakeVegetarian

Chocolate buttermilk layer cake with fudge icing, for the birthday party Harry missed.

Prep 25 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake the sponges up to 2 days ahead and keep wrapped; ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 225g plain flour
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 60g cocoa powder, plus 30g for the icing
  • 1 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml hot strong coffee
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g butter, softened
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 4 birthday candles
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, 60g cocoa and bicarb, then whisk in the eggs, buttermilk, oil and hot coffee to a thin batter.
  2. Divide between two lined 20cm tins and bake at 180°C until a skewer comes out clean.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Melt the chocolate, beat the butter with the icing sugar and remaining cocoa, then beat in the chocolate.
  5. Sandwich and cover the cakes with the icing and stand the candles on top.

Juno Skinner's champagne cocktail

Juno pours champagne holding her Beretta.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the champagne and glasses well; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 x 750ml bottle brut champagne
  • 60ml cognac
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 1 tsp Angostura bitters
  • 1 orange, for peel
  1. Drop a sugar cube soaked with a few drops of bitters into each chilled flute.
  2. Add 15ml cognac, top slowly with champagne and twist a strip of orange peel over each.

When to serve

Pour the champagne when Harry sweeps Juno into the tango at Khaled's party.
