Snacks

Matchmaker's tea and dumplings

Steamed pork dumplings with garlic chives and ginger, soy and black vinegar.

Prep 42 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 bunch garlic chives, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar
  1. Mix the pork, garlic chives and ginger.
  2. Fill the dumpling wrappers and pleat.
  3. Steam for 8 minutes.
  4. Serve with soy and Chinkiang vinegar, and pour the tea carefully.

Training camp edamameVegetarian

Wok tossed with garlic, chilli and soy. For Yao, Ling and Chien-Po.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Steam the edamame up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; toss in the wok with the garlic and chilli just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen edamame
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  1. Steam the edamame for 4 minutes.
  2. Toss in a hot wok with the garlic, chilli flakes and a splash of soy.
  3. Finish with plenty of flaky salt and serve in a big bowl.

Cri-Kee's lucky vegetable spring rollsVegetarian

Crisp rolls of cabbage, shiitake, carrot and glass noodles with sweet chilli.

Prep 26 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Roll up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled in one layer, or freeze up to 1 month; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rice vermicelli
  • 1/4 wombok, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 150g shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 bunch garlic chives
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 20 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Soak the vermicelli in hot water 5 minutes, drain and snip into short lengths.
  2. Stir-fry the wombok, carrot and mushrooms in a splash of the oil until wilted, then add the chopped garlic chives, soy and sesame oil.
  3. Toss with the noodles and cool completely.
  4. Mix the cornflour with a little water. Roll a spoonful of filling tightly in each wrapper, folding in the sides, and seal with the paste.
  5. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry the rolls in batches until golden. Drain and serve with sweet chilli sauce.

Mushu's breakfast congee

Ginger rice porridge, egg and bacon, happy to see you.

Prep 13 min, cook 102 min · Make ahead: Simmer the congee up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and loosen with a splash of stock when reheating; fry the eggs and bacon fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 200g jasmine rice
  • 2 L chicken stock
  • 1 piece ginger, sliced
  • 4 eggs
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  1. Simmer the rice in the stock with the ginger for 90 minutes, stirring often, until silky.
  2. Fry the eggs and bacon.
  3. Top each bowl with an egg and bacon smile, spring onion and soy.

Imperial City red-braised chicken (alcohol-free)

Chicken thighs braised glossy in soy, star anise and rock sugar, bok choy and rice.

Prep 2 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Braise the chicken up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; steam the bok choy and cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thigh fillets, in large pieces
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp chicken stock
  • 40g rock sugar or brown sugar
  • 4 slices ginger
  • 4 spring onions
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 baby bok choy
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in a wide pan.
  2. Add the ginger, spring onion pieces, star anise and cinnamon, then the soy sauces, stock, sugar and 250 ml water. Simmer covered for 20 minutes.
  3. Uncover and simmer, turning the chicken, until the sauce is glossy and sticky.
  4. Cook the rice and steam the halved bok choy for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve the chicken and sauce with the rice and bok choy.

Grandmother Fa's lotus mooncakesVegetarian

Golden baked pastry filled with sweet lotus seed paste.

Prep 45 min, cook 20 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Keep 2 days before eating, they soften
Recipe for 4
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 30ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp alkaline water
  • 150g plain flour
  • 400g lotus seed paste
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Mix the golden syrup, oil and alkaline water, then the flour into a dough.
  2. Rest the dough for 2 hours.
  3. Divide the paste into balls and wrap thin portions of dough around each. Press into moulds.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 10 minutes, egg wash, then bake 10 minutes more.

Drinks

Family temple iced jasmine lychee teaNo alcohol

Jasmine tea, lychee syrup. Poured for the ancestors in the family temple.

Prep 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the tea up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bags jasmine green tea
  • 1 L water
  • 1 tin lychees, 565g, drained with syrup kept
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 12 mint leaves
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in water at 80°C for 3 minutes, remove and chill.
  2. Stir some of the lychee syrup and the lime juice into the cold tea.
  3. Pour over ice in glasses with lychees and a few mint leaves.
