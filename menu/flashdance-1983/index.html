Richie's open mic pierogiesVegetarian

Potato and cheese pierogies with fried onions. Richie quits the grill for LA stand-up.

Prep 33 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Fill and shape the pierogies up to a day ahead and chill on a floured tray, or freeze; boil and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 125g sour cream, plus extra to serve
  • 60g butter
  • 500g floury potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  1. Mix the flour, egg, sour cream and half the butter, melted, into a soft dough and rest it.
  2. Mash the potato with the cheddar and season well.
  3. Roll the dough thin, cut 8cm rounds, fill, fold and pinch the edges shut.
  4. Boil the pierogies until they float, then fry in the rest of the butter with the onions until golden.
  5. Serve with sour cream and chives.

Steel mill welder's spark wings

Hot buffalo wings with blue cheese dip.

Prep 7 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead; bake the wings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings, jointed
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 40g butter
  • 100g blue cheese, crumbled
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 3 celery sticks, cut in batons
  1. Toss the wings with the baking powder and salt and bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp.
  2. Melt the butter into the hot sauce.
  3. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream and mayo.
  4. Toss the wings in the sauce and serve with the dip and celery.

Lobster dinner date tails

Grilled lobster tails, garlic butter and shoestring fries.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Mix the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead and chill; split and grill the tails to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 raw lobster tails
  • 100g soft butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 750g frozen shoestring fries
  1. Bake the fries at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Mash the butter with the garlic, parsley and lemon zest.
  3. Split the tails down the back, spread with half the butter and grill shell side down until the flesh turns white.
  4. Melt the rest of the butter for dipping and serve with lemon wedges and the fries.

Steel town fries-inside steak sandwich

Steak, provolone, vinegar slaw and fries, all in the bread.

Prep 8 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw up to a day ahead; cook the fries and steak to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g minute steaks
  • 8 thick slices Italian bread
  • 8 slices provolone
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 500g frozen thick-cut fries
  1. Bake the fries at 220°C until golden.
  2. Toss the cabbage with the vinegar, oil, sugar and salt.
  3. Sear the steaks in a very hot pan for a minute a side and lay the provolone on top to melt.
  4. Pile steak, chips, slaw and tomato between the bread and press down.

Jeanie's figure skater sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream with hot fudge and toasted almonds, for a skater who fell twice.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the fudge sauce up to 5 days ahead and rewarm; scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup
  • 50g flaked almonds, toasted
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Heat the cream and golden syrup, pour over the chocolate and stir until glossy.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into 4 chilled glasses.
  3. Pour over the hot fudge and finish with almonds and a cherry.
