Pass it on potato smilesVegetarian

Cheddar mashed potato rounds baked crisp with a pressed on smile.

Prep 20 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Mash, shape and cut the rounds up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes, boiled
  • 80g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 20g butter
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with the cheddar, flour, butter and salt, 20 min.
  2. Roll out 1cm thick and cut rounds.
  3. Press in two eyes and a wide smile with a straw and spoon.
  4. Bake at 220°C for 15 minutes until crisp.

Rose's late-shift popcorn chicken

Bite-size buttermilk fried chicken with a honey mustard dip.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Chicken can soak up to 8 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs, diced
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Soak the chicken in buttermilk for an hour.
  2. Season the flour with paprika, garlic powder and salt and toss the chicken through it.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C for 4 minutes, in batches.
  4. Mix the honey and Dijon and serve with the chicken.

Birthday party pulled pork sliders

Pulled pork in barbecue sauce on soft slider buns with apple slaw and pickles.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg boneless pork shoulder
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 200ml apple juice
  • 1 bottle barbecue sauce
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 green apple
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Smoky slow-roasted pork shoulder pulled and tossed in barbecue sauce, piled on soft slider buns with crunchy apple slaw and pickles. Rub a 1.5kg boneless pork shoulder with 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder and 2 tsp salt. Put in a covered dish with 200ml apple juice and roast at 150°C for 4 hours until it pulls apart. Shred and toss with 200ml barbecue sauce. Shred a quarter cabbage and 1 green apple into matchsticks and toss with 3 tbsp mayonnaise and 1 tbsp cider vinegar. Fill 12 split slider buns with pork, slaw and pickles.

Holly's dinner night salmon

Roast salmon with lemon dill butter and crushed potatoes.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and crush them with butter when reheated; cook the salmon and beans on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 90g butter
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 800g potatoes, boiled
  • 300g green beans
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and crush with some of the butter, 20 min.
  2. Roast the salmon skin side down at 220°C for 8 minutes.
  3. Melt some butter with the lemon juice and dill and spoon over, 5 min.
  4. Steam the beans, 7 min.

Late-shift miso ramen with soft eggs

Miso broth, ramen, pork belly, soy eggs.

How it is made
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 500g pork belly
  • 1.6 litres chicken stock
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 tub white miso
  • 4 serves fresh ramen noodles
  • 1 tin corn kernels
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Rich miso and chicken broth over springy ramen noodles with seared pork belly, jammy soy eggs, corn, spring onion and a drizzle of chilli oil. Boil 4 eggs for 6.5 minutes, cool in iced water, peel and soak in 100ml soy, 100ml water and 1 tbsp sugar for 2 hours. Slice 500g pork belly and pan fry until crisp. Heat 1.6 litres chicken stock with 2 grated garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger, then whisk in 4 tbsp white miso and 1 tbsp soy off the boil. Cook 4 serves ramen noodles. Divide noodles and broth into bowls and top with pork, halved eggs, corn, spring onion and chilli oil.

Smiling cookiesVegetarian

Sugar cookies iced with a wide red grin.

Prep 22 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Un-iced cookies keep 4 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 1 drop yellow food colouring
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar and one egg, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out, cut rounds and bake at 180°C for 8 minutes. Cool.
  3. Ice pale yellow with icing sugar mixed with water and a drop of yellow colouring.
  4. Pipe a wide red smile on each. A little unsettling.

Mustache's chamomile honey teaNo alcohol

Chamomile tea with honey, lemon and cinnamon, served hot.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Steep both teas with the honey, vanilla and lemon up to 1 day ahead, chilled; reheat and pour into mugs
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bags of chamomile tea
  • 800ml just-boiled water
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 120ml butterscotch
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 12 whole cloves
  1. Steep the chamomile and black tea bags in the water for 5 minutes, then remove.
  2. Stir in the honey, vanilla and the juice of one lemon.
  3. Pour into mugs. Finish with a cinnamon stick and a lemon wheel studded with cloves.
