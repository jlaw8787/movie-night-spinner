Snacks

Stovetop popcornVegetarian

Popcorn popped in coconut oil, tossed with melted butter and salt

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best popped and eaten the same night; measure the kernels and melt the butter up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  1. Heat the coconut oil with three kernels in a heavy covered pot.
  2. When they pop, add the rest of the kernels and shake constantly until the popping slows.
  3. Tip out and toss with the butter and salt.

Party chips and salsaVegetarian

Fresh tomato and jalapeño salsa with corn chips

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the salsa up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stir and serve with the chips on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 1 jalapeno, diced
  • 1/2 cup coriander, chopped
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 300g corn chips
  1. Dice the tomatoes, onion and jalapeno.
  2. Mix with the coriander, lime juice and salt.
  3. Leave to get juicy.
  4. Serve with the corn chips.

Mains

Stu's party lasagne

Beef lasagne with ricotta and mozzarella, baked golden

Prep 35 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Assemble a day ahead, bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 700g tomato passata
  • 250g lasagne sheets
  • 500g ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and garlic.
  2. Add the passata and simmer.
  3. Mix the ricotta with the egg.
  4. Layer the lasagne sheets, ricotta mix and sauce in a dish, finishing with mozzarella.
  5. Bake at 180C.
  6. Rest before cutting.

Sweets

Ghost mask cookiesVegetarian

Butter cookies iced white with a black piped face

Prep 40 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Undecorated cookies keep 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp black food colouring
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar and one egg, then mix in the flour.
  2. Roll out and cut into long ovals.
  3. Bake at 180C.
  4. Cool the cookies.
  5. Ice white with the icing sugar mixed with egg white.
  6. Pipe the face in black icing.

Woodsboro house party punchVegetarian

Vodka, cranberry, pineapple and lime punch topped with lemonade

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Stir the vodka, juices and lime juice together and chill in the punch bowl up to 1 day ahead; add the lemonade, ice and orange just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml vodka
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 3 limes, juiced
  • 750ml lemonade, cold
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  1. Stir the vodka, juices and lime juice together in a big punch bowl.
  2. Chill.
  3. Just before serving add the lemonade, ice and orange slices.
  4. Ladle into party cups. Swap the vodka for extra lemonade for anyone driving.
