Snacks

Baxter's wheel of cheeseVegetarian

Baked brie, honey, thyme, walnuts

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 500g brie wheel
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 small bunch thyme, leaves picked
  • 50g walnuts, roughly chopped
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Score the top of the brie, drizzle with honey and scatter over the thyme and walnuts.
  2. Bake at 180°C on a lined tray until molten. Serve with crackers. Baxter would approve, 15 min.

Carne asada plate

Lime steak, jack, avocado, sour cream

Prep 8 min, cook 33 min, 1 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 500g skirt steak
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 750g frozen chips
  • 150g Monterey Jack, grated
  • 1 avocado, mashed
  • 100g sour cream
  1. Mix the steak with the lime juice, garlic and cumin.
  2. Marinate for 1 hour.
  3. Grill the steak hot, 3 minutes a side, then rest and chop.
  4. Bake the chips on a tray at 220°C until crisp, 20 min.
  5. Cover with Monterey Jack and grill to melt, 5 min.
  6. Top with the steak, avocado and sour cream.

Mains

San Diego chicken tacos

Crisp chicken, corn tortillas, lime cream

Prep 8 min, cook 32 min
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken breast, cut into strips
  • 60g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 limes
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 200g cabbage, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  1. Cut the chicken into strips and coat in the flour mixed with paprika and cumin, then egg, then panko.
  2. Arrange on a lined tray, drizzle with oil and bake at 220°C, turning halfway, until golden and the thickest piece reaches 75°C, 20 min.
  3. Mix the sour cream with the juice of one lime for the crema, and toss the tomato and onion with the juice of the other lime.
  4. Warm the tortillas as the packet says and fill with chicken, cabbage, salsa and crema. Keep any chilli on the side, 3 min.

Sweets

ButterSCOTCH pudding

Self-saucing sponge, vanilla ice cream

Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 175g dark brown sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C conventional and grease a deep 1.5L baking dish with a little of the butter, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the flour with part of the sugar and the salt, then whisk melted butter, milk, egg and vanilla in another bowl and fold together just until combined.
  3. Spread into the dish. Mix the rest of the sugar with the cornflour and sprinkle over the batter, 5 min.
  4. Dissolve the golden syrup in the boiling water and pour gently over the back of a spoon without stirring.
  5. Bake on a tray until the sponge is set and a skewer in the sponge comes out clean. The sauce stays underneath, 33 min.
  6. Rest, then scoop into bowls with the warm butterscotch sauce and vanilla ice cream. All the butterscotch flavour, no whisky, 5 min.

Drinks

Glass Case of EmotionNo alcohol

Blackberry and lemon soda, no liquor

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, 15 min waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 125g blackberries
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 2 lemons, juiced to give 60ml, plus slices to serve
  • 800ml chilled sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the blackberries, sugar and water for 5 minutes, crushing the berries.
  2. Strain and cool completely, 15 min.
  3. Divide the syrup and lemon juice between ice-filled glasses, top with sparkling water, stir and taste for sugar. Serve with lemon slices. No alcohol.

When to serve

Serve the cheese when Ron finds out what Baxter did to the cheese.
