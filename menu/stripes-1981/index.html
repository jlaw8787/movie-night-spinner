Snacks

Boot camp crackers with pimento cheese

Pimento cheese, crackers, celery. That's the fact, Jack!

Prep 13 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g vintage cheddar, finely grated
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 80g roasted red capsicum from a jar, chopped
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 250g crackers
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Mix the cheddar with the mayonnaise, capsicum, Worcestershire, cayenne and pepper, keeping it a little chunky.
  2. Chill so the flavours settle.
  3. Spoon into a bowl, dust with paprika and serve with crackers and celery sticks.

Psycho's don't-call-me-Francis franks

Cocktail franks glazed in honey, mustard and tomato sauce.

Prep 6 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Mix the glaze up to 3 days ahead; fry and glaze the franks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 30 cocktail frankfurts
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  1. Score the frankfurts diagonally a few times each.
  2. Fry in the oil until the cuts open up.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, honey, mustard and Worcestershire and toss until sticky and glazed.
  4. Serve on toothpicks with a pot of wholegrain mustard.

Mains

Sgt Hulka's mess hall meatloaf

Glazed beef meatloaf baked in a loaf tin.

Prep 8 min, cook 102 min · Make ahead: Mix the meatloaf and press it into the tin up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake and make the mash and gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 3 onions, 1 grated and 2 sliced
  • 1 egg
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Mix the mince with the grated onion, egg, breadcrumbs, Worcestershire and salt. Press into a lined loaf tin.
  2. Spread with the tomato sauce mixed with the sugar and bake at 180°C.
  3. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with some of the butter and the milk.
  4. Fry the sliced onions in butter, stir in the flour, then the stock, and simmer to a gravy.
  5. Cook the peas and serve everything together.

Czech border chicken paprikash

Thighs braised in sweet paprika and sour cream, buttered egg noodles, after the big drill.

Prep 4 min, cook 51 min · Make ahead: Make the paprikash up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently; stir in the sour cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Brown the chicken in half the butter and set aside.
  2. Fry the sliced onions and capsicum in the same pan for 6 minutes, then stir in the paprika and flour for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes, stock and chicken and simmer for 30 minutes until tender.
  4. Thinly slice the cucumbers and toss with the vinegar, a pinch of sugar and half the dill.
  5. Cook the noodles, drain and toss with the rest of the butter.
  6. Stir the sour cream through the chicken off the heat, scatter with dill and serve with the noodles and cucumber salad.

Quick brown fox fudge cakeVegetarian

Moist chocolate cake with fudge frosting.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 60g cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 eggs
  • 330ml full cream milk
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100g butter
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa and bicarb. Whisk in the eggs, most of the milk, oil and boiling water.
  2. Divide between two lined 20cm tins and bake at 180°C.
  3. Cool the cakes.
  4. Melt the chocolate with the butter, then beat in the icing sugar and remaining milk until thick and glossy.
  5. Sandwich and cover the cakes with the icing and serve with whipped cream.

Drinks

EM-50 Urban Assault Irish coffeeVegetarian

Hot coffee, brown sugar and Irish whiskey topped with cream.

Prep 7 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Lightly whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled; warm the glasses and brew the coffee just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml strong brewed coffee
  • 8 tsp brown sugar
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Warm heatproof glasses with hot water, then tip it out.
  2. Stir the sugar and whiskey into each glass, then fill with hot coffee to near the top.
  3. Lightly whip the cream until it just thickens but still pours.
  4. Pour the cream slowly over the back of a spoon so it floats on top.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the platoon finishes its graduation drill.
