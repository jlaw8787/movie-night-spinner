Snacks

Nedry's finger sandwiches

Nedry's smuggling kit, hidden in plain sight.

Prep 19 min · Make ahead: Make up to 4 hours ahead, covered and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cream cheese, softened
  • 1 bunch chives, snipped
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 250g smoked chicken, sliced
  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Mix the cream cheese with the chives and the lemon zest.
  2. Mash the avocado with a squeeze of the lemon and the salt.
  3. Spread all the bread with the cream cheese. Top four slices with avocado and chicken and close with the rest.
  4. Press lightly, cut off the crusts and cut each into three fingers.
  5. Stack in a tin, cover and chill until serving.

Raptor claw chipsVegetarian

Muldoon's raptor pen, in the long grass.

Prep 17 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Crisps up to 1 day, guacamole on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 6 flour tortillas
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 avocados
  • 2 limes, juiced
  1. Cut the tortillas into curved claw shapes.
  2. Brush with oil and sprinkle with half the salt and the smoked paprika.
  3. Bake at 200°C on lined trays until crisp.
  4. Mash the avocados with the lime juice and remaining salt for the guacamole. Serve with the crisps.

Mains

Rosemary rack of lamb

Herb-crusted lamb rack roasted pink, pea and mint purée and baby potatoes, while Malcolm argues.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Crumb the lamb up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lamb racks
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 30g butter
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brush the lamb racks with Dijon, then press on the parsley and breadcrumbs.
  2. Toss the potatoes in oil and salt and put on a tray in a 220°C oven.
  3. Roast the lamb alongside the potatoes until 55°C inside, about 20 minutes.
  4. Rest the lamb 10 minutes while you boil the peas with the mint, drain and blitz with the butter. Slice the lamb and plate neatly.

Goat-free sticky barbecue pork ribs

Racks rubbed with brown sugar and paprika, baked then glazed, the T. rex gets the goat.

How it is made
  • 2 racks pork ribs (2kg)
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml barbecue sauce
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  1. Pork ribs rubbed with brown sugar, paprika and garlic, wrapped and baked until tender, then glazed with smoky barbecue sauce under a hot grill. Mix 3 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp salt and 1 tsp pepper. Rub over 2 racks pork ribs (about 2kg). Wrap each rack tightly in foil and bake at 150°C for 2.5 hours. Unwrap, brush thickly with 250ml barbecue sauce mixed with 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar and grill under high heat for 5 minutes until sticky and charred. Rest 5 minutes and cut between the bones.

The Founder’s sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla bean ice cream, hot fudge and crushed honeycomb, served soft, the freezers are down.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fudge up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 100g honeycomb, crushed
  1. Melt the chocolate with the cream over low heat for hot fudge, stirring until smooth.
  2. Leave the ice cream out for 10 minutes so it softens a little too much.
  3. Scoop three each into chilled coupes, pour over the fudge and scatter with crushed honeycomb.

Dino egg cookiesVegetarian

Cracked open in the hatchery.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 225g plain flour
  • 12 small chocolate eggs
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  1. Brown the butter in a small pan and cool for 10 minutes until just warm.
  2. Beat the butter with the sugar and egg, then mix in the flour to a soft dough.
  3. Wrap the dough around each chocolate egg, shape into ovals and place on a lined tray.
  4. Bake at 175°C until set and lightly golden, then cool.
  5. Mix the cocoa with a little water and flick over the cookies with a brush for speckles.

Tour car iced teaNo alcohol

Black tea with honey shaken with citrus and fresh herbs, in clear glasses, watch for ripples.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 12 mint leaves, plus extra to serve
  • 3 limes
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in boiling water for 5 minutes, remove and stir in the honey.
  2. Cool, then chill for 2 hours.
  3. Muddle the mint with the juice of 2 limes in a jug, add the tea and stir.
  4. Fill tall clear glasses with ice, pour in the tea and garnish with lime wheels and mint.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Hammond eats melting ice cream in the dark Visitor Center.
