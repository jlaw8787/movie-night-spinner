Snacks

Cheese pizza bitesVegetarian

Thin dough, tomato and plenty of mozzarella cut into squares, keep the change ya filthy animal

Prep 7 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic and oregano for 10 minutes and cool slightly.
  2. Stretch the dough thin on a tray and spread with the sauce, then cover with mozzarella.
  3. Bake on a hot tray at the oven's highest heat until bubbling.
  4. Cut into small squares.

Mains

Kevin's mac and cheeseVegetarian

Macaroni in cheddar sauce with a crisp crumb, the TV dinner he heats up all by himself

Prep 5 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g macaroni
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml full cream milk
  • 250g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  1. Cook the macaroni in boiling salted water 2 minutes short of the packet time and drain.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until thick.
  3. Stir in the cheddar and mustard, then fold through the macaroni.
  4. Tip into a baking dish and top with breadcrumbs.
  5. Bake at 200C until golden and bubbling.

Sweets

Kevin's sundae bar

Vanilla and chocolate scoops, hot fudge, caramel and sprinkles, eaten with no one to stop him

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and rewarm; whip the cream and set out the toppings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 1l chocolate ice cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100g mini marshmallows
  • 80g roasted peanuts
  • 4 tbsp sprinkles
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Take the vanilla and chocolate ice cream out of the freezer to soften for 10 minutes.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate with 150ml of the thickened cream over low heat, stirring, for hot fudge.
  3. Whip the rest of the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Chop the roasted peanuts and put them, the mini marshmallows, sprinkles and maraschino cherries in small bowls.
  5. Set everything out with big bowls and let everyone build their own sundae.

Drinks

Kevin's chocolate malted milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Milk blended thick with chocolate scoops, malt and syrup, for a night of junk and rubbish

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold full cream milk
  • 1l chocolate ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 5 tbsp chocolate syrup
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Blend the milk, six scoops of ice cream, malted milk powder and some of the chocolate syrup for 30 seconds until thick.
  2. Drizzle chocolate syrup inside tall glasses, pour in the shake and top with whipped cream and a cherry.

Fuller's colaVegetarianNo alcohol

Prep 5 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the cola overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/4l cola
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill the cola in the fridge overnight, or at least 4 hours.
  2. Slice the lemon and fill four tall glasses with ice, dropping a lemon slice into each.
  3. Pour the cola and go easy on the refills.
