Snacks

Fred and George's fireworks popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed with white chocolate and crackling popping candy.

Prep 2 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Pop the corn up to 1 day ahead, airtight; toss with butter and sprinkles on the night and add the popping candy at the last second
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 3 tbsp coloured sprinkles
  • 2 packets of popping candy
  1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pot and pop the corn, 6 min.
  2. Toss with the melted butter and sprinkles.
  3. Scatter popping candy over just before serving so it crackles.

Grimmauld Place steak and kidney pie

Chuck steak and lamb kidney in rich gravy under puff pastry.

Prep 50 min, cook 2 hr 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chuck steak, diced
  • 200g lamb kidneys, trimmed and chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Trim and chop the steak and kidneys.
  2. Brown the meat in the olive oil in batches, 15 min.
  3. Soften the onion.
  4. Stir in the flour, stock and Worcestershire sauce.
  5. Simmer until thick and tender, 2 hr.
  6. Spoon into a pie dish, cool slightly and cover with the pastry, 12 min.
  7. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C, 30 min.

Treacle tartVegetarian

Golden syrup, breadcrumbs and lemon in a short pastry case, served warm.

Prep 17 min, cook 50 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep at room temperature; warm slices gently before serving with cream
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 400g golden syrup
  • 100g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 lemon
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Line a tart tin with the pastry.
  2. Blind bake the case, 20 min.
  3. Warm the golden syrup with the breadcrumbs and lemon zest and juice, 5 min.
  4. Pour into the case.
  5. Bake at 180°C until just set, 30 min.
  6. Cool slightly, 15 min.
  7. Serve warm slices with a pour of cream.

Drinks

Pumpkin juiceNo alcohol

Pumpkin and apple juice with honey and cinnamon, chilled.

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min, 1 hr 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g butternut pumpkin, chopped
  • 1 L apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Chop the pumpkin.
  2. Roast at 200°C until soft, 30 min.
  3. Cool completely, 30 min.
  4. Blend with the apple juice, honey and cinnamon until smooth.
  5. Strain into a jug and chill well, 1 hr.
  6. Pour into goblets over ice.
