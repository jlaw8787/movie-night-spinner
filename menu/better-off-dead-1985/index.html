Mum's French dinner friesVegetarian

Thin double-fried fries in paper cones with tomato sauce

Prep 12 min, cook 16 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cut and soak the potatoes in cold water up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; drain, dry well and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • fine salt, to taste
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries.
  2. Soak in cold water for an hour, then dry well.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140°C until soft.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until golden and crisp.
  5. Toss with fine salt and pile into paper cones with tomato sauce.

Ski lodge cheese fondueVegetarian

Gruyère and Swiss melted in white wine, with baguette and apple to dip

Prep 12 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheeses and toss with the cornflour up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; cube the bread and apple and melt the fondue on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 250ml white wine
  • 200g gruyere, grated
  • 200g Swiss cheese, grated
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 baguette, cubed
  • 2 apples, cubed
  1. Cube the baguette and apple and toss the gruyere and Swiss cheese with cornflour.
  2. Rub a pot with the cut garlic clove.
  3. Warm the wine, then stir in the cheese gradually until smooth.
  4. Keep warm over low heat and dip in the baguette and apple.

Monique's mini croques-monsieurOn the printed menu

Ham and gruyère with Dijon and nutmeg béchamel, grilled till bubbling, in squares

How it is made
  • 25g butter
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 150g gruyere
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 150g leg ham
  1. Ham and gruyère sandwiches spread with Dijon and a nutmeg béchamel, grilled until bubbling and golden, cut into neat squares. Melt 25g butter, stir in 25g flour, whisk in 250ml milk and simmer until thick, then add nutmeg, salt and 30g grated gruyere. Spread 8 slices of white bread with Dijon. Fill 4 with 150g leg ham and 80g grated gruyere and close. Spread the béchamel on top, scatter with 40g more gruyere and grill for 4 to 5 minutes until bubbling and golden. Cut each into 4 squares.

Mains

Pig Burgers double cheeseburger

Two smashed patties, American cheese, pickles and tangy sauce

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls and mix the burger sauce up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; smash and cook on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 5 dill pickles
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls of 100g.
  2. Chop one pickle and mix it with the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and mustard into a burger sauce.
  3. Heat a heavy pan until smoking, add the balls, smash flat, season and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Flip, top each patty with cheese and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Toast the buns.
  6. Stack two patties per bun with sauce, sliced pickles and onion, and wrap in baking paper.

Mum's chicken and mushroom rice bake

Chicken thighs and mushrooms baked on rice in a thyme cream stock

Prep 12 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Brown the chicken and mushrooms up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; assemble with the rice, stock and cream and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 750ml hot chicken stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  1. Brown the chicken and mushrooms.
  2. Spread the rice in a baking dish and add the chicken and mushrooms.
  3. Pour over the hot stock and cream with thyme.
  4. Bake covered at 180°C for 40 minutes, then uncovered for 10 until golden on top.

French crêpesVegetarian

Thin crêpes with lemon and sugar, or banana and melted chocolate

Prep 5 min, cook 16 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the batter and rest it overnight in the fridge, up to 1 day ahead; cook the crepes just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml full cream milk
  • 50g butter, for the pan
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 bananas
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk until smooth.
  2. Rest for an hour.
  3. Cook thin crêpes in a pan brushed with butter, a minute a side.
  4. Fold with a squeeze of lemon and sugar, or with sliced banana and melted chocolate.

It's got raisins in itOn the printed menu

Wobbly green lime jelly set with raisins, two recipes whose pages ran together.

How it is made
  • 2 packets lime jelly crystals
  • 1 cup raisins
  • 1 tin crushed pineapple
  • 1 carrot
  1. Mum's magazine dinner, made the way she made it: two recipes run together and set into a wobbly green ring with raisins. Dissolve 2 packets lime jelly crystals in 500ml boiling water, stir in 250ml cold water and chill until just starting to set. Fold through 1 cup raisins, 1 drained 440g tin crushed pineapple and 1 grated carrot. Pour into an oiled ring mould and chill 4 hours until set. Turn out onto a plate and serve before it leaves the table.

Citron pressé, French fresh lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh lemon juice, sugar syrup and iced water, mixed at the table

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lemons and make the syrup up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; add the iced water at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 6 lemons
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml hot water
  • 1l iced water, to serve
  1. Squeeze the lemons into a jug.
  2. Dissolve the sugar in hot water and cool.
  3. Put the lemon juice, syrup and a jug of iced water on the table so everyone mixes their own glass.
