Baby carrots with herb sour cream dipVegetarian

Baby carrots with a chive, dill and lemon sour cream dip

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bag baby carrots (about 400 g)
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 4 sprigs dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Peel the carrots and stand them upright in a glass.
  2. Mix the sour cream with snipped chives, chopped dill and a squeeze of lemon.

Overnight bus sandwiches

Buttered white bread with ham, cheddar and yellow mustard

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled and wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 200g leg ham, sliced
  • 200g cheddar, sliced
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Butter the bread and fill with ham, cheddar and a stripe of mustard.
  2. Cut in halves and wrap each in baking paper.

Mains

Bacon and scrambled eggs on toast

Crisp bacon and soft scrambled eggs on buttered toast

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 8 eggs
  • 40g butter
  • 4 slices white bread
  • 1 bunch chives
  • salt, to taste
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  2. Whisk the eggs with salt and scramble slowly in butter until soft.
  3. Toast and butter the bread.
  4. Serve on tin plates with chives scattered over the eggs.

Sweets

Dunking doughnutsVegetarian

Fried yeast doughnut rings, golden and soft

Prep 20 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Best fresh on the day, dough can rise in the fridge overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, eggs, butter and warm milk into a soft dough and knead.
  2. Prove the dough until doubled, which is the first resting time.
  3. Roll out and cut into rings.
  4. Prove again until puffy.
  5. Heat the vegetable oil to 175C, fry until golden and roll in cinnamon sugar.
  6. Cool slightly and dunk properly.

Drinks

Cinnamon mochaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong coffee, hot milk and dark chocolate with cinnamon and brown sugar

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml strong coffee
  • 500ml milk
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped, plus extra to grate
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the strong coffee.
  2. Heat the milk with the chocolate, sugar and cinnamon, whisking until melted and steaming.
  3. Whisk in the coffee. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Pour into thick mugs, spoon over the cream and grate a little more chocolate on top.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Peter shows Ellie how to dunk a doughnut.
