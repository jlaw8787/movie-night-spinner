Snacks

Young carrots with herb sour cream dipVegetarian

Sweet baby carrots with a chive, dill and lemon sour cream dip.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch young carrots (about 400 g)
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 4 sprigs of dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Scrub the carrots, trim the tops and stand them upright in a glass, 5 min.
  2. Mix the sour cream with snipped chives, chopped dill and a squeeze of lemon.

Overnight bus sandwiches

Buttered white bread with ham, cheddar and yellow mustard.

Prep 12 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled and wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 200g leg ham, sliced
  • 200g cheddar, sliced
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Butter the bread and fill with ham, cheddar and a stripe of mustard.
  2. Cut in halves and wrap each in baking paper.

Walls of Jericho cheese and pickle toasts

Toasted cheddar and pickle on rye, passed either side of the Walls of Jericho blanket.

How it is made
  • 4 slices rye bread
  • 30g butter
  • 4 tbsp mustard pickle
  • 150g sharp cheddar
  1. Rye bread toasted under sharp cheddar with sweet mustard pickle, cut into fingers. Butter 4 slices of rye, spread with 4 tbsp mustard pickle, cover with 150g grated sharp cheddar and grill until bubbling and spotted brown. Cut into fingers and serve hot.

Mains

Bacon and scrambled eggs on toast

Crisp bacon and soft scrambled eggs on buttered toast.

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 8 eggs
  • 40g butter
  • 4 slices of white bread
  • 1 bunch chives
  • salt, to taste
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp, 10 min.
  2. Whisk the eggs with salt and scramble slowly in butter until soft.
  3. Toast and butter the bread, 4 min.
  4. Serve on tin plates with chives scattered over the eggs.

Roadside diner pot roast with gravy

Beef chuck braised for hours with gravy, a diner stop on the long trip north to New York.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 800g potatoes
  1. Beef chuck seared hard and braised for hours with onion, carrot and stock until it pulls apart, with potatoes cooked in the juices and a thick gravy. Heat the oven to 160°C. Season 1.5kg beef chuck and brown all over in oil in a casserole. Remove. Fry 2 onions and 3 carrots in chunks for 5 minutes, stir in 2 tbsp tomato paste and 2 tbsp plain flour, then 750ml beef stock, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce and thyme. Return the beef, cover and cook 2 hours. Add 800g halved potatoes and cook 1 hour more. Lift out the beef and vegetables and simmer the liquid until it thickens to gravy.

Sweets

Dunking doughnutsVegetarian

Fried yeast doughnut rings, golden and soft.

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fresh on the day, dough can rise in the fridge overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, eggs, butter and warm milk into a soft dough and knead, 15 min.
  2. Prove the dough until doubled, which is the first resting time, 1 hr.
  3. Roll out and cut into rings.
  4. Prove again until puffy, 30 min.
  5. Heat the vegetable oil to 175°C, fry until golden and roll in cinnamon sugar, 15 min.
  6. Cool slightly and dunk properly, 5 min.

Drinks

Cinnamon mochaNo alcohol

Strong coffee, hot milk and dark chocolate with cinnamon and brown sugar.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml strong coffee
  • 500ml milk
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped, plus extra to grate
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the strong coffee.
  2. Heat the milk with the chocolate, sugar and cinnamon, whisking until melted and steaming, 5 min.
  3. Whisk in the coffee. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Pour into thick mugs, spoon over the cream and grate a little more chocolate on top.

When to serve

Serve dessert at the diner breakfast on the road.
