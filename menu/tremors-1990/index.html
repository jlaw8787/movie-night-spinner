Snacks

Val and Earl's pole vault jerky

Soy and Worcestershire beef jerky, chewy enough to last a week up on a boulder.

Prep 20 min, cook 4 hr, 12 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 weeks, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef topside
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Slice the beef thin across the grain.
  2. Marinate with the soy, Worcestershire, sugar and pepper overnight in the fridge.
  3. Dry on racks in a low oven with the door ajar until leathery.

Chang's Market spiced nuts and crackling

Pork crackling with chilli salt, plus honey cayenne roasted peanuts.

Prep 5 min, cook 47 min, 2 hr 5 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork rind
  • 2 tsp salt flakes
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 250g raw peanuts
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Score the pork rind finely, rub with salt and leave uncovered in the fridge, 2 hr 5 min.
  2. Pat dry and roast at 230°C until blistered and crisp. Break into pieces and toss with chilli salt, 35 min.
  3. Toss the peanuts with the honey, cayenne, salt and oil.
  4. Roast at 180°C, stirring once, 12 min.

Mains

Sirloin, sausages, beans and slaw

Grilled sirloin and sausages with molasses baked beans and creamy cabbage slaw.

Prep 13 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Simmer the beans up to 2 days ahead and reheat; dress the slaw up to 3 hours ahead and grill the meat on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baked beans (2 tins)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp molasses
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 2 rump steaks
  • 4 thick beef sausages
  1. Simmer the baked beans with the onion and molasses, 20 min.
  2. Shred the cabbage and toss with the mayonnaise and vinegar.
  3. Grill the rump steaks and sausages over hot coals, 12 min.
  4. Slice the meat to share.

Burt's bunker franks and beans

Canned franks and beans, five years' worth.

How it is made
  • 8 franks
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tins cannellini beans (400g)
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  • 30g butter
  1. Smoky franks browned and simmered with onion, beans, molasses, mustard and smoked paprika until thick and glossy, with warm buttered cornbread. Slice 8 franks into coins and brown in a pot with 1 tbsp oil. Add 1 diced onion and 2 garlic cloves and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tsp smoked paprika, 3 tbsp molasses, 1 tbsp American mustard and 2 tbsp tomato paste. Add 2 drained 400g tins cannellini beans and 250ml stock and simmer 20 minutes until thick. Bake cornbread mix in a hot buttered pan at 200°C for 20 minutes.

Graboid chocolate dirt cupsVegetarian

Chocolate pudding, cookie crumbs, gummy graboids.

Prep 3 min, cook 10 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 100g chocolate biscuits, crushed
  • 1 bag gummy worms
  1. Whisk the milk, sugar, cocoa and cornflour in a pot over medium heat until thick, 10 min.
  2. Pour into four cups and chill, 2 hr.
  3. Top with crushed biscuit and push gummy worms in so they poke out.

Drinks

Perfection, Nevada bourbon iced tea

Honey sweetened iced tea with bourbon, peach and lemon.

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Tea up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 800ml boiling water
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 peach
  • 2 lemons
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water, remove, and stir in the honey.
  2. Cool and chill, 2 hr.
  3. Slice the peach and one lemon. Fill four jars with ice and the slices.
  4. Add bourbon to each, top with the tea, stir and serve with a squeeze of lemon.
