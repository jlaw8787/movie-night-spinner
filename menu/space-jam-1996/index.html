Snacks

Bugs Bunny's carrot sticksVegetarian

Baby carrots and cucumber sticks standing in garlic dill ranch dip cups

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 100ml mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 bag baby carrots
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers, cut into sticks
  1. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, garlic, dill and chives into a ranch dip.
  2. Spoon some into the bottom of small cups.
  3. Stand the carrots and cucumber sticks upright in each cup so everyone gets their own serve.

Basketball cheese ballsVegetarian

Cheddar and cream cheese balls rolled in paprika and corn chip crumbs

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 cup crushed corn chips
  • 1 box water crackers
  1. Mix the cheddar, cream cheese and spring onions and chill until firm enough to roll.
  2. Roll into small balls and coat in the paprika mixed with the crushed corn chips.
  3. Mark basketball lines with a chive or skewer. Chill and serve with water crackers.

Mains

Courtside meatball subs

Beef and pork meatballs in tomato sauce on rolls under melted mozzarella

Prep 30 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Meatballs up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 800g crushed tomatoes
  • 4 long bread rolls
  • 250g mozzarella, sliced or grated
  • 1 cup basil leaves, torn
  1. Mix the minces with the salt and roll into balls.
  2. Bake at 200C until browned.
  3. Simmer the meatballs in the crushed tomatoes.
  4. Pile into split rolls, cover with mozzarella and grill until bubbly. Finish with basil.

Sweets

Monstars chocolate puddingVegetarian

Chocolate pudding cups with cookie crumbs and gummy worms

Prep 15 min, cook 135 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml milk
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 4 chocolate biscuits, crushed
  • 12 gummy worms
  1. Whisk the milk, cornflour and sugar over heat until thick, then stir in the chocolate until smooth.
  2. Pour into cups and chill for 2 hours.
  3. Top with crushed biscuits and push in gummy worms so they look like they are crawling out.

Drinks

Michael's secret stuff orange coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Orange, lime, honey and mint with sparkling water, served in sports bottles

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make fresh, shake bottles on serving
Recipe for 4
  • 6 oranges, juiced
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp torn mint
  • 600ml cold sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Squeeze the oranges and lime into a jug. Stir in the honey until dissolved and add the mint.
  2. Divide between four sports bottles, add ice and top up with sparkling water. Shake gently.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Bugs hands round the water bottle marked Michael's secret stuff.
