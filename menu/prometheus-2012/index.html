Snacks

Isle of Skye smoked salmon oatcakes

Lemon and dill crème fraîche, folds of cured fish, capers and cracked pepper

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the crème fraîche up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; top the oatcakes just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 16 oatcakes
  • 200g smoked salmon slices
  • 1 tbsp baby capers, drained
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Zest the lemon. Mix the crème fraîche with the zest, a squeeze of lemon juice, a tablespoon of chopped dill and pepper.
  2. Spread over the oatcakes and top each with a fold of salmon, a few capers and a dill sprig. Finish with cracked pepper.

Janek's Christmas tree pigs in blankets

Chipolatas wrapped in streaky bacon, roasted sticky with honey and grain mustard

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Wrap the chipolatas up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; glaze and roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 pork chipolatas
  • 6 rashers streaky bacon
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut the bacon in half lengthways and wrap a strip around each chipolata. Lay on an oiled tray, seam side down.
  2. Mix the honey and mustard.
  3. Roast for 15 minutes.
  4. Brush with the glaze and roast until sticky and browned.
  5. Pull the rosemary into small sprigs and use them to spear each one.

Christmas Day landfall beef Wellington

Weyland's money buys the good fillet, even this far from Earth

Prep 30 min, cook 68 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Sear, wrap in prosciutto and duxelles and chill up to 1 day ahead; wrap in pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef eye fillet, centre cut
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 500g button mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 25g butter
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 8 slices prosciutto
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the beef well with salt and pepper. Sear in the oil in a hot pan until browned all over, then brush with the mustard.
  2. Blitz the mushrooms and garlic to a fine crumb. Cook in the butter with the thyme until all the liquid has gone and it holds together. Season.
  3. Cool the beef and mushrooms completely.
  4. Overlap the prosciutto on a sheet of cling film, spread with the mushrooms, sit the beef on top and roll up tightly in the film. Chill to firm.
  5. Heat the oven to 210C. Join the pastry sheets, unwrap the beef onto it and wrap, trimming the excess and sealing the seams underneath. Brush with egg and score the top.
  6. Bake on a hot tray until deep golden, about 35 minutes for medium rare.
  7. Rest before cutting into thick slices.

David's Lawrence of Arabia chicken

David, alone on the ship for two years, bleaching his hair to look like Peter O'Toole

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Rub the chicken with the spices up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 2 tsp baharat spice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 30g butter
  • 50g pistachios
  • 50g dried cranberries
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove
  1. Rub the chicken with the baharat, oil, lemon juice and salt and roast at 200C for 40 minutes until crisp.
  2. Soak the saffron in 2 tbsp hot water. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, rest for 5, then stir through the saffron and butter.
  3. Fold the chopped pistachios, cranberries and parsley through the rice.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with the crushed garlic and a pinch of salt.
  5. Serve the chicken on the jewelled rice with the garlic yoghurt.

Sweets

Black goo chocolate potsVegetarian

One drop changes everything, so keep the lids on

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and chill up to 2 days ahead, covered, the chill takes 3 hours; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, 70 percent, chopped
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 40g caster sugar
  • salt, a pinch
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 125g blackberries
  1. Heat the oven to 150C. Chop the chocolate into a heatproof bowl.
  2. Heat the cream and milk until steaming and pour over the chocolate. Stir until smooth.
  3. Whisk the yolks, sugar and salt, then whisk in the chocolate cream. Strain into 4 ramekins and sit them in a deep tray. Pour hot water halfway up the sides.
  4. Bake until set at the edges with a wobble in the middle.
  5. Lift out of the water and cool.
  6. Chill until set.
  7. Top each pot with a spoon of crème fraîche and a few blackberries.

Holloway's spiked nightcapVegetarian

David adds one drop when nobody is looking

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the honey syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep it in the fridge; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml honey
  • 60ml hot water
  • 240ml Scotch whisky
  • 3 lemons, juiced to make 80ml
  • 4 tsp blackcurrant cordial
  • 3 cup ice
  1. Stir the honey into the hot water until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Juice the lemons.
  3. Shake the whisky, honey syrup and lemon juice hard with half the ice.
  4. Strain into 4 tumblers over the rest of the ice and let a teaspoon of blackcurrant cordial sink into each.
