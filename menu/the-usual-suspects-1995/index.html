Keyser Soze Turkish lamb pide

The Turkish crime lord nobody has seen

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cook the spiced lamb up to 2 days ahead and chill; fill and bake the pide to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 handful flat-leaf parsley
  1. Brown the lamb with the onion, half the garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli, then stir in the tomato paste and cook until thick.
  2. Split the dough into 4 and roll each into a long oval.
  3. Spoon the lamb down the middle, fold the sides in and pinch the ends into boat shapes, then brush the edges with egg.
  4. Bake at 230C until the crust is golden and crisp.
  5. Toss the red onion with the sumac, stir the rest of the garlic through the yoghurt, and top the pide with both and the parsley.

Emerald beef empanadas

The Argentinians had no cocaine

Prep 30 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and the chimichurri a day ahead; fill and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 1 red capsicum, finely diced
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 60g green olives, chopped
  • 4 sheets frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh oregano leaves, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 125ml olive oil
  1. Brown the beef with the onion and capsicum, add the paprika and cumin and cook until dry, then cool and fold in the egg and olives.
  2. Cut 12cm rounds from the pastry, fill each with a spoonful, fold over and crimp the edges.
  3. Brush with egg and bake at 200C until deep golden.
  4. Stir the parsley, oregano, garlic, vinegar and oil with a pinch of salt and chilli to make the chimichurri, and serve it alongside.

Hungarian beef goulash

A burned Hungarian saw the devil's face

Prep 20 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Make the goulash up to 2 days ahead; it is better reheated. Cook the noodles to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, cut in 4cm pieces
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 375g wide egg noodles
  • 30g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Brown the beef in batches in the oil and set aside.
  2. Cook the onions slowly in the same pot until soft and golden, then add the garlic, paprika, caraway and tomato paste for a minute.
  3. Return the beef with the capsicum and stock, cover and simmer gently until the beef is very tender.
  4. Boil the noodles, drain and toss with the butter.
  5. Serve the goulash over the noodles with a spoon of sour cream.

Skokie deep-dish sausage pizza

Verbal's Skokie barbershop quartet

Prep 30 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead and the dough the night before; build and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g Italian pork sausages, skins removed
  • 250g mozzarella, sliced
  • 100g provolone, sliced
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 100g ricotta
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic, oregano and a pinch of salt until thick.
  2. Oil a deep 25cm cake tin and press the dough over the base and 4cm up the sides.
  3. Layer the mozzarella and provolone over the base, then crumble over the sausage with the fennel seeds and dot with ricotta.
  4. Spoon the tomato sauce over the top, scatter with parmesan and bake at 220C until the crust is deep golden.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, then cut into thick wedges.

Gold lighter creme bruleeVegetarian

Verbal lights up with his gold lighter

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake and chill the custards up to 2 days ahead; torch the sugar just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pouring cream
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 90g caster sugar, plus 4 tbsp for the tops
  1. Warm the cream with the vanilla bean until steaming, then take it off the heat.
  2. Whisk the yolks and 90g sugar until pale, then whisk in the warm cream and strain.
  3. Pour into 4 ramekins, set them in a roasting tin of hot water and bake at 150C until just set with a wobble.
  4. Chill well, then sprinkle each with sugar and torch until dark golden.

Kobayashi Porcelain espresso martiniVegetarian

Read the bottom of the coffee cup

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the espresso up to a day ahead; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml fresh espresso, cooled
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 2 tsp sugar syrup
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Shake the espresso, vodka, liqueur and syrup hard with ice in two batches.
  2. Strain into 4 chilled glasses and float 3 coffee beans on each.

When to serve

Pour the coffee when Verbal tells Kujan about picking beans in Guatemala.
