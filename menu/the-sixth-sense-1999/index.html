Roast pork and provolone sliders

Garlic fennel roast pork, broccolini and provolone on small buns

Prep 10 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Pork up to 1 day ahead, reheat in its juices
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pork shoulder
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 150g sharp provolone, sliced
  • 12 slider buns
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Rub the pork with garlic, rosemary and fennel seeds.
  2. Roast at 160°C for 3 hours. Rest and slice thin.
  3. Fry the broccolini in the olive oil with garlic and chilli.
  4. Pile the pork, greens and provolone into the buns and spoon over the pan juices.

Birthday party pigs in blanketsOn the printed menu

Cocktail frankfurts in buttery sesame puff, honey mustard, for the party with the red balloon

How it is made
  • 24 cocktail frankfurts
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar tomato relish
  1. Cocktail frankfurts rolled in buttery puff pastry with a sesame top, baked golden and served hot with honey mustard and tomato relish for dipping. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 2 sheets of puff pastry into 24 strips. Wrap each strip around a cocktail frankfurt and sit seam side down on a lined tray. Brush with beaten egg and scatter with sesame seeds. Bake for 18 to 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Mix 3 tbsp Dijon mustard with 2 tbsp honey and serve alongside a bowl of tomato relish.

Anniversary salmon with beurre blanc

Roast salmon in beurre blanc with crushed baby potatoes

Prep 17 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; crush, cook the salmon and whisk the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 shallot, finely diced
  • 100ml white wine
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 150g cold butter, diced
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes, drain and crush with olive oil and salt.
  2. Season the salmon and roast skin side down on an oiled tray for 8 minutes.
  3. Simmer the shallot in the wine and vinegar until a couple of tablespoons are left. Whisk in the cold butter a piece at a time over low heat.
  4. Blanch the asparagus for 2 minutes. Plate up and spoon the sauce over the fish.

Lynn's kitchen buttermilk pancakesOn the printed menu

Buttermilk pancakes with crisp bacon and maple syrup. Lynn turns her back; every cupboard is open.

How it is made
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g butter
  • 12 rashers streaky bacon
  • 125g blueberries
  • 1 bottle maple syrup
  1. Fluffy buttermilk pancakes stacked high with crisp streaky bacon, a pat of salted butter, fresh blueberries and plenty of warm maple syrup. Whisk 300g plain flour, 2 tbsp caster sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp bicarb and a pinch of salt. Whisk in 500ml buttermilk, 2 eggs and 50g melted butter until just combined and rest for 10 minutes. Fry 12 rashers of streaky bacon until crisp and keep warm. Brush a clean pan with butter and cook 80ml ladles of batter over medium heat for 2 minutes until bubbles show, then flip for 1 minute. Stack with the bacon, butter, blueberries and warm maple syrup.

Red tent chocolate strawberriesVegetarian

Strawberries dipped in dark chocolate and rolled in crushed freeze-dried raspberries

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g dry strawberries
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 1 packet freeze-dried raspberries, crushed
  1. Melt the chocolate.
  2. Dip each strawberry in the chocolate.
  3. Before it sets, roll half of each in the crushed raspberries so they are bright red.
  4. Chill on baking paper until set.

Ghostly white chocolate mousseVegetarian

White chocolate mousse set in glasses, topped with raspberries

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g white chocolate
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 egg white
  • 100g raspberries
  1. Melt the chocolate into a third of the cream, warmed, and cool.
  2. Whip the rest of the cream and whisk the egg white. Fold both through the chocolate.
  3. Spoon into glasses and set in the fridge for 4 hours.
  4. Top with a few raspberries.

Philly winter hot spiced apple ciderVegetarian

Apple juice simmered with cinnamon, cloves, star anise and orange, no alcohol

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Keeps 2 days chilled, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 6 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 3 star anise
  • 2 oranges, sliced
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 120ml dark rum, optional for the adults' mugs
  1. Put the apple juice, two of the cinnamon sticks, cloves, star anise, half the orange slices and the sugar in a pot.
  2. Bring to a simmer, then hold just below a simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Strain into mugs and add a cinnamon stick and orange slice to each. Stir rum into the adults' mugs if you like.
