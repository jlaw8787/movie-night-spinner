Ypres dugout leek and potato soupVegetarian

Leek and potato soup in a hot mug, the only warm thing in a flooded Flanders tunnel.

Prep 9 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 40g butter, plus extra for the bread
  • 2 leeks
  • 1 brown onion
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1.2 L vegetable stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Wash and slice the leeks, chop the onion and dice the potatoes.
  2. Melt the butter and soften the leeks and onion for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the potatoes, thyme and stock and simmer until the potato breaks up, 20 min.
  4. Pick out the thyme, blitz smooth, stir in the cream and season.
  5. Serve in mugs with sliced bread and butter.

Charters Towers corned beef rolls

Corned beef, cheddar, lettuce and mustard pickle in soft rolls.

Prep 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep covered and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 soft white rolls
  • 30g butter
  • 4 tbsp sweet mustard pickle
  • 300g corned beef
  • 100g vintage cheddar
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 tomato
  1. Split and butter the rolls.
  2. Spread each base generously with mustard pickle.
  3. Layer with thickly sliced corned beef, a slice of cheddar and a few leaves of iceberg lettuce.
  4. Add thinly sliced tomato and a crack of pepper, close and cut in half.

Messines trench corned beef hash

Crisp corned beef and potato with a fried egg, for the Red House raiders.

Prep 14 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in a hot pan
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400g corned beef
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 bottle of brown sauce
  1. Dice the potatoes, onion and corned beef.
  2. Boil the potatoes, then drain well, 8 min.
  3. Fry the onion in butter and oil, 5 min.
  4. Add the potatoes and corned beef and press flat.
  5. Cook over medium-high heat, turning in sections, until crusty, 10 min.
  6. Splash with Worcestershire and add chopped parsley.
  7. Fry the eggs and sit one on each serve with brown sauce, 5 min.

Armentières estaminet chicken and leek pie

Chicken and soft leeks in a creamy thyme sauce under golden puff, with buttered peas.

Prep 0 min, cook 61 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 2 sheets of frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 400g frozen peas
  1. Brown the chicken in half the butter, then add the leeks and cook until soft, 12 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream and thyme and simmer until thick. Cool a little, 10 min.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spoon into a pie dish, cover with pastry and brush with beaten egg, 5 min.
  4. Bake until puffed and golden, 30 min.
  5. Boil the peas and toss with the rest of the butter, 4 min.

Fruit cake from home with brandy butterVegetarian

Warm fruit cake with brandy butter and sharp cheddar.

Prep 31 min · Make ahead: Brandy butter up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp brandy
  • 800g fruit cake
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  1. Beat the soft butter with icing sugar until pale, then beat in the brandy a little at a time until fluffy.
  2. Chill the brandy butter for 20 minutes.
  3. Wrap the fruit cake in foil.
  4. Warm at 160°C, 15 min.
  5. Cut into thick slices and serve with the brandy butter and cheddar wedges.

Tunnellers' Anzac biscuitsVegetarian

Chewy oat, coconut and golden syrup biscuits, the kind sent to the front in tins.

Prep 19 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 150g plain flour
  • 60g desiccated coconut
  • 120g brown sugar
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  1. Mix the oats, flour, coconut and brown sugar, 6 min.
  2. Melt the butter with golden syrup, then stir in the bicarbonate of soda dissolved in boiling water so it foams, 3 min.
  3. Stir into the dry mix.
  4. Roll into balls and flatten slightly on lined trays.
  5. Bake at 170°C until golden, 13 min.
  6. Cool on the trays to crisp, 15 min.

Dugout cocoa with condensed milkNo alcohol

Hot milk cocoa with dark chocolate and condensed milk.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 900ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 80g dark chocolate
  • 120ml condensed milk
  1. Heat the milk in a saucepan until steaming but not boiling, 8 min.
  2. Whisk in the cocoa, chopped chocolate and condensed milk until smooth and frothy.
  3. Add a pinch of salt to bring out the chocolate.
  4. Pour into enamel mugs and serve straight away while piping hot.

When to serve

Serve the main when the tunnellers come up for air.
