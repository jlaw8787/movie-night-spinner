Hope woods pan trout with brown butter and almonds

Pan-fried trout with brown butter and almonds, Rambo living off the land in the Hope woods

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Toast the almonds up to 2 days ahead; cook the trout to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 small rainbow trout fillets, skin on
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 60g butter
  • 40g flaked almonds
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Pat the trout dry, season and dust the skin side with the flour.
  2. Fry skin side down in the hot oil until the skin is crisp, then flip for a minute and lift onto plates.
  3. Add the butter and almonds to the pan and cook until the butter smells nutty and the almonds are golden.
  4. Squeeze in the lemon, add the parsley and spoon over the trout.

Smoked salmon and corn chowder in tin mugs

Smoked salmon and corn chowder in tin mugs, December in the Hope woods

Prep 11 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make the base up to a day ahead; reheat and flake in the salmon to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 750ml vegetable stock
  • 250ml pouring cream
  • 2 corn cobs, kernels cut off
  • 250g hot-smoked salmon, flaked
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  1. Melt the butter and soften the onion and celery.
  2. Stir in the flour, then whisk in the stock and cream.
  3. Add the potatoes and corn and simmer until the potatoes are tender.
  4. Flake in the salmon, warm through, season with pepper and scatter with dill.
  5. Ladle into tin mugs and serve hot.

Highway diner patty melts on rye

Beef patty melts with brown onions and Swiss on rye, from the diner up the highway

Prep 5 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead; shape the patties the morning of and griddle to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Cook the onions slowly in half the butter with a pinch of salt until soft and brown.
  2. Shape the mince into 4 thin patties the size of the bread, season with salt, pepper and paprika, and sear hard for 2 minutes a side.
  3. Butter the outside of the bread, then build each melt: cheese, patty, onions, mustard, cheese.
  4. Griddle the melts over medium heat, pressing down, until the bread is crisp and the cheese runs, then cut each into quarters.

Mains

Knife-spear wild hog with charred apples and cider jus

Rambo spears a wild hog for supper

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Rub the pork the night before; roast on the day and make the jus while it rests
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg boneless pork shoulder (or wild boar), skin scored
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 3 red apples, quartered
  • 2 red onions, cut in wedges
  • 250ml dry apple cider
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 125ml milk
  1. Rub the pork all over with salt, the paprika, sugar and fennel, keeping the skin dry.
  2. Roast on a rack at 240°C for 30 minutes to blister the skin, then turn down to 160°C.
  3. Slow roast until a fork twists the meat easily, adding the apples and onions to the tray for the last 40 minutes.
  4. Rest the pork, then put the tray on the stove, add the cider and stock and boil, scraping, until syrupy.
  5. Meanwhile boil the potatoes, then mash with the butter and milk.
  6. Pull the pork into chunks and serve on the mash with the charred apples, onions and jus.

Gas station blaze s'mores skilletVegetarian

Melted chocolate and marshmallow over buttery biscuit crumbs. The gas station goes up in flames.

Prep 5 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Crush the biscuits and toast the crumbs up to 3 days ahead; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 250g large marshmallows
  • 150g digestive biscuits, crushed
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Mix the crushed biscuits with the melted butter and salt and toast in a dry pan until golden.
  2. Warm the cream in a small cast-iron pan, take it off the heat and stir in the chocolate until smooth.
  3. Pack the marshmallows over the chocolate and grill or blowtorch until blackened in spots.
  4. Scatter the crumbs over and serve straight from the pan with spoons.

First-light camp coffee with bourbon and mapleVegetarian

Strong camp coffee with bourbon and maple cream, come first light on the ridge

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the maple cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it cold
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml strong hot black coffee
  • 120ml bourbon
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup, plus extra to serve
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Whip the cream with a tablespoon of the maple syrup until just thick.
  2. Stir the bourbon and the rest of the maple into the hot coffee and pour into 4 enamel mugs.
  3. Float the maple cream on top and drizzle with a little more maple.
