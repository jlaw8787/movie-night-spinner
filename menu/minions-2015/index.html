Snacks

Minion banana fritters with honeyVegetarian

Banana chunks in a light soda water batter, fried and drizzled with honey.

Prep 14 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Crush the peanuts up to 3 days ahead; whisk the batter, cut the bananas and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 170ml soda water
  • 4 bananas
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 50g roasted peanuts
  1. Whisk the flour, cornflour, sugar, salt and soda water into a smooth batter.
  2. Cut the bananas into chunks and crush the peanuts.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C.
  4. Dip the banana in batter and fry in batches.
  5. Drain, drizzle with honey and scatter with peanuts.

Crown jewels cheese strawsVegetarian

Puff pastry twists with cheddar and parmesan.

Prep 13 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 100g cheddar
  • 100g parmesan
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brush the pastry with beaten egg.
  2. Cover with grated cheddar and parmesan.
  3. Cut into thin strips and twist each a few times.
  4. Bake on lined trays until golden.

Villain-Con chilli cheese hot dogs

Frankfurts in buns with beef chilli, cheddar and fried onions, for the Nelsons' road trip.

How it is made
  • 250g beef mince
  • 3 onions
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar mild chilli powder
  • 200g passata
  • 20g butter
  • 8 franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 jar American mustard
  1. Grilled franks in soft buns loaded with quick beef chilli, melted cheddar, sticky fried onions and mustard, wrapped in paper. Fry 250g beef mince with half a diced onion, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp mild chilli powder, add 200g passata and simmer 15 minutes until thick. Slowly fry 2 sliced onions in butter for 15 minutes until golden. Grill 8 franks until they split. Split and toast 8 hot dog buns, add the franks, spoon over chilli, top with 100g grated cheddar, onions and mustard. Wrap in paper.

Queen's coronation chicken

Poached shredded chicken in curry mayo with apricot jam and sultanas.

Prep 9 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken breast
  • 200g mayonnaise
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  • 50g sultanas
  • 300g cooked rice
  • 1 bag salad leaves
  1. Poach the chicken, then cool and shred.
  2. Poach.
  3. Cool.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise, yoghurt, curry powder, jam and sultanas and fold through the chicken.
  5. Serve with rice and salad leaves.

Scarlet's castle roast beef and Yorkshires

Topside cooked medium, with crisp batter puddings, potatoes and pan gravy, fit for the new queen.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef topside
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 300ml beef stock
  1. Beef topside roasted at 200°C to medium, rested, with Yorkshire puddings, roast potatoes and gravy. Rub a 1.5kg beef topside with oil, salt and pepper and roast at 200°C for 50 minutes for medium. Rest 15 minutes. Roast 1kg parboiled potatoes in hot oil for 50 minutes. Whisk 140g plain flour, 4 eggs and 200ml milk into a batter. Heat 1 tsp oil in each hole of a 12-hole muffin tin at 230°C, pour in the batter and bake 20 minutes without opening the oven. Make gravy from the pan juices, 1 tbsp flour and 300ml stock.

Minion hot fudge banana splitVegetarian

Bananas split under vanilla ice cream and hot chocolate fudge.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make the fudge sauce up to 5 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat gently; whip the cream and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream, for the fudge
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 4 bananas
  • 12 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped, for the topping
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Chop the chocolate. Heat the cream for the fudge and pour over the chocolate, stirring into a hot fudge.
  2. Heat.
  3. Whip the cream for the topping. Split the bananas lengthways into long dishes.
  4. Add three scoops of ice cream to each, pour over the fudge and top with cream and a cherry.

Drinks

King Bob's banana caramel thickshakeNo alcohol

Banana, vanilla ice cream, milk and caramel sauce, blended thick.

Prep 0 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the tall glasses in the freezer up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 6 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 5 tbsp caramel sauce
  • 1 can whipped cream
  1. Blend the bananas, ice cream, milk and some of the caramel until thick and smooth.
  2. Drizzle caramel inside four tall glasses and pour in the shake.
  3. Top with whipped cream, a banana slice and a final drizzle of caramel.
