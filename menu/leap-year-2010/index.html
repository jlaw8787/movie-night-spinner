Snacks

Dingle pub soda breadVegetarian

Buttermilk soda bread baked in a round, crusty outside, soft inside.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Best on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 450g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 50g salted butter, to serve
  1. Mix the flour, bicarb soda and salt.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough and shape into a round.
  3. Cut a deep cross and bake at 200°C until it sounds hollow.
  4. Serve warm with butter.

Irish smoked salmon

Buttered brown bread with smoked salmon, capers, lemon and black pepper.

Prep 6 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices brown bread
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Spread the bread thickly with butter.
  2. Top with folds of smoked salmon, a few capers and a crack of pepper.
  3. Squeeze over a little lemon and cut into triangles.

Potato farls with butter and chives

Fried potato cakes, hold the cow pat.

How it is made
  • 750g floury potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 120g plain flour
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Soft potato cakes of buttery mash and flour, cut into wedges and fried in butter until golden, served hot with salted butter and chives. Boil 750g floury potatoes until soft, drain and mash smooth with 40g butter and 1 tsp salt. While warm, mix in 120g plain flour and 2 tbsp chopped chives to a soft dough. Halve and pat each into a 1cm thick round on a floured bench, then cut into quarters. Cook in a dry or lightly buttered pan over medium heat for 4 minutes a side until golden and speckled. Serve hot with butter and more chives.

Mains

O'Bradycallaghan lamb stew

Lamb neck slow-cooked with potatoes, carrots and thyme.

Prep 25 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb neck pieces
  • 1kg potatoes, sliced
  • 3 carrots
  • 1 brown onion
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the potatoes, carrots and onion.
  2. Layer the lamb, potatoes, carrots and onion in a heavy pot with thyme, salt and pepper.
  3. Add the stock, cover and simmer until the potatoes break down and thicken it.
  4. Serve in deep bowls.

Dublin beef and stout pie

Chuck slow cooked in dark beer with onion, carrot and thyme under flaky puff, buttery mash.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg diced beef chuck
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 440ml can stout
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  1. Chuck steak slow cooked in stout with onions, carrots and thyme until dark and rich, baked under a flaky puff pastry lid, with buttery mash. Toss 1.2kg diced chuck in 3 tbsp flour and brown in batches in oil. Fry 2 sliced onions and 2 carrots for 5 minutes, add 1 tbsp tomato paste, 440ml stout, 300ml beef stock and thyme. Return the beef, cover and simmer 2.5 hours until tender, then cool slightly. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spoon into a pie dish, cover with a puff pastry sheet, brush with egg and bake 25 minutes. Serve with mash.

Sweets

Claddagh ring apple crumbleVegetarian

Cinnamon apples under an oat crumble, served with warm custard.

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 5 green apples
  • 30g butter, for the apples
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 70g butter, cold, for the crumble
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 100g plain flour
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 500ml custard
  1. Peel and slice the apples and cook with the butter and cinnamon.
  2. Spoon into a dish. Rub the cold butter into the oats, flour and sugar.
  3. Scatter over the apples and bake at 180°C.
  4. Warm the custard and serve alongside.

Drinks

February 29th Irish coffee

Hot coffee, brown sugar, whiskey and cream.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 800ml hot strong coffee (4 cups)
  • 120ml whiskey, for the grown-ups
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the coffee. Stir the sugar into each cup until dissolved.
  2. Add the whiskey for the grown-ups.
  3. Lightly whip the cream until just thick and pour it gently over the back of a spoon so it floats on top.
