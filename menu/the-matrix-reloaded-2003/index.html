Le Vrai gruyère gougèresVegetarian

Warm choux puffs baked with gruyère, nutmeg and black pepper.

Prep 20 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and recrisp at 180°C for 5 minutes, or freeze the raw mounds and bake from frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml water
  • 60g butter, diced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g gruyère, finely grated
  • nutmeg, a pinch
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C, line 2 trays and grate the gruyère.
  2. Bring the water, butter and salt to the boil. Tip in the flour all at once and beat over the heat for 2 minutes until the dough pulls away from the pan.
  3. Tip into a bowl and cool for 5 minutes.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy, then 60g of the gruyère, the nutmeg and pepper.
  5. Spoon or pipe 20 walnut-sized mounds onto the trays and top with the rest of the cheese.
  6. Bake for 25 minutes until puffed and deep golden, without opening the oven for the first 15.
  7. Serve warm.

Zion temple flatbreads with smoked eggplantVegetarian

Charred eggplant whipped with tahini, lemon and garlic, with warm torn flatbreads.

Prep 10 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; warm the flatbreads just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large eggplants
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • salt, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 4 flatbreads or pitas
  1. Char the eggplants over a gas flame or under a very hot grill, turning, until black and collapsed.
  2. Put them in a bowl, cover with a plate and steam for 10 minutes.
  3. Peel off the skin, chop the flesh and drain in a sieve.
  4. Mash with the tahini, lemon juice, garlic, 2 tbsp of the oil and salt to taste.
  5. Warm the flatbreads in a hot dry pan and brush with 1 tbsp of the oil.
  6. Spoon the dip into a bowl, drizzle with the last of the oil, dust with smoked paprika and parsley and serve with the torn bread.

Merovingian's coq au vin

Chicken braised in red wine with bacon, mushrooms and baby onions, over buttery mash.

Prep 19 min, cook 79 min · Make ahead: Braise up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled, it only gets better; reheat gently and make the mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thigh cutlets, skin on
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g bacon, cut into lardons
  • 30g butter, for the sauce
  • 12 pickling onions, peeled
  • 300g button mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500ml red wine
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 60g butter, for the mash
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 tbsp parsley, chopped
  1. Season the chicken and brown it skin side down in the oil in a large heavy casserole, in batches, then turn and brown the other side. Set aside.
  2. Fry the bacon in the same pot until crisp, then add the butter, onions and mushrooms and colour for 8 minutes. Add the garlic for the last minute.
  3. Stir in the flour and tomato paste for a minute, then pour in the wine and stock, scraping the base. Add the thyme and bay.
  4. Return the chicken skin side up, cover and simmer gently for 35 minutes, then uncover and simmer 10 minutes more to reduce the sauce.
  5. Meanwhile peel and chop the potatoes, boil until tender, drain and mash with the butter and warm milk, seasoning with salt.
  6. Taste the sauce, season, scatter with parsley and serve over the mash.

Persephone's gratin dauphinoisVegetarian

Potatoes baked slow in cream and garlic.

Prep 10 min, cook 82 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and reheat covered, then uncover to colour the top
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 20g butter
  1. Slice the potatoes thin and butter a baking dish.
  2. Warm the cream and milk with the garlic, nutmeg, salt and pepper.
  3. Layer the potatoes in the dish and pour the cream over.
  4. Bake at 170°C until tender all the way through and golden.

Chocolate cakeVegetarian

Dense dark chocolate and almond cake under a glossy chocolate ganache.

Prep 13 min, cook 63 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and glaze up to 2 days ahead and keep covered somewhere cool; bring to room temperature before slicing
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped, for the cake
  • 150g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 50g almond meal
  • 30g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • salt, a pinch
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped, for the ganache
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and grease and line a 20cm round tin.
  2. Melt the chocolate for the cake with the butter in a bowl over simmering water, stirring until smooth.
  3. Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and thick.
  4. Fold in the chocolate, then the almond meal, flour, cocoa and salt.
  5. Bake for 30 minutes until just set with a slight wobble in the middle.
  6. Cool completely in the tin.
  7. Bring the cream to a simmer, pour over the ganache chocolate, stand 2 minutes and stir until glossy. Pour over the cake and leave to set.
  8. Slice with a hot knife.

Merovingian's kir royale

Blackcurrant liqueur topped with chilled sparkling wine and a blackberry.

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Chill the wine and the flutes for at least 15 minutes ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60ml crème de cassis
  • 750ml dry sparkling wine, chilled
  • 4 blackberries
  1. Chill 4 flutes in the freezer.
  2. Pour 15ml of the cassis into each flute.
  3. Top slowly with the sparkling wine.
  4. Drop a blackberry into each glass and serve.
