Snacks

Mindil Beach laksa cups

Coconut chicken laksa with tofu puffs, rice noodles and bean sprouts.

Prep 0 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Simmer the paste, coconut milk and water up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat, then cook the chicken and noodles on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp laksa paste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 300g chicken thighs
  • 100g tofu puffs
  • 200g rice noodles
  • 150g bean sprouts
  • 500ml water
  1. Fry the laksa paste for 2 minutes, add the coconut milk and the water and simmer.
  2. Add the sliced chicken thighs and cook 8 minutes, then the tofu puffs.
  3. Divide the cooked rice noodles into bowls, ladle over the laksa and top with bean sprouts.

Darwin mango and prawn rice paper rolls

Fresh rolls of prawn, mango, cucumber and rice vermicelli.

Prep 35 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; roll up to 4 hours ahead under a damp cloth in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rice vermicelli
  • 400g cooked prawns
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch Vietnamese mint
  • 12 rice paper sheets
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp water
  1. Soak the rice vermicelli in boiling water 5 minutes, drain and cool.
  2. Peel and halve the cooked prawns.
  3. Slice the mangoes and the cucumber into strips.
  4. Dip a rice paper sheet in warm water for 10 seconds, lay flat, add prawns, mango, cucumber, noodles, mint and Vietnamese mint and roll tightly.
  5. Repeat until all the rolls are made.
  6. Mix the fish sauce, the lime juice, the sugar, the water, the chopped chilli and the garlic clove.

Darwin chilli crab sliders

Crab meat in chilli lime mayo in soft slider buns. A Darwin favourite before the road trip.

How it is made
  • 400g cooked crab meat
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle sriracha
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  1. Mix 400g cooked crab meat with 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise, 1 tbsp sriracha, the zest and juice of a lime and chopped coriander. Split 12 slider buns, toast them lightly and fill with shredded lettuce and the crab.

Mains

Tiwi Islands barbecued barramundi

Grilled barramundi topped with a green mango, mint and chilli salad.

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best grilled just before serving; shred the mango and slice the chilli up to 3 hours ahead and dress at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 720g barramundi fillets
  • 1 green mango
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  1. Score the barramundi fillets and season.
  2. Grill skin down over high heat for 5 minutes, flip for 2.
  3. Toss the shredded green mango with mint, the sliced chilli, the juice of the limes and the fish sauce and pile on top.

Lemon myrtle roast lamb for the wedding

Lamb leg rubbed with lemon myrtle, roasted pink over potatoes and pumpkin, salsa verde.

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 1 head garlic
  • olive oil
  • 2 tsp ground lemon myrtle
  • 1 tsp ground pepperberry
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 800g pumpkin
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 tin anchovies
  • 1 lemon
  1. Lamb leg studded with garlic and rubbed with lemon myrtle and pepperberry, roasted pink over potatoes and pumpkin, carved for the whole family with salsa verde. Heat the oven to 200°C. Make 12 small cuts in a 2kg lamb leg and push in slivers from 4 garlic cloves. Rub with 2 tbsp oil, 2 tsp ground lemon myrtle, 1 tsp ground pepperberry and salt. Roast on 1kg potatoes and 800g pumpkin in chunks for 1 hour 20 minutes for medium. Rest 20 minutes. Blitz parsley, mint, 1 tbsp capers, 1 anchovy, lemon juice and 100ml olive oil into salsa verde.

Lauren and Ned's mango pavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue with whipped cream, mango and passionfruit.

Prep 10 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Bake the meringue up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight once cool; whip the cream and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 mangoes
  • 3 passionfruit
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, add the caster sugar a spoon at a time until glossy, then fold in the cornflour and the vinegar.
  2. Shape a round and bake at 120°C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven.
  3. Top with the whipped cream, the sliced mangoes and the passionfruit.

Drinks

Top End frozen mango daiquiri

Frozen mango, white rum and lime with a chilli salt rim.

Prep 9 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the mango chunks up to 1 week ahead; blend with the rum, lime and syrup to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe mangoes
  • 160ml white rum
  • 4 limes
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • chilli flakes, to taste
  • 2 cups ice
  • 1 tbsp salt
  1. Freeze the flesh of the ripe mangoes in chunks for at least 2 hours.
  2. Mix the salt with a little chilli flakes, wet the rims of the glasses with lime and dip.
  3. Blend the frozen mango with the white rum, the lime juice, the sugar syrup and the ice until thick and slushy.
  4. Pour into the glasses and add a lime wheel.
  5. For non-drinkers, leave out the rum and blend in a little extra ice.

When to serve

Serve the pavlova when the wedding finally happens on the Tiwi Islands.
