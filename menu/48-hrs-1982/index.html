Snacks

Torchy's bar peanutsVegetarian

Peanuts roasted in the shell with salt and cayenne

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roast and season up to 3 days ahead, airtight; tip into a bucket on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peanuts in the shell
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Spread the peanuts on a tray and roast at 180°C.
  2. Toss straight away with salt and cayenne. Tip into a bucket and put out a bowl for the shells.

San Francisco sourdoughVegetarian

Warm torn sourdough with whipped roasted garlic butter

Prep 17 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Roast the garlic and whip the butter up to 2 days ahead, covered in the fridge; warm the bread just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 garlic head
  • 125g soft salted butter
  • 1 sourdough round
  1. Wrap the garlic head in foil and roast at 180°C.
  2. Squeeze the soft garlic into the butter and whip.
  3. Warm the sourdough at 200°C.
  4. Tear into chunks and serve with the butter.

Reggie's pressed bologna toastiesOn the printed menu

Bologna, American cheese and yellow mustard on white, griddled in butter under a heavy pan

How it is made
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 200g bologna
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 40g butter
  • yellow mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Bologna and American cheese on white bread with yellow mustard, griddled in butter under a heavy pan until golden, crisp and melted. Spread 8 slices white bread with yellow mustard on one side. Build 4 sandwiches with 3 slices bologna and 2 slices American cheese each. Butter the outsides. Cook in a pan over medium heat with another heavy pan pressed on top for 3 minutes a side until crisp and the cheese melts. Cut into triangles and serve with dill pickles.

Mains

San Francisco cioppino

Seafood stew of crab, clams, mussels, prawns and fish in tomato and white wine

Prep 21 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Make the tomato, fennel and wine base up to 2 days ahead and chill; bring to a simmer and add the seafood on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
  • 250ml white wine
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 1 cooked crab
  • 500g clams and mussels, scrubbed
  • 300g raw prawns
  • 300g firm white fish fillet
  • 1 sourdough loaf
  1. Slice the fennel and onion and crush the garlic.
  2. Soften in oil.
  3. Add the tomatoes, wine and stock and simmer.
  4. Add the crab, then the clams and mussels, then the prawns and fish, cooking until the shells open.
  5. Serve with sliced sourdough.

Jack's 3am bacon, eggs and hash brownsOn the printed menu

Crisp thick rashers, eggs fried in the fat, golden grated potato, buttered toast and hot sauce

How it is made
  • 800g potatoes
  • 12 thick bacon rashers
  • 8 eggs
  • 60g butter
  • 4 slices bread
  • hot sauce
  1. Thick bacon rendered crisp, eggs fried in the fat, golden hash browns of grated potato fried in butter, buttered toast and hot sauce. Grate 800g potatoes and squeeze out as much water as you can in a tea towel, then season. Fry in 40g butter and 2 tbsp oil in 4 flat patties for 6 minutes a side until deep golden. Fry 12 thick bacon rashers until crisp and keep warm. Fry 8 eggs in the bacon fat, basting the tops. Toast and butter 4 slices of bread and serve with hot sauce.

Jailhouse chocolate cakeVegetarian

One-bowl cocoa sponge with buttermilk and coffee, thick fudge icing, for a 48 hour pass

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 60g cocoa, for the cake
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml hot coffee
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 40g cocoa, for icing
  • 60g soft butter
  • 1 tbsp hot water
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a tin. Whisk the flour, sugar, cocoa and bicarbonate of soda.
  2. Add the oil, buttermilk, eggs and hot coffee and whisk to a smooth batter.
  3. Bake.
  4. Cool the cake completely.
  5. Beat the icing sugar, cocoa, butter and hot water until smooth.
  6. Ice the cake.

Drinks

Torchy's bourbon boilermakerVegetarian

Ice cold lager served with a shot of bourbon, bitters and orange peel

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the lagers up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; pour the bourbon shots to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long neck lagers
  • 120ml bourbon
  • 4 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 orange
  1. Chill the lagers until icy.
  2. Pour bourbon into shot glasses and add a dash of bitters to each.
  3. Twist a small strip of orange peel over each and serve with a cold beer.

When to serve

Pour drinks when the prison gates open on the furlough.
