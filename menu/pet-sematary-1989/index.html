Snacks

Porch peanuts, cheddar and pickled onionsVegetarian

Salted peanuts, sharp cheddar and pickled onions.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roast and salt the peanuts up to 2 days ahead, airtight; lay out the board on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peanuts in the shell
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Roast the peanuts at 180°C for 20 minutes and salt while hot.
  2. Lay out on an old tray with pickled onions, a wedge of cheddar and crackers for the porch.

Kite day picnic devilled eggs

Yolks whipped with mayo, mustard and relish, topped with crisp shallots, paprika and chives.

How it is made
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 1 jar sweet pickle relish
  • 2 shallots
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Devilled eggs whipped with mayo, mustard and a little relish, topped with crisp shallots, paprika and chives, packed into a picnic box. Boil 8 eggs for 10 minutes, cool in iced water, peel and halve. Mash the yolks with 4 tbsp mayonnaise, 2 tsp mustard, 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish, salt and pepper until smooth. Pipe back into the whites. Thinly slice 2 shallots and fry in oil until golden and crisp, then drain. Top each egg with paprika, crisp shallots and snipped chives, and chill until the picnic.

Mains

Ludlow baked beans with brown bread

Navy beans baked 4 hours with ham hock and molasses, brown bread, coleslaw.

Prep 15 min, cook 4 hours, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak overnight, bean dish keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g dried navy beans
  • 1 ham hock
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 80ml molasses
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1 L water
  • 1 loaf wholemeal bread
  • 1 bag coleslaw mix
  • 50g butter
  1. Soak the beans overnight in cold water, then drain.
  2. Put the beans, ham hock, onion, molasses, sugar, mustard and water in a covered dish.
  3. Bake at 150°C for 4 hours.
  4. Spread the brown bread with the butter and serve with the beans and coleslaw.

Louis's Thanksgiving turkey breast roast

Herb butter roast turkey breast, cornbread stuffing, mash and gravy, a quiet Thanksgiving.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg turkey breast roll
  • 120g butter
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 300g cornbread
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 egg
  • 450ml chicken stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Turkey breast roasted with herb butter until juicy, with cornbread stuffing, buttery mash, cranberry sauce and a rich pan gravy. Heat the oven to 180°C. Rub a 1.5kg turkey breast roll with 50g butter mashed with sage, thyme and garlic and roast for 75 minutes to 70°C inside, then rest 15 minutes. Mix 300g crumbled cornbread with 1 diced onion softened in butter, chopped sage, 1 egg and 150ml stock and bake for 30 minutes alongside. Mash 1kg potatoes with butter and milk. Make gravy from the tray juices, 1 tbsp flour and 300ml stock. Serve with cranberry sauce.

Sweets

Church's blueberry muffinsVegetarian

Blueberry muffins baked high with a crunchy sugar crust.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; warm in a 160°C oven for 5 minutes to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 200g blueberries
  • 2 tbsp cinnamon crumble topping
  1. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder. Stir in the buttermilk, butter and egg briefly.
  2. Fold in the blueberries and spoon into a 12-hole tin.
  3. Top with cinnamon crumble and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  4. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes.

Route 15 apple crispVegetarian

Cinnamon apples under a buttery oat crumble topping.

Prep 18 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Toss the apples and make the crumble topping up to 1 day ahead, store separately in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 apples, sliced
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 100g cold butter, cubed
  • 80g plain flour
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 300ml pouring cream
  1. Toss the apples with cinnamon, sugar and lemon juice in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, oats and brown sugar and scatter over.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 40 minutes.
  4. Serve with pouring cream.

Drinks

Jud's porch lager and apple cider shandy

Lager, sparkling apple cider and lemon, for when Jud says come and have a beer.

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Put the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; pour and serve straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles lager, 200ml needed per glass
  • 600ml sparkling apple cider
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 green apple
  1. Put four pint glasses in the freezer for 30 minutes.
  2. Pour cold lager into each, then top slowly with chilled sparkling apple cider.
  3. Add a squeeze of lemon and a thin slice of green apple and serve straight away.
