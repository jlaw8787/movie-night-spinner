Mozzarella risotto croquettes (supplì)

Fried risotto ovals with a mozzarella centre.

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the risotto the day before, fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 750ml chicken stock, hot
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 150g mozzarella, cut into cubes
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100ml napoli sauce
  1. Cook the rice into a risotto with the hot stock and parmesan. Spread out and chill, ideally overnight.
  2. Roll into ovals around a cube of mozzarella.
  3. Coat in beaten egg and panko.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until golden.
  5. Warm the napoli sauce and serve alongside.

Vespa ride bruschettaVegetarian

Toasted ciabatta rubbed with garlic, topped with tomato and basil.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Topping up to 2 hours, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 ciabatta
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 4 ripe tomatoes
  • 6 basil leaves
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the ciabatta, brush with oil and toast until golden.
  2. Rub each slice with a cut garlic clove.
  3. Chop the tomatoes with torn basil, oil and salt and pile on top.

Whipped ricotta, fig and honey crostiniOn the printed menu

Toasted bread with whipped ricotta, fresh figs and honey.

How it is made
  • 1 baguette
  • 250g fresh ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • 5 fresh figs
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 30g pistachios
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • olive oil
  1. Toasted baguette spread with lemony whipped ricotta, topped with fresh fig, a drizzle of honey, crushed pistachios and a few thyme leaves. Slice 1 baguette into 20 thin rounds, brush with olive oil and toast at 200°C for 8 minutes. Beat 250g fresh ricotta with the zest of 1 lemon, 2 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt until fluffy. Spread on the toasts. Cut 5 fresh figs into quarters and press one onto each. Drizzle with 2 tbsp honey, then scatter 30g crushed pistachios and a few thyme leaves. Serve with a glass of something sparkling.

Spaghetti cacio e pepeVegetarian

Spaghetti tossed with cracked black pepper, butter and cheese.

Prep 2 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 30g butter
  • 150g pecorino, finely grated
  1. Cook the spaghetti and keep a cup of the pasta water.
  2. Toast the pepper in butter.
  3. Add the pasta and a splash of the water, then toss in the pecorino off the heat until creamy.
  4. Serve straight away.

Barge party chicken cacciatoreOn the printed menu

Thighs braised in tomato, red wine, olives and capers, crusty bread, for Joe's big scoop.

How it is made
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 125ml red wine
  • 800g tinned tomatoes
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 80g kalamata olives
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 ciabatta
  1. Chicken thighs browned and braised in tomato, red wine, olives, capers and rosemary until falling apart, with crusty bread for the sauce. Season 8 bone-in chicken thighs and brown well in 2 tbsp olive oil for 8 minutes. Remove. Cook 1 sliced onion, 1 sliced red capsicum and 3 garlic cloves for 5 minutes. Add 125ml red wine and reduce by half, then 800g tinned tomatoes, 2 rosemary sprigs, 80g kalamata olives and 1 tbsp capers. Return the chicken, cover and simmer 40 minutes, uncovering for the last 10 to thicken. Scatter with parsley and serve with ciabatta.

Spanish Steps gelatoVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream and lemon sorbet with crushed amaretti.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Serve at once
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml lemon sorbet
  • 6 amaretti biscuits
  • 8 waffle cones
  1. Scoop the vanilla ice cream and lemon sorbet into waffle cones or small glasses.
  2. Crush the amaretti biscuits and scatter over the top.

Shakerato iced espressoVegetarianNo alcohol

Double espresso shaken with sugar syrup and ice until frothy.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml espresso, about 4 double shots
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Chill 4 martini glasses.
  2. Pull the espresso.
  3. Working in two batches, shake half the espresso and syrup hard with ice until thick and foamy, then strain into two glasses. Repeat.
  4. Twist a strip of lemon peel over each glass and serve immediately.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Ann sits on the Spanish Steps.
