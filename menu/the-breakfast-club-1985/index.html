Snacks

Andrew's triple-stack ham and cheese

Three toasted layers of ham, Swiss and cheddar with mustard mayo and pickles, a wrestler's lunch.

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce up to 2 days ahead, chilled; toast and build the stacks just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 12 slices of white bread
  • 400g leg ham
  • 4 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 2 apples
  • 1 lemon
  1. Mix the mayo with the wholegrain mustard.
  2. Toast the bread, 8 min.
  3. Build four stacks: toast, sauce, ham and Swiss, toast, sauce, ham, cheddar and pickles, then toast, 10 min.
  4. Press lightly, cut each into three fingers and pin with toothpicks.
  5. Slice the apples and toss with lemon juice to serve alongside.

Bender's share plate

Crackers, cheese and salami on a plate in the middle for anyone who forgot lunch.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Cube the cheddar up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; plate up just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 80g crackers
  • 100g cheddar, cubed
  • 80g sliced salami
  1. Cube the cheddar.
  2. Arrange the crackers, cheddar and salami on a shared plate in the middle of the table.

Claire's salmon sushi bento

Salmon and avocado maki with nigiri, soy and wasabi in a box, the lunch Bender mocks.

How it is made
  • 400g sushi rice
  • 80ml rice vinegar
  • 3 nori sheets
  • 300g sashimi salmon
  • 1 avocado
  • soy sauce
  • 1 tube wasabi
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  1. Salmon and avocado maki with salmon nigiri, soy, wasabi and pickled ginger, packed neatly into a bento box like it's Saturday detention at Shermer High. Rinse 400g sushi rice, cook with 480ml water, then fold through 80ml rice vinegar mixed with 1 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp salt and cool. Lay rice over 3 nori sheets, add strips of salmon and avocado, roll tight with a mat and cut each into 8. Shape 8 small rice oblongs, dab with wasabi and top with sliced sashimi salmon. Pack into 4 bento boxes with soy, wasabi and pickled ginger.

Mains

Brian's lunch

Soup in a thermos, PB and J with the crusts cut off.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Soup up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 800g tomatoes
  • 1 brown onion, quartered
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 80g peanut butter
  • 60g jam
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Halve the tomatoes and roast with the onion, garlic and oil at 200°C until softened and browned.
  2. Transfer to a pot with the stock and simmer, 10 min.
  3. Blitz smooth with the cream and season. Pour into warmed thermoses.
  4. Make four peanut butter and jam sandwiches with the white bread, cut off the crusts and serve with the soup.

Allison's cereal-crusted French toast

Her sugar and cereal sandwich, if you dare.

How it is made
  • 4 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ground cinnamon
  • 120g sweet corn cereal
  • 1 brioche loaf
  • 40g butter
  • 1 can whipped cream
  • 250g mixed berries
  • maple syrup
  1. Thick brioche dipped in vanilla custard, crusted in crushed sweet cereal and fried golden in butter, with whipped cream, berries and maple syrup. Whisk 4 eggs, 250ml milk, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp vanilla and a pinch of cinnamon. Crush 120g sweet corn cereal into coarse crumbs. Dip 8 thick slices brioche in the custard for 20 seconds a side, then press both sides into the cereal. Fry in 40g butter over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes a side until golden and crunchy. Serve with whipped cream, berries and maple syrup.

Sweets

Detention browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies cut in squares, for Vernon, the brownie hound.

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100g butter
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g plain flour
  • pinch of salt
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line an 18cm square tin. Melt the chocolate and butter gently and cool slightly, 8 min.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour and salt.
  3. Bake until the edges are set and the middle gently wobbles.
  4. Cool before cutting into four portions, 30 min.

Drinks

Detention strawberry milkshakeNo alcohol

Strawberries blended with milk and vanilla ice cream. Brian asks if milk will be made available.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best blended fresh; hull the strawberries up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries
  • 6 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 can whipped cream
  1. Hull the strawberries, keeping four for garnish.
  2. Blend the rest with the ice cream, milk and sugar until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses, top with whipped cream and a strawberry on the rim, and serve with straws.
