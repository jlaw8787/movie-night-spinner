Snacks

Lights-out tomato soup and cheese toastiesVegetarian

Cheddar toasties fried in butter and cut into fingers, with cups of roast tomato soup for dunking.

Prep 8 min, cook 62 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead and reheat; build and toast the toasties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 40g butter, soft
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Toss 1kg halved tomatoes, the onion and garlic with 2 tbsp olive oil in a roasting tin.
  2. Roast until soft and catching at the edges.
  3. Blend with 500ml vegetable stock, the sugar and salt, then simmer in a pot.
  4. Butter the outsides of 8 slices of bread and fill 4 sandwiches with 200g grated cheddar.
  5. Toast in a frying pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side until golden and melted, in batches.
  6. Cut into fingers and serve with the soup in cups.

Freddy's striped jumper skewersVegetarian

Cherry tomatoes, basil, bocconcini and green olives threaded in stripes, with balsamic glaze.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Thread up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge; dress with oil and glaze just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 220g baby bocconcini, drained
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 16 large green olives, pitted
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 16 small skewers
  1. Thread a cherry tomato, a basil leaf, a bocconcini and an olive onto each skewer, repeating so the red and green alternate.
  2. Lay on a platter, drizzle with the olive oil and balsamic glaze and sprinkle with salt.

Freddy's boiler room Salisbury steak

Beef and onion patties in a rich mushroom gravy with buttery mash and peas.

Prep 20 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; cooked patties in gravy keep 2 days and reheat well; mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion, finely diced, for the patties
  • 1 onion, sliced, for the gravy
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Mix 600g beef mince with the breadcrumbs, egg, diced onion, half the salt and the pepper, and shape into 4 thick ovals.
  2. Brown the patties in the oil for 4 minutes a side and set aside.
  3. Fry the sliced onion and mushrooms in the same pan until golden.
  4. Stir in the flour and tomato paste, add 500ml beef stock a little at a time, then return the patties and simmer covered. Meanwhile boil the potatoes until tender.
  5. Drain and mash the potatoes with the butter, milk and the rest of the salt, and cook the peas.
  6. Serve the patties and gravy over the mash with the peas.

Kristen's 1428 Elm Street gingerbread housesVegetarian

Gingerbread 1428 Elm Streets, iced white. Watch the windows at the very end.

Prep 20 min, cook 22 min, then 50 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the dough up to 3 days ahead; baked biscuits keep 1 week airtight; ice up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 125ml golden syrup
  • 1 egg, separated
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  1. Beat the butter and brown sugar until pale, then beat in the golden syrup and egg yolk.
  2. Sift in the flour, bicarb, ginger, cinnamon and cloves and mix to a soft dough.
  3. Wrap and chill.
  4. Heat the oven to 180°C. Roll the dough 4mm thick between sheets of baking paper and cut into house shapes with a knife and a card template.
  5. Bake until firm at the edges.
  6. Cool on a rack.
  7. Beat the icing sugar with the egg white and lemon juice until stiff, then pipe on roof lines, windows and a front door.

Westin Hills stay-awake iced coffeeNo alcohol

Cold brew, vanilla and milk on ice, for Kristen, who fights the orderlies to stay awake.

Prep 8 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep the coffee at least 12 hours ahead; strained, it keeps 4 days in the fridge, and the vanilla syrup keeps 2 weeks
Recipe for 4
  • 100g ground coffee, coarse
  • 800ml cold water
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml hot water
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250ml cold milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir 100g coarse ground coffee into 800ml cold water, cover and refrigerate overnight.
  2. Strain through a paper filter.
  3. Stir the sugar into 60ml hot water until dissolved, add the vanilla and cool.
  4. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice, pour in the cold coffee and syrup and finish with a splash of cold milk.
