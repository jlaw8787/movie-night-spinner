Snacks

Golden friesVegetarian

Thin-cut fries, twice fried and salted straight from the oil.

Prep 7 min, cook 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg russet potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries and soak in cold water.
  2. Dry well.
  3. Heat the oil to 140°C and fry the potatoes, then rest.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until golden and crisp.
  5. Salt straight away and serve hot.

Beef patties from the corner store

Spiced beef, curry powder and thyme in flaky golden pastry.

Prep 20 min, cook 43 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400g beef mince
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 125ml beef stock
  • 30g dry breadcrumbs
  • 4 sheets of frozen shortcrust pastry
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  1. Soften the onion in the oil, then brown the mince with the curry powder, thyme and salt.
  2. Add the stock and simmer until almost dry, then stir in the breadcrumbs and cool.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brush the pastry sheets with turmeric mixed into the beaten egg and cut two 12cm rounds from each.
  4. Spoon the filling onto half of each round, fold over and crimp with a fork. Brush the tops with the rest of the egg.
  5. Bake on lined trays until golden and flaky, about 25 minutes. Serve hot with hot sauce.

Mains

Big mick

Double beef, cheese, pickles, shredded lettuce, onion and house sauce on a three-piece bun. No sesame seeds.

Recipe for 4
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sweet relish
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 6 three-piece sesame burger buns
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 12 dill pickle slices
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, relish, mustard, paprika and vinegar for the sauce.
  2. Shape the mince into 8 thin patties.
  3. Smash the patties in a hot pan and add the cheese.
  4. Toast the buns.
  5. Build each with sauce, lettuce, onion, pickles and patties in the three-piece bun.

Sweets

Apple turnoversVegetarian

Flaky puff pastry filled with buttery cinnamon apple.

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Cook the apples with the butter, sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Cool.
  3. Cut the pastry into quarters, fill, fold into triangles and crimp.
  4. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C.

Drinks

Vanilla thick shakeNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream and cold full-cream milk, blended thick.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5 L vanilla ice cream
  • 600ml cold full cream milk
  1. Chill four tall glasses.
  2. Blend three scoops of ice cream with some of the milk until thick and smooth, one shake at a time.
  3. Pour into a chilled glass and serve with a wide straw.

When to serve
