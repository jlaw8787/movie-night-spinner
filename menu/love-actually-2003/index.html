Snacks

Nativity lobster bites

Lobster in lemon chive mayo piled into toasted mini brioche buns.

Prep 16 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lobster tails, cooked
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 6 brioche buns
  • 1 pinch smoked paprika
  1. Chop the lobster meat and chives. Zest the lemon.
  2. Toss the lobster with mayonnaise, zest and chives.
  3. Split and toast the brioche buns, 6 min.
  4. Pile in the lobster and dust with paprika.

Hot buffalo wings and blue cheese

Crisp wings in hot sauce butter with blue cheese dip, for the end of term.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken wings
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 120g sour cream
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch celery
  1. Chicken wings baked at 220°C for 45 minutes, tossed in hot sauce melted with butter, with blue cheese dip. Pat 1.5kg chicken wings dry and toss with 1 tbsp baking powder and 1 tsp salt. Bake on a rack over a tray at 220°C for 45 minutes, turning once, until crisp. Melt 60g butter with 125ml hot sauce. Mix 100g crumbled blue cheese with 120g sour cream, 2 tbsp mayo, a squeeze of lemon and pepper for the dip. Toss the hot wings in the sauce and serve with the dip and celery sticks.

London Christmas roast

Turkey breast rolled around sage stuffing, roasted with potatoes.

Prep 25 min, cook 2 hr 5 min · Make ahead: Make the stuffing, then stuff, roll and tie the turkey up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge. Peel the potatoes and trim the sprouts up to 1 day ahead, potatoes in cold water. Roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg turkey breast
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tbsp chopped sage
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g brussels sprouts
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 jar cranberry sauce
  1. Fry the chopped onion with sage and mix with the breadcrumbs for stuffing, 15 min.
  2. Butterfly the turkey, spread with stuffing, roll and tie.
  3. Peel the potatoes and trim the sprouts.
  4. Toss the potatoes in the olive oil and roast with the turkey at 180°C, 1 hr 30 min.
  5. Roast the sprouts for the last stretch, 20 min.
  6. Rest the turkey, slice and serve with cranberry sauce, 5 min.

Christmas Eve coq au vin

Chicken braised in red wine with bacon, mushrooms and baby onions.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg chicken thigh cutlets
  • 150g bacon lardons
  • 12 pickling onions
  • 250g button mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml red wine
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  1. Coq au vin of chicken, bacon, mushrooms and red wine simmered 90 minutes, with crusty bread. Brown 1.2kg chicken thigh cutlets in a heavy pot and set aside. Fry 150g bacon lardons, 12 peeled pickling onions and 250g button mushrooms until golden. Stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, then 500ml red wine, 250ml chicken stock, 2 garlic cloves and thyme. Return the chicken, cover and simmer 90 minutes until very tender. Season, scatter with parsley and serve with a warm crusty baguette.

Downing Street biscuitsVegetarian

Buttery rice flour shortbread fingers, half dipped in melted chocolate.

Prep 12 min, cook 33 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 50g rice flour
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flours.
  2. Press into a tin.
  3. Bake at 160°C, 25 min.
  4. Cut into fingers while warm and cool, 10 min.
  5. Melt the chocolate and dip half of each finger, 8 min.

Christmas trifle

Sponge soaked in sherry, raspberry jelly, custard and whipped cream.

Prep 16 min, 11 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sponge cake
  • 100ml sweet sherry
  • 1 packet raspberry jelly crystals
  • 500ml custard
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 125g raspberries
  1. Cube the sponge and press into a glass bowl, then splash with sherry.
  2. Make up the jelly and pour over the sponge.
  3. Chill until set, 3 hr.
  4. Spoon over the custard and top with whipped cream.
  5. Chill overnight and top with raspberries.

Mulled wine

Red wine warmed with orange, cinnamon, cloves and honey.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make it up to 2 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge; reheat gently without boiling
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 1 orange
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the orange.
  2. Warm the wine with orange, cinnamon, cloves and honey gently without boiling, 20 min.
  3. Ladle into mugs.
