Snacks

Queensboro Bridge bagels and lox

Toasted with cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and red onion, for the bench at dawn.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 4 bagels
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 small red onion, paper-thin slices
  • 1 handful dill sprigs
  1. Whip the cream cheese with the milk until light.
  2. Split and toast the bagels.
  3. Spread thickly, then layer with salmon, capers, onion and dill.
  4. Cut in half and serve on a board.

Isaac's crostiniVegetarian

Toasted baguette with ricotta and honey, or mushrooms and parmesan, for the opening at Elaine's.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to brush
  • 200g ricotta
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 40g parmesan, shaved
  1. Slice the baguette into 20 rounds, brush with oil and toast at 200°C.
  2. Whip the ricotta with the lemon zest.
  3. Fry the mushrooms with thyme and garlic until golden.
  4. Top 10 toasts with ricotta and a drizzle of honey.
  5. Pile the mushrooms on the other 10 with parmesan.

Mains

Chinese takeaway in bed

Isaac's list has the crabs at Sam Wo's.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in the soy and cornflour up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; slice the spring onions up to 1 day ahead. Cook the rice and stir-fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh, diced
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 10 dried chillies
  • 1 tsp Sichuan pepper
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 80g roasted peanuts
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Marinate the chicken in some of the soy and the cornflour.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Fry the chillies and Sichuan pepper in hot oil, then add the chicken and stir-fry.
  4. Add the vinegar, remaining soy, sugar and Shaoxing and toss until glossy.
  5. Add the peanuts and spring onions and serve with rice.

Isaac's list cheesecakeVegetarian

Dense lemon and vanilla cream cheese filling on a biscuit crumb, baked low and slow, chilled.

Prep 10 min, cook 80 min, then 22.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain biscuits
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 750g cream cheese
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 20cm tin. Bake at 160°C.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with sugar, eggs, sour cream, vanilla and lemon zest.
  3. Pour in and bake at 150°C.
  4. Cool in the oven with the door ajar.
  5. Chill overnight.

Manhattan cocktailVegetarian

Rye, sweet vermouth and bitters stirred and strained over a cherry, to Gershwin.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the rye, vermouth and bitters up to 1 week ahead and keep in the fridge. Stir over ice and strain to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml rye whiskey
  • 120ml sweet vermouth
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  • 1kg ice
  1. Chill four coupes.
  2. Stir the rye, vermouth and bitters over plenty of ice, one or two at a time.
  3. Strain into the coupes and drop in a cherry.

When to serve

Serve dessert as the Gershwin opening fades.
