Snacks

Bedroom floor pepperoni pizza

Pepperoni and mozzarella pizza baked crisp on a tray

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • 150g pepperoni, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Stretch the dough thin on a tray.
  2. Spread with sauce and scatter with mozzarella, pepperoni and oregano.
  3. Bake at 250C until bubbling.
  4. Cut and put back in a box.

Kiss cam ballpark hot dogs

Grilled franks in soft buns with slow-fried onions and yellow mustard

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Onions up to 1 day, grill franks fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Slice the onions and fry slowly in the oil until soft and golden.
  2. Grill the franks until they split.
  3. Tuck into soft buns with the onions and mustard and wrap in paper.

Mains

Peppercorn steak with roast potatoes

Seared sirloin, cracked pepper cream sauce, crisp potatoes, buttered asparagus

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day, cook steak fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns, cracked
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 30g butter
  1. Cut the potatoes into chunks, toss in the olive oil and roast at 220C until crisp.
  2. Sear the steaks and rest.
  3. Toast the cracked peppercorns in the pan, add the cream and simmer until thick.
  4. Steam the asparagus and toss with butter.
  5. Serve on white plates.

Sweets

Office party cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with piped vanilla buttercream and sprinkles

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 125g butter, softened, for the buttercream
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake in a lined muffin tin at 175C.
  3. Cool the cupcakes.
  4. Beat the buttercream butter, icing sugar and vanilla, pipe on top and finish with sprinkles.

Iced salted caramel latte for the bossVegetarianNo alcohol

Iced espresso, milk, salted caramel and cream, handed over each morning

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Caramel up to 1 week chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 60ml thickened cream, for the caramel
  • 20g butter
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 4 double shots espresso
  • 800ml milk
  • 100ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes, to finish
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Melt the sugar in a saucepan until amber, take off the heat and whisk in the cream, butter and salt. Cool.
  2. Pull the espresso.
  3. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice and add caramel and cold milk to each.
  4. Pour the espresso over, top with whipped cream and a pinch of salt.
