Welcome barbecue chicken skewers

Sticky barbecue chicken skewers cut into bites. The Boggses throw Edward a barbecue.

Prep 10 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to a day ahead; thread and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, cut in chunks
  • 125ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 8 bamboo skewers, soaked
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Mix the barbecue sauce, honey, paprika and oil and coat the chicken.
  2. Thread onto the skewers.
  3. Grill, turning and basting with the sauce, until sticky and cooked through, 12 min.
  4. Scatter with spring onion.

Topiary garden green goddess platterVegetarian

Crisp garden vegetables with herby green goddess dip, trimmed into neat shapes.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead; cut the vegetables up to 4 hours ahead and keep in iced water
Recipe for 4
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 cup parsley leaves
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp chives
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 1 bunch baby carrots, trimmed
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut in batons
  • 200g sugar snap peas
  • 1 head broccolini, trimmed
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  1. Blitz the yoghurt, mayo, herbs, lemon and garlic until smooth and green, and season.
  2. Blanch the broccolini and snap peas for a minute, then cool in iced water, 6 min.
  3. Stand the vegetables up in glasses and bowls like a clipped garden around the dip, 8 min.

Come home with me glazed meatloaf

Glazed beef meatloaf with buttery mashed potatoes and peas.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr 20 min · Make ahead: Shape the loaf up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 125ml tomato sauce, plus 2 tbsp for the mix
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled
  • 60g butter
  • 150ml milk
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Mix the mince, onion, carrot, breadcrumbs, egg, thyme and 2 tbsp tomato sauce with salt and shape into a loaf in a lined tin.
  2. Stir the tomato sauce, sugar and vinegar, spread half over the loaf and bake at 180°C, 30 min.
  3. Spread on the rest of the glaze and bake until sticky and cooked through, then rest, 25 min.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes with the butter and milk, cook the peas, and serve thick slices of loaf on top, 25 min.

Christmas ice sculpture glazed ham

Clove-studded maple-glazed ham, carved as fine as an ice angel.

Prep 20 min, cook 1 hr 1 min · Make ahead: Score the ham and make the glaze up to a day ahead; glaze and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2.5kg half leg ham on the bone
  • 125ml maple syrup
  • 1 orange, zested and juiced
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 30 whole cloves
  1. Peel the rind off the ham, leaving the fat, then score the fat in a diamond pattern and stud with cloves.
  2. Simmer the maple, orange, mustard and sugar until syrupy, 6 min.
  3. Bake the ham at 180°C, brushing with glaze every 15 minutes, until deep amber.
  4. Rest and carve at the table, 5 min.

Harmless as cherry pieVegetarian

Lattice cherry pie with vanilla ice cream.

Prep 20 min, cook 1 hr · Make ahead: Make the filling and pastry up to 2 days ahead; build and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen shortcrust pastry
  • 2 x 680g jars of pitted morello cherries, drained, 250ml juice kept
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp raw sugar
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Simmer the cherry juice, sugar and cornflour until thick, then stir in the cherries, lemon and vanilla and cool, 10 min.
  2. Line a pie dish with one pastry sheet, fill with the cherries and cut the other sheet into strips for a lattice.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with raw sugar and bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling, 50 min.
  4. Cool a little and serve with ice cream, 5 min.

Drinks

Pleased as punch pink lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade blushed pink with berries, mint and ice, pleased as punch.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the lemon and berry base up to a day ahead and chill; add the soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 150g raspberries
  • 1 L lemonade, chilled
  • 500ml soda water, chilled
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Crush half the raspberries with the sugar and lemon juice and strain into a punch bowl.
  2. Add the ice, lemonade and soda.
  3. Float the rest of the raspberries and the mint on top and ladle into glasses, 3 min.
