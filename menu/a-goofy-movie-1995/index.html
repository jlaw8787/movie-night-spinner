Snacks

Perfect Cast Fish Fingers

Panko-crusted white fish, lemon and a pickle and caper tartare.

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min
Recipe for 4
  • 600g white fish fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 2 dill pickles, chopped
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  1. Cut the fish into fingers and crumb them in flour, egg, then panko.
  2. Stir the mayonnaise with the pickles and capers for the tartare.
  3. Fry in batches in the vegetable oil at 180°C for about 3 minutes until golden, then drain.
  4. Serve with the tartare and lemon wedges.

Powerline Nachos

Corn chips, spiced beef, black beans, cheddar, guacamole, sour cream, salsa.

Prep 4 min, cook 21 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tsp mild taco spice
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 125ml water
  • 300g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Brown the mince with the onion, 8 min.
  2. Add the taco spice, black beans and water and simmer, 5 min.
  3. Spread the corn chips on a large tray, spoon over the mince and cover with cheddar, then bake until melted.
  4. Mash the avocados with lime and salt, dice the tomatoes with coriander, and top the nachos with guacamole, salsa and sour cream.

Mains

The RV Park Plate

Chargrilled beef sausages in rolls, fried onions, mustard, tomato sauce, buttered corn.

Prep 3 min, cook 32 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  • 8 long rolls
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Grill the sausages over hot coals or a barbecue, turning, until charred and cooked through.
  2. Fry the onions in the oil until soft and sweet.
  3. Grill the corn and brush with butter, 10 min.
  4. Serve the sausages in rolls with the onions, mustard and tomato sauce.

Sweets

Bigfoot S’mores

Toasted marshmallow and milk chocolate between digestive biscuits.

Prep 0 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 16 large marshmallows
  • 32 plain biscuits
  • 200g milk chocolate
  1. Toast the marshmallows on skewers over a gas flame or under a hot grill until golden.
  2. Squash each between two biscuits with a square of milk chocolate and leave 30 seconds so the chocolate starts to melt.

Drinks

Cherry SlushNo alcohol

Cherry syrup blended with crushed ice and a squeeze of lime.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml cherry cordial
  • 600g ice
  • 200ml cold water
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Blend the cordial, ice, water and lime juice until smooth and slushy.
  2. Pour into tall cups and serve with straws before it melts.
