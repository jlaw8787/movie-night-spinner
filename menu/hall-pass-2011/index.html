Not the Olive Garden spinach dipVegetarian

Hot spinach, artichoke and cheese dip with tortilla chips.

Prep 7 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Mix the dip up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; bake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 250g baby spinach
  • 250g cream cheese, softened
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 150g grated mozzarella
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 280g marinated artichoke hearts, drained
  • 1 bag tortilla chips
  1. Wilt the spinach in a dry pan, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, garlic, most of the mozzarella and the parmesan, then stir in the spinach and artichoke.
  3. Spread in a baking dish, top with the rest of the mozzarella and bake at 200°C until bubbling, 20 min.
  4. Serve hot with tortilla chips.

Night one food coma riblets

Sticky barbecue pork riblets and pickles.

Prep 11 min, cook 2 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Bake the riblets in foil up to 2 days ahead and chill; glaze and grill to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork riblets
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 250ml barbecue sauce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Rub the riblets with the paprika, garlic powder, sugar and salt.
  2. Wrap tightly in foil and bake at 160°C until tender, 2 hr.
  3. Brush with barbecue sauce and grill on high until sticky, turning once, 10 min.
  4. Serve with dill pickles.

Mains

Fake chow

Chicken chow mein, soft noodles, cabbage and soy. Nothing fake about this chow.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Slice the chicken and vegetables and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead; stir-fry to order
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chicken thigh fillets, thinly sliced
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 3 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 200g bean sprouts
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  1. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp soy and the cornflour.
  2. Cook the noodles for 1 minute, drain and toss with a little oil.
  3. Stir-fry the chicken in a very hot wok and set aside, 5 min.
  4. Stir-fry the cabbage, carrot, garlic and ginger, 3 min.
  5. Add the noodles, chicken, the rest of the soy, sugar and sesame oil and toss hot, 3 min.
  6. Finish with bean sprouts and spring onion.

Meanwhile, Cape Cod lobster rolls

Lobster in lemon mayo on buttered toasted rolls.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the lobster up to 4 hours ahead and keep it cold; toast the rolls to order
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked lobster meat
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp snipped chives
  • 4 soft long rolls
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bag potato crisps
  1. Chop the lobster and mix with the mayonnaise, lemon zest and juice, celery and chives; chill, 10 min.
  2. Butter the sides of the rolls and toast in a pan until golden, 5 min.
  3. Fill with the lobster and serve with lemon wedges and crisps.

Gary's golf course browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies, nothing extra baked in, safe for a round of golf.

Prep 10 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; they cut cleaner the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 80g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter, 5 min.
  2. Whisk in the sugar, then the eggs one at a time and the vanilla.
  3. Fold in the flour, cocoa and a pinch of salt and spread in a lined 20cm tin.
  4. Bake at 175°C until just set, then cool and cut into squares, 28 min.

Leigh's latteNo alcohol

Double espresso under silky steamed milk, from the Aussie barista Rick keeps dropping in on.

Prep 6 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best made to order; warm the cups while the machine heats
Recipe for 4
  • 8 shots of espresso
  • 600ml full cream milk
  1. Pull a double espresso into each warm cup, 4 min.
  2. Steam the milk until silky with only a little foam, 3 min.
  3. Pour the milk into the coffee in a steady stream.
