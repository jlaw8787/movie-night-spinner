Little Red's basket cheese and herb bunsVegetarian

Little Red filling her basket at the bakery for Granny, and eating half of it on the way

Prep 25 min, cook 117 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep in an airtight tin; warm at 180C for 5 minutes. The dough can do its first prove overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, warm milk and butter and knead for 10 minutes until smooth. Knead in two thirds of the cheddar with the chives and thyme.
  2. Cover and prove until doubled.
  3. Divide into 12 and roll into tight balls. Set them close together in a lined 23cm square tin.
  4. Cover and prove until puffy and touching. Heat the oven to 200C.
  5. Brush with the egg, scatter with the rest of the cheddar and bake until golden.
  6. Cool for 10 minutes, then pull apart into a cloth-lined basket.

Jack's cow-for-beans broad bean crostiniVegetarian

Jack swapping his cow for a handful of beans

Prep 20 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Crush the beans up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge with a film of oil on top; toast the bread up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen broad beans
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 tbsp mint, chopped
  • 80ml extra virgin olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 baguette, in 1cm slices
  • 150g ricotta
  1. Cook the broad beans in boiling water for 3 minutes, drain and cool in cold water.
  2. Pop the bright green beans out of their pale skins.
  3. Crush roughly with the garlic, lemon zest and juice, mint and half the oil, and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Brush the baguette slices with the rest of the oil and toast under the grill until golden.
  5. Spread each toast with ricotta and pile on the crushed beans.

Granny's cottage chicken and mushroom pot pie

Granny's cottage on the far side of the forest

Prep 25 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g boneless chicken thighs, in 3cm pieces
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 250g button mushrooms, halved
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 sprigs thyme, leaves picked
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Season the chicken and brown it in the butter and oil in a large frying pan. Lift out.
  2. Soften the leek, button mushrooms, garlic and thyme in the same pan.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream and mustard. Return the browned filling and simmer until thick.
  4. Stir in the parsley, season and spoon into a 2 litre pie dish. Cool for 10 minutes.
  5. Heat the oven to 200C. Lay the pastry over the top, overlapping the sheets, trim and press the edges, brush with the egg and cut a small steam hole.
  6. Bake until puffed and deep golden.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

King's festival roast pork with apples

Crackling loin on apples, onions and sage, cider gravy, buttered greens, before the pitch trap

Prep 20 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Score and salt the pork skin the day before and leave uncovered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork loin, skin on
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 4 apples
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 250ml dry cider
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300g green beans
  • 200g baby spinach
  • 20g butter
  1. Heat the oven to 240C. Score the skin, rub with the salt and sit the pork on the quartered apples, onions and sage in a roasting tin.
  2. Roast for 30 minutes until the skin crackles, then turn down to 180C.
  3. Roast for 1 more hour, then rest for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir the flour into the pan juices on the stove, add the cider and simmer for 4 minutes for gravy.
  5. Boil the beans for 4 minutes, then toss with the spinach and butter until wilted.
  6. Carve and serve with the roast apples, gravy and greens.

Rapunzel's golden apple plaitVegetarian

Rapunzel's long golden hair hanging from the tower window

Prep 30 min, cook 165 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep wrapped; the dough can do its first prove overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 400g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150ml warm milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g butter, softened, for the dough
  • 30g butter, softened, for the filling
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 green apples, peeled and grated
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, caster sugar, salt, warm milk and eggs, then knead in the dough butter bit by bit until smooth and glossy.
  2. Cover and prove until doubled.
  3. Squeeze the apple dry. Roll the dough out to 40cm by 30cm, spread with the filling butter and scatter with the brown sugar, cinnamon and apple.
  4. Roll up from a long side, cut in half lengthways and twist the two strands together, cut sides up, into a long plait. Set on a lined tray.
  5. Cover loosely and prove until puffy. Heat the oven to 180C.
  6. Bake until deep golden and cooked through.
  7. Brush with the warmed honey, cool a little and serve in slices with the cream, lightly whipped.

Milky White's vanilla milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla shake for Milky White, as white as milk

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the milk and glasses up to 1 day ahead; blend to order
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml cold milk
  • 6 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • pinch of ground nutmeg
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream, vanilla and honey until thick and frothy.
  2. Pour into 4 tall chilled glasses.
  3. Dust each with a pinch of nutmeg and serve with a straw.
