Chicago seafood restaurant oysters

Fresh shucked oysters on crushed ice with an eschalot and cracked pepper dressing

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Shuck just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 12 oysters, freshly shucked
  • 2 eschalots
  • 100ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns, cracked
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 cups crushed ice
  1. Finely dice the eschalots and stir into the vinegar with the cracked black peppercorns.
  2. Lay the oysters on crushed ice.
  3. Spoon a little dressing over each and serve with lemon wedges.

Karaoke bar wings

Sticky honey, soy and garlic wings, baked crisp, with sesame

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the wings with salt and bake at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Warm the honey, soy sauce and crushed garlic until sticky.
  3. Toss the wings through the glaze and scatter with sesame seeds.

Chicago-mix caramel cheese popcornOn the printed menu

Half in baked brown sugar toffee, half in butter and cheddar powder, tossed together

How it is made
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 100g butter
  • 120g brown sugar
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar cheddar cheese powder
  1. Fresh popcorn split in two: half tossed in bubbling caramel and baked crisp, half tossed in butter and cheddar powder, then mixed together in one big bowl. Pop 120g popcorn kernels in oil and split between 2 bowls. For the caramel half, boil 60g butter, 120g brown sugar and 2 tbsp golden syrup for 3 minutes, stir in a pinch of bicarb, toss through one bowl and bake on a tray at 120°C for 30 minutes, stirring twice, then cool. For the cheese half, toss the other bowl with 40g melted butter and 3 tbsp cheddar cheese powder and a pinch of salt. Mix both together in one big bowl.

Mains

Food critic's steak

Seared sirloin, cracked pepper cream sauce, thin fries

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day for the sauce, fry chips fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns, cracked
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries.
  2. Fry the fries twice in the oil until crisp.
  3. Season the steaks with salt, sear to your liking and rest.
  4. Toast the cracked peppercorns in the pan, add the cream and simmer until thick.
  5. Serve the steak with the fries, sauce and a green salad.

Union Station deep dish sausage pizzaOn the printed menu

Semolina dough pressed up a tin, mozzarella, Italian sausage and chunky tomato

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 50g fine semolina
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 300g mozzarella
  • 400g Italian sausages
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 40g parmesan
  1. Deep pan pizza dough pressed into a tin, layered with mozzarella, Italian sausage and chunky tomato sauce, baked at 220°C for 30 minutes. Mix 400g plain flour, 50g fine semolina, 7g dried yeast, 1 tsp salt, 60ml olive oil and 250ml warm water, knead 5 minutes and rise 1 hour. Press into an oiled 25cm cake tin and up the sides. Layer 300g sliced mozzarella on the base, then 400g Italian sausage meat pressed flat and browned. Top with a 400g tin crushed tomatoes simmered 10 minutes with garlic and oregano. Sprinkle with parmesan and bake at 220°C for 30 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.

Sweets

Crème brûlée and jelly

Torched vanilla crème brûlée beside a wobbly jelly. Jelly or brûlée, Jules says choose.

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min, then 4.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, torch just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet raspberry jelly crystals
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar, for the custard
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the top
  1. Make up the jelly as the packet says and set in small cups.
  2. Heat the cream with the split vanilla bean, whisk into the yolks and sugar, then strain into ramekins.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150°C until just set.
  4. Chill the custards until firm.
  5. Sprinkle with sugar, torch and serve with the jelly.

I Say a Little Prayer raspberry sparklerVegetarian

Raspberry purée topped with sparkling water. George starts the song and the restaurant joins in.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Purée up to 1 day, top with sparkling wine just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 750ml sparkling wine, cold
  1. Crush most of the raspberries with the sugar and lemon juice and push through a sieve.
  2. Spoon a spoonful of purée into each of 4 flutes.
  3. Slowly top with cold sparkling wine and drop a whole raspberry in each.
