Snacks

Choirboy's go-to-church wings

Crispy baked wings tossed in honey and hot sauce butter

Prep 15 min, cook 762 min · Make ahead: Dry the wings the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 50g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • salt, to taste
  1. Leave the wings uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Toss with garlic powder and salt.
  3. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  4. Melt the butter with honey and hot sauce.
  5. Toss the wings through until glossy.

Devilled eggsVegetarian

Eggs with mustard, sweet relish and hot sauce, dusted with smoked paprika

Prep 20 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp sweet relish
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • smoked paprika, to dust
  1. Boil the eggs, cool and peel.
  2. Halve and whip the yolks with mayonnaise, mustard, relish and hot sauce.
  3. Pipe back into the whites and dust with smoked paprika.
  4. Keep chilled until the show starts.

Mains

Baked macaroni and cheeseVegetarian

Macaroni baked with cheddar, evaporated milk and egg, golden on top

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; bake on the night and add 10 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 500g macaroni
  • 400g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml can evaporated milk
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  1. Boil the macaroni for 2 minutes less than the packet says and drain.
  2. Whisk the eggs with evaporated milk, melted butter and mustard powder.
  3. Mix with the pasta and most of the cheddar.
  4. Top with more cheese and bake at 180C until golden.

Jimmy Potter's lemon pound cakeVegetarian

Dense buttery lemon pound cake with fresh lemon zest

Prep 25 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep wrapped airtight; ice the cake and whip the cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g soft butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 lemons
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 120g icing sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale.
  2. Add the eggs one at a time with the lemon zest.
  3. Fold in the flour and baking powder and spoon into a lined loaf tin.
  4. Bake at 160C until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with lemon juice and pour over the warm cake.
  6. Whip the cream and serve with slices.

Backstage cherry limeadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemonade with lime, cherry syrup and crushed ice, topped with a cherry

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes and stir in the cherry syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice and lemonade to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 8 tbsp maraschino cherry syrup
  • 800ml lemonade
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Fill four glasses with crushed ice.
  2. Add lime juice and cherry syrup to each.
  3. Top with lemonade and add a cherry.
