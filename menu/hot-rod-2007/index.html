Cool beans black bean dipVegetarian

Baked black bean dip with cheddar, salsa, sour cream and corn chips

Prep 8 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Make the bean mix up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; top with cheese, bake and add the cold toppings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ground cumin, to taste
  • smoked paprika, to taste
  • 2 400g tins black beans
  • 1 lime
  • 150g cheddar
  • 150g fresh salsa
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 250g corn chips
  • 100ml water
  1. Fry the diced onion and the garlic cloves in oil for 5 minutes, add the cumin and the smoked paprika, then the drained tins black beans and the water.
  2. Simmer 5 minutes and mash roughly with lime juice and salt.
  3. Spread in a baking dish, cover with the grated cheddar and bake at 200°C for 10 minutes.
  4. Top with the salsa, the sour cream, sliced jalapeño and coriander.
  5. Serve with the corn chips.

I like to party chilli cheese dogs

Grilled franks in buns under beef chilli and melted cheddar

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat, grill the frankfurts and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • chilli powder, to taste
  • ground cumin, to taste
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 200ml passata
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 120g cheddar
  • 1 white onion
  • yellow mustard, to taste
  • 100ml water
  1. Brown the beef mince with the diced onion.
  2. Add the chilli powder, the cumin, the tomato paste, the passata and the water and simmer 15 minutes until thick.
  3. Grill the frankfurts until blistered.
  4. Toast the hot dog buns, add the franks, spoon over the chilli and top with the grated cheddar.
  5. Grill 1 minute to melt, then finish with diced white onion and yellow mustard.

Mains

No tool in this pool cheeseburgers

Burgers with cheese and pickle sauce. No tool in this pool

Prep 14 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties and mix the burger sauce up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • tomato sauce, to taste
  • mustard, to taste
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 4 sesame burger buns
  1. Shape the beef mince into patties slightly wider than the buns and season well.
  2. Grill on a hot barbecue 4 minutes a side, adding the slices of American cheese each for the last minute.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, the tomato sauce, the mustard and the chopped dill pickles.
  4. Toast the sesame buns, spread with sauce, add shredded lettuce, the patty and sliced pickles.

Rico's pyro-crew fish tacos

Crisp battered fillets in warm tortillas with lime slaw, avocado and chipotle mayo

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw and chipotle mayo up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g firm white fish fillets, in strips
  • 120g plain flour
  • 200ml soda water
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 250g shredded red cabbage
  • 2 limes
  • 1 avocado
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo, chopped
  1. Toss the cabbage with the juice of 1 lime and a pinch of salt. Mix the mayonnaise with the chipotle.
  2. Whisk the flour, paprika and soda water into a batter.
  3. Dip the fish in the batter and fry in hot oil until golden.
  4. Warm the tortillas and fill with slaw, fish, sliced avocado and chipotle mayo, with lime wedges.

Fifteen bus jump choc chip cookiesVegetarian

Dark choc chip cookies. One more bus than Evel Knievel

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Roll the dough into balls up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled, or freeze up to 1 month; bake on the day. Baked cookies keep up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • vanilla extract, to taste
  • 200g plain flour
  • bicarb soda, to taste
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Beat the soft butter with the brown sugar and the caster sugar, then the egg and the vanilla.
  3. Stir in the plain flour, a little bicarb, a pinch of salt and the chopped dark chocolate.
  4. Roll into balls and bake on lined trays for 10 to 11 minutes until golden at the edges but soft in the middle.
  5. Sprinkle with flaky salt and cool on the trays.
  6. Line up in a row to serve.

Safe-word cherry lime sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Cherry syrup, lime and soda. Safe word: whiskey

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Simmer, strain and cool the cherry syrup up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled; build the drinks over crushed ice with soda water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen pitted cherries
  • 100g sugar
  • 3 limes
  • 800ml soda water
  • 2 cups crushed ice, as needed
  1. Simmer the pitted frozen cherries with the sugar and the water for 10 minutes, mashing them.
  2. Strain and cool.
  3. Fill the tall glasses with crushed ice.
  4. Add the cherry syrup and the lime juice to each and top with the cold soda water.
  5. Stir and garnish with a cherry and a lime wedge.
