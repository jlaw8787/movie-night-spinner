Salami, cheddar and pickle grilled sandwiches

Grilled sourdough sandwiches with sharp cheddar, salami and sliced pickles.

Prep 14 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Assemble the toasties up to 4 hours ahead and keep wrapped in the fridge; cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 200g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 16 slices of salami
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Butter one side of every slice of sourdough.
  2. On the unbuttered side of half the slices layer the grated sharp cheddar, salami and pickles, then more cheddar. Top with the remaining bread, buttered side out.
  3. Cook in a pan over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes a side, pressing down, until golden and melted.
  4. Cut into fingers and serve with hot English mustard.

Welcome basket baked brie, honey walnuts

Brie baked soft under honey and toasted walnuts, a gift left for the guest.

How it is made
  • 200g brie
  • 40g walnuts
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 baguette
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 2 apples
  1. A whole brie baked until molten, topped with honey, toasted walnuts, thyme and chilli flakes, with crisp baguette toasts and apple slices for dipping. Heat the oven to 180°C. Score the top of a 200g brie and sit it on a lined tray. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until soft in the middle. Toast 40g walnuts in a dry pan and toss with 2 tbsp honey, thyme leaves and a pinch of chilli flakes. Slice a baguette thin, brush with oil and bake for 8 minutes until crisp. Spoon the walnuts over the brie and serve with the toasts and sliced apple.

Detroit-style pizza

Thick rectangular pan pizza with crisp cheesy edges and pepperoni.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, 35 min waiting · Make ahead: Simmer the tomato sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled, and cube the cheeses up to 1 day ahead; press out, prove and bake the dough on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, for the tin
  • 200g mozzarella, cubed
  • 150g cheddar, cubed
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  1. Press the dough into a rectangular tin oiled with the olive oil and prove for 30 minutes.
  2. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic into a sauce, 15 min.
  3. Cover the dough right to the edges with the cubed mozzarella, the cubed cheddar and the pepperoni.
  4. Bake at 250°C for 15 minutes.
  5. Stripe the tomato sauce on top.

LA galbi short ribs with kimchi rice

Beef in soy, pear and sesame, charred and wrapped in lettuce cups, LA Koreatown style.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg thin-cut beef short ribs
  • 1 nashi pear
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 200g kimchi
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 tub gochujang
  • 1 tub ssamjang
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  1. Thin-cut beef short ribs marinated in soy, pear, garlic and sesame, seared on a smoking grill, with kimchi fried rice, lettuce cups and ssamjang. Blend half a nashi pear, 1 small onion, 5 garlic cloves, 100ml soy sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar and 2 tbsp sesame oil. Marinate 1.2kg thin-cut LA-style beef short ribs for at least 4 hours. Grill over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes a side until charred. Fry 200g chopped kimchi in oil, add 600g cooked cold rice and 1 tbsp gochujang and stir-fry until crisp. Serve with cos leaves, sliced spring onion, sesame seeds and ssamjang.

Keith's tea and brown butter shortbreadVegetarian

Brown butter shortbread with the tea Tess never drinks.

Prep 28 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 90g caster sugar
  • 250g plain flour
  • 30g cornflour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 tbsp demerara sugar
  • 1 pinch flaky sea salt
  • 4 tsp loose black tea
  • 300ml milk
  1. Melt the butter and cook until nutty and golden brown. Chill until firm but soft, 20 min.
  2. Beat with the caster sugar until pale, then mix in the flour, cornflour and salt.
  3. Press into a lined 20cm square tin, sprinkle with demerara and flaky salt, prick all over.
  4. Bake at 160°C for 35 minutes.
  5. Cut into fingers while warm, 5 min.
  6. Brew a pot of black tea for 4 minutes and serve alongside with milk.

Sticky toffee date puddingVegetarian

Warm date sponge for the night the bedroom door opened.

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Sauce keeps 3 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dates, chopped
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 180g butter
  • 300g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Soak the dates in the boiling water with the bicarb, 10 min.
  2. Beat some of the butter with some of the sugar and the eggs, then fold in the flour and dates.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 30 minutes.
  4. Simmer the rest of the butter, sugar and the cream into a sauce and pour over, 10 min.

Barbary Street red wine sangria

Red wine, brandy, orange, saved till Tess is out of the shower.

Prep 13 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Chill up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml medium-bodied red wine
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 apple, diced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 300ml lemonade
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Combine the wine, brandy, sugar, orange, apple and cinnamon in a large jug. Stir to dissolve the sugar.
  2. Chill for at least 2 hours.
  3. Just before serving, add plenty of ice and top with lemonade. Pour into glasses with some of the fruit in each.

When to serve

Serve the tea when Keith makes Tess a cup on that first night.
