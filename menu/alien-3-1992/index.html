Snacks

EEV crash landing Scotch eggs

Soft-boiled eggs wrapped in sage and mustard pork sausage, breaded and fried golden.

Prep 23 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Boil, peel and wrap the eggs up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; crumb and fry on the night, or fry up to 1 day ahead and serve cold
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs
  • 400g pork sausage mince
  • 1 tbsp sage, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp hot English mustard
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 50g plain flour
  • 80g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • sea salt flakes, to taste
  1. Lower 4 of the eggs into a pot of boiling water and cook for 7 minutes.
  2. Lift into iced water, cool for 10 minutes, then peel.
  3. Mix the sausage mince with the sage, mustard and pepper. Divide into 4, flatten each piece in your palm and wrap it around a peeled egg, sealing the seams.
  4. Beat the last egg. Roll each wrapped egg in the flour, then the beaten egg, then the breadcrumbs.
  5. Heat the oil in a deep heavy pot to 170°C, 8 min.
  6. Fry 2 at a time for 6 to 7 minutes, turning, until deep golden and the sausage is cooked through. Drain, sprinkle with salt and cut in half to serve.

Mess line bubble and squeakVegetarian

Crisp potato and cabbage cakes fried in butter, with brown sauce.

Prep 17 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Shape the cakes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 700g floury potatoes
  • 250g savoy cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 4 tbsp brown sauce
  1. Peel the potatoes and cut into even chunks.
  2. Boil in salted water until tender, drain well and leave to steam dry.
  3. Mash roughly, leaving some lumps.
  4. Fry the onion and cabbage in half the butter until soft and catching at the edges.
  5. Mix into the potato, season well with salt and pepper and shape into 8 small flat cakes. Dust with the flour.
  6. Chill to firm up, 20 min.
  7. Fry in the oil and remaining butter over medium heat, 4 minutes a side, in batches, until crusted and golden.
  8. Serve hot with the brown sauce.

Fury 161 cottage pie

Ground beef, carrot and peas in rich gravy under cheddar-topped mash.

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Make the filling and mash up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; assemble and bake on the night, adding 10 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg floury potatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 700g beef mince
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Peel the potatoes and cut into chunks. Dice the onion, carrots and celery and crush the garlic.
  2. Heat the oil in a large heavy pan and brown the mince well, in batches, 10 min.
  3. Add the onion, carrot, celery and garlic and cook until soft.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste and flour and cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the stock, Worcestershire and thyme and simmer, partly covered, until thick. Meanwhile boil the potatoes in salted water until tender.
  6. Drain the potatoes and mash with the butter and milk. Season with salt and pepper. Heat the oven to 200°C, 8 min.
  7. Stir the peas into the mince, season and tip into a baking dish. Spread the mash on top, rough it up with a fork and scatter with the cheddar.
  8. Bake until bubbling and golden on top.
  9. Rest before serving, 5 min.

Fiorina bread and butter puddingVegetarian

Buttered bread and golden raisins baked in vanilla custard with nutmeg and a crunchy sugar top.

Prep 12 min, cook 48 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Assemble and soak up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bake on the night, adding 5 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of day-old white bread
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 60g sultanas
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 70g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp demerara sugar
  1. Butter a baking dish. Butter the bread on one side, trim the crusts if you like and cut into triangles.
  2. Layer the bread in the dish, buttered side up, scattering the sultanas between the layers.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream, caster sugar and vanilla and pour over the bread.
  4. Leave to soak, pressing the bread down once or twice. Heat the oven to 170°C, 20 min.
  5. Grate the nutmeg over and sprinkle with the demerara sugar.
  6. Bake until puffed, just set in the middle and golden on top.
  7. Rest before serving warm, 10 min.

Drinks

Leadworks molten hot chocolateNo alcohol

Dark chocolate and cocoa whisked into hot milk with cinnamon and a pinch of chilli.

Prep 1 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate base up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat gently and whisk to a froth before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • chilli powder, a pinch
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • salt, a pinch
  1. Whisk the milk, cocoa, brown sugar, cinnamon and chilli in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming.
  2. Take off the heat and whisk in the chocolate and salt until smooth.
  3. Return to low heat and whisk hard until frothy.
  4. Pour into 4 heavy mugs.
