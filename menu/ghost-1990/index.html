Hush puppiesVegetarian

Cornmeal fritters, honey butter. Oda Mae's a fake, until Sam turns up

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry ingredients and beat the honey butter up to 1 day ahead, keeping the butter chilled; mix the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 spring onions
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a bowl.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk, egg and sliced spring onions to a thick batter.
  3. Beat the softened butter with the honey until smooth.
  4. Heat the oil to 180C in a deep pan.
  5. Drop in teaspoons of batter and fry until golden, turning once. Drain on paper and serve hot with the honey butter.

Tribeca loft crostiniVegetarian

Garlic baguette, tomato and basil. Watch Sam float a penny for Molly

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Toast the baguette slices up to 1 day ahead, airtight; dress the tomatoes up to 2 hours ahead and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 16 baguette slices (about 1 baguette)
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • salt, to taste
  1. Grill the baguette slices until charred at the edges.
  2. Rub each warm toast with the cut garlic clove.
  3. Dice the tomatoes and dress with torn basil, salt and olive oil.
  4. Spoon the tomato onto the toasts just before serving so they stay crisp.

Sam's Tribeca loft pasta

Rigatoni with Italian sausage, fennel, white wine and cream

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the sausage and cream sauce up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat, boil the rigatoni and toss together
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Italian sausages
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 125ml white wine
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 500g rigatoni
  • 60g parmesan
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the rigatoni in water with a good pinch of salt until just tender, then drain.
  2. Meanwhile, remove the sausage skins, crumble the meat and brown it in a large pan.
  3. Add the sliced fennel and crushed garlic and cook until soft.
  4. Pour in the wine and let it bubble down, then stir in the cream.
  5. Toss the rigatoni through the sauce with the grated parmesan.

Pottery wheel pieVegetarian

Apple pie in hand-pleated pastry, shaped at the wheel. Ditto

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Assemble the pie on the tray up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 4 apples
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Heat the oven to 200C and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Roll the pastry sheet into a rough circle on the tray.
  3. Toss the sliced apples with the sugar and cinnamon and pile in the middle.
  4. Fold the edges up by hand, pleating as you go.
  5. Bake until golden. Cool a little before slicing.

Molly's studio mulled pinot noirVegetarian

Mulled red for Molly. Molly, you in danger, girl

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the wine, orange, spices and honey in the saucepan up to 1 day ahead, covered; warm gently to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml pinot noir
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 1 orange
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 star anise
  • 4 cloves
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Pour the pinot noir into a saucepan with the orange peel, the orange juice, cinnamon, star anise, cloves and honey.
  2. Warm over low heat without boiling, so the alcohol stays in.
  3. Strain into mugs and add a thin orange slice to each.
