Short Round's samosasVegetarian

Crisp fried cones of potato and peas. Hold on to your potatoes.

Prep 22 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Fill and shape up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 250g plain flour
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil, for the dough
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp tamarind chutney
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the flour with the dough oil, salt and enough water into a stiff dough. Rest while you cook the filling, 8 min.
  2. Cook the potatoes until tender, then toss with the peas, cumin, chilli, garam masala and salt, 15 min.
  3. Roll the dough, cut and shape into cones, fill and seal.
  4. Fry at 175°C until blistered and golden, in batches, 15 min.
  5. Serve with tamarind chutney.

Coiled chipolata snake with mango glaze

Chipolatas coiled into a snake, glazed with mango chutney and chilli, yoghurt dip.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Coil the chipolatas on the tray and mix the chutney glaze up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chipolatas
  • 3 tbsp mango chutney
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  1. Coil the chipolatas tightly on a tray, pinning with skewers.
  2. Brush with mango chutney mixed with the chilli. Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  3. Stir the cumin into the yoghurt and serve alongside. Surprise.

Maharaja's lamb curry

Yoghurt marinated lamb shoulder braised with onions and tomato.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hr 15 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate overnight, curry keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 piece ginger, 4cm, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 brown onions, sliced
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g basmati rice
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the lamb with the yoghurt, ginger, garlic and garam masala.
  2. Marinate overnight in the fridge.
  3. Cook the onions slowly in oil until deep brown, 25 min.
  4. Add the tomatoes, then the lamb with its marinade, and season with salt to taste. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours 30 minutes.
  5. Cook the rice and serve with the curry, 20 min.

Mayapore village thali

Chickpea masala, mustard seed dal and turmeric cauliflower, for the village missing its children.

How it is made
  • 200g yellow split peas
  • ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • chickpeas
  • 1 cauliflower
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 flatbreads
  • mango pickle
  1. Chickpeas in tomato onion masala, yellow dal tempered with mustard seed and cumin, turmeric roasted cauliflower, basmati, warm flatbread and mango pickle. Simmer 200g yellow split peas with turmeric in 1L water for 40 minutes, then pour over 1 tsp mustard seeds and 1 tsp cumin sizzled in 2 tbsp ghee. Soften an onion with garlic, ginger and 2 tsp garam masala, add 400g tomatoes and 2 tins chickpeas and simmer 20 minutes. Roast a cauliflower in florets with turmeric and oil at 220°C for 25 minutes. Cook 300g basmati. Warm 4 flatbreads and serve with mango pickle.

Sweets

Chilled mango brains

Mango puree and whipped cream set in dome moulds, turned out chilled.

Prep 25 min, 4 hr 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 4 gelatine leaves
  • 250ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 2 passionfruit
  1. Blitz the mangoes with the sugar into a purée.
  2. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water, then dissolve in a little warm purée and stir through the rest, 10 min.
  3. Fold in the whipped cream and spoon into dome moulds.
  4. Set in the fridge for 4 hours.
  5. Turn out and top with passionfruit pulp.

Sankara stone saffron mango lassiNo alcohol

Mango, yoghurt, honey, cardamom and saffron blended with milk.

Prep 8 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Steep the saffron in the warm milk up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and crush the pistachios; blend with ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 tbsp warm milk
  • 2 ripe mangoes
  • 400g natural yoghurt
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch ground cardamom
  • 30g pistachios, crushed
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Soak the saffron in the warm milk for 10 minutes.
  2. Blend the mangoes with the yoghurt, cold milk, honey, cardamom and saffron milk until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour over ice into four glasses and top with crushed pistachios.

When to serve
