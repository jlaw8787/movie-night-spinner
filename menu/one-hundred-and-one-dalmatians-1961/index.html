Snacks

Pongo and Perdita's spotty cheese strawsVegetarian

Puff pastry with cheddar, parmesan, black olives and poppy seeds.

Prep 16 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 50g black olives, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp poppy seeds
  1. Lay out one pastry sheet and brush with beaten egg.
  2. Scatter over the cheddar, parmesan, olives and poppy seeds.
  3. Lay the second sheet on top and press down firmly.
  4. Cut into strips, twist each a few times and lay on lined trays.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden and crisp.

Sooty puppy black sesame potato chips

Paper-thin crisps tossed in poppy seeds, cracked pepper and sea salt, a chimney disguise.

How it is made
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 1 packet black sesame seeds
  • 1 jar poppy seeds
  • 1 jar smoked salt
  1. Thin crisp potato chips tossed in a sooty seasoning of poppy seeds, cracked black pepper and sea salt, the puppies' chimney-soot disguise. Slice 4 large potatoes paper thin on a mandoline, soak in cold water 20 minutes, then dry thoroughly. Fry in small batches at 170°C for 3 minutes until crisp and golden, and drain. Grind 2 tbsp toasted black sesame seeds, 1 sheet of nori and 1 tsp smoked salt to a coarse dust. Toss the hot chips in the seasoning and serve in a big bowl.

Mains

Roger and Anita's shepherd's pie

Lamb mince with carrots and peas under buttery mash.

Prep 23 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, assemble and bake when needed
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Dice the onion and grate the carrots.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion and carrot in the olive oil.
  3. Add the peas and stock and simmer until thick, then season with salt and pepper.
  4. Peel and chop the potatoes, boil until tender, then mash with butter and milk.
  5. Spread the mince in a dish and top with the mash.
  6. Bake at 200°C until golden.

Nanny's roast beef and Yorkshire puds

Mustard-crusted topside roasted pink, puffed batter cups, crunchy potatoes and onion gravy.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef topside
  • 1 jar English mustard
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 200g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 1 brown onion
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Mustard-crusted beef topside roasted pink, with puffed Yorkshire puddings, crunchy roast potatoes and lashings of onion gravy. Rub a 1.5kg beef topside with 1 tbsp English mustard and salt and roast at 200°C for 50 minutes for medium, with 1kg parboiled potato chunks in oil around it. Rest 20 minutes. Whisk 150g plain flour, 3 eggs and 300ml milk. Heat 1 tsp oil in each hole of a 12-hole muffin tin at 230°C, pour in the batter and bake 20 minutes. Fry 1 sliced onion, add 1 tbsp flour and 400ml beef stock and simmer.

Barn honey shortbread with warm milk dipVegetarian

Buttery honey shortbread, with warm milk for dipping.

Prep 18 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Shortbread up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, soft
  • 70g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 300g plain flour
  • pinch of salt
  • 600ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Beat the butter, sugar and some of the honey until pale.
  2. Mix in the flour and salt.
  3. Press into a lined tin and prick all over.
  4. Bake at 160°C until pale gold.
  5. Cut into fingers while warm, then cool in the tin.
  6. Warm the milk with the remaining honey and the vanilla. Serve in small cups for dunking.

Half and half iced biscuitsVegetarian

Soft buttermilk biscuits iced half white, half chocolate.

Prep 25 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the egg.
  2. Mix in the flour and buttermilk.
  3. Spoon rounds onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 180°C until set and lightly golden.
  5. Cool on the trays.
  6. Mix the icing sugar with the water and vanilla into a glaze, then stir the cocoa into half of it to make a chocolate glaze. Ice half of each biscuit with each.
  7. Leave to set.

Drinks

Thunderbolt black and white shakeNo alcohol

Vanilla and chocolate shakes poured side by side, whipped cream and chocolate drops.

Prep 14 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; whip the cream and chill the glasses up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml chocolate ice cream
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 100ml whipped cream
  • 50g dark chocolate drops
  1. Blend the vanilla ice cream with half the milk and the vanilla. Pour into a jug.
  2. Blend the chocolate ice cream with the remaining milk and the cocoa. Pour into a second jug.
  3. Pour both shakes into tall glasses at the same time from either side so they sit half and half.
  4. Top with whipped cream and chocolate drops for spots.
