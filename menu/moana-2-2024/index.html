Snacks

Kakamora coconut prawns

Prawns crumbed in shredded coconut and panko, fried.

Prep 22 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 4 hours ahead and keep them chilled on a tray; fry just before serving, and mix the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 80g shredded coconut
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 limes
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Mix the coconut with the panko.
  3. Dip the prawns in beaten egg, then press into the coconut mix.
  4. Fry in the oil at 180°C in batches until golden.
  5. Stir lime juice into the sweet chilli sauce and serve for dipping.

Motunui taro chipsVegetarian

Paper thin taro fried crisp and tossed with sea salt.

Prep 27 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fry up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight; re-crisp in a 150°C oven for 5 minutes, then toss with the salt and chilli
Recipe for 4
  • 600g taro
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Peel the taro and slice very thin on a mandoline.
  2. Rinse and dry well.
  3. Fry in the oil at 170°C in batches until crisp and pale golden.
  4. Toss straight away with sea salt and chilli flakes.

Mains

Ocean voyage fish

Snapper marinated in coconut, lime and chilli, grilled in banana leaf.

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Marinate the snapper in the fridge up to 2 hours ahead; wrap in banana leaves and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 3 limes
  • 2 red chillies, chopped
  • 4 banana leaves
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 100ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Marinate the snapper in some of the coconut milk with the lime juice and chopped red chillies, 20 min.
  2. Soften the banana leaves over a flame or in hot water.
  3. Wrap each fillet in a leaf.
  4. Grill the parcels, 10 min.
  5. Cook the rice in the remaining coconut milk with the water and salt, 15 min.
  6. Serve the fish in its leaf.

Motunui banana leaf pulled pork

Garlic and sugar rubbed shoulder, slow roasted, charred pineapple, coconut slaw, homecoming feast.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 packet banana leaves
  • 1 roll foil
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1/2 cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 200ml coconut cream
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Pork shoulder rubbed with salt, garlic and brown sugar, slow roasted in banana leaves until it pulls apart, with charred pineapple and coconut slaw. Rub a 2kg pork shoulder with 2 tsp salt, 4 crushed garlic cloves and 2 tbsp brown sugar. Wrap in banana leaves, then foil, and roast at 150°C for 5 hours. Pull apart with forks and toss in the juices. Grill 8 pineapple rings until charred. Toss half a shredded cabbage and 1 grated carrot with 3 tbsp coconut cream, the juice of 1 lime and coriander. Serve together.

Haupia coconut pudding with mangoVegetarian

Set coconut milk pudding in cups, topped with diced mango.

Prep 3 min, cook 20 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 5 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 mangoes
  • 30g shredded coconut
  1. Whisk the coconut milk, sugar and cornflour over low heat until thick and glossy.
  2. Pour into cups.
  3. Chill until set.
  4. Toast the shredded coconut in a dry pan, 4 min.
  5. Dice the mango and top the puddings with mango and toasted coconut.

Nalo's pineapple coconut slushieNo alcohol

Coconut water, pineapple, lime and honey blended with ice.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Cut the pineapple up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml chilled coconut water
  • 1 small pineapple
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Cut the pineapple into chunks and keep four wedges for garnish.
  2. Blend the coconut water, pineapple, lime juice, honey and ice until smooth and slushy.
  3. Pour into glasses or hollowed coconut shells, garnish with a pineapple wedge and mint and serve with straws.
