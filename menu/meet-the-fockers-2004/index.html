Jack's RV ham croquetas

Creamy ham croquetas crumbed and fried, with lime garlic mayo.

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled before frying
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 120g plain flour
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200g leg ham, finely chopped
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 lime
  1. Melt the butter, stir in some of the flour for a couple of minutes, then slowly whisk in the milk until very thick.
  2. Stir in the ham, nutmeg and pepper. Spread on a tray, cover and chill.
  3. Shape into small logs, roll in the remaining flour, dip in egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until golden, working in batches.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise with the garlic and a squeeze of lime juice and serve for dipping.

Coconut Grove shrimp fritters

Crisp fritters of chopped shrimp, bell pepper and spring onion.

Prep 11 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Chop the prawns and dice the capsicum up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; mix the dipping sauce up to 2 days ahead. Mix the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g peeled green prawns, chopped
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 150ml soda water
  • 1/2 red capsicum, diced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 limes, juiced
  1. Mix the flour, egg, soda water, capsicum, spring onions, Cajun seasoning and salt into a thick batter, then fold in the prawns.
  2. Drop heaped tablespoons into oil at 180°C and fry, turning, until golden. Drain.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, sweet chilli sauce and lime juice for the sauce.

Wall of Gaylord mojo roast pork

Citrus garlic roast pork shoulder with black beans and fried banana.

Prep 10 min, cook 4 hours, then 10 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 garlic cloves
  • 2 oranges, juiced
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 3 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 1.5kg boneless pork shoulder
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 2 tins of black beans (400g each), drained
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 firm bananas, sliced
  • 20g butter
  1. Blitz the garlic, orange and lime juice, two teaspoons of the cumin, oregano, oil and salt. Rub over the pork.
  2. Marinate overnight in the fridge. This step is the day before.
  3. Roast covered at 160°C, then uncovered at 200°C.
  4. Fry the onion and capsicum, add the beans and remaining cumin and simmer.
  5. Cook the rice. Fry the banana slices in the butter.
  6. Rest and shred the pork and serve with beans, rice and bananas.

Bernie's arroz con pollo

Saffron rice, chicken, peas and bell pepper.

Prep 16 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Cook up to 1 day ahead and reheat covered with a splash of stock
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thigh cutlets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 700ml chicken stock
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 60g green olives
  1. Brown the chicken skin side down in the oil until crisp. Lift out.
  2. Soften the onion, capsicum and garlic in the same pot, then stir in the saffron, paprika and rice.
  3. Pour in the stock, sit the chicken on top, cover and simmer on low until the rice is tender and the chicken cooked.
  4. Scatter over the peas and olives, cover and rest off the heat.

Jinx and Moses key lime pieVegetarian

Tart lime custard in a biscuit crust.

Prep 17 min, cook 23 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain sweet biscuits
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 395g tin condensed milk
  • 7 limes (150ml juice and zest)
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 23cm pie dish.
  2. Bake the base at 180°C.
  3. Whisk the egg yolks with the condensed milk, then the lime juice and zest of two limes.
  4. Pour into the base and bake at 160°C until just set.
  5. Cool, then chill.
  6. Top with whipped cream and more lime zest.

Miami mint mojito

White rum, mint, lime and soda. Roz says rum cures teething.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Cut the limes and pick the mint up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled. Muddle and build each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 3 limes
  • 8 tsp caster sugar
  • 180ml white rum
  • 500ml soda water
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. In each tall glass muddle mint leaves with lime wedges and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Fill with crushed ice and pour in the rum.
  3. Top with soda water and stir from the bottom with a long spoon. Garnish with a mint sprig and a lime wheel.
