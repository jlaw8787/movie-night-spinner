Kensington tomato pie squaresVegetarian

Thick focaccia under sweet tomato sauce, from the bakery on the old block. The Balboas are back.

Prep 13 min, cook 50 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead; bake the pie up to 8 hours ahead, it is served at room temperature anyway
Recipe for 4
  • 400g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 2 tsp caster sugar, 1 for the dough, 1 for the sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300ml warm water
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, 2 for the dough, 2 for the sauce
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 30g pecorino, finely grated
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, a teaspoon of sugar, the water and 2 tbsp oil into a soft dough and knead for 5 minutes.
  2. Cover and leave to rise until doubled. Meanwhile simmer the tomatoes with 2 tbsp oil, the garlic, a teaspoon of oregano and the last of the sugar until thick.
  3. Oil a deep baking tray, press the dough out to the edges and leave to puff. Heat the oven to 220°C.
  4. Spread the sauce thickly over the dough right to the edges and bake until the base is crisp underneath.
  5. Cool to room temperature.
  6. Dust with the pecorino and the rest of the oregano and cut into squares.

Old neighbourhood roast pork rolls

Cheap, hot and better than anything they ate at the mansion

Prep 7 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Roast and slice the pork up to 2 days ahead and keep it in its juices in the fridge; reheat gently and cook the broccolini on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork neck, in one piece
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed, 4 for the pork, 2 for the greens
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
  • 1 tbsp rosemary, chopped
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 8 slices sharp provolone
  • 8 soft white rolls
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Rub the pork all over with 4 of the garlic cloves, the fennel seeds, rosemary, 1 tbsp oil and plenty of salt and pepper.
  2. Sit in a small roasting dish, pour in the stock, cover tightly with foil and roast until very tender.
  3. Rest, then slice as thinly as you can and return the slices to the juices.
  4. Fry the broccolini in the rest of the oil with the last 2 garlic cloves and the chilli flakes until tender and catching. Season.
  5. Split the rolls, pile in the pork, a slice of provolone and the broccolini, and spoon over some juices.

Tommy Gunn's Oklahoma chicken-fried steak

Pounded beef dredged twice in seasoned flour, pan-crisped, milk gravy and mash, at Rocky's table

Prep 7 min, cook 61 min · Make ahead: Make the mash up to 1 day ahead and reheat with a splash of milk; crumb the steaks up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; fry and make the gravy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g floury potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 70g butter, 40g for the mash, 30g for the gravy
  • 705ml full cream milk, 80ml for the mash, 125ml for the coating, 500ml for the gravy
  • 4 thin beef round steaks, about 600g
  • 190g plain flour, 150g for the coating, 40g for the gravy
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 300g green beans, trimmed
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender.
  2. Season the coating flour with the paprika, garlic powder and salt. Beat the eggs with the coating milk.
  3. Pound the steaks thin between sheets of baking paper. Dip each in flour, then egg, then flour again, pressing it on.
  4. Heat the oil in a large heavy frying pan and fry the steaks for 3 to 4 minutes a side, in batches, until deep golden. Keep warm.
  5. Pour off all but 1 tbsp of oil. Add the gravy butter and flour, cook for 1 minute, whisk in the gravy milk and pepper and simmer until thick. Season.
  6. Drain and mash the potatoes with the mash butter and milk. Steam the beans until tender.
  7. Plate the steaks with the mash and beans and pour over the gravy.

Balboa Christmas struffoliVegetarian

Christmas dinner at the Balboa mansion, and the old recipes too

Prep 20 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Fry the dough balls up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; toss in the warm honey on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 175g honey
  • 2 tbsp hundreds and thousands
  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, eggs, butter and both zests into a soft dough. Knead briefly, cover and rest.
  2. Roll the dough into ropes about 1cm thick and cut into 1cm pieces.
  3. Heat the oil to 170°C and fry the pieces in batches for about 2 minutes until golden. Drain.
  4. Warm the honey in a wide pan until runny.
  5. Tip in the fried balls and toss to coat, then pile into a wreath on a plate and scatter with the hundreds and thousands.
  6. Leave to set.

Paulie's citywide specialVegetarian

A cold beer and a shot, the Philly bar deal, on the champ's tab

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the beer overnight and the glasses in the freezer for 20 minutes before pouring
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles cold lager, 375ml each
  • 120ml rye whiskey
  1. Chill 4 beer glasses and 4 shot glasses in the freezer.
  2. Pour the whiskey into the shot glasses.
  3. Pour the lager into the beer glasses and serve each with a shot alongside.
