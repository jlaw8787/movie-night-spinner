Heart-rate watch cheese bread puffsVegetarian

Chewy tapioca and parmesan puffs. Keep it under 200 beats.

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 250g tapioca flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  1. Bring the milk and oil to the boil and pour over the tapioca flour, mixing well.
  2. Cool slightly, then beat in the eggs and parmesan to a sticky dough.
  3. Roll into balls and bake at 200C for 20 minutes until puffed and golden.
  4. Cool for a few minutes before serving.

Smash green guacamoleVegetarian

Chunky smashed avocado with lime, coriander, red onion and green chilli, corn chips

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 1 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 green chilli, chopped
  • salt, to taste
  • 1 bag corn chips, warmed
  1. Halve the avocados and smash roughly with a fork, leaving it chunky.
  2. Stir in the lime juice, coriander, onion, chilli and salt.
  3. Serve straight away with warm corn chips.

Rio street beef pastéisOn the printed menu

Crisp pastry pockets of spiced beef, olives and egg, street food for a run through Rocinha

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar green olives
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 packet spring roll pastry
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  1. Crisp blistered pastry pockets stuffed with spiced beef mince, olives and egg, fried hot and served with a squeeze of lime and hot sauce. Brown 400g beef mince with 1 diced onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp paprika and salt, then stir in 50g chopped green olives and 2 chopped boiled eggs and cool. Cut 4 thawed puff or spring roll pastry sheets into 12cm squares. Put a spoonful of filling on each, fold into triangles and press the edges with a fork. Fry in oil at 180C for 3 minutes until blistered. Serve with lime and hot sauce.

Mains

Brazilian steak with farofa

Grilled rump steak with toasted cassava farofa, tomato vinaigrette and black beans

Prep 27 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g rump steak
  • 200g cassava flour
  • 60g butter
  • 400g black beans, drained
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  1. Chop the tomatoes with the onion and vinegar for the vinaigrette.
  2. Toast the cassava flour in the butter until golden and nutty.
  3. Grill the steak over high heat and rest.
  4. Warm the beans. Slice the steak and serve with farofa, beans and the vinaigrette.

Culver campus pepperoni and mushroom pizzaOn the printed menu

Thin blistered crust, tomato, mozzarella, salami and garlic mushrooms, to slip past the guard

How it is made
  • 1kg pizza dough
  • 1 bottle passata
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 20g butter
  • 1 head garlic
  • 400g mozzarella
  • 150g pepperoni
  • 1 bottle chilli oil
  1. Thin crust blistered in a hot oven, topped with tomato sauce, mozzarella, pepperoni and garlic mushrooms, finished with chilli oil. Divide 1kg pizza dough into 4 and stretch thin. Spread each with 3 tbsp passata mixed with oregano and salt. Fry 200g sliced mushrooms in butter with 1 garlic clove. Top the bases with 400g grated mozzarella, 150g sliced pepperoni and the mushrooms. Bake on a hot tray at 250C for 10 minutes until blistered. Drizzle with chilli oil.

Rocinha chocolate brigadeirosVegetarian

Fudgy condensed milk chocolate truffles rolled in chocolate sprinkles

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 20g butter, plus extra for your hands
  • 100g chocolate sprinkles
  • 20 small paper cases
  1. Cook the condensed milk, cocoa and butter over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until it pulls away from the base in one mass.
  2. Tip onto a buttered plate and cool for 1 hour.
  3. With buttered hands, roll into 20 small balls and roll in the sprinkles.
  4. Sit each in a small paper case.

Drinks

Bottling plant caipirinha, no bloodVegetarian

Cachaça muddled with lime and sugar over crushed ice, topped with guaraná soda

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 8 tsp caster sugar
  • 180ml cachaca
  • 240ml cold guarana soda
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. For each drink, cut half a lime into wedges and muddle hard in a glass with the sugar.
  2. Fill with crushed ice, add the cachaca and stir well.
  3. Top with cold guarana soda and stir once more. For a booze-free version, skip the cachaca and fill up with more guarana.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when a drop of Bruce's blood lands in the soda bottle.
