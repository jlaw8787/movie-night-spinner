Shrek's onion ringsVegetarian

Buttermilk soaked onion rings in a panko crust, barbecue mayo dip

Prep 8 min, cook 24 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the onion rings in the buttermilk up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; crumb and fry just before serving, they are best straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 3 brown onions
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp barbecue sauce
  1. Cut the onions into thick rings and soak in the buttermilk.
  2. Soak.
  3. Dredge in flour, dip in beaten egg and press into panko.
  4. Fry in the oil at 180°C in batches until deep golden.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise and barbecue sauce to dip.

Swamp dipVegetarian

Hot baked spinach, artichoke and cream cheese dip with corn chips

Prep 17 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day, bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g spinach
  • 1 jar artichoke hearts
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 50g parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100g mozzarella
  • 1 bag corn chips
  1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Mix with chopped artichokes, cream cheese, sour cream, parmesan and crushed garlic.
  3. Top with mozzarella and bake at 200°C until bubbling.
  4. Serve with corn chips.

Gingy's gumdrop button gingerbread menOn the printed menu

Spiced gingerbread men with iced smiles and gumdrop buttons. Gingy keeps his, you get yours.

How it is made
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 350g plain flour
  • 1 jar ground ginger
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 egg
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 packet gumdrops
  1. Soft spiced gingerbread men piped with white icing smiles and finished with bright gumdrop buttons, one with a leg snapped off for the fans. Melt 125g butter, 100g brown sugar and 100g golden syrup. Stir into 350g plain flour, 2 tsp ground ginger, 1 tsp cinnamon and 1 tsp bicarb soda with 1 egg. Chill 1 hour, roll 5mm thick and cut gingerbread men. Bake at 180°C for 10 minutes and cool. Pipe faces with icing from 100g icing sugar and lemon juice, and stick on gumdrops as buttons.

Swamp stew

Beef chuck braised 3 hours with tomato paste and Worcestershire, peas, spinach

Prep 38 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 100g spinach
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Dice the beef, onions, garlic and celery.
  2. Brown the beef in batches, then soften the vegetables.
  3. Add the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and stock, return the beef, cover and braise at 160°C.
  4. Stir in the peas and spinach for the last 5 minutes so it turns green. Serve with crusty bread.

Lord Farquaad's Duloc roast chickenOn the printed menu

Garlic, rosemary and lemon marylands roasted golden in neat rows, crispy potatoes, salsa verde

How it is made
  • 4 chicken marylands
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 lemon
  • 140ml olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 jar capers
  1. Chicken marylands rubbed with garlic, rosemary and lemon, roasted golden in perfectly neat rows with crispy potatoes and a sharp herb salsa verde. Rub 4 chicken marylands with 2 crushed garlic cloves, chopped rosemary, lemon zest, oil and salt. Toss 1kg diced potatoes with oil. Lay the chicken and potatoes in neat rows on a tray and roast at 200°C for 50 minutes until the chicken reaches 75°C. Chop parsley, mint, capers and 1 garlic clove and mix with 80ml olive oil and lemon juice. Spoon over to serve.

Donkey's wafflesVegetarian

Buttermilk waffles with whipped cream, strawberries and maple syrup

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g butter
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries
  • maple syrup, to serve
  1. Separate the eggs. Whisk the flour, baking powder, buttermilk, egg yolks and melted butter.
  2. Whip the whites to soft peaks and fold through.
  3. Cook in a hot waffle iron until crisp, in batches.
  4. Whip the cream, slice the strawberries and serve with maple syrup.

Swamp juiceVegetarianNo alcohol

Green apple, cucumber, lime and mint blitzed with honey and ice

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; chop the apples and blitz everything with the ice just before serving, as the apples brown
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green apples
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 3 limes
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Core and chop the apples and cucumber.
  2. Blitz with the lime juice, mint, honey and ice until slushy.
  3. Pour into four glasses and serve straight away with a mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the waffles when Donkey promises to make them in the morning.
