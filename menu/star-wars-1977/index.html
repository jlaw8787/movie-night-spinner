Snacks

Mos Eisley cantina smoky snack mixVegetarian

Pretzels, rice crackers, chickpeas and nuts baked with smoky butter

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g pretzels
  • 100g rice crackers
  • 100g roasted chickpeas
  • 150g mixed nuts
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • pinch of chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 150°C. Toss the pretzels, crackers, chickpeas and nuts with the melted butter, smoked paprika, garlic powder, chilli flakes and brown sugar.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and bake, stirring twice.
  3. Cool before serving.

Death Star meatballs

Pork and beef meatballs in creamy mushroom Dijon sauce. That's no moon.

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled and reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork and beef mince
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the mince and roll into 20 balls, pressing a thumb dent into each.
  2. Bake at 220°C until browned.
  3. Fry the mushrooms in the olive oil in a wide pan, then add the stock, cream and Dijon and simmer.
  4. Add the meatballs and simmer through.

Ronto wrap

Grilled pork sausage, roast pork, pepper slaw and peppercorn sauce in pita

Prep 13 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; dress the slaw and grill the sausages on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 pork sausages
  • 300g sliced roast pork
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp cracked black peppercorns
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 150ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 4 thick pita breads
  1. Grill the sausages until cooked through.
  2. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayo and plenty of black pepper.
  3. Reduce the shallot, stock, cream and cracked pepper into a sauce.
  4. Warm the roast pork and the pitas.
  5. Fill the pitas with sausage, pork, slaw and sauce and wrap tightly.

Tatooine lamb, apricot and chickpea stewOn the printed menu

Lamb braised with cumin, apricots and chickpeas, slow cooked under two suns

How it is made
  • 1.2kg lamb shoulder
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 800ml chicken stock
  • 400g tin chickpeas
  • 100g dried apricots
  • 300g couscous
  • 20g butter
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Lamb shoulder braised with cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, tomato, chickpeas and dried apricots until tender, over fluffy couscous with mint. Brown 1.2kg lamb shoulder chunks in batches. Add 2 diced onions and 3 garlic cloves and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp ground coriander, 2 tsp smoked paprika and 2 tbsp tomato paste. Add 400g crushed tomatoes, 500ml stock, a drained 400g tin chickpeas and 100g dried apricots. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2.5 hours. Pour 300ml boiling stock over 300g couscous, cover 5 minutes, fluff with butter and serve with mint.

Aunt Beru's blue milk puddingVegetarian

Vanilla milk pudding tinted blue, topped with crushed shortbread

Prep 6 min, cook 9 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 60g cornflour
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 drop blue food colouring
  • 4 shortbread biscuits, crushed
  1. Whisk the cornflour into a little of the cold milk until smooth.
  2. Heat the rest of the milk with the sugar until steaming, then whisk in the cornflour mix and stir until thick.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the vanilla and blue colouring.
  4. Pour into four glasses and chill until set.
  5. Top with crushed shortbread.

Blue pineapple lime milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold milk blended with pineapple, lime and blue spirulina, no alcohol

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Nothing to do ahead; blend just before serving so it stays pale blue and frothy
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml full cream milk, cold
  • 200g frozen pineapple
  • 1 lime
  • 1/2 tsp blue spirulina
  • honey, to taste
  1. Blend the milk, pineapple, lime juice and spirulina until smooth and pale blue.
  2. Add a little honey if you like it sweeter and pour straight away.

When to serve

Serve the blue milk at the Lars homestead dinner table.
