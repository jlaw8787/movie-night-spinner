Snacks

Tractor chicken bites

Buttermilk chicken bites, honey to dip. Tractor chicken.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp honey, for dipping
  1. Marinate the chicken in the buttermilk in the fridge, at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. Mix the flour, paprika and salt. Lift the chicken out of the buttermilk and dredge in the flour.
  3. Fry in the oil at 180°C in batches until crisp and cooked through. Drain on paper and serve hot with the honey.

Chive corn frittersVegetarian

Corn fritters with chive sour cream to dip.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g corn kernels, drained
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Mix the corn, flour, eggs, spring onions and paprika into a thick batter.
  2. Fry spoonfuls in a little oil until golden on both sides.
  3. Stir the chives through the sour cream for the dip and serve the fritters warm.

Honky-tonk cheeseburger slidersOn the printed menu

Smashed beef, cheddar and pickles on soft buns.

How it is made
  • 500g frozen crinkle-cut fries
  • 500g chuck mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Little chuck patties smashed thin with melted cheddar, pickles, mustard and grilled onion on soft slider buns, with a tray of crinkle-cut fries. Cook 500g frozen crinkle-cut fries at 220°C for 20 minutes. Roll 500g chuck mince into 12 balls. Cook a thinly sliced onion on a hot flat plate until soft and brown. Smash the balls flat on the plate, season and cook 2 minutes, flip and add half a slice of cheddar. Toast 12 slider buns, add mustard and pickles, and stack in the patties and onions.

Reverend Moore's Sunday roast

Beef roasted with garlic and thyme, potatoes, gravy.

Prep 10 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef blade
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1kg potatoes, halved
  • 4 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  1. Rub the beef with the garlic, thyme and salt.
  2. Roast covered at 160°C for 2 hours, then add the potatoes and carrots to the tray.
  3. Roast for another hour until the beef is tender.
  4. Lift out the beef and vegetables to rest. Whisk the flour into the pan juices, add the stock and simmer into gravy. Serve after church.

Pepper gravy fried chicken with mash and slawOn the printed menu

Fried chicken with mash, pepper gravy and slaw.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 2L vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 0.25 cabbage
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  1. Buttermilk chicken fried crisp and golden, with creamy mashed potato and pepper gravy, buttered corn and a crunchy coleslaw. Soak 1.5kg chicken pieces overnight in 500ml buttermilk with salt. Dredge in 300g flour seasoned with paprika, garlic powder and pepper and fry at 165°C for 12 to 14 minutes until 74°C inside. Mash 1kg boiled potatoes with butter and milk. Make gravy with 30g butter, 2 tbsp flour, 400ml milk and lots of pepper. Boil 4 corn cobs and butter them. Toss shredded cabbage with mayo and vinegar.

Prom night caramel popcorn ballsVegetarian

Popcorn balls in brown sugar and golden syrup.

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 60g butter, plus extra for your hands
  • salt, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil and tip into a large buttered bowl.
  2. Boil the sugar, golden syrup and butter for 3 minutes, then stir in the salt and vanilla.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss with two spoons. Cool for 3 minutes.
  4. Butter your hands and press into 12 balls. Set on baking paper until firm, then wrap.

Pineapple orange punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Pineapple, orange and lemonade with lemon sorbet.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1l pineapple juice
  • 1l orange juice
  • 2 oranges, sliced
  • 1l cold lemonade
  • 500ml lemon sorbet
  1. Pour the pineapple and orange juice into a big punch bowl and add the sliced oranges.
  2. Top with the lemonade and float big scoops of sorbet on top so it foams.
