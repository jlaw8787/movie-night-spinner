Snacks

Inner Child toy shop popcornVegetarian

Buttered popcorn with sprinkles, chocolate buttons and pretzel pieces

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popping corn
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 3 tbsp sprinkles
  • 1 cup chocolate buttons
  • 1 cup broken pretzel pieces
  1. Pop the corn and toss with the melted butter.
  2. Add the sprinkles while the butter is warm so they stick.
  3. Stir in the chocolate buttons and pretzel pieces and tip into a bowl like a toy box.

Gorgonite pumpkin feta filo trianglesVegetarian

Roast pumpkin, feta and sage in crisp buttered filo

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g butternut pumpkin, diced
  • 150g feta, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp chopped sage
  • 1 packet filo pastry
  • 100g butter, melted
  1. Roast the pumpkin at 200°C until soft, then mash with the feta and sage.
  2. Cut buttered filo into strips, spoon the filling at one end and fold into triangles.
  3. Brush with butter and bake at 190°C until golden.

Chip Hazard's salami ration crackersOn the printed menu

Lined up like troops, sharp cheddar, crisped cured meat, a pickle slice and honey chilli mustard

How it is made
  • 16 slices salami
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 150g sharp cheddar
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Crackers lined up in rows like troops, topped with sharp cheddar, crisped salami, a slice of pickle and a dab of honey chilli mustard. Lay 16 slices of salami on a lined tray and bake at 200°C for 8 minutes until crisp, then cool. Mix 2 tbsp honey with 2 tbsp mild mustard. Top 16 crackers with a slice of sharp cheddar, a piece of crisp salami and a round of dill pickle. Add a dab of the honey mustard and line them up on a board in neat army rows.

Mains

Ohio barbecue ribs with corn and slaw

Foil-baked paprika pork ribs finished over coals, grilled corn, coleslaw

Prep 10 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Ribs wrapped up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork ribs
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1/4 small cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Rub the ribs with the paprika, sugar and salt, then wrap in foil.
  2. Bake at 150°C for 3 hours.
  3. Brush with barbecue sauce and finish over hot coals until sticky.
  4. Grill the corn. Toss the cabbage, carrot and mayonnaise into a crunchy coleslaw.

Power pole last stand chicken burgers

Fried chicken burger, Alan's house under siege

Prep 5 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken thigh fillets
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 500ml vegetable oil
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 100g dill pickles
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with salt for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Toss in the flour mixed with the paprika and fry in the oil at 170°C for 7 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and hot sauce for the comeback sauce.
  4. Toast the buns and fill with lettuce, tomato, chicken, pickles and sauce.

Army green peppermint squaresVegetarian

Cookie crumb base, green peppermint icing and dark chocolate on top

Prep 25 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 150g butter, melted, plus a little extra
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp peppermint essence
  • 1 tsp green food colouring
  • 200g dark chocolate, melted
  1. Mix the biscuits with most of the butter and press into a lined tin.
  2. Beat the icing sugar with a little butter, peppermint and green colouring and spread over.
  3. Pour the melted chocolate on top and chill until set.
  4. Cut into small squares.

Drinks

Commando Elite lime and mint fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Lime, mint and sugar syrup topped with soda water, served in camping mugs

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, mix fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml hot water
  • 1 cup mint leaves, plus 4 sprigs
  • 6 limes, 5 juiced and 1 sliced
  • 1l cold soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Muddle the mint leaves with the syrup in a jug. Add the lime juice and plenty of ice.
  3. Top with soda water and stir. Pour into four mugs with a lime wheel and mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the Commando Elite wake up in the toy shop.
