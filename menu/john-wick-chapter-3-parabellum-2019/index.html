Snacks

Zero's sushi counter

Hand shaped sushi rice topped with salmon or tuna, with pickled ginger and soy

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Rice and fish same day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp wasabi
  • 150g sashimi salmon
  • 150g sashimi tuna
  • 1 jar pickled ginger
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  1. Rinse the rice and cook it.
  2. Season with rice vinegar, sugar and salt and cool.
  3. Shape into fingers with a touch of wasabi under each.
  4. Slice the sashimi salmon and tuna and lay on top. Serve with pickled ginger and soy.

Amuse-bouche spiced olivesVegetarian

"Merely an amuse-bouche." Olives with preserved lemon, cumin and harissa, flatbread

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g green olives
  • 150g black olives
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp harissa
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Chop the preserved lemon rind and slice the garlic.
  2. Warm the olives in the oil with the lemon, cumin, harissa and garlic.
  3. Cool slightly and serve with flatbreads.

Library stack za'atar lavash and labnehOn the printed menu

Crisp book-sized flatbread sheets layered with whipped labneh, chilli oil, cucumber and mint

How it is made
  • 3 lavash breads
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 jar za'atar
  • 300g labneh
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Lavash cut into book-sized sheets and baked crisp with za'atar and olive oil, stacked with whipped labneh, chilli oil, cucumber and mint between the layers. Cut 3 lavash breads into rectangles the size of a paperback. Brush with olive oil, sprinkle with 2 tbsp za'atar and sea salt and bake at 180C for 8 minutes until crisp. Beat 300g labneh with lemon zest and a pinch of salt. Thinly slice a Lebanese cucumber. Stack 3 crackers high with labneh, cucumber, mint and a drizzle of chilli oil between each layer.

Mains

Moroccan lamb tagine

Lamb shoulder braised 3 hours with ras el hanout, dates and preserved lemon, couscous

Prep 35 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp ras el hanout
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 8 medjool dates
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 300g couscous
  • 40g flaked almonds
  1. Brown the lamb in batches and soften the onions.
  2. Add the ras el hanout, stock, dates and sliced preserved lemon.
  3. Cover and cook at 160C until the lamb is tender.
  4. Prepare the couscous and toast the almonds.
  5. Serve the tagine over couscous with the almonds.

Seven cuts salt and pepper squidOn the printed menu

Squid dusted in Sichuan salt and pepper and fried crisp, for seven cuts owed to the Bowery King

How it is made
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 800g squid tubes
  • 1 jar Sichuan peppercorns
  • 100g rice flour
  • 1.5L vegetable oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 lime
  1. Squid scored and dusted in rice flour, Sichuan pepper and salt, flash fried until crisp, then wok-tossed with chilli, garlic and spring onion. Steamed rice. Cook 300g jasmine rice. Cut 800g cleaned squid tubes open, score in a diamond pattern and cut into strips. Toast 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, grind with 2 tsp salt and mix with 100g rice flour. Toss the squid in the flour and fry at 190C for 60 seconds in batches. Stir-fry 3 sliced garlic cloves, 2 sliced chillies and 3 spring onions in a hot wok, toss in the squid and serve with rice and lime.

Sweets

Orange blossom semolina cakeVegetarian

Semolina and almond yoghurt cake soaked in orange blossom syrup

Prep 5 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g fine semolina
  • 100g almond meal
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 350g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 150ml water
  • 1 tbsp orange blossom water
  • 30g flaked almonds
  1. Mix the semolina, almond meal, yoghurt, part of the sugar and baking powder.
  2. Pour into a lined tin and bake at 180C.
  3. Boil the remaining sugar with the water, then add the orange blossom water.
  4. Pour the hot syrup over the cake and top with flaked almonds.

Moroccan mint teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Green tea steeped with fresh mint and sugar, poured from a height to froth

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Best brewed just before serving; measure the tea, mint and sugar into the pot up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp gunpowder green tea
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1l boiling water
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Put the tea, mint and sugar in a pot and add the boiling water.
  2. Steep.
  3. Pour from a height into little glasses so it froths.
