Snacks

Baseball team Korean fried chicken

Double fried wings in a sticky gochujang and soy glaze

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Simmer the glaze up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled, warm to toss; fry the wings just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 100g potato starch
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss in the potato starch.
  2. Fry in the oil at 160C.
  3. Rest.
  4. Fry again at 190C until very crisp.
  5. Simmer the gochujang, soy, honey and garlic until glossy.
  6. Toss the wings in the glaze and scatter with sesame seeds.

Spicy rice cakes

Chewy rice cakes simmered in a gochujang sauce (tteokbokki)

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Best straight from the pan as rice cakes firm up when cold; slice the fish cake and spring onions up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g rice cakes
  • 500ml stock
  • 4 dried anchovies
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150g fish cake, sliced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Soak the rice cakes in water.
  2. Simmer the stock with the anchovies, then add the gochujang, gochugaru and sugar.
  3. Add the rice cakes and fish cake and simmer until thick and glossy.
  4. Top with spring onion.

Mains

KTX to Busan kimchi fried rice

Bacon and kimchi fried with cold rice

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, chopped
  • 300g kimchi, chopped
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 30g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 sheet nori, torn
  1. Cook the rice and let it go cold.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  3. Add the kimchi and a splash of its juice.
  4. Add the cold rice, gochujang and butter and press flat to crisp the bottom.
  5. Top each bowl with a fried egg and torn nori.

Sweets

Su-an's birthday hotteokVegetarian

Yeast pancakes filled with brown sugar, cinnamon and peanuts

Prep 30 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Make the dough, fill and seal the balls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; press and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 220ml warm water
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 40g peanuts, chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and warm water into a dough and rest until risen.
  2. Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon and peanuts.
  3. Fill eight balls of dough with the mix and seal.
  4. Press flat in a pan brushed with the oil and fry until golden and the sugar melts inside.

Drinks

Banana milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Banana, milk and honey blended and strained smooth, no alcohol

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Blend, strain and chill the milk up to 1 day ahead; shake well before pouring
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bananas, ripe
  • 1l full cream milk, cold
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Blend the bananas with the milk, honey and salt until smooth.
  2. Strain through a sieve for a silky drink.
  3. Chill well.
  4. Pour into four glasses.

When to serve

Serve the snacks as the train pulls out of Seoul station.
