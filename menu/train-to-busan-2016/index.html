Snacks

Baseball team Korean fried chicken

Double fried wings in a sticky gochujang and soy glaze.

Prep 18 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Simmer the glaze up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled, warm to toss; fry the wings just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 100g potato starch
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss in the potato starch.
  2. Fry in the oil at 160°C.
  3. Rest.
  4. Fry again at 190°C until very crisp.
  5. Simmer the gochujang, soy, honey and garlic until glossy.
  6. Toss the wings in the glaze and scatter with sesame seeds.

Spicy rice cakes

Chewy rice cakes simmered in a gochujang sauce (tteokbokki)

Prep 2 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best straight from the pan as rice cakes firm up when cold; slice the fish cake and spring onions up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g rice cakes
  • 500ml stock
  • 4 dried anchovies
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150g fish cake, sliced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Soak the rice cakes in water.
  2. Simmer the stock with the anchovies, then add the gochujang, gochugaru and sugar.
  3. Add the rice cakes and fish cake and simmer until thick and glossy.
  4. Top with spring onion.

Station gimbap

Sesame rice rolls with egg, pickled radish, spinach and bulgogi beef, before the doors close.

How it is made
  • 400g short grain rice
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 200g thinly sliced beef
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 200g spinach
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 packet nori sheets
  • 1 packet yellow pickled radish
  1. Seaweed rolls of sesame rice packed with egg ribbon, pickled radish, carrot, spinach and bulgogi beef, sliced into rounds and brushed with sesame oil. Cook 400g short grain rice and season with 1 tbsp sesame oil and salt. Fry 200g thinly sliced beef with 2 tbsp soy and 1 tsp sugar. Cook 3 beaten eggs into a thin omelette and slice into strips. Blanch 200g spinach, squeeze and season with sesame oil. Julienne 1 carrot and quickly fry. Spread rice over 5 nori sheets, lay the fillings and strips of yellow pickled radish across, roll tightly, brush with sesame oil and slice.

KTX to Busan kimchi fried rice

Bacon and kimchi fried with cold rice.

Prep 12 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, chopped
  • 300g kimchi, chopped
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 30g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 sheet nori, torn
  1. Cook the rice and let it go cold.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  3. Add the kimchi and a splash of its juice.
  4. Add the cold rice, gochujang and butter and press flat to crisp the bottom.
  5. Top each bowl with a fried egg and torn nori.

Busan dwaeji gukbap pork and rice soup

Milky bone broth, tender sliced meat, chilli paste and kimchi, at the end of the line.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg pork bones
  • 600g pork belly
  • 1 onion
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece ginger
  • 300g short grain rice
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bunch garlic chives
  • 1 jar kimchi
  • 1 tub gochujang
  1. Milky pork bone broth with tender sliced pork, ladled over rice with spring onion, chilli paste and garlic chives, kimchi and salted prawns on the side. Blanch 1.5kg pork bones in boiling water for 10 minutes, drain and rinse. Cover with 3L fresh water, add 1 onion, 6 garlic cloves and a knob of ginger, and boil hard for 3 hours, topping up, until milky. Add 600g pork belly for the last 60 minutes, then slice. Strain the broth and season. Pour over bowls of cooked rice with the pork, spring onion and chives. Serve with kimchi and gochujang.

Sweets

Su-an's birthday hotteokVegetarian

Yeast pancakes filled with brown sugar, cinnamon and peanuts.

Prep 75 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the dough, fill and seal the balls up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; press and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 220ml warm water
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 40g peanuts, chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and warm water into a dough and rest until risen.
  2. Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon and peanuts.
  3. Fill eight balls of dough with the mix and seal.
  4. Press flat in a pan brushed with the oil and fry until golden and the sugar melts inside.

Drinks

Banana milkNo alcohol

Fresh banana blended with cold milk and honey, strained smooth. The train snack drink, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Blend, strain and chill the milk up to 1 day ahead; shake well before pouring
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bananas, ripe
  • 1 L full cream milk, cold
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Blend the bananas with the milk, honey and salt until smooth.
  2. Strain through a sieve for a silky drink.
  3. Chill well.
  4. Pour into four glasses.

When to serve

Serve the snacks as the train pulls out of Seoul station.
