Snacks

King Argotron's roast battle drumsticks

Garlic rosemary drumsticks glazed with honey and wholegrain mustard.

Prep 5 min, cook 54 min · Make ahead: Rub the drumsticks up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; roast and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 chicken drumsticks
  • vegetable oil, to taste
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • smoked paprika, to taste
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Rub the chicken drumsticks with the oil, the crushed garlic cloves, the chopped rosemary, the smoked paprika and salt.
  3. Roast on a lined tray for 35 minutes.
  4. Mix the honey and the wholegrain mustard, brush over and roast 10 minutes more until sticky.
  5. Wrap the bones in baking paper and serve on a board.

Honey soy chicken wings

Crisp baked chicken wings in a sticky honey soy glaze.

Prep 4 min, cook 59 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings with the baking powder and salt up to 1 day ahead and leave uncovered on a rack in the fridge to dry the skin; bake and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • soy sauce, to taste
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • sesame seeds, to taste
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C, 4 min.
  2. Toss the chicken wings with the baking powder and the salt and bake on a rack for 45 minutes, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Simmer the honey, the soy, the crushed garlic cloves, the grated ginger and the rice vinegar for 3 minutes until syrupy.
  4. Toss the wings in the glaze and scatter with sesame seeds and sliced spring onion.

Mains

Ronnie's double cheeseburgers and fries

Two smashed beef patties, cheese and pickle sauce, with paprika fries.

Prep 14 min, cook 41 min · Make ahead: Make the burger sauce up to 2 days ahead and roll the patties up to 1 day ahead, both chilled; bake the chips and smash the patties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen chips
  • paprika, to taste
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 cheese slices
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • tomato sauce, to taste
  • mustard, to taste
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 white onion
  • 4 burger buns
  1. Bake the frozen chips at 220°C for 25 minutes and toss with salt and paprika.
  2. Roll the beef mince into balls.
  3. Smash flat on a very hot plate, season and cook 2 minutes, flip, add cheese and cook 1 minute.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise, the tomato sauce, the mustard and chopped pickles.
  5. Toast the buns, spread with sauce and stack the patties with diced onion and pickles, 6 min.

Gayle's Sturdy Wings vegetable chilli with cornbreadVegetarian

Black and kidney beans, bell pepper and sweet potato with cumin, cornbread, eaten in KISS makeup.

Prep 9 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 3 days ahead, reheat gently; bake the cornbread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 400g sweet potato, diced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 800g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Soften the onion, garlic and capsicum in the oil.
  2. Add the sweet potato, cumin and chilli powder and cook for a minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and beans and simmer until the sweet potato is soft. Meanwhile bake the cornbread to the packet directions, 30 min.
  4. Serve with sour cream, cheddar and warm cornbread, 3 min.

Kiss-My-Anthia honey spice cakesVegetarian

Warm spiced honey cakes with orange honey glaze and thick cream.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake the cakes up to 1 day ahead in an airtight tin; warm them through, then glaze and serve with cream and almonds on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 230g honey
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • mixed spice, to taste
  • 80ml milk
  • 1 orange
  • 200ml thick cream
  • 30g flaked almonds
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C, 4 min.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, cool slightly, then whisk in the eggs, 4 min.
  3. Fold in the self-raising flour, the mixed spice and the milk.
  4. Divide between the greased muffin holes and bake 18 to 20 minutes.
  5. Warm the honey with the juice of the orange and spoon over the hot cakes, 6 min.
  6. Serve with thick cream and toasted flaked almonds.

Drinks

Minotaur ginger and lime fizzNo alcohol

Ginger and lime soda over ice, fizzing like a minotaur in a cape.

Prep 7 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Simmer, cool and strain the ginger syrup up to 5 days ahead in the fridge; add the lime juice and soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 100g ginger
  • 120g sugar
  • chilli flakes, to taste
  • 4 limes
  • 800ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 3 cups crushed ice
  1. Simmer the sliced ginger, the sugar, the water and a pinch of chilli flakes for 10 minutes.
  2. Cool and strain.
  3. Fill the tall glasses with crushed ice.
  4. Add the ginger syrup and the lime juice to each and top with the cold soda water.
  5. Stir and garnish with a lime wheel and mint.
