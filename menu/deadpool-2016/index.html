Sister Margaret's cheesy jalapeño nachosVegetarian

Corn chips under warm cheddar cheese sauce with pickled jalapeños

Prep 6 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 250g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeno juice
  • 300g corn chips
  • 1 handful of pickled jalapenos
  1. Warm the evaporated milk until steaming.
  2. Stir in the cheddar until smooth and add the jalapeno juice.
  3. Pour over the corn chips and scatter with jalapenos.

Skee-ball chilli lime caramel popcornVegetarian

Crunchy baked caramel popcorn with chilli powder, lime zest and salt

Prep 18 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 60g golden syrup
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 limes, zested
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil and spread on two lined trays.
  2. Boil the sugar, butter and golden syrup, then stir in the bicarbonate of soda, chilli, lime zest and salt.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss well.
  4. Bake at 120C, stirring every 15 minutes.
  5. Cool and break up.

Dopinder's potato and pea samosasOn the printed menu

Crisp fried triangles of spiced potato, cumin and green chilli, tamarind and mint chutneys

How it is made
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 jar cumin seeds
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 green chilli
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 jar garam masala
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 packet spring roll pastry
  • 1 jar tamarind chutney
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Crisp pastry triangles filled with spiced potato, peas, cumin seed, ginger and green chilli, fried until blistered, with tamarind and mint chutneys. Boil 600g potatoes, peel and dice. Fry 1 tsp cumin seeds in oil, add 1 tbsp grated ginger and 1 chopped green chilli, then the potatoes, 100g frozen peas, 1 tsp garam masala, coriander and salt, and cool. Cut 6 sheets of spring roll pastry into strips, fold into cones, fill and seal with flour paste. Fry at 175C for 4 minutes until golden. Serve with tamarind chutney and mint yoghurt.

Chimichangas

Fried flour tortillas packed with chipotle beef, black beans and cheddar

Prep 28 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days ahead, fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 chipotle chillies in adobo
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 1 tin of black beans, drained
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • sour cream, to serve
  • guacamole, to serve
  • salsa, to serve
  1. Braise the beef with the onion, chipotles and cumin until falling apart.
  2. Shred the beef.
  3. Fill the tortillas with beef, beans and cheddar and fold tight.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180C until crisp.
  5. Serve with sour cream, guacamole and salsa.

Blind Al's crispy beef and kimchi tacosOn the printed menu

Crispy shredded beef and kimchi in soft corn tortillas. Dinner with his flatmate Blind Al.

How it is made
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 tin tomato paste
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 limes
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 100g kimchi
  • 150g cheddar
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 1 jar hot salsa
  1. Smoky spiced beef mince in shells fried crisp to order, loaded with kimchi slaw, cheddar, pickled onion, sour cream and hot salsa, Seoul-meets-LA street style. Brown 600g beef mince with 1 diced onion and 2 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp oregano, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 150ml water and simmer 10 minutes. Fold 12 corn tortillas in half and shallow fry in hot oil for 1 minute a side until crisp. Quick-pickle a sliced red onion in lime juice and salt. Toss shredded iceberg with 100g chopped kimchi. Fill the shells with beef, kimchi slaw, cheddar, onion, sour cream and salsa.

Unicorn cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with rainbow buttercream swirls

Prep 15 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp pastel food colouring
  • 12 small gold fondant horns
  1. Heat the oven to 175C and line a 12-hole tin.
  2. Beat the cake butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  3. Bake until golden.
  4. Cool, beat the icing butter and icing sugar, split and colour with the pastel food colouring.
  5. Pipe rainbow stripes and add a fondant horn to each.

Sister Margaret's margaritaVegetarian

Tequila, orange liqueur and fresh lime shaken hard, salted rim, makes four

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; salt the rims and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 80ml orange liqueur
  • 100ml fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp salt, for the rim
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Salt the rims of four glasses.
  2. Shake the tequila, orange liqueur and lime juice hard with ice.
  3. Strain into the glasses.
