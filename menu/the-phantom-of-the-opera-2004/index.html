Opera managers' gougeresVegetarian

Choux puffs with grated alpine cheese and mustard, baked hollow, for the owners' first tour

Prep 19 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Pipe the gougeres and freeze them raw up to 1 month ahead, then bake from frozen for 5 extra minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 125ml milk
  • 100g butter
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g gruyere, finely grated
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Bring the milk, 125ml water, butter and salt to the boil, tip in the flour and beat over the heat until it comes away from the pan.
  2. Cool 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time, then 120g of the gruyere, the mustard and pepper.
  3. Pipe or spoon walnut-sized balls onto lined trays and scatter with the rest of the gruyere.
  4. Bake until puffed and deep golden, and serve warm.

Underground lake smoked trout rillettes

Carried across the black lake beneath the opera house

Prep 74 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge; take it out 20 minutes before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g hot-smoked trout
  • 150g butter, softened
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 bunch chives, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp baby capers, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
  • 100g cornichons
  1. Flake the trout, checking for bones.
  2. Mash with the butter, crème fraîche, lemon zest and juice, chives, capers and pepper, leaving it coarse.
  3. Pack into a dish, smooth the top and chill until set.
  4. Serve with the toasted baguette and cornichons.

Mains

The new patron's steak frites with bearnaise

Thick seared sirloin, double-fried chips, watercress and warm tarragon butter sauce, for Raoul

Prep 21 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the first fry of the chips up to 4 hours ahead; make the bearnaise and sear the steaks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sirloin steaks, about 250g each
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch tarragon, leaves chopped
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 180g butter, melted
  • 1kg large potatoes, cut into chips
  • 1 1/2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 bunch watercress
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips, rinse and dry, then fry at 140C in batches until soft but pale.
  2. Simmer the vinegar, shallot, half the tarragon and 2 tbsp water down to 1 tbsp and strain.
  3. Whisk the reduction and egg yolks in a bowl over simmering water until thick, then whisk in the melted butter in a thin stream. Stir in the rest of the tarragon and keep warm.
  4. Rub the steaks with the oil, half the salt and the pepper and sear in a very hot pan 3 minutes a side, then rest 5 minutes.
  5. Fry the chips again at 190C until crisp and golden, and toss with the rest of the salt.
  6. Serve the steaks with the chips, the bearnaise and watercress.

Masquerade sole meunière

Brown butter, lemon and capers, for the masked ball supper

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and reheat in butter; cook the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sole or flathead fillets, about 150g each
  • 50g plain flour
  • 100g butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 800g baby potatoes
  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, drain and toss with a knob of the butter.
  2. Season the fish and dust in the flour, shaking off the extra.
  3. Fry the fish in the oil and a little butter until golden on both sides. Lift onto warm plates.
  4. Add the rest of the butter to the pan and cook until nutty brown, then add the capers, juice of a lemon and chopped parsley. Spoon over the fish.

Red rose creme bruleeVegetarian

The single red rose tied with black ribbon

Prep 258 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake and chill the custards up to 2 days ahead; burn the sugar just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml thickened cream
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 140g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp rosewater
  • 100g raspberries
  • 1 handful edible rose petals, optional
  1. Heat the oven to 150C and warm the cream with the vanilla until steaming.
  2. Whisk the yolks with 80g of the sugar, pour in the hot cream, stir in the rosewater and strain.
  3. Crush the raspberries into 4 ramekins, pour over the custard and bake in a water bath until just set.
  4. Cool, then chill.
  5. Scatter the rest of the sugar over the tops, burn with a blowtorch or under a very hot grill and top with rose petals.

New Year's Eve kir royaleVegetarian

A splash of blackcurrant liqueur topped with chilled French sparkling wine, on the grand staircase

Prep 4 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the wine and glasses up to 1 day ahead; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 80ml blackcurrant liqueur
  • 750ml dry sparkling wine
  • 12 blackberries
  1. Chill the sparkling wine and 4 flutes.
  2. Pour 20ml blackcurrant liqueur into each flute.
  3. Top slowly with sparkling wine and drop in a few blackberries.
