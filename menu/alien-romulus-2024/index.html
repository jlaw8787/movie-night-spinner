Snacks

Jackson's Star miners' pasties

Crimped shortcrust pasties of diced beef, potato, swede and onion with plenty of black pepper.

Prep 23 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Make and crimp the pasties up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled, or freeze raw up to 1 month; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 350g beef skirt steak, finely diced
  • 1 potato, finely diced
  • 150g swede, finely diced
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 3 sheets of frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 20g butter
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • salt and plenty of black pepper, to taste
  1. Dice the steak, potato, swede and onion small and evenly.
  2. Mix together and season hard with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 4 large rounds from the pastry, pile the filling on one half of each, dot with the butter, fold over and crimp the edge, 12 min.
  4. Brush with the egg, cut a small steam hole and bake for 15 minutes, then turn the oven down to 170°C and bake 30 minutes more.
  5. Rest before serving, 5 min.

Andy's corny corn frittersVegetarian

Sweet corn and scallion fritters with lime sour cream.

Prep 11 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime sour cream up to 2 days ahead; fry the fritters just before serving, the batter can wait 2 hours in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 3 corn cobs
  • 1 egg
  • 80ml full cream milk
  • 75g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 bunch coriander, chopped
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 lime
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Stand each cob on its end and cut off the kernels, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the egg, milk, flour, baking powder and a good pinch of salt and pepper into a thick batter.
  3. Fold in the corn, spring onions and most of the coriander.
  4. Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry heaped spoonfuls for 3 minutes a side, in batches, until golden. Drain on paper towel.
  5. Mix the sour cream with the lime zest and juice, scatter the rest of the coriander over the fritters and serve, 3 min.

Jackson's Star never-sunrise beef and barley stew

Slow-braised beef chuck with carrot, celery and pearl barley in a thick gravy, with crusty bread.

Prep 21 min, cook 1 hr 54 min · Make ahead: Make the stew up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled, it gets better; loosen with a splash of water when you reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1.2 L beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 100g pearl barley, rinsed
  • 1 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Toss the beef in the flour with salt and pepper. Dice the onion, carrots and celery and crush the garlic.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot and brown the beef well in batches. Lift out, 12 min.
  3. Soften the onion, carrots, celery and garlic in the same pot.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, return the beef, add the stock, bay leaves and thyme and simmer, covered, on low, 1 hr 2 min.
  5. Stir in the barley and simmer, uncovered, until the barley is tender and the gravy thick, 40 min.
  6. Season, scatter with parsley and serve with torn crusty bread.

Renaissance station chicken katsu curry

Breaded chicken katsu with curry sauce and rice, hot from the station canteen.

Prep 5 min, cook 59 min · Make ahead: Make the curry sauce up to 2 days ahead and reheat; crumb the chicken up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 200ml vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 tbsp mild curry powder
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 300g short grain rice
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  1. Fry the chopped onion and carrots in 1 tbsp of the oil for 8 minutes, stir in 2 tbsp of the flour and the curry powder, then add the stock, soy and honey and simmer for 10 minutes. Blend smooth.
  2. Simmer the rice with 450ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, then rest for 5.
  3. Slice the cucumber thinly and toss with the rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar.
  4. Slice each breast in half through the middle. Dip in the rest of the flour, then beaten egg, then panko, 12 min.
  5. Shallow fry in the rest of the oil for 3 minutes a side until golden, then slice.
  6. Serve the chicken over rice with the curry sauce poured alongside and the cucumber.

Yvaga sunshine lemon drizzle cakeVegetarian

Buttery lemon loaf soaked in a crunchy lemon sugar drizzle while it is still warm.

Prep 14 min, cook 50 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; it gets stickier and better
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, softened
  • 175g caster sugar, for the cake
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 lemons, 2 zested, all 3 juiced
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp full cream milk
  • 100g caster sugar, for the drizzle
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a loaf tin with baking paper, 5 min.
  2. Beat the butter and cake sugar until pale and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time with the zest of 2 lemons, then fold in the flour and milk.
  4. Spoon into the tin and bake until risen and a skewer comes out clean, 45 min.
  5. Mix the juice of all 3 lemons with the drizzle sugar.
  6. Prick the hot cake all over with a skewer, pour over the drizzle and leave to cool in the tin, 30 min.

Cryo bay frozen lime slushieNo alcohol

Lime syrup blended with ice, topped with soda and mint.

Prep 5 min, cook 4 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Make and chill the lime syrup up to 1 week ahead; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 125ml water
  • 4 limes, juiced
  • 3 cups ice
  • 250ml soda water, chilled
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Simmer the sugar and water until dissolved, 4 min.
  2. Cool the syrup completely, 15 min.
  3. Blend the syrup, lime juice and ice until slushy.
  4. Pour into 4 glasses, top with soda water and add a mint sprig.
