Snacks

Marty Moose corn dogs

Cornmeal-battered franks fried golden, with mustard.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; dip and fry the franks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, egg and buttermilk into a batter.
  2. Pat the franks dry and push onto sticks.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C.
  4. Dip the franks in batter and fry until deep golden.
  5. Serve with mustard.

Family Truckster sweet and salty trail mixVegetarian

Peanuts, pretzels and cornflakes in honey butter, with chocolate and cranberries.

Prep 8 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200g roasted peanuts
  • 150g pretzel twists
  • 60g cornflakes
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 150g chocolate buttons
  • 100g dried cranberries
  1. Melt the butter with the sugar and honey.
  2. Toss with the peanuts, pretzels, cornflakes and salt on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 160°C, stirring halfway.
  4. Cool completely, then toss through the chocolate buttons and cranberries.
  5. Pack into paper bags or a lidded box.

Kansas cheese and chive corn fritters

Sweet kernel fritters with cheddar, fried crisp, chipotle sour cream, for the long drive west.

How it is made
  • 3 corn cobs
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml milk
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 500ml vegetable oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 tin chipotle in adobo
  • 1 lime
  1. Sweet corn fritters with cheddar and chives, fried crisp and golden, with a smoky chipotle sour cream for dipping. Mix 2 cups corn kernels (from 3 cobs) with 150g self-raising flour, 2 eggs, 125ml milk, 100g grated cheddar, chopped chives and salt. Heat 1cm oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Drop in heaped tablespoons and fry 2 minutes a side until golden. Drain on paper. Mix 200g sour cream with 1 chopped chipotle in adobo and a squeeze of lime for dipping.

Mains

Eddie's Kansas brisket bites

Smoked paprika brisket cubes roasted low and tossed in barbecue sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat covered
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef brisket point
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Cut the brisket into 4cm cubes.
  2. Rub with smoked paprika and salt.
  3. Roast at 140°C.
  4. Toss with barbecue sauce and brown sugar.
  5. Return to the oven until sticky.

Sweets

Walley World toffee applesVegetarian

Apples dipped in red hard toffee, served with a bag of fairy floss.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Wash, dry and stick the apples up to 1 day ahead; make and dip the toffee on the day and eat within 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 4 apples
  • 400g caster sugar
  • 125ml water
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 3 drops red food colouring
  • 1 bag fairy floss
  1. Push sticks into the apples.
  2. Boil the sugar, water and vinegar without stirring to 150°C.
  3. Add the red colouring.
  4. Dip and twirl each apple, then set on baking paper.
  5. Serve with fairy floss.

Drinks

Family Truckster strawberry lemonadeNo alcohol

Strawberry and lemon topped with soda water, with sliced fruit and ice.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the strawberry base up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add the cold and soda water and ice just before serving so it stays fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 300g strawberries
  • 5 lemons, juiced
  • 150ml sugar syrup
  • 1 L cold water
  • 500ml soda water
  • 1 cup ice
  1. Juice the lemons and blend most of the strawberries with the juice and syrup until smooth.
  2. Strain into a jug.
  3. Add the cold water and soda water and stir.
  4. Pour over ice with sliced strawberries and lemon.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the Griswolds pull out of the driveway in the Truckster.
