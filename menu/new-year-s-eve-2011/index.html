Snacks

Laura's prawn and avocado tostada bites

Prawn and avocado. First baby of the year wins $25,000

Prep 31 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Crisps and onion up to 1 day ahead, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 16 cooked king prawns
  • 3 limes
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch of coriander
  1. Cut small rounds from the tortillas with a 6cm cutter and brush with oil.
  2. Bake at 200C until crisp, then cool.
  3. Toss the peeled prawns with lime juice and chilli flakes.
  4. Mash the avocados with lime juice and salt.
  5. Thinly slice the red onion.
  6. Soak the onion in lime juice for 10 minutes.
  7. Top each crisp with avocado, a prawn, a few onion slivers and a coriander leaf.

Times Square halal cart chicken skewers

Spiced chicken on pita. Randy and Elise are stuck in the lift

Prep 28 min, cook 10 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 4 pita breads
  • 1 bottle of hot chilli sauce
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  1. Dice the chicken and toss with oil, lemon juice, cumin, turmeric, coriander, crushed garlic and salt.
  2. Marinate in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  3. Thread the chicken onto skewers.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise, yoghurt, vinegar, crushed garlic and a pinch of sugar into a white sauce.
  5. Grill the skewers, turning, until cooked through.
  6. Warm the pita and shred the lettuce.
  7. Serve the skewers on pita with white sauce, chilli sauce and lettuce.

Mains

Midnight garlic prawn linguine

Garlic prawn linguine. Stan sees the ball drop from the hospital roof

Prep 13 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Eat straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 400g linguine
  • 40g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 40g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 600g green prawns
  • 100ml white wine
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  1. Slice the garlic and chop the parsley.
  2. Cook the linguine in salted water until al dente.
  3. Toast the panko in oil until golden.
  4. Heat the butter and oil, add the garlic and chilli flakes and cook briefly.
  5. Add the prawns and cook until pink.
  6. Add the wine and bubble.
  7. Toss in the drained pasta with a splash of pasta water, lemon zest and juice and the parsley.
  8. Top with the crumbs.

Laura's record label party short ribs

Red wine short ribs over parmesan polenta, from Laura's catering at the record label party

Prep 6 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Braise up to 2 days ahead and reheat in the sauce
Recipe for 4
  • 1.6kg beef short ribs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 375ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 200g instant polenta
  • 1l milk
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  1. Heat the oven to 160C. Season the ribs and brown well in the oil in a heavy pot. Lift out.
  2. Fry the chopped onion, carrots and garlic for 5 minutes, stir in the tomato paste, then add the wine and stock and put the ribs back.
  3. Cover and braise in the oven for 3 hours until the meat falls off the bone.
  4. Bring the milk to the boil, whisk in the polenta and stir for 5 minutes, then beat in the parmesan and butter.
  5. Chop the parsley with the lemon zest and a little garlic for gremolata.
  6. Serve the ribs and sauce over the polenta with gremolata on top.

Sweets

Ingrid's resolutions chocolate fondue

Chocolate fondue, strawberries. Ingrid's list, ticked off with Paul

Prep 15 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Chocolate sauce up to 1 day ahead, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 1 orange
  • 250g strawberries
  • 100g marshmallows
  • 300g brownie
  • 100g pretzels
  1. Chop the chocolate and prepare the dippers on a platter.
  2. Heat the cream until steaming.
  3. Pour over the chocolate and leave to melt.
  4. Stir until glossy, then stir in orange zest and a pinch of salt.
  5. Keep warm in a fondue pot or a bowl over hot water.
  6. Serve with strawberries, marshmallows, brownie cubes and pretzels on skewers.

Drinks

Gin French 75 with champagneVegetarian

Gin, lemon, syrup, champagne on top. Masks optional.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml gin
  • 3 lemons
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 750ml champagne
  • 750ml sparkling white grape juice
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Chill the flutes. Shake the gin, lemon juice and sugar syrup with ice and strain into each flute.
  2. Top slowly with chilled champagne or sparkling wine.
  3. Add a long twist of lemon peel.
  4. For anyone not drinking, mix lemon juice and syrup with sparkling white grape juice.

When to serve

Serve the drinks as the ball drops at midnight.
