Snacks

Smoked salmon bites

Two dozen cucumber rounds with dill cream cheese, capers and cracked pepper.

Prep 19 min
Recipe for 4
  • 2 telegraph cucumbers
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill, plus sprigs
  • 1/2 lemon, zested
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the cucumbers into 1.5cm rounds and pat dry.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the chopped dill and lemon zest.
  3. Pipe or spoon onto each round.
  4. Top with salmon, capers, a dill sprig and pepper.

Toasted mini bagelsVegetarian

Three to an order, with scallion cream cheese and cracked pepper.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 mini bagels
  • 120g cream cheese
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Halve the bagels and toast under a medium grill until golden, 5 min.
  2. Whip the cream cheese with the spring onions and pepper.
  3. Spread over the eight halves and serve two halves per person.

Mains

Turkey and brie melt

Cranberry sauce on buttered sourdough, pressed hot until the cheese runs.

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 60g cranberry sauce
  • 300g sliced cooked turkey
  • 160g brie
  • 30g butter, softened
  1. Spread the sourdough with cranberry sauce.
  2. Divide the turkey and brie among four slices and close.
  3. Butter the outsides.
  4. Press in a hot sandwich pan or frying pan weighted with a second pan, turning once, until golden and the brie melts.
  5. Cut each sandwich in half.

Sweets

New York cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked rich and dense, with sugared fresh strawberries.

Prep 50 min, cook 1 hr, 1 day waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 180g digestive biscuits
  • 75g butter, melted
  • 600g cream cheese
  • 140g caster sugar
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  • 15g caster sugar for the berries
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a lined tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, sour cream, eggs and vanilla.
  3. Pour over the base and bake at 150°C until the centre gently wobbles, 1 hr.
  4. Cool, then chill overnight.
  5. Toss the strawberries with sugar and leave to macerate, 20 min.
  6. Cut four slices and serve with the strawberries and juices.

Fox Books cafe cappuccinoNo alcohol

Two shots of decaf espresso, steamed milk and a deep crown of foam.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 70g decaf coffee beans
  • 600ml full cream milk
  1. Grind the beans and brew four double espressos into warmed tall cups.
  2. Steam the milk in batches, with enough air for a deep foam cap, 5 min.
  3. Divide the milk among the cups and spoon the foam on top.
