Snacks

Publishing party salmon cucumber bites

Thick cucumber rounds with lemon dill cream cheese and capers, where Kathleen and Joe first bicker

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 telegraph cucumbers
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill, plus sprigs
  • 1/2 lemon, zested
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the cucumbers into 1.5cm rounds and pat dry.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the chopped dill and lemon zest.
  3. Pipe or spoon onto each round.
  4. Top with salmon, capers, a dill sprig and pepper.

Upper West Side bagelsVegetarian

Toasted minis with spring onion and pepper cream cheese, for a dog named Brinkley

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream cheese with the spring onions and pepper up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; toast the bagels just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 mini bagels
  • 120g cream cheese
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Halve the bagels and toast under a medium grill until golden.
  2. Whip the cream cheese with the spring onions and pepper.
  3. Spread over the eight halves and serve two halves per person.

Cash-only line caviar bliniOn the printed menu

Warm buckwheat pancakes, lemony crème fraîche, salmon roe and chives, from Zabar's

How it is made
  • 24 cocktail blini
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g salmon roe
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Warm buckwheat blini topped with crème fraîche, salmon roe and snipped chives, with a squeeze of lemon. Warm 24 cocktail blini on a tray at 180°C for 5 minutes. Mix 150g crème fraîche with the zest of half a lemon and a pinch of salt. Spoon a little onto each blini, top with half a tsp salmon roe and snipped chives. Serve straight away with lemon wedges.

Mains

Fox Books cafe sandwiches

Turkey, brie and cranberry pressed on sourdough, sold by the little shop's big rival

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill the sandwiches up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped in the fridge; butter the outsides and press them just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 60g cranberry sauce
  • 300g sliced cooked turkey
  • 160g brie
  • 30g butter, softened
  1. Spread the sourdough with cranberry sauce.
  2. Divide the turkey and brie among four slices and close.
  3. Butter the outsides.
  4. Press in a hot sandwich pan or frying pan weighted with a second pan, turning once, until golden and the brie melts.
  5. Cut each sandwich in half.

Sweets

Cafe cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked New York style with sugared strawberries, for the date with a book and a rose

Prep 50 min, cook 60 min, then 24.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 180g digestive biscuits
  • 75g butter, melted
  • 600g cream cheese
  • 140g caster sugar
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  • 15g caster sugar for the berries
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a lined tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar, sour cream, eggs and vanilla.
  3. Pour over the base and bake at 150°C until the centre gently wobbles.
  4. Cool, then chill overnight.
  5. Toss the strawberries with sugar and leave to macerate.
  6. Cut four slices and serve with the strawberries and juices.

Fox Books cafe cappuccinoVegetarianNo alcohol

A coffee order with lots of decisions

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Grind the beans up to 1 day ahead in an airtight container; brew and steam the milk to order
Recipe for 4
  • 72g decaf coffee beans
  • 600ml full cream milk
  1. Grind the beans and brew four double espressos into warmed tall cups.
  2. Steam the milk in batches, with enough air for a deep foam cap.
  3. Divide the milk among the cups and spoon the foam on top.
