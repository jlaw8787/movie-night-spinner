Snacks

Composure office crostiniVegetarian

Baguette, lemon pepper ricotta, roast cherry tomatoes.

Prep 2 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Components up to 1 day, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the baguette, brush with oil and toast at 200°C.
  2. Roast the tomatoes with oil and salt.
  3. Whip the ricotta with lemon zest and pepper.
  4. Spread on the toasts and top with tomatoes, basil and a little honey.

Princess Sophia's ham and pea arancini

Fried risotto balls with peas, ham and parmesan.

Prep 19 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, or freeze
Recipe for 4
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1 L chicken stock, hot
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 100g leg ham, diced
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 400g marinara sauce
  1. Cook the rice with the hot stock, added a ladle at a time, into a firm risotto.
  2. Stir through the peas, ham and parmesan, then spread out and cool.
  3. Roll into 16 balls and crumb in flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until golden.
  5. Warm the marinara and serve alongside.

Mains

Staten Island meatballs and rigatoni

Meatballs in tomato and basil sauce over rigatoni, for the Staten Island family weekend.

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cans of crushed tomatoes (400 g each)
  • 6 basil leaves
  • 500g rigatoni, cooked weight
  • 75g parmesan, grated
  1. Mix the mince with the breadcrumbs and egg, season and roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil.
  3. Add the tomatoes and torn basil and simmer.
  4. Cook the rigatoni, toss through the sauce with plenty of parmesan.

Diamond ball tenderloin and red wine

Beef roasted medium-rare, rich wine sauce, potato gratin and asparagus.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef eye fillet
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 200ml red wine
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 40g butter
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  1. Beef eye fillet roasted to medium-rare with a red wine sauce, potato gratin and asparagus. Season a 1kg beef eye fillet, sear all over in a hot pan and roast at 200°C for 20 to 25 minutes for medium-rare. Rest 10 minutes. Slice 1kg potatoes thinly, layer in a buttered dish with 300ml cream, garlic and salt and bake at 180°C for 60 minutes. Deglaze the beef pan with 200ml red wine, add 200ml beef stock and reduce by half, then whisk in 20g butter. Serve with steamed asparagus.

Yellow dress lemon tartVegetarian

Sweet pastry, baked lemon and cream, yellow as the gown.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 4 lemons
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream, for the filling
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped, to serve
  1. Brush the tart tin with the melted butter, press in the pastry and blind bake.
  2. Whisk the juice and zest of the lemons with the eggs, sugar and the cream for the filling.
  3. Pour into the case and bake at 150°C until just set.
  4. Chill until firm, then serve with whipped cream.

Drinks

Courtside ginger lime shandy

Lager, ginger beer and lime, cold enough for courtside.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep cold
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml lager
  • 750ml ginger beer
  • 2 limes
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Chill 4 tall cups or glasses.
  2. Squeeze a quarter of a lime into each, then pour in the lager, tilting the cup.
  3. Top with the ginger beer, drop in a lime wedge and mint sprig and serve straight away.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Ben takes Andie home to meet his family on Staten Island.
