Snacks

Trooper's bully beef rissoles with tomato relish

Bully beef from the ration tin, the troopers' standby on the ride to Beersheba

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Shape up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g potatoes
  • 340g tinned corned beef
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 egg
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g tomato relish
  1. Peel and boil the potatoes until tender, then drain and mash.
  2. Mix in the corned beef, grated onion, chopped parsley, egg and pepper.
  3. Shape into 12 small rissoles and roll in the breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in the oil for 4 minutes a side until crisp.
  5. Serve hot with the tomato relish.

Beersheba camp damper with honey butterVegetarian

Round damper loaf served warm with honey butter

Prep 23 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 160g butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour and salt.
  2. Stir in the milk to a soft dough and knead briefly.
  3. Shape into a round, cut a cross on top and dust with flour.
  4. Bake at 200C until it sounds hollow when tapped.
  5. Whip the soft butter with honey and sea salt.
  6. Serve the damper warm with the honey butter.

Meinertzhagen's decoy beef and stout stew

Chuck beef, carrot and onion slow-cooked in stout

Prep 30 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chuck steak
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 375ml stout
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch of thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  1. Dice the chuck, onions and carrots and toss the beef in flour.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  3. Add the onions, carrots and garlic and cook.
  4. Add the tomato paste, stout, stock, thyme and bay leaves.
  5. Cover and simmer gently until the beef is tender.
  6. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  7. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with butter and milk.
  8. Spoon the stew over the mash.

Dave Mitchell's Beersheba lamb and date pilaf

Lamb, rice and dates, for the new trooper Dave

Prep 20 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Simmer the lamb up to 2 days ahead; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 80g pitted dates, chopped
  • 50g flaked almonds
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the lamb in the oil in a heavy pot, add the onion and cook until soft.
  2. Add the cumin, cinnamon and 500ml stock and simmer covered until the lamb is tender.
  3. Stir in the rice, dates and the rest of the stock, cover and cook on low until the rice is done.
  4. Toast the almonds and scatter over with chopped parsley.

Camp golden syrup dumplingsVegetarian

Soft dumplings poached in golden syrup sauce

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 80g butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 egg
  • 80ml full cream milk
  • 125ml golden syrup
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 200ml thick cream
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the egg and milk to a soft dough.
  2. In a wide pan, bring the water, golden syrup, brown sugar, butter and a squeeze of lemon to the boil.
  3. Drop in spoonfuls of dough.
  4. Cover and simmer gently without lifting the lid.
  5. Serve in bowls with the sauce and thick cream.

Light horse Anzac biscuitsVegetarian

Chewy oat, coconut and golden syrup biscuits

Prep 20 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 150g plain flour
  • 80g desiccated coconut
  • 120g brown sugar
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  1. Mix the oats, flour, coconut and brown sugar.
  2. Melt the butter with golden syrup, then stir in bicarb dissolved in boiling water so it foams.
  3. Pour into the dry mix and stir well.
  4. Roll into balls and flatten slightly on lined trays, leaving room to spread.
  5. Bake at 170C until golden.
  6. Cool on the trays to crisp up.

Trooper's condensed milk billy teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong black tea sweetened with condensed milk

Prep 3 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Brew fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 395g condensed milk (tin)
  1. Bring the water to the boil in a billy or saucepan.
  2. Add the tea bags and take off the heat.
  3. Steep for a strong brew, then remove the bags.
  4. Spoon condensed milk into each enamel mug, pour over the hot tea and stir until creamy.

When to serve

Serve the billy tea when the horses line up for the charge.
