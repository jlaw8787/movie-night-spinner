Snacks

Trooper's bully beef rissoles with tomato relish

Bully beef from the ration tin.

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Shape up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g potatoes
  • 340g tinned corned beef
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 egg
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150g tomato relish
  1. Peel and boil the potatoes until tender, then drain and mash.
  2. Mix in the corned beef, grated onion, chopped parsley, egg and pepper.
  3. Shape into 12 small rissoles and roll in the breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in the oil for 4 minutes a side until crisp.
  5. Serve hot with the tomato relish.

Beersheba bazaar labneh and olivesVegetarian

Labneh swirled with olive oil and za'atar, olives and warm pita.

Prep 6 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Set out the labneh and olives up to 2 hours ahead; warm the pita to order
Recipe for 4
  • 500g labneh
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp za'atar
  • 200g mixed olives
  • 6 pita breads
  1. Spoon the labneh into a shallow dish, swirl with the oil and scatter with za'atar.
  2. Warm the pita in a dry pan, 5 min.
  3. Serve with the olives.

Meinertzhagen's decoy beef and stout stew

Chuck beef, carrot and onion slow-cooked in stout.

Prep 45 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chuck steak
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 375ml stout
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch of thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  1. Dice the chuck, onions and carrots and toss the beef in flour.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in oil, 15 min.
  3. Add the onions, carrots and garlic and cook, 5 min.
  4. Add the tomato paste, stout, stock, thyme and bay leaves.
  5. Cover and simmer gently until the beef is tender.
  6. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  7. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with butter and milk.
  8. Spoon the stew over the mash.

Dave Mitchell's Beersheba lamb and date pilaf

Lamb, rice and dates, for Dave, the new recruit.

Prep 0 min, cook 79 min · Make ahead: Simmer the lamb up to 2 days ahead; cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 80g pitted dates, chopped
  • 50g flaked almonds
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the lamb in the oil in a heavy pot, add the onion and cook until soft.
  2. Add the cumin, cinnamon and 500ml stock and simmer covered until the lamb is tender.
  3. Stir in the rice, dates and the rest of the stock, cover and cook on low until the rice is done.
  4. Toast the almonds and scatter over with chopped parsley, 4 min.

Jaffa orange and semolina cakeVegetarian

Syrupy orange and semolina cake, served warm. Jaffa oranges from the Palestine front.

Prep 0 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead; keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 oranges
  • 150g fine semolina
  • 100g almond meal
  • 75g self-raising flour
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Beat the butter with 200g of the sugar, then the eggs, orange zest, semolina, almond meal, flour and half the juice, 12 min.
  2. Bake in a lined 22cm tin until golden.
  3. Boil the rest of the juice with the rest of the sugar for 5 minutes and pour over the hot cake.

Light horse Anzac biscuitsVegetarian

Chewy oat, coconut and golden syrup biscuits.

Prep 20 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 150g plain flour
  • 80g desiccated coconut
  • 120g brown sugar
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  1. Mix the oats, flour, coconut and brown sugar, 5 min.
  2. Melt the butter with golden syrup, then stir in bicarb dissolved in boiling water so it foams, 3 min.
  3. Pour into the dry mix and stir well.
  4. Roll into balls and flatten slightly on lined trays, leaving room to spread.
  5. Bake at 170°C until golden.
  6. Cool on the trays to crisp up, 15 min.

Trooper's condensed milk billy teaNo alcohol

Strong black tea sweetened with condensed milk.

Prep 7 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Brew fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 395g condensed milk (tin)
  1. Bring the water to the boil in a billy or saucepan, 8 min.
  2. Add the tea bags and take off the heat.
  3. Steep for a strong brew, then remove the bags, 5 min.
  4. Spoon condensed milk into each enamel mug, pour over the hot tea and stir until creamy.

When to serve

Serve the billy tea when the line forms up for the charge.
