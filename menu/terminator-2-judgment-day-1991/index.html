Snacks

Border run chicken empanadas

Baked pastry turnovers filled with smoky chicken, egg and olives.

Prep 20 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Filled and uncooked up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken thigh mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 3 eggs
  • 50g pitted green olives, chopped
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  1. Boil two of the eggs until hard, then cool, peel and chop.
  2. Fry the chicken mince and onion with the cumin and paprika until browned.
  3. Stir in the chopped eggs and olives and cool the filling.
  4. Cut the pastry into 12 rounds, fill, fold and crimp the edges.
  5. Brush with the remaining beaten egg. Bake at 200°C until golden.

Biker bar bean and cheese quesadillasVegetarian

Crisp tortillas with refried beans, cheddar and jalapeños, salsa and sour cream.

Prep 10 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the quesadillas up to 2 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; toast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 400g refried beans
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños, sliced
  • 200g tomato salsa
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Spread each tortilla with refried beans over half, scatter with cheddar and jalapeños and fold over.
  2. Toast in a dry pan over medium heat, pressing down, until crisp and the cheese melts, turning once.
  3. Cut into wedges and serve with the salsa and sour cream.

Mains

Desert camp tacos

Lime and cumin marinated skirt steak in corn tortillas with dried chilli salsa.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 2 limes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 dried chillies
  • 1 tomato
  • 16 corn tortillas
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Marinate the steak in lime juice, three of the garlic cloves and the cumin in the fridge.
  2. Soak the dried chillies in hot water, then blitz with the tomato and remaining garlic for a salsa roja.
  3. Grill the steak on high heat, then rest and slice against the grain.
  4. Warm the tortillas and serve with the steak, salsa, onion and coriander.

Galleria food court double cheeseburger

Two thin smashed patties, American cheese, pickles, lettuce and tangy sauce.

Prep 7 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince balls and mix the sauce up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato relish
  • 1 tsp American mustard
  • 1 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1 jar dill pickles, sliced
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls and season well with salt and pepper.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, relish and mustard for the sauce.
  3. Heat a griddle or heavy pan until smoking, add the balls and smash flat with a spatula.
  4. Cook 2 minutes until crusty, flip and top each patty with a slice of cheese for 1 minute.
  5. Toast the buns, then stack two patties on each with sauce, lettuce and pickles. Wrap in baking paper for 2 minutes.

Enrique's camp churrosVegetarian

Fried churros rolled in cinnamon sugar with a hot chocolate dip.

Prep 3 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Best eaten within the hour
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 30g butter
  • pinch of salt
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  1. Boil the water, butter and salt, then beat in the flour until it forms a ball. Cool, then beat in the egg.
  2. Heat the oil to 180°C and pipe 12cm lengths with a star nozzle, fry until golden.
  3. Roll in the sugar mixed with the cinnamon.
  4. Heat the cream and stir in the chocolate until smooth for dipping.

Drinks

T-1000 frozen margaritaVegetarian

Tequila, lime, orange liqueur, frozen solid. Hasta la vista, baby.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the limes and mix the juice, tequila, liqueur and syrup up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; blend with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 5 limes
  • 180ml tequila
  • 60ml orange liqueur
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 4 cups ice
  • 1 tbsp salt flakes, for the rims
  1. Rub the glass rims with lime and dip in salt.
  2. Blend the tequila, the juice of the remaining limes, orange liqueur, sugar syrup and ice until smooth and slushy.
  3. Pour into the glasses and garnish with lime wheels. Serve immediately.
