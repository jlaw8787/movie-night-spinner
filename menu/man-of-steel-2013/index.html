Snacks

School bus corn frittersVegetarian

Pan fried sweetcorn fritters with scallion and paprika.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled; stir the chive dip up to 2 days ahead. Fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 can corn kernels, drained
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Mix the corn with flour, eggs, spring onion and paprika.
  2. Fry spoonfuls in a little oil until golden on both sides, 10 min.
  3. Stir chives through sour cream for the dip and serve the fritters warm.

Fishing trawler fish bites

Cod pieces dusted in paprika flour and fried crisp, with lemon.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Cut the cod into pieces up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Best dusted and fried just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cod fillets
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 2 lemons
  • 120g tartare sauce
  1. Cut the cod into bite-sized pieces and dust in flour mixed with paprika and salt.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until crisp, 8 min.
  3. Drain on paper and serve with lemon wedges and tartare sauce.

Roadside bar buffalo chicken tenders

Buttermilk tenders fried and tossed in hot sauce butter with blue cheese dip, roadside bar style.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken tenderloins
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch celery
  1. Buttermilk chicken tenders fried crisp and tossed in buttery hot sauce, with celery sticks and a cool blue cheese dip. Soak 800g chicken tenderloins in 250ml buttermilk with salt for 1 hour. Toss in 150g plain flour mixed with 1 tsp paprika and 1 tsp garlic powder, and fry at 180°C for 5 minutes until golden. Melt 60g butter with 120ml hot sauce and toss the chicken through. Mix 80g crumbled blue cheese with 100g sour cream and 2 tbsp mayo. Serve with celery sticks.

Martha's farmhouse pot roast

Beef chuck braised with carrots, celery and potatoes.

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef chuck
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 800g potatoes
  1. Brown the beef all over, 15 min.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, celery and stock.
  3. Cover and braise at 160°C, 2 hr.
  4. Add the potatoes and braise for the last hour, 1 hr.
  5. Slice and serve with the juices.

Daily Planet chicken club sandwich

Triple-decker of roast chicken, bacon, avocado and tomato, triangles, on Perry White's deadline.

How it is made
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 400g roast chicken breast
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 packet potato chips
  1. Triple-decker toasted club of roast chicken, crisp bacon, avocado, tomato, lettuce and mayo, cut into triangles with a pickle and chips. Fry 8 bacon rashers until crisp. Toast 12 slices of white bread. Slice 400g roast chicken breast, 2 tomatoes and 1 avocado. Spread 8 slices of toast with mayo. Build each sandwich: toast, lettuce, chicken and tomato, then toast, bacon and avocado, then a final slice. Hold with 4 toothpicks, cut into 4 triangles and serve with pickles and potato chips.

Kent farm apple pieVegetarian

Green apples with cinnamon and brown sugar under a woven pastry top.

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 green apples, sliced
  • 50g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Cook the apples with butter, sugar and cinnamon until just soft, then cool, 15 min.
  2. Line a pie dish with pastry, fill and weave a lattice top.
  3. Bake at 190°C until golden, 45 min.
  4. Serve warm with ice cream.

Kansas farm strawberry basil lemonadeNo alcohol

Blended strawberries and bruised basil in fresh lemonade.

Prep 30 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add ice at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 250g strawberries, hulled
  • 1/4 cup basil leaves
  • 200ml lemon juice (about 6 lemons)
  • 1 L cold water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar and water until dissolved, then cool, 10 min.
  2. Blend the strawberries with the syrup and strain.
  3. In a big jug, bruise the basil, add the strawberry syrup, lemon juice and cold water and stir.
  4. Add lots of ice and sliced strawberries and lemons and top with a basil leaf.
