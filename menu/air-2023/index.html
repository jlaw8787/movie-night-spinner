Snacks

Corner store chilli cheese taquitos

Crisp rolled tortillas with beef chilli and cheese.

Prep 13 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli filling up to 2 days ahead; roll and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 200g tomato passata
  • 150g tasty cheese, grated
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 200g tomato salsa
  1. Brown the mince with the onion, then add the cumin, chilli and passata and simmer until thick.
  2. Warm the tortillas so they bend, then fill each with chilli and cheese and roll up tight.
  3. Brush with oil and bake seam-side down at 220°C until crisp.
  4. Serve with sour cream and salsa.

High roller prawn cocktail

Cold king prawns in Marie Rose sauce on iceberg.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 2 days ahead; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked king prawns, peeled
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
  • 5 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 avocado, diced
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, Worcestershire and a squeeze of lemon together.
  2. Fill 4 coupes with lettuce and avocado.
  3. Pile on the prawns, spoon over the sauce and dust with paprika.
  4. Serve cold with lemon wedges.

Mains

Wilmington vinegar pulled pork

Carolina vinegar pork and slaw in soft white rolls.

Prep 30 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Cook the pork up to 2 days ahead and chill in its juices; reheat and pull to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder, boneless
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250ml apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 8 soft white rolls
  • 1/4 green cabbage, finely shredded
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Rub the pork with the sugar, paprika and salt and sit it in a lidded pot with 250ml water.
  2. Cover and cook at 160°C until it pulls apart easily.
  3. Shake the vinegar, chilli flakes and tomato sauce together, then pull the pork and toss it through.
  4. Dress the cabbage with the mayo and a splash of the sauce and pile into the rolls with the pork.

Olympic coach's steak frites

Grilled sirloin with fries and pepper sauce. Dinner with George Raveling.

Prep 3 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Par-cook the fries up to a day ahead; finish them and grill the steaks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 250g sirloin steaks
  • 1kg floury potatoes, cut in chips
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g butter
  • 1 French shallot, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Boil the fries for 5 minutes, drain, toss with 2 tbsp oil and roast at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Season the steaks and sear in the remaining oil to your liking, then rest.
  3. In the same pan melt the butter, soften the shallot, add the pepper, cream and mustard and bubble until thick.
  4. Serve the steaks with the fries and sauce.

Air Jordan red velvet cakeVegetarian

Red velvet layers with cream cheese and dark chocolate.

Prep 32 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Bake the layers up to 2 days ahead and wrap well; ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 250ml neutral oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 250g cream cheese, soft
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 100g dark chocolate, finely grated
  1. Whisk the flour, cocoa, bicarb and sugar, then beat in the oil, eggs, buttermilk, colouring and vinegar.
  2. Split between 2 lined 20cm tins and bake at 175°C until a skewer comes out clean, then cool.
  3. Beat the cream cheese, butter and icing sugar until fluffy.
  4. Sandwich and cover the cakes with the icing and press the grated chocolate around the sides.

Drinks

Every pair sold old fashioned

Bourbon stirred with bitters and orange peel. Deloris wants a cut.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to a week ahead; stir to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 8 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 4 wide strips orange peel
  • 4 dark cocktail cherries
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Stir the bourbon, syrup and bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain over fresh ice in 4 short glasses.
  3. Twist the orange peel over each glass and drop in with a cherry.
