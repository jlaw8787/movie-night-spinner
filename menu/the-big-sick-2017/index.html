Comedy club heckler masala friesVegetarian

Thin oven roasted chips with masala spice

Prep 10 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Cut and soak the chips up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; make the mint yoghurt up to 2 days ahead and roast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1/2 lime
  • 1 handful coriander
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 handful mint leaves
  • salt, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 220C. Cut the potatoes into thin chips and soak, then drain and dry well.
  2. Toss with the oil and roast on lined trays, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Toss hot with chaat masala, chilli, salt and lime juice. Scatter with chopped coriander and diced red onion.
  4. Blend the yoghurt with the mint and a pinch of salt for dipping.

Surprise guest samosasVegetarian

Fried pastry triangles of spiced potato, peas and onion

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Fill up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet spring roll pastry
  • 600g potatoes
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, for the filling
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 handful mint leaves
  • 1 handful coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp water
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and crush roughly.
  2. Fry the chopped onion in the oil for the filling, then stir through the potato, peas and garam masala. Cool.
  3. Cut the pastry into strips, fill and fold into triangles.
  4. Fry in the oil for frying at 180C in batches until crisp.
  5. Blitz the mint and coriander with the water, lemon juice and salt for the chutney.

Hospital cafeteria toasties with tomato soupOn the printed menu

Cheddar and mozzarella grilled crisp in dippers, roasted basil soup shots, with Emily's parents

How it is made
  • 1kg tomatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 400ml vegetable stock
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 120g cheddar
  • 80g mozzarella
  • 40g butter
  1. Cheddar and mozzarella toasties grilled crisp in butter and cut into dippers, with shots of roasted tomato and basil soup on the side. Roast 1kg halved tomatoes, 1 onion in wedges and 4 garlic cloves with oil and salt at 200C for 35 minutes. Blitz with 400ml stock and a handful of basil, simmer 5 minutes and season. Fill 8 slices of white bread with 120g grated cheddar and 80g grated mozzarella, butter the outsides and cook in a pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side. Cut into fingers and serve with the soup in small cups.

Kumail's mum's chicken biryani

Yoghurt marinated chicken layered with basmati and crisp fried onions

Prep 10 min, cook 75 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate and assemble a day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 500g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp garam masala
  • 2 brown onions
  • 100ml vegetable oil
  • 400g basmati rice
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 60ml warm milk
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the chicken with the yoghurt, garam masala and salt, then marinate in the fridge.
  2. Slice the onions and fry in the oil until crisp.
  3. Par-cook the basmati for a few minutes and drain.
  4. Layer the chicken, rice and onions in a heavy pot and drizzle with saffron soaked in the warm milk.
  5. Seal the pot and bake at 170C.

Chicago deep dish sausage pizzaOn the printed menu

Buttery crust up a tall tin, mozzarella, fennel sausage and garlic tomato, between Uber shifts

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 60g butter
  • 300g mozzarella
  • 400g Italian sausages
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • dried oregano
  • 40g parmesan
  1. A thick, buttery crust pressed up the sides of a deep tin, layered with mozzarella, fennel sausage and chunky garlic tomato sauce, baked until bubbling. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g yeast, 1 tsp salt, 60g melted butter and 230ml warm water, knead 5 minutes and rest 1 hour. Heat the oven to 220C. Press into an oiled 28cm cake tin and up the sides. Layer 300g sliced mozzarella, then 400g Italian sausage meat in flat patches. Simmer 400g crushed tomatoes with 2 garlic cloves and oregano for 10 minutes and spread over the top. Scatter 40g parmesan and bake 30 to 35 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.

Cardamom rice pudding (kheer)Vegetarian

Basmati rice simmered in milk with sugar and cardamom, served warm

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g basmati rice
  • 1 1/2l full cream milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
  • 50g pistachios, chopped
  1. Simmer the rice in the milk over low heat, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  2. Stir in the sugar and cardamom.
  3. Serve warm or chilled with pistachios scattered on top.

Ginger cardamom chaiVegetarianNo alcohol

Black tea simmered with ginger, cardamom, cinnamon and milk

Prep 5 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml water
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp raw sugar, or to taste
  1. Boil the water with the ginger, cardamom and cinnamon.
  2. Add the tea bags and simmer.
  3. Pour in the milk and sugar, simmer, then strain into cups.
