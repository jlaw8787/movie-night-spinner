Snacks

Spaceball One pretzelsVegetarian

Pretzel rods dipped in dark chocolate with a striped candy melt plaid.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 20 pretzel rods
  • 100g coloured candy melts
  1. Melt the dark chocolate, 5 min.
  2. Dip each pretzel two-thirds of the way and set on baking paper, 10 min.
  3. Melt the candy melts and pipe stripes across in two directions for plaid, 10 min.
  4. Let set, 5 min.

Yogurt's snack mix

Cereal, pretzel and peanut mix baked in honey butter, with mini marshmallows.

Prep 4 min, cook 23 min, 13 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cereal squares
  • 100g pretzels
  • 100g peanuts
  • 50g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 100g mini marshmallows
  1. Heat the oven to 140°C. Melt the butter with the honey, 3 min.
  2. Toss the cereal, pretzels and peanuts with the honey butter.
  3. Bake, stirring once, 20 min.
  4. Cool completely, then toss through the marshmallows, 13 min.

Mains

Pizza the Hutt

Deep pan pizza loaded with mozzarella, provolone, cheddar and pepperoni.

Prep 45 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheeses up to 1 day ahead, chilled; prove the dough, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pizza dough
  • 1 jar pizza sauce
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 100g provolone
  • 50g parmesan
  • 100g cheddar
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Oil a deep tray and press the dough in.
  2. Cover and prove until puffy, 30 min.
  3. Spread with sauce and pile on the mozzarella, provolone, parmesan, cheddar and pepperoni.
  4. Bake at 220°C until molten and golden, 20 min.

Moon diner corned beef hash and eggs

Crisp diced potatoes, onion and shredded salt beef, two sunny side up, toast, hello my baby.

How it is made
  • 800g potatoes
  • 30g butter
  • 1 onion
  • 400g cooked corned beef
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 bunch chives
  • hot sauce
  • 4 slices bread
  1. Diced potatoes fried crisp with onion and shredded corned beef, two runny fried eggs on top, chives, hot sauce and buttered toast. Boil 800g diced potatoes for 6 minutes and drain well. Fry in 3 tbsp oil and 30g butter for 12 minutes until golden. Add 1 diced onion and cook 5 minutes, then fold in 400g shredded cooked corned beef and press flat for 5 minutes to crisp the bottom. Fry 8 eggs. Serve the hash topped with two eggs each, chives, hot sauce and buttered toast.

Yoghurt parfaitVegetarian

Honey yoghurt, granola and berries, layered high, even with strawberries.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make just before serving so the granola stays crunchy
Recipe for 4
  • 500g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g granola
  • 250g mixed berries
  1. Whip the yoghurt with the honey and vanilla.
  2. Layer in four tall glasses with the granola and berries.
  3. Finish with berries on top and serve straight away.

Drinks

Perri-Air sparkling lime and mint coolerNo alcohol

Lime, mint and cucumber with sparkling water over ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Muddle the mint, cucumber and sugar syrup and juice the limes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 12 mint leaves
  • 4 slices of cucumber
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 4 limes
  • 600ml sparkling water
  • 1 bag ice
  • 4 mint sprigs, to garnish
  1. Muddle the mint, cucumber and sugar syrup in a jug.
  2. Add the juice of three limes and stir. Slice the last lime into wheels.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice, divide the mix between them and top with cold sparkling water.
