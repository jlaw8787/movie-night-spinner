Snacks

Bush damper with golden syrupVegetarian

Round oven-baked damper loaf, served with butter and golden syrup

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Rub the butter into the flour and salt up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; add the milk and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, cold, for the dough
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 300ml milk
  • 50g butter, for serving
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Rub the cold butter into the flour and salt.
  2. Mix in the milk to a soft dough, shape into a round and score a cross.
  3. Bake until it sounds hollow when tapped.
  4. Serve warm with the butter for serving and golden syrup.

Wally's Walkabout Creek snags

Walkabout Creek welcomes Mick home. Beef snags in white bread, fried onions, tomato sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; slice the onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 2 brown onions
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Grill the sausages slowly over medium heat, turning often, and fry the onions in oil until soft and golden at the same time.
  3. Wrap each sausage in a slice of bread with onions and a squeeze of tomato sauce.

Mains

Dynamite-caught campfire barra

Whole barramundi stuffed with lemon, ginger and coriander, wrapped in paperbark, cooked over coals.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Stuff and wrap the fish in foil up to 4 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge; cook over the coals on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg whole barramundi, cleaned
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 50g butter
  1. Stuff the barramundi with lemon slices, sliced ginger and the coriander.
  2. Dot with butter and wrap well in foil.
  3. Cook over hot coals or a hot grill, turning once.
  4. Rest briefly before opening the parcel.

I am home pavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue with whipped cream, passionfruit and sliced mango

Prep 28 min, cook 90 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and cool the meringue up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the turned-off oven or an airtight container; whip the cream and top on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 1 mango
  1. Whip the egg whites to soft peaks.
  2. Add the sugar gradually until glossy, then fold in the cornflour and vinegar.
  3. Pile onto a lined tray and shape into a round.
  4. Bake at 120C.
  5. Turn the oven off and leave the pavlova to cool inside.
  6. Top with whipped cream, passionfruit and sliced mango.

Drinks

Top End lemon myrtle gin and tonicVegetarian

Gin and tonic with a lemon myrtle sugar rim and a squeeze of lime

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the lemon myrtle sugar for the rims up to 1 week ahead in a jar; build the glasses to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground lemon myrtle
  • 4 limes
  • 1 tray ice
  • 180ml gin
  • 600ml tonic water
  1. Mix the sugar and lemon myrtle on a plate. Run a lime around each glass rim and dip.
  2. Fill the glasses with ice, add gin and lime juice.
  3. Top with tonic and add a lime wheel.
