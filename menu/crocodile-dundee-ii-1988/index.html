Snacks

Billabong yabby rolls

Yabby tails in lemon mayo on buttered soft rolls.

Prep 12 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Mix the filling up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fill the rolls to order
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked yabby, bug tail or prawn meat
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 8 soft rolls
  • 30g butter
  1. Chop the yabby meat and mix with the mayonnaise, lemon zest and juice and chopped chives.
  2. Split, butter and lightly toast the rolls.
  3. Fill the rolls and serve cold.

Wally's Walkabout Creek snags

Beef snags in white bread, fried onions, tomato sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; slice the onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick beef sausages
  • 2 brown onions
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  1. Slice the onions.
  2. Grill the sausages slowly over medium heat, turning often, and fry the onions in oil until soft and golden at the same time.
  3. Wrap each sausage in a slice of bread with onions and a squeeze of tomato sauce.

Dynamite-caught campfire barra

Whole barramundi stuffed with lemon, ginger and coriander, wrapped in paperbark, cooked over coals.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Stuff and wrap the fish in foil up to 4 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge; cook over the coals on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg whole barramundi, cleaned
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 knob ginger, sliced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 50g butter
  1. Stuff the barramundi with lemon slices, sliced ginger and the coriander.
  2. Dot with butter and wrap well in foil.
  3. Cook over hot coals or a hot grill, turning once.
  4. Rest briefly before opening the parcel.

Bush honey macadamia pancakesVegetarian

Thick pancakes under toasted macadamias and honey, a camp breakfast.

Prep 7 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml milk
  • 30g butter
  • 80g macadamias
  • 4 tbsp honey
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, eggs and milk into a thick batter.
  2. Toast and roughly chop the macadamias in a dry pan.
  3. Cook ladlefuls in a buttered pan for 2 minutes a side, in batches.
  4. Stack and top with the macadamias and honey.

Top End gin and tonic

Over plenty of ice with a squeeze of lime, cold enough for the Top End.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the lemon myrtle sugar for the rims up to 1 week ahead in a jar; build the glasses to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground lemon myrtle
  • 4 limes
  • 1 tray ice
  • 180ml gin
  • 600ml tonic water
  1. Mix the sugar and lemon myrtle on a plate. Run a lime around each glass rim and dip.
  2. Fill the glasses with ice, add gin and lime juice.
  3. Top with tonic and add a lime wheel.
