Snacks

San Gennaro sausage bites

Charred and sliced, straight from the street feast

Prep 11 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Cook up to 2 hours ahead and reheat in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 500g Italian sausages
  • 1 red capsicum, in chunks
  • 1 onion, in chunks
  • 50g hot mustard
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Grill the sausages over high heat, turning, until blistered and cooked through.
  2. Toss the capsicum and onion in oil and grill until charred at the edges.
  3. Slice the sausages thick and thread onto toothpicks with the capsicum and onion.
  4. Serve with the hot mustard.

Fried calamari

Crisp squid rings with lemon, shared round the bar

Prep 9 min, cook 8 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak up to 2 hours ahead; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g squid tubes
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine semolina
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 2 lemons, cut in wedges
  1. Slice the squid into rings and soak in the buttermilk for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the semolina, flour and plenty of salt.
  3. Drain the squid and toss in the flour mix.
  4. Fry in 190°C oil in batches for 90 seconds each until pale gold.
  5. Salt and serve with lemon wedges.

Penne arrabbiataVegetarian

Chilli tomato penne, as hot as Johnny Boy's temper

Prep 5 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried chilli flakes
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g crushed tomatoes
  • 500g penne
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 50g pecorino, grated
  • salt, to taste
  1. Gently fry the garlic and chilli in the olive oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes, season well and simmer.
  3. Cook the penne until al dente and toss through the sauce with a splash of pasta water and parsley.
  4. Finish with the pecorino.

ZeppoleVegetarian

Icing sugar, straight from the street feast

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Batter can rise up to 1 hour; eat hot
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 200ml warm water
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 60g icing sugar
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm water into a sticky batter.
  2. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  3. Drop teaspoons into 175°C oil and fry in batches for 3 minutes until puffed and golden.
  4. Drain, tip into a paper bag with icing sugar, shake and eat hot.

Little Italy red wine with white peachesVegetarian

Light red over sugared fruit, soaked and spooned into tumblers, on Johnny Boy's tab

Prep 12 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe white peaches
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 750ml light Italian red, such as Lambrusco secco or young Chianti
  1. Halve, stone and thinly slice the peaches into a large jug. Sprinkle with the sugar and leave for 10 minutes.
  2. Pour over the light Italian red and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  3. Serve in tumblers with a spoonful of peach in each. Eat the peaches last.
