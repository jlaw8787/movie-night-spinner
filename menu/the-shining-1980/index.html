Snacks

Overlook storeroom antipasto board

Warm orange olives, salami, provolone, pickles, artichokes and grissini.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Warm and cool the olives up to 3 days ahead, covered in the fridge; slice the meat and cheese and arrange the board on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g mixed olives
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 1 orange, peel in strips
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 150g salami
  • 150g provolone
  • 200g giardiniera pickled vegetables
  • 100g marinated artichokes
  • 1 packet grissini
  • 8 cornichons
  1. Warm the olives in the oil with orange peel, garlic and chilli flakes, then cool.
  2. Slice the salami and provolone.
  3. Arrange on a big board in tidy rows with the olives, giardiniera, artichokes, grissini and cornichons.
  4. Serve at room temperature.

Gold Room spiced nutsVegetarian

Mixed nuts roasted with rosemary, brown sugar, butter and cayenne.

Prep 12 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 400g mixed unsalted nuts
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Toss the nuts with the butter, sugar, rosemary, salt and cayenne.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 180°C, stirring halfway.
  3. Cool so the coating sets.
  4. Serve warm in small bowls.

Hedge maze herb crackers and feta dip

Parmesan and rosemary squares with a snow-white feta dip, for the snowed-in caretakers.

How it is made
  • 80g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  1. Parmesan and herb crackers scored with a maze pattern and studded with rosemary and parsley, with a snowy feta dip dip. Rub 80g cold butter into 150g plain flour, 40g grated parmesan, 1 tbsp chopped rosemary, 1 tbsp chopped parsley and half a tsp salt, then add 2 to 3 tbsp cold water to bring it together. Chill 20 minutes, roll 3mm thick between baking paper and cut into squares. Score a maze into each with a knife. Bake at 180°C for 12 minutes. Whip 150g feta with 100g Greek yoghurt and lemon zest.

Mains

Hallorann's roast leg of lamb

Garlic and rosemary studded lamb leg with roast potatoes and pan gravy.

Prep 20 min, cook 95 min · Make ahead: Stud and rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; roast and make the gravy on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1kg potatoes, quartered
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  1. Stud the lamb with garlic and rosemary and rub with oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast at 200°C with the potatoes around it.
  3. Rest.
  4. Stir the flour into the pan juices, add the stock and simmer for gravy.
  5. Carve and serve.

Wendy's breakfast in bed tray

Scrambled eggs on sourdough, crisp bacon and coffee, carried up to Jack in bed.

How it is made
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 50g butter
  • 8 eggs
  • 60ml cream
  • 4 slices sourdough
  • 6 oranges
  • 1 packet ground coffee
  1. Soft buttery scrambled eggs on sourdough with crisp bacon, grilled tomato and mushrooms, fresh orange juice and a pot of coffee, all on one tray. Fry 8 rashers bacon until crisp. Halve 4 tomatoes and grill 6 minutes. Sauté 250g mushrooms in 20g butter. Whisk 8 eggs with 60ml cream and salt and cook over low heat in 30g butter, stirring slowly, for 4 to 5 minutes until just set. Toast 4 slices sourdough. Plate on trays with a glass of fresh orange juice squeezed from 6 oranges and a pot of coffee.

Hallorann's chocolate ice creamVegetarian

Dark, rich chocolate custard ice cream in a cold bowl.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, then 10 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 120g caster sugar
  1. Warm the cream, milk, chocolate and cocoa until melted.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar.
  3. Pour the warm mix over, return to the pan and cook, stirring, to 82°C.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Churn, or freeze and stir every 30 minutes.

Drinks

Lloyd's bourbon old fashioned

Bourbon, demerara syrup and bitters stirred over a big popsicle, orange peel.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Measure the bourbon, syrup and bitters into a jug up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; stir each drink over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 40ml demerara sugar syrup
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 orange
  • 4 large ice blocks
  • 2 cups ice
  1. For each drink, measure 60ml bourbon, 10ml demerara sugar syrup and 2 dashes aromatic bitters into a mixing glass.
  2. Stir each drink with ice in a mixing glass for 20 seconds.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice block.
  4. Squeeze a strip of orange peel over the top and drop it in.
