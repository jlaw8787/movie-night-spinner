Snacks

Oysters natural

Freshly shucked, straight off the Cape

Prep 50 min · Make ahead: Mignonette up to 1 day, shuck just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 80ml red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 24 freshly shucked oysters
  • 1kg crushed ice
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  1. Mix the shallots, vinegar and plenty of cracked pepper. Rest to let the flavours settle.
  2. Shuck the oysters, keeping the liquor in the shell.
  3. Lay the oysters on crushed ice with lemon wedges and the mignonette in a small bowl with a spoon.

Fruit market corn and zucchini frittersOn the printed menu

Crisp zucchini and herb patties with feta, whipped feta and lemon, from Allen's produce business

How it is made
  • 2 zucchini
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g feta
  • 80g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon
  • vegetable oil
  1. Crisp corn, zucchini and herb fritters fried golden, with whipped feta and lemon for dolloping on top. Grate 2 zucchini, salt for 10 minutes and squeeze dry. Mix with 200g corn kernels, 2 spring onions, mint, 100g self-raising flour, 2 eggs and 50g crumbled feta. Shallow-fry spoonfuls in hot oil for 2 to 3 minutes a side. Blitz 150g feta with 80g Greek yoghurt and lemon zest and serve on the side.

Mains

Fish and chip cones

Cape Cod, where they first meet

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cut the chips and keep them in cold water up to 1 day ahead; cut the flathead into strips up to 4 hours ahead, chilled; batter and fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 300ml cold lager
  • 600g potatoes
  • 500g flathead fillets, cut in strips
  • 2l vegetable oil for frying
  • 100g tartare sauce
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  1. Whisk the flour, cornflour and lager to a batter.
  2. Cut the potatoes into chips, rinse and dry them.
  3. Fry the chips in the oil twice, first at a lower heat, then hot to crisp.
  4. Dip the flathead strips in batter and fry in the oil at 180°C until golden.
  5. Serve in paper cones with tartare and lemon.

Whole lobster

Whole lobster with drawn butter, shell optional. Madison learns English from TV.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Mix the garlic butter up to 2 days ahead and chill; boil, split and grill the lobsters on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 raw lobsters, about 800g each
  • 200g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  1. Boil the lobsters in salted water, allowing about 8 minutes per 500g of weight.
  2. Melt half the butter with the garlic. Keep the rest for drawn butter.
  3. Split the lobsters lengthways and crack the claws. Brush the flesh with the garlic butter.
  4. Grill flesh-side up until hot and lightly golden.
  5. Serve with drawn butter and lemon.

Tarragon chicken salad croissantsOn the printed menu

Poached chicken, mayo, celery, grapes and herbs in buttery pastry, for the Bloomingdale's spree

How it is made
  • 600g chicken breast
  • 120g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 100g seedless grapes
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 large croissants
  • 1 butter lettuce
  1. Poached chicken folded with mayo, celery, grapes and fresh tarragon, piled into buttery croissants with crisp lettuce. Poach 600g chicken breast in barely simmering salted water for 15 minutes, rest in the liquid 10 minutes, then cool and shred. Mix with 120g mayonnaise, 2 diced celery sticks, 100g halved grapes, 2 tbsp chopped tarragon, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Split 4 large croissants, line with butter lettuce and fill with the chicken salad.

Sweets

Ocean blue jelly cups

Ocean blue jelly set in small cups, like Madison's home under the sea

Prep 15 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 6 gelatine leaves
  • 400ml coconut milk, warm
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 10 dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 400ml hot water
  • 1 bag gummy fish
  1. Bloom 3 gelatine leaves in cold water and stir into the warm coconut milk with half the sugar.
  2. Pour into four glasses and chill until set.
  3. Steep the pea flowers in the hot water with the remaining sugar, strain, and stir in 3 bloomed gelatine leaves.
  4. Cool to room temperature, then pour over the set coconut layer.
  5. Chill until firm, then top with gummy fish.

Blue lagoonVegetarian

Vodka and curaçao topped with lemonade over ice, lemon wheel, for the dive off the pier

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best built to order; cut the lemon wheels up to 1 day ahead and keep the lemonade cold
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml vodka
  • 80ml blue curaçao
  • 600ml lemonade
  • 1 lemon, cut into wheels
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Add vodka and blue curaçao to each.
  3. Top with lemonade, stir gently and garnish with a lemon wheel.
