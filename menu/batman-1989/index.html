Gotham anniversary popcornVegetarian

Popcorn drizzled with purple and green white chocolate

Prep 33 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 1 drop purple food colouring
  • 1 drop green food colouring
  1. Pop the kernels in a covered pot and split between two trays.
  2. Melt the white chocolate, divide in two and tint one half purple and the other green.
  3. Drizzle one over each tray and toss. Let set.
  4. Break up and mix together in a big bowl.

Bat-shaped chips and black bean dipVegetarian

Bat shaped fried corn chips with chipotle black bean dip

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, chips airtight, dip chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 800g black beans (2 tins), drained
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 limes
  • 1 chipotle in adobo
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the tortillas with a bat cutter.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180C in batches until crisp, then salt.
  3. Blitz the beans with garlic, lime juice and chipotle until smooth.
  4. Serve the dip with the bats.

Flugelheim beet-cured salmon on ryeOn the printed menu

Beetroot and gin-cured fish on dark bread, horseradish crème fraîche and splattered herb oil

How it is made
  • 400g skinless salmon fillet
  • 100g salt
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 raw beetroot
  • 2 tbsp gin
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 shallot
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 4 slices dark rye
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 jar horseradish
  • olive oil
  1. Beetroot and gin-cured salmon on rye with horseradish crème fraîche, pickled shallot and a splash of green herb oil, splattered like the gallery. Two days ahead, mix 100g salt, 80g sugar, 1 grated raw beetroot and 2 tbsp gin and pack around a 400g salmon fillet. Cover, weigh down and refrigerate 48 hours, then rinse and slice thin. Blitz a bunch of parsley with 80ml oil and strain. Slice 1 shallot thin and soak in 2 tbsp vinegar. Cut 4 slices rye into 16 squares, spread with crème fraîche mixed with horseradish, top with salmon and shallot, and splash herb oil on the plate.

Manor roast beef and Yorkshire pudding

Rare rib of beef, roast potatoes, Yorkshire puddings and red wine jus

Prep 5 min, cook 3.5 hours, then 24 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the beef the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib, bone in
  • 2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml vegetable oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 250ml red wine
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Salt the beef and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Roast at 120C until 52C inside, then rest.
  3. Peel and chop the potatoes and roast them in some of the oil.
  4. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk and bake in hot oiled tins at 220C until puffed.
  5. Blast the beef at 260C for 10 minutes. Reduce the red wine with the stock for a jus.

Gotham back-street ReubenOn the printed menu

Corned beef, sauerkraut and Swiss on rye, Russian dressing, ever dance with the devil

How it is made
  • 4 tbsp mayo
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 jar horseradish
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 500g shaved corned beef
  • 1 jar sauerkraut
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 40g butter
  1. Piled-high corned beef, tangy sauerkraut and melted Swiss on rye with house Russian dressing, griddled in butter until crisp, with dill pickles. Mix 4 tbsp mayo, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tbsp horseradish, 1 tbsp chopped pickles and a dash of Worcestershire for Russian dressing. Spread over 8 slices rye. Layer 4 slices with 125g shaved corned beef each, 60g drained sauerkraut and 2 slices Swiss cheese, then close. Butter the outsides and cook in a pan over medium heat with a weight on top for 4 minutes a side until crisp and melted. Halve and serve with pickles.

Joker's birthday cakeVegetarian

Chocolate cake with purple and green buttercream and a crooked smile

Prep 23 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Cake up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 450g butter, soft
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 50g cocoa
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp purple food colouring
  • 1 tsp green food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and prepare two tins. Beat half the butter with the sugar, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa and milk, divide between the tins and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and tint most purple and a little green.
  5. Sandwich and cover the cake, then pipe a crooked smile.

Alfred's Earl Grey gin sourVegetarian

Earl Grey infused gin shaken with lemon, honey and egg white

Prep 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Gin infuses ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml gin
  • 2 Earl Grey tea bags
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 3 lemons
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in the gin for an hour, then remove.
  2. Dissolve the honey in the hot water. Juice the lemons.
  3. Shake the gin, lemon juice, honey syrup and egg whites hard without ice, then add ice and shake again.
  4. Strain into chilled coupes and twist lemon peel over each.

When to serve

Serve dessert when the Joker throws his parade with the balloons.
