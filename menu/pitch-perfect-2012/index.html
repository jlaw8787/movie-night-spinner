Egg breath devilled eggsVegetarian

Alice says Chloe's breath smells like egg

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill the eggs up to a day ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp chives, chopped
  1. Simmer the eggs for 9 minutes, then cool them in iced water and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs and mash the yolks with the mayo, mustard and lemon juice.
  3. Pipe or spoon the filling back into the whites.
  4. Dust with paprika and scatter with chives.

Cups audition popcorn chicken

Beca auditions with Cups

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 6 hours ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chicken thigh fillets, cut in small pieces
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 100g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with a pinch of salt.
  2. Mix the flours, garlic powder and paprika and toss the chicken through until coated.
  3. Fry in batches in oil at 180C until golden and cooked through.
  4. Stir the sriracha into the mayo and serve the chicken in paper cups with the dip.

Mains

Sabotage burritos

Bumper throws a burrito at Fat Amy

Prep 8 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Cook the beef and rice up to 2 days ahead; reheat and roll to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 200g long-grain rice
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 125g sour cream
  • 250ml tomato salsa
  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Brown the mince and onion with the spices, then stir in the beans and a splash of water and simmer.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill each with rice, beef, cheese, sour cream and salsa.
  4. Roll up tightly, tucking in the ends, and toast seam side down in a hot pan.

Ladies of the '80s pulled pork sliders

The riff-off in the empty pool

Prep 40 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Slow-cook the pork up to 2 days ahead and reheat with its juices; make the slaw on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 125ml apple juice
  • 12 soft slider rolls
  • 1/4 red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup dill pickle slices
  1. Rub the pork with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Put it in a lidded pot with the apple juice and half the sauce and bake at 150C until it pulls apart.
  3. Shred the pork and toss with the rest of the sauce.
  4. Mix the cabbage with the mayo.
  5. Fill the rolls with pork, slaw and pickles.

Finish-him-like-a-cheesecake cheesecakeVegetarian

Fat Amy vows to finish Bumper

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min, then 65 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and chill; warm the sauce to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 750g cream cheese, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 300g frozen mixed berries
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a lined 22cm springform tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and 170g sugar until smooth, then beat in the eggs, sour cream, vanilla and zest.
  3. Pour over the base and bake at 150C until just wobbly in the middle, then cool in the oven with the door ajar.
  4. Simmer the berries with the rest of the sugar until saucy and serve warm over slices.

Fat Amy promises a pitch-slapping

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the cranberry and lime up to a day ahead; add the vodka and soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 2 limes
  • 500ml soda water
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Stir the vodka, cranberry and lime juice in a jug.
  2. Pour over ice in red cups and top with soda.
