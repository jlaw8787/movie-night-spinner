Snacks

Stadium game day pretzelsVegetarian

Soft pretzels, rock salt. Gotham Rogues kick-off, then the field falls.

Prep 10 min, cook 22 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fresh, up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp bicarb soda
  • 1 tbsp rock salt
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and warm water into a dough.
  2. Rest until doubled.
  3. Shape into pretzels, dip in boiling water with bicarb soda and sprinkle with rock salt.
  4. Bake at 220°C until deep brown.
  5. Brush with butter and serve with mustard.

Marinated mozzarella and tomato picksVegetarian

Bocconcini and tomato. Five months in the Pit, then the climb.

Prep 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g bocconcini pearls
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup basil leaves, torn
  • 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  1. Toss the bocconcini with olive oil, basil and chilli flakes.
  2. Leave to marinate.
  3. Thread onto toothpicks with cherry tomatoes.
  4. Arrange on a dark plate and drizzle over the leftover oil.

Wayne Manor smoked salmon blinis

Salmon blinis, for the night the pearls go missing.

How it is made
  • 100g buckwheat flour
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 30g butter
  • 150g crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Warm buckwheat blinis topped with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, lemon zest and dill, passed round on silver trays. Whisk 100g buckwheat flour, 50g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 egg and 200ml milk into a thick batter. Drop teaspoons into a buttered pan over medium heat and cook 1 minute a side, making about 24. Cool slightly. Mix 150g crème fraîche with the zest of 1 lemon. Top each blini with a little crème fraîche, a fold of smoked salmon from 200g and a sprig of dill.

Mains

Blake's Gotham meatball hero

Meatballs and provolone. Blake's legal first name is Robin.

Prep 30 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Meatballs and sauce up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml passata
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 4 long rolls
  • 8 slices provolone
  1. Mix the mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, parmesan, one garlic clove and salt and roll into sixteen meatballs.
  2. Brown in olive oil.
  3. Add the passata, remaining garlic and a few basil leaves and simmer.
  4. Split and toast the rolls.
  5. Fill with meatballs and sauce, lay provolone over and grill until melted.
  6. Finish with fresh basil.

Gotham rib eye with creamed spinach

Seared rib eye with creamed spinach, for the rich before the storm.

How it is made
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 x 500g rib eye steaks
  • 40g butter
  • 1 head garlic
  • 500g spinach
  • 200ml cream
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Rib eye seared hard and basted with garlic butter, with nutmeg creamed spinach and crispy smashed potatoes, steakhouse style. Boil 1kg baby potatoes 15 minutes, smash flat and roast in oil at 220°C for 35 minutes. Season 2 x 500g rib eyes, sear 4 minutes a side in a very hot pan, then baste with 40g butter and 2 crushed garlic cloves for 1 minute. Rest 10 minutes. Wilt 500g spinach, squeeze dry and chop, then simmer with 200ml cream, a pinch of nutmeg and 30g parmesan until thick.

Florence café affogatoVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream drowned in hot espresso, amaretti on the side.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Scoop the ice cream into the glasses and freeze up to 1 day ahead; brew and pour the espresso at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 4 shots hot espresso
  • 8 amaretti biscuits
  1. Brew a strong pot of espresso.
  2. Put a big scoop of ice cream into each glass and set out amaretti alongside.
  3. At the table, pour a hot shot of espresso over each scoop and eat straight away.

Drinks

Shakerato iced espressoNo alcohol

Iced espresso. In Florence, Alfred spots Bruce and Selina.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week
Recipe for 4
  • 320ml strong coffee (8 espresso shots)
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the espresso and make a syrup by dissolving the sugar in the hot water.
  2. Shake the coffee and syrup in portions with ice very hard until thick and foamy.
  3. Strain into chilled glasses and twist a strip of lemon peel over each.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Selina Kyle slips out of Wayne Manor with the pearls.
