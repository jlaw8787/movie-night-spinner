Snacks

Letter Q quince and cheddar quesadillasVegetarian

Gloria wins on foods starting with Q

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the tortillas up to 4 hours ahead and chill; toast to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 150g quince paste, thinly sliced
  • 1 small red onion, finely sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 lime, cut in wedges
  • 1 cup rocket leaves
  1. Scatter half of each tortilla with cheddar, quince and onion, then fold over.
  2. Toast in a buttered pan over medium heat until golden and the cheese melts.
  3. Cut into wedges and serve with rocket and lime.

Cat in an oven buttermilk biscuitsVegetarian

Sidney explains what makes a biscuit

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Rub the butter into the flour up to 2 days ahead and chill; add the buttermilk and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100g cold butter, diced, plus 60g soft for the honey butter
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour, baking powder and salt until it looks like rough crumbs.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk, pat out 3cm thick, fold twice and cut 8 rounds.
  3. Bake close together at 220C until tall and golden.
  4. Beat the soft butter with the honey and a pinch of salt and serve with the hot biscuits.

Mains

Hustler's carne asada tacos

Billy plays like he can't play

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Marinate the steak overnight and make the salsa up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g beef skirt steak
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 250g salsa verde
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  • 1 cup coriander leaves
  1. Rub the steak with the lime juice, garlic, cumin, oil and salt and leave at least 30 minutes.
  2. Grill on a very hot pan 3 minutes a side, then rest and slice thinly across the grain.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  4. Fill with steak, salsa verde, onion and coriander.

Stucci brothers meatball hero

The Stucci brothers want their money

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 2 days ahead; reheat and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 40g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700ml tomato passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 150g mozzarella, sliced
  1. Mix both minces with the breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, half the garlic and salt, and roll into 16 balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil, then add the passata, oregano and remaining garlic.
  3. Simmer gently until cooked through and the sauce is thick.
  4. Fill the split rolls with meatballs and sauce, top with mozzarella and grill until melted.

Sweets

Slam dunk churros with hot chocolateVegetarian

Billy bets his share that he can dunk

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the chocolate up to 2 days ahead and reheat; pipe and fry the churros to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar, plus 100g for coating
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 300ml milk
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  1. Bring the water, butter and 1 tbsp sugar to the boil, beat in the flour until it forms a ball, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  2. Pipe 10cm lengths into oil at 180C and fry until deep gold.
  3. Roll the hot churros in the cinnamon and sugar.
  4. Heat the milk with the cornflour and whisk in the chocolate until thick, then serve for dunking.

Gloria's sympathy watermelon agua frescaVegetarianNo alcohol

Gloria wants sympathy not a glass of water

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain up to a day ahead and chill; pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg seedless watermelon, cubed
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 500ml cold water
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Blend the watermelon with the lime juice, sugar, water and half the mint until smooth.
  2. Strain into a jug and chill.
  3. Pour over ice with the rest of the mint.
