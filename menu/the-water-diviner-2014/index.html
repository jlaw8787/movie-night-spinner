Ayshe's hotel meze with smoky eggplantVegetarian

Smoky eggplant dip, hummus, olives, feta and sumac flatbread

Prep 17 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Eggplant dip up to 1 day ahead, grill the bread fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggplants
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 200g hummus
  • 150g kalamata olives
  • 150g feta
  1. Char the eggplants directly over a gas flame or under a hot grill, turning, until collapsed.
  2. Cool, then scoop out the flesh.
  3. Mash with tahini, crushed garlic, lemon juice and salt.
  4. Spoon into a bowl with olive oil and parsley.
  5. Brush the flatbreads with oil mixed with sumac.
  6. Grill the flatbreads a minute each side.
  7. Serve with hummus, olives and feta.

Istanbul hotel red lentil soupVegetarian

Red lentil soup, flatbread. Ayshe says to bribe a fisherman

Prep 10 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead; thin with water when reheating
Recipe for 4
  • 250g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1l vegetable stock
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Soften the onion and carrot in half the butter for 6 minutes, then stir in the tomato paste and cumin.
  2. Add the lentils and stock and simmer 20 minutes until the lentils fall apart.
  3. Blend smooth and season.
  4. Melt the rest of the butter with the paprika and drizzle over each bowl. Serve with lemon wedges and warm flatbread.

Major Hasan's lamb kofte

Spiced lamb skewers on flatbread with garlic yoghurt and chilli butter

Prep 35 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Mix up to 1 day ahead, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g lamb mince
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 lemon
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Mix the lamb mince with grated onion, crushed garlic, cumin, sumac, paprika, parsley and salt.
  2. Shape onto skewers.
  3. Grill, turning.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic.
  5. Toss the diced tomatoes and sliced red onion with parsley and lemon.
  6. Melt the butter with chilli flakes.
  7. Serve on warm flatbread with yoghurt, salad and chilli butter.

Ayshe's chicken pilaf

Chicken pilaf. Joshua follows his dream to the windmill

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Cook up to 1 day ahead and reheat covered with a splash of water; add the almonds and dill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 600ml chicken stock
  • 40g currants
  • 40g slivered almonds, toasted
  • 1 small bunch dill
  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
  2. Brown the chicken in the butter, add the onion and spices and cook until soft.
  3. Stir in the rice and currants, pour in the stock, cover and simmer on low until the rice is tender.
  4. Rest off the heat, covered, then fluff and finish with toasted almonds and dill.

Gallipoli Anzac biscuitsVegetarian

Oat and golden syrup. Joshua explains cricket on the train

Prep 20 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 150g plain flour
  • 80g desiccated coconut
  • 120g brown sugar
  • 125g butter
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  1. Mix the oats, flour, coconut and brown sugar.
  2. Melt the butter with golden syrup, then stir in the bicarbonate of soda dissolved in a splash of boiling water so it foams.
  3. Pour into the dry mix and stir well.
  4. Roll into balls and flatten slightly on lined trays, leaving room to spread.
  5. Bake at 170C until golden.
  6. Cool on the trays to crisp up.

Istanbul pistachio and walnut baklavaVegetarian

Layered filo with pistachio and walnut, soaked in honey syrup

Prep 33 min, cook 225 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep covered at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pistachios
  • 100g walnuts
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g butter
  • 16 sheets filo pastry
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  1. Blitz the pistachios and walnuts with cinnamon until finely chopped.
  2. Brush a 20 x 30cm tin with melted butter.
  3. Layer buttered filo sheets, scatter with half the nuts, add more buttered sheets, the rest of the nuts and a final layer of filo.
  4. Cut into diamonds.
  5. Bake at 170C.
  6. Simmer the sugar, water, honey and lemon juice into a syrup.
  7. Pour the syrup over the hot baklava and leave to soak and cool.

Divining rod spiced apple teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Black tea, apple and spice. Told you there was water there.

Prep 10 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples
  • 60g dried apple
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 black tea bags
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon
  1. Dice the apples.
  2. Simmer the water with the apples, dried apple, cinnamon sticks and cloves.
  3. Take off the heat, add the tea bags and steep.
  4. Strain and stir in honey and a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Pour into small heatproof glasses and serve hot with a thin apple slice.
