Snacks

Barbossa's green applesVegetarian

Barbossa can't taste a thing until the curse lifts. Apple?

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best made just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green apples, sliced
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 200g soft caramels
  • 3 tbsp thickened cream
  • 1 pinch of flaky salt
  • 150g sharp cheddar
  1. Slice the apples and toss in lime juice.
  2. Melt the caramels with the cream and salt until smooth.
  3. Break the cheddar into shards.
  4. Serve the apples with the warm dip and cheddar.

Aztec gold fried plantain coinsVegetarian

Twice-fried smashed green plantain, salted and brushed with edible gold

Prep 14 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  • 1 pinch of edible gold lustre
  1. Peel the plantains and slice into thick coins.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 160C for a few minutes until just tender.
  3. Smash each coin flat.
  4. Fry again at 190C until golden and crisp.
  5. Salt, and brush a few with gold lustre.

Tortuga prawn and capsicum frittersOn the printed menu

Prawn and capsicum fritters, with the sweet, proliferous bouquet of Tortuga.

How it is made
  • 400g raw peeled prawns
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 jar ground allspice
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Chopped prawns in a spiced batter with capsicum, onion and chilli, fried into crisp golden fritters, with a lime and hot sauce mayo. Roughly chop 400g raw peeled prawns. Whisk 150g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 egg, 150ml milk, half a teaspoon of allspice and salt. Fold in the prawns, half a diced red capsicum, 3 sliced spring onions, 1 chopped red chilli and thyme leaves. Drop spoonfuls into oil at 180C and fry 3 minutes until golden. Mix 120g mayonnaise with lime zest and juice and 1 tsp hot sauce to dip.

Caribbean jerk chicken

Charcoal-grilled scotch bonnet jerk chicken legs with coconut rice and beans

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 scotch bonnet chillies
  • 2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 4 spring onions
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 4 chicken marylands
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tin of kidney beans, drained
  • 400ml coconut milk
  1. Blitz the chillies, allspice, thyme, spring onions, garlic and lime juice into a paste.
  2. Coat the chicken and marinate overnight in the fridge.
  3. Grill slowly over coals, turning, until charred and cooked through.
  4. Cook the rice with the kidney beans, coconut milk and a little water.
  5. Serve the chicken on the rice.

Port Royal coconut snapper stewOn the printed menu

Snapper stewed in coconut milk with thyme, for Norrington's promotion at the fort. Mind the corset.

How it is made
  • 800g snapper fillets
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 1 tin kidney beans
  • 670ml coconut milk
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar ground allspice
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 scotch bonnet chilli
  • 2 ripe plantains
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Snapper simmered gently in coconut milk with tomato, capsicum, thyme, allspice and a whole chilli, with rice and peas and fried plantain. Cook 300g rice with 1 drained tin kidney beans, 270ml coconut milk, 300ml water and thyme for 18 minutes. Soften 1 onion, 1 capsicum and 3 garlic cloves in oil, add half a teaspoon of allspice, 2 diced tomatoes, 400ml coconut milk and 1 whole scotch bonnet and simmer 10 minutes. Add 800g snapper chunks and poach 6 minutes. Fry 2 sliced ripe plantains in oil until golden. Finish the stew with lime and serve.

Port Royal pineapple tartsVegetarian

Small shortcrust tarts filled with thick cinnamon pineapple jam

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pineapple, grated
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 18 small shortcrust tart cases
  1. Heat the oven to 180C.
  2. Cook the pineapple with the sugar and cinnamon stick until thick, then cool.
  3. Remove the cinnamon and fill the tart cases.
  4. Bake until the pastry is golden.

Pirate's rum punchVegetarian

Dark rum, lime, pineapple and orange with nutmeg. Elizabeth burned the rest for a signal fire.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix everything but the ice in the jug up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 limes, juiced
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 300ml pineapple juice
  • 300ml orange juice
  • 2 cup ice
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg, freshly grated
  1. Mix the lime juice, syrup, rum, pineapple juice and orange juice in a jug.
  2. Pour over ice and grate nutmeg on top.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Jack and Elizabeth are marooned on the island.
