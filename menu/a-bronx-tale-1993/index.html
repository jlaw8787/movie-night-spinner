Snacks

Chez Bippy provolone-stuffed fried olivesVegetarian

Bar food from Sonny's place, Chez Bippy

Prep 30 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 large pitted green olives
  • 80g sharp provolone
  • 40g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 80g fine breadcrumbs
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 1/2 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • chilli flakes, to dust
  1. Drain the olives and pat dry. Cut the provolone into small sticks and push one into each olive.
  2. Roll in flour, dip in beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs.
  3. Stir the lemon zest, juice and garlic into the mayonnaise to make the aioli.
  4. Heat the oil to 180C and fry in two batches until deep golden, then drain.
  5. Dust with chilli flakes and serve with the aioli.

Doo-wop garlic knotsVegetarian

Eaten on the stoop while the street corner singers warm up

Prep 20 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Best fresh; reheat at 180C for 5 minutes if needed
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pizza dough
  • 50g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  1. Cut the dough into 12 strips and tie each in a loose knot.
  2. Place on a lined tray, cover and prove in a warm place for 30 minutes.
  3. Bake at 230C for 12 minutes until golden.
  4. Melt the butter with the garlic over low heat for 2 minutes.
  5. Toss the hot knots with the garlic butter, parsley and parmesan.

Mains

Sonny's Sunday rigatoni

Gravy and meatballs at the neighbourhood boss's table

Prep 30 min, cook 130 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day ahead, better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork and beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 400g pork ribs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 800g tinned tomatoes
  • 200ml water
  • 10 basil leaves
  • 400g rigatoni
  1. Mix the mince with egg, breadcrumbs, salt and pepper and shape into 12 meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs and ribs in olive oil in a heavy pot, turning, then set aside.
  3. Soften the onion, add the garlic, then the tomatoes, water and basil.
  4. Return the meat, cover partly and simmer gently, stirring and adding water if needed. Check the ribs are tender and the meatballs cooked through.
  5. Boil the rigatoni, drain and toss with the sauce. Serve the meat on a platter.

Arthur Avenue cannoliVegetarian

A box from Belmont's bakery for Sonny

Prep 20 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead; fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g fresh ricotta
  • 40g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 30g mini chocolate chips
  • 8 small cannoli shells
  • 30g pistachios, finely crushed
  1. Drain the ricotta in a lined sieve in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  2. Beat with icing sugar and vanilla until smooth, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  3. Pipe into the shells just before serving.
  4. Dip the ends in crushed pistachios. Serve two per person.

Espresso with anisetteVegetarian

Short black, anisette on the side, lemon curl, the saddest thing is wasted talent

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best brewed just before serving; cut the lemon curls up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 36g espresso beans
  • 60ml anisette or sambuca
  • 1 lemon
  1. Brew four short espressos into warmed demitasse cups.
  2. Cut four small curls of lemon peel, avoiding the white pith.
  3. Serve the anisette beside each cup for pouring in, with a lemon curl.

When to serve

Do the car door test before dessert comes out.
