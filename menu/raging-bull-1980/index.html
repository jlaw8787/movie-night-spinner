Bronx devilled eggs with crisp pancetta

Halved boiled eggs filled with mustard mayo, topped with fried cured pork, paprika and chives

Prep 6 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 6 slices pancetta
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp snipped chives
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes, then cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Fry the pancetta in a dry pan until crisp, drain and crumble.
  3. Halve the eggs and mash the yolks with mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  4. Pipe or spoon back into the whites. Top with pancetta, paprika and chives.

Joey's club prawns

Poached iced prawns with a sharp tomato, horseradish and lemon sauce, Copacabana style

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g peeled green prawns
  • 150ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tsp horseradish
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Poach the prawns in salted water for 2 minutes and chill in iced water.
  2. Mix the tomato sauce with horseradish and the juice of 1 lemon.
  3. Serve the prawns on ice with the sauce and wedges from the other lemon.

Jake's steak

Cooked properly this time, nobody gets yelled at

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Salt the steaks the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg porterhouse steaks (2 x 600g)
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 50g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  1. Salt the steaks and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Sear in a smoking pan for 3 minutes a side, then add butter, garlic and thyme and baste until 52C inside.
  3. Rest for 8 minutes.
  4. Slice off the bone to share.

Ricotta cakeVegetarian

Baked ricotta with lemon and vanilla, icing sugar, for Vickie at the pool

Prep 8 min, cook 60 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 750g fresh ricotta
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • icing sugar, to dust
  1. Drain the ricotta for 2 hours.
  2. Beat with sugar, eggs, lemon zest and vanilla.
  3. Pour into a lined 22cm springform tin and bake at 160C for 60 minutes.
  4. Cool in the oven with the door ajar, then dust with icing sugar.

Jake's Miami club rum punchVegetarian

Golden rum, orange, pineapple, lime and grenadine on crushed ice, for the nightclub stand-up set

Prep 5 min, then 32 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml golden rum
  • 240ml orange juice
  • 240ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 30ml grenadine
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Stir the rum, juices and grenadine in a jug and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  2. Fill four tall glasses with crushed ice and pour the punch over.
  3. Finish each with an orange slice and a cherry on a pick.
