Paul Bunyan cheese curdsVegetarian

Beer battered fried cheese curds with a dill sour cream dip.

Prep 12 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the ranch up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; make the batter and fry the curds just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 120g plain flour
  • 375ml lager
  • 300g cheese curds
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  1. Whisk the flour with some of the cold lager into a batter.
  2. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, dill and chives for ranch.
  3. Heat the oil to 185°C, 7 min.
  4. Dip the curds and fry for about a minute until puffed, 5 min.
  5. Serve hot with the ranch.

Marge's lunch buffet relish trayVegetarian

Pickles, olives, radishes and crisp vegetables on ice, with ranch to dip.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Cut the vegetables up to 1 day ahead and keep them in cold water in the fridge; mix the dip up to 2 days ahead and set the tray on ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 jar dill pickle spears
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 3 celery sticks
  • 2 carrots
  • 200g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Cut the celery and carrots into sticks and trim the radishes.
  2. Arrange the pickles, olives and vegetables on a tray of ice.
  3. Mix the sour cream with dill and salt for a dip.
  4. Serve like a church buffet, you betcha.

Potato bite hotdish bites

Crispy potato bites over a beef, corn and mushroom bake, in a muffin tin.

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 150g mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 200ml milk
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 500g frozen potato gems
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 jar tomato relish
  1. Beef mince and onion in a creamy mushroom sauce, baked in muffin tins under crunchy potato gems and melted cheddar, with tomato relish. Brown 400g beef mince with 1 diced onion and 150g sliced mushrooms. Stir in 1 tbsp flour, then 200ml milk and 100ml cream and simmer 5 minutes until thick, with salt, pepper and a dash of Worcestershire. Spoon into 12 greased muffin holes. Top each with 3 or 4 frozen potato gems and a pinch of grated cheddar from 100g. Bake at 210°C for 25 minutes until the tots are crisp. Serve with tomato relish.

Walleye fish fry

Lager battered walleye fillets, twice-fried chips, cabbage slaw and tartare.

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cut the chips up to 4 hours ahead and keep them in cold water, then dry well; make the slaw up to 4 hours ahead, batter and fry the fish to order
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 375ml lager
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1/2 cup tartare sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips and dry well.
  2. Shred the cabbage and toss with mayonnaise and vinegar.
  3. Fry the chips in the vegetable oil twice, once to cook through and once to crisp, 20 min.
  4. Whisk the flour with cold lager into a batter, dip the flathead fillets and fry until golden, 10 min.
  5. Serve with tartare.

Carvery roast beef

Top round roasted pink and carved thin with gravy and mash, from the lunch line.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef topside
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 400g frozen corn
  • 8 dinner rolls
  1. Top round roasted pink and carved thin, with rich gravy, buttery mashed potato, sweet corn and soft dinner rolls, buffet plates piled high. Rub a 1.5kg beef topside with oil, salt and pepper. Sear in a hot roasting tin, then roast at 200°C for 40 to 45 minutes until 52°C inside. Rest 20 minutes, then carve thin. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml milk. Make gravy from the pan juices with 1 tbsp flour and 400ml beef stock. Heat 400g frozen corn with butter. Serve with warm dinner rolls.

Minnesota apple lattice pieVegetarian

Granny Smith apple pie with brown sugar, cinnamon and a lattice top.

Prep 35 min, cook 50 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg Granny Smith apples
  • 120g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 4 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Peel and slice the apples and toss with the sugar, cinnamon, cornflour and lemon juice.
  2. Line a 23cm pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill.
  3. Cut the remaining sheets into strips, weave a lattice, brush with egg and sprinkle with sugar.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling, 50 min.
  5. Rest before serving with ice cream, 30 min.

Norm's three-cent stamp thermos mochaNo alcohol

Hot mocha made with strong coffee, milk and dark chocolate.

Prep 12 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the mocha up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and reheat into the thermos; whip the maple cream up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml brewed coffee
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Brew the coffee.
  2. Heat the milk until steaming and whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth, 5 min.
  3. Stir in the coffee and pour into a warmed thermos.
  4. Whip the cream with maple syrup to soft peaks.
  5. Pour into mugs and spoon the cream on top.
