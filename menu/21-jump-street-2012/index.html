Snacks

Cafeteria pizza squares

Thick tray pizza with pepperoni and mozzarella, cut into squares.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Reheat in the oven, up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  1. Press the dough into an oiled tray right to the edges.
  2. Spread with crushed tomatoes mixed with oregano and top with mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Bake at 240°C until bubbling and golden, 15 min.
  4. Cut into squares.

Peter Pan rehearsal quesadillasVegetarian

Crisp tortillas filled with melted cheddar, scallion and pickled jalapeños.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheddar and slice the spring onions up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; fill and cook just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 200g cheddar
  • 2 spring onions
  • 4 tbsp pickled jalapenos
  • 4 tbsp tomato salsa
  1. Grate the cheddar and slice the spring onions.
  2. Cover half of each tortilla with cheddar, spring onion and jalapenos, then fold over.
  3. Cook in a dry pan until crisp and melted, 10 min.
  4. Cut into wedges and serve with salsa.

Mains

Prom night chicken

Crisp chicken with pan gravy, the prom night Eric's big deal goes down.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 45 min · Make ahead: Gratin can be made 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 450ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 40g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 300g green beans
  1. Slice the potatoes thin, layer in a dish with part of the cream and the garlic, and bake at 160°C, 1 hr 15 min.
  2. Pan-roast the chicken in the butter with thyme until cooked through, 15 min.
  3. Fry the mushrooms in the same pan and add the remaining cream to make a sauce, 8 min.
  4. Steam the beans and serve everything together, 7 min.

Schmidt's house party cheeseburger sliders

Smashed patties, melted cheddar, butter onions and mustard mayo on buns while his parents are away.

How it is made
  • 2 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 600g beef mince
  • whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 12 slider buns
  • dill pickles
  1. Smashed beef sliders with melted cheddar, slow-cooked butter onions, pickles and a mustard mayo on soft slider buns. Cook 2 sliced onions in 30g butter over low heat for 20 minutes. Roll 600g beef mince into 12 balls. Mix 3 tbsp mayo with 1 tbsp American mustard. Smash the balls thin on a smoking griddle, season and cook 90 seconds, flip and top with cheddar. Fill 12 toasted slider buns with patties, onions, pickles and mustard mayo.

Sweets

Graduation cupcakesVegetarian

Chocolate cupcakes piped with vanilla buttercream.

Prep 28 min, cook 18 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead, frost on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 275g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 30g cocoa
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 12 lolly buttons
  1. Beat part of the butter and the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, cocoa and milk.
  2. Bake 12 at 175°C until springy, 18 min.
  3. Cool completely, 20 min.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar into vanilla buttercream and pipe on.
  5. Top with a square of chocolate and a lolly button.

HFS test tube fruit jellies

Raspberry, lime and orange juice jellies set in layers, chemistry class favourite.

How it is made
  • 3 tsp gelatine powder
  • 200ml raspberry juice
  • caster sugar
  • 200ml lime cordial
  • 200ml orange juice
  • 12 small plastic cups
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Three layers of raspberry, lime and orange juice jelly set in little cups like a test tube rack, topped with whipped cream. Soften 3 tsp gelatine powder in 3 tbsp cold water for each layer. Warm 200ml raspberry juice with 1 tbsp sugar, stir in one lot of gelatine and pour into 12 small cups. Chill 1 hour. Repeat with 200ml lime cordial mixed with water, then with 200ml orange juice, chilling 1 hour between. Top with a dot of whipped cream.

Jenko's AP chem malted shakeNo alcohol

Chocolate ice cream, milk and malt powder blended thick in a chocolate lined glass.

Prep 13 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Melt the chocolate and drizzle the glasses up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead, blend to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g dark chocolate
  • 800ml cold milk
  • 8 scoops of chocolate ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 packet malt balls
  1. Melt the chocolate and drizzle it inside four tall glasses, then chill, 15 min.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream and malted milk powder until thick.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Pour into the glasses, top with whipped cream and a few crushed malt balls.
