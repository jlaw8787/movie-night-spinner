Snacks

Rally night pigs in blankets

Cocktail franks in puff pastry, honey mustard dip

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Wrap the pastry around the cocktail frankfurts up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; mix the dip up to 3 days ahead; brush with egg and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 24 cocktail frankfurts
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Cut the thawed puff pastry into strips.
  2. Brush each strip with a little Dijon mustard and wrap around the cocktail frankfurts.
  3. Lay seam-side down on a lined tray, brush with the beaten egg and sprinkle with the sesame seeds.
  4. Bake at 200C for 18 minutes until puffed and golden.
  5. Stir the wholegrain mustard into the honey and the mayonnaise for dipping and serve warm.

Rock the Vote backstage crostiniVegetarian

Cheddar, apple and walnut toasts. Mike takes the stage as Al

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Bake the crostini up to 2 days ahead, airtight, and toast the walnuts ahead; slice the apples and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 50g walnuts
  • 2 red apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Slice the baguette into thin rounds, brush with the olive oil and bake at 190C for 8 minutes until crisp.
  2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 3 minutes and chop.
  3. Thinly slice the red apples and toss with the juice of half a lemon so they stay white.
  4. Top each toast with a slice of the vintage cheddar and a slice of apple.
  5. Scatter with walnuts, drizzle with the honey and finish with cracked pepper.

Mains

Hideout cabin salmon with dill butter

Foil-baked salmon over checkers. Never move your back row

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix the dill butter up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled; seal the salmon in foil up to 4 hours ahead; bake and fry the beans on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g green beans
  1. Mash the soft butter with the chopped dill, the zest of the lemon and the crushed garlic clove.
  2. Boil the baby potatoes for 15 minutes, drain, crush lightly and toss with the olive oil and salt.
  3. Lay the salmon fillets on squares of foil, top each with a slice of the butter and a lemon slice, then seal.
  4. Bake at 200C for 12 minutes.
  5. Fry the green beans in a hot pan with a splash of oil for 4 minutes until charred.

Drake Sabitch's backwoods chilli

Drake, the jumpy vet who lives in an old school bus with a grill

Prep 25 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and reheat; bake the cornbread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tinned kidney beans, drained
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches, then fry the chopped onion and garlic with the cumin for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the chipotle, tomatoes and 250ml water, cover and simmer for 1 and a half hours until the beef falls apart. Stir in the beans for the last 15 minutes.
  3. Mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder and a pinch of salt with the buttermilk, egg and melted butter and bake in a hot greased tin at 200C for 20 minutes.
  4. Serve the chilli with wedges of cornbread and sour cream.

Not-a-pudding apple crumbleVegetarian

Cinnamon apple oat crumble. Not a pudding, Mike

Prep 25 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Rub the crumble topping up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; toss the apples and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 Granny Smith apples
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 600ml vanilla custard
  1. Peel and slice the Granny Smith apples and toss with the caster sugar, the cinnamon and the juice of half a lemon.
  2. Tip into the baking dish.
  3. Rub the cold butter into the plain flour, then stir in the rolled oats and the brown sugar until clumpy.
  4. Scatter over the apples.
  5. Bake at 180C for 40 minutes until golden and bubbling at the edges.
  6. Warm the ready-made custard and pour over each bowl.

Seattle iced coffee with maple creamVegetarianNo alcohol

Iced coffee for the night drive into Seattle to see Al

Prep 10 min, cook 720 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g ground coffee
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1l water
  1. The night before, stir the coarsely ground coffee into the cold water in a jug, cover and leave in the fridge for 12 hours.
  2. Strain through a fine sieve lined with paper towel.
  3. Whisk the thickened cream with the maple syrup until just thickened but still pourable.
  4. Fill the glasses with ice, pour in the iced coffee and slowly pour the maple cream over the back of a spoon so it floats.
