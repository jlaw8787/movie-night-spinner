Snacks

Boardwalk pretzel bitesVegetarian

Warm soft pretzel bites brushed with butter and coarse salt, with a hot cheddar sauce and yellow mustard for dipping.

Prep 15 min, cook 33 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best eaten warm, same day
Recipe for 4
  • 265g plain flour
  • 4g instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 160ml warm water
  • 1l water, for boiling
  • 30g bicarbonate of soda
  • 35g butter
  • 150ml milk
  • 100g grated cheddar
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp coarse salt, to finish
  1. Mix the flour (keeping a spoonful back for the cheese dip), yeast, sugar, salt and warm water, then knead until smooth.
  2. Cover and rise until doubled.
  3. Roll into ropes and cut into 24 bites. Bring the boiling water and bicarbonate of soda to a simmer.
  4. Boil the bites in batches for 20 seconds, then drain on baking paper.
  5. Bake at 220°C until deep golden, then brush with melted butter and coarse salt.
  6. For the dip, melt butter, stir in the remaining flour, whisk in the milk and simmer. Off the heat, stir in the cheddar and serve with mustard.

Street-cart cornVegetarian

Corn ribs roasted until charred and curling, brushed with chilli butter and smoked salt, finished with plenty of lime.

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 1 lime
  • 30g butter
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 pinch smoked salt
  1. Quarter the cobs lengthways on a secure board with a sturdy sharp knife (use whole cobs if easier). Melt the butter with the chilli flakes and smoked salt.
  2. Brush the corn with the chilli butter.
  3. Roast at 220°C, turning once, until tender and charred at the edges. Finish with lime juice.

Mains

Coney Island chilli dogs

Griddled frankfurts under loose spiced beef sauce, mustard and onion, can you dig it

Prep 18 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 100ml water
  • 8 beef franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 600g shoestring fries
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • salt, to taste
  1. Dice the onion and keep a quarter aside for topping. Mince the garlic.
  2. Fry the rest of the onion in the oil with the garlic, then add the mince and brown well.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, cumin, smoked paprika, tomatoes and water and simmer. Season with salt.
  4. Bake the fries as directed on the packet.
  5. Griddle the franks until piping hot and warm the buns. Fill with franks and chilli and finish with onion and mustard.

Chocolate-dipped ice-cream sandwichesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, chewy chocolate cookies and dark chocolate.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 75 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 8 large chewy chocolate cookies
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Soften the ice cream just enough to scoop and sandwich between the cookies to make 4 sandwiches.
  2. Freeze on a lined tray until firm.
  3. Melt the chocolate gently and cool until just warm. Dip half of each sandwich.
  4. Return to the freezer to set.

Cherry colaVegetarianNo alcohol

Ice-cold cola, cherry syrup and a squeeze of lime.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cola
  • 60ml cherry syrup
  • 1 lime
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice, add cherry syrup to each, top with cola and squeeze in a lime wedge. Stir once and taste before adding more syrup.
