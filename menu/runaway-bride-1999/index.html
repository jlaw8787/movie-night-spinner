Snacks

Maryland crab cakes

Crab cakes with mayo, Dijon and scallion, pan fried.

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min, then 32 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncooked, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g crab meat
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 lemons
  1. Mix the crab with the mayonnaise, Dijon, sliced spring onion and breadcrumbs.
  2. Shape into small cakes and chill for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Pan-fry in the vegetable oil until golden on both sides, 10 min.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges.

Diner hash brownsVegetarian

Grated potato and onion cakes fried in butter.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in a hot oven
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 100g butter
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Grate the potatoes and onion, then squeeze out as much liquid as you can.
  2. Season and shape into small flat cakes.
  3. Fry in butter over medium heat until crisp on both sides, 20 min.
  4. Serve hot with sour cream and chives.

Hardware store caramel popcorn

Kernels in buttery brown sugar toffee, baked crunchy and salted, for the FedEx truck getaway.

How it is made
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 100g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  1. Popcorn coated in a buttery brown sugar caramel, baked crunchy, salted and scooped into brown paper bags like a shop counter treat. Pop 100g popcorn kernels in 2 tbsp oil in a lidded pot and tip into a large roasting tin. Boil 100g butter, 200g brown sugar, 60ml golden syrup and half a teaspoon salt for 4 minutes, then stir in half a teaspoon bicarb so it foams. Pour over the popcorn and toss. Bake at 120°C for 45 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes, then spread out to cool. Sprinkle with flaky salt and scoop into paper bags.

Eggs Benedict

Poached eggs and hollandaise on toasted English muffins.

Prep 2 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Hollandaise is best fresh, keep warm up to 30 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 11 eggs (3 yolks for the sauce, 8 for poaching)
  • 150g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 English muffins
  • 200g leg ham
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Melt the butter, 3 min.
  2. Whisk the 3 yolks with lemon juice over a gentle simmer, then slowly pour in the butter until thick, 7 min.
  3. Poach the eggs in barely simmering water, 3 min.
  4. Toast the muffins and top with ham and the eggs, 5 min.
  5. Spoon over the hollandaise and finish with chives.

Rehearsal dinner lemon roast chicken

A whole chicken with thyme butter, stuffed with garlic and citrus, crushed potatoes and green beans.

Prep 5 min, cook 106 min · Make ahead: Stuff and season the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 1kg small potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g green beans
  • 30g flaked almonds
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub the chicken with butter mixed with thyme leaves and salt and stuff it with a halved lemon and half the garlic, 10 min.
  2. Roast the chicken for 1 hour 15 minutes with the halved potatoes tossed in oil around it, until the juices run clear.
  3. Rest the chicken for 15 minutes and crush the potatoes with a fork in the pan juices.
  4. Boil the beans for 4 minutes, drain and toss with the rest of the garlic, sliced and fried in a little oil, the almonds and the other lemon.
  5. Carve and serve with the potatoes and beans.

Abandoned wedding cakeVegetarian

Butter sponge layers with strawberry jam and whipped cream.

Prep 35 min, cook 25 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead, ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 150g strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp milk
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour. Divide between 2 tins.
  2. Bake at 175°C until golden and springy, 25 min.
  3. Cool completely, 40 min.
  4. Whip the cream and sandwich the cakes with jam and cream.
  5. Ice white with icing sugar mixed with milk.

Hale diner orange creamsicle shakeNo alcohol

Orange juice and zest blended with vanilla ice cream, milk and honey.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best made just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 oranges
  • 6 scoops of vanilla ice cream (about 600 ml)
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Squeeze the oranges for juice and zest one of them.
  2. Blend the juice and zest with the ice cream, milk and honey until smooth and frothy.
  3. Pour into 4 tall glasses with an orange slice on the rim and a straw.
