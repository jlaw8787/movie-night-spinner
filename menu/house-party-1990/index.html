Snacks

House party chicken wings

Baked wings in hot butter sauce with celery sticks and blue cheese dip

Prep 20 min, cook 758 min · Make ahead: Dry the wings the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 3 celery sticks
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  1. Dry the wings uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  3. Melt the hot sauce with the butter and honey.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream for the dip.
  5. Toss the wings in the sauce and serve with celery sticks and the dip.

Kick step party sub

Salami, ham and provolone with lettuce, tomato and red wine vinegar, sliced thick

Prep 20 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Assemble and press the sub up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped and chilled; slice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 long Italian loaf
  • 100g salami
  • 150g leg ham
  • 100g provolone
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Split the loaf lengthways.
  2. Layer with salami, ham, provolone, shredded lettuce, sliced tomato and onion.
  3. Drizzle with oil and vinegar and season.
  4. Press under a board, then cut into thick slices.

Mains

Bilal's DJ booth pizza

Thin pepperoni pizza. Chill, don't bump the table.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Grate the mozzarella and measure out the pepperoni up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; stretch the dough and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  1. Stretch the dough into 2 thin bases.
  2. Spread with sauce, then add mozzarella, pepperoni and oregano.
  3. Bake on a hot steel at maximum heat.
  4. Cut into squares and leave in the box on the bench.

Sweets

Party sheet cakeVegetarian

Stab tried to burn the house down. Chocolate cake and frosting

Prep 30 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 350g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 90g cocoa
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Beat part of the butter with the sugar until pale and add the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, some of the cocoa and the milk.
  3. Bake in a tray at 175C and cool.
  4. Beat the remaining butter with the icing sugar and remaining cocoa.
  5. Spread over the cake and cover with sprinkles.

Drinks

Pineapple passionfruit party punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Pineapple, orange, passionfruit and lime with frozen pineapple, topped with lemonade

Prep 15 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Freeze the pineapple chunks on a tray up to 1 week ahead; mix the juices and passionfruit up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; add ice, frozen pineapple and lemonade just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pineapple chunks
  • 750ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 2 limes
  • 1l cold lemonade
  • 1 orange
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Cut the pineapple chunks and freeze on a tray.
  2. Freeze for 2 hours.
  3. Mix the pineapple juice, orange juice, passionfruit pulp and lime juice in a big bowl.
  4. Add ice and the frozen pineapple.
  5. Just before serving top with cold lemonade and float sliced orange and torn mint.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the dance-off starts at the party.
