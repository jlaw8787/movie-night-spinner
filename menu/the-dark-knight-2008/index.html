Snacks

Penthouse fundraiser canapés

Beef and horseradish cream on toast, salmon and crème fraîche on blinis

Prep 5 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g eye fillet
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 4 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 12 blinis
  • 100g crème fraîche
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Sear the eye fillet in the olive oil in a hot pan to rare, then rest and slice thin.
  2. Toast the baguette slices.
  3. Top the toast with beef and horseradish cream.
  4. Top the blinis with crème fraîche, smoked salmon and chives.
  5. Serve on black trays.

Hoisin pork belly bites

Roast pork belly cubes glazed with hoisin and honey, quick pickled cucumber

Prep 12 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork belly, cubed
  • 3 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp five spice
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 100ml rice vinegar
  • 24 toothpicks
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Toss the pork in the hoisin, honey and five spice.
  2. Roast, glazing twice, until sticky and cooked through.
  3. Quick pickle the cucumber in the vinegar.
  4. Serve the pork on toothpicks with the cucumber.

Mains

Joined-tables salmon with caper butter

Pan-seared salmon in foaming caper butter with crushed baby potatoes

Prep 7 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 salmon fillets, skin on
  • 80g butter
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 100g rocket
  • 40g parmesan, shaved
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and crush with oil and salt.
  2. Season the salmon and cook skin side down in a hot oiled pan, then flip.
  3. Wipe the pan, melt the butter until it foams brown, then add the capers, lemon juice and parsley.
  4. Toss the rocket with parmesan, oil and lemon.
  5. Plate the salmon on the potatoes and spoon over the butter.

Sweets

Penthouse fundraiser lemon tartVegetarian

Baked lemon cream tart in a sweet pastry case, dusted with icing sugar

Prep 7 min, cook 55 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sheet of sweet shortcrust pastry, for a 23cm tart
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml lemon juice
  • 2 lemons, zested
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Line a 23cm tart tin with the pastry and blind bake.
  2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, lemon juice, zest and cream.
  3. Pour in and bake at 150C until just set.
  4. Chill the tart well.
  5. Dust with icing sugar to serve.

Drinks

Alfred's smoky Scotch and ginger sourVegetarian

Blended Scotch, lemon and honey ginger syrup shaken and poured over one big ice cube

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g honey
  • 80ml water
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 200ml blended Scotch whisky
  • 80ml lemon juice
  • 20ml smoky Islay whisky
  • 4 pieces candied ginger
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Simmer the honey, water and ginger, then cool and strain.
  2. For each drink shake Scotch, lemon juice and syrup hard over ice.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in a tumbler.
  4. Float the smoky whisky on top and garnish with candied ginger.
