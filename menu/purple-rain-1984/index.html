Snacks

Not-Lake-Minnetonka fried fish bites

Crisp battered fish bites with tartare and lemon.

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare up to 2 days ahead; batter and fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g firm white fish fillets, cut in bite-size pieces
  • 100g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 200ml chilled soda water
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 tbsp gherkins, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  1. Stir the mayo with the capers, gherkins and a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Whisk the flours, a pinch of salt and the soda water into a light batter.
  3. Dip the fish and fry in oil at 180°C in batches until crisp, 12 min.
  4. Drain, salt and serve with the tartare and lemon wedges.

What's-the-password soft pretzelsVegetarian

Salted dough twists baked deep brown, with hot cheddar to dip.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Shape the bites up to 4 hours ahead and chill; boil and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ready-made pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 150g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp hot English mustard
  1. Roll the dough into ropes and cut into 3cm bites.
  2. Drop them in batches into simmering water with the bicarb for 30 seconds, then drain.
  3. Brush with egg, sprinkle with salt and bake at 220°C until deep brown, 15 min.
  4. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick, then melt in the cheddar and mustard, 15 min.

Jungle Love jerk chicken

Jerk chicken thighs, rice and peas, charred lime.

Prep 8 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to a day ahead; cook the rice and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 tbsp jerk seasoning paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g long-grain rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 400g tin red kidney beans, drained
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 thyme sprigs
  • 2 limes, halved
  1. Rub the chicken with the jerk paste and oil.
  2. Simmer the rice with the coconut milk, beans, spring onions, thyme and 250ml water, covered, until tender, 20 min.
  3. Grill the chicken over high heat, turning, until charred and cooked through, 15 min.
  4. Char the lime halves on the grill, 3 min.
  5. Serve the chicken on the rice and peas with the lime.

Keep-the-change Jucy Lucy burgers

Cheese-stuffed Minneapolis beef burgers, pickles, onions.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Shape and stuff the patties up to a day ahead and chill; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 brown onion, finely sliced
  • 1/2 cup dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp American mustard
  1. Divide the mince into 8 thin patties.
  2. Fold 2 slices of cheese into a square on 4 patties, top with the others and pinch the edges tightly shut.
  3. Cook in a hot pan, 5 minutes a side, pricking once so the steam escapes.
  4. Serve in buns with onion, pickles and mustard, and warn everyone the cheese is molten.

Purple Rain blackberry cobblerVegetarian

Blackberry and blueberry cobbler with vanilla cream.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Mix the topping up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen blackberries
  • 250g frozen blueberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 80g cold butter, diced
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 300ml pouring cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Toss the berries with 100g of the sugar and the cornflour and tip into a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and the rest of the sugar, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough, 8 min.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over the fruit and bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling, 35 min.
  4. Stir the vanilla into the cream and pour over to serve.
