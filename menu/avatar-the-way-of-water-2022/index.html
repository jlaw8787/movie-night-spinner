Reef village garlic prawns

Prawn skewers grilled with garlic, chilli, lime and butter.

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Toss and skewer the prawns up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 3 limes
  • 60g butter
  1. Soak 8 skewers. Crush the garlic, chop the red chillies, zest a lime and cut the rest into wedges. Melt the butter, 10 min.
  2. Toss the prawns with garlic, chilli, zest and butter and thread onto skewers.
  3. Grill a couple of minutes a side until charred.
  4. Serve with lime wedges and spoon over any butter left in the pan.

Tulkun tropical fruitVegetarian

Mango, pineapple and papaya with passionfruit, lime juice and toasted coconut.

Prep 18 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Cut the fruit up to 4 hours ahead and chill; toast the coconut up to 2 days ahead in a jar and add the lime and coconut to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 2 limes
  • 3 tbsp shredded coconut
  1. Toast the coconut in a dry pan, 4 min.
  2. Cut the mangoes, pineapple and papaya into wedges and halve the passionfruit.
  3. Pile onto a platter, squeeze over lime juice and scatter with coconut.
  4. Chill until serving.

Ilu ride sesame nori crisps

Seaweed sheets with sesame oil, honey and chilli salt, toasted crisp, for a first lesson on the reef.

How it is made
  • 1 pack nori sheets
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar sesame seeds
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  1. Nori sheets brushed with sesame oil, sprinkled with sesame seeds and salt, toasted in a dry pan until crisp and cut into strips. Brush 8 nori sheets lightly on one side with 2 tbsp sesame oil mixed with 1 tsp honey. Sprinkle with 2 tbsp sesame seeds, a pinch of chilli flakes and flaky salt. Stick 2 sheets together oiled sides out, then toast in a dry pan over medium heat for 1 minute a side, or bake at 150°C for 8 minutes, until crisp. Cut into strips with scissors and serve straight away.

Metkayina coconut fish

Snapper poached in coconut milk with lemongrass, red chilli and lime.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Simmer the lemongrass and chilli in the coconut milk up to 1 day ahead; add the fish and cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 stalks of lemongrass
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 1 red chilli
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 2 limes
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 handful mixed fresh herbs
  1. Bruise the lemongrass and slice the chilli.
  2. Simmer the lemongrass and chilli in the coconut milk, 10 min.
  3. Add the snapper fillets and poach gently, 10 min.
  4. Steam the rice, 15 min.
  5. Squeeze in lime juice and serve over the rice with fresh herbs.

Sully family chilli lime chicken

Thighs in garlic, fish sauce and brown sugar, chargrilled, mango salad, for the family fortress.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 limes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bottle fish sauce
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Chicken thighs marinated in lime, garlic and chilli, grilled until charred, sliced over a salad of mango, mint and red onion. Marinate 1kg chicken thigh for 1 hour with the juice of 2 limes, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 chopped red chilli, 1 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tbsp brown sugar and 2 tbsp oil. Grill over high heat 5 to 6 minutes a side until charred and cooked through, then rest. Toss 2 diced mangoes with a thinly sliced red onion, mint, coriander, lime juice and a pinch of salt. Slice the chicken over the salad.

Lagoon coconut pudding

Coconut milk set with gelatine in glasses, topped with diced mango.

Prep 18 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 4 gelatine sheets
  • 2 mangoes
  • 3 passionfruit
  1. Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water. Dice the mangoes, 8 min.
  2. Warm the coconut milk with sugar until dissolved.
  3. Squeeze the gelatine and stir in.
  4. Pour into glasses and chill until set.
  5. Top with mango and passionfruit pulp.

Reef coconut pineapple crushNo alcohol

Frozen pineapple, coconut water and coconut cream blended with lime and mint.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; juice the lime and pick the mint up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g frozen pineapple
  • 500ml coconut water
  • 100ml coconut cream
  • 1 lime
  • 1 handful mint
  • 1 cup ice
  1. Juice the lime.
  2. Blend the pineapple, coconut water, coconut cream, lime juice, mint and ice until thick and slushy.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses and finish with a pineapple wedge and mint sprig.
