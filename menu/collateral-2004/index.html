Snacks

Cab driver's pretzel, peanut and jerky mix

Max, I do this for a living. Spiced nuts and jerky

Prep 5 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g pretzels
  • 150g salted peanuts
  • 100g cashews
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 100g beef jerky
  1. Toss the pretzels, salted peanuts and cashews with melted butter, paprika and sugar.
  2. Roast at 160C.
  3. Cool.
  4. Tip into paper bags with strips of jerky.

Fever nightclub fried chicken

Peter Lim's nightclub. Wings glazed in gochujang honey

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; do the first fry up to 2 hours ahead, then fry again and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 100g potato starch
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Toss the wings in the starch.
  2. Heat the oil to 160C and fry the wings in batches, then rest.
  3. Fry again at 190C until shattering.
  4. Simmer the gochujang, soy, honey and garlic until glossy.
  5. Toss the wings in the sauce and top with sesame.

Daniel's jazz club smoky devilled eggsOn the printed menu

Yolks whipped with mayo, mustard and chipotle, crispy bacon, paprika, warm orange and fennel olives

How it is made
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 1 tin chipotles in adobo
  • 2 rashers bacon
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 250g mixed olives
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 orange
  • 1 jar fennel seeds
  1. Devilled eggs whipped with mayo, mustard and chipotle, topped with crispy bacon and smoked paprika, with warm olives in orange and fennel alongside. Boil 8 eggs for 10 minutes, cool and peel. Halve and whip the yolks with 4 tbsp mayo, 1 tsp mustard and 1 chopped chipotle in adobo, then pipe back in. Top with crumbled crispy bacon from 2 rashers and smoked paprika. Warm 250g mixed olives in 3 tbsp olive oil with strips of orange peel and 1 tsp fennel seeds for 5 minutes and serve alongside.

Mains

Late night carne asada tacos

Lime and orange marinated skirt steak, seared and sliced into corn tortillas

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the steak in the fridge up to 1 day ahead (at least 2 hours); sear, slice and warm the tortillas on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g skirt steak
  • 2 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 16 corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 jar salsa roja
  1. Marinate the steak in lime and orange juice, garlic and cumin.
  2. Sear over very high heat.
  3. Rest, then slice against the grain.
  4. Warm the tortillas. Chop the onion and coriander.
  5. Serve in tortillas with onion, coriander and salsa roja.

Ida's hospital chicken noodle soupOn the printed menu

Golden thigh broth with carrot and celery, egg pasta, dill and lemon, for Max's mum

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs on the bone
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 jar bay leaves
  • 200g egg noodles
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  1. Chicken thighs simmered into a golden broth with onion, carrot and celery, shredded back in with egg noodles, dill, black pepper and a squeeze of lemon. Put 1kg chicken thighs on the bone in a pot with 2L water, an onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks and a bay leaf. Simmer gently for 1 hour, skimming. Lift out the chicken, shred the meat and dice the vegetables. Return both to the strained broth, add 200g egg noodles and simmer 5 minutes. Season well and finish with chopped dill and lemon juice.

Sweets

Island Limos coconut tartVegetarian

Coconut custard in shortcrust, gotta be perfect

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 200g desiccated coconut
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 200ml coconut milk
  1. Line a tart tin with the pastry and blind-bake.
  2. Whisk the coconut, eggs, sugar, melted butter and coconut milk.
  3. Pour in and bake at 170C until golden and set.
  4. Cool.

Drinks

Max's cab Vietnamese iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Twelve years in this cab. Coffee, condensed milk and ice

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Brew the coffee up to 1 day ahead and chill it, then reheat until hot before pouring over the condensed milk
Recipe for 4
  • 80g dark roast ground coffee
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 8 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the ground coffee with the boiling water to make very strong coffee.
  2. Put condensed milk and a pinch of salt in each of four tall glasses.
  3. Pour hot coffee into each and stir until combined.
  4. Fill with ice, stir again and serve with a straw.

When to serve

Serve the mains when they pull up at the jazz club.
