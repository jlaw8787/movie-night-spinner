Quarry day Italian sub rolls

Long soft rolls dressed with oregano vinaigrette and layered Italian style.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, wrapped tight
Recipe for 4
  • 4 long soft rolls
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 12 slices of provolone
  • 16 slices of salami
  • 12 slices of leg ham
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 4 tbsp pickled peppers
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Whisk the oil, vinegar, oregano, salt and pepper.
  2. Split the rolls and drizzle the insides with half the dressing.
  3. Layer with provolone, salami and ham.
  4. Top with lettuce, tomato, red onion and pickled peppers, drizzle with the rest of the dressing and close, 6 min.
  5. Wrap each tightly in baking paper and halve.

Red balloon roast tomato ricotta toastVegetarian

Ricotta and blistered roast tomatoes on toasted sourdough, the reddest thing on the table.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roast the tomatoes and whip the ricotta up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; toast the sourdough just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cherry tomatoes on the vine
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 250g ricotta, whipped
  • 1 sourdough loaf, sliced and toasted
  1. Roast the tomatoes at 200°C for 10 minutes with olive oil and thyme.
  2. Toast the sourdough, 5 min.
  3. Spread fresh ricotta over the toast and top with the tomatoes. You'll float too, 15 min.

Paper boat loaded beef nachos

Corn chips under spiced ground beef, cheddar, black beans, pico de gallo, guac and sour cream.

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 tin tomato paste
  • 300g corn chips
  • 1 tin black beans
  • 200g cheddar
  • 2 avocados
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 200ml sour cream
  1. Corn chips piled into paper boats with spiced beef mince, melted cheddar, black beans, pico de gallo, guacamole and sour cream, eaten with your fingers. Brown 400g beef mince with 1 diced onion, 2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika and 2 tbsp tomato paste, add 100ml water and simmer 10 minutes. Layer 300g corn chips on a tray with the beef, 1 drained tin black beans and 200g grated cheddar and bake at 200°C for 8 minutes. Mash 2 avocados with lime and salt. Dice 2 tomatoes with red onion and coriander. Scoop into paper boats and top with guacamole, pico and sour cream.

Maine lobster rolls

Lobster or shrimp salad with mayo, celery and chives in a buttered toasted bun.

Prep 14 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the lobster filling up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; butter and toast the buns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g cooked lobster or prawn meat, chopped
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 celery stick, diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 30g butter
  1. Fold the lobster with the mayonnaise, lemon juice, celery and chives.
  2. Butter and toast the cut sides of the buns, 8 min.
  3. Pile the filling in.

Derry diner double cheeseburgers

Two griddled patties, cheese, pickles and tangy sauce, crinkle-cut fries, beep beep.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 cheese slices
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  • 800g frozen crinkle-cut chips
  1. Two thin smashed beef patties with melted cheese, pickles, onion and a tangy burger sauce in a toasted soft bun, with crinkle-cut chips. Roll 800g beef mince into 8 balls. Mix 4 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tbsp mustard and 1 tbsp chopped pickles for the sauce. Bake 800g frozen crinkle-cut chips at 220°C for 25 minutes. Heat a heavy pan until smoking, smash the balls thin, salt and cook 2 minutes, flip, top with cheese slices and cook 1 minute. Toast 4 buns, spread with sauce, and stack two patties with pickles and finely diced onion.

Red balloon cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cake and cream cheese balls on sticks, dipped in red candy coating.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min, 40 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 vanilla cake, 500g, crumbled
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 50g icing sugar
  • 300g red candy melts
  • 16 lollipop sticks
  • 1 ball string
  1. Crumble the cake into the cream cheese beaten with the icing sugar.
  2. Roll into balls, push in lollipop sticks and chill, 40 min.
  3. Melt the candy melts and dip the pops, 15 min.
  4. Tie a bit of string to each like a balloon.

Not grey water strawberry lemonadeNo alcohol

Strawberry and lemon juice with sugar and soda, served cold.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the strawberry syrup up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; squeeze the lemons and top with soda water on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries, hulled, plus extra to serve
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 6 lemons
  • 1 L chilled soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 3 cups ice
  1. Simmer the strawberries with the sugar and water for 5 minutes. Crush and strain to a syrup, then cool.
  2. Squeeze the lemons into a jug and add the syrup. Stir.
  3. Add plenty of ice, sliced strawberries and lemon wheels, then top with soda water and a few mint leaves.
