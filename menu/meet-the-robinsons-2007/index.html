Snacks

Lewis's peanut butter and jam bitesVegetarian

Crustless bread with peanut butter and strawberry jam, cut into small triangles.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the sandwiches up to 3 hours ahead and keep covered and chilled. Cut and stack just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 1 jar peanut butter
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  1. Spread the bread with butter, peanut butter and jam.
  2. Cut off the crusts.
  3. Slice each sandwich into four small triangles and stack on a plate.

Uncle Art's space pizza bites

Small pizza rounds with sauce, mozzarella, pepperoni and oregano.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut the dough rounds up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge. Top and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 1 jar pizza sauce
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 150g pepperoni
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Roll the dough thin and cut into small rounds, 10 min.
  2. Top each with sauce, grated mozzarella, a slice of pepperoni and oregano.
  3. Bake until crisp and bubbling, 10 min.

Science fair chicken nuggets and dips

Parmesan-breaded chicken nuggets with three dipping sauces.

How it is made
  • 600g chicken breast
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko crumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 can olive oil spray
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle barbecue sauce
  • 150g garlic aioli
  1. Chicken breast nuggets in crunchy parmesan panko, baked golden and served with three test tube dips: honey mustard, barbecue and garlic aioli. Cut 600g chicken breast into nugget pieces. Dust in 50g plain flour, dip in 2 beaten eggs, then coat in 150g panko mixed with 30g grated parmesan and 1 tsp paprika. Lay on a lined tray, spray with oil and bake at 220°C for 15 minutes, turning once, until golden. Mix 2 tbsp honey with 2 tbsp Dijon and 2 tbsp mayo. Serve with that, barbecue sauce and garlic aioli in small glasses.

Mains

Meatball cannon dinner

Beef and pork meatballs simmered in tomato and basil, over spaghetti.

Prep 25 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; simmer in the tomatoes up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently. Cook the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cans of crushed tomatoes
  • 1 handful basil
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Mix the minces with parmesan and egg, roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in oil, 10 min.
  3. Add the tomatoes and torn basil and simmer, 40 min.
  4. Cook the spaghetti and pile the meatballs on top, 12 min.

Time machine chicken tacos with lime slaw

Grilled lime chicken tacos, a taste of the future.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 limes
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/4 red cabbage
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 150g sour cream
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Chicken thighs grilled with lime and chilli, tucked into soft tortillas with slaw, avocado and crema. Marinate 700g chicken thigh with the juice of 1 lime, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 crushed garlic clove and oil for 30 minutes. Grill 5 minutes a side and slice. Toss a quarter of a red cabbage, finely shredded, with the juice of 1 lime, coriander and salt. Mix 150g sour cream with lime zest and a pinch of salt for crema. Warm 12 small flour tortillas and fill with chicken, slaw, sliced avocado and crema.

Sweets

Robinsons' party cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with bright buttercream, frosted for a very loud dinner party.

Prep 12 min, cook 28 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cakes
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml full cream milk
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 125g butter, softened, for the buttercream
  • food colouring, a few drops
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a muffin tin. Beat the cake butter and sugar, then the eggs, then fold in flour and milk, 10 min.
  2. Bake, 18 min.
  3. Cool completely, 20 min.
  4. Beat the buttercream butter with the icing sugar and colouring and pipe on top. Add a little icing arrow to each.

Bowler Hat Guy's grape smoothieNo alcohol

Frozen red grapes, banana, grape juice, yoghurt. Doris the hat's idea.

Prep 8 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Freeze the grapes overnight, or up to 1 week ahead in a freezer bag. Blend each batch to order
Recipe for 4
  • 500g red grapes
  • 1 banana
  • 250ml grape juice
  • 200g vanilla yoghurt
  1. Wash the grapes and pull from the stems.
  2. Freeze overnight.
  3. Blend with the banana, grape juice and yoghurt until thick.
  4. Pour into four glasses and serve with straws.
