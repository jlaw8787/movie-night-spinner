Snacks

Knuckles's marmalade sandwichesVegetarian

Seville orange marmalade boiled from scratch, spread on buttered bread.

Prep 20 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Marmalade keeps for months
Recipe for 4
  • 500g Seville oranges
  • 1.2 L water
  • 1kg caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 80g butter
  1. Simmer the whole oranges in the water until soft.
  2. Halve, scoop out the pips and shred the peel.
  3. Return to the liquid with the sugar and lemon juice and boil hard to 105°C.
  4. Jar and cool.
  5. Butter the bread thickly, spread with marmalade and cut into triangles.

Prison kitchen leek and potato soupVegetarian

Thick leek and potato soup in tin mugs, with buttered toast soldiers.

Prep 7 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 2 days ahead; reheat and add the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 40g butter
  • 3 leeks, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 600g potatoes, diced
  • 1 L vegetable stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Melt the butter in a large pot and cook the leeks and onion gently for 10 minutes without colouring.
  2. Add the potatoes and stock and simmer 20 minutes until soft.
  3. Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and season.
  4. Toast and butter the bread and cut into soldiers.
  5. Serve the soup in mugs with snipped chives and the soldiers.

Mains

Dish of the day: bear pie (it's chicken)

Chicken, leek and mushroom in a creamy Dijon sauce under pastry.

Prep 20 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, diced
  • 2 leeks
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Brown the chicken in some of the butter, then add the sliced leeks and mushrooms and soften.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream, Dijon and thyme and simmer until thick.
  3. Cool, tip into a pie dish, top with puff pastry and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  5. Toss the peas in the remaining butter and serve alongside.

Funfair sausage and onion rolls

Thick pork sausages grilled sticky with buttery onions and English mustard, funfair style.

How it is made
  • 3 brown onions
  • 40g butter
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 8 thick pork sausages
  • 8 soft white rolls
  • 1 jar English mustard
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Thick pork sausages grilled slowly until sticky and browned, piled into soft white rolls with sweet buttery onions and English mustard or tomato sauce. Cook 3 sliced onions in 40g butter with a pinch of brown sugar over low heat for 25 minutes until soft and golden. Grill 8 thick pork sausages over medium heat for 15 minutes, turning often, until browned all over. Split 8 soft white rolls, add a sausage and a heap of onions, and serve with English mustard and tomato sauce.

Barbershop scone fingersVegetarian

Scone fingers with strawberry jam and clotted cream, for visiting day.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cold butter
  • 350g self-raising flour
  • 280ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  • 200g clotted cream
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then mix in most of the milk lightly.
  2. Pat out and cut into fingers.
  3. Glaze with milk and bake at 220°C.
  4. Serve with strawberry jam and clotted cream.

One red sock pink Victoria spongeVegetarian

Raspberry jam and cream sponge, dyed pink. One red sock turned the whole prison pink.

Prep 28 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Bake the day before, fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 jar raspberry jam
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 bottle pink food colouring
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 180°C.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Whip the cream and mix the icing sugar with a little water and pink colouring.
  5. Fill with jam and cream and top with pale pink icing.

Drinks

Cell block orange iced teaNo alcohol

Black tea sweetened with honey and lemon, over ice and orange slices.

Prep 6 min, cook 4 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 English breakfast tea bags
  • 1.2 L boiling water
  • 4 tbsp orange marmalade
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 orange
  • 250ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water for 4 minutes and remove.
  2. Stir in the marmalade and lemon juice until dissolved.
  3. Chill until very cold.
  4. Fill four tall glasses with ice and orange slices, pour in the tea and add a splash of soda water.
