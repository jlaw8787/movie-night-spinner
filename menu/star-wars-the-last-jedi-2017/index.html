Snacks

Luke's lemon dill salmon skewers

Marinated salmon chunks grilled on skewers, with a dill yoghurt dip

Prep 5 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 2 hours, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g salmon fillet, skin off
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 3 tbsp chopped dill, plus extra for the dip
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  1. Cut the salmon into chunks and marinate in the lemon juice, dill and oil for 20 minutes.
  2. Thread onto skewers and grill for 2 minutes a side.
  3. Stir more dill and lemon through the yoghurt for a dip.

Crait red salt chipsVegetarian

Paper-thin fried potato chips with sea salt and smoked paprika

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Slice the potatoes paper thin on a mandoline and rinse well. Dry thoroughly.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 170C in batches until crisp and golden.
  3. Toss straight away with salt and smoked paprika so the red shows through like the salt flats.

Canto Bight prosciutto gorgonzola figsOn the printed menu

Stuffed with blue cheese, wrapped in cured ham, grilled blistered, hot honey on a gold tray

How it is made
  • 12 fresh figs
  • 120g gorgonzola
  • 6 slices prosciutto
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 1 bunch thyme
  1. Fresh figs split and filled with gorgonzola, wrapped in prosciutto and blistered under the grill, finished with hot honey and cracked pepper on a gold tray. Cut a cross in the top of 12 fresh figs and press about 10g gorgonzola into each, 120g in all. Wrap each fig in half a slice of prosciutto. Grill on a lined tray for 5 minutes until the prosciutto crisps and the cheese melts. Warm 3 tbsp honey with a pinch of chilli flakes and drizzle over. Finish with cracked pepper and thyme leaves.

Mains

Resistance chicken rice bowls

Lemon oregano chicken, roast red pepper, cucumber and feta over rice

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Chicken can marinate up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  1. Marinate the chicken in lemon juice, oregano and oil while the oven heats.
  2. Roast the capsicums at 220C until soft and blistered, then peel and slice into strips.
  3. Cook the rice. Grill the chicken until cooked through, then slice.
  4. Build bowls of rice with the chicken, capsicum, cucumber and feta and drizzle with tahini.

Chewie's not-a-porg roast poussinOn the printed menu

Chewie roasting his dinner over the fire as the porgs stare

How it is made
  • 4 poussins
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 1 bunch kale
  • 1 lemon
  1. Whole spatchcocked poussins rubbed with garlic, thyme and smoked paprika, charred over the coals, with crisp smashed potatoes and lemony greens. No porgs harmed. Cut the backbones out of 4 poussins and press flat. Rub with 4 crushed garlic cloves, thyme, 2 tsp smoked paprika, olive oil and salt. Boil 1kg baby potatoes for 15 minutes, smash flat on an oiled tray and roast at 220C for 35 minutes. Grill or roast the poussins at 220C for 30 minutes until the juices run clear. Wilt a bunch of kale with lemon and garlic.

Sweets

Green pistachio milk panna cotta

Pistachio panna cotta set in glasses with crushed pistachios and honey

Prep 20 min, then 5.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g pistachios, plus extra crushed to serve
  • 250ml milk
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 3 gelatine sheets, soaked
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Blend the pistachios with the milk until fine, then strain.
  2. Warm the pistachio milk with the cream and sugar. Stir in the soaked gelatine until dissolved.
  3. Pour into glasses and set overnight.
  4. Top with crushed pistachios and a drizzle of honey.

Drinks

Throne room red punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Let the past die. Cranberry and pomegranate juice, soda water, no alcohol

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1l cranberry juice
  • 1l pomegranate juice
  • 1l soda water
  • 1 pomegranate, seeds only
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and a spoon of pomegranate seeds.
  2. Pour in equal parts cranberry and pomegranate juice to halfway, then top with soda water.
  3. Stir once so it swirls deep red.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Chewie roasts his dinner while the porgs watch.
