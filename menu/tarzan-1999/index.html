Snacks

Gorilla family fruit platterVegetarian

Jane met the baboons first. Banana, mango, pineapple, papaya.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Cut the mangoes, pineapple and papaya up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; slice the bananas and finish with passionfruit, lime and chilli salt just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 bananas
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 2 passionfruit
  • 1 lime
  • 1 pinch chilli salt
  1. Cut the bananas, mangoes, pineapple and papaya into big chunks.
  2. Pile on banana leaves with the passionfruit pulp, a squeeze of lime and a pinch of chilli salt.

Terk's camp raid crackersVegetarian

Crunchy roasted chickpeas and cashews with curry powder and honey

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Keeps 4 days airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins chickpeas, drained and dried
  • 150g cashews
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Toss the chickpeas with the cashews, oil, curry powder and honey.
  2. Roast at 180C for 25 minutes until crunchy.
  3. Tip out messily on a board.

Mains

Professor Porter's expedition curry

Porter came all this way to study gorillas. Chicken, tomato, coconut.

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 piece ginger, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1kg chicken thighs, diced
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 tbsp mango chutney
  1. Soften the onion in oil with the ginger, garlic and curry powder.
  2. Add the chicken, tomatoes and coconut milk.
  3. Simmer for 40 minutes.
  4. Cook the rice and serve with mango chutney.

Treehouse river fish and coconut stew

Firm white fillets in coconut milk with tomato, ginger and spinach, over rice, safe from Sabor

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 1 day ahead; add the fish and spinach on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g firm white fish fillets, in chunks
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 20g ginger, grated
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Soften the onion, garlic, ginger and chilli in the oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes and coconut milk and simmer. Meanwhile steam the rice.
  3. Add the fish and simmer gently until just cooked.
  4. Stir through the spinach and lime juice and serve over rice.

Sweets

Banana frittersVegetarian

Thick banana slices fried in light batter, rolled in cinnamon sugar, honey drizzle

Prep 12 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the cinnamon sugar up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 200ml sparkling water
  • 4 bananas, thickly sliced
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Whisk the flour with the sparkling water into a batter.
  2. Dip the banana slices in the batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 180C until puffed.
  3. Roll in cinnamon sugar and drizzle with honey.

Drinks

Jane's peach iced teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Black iced tea sweetened with peach and honey syrup

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bags black tea
  • 1l boiling water
  • 3 ripe peaches
  • 80g honey
  • 100ml water
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 sprig mint
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in boiling water for 4 minutes, remove and cool.
  2. Simmer two sliced peaches with the honey and water for 5 minutes, crush and strain.
  3. Stir the syrup and lemon juice into the tea and chill.
  4. Serve over ice with fresh peach slices and mint.

When to serve

Serve the tea when Terk trashes Jane's camp.
