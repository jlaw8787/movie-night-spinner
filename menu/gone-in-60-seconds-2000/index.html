Car-crusher smash burger sliders

Calitri's car crusher, waiting for Kip

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce and slice the onion up to a day ahead; roll the beef into balls the morning of and smash to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince, 20 per cent fat
  • 8 soft slider buns
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 1 white onion, very thinly sliced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp American mustard
  • 20g butter
  1. Mix the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and mustard for the sauce.
  2. Roll the mince into 8 loose balls and season well with salt.
  3. Heat a heavy pan until smoking, add the balls, lay onion on each and smash flat with a spatula as hard as you can.
  4. Cook until the edges are dark and lacy, flip, add the cheese and cook a minute more.
  5. Butter and toast the buns, then build with sauce, pickles and a patty.

Otto's dog, chilli and cheese

Otto's dog swallows the keys

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced, to serve
  1. Brown the mince, breaking it up, then add the onion and garlic and cook until soft.
  2. Stir in the chilli powder and cumin, add the tomatoes and a splash of water, and simmer until thick.
  3. Grill the franks, turning, until blistered.
  4. Tuck them into the toasted rolls, spoon over the chilli and top with cheddar and raw onion.

Long Beach dock street tacos

They threw Calitri out of England and France

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw dressing and crema up to a day ahead; cook the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g firm white fish fillets, such as flathead
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 limes
  • 2 cup red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  • 125g sour cream
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Rub the fish with the chipotle, cumin, oil and salt.
  2. Toss the cabbage with the juice of one lime and a pinch of salt; mix the sour cream with a squeeze of lime.
  3. Sear the fish in a hot pan until it flakes, about 3 minutes a side, then break into chunks.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan, then fill with fish, slaw, crema and coriander, with lime wedges.

Heist night barbecue ribs with buttermilk slaw

Sphinx's one speech: I say poor us

Prep 25 min, cook 170 min · Make ahead: Bake the ribs in foil up to a day ahead and chill; glaze and char on the barbecue to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork spare ribs, in racks
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/4 green cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Mix the sugar, paprika, garlic powder, cayenne and 2 teaspoons of salt and rub all over the ribs.
  2. Wrap the racks tightly in foil and bake at 150C until the meat pulls back from the bones.
  3. Whisk the buttermilk, mayonnaise and a tablespoon of the vinegar, and toss with the cabbage and carrot.
  4. Mix the barbecue sauce with the rest of the vinegar, brush over the ribs and char on a hot barbecue or under the grill, basting, until sticky.
  5. Cut between the bones and serve with the slaw.

Sixty-second chocolate mug cakes with salted caramelVegetarian

Eleanor is the unicorn says Donny

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Weigh out the dry mix up to a week ahead; mix and microwave to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 tbsp self-raising flour
  • 8 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 8 tbsp milk
  • 6 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 8 squares dark chocolate
  • 4 tbsp salted caramel sauce
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar and cocoa in a bowl, then beat in the eggs, milk and oil until smooth.
  2. Divide between 4 large microwave-safe mugs and push 2 squares of chocolate into the middle of each.
  3. Microwave each mug on high for about 60 seconds, until risen but still soft in the centre.
  4. Top each with a spoon of salted caramel and a scoop of ice cream and serve straight away.

Sway's michelada with Tecate and limeVegetarian

Sway fixes cars by day, pours drinks by night

Prep 8 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the tomato and lime base up to 4 hours ahead and keep it cold; top with beer to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 x 355ml cans Tecate or other Mexican lager
  • 250ml tomato juice
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp chilli salt
  • 1 cup ice
  1. Run a lime wedge round the rim of 4 glasses and dip in the chilli salt.
  2. Mix the tomato juice, the juice of 2 limes, the hot sauce and soy sauce.
  3. Fill the glasses with ice, add the tomato mix and top slowly with the cold beer.
