Snacks

Hot honey-roasted nutsVegetarian

Mixed nuts glazed with butter, sugar and cinnamon, in paper cones for the bus ride.

Prep 8 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 300g mixed nuts
  1. Melt the butter with the honey, sugar, cinnamon and salt.
  2. Toss through the nuts, spread on a lined tray and roast at 170°C, stirring halfway.
  3. Cool so they crisp, then pour into paper cones.

Bureau men's rooftop crostiniVegetarian

Keep your hat on, it opens the doors.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g baguette
  • 120g soft goat's cheese
  • 30ml thickened cream
  • 30g honey
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Cut the baguette into 8 slices and toast at 200°C until golden.
  2. Beat the goat's cheese with the cream until light.
  3. Warm the honey gently.
  4. Spread the cheese on the toast, drizzle with honey and finish with thyme and pepper.

Mains

Manhattan steak frites

Seared sirloin under shallot red wine butter, double-fried chips, the date the plan won't allow.

Prep 18 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Shallot butter up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 2 shallots, finely diced
  • 80ml red wine
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 L neutral oil for frying
  • 800g sirloin steak, in four pieces
  • 1 tbsp oil for searing
  1. Simmer the shallots with the wine until almost dry. Cool, then mix into the butter with the salt and chill.
  2. Cut the potatoes into frites, rinse and dry thoroughly.
  3. Fry in oil at 140°C, then drain and cool.
  4. Fry again at 185°C until crisp. Salt lightly.
  5. Season the steaks and sear hard in a hot pan with the oil to your preferred doneness.
  6. Rest the steaks, then slice and top with the shallot red wine butter. Serve with frites.

The Chairman's chocolate fondantVegetarian

Dark chocolate and butter cake baked hot so the centre stays molten.

Prep 8 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 145g butter, plus extra for the moulds
  • 125g dark chocolate
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 30g plain flour
  1. Butter four 150ml moulds. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Melt the chocolate with the butter gently and cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the eggs, yolks and sugar until pale. Stir in the chocolate mixture, then fold in the flour.
  4. Divide among the moulds and bake until the edges are set and centres stay soft.
  5. Rest briefly, loosen the edges and turn onto plates. Serve immediately.

Election night French 75Vegetarian

Gin, lemon and sugar shaken and topped with brut in flutes, for the concession speech.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead and keep it chilled; shake and top with champagne to order
Recipe for 4
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 40ml hot water
  • 120ml gin
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 1 bag ice
  • 360ml cold brut champagne
  • 1 lemon, for peel strips
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Shake the gin, lemon juice and cooled syrup with ice.
  3. Strain evenly into 4 chilled flutes and top each with champagne, pouring slowly.
  4. Finish with a long strip of lemon peel.
