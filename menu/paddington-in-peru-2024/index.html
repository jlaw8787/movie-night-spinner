Snacks

Marmalade, ham and cheddar toasties

Grilled sandwiches of thick-cut marmalade, ham and cheddar.

Prep 12 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices thick white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 1 jar thick-cut orange marmalade
  • 150g sliced ham
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  1. Butter the slices of thick white bread on one side.
  2. Spread the thick-cut marmalade on the unbuttered sides, then add the sliced ham and the grated vintage cheddar.
  3. Close with the other slices, butter side out.
  4. Cook in a frying pan over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes a side, pressing down, until golden and the cheese melts.
  5. Cut into squares.

Peruvian chicken causa bites

Lime and ají amarillo mashed potato layered with shredded chicken.

Prep 0 min, cook 32 min, then 83 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 300g chicken breast
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 avocado
  1. Boil the potatoes, mash smooth with the juice of the limes, the ají amarillo paste, the oil and salt.
  2. Mix the cooked shredded chicken breast with the mayonnaise.
  3. Layer potato, chicken, sliced avocado and more potato in a tin, chill 1 hour and cut into squares.

Choclo with queso fresco and ají sauce

Sweet Andean corn cobs with queso fresco and ají sauce, river picnic food.

How it is made
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 250g haloumi
  • 2 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 100g feta
  • 80ml evaporated milk
  • 2 plain crackers
  • 1 lime
  1. Fat corn cobs boiled and charred, served with slabs of grilled fresh white cheese and a creamy, mild yellow chilli and lime sauce. Boil 4 corn cobs (or Peruvian choclo if you can get it) for 8 minutes, then char on a hot grill pan for 4 minutes, turning. Slice 250g haloumi or queso fresco and grill 1 minute a side. Blend 2 tbsp ají amarillo paste with 100g feta, 80ml evaporated milk, 2 crackers and the juice of half a lime until smooth. Cut the cobs in thirds and serve with the cheese and sauce.

Beef lomo saltado with chips

Wok-seared beef, red onion and tomato with soy, over crisp chips.

Prep 0 min, cook 49 min · Make ahead: Slice the beef, onion and tomatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake the chips and sear in the wok just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g frozen chips
  • 600g beef rump
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 300g long grain rice
  1. Bake the frozen chips until crisp.
  2. Sear the sliced beef rump in a smoking wok, add the sliced red onion and the tomatoes in wedges, then the soy and the vinegar.
  3. Toss through the chips and serve with the cooked rice.

Ají de gallina with rice and egg

A creamy Peruvian chicken dish for the family.

How it is made
  • 700g chicken breast
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 3 slices white bread
  • 250ml evaporated milk
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • ground turmeric
  • 50g walnuts
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1.5 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 eggs
  • 80g black olives
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Shredded chicken in a creamy yellow chilli, walnut and parmesan sauce thickened with bread, over rice with boiled egg, black olives and a sprinkle of coriander. Poach 700g chicken breast in 1L stock for 15 minutes and shred, keeping the stock. Soak 3 slices of white bread in 250ml evaporated milk. Fry 1 diced onion and 2 garlic cloves, add 3 tbsp ají amarillo paste and 1 tsp turmeric. Blend with the soaked bread and 50g walnuts, return to the pan with 300ml stock and simmer 10 minutes. Stir in the chicken and 40g parmesan. Serve over rice with halved boiled eggs and olives.

Sweets

AlfajoresVegetarian

Crumbly shortbread cookies sandwiched with dulce de leche.

Prep 24 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Bake the biscuits up to 5 days ahead, airtight; sandwich with the dulce de leche within a day of serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 150g cornflour
  • 100g plain flour
  • 300g dulce de leche
  • 50g desiccated coconut
  1. Beat the butter with the icing sugar, then mix in the cornflour and the flour.
  2. Roll 5mm thick, cut the rounds and bake at 170°C for 10 minutes until pale.
  3. Sandwich in pairs with the dulce de leche and roll the edges in coconut.

Drinks

Chicha moradaNo alcohol

Purple corn drink simmered with pineapple, cinnamon and clove.

Prep 3 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Simmer, strain and chill up to 3 days ahead in a jug in the fridge; add ice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dried purple corn
  • 1 pineapple
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 limes
  • 2 L water
  1. Simmer the dried purple corn with the peel and core of the pineapple, the cinnamon sticks and the cloves in the water for 45 minutes.
  2. Strain, stir in the sugar and the juice of the limes, and chill.
  3. Serve over ice.

When to serve

Serve the marmalade sandwiches when Paddington pulls one from under his hat.
