Fishing cabin cornmeal fish bites with tartare

Cornmeal fish bites with tartare, for the weekend at the fishing cabin

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the tartare up to 2 days ahead; crumb and fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g flathead or basa fillets, in chunks
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 120g fine cornmeal (polenta)
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp gherkins, chopped
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, gherkins, capers and a squeeze of lemon into a tartare.
  2. Soak the fish in the buttermilk while you mix the cornmeal, flour, Cajun seasoning and salt.
  3. Roll the fish in the cornmeal mix to coat well.
  4. Fry in batches at 180C until golden and cooked through, then drain and serve with the tartare and lemon.

Thunderbird onion rings with comeback sauceVegetarian

Onion rings with comeback sauce, for Louise's 1966 Thunderbird convertible

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead; batter and fry the rings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large white onions, in thick rings
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 250ml chilled soda water
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce and hot sauce into a comeback sauce.
  2. Whisk the flour, cornflour, paprika and salt with the soda water into a light batter.
  3. Dip the rings and fry in batches at 180C until crisp and golden.
  4. Drain, salt well and serve hot with the sauce.

Mains

Leave-Darryl-a-note chicken-fried steak

Crumbed steak fried crisp with gravy and mash. Thelma leaves Darryl a note.

Prep 8 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Pound and crumb the steaks up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 thin beef minute steaks
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml vegetable oil
  • 30g butter
  • 400ml milk
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with a knob of butter and a splash of milk; keep warm.
  2. Season 100g flour with the garlic powder and salt, and whisk the eggs with the buttermilk.
  3. Dredge the steaks in flour, then egg, then flour again, and fry in the hot oil until crisp, 3 minutes a side.
  4. Pour off all but 2 tbsp of the fat, stir in the butter and remaining flour, then whisk in the milk and pepper and simmer into gravy.
  5. Blanch the beans and serve with the steaks, mash and gravy.

Mexico-bound pork carnitas tacos

Slow-cooked pork carnitas tacos with lime. Louise is set on Mexico.

Prep 20 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Braise the pork up to 2 days ahead; crisp it in a hot pan to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder, in large chunks
  • 2 oranges, juiced
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 limes
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 200g tomatillo salsa verde
  • 1 cup coriander leaves
  1. Season the pork well and put it in a heavy pot with the orange juice, garlic, cumin, oregano, bay and enough water to just cover.
  2. Simmer, covered, until the pork falls apart, then uncover and boil until the liquid is gone and the fat sizzles.
  3. Toss the onion with the juice of 2 limes and a pinch of salt and leave to pickle.
  4. Shred the pork and crisp it in its own fat in a hot pan.
  5. Warm the tortillas and fill with pork, pickled onion, salsa verde and coriander.

Wild Turkey pecan pie with vanilla ice creamVegetarian

Thelma adds two bottles of Wild Turkey

Prep 5 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep in the fridge; warm a slice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 23cm shortcrust pie case, blind baked
  • 200g pecans
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 180ml golden syrup
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp Kentucky straight bourbon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Toast the pecans at 180C for 6 minutes, then scatter them in the pie case.
  2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, golden syrup, butter, bourbon, vanilla and a pinch of salt.
  3. Pour over the pecans and bake at 170C until just set with a slight wobble.
  4. Cool until warm and serve in wedges with the ice cream.

By the sea margaritasVegetarian

Tequila, lime and orange liqueur with a salt rim, margaritas by the sea

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the limes up to 4 hours ahead and chill; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml blanco tequila
  • 90ml triple sec
  • 120ml fresh lime juice
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 2 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Run a lime wedge round 4 glasses and dip the rims in salt.
  2. Shake the tequila, triple sec, lime juice and syrup hard with ice.
  3. Strain over fresh ice and serve.

When to serve

Pour the margaritas when Louise says they'll be drinking them by the sea.
