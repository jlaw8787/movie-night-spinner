Sunset Strip shrimp cocktail

Shellfish on iceberg, cucumber and avocado with red horseradish cocktail sauce, lemon and saltines.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the cocktail sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; build the glasses just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked king prawns, peeled
  • 1 iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
  • 1/2 telegraph cucumber, diced
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 150ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot chilli sauce
  • 1 lemon, half juiced, half in wedges
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 packet saltine crackers
  1. Stir the tomato sauce, horseradish, Worcestershire sauce, chilli sauce and lemon juice together for the cocktail sauce.
  2. Split the lettuce, cucumber and avocado between 4 coupes or wide glasses.
  3. Fold the prawns through half the sauce, saving 4 to hook on the rims.
  4. Pile the prawns into the glasses, spoon over the rest of the sauce, 3 min.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges and saltine crackers.

Troubadour cheese and pineapple hedgehogVegetarian

Sharp cheddar, pineapple and pickled onions on cocktail sticks in a half grapefruit, with crackers.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Cube the cheese and drain the pineapple up to 1 day ahead; spike the hedgehog within an hour of serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 grapefruit
  • 300g sharp cheddar, in 2cm cubes
  • 440g tin pineapple chunks, drained
  • 12 pickled onions
  • 12 cherry tomatoes
  • 8 cocktail cherries
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 1 packet cocktail sticks
  1. Halve the grapefruit, wrap the cut side of one half in foil and sit it cut side down on a plate.
  2. Thread cocktail sticks with cheddar and pineapple, cheddar and pickled onion, and some with a cherry tomato or cocktail cherry on top.
  3. Spike them all over the grapefruit and serve with the crackers.

Mains

Hollywood party beef stroganoff

Seared beef sirloin strips with mushrooms, onion, paprika, mustard and sour cream.

Prep 4 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Best made on the night; slice the beef and mushrooms up to 1 day ahead and keep them covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 700g beef rump, in thin strips
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 40g butter
  • 1 brown onion, thinly sliced
  • 300g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g wide egg noodles
  • 1/2 bunch flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Toss the beef with the flour, paprika and a little salt and pepper.
  2. Sear the beef in batches in 2 tbsp of the oil over very high heat, 1 minute a side, and lift out.
  3. Melt the butter with the rest of the oil and cook the onion and mushrooms until browned, 8 min.
  4. Add the garlic, tomato paste and mustard for a minute, pour in the stock and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Return the beef and its juices, take off the boil and stir in the sour cream. Season with salt and pepper, 3 min.
  6. Cook the noodles while the sauce simmers, drain and serve the stroganoff over them with parsley, 10 min.

Bernie Taupin's chicken Kiev

Breaded chicken filled with garlic parsley butter, with peas and mashed potatoes.

Prep 2 min, cook 1 hr 13 min · Make ahead: Fill and crumb the Kievs up to 1 day ahead and chill; this keeps the butter in
Recipe for 4
  • 4 small chicken breasts
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Mash the butter with the crushed garlic and chopped parsley, roll into a log in baking paper and freeze for 20 minutes.
  2. Cut a deep pocket in each breast, push in a slice of the butter and press closed. Dip in flour, egg and panko, twice.
  3. Brown in the oil for 2 minutes a side, then bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  4. Boil the peeled potatoes for 20 minutes and mash with milk and salt. Cook the peas for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve the Kievs with the mash and peas, butter running out.

Opening night strawberry sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla and strawberry ice cream, berries, raspberry sauce, whipped cream, nuts and a cherry.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the raspberry sauce up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; build the glasses just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen raspberries
  • 60g icing sugar
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries, hulled and halved
  • 125g blueberries
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml strawberry ice cream
  • 40g roasted peanuts, chopped
  • 4 wafer biscuits
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Simmer the raspberries with the icing sugar and a squeeze of lemon, push through a sieve and cool, 15 min.
  2. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  3. Layer raspberry sauce, strawberries, blueberries and the vanilla ice cream in 4 tall glasses, then more fruit and sauce and the strawberry ice cream.
  4. Top with cream, a drizzle of sauce, the peanuts, a wafer and a cherry.

Drinks

Opening night tequila sunrise

Tequila and fresh orange juice over ice, grenadine sunk to the bottom in a sunrise.

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the juice up to 1 day ahead and keep it cold; pour the grenadine to order so the colours stay separate
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml silver tequila
  • 6 oranges, 5 juiced and 1 in wheels
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 80ml grenadine
  • 2 cups ice
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Squeeze the oranges and lime and stir the juices together.
  2. Fill 4 tall glasses with ice, pour in the tequila and top with the juice.
  3. Pour the grenadine slowly down the inside of each glass so it sinks and rises up in a red to orange sunrise.
  4. Garnish with an orange wheel and a cocktail cherry.
