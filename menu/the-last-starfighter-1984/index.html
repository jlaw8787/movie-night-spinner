Snacks

High score loaded hot dogs

Chilli, cheese and onion hot dogs.

Prep 4 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 200g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  1. Brown the mince with the spices, add the tomatoes and simmer until thick, 15 min.
  2. Grill the franks until blistered and toast the rolls, 8 min.
  3. Fill, spoon over the chilli and top with cheese and onion.

Tumbleweed onion stringsVegetarian

A tangle of crisp onion with ranch dip.

Prep 8 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the onions and soak them in the buttermilk up to 4 hours ahead; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 large brown onions, very thinly sliced
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo, chopped
  1. Soak the onion in the buttermilk for 10 minutes.
  2. Toss handfuls in the flour mixed with paprika and salt and fry at 180°C until golden, 10 min.
  3. Stir the chipotle through the mayo and serve with the hot onions piled high.

Death Blossom pizza

Pepperoni pizza cut into petals, opened like the Death Blossom.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the dough the day before and let it rise slowly in the fridge; top and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 250g balls of pizza dough
  • 200ml tomato passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • 120g sliced pork pepperoni
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Stretch the dough into 2 rounds on oiled trays.
  2. Spread with passata, sprinkle with oregano and top with mozzarella, pepperoni and capsicum.
  3. Bake at 250°C until blistered, then cut each into 8 long petal slices from the centre out, 15 min.

Meant for Vegas steak sandwich

Seared steak, fried onions and cheese on a roll.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cook the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; sear the steak to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g rump steak
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 8 slices of provolone
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 cup rocket leaves
  1. Cook the onions slowly in the butter until soft and sweet, 15 min.
  2. Season and sear the steak 3 minutes a side, rest, then slice thinly.
  3. Split and toast the rolls, spread with mustard and pile on steak, onions, cheese and rocket, 5 min.

Green moon of Galan key lime pieVegetarian

Sharp lime custard in a crumb crust with whipped cream.

Prep 18 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to a day ahead and chill; top with cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 395g tin sweetened condensed milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 5 limes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 22cm pie tin.
  2. Whisk the condensed milk, yolks, lime zest and 125ml lime juice until thick.
  3. Pour into the base and bake at 160°C until just set, then chill, 15 min.
  4. Whip the cream and spoon on top with more zest.
