Room-temperature sardines on rye

Sardine toasts with lemon butter and pickled shallot, served at room temperature.

Prep 20 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Pickle the shallots up to 3 days ahead; toast and build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 110g tins of sardines in olive oil
  • 8 slices of dark rye bread
  • 50g butter, softened
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 60ml white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 1/2 cup dill sprigs
  1. Warm the vinegar with the sugar and a pinch of salt, pour over the shallots and leave to pickle.
  2. Mash the butter with the lemon zest and a pinch of pepper.
  3. Toast the rye and spread with the lemon butter.
  4. Lay the sardines on top, squeeze over a little lemon and finish with shallots and dill.

Femur-cut roast bone marrow

Roast marrow bones scooped onto toast with caper salad.

Prep 9 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Soak the bones in salted water overnight; roast to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 beef marrow bones, cut lengthways
  • 1 cup flat-leaf parsley leaves
  • 1 shallot, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 sourdough baguette, sliced
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Pat the bones dry, season and stand them cut side up on a tray.
  2. Roast at 220°C until the marrow is soft and just starting to bubble.
  3. Toss the parsley, shallot and capers with the oil and lemon juice.
  4. Toast the bread and serve with the bones, the salad and salt for scooping and spreading.

Art's fridge-raid devilled eggs

Paprika yolks, crisp bacon, chives. Art eats everything in Ray's fridge.

Prep 12 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill the eggs up to a day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; add the bacon to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 3 rashers of streaky bacon
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water, cook 10 minutes, then cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp, then crumble.
  3. Halve the eggs, mash the yolks with the mayo, mustard and vinegar and season well.
  4. Pipe or spoon back in and finish with paprika, bacon and chives.

Herr Klopek's paprika goulash

Slow-cooked beef goulash, sour cream, noodles.

Prep 28 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Make the goulash up to 2 days ahead; it is better reheated. Cook the noodles to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, cut in chunks
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 red capsicums, sliced
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 400g wide egg noodles
  • 30g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  1. Toss the beef in the flour and brown it hard in the oil in batches.
  2. Soften the onions and garlic in the same pot, then stir in the paprika, caraway and tomato paste for a minute.
  3. Return the beef with the capsicum and stock, cover and simmer gently until the beef falls apart.
  4. Boil the noodles, drain and toss with the butter and parsley.
  5. Serve the goulash over the noodles with a spoon of sour cream.

Pizza dude pepperoni pie

Pepperoni, mozzarella, chilli flakes. Ricky called the pizza dude for the show.

Prep 19 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead; bring the dough to room temperature an hour before baking
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 400g balls of pizza dough
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 150g sliced pepperoni salami
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  1. Mix the tomatoes, garlic, oregano and a pinch of salt for the sauce.
  2. Stretch the dough into 2 large rounds on oiled trays.
  3. Spread with sauce, cover with mozzarella and pepperoni and bake at 250°C until blistered.
  4. Warm the honey with the chilli, drizzle over and finish with parmesan.

Rumsfield's porch browniesVegetarian

There go the brownies. Fudgy walnut brownies, caramel sauce.

Prep 14 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150g butter
  • 250g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 80g walnuts, toasted and chopped
  • 120g caramel sauce
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Melt the chocolate and butter, then beat in the sugar and the eggs one at a time.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa, walnuts and a pinch of salt and spread in a lined 20cm tin.
  3. Swirl spoonfuls of the caramel through the top and bake at 170°C until just set.
  4. Cool, cut into squares and serve with softly whipped cream.

Hans's iced coffee floatsNo alcohol

Iced coffee floats with vanilla ice cream, mind your lap.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the cold brew the night before and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml cold brew coffee
  • 250ml milk
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 1 tsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Stir the cold brew, milk and maple syrup together.
  2. Half fill 4 tall glasses with ice and pour in the coffee.
  3. Float 2 scoops of ice cream in each, top with whipped cream and dust with cocoa.
