Pastuzo's marmalade sandwichesVegetarian

Coarse orange marmalade on buttered white bread, his emergency snack

Prep 7 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, wrap well
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 60g soft butter
  • 4 tbsp Seville orange marmalade
  1. Butter the bread right to the edges.
  2. Spread four slices with marmalade and close.
  3. Leave the crusts on, cut into squares and wrap in baking paper. Keep one under your hat for emergencies.

Darkest Peru beef empanadasOn the printed menu

Pastries of beef mince, raisins and olives, coriander lime dip, packed for the boat to London

How it is made
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 40g raisins
  • 50g green olives
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lime
  • 1 jalapeno
  1. Golden baked pastries filled with beef mince, onion, cumin, paprika, raisins and green olives, with a zingy coriander and lime dipping sauce. Fry 400g beef mince with a diced onion until browned. Add 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 40g raisins and 50g chopped green olives and cook 5 minutes, then cool. Cut 12 rounds from 3 sheets shortcrust, fill, fold and crimp with a fork. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes. Blitz a bunch of coriander with 3 tbsp mayo, lime juice and a little jalapeno for dipping.

Mains

The Browns' shepherd's pie

Lamb and carrot pie in Worcestershire gravy under mashed potato

Prep 17 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 750g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Brown the lamb mince, then add the diced onion and carrots.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and stock and simmer until thick.
  3. Boil the potatoes, mash with most of the butter and the milk.
  4. Tip the filling into a baking dish, spread the mash over and rough up the top with a fork.
  5. Bake at 200°C until golden.
  6. Toss the peas with the last of the butter and serve.

Jungle roast chicken and green sauceOn the printed menu

Garlic, cumin and lime marinade, crackling skin, coriander jalapeño dip, before the earthquake

How it is made
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 3 limes
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1.5kg chicken pieces
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 jalapeno
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 20g parmesan
  1. Chicken marinated in garlic, cumin, paprika, lime and soy, roasted until the skin crackles, with a creamy coriander and jalapeno sauce and crispy potatoes. Blitz 4 garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp paprika, the juice of 2 limes, 2 tbsp soy and 2 tbsp oil. Rub over 1.5kg chicken pieces and marinate overnight. Roast at 200°C for 45 minutes. Toss 1kg chunked potatoes in oil and salt and roast alongside for 45 minutes. Blitz a bunch of coriander, a jalapeno, 4 tbsp mayo, 2 tbsp grated parmesan and lime juice for the green sauce.

Antique shop elevenses Dundee cakeVegetarian

Fruit cake with orange zest, topped with whole almonds

Prep 25 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 200g plain flour
  • 300g mixed dried fruit
  • 1 orange
  • 50g whole almonds
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until light, then add the eggs one at a time.
  2. Fold in the flour, dried fruit and orange zest.
  3. Spread in a lined 20cm round tin and arrange the almonds in circles on top.
  4. Bake at 150°C until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool in the tin and cut into small slices.

Marmalade bread and butter puddingVegetarian

Marmalade bread baked in a vanilla custard until golden

Prep 13 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Layer the bread and pour on the custard up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; bake on the day and serve fresh from the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 50g butter
  • 4 tbsp Seville orange marmalade
  • 4 eggs
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 60g caster sugar
  1. Butter the bread and spread with marmalade, then layer in a dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream and sugar and pour over.
  3. Rest so the bread soaks it up.
  4. Bake at 170°C until set and golden.

Drinks

Elevenses cocoa with orange and creamVegetarianNo alcohol

Hot cocoa with orange zest, served in china cups with cream

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the cocoa up to 2 days ahead and chill it; reheat gently, whip the cream and top to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1l milk
  • 1 orange
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the cocoa and sugar with a splash of milk into a smooth paste.
  2. Add the rest of the milk, orange zest and a pinch of salt and heat, whisking, until steaming but not boiling.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Strain into four cups and top with cream and a sprinkle of extra zest.

When to serve

Serve the sandwiches when Paddington takes one out from under his hat.
