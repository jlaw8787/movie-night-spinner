Snacks

Beef bitterballen

Crisp fried balls of shredded beef in a thick creamy filling.

Prep 3 min, cook 4 hours, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Fill the day before, chill overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef chuck
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 50g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 egg
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Braise the beef with the onion and thyme until it falls apart, then shred.
  2. Cook the butter with the flour, add the stock and the beef.
  3. Chill overnight.
  4. Roll into balls and crumb in egg and panko.
  5. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C.
  6. Serve with grainy mustard.

Lake Como antipasto

Bresaola, burrata, chargrilled zucchini, figs, rocket and olives with breadsticks.

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g bresaola
  • 2 burrata
  • 4 figs
  • 2 zucchini, sliced lengthways
  • 60g rocket
  • 1 packet breadsticks
  • 1 jar olives
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  1. Brush the zucchini with a little of the oil, salt it and chargrill until striped and tender.
  2. Lay the bresaola on a board with the burrata, halved figs, breadsticks and olives.
  3. Drizzle with good olive oil and a squeeze of lemon.

Risotto alla milanese

Saffron risotto finished with butter and parmesan.

Prep 26 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Dice the onion and grate the parmesan up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; the risotto itself is best made and served straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion
  • 70g butter
  • 350g carnaroli rice
  • 125ml white wine
  • 1.2 L chicken stock
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 60g parmesan
  1. Soften the diced onion in some of the butter.
  2. Add the rice and toast, then add the wine.
  3. Add the hot stock ladle by ladle, with the saffron steeped in, stirring.
  4. Finish with the rest of the butter and the parmesan.

Rome pork saltimbocca with sage butter

Thin escalopes with prosciutto, seared crisp, white wine and lemon, over garlicky crushed potatoes.

How it is made
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 8 pork escalopes
  • 8 slices prosciutto
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml white wine
  • 1 lemon
  1. Pork escalopes pounded thin, pressed with prosciutto and sage, seared crisp in butter and finished with white wine and lemon, over garlicky crushed potatoes. Boil 800g small potatoes for 15 minutes, crush with olive oil, garlic and parsley. Pound 8 pork escalopes thin, lay a slice of prosciutto and a sage leaf on each and secure with a toothpick. Dust the underside with flour. Fry prosciutto side down in 40g butter for 2 minutes, flip for 1 minute. Add 100ml white wine and a squeeze of lemon, bubble, swirl in 20g butter and spoon over.

Espresso tiramisuVegetarian

Espresso soaked sponge fingers layered with mascarpone cream.

Prep 30 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500g mascarpone
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 24 sponge finger biscuits
  • 300ml strong brewed espresso coffee
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  1. Brew the coffee and cool.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale, fold in the mascarpone and whipped cream.
  3. Dip the sponge fingers and layer with the cream in a dish.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Dust with cocoa.

Amsterdam stroopwafel affogato

Stroopwafels and ice cream under hot espresso, canalside.

How it is made
  • 1 packet ground espresso coffee
  • 1 packet stroopwafels
  • 1 tub vanilla ice cream
  • 1 tin caramel
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  1. Caramel stroopwafels warmed over hot espresso until soft and chewy, served with vanilla ice cream drowned in espresso and a drizzle of salted caramel. Brew 4 hot espressos. Rest a stroopwafel on top of each cup for 2 minutes so the caramel softens. Put a scoop of vanilla ice cream in four small glasses. Warm 3 tbsp thick caramel with a pinch of sea salt. Pour an espresso over each scoop, drizzle with caramel and serve with two warm stroopwafels each.

Orange spritz

Bitter orange aperitivo, prosecco and soda over ice with an orange slice.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Slice the orange and chill the aperitivo and prosecco up to 1 day ahead; build in glasses over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml orange aperitivo
  • 750ml prosecco
  • 200ml soda water
  • 1 orange
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four big wine glasses with ice.
  2. Add the aperitivo, prosecco and a splash of soda water.
  3. Add an orange slice.
