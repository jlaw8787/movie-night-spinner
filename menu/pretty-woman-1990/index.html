Snacks

Strawberries and champagneVegetarian

Chilled ripe strawberries with sugar for dipping, cold sparkling wine alongside

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  1. Wash and properly dry the ripest strawberries and chill.
  2. Pile them into a glass bowl with a tiny dish of caster sugar for dipping.
  3. Open a very cold bottle of sparkling wine alongside.

Garlic butter escargot or mushrooms

Garlic, shallot and brandy butter, snails or mushrooms. Slippery little suckers.

Prep 13 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 20 escargot
  • 100g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 shallot
  • 2 tbsp brandy
  • 1 baguette
  1. Mash the butter with crushed garlic, chopped parsley, finely chopped shallot and a splash of brandy.
  2. Put the escargot in a baking dish and cover each one with the butter.
  3. Bake at 200C until sizzling. Serve with baguette.

Mains

Business dinner eye fillet and red wine jus

Seared eye fillet in red wine jus, with creamy potato gratin

Prep 15 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Bake the potato gratin up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and reheat; sear and roast the fillet and finish the jus on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 800g eye fillet
  • 2 shallots
  • 250ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 40g butter
  • 300g green beans
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the potatoes thin and prepare the shallots and beans.
  2. Layer the potatoes with the cream, crushed garlic and salt, and bake at 160C.
  3. Sear the eye fillet on all sides, then roast at 200C to 54C inside.
  4. Rest the beef. Chop the shallots and soften in the pan.
  5. Add the wine and reduce by half, then add the stock and reduce until glossy. Whisk in some of the butter.
  6. Steam the beans and toss with the remaining butter.

Sweets

Chocolate-dipped strawberriesVegetarian

Strawberries dipped in dark chocolate with a white chocolate drizzle

Prep 15 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 50g white chocolate
  1. Wash and completely dry the strawberries.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate, dip each strawberry and set on baking paper.
  3. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle thin lines across them. Chill until set.

Drinks

Penthouse champagne cocktailVegetarian

Champagne over a bitters soaked sugar cube with a splash of brandy

Prep 6 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Chill the bottle in the fridge up to 1 day ahead, or in an ice bucket for 30 minutes; build the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml champagne
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 8 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 60ml brandy
  • 1 orange
  1. Chill the bottle in an ice bucket.
  2. Drop a sugar cube into each of four flutes and soak with bitters.
  3. Add the brandy, then slowly top with champagne.
  4. Twist a strip of orange peel over each glass and drop it in.

When to serve

Serve the strawberries and bubbles when Edward orders them up to the penthouse.
