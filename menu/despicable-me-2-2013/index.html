Snacks

Salsa and Salsa nachosVegetarian

Corn chips, beans, cheddar, salsa and avocado. Guac from a chip hat optional

Prep 18 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheddar and slice the jalapenos up to 1 day ahead; assemble and bake on the night and smash the avocado at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bags corn chips
  • 1 can black beans
  • 250g cheddar
  • 1 jar jalapenos
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Drain the beans, grate the cheddar and slice the jalapenos. Smash the avocado.
  2. Layer the chips on a tray with beans, cheese and jalapenos.
  3. Bake at 200C until melted.
  4. Top with salsa, avocado and sour cream.

Mains

Eduardo's chicken enchiladas

Shredded chicken enchiladas. Not Pollito. He had a rough night

Prep 32 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli sauce and cook and shred the chicken up to 2 days ahead, chilled; roll, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 200g cheddar
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Toast the chillies in a dry pan, soak in hot water, then blend with the tomatoes.
  2. Simmer the chicken in the sauce, then shred it.
  3. Roll the chicken in tortillas and place in a dish.
  4. Cover with more sauce and grated cheddar.
  5. Bake at 190C.
  6. Serve with sour cream.

Sweets

Gru's jelly tartsVegetarian

Berry jam tarts from Gru's jams and jellies line

Prep 17 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 300g mixed berries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  1. Simmer the berries with sugar and a squeeze of lemon until jammy, then cool.
  2. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a patty tin.
  3. Fill with the jam.
  4. Bake at 190C until the pastry is golden.

Evil purple minion jelly cups

Grape jelly, cream and banana. PX-41 turned the minions purple

Prep 16 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Make and set the jelly in the cups up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; whip the cream and add the banana on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets grape jelly crystals
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 bananas
  1. Make up the jelly as the packet says and pour into clear cups.
  2. Chill until set.
  3. Whip the cream and swirl on top.
  4. Add two banana slices to each like goggles.

Drinks

Minion banana caramel smoothieVegetarianNo alcohol

Frozen banana, vanilla yoghurt, milk and dates, blended thick

Prep 12 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Slice and freeze the bananas up to 1 week ahead; blend with the milk, yoghurt and dates to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200g vanilla yoghurt
  • 4 medjool dates
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 4 tbsp caramel sauce
  1. Peel, slice and freeze the bananas.
  2. Freeze.
  3. Blend the bananas with milk, yoghurt, pitted dates and cinnamon until thick.
  4. Drizzle caramel inside four glasses, pour in the smoothie and top with a banana slice.
