Snacks

Salsa and Salsa nachosVegetarian

Corn chips, beans, cheddar, salsa and avocado. Guac from a chip hat optional.

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheddar and slice the jalapenos up to 1 day ahead; assemble and bake on the night and smash the avocado at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bags corn chips
  • 1 can black beans
  • 250g cheddar
  • 1 jar jalapenos
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 jar tomato salsa
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Drain the beans, grate the cheddar and slice the jalapenos. Smash the avocado.
  2. Layer the chips on a tray with beans, cheese and jalapenos.
  3. Bake at 200°C until melted.
  4. Top with salsa, avocado and sour cream.

Agnes's fairy princess star fairy breadOn the printed menu

White bread buttered to the edges and pressed into hundreds and thousands, for Agnes's party.

How it is made
  • 12 slices soft white bread
  • 100g butter
  • 1 jar hundreds and thousands
  1. Soft white bread spread with butter and pressed into hundreds and thousands, cut into stars with a cutter and piled high for the party. Soften 100g butter until very spreadable. Spread it right to the edges of 12 slices soft white bread. Tip 1 jar hundreds and thousands onto a plate and press each slice butter side down into them so the top is fully coated. Cut out stars with a large star cutter, or cut into triangles with the crusts off. Pile onto a platter and serve straight away so the colours don't run.

Mains

Eduardo's chicken enchiladas

Shredded chicken enchiladas. Not Pollito. He had a rough night.

Prep 17 min, cook 65 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli sauce and cook and shred the chicken up to 2 days ahead, chilled; roll, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 200g cheddar
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Toast the chillies in a dry pan, soak in hot water, then blend with the tomatoes.
  2. Simmer the chicken in the sauce, then shred it.
  3. Roll the chicken in tortillas and place in a dish.
  4. Cover with more sauce and grated cheddar.
  5. Bake at 190°C.
  6. Serve with sour cream.

Gru's lab spaghetti and meatballsOn the printed menu

Beef and pork balls simmered in tomato sauce over pasta, for Margo, Edith and Agnes.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 60g parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Spaghetti and beef meatballs simmered 40 minutes in tomato sauce, a big pot for the whole household. Mix 300g beef mince and 300g pork mince with 1 egg, 40g breadcrumbs, 30g grated parmesan, 2 crushed garlic cloves and salt. Roll into 20 meatballs and brown in olive oil. Soften 1 diced onion, add 2 tins crushed tomatoes, a pinch of sugar and a handful of basil and simmer 15 minutes. Add the meatballs, cover and simmer 40 minutes. Toss with 500g cooked spaghetti and serve with plenty of parmesan.

Sweets

Jelly tartsVegetarian

Berry jam tarts from Gru's jams and jellies line.

Prep 17 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 300g mixed berries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  1. Simmer the berries with sugar and a squeeze of lemon until jammy, then cool.
  2. Cut the pastry into rounds and press into a patty tin.
  3. Fill with the jam.
  4. Bake at 190°C until the pastry is golden.

Evil purple minion jelly cups

Grape jelly, cream and banana. PX-41 turned the minions purple.

Prep 16 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and set the jelly in the cups up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; whip the cream and add the banana on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets grape jelly crystals
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 bananas
  1. Make up the jelly as the packet says and pour into clear cups.
  2. Chill until set.
  3. Whip the cream and swirl on top.
  4. Add two banana slices to each like goggles.

Drinks

Minion banana caramel smoothieVegetarianNo alcohol

Frozen banana, vanilla yoghurt, milk and dates, blended thick.

Prep 9 min, cook 3 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Slice and freeze the bananas up to 1 week ahead; blend with the milk, yoghurt and dates to order
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200g vanilla yoghurt
  • 4 medjool dates
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 4 tbsp caramel sauce
  1. Peel, slice and freeze the bananas.
  2. Freeze.
  3. Blend the bananas with milk, yoghurt, pitted dates and cinnamon until thick.
  4. Drizzle caramel inside four glasses, pour in the smoothie and top with a banana slice.
