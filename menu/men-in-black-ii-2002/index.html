Ben's Famous pizza bites

Mini pizza rounds, mozzarella, pepperoni. Two slices, says Serleena

Prep 20 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Cut the dough rounds up to 2 hours ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; top and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 100ml pizza sauce
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 16 slices pepperoni
  • 2 tbsp plain flour, for dusting
  1. Heat the oven to 230°C with a tray inside.
  2. Roll out the dough on a bench dusted with the plain flour and cut into rounds.
  3. Spread each round with sauce, then add mozzarella and a slice of pepperoni.
  4. Bake on the hot tray until the bases are crisp and the cheese bubbles.

Truro post office sausage rolls

Pork and fennel rolls, by Kevin Brown, postmaster

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roll and cut up to a day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; brush with egg and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 40g dried breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line two trays.
  2. Mix the pork, onion, fennel seeds, breadcrumbs, 1 egg and a good pinch of salt and pepper.
  3. Cut each pastry sheet in half. Lay a log of filling along each half, roll up and press the seam down underneath.
  4. Cut each roll into 4, brush with the other egg, beaten, and bake for 25 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve hot with tomato sauce.

Ben's Famous pizza

Thin crust, sausage. Ask Ben about the Light of Zartha

Prep 15 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Keep the dough in the fridge up to 2 days ahead and crumble the sausage meat up to 1 day ahead; stretch, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough, cold-fermented
  • 200g crushed tomato
  • 250g low-moisture mozzarella
  • 3 Italian sausages
  • salt, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to its highest setting with a steel or tray inside.
  2. Stretch the dough thin into two bases and spread with the crushed tomato, seasoned with salt.
  3. Top with mozzarella and crumbled sausage meat, skins removed.
  4. Bake each pizza at max heat until the crust blisters.

Pug cookiesVegetarian

Frank sings I Will Survive in the car. Brown butter rounds

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and store airtight; pipe the pug faces on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Brown the butter in a pan and leave to cool.
  2. Beat the cooled butter with the sugar and egg, then mix in the flour and bicarbonate of soda.
  3. Roll into balls and place on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 180°C until set.
  5. Cool the cookies on a rack.
  6. Melt the chocolate and pipe pug faces on each cookie.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the icing up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered; dip the doughnuts on the day so the glaze stays fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp strong espresso
  • 10g butter, melted
  • 8 plain cake doughnuts
  1. Mix the icing sugar, espresso and melted butter until smooth.
  2. Dip the top of each doughnut in the icing.
  3. Leave to set.

Worm guys' iced espresso shakesVegetarianNo alcohol

Espresso, vanilla ice cream, milk and caramel, topped with cream and gummy worms

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Brew and cool the espresso up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; blend and whip the cream to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 espressos (about 120 ml)
  • 6 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 300ml cold milk
  • 6 tbsp caramel sauce
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 8 gummy worms
  1. Brew the espressos and cool.
  2. Blend with the ice cream, milk and some of the caramel until thick and smooth.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Pour into tall glasses, top with cream, drizzle with caramel and hang gummy worms over each rim.
