Snacks

Mei's dad's dumplings

Pork mince, wombok, ginger and spring onion, pan fried.

Prep 47 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat the dumplings up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, or freeze on a tray up to 1 month; fry and steam on the night, adding a couple of minutes from frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 300g wombok, shredded
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100ml water
  • 3 tbsp black vinegar
  1. Mix the pork with the wombok, ginger, spring onion and salt.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat.
  3. Fry in oil until crisp underneath.
  4. Add a splash of water, cover and steam.
  5. Serve with black vinegar.

Temple red bean bunsVegetarian

Steamed bao buns filled with sweet red bean paste.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill the buns up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; steam just before serving, adding a minute
Recipe for 4
  • 12 frozen bao buns
  • 150g sweet red bean paste
  1. Gently open each bao bun and spoon in some red bean paste. Pinch the edges closed.
  2. Steam in a lined basket until soft and puffy.
  3. Serve warm in the basket.

4*Town salt and pepper popcorn chicken

Thigh bites in soy and five spice, sweet potato flour crust, fried crisp with basil.

How it is made
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 jar five spice
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 100g sweet potato flour
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 1 jar white pepper
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  1. Bite-size chicken thigh marinated in soy and five spice, coated in sweet potato flour, fried crisp and tossed with fried basil and chilli salt. Cut 600g chicken thigh into bite-size pieces and marinate 30 minutes with 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp five spice, 2 grated garlic cloves and 1 tsp sugar. Toss in 100g sweet potato flour or cornflour. Fry at 180°C for 4 minutes until crisp, then drain. Fry a handful of basil leaves for 10 seconds. Mix 1 tsp salt, half a teaspoon white pepper and half a teaspoon chilli powder and toss through the chicken and basil. Serve in paper bags.

Mains

Lee family soy chicken

Chicken thighs braised in soy, ginger and star anise, over jasmine rice.

Prep 18 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 125ml soy sauce
  • 250ml water
  • 1 piece ginger (3cm), sliced
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 2 bunches bok choy
  • 2 garlic cloves
  1. Slice the ginger.
  2. Brown the chicken in the oil.
  3. Add the soy sauce, water, ginger, star anise and sugar.
  4. Braise until glossy and tender.
  5. Cook the jasmine rice.
  6. Stir-fry the bok choy with garlic.

Toronto beef and broccoli noodles

Rump seared in a smoking wok, garlic greens, oyster sauce, egg noodles.

How it is made
  • 500g rump steak
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 box cornflour
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 bottle oyster sauce
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Beef strips seared hot in a wok, broccoli and garlic tossed through, finished with oyster sauce and served over egg noodles. Slice 500g rump steak thinly and toss with 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp cornflour and 1 tsp oil. Blanch 1 large head broccoli in florets for 1 minute and drain. Loosen 400g fresh egg noodles in hot water. Sear the beef in a smoking wok in 2 batches for 1 minute each and set aside. Stir-fry 3 garlic cloves and 1 tbsp ginger for 30 seconds, add the broccoli, beef, 3 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp soy and 60ml water and toss. Serve over the noodles.

Sweets

Red moon ritual mango pudding

Mango puree set with gelatine and evaporated milk.

Prep 25 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 mangoes
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 4 gelatine sheets
  • 250ml evaporated milk
  1. Blend 2 mangoes into a puree.
  2. Warm the puree with the sugar.
  3. Soak the gelatine sheets until soft.
  4. Squeeze the gelatine and stir into the warm puree until dissolved.
  5. Stir in the evaporated milk.
  6. Pour into glasses.
  7. Chill until set.
  8. Dice the remaining mango and use to top the puddings.

Drinks

4*Town concert bubble teaNo alcohol

Black tea over ice with tapioca pearls soaked in brown sugar syrup.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Brew and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead; cook the pearls and soak them in the syrup up to 4 hours ahead at room temperature, never in the fridge. Assemble to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 100g tapioca pearls
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Cook the tapioca pearls as the packet says.
  2. Soak the pearls in a syrup of brown sugar and water.
  3. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water, then remove.
  4. Cool the tea.
  5. Spoon pearls into glasses, add ice, pour over the tea and top with milk. Serve with wide straws.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the Lee family sits down to dinner.
