Snacks

Cream of tomato soup with cheese toastsVegetarian

Cream of tomato soup with cheese toasts. Annie's rant spills the soup.

Prep 12 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead and reheat; grill the toasts to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 800g tinned whole tomatoes
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 125ml pouring cream
  • 8 slices baguette
  • 100g vintage cheddar, grated
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the butter.
  2. Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar and simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and season.
  4. Pile the cheddar on the baguette slices and grill until bubbling, then float them on the soup.

Dirty birdie barbecue wings

Crisp paprika barbecue wings. Annie calls Paul a dirty bird.

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Season the wings up to a day ahead and chill; bake and glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings, jointed
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 125ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220C, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Toss with the warmed barbecue sauce and honey.
  4. Mix the sour cream, mayo, chives and garlic into a ranch dip and serve.

Mains

Snowed-in meatloaf with buttery mash

Paul's pills go into Annie's wine

Prep 8 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Shape the meatloaf up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 egg
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 60ml milk, plus 125ml for the mash
  • 2 tsp thyme leaves
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled
  • 50g butter
  • 200g green beans
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in 60ml milk, then mix with the beef, onion, garlic, egg, thyme and salt and shape into a loaf in a lined tin.
  2. Stir the tomato sauce, sugar and vinegar and spread half over the loaf.
  3. Bake at 180C, glazing again halfway, until cooked through.
  4. Meanwhile boil and mash the potatoes with the butter and milk and steam the beans.
  5. Rest the loaf for 10 minutes, slice thickly and serve with the mash and beans.

Silver Creek beef pot roast

Beef pot roast with carrots and potatoes. Annie's sow is called Misery.

Prep 30 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Cook the pot roast up to 2 days ahead and chill in its gravy; reheat gently to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions, cut in wedges
  • 4 carrots, in chunks
  • 600g small potatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 3 thyme sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  1. Season the beef and brown it well all over in the oil, then lift out.
  2. Soften the onions and garlic, stir in the tomato paste and flour, then the stock.
  3. Return the beef with the thyme and bay, cover and cook at 160C for 2 and a half hours.
  4. Add the carrots and potatoes and cook until everything is tender.
  5. Slice or pull the beef and serve with the vegetables and gravy.

Due south self-saucing chocolate puddingVegetarian

Chocolate self-saucing pudding. The penguin always faces due south.

Prep 16 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter and the sugar topping up to 4 hours ahead; pour the water over and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 30g cocoa powder, plus 2 tbsp for the topping
  • 185ml milk
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 375ml boiling water
  • 300ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Whisk the flour, caster sugar and 30g cocoa, then whisk in the milk, butter, egg and vanilla and spread in a buttered dish.
  2. Mix the brown sugar with the rest of the cocoa and scatter it over the batter.
  3. Pour the boiling water gently over the back of a spoon.
  4. Bake at 180C until the top is firm and the sauce bubbles underneath.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve with ice cream.

Finished manuscript champagneVegetarian

Chilled Champagne with a twist of lemon. Paul's ritual when a book is done.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the champagne and the glasses well
Recipe for 4
  • 1 x 750ml bottle brut champagne
  • 1 lemon, for peel
  1. Twist a strip of lemon peel over each chilled flute and drop it in.
  2. Pour the champagne slowly and serve straight away.

When to serve

Pour the champagne when Paul finishes the book and sends Annie for a glass.
