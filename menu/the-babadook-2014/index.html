Mister Babadook black pepper popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in butter, black pepper and grated parmesan.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Best popped and tossed just before serving; grate the parmesan up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a lidded pot, shaking.
  2. Toss straight away with the butter, pepper, parmesan and salt so it all sticks while the corn is hot.

Samuel's lunch box fairy breadVegetarian

Buttered white bread coated in sprinkles, crusts off, cut into triangles.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best made within an hour of serving so the bread stays soft; soften the butter ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 1/2 cup hundreds and thousands
  1. Spread the bread with butter right to the edges.
  2. Press face down into a plate of hundreds and thousands.
  3. Trim the crusts and cut into triangles. Make it close to serving so it stays soft.

Pop-up book crackers and olive tapenade

Crisp flatbread draped like pages, black olive and anchovy spread, whipped feta, ba-ba-dook.

How it is made
  • 2 lavash sheets
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 200g pitted kalamata olives
  • 1 tin anchovies
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  1. Lavash crackers baked folded like an open book, with a jet black olive and anchovy tapenade and a whipped feta for the white pages. Cut 2 lavash sheets into 16 rectangles, score each down the middle, brush with olive oil and salt and drape over a rolling pin so they bake half open. Bake at 180°C for 8 minutes. Blitz 200g pitted kalamata olives, 4 anchovies, 1 garlic clove, 1 tbsp capers, lemon juice and 60ml olive oil to a paste. Whip 150g feta with 100g Greek yoghurt. Serve both with the crackers.

Amelia's slow-cooked stew

Lamb shanks braised with red wine, tomato and rosemary.

Prep 20 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lamb shanks
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 250ml red wine
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 rosemary sprigs
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the shanks in oil, 12 min.
  2. Soften the onion and carrots.
  3. Add the wine, tomatoes and rosemary, return the shanks, cover and braise at 160°C, 3 hr.
  4. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with butter, 20 min.

Midnight cheese toasties

Sourdough toasties with vintage cheddar and ham.

How it is made
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 60g butter
  • 250g vintage cheddar
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 150g shaved leg ham
  • 30g parmesan
  • 150g tomato chutney
  1. Sourdough toasted in butter with vintage cheddar, a smear of Dijon and shaved leg ham, the outside crusted with parmesan, with tomato chutney for dipping. Butter one side of 8 slices sourdough. Mix 250g grated vintage cheddar with 2 tbsp mayonnaise. Lay 4 slices buttered side down, spread with Dijon, top with the cheese mix and 150g shaved leg ham, and close. Sprinkle 30g finely grated parmesan onto the buttered outsides. Cook in a pan over medium-low heat for 4 minutes a side, pressing down, until deep golden. Rest 1 minute, cut and serve with 150g tomato chutney.

Sweets

Samuel's birthday jelly slice

Biscuit base, lemon condensed milk layer and raspberry jelly on top.

Prep 25 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 125g butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp powdered gelatine
  • 60ml hot water
  • 395g sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 1 packet raspberry jelly crystals
  1. Press the crushed biscuits and butter into a lined tin.
  2. Dissolve the gelatine in the hot water, mix with the condensed milk and lemon juice and spread over.
  3. Chill until set, 1 hr.
  4. Make the jelly as the packet says, cool completely and pour over, 9 min.
  5. Chill until firm, 3 hr.

Drinks

Honey brandy nightcap

Warm milk with honey, cinnamon and brandy, nutmeg on top.

Prep 9 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Warm and infuse the milk up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; reheat, whisk and add the brandy on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 80ml brandy
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg, freshly grated
  1. Warm the milk with the honey, cinnamon and vanilla until steaming without boiling, 5 min.
  2. Infuse and remove the cinnamon, 5 min.
  3. Whisk until frothy and pour into four mugs.
  4. Add brandy to each mug and finish with nutmeg. Leave the brandy out for the kids.
