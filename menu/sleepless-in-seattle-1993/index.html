Snacks

Northwest salmon spread

Hot-smoked fish worked into cream cheese and crème fraîche with dill, capers, lemon and horseradish. Toasted bagel chips. Flown in overnight from Seattle.

Prep 18 min, cook 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 200g hot-smoked salmon
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 2 bagels
  • 1 tbsp oil
  1. Flake the salmon into the cream cheese and crème fraîche.
  2. Add lemon juice, dill, capers and horseradish and mix.
  3. Slice the bagels thin and brush with oil.
  4. Bake at 180°C into crisp chips, 10 min.
  5. Serve the chips with the dip.

Late show popcornVegetarian

Popped in coconut oil, tossed with browned butter and sea salt.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt
  1. Cook the butter until nutty and golden, 5 min.
  2. Heat the coconut oil with 3 kernels in a lidded pot, 3 min.
  3. Add the rest of the kernels once they pop, cover and shake until popping slows, 4 min.
  4. Toss with the brown butter and flaky salt, 3 min.

Mains

Baked salmon

Fillet roasted under parsley, grated lemon peel and bread crumbs. Steamed green beans, warm butter spooned over. Mild. Nothing to sneeze at.

Prep 5 min, cook 25 min
Recipe for 4
  • 50g dried breadcrumbs
  • 1 handful parsley, chopped
  • 2 lemons, 1 zested, 1 juiced, 1 in wedges
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 300g green beans
  • 60g butter
  1. Mix the breadcrumbs with parsley, lemon zest and oil.
  2. Press onto the salmon and roast at 200°C, 12 min.
  3. Steam the beans, 8 min.
  4. Melt the butter with the lemon juice and spoon over everything, 5 min.

Valentine heartsVegetarian

Browned butter sugar cookies, hand cut, iced red and white.

Prep 18 min, cook 18 min
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs, 1 whole and 1 white
  • 250g plain flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 drop red food colouring
  1. Brown the butter and cool, 12 min.
  2. Beat with the sugar and whole egg, then mix in the flour.
  3. Roll to 5mm and cut into hearts.
  4. Bake at 180°C, 9 min.
  5. Cool completely, 15 min.
  6. Mix the icing sugar with the egg white. Tint half red and ice the cookies.

Iced mochaNo alcohol

Strong coffee with dark chocolate melted through, cold milk over ice, whipped cream and cocoa. The way they take it in Seattle.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml strong brewed coffee
  • 80g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the hot coffee with the chocolate and sugar until melted.
  2. Cool, 15 min.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Fill 4 glasses with ice and add the mocha and milk.
  5. Top with whipped cream and dust with cocoa.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Jonah calls the late-night radio show on Christmas Eve.
