Snacks

Pass-the-asparagus tempura spearsVegetarian

Lester wants someone to pass the asparagus

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Trim the asparagus and mix the mayo up to a day ahead; batter and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches asparagus, trimmed
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 180ml ice-cold soda water
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  1. Stir the mayonnaise with the lemon zest and a squeeze of juice.
  2. Whisk the flour, cornflour, a pinch of salt and the soda water into a lumpy batter.
  3. Dip the spears and fry in oil at 180°C in batches until pale gold and crisp.
  4. Salt and serve straight away with the lemon mayo.

Smiley sauce onion ringsVegetarian

Onion rings with a tangy Smiley sauce. Lester offers Carolyn Smiley sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead; batter and fry the rings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large brown onions, cut in thick rings
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp sriracha
  • 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, sriracha and relish into Smiley sauce.
  2. Whisk the flour, paprika, salt and lager into a smooth batter.
  3. Dust the rings in a little flour, dip in batter and fry at 180°C in batches until golden.
  4. Drain, salt and serve with the sauce.

Mr. Smiley's fry cook cheeseburgers

Lester quits and becomes a fry cook

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties and make the sauce up to a day ahead; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 sesame burger buns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1kg frozen crinkle-cut chips
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Shape the mince into 4 thin patties and season well.
  3. Griddle the patties in a very hot pan, flip, add the cheese and cover to melt.
  4. Toast the buns, spread with mayo and sauce and build with lettuce, tomato, pickles and the patties.

Real estate King popcorn prawn baskets

Popcorn prawns with slaw and horseradish sauce. The real estate King orders at the window.

Prep 8 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce and slaw up to a day ahead; crumb and fry the prawns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g raw peeled prawns
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp Old Bay seasoning
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 1/4 white cabbage, shredded
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  1. Stir the tomato sauce and horseradish with a squeeze of lemon, and toss the cabbage with the mayo.
  2. Coat the prawns in seasoned flour, egg, then panko mixed with the Old Bay.
  3. Fry at 180°C in batches until crisp and pink inside.
  4. Pile into paper-lined baskets with the slaw, sauce and lemon.

Rose and raspberry soft-serve sundaesVegetarian

Raspberry and rose soft-serve sundaes. Carolyn snips the roses off her bushes.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead and chill; scoop to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g frozen raspberries
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp rosewater
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp dried edible rose petals
  • 30g pistachios, chopped
  1. Simmer the raspberries and sugar until jammy, then stir in the rosewater and cool.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into tall cups.
  3. Spoon over the sauce and scatter with rose petals and pistachios.

Firebird cherry thickshakesVegetarianNo alcohol

Cherry ice cream thickshakes blended thick. The car I've always wanted. I rule.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses; blend to order
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml milk
  • 200g frozen pitted cherries
  • 2 tbsp cherry syrup
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk, frozen cherries and syrup until thick.
  2. Pour into chilled glasses and top each with a cherry.
