Snacks

Sal's garlic knotsVegetarian

Tied pizza dough baked hot, tossed in garlic butter, parsley and parmesan, for Mookie's delivery run

Prep 20 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm, same day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 50g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 30g grated parmesan
  1. Cut the dough into 12 strips and tie each into a knot.
  2. Rest the knots on a tray to prove until puffy.
  3. Bake at 230C until golden.
  4. Melt the butter with the garlic and chopped parsley, toss the hot knots through and shower with parmesan.

Chilli-lime watermelon and feta skewersVegetarian

Ice-cold watermelon, salty white cheese and mint, citrus and spiced salt, waaake up

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cube up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg seedless watermelon, chilled
  • 200g firm feta
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 16 skewers
  1. Cut the watermelon into 3cm cubes and the feta into 2cm cubes.
  2. Thread onto the skewers, alternating with small mint leaves.
  3. Mix the salt, chilli flakes and lime zest, squeeze the lime juice over the skewers and sprinkle with the chilli salt. Serve straight from the fridge.

Mains

Sal's Famous slice

Pepperoni and extra cheese, straight from the wall of fame

Prep 20 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Dough can be made a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 250g grated low-moisture mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Rest the dough at room temperature for an hour. Season the tomatoes with salt.
  2. Stretch the bases thin and spread with the sauce. Top with the mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Bake on a preheated tray at the oven's highest setting until bubbling and charred in spots. Sprinkle with oregano and cut big.

Cherry shaved iceVegetarian

Crushed ice soaked in homemade dark fruit syrup with lemon, from the street cart

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g cherries, pitted
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1kg ice
  1. Pit the cherries.
  2. Simmer the cherries with the sugar, water and lemon juice, then strain.
  3. Cool and chill the syrup until cold.
  4. Shave or crush the ice into four cups and pour the syrup over just before serving.

Drinks

Heatwave lager and lemonade shandyVegetarian

Da Mayor and his cold beer on the hottest day of the year

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Lemonade up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles lager
  • 3 lemons
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 300ml cold water
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Juice the lemons and stir the juice with the sugar and cold water until dissolved. Keep a few lemon wheels for the glasses.
  2. Chill 4 tall glasses in the freezer.
  3. Half-fill each glass with ice-cold lager, top with lemonade, then add ice and a lemon wheel. Serve straight away.

When to serve

Turn the fan on and pour the drinks when Mookie heads in to work at Sal's.
