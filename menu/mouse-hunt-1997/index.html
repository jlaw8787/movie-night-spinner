Snacks

Cheese boardVegetarian

String cheese, aged cheddar, gouda and blue with crackers, grapes and nuts.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, cover and keep cool
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sticks of string cheese
  • 150g aged cheddar
  • 150g gouda
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 300g green grapes
  • 50g mixed nuts
  1. Cut the cheddar, gouda and blue cheese into wedges.
  2. Arrange the cheeses and string cheese on a wooden board with the crackers, grapes and a little bowl of nuts.
  3. Leave one small wedge of cheese on its own at the edge as bait.

Spaghetti fritters

Pasta tangles bound with egg, parmesan and parsley, fried into crisp nests, smoky tomato basil dip.

How it is made
  • 200g spaghetti
  • 3 eggs
  • 60g parmesan
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 300ml passata
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Tangles of spaghetti bound with egg, parmesan and parsley, fried into crisp golden nests and served with a smoky tomato and basil dipping sauce. Cook 200g spaghetti, drain and cool. Toss with 3 beaten eggs, 60g grated parmesan, chopped parsley and pepper. Heat 1cm oil in a frying pan and drop in twisted forkfuls, flattening slightly. Fry 2 minutes a side until crisp and golden. Simmer 300ml passata with a garlic clove, half a tsp smoked paprika and basil for 10 minutes to dip.

Ernie's spinach ricotta stuffed chicken

Chicken breast stuffed with spinach and ricotta, mushroom cream sauce, potato gratin.

Prep 15 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Gratin up to 1 day, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 250g ricotta
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 20g butter
  • 600ml thickened cream
  • 1kg potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and mix with the ricotta. Cut a pocket in each chicken breast and fill.
  2. Layer the potatoes in a dish with most of the cream and bake at 180°C, 1 hr.
  3. Sear the chicken in oil, then roast at 200°C alongside for the last 20 minutes.
  4. Cook the mushrooms in butter, add the remaining cream and simmer until thick. Serve over the chicken.

LaRue mansion pork loin with crackling

Pork loin rolled with sage and garlic, roast apples and crackling, for the LaRue mansion auction.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg rolled pork loin
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 5 apples
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  1. Pork loin rolled with sage and garlic, roasted with apples and potatoes until the crackling shatters, with a sharp apple and mustard sauce. Score the skin of a 1.5kg rolled pork loin, rub with salt and dry in the fridge for an hour. Push sage leaves and garlic slivers into the meat. Roast at 240°C for 25 minutes, then at 180°C for 1 hour with 1kg halved potatoes and 3 quartered apples. Simmer 2 more chopped apples with sugar and water until soft, then stir in 1 tsp Dijon. Rest the pork 10 minutes and carve.

LaRue house lemon curd cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked cream cheese cheesecake on a cookie crust, spread with lemon curd.

Prep 22 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 500g cream cheese
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 tbsp lemon curd
  1. Mix the crushed biscuits with the butter and press into a tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar, then the eggs. Pour over the base.
  3. Bake at 160°C for 50 minutes, then cool in the oven. Chill until firm.
  4. Spread with lemon curd before serving.

Drinks

Mouse-sized honey vanilla shakesNo alcohol

Honey vanilla milkshakes in small glasses, with a cheese cracker on each rim.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml cold milk
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ground cinnamon, a pinch
  • 8 round cheese biscuits
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream, honey and cinnamon until thick and frothy.
  2. Pour into eight small glasses. Cut a slit in each biscuit and perch one on each rim.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the brothers set their first mouse trap with cheese.
