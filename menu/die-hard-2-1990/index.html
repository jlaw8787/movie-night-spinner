Snacks

Virginia ham biscuits

Warm buttermilk biscuits with Dijon butter and thin sliced ham.

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Bake the biscuits up to 1 day ahead, airtight, and warm in a 160°C oven; fill with the Dijon butter and ham on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 100g cold butter
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 200g thin-sliced leg ham
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub the cold butter into the flour and salt, 8 min.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk and pat out the dough thick. Cut into rounds.
  3. Bake until risen and golden, 12 min.
  4. Split warm, spread with butter mixed with Dijon and fill with ham, 5 min.

Snowbound loaded potato skins

Crisp potato skins with cheddar and bacon, sour cream and chives.

Prep 22 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Bake the potatoes and cook the bacon up to 1 day ahead, chilled; crisp, fill and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 small potatoes
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g bacon
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 small bunch chives
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C until tender, 45 min.
  2. Cook the bacon until crisp and crumble it, 8 min.
  3. Halve the potatoes, scoop out most of the flesh and brush with butter.
  4. Crisp the skins at 220°C, 10 min.
  5. Fill with cheddar and bacon and bake until melted, 5 min.
  6. Top with sour cream and chives.

Dulles departure lounge buffalo wings

Dried and baked until crackling, tossed in hot sauce and butter, blue cheese dip and celery.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg chicken wings
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 60g butter
  • 120ml hot sauce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 100g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch celery
  1. Wings dried and baked until crackling, tossed in hot sauce melted with butter, served with blue cheese dip and celery sticks. Pat 1.2kg chicken wings dry, toss with 2 tsp baking powder and 1 tsp salt and leave uncovered in the fridge for a few hours. Bake on a rack at 220°C for 45 minutes, turning once, until crisp. Melt 60g butter with 120ml hot sauce and toss the wings through. Mix 100g crumbled blue cheese with 100g sour cream, 2 tbsp mayo and a squeeze of lemon. Serve with 4 celery sticks cut into batons.

General Esperanza's snowbound chilli

Slow braised beef chuck and ground beef with tomatoes and kidney beans, sour cream.

Prep 58 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Better made the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 600g diced beef chuck
  • 400g beef mince
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tins of chopped tomatoes (400g each)
  • 2 tins of kidney beans (400g each)
  • 120g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the chuck and mince in the neutral oil in batches, 15 min.
  2. Add the chopped onion, chilli powder and cumin, then the tomatoes.
  3. Braise gently, covered, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Add the drained kidney beans for the last stretch.
  5. Serve with sour cream and grated cheddar.

Holly's in-flight chicken in white wine

Thighs braised with mushrooms and tarragon cream, buttered rice and green beans.

Prep 10 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Make the chicken up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thigh fillets
  • 30g butter
  • 2 shallots
  • 250g button mushrooms
  • 150ml dry white wine
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 300g green beans
  1. Season the chicken and brown in the butter. Lift out, 10 min.
  2. Fry the sliced shallots and mushrooms for 5 minutes, then add the wine and bubble for 1 minute.
  3. Add the stock, put the chicken back and simmer for 20 minutes. Stir in the cream and chopped tarragon for the last 3 minutes.
  4. Simmer the rice with 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, then rest for 5.
  5. Boil the beans for 4 minutes and serve with the chicken and rice.

Christmas cookiesVegetarian

Ginger and cinnamon cookies, iced white like the snowed-in runways at Dulles.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min, then 75 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 200g icing sugar
  1. Melt the butter, sugar and golden syrup together. Stir in the flour, ground ginger, ground cinnamon and bicarbonate of soda to a dough, 10 min.
  2. Chill the dough, 1 hr.
  3. Roll out and cut into planes and snowflakes.
  4. Bake at 180°C, 10 min.
  5. Cool on a rack, 15 min.
  6. Mix the icing sugar with a little water and ice the cookies.

Drinks

McClane's bourbon eggnog

Eggnog with bourbon and nutmeg, the regular Christmas he wanted.

Prep 6 min, cook 13 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the eggnog, stir in the bourbon and chill up to 2 days ahead; stir well, pour and grate the nutmeg to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 500ml milk
  • 250ml cream
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  • 120ml bourbon
  • fresh nutmeg, to taste
  1. Whisk the yolks and sugar until pale.
  2. Warm the milk, cream and nutmeg until steaming, then slowly whisk into the yolks, 9 min.
  3. Return to the pan and stir over low heat until it coats a spoon, without boiling, 4 min.
  4. Cool, then stir in the bourbon, 20 min.
  5. Chill, 2 hr.
  6. Pour into tumblers and grate fresh nutmeg on top.

When to serve

Serve dinner while Holly's plane circles overhead, running out of fuel.
