Snacks

Hot dogs

Grilled hot dogs with yellow mustard and warm sauerkraut.

Prep 6 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauerkraut and caraway up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled to warm through; grill the franks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 300g sauerkraut
  • 1 pinch caraway seeds
  • 1 jar yellow mustard
  1. Warm the sauerkraut with the caraway seeds.
  2. Grill the franks until they split.
  3. Tuck into the buns with mustard and sauerkraut and wrap in baking paper.

London pub ploughman's

Aged cheddar, ham, pickled onions, chutney and crusty bread.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Cut the cheddar up to 1 day ahead and keep it wrapped in the fridge; slice the bread and lay the board just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200g aged cheddar
  • 150g leg ham
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 100g butter
  1. Cut the cheddar into wedges and slice the bread thickly.
  2. Lay the cheese, ham, onions and chutney on a board with the bread and butter.

Brooklyn soft pretzels with mustard

Soft pretzel knots with rock salt and mustard, for a kid from Brooklyn who can do this all day.

How it is made
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 40g butter
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar rock salt
  • 1 jar English mustard
  • 1 jar American mustard
  1. Pretzel dough shaped into knots, boiled in bicarb water, rock salt on top, baked at 220°C for 12 minutes, with mustard. Mix 500g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp salt, 40g melted butter and 300ml warm water and knead 5 minutes. Rise 1 hour. Divide into 8, roll into long ropes and twist into pretzel knots. Boil 2L water with 60g bicarb and dip each pretzel for 20 seconds. Lay on lined trays, brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with rock salt. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes until deep brown. Serve warm with hot English and American mustard.

Mains

Army mess hall stew

Beef stew in Italy, the night Steve hears Bucky's unit is missing.

Prep 33 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 carrots
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 L beef stock
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Dice the onion, carrots and potatoes.
  2. Brown the beef in batches in oil.
  3. Add the vegetables and stock and simmer until thick and tender.
  4. Ladle into bowls and serve with crusty bread.

Peggy's chicken and leek pie

Chicken thigh in a creamy thyme white sauce under a puff pastry lid, Camp Lehigh style.

Prep 8 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; top with pastry and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, then add the leeks and cook until soft.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, cream, mustard and thyme leaves and simmer until thick. Cool a little.
  3. Spoon into a pie dish, lay the pastry over, trim, crimp and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and deep golden.

Sweets

Shield cherry and blueberry pieVegetarian

Cherry and blueberry under a lattice top, red, white and blue.

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to 1 day ahead, keep it covered and warm it in a 160°C oven for 15 minutes; serve with ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 400g frozen cherries
  • 200g blueberries
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the cherries and blueberries with the sugar and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with pastry, fill, and weave a lattice from the rest.
  3. Bake at 190°C until bubbling.
  4. Rest the pie, then serve warm with ice cream.

Drinks

Brooklyn vanilla malted milkshakeNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream blended with milk and malted milk powder.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the glasses and the milk up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 5 tbsp malted milk powder
  1. Chill four tall glasses.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream and malted milk powder until frothy.
  3. Pour into the glasses and serve straight away.
