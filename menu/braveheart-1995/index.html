Snacks

Oatcakes and cheddarVegetarian

Oatcakes and cheddar for Hamish, who trains with stones

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Oatcakes up to 2 days ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g rolled oats
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  • 4 tbsp apple chutney
  1. Mix the oats with the melted butter, salt and a splash of hot water.
  2. Roll thin and cut into rounds.
  3. Bake at 180C for 20 minutes and cool.
  4. Serve with sliced cheddar and apple chutney.

Thistle-topped smoked salmon

Smoked salmon squares, with Murron's thistle kept close

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, cover and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 50g soft butter
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 6 slices wholemeal bread
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp capers
  1. Mash the butter with the lemon zest.
  2. Spread on the bread and top with smoked salmon, dill and capers.
  3. Cut into neat squares.

Highland haggis bon bons, whisky creamOn the printed menu

Spiced lamb and oat balls crumbed and fried crunchy, mustard cream dip, Stephen wants seconds

How it is made
  • 400g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 1 jar allspice
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 30ml Scotch whisky
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  1. Spiced lamb and oat haggis rolled into balls, crumbed and fried crunchy, with a whisky, wholegrain mustard and cream dipping sauce. Fry 400g lamb mince with a diced onion, then add 80g toasted oats, 1 tsp allspice, 1 tsp pepper, half a tsp nutmeg and 150ml beef stock and cook until thick. Cool and roll into 20 balls. Roll in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs and fry at 175C for 3 minutes until crisp. Simmer 150ml cream with 2 tbsp whisky and 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard for 3 minutes for the dip.

Mains

Scotch broth and lamb

Lamb neck simmered with barley, split peas, leeks, carrots and swede

Prep 20 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb neck
  • 2 1/2l water
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 100g split peas
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 swede, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Put the lamb, water, barley and split peas in a large pot and bring to a simmer.
  2. Simmer gently for 1 hour, skimming now and then.
  3. Add the leeks, carrots and swede and simmer for another hour.
  4. Pull the meat from the bone and return it to the pot. Season and finish with parsley.
  5. Serve with crusty bread.

Stirling venison with neeps and tattiesOn the printed menu

Backstrap seared rare and sliced, buttery mashed swede, creamy potato and peppery whisky cream sauce

How it is made
  • 600g swede
  • 600g potatoes
  • 70g butter
  • 80ml milk
  • 800g venison backstrap
  • 1 jar cracked black pepper
  • 40ml Scotch whisky
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Venison backstrap seared rare and sliced, with buttery mashed swede, creamy mashed potato and a peppery whisky cream sauce. Boil 600g diced swede and 600g potatoes in separate pots until soft. Mash the swede with 30g butter and pepper, and the potatoes with 40g butter and 80ml milk. Season 800g venison backstrap with cracked pepper and sear in a hot pan 3 minutes a side for rare, then rest 8 minutes. Add 40ml whisky to the pan, then 150ml cream and 1 tsp cracked pepper and simmer 2 minutes. Slice and spoon over.

Scottish shortbreadVegetarian

Buttery shortbread with rice flour, cut while warm

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 130g caster sugar, plus extra to sprinkle
  • 300g plain flour
  • 60g rice flour
  1. Beat the butter with most of the sugar until pale, then work in the flour and rice flour.
  2. Press into a tin and prick all over.
  3. Bake at 150C for 40 minutes.
  4. Cut while warm and sprinkle with caster sugar.

Wallace's heather honey whisky toddyVegetarian

Hot Scotch whisky, honey and lemon with clove studded lemon wheels

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Stud the lemon wheels with the cloves up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; build the glasses and top with just-boiled water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemon wheels
  • 12 whole cloves
  • 160ml Scotch whisky
  • 4 tbsp heather honey
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 600ml just-boiled water
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  1. Stud the lemon wheels with the cloves.
  2. Into four heatproof glasses put whisky, honey and lemon juice.
  3. Top with just-boiled water and stir until the honey dissolves. Add a studded lemon wheel and a cinnamon stick to each.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Wallace roars for freedom before Stirling.
