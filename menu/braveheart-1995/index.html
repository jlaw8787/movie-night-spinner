Snacks

Oatcakes and cheddarVegetarian

Crisp Scottish oatcakes with sharp cheddar, sturdy enough for stone throwing.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Oatcakes up to 2 days ahead, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g rolled oats
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  • 200g sharp cheddar
  • 4 tbsp apple chutney
  1. Mix the oats with the melted butter, salt and a splash of hot water.
  2. Roll thin and cut into rounds.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 20 minutes and cool.
  4. Serve with sliced cheddar and apple chutney.

Thistle-topped smoked salmon

Smoked salmon squares, with Murron's thistle kept close.

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, cover and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 50g soft butter
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 6 slices of wholemeal bread
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp capers
  1. Mash the butter with the lemon zest.
  2. Spread on the bread and top with smoked salmon, dill and capers.
  3. Cut into neat squares.

Highland haggis bon bons, mustard cream

Spiced lamb and oat balls crumbed and fried crunchy, mustard cream dip, Stephen wants seconds.

How it is made
  • 400g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  1. Spiced lamb and oat haggis rolled into balls, crumbed and fried crunchy, with a wholegrain mustard and cream dipping sauce. Fry 400g lamb mince with a diced onion, then add 80g toasted oats, 1 tsp pepper, half a tsp nutmeg and 150ml beef stock and cook until thick. Cool and roll into 20 balls. Roll in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs and fry at 175°C for 3 minutes until crisp. Simmer 150ml cream with 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard for 3 minutes for the dip.

Mains

Scotch broth and lamb

Lamb neck simmered with barley, split peas, leeks, carrots and turnip.

Prep 18 min, cook 2 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb neck
  • 2.5 L water
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 100g split peas
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 turnip, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Put the lamb, water, barley and split peas in a large pot and bring to a simmer, 10 min.
  2. Simmer gently for 1 hour, skimming now and then.
  3. Add the leeks, carrots and turnip and simmer for another hour, 1 hr.
  4. Pull the meat from the bone and return it to the pot. Season and finish with parsley.
  5. Serve with crusty bread.

Stirling venison with barley and kale

Backstrap seared rare and sliced over buttered barley and kale, with a peppery cream sauce.

How it is made
  • 250g pearl barley
  • 1 bunch kale
  • 40g butter
  • 800g venison backstrap
  • 1 jar cracked black pepper
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Venison backstrap seared rare and sliced over buttered pearl barley and kale, with a peppery cream sauce. Simmer 250g pearl barley in salted water for 35 minutes until tender, then drain. Wilt 1 bunch shredded kale in 40g butter and stir through the barley. Season 800g venison backstrap with cracked pepper and sear in a hot pan 3 minutes a side for rare, then rest 8 minutes. Add 150ml cream and 1 tsp cracked pepper to the pan and simmer 2 minutes. Slice and spoon over.

Sweets

Bramble crowdie with toasted oatsVegetarian

Soft crowdie cheese whipped with heather honey, brambles and toasted oats.

Prep 10 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Toast the oats up to 3 days ahead, airtight; layer just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g rolled oats
  • 300g crowdie or ricotta
  • 3 tbsp heather or local honey
  • 250g blackberries
  1. Toast the oats in a dry pan until golden, then cool, 4 min.
  2. Beat the crowdie with the honey until smooth.
  3. Layer into glasses with the blackberries and oats, finishing with berries and honey.

Drinks

Wallace's hot spiced heather mead

Honey mead warmed with clove and lemon peel, poured hot into cups.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Warm just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml mead
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp heather or local honey
  1. Peel two strips of lemon peel.
  2. Warm the mead with the cloves and peel until steaming, without boiling, 8 min.
  3. Stir in the honey and pour into cups.
