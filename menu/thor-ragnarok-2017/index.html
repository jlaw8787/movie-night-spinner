Snacks

Grandmaster's rainbow fruit skewersVegetarian

Watermelon, kiwi, pineapple and dragonfruit with lime, honey and a pinch of chilli

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1/4 watermelon, cubed
  • 2 kiwifruit, peeled
  • 1/4 pineapple, cubed
  • 1 dragonfruit, peeled
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch of chilli flakes
  • 12 bamboo skewers
  1. Cut the watermelon, kiwifruit, pineapple and dragonfruit into chunks.
  2. Thread onto skewers in a rainbow.
  3. Drizzle with lime juice, honey and chilli.

Korg's Kiwi sausage rolls

Pork, onion and carrot sausage rolls with sesame, tomato sauce for dipping

Prep 22 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 tsp mixed dried herbs
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a tray.
  2. Mix the sausage mince with the onion, carrot and herbs.
  3. Roll in the pastry sheets, egg wash and sprinkle with sesame.
  4. Cut into 16 pieces.
  5. Bake until golden and serve warm with tomato sauce.

Scrapper 142's salt and pepper calamariOn the printed menu

Squid strips in rice flour, Sichuan pepper and chilli, fried crisp with garlic, lime mayo

How it is made
  • 600g squid tubes
  • 100g rice flour
  • 1 jar Sichuan peppercorns
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lime
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Squid strips dusted in rice flour, salt, Sichuan pepper and chilli, fried crisp and tossed with fried garlic and spring onion, with lime mayo. Cut 600g cleaned squid tubes into strips and pat dry. Mix 100g rice flour with 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp ground Sichuan pepper and half a teaspoon of chilli powder. Toss the squid through. Fry in batches at 190°C for 60 to 90 seconds until crisp and pale golden. Fry 4 sliced garlic cloves and 1 sliced red chilli for 30 seconds and toss through with spring onion. Mix 100g mayonnaise with lime juice to dip.

Mains

Contest of Champions roast

Garlic rosemary lamb shoulder roasted 5 hours until it pulls apart, mint yoghurt

Prep 18 min, cook 5 hours · Make ahead: Can rest, covered, for 30 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg lamb shoulder
  • 4 cloves of garlic, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fresh rosemary
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 handful of mint, chopped
  1. Cut slits in the lamb and push in the garlic, rosemary and lemon.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C until the lamb pulls apart.
  3. Roast the potatoes in oil alongside for the last hour.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with the mint.
  5. Rest the lamb and pull apart to serve.

Asgardian roast beef rib with YorkshiresOn the printed menu

Bone-in rib cooked pink and carved, tall puddings, red wine gravy, before Hela arrives

How it is made
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 150ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  1. A bone-in beef rib roasted to pink and carved at the table, with tall Yorkshire puddings, crisp potatoes, roast carrots and red wine gravy. Season a 2kg beef rib roast and roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then 160°C for about 60 minutes to 52°C inside. Rest 25 minutes. Roast 1kg parboiled potatoes and 500g carrots in hot oil at 220°C while the beef rests. Whisk 3 eggs, 150ml milk and 120g plain flour, pour into a smoking oiled 12-hole tin and bake 20 minutes. Simmer the tray juices with 1 tbsp flour, 150ml red wine and 300ml beef stock for gravy.

Kiwi afghan biscuitsVegetarian

Chocolate cornflake cookies with chocolate icing and a walnut half on top

Prep 22 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 180g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa, for the biscuits
  • 50g cornflakes
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp cocoa, for the icing
  • 8 walnut halves
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a tray.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour, cocoa and cornflakes.
  3. Spoon 16 mounds onto the tray and bake.
  4. Cool, then mix the icing sugar and cocoa with a little water.
  5. Ice each biscuit and top with half a walnut.

Drinks

Never-empty mug of honey lager punchVegetarian

Cold lager mixed with honey ginger syrup, lemon and apple juice, served in big mugs

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 80g honey
  • 80ml water
  • 1 piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 200ml cloudy apple juice
  • 6 bottles cold lager
  • 1 lemon, cut into wheels
  1. Simmer the honey, water and ginger, then cool and strain.
  2. Chill a large jug.
  3. Mix the syrup, lemon juice and apple juice in the jug.
  4. Slowly pour in the lager and stir gently once.
  5. Pour into big mugs with a lemon wheel and keep a second jug ready.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Doctor Strange hands Thor the mug that never runs out.
