Tucker and Dale vs. Evil (2010) movie night dinner menu

Snacks

Lake catfish bites with comeback sauce

Dale pulls Allison out of the lake

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead; crumb and fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g skinless basa or catfish fillets, cut in chunks
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 lemon
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, hot sauce and a squeeze of lemon into comeback sauce.
  2. Soak the fish in the buttermilk while you mix the cornmeal, flour, paprika and salt.
  3. Toss the fish in the cornmeal and fry in hot oil in batches until crisp.
  4. Drain, salt and serve with the sauce and lemon.

Gas station chilli dogs with cheese

Dale says hi holding a scythe

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Brown the mince with half the onion, add the spices and tomatoes and simmer until thick.
  2. Grill the franks until blistered.
  3. Fill the rolls, spoon over the chilli and top with cheddar, the rest of the onion and mustard.

Mains

Wood chipper pulled pork rolls with slaw

Mike dives into the wood chipper

Prep 20 min, cook 240 min · Make ahead: Cook and pull the pork up to 2 days ahead; reheat in its juices and make the slaw on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg boneless pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml apple juice
  • 125ml barbecue sauce
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 green apple, cut in matchsticks
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 8 soft white rolls
  1. Rub the pork with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Put it in a lidded pot with the apple juice and cook at 150C until it falls apart.
  3. Shred with two forks and stir through the barbecue sauce and some of the juices.
  4. Toss the cabbage and apple with the mayonnaise and vinegar.
  5. Pile the pork and slaw into the rolls.

Beehive hot honey grilled chicken

Tucker saws into a beehive

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to a day ahead; mix the hot honey up to a week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg boneless chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 125ml honey
  • 2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 20g butter
  1. Toss the chicken with the oil, paprika, garlic and salt.
  2. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes and vinegar and set aside.
  3. Grill the chicken and corn over medium-high heat, turning, until charred and cooked through.
  4. Brush the chicken with the hot honey for the last few minutes, butter the corn and serve.

Sweets

Chamomile honey cake with whipped creamVegetarian

Dale throws the chamomile tea at Chad

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Bake the cake up to 2 days ahead and keep it covered; whip the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chamomile tea bags
  • 125ml boiling water
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 125ml honey, plus extra to drizzle
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Steep the tea bags in the boiling water for 10 minutes, then squeeze them out.
  2. Beat the butter, sugar and honey until pale, then beat in the eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour and the tea, pour into a lined 20cm tin and bake at 170C until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Cool, then serve in wedges with whipped cream and more honey.

That's a PBR buddy

Prep 2 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the cans overnight in the esky
Recipe for 4

  • 2 bags ice
  1. Bury the cans in ice in the esky.
  2. Crack one each and pass them round the fire.
