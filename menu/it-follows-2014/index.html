Snacks

8 Mile coney dog bites

Cocktail franks topped with spiced beef chilli.

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and reheat; simmer the franks and build the bites on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1/2 brown onion, diced, for the chilli
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 150ml water
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 16 mini bread rolls
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1/2 brown onion, finely diced, for the topping
  1. Brown the mince with the onion, cumin and paprika.
  2. Add the water and simmer.
  3. Simmer the franks.
  4. Tuck the franks into split mini rolls, spoon over chilli, add mustard and the finely diced onion.

Above-ground pool chips and dipVegetarian

Slow cooked onions folded into sour cream and cream cheese dip.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dip keeps 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 bag ridged potato chips
  1. Cook the onions in the butter over low heat until jammy.
  2. Cool and chop.
  3. Fold into the sour cream and cream cheese with salt, pepper and chives.
  4. Chill the dip.
  5. Serve with a big bag of ridged potato chips.

Detroit-style pepperoni pizza squares

Thick airy pan base, crunchy caramelised cheese edges, cupped pepperoni and chunky tomato stripes.

How it is made
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 80g pepperoni
  • 300g mozzarella
  • 300g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  1. Thick, airy pan pizza with crunchy caramelised cheese edges, cupped pepperoni and stripes of chunky tomato sauce on top, cut into small squares. Press 500g pizza dough into an oiled 23 x 33cm tin and prove 1 hour. Push the dough to the corners, cover with 80g sliced pepperoni, then 300g cubed mozzarella right to the edges so it touches the tin. Bake at 250°C for 14 minutes until the edges are dark and crisp. Simmer 300g crushed tomatoes with garlic, oregano and a pinch of sugar for 15 minutes and spoon over in two stripes. Cut into 16 squares.

Mains

Late-night Greek diner gyros

Marinated pork shoulder in warm pitas with tzatziki, tomato and red onion.

Prep 25 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the pork up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and mix the tzatziki up to 1 day ahead; griddle and wrap on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g pork shoulder steaks
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1/2 cucumber, grated
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed, extra
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  1. Mix the garlic, oregano, lemon juice and oil and marinate the pork.
  2. Griddle the pork, turning once, and slice thin.
  3. Mix the yoghurt with the cucumber and garlic for tzatziki.
  4. Warm the pitas and wrap the pork with tzatziki, tomato and red onion.

Lake trip beer-battered fish tacos

Crisp fried cod in corn tortillas, lime slaw, pickled onion, avocado crema, for the getaway.

How it is made
  • 1 red onion
  • 4 limes
  • 1 small red cabbage
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 avocado
  • 100g sour cream
  • 150g plain flour
  • 375ml beer
  • 600g flathead fillets
  • 1.5L vegetable oil
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  1. Crisp beer-battered flathead in warm corn tortillas with lime slaw, pickled onion, avocado crema and a hit of hot sauce. Slice 1 red onion and soak in the juice of 2 limes with salt for 20 minutes. Shred a quarter red cabbage and toss with lime juice and coriander. Blitz 1 avocado with 100g sour cream and lime. Whisk 150g plain flour with 250ml cold beer and salt. Cut 600g flathead into strips, dip in batter and fry at 180°C for 3 minutes. Warm 12 corn tortillas and fill with fish, slaw, onion, crema and hot sauce.

Sweets

Ice cream sandwichesVegetarian

Chocolate cookies sandwiching vanilla ice cream, edges rolled in mini choc chips.

Prep 20 min, cook 14 min, then 75 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 150g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 100g mini choc chips
  1. Beat the butter and sugar with the egg, then mix in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Scoop eight cookies onto lined trays.
  3. Bake at 175°C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Sandwich with thick slices of ice cream and roll the edges in choc chips.
  6. Freeze.

Drinks

Hairdryer plan poolside lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade with sliced lemon and plenty of ice, no alcohol.

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 1 week ahead in the fridge and juice the lemons up to 1 day ahead; mix with water and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 6 lemons
  • 1 L cold water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool.
  2. Juice five lemons and mix with the syrup and cold water in a big jug.
  3. Add sliced lemon and plenty of ice, pour into four glasses.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the gang heads to the public pool for the last stand.
