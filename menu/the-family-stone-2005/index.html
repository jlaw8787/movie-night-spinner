Snacks

Stone family pigs in blankets

Bacon wrapped sausages glazed in maple, cranberry mustard dip.

Prep 23 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Wrap up to 1 day ahead, roast fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 20 chipolatas
  • 10 rashers streaky bacon
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 150g cranberry sauce
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Cut the bacon rashers in half and wrap each chipolata.
  2. Lay on a lined tray and roast at 200°C, turning once, until crisp.
  3. Brush with maple syrup for the last few minutes.
  4. Mix the cranberry sauce with wholegrain mustard for dipping and serve hot on toothpicks.

Dinner table garlic herb pull-apartVegetarian

Pull-apart loaf stuffed with garlic and herb butter, baked golden.

Prep 20 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Prepare up to 1 day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g crusty loaf
  • 100g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Cut the loaf in deep diagonal slices every 2cm, without cutting through the base.
  2. Mash the soft butter with crushed garlic, parsley and parmesan.
  3. Push the butter into every cut and wrap in foil.
  4. Bake at 190°C.
  5. Open the foil and bake until crisp and golden.

Mains

Ham, spinach and gruyère strata

Bread and egg bake with ham and gruyère, made the night before.

Prep 22 min, cook 50 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Assemble the night before, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 20g butter
  • 6 thick slices day-old bread
  • 200g leg ham
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 150g gruyère
  • 6 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar of tomato relish
  1. Butter a 2L baking dish.
  2. Tear the bread and layer with the diced ham, spinach and grated gruyère.
  3. Whisk the eggs with milk, Dijon, salt and pepper and pour over.
  4. Press down and cover.
  5. Chill for at least 2 hours or overnight.
  6. Bake at 180°C until puffed and golden.
  7. Rest before serving with tomato relish.

Sybil's lemon and thyme roast chicken

Roast chicken, Christmas Eve, and one very wrong choice of words.

Prep 13 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Make the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead; butter the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 60g butter, soft
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 2 lemons
  • 1kg potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Mix the butter with the garlic, thyme leaves, zest of a lemon and salt.
  2. Push the butter under the chicken skin and put the halved lemons and thyme stalks in the cavity.
  3. Toss the potatoes with the oil and salt in a roasting tin and sit the chicken on top.
  4. Roast at 200°C until the juices run clear.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes, then carve and spoon the pan juices over.

Christmas morning cinnamon scrollsVegetarian

Soft dough rolled with brown sugar and spice, cream cheese icing, unwrapped with the framed photos.

Prep 33 min, cook 22 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Roll the night before and chill, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 90g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 120g cream cheese
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Roll the dough into a 30 x 40cm rectangle.
  2. Spread with soft butter and sprinkle with brown sugar mixed with cinnamon.
  3. Roll up from the long side and cut into scrolls.
  4. Pack into a greased 24cm tin and cover.
  5. Rest until puffy.
  6. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  7. Beat the cream cheese, butter, icing sugar and vanilla and spread over the warm scrolls.

Drinks

Stone house eggnog latteNo alcohol

Strong coffee, eggnog milk, nutmeg. Meredith can make more.

Prep 13 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Eggnog base up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 400ml brewed coffee
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  1. Heat the milk with cream, sugar, cinnamon stick and vanilla until steaming.
  2. Whisk the egg yolks in a bowl and pour in the hot milk while whisking.
  3. Return to the pan and stir over low heat until slightly thick.
  4. Brew strong coffee and divide between mugs.
  5. Froth the eggnog milk, pour over and grate nutmeg on top.
