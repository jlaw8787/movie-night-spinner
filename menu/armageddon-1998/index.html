Snacks

AJ and Grace's honey animal crackersVegetarian

Crisp honey butter cookies cut into small animal shapes.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 250g plain flour
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Beat the butter, sugar and honey, then mix in the flour to a dough, 10 min.
  2. Roll thin and cut with small animal cutters.
  3. Bake until pale golden, 10 min.
  4. Cool on a rack and pile into a bowl, 5 min.

Oil rig jerky

Beef marinated overnight in soy, brown sugar and pepper, dried low and slow.

Prep 20 min, cook 4 hr, 12 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 weeks, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef topside
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns, cracked
  1. Slice the beef thin across the grain.
  2. Marinate with the soy, sugar and cracked black peppercorns overnight in the fridge.
  3. Lay on racks and dry in a low oven with the door ajar until leathery but bendy.

Mains

Rib eye with onion gravy and mashed potatoes

Seared rib eyes, buttery mashed potato and caramelised onion gravy.

Prep 7 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Make the mash and gravy up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently; sear the steaks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with most of the butter and the milk, 25 min.
  2. Cook the onions in butter until deep golden, stir in the flour, then add the stock and simmer, 25 min.
  3. Sear the steaks hard in the vegetable oil in a smoking pan, 8 min.
  4. Rest the steaks and pour the gravy over, 7 min.

Oil rig chuck chilli with cornbread

Hearty red chilli with cornbread, thick enough to hold up on an oil rig.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 onions
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 400g tin kidney beans
  • 1 packet cornbread mix
  • 30g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Beef chuck chunks braised with onion, garlic, chilli, cumin, tomato and kidney beans until tender, with sour cream and warm buttered cornbread. Brown 1.2kg beef chuck in 3cm chunks in batches. Add 2 diced onions and 4 garlic cloves and cook 5 minutes, then 2 tbsp chilli powder, 2 tsp cumin and 2 tbsp tomato paste. Add 800g crushed tomatoes and 250ml beef stock, cover and braise at 150°C for 2.5 hours. Stir in a drained 400g tin kidney beans for the last 20 minutes. Bake cornbread mix in a hot buttered pan at 200°C for 20 minutes. Serve with sour cream.

Sweets

Animal crackers and creamVegetarian

Pink-dipped animal cookies with whipped cream, crunchy enough to pass as crackers.

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Biscuits up to 2 days, whip cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 250g plain flour
  • 250g pink or white candy melts
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Mix the butter, sugar and flour to a dough, roll and cut animal shapes, 20 min.
  2. Bake until pale golden, then cool, 15 min.
  3. Melt the candy melts, dip each biscuit half and add sprinkles, 15 min.
  4. Whip the cream and serve in a bowl alongside.

Drinks

Drillers' smoky tea and gingerNo alcohol

Butterscotch, lime, orange zest and ginger beer.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; build over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml butterscotch
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 4 dashes of aromatic orange zest
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water for 5 minutes, remove them and chill the tea.
  2. Fill four cooler glasses with ice.
  3. Add 40ml of the tea, a drop of vanilla and a squeeze of lime to each.
  4. Top with cold ginger beer, stir once from the bottom and garnish with a lime wedge.

When to serve

Serve dessert when AJ and Grace say goodbye at the launch.
