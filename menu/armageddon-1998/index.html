Armageddon (1998) movie night dinner menu

Snacks

AJ and Grace's honey animal crackersVegetarian

Crisp honey butter cookies cut into small animal shapes

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 250g plain flour
  1. Heat the oven to 170C. Beat the butter, sugar and honey, then mix in the flour to a dough.
  2. Roll thin and cut with small animal cutters.
  3. Bake until pale golden.
  4. Cool on a rack and pile into a bowl.

Oil rig jerky

Beef marinated overnight in soy, brown sugar and pepper, dried low and slow

Prep 30 min, cook 1000 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 weeks, airtight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef topside
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns, cracked
  1. Slice the beef thin across the grain.
  2. Marinate with the soy, sugar and cracked black peppercorns overnight in the fridge.
  3. Lay on racks and dry in a low oven with the door ajar until leathery but bendy.

Mains

Rib eye with onion gravy and mash

Seared rib eyes, buttery mashed potato and caramelised onion gravy

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the mash and gravy up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently; sear the steaks just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with most of the butter and the milk.
  2. Cook the onions in butter until deep golden, stir in the flour, then add the stock and simmer.
  3. Sear the steaks hard in the vegetable oil in a smoking pan.
  4. Rest the steaks and pour the gravy over.

Sweets

Animal crackers and creamVegetarian

Pink-dipped animal cookies with whipped cream. Not a cracker, says AJ.

Prep 40 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Biscuits up to 2 days, whip cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 250g plain flour
  • 250g pink or white candy melts
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Heat the oven to 170C. Mix the butter, sugar and flour to a dough, roll and cut animal shapes.
  2. Bake until pale golden, then cool.
  3. Melt the candy melts, dip each biscuit half and add sprinkles.
  4. Whip the cream and serve in a bowl alongside.

Drinks

Drillers' whiskey ginger highballVegetarian

Make your peace with God, AJ. Bourbon, lime, bitters and ginger beer.

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Build to order; juice the limes and chill the ginger beer up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml ginger beer
  • 4 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill four highball glasses with ice.
  2. Add bourbon, a squeeze of lime and a dash of bitters to each.
  3. Top with cold ginger beer, stir once from the bottom and garnish with a lime wedge.

When to serve

Serve dessert when AJ does the animal cracker scene.
