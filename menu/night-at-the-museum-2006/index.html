Snacks

Dinosaur bone breadsticksVegetarian

Knotted garlic parmesan breadsticks shaped like bones, tomato dipping sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Bake the day you serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 50g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  1. Roll the dough into ropes and knot both ends into bone shapes.
  2. Brush with garlic butter and sprinkle with grated parmesan.
  3. Bake at 220C until golden.
  4. Simmer the crushed tomatoes to dip.

Easter Island watermelon and feta skewersVegetarian

Chilled watermelon and feta cubes threaded with mint on short skewers

Prep 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Assemble up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1/4 watermelon
  • 200g feta
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  1. Cut the watermelon and feta into cubes.
  2. Thread onto short skewers with a mint leaf between each.
  3. Chill on a platter.
  4. Just before serving, squeeze over lime juice, drizzle with honey and add flaky salt and chilli flakes.

Roman legion warm olives and focacciaOn the printed menu

Mixed olives warmed in oil with lemon peel, garlic, oregano and chilli, rosemary bread to dip

How it is made
  • 300g mixed olives
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 400g rosemary focaccia
  • 1 bottle balsamic vinegar
  1. Mixed olives warmed in olive oil with lemon peel, garlic, oregano and chilli, with torn rosemary focaccia for dipping in the oil and balsamic. Warm 300g mixed olives in a small pan with 80ml olive oil, 3 strips lemon peel, 2 sliced garlic cloves, 1 tsp dried oregano and a pinch of chilli flakes over low heat for 8 minutes. Warm a 400g rosemary focaccia at 180C for 6 minutes and tear into pieces. Serve the olives in their oil, with a small dish of balsamic for dipping.

Mains

Manhattan hot dog cart

Franks in steamed buns with onion tomato sauce, spicy mustard, warm sauerkraut

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Soften the onions and cook them down with the tomato paste up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; reheat, simmer the franks and fill the buns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 4 tbsp American mustard
  • 200g sauerkraut
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Soften the sliced onions in oil.
  2. Add the tomato paste and a splash of water and cook down.
  3. Simmer the franks in water.
  4. Warm the sauerkraut and steam the buns.
  5. Fill the buns with franks, mustard, sauerkraut and onions.

Jedediah's Wild West barbecue slidersOn the printed menu

Small patties glazed in smoky sauce, cheddar, pickles and crispy onions on soft buns, apple slaw

How it is made
  • 700g beef mince
  • 1 bottle barbecue sauce
  • 6 slices cheddar
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 green apple
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 tub fried shallots
  1. Mini beef patties glazed with smoky barbecue sauce, melted cheddar, pickles and crispy fried onions on soft slider buns, with a crunchy apple slaw. Shape 700g beef mince into 12 small patties and season. Grill on a hot plate 2 minutes a side, brushing with 80ml barbecue sauce, and top with cheddar to melt. Toss half a shredded cabbage and a julienned green apple with 3 tbsp mayo and lemon juice. Fill 12 toasted slider buns with patties, pickles and a handful of fried shallots. Serve the slaw alongside.

Dexter's banana breadVegetarian

Thick sliced banana bread, toasted and buttered

Prep 7 min, cook 68 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 140g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  1. Mash the bananas with part of the melted butter, the sugar and eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour and bicarb, then pour into a lined loaf tin.
  3. Bake at 170C.
  4. Cool, slice thick, toast and spread with the remaining butter.

Drinks

Night guard thermos hot chocolateNo alcohol

Hot chocolate with dark chocolate, cocoa and cinnamon, kept hot in a thermos

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make up to 1 day ahead and chill; reheat gently until steaming and pour into the warmed thermos
Recipe for 4
  • 1l milk
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 1 bag marshmallows
  1. Heat the milk until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chopped chocolate, cocoa, sugar and cinnamon until smooth.
  3. Pour into a warmed thermos to keep hot, then serve in mugs topped with marshmallows.
