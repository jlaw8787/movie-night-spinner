Under-the-L Chicago dogs

Beef frank, poppy seed bun, the full garden.

Prep 14 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Chop the toppings up to 4 hours ahead and keep cold; simmer the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 all-beef frankfurts
  • 8 poppy seed split-top rolls
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp bright green sweet relish
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  • 2 tomatoes, cut in thin wedges
  • 8 dill pickle spears
  • 8 pickled sport peppers or small hot pickled chillies
  • 1 tsp celery salt
  1. Bring a pot of water to a bare simmer, add the franks and heat through without boiling, 8 min.
  2. Steam or warm the rolls so they stay soft, 3 min.
  3. Tuck a frank into each roll and dress in order: mustard, relish, onion, tomato wedges, a pickle spear and two peppers.
  4. Finish with a pinch of celery salt. No tomato sauce.

Provasic-free chicken liver pate with toast

Silky chicken livers blitzed with butter and thyme, on toasted rye.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the pate up to 3 days ahead and keep it sealed with butter in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken livers, trimmed
  • 150g butter, softened
  • 2 French shallots, finely diced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 4 thyme sprigs, leaves picked
  • 60ml brandy
  • 8 slices of rye bread, toasted
  • 8 cornichons
  1. Melt 30g of the butter and soften the shallot, garlic and thyme, 5 min.
  2. Turn up the heat, add the livers and cook until browned outside and just pink inside, 5 min.
  3. Add the brandy and let it bubble off, then cool for 5 minutes.
  4. Blend with the rest of the butter and plenty of salt and pepper until silky, press into a dish and chill, 10 min.
  5. Serve with the rye toast and cornichons, 5 min.

Cook County Italian beef, dipped, with hot giardiniera

Thin roast beef, garlicky jus, pickled chilli veg.

Prep 30 min, cook 1 hr 55 min · Make ahead: Roast and slice the beef and make the giardiniera up to 2 days ahead; reheat the beef in the jus to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef blade or topside roast
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 1 L beef stock
  • 4 long crusty Italian rolls
  • 1 red capsicum, finely diced
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 2 long green chillies, sliced
  • 125ml white vinegar
  • 125ml olive oil
  1. For the giardiniera, toss the capsicum, celery, carrot and chillies with a tablespoon of salt, leave 15 minutes, rinse, then cover with the vinegar and oil.
  2. Rub the beef with the garlic, oregano, chilli and fennel, sit it in a roasting dish and pour the stock around it.
  3. Roast covered at 160°C until just tender, then rest and chill if you can, so it slices thinly, 1 hr 40 min.
  4. Slice the beef as thin as possible and warm it in the hot pan juices, 10 min.
  5. Pile into the split rolls, dip the whole sandwich in the jus and top with giardiniera.

St Patrick's Day corned beef with buttered cabbage

Corned beef, cabbage, potatoes, mustard sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 2 hr 50 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Simmer the beef up to 2 days ahead and chill it in its liquid; reheat in the liquid and cook the vegetables on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg corned silverside
  • 1 brown onion, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 600g small potatoes
  • 1/2 green cabbage, cut in wedges
  • 40g butter
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  1. Rinse the silverside, cover with cold water and add the onion, bay, sugar and vinegar.
  2. Simmer very gently until a skewer slides in easily, 2 hr 30 min.
  3. Lift the beef out to rest and cook the potatoes in the liquid, adding the cabbage for the last few minutes, 20 min.
  4. Drain, toss the cabbage and potatoes with the butter, warm the cream with the mustard and serve with thick slices of beef, 20 min.

Think-me-up chocolate donuts with sprinklesVegetarian

Yeast donuts, chocolate glaze, rainbow sprinkles. Gerard wants a donut with little sprinkles.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, 1 hr 35 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the dough the night before and prove it in the fridge; fry and glaze to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 375g plain flour
  • 7g sachet dried yeast
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 185ml warm milk
  • 1 egg
  • 50g butter, melted
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 80ml thickened cream
  • 3 tbsp rainbow sprinkles
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and a pinch of salt, then add the milk, egg and butter and knead until smooth and springy.
  2. Cover and leave somewhere warm until doubled, 1 hr.
  3. Roll out 1.5cm thick, cut 8 rings and prove for 30 minutes more.
  4. Fry at 175°C for a minute a side until golden, then drain, 10 min.
  5. Melt the chocolate into the warm cream, dip the tops of the donuts and shower with sprinkles, 20 min.
