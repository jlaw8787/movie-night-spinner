Snacks

Coconut pirate crusted prawns

Prawns coated in panko and shredded coconut, fried, with sweet chilli lime dip

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 3 hours ahead and keep them chilled on a tray; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, peeled
  • 4 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 60g shredded coconut
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Set up a station: flour, beaten egg, then the panko mixed with the coconut.
  2. Dip each prawn in flour, egg, then the crumb mix.
  3. Fry in the oil at 180C for 2 minutes, in batches.
  4. Serve with sweet chilli sauce mixed with lime juice.

Tamatoa's shiny poke cups

Diced tuna in soy and sesame over sushi rice with cucumber

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g sushi rice
  • 300g sashimi tuna, diced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  1. Cook the sushi rice and cool.
  2. Toss the tuna with soy, sesame oil, spring onion and chilli.
  3. Spoon the rice into small cups and top with tuna and cucumber.

Te Fiti fish in coconut

Snapper poached in coconut milk with lime leaf, ginger and chilli

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Simmer the coconut broth with the lime leaves, ginger and chilli up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; reheat, poach the snapper and cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 3 kaffir lime leaves
  • 1 piece ginger, sliced
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 600g snapper fillets
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Simmer the coconut milk with lime leaves, ginger and chilli for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the snapper and poach gently for 8 minutes.
  4. Finish with lime juice and coriander.

Sweets

Coconut haupiaVegetarian

Set coconut milk pudding cut into squares, topped with toasted coconut

Prep 10 min, cook 190 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 60g cornflour
  • 30g shredded coconut, toasted
  • 2 mangoes, diced
  1. Whisk the coconut milk, sugar and cornflour together in a pot.
  2. Stir over medium heat until very thick.
  3. Pour into a tray and chill for 3 hours.
  4. Cut into squares and top with toasted coconut and diced mango.

Drinks

Coconut, pineapple and lime crushVegetarianNo alcohol

Coconut water, pineapple, lime and mint blended slushy, served in coconut halves

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chop the pineapple and pick the mint up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml chilled coconut water
  • 400g fresh pineapple chunks
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 handful mint leaves, plus sprigs
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Blend the coconut water, pineapple, lime juice, mint, honey and ice until slushy.
  2. Pour into halved coconuts or tall glasses.
  3. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a mint sprig.
