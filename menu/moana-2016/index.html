Snacks

Coconut pirate crusted prawns

Prawns coated in panko and shredded coconut, fried, with sweet chilli lime dip

Prep 17 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 3 hours ahead and keep them chilled on a tray; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g green prawns, peeled
  • 4 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 60g shredded coconut
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Set up a station: flour, beaten egg, then the panko mixed with the coconut.
  2. Dip each prawn in flour, egg, then the crumb mix.
  3. Fry in the oil at 180°C for 2 minutes, in batches.
  4. Serve with sweet chilli sauce mixed with lime juice.

Tamatoa's shiny poke cups

Diced tuna in soy and sesame over sushi rice with cucumber

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 hour ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g sushi rice
  • 300g sashimi tuna, diced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  1. Cook the sushi rice and cool.
  2. Toss the tuna with soy, sesame oil, spring onion and chilli.
  3. Spoon the rice into small cups and top with tuna and cucumber.

Motunui taro chips with coconut lime dipOn the printed menu

Thin slices fried crisp with chilli salt, cool coconut yoghurt, before Moana sails past the reef

How it is made
  • 600g taro
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 250g coconut yoghurt
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 garlic bulb
  1. Thin taro slices fried crisp and dusted with chilli salt, with a cool dip of coconut yoghurt, lime zest, coriander and a touch of garlic. Peel 600g taro and slice paper thin on a mandoline, then rinse and pat completely dry. Fry in batches in oil at 175°C for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp and pale golden, then drain and toss with salt and a pinch of chilli powder. Mix 250g coconut yoghurt with the zest and juice of a lime, chopped coriander, 1 small grated garlic clove and salt for the dip.

Te Fiti fish in coconut

Snapper poached in coconut milk with lime leaf, ginger and chilli

Prep 8 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Simmer the coconut broth with the lime leaves, ginger and chilli up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; reheat, poach the snapper and cook the rice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 3 kaffir lime leaves
  • 1 piece ginger, sliced
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • 600g snapper fillets
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Simmer the coconut milk with lime leaves, ginger and chilli for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the snapper and poach gently for 8 minutes.
  4. Finish with lime juice and coriander.

Maui's huli huli chickenOn the printed menu

Thighs basted in pineapple, soy and ginger, grilled sticky, coconut rice, you're welcome

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thigh fillets
  • 200ml pineapple juice
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 270ml coconut milk
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  1. Chicken thighs marinated in pineapple, soy, ginger and brown sugar, grilled and basted until sticky and charred, with coconut rice and grilled pineapple. Mix 200ml pineapple juice, 80ml soy sauce, 60g brown sugar, 3 tbsp tomato sauce, 2 tbsp grated ginger and 3 crushed garlic cloves. Marinate 1kg chicken thigh fillets in half for 4 hours and simmer the rest until syrupy for basting. Cook 300g jasmine rice with 270ml coconut milk and 250ml water. Grill the chicken for 5 minutes a side, basting often, with 8 pineapple slices. Serve with the rice and sliced spring onion.

Coconut haupiaVegetarian

Set coconut milk pudding cut into squares, topped with toasted coconut

Prep 5 min, cook 17 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 60g cornflour
  • 30g shredded coconut, toasted
  • 2 mangoes, diced
  1. Whisk the coconut milk, sugar and cornflour together in a pot.
  2. Stir over medium heat until very thick.
  3. Pour into a tray and chill for 3 hours.
  4. Cut into squares and top with toasted coconut and diced mango.

Coconut, pineapple and lime crushVegetarianNo alcohol

Coconut water, pineapple, lime and mint blended slushy, served in coconut halves

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chop the pineapple and pick the mint up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; blend with the ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml chilled coconut water
  • 400g fresh pineapple chunks
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 handful mint leaves, plus sprigs
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Blend the coconut water, pineapple, lime juice, mint, honey and ice until slushy.
  2. Pour into halved coconuts or tall glasses.
  3. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a mint sprig.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the coconut pirates attack the boat.
