Snacks

Carnival night boiled peanutsVegetarian

Noah dangling off the Ferris wheel

Prep 5 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Boil up to 2 days ahead and keep them in their brine in the fridge; warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g raw peanuts in the shell
  • 80g sea salt
  • 2 tbsp Old Bay seasoning
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  1. Rinse the peanuts and put them in a big pot with 3 litres of water, the salt, Old Bay and bay leaves.
  2. Weigh them down with a plate, bring to the boil and simmer until the shells are soft and the nuts are tender.
  3. Leave them in the brine for 10 minutes, then drain and serve warm with lemon.

Lowcountry crab cakes with remoulade

Noah wrote her 365 letters

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Shape the cakes and make the remoulade up to a day ahead; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g blue swimmer crab meat, picked
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise, plus 125g for the remoulade
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  1. Mix the crab, egg, 3 tbsp mayo, mustard, half the panko, spring onions and half the lemon juice, then shape into 8 small cakes.
  2. Press the cakes into the rest of the panko and chill for 10 minutes.
  3. Stir the 125g mayo with the capers, paprika and remaining lemon for the remoulade.
  4. Fry the cakes in the oil until golden on both sides and serve with the remoulade.

Mains

Bird for bird buttermilk fried chicken

If you're a bird I'm a bird

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk overnight; make the slaw dressing a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in, skin on
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1/4 green cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with a big pinch of salt, at least 2 hours or overnight.
  2. Mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt, and dredge the chicken until shaggy.
  3. Fry in oil at 170C, turning, until deep gold and cooked through.
  4. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes and toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayo and vinegar.
  5. Drizzle the chicken with hot honey and serve with the slaw.

Windsor house shrimp and grits

Noah rebuilds the Windsor house for Allie

Prep 8 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Cook the grits up to a day ahead and loosen with hot milk; cook the prawns to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g green prawns, peeled
  • 4 rashers smoky bacon, chopped
  • 180g polenta or white grits
  • 500ml milk
  • 50g butter
  • 60g cheddar, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  1. Bring the milk and 500ml water to a simmer and whisk in the grits; cook, stirring, until thick and soft, then beat in half the butter and the cheddar.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp, then add the garlic and the rest of the butter.
  3. Add the prawns and cayenne and cook just until pink, then squeeze over the lemon.
  4. Spoon the prawns over the grits and scatter with spring onion.

Sweets

Every day after peach cobblerVegetarian

Noah reads her the notebook every day

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Rub the topping together up to 2 days ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 ripe peaches, sliced (or 2 x 825g tins, drained)
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 90g cold butter, diced
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 300ml pouring cream
  1. Toss the peaches with the brown sugar, cornflour and cinnamon and tip into a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and caster sugar, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over the peaches and bake at 190C until golden and bubbling.
  4. Serve warm with cold cream.

Drinks

Middle of the street bourbon sweet teaVegetarian

Allie and Noah lie down in the street

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Brew and sweeten the tea up to 2 days ahead and chill; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 ripe peach, sliced
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in 800ml just-boiled water, then stir in the sugar and cool.
  2. Fill 4 glasses with ice, peach and lemon.
  3. Pour in the bourbon and top with the sweet tea.

When to serve

Serve the cobbler when Allie remembers it is their story.
