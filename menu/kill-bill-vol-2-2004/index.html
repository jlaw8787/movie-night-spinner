Snacks

Five Point Palm rice bowlVegetarian

Short grain rice with furikake, a soft boiled egg and a pickled plum.

Prep 7 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Boil and peel the eggs up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; cook the rice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g short grain rice
  • 360ml water
  • 2 tbsp sake
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp furikake
  • 4 pickled plums
  1. Wash the rice until the water runs clear.
  2. Cook with the water and sake.
  3. Boil the eggs for a soft yolk, cool and peel.
  4. Serve the rice in small bowls with furikake, a pickled plum and a halved egg.

Jalapeño cheese dip with chips, jerky

Warm cheddar and jalapeño dip with corn chips and beef jerky.

Prep 9 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Grate the cheddar and chop the jalapeños up to 1 day ahead; melt the dip fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 250g cheddar
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeño juice
  • 4 pickled jalapeños
  • 1 bag corn chips
  • 100g beef jerky
  1. Grate the cheddar and chop the jalapeños.
  2. Warm the evaporated milk until steaming, then stir in the cheddar until smooth.
  3. Add the jalapeño juice and jalapeños.
  4. Pour into plastic bowls with corn chips and jerky on the side.

El Paso chapel beef cheek tacos

Chilli, garlic and cumin braise shredded into corn tortillas, onion, coriander, lime, salsa roja.

How it is made
  • 4 dried guajillo chillies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1kg beef cheeks
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tin chipotles in adobo
  • 16 corn tortillas
  • 1 white onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 3 limes
  1. Beef cheeks braised with dried chillies, garlic and cumin until they shred, in warm corn tortillas with white onion, coriander, lime and a fierce salsa roja. Toast 4 dried guajillo chillies, soak in hot water for 20 minutes and blitz with 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin and 400g crushed tomatoes. Brown 1kg beef cheeks, pour over the sauce with 500ml beef stock, cover and braise at 150°C for 4 hours. Shred in the sauce. For salsa roja, blitz 2 charred tomatoes, 2 chipotles in adobo and lime. Serve in 16 warm corn tortillas with diced white onion, coriander and lime.

Mains

Hacienda carnitas

Slow cooked pork shoulder crisped in a pan, with charred tomatillo salsa.

Prep 15 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Simmer the pork in the lard up to 2 days ahead and cool it in the fat in the fridge; make the salsa and crisp the pork on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 500g lard
  • 1 orange
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • salt, to taste
  • 400g tomatillos
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 16 corn tortillas
  1. Cut the pork into big chunks.
  2. Simmer slowly in the lard with the halved orange, garlic, bay leaves and salt.
  3. Char the tomatillos and chilli, then blitz with coriander.
  4. Crisp the pork in a hot pan.
  5. Warm the tortillas and serve with the pork and salsa.

Emilio the goldfish tuna melts

A fish not flapping on the carpet.

How it is made
  • 2 x 185g tins tuna
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 slices sourdough
  • 30g butter
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 green apple
  • 1 small cabbage
  1. Tuna mixed with mayo, celery, spring onion and lemon, piled on buttered sourdough under bubbling cheddar, cut into fingers with a crunchy apple slaw. Drain 2 x 185g tins tuna and mix with 4 tbsp mayo, a finely diced celery stick, 2 sliced spring onions, lemon juice and pepper. Butter 4 thick slices sourdough and toast under the grill. Pile on the tuna and cover with 150g grated cheddar. Grill 3 minutes until bubbling and cut into fingers. Toss a julienned apple and quarter of a shredded cabbage with 2 tbsp mayo and lemon for slaw.

Pai Mei's Chinese almond cookiesVegetarian

Crumbly almond cookies, each topped with a whole almond and baked golden.

Prep 10 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 120g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 220g plain flour
  • 50g almond meal
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 20 almonds
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar and one egg, add the almond extract, then mix in the flour, almond meal and baking powder.
  2. Roll into 20 balls and press an almond into each.
  3. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 175°C.

Drinks

Black mamba grapefruit paloma

Tequila, fresh pink grapefruit and lime in a chilli salt rimmed glass.

Prep 17 min · Make ahead: Juice the grapefruits and limes up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; rim the glasses and build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml blanco tequila
  • 2 pink grapefruits
  • 2 limes
  • 40ml agave syrup
  • 500ml soda water
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Mix the salt with the chilli powder on a plate.
  2. Rub the rims of four tall glasses with lime, dip in the salt and fill with ice.
  3. Juice the grapefruits and limes.
  4. Pour the tequila, grapefruit juice, lime juice and agave syrup into each glass.
  5. Top with soda water, stir and add a grapefruit wedge.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Budd opens the money case and the black mamba is inside.
