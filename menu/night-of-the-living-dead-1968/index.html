Snacks

Barbra's cemetery sandwiches

Egg and cress, ham and mustard on white, crusts off.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mash the egg filling up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; build and wrap the sandwiches up to 3 hours before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggs
  • 20g mayonnaise
  • 10g cress, chopped
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 25g butter, softened
  • 80g leg ham
  • 10g Dijon mustard
  1. Boil the eggs, cool, peel and mash with the mayonnaise.
  2. Stir in the cress.
  3. Butter the bread.
  4. Make two egg-and-cress sandwiches and two ham and mustard sandwiches.
  5. Trim the crusts and cut each sandwich into four small pieces.
  6. Wrap in baking paper for a 1968-style picnic.

Black and white popcornVegetarian

Black and white like the film stock. The blood on set was chocolate syrup.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best popped just before serving; it stays crisp for 4 hours in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 60g popcorn kernels
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 10g black sesame seeds
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot over medium heat, shaking regularly, then divide in half.
  2. Toss one half with half the butter and the salt.
  3. Toss the other half with the remaining butter, pepper and black sesame seeds.
  4. Arrange side by side in a shared bowl.

Farmhouse sardine and cheddar crackers

Saltines with lemony mashed tinned fish, sharp cheese and pickled onion, as the boards go up.

How it is made
  • 1 red onion
  • 80ml white vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 x 110g tins sardines
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 150g sharp cheddar
  • 1 box saltine crackers
  1. Saltine crackers piled with tinned sardines mashed with lemon and parsley, sharp cheddar, and a quick pickled red onion, laid out on a chopping board. Thinly slice 1 red onion and soak it in 80ml white vinegar with 1 tsp sugar and a pinch of salt for 20 minutes. Drain 2 x 110g tins sardines and mash lightly with the juice of half a lemon, chopped parsley and pepper. Slice 150g sharp cheddar. Top 24 saltine crackers with sardine, a slice of cheddar and a few pickled onion rings and lay them out on a chopping board.

Mains

Cellar or upstairs farmhouse stew

Beef chuck browned and braised slow with onion, carrot, potato, stock and thyme.

Prep 23 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck, cut into chunks
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 500g potatoes, in chunks
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 200g crusty Italian bread
  1. Cut the beef and season it.
  2. Brown in batches in the oil in a heavy casserole.
  3. Add the onion, carrots, potatoes, stock and thyme.
  4. Cover and braise at 160°C, stirring once, until the beef is tender.
  5. Serve with crusty bread for soaking up the stock.

Ben's beef and bean chilli with cornbread

Mince simmered with cumin and kidney beans over sour cream and cheddar, hot buttered cornbread.

How it is made
  • 700g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 2 x 400g tins tomatoes
  • 400g tinned kidney beans
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 80g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar
  1. Beef mince chilli simmered with cumin, chilli and kidney beans, ladled into bowls over sour cream and cheddar, with hot buttered cornbread on the side. Brown 700g beef mince in batches. Cook 1 diced onion and 3 garlic cloves 5 minutes, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp chilli powder and 1 tsp smoked paprika, then 2 x 400g tins tomatoes and 400g drained kidney beans. Simmer 45 minutes. Mix 150g cornmeal, 100g plain flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 300ml buttermilk, 1 egg and 60g melted butter, pour into a hot buttered pan and bake at 220°C for 20 minutes. Serve with sour cream and cheddar.

Sweets

Pantry peach cobbler with creamVegetarian

Tinned peaches under a cinnamon scone top. Nobody goes down to the cellar for cream.

Prep 15 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Mix the peach filling and rub in the topping up to 1 day ahead, keeping them separate in the fridge; assemble and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1650g tinned peach halves (2 tins)
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 80g cold butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 120ml milk
  • 1 tbsp raw sugar
  • 300ml pouring cream
  1. Drain the peaches, keeping a little syrup, and toss with the syrup and cornflour in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, sugar and cinnamon, then mix in the milk to a soft dough.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over the peaches and sprinkle with raw sugar.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  5. Serve warm with cream.

Drinks

Long night spiked percolator coffee

Strong stovetop brew with brown sugar and Irish whiskey, lightly whipped cream floated on top.

Prep 11 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Brew the coffee up to 1 day ahead and chill it; reheat, whip the cream and add the whiskey on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml strong brewed coffee
  • 2 tsp brown sugar
  • 120ml Irish whiskey
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Brew the coffee in a stovetop pot or plunger.
  2. Warm four heatproof glasses with hot water and tip it out.
  3. Put sugar and whiskey in each glass, add hot coffee and stir.
  4. Whip the cream until just pourable and float over the back of a spoon. Skip the whiskey for anyone who wants it plain.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when everyone boards up the windows.
