Snacks

Rhode Island clam cakes

Fried clam fritters with chopped clams in a light batter.

Prep 3 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Drain the clams and mix the dry ingredients up to 1 day ahead; mix the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tin chopped clams
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 4 tbsp tartare sauce
  1. Drain the clams, keeping the juice.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder, egg and some of the clam juice, then fold in the clams.
  3. Spoon into oil at 180°C and fry until puffed and golden, 10 min.
  4. Serve with lemon and tartare sauce.

Hide and clap cornmeal johnnycakesVegetarian

Crisp cornmeal griddle cakes and butter.

Prep 3 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving so the edges stay crisp; nothing keeps well once griddled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g fine cornmeal
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  • 300ml boiling water
  • 100ml milk
  • 60g butter, plus extra to serve
  • 4 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Pour the boiling water over the cornmeal and salt and stir. Loosen with the milk.
  2. Cook spoonfuls in butter on a griddle until crisp at the edges, 15 min.
  3. Serve with butter and maple syrup.

Mains

Warrens' New England fish bake

Cod fillets under a buttery cracker and lemon crust, with boiled potatoes.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Mix the crumb topping up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; press it on the cod and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cod fillets
  • 100g crackers, crushed
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 800g potatoes, boiled
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Lay the cod in a dish. Mix the crackers with melted butter, lemon zest and parsley and press on top.
  2. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes.
  3. Boil the potatoes until tender and cook the peas. Toss both with butter, 20 min.

Perron farmhouse Yankee pot roast

Chuck braised with onion, carrot and red wine, carved thick, for all seven at the table.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 brown onions
  • 4 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 250ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 800g potatoes
  1. Beef chuck seared dark and braised for hours with onion, carrot, celery and red wine, carved thick with potatoes, the braising gravy spooned over. Heat the oven to 160°C. Season a 1.5kg beef chuck roast and sear hard on all sides in a heavy pot. Add 2 onions in wedges, 4 carrots and 2 celery sticks in chunks and 2 garlic cloves. Pour in 250ml red wine and 500ml beef stock, add thyme and 2 bay leaves, cover and braise for 3 hours. Add 800g halved potatoes for the last hour. Lift out the meat and veg, reduce the liquid by a third and season for gravy.

Harrisville farmhouse apple crispVegetarian

Cinnamon apples under a buttery oat crumble topping.

Prep 18 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the topping up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; slice and toss the apples on the night and bake
Recipe for 4
  • 6 apples, sliced
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 100g cold butter, cubed
  • 80g plain flour
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 300ml pouring cream
  1. Toss the apples with cinnamon, sugar and lemon juice in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, oats and brown sugar and scatter over, 7 min.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 40 minutes.
  4. Serve with pouring cream.

Music box raspberry thumbprint shortbread

Buttery shortbread rounds with a thumbprint of raspberry jam.

How it is made
  • 200g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  • 250g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 1 jar raspberry jam
  • 1 bag icing sugar
  1. Buttery vanilla shortbread rounds with a thumbprint of raspberry jam baked in the middle, dusted with icing sugar and kept in a tin. Beat 200g soft butter with 100g caster sugar and 1 tsp vanilla until pale. Mix in 250g plain flour and 50g cornflour to a soft dough. Roll into 20 balls, flatten slightly on lined trays and press a hollow in each with your thumb. Fill each with half a teaspoon of raspberry jam. Bake at 170°C for 15 to 18 minutes until pale golden at the edges, cool on the tray and dust with icing sugar.

Rhode Island coffee cabinetNo alcohol

Coffee syrup blended with milk and ice cream.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml strong brewed coffee
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  • 50g dark chocolate, to grate
  1. Simmer the coffee with the sugar for 10 minutes until syrupy, then cool completely.
  2. Blend some of the syrup with the cold milk and ice cream until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour into chilled glasses, top with whipped cream and grate chocolate over each.

When to serve

Serve the mains when every clock in the house stops at 3:07.
