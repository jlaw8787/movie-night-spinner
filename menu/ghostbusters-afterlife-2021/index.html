Snacks

Summerville fried okraVegetarian

Cornmeal crusted fried okra rounds with hot sauce

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; slice the okra and mix the coating up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g okra, sliced into rounds
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Toss the okra in buttermilk.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, flour, salt and pepper and coat the okra.
  3. Fry in the oil at 180C for 3 minutes, in batches, until crisp.
  4. Serve with hot sauce.

Farmhouse pan cornbread, honey butterOn the printed menu

Corn and jalapeño batter in hot cast iron, flaky salt

How it is made
  • 200g fine polenta
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g butter
  • 1 tin corn kernels
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Golden cornbread baked in a hot cast iron pan with corn kernels and jalapeño, served warm in wedges with whipped honey butter and flaky salt. Heat the oven to 210C with a 25cm cast iron pan inside. Mix 200g fine polenta, 150g plain flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder and 1 tsp salt. Whisk 300ml buttermilk, 2 eggs and 80g melted butter, stir into the dry mix with 150g corn kernels and 1 chopped jalapeño. Add 20g butter to the hot pan, pour in the batter and bake 20 to 25 minutes. Beat 100g soft butter with 2 tbsp honey and serve with the warm wedges.

Mains

Ecto-1 double smash burgers

Smashed beef patties with American cheese, pickles and burger sauce, with fries

Prep 30 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Mix the burger sauce up to 3 days ahead in the fridge and shape the mince into balls up to 1 day ahead; fry the chips and smash the patties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sweet relish
  • 1kg crinkle-cut potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Fry the potatoes in the oil once at a gentle heat until soft, drain, then fry again at a higher heat until crisp.
  2. Shape the mince into balls. Mix the mayonnaise and relish for burger sauce.
  3. Smash the balls into thin patties on a smoking griddle and add cheese.
  4. Stack two patties per bun with pickles, onion and sauce.

Oklahoma chicken fried steak with gravyOn the printed menu

Tenderised steak fried crisp, peppery white gravy, mash and green beans

How it is made
  • 4 x 150g beef minute steaks
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  • 60g butter
  1. Tenderised beef steak dredged twice in seasoned flour, fried crunchy and smothered in peppery white country gravy, with mashed potato and green beans. Pound 4 x 150g thin beef minute steaks. Season 200g plain flour with salt, pepper and 1 tsp garlic powder. Dip the steaks in flour, then in 2 eggs beaten with 200ml buttermilk, then back in the flour. Shallow fry in 1cm oil at 180C for 3 minutes a side and drain. Pour off all but 3 tbsp fat, stir in 3 tbsp of the flour, whisk in 500ml milk and simmer until thick with lots of black pepper. Serve with mash and buttered beans.

Sweets

Mini marshmallow men

Marshmallows on skewers with chocolate faces, toasted if you like

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Stack and pipe up to 1 day ahead, airtight at room temperature; blowtorch half just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 large marshmallows
  • 12 bamboo skewers
  • 50g dark chocolate, melted
  1. Stack two marshmallows on each skewer.
  2. Pipe eyes and a smile on each with the melted chocolate.
  3. Toast half of them with a blowtorch for the full effect.

Toasted marshmallow pieVegetarian

Chocolate ganache in a cookie crust, topped with a tall marshmallow meringue, torched

Prep 25 min, cook 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Base and filling up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250g digestive biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 3 egg whites
  • 150g caster sugar
  1. Mix the crushed biscuits with the melted butter and press into a pie tin.
  2. Melt the chocolate with the cream, pour in and chill to set.
  3. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar over hot water, then beat stiff.
  4. Pile tall and torch until blistered.

Chocolate malt shakesVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream, milk, malt powder and chocolate sauce blended thick

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 6 tbsp chocolate sauce
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Blend the ice cream with the milk, malt, cocoa and some chocolate sauce until thick and smooth.
  2. Drizzle chocolate sauce inside tall chilled glasses.
  3. Pour in the shakes and top with whipped cream and a cherry.

When to serve

Serve the sweets when the tiny marshmallow men get loose in the supermarket.
