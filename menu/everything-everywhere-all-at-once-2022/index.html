Everything bagel bitesVegetarian

Mini bagels with spring onion cream cheese and everything seasoning.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Beat the cream cheese with the spring onions up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; toast and spread just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 mini bagels
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 3 spring onions
  • 2 tbsp everything bagel seasoning
  1. Halve and toast 8 mini bagels.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with the finely sliced spring onions.
  3. Spread thickly on each half and shower generously with the everything bagel seasoning.

Chinese New Year dumplings

Pan-fried then steamed, with black vinegar and chilli oil.

Prep 39 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat the dumplings up to 1 day ahead on a floured tray in the fridge; fry and steam just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 cabbage
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 3 tbsp black vinegar
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  • 125ml water
  1. Mix the pork mince with the finely chopped cabbage, the soy sauce and the sesame oil.
  2. Fill the dumpling wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Fry flat side down until golden, add the water, cover and steam 6 minutes.
  4. Serve with black vinegar and chilli oil.

Hot dog fingers in puff pastry

Thin frankfurts in cheddar puff strips, baked flaky, mustard to dunk, eaten with your feet.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 60g cheddar
  • 1 egg
  • sesame seeds
  • mustard
  • tomato sauce
  1. Long thin frankfurts wrapped in strips of puff pastry with cheddar, baked golden and flaky, with mustard and tomato sauce for dunking. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 2 thawed puff pastry sheets into 1.5cm strips. Halve 8 thin frankfurts lengthways if thick, or use 16 cocktail franks. Lay a little grated cheddar (60g total) along each strip and wind it around a frank. Brush with beaten egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and bake on lined trays for 15 to 18 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve with mustard and tomato sauce.

Hibachi steak and prawn fried rice

Seared sirloin and prawns with zucchini, egg fried rice, garlic butter.

Prep 11 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Cook and chill the rice the day before; sear the steak and prawns and fry the rice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 500g beef sirloin
  • 300g green prawns
  • 60g butter
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 2 eggs
  1. Cook the rice the day before.
  2. Sear the sliced sirloin and the peeled prawns on a smoking plate with the garlic butter and soy.
  3. Add sliced zucchini and onion.
  4. Fry the rice with the eggs, butter and soy alongside, and serve everything together.

Longevity noodles with chicken and shiitake

Long egg noodles wok tossed with thigh, bok choy and oyster sauce, never cut.

How it is made
  • 400g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • soy sauce
  • 450g fresh egg noodles
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 150g shiitake mushrooms
  • 3 baby bok choy
  • vegetable oil
  1. Long egg noodles stir-fried with chicken, shiitake, bok choy and oyster sauce, kept whole and never cut for a long life. Slice 400g chicken thigh and toss with 1 tsp cornflour and 1 tbsp soy. Soak 450g fresh long egg noodles in hot water 1 minute and drain. Mix 3 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp sesame oil. Stir-fry the chicken in a hot wok 4 minutes. Add 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp ginger and 150g sliced shiitake for 2 minutes, then 3 halved bok choy. Add noodles and sauce and toss 2 minutes without cutting.

Sesame ballsVegetarian

Fried glutinous rice balls coated in sesame, filled with red bean paste.

Prep 30 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Fill and shape the balls up to 4 hours ahead under a damp cloth; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g glutinous rice flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 200g red bean paste
  • 80g sesame seeds
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 200ml water
  1. Mix the glutinous rice flour with the sugar and the hot water into a soft dough.
  2. Wrap the balls around teaspoons of red bean paste, wet and roll in the sesame seeds.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 160°C for 6 minutes, pressing gently, until puffed and golden.

Bubble teaNo alcohol

Chilled black milk tea with brown sugar tapioca pearls.

Prep 4 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead; cook the pearls within 4 hours of serving, as they go hard in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 150g tapioca pearls
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 250ml milk
  • 810ml water
  1. Brew the black tea bags in the boiling water for 5 minutes and chill.
  2. Cook the tapioca pearls as the packet says and toss in the brown sugar melted with the water.
  3. Spoon pearls into glasses, add ice, the tea and the milk, and serve with wide straws.
