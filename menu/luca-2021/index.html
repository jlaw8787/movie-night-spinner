Snacks

Harbour zucchini frittersVegetarian

Zucchini ricotta fritters with parmesan, fried in olive oil

Prep 19 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Grate, squeeze and mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled. Fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 zucchini
  • 200g ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 80g plain flour
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  1. Grate the zucchini and squeeze dry.
  2. Mix with the ricotta, egg, parmesan and flour.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in olive oil until golden on both sides, in batches.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges.

Mains

Pesto pasta with potato and green beansVegetarian

Linguine tossed in basil pesto with potato and green beans

Prep 19 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Make the pesto up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled under a thin layer of olive oil. Boil the potatoes, linguine and beans on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g linguine
  • 2 bunches basil
  • 50g pine nuts
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 300g potatoes, sliced
  • 200g green beans, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp salt, for the cooking water
  1. Blitz the basil, pine nuts, garlic, parmesan and olive oil into pesto.
  2. Slice the potatoes and trim the beans.
  3. Boil the potatoes in water salted with the salt, then add the linguine and beans to the same pot.
  4. Drain and toss with the pesto.

Sweets

Portorosso gelato cupsVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream and lemon sorbet with crushed amaretti and lemon zest

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Scoop the ice cream and sorbet into the cups up to 2 hours ahead and keep in the freezer. Add the amaretti and zest just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml lemon sorbet
  • 8 amaretti biscuits
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Scoop the ice cream and sorbet side by side into small cups.
  2. Crush the amaretti over the top.
  3. Finish with a little lemon zest.

Lemon ricotta cakeVegetarian

Baked ricotta cake with lemon zest

Prep 16 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ricotta
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 lemons, zested
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Beat the ricotta, sugar, eggs and lemon zest until smooth.
  2. Fold in the flour.
  3. Pour into a lined tin.
  4. Bake at 170C until set.
  5. Cool the cake.
  6. Dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Portorosso lemon and basil sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Basil sugar syrup mixed with lemon and soda

Prep 15 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  • 5 lemons
  • 700ml cold soda water
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Simmer the sugar and water until dissolved.
  2. Add the basil, take off the heat and steep, then strain.
  3. Chill the syrup.
  4. Mix the syrup with the lemon juice.
  5. Fill glasses with ice, divide the lemon mix between them and top with soda water. Twist a strip of peel over each glass and add a small basil sprig.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the Portorosso Cup gets to the pasta-eating leg.
