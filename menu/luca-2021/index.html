Snacks

Harbour zucchini frittersVegetarian

Zucchini ricotta fritters with parmesan, fried in olive oil.

Prep 19 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Grate, squeeze and mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead and keep chilled. Fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 zucchini
  • 200g ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 80g plain flour
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  1. Grate the zucchini and squeeze dry.
  2. Mix with the ricotta, egg, parmesan and flour.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in olive oil until golden on both sides, in batches, 12 min.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges.

Vespa dream rosemary focaccia

Dough dimpled with oil, herbs and sea salt, baked golden, for boys saving for a scooter.

How it is made
  • 500g bread flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  1. Focaccia dough proved twice, dimpled with olive oil, rosemary and sea salt, baked at 220°C for 20 minutes until golden. Mix 500g bread flour, 7g dried yeast, 10g salt and 400ml lukewarm water into a wet dough and rest 30 minutes. Stretch and fold it 4 times over the next hour, then pour 60ml olive oil into a 30 x 20cm tin, tip in the dough and leave to rise 1 hour. Dimple all over with oiled fingers, push in rosemary sprigs, drizzle with more oil and sprinkle with sea salt. Bake at 220°C for 20 minutes until deep golden.

Mains

Pesto pasta with potato and green beansVegetarian

Linguine tossed in basil pesto with potato and green beans.

Prep 19 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Make the pesto up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled under a thin layer of olive oil. Boil the potatoes, linguine and beans on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g linguine
  • 2 bunches of basil
  • 50g pine nuts
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 300g potatoes, sliced
  • 200g green beans, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp salt, for the cooking water
  1. Blitz the basil, pine nuts, garlic, parmesan and olive oil into pesto.
  2. Slice the potatoes and trim the beans.
  3. Boil the potatoes in water salted with the salt, then add the linguine and beans to the same pot, 14 min.
  4. Drain and toss with the pesto.

Riviera fish, prawn and mussel stew

Seafood simmered in tomato, garlic and fish stock with fennel until the shells open, crusty bread.

How it is made
  • 1 onion
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 650ml fish stock
  • 500g firm white fish fillets
  • 8 green prawns
  • 500g mussels
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Fish, prawns and mussels simmered in a tomato, garlic and fish stock broth for 10 minutes until the shells open, with crusty bread. Soften 1 sliced onion, 1 sliced fennel bulb and 4 sliced garlic cloves in olive oil for 8 minutes. Add a pinch of chilli, a 400g tin crushed tomatoes and 650ml fish stock and simmer 15 minutes. Add 500g firm white fish in chunks and 8 green prawns, simmer 3 minutes, then add 500g cleaned mussels, cover and cook 4 to 5 minutes until the shells open. Throw away any that stay shut. Finish with parsley and serve with toasted crusty bread.

Portorosso gelato cupsVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream and lemon sorbet with crushed amaretti and lemon zest.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Scoop the ice cream and sorbet into the cups up to 2 hours ahead and keep in the freezer. Add the amaretti and zest just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml lemon sorbet
  • 8 amaretti biscuits
  • 1 lemon, zested
  1. Scoop the ice cream and sorbet side by side into small cups.
  2. Crush the amaretti over the top.
  3. Finish with a little lemon zest.

Lemon ricotta cakeVegetarian

Moist baked ricotta cake bright with lemon zest, dusted with icing sugar.

Prep 16 min, cook 45 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ricotta
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 lemons, zested
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Beat the ricotta, sugar, eggs and lemon zest until smooth.
  2. Fold in the flour.
  3. Pour into a lined tin.
  4. Bake at 170°C until set, 45 min.
  5. Cool the cake, 30 min.
  6. Dust with icing sugar.

Lemon and basil sodaNo alcohol

Basil sugar syrup mixed with lemon and soda.

Prep 11 min, cook 20 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  • 5 lemons
  • 700ml cold soda water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Simmer the sugar and water until dissolved, 5 min.
  2. Add the basil, take off the heat and steep, then strain, 15 min.
  3. Chill the syrup, 30 min.
  4. Mix the syrup with the lemon juice.
  5. Fill glasses with ice, divide the lemon mix between them and top with soda water. Twist a strip of peel over each glass and add a small basil sprig.
