Snacks

Spice-dusted flatbreadsVegetarian

Flatbreads baked crisp and dusted with hazelnut dukkah, labneh to dip

Prep 20 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Make the dukkah up to 1 week ahead, airtight; brush and bake the flatbreads just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 50g hazelnuts
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 250g labneh
  • salt, to taste
  1. Toast the hazelnuts and sesame seeds.
  2. Pound with cumin, cinnamon and salt to make dukkah.
  3. Brush the flatbreads with oil.
  4. Bake at 220C until blistered.
  5. Sprinkle with the dukkah and serve with labneh.

Arrakeen date platterVegetarian

Medjool dates stuffed with goat cheese and walnut, with dried apricots and figs

Prep 12 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Fill the dates up to 1 day ahead, covered and chilled; drizzle with honey and zest and add the dried fruit on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 12 medjool dates
  • 100g soft goat's cheese
  • 12 walnuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 orange
  • 100g dried apricots
  • 100g dried figs
  1. Split the dates and fill with goat's cheese and a walnut.
  2. Drizzle with honey and orange zest.
  3. Arrange on a board with the dried apricots and figs.

Mains

Desert lamb tagine

Lamb braised with ras el hanout, saffron, apricots and chickpeas, over couscous

Prep 25 min, cook 184 min · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg lamb shoulder, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp ras el hanout
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 500ml water
  • 150g dried apricots
  • 400g can chickpeas, drained
  • 300g couscous
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  1. Brown the lamb in batches.
  2. Soften the onions with ras el hanout and saffron.
  3. Add the water, apricots and chickpeas.
  4. Cook covered until the lamb is tender.
  5. Prepare the couscous and serve under the tagine, scattered with parsley.

Sweets

Sandworm cinnamon scrollsVegetarian

Soft cinnamon scrolls finished with a cardamom icing glaze

Prep 40 min, cook 77 min · Make ahead: Shape the scrolls up to 1 day ahead and chill overnight; bake and glaze on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 110g butter, divided
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, warm milk and some butter into a dough.
  2. Prove for an hour.
  3. Roll out and spread with butter, brown sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Roll up and cut thick spirals.
  5. Bake at 190C.
  6. Glaze with icing sugar and cardamom mixed with a little water.

Every drop counts orange blossom coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh orange and lemon juice with honey cardamom syrup and orange blossom water

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make and cool the cardamom honey syrup up to 3 days ahead, chilled; juice the oranges, mix and pour over ice on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 80ml water
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 4 oranges, plus 1 for slices
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/2 tsp orange blossom water
  • 600ml chilled still water
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the honey with water and cardamom, then strain.
  2. Cool the syrup and squeeze the oranges and lemon.
  3. Stir the juice, syrup and orange blossom water together in a jug.
  4. Add chilled water.
  5. Fill four glasses with ice, pour carefully and top each with mint and an orange slice.
