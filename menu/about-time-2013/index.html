Snacks

Cornish beach crab and cress sandwiches

Lemon crab mayo and cress on buttered soft white bread

Prep 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep covered and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g crab meat
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 30g butter
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 punnet of cress
  1. Mix the crab with mayonnaise, lemon zest and juice, cayenne and salt.
  2. Butter the bread slices.
  3. Spread the crab over half the slices, add thin cucumber slices and cress, then close.
  4. Press gently, trim the crusts and cut each sandwich into fingers.

Cornish beef and swede pasties

Shortcrust pasties of beef, potato, swede and onion, brown sauce

Prep 45 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Assemble a day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g rump steak
  • 1 potato
  • 100g swede
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 4 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 20g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle of brown sauce
  1. Finely dice the rump, potato, swede and onion and mix with salt and pepper.
  2. Cut rounds from the pastry sheets.
  3. Spoon filling onto one half of each, add a small knob of butter, fold over and crimp the edge.
  4. Brush with beaten egg and cut a small slit.
  5. Bake at 190C until golden.
  6. Serve with brown sauce.

Mains

Dinner-in-the-dark lamb shanks with mash

Red wine braised lamb shanks over buttery mashed potato

Prep 43 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lamb shanks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 250ml red wine
  • 400g crushed tomatoes (tin)
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch of rosemary
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  1. Dice the onion, carrots and celery.
  2. Brown the lamb shanks in oil.
  3. Add the onion, carrots, celery and garlic and cook.
  4. Add wine, crushed tomatoes, stock, rosemary and bay leaves.
  5. Cover and braise at 160C until the meat falls from the bone.
  6. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  7. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with butter and milk.
  8. Reduce the sauce on the stove and spoon over the shanks.

Sweets

Strawberry passionfruit Eton messVegetarian

Strawberries, passionfruit, whipped cream and crushed meringue

Prep 23 min · Make ahead: Fruit up to 2 hours ahead, assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g strawberries
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 passionfruit
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 6 meringue nests
  1. Slice the strawberries and toss with sugar and passionfruit pulp.
  2. Leave to macerate for 15 minutes.
  3. Whip the cream with vanilla to soft peaks.
  4. Roughly break the meringue nests.
  5. Gently fold the meringue, most of the strawberries and their juices through the cream so it streaks.
  6. Spoon into glasses and top with the rest of the berries.

Dad's ping pong Earl Grey iced teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Earl Grey with honey and lemon, topped with soda water over ice

Prep 14 min, cook 3 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 5 Earl Grey tea bags
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 lemons
  • 300ml soda water
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in boiling water with honey.
  2. Leave to steep for 5 minutes, then remove the bags.
  3. Cool, then chill for 1 hour.
  4. Fill tall glasses with ice and lemon slices.
  5. Pour in the tea to three-quarters full and top with a splash of soda water.
  6. Garnish with a lemon wheel.
