Grilled Hmong pork sausage with chilli dip

Lemongrass pork sausage and fiery jeow.

Prep 8 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Mix and shape the sausage up to a day ahead; make the dip up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork mince, not too lean
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, white part finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 3 long red chillies
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 cup coriander, chopped
  1. Mix the pork with the lemongrass, half the garlic, spring onion, fish sauce, chilli flakes and a little salt.
  2. Shape into thick logs on skewers and chill while the grill heats, 10 min.
  3. Char the whole chillies on the grill, then pound them with the rest of the garlic, lime juice, coriander and salt into a dip, 7 min.
  4. Grill the sausages, turning, until browned and cooked through, then slice and serve with the dip, 20 min.

Pounded green papaya salad

Shredded papaya pounded with chilli and lime, a barbecue side.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Shred the papaya up to a day ahead and keep it in cold water; pound and dress to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 green papaya, peeled and shredded
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 small red chillies
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 8 cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 100g snake beans, cut short
  • 30g roasted peanuts, crushed
  1. Pound the garlic, chillies and palm sugar in a mortar.
  2. Add the beans and tomatoes and bruise them lightly.
  3. Add the papaya, fish sauce and lime juice and pound and toss until it softens a little.
  4. Pile onto a plate and scatter with peanuts.

Lemongrass chicken skewers with sticky rice

Charred chicken with herbs and lime.

Prep 11 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to a day ahead; soak the rice overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, cut in strips
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400g glutinous rice, soaked overnight
  • 1 cup mint and coriander leaves
  • 2 limes
  • 12 bamboo skewers, soaked
  1. Steam the drained rice in a lined steamer until tender and sticky, 30 min.
  2. Mix the lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, sugar and oil and toss the chicken through.
  3. Thread onto skewers and grill over high heat, turning, until charred and cooked through, 15 min.
  4. Serve with the sticky rice, herbs and lime wedges.

Kowalski's kielbasa and kraut

Smoked kielbasa sausages with buttery sauerkraut and mustard.

Prep 3 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Warm the sauerkraut with the onion up to a day ahead; grill the kielbasa to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g smoked kielbasa sausage
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 500g jar sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 4 crusty rye rolls
  • 4 tbsp hot mustard
  1. Cook the onion slowly in the butter, then add the sauerkraut and caraway and warm through, 15 min.
  2. Score the kielbasa and grill, turning, until blistered and hot through, 10 min.
  3. Serve on the rolls with the kraut and mustard.

Coconut tapioca with pandan jellyVegetarian

Chilled coconut milk and green pandan jelly.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the jelly and tapioca up to 2 days ahead and chill; add the coconut milk to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 80g small tapioca pearls
  • 2 tsp agar agar powder
  • 500ml water
  • 1 tsp pandan extract
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  1. Boil the tapioca in plenty of water until clear, then rinse in cold water, 15 min.
  2. Boil the water with the agar, half the sugar and the pandan, pour into a tray and chill until set, then cut into strips, 10 min.
  3. Warm the coconut milk with the rest of the sugar and the salt until dissolved, then chill, 5 min.
  4. Spoon tapioca and jelly into glasses, add ice and pour over the coconut milk.
