Snacks

Oxford pub ploughman's

Aged cheddar, ham, pickled onions, chutney, apple and crusty bread

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Lay out the cheddar, ham, pickled onions and chutney up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; slice the apple and add the bread just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g aged cheddar
  • 150g leg ham, sliced
  • 1 jar of pickled onions
  • 4 tbsp tomato chutney
  • 1 green apple, sliced
  • 1 crusty loaf
  • 50g butter
  1. Lay out the cheddar, ham, pickled onions, chutney and apple on a board.
  2. Add thick slices of crusty bread and a dish of butter.

Ham and béchamel croquettes

Ham croquettes with a creamy centre, crumbed and fried

Prep 5 min, cook 28 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day uncrumbed, chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 80g plain flour
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 200g leg ham, chopped
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil for frying
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and whisk in the milk until very thick.
  2. Add the ham and spread out to cool.
  3. Chill overnight or at least a few hours until firm.
  4. Shape into logs and crumb in egg and panko.
  5. Fry in the oil until golden.

Argentine beef empanadas with chimichurriOn the printed menu

Erik's visit to the bar in Argentina

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar green olives
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. Golden baked pastry half-moons filled with cumin-spiced beef, onion, olives and egg, served with a sharp parsley and garlic chimichurri. Brown 500g beef mince with 1 diced onion, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp paprika, then add 50g chopped green olives and 1 chopped boiled egg and cool. Cut 12 rounds from 3 shortcrust sheets, fill, fold and crimp the edges. Brush with egg and bake at 200C for 20 minutes. For the chimichurri, mix a chopped bunch of parsley, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp chilli flakes, 3 tbsp red wine vinegar and 100ml olive oil.

Mains

Hellfire Club pepper steak

Seared rib eye steaks with crisp roast potatoes

Prep 24 min, cook 61 min · Make ahead: Parboil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and chill; roast them, sear the steaks and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 rib eye steaks, about 500g each
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tbsp crushed peppercorns
  • 60ml brandy
  • 200ml cream
  • 100ml beef stock
  1. Parboil the potatoes, then shake to rough up.
  2. Roast in hot oil at 220C until crisp.
  3. Season the steaks and sear in a very hot pan, basting with butter for the last minute.
  4. Rest the steaks.
  5. In the same pan add the peppercorns and brandy, then cream and stock, and simmer until thick.
  6. Slice and spoon the sauce over.

Xavier mansion lemon roast chickenOn the printed menu

Whole bird roasted golden with thyme, mustardy potato salad, buttered beans, for the young recruits

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 50g butter
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 lemons
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 300g green beans
  1. Whole chicken roasted with lemon and thyme until golden, carved with a mustardy potato salad and buttered green beans for the team. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with butter, salt and thyme, stuff with a halved lemon and roast at 200C for 75 minutes, then rest 10 minutes. Boil 1kg baby potatoes for 15 minutes, halve and toss warm with 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard, chives and a squeeze of lemon. Blanch 300g green beans for 3 minutes and toss with butter. Carve the chicken and pour over the pan juices.

Sweets

Chess game chocolate squaresVegetarian

Dark and white chocolate set with condensed milk, cut into squares

Prep 15 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 395g condensed milk
  • 40g butter
  1. Melt the dark chocolate with half the condensed milk and half the butter and set in a lined tray.
  2. Repeat with the white chocolate and the rest in another tray.
  3. Set in the fridge.
  4. Cut into squares and arrange like a chess board.

Drinks

Sixties gin martini with olivesVegetarian

Gin and dry vermouth stirred ice cold, served with olives

Prep 5 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Put the martini glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; stir the gin and vermouth with ice and strain to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 8 green olives
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Put the martini glasses in the freezer.
  2. Fill a jug with ice, add gin and vermouth and stir steadily until very cold.
  3. Strain into the frozen glasses and add a toothpick with two olives to each.
