Snacks

Green Place seed crackersVegetarian

Crisp oat and seed crackers with sunflower, pumpkin, sesame, linseed and chia

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 50g sunflower seeds
  • 50g pumpkin seeds
  • 50g sesame seeds
  • 30g linseeds
  • 30g chia seeds
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 250ml water
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix all the seeds, oats and salt with the water.
  2. Rest for 30 minutes so the chia and linseeds thicken it.
  3. Spread thin on a lined tray.
  4. Bake at 160°C for 1 hour, until crisp. Break into shards.

Wasteland lizard skewers

Chicken thigh strips with smoked paprika and cumin, grilled until blackened

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Chicken can marinate up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the chicken into strips and rub with the paprika, cumin and chilli. Thread onto skewers.
  2. Grill hot until blackened at the edges.
  3. Mix the yoghurt with the garlic and salt for a dip.

War Boy chrome butter popcornOn the printed menu

Tossed in brown butter, smoked salt and chilli, dusted with edible silver lustre, witness me

How it is made
  • 150g popping corn
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar smoked salt
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 jar edible silver lustre
  1. Popcorn tossed in brown butter, smoked salt and a little chilli, dusted with edible silver lustre so it shines like a War Boy's grin. Pop 150g popping corn in 2 tbsp oil in a covered pot. Melt 60g butter and cook until nut brown. Toss the popcorn with the brown butter, 1 tsp smoked salt and a pinch of chilli flakes. Spread on a tray, cool for 5 minutes and dust lightly with 1 tsp edible silver lustre, tossing to coat.

Mains

Harissa lamb shoulder with hummus

Slow roast harissa lamb shoulder pulled apart, hummus, charred peppers, flatbread

Prep 18 min, cook 5.5 hours · Make ahead: Lamb up to 1 day ahead, reheat covered
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg lamb shoulder
  • 3 tbsp harissa
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 2 red capsicums
  • 6 flatbreads
  • salt, to taste
  1. Rub the lamb with the harissa, garlic and cumin.
  2. Roast covered at 150°C for 5 hours until it pulls apart.
  3. Blitz the chickpeas with the tahini, a splash of water and salt into hummus.
  4. Char the capsicums over a flame, peel and slice.
  5. Blister the flatbreads in a hot pan and serve everything together.

Gas Town smoky beef and black bean stewOn the printed menu

Chuck braised in chipotle and smoky paprika with beans, charred damper, what a lovely day

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 tin chipotles in adobo
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tins black beans
  • 400g self-raising flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 lime
  1. Beef chuck braised dark and thick with smoked paprika, chipotle, tomato and black beans, with charred damper and lime sour cream. Brown 1.2kg diced beef chuck in batches. Soften 2 onions and 4 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 chopped chipotle in adobo, 400g crushed tomatoes and 500ml beef stock. Return the beef, cover and braise at 160°C for 2.5 hours, then add 2 drained tins black beans and cook 30 minutes more. Mix 400g self-raising flour with 250ml milk and salt, shape and bake at 200°C for 30 minutes. Mix sour cream with lime.

Sweets

Desert date barsVegetarian

Date, oat and almond bars topped with dark chocolate and sea salt

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g medjool dates, pitted
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 100g almonds
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  1. Blitz the dates, oats, almonds and honey until sticky.
  2. Press firmly into a lined tray.
  3. Melt the chocolate and pour over. Sprinkle with sea salt.
  4. Chill until set, then cut into bars.

Drinks

Aqua Cola cucumber lime coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Cucumber, lime and mint muddled with sugar, topped up and served over ice

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Slice the cucumber and limes up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled. Muddle, add ice and top with sparkling water just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 3 limes, sliced
  • 1 handful mint
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2l sparkling water
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Muddle the cucumber, limes, mint and sugar in two big jugs until fragrant.
  2. Fill with ice and top with cold sparkling water.
  3. Stir and pour freely for everyone.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Immortan Joe opens the water for the crowd below.
