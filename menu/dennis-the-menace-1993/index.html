Snacks

Night-blooming flower canapés

Cucumber rounds with cream cheese and smoked salmon, egg salad fingers on white bread.

Prep 33 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled and covered
Recipe for 4
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp dill sprigs
  • 4 eggs
  • salt, to taste
  • 8 slices of white bread
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, then cool and peel.
  2. Slice the cucumbers into thick rounds and top each with cream cheese, a fold of salmon and a sprig of dill.
  3. Mash the eggs with salt and spread on the bread. Cut off the crusts and slice into fingers.

Slingshot gruyère puffs with honey chilli

Crisp choux with cheese and pepper, baked hollow, chilli drizzle, flaky salt, pocket-sized ammo.

How it is made
  • 150g plain flour
  • 100g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 100g gruyère
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  1. Crisp little choux puffs loaded with gruyère and cracked pepper, baked hollow and hit with a drizzle of chilli honey and flaky salt. Pocket-sized ammo. Bring 250ml water and 100g butter to the boil, beat in 150g plain flour until it leaves the sides, then cool 5 minutes. Beat in 4 eggs one at a time, then 100g grated gruyère and cracked pepper. Pipe 30 small balls onto lined trays and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until puffed and hollow. Warm 3 tbsp honey with a pinch of chilli flakes, drizzle over and finish with sea salt.

Mains

Lamb chops with mint sauce

Grilled rosemary lamb chops, mashed potatoes, peas and mint sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 lamb loin chops
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 800g potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 40g butter
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 4 tbsp mint sauce
  1. Rub the chops with rosemary, garlic and salt.
  2. Boil the potatoes until soft, drain and mash with the butter, 20 min.
  3. Grill the chops, 3 minutes a side, then rest. Cook the peas.
  4. Serve everything with a spoon of mint sauce, in peace, with no interruptions at all.

Switchblade Sam's campfire sausages

Pork sausages charred over the fire with sticky onions and mustard mayo, campfire style.

How it is made
  • 3 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 8 thick pork sausages
  • 8 long rolls
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Thick pork snags charred over the fire, in toasted rolls with sticky caramelised onions, smoky mustard mayo and crunchy pickles. Slice 3 onions and cook slowly in 30g butter with 1 tbsp brown sugar for 25 minutes until sticky and dark. Mix 4 tbsp mayonnaise with 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard and half a tsp smoked paprika. Grill 8 thick pork sausages over medium coals or a barbecue for 15 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through. Toast 8 long rolls, spread with the mayo and fill with sausages, onions and sliced dill pickles.

Mrs Wilson's cookiesVegetarian

Oat cookies with dark chocolate chips, golden at the edges.

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days chilled, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 egg
  • 150g plain flour
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 150g dark chocolate chips
  1. Cream the butter, sugar and vanilla, then beat in the egg. Fold in the flour, oats and chocolate chips.
  2. Roll into balls and bake at 180°C until golden at the edges, 12 min.
  3. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes.

Apple pieVegetarian

Lattice-top apple pie with cinnamon, served warm.

Prep 15 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 6 green apples, peeled and sliced
  • 50g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Cook the apples with the butter, sugar and cinnamon until just soft, then cool, 10 min.
  2. Line a pie dish with 2 sheets of pastry, fill, and cut the last sheet into strips for a lattice.
  3. Bake at 190°C for 45 minutes. Serve warm with ice cream.

Drinks

Backyard lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh lemonade over ice with a halved strawberry in each glass.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, mix fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml hot water
  • 6 lemons, juiced
  • 1.5 L cold water
  • 4 strawberries, halved
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool, 15 min.
  2. Juice the lemons into a jug, add the syrup and cold water and stir.
  3. Pour into tall glasses over ice and drop a halved strawberry into each.
