Snacks

Fig prosciutto, olive and cheddar picks

Prosciutto wrapped figs, warm orange olives and cheddar shards on toothpicks

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 figs, halved
  • 100g prosciutto
  • 200g olives
  • 1 orange, peel in strips
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 150g vintage cheddar
  1. Wrap each fig half in a piece of prosciutto.
  2. Warm the olives in a small pan with the orange peel and fennel seeds until fragrant.
  3. Break the cheddar into shards and push everything onto toothpicks so nobody gets greasy fingers on the cards.

Vesper's train dinner saladVegetarian

Shaved fennel, orange segments and toasted hazelnuts on butter lettuce, Dijon dressing

Prep 20 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Toast the hazelnuts and whisk the dressing up to 1 day ahead, nuts airtight and dressing chilled; cut the fennel and orange and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 60g hazelnuts
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 butter lettuce
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Toast the hazelnuts at 170C for 8 minutes, cool and roughly chop.
  2. Shave the fennel paper thin and cut the oranges into segments.
  3. Whisk the mustard, vinegar and oil into a dressing and season with salt and pepper. Toss the lettuce, fennel, orange and nuts together with the dressing at the last second.

Mains

Balkan grilled lamb

Garlic rosemary lamb chops grilled hot, roasted red peppers, garlic yoghurt

Prep 20 min, cook 765 min · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg lamb shoulder chops
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 red capsicums
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • salt, to taste
  1. Crush 4 of the garlic cloves and mix with the chopped rosemary, lemon juice and zest and the oil. Rub over the lamb.
  2. Cover and marinate in the fridge overnight.
  3. Roast the capsicums at 220C until blistered, then peel and slice.
  4. Grill the lamb hot until charred outside and pink inside.
  5. Crush the last garlic clove into the yoghurt with a pinch of salt. Serve with the lamb and capsicums.

Chocolate fondantVegetarian

Warm chocolate cakes, molten centre. Nothing sinister.

Prep 20 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 125g dark chocolate
  • 140g butter, plus extra for the moulds
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 30g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp cocoa, for dusting the moulds
  1. Butter 4 moulds and dust with cocoa. Melt the chocolate and butter together and let it cool a little.
  2. Whisk the eggs, yolks and sugar until pale, then combine with the chocolate and fold in the flour.
  3. Divide between the moulds and bake at 220C for 9 minutes.
  4. Turn out straight away so the centres run.

The Vesper gin and vodka martiniVegetarian

Shaken with white vermouth. Once you've tasted it, that's all you want to drink.

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best shaken to order; keep the spirits and coupes in the freezer up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 80ml vodka
  • 40ml white vermouth
  • 1 lemon, peel in long strips
  • 1 cup ice
  1. Shake the gin, vodka and vermouth hard with ice until very cold, in batches if needed.
  2. Strain into deep chilled coupes and add a long strip of lemon peel to each.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Bond sends for the barman at the poker table.
