Leo's office liver pâté toasts

Chicken livers flamed with brandy, blitzed with butter and thyme on toast, what's the rumpus

Prep 9 min, cook 14 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Pâté up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g chicken livers
  • 30g butter, for searing
  • 2 shallots, chopped
  • 3 sprigs thyme, leaves picked
  • 40ml brandy
  • 150g soft butter
  • 50g clarified butter, melted
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Trim the livers and sear in butter for 2 minutes a side until still pink.
  2. Add the shallots and thyme, then the brandy, and let it bubble off.
  3. Blitz with the soft butter until silky, season and press into a dish.
  4. Pour over the clarified butter and chill for 4 hours.
  5. Toast the rye and serve with the pâté.

Johnny Caspar's cheese strawsVegetarian

Puff pastry twists with aged cheddar, parmesan and cayenne, for a gripe about a fixed fight

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Cut and twist up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 80g aged cheddar, grated
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Brush one sheet of pastry with egg and scatter with cheddar, parmesan and cayenne.
  2. Lay the other sheet on top and press down. Cut into 1.5cm strips and twist each.
  3. Bake at 220C for 12 minutes until puffed and golden.

Leo's slow-cooked Irish stew

Leo's house, stewed low and slow

Prep 26 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days ahead, better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg lamb neck chops
  • 2 brown onions, sliced
  • 3 carrots, in chunks
  • 800g potatoes, in thick slices
  • 375ml stout
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the lamb in batches in oil.
  2. Layer in a heavy pot with the onions, carrots and potatoes.
  3. Pour over the stout and stock, cover and braise at 160C for 2 hours until tender.
  4. Finish with parsley.

Whisky bread puddingVegetarian

Brioche and whiskey-soaked raisins in custard, look in your heart

Prep 12 min, cook 40 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 80g raisins
  • 60ml Irish whiskey
  • 8 slices stale brioche
  • 40g butter, soft
  • 4 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 200ml cream
  • 80g caster sugar
  1. Soak the raisins in the whiskey for 30 minutes.
  2. Butter the brioche and layer in a dish with the raisins.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream and sugar and pour over.
  4. Rest for 30 minutes.
  5. Bake at 170C until puffed and golden.

Tom Reagan's hot Irish whiskey toddyVegetarian

Tom never puts his glass down, whatever the weather

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Cut and stud the lemon wheels up to 1 day ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; build each glass with the hot water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml water
  • 4 lemons
  • 12 whole cloves
  • 180ml Irish whiskey
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  1. Boil the water. Cut four lemon wheels and stud each with cloves.
  2. Into four heatproof glasses put whiskey, honey and lemon juice.
  3. Top with the hot water and stir until the honey dissolves.
  4. Float a studded wheel on top, add a cinnamon stick and serve hot.

When to serve

Plate up when Tom walks Bernie into the woods at Miller's Crossing.
