Snacks

Pentagon kitchen tomato soup and toasties

Roasted tomato soup with toasted sandwiches for dunking.

Prep 10 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Soup up to 2 days, toasties fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg tomatoes, halved
  • 1 brown onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 50ml cream
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter
  • 160g cheddar, grated
  1. Toss the tomatoes, onion and garlic in oil.
  2. Roast at 200°C until soft and browned.
  3. Blend with the stock, sugar and salt, then reheat and stir in the cream.
  4. Butter the bread, then make cheddar toasties, cooking each side in a frying pan.
  5. Cut into fingers and serve with the soup.

Gruyère cheese puffs (gougères)Vegetarian

Baked choux puffs with gruyère and cracked pepper.

Prep 15 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 100g gruyère, grated
  • 1/2 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Bring the water and butter to the boil and beat in the flour until smooth.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the gruyère and pepper.
  3. Pipe small balls onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.

Mains

Xavier mansion beef bourguignon

Beef braised in red wine with bacon and mushrooms.

Prep 18 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 250g mushrooms
  • 750ml red wine
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  1. Brown the beef and bacon.
  2. Add the mushrooms and cook until golden.
  3. Pour in the red wine.
  4. Simmer gently until tender and glossy.
  5. Boil and mash the potatoes with plenty of butter and serve under the beef.

White House lawn burgers and potato salad

Chargrilled beef patties with cheddar, tomato and pickles, creamy mustard potatoes with spring onion.

How it is made
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 600g beef mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Chargrilled beef burgers with cheddar, tomato and pickles in toasted buns, with a creamy mustard and spring onion potato salad. Boil 1kg halved baby potatoes for 15 minutes, drain and cool. Toss with 4 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, 3 sliced spring onions and salt. Shape 600g beef mince into 4 patties and season. Grill over high heat for 4 minutes a side, adding a slice of cheddar for the last minute. Toast 4 buns and fill with lettuce, tomato, pickles, the patties and tomato sauce.

Sweets

Fondue au chocolat

Melted dark chocolate and cream for dipping strawberries, marshmallows and sponge.

Prep 2 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Hull the strawberries and cube the cake up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; melt the chocolate and cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 250ml cream
  • 250g strawberries
  • 100g marshmallows
  • 1/2 sponge cake, cubed
  1. Melt the chocolate gently with the cream in a fondue pot or small pan.
  2. Keep warm at the table.
  3. Set out strawberries, marshmallows and cake for dipping on forks.

Drinks

Mystique blue margarita

Tequila, blue curaçao and lime shaken, with a salted rim.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the tequila, blue curacao, lime juice and sugar syrup up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; salt the rims and shake each drink with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 80ml blue curacao
  • 5 limes
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 2 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Rub the rims of the glasses with lime and dip in salt.
  2. For each drink, measure 45ml tequila, 20ml blue curacao, the juice of 1 lime and 10ml sugar syrup into a shaker.
  3. Shake each drink hard with ice for 10 seconds, then strain over fresh ice.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel.
