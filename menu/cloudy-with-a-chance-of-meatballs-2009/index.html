Snacks

Swallow Falls sardine toast

Mashed sardines with lemon and parsley on sourdough toast, sliced radish.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mash the sardine spread up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; toast the sourdough and slice the radish on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tins of sardines
  • 30g soft butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 8 slices of sourdough
  • 1 bunch radishes
  1. Toast the sourdough, 5 min.
  2. Mash the drained sardines with the butter, lemon juice and chopped parsley.
  3. Spread on the toast and top with thinly sliced radish and black pepper, 5 min.

Cheeseburger rain sliders

Smashed beef patties with American cheese, pickles and onion on slider buns.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls and mix the burger sauce up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; smash and cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 12 slices of American cheese
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 brown onion
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Roll the mince into 12 small balls.
  2. Smash on a hot griddle and cook briefly, then melt a slice of cheese on each, 10 min.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise into a burger sauce and slice the pickles and onion.
  4. Fill the buns and stack them high on a platter.

Mains

Giant meatballs and spaghetti

Big beef and pork meatballs simmered in tomato sauce over spaghetti.

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Sauce and meatballs up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 slices of white bread
  • 80g parmesan
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 2 tins of crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 500g spaghetti
  1. Soak the bread in a little water and squeeze it out. Mix with the minces, half the parmesan and parsley, 10 min.
  2. Roll into big balls and brown all over, 15 min.
  3. Simmer in the tomatoes with basil, 45 min.
  4. Cook the spaghetti, 10 min.
  5. Pile over the pasta and top with the rest of the parmesan, 5 min.

Sweets

Ice cream snow dayVegetarian

Vanilla, chocolate and strawberry ice cream with sprinkles, wafers and hot fudge.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fudge up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml chocolate ice cream
  • 500ml strawberry ice cream
  • 1 jar sprinkles
  • 1 packet wafer biscuits
  1. Melt the chocolate gently with the cream for hot fudge, 5 min.
  2. Scoop the ice creams into a big mountain on a platter.
  3. Cover with sprinkles, stick in wafers and pour over the hot fudge.

Jelly palace rainbow fruit cubes

Wobbly orange, raspberry and lime blocks with cream turrets, for Flint and Sam's date.

How it is made
  • 1 tin gelatine powder
  • 300ml orange juice
  • 300ml raspberry juice
  • 1 bottle lime cordial
  • 1 packet caster sugar
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Wobbly cubes of orange, raspberry and lime jelly made with real juice, stacked into a castle on a platter with whipped cream turrets. For each colour, soften 4 tsp gelatine in 3 tbsp cold water, then dissolve in 300ml warm juice with 2 tbsp sugar: orange juice, raspberry juice and lime cordial diluted with water. Pour each into its own lined loaf tin and chill 4 hours until firm. Cut into 3cm cubes and stack into a castle on a platter. Pipe whipped cream from 200ml cream on top.

Drinks

Rainbow lemonadeNo alcohol

Raspberry, orange and lime syrups layered under lemonade.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Blitz and strain the raspberry layer and mix the orange and lime layers up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; layer in the glasses and top with lemonade just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g frozen raspberries
  • 200ml orange juice
  • 2 limes
  • 1 L lemonade
  • 100g caster sugar
  1. Blitz the raspberries with a third of the sugar and strain.
  2. Mix the orange juice with a third of the sugar.
  3. Mix the lime juice with the rest of the sugar until dissolved, 5 min.
  4. Spoon a layer of each into four tall glasses and top slowly with lemonade so the colours run together.
