Snacks

Aunt Voula's tiropita trianglesVegetarian

Crisp buttered filo filled with feta, ricotta, egg and mint, for the whole Portokalos clan

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Fold and freeze the unbaked triangles on a tray up to 1 month ahead; bake from frozen for 5 minutes longer
Recipe for 4
  • 200g feta, crumbled
  • 100g ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp mint, chopped
  • black pepper, to taste
  • 8 sheets filo pastry
  • 100g butter, melted
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C and line 2 trays. Mix the feta, ricotta, egg, mint and a good grind of pepper.
  2. Lay out 1 filo sheet, brush with butter, lay a second on top and brush again. Cut lengthways into 3 strips. Repeat with the rest.
  3. Put a heaped teaspoon of filling at the end of each strip and fold over and over into a triangle. Brush the tops with butter.
  4. Bake until crisp and golden.
  5. Cool for 5 minutes before serving.

Dancing Zorba's keftedes with tzatziki

Fried beef balls with mint and oregano, cool garlic cucumber yoghurt and lemon

Prep 28 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Roll the keftedes up to 1 day ahead and chill on a tray; make the tzatziki up to 2 days ahead; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 garlic clove, grated, for the tzatziki
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, plus wedges to serve
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 small brown onion, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 50g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp mint, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 50g plain flour
  • 125ml olive oil, for frying
  1. Mix the yoghurt, cucumber, grated garlic, lemon juice and dill for the tzatziki and chill.
  2. Mix the beef mince, onion, crushed garlic, breadcrumbs, milk, egg, mint and oregano with salt and pepper.
  3. Roll into walnut-sized balls and roll them in the flour.
  4. Fry in batches in the oil over medium high heat, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  5. Drain on paper towel and serve hot with the tzatziki and lemon wedges.

Mains

Gus's front-yard lamb with lemon potatoes

Butterflied leg in lemon, garlic and oregano, charred, with roast lemon potatoes

Prep 5 min, cook 75 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the lamb up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; cut the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead and keep in cold water
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg butterflied lamb leg
  • 80ml olive oil, for the lamb
  • 2 lemons, juiced, for the lamb
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed, for the lamb
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano, for the lamb
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1.2kg potatoes, in thick wedges
  • 60ml olive oil, for the potatoes
  • 2 lemons, juiced, for the potatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced, for the potatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, for the potatoes
  • 250ml chicken stock
  1. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano and salt for the lamb, rub all over the lamb and leave to marinate.
  2. Marinate in the fridge, then bring back to room temperature.
  3. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the potatoes in a roasting tin with the potato oil, lemon juice, garlic and oregano and pour in the stock.
  4. Roast the potatoes, turning twice, until golden and the liquid has gone.
  5. Meanwhile heat a barbecue to high and cook the lamb for 10 to 12 minutes a side until charred outside and pink inside.
  6. Rest the lamb under foil for 15 minutes.
  7. Carve thickly and serve with the lemon potatoes and the pan juices.

Nick's chicken souvlaki and village salad

Lemon oregano chicken skewers, pita and horiatiki

Prep 17 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken overnight and make the tzatziki up to 2 days ahead, both chilled; grill and make the salad on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets, in chunks
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 8 metal or soaked bamboo skewers
  • 4 pita breads
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 80g kalamata olives
  • 150g feta
  1. Marinate the chicken in the juice of a lemon, 3 tbsp of the oil, the garlic, half the oregano and salt for at least 1 hour.
  2. Thread onto skewers and grill 10 to 12 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through.
  3. Grate half the cucumber, squeeze it dry and stir into the yoghurt with a squeeze of lemon and salt.
  4. Cut the rest of the cucumber, the tomatoes, onion and capsicum into chunks, add the olives, top with the feta and dress with the last oil and oregano.
  5. Warm the pitas on the grill and serve with the skewers, tzatziki and salad.

Harriet's honey walnut ring cakeVegetarian

Nut, orange and cinnamon butter sponge soaked in sticky citrus syrup

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and soak the cake up to 3 days ahead and keep it covered at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar, for the cake
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 oranges, 1 zested and both juiced
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g walnuts, chopped
  • 125g Greek yoghurt
  • 200g caster sugar, for the syrup
  • 200ml water, for the syrup
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and butter and flour a 24cm Bundt tin.
  2. Beat the butter and cake sugar until pale and fluffy, then beat in the eggs one at a time with the orange zest.
  3. Fold in the flour, cinnamon, walnuts and yoghurt and spoon into the tin.
  4. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Meanwhile simmer the syrup sugar, water, honey, orange juice and cinnamon stick for 5 minutes, then cool.
  6. Cool the cake in the tin for 10 minutes, turn out onto a plate and spoon the cool syrup slowly over the hot cake.
  7. Leave to soak before slicing.

Drinks

Toula's iced coffee frappeVegetarianNo alcohol

Instant coffee shaken to a thick foam, poured over ice and topped with cold milk

Prep 7 min · Make ahead: Chill the milk and glasses up to 1 day ahead; shake the coffee foam to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp instant coffee
  • 4 tsp caster sugar
  • 120ml cold water
  • 4 cups ice
  • 400ml cold milk
  1. Shake the coffee, sugar and cold water hard in a sealed jar until it turns into a thick, pale foam.
  2. Fill 4 tall glasses with the ice.
  3. Spoon the coffee foam over the ice.
  4. Top slowly with the cold milk and serve with a straw.

When to serve
