Snacks

Minion banana bitesVegetarian

Banana coins dipped in honey yoghurt, rolled in peanuts or sprinkles, frozen.

Prep 16 min, cook 4 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 3 bananas
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 60g roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 2 tbsp yellow sprinkles
  1. Line a tray with baking paper and slice the bananas into thick coins.
  2. Mix the yoghurt and honey.
  3. Dip each coin, then roll half in crushed peanuts and half in sprinkles.
  4. Freeze on the tray until firm.
  5. Serve straight from the freezer.

Super Silly Fun Land corn dogs

Cornmeal franks, fried golden. Win Agnes a fluffy unicorn.

Prep 8 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Batter can be made 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 wooden sticks
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • mustard, to serve
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, egg and buttermilk into a thick batter in a tall glass.
  2. Pat the beef frankfurts dry and push each onto a stick.
  3. Heat the oil to 180°C in a deep pot.
  4. Dip each frank in batter and fry a few at a time until golden.
  5. Drain and serve with mustard and tomato sauce.

Meatballs in tomato sauce

Beef meatballs in tomato sauce, shrunk from the Moon.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 50g breadcrumbs, soaked in milk
  • 40g parmesan, grated, for the meatballs
  • 1 handful of parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tins of crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 handful of basil leaves
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 40g parmesan, grated, for serving
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Mix both minces with the soaked breadcrumbs, parmesan, parsley and seasoning.
  2. Roll into balls and brown in the oil in batches.
  3. Add the garlic, tomatoes and basil to the pan and simmer the meatballs gently.
  4. Cook the spaghetti in salted water while the sauce finishes.
  5. Toss the pasta with the sauce and serve with extra parmesan.

Gru's lab crumbed chicken tendersOn the printed menu

Parmesan-crumbed chicken tenders with honey mustard. Vector stole the shrink ray, not these.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken tenderloins
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko crumbs
  • 40g parmesan
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  1. Chicken tenderloins in a crunchy parmesan and panko crumb, baked golden, with honey mustard, tomato sauce and a crisp potato wedge pile. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut 1kg potatoes into wedges, toss with oil, paprika and salt, and roast for 35 minutes. Dip 800g chicken tenderloins in flour, then 2 beaten eggs, then 150g panko mixed with 40g grated parmesan. Lay on an oiled tray, spray with oil and bake for 15 to 18 minutes, turning once, until golden and cooked. Mix 3 tbsp Dijon with 3 tbsp honey and serve with tomato sauce.

Agnes's so fluffy unicorn cakeVegetarian

Vanilla layer cake, buttercream. The girls' piggy bank funds the Moon heist.

Prep 40 min, cook 30 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, undecorated layers can be made earlier
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 250g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 450g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp pastel food colouring
  • 1 piece of gold fondant
  1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line two cake tins.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and milk.
  3. Divide between the tins and bake until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Cool the cakes completely.
  5. Beat the icing butter with the icing sugar and vanilla, split and colour in pastel shades.
  6. Sandwich and cover the cake, pipe a mane down one side and add a fondant horn.

Door-to-door chewy coconut cookiesOn the printed menu

Chewy coconut cookies, baked by the cookie robots.

How it is made
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle vanilla extract
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 120g desiccated coconut
  • 150g dark chocolate
  1. Chewy coconut cookies with golden edges, half dipped in dark chocolate and packed into little boxes just like the girls sell. Heat the oven to 170°C. Beat 125g soft butter with 150g brown sugar until creamy, then beat in 1 egg and 1 tsp vanilla. Mix in 150g plain flour, half a teaspoon of bicarb and 120g desiccated coconut. Roll into 20 balls, flatten slightly on lined trays and bake for 12 minutes until golden at the edges. Cool, then dip half of each in 150g melted dark chocolate and set on paper.

Warm milk, honey, cinnamon and vanilla.

Prep 3 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best served fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Warm the milk gently with the honey, cinnamon stick and vanilla until steaming, never boiling.
  2. Remove the cinnamon and pour into four mugs.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Gru takes the girls to the theme park.
