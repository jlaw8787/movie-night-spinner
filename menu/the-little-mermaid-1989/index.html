Snacks

Dinglehopper garlic breadVegetarian

Garlic parsley butter baguette baked in foil, cut in chunks with a fork stuck in

Prep 11 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Wrap up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  1. Mash the butter with crushed garlic and chopped parsley.
  2. Split the baguette, spread, and wrap in foil.
  3. Bake at 200C, then cut into chunks and stick a fork in each.

Seashell pasta saladVegetarian

Pasta shells with peas, cherry tomatoes, basil and lemon, served cold

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pasta shells
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cook the pasta shells al dente, drain and cool.
  2. Blanch the peas and halve the tomatoes.
  3. Toss everything with torn basil, lemon juice and olive oil. Season and serve cold.

Grotto prawn cocktail cupsOn the printed menu

Ariel's grotto, Sebastian safely off the menu

How it is made
  • 500g green prawns
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 jar paprika
  1. Poached prawns piled over crisp iceberg and avocado with a pink Marie Rose sauce, lemon and a sprinkle of paprika, served in glass cups. Poach 500g green prawns in simmering salted water for 2 minutes, plunge into iced water, then peel. Mix 120g mayonnaise, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 tsp Worcestershire, a squeeze of lemon and a dash of hot sauce. Shred half an iceberg and dice 1 avocado into 4 glasses. Pile on the prawns, spoon over the sauce and dust with paprika. Serve with lemon wedges.

Mains

Eric's castle chicken with tarragon cream

Seared chicken breast in a shallot, lemon and tarragon cream sauce

Prep 8 min, cook 44 min · Make ahead: Season the chicken and chop the shallot up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; cook everything on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 shallot
  • 50g butter
  • 150ml chicken stock
  • 1 lemon
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp chopped tarragon
  • 800g new potatoes
  • 300g green beans
  1. Season the chicken and chop the shallot.
  2. Sear the chicken in oil, then finish in a 200C oven and rest.
  3. In the same pan cook the shallot in some of the butter, add the stock and lemon juice and reduce by half.
  4. Add the cream and tarragon and simmer.
  5. Boil the potatoes and toss in the rest of the butter. Steam the beans.

Chef Louis's snapper meunièreOn the printed menu

Chef Louis's kitchen, fish only, no crab

How it is made
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 60g butter
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Snapper fillets dusted in flour and pan-fried until the skin crackles, finished in nutty brown butter with lemon, capers and parsley, with crushed potatoes. Boil 800g baby potatoes until tender, crush lightly with olive oil and salt. Pat 4 snapper fillets dry, season and dust the flesh in flour. Fry skin side down in 2 tbsp oil for 4 minutes until crisp, flip for 1 minute and lift out. Add 60g butter to the pan and cook until nut brown, then add 1 tbsp capers, the juice of 1 lemon and chopped parsley. Spoon over the fish.

Sweets

Sea-glass jelly cups

Blue jelly with gummy fish, whipped cream, shortbread sand, white chocolate shell

Prep 18 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add toppings just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 packets blue jelly crystals
  • 1 bag gummy fish
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 packet shortbread
  • 50g white chocolate
  1. Make the jelly as the packet says and pour half into four glasses.
  2. Chill until just setting, push in gummy fish, add the rest and set.
  3. Whip the cream and crush the shortbread.
  4. Top with cream, shortbread for sand and a white chocolate shell.

Lagoon punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Pineapple juice, coconut water and lemonade with a splash of blue curaçao syrup

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Mix the pineapple juice and coconut water up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice, lemonade and syrup just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml coconut water
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 2 tbsp blue curaçao syrup
  • ice, to serve
  1. Mix the pineapple juice and coconut water in a jug with ice.
  2. Top with lemonade and swirl in the curaçao syrup. Pour into four glasses.
