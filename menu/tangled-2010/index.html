Snacks

Pork and sage sausage rolls

Puff pastry rolls of pork mince, onion and sage.

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1/4 cup sage, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp English mustard
  1. Mix the pork with the onion, sage and salt.
  2. Halve the pastry sheets and lay the filling along each piece, then roll up and seal.
  3. Brush with beaten egg and cut into pieces.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve hot with English mustard.

Maximus's crisp apple and fennel saladVegetarian

Shaved apple, fennel and celery with toasted walnuts, rocket, cheddar and honey mustard.

Prep 13 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Toast the walnuts and whisk the dressing up to 2 days ahead; slice and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 crisp red apples
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 bunch celery, inner stalks and leaves
  • 80g walnuts
  • 100g rocket
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp wholegrain mustard
  • 80g aged cheddar, shaved
  1. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan until fragrant, then chop roughly.
  2. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil, honey, mustard and a pinch of salt.
  3. Thinly slice the apples, fennel and inner celery stalks and toss straight into the dressing.
  4. Fold through the rocket and celery leaves, then top with the walnuts and shaved cheddar.

Mother Gothel's hazelnut soup

Potato and toasted hazelnut soup with celery.

Prep 18 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 150g hazelnuts
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  1. Toast the hazelnuts and rub off the skins, 10 min.
  2. Soften the onion and celery in the butter.
  3. Add the potatoes, most of the hazelnuts and the stock.
  4. Simmer until the potato is soft.
  5. Blend smooth and stir in the cream.
  6. Top with crushed hazelnuts and chives.

Flynn's smoulder smoked paprika chicken

Spatchcocked bird with garlic and lemon, roasted to a smoky char over crispy potatoes, yoghurt.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Chicken spatchcocked and rubbed with smoked paprika, garlic and lemon, roasted with a smoky char, over crispy potatoes with herby yoghurt. Cut out the backbone of a 1.8kg chicken and press flat. Rub with 2 tsp smoked paprika, 3 crushed garlic cloves, the zest of 1 lemon, olive oil and salt. Lay over 1kg sliced potatoes tossed in oil in a roasting tin and roast at 220°C for 50 minutes until charred and cooked through. Rest 10 minutes. Mix 200g Greek yoghurt with lemon juice, parsley and mint to serve.

Floating lantern cakesVegetarian

Whole boiled orange and almond meal cake.

Prep 4 min, cook 103 min, then 33 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 3 oranges
  • 6 eggs
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250g almond meal
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Boil 2 whole oranges until very soft.
  2. Cool, then blend smooth, 33 min.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the sugar and baking powder.
  4. Fold in the orange puree and almond meal.
  5. Spoon into muffin tins and bake at 170°C until set.
  6. Simmer the juice of the remaining orange with the honey for the syrup and glaze the cakes, 10 min.

Corona sun lilac cupcakes

Lemon sponges piped with pale purple blueberry buttercream, the royal emblem in gold sugar.

How it is made
  • 275g butter
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 lemon
  • 125ml milk
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 jar blueberry jam
  • 100g yellow fondant
  • 12 paper cases
  1. Lemon cupcakes piped with lilac blueberry buttercream and topped with a golden sugar sun, the royal emblem of Corona. Beat 125g soft butter with 150g caster sugar, add 2 eggs, the zest of 1 lemon, 125ml milk and 200g self-raising flour. Spoon into 12 paper cases and bake at 175°C for 18 minutes, then cool. Beat 150g butter with 300g icing sugar and 2 tbsp blueberry jam until fluffy and lilac. Pipe onto the cakes and top each with a yellow fondant sun cut with a small cutter.

Corona lavender coolerNo alcohol

Lavender syrup and lemon juice over ice.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 1 tbsp dried culinary lavender
  • 2 lemons
  • 800ml soda water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and lavender for a few minutes, 5 min.
  2. Cool and strain, 20 min.
  3. Fill glasses with ice, add syrup and lemon juice to each.
  4. Top with soda water.
