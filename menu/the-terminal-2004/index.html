Snacks

Viktor's cracker sandwiches with hot mustardVegetarian

Viktor lives on crackers and mustard

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Slice the cheese and pickles up to a day ahead; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 24 square salted crackers
  • 150g smoked cheddar, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp hot English mustard
  • 2 tbsp butter, soft
  • 8 cornichons, sliced
  • 1 tbsp chives, finely chopped
  1. Beat the mustard into the soft butter.
  2. Spread half the crackers with the mustard butter and top with cheddar and cornichon.
  3. Close with the remaining crackers, pressing so the mustard just squeezes through the holes, and scatter with chives.

Krakozhia coup potato dumplingsVegetarian

A coup in Krakozhia mid-flight

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fill the dumplings up to a day ahead and chill, or freeze them; boil and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 150ml warm water
  • 400g floury potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 50g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  1. Mix the flour, egg, water and a pinch of salt to a soft dough and rest it 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the mash with the cheddar and half the onion, fried soft, and season well.
  3. Roll the dough thin, cut 8cm rounds, fill and pinch shut.
  4. Boil the dumplings until they float, then fry in the butter with the rest of the onion until golden.
  5. Serve with sour cream and dill.

Mains

Luggage cart quarters cheeseburgers

Viktor returns luggage carts for quarters

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to a day ahead and chill between baking paper; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince, 20 percent fat
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 1 white onion, thinly sliced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Shape the mince into 4 thin patties a little wider than the buns and season well.
  2. Grill over high heat 3 minutes, flip, add the cheese and cook 2 minutes more.
  3. Toast the buns and spread with mayo and tomato sauce.
  4. Build with lettuce, tomato, the patty, pickles and onion.

Enrique's catering truck chicken and rice

Enrique trades meals for help with Dolores

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to a day ahead; roast and cook the rice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in, skin on
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g long-grain rice
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads
  • 600ml vegetable stock
  • 200g green beans, trimmed
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  1. Rub the chicken with the paprika, garlic, lemon, oil and salt.
  2. Roast skin-side up at 210C until crisp and cooked through.
  3. While it roasts, soften the onion, stir in the rice and saffron, add the stock and simmer, covered, until tender.
  4. Steam the beans for the last 5 minutes and serve everything together, airline tray style.

Great Day in Harlem New York cheesecakeVegetarian

Viktor gets Benny Golson's autograph

Prep 25 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead and chill overnight; it slices best cold
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 750g cream cheese, soft
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 680g jar pitted sour cherries, drained, 125ml juice kept
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  1. Mix the crumbs and butter and press into a lined 22cm springform tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth, then beat in the eggs, sour cream, vanilla and zest.
  3. Pour over the base and bake at 150C until just set with a wobble in the middle, then cool in the turned-off oven.
  4. Simmer the cherry juice with the cornflour until glossy, stir in the cherries and spoon over the chilled cake.

Stallion iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Viktor tells Dolores she is a stallion

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make the cold brew the night before; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold brew coffee
  • 4 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 200ml cold milk
  • 2 cup ice
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Spoon the condensed milk into 4 tall glasses and fill with ice.
  2. Pour over the cold brew, then the milk, and dust with cinnamon.
  3. Stir at the table.
