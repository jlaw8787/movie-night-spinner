Joe's Pizza garlic knotsVegetarian

Tied dough strips baked hot, garlic butter and parsley, delivered in under 29 minutes

Prep 15 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm, same day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pizza dough
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Cut the dough into 8 strips and tie into knots.
  2. Cover on a lined tray and prove until puffy.
  3. Bake at 230C until golden.
  4. Melt the butter with the garlic over low heat, then toss the hot knots in the garlic butter and parsley.

Potato knishesVegetarian

Mashed potato and fried onion wrapped in thin dough, egg washed, baked golden, mustard alongside

Prep 20 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat in the oven
Recipe for 4
  • 700g potatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Peel and boil the potatoes until tender, then drain.
  2. Fry the onion in the butter until soft. Mash the potatoes with the onion, salt and pepper.
  3. Cut each pastry sheet into 4 squares, put a mound of potato on each, fold up and pinch closed.
  4. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200C until golden. Serve hot with yellow mustard.

Mains

Thirty-minute pepperoni pie

Joe's Pizza, late again

Prep 20 min, cook 1620 min · Make ahead: Dough needs 1 to 2 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 380g bread flour
  • 2g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 245ml cool water
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 200g low-moisture mozzarella, grated
  • 100g thinly sliced cupping pepperoni
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and water, then knead for about 8 minutes. Divide into 2 balls.
  2. Cold-ferment the covered balls in the fridge for 24 hours (up to 48 is fine).
  3. Bring the dough to room temperature for 2 hours.
  4. Heat a pizza steel in the oven at its maximum temperature, about 250 to 275C.
  5. Stretch each ball thin, spread with half the tomatoes and top with half the mozzarella and pepperoni. Bake one at a time on the hot steel until the crust is cooked and crisp. Cut into wedges.

Sweets

Aunt May's pancakesVegetarian

Butter and syrup for her boy

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 20g sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 180ml buttermilk
  • 30g butter
  • 80ml maple syrup
  1. Separate the eggs. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the buttermilk and fold into the flour. Whip the whites to soft peaks and fold through gently.
  3. Cook eight small pancakes in batches in a little butter over medium heat, until bubbles set, then flip and cook briefly.
  4. Stack two per person and serve with maple syrup.

Iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Cold brew over ice with milk and vanilla ice cream, for a man running late, always

Prep 10 min, cook 735 min · Make ahead: Cold brew keeps 3 days in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 80g coarsely ground coffee beans
  • 800ml cold water
  • 400g ice
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 200ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Stir the coffee and cold water together in a jar and cover.
  2. Steep in the fridge for 12 hours (up to 18).
  3. Strain through a coffee filter.
  4. Divide the ice between four glasses, add cold brew and milk to each, then top with ice cream.
