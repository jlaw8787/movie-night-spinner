Snacks

Lighthouse dad's fish bites

Cod in lager batter, fried crisp, lighthouse supper on a stormy night.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Cut the cod up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cod fillets
  • 300g plain flour
  • 300ml ice-cold lager
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 2 lemons
  • 120g tartare sauce
  1. Cut the cod into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Whisk some of the flour with the lager to a thin batter.
  3. Dust the fish in flour, dip in batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until crisp, 8 min.
  4. Drain and serve with lemon wedges and tartare.

Tom and Arthur's bar wings

Crispy baked wings in hot butter sauce, blue cheese dip.

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Wings can dry overnight in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 50g butter
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 100g blue cheese
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 4 celery sticks
  1. Pat the wings dry and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight if you can.
  2. Bake at 220°C until crisp, 45 min.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and toss the wings through, 5 min.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream and serve with celery.

Sicilian rose petal arancini

Saffron risotto balls with molten mozzarella, breaded and fried, edible flowers, Mera eats hers.

How it is made
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 40g parmesan
  • 125g mozzarella
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko crumbs
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 punnet edible rose petals
  1. Golden saffron risotto balls with molten mozzarella centres, crumbed and fried crisp, on a plate scattered with edible rose petals. Cook 300g arborio rice into a risotto with 1 diced onion, a pinch of saffron and 1L stock, stir in 40g parmesan and chill overnight. Shape into 16 balls around cubes of mozzarella from 125g. Roll in flour, beaten egg and panko crumbs. Fry at 180°C for 4 minutes until deep golden. Serve with lemon wedges and scatter the plate with edible rose petals.

Mains

Atlantis seafood platter

Shrimp, mussels, scallops and fish in garlic butter, fit for Atlantis.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Scrub the mussels and peel the prawns up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; roast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g green prawns
  • 500g mussels, scrubbed
  • 300g scallops
  • 400g white fish fillets, in chunks
  • 150g garlic butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 loaf of crusty bread
  1. Spread the green prawns, mussels, scallops and white fish in a big roasting tray and dot with garlic butter.
  2. Roast at 220°C until the mussels open, 10 min.
  3. Squeeze over lemon and serve with crusty bread.

Amnesty Bay lobster rolls

Sweet chunks tossed with lemon mayo, celery and chives in butter-toasted buns, paprika.

How it is made
  • 4 cooked lobster tails
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch celery
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 4 soft hot dog rolls
  • 40g butter
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  1. Sweet chunks of lobster tossed with lemon mayo, celery and chives, piled high into butter-toasted rolls with a pinch of paprika. Chop the meat from 4 cooked lobster tails, about 500g, into chunks. Mix 4 tbsp mayo with the zest and juice of half a lemon, 1 finely diced celery stick, 2 tbsp chopped chives, salt and pepper, and fold through the lobster. Split 4 soft hot dog rolls on top, brush the sides with 40g melted butter and toast in a frying pan until golden. Fill with the lobster and dust with paprika.

Sweets

Trident sea salt caramel tartVegetarian

Sweet pastry case with salted caramel and a dark chocolate layer.

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min, 8 hr 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 150ml cream
  • 60g butter
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150ml cream for ganache
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Blind bake a pastry case, 25 min.
  2. Melt the sugar to amber caramel, whisk in the cream and butter and pour into the case, 15 min.
  3. Chill until firm, 30 min.
  4. Pour hot cream over the chocolate and stir to a ganache, then pour over the caramel.
  5. Set overnight.
  6. Finish with sea salt flakes.

Ocean Master Blue Hawaiian with rum and pineapple

White rum, blue curaçao, pineapple and coconut cream over crushed ice.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the rum, curacao, pineapple juice and coconut cream in a jug up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake with ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml white rum
  • 80ml blue curacao
  • 360ml pineapple juice
  • 120ml coconut cream
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. For each drink, measure 30ml white rum, 20ml blue curacao, 90ml pineapple juice and 30ml coconut cream into a shaker.
  2. Shake each drink hard with ice until frothy.
  3. Strain into tall glasses full of crushed ice and garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
