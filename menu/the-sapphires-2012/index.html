Snacks

Talent quest party pies and sauce

Bite-size gravy beef pies, for the contest the girls didn't win

Prep 40 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce, for the filling
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Brown the mince and onion, stir in the flour, Worcestershire, tomato sauce and stock and simmer until thick.
  2. Cool the filling.
  3. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut 24 rounds from the shortcrust and press into mini muffin tins.
  4. Fill, top with rounds of puff pastry, pinch the edges and brush with egg.
  5. Bake until golden and serve hot with tomato sauce.

Songbirds' damper with syrupVegetarian

Warm damper and golden syrup. Kay names the group from Cynthia's ring.

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cold butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300ml milk
  • 4 tbsp golden syrup
  • extra butter, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Rub the butter into the flour and salt.
  2. Mix in the milk with a knife until just combined.
  3. Shape, score and bake.
  4. Serve warm with butter and golden syrup.

Mains

Army mess roast

Topside roast, pumpkin and gravy. Dave swore to guard the girls with his life.

Prep 25 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Rest 20 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef topside
  • 800g potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 600g pumpkin, cut in pieces
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g peas
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Season the beef and roast until 52C in the middle.
  2. Rest the beef under foil.
  3. Roast the potatoes and pumpkin in oil.
  4. Cook the peas.
  5. Stir the flour into the roasting tin, add the stock and simmer into gravy.

Saigon chicken pho

Clear ginger and star anise broth, rice noodles, herbs and lime, between shows at the base

Prep 20 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Make the broth up to 2 days ahead and keep in the fridge; skim and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 brown onion
  • 50g ginger
  • 3 star anise
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 400g dried rice noodles
  • 200g bean sprouts
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 limes
  • 1 red chilli
  1. Char the halved onion and ginger in a dry pan, then toast the star anise and cinnamon for 1 minute.
  2. Put the chicken, onion, ginger and spices in a pot with 2 litres of water and simmer gently for 50 minutes, skimming.
  3. Lift out the chicken and shred the meat. Strain the broth and season with the fish sauce and sugar.
  4. Soak or cook the noodles as the packet says and divide between bowls.
  5. Top with the chicken, pour over the hot broth and serve with bean sprouts, herbs, lime and chilli.

Sweets

Cummeragunja lamingtonsVegetarian

Butter sponge squares in chocolate and coconut, a taste of Cummeragunja

Prep 50 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 30g cocoa
  • 200g desiccated coconut
  1. Heat the oven to 180C and line a tin. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake the sponge and cool.
  3. Cut into cubes.
  4. Mix the icing sugar, cocoa and a little milk into icing.
  5. Dip each cube in icing and roll in coconut.
  6. Set on a rack.

Drinks

Dave Lovelace's Saigon iced coffeeVegetarianNo alcohol

Strong coffee on condensed milk and ice. Dave slept in his car again.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best served fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g dark ground coffee
  • 500ml hot water
  • 8 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Brew the ground coffee with the hot water to make very strong coffee.
  2. Put the condensed milk in 4 glasses, pour over the hot coffee and stir.
  3. Fill with ice and serve straight away.
