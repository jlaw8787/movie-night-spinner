Snacks

Talent quest party pies and sauce

Bite-size gravy beef pies, for the contest the girls didn't win.

Prep 13 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce, for the filling
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Brown the mince and onion, stir in the flour, Worcestershire, tomato sauce and stock and simmer until thick.
  2. Cool the filling.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut 24 rounds from the shortcrust and press into mini muffin tins.
  4. Fill, top with rounds of puff pastry, pinch the edges and brush with egg.
  5. Bake until golden and serve hot with tomato sauce.

Murray cod bites with lemon

Crumbed Murray cod pieces with lemon and tartare. River fish from home, Cummeragunja on the Murray.

Prep 1 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Crumb up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fry to order
Recipe for 4
  • 500g Murray cod or firm white fish fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko crumbs
  • 250ml vegetable oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar tartare sauce
  1. Cut the fish into bite-size pieces, dust in flour, dip in egg and coat in panko.
  2. Shallow fry in hot oil for 3 minutes until golden, in batches.
  3. Serve with lemon wedges and tartare.

Saigon pork bánh mì bites

Crusty rolls with pâté and mayo, roast pork, pickled carrot and daikon, cucumber, coriander, chilli.

How it is made
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 small daikon
  • 1 bottle rice vinegar
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 600g pork scotch fillet
  • 1 bottle fish sauce
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar honey
  • 8 short crusty rolls
  • 100g chicken liver pâté
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  1. Short crusty rolls spread with pâté and mayo, stuffed with roast pork, pickled carrot and daikon, cucumber, coriander and chilli. Julienne 1 carrot and a small daikon and pickle in 100ml rice vinegar, 2 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp salt for 30 minutes. Rub 600g pork scotch fillet with fish sauce, garlic and honey and roast at 200°C for 30 minutes, then rest and slice thin. Split 8 short crusty rolls and spread with 100g chicken liver pâté and mayonnaise. Fill with pork, drained pickles, cucumber, coriander and sliced chilli, and drizzle with soy.

Mains

Army mess roast

Topside roast, pumpkin and gravy. Dave swore to guard the girls with his life.

Prep 0 min, cook 2 hours · Make ahead: Rest 20 minutes before carving
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef topside
  • 800g potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 600g pumpkin, cut in pieces
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g peas
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Season the beef and roast until 52°C in the middle.
  2. Rest the beef under foil.
  3. Roast the potatoes and pumpkin in oil.
  4. Cook the peas.
  5. Stir the flour into the roasting tin, add the stock and simmer into gravy.

Saigon chicken pho

Clear ginger and star anise broth, rice noodles, herbs and lime, between shows at the base.

Prep 8 min, cook 68 min · Make ahead: Make the broth up to 2 days ahead and keep in the fridge; skim and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 brown onion
  • 50g ginger
  • 3 star anise
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 400g dried rice noodles
  • 200g bean sprouts
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 limes
  • 1 red chilli
  1. Char the halved onion and ginger in a dry pan, then toast the star anise and cinnamon for 1 minute.
  2. Put the chicken, onion, ginger and spices in a pot with 2 litres of water and simmer gently for 50 minutes, skimming.
  3. Lift out the chicken and shred the meat. Strain the broth and season with the fish sauce and sugar.
  4. Soak or cook the noodles as the packet says and divide between bowls.
  5. Top with the chicken, pour over the hot broth and serve with bean sprouts, herbs, lime and chilli.

Sweets

Cummeragunja lamingtonsVegetarian

Butter sponge squares in chocolate and coconut, a taste of Cummeragunja.

Prep 28 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 30g cocoa
  • 200g desiccated coconut
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a tin. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake the sponge and cool.
  3. Cut into cubes.
  4. Mix the icing sugar, cocoa and a little milk into icing.
  5. Dip each cube in icing and roll in coconut.
  6. Set on a rack.

Drinks

Dave Lovelace's Saigon iced coffeeNo alcohol

Strong coffee on condensed milk and ice. Dave slept in his car again.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best served fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 60g dark ground coffee
  • 500ml hot water
  • 8 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Brew the ground coffee with the hot water to make very strong coffee.
  2. Put the condensed milk in 4 glasses, pour over the hot coffee and stir.
  3. Fill with ice and serve straight away.
