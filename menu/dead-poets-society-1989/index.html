Carpe diem Welsh rarebit on toastVegetarian

Sharp Vermont cheddar sauce grilled on toast.

Prep 2 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese sauce up to 2 days ahead and chill; spread and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp hot English mustard
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 4 thick slices of sourdough
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk until thick, 5 min.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in both mustards, the cheese and yolk until smooth, 3 min.
  3. Toast the bread on one side, spread the cheese mix thickly on the other and grill until bubbling and spotted brown, 7 min.
  4. Dust with cayenne and serve hot.

Food for worms pea and ham soup

Split peas slow-cooked with a smoky hock. We are food for worms lads.

Prep 13 min, cook 1 hr 50 min · Make ahead: Make the soup up to 3 days ahead; it thickens, so loosen with water when you reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 500g green split peas, rinsed
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 L water
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Put the hock, peas, vegetables, bay leaves and water in a big pot and bring to the boil, 10 min.
  2. Simmer gently, stirring now and then, until the peas collapse and the meat falls off the bone, 1 hr 40 min.
  3. Lift out the hock, shred the meat and stir it back in, then season.
  4. Serve with thick slices of the loaf.

Opening night roast chicken with bread sauce

Golden roast chicken with buttered peas.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 35 min · Make ahead: Make the bread sauce up to a day ahead and reheat with a splash of milk; roast the chicken on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 800g small potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 brown onion, studded with 4 cloves
  • 100g fresh white breadcrumbs
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  1. Rub the chicken with butter and salt, stuff with lemon and thyme, and roast at 200°C with the oiled potatoes around it, 1 hr 15 min.
  2. Warm the milk with the clove-studded onion and leave to infuse, 5 min.
  3. Lift out the onion, stir in the breadcrumbs and a knob of butter and cook until thick, 5 min.
  4. Rest the chicken, boil the peas and serve everything with the bread sauce, 15 min.

Sweaty-toothed madman shepherd's pie

Ground lamb under a cheddar mashed potato crust.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 22 min · Make ahead: Make the filling and mash up to 2 days ahead and chill; assemble and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g lamb mince
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 80g cheddar, grated
  1. Brown the mince in the oil, add the onion and carrot and cook until soft, 12 min.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste and flour, then the stock and Worcestershire, and simmer until thick, 20 min.
  3. Boil and mash the potatoes with the butter and milk, 25 min.
  4. Spoon the filling into a dish, spread over the mash, scatter with cheddar and bake at 200°C until golden, 25 min.

O Captain apple pie with Vermont cheddarVegetarian

Spiced apple pie with a wedge of sharp cheddar.

Prep 22 min, cook 1 hr · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to a day ahead and warm it through to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 6 Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 30g butter, diced
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 150g sharp cheddar, in wedges
  1. Line a 23cm pie dish with one sheet of pastry.
  2. Toss the apples with the sugar, flour, spices and lemon juice and pile them in, dotted with butter.
  3. Cover with the second sheet, crimp, cut steam holes and brush with egg, 10 min.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden and bubbling, then cool a little, 50 min.
  5. Serve warm with a wedge of cheddar.

Dead Poets cave hot chocolateNo alcohol

Thick cocoa poured from a thermos.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make it and keep it hot in a thermos for up to 4 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Whisk the cocoa and sugar into a little of the milk to a paste.
  2. Heat the rest of the milk, whisk in the paste and chocolate and stir until smooth and steaming, 10 min.
  3. Add the vanilla and pour into a hot thermos or mugs.
