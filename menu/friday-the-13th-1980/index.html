Jersey diner disco fries

Fries with rich brown gravy and melted mozzarella.

Prep 9 min, cook 41 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy up to 3 days ahead and reheat; bake the chips on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen thick-cut chips
  • 40g butter
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 30g plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp, 30 min.
  2. Meanwhile soften the onion in the butter.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook until nut brown, 3 min.
  4. Whisk in the stock, soy sauce and pepper and simmer until smooth and thick, 8 min.
  5. Pile the chips in a dish, scatter with mozzarella and ladle the hot gravy over.

Lakeside grilled cornVegetarian

Charred sweetcorn with chilli lime butter, parmesan and cilantro.

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli lime butter up to 1 week ahead in the fridge; boil and grill the corn on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 1 lime, zested and juiced
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 corn cobs, husked and halved
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 2 tbsp coriander, chopped
  1. Mash the butter with the lime zest and juice, chilli powder, paprika and salt.
  2. Boil the corn for 4 minutes.
  3. Char on a very hot grill or barbecue, turning, 10 min.
  4. Brush with the butter and roll in parmesan and coriander.

Mains

Annie's camp cook sloppy joes

Ground beef in a sweet and tangy tomato sauce with onion and bell pepper.

Prep 13 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and reheat; toast the buns on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml water
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 20g butter
  • 1/2 cup dill pickles, sliced
  1. Brown the mince in the oil, breaking it up, 10 min.
  2. Add the onion, capsicum and garlic and cook until soft, 6 min.
  3. Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, water, sugar, mustard, vinegar, Worcestershire, chilli powder and salt and simmer until thick, 20 min.
  4. Toast the buns in the butter, 4 min.
  5. Pile in the filling and top with pickles.

Steve Christy's opening week cookout chicken

Lemon, garlic and oregano chicken grilled over coals, Steve's cookout before the storm.

Prep 9 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken and make the potato salad up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g chat potatoes
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Marinate the chicken in the juice of 1 lemon, the garlic, oregano and 2 tbsp oil, 5 min.
  2. Boil the potatoes until tender, cool a little and toss with the mayonnaise and spring onion, 20 min.
  3. Grill the chicken over the coals until charred and cooked through, 15 min.
  4. Dress the cos with the last of the oil and lemon and serve with the chicken and potatoes.

Crystal Lake blueberry cobblerVegetarian

Blueberries baked under a buttery buttermilk graham cracker crust, with vanilla ice cream.

Prep 11 min, cook 45 min, 12 min waiting · Make ahead: Rub the butter into the flour up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; add the buttermilk and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750g blueberries, fresh or frozen
  • 100g caster sugar, for the fruit
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 50g caster sugar, for the topping
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 90g cold butter, cubed
  • 160ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp demerara sugar
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Toss the blueberries with the sugar for the fruit, lemon zest and juice and cornflour in a 25cm ovenproof pan, 5 min.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, sugar for the topping and salt.
  3. Stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough.
  4. Dollop over the fruit and sprinkle with demerara sugar.
  5. Bake until golden and bubbling, 40 min.
  6. Rest, then serve with the ice cream, 12 min.

Counsellors' cabin lemon squashNo alcohol

Ginger beer topped with homemade lemonade, with lemon slices.

Prep 6 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the lemonade base of syrup and lemon juice up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; add the soda and pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml hot water
  • 4 lemons, juiced
  • 400ml soda water, chilled
  • 750ml ginger beer, cold
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  1. Stir the sugar into the hot water until dissolved, then cool, 20 min.
  2. Mix the syrup with the lemon juice and soda water.
  3. Half fill four chilled glasses with the ginger beer, top with the lemonade and add a lemon slice.
