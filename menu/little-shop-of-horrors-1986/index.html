Snacks

Skid Row florist cruditésVegetarian

Radish roses, carrot ribbons, cucumber and peppers with a herb sour cream dip.

Prep 27 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; cut the vegetables up to 1 day ahead and keep in iced water in the fridge. Arrange in the vase on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 200g baby capsicums
  • 200g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  1. Cut the radishes into roses.
  2. Peel the carrots into ribbons and slice the cucumber and baby capsicums.
  3. Blitz the sour cream with parsley, chives and lemon juice for the dip.
  4. Arrange the vegetables in a vase like flowers.

Venus flytrap tacos

Taco shells with seasoned beef, lettuce and cheddar, red pepper tongues.

Prep 28 min · Make ahead: Cook the mince up to 2 days ahead and reheat; shred the lettuce and cut the capsicum up to 1 day ahead. Warm the shells and fill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g beef mince
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 12 small taco shells
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 1 red capsicum
  1. Brown the mince with the cumin.
  2. Shred the lettuce and cut the capsicum into strips.
  3. Warm the shells.
  4. Fill with mince, lettuce and cheddar.
  5. Hang a strip of red capsicum out of each like a tongue.

Sesame chilli sugar snaps

Sugar snap peas tossed with sesame, chilli and lime, little green pods. “Feed me!”

How it is made
  • 400g sugar snap peas
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 lime
  • 1 box flaky salt
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tub white miso
  1. Crisp sugar snap peas flashed in boiling water, tossed with sesame oil, chilli, lime and flaky salt, with a creamy miso mayo for dipping. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and blanch 400g sugar snap peas for 30 seconds, then plunge into iced water and drain well. Toss with 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds, half a sliced red chilli, a squeeze of lime and flaky salt. Mix 100g mayonnaise with 1 tbsp white miso and 1 tsp lime juice for dipping.

Mains

Audrey II meatballs

Beef and pork meatballs in tomato sauce over spaghetti with parmesan.

Prep 33 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and simmer them in the tomatoes up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat gently. Cook the spaghetti just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 slices white bread, soaked in milk
  • 40g parmesan, grated, for the meatballs
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  • 800g crushed tomatoes (2 tins)
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 40g parmesan, grated, to serve
  1. Mix the minces with the soaked bread, parmesan and parsley.
  2. Roll into balls.
  3. Brown the meatballs.
  4. Simmer in the tomatoes.
  5. Cook the spaghetti.
  6. Toss with the sauce and the parmesan to serve.

Audrey's crumbed chicken TV dinner

Crumbed chicken, mash and peas on a tray. Somewhere that's green, on a tray.

How it is made
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g panko crumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  • 500ml vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml milk
  • 300g frozen peas and corn
  • 1 packet gravy mix
  1. Golden crumbed chicken schnitzel with buttery mashed potato, peas and corn and a little pot of gravy, all served on a sectioned tray like a fifties TV dinner. Flatten 2 large chicken breasts and cut each into 2. Dip in flour, 2 beaten eggs, then 150g panko mixed with parmesan. Shallow fry in 1cm oil for 3 minutes a side until golden and cooked. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml milk. Cook 300g frozen peas and corn with butter. Make 300ml gravy from a packet mix. Arrange on 4 trays in sections.

Sweets

Green velvet cupcakesVegetarian

Green cocoa buttermilk cupcakes with cream cheese frosting.

Prep 27 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Frost on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 125ml buttermilk
  • 4 drops green food colouring
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp red sanding sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk and green colouring.
  3. Spoon into 12 cases and bake at 175°C.
  4. Cool the cupcakes.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar.
  6. Frost and add a line of red sugar.

Drinks

Lime sherbet punchNo alcohol

Pineapple, lime and lemonade punch with lime sorbet floating, no alcohol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes and mix with the pineapple juice up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled. Add the lemonade, sorbet and mint just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice, cold
  • 4 limes
  • 1.25 L lemonade, cold
  • 1 L lime sorbet
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  1. Juice three limes and slice the fourth thin.
  2. Pour the pineapple juice and lime juice into a punch bowl.
  3. Add the lemonade slowly so it keeps its fizz.
  4. Drop in scoops of lime sorbet so they float and froth up the top.
  5. Scatter with torn mint and lime slices and ladle into glasses straight away.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the plant first starts talking to Seymour.
