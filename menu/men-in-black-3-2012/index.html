Snacks

Mr Wu's pork and prawn dumplings

Steamed pork, prawn and ginger dumplings

Prep 26 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Fill and pleat the dumplings up to 1 day ahead, keep covered on a lined tray in the fridge; steam just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pork mince
  • 150g green prawns
  • 1 packet dumpling wrappers
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, for the filling
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, for dipping
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Peel and chop the prawns. Mix with the pork, ginger and soy.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Steam in a lined bamboo basket.
  4. Serve hot with the dipping soy sauce and chilli oil.

Agent K's shrimp and bok choy

Green prawns and baby bok choy wok-tossed with garlic, ginger and oyster sauce

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Peel and devein the prawns up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; wok-toss on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g green prawns
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 small knob ginger, in matchsticks
  • 2 baby bok choy, halved
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  1. Peel and devein the prawns, leaving the tails on, and toss with the cornflour.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok until smoking and fry the prawns for 2 minutes until just pink, then lift out.
  3. Fry the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, add the bok choy and a splash of water and toss for 2 minutes.
  4. Return the prawns with the oyster sauce and soy, toss and serve.

Mains

Wu's real Earth fish

Steamed snapper with ginger, spring onion and sizzling soy

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Shred the spring onions and cut the ginger up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; steam the fish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 4 skin-on snapper fillets
  • 1 thumb ginger, in matchsticks
  • 4 spring onions, finely shredded
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  1. Cook the jasmine rice.
  2. Lay the snapper on a heatproof plate over half the ginger and steam over a wok of simmering water until just flaking.
  3. Scatter with the spring onions and the rest of the ginger. Mix the soy with the sugar and 2 tbsp of the steaming water.
  4. Heat the oil until smoking, pour it over the fish so it sizzles, spoon over the soy and serve with the rice.

Sweets

Diner apple pieVegetarian

Cinnamon green apples under a lattice crust

Prep 34 min, cook 80 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and keep covered; rewarm in a 160C oven for 15 minutes and serve with ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 6 green apples
  • 50g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Peel, core and slice the apples.
  2. Cook with butter, sugar and cinnamon until just soft.
  3. Cool the filling for 20 minutes.
  4. Line a pie dish with pastry, fill and weave a lattice on top.
  5. Bake at 190C.
  6. Serve warm with ice cream.

Drinks

Black suit espresso martiniVegetarian

Vodka, coffee liqueur and strong espresso shaken ice cold

Prep 9 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Brew and cool the coffee up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; chill the glasses and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml vodka
  • 120ml coffee liqueur
  • 200ml strong coffee
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill four martini glasses in the freezer.
  2. Make strong espresso or coffee.
  3. Cool the coffee for 5 minutes.
  4. Shake two drinks at a time with vodka, liqueur, coffee, syrup and ice for 20 seconds.
  5. Strain into the glasses and float three coffee beans on the foam. Repeat.

When to serve

Serve dessert when young K and J stop for pie at the diner.
