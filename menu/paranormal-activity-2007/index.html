Snacks

3am toasted cheese sandwichesVegetarian

Cheddar and Dijon sandwiches toasted golden in a pan

Prep 14 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Butter and fill up to 4 hours ahead, wrapped in the fridge; toast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  1. Butter the outsides of the bread.
  2. Fill the sandwiches with cheddar and a thin spread of Dijon.
  3. Toast in a pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side.
  4. Cut into strips.

Ouija board crackersVegetarian

The planchette moves on its own while they're out.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Bake the crackers up to 3 days ahead, airtight; whip the feta dip up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lavash breads
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  1. Brush the lavash with oil.
  2. Stencil letters and numbers on with smoked paprika.
  3. Bake at 180C until crisp.
  4. Whip the feta with the yoghurt to dip. Don't ask it anything.

Mains

Late night takeaway pizza

Eat it before Night 21.

Prep 20 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best baked fresh; portion the dough and slice the mushrooms up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 250g mozzarella
  • 100g pepperoni
  • 150g mushrooms, sliced
  1. Stretch the dough into two thin bases.
  2. Spread with tomatoes and top with mozzarella, pepperoni and mushrooms.
  3. Bake on a hot steel at max heat for 7 minutes. Rewatch the tape.

Sweets

3am chocolate potsVegetarian

Katie stands by the bed for two hours. Eat slowly.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 200ml milk
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 60g caster sugar
  1. Warm the cream and milk, pour over the chocolate and stir smooth.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar and combine.
  3. Pour into small cups and bake in a water bath at 150C for 30 minutes.
  4. Chill until set.

Drinks

Night-watch espresso martinisVegetarian

Stay up for the night footage.

Prep 17 min · Make ahead: Brew and chill the espresso up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; shake with the vodka, liqueur and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 shots espresso coffee
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 20ml sugar syrup
  • 6 coffee beans
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Make the espresso and cool for 5 minutes.
  2. For each drink, measure 1 shot espresso coffee, 40ml vodka, 20ml coffee liqueur and 5ml sugar syrup into a shaker.
  3. Shake half the batch at a time with plenty of ice for 20 seconds until frothy.
  4. Strain into chilled coupes and repeat. Float coffee beans on each.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the camera is set up on the tripod for the first night.
