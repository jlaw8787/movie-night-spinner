Snacks

Share-a-croissant ham and cheese croissants

The crew shares a croissant without Emmet.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fill the croissants up to 4 hours ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large butter croissants
  • 150g shaved leg ham
  • 100g gruyere, grated
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 20g butter, melted
  1. Split the croissants and spread the insides with mustard.
  2. Fill with the ham and most of the cheese.
  3. Brush the tops with butter, scatter over the rest of the cheese and bake at 180°C until melted and crisp.
  4. Cut in half and serve warm.

Barry's sports bar chicken wings

Honey soy wings with garlic and paprika. Barry's plan for after work tonight.

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Marinate the wings up to a day ahead; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings, tips off and jointed
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  1. Toss the wings with the honey, soy, garlic and paprika.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and bake at 200°C, turning once, until sticky and dark.
  3. Stir the sour cream, mayo, chives and lemon juice together for the dip.

Mains

Taco Tuesday beef tacos

Beef tacos for Taco Tuesday, the day the Kragle is set for.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cook the beef up to 2 days ahead and reheat; make the salsa on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cup coriander, chopped
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 limes
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  1. Brown the mince and onion, then stir in the cumin, paprika and tomato paste with a splash of water and simmer until thick.
  2. Mix the tomatoes and coriander with a squeeze of lime for the salsa.
  3. Stir the sour cream with the zest and juice of the other lime.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  5. Fill with lettuce, beef, salsa, cheese and crema.

Giant sausages with fried onions

Giant sausages and Emmet left behind.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Fry the onions up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the sausages to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 thick pork sausages
  • 4 long crusty rolls
  • 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 4 tbsp American mustard
  1. Fry the onions slowly in half the butter until soft and golden.
  2. Grill the sausages, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  3. Split and butter the rolls.
  4. Tuck in the sausages and top with onions, sauce and mustard.

Sweets

Cloud Cuckoo Land rainbow sundaesVegetarian

Rainbow ice cream sundaes with berry sauce. No rules in Cloud Cuckoo Land.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the berry sauce up to 3 days ahead and chill; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 250g frozen mixed berries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 12 large marshmallows
  • 4 tbsp rainbow sprinkles
  • 4 ice cream wafers
  1. Simmer the berries and sugar until saucy, then cool.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into 4 tall glasses and spoon over the berry sauce.
  3. Toast the marshmallows under a hot grill or with a kitchen torch and pile them on.
  4. Finish with sprinkles and a wafer.

Drinks

Overpriced $37 hot chocolateNo alcohol

Rich hot chocolate made with real chocolate. Larry the Barista charges $37.

Prep 3 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Grate the chocolate up to a day ahead; heat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L milk
  • 150g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder, plus extra to dust
  • 125ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Heat the milk until steaming, then whisk in the chocolate and cocoa until smooth.
  2. Pour into 4 mugs, top with whipped cream and dust with cocoa.

When to serve

Pour the hot chocolate when Larry the Barista tells Emmet that's $37.
