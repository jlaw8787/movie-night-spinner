Number 5's more input popcornVegetarian

Popcorn in two flavours, buttered and salted, and parmesan with cracked pepper.

Prep 6 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 150g popcorn kernels
  • 40g butter, melted
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • salt, to taste
  1. Heat the oil in a large pot with 3 kernels, covered, until they pop, 3 min.
  2. Add the rest of the kernels, cover and shake until the popping slows, 5 min.
  3. Toss half with the melted butter and salt.
  4. Toss the other half with the parmesan and cracked pepper.

Stephanie's food truck egg rolls

Fried egg rolls of pork, cabbage, carrot and spring onion, more input for Number 5.

Prep 16 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Rolled and uncooked up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 12 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Fry the pork mince until browned, breaking it up, 6 min.
  2. Add the cabbage, carrot, spring onions and soy sauce and cook until dry. Cool the filling, 10 min.
  3. Roll a spoonful into each wrapper, sealing the edge with water.
  4. Heat the oil to about 5cm deep and fry in batches until golden, 8 min.
  5. Drain on paper towel.

Oregon coast salted hazelnut brittle

Toasted nuts set in thin amber caramel with butter and bicarb, snapped into shards, flaky salt.

How it is made
  • 200g hazelnuts
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • 30g butter
  • bicarb soda
  • flaky sea salt
  1. Toasted hazelnuts set in a thin amber caramel with butter and a pinch of bicarb for crunch, snapped into shards and finished with flaky salt. Toast 200g hazelnuts at 180°C for 10 minutes, rub off the skins and roughly chop. Line a tray with baking paper. Cook 250g caster sugar, 80ml water and 2 tbsp golden syrup in a heavy pan without stirring until deep amber, about 10 minutes. Stir in 30g butter, half a tsp bicarb soda and the nuts. Pour onto the tray, spread thin, sprinkle with flaky salt and cool 30 minutes before snapping.

Food truck double burger

Smashed onion beef patties with American cheese, pickles and mustard.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Roll the mince into balls and slice the onion up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; smash and cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  1. Roll the mince into eight balls and slice the onion very thinly.
  2. Heat a griddle until very hot. Put a few onion slices on each ball and smash flat, onion side down, 3 min.
  3. Flip after 90 seconds, add a slice of cheese and cook briefly.
  4. Toast the buns, 3 min.
  5. Stack two patties per bun with pickles and mustard.

Three Stooges chilli cheese dogs

Griddled franks in steamed buns under beef chilli, melted cheddar, onion and mustard.

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 8 hot dog franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 150g cheddar
  • yellow mustard
  1. Franks griddled until blistered in steamed buns, smothered with beef chilli, melted cheddar and diced onion, with a stripe of mustard. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 diced onion, add 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 200ml water and simmer 25 minutes until thick. Griddle 8 franks for 6 minutes until blistered. Steam 8 hot dog buns. Fill each with a frank, a spoon of chilli and grated cheddar, then grill 1 minute to melt. Top with diced onion and mustard.

Blackberry cobblerVegetarian

Baked blackberries under a soft biscuit topping, with vanilla ice cream.

Prep 12 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g frozen blackberries
  • 130g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon
  • 80g cold butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 120ml milk
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Toss the blackberries with some of the sugar, the cornflour and lemon juice in a baking dish, 5 min.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and remaining sugar, then stir in the milk to a soft batter.
  3. Dot the batter over the fruit.
  4. Bake until golden on top and bubbling at the edges, 35 min.
  5. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

Stephanie's raspberry lemon crushNo alcohol

Muddled raspberry and lemon over ice, soda on top, no squashing required.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Muddle the raspberries with the syrup and stir in the lemon juice up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add crushed ice and soda water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g raspberries
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 120ml fresh lemon juice (about 4 lemons)
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lemon, sliced into wheels
  1. Muddle the raspberries with the sugar syrup in a jug until pulpy.
  2. Stir in the lemon juice.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with crushed ice and divide the raspberry mix between them.
  4. Top with cold soda water, stir gently and garnish with a lemon wheel and mint.
