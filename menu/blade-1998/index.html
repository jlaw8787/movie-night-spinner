Whistler's garlic breadVegetarian

Baguette spread with roast garlic, butter and parmesan, then baked.

Prep 5 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Roast the garlic and mix the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled; spread the baguette and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  1. Roast the whole garlic head at 200°C until soft.
  2. Squeeze the garlic into the butter with parsley and parmesan.
  3. Split the baguette, spread thickly and bake at 200°C until crisp.

Silver stake garlic shrimp skewers

Jumbo shrimp threaded on skewers and charred in a whole lot of garlic butter.

Prep 5 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Thread the prawns and make the garlic butter up to 6 hours ahead, both chilled; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g green king prawns, peeled with tails on
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 12 bamboo skewers
  1. Soak the skewers in water for 15 minutes and thread the prawns onto them.
  2. Warm the butter with the garlic, lemon zest and chilli flakes, 3 min.
  3. Grill the skewers on a very hot pan or barbecue 2 minutes a side, brushing with the garlic butter.
  4. Squeeze over the lemon juice and scatter with chopped parsley.

Daywalker garlic steak

Rib eyes basted in butter and crushed garlic, buttery mash.

Prep 0 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Roast the garlic and make the mash up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and reheat gently; sear the steaks and steam the broccolini on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 100g butter
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter for mash
  • 2 bunches of broccolini
  1. Roast garlic cloves for the mash while the oven is on, 5 min.
  2. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter and roasted garlic, 30 min.
  3. Sear the steaks in a hot pan, then add butter and crushed garlic and baste for two minutes, 10 min.
  4. Rest the steaks and steam the broccolini, 5 min.

Whistler's garlic chipotle beef chilli

Chuck simmered hours with kidney beans and a whole garlic head, sour cream, buttery cornbread.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 can chipotles in adobo
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 cans kidney beans
  • 200g cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 80g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  1. Beef chuck simmered for hours with kidney beans, chipotle and a whole head of garlic, with sour cream and buttery cornbread. Brown 1kg diced beef chuck in batches. Soften 2 onions and a whole head of chopped garlic, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 chopped chipotles in adobo, 2 cans crushed tomatoes, 500ml beef stock and the beef. Cover and simmer 2.5 hours, adding 2 cans kidney beans for the last 30 minutes. Mix 200g cornmeal, 150g plain flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 2 eggs, 300ml milk and 80g melted butter and bake at 200°C for 22 minutes.

Blood orange tartVegetarian

Sweet pastry case filled with blood orange curd.

Prep 0 min, cook 55 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 6 blood oranges
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 80g butter
  • 100ml water
  1. Blind bake a pastry case, 25 min.
  2. Whisk the blood orange juice, sugar, eggs, cornflour and butter over heat until thick and pour into the case.
  3. Chill to set, 1 hr.
  4. Simmer blood orange slices in a sugar syrup made with the water and arrange on top, 15 min.

Drinks

Blood rave pomegranate sangria

Red wine, pomegranate and brandy with blood orange.

Prep 10 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, add ice at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 250ml pomegranate juice
  • 60ml brandy
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 blood oranges, sliced
  • 1/2 pomegranate, seeds only
  • 300ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Mix the wine, pomegranate juice, brandy, sugar and cinnamon in a big jug.
  2. Add the oranges and pomegranate seeds.
  3. Chill for at least two hours, 2 hr.
  4. Add plenty of ice and top with soda water just before serving.
