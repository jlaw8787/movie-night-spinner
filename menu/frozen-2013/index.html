Snacks

Honey roasted carrots with whipped fetaVegetarian

Baby carrots roasted with honey and thyme, whipped feta and yoghurt dip

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Whip the feta dip up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; roast the carrots on the night and serve warm
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches baby carrots
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  1. Toss the carrots with honey, thyme and melted butter.
  2. Roast at 200C for 25 minutes until caramelised.
  3. Whip the feta with the yoghurt for the dip. Share one with the reindeer.

Mains

Arendelle meatballs

Beef and pork meatballs with allspice in cream gravy, served with potatoes

Prep 25 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; brown, simmer and boil the potatoes on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 50g breadcrumbs, soaked in milk
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1/2 tsp allspice
  • 90g butter
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 tbsp lingonberry jam
  1. Mix the mince with the breadcrumbs, onion and allspice and roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in butter.
  3. Simmer in the stock and cream.
  4. Boil the potatoes, mash with some butter and serve with lingonberry jam.

Sweets

Coronation dark and white trufflesVegetarian

Dark chocolate truffles rolled in cocoa, white chocolate ones in icing sugar

Prep 30 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 5 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 200g white chocolate
  • 135ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 3 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Make the dark ganache by pouring hot cream over the dark chocolate. Make the white ganache the same way with the white chocolate.
  2. Chill both for 3 hours.
  3. Roll the dark ones in cocoa and the white ones in icing sugar like snow.
  4. Pile on a silver plate.

Eternal winter school breadVegetarian

Soft cardamom yeast buns filled with thick custard

Prep 33 min, cook 12 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the buns up to 1 day ahead, airtight; ice and dip in coconut on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 450g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 250ml warm milk
  • 1 egg
  • 60g butter
  • 300ml thick custard
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 50g desiccated coconut
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, cardamom, sugar, milk, egg and butter into a dough and knead.
  2. Prove for 1 hour.
  3. Shape into buns and press a hollow in each. Fill with thick custard.
  4. Bake at 200C for 12 minutes.
  5. Cool, then spread icing on top and dip in coconut.

Drinks

Oaken's cardamom hot chocolateNo alcohol

Hot milk steeped with cardamom and cinnamon, finished with chocolate

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Steep and strain the spiced milk up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; reheat and whisk in the chocolate to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l full cream milk
  • 4 cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  • 1 handful mini marshmallows
  1. Warm the milk with the cardamom and cinnamon until steaming.
  2. Turn off the heat and steep for 10 minutes. Strain.
  3. Return to the heat and whisk in the chocolate, sugar and salt until smooth and frothy.
  4. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream, marshmallows and grated chocolate.

When to serve

Serve the chocolate when Anna arrives at the coronation party.
