Snacks

Ham and cheese puff pastry triangles

Puff pastry parcels filled with ham and cheddar

Prep 18 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Fill, fold and press the triangles up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; brush with egg and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g cheddar
  • 1 egg
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Cut the pastry into squares and grate the cheese.
  2. Fill each with ham and cheddar, fold into triangles and press the edges with a fork.
  3. Brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake until golden.

Purp fruit platterVegetarian

Purple plums, figs and red grapes with honey and lime

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Halve the plums and figs up to 2 hours ahead, covered; drizzle with honey and lime just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 plums
  • 500g red grapes
  • 4 fresh figs
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime
  1. Halve the plums and figs.
  2. Pile on a platter with bunches of grapes.
  3. Drizzle with honey and squeeze over lime juice.

Mains

Silver's galley stew

Old family recipe: beef chuck, onion, carrot, stout, potato.

Prep 17 min, cook 195 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 600g potatoes
  • 375ml stout
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Chop the onions, carrots and potatoes.
  2. Brown the beef in oil.
  3. Add the onions, carrots, stout and water to cover and simmer.
  4. Add the potatoes and simmer until thick.
  5. Serve in bowls with crusty bread.

Sweets

Flint's trove browniesVegetarian

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies cut into rectangles

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 bag chocolate coins
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a tin. Melt the chocolate and butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs, then fold in the flour.
  3. Bake.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Cut into rectangles and pile chocolate coins on top.

Drinks

Etherium blueberry lime fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Blueberry syrup and lime over ice, topped with fizz

Prep 7 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the blueberry syrup up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; add lemonade, lime and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g blueberries
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml lemonade
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer most of the blueberries with sugar and water, crushing them, then strain and cool.
  2. Cool.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice and the remaining berries.
  4. Add lime juice to each, then pour in the lemonade.
  5. Spoon the syrup slowly over the top so it sinks in layers.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Silver serves up his stew in the ship's galley.
