Snacks

Bacalaitos

Lacy salt cod fritters with garlic and fresh cilantro.

Prep 2 min, cook 23 min, 1 day waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 250g salt cod
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 300ml water
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 handful coriander, chopped
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Soak the salt cod in cold water for 24 hours, changing the water three times, then drain and flake.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, water, garlic and coriander into a loose batter and fold in the cod.
  3. Fry wide flat spoonfuls in 180°C oil in batches until golden and lacy, 15 min.
  4. Drain and salt.

TostonesVegetarian

Green plantain fried twice, smashed flat, spooned with garlic and lime mojo.

Prep 11 min, cook 14 min
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 500ml neutral oil, for frying
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 60ml olive oil
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Peel the plantains and cut into 3cm chunks.
  2. Fry at 160°C for 4 minutes until soft.
  3. Smash flat with a heavy glass and fry again at 190°C until crisp. Salt well, 8 min.
  4. Mix the garlic, lime juice and olive oil and spoon over.

Pernil

Garlic and oregano pork shoulder, roasted five hours to crackling. Rice with pigeon peas.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 hr 45 min, 8 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg pork shoulder, skin on
  • 8 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar
  • 400g long grain rice
  • 400g tin pigeon peas
  • 600ml chicken stock
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  1. Stab the pork all over and rub with garlic, oregano, salt and vinegar. Marinate overnight in the fridge.
  2. Roast skin up at 160°C for 5 hours.
  3. Raise the oven to 230°C for 20 minutes to crackle the skin.
  4. Cook the rice with the drained pigeon peas, stock and onion until tender, and serve alongside the sliced pork, 25 min.

Paradise flanVegetarian

Coconut caramel custard, baked slow, turned out cold.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 5 min, 10 hr waiting
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 395g tin condensed milk
  • 270ml coconut milk
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  • 5 eggs
  1. Melt the sugar in a pan until deep amber and pour into a ring tin, 8 min.
  2. Blend the condensed milk, coconut milk, evaporated milk and eggs and pour over the caramel.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 160°C for 50 minutes until just set.
  4. Chill overnight, run a knife around the edge and turn out onto a plate.

Paradise punch

Dark rum, pineapple, fresh lime and spiced syrup, long over ice, nutmeg grated on top.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min
Recipe for 4
  • 50g brown sugar
  • 50ml water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 360ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml lime juice
  • aromatic bitters, 4 dashes
  • 1 bag ice
  • nutmeg, freshly grated, to taste
  • 1 lime, in wheels
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and cinnamon for 3 minutes, then cool.
  2. In a jug, stir the rum, pineapple juice, lime juice, 40ml of the syrup and bitters.
  3. Pour over ice into four tall glasses, grate nutmeg on top and garnish with a lime wheel.

Piña colada

Rum, cream of coconut, pineapple, crushed ice.

When to serve

Have dessert out before Benny Blanco finds Carlito at Grand Central.
