Snacks

Frank the Tank's wings

Crisp baked wings tossed in hot sauce butter and honey, blue cheese dip

Prep 15 min, cook 765 min · Make ahead: Dry the wings overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 60g butter
  • 80ml hot sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 80g blue cheese
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 celery sticks
  1. Dry the wings uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce and honey and toss the wings.
  4. Mash the blue cheese into the sour cream to dip.
  5. Serve with celery sticks.

Ring of fire jalapeño poppers

Jalapeños stuffed with cream cheese and cheddar, wrapped in bacon and baked

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Fill the jalapenos up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; wrap in bacon and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 12 jalapenos
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 60g cheddar
  • 12 rashers bacon
  1. Halve and seed the jalapenos.
  2. Mix the cream cheese with the grated cheddar and fill each half.
  3. Wrap in half a rasher of bacon each.
  4. Bake at 200C until the bacon is crisp.

Mains

Backyard brisket

Dry rubbed brisket cooked 8 hours until it wobbles, with white bread and pickles

Prep 20 min, cook 560 min · Make ahead: Cook the day before, slice cold and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef brisket
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 2 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 bottle barbecue sauce
  1. Rub the brisket with salt, pepper and paprika.
  2. Wrap tightly.
  3. Cook at 140C until it wobbles.
  4. Rest for an hour.
  5. Slice thick and serve with white bread, pickles and barbecue sauce.

Sweets

Blue's birthday sheet cakeVegetarian

Chocolate tray cake covered in chocolate buttercream

Prep 30 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Bake a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 350g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 90g cocoa
  • 200ml full cream milk
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 jar sprinkles
  1. Beat part of the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, part of the cocoa and the milk.
  2. Bake in a tray at 175C.
  3. Cool.
  4. Beat the remaining butter, icing sugar and cocoa, spread on and cover with sprinkles.

Drinks

Frank's lemonade punch, ice includedVegetarian

Bourbon, lemon and peach nectar punch topped with cold lager

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the bourbon, lemon juice, syrup and peach nectar up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice, lager and garnish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 200ml bourbon
  • 4 lemons
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 500ml peach nectar
  • 750ml cold lager
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Stir the bourbon, lemon juice, syrup and peach nectar in a big bowl.
  2. Just before serving, add plenty of ice and top with lager.
  3. Float lemon wheels and mint on top and ladle into red plastic cups.
