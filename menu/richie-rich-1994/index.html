Snacks

Gold coin crackersVegetarian

Thyme parmesan crisps stacked like coins, with fig jam and brie

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp chopped thyme
  • 4 tbsp fig jam
  • 200g brie
  1. Mix the parmesan with the thyme. Spoon small rounds onto a lined tray and flatten into coins.
  2. Bake at 190C until golden and lacy, then cool on the tray.
  3. Serve stacked like coins with fig jam and a wedge of brie.

Mansion caviar blinis

Warm blinis with crème fraîche, salmon roe, chives and a squeeze of lemon

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 tbsp snipped chives
  • 1 lemon
  1. Warm the blinis in a low oven for 5 minutes.
  2. Top each with a small spoon of crème fraîche, a little salmon roe and a few chives.
  3. Add a tiny squeeze of lemon and lay them out in neat rows.

Mains

Richie's home burger bar

Smashed cheeseburgers on toasted buns, secret sauce under analysis

Prep 25 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp chopped pickles, for the sauce
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into fries
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Toss the potatoes, cut into fries, in oil and bake at 220C, turning once, until golden.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the chopped pickles for the special sauce. Roll the mince into 4 balls.
  3. Smash the balls flat on a very hot pan. Season, flip after 2 minutes and add the cheese.
  4. Build on toasted buns with onion, pickles and sauce. Serve with the fries.

Sweets

Gold vault sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, salted caramel sauce, crushed honeycomb and a gold chocolate coin

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Caramel up to 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • salt, a small pinch
  • 100g honeycomb, crushed
  • 4 gold chocolate coins
  1. Melt the sugar in a dry pan to amber, then whisk in the cream and salt. Cool slightly.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into tall glasses and pour over the caramel.
  3. Scatter with crushed honeycomb and top each sundae with a gold chocolate coin.

Backyard baseball milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick vanilla malted milkshake in a tall chilled glass

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 big scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and malt powder until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into tall chilled glasses, add a straw to each and serve straight away.
