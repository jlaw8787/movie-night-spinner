Snacks

Gold coin crackersVegetarian

Thyme parmesan crisps stacked like coins, with fig jam and brie.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp chopped thyme
  • 4 tbsp fig jam
  • 200g brie
  1. Mix the parmesan with the thyme. Spoon small rounds onto a lined tray and flatten into coins.
  2. Bake at 190°C until golden and lacy, then cool on the tray.
  3. Serve stacked like coins with fig jam and a wedge of brie.

Mansion caviar blinis

Warm blinis with crème fraîche, salmon roe, chives and a squeeze of lemon.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 16 blinis
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 50g salmon roe
  • 1 tbsp snipped chives
  • 1 lemon
  1. Warm the blinis in a low oven for 5 minutes.
  2. Top each with a small spoon of crème fraîche, a little salmon roe and a few chives.
  3. Add a tiny squeeze of lemon and lay them out in neat rows.

Professor Keenbean's popcorn chicken

Bite-size crumbed chicken fried crisp, honey mustard. Lab snack, impervious spray not included.

How it is made
  • 600g chicken thighs
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g cornflakes
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 30g parmesan
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bottle sriracha
  • 1 jar mayonnaise
  • 2L vegetable oil
  1. Bite-sized chicken thigh soaked in buttermilk, fried in a crunchy cornflake crust, tossed in a parmesan and garlic dust with a honey sriracha dip. Cut 600g chicken thighs into 2cm pieces and soak in 250ml buttermilk with salt for 1 hour. Crush 100g cornflakes and mix with 50g plain flour and 1 tsp garlic powder. Toss the chicken in the crumbs and fry at 175°C for 4 minutes until golden. Toss hot with 30g finely grated parmesan and a pinch of salt. Mix 3 tbsp honey with 1 tsp sriracha and 2 tbsp mayonnaise to dip.

Mains

Richie's home burger bar

Smashed cheeseburgers on toasted buns, secret sauce under analysis.

Prep 8 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, cook fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp chopped pickles, for the sauce
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into fries
  • 2 tbsp oil
  1. Toss the potatoes, cut into fries, in oil and bake at 220°C, turning once, until golden.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the chopped pickles for the special sauce. Roll the mince into 4 balls.
  3. Smash the balls flat on a very hot pan. Season, flip after 2 minutes and add the cheese.
  4. Build on toasted buns with onion, pickles and sauce. Serve with the fries.

Rich family mini beef Wellingtons

Seared fillet in mushroom duxelles, prosciutto and golden puff, thyme jus and buttery mash.

How it is made
  • 600g beef fillet
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 8 slices prosciutto
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  1. Seared beef fillet wrapped in mushroom duxelles, prosciutto and golden puff pastry, with a glossy beef and thyme jus and buttery mash. Sear 4 x 150g beef fillet steaks hard for 1 minute a side and cool. Fry 300g finely chopped mushrooms with garlic and thyme until dry. Lay 2 slices prosciutto on each, spread with mushroom, add a steak and wrap. Wrap each in a square of puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 220°C for 18 minutes. Reduce 400ml beef stock with thyme to a jus. Serve with mash.

Sweets

Gold vault sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, caramel sauce, crushed honeycomb and a gold chocolate coin.

Prep 3 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Caramel up to 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • salt, a small pinch
  • 100g honeycomb, crushed
  • 4 gold chocolate coins
  1. Melt the sugar in a dry pan to amber, then whisk in the cream and salt. Cool slightly.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into tall glasses and pour over the caramel.
  3. Scatter with crushed honeycomb and top each sundae with a gold chocolate coin.

Drinks

Backyard baseball milkshakeNo alcohol

Thick vanilla malted milkshake in a tall chilled glass.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 4 big scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 500ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk and malt powder until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into tall chilled glasses, add a straw to each and serve straight away.
