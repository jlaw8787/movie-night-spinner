Snacks

Rosemary spiced nuts and pretzelsVegetarian

Almonds, cashews and pretzels baked with rosemary, brown sugar and flaky salt

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep in an airtight container
Recipe for 4
  • 150g almonds
  • 150g cashews
  • 100g pretzels
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • flaky salt, to taste
  1. Toss the almonds, cashews and pretzels with the oil, rosemary, brown sugar and salt on a lined tray.
  2. Bake at 180C, stirring once, until golden and fragrant. Cool on the tray, then bag them up.

Jumping castle party pies

Mini beef pies with puff pastry tops and tomato sauce

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  1. Cook the onion in the oil, add the mince and brown it well. Stir in the flour, then the stock, and simmer until thick. Cool completely.
  2. Cut rounds from the shortcrust to line a patty tin and rounds from the puff for the lids. Fill the cases, top with the lids and press the edges to seal.
  3. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden. Squirt tomato sauce on top to serve.

Howard's beer-braised bratwurst bitesOn the printed menu

Sausages poached in lager and onion, grill-charred, hot mustard, for the cage fighter brothers

How it is made
  • 3 brown onions
  • 8 bratwurst
  • 375ml lager
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar hot English mustard
  1. Bratwurst simmered in lager with onions, then grilled until charred, sliced into bites and served on toothpicks with hot mustard and the sticky beer onions. Slice 3 onions and put in a wide pan with 8 bratwurst, 375ml lager and 20g butter. Simmer gently for 15 minutes. Lift out the sausages and grill over high heat until charred all over. Keep boiling the onions until the liquid is gone and they turn sticky and golden. Slice the bratwurst into thick bites, spear with toothpicks and serve with the onions and hot mustard.

Mains

Nativity play Christmas ham

Brown sugar and Dijon glazed ham with potato salad and green beans

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, glaze and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 3kg leg ham
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 oranges, juiced
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 300g green beans, trimmed
  • 150g mayonnaise
  1. Score the ham skin and rind. Mix the brown sugar, Dijon and orange juice into a glaze and brush it over.
  2. Bake at 180C, basting often, until the glaze is sticky and deep golden.
  3. Meanwhile boil the potatoes until tender, adding the beans for the last few minutes. Drain, cool and toss with the mayonnaise.
  4. Rest the ham for 10 minutes, then carve and serve with the potato salad.

Church supper beef lasagneOn the printed menu

Slow red wine ragù layered with pasta, béchamel and mozzarella, baked bubbling and rested

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 150ml red wine
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 50g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 50g parmesan
  • 300g lasagne sheets
  • 200g mozzarella
  1. Beef ragù simmered slowly with tomato and red wine, layered with lasagne sheets, béchamel and mozzarella, baked bubbling and rested before slicing. Brown 800g beef mince with an onion, carrot and 3 garlic cloves. Add 150ml red wine, 2 cans crushed tomatoes and oregano and simmer 2 hours. Melt 50g butter, stir in 50g flour, whisk in 600ml milk and simmer until thick, adding nutmeg and 50g parmesan. Layer ragù, 300g lasagne sheets and béchamel in a dish, finishing with béchamel and 200g mozzarella. Bake at 180C for 45 minutes and rest 20.

Sweets

Family games night trifle

Sherry sponge, raspberry jelly, custard, cream and fresh raspberries

Prep 10 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Best made the night before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 sponge cake, cut into chunks
  • 60ml sweet sherry
  • 1 packet raspberry jelly crystals
  • 500ml custard
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 125g raspberries
  1. Put the sponge chunks in a trifle bowl and drizzle with the sherry.
  2. Make up the jelly following the packet, pour it over the sponge and chill until set.
  3. Spoon over the custard, then the whipped cream. Chill overnight so it settles.
  4. Top with the raspberries just before serving.

Drinks

Cranberry, orange and rosemary punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry and orange juice with honey rosemary syrup and frozen cranberries

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 3 days, mix the punch on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 100ml water
  • 4 rosemary sprigs, plus extra to float
  • 750ml cranberry juice
  • 500ml fresh orange juice
  • 100g frozen cranberries
  • 2 oranges, sliced
  • 1l cold ginger ale
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the honey, water and 2 rosemary sprigs for 3 minutes, then cool and remove the rosemary.
  2. Mix the cranberry juice, orange juice and syrup in a big bowl. Add plenty of ice, the frozen cranberries and sliced oranges.
  3. Top with the ginger ale just before serving and float a few rosemary sprigs on top.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the fog cancels every flight out of San Francisco.
