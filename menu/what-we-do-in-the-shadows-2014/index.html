Snacks

Nick's hot chipsVegetarian

Thick double-fried chips served with garlic mayo

Prep 35 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • salt, to taste
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1 lemon
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips, soak and dry.
  2. Fry in the oil at 150C, rest, then fry at 190C until crunchy. Salt well.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with crushed garlic and a squeeze of lemon for aioli.
  4. Serve the aioli on the side.

Blood orange and beetroot crostiniVegetarian

Roasted beetroot, goat's cheese and blood orange on toasted baguette

Prep 7 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Beetroot up to 2 days ahead, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 beetroot
  • 1 baguette
  • 150g goat's cheese
  • 2 blood oranges
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Wrap the beetroot in foil and roast at 200C.
  2. Cool, peel and dice.
  3. Slice the baguette into rounds and toast.
  4. Spread with goat's cheese, top with beetroot and blood orange segments and drizzle with honey.

Garlic-free tomato and burrata bruschettaOn the printed menu

Grilled ciabatta, marinated cherry tomatoes, torn soft cheese and basil, vampire safe

How it is made
  • 400g mixed cherry tomatoes
  • olive oil
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 ciabatta
  • 250g burrata
  • sea salt flakes
  1. Grilled ciabatta piled with marinated cherry tomatoes and torn burrata, finished with basil, olive oil and flaky salt, not a clove of garlic in sight. Halve 400g mixed cherry tomatoes and toss with 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, torn basil and salt. Leave 15 minutes. Slice 1 ciabatta into 12 thick slices, brush with olive oil and grill or chargrill 2 minutes a side until marked. Tear 2 burrata balls (about 250g) over the toasts, spoon the tomatoes and their juices on top, and finish with more basil, a drizzle of oil and flaky salt.

Mains

Rare roast beef rolls

Rare sirloin sliced thin in soft rolls with horseradish cream

Prep 5 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Beef up to 1 day ahead, assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef sirloin
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 4 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 100g rocket
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the beef and sear on all sides in the olive oil in a hot pan.
  2. Roast at 200C for rare.
  3. Rest the beef, then slice thin.
  4. Fill the rolls with beef, horseradish cream and a handful of rocket each.

Black pudding with mash and onion gravyOn the printed menu

Fried black pudding with mash and onion gravy. A properly dark supper for the flat.

How it is made
  • 500g black pudding
  • 3 brown onions
  • 90g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 green apple
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  1. Thick slices of black pudding fried crisp, over buttery mash with caramelised onions, red wine gravy and a sharp apple and parsley salad. Slowly cook 3 sliced onions in 30g butter for 25 minutes until golden. Stir in 1 tbsp flour, 150ml red wine and 300ml beef stock and simmer 10 minutes. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 60g butter and 100ml warm milk. Slice 500g black pudding 1.5cm thick and fry in a little oil 2 minutes a side until crisp. Toss 1 thinly sliced green apple with parsley and lemon. Serve the pudding on the mash with the gravy and salad.

Unholy Masquerade gateauVegetarian

Chocolate sponge, morello cherries, whipped cream and dark chocolate

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 680g morello cherries, in syrup
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 50g dark chocolate
  1. Whisk the eggs and sugar until thick, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  2. Bake in a 20cm tin at 180C.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Split the cake in two and soak with the cherry syrup.
  5. Whip the cream, fill with cherries and cream, cover in cream and grate dark chocolate on top.

Fresh blood orange juice topped with soda water over ice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 7 blood oranges
  • 600ml soda water
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Squeeze 6 of the blood oranges.
  2. Fill 4 wine glasses with ice and pour the juice into each.
