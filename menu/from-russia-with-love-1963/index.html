Kerim Bey's sesame simitVegetarian

Istanbul's street bread, bought on the way to the bazaar

Prep 120 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and warm at 180C for 5 minutes; the dough can also rise slowly in the fridge overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 350g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 220ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 80ml grape molasses or date syrup
  • 80ml water, for the dip
  • 120g sesame seeds
  • 200g feta
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, warm water and oil and knead for 10 minutes until smooth.
  2. Cover and leave to rise for an hour until doubled.
  3. Heat the oven to 220C. Divide into 8, roll each into two thin ropes, twist them together and join the ends into a ring.
  4. Dip each ring in the molasses mixed with the water, then press into the sesame seeds on both sides.
  5. Rest on lined trays for 15 minutes.
  6. Bake for 15 minutes until deep golden.
  7. Cool slightly and serve with the feta.

Bosphorus stuffed mussels

Shells packed with spiced rice, pine nuts, currants, dill and mint, Galata waterfront style

Prep 75 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook the spiced rice up to 1 day ahead; stuff and steam the mussels on the day, they are best at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 150g medium grain rice
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts
  • 2 tbsp currants
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 450ml water
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried mint
  • 24 large black mussels
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  1. Soak the rice in hot water for 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Scrub and debeard the mussels, throwing out any that stay open when tapped.
  3. Fry the onion and pine nuts in the oil for 8 minutes until golden. Add the rice, allspice, cinnamon, tomato paste, sugar, salt, pepper, currants and 250ml of the water, cover and simmer for 10 minutes until the water is absorbed but the rice is still firm. Stir in the dill and mint.
  4. Spread the rice on a tray to cool.
  5. Slide a small knife into each mussel at the flat edge and open it without separating the shells. Fill with a spoonful of rice and close.
  6. Pack the mussels into a pot, pour in the rest of the water, weigh down with a plate, cover and steam for 20 minutes.
  7. Cool for 10 minutes and serve with the lemon wedges.

Orient Express dining car sole

The dinner where Bond should have watched the wine order

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best cooked just before serving; boil the potatoes and trim the beans up to a few hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g new potatoes
  • 250g green beans, trimmed
  • 600g sole or flounder fillets
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100g butter
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, about 15 minutes. Drain and keep warm.
  2. Blanch the beans in boiling water for 3 minutes and drain.
  3. Season the fish with salt and pepper and dust lightly with the flour.
  4. Fry the fillets in the oil and 20g of the butter for 2 minutes a side, in batches. Keep warm on plates.
  5. Wipe the pan, add the rest of the butter and cook until it smells nutty and turns light brown. Add the juice of 1 lemon, the capers and parsley.
  6. Spoon the butter over the plates and serve with the potatoes, beans and the other lemon in wedges.

Kerim Bey's camp supper lamb shish

Chargrilled skewers of spiced meat, capsicum and onion, bulgur and yoghurt, before the camp shootout

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Marinate the lamb up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb leg, diced
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 1 red onion
  • 250g coarse bulgur
  • 20g butter
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Toss the lamb with the paprika, cumin, 2 tbsp of the oil, half the lemon juice and salt. Thread onto skewers with chunks of capsicum and onion.
  2. Fry the bulgur in the butter for 1 minute, add 500ml water and salt, cover and simmer for 15 minutes, then rest for 5.
  3. Dice the tomatoes and cucumber and mix with chopped parsley, the rest of the oil and lemon.
  4. Grill the skewers on a very hot grill for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until charred.
  5. Serve on the pilaf with yoghurt and the salad.

Grand Bazaar rose Turkish delightVegetarian

Bought by the box in the bazaar while somebody watches Bond

Prep 736 min, cook 83 min · Make ahead: Cook and leave to set overnight at room temperature, at least 12 hours; keeps 2 weeks airtight in its dusting sugar
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tsp neutral oil, for the tin
  • 600g caster sugar
  • 800ml water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 100g cornflour, for the jelly
  • 1 tsp cream of tartar
  • 1 1/2 tbsp rosewater
  • pink food colouring, a few drops
  • 50g shelled pistachios
  • 3 tbsp icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour, for dusting
  1. Oil a 20cm square tin and line it with baking paper.
  2. Boil the sugar, 200ml of the water and the lemon juice to 115C on a sugar thermometer.
  3. In a second heavy pan whisk the cornflour for the jelly and the cream of tartar into the remaining 600ml water and cook, stirring, until it becomes a thick paste.
  4. Slowly whisk the hot syrup into the paste. Simmer very gently for an hour, stirring often, until pale gold and very thick.
  5. Stir in the rosewater, colouring and pistachios and pour into the tin.
  6. Leave uncovered at room temperature to set overnight.
  7. Mix the icing sugar and cornflour for dusting. Cut into cubes with an oiled knife and toss in the mix.

Kerim Bey's raki and pomegranateVegetarian

The station chief pours for his friends, cloudy and red

Prep 8 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the juice and soda and pick the pomegranate seeds up to 1 day ahead; build in the glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cup ice
  • 120ml raki
  • 30ml lemon juice
  • 20ml sugar syrup
  • 200ml pomegranate juice, chilled
  • 200ml soda water, chilled
  • 4 mint sprigs
  • 2 tbsp pomegranate seeds
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice.
  2. Add the raki, lemon juice and sugar syrup and stir until it turns cloudy.
  3. Top with the pomegranate juice and soda.
  4. Finish with a mint sprig and a few pomegranate seeds.
