Snacks

Baloo's banana bitesVegetarian

Frozen chocolate banana bites, the bare necessities.

Prep 19 min, cook 4 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep frozen
Recipe for 4
  • 3 bananas
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100g peanuts, crushed
  • 30g desiccated coconut
  1. Slice the bananas into thick rounds.
  2. Melt the chocolate gently, 4 min.
  3. Mix the crushed peanuts with the coconut.
  4. Dip each banana round halfway into the chocolate, then roll in the nut mix. Lay on a lined tray.
  5. Freeze until firm, 30 min.

King Louie's fruit platterVegetarian

Mango, pineapple, papaya and lychees with lime and salt.

Prep 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 banana leaves
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1 papaya
  • 1 can lychees, 400g, drained
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • chilli powder, a pinch
  1. Lay the banana leaves on a platter.
  2. Cut the mango, pineapple and papaya into wedges.
  3. Pile the fruit and lychees on the leaves.
  4. Squeeze over lime juice and sprinkle with salt mixed with a pinch of chilli.

Kaa's spinach and feta pastry coils

Spinach and feta rolled in puff pastry coils, baked golden.

How it is made
  • 300g spinach
  • 200g feta
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 200g natural yoghurt
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  1. Spinach, feta and dill wrapped in flaky pastry and coiled up like Kaa, baked golden, with a cooling mint yoghurt and a hit of chilli. Wilt 300g spinach, squeeze dry and chop. Mix with 200g crumbled feta, 1 egg, chopped dill and pepper. Cut 2 puff pastry sheets into 4 long strips each, spoon filling along each strip and roll over to enclose. Coil each strip into a snail shape on a lined tray, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 18 minutes. Mix 200g yoghurt with chopped mint and a pinch of chilli flakes to serve.

Man village chicken curry

Chicken in coconut and garam masala.

Prep 19 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (400g)
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 4 naan breads
  1. Dice the chicken and grate the ginger and garlic.
  2. Soften the ginger and garlic in the oil.
  3. Add the garam masala and fry for a minute.
  4. Add the chicken, tomatoes and coconut milk and simmer until the chicken is cooked through, 30 min.
  5. Cook the basmati rice according to the packet, 15 min.
  6. Warm the naan and serve with the curry and rice, 5 min.

Man village tadka dal with roti

Red lentils silky with turmeric and ginger, with sizzling cumin ghee.

How it is made
  • 300g red lentils
  • 1 jar ground turmeric
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 40g ghee
  • 1 jar cumin seeds
  • 1 packet dried chillies
  • 1 brown onion
  • 8 roti
  • 1 jar lime pickle
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Red lentils simmered silky with turmeric, garlic and ginger, finished with sizzling cumin, chilli and ghee, with rice, warm roti and lime pickle. Rinse 300g red lentils and simmer in 1.2L water with 1 tsp turmeric, 2 crushed garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger for 25 minutes until soft, then season. Cook 300g basmati rice. Heat 40g ghee, add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 dried chilli and 1 sliced onion and fry until golden, then pour over the dal sizzling. Warm 8 roti and serve with lime pickle and coriander.

Sweets

Coconut mango pudding

Set coconut milk with mango, cool enough for a burning tail.

Prep 10 min, cook 14 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 4 gelatine sheets
  • 2 mangoes
  • 30g shredded coconut
  1. Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water until soft, 5 min.
  2. Warm the coconut milk with the sugar until dissolved, 5 min.
  3. Squeeze the gelatine and stir it into the warm milk until melted.
  4. Pour into glasses and chill until set (about 4 hours).
  5. Toast the shredded coconut in a dry pan, 4 min.
  6. Dice the mango and top the puddings with mango and toasted coconut.

Drinks

Colonel Hathi's dawn patrol mango lassiNo alcohol

Ripe mango, yoghurt and cardamom blended over ice, for Colonel Hathi's dawn patrol.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Peel and cut the mango up to 1 day ahead, chilled; blend and pour over ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 ripe mangoes
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 250ml cold milk
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Peel the mangoes and cut the flesh from the stones.
  2. Blend the mango with the yoghurt, milk, sugar and cardamom until smooth.
  3. Pour over ice in tall glasses.
