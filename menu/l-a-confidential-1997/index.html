Formosa Cafe pork egg rolls with hot mustard

Crisp rolls, pork and cabbage, Chinese mustard.

Prep 21 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to a day ahead; roll up to 4 hours ahead and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pork mince
  • 2 cups wombok, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 12 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard powder
  1. Stir-fry the pork until browned, add the wombok, carrot, spring onion and ginger and cook until wilted, then season with the soy and sesame oil and cool, 12 min.
  2. Lay out a wrapper, add a spoon of filling, fold in the sides and roll up tightly, sealing with a dab of water.
  3. Mix the mustard powder with a little cold water to a smooth, hot paste.
  4. Fry the rolls in oil at 180°C in batches until deep golden, then drain, 12 min.
  5. Serve hot with the mustard.

Frolic Room devilled eggs with paprikaVegetarian

Creamy yolks, mustard, cayenne, chives. Jack leaves his payola as a tip.

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill the eggs up to a day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; dust with paprika to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes, then cool in iced water.
  2. Peel, halve lengthways and tip the yolks into a bowl.
  3. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, cayenne and a pinch of salt until smooth.
  4. Pipe or spoon back into the whites, dust with paprika and top with chives.

Nite Owl chilli size with onions and cheddar

Grilled patty under beef chilli, cheese, onion.

Prep 10 min, cook 1 hr · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and reheat; grill the patties to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 4 thick slices of white bread, toasted
  • 120g cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  1. Brown 400g of the mince with the onion and garlic, then add the chilli powder and cumin, 10 min.
  2. Add the tomatoes, beans and stock and simmer until thick and dark, 45 min.
  3. Shape the rest of the mince into 4 thick patties, season and grill or pan-fry until browned and just cooked, 10 min.
  4. Put each patty on a slice of toast, ladle over the chilli and top with cheddar and raw onion, 5 min.

Ghost of Christmas Past ham steak with grilled pineapple

Brown sugar glaze, pineapple, buttered greens.

Prep 2 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Mix the glaze up to 3 days ahead; grill the ham to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 thick ham steaks
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cloves
  • 4 fresh pineapple rings
  • 300g green beans
  • 30g butter
  • 600g small potatoes
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, adding the beans for the last 3 minutes, then drain and toss with the butter.
  2. Mix the sugar, mustard, vinegar and cloves into a glaze.
  3. Sear the ham steaks in a hot pan, brush with glaze and turn until sticky and caramelised, 6 min.
  4. Grill the pineapple in the same pan until charred, and serve on the ham with the potatoes and beans, 4 min.

Rollo Tomasi's cherry pie à la modeVegetarian

Lattice cherry pie, vanilla ice cream.

Prep 23 min, cook 45 min, 12 min waiting · Make ahead: Bake the pie up to a day ahead and keep it at room temperature; warm slices to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of frozen shortcrust pastry, thawed
  • 2 x 680g jars of pitted morello cherries, drained, 125ml juice kept
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1/2 tsp almond essence
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  1. Line a 22cm pie dish with one sheet of pastry and chill, 8 min.
  2. Warm the cherries, kept juice, sugar, cornflour and lemon juice, stirring, until thick and glossy, then stir in the almond and cool, 12 min.
  3. Fill the pie, cut the second sheet into strips and weave a lattice over the top, then brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until the pastry is deep golden and the filling bubbles, 45 min.
  5. Cool a little and serve warm with ice cream, 5 min.

All the whiskey in Ireland coffee

Hot coffee, Irish whiskey, brown sugar, cream.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Lightly whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep it cold
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml strong hot coffee
  • 160ml Irish whiskey
  • 4 tsp brown sugar
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Warm 4 glasses with hot water, then tip it out.
  2. Stir a teaspoon of sugar and 40ml whiskey into each, then fill with coffee.
  3. Lightly whip the cream and float it over the back of a spoon.
