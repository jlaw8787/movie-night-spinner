Snacks

Knowhere market dumplings

Pan-fried pork and cabbage dumplings with chilli oil and soy sauce

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 200g wombok, finely shredded
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100ml water
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  1. Mix the pork with the wombok, ginger and salt.
  2. Fill the wrappers and pleat closed.
  3. Fry in oil until crisp underneath.
  4. Add a splash of water, cover and steam until cooked through.
  5. Serve with chilli oil and soy sauce.

Rocket's smoky roasted chickpeas and nutsVegetarian

Crunchy roasted chickpeas and peanuts with smoked paprika and garlic

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roast up to 5 days ahead, cool completely and keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 2 cans chickpeas
  • 150g peanuts
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Drain and dry the chickpeas very well.
  2. Toss with the peanuts, oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder and salt and roast at 200C, shaking the tray now and then, until crunchy.
  3. Cool and pile into a bowl.

Mains

Orgocorp crackling pork belly with mash

Crackling roast pork belly with apple sauce and buttery mashed potatoes

Prep 50 min, cook 195 min · Make ahead: Pork can be salted a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork belly
  • 2 tbsp salt flakes
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g butter
  • 3 green apples, peeled and chopped
  • 100ml water
  1. Score the skin and rub with salt, then dry in the fridge overnight if you can.
  2. Roast at 160C until tender.
  3. Blast at 240C until the crackling bubbles.
  4. Meanwhile boil the potatoes and mash with butter.
  5. Simmer the apples with water into a sauce.
  6. Slice the pork and serve over the mash with apple sauce.

Sweets

Captain Rocket's chocolate fudge cakeVegetarian

Chocolate cake with fudge icing, sprinkles and one little candle

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 50g cocoa
  • 250ml milk
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, then the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa and milk and pour into a lined tin.
  3. Bake at 175C and cool completely.
  4. Beat a fudge icing from extra butter, icing sugar and cocoa and spread over the cake.
  5. Top with sprinkles and one little candle.

Drinks

Kraglin's rum and ginger grogVegetarian

Ginger beer with lime and bitters, dark rum floated on top

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best built to order; squeeze the lime juice up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 4 limes
  • 4 dashes bitters
  • 750ml ginger beer
  • 180ml dark rum
  • 4 pieces crystallised ginger
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and squeeze lime juice into each.
  2. Add a dash of bitters and ginger beer to each glass.
  3. Float dark rum over the top so it clouds down through the glass.
  4. Garnish with a lime wedge and crystallised ginger.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the Guardians land on Counter-Earth.
