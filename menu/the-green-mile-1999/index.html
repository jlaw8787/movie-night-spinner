Mrs Edgecomb's cornbread with honey butterVegetarian

Cornbread baked in cast iron, honey butter melting on the warm slices.

Prep 5 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Bake up to a day ahead and warm in the oven; beat the honey butter up to a week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 150g fine cornmeal (polenta)
  • 125g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g butter, plus 80g soft butter for the honey butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Heat a 23cm cast iron pan in a 220°C oven with 20g of the butter in it, 5 min.
  2. Whisk the dry ingredients with a good pinch of salt, then whisk in the buttermilk, eggs and 100g melted butter.
  3. Pour into the hot pan and bake until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Beat the soft butter with the honey and salt flakes and serve with the warm cornbread, 5 min.

Cornmeal fried okra with comeback sauceVegetarian

Crisp cornmeal okra, spicy comeback sauce. A Cold Mountain supper.

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the comeback sauce up to 3 days ahead; fry the okra to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g fresh okra, sliced thickly
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine cornmeal (polenta)
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 lemon
  1. Stir the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, hot sauce and a squeeze of lemon into a comeback sauce.
  2. Soak the okra in the buttermilk while you mix the cornmeal, flour, paprika and salt, 5 min.
  3. Toss the okra in the cornmeal mix to coat well.
  4. Fry in batches in oil at 180°C until crisp and golden, then drain and salt.
  5. Serve hot with the sauce.

Mr Jingles' pimento cheese and crackersVegetarian

Sharp cheddar mixed with roasted capsicum, served with crackers.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Make up to 3 days ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 250g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 100g cream cheese, softened
  • 80g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 100g roasted red capsicum, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 box small salted crackers
  • 2 celery sticks, cut in batons
  1. Beat the cream cheese and mayonnaise until smooth.
  2. Fold in the cheddar, capsicum, paprika and hot sauce and season with pepper.
  3. Pile into a bowl and serve with the crackers and celery.

Mains

Last meal meatloaf with mash and gravy

Glazed meatloaf, buttery mash, onion gravy. The meal before the Mile.

Prep 5 min, cook 102 min · Make ahead: Shape the meatloaf up to a day ahead and chill; bake and make the mash to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef and pork mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated, plus 1 sliced for the gravy
  • 60g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 125ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1kg floury potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch silverbeet, shredded
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk, then mix with the mince, grated onion, egg, thyme and plenty of salt and pepper, 10 min.
  2. Shape into a loaf on a lined tray, stir the tomato sauce, sugar and vinegar together and brush half over.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 1 hour, brushing on the rest of the glaze halfway, then rest it.
  4. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with half the butter and a splash of the milk.
  5. Fry the sliced onion in the rest of the butter until brown, stir in the flour, then whisk in the stock and simmer into gravy.
  6. Wilt the silverbeet in a hot pan, slice the meatloaf thickly and serve with the mash, gravy and greens.

Top Hat peach cobbler with vanilla ice creamVegetarian

Spiced peaches under a biscuit crust.

Prep 18 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry topping up to 2 days ahead; assemble and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 825g tins of sliced peaches, drained
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 90g cold butter, cubed
  • 180ml buttermilk
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the peaches with the brown sugar, cinnamon, cornflour and lemon juice in a baking dish, 5 min.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and caster sugar, then stir in the buttermilk to a soft dough.
  3. Drop spoonfuls of dough over the peaches, leaving gaps for the juice to bubble through, 5 min.
  4. Bake at 190°C until the topping is golden and the fruit is bubbling.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes and serve warm with the ice cream.

Faded limes coolerNo alcohol

Lime, mint, raw sugar and soda, the colour of faded limes on the floor.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the lime syrup up to 3 days ahead; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 6 limes
  • 100g raw sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 750ml soda water, chilled
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Heat the sugar and water with strips of lime peel until dissolved, then cool and strain.
  2. Squeeze the limes and stir the juice into the syrup.
  3. Pour over ice and mint in 4 glasses and top with soda.
