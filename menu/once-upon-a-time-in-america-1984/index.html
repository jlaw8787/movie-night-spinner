Snacks

Delancey Street knishesVegetarian

Hot mustard, Lower East Side street food

Prep 11 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead; reheat at 180°C for 10 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 500g potatoes, peeled
  • 30g butter
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • salt and white pepper, to taste
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 40g hot English mustard
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain and mash.
  2. Cook the onion in the butter over low heat until golden, then mix into the mash with salt and white pepper.
  3. Cut each pastry sheet into 4 squares, fill, fold into parcels and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden and puffed.
  5. Serve with the hot mustard.

Bagel crisps with schmear

Lower East Side breakfast

Prep 12 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Crisps up to 2 days airtight; schmear up to 1 day chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 day-old bagels
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 150g smoked salmon
  1. Slice the bagels into thin rounds and brush with olive oil.
  2. Bake at 180°C until crisp.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with chives and lemon zest.
  4. Serve the crisps with the schmear and smoked salmon on the side.

Lower East Side herring and rye bitesOn the printed menu

Pickled herring on buttered dark bread, sour cream, dill and onion, for Noodles and the boys

How it is made
  • 6 slices dark rye bread
  • 30g butter
  • 250g pickled herring fillets
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 dill pickles
  • 1 small red onion
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Pickled herring on buttered dark rye with sour cream, red onion, dill and a sliced dill pickle for crunch. Cut 6 slices dark rye into quarters and toast lightly. Butter each piece thinly. Drain 250g pickled herring fillets and cut into 24 bite-sized pieces. Mix 100g sour cream with 1 tsp lemon juice and a pinch of pepper. Spoon a little sour cream onto each toast, top with herring, a slice of dill pickle and a few rings of very thinly sliced red onion. Finish with chopped dill.

Fat Moe's back room brisket

Slow braised with onions and gravy

Prep 5 min, cook 4.5 hours · Make ahead: Cook up to 2 days ahead, slice cold and reheat in the gravy
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg beef brisket
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 3 brown onions, sliced
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  1. Season the brisket and sear hard on all sides.
  2. Spread the onions in a roasting dish and sit the brisket on top. Pour over the stock whisked with tomato paste and paprika.
  3. Cover and braise at 150°C for 4 hours.
  4. Rest for 20 minutes, slice against the grain.
  5. Boil the juices for 10 minutes to make gravy.

Patsy's charlotte russeVegetarian

Sponge, whipped cream and a cherry, try not to eat it on the stairs

Prep 25 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 6 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g sponge cake
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 100g mascarpone
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 8 maraschino cherries
  • 8 paper cups
  1. Cut 8 rounds of sponge to fit the bottom of the paper cups.
  2. Whip the cream with mascarpone, icing sugar and vanilla to firm peaks.
  3. Pipe high over the sponge in a tall swirl and top each with a cherry.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

Fat Moe's rye whiskey sour in teacupsVegetarian

Rye shaken with lemon and egg white, poured into china cups so it passes for tea

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 50ml hot water
  • 120ml rye whiskey
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 1 egg white
  • aromatic bitters, 8 drops
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water and cool to make syrup.
  2. Shake the whiskey, lemon juice, 40ml of the syrup and egg white without ice for 15 seconds, then with ice for 15 seconds.
  3. Strain into four china teacups, dot the foam with bitters and serve straight away.

When to serve

Bring the charlotte russe out when young Patsy sits on the stairs and eats the one he bought for Peggy.
