Cookfire buttermilk biscuitsVegetarian

Hot buttermilk biscuits. Better than a ship's biscuit.

Prep 21 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Rub in the butter and cut the rounds up to 1 day ahead, chilled on a tray; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g cold butter, diced
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 50g butter, for spreading
  • 3 tbsp honey
  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour and salt.
  2. Stir in the buttermilk until it just comes together.
  3. Pat out and cut into rounds.
  4. Bake at 220°C until tall and golden.
  5. Split while warm and spread with butter and honey.

Grandmother Willow's harvest nutsVegetarian

Maple-roasted pecans and walnuts. The spinning arrow, straight from her dream.

Prep 7 min, cook 12 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Roast and cool completely so they set, then keep up to 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 100g pecans
  • 100g walnuts
  • 50g pepitas
  • 4 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Toss the pecans, walnuts and pepitas with the maple syrup and salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray.
  3. Roast at 170°C for a few minutes, stirring once.
  4. Cool completely so they set.
  5. Break into rough clusters.

Powhatan corn fritters with lime cremaOn the printed menu

Sweet kernels, spring onion and chilli fried crisp and lacy, zingy sour cream dip, smoked salt

How it is made
  • 400g corn kernels
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 80ml milk
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 lime
  • 1 jar smoked salt
  1. Sweet corn fritters with spring onion and chilli, fried crisp and lacy, with a zingy lime and sour cream dip and a pinch of smoked salt. Mix 400g corn kernels with 100g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 2 eggs, 80ml milk, 3 sliced spring onions, 1 chopped red chilli and salt. Heat oil in a frying pan over medium heat and fry heaped tablespoons for 2 minutes a side until golden and crisp. Drain on paper towel. Mix 200g sour cream with the zest and juice of 1 lime. Sprinkle the fritters with smoked salt and serve with the crema.

Virginia harvest stew

Chicken thighs, pumpkin, corn, kidney beans and tomato with smoked paprika

Prep 25 min, cook 68 min · Make ahead: Stew up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thighs, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 500g butternut pumpkin, diced
  • 1 can corn kernels
  • 1 can kidney beans, drained
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Dice the chicken and pumpkin.
  2. Brown the chicken in the oil.
  3. Add the pumpkin, corn, beans, tomatoes and paprika.
  4. Simmer until thick and the pumpkin is tender.
  5. Meanwhile mix the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, egg, milk and oil into a batter.
  6. Pour into a greased tin and bake at 200°C until golden.
  7. Serve the stew with slices of cornbread.

Jamestown beef and onion pieOn the printed menu

Beef and onion pie for the settlers digging for Ratcliffe's gold.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 3 brown onions
  • 600ml beef stock
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar English mustard
  1. Beef chuck slow cooked with sweet onions, thyme and stock until rich, baked under a golden puff pastry lid with mustard on the side. Toss 1kg diced beef chuck in 2 tbsp plain flour and brown in batches. Soften 3 sliced onions, then add the beef, 600ml beef stock, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce and thyme. Cover and simmer 2 hours until tender and thick, then cool slightly. Spoon into a pie dish, cover with a puff pastry sheet, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes. Serve with hot English mustard.

Maple roasted peachesVegetarian

Peach halves roasted with butter and maple syrup

Prep 10 min, cook 19 min · Make ahead: Roast the peaches up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; warm in the oven for 5 minutes and serve with ice cream
Recipe for 4
  • 4 ripe peaches
  • 50g butter
  • 4 tbsp maple syrup
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 50g pecans, crushed
  1. Halve the peaches and remove the stones.
  2. Sit cut side up in a baking dish, dot with butter and drizzle with maple syrup.
  3. Roast at 200°C until soft and syrupy.
  4. Crush the pecans.
  5. Serve warm with ice cream and pecans on top.

Flit's blueberry mint coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Blueberries and mint. Flit hovers over the first taste.

Prep 11 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Muddle the berries and stir in the cranberry juice, then chill up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g blueberries
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 500ml sparkling water
  • 3 cups crushed ice
  1. Muddle most of the blueberries with the mint, lime juice and honey in a jug until the berries burst.
  2. Stir in the cranberry juice and chill.
  3. Fill glasses with crushed ice and pour in the berry mix to halfway.
  4. Top with sparkling water and garnish with blueberries and a mint sprig.
