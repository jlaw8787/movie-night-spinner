Snacks

Nai Nai's pan-fried pork dumplings

Pork and ginger dumplings, crisp underneath, with black vinegar and chilli oil.

Prep 48 min, cook 8 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Filling up to 1 day ahead, fill and fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 200g wombok
  • 400g pork mince
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 30 round dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  1. Finely chop the wombok and salt it.
  2. Leave the wombok for 10 minutes, then squeeze dry.
  3. Mix with the pork mince, ginger, spring onions, soy and sesame oil.
  4. Fill the dumpling wrappers, wet the edges and pleat closed.
  5. Fry in oil until golden underneath.
  6. Add water, cover and steam until dry, 6 min.
  7. Mix the black vinegar, soy and chilli oil for dipping.

Changchun smashed cucumber with chilli oilVegetarian

Smashed cucumber in garlic, black vinegar and chilli oil dressing.

Prep 11 min, cook 3 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 bunch of coriander
  1. Smash the cucumbers with the flat of a knife until they split, then cut into bite-size chunks.
  2. Toss with salt and sugar.
  3. Drain in a sieve for 15 minutes.
  4. Mix the crushed garlic, black vinegar, soy, sesame oil and chilli oil.
  5. Toss the cucumbers in the dressing and top with toasted sesame seeds and coriander.

Mains

Wedding banquet crispy sweet and sour pork

Twice-fried pork with sweet pepper and pineapple in sweet and sour sauce.

Prep 26 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Eat straight away for the best crunch
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pork scotch fillet
  • 1 egg
  • 100g potato starch
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 150g pineapple
  • 4 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Slice the pork thinly, season and toss in egg white, then potato starch.
  2. Heat the oil to 180°C and fry the pork in batches, then rest, 3 min.
  3. Fry again in the oil until very crisp.
  4. Julienne the carrot and slice the capsicum and garlic.
  5. Stir-fry the capsicum, carrot, garlic and pineapple.
  6. Mix the rice vinegar, sugar, tomato sauce, soy and cornflour with water.
  7. Add the sauce and bubble until glossy, 3 min.
  8. Cook the rice, 15 min.
  9. Toss in the pork and serve with rice.

Banquet steamed fish with ginger and spring onion

Whole snapper steamed over ginger, soy and sizzling oil, shredded spring onion.

Prep 6 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Shred the ginger and spring onion up to 4 hours ahead; steam the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 whole snapper, about 900g, cleaned
  • 40g ginger, cut in fine matchsticks
  • 4 spring onions, finely shredded
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Score the fish twice on each side and lay on a plate on half the ginger.
  2. Steam over rapidly boiling water until the flesh at the thickest part flakes, 12 min.
  3. Warm the soy with the sugar and 2 tbsp water. Pour off the steaming liquid and spoon the soy over the fish, 3 min.
  4. Pile the rest of the ginger, the spring onion and coriander on top. Heat the oil until smoking and pour it over so it sizzles, 3 min.

Banquet red bean sesame ballsVegetarian

Chewy sesame rice balls filled with sweet red bean paste.

Prep 35 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 hours ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 250g glutinous rice flour
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 200g red bean paste (tin)
  • 80g white sesame seeds
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  1. Mix the rice flour with sugar, stir in hot water and knead to a smooth, soft dough.
  2. Divide into balls, flatten each, fill with red bean paste and roll closed.
  3. Wet lightly and roll in sesame seeds.
  4. Fry in oil at 160°C, pressing gently against the side of the pot so they puff, until golden, 8 min.

Iced jasmine lychee teaNo alcohol

Jasmine tea brewed strong, chilled and shaken with lychee syrup.

Prep 11 min, cook 3 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 jasmine tea bags
  • a 565g tin lychees
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Steep the jasmine tea bags in water just off the boil, 3 min.
  2. Leave for 3 minutes, then remove the bags and cool.
  3. Drain the lychees, keeping some syrup.
  4. Stir the syrup and lime juice into the tea.
  5. Chill until cold, 1 hr.
  6. Fill glasses with ice and a few lychees and pour over the tea.
