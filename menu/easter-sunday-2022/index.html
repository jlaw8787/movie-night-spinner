Snacks

Valencia family pork lumpia

Crisp fried pork spring rolls with vinegar and sweet chilli dips

Prep 41 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Roll up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 20 spring roll wrappers
  • 100ml white vinegar
  • 1 red chilli
  • 150ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Mix the pork mince with grated carrot, onion, garlic, spring onions, egg, soy and pepper.
  2. Lay filling along the edge of each wrapper in a thin line and roll up tightly, sealing with water.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C until deep golden.
  4. Mix the white vinegar with crushed garlic, chilli and pepper.
  5. Serve with the vinegar and sweet chilli sauce.

Easter party pancit with calamansi

Stir-fried rice noodles with chicken, Chinese sausage and vegetables

Prep 30 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 250g rice vermicelli
  • 300g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 Chinese sausage
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 100g snow peas
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 limes
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Soak the vermicelli in hot water, then drain.
  2. Slice the chicken, sausage, onion, cabbage and carrot.
  3. Stir-fry the chicken and sausage in oil.
  4. Add the onion, garlic, cabbage, carrot and snow peas.
  5. Add the stock, soy and oyster sauce, then the noodles, and toss until absorbed.
  6. Serve with lime or calamansi wedges and spring onion.

Mains

Mum's Easter chicken adobo

Chicken braised in garlic, vinegar, soy and bay leaves

Prep 22 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Adobo up to 2 days ahead, fry the rice fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg bone-in chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 12 garlic cloves
  • 120ml white vinegar
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 4 eggs
  1. Crush the garlic.
  2. Brown the chicken in oil.
  3. Add the garlic, vinegar, soy, bay leaves, peppercorns and water.
  4. Simmer uncovered, turning, until the sauce is sticky.
  5. Fry the cooked rice with crushed garlic in oil until crisp in spots.
  6. Fry the eggs.
  7. Serve the adobo over the rice with an egg on top.

Valencia family garlic fried riceVegetarian

Garlic fried rice, crisp garlic and day-old rice, for the adobo sauce

Prep 5 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Cook the rice 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; fry on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 8 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  1. Cook the rice the day before, spread it out and chill it uncovered so it dries.
  2. Fry the garlic in the oil over medium heat until golden. Lift out half for the top.
  3. Add the cold rice, break up any clumps and fry hot with salt until it crackles.
  4. Fry the eggs and serve on the rice with the crisp garlic and spring onion.

Tita's silky leche flanVegetarian

Smooth caramel topped egg custard made with condensed milk

Prep 6 min, cook 68 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 395g condensed milk (tin)
  • 370ml evaporated milk (tin)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Melt the sugar in a pan until deep amber and pour into a 20cm dish, tilting to coat.
  2. Whisk the egg yolks with condensed milk, evaporated milk and vanilla without making bubbles, then strain over the caramel.
  3. Cover with foil.
  4. Bake in a water bath at 160°C until just set.
  5. Cool, then chill overnight.
  6. Turn out onto a plate.

Drinks

Calamansi lime and honey coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Calamansi juice, honey and sparkling water over ice

Prep 21 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 150ml calamansi juice
  • 3 limes
  • 1 mandarin
  • 800ml soda water
  • 1 bag of crushed ice
  1. Stir the honey into hot water until dissolved.
  2. Squeeze the calamansi juice, or use lime and mandarin juice.
  3. Mix the juice and honey syrup in a jug.
  4. Chill until cold.
  5. Fill glasses with crushed ice, add the juice and top with cold soda water.
  6. Garnish with lime wheels.

When to serve

Serve the main when the whole family sits down for Easter dinner.
