Dawn Treader cheddar oat biscuitsVegetarian

Crisp biscuits of oats, flour and vintage cheddar

Prep 16 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 80g cold butter
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 80g vintage cheddar, grated, for the dough
  • 3 tbsp cold milk
  • 150g vintage cheddar, sliced, for the topping
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Rub the butter into the flour, oats and salt, then stir in grated cheddar and milk to form a dough.
  2. Roll 5mm thick, cut into squares and prick with a fork.
  3. Bake until golden.
  4. Cool.
  5. Top each with a slice of cheddar, a spoon of chutney and a slice of pickle.

Lone Islands market olivesVegetarian

Green and kalamata olives warmed in olive oil with lemon peel, garlic and chilli

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Warm the olives with the zest, garlic and chilli up to 3 days ahead, kept chilled; rewarm and serve with bread
Recipe for 4
  • 150g green olives
  • 150g kalamata olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Peel strips of lemon zest and slice the garlic.
  2. Warm the olives gently in oil with the zest, garlic and chilli.
  3. Tip into a bowl and serve warm with torn bread.

Dufflepud garlic butter mushroomsOn the printed menu

Button caps stuffed with parsley butter, crumbs and parmesan, baked bubbling, for invisible hosts

How it is made
  • 20 large button mushrooms
  • 80g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 40g breadcrumbs
  • 30g parmesan
  1. Button mushrooms stuffed with garlic butter, breadcrumbs and parmesan, baked at 200°C for 12 minutes. Remove the stalks from 20 large button mushrooms and chop the stalks finely. Mix 80g soft butter with 2 crushed garlic cloves, the chopped stalks, chopped parsley and salt. Fill the mushroom caps with the butter. Mix 40g breadcrumbs with 30g grated parmesan and press on top. Bake on a tray at 200°C for 12 minutes until bubbling and golden. Serve hot with toothpicks.

Mains

Sea voyage fish

Crisp skin snapper in lemon caper butter, lightly crushed baby potatoes

Prep 5 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Boil and crush the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, kept chilled, and reheat in a pan; fry the fish on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 60g butter
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • salt, to taste
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, crush lightly and season with salt.
  2. Pan-fry the fish in the olive oil, skin side down until crisp, then flip for a minute.
  3. Add butter, lemon juice and capers to the pan.
  4. Spoon over the fish and serve with the potatoes.

Aslan's table roast chicken and grapesOn the printed menu

Lemon and herb roast chicken with roast grapes and potatoes, enough for all at Aslan's table

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 40g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 500g red grapes
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Roast chicken with lemon and herbs, roast potatoes, grapes roasted on the vine, and crusty bread laid out on a long table. Rub a 1.8kg chicken with 40g soft butter, lemon zest, thyme and salt and stuff with the halved lemon. Roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potatoes in chunks, rough them up and roast in oil for 50 minutes. Add 4 small bunches of red grapes on the vine to the chicken tray for the last 15 minutes until they blister. Rest the chicken 10 minutes and lay everything out on a board with torn crusty bread.

Sea of lilies pavlovaVegetarian

Meringue topped with whipped cream and thin slices of white peach

Prep 26 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and cool the meringue up to 2 days ahead, airtight; whip the cream and top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 3 white peaches
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Shape into a round on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120°C, then cool in the oven.
  4. Whip the cream and slice the peaches thinly.
  5. Top the pavlova with cream and peach slices.

Drinks

Sweet water elderflower lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Lemon juice and elderflower cordial with cucumber ribbons and mint

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Mix the lemon juice, cordial and water up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add the mint, ice, cucumber and soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lemons
  • 100ml elderflower cordial
  • 700ml cold water
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 handful mint
  • 330ml soda water
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the lemons. Peel the cucumber into long ribbons.
  2. Mix the lemon juice, cordial and water in a jug and tear in the mint.
  3. Fill four glasses with ice and cucumber ribbons.
  4. Pour in the lemonade and top with a splash of soda water.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the crew lays the seven swords on Aslan's table.
