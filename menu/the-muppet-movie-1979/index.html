Fozzie's wocka wocka nachosVegetarian

Corn chips under black beans and melted cheddar. Better than Fozzie's jokes.

Prep 17 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Make the beans and the salsa up to a day ahead; build and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 230g bag plain corn chips
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 lime
  • 125g sour cream
  • 1 avocado
  1. Warm the beans with the cumin and a splash of water, mashing a few, 5 min.
  2. Mix the tomato and onion with half the lime juice and a pinch of salt.
  3. Layer the chips, beans and cheese twice in a baking dish and bake at 200°C until the cheese bubbles, 10 min.
  4. Mash the avocado with the rest of the lime and top the nachos with salsa, avocado and sour cream, 8 min.

Bogen County Fair corn cobsVegetarian

Corn cobs dripping with honey chilli butter, fit for Miss Bogen County.

Prep 6 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Mix the butter up to 3 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; grill the corn to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs, husks off, halved
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp mild chilli flakes
  • 1 lime, cut in wedges
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  1. Mash the butter with the honey, chilli and a pinch of salt.
  2. Boil the corn for 4 minutes, then drain.
  3. Grill or pan-char the cobs, turning, until spotted brown all over, 8 min.
  4. Brush with the honey butter, scatter with parmesan and serve with lime.

Not-frog-legs fried chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken drumsticks, guaranteed frog-free.

Prep 23 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk up to a day ahead; dredge and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp mild hot sauce
  1. Soak the drumsticks in the buttermilk with a big pinch of salt for at least an hour.
  2. Mix the flour with the paprika, garlic powder and salt, and dredge the drumsticks well.
  3. Fry in oil at 160°C in batches, turning, until deep golden and cooked through, 25 min.
  4. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the mayo and vinegar.
  5. Drain the chicken and serve with the slaw and hot sauce.

Desert campfire chilli

Beef and bean chilli with cornbread, made for a campfire under the desert stars.

Prep 10 min, cook 115 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 2 days ahead and reheat; bake the cornbread on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 750g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 800g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 150g fine polenta
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g butter, melted
  1. Brown the mince in batches, then soften the onion and garlic in the same pan, 15 min.
  2. Stir in the spices, tomatoes, beans and stock and simmer gently, stirring now and then, 1 hr 15 min.
  3. Whisk the polenta, flour, milk, eggs, butter and a pinch of salt into a batter.
  4. Pour into a hot greased ovenproof frying pan and bake at 200°C until golden and springy, 25 min.
  5. Serve the chilli in bowls with wedges of cornbread.

Rainbow Connection sundaesVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, strawberries and rainbow sprinkles. Banjo optional.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make the fudge sauce up to 3 days ahead and rewarm
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup
  • 250g strawberries, sliced
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 3 tbsp rainbow sprinkles
  • 4 glace cherries
  1. Heat the cream and golden syrup, pour over the chocolate and stir until glossy, 5 min.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into 4 tall glasses with the strawberries and banana.
  3. Pour over the warm fudge sauce, shower with sprinkles and top each with a cherry, 5 min.

Idaho sparkling grape fizzNo alcohol

Sparkling grape juice poured over frozen grapes. A very good year, apparently.

Prep 4 min · Make ahead: Freeze the grapes and chill the juice the day before; pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml sparkling white grape juice
  • 250ml soda water
  • 1 cup green grapes, frozen
  • 1 lemon
  1. Drop a few frozen grapes into 4 chilled glasses.
  2. Fill two thirds with the grape juice, top with soda and add a twist of lemon.
