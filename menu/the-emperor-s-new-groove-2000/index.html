Snacks

Kronk's spinach puffsVegetarian

Puff pastry squares filled with spinach, feta and ricotta

Prep 30 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the puffs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; egg wash and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g spinach
  • 150g feta
  • 150g ricotta
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Wilt the spinach and squeeze dry.
  2. Mix with the feta, ricotta, garlic and nutmeg.
  3. Cut the pastry into squares, spoon on the filling, fold and egg wash.
  4. Bake at 200°C for 18 minutes. Pull the lever.

Pacha's village quinoa saladVegetarian

Quinoa with corn, black beans, tomato, red onion and coriander in lime dressing

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 200g quinoa
  • 200g corn kernels
  • 1 tin black beans, drained
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  1. Cook the quinoa and cool.
  2. Toss with the corn, black beans, tomato, red onion and coriander.
  3. Dress with lime juice, olive oil and cumin.

Andean potatoes with huancaína sauceOn the printed menu

Baby potatoes in creamy yellow chilli and feta, egg and olives, pull the lever Kronk

How it is made
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g feta
  • 1 tin evaporated milk
  • 1 jar aji amarillo paste
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar black olives
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Baby potatoes boiled and halved, covered in a creamy yellow chilli and feta huancaína sauce, topped with boiled egg, black olives and parsley. Boil 1kg baby potatoes for 15 minutes until tender, then halve. Boil 2 eggs for 10 minutes, peel and quarter. Blend 150g feta, 150ml evaporated milk, 2 tbsp aji amarillo paste, 4 crackers, 1 garlic clove and 2 tbsp oil until smooth and thick like custard. Arrange the potatoes on a platter, pour over the sauce and top with the eggs, sliced black olives and chopped parsley.

Mains

Mudka's Meat Hut cheeseburger and chips

Grilled cheddar burgers with grilled onion and pickles, twice fried chips

Prep 5 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Cut the chips up to 1 day ahead and keep them in cold water in the fridge, dried well before frying; shape the patties up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 4 burger buns
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chips
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Fry the potato chips in the oil once at a gentle heat until soft, then again hot until crisp.
  2. Shape the mince into patties and grill hot.
  3. Melt the cheddar on top and grill the onion.
  4. Serve on toasted buns with pickles. Special for two, or four.

Peruvian pollo a la brasa with green sauceOn the printed menu

Whole bird in cumin, garlic and lime, roasted dark, chips and coriander dip, poison-free

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 3 limes
  • 800g frozen chips
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 green chillies
  • 60g feta
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  1. Whole chicken marinated in cumin, paprika, garlic, lime and soy, roasted until the skin is dark and crisp, with chips and a spicy green coriander sauce. Blend 5 garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp dried oregano, 3 tbsp soy, the juice of 2 limes and 2 tbsp oil. Rub over and under the skin of a 1.8kg butterflied chicken and marinate 4 hours. Roast at 220°C for 45 to 50 minutes. Bake 800g frozen chips alongside. Blend a bunch of coriander, 2 green chillies, 60g feta, 3 tbsp mayonnaise, lime juice and a garlic clove for the sauce.

Yzma's diner birthday cakeVegetarian

Dense chocolate cake with chocolate frosting

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min, then 35 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Cake keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 280g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 70g cocoa
  • 300g icing sugar
  1. Melt the chocolate with most of the butter, whisk in the eggs and sugar and fold in the flour and some of the cocoa.
  2. Bake at 175°C for 35 minutes, then cool.
  3. Beat the icing sugar with the rest of the cocoa and butter into a frosting.
  4. Frost the cake and add candles.

Chicha moradaVegetarianNo alcohol

Peruvian purple corn drink simmered with pineapple peel and cinnamon, chilled

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Serve chilled, keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 250g purple corn
  • 1 pineapple, peel only for the pot
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 2l water
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 apple, diced
  1. Put the purple corn, pineapple peel, cinnamon and cloves in a pot with the water.
  2. Simmer for 45 minutes.
  3. Strain, sweeten with sugar and chill.
  4. Serve with lime juice and diced apple.

When to serve

Serve the spinach puffs when Kronk brings them out at dinner.
