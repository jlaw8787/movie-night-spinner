Snacks

Cuban Pete coconut shrimp and plantain chips

Coconut crumbed shrimp and thin fried plantain chips with sweet chilli sauce.

Prep 22 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 2 hours ahead, chilled; slice and fry the plantains and prawns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g green prawns, peeled
  • 80g plain flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 60g shredded coconut
  • 60g dried breadcrumbs
  • 2 green plantains
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Mix the coconut and breadcrumbs. Dip the prawns in flour, egg, then the crumb mix.
  2. Peel the plantains and slice thin.
  3. Fry the prawns in the oil at 180°C in batches.
  4. Fry the plantain slices until crisp.
  5. Serve both with sweet chilli.

Green cheese puffsVegetarian

Cheesy choux puffs, tinted green with basil and spinach.

Prep 12 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day airtight, crisp in the oven to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 50g spinach
  • 2 tbsp water, for the puree
  • 125ml water, for the dough
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g Gruyère, grated
  1. Blitz the basil and spinach with the water for the puree.
  2. Boil the water for the dough with the butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute. Cool.
  3. Beat in the eggs, green puree and Gruyère.
  4. Pipe onto trays and bake at 200°C.

Mains

Milo's steak

Rump steak with melting garlic butter and double-fried chips.

Prep 15 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Mash the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead, chilled; fry the chips the first time up to 2 hours ahead, then finish the chips and sear the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips. Mash the butter with the garlic.
  2. Fry the chips in the oil at 140°C until soft and pale.
  3. Fry again at 185°C until crisp.
  4. Season the steaks and sear hard, then rest.
  5. Top each with a slice of garlic butter. Save the trimmings for the dog.

Stanley's Edge City griddled cheeseburger

Smashed beef patties with green chilli mayo on a griddled bun.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 20g butter
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 tbsp mayo
  • 1 jar pickled green chillies
  • smoked paprika
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Double smashed patties with melted cheddar, grilled onions, pickles and a smoky green chilli mayo on a toasted bun, as loud as a zoot suit. Roll 800g beef mince into 8 balls. Slowly cook 2 sliced onions in butter for 15 minutes. Mix 4 tbsp mayo with 1 finely chopped pickled green chilli and a pinch of smoked paprika. Smash each ball flat on a smoking griddle, season and cook 2 minutes, flip and top with cheddar for 1 minute. Toast 4 buns and stack two patties each with onions, pickles and the green chilli mayo.

Sweet tooth bananas FosterVegetarian

Bananas flamed in butter and brown sugar over ice cream, for Daddy's sweet tooth.

Prep 2 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Flamed rum, stand back and keep hair tied
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 bananas
  • 60ml dark rum
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 40g pecans, toasted
  1. Melt the butter, sugar and cinnamon in a wide pan until bubbling.
  2. Halve the bananas lengthways and cook, spooning the sauce over.
  3. Pour in the rum and carefully light it with a long match, standing back.
  4. When the flames die down, spoon over bowls of ice cream and scatter with pecans.

Drinks

Smokin' Bongo cocktail

Melon liqueur and vodka with lime, topped with lemonade and an umbrella.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the melon liqueur, vodka and lime juice in a jug up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice and lemonade just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml melon liqueur
  • 120ml vodka
  • 2 limes
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 4 cocktail umbrellas
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Add melon liqueur, vodka and a squeeze of lime to each.
  3. Top with lemonade, stir and add an umbrella.
