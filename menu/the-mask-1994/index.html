Snacks

Cuban Pete coconut prawns and plantain chips

Coconut panko prawns and thin fried plantain chips with sweet chilli sauce

Prep 30 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Crumb the prawns up to 2 hours ahead, chilled; slice and fry the plantains and prawns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g green prawns, peeled
  • 80g plain flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 60g shredded coconut
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 green plantains
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Mix the coconut and panko. Dip the prawns in flour, egg, then the crumb mix.
  2. Peel the plantains and slice thin.
  3. Fry the prawns in the oil at 180C in batches.
  4. Fry the plantain slices until crisp.
  5. Serve both with sweet chilli.

Green cheese puffsVegetarian

Green choux puffs. The mask only works at night

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day airtight, crisp in the oven to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 50g spinach
  • 2 tbsp water, for the puree
  • 125ml water, for the dough
  • 50g butter
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g Gruyere, grated
  1. Blitz the basil and spinach with the water for the puree.
  2. Boil the water for the dough with the butter, beat in the flour and cook a minute. Cool.
  3. Beat in the eggs, green puree and Gruyere.
  4. Pipe onto trays and bake at 200C.

Mains

Milo's steak

Rump, garlic butter, chips. Milo fetches the cell keys

Prep 15 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Mash the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead, chilled; fry the chips the first time up to 2 hours ahead, then finish the chips and sear the steaks on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 4 rump steaks
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips. Mash the butter with the garlic.
  2. Fry the chips in the oil at 140C until soft and pale.
  3. Fry again at 185C until crisp.
  4. Season the steaks and sear hard, then rest.
  5. Top each with a slice of garlic butter. Save the trimmings for the dog.

Sweets

Sweet tooth bananas FosterVegetarian

Flamed bananas, ice cream. "Daddy's got a sweet tooth tonight!"

Prep 5 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Flamed rum, stand back and keep hair tied
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 bananas
  • 60ml dark rum
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 40g pecans, toasted
  1. Melt the butter, sugar and cinnamon in a wide pan until bubbling.
  2. Halve the bananas lengthways and cook, spooning the sauce over.
  3. Pour in the rum and carefully light it with a long match, standing back.
  4. When the flames die down, spoon over bowls of ice cream and scatter with pecans.

Smokin' Bongo cocktailVegetarian

Melon liqueur and vodka with lime, topped with lemonade and an umbrella

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the melon liqueur, vodka and lime juice in a jug up to 1 day ahead, chilled; add ice and lemonade just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml melon liqueur
  • 120ml vodka
  • 2 limes
  • 500ml lemonade
  • 4 cocktail umbrellas
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Fill four tall glasses with ice.
  2. Add melon liqueur, vodka and a squeeze of lime to each.
  3. Top with lemonade, stir and add an umbrella.
