Gruyère gougèresVegetarian

Light cheese choux puffs with gruyère and a touch of nutmeg.

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Pipe the mounds onto the trays up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g gruyère
  • nutmeg, to taste
  • 250ml water
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Bring the water and the butter to the boil, beat in the plain flour until it pulls away from the sides, then cool 5 minutes.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy, then the grated gruyère, pepper and a pinch of nutmeg.
  4. Pipe the small mounds onto lined trays, top with more gruyère and bake 22 to 25 minutes until puffed and golden.
  5. Serve warm.

New England clam chowder cups

Clams, smoky bacon and potato in creamy broth with thyme, oyster crackers and chives.

How it is made
  • 120g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml fish stock
  • 400ml milk
  • 400g potatoes
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg vongole clams
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 packet oyster crackers
  1. Clams, smoky bacon, potato and onion simmered in a thick creamy broth with thyme, served in small cups with oyster crackers and chives. Fry 120g diced bacon until crisp. Add 1 diced onion and 2 celery sticks and cook 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour, then 300ml fish stock, 400ml milk, 400g diced potato and thyme. Simmer 15 minutes. Add 1kg vongole clams, cover and cook 5 minutes until open, then remove the shells. Stir in 150ml cream and season with pepper. Ladle into small cups and top with chives and oyster crackers.

Harlan's birthday roast beef

Garlic thyme beef rib roast, roast potatoes, horseradish cream.

Prep 7 min, cook 118 min · Make ahead: Rub the beef with the garlic, thyme and salt up to 1 day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge; roast and rest on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg beef rib roast
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 tbsp horseradish cream
  1. Rub the beef rib roast with the crushed garlic cloves, thyme and salt.
  2. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then 180°C for 45 minutes for medium rare, and rest 20 minutes.
  3. Roast the potatoes in chunks alongside.
  4. Carve at the table with the horseradish cream.

Buttered lobster rolls

Lobster in lemon, chive and celery mayo, on griddled brioche buns for lunch at the house.

How it is made
  • 2 cooked lobsters
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 brioche hot dog rolls
  • 30g butter
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • paprika
  1. Chunks of cooked lobster in a lemon, chive and celery mayo, piled into buttered, griddled brioche hot dog rolls with crisp lettuce and a pinch of paprika. Cut the meat from 2 cooked lobsters, about 500g, into chunks. Mix with 80g mayo, 1 finely diced celery stick, 1 tbsp chopped chives, the zest and juice of half a lemon, salt and pepper. Chill 15 minutes. Butter the sides of 4 brioche hot dog rolls and toast in a pan until golden. Line with shredded cos, pile in the lobster and finish with a pinch of paprika and more chives.

Donut holesVegetarian

Yeast donut holes fried golden and rolled in cinnamon sugar.

Prep 86 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Mix the dough up to 1 day ahead and leave covered in the fridge; shape, prove and fry on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 130g caster sugar
  • 160ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  1. Mix the flour, the dried yeast, the sugar, the warm milk, the egg and the soft butter into a soft dough and prove 1 hour.
  2. Roll into small balls, prove 20 minutes, then fry in the vegetable oil at 175°C for 2 minutes until golden.
  3. Roll in the sugar mixed with the cinnamon while hot.

Harlan's 85th birthday cakeVegetarian

Chocolate layers frosted with dark chocolate buttercream.

Prep 17 min, cook 84 min · Make ahead: Bake and cool the layers up to 2 days ahead, wrapped; make the chocolate frosting, stack and add the candles on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 60g cocoa powder
  • 250ml milk
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 150g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the self-raising flour, the cocoa and the milk.
  2. Bake in two 20cm tins at 175°C for 30 minutes.
  3. Melt the dark chocolate with the butter, beat in the icing sugar, then stack and frost the layers and add candles.

Drinks

Maple bourbon coffee with whipped cream

Hot coffee with maple syrup and bourbon, topped with whipped cream.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 4 hours ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; brew the coffee and build each mug to order
Recipe for 4
  • ground coffee, to taste
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 80ml maple syrup
  • 120ml bourbon
  • nutmeg, to taste
  1. Brew the strong coffee.
  2. Whip the cream with the maple syrup to soft peaks.
  3. Into each of the big mugs pour the maple syrup and the bourbon, then the hot coffee and stir.
  4. Spoon the cream on top so it floats and grate over a little nutmeg.
  5. Leave out the bourbon for non-drinkers.
  6. Make sure everyone gets the right mug.
