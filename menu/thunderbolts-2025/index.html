Snacks

Anybody hungry? trail mixVegetarian

Almonds, cashews, apricot and chocolate chips. No cactus berries.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g almonds
  • 150g cashews
  • 150g dried apricots
  • 100g dark chocolate chips
  1. Roast the almonds and cashews at 180°C and cool.
  2. Chop the apricots, then mix with the nuts and chocolate chips.
  3. Portion into small paper bags.

Red Guardian's picklesVegetarian

Dill and garlic cucumber spears. Go Thunderbolts.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 weeks, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 250ml white vinegar
  • 250ml water
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 loaf rye bread
  • 100g salted butter
  1. Cut the cucumbers into spears and slice the garlic.
  2. Pack the cucumbers, dill and garlic into a jar.
  3. Heat the vinegar, water, sugar and salt until dissolved, then pour over.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Serve with rye bread and butter.

Vault escape smash burger slidersOn the printed menu

Smashed beef sliders with cheddar, ready before the incinerator countdown

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 3 slices cheddar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 onion
  1. Beef patties smashed thin on a hot grill, cheddar melted on, tucked into slider buns with pickles and tomato sauce. Roll 500g beef mince into 12 balls. Smash each flat onto a smoking hot greased flat plate, season and cook 2 minutes until a dark crust forms. Flip, top with a quarter slice of cheddar and cook 1 minute more. Toast 12 slider buns cut side down. Mix 3 tbsp mayo with 1 tbsp tomato sauce and 1 tbsp mustard. Build with sauce, a patty, sliced dill pickle and a few thin onion rings.

Mains

Beef and mushroom stroganoff

Rump, mushrooms, sour cream. Alexei: "I'll make you something."

Prep 8 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Sauce keeps up to 2 days chilled, reheat gently without boiling; cook the noodles fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef rump, thinly sliced
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Slice the onion and mushrooms.
  2. Sear the beef in the oil in batches and set aside.
  3. Cook the onion and mushrooms until golden and stir in the paprika.
  4. Stir in the sour cream and return the beef, warming through without boiling.
  5. Cook the noodles, toss in butter and serve the stroganoff over the top.

New York chicken shawarma wrapsOn the printed menu

Chicken thighs charred and rolled in flatbread with pickles and garlic sauce, for the rebrand

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 4 large flatbreads
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Chicken thighs marinated in lemon, garlic and shawarma spices, charred and sliced into flatbread with lettuce, tomato, pickles and garlic sauce. Marinate 800g chicken thigh for 1 hour with the juice of 1 lemon, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cinnamon and 3 tbsp olive oil. Grill on high heat 5 minutes a side until charred, rest and slice. Mix 150g Greek yoghurt with 1 crushed garlic clove, lemon juice and salt for the sauce. Warm 4 large flatbreads and fill with lettuce, tomato, pickled turnip or dill pickles, chicken and sauce. Roll tightly in foil.

Sweets

Bob's Void black sesame cookiesVegetarian

Buttery black sesame cookies. Hug included.

Prep 12 min, cook 12 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 50g black sesame seeds
  • 150g butter
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 200g plain flour
  • 100g white chocolate
  1. Grind the sesame seeds fine.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour and sesame and roll into a log.
  3. Chill the log.
  4. Slice into rounds and bake at 170°C.
  5. Cool, melt the chocolate and dip half of each cookie.

Drinks

Red Guardian cherry tea punchVegetarian

Black tea sweetened with cherry jam, chilled

Prep 12 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Steep, sweeten and chill the tea up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; add the lemon juice and vodka, then top with soda and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 black tea bags
  • 600ml boiling water
  • 4 tbsp cherry jam
  • 1 lemon
  • 160ml vodka
  • 330ml soda water
  • 150g frozen cherries
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags for 5 minutes, remove them and stir in the jam.
  2. Chill well, then strain out any fruit.
  3. Mix with lemon juice and vodka.
  4. Fill four glasses with ice, pour in the tea, top with soda water and garnish with a lemon slice and cherries.
