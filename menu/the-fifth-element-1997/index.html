Snacks

Noodle boat dumplings

Pan fried pork dumplings with a black vinegar and chilli oil dip

Prep 3 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the dipping sauce up to 3 days ahead in the fridge; cook the dumplings from frozen just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 24 frozen pork dumplings
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 120ml water
  • 3 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  1. Mix the black vinegar, chilli oil and spring onion for dipping.
  2. Heat the oil in a large non-stick pan and add the dumplings flat side down until golden.
  3. Add the water, cover and steam until the water is gone.
  4. Fry one more minute to re-crisp the bottoms and serve with the dip.

Meat popsicle prawn skewers

Garlic and lime prawn skewers grilled, served on ice with sweet chilli sauce

Prep 25 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Prawns can marinate up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 16 king prawns
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100ml sweet chilli sauce
  • 8 skewers
  1. Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on.
  2. Toss with the garlic, chilli, zest and juice of one lime and the oil.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill or griddle on high until pink and charred.
  5. Serve on ice with the sweet chilli sauce and wedges of the other lime.

Flying noodle boat noodles

Wok fried pork, cabbage and carrot with udon in oyster and soy sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Marinate the sliced pork in some of the soy sauce up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and prep the cabbage and carrot; stir-fry to order
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork shoulder
  • 5 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
  • 800g fresh udon noodles
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Slice the pork thin and toss with some of the soy sauce.
  2. Prep the cabbage and carrot.
  3. Stir-fry the pork in a smoking wok with the oil.
  4. Add the cabbage and carrot and stir-fry briefly.
  5. Add the udon, oyster sauce, remaining soy sauce and wine and toss until glossy.
  6. Finish with spring onion.

Sweets

Diva Plavalaguna blue jelly

Coconut milk jelly under blue flower jelly that turns purple with lemon

Prep 20 min, cook 305 min · Make ahead: Make the day before, lemon at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml coconut milk
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 4 gelatine leaves
  • 2 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 300ml boiling water
  • 1 lemon
  1. Soften two gelatine leaves in cold water. Warm the coconut milk with some of the sugar and stir in the squeezed gelatine.
  2. Pour into four glasses and chill until set.
  3. Steep the butterfly pea flowers in the boiling water, strain and stir in the remaining sugar and two softened gelatine leaves.
  4. Cool, then pour gently over the set coconut layer and chill until set.
  5. At the table, squeeze lemon over the top and watch it turn purple.

Leeloo's dirty martiniVegetarian

Gin, dry vermouth and olive brine stirred and served with green olives

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Glasses frozen first
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml gin
  • 40ml dry vermouth
  • 30ml green olive brine
  • 12 green olives
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Put four martini glasses in the freezer.
  2. Stir the gin, vermouth and brine over ice in a jug until very cold.
  3. Strain into the frozen glasses and garnish each with three olives on a pick.
