Snacks

Bartertown trade platterVegetarian

Dates stuffed with goat's cheese and almond, dried figs, cheddar and crackers.

Prep 6 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill the dates with goat's cheese and almond up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge. Halve the figs and lay the board out on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 medjool dates
  • 100g goat's cheese
  • 12 almonds, toasted
  • 8 dried figs
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Split the dates and fill each with goat's cheese and a toasted almond.
  2. Halve the dried figs.
  3. Lay out on a rough board with shards of cheddar and crackers.

Flatbread with wild herbsVegetarian

Blistered yoghurt flatbread with oil and wild herbs to dip.

Prep 15 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Make the dough up to 4 hours ahead and chill; cook to order
Recipe for 4
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp dried herbs
  1. Mix the flour, yoghurt and a pinch of salt into a soft dough.
  2. Divide into 6 and roll each out thin.
  3. Cook in a dry hot pan for 2 minutes a side until blistered.
  4. Brush with oil and serve with dried herbs and more oil to dip.

Underworld pork crackling

Salted pork rind roasted until blistered and shattering, with a sharp apple and mustard dip.

How it is made
  • 800g pork rind
  • 1 box salt
  • 2 apples
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  1. Strips of pork rind salted and dried overnight, roasted until blistered and shatteringly crisp, with a sharp apple and mustard dipping sauce. Score 800g pork rind, rub with 1 tbsp salt and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Pat dry, cut into strips and roast on a rack at 240°C for 30 minutes until blistered, then rest. Simmer 2 peeled, chopped apples with 2 tbsp sugar and 50ml water for 10 minutes and mash, then stir in 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard and a splash of cider vinegar. Break the crackling into shards and serve with the sauce.

Mains

Aunty Entity's lamb

Garlic and rosemary lamb leg with roast potatoes and homemade mint sauce.

Prep 15 min, cook 1 hr 25 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Stud and rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; make the mint sauce up to 2 days ahead. Roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves, chopped
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp boiling water
  1. Stud the lamb with garlic and rosemary, rub with oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 180°C until 58°C inside, with the potatoes alongside, 1 hr 20 min.
  3. Rest, 20 min.
  4. Mix the mint with sugar, vinegar and boiling water for mint sauce.
  5. Carve and serve.

Underworld roast pork rolls

Slow roast pork carved into soft buns with slaw, from the pigs that power Bartertown.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 box salt
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle cider vinegar
  • 8 soft rolls
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  1. Pork shoulder rubbed with paprika and brown sugar, roasted low until it pulls apart, piled into soft rolls with tangy slaw and barbecue sauce. Rub a 2kg pork shoulder with 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 1 tbsp salt and 1 tsp garlic powder. Roast covered at 150°C for 5 hours until it pulls apart, then shred with the pan juices. Toss half a shredded cabbage and 1 grated carrot with 100g mayonnaise and 1 tbsp cider vinegar. Fill 8 soft rolls with pork, slaw and 150ml barbecue sauce.

Sweets

Thunderdome rock cakesVegetarian

Craggy currant rock cakes with a sugar crust, two cakes enter, one leaves.

Prep 7 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 300g self-raising flour
  • 110g sugar
  • 100g currants
  • 1 tsp mixed spice
  • 1 egg
  • 4 tbsp milk
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in 100g of the sugar, the currants and spice, 8 min.
  2. Mix in the egg and milk to a stiff dough and drop rough heaps on a lined tray.
  3. Sprinkle with the rest of the sugar and bake until golden, 15 min.

Drinks

Fiery fresh ginger beerNo alcohol

Fresh ginger syrup, lemon and soda water.

Prep 11 min, cook 10 min, 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Simmer, cool and strain the ginger syrup up to 5 days ahead and keep in the fridge; add the lemon juice then. Top with soda water and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 100g fresh ginger, grated
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 300ml water
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 800ml soda water, cold
  • 1 lime
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Grate the ginger.
  2. Simmer with the sugar and water, 10 min.
  3. Cool, then strain, pressing hard on the ginger, 20 min.
  4. Stir in the lemon juice.
  5. Divide the syrup between tall glasses of ice and top with soda water.
  6. Stir and add a lime wedge and a thin slice of ginger.
