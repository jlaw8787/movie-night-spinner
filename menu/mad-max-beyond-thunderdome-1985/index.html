Snacks

Bartertown trade platterVegetarian

Dates stuffed with goat's cheese and almond, dried figs, cheddar and crackers

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Fill the dates with goat's cheese and almond up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge. Halve the figs and lay the board out on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 12 medjool dates
  • 100g goat's cheese
  • 12 almonds, toasted
  • 8 dried figs
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 1 packet crackers
  1. Split the dates and fill each with goat's cheese and a toasted almond.
  2. Halve the dried figs.
  3. Lay out on a rough board with shards of cheddar and crackers.

Crack in the Earth damperVegetarian

Oven-baked damper loaf with butter and golden syrup

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best baked on the day; rub the butter into the flour up to 3 hours ahead and add the milk when ready to bake. Eat warm, or keep wrapped for the next day and toast
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  1. Rub half the butter into the flour and salt.
  2. Mix in the milk with a knife until just combined.
  3. Shape into a round and score.
  4. Bake at 200C until hollow when tapped.
  5. Serve with the remaining butter and golden syrup.

Mains

Aunty Entity's lamb

Garlic and rosemary lamb leg with roast potatoes and homemade mint sauce

Prep 20 min, cook 110 min · Make ahead: Stud and rub the lamb up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; make the mint sauce up to 2 days ahead. Roast on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves, chopped
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp boiling water
  1. Stud the lamb with garlic and rosemary, rub with oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 180C until 58C inside, with the potatoes alongside.
  3. Rest.
  4. Mix the mint with sugar, vinegar and boiling water for mint sauce.
  5. Carve and serve.

Sweets

Thunderdome lamingtonsVegetarian

Jam filled sponge squares in chocolate icing and coconut, with cream

Prep 60 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, soft
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 30g cocoa
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 200g desiccated coconut
  • 1/2 cup strawberry jam
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Beat the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk.
  2. Bake the sponge at 180C.
  3. Cool and cut into squares.
  4. Split and fill with jam.
  5. Mix the icing sugar, cocoa and a little milk, dip the squares and roll in coconut.
  6. Pipe cream in the middle.

Bartertown fiery fresh ginger beerVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh ginger syrup, lemon and soda water, no alcohol

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Simmer, cool and strain the ginger syrup up to 5 days ahead and keep in the fridge; add the lemon juice then. Top with soda water and ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 100g fresh ginger, grated
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 300ml water
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 800ml soda water, cold
  • 1 lime
  • 2 cup ice
  1. Grate the ginger.
  2. Simmer with the sugar and water.
  3. Cool, then strain, pressing hard on the ginger.
  4. Stir in the lemon juice.
  5. Divide the syrup between tall glasses of ice and top with soda water.
  6. Stir and add a lime wedge and a thin slice of ginger.
