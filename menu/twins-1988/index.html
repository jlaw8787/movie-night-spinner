Rambo-sized green chile quesoVegetarian

Green chile cheese dip with warm corn chips, a Rambo-sized bowl

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the queso up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently with a splash of milk
Recipe for 4
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 brown onion, finely diced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 200g jar roasted green chiles, chopped
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 250ml evaporated milk
  • 200g tasty cheese, grated
  • 100g Monterey Jack or mozzarella, grated
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 230g bag corn chips
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the butter.
  2. Toss the cheeses with the cornflour.
  3. Add the evaporated milk and cumin to the pan, bring to a simmer, then stir in the cheese a handful at a time until smooth.
  4. Stir through the green chiles, season and serve hot with the chips, warmed in the oven.

Split-in-two loaded potato skinsVegetarian

Potato skins loaded with cheddar and bacon. One embryo split in two.

Prep 5 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Bake and scoop the potatoes up to a day ahead; fill and crisp to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 medium potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g sour cream
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeños, chopped
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C until soft, then cool a little.
  2. Halve them and scoop out most of the flesh, leaving a thin wall. Keep the flesh for another day.
  3. Brush inside and out with oil, season with salt and paprika and bake until crisp.
  4. Fill with cheddar and bake until bubbling, then top with sour cream, spring onion and jalapeños.

Green chile cheeseburgers

Cheeseburgers with roasted green chile. The loan sharks want their money.

Prep 5 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to a day ahead and chill between baking paper; griddle to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince, 20 per cent fat
  • 1 brown onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 150g jar roasted green chiles, chopped
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 4 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Cook the onion slowly in the butter until soft and golden, then push to the side of the griddle.
  2. Shape the mince into 4 thin patties and season hard.
  3. Griddle on high for 3 minutes, flip, pile on the green chile and cheese and cook until melted.
  4. Toast the buns, spread with mayo and build with lettuce, tomato, patty and onions.

Thanksgiving hot turkey plate

Hot turkey with mash and gravy. Vincent asks about Thanksgiving dinner.

Prep 5 min, cook 43 min · Make ahead: Make the gravy and the mash up to a day ahead and reheat; warm the turkey in the gravy to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g roast turkey breast, sliced
  • 1kg floury potatoes, peeled
  • 80g butter
  • 125ml milk
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 4 thick slices white bread
  • 150g cranberry sauce
  • 200g frozen peas
  1. Boil the potatoes until soft, then mash with 50g butter and the hot milk.
  2. Cook the rest of the butter with the flour, whisk in the stock and thyme and simmer to a gravy.
  3. Warm the turkey slices gently in the gravy.
  4. Cook the peas and toast the bread.
  5. Lay turkey on the toast, smother with gravy and serve with mash, peas and cranberry.

Six-father sopaipillasVegetarian

Puffy fried sopaipillas with honey. We had six fathers, says Julius.

Prep 11 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the dough up to a day ahead and chill; roll and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp butter, plus 60g softened
  • 180ml warm water
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Rub 1 tbsp butter into the flour, baking powder and salt, then mix in the water to a soft dough and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Roll out 5mm thick and cut into 12 squares.
  3. Fry a few at a time at 190°C, spooning oil over until they puff, then flip and drain.
  4. Whip the softened butter with the honey, toss the sopaipillas in cinnamon sugar and serve with the honey butter.

Where they made the milkshakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick vanilla malt shake with whipped cream, served in the lab

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer; blend to order
Recipe for 4
  • 8 big scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  1. Blend the ice cream, milk, malt powder and vanilla until thick and smooth.
  2. Pour into 4 frosted glasses.
  3. Top with whipped cream and a cherry and serve with long spoons and straws.
