Pimento cheese with crackers

Cheddar and roasted bell pepper spread with crackers.

Prep 17 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 jar of roasted red capsicum
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 200g crackers
  • 1 bunch of celery
  • 1 jar of cornichons
  1. Finely grate the cheddar.
  2. Mix with the mayonnaise, chopped capsicum, Worcestershire, cayenne and black pepper until combined but still chunky.
  3. Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  4. Spoon into a bowl, dust with paprika and serve with crackers, celery sticks and cornichons.

Backyard brunch fried green tomatoesVegetarian

Cornmeal crumbed green tomatoes fried crisp, with a buttermilk herb dressing.

Prep 23 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the dressing up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green tomatoes, thickly sliced
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine polenta
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 125ml vegetable oil
  • 100g whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lemon
  1. Salt the tomato slices and leave 10 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Dip each slice in 150ml of the buttermilk, then in the polenta, flour and paprika mixed.
  3. Fry in the oil over medium heat for 3 minutes a side until golden. Drain.
  4. Whisk the rest of the buttermilk with the mayonnaise, chopped chives and a squeeze of lemon, 3 min.
  5. Serve the tomatoes hot with the dressing.

Park picnic ham and spinach frittata

Baked frittata with ham, potato, spinach and feta.

Prep 16 min, cook 33 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat gently or serve at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 300g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g leg ham
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 8 eggs
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 100g feta
  • 100g rocket
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar of tomato relish
  1. Dice the potato, onion and ham and crumble the feta.
  2. Boil the potato until tender, then drain.
  3. Fry the onion in oil in an ovenproof pan, add the potato, ham and spinach and stir until wilted.
  4. Whisk the eggs with cream and salt, pour over and scatter with feta.
  5. Cook on low heat until the edges set.
  6. Bake at 180°C until set and golden.
  7. Serve with rocket dressed in lemon and relish.

Atlanta fried chicken and waffles

Crisp fried chicken on waffles with syrup, Atlanta brunch style.

Prep 9 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in the buttermilk overnight and mix the waffle batter up to 2 hours ahead; fry and cook the waffles just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken tenderloins
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 125ml maple syrup
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 1 lemon
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with the hot sauce and a pinch of salt for at least 1 hour.
  2. Whisk the self-raising flour, eggs, milk and melted butter into a smooth batter.
  3. Cook the waffles in a hot greased waffle iron until crisp and keep warm in a low oven.
  4. Dredge the chicken in the plain flour mixed with the paprika, salt and pepper, and fry at 170°C for 5 to 6 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Warm the maple syrup with a dash of hot sauce. Stack the chicken on the waffles, pour over the maple and serve with the spinach dressed in lemon juice and a little oil, 4 min.

Breakfast in bed almond croissantsVegetarian

Almond croissants warm from the oven, carried in on a tray for breakfast in bed.

Prep 16 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Syrup and almond cream up to 1 day ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 80g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 100g almond meal
  • 4 croissants
  • 30g flaked almonds
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  • 1 jar of strawberry jam
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and vanilla into a light syrup, then cool, 5 min.
  2. Beat the butter, remaining sugar, egg and almond meal into a smooth paste.
  3. Split the croissants, brush the insides with syrup and spread with half the almond cream.
  4. Close the croissants, spread the rest of the almond cream on top and scatter with flaked almonds, 3 min.
  5. Bake at 180°C until golden.
  6. Dust with icing sugar and serve with jam.

On-air proposal blood orange mimosa

Blood orange juice, orange liqueur and sparkling wine.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Juice up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 blood oranges
  • 40ml orange liqueur
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 500ml soda water
  1. Juice the oranges and strain.
  2. Pour juice and orange liqueur into each flute.
  3. Slowly top with chilled sparkling wine and garnish with an orange wheel.
  4. For the kids, mix the leftover juice with soda water.
