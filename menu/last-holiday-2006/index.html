Georgia's Joint crab cakes, lemon aioli

Panko crumbed crab cakes with spring onion and lemon aioli

Prep 28 min, cook 36 min · Make ahead: Shape up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 400g crab meat
  • 7 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 50g rocket
  • 1 bunch of parsley
  1. Mix the crab with mayonnaise, egg, spring onions, lemon zest, Dijon and panko.
  2. Shape into small cakes.
  3. Chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll in more panko.
  5. Fry in oil over medium heat until golden each side.
  6. Mix the mayonnaise with crushed garlic and lemon juice.
  7. Serve with the aioli and a few dressed rocket and parsley leaves.

Karlovy Vary mushroom and gruyère tartsVegetarian

Mushroom and gruyère puff squares fit for a New Year's Eve feast

Prep 20 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 300g mixed mushrooms
  • 30g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch of thyme
  • 100g gruyère
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle of truffle oil
  1. Cut the pastry into squares, score a border on each and prick the middle.
  2. Fry the mushrooms in butter with garlic and thyme until golden.
  3. Spoon onto the pastry centres and top with grated gruyère.
  4. Brush the borders with egg.
  5. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden.
  6. Drizzle with a few drops of truffle oil.

Chef Didier's duck with orange sauce

Crisp duck, orange honey sauce, potatoes. Didier never repeats a menu.

Prep 10 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Serve straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 4 duck breasts
  • 800g potatoes
  • 3 oranges
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 30ml orange liqueur
  • 200ml chicken stock
  • 20g butter
  • 300g green beans
  1. Score the duck skin and season.
  2. Place skin-side down in a cold pan and cook over medium heat to render the fat.
  3. Flip and roast at 200C.
  4. Rest the duck.
  5. Fry the par-boiled potatoes in some of the duck fat until crisp.
  6. Pour off the rest of the fat, add orange juice, honey, orange liqueur and stock and reduce until glossy.
  7. Whisk in the butter.
  8. Steam the green beans and serve alongside.

Grandhotel Pupp beef goulash

Paprika beef braised soft with buttered noodles, Czech comfort at the grand hotel

Prep 20 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Cook the goulash up to 2 days ahead, it gets better; boil the noodles on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, in large cubes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 300g wide egg noodles
  • 30g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches. Lift out.
  2. Cook the onions slowly in the same pot until soft and golden, then add the garlic, paprika, caraway and tomato paste.
  3. Return the beef, add the stock, cover and simmer gently until the beef falls apart.
  4. Boil the noodles, toss with the butter and serve under the goulash with sour cream.

Dessert trolley chocolate mousseVegetarian

Rich dark chocolate mousse with whipped cream

Prep 31 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 30g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 40g caster sugar
  • 125g raspberries
  • 4 thin wafer biscuits
  1. Melt the chocolate with butter and cool slightly.
  2. Whisk in the egg yolks one at a time.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks and fold in.
  4. Whisk the egg whites with sugar to soft peaks and fold through gently in two batches.
  5. Spoon into glasses.
  6. Chill for 3 hours.
  7. Top with whipped cream, raspberries and a thin wafer biscuit.

Grandhotel champagne cocktailVegetarian

Sugar cube, bitters, brandy and champagne for a roulette win

Prep 9 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 1 bottle of aromatic bitters
  • 60ml brandy
  • 750ml champagne
  • 1 orange
  • 750ml sparkling apple juice
  1. Place a sugar cube in each flute and soak with bitters.
  2. Add brandy to each glass.
  3. Slowly top with chilled champagne or sparkling wine.
  4. Twist a strip of orange peel over each glass and drop it in.
