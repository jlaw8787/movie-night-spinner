Miami croquetas

Ham croquetas. Julie Mott will only talk to Mike

Prep 27 min, cook 20 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill overnight, up to 1 day before frying
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 40g plain flour, for the bechamel
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 120g leg ham, finely chopped
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  • 20g plain flour, for dusting
  • 1 egg
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1l neutral oil, for frying
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for the bechamel and cook briefly. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook until a very thick bechamel forms.
  2. Stir in the ham and nutmeg.
  3. Spread in a shallow dish, cool promptly and refrigerate overnight.
  4. Shape into logs.
  5. Dust with the flour for dusting, dip in beaten egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  6. Fry in 180°C oil until golden and hot throughout. Drain.

Fried sweet plantainsVegetarian

Sweet plantain, which nobody argues over like a french fry

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; cut plantain browns quickly, so peel and slice at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 2 very ripe plantains
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Peel the plantains and cut diagonally into slices.
  2. Fry in batches in the hot oil until caramelised and soft.
  3. Drain and finish with sea salt.

Chopped prawns, capsicum and chilli in light batter, fried crisp, citrus dip, whatcha gonna do

How it is made
  • 400g green prawns
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 125ml milk
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 onion
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 red chilli
  • 120g mayo
  • 2 limes
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Chopped prawns in a light batter with capsicum, onion, celery and chilli, fried in spoonfuls until crisp, with a zesty lime and chilli mayo. Finely chop 400g peeled green prawns. Whisk 150g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 egg and 125ml milk into a thick batter. Fold in the prawns, half a finely diced red capsicum, half a diced onion, 1 diced celery stick, 1 chopped chilli and salt. Drop tablespoons into oil at 180°C and fry 3 minutes until golden. Mix 120g mayo with the zest and juice of 1 lime and a pinch of cayenne.

Mike and Marcus swap Cuban sandwich

Soft long roll with mustard, mojo pork, ham, Swiss and pickles, buttered and pressed flat

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours, then 8.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate and roast the pork ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 700g boneless pork shoulder
  • 1 orange
  • 1 lime
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 100ml water
  • 4 long soft rolls
  • 40g yellow mustard
  • 120g leg ham
  • 120g Swiss cheese
  • 80g sliced dill pickles
  • 30g butter
  1. Rub the pork with the orange and lime juice, crushed garlic, oregano, cumin and salt.
  2. Marinate overnight in the fridge.
  3. Put in a covered roasting dish with the water and roast at 160°C until tender.
  4. Rest the pork, then shred.
  5. Split the rolls, spread with mustard and fill with pork, ham, Swiss cheese and pickles.
  6. Butter the outsides and press in a hot sandwich press until crisp and the filling is piping hot.

Not in Cleveland jerk chicken with rice and peasOn the printed menu

Scotch bonnet and allspice thighs grilled slow until charred, coconut rice and kidney beans

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 scotch bonnet chilli
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ground allspice
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 limes
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 400g tin kidney beans
  1. Chicken thighs marinated overnight in scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme, garlic and lime, grilled slowly until charred, with coconut rice and kidney beans. Blend 4 spring onions, 1 scotch bonnet, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp allspice, 1 tbsp thyme leaves, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar and the juice of 2 limes. Coat 1kg chicken thighs and marinate overnight. Grill over medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through. Simmer 300g rice with a 400ml tin coconut milk, 200ml water, a drained 400g tin kidney beans and a thyme sprig, covered, for 18 minutes.

Key lime pieVegetarian

Biscuit crust with a tangy condensed milk and citrus custard, baked briefly, chilled, whipped cream

Prep 23 min, cook 25 min, then 4.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and chill the pie up to 1 day ahead; whip the cream and spread it over just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g digestive biscuits
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 400g sweetened condensed milk (1 tin)
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 120ml lime juice
  • 2 limes, zested
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 20cm pie dish.
  2. Bake the crust at 180°C.
  3. Whisk the condensed milk with the yolks, lime juice and zest and pour into the crust.
  4. Bake at 160°C until just set.
  5. Cool, then refrigerate.
  6. Softly whip the cream and spread over the pie.

Mike Lowrey's ocean view mojitoVegetarian

Mint, lime, rum. Still no cup holder in the Porsche.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Squeeze the lime juice up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; muddle, add the rum and top with soda water to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 large bunch mint
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 60ml lime juice
  • 180ml white rum
  • 500g crushed ice
  • 400ml soda water
  1. Gently muddle the mint leaves with the syrup and lime juice in a jug, without shredding the mint.
  2. Add the rum and divide between glasses filled with crushed ice.
  3. Top with soda water, stir gently and garnish with a mint sprig.
