Church shootout canapés

Smoked salmon and crème fraîche on rye, doves optional

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 slices rye bread
  • 30g butter, softened
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 100ml crème fraîche
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • black pepper, to taste
  1. Butter the rye and cut into small squares.
  2. Top each with a fold of smoked salmon, a small spoon of crème fraîche, a dill sprig and pepper.

Honey lime peach wedges. Castor eats them for hours.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Best cut just before serving so the peaches stay firm; mix the honey and lime juice up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 ripe peaches
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 pinch flaky salt
  1. Cut the peaches into wedges and arrange on a plate.
  2. Warm the honey with lime juice and drizzle over.
  3. Scatter with torn mint and a pinch of flaky salt.

Magnetic boot cheddar, apple and chutney stacksOn the printed menu

Water crackers with sharp aged cheese, thin crisp apple, spiced tomato relish and cracked pepper

How it is made
  • 200g aged cheddar
  • 2 green apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  • 1 bunch thyme
  1. Crisp water crackers stacked with aged cheddar, thin green apple slices and spiced tomato chutney, finished with cracked pepper, served on a steel tray. Slice 200g aged cheddar into thin squares. Core 2 green apples, slice thin and toss with lemon juice. Lay 24 water crackers on a steel tray. Top each with a slice of cheddar, a slice of apple and half a teaspoon of tomato chutney. Crack black pepper over and scatter with thyme leaves.

Castor's lavish dinner

Lobster and champagne, he takes over the life

Prep 11 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Split the lobster tails and make the garlic parsley butter up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; grill just before serving and dress the salad at the last minute
Recipe for 4
  • 4 lobster tails
  • 80g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 150g salad leaves
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Split the lobster tails lengthways.
  2. Melt the butter with the garlic and chopped parsley and brush over the lobster flesh.
  3. Grill the lobster flesh side up for 6 to 8 minutes until just white.
  4. Whisk the juice of the lemons with the olive oil into a vinaigrette, toss the salad leaves and serve alongside.

Archer family lemon thyme roast chickenOn the printed menu

Castor, as Sean, at a candlelit dinner with Eve

How it is made
  • 1 whole chicken (1.8kg)
  • 50g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • olive oil
  1. Dry-brined chicken with lemon and thyme butter under the skin, roasted golden, with pan-roasted potatoes and carrots and a gravy from the juices. Salt a 1.8kg chicken all over and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Mash 50g soft butter with lemon zest and thyme and push it under the breast skin. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then 180°C for 50 minutes with 800g halved potatoes and 4 carrots tossed in oil around it. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan juices, add 300ml chicken stock and simmer until thick.

Peach cobblerVegetarian

Baked cinnamon peaches under a buttermilk biscuit topping

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Toss the peaches in the dish and rub the butter into the flour up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; add the buttermilk, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 peaches
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 80g butter, cold
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml buttermilk
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Toss the sliced peaches with the sugar, lemon juice and cinnamon in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and stir in the buttermilk.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over the fruit and bake.

Peach BelliniVegetarian

Fresh peach purée topped with chilled prosecco

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the peach puree up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; top with chilled prosecco just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe peaches
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 350ml prosecco, chilled
  1. Blend the peaches with the sugar and lemon juice until smooth and strain.
  2. Put purée in each flute.
  3. Top slowly with prosecco and stir once.
