Snacks

Canteen grilled cheese with tomato soupVegetarian

Cheddar griddled crisp on white bread, cut in halves, a cup of tomato soup to dunk.

Prep 7 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Best made just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 200g cheddar, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tins of tomato soup
  1. Butter the bread on the outside and fill with the cheddar.
  2. Griddle in a hot pan until golden on both sides and melted inside, 8 min.
  3. Heat the soup, 5 min.
  4. Cut the sandwiches in half and serve with cups of soup.

Castor's peach slicesVegetarian

Ripe peach wedges drizzled with warm honey and lime.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Best cut just before serving so the peaches stay firm; mix the honey and lime juice up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 ripe peaches
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 pinch flaky salt
  1. Cut the peaches into wedges and arrange on a plate.
  2. Warm the honey with lime juice and drizzle over, 3 min.
  3. Scatter with torn mint and a pinch of flaky salt.

Magnetic boot cheddar, apple and chutney stacks

Water crackers with sharp aged cheese, thin crisp apple, spiced tomato relish and cracked pepper.

How it is made
  • 200g aged cheddar
  • 2 green apples
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 1 jar tomato chutney
  • 1 bunch thyme
  1. Crisp water crackers stacked with aged cheddar, thin green apple slices and spiced tomato chutney, finished with cracked pepper, served on a steel tray. Slice 200g aged cheddar into thin squares. Core 2 green apples, slice thin and toss with lemon juice. Lay 24 water crackers on a steel tray. Top each with a slice of cheddar, a slice of apple and half a teaspoon of tomato chutney. Crack black pepper over and scatter with thyme leaves.

Mains

Erewhon canteen sloppy joes

Beef simmered in a sweet tomato sauce, piled on soft buns, eaten in magnetic boots.

Prep 10 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 250ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 6 soft burger buns
  1. Brown the mince with the onion and capsicum, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the tomato sauce, sugar, Worcestershire and mustard and simmer until thick. Season, 15 min.
  3. Toast the buns lightly and pile the beef in, 3 min.

Canteen lemon thyme roast chicken

Lemon and thyme roast chicken with crisp potatoes, carved onto the tray.

How it is made
  • 1 whole chicken (1.8kg)
  • 50g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 800g potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • olive oil
  1. Dry-brined chicken with lemon and thyme butter under the skin, roasted golden, with pan-roasted potatoes and carrots and a gravy from the juices. Salt a 1.8kg chicken all over and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Mash 50g soft butter with lemon zest and thyme and push it under the breast skin. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes, then 180°C for 50 minutes with 800g halved potatoes and 4 carrots tossed in oil around it. Rest the chicken 10 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan juices, add 300ml chicken stock and simmer until thick.

Peach cobblerVegetarian

Baked cinnamon peaches under a buttermilk biscuit topping.

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Toss the peaches in the dish and rub the butter into the flour up to 4 hours ahead, covered in the fridge; add the buttermilk, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 6 peaches
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 80g butter, cold
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml buttermilk
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Toss the sliced peaches with the sugar, lemon juice and cinnamon in a baking dish, 12 min.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and stir in the buttermilk.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over the fruit and bake, 35 min.

Drinks

Peach sparklerNo alcohol

Strained peach purée topped with cold bubbles. Castor could eat a peach for hours.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the peach puree up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; top with the chilled sparkling apple at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe peaches
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 350ml sparkling apple juice, chilled
  1. Blend the peaches with the sugar and lemon juice until smooth and strain.
  2. Put purée in each flute.
  3. Top slowly with the sparkling apple and stir once.
