Bernardo's tostones with garlic mojoVegetarian

Twice-fried green plantain smashed flat, salted hot, with a sharp garlic, lime and olive oil mojo.

Prep 21 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: First fry and smash up to 4 hours ahead, make the mojo up to 1 day ahead; do the second fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green plantains
  • 750ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 6 cloves of garlic
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 small bunch coriander, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Peel the plantains by slitting the skin lengthways and prising it off, then cut into 3cm pieces.
  2. Heat 3cm of the vegetable oil in a heavy pot to 160°C, 8 min.
  3. Fry the plantain in batches until pale gold but still soft inside, then drain, 8 min.
  4. Smash each piece flat to about 1cm with the base of a glass.
  5. Crush the garlic to a paste with half the salt, then stir in the olive oil, lime juice and coriander.
  6. Turn the oil up to 180°C and fry the smashed plantain again until crisp and golden, 6 min.
  7. Drain on paper towel, sprinkle with the rest of the salt and serve hot with the mojo.

Jets' corner cart hot dogs with onion sauce

Simmered franks in soft rolls with a sweet, spicy tomato and onion sauce and yellow mustard.

Prep 8 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Onion sauce up to 3 days ahead in the fridge, reheat with a splash of water; simmer the frankfurts to order
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 long soft rolls
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 125ml water
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Halve and thinly slice the onions.
  2. Cook the onions in the oil over medium heat until soft and pale gold, 10 min.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, chilli powder, cinnamon, sugar, vinegar and water and simmer until thick and jammy, 15 min.
  4. Simmer the frankfurts in a pot of barely bubbling water until hot through, 6 min.
  5. Warm the rolls in a low oven, 3 min.
  6. Tuck a frankfurt in each roll, spread with mustard and spoon over the onion sauce.

Maria's pernil with arroz con gandules

Slow-roasted pork shoulder rubbed with garlic, oregano, cumin and citrus, with sofrito rice.

Prep 10 min, cook 5.5 hours, then 12.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the pork overnight, up to 1 day ahead, in the fridge; roast and make the rice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg boneless pork shoulder, skin on
  • 10 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 3 limes, 2 juiced and 1 in wedges
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 1 brown onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green capsicum, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch coriander, stalks chopped, leaves picked
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 cups long grain rice
  • 400g tin pigeon peas, drained
  • 100g green olives
  • 750ml vegetable stock
  1. Blend the garlic, oregano, cumin, paprika, salt, pepper, 60ml of the olive oil, the lime juice and orange juice to a paste.
  2. Score the pork skin, rub the paste all over and into the cuts, cover and marinate in the fridge overnight.
  3. Take the pork out of the fridge to come to room temperature and heat the oven to 160°C, 30 min.
  4. Roast skin up for 4 hours, then turn the oven to 230°C for 25 minutes to crackle the skin.
  5. While it roasts, soften the onion, capsicum and coriander stalks in the rest of the oil, then stir in the tomato paste, turmeric and rice for 2 minutes.
  6. Add the pigeon peas, olives and stock, bring to the boil, cover and cook on low for 20 minutes, then rest 10 minutes off the heat.
  7. Rest the pork 15 minutes, pull into chunks with the crackling and serve over the rice with coriander leaves and lime wedges.

Officer Krupke's corned beef and cabbage

Corned beef simmered with cabbage, carrots and potatoes. Gee, Officer Krupke, here's dinner.

Prep 5 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Cook the beef up to 2 days ahead; reheat sliced in the cooking liquid
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg corned silverside
  • 1 onion, studded with 4 cloves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 4 carrots, halved
  • 800g potatoes, halved
  • 1/2 green cabbage, in wedges
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp hot English mustard
  1. Cover the beef with water, add the onion, peppercorns, sugar and vinegar and simmer gently, 2 hr 30 min.
  2. Add the carrots and potatoes for the last part of cooking.
  3. Steam the cabbage wedges until tender and toss with butter, 10 min.
  4. Slice the beef and serve with the vegetables and mustard.

Bridal shop coconut flanVegetarian

Baked coconut and vanilla custard set over dark caramel, chilled and turned out.

Prep 8 min, cook 82 min, then 6.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake and chill up to 2 days ahead, left in the tin; turn out just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 395g tin condensed milk
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch of salt
  1. Melt the sugar with the water over medium heat, without stirring, until deep amber, 12 min.
  2. Pour the caramel into a 20cm round cake tin and swirl to coat the base, then heat the oven to 160°C, 5 min.
  3. Whisk the condensed milk, coconut milk, eggs, vanilla and salt until smooth, then strain into the tin.
  4. Sit the tin in a roasting dish and pour in hot water to come halfway up the sides.
  5. Bake until just set with a slight wobble in the middle, 55 min.
  6. Lift out of the water and cool on a rack, 30 min.
  7. Chill in the fridge until firm, 6 hr.
  8. Run a knife around the edge and turn out onto a plate with a lip to catch the caramel.

Doc's candy store egg creamNo alcohol

Homemade chocolate syrup, cold milk and fizzing soda water, stirred hard for a white foam head.

Prep 8 min, cook 6 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and cool the chocolate syrup up to 1 week ahead, kept in the fridge; build each glass to order so the foam holds
Recipe for 4
  • 50g cocoa powder
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150ml water
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 500ml very cold full cream milk
  • 1 L cold soda water
  1. Simmer the cocoa, sugar, water and salt, stirring, until smooth and glossy, then stir in the vanilla, 6 min.
  2. Cool the chocolate syrup completely, 30 min.
  3. Spoon 2 tbsp syrup into each of 4 tall glasses and add the cold milk.
  4. Pour in the soda water in a fast stream while stirring hard with a long spoon until a thick white foam rises.
  5. Serve straight away, with the spoon still in.
