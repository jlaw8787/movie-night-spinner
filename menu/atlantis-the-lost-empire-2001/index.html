Snacks

Chuck wagon beans

Navy beans, bacon, molasses and Dijon.

Prep 1 min, cook 2 hr, 16 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 400g dried navy beans
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  1. Soak the beans overnight.
  2. Soak, 8 hr.
  3. Simmer the beans in fresh water until tender, 50 min.
  4. Fry the bacon with the chopped onion, then stir in the beans, molasses, mustard and water to cover, 10 min.
  5. Simmer until thick and smoky, 1 hr.
  6. Serve in tin cups.

Vinny's office supply cheese, salami and cracker tins

Cheddar cubes, salami, water crackers and pickled onions in small tins.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Pack the tins with cheese, salami and onions up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; add the crackers just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 200g cheddar
  • 150g salami
  • 1 jar pickled onions
  1. Cube the cheddar and slice the salami.
  2. Pack small tins with crackers, cheese, salami and pickled onions.
  3. Close the lids and hand them out.

Mains

Lime grilled fish with mango salsa

White fish with lime and garlic, mango chilli salsa, jasmine rice.

Prep 19 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Make the mango salsa up to 4 hours ahead and keep chilled; cook the rice and grill the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 white fish fillets
  • 3 limes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • salt, to taste
  1. Rub the fish with lime juice, crushed garlic and salt.
  2. Dice the mango, chilli and red onion and mix with lime juice for salsa.
  3. Cook the rice in the coconut milk with a pinch of salt and a splash of water, 18 min.
  4. Grill the fish until just cooked, 8 min.
  5. Serve the fish on the rice with the salsa.

Cookie's campfire chilli and cornbread

Cookie calls it Caesar salad, escargot and spring rolls.

How it is made
  • 1kg beef chuck
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 2 tins crushed tomatoes
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 2 tins kidney beans
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 150g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 60g butter
  1. Beef chuck browned and simmered 3 hours with kidney beans, chilli, cumin and tomato until thick, served with cornbread from a cast iron pan. Brown 1kg diced beef chuck in batches. Fry 2 onions and 4 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika and 2 tsp chilli powder. Return the beef with 2 tins crushed tomatoes and 250ml beef stock and simmer covered for 2.5 hours. Add 2 drained tins kidney beans for the last 30 minutes. Mix 150g cornmeal, 150g flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 egg, 250ml buttermilk and 60g melted butter and bake in a hot cast iron pan at 220°C for 20 minutes.

Sweets

Heart of Atlantis meringueVegetarian

Meringue topped with whipped cream, blueberries and passionfruit.

Prep 24 min, cook 1 hr 30 min · Make ahead: Bake and cool the meringue up to 2 days ahead, airtight; top with cream, blueberries and passionfruit just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g blueberries
  • 3 passionfruit
  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until glossy.
  2. Shape into a round on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120°C, then cool in the oven, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Whip the cream and pulp the passionfruit.
  5. Top the meringue with cream, blueberries and passionfruit.

Drinks

Condensed milk iced coffeeNo alcohol

Strong plunger coffee over ice with sweetened condensed milk.

Prep 9 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Brew and cool the coffee up to 2 days ahead in the fridge; stir with condensed milk and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml strong coffee
  • 8 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 bag ice
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  1. Brew strong coffee in a plunger or stovetop pot.
  2. Cool for 10 minutes.
  3. Put condensed milk in four tin mugs, fill with ice and pour the coffee over.
  4. Stir well and finish with cinnamon.
