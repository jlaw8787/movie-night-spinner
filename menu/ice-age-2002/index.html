Snacks

Tae Kwon Dodos' last melonVegetarian

Chilled watermelon wedges with feta, mint, lime and flaky salt.

Prep 10 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Chill the melon well
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg watermelon, cut into wedges
  • 100g feta, crumbled
  • 1 handful mint leaves, torn
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt
  1. Cut the watermelon into thick wedges and chill well, 1 hr.
  2. Crumble the feta over, scatter mint, squeeze over the lime and finish with flaky salt. Serve very cold.

Manny's campfire corn with herb butterVegetarian

Corn cobs charred over the coals, brushed with garlic and herb butter.

Prep 7 min, cook 15 min, 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the herb butter up to 3 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs, husks on
  • 80g butter, soft
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 lime
  1. Soak the corn in cold water for 15 minutes.
  2. Mash the butter with the garlic, chopped parsley, lime zest and a pinch of salt.
  3. Cook the corn in the husks over hot coals or a barbecue for 15 minutes, turning, until charred.
  4. Peel back the husks and brush the hot corn with the herb butter. Serve with lime wedges.

Mains

Leave the mammoth alone slow-cooked beef ribs

Short ribs baked 4 hours in foil, then glazed with barbecue sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 4 hr 20 min · Make ahead: Rub and wrap the ribs up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; bake and glaze on the night, with the slaw mixed fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef short ribs
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Rub the ribs with salt, pepper and smoked paprika and wrap in foil.
  2. Bake at 150°C for 4 hours.
  3. Unwrap, brush with barbecue sauce and glaze under a hot grill, 10 min.
  4. Shred the cabbage and toss with the mayonnaise for slaw.

Sid's root vegetable and lentil stew

Root veg and lentils simmered with smoked paprika. Sid sees a tree, eats a leaf.

How it is made
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 parsnip
  • 500g pumpkin
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 200g brown lentils
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 litre vegetable stock
  • 60g baby spinach
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Carrot, parsnip, pumpkin and brown lentils simmered with tomato, smoked paprika and thyme until thick, with crusty bread and a dollop of herby yoghurt. Soften 1 diced onion, 2 garlic cloves and 2 diced celery sticks in olive oil. Add 2 carrots, 1 parsnip and 500g pumpkin, all in chunks, with 1 tsp smoked paprika and thyme. Add 200g rinsed brown lentils, 1 tin crushed tomatoes and 1 litre vegetable stock and simmer 35 minutes until thick. Stir in a handful of baby spinach. Mix 200g Greek yoghurt with chopped parsley and serve with crusty bread.

Scrat's chocolate hazelnut acornsVegetarian

Dark chocolate acorn truffles in hazelnuts, worth chasing over a glacier.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min, 3 hr waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 250g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 100g hazelnuts, toasted and chopped
  • 6 pretzel rods
  1. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate, then stir smooth, 10 min.
  2. Chill for 3 hours.
  3. Roll into small ovals and coat the bottom half in hazelnuts.
  4. Push a short piece of pretzel rod in the top as the stalk.

Ice age coconut marshmallow cupcakes

Vanilla cupcakes covered in marshmallow and rolled in coconut.

Prep 22 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Bake the cupcakes up to 1 day ahead and keep airtight; melt the marshmallow and roll in coconut on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g marshmallows
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 100g desiccated coconut
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs and vanilla and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in a muffin tin at 180°C for 18 minutes. Cool.
  3. Melt the marshmallows with the water, 6 min.
  4. Spread over the tops and roll in coconut so they look like snowballs.

Vanilla ice cream, caramel and peanut butter blended with milk.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Best blended just before serving; chill the milk and the glasses the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 3 tbsp caramel sauce, plus extra to drizzle
  • 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  1. Blend the milk, ice cream, caramel and peanut butter until thick.
  2. Pour into glasses and drizzle a little extra caramel on top.
