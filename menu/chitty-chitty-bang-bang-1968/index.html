Snacks

Breakfast machine Scotch eggs

Soft-centred boiled eggs wrapped in parsley and mustard sausage meat, crumbed and fried golden.

Prep 18 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Wrap and crumb the eggs up to 1 day ahead and chill; fry on the day. Cooked, they keep 2 days in the fridge for a cold picnic
Recipe for 4
  • 5 eggs, 4 for boiling and 1 beaten
  • 400g pork sausage mince
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • black pepper, to taste
  • 50g plain flour
  • 80g dry breadcrumbs
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp English mustard, to serve
  1. Lower 4 eggs into boiling water for 6 1/2 minutes, then cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Mix the sausage mince with the parsley, mustard powder and pepper, divide into 4 and flatten each into a patty on wet hands.
  3. Wrap a patty around each egg, sealing it well.
  4. Roll in the flour, then the beaten egg, then the breadcrumbs.
  5. Chill for 20 minutes so they hold their shape. Heat the oil in a deep pan to 170°C.
  6. Fry 2 at a time, turning, until deep golden and cooked through, about 7 minutes.
  7. Drain, halve and serve warm with the mustard, 3 min.

Seaside picnic cucumber sandwichesVegetarian

Crustless white bread fingers with thin salted cucumber and lemon and dill cream cheese.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the dill cream cheese up to 2 days ahead; make the sandwiches up to 2 hours ahead, cover with a damp tea towel and chill
Recipe for 4
  • 1 telegraph cucumber, very thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 125g cream cheese, softened
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • white pepper, to taste
  • 8 slices of white bread
  1. Toss the cucumber with the salt in a colander and leave to drain, 15 min.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, butter, dill, lemon zest and a little white pepper until smooth.
  3. Spread over the bread, pat the cucumber dry and layer it over 4 slices, then close the sandwiches.
  4. Cut off the crusts and cut each sandwich into 3 fingers.

Baron Bomburst's birthday schnitzel

Thin crumbed pork schnitzel fried golden in butter.

Prep 18 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Make the potato salad up to 4 hours ahead and keep at room temperature; crumb the schnitzels up to 4 hours ahead and chill; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g kipfler potatoes
  • 1 small red onion, finely chopped
  • 125ml vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil, for the potatoes
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 600g pork loin steaks
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g dry breadcrumbs
  • 150ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 30g butter
  • 2 lemons, in wedges
  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender.
  2. Warm the stock with the vinegar, mustard and onion. Slice the potatoes while hot, pour the dressing over, add the oil and chives, season and leave to soak, 8 min.
  3. Pound the pork between sheets of baking paper to 5mm thin and season.
  4. Dip each in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, pressing them on.
  5. Fry in the oil and butter over medium high heat, 2 to 3 minutes a side, in batches, until golden and crisp.
  6. Serve with lemon wedges and the warm potato salad.

Lord Scrumptious's roast beef and Yorkshires

Roast beef with Yorkshire puddings and gravy. For Truly's father, the sweet factory lord.

Prep 9 min, cook 76 min · Make ahead: Make the Yorkshire batter up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef sirloin roast
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1kg roasting potatoes
  • 140g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 200ml milk
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp horseradish cream
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk 120g of the flour, the eggs, milk and a pinch of salt into a smooth batter and leave to stand, 5 min.
  2. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub the beef with 1 tbsp of the oil and salt and roast for 45 minutes for medium rare, with the parboiled potatoes in 1 tbsp of oil on another tray.
  3. Rest the beef. Turn the oven to 230°C, put a drop of the rest of the oil in a 12-hole muffin tin and heat for 5 minutes, fill with batter and bake for 18 minutes until puffed.
  4. Stir the rest of the flour into the beef pan, whisk in the stock and simmer for 4 minutes for gravy. Whip the cream softly and fold in the horseradish.
  5. Carve and serve with the Yorkshires, potatoes, gravy and horseradish cream.

Toymaker's sticky gingerbread with custardVegetarian

Dark, sticky golden syrup gingerbread with ginger and cinnamon.

Prep 8 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep wrapped; it gets stickier. Warm through and heat the custard to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 125g brown sugar
  • 175g golden syrup
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 egg
  • 200ml milk
  • 500ml vanilla custard
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a 20cm square tin, 5 min.
  2. Melt the butter, brown sugar and golden syrup together over low heat, 5 min.
  3. Mix the flour, ginger, cinnamon and bicarbonate of soda, then whisk in the syrup mixture, the egg and the milk to a smooth batter.
  4. Pour into the tin and bake until risen and springy.
  5. Warm the custard in a small pan, 5 min.
  6. Cut the gingerbread into squares and serve warm with the hot custard, 3 min.

Jeremy and Jemima's lemon barley waterNo alcohol

Old-fashioned cloudy barley water with lemon zest and juice.

Prep 6 min, cook 35 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make and chill up to 3 days ahead; stir in the lemon juice on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 1.5 L water
  • 3 lemons, zested and juiced
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, sliced, to serve
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Rinse the barley, then simmer it with the water and lemon zest for 30 minutes.
  2. Strain into a jug over the sugar and stir until it dissolves.
  3. Cool, then chill until cold.
  4. Stir in the lemon juice and serve over ice with lemon slices.
