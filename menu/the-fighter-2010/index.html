Beer-battered onion rings with malt aioliVegetarian

Thick onion rings in crisp lager batter, malt aioli for dipping

Prep 8 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Cut the onions and mix the malt aioli up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large brown onions
  • 200g plain flour, divided
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the onions into 1cm rings and separate.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder and salt with cold lager until just smooth.
  3. Dust the rings in flour, dip in batter and fry in the oil in batches at 180C until golden, salting straight away.
  4. Mix the mayonnaise with malt vinegar and crushed garlic for the dip.

North Shore roast beef sliders

Thin-sliced roast beef, barbecue mayo and American cheese on slider buns

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min · Make ahead: Roast and slice the beef up to 1 day ahead and keep it in the fridge; warm it gently, mix the sauce and fill the buns on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef topside
  • 3 tbsp barbecue sauce
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 12 slider buns
  • 12 slices American cheese
  1. Roast the beef at 200C until 52C in the centre.
  2. Rest the beef, then slice paper thin.
  3. Mix the barbecue sauce with mayonnaise.
  4. Pile the beef into the buns with the sauce and a slice of cheese.

New England clam chowder cupsOn the printed menu

Bacon, onion, celery and potato in clam juice and milk, finished with cream, oyster crackers

How it is made
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 2 cans baby clams
  • 300ml full cream milk
  • 2 potatoes
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 packet oyster crackers
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Bacon rendered, onion, celery and potato softened, clam juice and milk added, clams and cream at the end, served in small cups with oyster crackers. Fry 150g diced bacon until crisp. Add a diced onion and 2 celery sticks and soften 5 minutes. Stir in 2 tbsp flour, then 2 cans baby clams with their juice drained into 300ml, plus 300ml milk and 2 diced potatoes. Simmer 15 minutes until the potato is tender. Add the clams and 150ml cream and heat through without boiling. Season with pepper and serve in cups with oyster crackers and chives.

Alice Ward's baked ziti

Ziti baked in Italian sausage tomato sauce with melted cheese

Prep 8 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; bake from cold and add 10 minutes
Recipe for 4
  • 400g Italian sausages
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 700g passata
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes
  • 500g penne or ziti
  • 250g ricotta
  • 200g mozzarella, grated
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  1. Squeeze the sausages from their skins and brown, breaking them up.
  2. Add garlic, passata and chilli flakes and simmer.
  3. Boil the penne or ziti for 2 minutes less than the packet says.
  4. Toss with the sauce, spread in a baking dish and dot with ricotta.
  5. Top with mozzarella and parmesan and bake at 200C.

Alice's Sunday pot roast and gravyOn the printed menu

Chuck seared dark, braised in red wine and thyme till tender, spuds cooked in the juices

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 250ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 box cornflour
  1. Beef chuck seared dark and braised with onion, carrot, celery, red wine and thyme until fork tender, with potatoes cooked in the juices and a thick gravy. Season a 1.5kg beef chuck roast and sear on all sides in a heavy casserole. Add 2 quartered onions, 3 carrots, 2 celery sticks, 250ml red wine, 500ml beef stock and thyme. Cover and braise at 160C for 2 hours. Add 800g halved potatoes and cook 1 hour more. Lift out the meat and veg, then simmer the liquid with 1 tbsp cornflour mixed in water until thick. Slice and serve with the gravy.

Boston cream pieVegetarian

Sponge layers filled with vanilla custard under chocolate glaze

Prep 17 min, cook 6 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 7 eggs, 4 whole and 3 yolks
  • 230g caster sugar, divided
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Whisk the whole eggs and some of the sugar until thick and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175C and cool.
  3. Cook the milk, yolks, sugar and cornflour until thick and cool.
  4. Cool the custard.
  5. Melt the chocolate with the cream.
  6. Fill the cake with custard and pour over the chocolate.

Charlene's bar black and tanVegetarian

Pale ale topped with Irish stout poured over the back of a spoon

Prep 6 min · Make ahead: Chill the cans and the pint glasses up to 1 day ahead; pour just before serving so the layers hold
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cans pale ale (375 ml)
  • 800ml Irish stout
  1. Chill four pint glasses in the freezer for 15 minutes.
  2. Pour the pale ale into each glass, tilting it so the head stays small.
  3. Hold a spoon upside down just above the beer and pour the stout slowly over the back of it so it floats in a dark layer.
  4. Serve straight away before the layers mix.
