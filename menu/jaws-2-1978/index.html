Snacks

Sailing trip sandwiches

Chicken salad with avocado and cos in soft rolls, wrapped for the boat

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, wrap tight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g roast chicken, torn
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 4 soft bread rolls
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cos lettuce
  1. Mix the chicken with the mayonnaise and lemon juice.
  2. Pile into the rolls with sliced avocado and crisp cos.
  3. Wrap tight in paper for the boat.

Amity Island fried clams

Buttermilk soaked clams or squid, cornmeal crust, tartare and lemon

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Cut the calamari into strips up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; soak, dredge and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g calamari, cut into strips
  • 200ml buttermilk
  • 100g fine cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp tartare sauce
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  1. Soak the calamari in buttermilk for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the cornmeal and flour and dredge the calamari.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil to 185°C and fry for 90 seconds, in batches.
  4. Serve with tartare and lemon wedges.

Shark fin chips with prawn cevicheOn the printed menu

Baked corn tortilla triangles with lime-cured seafood, tomato, red onion, chilli and avocado

How it is made
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 400g raw peeled prawns
  • 5 limes
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Tortillas cut into fin shapes and baked crisp, with a lime-cured prawn ceviche of tomato, red onion, chilli, avocado and coriander to scoop. Cut 8 corn tortillas into triangles, brush with oil, salt and bake at 190°C for 10 minutes until crisp. Dice 400g raw peeled prawns and cover with the juice of 5 limes for 20 minutes until opaque. Drain off half the juice and fold in 2 diced tomatoes, half a finely diced red onion, 1 chopped red chilli, 1 diced avocado and a handful of coriander. Season with salt and serve cold with the chips standing up like fins.

Mains

Cable Junction clambake

Clams, prawns, smoked sausage, corn and potatoes steamed in one pot

Prep 7 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Scrub the clams and slice the sausage up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; boil and steam it all on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g new potatoes
  • 4 corn cobs, halved
  • 200g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 60g butter
  • 1kg clams
  • 500g green prawns
  1. Boil the potatoes for 10 minutes in a big pot.
  2. Add the corn, sausage, butter and a splash of water.
  3. Add the clams and prawns, cover and steam for 8 minutes.
  4. Tip it all onto the table.

Chief Brody's backyard cheeseburgersOn the printed menu

Thick chargrilled patties, cheddar, grilled onions, tomato, pickles and smoky mayo in sesame buns

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 2 brown onions
  • 20g butter
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle barbecue sauce
  • 4 sesame burger buns
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Thick chargrilled beef patties with melted cheddar, grilled onions, beefsteak tomato, lettuce, pickles and smoky barbecue mayo in toasted sesame buns. Season 800g beef mince with salt and pepper and shape 4 thick patties with a dimple in the middle. Cook 2 sliced onions slowly in butter for 15 minutes until golden. Grill the patties over high heat for 4 minutes a side, adding a slice of cheddar for the last minute. Mix 4 tbsp mayonnaise with 2 tbsp barbecue sauce. Toast 4 sesame buns and stack with lettuce, tomato, patty, onions, pickles and the mayo.

Sweets

Blueberry cobblerVegetarian

Blueberries under a soft biscuit topping, served with vanilla ice cream

Prep 17 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Rub the butter into the flour up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; assemble and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 500g blueberries
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 80g butter, cold
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 1 tub vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the blueberries with the sugar and lemon juice in a dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour, add the milk and drop spoonfuls over the fruit.
  3. Bake at 190°C for 40 minutes.
  4. Serve warm with scoops of vanilla ice cream.

Drinks

Amity sailing day rum punchVegetarian

Golden rum, pineapple, orange and lime juice with grenadine, over ice

Prep 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Stir and chill the punch up to 1 day ahead; add ice and garnishes to serve. Make the rum-free jug the same way for the younger crew
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml golden rum
  • 400ml pineapple juice
  • 200ml orange juice
  • 80ml lime juice
  • 40ml grenadine
  • 1/4 whole nutmeg
  • 4 pineapple wedges
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 3 cups ice
  • 400ml soda water, for the rum-free jug
  1. Stir the rum, juices and grenadine in a large jug.
  2. Chill for an hour.
  3. Fill tall glasses with ice, pour in the punch and finish with grated nutmeg, a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
  4. For the younger crew, make a second jug without the rum and top it with soda water.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the teenagers set sail.
