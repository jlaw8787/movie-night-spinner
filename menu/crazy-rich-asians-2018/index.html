Snacks

Newton Circus chicken satay

Chicken grilled in a coconut milk, turmeric and lemongrass marinade, peanut sauce.

Prep 20 min, cook 8 min, 2 hr waiting · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, cut into strips
  • 200ml coconut milk, for the marinade
  • 2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 stalks of lemongrass
  • 100g peanut butter
  • 100ml coconut milk, extra for the sauce
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 12 bamboo skewers
  1. Chop the lemongrass finely and mix with the coconut milk for the marinade and the turmeric. Add the chicken, which is the start of the marinating.
  2. Marinate in the fridge, 2 hr.
  3. Soak the skewers in water, then thread on the chicken, 5 min.
  4. Grill until charred and cooked through, 8 min.
  5. Warm the peanut butter with the extra coconut milk, lime juice and sugar into a thick dipping sauce, 5 min.

Family dumpling making

Ground pork, wombok, ginger and spring onion dumplings.

Prep 40 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, freeze uncooked
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pork mince
  • 200g wombok (Chinese cabbage), shredded
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30 dumpling wrappers
  • 60ml black vinegar
  1. Salt the wombok, squeeze out the water, then mix with the pork, ginger, sliced spring onion and salt.
  2. Fold spoonfuls of filling into the wrappers around the table together.
  3. Boil in batches until they float, then a minute more, 10 min.
  4. Serve with black vinegar for dipping.

Mahjong table garlic chilli peanuts

Raw nuts fried golden with crisp garlic, dried chillies and curry leaves, served warm and salted.

How it is made
  • 300g raw peanuts
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 pack dried chillies
  • 1 pack curry leaves
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  1. Raw peanuts fried with crisp garlic, dried chilli and curry leaves, tossed with salt and a pinch of sugar, served warm in small bowls. Heat 3 tbsp oil in a wok over medium heat and fry 300g raw peanuts for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring often, until golden. Add 6 thinly sliced garlic cloves, 4 dried chillies broken up and a handful of curry leaves and fry 1 to 2 minutes more until the garlic is crisp. Drain on paper towel, toss with 1 tsp salt and half a teaspoon of sugar and cool slightly so they crunch.

Mains

Singapore chilli prawns

Prawns in a garlic, ginger, chilli and tomato sauce with swirled egg.

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 1 day, add prawns just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg green prawns
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 red chillies
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 200ml water
  • 2 eggs
  • 8 soft buns
  1. Crush the garlic, grate the ginger and chop the chillies.
  2. Fry the aromatics in the oil until fragrant, 3 min.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, sugar and water and simmer, 5 min.
  4. Add the prawns and cook until pink, 4 min.
  5. Swirl in the beaten eggs and serve with soft buns to mop up the sauce.

Wedding banquet prawn and chicken noodles

Fresh egg noodles wok tossed with shellfish, sliced thigh and choy sum in oyster sauce.

How it is made
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 300g chicken thigh fillets
  • 300g green prawns
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 box cornflour
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 bunches choy sum
  • 1 bottle oyster sauce
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Egg noodles stir-fried with prawns, chicken and greens in oyster and soy sauce, piled high on a platter. Loosen 400g fresh egg noodles in hot water. Slice 300g chicken thigh and toss with 1 tsp soy and 1 tsp cornflour. Stir-fry in a smoking wok with oil for 3 minutes and set aside. Stir-fry 300g peeled green prawns for 2 minutes and set aside. Fry 3 garlic cloves, then add 2 bunches chopped choy sum stems and leaves and 60ml water. Add the noodles, chicken, prawns, 3 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp soy and 1 tsp sesame oil and toss. Pile high on a platter.

Sweets

Pandan chiffon cakeVegetarian

Light coconut and pandan sponge cake.

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min, 40 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs, separated
  • 60g caster sugar, for the yolks
  • 100g caster sugar, for the whites
  • 120ml coconut milk
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp pandan paste
  • 150g plain flour
  1. Whisk the egg yolks with the smaller amount of sugar, the coconut milk, oil and pandan paste, then fold in the flour.
  2. Whisk the egg whites with the larger amount of sugar to stiff peaks and fold in gently.
  3. Pour into an ungreased tube tin and bake at 170°C, 45 min.
  4. Cool upside down in the tin before turning out, 40 min.

Iced condensed milk coffeeNo alcohol

Strong coffee stirred with condensed milk, poured over ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Coffee up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp ground coffee
  • 4 tbsp condensed milk
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Brew strong coffee.
  2. Stir the condensed milk into each cup of hot coffee until dissolved. Cool slightly, 3 min.
  3. Pour over glasses packed with ice, stir well and serve.
