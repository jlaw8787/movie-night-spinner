Snacks

Star Lasso Experience popcornVegetarian

Popcorn tossed in brown butter, smoked paprika and chipotle

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Brown the butter and stir in the spices up to 3 days ahead, kept chilled; melt it, pop the corn and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 120g popcorn kernels
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 pinch chipotle powder
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 2 tbsp oil, for popping
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a covered pot.
  2. Cook the butter until golden and nutty, then stir in the paprika, chipotle and salt.
  3. Toss through the popcorn and fill striped paper bags.

Chuckwagon beans

Pinto beans with bacon, chipotle, molasses and tomato paste, cooked in cast iron

Prep 10 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tins pinto beans, drained
  • 1 chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 2 tbsp molasses
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250ml water
  1. Fry the bacon and onion in a cast-iron pot until golden.
  2. Add the beans, chipotle, molasses, tomato paste and water.
  3. Simmer for 30 minutes until thick and smoky.
  4. Serve in tin cups.

Haywood ranch smoky beef jerkyOn the printed menu

Thin topside in soy, sugar, pepper and paprika, dried low until chewy, for the horse wranglers

How it is made
  • 800g beef topside
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 jar black peppercorns
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  1. Thin strips of topside marinated in soy, Worcestershire, brown sugar, black pepper and smoked paprika, dried low in the oven until chewy and smoky. Freeze 800g beef topside for 1 hour, then slice across the grain into 4mm strips. Mix 80ml soy sauce, 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp cracked black pepper, 2 tsp smoked paprika and 1 tsp garlic powder. Toss the beef through and marinate overnight. Pat dry and lay on racks over trays. Dry at 75°C with the oven door propped open a crack for 3 to 4 hours until leathery but still bendy.

Mains

Haywood Ranch grilled tri-tip and beans

Rump cap grilled over coals and sliced, with warm pinto beans

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Rub the rump cap with salt, pepper and garlic up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; make the salsa up to 4 hours ahead and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg rump cap
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tins pinto beans
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 1 lime, juiced
  1. Rub the rump cap with salt, pepper and garlic.
  2. Grill over coals, fat side first, until 54°C inside.
  3. Rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Warm the beans. Mix the tomatoes, onion, coriander and lime for salsa.
  5. Slice the meat and serve.

Angel's night shift chicken tinga burritos

Chipotle chicken tinga, rice and beans in flour tortillas. Angel's night watch on the clouds.

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the tinga up to 2 days ahead and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chipotle in adobo, chopped
  • 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 200g long grain rice
  • 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil, add the chipotle, tomatoes and chicken and simmer until tender. Meanwhile cook the rice.
  2. Shred the chicken into the sauce and cook until thick.
  3. Warm the beans and tortillas.
  4. Fill the tortillas with rice, beans, chicken, cheese and lettuce and roll up tight.

Sweets

Jean Jacket flying saucer cookies

Chocolate cookies with marshmallow, shaped like Jean Jacket.

Prep 5 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Bake and cool the cookies up to 2 days ahead, airtight; sandwich with the marshmallows and warm just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 180g plain flour
  • 8 marshmallows
  • 1 tbsp silver sprinkles
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter, beat in the sugar and egg, then the flour.
  2. Shape small rounds and bake at 175°C for 9 minutes. Cool.
  3. Sandwich pairs of cookies with a marshmallow each and warm for 1 minute to melt.
  4. Roll the edges in silver sprinkles.

Jupe's watermelon agua frescaVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh watermelon and lime blended and strained, served over ice with mint

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add the cold water and ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg watermelon flesh
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 250ml cold water
  • 1 sprig mint
  • 2 cups ice
  1. Blend the watermelon with the lime juice and sugar.
  2. Strain into a jug and add cold water.
  3. Serve over ice with mint.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when the crowd sits down at the western theme park show.
