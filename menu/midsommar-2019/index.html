Snacks

May Queen's lucky herring and new potatoes

Pickled herring with dill new potatoes, sour cream, chives and red onion.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled and warm through to serve; mix the sour cream and chives up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 600g new potatoes
  • 2 sprigs of dill
  • 250g pickled herring
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  1. Boil the potatoes with the dill until tender, 20 min.
  2. Serve warm with the herring, 3 min.
  3. Add a bowl of sour cream with chives and thinly sliced red onion.

Hårga flower crown cruditésVegetarian

Radish, cucumber and carrot ring with edible flowers and a dill sour cream dip.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into batons
  • 1 bunch baby carrots
  • 1 punnet edible flowers
  • 200g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Arrange the radishes, cucumber batons and baby carrots in a ring on a platter.
  2. Tuck edible flowers between them like a crown.
  3. Mix the sour cream with dill and salt for the dip in the middle.

Midsummer aged cheese tart with dill cream

Aged cheese baked in a crisp tart with dill cream, at the long tables in the midsummer sun.

How it is made
  • 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250ml cream
  • 150g aged cheddar
  • 50g parmesan
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 50g salmon roe
  1. A crisp shortcrust tart of aged cheddar and parmesan custard baked until just set, served in wedges with dill sour cream and salmon roe. Heat the oven to 190°C. Line a 23cm tart tin with a sheet of shortcrust pastry, prick, line with paper and weights and blind bake for 15 minutes, then 5 minutes without. Whisk 3 eggs, 250ml cream and plenty of pepper, then stir in 150g grated aged cheddar and 50g parmesan. Pour into the shell and bake at 170°C for 25 minutes until just set. Mix 150ml sour cream with chopped dill. Serve wedges warm with the dill cream and a spoon of salmon roe.

Mains

Ninety-year feast dill gravlax

Salmon cured 48 hours in salt, sugar and dill, with mustard sauce.

Prep 20 min, 2 days waiting · Make ahead: Needs 48 hours curing, keeps 4 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g salmon fillet, skin on
  • 50g rock salt
  • 80g caster sugar, 50g for the cure and the rest for the sauce
  • 2 bunches of dill
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 loaf rye bread
  1. Mix the salt, most of the sugar, chopped dill and white pepper and pack over the salmon.
  2. Weight the salmon and cure in the fridge for 48 hours.
  3. Wipe off the cure and slice thin.
  4. Mix the Dijon, remaining sugar, vinegar, oil and dill for the sauce. Serve with rye bread.

Hårga open-fire lamb with dill potatoes

Butterflied lamb grilled over coals, sliced pink, with dill potatoes.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg butterflied lamb leg
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg new potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  1. Butterflied lamb leg marinated in garlic, rosemary and lemon, grilled over coals and sliced pink, with buttery dill new potatoes and a mustard herb sauce. Marinate a 1.5kg butterflied lamb leg in 4 crushed garlic cloves, chopped rosemary, the zest and juice of a lemon, 3 tbsp olive oil and salt for 2 hours. Grill over medium-hot coals for 10 to 12 minutes a side until 58°C inside, then rest 10 minutes. Boil 1kg new potatoes until tender and toss with 50g butter and chopped dill. Whisk 2 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp vinegar, 80ml oil and chopped parsley for the sauce. Slice the lamb and serve.

Sweets

May Queen strawberry cakeVegetarian

Sponge layers filled with custard, whipped cream and strawberries.

Prep 33 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Assemble on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g plain flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 250ml custard
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 500g strawberries, sliced
  • 8 whole strawberries to top
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake at 175°C for 25 minutes, then cool completely.
  3. Split the sponge into three and brush each layer with milk.
  4. Fill with custard, whipped cream and sliced strawberries.
  5. Top with whole berries.

Drinks

Elderflower and strawberry gin punch

Crushed strawberries with gin and elderflower cordial, served pale and cold.

Prep 25 min · Make ahead: Leave out the gin for a booze-free jug
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries, hulled
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 200ml gin
  • 100ml elderflower cordial
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 750ml chilled sparkling water
  • 4 cups ice
  • 1 punnet edible flowers
  1. Crush the strawberries with the sugar in a jug and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Add the gin, cordial and lemon juice and stir.
  3. Fill the jug with ice and top with sparkling water. Pour into glasses with a few edible flowers on top.

When to serve

Serve the feast when Dani is crowned May Queen and the table toasts her.
