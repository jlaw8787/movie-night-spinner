Dreamhouse pink shrimp cocktail cups

Jumbo shrimp in pink cocktail sauce, served in small cups.

Prep 18 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Peel the prawns and mix the pink sauce up to 1 day ahead, separately in the fridge; shred the cos, dice the avocado and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g cooked king prawns
  • 120g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce, to taste
  • hot sauce, to taste
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 1 avocado
  • paprika, to taste
  1. Peel and devein the cooked king prawns, leaving a few tails on for the tops.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, the tomato sauce, the Worcestershire, a few drops of hot sauce and the juice of half a lemon until pink and smooth.
  3. Shred the cos heart and divide between the small glasses.
  4. Add diced avocado and the prawns, spoon over the sauce, dust with paprika and add a lemon wedge.

Beach Ken watermelon and feta skewersVegetarian

Watermelon, feta and cucumber cubes threaded with fresh mint.

Prep 20 min, 1 hr 5 min waiting · Make ahead: Cut and chill the watermelon, feta and cucumber up to 1 day ahead, kept separately in the fridge; thread the skewers and dress just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 quarter watermelon
  • 200g feta
  • 1 continental cucumber
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 lime
  • olive oil, to taste
  • chilli flakes, to taste
  • 12 skewers
  • 1 tsp salt
  1. Cut a quarter of a watermelon into 3cm cubes and chill well, 1 hr 5 min.
  2. Cut the firm feta and the continental cucumber into similar cubes.
  3. Thread onto the skewers in the order watermelon, mint leaf, feta, cucumber, watermelon.
  4. Lay on a platter, squeeze over the juice of the lime and drizzle with the olive oil.
  5. Mix the salt with a little chilli flakes and sprinkle over just before serving.

Girls' night beets pink devilled eggs

Whites soaked magenta in pickling liquid, whipped mustard yolks, chives and crispy shallots.

How it is made
  • 8 eggs
  • 1 jar sliced beetroot
  • 125ml white vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 2 tbsp fried shallots
  1. Boiled egg whites soaked hot pink in beetroot pickling liquid, filled with whipped mustard yolks and topped with chives and crispy shallots. Boil 8 eggs for 10 minutes, cool and peel. Mix 250ml liquid from a jar of sliced beetroot with 125ml white vinegar and 1 tbsp sugar, add the whole eggs and soak in the fridge for 2 to 4 hours until bright pink. Halve and pop out the yolks. Mash with 80g mayo, 2 tsp Dijon and salt until smooth, then pipe back in. Top with snipped chives and 2 tbsp fried shallots.

Pink beets pastaVegetarian

Penne in a roasted beets, ricotta and parmesan sauce.

Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr 15 min · Make ahead: Roast and blend the beetroot sauce up to 2 days ahead and keep chilled; warm it with a splash of pasta water and toss through the penne on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 beetroots
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 40g parmesan
  • 500g penne
  • 100g goat's cheese
  1. Roast the beetroot wrapped in foil at 200°C for 50 minutes, peel and blend with the ricotta, the garlic clove, the parmesan and a splash of pasta water.
  2. Toss through the cooked penne until glossy and pink, and crumble the goat's cheese over the top.

Mojo Dojo sticky barbecue ribs

Pork rubbed with paprika and sugar, baked low, glazed and charred, for the beach-off.

How it is made
  • 2kg pork ribs
  • 100g brown sugar
  • smoked paprika
  • garlic powder
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 60ml cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • foil
  1. Pork ribs rubbed with paprika, brown sugar and garlic, baked low until tender, then glazed with a sticky homemade barbecue sauce until charred at the edges. Heat the oven to 150°C. Mix 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp salt and pepper and rub over 2kg pork ribs. Wrap tightly in foil and bake 2.5 hours. Simmer 200ml tomato sauce, 60ml cider vinegar, 60g brown sugar, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and 1 tsp smoked paprika for 10 minutes. Unwrap the ribs, brush with sauce and roast at 220°C for 15 minutes, basting twice, until sticky.

Pink strawberry wafflesVegetarian

Waffles, cream and strawberry sauce, minus the burnt one.

Prep 40 min · Make ahead: Blitz the strawberry sauce up to 1 day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge; make the waffles and whip the cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 300g waffle mix
  • 400g strawberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Make the waffles from the waffle mix.
  2. Blitz the strawberries with the sugar for a pink sauce.
  3. Whip the cream.
  4. Stack the waffles with cream, the sliced strawberries and the pink sauce poured over.

Dreamhouse strawberry shake with sprinklesNo alcohol

Strawberry shake, white chocolate and sprinkle rim. The milk is fresh.

Prep 8 min, cook 4 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Dip and chill the glass rims up to 1 day ahead; blend the shake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 50g white chocolate
  • 3 tbsp sprinkles
  • 350g strawberries
  • 500g vanilla ice cream
  • 300ml milk
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 100ml thickened cream
  1. Melt the white chocolate and dip the rims of the tall glasses in it, then into the sprinkles, 4 min.
  2. Chill the glasses 10 minutes.
  3. Blend the hulled strawberries with the vanilla ice cream, the cold milk and the honey until thick and pink.
  4. Pour into the glasses, top with whipped cream and a halved strawberry.
