Snacks

Eat-first-talk-second noodle wormsVegetarian

Soy and sesame noodles that wriggle like worms. Jesús serves Judy and Nick worms.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the sauce up to 3 days ahead and chill; cook the noodles to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 400g fresh udon noodles
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  1. Whisk the soy, sesame oil, honey and garlic together.
  2. Cook the udon in boiling water until loose and tender, then drain.
  3. Toss the hot noodles in the sauce.
  4. Pile into small bowls and finish with spring onion and sesame seeds.

Zootennial Gala chicken pot pies

Chicken, leek and carrot pot pies, for crashing the Zootennial Gala

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, then add the leek and carrot and cook until soft.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock and cream, and simmer until thick.
  3. Stir in the peas, season and spoon into 4 small ovenproof dishes.
  4. Top each with a round of pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes and serve.

Mains

Neighsayer 2 movie night pizza

Crisp pizza straight from a hot tray, for Nick's Neighsayer 2 movie night

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Slice the toppings and grate the cheese up to a day ahead; stretch and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 250g balls pizza dough
  • 250ml pizza sauce
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 120g pepperoni, sliced
  • 150g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven as hot as it goes with a tray inside.
  2. Stretch the dough into 2 rounds and spread with sauce.
  3. Top with mozzarella, pepperoni, mushrooms and oregano.
  4. Bake on the hot tray until blistered and bubbling.
  5. Drizzle with oil and cut into slices.

Marsh Market fish tacos

Russ snaps up a fish at Marsh Market

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the crema and shred the cabbage up to a day ahead; fry the fish to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g firm white fish fillets, cut in strips
  • 100g plain flour
  • 150ml chilled soda water
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 125g sour cream
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Stir the sour cream with the zest and juice of a lime.
  2. Whisk the flour, paprika, a pinch of salt and the soda water into a light batter.
  3. Dip the fish and fry in hot oil in batches until crisp.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  5. Fill with cabbage and fish, add the crema and finish with coriander and lime.

Slinging pawpsiclesVegetarian

Strawberry and honey ice blocks. Hoggbottom says go back to slinging pawpsicles.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Freeze at least 6 hours or up to a week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries, hulled
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 125ml apple juice
  1. Blend the strawberries with the honey and apple juice until smooth.
  2. Layer the strawberry mix and yoghurt in ice pop moulds, swirling with a skewer.
  3. Push in the sticks and freeze until solid.

Drinks

Vending machine carrot sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Carrot, orange and ginger soda. Nick's giant can from the ZPD machine.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices up to a day ahead and chill; top with soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml carrot juice
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 500ml soda water
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Stir the carrot juice, orange juice, ginger and honey together.
  2. Pour over ice in 4 tall glasses and top with soda.
