Snacks

Pippin's hobbit fry-up

Bacon, fried mushrooms, grilled tomatoes and marmalade toast, served mid-morning

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 rashers bacon
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 30g butter, for frying
  • 2 tomatoes, halved
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 40g butter, for the toast
  • 4 tbsp marmalade
  1. Grill the bacon and tomatoes.
  2. Melt the butter for frying in a pan and fry the mushrooms until golden.
  3. Toast the bread and spread with the butter for the toast and the marmalade.
  4. Serve on a big platter.

Farmer Maggot's gruyère stuffed mushroomsVegetarian

Field mushrooms filled with garlic breadcrumbs and gruyère, roasted

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 4 large field mushrooms
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 80g gruyere, grated
  • 30g butter, melted
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Remove the mushroom stems.
  2. Mix the breadcrumbs, garlic, thyme, gruyere and butter.
  3. Pile into the caps and roast until bubbling.
  4. Rest briefly and serve.

Mains

Prancing Pony pie

Beef braised in dark ale under puff pastry, with buttery mash

Prep 30 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 375ml dark ale
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 sheet of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled
  • 50g butter
  • 300g peas
  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches, then soften the onions with the thyme.
  2. Add the ale and braise until the beef is tender.
  3. Thicken with the flour, pour into a pie dish, top with pastry and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden.
  5. Boil and mash the potatoes with the butter and cook the peas.

Sweets

Shire seed cakeVegetarian

Buttery loaf cake with caraway seeds, sliced thick and buttered

Prep 15 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, softened
  • 175g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp caraway seeds
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • extra butter, to serve
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line a loaf tin.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, caraway seeds and milk.
  3. Bake in the loaf tin until a skewer comes out clean.
  4. Slice thick and spread with butter.

Drinks

Prancing Pony hot buttered aleVegetarian

Brown ale warmed with honey, cinnamon, cloves and nutmeg, whisked with butter

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 4 bottles brown ale
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 30g butter
  1. Warm the ale with the honey, cinnamon, cloves and nutmeg until steaming, never boiling.
  2. Whisk the yolks, whisk in a ladle of hot ale, then whisk back into the pan with the butter and stir until slightly thickened.
  3. Pour into tankards. For a booze free version use apple juice instead of ale.
