Snacks

Sewer pizza rolls

Pinwheels of dough, red sauce, pepperoni and mozzarella, baked golden. Pizza dude's got 30 seconds

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Roll up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml pizza sauce
  • 80g sliced pepperoni
  • 150g grated mozzarella
  1. Roll the dough into a thin rectangle and spread with the sauce.
  2. Cover with the pepperoni and mozzarella, roll up tightly from the long side, slice into 12 rounds and place cut side up on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 220C until golden.

Splinter's burrata with hot honeyVegetarian

Burrata torn over charred sourdough with hot honey, cracked pepper and basil. Very wise, very old school

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Make the hot honey up to a week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 balls of burrata
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Take the burrata out of the fridge to lose its chill.
  2. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes until runny.
  3. Brush the sourdough with oil and char on a hot grill pan, 2 minutes a side.
  4. Tear the burrata over the toast, spoon the hot honey over, crack pepper on top and add the basil.

Mains

Cowabunga hot honey pepperoni pie

Thin crust, red sauce, mozzarella and cup-and-char pepperoni, hot honey poured on at the table. Splinter always liked Cowabunga

Prep 20 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Stretch the bases up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg pizza dough
  • 300ml pizza sauce
  • 250g grated mozzarella
  • 200g thin sliced pepperoni
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 bunch basil
  1. Divide the dough in two and stretch each thin on an oiled tray.
  2. Spread with the sauce, scatter the mozzarella and cover with the pepperoni.
  3. Bake at the oven's highest setting until the edges char and the pepperoni curls into cups.
  4. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes and pour over at the table with the basil.

Sweets

Pizza cookieVegetarian

Choc chip cookie baked in a pizza tray, chocolate drizzle and lollies, cut in wedges

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake up to a day ahead, top before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 150g choc chips
  • 50g dark chocolate
  • 100g lollies
  1. Beat the butter, sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips.
  2. Press into a greased pizza tray and bake at 180C.
  3. Drizzle with melted chocolate, scatter the lollies and cut into wedges.

Channel 3 green apple sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Fresh green apple and lime juice over ice, topped with soda. April O'Neil, Channel 3 news

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Juice up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 green apples
  • 2 limes
  • 800ml soda water
  1. Blend the apples with the lime juice and 200ml water, then strain.
  2. Pour over ice in 4 glasses and top with soda water.
