Snacks

Oozma Kappa misfit wings

Crisp baked wings tossed in barbecue sauce and honey, sour cream dip

Prep 13 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Toss the wings with the salt up to 1 day ahead and leave them uncovered in the fridge for crisper skin; mix the ranch dip up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 125ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Toss the wings with salt.
  2. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  3. Warm the barbecue sauce with the honey.
  4. Toss the wings through the sauce.
  5. Mix the sour cream with the mayonnaise and chives for a ranch dip and serve alongside.

Scare Games cheese friesVegetarian

Crisp oven chips under a hot cheddar sauce

Prep 6 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese sauce up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat with a splash of milk; bake the chips fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp pickled jalapenos
  1. Bake the chips at 220C until crisp.
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick and stir in the cheddar.
  3. Slice the spring onions.
  4. Pour the sauce over the chips and top with spring onion and jalapenos.

Mains

Monsters Inc. field trip spaghetti

Beef meatballs in tomato sauce over spaghetti, garlic butter baguette

Prep 34 min, cook 62 min · Make ahead: Make the meatballs and sauce up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled and reheat; boil the spaghetti and bake the garlic bread on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  • 1 baguette
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  1. Roll the mince into meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer.
  4. Split the baguette and spread with garlic butter.
  5. Bake the bread until crisp.
  6. Boil the spaghetti until al dente.
  7. Toss the sauce through the spaghetti with parmesan and serve with the bread.

Sweets

Scream can cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with blue buttercream

Prep 32 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 125g butter for buttercream, softened
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp blue food colouring
  • 2 tbsp purple sprinkles
  • 12 cupcake cases
  1. Beat the cake butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk.
  3. Spoon into cupcake cases.
  4. Bake at 175C.
  5. Cool completely.
  6. Beat the buttercream butter, icing sugar and blue colouring.
  7. Pipe with a grass tip for shaggy fur and add purple sprinkles.

Drinks

Mike's green apple coolerVegetarianNo alcohol

Apple juice, soda water, lime and mint over ice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the apple juice, lime juice and mint up to 3 hours ahead and keep it chilled; add the soda water, colouring and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml cloudy apple juice
  • 400ml soda water
  • 1 lime
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • green food colouring, a few drops
  • 1 green apple
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Pour the apple juice and soda water into a jug with the lime juice and mint.
  2. Stir in a few drops of green colouring.
  3. Slice the apple into thin rounds.
  4. Pour over ice with a round of green apple floating on top.
