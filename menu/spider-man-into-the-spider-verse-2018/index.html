Snacks

Spider-Ham's ham and pineapple pinwheels

Puff pastry rolls with ham, pineapple and mozzarella.

Prep 16 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Roll up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150g leg ham, chopped
  • 100g drained crushed pineapple
  • 120g mozzarella, grated
  1. Lay the pastry flat and spread each sheet with tomato paste.
  2. Scatter with the ham, pineapple and mozzarella.
  3. Roll up tightly and slice into 2cm pinwheels.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.

Chopped cheese rolls

Chopped griddled beef and onion with American cheese in hero rolls, lettuce and tomato.

Prep 10 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Shred the lettuce and slice the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; cook the mince and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 hero rolls
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • salt, to taste
  1. Prep the lettuce and tomatoes.
  2. Fry the mince and onion on a hot griddle, chopping it up with two spatulas, and season well.
  3. Lay the cheese over, let it melt and chop it through.
  4. Pile into the rolls with lettuce, tomato, tomato sauce and mayonnaise.

Brooklyn bodega hot plantain chips

Green plantains shaved and fried crisp with lime, chilli and smoked salt.

How it is made
  • 3 green plantains
  • 2 limes
  • chilli powder
  • smoked salt
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar pickled jalapeños
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 litres vegetable oil
  1. Green plantains shaved thin and fried crisp, tossed in lime, chilli and smoked salt, with a creamy pickled jalapeño and coriander dip. Peel 3 green plantains and slice very thin on a mandoline. Fry in batches at 175°C for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp. Toss hot with the zest of 1 lime, half a tsp chilli powder and smoked salt. Blitz 150g sour cream with 3 tbsp pickled jalapeños, a handful of coriander and lime juice for the dip.

Peter B. Parker's burger

Two smashed patties with bacon, onion rings and barbecue sauce.

Prep 8 min, cook 21 min · Make ahead: Slice and soak the onion in the milk up to 4 hours ahead, chilled; fry the rings and bacon and smash the patties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 large onion, thickly sliced
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 L vegetable oil
  • 8 rashers of bacon
  • 800g beef mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 tbsp barbecue sauce
  1. Slice the onion into rings and soak in the milk.
  2. Dredge the onion rings in flour and fry in the oil until golden.
  3. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  4. Smash eight patties from the mince on a hot griddle and top with cheese.
  5. Stack two per toasted bun with bacon, onion rings and barbecue sauce.

Aunt May's fluffy buttermilk pancakes

Tall stacks lightened with egg whites, bacon, butter, berries and maple syrup, safe house breakfast.

How it is made
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • bicarb soda
  • 450ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 90g butter
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 250g mixed berries
  • maple syrup
  1. Tall, fluffy buttermilk pancakes with whipped egg whites folded through, stacked with crispy bacon, salted butter, berries and warm maple syrup. Whisk 300g plain flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp bicarb and a pinch of salt. Mix 450ml buttermilk, 2 egg yolks and 50g melted butter, stir into the dry mix and rest 15 minutes. Whip 2 egg whites to soft peaks and fold through. Cook ladlefuls in butter over medium heat until bubbles set, then flip. Grill 8 bacon rashers. Stack with butter, berries, bacon and maple syrup.

Bodega cookiesVegetarian

Big brown butter chocolate chip cookies with soft middles and flaky salt.

Prep 9 min, cook 11 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days chilled, baked cookies up to 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g choc chips
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Brown the butter and cool.
  2. Beat with the sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips.
  3. Rest the dough overnight in the fridge.
  4. Scoop 10 big balls and bake at 180°C so the middles stay soft.
  5. Add flaky salt.

Coconut milk coquitoNo alcohol

Chilled coconut milk, condensed milk and cinnamon, poured over ice in Brooklyn.

Prep 5 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 200ml sweetened condensed milk
  • 200ml evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch of ground nutmeg
  1. Blend the coconut milk, condensed milk, evaporated milk, vanilla, half the cinnamon and the nutmeg until smooth.
  2. Chill.
  3. Serve in small glasses with a dusting of cinnamon.
