Snacks

Book's one-eyed devilled eggsVegetarian

Hard-boiled eggs filled with creamy mustard yolk.

Prep 19 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Boil, peel and fill the eggs up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; add the olive eyes and paprika to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 6 pitted black olives, halved
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp chives, finely chopped
  1. Lower the eggs into a pan of boiling water and boil for 10 minutes.
  2. Cool in a bowl of iced water, then peel and halve lengthways, 5 min.
  3. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise and mustard and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Spoon or pipe the filling back into the whites.
  5. Press an olive half into the middle of each egg for an eye, dust with paprika and scatter with chives.

Mary's robot vacuum corn dogs

Franks on sticks dipped in sweet polenta batter and fried golden.

Prep 6 min, cook 27 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 2 hours ahead and keep it in the fridge; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 bamboo skewers
  • 150g fine polenta
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 250ml milk
  • 1.5 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml tomato sauce
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  1. Whisk the polenta, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt, then whisk in the egg and milk to a thick batter. Pour into a tall glass.
  2. Heat the oil in a deep heavy pan to 180°C, 10 min.
  3. Pat the frankfurts dry and push a skewer into each.
  4. Dip each one in the batter to coat and fry 2 or 3 at a time, turning, until deep golden, about 3 minutes.
  5. Drain on paper towel and serve hot with the tomato sauce and mustard.

Winifred's cauldron beef and pumpkin stew with herb dumplings

Slow-simmered beef with carrots and butternut squash in a thick thyme gravy.

Prep 27 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the stew up to 2 days ahead and chill; reheat until bubbling and add the dumplings on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, in 4cm cubes
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 carrots, in chunks
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 L beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 600g butternut pumpkin, in 3cm chunks
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 60g cold butter, diced
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 150ml milk
  1. Toss the beef in the plain flour with salt and pepper and brown well in batches in the oil in a heavy pot. Lift out, 15 min.
  2. Cook the onion, garlic and carrots in the same pot until soft, 8 min.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, then the stock, bay leaves and thyme. Return the beef, cover and simmer gently until tender, 1 hr 30 min.
  4. Add the pumpkin and simmer, uncovered, until just soft, 20 min.
  5. Meanwhile rub the butter into the self-raising flour with the parsley and a pinch of salt, stir in the milk to a soft dough and roll into 8 balls.
  6. Sit the dumplings on top of the stew, cover and simmer until puffed and cooked through, 20 min.
  7. Season the stew and ladle into bowls with 2 dumplings each, 4 min.

Becca's birthday pumpkin and sage mac and cheeseVegetarian

Pumpkin and cheddar mac with crisp sage, for Becca's sixteenth.

Prep 0 min, cook 63 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g pumpkin, diced
  • 400g macaroni
  • 40g butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 600ml milk
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Roast the pumpkin in half the oil until soft, then mash, 25 min.
  2. Boil the macaroni until just short of done and drain, 10 min.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk until thick, then add the pumpkin and most of the cheddar, 8 min.
  4. Fold in the pasta, tip into a dish and top with the rest of the cheddar, crumbs and sage leaves tossed in the last of the oil. Bake until golden, 20 min.

Sanderson green potion punchNo alcohol

Bright green kiwi, apple and lime with bruised mint, topped with lemonade and frozen grape eyeballs.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the kiwi base up to 4 hours ahead and chill; freeze the grape skewers up to 1 week ahead; top with lemonade to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 kiwifruits, peeled
  • 500ml apple juice
  • 2 limes, 1 juiced and 1 sliced into wheels
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 12 green grapes, frozen on 4 skewers
  • 4 cups ice
  • 750ml lemonade
  1. Blend the kiwifruit with the apple juice and lime juice until smooth.
  2. Strain into a jug through a sieve, pressing on the pulp.
  3. Bruise half the mint and stir it into the jug.
  4. Fill 4 glasses with the ice, pour in the green potion and top with the lemonade.
  5. Garnish each with a frozen grape skewer, a lime wheel and a mint sprig.
