Hard Deck bar nachosVegetarian

Corn chips baked with black beans, cheddar and jalapeños, guacamole and salsa

Prep 12 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the tomato salsa up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; mash the avocado, bake the nachos and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 300g corn chips
  • 1 tin black beans, drained
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeños
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Layer the corn chips on a tray with the beans, cheddar and jalapeños. Bake at 200°C for 8 minutes.
  2. Mash the avocados with the lime juice. Mix the tomato, onion and coriander.
  3. Spoon the avocado, tomato salsa and sour cream over the hot nachos.

Don't think, just do loaded fries

Crisp fries topped with melted cheddar and crisp bacon

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cook the bacon up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; bake the chips, top and grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg frozen chips
  • 4 rashers bacon, diced
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp pickled jalapeño slices
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  1. Bake the chips at 220°C until crisp.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  3. Top the chips with cheddar and bacon and grill for 2 minutes to melt.
  4. Finish with sour cream, jalapeño and chives.

Dogfight football chilli cheese dogsOn the printed menu

Frankfurts charred over coals in toasted buns, spiced beef, melted cheddar, buttery onions, mustard

How it is made
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 20g butter
  • 8 franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 120g cheddar
  • 1 bottle American mustard
  1. Franks charred over coals in toasted buns, smothered in quick beef chilli, melted cheddar, buttery onions and yellow mustard. Brown 300g beef mince with a diced onion, add 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 200ml water and simmer 15 minutes. Cook a sliced onion in 20g butter until soft. Grill 8 franks over coals until blistered. Toast 8 buns, add the franks, spoon over chilli, top with 120g grated cheddar and the onions, and finish with mustard.

Penny's garlic butter grilled prawns

Butterflied prawns grilled in garlic chilli butter, with cos and cucumber salad

Prep 19 min, cook 11 min · Make ahead: Butter up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg green prawns in the shell
  • 80g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 cos lettuce, torn
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Butterfly the prawns through the shell and devein.
  2. Melt the butter with the garlic, chilli, zest of one lemon and parsley.
  3. Brush the prawns with the butter and grill for 2 minutes a side.
  4. Toss the cos and cucumber with lemon juice and oil. Serve with the prawns and crusty bread for the butter.

Hondo's hangar barbecue ribsOn the printed menu

Pork rubbed with paprika, sugar and garlic, baked low, smoky bourbon glaze, pickle slaw

How it is made
  • 2kg pork ribs
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 packet brown sugar
  • 1 jar garlic powder
  • 200ml barbecue sauce
  • 30ml bourbon
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 small cabbage
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  1. Pork ribs rubbed with paprika, brown sugar and garlic, baked low and slow, lacquered with smoky bourbon barbecue glaze and charred, with crunchy pickle slaw. Mix 2 tbsp smoked paprika, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp garlic powder and 2 tsp salt and rub over 2kg pork ribs. Wrap in foil and bake at 150°C for 2.5 hours. Simmer 200ml barbecue sauce with 2 tbsp bourbon and 1 tbsp honey for 3 minutes. Brush on and grill hot for 5 minutes. Toss half a shredded cabbage with chopped pickles, 3 tbsp mayo and a splash of pickle juice.

Dagger squad beach s'mores

Toasted marshmallows with salted caramel between sweet cookies

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Spread the biscuits with caramel and salt up to 1 day ahead, airtight; toast the marshmallows and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 marshmallows
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits
  • 100g thick caramel
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  • 200g milk chocolate
  1. Spread half the biscuits with a little caramel and a pinch of sea salt.
  2. Toast the marshmallows over a fire or under the grill until golden.
  3. Sandwich each marshmallow with a square of milk chocolate between two biscuits and squash together.

Penny's Hard Deck bourbon smashVegetarian

Bourbon shaken with muddled lemon, mint and sugar syrup, over ice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Cut the lemon wedges and pick the mint up to 3 hours ahead; muddle and shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 24 mint leaves, plus sprigs to serve
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 2 cups crushed ice
  1. For each drink muddle lemon wedges with sugar syrup and mint in a shaker.
  2. Add bourbon and ice, shake hard and pour everything into a tumbler.
  3. Top with crushed ice and add a big sprig of mint.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when everyone heads to the Hard Deck bar.
