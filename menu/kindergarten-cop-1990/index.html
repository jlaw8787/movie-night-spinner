Snacks

Lunch box crackers and cheeseVegetarian

Cheddar cubes, apple slices and crackers.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Cube the cheddar up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; slice and toss the apples on the morning
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar
  • 2 apples
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 packet crackers (about 150g)
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  1. Cut the cheddar into small cubes.
  2. Slice the apples and toss in lemon juice so they do not brown, 5 min.
  3. Pack each lunch box with cheese, crackers, apple and tomatoes.

Ferret-free fruit cupsVegetarian

Diced mango and pineapple, strawberries and grapes in orange juice.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep cold
Recipe for 4
  • 1 mango
  • 1/4 pineapple
  • 250g strawberries
  • 200g grapes
  • 1 orange
  • 4 mint leaves
  1. Dice the mango and pineapple, halve the strawberries and grapes.
  2. Toss together with the orange juice.
  3. Spoon into cups, top with mint and keep cold until serving.

Mains

Astoria school lunch fish fingers

Crumbed white fish fingers, oven chips, peas and tartare sauce.

Prep 17 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Crumb the fish fingers up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled; fry the fish and roast the chips fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g firm white fish fillets
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g dried breadcrumbs
  • 250ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 120g tartare sauce
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips, toss in oil and roast at 220°C, 38 min.
  2. Cut the fish into fingers and crumb in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  3. Shallow-fry the fish in batches, 10 min.
  4. Cook the peas, 5 min.
  5. Serve with tartare sauce.

Rump steak and loaded spuds

Seared sirloin sliced over green salad, baked potatoes topped with sour cream, cheddar and chives.

How it is made
  • 4 rump steaks (250g each)
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 lemon
  • 200g sour cream
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 bunch chives
  • olive oil
  1. Rump steak seared hard and rested, sliced over a crunchy green salad, with baked potatoes loaded with sour cream, cheddar and chives. Prick 4 large potatoes, rub with oil and salt and bake at 200°C for 60 minutes. Season 4 rump steaks (250g each) and sear in a smoking pan for 3 to 4 minutes a side, then rest 5 minutes. Toss cos lettuce, cucumber and cherry tomatoes with olive oil and lemon. Split the potatoes and load with sour cream, grated cheddar and chives. Slice the steak and serve.

Sweets

Kimble's class cupcakesVegetarian

Vanilla cupcakes with swirled red, yellow and blue buttercream and sprinkles.

Prep 18 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter, softened, for the cake
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 125ml milk
  • 150g butter, softened, for icing
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 6 drops of food colouring, primary colours
  • 1 jar sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 12-hole tin. Beat the butter for the cake with the sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour and milk, 12 min.
  2. Bake the cupcakes, 18 min.
  3. Cool on a rack, 20 min.
  4. Beat the butter for the icing with the icing sugar, divide and tint with the food colouring in primary colours.
  5. Swirl on the icing and add sprinkles.

Drinks

Big and strong chocolate malt milkshakeNo alcohol

Chocolate malted milkshake blended with vanilla ice cream.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; blend each batch just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 4 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 100ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 1 tbsp chocolate sprinkles
  1. Whip the cream.
  2. Blend the milk, ice cream, cocoa, malted milk powder and sugar until thick and frothy.
  3. Pour into glasses, top with whipped cream and sprinkles, and serve with straws.
