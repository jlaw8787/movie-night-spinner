Snacks

Santa Poco street cornVegetarian

Charred corn with lime mayo, crumbled feta, chilli powder and coriander.

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the lime mayonnaise and crumble the feta up to 1 day ahead, chilled; grill the corn and assemble just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 100g feta
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Grill the corn on a hot barbecue or grill pan, turning, until charred in spots.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise with the lime juice and crumble the feta.
  3. Brush the cobs with the mayonnaise, roll in feta and dust with chilli powder and chopped coriander.

Singing bush salsaVegetarian

Charred tomatillo and green chilli salsa with warm fried tortillas.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 400g tomatillos
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1/2 brown onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 lime
  • 300g corn chips
  1. Char the tomatillos and chillies under a hot grill until blistered.
  2. Blitz with the onion, garlic, coriander and lime juice until chunky. Season to taste.
  3. Serve with warm fried tortillas.

Cantina loaded black bean tostadas

Crisp fried tortillas, cumin beans and cheddar, pico de gallo and guacamole, for My Little Buttercup.

How it is made
  • 1 tin black beans
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 300g corn chips
  • 200g cheddar
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  • 2 avocados
  • 150g sour cream
  1. Corn chips layered with spiced black beans and melted cheddar, topped with pico de gallo, pickled jalapeno, guacamole and sour cream. Warm a drained tin of black beans with 1 tsp cumin and a splash of water and mash roughly. Layer 300g corn chips, the beans and 200g grated cheddar on a tray and bake at 200°C for 8 minutes. Dice 3 tomatoes, half a red onion and coriander with lime and salt for pico. Mash 2 avocados with lime. Top the nachos with pico, sliced pickled jalapeno, guacamole and 150g sour cream.

Mains

El Guapo's birthday tacos

Achiote and orange pork shoulder roasted slow.

Prep 33 min, cook 4 hours · Make ahead: Pork can be cooked 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg pork shoulder
  • 50g achiote paste
  • 2 oranges
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 lime
  • 16 corn tortillas
  • 1 jar habanero salsa
  1. Rub the pork with the achiote paste, orange juice and crushed garlic and marinate in the fridge overnight, or at least an hour if short on time.
  2. Wrap the pork tightly in foil and roast at 160°C until falling apart.
  3. Slice the red onion thin and quick-pickle in lime juice with a pinch of salt.
  4. Shred the pork in its juices and warm the tortillas.
  5. Serve in tortillas with the pickled onion and habanero salsa.

Hollywood red chilli chicken enchiladas

Shredded thighs and cheese in corn wraps, smoky sauce, the night Flugelman fires them.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 jar chilli powder
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 tin chipotles in adobo
  • 700g passata
  • 200ml chicken stock
  • 250g grated tasty cheese
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch coriander
  1. Poached chicken and cheese rolled in corn tortillas, smothered in a smoky red chilli sauce and more cheese, baked bubbling, with sour cream and coriander. Poach 800g chicken thighs in salted water for 20 minutes and shred. Soften a diced onion and 2 garlic cloves, add 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 chipotle in adobo, 700g passata and 200ml stock and simmer 15 minutes. Mix the chicken with a little sauce and 100g cheese. Roll in 12 corn tortillas, lay in a dish, cover with sauce and 150g cheese and bake at 190°C for 20 minutes. Top with sour cream and coriander.

Sweets

A plethora of churrosVegetarian

Fried cinnamon sugar churros with dulce de leche for dipping.

Prep 5 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml water
  • 60g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 L vegetable oil
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 jar dulce de leche
  1. Boil the water and butter, tip in the flour and beat to a smooth ball. Cool slightly, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Pipe the dough with a star nozzle into 180°C oil and fry until golden, turning once.
  3. Toss in the sugar and cinnamon while hot.
  4. Serve with dulce de leche for dipping and a candle in the pile.

Drinks

El Guapo's margaritas

Tequila, orange liqueur, lime, salt rim.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Juice the limes up to 1 day ahead, chilled; shake the drinks to order
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml tequila
  • 80ml orange liqueur
  • 4 limes
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Juice the limes and rim four glasses with a lime wedge and salt.
  2. Shake the tequila, orange liqueur and lime juice with ice in batches, one drink at a time.
  3. Strain into the glasses over fresh ice.

When to serve

Serve the mains when El Guapo asks if he has a plethora of piñatas.
