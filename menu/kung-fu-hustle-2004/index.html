Snacks

Pigsty Alley pork buns

Soft pleated buns filled with char siu pork in oyster and hoisin sauce

Prep 33 min, cook 12 min, then 80 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Fill and steam on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar, for the dough
  • 220ml warm water
  • 300g char siu pork
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 tsp caster sugar, for the filling
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, the caster sugar for the dough and warm water into a dough and knead.
  2. Prove the dough.
  3. Chop the pork and mix with the oyster sauce, hoisin and the caster sugar for the filling.
  4. Divide the dough into 12 rounds, fill and pleat shut.
  5. Prove again.
  6. Steam.

The baker's doughnutsVegetarian

Light fried Chinese dough sticks, crisp outside and airy inside

Prep 14 min, cook 20 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Dough the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200ml water
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 1l soy milk
  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and water and knead briefly.
  2. Rest overnight in the fridge.
  3. Cut strips, press two together and stretch.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 190°C in batches until puffed and golden.
  5. Warm the soy milk and serve alongside.

Axe Gang salt and pepper pork

Crisp fried pork belly wok tossed with garlic, chilli and Sichuan pepper salt

Prep 20 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Cut the pork and slice the garlic, chillies and spring onions up to 1 day ahead, chilled; coat in starch, fry and toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork belly
  • 100g potato starch
  • 2l vegetable oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 tsp Sichuan pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Cut the pork into strips and toss in the potato starch. Slice the garlic, chillies and spring onions.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches until crisp.
  3. Cook the rice.
  4. Grind the Sichuan pepper with the salt. Wok-toss the pork with the garlic, chillies, spring onion and salted pepper.

Landlady's prawn wonton noodle soupOn the printed menu

Pork and prawn dumplings in ginger chicken broth over thin egg strands, choy sum, chilli oil

How it is made
  • 250g pork mince
  • 200g green prawns, peeled
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 bottle sesame oil
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 packet wonton wrappers
  • 1.5L chicken stock
  • 400g thin egg noodles
  • 2 bunches choy sum
  • 1 jar chilli oil
  1. Pork and prawn wontons in a clear ginger and chicken broth, on thin egg noodles with choy sum, spring onion and a dash of chilli oil. Mix 250g pork mince with 200g chopped green prawns, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp sesame oil, grated ginger and chopped spring onion. Fill 32 wonton wrappers and fold. Simmer 1.5L chicken stock with sliced ginger and 2 tbsp soy. Boil the wontons for 3 minutes, then cook 400g thin egg noodles for 2 minutes and blanch 2 bunches choy sum. Divide everything into bowls, ladle over the broth and add spring onion and chilli oil.

Mango sagoVegetarian

Chilled coconut sago pearls topped with mango puree and diced mango

Prep 14 min, cook 15 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g sago
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 mangoes
  1. Boil the sago until clear, then rinse and chill.
  2. Chill.
  3. Mix the sago with the coconut milk and sugar.
  4. Blitz one mango and dice the other.
  5. Spoon the sago into glasses and top with mango purée and cubes.

Lollipop girl's lemon swirl lollipopsOn the printed menu

Clear hard sugar and glucose boiled with lemon, swirled red and yellow, set on sticks

How it is made
  • 400g white sugar
  • 150ml liquid glucose
  • 1 bottle lemon extract
  • 1 packet red and yellow food colouring
  • 12 lollipop sticks
  1. Clear lollipops boiled from sugar and glucose, flavoured with lemon and swirled with red and yellow colour, set on baking paper. Line two trays with baking paper and lay out 12 lollipop sticks. Boil 400g sugar, 150ml liquid glucose and 125ml water without stirring until it reaches 150°C on a sugar thermometer. Take off the heat and stir in 1 tsp lemon extract. Spoon rounds over the end of each stick, then drag a toothpick dipped in red and yellow colour through each for a swirl. Cool 20 minutes until hard.

Chrysanthemum teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Dried chrysanthemum flowers steeped with rock sugar, served warm or iced

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Steep and strain up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled; reheat or pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 15g dried chrysanthemum
  • 50g rock sugar
  • 1l hot water
  • 2 cups ice, if serving cold
  1. Steep the chrysanthemum flowers in hot water with the rock sugar.
  2. Strain and serve warm in cups, or chill and pour over ice.
