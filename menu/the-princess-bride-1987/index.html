Snacks

Max's sanga

Sliced pink lamb backstrap, mint mayo, lettuce and tomato on toasted sourdough

Prep 14 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Sear and slice the lamb up to 1 day ahead in the fridge and mix the mint mayonnaise up to 2 days ahead; toast the sourdough and build on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g lamb backstrap
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  1. Sear the lamb to pink in a hot pan.
  2. Rest, then slice thin.
  3. Toast the sourdough. Mix the mayonnaise with chopped mint.
  4. Build with crisp cos, ripe tomato and the lamb. When the mutton is nice and lean.

Fire Swamp chorizo and honey chilliOn the printed menu

Thick rounds fried crisp, glazed with chilli honey and lemon, watch for flame spurts

How it is made
  • 300g chorizo
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  1. Chorizo fried until crisp and charred, glazed with hot honey and lemon, served sizzling in a pan with crusty bread to mop it up. Slice 2 chorizo sausages (about 300g) into thick rounds on the diagonal. Fry in a dry pan over medium-high heat for 4 to 5 minutes until crisp and the oil runs. Add 2 tbsp honey, half a tsp chilli flakes and a squeeze of lemon and toss for 30 seconds until sticky. Scatter with parsley and serve in the pan with a sliced baguette.

Fezzik's giant pie

Beef chuck slow cooked in Worcestershire gravy and baked under pastry

Prep 30 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Filling up to 2 days, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 600ml beef stock
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Dice the beef and toss with the flour. Slice the onions.
  2. Brown the beef in batches.
  3. Add the onions and thyme and cook briefly, then add the Worcestershire sauce and stock. Cover and simmer gently until tender and thick.
  4. Pour into a large pie dish and cool slightly.
  5. Cover with puff pastry, brush with beaten egg and bake at 200C.

Humperdinck's wedding feast rib roastOn the printed menu

Garlic and herb crusted beef cooked pink, carved at the table, Yorkshire puddings, as you wish

How it is made
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 140g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 250ml milk
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar horseradish
  1. Beef rib roast crusted with garlic and herbs, roasted pink and carved at the table, with Yorkshire puddings, roast potatoes and horseradish cream. Rub a 2kg beef rib roast with salt, pepper, garlic and rosemary and leave uncovered overnight. Roast at 220C for 20 minutes, then 160C until 52C inside, about 60 minutes, and rest 20 minutes. Roast 1kg potatoes. Whisk 140g flour, 3 eggs and 250ml milk, pour into 12 hot oiled muffin holes and bake at 230C for 20 minutes. Mix 150g sour cream with horseradish.

Miracle pills chocolate trufflesVegetarian

Dark chocolate truffles, flaky salt, cocoa. For the mostly dead.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 1 pinch flaky salt
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  1. Chop the chocolate. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate with the salt. Stir smooth.
  2. Chill until firm.
  3. Roll into small balls and coat in cocoa. Have fun storming the castle.

Cliffs of Insanity cakeVegetarian

Three chocolate layers, caramel and rough ganache. He didn't fall? Inconceivable!

Prep 15 min, cook 46 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 350g dark chocolate
  • 200g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 200g caramels
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Melt part of the chocolate with the butter, whisk in the eggs and sugar, then fold in the flour.
  2. Bake in three tins at 170C.
  3. Cool the cakes.
  4. Melt the caramels with some of the cream for the filling.
  5. Make ganache with the remaining chocolate and cream. Stack tall and spread the ganache roughly over the sides.

Iocane-free spiced grape gobletsVegetarianNo alcohol

Warm red grape juice with cinnamon, star anise, orange peel and honey, no alcohol

Prep 6 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Warm and strain the juice up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat or pour over ice to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1l red grape juice
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 star anise
  • 2 strips orange peel
  • 1 tbsp honey
  1. Put the grape juice, cinnamon stick, star anise, orange peel and honey in a saucepan.
  2. Warm gently without boiling, then strain.
  3. Serve warm in four goblets, or chill and pour over ice. Set out two goblets each and let someone swap them around while nobody's looking.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Vizzini and the man in black do the battle of wits.
