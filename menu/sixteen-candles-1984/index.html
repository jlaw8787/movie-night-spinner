Snacks

Wedding reception canapés

Ham quiche tartlets and smoked salmon cucumber rounds.

Prep 18 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Tartlets up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 2 eggs
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 80g leg ham, diced
  • 1 small bunch chives
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 150g smoked salmon
  1. Cut rounds from the pastry and line a 12-hole tin.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the cream, add the ham and chives and fill the cases.
  3. Bake at 180°C until set and golden.
  4. Cut thick cucumber rounds and top with smoked salmon.

Pan fried pork dumplings

Golden pork dumplings steamed in the pan, soy and black vinegar dip.

Prep 4 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; mix the dipping sauce up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 24 frozen pork dumplings
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100ml water
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  1. Mix the soy, black vinegar and spring onion for dipping.
  2. Fry the dumplings flat side down in a little oil until golden.
  3. Add water, cover and steam.
  4. Uncover and crisp again.

Floppy disk bet cinnamon pretzel bites

Soft knots baked deep brown, rolled in butter and spiced sugar, warm caramel dip.

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 60g bicarb soda
  • 50g butter
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150ml caramel sauce
  1. Soft pretzel bites dipped in a bicarb bath and baked deep brown, rolled in butter and cinnamon sugar, with a warm caramel dip. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 250ml warm water, knead and prove 1 hour. Roll into ropes, cut into 3cm bites and dip in 2 litres boiling water with 60g bicarb soda for 30 seconds. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes. Toss in 50g melted butter, then 80g caster sugar mixed with 2 tsp cinnamon. Warm 150ml store-bought caramel sauce with a pinch of salt for dipping.

Mains

Ginny's wedding buffet chicken

Chicken thighs braised with mushrooms, thyme, white wine and cream, rice pilaf.

Prep 15 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 200ml white wine
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 20g butter
  • 600ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil.
  2. Add the mushrooms, thyme and wine and braise.
  3. Cook the rice with the butter and stock for a pilaf.
  4. Stir the cream into the chicken and serve with the pilaf.

Grandparents-in-my-room pot roast

Chuck braised for hours with onion, carrot and stock until falling apart, buttery potatoes and gravy.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef chuck roast
  • 2 onions
  • 4 carrots
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • bay leaves
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  1. Beef chuck braised for hours with onion, carrot and stock until it falls apart, with buttery potatoes and a rich gravy from the pot. Season a 1.5kg chuck roast and brown it all over in a heavy pot. Add 2 quartered onions, 4 carrots in chunks and 3 garlic cloves, then 750ml beef stock, 2 tbsp tomato paste, thyme and bay. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 hours. Add 800g halved potatoes and cook 1 hour more. Lift out the meat and veg and thicken the liquid with 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with water.

Sweets

Sixteen candles birthday cakeVegetarian

Two-layer butter cake with strawberry buttercream and sixteen candles.

Prep 18 min, cook 42 min · Make ahead: Cake up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 450g butter, soft
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 150g strawberries
  • 450g icing sugar
  • 16 birthday candles
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and prepare two tins. Beat half the butter with the sugar, then the eggs.
  2. Fold in the flour and milk, divide between the tins and bake.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Blitz the strawberries and beat into the remaining butter with the icing sugar.
  5. Fill and cover the cake and add exactly sixteen candles.

Drinks

Shermer High dance tropical punchNo alcohol

Pineapple, orange, cranberry and passionfruit punch topped with lemonade, no alcohol.

Prep 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Base up to 1 day, add lemonade and ice at serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 300ml cranberry juice
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 2 limes
  • 1 L lemonade
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 150g frozen mixed berries
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Mix the pineapple, orange and cranberry juices with the passionfruit pulp and lime juice in a punch bowl.
  2. Chill.
  3. Just before serving add the lemonade, plenty of ice, orange slices and berries.
  4. Ladle into cups.
