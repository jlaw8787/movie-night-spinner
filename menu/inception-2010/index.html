Snacks

Paris café chocolate and ham croissants

Puff pastry crescents filled with dark chocolate, or ham and gruyere

Prep 25 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Roll the croissants up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; brush with egg and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100g leg ham
  • 100g gruyere
  • 1 egg
  1. Cut the pastry into long triangles.
  2. Lay a stick of chocolate, or ham and gruyere, at the wide end and roll up towards the point.
  3. Curve into crescents and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden.

Spinning top meatballs

Lamb and beef meatballs with cumin and parsley, garlic yoghurt to dip

Prep 25 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; mix the garlic yoghurt up to 2 days ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g lamb mince
  • 250g beef mince
  • 4 cloves garlic, divided
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the mince with crushed garlic, cumin, parsley and salt.
  2. Roll into small balls.
  3. Bake at 200C.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic.
  5. Spear each meatball upright on a toothpick and serve with the yoghurt.

Mains

Saito's teriyaki salmon bento box

Glazed salmon, sushi rice, pickled ginger and rice vinegar cucumber

Prep 35 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Simmer the glaze up to 3 days ahead and boil the eggs up to 1 day ahead; cook the rice and grill the salmon on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 4 tbsp pickled ginger
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 eggs
  1. Simmer the soy, mirin and sugar until syrupy.
  2. Cook the sushi rice.
  3. Grill the salmon, brushing with the glaze until sticky.
  4. Soft boil the eggs, peel and halve.
  5. Slice the cucumber thinly and dress in rice vinegar.
  6. Pack boxes with rice, salmon, pickled ginger, cucumber and egg.

Sweets

Dream bigger layer cakeVegetarian

Three thin sponges layered with chocolate mousse and coffee cream

Prep 45 min, cook 247 min · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 600ml thickened cream, divided
  • 60ml cold espresso
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake 3 thin sponges at 175C and cool.
  3. Make a chocolate mousse with melted chocolate and whipped cream.
  4. Make a coffee cream with whipped cream and cold espresso.
  5. Layer the sponges with mousse and coffee cream.
  6. Chill for 4 hours.
  7. Slice to show every level.

Drinks

Long-haul espresso martiniVegetarian

Espresso, vodka and coffee liqueur shaken until frothy, three beans on top

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Brew and cool the espresso up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake with the vodka and coffee liqueur to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml strong espresso, cooled slightly
  • 180ml vodka
  • 60ml coffee liqueur
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the espresso and let it cool slightly.
  2. Shake the espresso, vodka and coffee liqueur very hard with ice until frothy, in batches.
  3. Strain into chilled glasses.
  4. Float three coffee beans on the foam of each.
