Snacks

Paris café chocolate and ham croissants

Puff pastry crescents filled with dark chocolate, or ham and gruyere

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Roll the croissants up to 1 day ahead and keep covered in the fridge; brush with egg and bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100g leg ham
  • 100g gruyere
  • 1 egg
  1. Cut the pastry into long triangles.
  2. Lay a stick of chocolate, or ham and gruyere, at the wide end and roll up towards the point.
  3. Curve into crescents and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.

Spinning top meatballs

Lamb and beef meatballs with cumin and parsley, garlic yoghurt to dip

Prep 22 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; mix the garlic yoghurt up to 2 days ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 250g lamb mince
  • 250g beef mince
  • 4 cloves garlic, divided
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • salt, to taste
  1. Mix the mince with crushed garlic, cumin, parsley and salt.
  2. Roll into small balls.
  3. Bake at 200°C.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic.
  5. Spear each meatball upright on a toothpick and serve with the yoghurt.

Mombasa street samosas with chutneyOn the printed menu

Spiced potato, peas and green chilli in pastry triangles, fried blistered, for a market chase

How it is made
  • 600g potatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 jar cumin seeds
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 jar garam masala
  • 12 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 lime
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  • 2L vegetable oil
  1. Spiced potato, peas and onion with garam masala and green chilli, folded into pastry triangles and fried until blistered, with a lime and coriander chutney. Boil 600g diced potatoes until tender. Fry an onion with 1 tsp cumin seeds, a chopped green chilli and 1 tsp grated ginger, add the potato, 100g peas and 2 tsp garam masala and mash lightly. Cut 12 spring roll wrappers in half, fold into cones, fill and seal with flour paste. Fry at 180°C until crisp. Blitz a bunch of coriander, a green chilli, lime juice, salt and 2 tbsp yoghurt for chutney.

Saito's teriyaki salmon bento box

Glazed salmon, sushi rice, pickled ginger and rice vinegar cucumber

Prep 8 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Simmer the glaze up to 3 days ahead and boil the eggs up to 1 day ahead; cook the rice and grill the salmon on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 4 tbsp pickled ginger
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 eggs
  1. Simmer the soy, mirin and sugar until syrupy.
  2. Cook the sushi rice.
  3. Grill the salmon, brushing with the glaze until sticky.
  4. Soft boil the eggs, peel and halve.
  5. Slice the cucumber thinly and dress in rice vinegar.
  6. Pack boxes with rice, salmon, pickled ginger, cucumber and egg.

Snow fortress red wine beef stewOn the printed menu

Chuck braised with carrot, thyme and bay till it falls apart, mash, gremolata, Piaf counting down

How it is made
  • 1.2kg beef chuck
  • 2 brown onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 300ml red wine
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 lemon
  1. Beef chuck browned and braised with onion, carrot, garlic, red wine, thyme and bay until it falls apart, served over buttery mash with gremolata. Brown 1.2kg diced beef chuck in batches. Soften 2 onions, 2 carrots and 4 garlic cloves, stir in 2 tbsp tomato paste, then 300ml red wine and 500ml beef stock with thyme and 2 bay leaves. Return the beef, cover and simmer for 3 hours. Mash 1kg boiled potatoes with 60g butter and 100ml milk. Chop parsley with lemon zest and garlic for gremolata and scatter over the stew.

Dream bigger layer cakeVegetarian

Three thin sponges layered with chocolate mousse and coffee cream

Prep 27 min, cook 25 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 600ml thickened cream, divided
  • 60ml cold espresso
  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until thick and fold in the flour.
  2. Bake 3 thin sponges at 175°C and cool.
  3. Make a chocolate mousse with melted chocolate and whipped cream.
  4. Make a coffee cream with whipped cream and cold espresso.
  5. Layer the sponges with mousse and coffee cream.
  6. Chill for 4 hours.
  7. Slice to show every level.

Drinks

Long-haul espresso martiniVegetarian

Espresso, vodka and coffee liqueur shaken until frothy, three beans on top

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Brew and cool the espresso up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; shake with the vodka and coffee liqueur to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml strong espresso, cooled slightly
  • 180ml vodka
  • 60ml coffee liqueur
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Brew the espresso and let it cool slightly.
  2. Shake the espresso, vodka and coffee liqueur very hard with ice until frothy, in batches.
  3. Strain into chilled glasses.
  4. Float three coffee beans on the foam of each.
