Snacks

Library book cheese stacksVegetarian

Rosemary lavash crisps stacked with sharp cheddar and thin apple slices.

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Crisps up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 lavash breads
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 200g vintage cheddar
  • 2 apples
  1. Cut the lavash into neat rectangles, brush with oil and sprinkle with chopped rosemary.
  2. Bake at 180°C until crisp, then cool, 10 min.
  3. Stack with slices of cheddar and thin apple slices like a little pile of books.

Mains

Matilda's pancakesVegetarian

Buttermilk pancakes stacked with warm blueberries, butter and maple syrup.

Prep 3 min, cook 31 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g butter
  • 250g blueberries
  • maple syrup, to serve
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, buttermilk and eggs.
  2. Rest the batter, 15 min.
  3. Cook ladlefuls in butter until bubbles set, then flip, 20 min.
  4. Warm the blueberries in a pan, 3 min.
  5. Stack high with butter, maple syrup and the blueberries.

Wormwood Motors chilli dogs

Chilli franks, a great deal from Harry.

How it is made
  • 2 onions
  • 30g butter
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 jar mild chilli powder
  • 200g tomato passata
  • 8 franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 100g cheddar
  • 1 jar American mustard
  1. Griddled franks in toasted buns, smothered in beef chilli and cheddar with jammy onions and mustard, a deal even Harry couldn't fiddle. Cook 2 sliced onions in 30g butter for 25 minutes until jammy. Brown 300g beef mince with 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp mild chilli powder and 200g passata and simmer 15 minutes. Simmer 8 franks 5 minutes, then sear on a griddle. Toast 8 hot dog buns. Fill each with a frank, chilli, onions, grated cheddar from 100g and a stripe of mustard.

Sweets

Cottage tea sconesVegetarian

Warm scones, jam and cream, for tea at Miss Honey's.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cold butter
  • 350g self-raising flour
  • 250ml full cream milk
  • 1 jar strawberry jam
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Rub the butter into the flour.
  2. Mix in the milk lightly with a knife, pat out, cut into rounds and pack close on a tray.
  3. Bake at 220°C, 12 min.
  4. Whip the cream and serve the scones warm with jam and cream, 5 min.

Bruce Bogtrotter's chocolate cakeVegetarian

Chocolate buttermilk cake, thick frosting, for Bruce Bogtrotter to finish.

Prep 0 min, cook 77 min, then 45 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 300g dark chocolate
  • 300g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 200g plain flour
  • 40g cocoa
  • 1 tsp bicarb soda
  • 150g icing sugar
  1. Melt part of the chocolate with part of the butter. Whisk in the eggs, sugar and buttermilk, then fold in the flour, cocoa and bicarb, 20 min.
  2. Bake at 170°C, 45 min.
  3. Cool the cake, 45 min.
  4. Beat melted chocolate with the remaining butter and icing sugar to make the icing, and cover the cake.

Drinks

Trunchbull's newt-free watermelon coolerNo alcohol

Watermelon, lime and mint blended and strained, topped with sparkling water.

Prep 18 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain the watermelon mix up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled. Top with sparkling water and ice just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg seedless watermelon
  • 2 limes
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 500ml sparkling water
  • ice, to serve
  1. Cut the watermelon flesh into chunks.
  2. Blend with the lime juice and mint until smooth.
  3. Strain into a jug, pressing on the pulp. Add honey if the melon is not very sweet.
  4. Top with sparkling water and plenty of ice. Pour into four glasses with mint sprigs.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Bruce Bogtrotter faces the cake.
