Snacks

Surrey cottage cheese boardVegetarian

Aged cheddar, brie and blue cheese with oatcakes, quince paste, apple and walnuts.

Prep 40 min · Make ahead: Set the board up to 2 hours ahead, covered, and slice the apple on the night; take the cheese out 30 minutes before eating
Recipe for 4
  • 150g aged cheddar
  • 150g brie
  • 150g blue cheese
  • 1 packet oatcakes
  • 1 small tub quince paste
  • 1 apple
  • 100g walnuts
  1. Bring the cheddar, brie and blue cheese to room temperature.
  2. Arrange the cheddar, brie and blue cheese on a wooden board with oatcakes, quince paste, sliced apple and walnuts.
  3. Set it by the fire with a glass of something warm.

Rosehill Cottage Welsh rarebit

Mustard cheddar toast. Amanda lasts one quiet night, then stays.

Prep 1 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the ale cheese mixture up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled; spread on toast and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 80ml ale
  • 200g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce
  • 6 slices sourdough
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook 1 minute, then whisk in the ale.
  2. Add the cheddar, mustard and Worcestershire and stir over low heat until thick and smooth.
  3. Toast the sourdough.
  4. Spread the cheese mixture thickly to the edges and grill until bubbling and browned in spots.
  5. Cut in half and serve hot.

LA pool house green goddess crudités

Baby carrots, radishes and snow peas on ice, avocado herb dip, for Amanda who can't cry.

How it is made
  • 1 avocado
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tin anchovies
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 bunches baby carrots
  • 1 bunch radishes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 150g snow peas
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Baby carrots, radishes, cucumber and snow peas on ice around a bright green goddess dip of avocado, herbs, lemon and Greek yoghurt. Blitz an avocado, 150g Greek yoghurt, a handful each of parsley, basil and chives, a garlic clove, 2 anchovies, the juice of a lemon and salt until smooth and green. Trim 2 bunches baby carrots and a bunch of radishes, cut a Lebanese cucumber into batons and trim 150g snow peas. Arrange the vegetables over a tray of crushed ice with the dip in the middle.

Mains

Mr Napkin Head's family pastaVegetarian

Spaghetti in a slow-simmered tomato and butter sauce with basil and parmesan.

Prep 8 min, cook 57 min · Make ahead: Make the tomato sauce up to 3 days ahead and reheat; cook the spaghetti on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g tins crushed tomatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 1 brown onion
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 100g parmesan, grated
  • salt, to taste
  1. Peel and halve the onion.
  2. Simmer the tomatoes with butter and onion, stirring now and then.
  3. Remove the onion and season.
  4. Cook the spaghetti.
  5. Toss with the sauce and finish with torn basil and plenty of parmesan.

Arthur's lemon thyme roast chicken

Chicken roasted with lemon, thyme and garlic.

How it is made
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 100g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 jar honey
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Whole chicken rubbed with lemon and thyme butter, roasted golden, with crisp roast potatoes, honey glazed carrots and gravy made from the pan. Mix 60g soft butter with lemon zest, chopped thyme and salt and push under the skin of a 1.8kg chicken. Put the halved lemon inside. Roast at 200°C for 75 minutes. Parboil 1kg potatoes, rough them up and roast in oil alongside for an hour. Glaze 500g carrots with butter and honey. Rest the chicken, then stir flour into the pan juices and whisk in 300ml stock for gravy.

Sweets

Sticky toffee puddingVegetarian

Warm date sponge soaked in hot toffee sauce.

Prep 12 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Bake the pudding up to 2 days ahead, covered; make the toffee sauce up to 3 days ahead and reheat both to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 200g pitted dates, chopped
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 1 pinch bicarb soda
  • 60g butter, for the pudding
  • 150g brown sugar, for the pudding
  • 2 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 100g butter, for sauce
  • 150g brown sugar, for sauce
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  1. Soak the dates in boiling water with bicarb soda.
  2. Cream the pudding butter and pudding brown sugar, add the eggs, dates and flour.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Simmer the butter and brown sugar for the sauce with the cream into a toffee sauce.
  5. Poke holes in the pudding and pour over the hot sauce.
  6. Serve with ice cream.

Drinks

Cottage mulled wine

Red wine warmed with orange, cinnamon, cloves, star anise and honey.

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Slice the orange and measure out the spices up to 1 day ahead; warm everything together on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml red wine
  • 1 orange
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 star anise
  • 2 tbsp honey
  1. Pour the wine into a pot with sliced orange, cinnamon, cloves, star anise and honey.
  2. Warm gently, never letting it boil.
  3. Ladle into mugs by the fire.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Amanda opens the door of the snowy Surrey cottage.
