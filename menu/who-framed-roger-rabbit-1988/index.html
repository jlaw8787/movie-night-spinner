Ink and Paint Club canapés

Piped egg halves, smoked salmon on rye squares, brown sugar glazed cocktail franks.

Prep 12 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 4 slices rye bread
  • 20g butter
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool, peel and halve.
  2. Whip the yolks with mayonnaise and pipe back into the whites.
  3. Butter the rye squares and top with smoked salmon.
  4. Glaze the franks in a pan with the sugar and a splash of water.
  5. Serve on silver trays.

Honey roasted carrots with whipped fetaVegetarian

Baby carrots roasted with honey, cumin and thyme, feta and yoghurt dip.

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches baby carrots
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  1. Toss the carrots with the honey, cumin, thyme and oil.
  2. Roast at 200°C until caramelised.
  3. Whip the feta with the yoghurt for the dip.

Toon-safe French onion dipOn the printed menu

Slow caramelised onion in sour cream and cream cheese with chives, crinkle-cut chips and pretzels.

How it is made
  • 3 brown onions
  • 30g butter
  • 250g sour cream
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 175g crinkle-cut chips
  • 150g pretzels
  1. Slow-caramelised onion folded through sour cream and cream cheese with chives and Worcestershire, with crinkle-cut chips and pretzels for scooping. Slice 3 brown onions thinly and cook in 30g butter over low heat for 40 minutes until deep brown and jammy, then cool and chop. Beat 250g sour cream with 100g cream cheese, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce and salt, then fold in the onion and chopped chives. Chill 30 minutes. Serve with a 175g bag crinkle-cut chips and 150g pretzels.

Valiant & Valiant pastrami on rye

Steamed pastrami, mustard and melted Swiss on rye, dill pickle on the side.

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Steam the pastrami up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; pile onto the rye and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pastrami
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 4 dill pickles
  1. Steam the pastrami until glossy.
  2. Pile onto rye spread with mustard and top with Swiss cheese.
  3. Grill until the cheese melts. Serve each with a dill pickle.

Red Car diner glazed meatloafOn the printed menu

Sticky tomato topped beef and pork sliced thick over mash, before Doom's freeway arrives.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 180ml milk
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 packet gravy mix
  1. Beef and pork meatloaf with a sticky tomato and brown sugar glaze, sliced thick over creamy mash with buttered green beans and gravy. Mix 500g beef mince, 250g pork mince, 1 grated onion, 1 egg, 60g breadcrumbs soaked in 80ml milk, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and salt. Press into a loaf tin and spread with 100ml tomato sauce mixed with 2 tbsp brown sugar. Bake at 180°C for 60 minutes and rest. Mash 1kg potatoes with 60g butter and 100ml milk. Steam 300g green beans. Serve with gravy.

Jessica Rabbit's red velvet cakeVegetarian

Red velvet layer cake with thick cream cheese frosting.

Prep 35 min, cook 30 min, then 40 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 350g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk and red colouring.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175°C.
  3. Cool the cakes completely.
  4. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar for the frosting and layer the cakes.
  5. Pour a glossy red icing over the top.

Eddie's ginger beer on the rocksVegetarianNo alcohol

Ginger beer over a big ice block with lime and a drop of vanilla, no alcohol.

Prep 9 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook; chill the ginger beer and tumblers up to 1 day ahead and pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 8 drops vanilla extract
  • 800ml ginger beer
  • large ice blocks, to serve
  1. Put one large ice block in each of four heavy tumblers.
  2. Squeeze lime juice into each and add the vanilla.
  3. Top with cold ginger beer, poured slowly. Stir once and add a lime wheel.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Jessica takes the stage.
