Ink and Paint Club canapés

Piped egg halves, smoked salmon on rye squares, brown sugar glazed cocktail franks

Prep 20 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 4 slices rye bread
  • 20g butter
  • 100g smoked salmon
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  1. Boil the eggs hard, cool, peel and halve.
  2. Whip the yolks with mayonnaise and pipe back into the whites.
  3. Butter the rye squares and top with smoked salmon.
  4. Glaze the franks in a pan with the sugar and a splash of water.
  5. Serve on silver trays.

Honey roasted carrots with whipped fetaVegetarian

Baby carrots roasted with honey, cumin and thyme, feta and yoghurt dip

Prep 13 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bunches baby carrots
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g feta
  • 100g Greek yoghurt
  1. Toss the carrots with the honey, cumin, thyme and oil.
  2. Roast at 200C until caramelised.
  3. Whip the feta with the yoghurt for the dip.

Mains

Valiant & Valiant pastrami on rye

Steamed pastrami, mustard and melted Swiss on rye, dill pickle on the side

Prep 8 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Steam the pastrami up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; pile onto the rye and grill on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g pastrami
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 2 tbsp American mustard
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 4 dill pickles
  1. Steam the pastrami until glossy.
  2. Pile onto rye spread with mustard and top with Swiss cheese.
  3. Grill until the cheese melts. Serve each with a dill pickle.

Jessica Rabbit's red velvet cakeVegetarian

Red velvet, cream cheese frosting. "I'm not bad. I'm just drawn that way."

Prep 35 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp red food colouring
  • 250g cream cheese
  • 350g icing sugar
  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then fold in the flour, cocoa, buttermilk and red colouring.
  2. Bake in two tins at 175C.
  3. Cool the cakes completely.
  4. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar for the frosting and layer the cakes.
  5. Pour a glossy red icing over the top.

Eddie's ginger beer on the rocksVegetarianNo alcohol

Ginger beer over a big ice block with lime and a drop of vanilla, no alcohol

Prep 9 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook; chill the ginger beer and tumblers up to 1 day ahead and pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 2 limes
  • 8 drops vanilla extract
  • 800ml ginger beer
  • large ice blocks, to serve
  1. Put one large ice block in each of four heavy tumblers.
  2. Squeeze lime juice into each and add the vanilla.
  3. Top with cold ginger beer, poured slowly. Stir once and add a lime wheel.

When to serve

Serve the drinks when Jessica takes the stage at the Ink and Paint Club.
