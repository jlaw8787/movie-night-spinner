Snacks

Harvest offering seed crackersVegetarian

Sunflower, pumpkin, sesame, flax and poppy seeds baked into crisp crackers.

Prep 10 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 60g sunflower seeds
  • 60g pepitas
  • 60g linseeds
  • 60g sesame seeds
  • 40g poppy seeds
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250ml water
  1. Mix the sunflower seeds, pepitas, linseeds, sesame seeds and poppy seeds with the salt and water.
  2. Rest the mixture.
  3. Spread thin on lined trays.
  4. Bake at 160°C until crunchy.
  5. Cool and break into shards.

Berry and feta skewersVegetarian

Blueberries, raspberries, feta and mint on toothpicks.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 125g blueberries
  • 125g raspberries
  • 150g feta
  • 12 mint leaves
  • 12 toothpicks
  1. Cut the feta into small cubes.
  2. Thread blueberries, raspberries, feta and a mint leaf onto toothpicks.
  3. Lay them out on a long platter in neat marching lines.

P.T. Flea's circus caramel popcorn

Kernels in crunchy brown sugar and butter toffee, baked glassy, sea salt, striped cones.

How it is made
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 100g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 tin golden syrup
  • 1 box bicarb soda
  • 1 jar sea salt flakes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Popcorn coated in a crunchy brown sugar and butter caramel, baked until glassy, sprinkled with sea salt and handed out in striped cones. Pop 100g popcorn kernels in 2 tbsp oil in a lidded pot and tip into a big roasting tin. Boil 100g butter, 200g brown sugar, 60ml golden syrup and a pinch of salt for 4 minutes, then stir in half a teaspoon of bicarb so it foams. Pour over the popcorn and toss well. Bake at 120°C for 45 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes, then spread out to cool and break apart. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Mains

Colony corn chowder

Bacon, potato and corn chowder finished with cream.

Prep 22 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 500g potatoes, diced
  • 800g corn kernels (2 cans)
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 L water
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Dice the bacon, onion and potatoes.
  2. Fry the bacon in the olive oil until crisp.
  3. Soften the onion.
  4. Add the potatoes, corn, thyme and water and simmer.
  5. Stir in the cream and blitz a little for body.
  6. Serve topped with chives.

Bug City chicken stir-fry noodles

Egg noodles wok-tossed with sliced thigh, capsicum and broccoli in oyster sauce, in takeaway boxes.

How it is made
  • 400g fresh egg noodles
  • 500g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 1 box cornflour
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bottle oyster sauce
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Egg noodles stir-fried with chicken, capsicum and broccoli in an oyster sauce, served in takeaway boxes. Soak 400g fresh egg noodles in hot water for 2 minutes and drain. Slice 500g chicken thigh and toss with 1 tbsp soy and 1 tsp cornflour. Stir-fry in a very hot wok with oil for 4 minutes and set aside. Stir-fry 1 sliced red capsicum, 1 head broccoli in small florets and 2 garlic cloves for 3 minutes with a splash of water. Add the noodles, chicken, 3 tbsp oyster sauce and 1 tbsp soy and toss for 2 minutes. Serve in takeaway boxes with spring onion.

Heimlich's butterfly cookiesVegetarian

Buttery cookies cut into butterflies, iced in bright colours.

Prep 30 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 150g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • food colouring, a few drops
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until creamy.
  2. Mix in the flour to a soft dough.
  3. Roll out and cut into butterfly shapes.
  4. Bake at 160°C until pale golden.
  5. Cool completely.
  6. Mix the icing sugar, water and colouring and ice in bright colours.

Dewdrop coolerNo alcohol

Cucumber, mint and lime blended, strained and topped with soda.

Prep 11 min · Make ahead: Blend and strain up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; add the ice and soda water just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml soda water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Blend the cucumber, mint, lime juice and sugar.
  2. Strain into a jug.
  3. Pour over ice and top with soda water.
