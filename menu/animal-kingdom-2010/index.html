Snacks

Melbourne souvlaki bites

Lemon garlic lamb grilled and wrapped in pita with tomato, onion and garlic yoghurt

Prep 25 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Marinate the lamb up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill and wrap just before serving, best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 600g lamb shoulder, cut in strips
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 3 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 pita breads, torn
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 clove of garlic, for the yoghurt
  • 1 box toothpicks
  1. Marinate the lamb in the lemon juice, garlic, oregano and oil.
  2. Mix the yoghurt with the garlic.
  3. Grill the lamb on high until charred.
  4. Wrap small pieces in warm pita with tomato, onion and garlic yoghurt.
  5. Pin with toothpicks.

Suburban dip and chipsVegetarian

Onion and sour cream dip. Smurf has a word for Leckie in the supermarket

Prep 15 min, cook 100 min · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 onions, thinly sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 125g cream cheese, softened
  • 1 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 large bag ridged potato chips
  1. Cook the onions in the butter over low heat until deep golden.
  2. Cool completely.
  3. Fold through the sour cream, cream cheese, chives and salt.
  4. Chill.
  5. Serve with a big bowl of chips.

Mains

Smurf's Melbourne chicken parma

Crumbed chicken breast under ham, tomato sauce and melted mozzarella

Prep 25 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts, halved horizontally
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 150ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 4 slices of ham
  • 120g mozzarella, grated
  • 600g hot chips
  • 1 bag of green salad leaves
  1. Crumb the chicken in flour, egg and panko.
  2. Shallow fry in the vegetable oil until golden and cooked through.
  3. Simmer the tomato with the garlic.
  4. Top with ham, tomato and mozzarella and grill until bubbling.
  5. Serve with hot chips and a green salad.

Acquittal party vanilla sliceVegetarian

Custard slice for the acquittal. The Codys pop the champagne

Prep 45 min, cook 200 min · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 750ml milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 80g cornflour
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 passionfruit, pulp
  1. Heat the oven to 200C. Bake the pastry flat between two trays until crisp.
  2. Whisk the milk, eggs, sugar and cornflour over heat until very thick.
  3. Spread the custard between the pastry sheets in a lined tin.
  4. Ice with icing sugar mixed with passionfruit pulp.
  5. Chill overnight and cut.

Leckie's lemon, lime and bittersVegetarian

Lime, lemon and bitters over ice, no alcohol. Have you worked out where you fit?

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Mix the cordial and lemon juice in a jug up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice, bitters and lemonade to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 80ml lime cordial
  • 1 lemon
  • 16 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1l cold lemonade
  • 4 cup ice
  • 4 pots of cold lager, optional
  1. Fill 4 pot glasses with ice.
  2. Add the cordial, lemon juice and bitters to each.
  3. Top with lemonade, stir once and add a lemon wedge. A pot of lager on the side for anyone after one.
