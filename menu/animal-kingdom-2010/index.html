Melbourne souvlaki bites

Lemon garlic lamb grilled and wrapped in pita with tomato, onion and garlic yoghurt.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate the lamb up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; grill and wrap just before serving, best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 600g lamb shoulder, cut in strips
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 3 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 pita breads, torn
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 150g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 clove of garlic, for the yoghurt
  • 1 box toothpicks
  1. Marinate the lamb in the lemon juice, garlic, oregano and oil.
  2. Mix the yoghurt with the garlic.
  3. Grill the lamb on high until charred.
  4. Wrap small pieces in warm pita with tomato, onion and garlic yoghurt.
  5. Pin with toothpicks.

Suburban dip and chipsVegetarian

Onion and sour cream dip. Picked up from the supermarket.

Prep 7 min, cook 40 min, then 75 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Dip up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 onions, thinly sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 300ml sour cream
  • 125g cream cheese, softened
  • 1 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 large bag ridged potato chips
  1. Cook the onions in the butter over low heat until deep golden.
  2. Cool completely.
  3. Fold through the sour cream, cream cheese, chives and salt.
  4. Chill.
  5. Serve with a big bowl of chips.

Deal or No Deal pork and fennel sausage rolls

Pork and fennel in pastry, on the couch with the telly on.

How it is made
  • 600g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 apple
  • 1 jar fennel seeds
  • 1 bag breadcrumbs
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 3 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1 packet sesame seeds
  • 1 jar tomato relish
  1. Pork mince with fennel seed, grated apple and herbs rolled in flaky puff, sesame on top, baked golden and served hot with tomato relish. Mix 600g pork mince with 1 grated onion, 1 grated apple, 2 tsp toasted crushed fennel seeds, 40g breadcrumbs, chopped parsley, salt and pepper. Halve 3 sheets puff pastry, pipe a log of filling along each strip, roll up and seal. Cut each into 4, brush with egg and sprinkle sesame seeds. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes until golden. Serve with tomato relish.

Smurf's Melbourne chicken parma

Crumbed chicken breast under ham, tomato sauce and melted mozzarella.

Prep 3 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts, halved horizontally
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 150ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 4 slices of ham
  • 120g mozzarella, grated
  • 600g hot chips
  • 1 bag of green salad leaves
  1. Crumb the chicken in flour, egg and panko.
  2. Shallow fry in the vegetable oil until golden and cooked through.
  3. Simmer the tomato with the garlic.
  4. Top with ham, tomato and mozzarella and grill until bubbling.
  5. Serve with hot chips and a green salad.

Roast lamb with mint sauce

Leg studded with garlic and rosemary, cooked pink, crisp potatoes and pumpkin, peas and proper gravy.

How it is made
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 600g pumpkin
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 400ml beef stock
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bottle malt vinegar
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Lamb leg studded with garlic and rosemary and roasted pink, with crisp potatoes and pumpkin, peas, a proper gravy and sharp homemade mint sauce. Heat the oven to 220°C. Stud a 2kg lamb leg with garlic slivers and rosemary, rub with oil and salt and roast 20 minutes, then at 180°C for 60 minutes. Roast 1kg potatoes and 600g pumpkin in oil alongside. Rest the lamb 20 minutes. Make gravy from the tray juices, 1 tbsp flour and 400ml stock. Pour 60ml boiling water over 2 tbsp sugar, stir in a cup of chopped mint and 80ml malt vinegar for the sauce. Cook 300g peas.

Acquittal party vanilla sliceVegetarian

Custard slice for the acquittal. The Codys pop the champagne.

Prep 15 min, cook 32 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry
  • 750ml milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 80g cornflour
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 passionfruit, pulp
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Bake the pastry flat between two trays until crisp.
  2. Whisk the milk, eggs, sugar and cornflour over heat until very thick.
  3. Spread the custard between the pastry sheets in a lined tin.
  4. Ice with icing sugar mixed with passionfruit pulp.
  5. Chill overnight and cut.

Leckie's lemon, lime and bitters

Lime, lemon and bitters over ice, no alcohol, for the drive home.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Mix the cordial and lemon juice in a jug up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice, bitters and lemonade to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 80ml lime cordial
  • 1 lemon
  • 16 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 L cold lemonade
  • 4 cups ice
  • 4 pots of cold lager, optional
  1. Fill 4 pot glasses with ice.
  2. Add the cordial, lemon juice and bitters to each.
  3. Top with lemonade, stir once and add a lemon wedge. A pot of lager on the side for anyone after one.
