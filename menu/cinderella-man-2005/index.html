Snacks

Paid-for salami and ricotta crostini

Toasted baguette with whipped lemon ricotta and salami.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Toast the baguette rounds up to 1 day ahead in an airtight tin and whip the ricotta up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to finish
  • 200g ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • 150g salami
  • 4 pickled onions
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • salt and cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Slice the baguette into 16 thin rounds and brush with olive oil.
  2. Toast at 200°C until golden, 6 min.
  3. Whip the ricotta with lemon zest, salt and a drizzle of olive oil until smooth.
  4. Spread on the toasts and top each with a folded slice of salami.
  5. Finish with a sliver of pickled onion, cracked pepper and parsley leaves.

Relief line bread and dripping

Thick white bread fried golden in beef dripping with salt and pepper.

Prep 2 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Best fried just before serving; it goes soft if it sits
Recipe for 4
  • 100g beef dripping
  • 8 slices of white bread
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • cracked black pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the dripping in a heavy pan.
  2. Fry the bread slowly until golden and crisp on both sides, 12 min.
  3. Drain and sprinkle with flaky salt and cracked pepper.

Ringside franks with caraway kraut

Sausages griddled blistered, steamed buns, yellow mustard and sauerkraut, for the fight crowd.

How it is made
  • 1 brown onion
  • 20g butter
  • 400g sauerkraut
  • 1 jar caraway seeds
  • 8 franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 bottle yellow mustard
  • 1 roll baking paper
  1. Franks simmered then griddled until blistered, in steamed buns with yellow mustard and sauerkraut warmed with caraway and onion, wrapped in baking paper. Fry a sliced onion in 20g butter until soft, add 400g drained sauerkraut and 1 tsp caraway seeds and warm through for 10 minutes. Simmer 8 franks in water for 5 minutes, then griddle until blistered. Steam 8 hot dog buns over the simmering water for 1 minute. Fill each bun with a frank, a line of yellow mustard and a spoon of the kraut. Wrap each in baking paper to serve.

Mains

Mae's stretch-it stew

Beef shin, carrots, potatoes and pearl barley simmered 3 hours.

Prep 27 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Better the next day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef shin, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 400g potatoes
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 1.2 L beef stock
  • 3 sprigs of thyme
  • 4 slices of white bread, to serve
  1. Brown the beef in batches, 10 min.
  2. Chop the onions, carrots and potatoes.
  3. Add the vegetables, barley, stock and thyme.
  4. Simmer until thick, 3 hr.
  5. Serve with bread.

Cinderella Man's rib eye with mashed potatoes and gravy

Thick rib eye with buttery mashed potatoes and gravy, a steak dinner worth fighting for.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 120g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  • 4 rib eye steaks
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 bag plain flour
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch mint
  1. Rib eye seared hard and basted in butter and thyme, rested, with buttery mashed potato, minted peas and a rich gravy made from the pan juices. Boil 1kg potatoes and mash with 80g butter and 100ml hot milk. Season 4 rib eye steaks and sear in a smoking pan for 3 minutes, then flip, add 40g butter and thyme and baste for 3 minutes. Rest 5 minutes. Stir 1 tbsp flour into the pan, whisk in 300ml beef stock and simmer until thick. Cook 300g frozen peas with butter and chopped mint. Serve together.

Bread puddingVegetarian

Day-old bread baked in cinnamon custard with golden raisins.

Prep 8 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Assemble and soak up to 1 day ahead in the fridge, then bake when needed; leftovers reheat well
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of day-old white bread
  • 20g butter
  • 80g sultanas
  • 3 eggs
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Tear the bread into a dish greased with the butter and scatter with sultanas.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, sugar and cinnamon and pour over.
  3. Soak for 20 minutes.
  4. Bake at 170°C, 40 min.

Drinks

Warm honey and nutmeg milkNo alcohol

Warm milk for the kids, with honey, cinnamon and vanilla.

Prep 5 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the milk, honey, cinnamon and vanilla up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled in the pan; warm, froth and grate the nutmeg to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 whole nutmeg, to grate
  1. Pour the milk into a saucepan with honey, cinnamon and vanilla.
  2. Warm gently, stirring, until steaming but not boiling, 8 min.
  3. Remove the cinnamon stick.
  4. Whisk hard until frothy.
  5. Pour into four mugs and grate fresh nutmeg over each.
