Snacks

Chimichanga bites

Fried tortilla rolls of shredded chipotle beef and cheddar.

Prep 20 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Beef up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef chuck
  • 2 chipotle chillies in adobo, chopped
  • 500ml water
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 12 small flour tortillas
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 150ml sour cream
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  1. Put the beef in a pot with the chipotle and water.
  2. Braise gently until it shreds easily, then shred, 3 hr.
  3. Roll small amounts of beef and cheddar in the tortillas, folding in the sides.
  4. Fry seam side down in the vegetable oil until crisp all over, 8 min.
  5. Serve with sour cream and tomato salsa.

Dopinder's taxi samosasVegetarian

Crisp fried triangles filled with spiced potato and peas.

Prep 17 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, uncooked and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g potatoes
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 12 spring roll pastry sheets
  • 1 L vegetable oil for frying
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  1. Boil and crush the potatoes with the peas and garam masala, then cool, 20 min.
  2. Cut the pastry into strips, spoon filling at one end and fold into triangles.
  3. Fry in the vegetable oil until golden and crisp, 5 min.
  4. Mix the tamarind with sugar and water to serve alongside.

Mains

Wade and Vanessa's steak frites

Seared sirloin in a red wine pan sauce, oven fries.

Prep 2 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Cut the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep them in cold water, then dry well before roasting; sear the steaks and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 50g butter
  • 150ml red wine
  • 250ml beef stock
  1. Cut the potatoes into chips, toss in oil and roast until crisp, 20 min.
  2. Sear the steaks in a very hot pan, add butter and baste for a minute, then rest, 8 min.
  3. Add the red wine and stock to the pan and reduce until glossy, 10 min.
  4. Slice the steak and serve by candlelight.

Firefist's flame-grilled peri peri chicken

Chicken grilled over flame in peri peri chilli and lemon, hot as a fire fist.

Prep 33 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken up to 1 day ahead in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 red chillies
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 60ml olive oil
  • 300g basmati rice
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 300g red cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  1. Blitz the chillies, garlic, juice of 1 lemon, paprika, vinegar, 3 tbsp of the oil and a pinch of salt. Toss with the chicken and leave for 20 minutes.
  2. Simmer the rice with the turmeric and 600ml water, covered, for 12 minutes, then rest for 5.
  3. Cook the chicken in a hot grill pan with the rest of the oil for 5 minutes a side until charred and cooked through.
  4. Shred the cabbage and grate the carrots, then toss with the mayonnaise and a squeeze of the other lemon.
  5. Slice the chicken and serve over the rice with the slaw.

Sweets

Red suit raspberry cheesecakeVegetarian

Baked cheesecake on a graham cracker base with raspberry.

Prep 35 min, cook 60 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sweet biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 500g cream cheese
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g raspberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar for the sauce
  1. Crush the biscuits with the butter and press into a lined tin.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar, then beat in the eggs and pour over the base.
  3. Bake at 160°C, then turn off the oven and cool inside, 50 min.
  4. Chill overnight.
  5. Simmer the raspberries with sugar, push through a sieve and spoon over the top, 10 min.

Drinks

Cable's cold brew espresso martini

Vodka, coffee liqueur and cold brew shaken until frothy.

Prep 7 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Chill the martini glasses in the freezer up to 1 day ahead; the cold brew keeps 3 days in the fridge, shake each drink to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml cold brew coffee
  • 160ml vodka
  • 80ml coffee liqueur
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 12 coffee beans
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Chill 4 martini glasses in the freezer, 30 min.
  2. For each drink, measure 60ml cold brew coffee, 40ml vodka, 20ml coffee liqueur and 10ml sugar syrup into a shaker.
  3. Shake each drink very hard with plenty of ice for 15 seconds so it foams, then strain into the glass.
  4. Float three coffee beans on top of each. Make it in batches of one or two.
