Garlic parmesan knotsVegetarian

Pizza dough knots brushed with garlic butter and parmesan, marinara.

Prep 28 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Shape and tie the knots up to 4 hours ahead and keep them covered in the fridge; bake and brush with garlic butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 60g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • 300ml marinara sauce
  1. Cut the dough into strips, roll each into a rope and tie into a knot.
  2. Lay on a lined tray and rest, 15 min.
  3. Bake at 220°C until golden, 12 min.
  4. Melt the butter with the garlic and parsley, brush over the hot knots and scatter with parmesan, 3 min.
  5. Warm the marinara and serve for dipping, 3 min.

Hallmark card cheese and tomato pinwheels

Puff pastry spirals with sundried tomato pesto, ham, basil and cheese.

Prep 12 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Roll and chill the log up to 1 day ahead, wrapped in the fridge; slice, glaze and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of puff pastry, thawed
  • 4 tbsp sundried tomato pesto
  • 8 slices of leg ham
  • 1 small bunch basil
  • 120g tasty cheese, grated
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Lay the pastry flat and spread with the pesto. Top with ham, basil and cheese.
  2. Roll up tightly and chill, 15 min.
  3. Cut each roll into slices and lay cut-side down on a lined tray. Brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden, 18 min.

Pepperoni mushroom pizza

Pepperoni, garlic mushroom and mozzarella pizza with chilli oil.

Prep 15 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Fry the mushrooms up to 1 day ahead and keep them chilled; stretch, top and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 750g pizza dough
  • 200ml pizza sauce
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g mozzarella, grated
  • 120g sliced pepperoni
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil, to drizzle
  1. Divide the dough in two and stretch each into a round on oiled trays. Spread with pizza sauce.
  2. Fry the mushrooms with the garlic in a little oil, 4 min.
  3. Top the bases with mozzarella, pepperoni and mushrooms.
  4. Bake at 240°C until blistered, 13 min.
  5. Scatter with oregano and drizzle with chilli oil.

Emilio's very, very sneaky baked ziti

Tubes baked with Italian sausage, sugo, ricotta and blistered mozzarella, served from behind you.

Prep 5 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g ziti or penne
  • 400g Italian pork sausages, skins off
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700g tomato passata
  • 250g ricotta
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 100g rocket
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Boil the pasta until just short of done and drain, 12 min.
  2. Brown the sausage in the oil, add the onion and garlic, then the passata, and simmer, 15 min.
  3. Toss the pasta with the sauce, layer into a dish with spoonfuls of ricotta and top with mozzarella and parmesan.
  4. Bake until bubbling and blistered. Serve with rocket dressed with oil, 25 min.

New York baked cheesecake with berriesVegetarian

Baked cheesecake on a graham cracker base with warm berry sauce.

Prep 18 min, cook 60 min, then 4.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g plain sweet biscuits
  • 80g butter, melted
  • 500g cream cheese, soft
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g frozen mixed berries
  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a lined 20cm springform tin. Chill, 10 min.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with most of the sugar, then the eggs, sour cream, lemon juice and vanilla.
  3. Pour over the base and bake at 150°C until just set, 55 min.
  4. Cool in the oven with the door ajar, 45 min.
  5. Chill until firm, 3 hr 30 min.
  6. Simmer the berries with the remaining sugar and spoon over, 5 min.

Cola float with whipped creamNo alcohol

Cola poured over vanilla ice cream, whipped cream and a cherry.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead and keep it chilled, and chill the glasses in the freezer; pour the cola just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 1.3 L cold cola
  • 100ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 4 cocktail cherries
  1. Chill tall glasses in the freezer, 15 min.
  2. Add ice cream to each glass.
  3. Slowly pour cola down the side until it foams to the top, letting it settle and topping up.
  4. Finish with whipped cream and a cherry. Serve with straws and long spoons.
