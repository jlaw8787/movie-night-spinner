Snacks

Golden ticket chocolate barsVegetarian

Dark and milk chocolate bars with flaky salt, wrapped with a gold ticket

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 1 box sea salt flakes
  • 1 roll gold foil
  1. Melt both chocolates gently and pour into bar moulds.
  2. Sprinkle with a little flaky salt and set in the fridge.
  3. Wrap each in paper, slipping a gold foil ticket under one wrapper.

Veruca's nut room cinnamon nutsVegetarian

Walnuts, pecans and almonds coated in cinnamon sugar and roasted

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 1 egg white
  • 100g walnuts
  • 100g pecans
  • 100g almonds
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Whisk the egg white until frothy and toss with the walnuts, pecans and almonds.
  2. Coat in the sugar, cinnamon and a pinch of salt.
  3. Roast at 160°C for 20 minutes, stirring twice.
  4. Cool and break apart.

Bucket family cabbage and bacon soupOn the printed menu

Savoy, carrot and potato in chicken stock with smoky lardons, richer than Charlie gets at home

How it is made
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1.5L chicken stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 savoy cabbage
  • 30g butter
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. A proper version: savoy cabbage, onion, carrot and potato simmered in chicken stock with smoky bacon and thyme, finished with butter, pepper and crusty bread. Fry 150g diced bacon in a large pot until golden. Add a diced onion and 2 diced carrots and soften 5 minutes. Add 2 diced potatoes, 1.5L chicken stock and thyme and simmer 15 minutes. Add half a savoy cabbage, shredded, and simmer 10 minutes more. Stir in 30g butter, season with plenty of pepper and serve with crusty bread.

Mains

Augustus Gloop's bratwurst and pretzels

Grilled bratwurst with apple caraway sauerkraut and warm pretzels

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Simmer the sauerkraut up to 2 days ahead and keep it chilled to rewarm; grill the bratwurst and warm the pretzels on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1 jar sauerkraut, 500g
  • 1 green apple
  • 125ml apple juice
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 8 bratwurst
  • 4 soft pretzels
  • 1 jar wholegrain mustard
  1. Simmer the sauerkraut with the grated apple, apple juice and caraway.
  2. Grill the bratwurst over medium heat, turning, until browned all over.
  3. Warm the pretzels at 180°C.
  4. Serve together with plenty of wholegrain mustard.

Three-course gum roast beef dinnerOn the printed menu

The gum that tastes of tomato soup and roast beef

How it is made
  • 1kg tomatoes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 4 baking potatoes
  • 1.2kg sirloin roast
  • 40g butter
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Little cups of roast tomato and basil soup to start, then sirloin roasted pink and sliced, with baked potatoes loaded with butter and sour cream. Roast 1kg halved tomatoes, an onion and 3 garlic cloves at 200°C for 35 minutes, then blitz with 300ml stock and basil for soup. Bake 4 potatoes at 200°C for 1 hour. Season a 1.2kg sirloin, sear all over and roast at 200°C to 52°C inside, about 30 minutes, then rest 15 minutes and slice. Split the potatoes and load with butter, sour cream and chives.

Blueberry pie and ice creamVegetarian

Lattice topped blueberry pie served with ice cream

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Bake the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen blueberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the blueberries with the sugar, lemon juice and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two sheets of pastry and fill.
  3. Weave a lattice from the other two sheets.
  4. Bake at 190°C.
  5. Cool before slicing, then serve with ice cream.

Waterfall hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Thick frothy hot chocolate made with milk, cream and two chocolates

Prep 8 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best whisked fresh; chop the chocolate up to 1 week ahead and keep it airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 800ml full cream milk
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 50g milk chocolate
  1. Heat the milk and cream until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chopped chocolate and keep whisking hard until thick and frothy like the waterfall.
  3. Pour into four mugs.
