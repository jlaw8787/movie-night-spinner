Snacks

Liquid heat chicken wings

Fiery hot wings, cooling ranch, celery. The Tri-Lambs' liquid heat payback.

Prep 5 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Make the ranch up to 2 days ahead; bake the wings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken wings, split
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 100ml hot sauce
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 50g butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 150ml ranch dressing
  • 4 celery sticks, cut in batons
  1. Dry the wings well and toss with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Bake on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp, 45 min.
  3. Melt the butter with the hot sauce, cayenne and honey, 5 min.
  4. Toss the wings in the sauce and serve with ranch and celery.

Party crasher pulled pork buns

Slow pulled pork, slaw, soft rolls.

Prep 19 min, cook 3 hr 40 min · Make ahead: Cook and pull the pork up to 2 days ahead and reheat in its juices; build to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5kg pork shoulder, boneless
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml smoky barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Rub the pork with the sugar, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Put it in a lidded pot with a cup of water and cook at 150°C until it falls apart, 3 hr 30 min.
  3. Shred the pork and stir through the barbecue sauce and half the vinegar.
  4. Toss the cabbage with mayo and the remaining vinegar.
  5. Pile the pork and slaw into the buns.

Lambda Lambda Lambda lamb gyros

Spiced lamb with tzatziki, tomato and onion in warm pita, a frat house feast.

Prep 17 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Marinate the lamb and make the tzatziki up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 800g lamb leg steaks, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, grated and squeezed
  • 8 pita breads
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 300g frozen chips
  1. Toss the lamb with two thirds of the garlic, the oregano, paprika, lemon juice, oil and salt.
  2. Mix the yoghurt with the cucumber, the remaining garlic and salt.
  3. Bake the chips until crisp, 20 min.
  4. Sear the lamb in batches in a very hot pan until charred at the edges, 8 min.
  5. Warm the pitas and wrap with lamb, tzatziki, tomato, onion and a few chips.

Sonoran hot dogs

Bacon-wrapped franks with pinto beans, salsa and jalapeños.

Prep 23 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Wrap the franks in bacon up to a day ahead and chill; cook to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 rashers of streaky bacon
  • 8 soft split-top bread rolls
  • 400g tin pinto beans, drained
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 1/4 cup pickled jalapenos
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Wrap each frankfurt tightly in a rasher of bacon.
  2. Cook in a hot pan, turning, until the bacon is crisp all over, 12 min.
  3. Warm the beans and the rolls, 5 min.
  4. Fill the rolls with the franks, then beans, tomato, onion, jalapenos, mustard and a stripe of mayo.

Tri-Lamb charity banana cream piesVegetarian

Eat a pie for charity, says Takashi. Biscuit crust, banana custard, whipped cream.

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the custard and crust up to a day ahead; fill and top on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain sweet biscuits, crushed
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 500ml milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 40g cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 bananas, sliced
  • 300ml thickened cream
  1. Mix the biscuit crumbs with the butter and press into 4 small pie tins, then chill, 10 min.
  2. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour, pour on the hot milk, then stir over heat until thick and add the vanilla, 15 min.
  3. Cool the custard with cling wrap on the surface, 5 min.
  4. Layer banana and custard in the crusts.
  5. Whip the cream and pile it high on top.

Omega Mu party punchNo alcohol

Pineapple, lime and dry ginger, lots of ice.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the juices and chill up to a day ahead; add the dry ginger to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L pineapple juice
  • 3 limes
  • 1.3 L dry ginger mixer
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 3 cups ice cubes
  1. Stir the pineapple juice and lime juice in a punch bowl.
  2. Add the orange slices and ice.
  3. Top with the dry ginger just before serving.
