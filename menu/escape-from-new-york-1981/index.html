Snacks

Wall Watch Jerky

Thin topside in soy, Worcestershire, smoked paprika and brown sugar, dried low until chewy.

Prep 0 min, cook 3.5 hours, then 13 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the beef until firm, then marinate it overnight in the fridge, so start the day before; dried jerky keeps 1 week in an airtight jar
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef topside
  • 80ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  1. Freeze the beef until firm.
  2. Slice 3mm thick across the grain and mix the marinade from the soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, smoked paprika, chilli flakes and garlic powder.
  3. Marinate the beef in the marinade overnight in the fridge.
  4. Pat dry, lay on racks over trays and dry at 75°C with the oven door slightly open until leathery but still bendy.

Tins and Saltines

Eating out of tins in a city with no shops.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 220g tinned sardines in oil
  • 24 saltine crackers
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  1. Drain the sardines and chop the parsley.
  2. Lay a sardine on each cracker, squeeze over lemon, add a drop of hot sauce and some parsley. Serve on the tin lids.

Drum-fire chilli corn fritters

Cooked over the drum fires on Manhattan's empty streets.

How it is made
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 125ml milk
  • 400g corn kernels
  • 3 spring onions
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 tin chilli powders in adobo
  • 150g sour cream
  • vegetable oil
  1. Crisp corn and spring onion fritters spiked with jalapeño, fried in spoonfuls and served with smoky chilli powder sour cream. Mix 150g self-raising flour, 2 eggs and 125ml milk into a thick batter. Fold in 400g corn kernels, 3 sliced spring onions, 1 chopped jalapeño and salt. Shallow-fry heaped tablespoons in 1cm hot oil for 2 minutes a side until golden, then drain. Stir 1 chopped chilli powder into 150g sour cream and serve alongside.

Mains

The Duke's Stew

Smoky sausage, potato, carrot, cabbage and beans simmered thick with paprika, served in tin mugs.

Prep 12 min, cook 38 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, reheat well
Recipe for 4
  • 500g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 600g potatoes, diced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 400g tinned cannellini beans
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Dice the vegetables and slice the sausage. Shred the cabbage.
  2. Brown the sausage in a big pot, then add the onion, carrots and potatoes and cook.
  3. Add the paprika, beans, tomatoes and stock and simmer, adding the cabbage for the last 10 minutes.
  4. Season and serve in mugs.

Duke of New York chilli

Chuck braised slow with chilli powder, cumin, tomato and kidney beans, fit for A Number One.

How it is made
  • beef chuck
  • chilli powder
  • ground cumin
  • crushed tomatoes
  • kidney beans
  • cornbread mix
  1. Beef chuck in chunks browned hard, braised 2.5 hours with onion, chilli powder, cumin, oregano, tomato and kidney beans. Cornbread on the side.

Sweets

Ration BarVegetarian

Chewy golden syrup oats under dark chocolate and flaky salt, call me Snake.

Prep 0 min, cook 35 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 250g rolled oats
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • flaky sea salt, to taste
  1. Melt the butter with the golden syrup and sugar, stir in the oats and salt, and press firmly into a lined 20cm square tin.
  2. Bake at 180°C until golden.
  3. Melt the chocolate, spread over the warm slice and sprinkle with flaky salt.
  4. Cool, then chill until firm. Cut into 16 bars.

Drinks

White Lightning

Clear corn whiskey shaken with lemon and sugar, in jam jars, for the bridge run.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the base up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml corn or white whiskey
  • 3 lemons
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Juice the lemons and stir the sugar into the juice with a little warm water.
  2. Shake the whiskey with the lemon mix and ice, then strain into 4 ice-filled jam jars. Top up with lemonade and lemon for anyone not drinking.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the Duke and his followers confront Snake in the street.
