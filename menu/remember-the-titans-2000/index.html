Snacks

Gettysburg training camp hoagies

Ham, salami and provolone sub with oregano vinaigrette and pickled peppers

Prep 23 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep wrapped and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 long baguettes
  • 150g leg ham
  • 100g salami
  • 100g provolone
  • 1/4 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 jar of pickled peppers
  1. Whisk the olive oil, red wine vinegar, oregano and pepper into a dressing.
  2. Split the baguettes and drizzle the insides with half the dressing.
  3. Layer with sliced ham, salami and provolone.
  4. Top with shredded iceberg, sliced tomato, thin red onion and pickled peppers, then the rest of the dressing.
  5. Press closed and wrap tightly.
  6. Rest wrapped for 10 minutes.
  7. Cut each baguette into pieces.

Titans game day chilli dogs

Franks in toasted buns with beef chilli and melted cheddar

Prep 15 min, cook 26 min · Make ahead: Chilli up to 2 days ahead, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 2 onions
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 200g tomato passata
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 120g cheddar
  • 1 bottle of American mustard
  1. Dice the onions.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion.
  3. Add the cumin, paprika, chilli powder, passata and water and simmer until thick.
  4. Grill the frankfurts.
  5. Toast the hot dog buns.
  6. Fill each with a frankfurt, spoon over the chilli, scatter with grated cheddar and grill to melt.
  7. Top with diced white onion and mustard.

Mains

Coach Boone's Virginia fried chicken

Buttermilk fried chicken with pepper cream gravy. Boone's camp rule: perfect every day.

Prep 33 min, cook 35 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg chicken pieces
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 1 bottle of hot sauce
  • 230g plain flour
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 90g butter
  • 500ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1l vegetable oil
  1. Combine the chicken, buttermilk, salt and hot sauce in a bowl.
  2. Soak in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  3. Toss the chicken in flour mixed with paprika, garlic powder and salt.
  4. Fry in the vegetable oil at 165°C in batches until golden and cooked through.
  5. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  6. Boil the potatoes until tender and mash with butter and milk.
  7. Make gravy with butter, flour, milk and lots of pepper.
  8. Warm the honey with chilli flakes to drizzle over the chicken.

Sunshine's California fish tacos

Chilli lime grilled fillets in corn tortillas, slaw, avocado and lime crema, Huntington Beach style

Prep 13 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Make the slaw and crema up to 4 hours ahead; cook the fish just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 600g firm white fish fillets
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 limes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 300g white cabbage
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 avocados
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jalapeño
  1. Rub the fish with the chilli, cumin, the juice of 1 lime, the oil and a pinch of salt.
  2. Shred the cabbage and toss with the juice of 1 lime, chopped coriander and sliced jalapeño.
  3. Mix the sour cream with the zest and juice of the last lime.
  4. Cook the fish in a hot grill pan for 3 minutes a side, then break into chunks.
  5. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan and fill with fish, slaw, sliced avocado and crema.

Sweets

Alexandria peach cobblerVegetarian

Cinnamon peaches under a buttermilk biscuit topping, ice cream

Prep 20 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sliced peaches (820g tins)
  • 60g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 lemon
  • 80g butter
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  1. Drain the peaches and toss with brown sugar, cinnamon, cornflour and lemon juice.
  2. Tip into a baking dish.
  3. Rub the cold butter into the flour and sugar, then stir in the buttermilk.
  4. Drop spoonfuls over the peaches and sprinkle with sugar mixed with cinnamon.
  5. Bake at 190°C until golden.
  6. Cool slightly and serve warm with ice cream.

Drinks

Sunshine's sweet tea lemonadeVegetarianNo alcohol

Sweet black tea mixed with fresh lemonade and mint

Prep 13 min, cook 5 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 160g caster sugar
  • 6 lemons
  • 1 bunch of mint
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Steep the tea bags in boiling water with the sugar.
  2. Remove the bags and chill until cold.
  3. Squeeze the lemons and stir the sugar into the lemon juice and cold water until dissolved.
  4. Fill tall glasses with ice, pour in the tea to halfway and top with the lemonade.
  5. Garnish with lemon wheels and mint.

When to serve

Serve the main after the 3am Gettysburg run.
