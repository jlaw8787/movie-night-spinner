Moscow cabbage pirozhkiVegetarian

Soft baked buns filled with buttery cabbage, onion, egg and dill.

Prep 10 min, cook 45 min, 1 hr 20 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; the baked buns keep 1 day airtight and reheat well, or freeze them for a month
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250ml milk, warmed
  • 60g butter, melted, for the dough
  • 1 egg, for the dough
  • 2 eggs, for the filling
  • 500g white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 40g butter, for the filling
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and 1 tsp of the salt with the warm milk, melted butter and egg for the dough, and knead until smooth, 12 min.
  2. Cover and prove until doubled. Meanwhile boil the eggs for the filling for 9 minutes, and cook the cabbage and onion slowly in the butter until soft and sweet.
  3. Season the cabbage with the rest of the salt and the pepper, cool a little, then mix in the chopped eggs and dill, 10 min.
  4. Divide the dough into 16, flatten each piece, fill, pinch closed and set seam side down on lined trays, 20 min.
  5. Rest the buns while the oven heats to 200°C, 15 min.
  6. Brush with the beaten egg and bake until golden, 18 min.

Train carriage herring on black bread

Pickled herring on buttered rye with red onion and dill, a cold bite for the siding.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Slice the onion and pick the dill up to 2 hours ahead; build just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices of dark rye bread
  • 40g butter
  • 300g pickled herring fillets
  • 1 red onion, in thin rings
  • 1/2 bunch dill
  1. Butter the rye and cut each slice in half.
  2. Top with herring, onion rings and dill, grind over pepper and serve cold.

Kremlin chicken Kiev with buttered peas

Crumbed chicken breast round a core of garlic butter, with buttered peas, cut at the table.

Prep 10 min, cook 26 min, 35 min waiting · Make ahead: Make the garlic butter up to 3 days ahead; crumb the chicken up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g soft butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/2 bunch flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 50g plain flour
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 150g dried breadcrumbs
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 500g frozen peas
  • 20g butter, for the peas
  1. Mash the butter with garlic and parsley, roll into a log and freeze, 20 min.
  2. Butterfly and pound the chicken thin, wrap each round a quarter of the butter and chill, 15 min.
  3. Dip in flour, egg and crumbs twice.
  4. Fry until golden, then bake at 200°C, 15 min.
  5. Cook the peas and toss with butter. Serve with the chicken, 6 min.

Benji's Kremlin mushroom stroganoffVegetarian

Mushrooms in paprika sour cream over egg noodles, for Benji and Ethan walking into the Kremlin.

Prep 12 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g mixed mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 100g dill pickles
  1. Brown the sliced mushrooms in half the butter in batches and lift out, 10 min.
  2. Fry the sliced onion and garlic in the rest of the butter for 5 minutes, then stir in the paprika and flour.
  3. Add the stock and mustard, put the mushrooms back and simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in the sour cream off the heat.
  4. Cook the noodles, drain and toss with a little butter, 10 min.
  5. Serve over the noodles with dill and pickles on the side.

Siding syrniki with sour cream and jamVegetarian

Golden cottage cheese pancakes with sour cream and cherry jam, still warm from the pan.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Mix the batter up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 150g plain flour
  • 40g butter
  • 200g sour cream
  • 4 tbsp cherry jam
  1. Mix the cottage cheese, eggs, sugar, salt and 100g of the flour.
  2. Shape 12 small thick rounds in the rest of the flour, 8 min.
  3. Fry in butter until golden, 3 minutes a side, 15 min in batches.
  4. Serve warm with sour cream and jam.

Samovar tea with lemon and jamNo alcohol

Strong black tea poured hot over lemon, a spoon of jam on the side to stir in.

Prep 3 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Slice the lemons up to 2 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tsp strong black tea leaves
  • 1 L boiling water
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 12 sugar cubes
  • 4 tbsp cherry jam
  1. Brew the tea in the boiling water, 4 min, and strain.
  2. Pour over a lemon slice in each glass and serve with sugar and jam.
