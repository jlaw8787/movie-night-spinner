Moscow cabbage pirozhkiVegetarian

Warm from a Moscow street stall after the prison break

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, then 95 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; the baked buns keep 1 day airtight and reheat well, or freeze them for a month
Recipe for 4
  • 500g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250ml milk, warmed
  • 60g butter, melted, for the dough
  • 1 egg, for the dough
  • 2 eggs, for the filling
  • 500g white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 40g butter, for the filling
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and 1 tsp of the salt with the warm milk, melted butter and egg for the dough, and knead until smooth.
  2. Cover and prove until doubled. Meanwhile boil the eggs for the filling for 9 minutes, and cook the cabbage and onion slowly in the butter until soft and sweet.
  3. Season the cabbage with the rest of the salt and the pepper, cool a little, then mix in the chopped eggs and dill.
  4. Divide the dough into 16, flatten each piece, fill, pinch closed and set seam side down on lined trays.
  5. Rest the buns while the oven heats to 200C.
  6. Brush with the beaten egg and bake until golden.

Brij Nath's party onion bhajisVegetarian

Shredded rings in spiced chickpea batter, fried crisp, mint yoghurt, while Jane works the room

Prep 22 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the yoghurt dip up to 1 day ahead; slice the onions in the morning but fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150g chickpea flour
  • 2 tbsp rice flour
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup coriander, chopped, for the bhajis
  • 100ml water
  • 200g Greek yoghurt
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves, for the dip
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Toss the onions with the salt and leave to soften.
  2. Stir in the chickpea flour, rice flour, cumin, turmeric, chilli powder, green chilli and coriander, then add the water to make a thick, sticky batter.
  3. Blend the yoghurt with the mint, coriander for the dip and lime juice.
  4. Heat the oil to 175C in a deep pan.
  5. Fry heaped spoonfuls of batter in batches until deep golden and crisp.
  6. Drain and serve hot with the yoghurt.

Dubai lamb machboos

A Dubai dinner from the night of the sandstorm

Prep 25 min, cook 105 min · Make ahead: Cook the lamb in its broth up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; cook the rice and fry the topping on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g basmati rice
  • 1kg boneless lamb shoulder, in 5cm pieces
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 onions, sliced, one kept for the topping
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 6 cardamom pods, cracked
  • 2 dried limes, pierced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2l water
  • 50g slivered almonds
  • 50g sultanas
  • 1/2 cup coriander leaves
  1. Rinse the rice and leave it to soak.
  2. Brown the lamb in batches in 2 tbsp of the oil.
  3. Add two of the onions and cook until soft.
  4. Stir in the garlic, ginger, spices, dried limes, tomatoes, tomato paste and salt.
  5. Add the water, cover and simmer until the lamb is tender.
  6. Lift out the lamb and keep warm. Measure 750ml of the broth back into the pot, add the drained rice, cover and cook on low.
  7. Rest the rice off the heat. Meanwhile fry the last onion in the rest of the oil until deep brown, adding the almonds and sultanas at the end.
  8. Pile the rice on a platter and top with the lamb, onions, almonds, sultanas and coriander.

Benji's Kremlin mushroom stroganoffVegetarian

Benji and Ethan walking into the Kremlin behind a fake hallway screen

Prep 12 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Slice the mushrooms up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 800g mixed mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 250ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 200g sour cream
  • 400g egg noodles
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 100g dill pickles
  1. Brown the sliced mushrooms in half the butter in batches and lift out.
  2. Fry the sliced onion and garlic in the rest of the butter for 5 minutes, then stir in the paprika and flour.
  3. Add the stock and mustard, put the mushrooms back and simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in the sour cream off the heat.
  4. Cook the noodles, drain and toss with a little butter.
  5. Serve over the noodles with dill and pickles on the side.

Dubai sandstorm luqaimatVegetarian

Sweet dumplings for the drive out of the sandstorm

Prep 15 min, cook 30 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best fried and eaten the same night; the batter can prove slowly in the fridge for up to 8 hours
Recipe for 4
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads
  • 2 tbsp milk, warmed
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125ml date syrup
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
  1. Soak the saffron in the warm milk.
  2. Whisk the flour, cornflour, yeast, sugar, cardamom and salt with the saffron milk and warm water into a thick, smooth batter.
  3. Cover and leave until bubbly and doubled.
  4. Heat the oil to 175C in a deep pan.
  5. Drop in small spoonfuls of batter in batches and fry, turning, until deep golden.
  6. Drain, toss with the warmed date syrup and scatter with sesame seeds.

Mumbai party mango gin coolerVegetarian

Gin shaken with fruit purée, lime and cardamom syrup, topped with soda

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the cardamom syrup up to 1 week ahead and blend the mango up to 1 day ahead, both in the fridge; shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 large mango, flesh only
  • 240ml gin
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 cup ice
  • 400ml soda water, chilled
  • 1 lime, cut into wheels
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and cardamom until dissolved, then cool and strain.
  2. Blend the mango until smooth.
  3. Shake the gin, mango, lime juice and syrup hard with ice, in two batches.
  4. Strain into four tall glasses of ice, top with soda and garnish with lime wheels.
