Snacks

Tony's garlic breadVegetarian

Italian loaf spread with butter, parsley and parmesan, baked till crisp at the edges

Prep 10 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Spread up to 1 day ahead, bake just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 loaf Italian bread
  • 100g butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  1. Mash the butter with the garlic, parsley and parmesan.
  2. Split the loaf and spread thickly.
  3. Bake at 200°C until crisp at the edges, then cut into thick slices and serve in the alley behind the restaurant.

Antipasto for two

Salami and provolone folded on a board, olives, artichoke, roasted capsicum and torn bread

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 150g salami, sliced
  • 150g provolone, sliced
  • 200g kalamata olives
  • 1 jar marinated artichoke hearts
  • 2 red capsicums, roasted and cut into strips
  • 1 loaf Italian bread
  1. Fold the salami and provolone onto a board.
  2. Add the olives, artichoke hearts and strips of roasted capsicum.
  3. Tear the bread and set it beside the board. Serve by candlelight.

Mains

Spaghetti and meatballs

Beef and pork balls with parmesan, simmered in slow tomato and basil, on one big plate

Prep 23 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Sauce and meatballs up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 300g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 60g parmesan, grated, plus extra to serve
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • 800g crushed tomatoes (2 cans)
  • 1 small bunch basil, torn
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Mix both minces with the parmesan, parsley and egg and roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the oil in batches.
  3. Add the tomatoes and basil and simmer gently until the sauce is thick and the balls are cooked through.
  4. Cook the spaghetti until al dente and pile on one big plate with the sauce. Leave the last meatball for your date.

Tramp's back door chicken with salsa verdeOn the printed menu

Roast chicken thighs with salsa verde, the kind Tramp begs for on his rounds

How it is made
  • 1kg bone-in chicken thighs
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1kg baby potatoes
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 jar capers
  1. Lemon, garlic and oregano chicken thighs roasted crisp-skinned, with smashed potatoes and a sharp caper salsa verde, the pick of the back-door rounds. Toss 1kg bone-in chicken thighs with the zest and juice of 1 lemon, 4 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp dried oregano, olive oil and salt. Roast skin up at 200°C for 40 minutes until crisp. Boil 1kg baby potatoes 15 minutes, smash flat and roast alongside in oil for 30 minutes. Chop a bunch of parsley, a handful of basil, 1 tbsp capers and 1 garlic clove, and mix with lemon juice and 80ml olive oil. Spoon over the chicken.

Sweets

Candlelit cannoliVegetarian

Ricotta cannoli with chocolate chips and pistachio, for the end of Bella Notte

Prep 20 min, then 12 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Ricotta drained a day ahead, fill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ricotta
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 50g dark chocolate chips
  • 12 cannoli shells
  • 100g pistachios, crushed
  1. Tip the ricotta into a sieve over a bowl.
  2. Drain overnight in the fridge (included in cook time).
  3. Beat the ricotta with the icing sugar and orange zest, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Spoon into a piping bag, fill the shells just before serving, and dip each end in pistachios.

Jim Dear's doughnuts with coffee caramelOn the printed menu

Doughnuts with coffee caramel, hatbox size. Jim Dear's Christmas surprise

How it is made
  • 400g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 90g butter
  • 200ml milk
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 jar ground cinnamon
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 jar instant coffee
  1. Hot cinnamon sugar doughnuts made for dunking, with a silky coffee caramel dip, the breakfast Lady sits begging beside. Mix 400g plain flour, 7g dried yeast, 50g sugar, 1 egg, 40g soft butter and 200ml warm milk into a dough, knead 8 minutes and prove 1 hour. Roll 1cm thick, cut 12 rings and prove 20 minutes. Fry at 175°C for 1 minute a side and toss in 100g sugar with 2 tsp cinnamon. For the dip, melt 150g brown sugar with 50g butter, add 150ml cream and 1 tsp instant coffee and simmer 3 minutes.

Tony's blood orange sodaVegetarianNo alcohol

Blood orange juice and rosemary syrup topped with soda. Joe and Tony serenade the table

Prep 17 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 80ml water
  • 2 rosemary sprigs, plus small sprigs to serve
  • 500ml blood orange juice
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1l soda water
  • 1 blood orange, sliced
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and rosemary for 3 minutes.
  2. Cool and strain.
  3. Mix the blood orange juice, syrup and lemon juice in a jug.
  4. Fill glasses with ice, pour in the juice to halfway, top with soda water and garnish with an orange slice and a rosemary sprig.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Tony brings out the spaghetti at the back of the restaurant.
