Snacks

Polar Express turkey and cranberry rolls

Warm rolls with roast turkey, brie, cranberry sauce and spinach

Prep 20 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 1 day ahead, warm to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 soft dinner rolls
  • 8 tbsp cranberry sauce
  • 300g roast turkey, sliced
  • 150g brie, sliced
  • 1 handful of baby spinach
  • 8 paper tickets, hole punched
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Split the rolls and spread the bases with cranberry sauce.
  2. Layer with turkey, brie and spinach and close.
  3. Wrap each in foil and warm until the cheese softens.
  4. Unwrap and rewrap in baking paper with a paper ticket tucked in.

Mains

North Pole chicken pot pie

Creamy chicken and vegetable pie under golden puff pastry

Prep 25 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 sheet of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in the oil.
  2. Add the onion, carrots and celery and soften.
  3. Stir in the flour, cream, thyme and peas and simmer until thick.
  4. Pour into a pie dish, top with pastry and egg wash.
  5. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden.

Sweets

Golden ticket shortbreadVegetarian

Buttery shortbread cut like tickets with gold sprinkles. Mind the wind.

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g butter, softened
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 350g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp gold sprinkles
  1. Heat the oven to 160C and line trays. Beat the butter and icing sugar, then mix in the flour to a soft dough.
  2. Roll out and cut into ticket rectangles.
  3. Punch a hole in each with a straw.
  4. Bake until pale golden.
  5. Sprinkle with caster sugar and gold sprinkles while warm.

Sleigh bell sugar cookiesVegetarian

Bell-shaped sugar cookies with white icing, silver pearls and a red ribbon

Prep 40 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 days, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp silver cachous
  • 1 drop red food colouring
  1. Heat the oven to 180C. Beat the butter and sugar, then mix in the flour.
  2. Chill, roll and cut into bell shapes.
  3. Bake and cool.
  4. Ice white with icing sugar and water.
  5. Press in cachous and pipe a red ribbon with tinted icing.

Drinks

The hot chocolateVegetarianNo alcohol

Dark and milk chocolate melted into milk and cream, poured high so it froths

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml milk
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 100g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 100g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 4 tbsp whipped cream
  1. Heat the milk and cream until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate until thick and smooth.
  3. Pour from a height into mugs so it froths.
  4. Top each with whipped cream.

When to serve

Serve the hot chocolate when the waiters dance down the carriage.
