Wonder Boy's Thebes spanakopitaVegetarian

Filo triangles filled with spinach, feta and dill

Prep 40 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Fill and fold the triangles up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge, or freeze up to 1 month; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g spinach
  • 200g feta
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 egg
  • 6 sheets filo pastry
  • 60g butter, melted
  1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Mix with the feta, dill, spring onions and egg.
  3. Cut the filo into strips, brush with butter, add a spoon of filling and fold into triangles.
  4. Bake at 190°C for 25 minutes.

Hydra heads haloumiVegetarian

Griddled haloumi, honey, chilli, thyme. Cut one, get two

Prep 5 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Best griddled just before serving; slice the haloumi up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g haloumi, in thick slices
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 lemon
  1. Griddle the haloumi in a hot dry pan for 2 minutes a side until golden.
  2. Drizzle with honey, sprinkle with chilli flakes and thyme, and squeeze over lemon.
  3. Cook more as fast as it disappears.

Phil's lamb keftedes with tzatzikiOn the printed menu

Minted mince balls with feta, pan-fried crusty, lemon to squeeze, zero to hero

How it is made
  • 500g lamb mince
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bag breadcrumbs
  • 60g feta
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Little lamb meatballs with mint, oregano and feta, pan-fried until crusty and served on skewers with cool garlic tzatziki and lemon. Mix 500g lamb mince with 1 grated onion, 1 egg, 40g fresh breadcrumbs, 60g crumbled feta, chopped mint, 1 tsp dried oregano and salt. Roll into 24 balls and fry in olive oil for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through. Grate 1 Lebanese cucumber, squeeze dry and mix with 250g Greek yoghurt, 1 grated garlic clove, lemon and dill. Serve the keftedes on skewers with the tzatziki.

Phil's training island souvlaki

Lemon oregano lamb skewers with cucumber garlic yoghurt

Prep 8 min, cook 12 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Lamb can marinate up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg lamb leg, diced
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 4 garlic cloves, 3 for the marinade and 1 for the sauce
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, grated
  • 4 pita breads
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  1. Marinate the lamb in the lemon juice, garlic, oregano and oil.
  2. Leave to marinate for 2 hours.
  3. Thread onto skewers and grill hot.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with the grated cucumber and garlic.
  5. Serve in warm pita with tomato and red onion.

Hades's fiery chilli garlic prawnsOn the printed menu

Chilli prawns. Whoa, is my hair out?

How it is made
  • 800g peeled green king prawns
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 150g feta
  • 300g orzo
  • 1 lemon
  • 30g butter
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  1. King prawns baked in a bubbling chilli, garlic and tomato sauce with crumbled feta, served with crusty bread and a lemony orzo. Heat the oven to 200°C. Soften 1 diced onion and 4 sliced garlic cloves in olive oil, add 1 tsp chilli flakes and 1 chopped red chilli, then 1 tin crushed tomatoes and oregano and simmer 10 minutes. Stir in 800g peeled green king prawns, tip into a baking dish, crumble 150g feta over and bake 10 minutes. Cook 300g orzo and toss with lemon zest, butter and parsley. Serve with crusty bread.

Ambrosia loukoumades (honey puffs)Vegetarian

Fried yeast dough puffs tossed in warm honey and cinnamon

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the batter up to 2 hours ahead and leave to rest, covered; fry and toss in warm honey just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g honey, warmed
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 60g walnuts, chopped
  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and water to a thick batter.
  2. Rest the batter for an hour.
  3. Drop spoonfuls into 175°C oil and fry until puffed and golden.
  4. Toss in warm honey with cinnamon and scatter over the walnuts.

Olympus golden peach and honey fizzVegetarianNo alcohol

Peach nectar, honey, lemon and orange blossom water over ice

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Mix the nectar, honey water, lemon juice and orange blossom water up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; add ice and sparkling water to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp warm water
  • 600ml peach nectar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1/2 tsp orange blossom water
  • 1 ripe peach, sliced
  • 500ml chilled sparkling water
  • 2 cups ice
  • 4 sprigs mint
  1. Stir the honey into the warm water until dissolved.
  2. In a jug mix the peach nectar, honey water, lemon juice and orange blossom water.
  3. Fill glasses with ice and peach slices, pour in the nectar to two thirds and top with sparkling water. Add a mint sprig.
