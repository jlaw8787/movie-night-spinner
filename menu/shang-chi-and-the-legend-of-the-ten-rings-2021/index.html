Snacks

Katy's family chicken congee

Jasmine rice porridge, ginger, chicken and fried shallots. Shaun eats here daily.

Prep 5 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats with extra stock
Recipe for 4
  • 200g jasmine rice
  • 2l chicken stock
  • 1 5cm piece ginger, sliced
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 4 tbsp crispy fried shallots
  1. Rinse the rice and put in a big pot with the stock and ginger.
  2. Bring to the boil, then simmer gently, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  3. Poach the chicken breasts in the congee, then shred.
  4. Ladle into bowls and top with chicken, spring onion, soy, sesame oil and fried shallots.

Bus fight sticky pork bao

Hoisin honey roast pork in steamed buns with cucumber, spring onion and peanuts

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Pork can marinate up to 1 day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g pork scotch fillet
  • 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 12 frozen bao buns
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, cut into sticks
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 50g roasted peanuts, crushed
  1. Mix the hoisin, honey, soy and garlic and coat the pork.
  2. Marinate in the fridge.
  3. Roast at 200C, basting with the marinade twice, until sticky and charred.
  4. Rest and slice thinly, and steam the bao buns.
  5. Fill with pork, cucumber, spring onion and peanuts.

Mains

Ta Lo steamed ginger snapper

Whole snapper steamed with ginger and spring onion, soy and sizzling oil, rice and bok choy

Prep 13 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Score the snapper and slice the ginger and spring onions up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; steam and finish with the hot oil to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg whole snapper, scaled and gutted
  • 1 5cm piece ginger, sliced
  • 4 spring onions
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 1 bunch bok choy
  1. Score the snapper and lay over the ginger and half the spring onion on a plate.
  2. Steam until the flesh is opaque at the bone.
  3. Cook the rice and steam the bok choy.
  4. Top the fish with fresh spring onion, pour over the soy, then smoking hot oil.

Sweets

Dragon scale mango pudding

Dragon scales, like the armour they make in Ta Lo.

Prep 20 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 mangoes
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 4 gelatine sheets
  • 375ml evaporated milk
  1. Blitz two mangoes with the sugar into a puree.
  2. Soak the gelatine, dissolve in a little warm puree and stir through the rest with the evaporated milk.
  3. Pour into glasses and set in the fridge.
  4. Top with thin mango slices laid like scales.

Drinks

Katy's brown sugar boba milk teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Black milk tea with chewy tapioca pearls in brown sugar syrup

Prep 5 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Pearls up to 4 hours; assemble fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g tapioca pearls
  • 100g dark brown sugar
  • 60ml water
  • 3 black tea bags
  • 300ml boiling water
  • 600ml cold milk
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Cook the pearls as the pack says and drain.
  2. Simmer the brown sugar with water, add the pearls and cook until sticky.
  3. Brew the tea in the boiling water and chill.
  4. Spoon the pearls and syrup into glasses, swirl up the sides, add ice and pour over the milk mixed with the tea.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Shang-Chi and Katy have breakfast with her family.
