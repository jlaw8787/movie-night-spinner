Snacks

Bocconcini snowman skewersVegetarian

Mozzarella balls with tomato hats and basil scarves, drizzled with olive oil

Prep 25 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g bocconcini
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  1. Crack the peppercorns and halve the cherry tomatoes.
  2. Stack three bocconcini on each skewer and top with a half tomato as a hat.
  3. Press in peppercorn eyes, tie a basil leaf as a scarf and drizzle with olive oil.

Mains

Turkey done right

Brined turkey breast rolled with sage butter, stuffing, roast potatoes, gravy

Prep 20 min, cook 718 min · Make ahead: Brine the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg turkey breast
  • 60g butter
  • 1 bunch sage
  • 1 packet stuffing mix
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml chicken stock
  1. Brine the turkey overnight in salted water in the fridge.
  2. Mix the butter with chopped sage, spread over the turkey and roll. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  3. Roast the turkey at 170C until 70C inside, with the potatoes in the tray for the last hour.
  4. Rest the turkey for 20 minutes. Bake the stuffing alongside as the packet says.
  5. Make gravy with the pan juices and stock.

Sweets

Santa's milk and cookiesVegetarian

Brown butter choc chip cookies, no nuts. Scott was lactose intolerant.

Prep 11 min, cook 503 min · Make ahead: Dough up to 2 days, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g choc chips
  • 1l full cream milk
  1. Brown the butter in a pan and leave to cool.
  2. Beat with the sugar and egg, then mix in the flour, bicarb and choc chips.
  3. Rest the dough overnight in the fridge.
  4. Shape into 16 cookies and bake at 180C until golden at the edges.
  5. Serve with cold milk.

North Pole gingerbreadVegetarian

Spiced gingerbread cut into elf shapes

Prep 40 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 5 days in a tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g icing sugar
  • red and green food colouring, a few drops of each
  1. Melt the butter, sugar and golden syrup, then stir in the flour, ground ginger, cinnamon and bicarb.
  2. Chill the dough.
  3. Roll out and cut into elves and trees.
  4. Bake at 180C until firm.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with a little water, tint red and green and ice the cooled biscuits.

Drinks

North Pole cocoaVegetarianNo alcohol

Cocoa with milk, cream, cinnamon and vanilla. Took 1,200 years to get right.

Prep 4 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the cocoa, sugar, salt and cinnamon in a jar up to 1 month ahead; warm the milk and cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp cocoa
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 900ml full cream milk
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 1 pinch cinnamon
  • 1 drop vanilla extract
  1. Whisk the cocoa, sugar and salt with a splash of the milk into a paste.
  2. Add the rest of the milk, cream, cinnamon and vanilla. Warm to drinking temperature without boiling.
  3. Pour into mugs.

When to serve

Serve the cocoa when an elf brings Scott a cup of his own recipe.
