Alva's fish shop bites

Smoked trout, cream cheese and dill on rye crispbread with pickled onion.

Prep 13 min · Make ahead: Mix the trout and cream cheese up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; spread on the crispbreads just before serving so they stay crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 150g smoked trout
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 small bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 12 rye crispbreads
  • 6 pickled onions, sliced
  1. Flake the trout into the cream cheese with chopped dill and lemon juice.
  2. Spread on the crispbreads.
  3. Top with slices of pickled onion.

Krum and Ellingboe feud meatballs

Pork and beef meatballs with allspice, served on toothpicks with cranberry sauce.

Prep 32 min · Make ahead: Mix and roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brown in butter just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork and beef mince
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 60ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1/4 tsp ground allspice
  • 20g butter
  • 100g cranberry sauce
  • salt, to taste
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in milk, 5 min.
  2. Mix the mince with the soaked crumbs, egg, onion, allspice and salt.
  3. Roll into 24 small balls.
  4. Brown in butter, 10 min.
  5. Serve on toothpicks with cranberry sauce.

Sámi flatbread with smoked salmon

Thin rye and barley rounds from a dry pan, cured fish, sour cream, dill and lingonberry.

How it is made
  • 120g rye flour
  • 80g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 40g butter
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 150g sour cream
  • 1 jar lingonberry jam
  • 1 bunch dill
  1. Thin rye and barley flatbreads cooked in a hot dry pan, spread with butter and topped with smoked salmon, sour cream, dill and lingonberry jam. Mix 120g rye flour, 80g barley or plain flour, half a tsp baking powder, a pinch of salt, 20g melted butter and about 150ml warm milk into a soft dough. Divide into 8 and roll very thin. Cook in a hot dry pan for 1 to 2 minutes a side until speckled. Butter while warm and top with 200g smoked salmon, sour cream, a little lingonberry jam and dill.

Klaus's juniper beef stew with mash

Beef chuck braised with juniper, blackcurrant jam and carrots, over mashed potatoes.

Prep 20 min, cook 3 hr 30 min · Make ahead: Stew better the next day
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 brown onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 6 juniper berries, crushed
  • 3 sprigs of thyme
  • 1 tbsp blackcurrant jam
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml full cream milk
  1. Brown the beef in batches, 15 min.
  2. Slice the onions and chop the carrots, then cook with the beef, 10 min.
  3. Stir in the flour, juniper, thyme, jam and stock.
  4. Cover and simmer until the beef falls apart, 3 hr.
  5. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter and milk, 20 min.
  6. Serve the stew over the mash.

Spoilt postmaster's duck with cherry sauce

Crisp-skinned roast duck under glossy cherry sauce, the spoilt son's dinner.

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 4 duck breasts
  • 200g frozen pitted cherries
  • 1 bottle balsamic vinegar
  • 1 jar honey
  • 150ml chicken stock
  1. Duck breasts scored and rendered skin side down until crisp, finished in the oven, sliced over a creamy potato gratin with a glossy cherry and balsamic sauce. Layer 1kg thinly sliced potatoes in a dish with 300ml cream, garlic and salt and bake at 180°C for 1 hour. Score the skin of 4 duck breasts and start skin side down in a cold pan, then cook over medium heat for 8 minutes to render. Flip and roast at 200°C for 6 minutes. Rest. Pour off most of the fat, add 200g pitted cherries, 2 tbsp balsamic, 1 tbsp honey and 150ml stock and reduce until glossy.

Dear Mr Klaus gingerbread envelopesVegetarian

Gingerbread cookies cut as envelopes with white icing and a red seal.

Prep 21 min, cook 15 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Make and chill the dough up to 2 days ahead; bake the envelopes up to 4 days ahead, airtight, and pipe the icing on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 100g golden syrup
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 drop red food colouring
  1. Melt the butter, brown sugar and golden syrup, 5 min.
  2. Stir in the flour, ginger and cinnamon.
  3. Chill the dough, 30 min.
  4. Roll and cut into envelopes.
  5. Bake at 180°C, 10 min.
  6. Pipe with white icing and add a red seal.

Márgu's warm lingonberry cordialNo alcohol

Lingonberry and orange drink with cinnamon, served warm or cold.

Prep 9 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g frozen lingonberries or cranberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 L water, divided
  • 2 oranges
  • 500ml soda water, for serving cold
  1. Simmer the berries with sugar, cinnamon and half the water, 10 min.
  2. Mash well and strain through a sieve, pressing on the fruit.
  3. Add the remaining water and orange juice.
  4. To serve warm, heat gently and pour into mugs with an orange slice, 5 min.
  5. To serve cold, pour over ice and top with soda water.
