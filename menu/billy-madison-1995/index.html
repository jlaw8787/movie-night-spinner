Snacks

Grade one ham and cheese pinwheels

Tortilla rolls with ham, cheddar, spinach and mustard cream cheese

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep rolled and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp American mustard
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 8 slices leg ham
  • 60g baby spinach
  • 120g cheddar, grated
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 8 cornichons
  1. Mix the cream cheese and mustard until smooth, then spread thinly over the tortillas right to the edges.
  2. Layer ham, spinach and grated cheddar over each tortilla.
  3. Roll up tightly and wrap in cling film.
  4. Chill so the rolls hold their shape.
  5. Trim the ends, cut each roll into pinwheels and serve with cherry tomatoes and cornichons.

Madison Hotel mini club sandwiches

Triple-decker toast with chicken, bacon, lettuce and tomato

Prep 20 min, cook 2 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours, keep covered and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 rashers bacon
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 200g sliced cooked chicken
  • 1 small iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 bag salted potato crisps
  1. Grill the bacon until crisp.
  2. Toast the bread and spread with mayonnaise.
  3. Build each sandwich with toast, chicken, lettuce and tomato, then toast, bacon and lettuce, then the last slice of toast.
  4. Press down gently, pin with toothpicks, trim the crusts and cut into squares. Serve with crisps.

Mains

Extra sloppy chilli cheese dogs

Chilli and cheese on hot dogs, extra sloppy for yous

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead, keep chilled, or freeze up to 1 month; reheat, then grill the dogs and assemble on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 onion, finely diced, to serve
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 200g tomato passata
  • 8 frankfurts
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 120g cheddar, grated
  • American mustard, to serve
  1. Brown the mince with the diced onion, then add the cumin, smoked paprika, chilli powder, passata and a splash of water.
  2. Simmer until thick.
  3. Grill the frankfurts, turning, and toast the split buns.
  4. Fill the buns with frankfurts and chilli, top with cheddar and grill until melted.
  5. Finish with raw diced onion and a squeeze of mustard.

Grade three crispy chicken tenders

Honey mustard to dip. Back at school at 27

Prep 20 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Crumb the tenders up to 1 day ahead and keep them in the fridge; bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken tenderloins
  • 60g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g cornflakes, crushed
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp American mustard
  • 3 carrots, cut in sticks
  1. Mix the cornflakes with the parmesan, paprika and salt.
  2. Dip the chicken in flour, beaten egg and the cornflake crumb and lay on an oiled tray. Drizzle with oil.
  3. Bake at 220C, turning once, until crisp and cooked through.
  4. Stir the honey into the mustard and serve with the tenders and carrot sticks.

Sweets

Snack pack chocolate pudding cupsVegetarian

Chocolate pudding cups. No trading it for a banana

Prep 15 min, cook 125 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled with cling film on the surface
Recipe for 4
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 30g cornflour
  • 600ml full cream milk
  • 80g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 4 chocolate biscuits, crushed
  • 2 tbsp rainbow sprinkles, to top
  • 1 pinch salt
  1. Whisk the sugar, cocoa, cornflour and salt in a saucepan, then slowly whisk in the milk.
  2. Stir over medium heat until it boils and thickens.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the chocolate and vanilla until smooth. Pour into cups and press cling film on the surface.
  4. Chill until set.
  5. Whip the cream and top each cup with cream, crushed biscuits and sprinkles.

Drinks

Back to school chocolate malt shakeVegetarianNo alcohol

Vanilla ice cream, chocolate sauce and malt blended thick

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer and whip the cream up to 2 hours ahead; blend just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 tbsp chocolate sauce, plus extra to finish
  • 600ml full cream milk, cold
  • 6 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 150ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Chill tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Drizzle chocolate sauce down the inside of each glass.
  3. Blend the milk, ice cream, malted milk powder and cocoa until thick and smooth, then pour into the glasses.
  4. Top with whipped cream, more chocolate sauce and a sprinkle of malt powder.
