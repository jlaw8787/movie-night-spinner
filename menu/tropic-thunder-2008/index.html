Snacks

Alpa Chino's peanut barsVegetarian

Peanut caramel bars coated in milk chocolate.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, 45 min waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 80g butter
  • 250g roasted peanuts
  • 150g milk chocolate
  1. Boil the sugar, golden syrup and butter together for 3 minutes.
  2. Stir in the peanuts and press into a lined tray.
  3. Pour over the melted milk chocolate.
  4. Chill until firm, then cut into bars, 45 min.

Four Leaf's jungle banh mi bites

Baguette bites with pork pâté, char siu, pickled carrot, cucumber and cilantro.

Prep 30 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Pickles up to 2 days ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1 carrot
  • 100g daikon
  • 100ml white vinegar
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 small baguettes
  • 100g pork pate
  • 200g char siu pork
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 1 red chilli
  1. Cut the carrot and daikon into matchsticks and quick-pickle in the vinegar and sugar.
  2. Split the baguettes and spread with pate.
  3. Fill with char siu, drained pickles, cucumber, coriander and chilli, 10 min.
  4. Cut into bites.

Mains

Flaming Dragon green curry

Chicken thigh green curry with Thai eggplant in coconut milk.

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Curry up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp green curry paste
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 800g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar
  • 200g Thai eggplant
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Fry the curry paste in the thick top of the coconut milk until it splits, 5 min.
  2. Add the chicken, the rest of the coconut milk, fish sauce, palm sugar and eggplant. Simmer, 15 min.
  3. Cook the rice by the absorption method, 15 min.
  4. Stir through the Thai basil and serve over rice.

Base camp beef pho

Star anise ginger broth over rice noodles and raw meat.

How it is made
  • 1.5kg beef soup bones
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1 jar star anise
  • 1 jar cinnamon sticks
  • 1 jar whole cloves
  • 1 bottle fish sauce
  • 400g rice noodles
  • 300g eye fillet
  • 200g bean sprouts
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 2 limes
  • 2 red chillies
  1. Spiced beef broth with charred onion, ginger, star anise and cinnamon over rice noodles and paper-thin raw beef, with bean sprouts, Thai basil, lime and chilli. Char a halved onion and a 6cm piece of ginger under the grill. Simmer 1.5kg beef bones in 3L water for 3 hours with the onion, ginger, 4 star anise, a cinnamon stick and 4 cloves, skimming. Strain and season with 3 tbsp fish sauce and 1 tbsp sugar. Soak 400g rice noodles and divide into bowls with 300g paper-thin raw eye fillet. Pour over the boiling broth. Serve with sprouts, Thai basil, lime and chilli.

Mango sticky riceVegetarian

Sweet coconut sticky rice with sliced mango, coconut cream and sesame seeds.

Prep 30 min, cook 25 min, 10 hr waiting · Make ahead: Rice soaks overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g glutinous rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  1. Soak the rice overnight in cold water.
  2. Drain and steam until tender and glossy, 25 min.
  3. Warm the coconut milk with the caster sugar and a pinch of salt, stir into the rice and rest, 20 min.
  4. Slice the mangoes, spoon a little salty coconut cream alongside and scatter with sesame seeds.

Portnoy's mango raspberry fruit jellies

He wants jelly beans very, very badly.

How it is made
  • 250g mango purée
  • 250g frozen raspberries
  • 25g fruit pectin
  • 600g caster sugar
  • 1 jar liquid glucose
  • 1 jar citric acid
  • 200g jelly beans
  1. Soft fruit pastilles of mango and raspberry purée set with pectin, cut into cubes and rolled in sugar, piled in a bowl with jelly beans. Line a 20cm square tin with baking paper. Bring 250g mango purée and 250g raspberry purée to the boil with 25g pectin mixed into 50g sugar. Add 450g sugar and 2 tbsp glucose and boil, stirring, to 107°C. Stir in 1 tsp citric acid and pour into the tin. Set at room temperature for 4 hours, cut into 2cm cubes and roll in caster sugar. Serve with 200g jelly beans.

Drinks

Booty Sweat passionfruit ginger fizzNo alcohol

Passionfruit, lime and fresh ginger syrup served over ice.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Syrup up to 1 week ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 100ml water
  • 1 piece fresh ginger, 5cm
  • 4 passionfruit
  • 2 limes
  • 600ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Simmer the sugar, water and sliced ginger for 5 minutes and cool.
  2. Mix the syrup with passionfruit pulp and lime juice.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice, divide the mix between them and top with soda water.
  4. Stir and add a mint sprig.
