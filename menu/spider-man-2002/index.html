Snacks

Bone Saw egg and cheese rollsVegetarian

Three minutes in the ring, then breakfast

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best eaten straight away
Recipe for 4
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 8 eggs
  • 20g butter
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Split the rolls and toast lightly.
  2. Fry the eggs in the butter, breaking the yolks, and lay two slices of cheese over each pair to melt.
  3. Stack the eggs into the rolls with salt, pepper and a dash of hot sauce.
  4. Wrap each roll in foil for a couple of minutes so it all steams together.

Thanksgiving sliders

Turkey and cranberry for the awkward Osborn dinner

Prep 5 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Stuffing squares up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 170g stuffing mix
  • 8 slider buns
  • 300g sliced roast turkey
  • 150g cranberry sauce
  1. Make the stuffing mix as the packet says and press into a tray.
  2. Bake at 190C until crisp, then cut into 8 squares.
  3. Toast the slider buns and warm the turkey. Fill each bun with turkey, cranberry sauce and a square of stuffing.

Mains

Aunt May's Thanksgiving roast

Brined turkey breast, stuffing, pan gravy, roast potato and pumpkin, before Norman sees the cut

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min, then 12.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Brine the turkey overnight
Recipe for 4
  • 1.2kg turkey breast
  • 2l water, for brine
  • 100g cooking salt
  • 30g butter
  • 800g potatoes, in chunks
  • 600g pumpkin, in chunks
  • 170g stuffing mix
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  1. Stir the salt into the water and brine the turkey breast, covered, in the fridge.
  2. Brine the turkey overnight.
  3. Pat the turkey dry, rub with butter and cut the potatoes and pumpkin into chunks.
  4. Roast the turkey at 170C until 70C inside, with the potato and pumpkin roasting alongside.
  5. Rest the turkey, covered. Bake the stuffing mix as the packet says.
  6. Make gravy from the pan juices, flour and stock. Slice the turkey and serve with everything.

Sweets

Forest Hills pancakesVegetarian

Aunt May's Forest Hills kitchen, with syrup

Prep 5 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 400ml buttermilk
  • 60g butter
  • 150ml maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Add the eggs, buttermilk and some melted butter.
  2. Rest the batter.
  3. Cook ladlefuls in a buttered pan over medium heat until bubbles set, then flip. Keep the stack warm.
  4. Stack high and pour over the maple syrup.

Daily Bugle cherry lime rickeyVegetarianNo alcohol

Cherry syrup and citrus over ice with soda and a maraschino, for a photo-hungry editor

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Best made fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 5 limes
  • 120ml cherry syrup
  • 800ml soda water
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Squeeze 4 limes and cut the last into wedges.
  2. Into each of 4 tall glasses of ice pour the lime juice and cherry syrup, top with soda water, stir once and drop in a cherry and a lime wedge.
