Warren's baseball aranciniVegetarian

Crumbed risotto balls with a mozzarella middle, like little baseballs

Prep 50 min, cook 25 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Make the risotto up to 2 days ahead and chill; shape, crumb and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 300g arborio rice
  • 1l hot vegetable stock
  • 50g parmesan, finely grated
  • 125g mozzarella, cut in 12 cubes
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g dry breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 400g jar passata
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 small bunch basil
  1. Soften the onion in the butter, stir in the rice, then add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, until creamy and just tender.
  2. Stir in the parmesan, season, spread on a tray and chill until firm.
  3. Roll into 12 balls, pushing a cube of mozzarella into the middle of each.
  4. Roll in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
  5. Simmer the passata with the garlic and a few basil leaves while the oil heats.
  6. Fry the arancini at 180°C in batches until deep golden, drain and serve with the marinara and torn basil.

Pat Healy's architect garlic knotsVegetarian

Garlic butter knots with parmesan and marinara. Healy spies on Mary in Miami

Prep 17 min, cook 3 min · Make ahead: Tie the knots up to a day ahead and chill on the tray; bake and toss in the butter to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g ball pizza dough
  • 60g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 30g parmesan, finely grated
  • 300ml marinara sauce
  1. Cut the dough into 12 strips, roll each into a rope and tie in a loose knot.
  2. Prove on a lined tray for 10 minutes, then bake at 220°C until golden.
  3. Melt the butter with the garlic for a minute and stir in the parsley.
  4. Toss the hot knots in the garlic butter and parmesan and serve with warm marinara.

Warren's franks and beans

Smoky franks with molasses baked beans. Ted's prom night zipper disaster

Prep 5 min, cook 73 min · Make ahead: Make the beans up to 3 days ahead; they get better. Grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 smoked frankfurts
  • 3 rashers smoky bacon, chopped
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 x 400g tins cannellini beans, drained
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 250ml water
  • 200g coleslaw mix
  • 3 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Fry the bacon and onion in an ovenproof pot until soft and golden.
  2. Stir in the beans, tomatoes, molasses, sugar, mustard, vinegar and water and bring to a simmer.
  3. Bake uncovered at 170°C, stirring once, until thick and dark.
  4. Toss the slaw with the mayo, and grill the franks until blistered.
  5. Serve the franks on the beans with slaw alongside.

Puffy's body-cast chicken cordon bleu

Crumbed chicken stuffed with ham and Swiss. Puffy ends up in a full body cast

Prep 11 min, cook 39 min · Make ahead: Fill and crumb the chicken up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 4 slices leg ham
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
  • 150g green beans
  1. Cut a deep pocket into each breast and fill with a folded slice of ham and cheese.
  2. Dust in flour, dip in egg and press into the panko.
  3. Brown in the oil on both sides, then bake at 200°C until cooked through.
  4. Warm the cream with the mustard and season, and steam the beans.
  5. Slice the chicken and serve with the beans and the mustard cream.

Hair gel meringues with lemon curdVegetarian

Vanilla meringues with whipped cream and lemon curd, swirled tall

Prep 20 min, cook 90 min · Make ahead: Bake the meringues up to 5 days ahead and keep airtight; whip the cream to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 175g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 200g lemon curd
  • 125g blueberries
  1. Whisk the whites to soft peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  2. Whisk in the vinegar, cornflour and vanilla and spoon 8 swirled mounds onto lined trays.
  3. Bake at 120°C until crisp outside, then turn the oven off and let them cool inside.
  4. Whip the cream with a little vanilla, and top each meringue with cream, lemon curd and blueberries.

Senior prom punchVegetarian

Pineapple and ginger beer punch with rum and lime sorbet. Mary asks Ted to the prom

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Chill everything well; build the punch just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 180ml white rum
  • 500ml pineapple juice
  • 500ml ginger beer
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 4 scoops lime sorbet
  • 1 tray ice
  1. Stir the rum, pineapple and lime juice in a punch bowl with ice.
  2. Top with the ginger beer.
  3. Float the sorbet on top and ladle into cups.
