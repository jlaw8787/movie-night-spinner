Reed's flower shop goat's cheese toastsVegetarian

Sourdough hearts. Reed proposes, Morley says yes, then leaves

Prep 20 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Whip the cheeses up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled; toast, assemble and add the hot honey just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g goat's cheese, soft
  • 50g cream cheese
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 1 punnet edible flowers
  1. Cut hearts from the sourdough, brush with oil and toast at 190C until crisp.
  2. Whip the goat's cheese with the cream cheese until smooth.
  3. Warm the honey with the chilli flakes.
  4. Spread the toasts with the cheese, drizzle with hot honey and scatter with thyme leaves and edible flowers.

Reed's flower shop zucchini flowersVegetarian

Ricotta-filled flowers. Harrison orders two bouquets, quietly

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Fill the flowers up to 3 hours ahead; batter and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 12 zucchini flowers
  • 250g ricotta
  • 40g parmesan, grated
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch basil
  • 100g plain flour
  • 170ml cold soda water
  • 500ml vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Mix the ricotta, parmesan, lemon zest, chopped basil and pepper.
  2. Remove the stamens and spoon a little filling into each flower, twisting the petals closed.
  3. Whisk the flour and cold soda water into a thin batter.
  4. Heat the oil to 180C. Dip the flowers in batter and fry in batches for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp. Drain.
  5. Salt lightly and serve with lemon wedges.

Valentine's sirloin with garlic butter

Garlic butter sirloin. Holden lends Kate his limo

Prep 20 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep in the fridge; sear the steaks and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g baby potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 70g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 small bunch thyme
  • 150ml red wine
  • 150ml beef stock
  • 300g green beans
  1. Boil the potatoes until just tender, drain, smash flat on a tray, drizzle with oil and roast at 220C.
  2. Season the steaks and sear in a hot pan on both sides.
  3. Add most of the butter, the garlic and thyme and baste briefly. Rest the steaks.
  4. Add the wine and stock to the pan and reduce by half.
  5. Saute the beans in the remaining butter. Serve with the steaks and potatoes.

Edgar and Estelle's anniversary salmon

Edgar and Estelle, back together after a rough Valentine's Day

Prep 15 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes up to 1 day ahead; cook the salmon just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 salmon fillets, skin on
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g kipfler potatoes
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 1 shallot
  • 80ml white wine vinegar
  • 100g cold butter, diced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes until tender, then halve them.
  2. Simmer the chopped shallot in the vinegar until almost gone, then whisk in the butter a piece at a time over low heat with a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Salt the salmon skin and cook skin side down in the oil for 5 minutes until crisp, then flip for 2 minutes.
  4. Blanch the asparagus for 2 minutes.
  5. Plate the salmon on the potatoes and asparagus, spoon over the sauce and scatter with chives.

Pistachio chocolate-dipped strawberriesVegetarian

Dark chocolate, pistachio. Edison's flowers go to Rani instead

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Dip and chill up to 4 hours ahead, kept on baking paper in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 500g strawberries, stalks on
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 50g pistachios, finely chopped
  • 50g white chocolate
  • 10g freeze-dried raspberries, crushed
  1. Wash the strawberries and dry them completely.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate in a bowl over simmering water.
  3. Dip each berry two-thirds of the way in, let the excess drip off and roll the base in pistachios. Lay on baking paper.
  4. Melt the white chocolate and drizzle over with a fork, then dust with crushed raspberry.
  5. Chill until set.

Valentine's red velvet cupcakesVegetarian

Red velvet cupcakes with cream cheese frosting

Prep 40 min, cook 24 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep covered in a cool place
Recipe for 4
  • 175g butter, soft (115g for the cakes, 60g for the icing)
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tube red food colouring paste
  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 160ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 200g cream cheese
  • 250g icing sugar
  1. Beat the cake butter with the sugar, add the eggs, vanilla and red colouring.
  2. Fold in the flour, cocoa, bicarbonate of soda and the buttermilk mixed with the vinegar.
  3. Divide into cupcake cases and bake at 180C.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Beat the cream cheese, remaining butter and icing sugar until fluffy and pipe on.
  6. Crumble one cupcake over the rest to finish.

Kara's I Hate Valentine's Day pink fizzVegetarian

Sparkling rosé with grapefruit juice, raspberries and rosemary

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Best poured just before serving; chill the sparkling rose and pink lemonade the day before
Recipe for 4
  • 12 raspberries
  • 120ml pink grapefruit juice
  • 480ml chilled sparkling rose
  • 4 small sprigs rosemary
  • 500ml pink lemonade (for anyone not drinking)
  1. Drop raspberries into each flute and add a splash of grapefruit juice.
  2. Slowly top with chilled sparkling rose. For anyone not drinking, use pink lemonade instead.
  3. Rub a small rosemary sprig between your hands and add one to each glass.
