Snacks

Chicken satay

Chicken thigh skewers marinated in coconut and turmeric, peanut sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 8 min, 2 hr 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day, grill fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, cut into strips
  • 200ml coconut milk, for the marinade
  • 2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 stalks of lemongrass
  • 100g peanut butter
  • 100ml coconut milk, for the sauce
  • 2 limes
  • 12 bamboo skewers
  1. Chop the lemongrass and mix with the coconut milk for the marinade and the turmeric. Add the chicken and marinate in the fridge, 2 hr 10 min.
  2. Soak the skewers in water, then thread on the chicken, 5 min.
  3. Grill until charred and cooked through, 8 min.
  4. Warm the peanut butter with the coconut milk for the sauce and the lime juice into a thick sauce, 5 min.

Beer pong prawn crackers

Puffed prawn crackers fried crisp, sweet chilli sauce for dipping.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Crackers keep a day in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 1 packet prawn crackers
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150ml sweet chilli sauce
  1. Heat the oil to 190°C, 5 min.
  2. Drop in the prawn crackers a few at a time. They puff up in seconds. Scoop out and drain on paper.
  3. Pile into a big bowl with sweet chilli sauce for dipping.

Nori crisps with sesame salt

Nori pressed onto rice paper, fried shatteringly crisp, dusted with toasted seeds and chilli.

How it is made
  • 6 nori sheets
  • 6 rice paper sheets
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • chilli flakes
  1. Nori sheets sandwiched with rice paper, brushed with sesame oil, fried until shatteringly crisp and dusted with toasted sesame, chilli and salt. Brush 6 rice paper sheets lightly with water and press a nori sheet onto each. Cut into 5cm strips. Heat 3cm vegetable oil in a wok to 180°C and fry a few strips at a time for 10 to 15 seconds until they puff and crisp. Drain on paper towel. Toast 2 tbsp sesame seeds in a dry pan and crush with 1 tsp salt and half a tsp chilli flakes. Brush the crisps with a little sesame oil and dust with the sesame salt.

Gede's seaweed farm nasi goreng

Wok-fried rice with chicken, garlic and red chilli.

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Best fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 295g jasmine rice
  • 400g chicken thighs, diced
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 red chillies
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp kecap manis
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  1. Dice the chicken, crush the garlic, chop the chillies and slice the cucumber.
  2. Stir fry the chicken with the garlic and chilli in the oil in a very hot wok, 6 min.
  3. Add the cold rice and kecap manis and toss until smoky.
  4. Fry the eggs and serve on top with cucumber slices, 4 min.

Banana leaf snapper with sambal matah

Snapper with turmeric, chilli and lime, grilled in banana leaves, lemongrass sambal, coconut rice.

How it is made
  • 4 snapper fillets
  • 1 head garlic
  • 4 red chillies
  • 2cm fresh turmeric
  • 2 limes
  • banana leaves
  • 1.5 cups jasmine rice
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 4 eschalots
  • 2 lemongrass stalks
  • 3 makrut lime leaves
  • vegetable oil
  1. Snapper fillets rubbed with turmeric, chilli, garlic and lime, grilled in banana leaves, topped with a raw lemongrass and eschalot sambal, with coconut rice. Pound 2 garlic cloves, 2 red chillies, 2cm turmeric and the juice of 1 lime into a paste and rub over 4 snapper fillets. Wrap each in a softened banana leaf and grill or barbecue 10 minutes, turning once. Cook 1.5 cups jasmine rice with 200ml coconut milk and water. For the sambal, finely slice 4 eschalots, 2 lemongrass stalks, 2 chillies and 3 makrut lime leaves, mix with 3 tbsp oil, lime juice and salt. Spoon over the fish.

Black rice puddingVegetarian

Slow-simmered black rice, palm sugar, warm coconut milk.

Prep 5 min, cook 40 min, 8 hr waiting · Make ahead: Make the night before, serve warm or chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 200g black rice
  • 1 L water
  • 80g palm sugar, or to taste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes
  1. Soak the black rice overnight, which is the resting time.
  2. Simmer in the water until thick and soft, 40 min.
  3. Stir in palm sugar to taste.
  4. Warm the coconut milk with a pinch of salt and pour over each bowl, 3 min.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml coconut water
  • 500ml pineapple juice

  • 1 handful mint leaves

  1. Fill tall glasses with ice and mint leaves.
