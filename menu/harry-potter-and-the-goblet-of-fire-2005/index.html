Snacks

World Cup campsite sausages

Sticky pan fried sausages and golden onions in soft rolls with brown sauce.

Prep 3 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 8 soft rolls
  • brown sauce, to serve
  1. Fry the sausages slowly in a heavy pan until sticky.
  2. Fry the onions in the butter until golden.
  3. Fill the rolls with sausage and onions and add a good squirt of brown sauce, 3 min.

Dragon task chilli bitesVegetarian

Crisp haloumi glazed with honey and chilli, on toothpicks. Hungarian Horntail heat.

Prep 7 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Cut the haloumi up to 1 day ahead; fry and glaze just before serving, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 400g haloumi, cut in cubes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 20 toothpicks
  1. Cut the haloumi into bite-size cubes and pat dry.
  2. Fry in the oil in a hot pan until golden on two sides.
  3. Add the honey and chilli and toss until sticky. Squeeze over lemon and spear onto toothpicks.

Smoked salmon blinis

Buckwheat pancakes, crème fraîche, salmon and roe on silver trays at the Yule Ball.

How it is made
  • 100g buckwheat flour
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter
  • 200ml crème fraîche
  • 200g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 50g salmon roe
  1. Warm buckwheat blinis topped with crème fraîche, smoked salmon, dill, lemon zest and a little salmon roe, laid out on silver trays. Whisk 100g buckwheat flour, 50g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder and a pinch of salt with 200ml milk and 1 egg into a thick batter. Cook teaspoons in a buttered pan for 1 minute a side to make about 30 blinis. Cool slightly. Top each with a little crème fraîche, a curl of smoked salmon, a sprig of dill, lemon zest and a few beads of salmon roe.

Mains

Yule Ball roast

Rib roast with roast potatoes, Yorkshire puddings and honey glazed carrots.

Prep 0 min, cook 3 hours · Make ahead: Gravy and carrots can be made 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 4 carrots, cut in batons
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Season the beef and roast for the first stage, 20 min.
  2. Drop the oven to 160°C and roast the beef until 52°C in the middle.
  3. Rest the beef under foil, 20 min.
  4. Roast the potatoes in oil alongside the beef, 45 min.
  5. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk and bake Yorkshires in hot oiled tins at 220°C, 20 min.
  6. Glaze the carrots with honey and roast, 25 min.
  7. Make gravy by simmering the beef stock and balsamic vinegar with the pan juices.

French school bouillabaisse with rouille

Snapper, mussels and prawns in saffron broth, garlicky red sauce, for Fleur and her schoolmates.

How it is made
  • 600g snapper fillets
  • 500g mussels
  • 8 green king prawns
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 packet saffron threads
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tin crushed tomatoes
  • 1.2 litres fish stock
  • 1 roasted capsicum
  • 1 bag breadcrumbs
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 baguette
  1. Snapper, mussels and prawns simmered in a saffron, fennel and tomato broth, with toasted baguette and a garlicky red rouille to stir through. Soften 1 onion, 1 fennel bulb and 4 garlic cloves in olive oil. Add a pinch of saffron, 1 strip orange zest, 1 tin crushed tomatoes and 1.2 litres fish stock and simmer 20 minutes. Add 600g snapper chunks for 3 minutes, then 500g mussels and 8 green prawns for 4 minutes until opened. Blend 1 roasted capsicum, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp breadcrumbs, a pinch of chilli and 80ml olive oil for rouille. Serve with toasted baguette.

Ice palace pavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue with a soft centre, topped with whipped cream.

Prep 3 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Base up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 50g white chocolate, shaved
  • 250g mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 120°C. Whip the egg whites to soft peaks, add the sugar gradually until glossy, then fold in the cornflour.
  2. Shape on baking paper and bake for 90 minutes.
  3. Turn off the oven and cool the pavlova inside, 30 min.
  4. Top with whipped cream, white chocolate and berries, dusted with icing sugar.

Mulled apple punchNo alcohol

Apple juice simmered with cinnamon, cloves and orange peel, served warm.

Prep 5 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.5 L cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 orange, peel only
  • 2 star anise
  1. Simmer the apple juice with the cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and star anise, 15 min.
  2. Strain and serve warm in mugs, 5 min.
