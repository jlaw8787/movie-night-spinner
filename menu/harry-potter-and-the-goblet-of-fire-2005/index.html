Snacks

World Cup campsite sausages

Sticky pan fried sausages and golden onions in soft rolls with brown sauce

Prep 10 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 8 pork sausages
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 30g butter
  • 8 soft rolls
  • brown sauce, to serve
  1. Fry the sausages slowly in a heavy pan until sticky.
  2. Fry the onions in the butter until golden.
  3. Fill the rolls with sausage and onions and add a good squirt of brown sauce.

Dragon task chilli bitesVegetarian

Crisp haloumi glazed with honey and chilli, on toothpicks. Hungarian Horntail heat

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Cut the haloumi up to 1 day ahead; fry and glaze just before serving, best warm
Recipe for 4
  • 400g haloumi, cut in cubes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 20 toothpicks
  1. Cut the haloumi into bite-size cubes and pat dry.
  2. Fry in the oil in a hot pan until golden on two sides.
  3. Add the honey and chilli and toss until sticky. Squeeze over lemon and spear onto toothpicks.

Mains

Yule Ball roast

Rib roast with roast potatoes, Yorkshire puddings and honey glazed carrots

Prep 40 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Gravy and carrots can be made 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg beef rib roast
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g plain flour
  • 300ml milk
  • 4 carrots, cut in batons
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 200ml red wine
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Heat the oven to 220C. Season the beef and roast for the first stage.
  2. Drop the oven to 160C and roast the beef until 52C in the middle.
  3. Rest the beef under foil.
  4. Roast the potatoes in oil alongside the beef.
  5. Whisk the eggs, flour and milk and bake Yorkshires in hot oiled tins at 220C.
  6. Glaze the carrots with honey and roast.
  7. Make gravy by simmering the red wine with the pan juices.

Sweets

Ice palace pavlovaVegetarian

Crisp meringue with a soft centre, topped with whipped cream

Prep 30 min, cook 120 min · Make ahead: Base up to 1 day, top just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 300ml thickened cream, whipped
  • 50g white chocolate, shaved
  • 250g mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Heat the oven to 120C. Whip the egg whites to soft peaks, add the sugar gradually until glossy, then fold in the cornflour.
  2. Shape on baking paper and bake for 90 minutes.
  3. Turn off the oven and cool the pavlova inside.
  4. Top with whipped cream, white chocolate and berries, dusted with icing sugar.

Mulled apple punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Apple juice simmered with cinnamon, cloves and orange peel, served warm. Cedric rules.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 orange, peel only
  • 2 star anise
  1. Simmer the apple juice with the cinnamon, cloves, orange peel and star anise.
  2. Strain and serve warm in mugs.

When to serve

Serve the feast at the Yule Ball.
