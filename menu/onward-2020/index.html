Crumbed mozzarella sticksVegetarian

Mozzarella in a double crumb, fried, with a dipping sauce.

Prep 27 min, cook 14 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Crumb and freeze the sticks up to 1 week ahead; fry from frozen and warm the marinara on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 400g firm mozzarella
  • 50g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 300g marinara sauce
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Cut the mozzarella into sticks.
  2. Roll in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs twice.
  3. Freeze the sticks, 30 min.
  4. Heat the oil to 180°C, 8 min.
  5. Fry in batches until golden.
  6. Warm the marinara and serve alongside, 4 min.

Burger Shire sliders

Small beef patties with cheese and pickles on slider buns.

Prep 16 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Shape the patties up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; cook and build just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 6 slices of cheddar
  • 12 slider buns
  • 12 dill pickle slices
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  1. Shape the mince into small patties and season.
  2. Cook in the oil on a hot pan, turning once, and top each patty with half a slice of cheddar at the end, 12 min.
  3. Put into the buns with pickles and tomato sauce.

Guinevere road trip pizza scrolls

Puff pastry scrolls of ham, tomato and mozzarella, for the road trip in Barley's van, Guinevere.

How it is made
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 150g ham
  • 150g mozzarella
  1. Puff pastry spread with tomato paste, ham and mozzarella, rolled into scrolls, baked golden and wrapped in paper for the road trip. Lay 2 sheets puff pastry on the bench. Spread each with 2 tbsp tomato paste mixed with half a teaspoon of dried oregano. Scatter with 150g chopped ham and 150g grated mozzarella, leaving a 2cm border on one edge. Roll up tightly towards the border, then cut each log into 8 scrolls. Lay cut side up on a lined tray and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Cool 5 minutes before eating.

Questing chicken drumsticks

Honey thyme chicken drumsticks roasted with potatoes and carrots.

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Toss the drumsticks with the honey, garlic, thyme and oil up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; roast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 4 carrots, sliced
  1. Toss the drumsticks with the honey, garlic, thyme and some of the oil.
  2. Chop the potatoes and slice the carrots.
  3. Roast at 200°C alongside the vegetables, turning once, 40 min.
  4. Serve on one big tray.

New Mushroomton mushroom and leek pie

Mixed fungi and leek cooked down in butter, thyme and cream, under puff and baked golden.

How it is made
  • 750g mixed mushrooms
  • 50g butter
  • 2 leeks
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 100ml vegetable stock
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Mixed mushrooms and leek cooked down in butter with thyme and cream, spooned into a dish, topped with puff pastry and baked at 200°C for 25 minutes. Melt 50g butter in a big pan and cook 750g mixed mushrooms, torn or sliced, over high heat until golden. Add 2 sliced leeks, 2 garlic cloves and a few thyme sprigs and cook 8 minutes until soft. Stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, then 150ml cream and 100ml vegetable stock and simmer until thick. Season well and spoon into a pie dish. Top with 1 sheet puff pastry, brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C for 25 minutes.

Dad's day sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream under hot dark chocolate fudge.

Prep 10 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Make the hot fudge up to 3 days ahead in the fridge and rewarm it; whip the cream, scoop and build just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 150g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 150ml whipped cream
  • 2 tbsp sprinkles
  1. Heat the cream until steaming.
  2. Pour over the chocolate and stir into hot fudge.
  3. Scoop ice cream into tall glasses.
  4. Pour over the fudge and top with whipped cream and sprinkles.

Quest potion punchNo alcohol

Apple juice, blackcurrant cordial and soda water over ice.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Chill the apple juice and cordial up to 1 day ahead; add ice and soda water just before serving so it stays fizzy
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cups ice
  • 500ml apple juice
  • 80ml blackcurrant cordial
  • 500ml soda water
  1. Fill glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in apple juice to halfway.
  3. Add a splash of blackcurrant cordial and top with soda water.
  4. Stir until it turns purple like a potion.
