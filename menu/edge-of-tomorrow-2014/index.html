Snacks

Forward base bacon rolls

Crisp bacon in buttered soft rolls with brown sauce, wrapped to stay warm.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead and reheat in a hot pan; butter and fill the rolls just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick rashers bacon
  • 4 soft white rolls
  • 60g butter
  • 4 tbsp brown sauce
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp at the edges.
  2. Split the rolls and butter them generously.
  3. Stuff two rashers into each roll with a good squeeze of brown sauce.
  4. Wrap tightly in paper so they stay warm.

Normandy beach mussels

Mussels steamed in white wine, garlic and butter, with parsley and crusty bread.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Chop the eschalots and garlic up to 1 day ahead; steam the mussels just before serving, they do not hold
Recipe for 4
  • 2 eschalots
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 60g butter, divided
  • 1kg cleaned mussels
  • 200ml white wine
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 loaf crusty bread
  1. Chop the eschalots and garlic.
  2. Soften in some butter in a big pot.
  3. Add the mussels and wine, cover and steam until open.
  4. Toss through chopped parsley and a knob of butter.
  5. Serve with crusty bread to mop up.

Same day again Welsh rarebitVegetarian

Cheddar, mustard and ale sauce grilled bubbling on toast. The same breakfast, every single morning.

Prep 2 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese mix up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 80ml milk
  • 250g vintage cheddar, grated
  • 2 tsp English mustard
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 thick slices sourdough
  • 2 spring onions
  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then whisk in the milk until thick.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in the cheddar, mustard, paprika and egg yolk.
  3. Toast the bread on one side under a hot grill, spread the cheese mix on the other side and grill for 4 minutes until bubbling and browned in spots.
  4. Cut into fingers and scatter with sliced spring onion.

Mains

Angel of Verdun chicken, cider and cream

Chicken thighs and mushrooms simmered in apple cider and cream, with baguette.

Prep 17 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Brown and simmer the chicken in the cider up to 2 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; reheat gently, stir in the cream and serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 30g butter
  • 2 eschalots, sliced
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 375ml apple cider
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 baguette
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Season the chicken.
  2. Brown in butter, 4 minutes a side, then set aside.
  3. Soften the eschalots and mushrooms in the pan.
  4. Pour in the cider and scrape up the browned bits.
  5. Return the chicken, cover and simmer.
  6. Stir in the cream and simmer until glossy.
  7. Scatter with parsley and serve with a warm baguette.

Farmhouse beef and mushroom stew with mash

Chuck braised slow with mushrooms, carrot and thyme, over buttery potato, for the country hideout.

Prep 14 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the stew up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 carrots
  • 250g Swiss brown mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Toss the beef in the flour with salt and pepper and brown it in batches in the oil in a heavy pot.
  2. Fry the chopped onion, carrots, mushrooms and garlic for 5 minutes, stir in the tomato paste, then add the stock and thyme and put the beef back in.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 2 hours until the beef is tender.
  4. Boil the peeled potatoes for 20 minutes, drain and mash with the butter and milk.
  5. Serve the stew over the mash.

Sweets

J Squad sticky date puddingVegetarian

Warm date sponge under hot butterscotch sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Bake the pudding up to 2 days ahead and keep airtight; reheat it and make the hot sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 200g pitted dates, chopped
  • 1 pinch bicarb soda
  • 60g butter, for the pudding
  • 150g brown sugar, for the pudding
  • 2 eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 100g butter, for sauce
  • 100g brown sugar, for sauce
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 500ml custard
  1. Soak the dates in boiling water with bicarb soda.
  2. Cream the pudding butter and brown sugar, add the eggs, dates and flour.
  3. Bake at 180°C.
  4. Simmer the butter and brown sugar for the sauce with the cream into a hot sauce.
  5. Pour the sauce over the pudding and serve with custard.

Drinks

Gunfire: army tea with dark rum

Strong stewed black tea with brown sugar and dark rum, in metal mugs.

Prep 3 min, cook 7 min · Make ahead: Best brewed fresh and hot; put the sugar and rum in the mugs up to 1 hour ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1.2 L freshly boiled water
  • 8 tsp brown sugar
  • 120ml dark rum
  • 200ml full cream milk
  1. Warm a big teapot with boiling water and tip it out.
  2. Add the tea bags and freshly boiled water and leave to stew until properly strong.
  3. Put sugar and rum into each of four metal mugs.
  4. Pour in the tea, add a splash of milk and stir well.
