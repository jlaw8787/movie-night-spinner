Snacks

Forward base bacon rolls

Crisp bacon in buttered soft rolls with brown sauce, wrapped to stay warm.

Prep 7 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Fry the bacon up to 1 day ahead and reheat in a hot pan; butter and fill the rolls just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick rashers of bacon
  • 4 soft white rolls
  • 60g butter
  • 4 tbsp brown sauce
  1. Fry the bacon until crisp at the edges, 10 min.
  2. Split the rolls and butter them generously.
  3. Stuff two rashers into each roll with a good squeeze of brown sauce.
  4. Wrap tightly in paper so they stay warm.

Corned beef hash with a fried egg

Corned beef and potato hash fried crisp in patties, a fried egg on each.

Prep 7 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Boil the potatoes and shape the patties a day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 340g tin corned beef
  • 600g potatoes, boiled and cooled
  • 1 brown onion, finely diced
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Mash the corned beef roughly with the potatoes and onion, season and shape into 4 patties.
  2. Fry the patties in half the oil until crisp on both sides, 12 min.
  3. Fry the eggs in the rest of the oil, 4 min.
  4. Sit an egg on each patty.

Mains

Angel of Verdun chicken and leek pie

Chicken and leek in a creamy sauce under a golden puff pastry lid.

Prep 7 min, cook 53 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead; top and bake on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chicken thighs, diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 40g butter
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, then add the leeks and cook until soft, 8 min.
  2. Stir in the flour.
  3. Add the stock and cream and simmer until thick. Season and tip into a pie dish to cool a little, 15 min.
  4. Lay the pastry over, trim and brush with beaten egg.
  5. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden, 30 min.

Farmhouse beef and mushroom stew with mash

Chuck braised slow with mushrooms, carrot and thyme, over buttery potato, for the country hideout.

Prep 14 min, cook 2 hr 33 min · Make ahead: Make the stew up to 2 days ahead and reheat gently; mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 brown onion
  • 3 carrots
  • 250g Swiss brown mushrooms
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Toss the beef in the flour with salt and pepper and brown it in batches in the oil in a heavy pot, 12 min.
  2. Fry the chopped onion, carrots, mushrooms and garlic for 5 minutes, stir in the tomato paste, then add the stock and thyme and put the beef back in.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 2 hours until the beef is tender.
  4. Boil the peeled potatoes for 20 minutes, drain and mash with the butter and milk.
  5. Serve the stew over the mash.

Army brew with condensed milkNo alcohol

Strong black tea stewed in a big pot, sweetened with condensed milk, in metal mugs.

Prep 2 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Brew just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 1.2 L boiling water
  • 4 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  1. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water in a big pot until very strong.
  2. Stir a tablespoon of condensed milk into each mug and pour the tea over.
