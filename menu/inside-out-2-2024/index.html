Snacks

Cheese, crackers and fruit platterVegetarian

Cheddar, crackers, grapes, almonds, carrots, hummus. A real pickle, Bloofy.

Prep 16 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cube the cheddar and wash the grapes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; arrange the board on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 500g red grapes
  • 150g roasted almonds
  • 200g baby carrots
  • 200g hummus
  1. Cut the cheddar into cubes.
  2. Arrange the cheddar, crackers, red grapes, roasted almonds, baby carrots and hummus on one big board, filling every gap.
  3. Add a few more crackers just in case.

Joy's honey butter sunshine popcornVegetarian

Popcorn coated in honey butter, brown sugar and turmeric

Prep 14 min, cook 32 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 80g butter
  • 80g honey
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp flaky salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a lidded pot and tip into a large roasting tin.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, brown sugar and turmeric and bubble.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss well.
  4. Bake at 150C, stirring halfway.
  5. Spread on baking paper to cool and crisp.
  6. Sprinkle with flaky salt and serve in a big bowl.

Mains

Family taco night

Spiced beef mince tacos in crisp shells with toppings

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Cook the mince up to 2 days ahead and reheat; chop the toppings up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 100ml water
  • 12 taco shells
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Brown the mince with the cumin and paprika.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste and water and simmer.
  3. Shred the lettuce, dice the tomato and grate the cheddar.
  4. Warm the taco shells.
  5. Set everything out with the sour cream so everyone builds their own.

Sweets

Fire Hawks hockey puck browniesVegetarian

Fudgy brownies, a team Anger can get behind

Prep 20 min, cook 58 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 180g butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour and cocoa.
  4. Pour into a lined 20cm tin.
  5. Bake at 170C.
  6. Cool before cutting.
  7. Cut into squares.

Drinks

Oui-Oui iced chamomile apple teaVegetarianNo alcohol

Honey and apple juice over ice. Ennui says non.

Prep 6 min, cook 34 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chamomile tea bags
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 500ml apple juice
  • 4 cup ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water, then remove.
  2. Stir in the honey.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Mix with the apple juice and pour over ice in tall glasses.
