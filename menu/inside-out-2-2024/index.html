Snacks

Cheese, crackers and fruit platterVegetarian

Cheddar, crackers, grapes, almonds, carrots and hummus, a real pickle of a platter.

Prep 8 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Cube the cheddar and wash the grapes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; arrange the board on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 200g cheddar
  • 1 box water crackers
  • 500g red grapes
  • 150g roasted almonds
  • 200g baby carrots
  • 200g hummus
  1. Cut the cheddar into cubes.
  2. Arrange the cheddar, crackers, red grapes, roasted almonds, baby carrots and hummus on one big board, filling every gap.
  3. Add a few more crackers just in case.

Joy's honey butter sunshine popcornVegetarian

Popcorn coated in honey butter, brown sugar and turmeric.

Prep 5 min, cook 41 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 100g popcorn kernels
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 80g butter
  • 80g honey
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp flaky salt
  1. Pop the kernels in the oil in a lidded pot and tip into a large roasting tin.
  2. Melt the butter with the honey, brown sugar and turmeric and bubble, 5 min.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and toss well.
  4. Bake at 150°C, stirring halfway, 15 min.
  5. Spread on baking paper to cool and crisp, 15 min.
  6. Sprinkle with flaky salt and serve in a big bowl.

Mains

Family taco night

Spiced ground beef tacos in crisp shells with toppings.

Prep 30 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Cook the mince up to 2 days ahead and reheat; chop the toppings up to 1 day ahead and keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 600g beef mince
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 100ml water
  • 12 taco shells
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 200g cheddar, grated
  • 300ml sour cream
  1. Brown the mince with the cumin and paprika, 10 min.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste and water and simmer, 10 min.
  3. Shred the lettuce, dice the tomato and grate the cheddar.
  4. Warm the taco shells, 4 min.
  5. Set everything out with the sour cream so everyone builds their own, 4 min.

Camp cafeteria chicken broccoli pasta bake

Penne with chicken, green florets and creamy parmesan garlic sauce, baked, for lunch with Val.

How it is made
  • 400g penne
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 700ml full cream milk
  • 60g parmesan
  • 1 barbecue chicken
  • 150g mozzarella
  1. Penne mixed with chicken, broccoli and a creamy parmesan and garlic sauce, topped with cheese and baked at 200°C for 20 minutes. Cook 400g penne until 2 minutes short of al dente, adding 1 head broccoli in florets for the last 2 minutes, then drain. Melt 40g butter with 2 crushed garlic cloves, stir in 3 tbsp plain flour, then whisk in 700ml milk until thick and stir in 60g parmesan. Fold in the pasta, broccoli and 400g shredded cooked chicken. Tip into a baking dish, top with 150g grated mozzarella and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Sweets

Fire Hawks hockey puck browniesVegetarian

Dense dark chocolate brownies cut like hockey pucks, a team Anger can get behind.

Prep 14 min, cook 34 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 180g butter
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g plain flour
  • 30g cocoa
  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter, 6 min.
  2. Whisk in the sugar and eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour and cocoa.
  4. Pour into a lined 20cm tin.
  5. Bake at 170°C, 28 min.
  6. Cool before cutting, 30 min.
  7. Cut into squares.

Drinks

Oui-Oui iced chamomile apple teaNo alcohol

Chamomile tea, honey and apple juice over ice.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, 30 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 4 chamomile tea bags
  • 500ml boiling water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 500ml apple juice
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in the boiling water, then remove.
  2. Stir in the honey.
  3. Cool completely, 30 min.
  4. Mix with the apple juice and pour over ice in tall glasses.
