Snacks

Abe's delicacy devilled eggsVegetarian

Abe loves them rotten. These are fresh. Dijon and dill

Prep 15 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 8 dill sprigs
  1. Boil the eggs, cool and peel.
  2. Halve and mash the yolks with mayonnaise, Dijon and dill until smooth.
  3. Pipe back into the whites and finish with paprika and a sprig of dill.

Fireproof red chilli wings

Second date, no tongues. Wings glazed in honey and soy

Prep 5 min, cook 50 min · Make ahead: Salt the wings up to 1 day ahead and leave uncovered in the fridge; bake and glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30g butter
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 red chillies, finely chopped
  1. Toss the wings with salt.
  2. Bake at 220C until crisp.
  3. Warm the butter with honey, soy sauce and the chopped red chillies until sticky and toss the wings through until glossy.

Mains

Beef and beetroot borscht

Rasputin's back from 1944. Beetroot soup with dill

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 3 beetroot, grated
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1/4 small cabbage, shredded
  • 400g potatoes, diced
  • 1 1/2l beef stock
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Grate the beetroot and carrots, shred the cabbage and dice the potatoes.
  2. Simmer the beetroot, carrots, cabbage and potatoes in the stock until tender.
  3. Season with dill and vinegar.
  4. Serve in bowls with sour cream.

Sweets

Give-him-the-candy brown butter cookiesVegetarian

Myers brings Baby Ruth bars. These are cookies

Prep 50 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 150g butter
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp bicarb soda
  • 200g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Brown the butter until nutty, then cool.
  2. Beat with both sugars, the egg and vanilla.
  3. Stir in the flour, bicarb soda and chocolate, then chill.
  4. Chill the dough.
  5. Roll into sixteen balls and bake at 180C until golden at the edges but soft in the middle.
  6. Sprinkle with sea salt.

Drinks

Cat lover's strawberry malted milkVegetarianNo alcohol

Strawberries blended with milk, malt powder and vanilla ice cream

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Hull the strawberries and chill the milk up to 1 day ahead; blend to order
Recipe for 4
  • 250g strawberries, hulled, for blending
  • 800ml cold milk
  • 4 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  • 2 strawberries, halved, to finish
  1. Blend the hulled strawberries, milk, malted milk powder, honey and ice cream until smooth and frothy.
  2. Pour into chilled tall glasses and top each with a halved strawberry.
