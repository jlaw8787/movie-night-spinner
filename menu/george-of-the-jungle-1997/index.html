Snacks

Ape's jungle banana breadVegetarian

Cinnamon banana bread baked in a loaf tin

Prep 15 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 days, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 100g butter, melted, plus extra to serve
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 250g self-raising flour
  1. Mash the bananas with the sugar, melted butter, eggs and cinnamon. Fold in the flour until just combined.
  2. Pour into a lined loaf tin and bake at 175C.
  3. Cool a little, then slice thick and butter.

Safari tropical fruitVegetarian

Mango, pineapple, papaya and passionfruit with lime and torn mint

Prep 15 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 pineapple
  • 1/2 papaya
  • 3 passionfruit
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 tbsp torn mint
  1. Peel and cube the mangoes, pineapple and papaya into a big bowl.
  2. Scoop over the passionfruit pulp, squeeze over the lime juice and toss gently with mint.
  3. Chill for 20 minutes before serving.

Mains

San Francisco cioppino with garlic toast

Fish, prawn and mussel stew in fennel tomato broth, with garlic toast

Prep 25 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Base up to 2 days, add seafood fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 1 fennel bulb, sliced
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, 3 sliced and 1 halved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 400g white fish fillets, in chunks
  • 300g green prawns
  • 500g mussels, cleaned
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 loaf sourdough, sliced
  1. Soften the fennel, onion and sliced garlic in the oil with the chilli.
  2. Add the tomatoes and stock and simmer.
  3. Add the fish, prawns and mussels, cover and cook until the mussels open. Stir through the parsley.
  4. Toast the sourdough, rub with the cut garlic clove and serve alongside.

Jungle tree crash coconut puddingVegetarian

Coconut rice pudding with mango and toasted coconut. George not feel so good.

Prep 10 min, cook 150 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g arborio rice
  • 800ml coconut milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 1/2 cup shredded coconut, toasted
  • 1 mango, sliced
  1. Simmer the rice with the coconut milk, sugar and vanilla, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  2. Chill for 2 hours.
  3. Spoon into bowls and top with toasted coconut and fresh mango.

Shep's jungle smoothieVegetarianNo alcohol

Banana, mango, Greek yoghurt and honey blended thick

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Make fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2 bananas
  • 1 mango
  • 300g Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 handful ice
  1. Peel the bananas and mango and drop into a blender with the yoghurt, honey and ice.
  2. Blend until thick and smooth, adding a splash of water if too thick, then pour into tall glasses with straws.
