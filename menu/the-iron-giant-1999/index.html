Snacks

Annie's diner clam chowder

Clams, potato and bacon in creamy broth with crackers, Rockwell diner style.

Prep 13 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Base up to 1 day ahead; add the clams to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 4 rashers bacon
  • 1 brown onion
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 500ml fish stock
  • 400g potatoes
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1kg vongole (clams)
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 1 packet water crackers
  1. Dice the bacon, onion, celery and potatoes.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp, then add the onion and celery and soften.
  3. Stir in the flour, then add the stock, potatoes and bay leaf and simmer.
  4. Add the clams, cover and cook until they open, then pull most of the meat from the shells.
  5. Stir in the cream, warm through, season and serve with crackers.

Kent Mansley's devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs with mustard yolk and paprika, for Kent Mansley, the man from the government.

Prep 10 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, then cool in iced water and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs and mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  3. Pipe or spoon back into the whites, dust with paprika and scatter with chives.

Mains

Diner meatloaf

Beef and pork meatloaf with tomato glaze, served with mash and peas.

Prep 15 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Loaf up to 1 day, keep chilled and reheat
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 80ml tomato sauce
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 300g frozen peas
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Mix both minces with the breadcrumbs, egg, onion, salt and pepper.
  2. Shape in a tin, glaze with tomato sauce and bake at 180°C.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash with the butter. Cook the peas.
  4. Slice the loaf thick and serve with mash and peas.

Rockwell harbour lobster rolls

Lobster with mayo, lemon and chives in buttered toasted buns, potato chips and a pickle.

Prep 12 min, cook 4 min · Make ahead: Dress the lobster up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500g cooked lobster or crayfish meat
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick, finely diced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 4 hot dog rolls
  • 40g butter
  • 150g salted potato chips
  • 4 dill pickles
  1. Chop the lobster and mix with the mayonnaise, celery, a squeeze of lemon and chopped chives.
  2. Butter the outside of the rolls and toast in a hot pan until golden.
  3. Pile the lobster into the rolls and serve with chips and a pickle.

Dean's scrap metal peanut brittleVegetarian

Crunchy peanut brittle broken into jagged shards, brushed with edible silver.

Prep 4 min, cook 16 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 120ml liquid glucose
  • 80ml water
  • 250g roasted peanuts
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt
  • 1 tsp silver edible lustre
  1. Line a tray with baking paper. Heat the sugar, glucose and water in a heavy pan to 150°C on a sugar thermometer.
  2. Off the heat, quickly stir in the peanuts, butter and bicarb.
  3. Pour onto the tray and spread thin. Sprinkle with salt and brush lightly with silver lustre.
  4. Cool, then smash into jagged shards.

Diner pie bitesVegetarian

Muffin tin apple pies with cinnamon and a lattice top.

Prep 18 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples, diced
  • 50g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 sheets shortcrust pastry
  1. Cook the apples with the sugar and cinnamon until soft, then cool.
  2. Cut rounds from two pastry sheets and line a 12-hole muffin tin. Fill with the apple.
  3. Cut the third sheet into strips and lay a lattice over each.
  4. Bake at 190°C until golden.

Beatnik coffee shop biscottiVegetarian

Almond and orange biscotti baked twice until crisp.

Prep 10 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g almonds
  • 1 orange, zested
  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, eggs, almonds and orange zest into a dough.
  2. Shape into a log on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 170°C.
  4. Cool, slice and bake again, turning once.
  5. Cool completely to crisp up.

Hogarth's buzzing espresso milkshakeNo alcohol

Espresso, vanilla ice cream and malt blended thick, topped with whipped cream.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Use decaf for younger kids
Recipe for 4
  • 2 shots espresso coffee
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 3 tbsp malted milk powder
  • 1 can whipped cream
  • 1 small bag chocolate-coated coffee beans
  1. Brew the espresso and cool.
  2. Blend with the ice cream, milk and malted milk powder until thick.
  3. Pour into four tall glasses and top with whipped cream and chocolate-coated coffee beans, or sprinkles for little ones.
