Snacks

Detention sweet and salty snack mixVegetarian

Honey popcorn baked crisp and tossed with a bag of salty snacks.

Prep 17 min, cook 18 min · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 80g popping corn
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 20g butter
  • 170g ridged potato chips
  • 150g pretzels
  • 150g chocolate-coated peanuts
  • 200g sour lollies
  1. Pop the corn in the oil in a covered pot.
  2. Warm the honey with the butter and a pinch of salt and toss through the popcorn, 4 min.
  3. Bake at 160°C, then cool, 18 min.
  4. Toss with the chips, pretzels and chocolate peanuts in a big bowl and put the lollies in a small bowl alongside.

Bravestone's mango salsa and chipsVegetarian

Mango, red onion, chilli, coriander and lime salsa with corn chips.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 4 hours ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 2 mangoes
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 2 limes
  • 230g corn chips
  1. Dice the mangoes and the onion and chop the chilli and coriander.
  2. Mix with lime juice and salt.
  3. Rest so the juices come out, then serve with corn chips, 10 min.

Jungle bazaar chicken skewers

Thigh marinated in yoghurt, garlic and paprika, charred, flatbread, grab one before the motorbikes.

How it is made
  • 800g chicken thighs
  • 350g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 jar paprika
  • 1 jar ground cumin
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 8 skewers
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Chicken thigh marinated in yoghurt, garlic, paprika and lemon, grilled on skewers until charred, with garlic yoghurt dip and warm flatbread. Cut 800g chicken thighs into chunks and marinate for 2 hours in 150g Greek yoghurt, 2 crushed garlic cloves, 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, lemon juice and salt. Thread onto 8 skewers and grill on high for 10 to 12 minutes, turning, until charred. Mix 200g Greek yoghurt with a grated garlic clove, lemon and mint. Warm 4 flatbreads on the grill.

Jungle chicken curry

Chicken thighs in red curry coconut sauce with lemongrass and lime leaves.

Prep 5 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Curry up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 800g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 1 lemongrass stalk
  • 4 kaffir lime leaves
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar
  • 300g jasmine rice
  1. Fry the curry paste in the thick top of the coconut milk until fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken, the remaining coconut milk, bruised lemongrass, torn lime leaves, fish sauce and palm sugar.
  3. Simmer gently until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Cook the rice and serve alongside, 20 min.

Bravestone's chimichurri sirloin steak

Garlic, chilli and lime beef grilled hot and sliced, sharp parsley sauce, served with a smoulder.

How it is made
  • 1kg rump steak
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 red chilli
  • 1 lime
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 jar dried oregano
  • 1 jar chilli flakes
  • 1 bottle red wine vinegar
  • 1 bottle extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 40g butter
  1. Rump marinated in garlic, chilli and lime, grilled over high heat and sliced, with a sharp parsley chimichurri and buttered charred corn. Marinate 1kg rump steak in 2 crushed garlic cloves, 1 chopped chilli, the juice of a lime and 2 tbsp oil for 1 hour. Chop a bunch of parsley with 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp dried oregano, chilli flakes, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar and 100ml olive oil. Grill the steak 4 minutes a side and rest 8 minutes. Grill 4 corn cobs until charred and roll in butter. Slice the steak and spoon over chimichurri.

Mouse Finbar's cakeVegetarian

Dense chocolate layer cake covered in chocolate ganache.

Prep 10 min, cook 50 min, then 30 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Keeps 3 days
Recipe for 4
  • 350g dark chocolate
  • 200g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 150ml thickened cream
  1. Melt part of the chocolate with the butter, whisk in the eggs and sugar and fold in the flour, 15 min.
  2. Bake in two tins at 170°C, 30 min.
  3. Cool completely, 30 min.
  4. Melt the remaining chocolate with the cream into a ganache, 5 min.
  5. Layer the cakes and cover with the ganache.
