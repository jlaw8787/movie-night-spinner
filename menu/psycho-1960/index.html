Snacks

Norman's parlour sandwiches

Crustless ham and Swiss triangles on buttered white bread with Dijon

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Make the sandwiches up to 3 hours ahead, covered with damp paper towel in the fridge; cut off the crusts and cut into triangles just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 30g butter, soft
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 200g leg ham, sliced
  • 100g Swiss cheese, sliced
  1. Butter the bread and spread with Dijon.
  2. Fill with ham and Swiss cheese.
  3. Cut off the crusts and cut into neat triangles.
  4. Serve on a plate with a glass of milk alongside.

Mother's pickled onionsVegetarian

Pickling onions in sweet malt vinegar with peppercorns

Prep 10 min, cook 7 min, then 7 days to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the peeled onions in salted water overnight, then pickle and leave for a week before eating
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pickling onions
  • 500ml malt vinegar
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 tbsp salt, for soaking
  1. Peel the onions and soak in water with the salt overnight.
  2. Rinse and pack into a jar.
  3. Boil the vinegar with the sugar and peppercorns.
  4. Pour over hot, seal, and leave a week before eating.

Mains

Fairvale chicken and dumplings

Chicken, carrots and celery in a creamy broth with self-raising dumplings

Prep 25 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Make the chicken stew with the cream up to 2 days ahead, keep chilled; reheat and cook the dumplings on top on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 1/2l chicken stock
  • 100ml thickened cream
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 150ml full cream milk
  1. Dice the carrots, celery and onion.
  2. Simmer the chicken with the vegetables and stock.
  3. Shred the meat and add the cream.
  4. Rub the butter into the flour, then mix in parsley and milk.
  5. Drop spoonfuls on top, cover and cook.

Marion's lunch-hour steak sandwichOn the printed menu

Minute steak, caramelised onions, Swiss and horseradish mayo on rye, before Marion takes the cash

How it is made
  • 2 onions
  • 30g butter
  • 600g frozen shoestring fries
  • 4 minute steaks
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 jar horseradish
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Seared minute steak with caramelised onions, Swiss cheese and horseradish mayo on toasted rye, with a pile of shoestring fries and dill pickles. Cook 2 sliced onions in 30g butter over low heat for 20 minutes until golden. Fry 600g frozen shoestring fries at 220°C for 20 minutes. Season 4 minute steaks and sear in a smoking pan 1 minute a side. Lay a slice of Swiss cheese on each and cover for 30 seconds. Mix 80g mayonnaise with 1 tbsp horseradish. Toast 8 slices rye bread and build with mayo, steak and onions. Serve with fries and pickles.

Shower cap swirled meringuesVegetarian

Crisp white meringues piped in tall swirls

Prep 19 min, cook 8 min, then 90 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in an airtight tin, fill on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 3 egg whites
  • 180g caster sugar, for the meringue
  • 200g raspberries
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 40g caster sugar, for the raspberry sauce
  1. Whisk the egg whites and the caster sugar for the meringue over simmering water until hot and dissolved.
  2. Beat to stiff peaks.
  3. Pipe tall swirls onto lined trays.
  4. Bake at 100°C for 90 minutes, then cool in the oven.
  5. Blitz the raspberries with lemon juice and the caster sugar for the sauce and drizzle over.

Drinks

Parlour brandy milk punchVegetarian

Brandy, cold milk and vanilla shaken with ice

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Mix the milk, brandy, syrup and vanilla in a jug up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled; shake with ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 480ml full cream milk, cold
  • 180ml brandy
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup ice
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg, freshly grated
  1. Shake each drink with milk, brandy, syrup, vanilla and ice for 15 seconds.
  2. Strain into small glasses over a large ice cube.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top. Leave the brandy out for a plain vanilla milk.
