Snacks

Fried green tomatoesVegetarian

Cornmeal crusted green tomato slices fried golden, with a mayo dip.

Prep 15 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 green tomatoes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g fine cornmeal
  • 200ml vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp chopped pickles
  1. Slice the tomatoes thickly and salt lightly.
  2. Mix the mayonnaise, lemon juice and pickle for a remoulade.
  3. Dip the slices in beaten egg, then in cornmeal.
  4. Fry in hot oil until golden on both sides and drain on paper, 5 min.

Spiced pecansVegetarian

Pecans roasted with butter, brown sugar and cayenne.

Prep 8 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 week, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 300g pecans
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  1. Toss the pecans with the melted butter, sugar, cayenne and salt.
  2. Spread on a lined tray and roast at 170°C, stirring once, 12 min.
  3. Cool on the tray until glossy and crisp, then break apart, 8 min.

Re-enactment pan cornbread

Cornmeal and buttermilk batter baked in sizzling cast iron, with honey butter, for Mel's homecoming.

How it is made
  • 160g butter
  • 150g cornmeal
  • 120g plain flour
  • 1 bag caster sugar
  • 1 tin baking powder
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 jar honey
  1. Cornmeal, flour, buttermilk, egg and melted butter mixed briefly, baked in a hot cast iron pan at 220°C for 20 minutes, with honey butter. Put a 25cm cast iron pan with 20g butter into the oven at 220°C to heat. Mix 150g cornmeal, 120g plain flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder and half a teaspoon salt. Whisk in 300ml buttermilk, 1 egg and 60g melted butter until just combined. Pour into the hot pan so it sizzles and bake 20 minutes until golden. Beat 80g soft butter with 2 tbsp honey and a pinch of salt and serve with warm wedges.

Earl and Pearl's fried chicken

Buttermilk soaked drumsticks in paprika flour, fried crisp.

Prep 5 min, cook 48 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Soak the night before, fry fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour, for the dredge
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp plain flour, for the gravy
  1. Soak the drumsticks in buttermilk in the fridge overnight, which happens the night before and is not counted here.
  2. Dredge the drumsticks in flour seasoned with smoked paprika and salt.
  3. Boil the potatoes for the mash and mash with butter, 22 min.
  4. Fry the chicken in the vegetable oil at 170°C until crisp and cooked through, 18 min.
  5. Make a gravy by thickening the stock with flour in a saucepan.

Cornmeal fish and slaw

Catfish dipped in buttermilk and a cayenne crumb, fried crisp, tangy cabbage salad and hot sauce.

How it is made
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  • 100g cornmeal
  • 50g plain flour
  • 1 jar sweet paprika
  • 1 jar cayenne pepper
  • 700g catfish or flathead fillets
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 1/4 green cabbage
  • 1/4 red cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 jar whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 bottle apple cider vinegar
  • 2 lemons
  1. Catfish fillets dipped in buttermilk and crunchy cayenne cornmeal, fried crisp, with a tangy buttermilk slaw, hot sauce and lemon. Mix 100g cornmeal, 50g plain flour, 1 tsp paprika, half a teaspoon cayenne and salt. Dip 700g catfish fillets in 200ml buttermilk, then press into the cornmeal. Shallow fry in 1cm of oil at 180°C for 2 to 3 minutes a side until crisp. For slaw, toss a quarter each of green and red cabbage, finely shredded, and 1 grated carrot with 100ml buttermilk, 3 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp cider vinegar and 1 tsp sugar. Serve with lemon wedges and hot sauce.

Lightning glass pecan pieVegetarian

Pecans in a golden syrup and egg filling in a shortcrust case.

Prep 3 min, cook 70 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, serve at room temperature
Recipe for 4
  • 2 sheets of shortcrust pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g golden syrup
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 200g pecans
  • 300ml pouring cream, to serve
  1. Line a pie dish with shortcrust and blind bake, 20 min.
  2. Whisk the eggs with golden syrup, brown sugar and melted butter, 5 min.
  3. Scatter the pecans in the case and pour over the filling.
  4. Bake at 170°C until set, 45 min.
  5. Cool a little and serve with cream.

Pigeon Creek porch sweet teaNo alcohol

Black tea sweetened while hot, poured over plenty of ice.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 2 L water, half boiling and half cold
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Brew the tea bags in boiling water and remove the bags.
  2. Stir in the sugar while hot, then add cold water.
  3. Cool and pour over plenty of ice in tall glasses with lemon slices, 3 min.
