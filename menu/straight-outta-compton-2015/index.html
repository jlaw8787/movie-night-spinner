Snacks

Bread and butter fried chicken sliders

Cube tells Dre he's their bread and butter

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Soak the chicken in buttermilk up to a day ahead; flour and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, cut in pieces
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 8 soft slider rolls
  • 1/2 cup bread and butter pickles
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with a big pinch of salt.
  2. Toss in the flour seasoned with the paprika and cayenne until shaggy.
  3. Fry in oil at 170C until deep golden and cooked through.
  4. Fill the rolls with mayo, chicken and pickles.

Ruthless Records chilli cheese fries

We N.W.A. They Ruthless

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; bake the fries to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 750g frozen crinkle-cut fries
  • 400g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  1. Bake the fries at 220C until crisp.
  2. Brown the mince and onion, add the spices and tomatoes and simmer until thick.
  3. Pile the fries on a tray, spoon over the chilli and scatter with cheese.
  4. Grill until melted and finish with spring onion.

Mains

Compton carne asada tacos

Eazy raps Boyz-n-the-Hood

Prep 20 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Marinate the steak up to a day ahead; grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 700g skirt steak
  • 3 limes
  • 1 orange
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 250ml salsa verde
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  • 1 cup coriander, chopped
  1. Marinate the steak in the juice of 2 limes and the orange with the garlic, cumin, oil and salt.
  2. Grill over high heat, 3 minutes a side, rest it, then chop small.
  3. Warm the tortillas on the grill.
  4. Top with steak, salsa verde, onion and coriander, with lime wedges.

Detroit show coney dogs

Detroit bans the song

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Make the chilli up to 3 days ahead and reheat; grill the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 beef frankfurts
  • 8 split-top bread rolls
  • 300g beef mince
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 white onion, finely diced
  1. Brown the mince, breaking it up finely, then add the spices, tomato paste and a cup of water and simmer until saucy.
  2. Grill the franks until blistered.
  3. Warm the rolls, add the franks and spoon over the chilli.
  4. Finish with mustard and raw onion.

Sweets

Afterschool Special peach cobblerVegetarian

Eazy admits he likes Afterschool Specials

Prep 15 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Make the topping up to a day ahead and chill; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 825g tins sliced peaches, drained
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 80g cold butter, diced
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 500ml vanilla ice cream
  1. Toss the peaches with half the sugar, the cinnamon and cornflour in a baking dish.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and the rest of the sugar, then stir in the buttermilk.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over the peaches and bake at 190C until golden and bubbling.
  4. Serve warm with ice cream.

Drinks

Aftermath watermelon agua frescaVegetarianNo alcohol

Dre walks away to start Aftermath

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Blend and chill up to a day ahead; stir and pour over ice
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg seedless watermelon, cubed
  • 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml cold water
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 2 cup ice cubes
  1. Blend the watermelon with the lime juice, sugar and water until smooth, then strain.
  2. Pour over ice and mint in 4 tall glasses.
