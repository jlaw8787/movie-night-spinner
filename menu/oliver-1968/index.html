Fagin's toasting fork sausages

Pan-fried pork sausages on buttered toast with slow-cooked sticky onions and brown sauce.

Prep 0 min, cook 46 min · Make ahead: Cook the sticky onions up to 3 days ahead and keep them in the fridge; fry the sausages and toast on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick pork sausages
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 brown onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 4 thick slices crusty white bread
  • 30g butter
  • 80g brown sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Cook the onions slowly in 1 tbsp of the oil with the salt for 20 minutes until soft and golden, then stir in the brown sugar and vinegar and cook 5 minutes more until sticky.
  2. Fry the sausages in the rest of the oil in another pan over medium heat, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  3. Toast the bread and butter it.
  4. Pile the onions and sausages on the toast and serve with brown sauce.

Mr Brownlow's breakfast crumpetsVegetarian

Griddled crumpets with salted butter and golden syrup, Mr Brownlow's breakfast.

Prep 75 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Cook the crumpets up to 2 days ahead, cool and keep airtight, then toast them to order
Recipe for 4
  • 300ml milk
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 80g salted butter
  • 80ml golden syrup
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  1. Warm the milk with 100ml water, whisk in the yeast and 1 tsp of the sugar and leave until frothy.
  2. Beat into the flour and salt to a thick, smooth batter, cover and leave until bubbly.
  3. Whisk in the bicarb soda dissolved in 2 tbsp warm water.
  4. Grease 4 egg rings and a heavy pan with a little of the butter and cook the batter in the rings over low to medium heat until set and full of holes, then flip briefly. Repeat.
  5. Mix the rest of the sugar with the cinnamon and serve the crumpets hot with butter, golden syrup and cinnamon sugar.

Workhouse governors' roast chicken and bread sauce

Lemon and thyme roast chicken with roast potatoes and carrots.

Prep 0 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Infuse the milk for the bread sauce up to 1 day ahead; roast and finish the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg whole chicken
  • 60g butter, softened
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 500g carrots, in chunks
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 small brown onion, halved and studded with 4 cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 100g fresh white breadcrumbs
  • 1 pinch of ground nutmeg
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Rub the chicken with the butter and half the salt and pepper, push the lemon and thyme into the cavity and sit it in a roasting dish.
  2. Roast the chicken, adding the potatoes and carrots tossed with the oil and the rest of the salt and pepper for the last hour.
  3. Warm the milk with the studded onion and bay leaf, leave to infuse 20 minutes, then remove them and stir in the breadcrumbs and nutmeg. Simmer gently until thick.
  4. Rest the chicken 15 minutes, cook the peas and serve with the vegetables and bread sauce.

Three Cripples pie and mash

Beef and onion pies with mash and parsley liquor, Nancy leading Oom-Pah-Pah round the tables.

Prep 12 min, cook 2.5 hours · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; fill and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 700g beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 brown onions
  • 500ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sheets shortcrust pastry
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 tbsp malt vinegar
  1. Toss the beef in 1 tbsp of the flour and brown in the oil. Add the sliced onions for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the stock and Worcestershire, cover and simmer for 1 and a half hours until tender and thick. Cool.
  3. Line four pie tins with shortcrust, fill, top with puff pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 30 minutes.
  4. Boil the peeled potatoes for 20 minutes and mash with half the butter and a splash of the milk.
  5. Melt the rest of the butter, stir in the rest of the flour, whisk in the rest of the milk and simmer for 3 minutes, then blend with the parsley and vinegar for the liquor.
  6. Serve each pie with a scoop of mash and the liquor poured over.

Workhouse porridge bruleeVegetarian

Creamy vanilla and cinnamon oat pudding over stewed apples, with a crackling burnt sugar top.

Prep 0 min, cook 29 min · Make ahead: Stew the apples up to 2 days ahead; cook the oats and burn the sugar on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 3 apples, peeled and diced
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 600ml milk
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 4 tbsp caster sugar
  1. Simmer the apples with the brown sugar, cinnamon and 2 tbsp water until soft and split between 4 heatproof bowls.
  2. Simmer the oats with the milk, cream, sugar, vanilla and salt, stirring, until thick and creamy.
  3. Spoon over the apples, smooth the tops and leave to set.
  4. Scatter the caster sugar over the tops and burn with a blowtorch or under a very hot grill until glassy.

Artful Dodger's hot ginger cordialNo alcohol

Fresh ginger, lemon, honey and cinnamon simmered into a cordial, lengthened with hot apple juice.

Prep 1 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Make and strain the ginger cordial up to 5 days ahead and keep it in the fridge; heat with the apple juice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 60g fresh ginger, sliced
  • 3 lemons, 2 juiced and peeled, 1 sliced
  • 80g honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 L apple juice
  1. Simmer the ginger, lemon peel and juice, honey, cinnamon and 300ml water, then strain.
  2. Heat the apple juice until steaming and stir in the ginger cordial.
  3. Pour into 4 mugs and add a slice of lemon.
