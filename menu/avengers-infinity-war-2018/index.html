Snacks

Wakanda battle suya

Peanut spiced beef skewers, grilled hot.

Prep 17 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Grind the spice mix up to 1 week ahead and keep airtight. Coat and skewer the beef up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; grill just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 80g roasted peanuts, ground
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500g beef rump, thinly sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  1. Grind the peanuts with the paprika, ginger, chilli flakes and salt.
  2. Coat the beef in the spice and thread onto skewers.
  3. Grill hot until charred, 8 min.
  4. Serve with sliced red onion and tomato.

School bus ham and cheese pizza scrolls

Soft bread scrolls rolled with ham, cheddar and oregano.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 60g cold butter, cubed
  • 400g self-raising flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150g ham, chopped
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then mix in the milk to a soft dough.
  2. Roll out to a 30 x 40cm rectangle and spread with tomato paste.
  3. Scatter with ham, cheddar and oregano, then roll up from the long side.
  4. Cut into 12 slices and lay cut side up in a lined tin.
  5. Bake at 210°C until golden, 20 min.

Mains

Thanos's farm stewVegetarian

Pumpkin and chickpea stew with flatbread, for the sunrise on Thanos's farm.

Prep 20 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 1 brown onion, chopped
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g butternut pumpkin, diced
  • 2 tins of chickpeas, drained
  • 400g tin crushed tomatoes
  • 500ml water
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 4 flatbreads
  1. Soften the onion with the cumin in the oil.
  2. Add the pumpkin, chickpeas, tomatoes and water and simmer until the pumpkin is tender, 25 min.
  3. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
  4. Warm the flatbreads and serve alongside, 3 min.

Wakandan chicken with jollof and plantain

Thighs in ginger, garlic and chilli over coals, smoky tomato rice, fried plantain, hold the line.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 limes
  • 1 brown onion
  • 1 tube tomato paste
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1 jar smoked paprika
  • 1 jar curry powder
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 2 ripe plantains
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  1. Chicken thighs marinated in ginger, garlic, chilli and lime, grilled over coals, with smoky tomato jollof rice and fried sweet plantain. Marinate 1kg chicken thighs in 2 tbsp grated ginger, 3 garlic cloves, 1 chopped chilli, the juice of 2 limes and oil for 1 hour. Fry 1 onion, add 2 tbsp tomato paste, 400g crushed tomatoes, 1 tsp smoked paprika and 1 tsp curry powder, then 300g rice and 500ml stock. Cover and cook gently 25 minutes. Grill the chicken 6 minutes a side. Fry 2 sliced ripe plantains in oil until caramelised.

Infinity Stone fruit jelly cups

Six colours of fruit jelly layered in clear cups. All six stones, one snap.

Prep 30 min, cook 10 min, then 4 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 3 packets of jelly crystals (red, orange, green)
  • 6 tsp gelatine powder
  • 750ml apple juice
  • 250ml purple grape juice
  • 1 tbsp dried butterfly pea flowers
  • 250ml boiling water
  • 125g raspberries
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 150g green grapes
  • 1 can pineapple pieces, drained
  • 200g purple grapes
  • 125g blueberries
  • 200ml cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Make up the red, orange and green jellies as the packets say.
  2. Steep the pea flowers in the boiling water, strain, then dissolve some gelatine in each of the apple, grape and blue liquids, 10 min.
  3. Divide the fruit between glasses by colour: raspberries for the red, diced mango and drained pineapple for the orange, green grapes for the green, purple grapes for the purple and blueberries for the blue. Pour the matching jelly over each.
  4. Chill until set, at least 4 hours.
  5. Whip the cream with the vanilla and spoon on top to serve.

Drinks

The rabbit's pineapple ginger coolerNo alcohol

Pineapple, ginger and lime. Thor says the rabbit is the smart one.

Prep 15 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 1 pineapple
  • 1 5cm piece ginger, peeled
  • 2 limes
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 500ml cold water
  • 200ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Peel and chop the pineapple, then blend with the ginger, lime juice, honey and water until smooth.
  2. Strain through a sieve, pressing on the pulp.
  3. Chill for an hour.
  4. Pour into glasses of ice, top with soda water and garnish with mint and a pineapple wedge.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Thor lands in Wakanda with Stormbreaker and Groot.
