Snacks

Food court sausage rolls and potato skins

Puff pastry wrapped cocktail franks and stuffed baked potato skins

Prep 65 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Wrap the franks in pastry and bake the potatoes up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; bake the rolls and grill the filled skins on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 16 cocktail franks
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 800g small potatoes
  • 150g cheddar, grated
  • 150g bacon, diced and crisped
  • 150ml sour cream
  1. Wrap the franks in strips of puff pastry.
  2. Bake at 200C until golden.
  3. Bake the potatoes until soft.
  4. Halve the potatoes, scoop out a little and fill with cheddar and bacon.
  5. Grill until bubbling and serve both with sour cream.

Marinated olives and fetaVegetarian

Green and kalamata olives and cubed feta marinated in lemon peel and oregano

Prep 7 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g green olives
  • 150g kalamata olives
  • 200g feta, cubed
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 pita breads
  1. Toss the olives and feta with strips of lemon peel, oregano and olive oil.
  2. Leave to marinate.
  3. Serve in a shallow bowl with warm pita torn into pieces.

Mains

Fourth of July sticky pork ribs

Slow baked pork ribs glazed with barbecue sauce, with potato salad

Prep 25 min, cook 180 min · Make ahead: Bake the ribs covered up to 1 day ahead and chill, and make the potato salad up to 1 day ahead; brush with sauce, grill and cook the corn on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2kg pork ribs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150ml barbecue sauce
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 4 corn cobs
  1. Rub the ribs with salt.
  2. Bake covered at 150C.
  3. Brush with barbecue sauce and grill until sticky.
  4. Boil the potatoes, cool and dress with mayonnaise and spring onion.
  5. Grill the corn and serve it all picnic style.

Sweets

1984 food court cinnamon rollsVegetarian

Sweet dough scrolls with cinnamon brown sugar butter

Prep 20 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, warm before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 500g sweet bread dough or pizza dough
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100g cream cheese
  • 120g icing sugar
  1. Roll the dough into a rectangle and spread with butter mixed with sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Roll up and cut eight scrolls.
  3. Prove until puffy.
  4. Bake at 180C until golden.
  5. Ice with cream cheese beaten with icing sugar.

Drinks

Wishing stone champagne cocktailVegetarian

Sugar cube and orange bitters topped with sparkling wine or sparkling grape juice

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Nothing to cook; chill the sparkling wine, grape juice and flutes up to 1 day ahead and build each glass to order
Recipe for 4
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 4 dashes orange bitters
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 750ml sparkling grape juice
  1. Drop a sugar cube into each flute and add a dash of orange bitters.
  2. Pour sparkling wine for the grown-ups or sparkling grape juice for everyone else. Be careful what you wish for.
