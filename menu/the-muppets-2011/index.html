Snacks

Swedish Chef meatballs

Pork and beef meatballs browned and simmered in cream with allspice

Prep 26 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brown them and simmer in the cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pork mince
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1/4 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 100ml water
  • 120g lingonberry jam
  1. Mix the mince with the onion and allspice, then roll into small balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the olive oil.
  3. Add the cream and a splash of water and simmer.
  4. Serve on toothpicks with lingonberry jam.

Smalltown diner friesVegetarian

Thick fries fried twice, salted, served in paper cones

Prep 24 min, cook 16 min · Make ahead: Cut the potatoes up to 4 hours ahead and keep them in cold water; drain, dry and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  1. Cut the potatoes into thick fries, rinse and dry.
  2. Fry in the vegetable oil at 140C until soft.
  3. Rest the fries.
  4. Fry again in the oil at 190C until crisp.
  5. Toss with sea salt and pile into paper cones with tomato sauce on the side.

Mains

Steak frites with bearnaise

Seared sirloin, thin fries and tarragon bearnaise sauce

Prep 19 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Cut the potatoes and do the first fry up to 3 hours ahead; sear the steaks and make the bearnaise just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 3 tbsp tarragon, chopped
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 150g butter, melted
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Cut the potatoes into thin fries.
  2. Fry twice in the vegetable oil until crisp.
  3. Season the steaks with salt and pepper and sear.
  4. Rest the steaks.
  5. Reduce the vinegar with the tarragon.
  6. Whisk in the egg yolks over gentle heat.
  7. Slowly add the melted butter, whisking.
  8. Serve the steak over the fries.

Sweets

Green peppermint biscuit sliceVegetarian

Biscuit base topped with green peppermint icing

Prep 20 min, cook 11 min, then 2.5 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sweet biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted, for the base
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 1/2 tsp peppermint essence
  • green food colouring, a few drops
  • 200g dark chocolate
  1. Crush the biscuits and mix with the melted butter.
  2. Press into a lined tin.
  3. Chill the base.
  4. Beat the icing sugar with the softened butter, peppermint essence and green colouring.
  5. Spread over the base.
  6. Melt the chocolate and pour on top.
  7. Chill until set.
  8. Cut into squares.

Drinks

Rainbow Connection punchVegetarianNo alcohol

Cranberry, pineapple and orange juice layered and topped with lemonade

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Chill the juices and lemonade up to 1 day ahead; layer the glasses just before serving so the colours stay separate
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cup ice
  • 250ml cranberry juice
  • 250ml pineapple juice
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 500ml lemonade
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in the cranberry juice first.
  3. Slowly add the pineapple juice and then the orange juice over the back of a spoon so they layer.
  4. Top with lemonade.
