Snacks

Swedish Chef's flying meatballs

Pork and beef meatballs simmered in cream gravy, lingonberry jam on the side. Bork, bork, bork.

Prep 26 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Roll the meatballs up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brown them and simmer in the cream just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 250g pork mince
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1/4 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 100ml water
  • 120g lingonberry jam
  1. Mix the mince with the onion and allspice, then roll into small balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs in the olive oil, 8 min.
  3. Add the cream and a splash of water and simmer, 10 min.
  4. Serve on toothpicks with lingonberry jam.

Balcony box popcornVegetarian

Buttered popcorn in paper bags. Statler and Waldorf hated it, then finished the bag.

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Pop it up to 2 hours ahead and keep it loose in a dry bowl; bag it to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 150g popping corn
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 60g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 8 paper bags
  1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pot with three kernels until they pop, 3 min.
  2. Add the rest of the kernels, cover and shake over the heat until the popping slows, 4 min.
  3. Toss with the melted butter and salt and fill the paper bags.

Fozzie's pull-apart cheese cob

Round loaf stuffed with cheddar, mozzarella and garlic butter. Tear and share, wocka wocka.

How it is made
  • 1 cob loaf
  • 100g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 150g cheddar
  • 150g mozzarella
  1. A cob loaf cut deep in a crisscross, stuffed with cheddar, mozzarella and garlic chive butter, baked until bubbling for everyone manning the phones. Cut a cob loaf in a crisscross pattern about 3cm apart, going deep but not through the base. Melt 100g butter with 3 crushed garlic cloves and a handful of chopped chives. Push 150g grated cheddar and 150g grated mozzarella into the cuts and spoon the garlic butter over. Wrap in foil and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes, then open the foil and bake 10 minutes more until the cheese bubbles. Put it in the middle of the table and pull apart.

Mains

Animal's drumstick chicken

Sticky honey and soy chicken drumsticks, eaten with your hands. Drum solo optional.

Prep 11 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Coat the drumsticks in the glaze up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; roast just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 12 chicken drumsticks
  • 80g honey
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  1. Mix the honey, soy sauce, garlic and sesame oil and coat the drumsticks.
  2. Roast on a lined tray at 200°C, turning and basting twice, until sticky and dark, 40 min.
  3. Scatter with sesame seeds and spring onion and serve with napkins.

Miss Piggy's croque monsieur

Ham and gruyère grilled sandwich under bubbling cheese sauce. Moi insists on a knife and fork.

Prep 11 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese sauce up to 1 day ahead and keep it chilled; build and grill to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 thick slices of white bread
  • 200g sliced ham
  • 200g gruyère, grated
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 400ml milk
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 tsp grated nutmeg
  • 1 bag mixed salad leaves
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, whisk in the milk and cook until thick, 8 min.
  2. Stir in half the gruyère, the mustard and nutmeg.
  3. Make four sandwiches of bread, ham and a little sauce, top with the rest of the sauce and cheese, 6 min.
  4. Grill at 220°C until bubbling and browned, 7 min.
  5. Serve with the salad leaves.

Sweets

Kermit's green peppermint sliceVegetarian

Cookie base, green peppermint frosting and dark chocolate on top. It's not easy being green.

Prep 20 min, cook 11 min, 2 hr 15 min waiting · Make ahead: Up to 5 days, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g sweet biscuits
  • 100g butter, melted, for the base
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 50g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 1/2 tsp peppermint essence
  • green food colouring, a few drops
  • 200g dark chocolate
  1. Crush the biscuits and mix with the melted butter.
  2. Press into a lined tin.
  3. Chill the base, 15 min.
  4. Beat the icing sugar with the softened butter, peppermint essence and green colouring.
  5. Spread over the base.
  6. Melt the chocolate and pour on top, 6 min.
  7. Chill until set, 2 hr.
  8. Cut into squares.

Drinks

Rainbow Connection punchNo alcohol

Cranberry, pineapple and orange juice layered into a rainbow, lemon-lime soda on top.

Prep 12 min · Make ahead: Chill the juices and lemonade up to 1 day ahead; layer the glasses just before serving so the colours stay separate
Recipe for 4
  • 4 cups ice
  • 250ml cranberry juice
  • 250ml pineapple juice
  • 250ml orange juice
  • 500ml lemonade
  1. Fill tall glasses with ice.
  2. Pour in the cranberry juice first.
  3. Slowly add the pineapple juice and then the orange juice over the back of a spoon so they layer.
  4. Top with lemonade.
