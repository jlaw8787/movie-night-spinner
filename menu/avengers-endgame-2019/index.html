Snacks

Lake house chicken salad rolls

Poached chicken, celery and chives in mayo on soft buttered rolls.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 5 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 celery sticks, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 soft dinner rolls
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lettuce
  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 1 punnet of strawberries
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Poach the chicken in simmering salted water, then cool and shred, 20 min.
  2. Mix with the mayonnaise, celery, chives, lemon juice and seasoning.
  3. Split and butter the rolls and fill with lettuce and chicken salad.
  4. Toss the apple in lemon and pack in a basket with the strawberries.

Compound kitchen tacos

Hard shells with spiced beef, shredded lettuce, cheddar and tomato.

Prep 27 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp taco spice mix
  • 12 hard taco shells
  • 1 small lettuce, shredded
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  1. Brown the mince with the cumin and taco spices, 12 min.
  2. Warm the taco shells, 3 min.
  3. Shred the lettuce and dice the tomato.
  4. Fill the shells with the mince, lettuce, tomato and grated cheddar and lay them out.

New Asgard loaded potato skins

Potato skins baked crisp with cheese, bacon and sour cream, couch food in New Asgard.

How it is made
  • 6 medium potatoes
  • 1 bottle vegetable oil
  • 6 rashers bacon
  • 200g cheddar
  • 200ml sour cream
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 bottle hot sauce
  1. Baked potato skins scooped and crisped, filled with cheddar and crispy bacon, finished with sour cream, spring onion and a hit of hot sauce. Bake 6 medium potatoes at 200°C for 1 hour. Halve, scoop out most of the flesh and brush the skins inside and out with oil and salt. Bake cut side down for 10 minutes, then turn over. Fry 6 rashers bacon until crisp and chop. Fill the skins with 200g grated cheddar and bacon and bake 8 minutes until bubbling. Top with sour cream, sliced spring onion and a few drops of hot sauce.

Morgan's cheeseburgers

Smashed double cheeseburgers with pickles and onion, crinkle-cut fries.

Prep 17 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 4 tbsp pickles
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  • mustard, to serve
  • 1kg crinkle cut potatoes
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls.
  2. Fry the potatoes in the vegetable oil once at a low heat, then again until crisp, 20 min.
  3. Smash the balls thin on a smoking griddle, flip and top with cheese, 8 min.
  4. Toast the buns, 3 min.
  5. Stack two patties per bun with pickles, onion, sauce and mustard.

Camp Lehigh 1970 glazed meatloaf

Beef and pork under a sticky tomato glaze, mashed potatoes, peas and onion gravy, army mess style.

How it is made
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 bag breadcrumbs
  • 100ml milk
  • 2 brown onions
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bottle tomato sauce
  • 1 bag brown sugar
  • 1 bottle white vinegar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 box cornflour
  1. A 1970 mess hall meatloaf of beef and pork with a sticky tomato glaze, sliced thick with mashed potato, buttered peas and onion gravy. Soak 60g breadcrumbs in 100ml milk. Mix lightly with 500g beef mince, 300g pork mince, 1 grated onion, 1 egg, 1 tbsp Worcestershire and salt. Shape on a lined tray. Mix 100ml tomato sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tbsp vinegar, brush half over and bake at 180°C for 50 minutes, adding the rest halfway. Serve with mash, buttered peas and gravy from 1 sliced onion, 300ml beef stock and 1 tbsp cornflour.

Tony and Morgan's cakeVegetarian

Two-layer chocolate cake with fudge frosting and 3000 piped on top.

Prep 8 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200g butter, for the cake
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 100g butter, softened, for the icing
  • 1 tube red icing
  1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line two tins, 5 min.
  2. Melt the chocolate and the butter for the cake, whisk in the eggs and sugar and fold in the flour, 12 min.
  3. Bake in two tins, 30 min.
  4. Cool, then beat the icing sugar, cocoa and the softened butter into fudge icing, 15 min.
  5. Layer and cover the cake and pipe 3000 on top in red.

Lake house juice pop slushies

Frozen orange juice blended with mango, passionfruit and apple, vodka optional.

Prep 10 min, then 8 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Freeze the cubes the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 1 mango, chopped
  • 3 passionfruit, pulp
  • 200ml cold apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 120ml vodka, optional for adults
  1. Freeze the orange juice in ice cube trays overnight.
  2. Blend the juice cubes with the mango, passionfruit, apple juice and honey until slushy.
  3. Pour into tall cups with a straw. Stir vodka into any adult cups if you like.
