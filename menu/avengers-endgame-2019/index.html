Snacks

Lake house chicken salad rolls

Poached chicken, celery and chives in mayo on soft buttered rolls

Prep 25 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 5 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 celery sticks, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp chives, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 8 soft dinner rolls
  • 30g butter
  • 1 lettuce
  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 1 punnet of strawberries
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Poach the chicken in simmering salted water, then cool and shred.
  2. Mix with the mayonnaise, celery, chives, lemon juice and seasoning.
  3. Split and butter the rolls and fill with lettuce and chicken salad.
  4. Toss the apple in lemon and pack in a basket with the strawberries.

Compound kitchen tacos

Hard shells with spiced beef, shredded lettuce, cheddar and tomato

Prep 15 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Best served warm
Recipe for 4
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp taco spice mix
  • 12 hard taco shells
  • 1 small lettuce, shredded
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  1. Brown the mince with the cumin and taco spices.
  2. Warm the taco shells.
  3. Shred the lettuce and dice the tomato.
  4. Fill the shells with the mince, lettuce, tomato and grated cheddar and lay them out.

Mains

Morgan's cheeseburgers

Smashed double cheeseburgers with pickles and onion, crinkle-cut fries

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 4 tbsp pickles
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • tomato sauce, to serve
  • mustard, to serve
  • 1kg crinkle cut potatoes
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  1. Roll the mince into 8 balls.
  2. Fry the potatoes in the vegetable oil once at a low heat, then again until crisp.
  3. Smash the balls thin on a smoking griddle, flip and top with cheese.
  4. Toast the buns.
  5. Stack two patties per bun with pickles, onion, sauce and mustard.

Tony and Morgan's cakeVegetarian

Two-layer chocolate cake with fudge frosting and a piped heart on top

Prep 30 min, cook 60 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days
Recipe for 4
  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200g butter, for the cake
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 100g butter, softened, for the frosting
  • 1 tube red icing
  1. Heat the oven to 170C and line two tins.
  2. Melt the chocolate and the butter for the cake, whisk in the eggs and sugar and fold in the flour.
  3. Bake in two tins.
  4. Cool, then beat the icing sugar, cocoa and the softened butter into fudge frosting.
  5. Layer and cover the cake and pipe 3000 on top in red.

Lake house juice pop slushiesVegetarian

Frozen orange juice blended with mango, passionfruit and apple, vodka optional

Prep 15 min, cook 480 min · Make ahead: Freeze the cubes the night before
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml orange juice
  • 1 mango, chopped
  • 3 passionfruit, pulp
  • 200ml cold apple juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 120ml vodka, optional for adults
  1. Freeze the orange juice in ice cube trays overnight.
  2. Blend the juice cubes with the mango, passionfruit, apple juice and honey until slushy.
  3. Pour into tall cups with a straw. Stir vodka into any adult cups if you like.
