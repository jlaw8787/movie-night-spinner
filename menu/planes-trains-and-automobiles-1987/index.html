Snacks

Braidwood Inn vending machine mix

Salted peanuts, pretzels, chocolate bars and jerky tipped into a bowl, the motel snack haul

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 days in a sealed container
Recipe for 4
  • 60g salted peanuts
  • 60g pretzels
  • 60g chocolate bars, chopped
  • 40g beef jerky
  1. Chop the chocolate bars into chunks.
  2. Tip the salted peanuts, pretzels, chocolate and beef jerky into a shared bowl and mix gently.
  3. Divide between four people for the motel snack break.

Shower curtain ring onion ringsVegetarian

Thick onion slices soaked in buttermilk, dipped in beer batter and fried puffed and golden

Prep 20 min, cook 40 min · Make ahead: Slice and soak the onions in the buttermilk up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; whisk the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 2 brown onions
  • 150ml buttermilk
  • 120g plain flour
  • 150ml lager
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1l neutral oil, for frying
  1. Slice the onions into thick rings and soak in the buttermilk.
  2. Whisk the flour, lager and salt into a batter.
  3. Heat the oil to 185C.
  4. Drain the rings, dip in the batter and fry in small batches until puffed and golden.
  5. Drain and serve hot.

Mains

Thanksgiving turkey sliders

Brined roast breast in soft buns, cranberry, stuffing and gravy, worth the long trip home

Prep 45 min, cook 450 min · Make ahead: Turkey brines the day before. Cooked turkey keeps 2 days chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 700g turkey breast
  • 30g salt
  • 1l cold water
  • 150g stuffing mix
  • 20g butter
  • 20g plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 8 soft slider buns
  • 80g cranberry sauce
  1. Dissolve the salt in the water, submerge the turkey and refrigerate for 6 hours.
  2. Drain, pat dry and roast at 170C until the centre reaches 70C for 2 minutes.
  3. Make the stuffing mix with the packet water amount, spread in a small dish and bake beside the turkey, then cut into squares.
  4. Rest the turkey and slice.
  5. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for a minute, whisk in the stock and simmer for gravy.
  6. Fill the buns with turkey, cranberry sauce, stuffing squares and a spoonful of gravy.

Del's Wichita fried chicken and mash

Panko-crumbed breast, creamy mash, peppery white gravy and green beans, Del knows a place

Prep 25 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Crumb the chicken up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 2 large chicken breasts, halved and flattened
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g panko breadcrumbs
  • 150ml vegetable oil
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 300ml milk
  • 300g green beans
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  1. Boil the potatoes until soft, then mash with half the butter and 100ml milk.
  2. Dust the chicken in flour, dip in beaten egg and coat in panko. Fry until golden and cooked through.
  3. Melt the rest of the butter in the pan, stir in 2 tbsp of the flour, then whisk in the rest of the milk and the pepper for the gravy.
  4. Boil the beans and serve with the chicken, mash and gravy.

Sweets

Thanksgiving pumpkin pieVegetarian

Roast pumpkin purée with eggs, brown sugar, cream and warm spices in shortcrust, whipped cream

Prep 40 min, cook 140 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day chilled. Whip the cream on the day
Recipe for 4
  • 450g peeled pumpkin
  • 250g sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 2 eggs
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 200ml thickened cream
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • 0.13 tsp nutmeg
  1. Roast the pumpkin at 200C until soft, then puree.
  2. Line a 20cm pie dish with the pastry and blind-bake with paper and weights at 180C, then remove the weights and bake again.
  3. Whisk the puree with eggs, sugar, half the cream, cinnamon, ground ginger and nutmeg.
  4. Pour into the crust and bake at 170C until just set.
  5. Cool, whip the remaining cream softly and serve.

Drinks

Home for Thanksgiving apple cider punchVegetarian

Cloudy juice warmed with cinnamon, cloves and orange, bourbon for adults, those aren't pillows

Prep 10 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Simmer the juice and spices up to 1 day ahead, strain and keep in the fridge; reheat gently and add bourbon to the mugs
Recipe for 4
  • 1 1/2l cloudy apple juice
  • 3 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 120ml bourbon
  1. Put the apple juice, two cinnamon sticks, cloves, orange and sugar in a saucepan.
  2. Simmer gently for 15 minutes without boiling.
  3. Strain into a jug and pour into four mugs.
  4. Add bourbon to each adult mug and serve with a fresh cinnamon stick.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Neal and Del wake up holding hands.
