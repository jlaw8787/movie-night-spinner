Snacks

Stuffed Cherry TomatoesVegetarian

A dozen, filled with feta beaten smooth with basil, olive oil and lemon peel.

Prep 30 min
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cherry tomatoes
  • 150g feta
  • 1 handful basil, plus 24 small leaves
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Cut the tops off the tomatoes and scoop out the seeds.
  2. Blitz the feta with a handful of basil, the lemon zest and the oil until smooth.
  3. Pipe into the tomatoes and top each with a small basil leaf.

Dynamite Wings

Oven-crisped, tossed in honey, butter, lime and sweet chilli sauce, with hot pepper flakes and cilantro.

Prep 2 min, cook 53 min
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 limes, zested and juiced
  • 1 handful coriander
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Roast on a rack at 220°C, turning once, until crisp and cooked through, 45 min.
  3. Melt the butter with the honey, sweet chilli sauce, chilli flakes, lime zest and juice, 3 min.
  4. Toss the wings through and scatter with coriander.

Mains

Turkey & Swiss on a Kaiser

Roast breast, Swiss, iceberg, tomato, mayo and mustard. Wrapped to go.

Prep 15 min
Recipe for 4
  • 4 kaiser rolls
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 400g sliced turkey
  • 8 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Split the rolls and spread with mayonnaise and mustard.
  2. Fill with turkey, Swiss, lettuce and tomato and season.
  3. Wrap each tightly in paper and cut in half.

Sweets

Clown CakeVegetarian

Jam sponge under white buttercream, with a piped face and a cherry nose.

Prep 30 min
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sponge cake
  • 100g raspberry jam
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1 drop food colouring
  • 1 maraschino cherry
  1. Split the sponge and fill with jam.
  2. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and spread all over the cake.
  3. Tint a little buttercream with the food colouring and pipe a clown face on top.
  4. Press in the cherry for the nose.

Drinks

Bourbon Smash

Bourbon shaken hard with fresh mint, lemon and sugar, over crushed ice.

Prep 10 min
Recipe for 4
  • 12 mint leaves, plus 4 sprigs
  • 1 lemon, cut into 4 wedges
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Muddle the mint leaves and lemon wedges with the syrup in a shaker.
  2. Add the bourbon and ice and shake hard.
  3. Double strain into 4 glasses filled with crushed ice.
  4. Top with a sprig of mint and serve with short straws.

When to serve

Serve the mains when the cabbie who speaks no English drives them nowhere.
