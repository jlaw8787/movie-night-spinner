Snacks

Clown nose cherry tomatoesVegetarian

Hollowed little tomatoes with basil whipped feta, for a bank robber in greasepaint

Prep 30 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 3 hours, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 24 cherry tomatoes
  • 150g feta
  • 1 handful basil, plus 24 small leaves
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Cut the tops off the tomatoes and scoop out the seeds.
  2. Blitz the feta with a handful of basil, the lemon zest and the oil until smooth.
  3. Pipe into the tomatoes and top each with a small basil leaf.

Airport bar chilli-lime wings

Oven-crisp wings in spicy honey butter, coriander and citrus, so close to the departure gate

Prep 10 min, cook 55 min · Make ahead: Dry the wings and toss with the baking powder and salt up to 1 day ahead, uncovered on a rack in the fridge; roast and toss in the glaze on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 30g butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 limes, zested and juiced
  • 1 handful coriander
  1. Pat the wings dry and toss with the baking powder and salt.
  2. Roast on a rack at 220C, turning once, until crisp and cooked through.
  3. Melt the butter with the honey, sweet chilli sauce, chilli flakes, lime zest and juice.
  4. Toss the wings through and scatter with coriander.

Mains

Bodega sandwiches

Kaiser rolls of turkey, Swiss, tomato and mustard, for a city that won't let you leave

Prep 15 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours, wrapped
Recipe for 4
  • 4 kaiser rolls
  • 80g mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 400g sliced turkey
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1/2 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  1. Split the rolls and spread with mayonnaise and mustard.
  2. Fill with turkey, Swiss, lettuce and tomato and season.
  3. Wrap each tightly in paper and cut in half.

Sweets

Clown cakeVegetarian

Red nose and all

Prep 30 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 400g sponge cake
  • 100g raspberry jam
  • 200g butter, softened
  • 400g icing sugar
  • 1 drop food colouring
  • 1 maraschino cherry
  1. Split the sponge and fill with jam.
  2. Beat the butter with the icing sugar and spread all over the cake.
  3. Tint a little buttercream with the food colouring and pipe a clown face on top.
  4. Press in the cherry for the nose.

Drinks

Wheels-up bourbon smashVegetarian

A toast to finally leaving New York

Prep 10 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Cut the lemon and pick the mint up to 1 day ahead in the fridge; muddle and shake to order
Recipe for 4
  • 12 mint leaves, plus 4 sprigs
  • 1 lemon, cut into 4 wedges
  • 40ml sugar syrup
  • 180ml bourbon
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Muddle the mint leaves and lemon wedges with the syrup in a shaker.
  2. Add the bourbon and ice and shake hard.
  3. Double strain into 4 glasses filled with crushed ice.
  4. Top with a sprig of mint and serve with short straws.
