Snacks

The headwaiter's famous tossed salad

Cos and butter lettuce, anchovy parmesan dressing, egg, croutons

Prep 21 min, cook 17 min · Make ahead: Dressing and croutons up to 1 day ahead, toss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 1 bread roll
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 50g parmesan
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 1 butter lettuce
  1. Tear the bread roll into chunks, toss with olive oil and crushed garlic.
  2. Bake at 190C until golden.
  3. Cook the eggs, then cool and halve.
  4. Mash the anchovies with garlic, then whisk in Dijon, lemon juice, mayonnaise and parmesan.
  5. Tear the cos and butter lettuce into a big bowl.
  6. Toss with the dressing and croutons and top with the eggs and more parmesan.

Opening night gruyère gougèresVegetarian

Gruyère puffs for the Ziegfeld audition, where Hannah meets Nadine

Prep 25 min, cook 37 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 150g plain flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 150g gruyère
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  1. Bring the water, butter and salt to the boil.
  2. Beat in the flour until it forms a ball and cook.
  3. Cool before beating in the eggs.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then most of the gruyère, the Dijon and cayenne.
  5. Pipe small mounds on lined trays and sprinkle with the rest of the gruyère.
  6. Bake at 200C until puffed and golden.

Mains

Easter Sunday roast lamb with mint sauce

Garlic rosemary roast lamb leg, roast potatoes, peas, mint sauce

Prep 38 min, cook 159 min · Make ahead: Mint sauce up to 1 day ahead, roast fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb leg
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch of rosemary
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.2kg potatoes
  • 1 bunch of mint
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 4 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 20g butter
  1. Make small cuts all over the lamb and push in slivers of garlic and rosemary sprigs.
  2. Rub with oil and salt.
  3. Roast at 190C for medium.
  4. Rest the lamb.
  5. Peel the potatoes and parboil them.
  6. Roast the potatoes in oil alongside.
  7. For mint sauce, chop the mint with sugar, add boiling water and malt vinegar.
  8. Toss the peas in butter and serve alongside.

Couple of Swells chicken à la King

Breast, mushrooms and capsicum in sherry cream, puff shells, buttered peas, eaten in tramp costume

Prep 20 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Make the chicken mixture up to 1 day ahead and reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 700g chicken breast
  • 50g butter
  • 200g button mushrooms
  • 1 red capsicum
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 60ml dry sherry
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 8 vol-au-vent cases
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 bunch parsley
  1. Dice the chicken and cook in half the butter for 6 minutes. Lift out.
  2. Fry the sliced mushrooms and diced capsicum in the rest of the butter for 5 minutes, then stir in the flour.
  3. Whisk in the stock, cream and sherry and simmer for 5 minutes. Put the chicken back, take off the heat and stir in the egg yolk.
  4. Warm the pastry cases at 180C for 5 minutes. Cook the peas for 3 minutes.
  5. Spoon the chicken into the cases, scatter with parsley and serve with the peas.

Sweets

Hot cross bun bread and butter puddingVegetarian

Hot cross buns in custard. Don drums the whole toy shop for a bunny

Prep 15 min, cook 52 min · Make ahead: Assemble up to 4 hours ahead, bake fresh
Recipe for 4
  • 6 hot cross buns
  • 30g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 400ml full cream milk
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 orange
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam
  1. Butter the halved hot cross buns and layer them in a baking dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, cream, sugar, vanilla and orange zest and pour over.
  3. Stand for 15 minutes.
  4. Bake at 170C until golden and just set.
  5. Warm the apricot jam with a splash of water.
  6. Brush over the top and serve warm with cream.

Drinks

Easter bonnet pink fizz punchVegetarian

Pink cranberry grapefruit punch, for a peach (or a lemon)

Prep 22 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Base up to 4 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 500ml cranberry juice
  • 300ml pink grapefruit juice
  • 250g strawberries
  • 750ml sparkling wine
  • 1l lemonade
  • 1 bag of ice
  1. Slice the strawberries.
  2. In a punch bowl, mix the cranberry juice, grapefruit juice and strawberries.
  3. Chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Add plenty of ice.
  5. For the adults, top glasses with sparkling wine. For everyone else, top with lemonade.
  6. Garnish each glass with a strawberry on the rim.

When to serve

Serve the sweets when the Easter Parade fills Fifth Avenue.
