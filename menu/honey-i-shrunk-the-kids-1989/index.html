Snacks

Giant Cheerio puffed rice ring

Marshmallow puffed rice treat pressed in a ring mould, cut into wedges.

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, airtight
Recipe for 4
  • 60g butter
  • 250g marshmallows
  • 150g puffed rice cereal
  1. Melt the butter with the marshmallows over low heat, 6 min.
  2. Stir through the puffed rice until coated.
  3. Press firmly into a greased ring mould.
  4. Set, then turn out and cut into wedges at the table, 1 hr.

Mains

Giant pancakesVegetarian

One big buttermilk pancake cut into wedges, butter and maple syrup.

Prep 2 min, cook 28 min · Make ahead: Mix the dry ingredients up to 1 day ahead; whisk in the buttermilk and eggs and cook on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 500ml buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g butter
  • 120ml maple syrup
  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, buttermilk and eggs into a batter.
  2. Pour into a large buttered pan over low heat, cover and cook, 8 min.
  3. Flip with a plate and cook the other side. Repeat for the remaining batter, 15 min.
  4. Cut into wedges with butter and maple syrup.

Szalinski backyard burgers and corn

Beef patties grilled over coals with cheese and salad, buttered cobs, dodge the lawn mower.

How it is made
  • 800g beef mince
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 corn cobs
  • 30g butter
  • 1 cos lettuce
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • tomato sauce
  1. Juicy beef burgers grilled over coals with melted cheese, lettuce and tomato on toasted buns, with buttered corn on the cob and a crunchy green salad. Mix 800g beef mince with salt and pepper and shape into 4 patties. Grill over hot coals 4 minutes a side, adding a slice of cheese to each for the last minute. Grill 4 corn cobs for 10 minutes, turning, and brush with butter. Toss cos lettuce, cucumber and cherry tomatoes with a dressing of 3 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp vinegar and 1 tsp mustard. Toast 4 buns and fill with lettuce, tomato, patty and sauce.

Sweets

Giant cream cookieVegetarian

Two big dark chocolate cookies filled with vanilla buttercream.

Prep 8 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter, soft
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 large dark chocolate biscuits (about 20cm across)
  1. Beat the butter, icing sugar and vanilla until fluffy.
  2. Spread thickly over one dark chocolate cookie and sandwich with the other, pressing gently.
  3. Chill, then cut into wedges, 25 min.

Backyard s'mores

Toasted marshmallow and milk chocolate squashed between plain cookies.

Prep 4 min, cook 9 min · Make ahead: Best toasted fresh; nothing to do ahead except set out the biscuits and chocolate
Recipe for 4
  • 16 large marshmallows
  • 32 plain biscuits
  • 200g milk chocolate
  1. Toast the marshmallows on skewers over a gas flame or under the grill until golden, 8 min.
  2. Squash each between two biscuits with a square of chocolate.
  3. Leave briefly so the chocolate melts.

Drinks

Sprinkler watermelon pink lemonadeNo alcohol

Fresh watermelon and lemon juice topped with soda water.

Prep 15 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 800g seedless watermelon chunks
  • 4 lemons
  • 80ml sugar syrup
  • 400ml soda water
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Blitz the watermelon until smooth and strain into a jug.
  2. Stir in the juice of the lemons and the sugar syrup.
  3. Fill four tall glasses with ice, pour the mix two thirds of the way and top with soda water.
  4. Garnish with lemon wheels and mint.
