Snacks

Grilled haloumi with honeyVegetarian

Golden griddled haloumi with honey, thyme and lemon, eaten hot

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 2 blocks haloumi, sliced 1cm thick
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 lemon
  1. Slice the haloumi.
  2. Cook in a hot dry pan or griddle until golden and marked.
  3. Lay on a plate, drizzle with honey, scatter with thyme and squeeze over a little lemon.

Rosie's cookbook spanakopitaVegetarian

Spinach, feta and dill folded in crisp buttered filo triangles

Prep 35 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spinach
  • 250g feta, crumbled
  • 1 egg
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 10 sheets of filo pastry
  • 100g butter, melted
  1. Heat the oven to 190C. Wilt the spinach and squeeze dry.
  2. Mix with the feta, egg, spring onions and dill.
  3. Brush the filo with butter and cut into strips.
  4. Fold the filling into triangles.
  5. Bake until golden.

Mains

Wedding lamb kleftiko

Lamb shoulder with garlic and oregano, slow-baked in paper over potatoes

Prep 20 min, cook 250 min · Make ahead: Rest 15 minutes, open at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 6 cloves of garlic, sliced
  • 2 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 1 roll baking paper
  1. Rub the lamb with the garlic, oregano, lemon juice and oil.
  2. Sit on the potatoes and wrap tightly in baking paper, then foil.
  3. Roast at 160C until it falls apart.
  4. Rest, then open at the table so the steam rises.

Sweets

Loukoumades, Greek honey doughnutsVegetarian

Fried yeast dough puffs tossed in warm honey, cinnamon and chopped walnuts

Prep 20 min, cook 75 min · Make ahead: Best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1l vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g honey
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 60g walnuts, chopped
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and water to a sticky batter and rest until bubbly.
  2. Heat the oil.
  3. Drop spoonfuls into the oil and fry until puffed and golden.
  4. Toss in warm honey with cinnamon.
  5. Scatter with walnuts.

Drinks

Pepper's Villa Donna ouzoVegetarian

Ouzo over ice topped with lemonade and mint, kids' glasses without the ouzo

Prep 5 min, cook 0 min · Make ahead: Chill the ouzo and lemonade up to 1 day ahead; nothing else keeps. Build each glass over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml ouzo
  • 800ml cold lemonade
  • 2 cup ice
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice.
  2. Pour ouzo into each and watch it turn cloudy.
  3. Top with lemonade and a mint sprig. Make the kids' glasses with lemonade and mint only.

When to serve

Serve the feast when Donna and Sam marry on the island.
