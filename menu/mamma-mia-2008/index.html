Snacks

Grilled haloumi with honeyVegetarian

Golden griddled haloumi with honey, thyme and lemon, eaten hot.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Best served hot
Recipe for 4
  • 2 blocks of haloumi, sliced 1cm thick
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 lemon
  1. Slice the haloumi.
  2. Cook in a hot dry pan or griddle until golden and marked, 5 min.
  3. Lay on a plate, drizzle with honey, scatter with thyme and squeeze over a little lemon.

Rosie's cookbook spanakopitaVegetarian

Spinach, feta and dill folded in crisp buttered filo triangles.

Prep 25 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, reheat to crisp
Recipe for 4
  • 500g spinach
  • 250g feta, crumbled
  • 1 egg
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 10 sheets of filo pastry
  • 100g butter, melted
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Wilt the spinach and squeeze dry, 10 min.
  2. Mix with the feta, egg, spring onions and dill.
  3. Brush the filo with butter and cut into strips.
  4. Fold the filling into triangles.
  5. Bake until golden, 25 min.

Kalokairi taverna mezze plate

Tzatziki, smoky eggplant dip and whipped feta with pita, for three dads in the old goat-house.

How it is made
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic bulb
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 eggplant
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • olive oil
  • 150g feta
  • chilli flakes
  • 4 pita breads
  • dried oregano
  • 200g kalamata olives
  1. Thick garlicky tzatziki, smoky charred eggplant dip and whipped feta with chilli, with olives and grilled pita brushed with oregano oil. Grate 1 Lebanese cucumber, squeeze dry and mix with 250g Greek yoghurt, 1 grated garlic clove, dill and lemon. Char 1 eggplant over a flame until collapsed, scoop and mash with 2 tbsp tahini, garlic, lemon and olive oil. Blend 150g feta with 3 tbsp yoghurt and top with chilli flakes. Brush 4 pita breads with oil and oregano and grill. Serve with olives.

Wedding lamb kleftiko

Lamb shoulder with garlic and oregano, slow-baked in paper over potatoes.

Prep 12 min, cook 4 hr 8 min, 10 min waiting · Make ahead: Rest 15 minutes, open at the table
Recipe for 4
  • 2kg lamb shoulder
  • 6 cloves of garlic, sliced
  • 2 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 1kg potatoes, in chunks
  • 1 roll baking paper
  1. Rub the lamb with the garlic, oregano, lemon juice and oil.
  2. Sit on the potatoes and wrap tightly in baking paper, then foil.
  3. Roast at 160°C until it falls apart, 4 hr.
  4. Rest, then open at the table so the steam rises, 10 min.

Kalokairi charred octopus and Greek salad

Octopus charred with lemon and oregano beside a Greek salad.

How it is made
  • 1.2kg cleaned octopus
  • bay leaves
  • red wine vinegar
  • olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  • dried oregano
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 green capsicum
  • 100g kalamata olives
  • 200g feta
  • 200g fava dip
  1. Octopus simmered tender, then charred over high heat with lemon, oregano and olive oil, with a chunky Greek salad and fava dip. Simmer 1.2kg cleaned octopus in water with a bay leaf and a splash of vinegar for 50 minutes until tender, then cool in the liquid. Cut into tentacles, toss with oil and grill over high heat for 3 minutes a side until charred. Dress with lemon, olive oil and oregano. Toss tomato, cucumber, red onion, capsicum, olives and a slab of feta with oil and oregano. Serve with store-bought fava or hummus.

Loukoumades, Greek honey doughnutsVegetarian

Fried yeast dough puffs tossed in warm honey, cinnamon and chopped walnuts.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Best eaten warm
Recipe for 4
  • 250g plain flour
  • 7g dried yeast
  • 300ml warm water
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 150g honey
  • 1 pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 60g walnuts, chopped
  1. Mix the flour, yeast and water to a sticky batter and rest until bubbly, 1 hr.
  2. Heat the oil, 8 min.
  3. Drop spoonfuls into the oil and fry until puffed and golden, 12 min.
  4. Toss in warm honey with cinnamon, 3 min.
  5. Scatter with walnuts.

Pepper's Villa Donna ouzo

Ouzo over ice topped with lemonade and mint, kids' glasses without the ouzo.

Prep 4 min · Make ahead: Chill the ouzo and lemonade up to 1 day ahead; nothing else keeps. Build each glass over ice to order
Recipe for 4
  • 120ml ouzo
  • 800ml cold lemonade
  • 2 cups ice
  • 4 sprigs of mint
  1. Fill 4 glasses with ice.
  2. Pour ouzo into each and watch it turn cloudy.
  3. Top with lemonade and a mint sprig. Make the kids' glasses with lemonade and mint only.
