Bazooka popcorn chicken

Crunchy popcorn chicken with spicy mayo, fired out of a Klown bazooka.

Prep 16 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Marinate the chicken in buttermilk up to a day ahead; crumb and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, in 2cm pieces
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 50g cornflour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 L vegetable oil, for frying
  • 125g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  1. Soak the chicken in the buttermilk with a big pinch of salt.
  2. Toss the pieces in the flour, cornflour, paprika and garlic powder until shaggy.
  3. Fry in batches at 180°C until golden and cooked through.
  4. Stir the sriracha into the mayo and serve in paper cones.

Farmer Green's hot dog tacos

Charred franks in tortillas with pickled onion. Farmer Green thinks it's Halley's Comet.

Prep 16 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Pickle the onion up to 3 days ahead; char the franks to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 6 beef frankfurts, sliced lengthways
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 125ml apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 small bunch coriander
  1. Cover the onion with the vinegar, sugar and a pinch of salt and leave to pickle.
  2. Char the frankfurts in a hot pan until blistered.
  3. Warm the tortillas, sprinkle with cheese so it softens, and add the franks.
  4. Zigzag mustard and tomato sauce over and top with pickled onion and coriander.

Klown delivery pepperoni pizza

Crisp pepperoni pizza with tomato and mozzarella.

Prep 13 min, cook 22 min · Make ahead: Make the sauce up to 3 days ahead; stretch, top and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 400g tinned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250g mozzarella, torn
  • 120g hot salami, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 handful basil leaves
  1. Simmer the tomatoes with the garlic, oregano and a pinch of salt until thick, then cool.
  2. Stretch the dough into 2 rounds on oiled trays.
  3. Spread with sauce and top with mozzarella and salami.
  4. Bake at the hottest oven setting until the base is crisp and the cheese bubbles.
  5. Drizzle with oil, scatter chilli and basil and slice.

Cotton candy cocoon pavlovaVegetarian

Pink meringue with cream, berries and fairy floss, like the Klowns' cocoons.

Prep 25 min, cook 85 min · Make ahead: Bake the meringue up to 2 days ahead and keep it airtight; top with cream and floss just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 4 egg whites
  • 220g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 2 drops pink food colouring
  • 300ml thickened cream
  • 250g strawberries, halved
  • 125g raspberries
  • 30g pink fairy floss
  1. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, then add the sugar a spoon at a time until thick and glossy.
  2. Fold in the cornflour, vinegar and colouring and spoon into a 20cm round on a lined tray.
  3. Bake at 120°C, then turn the oven off and leave it to cool inside.
  4. Whip the cream, pile it on with the berries and wrap the top in fairy floss at the table.

Crazy straw cherry punch

Cherry vodka, cranberry and lime soda. Klowns sip victims through a straw.

Prep 8 min · Make ahead: Mix the vodka and juices up to 4 hours ahead; add the soda to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 160ml vodka
  • 300ml cranberry juice
  • 2 tbsp cherry syrup
  • 300ml lime soda
  • 8 maraschino cherries
  • 2 cups ice cubes
  1. Stir the vodka, cranberry juice and cherry syrup in a jug.
  2. Pour over ice, top with lime soda, add cherries and a crazy straw each.
