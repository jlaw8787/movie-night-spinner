Snacks

Junkyard sandwiches

Buttered white bread with mustard, ham, cheddar and pickles, wrapped to go.

Prep 10 min, cook 5 min · Make ahead: Make and wrap up to 1 day ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 30g butter, soft
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 200g leg ham
  • 100g cheddar, sliced
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  1. Butter the bread and spread with mustard.
  2. Fill with ham, cheddar and pickles.
  3. Cut in half and wrap each tightly in baking paper.

Trail walk devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs with mustard and paprika, packed for the walk along the tracks.

Prep 18 min, cook 12 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill up to a day ahead, covered in the fridge; dust with paprika to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 bunch chives
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes.
  2. Cool them in iced water, then peel.
  3. Halve the eggs lengthways and tip the yolks into a bowl.
  4. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt and pepper until smooth.
  5. Spoon the filling back into the whites and dust with paprika and snipped chives.

Mains

Railway line hamburger patties

Thick beef patties on white bread with soft fried onions and tomato sauce.

Prep 10 min, cook 25 min · Make ahead: Season and shape the patties up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; cook the onions and patties on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 800g beef mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  1. Season the mince and shape into thick patties.
  2. Fry the onions in oil until soft and sweet.
  3. Cook the patties over hot coals or in a smoking pan until cooked through, turning once.
  4. Serve between thick slices of white bread with the onions and tomato sauce.

Pan-charred franks with onion relishOn the printed menu

Smoky sausages blackened in a skillet, soft buns, jammy onion, mustard and pickles.

How it is made
  • 3 onions
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 8 franks
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 1 jar American mustard
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  1. Smoky franks charred in a hot pan, tucked into soft buns with sticky onion relish, mustard and crunchy pickles. Slice 3 onions thinly and cook in 30g butter over low heat for 25 minutes until jammy, then add 1 tbsp brown sugar and 2 tbsp cider vinegar and cook 2 minutes more. Score 8 franks and cook in a hot cast iron pan for 6 minutes, turning, until blistered. Warm 8 hot dog buns in the pan. Fill each bun with a frank, a spoon of onion relish, mustard and sliced pickles.

Sweets

Campfire marshmallows

Toasted marshmallows dipped in dark chocolate, squashed between digestive biscuits.

Prep 2 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Melt the chocolate up to 1 day ahead and re-warm gently; toast the marshmallows fresh over the fire
Recipe for 4
  • 16 marshmallows
  • 100g dark chocolate, melted
  • 32 digestive biscuits
  1. Melt the chocolate in a bowl over hot water.
  2. Toast the marshmallows on sticks over the fire or a gas flame until blistered.
  3. Dip in the chocolate and squash between two biscuits. Eat while still gooey.

Blueberry pieVegetarian

Lattice topped blueberry pie, cooled before slicing.

Prep 25 min, cook 50 min, then 3 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep covered
Recipe for 4
  • 750g blueberries
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 4 sheets sweet shortcrust pastry
  1. Toss the blueberries with sugar, lemon juice and cornflour.
  2. Line a pie dish with two pastry sheets and fill.
  3. Cut the remaining sheets into strips and weave a lattice on top.
  4. Bake at 190°C until deep golden and bubbling.
  5. Cool completely before cutting, at least 3 hours.

Homemade cherry gummiesOn the printed menu

Soft chews of real cherry juice and gelatine in sour sugar, for the Pez debate.

How it is made
  • 350ml cherry juice
  • 130g caster sugar
  • 30g powdered gelatine
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 jar citric acid
  1. Soft cherry gummies made with real cherry juice and gelatine, rolled in sour sugar, the kind of lolly the boys argue about by the fire. Heat 250ml cherry juice with 80g caster sugar until dissolved. Sprinkle 30g powdered gelatine over 100ml cold cherry juice, leave 5 minutes, then stir into the hot juice until clear. Add 1 tbsp lemon juice and pour into a lightly oiled 20cm square tin. Chill 3 hours until firm. Cut into squares and roll in 50g caster sugar mixed with 1 tsp citric acid.

Castle Rock sarsaparilla floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Sarsaparilla poured over vanilla ice cream.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the glasses in the freezer up to 1 hour ahead; scoop and pour to order
Recipe for 4
  • 1 L vanilla ice cream
  • 1.25 L sarsaparilla, cold
  1. Chill four tall glasses in the freezer.
  2. Scoop the ice cream into each glass.
  3. Pour the sarsaparilla slowly down the side, let the foam settle, then top up. Serve with a straw and long spoon.

When to serve

Serve dessert when Gordie tells the pie-eating contest story at the campfire.
