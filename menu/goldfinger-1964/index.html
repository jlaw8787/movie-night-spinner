Golden Welsh rarebit fingersVegetarian

Mustard cheese sauce grilled golden on toast fingers, the savoury after Bond's round.

Prep 1 min, cook 13 min · Make ahead: Make the cheese mix up to 2 days ahead and chill; toast and grill to order
Recipe for 4
  • 20g butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 100ml milk
  • 200g sharp cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 6 slices of white bread
  1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then the milk, and cook until thick, 4 min.
  2. Off the heat stir in the cheddar, mustard and Worcestershire, 3 min.
  3. Toast the bread, spread thickly with the cheese and grill until bubbling and golden, 6 min.
  4. Cut into fingers and serve hot.

Seventeenth hole crab salad cups

Crab salad with celery, chives and lemon in baby cos lettuce leaves.

Prep 20 min · Make ahead: Up to 2 hours ahead, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 300g crab meat
  • 3 tbsp whole egg mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stick
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 pinch paprika
  • 2 baby cos lettuce
  1. Finely dice the celery and chop the chives.
  2. Mix the crab with mayonnaise, celery, chives, lemon zest and juice and paprika.
  3. Spoon into baby cos leaves and serve cold.

Clubhouse prawn toasts

Sesame prawn triangles fried crisp, with hot mustard for dipping.

How it is made
  • 300g green prawns, peeled
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • 1 piece fresh ginger
  • 1 egg
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • 4 slices white bread
  • 50g sesame seeds
  • 1L vegetable oil
  • 4 slices rye bread
  • 30g butter
  • 150g smoked salmon
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 jar capers
  • 1 jar hot English mustard
  • 1 lime
  1. Crisp sesame prawn toasts with hot English mustard and lime, alongside smoked salmon on buttered rye with dill and capers, like a 1960s clubhouse spread. Blitz 300g peeled green prawns with a spring onion, grated ginger, 1 egg white, 1 tsp soy and salt. Spread on 4 slices white bread, press into 50g sesame seeds and cut into triangles. Fry at 180°C for 2 minutes a side. Butter 4 slices rye, top with 150g smoked salmon, dill and capers and cut into fingers. Serve with sweet chilli sauce mixed with lime juice.

Mains

Auric's peppered sirloin

Seared sirloin in bourbon pepper cream sauce, with roast potatoes.

Prep 3 min, cook 1 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Cut the potatoes up to 1 day ahead and keep them in cold water in the fridge, dried well before roasting; sear the steaks and make the sauce on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 sirloin steaks
  • 60ml bourbon
  • 1 tsp crushed black peppercorns
  • 125ml beef stock
  • 200ml thickened cream
  1. Roast the potatoes in oil at 220°C, 45 min.
  2. Sear the steaks hard and rest, 12 min.
  3. Deglaze the pan with bourbon and the crushed black peppercorns.
  4. Add the stock and cream and reduce, 10 min.
  5. Serve the steaks with potatoes and sauce.

Grand Slam Dover sole with lemon butter

Pan-fried sole in brown butter with lemon and parsley.

How it is made
  • 4 sole or flathead fillets
  • plain flour
  • 80g butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 750g new potatoes
  1. Dust 4 sole fillets (or flathead) in seasoned flour and fry in 40g butter for 2 minutes a side until golden. Lift out, add 40g more butter to the pan and cook until nutty, then add the juice of a lemon and chopped parsley. Pour over the fish and serve with boiled new potatoes.

Sweets

Jill's gold trufflesVegetarian

Dark chocolate truffles rolled in cocoa and edible gold dust.

Prep 12 min, cook 8 min, 3 hr waiting · Make ahead: Keeps 1 week in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 250g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 jar edible gold lustre dust
  1. Heat the cream and pour over the chopped chocolate. Stir smooth, 8 min.
  2. Chill, 3 hr.
  3. Roll into balls and dust in cocoa.
  4. Roll in edible gold lustre until they shine.

Drinks

Mint julep

Bourbon, mint and sugar syrup over crushed ice in a frosted cup.

Prep 10 min · Make ahead: Make the sugar syrup up to 2 weeks ahead in the fridge; press the mint and build the cups to order
Recipe for 4
  • 240ml bourbon
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 60ml sugar syrup
  • 1 bag crushed ice
  1. Gently press the mint leaves with sugar syrup in metal cups.
  2. Add the bourbon and pack with crushed ice.
  3. Stir until the cup frosts and top with a big mint sprig.
