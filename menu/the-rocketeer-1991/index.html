Bulldog Cafe onion ringsVegetarian

Buttermilk soaked onion rings fried in a lager batter

Prep 35 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Soak the onion rings in the buttermilk up to 4 hours ahead in the fridge; whisk the batter and fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 3 brown onions
  • 300ml buttermilk
  • 150g plain flour
  • 250ml cold lager
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • salt, to taste
  1. Cut the onions into thick rings and soak in the buttermilk.
  2. Whisk the flour with the cold lager.
  3. Dip the rings in the batter and fry in the vegetable oil at 185°C in batches until puffed and golden.
  4. Salt and serve hot.

South Seas Club canapés

Coconut panko prawns with pineapple, red onion, chilli and lime salsa

Prep 12 min, cook 23 min · Make ahead: Make the pineapple salsa and crumb the prawns up to 3 hours ahead, chilled; fry just before serving
Recipe for 4
  • 400g green prawns, peeled
  • 80g plain flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 60g shredded coconut
  • 60g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1l vegetable oil
  • 1/4 pineapple, diced
  • 1 small red onion, diced
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 1 lime
  1. Mix the coconut and panko. Dip the prawns in flour, egg, then the crumb mix.
  2. Dice the pineapple, red onion and chilli and mix with lime juice for a salsa.
  3. Fry the prawns in the vegetable oil at 180°C in batches.
  4. Serve together.

Chaplin Field egg and bacon rollsOn the printed menu

Soft buns of egg mayo, crisp rashers and chives, wrapped in paper for the air show

How it is made
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tsp mild mustard
  • 1 bunch chives
  • 6 bacon rashers
  • 8 soft milk rolls
  • paprika
  1. Soft milk rolls stuffed with creamy egg salad, crisp bacon, chives and a pinch of paprika, wrapped in baking paper like a pilot's lunch. Boil 8 eggs for 10 minutes, cool in iced water, peel and chop. Mix with 4 tbsp mayo, 1 tsp mustard, 2 tbsp chives, salt and pepper. Grill 6 bacon rashers until crisp and crumble. Split 8 soft milk rolls, fill with egg salad, scatter with bacon and a pinch of paprika. Wrap each in baking paper and twist the ends.

South Seas Club tiki chicken

Pineapple and soy marinated chicken thighs glazed, with coconut rice

Prep 5 min, cook 46 min, then 2 hours to rest or set · Make ahead: Marinate up to 1 day ahead
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 250ml pineapple juice
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 1 knob ginger, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 300g jasmine rice
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 200ml water
  1. Marinate the chicken in the pineapple juice, soy, ginger and garlic.
  2. Grill the chicken until cooked through, turning.
  3. Simmer the marinade until reduced to a glaze and brush over.
  4. Cook the rice in the coconut milk and water.
  5. Serve the chicken over the rice.

Bulldog Cafe cheeseburger and chipsOn the printed menu

Thick patty, melted cheddar, lettuce, pickles and tangy sauce on a toasted bun, twice-fried spuds

How it is made
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 800g chuck mince
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 tbsp mayo
  • tomato sauce
  • 1 jar dill pickles
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 litres vegetable oil
  1. Thick patty griddled to medium with melted cheddar, lettuce, tomato, pickles and a tangy special sauce on a toasted bun, double-fried chips alongside. Cut 1kg potatoes into chips, soak 30 minutes, dry and fry at 140°C for 6 minutes. Shape 800g chuck mince into 4 thick patties and season. Griddle 4 minutes a side, add 2 slices cheddar each and cover 1 minute. Mix 4 tbsp mayo with 1 tbsp tomato sauce and 1 tbsp chopped pickles. Fry the chips at 190°C for 3 minutes and salt. Toast 4 buns and build with sauce, lettuce, tomato, patty and pickles.

Rocket sundaeVegetarian

Vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, whipped cream and a cherry, with a sparkler

Prep 9 min, cook 6 min · Make ahead: Sauce up to 2 days, reheat gently
Recipe for 4
  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 400ml thickened cream
  • 1l vanilla ice cream
  • 4 maraschino cherries
  • 4 sparklers
  1. Melt the chocolate with some of the cream into a fudge sauce.
  2. Whip the rest of the cream.
  3. Scoop the ice cream into four tall glasses and pour over the fudge.
  4. Top with whipped cream and a cherry. Push a sparkler into each and light it at the table.

Bulldog Cafe root beer malt floatVegetarianNo alcohol

Root beer over vanilla ice cream with a spoon of malted milk powder

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the mugs first
Recipe for 4
  • 1l root beer
  • 8 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4 tsp malted milk powder
  1. Put four mugs in the freezer.
  2. Add two scoops of ice cream and a teaspoon of malt powder to each.
  3. Pour the cold root beer slowly down the side so the foam rises without spilling. Serve with long spoons and straws.

When to serve

Serve the mains when Cliff and Peevy eat at the Bulldog Cafe.
