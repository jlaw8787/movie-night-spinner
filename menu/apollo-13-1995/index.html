Snacks

Moon landing party devilled eggsVegetarian

Devilled eggs piped high and dusted with paprika.

Prep 13 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Boil and fill the eggs up to 6 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge
Recipe for 4
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp chives, snipped
  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 10 minutes, then cool in iced water.
  2. Peel and halve the eggs and mash the yolks with the mayo, mustard, vinegar and salt.
  3. Pipe or spoon back into the whites, dust with paprika and scatter with chives.

Square-peg ravioli in a round bowlVegetarian

Ricotta ravioli in hot parmesan broth.

Prep 3 min, cook 14 min · Make ahead: Make the broth up to 2 days ahead; cook the ravioli to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 375g fresh square ricotta and spinach ravioli
  • 1 L vegetable stock
  • 40g parmesan rind or block, plus extra to grate
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup basil leaves
  1. Simmer the stock with the parmesan and garlic for 10 minutes, then fish out the rind.
  2. Drop in the ravioli and cook until they float.
  3. Ladle into round bowls, add a squeeze of lemon, oil, basil and grated parmesan.

Mains

Mission Control smoked brisket

Texas brisket, sliced thick with pickles and white bread.

Prep 10 min, cook 4.5 hours · Make ahead: Cook the brisket up to 2 days ahead and chill in its juices; slice and reheat covered to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1.8kg beef brisket
  • 2 tbsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 250ml beef stock
  • 8 slices white bread
  • 1 cup dill pickle slices
  • 1 white onion, thinly sliced
  • 125ml smoky barbecue sauce
  1. Rub the brisket all over with the pepper, salt, paprika and garlic powder.
  2. Sit it fat side up in a roasting tin, pour the stock around, cover tightly with foil and cook at 140°C.
  3. Uncover and roast at 200°C until the bark darkens.
  4. Rest for 20 minutes, then slice across the grain.
  5. Serve with white bread, pickles, onion and barbecue sauce.

Aquarius lifeboat chicken pot pies

Chicken and leek under a golden puff lid, hot enough for a freezing lifeboat.

Prep 5 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and chill; top with pastry and bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 30g butter
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 375ml chicken stock
  • 125ml thickened cream
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 sheets frozen puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  1. Brown the chicken in the butter, then add the leek and carrot and cook until soft.
  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock and cream, and simmer until thick.
  3. Stir in the peas, season and spoon into 4 pie dishes.
  4. Top with pastry, brush with egg and bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.

Odyssey heat shield crème brûléeVegetarian

Vanilla custard under a scorched sugar crust.

Prep 8 min, cook 45 min · Make ahead: Bake the custards up to 2 days ahead and chill; burn the sugar to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 600ml pouring cream
  • 1 vanilla bean, split
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 80g caster sugar, plus 4 tbsp for the tops
  1. Warm the cream with the vanilla seeds and pod until steaming, then take off the heat.
  2. Whisk the yolks and 80g sugar, then whisk in the cream and strain into 4 ramekins.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 150°C until just set with a wobble, then chill.
  4. Sprinkle each with sugar and scorch with a blowtorch or under a hot grill until dark amber.

Lovell's moon night champagne

Brut sparkling with a sugar cube and bitters.

Prep 5 min · Make ahead: Chill the sparkling well; build to order
Recipe for 4
  • 750ml brut sparkling wine
  • 4 sugar cubes
  • 1 tsp Angostura bitters
  • 1 orange
  1. Drop a sugar cube into each of 4 flutes and shake a dash of bitters onto each.
  2. Top slowly with the cold sparkling and add a twist of orange peel.
