Snacks

Ham and gruyère crêpes

Thin crêpes folded around ham and melted gruyère.

Prep 8 min, cook 23 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Batter up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 375ml milk
  • 30g butter, for the pan
  • 150g leg ham
  • 150g gruyère, grated
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  1. Whisk the flour, eggs and milk into a smooth batter.
  2. Rest the batter.
  3. Cook thin crepes in a pan, brushing it with the butter between each one.
  4. Fill half with ham and gruyère, fold and warm until melted.
  5. Sprinkle the rest with sugar and lemon juice and fold into quarters.

Brie, fig jam and tomato crostiniVegetarian

Toasts topped with brie and fig jam, and tomato with basil, two bites each.

Prep 15 min, cook 8 min · Make ahead: Toast the slices up to 1 day ahead, airtight; top on the night, adding the tomato last
Recipe for 4
  • 1 baguette
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150g brie
  • 2 tbsp fig jam
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup basil leaves
  • salt, to taste
  1. Slice the baguette and brush with oil.
  2. Toast the slices until golden.
  3. Top half with brie and fig jam.
  4. Top the rest with chopped tomato, torn basil and salt.

Hugo, Victor and Laverne's croque bites

Ham and cheese bites on sticks, cut for the Feast of Fools.

How it is made
  • 40g butter
  • 1 packet plain flour
  • 200ml milk
  • 1 jar ground nutmeg
  • 1 loaf white bread
  • 1 jar Dijon mustard
  • 150g sliced ham
  • 140g gruyère
  • 1 jar cornichons
  1. Little ham and gruyère toasties smothered in béchamel and grilled until bubbling and blistered, cut into bites with cornichons. Melt 20g butter, stir in 1 tbsp plain flour, then whisk in 200ml milk and cook until thick, and season with nutmeg. Spread 4 slices of bread with Dijon mustard, top with 150g ham and 80g grated gruyère, and close with 4 more slices. Toast in a buttered pan for 2 minutes a side. Spread the tops with béchamel, scatter with 60g more gruyère and grill until bubbling. Cut into 6 bites each.

Mains

Bell tower beef and mushroom braise

Beef, bacon and mushrooms braised slowly in a rich gravy.

Prep 51 min, cook 3.5 hours · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, reheats well
Recipe for 4
  • 150g bacon, diced
  • 1.2kg beef chuck, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 12 baby onions, peeled
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 100ml milk
  1. Dice the bacon and beef and peel the baby onions.
  2. Brown the bacon in the oil, then the beef in batches.
  3. Add the onions and mushrooms and colour lightly.
  4. Stir in the flour and tomato paste, then add the stock, vinegar, thyme and bay leaves.
  5. Cover and simmer gently until the beef is tender.
  6. Peel and boil the potatoes, then mash with butter and warm milk.
  7. Serve the braise over the mash.

Clopin's garlic and olive chicken

Olive and lemon chicken, crusty bread.

How it is made
  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 1 bottle olive oil
  • 1 head garlic
  • 100g green olives
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch thyme
  • 1 packet bay leaves
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • 1 crusty loaf
  1. Chicken thighs braised with garlic, green olives, lemon and herbs until tender and saucy, with crusty bread to mop it all up. Season 1kg chicken thighs and brown skin side down in olive oil for 6 minutes, then turn. Add 10 whole peeled garlic cloves, 100g green olives, 1 sliced lemon, thyme and 2 bay leaves. Pour in 300ml chicken stock, cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes, then uncover and bubble for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens. Scatter with parsley and serve with crusty bread.

Sweets

King of Fools madeleinesVegetarian

Lemon butter shells. Crowned King of Fools at the festival.

Prep 17 min, cook 13 min, then 60 min to rest or set · Make ahead: Best eaten the day they are baked
Recipe for 4
  • 100g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 100g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  1. Melt the butter and cool.
  2. Whisk the eggs, sugar and lemon zest until thick.
  3. Fold in the flour and butter.
  4. Rest the batter.
  5. Spoon into greased shell tins.
  6. Bake at 200°C until puffed and golden.
  7. Dust with icing sugar.

Drinks

Djali's grenadine lime sirop sodaNo alcohol

Homemade pomegranate syrup and lime over ice, topped with soda.

Prep 10 min, cook 10 min · Make ahead: Grenadine up to 1 week, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250ml pomegranate juice
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 lime
  • 1 L soda water
  • 4 cups ice
  1. Simmer the pomegranate juice and sugar until slightly syrupy, then add a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Cool the grenadine.
  3. Fill tall glasses with ice and add lime juice to each.
  4. Top with soda water, then slowly pour the grenadine down the side of each glass so it settles at the bottom. Serve with a spoon.

When to serve

Serve the snacks when Quasimodo sneaks down to the Festival of Fools.
