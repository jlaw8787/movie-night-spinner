Snacks

Cheddar and chive cheese ball

A ball of cheddar and cream cheese rolled in chives, with crackers.

Prep 7 min, cook 8 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 2 days, keep wrapped and chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 250g cream cheese, soft
  • 150g vintage cheddar, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 bunch chives, chopped
  • 80g pecans
  • 200g water crackers
  • 4 celery sticks, cut into batons
  1. Beat the cream cheese, cheddar, Worcestershire, garlic powder and most of the chives until smooth.
  2. Shape into a ball and wrap in cling film. Chill until firm, 1 hr.
  3. Toast the pecans in a dry pan, chop finely and mix with the remaining chives, 8 min.
  4. Roll the cheese ball in the nuts and serve with crackers and celery.

Eternal Deathslayer III pizza scrolls

Rolled pizza dough with pepperoni, tomato and mozzarella, baked golden and sliced.

Prep 20 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Roll, cut and pack the scrolls into the tin up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; brush with butter and bake on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 100ml pizza sauce
  • 100g sliced pepperoni
  • 150g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 20g butter, melted
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 300ml marinara sauce
  1. Roll the dough on a floured bench into a large rectangle.
  2. Spread with pizza sauce and scatter with pepperoni, mozzarella and oregano.
  3. Roll up tightly from the long side and cut into 12 slices. Pack into a greased 24cm tin.
  4. Brush with the melted butter and garlic, then bake at 200°C until golden, 20 min.
  5. Warm the marinara and serve for dunking, 3 min.

Mains

Glazed meatloaf with mashed potatoes

Beef and pork loaf baked under a sticky glaze, creamy mashed potatoes alongside.

Prep 13 min, cook 1 hr 28 min · Make ahead: Mix the loaf and press it into the tin up to 1 day ahead, covered in the fridge; glaze, bake and make the mash on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 60g breadcrumbs
  • 180ml full cream milk
  • 500g beef mince
  • 300g pork mince
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 80g butter
  • 300g frozen peas
  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in some of the milk, then mix with the minces, onion, egg, Worcestershire, salt and pepper, 8 min.
  2. Press into a lined loaf tin. Mix the tomato sauce, sugar and vinegar and spread over the top.
  3. Bake at 180°C, then rest, 1 hr.
  4. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with most of the butter and the rest of the milk, 20 min.
  5. Cook the peas and toss with the remaining butter. Slice the meatloaf and serve with mash and peas, 8 min.

Cinnamon tea cakeVegetarian

Warm cinnamon butter cake and cream for the Antiques Roadshow marathon.

Prep 15 min, cook 35 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 1 day ahead and wrap when cool; warm gently in the oven and whip the cream on the night
Recipe for 4
  • 120g butter, soft
  • 180g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 150ml full cream milk
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 200ml thickened cream, whipped
  1. Beat most of the butter and most of the sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  2. Fold in the flour alternately with the milk and spread into a lined 20cm round tin.
  3. Bake at 180°C until a skewer comes out clean, 32 min.
  4. While hot, brush with the remaining melted butter and sprinkle with the remaining sugar mixed with cinnamon.
  5. Serve warm in wedges with whipped cream.

Drinks

Grandma's tea tin peach iced teaNo alcohol

Plain black tea, peach and lemon over ice.

Prep 5 min, cook 5 min, 1 hr waiting · Make ahead: Up to 1 day, keep chilled
Recipe for 4
  • 6 black tea bags
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 400ml peach nectar
  • 2 lemons, 1 juiced and 1 sliced
  • 1 peach, sliced
  • 1 small bunch mint
  • 1 bag ice
  1. Steep the tea bags with the sugar in boiling water, 5 min.
  2. Remove the bags, cool, then chill, 1 hr.
  3. Stir in the peach nectar and lemon juice.
  4. Fill glasses with ice and peach slices, pour over the tea and garnish with mint and lemon wheels.
