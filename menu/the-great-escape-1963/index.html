Snacks

Tom, Dick and Harry sausage rolls

Flaky pork sausage rolls in three batches, named Tom, Dick and Harry.

Prep 18 min, cook 30 min · Make ahead: Roll and chill them up to a day ahead, or freeze; bake to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 500g pork sausage mince
  • 1 brown onion, grated
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 40g fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp dried sage
  • 3 sheets of puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 4 tbsp HP Sauce
  1. Mix the mince, onion, carrot, breadcrumbs and sage with salt and pepper.
  2. Halve the pastry sheets, lay a log of filling along each and roll up, sealing with egg.
  3. Cut into short lengths, brush with egg and bake at 200°C until puffed and golden, 30 min.
  4. Serve hot with the brown sauce, 3 min.

Dirt-in-plain-sight lentil dipVegetarian

Brown lentil and onion dip with garlic toasts, dark as tunnel dirt.

Prep 5 min, cook 20 min · Make ahead: Make the dip up to 2 days ahead; toast the bread to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 2 x 400g tins of brown lentils, drained
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Blitz the lentils with 1 garlic clove, the spices, 2 tbsp oil, lemon juice and a splash of water until smooth.
  2. Brush the bread with the rest of the oil and toast until golden, 8 min.
  3. Rub the toasts with the other cut garlic clove.
  4. Spoon the dip into a bowl, scatter with parsley and serve with the toasts.

Escape committee beef and barley stew

Slow beef and barley stew with herb dumplings.

Prep 20 min, cook 2 hr 10 min · Make ahead: Make the stew up to 2 days ahead and reheat; add the dumplings to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg chuck steak, in chunks
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 brown onions, diced
  • 3 carrots, in chunks
  • 100g pearl barley
  • 1.3 L beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 60g cold butter, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  1. Toss the beef in seasoned flour and brown it in batches in the oil, 15 min.
  2. Add the onions and carrots, then the barley, stock and bay leaves, and simmer covered until tender, 1 hr 45 min.
  3. Rub the butter into the self-raising flour with the parsley and salt, then stir in enough cold water for a soft dough.
  4. Drop spoonfuls onto the stew, cover and simmer until the dumplings are puffed, 25 min.

Swiss border rosti with bratwurst

Potato rosti with bratwurst. Hilts rides for the Swiss fence.

Prep 3 min, cook 47 min · Make ahead: Par-boil the potatoes the day before and chill; grate and fry to serve
Recipe for 4
  • 1kg waxy potatoes
  • 60g butter
  • 8 bratwurst sausages
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 4 tbsp German mustard
  • 1 cup sauerkraut
  1. Par-boil the whole potatoes for 10 minutes, cool, then grate coarsely and season.
  2. Melt half the butter in a pan, press in the potato and fry until a golden crust forms, then flip and crisp the other side with the rest of the butter, 20 min.
  3. Fry the bratwurst and onion until browned, 12 min.
  4. Cut the rosti into wedges and serve with the bratwurst, onion, sauerkraut and mustard.

Hilts' cooler jam dropsVegetarian

Buttery jam drop biscuits, one for every bounce of the baseball off the cooler wall.

Prep 15 min, cook 15 min · Make ahead: Bake up to 3 days ahead and keep in an airtight tin
Recipe for 4
  • 125g butter
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 100g raspberry jam
  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the egg and vanilla and mix in the flour.
  2. Roll into 24 balls on lined trays, press a dip in each and fill with jam.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 12 to 15 minutes until golden, then cool on the trays.
