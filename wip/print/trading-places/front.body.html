IN THE OFFICE

Christmas on the floor, Friday at three

THE PARTNERS INVITE all staff, with husbands and wives, to the Christmas party on Friday. The buffet opens on the floor at three, sent over by the Heritage Club kitchen with a carver for the roast, and the bar stays open until six. Please clear your desks and lock your drawers before you leave them.

WELCOME. Mr. William Ray Valentine has joined the firm and takes the desk lately held by Mr. Louis Winthorpe III, who has left us.

Mr. Mortimer Duke, left, and Mr. Randolph Duke take Mr. Valentine through the business over breakfast: coffee, wheat, pork bellies and orange juice.
