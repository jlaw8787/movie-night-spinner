THE WAGER BOOK

Wagers between members are entered in the book kept behind the bar, signed by both parties and settled on the day agreed.

The customary stake is one dollar.

HOUSE RULES

Jackets and ties in all club rooms.

Guests remain with the member who signed them in.

No cash is taken. All charges are signed to the member.

Business papers are not to be shown in the Dining Room.

HEAD STEWARD’S NOTICE
The Dining Room closes at 4 P.M. on Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve.
Closing prices from the exchanges are posted in the Library at 5 P.M. Pork bellies and orange juice added at members’ request.

Members’ accounts are rendered on the first of each month.
