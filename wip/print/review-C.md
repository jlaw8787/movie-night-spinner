## the-french-connection, 9.2, PASS
Narcotics Bureau surveillance report, two pages, with the restaurant menu copied down from the sidewalk and Doyle's own pizza and coffee as "expenses". The best object in this batch: it could only belong to this film, the halftone of Charnier is a believable attached surveillance photo, and every egg checks out (Santa suit, Frog One, Nicoli, Sal Boca, Poughkeepsie, Simonson, Mulderig and Klein, the shuttle wave). Restaurant left unnamed, correct.
1. Distribution list uses spaced hyphens as dashes ("2 - Bureau file", "1 - W. Simonson") and "FILED BY ____". Set them as "2 copies, Bureau file" or tab-aligned "2  Bureau file". Same spirit as the dash rule.
2. Special Elite is a distressed modern "typewriter" face and every character is identically roughed. Add a little per-line baseline wobble and ink weight variation (or use a cleaner period Courier-style face with a soft ink spread) so it reads as a real IBM Selectric/Underwood on a carbon set, not a font.
3. P2 diagram box has empty space below "me / pizza" and the coffee stain sits on nothing. Tighten the diagram height or add the second car or doorway mark, and add a typed time stamp to each observation line (a. 12:40, b. 1:15...) which every real surveillance log carries.

## serpico, 9.1, PASS
Green-tint columnar ledger: front is Perlmutter's price sheet, back is the March "on the arm" cop tab where Frank pays. Concept is film-specific and the arithmetic is right on every line and both totals (40.25 on the arm, 17.15 paid). Lean roast beef for the "new kid", Leslie, Keough, Blair from City Hall, the sheepdog and opera are all good.
1. Kalam handwriting carries the whole sheet, so every "a" and "e" is identical and it reads as a web font. Mix two hands (the counterman and M.P.) or vary size and slant per entry, and let numbers sit a little off the rules.
2. Food fixes a chef will catch: lox is salt-cured, not smoked, so "smoked salmon" is wrong (write "silky cured lox"). "Iced coffee float" never says coffee and "steeped overnight" is cold brew wording; write "Coffee steeped overnight in the icebox...". "Steamed" appears in the HOT MEATS head, the pastrami line and the hero line; cut two.
3. Confirm "Spanish class" and "No mice in the store" against the film; drop either if not certain. Also check "Perlmutter's" is not a real New York deli name.

## ghost-dog, 9.0, PASS
Jersey City Chinese and Japanese take-out trifold, red one-colour on mint stock, with "rent due!!" over the back room (the mob's unpaid rent) and pigeons on the cover. Reads like a real 1999 take-out menu, dense, correct conventions (L-numbers, pt/qt, free-with-order ladder, coupon, phone order block).
1. Period food wording: edamame and a "frothy shot of whisked Japanese matcha" in a 1999 Jersey City take-out are 2010s. Recipe is locked, so reword: "Iced Green Tea Lemonade, fresh lemons, honey, green tea, shaken" and move edamame to the Japanese Kitchen box as "Salted Soybeans (Edamame)". Remove edamame from the $35 free-item ladder (use "Qt. Pork Fried Rice").
2. The cover pigeons are crude blobs and the soup bowl doodle is weak; paint the birds as a proper one-colour woodcut or brush-ink pigeon flight (they are the film's key image) with slight misregistration of the red disc.
3. Confirm "Autumn Moon" is not named or contradicted in the film, and add one more definite egg on the outside (the ice cream truck or the rooftop coop is safer than invented lines). "Toasted Coconut Ice Cream" description never says coconut; write "toasted coconut flakes".

## pelham, 8.9, REDO
Purple spirit-duplicator lunch run sheet for the Transit command center, 1974. Strong object and the eggs are all right (Garber, Patrone, Correll, Inspector Daniels, Tokyo visitors, the mayor's flu, Gesundheit, the 1.23 hero). Arithmetic on the order block is correct.
1. Headings are Architects Daughter, a 2010s "hand-drawn" web font, and they make the sheet look like a Canva template. Hand-letter heads in a period ditto stencil/lettering-guide style, or type them all-caps on the same typewriter, and add real ditto behaviour: faded right side, slight smear, uneven purple density.
2. Food copy: "Chicken noodle soup" never mentions noodles; "Cheese danish" never mentions cheese ("sweet farmer cheese and lemon filling"); jelly doughnuts are not "warmed through", they are fresh from the fryer.
3. Dead zone right column below rugelach (only a weak hero doodle, well over 10% of that column). Fill with a typed "Pastry run Fri." box, the deli phone line, or a "Who owes" IOU list, and drop the clip-art sandwich.

## die-hard-with-a-vengeance, 8.8, REDO
Pale green photocopied school breakfast and lunch calendar plus Parents Association news, June 1995. Good fit for the school bomb thread; the permission slip from "Z. Carver (uncle)" with "call the shop" and Raymond and Dexter as lunchroom helpers are great. Arial and Times on a PC-made school sheet is honestly period.
1. Easter egg doubt: the school name "Chester A. Arthur Elementary", the "21st President" and "number 21" lines, and "Mrs. Gaines" are not confirmed in the film (Wikipedia only says "one of the city's elementary schools"). If not certain, use a generic P.S. number and drop the 21 jokes. Keep St. Ives, Simon Says, the water jug relay, Raymond and Dexter, Carver's shop.
2. Art is weak 2020s doodle clip art (stick-figure lunch line, sunglasses sun, flat burger). Replace with 1990s school clip-art style (heavier black line, solid fills) or simply more text, as real district menus did.
3. Food wording: "flaky salt" on caramel is 2010s; "yellow sponge" is British, use "yellow cake"; "Cook's choice, Ask on the line!" is filler. Alfa Slab One headline reads as a modern display face; a 1995 office would have used a bolder Times, Arial Black or Cooper.

## king-of-new-york, 8.7, REDO
Souvenir journal for Frank White's South Bronx hospital benefit, cover plus page 3 with menu and ads. Right event, right $16 million goal, sensible to carry a photo.
1. Left edge of both faces: hard white staple bars and a blurred, smeared green vignette read as rendering artefacts, not a saddle-stitched journal. Either draw proper staples on the spine with a shadow and fold, or remove them; replace the blur with real edge wear.
2. The progress "thermometer" with a circle knob and tick labels looks like a web UI progress bar. Redraw as a period printed thermometer or a plain typeset "Pledged to date" line. The cover engraving is good but the windows are a mechanical grid; break the pattern with lit and dark rooms, blinds, a sign or two.
3. Copy: "no MSG" on a black-tie dumpling course is wrong register; "baked" repeats in the cheesecake note and the bakery ad; confirm "Hot fried chicken, Good luck, Frank!" and "Next stop, City Hall" are definite film references or cut them. A real benefit journal is ad-dense: fill page 3 with more real-feeling paid ads ("Compliments of" boxes, varied sizes and borders).

## midnight-cowboy, 8.6, REDO
1969 Florida bus company timetable and meal stop guide. Right object for Joe and Ratso, fares and schedule are plausible and the times work (28.5 hours, Miami arrival in the morning).
1. Styling is 2020s "retro poster": League Gothic with offset orange drop shadows, flat colour panels, a transit-app route line with circle stops. Rebuild like a real 1969 bus folder: smaller dense timetable type, ruled tables, one or two spot inks with misregistration, no shadowed display type.
2. Food is out of period and place. "Leg ham" is Australian (US: "baked ham"). Coconut water, doughnut holes with "coffee custard" and the flourless whole-orange almond cake are modern restaurant dishes; reword as close as the recipes allow ("Orange almond cake, Florida oranges, ground almonds", "Doughnut holes with coffee cream for dunking", "Pineapple and coconut cooler"). Rum at a bus meal stop is unlikely; cut it.
3. Dead space at the bottom of all three inside panels (about 12 to 15%). Add stop-by-stop rest station notes, baggage rules or a small route map of the coast, and give the cover bus a real 1969 coach silhouette (ribbed sides, raised deck) rather than a generic box.

## leon, 8.5, REDO
Little Italy grocery weekly circular, 1994: milk, pig oven mitt, the plant, Tony's spaghetti. Good egg choices (milk, the plant "no roots", the pig).
1. It looks like a 2020s flat graphic: clean green bars, crisp starbursts, cream card stock and only nine items. A 1994 circular is cheap newsprint, four-colour with misregistration, crowded with 30 or more items, product photos or line cuts, price blasts everywhere. Redo it as dense newsprint.
2. Food: "leg ham" is Australian (US: "boiled ham" or "Virginia ham"); "Antipasto pint" is olives and cornichons only, so rename "Olive and cornichon pint"; cornichons appear twice; "We'll warm it while you wait" is odd at a deli counter; "terracotta tumbler" is a stretch for a dirt cup (say "clay pot").
3. Back face: the baguette tray drawing is crude and the "Tony's" box set in DM Serif Display reads like a website card. Tony's would not advertise a quart of spaghetti on a grocery circular in that style; make it a small co-op ad in a period face, or move Tony's spaghetti into a "Hot Food To Go" box.

## dog-day-afternoon, 8.5, REDO
Bank savings deposit slip used as the lunch order during the siege, Aug 22, 1972. Clever and full of good eggs (Sylvia, Mulvaney's diabetes, Howard's asthma, the barbershop, the air conditioning, Wyoming, Jenny's husband, pizza delivery).
1. The slip is unfinished: "Total from other side", "Total" and "Total, carry to other side" are all blank. Fill them in hand, and fix prices: "Sodas, by the case .20" is impossible (a case was about $2 to $3 in 1972).
2. Typewriter text is Cutive Mono with wide letter-spacing in pale grey, low contrast and webby. Use a period typewriter face at normal spacing in darker black ribbon ink, and swap Cinzel (a modern web favourite) for a proper engraved bank roman. A real deposit slip is also smaller and denser; consider a cheque-book size sheet.
3. Food out of period: "chili salt on the rim" beer and "low-moisture mozzarella" are modern. Reword within the recipe ("Cold beer, lime wedge, salt on the side"; "Pepperoni pies, large, thin crust, plenty of mozzarella"). "Aspirin" note is not a known film detail; cut it unless certain. Check "Borough Savings Bank" is not a real bank.
