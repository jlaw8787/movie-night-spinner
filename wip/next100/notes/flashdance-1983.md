# Flashdance (1983)

Layout: bartab (kept). Venue: Mawby's, the neighbourhood bar and grill in Pittsburgh where Alex dances at night (the plan's lead, checked).

## Venue
- Wikipedia: Alex "works as a nightly cabaret performer at Mawby's, a neighborhood bar and grill"; Jake Mawby in the cast; Richie Blazek is the short-order cook there. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flashdance
- Filmsite: the water dance happens at the bar. https://filmsite.org/flashdance.html

## Facts and quotes used

- Alex Owens, 18, welder at a Pittsburgh steel mill (wings line, number 18); Grunt the pit bull; Jeanie training as a figure skater, the skating competition (sundae, wink); Richie, short-order cook, hopes to become a stand-up comic (pierogies line); Hanna Long, retired ballerina; breakdancing seen on the Pittsburgh streets; the Pittsburgh Conservatory of Dance and Repertory audition (slip prop).
