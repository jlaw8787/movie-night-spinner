# Zombieland (2009)

Layout: arcade (kept). Venue: Pacific Playland, the Los Angeles amusement park the sisters head for (the plan's lead). The arcade's prize counter suits the park's game booths, where Tallahassee lures the zombies.

## Venue
- Wikipedia: "the Pacific Playland amusement park in Los Angeles, an area supposedly free of zombies"; the sisters "activate all the rides and lights"; Tallahassee "lures the zombies to a game booth"; the drop tower. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombieland

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombieland, plot and rules read raw):
- The outbreak began as a strain of mad cow disease (cheeseburger line).
- The gang goes to Bill Murray's house in Hollywood (cue). Monopoly game.
- Rules: 1 Cardio, 2 Double tap, 4 Seatbelts, 17 Don't be a hero changed to Be a hero, 31 Check the back seat, 32 Enjoy the little things (winks, potato skins line, corn dog line, number 32).
- Sign "Rides on. Lights on." and ticket "Supposedly zombie free" from the Pacific Playland lines above.
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Zombieland):
- Note, Columbus: "I used to avoid people like they were zombies before they were zombies." (Wikiquote sets "before" in italics.)
- Tallahassee: "Time to nut up or shut up." (shake line).
- Tallahassee on "the last box of Twinkies that anyone will enjoy in the whole universe"; he searches a fried-Twinkie stand (Twinkie line and dish).
- Bill Murray: "Just played nine holes on the Riviera." (club sandwich name and line).
- Wichita: "Oooh, Free Parking! Which, coincidentally, is the best thing about living in Zombieland." (house popcorn).
- Tagline: "Welcome to Zombieland." (T).

## Unsure
- House drink note "Every machine switched on" is a nod to the sisters turning on the rides and lights; Wikipedia does not mention slushie machines.
- The arcade's high score initials ("COL") are the layout's own.
