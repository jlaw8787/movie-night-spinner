# Revenge of the Nerds (1984)

Layout: stadium (kept), with the fight scoreboard relabelled Tri-Lambs v Alpha Betas. Venue: Greek Games Concessions, Homecoming carnival, Adams College.

## Venue
The plan's lead holds up. The nerds beat the Alpha Betas at the Homecoming carnival (Wikipedia's name) which Wikiquote's scene calls the Greek Games, with a pie stall for charity. A concession stand at the games suits the stadium layout. The board's Tri-Lambs row is the layout's dish count, so the nerds lead, which matches the film (they win overall).
- Wikipedia: "the only way to gain justice is to replace Stan as Council president by winning the Homecoming carnival"; "the nerds outsell the Alpha Betas by selling pies". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revenge_of_the_Nerds
- Wikiquote: "[Alpha Betas have set up a kissing booth for the Greek Games]". https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Revenge_of_the_Nerds
