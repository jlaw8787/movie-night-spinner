# Straight Outta Compton (2015)

Layout: truck (kept). Venue: Compton, 1986 (the plan's lead "Compton", named plainly with the year the story starts; no proper venue name invented).

## Venue
- Wikipedia: the film opens "In 1986, Compton, California", where Eazy-E, Dr. Dre and Ice Cube start Ruthless Records and form N.W.A. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight_Outta_Compton_(film)
- The film names no restaurant or truck; the truck layout reads as Compton street food, and the sign says only "Compton, California. Where N.W.A started".

Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Straight_Outta_Compton_(film)):
- Note, Ice Cube: "Speak a little truth and people lose their minds." (verbatim, watching a crowd destroy their records).
- Cube to Dre: "You they bread and butter, they're gonna take care of you." (sliders).
- Cube: "I'd rather be broke than to get fucked. I told you not to sign that shit, Dre." (slip prop "Contract: not signed").
- Eazy-E: "I like Afterschool Specials." (cobbler).
- Tagline "The world's most dangerous times created the world's most dangerous group." (T).
Wikipedia (link above, plot read raw):
- Eazy narrowly evades a police raid in 1986 (wink, number 1986); Dre convinces Eazy to fund Ruthless Records (chilli fries); Jerry Heller manages the group; Cube won't sign without a lawyer and leaves; Detroit police forbid the song and the group performs it anyway (coney dogs, cue); "No Vaseline"; Suge Knight and Death Row; The Chronic; Dre leaves Death Row to form Aftermath (agua fresca).
