# The Replacements (2000)

Layout: stadium (kept). Venue: Nextel Stadium Concessions, a Washington Sentinels home game (changed from the lead "Washington Sentinels" to the stadium the film names).

## Venue
The Sentinels' home ground is called Nextel Stadium in the film (shot at PSINet Stadium in Baltimore). The last game against Dallas is played there: Falco "returns to the stadium" at halftime.
- Wikipedia: "The stadium was known as Nextel Stadium in the movie." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Replacements_(film)
