# Mars Attacks! (1996)

Layout: banquet (kept). Venue: Galaxy Casino, "Art Land's Galaxy Casino, Las Vegas" (changed from the lead).

## Venue
No White House state dinner is confirmed in any source read. Wikipedia's cast names "Art Land, the owner of Galaxy Casino in Las Vegas" and "a rude gambler who frequents Galaxy Casino"; Byron is a casino employee there and Tom Jones performs in the showroom. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mars_Attacks! ; review confirming Galaxy and the showroom: https://moviemagg.blogspot.com/2015/07/mars-attacks-tim-burton.html
