# The Terminal (2004)

Layout: foodcourt (kept). Venue: JFK Terminal Food Court, John F. Kennedy International Airport, New York (changed from the lead "JFK Gate 67": no source I opened gives the gate number, and the food court is where Viktor eats). The layout's mall words are remapped: "Gate directory", "Spotted around the terminal", "You are here: the transit lounge", and "Heard in Dixon's office" over Viktor's line.

## Venue
Viktor is stranded inside JFK's international terminal for nine months; Dixon tells him to stay in the transit lounge, and he lives off the terminal's food outlets. The set was a full working terminal with real outlets.
- Wikipedia: Dixon "instructs Viktor to stay in the transit lounge"; "Viktor finds a gate under renovation and makes it his home." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Terminal
- Live Design: the set held 35 retailers "including Starbucks, Burger King, Borders Books, Hugo Boss, and Hudson Newsstand"; "There he eats, sleeps, shops". https://www.livedesignonline.com/lounge-act-terminal-set

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Terminal):
- Viktor Navorski from Krakozhia; a coup while he was in the air leaves his passport invalid (dumpling line, D).
- He has only his luggage and a Planters peanut can (house peanuts note, wink).
- A gate under renovation becomes his home (wink).
- Enrique Cruz, a food service truck driver, gives him free meals in exchange for help with Dolores Torres (chicken line).
- The can holds "A Great Day in Harlem"; his father collected 56 of 57 autographs and Viktor needs Benny Golson's (cheesecake name and line, number 57, wink, cue).
- Nine months after arriving (T). One-day emergency visa (prop line). Gupta delays the plane by running in front of it (wink).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Terminal):
- Note, Viktor: "Is home. I am not afraid from my home." (verbatim, said to Frank Dixon).
- Viktor: "Officer Torres, my friend says you are a stallion." (drink name and line).
- Viktor: "I'm afraid from, uh... Dracula!" (wink).
Different Drummer (https://differentdrummer-website.squarespace.com/main/the-terminal-airport-cannelloni-recipe-):
- Viktor eats saltine cracker sandwiches "made luscious with gobs of mustard oozing out the holes on top" (cracker dish and line).
- Collecting luggage-cart quarters lets him move on to Burger King (burger line).
