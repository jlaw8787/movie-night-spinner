# Scary Movie (2000)

Layout: invite (kept). Venue: Cindy's House Party.

## Venue
The plan lead was "Buffy's party", but the Wikipedia plot has the party at Cindy's: "Cindy has a house party, hoping for safety in numbers" (Tina is killed fetching beer from the garage, the killer gets stoned with Shorty's crew in the basement). Cindy's dad leaves town earlier (Wikiquote dialogue). Buffy hosts nothing; she has the pageant. An invite to Cindy's party suits the invite layout.
