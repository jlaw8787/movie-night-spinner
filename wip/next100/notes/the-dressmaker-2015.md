# The Dressmaker (2015)

Layout: tearoom (kept). Venue: Pratt's Store, Dungatar, 1951, the town shop where Gertrude works the counter and Mr Pratt shows William his mother's unpaid account (changed from the lead's plain "Dungatar" to a named place in it).

## Venue
- GradeSaver summary part 1: "At Pratt's store, shop girl Gertrude is excited to see him. Mr. Pratt shows William his mother's unpaid account, totaling 347 pounds." https://www.gradesaver.com/the-dressmaker-2015-film/study-guide/summary-part-1
- NFSA clip notes: Muriel Pratt, "the local shopkeeper", up a ladder at the "Golden Fleece" sign; a crate of fabrics arrives for Tilly from overseas. https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/curated/dressmaker-haute-couture-arrives

## Unsure
- The second still shows Tilly in a yellow coat with flames behind her; the caption reads "Tilly and the fire on the hill" on the reading that it is the house fire at the end.
- The tearoom layout prints prices in cents; Australia used pounds in 1951 (layout style, not a claim).
- The note uses Wikiquote's singular "bastard"; GradeSaver hears it as plural.
