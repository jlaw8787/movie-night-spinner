# The Wolf of Wall Street (2013)

Layout: galley (kept). Venue: The Naomi, Jordan's yacht (the plan's lead), where he throws lobsters after the FBI agents and later sails for Monaco.

## Venue
- boatbookings.com: the yacht is "Named after the love of his life Naomi (Nadine in reality)", played by the yacht Lady M. https://www.boatbookings.com/blog/tag/the-wolf-of-wall-street
- Wikipedia: the 1996 yacht trip to Italy; Jordan orders captain Ted to sail to Monaco; the ship capsizes in a storm. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wolf_of_Wall_Street_(2013_film)
