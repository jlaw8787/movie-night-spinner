# She's All That (1999)

Layout: prom (kept). Venue: The Senior Prom, Southern California (changed from the plan's "Harrison High prom": neither Wikipedia page names the school, so the venue is descriptive).

## Venue
Wikipedia: the bet is to turn Laney into "the prom queen in six weeks"; Laney is nominated for prom queen along with Taylor; at the dance Zack takes the stage with Taylor as Prom King and Queen; the school is "his Southern California high school". The film ends at graduation, so it is the seniors' prom. No school name appears in Wikipedia or Wikiquote, so "Harrison High" was not used (round 2 rule: no invented names).
