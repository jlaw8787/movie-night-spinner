# American Graffiti (1973)

Layout: drivein (kept). Venue: Mel's Drive-In, Modesto (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Wikipedia: the friends meet "at Mel's Drive-In in Modesto, California", and the Wolfman asks the blonde to call Curt "at the Mel's Drive-In payphone", where the payphone wakes him at dawn.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Graffiti (plot, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Graffiti):
- Sunday, 2 September 1962, last day of summer vacation (house drink note, number 62).
- Curt chases the blonde in the white Ford Thunderbird; Terry cruises in Steve's car and calls himself "Terry the Tiger"; John says Carol is a cousin he is babysitting; the Pharaohs rip out a police car's back axle (winks).
- The station man says the Wolfman "is everywhere"; the Wolfman reads Curt's message on air (chilli dog line, cue, wink).
- The drag race on Paradise Road (fries line).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/American_Graffiti):
- Tagline "Where were you in '62?" (T, onion rings note).
- Note, Steve: "You just can't stay seventeen forever." (verbatim, end of his line to Curt).
- Vic: "you want a knuckle sandwich?" Terry: "I'm waitin' for a double Chubby Chuck..." (sliders, burger, carhop slip).
- Terry: "I lost my I.D. in... in a flood and I'd like to get some Old Harper" (drink name and line).
- Blonde: "A white '56. I saw you on Third Street..." (sundae name and line).

## Unsure
- Old Harper is a real whisky brand; the drink uses Kentucky bourbon as the stand-in.
- Onion rings and cherry cola are plain house lines; prices are the layout's era prices.
