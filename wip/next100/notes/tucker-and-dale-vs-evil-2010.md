# Tucker and Dale vs. Evil (2010)

Layout: campfire (kept). Venue: Tucker and Dale's Vacation Home, the run-down lakefront cabin in West Virginia they have just bought (the plan's lead). Tucker's own word for it is "vacation home". The summer camp stock labels are remapped to the cabin (Porch snacks, Cabin mains, From the esky, Dinner bell, Told to the officer, Talk around the fire, Ask Tucker about, Fishing season 2010, Welcome to).

## Venue
- Wikipedia: "Tucker and Dale, two well-meaning hillbillies who recently bought an abandoned lakefront cabin in the woods" in West Virginia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucker_%26_Dale_vs._Evil
- Mutant Reviewers quotes, Tucker: "Tell 'er that you got a vacation home. That'll prob'ly impress 'er." https://mutantreviewersmovies.com/2012/02/03/justin-does-tucker-and-dale-vs-evil

## Facts and quotes used
Wikiquote has no page for this film (en.wikiquote.org search returns nothing), so quotes come from Mutant Reviewers' quote list (https://mutantreviewersmovies.com/2012/02/03/justin-does-tucker-and-dale-vs-evil):
- Note, Tucker: "Oh hidy ho officer, we've had a doozy of a day."
- Dale: "That's a PBR Buddy." (drink line, comma-free as printed there).
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucker_%26_Dale_vs._Evil, plot read raw):
- The hillbillies save Allison from drowning at the lake where they are fishing (catfish line).
- Tucker hits a beehive while cutting wood and swings the chainsaw wildly (chicken line, cue).
- Mike dives into a wood chipper (pulled pork line).
- Dale throws a box of chamomile tea at Chad, who has an asthma attack (cake line).
- The "Memorial Day Massacre" 20 years before (wink, number 20); Dale's dog Jangers (wink); Allison and Dale dig an outhouse hole (wink); Sheriff Gurr killed by a loose beam (wink).
Tropedia (https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tucker_and_Dale_vs._Evil):
- At the gas station Dale grabs a scythe to look casual when he approaches the college girls (chilli dog line).
- Dale: "His bowling fingers!" after Chad cuts off two of Tucker's fingers (wink; Wikipedia confirms the two fingers).
- The cabin is full of cobwebs, which the two call "just a little dusty" (prop line, house snack note).

## Unsure
- "Told to the officer": the quote list does not say who Tucker says it to; the heading only repeats the "officer" in the line.
- The chilli dog line uses Tropedia's scythe detail; Wikipedia only says they meet at a gas station.
- House lines (beer nuts, sweet iced tea) and "Cracked one each round the fire" are plain menu jokes.
