# American Beauty (1999)

Layout: takeaway (kept). Venue: Mr. Smiley's, the burger drive-thru where Lester takes a job as a fry cook (the plan's lead). The takeaway's delivery wording is remapped to a drive-thru (Drive-thru open, Pull up to the window, Ask at the window about, Talk in the kitchen, Name badge, Hot off the fryer, handed out the window, In the car park).

## Venue
