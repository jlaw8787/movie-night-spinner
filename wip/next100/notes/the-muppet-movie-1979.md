# The Muppet Movie (1979)

Layout: placemat (kept). Venue: El Sleezo Cafe, "El Sleezo Cafe, on the road to Hollywood" (changed from the lead).

## Venue
The lead, Doc Hopper's French Fried Frog Legs, is only pitched and advertised in the film (Filmsite: Hopper wants Kermit as spokesperson for his chain; TV Tropes: Hopper's commercial in a frog suit). No Doc Hopper's restaurant is shown. The El Sleezo Cafe is named on screen, where Kermit meets Fozzie doing his act.
- Wikiquote: "The El Sleezo Cafe. Hmm, Foreign food." and the owner's "toughest, meanest, filthiest pest hole" line. https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Muppet_Movie
- Wikipedia cast: "a Waiter at El Sleezo Café where Fozzie is doing his act". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Muppet_Movie
