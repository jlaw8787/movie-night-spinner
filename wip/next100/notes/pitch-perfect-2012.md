# Pitch Perfect (2012)

Layout: invite (kept). Venue: The Riff-Off at the Empty Pool, Barden University (the lead's "The riff-off", with the place the film shows; descriptive name, the film gives the spot no proper name).

## Venue
- Wikipedia: "The Bellas face off against other a cappella groups in a competitive 'riff-off'." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_Perfect
- Yahoo (Daily Beast oral history): the riff-off is in an empty swimming pool, "the acoustics are the best there"; categories used were "Ladies of the '80s" and "Songs about Sex"; groups: Bellas, Treblemakers, BU Harmonics, High Notes. https://ca.news.yahoo.com/movie-magic-oral-history-pitch-081238192.html
