# The Truman Show (1998)

Layout: home (kept). Venue: Truman and Meryl's Kitchen, Seahaven Island (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Truman and Meryl's house on Seahaven Island is where the marriage scenes play out: the confrontation where he holds her at knifepoint and she breaks character happens "there" at home (Wikipedia), and the Mococoa pitch happens mid fight with Meryl (Fictional Companies wiki). The town is named in the Wikipedia plot as Seahaven Island.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Truman_Show (plot, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Truman_Show):
- Seahaven Island set inside a soundstage; Christof the creator; Fiji, where he was told Sylvia had moved (kokoda line).
- Thirtieth anniversary of the show (number 30).
- Stage light falls from the sky; rain falling only over him; his father put on a bus (winks).
- His boat strikes the wall of the soundstage; staircase to an exit door (fish pie line, "door in the painted sky" wink).
- Sylvia joins the "Free Truman" group (badge line).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Truman_Show):
- Note, Truman: "Good morning, and in case I don't see ya: Good afternoon, good evening, and good night!" (verbatim); muffins note "And in case I don't see ya."
- Tagline "On the air, unaware." (T).
- Christof: "Cue the sun!" (house juice name).
- Marlon, holding a beer can close to the camera: "Now that's a beer." (drink name and line).
- Last lines, the security guards: "You want another slice?" (pizza line).
Fictional Companies wiki (https://fictionalcompanies.fandom.com/wiki/Mococoa):
- When Truman confronts his wife about his reality she tries to change the subject by advertising Mococoa (dessert line, cue).
Erica Huff substack (https://ericahuff.substack.com/p/the-truman-show-macaroni-and-mococoa):
- Meryl offers "I made macaroni!" while Truman is irritated about his father (macaroni bake); Mococoa's "upper slopes of Mount Nicaragua" line (dessert name).
Cinemablend (https://cinemablend.com/movies/iconic-moments-from-the-truman-show):
- Lauren wears a button saying "How's It Going To End" (prop, wink).

## Unsure
- The Mount Nicaragua wording comes from a food blog quoting the ad, not Wikiquote; used unquoted in a dish name only.
- The badge pairs Lauren's button line with "Free Truman" (the activist group's name); they are two different things in the film.
- House juice and muffins are plain menu jokes on Christof's and Truman's lines.
