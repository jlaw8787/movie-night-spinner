# Never Been Kissed (1999)

Layout: newspaper (kept). Venue: Chicago Sun-Times Newsroom (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Wikipedia: Josie Geller "is an insecure 25-year-old copy editor for the Chicago Sun-Times"; her editors assign her undercover, fit her with a hidden camera, "and soon the whole office becomes obsessed with her story", watching her prom through the camera. The newsroom is shown on screen.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Never_Been_Kissed (plot, read raw)
