# Walk the Line (2005)

Layout: canteen (kept). Venue: Folsom State Prison, "Folsom State Prison, California, 1968" (the lead, kept).

## Venue
The film opens and peaks with Cash's 1968 show at Folsom State Prison, and Wikiquote has the warden's request and the yellow water line there.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walk_the_Line ; https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Walk_the_Line
