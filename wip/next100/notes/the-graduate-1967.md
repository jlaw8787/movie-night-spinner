# The Graduate (1967)

Layout: hotel (kept). Venue: The Taft Hotel (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Wikipedia: Benjamin "invites Mrs. Robinson to the Taft Hotel, where he registers under the surname Gladstone", and later takes Elaine to the Taft for a late-night drink where "the staff greet Benjamin as Mr. Gladstone".
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Graduate (plot, read raw)
