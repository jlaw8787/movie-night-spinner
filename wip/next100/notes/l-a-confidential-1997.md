# L.A. Confidential (1997)

Layout: motel (kept). Venue changed from the plan's Nite Owl coffee shop to the Victory Motel. The Victory Motel is the film's only real motel: Dudley Smith's run-down base for off-the-books interrogations and the site of the final shootout. A motel postcard from there fits the layout far better than a coffee shop does. The Nite Owl still carries the main and the cue, and the Formosa Cafe and Frolic Room carry the starters.
