# From Dusk Till Dawn (1996)

Layout: bartab (kept). Venue: Titty Twister (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Wikipedia: "an all-night strip club in the desert called Titty Twister", where the bartender tells Seth "only bikers and truckers are allowed". A bar tab from that bar is the bartab layout as is.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/From_Dusk_till_Dawn (plot and production, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia:
- "Seth also returns to the hotel with Big Kahuna Burgers" (slider name and line).
- "Chango Beer and Sex Machine's codpiece gun are references to Rodriguez's 1995 film Desperado" (Chango michelada, drink line).
- Only bikers and truckers allowed; Jacob insists his motor home licence makes him a truck driver (sign, cue).
- The doorman tries to block them (prop line). Carlos escorts them to the hideout town of El Rey; Seth negotiates the price with Carlos (taco line).
- Seth kills Santanico by shooting a wooden chandelier that impales her (churros line); Kate shoves her cross pendant down the doorman's throat; Frost tells a war story (winks).
- The Titty Twister is the top of a buried Aztec temple (mole line, wink).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/From_Dusk_till_Dawn):
- Note (quote), Seth: "All right, ramblers... Let's get ramblin'."
- Earl McGraw: "microwave food'll kill you faster than a bullet" (burrito line).
- Seth: "I'm gonna sit here and I'm gonna finish this bottle." (house tequila note)
- "Benny's World of Liquor" (wink). Tagline "A terrifying evil has been unleashed." (T, first sentence)
