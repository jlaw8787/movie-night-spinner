# That Thing You Do! (1996)

Layout: diner (kept). Venue: Villapiano's, Erie, Pennsylvania (the plan's lead, checked): the red-booth Italian place where the band plays its first paid gigs after the talent show. Menu leans Italian (garlic bread, antipasto, spaghetti and meatballs, pizza, cannoli, Chianti).

## Venue
- Wikiquote: Villapiano lays money on the bar, "A bonus... To entice you back! The word is out on you O-neders!" https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/That_Thing_You_Do!
- Wikipedia: the talent show win "earns them a paying gig at a local pizza parlor". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/That_Thing_You_Do!
- I Am Not A Stalker: Villapiano's is the red-boothed Italian restaurant, the "spaghetti place out by the airport", where the band played its first two paid gigs (filmed at the Palomino Club, North Hollywood). https://iamnotastalker.com/2014/01/27/villapianos-from-that-thing-you-do
- Diner stock remapped: "Hot coffee, homemade pie, booths by the window. Est. 1956." to "Red booths, spaghetti and a band on the little stage. Erie, 1964." (no invented founding year); the special's "cup of coffee and a slice from the pie case" to "garlic bread on the side"; "Open all night, daily" to "Red booths, Erie"; "Overheard at the counter" to "Coming up"; "Refills on the coffee." to "Breadsticks are free."; "The Pie Case / baked today" to "Dolci / made today"; "The Fountain" to "The Bar". Photo caption set to "The band on stage, guitars up."

## Unsure
- No food is named at Villapiano's beyond it being a pizza parlor (Wikipedia) and a spaghetti place (I Am Not A Stalker); dishes are Italian menu staples and the lines claim scenes.
