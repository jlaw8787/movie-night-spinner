# This Is Spinal Tap (1984)

Layout: deli (kept). Venue: Vandermint Auditorium Backstage, "Chapel Hill, North Carolina. US tour, 1982" (the lead "Backstage catering" made specific).

## Venue
The backstage spread where Nigel fights the miniature bread is at the Vandermint Auditorium, University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill.
- Filmsite: Vandermint Auditorium, Chapel Hill; Nigel complains about "miniature bread" with the meat hanging out and pimento-less olives. https://filmsite.org/thisisspinaltap.html

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Is_Spinal_Tap): 1982 US tour; Smell the Glove released with a plain black cover; amps go to 11, "one louder"; Stonehenge prop 18 inches not feet; billed beneath a puppet show; drummers dying.
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/This_Is_Spinal_Tap):
- Note, Viv Savage: "Have... a good... time... all the time. That's my philosophy, Marty."
- Tagline: "Does for rock and roll what "The Sound of Music" did for hills."
- Nigel: "None more black." (pastrami name, coffee); "These go to 11" (drink, wink).
Filmsite (same URL): Xanadu Star Theater, Cleveland, lost backstage, "Hello Cleveland!" (prop, wink); the two-word Shark Sandwich review (flake rolls line; the word itself is not printed); canned tuna fish among the end-credits topics (house tuna).
Fan transcript (https://spinaltapfan.com/scripts/tist411.txt), used only to settle wording: Nigel "I'm a real fish nut... tuna... no bones!" (house tuna note, unquoted); "Authorized Personnel Only" door sign (prop line).
