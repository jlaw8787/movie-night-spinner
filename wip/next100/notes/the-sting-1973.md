# The Sting (1973)

Layout: diningcar (kept). Venue: The 20th Century Limited, Lonnegan's private poker game, 1936 (the plan's lead, named).

## Venue
Wikipedia: "Aboard the opulent 20th Century Limited Gondorff, posing as the boorish Chicago bookie 'Shaw', buys into Lonnegan's private, high-stakes poker game", out-cheats him and takes $15,000. The train is named in the film's plot, so the dining car layout fits as is.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sting (plot, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sting):
- September 1936 (sign, number); Shaw had a pickpocket steal Lonnegan's wallet before the game (wink); Kid Twist poses as "Les Harmon" at Western Union (wink).
- "Place it on Lucky Dan"; place means finish second (prime rib name and line, cue, wink).
- Loretta the diner waitress is Salino; the black-gloved man was hired by Gondorff to protect Hooker (pie name, wink).
- Hooker and Gondorff rise from fake bullets amid cheers and laughter (wink).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Sting):

- Gondorff: "Always drink gin with the mark, son." (drink name and line).
- Floyd: "I KNOW I gave him four THREES." (crab cakes name and line).

- Gondorff entering the poker game late; Lonnegan: "The name's Lonnegan, DOYLE Lonnegan!" (house cashews and coffee notes).
- Tagline "...all it takes is a little Confidence." (T).
