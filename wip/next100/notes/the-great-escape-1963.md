# The Great Escape (1963)

Layout: canteen (kept). Venue: Stalag Luft III, the prisoners' mess (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Wikipedia: the POWs "are moved to a new camp" (linked to Stalag Luft III) under Colonel von Luger. The canteen is the camp itself; "Prisoners' mess" in the sign is descriptive.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Escape_(film) (plot, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Escape_(film)):
- Bartlett proposes breaking out 250 men (stew line); three tunnels, Tom, Dick and Harry (sausage rolls, wink).
- Ashley-Pitt conceals the excavated dirt in plain sight (dip name and line); Blythe forges documents; Cavendish leads a choir to mask the sounds (winks, house tea note).
- Hilts, Hendley and Goff brew potato moonshine and celebrate the Fourth of July (drink, cue).
- The exit comes up 20 feet short of the woods (wink).
- 76 escapees make it through the tunnel (number).
- Hilts rides a stolen motorcycle for the Swiss border and fails to clear the frontier fence (rosti line, wink).
- Hilts in the cooler resumes his solo game of catch (ice cream sandwich name and line, wink).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Great_Escape_(film)):
- Note, Hilts: "I haven't seen Berlin yet. From the ground, or from the air." (verbatim, first two sentences).
- Von Luger: "We have in effect put all our rotten eggs in one basket. And we intend to watch this basket carefully." (house eggs).
- Von Luger: "Seventeen escape attempts." Hilts: "Eighteen, sir." (prisoner tag).
- Bartlett of the homemade vodka: "It's shattering!" (drink line).
- Tagline "Put a fence in front of these men...and they'll climb it." (T).
