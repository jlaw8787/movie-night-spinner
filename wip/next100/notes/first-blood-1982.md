# First Blood (1982)

Layout: campfire (kept). Venue: Search Party Mess, Hope, the command post below the ridge where Teasle runs the manhunt and Trautman talks to Rambo over the radio. The plan's "survival camp in the Hope woods" became the search party's own mess, since that is a real place in the film (the posse, state police and National Guard camp at the foot of the ridge) and it suits a camp mess card.

## Facts and quotes used
- Rambo arrives in Hope, Washington, Teasle drives him out, arrest for vagrancy, Galt, escape with his knife, mine, gas station explosion, blackout, Trautman. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Blood (plot, read raw)
- Note (quote): "All I wanted was something to eat, but the man kept pushing, sir." Rambo to Trautman on the radio. https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/First_Blood
- "There's a diner about thirty miles up the highway." Teasle. Same Wikiquote page (dish line and wink).
- "They drew first blood, not me." Rambo. Same page (wink).
- "Covey leader calling Raven." Trautman on the radio. Same page (wink).
- "Come first light I'm gonna put every man I got up on that ridge." Teasle. Same page (drink line).
- "eat things that would make a billy goat puke" Trautman. Same page (house line, paraphrased as reported speech).
- "Vagrancy, wasn't it?" Trautman; concealed weapon (the knife). Wikiquote and https://filmsite.org/firstblood.html (prop: vagrancy, one knife).
- "Two hundred men against your boy" Teasle. Wikiquote (wink).
- "In town you're the law. Out here it's me." Rambo. Wikiquote (strip heading).
- Rambo spears a wild hog with his knife tied to a branch and cooks it; he stitches his wound with the hook and line from the knife handle. https://rambo.fandom.com/wiki/John_Rambo_on_First_Blood?oldid=7554
- Gas station goes up in flames: Wikipedia plot ("destroys his stolen truck in a gas-station explosion").

## Unsure
- The hog and fishing line details come from the Rambo Fandom page only (Wikipedia plot does not mention them). I am confident both are in the film but did not see a second source.
- Rainier beer (house drink) is a Washington brand used as a plain menu item, not claimed to be in the film.
- Stills strip uses still numbers 2, 5, 0 (the town arrival, the knife, the woods) to avoid TMDB artwork stills.
