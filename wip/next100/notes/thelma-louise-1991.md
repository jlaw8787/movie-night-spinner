# Thelma & Louise (1991)

Layout: diner (kept). Venue: Louise's Diner, the Arkansas diner where Louise waits tables at the start (changed from the lead's generic "road trip diner"). The film never gives the diner a name that I could find, so the venue reads as "Louise's".

## Venue
- AFI Catalog: "the Arkansas diner where Louise works was filmed at DuPar's Restaurant in Thousand Oaks"; "Louise drinks coffee and tries to formulate a getaway plan." https://catalog.afi.com/Film/59064-THELMA-LOUISE
- Shmoop scene 1: Louise is "waiting tables with toughness and grace in a little old diner in who-knows-where, Arkansas." https://www.shmoop.com/study-guides/thelma-and-louise/scene-1-summary.html
