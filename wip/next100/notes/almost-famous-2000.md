# Almost Famous (2000)

Layout: hotel (kept). Venue: Continental Hyatt House, Sunset Strip, Los Angeles (changed from the lead's general "The tour hotels, Stillwater" to one named hotel the story uses).

## Venue
- Movie-locations: "Following the band to Los Angeles, Miller stays in the Continental Hyatt House"; the tour also stops at the King of the Road Motor Lodge in Tempe (the "Don't take drugs!" message), the Plaza (Penny's overdose) and the Gramercy Park Hotel. https://www.movie-locations.com/movies/a/Almost-Famous.php
- Wikipedia (production): the "golden god" line was inspired by Robert Plant at the "Riot House", the Hyatt's nickname (dish name). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almost_Famous
