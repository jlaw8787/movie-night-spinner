# The Notebook (2004)

Layout: invite (kept). Venue: Windsor Plantation, Seabrook, South Carolina (changed from the lead "Seabrook summer dance", which neither source shows). The invite is stamped 1940, the summer the story opens (layout year remapped with words "2004" to "1940").

## Venue
Noah takes Allie to Windsor Plantation in the summer of 1940 and tells her his dream of repairing the abandoned house; after the war he rebuilds it, and Allie finds him there seven years later.
- Wikipedia: "He takes her to Windsor Plantation, telling her about his dream to repair its abandoned house." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Notebook

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Notebook):
- The story opens in 1940 at a carnival in Seabrook Island, South Carolina (sign, peanuts dish name, year).
- Noah writes to Allie every day for a year; her mother intercepts the letters (T, crab line).
- Noah rebuilds the Windsor Plantation house and can't sell it (shrimp and grits line).
- Duke is Noah; he reads her the notebook daily (dessert line, wink); Allie remembers it is their story (cue).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Notebook_(2004_film)):
- Note, Young Noah: "Yes. It wasn't over. It still isn't over." (printed as "It wasn't over. It still isn't over.").
- "I wrote you 365 letters. I wrote you every day for a year." (crab line, number 365, prop, wink).
- Noah hangs from the Ferris wheel asking "Will you go out with me?" (peanut line, wink).
- "If you're a bird, I'm a bird." (chicken line, printed without the comma as a nod).
- Allie lying in the middle of the street: "What happens if a car comes?" Noah: "We die." (drink line, wink).
- Allie: "Did you write that?" Duke: "No, that was Walt Whitman." (wink).

## Unsure
- The T line ("He wrote to her every day for a year.") is a plain line from the plot, not the film's tagline.
- The prop card "My Dearest Allie, Letter 365 of 365" borrows the opening of Noah's last letter on Wikiquote; the film does not number the letters.
- House lines (pimento cheese, Coca-Cola) are plain menu jokes.
