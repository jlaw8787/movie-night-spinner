# Commando (1985)

Layout: galley (kept, words remapped from a ship to an airliner). Venue: Flight to Val Verde, "Eleven hours to Val Verde".

## Venue
The plan lead was the seaplane, which has no galley. The flight to Val Verde has one: the cabin announcement promises beverages, then dinner, then the film, and Matrix kills Henriques in his seat ("dead tired") before jumping off at takeoff. Cindy is an off-duty flight attendant too. The galley layout stays; its ship words (Ship's mess, From the wheelhouse, Mess call, Printed by the ship's system, Deck 3) are remapped to airliner words.
