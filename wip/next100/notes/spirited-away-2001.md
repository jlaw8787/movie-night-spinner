# Spirited Away (2001)

Layout: hotel (kept). Venue: Yubaba's Bathhouse, the bathhouse for the spirits that the witch Yubaba runs (the plan's lead, named for its owner since the film's Japanese name for it was not on the pages I opened). Family film: everything alcohol-free.

## Venue
- Wikipedia: "the witch who runs the bathhouse"; Yubaba: "It's a bathhouse for the spirits. It's where they come to replenish themselves." (Wikiquote) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirited_Away and https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Spirited_Away

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (plot read raw, link above):
- Chihiro is ten (number 10); her parents eat at an empty restaurant and turn into pigs (pork buns line); No-Face gives the workers gold for food (sushi line); Yubaba takes her name; the polluted river spirit; Boh turned into a mouse; Zeniba's hairband; Haku is the spirit of the Kohaku River (winks); train tickets from Kamaji to see Zeniba (ticket prop).
Wikiquote (link above):
- Note, Yubaba: "This is a high-class place I'm running here." (verbatim).
- Kamaji to Lin: "What if I give you this roasted newt, hmm?" (yakitori line).
- Zeniba: "So, you all made it. Sit down, I'll make some tea." (drink line).
- Yubaba: "It's a bathhouse for the spirits." (sign).
Tofugu (https://www.tofugu.com/japan/spirited-away-food/):
- Haku gives Chihiro onigiri "after she finds out her parents really were turned into pigs" (cue, onigiri line).
- Lin feeds konpeito to the soot sprites (house candies); Lin gives Chihiro an anman, a red bean bun (dessert line); No-Face's feast includes a sushi platter.
