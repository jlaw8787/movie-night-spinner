# Cast Away (2000)

Layout: campfire (kept). Venue: Chuck and Wilson's Island Camp, the island where Chuck lives for four years (the plan's lead, "The island, Chuck and Wilson"). The summer camp stock labels are remapped to the island (Off the reef, Island mains, To drink, On Memphis time, Said to Wilson, Washed up on the beach, Ask Wilson about, Welcome to, Tonight's feature).

## Venue
- Wikipedia: the FedEx cargo plane "crashes into the Pacific Ocean, leaving Chuck as the only survivor"; he washes ashore on a deserted island; "the plane was so far off course". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cast_Away
- Wikiquote intro: "stranded on an island after his plane crashes on a flight over the South Pacific" (sign). https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Cast_Away

## Facts and quotes used
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Cast_Away):
- Note, Chuck to Wilson: "You wouldn't have a match by any chance, would you?"
- Chuck: "Gotta love crab." and "Coconut milk is a natural laxative." (crab line, house coconut milk).
- Chuck lights a fire successfully (stage direction) (cue).
- Chuck: "I'm always gonna keep this on Memphis time. Kelly time." (ribs line, cue heading).
- Stan: "One Dr. Pepper, two cups of ice." Chuck: "I like ice." (drink name, line and note).
- Stan pouring wine: "It's just grape juice. Right, Chuck?" Chuck: "it's like a 1992 full-bodied grape juice." (house drink).
- "We'll just have to make some more out of videotape." (rope wink).
- Pilot: "Tahiti Control, FedEx 88." (number 88).
- Tagline: "At the edge of the world, his journey begins." (T).
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cast_Away, plot read raw):
- Chuck learns firestarting, spearfishing and opening coconuts (ceviche line, snapper line).
- He does not open the package with golden wings painted on it, and later returns it (pavlova line, prop: never opened).
- He pulls his tooth with a rock and the blade of an ice skate (wink); the Wilson volleyball (wink); Kelly's grandfather's pocket watch (wink).

## Unsure
- "Chuck learns to spear fish": Wikipedia says spearfishing; the dish is a snapper, the film's fish is not named.
- The ribs and pavlova are menu dishes tied to Memphis and the package; neither food is claimed in the film.
- Wikipedia calls the wings pink in the opening and golden later; the prop only says "Angel wings on the box".
