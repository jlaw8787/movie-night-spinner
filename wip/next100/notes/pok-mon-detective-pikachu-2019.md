# Pokémon Detective Pikachu (2019)

Layout: cafe (kept). Venue: Hi-Hat Cafe, Ryme City, the all-night diner Tim and Pikachu visit, with the Noctowl neon sign and a Jigglypuff singing a customer to sleep (a named place for the lead's "Ryme City cafe"). Family film: everything alcohol-free (the mocha has coffee for grown-ups; kids skip the shot).

## Venue
- Looper: "Tim and Pikachu visit a diner called the Hi-Hat Cafe", Noctowl neon on the facade, a Jigglypuff in the cafe "singing to a patron who falls asleep on the table"; a Snorlax asleep in the road; the city's 10th anniversary parade. https://www.looper.com/152549/details-you-missed-in-pokemon-detective-pikachu
- Bulbapedia lists Hi-Hat Café among the film's locations. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hi-Hat_Café
