# Dirty Harry (1971)

Layout: truck (kept). Venue: Burger Den, "Pine and Battery, San Francisco".

## Venue
The plan lead was "Harry's hot dog stand lunch". The real place is the Burger Den, a corner burger joint where Harry sits at the counter for a hot dog before the bank robbery (Reel SF). Street signs behind him read Pine and Battery (Reel SF; the scene was shot on the Universal back lot). The truck layout's window-and-board look suits a corner snack bar, so it stays.
