# The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023)

Layout: placemat (kept). Venue: Punch-Out Pizzeria, Brooklyn (the plan's lead, checked). Family film: everything alcohol-free.

## Venue
- Super Mario Wiki: at Punch-Out Pizzeria Mario and Luigi watch their plumbing commercial on the wall TV, Giuseppe plays the Jump Man arcade cabinet, Spike eats a slice while insulting them; late in the film Bowser knocks Mario into the pizzeria and the TV replays the commercial. https://mariowiki.com/Punch-Out_Pizzeria
- Wikiquote has the Spike scene (napkin, "Brooklyn's favorite failures"). https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Super_Mario_Bros._Movie

## Facts and quotes used
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Super_Mario_Bros._Movie):
- Note, Donkey Kong: "Ha! He got the Blue Mushroom!" (verbatim, after Peach stuffs the Mini Mushroom into Bowser's mouth; dessert, cue).
- Spike: "Well, well, well, if it isn't Brooklyn's favorite failures, the Stupid Mario Brothers." (meatball heroes line, spelt favourite on the menu, no quote marks).
- Luigi answers the phone "Hello, Super Mario Brothers" (badge title). "Fire Peach" (arancini line). Bowser singing "Peaches", Kamek interrupts (iced tea; song title only, no lyrics).
Super Mario Wiki (Punch-Out page above):
- Giuseppe on the Jump Man cabinet (garlic knots). The brothers' usual order, "A large half red pepper and half green pepper pizza", cited by the wiki from the tie-in site SMBPlumbing.com (pizza).
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Super_Mario_Bros._Movie, plot read raw):
- Brooklyn plumbers, the Warp Pipe from the sewer to the Mushroom Kingdom (house breadsticks), Cat Suit fight with Donkey Kong, go-karts on Rainbow Road and the blue-shelled Koopa General (house soda, wink), Toad's Ice Flower in the bouquet, Luigi saves Mario with a manhole cover, Peach shrinks Bowser with a blue Mini Mushroom (winks).

## Unsure
- The usual pizza order comes from the tie-in website, not a line in the film; the dish line says "The brothers' usual at Punch-Out", which matches the site.
- Prop line "Leaky faucets fixed" is a plain business-card line, not a quote.
