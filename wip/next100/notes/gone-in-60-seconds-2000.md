# Gone in 60 Seconds (2000)

Layout: truck (kept). Venue: Otto's Garage, where Memphis and Otto put the crew together and plan the 50 cars. Kept the plan's venue lead; the truck board reads as the heist night menu on the garage wall. The board headings come from flavour look (Starters, Off the Grill, Sweet, Sway's Bar; board title Heist Night) and stock lines are remapped in words.

## Facts and quotes used
- 50 cars in 72 hours for Calitri, Kip in danger of the car crusher, Otto as mentor, crew (Donny, Sphinx, Sway), list written in UV paint found with a blacklight, cars delivered to Atley at the docks, Otto's dog eats and passes the keys, Toby shot, Eleanor saved for last, tow truck ramp jump on the Vincent Thomas Bridge, closing barbecue. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gone_in_60_Seconds_(2000_film) (plot, read raw)
- Car 50 is a 1967 Shelby GT500, code name Eleanor, the last on the list. Same Wikipedia page and https://Filmsite.org/gonein60seconds.html
- Note (quote): "Eleanor is Memphis' unicorn." Donny. https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Gone_in_60_Seconds
- Sphinx is mute until his one speech at the barbecue. Wikiquote (his speech) and https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gone_in_Sixty_Seconds
- Sway is a mechanic by day and a bartender by night; she feeds the injured Toby at the barbecue. Filmsite page above. Wikiquote has Sway: "I've got two jobs."

## Unsure
- Otto's shop is never given a trade name in my sources, so the venue is just "Otto's Garage".
- "50 cars, 72 hours, one night": 72 hours is the deadline from Wikipedia; the crew chooses to steal them all in one night (also Wikipedia).
- Tecate and Corona are plain LA menu items, not claimed to be in the film.
