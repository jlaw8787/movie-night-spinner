# Twins (1988)

Layout: diner (kept). Venue: Sun God Lodge, Paseo del Pueblo Sur, Taos, New Mexico (changed 15 Nov, see Round 1 fix below; was Roadside Cafe, Arroyo Seco).

## Venue
The road trip from Los Angeles through New Mexico to Houston is the heart of the film. 80s Film Locations lists an unnamed roadside cafe at 619 NM-150, Arroyo Seco, used for the road trip, near the Sun God Lodge and the Mabel Dodge Luhan House in Taos. No source names the cafe or says what is eaten there, so it is called "Roadside Cafe". Food is New Mexican diner food (green chile, sopaipillas).
