# The Prestige (2006)

Layout: tearoom (kept). Venue: The Grand Scala, the London theatre on Angier's handbill for The Real Transported Man, served as an interval tea upstairs. The plan's lead was a Victorian theatre in London; this is the film's own named one. Courses are renamed for Cutter's three acts (The Pledge, The Turn, The Prestige), and prices print in shillings and pence (flavour coin "lsd").
