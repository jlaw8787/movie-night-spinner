# The Flintstones (1994)

Layout: drivein (kept). Venue: Bedrock Drive-In (changed from the lead's RocDonald's). Family film: everything alcohol-free.

## Venue
The lead's RocDonald's comes from the McDonald's tie-in marketing (Wikipedia, Marketing); only the fandom wiki lists it as a film location, with no scene. The drive-in is a scene sources confirm: the film opens and closes at the Bedrock Drive-In, the family gets something to eat there, and the screen advertises Tar Wars. The drivein layout fits a drive-in theatre as is.
- MeTV: "Fred drives (scampers) his family to the drive-in theater", the movie showing is Tar Wars. https://metv.com/lists/8-things-you-probably-forgot-about-the-live-action-flintstones-movie
- Flintstones fandom wiki: the film opens and ends at "the Bedrock Drive-In Theater"; at the end the family "gets something to eat"; Tar Wars advertised at the drive-in. https://flintstones.fandom.com/wiki/The_Flintstones_(film)

## Unsure
- No source says what the family eats at the drive-in; the bronto ribs line claims only the drive-in night, not the food.
- The ice cream truck detail comes from Tropedia only.
- The bay number 38 on the ticket box is the age line, not a number from the film.
