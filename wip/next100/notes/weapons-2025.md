# Weapons (2025)

Layout: canteen (kept). Venue: Maybrook Elementary Cafeteria, the school where Justine Gandy teaches third grade (the plan's lead, Maybrook Elementary). The sign includes "School" so the canteen prints school periods, a lunch ticket and a main tray. The prop is Mrs. Gandy's roll call: present 1, absent 17.

## Venue
- Wikipedia: seventeen children in Maybrook, Pennsylvania, all students of Justine Gandy's third-grade class, vanish; only Alex Lilly remains. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons_(2025_film)
- Atlas of Wonders: Brockett Elementary School in Tucker stood in for "Maybrook Elementary School" in the film; the liquor store is "USA Liquors". https://www.atlasofwonders.com/2025/10/where-was-weapons-filmed.html
