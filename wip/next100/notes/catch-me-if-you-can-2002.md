# Catch Me If You Can (2002)

Layout: airline (kept). Venue: Pan Am First Class, "Pan American World Airways, Miami to Madrid".

## Venue
Frank poses as Pan Am pilot Frank Taylor and escapes from Miami to Madrid after walking through the airport with eight new stewardesses (Wikipedia plot). Pan Am first class is the film's glamour, and the airline layout fits it. Food follows Maxim's of Paris catering for Pan Am's President Special first class (filet mignon plated from the aisle cart, hot hors d'oeuvres at cocktail time, Charles Heidsieck champagne).
