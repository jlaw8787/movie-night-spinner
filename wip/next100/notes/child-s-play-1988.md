# Child's Play (1988)

Layout: invite (kept). Venue: Andy Barclay's Birthday, Chicago (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Wikipedia: "Widow Karen Barclay's six-year-old son Andy wants a Good Guy doll for his birthday"; Karen buys one from a peddler and gives it to him. The film is set in Chicago. An invitation to Andy's birthday fits the invite layout; Chucky signs the RSVP.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child%27s_Play_(1988_film) (plot, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia:
- Norris chases Charles Lee Ray, the "Lakeshore Strangler", through Chicago (hot dog line, wink).
- Ray invokes Damballa and a voodoo spell; lightning strikes the toy store (griot line, wink).
- Andy wants a Good Guy doll for his birthday (mac and cheese line). Andy is six (number 6).
- The doll moves and talks without batteries; Andy rides a train to Eddie's (winks).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Child%27s_Play_(1988_film)):
- Note (quote), Chucky's repeated line: "Hi, I'm Chucky. Wanna play?"
- Karen: "the batteries fell out" of the box (prop line, wink). Chucky: "We're friends until the end, remember?" (wink "Friends till the end", unquoted).
- Tagline: "Andy Barclay has a new playmate who's in no mood to play." (T)
Point Horror recap #194 (https://www.pointhorror.com/?p=5847, read with WebFetch):
- Birthday morning: "blackened toast with an ice cream scoop's worth of butter", cereal with milk "overflowing" and "a disgusting amount of sugar dumped in", and orange juice, while Andy watches the Good Guys cartoon (toast line, house cereal, mimosa line).
- Karen "haggles the peddler down from fifty bucks to thirty" (prop line).
- Maggie is "eating Andy's birthday cake" while she babysits (cake line, cue).
- Andy gets on the L train (wink). John Bishop is "about to make tea" when Chucky appears (house tea).
