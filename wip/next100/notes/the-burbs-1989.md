# The 'Burbs (1989)

Layout: invite (kept). Venue: The Klopek House, "A welcome visit, Mayfield Place".

## Venue
The plan lead was a backyard barbecue on Mayfield Place. No barbecue is in the plot pages. The best real food scene is Carol's welcome visit to the Klopeks, where Ray eats the pretzel and the room-temperature sardine, Hans makes coffee and Rumsfield's brownies go through the porch. An invite to that visit fits the invite layout. Mayfield Place is the cul-de-sac's name (Wikipedia, production).
