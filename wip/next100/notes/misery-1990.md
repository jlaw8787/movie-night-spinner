# Misery (1990)

Layout: home (kept). Venue: The Wilkes Farmhouse, outside Silver Creek, Colorado (the plan's lead, "Annie's farmhouse"; named "The Wilkes Farmhouse" because the home layout prints "From the <venue>").

## Venue
Wikipedia: after the crash "Annie Wilkes finds him and brings him to her remote home", where he is kept until "the telephone lines are reconnected and the local roads are cleared". The farmhouse kitchen suits the home layout.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misery_(film) (plot, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia:
- Paul finishes a manuscript at a cabin in Silver Creek, Colorado, and crashes his Mustang in a blizzard (sign, wink).
- Annie buys typing paper; Paul insists on smudge-free paper (wink, house coffee note). He uses a bobby pin on the door (wink).
- He knocks the penguin figurine and puts it back facing the wrong way (pudding line, wink).
- He pours his crushed painkillers into her wine glass; she spills it before drinking (meatloaf line).
