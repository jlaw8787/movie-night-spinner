# Killer Klowns from Outer Space (1988)

Layout: candybar (kept). Venue: The Big Top, Crescent Cove (the plan's lead, "the klowns' big top").

## Venue
Wikipedia: Farmer Green "stumbles upon a circus tent-like structure", and Mike and Debbie find "it is the object and a spaceship". Farmer Green on Wikiquote: "Where's the dang ticket booth?" A candy bar at the big top fits the candybar layout; its cinema "From the booth" label is remapped to "From the station desk" for Mooney's line.
