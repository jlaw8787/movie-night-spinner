# Hot Shots! Part Deux (1993)

Layout: banquet (kept). Venue: President Benson's State Dinner, in honour of Prime Minister Soto.

## Venue
The plan's lead was Saddam's palace dinner. I found no dinner at the palace on any page I opened: the palace scenes are the break-in, the fireplace swing and the sword fight. The film's real banquet is President Benson's dinner with the Japanese Prime Minister, which spoofs George H. W. Bush being sick at a Tokyo state dinner. Soto, Benson's speech to Japanese businessmen and the "first lady" exchange all belong to that thread, so the banquet is the President's state dinner.
- TV Tropes: "After losing his lunch to the sight of dinner, President Benson recovers his dentures from the bile". https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Film/HotShotsPartDeux
- Wikipedia, George H. W. Bush vomiting incident: "The incident is spoofed in the 1993 comedy film Hot Shots! Part Deux." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_H._W._Bush_vomiting_incident
- Wikiquote: Soto: "President Benson, where's your first lady?"; Benson to a roomful of Japanese businessmen: "begging you not to make such good cars." https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Hot_Shots!_Part_Deux
