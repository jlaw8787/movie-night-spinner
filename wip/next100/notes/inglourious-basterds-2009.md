# Inglourious Basterds (2009)

Layout: cafe (kept). Venue: Chez Maurice, Paris, the restaurant where Goebbels lunches with Zoller and Shosanna and Landa orders the strudel (changed from the lead's unnamed "Paris cafe").

## Venue
- movie-locations.com names the strudel restaurant "Chez Maurice" (shot at Cafe Einstein, Berlin) and the basement bar "La Louisiane". https://movie-locations.com/movies/i/Inglourious-Basterds.php
- Inglourious Basterds Wiki, Chapter Three: Goebbels and his translator at "a Parisian restaurant"; Landa orders two strudels, an espresso for himself, a glass of milk for "Emmanuelle", and says "Wait for the cream." https://inglouriousbasterds.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_Three
- Mashed also mentions Chez Maurice. https://mashed.com/592427/the-truth-about-the-strudel-scene-in-inglourious-basterds
