# The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014)

Layout: hotel (kept). Venue: The Grand Budapest Hotel, Zubrowka, 1932 (the plan's lead). Year stamps remapped from 2014 to 1932 with words ("In your suite 1932"); the hotel layout's stock "From the grill" reads "Mains".

## Venue
Monsieur Gustave is concierge of the Grand Budapest in 1932 when Zero starts as lobby boy; the hotel is the film's centre and is shown throughout.
- Wikipedia: "In 1932, Zero flees his war-ravaged country to work as a lobby boy for Monsieur Gustave H., the philandering concierge at the Grand Budapest Hotel." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grand_Budapest_Hotel

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grand_Budapest_Hotel):
- Zubrowka (sign); the main story is set in 1932 (sign, year).
- Madame D.'s will is read at Schloss Lutz by Deputy Kovacs, leaving Boy with Apple to Gustave (duck line, wink).
- Gustave is jailed; Zero places digging tools inside pastries made by Mendl's apprentice baker Agatha (dessert line).
- Checkpoint Nineteen is the prison Gustave breaks out of with Ludwig's gang (drink line).
- The Society of the Crossed Keys, a fraternity of concierges, helps them (trout line, prop).
- Agatha and Zero fall from a balcony into a van full of pastries (wink, cue).
- Pastry section: the courtesan au chocolat mirrors the religieuse, choux with mocha or chocolate glaze and vanilla custard (dessert recipe).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Grand_Budapest_Hotel):
- Note, Gustave: "Rudeness is merely an expression of fear." (verbatim).
- Gustave: "A lobby boy is completely invisible, yet always in sight." and "anticipates the client's needs before the needs are needed." (consommé line, wink, house almonds note).
- Gustave: "When you're young, it's all filet steak, but as the years go by, you have to move on to the cheap cuts." (goulash name and line).
- Gustave: "Boy with Apple, stolen (by us)." (wink). Henckels: "Nobody move; everybody's under arrest." (wink).
- Gustave: "winning the loyalty of a woman like that for nineteen consecutive seasons." (wink, number 19).
GradeSaver prison summary (https://www.gradesaver.com/the-grand-budapest-hotel/study-guide/summary-part-3-prison): "Zero and Agatha bake some tools into cookies and cakes to be snuck into the prison." (dessert line).

## Unsure
- The T line ("A concierge, a lobby boy and a stolen painting.") is a plain line, not the film's tagline.
- Zubrowka bison grass vodka is a real Polish brand that shares the country's name; the film doesn't serve it.
- House almonds and hot chocolate are plain menu jokes.
