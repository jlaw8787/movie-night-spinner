# They Live (1988)

Layout: canteen (kept). Venue: Justiceville Soup Kitchen, the soup kitchen at the church beside the Los Angeles shanty town where Frank takes Nada (the plan's lead). The timetable strip runs on the hidden messages Nada reads through the glasses.

## Venue
- Wikipedia: Frank "invites him to live in a shanty town near a church", which is later destroyed in a police raid. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/They_Live
- IMDb plot summary: "After eating at the soup kitchen and spending the night" and "Nada discovers that the church's soup kitchen is a front". https://m.imdb.com/title/tt0096256/plotsummary
- Black Gate: Frank "introduces him to the shanty town and homeless shelter of Justiceville". https://www.blackgate.com/2018/05/12/carpenter-they-live-1988

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/They_Live, plot read raw):
- Nada finds work at a construction site; Frank later comes to give him his paycheck (egg and bacon roll line).
- The sunglasses show hidden messages in the media telling people to consume and conform (meatloaf line).
- The hacker says the only way is to shut off the signal at its source; Nada blasts the transmitter on the roof of Cable 54 (prop card, number 54).
- Nada and Frank's long fight (cue). IMDb summary also has the long hand-to-hand fight; Black Gate calls it "the protracted alley fight".
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/They_Live):
- Note, Nada: "I have come here to chew bubble gum and kick ass... and I'm all out of bubble gum." (also the sundae line).
- Graffiti: "They live, we sleep" (T).
- Billboard and magazine messages: "OBEY", "CONSUME", "WORK 8 HOURS", "SLEEP 8 HOURS", "STAY ASLEEP"; on US currency "THIS IS YOUR GOD" (timetable winks, footer).
- Nada: "either put on these glasses, or start eating that trash can." (chilli line, as a nod without quote marks).
- Frank: "The steel mills were laying people off left and right. They finally went under." (boilermaker line).

## Unsure
- The winks "Signs that say obey" and footer "On every billboard: Stay asleep" lean on Wikiquote's list of "Subliminal Messages on Billboards and Magazines"; it does not say which message was on which.
- The meatloaf "sign says CONSUME": Wikiquote lists CONSUME among the billboard and magazine messages.
- House lines (day-old bread, tin mug coffee) are plain menu jokes.
