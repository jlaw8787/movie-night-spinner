# The Usual Suspects (1995)

Layout: precinct (kept). Venue: Sgt. Rabin's Office (the plan's lead, "Kujan's interview room", named for the room it really is).

## Venue
Kujan questions Verbal "in a borrowed office belonging to LAPD police sergeant Jeff Rabin", and Verbal builds his story from the clutter in that office. The precinct layout fits as is.
