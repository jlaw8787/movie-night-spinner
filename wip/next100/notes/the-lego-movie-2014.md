# The Lego Movie (2014)

Layout: foodcourt (kept). Venue: Downtown Bricksburg (the lead's Bricksburg, with "Downtown" as an honest description; the film names no food court). Family film: everything alcohol-free. The food court reads as Emmet's morning street: Larry the Barista's coffee shop, the crew's after-work sports bar, croissants and giant sausages.
