# The Fast and the Furious (2001)

Layout: deli (kept; first film on this layout). Venue: Toretto's Market and Cafe, run by Mia, where Brian orders the tuna every day (the plan's lead). The first main is the tuna on white, so the board's "How we stack it" panel shows the tuna sandwich build; both mains are sandwiches, so the course prints as "Sandwiches". Drinks heading is "From the Fridge". The Race Wars ticket prints on the red ticket.

Deli render check: poster, letter board, ticket, stack chart, scenes box and receipt all print cleanly; no cut lines. The stack chart prints shopping lines with their quantities (layout behaviour).
