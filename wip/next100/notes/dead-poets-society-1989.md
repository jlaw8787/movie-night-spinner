# Dead Poets Society (1989)

Layout: canteen (kept). Venue: Welton Academy Dining Hall, Vermont, autumn 1959 (the plan's lead). The canteen reads it as a school: periods, lunch ticket, main tray, hall pass. Prices print in cents for the 1950s.

## Venue
- Wikipedia: "In the fall of 1959, Todd Anderson begins his junior year of high school at Welton Academy, an Episcopal all-male preparatory boarding school in Vermont." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Poets_Society
- Tom Schulman's screenplay: "INT. THE WELTON DINING ROOM - DAY. On the dais in the front of the room is the teacher's dining table. Below them are the students' tables." Keating and McAllister talk there, and the boys eat at a table below. https://cinephiliabeyond.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Dead-Poets-Society-by-Tom-Schulman-Undated.pdf
- Wikiquote has the same Keating and McAllister exchange from the film ("Freethinkers at seventeen?"). https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Dead_Poets_Society

## Facts and quotes used
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Dead_Poets_Society):
- Note, Keating: "No matter what anybody tells you, words and ideas can change the world."
- Keating: "Because we are food for worms, lads." (soup name and line, comma dropped).
- Keating stands on his desk: "I stand upon my desk to remind myself that we must constantly look at things in a different way." (cue, wink).
- Todd: "A sweaty-toothed madman with a stare that pounds my brain." (shepherd's pie name and line).
- Charlie's phone call from God; Keating: "Ding! Thank you for playing" (winks).
- Neil: "He's making me quit the play at Henley Hall." (roast chicken line).
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Poets_Society, plot read raw):
- Keating teaches carpe diem, "seize the day" (rarebit line, T).
- He has them rip out the introduction of their poetry books and walk in the courtyard (winks).
- Neil restarts the Dead Poets Society; they sneak off campus to a cave to read poetry (drink name and line, hall pass).
- Neil wins the role of Puck in A Midsummer Night's Dream (roast chicken line).
- Todd stands on his desk and says "O Captain! My Captain!" (pie name and line, written without the exclamation marks).
- Set in 1959 Vermont (sign, number 1959).

## Unsure
- The dining hall comes from the screenplay; the shooting script may differ from the final cut in detail.
- The hall pass "Signed, N. Perry" is a menu joke on Neil restarting the club; there is no such pass in the film.
- Roast chicken, rarebit and Vermont milk are period school food, not dishes named in the film.
