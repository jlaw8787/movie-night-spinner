# I Know What You Did Last Summer (1997)

Layout: takeaway (kept). Venue: Ollie's Oyster Bar, Southport (changed from the plan's lead, "Southport fish shack, Croaker Queen").

## Venue
Movie Locations: Helen and her friends "celebrate her win at the fictional 'Ollie's Oyster Bar'" (the Old American Fish Company building, 150 Yacht Basin Drive, Southport), and the town is celebrating the "Fourth of July Croaker Festival". Ollie's is a seafood place the film itself shows, so it replaces the generic fish shack. The takeaway layout still fits; its delivery wording is remapped to a takeaway window on the waterfront.
