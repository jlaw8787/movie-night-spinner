# Cocktail (1988)

Layout: tiki (kept). Venue: The Beach Bar, Jamaica (the plan's lead "Jamaica beach bar", written as a plain description because the film never names the bar).

## Venue
Two years after the split with Doug, Brian works at a beachside bar in Jamaica, saving for his own place. The film shot on Jamaica's North Shore (Jamaica Inn, Dragon Bay Beach, Sandals Royal Plantation), but no bar name is given on screen.
- Wikipedia: "Two years later, Brian is working at a beachside bar in Jamaica, hoping to save enough for his own establishment." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocktail_(1988_film)
- IMDb plot summaries: one calls it a "resort Tiki Bar". https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0094889/plotsummary

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocktail_(1988_film)):
- Brian at a beachside bar in Jamaica (skewer line, sign, prop line "Saving for his own bar", number 2 for the two years).
- He meets artist Jordan Mooney in Jamaica (prawn line).
- Doug shows up married to Kerry (curry line).
- Doug bets Brian he can't attract Bonnie (snapper line, wink).
- Uncle Pat lends him the money for his own bar (wink).
- Flanagan's Cocktails & Dreams opens; Jordan whispers she is expecting twins; Brian says drinks are on the house (dessert line, wink).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Cocktail_(1988_film)):
- Note, Doug: "Coughlin's Law: Never show surprise, never lose your cool." (verbatim).
- Doug: "Coughlin's Diet: Cocktails and dreams." and "A bartender is the aristocrat of the working class." (winks).
- Brian: "I am the world's last barman poet!" and the poem's "The Sex On The Beach...the Schnapps made from peach!" and "Bar's open!!" (drink name and line, sign, prop, cue).
- Tagline: "When he pours, he reigns." (T).

## Unsure
- The cue says Brian recites his barman poet poem; Wikiquote gives the poem but not where it is performed (it is in New York, not Jamaica).
- The dessert line is Flanagan's opening in New York, not the Jamaica bar.
- "From the old pro" replaces the tiki layout's stock "From the host" over Doug's line.
- House plantain chips and Red Stripe are plain menu jokes.
