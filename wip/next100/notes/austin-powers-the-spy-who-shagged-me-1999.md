# Austin Powers: The Spy Who Shagged Me (1999)

Layout: club (kept). Venue: Austin's Pad, Swinging London, 1969.

## Venue
The plan's lead, the Electric Psychedelic Pussycat Swingers Club, is the first film's club (1967), not this one. In this film Austin lands in 1969 at a party in his own London pad, where Felicity saves him from Robin Swallows. A swinging party at the pad suits the club layout, so the venue is Austin's Pad.
- Wikipedia: "He arrives at his London residence, where he evades an assassination attempt ... with the help of CIA agent Felicity Shagwell." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Powers:_The_Spy_Who_Shagged_Me
- JH Movie Collection fandom plot: "Austin arrives at a party in his London pad". https://the-jh-movie-collection-official.fandom.com/wiki/Austin_Powers:_The_Spy_Who_Shagged_Me
