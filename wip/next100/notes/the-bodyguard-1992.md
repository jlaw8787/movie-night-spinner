# The Bodyguard (1992)

Layout: club (kept). Venue: Rachel Marron, Live (changed from the lead "Rachel's concert club": the film never names the club, so the menu is the show itself, not a made-up club name).

## Venue
Rachel plays a club show that ends in a riot, and Frank carries her out (the poster image). The club is never named on screen; it was shot at the Mayan in downtown Los Angeles. The menu uses an honest show name rather than inventing a club.
- Wikipedia: "he rescues her from danger after a riot erupts at one of her concerts". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bodyguard_(1992_film)
- movie-locations.com: the Mayan, "where Rachel defies the threats to perform"; Joe Jost's in Long Beach, where Frank and Rachel stop for a drink. https://movie-locations.com/movies/b/Bodyguard.php
