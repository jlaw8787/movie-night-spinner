# Edward Scissorhands (1990)

Layout: home (kept). Venue: The Boggs House, the pastel street below the castle (the plan's lead "Peg's house, the pastel suburb"; written as the family's house, not a made-up street name). Family film (fairytale): every dish and drink is alcohol-free.

## Venue
Peg Boggs takes Edward home to her house in the pastel suburb below the inventor's mansion; the welcome barbecue, the hedges and the Christmas ice carving all happen around it.
- Wikipedia: Edward lives in a Gothic mansion "perched on a hill overlooking Peg's stereotypically pastel suburban neighborhood"; "Peg offers to take Edward to her home." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Scissorhands

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Scissorhands):
- Peg Boggs, a door-to-door Avon saleswoman, drives up to the mansion (pie line, prop, wink).
- The Boggses throw a neighbourhood barbecue welcoming him (skewer line).
- He trims hedges into topiaries (platter line, wink).
- At Christmas he carves an ice sculpture modelled after Kim; the shavings fall like snow (ham line, cue, wink).

- Peg: "Edward... I think you should just come home with me." (meatloaf name and line).
- Bill: "I'm just as pleased as punch." (drink name and line).
- Bill: "Sweetheart, you can't buy the necessities of life with cookies." (house milk note).
