# The Monster Squad (1987)

Layout: invite (kept). Venue: The Monster Squad Clubhouse, the kids' treehouse (the plan's lead). The invite reads as a club meeting: "Treehouse meeting. Members only."

## Venue
The club meets in its own clubhouse, which Dracula later destroys. IMDb's plot summary and Horror Obsessive both describe it as a treehouse.
- Wikipedia: "In revenge for their meddling, Dracula destroys the Squad's clubhouse". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Monster_Squad
- IMDb plot summary: the Squad meets in a tree clubhouse. https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093560/plotsummary
- Horror Obsessive: "Some glass ceilings are the floor of a treehouse." https://horrorobsessive.com/2020/12/09/the-monster-squad-im-in-the-club-arent-i/

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Monster_Squad, plot read raw):
- Sean Crenshaw is club leader; he presents the general his business card, "The Monster Squad" (prop card: Sean Crenshaw, club leader).
- Van Helsing's diary is written in German; Scary German Guy translates it (wink, bratwurst line).
- The amulet "composed of concentrated goodness", vulnerable at midnight (wink, float name and line, number 12 for midnight).
- Eugene's letter to the Army asking for help (wink).
- Frankenstein's monster accepts Phoebe's gift of a stuffed animal before the portal closes (ice cream sandwich line).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Monster_Squad):
- Note, Horace: "Wolf Man's got nards!" (also the meatball sub line, and the cue: Horace kicks the Wolf Man in the groin on Sean's order).
- Tagline: "Never send a man to do a kid's job." (T).
- Horace shoots the Gill-man dead (fish bites line).
- "Monster test" (Sean, Patrick, Horace in unison) (wink).
- Sean: "Wolf Man can not drive a car." (wink).
IMDb plot summary (https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093560/plotsummary):
- Garlic pizza is used to burn a vampire's face during the kids' escape (pizza scroll line). Horror Obsessive also mentions the garlic-covered pizza.
