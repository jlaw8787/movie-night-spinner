# A League of Their Own (1992)

Layout: stadium (kept). Venue: Rockford Peaches Concessions, a Peaches home game in 1943 (the plan's lead, "Rockford Peaches game"). Year stamps remapped from 1992 to 1943 with words, including the footer credit.

## Venue
The film follows the Rockford Peaches through the 1943 season of the All-American Girls Professional Baseball League. The home ground is never named on screen (tryouts are at Harvey Field in Chicago), so the stand is named for the team rather than a made-up ballpark.
- Wikipedia: "They and five others constitute the Rockford Peaches"; the flashback is to 1943. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_League_of_Their_Own
