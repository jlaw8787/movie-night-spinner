# Arachnophobia (1990)

Layout: home (kept). Venue: The Jennings Farmhouse, Canaima, California (the plan's lead).

## Venue
Wikipedia: the spider's crow falls dead "outside the barn of Ross Jennings, a family physician who has moved from San Francisco"; the first nest is in Ross's barn and the second in his wine cellar, where Ross falls through the floor. The family's farmhouse suits the home layout.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arachnophobia_(film) (plot, read raw)
