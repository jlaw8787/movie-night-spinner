# White Men Can't Jump (1992)

Layout: truck (kept). Venue: Venice Beach Courts (the plan's lead), a food truck by the courts in Venice Beach, Los Angeles.

## Venue
The story opens on the Venice Beach courts, where Billy first hustles Sidney, and the film shot there. Wikipedia's plot names no court, so the venue rests on these two pages.
- Courts of the World: the Venice Beach courts "played a key role in the popular '90s hit White Men Can't Jump." https://www.courtsoftheworld.com/blog/must-hoop-the-venice-beach-basketball-courts/
- Spoilertown: Sidney "dominates the Venice Beach courts until Billy Hoyle, a white newcomer, beats him". https://spoilertown.com/white-men-cant-jump-1992
- TV Tropes: the two "work the playground courts of Los Angeles." https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Film/WhiteMenCantJump

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Men_Can't_Jump):
- Billy hustles streetballers who assume he can't play well because he is white (taco line).
- Billy owes the Stucci brothers a gambling debt (hero line).
- They win a two-on-two tournament, grand prize $5,000 (prop, number 5).
- Billy bets his share of the $5,000 that he can dunk, and fails (churros line).
- Billy sinks a hook shot from beyond half court to get Gloria on Jeopardy! (wink).
- Gloria comes back with "Foods That Begin With the Letter Q" and wins $14,100 (quesadilla line, wink, cue).
- "The King" and "The Duck" (wink).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/White_Men_Can't_Jump):
- Note, Sidney: "You either smoke or you get smoked." (from "you either smoke or you get smoked. And you got smoked.").
- Sidney: "You can put a cat in an oven, but that don't make it a biscuit." (biscuit name and line).
- Gloria: "I don't want a glass of water, I want you to sympathize." (agua fresca name and line).
- Gloria: "What's a quince? It's a food, Billy, that starts with the letter Q" (quince in the quesadillas).
- Rhonda: the Vista View apartments (wink). Sidney: "I have four words for you: 'Listen to the Woman'." (wink).

## Unsure
- Wikipedia's plot does not place the courts; Venice Beach rests on Courts of the World and Spoilertown (Spoilertown's summary gets other plot points wrong, such as the dunk bet).
- The T line ("They hustle the courts of Los Angeles.") is a plain line from TV Tropes' wording, not a tagline.
- "Heard on the court" replaces the truck's stock "Overheard at the window" over Sidney's line.
- House Cheetos and Budweiser are plain menu jokes.
