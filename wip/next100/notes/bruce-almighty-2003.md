# Bruce Almighty (2003)

Layout: diner (kept). Venue: The Buffalo Diner (the diner where Bruce parts his tomato soup), Buffalo, New York.

## Venue
The plan's lead holds up. Bruce first tests his powers by parting a bowl of tomato soup in a diner, and God turns up there. The film does not name the diner on any page I opened, so it is called "The Buffalo Diner" after the film's city.
- TV Tropes: Bruce parts his tomato soup; "The diner where Bruce realizes God really did make him almighty"; he "orders soup and proceeds to part it with his powers, but never actually eats any". https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Film/BruceAlmighty
- Wikipedia: Bruce is a reporter for Eyewitness News on WKBW-TV in Buffalo. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Almighty
