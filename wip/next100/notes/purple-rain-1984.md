# Purple Rain (1984)

Layout: club (kept). Venue: First Avenue, Minneapolis (the plan's lead, checked). Stock "Your host for the evening" remapped to "From the stage", since The Kid is a house act, not the host (Billy Sparks owns the club).

## Venue
- Wikipedia: The Kid spends his nights "performing at the First Avenue nightclub"; the Revolution, the Time and the Modernaires hold the club's three house band slots; Billy Sparks is the club's owner. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Rain_(film)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Purple_Rain_(film)):
- Note, The Kid: "I'd like to dedicate this to my father, Francis L. It's a song the girls in the band wrote, Lisa and Wendy." (verbatim; cobbler line, cue).
- The Kid's puppet (wink). Tagline "Before he created the music, he lived every bit of it." (T).
Wikipedia (link above, plot read raw):
- Apollonia 6 debuts at the club (sparkler, number 6); Billy warns The Kid his First Avenue slot is at risk (house bourbon); the "Slow Groove" cassette from Wendy and Lisa; the box of his father's compositions; the motorcycle (winks).
Ultimate Prince (https://ultimateprince.com/purple-rain-moments/):
- Apollonia has to "purify" herself in a freezing lake, and it "ain't Lake Minnetonka" (fish bites).
- Morris and Jerome's "What's the password?" routine (pretzel bites).
- Morris tells a waitress to "keep the change" and then has Jerome get his change (burgers).
- The Time rock the club with "Jungle Love" (jerk chicken; song title only).
- Morris asks for a mirror (house popcorn note).
