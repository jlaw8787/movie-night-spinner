# The Cable Guy (1996)

Layout: tavern (kept). Venue: Medieval Times.

## Venue
Medieval Times is where Chip takes Steven (Wikipedia plot), with the serving wench, the no-utensils joke, the Pepsi and the joust. It is the film's best food scene, and the tavern layout fits a medieval feast hall.
