# Air (2023)

Layout: canteen (kept). Venue: Nike, Beaverton (the plan's lead), Nike headquarters in Beaverton, Oregon, in 1984. Year stamps remapped from 2023 to 1984 with words.

## Venue
The Jordans come to Nike's Beaverton headquarters for the pitch, and most of the film plays out in Nike's offices there.
- Wikipedia: "the Jordans have scheduled a meeting at Nike's Beaverton, Oregon headquarters the following Monday." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_(2023_American_film)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_(2023_American_film)):
- 1984, Nike's basketball division close to shutting down; Rob Strasser, Phil Knight, Sonny Vaccaro (sign, D).
- Jordan the third pick in the 1984 draft (prop line).
- Vaccaro watches Jordan's highlights alongside an Arthur Ashe commercial for his Head rackets (wink).
- Dinner with George Raveling, who coached Jordan at the Olympics, asking for his support (steak line).
- Vaccaro travels to Wilmington, North Carolina, and convinces Deloris (pulled pork line).
- Peter Moore names the prototype "Air Jordan" (cake line).
- Knight assigns the basketball division's entire $250,000 budget (wink, prop, number 250).
- Adidas matches and adds a Mercedes Benz 380SL (wink).
- Deloris calls Vaccaro: Michael signs on condition he earns a percentage of every Air Jordan sold (drink line, cue).
TV Tropes (https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Film/Air2023):
- Note, Sonny: "A shoe is just a shoe until somebody steps into it." Also on American Rhetoric's transcript of the pitch (https://www.americanrhetoric.com/MovieSpeeches/moviespeechairthejordanpitch.htm).
- Sonny talks basketball with a 7-Eleven clerk who is dismissive of Jordan early on (taquito line, wink).
- Phil Knight goes barefoot in his office (wink).
Rolling Stone fact check (https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-features/air-ben-affleck-fact-check-matt-damon-nike-michael-jordan-air-jordan-sonny-vaccaro-phil-knight-viola-davis-1234710002/):
- Sonny makes a big win in Vegas (prawn cocktail line).
