# Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975)

Layout: tavern (kept). Venue: Camelot (the plan's lead). Family film (fairytale): every dish and drink is alcohol-free; the rabbit pie braises in apple juice.

## Venue
Wikipedia: "Arthur leads the knights to Camelot, but changes his mind after the knights in the castle perform a musical number, deeming it 'a silly place'." Camelot is shown on screen, so it stands; the menu leans on the joke that they never go in. AD 932 is the year in the Wikipedia plot.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monty_Python_and_the_Holy_Grail (plot, read raw)

## Facts and quotes used
Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monty_Python_and_the_Holy_Grail):
- AD 932; Patsy bangs coconut shells while Arthur mimes riding (prawns line); the Trojan Rabbit; Tim the Enchanter; the Bridge of Death (winks).
- The Rabbit of Caerbannog kills Bors, Gawain and Ector (pie line "sees off three knights", cue); the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch from Brother Maynard.
- The bridgekeeper's question on the airspeed of an unladen swallow (wings name and line).
Wikiquote (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Monty_Python_and_the_Holy_Grail):
- Note, Arthur: "On second thought, let's not go to Camelot. It is a silly place." (verbatim).
- Frenchman: "Your mother was a hamster and your father smelt of elderberries!" (crumble name and line).
- Head Knight: "We want... a shrubbery!!" (drink).
- Cleric: "And the people did feast upon the lambs, and sloths, and carp, and anchovies" (lamb name and line); "Then shalt thou count to three, no more, no less", then Arthur's "One... two... five!" (prop "Count to three / Not five").
- Black Knight: "'Tis but a scratch." (wink); Brother Maynard: "Armaments, chapter two" (wink "Book of Armaments").
- Dennis: "Help, help, I'm being repressed!" (house bread note).
- Galahad on the bridge: "Blue, no!" (house lemonade "Blue lemonade, or yellow").
- Tagline "Sets The Cinema Back 900 Years!" (T).
