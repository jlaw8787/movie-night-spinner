== Pattern notes (fix once per template, not per film) ==
A. Banquet template: logo panel always overlaps poster bottom, poster tiny in black arch. Breaks rule 1 on every banquet menu.
B. Invite template: poster cut to half width and covered by a black or white logo panel. Breaks rule 1 on every invite menu.
C. Lab template: left half of the hero plate is a blurred smear. Poster is cropped on the right with the ACCESS badge over its title (worst on The Dish, The Fly, The Martian, Total Recall).
D. Tavern template: blurred smear beside the poster, and the round photo is often a crop of the poster, not a new frame.
E. Saloon template: Candy bar tab clipped off the left edge on every saloon menu.
F. Cafe and truck templates: large empty dark areas, truck menu boards half empty.
G. No food names were flagged. Dish names read as real food throughout.
