Patterns seen across many menus (fix once, applies to all):
A. Photo-booth strips (prom style) show cropped, near-black or blue-smear frames.
B. Diner style "THE MENU" banner hides the back photo behind big title text.
C. Lab style badge photo is a tiny crop of the poster (poster repeated as photo).
D. Tavern style leaves a large empty column under the short menu text.
E. Banquet style: price/"As table" line is covered by the photo or place card.
F. Canteen style top time strip cuts text off mid sentence.
G. Truck style black menu board is mostly empty.
