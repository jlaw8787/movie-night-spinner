# Quality pass (owner, Oct 26, 18:58): "We created rules and standards and we have thrown it out the window."
Nothing changes and nothing goes live in this pass. You are a reviewer. You only report.

You get contact sheets of live library menus (both faces of each menu, as the app shows them). Check every menu against these rules (from wip/print/STYLE-LOCK.md, wip/genre/GENRE-BRIEF.md and wip/genre/POSTER-HERO.md):

1. POSTER: the main picture on the front is the film's own poster. It is shown whole, with the title and faces not cropped, and no logo is laid over it.
2. PHOTOS: every other photo is a real, clear frame from this film. Fail it if it is a promo cut-out on a white background, near black, blurry or smeared, an odd crop (part of a face, a beard, a hand, an empty corner), the same image twice on one menu, or the poster repeated as a "photo".
3. NO FLAT ART: no drawn, flat-vector or clip-art pictures (STYLE-LOCK rule 25). Type, rules, borders, paper and real photos only.
4. LOGO: the film's title logo is readable on its panel, in its own colours, not dark-on-dark or light-on-light.
5. TEXT: readable at a glance, with nothing overlapping, cut off, running off the card or hidden behind a photo.
6. REAL OBJECT: the menu looks like a real printed menu from its venue. Fail big empty areas, broken layouts or a face that looks unfinished.
7. FOOD: dish names say what the food is. Flag any that don't.

Report only failures, one line each, in this format:
`Film (Year) | rule number | what is wrong, in under 15 words | severity: high (the owner would notice at once) or low`
If a whole sheet passes, write "sheet N: all pass". Be strict: if you hesitate, report it as low.
Write your report to the output file you are given, and keep the chat reply under 40 words.
