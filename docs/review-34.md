# Review 34: Reel Dinner

**Overall: 8.0 out of 10**

I rendered 159 films in the app at 1280 wide and again at 390 by 844 on a phone, pulled the printed card text, the print list, the phone menu and the Prep sheet for every one, and read the lot. The sample covers 33 of the 40 shared layouts plus 20 hand-built cards, with 30 Australian films, a run of British ones, period films from 1934 to 1963, kids' films and 2024 to 2025 releases, including every film on the must-check list. I also read film pages in menu/ and scanned all 1,004 of them for dashes and unit slips. Photos loaded through the proxy, so the shots below are what a user sees.

Scores by area:

| Area | Score |
|---|---|
| True to the film (venue, facts, quotes) | 8.0 |
| Dish notes (dish first, no repeated touch) | 7.0 |
| Prices, order and era money | 8.5 |
| Template wording that suits the venue | 6.5 |
| Australian English, no dashes | 9.0 |
| Desktop readability and layout | 8.0 |
| Phone menu | 8.5 |
| Prep sheet and shopping list | 8.0 |
| Film pages | 8.5 |

The gap between the two kinds of card is the story. The hand-built cards sit around 9.3. The shared layouts sit around 7.5 and pull the average down.

## What is strong

- The hand-built cards are the best thing in the app. Jurassic Park is a real fold-out park guide with a dining map, "$19.93" rack of lamb and "Hold on to your butts." on the coffee (shot 01). The Big Lebowski puts the menu at Hollywood Star Lanes, 5227 Santa Monica Blvd, and every quote is right: "Mark it zero!", "This is not 'Nam. This is bowling. There are rules.", "Is this your homework, Larry?" (shot 02). Home Alone is a Little Nero's docket for 671 Lincoln Ave, "Paid $12. Keep the change, you filthy animal." Back to the Future is Lou's Cafe on Saturday November 5 1955 with a 35¢ cheeseburger and "Joey's cake. He didn't make parole." The Godfather is Louis' in the Bronx, "THE BEST Veal IN THE CITY". Die Hard is the Nakatomi party invitation with the fists-with-your-toes tip from the man in 4B.
- Era money is handled with real care. Gallipoli is in pence and shillings ("Uncle Jack's lamb roast 2s"), The Man from Snowy River and Australia (1939) too, The Dish (1969) is correctly in decimal dollars ("$0.50" scones, three years after the changeover), Goldfinger's shillings add up ("TOT £1 14s 6d" is right), Mary Poppins is "Cherry Tree Lane roast 2s", Tangled and Shrek use gold coins, Gladiator uses denarii.
- Running totals add up. Hall Pass "CARRIED OVER $45.75" then "RUNNING TAB $59.50" is correct, and so is Goldfinger's yardage column.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere: not on 159 cards, not on the phone menus, not on the prep sheets, not in the 1,004 film pages. No US spellings turned up (color, flavor, favorite) apart from in-world names.
- Most signed quotes are real and correctly attributed: Kingsman "Manners maketh man.", Caddyshack "So I got that goin' for me, which is nice.", Goldfinger "No, Mr. Bond, I expect you to die!", Tombstone "I have not yet begun to defile myself.", The Godfather "A little bit of sugar, and that's my trick.", Four Weddings' toast from Gareth, Shaun's "Have a nice cold pint, and wait for all of this to blow over."
- The Castle is spot on as an Aussie home card: rissoles, the pool room prawns, moselle from the cask "Bonnie Doon, on ice", and no prices, which is right for a family kitchen.
- The phone menu is clean and quick: course tabs, one card per dish, "How to make it" folds, a showtime cue, and a button to the full printed menu. Nothing ran off the side on 158 of 159 films.
- The full-size viewer fixes the small preview problem: click the card and Grease's tri-fold reads well with zoom controls (shot 13).
- The Prep sheet is a proper working document: serves count, total hands-on time, longest time on the night, shopping list by aisle with tick boxes, make-ahead notes and timed steps (shot 11). Guest numbers and diet swaps flow through.
- Film pages are tidy and match the app: same dishes, full recipe for 4, a preview image that matches the current card (I checked Muriel's Wedding against the live card), and the poster (shot 15).

## What holds it back

1. **The same film touch is printed over and over on one card.** This is the most common fault in the shared layouts.
   - Con Air: the bunny turns up five times. "Bunny cake", "Ice-cold fizzy orange. The bunny rides home.", the quote "Put the bunny back in the box.", the cue "Serve dessert when Poe finally hands over the bunny." and the footer "Cameron Poe carrying a stuffed bunny for his daughter." (shot 04)
   - Lilo & Stitch: Pudge five times. "Pudge's peanut butter and banana toasties", "Never for Pudge.", "Pudge gets peanut butter. The rest of us get Spam.", the cue about "Pudge the fish", and the surf report "SPOTTED Pudge, who controls the weather". (shot 05)
   - Crocodile Dundee: the knife five times. "Crisp skin barra. Bring a big knife.", the pass "Bring your own knife", the quote "That's not a knife. That's a knife." and the footer "The big knife on a New York street."
   - Shaun of the Dead prints the same quote twice, once in the pint note ("Have a nice cold pint, and wait for all of this to blow over.") and again as the signed stamp from Shaun. (shot 09)
   - Cars: "Tractor tipping corn", Mater's fridge note about tractor tipping, and "Overheard at the counter: Mater, tractor tipping at night."
   - Airplane!: "Ted's drinking problem biscotti" then "Scotch and soda. Ted's drinking problem." The Devil Wears Prada: "Emily's diet food" then "Emily's diet." Chinatown: "Gittes' nose bandage, courtesy of a knife." on the gin rickey and "Gittes' bandaged nose, courtesy of a sharp blade." in the footer. Braveheart: Murron's thistle three times. Babe: "that'll do" on the pie and "That'll do, pig." on the milk.

2. **Template wording that does not suit the venue.** The shared layouts carry fixed labels, and they leak onto films where they make no sense.
   - Campfire layout prints "CHOW LINE", "MESS HALL MAINS", "TRAIL SNACKS", "CANTEEN DRINKS" and "SESSION OF" on Shrek's swamp, Wolf Creek, Braveheart, Ice Age and Dune: Part Two. On Gallipoli it reads "CHOW LINE / ANZAC COVE MESS" and "SESSION OF 1981", an American army term on an Anzac card. (shot 08)
   - Tearoom layout: Tangled's Snuggly Duckling, a pub full of thugs, becomes "Luncheon and Tea", "Come in, sit down, tea's on" and "Prices include tea for one." (shot 07) Up is "FREDRICKSEN HOUSE KITCHEN, Tea at home, in the front room" yet charges "$15.50" for a roast with "COUNTER 09" and "Form TR-09". (shot 06)
   - Kiosk layout: Lilo & Stitch is "Pelekai Family Table" under a "BEACH KIOSK" banner with "Kiosk licence 626".
   - Pub layout: Kingsman's Black Prince is a Kennington local but the card says "FINE COUNTRY DINING".
   - Lab layout: The Dish and Thunderbolts* both get a "HOT MESS" heading over the mains.
   - The Other Guys: a precinct breakroom marked "Coins only" and "Exact change only" sells a "$12.95" tuna bowl and an "$11.00" whiskey sour.

3. **Too many dish notes lead with the film, or never say what the dish is.** The rule is dish first, film touch second.
   - The Lego Movie: "The crew shares a croissant without Emmet. Warm croissants with leg ham...", "The Kragle is set for Taco Tuesday. Spiced beef...", "Giant sausages and Emmet left behind. Thick pork sausages..."
   - This Is Spinal Tap: "Backstage, the olives came with no pimentos. Pimento-stuffed olives...", "The Shark Sandwich review ran to two words. Crisp battered flake with tartare."
   - Miracle on 34th Street: "Macy's own Santa sends you to Gimbels. Beef chuck in stock..." and "For the house with the cane inside. Fruit pudding steamed 4 hours".
   - Crocodile Dundee II: "Rico wants the photos at the subway. Mick wants lunch. Rump on Turkish..."
   - Jurassic Park: "Life finds a way. Chocolate egg centres." and "Clever girl. Claw-cut chips, lime guacamole."
   - No dish at all: Cars 2 "Takoyaki with Japanese mayo: Mater's wasabi mix-up, not on this plate.", The Fast and the Furious "Fried dill pickle chips with ranch: Mia rings it up with the tuna.", Amélie "Puy lentil salad with shallots: Amélie's hand goes in the grain sack first.", Roman Holiday "Champagne, for lunch: Joe gets cold coffee. Ann's order at the café.", The Fantastic Four "Cherry lime rickey: A toast to Franklin, the newest Richards."
   - Lower-case starts slip through: Back to the Future Part III "brisket and beans at the 1885 festival", Dumb and Dumber "lobster and the works on someone else's money", Con Air "thin beef in soy...".
   - Name and note disagree: Fargo "Walleye fish fry: Lager battered flathead fillets". Inside Out 2 "Oui-Oui iced chamomile apple tea: Honey and apple juice over ice" (no chamomile).

4. **Some facts and quotes are wrong or cannot be placed.**
   - Thunderball signs a line in quote marks to Emilio Largo: "Eat and drink, Mr. Bond. My terrace is the finest in Nassau, and my pool is very well stocked." I cannot place this in the film. It reads as invented and should be a note, not a quote.
   - Braveheart labels "Every man dies. Not every man really lives." as "SAID BEFORE STIRLING". Wallace says it to Isabella in his cell, not before Stirling.
   - Muriel's Wedding: "Van Arkle wedding alternate drop". The swimmer is David Van Arckle.
   - Storm Boy spells the father two ways on one card: "Hideaway Tom's crisp fish and chips" and the quote signed "Hide Away Tom".
   - Goldfinger sits the menu at an English golf club priced in shillings, then fills it with "Miami Beach crab salad cups", "Kentucky ham biscuits", "Fort Knox cornbread bars", fried chicken and a mint julep. The money says Sandwich, Kent. The food says Kentucky.
   - Gallipoli calls itself "Anzac Cove Mess" but serves "Uncle Jack's lamb roast" with pumpkin and peas, "Cairo market hummus and baba ganoush" with dukkah, and sells the rum ration for "4d".
   - American Graffiti's Mel's Drive-In sells "Toad's Old Harper and cola" for "25¢", cheaper than the onion rings. Toad buys the Old Harper at a liquor store; Mel's does not pour bourbon.
   - Lines I could not verify and would check before they stay in quote marks: Strictly Ballroom "Shall we dance?" signed Doug Hastings, Paddington 2 "You don't have to tell me about hard stares, I practically invented them." signed Knuckles McGinty, Matilda "Shall we go inside and have tea and cookies?" signed Miss Honey, Gladiator II "Rome will fall. I need only give it a push." signed Macrinus.
   - Real lines printed without quote marks, so the reader cannot tell them from invented notes: Grease "LEFT ON THE COUNTER Tell me about it, stud. Sandy", Chinatown "A WORD FROM JAKE GITTES Fine. As long as you don't serve the chicken that way."

5. **Australian films draw from the same small larder.** Across the 30 Australian films I read, damper is on 11, sausage rolls on 8, pavlova on 7, barramundi on 7, lamingtons on 5, chicken salt on 5, golden syrup dumplings on 4, party pies on 4 and billy tea on 4. Gallipoli, Wolf Creek, Mad Max 2 and Storm Boy all serve golden syrup dumplings. Wolf Creek and The Man from Snowy River both open with damper and golden syrup. For an Australian user this is where the app looks most generic, which is the opposite of what it should be.

6. **The printed card and the phone menu do not always show the same dishes.** The pub layout drops dishes off the printed card while the phone and print list keep them.
   - Kingsman: "Potted prawns on hot toast" is on the phone menu but not the printed card. "Napoleon brandy, neat" is in the print list but not printed.
   - Hot Fuzz: "Cheese and onion crisps with pickled onions" and "Pint of local bitter" are missing from the card.
   - Shaun of the Dead: "Cheese and onion toastie with brown sauce" and "A proper cup of tea, milk and two" are missing.
   - The same thing happens on Crocodile Dundee, Phar Lap, Red Dog, Footloose and Strictly Ballroom.
   - The pub, kiosk, prom and deli layouts also strip real hyphens, so the card reads "Full English fry up", "Cornetto style strawberry ripple cones", "Pan Pacific canapés", "Liquor store hold up burritos", "Shell on, fried hard" and "Nani's luau slow roast kalua pork".

7. **Prices that do not sit right.**
   - Wolf Creek (2005): "Mick's camp sausage and onion sandwiches $15.50" next to "Outback roadhouse pies $6.50" and "Campfire damper $7.50". A sausage sandwich at more than double a pie is not a roadhouse price.
   - Braveheart: "Scotch broth and lamb 9 pennies" costs more than "Stirling venison with neeps and tatties 6 pennies".
   - Matilda: "Matilda's pancakes $8.75" at a 1996 school canteen, more than double the chilli dogs.
   - American Graffiti (1962): "The double Chubby Chuck $2.50" and chilli dogs at "$1.25" are too dear for a 1962 drive-in.
   - Thunderball: "Palmyra grilled lobster tails 5s 5d" is the same price as "Junkanoo stew chicken 5s 5d".
   - Up: a home kitchen charging "$15.50" for roast sirloin.

8. **Prep sheet slips.**
   - Header times contradict the steps. Ratatouille's crème brûlée header says "Prep 5 min, cook 13 min, then 1 hr to set, cool and chill", but the steps bake for 35 min and chill for 3 hr (shot 11). Hall Pass brownies say "cook 5 min, then 28 min to set and cool", but the 28 minutes is the bake. Grease's chilli cheese fries, Rush Hour's spring rolls and Shutter Island's chowder are also out by 10 to 60 minutes.
   - The shopping list is often written like the recipe, not like a list you shop from: Hall Pass "8 shots of espresso", "3 cups shredded cabbage", "1 tbsp grated ginger"; Ratatouille "1 lemon, half juiced", "1 small bunch chives, snipped", "100g caster sugar, plus extra for the tops"; Kingsman "2 tbsp chopped mint", "500g strawberries, hulled and sliced", "8 dill pickle slices"; Frozen "4 tbsp whipped cream".
   - Default picks leave out a main that is on the card. Hall Pass shows two mains but the prep sheet covers "5 DISHES" and leaves out the "Meanwhile, Cape Cod lobster rolls". It is still in the Choose dishes list, but someone printing the card and the prep sheet together will be a main short.

9. **Readability and layout.**
   - Tombstone on the phone: the header "TOMBSTONE" runs off the right edge of a 390 wide screen (shot 03).
   - In the desktop preview the tri-fold and two-page layouts shrink the notes to about 6 to 7 px. Grease's notes measure 4.5 to 6.4 px, The Castle 7 px, Tangled and Gallipoli 7.2 px (shots 07, 08, 12). The full-size view fixes it, but the preview is what people read first. Muriel's Wedding puts thin handwriting-style notes in grey on a dark board, which is hard going even at full size (shot 14).
   - Course order: Ratatouille's card puts "Le dessert" under the starters in the left column, before "Les plats" in the right (shot 10).
   - Big empty gaps: Up's "Sandwiches and savouries" has one item, Lilo & Stitch has a third of the left column blank, and Grease's middle panel has white space under one burger.
   - Spelling mixes on one screen: the Jurassic Park phone menu says "Visitor Center Restaurant" in the header and "the dark visitor centre" in the cue (shot 16).

10. **Film page details.**
    - 389 film pages print "2 cup ice", "4 cup mint", "1 cup basil" (the app itself says "3 cups"). Hall Pass: "3 cup shredded cabbage".
    - Temperatures are "200C" on the pages (about 6,000 times) and "200°C" in the app.
    - "The menu comes from Anzac Cove Mess, straight out of the film." and "from Porpoise Spit Cafe, straight out of the film." claim a made-up venue is in the film.

## What it would take to reach 9.5

1. Put a one-touch-per-card rule into the build. If a name or phrase (bunny, Pudge, knife, tractor tipping, Emily's diet) is in a dish name, a note, the cue, a stamp or the footer, it may appear once more at most. The signed quote must never repeat a note. Fix Con Air, Lilo & Stitch, Crocodile Dundee, Shaun of the Dead, Cars, Airplane!, The Devil Wears Prada, Chinatown, Braveheart and Babe first.
2. Give each shared layout venue-neutral labels, or swap in a set per venue type. No "Chow line", "Mess hall" or "Session of" unless the venue is a mess. No "Prices include tea for one" or "Counter" or "Form" on a home or a pub. No prices on a family table or a home kitchen. Re-home Tangled to the tavern layout, Up and Lilo & Stitch to a home layout without prices, Gallipoli to a field kitchen or a ration card with Australian army words, and fix Kingsman's "FINE COUNTRY DINING".
3. Rewrite every note that leads with the film or never names the food, so it reads food first, then one short film touch. Start with The Lego Movie, This Is Spinal Tap, Miracle on 34th Street, Crocodile Dundee II, Cars 2, The Fast and the Furious, Amélie, Roman Holiday, The Fantastic Four and Jurassic Park's gift shop. Capitalise the lower-case notes and make the name match the note (Fargo walleye, Inside Out 2 chamomile).
4. Quote audit: every line in quote marks must be checked against the film. Move the Thunderball Largo line to a note. Fix Braveheart's "SAID BEFORE STIRLING". Verify or drop the Strictly Ballroom, Paddington 2, Matilda and Gladiator II lines. Put quote marks on real lines like Grease "Tell me about it, stud." Fix "Van Arckle" and the Storm Boy "Hide Away Tom" spelling.
5. Widen the Australian larder. Give each Aussie film food from its own place and time: Coorong mullet and Goolwa cockles for Storm Boy, Pilbara and Dampier food for Red Dog, Kings Cross and Bondi for Two Hands, 1915 Egypt camp and bully beef for Gallipoli without the Sunday roast, Parkes country show for The Dish. Cap damper, pavlova, lamingtons and golden syrup dumplings so no two films in the same genre share them.
6. Make the printed card show every dish in the print list, and keep the phone menu and the card in step. If a layout runs out of room, shrink or split it, do not drop dishes. Stop the hyphen stripping on the pub, kiosk, prom and deli layouts.
7. Run a price sanity pass per card: mains above snacks and sweets, sandwiches below hot plates, no duplicate prices between a lobster and a chicken stew, and period checks on American Graffiti, Matilda, Wolf Creek and Braveheart. No prices where the venue is a home.
8. Recalculate the prep header from the steps (prep, cook, then wait) so it can never disagree with them. Turn the shopping list into buyable units (a cabbage, a knob of ginger, 250g coffee beans, a bunch of chives, a lemon) and keep cutting words for the recipe. Tick every dish printed on the card by default, or say on the card which ones the prep sheet covers.
9. Fix the Tombstone phone header, lift preview note text to at least 9 px or show a larger first panel, raise the contrast on Muriel's Wedding notes, fix the Ratatouille column order, and fill the gaps on Up, Lilo & Stitch and Grease.
10. On film pages, pluralise cups, use "°C" to match the app, and only say "straight out of the film" when the venue really is in the film.
