# Reel Dinner, review 47

## What I looked at

- The full dump at /tmp/r46/final.json, all 1,000 cards, swept in Python for note length and shape, dashes, printed prices (about 2,200 parsed by layout, decade and currency), British and Australian words on American cards and the reverse, alcohol on family, kids, Christmas and wizard cards, repeated names, stock layout lines against each venue, dishes from other scenes, anachronisms against the setting year, card text against phone text, and duplicate lines in shopping lists.
- Read about 150 cards dish by dish: hand-built one-offs (Godfather, Back to the Future, Pulp Fiction, The Big Lebowski, Home Alone, Die Hard, Ratatouille, Elf), every Harry Potter and Fantastic Beasts card, 20 kids and family, 14 Christmas, 12 British, 14 Australian, 24 prisons and messes, and two seeded random draws of 30 shared-layout cards.
- Opened 15 films live at 1280 and 390 wide: Zoolander, John Wick: Chapter 4, Little Shop of Horrors, The Shawshank Redemption, Apollo 13, Hot Shots!, Gremlins, Crazy Rich Asians, The Conjuring 2, Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban, The Big Lebowski, Onward, Say Anything..., The Flash, The Castle. For each: the printed card, the full-size view, the phone menu with a method open, and the Prep sheet. Then one film per layout (40 layouts) to test the full-size view.
- Checked 50 random film pages under menu/ against their cards, plus 10 one-off pages.
- Screenshots: docs/review-47-01.png to docs/review-47-12.png.

## What is strong

- Note discipline. Across 6,967 dishes there is not one em or en dash, every note ends in a full stop, the longest is 19 words and the mean is 11. Most lead with food and add one short touch: Under Siege "Ryback's spit-free bouillabaisse", The Green Mile "Last meal meatloaf ... The meal before the Mile", National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation "no cat food inside".
- The hand-built one-offs are the best thing in the product. Back to the Future prints "TODAY · SATURDAY NOVEMBER 5 1955" (correct day), "COFFEE, NO SUGAR ... No Pepsi Free" and a 35¢ cheeseburger. Home Alone's Little Nero's ticket has the $11.80 pizza paid with $12. Die Hard's Nakatomi party sheet is a genuine piece of prop design.
- Australian cards sound like home without trying: The Castle ("Sal's rissoles", "Chilled moselle from the cask, by the power lines"), Kenny, Wolf Creek ("Servo dim sims", "Bundaberg rum and dry"), Animal Kingdom ("Jatz, cabana and cheddar platter"), Storm Boy, Top End Wedding, Gallipoli ("Egyptian Stella lager", "from the dixie").
- Old money works: The Dressmaker in pence and shillings, Newsies at 5¢ to 30¢, The Slaughtered Lamb in pence and pounds, Tombstone and Unforgiven at 5¢ to 35¢.
- Alcohol rules hold. Family, Christmas and wizard cards use juice punches, cream sodas and butterbeer. Ratatouille turns Ego's 1947 Cheval Blanc into "Grape juice, 1947", which is a good call.
- The phone menu at 390 is the most polished surface: no sideways scroll, minimum 12px text, course tabs, and the method opens in place (review-47-07, 09, 10).
- Film pages are faithful. 50 of 50 sampled match the card.

## What holds it back

1. **Full-size view is broken on about 200 desktop cards.** On layouts that page the preview (cafe, club, takeaway, invite, placemat, pub, diningcar, bartab), "Click the menu to read it full size" opens the whole sheet with the same clip still applied and no page buttons. Zoolander shows the starters on the left and black on the right, and the footer cuts off at "Serve the drinks when the bo" (review-47-01). Gremlins at FIT shows one small panel in the bottom left of an empty sheet (review-47-02). Hot Shots! shows black on the left half (review-47-03). Onward shows only the snacks (review-47-04). Layouts that use the pager in the zoom (Apollo 13, Little Shop of Horrors) work fine (review-47-05, 06).

2. **Too many cards are a tour of the film rather than one venue's menu.** Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade at "Henry Jones's Study" prints "Venice cod ... toasts", "Austrian castle pretzels", "Circus train ... popcorn balls", "Zeppelin dining room schnitzel", "Hatay lamb kebabs" and "Petra rose and pistachio cake". Raiders of the Lost Ark at The Raven in Nepal prints "Cairo dates", "Cairo market falafel" and "Sallah's koshari". John Wick: Chapter 4 at The Osaka Continental opens with "Bar currywurst" and serves "Steak frites", "Croissant pudding" and a "Cognac sidecar" from Berlin and Paris. Indiana Jones and the Kingdom of the Crystal Skull at "Hangar 51 Canteen" serves "Peruvian ceviche cups" and "Peruvian lomo saltado". Walk the Line at Folsom State Prison serves "Hendersonville Thanksgiving turkey" and "Sun Records Memphis dry-rub ribs". Harry Potter cards do it every time: Prisoner of Azkaban at the Three Broomsticks has "One-Eyed Witch coconut ice ... from the Honeydukes jar"; Chamber of Secrets at the Burrow has "Dursley dinner violet cream puffs"; Secrets of Dumbledore at the Bhutan Election Hall has "Berlin pretzels", "Berlin schnitzel" and "Hog's Head sausage rolls".

3. **Stock lines that do not suit the venue.** Little Shop of Horrors runs the diner layout at "Mushnik's Skid Row Florist": "Open 7 a.m. to 11 p.m.", "Counter service", "Now showing above the counter", priced 95¢ tacos. Priced menus with "Prices subject to change" print at a video store (Scream), a gym (Dodgeball), a comic shop with "Cash only, no refunds" (True Romance), garages (Iron Man 3, Transformers, Hot Rod), a pawn shop (The Crow), a flower shop that delivers (Valentine's Day) and a Christmas shop selling "Petra's stuffed peppers with yoghurt £13.50" (Last Christmas). Family kitchens get trade lines: The Flash prints "Open 6 a.m. to midnight" and "Refills on the coffee." at the Allen Family Kitchen; Hereditary and Saw run the lab layout at home with "Guest No. 18, All rooms"; Rosemary's Baby, The Others and Batman & Robin get "Hang on your door". Every kiosk card prints "Kitchen shuts" even at Diane Court's House and Ben's Roommate House.

4. **Restaurant food in prisons, messes and canteens.** The Italian Job at "Mr Bridger's Prison Table" serves Turin bagna cauda, beef agnolotti in brown butter and bonet. Face/Off at the Erewhon Prison Canteen serves "Church shootout canapés: Smoked salmon and crème fraîche on rye" and "Archer family lemon thyme roast chicken ... carved at a candlelit table". The Suicide Squad at the Belle Reve Mess Hall serves "Bocconcini, cherry tomato and basil on toothpicks, balsamic dots". The Silence of the Lambs at the State Hospital Canteen serves lamb shanks in red wine and red wine poached pears. Shawshank's mess hall closes on "Rock hammer rocky road".

5. **Australian words and units on American, British and other cards.** Shrek "Lord Farquaad's Duloc roast chicken ... marylands". The Sound of Music "Uncle Max's cheese kransky board". The Wolverine and Crazy Rich Asians "wombok". Young Frankenstein "Short black espresso" and Inglourious Basterds "A short black, Landa's way". Titanic "Seared tied eye fillet". Edge of Tomorrow (Heathrow) "J Squad sticky date pudding". Grease "Pink Ladies coconut ice". Dumbo "Pink elephant marshmallow slice". Me, Myself & Irene "Hank's shout", and the bar-tab footer "Ring the bell, shout the room" on Hall Pass and Onward. The Flash and Uncle Buck "Now showing in the lounge room", and The Flash "save his mum" and "run to the shop" (review-47-12). Up's cue "sit on the kerb". Every American kiosk card gives the weather in Celsius: Say Anything... "Temp 26°C", The Sandlot "23°C", Raising Arizona "24°C". British spellings on American cards: The Warriors "SHOW YOUR COLOURS", Die Hard "A night patrol favourite", Iron Man 3 "Armour Mark 42", Jurassic Park "In the Visitor Center theatre" (both spellings in one line).

6. **Prices that break their own setting.** The bar-tab layout prices 2005 to 2020 mains at a median $26.10 against $13.25 on placemats of the same years. Onward's family tavern charges "$14.00" for mozzarella sticks, "$30.25" for a mushroom pie and "$14.75" for a sundae (review-47-04). Creed's Philly cheesesteaks are "$26.00"; Horrible Bosses' dive bar charges "$16.00" for a doughnut; Deadpool's chimichangas are "$26.50". The Rock pours "Anchor Steam beer on tap $16.50". Going the other way, Forgetting Sarah Marshall's Hawaii resort sells a mai tai for "$4.50" and a Blue Hawaii for "$2.75" next to "$7.50" grilled pineapple, and Batman's 1989 museum café sells a Kir royale for "$1.00". Euro cards misfire: Leap Year's Dingle pub charges "€9.75" for a pint of Murphy's and "€10.25" for potato farls; Spider-Man: Far From Home prices "Tower Bridge cod and chips €26.00" in euro, in London. Ocean's Eleven's Bellagio charges "$29.00" for matzo ball soup. Cars charges "$6.50" for corn and "$1.95" for a float.

7. **Period food slips.** Peter Pan (1904 London) "Minted pea and feta toasts ... smashed peas". Who Framed Roger Rabbit (1947) "Honey roasted carrots with feta dip" and "Toon-safe French onion dip". It Happened One Night (1934) "Young carrots with herb sour cream dip" and "Cinnamon mocha". Oppenheimer (1943) "Blue corn chips". Monty Python and the Holy Grail "Patsy's coconut prawns ... lime mayo" and "Unladen swallow sticky wings ... honey and soy". The Big Lebowski's 1991 bowling snack bar serves "Persian rug flatbread ... feta dip, pomegranate" and a "Nihilist lingonberry Dutch baby ... whipped crème fraîche". Feta turns up in 70 cards, often standing in for cotija or queso (Coco "Marigold bridge corn ... feta", Encanto "Pandebono ... feta").

8. **A few notes fail the food-first rule or misname the dish.** Face/Off "Peach sparkler :: Castor could eat a peach for hours." Charlie and the Chocolate Factory "Three-course gum roast beef dinner :: The gum that tastes of tomato soup and roast beef." Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban "Tea leaf reading biscuits :: Slice and bake Earl Grey and lemon biscuits." (a method, not a note). The Goonies "Goon Docks crispy fish and chips :: Crunchy battered fish in warm tortillas with lime slaw" (that is a fish taco). Bridget Jones's Diary puts "Bridget's not-blue leek soup" under mains.

9. **Template glitches and drift.** Double commas print on Man of Steel "Martha's farmhouse pot roast,, with a cup of coffee" and The Flash "Nora's spaghetti with tomato sauce,, with a cup of coffee" (review-47-12). "a apple gravy" on the Gremlins phone menu and "into a icing" on The Emperor's New Groove. Friday the 13th prints "Counsellors' cabin shandy" on the card but "Counsellors' cabin lemon squash" on the phone, and four other card dishes do not appear on their phone menu. 83 notes differ between card and phone, and 92 cards say shrimp while the recipe says prawns.

10. **Shopping list tidiness.** About 90 lists split one ingredient across two lines ("1 brown onion" and "2 onions" on The Departed and Shawshank, review-47-08). Boy lists "1 L vanilla ice cream, slightly softened" and "1 L vanilla ice cream". Coming to America lists "white vinegar" and "vinegar". Headings switch between "SHOPPING LIST" and "Shopping list" from film to film. Zoolander's "Day spa cucumber bites" says "cook 6 min" with nothing cooked.

## To reach 9.5

1. Fix the full-size view: when a paged preview opens full size, either clear the clip and show the whole sheet, or carry the page buttons into the zoom (as Apollo 13 and Little Shop already do). Test one film per layout at 1280.
2. Pin every card to one venue. For each card, any dish whose name carries another place (Berlin, Paris, Cairo, Hog's Head, Honeydukes, Folsom's Memphis ribs) moves to the phone extras or is rewritten as something that venue would sell. Start with every Indiana Jones, Harry Potter, Fantastic Beasts, John Wick and Ocean's card.
3. Give non-food venues their own stock lines. No prices, "Prices subject to change", "Form No.", counter, booth, delivery or "Kitchen shuts" lines at homes, shops, garages, offices, studios and gyms. Re-home florists, comic shops and garages onto the home or party layout.
4. Make prisons, messes, hospital canteens and school canteens serve tray food: stews, loaf, beans, cornbread, sheet cake, jelly, cordial. Rewrite The Italian Job, Face/Off, The Suicide Squad, The Silence of the Lambs and Shawshank's dessert.
5. Run an American-card word filter and fix every hit: marylands, kransky, wombok, short black, eye fillet, slice, coconut ice, sticky date, shout, lounge room, mum, kerb, shop, colour, favourite, armour, theatre, neighbour. Show Fahrenheit on American kiosk cards and say "sunny" not "Fine".
6. Price the bar-tab layout and the outliers to the film's year and country: reset Onward, Creed, Horrible Bosses, Deadpool, A Star Is Born, The Rock, Leap Year, Spider-Man: Far From Home and Ocean's Eleven, and make sure no drink undercuts a snack by half at a resort or museum.
7. Add an anachronism check keyed to the setting year: no feta, sourdough, blini, smashed peas, sour cream dips, mocha or lime mayo before about 1960 unless the venue is Greek, Russian or similar.
8. Fix the four notes that skip the food (Face/Off peach sparkler, Charlie gum dinner, Azkaban tea leaf biscuits, Goonies fish and chips) and move soups out of mains.
9. Fix the double-comma join, "a apple" and "a icing", and make card dish names and phone dish names identical.
10. Merge shopping-list lines by base ingredient, pick one heading case, and set cook time to zero on no-cook dishes.
