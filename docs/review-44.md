# Reel Dinner, review 44

What I looked at: the photo-on dump of all 1,000 cards (/tmp/r43b/cards.json, branch fix/review-43 as it stands), swept in Python for every area below (dashes, alcohol on kids, Christmas and wizard films, regional words on printed cards and in recipes, prices and currency against venue type and set year, anachronistic foods, film word and name repeats, note shape, phone text against print, Prep sheet timings and triggers, and every built film page compared line by line with its card and phone text, including the 97 hand-built one-offs). I read 158 cards dish by dish (22 hand-built one-offs, 22 kids and family, 12 Christmas, 10 wizard and fantasy, 16 British, 16 Australian, 60 random). I opened 11 films in Chrome at 1280 and 390 (Home Alone, Goldfinger, The Godfather, Finding Nemo, Back to the Future, My Cousin Vinny, Oliver!, Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, The Castle, Wonder Woman, Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure): printed card, phone text menu with a recipe open, and the Prep sheet on both widths. I did not open the handoff, earlier reviews, ripple notes, audit folder or git history.

## What is strong

- No em or en dashes on any card face, phone menu, Prep sheet, film page or site copy. The only three in app.js are inside regular expressions and never print.
- Kids, family, Christmas and wizard films are dry on the printed card and phone: 229 films checked. The data still says "Pale ale" for the Return of the King cup and "alcohol-free stout" for Violent Night, but both print as ginger beer.
- Local money and era are a real strength: Goldfinger in shillings ("2s 5d", total "£1 12s 5d"), Klaus in "70 kr", Raiders in rupees, Prisoner of Azkaban in Sickles, Phar Lap in pence, The Sapphires in "30c". Godfather prices are period cents (".65", "1.72").
- Australian cards read like home: Finding Nemo's "Coral reef fairy bread" and "Cheese and Vegemite scrolls", The Castle's "Pool room prawns", Animal Kingdom's "Jatz, cabana and cheddar platter", The Dish's "Show day hot jam doughnuts" at "$0.45". British cards are just as sure: Hot Fuzz's ploughman's at "£4.50", Shaun of the Dead's "Corner shop crisps".
- The 97 hand-built one-offs are the best work on the site. Godfather's "THE BEST Veal IN THE CITY" roundel, Do the Right Thing's "Plain Slice .95" with "EXTRA CHEESE COSTS EXTRA", Lady and the Tramp's "Regulars at the back door eat free", Road to Perdition's bus ticket "to PERDITION".
- Notes mostly do the job in one line and one gag: Home Alone's "Buzz ate the last plain cheese. Not this time.", Cast Away's "A 1992 full-bodied grape juice", Free Willy's "Nobody steals a whale chilli cheese dogs".
- Recipes and shopping stay in Australian words under American cards, as intended: Godfather's "150g drained roast capsicum", "40g icing sugar".
- Phone at 390 is tidy on all 11 films: page width stays at 390, smallest text 12.5px, "How to make it" buttons about 44px tall, extras clearly flagged "On the menu, not in tonight's five".
- Film pages for the 900 generated films carry every printed dish, extras included, and all 7,488 dish names match the card.

## What holds it back

1. **Dishes wander away from the venue.** The card names one real place, then serves food from all over the film. In my read of 136 generated cards, about 55 (roughly 40%) had at least one dish from a different place or scene. Skyfall Lodge serves "Macau casino dumplings" and bao "eaten high above Shanghai". Oliver! at Fagin's Den serves "Mr Brownlow's breakfast crumpets", "Workhouse governors' roast chicken" and "Three Cripples pie and mash". Goldfinger's English golf club serves "Miami Beach crab salad cups" and "Kentucky stud farm steak". Wonder Woman's Themyscira Great Hall serves Belgian "Veld frites" and beer stew. Lion's Hobart home serves "Station chai... at the station where Saroo gets lost". Bill & Ted's Circle K serves "Napoleon's chicken Marengo". Taken's LA birthday party serves Paris croque monsieur. Bohemian Rhapsody's Wembley Stadium serves Parsi home cooking "from his mother's table" and prints no prices.

2. **Template words that do not fit the room.** "Now showing" prints on 512 cards. On the 30 kiosk cards it sits under headers like "BAR AND GRILL" for Let the Right One In's courtyard kiosk and The Karate Kid's tournament snack bar, and "KIOSK" for Happy Feet's penguin colony and Halloween Town Square. 27 kiosk and snack cards never print the venue name at all (Lethal Weapon 2, Finding Nemo, Say Anything...). The campfire template says "Left by the fire" and "Fireside Snacks" in E.T.'s closet, A Quiet Place's cellar and Tucker and Dale's holiday home. "Afters" (British) heads the dessert line on 25 American bar cards (Fargo, Top Gun, Road House, Fight Club). 33 lab cards print "Plate A Now showing, 2015" run together. 10 stadium cards print "Hawkers in every aisle", including Space Jam's locker room. 41 banquet cards end on "Form BF 22.". 16 banquet cards stack "A note from the host" over "A note from Diana". Eight cards print lines like "Ask the desk about Powder on the floor for footprints".

3. **Prices miss on both sides.** About 30 public places where people pay print no prices: Casablanca's Rick's Café, The Terminal's JFK food court, Roman Holiday's Caffe Rocca, Orient Express dining car, Toretto's Market and Cafe, The Prancing Pony, Venom's corner store, Twisters' rodeo concessions. Harold & Kumar's White Castle prints "30 sliders" but no price. About 10 offices, labs and squad rooms charge staff: The Usual Suspects' "Sgt. Rabin's Office", Dirty Work's "Revenge Office", Saw II's "Homicide Squad Breakroom", The Fly's "Brundle's Warehouse Lab", Men in Black II's "MIB Headquarters". Mad Max 2 prices 4 of its 9 dishes.

4. **One-off menus lose printed dishes on the phone and film page.** On hand-built cards the phone menu and film page show only the cooked five, so roughly 280 printed dishes on about 60 of the 97 one-offs vanish. The Godfather's phone and page have no "Veal scaloppine", the dish the card's roundel is built around. A Bronx Tale loses "Sonny's Sunday rigatoni", Once Upon a Time in America loses "Moe's brisket". Generated cards already show extras on both, so the one-offs are the odd ones out. The Three Men and a Baby page drops one of its five cooked dishes.

5. **Film page recipes differ from the app on 64 dishes.** Same dish, different quantity: Newsies' page says "8 tsp yellow mustard" where the app says "3 tbsp". Meet the Robinsons says "1/2 jar pizza sauce" where the app says "1 jar". Night of the Living Dead says "1650g tinned peach halves" where the app says "1.7kg". Sinners says "8 440 ml cans of stout". Hand-built pages also reword notes against the card on 8 dishes: My Cousin Vinny ("Butter & sharp cheddar." against the card's "butter & sharp cheddar"), When Harry Met Sally's "Chef salad". Me, Myself & Irene's page lists "An ice-cold beer" that the card never prints. 27 generated pages say "The menu is set at" a venue the card never names. Four accented titles get broken slugs: "am-lie-2001", "l-on-the-professional-1994", "les-mis-rables-2012", "pok-mon-detective-pikachu-2019".

6. **Aussie and British words on American cards.** At least 36 American-set cards print words they should not: "icing" on 15 lines (Dragnet's "vanilla icing", Back to the Future's "VANILLA ICING", 48 Hrs.' "fudge icing"), "rashers" on 48 Hrs., Thor and Unbreakable, "choc chip" on Fast Times, Ant-Man and the Wasp and 22 Jump Street, "pouring cream" and "tyre marks" on My Cousin Vinny, "Takeaway orders" on Die Hard with a Vengeance, and "Sweets" as a section head on about 100 American cards. In the other direction, about 40 recipe steps say "cookies" where the cook at home reads "biscuits" ("sandwich between the cookies", Speed, X2, The Warriors). There is also one "plastic wrap" (Revenge of the Nerds) and one "half-and-half" (The Big Lebowski).

7. **Prep sheet hands-on time is wrong when a dish shows only "Cook".** 191 dishes on 170 cards show a "Cook" time and no prep time, and their work drops out of hands-on. Oliver! claims "hands-on 13 min" for a roast chicken with bread sauce, sticky onions and a porridge brulee. The opposite error shows on 9 dishes with inflated prep: Bad Santa's turkey at "Prep 3 hr 3 min" (card hands-on 3 hr 47 min), Knocked Up's cupcakes at "Prep 2 hr 24 min", Harlem Nights' chicken and waffles at "Prep 2 hr 18 min". Home Alone says "start the day before" only because the cola is chilled overnight.

8. **Notes that break the shape.** 55 notes repeat the character from the dish name ("Mr Brownlow's breakfast crumpets", note ends "Mr Brownlow's breakfast"; Madagascar's "Alex's steak... Sweet, juicy steak."). About 10 open on the gag or plot, not the food: Flashdance's "Alex's water dance on the chair. Gin, cucumber...", Critters' "Krites roll up and fire quills.", Cast Away's "Chuck learns to spear fish.", I Love You, Man's lager note "Lime for Limelight, played at full volume." A few recap plot: American Beauty's "Lester quits and becomes a fry cook", Final Destination 2's "Only new life can defeat Death". On the phone, four notes lose their first word: Mulan "water sweetened with...", Logan "poured cold over ice...", Arrival "swirled with sweet huckleberry syrup", I Love You, Man "for Limelight...".

9. **Film words and names pile up through template slots.** About 60 cards print a film word on 3 or more pieces and about 90 print a character name on 4 or more, mostly because the prop, toast list, note signature and cue each add one more on top of the dishes. Wonder Woman has "lasso" three times ("golden as the Lasso of Truth", "The lasso of truth", "Lasso Tag"). Toy Story has "the claw" four times. Bambi has "Thumper" five times. Top End Wedding has "Lauren" six times. The Sting has "Lonnegan" five times.

10. **A handful of period slips.** Goldfinger (1964) and Enter the Dragon (1973) both serve "sweet chilli". The Greatest Showman (set 1860) has red velvet "with cream cheese frosting". Fright Night and Fletch (both 1985) have arugula. Carrie, The Warriors, Sixteen Candles and Purple Rain (1976 to 1984) have "pretzel bites". The Witch (1630) has eggs "set in ramekins" with "sourdough fingers", on a card whose note says "supper is thin tonight".

11. **Small print on landscape one-offs at 1280.** Back to the Future's clock tower flyer and guest check render at about 7px on desktop (5 text runs under 9px). They can be read through "click the menu to read it full size", but nothing else on the 11 films I opened was that small.

## To reach 9.5

1. **Lock dishes to the venue.** For each generated card, any dish whose name or note sets it somewhere else in the film either moves the card to a venue that fits it or gets swapped for a dish that belongs at the named place and year. Start with the films named above, then sweep the rest for place words in dish names and notes that are not the venue's place. Done right, this alone is worth about +0.3 overall.
2. **Fix the template slots per venue type.** Drop "Now showing, YEAR" from kiosk, home, lab and banquet cards, or move it off the venue header. Kiosk headers should follow the venue ("SNACK BAR", "STALL", "CORNER STORE") and always print the venue name. Campfire wording should only appear where there is a fire. Change "Afters" to "Dessert" on American bars, fix the "Plate A Now showing" join, drop "Form BF NN.", and remove the doubled "A note from" heading.
3. **Price rule by venue type.** Every public place gets prices in local money for its year: Rick's in francs, Caffe Rocca in lire, JFK food court in dollars, The Prancing Pony in pennies. Offices, labs, squad rooms, safehouses and homes print none. Price all dishes or none on a card (Mad Max 2).
4. **Show every printed dish on one-off phone menus and film pages**, with the same "On the menu, not in tonight's five" line the generated cards use. Fix the Three Men and a Baby page.
5. **Build film page recipes from the same tidied quantities the app shows** (no "8 tsp", "1/2 jar" or "1650g"). Make one-off page notes copy the card text exactly. Remove the Me, Myself & Irene beer from its page. Slug accented titles properly ("amelie-2001", "leon-the-professional-1994", "les-miserables-2012", "pokemon-detective-pikachu-2019") and redirect the old ones.
6. **Word swaps on American cards:** icing to frosting or glaze, rashers to bacon strips, choc chip to chocolate chip, pouring cream to cream, Takeaway to Takeout, "Sweets" to "Desserts". In recipe steps: cookies to biscuits, plastic wrap to cling wrap, half-and-half to cream and milk.
7. **Prep sheet maths:** count "Cook only" dishes' hands-on steps in the total, cap prep at the hands-on step times so rising and resting stay out of prep, and only say "start the day before" when a dish needs it, not for chilling a drink.
8. **Notes pass:** remove the repeated character from the 55 notes, put the food first on the 10 that open on a gag, cut the plot recaps, and fix the phone's first-word trim (it strips a word that repeats the dish name).
9. **Count template slots in the film word and name limits**, then trim the props or toast lines on the 60 or so cards over the film word limit and the 90 or so over the name limit.
10. **Period fixes** on the 10 films above, and give the landscape one-offs a minimum print size of about 9px at 1280.
