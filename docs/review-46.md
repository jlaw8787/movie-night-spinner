# Reel Dinner, review 46

## What I looked at

- The photo-on dump of all 1,000 cards (/tmp/r45/final.json), swept in Python for: note length and shape, dashes, prices by venue, money and setting year, American words on British and Australian cards and the reverse, alcohol on kids, family, Christmas and wizard cards, name pile-ups, and template stock lines repeated across cards and the venues they land on.
- About 125 cards read dish by dish: roughly 20 hand-built one-offs (Godfather, Goodfellas, Pulp Fiction, Big Lebowski, Back to the Future, Jurassic Park, Die Hard, Home Alone, Home Alone 2, Elf, Hook, Ratatouille, Kung Fu Panda, Wall-E, Coming to America, Friday, TMNT III, A Goofy Movie, Ghostbusters, Lady and the Tramp), 14 kids and family, 14 Christmas, 12 British, 12 Australian, 16 prisons, messes and galleys, and two random draws of 30 shared-layout cards.
- The live site on my own server at 1280 and 390 wide: Zoolander, Neighbors, Men in Black II, The Matrix, The Suicide Squad, First Blood, Tango & Cash, Running Scared and E.T. (the notes shortened after the dump), plus Home Alone, Ghost, Stripes, John Wick: Chapter 4, Little Shop of Horrors, The Shawshank Redemption, Pulp Fiction, Apollo 13, Hook and The Great Escape. I looked at the printed card, the phone menu with a method open and the Prep sheet on desktop and phone.
- The built film pages: every menu/<slug>/index.html checked against the card's dish names and notes, and a dash scan across all of them.
- Screenshots: docs/review-46-01.png to docs/review-46-12.png.

## Overall: 8.6 / 10

| Area | Score | One line |
|---|---|---|
| True to the film | 8.5 | Hand-built cards are superb and the iconic dishes turn up almost everywhere, but too many shared cards mix venues or serve restaurant food in a prison or mess. |
| Dish notes | 8.5 | Mostly food first, short and with one good touch; a handful of text bugs and notes that skip the food or end on a bare plot line. |
| Prices | 9.0 | Shillings in Nassau, yen in Osaka, pence in 1930s Melbourne, nothing at homes and messes; a few prices off for venue or decade. |

## What is strong

- The hand-built cards are the best thing on the site. Pulp Fiction ("Martin & Lewis shake, $5, No bourbon"), Back to the Future (Lou's at 35 cents, "MILK, CHOCOLATE", "No Pepsi Free"), Die Hard (the 30th floor party, "make fists with your toes", the Twinkie gag), Big Lebowski, Goodfellas, Ghostbusters (the work order adds up to $41.50) and Coming to America read like real props from the film.
- Money and era are handled with real care: Thunderball prices in shillings and pence because the Bahamas used the pound in 1965, Phar Lap and The Dressmaker in pre-decimal, Klaus in kroner, Kung Fu Panda in coins, Treasure Planet in drubloons, homes, messes, invitations and receptions with no prices at all.
- British and Australian voice is right. The Castle (rissoles, "What a beautiful night", moselle from the cask), Muriel's Wedding (the alternate drop), Animal Kingdom (parma, Jatz and cabana), Shaun of the Dead, Hot Fuzz (Angel's cranberry juice), Kingsman ("Burgers on fine china") all sound like their places.
- Dry cards really are dry. Every kids, family, Christmas and wizard card I checked prints alcohol-free versions (cooked custard eggnog, spiced apple juice instead of cider, butterscotch cream soda), with Die Hard and Love Actually rightly left alone.
- No em or en dashes anywhere: not in the dump, not on the live cards I opened, not on any of the 1,000 film pages.
- The Prep sheet is genuinely useful: guests stepper, vegetarian and no-alcohol toggles, "5 of 6 dishes picked", aisle-grouped shopping list, step timings and make-ahead lines.

## What holds it back

1. **Template stock lines that do not suit the venue.** The takeaway layout prints "WE DELIVER", "PHONE YOUR ORDER 555 0231", "Free delivery over ¥3,000" and "Talk on the delivery run" on John Wick: Chapter 4's Osaka Continental, a hotel (screenshot 05). The same block lands on Everything Everywhere All at Once (Wang Laundromat), Crazy Rich Asians (Newton Food Centre, a hawker centre), Gremlins (Dorry's Tavern), Kung Fu Hustle (Pig Sty Alley Canteen) and Blade Runner 2049 (Sapper's Protein Farm). The diner layout prints "The window booth at Mushnik's Skid Row Florist", "Booth service" and "Prices subject to change. Refills on the coffee." on Little Shop of Horrors, a flower shop (screenshot 06). The newspaper layout prints "THE LUNCH COUNTER MENU" and "Printed offset at the counter" on The Conjuring 2's family home in Enfield, Captain America's apartment and Gittes' office in Chinatown. The tearoom layout prints "The Tea Room, top floor" and "Luncheon from noon. Afternoon tea 3 till 5" on Ghost's Tribeca loft. Hot Shots! is a "Navy Base Mess Hall" printed as an airline ("Flight 737, Now boarding", "SEAT 16A WINDOW", "Snacks at cruising altitude"). The Dish prints "MESS OPEN" and "Ration sheet" at the Parkes telescope. The canteen and galley layouts print a lone "Issue" in the price column of the first dish only (First Blood, Shawshank; screenshots 04 and 07).
2. **Prisons and messes serving restaurant food, and one venue that is really three.** Shawshank's mess hall serves "Zihuatanejo guacamole" and "Zihuatanejo grilled fish tacos" (screenshot 07). Tango & Cash's "Prison Mess Hall Block C" serves "Tango's tailored rib eye" and "LA street tacos". The Suicide Squad's Belle Reve mess serves "Ratcatcher's honey baked camembert". Edge of Tomorrow's base mess serves mussels in white wine and "army tea with dark rum". The Great Escape's POW mess pours "Fourth of July potato moonshine" (screenshot 11), against the no-spirits rule. Apollo 13 sets its menu in "Aquarius, the Lifeboat" and serves smoked brisket, crème brûlée and "Lovell's moon night champagne". Shared cards also mix venues on one menu: The Terminator at Tech Noir serves "Big Jeff's burger" and "Big Jeff's cherry pie"; The Big Sick's family kitchen serves comedy club fries, hospital cafeteria sandwiches and Chicago deep dish; Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban at the Three Broomsticks serves "Leaky Cauldron stew" and "Knight Bus" toasties but no Butterbeer, the one drink that venue is known for; John Wick: Chapter 4 heads its card "Osaka. Berlin. Paris. Dinner."
3. **Text bugs on live cards.** First Blood: "Highway diner patty melts on rye: Beef, Swiss and slow onions on griddled ginger." (screenshot 04, and on the film page, screenshot 09). Stripes: "EM-50 Urban Assault Irish-style hot chocolate: Hot coffee, brown sugar and vanilla topped with cream." Jingle All the Way: "under crisp crispy potato bites". The Matrix prints both "Cypher's steak bites" and "Cypher's steak". Lady and the Tramp: "Blood orange soda: Rosemary syrup, lemon, soda water." with no blood orange in it. The Goonies note ends "Hey you guys!" rather than a full stop.
4. **Notes that skip the food or end on a bare plot line.** Klaus: "Pickled herring on rye crispbread: Only herring and burbot today, says Alva." First Blood: "Come first light, every man on the ridge. Strong black coffee, maple, maple cream." Kung Fu Panda's hand-built notes rarely say what the dish is ("Oogway's peaches: From the tree where Oogway sits."; "Valley of Peace kung pao chicken: The busy village below the Jade Palace."). Hook: "For Tink. She once drank poison for Peter." Plot tails: It Follows "Yara reads The Idiot between bites.", The Breakfast Club "Brian asks if milk will be made available.", That Thing You Do! "Mr White calls them the one-hit Wonders.", Running Scared "sliced before the showdown in the Illinois Center atrium."
5. **Australian words on American cards.** Hook: "with cheesy damper"; Soul: "garlicky sautéed silverbeet"; The Incredibles: "Edna Mode's choc cake"; Big Lebowski: "Big choc chip cookies"; E.T.: "Leg ham, Swiss and mustard butter"; Christmas Vacation: "Leg ham studded with cloves" and "lime mould"; Bad Santa: "Thurman's toasted peanut butter and jam"; I Am Legend and To All the Boys: "yoghurt"; Dragnet and Friday the 13th: "lemon squash"; Walk the Line: "Lemon barley water"; Poltergeist: "Steak on the bench"; Zoolander: "a centre for ants". Going the other way, The Conjuring 2 (Enfield) prints "Ground lamb braised with carrot" in a shepherd's pie.
6. **Prices and period slips.** Red Dog prints counter meals at $4.50 and the bar at 85 cents on a card headed "Now showing, 2011", next to "Loaded pub chips with gravy and cheddar" that don't belong in the 1970s Pilbara. John Wick: Chapter 4 prices a cognac sidecar at ¥610 at the Continental. Home Alone 2 serves "Lobster mac and cheese" at the 1991 Plaza and prices cocoa at "4.11". The Greatest Showman (1860s) has "red velvet cake"; Dial of Destiny (1969) has a limoncello prosecco spritz.
7. **A Christmas Story's cue is in the wrong place.** "Serve the duck when Ralphie and the family first visit the department store Santa." The Chinese turkey is the last scene of the film, not the Santa visit.
8. **Venue name pile-ups on a few cards.** Most cards keep names to two or three, but some repeat the venue's owner far more: "Dex" nine times on Attack of the Clones, "Rick" eight on Casablanca, "Tiana" eight on The Princess and the Frog, "Sonny" eight on Big Daddy.
9. **Desktop easel crops the card.** At 1280 the card sits in a 760px easel that cuts multi-panel cards mid-panel: Zoolander's poster loses its right edge and frame (screenshot 01), and John Wick: Chapter 4's cover is split across pages 1 and 2. The menu starts about 1,100px down the page under the poster. Rustic layouts set dish notes at about 11px (First Blood, screenshot 04). "Click the menu to read it full size" helps, but the default view should not need it.

## To reach 9.5

1. Give every shared layout a venue type and stop the stock block from printing when it doesn't match: no delivery, phone or "driver" lines unless the venue delivers; no "booth", "refills on the coffee" or "window" lines unless it's a diner or counter; no "Tea Room, top floor" unless it's a tea room; no flight or seat lines unless it's a plane. Hot Shots!, The Dish, The Conjuring 2, Little Shop of Horrors, Ghost and the six takeaway mismatches above are the first to fix. Drop the lone "Issue" price word or print it on every row.
2. Run a venue pass on prison, mess, galley and canteen cards: canteen food only, beer at most where the film shows it, no spirits. Fix Shawshank, Tango & Cash, The Suicide Squad, Edge of Tomorrow, The Great Escape, Police Academy (Blue Oyster oysters) and Stripes. Move Apollo 13 to a venue where people actually eat (the launch party at the Lovells' house).
3. Make each shared card one venue: move off-venue dishes (Big Jeff's at Tech Noir, Leaky Cauldron at the Three Broomsticks, hospital and comedy club food in The Big Sick's kitchen) or swap them for that venue's food. Add Butterbeer to Prisoner of Azkaban.
4. Fix the text bugs: First Blood "griddled ginger" to "griddled rye", Stripes name or note, Jingle All the Way "crisp crispy", the second Matrix steak, Lady and the Tramp's blood orange soda, the Goonies exclamation. Then add a check that the note mentions the main ingredient in the dish name.
5. Rewrite the notes that don't lead with food (Klaus herring, First Blood coffee, the Kung Fu Panda set, Hook's Tink line) and cut bare plot tails to a food-tied touch or nothing.
6. Sweep American cards for damper, silverbeet, choc, leg ham, mould, jam with peanut butter, yoghurt, lemon squash, barley water, bench, centre; sweep British cards for "ground" meat.
7. Fix Red Dog's year line or its prices, raise the Continental's drink prices, swap the lobster mac at the 1991 Plaza and the red velvet in The Greatest Showman.
8. Move A Christmas Story's cue to the Chinese restaurant at the end.
9. Cap a venue owner's name at three prints per card by using "the diner", "the café" and so on after the masthead.
10. On desktop, size the easel so each page shows a whole panel (or snap pages to panel edges), lift the smallest note type to 13px, and bring the menu up so the first screen shows food, not only the poster.
