# Review 37: Reel Dinner

**Overall: 7.2 out of 10**

I read 186 printed cards in full (all nine hand-built favourites, 23 Australian or Aussie-set films, 31 kids and family films, and 120 picked at random across 58 layouts from 1940 to 2025), plus the phone menu and the prep sheet with shopping list for every one of them, desktop at 1280 for six films, the phone at 390 for four, and two film pages at 1280 and 390. Kids films: no beer, wine or spirits are served on any kids card I read, and most print "Soft drinks only". Alcohol words still turn up, though (see faults 14 and 15).

## What is strong

- The hand-built cards are excellent. Annie Hall's Mandel's deli ("Shelled. None behind the refrigerator.", "Never leaves Manhattan. We need the eggs.", "Ask for Max"), Casino's Tangiers coffee shop with Ace's blueberry rule, the Belmore cafeteria trip sheet for Taxi Driver, Premium Rush's halal truck ("Detective Monday, never.") and Ghostbusters' work order all read like real props.
- Many facts are right. Die Hard prints "Christmas Eve, Saturday, December 24" (correct for 1988), Back to the Future has "SATURDAY NOVEMBER 5 1955", Phar Lap has "Bar shuts at six" and "Bobby won't eat without Tommy", Lion has the milk "Bought with coal off the trains", The Dish has the band playing Hawaii Five-0, and Manhattan has "Sam Wo's crabs made Isaac's list".
- The Australian cards mostly land: The Castle's rissoles cue ("Serve the mains when Darryl asks what it's called"), the Gallipoli mess with "Issue" in place of prices, Two Hands' running tab that adds up exactly ($47.25 then $60.75), and Priscilla at the Palace Hotel "Est. 1889".
- Currencies fit the setting: roubles for GoldenEye, baht for Kickboxer, lire for Roman Holiday, shillings and pence for Phar Lap and The Dressmaker, gold coins at the Continental, grubs for The Lion King, tickets at Pizza Planet.
- No em or en dashes anywhere in the 186 cards, phone menus or prep sheets. The spelling is Australian throughout (yoghurt, capsicum, rocket, chilli, tomato sauce, icing sugar).
- The phone menu at 390 is clean and big, has tabs by course, and no text runs off the side (05, 07, 08, 15).

## What holds it back

1. **Banquet cards charge guests who would never pay.** How the Grinch Stole Christmas, the Whobilation feast: "The whole feast, per cover $68.50". Batman v Superman, under "The honour of your company": "$89.50" per cover. Flash Gordon, Ming's palace: "$29.50". Mulan, Imperial Palace: "31 taels". The Hobbit, Thorin's hall: "30 silver pennies". George of the Jungle charges "$11.00" for cioppino in George's own treehouse. These are invitations and private homes, so they should print no prices.
2. **Halloween (2018) is a school dance that sells dinner and alcohol.** "Haddonfield High Halloween Dance" sells "Laurie's safe room chilli $18.40", "roast chicken and pumpkin $21.00" and "Haddonfield rye and ginger $3.95" ("Rye, ginger beer and lime"). A high school gym would not sell rye, and the prices are restaurant prices (14).
3. **Ghostbusters' total is wrong.** The fees printed are 6.00, 5.00, 4.00, 7.00, 9.00, 5.50 and 5.00, which add up to 41.50, but the card prints "Total $45.00" (13).
4. **Forrest Gump's cue is wrong.** It says "Serve the prawns when Bubba starts his list on the boat." Bubba recites his shrimp list in the army and dies in Vietnam, before there is a boat.
5. **Back to the Future puts 1985 into 1955 Lou's Cafe.** "JOEY'S CAKE ... He didn't make parole. Eat it anyway" (Joey's cake is in the 1985 McFly kitchen) and "DOC'S BREAKFAST ... Great Scott, no robot arm" (Doc's breakfast machine is also 1985, and Doc never eats at Lou's). The cake note also never says what the cake is.
6. **Notes mention food the card does not serve.** Finding Dory, Hank: "Pass the prawns and nobody poke me." No prawns are on the card. Galaxy Quest, Guy: "Pass the bread rolls." No rolls are on the card. John Wick: Chapter 2: "Serve the wine when John visits the sommelier", but the only drink is a Negroni.
7. **The hotel template is used on places that are not hotels.** Insidious, "Lambert Family Home", prints "Service at any hour, no questions", "From the front desk", "Last order 1 am", "Room 410" and "Wake-up call". Annie's Warbucks Mansion prints "Room 482" and "From the front desk" over a note from Miss Hannigan. The Dark Knight's "Wayne Penthouse Fundraiser" prints "In your suite, 2008" and "Room 408".
8. **The motel template contradicts itself.** Scream (2022) prints "Room Key: ... Room 7, checkout 10am" and then "Checkout 11". Barbarian's Airbnb and No Time to Die's "Bond's Cabin" both read "Coffee shop, open early and late" / "Coffee shop, open all night".
9. **The stadium scoreboard is the same on every film.** The Mighty Ducks, Bring It On, The Naked Gun, A League of Their Own and The Replacements all print "Home 3 2 1 2 8 / Guests 0 0 0 0 0". Bring It On is a cheer competition with no score, and A League of Their Own's card is "World Series, Game 7, Peaches v Racine Belles", which Racine wins. The section map also prints "SEC 104 Courtside" for an ice rink (Mighty Ducks) and a baseball park (Naked Gun).
10. **Notes repeat the dish name or a prop across the card.** American Beauty: "Pass-the-asparagus tempura spears ... Would someone pass the asparagus?" and "+ Pass the asparagus" in the kitchen talk. Zombieland: "Enjoy the little things mini corn dogs ... Enjoy the little things.", "Nut up or shut up peanut butter shakes ... Time to nut up or shut up.", and "Blue raspberry slushies, frozen hard. Frozen hard, for the road." Furiosa: "Bullet Farm chorizo ... from the Bullet Farm", "Gastown blackened chicken ... from Gastown". Mr. Deeds: "very, very sneaky" on both the ziti and the pink lemonade. What We Do in the Shadows: "Werewolves, not swearwolves" twice. Chinatown: Gittes' nose bandage twice. Unbreakable: "never had a sick day" and then "a man who's never sick".
11. **Some notes lead with plot or never name the food.** A League of Their Own: "Betty Spaghetti warming up to come in. Pork and beef meatballs..." Bring It On: "The Spirit Stick never touches the ground. Cinnamon sugar churros..." TMNT II: "Splinter's Special 13.50 / “Pizza's OK,” he says." (no ingredients). Set It Up: "Classic negroni $4.75 / Cook the meat first. Rick and Kirsten's date." Step Brothers: "Fancy sauce glazed meatloaf / First dinner, fancy sauce guarded by Brennan." Harold & Kumar: "Crispy fish sliders with tartare / On the side of Harold's thirty sliders and four large Cherry Cokes." Ghostbusters: "Slimer's slime dip / Green and greedy. Caught at the Sedgewick Hotel".
12. **Some notes just tack on a quote.** The Big Lebowski: the wings note is only "“This is not 'Nam. This is bowling. There are rules.” Walter". Jurassic Park: "Brewed strong and black. “Hold on to your butts.”", "Life finds a way.", "Clever girl." Galaxy Quest: "...chips, never give up, never surrender." The Dark Knight: "...roast capsicum to scoop, why so serious." American Beauty: "Cherry and vanilla blended thick. “I rule!”"
13. **Prices are wrong for the era, the place or the order.** American Beauty's 1999 Mr Smiley's drive-thru: "fry cook cheeseburgers $11.50", "popcorn prawn baskets $12.50". Bad Boys' police breakroom: "Cafecito ... $5.50" and a "$6.95" mojito. Casino Royale's high-stakes casino: "The Vesper ... €3.50", cheaper than the "€4.75" olive picks. The Hangover's Caesars suite: "Caesar salad ... $3.25" but "hash browns $5.75". Sweet Home Alabama's small-town tavern in 2002: "Spiced pecans $10.25", "Cornmeal fish and slaw $19.50". The Sandlot in 1962: "Backyard double cheeseburgers $1.95", "hot dogs $1.00". Willy Wonka in 1971: "Combo 5 ... $8.25". Zootopia: "Clawhauser's doughnut holes $9.00" next to a "Strawberry banana thickshake $3.50".
14. **Kids films still print alcohol words.** Ratatouille, phone menu and prep: "Pour into wine glasses over ice", under the heading "La cave, The cellar". Annie: "ginger beer over ice, no alcohol." George of the Jungle: "Overheard at the bar: Tookie the toucan". Who Framed Roger Rabbit: "Eddie's ginger beer on the rocks".
15. **A psychiatric ward serves wine.** The Silence of the Lambs' "State Hospital Canteen" meal order for "PATIENT: HANNIBAL LECTER" lists "Red wine and blood orange sangria" and "Glass of Chianti Classico". The cue already does the Chianti joke, so the ward menu does not need the wine.
16. **Food from the wrong country.** Sweet Home Alabama: "Cornmeal fish ... Flathead" (Alabama would use catfish). Raising Arizona: "Moses' yabby and corn boil ... No fowl? Then we ate crawdad." (the note itself says crawdad). Stranger than Fiction in Chicago: "Crisp-skin barramundi". Spider-Man: Far From Home at a "Venice Canal Cafe": "Tower Bridge flathead and chips €26.00", "Netherlands tulip field fries", "Prague trdelník".
17. **Food from the wrong era.** The Karate Kid (1984): "Bonsai broccolini" (broccolini came out in the 1990s), plus edamame, togarashi and wasabi mayo at a Reseda kiosk. Willy Wonka (1971): "Golden goose salted caramel eggs". Wall Street (1987): "Bluestar espresso tonic". Who Framed Roger Rabbit (1947): "Honey roasted carrots with whipped feta". The Big Lebowski (1991 bowling alley snack bar): "za'atar, sumac and nigella, whipped feta". Fantastic Beasts (1932 Berlin): "Panko crumbed pork loin". A League of Their Own (1943): "meatball sliders". The Dressmaker (1951): "wattleseed cream", which also contradicts the dish name "with vanilla cream".
18. **Template lines are broken.** Notting Hill: "From the Max and Bella's Dinner Party." The Wolverine: "Yashida Estate, Tokyo. Tokyo. Bullet train." Pirates and A Christmas Carol print "A note from Tia Dalma" and "A note from Mr Fezziwig" twice each. Grandma's Boy prints "Carried over", "Brought forward" and "Running tab" with no amounts. Finding Dory, Mrs. Doubtfire and Super Mario print "*With a grown-up's meal." with no asterisk anywhere above it. Every club card has the same "No discussing [X] at the tables." (Sapphires, Rocketman, GoldenEye, The Mask, Dick Tracy, Moulin Rouge). Beverly Hills Cop II and Bad Boys put an Aussie "Honour box by the kettle" in American police stations. The Dressmaker and Miracle on 34th Street print "Prices include tea for one." above a priced pot of tea.
19. **Names are misspelt.** Muriel's Wedding: "Van Arkle wedding alternate drop" (the character is David Van Arckle). Raising Arizona: "Nathan Arizona was born Huffheinz" (Huffhines in the film). E.T.: "for the day E.T. Learns English" and "E.T. Raids the fridge" have stray capitals after "E.T.". John Wick: "Good for one night, one favor" is US spelling.
20. **Venue mix-ups on the Aussie cards.** Crocodile Dundee's Walkabout Creek pub serves "Street cart hot dogs ... off the cart" and "New York baked cheesecake". Priscilla's Broken Hill pub serves "Dugout" pies and "Coober Pedy opal jelly gems". Pirates of the Caribbean's voodoo shack serves "Chook and snags in dark roux", which is heavier slang than the rest of the site.
21. **Shopping list faults.** The Last Dragon and Chinatown: "16 spring rolls of wrappers"; Ace Ventura 2: "6 spring rolls of wrappers". Halloween and Unbreakable: "1 5cm piece ginger". Wolf Creek lists "2 sheets of puff pastry" under Bakery and again under Frozen. Bad Boys lists "120g leg ham" under Meat and again under Deli, plus "1 L neutral oil, for frying" and "1 bottle vegetable oil". Crocodile Dundee lists "1 bottle neutral oil" and "1 bottle vegetable oil". The Big Lebowski lists "160ml milk" and "60ml full-cream milk". Wolf Creek gives "sweetened condensed milk" and "tomato sauce" with no amount.
22. **Prep sheet times and wording.** The Castle (09): "Slice the kabana on the diagonal. 7 min", "Cut the cheese into cubes. 6 min", which totals "Takes 20 min" for cheese on sticks. Its method step reads "Lay out on a plastic platter with crackers. It is the vibe." The film line belongs in a note, not a cooking step. The "Make ahead" labels print at 10px on desktop and phone.
23. **Film pages do not match the cards.** Pulp Fiction's page (12) lists "Buddy Holly's chicken basket", "Mamie Van Doren devilled eggs" and "Jack Rabbit Slim's fries", but the card prints "Buddy Holly chicken", "Mamie's devilled eggs" and "Slim's fries". The Castle's page (11) tags "Bonnie Doon lager and lime shandy" as "Vegetarian", and only some dishes carry the "On the printed menu" badge even though every dish listed is on the printed menu.
24. **The Godfather has a desktop flaw.** The pushcart photo header has visible blurred patches over the shop signs (03), and the phone tagline says "A Sunday at the Corleone table." on a card for Louis' restaurant.
25. **Dishes repeat across films.** Blinis with crème fraîche and smoked salmon turn up at Die Hard, Strictly Ballroom, Kenny, Batman v Superman, the Slug Club, The Dark Knight and John Wick. Gruyère choux "cheese puffs" turn up in Stuart Little, Peter Pan, White Christmas, Dick Tracy, Fantasia and Fantastic Beasts. Za'atar flatbread turns up in The Big Lebowski, Rogue One, Eternals and Aladdin. In a run of films these start to feel like a stock list.

## To reach 9.5

- Strip prices from every banquet, invitation, private home and school event, and check that every total adds up.
- Make each note food first, then one fresh film touch. Do not reuse the dish name, a prop already on the card, or a bare quote, and do not mention any dish the card does not serve.
- Give hotel, motel, stadium, precinct and club their own wording per venue (home, rental, cheer meet, hockey rink, US station) and fix the broken lines in fault 18.
- Run a period and place check on ingredients (broccolini, salted caramel, whipped feta, panko, za'atar, espresso tonic, sliders) and on fish (flathead, barramundi, yabbies outside Australia).
- Scrub kids cards for "wine", "bar", "on the rocks" and "no alcohol".
- Merge duplicate shopping lines, fix "spring rolls of wrappers" and "1 5cm piece", and make prep times realistic.
- Make film page dish names match the card, and drop the "Vegetarian" tag from drinks.
- Fix the facts in faults 4, 5, 6 and 19.
