# Review 52: what prints

## What I looked at

- Swept every card, phone menu and Prep sheet in the photo-off dumps (`/tmp/r52/after-a.json`, `after-b.json`, with `late.json` taking over for its ten films) in Python: em and en dashes, note shape, prices by decade and currency, alcohol on dry cards, period food against the setting year, template lines against the venue, doubled words, wrong-country food words, dish names against their notes, and phone wording and prices against the printed card on the hand-built one-offs.
- Read about 110 cards dish by dish: all 42 named films, 36 from a random draw of 40 (seed 52), ten Australian, nine British, kids and Christmas, prisons, messes and hospitals, period clubs and nine hand-built one-offs.
- Desktop at 1280x800 and phone at 390x844 with Playwright on 15 films (Rocky, Casablanca, Goldfinger, Hot Fuzz, The Green Mile, Toy Story, Clueless, Some Like It Hot, Escape from New York, Back to the Future, Anchorman, Ghost, Crocodile Dundee, Serpico, Shutter Island, Juno, Thor: Ragnarok, The Water Diviner, Klaus, Pulp Fiction): page overflow, smallest drawn text in the menu column, the phone menu with its first method open, and the Prep sheet on both.
- Opened the full size view at 1280x800 on one film for each of the 40 layouts in `data/film-format.json`, stepped back to page 1, turned every page and logged which text never lands whole on a page and the best size each bit reaches. Then repeated it on four more pub and cafe cards and two hospital cards to see if the faults were layout-wide.
- Film pages: compared all 995 live `menu/<slug>/index.html` pages (5 "Moved" redirects ignored) with the cards for venue line, dish names and notes, checked titles, canonical links, images and internal links, and read pages by hand.

The Italian Job and The Silence of the Lambs were skipped. The by-design calls (American words on American cards, year Easter eggs, shop venues, whole-film menus, show all dishes cook 5, dry kids, Christmas and institutional cards, no prices in homes, messes, prisons and camps) were not scored down.

## Overall: 7.9 / 10

| Area | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| True to the film | 8.5 | The winks are sharp and specific almost everywhere, and the hand-built one-offs are excellent, but a handful of cards put words in the wrong mouth or misquote the film. |
| Dish notes | 8.0 | Shape is clean on all 1,000 (food first, full stop, nothing over 115 characters), but about 25 dish names promise a flavour or a dish the note never delivers, and two notes print a doubled word. |
| Prices | 8.0 | Currency and decade are right nearly everywhere and the tabs add up, but some prices sit well off the setting and several hand-built cards contradict themselves. |
| Template wording that suits the venue | 7.0 | Most layouts fit, but a dozen or so cards carry a template voice that fights the place: tea room at a Tribeca loft, ship's rations at a home kitchen, police roster at a revenge business. |
| Period-correct food | 8.5 | Strong on the details (six o'clock closing at Randwick, £sd before 1971, Stella in 1915 Cairo), with a few clear slips in the 1880s to 1960s. |
| Words that fit where the film is set | 6.5 | House rule 1 asks for chili on American cards, yet 259 American cards print chilli, including chilli dogs and Texas chilli, plus a scatter of Australian words like cobs, docket and cinnamon scrolls. |
| Desktop readability | 6.5 | The desktop column is fine on all 15 films, but the full size view splits pub and cafe mastheads and footers across Left side and Right side, and several layouts only show some text at 5.6 to 9.9px. |
| Phone menu | 8.5 | No overflow, 11 to 12px floor and the method opens cleanly, but hand-built phone menus drift from their cards on prices and notes. |
| Prep sheet and shopping list | 9.0 | Clear aisles, honest timings, Australian words for the shopper, 11px floor and almost no doubled items. |
| Film pages | 8.5 | One title format with the year on all 995, canonical, one h1, JSON-LD and no broken images, but 8 venue lines name a different place from the card and half the descriptions capitalise mid-sentence. |

## What is strong

- No em or en dash anywhere in 1,000 cards, phone menus or Prep sheets, or on any film page.
- Note shape holds across every printed dish: food first, at most one film touch, a full stop at the end. Median 68 characters, none over 115.
- The hand-built one-offs read like real menus from the film: Pulp Fiction ("Martin & Lewis shake / Vanilla. Mia's order. / $5 / No bourbon"), Back to the Future ("FRENCH FRIES / 15¢ / Hand cut, fried twice. Ready at 88 miles an hour.", and "SATURDAY NOVEMBER 5 1955" is the right weekday), Home Alone 2 ("TOTAL $967.43"), Casino ("Same number of blueberries in every muffin"), The Godfather Part II, The Untouchables, Sleepless in Seattle, Manhattan.
- Australian and British cards are a real strength: The Castle ("Chilled moselle from the cask"), Phar Lap ("Bar shuts at six", prices in d and s), Crocodile Dundee ("Walkabout Creek cold pot"), Muriel's Wedding ("Van Arckle wedding alternate drop"), Hot Fuzz, Shaun of the Dead, Snatch ("Mini pie, mash and liquor ... chilli vinegar"), Paddington 2 ("Dish of the day: bear pie (it's chicken)").
- Money adds up where it is shown: Goldfinger's scorecard (9s 10d out, £1 3s in, 895 yards), the Road House and Star Wars running tabs.
- Dry cards are dry where they should be: kids, wizard and Christmas cards say "Soft drinks only", prisons print "Issue" (The Green Mile, Paddington 2, The Great Escape).
- The desktop column never overflows and its smallest text sat between 10.1px (Back to the Future) and 30px on all 15 films.
- Film pages are tidy: 995 titles in one format with the year, one h1, canonical link, JSON-LD, no missing images, dish names and notes match the cards (one exception).

## What holds it back

1. **Chilli on American cards.** Rule 1 lists chili as the American word, but 259 American cards print chilli and only 8 print chili. The dish itself reads worst: The Warriors "Coney chilli dogs" and "any chilli dog", The Naked Gun "Ballpark chilli dogs $1.95", Brewster's Millions "Spike's Chilli Dogs", Wayne's World "Noah's Arcade loaded chilli dogs", Dazed and Confused "Frito pie with Texas chilli", Tommy Boy "Cincinnati chilli spaghetti", Tombstone "Earp brothers' chilli", Galaxy Quest "Mess hall chilli", Serendipity "Foot-long chilli cheese dog". The other way round, The Incredible Hulk (Rio, reais) prints "green chili".
2. **Other Australian words on American cards.** The cafe layout prints "Scrawled on the back of a docket" on Easy A, Venom: Let There Be Carnage and Clerks III. "cobs" on 17 American cards (Tommy Boy "chilli butter cobs", Napoleon Dynamite "cobs in paprika butter", Terminator 2 "Grilled corn cobs"). "cinnamon scrolls" on Bridesmaids and Doctor Strange. Toy Story "Army men pea and broad bean salad". Se7en "A big red shiraz". Alien: Romulus and Arthur Christmas pasties with "swede". A Goofy Movie "Grilled beef sausages in rolls, fried onions, mustard, sauce". Poltergeist "across the kitchen bench". Spider-Man 2 "any floor, by stairs, lift or wall".
3. **Full size view cuts pub and cafe text in half.** On every pub card tested the masthead and footer straddle the Left side / Right side split, so they never print whole on either page: Hot Fuzz shows "THE CROWN, S" and "FOOD SERVED", Crocodile Dundee loses "WALKABOUT CREEK HOTEL" and "BISTRO, BOTTLESHOP, COUNTER MEALS", Shaun of the Dead loses "THE WINCHESTER PUB". The same pages print "No. 2007" at 5.6px. On every cafe card tested the Showtime line is split (Juno, Easy A, Encanto, Unbreakable). The deli layout splits "Toretto's Market and Cafe" (The Fast and the Furious) and bowling splits "National Bowling Stadium" (Kingpin).
4. **Full size view text that only shows tiny.** Some layouts open on an overview page and never show parts of it larger: hospital (Rain Man "SLIP" 7.3px, "SCENES TO WATCH FOR" and "Images: TMDB." 8px; Shutter Island "BADGE" 7.3px, 23 bits under 12px), prom (Monsters University "No. 2013" 6.6px), foodcourt (Wonder Woman 1984 "Wishes not included" 8.7px, "Ask for Max Lord" 9.3px), dimsum (Raya and the Last Dragon "SCENES TO WATCH FOR", "AT THE NEXT TABLE" 9.3px), kiosk (The Incredible Hulk weather panel 8 to 9.9px), canteen (X-Men timetable 11px).
5. **Template voice that fights the venue.** Ghost: tea room layout at "Sam and Molly's Tribeca Loft" prints "Tea at home, in the front room", "Tea's on when the kettle boils" and a "Tea and drinks" heading over a mulled pinot noir. Captain Marvel: galley layout at "Maria Rambeau's Kitchen" prints "Issued DAY 995", "Ration" and "Form G-1995". Dirty Work: precinct layout at "Revenge Office" prints "Duty Roster", "Surveillance", "Day Watch", "Coffee can by the pot". Alien 3: the same precinct lines at a prison mess, with "Division / Special Investigations, 1992". Pretty in Pink: club layout at Trax Records prints "VIP", "After the Show", "From the Bar". La La Land: a loyalty card at a jazz club ("Ten stamps and your next cup is on us") next to an "$11.00" old fashioned. Weekend at Bernie's: "Pupu Platters" and "Ring the gong" at a Hamptons beach house. Rocky's stadium map lists "SEC 101 Ringside" and "SEC 104 Ringside". Mary Poppins Returns prints "From the 17 Cherry Tree Lane." Mission: Impossible, Ghost Protocol prints a cut-off footer "Mission: Impossible, Ghost..., 2011 Form DC-130".
6. **Alcohol on institutional cards.** Rule 6 makes workplace canteens, schools and messes dry, but How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days (Composure Magazine Lunchroom) prints "Diamond ball tenderloin and red wine" and a "Courtside ginger lime shandy / Lager, ginger beer and lime"; 10 Things I Hate About You (Padua High canteen) prints "Prom night vodka penne $3.10"; RoboCop (Metro West Precinct breakroom) prints a rye shot "chased with cold lager"; Gallipoli (Mena Camp Mess) prints "Egyptian Stella lager". X2 (Xavier's School Kitchen) and Singin' in the Rain (studio commissary) cook with red wine.
7. **Dish names that promise what the note does not.** The Martian "Hermes lemon chicken with roast pumpkin" / "Brined breast roasted with garlic over spinach, feta and pepitas" (no lemon, no pumpkin). Avatar: The Way of Water "Sully family chilli lime chicken" (no chilli, no lime). The Incredible Hulk "Culver campus pepperoni and mushroom pizza" / "... mozzarella, salami ...". IT "Red balloon cupcakes" / "Vanilla cake and cream cheese balls on sticks". My Fair Lady "Flower girl's violet Victoria sponge" with no violet. Shutter Island "Dr Cawley's cocoa Alexander" / "Black tea, chocolate and cream". Home Alone 2 "Lobster Newburg" / "Butter-poached tail meat and elbows in Gruyère and cheddar sauce" (that is lobster mac, and the phone menu says something else). Lemon roast chickens with no lemon in the note on Oliver & Company, Labyrinth and Bridesmaids. Doubled words: The Parent Trap "Grilled grilled cheese sandwiches", Return of the Jedi phone "spiced plum sauce sauce".
8. **Hand-built phone menus disagree with their cards.** Ten phone lines print a price the card does not: Serpico "Nova on a bagel" 1.10 on the phone, 1.00 on the card; Home Alone 2 "Minibar mix" 9.67 against 9.75; Working Girl "Crab cakes" 13.75; Julie & Julia "Kir" $3.65; Taxi Driver, Dog Day Afternoon, Inside Man, 16 Blocks, You've Got Mail and Ghostbusters II the same. About 32 hand-built phone notes are reworded from the card. Serpico's own guest check charges "1 pastrami rye 1.35" and "1 coffee .15" against a menu that says 1.25 and .10.
9. **Prices off the setting.** Back to the Future Part II sets Cafe 80s in 2015 at "Cafe 80's fries $1.75" and chicken tenders $2.25. Road House 1989 has a sirloin with loaded potato at $4.75 while 50 First Dates (2004, a breakfast cafe) has "Loco moco $15.50" and "Huli huli pineapple soy chicken $18.00". Napoleon Dynamite's school cafeteria charges "$6.15" for a quesadilla and "$7.00" for sirloin. Bad Santa's food court turkey is "$16" next to "$3" nachos. Ladies in Black has the champagne punch at 1/11, cheaper than the egg sandwiches at 2/8.
10. **Period slips.** Grease (1958) "chilli cheese fries / 30¢", American Graffiti (1962) "Paradise Road chilli cheese fries", Walk Hard (1965) "shoestring cheese fries". Chicago (1920s) "Razzle dazzle sausage pie ... in a deep crust", deep dish being a 1940s idea. The Warriors (1979) "Soft pretzel bites". A craft-bar "big ice cube" on Tombstone, The Good, the Bad and the Ugly, The Shining and Who Framed Roger Rabbit. The Witch (1630s homestead with failed corn) mulls cider with star anise and bakes apples with dates.
11. **Words put in the wrong mouth.** Thor: The Dark World signs the note at Jane's flat from "Frigga". Love Actually sticks "every single biscuit in Number 10 is mine tonight" from the Prime Minister on Karen and Harry's fridge. Encino Man prints the catchphrase two ways on one card, "wheez the juice" and "Weasin' the juice", and the film says "wheeze".
12. **Film pages.** Eight venue lines name a different place from the card: The Taking of Pelham One Two Three ("Transit Authority Command Centre" against Motorman Coffee Shop), Tootsie ("The Southwest General Green Room" against The Tea Room), Friday ("126th St. Market" against Normandie & Western Market), Mickey Blue Eyes ("Vito's" against La Trattoria), plus After Hours, Escape from New York, Quick Change and The Warriors. Die Hard's page says "arugula" where the card says "watercress". Good Will Hunting's page leaves out two card dishes. 481 meta descriptions capitalise the second dish mid-sentence ("Mean man spiced peanuts and Biscuits and sausage gravy"). 158 pages list shopping quantities in a recipe ("1 packet caster sugar", "1 jar rock salt"), and Rocky's pretzels list 520g flour against 500g in the method.
13. **Phone card preview.** On Anchorman the second page of the card preview stops part way through a row, so "D3 Iced tea" shows only its top edge.

## To reach 9.5

1. Swap chilli to chili on every American card (card text, phone menu notes and film page notes), starting with the dish names: chili dog, chili cheese fries, Texas chili, Cincinnati chili, beef chili. Leave chilli in the recipes and shopping lists. Fix The Incredible Hulk's "green chili" to chilli.
2. Run an American word list over American cards and swap: docket to check or receipt (make the cafe line country-aware), cobs to ears of corn, cinnamon scrolls to cinnamon rolls, broad beans to fava beans, shiraz to syrah or "a big red", swede to rutabaga, "sauce" to ketchup on A Goofy Movie, kitchen bench to counter, lift to elevator.
3. Fix the full size view split for the pub, cafe, deli and bowling layouts: either keep the masthead, Showtime line and footer whole on one side, or add a whole-page view as page 1 at a readable zoom. Check all 10 pub, 32 cafe, 2 deli and 1 bowling cards after.
4. Give every bit of text a page where it reads at 12px or more: for hospital, prom, foodcourt, dimsum and kiosk, add the side panels (Scenes to watch for, Slip, Badge, weather, "No." stamps) to one of the later pages, or raise the overview zoom. Raise the canteen timetable from 11px.
5. Make the dry rule hold on institutional cards: drop the red wine dish and shandy from How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days, rename the 10 Things vodka penne, swap RoboCop's rye and lager and Gallipoli's Stella for soft drinks, and cook X2 and Singin' in the Rain without wine.
6. Re-skin the dozen cards whose template fights the venue: Ghost off the tea room layout (or rewrite its tea lines for a loft dinner), Captain Marvel off the galley, Dirty Work and Alien 3 off the precinct roster lines, Pretty in Pink and La La Land onto wording that fits a record shop and a jazz club, Weekend at Bernie's off tiki. Fix Rocky's repeated "Ringside", Mary Poppins Returns "From the 17 Cherry Tree Lane." and the cut-off Ghost Protocol footer.
7. Run a name against note pass and fix every dish where the name promises a flavour or dish the note leaves out: The Martian, Avatar: The Way of Water, The Incredible Hulk pizza, IT cupcakes, My Fair Lady sponge, Shutter Island cocoa Alexander, Home Alone 2 Lobster Newburg, and the lemon chickens. Fix "Grilled grilled cheese" and "plum sauce sauce".
8. Make hand-built phone menus read off the same numbers and notes as the card: fix the ten phone prices and the 32 reworded notes, and correct Serpico's guest check to 1.25 and .10.
9. Bring the outlier prices back into line with the setting year and venue: Back to the Future Part II, 50 First Dates, Napoleon Dynamite, Bad Santa, Ladies in Black, Road House.
10. Fix the period slips: no cheese fries before about 1975, no deep-crust pie on Chicago, plain pretzels on The Warriors, a plain glass of ice instead of "one big ice cube" before 2000, and a plainer 1630s supper on The Witch.
11. Fix the voices: sign Thor: The Dark World's note from Jane or Thor, move Love Actually's note to Number 10 or sign it from Karen, and spell Encino Man's line "Wheeze the juice" once.
12. Rebuild the eight film page venue lines from the card, fix Die Hard's arugula, add Good Will Hunting's two dishes, lower-case the second dish in descriptions, and give page recipes for extra dishes real quantities that match the method.
13. Stop the phone card preview pages part way through a row (Anchorman page 2), and check the other multi-page phone previews for the same.
