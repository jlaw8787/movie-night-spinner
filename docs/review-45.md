# Reel Dinner, review 45

## What I looked at

I went through the photo-on dump of all 1,000 cards (/tmp/r44/final.json) in Python, checking note length, notes that carry a second plot sentence, dashes, prices by layout and venue, money and era, food words by setting, alcohol on kids, family, Christmas and wizard cards, name pile-ups, template lines and dish names that show up on lots of cards. I then read about 130 cards dish by dish. That covered hand-built one-offs (The Godfather, Goodfellas, Pulp Fiction, When Harry Met Sally, Die Hard, Ratatouille, Back to the Future, Taxi Driver, Raging Bull, The Untouchables), kids and family (Toy Story, Frozen, Moana, Shrek, Matilda, Up, Coco, The Lion King, Jumanji), Christmas (Home Alone, Elf, Bad Santa, Office Christmas Party, Christmas Vacation, The Santa Clause, Miracle on 34th Street, Klaus), British (Hot Fuzz, Shaun of the Dead, Notting Hill, Love Actually, Snatch, Paddington, Wallace & Gromit, Harry Potter), Australian (The Castle, Animal Kingdom, Crocodile Dundee, Muriel's Wedding, The Dressmaker, Red Dog, Storm Boy, Gallipoli, Kenny, Two Hands, Wolf Creek, The Dish) and two random draws of 45. I opened nine films in Chrome through Playwright at 1280 and 390 wide (The Castle, Hot Fuzz, The Godfather, Home Alone, Paddington, The Rock, Spirited Away, Beerfest, Taxi Driver). On each I looked at the printed card, the phone menu with a method open, and the Prep sheet. None of them threw a page error. I swept all 1,000 built film pages and compared four of them (The Castle, Hot Fuzz, The Godfather, Spirited Away) line by line against the card, the phone menu and the Prep sheet.

## Overall: 7.4 / 10

| Area | Score | One line |
|---|---|---|
| True to the film | 6.5 | The 97 hand-built cards are superb. Too many generated cards are a grab-bag of scenes, or serve food the named venue never would (prison messes pouring Scotch). |
| Dish notes | 6.5 | Food comes first and the median is 68 characters, but 71 notes hit 100 or 101, and hundreds tack on a bare plot sentence. |
| Prices | 7.5 | Era and local money are mostly right (£sd for 1951 Victoria, francs for Amélie, yen in Tokyo). Beerfest prices Munich in dollars, and a few venues where nobody pays still print prices. |
| Template wording that suits the venue | 6.5 | Shared layouts leak their stock lines onto the wrong venues ("Plate A, samples for four" in a family kitchen, a bathroom cell and a bakery). |
| Period-correct food | 7.5 | Mostly sound, with a scatter of modern café plates on period or odd-setting cards. |
| Words that fit the setting and no dashes | 7.0 | No dashes anywhere, and the British and Australian cards are clean. About 35 American cards print "yoghurt", and a handful print "icing", "tomato sauce" for ketchup, "napoli" or "cachous". |
| Desktop readability at 1280 | 8.5 | Cards read cleanly at reading size with good contrast. No errors. |

| Film pages | 8.0 | All 1,000 pages are built, every printed dish is there, no dashes, recipe schema in place. Times are written three different ways across the surfaces. |

## What is strong

- The hand-built one-offs are the best thing on the site. Louis' on The Godfather prints the veal at "1.72" and "The family dinner 2.50". Taxi Driver's Belmore cafeteria has a real trip sheet ("6:05 P / Garage / Pk Av S / 1.35"). When Harry Met Sally's guest check adds up to "$18.70". Back to the Future has "COFFEE, NO SUGAR / 5¢ / Black, nothing added. No Pepsi Free."
- Australian cards sound like home without going ocker. Animal Kingdom has "Smurf's Melbourne chicken parma" and "Jatz, cabana and cheddar platter". The Castle has "Sal's rissoles" and "Chilled moselle from the cask". Two Hands has "Pando's crew potato scallops ... chicken salt, tomato sauce and lemon". The Dressmaker prices in "2s" and "3s 7d".
- British cards are clean of American words. Hot Fuzz prints "£9.25" steak and ale pie "served with chips", and Shaun of the Dead has "Corner shop crisps".
- The kids, Christmas and wizard rule holds. Every one I checked prints dry, and the printed names are renamed properly ("Bag End hot spiced apple juice", "Captain Smollett's ginger beer fruit punch"). Die Hard and Love Actually keep their bar, as the rule allows.
- Not a single em or en dash in the dump or in any of the 1,000 film pages.
- The Prep sheet is properly useful. The Castle groups "800g beef mince", "750g cooked king prawns" and "300ml thickened cream" by aisle, gives every step a time ("Slice the kabana on the diagonal. 3 min"), and shows make-ahead notes up front.
- The phone menu at 390 is easy to cook from: big type, a "How to make it" toggle per dish, and the same amounts as the Prep sheet.
- Dish variety is wide. The most repeated dish name across 1,000 films is "mango lassi", and it appears four times.

## What holds it back

1. **Venues serving food they never would (generated cards).** Prison and military messes pour cocktails and plate restaurant mains. The Rock's "Alcatraz Island Mess Hall" has "Mason's hotel room service: Butter-basted rib eye with béarnaise and frites" and "Single malt Rob Roy". Chicago's "Cook County Jail Kitchen" has "Devils on horseback" and "Hotel Cicero gin fizz". Saw's "Abandoned Bathroom Cell" has "Dr Gordon's roast chicken dinner" and "Tapp's stakeout coffee-glazed doughnuts". Under Siege's "USS Missouri Galley" pours "USS Missouri navy grog: Dark and white rum", on a US Navy ship, which has been dry since 1914. Fantastic Beasts' "Kowalski Quality Baked Goods" is a bakery selling "Polish pierogi" and "1926 New York beef pot roast". Jumanji's "Parrish Shoe Factory" canteen has "Van Pelt's juniper venison and redcurrant" at "$6.25".
2. **Grab-bag cards that pull from every scene instead of one venue.** Love Actually's "Arrivals Hall Cafe" has "Wisconsin buffalo wings", "Jamie's French cottage coq au vin" and "Baileys on ice". X-Men: First Class's "Xavier Mansion Dining Room" has "Oxford pub ploughman's" and "Argentine beef empanadas ... for Erik's visit to the bar in Argentina". Mission: Impossible Ghost Protocol's "IMF Rescue Train Carriage" serves Moscow pirozhki, Mumbai bhajis and Dubai machboos. Beverly Hills Cop's "Beverly Palms Hotel" has "Detroit coney dogs" and "Serge's gallery lunch chicken Caesar". Enchanted's "The King and Queen's Ball" serves "Pancakes, bacon and scrambled eggs". Ghost's "Oda Mae's Reading Room" serves "Sam's Tribeca loft pasta" and "Molly's studio mulled pinot noir".
3. **Notes with a plot sentence bolted on.** 364 notes run to two sentences, and a big share of the second sentences are plot. American Beauty: "Griddled beef patty and American cheese. Lester quits and becomes a fry cook." Starship Troopers: "Whole roast cauliflower, tahini and pomegranate. Zim captures the Brain Bug." Spirited Away: "Salted rice balls, umeboshi, nori. Haku gives Chihiro rice balls." Red Heat: "Cranberry and vodka over ice. Pour before the bus hits the freight train." The Witches: "Dark chocolate cake under a cream glaze. His bar had Formula 86." Some notes open with the film and never get to the food, like The Avengers "Frozen mint lemonade: Ice cold after a fight over Manhattan." Chinatown's footer gives away the ending: "The slap: “My sister, my daughter.”"
4. **71 notes at 100 or 101 characters** against the under-100 rule (33 at exactly 100). Examples: Wonder Woman "Chilled white wine stirred with warm honey and orange peel, old Greek oenomeli for a warrior's toast." and Hairspray "Buttermilk-soaked with a craggy crust, honey hot sauce, mashed potatoes, gravy, for Wilbur and Tracy."
5. **Dish names that don't match their notes.** Home Alone "Big glass of milk: Ice cold milk, chocolate malt and a scoop of ice cream, blended" is a milkshake. The Rock "Dark chocolate fudge squares: Dense baked brownie squares". Red Dog "ruby grapefruit radler: Ginger beer topped with fresh ruby grapefruit soda" has no beer, and the note is missing its full stop. Bad Santa "Willie's vanilla tea and ginger" has no vanilla.
6. **Template lines on the wrong venue.** All 33 lab cards print "Plate A, samples for four", including Hereditary's "Graham Family Kitchen" (with "Lv 18"), Saw's bathroom ("Ration sheet. Hot food. Eat it fast.") and the Kowalski bakery ("Bakery board. Hot, filling and plenty of it."). The takeaway layout puts "The first of every course for two, on the table by the opening credits" and order numbers on home kitchens (The Heat, The Big Sick, A Christmas Story, Blue Beetle). The kiosk layout puts a "Surf report" on Jurassic World Rebirth's "Saint-Hubert Research Dock" and About Time's Cornwall beach house. The Rock prints "Issue" six times in the price column. Close Encounters prints "Now showing / Now showing". Uncle Buck repeats "Pancakes the size of a hubcap." and prints a synopsis line on the card.
7. **Prices.** Beerfest is set in Munich but every price is in dollars ("$3.50", "$17.00", "Running tab $50.25"). Office Christmas Party says "the party food's on the company card" and then prices sliders at "$25" and salmon at "$34". Venues where nobody pays still print prices: Starship Troopers "FedNet" ($6.00 to $12.00), Jurassic World Rebirth's research dock ($2.75 to $18.50), Hitch's "Hitch Date Consulting" ($3.75 to $15.50). Iron Man's staff cafeteria sells a "scotch penicillin" for "$1.95" in 2008.
8. **Australian words on American cards.** "yoghurt" turns up on about 35 US-set cards: Iron Man "Yoghurt flatbreads", Ghostbusters II, Tootsie, Spaceballs "Yoghurt parfait", The Big Lebowski, Office Space, Gremlins, Inside Out, The Avengers, Watchmen, Who Framed Roger Rabbit. Back to the Future has "Butter cake with vanilla icing", Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 "fudge icing", Die Hard "red and green icing". Juno has "pickles and tomato sauce on small buns", Beethoven "corn dogs with tomato sauce", The Untouchables "tomato sauce on the side". Signs "under napoli, ham and mozzarella", Jingle All the Way "royal icing and cachous", Wall-E "golden haloumi". The other way round, the recipes leak "ice the cookies" into about 35 method steps.
9. **Film words and names piling up.** The Heat prints "Mullins" 11 times, including "Ashburn and Mullins whiskey sour" and "Pint of Boston lager ... Mullins family style". Fletch prints "Fletch" 10 times, Uncle Buck "Buck" 9, She's All That "Laney" 9, Walk Hard "Cox" 9 and "Dewey" 8, Chinatown "Gittes" 8. Close Encounters stacks mashed potato three ways: "Mashed potato mountain", fritters "piled up like a mashed potato mountain" and chicken with "mashed potato on the side for sculpting".
10. **Period slips.** Starship Troopers "roast cauliflower, tahini and pomegranate". Toy Story (1995) "Army men pea and edamame salad". Kenny (2006) "Nashville style" hot chicken and a "michelada". The Untouchables' 1930 banquet serves a "Repeal Day old fashioned", and repeal came in 1933. Close Encounters' 1977 campground cafe puts "pâté" on its crackers.
11. **Timing words don't agree across surfaces.** Hot Fuzz's ploughman's reads "Takes 15 min, then 1 hr to chill" on the Prep sheet when the step is warming the cheddar to room temperature, "Prep 15 min, 1 hr waiting" on the phone, and "then 60 min to rest or set" on the film page. 351 film pages print long times in minutes ("cook 88 min" for The Godfather's gravy, "cook 103 min" for Goodfellas) while the Prep sheet prints "1 hr 28 min". The 97 hand-built film pages have no "The menu is set at" line.

## To reach 9.5

1. Run a venue check over the 903 generated cards. Prisons, messes, galleys, labs and break rooms get canteen food and no spirits. A US Navy galley gets no alcohol. A bakery sells baked goods. Fix The Rock, Chicago, Saw, Under Siege, Fantastic Beasts, Jumanji and The Santa Clause first.
2. Hold each generated card to one venue and one night. Cut dishes named for other locations or scenes (Love Actually, X-Men: First Class, Ghost Protocol, Beverly Hills Cop, Enchanted, Ghost, Bridesmaids, Rocketman) and swap in what that venue would actually serve.
3. Rewrite the 364 two-sentence notes as one food line with at most a short film touch, no plot. Trim the 71 notes at 100 or 101. Pull the Chinatown ending out of the footer.
4. Make every dish name match its note (Home Alone milk, The Rock fudge squares, Red Dog radler, Bad Santa vanilla tea), and fix the two lowercase "sloppy joes" names on Little Giants and Hellboy.
5. Strip stock lines that don't fit from the shared layouts. Lab: drop "Plate A, samples for four", "Lv" and "Ration sheet" unless the venue is a lab or a mess. Takeaway: no order numbers or "first of every course for two" on home kitchens. Kiosk: no surf report off the beach. Canteen: one "Issue" label, not six.
6. Prices: Beerfest in euros. No prices at FedNet, the Jurassic research dock, Hitch's office or the Office Christmas Party ("on the company card"). Bring the Iron Man cocktail and Beverly Palms room service up to their year.
7. Run a US word pass on the cards: yoghurt to yogurt, icing to frosting on US cakes, tomato sauce to ketchup where it's ketchup, plus napoli, cachous and haloumi. Then run an Australian pass on the method steps: "ice the cookies" becomes "ice the biscuits".
8. Cap a name or film word at three uses per card across dish names, notes, house lines, cue and footer (The Heat, Fletch, Uncle Buck, She's All That, Walk Hard, Chinatown, Close Encounters).
9. Use one timing format on the Prep sheet, phone and film page ("1 hr 28 min", "1 hr waiting"). Pick the waiting label from the step itself (room temperature is not "to chill"). Add the "set at" line to the 97 hand-built film pages.
