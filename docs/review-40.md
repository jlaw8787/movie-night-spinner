# Review 40: Reel Dinner

**Overall score: 7.3 out of 10**

Method: I dumped what all 1,000 cards really print (card faces, dish list and notes, phone menu, prep sheet and shopping list) with `scripts/dump-cards.cjs`, then ran my own sweeps over the dumps and the 1,000 built film pages. The sweeps covered dashes, alcohol on family cards, price order and gaps, plot tails in notes, notes repeating or contradicting the dish, era words against the setting, Australian words on American cards, double buys and method words on the shopping list, overnight steps, template words against the venue, and page against card. I read 155 cards in full: 25 hand-built one-offs, 22 Australian films, 33 kids and family films and 75 others from 1934 to 2025. Every example quoted below was checked on the live card, phone menu, prep sheet or film page. There are 12 screenshots, `docs/review-40-01.png` to `docs/review-40-12.png`.

## What is strong

- **The hand-built one-offs are the best thing on the site.** The Godfather prints Louis' in the Bronx with "THE BEST Veal IN THE CITY", a 1.72 veal scaloppine and a "House customs" box that ends "Luca sleeps with the fishes." Home Alone is a Little Nero's delivery docket for 671 Lincoln Ave, Winnetka, $11.80, "Paid $12. Keep the change, you filthy animal." Home Alone 2 is a Plaza room service card with a guest charge of $967.43 and a rule that any tab over $900 needs a grown-up to sign. Die Hard is a Nakatomi 30th floor party card with a building directory and the "fists with your toes" tip.
- **Facts and dates hold up.** Back to the Future prints "SATURDAY NOVEMBER 5 1955", Die Hard prints "Christmas Eve, Saturday, December 24" and Goodfellas prints "SUNDAY, MAY 11TH, 1980". All three weekdays are correct. Dog Day Afternoon's coupon is "Good through Labor Day 1972".
- **Easter eggs land.** This Is Spinal Tap has "One louder rum and Coke ... over 11 ice cubes" and "Black coffee, none more black". White Men Can't Jump has "Cat in an oven buttermilk biscuits". Revenge of the Sith has "Mustafar lava cakes: High ground: molten dark chocolate cakes". Beauty and the Beast has "Gaston's eggs: Twelve eggs halved and piped". Mulan has Mushu's congee "egg and bacon, happy to see you".
- **Australian cards feel local and of their time.** Phar Lap prices in shillings and pence (10d sandwiches, 1s 2d roast beef rolls). The Dressmaker has footy final party pies at 9d. The Castle has rissoles, a pool room prawn platter and moselle from the cask. Red Dog prints a 1970s Dampier pub board with Pilbara barra at $3.75.
- **Prices are mostly right.** On the 378 cards that carry prices I found no starter or sweet dearer than a main. Decades read true: 35¢ cheeseburgers at Lou's Cafe in 1955, 15¢ to 85¢ at the 1934 auto camp, 9d to 3s 7d at Pratt's Store.
- **No em or en dashes anywhere.** I found zero across 1,000 card faces, phone menus, prep sheets and film pages. Spelling is Australian throughout ("colour", "flavour", "yoghurt", "caramelised").
- **For the shared layouts, page and card match.** Of 6,974 printed dish names on shared-layout cards, only 2 are missing from the film page (both on Good Will Hunting), and only 3 notes differ.
- **The phone menu opens on the food.** At 390 wide the page lands on the first menu page ("To Start" on The Castle, "Snacks" on Turner & Hooch) and reads at normal size, with section tabs below.

## What holds it back

1. **Plot recap still rides on hundreds of notes.** 612 printed notes on 407 cards have a second sentence. In 353 of them (272 cards) that sentence has nothing to do with the food. A further 138 notes on 129 cards end in a comma clause of plot ("before", "after", "when", "where").
   - Speed, Harry's pop quiz chicken burgers: "Crumbed chicken burgers with spicy slaw. Pop quiz: Harry asks, Jack says shoot the hostage."
   - The Amazing Spider-Man, Queens glazed meatloaf: "Beef and pork meatloaf glazed with tomato, buttery mash. Dinner with Ben on responsibility."
   - Jurassic World Dominion, Sweet almond milk: "Almond milk sweetened with honey and cinnamon. Maisie named the baby Beta."
   - Edward Scissorhands, Topiary garden platter: "Crisp garden vegetables with herby green goddess dip. Edward trims the hedges into animals."
   - The Incredibles, Jack-Jack's corn puffs: "... paprika and chilli, before the baby bursts into flame."
   - Some notes do not lead with food at all: Tropic Thunder, "Portnoy's mango raspberry fruit jellies: He wants jelly beans very, very badly."; Friendship, "Cold can of craft IPA: Austin's beer invite, accepted by Tami on Craig's behalf."; Kung Fu Panda 2, "Peach blossom iced tea: From the Sacred Peach Tree, Oogway's old spot."
   - 32 notes repeat the dish name, for example Good Will Hunting "Caramel apple crumble with vanilla custard: Caramel apple crumble with vanilla custard, made with a bushel of them apples." and Wolverine "Team X bacon dates: Bacon dates for Team X, before Logan walks out."

2. **Film pages for hand-built menus show a different menu from the card.** On 96 of the 97 hand-built one-offs, the film page's "The printed menu" section carries notes that differ from the card: 488 of the 524 dishes that appear on both. On 81 of those pages, 147 dishes are listed that the card does not print at all.
   - The Godfather page: "Louis' veal scaloppine: Lemon and capers, ordered at the quiet Bronx table". The card: "Veal scaloppine: Thin cutlets pan-fried in butter, with Marsala wine and mushrooms." The page also contradicts the card on the sauce.
   - The Goodfellas page lists Karen's prawn cocktail, Steak pizzaiola, Lemon ice and Jimmy's Scotch and ginger highball. None of them is on the card.
   - Recipes differ too. The Return of the King card and phone print "Pale ginger beer over honey ginger syrup" with ginger beer in the recipe, while the page recipe calls for "4 375ml bottles alcohol-free pale ale" (screenshot 08). On Turner & Hooch, the page note says "Cod in crisp batter" and the recipe under it says "8 flathead fillets" (screenshot 11).

3. **Alcohol on kids, Christmas and wizard films.** 13 of these films print alcohol on the card, phone menu or prep sheet, and 7 more show it only on the film page.
   - Kids and family: Red Dog "Red Dog ruby grapefruit shandy $0.70: Lager topped with fresh ruby grapefruit soda" with "1 L cold lager" under Bottle shop (screenshot 04); Treasure Planet "Beef chuck stewed with ... stout" with "375ml stout" under Bottle shop; School of Rock "Dewey takes the principal out for a beer"; Phar Lap "Trainer's whisky and dry ginger"; Turner & Hooch blood orange mimosa with "360ml sparkling wine"; Father of the Bride wine; Flash Gordon red wine sauce; The Man from Snowy River red wine gravy; Hocus Pocus clams steamed in white wine.
   - Christmas: Die Hard prints an Old Fashioned with 240ml bourbon on the list and "Champagne by the bottle". Love Actually prints mulled wine, Baileys and a sherry trifle.
   - Pages only: The Two Towers (red wine gravy), Deathly Hallows Part 1 (250ml red wine), The Desolation of Smaug (figs "stewed in claret"), It's a Wonderful Life (martinis), Miracle on 34th Street 1947 (red wine), Bad Santa and Four Christmases (beer).
   - The swap leaves odd text behind. The Fantastic Beasts phone recipe now reads "Pour into teacups and add a splash of cucumber juice to the grown-ups' cups."

4. **Prep sheets call steps overnight but plan them the same day.** 66 prep sheets have a step marked "overnight" while the header only says to start a few hours earlier.
   - Arthur: "Cool, then chill overnight. 8 hr" under a header of "start 7 hr 13 min earlier, for waiting" (screenshot 07).
   - The Godfather: "Make the risotto the day before" under "start 20 min earlier".
   - Shutter Island: "Soak the beans overnight (not counted in the time)".
   - Kickboxer and Gran Torino: "glutinous rice, soaked overnight" with no plan for it at all.
   - **Method words on the shopping list.** About 130 lines on about 115 lists, for example Big "2 limes, zested, then cut into wedges", Rocketman "6 oranges, 5 juiced and 1 in wheels", The Ring "450ml thickened cream, 300ml for the pots and the rest for piping", Captain Marvel "4 eggs, separated", Red One "4 rashers of bacon, cooked and chopped".
   - **Things bought twice.** About 50 lists. 34 lists carry "1 packet sugar" next to caster sugar (Donnie Brasco, Scarface). 12 carry "1 bag ice" next to "4 large ice cubes" (The Untouchables, Wall Street). 7 carry lemon or lime juice "about 4 lemons" plus whole lemons (Short Circuit, Man of Steel). Home Alone has "4 maraschino cherries" and "4 cocktail cherries". Step Brothers has thickened cream twice.

5. **Food from the wrong decade.** My sweep flagged 187 cards and I confirmed about 60 by hand.
   - "Sliders" print on 38 cards set before the mid 2000s: Planes, Trains and Automobiles (1987) turkey sliders, Hairspray (1962) sloppy joe sliders at $1.00, Oppenheimer (1940s) green chile cheeseburger sliders, Shutter Island (1954) lobster roll sliders.
   - Cold brew: Speed (1994) "Annie's coffee: Overnight cold brew" (screenshot 10) and A Nightmare on Elm Street 3 (1987).
   - Edible glitter: Superman (1978), Hook (1991), Priscilla (1994).
   - Rocky III (1982): tiramisu, balsamic bruschetta on ciabatta, and fish tacos with lime crema.
   - Chinatown (1937): "Albacore Club seared tuna crostini ... lemon aioli".
   - Brown butter choc chip cookies: Fast Times (1982), The Big Lebowski (1991).
   - Joker (1981) salted caramel popcorn, Flashdance (1983) cucumber and elderflower gin fizz, Kick-Ass and Captain Marvel (1995) hot honey, Desperado (1995) mezcal margarita.

6. **Australian words on American cards.** 13 cards print them.
   - Close Encounters "Potato gems with ketchup", Fargo "Potato gem hotdish bites", Jingle All the Way "beef gem hotdish".
   - Smokey and the Bandit and BASEketball "ice cold from the esky".
   - Despicable Me 2 "fairy bread ... hundreds and thousands".
   - Kick-Ass "Rockmelon cubes", Halloween "Lolly bowl's full", 21 Jump Street "sour lollies", Car Wash "fruit ice blocks", Flubber "orange kumara wedges", Four Christmases "Jumping castle party pies", Total Recall pavlova.
   - Behind the cards, 17 American and British recipes swap the fish named in the note for flathead: It Follows, Rocky III and 22 Jump Street "cod", Gollum's "crispy trout", Mrs Beaver's "Trout fried in butter", Rudy "perch", Sweet Home Alabama "Catfish".

7. **Template words that do not suit the venue.** About 20 cards.
   - Prom and formal headings ("FORMAL, CLASS OF", "Before the Dance", "Last Dance Sweets") at house parties and frat houses: Weird Science "PROM NIGHT, CLASS OF 1985" at Wyatt's House (screenshot 05), Neighbors "FORMAL, CLASS OF 2014", Superbad, Old School, Legally Blonde, Monsters University, and Barbie "FORMAL, CLASS OF 2023" in the Dreamhouse kitchen.
   - Takeaway wording ("Order by number", "packed in one bag") at family homes: Turning Red (Lee Family Temple), Blue Beetle (Reyes Family Kitchen), The Big Sick, The Heat, and Big Daddy (Sonny's Apartment).
   - Prices at a home: Always Be My Maybe "Kim Family Kitchen" with "Prices subject to change", and Julie & Julia "Priced at cost" at Julie's flat. Prices at a mess: Escape from New York's "Watch Mess", where coffee is .70.
   - Fantastic Beasts shows "MESS OPEN" and "RATIONS" on Kowalski's bakery (screenshot 06).
   - A Christmas Story's Chop Suey Palace serves "Meatloaf bites" and "Prosciutto wrapped breadsticks and ricotta".

8. **Notes that contradict the dish, and dishes that are not dishes.**
   - Bridesmaids "Milwaukee beer-battered cheese curds: Lager battered haloumi cubes" (screenshot 09).
   - Home Alone "Big glass of milk", where the recipe is a chocolate malted shake with ice cream and syrup.
   - Home Alone pizza "cut in triangles", where the recipe says "Cut into small squares".
   - Superbad "Jules's party pepperoni honey chilli pizza: A slice, before the sleepover and the pizza bagels."
   - Plain items with no cooking: Dumb and Dumber "Mutt Cutts road snacks: Chips eaten in a van shaped like a dog.", Animal Kingdom "Suburban dip and chips: ... Picked up from the supermarket.", Child's Play "Cereal with far too much sugar", Scary Movie "Cheese and crackers on the coffee table", Captain America "Hot dogs".
   - One fact slip: About Time gives the Cornwall water as 23°C.

9. **Smaller price gaps.** Prices are generally good, but a few cards leave one item unpriced among priced ones: Miracle on 34th Street (1994) "Hot spiced cider", and Jurassic World "Main Street cinnamon sugar pretzels". Airline cards (Die Hard 2, Jackie Brown, Captain America: Civil War, Final Destination) charge for starters, print "Included" for mains and leave drinks blank. Desperado prices a 1995 cantina at $15 to $19 a main.

10. **Desktop has small text on a few layouts.** At 1280 the tearoom layout prints notes in small italics (Bridesmaids, screenshot 09). Some phone card pages leave half the page blank under three dishes (The Castle, screenshot 03).

## To reach 9.5

1. Cut every second sentence and plot tail that has no tie to the food: about 490 notes on about 350 cards. Keep one food gag at most ("One bowl per universe." is fine, "Dinner with Ben on responsibility." is not). Rewrite the 32 notes that repeat the dish name and the notes that do not lead with food.
2. Rebuild the film pages for the 97 hand-built menus from what the card prints: same dishes, same notes, and recipes that match the phone menu. Drop the 147 dishes the card never prints.
3. Clear all alcohol from the 13 kids, Christmas and wizard cards (including cooking wine and stout) and from the 7 pages. Make the page recipe use the same no-alcohol swap as the card. Rewrite swap leftovers such as "cucumber juice to the grown-ups' cups".
4. On the prep sheet, any step that says overnight, the day before or 8 hours or more should put the dish under "start the day before", or the wording should drop "overnight". Fix the 66 sheets.
5. Strip method words from the shopping list (zested, juiced, separated, "for the pots and the rest for piping") and merge sugar with caster sugar, ice bags with ice cubes, juice with whole fruit, and the two kinds of cherries.
6. Run a per-decade banned list against the setting year (sliders, cold brew, edible glitter, hot honey, brown butter cookies, salted caramel, elderflower, mezcal, lime crema, ciabatta, tiramisu before the late 1980s) and swap in food of the time.
7. On cards set outside Australia, swap Australian words (potato gems, esky, lollies, rockmelon, fairy bread, kumara, ice blocks, party pies). Make every fish recipe use the fish its note names.
8. Give the prom template a party variant for house and frat parties ("The Spread", no "Class of" or "Before the Dance"). Give family homes a home variant with no ordering, bagging or prices. Fix Fantastic Beasts (bakery, not mess), Escape from New York (no prices at a mess) and A Christmas Story (Chinese restaurant food).
9. Fix the note contradictions (Bridesmaids, Home Alone milk and pizza, Superbad), replace the plain non-dishes with real dishes, and price every line on the cards with gaps.
