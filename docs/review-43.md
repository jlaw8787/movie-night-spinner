# Reel Dinner, review 43

I reviewed what is live on main (ed786311) and nothing else: the photo-on dump of all 1,000 cards (/tmp/r43/dump.json), the 1,000 built film pages under menu/, and the running site. I wrote Python sweeps over every card, phone menu, prep sheet and film page for dashes, alcohol on dry cards, prices by layout, money and setting year, setting words both ways, recipe and shopping words, note endings, repeated names, plot tails, name against note clashes, period foods, template words against venue, overused film words, broken quantities, untimed steps and film page against card. I then read 215 cards dish by dish (a stratified sample across all 134 layouts, with every hand-built one-off layout in it, plus kids, Christmas and wizard films) and opened 17 films in Chrome through Playwright at 1280 and at 390 wide: Ghost, The Green Mile, The Godfather, Treasure Planet, Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again, Better Off Dead, Iron Man, Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone, Frozen, The Great Outdoors, Mad Max, Casino Royale, Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels, Watchmen, Napoleon Dynamite, Die Hard and Uncle Buck. For each I checked the printed card, the phone text menu with a recipe opened, the Prep sheet on screen at both widths, and the real "Save PDF" output (menu and prep sheet, rendered to images). I also opened three film pages on a phone and tested the Prep sheet's "Choose dishes" picker.

## Overall: 7.8 / 10

| Area | Score | One line |
|---|---|---|
| True to the film | 8.0 | Rich Easter eggs and real venues, but a run of dishes that belong to another place, plus a printed placeholder ("Comedian's Name cookies"). |
| Dish notes | 8.0 | Mostly lead with the food; still some name repeats, plot tails, quote-first notes and 18 notes with no full stop. |
| Prices | 7.5 | Era tracking is good, but prisons, camps and a private card game charge, euros print in 1969 and 1979, and "crowns" is a catch-all. |

## What is strong

- Zero em dashes and zero en dashes across all cards, phone menus, prep sheets and all 1,000 film pages.
- Dry films are dry. Across 244 kids, family, fairy tale, Christmas and wizard films I found no alcohol on any card, phone menu or prep sheet; the dry swaps read naturally ("God bless us mulled grape punch", "Mulled apple punch").
- Prices follow the year the film is set, not the year it was released, in the large majority of cards: Grease prints "Frosty Palace burger 80¢", The Green Mile prints 1935 cents, Goldfinger and Dr. No print shillings and pence, Ladies in Black prints "2/11", Amélie prints francs, Kill Bill prints yen.
- The hand-built one-offs are the best work on the site: The Godfather ("Leave the gun. Take the cannoli."), Casino's "Count them. We did." muffin, Back to the Future's "No Pepsi Free", Goodfellas' prison-kitchen timeline, Do the Right Thing, Moonstruck.
- Easter eggs land often: Superman II "Keep it on the flame, Rock.", Ferris Bueller "Abe Froman's hot sausages", Waitress "I Hate My Husband pie", The Green Mile "the colour of faded limes on the floor", Toy Story 2 "Al's Toy Barn honey butter fried chicken".
- The Prep sheet for the cooked dishes is close to professional: 99 percent of method steps carry a time, make-ahead lines are sensible, a "Bottle shop" aisle exists, and the printed A4 sheet is tidy (screenshot 02).
- Desktop and phone layouts held up in every film I opened: no sideways scroll at 390, no clipped text, body text at 16px on the phone menu.
- Film words are rarely overused: only 7 cards use one film word four times.

## What holds it back

1. **Printed dishes with no real recipe and no shopping (1,080 dishes, 636 films, 598 of them mains).** The card prints a dish, often the hero main with a price, but the phone shows "How to make it / Not on tonight's cook" and a one-line method, the Prep sheet leaves it out ("5 of 6 dishes picked"), and the shopping list has none of its ingredients. Ticking it in "Choose dishes" only adds "From the printed menu, buy to suit" and an unquantified list. Example: Ghost prints "Rita Miller's pot roast $11.50"; ticked, the sheet shows "beef chuck roast / brown onions / carrots / potatoes / beef stock" with no amounts. By course: 598 mains, 440 starters, 42 sweets. This breaks standard 9 (every ingredient listed) for most of the catalogue.

2. **American-set cards speak Australian or British (202 of about 709 cards).** Clear cases only, counted by film: "spring onion" on 48 US cards ("Ginger and spring onion on white bread", Three Men and a Baby), "icing sugar" on 20 ("Fried dough, shaken hot in icing sugar.", Mean Streets), "rump" on 17 ("Rump in butter and garlic", Goodfellas), "spuds" on 15, "golden syrup" on 13, "thickshake" on 9 ("Jenko's AP chem malt thickshake", 21 Jump Street), "cos" on 8 ("Cos, coddled egg, anchovy and Parmesan", Trading Places), "fairy floss and toffee apples" on 7 ("Carnival fairy floss and toffee apples", The Sandlot), plus "Marylands brushed with smoky sauce" (The Great Outdoors, Wisconsin), "Chicken salted fries with onion gravy." (Screwed, Pittsburgh), "Corned silverside, cabbage, spuds" (The Fugitive, Chicago), "Salt beef sliced thin and piled high on rye" (Spider-Man 3, New York), "Tinned franks and beans" (Tremors), "Lime cordial, lemonade and vanilla ice cream." (Batman Forever), "Truro post office sausage rolls" (Men in Black II). Some cards contradict themselves: Uncle Buck "Bowling night cheesy gravy chips" over "Hot fries under melted cheddar", I Know What You Did Last Summer "Croaker Queen fried whiting and chips" over "thick-cut fries", Almost Famous "Riot House room service steak Diane ... chips". The reverse (British cards with American words) is rare: "Slice and bake Earl Grey and lemon cookies." (Prisoner of Azkaban), "cream cheese frosting" (Wallace & Gromit), "Veld frites ... crisp double-fried fries" (Wonder Woman's London and Belgium).

3. **Phone recipes mostly have no times (20,445 of 30,245 phone steps; 2,393 of 7,489 recipes have no time at all).** The phone recipe also has no "Prep, cook" line, though the film page and Prep sheet have one. Examples as printed on the phone: "Pour into the hot pan and bake until golden and a skewer comes out clean." (The Green Mile cornbread), "Brush with egg and bake until golden." (Philosopher's Stone pumpkin pasties), "Bake at 180°C until crisp." (Once Upon a Time in America bagel crisps). Most users are on the phone, so this is the recipe most people cook from.

4. **Template words that do not suit the venue.** The canteen layout prints a school day ("9:00 / 10:30 / Lunch / 2:00 / Reports / 3:30 / Home time") on 26 non-school venues: Stark Industries Cafeteria (Iron Man), Central Park Zoo Canteen (Madagascar), Nike, Beaverton (Air), St. Katherine's Convent Kitchen (Sister Act), Jackson's Star Colony Canteen (Alien: Romulus), Ice Box Cafeteria (Deadpool 2), Justiceville Soup Kitchen (They Live). The lab layout prints "Ration sheet. Results may vary. Seconds encouraged." and "EXPERIMENT IN PROGRESS" on paying staff cafeterias (Pym Technologies Break Room, Oscorp Staff Cafeteria, CIA Basement Cafe, Rekall Memory Lounge) and "Ration sheet. Clearance required past this line." on the Room of Requirement. The takeaway layout prints "Your fortune" on a Shawarma Joint (The Avengers), Newton Food Centre (Crazy Rich Asians), Reyes Family Kitchen (Blue Beetle), Nanjiani Family Kitchen (The Big Sick) and Sonny's Apartment (Big Daddy). The pub layout prints "Puddings" on Walkabout Creek Hotel, Palace Hotel Broken Hill, Dampier Pub and The Randwick Hotel, and on a Canadian brewery (Strange Brew). The tearoom layout prints "Tea at home, in the front room" on Arendelle Castle Kitchen (Frozen). The tavern line "Rooms above, stabling round the back. Coin of the realm only." prints on a spaceport inn (Treasure Planet). The banquet layout turns Mooby's fast food counter (Dogma) into "The courses in procession" with "One of everything $47.50". The newspaper layout prints "35 CENTS" on 284 Green Street, Enfield, August 1977 (The Conjuring 2).

5. **People pay where nobody pays, and the money is wrong in places.** Prices print at a prison: The Green Mile, Cold Mountain Penitentiary ("Last meal meatloaf with mash and gravy 20¢") and Deadpool 2's Ice Box ("$11.50"); at a training camp (Remember the Titans, "$1.30"); at an expedition mess (Atlantis, "20¢"); at a private card game (Lock, Stock, "Harry's Back Room Card Game", "£2.25" under "Bill of Fare"); and on a dentist's wagon (Django Unchained). Euros print before the euro existed: Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny, Sicily 1969 ("Syracuse swordfish with caponata €26.00") and Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again, Kalokairi 1979 ("Ouzo over ice and water €0.50"). El Mariachi's Mexican border cantina prints dollars ("Red chilli chicken enchiladas $14.00"). Minions' Orlando 1968 card prints "Queen's coronation chicken $1.95". "Crowns" with cents is a stock currency for 13 unrelated worlds (Middle-earth, Berk, Arendelle's neighbours, Inca Peru, the Caribbean, Treasure Planet's "3.40 crowns", where the film's money is drubloons). Level is off in places: Almost Famous, set 1973, "Golden god cheeseburgers $10.50"; Casino Royale's high-stakes casino "The Vesper gin and vodka martini €4.75"; Ferris Bueller's snooty Chez Quis "French 75 with lemon twist $1.25", the same as the lemonade.

6. **Dishes that belong to another place.** Minions' Villain-Con Cafe in Orlando serves "Queen's coronation chicken", "Scarlet's castle roast beef and Yorkshires" and "Crown jewels cheese straws". Wonder Woman's Themyscira Great Hall serves "London 1918 pasties" and "Charlie's Cullen skink". Arrival's Montana Army Camp serves "Kangaroo skewers with pepperberry". Rocketman's Troubadour, West Hollywood 1970, serves "Sunset Strip shrimp cocktail" with "pink Marie Rose sauce" and a "Dodger Stadium knickerbocker glory". Dial of Destiny's Syracuse Dockside Cafe serves "Apollo 11 parade soft pretzels ... spicy brown mustard". Pirates of the Caribbean: At World's End (1720s) serves "Singapore chilli crab ... sweet chilli tomato sauce" and Dead Man's Chest serves "Hush puppies".

7. **Notes that break the house style.** 18 notes print with no full stop, all on the ski lodge layout: Better Off Dead ("Thin double-fried fries in paper cones with ketchup") and Hot Tub Time Machine ("Gruyère and emmental melted with white wine, bread and boiled potatoes for dunking"). About 44 notes restate the name: "Cheese and tomato grilled sandwiches, cut in triangles" over "Cheese and tomato grilled sandwiches cut in triangles, eaten fast before anyone shouts squirrel." (Up), "Buck's family-size beef lasagne" over "Family-size beef lasagne baked bubbling in the oven" (Uncle Buck), "Milo, made strong" over "Hot Milo made strong and malty" (The Babadook). Plot tails survive: "eaten slowly while Katie stands by the bed for two hours." (Paranormal Activity), "as Joel screams." (Addams Family Values), "Kramer built Scolex the claw." (Inspector Gadget), "for the Nelsons' road trip." (Minions), "Pot roast, with her sisters crowding round the table." (Ghost, which is also nearly the name again). Some notes lead with a line, not food: "Burt's toast: broke into the wrong rec room." (Tremors, "Cold longneck beer"), "There go the brownies. Fudgy walnut brownies, caramel sauce." (The 'Burbs). To dodge repeating a name, notes slip into vague words: "Hollowed rounds", "Corn rounds", "Whole bird", "cured fish" under "salmon" in about 15 names.

8. **Plain or pun dishes still print.** Plain: "Chocolate mousse" (Kate & Leopold), "Mud cake" (Predator), "Onion rings" (The Rocketeer), "Crab cakes" (Wedding Crashers, The Exorcist), "Chicken soup" (Batman & Robin), "Blueberry pie" (Stand by Me), "Steak frites" (Ferris Bueller). Puns: "Pottery wheel pie" (Ghost), "Dad burnt the turkey pancake stack" (The Santa Clause, whose note is pancakes and bacon), "Fifty bucks and a case of beer" (Almost Famous), "Mexi-can, never Mexi-can't." (Once Upon a Time in Mexico). A placeholder prints on the card: "Comedian's Name cookies" (Watchmen; the film page says "Comedian's badge cookies").

9. **Modern trend words on period cards (about 40 cards).** "Smashed beef patties" on Dragnet (1987), Coneheads (1993), The Mask (1994) and Independence Day (1996, "on brioche"); "flaky salt" on 20 cards set before 2000; cake pops as "Neuralyser cake truffles ... balls on sticks" (Men in Black, 1997); "Agamemnon's whipped feta feast platter" (Time Bandits, 1981); "Pulled pork rolls" (Mad Max Beyond Thunderdome, 1985); "Army men pea and edamame salad" (Toy Story, 1995).

10. **Film pages drift from the hand-built cards (57 of 97 hand-built films).** Generated cards match their pages exactly, but on hand-built cards the page joins or rewrites the card's words: Donnie Brasco card "Cheese fingers breaded twice" becomes "crumbed twice" on the page; Scarface "breaded and fried golden" becomes "crumbed"; Casino card "Buttermilk batter. Count them. We did." becomes "Buttermilk batter, ten berries in every one."; The Godfather "Ruby citrus syrup, cold seltzer." gains "An orange on every table." Dry renames also drift: card "Grinch green fruit punch", page "Grinch green punch"; card "Rainbow Connection fruit punch", page "Rainbow Connection punch"; card "Bacchus grape and rosemary soda spritz", list "Bacchus grape and rosemary spritz".

11. **Small recipe and shopping slips.** Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem prints "1 dill pickles", "1 tomato ketchup", "1 American mustard"; one other film prints "1 maraschino cherries". Recipes and shopping for the Australian cook still say "fries" (226 recipe lines, 30 shopping lines, e.g. "600g frozen crinkle-cut fries", Grease), "frosting" (11), "cookies" (7 shopping lines) and "skillet" (The Muppet Movie).

## To reach 9.5

1. Give every printed dish a full recipe. For the 1,080 "Not on tonight's cook" dishes, write quantities and timed steps, let "Choose dishes" add them properly, and put their ingredients on the shopping list when picked. Start with the 598 mains.
2. Add times to every phone recipe step and a "Prep x min, cook y min" line at the top of each phone recipe, reusing the Prep sheet timings that already exist.
3. Run a setting-word pass on American-set cards only: spring onion to scallions or green onions, icing sugar to powdered sugar, rump to sirloin, cos to romaine, thickshake to shake or malt, fairy floss to cotton candy, toffee apples to candy apples, golden syrup to corn syrup, cordial to syrup, tinned to canned, spuds to potatoes, chips (meaning fries) to fries, Marylands to leg quarters, chicken salt out, salt beef to corned beef, silverside out, sausage rolls replaced. Make dish names and notes agree (Uncle Buck, I Know What You Did Last Summer). Leave Australian-set cards (Finding Nemo, Anyone But You, The Fall Guy) alone.
4. Split template wording by venue type: a work-shift timetable ("Shift starts / Break / Lunch / Knock-off") for workplaces on the canteen layout, "Menu" in place of "Ration sheet" on paying cafeterias, "Your fortune" only on Chinese takeaway, "Desserts" or "Sweets" on Australian pubs, no front-room wording on castle kitchens, no stabling on spaceports, a counter board for Mooby's, pence for the Enfield paper.
5. Strip prices from prisons, camps, expedition messes, private games and wagons (The Green Mile, Deadpool 2, Remember the Titans, Atlantis, Lock Stock, Django). Fix the money: lire for Dial of Destiny 1969, drachmas for Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again 1979, pesos for El Mariachi and Desperado, drubloons for Treasure Planet, a film-true coin (or whole numbers, no cents) for the other "crowns" worlds. Reprice Almost Famous to 1973, and lift Casino Royale and Chez Quis to their venue level.
6. Swap the out-of-place dishes for food of the venue: Minions (Orlando 1968), Wonder Woman (Themyscira), Arrival (kangaroo), Rocketman (Marie Rose, knickerbocker glory), Dial of Destiny (pretzels), Pirates (chilli crab, hush puppies).
7. Restore the full stop on the ski lodge layout, rewrite the 44 name-repeat notes, cut the plot tails and quote-first notes listed above, and replace the vague dodges ("Hollowed rounds", "Whole bird") with the real food word.
8. Replace the plain and pun names listed in item 8 with complete dishes tied to the film, and fix "Comedian's Name cookies".
9. Swap the trend words on period cards: "griddled patties" for smashed, plain "salt" for flaky salt, drop cake truffles on sticks, whipped feta, pulled pork rolls and edamame from pre-2000 settings.
10. Build hand-built film pages from the printed card text, line for line, and give dry renames one name used everywhere.
11. Fix the broken quantities (Mutant Mayhem, maraschino cherries) and put recipes and shopping into Australian words: chips, icing, biscuits, frying pan.
