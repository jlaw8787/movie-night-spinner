# Review 36: Reel Dinner

**Overall: 8.2 out of 10**

I dumped and read the printed card for 178 films in full, plus the print list, phone menu and prep sheet text for each. The sample covered the hand-built favourites (The Big Lebowski, Jurassic Park, Home Alone, Back to the Future, Die Hard, Ratatouille, The Godfather, Goodfellas, Pulp Fiction, Elf, Ghostbusters), 41 Australian films (The Castle, Muriel's Wedding, Red Dog, The Dish, Gallipoli, both Crocodile Dundees, all five Mad Max films, Priscilla, The Sapphires, Phar Lap, Wolf Creek, Australia, The Dressmaker, Ladies in Black, Storm Boy, The Lighthorsemen, Beneath Hill 60, Danger Close, Animal Kingdom, Two Hands, Kenny, The Wog Boy and more), British films, 1930s to 1960s classics, eleven westerns, Gladiator, Gladiator II and Braveheart, kids' films, and 25 releases from 2024 to 2026. That touched 48 layouts, about 35 of them shared. I ran scans for dashes, US spellings, quote marks, note endings and price order, checked prep sheet header times against step times across every dish block, then looked at the site in Chrome at 1280 wide and at 390 by 844 on a phone, including two film pages. I did not read the handoff, ripple or earlier review docs.

## What is strong

- The best cards are a real pleasure to read. Each one knows its film and its venue. The Godfather is set at Louis' in the Bronx with "THE BEST Veal IN THE CITY", "We cook with Genco Pura olive oil", "Old-fashioned plumbing out back" and "Luca sleeps with the fishes." Goodfellas is Henry's last day, "SUNDAY, MAY 11TH, 1980, 6:55 A.M.", with "Veal, beef and pork. Go easy on the onions" and "Witness protection. Never again."
- The small facts are often spot on. Back to the Future prints "SATURDAY NOVEMBER 5 1955", and Die Hard prints "Christmas Eve, Saturday, December 24", and both days are right. Roman Holiday puts Joe and Ann at Caffe Rocca on Piazza della Rotonda. North by Northwest serves the brook trout Thornhill really orders in the dining car. Saturday Night Fever ends on Tony's "$4.00" raise from the paint shop. Monty Python gets Galahad's "Blue. No, yellow" right.
- Australian films get proper care. The Dish has the band playing Hawaii Five-0 for the ambassador and "Eat it before the power cut." Danger Close has "Delta Company is relieving Bravo at the rubber plantation today" and the Little Pattie concert. Animal Kingdom's "Deal or No Deal pork and fennel sausage rolls" is a sharp nod to J's mum. The Dressmaker opens on "I'm back, you bastard." Gallipoli, Lighthorsemen, Beneath Hill 60 and Danger Close all print "Issue" instead of prices, which is right for an army mess.
- Quotes are mostly real and in the right mouth: "Deserve's got nothing to do with it." (Unforgiven), "I have not yet begun to defile myself." (Tombstone), "Practically perfect people never permit sentiment to muddle their thinking." (Mary Poppins), "We all go a little mad sometimes. Haven't you?" (Psycho).
- Prep sheets add up. Every header I checked matches its step times. The Castle's rissoles say "Prep 28 min, cook 50 min" and the steps come to 78 minutes. Shopping lists are grouped by aisle and use Australian wording such as "250g tasty cheese", "300ml thickened cream" and "1.5 L Australian lager (4 stubbies of 375 ml)".
- The phone menu is clean and easy to use. It has large type, tabs for each course, a "How to make it" section under each dish and a clear "Not on tonight's cook" for extras. Film pages read well, show the printed card and give recipes for every dish.
- I found no em or en dashes in any card, phone menu, prep sheet or film page.

## What holds it back

1. **Prices where nobody pays.** About one card in nine charges for food in a place where nobody pays. The Great Escape's POW mess charges "Escape committee beef and barley stew 12 pfennigs" and adds "Prices subject to change." Young Guns' Tunstall Ranch Cookhouse and Seabiscuit's Ridgewood Ranch Cookhouse charge the hands. Shrek charges "9 gold coins" in his own swamp. Dune: Part Two's Sietch Tabr says "Fremen only" and charges 17 solari. Project Hail Mary charges $12.25 on a one-man ship ("Meals for one, 11.9 light years out"). Apollo 13 says "paid on splashdown". The Matrix says "Crew only" and lists "Cypher's steak 101 cr". The Shining charges the snowed-in caretakers for room service. Harry Potter charges "10 Galleons 4 Sickles" per cover for the Hogwarts feast. Pinocchio charges in shillings at Geppetto's workshop. Some cards contradict themselves on the page: Red One says "Compliments of the captain" next to $13.25, Superman says "FREE. TAKE ONE." next to a $21.50 pot roast in the Daily Planet break room, Boy says "Kai provided" over $7.00 mains, and Kenny says "Lunch is on me" over a price list.
2. **Prices wrong for the era or place.** The Menu prices Hawthorn à la carte from $10 to $29 under "Tasting menu. No substitutions." Lost in Translation has a "Wagyu katsu sando ¥410" and "Hibiki whisky on the rocks ¥500" at the Park Hyatt. Sinners prices 1932 red beans and rice at $1.50, dearer than the pork chops at $1.25. Weapons prices a third grade school lunch at "$9.25" and "$11.40" and lists "A brown-bag nip from USA Liquors $2.00" on a primary school canteen menu. Crazy Rich Asians has $23 hawker noodles and "We deliver, phone 555 0181" for a Singapore hawker centre. Mad Max puts fish and chips at $5.25 against a $1.95 pie in 1979. Easter Parade's Manhattan (25¢) is cheaper than the fruit punch (40¢). Goldfinger's mint julep (5s 5d) costs more than the Dover sole (4s 8d).
3. **Shared template wording that does not fit the venue.** The galley layout prints "Laser issue, fleet standard", "DECK 7" and "From the wheelhouse" on Moana 2's voyaging canoe and Red One's North Pole Command. The club layout prints "Two drink minimum at the stage tables. Cover charge after nine. No discussing ... at the tables." on Casablanca, Moulin Rouge!, The Sapphires' Vietnam soldiers' club, Sinners' 1932 juke joint and KPop Demon Hunters' backstage catering. The lab layout prints "Lv 69 / Badge / All areas / Plate A Now showing, 2000" on The Dish. The hotel layout prints "In your suite, 2014 ... A tray charge applies to every order" for a 1919 Istanbul guesthouse in The Water Diviner. The Babadook's home gets a cafe loyalty card. Notting Hill's travel bookshop serves "Creamy tarragon chicken £3.75". Oliver!'s den is set out as a newspaper. Phar Lap's 1930 Randwick Hotel serves "Counter meals 12 to 2 and 6 to 8", but NSW pubs shut at six then. Every tearoom card runs two lines together as "The bell for the picture Serve the mains when..." (Ladies in Black, The Dressmaker, Mary Poppins, Paddington, Frozen, Snow White, Miracle on 34th Street, Peter Rabbit). Both diner cards print a stray space before a comma: "Flo's café burgers, $8.25 , with" and "Phil's diner feast, $7.50 , with". The Lighthorsemen prints a "When to serve" heading with no cue under it.
4. **Quotes tacked onto dish notes.** Twenty-five sampled cards end a dish note with a famous line that has nothing to do with the dish. The Castle has "Carrot and thyme mince in flaky puff ... “How's the serenity?”". Babe has "Warm milk spiced with cinnamon and honey. “That'll do, pig. That'll do.”". Dirty Dancing has "Two thin smashed patties ... “Nobody puts Baby in a corner.”". Oppenheimer has "Pork, potato and chilli stew. “Now I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds.”". The Shining has "Chilled prawns in Marie Rose sauce. “All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy.”". Other real lines appear with no quote marks at all, such as Mad Max 2's "Just here for the gasoline", Top Gun's "I could tell you, but then I'd have to kill you" and Titanic's "Draw me like one of your French girls". Sixty cards use straight quotes and sixty use curly ones. Nine mix both, and Top Gun mixes them inside a single panel.
5. **Notes that skip the dish or repeat the film.** Chicken Run's leek pie note says only "Chickens go in, pies come out. Not this one." Star Wars has "Pass the bread. Han shot first." Minecraft has "The golden apples were already taken." Dune has "Bread in the deep desert sietch." Shrek's "Onion dip" is described as spinach and artichoke dip. Beneath Hill 60's "chicken and leek pie" note never mentions chicken. KPop's "sugar-dusted hot dogs" have no sugar in the note. Repetition turns into noise: Red Dog says "Dampier" four times, Shaun of the Dead says "Winchester" four times, Titanic says "first class" five times, and Apollo 13 has "Houston, we have a problem smoked brisket ... Houston, we have a problem."
6. **Period and place slips in the food.** Braveheart serves "neeps and tatties", a whisky toddy and shortbread in the 1290s. Phar Lap's 1930 Sydney pub serves tequila, rocket and toasted baguette. Babe's "Shepherd's pie" is "Beef mince in tomato gravy", which is cottage pie, and it is on a sheep farm. The Great Escape serves "black bean dip" and "Ice-cold vodka and apple". Citizen Kane serves pavlova in 1890s New York. Elvis puts "Graceland" meatloaf in 1955, two years before he bought the place. Back to the Future Part III serves a yoghurt dip in 1885. Boy serves garlic aioli in 1984 Waihau Bay. Danger Close's 1966 army mess serves caramel pork and prawn rice paper rolls.
7. **Fact errors.** Bridget Jones is headed "Pamela's Annual Turkey Curry Buffet", but in the film it is Una Alconbury's. White Christmas calls the liverwurst and buttermilk "Emma's midnight snack", but it is Bob and Betty's. Ghostbusters' work order totals "$45.00" while its lines add up to $41.50. Cool Runnings misspells "Yul Brenner". Rocky heads a title fight concessions card "Mighty Mick's Gym". The Menu's "breadless bread plate" comes "with warm sourdough, bread included", which kills the joke. Lebowski's bowling alley grill sells an "In-N-Out double-double".
8. **Spelling and house-style slips.** One card mixes "Mr. Bridger" and "Mr Bridger" (The Italian Job), and another mixes "Mr. Poppy" and "Mr Poppy" (Nativity!). Other cards use "Mrs. Gandy" and "Mr. McGregor". Storm Boy has "pippis" in the dish name and "Pipis" in the note. Happy Feet prints "-30 C" where every other card uses "°C". Barbie prints "gynecologist", The Great Escape's British camp prints "cookies", and Sydney-set Finding Nemo prints "oven fries". Coco mixes "chile" and "chilli", and Project Hail Mary mixes "puree" and "purée". Rocky has "After the 4 a.m. Run up the steps." with a stray capital R. Bad Santa drops the full stop on two notes.
9. **Desktop readability.** Notes are 12px italic on Goldfinger's clubhouse card and 13px on Gladiator's tavern card at 1280 wide, which is hard going. The canteen layout looks soft and slightly blurred on screen (The Great Escape, Weapons), so the grey notes are harder to read than the bold dish names.
10. **Small prep sheet slips.** Home Alone's sundae is headed "10 min to freeze first", but the step softens the ice cream. Gladiator II's dates call for "a grind of black pepper", which is not in the list. Happy Feet's fish pie says "Poach ... for 5 minutes" next to "19 min" and never boils the potatoes, and its iceberg wedge uses lemon that is not in the list. Some shopping lines are not things you buy: "4 small cups of cold water" (The Wog Boy), "600ml just-boiled water" (Braveheart, True Grit), "1 cup hot gravy" (Kenny) and "1 tsp instant coffee, dissolved in 2 tbsp hot water" (The Grand Budapest Hotel).

## What it would take to reach 9.5

1. Add a "who pays" flag per venue and print "Issue", "On the house" or no price wherever nobody pays: POW camps, ranch cookhouses, crews, homes, sietches, ships and school feasts. Check that no card says "free" or "on me" next to a price.
2. Sanity-check prices against the venue's real level, not just the era: Hawthorn as a fixed tasting price, the Park Hyatt at Tokyo hotel prices, hawker plates at hawker prices, and plain school lunch prices with no alcohol. Mains should cost more than drinks and sides.
3. Give each shared layout a few wording options per venue type, so that canoes, juke joints, observatories and guesthouses stop getting laser, cover-charge, clearance-badge and room-service lines. Fix the tearoom "The bell for the picture" run-on, the diner " , with" space and the empty Lighthorsemen cue.
4. Move every famous line into the quote panel or the showtime cue. A dish note should only describe the food. Pick one quote style (curly) and use it everywhere.
5. Rewrite the notes that do not describe the dish, and cap the film's place or name at once per course.
6. Run a period check on each card's dishes and ingredients against its setting year. Start with Braveheart, Phar Lap, Babe, The Great Escape, Citizen Kane, Elvis and Back to the Future Part III.
7. Fix the fact errors listed above. Add a total check to the work order, bar tab and combo layouts.
8. Run a house-style pass: Mr and Mrs with no full stop, "°C" everywhere, Australian spellings outside quotes, one spelling per word on a card, and a full stop at the end of every note.
9. Lift note text to at least 14px on screen at 1280 wide, and stop the canteen layout rendering soft.
10. Tighten the prep sheets so every ingredient a step uses is in its list, step text agrees with step times, and the shopping list only has things you can buy.
