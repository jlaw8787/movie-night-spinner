# Review 35: Reel Dinner

**Overall: 7.3 out of 10**

I rendered 154 films in full in Chrome (printed card at 1280 wide, phone menu at 390 by 844, Prep sheet and shopping list) across 36 layouts, including the hand-built cards, Australian films (Crocodile Dundee, Gallipoli, Muriel's Wedding, Storm Boy, Wolf Creek, The Castle, The Dish, Phar Lap, Animal Kingdom, Top End Wedding, Young Einstein, Lion, The Babadook, Strictly Ballroom, Priscilla), British films (Shaun of the Dead, Hot Fuzz, Kingsman, Notting Hill, Paddington 2, Snatch, Lock, Stock, Four Weddings, Love Actually, Goldfinger, Skyfall), period films (Braveheart, Gladiator, Tombstone, Gone with the Wind, The Great Escape), kids' films (Up, Tangled, Ratatouille, The Lego Movie, Lilo & Stitch, Frozen, Toy Story, Finding Nemo) and recent releases (Wicked, Dune: Part Two, Inside Out 2, Moana 2, Sinners, Weapons, Project Hail Mary). All 22 required films are in. I read the card text of a further 40 or so from audit/cards, scanned all 1,000 cards in audit/cards for repeated touches, template words and price order, scanned all 1,001 pages in menu/ for dashes, unit slips and whether their dishes match the printed card, and checked six doubtful quotes against the transcripts. Screenshots are docs/review-35-01.png to docs/review-35-16.png.

## What is strong

1. The hand-built cards are the best thing in the app. The Big Lebowski at Hollywood Star Lanes ("Mark it zero!", "Is this your homework, Larry?", "Careful, man, there's a beverage here!", the Folgers coffee brisket, Shomer shabbos), Ratatouille at Gusteau's, Jurassic Park's Visitor Center guide and island map (01) and Home Alone's Little Nero's order slip all read like real props from the film.
2. Venues are well chosen and mostly spot on: the Winchester, the Black Prince, the Crown in Sandford, the Snuggly Duckling, Walkabout Creek Hotel, the Oriental Saloon, the Palace Hotel in Broken Hill, Mel's Drive-In, the Frosty Palace, the Randwick Hotel.
3. Most quotes I checked are real and word for word: "I have not yet begun to defile myself." (Tombstone), "No, Mr. Bond, I expect you to die!" (Goldfinger), "The radio will tell you you need this or that and a thousand other things." (Storm Boy, Hide-Away Tom), "What did I say about singing?" (Shrek), "Croissants? I love croissants!" (The Lego Movie), "Have a nice cold pint, and wait for all of this to blow over." (Shaun of the Dead).
4. No em or en dashes anywhere: not in the 1,000 cards, not in the phone text, not in the 1,001 film pages, not in index.html or the visible app strings.
5. Desktop and phone render cleanly. None of the 154 films threw a page error, none scrolled sideways on the phone, and the phone menu (02, 10, 15) is big, clear and easy to tap, with recipes folded under "How to make it".
6. Era money is handled with care on most cards: shillings and pence for Gallipoli and Phar Lap, pennies for Braveheart, cents for American Graffiti and Grease, studs in The Lego Movie, gold coins in Tangled and Shrek.

## What holds it back

1. **Private homes print shop prices and trade wording.** About 150 cards set in a family kitchen, house, flat or cottage still print prices, licence numbers and service lines from a shop or mess template.
   - Hereditary, "Graham Family Kitchen" (03): "Ration sheet. Eat with your own spoon.", "QUARANTINE", "RATIONS", "SWEET ISSUE", "CANTEEN", "Return tray to the hatch. Rations subject to supply." and "The family dinner roast chicken $21.00".
   - The Babadook, "Amelia's Kitchen" (04): "Amelia's slow-cooked stew $18.00", "Milo, made strong $6.00", "Prices subject to change", "Board No. 2014".
   - Say Anything..., "Diane Court's House": "BAR AND GRILL" and "Kiosk licence 1989".
   - Insidious, "Lambert Family Home": "A tray charge applies to every order."
   - The Conjuring, "Perron Farmhouse Kitchen": "Bill No. 307" and "Prices subject to change".
   - National Treasure, "Gates Family House": "Ration sheet. Hot, filling and plenty of it." and "MESS OPEN".
   - The Dish, "Parkes Radio Telescope": "Ration sheet", "SWEET ISSUE", "Return tray to the hatch. Rations subject to supply." A country observatory in 1969 is not a ration mess.
   - Weapons, "Maybrook Elementary Cafeteria, third grade" (14): "Pennsylvania Dutch chicken pot pie $11.40", "Aunt Gladys's sloppy joes $9.25" and "A brown-bag nip from USA Liquors $2.00" on a primary school lunch menu.
2. **Film pages do not match the app.** 233 of the 1,000 film pages list dishes that are not on the printed card, and many of them carry an "On the printed menu" tag that is false.
   - Home Alone (12): the page shows the Little Nero's card, then lists "Cheese pizza bites", "Booby-trap paint can popcorn (On the printed menu)", "McCallister Christmas ham (On the printed menu)" and "Christmas cookies (On the printed menu)". The card and the app sell "Large cheese pizza", "Mac and cheese dinner" and "The giant sundae".
   - Tommy Boy: "Tommy's tight coat sausage rolls (On the printed menu)" is not on the card.
   - Pulp Fiction: the page has "Buddy Holly's chicken basket", "Big Kahuna Hawaiian burger" and "Diner pancakes". The card prints "BUDDY HOLLY CHICKEN", "BIG KAHUNA BURGER" and "COFFEE SHOP STACK".
   - Crocodile Dundee's page says "New York baked cheesecake ... Prep 10 min, cook 16 min, then 4.5 hours to rest or set". The bake alone is 55 minutes, and "4.5 hours" breaks the app's "4 hr 30 min" style.
3. **Prep sheet times disagree with their own steps.** In 10 of the 154 sheets the cook time is shorter than one of the cooking steps.
   - Crocodile Dundee (06): "New York baked cheesecake: Prep 10 min, cook 16 min", yet the step reads "bake at 160°C until set with a slight wobble. 55 min".
   - Ratatouille: "Colette's crème brûlée: Prep 5 min, cook 13 min", yet the step reads "bake until set with a slight wobble. 35 min".
   - Wolf Creek: "Kimberley roadhouse vanilla slice: Prep 5 min, cook 10 min", yet the step reads "bake between two trays at 200°C until flat and golden, then cool. 25 min".
   - Jurassic Park: "Tour car iced tea: Prep 10 min, cook 5 min, then 2 hr to cool and chill", while the step says "Cool, then chill for 2 hours (included in cook time)".
   - A phantom "Prep 5 min" is added to dishes whose steps are all cooking. Gallipoli's hash says "Prep 5 min, cook 43 min" and its steps add to 43. The same goes for Lilo & Stitch's loco moco, the Conjuring fish bake and Up's roast sirloin. Crocodile Dundee's garlic prawns say "prep 5 min" with no prep step.
   - Weapons: "Chocolate milk in the carton: Takes 5 min ... Chill the cartons until icy. 1 min".
   - Shaun of the Dead (07): "LONGEST ON THE NIGHT 4 HR" comes from chilling the beer ("Pint at the Winchester: Takes 5 min, then 3 hr to chill").
   - The header line "START 4 HR 13 MIN EARLIER, FOR WAITING" (Crocodile Dundee) and "START 3 HR 18 MIN EARLIER, FOR WAITING" (Home Alone) is clumsy and does not tell you what to start.
4. **Shopping lists read like ingredient lists, not lists you shop from.**
   - Braveheart (11): "2 lemons" and "4 lemon wheels" on separate lines, "300g butter" and "50g soft butter" on separate lines, and "600ml just-boiled water" under PANTRY.
   - Crocodile Dundee (06): "5 lemons", "60ml lime juice" and "4 lime wedges".
   - Project Hail Mary: "2 eggs" and "7 egg whites", "1/2 cup frozen peas", "2 cups ice cubes", "100g mozzarella, cut in small cubes".
   - Shaun of the Dead: "1 egg white" and "2 eggs", and "1.3 L English ale".
   - Home Alone: "1.4 L full cream milk" and "1.3 L cola".
   - Jurassic Park: "4 ripe avocadoes".
   - Anchorman: "2 lemons, juiced to give 60ml, plus slices to serve" and "1 avocado, mashed".
5. **Made-up lines are not printed as "A note from <name>".** 164 cards sign an invented line "from <name>" under a header such as "STUCK ON THE FRIDGE" or "PINNED UP IN THE DUGOUT". Only 175 cards use the "A note from" wording the standard asks for.
   - The Castle: "Rissoles are on, the family's home ... What a beautiful night." / "from Darryl Kerrigan".
   - Gallipoli: "Frank, eat what you can and keep your legs ready." / "from Archy Hamilton".
   - Wicked: "Roommate, sit by me..." / "from Galinda".
   - Top End Wedding: "Ten days, a wedding to plan and Mum's gone missing..." / "from Lauren".
   - Clerks blends the real "I assure you, we're open" into one of these invented notes.
6. **Quotes and facts that are wrong.**
   - Wolf Creek prints "Where you from, mate?" in quote marks as Mick Taylor's line. It is not in the transcript. The film's most famous line, "Head on a stick", is missing.
   - The Lego Movie: "Overpriced $37 hot chocolate ... Larry the Barista charges Emmet $37". In the film it is overpriced coffee ("Drink overpriced coffee. Here you go. That's $37."), and the card then prices the $37 drink at "5 studs".
   - Several real lines are printed loose and garbled instead of in quote marks:
     - Muriel's Wedding: "alternating, you're terrible Muriel."
     - Wolf Creek: "That's not a knife, this is." The film line is "That's not a knife. This is a knife."
     - Braveheart: "They'll never take our freedom!"
     - Tombstone: "Skin that smokewagon and see what happens."
     - The Castle: "how's the serenity" tacked onto a party pie note.
   - Iconic moments missing:
     - Jurassic Park has "only the very best" but not "spared no expense".
     - Tangled has no frying pan.
     - Wolf Creek has no "head on a stick".
7. **The same touch is printed over and over on one card.**
   - Turner & Hooch names Hooch in seven dish notes and runs the car gag three times: "Don't eat the car, Hooch", "Serve the mains when Hooch eats the car" and "Clean car, until Hooch gets in".
   - Step Brothers prints "Catalina Wine Mixer" seven times, including "CATALINA WINE MIXER CANAPÉS" and "CATALINA WINE MIXER STRAWBERRY ROSÉ SPRITZ".
   - The Lost World: Jurassic Park prints "San Diego waterfront" five times, down to "SAN DIEGO WATERFRONT CARNE ASADA".
   - Sinners prints "juke joint" six times.
   - Grease prints "Frosty Palace" on the chilli cheese fries, the burger, the sundae, the special and the cue, which ends up as just "Serve the burgers at the Frosty Palace."
   - Up has the grape soda cap three times: "A grape soda cap pinned on as a badge", "Grape soda cap jellies" and "Ellie's grape soda floats".
8. **Price order slips.**
   - Crocodile Dundee: "Steak sandwich with the lot $8.95" is dearer than "Walkabout Creek barbecued barra $6.25".
   - Flashdance: "Steel town fries-inside steak sandwich $9.75" is dearer than "Lobster dinner date tails $8.25".
   - North by Northwest: "Mount Rushmore hot roast beef sandwich" is dearer than "Dining car brook trout".
   - Psycho: "Marion's lunch-hour steak sandwich $2.25" is dearer than "Fairvale chicken and dumplings $1.75".
   - Wolf Creek: "Campfire damper $7.50" is dearer than "Outback roadhouse pies $6.50".
   - Goldfinger, 1964: a "Mint julep 5s 5d" costs nearly as much as the "Kentucky stud farm steak 6s".
9. **Food first, then the film, is not always kept.**
   - Jurassic Park: "Clever girl. Claw-cut chips, lime guacamole.", "Life finds a way. Chocolate egg centres." and "Hold on to your butts." as the whole note on the coffee.
   - Goldfinger crams two dishes into one note: "Sesame prawn triangles with sweet chilli, smoked salmon on rye".
   - Crocodile Dundee puts "Street cart hot dogs ... off the cart" and "New York baked cheesecake" on the Walkabout Creek counter meals list.
10. **Card and phone still drift on some hand-built films.**
    - Jurassic Park prints 12 items. The phone shows 5, and "Chilled prawn cocktail", "Grilled mahi-mahi", "Casado plate", "Junior explorer plate", "Fresh-squeezed lemonade" and "Hot Tarrazú coffee" never appear on the phone or in the Prep sheet.
    - Anchorman's phone sells "Ron's butterscotch pud" and "Well, I never!" where the card prints "ButterSCOTCH pudding" and "Glass Case of Emotion".
    - Grease's Prep sheet says only "Greased Lightnin' fried chicken basket, Rydell pink coconut ice" are left off, but "Tuna melt on white bread" and "Lime rickey" are also on the card and the phone.
11. **Australian English slips.**
    - Sixteen cards say "black tea" and then add milk. Gallipoli has "Strong black tea with condensed milk", Storm Boy has "Strong black tea with honey and milk" and Mrs. Doubtfire has "Black tea brewed in a warmed pot, milk". In Australia black tea means no milk.
    - Storm Boy spells the dish "Coorong garlic chilli pippis" and the note "Pipis".
    - Jurassic Park's shopping list has "avocadoes".
12. **Desktop text is small.** At 1280 wide the dish notes on dense cards (Weapons 14, Hereditary 03, Goldfinger 05) sit at about 11 px. You can read them, but you need "Click the menu to read it full size" to read them comfortably.

## What it would take to reach 9.5

1. Give every private home, flat, cottage and family table a home template. No prices, no "Prices subject to change", no licence, bill, board, tray charge or ration lines. Do it for all 150 or so cards, starting with Hereditary, The Babadook, Say Anything..., Insidious, The Conjuring, National Treasure, Silver Linings Playbook and Mrs. Doubtfire. Move The Dish off the lab template. Take the alcohol and the adult prices off Weapons.
2. Rebuild the 233 film pages from the same dish list the app prints. Never show "On the printed menu" for a dish that is not on the card. Use the app's time wording on the page ("4 hr 30 min", not "4.5 hours").
3. Make the Prep sheet header come from the steps. Cook time should be the sum of the oven, hob and fryer steps. Prep should be the sum of the hands-on steps, and should not appear when there are none. Fix the cheesecake, crème brûlée, vanilla slice, iced tea and chocolate milk cases. Leave chilling drinks out of "Longest on the night". Replace "START ... EARLIER, FOR WAITING" with a plain line such as "Start the cheesecake 5 hours before the film".
4. Turn shopping lists into what you buy. Lemons and limes, not wheels, wedges or juice. Whole eggs, not egg whites and eggs on separate lines. One butter line. No water. Buyable sizes (2 L milk, 1.25 L cola, a six-pack of ale). No prep words ("cut in small cubes", "mashed", "juiced to give 60ml"). Spell avocados correctly.
5. Reprint every invented line as "A note from <name>" (164 cards). Keep real lines only in quote marks and word for word: Muriel's Wedding "You're terrible, Muriel.", Wolf Creek "That's not a knife. This is a knife.", Braveheart, Tombstone, The Castle "How's the serenity?". Remove Wolf Creek's "Where you from, mate?" and add "Head on a stick". Change The Lego Movie drink to overpriced coffee at $37, or drop the price gag. Add "spared no expense" to Jurassic Park and the frying pan to Tangled.
6. Cap a film touch at once per card. Fix Turner & Hooch, Step Brothers, The Lost World, Sinners, Grease and Up first, then run the repeat check across all 1,000 cards (279 cards show a venue or name three or more times).
7. Enforce price order: sandwiches under hot plates, lobster over a steak sandwich, damper under a meat pie, a cocktail well under a steak. Recheck Crocodile Dundee, Flashdance, North by Northwest, Psycho, Wolf Creek and Goldfinger.
8. Keep food first in every note on the hand-built cards (Jurassic Park's chips, cookies and coffee). Make Jurassic Park's phone and Prep sheet carry every printed dish, or print only the cooked five. Match Anchorman's names between card and phone. Make the "Also on the menu" line list every printed dish left out.
9. Change "black tea ... milk" to "strong tea with milk" on the 16 cards, and fix "pippis" to "pipis".
10. Raise dish note text on the dense desktop layouts to at least 12 px so a 1280 wide screen reads without zooming.
