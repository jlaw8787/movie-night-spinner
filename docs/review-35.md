# Review 35: Reel Dinner

**Overall: 6.5 out of 10**

I sampled 160 films with `scripts/dump-cards.cjs` (printed card text, print list, phone menu and prep sheet for each): 18 hand-built menus (Jurassic Park, The Big Lebowski, Home Alone, Back to the Future, Ratatouille, Die Hard, The Godfather, Goodfellas, Pulp Fiction, Elf and others), 36 Australian films (Gallipoli, Wolf Creek, Storm Boy, The Castle, Muriel's Wedding, Red Dog, The Dish, both Crocodile Dundees, all five Mad Max films, Priscilla, Strictly Ballroom, Two Hands, Phar Lap, Australia, The Man from Snowy River, The Lighthorsemen, Beneath Hill 60, Danger Close, Kenny, Lion, Animal Kingdom, Ladies in Black, The Dressmaker, The Water Diviner, The Wog Boy, Young Einstein, Top End Wedding, The Sapphires, Babe, Happy Feet, The Babadook), 15 British films, 30 period films (1934 to 1969, eight westerns, Braveheart, both Gladiators), 17 kids' films, 16 releases from 2024 and 2025, and enough others to cover 41 of the shared layouts. I read every printed card in full, checked the print list against the card and the phone menu by script, parsed every prep sheet for header times against step times, and scanned for dashes and spelling. I took desktop shots of 28 cards at 1280 wide and measured the on-screen size of note text, phone shots of 8 films at 390 by 844, a prep sheet, and one film page. The craft is real and many cards are genuinely good. But the same handful of faults turns up on most cards I opened: era money that is plainly wrong, real lines tacked onto dish notes without quote marks, one film touch repeated until it is noise, shared-layout wording that does not suit the venue, and dish notes printed too small to read on desktop.

## What is strong

- Venues are mostly real places from the film's world, with good detail. The Castle sits at "THE CASTLE, COOLAROO". Priscilla stops at "PALACE HOTEL, BROKEN HILL" with "murals on every wall. Est. 1889." Unforgiven is "Greely's Beer Garden" and signs off with "Talk of the town: Ned Logan's coffin outside the saloon." Notting Hill is "The Travel Book Co." and Roman Holiday is "Caffe Rocca" on "Piazza della Rotonda".
- The best touches show someone watched the film. Lion: "Warm saffron and cardamom milk. Bought with coal off the trains." Love Actually: "Gift Tag / To Mia, from Harry / Wrapped by Rufus, with care". Phar Lap: "Bobby won't eat without Tommy." The Lighthorsemen: "The charge, and the Turks firing too high". Storm Boy: "For Mr Proud, Ponder and Percival." The Dish: "The ambassador's glass while the band plays Hawaii Five-0."
- Most signed quotes are real and in quote marks: "I'm back, you bastard." (The Dressmaker), "That's not a knife. That's a knife." (Crocodile Dundee), "Deserve's got nothing to do with it." (Unforgiven), "Have you ever fired two guns whilst jumping through the air?" (Hot Fuzz), "A life lived in fear is a life half-lived." (Strictly Ballroom).
- The hand-built cards are the high point. The Big Lebowski at "5227 Santa Monica Blvd" puts quotes in quote marks under every dish ("Careful, man, there's a beverage here!") and keeps the description first. Jurassic Park prices the lamb at "$19.93" and Home Alone's ticket reads "1 LG CHEESE $11.80 / Paid $12."
- The shared layouts that suit their venue really work: Goldfinger's scorecard ("Hole / Dish / Yds / Par", prices in "3s 7d", "19th Hole"), the Two Hands bar tab whose "Running tab $60.75" actually adds up, and the American Werewolf tab ("Brought forward £9.20", "Running tab £12.65").
- I found no em or en dashes anywhere on 160 cards, phone menus, prep sheets or film pages. Pre-decimal money is handled properly where it is used ("2/11", "1s 5d", "6 piastres", "L. 350", "S/ 72.00").
- The prep sheet is well made (shot 15): shopping by aisle, timed steps, make-ahead notes, and a sensible "Tip: Make lemon, lime and lemonade ones for the kids and the driver." The card, phone menu and print list agree in 158 of 160 films.

## What holds it back

1. **Era money is wrong on many modern and mid-century cards.** Red Dog (2011): "Mining camp curried egg sandwiches $1.75", "Pilbara grilled barra with lime slaw $3.95", "Red Dog ruby grapefruit radler $0.75". Those are 1975 prices on a 2011 pub (shot 03). The Dish (1969): "Parkes country scones $0.50", "Mayor's roast lamb cutlets and mint sauce $2.50", several times too dear for a 1969 staff mess (shot 11). Grease (1958 setting): "Frosty Palace burger $1.50" against "Greased Lightnin' fried chicken basket $1.00" (shot 02). It's a Wonderful Life (1946): "Coconut macaroons 70¢", "Gower's chocolate sundae 80¢". The Sapphires (1968): "Army mess roast $2.25". Croc Dundee: barra "$6.25" under a steak sandwich at "$8.95". Order is off too. Roman Holiday puts "Champagne, for lunch L. 100" at the same price as "Fried zucchini flowers". Psycho mixes "50¢" with "1.75" and "2.25" on one card. Singin' in the Rain writes "60c" where every other US card uses "¢".
2. **Prices where nobody pays.** Army camps, homes and post-apocalypse sites carry price lists: Gallipoli's "Mena Camp Mess" ("Bully beef and onion fritters 7d"), The Lighthorsemen's "Beersheba Approach Camp", Beneath Hill 60, Danger Close's "Nui Dat Mess Tent" ("D Company field kitchen beef and beans $1.75"), the Harrison and Faraway Downs station kitchens, Bridget Jones's family turkey curry buffet, Mad Max 2, Fury Road ("The whole feast, per cover $89.50") and Furiosa ("War Rig barbecue lamb $21.00").
3. **The layout's own wording contradicts the film.** Mad Max Beyond Thunderdome prints "BARTERTOWN, TRADE OR LEAVE" and then "CASH ONLY. NO CHANGE GIVEN." (shot 04). Priscilla's outback pub gets the American diner layout with "Booth service", "Refills on the coffee", "Blue Plates" and "The Fountain" (shot 06). Love Actually's arrivals hall cafe prints mains as "Included" and "A note from Prime Minister" (missing "the"). Paddington in Peru puts "In your suite", "Wake-up call" and "A tray charge applies to every order" on the Home for Retired Bears. The Sapphires' Vietnam soldiers' club has "Two drink minimum at the stage tables. Cover charge after nine." Kenny's portaloo depot is a food truck ("Made in the truck today."). It's a Wonderful Life runs a "LUNCH COUNTER MENU" at the Bailey Building and Loan. Roman Holiday's Roman cafe heads its mains "Plats du jour". Australia and Snowy River stations carry Wild West "Wanted at the head table / Reward £50" posters.
4. **Real lines are tacked onto dish notes without quote marks**, which breaks the rule and makes notes read oddly. Muriel's Wedding: "Chicken in mushroom cream or eye fillet with red wine jus, alternating, you're terrible Muriel." (shot 05). The Castle: "Carrot and thyme mince in flaky puff, little beef pastries and tomato sauce, how's the serenity." (shot 01). Fury Road: "dusted with edible silver lustre, witness me." and "charred damper, what a lovely day." Gladiator: "honey chilli oil, are you not entertained." Grease: "Rizzo's pick. Look at me, I'm Sandra Dee." Babe: "under golden puff, that'll do." and "That'll do, pig." on the same card. Wizard of Oz: "Pay no attention to that man behind the curtain.", "There's no place like home." and "Toto, I've a feeling we're not in Kansas anymore." Lock, Stock: "It's been emotional." Jurassic Park: "Hold on to your butts." Home Alone: "Keep the change, you filthy animal." Wolf Creek misquotes its line as well: "That's not a knife, this is."
5. **Quotes in quote marks that are not the film's words.** Breakfast at Tiffany's: "I'm like Cat, here. We're a couple of no-name slobs." The real line is "I'm like Cat here, a no-name slob." The Castle's house line rewrites the film as "It's the serenity." Crocodile Dundee II's "I am home." (Sue Charlton) and Red Dog's "All the sad faces in the world ain't gonna work, so you can just stop." (John Grant) are not lines I can place in those films.
6. **One film touch repeated until it is noise.** Counted on the printed card: "Frosty Palace" 11 times on Grease, "Mick" 14 times on Crocodile Dundee II, "Beersheba" 9 on The Lighthorsemen, "Parkes" 8 on The Dish ("Parkes country scones", "Parkes show hot jam doughnuts", "for when Parkes gets the signal", "Parkes pub lemon lime shandy"), "Istanbul" 8 on The Water Diviner, "Slaughtered Lamb" 8 on American Werewolf, "marmalade" 8 on Paddington 2, "pink" 10 on Legally Blonde, "Rick's" about 8 on Casablanca. Croc II says "back in the bush" in the header, the pancakes and the rum. Wolf Creek says last servo, last fuel stop and petrol station. Hot Fuzz names cranberry juice in the drink name, its note and the cue. Roman Holiday's champagne order turns up in the header, the crostini note and the champagne note. Animal Kingdom's "where you fit" appears four times.
7. **Some notes never say what the dish is.** Young Einstein: "Leatherwood honey and cheddar board / Eaten in the shed where he split the beer atom." Casablanca: "Lamb and apricot tagine with couscous / Of all the gin joints, she ordered the lamb." Django Unchained: "Candyland white cake / We're havin' white cake. Two layers, buttercream". Jurassic Park: "Hot Tarrazú coffee / Hold on to your butts." Paddington in Peru: "Choclo with queso fresco and ají sauce / Andean corn. I appear to be driving the boat." Top End Wedding's "Lemon myrtle roast lamb" note never mentions lemon myrtle. Notes also flip between ending with a full stop and not (Snowy River: "Earn your right to the mountain first", "No wallaby", "Clancy leads the muster"). The Water Diviner prints a lowercase sentence start: "Red lentil soup, flatbread. bribe a fisherman to cross." (shot 14).
8. **Period food slips.** Gladiator and Gladiator II put tomato and chilli in ancient Rome ("Pork snags, lentils, tomato", "honey chilli oil", "harissa yoghurt"). Phar Lap's 1930 pub serves "Toasted baguette with chutney" as crostini and "rocket" and "Tequila, pink grapefruit and lime". Miracle on 34th Street (1947) braises the pot roast "in stock and balsamic". Some Like It Hot (1929) has "crispy pancetta" and "Dijon mayo". Croc II (1988) pours a "lemon myrtle gin and tonic" and serves steak "on Turkish" with corn chips. The Dressmaker (1951) has "wattleseed cream" and "bush tomato chutney". Breakfast at Tiffany's (1961) has panko. The Birds (1963) has "goat cheese filo cups".
9. **Facts that do not match.** Four Weddings sets the card at "Carrie and Hamish's Wedding, Glenthrist Castle" but cues "the first wedding reception" and tells Charles "Best man. Please try not to be late." That was the first wedding, not Carrie's (shot 10). Danger Close's timeline has "0900 Guns called in danger close" and "1500 Iroquois drop ammo in the rain". The battle and the ammo drop were in the late afternoon and evening (shot 07). Mary Poppins puts "tuppence a bag on the steps of St Paul's" on cheese straws, but that was bird feed. Muriel's groom is spelt "Van Arckle" (the film has Van Arkle).
10. **Desktop dish notes are too small to read on many cards.** I measured the rendered note text in the 1280 preview. It was 7.0px on Mad Max Beyond Thunderdome (shot 04), 7.3px on Goldfinger and The Big Lebowski, 7.9px on Four Weddings and Snatch, 8.4px on Jurassic Park (shot 09), 9.3px on Red Dog, Phar Lap and Paddington 2, and 5.7px for a caption on Home Alone. Casablanca and North by Northwest render the same kind of text at 17 to 19px. Two cards side by side differ by a factor of 2.5. Faces also differ within a card: on The Castle the left recipe card's notes are visibly larger than the right card's (shots 01 and 12).
11. **Mixed spelling and units.** Chips and fries both appear on one card: Grease ("chilli cheese fries", "crinkle-cut chips"), Kenny ("Splashdown festival hot chips / Twice-fried fries"), Paddington in Peru ("Bake the frozen fries... Toss through the chips"), Caddyshack's film page ("shoestring chips" and "shoestring fries"). Casablanca writes "Rick's Cafe Americain" and "Café Américain mint tea" on the same card. Knives Out says "Donut holes" and Kingsman "mustard ketchup sauce". Temperatures appear as "23 C" and "-30 C" (Storm Boy, Happy Feet) but "200°C" in recipes. Volumes appear as "1 1/2l" and "375 ml" on The Castle page and as "1.5 L" on its prep sheet.
12. **The prep sheet does not cook the card's mains.** On 111 of the 141 shared-layout cards in my sample, the card prints two mains but the default five cooks only one. The other is marked "Not on tonight's cook" (The Castle's "Kerrigan backyard barbie", Muriel's "Van Arckle wedding alternate drop", Croc Dundee's "Steak sandwich with the lot"). Every hand-built card I checked was covered.
13. **Prep times and shopping lines.** In 59 of about 800 dish blocks the header does not match the steps, usually because of a stray "Prep 5 min" (Ladies in Black: "Prep 5 min, cook 1 hr" over steps of 7, 45 and 8 minutes). One step contradicts itself: "simmer 30 minutes. 45 min". Mad Max's onions show "leave a week. 156 hr". The shopping list has 784 spoon-measure lines across the sample, which are not things you buy: "1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce", "4 tbsp mayonnaise", "2 tbsp tomato sauce", "4 cups crushed ice" (The Castle, shot 15). The film page for the same dish says "1 bottle Worcestershire sauce".
14. **Phone details.** At 390 wide nothing overflows sideways, but the floating "Skip to the wheel" button sits over the first dish name ("Ayshe's hotel meze with smoky eggplant", shot 14). The Water Diviner's venue line is set in all-caps script that is hard to read. On the printed card at phone size, Goldfinger's italic notes are about 5px and need the zoom (shot 13).
15. **Two print-list mismatches.** Miracle on 34th Street prints "Kris Kringle's maple mulled spiced apple juice" but the print list says "Kris Kringle's maple mulled cider". Unforgiven prints "Saloon bread pudding, whiskey sauce" but the list says "Big Whiskey bread pudding, whiskey sauce".

## What it would take to reach 9.5

1. Set a price band for each era and place (for example 1950s US diner, 1969 Australian canteen, 2011 regional pub) and check every card against it. Starters must cost less than mains, and drinks must make sense against food. Red Dog, The Dish, The Sapphires, Grease, It's a Wonderful Life and Croc Dundee should be fixed first.
2. Remove prices from venues where nobody pays (army camps, stations, family homes, the Wasteland, the Citadel) or replace them with a ration or chit line, as Danger Close half does already.
3. Give each shared layout a list of lines that must not appear for certain venue types, and fix the known clashes: Bartertown "Cash only", diner lines at the Palace Hotel, "Included" and "A note from Prime Minister" on Love Actually, room service at the Home for Retired Bears, cover charges at a soldiers' club, the food truck for Kenny, the lunch counter at the Building and Loan, and "Plats du jour" in Rome.
4. Run one pass over every dish note. Any line the film actually says goes in quote marks, either moved to the signed quote spot or written as a voice note that does not copy the line. No real line should be the last clause of a dish description. Fix the misquotes: Breakfast at Tiffany's, "It's the serenity", "That's not a knife, this is."
5. Cap any one film word (venue, place or character name) at about three appearances on a printed card, counting the header, names, notes, cue, stamps and footer. Rename where it runs over: "Frosty Palace" on Grease, "Parkes" on The Dish, "Mick" on Croc II, "Beersheba", "Istanbul", "Slaughtered Lamb", "Rick's".
6. Check that every note opens with what the dish is. Fix Young Einstein, Casablanca, Django, Jurassic Park coffee, Paddington in Peru and Top End Wedding, give every note the same ending punctuation, and capitalise "bribe".
7. Run a period-food check on pre-1960 and ancient settings: no tomato or chilli in Rome; no balsamic, pancetta, panko, rocket, crostini, wattleseed, bush tomato, lemon myrtle gin, Turkish bread or corn chips before they were common.
8. Fix the factual slips: the Four Weddings venue against its cue and best man line, the Danger Close timeline, the Mary Poppins tuppence, and the Van Arkle spelling.
9. Set a floor for note text in the desktop preview of about 11px rendered. Shrink photos or reflow before shrinking type, and make both faces of a card use the same note size.
10. Pick one word for chips and fries per card (chips for Australian and British settings), one way to write cents, degrees with the ° sign everywhere, and one style for volumes ("1.5 L", "375ml").
11. Make the default five cook every main printed on the card, or print only the mains it cooks.
12. Remove the phantom "Prep 5 min" so headers equal step totals. Show long waits in days. Round the shopping list up to things you buy ("1 bottle Worcestershire sauce", "1 jar mayonnaise", "1 bag ice").
13. Move "Skip to the wheel" so it does not cover the first dish on phones, use plain capitals for script venue lines, and align the two print-list names on Miracle on 34th Street and Unforgiven.
