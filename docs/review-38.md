# Review 38: Reel Dinner

**Overall: 6.3 out of 10**

I read 181 printed cards in full from the dump, with the phone menu and prep sheet for each: all 95 hand-built one-off layouts, 20 Australian or Australian-set films, 30 kids and family films (Disney, Pixar, DreamWorks, Ghibli, Paddington and the like) and about 120 picked at random across the shared layouts from 1940 to 2025. On top of that I ran scripts over all 1,000 cards for dashes, alcohol words on kids cards, price order, missing prices, broken punctuation, shopping list duplicates and timings, and compared all 1,000 film pages with their cards. Every fault quoted below was checked against the live page. I took 16 screenshots: desktop at 1280 (01 to 06, 15, 16), phone at 390 (07 to 10), film pages (11 at 1280, 12 at 390) and prep sheets (13, 14).

## What is strong

- The 95 hand-built menus are the best thing in the app. The Godfather (02) has "THE BEST Veal IN THE CITY" at Louis', a bomboniera for Connie and Carlo, Nazorine's cannoli box and "Luca sleeps with the fishes." under house customs. Home Alone 2 prints the real "$967.43" room service total. Casino's "Same number of blueberries in every muffin." and Once Upon a Time in America's "Repeal is coming; we'll give it a funeral." are proper film-buff touches.
- Most showtime cues land on a real, findable scene: Beetlejuice "when the dinner party breaks into the Banana Boat song", Léon "Pour a glass of milk every time Léon does", Raging Bull "when Jake starts complaining his steak is overcooked", Lion "when Saroo sees the jalebi at the party in Melbourne".
- The Australian cards mostly sound like home: The Castle's "Pool room prawns" and "Chilled moselle from the cask", Mad Max's "chicken salt", Phar Lap's "Bar shuts at six", The Dish's sherry "while the band plays Hawaii Five-0".
- Kids and family cards are clean of real drinks. Every kids-theme drink is soft, and recipes that would use wine (coq au vin in The Aristocats) use grape juice.
- Only one dash fault turned up in 1,000 cards, and spelling is Australian throughout (capsicum, coriander, tomato sauce, icing sugar, tyre).
- The phone menu at 390 is tidy and readable (07, 09): big tabs by course, a clear note, a "How to make it" fold and the cue at the bottom.
- Guest checks on the hand-built cards add up: Moonstruck 30.75, After Hours 9.15, Ghostbusters $41.50, Serpico 1.50.

## What holds it back

1. **Dish notes often end on a bolted-on plot fact or quote instead of a food touch.** This happens across hundreds of shared-layout cards. Friday: "Grill-charred. Bye, Felisha." and "Drumsticks, corn, potato salad. Deebo, keep walking." Bolt: "Chicken bowl. No green eyed man." and "Shortbread bolts. The power of Styrofoam." The Man from Snowy River (10): "Currant pudding, custard. Curly lets the colt loose." and "Billy tea, milk and sugar. Clancy leads the muster." Meet the Parents: "Extra milk. You can milk just about anything with nipples." on an iced latte. Goodfellas: "Rump in butter and garlic, rare or medium. Funny how?"
2. **Notes repeat the dish name.** She's All That (05): "Beach day fish tacos" with the note "Grilled fish tacos for the beach day Laney says yes to. Grilled fish tacos." and "Breakfast muffins ready for Simon. Get up, Simon. Warm blueberry muffins." Shaun of the Dead: "A cold pint of lager, pulled at the bar." Peter Rabbit 2: "Chicken, leek and bacon. Maybe he really is a bad seed." Grandma's Boy: "Cheddar and chive cheese ball. Dante can't put Alex up, he's getting a lion." Names also repeat across a card: Raya has "Boun's Prawn Boat", "Boun's prawn boat bites" and "Boun's prawn congee". Office Christmas Party has Mary three times. Aladdin (1992) has Agrabah in three dish names.
3. **Broken text on the printed card and the phone.** Us (09): "Artichoke hearts fried in a light batter with lemon aioli. Red's answer:." Alien: Resurrection: "Lemon vodka over ice. Ask Ripley what she did:." Hook (16): "lime and honey drizzle..", "cut into triangles..", "piped on top..". The Untouchables: "the night Ness finally drinks.. Here endeth the lesson". Toy Story 4: "Ducky says there's so much... Fluff." 29 notes have no full stop, for example Bad Santa "baked slow and cut in slices" and Office Christmas Party "with cinnamon and nutmeg".
4. **People pay at homes, studies, banquets and private events.** Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade (04): "Henry Jones's Study", 25¢ to 85¢, "Bill No. 1938, 1989". One Hundred and One Dalmatians (15): "Pongo's London Flat" with "Prices subject to change. Service included." Fantasia: "The Sorcerer's Study" in shillings. The Great Mouse Detective: "Basil's Baker Street Digs" at "1s 2d". Also Big Daddy "Sonny's Apartment", Easy A "Olive's Webcam Room", Blade "Whistler's Safehouse", Pineapple Express "Saul's Place", Storm Boy (03) "Coorong Shack". Private and invited events charge too: Hook's "The Neverland Feast" at 2.50, Frequency's "The firehouse invites the whole block" at 15¢ a shandy, Citizen Kane's "Inquirer Welcome Dinner", Analyze This's "A private dinner of the families" with "Jelly's rye 6 · House red 7", and the senior proms in She's All That (05) and There's Something About Mary.
5. **Prices out of place.** You've Got Mail (06): "Turkey and brie melt 28.75" and "Toasted mini bagels 9.75" at a 1998 bookshop cafe. American Graffiti: "The double Chubby Chuck $2.50" in 1962, with most other dishes unpriced. Spider-Man: Homecoming school cafeteria mains at $9.90 and $11.50. Ant-Man and the Wasp: "San Francisco custard egg tarts" at $13.25. Ghostbusters II: "Liberty hot dogs 18.86" above the goulash at 16.00. Two Hands puts "Bondi fish and chips" ($6.50) and "Kings Cross pie floaters" ($7.50) under starters. Quick Change's guest check charges "2 dynamite wings 11.90" when the same card prices them at 4.25. The year-as-price gag shows up too often to stay funny: Splash "19.84", Trading Places "19.83", Tootsie "19.82", Working Girl "19.88 a head", Bugsy "1.46". 49 cards price some dishes and leave others blank.
6. **Shared templates print words that do not fit the venue.** Finding Nemo (08) is set in the "Dentist's Fish Tank" but prints "BEACH KIOSK", "Thanks for keeping the beach clean. Kiosk licence 42." and a surf report. Storm Boy (03) prints "Kiosk licence 1976" and "Swell 3 ft" at Hide-Away Tom's shack. Muriel's Wedding (01) has a cafe loyalty card titled "Wedding Invitation" that says "Muriel and David. Fill the card, win a cup. Easy." Grandma's Boy has "Tab's chalked on the wall" and "Behind the bar" at "Grandma Lilly's House". Hercules gets "SEC 101 Lower Bowl" and "Club Level" at Phil's training camp. The Breakfast Club gets "Access Lv 84 / Badge" in a school library. Geppetto's Workshop and the Notre Dame bell tower carry "Coin of the realm only. Bill No.". Office Christmas Party prints "Cover at the door, no dress code".
7. **Food out of period or place.** Jaws: "Quint's chilli lime lager michelada" on the Orca in 1975. The Big Lebowski: whipped feta, pomegranate and nigella flatbread at a 1991 bowling alley. One Hundred and One Dalmatians (15): "smoked salt and crumbled nori" in 1961 London. Princess and the Frog: "bananas foster" in 1926 (the dish dates from 1951). Rosemary's Baby: turmeric "Herbal tonic shots" in 1968. Gallipoli: hummus, dukkah and "sumac onions" from a 1915 camp dixie. Muriel's Wedding: "chilli lime salt" and a coconut yoghurt dip in 1994. Two Hands: pie floaters belong to Adelaide, not Kings Cross. Brave: "neeps and tatties" described as "mashed swede and parsnip", when tatties are potatoes.
8. **Card, phone and prep disagree on cues.** Goodfellas prints "Showtime: Plate up the mains when young Henry starts running errands at the cab stand." but the phone and prep cue is "when Paulie starts slicing garlic in the prison kitchen". Ghostbusters prints "Serve the meatball subs when the team settles in at the firehouse." but the cue is "Crack the drinks when Ecto-1 rolls out". The Untouchables telegram and cue name two different meetings.
9. **Prep timings and steps.** Donnie Brasco (14): "Cook the rice. 5 min". The Muppet Christmas Carol braises red cabbage in "15 min". Carlito's Way: "Marinate overnight in the fridge. 15 min". Wonder Woman: "Strain and chill overnight, then churn. 25 min". Dirty Dancing (13) times "Keep ice cold until serving. 25 min" and "Chill the dip. 30 min" as method steps, which inflates "hands-on 1 hr 20 min".
10. **Shopping list doubles up.** 90 lists have the same thing twice. Dirty Dancing (13): "1kg watermelon" and "1kg watermelon flesh", "1 orange" under Produce and "7 oranges" under Drinks, "1 bunch mint" plus a mint line in the recipe. The same orange split appears in Annie, Sixteen Candles, Animal House, School of Rock and others. Other examples: Top Gun "250ml cold lager" and "4 bottles of lager", The Fall Guy "1 can lager" and "250ml lager", 68 lists with two or three salt lines (The Warriors: "salt / 1 tsp coarse salt / 1 pinch smoked salt"). Method wording leaks into the list: My Cousin Vinny "salt and pepper, to taste (for the cornmeal)", Muriel's Wedding "1 punnet of fresh edible flowers, to decorate".
11. **Film pages do not match the card.** The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (12): the card serves "Prancing Pony hot buttered apple" but the page has "Prancing Pony hot buttered ale" with "4 bottles brown ale", and the page pie is braised in "375ml dark ale". The Two Towers and The Return of the King do the same ("mead punch", "ginger ale cup"). Ratatouille's page lists "A good Bordeaux" and Bad Santa's lists "Willie's bourbon and ginger highball", neither of which is on the card. Anchorman's page lists "Well, I never!" as a recipe. The Godfather page (11) leaves out the veal scaloppine, the dish the card is built around.
12. **Desktop text is small at 1280.** In the full-size view at FIT (01, 03, 05), dish notes print at roughly 7 to 10 px, so you need the + button to read the card. On Storm Boy (03) the "See you at sunset" note sits over the drinks column and pushes the 50c prices inward.
13. **Phone overlays.** On the phone the sticky "Skip to the wheel" button covers a "Goes well with" row, and the floating "Spin the wheel" button covers footer text.
14. **Smaller wording slips.** "a la king" (The Dressmaker) and "a la mode" (L.A. Confidential) are missing the accent. "gruyere" and "gruyère" are both used on The Aristocats card. Three Men and a Baby has "Diapers, harder.". "Grandma's biscuits :: Fluffy buttermilk biscuits" (Happy Gilmore) and "Meeko's biscuits" (Pocahontas) will read as sweet biscuits to an Australian. The Muppet Treasure Island kids card says "Rum free, Billy." Beetlejuice prints "B I O - E X O R C I S T" with a spaced hyphen.

## To reach 9.5

- Rewrite every shared-layout note as food first, then one short touch that links the dish to the film. Drop plot recaps and bare quotes, never repeat the dish name or a name already used on the card, and run a check that rejects "..", ":." and missing full stops.
- Give each venue type a no-prices flag (home, study, flat, digs, safehouse, invitation, banquet, feast, prom, school event) and strip "Prices subject to change", "Bill No.", "Service included" and "Coin of the realm" from those cards.
- Add venue variants to the kiosk, bartab, stadium, lab and tavern templates so a shack, a grandmother's house, a training camp or a school library does not get a surf report, a bar tab or a lower bowl.
- Set price bands by decade, place and venue level. Make mains dearer than starters and sweets, price every dish or none, retire the year-as-price joke on all but one or two cards, and make guest checks match the menu.
- Run a period pass: no micheladas, nigella, nori salt, turmeric shots, cake pops or espresso tonics before they existed. Swap pie floaters, mushy peas and Kings Cross clashes for food that fits the place.
- Use one cue per film and print the same words on the card, the phone and the prep sheet.
- Time method steps only for hands-on work, show overnight steps as "start the day before", and give braises and rice real times.
- Merge shopping lines by ingredient across aisles (oranges, watermelon, lager, cream, salt), keep one salt and one pepper line, and keep "to taste" and "to decorate" out of the list.
- Build film pages from the same dish list as the card, alcohol-free versions included, and fail the build when a page name is missing from its card.
- At 1280, open the full-size view at a zoom where notes are at least 13 px, and keep sticky notes off price columns. On the phone, hide "Skip to the wheel" once the user has scrolled past the wheel.
