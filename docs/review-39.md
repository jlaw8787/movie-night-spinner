# Review 39: Reel Dinner

**Overall: 6.5 out of 10**

I read about 185 cards in full from the dumps: every hand-built one-off I could find in films 500 to 999 (Lady and the Tramp, both Turtles one-offs, A Goofy Movie, There's Something About Mary, She's All That, Kingpin) plus about 30 of the hand-built ones in films 0 to 249 (the Godfather run, Casino, Mean Streets, The Untouchables, Analyze This and others), around 30 Australian and New Zealand films, around 35 kids and family films, and a random spread of shared layouts from 1937 to 2026, with their phone menus and prep sheets. The b dump was used as supplied. The a dump never appeared, so I ran scripts/dump-cards.cjs myself for films 0 to 499 (photos off, otherwise the same). I ran my own scripts over all 1,000 dumps for dashes, alcohol on family cards, price order and gaps, broken punctuation, plot tails, repeated dish names, missing cues and venue wording. I ran planShopping live for all 1,000 films and compared every built film page with what its card prints. Every fault quoted below was checked against the live card on a local server (fix/review-38), apart from the missing cues, which come from the full-card dump because the desktop half view only shows part of a card. Screenshots: docs/review-39-01.png to 04 (desktop full-size view at 1280), 05 to 07 (phone at 390), 08 to 09 (film pages), 10 to 11 (prep sheets).

## What is strong

- The best hand-built cards are excellent and feel like real venue menus. This Is Spinal Tap: "None more black pastrami on dark rye", "One louder rum and Coke: Dark rum and Coca-Cola over 11 ice cubes", "Stonehenge brownie trilithons". Casino's Tangiers coffee shop with "Same number of blueberries in every muffin. No exceptions." The Godfather's Louis' with "We cook with Genco Pura olive oil" and "Leave the gun. Take the cannoli." Donnie Brasco's Sakura Gardens with "PLEASE REMOVE YOUR SHOES".
- Period money is done properly in many places: Dr. No prices in shillings and pence and the bar tab adds up (12s 6d carried, 18s 6d running); Phar Lap's Randwick Hotel has "Bar shuts at six"; Luca prices in lire; Turtles III in mon ("Prices in mon. Coins on a string are fine.").
- Bar tab arithmetic is right on every card I checked by script (28 subtotals, none wrong).
- Australian cards are true to place: The Castle ("Bonnie Doon cheese and kabana", "Chilled moselle from the cask"), The Dish (lamingtons, cheese scrolls, 1969 prices in decimal cents), Storm Boy (smoked Coorong mullet, pipis, chicken salt), The Dressmaker ("I'm back, you bastard.", party pies at the footy final), Crocodile Dundee's Walkabout Creek counter meals.
- Good food-first Easter eggs: Oppenheimer's martini "honey and lime on the rim", Silence of the Lambs "Chicken liver pâté and smashed broad beans", Catch Me If You Can "Bucket of cream pots de creme", Everything Everywhere "eaten with your feet", Father of the Bride's "Late-night basketball malted milkshake".
- Family cards are alcohol-free on the card, phone and prep across all kids, Christmas, wizard and fairytale films (my script found nothing real; Return of the King prints "Pale ginger beer").
- No em or en dashes anywhere in 1,000 cards, phone menus or prep sheets. Australian words hold up: capsicum, coriander, tomato sauce, wombok, rockmelon, esky, patty pans.
- Grandma's Boy shows the venue rules can work: the bar tab layout at Grandma Lilly's House prints "Help yourself" and "From the fridge", no prices, no tab.
- The phone menu at 390 is tidy: no floating bars, no sideways scroll, readable notes (05 to 07).

## What holds it back

1. **Film pages do not match the cards, and 26 family pages print alcohol.** All 1,057 "house" dishes printed on 622 cards are missing from their film pages (Inspector Gadget's card prints "Loaded potato skins with bacon" and "Vanilla cola", the page has neither). Of 5,909 dish notes that are on the pages, 879 (on 212 pages) are not what the card prints. Worse, 26 kids, Christmas and wizard pages describe alcohol the card has removed, sometimes under a "No alcohol" tag: Last Christmas "Covent Garden mulled grape juice [No alcohol] Red wine simmered with orange, cinnamon, star anise and clove" (screenshot 08), The Desolation of Smaug "Cold milk shaken with bourbon and honey syrup", Office Christmas Party eggnog "with dark rum, brandy and nutmeg", Violent Night "Cold stout with a whisky shot", Deathly Hallows Part 2 "stewed with onions, carrots and stout", The Sword in the Stone "Beef chuck in brown ale". The pages look like they were built from older data.

2. **Notes still bolt plot or quotes onto the food.** Of 6,969 dish notes on shared-layout cards, 462 end on a sentence with no food in it. In my samples about two in three are plain plot recap with no link to the dish: Inspector Gadget "Slow-cooked chipotle beef and bean chilli. Kramer built Scolex the claw.", Jurassic World Dominion "Almond milk sweetened with honey and cinnamon. Maisie named the baby Beta.", Captain America: Civil War "Apple juice and sparkling water. Peter rates Bucky's metal arm.", The Wolf of Wall Street "Aunt Emma holds the Swiss money." Some notes have no food at all: Dr. No "Escovitch fish with fried bammy: Underneath the mango tree, Honey's song.", The Fall Guy "Stunt crew party pies: A pie between takes on set.", Dune: Part Two "Sietch flatbread with date butter: Bread in the deep desert sietch.", The Lighthorsemen "Bully beef from the ration tin.", Boy "Fish shop hot chips with tomato sauce: The fish and chip shop down the road from Waihau Bay.", Running Scared "Lime pie: Pie first, then the showdown in the State of Illinois Center atrium." Straight Outta Compton is the worst whole card: "Cube tells Dre he's their bread and butter.", "Chilli, cheddar. We N.W.A.", "Skirt steak, salsa. Eazy raps Boyz-n-the-Hood." 24 notes end on a bare quote with "!" or "?" (Alice in Wonderland "Off with her head!", Starship Troopers "Would you like to know more?") and 24 run to three sentences (The Suicide Squad "Date night. Killing kids? Kind of a red flag."). The hand-built one-offs often put the plot line before the food: She's All That "Laney in the falafel hat. Falafel in pita with tahini.", and several drop full stops altogether (The Untouchables "Fried in the hotel kitchen, tomato sauce on the side", also Big Night, My Cousin Vinny, Back to the Future, Goodfellas).

3. **Notes repeat the dish name or contradict it.** 413 notes restate the dish name (348 with three or more of the same words in a row, 65 that only reword it): Wallace & Gromit "Cheese, potato and onion pasties: Cheese, potato and onion pasties, baked hot.", Storm Boy "Lemon cordial in a tin mug: Lemon cordial poured in a tin mug.", Walk the Line "Black coffee, like the suit: Strong black coffee, like the suit.", Aladdin (2019) "Sumac and sesame flatbreads with hummus and labneh: Charred sumac and sesame flatbread with hummus and labneh." Some say the opposite of the name: The Great Escape "Dirt-in-plain-sight lentil dip: Black bean dip with garlic toasts", Black Widow "Red Guardian's black tea with cherry jam: Strong black tea stirred with strawberry jam", Running Scared "Conch fritters: Chopped prawn in a capsicum, onion and celery batter". One note is cut short: Dreamgirls "with yellow mustard and chopped." Narnia serves the same dish twice ("Mr Tumnus's tea and toast: Buttered toast fingers with mashed sardine" and "Sardines on toast").

4. **Template wording does not suit the venue.** Family homes print as delivery takeaways: The Big Sick's "Nanjiani Family Kitchen" says "We deliver / Phone your order 555 0171 / Free delivery over $25" (screenshot 05), and the same for Big Daddy (Sonny's Apartment), Blue Beetle (Reyes Family Kitchen), The Heat (Mullins Family Kitchen) and Turning Red (Lee Family Temple, with $20.50 mains). Dogma's Mooby's fast food is a formal "Bill of Fare / Second Course · The Remove / Entremets" with "The whole feast, per cover $47.50" (screenshot 03). National Treasure's Gates Family House says "Return tray to the hatch. Snacks subject to supply." Jay and Silent Bob's Quick Stop says "Made in the truck today." Black Sheep's campaign truck is a "Dining Car / TRAIN 96". Hocus Pocus 2's birthday in the woods is headed "FORMAL, CLASS OF 2022". About Time's family beach house is "BEACH SNACKS". 48 Hrs.' Torchy's bar and Alien 3's mess hall use the police "Case file" layout. Lady and the Tramp prints "No grape juice, all fizz" over the 1910 drinks (screenshot 04).

5. **Showtime cue missing or broken on the card.** 86 cards print no showtime cue while the phone menu and prep sheet do: 73 hand-built one-offs (The Godfather, Casino, Scarface, Analyze This, A Bronx Tale, Lady and the Tramp, A Goofy Movie and more), 10 bar tabs (Dr. No, Les Misérables, Friday the 13th Part 2, A Star Is Born, Onward, Hall Pass) and 3 others. Les Misérables' cue is also set at the Bishop of Digne's table while the card is Thénardier's Inn. Twins prints a broken cue on all three: "Bring out the burgers when Griddled until done."

6. **Prices follow the layout, not the venue.** The cafe layout gives the same $18 and $22 mains to Toy Story 4's antique shop, Venom 2's corner store ("Bacon and scrambled eggs on toast $18.00"), Zootopia and Despicable Me 2. The bar tab gives near-identical prices to a 1990 Philly corner bar (Rocky V), the Titty Twister in 1996 Mexico and Se7en. Places where nobody pays still print prices: Glass Onion's private island ("Puzzle box lamb souvlaki $24.75"), Godfather Part II's presidential New Year's party ("EVERYBODY GETS ONE", then "Prawn cocktail 1.25"), Analyze This's private dinner of the families ("House red 7", "Espresso with sambuca, 4.00"). Catch Me If You Can's Pan Am first class prices some dishes and leaves the filet mignon and pots de creme blank; Mad Max prices everything but the two drinks. Odd orders: The Last Starfighter "Bubble gum from the counter jar $2.00" costs more than the hot dog ($1.75); Hop sells rocky road as a $8.75 starter; Pokémon Detective Pikachu charges $3.50 for iced water.

7. **Food out of its time and place.** 2010s cafe trends turn up in old films: chipotle mayo in a 1981 Crystal Lake bar and a 1984 trailer park store; "hot honey" fried chicken at a 1947 South Carolina plantation (The Notebook) and on 1980 wings (Anchorman 2, also "sriracha" and "Panko"); "Thin cold-fermented base ... crisp cupped pepperoni and chilli honey" in Last Action Hero (1993); "whipped ricotta" in The Money Pit (1986); salted caramel at 1968 Villain-Con (Minions); butterfly pea lemonade and a skillet cookie in Beethoven (1992); poke in Point Break (1991). Place slips too: a whole roast snapper in Lake-town (The Desolation of Smaug), buttermilk scones and a "Jamestown beef and onion pie" at the Powhatan feast, ice cream sandwiches and potato moonshine at Stalag Luft III, Manhattans in Tango & Cash's prison mess, Thor: The Dark World's "Asgard Banquet Hall" serving only London pub food ("Thor took the Tube here."). Weak or plain items are still listed as dishes: "Bubble gum from the counter jar", "A jug of cold milk" (Beethoven), "Real-world tap water" (Ready Player One), "Lollipop Guild lollipops" (The Wizard of Oz), "Der Klown fennel and apple salad" as a main (Krampus).

8. **Shopping lists still buy things twice and carry method words** (checked live with planShopping on all 1,000 films). Butter and soft butter as two lines on 26 lists (Die Hard, Hook, Paddington); the same ice cream bought twice on 14 (Speed: "1 L vanilla ice cream" and "4 scoops of vanilla ice cream"); a cheese twice on about 27 (Rocky: "6 slices of provolone@Dairy" and "150g provolone@Deli"); white bread twice on 6 (Cinderella Man: "12 slices of white bread" and "8 slices of day-old white bread", screenshot 10); egg yolks or whites listed beside eggs on 80 (Mickey Blue Eyes buys "5 eggs", "4 egg yolks" and "4 egg whites", screenshot 11); KPop Demon Hunters buys "1 bag caster sugar" and "1 packet caster sugar". Salt or pepper twice on a few (Cinderella Man "salt and cracked black pepper" plus "sea salt flakes" plus "cracked black pepper"; Get Shorty, Once Upon a Time in America). "vegetable oil, for frying" is on 160 lists and "1 pinch" lines (saffron, salt, oregano) on 156; also "1 whole nutmeg, to grate" and "300ml strong espresso, cooled". Timings are mostly realistic, but a few overnight steps are timed short ("Pour into the case and chill overnight. 4 hr" in Dead Man's Chest, "set overnight. 5 hr 20 min" in The Last Jedi) and Lady and the Tramp prints "Drain overnight in the fridge (included in cook time)."

9. **Small English and readability slips.** Knives Out prints "A case with a hole in the center". "Ketchup" in A Goofy Movie and the Mutant Mayhem card, whose recipe also lists "1 dill pickles", "1 tomato ketchup", "1 American mustard". Narnia's Turkish delight is "Chewy rosewater candy". On desktop a few small notes open under 13px in the full-size view: Splash 11.5, Inside Man 11.6, Lady and the Tramp 11.6 (the red italic sub-notes), Lethal Weapon 12.5. Main notes measured 13px or more on 33 of 34 films.

## To reach 9.5

- Rebuild every film page from the same data the card prints, house dishes included, and fail the build if any page note differs from the card or any family page names an alcohol.
- Cut every note tail that is plot with no tie to the dish, every bare quote, and every note that only restates the dish name; fix the contradictions (lentil, cherry, conch) and the Dreamgirls cut-off; give the hand-built one-offs the same food-first, full-stop rule.
- Print the showtime cue on all 86 cards that lack it and fix the Twins cue.
- Map venue type to wording: no takeaway delivery block on a home or temple, no tray hatch at a house, no banquet courses at a fast food counter, no prom heading on a birthday, no truck wording at a shop.
- Price by venue and decade, not by layout; no prices at private islands, parties and private dinners; price every dish or none.
- Sweep for post-2010 food words (chipotle mayo, hot honey, sriracha, whipped ricotta or feta, salted caramel, butterfly pea, skillet cookie, cold-fermented, poke, street corn) in films set before 2000, and replace plain items with real dishes.
- Merge shopping lines by ingredient (butter, ice cream flavour, cheese, bread, eggs), keep one salt and one pepper line, and drop "for frying", "pinch", "to grate" and "cooled" from the list; time overnight steps as the night before.
- Lift the smallest desktop sub-notes to 13px.
