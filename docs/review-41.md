# Reel Dinner, review 41

Independent cold review of branch fix/review-40, scored on what the site prints today. Sources: a dump of all 1,000 cards taken 12:36pm today (photos on), with Kingsman: The Secret Service (2014), Red Dog (2011) and Turner & Hooch (1989) dumped again after their changes; the built film pages in menu/<slug>/index.html; and a browser at 1280 desktop and 390 phone. I wrote my own sweeps over every card face, print list, phone menu, prep sheet and film page, then read 160 cards in full (130 shared layouts across all 40 layouts, eras from 1937 to 2026, 30 kids, family, Christmas and wizard cards, and 30 hand-built one-offs).

## Overall: 7.9 / 10

## What is strong

- No em or en dashes on any card face, phone menu, prep sheet or film page across all 1,000 films.
- Alcohol on kids, fairytale, Christmas and wizard cards and phone menus is clean: every hit I found on those surfaces was a false alarm (cocktail franks, Scotch eggs, scotch bonnet, ginger ale, mulled grape juice).
- The hand-built one-offs are the best thing on the site. The Godfather's Louis' card ("We cook with Genco Pura olive oil. Prices subject to change. OLinville 2-1972"), the Slaughtered Lamb bar tab for An American Werewolf in London where the snacks really do add to the "Carried over £8.50" and the full tab to "£11.95" (screenshot 08), and the Caddyshack scorecard where the yardage doubles as the price.
- Prices mostly behave. Dirty Dancing's 1963 resort prices sit in cents and dollars ("Watermelon sorbet 75¢", "Sunday buffet brisket $1.95"), Singin' in the Rain's 1952 commissary runs from "Sparkling lemonade in coupes 5¢" to "Premiere night roast beef with Yorkshires 45¢", Pretty Woman's room service is in 1990 hotel money.
- Prep sheets plan the night well. 167 sheets say "start the day before" at the top when a dish needs it, steps carry times, make-ahead notes are practical, and only 7 shopping lists buy the same thing twice.
- Desktop at 1280 is clear and calm (screenshots 01, 08, 09).
- Template words mostly fit the venue now: hotel words only print at hotels, house parties on the prom layout drop "Before the Dance", home cards say "Serves everyone on the couch".

## What holds it back

1. **Film pages do not match the card (130 pages, 133 dishes).** On shared layouts, 130 film pages list a dish the printed card and phone menu do not carry. Turner & Hooch shows "Cypress Beach beer-battered fish and chips" with "300ml lager" in the recipe, and the card has only one main, "Steak, and the bone for Hooch" (screenshot 03). Others: Hocus Pocus "Billy Butcherson's clam chowder" (125ml white wine), The Man from Snowy River "Harrison's homestead roast" (125ml red wine), Batman Returns "Penguin's sewer fish and chips" (300ml lager), Jaws "Shark fin tortilla chips with salsa", Forrest Gump "Bus stop ham and honey mustard biscuits".

2. **Alcohol on 20 kids, family, Christmas and wizard film pages that the card has already cleaned.** Violent Night's page note says "Ginger beer with a smoky maple tea shot", but the recipe right under it says "4 cans cold alcohol-free stout (440ml each)" and "Pour the alcohol-free stout into each glass" (screenshot 04). Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them: "100ml non-alcoholic gin, for the grown-ups" (the phone says "100ml cucumber juice"). The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King: "4 375ml bottles alcohol-free pale ale" (phone: ginger beer). The Two Towers: "200ml alcohol-free beer" plus "150ml red wine". Miracle on 34th Street (1947): "1 tbsp red wine" (phone: "1 tbsp grape juice"). Also The Holiday, Krampus, It's a Wonderful Life, Flash Gordon, The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug ("Elvenking's baked brie with red wine figs"), Bad Santa, Four Christmases ("Howard's beer-braised bratwurst bites"), School of Rock, Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows: Part 1, The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies ("Bag End hot spiced apple cider"), Miracle on 34th Street (1994) ("Hot spiced cider").

3. **Dish notes still end on plot recap (about 190 notes on about 150 films).** My sweep flagged 321 notes with a non-food tail; on reading them roughly 6 in 10 are plain recap with no tie to the dish. Examples as printed:
   - The Fugitive: "Silky livers, butter, thyme, toasted rye. Kimble finds Provasic damages the liver."
   - Close Encounters of the Third Kind: "Chicken roasted with potatoes and green beans. The family dinner that goes off the rails."
   - Megamind: "Breast filled with spinach and ricotta, lemon butter sauce, herbed rice, Bernard isn't who he seems."
   - Captain America: The First Avenger: "Beef stew in Italy, the night Steve hears Bucky's unit is missing."
   - Maleficent: "Pork, crackling and apples. Stefan took her wings for a crown."
   - Ladies in Black: "Capsicums stuffed with paprika beef, pork and rice in tomato sauce, from Rudi, who proposes to Fay."
   - Uncle Buck: "Oranges squeezed fresh and poured cold. Poured before Buck locks Bug in the boot."
   - The phone menu adds more on 87 dishes, for example The Dark Knight "Bruce turns up with his ballerina date." and John Wick "Ask Charlie about a dinner reservation for twelve."

   A smaller group does not lead with food at all: Young Frankenstein "The hermit pours hot soup into the monster's lap.", Gran Torino "For anyone not on Walt's beer.", Edward Scissorhands "You can't buy the necessities of life with cookies.", Kill Bill: Vol. 2 "A fish not flapping on the carpet.", Encino Man "Corn Nuts by the bag, says Stoney." (for buttered corn on the cob), Cast Away "Coconut milk is a natural laxative.", Clerks III "Iced tea for the man who just had a heart attack."

4. **Notes that just repeat the dish name (86).** Rocky II "Chianti in a straw flask": "Chianti red wine in a straw flask." Waitress "Cheddar and apple hand pies": "Hand pies filled with cheddar and apple." Serendipity "Foot-long chilli cheese dog": "Foot-long hot dog with chilli and cheese." Free Willy "Grilled corn with butter and paprika": "Corn on the cob with butter and paprika." The Hunchback of Notre Dame crostini: "Brie and fig, tomato and basil."

5. **Notes cut mid-sentence (2 cards, 11 phone menus).** The card and phone for Practical Magic print "Lavender lemonade / with lavender syrup. Plant lavender for luck." The Nutty Professor card prints "with a raspberry syrup float that turns it pink as you stir". Phone only: Total Recall "with a splash of cherry syrup over ice." (screenshot 05), Shrek 2, Mulan, Logan, Arrival, Free Willy, Onward, Next Goal Wins ("water from the green nut."), I Love You, Man.

6. **Australian words on cards set elsewhere (about 330 non-Australian cards).** The brief allows Australian product names in recipes and shopping lists, but these are on the printed card:
   - Forrest Gump: "Barbecue prawns", "Bubba's prawn gumbo", "Fifteen Dr Peppers". It is a shrimp film (screenshots 02, 09).
   - US school canteens print "Maths", "Sport" and "Pudding": Napoleon Dynamite (screenshot 01), Heathers, 10 Things I Hate About You, Spider-Man: Homecoming, Clueless, Mean Girls.
   - Superbad: "spun in a car park", "Corner shop Worcestershire snack mix". Deadpool: "Dinner with his flatmate Blind Al." Home Alone: "Suburb / Winnetka". Fantastic Four: "Cob loaf". Good Burger: "choc tops". The Notebook: "Blue swimmer crab cakes" in South Carolina. Batman Forever: "eye fillet", "capsicum", "sweet chilli". Fright Night: "steak with rocket".
   - "Tater tots" still prints on Napoleon Dynamite on the card ("Tater Tots Served Daily", "Serve the tater tots at lunch"), phone, prep sheet and film page, so it fails the spelling test.

7. **Recipe line bugs on phone, prep and pages.** "star anises" (23 films, for example Ghost "2 star anises"), "passionfruits" (45 phone lines), "trouts" (A Quiet Place "2 whole trouts"), "bratwursts", "chorizos", "zucchinis", "soft roll rolls" (Good Will Hunting, The Flintstones, Never Been Kissed, Straight Outta Compton pages), "1 black tea bags" and "2 tin crushed tomatoes" (pages), "1 bowl olive" (The Wog Boy), "8 whole strawberries to tops" (Midsommar), "1 L water at 80C" with no degree sign (4 phones). Doubled words: Minions "5 tbsp caramel sauce sauce", The 'Burbs "120g caramel sauce sauce", Raising Arizona "2 tsp chilli chilli powder", The Terminal "600ml iced coffee coffee", The Shining "with a snowy feta dip dip." Page and phone also disagree on amounts in a few: Newsies "8 tsp yellow mustard" against "3 tbsp yellow mustard", Meet the Robinsons "1/2 jar peanut butter" against "1 jar peanut butter".

8. **Phone tab names hide the course on 100 plus films.** Forrest Gump's tabs read "Issued / All hands / Rationed / Logged" (screenshot 02); Lethal Weapon 3 "Before Midnight / The Night Shift / The Dessert Case / Behind the Counter"; Newsies "Early edition / Front page / Sweet stuff / Late extra"; Wall Street and 22 others put sweets under "On the board tonight"; 35 club cards put sweets under "After the Show". A cook at the bench cannot tell which tab holds the drinks.

9. **Template words that do not suit the venue.**

   - Lab layout at a home or a pantry: I Am Legend "Ration sheet. Hot food. Eat it fast." and "Canteen" for the drinks at Robert Neville's pantry; Despicable Me, Inside Out and Saw carry "Return tray to the hatch. Rations subject to supply."
   - Canteen school words at a mansion or university: Atlantis: The Lost Empire (Whitmore's Lunch Hall) and Wicked (Shiz University) get "Period" and "Lunch ticket".
   - Tango & Cash's prison mess hall serves "Tango's sharp-dressed Manhattan".
   - Small but odd: Hocus Pocus 2 "Photo booth" at a birthday in the woods; Napoleon Dynamite's card prints "Main tray" above "Starters".

    - The Grinch: "Donna Who's after-night-shift mac and cheese $22.00" against "Whoville roast beast $22.25".

11. **Period slips.**
    - Diners from 1958 to 1973 serve 2010s "smashed patties": La Bamba "Two smashed patties with American cheese and pickles, from a 1950s diner", Dirty Dancing 1963, American Graffiti 1973.
    - Dirty Dancing 1963: "Cold watermelon wedges with feta, mint, lime and flaky salt".
    - The Rocky Horror Picture Show 1975: "Red lips cranberry cosmopolitan" (the drink dates from the mid 1980s).
    - Films with no entry in data/film-set-year.json are judged by release year, so period films slip through: Three Amigos! (set 1916) "El Guapo's margaritas", Brave "DunBroch braised lamb, neeps and tatties" (no potatoes in early Scotland), Pinocchio "Spaghetti and meatballs", Hercules "Greek frappé", Mulan "Spring onion pancakes with chilli oil" and "Training camp edamame".

12. **True to the film slips.**
    - Jingle All the Way prints "Dementor not invited" on its parade ticket, a Harry Potter line.
    - Good Burger has two fries on one card: "Otis's fries with Ed's sauce" and "Fries with Ed's secret sauce".
    - Wish has "Rosas market bread" (toast with garlic and tomato) and "Pan con tomate with manchego" (toast with tomato) side by side.
    - Hand-built Inside Man and Big name a dish one way on the card and another on the phone ("Delivery pizzettes" against "Party pizzettes", "Big city hot dog" against "Corner cart hot dog").

13. **Prep and shopping list slips.**
    - Method words on about 120 shopping lists: "3 sheets of frozen puff pastry, thawed" (46 lists say thawed), "1.2kg chicken wings, tips off, split" (Bruce Almighty, screenshot 07), "500g mussels, cleaned", "1 vanilla bean, split", "4 passionfruits, pulp", "1 bag corn chips, warmed", "1 Lebanese cucumber, in ribbons".
    - Bruce Almighty's iced coffee is headed "Takes 10 min" with no day-before flag anywhere, then says "leave in the fridge for 12 hours, then strain. 4 min" (screenshot 06).
    - The Godfather's arancini "You need" list starts at "400g cold cooked risotto", but the method cooks it from arborio, butter, stock and parmesan that the list never names.

## To reach 9.5

1. Rebuild every film page from the same data the card and phone use. Drop the 133 page-only dishes on 130 pages, and clear beer, stout, ale, gin, cider and red wine from the 20 kids, family, Christmas and wizard pages so the page, card and phone say the same thing word for word.
2. Rewrite the dish notes. Cut the plot-recap tails (about 190), rewrite the 86 notes that restate the name, fix the 13 notes cut mid-sentence, and make every note open on food.
3. Clear Australian words off cards set outside Australia: prawns to shrimp in US settings (Forrest Gump first), Maths, Sport and Pudding off US school canteens, plus car park, corner shop, flatmate, boot, Suburb, cob loaf, choc tops, eye fillet, rump, capsicum and rocket where the film is American. Swap "tater tots" on Napoleon Dynamite for "tots" on all four surfaces.
4. Fix the recipe line generator: no plurals on uncountable or already-plural items (star anise, passionfruit, trout, bratwurst, chorizo, zucchini), no doubled words, keep the degree sign. Strip method words from shopping lists and keep them in the "You need" lines.
5. Give every phone tab a plain course word (for example "Issued, starters" or just "Starters") so a cook can find drinks and sweets at a glance.
6. Fix the remaining template slips: the lab layout on Fantastic Beasts, I Am Legend, Despicable Me, Inside Out and Saw; school words at Atlantis and Wicked; "Dementor not invited" on Jingle All the Way; duplicate dishes on Good Burger and Wish.
7. Tidy the price outliers listed above, and give Bad Santa's food court a currency sign.
8. Add story years to data/film-set-year.json for the obvious period films (Titanic, Braveheart, Brave, Three Amigos!, Pinocchio, Hercules, Mulan, Gone with the Wind, The Prestige, Pirates of the Caribbean), then fix what that turns up. Swap smashed patties off the pre-1980 diners.
9. Plan Bruce Almighty's iced coffee as a day-before job, and list the risotto ingredients in The Godfather's arancini.
