Help and support

Last updated 11 November 2026

What Reel Dinner is. Spin a wheel of 900 films and get a themed dinner menu to match, with recipes, timings and a shopping list. It is free, there is no account to make, and your picks stay on your device.

Get help

Email privacy@reeldinner.com. It is the one address for everything: problems, questions, privacy requests and feedback.

It helps if you tell us:

  • What you were doing and what went wrong
  • The film, if it is about a menu
  • Your phone or computer and browser, for example iPhone and Safari
  • Your member number, if it is about your member card. Never send your PIN.

Reel Dinner is run by one person, so a reply can take a few days.

Picking a film

How do I spin?

On the Box office tab, tap Spin in the middle of the wheel. Use Tonight's programme to choose which films are on the wheel. To skip the wheel, type a title in Find a film.

Can I pick a film another way?

Yes. Galaxy Video has every film on the shelves, sorted into aisles, with search and filters. Tap a tape to read the back. The Candy bar starts from food instead: tap a treat and it finds films that serve it.

How do I stop seeing films I have already watched?

Open My movie checklist under the wheel, tick the films you have seen, and leave Skip watched films when spinning switched on. You can also tap Mark as watched under More options on any menu.

Menus, prep and printing

How do I lock in a film for the week?

Open the film's menu and tap Lock in for this week. It shows in the This week's film panel, where you pick the cook night (Friday, Saturday or Sunday) and the showtime, open the shopping list, or clear it.

Can I get a reminder?

Yes. Tap Add to calendar on the This week's film panel. On an iPhone, iPad or computer it saves a calendar event with reminders for when to start cooking and any prep to do ahead. On Android it saves the file and gives you Google Calendar links to add the night and each reminder.

How do I print the menu or save a PDF?

Tap Save PDF under the menu and choose Menu only, Prep sheet only, or Menu and prep sheet. Print makes the menu and prep sheet in one file and opens it ready to print. More options also has Save image, Print to fold for some menus, Share this menu and Copy text.

How do I change the number of guests?

Open the Prep sheet and shopping list tab and use the plus and minus buttons next to Guests. Recipes and the shopping list scale from 1 to 60. To change the starting number for every film, open Settings and set Usual guests. Vegetarian and No alcohol are in the same two places.

Kids mode

What does kids mode do?

It shows only films rated G or PG on the wheel and in Galaxy Video, and every menu, prep sheet and shopping list uses the alcohol-free versions of its drinks and recipes. Turn it on in Settings, or with the Kids mode switch in Galaxy Video's filters.

Your member card

What is the member card for?

Your Galaxy Video member card lives on your device and keeps your rentals and stats. If you want your picks on another phone or computer, open Galaxy Video, scroll to your card and tap Put my card online, then choose a 4-digit PIN. On the other device, tap Use my card from another device and enter your member number and PIN. Your watched films, favourites, ratings, history, weekly plan and kids mode then stay in step. It is optional and asks for no email or phone number.

How do I delete my online card?

Open your member card in Galaxy Video and tap Delete my online card, then tap again to confirm. The button only shows while you are signed in, so if you signed out, sign in again with Use my card from another device first. The online copy is removed straight away. Your picks stay on this device until you clear them. If you no longer have the device, email us your member number and we will delete it.

I forgot my PIN.

We cannot tell you your PIN. Email us your member number and we can delete the online copy so you can start again. If the app says your card is locked, email us your member number too.

Requesting a movie

A film I want is not on the wheel.

Tap Request a movie, on the Box office tab or at Galaxy Video under Not on the shelves. Enter the title and year, and your name and a note if you like, then tap Send request. We read every request, but we cannot promise to add every film.

Offline and your data

Why is a poster missing?

Posters and film photos load from TMDB over the internet. With no signal, a poster you have not viewed recently may not show. The menu, prep sheet and shopping list still work for films saved on your device.

Can I use it with no signal?

Menus you have opened, and anything you tap Save to favourites on, are kept on your device. Offline, the wheel only lands on films whose menus are saved. Open a few films while you are online and they will be there next time.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in your browser or app storage. That covers watched films, favourites, ratings, ticket stubs, your weekly plan, shopping ticks and settings. Nothing leaves your device unless you put your member card online or send a film request. To clear it, open Settings and use Clear ticket stubs, Clear saved menus or Reset everything.
