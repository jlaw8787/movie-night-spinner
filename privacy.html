Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version. You can use Reel Dinner without an account. Your picks stay on your device unless you choose to put your member card online. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What stays on your device

Reel Dinner saves these in your browser or app storage, on your device only:

  • Films you have marked as watched, your favourites and your star ratings
  • Your spin history, weekly plan, shopping ticks and pantry items
  • Your settings, such as diet, guests, sound and kids mode
  • Your member card number and the name you type on it, if you add one

You can clear all of it at any time from Settings, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

The online member card (optional)

If you choose "Put my card online", we store a copy so you can use your card on another device. That copy holds your member number, a scrambled version of your 4-digit PIN (we cannot read the PIN itself), the name on your card if you added one, and your watched list, favourites, ratings, history, weekly plan, your rentals (how many, which aisles, and the tapes you have out), the treats you ticked at the counter, and your kids mode and skip watched films settings.

It is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in Sydney. We do not ask for your email, phone number or address.

Deleting it. Open your member card in Galaxy Video and choose "Delete my online card". The online copy is removed straight away. Your picks stay on your device until you clear them.

Request a movie

When you send a film request, we receive the film title, the year, and your name and note if you add them. It is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in Sydney. We use it only to decide which films to add.

Other services the site talks to

Like any website, loading Reel Dinner sends your IP address and basic device details to the services that deliver it. We do not get a copy of that for tracking, and we do not build profiles.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site
  • TMDB supplies film posters and photos
  • YouTube plays a trailer, only when you press play
  • Links to JustWatch, TMDB and streaming services open those sites, which have their own policies

Children

Reel Dinner is made for households planning a movie night. Kids mode shows only G and PG films and makes every menu alcohol-free; it collects nothing extra. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about your online member card, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us with your member number and we will sort it out. If you are in Australia and are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
