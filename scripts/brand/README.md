Galaxy Video logo, flat print version

galaxy-video.html is the stacked logo, galaxy-video-wide.html the horizontal one. Both are plain SVG.
Render with: python3 render.py galaxy-video.html out.png (needs Playwright and the Google fonts).

Exports in assets/brand: galaxy-video-1200/800/480.webp (stacked, on its purple plate)
and galaxy-video-wide-1200/600.webp (wide, rounded plate).
galaxy-video-wide-clear.png is the wide logo with no plate, used for the lit shop sign
(scripts/compose-shopfront-real.py). The member card and case sticker
(scripts/compose-brand-extras.py) use the stacked logo with no plate, rendered at 1536x1024.
